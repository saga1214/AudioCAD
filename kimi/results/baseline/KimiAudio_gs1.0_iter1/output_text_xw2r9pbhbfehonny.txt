Sure, here’s a checklist for you:

1. **Equipment Check**: Make sure your phone is fully charged and has enough storage space. If you have a camera, bring that too.

2. **Permissions**: Ask the nursing home if it’s okay to film the show. They might have specific rules or need to inform residents.

3. **Lighting**: Ensure the room is well-lit. Natural light is best, but if not, use a good camera with good low-light capabilities.

4. **Sound**: Good sound quality is crucial. If your phone’s mic isn’t great, consider using an external mic.

5. **Backup**: Bring a spare battery and maybe a portable charger.

6. **Content**: Rehearse your show a few times to ensure it’s smooth and engaging. Practice your timing.

7. **Engagement**: Make sure to interact with the audience. They love it when you acknowledge them.

8. **Timing**: Keep an eye on the time. Try to finish within the allotted time frame.

9. **Post-Show**: Thank the audience and the nursing home staff for their support.

10. **Upload**: After the show, upload the video to your YouTube channel, making sure to add a description with tags like “puppet show,” “children’s entertainment,” etc.

11. **Promotion**: Share the video on social media and with your network.

12. **Feedback**: Ask for feedback from the nursing home staff or residents to improve future shows.

Remember, the goal is to have fun and bring joy, so enjoy the process!.
