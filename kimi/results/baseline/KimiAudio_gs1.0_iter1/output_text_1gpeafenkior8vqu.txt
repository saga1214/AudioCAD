Sure, here's a suggested itinerary for deeply exploring Tokyo and Osaka, with a day trip to Nara and Kyoto from Osaka:

**Tokyo (5 days)**

Day 1: Arrival and Exploring Shinjuku
- Check into your hotel
- Visit Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building for a free view of the city
- Explore Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden
- Dinner in Shinjuku

Day 2: Asakusa and Ueno
- Asakusa: Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Shopping Street
- Ueno: Ueno Park, Tokyo National Museum
- Dinner in Ueno

Day 3: Shibuya and Harajuku
- Shibuya: Shibuya Crossing, Hachiko Statue
- Harajuku: Takeshita Street, Meiji Shrine
- Dinner in Harajuku

Day 4: Akihabara and Odaiba
- Akihabara: Explore electronics and anime culture
- Odaiba: Miraikan Science Museum, Palette Town
- Dinner in Akihabara

Day 5: Day Trip to Nikko
- Travel to Nikko (about 2 hours by train)
- Visit Toshogu Shrine, Rinno-ji Temple
- Return to Tokyo in the evening

**Osaka (4 days)**

Day 6: Osaka Castle and Dotonbori
- Osaka Castle
- Explore Dotonbori for food and nightlife
- Dinner in Dotonbori

Day 7: Universal Studios Japan
- Full day at Universal Studios Japan
- Dinner in Universal City

Day 8: Nara and Kyoto
- Travel to Nara (about 30 minutes by train)
- Visit Todai-ji Temple, Nara Park
- Travel to Kyoto (about 1 hour by train)
- Explore Kyoto: Fushimi Inari Shrine, Kiyomizu-dera Temple
- Dinner in Kyoto

Day 9: Osaka and Departure
- Explore Osaka: Shinsaibashi, Shitennoji Temple
- Travel to Osaka Station for your departure

This schedule allows for a good balance of sightseeing, cultural experiences, and relaxation. Remember to check train schedules and book tickets in advance, especially for popular attractions. Enjoy your trip!
