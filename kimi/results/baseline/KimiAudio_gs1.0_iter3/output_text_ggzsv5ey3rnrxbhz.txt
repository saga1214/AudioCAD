While it's true that PFAS are persistent, there are steps you can take to reduce your exposure and potentially lower your levels over time.

First, focus on the major sources we've discussed: non-stick cookware, stain-resistant fabrics, certain cosmetics, and tap water.

Second, consider a comprehensive lifestyle change. This might include eating a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains, which can help support your body's natural detoxification processes.

Lastly, if you're concerned about your current levels, you might want to consider consulting with a healthcare professional about potential testing and treatment options. They can provide personalized advice based on your specific situation.

Remember, it's about making gradual, sustainable changes rather than trying extreme measures like juice cleanses or water fasts.
