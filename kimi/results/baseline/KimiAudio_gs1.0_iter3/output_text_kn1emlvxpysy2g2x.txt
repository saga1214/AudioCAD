1. **Pinot Noir**:
   - **Storage**: 55 degrees Fahrenheit (13 Celsius)
   - **Serving**: 55 to 60 degrees Fahrenheit (13 to 16 Celsius)
   - **Best for**: Cold weather

2. **Hon-Mirin**:
   - **Storage**: 60 to 68 degrees Fahrenheit (16 to 20 Celsius)
   - **Serving**: 68 to 75 degrees Fahrenheit (20 to 24 Celsius)
   - **Best for**: Room temperature, not typically served cold

3. **Chianti**:
   - **Storage**: 55 degrees Fahrenheit (13 Celsius)
   - **Serving**: 55 to 60 degrees Fahrenheit (13 to 16 Celsius)
   - **Best for**: Cold weather

4. **Pinot Grigio**:
   - **Storage**: 50 to 55 degrees Fahrenheit (10 to 13 Celsius)
   - **Serving**: Chilled, around 45 to 50 degrees Fahrenheit (7 to 10 Celsius)
   - **Best for**: Cold weather

5. **Champagne**:
   - **Storage**: 45 to 50 degrees Fahrenheit (7 to 10 Celsius)
   - **Serving**: 45 to 50 degrees Fahrenheit (7 to 10 Celsius)
   - **Best for**: Cold weather

6. **Sauvignon Blanc**:
   - **Storage**: 50 to 55 degrees Fahrenheit (10 to 13 Celsius)
   - **Serving**: Chilled, around 45 to 50 degrees Fahrenheit (7 to 10 Celsius)
   - **Best for**: Cold weather

For hot weather, **Sauvignon Blanc** and **Champagne** are great choices because they’re best served cold. For cold weather, **Pinot Noir**, **Chianti**, and **Pinot Grigio** are ideal because they’re best served at cooler temperatures.
