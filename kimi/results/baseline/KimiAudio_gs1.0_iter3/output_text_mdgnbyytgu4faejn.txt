A skills section is very important, especially if you're changing careers. It shows the hiring manager that you have the right skills for the job, even if you don't have direct experience.

Here's a basic format for a resume:

1. Contact Information: Your name, email, phone number, and city.
2. Professional Summary: A short paragraph highlighting your most relevant skills and career goals.
3. Relevant Experience: A section listing your older jobs that are a perfect match for the roles you're applying for now.
4. Additional Work History: A section listing your more recent jobs.
5. Education: A section listing your degrees, including any in progress.
6. Skills: A section listing your relevant skills.
7. Awards and Certificates: A section listing any relevant awards or certificates.

Remember to keep your resume concise and easy to read. Use bullet points and clear headings to make it easy for the hiring manager to scan. Good luck!
