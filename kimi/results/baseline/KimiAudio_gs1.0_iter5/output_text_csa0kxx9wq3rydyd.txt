For the main course, you'll need your smoker and your oven for the prime rib roast. You'll also need your oven for the baked macaroni and cheese and the bread stuffing. For the sides, you'll need your toaster oven for the roasted asparagus and your Instant Pot for the cranberry sauce. For the salads, you can use your stovetop or microwave. And for the desserts, you'll need your oven for the no-bake pumpkin cheesecake and the slow cooker for the apple crisp.
