Austin's got plenty of laid-back spots. If you're in the mood for something casual, you might want to check out places like Home Slice Pizza for a slice of New York-style pizza, or Franklin Barbecue for some great barbecue. If you're into Tex-Mex, Torchy's Tacos is a local favorite. They're all pretty chill and should give you a good taste of the city. Enjoy your trip!
