Got it!Here are the recipes for the cocktails you mentioned:

**Whiskey Sour:**
- 2 oz bourbon
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice
- 1/2 oz simple syrup
- 1 egg white (optional)
- Ice
- Cherry or orange slice for garnish

Shake the bourbon, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice. If using egg white, dry shake first without ice, then add ice and shake again. Strain into a rocks glass over ice and garnish.

**Aviation:**
- 2 oz gin
- 1/2 oz maraschino liqueur
- 1/4 oz crème de violette
- 1/2 oz fresh lemon juice
- Ice
- Cherry for garnish

Shake the gin, maraschino liqueur, crème de violette, and lemon juice with ice. Strain into a cocktail glass and garnish.

**French 75:**
- 1 oz gin
- 1/2 oz lemon juice
- 1/2 oz simple syrup
- Ice
- Sparkling wine
- Cherry or lemon twist for garnish

Shake the gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice. Strain into a champagne flute and top with sparkling wine. Garnish with a cherry or lemon twist.

**Paper Plane:**
- 1 oz bourbon
- 1 oz Aperol
- 1 oz Amaro Nonino
- 1/2 oz fresh lemon juice
- Ice
- Orange peel for garnish

Shake the bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and lemon juice with ice. Strain into a coupe glass and garnish with an orange peel.
