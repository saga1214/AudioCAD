Sure, here's a shopping list for your nautical-themed pirate party:

**Snacks:**
- Mini sliders (theme them as "buried treasure" sandwiches)
- Small rolls
- Veggie sticks (carrots, celery, cucumbers)
- Cheese cubes
- Goldfish crackers
- Grapes (theme them as "pirate jewels")
- Pretzel rods (theme them as "pirate ship masts")
- Juice boxes (theme them as "pirate punch" or "sea water")
- Water bottles
- Snack containers (theme them as "pirate treasure chests")

**Decorations:**
- Pirate-themed tablecloth
- Balloons (blue and red for a nautical theme)
- Pirate flags or banners
- Treasure chest decorations
- Pirate-themed plates, cups, and napkins

**Activities:**
- Treasure hunt
- Pirate-themed games (e.g., "Walk the Plank," "Pin the Eye Patch on the Pirate")
- Pirate-themed crafts (e.g., making pirate hats, eye patches, or treasure maps)

**Miscellaneous:**
- Trash bags
- Sunscreen
- Hats and sunglasses for sun protection
- First-aid kit

Remember to check with the parents of the other children attending the party to ensure that any food allergies or dietary restrictions are accommodated. Have a great party!!
