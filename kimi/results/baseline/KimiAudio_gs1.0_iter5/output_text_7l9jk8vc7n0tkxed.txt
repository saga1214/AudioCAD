1. **Identify the Source**: Determine where the blockage is occurring. Is it in a sink, toilet, or shower?

2. **Gather Tools**: You'll need a plunger, a snake (also known as an auger), and possibly some baking soda and vinegar.

3. **Plunger**: For a sink or toilet, use the plunger to create suction. Make sure the plunger is fully submerged in water and give it several strong, steady pumps.

4. **Snake**: If the plunger doesn't work, use the snake to reach deeper into the pipes. Insert the snake into the drain and rotate it as you push it further in. Pull it back out to see if the blockage has been dislodged.

5. **Baking Soda and Vinegar**: If the snake doesn't work, mix baking soda and vinegar to create a chemical reaction that can help break down the blockage. Pour the mixture down the drain and let it sit for a few hours before trying to plunge again.

6. **Call a Professional**: If all else fails, it might be time to call a professional plumber. They have specialized tools and expertise to handle severe blockages.

Remember, patience is key. Sometimes these issues take time to resolve.
