For a retro tech theme, you could do a “Silicon Valley Salad” with mixed greens, a “Pixel Pesto Pasta” for a fun twist on pesto, and “Circuit Board Chips” with a side of “Binary Dip” (like a garlic aioli). For snacks, you could have “Data Bits” (mini pretzels) and “Byte Bites” (mini quiches). These are simple, themed, and should be enjoyable for everyone.
