That's a great approach. You want to show respect while also being clear about your concerns.

Here are some pointers:

1. Start with a polite greeting and express your appreciation for his time.
2. State your purpose: you want to understand the basis for your grade.
3. Be specific about what you're questioning. For example, you could say, "I'm concerned about the subjectivity in grading, especially since the exam was more essay-based."
4. Show that you're open to feedback and learning. You could say, "I'm eager to improve for future exams and would appreciate any guidance you can offer."
5. End with a positive note, expressing your commitment to the course and your desire to learn.

Remember, the goal is to have a productive conversation, not to argue or accuse. Good luck!
