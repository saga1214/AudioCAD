Sure, here are the two separate lists:

**20-mile segment:**
- Base layers (top and bottom)
- Mid-layer
- Wind-resistant outer layer
- Good headlamp with extra batteries
- Gloves
- Warm hat
- Buff or gaiter
- Trail shoes
- Light insulated jacket
- Small running pack or vest

**12-mile segment:**
- Same as above, but you can consider bringing a thin insulated jacket if you're still feeling cold after the 20-mile segment.
