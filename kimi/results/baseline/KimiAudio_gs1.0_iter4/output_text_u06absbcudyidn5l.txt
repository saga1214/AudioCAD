Sure, I'd be happy to explain it again. Vasculitis is a condition where the blood vessels in your body become inflamed. This can happen in different sizes of blood vessels, which is why there are different types of vasculitis.

As for other potential causes of redness, there are many possibilities. Some common ones include rosacea, eczema, or even certain medications. It's really important to see a dermatologist to get a proper diagnosis.

Regarding vasculitis symptoms, they can vary depending on which blood vessels are affected. Common symptoms include fever, fatigue, and skin rashes or sores. The inflammation can also cause pain or aching in the affected area. It's a complex condition, and it's important to work with a healthcare professional to manage it.
