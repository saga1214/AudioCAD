For tailoring, a good set of measuring tapes or a new sewing machine accessory could be great. For fountain pen restoration, maybe a set of precision tools or a rare pen to restore. For woodworking or clay, a new tool or a class to learn a new skill could be perfect. And for pottery, a wheel or a kiln could be a dream come true. It really depends on where you are in your journey and what you're looking to improve or explore next.
