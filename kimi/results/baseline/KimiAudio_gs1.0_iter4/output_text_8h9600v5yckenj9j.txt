For Halloween, you can't go wrong with classics like Reese's Peanut Butter Cups, Snickers, M&M's, and Kit Kats. Those are always crowd-pleasers. If you want to mix it up a bit, consider adding sour candies like Sour Patch Kids or Sour Skittles. For something a little different, you could also get candy bars like Twix or 3 Musketeers. If you're looking for something a bit more unique, consider getting candy that's shaped like Halloween characters or has Halloween-themed packaging. Just make sure to check the expiration dates and buy in bulk if you're planning to have a lot of guests.
