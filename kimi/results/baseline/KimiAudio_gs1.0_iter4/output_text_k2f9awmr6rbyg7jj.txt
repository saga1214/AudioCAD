Ingredients:
- 2 slices of bread
- 1/4 cup of artificial crab meat
- 1 tablespoon of mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon of Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon of lemon juice
- 1/4 teaspoon of garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Lettuce, tomato, and any other desired toppings

Instructions:
1. Toast the bread slices to your desired level of crispiness.
2. In a small bowl, mix together the artificial crab meat, mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Spread the mixture evenly on one side of each bread slice.
4. Add your desired toppings, such as lettuce and tomato.
5. Place the bread slices with the mixture facing down on top of the toppings.
6. Cut the sandwich in half and serve immediately.

Enjoy your crab sandwich!.
