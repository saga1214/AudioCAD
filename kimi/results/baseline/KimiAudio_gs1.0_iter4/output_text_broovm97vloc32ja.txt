If you have both the text and illustrations ready, you can definitely submit a complete project. In fact, many publishers are looking for self-contained, fully realized picture books. This is especially true for independent publishers or smaller presses.

You would still need to find the right publisher, but once you do, you can submit your complete project. They'll evaluate it based on its own merits, and if they like it, they'll offer you a contract. If not, they'll let you know and you can try elsewhere.
