Congratulations on assembling your coffee table!!That's a great spot for a variety of snacks!Here are some ideas for snacks you could consider filling your coffee table with:

1. **Nuts and Seeds**: Almonds, cashews, peanuts, sunflower seeds, pumpkin seeds, etc.
2. **Dried Fruits**: Apricots, cranberries, raisins, dates, etc.
3. **Chips and Crackers**: A mix of flavors and types, like tortilla chips, potato chips, pretzels, and various crackers.
4. **Cookies and Biscuits**: A selection of sweet treats like chocolate chip cookies, oatmeal raisin cookies, shortbread, etc.
5. **Candy**: A mix of hard candies, gummies, chocolates, and other sweets.
6. **Popcorn**: Either plain or flavored, this is a fun and easy snack to munch on.
7. **Trail Mix**: A mix of nuts, seeds, and dried fruits, often with a bit of chocolate or candy pieces.
8. **Cheese and Crackers**: A simple but satisfying snack that can be easily customized with different types of cheese and crackers.
9. **Energy Bars**: For a healthier option, consider energy bars or protein bars.
10. **Fresh Fruit**: If you prefer something lighter, consider adding some fresh fruit like apples, oranges, or bananas.

Remember to consider your guests' dietary restrictions and preferences when choosing snacks. Enjoy your coffee table and the snacks!
