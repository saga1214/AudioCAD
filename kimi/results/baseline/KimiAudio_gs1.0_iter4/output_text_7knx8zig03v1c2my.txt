For treats, you want something small, healthy, and that your dog loves. Try pieces of cooked chicken, cheese, or even baby carrots. Avoid anything too fatty or sugary.

Clicker training is a method that uses a sound (like a clicker) to mark the exact moment your dog does something right, followed by a reward. For example, if you want to teach your dog to sit, you click the moment their bottom touches the ground, then give them a treat. The clicker sound helps them associate the action with the reward.

It's a fun and effective way to train, and it's great for building a strong bond with your dog. Just remember to keep the training sessions short and positive!.
