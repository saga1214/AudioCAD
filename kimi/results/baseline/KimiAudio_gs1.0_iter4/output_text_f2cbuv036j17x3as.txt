For brakes, you want to check the wear on the brake pads and rotors. If the pads are worn down to the metal or if you hear a grinding noise when you brake, it’s time to replace them. You can do this yourself with some basic tools, but if you’re not comfortable, it’s best to take it to a mechanic. As for spark plugs, you can check them by removing the spark plug wires and looking at the plugs. If they’re black or wet, it could mean there’s a problem with the fuel or ignition system. You can replace them yourself with the right tools and a bit of patience, but again, if you’re unsure, it’s always best to get a professional to do it. Safety first!
