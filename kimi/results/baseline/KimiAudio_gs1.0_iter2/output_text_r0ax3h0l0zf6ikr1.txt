   - Listen to your 90s hip-hop playlist to get pumped up.
   - Do some light vocal exercises to warm up your voice.
   - Take a few deep breaths to center yourself. Focus on the present moment and the task at hand.

3. **On the Day:**
   - Arrive early to get settled and familiar with the environment.
   - Do a quick run-through of your notes and key points.
   - Stay hydrated and keep some snacks handy for energy.

4. **Dress Code:**
   - For a sunny day, opt for lightweight, breathable clothing. A light-colored, moisture-wicking shirt and comfortable pants or shorts would be good.
   - Wear a hat and sunglasses to protect yourself from the sun.
   - Make sure your shoes are comfortable and provide good support.
