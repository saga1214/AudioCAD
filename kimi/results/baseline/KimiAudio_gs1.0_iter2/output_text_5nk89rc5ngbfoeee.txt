For an end-of-year celebration, flexibility is key. A picnic at the school or a nearby park can be great, especially if you can set up some fun activities like you mentioned. If weather's a concern, a simple indoor gathering with crafts or games can be just as fun. Snacks are always a hit, but keep it simple. Maybe a potluck where parents bring something they're comfortable with? It's all about celebrating the year's fun and making it memorable for everyone. And remember, it's okay to keep it small and sweet. The goal is to celebrate, not stress!
