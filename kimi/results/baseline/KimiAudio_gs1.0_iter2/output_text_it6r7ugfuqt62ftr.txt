Considering you're looking for a mix of activities and relaxation, I'd recommend a trip to Napa Valley. It's about a 2-hour drive from San Francisco, so you'll have plenty of time to enjoy the journey.

For accommodations, I'd suggest the Napa Valley Lodge. It's a beautiful, family-friendly resort with comfortable rooms and a great location.

Here's a rough itinerary for your 4-5 day trip:

Day 1: Arrival and Relaxation
* Check-in at the Napa Valley Lodge
* Spend the afternoon exploring the town of Napa and its charming shops and restaurants
* Enjoy a relaxing dinner at one of the many excellent restaurants in the area

Day 2: Wine Country
* Take a guided wine tour or visit some of the famous wineries like Opus One or Silver Oak
* Enjoy a picnic lunch at a scenic spot
* Return to the lodge for a relaxing evening

Day 3: Outdoor Adventures
* Take a hot air balloon ride over the vineyards (book in advance to ensure availability)
* Visit the nearby Lake Hennessey for a scenic hike or picnic
* Return to the lodge for a relaxing evening

Day 4: More Wine and Relaxation
* Visit another winery or take a wine and food pairing tour
* Enjoy a leisurely lunch at a winery or a local restaurant
* Return to the lodge for a relaxing evening

Day 5: Departure
* Spend the morning shopping for souvenirs or exploring the town of Napa
* Depart for home, feeling refreshed and rejuvenated

For dining, I'd recommend trying some of the local favorites like The French Laundry, Bistro Don Giovanni, or The French Laundry's sister restaurant, The Bistro. Be sure to make reservations well in advance, as these restaurants are quite popular.

This itinerary should give you a good balance of relaxation, adventure, and wine country charm. Enjoy your trip!
