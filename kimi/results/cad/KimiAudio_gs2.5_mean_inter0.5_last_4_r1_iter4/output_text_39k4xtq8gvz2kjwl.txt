Here is a synopsis of the characters and their roles in the story:

1. Leo Vance: A brave explorer and archaeology professor at Kingsbridge University. He is the descendant of Captain Elias Vance, who first discovered the Sunstone. Leo leads the expedition to Eldoria to find the treasure and protect it from Silas Croft.

2. Elena Castillo: A kind woman and local guide who helps Leo and his team navigate the jungle. She plays a crucial role in solving the ancient puzzles and outsmarting Silas Croft.

3. Jack "Hammer" Stone: Leo's trusted friend and a skilled local guide. He provides muscle and support during the expedition.

4. Arthur Finch: A brilliant mapmaker and Leo's colleague at Kingsbridge University. He decodes the complex numerical sequences left by Elias Vance.

5. Javier Rios: A local guide who knows the jungle's hidden trails. He helps Leo and his team find the lost Sunstone of El Dorado.

6. Silas Croft: The villain and Augustus Croft's direct descendant. He is hunting for the Sunstone with a ruthless obsession and brings a team of dangerous henchmen to help him.

7. Marcus Kane: One of Silas Croft's henchmen. He is cunning and resourceful, helping Silas in his quest for the treasure.

8. Brutus Thorne: Another henchman of Silas Croft. He is a brute and a tracker, providing physical strength and hunting skills to the villain's team.

9. Rex Gunner: A cold-hearted tracker and henchman of Silas Croft. He is skilled in tracking and hunting, aiding Silas in his pursuit of the Sunstone.

10. Ivan Petrov: The deadliest sniper and henchman of Silas Croft. He provides long-range support and protection for the villain's team.

These characters, with their unique skills and motivations, create a thrilling adventure as Leo Vance and his team race against time to find and protect the glowing Sunstone of El Dorado.
