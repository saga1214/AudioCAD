Oh, cooking!—what a great idea!!Here are five dinner options that are easy and delicious:

1. **One-Pot Pasta**: A quick and easy pasta dish that's perfect for a relaxing evening. Simply cook pasta, tomato sauce, and your choice of protein (like chicken or shrimp) in one pot.

2. **Grilled Chicken and Veggies**: Marinate chicken breasts and your favorite vegetables (like bell peppers, zucchini, and onions) in olive oil, salt, and pepper. Grill until cooked through, and serve with a side salad.

3. **Tacos**: Make a big batch of taco seasoning and cook ground beef or chicken. Serve with tortillas, shredded cheese, lettuce, and your favorite toppings.

4. **Baked Salmon and Roasted Vegetables**: Season salmon fillets with lemon juice, salt, and pepper, and bake in the oven until cooked through. Roast a variety of vegetables (like broccoli, carrots, and Brussels sprouts) with olive oil and seasonings.

5. **Stuffed Bell Peppers**: Fill bell peppers with a mixture of cooked rice, ground beef or turkey, and your favorite spices. Bake until the peppers are tender and the filling is heated through.
