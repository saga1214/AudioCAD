Absolutely, the older issues are incredibly rare and valuable. For Luke Cage, a must-have from that era is *Captain America* number thirty-eight, his first appearance. You could also look for *All-American Comics* number sixteen, which features the first appearance of the Atom. *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, the first appearance of Batman, is a legendary key issue, though it's extremely rare and expensive.

For good buys from that era, *Action Comics* number twenty-five, the first appearance of Superman, is always in high demand. *Marvel Comics* number fifteen, the first appearance of the Fantastic Four, is another solid, affordable buy. Another classic is *The Adventures of Superman* number four, his first appearance in a solo book. Even a more modern choice like *Marvel Mystery Comics* number thirty, which brought him back to prominence, is smart and affordable.

Just remember, for any of these, the price under five hundred dollars would be for a mid-grade copy. The value is always determined by the comic's condition, so focus on professionally graded books.
