If you're both the author and illustrator, you're in a unique position. Many children's books are self-published by the author-illustrator, especially in the early stages of your career. This way, you maintain full creative control and can keep more of the profits.

However, if you're aiming for a traditional publishing route, it's **very** rare for an author to also be the illustrator. Publishers usually prefer to work with a team, so they'll hire a separate illustrator. But if you're exceptionally talented, you might be able to convince them to let you do both roles. It's worth mentioning in your query letter.
