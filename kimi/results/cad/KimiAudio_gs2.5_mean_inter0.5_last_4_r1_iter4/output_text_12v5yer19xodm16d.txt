Sure, here are some drinks and snacks that might help you stay awake and energized:

Drinks:
1. Coffee: A classic pick-me-up.
2. Tea: Like green tea or black tea, which have caffeine.
3. Energy drinks: They're designed to give you a boost.
4. Lemon water: Refreshing and can help with hydration.
5. Coconut water: Good for electrolytes and hydration.

Snacks:
1. Nuts: Like almonds or cashews, they're high in protein and healthy fats.
2. Dark chocolate: Contains caffeine and can be a quick energy boost.
3. Fresh fruit: Like an apple or banana, they're easy to digest and provide natural sugars.
4. Cheese and crackers: A simple snack that's easy to grab.
5. Energy bars: Look for ones with natural ingredients and no added sugars.

Remember, it's important to stay hydrated and eat balanced meals to keep your energy levels up.
