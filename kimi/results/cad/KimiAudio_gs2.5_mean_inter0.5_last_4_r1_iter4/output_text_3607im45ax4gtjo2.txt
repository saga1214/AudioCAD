Dealing with a crazy neighbor can be really tough.

One piece of advice is to try to stay calm and not let her actions get to you. It's hard, but try to focus on the kids' well-being and their right to play outside.

Communicate with CPS professionals honestly and cooperate with them. They'll likely have some insight on how to handle the situation.

And finally, try to find some support from friends, family or neighbors. Sharing your stress with others can help you feel less alone.
