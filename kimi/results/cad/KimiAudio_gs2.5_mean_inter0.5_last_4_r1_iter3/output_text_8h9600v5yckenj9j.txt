For Halloween parties, you can't go wrong with classics like Reese's Pieces, M&M's, and Snickers. Those are always crowd-pleasers. If you want something a bit spookier, consider getting candy corn, sour candies, or even candy that looks like spiders or bats. If you're looking for something a bit more unique, consider getting a variety pack of different candies. That way, you're covering all bases and giving guests a chance to try something new. And remember, it's always better to have a little extra than not enough, so you can always swap out the leftovers for something else if needed.
