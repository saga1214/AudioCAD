Vegas is all about the experience, so here are a few tips to make the most of it:

1. **Shows and Entertainment:** There are countless shows to choose from, from Cirque du Soleil to magic shows. Check out what's playing and book tickets in advance.

2. **Dining:** From celebrity-chef restaurants to local eateries, there's a wide variety of food options. Make reservations for popular spots.

3. **Casinos:** Even if neither of you is a big gambler, the atmosphere in the casinos is part of the Vegas experience. Try your luck or just enjoy the lights and sounds.

4. **Attractions:** There are plenty of attractions like the High Roller observation wheel, the Fountains of Bellagio, or even the Hoover Dam.

5. **Nightlife:** If you're up for it, there are plenty of nightclubs and bars to explore.

6. **Shopping:** The Strip has some great shopping options, from high-end boutiques to quirky shops.

7. **Day Trips:** Consider a day trip to the Grand Canyon or Hoover Dam for some amazing views.

Remember to pace yourselves, especially if you're not used to the bright lights and fast pace of Vegas. It can be overwhelming, so take breaks and enjoy each other's company.
