To avoid setting off the smoke alarm, follow these simple steps:

1. Read the package instructions for the specific noodles you're using. Some need to be soaked, others need to be boiled for a certain time.
2. Fill a large pot with enough water to cover the noodles, and bring it to a boil.
3. Add the noodles and cook according to the package directions. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking.
4. Drain the noodles in a colander and rinse with cold water to stop the cooking process.
5. Pat the noodles dry with a paper towel to remove excess moisture.

This way, you'll have perfectly cooked noodles without any smoke alarms involved!<|im_msg_end|>
