You're close with "saccadic"!. The correct pronunciation is "sah-KAH-dik." 

To answer your question: Yes, it's accurate to say that the eye sees continuously, but it's more precise to say that the brain perceives motion as continuous. The eye itself doesn't see continuously; it makes quick jumps (saccades) between points of focus. The brain then stitches together these jumps and fills in the gaps, creating a seamless perception of motion. So, while the eye is moving and making these quick shifts, the brain processes it in a way that feels smooth and continuous to you.
