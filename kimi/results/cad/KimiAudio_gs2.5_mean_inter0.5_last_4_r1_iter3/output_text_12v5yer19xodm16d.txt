Sure, here are five drinks and five snacks that might help you stay awake and energized:

Drinks:
1. Coffee: A classic pick-me-up.
2. Tea: Like green tea, which has caffeine and L-theanine for a balanced energy boost.
3. Energy drinks: They're designed to give you a quick energy boost.
4. Lemon water: Refreshing and can help with hydration.
5. Coconut water: Good for electrolytes and hydration.

Snacks:
1. Nuts: Like almonds or cashews, they're high in protein and healthy fats.
2. Fresh fruit: Apples or bananas are quick and easy.
3. Dark chocolate: Contains caffeine and can be a quick energy boost.
4. Cheese and crackers: A good mix of carbs and protein.
5. Energy bars: Look for ones with natural ingredients and no added sugars.

Remember, it's important to stay hydrated and eat balanced meals to keep your energy levels up.
