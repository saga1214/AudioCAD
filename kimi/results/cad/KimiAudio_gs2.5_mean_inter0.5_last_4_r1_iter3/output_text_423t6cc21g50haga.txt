A new trip sounds like just what you need!!Here are a few ideas:

1. **Italy**: If you love Italian, you might want to go back. Rome, Florence, Venice, and the Amalfi Coast are must-visits. You could stay in a cozy B&B or a charming Airbnb. Activities could include exploring ancient ruins, visiting art galleries, or taking a gondola ride in Venice.

2. **Japan**: If you're into languages and culture, Japan is a fascinating destination. You could stay in a traditional ryokan or a modern hotel. Activities might include visiting temples, trying sushi, or exploring bustling cities like Tokyo or Kyoto.

3. **New Zealand**: If you're into nature, New Zealand offers stunning landscapes. You could stay in a cabin or a lodge. Activities might include hiking, visiting fjords, or exploring the beautiful beaches.

4. **Spain**: If you're into history and culture, Spain is a great choice. You could stay in a charming hotel or a cozy apartment. Activities might include visiting the Alhambra, exploring Barcelona, or enjoying the beaches of the Costa Brava.

5. **Australia**: If you're into adventure, Australia offers a lot. You could stay in a beachside resort or a city hotel. Activities might include surfing, visiting the Great Barrier Reef, or exploring the Outback.

Getting there depends on your starting point. You could fly, take a train, or even drive if you're close enough. Always check the latest travel guidelines and restrictions before planning your trip.

Remember, travel is a great way to break anxiety by giving you new experiences and perspectives. Safe travels!
