Vancouver is a fantastic base for exploring British Columbia!

Here are five road trip options:

1. **Okanagan Valley**: Visit Kelowna for wine tasting, and explore the scenic Okanagan Lake.
2. **Cypress Mountain**: Just outside Vancouver, this mountain offers skiing in winter and hiking in summer.
3. **Tofino**: Head to the west coast for beautiful beaches, surfing, and a laid-back vibe.
4. **Victoria**: The capital city is a must-visit, with its beautiful gardens, historic architecture, and fine dining.
5. **Kamloops**: Explore the scenic Fraser Canyon, and visit the Kamloops Wildlife Park.

For must-try activities, don't miss:

* **Whistler**: A world-renowned ski resort in winter, and a great hiking spot in summer.
* **Capilano Suspension Bridge**: An iconic suspension bridge with stunning views.
* **Granville Island**: A bustling marketplace with local food, crafts, and street performers.
* **Vancouver Aquarium**: Home to thousands of marine animals, including beluga whales and sea otters.

And for the fancy restaurants, try:

* **The Marine Room**: Located at the Fairmont Waterfront, it offers stunning views and exquisite seafood.
* **The Greenhouse**: A farm-to-table restaurant with a focus on local, organic ingredients.
* **The Flying Pig**: A gastropub with a wide selection of craft beers and delicious pub fare.

Enjoy your adventures in British Columbia!
