Absolutely, the older issues are incredibly rare and valuable. For Luke Cage, a must-have from that era is *Captain America* number thirty-eight, his first appearance. You could also look for *All-American Comics* number sixteen, which features the first appearance of the Atom. *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, the first appearance of Batman, is a legendary key issue, though it's extremely rare and expensive in that price range.

For good buys from that era, *Action Comics* number twenty-six, the first appearance of Superman, is always in high demand. *Marvel Comics* number fifteen, the first appearance of the Fantastic Four, is another solid, affordable buy. Another classic is *The Adventures of Captain Marvel* number one, his first appearance. For a more modern choice from that era, *Marvel Mystery Comics* number thirty-one introduced the Human Torch and is always in high demand.

Other great investments under five hundred dollars include *Whiz Comics* number seven, which features the first appearance of the Justice Society of America. Another great one is *Detective Comics* number thirty-nine, his first appearance as Robin. And one more to consider is *Showcase* number forty-seven, which brought him back to prominence.
