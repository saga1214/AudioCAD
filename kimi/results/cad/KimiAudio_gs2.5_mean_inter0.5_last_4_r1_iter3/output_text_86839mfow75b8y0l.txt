When I mentioned that, I was talking about Quincy Jones and Bruce Swedien. While Max Martin, Taylor Swift, Sia, Ryan Tedder are all fantastic songwriters in terms of melody, lyrics, and structure, Quincy Jones, as a producer/songwriter, and Bruce Swedien, as an engineer, are particularly noteworthy for their contributions to the production side of songwriting. They worked together on some of the most iconic pop and R&B records of all time, and their collaboration is a perfect example of how production choices can enhance the emotional impact and overall feel of a song.
