For brakes, you want to check the wear on the brake pads and rotors. If the pads are worn down to the metal or if you hear a grinding noise when you brake, it’s time to replace them. You can usually see the wear on the pads by looking through the wheel wells. For spark plugs, you’ll need to remove the spark plug wires, take out the old plugs, and inspect them for wear. If they’re black or wet, it might be time to replace them. You can do this yourself if you’re comfortable with the process, but it’s often easier to take the car to a mechanic for a tune-up.
