The skills section is actually optional, but it's a great way to highlight your most relevant qualifications for the job you're applying for. You can simply add it right after your education section, right under your awards line. List 5-10 of your most relevant skills, and make sure to match them to the job description.

As for the overall format, here's a basic structure:

1. Your name and contact information at the top
2. A professional summary that highlights your most relevant skills and experience
3. Your education section, listing your degrees and any awards/certificates right underneath
4. Your additional experience section, listing each employer separately even if roles were similar
5. A skills section, matching your skills to the job description
6. Finally, any relevant projects or volunteer work that showcases your skills

Remember to keep everything concise and easy to read, with bullet points and clear headings. Good luck with your resume!
