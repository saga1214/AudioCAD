Costa Rica definitely has some amazing diving spots. You’ve got the Pacific coast with its coral reefs, and the Caribbean side with its barrier reefs and shipwrecks. If you want to see turtles, whale sharks, and other tropical marine life, you might want to focus on the Pacific. If you’re interested in shipwrecks and barrier reefs, the Caribbean could be the way to go. There are also some great liveaboard options if you want to dive multiple sites in one trip.
