For treats, you want something small, healthy, and that your puppy loves. Peanut butter, cheese, or small pieces of cooked chicken are good options. Avoid anything too salty or sugary.

Clicker training is a method that uses a sound (like a clicker) to mark the exact moment your dog does something right, followed by a reward. For example, if you want to teach your dog to sit, you click the moment they sit down and then give them a treat. The sound of the clicker helps them associate it with the action they just did, making it easier for them to learn.

Remember, consistency is key with training. Keep it fun and positive, and you'll be well on your way to a well-behaved and happy pup named Galileo!
