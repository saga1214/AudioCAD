Here is a synopsis of the characters and their roles in the story:

1. Leo Vance: A brave explorer and archaeology professor at Kingsbridge University. He is the descendant of Captain Elias Vance and the main protagonist of the story. Leo is determined to protect the Sunstone of El Dorado from Silas Croft and his henchmen.

2. Elena Castillo: A kind woman and local guide who helps Leo and his team navigate the jungle of Eldoria. She is knowledgeable about the ancient puzzles and the hidden trails of the temple.

3. Jack "Hammer" Stone: Leo's trusted friend and a skilled local guide. Jack is strong and reliable, providing muscle and support to the team.

4. Arthur Finch: A brilliant mapmaker and Leo's colleague at Kingsbridge University. Arthur decodes the complex numerical sequences left by Elias Vance, helping the team solve the ancient puzzles.

5. Javier Rios: A local guide who knows the jungle's hidden trails. Javier helps Leo and his team navigate the dangerous jungle.

6. Silas Croft: The villain and Augustus Croft's direct descendant. Silas is a ruthless treasure hunter who wants to steal the Sunstone of El Dorado for himself. He brings a team of dangerous henchmen to help him in his quest.

7. Marcus Kane: One of Silas Croft's henchmen. Marcus is cunning and resourceful, helping Silas in his pursuit of the Sunstone.

8. Brutus Thorne: Another henchman of Silas Croft. Brutus is a brute and a fighter, providing muscle and intimidation to Silas's team.

9. Rex Gunner: A cold-hearted tracker and henchman of Silas Croft. Rex is skilled in tracking and hunting, helping Silas in his quest for the Sunstone.

10. Ivan Petrov: The deadliest sniper and henchman of Silas Croft. Ivan provides long-range support and protection to Silas's team.

These characters, along with their roles, form the core of the adventure story, where Leo Vance and his team must outsmart Silas Croft and his henchmen to protect the Sunstone of El Dorado.
