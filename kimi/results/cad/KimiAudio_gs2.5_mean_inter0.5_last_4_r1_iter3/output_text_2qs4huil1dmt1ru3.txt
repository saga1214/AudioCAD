Drywall items are pretty basic and won’t cost much, drywall compound can range from $5 to $10, putty knife included in most kits is free, sander sponge or paper about $5, caulking gun if needed is around $10. Cake items flour about $2, sugar $3, baking powder $2, butter $5, eggs usually less than $1, milk $2, vanilla extract $1, pans about $10. Frosting powdered sugar $1, more butter frosting $3 cocoa powder if needed $2. Altogether, bring around $40-$50, prices can vary by store.

Remember to check prices and sizes at your local store. Have a great shopping trip!
