Certainly!Here are some breeds and hybrids that are known for being easy to train, good with children, and generally get along well with other dogs and pets:

1. **Labrador Retriever**: Known for their friendly and outgoing nature, Labs are great with kids and other pets.
2. **Golden Retriever**: Similar to Labradors, Golden Retrievers are friendly, intelligent, and easy to train.
3. **Cocker Spaniel**: These dogs are known for their gentle and affectionate nature, making them great with children and other pets.
4. **Beagle**: Beagles are friendly, curious, and generally good with children and other pets.
5. **Poodle**: Poodles are highly intelligent and easy to train, and they come in various sizes (Toy, Miniature, Standard).
6. **Bichon Frise**: Bichon Frises are playful, gentle, and adaptable, making them great for families.
7. **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel**: These dogs are friendly, gentle, and good with children and other pets.
8. **Boxer**: Boxers are energetic but also very loyal and good with children.
9. **Boston Terrier**: Known for their friendly and affectionate nature, Boston Terriers are great with families.
10. **Corgi**: While they can be energetic, Corgis are generally good with children and other pets, and they are known for their intelligence and trainability.

Remember, while these breeds have generally good temperaments, individual personalities can vary. Visiting potential candidates at shelters or rescues will help you find the best match for your lifestyle.
