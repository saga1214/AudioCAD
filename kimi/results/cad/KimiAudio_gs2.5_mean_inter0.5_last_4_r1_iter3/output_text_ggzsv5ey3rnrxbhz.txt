It's a good question. There's no known way to completely eliminate PFAS from your body, but a healthy diet and lifestyle can help reduce the impact. And yes, avoiding the sources we've discussed is the best way to limit exposure.

To summarize the key things to avoid: non-stick cookware, stain-resistant and waterproof products, certain shampoos and cosmetics, intensive filter systems that remove minerals, and tap water if it's not certified to reduce PFAS. And always check for certifications like NSF or WQA when shopping for shampoos, cosmetics, and electronics.
