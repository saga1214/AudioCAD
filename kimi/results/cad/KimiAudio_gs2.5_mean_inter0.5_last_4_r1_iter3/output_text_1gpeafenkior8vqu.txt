Sure, here’s a deeper dive into each city and a few day trips. This assumes you want to really immerse yourself in Tokyo and Osaka, with a bit of extra time for day trips.

**Tokyo (5 Days Deep Dive)**

Day 1: Arrive, check in, Shinjuku and Kabukicho
Day 2: Asakusa, Ueno Park, Akihabara
Day 3: Shibuya, Harajuku, Omotesando
Day 4: Temples and shrines (Sensoji, Meiji Shrine), Roppongi, Tokyo Tower
Day 5: Day trip to Nikko or Kamakura, return to Tokyo

**Osaka (4 Days Deep Dive)**

Day 1: Osaka Castle, Dotonbori, Shinsekai
Day 2: Universal Studios Japan (if visiting), Day trip to Kyoto (e.g., Fushimi Inari, Kiyomizu-dera)
Day 3: Food tour Dotonbori, Tottori Sand Dunes (if interested in nature)
Day 4: Nara (Todai-ji, Nara Park), return to Osaka

This schedule allows for a good mix of city exploration and day trips, but keep in mind that traveling between places can take time, especially if you’re using trains. You might want to adjust based on your travel style and interests. Enjoy your trip!
