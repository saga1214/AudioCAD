If you're both the writer and illustrator, you're in a great position. Many children's books are self-published by the author-illustrator, and these can be very successful. However, if you're aiming for a traditional publishing route, it's **very** rare for an author to also be the illustrator. Publishers usually prefer to work with specialists in each field.

If you do decide to go the traditional route, you'd still query for an agent, but this time looking for ones who represent *both* authors and illustrators. They would help you find a publisher who appreciates your art. But remember, if you're also the illustrator, you'll still earn a commission-based agent fee once your book sells.
