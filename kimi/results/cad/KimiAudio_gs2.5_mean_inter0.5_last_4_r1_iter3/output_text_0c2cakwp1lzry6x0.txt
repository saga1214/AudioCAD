For the TV, measure where you want it and mark the spot. Use a stud finder to locate wall studs, and then use a stud bracket to mount the TV securely. For hanging planters, use sturdy hooks or brackets that can support their weight. And for floating shelves, you'll need to drill into the wall and use wall anchors to create a secure base. Always follow the manufacturer's instructions and consider getting help if you're not comfortable with heights or heavy lifting. Safety first!
