Both are equally important.

*Connecting* with the client is crucial for his engagement and safety. It's how you build trust and make the session enjoyable. Sensory or music activities sound promising based on what you know about him.

At the same time, it's absolutely vital to communicate the challenges you're facing. The cluttered environment and lack of parental supervision are serious concerns. Make sure to document these issues and discuss them with your BCBA. They can provide guidance on how to manage these challenges while still providing the best possible care for your client.
