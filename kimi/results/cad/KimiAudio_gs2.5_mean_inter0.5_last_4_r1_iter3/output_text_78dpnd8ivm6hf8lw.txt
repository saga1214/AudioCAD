Let's recap:

- **Heart:** The gas station/engine and engine. It pumps the blood (cars) all around your body.
- **Lungs:** The fresh fueling station. Cars pick up oxygen here before starting their journey.
- **Kidneys:** The big car washes or checkpoints. They clean out waste and extra stuff from the blood (cars).
- **Liver:** The special service station. It processes and sorts the fuel and junk the blood (cars) have picked up, making sure everything is balanced and in good condition.
