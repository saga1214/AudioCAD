Congratulations on finishing your project!!If you don’t like sweet or chocolatey snacks, here are some savory options you might enjoy from a vending machine:

1. **Nuts**: Almonds, cashews, or mixed nuts are great choices.
2. **Cheese**: String cheese, cheese sticks, or even a small block of cheddar.
3. **Veggie sticks**: Carrot, celery, or cucumber sticks with a side of hummus.
4. **Pretzels**: Plain or flavored, they’re a good salty snack.
5. **Chips**: Look for non-sweet flavors like sour cream and onion, barbecue, or salt and vinegar.
6. **Jerky**: Beef, turkey, or chicken jerky can be a satisfying snack.
7. savory popcorn flavors like cheese or caramel-free.

Enjoy your snack and happy filling of your new coffee table!
