For the TV, find a spot centered over your seating area and measure the distance for proper viewing height. Use a stud finder to locate wall studs, then mount the TV to the wall using a stud finder and screws. For hanging planters, use a stud finder to find a stud, then attach a bracket to the wall and hang the planter. Floating shelves: measure the distance between studs, then use a level and screws to attach brackets to the wall. Hang the shelves and secure them to the brackets. Always use safety straps for heavy items. If you're not comfortable with heights or DIY, consider hiring a professional.
