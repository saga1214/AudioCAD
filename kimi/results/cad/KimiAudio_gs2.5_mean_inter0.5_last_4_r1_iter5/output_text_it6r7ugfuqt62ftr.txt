Northern California has so many beautiful places to visit. Here's a suggestion for a 4-5 day trip:

First, consider a trip to Lake Tahoe. It's stunning, especially in the fall. You could stay at a cozy cabin or a luxurious resort. There are plenty of activities like hiking, biking, and of course, swimming.

For a day trip, consider a visit to the Redwoods. They're breathtaking and offer a completely different experience from the lake.

For dining, Northern California is known for its farm-to-table restaurants. Look for places that use local, organic ingredients.

Remember, Northern California is known for its beautiful fall foliage, so you'll want to pack layers, even if it's warm during the day.
