When I mentioned that, I was talking about Quincy Jones and Bruce Swedien. While Max Martin, Taylor Swift, Sia, Ryan Tedder, Gamble and Huff all have incredible songwriting skills, Quincy Jones was also a producer and arranger who worked closely with songwriters to bring their songs to life. His ability to enhance the emotion and dynamics of a song through production choices is something you can learn from. Bruce Swedien, as a producer, helped bring Quincy's vision to life, and their collaboration on iconic albums like "Thriller" and "Off The Wall" is a great example of how production can elevate a song.
