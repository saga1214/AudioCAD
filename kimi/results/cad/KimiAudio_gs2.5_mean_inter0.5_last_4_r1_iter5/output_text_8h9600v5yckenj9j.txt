For crowd-pleasers, you can't go wrong with classics like Reese's Peanut Butter Cups, M&M's, and Snickers bars. Sour candies like Sour Patch Kids and Warheads are always a hit, especially with kids. If you want to mix it up a bit, consider getting some gummy bears or worms, or maybe some chocolate-covered pretzels for a different texture. If you're looking for something a bit more unique, consider getting a variety pack of candy so you can cater to different tastes. And remember, it's always a good idea to have a mix of small and large candies to keep things interesting.
