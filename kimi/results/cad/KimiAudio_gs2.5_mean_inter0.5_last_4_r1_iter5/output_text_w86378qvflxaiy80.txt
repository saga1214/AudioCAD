On the other hand, we also need to find ways to make conservation compatible with human livelihoods. This could involve things like sustainable farming practices, ecotourism, or compensation schemes for people whose land is protected.

Ultimately, preserving biodiversity hotspots is about finding a way to protect these critical areas while also supporting the people who live there. It's a complex challenge, but it's one that's absolutely essential for the health of our planet and our own future.
