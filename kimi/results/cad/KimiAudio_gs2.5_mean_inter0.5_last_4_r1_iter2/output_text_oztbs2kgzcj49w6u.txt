There are several great ways to learn more about the canal and its history in Lockport.

One option is to visit the Erie Canal Museum, which is dedicated to the history of the canal and its impact on the region. They have exhibits, artifacts, and even a replica of a canal boat.

Another option is to take a guided tour of the canal. There are several companies that offer boat tours that will take you through the locks and provide detailed information about the history and engineering of the canal.
