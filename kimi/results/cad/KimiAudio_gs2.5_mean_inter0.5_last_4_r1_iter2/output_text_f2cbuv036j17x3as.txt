For brakes, you want to check the wear on the brake pads and rotors. If the pads are worn down to the metal or if you hear a grinding noise when you brake, it’s time to replace them. You can do this yourself if you’re comfortable with automotive work, but it’s often best to have a professional mechanic handle it. As for spark plugs, they usually last about 30,000 to 100,000 miles, depending on the type and conditions. You can check them by removing the spark plug wires and looking at the spark plugs. If they’re fouled or worn out, you’ll need to replace them. Again, this is a job that’s better left to a professional if you’re not experienced. But it’s great that you’re thinking ahead and wanting to keep your camper van in top shape for the trip!.
