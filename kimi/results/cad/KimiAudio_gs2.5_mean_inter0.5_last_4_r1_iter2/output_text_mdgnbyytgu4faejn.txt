The skills section is actually optional, but it's a great way to highlight your most relevant qualifications for the job you're applying to. You can include it right after your education section, right under awards/certificates.

And here's a quick breakdown of the typical resume format:

1. Contact Information: Your name, email, phone number, and city.
2. Professional Summary: A brief paragraph highlighting your most relevant skills and career goals.
3. Additional Experience: A list of employers separated, focusing on the years you worked in your target field.
4. Education: Your degrees, expected graduation date, awards/certificates, and school information.
5. Skills: A list of relevant skills according to the job description.
