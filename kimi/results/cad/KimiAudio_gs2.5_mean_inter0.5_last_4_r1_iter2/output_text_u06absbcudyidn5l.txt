Rosacea is a common cause of chronic skin redness. It's a chronic skin condition that causes redness, acne-like symptoms, and sometimes visible blood vessels. It's not fully understood, but it's thought to be related to an overactive immune system and increased sensitivity to certain triggers.

Regarding vasculitis, it's an autoimmune condition where the body's immune system attacks the blood vessels, causing inflammation. It can affect any part of the body, but it's most commonly seen in the skin, joints, and kidneys. It's not fully understood, but it's thought to be related to an overactive immune system.
