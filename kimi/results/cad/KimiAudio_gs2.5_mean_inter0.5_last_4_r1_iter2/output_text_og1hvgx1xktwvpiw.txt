Okay, let me clarify. The classic recipe for both the Boulevardier and the Last Word can be equal parts, depending on your preference. The original classic recipe for the Boulevardier is one and a quarter ounces whiskey, one ounce sweet vermouth, and one ounce Campari. For the Last Word, it's equal parts gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. So, you can choose either based on your taste.
