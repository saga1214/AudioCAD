Vancouver is a fantastic base for exploring British Columbia!

Here are five road trip options:

1. **Okanagan Valley**: Visit Kelowna for wine tasting, and explore the scenic Okanagan Lake.
2. **Cypress Mountain**: Just outside Vancouver, this mountain offers skiing in winter and hiking in summer.
3. **Victoria**: The capital city is a short drive away, with its beautiful gardens and historic architecture.
4. **Tofino**: Head to the west coast for stunning beaches and surfing.
5. **Kootenay National Park**: For a more rugged adventure, explore the mountains and glaciers of Kootenay.

For activities, try kayaking in Stanley Park, visiting the Capilano Suspension Bridge, and exploring Granville Island.
