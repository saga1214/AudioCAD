Starting YouTube content for kids is a good next step.

Before Friday’s show at Sunnyside Assisted Living, here’s what you should do:

1. **Check Permissions:** Make sure you have permission to record at Sunnyside Assisted Living. Some places might have specific rules about recording.

2. **Test Your Equipment:** Bring your phone and see how it looks. Maybe practice a few skits at home to get comfortable with the camera angle and lighting.

3. **Charge Your Phone:** Make sure your phone is fully charged or bring a portable charger.

4. **Backup Plan:** Have a backup plan if the phone runs out of battery. Maybe bring a small camera or a spare phone.

5. **Lighting:** Try to find a well-lit area for filming. Natural light is best, but if not, use a good light source.

6. **Sound Quality:** Make sure the sound is clear. If the room is too echoey, consider using a small microphone or a good pair of earphones with a built-in mic.

7. **Timing:** Keep it short. Plan your skits to fit within that five to ten-minute sweet spot.

8. **Engagement:** Think about engaging the audience. Maybe have a few moments where you interact with the viewers or the residents.

9. **Post-Recording:** Have a plan for uploading right after the show. If you can’t do it immediately, make sure to schedule it for soon after.

10. **Promotion:** Share the channel with people already loving your work—like that influencer family—to get some initial views.

So, test everything, make sure you’re comfortable, and have fun. You’ll do great.
