Here are two approaches:

1. **Email**: You could send an email to your boss, stating that you'd like to discuss your performance and future opportunities. This gives you some time to compose your thoughts and can also serve as a record of your request.

2. **Schedule a meeting**: Alternatively, you could suggest a meeting. This gives you more control over the timing and allows you to prepare more thoroughly. You could say something like, "I'd like to discuss my progress and some ideas for improving my time management."
