Sure, here’s a deeper dive into each city, plus a couple of day trips:

**Tokyo (Deep Dive, 6 Days)**

Day 1-2: Central Tokyo
- Explore Shinjuku ( department stores, Omoide Yokocho)
- Asakusa (Senso-ji Temple)
- Ueno Park and Zoo
- A day trip to Nikko (Toshogu Shrine, Lake Chuzenji)

Day 3-4: North and East Tokyo
- Akihabara (electronics, anime)
- A day trip to Kamakura (temples, beaches)

Day 5-6: South and West Tokyo
- Roppongi (Museum of Contemporary Art)
- Harajuku (Shibuya, Takeshita Street)
- A day trip to Yokohama (Minato Mirai 21, Chinatown)

**Osaka (Deep Dive, 5 Days)**

Day 1-2: Main Attractions
- Osaka Castle
- Dotonbori for food
- Universal Studios if applicable

Day 3: Explore Districts
- Namba (shopping, nightlife)
- Umeda (sky building, shopping)
- Abeno Harukas (sky observation deck)

Day 4: Kyoto Day Trip
- Take a day trip to Kyoto (Fushimi Inari, Kinkaku-ji, Arashiyama)

Day 5: Nara Day Trip
- Visit Nara Park (Deer Park, Todai-ji Temple)
- Explore nearby temples and shrines

This schedule gives you a good mix of Tokyo’s diverse districts, a deep dive into Osaka’s food and culture, and a couple of day trips to Kyoto and Nara. Adjust based on your interests and travel style. Enjoy your trip!
