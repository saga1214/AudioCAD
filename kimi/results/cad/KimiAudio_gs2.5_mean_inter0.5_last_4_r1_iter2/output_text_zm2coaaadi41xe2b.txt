For Asian noodle places in Dallas, you’ve got a few great options. Pho 84 is a popular spot for Vietnamese pho, and they’ve got a good variety of noodle dishes too. If you’re in the mood for something spicy, try Szechuan Noodle House—they’ve got some fire in their noodles!

For protein bars, RXBAR is a popular choice among voice actors for its clean ingredients and good protein content. Quest Bars are another option, they’ve got a good balance of protein and fiber. And if you’re looking for something a bit more natural, Kind Bars are a good choice too. They’re made with wholesome ingredients and are easy on the stomach. Enjoy your meal and snacks!
