For kosher dietary laws, you'll want to make sure your snacks are free from pork, shellfish, and any meat products. If you're bringing weenies in bacon, you'll need to switch to kosher-certified bacon or find a different protein. Dark chocolate should be fine, as long as it's not mixed with any non-kosher ingredients. Fruits and hummus are usually kosher, but double-check if they're prepared in a kosher-certified kitchen. If you're unsure, it's always a good idea to ask or to have a few options that are clearly kosher.
