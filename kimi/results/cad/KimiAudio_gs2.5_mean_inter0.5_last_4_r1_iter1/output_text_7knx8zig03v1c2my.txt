For treats, you want something small, healthy, and that your puppy loves. Peanut butter, cheese, or small pieces of cooked chicken are good options. Avoid anything too fatty or sugary.

Clicker training is a fun way to teach your dog. You use a small device that makes a clicking sound. When your dog does something you want, like sitting, you click the device and immediately give them a treat. Over time, they learn that the click means "good job" and they associate it with getting a treat. It’s a great way to reinforce good behavior and can be used for all sorts of tricks and commands.
