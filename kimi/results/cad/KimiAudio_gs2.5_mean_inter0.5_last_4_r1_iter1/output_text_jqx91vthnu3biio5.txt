For color-coding, it's a bit of both. Using different colors can help with memory, making it easier to recall which color corresponds to which task or project. But it also helps reduce mental load by visually distinguishing between different types of work, so you don’t have to spend as much time mentally categorizing.

As for switching between playlists, it's mostly personal preference. Some people find variety keeps them engaged, while others prefer a consistent sound to stay in the zone. There’s no one-size-fits-all answer. You might need to experiment a bit to see what works best for you.

For tackling physical documents, both the environment and breaking tasks into chunks contribute to a sense of progress. A clean workspace can make you feel more organized and productive, while breaking tasks into manageable chunks can make them feel less overwhelming. It’s a combination of both that can really boost your sense of accomplishment.
