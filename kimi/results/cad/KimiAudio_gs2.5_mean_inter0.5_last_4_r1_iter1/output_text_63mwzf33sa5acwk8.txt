Vegas is all about the experience, so here are a few tips to make the most of it:

1. **Shows and Entertainment:** There are countless shows to choose from, from Cirque du Soleil to magic shows. Check out what's playing and book tickets in advance.

2. **Dining:** From celebrity-chef restaurants to local eateries, there's a wide variety of food options. Make reservations for the places you want to go.

3. **Casinos:** Even if neither of you are big gamblers, the atmosphere is fun. Try your luck or just enjoy the sights.

4. **Attractions:** The High Roller observation wheel, the Fountains of Bellagio, and the LINQ Promenade are must-sees.

5. **Nightlife:** There are plenty of bars, clubs, and lounges. You could also try a rooftop bar for a view of the Strip.

6. **Shopping:** From high-end boutiques to quirky shops, there's something for everyone.

7. **Day Trips:** Consider a day trip to the Hoover Dam or the Grand Canyon.

Remember, Vegas is all about the experience, so don't be afraid to try new things and make memories together.
