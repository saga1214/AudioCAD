Got it!Here are the classic recipes for Old Fashioned Paper Plane Whiskey Sour Aviation, and now the Whiskey Sour with egg white:

1. Classic Old Fashioned:
- 2 oz bourbon/rum
- Sugar cube/simple syrup
- 2 dashes Angostura Btters
- Orange twist

Stir with ice in rocks glass over ice usually with cherry or peel.

2. Classic Paper Plane:
- Equal parts bourbon/Aperal Nonioni lemon juice

Shake with ice, strain into Cupe glass.

3. Whiskey Sour:
- 2 oz bourbon
- 0.75 oz lemon juice
- 0.5 oz simple syrup

Shake with ice, strain into rocks glass over ice, garnish with cherry/orange slice.

4. Aviation:
- 2 oz gin
- 0.25 oz liqueur
- 0.25 oz crme de violette
- 0.5 oz lemon juice

Shake with ice, strain into cocktail glass, garnish with cherry.

5. Whiskey Sour with Egg White:
- 2 oz bourbon
- 0.75 oz lemon juice
- 0.5 oz simple syrup
- 1 egg white

Dry shake (without ice) with egg white, bourbon, lemon juice, and simple syrup. Add ice, shake again, then strain into rocks glass over ice. Garnish with cherry/orange slice.
