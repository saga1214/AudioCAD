Here's a **themed, allergy-friendly shopping list** for your nautical pirate birthday party, focusing on **sand-resistant, kid-friendly foods** and strong pirate immersion:

---

### **Main Course (Pirate's Plunder Platter)**
*   **Mini Tuna Salad Sliders:** (3-4 per child + 1-2 extras)  
    *   1 lb roll mix (for mini slider buns)  
    *   1 can tuna (packed in water, drained)  
    *   2-3 tbsp mayo  
    *   1 tbsp finely chopped celery (optional crunch)  
    *   Salt & pepper to taste  
*   **Mini Chicken Salad Sliders:** (3-4 per child + 1-2 extras)  
    *   1 lb roll mix  
    *   1.5 cups shredded cooked chicken (rotisserie works)  
    *   1/4 cup finely chopped celery or grapes  
    *   2 tbsp mayo  
    *   1 tbsp finely chopped pickles  
    *   Salt & pepper to taste  
*   **Pirate's Scoop Chips:** (3-4 per child)  
    *   1 large bag tortilla chips (thick, sturdy kind like Fritos)  
    *   1 small can black beans (drained & rinsed)  
    *   1/4 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or Monterey Jack)  
    *   1/4 cup salsa (small cup for dipping)  
    *   1/4 cup sour cream (small cup for dipping)  
    *   **Presentation:** Serve in a shallow "treasure chest" bowl.

### **Sides & Snacks (Treasure Chest Tots)**
*   **"Pirate's Jewels" Veggie Sticks:** (2-3 per child)  
    *   1 bag baby carrots  
    *   1 bag cucumber sticks  
    *   1 bag bell pepper strips (any color)  
    *   1 small container hummus or ranch dip (in a small sealed container)
*   **Cheese Cubes:** (1-2 per child)  
    *   1 block cheddar or mozzarella cheese (cubed)
*   **Crackers:** (1-2 per child)  
    *   1 box sturdy crackers (like Triscuits or simple graham crackers)
*   **"Gold" Gummy Treasure:** (1-2 per child)  
    *   1 bag gummy bears/wow berries (in gold or pirate-themed colors)
*   **Pirate Snack Packs:** (Pre-pack for each child)  
    *   1 mini slider  
    *   1-2 cheese cubes  
    *   1-2 crackers  
    *   1-2 veggie sticks  
    *   1-2 gummy candies  
    *   1 small chip scoop

### **Desserts (Cursed Cupcakes?)**
*   **Mini Pirate Flag Cupcakes:** (1 per child + 1-2 extras)  
    *   1 box white cake mix  
    *   1 box frosting mix (chocolate or vanilla)  
    *   **Thematic Topping:** Small plastic pirate flag (yellow & black) per cupcake (attach with a toothpick)  
    *   **Optional:** Mini chocolate chips sprinkled on top.
*   **"Gold" Gummy Treasure:** (1-2 per child) - Reuse from snack.

### **Drinks (Salty Sea Water & Rum Punch)**
*   **Juice Boxes:** (1 per child)  
    *   Assorted flavors (apple, strawberry, orange, etc.)
*   **"Pirate Punch" Station:**  
    *   4-6 cups water  
    *   2-3 cups fruit punch (low sugar if possible)  
    *   **Optional:** 1/4 cup lemonade powder (for "rum" effect)  
    *   **Presentation:** Serve in individual cups (pre-portioned 1/4 or 1/3 cup each) so kids don't spill.
*   **Water Bottles:** (1 per child) - For the kids to refill.

### **Allergy Focus (Watermelon-Free)**
*   **Avoid:** Watermelon, cantaloupe, honeydew, watermelon jello, watermelon in smoothies.
*   **Safe Fruit Options:** Grapes, apple slices, orange wedges, berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries), pineapple chunks, melon (cantaloupe is generally safe, but double-check allergy if unsure).
*   **Label Everything:** Clearly mark any food that contains the allergen (if applicable, though none on this list).

### **Packaging & Presentation (Themed!)**
*   **Food Containers:** Small compartmentalized containers or bento boxes for pre-packed snack packs.
*   **Drinks:** Use small individual juice boxes and pre-portioned punch cups.
*   **Decor:** "Treasure Chest" bowls for chips/salad, small pirate flag toppers for cupcakes, themed napkins ("X Marks the Spot"), plastic treasure chests for snacks.
*   **Utensils:** Plastic forks and spoons (small size).

### **Practical Tips**
1.  **Prep Ahead:** Assemble sliders, cheese cubes, veggie sticks, and snack packs the day before. Store sliders in the fridge until ready to serve.
2.  **Keep it Cold:** Pack drinks and perishable items (sliders, cheese) in a cooler.
3.  **Napkins:** Ample small paper napkins and hand sanitizer.
4.  **Thematic Touch:** Have a simple pirate activity (like a "pirate map" scavenger hunt) to keep the immersion going between food stops!

This list provides a **variety of textures and flavors**, minimizes mess, caters to the allergy, and brings the pirate nautical theme to life at the beach! Have a fantastic party! 🏴‍☠️🎉
