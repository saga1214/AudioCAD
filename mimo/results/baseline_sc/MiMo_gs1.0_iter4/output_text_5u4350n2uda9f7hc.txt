You're absolutely right to think about weight. I should have mentioned that earlier. For a short stay, you want something light and easy to move.

Succulents are great because they are very lightweight. A small pot with a succulent plant will only weigh a couple of pounds, so it's easy to carry up and down the stairs. You can easily take it home with you.

The bird's nest snake plant is also lightweight, but it can be a bit heavier than a succulent, maybe around three to five pounds. It's still manageable, but it might be a little more effort to carry.

So for something super easy to move, I'd still recommend the succulents. They are the lightest option and will be a great, easy-care plant for your short trip.
