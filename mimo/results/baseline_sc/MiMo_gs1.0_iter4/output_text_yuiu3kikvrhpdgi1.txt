You got it. For a beginner-friendly edible garden in St. Pete, I'd suggest these vegetables. First, lettuce and spinach—they're fast-growing greens, usually ready in about a month. Then radishes, which are even quicker, maybe two to four weeks. For herbs, basil and mint are great choices. Mint is a bit invasive, so it's best to keep it in a pot. And finally, tomatoes and peppers. They take a bit longer, maybe two to three months, but they're well worth the wait. The key is to start with something easy and just build from there. Happy gardening!
