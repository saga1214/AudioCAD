You can absolutely submit a complete manuscript with your own illustrations. In fact, many publishers are very interested in artists who also write. However, the process is a bit different.

First, it's crucial to understand that most traditional publishers do not accept unsolicited work. They typically require an agent to represent you before they'll even look at your book. So your first step should be to find an agent.

Once you have an agent, they will be your advocate and will submit your completed package to publishers for you.

If you're determined to go it alone, you could try submitting to publishers directly, but it's much more difficult. Another option is to self-publish. With self-publishing, you have complete creative control, but you are also responsible for everything from design to marketing. You can use platforms like Amazon's KDP to do this.
