### 1. **Metal Cutting Tools**
   - **Aviation Snips**  
     *Best for:* Thin sheet metal (up to 16–20 gauge). Ideal for curves and precision cuts.  
     *Where to Buy:* Home Depot, Lowe's, or Ace Hardware (Indianapolis locations).  
   - **Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel**  
     *Best for:* Thicker metal, straight cuts, and power.  
     *Where to Buy:* Home Depot, Lowe's, or Menards.  

### 2. **Drywall Fasteners**
   - **1.25" Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws**  
     *Why:* Superior holding power, prevents nail pops, and easy to set with a screw gun.  
     *Where to Buy:* Home Depot, Lowe's, or Tractor Supply Co.  
   - **Alternative: Ring-Shank Nails**  
     *Why:* Better grip in wood studs than smooth nails. Use if screws aren’t an option.  
     *Where to Buy:* Ace Hardware or Tractor Supply Co.  

### 3. **Insulation**
   - **Fiberglass Batts (Pre-Cut Rolls)**  
     *Why:* Cost-effective, easy to install, and widely available.  
     *Where to Buy:* Home Depot, Lowe’s, or Menards.  
   - **Mineral Wool**  
     *Why:* Fire-resistant, great for soundproofing.  
     *Where to Buy:* Home Depot or Ace Hardware.  
   - **Spray Foam**  
     *Why:* Best air-sealing performance (ideal for energy efficiency).  
     *Where to Buy:* Home Depot (for DIY) or a local insulation supplier.  

### 4. **Big-Box Store with Best Selection**  
   **Home Depot**  
   *Why:* Widest variety of all items (tools, fasteners, insulation) in one place. Staff can provide guidance, and they offer online pickup for in-store inventory.  

### Pro Tips:
- **Drywall Screws:** Use a **screw gun** for consistent depth and a small dimple (helps with mud adhesion).  
- **Metal Cutting:** For curves, **aviation snips** win; for thick metal, **angle grinder + cut-off wheel** is unbeatable.  
- **Insulation:** Measure your wall cavities first—fiberglass batts fit standard 4-inch depths.
