### **Prevention Through a Secure Culture**  
*(with Detection and Response as its essential partners)*  

Here’s why:  
1. **Prevention is proactive and scalable**:  
   - A strong security culture (via training, awareness, and policies) empowers employees to *prevent* attacks before they happen.  
   - It turns your workforce into a defense force.  

2. **Detection and Response *depend* on prevention**:  
   - Without a secure foundation, detection tools are overwhelmed, and response plans are reactive.  
   - Example: Phishing detection fails if employees aren’t trained to recognize scams.  

3. **It’s the bedrock of all other efforts**:  
   - Testing, technology, and outsourcing *all* work best when the human element is secure.  
   - A culture of security ensures policies are followed, tools are used correctly, and incidents are reported promptly.  

### Why Not Just One?  
- **Detection** is vital, but it’s reactive—it catches what slips through prevention.  
- **Response** is critical, but it’s too late if prevention fails.  
- **Technology/testing/outsourcing** are enablers, but they can’t replace human vigilance.

> **"A robust cybersecurity program starts with prevention—fostering a culture where every employee is empowered to protect the organization. Detection and response are essential for resilience, but prevention is the foundation that makes them effective."**
