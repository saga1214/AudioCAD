Of course. Having a skills section is highly recommended. You should place it right after your professional summary, but before your work experience. This way, the hiring manager can quickly scan for the specific skills they're looking for. For the format, here’s a simple way to structure it. 

Start with a heading that says "Skills." Underneath, you can break it down into subheadings. For example, have a section for "Technical Skills," where you list your hard skills like software or languages. Then, a section for "Soft Skills," which includes things like communication and leadership. Finally, a section for "Certifications." This structure is very clear and makes it easy to find the information you want.
