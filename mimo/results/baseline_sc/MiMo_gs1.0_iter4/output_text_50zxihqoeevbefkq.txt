Next, for appetizers: you'll need ground beef or pork for the sliders, along with buns and toppings like cheddar cheese and caramelized onions. For the shrimp cocktail, you'll need shrimp, cocktail sauce, and a block of cream cheese for the cucumber boats. And you'll need the ingredients for pigs in a blanket, which are sausage, crescent rolls, and a little mustard for brushing.

For the main dish and sides: you'll need a ham, either a spiral-cut or a pre-cooked one for convenience. For the potatoes, you'll need potatoes, butter, heavy cream, salt, and pepper. For the roasted asparagus, you'll need asparagus spears, olive oil, salt, and pepper. And as the clean alternative to carrots, you'll need carrots, olive oil, salt, and pepper.

For dessert: a simple fruit salad is always a good choice, so you'll need various seasonal fruits like berries, melon, and apples.

For other party supplies: you'll need some plastic flutes for the kids' grape juice, some festive napkins, and a trash can with a lid for easy cleanup.

Finally, for a few extras: don't forget plates, cups, and utensils for everyone, and maybe some party favors like mini champagne glasses or stickers.
