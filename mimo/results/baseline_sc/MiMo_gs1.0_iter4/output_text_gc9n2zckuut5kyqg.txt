Absolutely! Here are some budget-friendly dessert ideas that are kid-friendly, healthy-conscious, and diabetes-aware, all while keeping costs down:

### 🍓 Budget-Friendly & Diabetes-Friendly Desserts
1. **Fresh Fruit Skewers**  
   *Cube watermelon, grapes, pineapple, berries, and melon. Thread onto skewers.*  
   - **Cost:** Very low (seasonal fruits are cheapest).  
   - **Bonus:** Add a side of *unsweetened yogurt dip* or *honey drizzle* (use sparingly).

2. **DIY Yogurt Parfaits**  
   *Layer plain Greek yogurt, granola (low-sugar), and fresh fruit in cups.*  
   - **Cost:** Granola can be homemade (oats + nuts + cinnamon + minimal honey) or store-bought in bulk.  
   - **Kid-Friendly:** Let kids assemble their own!

3. **Mini Fruit Cups**  
   *Hollow out small melons (like cantaloupe or honeydew), fill with a mix of berries, banana slices, and a dollop of yogurt.*  
   - **Cost:** Melons are affordable, and berries are often discounted near expiration dates.

4. **Oatmeal Cookies**  
   *Make simple cookies with oats, mashed banana or applesauce (instead of oil/butter), cinnamon, and a touch of honey.*  
   - **Cost:** Oats and bananas are pantry staples.  
   - **Tip:** Use *dark chocolate chips* sparingly (or omit).

5. **Frozen Yogurt Bark**  
   *Spread Greek yogurt on parchment, top with berries, chia seeds, and a sprinkle of dark chocolate shavings. Freeze, then break into pieces.*  
   - **Cost:** Yogurt and berries are inexpensive. Chocolate shavings add festive flair without cost.

6. **Banana "Sushi"**  
   *Slice bananas, spread with nut butter, add a few berries, roll in shredded coconut or chia seeds, and freeze.*  
   - **Cost:** Bananas and nut butter are budget-friendly.

7. **Homemade Granola Bars**  
   *Mix oats, seeds, honey, and a little peanut butter. Press into molds, bake, then break into bars.*  
   - **Cost:** Oats and seeds are cheap. Pre-make ahead.

### 💡 Budget-Saving Tips
- **Buy in Bulk:** Purchase seasonal fruits/vegetables in large quantities.  
- **DIY Decorations:** Use fresh mint leaves or edible flowers to garnish.  
- **Donations:** Ask local bakeries or grocery stores for *donated desserts* in exchange for promotion.  
- **Water Station:** Keep infused water (cucumber, lemon, berries) free and abundant.  
- **Portion Control:** Serve desserts in small portions to reduce waste.

### 🍕 Example Menu for Low Cost
- **Main:** BBQ burgers/hot dogs (use lean ground turkey or plant-based patties to save).  
- **Sides:** Baked beans, potato salad (homemade), and fruit salad.  
- **Desserts:** Fruit skewers + yogurt parfaits + mini oatmeal cookies.  
- **Drinks:** Water, lemonade, and herbal tea (no sugar packets).
