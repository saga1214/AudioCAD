That's fantastic that you've done all the prep work! You've got all the essentials, but a few more things will make the job much easier and safer.

First, you'll need a drill with the right screws for your cabinet material, so a drill bit is essential. Then, you'll need a stud finder. That's super important for finding the studs in the wall to make sure the cabinets are securely anchored. You'll also need a measuring tape to make sure everything is spaced correctly.

As for safety, definitely wear safety glasses to protect your eyes from any dust or debris, and a dust mask to avoid inhaling sawdust.

You've got a great start, and with those few extra items, you'll be all set to get those cabinets installed!
