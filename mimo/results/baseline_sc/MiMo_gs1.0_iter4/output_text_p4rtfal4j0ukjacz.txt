I understand your point. If this is your only meal, then 450 milligrams of sodium in that single portion isn't necessarily excessive. However, sodium intake is generally recommended to be lower for long-term health. For instance, health organizations often suggest keeping it under 2,300 milligrams per day, and aiming for 1,500 milligrams for better heart health.
