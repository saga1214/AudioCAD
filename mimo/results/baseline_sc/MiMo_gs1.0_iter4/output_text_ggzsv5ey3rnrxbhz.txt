I hear how worried you are, and it's completely understandable to want to know how to get these chemicals out of your body. 

The reality is that because these chemicals are so persistent, there isn't a single magic solution like a juice cleanse to make them disappear. Your body's natural detoxification process is what handles them, and that takes time. The most effective thing you can do is to reduce your exposure in the first place. That's why I keep coming back to the list of things to avoid. 

For example, you should avoid non-stick cookware, grease-resistant food packaging, and products labeled as stain or water-resistant. You should also avoid certain cosmetics and dental floss, and filter your tap water. These are the things you can control right now. 

While there's no quick fix, consistent lifestyle choices like eating a healthy diet rich in fiber and certain nutrients can support your body's natural ability to process these chemicals over time. The key is really prevention.
