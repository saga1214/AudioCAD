Great job documenting the house! Here's what to prioritize next, building on your progress:

1.  **Secure Water & Plumbing (Highest Priority):**
    *   **Turn on Water:** Open all faucets (bathrooms, kitchen, outside) to bleed air from the pipes and ensure everything works.
    *   **Check for Leaks:** Look under sinks, around toilets, and at the water heater for any drips or signs of leaks. Fix anything obvious immediately.
    *   **Set Up Essentials:** Run the shower for a few minutes to clear sediment. Ensure the toilet flushes properly.

2.  **Set Up Power (Very High Priority):**
    *   **Plug in the Fridge/Freezer:** Unplugged during the move will have caused the contents to spoil. Get them plugged in ASAP.
    *   **Charge Essential Electronics:** Phones, laptops, doorbells, etc.
    *   **Plug in Any Security/Smart Devices:** If you have cameras, smart plugs, or other automation set up.

3.  **Address Trash & Recycling (Essential for Daily Function):**
    *   **Find Bins:** Locate existing trash and recycling bins (if provided).
    *   **Set Up New Bins:** If you brought your own, place them out immediately. If not, take a run to the store or local supplier.
    *   **Empty Old Trash:** If you brought belongings in bins, empty those out and clean them.

4.  **Unpack Your "Open First" Box:**
    *   **Essentials Only:** This box should contain toiletries, phone chargers, basic tools, snacks, water, a corkscrew, and maybe some basic cleaning supplies. Get this done *before* tackling the rest of the unpacking.

5.  **Start Setting Up Key Rooms ( bedrooms, kitchen, bathroom):**
    *   **Bedrooms:** Unpack beds, make beds, unpack bedding. This provides immediate comfort.
    *   **Kitchen:** Unpack kitchen essentials (utensils, dishes, basic cookware, spices). Set up the stove (if you cook) and toaster. Clean counters.
    *   **Bathroom:** Unpack towels, toilet paper, shower items, toiletries. Clean the toilet and sink/bath.

**Why this order?**

*   **Water & Power:** You need lights (power) to see what you're doing with the water (plumbing), and you need water to flush toilets, wash, and cook. A fridge without power spoils food.
*   **Trash/Recycling:** You'll generate waste immediately as you unpack and use the house. It needs a place to go.
*   **"Open First" Box:** You need essentials to function *while* you set up the house (e.g., charge your phone to call for help, use the bathroom, get a snack).
*   **Key Rooms First:** Getting comfortable beds and a functional kitchen/bathroom allows you to relax and feel settled quickly.

**Once you have these basics running, you can focus on unpacking the rest of the truck, setting up living areas, and tackling the long-term tasks like unpacking boxes and decorating.** Great job getting the inspection done!
