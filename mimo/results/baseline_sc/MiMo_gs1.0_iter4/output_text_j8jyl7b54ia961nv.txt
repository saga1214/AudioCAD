You're absolutely right to highlight public resistance. It's the single biggest factor that could derail a vaccination campaign. So, let's adjust our analysis to reflect that. 

If we were to create a weighted score, the vaccination campaign would likely be ranked lower because the difficulty of public acceptance would be given a much higher weight. The sugar reduction campaign, while challenging, would score better on this specific point.

So, with public resistance as the primary factor, I would choose the sugar reduction campaign. It's a more predictable challenge, and it avoids the intense political and social firestorm that a vaccination campaign can provoke. The trade-off is that the impact of the sugar campaign might be slower and less dramatic, but it's a more sustainable path for a public health department in the long run.
