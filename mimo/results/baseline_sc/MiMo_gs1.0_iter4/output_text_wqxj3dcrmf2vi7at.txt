Here's a comprehensive list of treat ideas for Princess, categorized for easy reference:

### 🍽️ Homemade Treats (Easy to Make & Customize)
1.  **Freeze-Dried Liver/Beef:** (Store-bought *or* make your own by air-drying lean ground beef/liver). High-value, crunchy, shelf-stable.
2.  **Pumpkin or Sweet Potato Bites:** Puree, mix with a *tiny* bit of flour (or use no flour, roll into small balls), freeze on a tray, then transfer to a bag. Great frozen treats & training rewards.
3.  **Peanut Butter & Oat Cookies:** Mix PB (xylitol-free!) with rolled oats, roll into balls, flatten slightly. Bake until firm.
4.  **Cheese Cubes:** Small cubes of mild cheddar, mozzarella, or cottage cheese. *Ensure no garlic/onion.*
5.  **Yogurt Pops:** Dip a small piece of yogurt (plain, Greek, or regular) into a mix of mashed banana or pumpkin, roll in oats or coconut flakes. Freeze.
6.  **Banana Bites:** Small pieces of banana, optionally rolled in a *tiny* bit of oats or coconut flakes.
7.  **Cooked Chicken/ Turkey:** Small, bite-sized pieces of plain, boiled chicken or turkey breast. Very high-value.
8.  **Sweet Potato Chips:** Thinly slice sweet potato, bake at low temp (200°F/95°C) until crisp. Break into small pieces.
9.  **Cheese & Veggie Sticks:** Thinly sliced cucumber, zucchini, or carrot sticks. Pair with small cheese cubes for a "snack stick."
10. **Air-Popped Popcorn:** Lightly buttered or plain, popped in a dog-safe popcorn maker (ensure no xylitol).

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands (Look for These)
*   **Training Pellets:** (e.g., **Ziwi Peak**, **Life Savers**, **Greenies** training treats). Tiny, nutritious, perfect for training.
*   **Freeze-Dried Meat:** (e.g., ** Wellness Freeze-Dried Liver**, **Zuke's Mini Training Bites**, **Ziwi Peak Freeze-Dried Tuna**). Highly concentrated, great for high-value rewards.
*   **Grain-Free Bites:** (e.g., **Blue Buffalo Training Bites**, **Acana Training Bites**, **Orijen Training Bites**). Look for recipes without chicken.
*   **Organic Treats:** (e.g., **Farmina N&D**, **Barf & Fido**). Often use higher-quality ingredients.
*   **Protein Bars:** (e.g., **YumEarth Dog Treats**, **Earth Animal**). Convenient for on-the-go.

### 🧠 For Training & High-Value Rewards (Use These!)
*   **Cooked Chicken/Turkey:** The gold standard for training.
*   **Cheese Cubes:** Especially if Princess loves cheese.
*   **Peanut Butter:** Use *small* amounts.
*   **Freeze-Dried Liver/Tuna:** Extremely enticing.
*   **Specialty Training Treats:** Specifically designed to be small and high-value.

### 📋 Tips for Treating Princess
1.  **Portion Control:** Always cut treats into *very* small pieces. Aim for treats to be less than 10% of her daily calories.
2.  **No Xylitol:** **NEVER** use xylitol (found in some sugar-free products) - it's deadly.
3.  **Consult Your Vet:** Especially if Princess has health issues, allergies, or dietary restrictions.
4.  **Hydration:** Offer fresh water, especially if offering dry treats.
5.  **Training Focus:** Use the smallest, highest-value treats during training sessions.
6.  **Safety First:** Supervise if she's eating new treats, especially hard ones like rawhide or very large pieces.

Choose treats based on Princess's preferences (does she love chicken more than cheese?), dietary needs, and what you have time to make or buy! Enjoy rewarding your corgi princess! 🐾
