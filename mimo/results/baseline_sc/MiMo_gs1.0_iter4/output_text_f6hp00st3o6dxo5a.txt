You're touching on a very subtle but important point. The mutation in the egg is a great way to think about it. However, it's a bit more complex than that.

The key thing to remember is that the egg itself isn't a living organism. It's a vessel carrying a set of instructions. The *potential* for a chicken was in the egg, but the first living creature to be a chicken was the one that hatched from it.

So, while the egg was the necessary condition, the chicken was the first to *emerge* as a chicken. It's like saying a seed contains the DNA of a tree, but the first tree grows from the seed. The seed was first, but the tree was the first to exist.
