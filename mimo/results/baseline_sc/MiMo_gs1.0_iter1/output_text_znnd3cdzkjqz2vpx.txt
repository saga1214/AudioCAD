Okay, you've got all the basics down, but I'd strongly recommend adding a few more tools to your list to make the job smoother and safer. First, you'll definitely need a stud finder, since you'll be screwing the cabinets into the wall studs. And you'll need a measuring tape to make sure you get the spacing between cabinets right and to check that everything is level. Also, a utility knife is really useful for trimming any excess material like the backing from the base cabinets. You'll also want a small hammer to gently tap the nails into the wood, and some painter's tape to protect the wall paint from scratches. Oh, and don't forget a box cutter for cutting the backs of the cabinets to fit. And lastly, a level is always good to have on hand just to double-check everything. You're all set to get started!
