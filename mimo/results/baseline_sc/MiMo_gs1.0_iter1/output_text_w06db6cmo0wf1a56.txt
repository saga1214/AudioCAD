*   **Individual Hummus Cups:** 10 cups
*   **Baby Carrots:** 1 large bag (ready to eat)
*   **Individual Trail Mix:** 10 bags (look for lower-sugar options)
*   **Pretzels:** 2 large bags (mini pretzel twists or sticks)
*   **Rice Cakes:** 10 packs (plain or lightly salted)
*   **Individual Nut Butter Packets:** 10 (almond or peanut)
*   **Fresh Fruit:** 1 bag of pre-cut fruit cups (grapes, melon, berries) or 1 large container of berries
*   **Granola Bars:** 10 boxes (look for lower-sugar, whole-grain options)

**Main Food (Calzones!):**
*   **Mini Calzones:** Order 10-12 from a local bakery or pizza place. Specify:
    *   **Fillings:** Include at least one vegetarian option (e.g., cheese, spinach & ricotta) and one meat option (e.g., sausage, pepperoni). Ask about any common allergies.
    *   **Sauce:** Ensure it's a light sauce to prevent mess.
    *   **Preparation:** Ask if they can be pre-baked (best for reheating) or if they are fresh. **Crucial:** Ask if they can be cut into small squares or triangles for easier eating on the go.

**Dessert:**
*   **Cupcakes:** 12-15 cupcakes (baked the day before for best quality, bring in a sturdy container) - **OR**

*   **Utensils:** Pack of 10+ reusable bamboo forks/spoons (for cheese, grapes, maybe sandwiches).
*   **Towels:** A few small, reusable hand towels (for spills).
*   **Ziplock Bags:** Assorted sizes for organizing snacks.
*   **Labels:** For cooler compartments.

**Key Tips for Packing:**

3.  **Dessert Safety:** Keep cupcakes/brownies/mini cheesecakes cool and dry.
4.  **Ice Management:** Keep ice packs frozen until needed. Empty coolers when not in use.
5.  **Portion Control:** Pre-portion snacks into individual bags for easy grab-and-go.
6.  **Hydration Focus:** Water is essential. Make sure everyone has a reusable water bottle.
