That's a smart approach! Here are some budget-friendly food and dessert ideas that are still family-friendly and diabetes-conscious:

### **Budget-Friendly Main Dishes**
1. **Grilled Chicken or Veggie Skewers**:  
   - Marinate chicken breasts or bell peppers/zoombinis in olive oil, lemon juice, and herbs.  
   - Serve with a side salad (mixed greens, cucumbers, tomatoes) or whole-grain rolls.  
   - *Cost-saver*: Buy chicken breasts in bulk and portion them yourself.

2. **Bean Burger or Veggie Patties**:  
   - Make your own from black beans, oats, and veggies (or buy store-bought).  
   - Serve with baked sweet potato fries or roasted veggies.  

3. **Pasta Salad**:  
   - Use whole-grain pasta, toss with cherry tomatoes, olives, feta, and olive oil dressing.  
   - *Budget tip*: Buy pasta in bulk and add protein like canned chickpeas or tuna.

4. **Potato Salad**:  
   - Opt for a light vinaigrette-based version (instead of mayo-heavy) with veggies like celery and carrots.

### **Budget Sides & Drinks**
- **Veggie Platter**:  
  - Fresh fruit (watermelon, berries), carrot sticks, cucumber, and bell peppers.  
  - Serve with hummus or Greek yogurt dip.  
- **Water Station**:  
  - Set up free water dispensers with lemon/lime slices.  
- **Iced Tea/Lemonade**:  
  - Brew in bulk using tea bags or lemons + sugar-free sweetener.

### **Kid-Friendly & Budget Desserts**
1. **Fruit Skewers**:  
   - Thread grapes, melon balls, pineapple, and berries on toothpicks.  
   - *Bonus*: Add a sprinkle of chia seeds for texture.  
2. **Mini Muffins**:  
   - Bake banana oat muffins (using mashed bananas instead of sugar) or zucchini bread.  
   - *Cost-saver*: Use frozen bananas or zucchini.  
3. **DIY Sundae Bar**:  
   - Offer small cups of vanilla yogurt or ice cream, and toppings like:  
     - Fresh berries, chopped nuts, sprinkles, or crushed pretzels.  
   - *Budget tip*: Use sugar-free yogurt to reduce cost.  
4. **Fruit Popsicles**:  
   - Blend fruit (mango, pineapple, berries) with a splash of milk, pour into molds, and freeze.  
   - *No added sugar needed* if using ripe fruit.

### **Maximizing Your Budget**
- **Potluck Approach**:  
  - Ask family members to bring specific dishes (e.g., one group brings salads, another brings desserts).  
- **Local Donations**:  
  - Contact grocery stores or bakeries for food/drink donations (many support fundraisers).  
- **Bulk Buying**:  
  - Purchase rice, beans, pasta, and spices in bulk for sides.  
- **DIY Decor**:  
  - Use homemade signs or digital invitations to cut costs.

### **Final Tip**
Set up a **"Healthy Swap" Station**:  
- Display a board with diabetes-friendly alternatives to sugary snacks (e.g., "Swap cookies for apple slices + peanut butter!").  
- This subtly educates attendees while keeping desserts optional.
