Of course. Let's break down the potential causes for the redness and inflammation you're describing. 

First, it's important to remember that chronic redness can be caused by many things. While vasculitis is one possibility, it's not the only one. Other common causes include conditions like rosacea, which often affects the face, or even just sun damage. It could also be an allergic reaction, contact dermatitis from something you're using, or even an underlying infection. 

Now, to understand vasculitis better, let's talk about what it is. It's an autoimmune disease, which means your immune system mistakenly attacks your own blood vessels. This causes inflammation. The size of the blood vessel affected is what helps doctors classify it. There are large-vessel types, medium-vessel types, and small-vessel types. The symptoms you might see depend on which vessels are involved and where they are. Common signs across many types include fever, fatigue, and skin rashes. Because the vessels are inflamed, blood flow can be restricted, which can lead to serious complications, like tissue damage or organ problems. That's why getting an accurate diagnosis is so important.

As for supplements, things like collagen or zinc can help with hair and nails, but they won't treat the root cause of inflammation like vasculitis. For that, you'd need to work with a doctor to manage the underlying condition.
