That's a very interesting take, but it's important to remember that DNA doesn't just exist in an egg. It's in the DNA of the parent bird's cells that produced the egg. So, the DNA that defined the first chicken was already in the parent bird, which was not a chicken. That parent bird laid the egg, and the first chicken hatched from it. The chicken was still first.

Also, you can't say that the egg *contained* the DNA of the first chicken. The egg contained the DNA of the parent bird, which *was not* a chicken. The DNA just happened to have the potential to create the first chicken. The egg was the container, but the chicken came out of it.
