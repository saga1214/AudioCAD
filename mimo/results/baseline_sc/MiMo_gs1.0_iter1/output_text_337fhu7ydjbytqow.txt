Here's a clear packing list separated for the **20-mile segment (daytime)** and the **12-mile segment (nighttime)**:

### 20-Mile Segment (Daytime)
1.  **Base Layer (Moisture-Wicking):** Lightweight synthetic or merino wool top & bottom (e.g., short-sleeve shirt, tights).
2.  **Mid-Layer (Insulation):** Fleece jacket or lightweight down/synthetic vest (adjustable).
3.  **Outer Layer (Wind/Water Resistance):** Lightweight windbreaker or rain jacket (synthetic or treated nylon).
4.  **Headwear:** Cap or visor (for sun).
5.  **Handwear:** Light gloves or fingerless gloves.
6.  **Footwear:** Lightweight trail running shoes or sandals (if comfortable).
7.  **Lighting (Optional but Recommended):** Small headlamp or handheld light (for early morning/low light).
8.  **Small Pack/Vest:** Daypack or vest to carry layers as needed.
9.  **Spare Socks:** Extra pair of moisture-wicking socks.
10. **First-Aid Kit:** Basic kit (blister care, bandages, antiseptic wipes).

### 12-Mile Segment (Nighttime)
1.  **Base Layer (Moisture-Wicking):** **Essential.** Lightweight synthetic or merino wool top & bottom.
2.  **Mid-Layer (Insulation):** **Essential.** Fleece jacket or lightweight down/synthetic jacket (puffy).
3.  **Outer Layer (Wind/Water Resistance):** **Essential.** Lightweight windbreaker or rain jacket (synthetic or treated nylon).
4.  **Headlamp:** **Essential.** Bright headlamp with extra batteries (high lumen recommended).
5.  **Warm Hat:** **Essential.** Insulated hat or balaclava to cover ears.
6.  **Gloves:** **Essential.** Warm, insulated gloves (consider touch-screen tip).
7.  **Socks:** **Essential.** Extra pair of moisture-wicking socks (consider wool blend).
8.  **Footwear:** Sturdy trail running shoes (ensure they are broken in!).
9.  **Small Pack/Vest:** Daypack or vest (to carry layers and lighting).
10. **First-Aid Kit:** Basic kit (blister care, bandages, antiseptic wipes).
11. **Spare Batteries:** For headlamp (if possible).

### Key Differences Highlighted
*   **Warmth:** The 12-mile list adds **essential** warmth layers (puffy jacket, warm hat, insulated gloves) and **extra socks**.
*   **Lighting:** The 12-mile list requires a **bright headlamp with extra batteries** (daylight is gone).
*   **Footwear:** The 12-mile list emphasizes **sturdiness** for potentially tired legs at night.
*   **Packability:** The 12-mile list benefits from a **vest** for easy access to warmth and light.

### Remember
*   **Check the Forecast:** Verify the actual temperature and wind chill for both segments closer to race day.
