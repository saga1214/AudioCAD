Okay, great job on the prep work. Since you're going from black to off-white, that primer is critical. I'd recommend two main things before you apply it. First, do a quick final check to make sure all the dust from the sanding is completely gone. You can wipe the surfaces down with a tack cloth. Second, and this is very important, make sure you're using a primer specifically designed for covering dark colors. It will help you get a smooth, even finish in one coat. Once that's on, you'll be all set.
