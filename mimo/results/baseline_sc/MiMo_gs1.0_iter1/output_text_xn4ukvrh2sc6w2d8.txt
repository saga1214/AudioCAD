Here are the classic, detailed recipes for your cocktail reception, formatted for easy printing or sharing with your bartenders. Each includes ingredients, measurements, and preparation steps:

### 1. **Old Fashioned**  
*(Bourbon, Sweet, Bitters)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Buffalo Trace)  
  - 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 4–5 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (for garnish)  
- **Steps**:  
  1. Muddle sugar cube and ½ oz water in a mixing glass until dissolved.  
  2. Add bourbon and bitters.  
  3. Stir with ice for 20–30 seconds until chilled.  
  4. Strain into a rocks glass over a single large ice cube.  
  5. Express orange peel over the drink, rub rim, and garnish.  

---

### 2. **Paper Plane**  
*(Bitter, Citrus, Balanced)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Bulleit)  
  - ¾ oz Aperol  
  - ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Steps**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a coupe glass over crushed ice.  
  3. Garnish with a lemon twist (express oils over drink).  

---

### 3. **Whiskey Sour**  
*(Bright, Tart, Silky)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Woodford Reserve)  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - ½ oz simple syrup  
  - 1 egg white *(optional, for foam)*  
- **Steps**:  
  1. **With egg white**:  
     - Dry shake (shaken without ice) all ingredients in a shaker.  
     - Add ice, shake again, and strain into a chilled glass.  
     - Garnish with lemon twist.  
  2. **Without egg white**:  
     - Shake with ice, strain, and garnish as above.  

---

### 4. **Gin Cocktail Recommendation: Gin Smash**  
*(Seasonal, Herbal, Kentucky Twist)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz gin (e.g., Hendrick’s or Kentucky 75)  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 4–5 muddled mint leaves  
  - 2 dashes orange bitters  
  - *Bourbon-bourbon-bitters twist* (see below)  
- **Steps**:  
  1. Muddle mint leaves with ½ oz water in a shaker.  
  2. Add gin, lemon juice, simple syrup, and bitters.  
  3. Shake with ice, strain into a rocks glass over ice.  
  4. **Bourbon-bourbon-bitters twist**: Rub bourbon, bourbon, and bitters on the glass rim, then garnish with mint sprig.  

---

### **Key Tips for Bartenders**:  
- **Ice**: Use large, clear ice cubes for clarity and slow dilution.  
- **Prep**: Make simple syrup (equal parts sugar/water) and lemon juice ahead.  
- **Garnish**:  
  - Old Fashioned: Orange peel.  
  - Paper Plane: Lemon twist.  
  - Whiskey Sour: Lemon twist or cherry.  
  - Gin Smash: Mint sprig + bourbon-bourbon-bitters rim.  
- **Glassware**: Rocks glasses for Old Fashioned/Whiskey Sour, coupes for Paper Plane, highball for Gin Smash.
