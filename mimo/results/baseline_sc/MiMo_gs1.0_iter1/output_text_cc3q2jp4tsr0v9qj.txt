Great job documenting! Here's what to prioritize next on your first moving-day checklist:

### 1. **Setup Utilities Immediately**  
   - **Power**: Unpack extension cords & power strips first. Plug in the fridge, lights, and chargers ASAP.  
   - **Water**: If no water works, buy bottled water for drinking/washing. Contact the HOA to confirm water service details.  
   - **Internet**: Install the router early. Test speed after setting up your package (aim for 500 Mbps+ for 5 streamers).  

### 2. **"Open First" Essentials**  
   - **Kitchen**: Coffee maker, dishes, and snacks.  
   - **Bathroom**: Toilet paper, soap, towels.  
   - **Bedrooms**: Sheets, pillows, and a cozy blanket.  
   - **Living Area**: Couch cushions and a trash can.  

### 3. **Strategic Unpacking**  
   - **Room-by-room**: Tackle one room at a time (e.g., kitchen → bedroom → living room).  
   - **Label Boxes**: Mark contents clearly (e.g., "FRIDGE - DRINKS").  
   - **Keep Moving Supplies Handy**: Set aside a box with tape, scissors, and a notepad for room labels.  

### 4. **HOA Paperwork**  
   - Submit registration documents today to avoid fines. Confirm rules on trash pickup, pet permits, and landscaping.  

### 5. **Self-Care Break**  
   - **Takeout Dinner**: Celebrate! You’ve earned it.  
   - **Hydrate**: Drink water constantly—moving is dehydrating.  
   - **Rest**: Set a 30-minute break timer. Even 5 minutes of fresh air helps.  

### 6. **Safety & Cleanup**  
   - **Secure Moving Truck**: Ensure it’s locked and parked legally.  
   - **Clean Up**: Sweep floors, wipe dust, and trash everything.  

### Pro Tip:  
**Avoid the "All-at-Once" Trap**. Focus on one priority at a time. Power first, then water, then essentials. You’ll make progress without burnout!  

Once utilities are live and you’ve set up beds, you can tackle the rest. You’ve got this! 🏡✨
