Okay, so the core of your question is about reducing exposure and removing these chemicals from your body. Let's tackle that. 

First, it's important to understand that 'forever chemicals' are, well, persistent. They don't break down easily, and your body can't get rid of them very fast. So, a 'juice cleanse' or a water fast won't magically make them disappear. The best thing you can do is focus on preventing more from getting in in the first place.

Now, for a practical list of things to avoid, based on what we've discussed:

First, definitely avoid non-stick cookware. Switch to stainless steel, cast iron, or ceramic. 

Second, steer clear of grease-proof packaging, like those takeout boxes and microwave popcorn bags.

Third, limit anything with a 'stain-resistant' label, such as carpets, furniture, and outdoor clothes.

Fourth, choose personal care products carefully. Look for brands that are certified as free of PFAS, like PFAS-free shampoo and toothpaste.

And fifth, use a certified water filter. This is key for removing them from your drinking water, and as we talked about, carbon filters are a good choice that keep the minerals in.

So, the main takeaway is that prevention is much more effective than trying to detox. Reducing your intake is the best strategy.
