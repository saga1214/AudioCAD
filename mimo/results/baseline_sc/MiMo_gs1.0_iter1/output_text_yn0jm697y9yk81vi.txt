### **Tools**
1. **Aviation Snips**  
   *Best for:* Cutting thin sheet metal (up to 18 gauge).

2. **Angle Grinder with Metal-Cutting Wheel**  
   *Best for:* Thicker metal or straight cuts.

3. **Jigsaw with Metal-Blade Kit**  
   *Best for:* Curves/complex shapes.  
   *Where to buy:* Ace Hardware, Menards.  

### **Drywall Fasteners**
1. **1-1/4" Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws**  
   *Best for:* Secure hold in wood studs (prevents nail pops).  
   *Recommended Brands:* GRK, Simpson Strong-Tie.  
   *Where to buy:* Home Depot, Menards, Lowe's.  

### **Insulation**
1. **Fiberglass Insulation Batts (1/2" or 5/8" thick)**  
   *Best for:* Most walls (R-11 to R-13).  
   *Where to buy:* Home Depot, Lowe's, Ace Hardware.  
2. **Mineral Wool Insulation (Optional)**  
   *Best for:* Soundproofing/fire resistance.  
   *Where to buy:* Tractor Supply Co., Menards.

- **Home Depot (30+ locations):** Best for tools, screws, and fiberglass.
