No problem! Here's a complete, categorized recap of all the measurements we discussed for your tailoring business:

---

### **1. Core Body Measurements (Every Fitting)**  
- **Bust** (around the fullest part)  
- **Natural Waist** (the narrowest part of the torso)  
- **Hips** (widest part of the hips, usually at the level of the belly button or slightly lower)  

---

### **2. Garment-Specific Measurements**  
#### **For Tops & Jackets:**  
- **Sleeve Length** (from shoulder seam to wrist)  
- **Shoulder Width** (distance between shoulder points)  
- **Neck** (for collars, under the chin)  
- **Bicep** (for sleeve comfort)  
- **Upper Back (Shoulder to Shoulder)**  

#### **For Pants & Skirts:**  
- **Inseam** (inner leg length, from crotch to ankle)  
- **Thigh** (widest part of the thigh)  
- **Front Rise** (distance from crotch to waistband)  
- **Back Rise** (distance from waistband to crotch)  
- **Hip Ease** (comfort room for sitting)  

---

### **3. Special Cases (Like Your 6'4" Client)**  
- **Back Rise** (critical for tall clients to prevent pulling)  
- **Hip & Thigh Ease** (add 1–2 inches for comfort when sitting)  
- **Inseam Adjustment** (shorten by 1–3 inches for ankle-length pants)  

---

### **Key Tips:**  
- **Always measure over clothes** for accuracy.  
- **Use a flexible tape measure** (not a ruler!).  
- **Measure clients with the garment** (e.g., have them sit in the pants to test hip/thigh fit).
