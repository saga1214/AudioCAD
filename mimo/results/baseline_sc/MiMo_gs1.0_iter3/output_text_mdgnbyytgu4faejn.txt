Okay, let's break this down. A well-structured resume is key to making a good impression. Here’s a standard format you can follow.

First, at the very top, you'll have your name, and right below it, your contact info: email, phone number, city, and a link to your LinkedIn profile. You can also add a link to your portfolio if you have one.

Next, right under your name, you'll have a professional summary. This is a short paragraph, about three to five sentences, that highlights your key qualifications and career goals, especially for the jobs you're targeting now.

After that comes the experience section. This is where you list your jobs, from most recent to oldest. For each job, include the company name, your title, the dates you worked there, and a few bullet points describing your key responsibilities and achievements. You can group similar roles together under a single heading if they were in the same industry.

Then, you'll have a skills section. List your technical and soft skills here. It's a good idea to tailor this section to match the keywords in the job description you're applying for.

For your education, list your degrees, schools, graduation dates, and any honors or awards you received, like a scholarship or being in the Dean's List.

You can also add an optional section for certifications, volunteer work, or projects that are relevant to the job.

Finally, at the very bottom, you can list your references, with a note that you are available to provide them upon request.

This format helps keep your resume clean, professional, and easy to read.
