Okay, you've got the essentials, but I'd strongly recommend adding a few more things to your toolbox for this project. First, a stud finder is essential for locating the studs to screw into the wall. Second, a tape measure to make sure the cabinet overhangs are the right length. Third, a pencil to mark measurements on the wall. And fourth, a level to make sure everything is perfectly straight. You'll also want some painter's tape to avoid scuffing the walls, and a box of shims to adjust the cabinets for a level and secure fit. Safety first, so a dust mask is a good idea, and you'll need a drill and screws for the cabinets, not just a hammer. Oh, and of course, you'll need a hammer to tap things into place, and a screwdriver for the upper cabinets. You've got a great plan going, just double-check you have all these tools ready to go. Good luck with the installation!
