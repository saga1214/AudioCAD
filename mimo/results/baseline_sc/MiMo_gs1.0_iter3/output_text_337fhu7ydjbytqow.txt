    *   **1x** Warm hat or cap (to cover ears).
    *   **1x** Buff or neck gaiter (versatile for warmth, wind, sweat management).
5.  **Footwear (Essential):**
    *   **1x** Trail running shoes with excellent grip for hilly terrain.
    *   **1x** Extra pair of socks (quick-dry, merino wool blend).
