### **Umami-Packed & Adventurous Snacks**  
1. **Kimchi Seaweed Wraps**  
   - Use *kimchi* (fermented spicy cabbage) + *dried seaweed sheets* (nori).  
   - Wrap in nori with avocado, crispy wonton strips, and a drizzle of **spicy honey**.  
   - *Bonus*: Serve with *tangy tamarind dip* (mix tamarind paste with lime, sugar, and chili).  

2. **Sweet & Sour Tamarind Bites**  
   - Toss **tofu cubes** or **dried shrimp** in a tamarind glaze (tamarind paste + brown sugar + lime juice).  
   - Serve with *pork rinds* or *rice crackers* for crunch.  

3. **Spicy Wasabi Pea & Miso Popcorn**  
   - **Miso-flavored popcorn** (top with white miso paste) + **wasabi peas** (Japanese-style).  
   - Garnish with sesame seeds and a sprinkle of *fish sauce powder* for extra umami.  

4. **Dried Squid & Sesame Nibbles**  
   - Lightly salt **dried squid** and bake until crispy.  
   - Serve with **black sesame brittle** (toasted sesame seeds + sugar syrup).  

5. **Matcha-Glazed Dried Fruits**  
   - Glaze **apricots, figs, or mango** with matcha powder, honey, and a pinch of sea salt.  
   - Pair with *yuzu-marinated grapes* (lime + orange zest + soy sauce).  

---

### **Interactive & Shareable Stations**  
- **DIY Yakitori Skewers**:  
  Marinated chicken thigh strips (soy + mirin + ginger) + *mushroom skewers*.  
  Serve with *torn nori* and *wasabi mayo*.  
- **Takoyaki (Octopus Balls) Bar**:  
  Pre-make batter (tempura flour + egg + octopus + tempura scraps).  
  Guests assemble mini octopus balls on skewers, grill, and serve with *tangy mayo* and *sriracha*.  
- **Sake-Infused Olives**:  
  Soak olives in *dry sake* for 1 hour, then chill. Garnish with *ginger slices*.
