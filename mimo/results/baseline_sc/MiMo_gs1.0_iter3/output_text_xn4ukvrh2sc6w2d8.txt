Alright, here are the detailed recipes for the cocktails you're planning. I've included all the ingredients and measurements, and I've noted which ones are optional.

First up is the Old Fashioned. You'll need two ounces of bourbon, a sugar cube, or half an ounce of simple syrup. Then, two to three dashes of Angostura bitters, and an orange peel for the garnish. To make it, you muddle the sugar cube with a splash of bitters, add the rest of the bourbon, stir it with ice, and then garnish with the orange peel.

Next, the Paper Plane. You'll use two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of Aperol, half an ounce of Amaro Nonino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Just shake it with ice and strain into a coupe glass. No garnish needed.

For the Whiskey Sour, you'll use two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. If you're using the egg white, which is optional, you'll add that to the shaker along with the other ingredients and do a dry shake first before shaking with ice. You can garnish it with a lemon twist or a cherry.

Then there's the Aviation. For this, you'll need two ounces of gin, half an ounce of maraschino liqueur, a quarter-ounce of crème de violette, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Shake it with ice and strain into a coupe glass. You can use a maraschino cherry or a lemon twist for a garnish.

And finally, the French 75. You'll mix one and a half ounces of gin, half an ounce of lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup in a shaker. Shake it with ice, and then strain it into a champagne flute. Top it up with about one to two ounces of prosecco or champagne, and you can garnish with a lemon twist.
