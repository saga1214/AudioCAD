Of course, I can clarify that. There are many things that can cause skin redness, including rosacea, sun damage, or contact dermatitis. Vasculitis is another possibility.

To explain it more simply, vasculitis is an autoimmune condition where the body's immune system mistakenly attacks the walls of blood vessels. This inflammation can affect them at different levels, which is what you were asking about. Large vessels might be involved in conditions like giant cell arteritis. Medium-sized vessels are affected in diseases like polyarteritis nodosa. And inflammation of the smallest vessels is seen in conditions like vasculitis in the kidneys or lungs.

Symptoms can vary depending on which vessels are affected, but common ones include fever, fatigue, and skin rashes. The cause is the immune system malfunction, and the inflammation can lead to serious complications like organ damage.
