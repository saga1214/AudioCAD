Here's a concise summary of the key advice for caring for your cats on a tight budget:

1.  **Preventative Care is Key:** Focus on hygiene (clean bowls, litter, water) and basic nutrition to avoid illness and vet bills.
2.  **Food Stretching & Supplementing:**
    *   **Mix & Match:** Combine a small amount of quality food with cheaper fillers (like plain cooked rice or oats) to stretch portions.
    *   **Supplement Safely:** When possible, add safe, plain table scraps (cooked chicken/fish, no salt/seasoning) or tiny bits of canned tuna (in water, no oil) occasionally.
    *   **Bulk Buying:** Look for larger bags of acceptable quality food for better value per serving.
3.  **Free/Cheap Enrichment:** Use household items (string, paper, cardboard boxes) to keep cats entertained and active, promoting health.
4.  **Weight Management:** Prevent obesity (a common cause of expensive health problems) by controlling portions and encouraging play.
5.  **Monitor Closely:** Be vigilant for early signs of illness (lethargy, changes in appetite, litter box issues, changes in coat). Catching problems early *before* they worsen can sometimes mean managing at home or catching them when care is still affordable.
6.  **Seek Community Resources:** Actively look for local pet food banks, shelters offering food, or charities providing pet supplies or food assistance.
7.  **Prioritize Essentials:** Focus on basic needs (food, water, shelter, hygiene) first. Supplement with enrichment and safe food mixes when possible.
8.  **Preventative Vet Care is Worth It:** Prioritize essential preventative care like spaying/neutering and core vaccinations when funds allow, as they prevent major, costly health problems later.

**In short:** Focus on prevention and hygiene, stretch food smartly (mixing, supplements, bulk), keep cats active/healthy, watch closely for early illness, leverage community resources, and prioritize essential preventative care when possible.
