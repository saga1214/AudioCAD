Let's break it down. First, connecting with the client is still the absolute priority. That relationship is the bedrock of any effective therapy. It's what builds trust and makes him willing to try new things.

Second, and this is incredibly important, you need to report the lack of supervision and the environmental hazards to the BCBA immediately. The safety of the client and yourself is the top priority. A cluttered environment with uncontrolled dogs is a major risk. 

You should frame this as a safety and ethical issue. This shows you're being proactive and responsible.
