Okay, let's see where you are in the process. You've removed the hardware, and you've already done the light sanding. That's great. Before you apply that primer, you need to make sure the surface is perfectly clean and dry. Any dust or grease will prevent the primer from sticking properly.

After you've cleaned and dried the surface, you'll apply your primer. I'd recommend using a brush to get into all the corners and crevices, and then use a roller for the main surfaces. Once you've applied it, you'll need to let it dry completely. The instructions on the can will tell you how long that takes, but it's usually a few hours, sometimes overnight for the best results.

Once it's fully dry, you can proceed with your paint.
