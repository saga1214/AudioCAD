Here's a detailed shopping list for your nautical/pirate-themed birthday party, focusing on kid-friendly, sand-safe, allergy-conscious, and immersive foods and drinks:

**Theme Elements:**
*   **"Buried Treasure" Containers:** Small, decorated containers or boxes for appetizers/snacks.
*   **"Pirate Ship" Flags:** Small fabric flags or paper cutouts for napkins and packaging.
*   **"Cannonball" Plates/Cups:** Small plastic plates, cups, or even small bowls shaped like cannonballs.
*   **"Treasure Map" Tablecloth:** A simple tablecloth with a map pattern.
*   **"Captain's Log" Sign:** A small sign for the food table.
*   **Cookie Cutters:** Pirate ship, skull, or treasure chest shapes for sandwiches/wraps.

**Shopping List:**

**I. Appetizers & "Ship's Rations" (Sand-Safe & Easy):**

1.  **Mini Sandwiches / "Buried Treasure" Sandwiches:**
    *   1 loaf or 2 loaves of Bread (Choose sturdy sandwich bread like Italian or whole wheat)
    *   1 lb Ham (diced or thinly sliced, pre-cooked & safe)
    *   1 lb Cheese (Cheddar, Swiss, or Colby Jack cubes or slices)
    *   1 lb Turkey (diced or thinly sliced, pre-cooked & safe)
    *   1-2 cups Lettuce (Romaine or Butter - pre-washed & dry)
    *   1-2 cups Sliced Tomato (pre-washed & dry)
    *   1-2 cups Sliced Cucumber
    *   1 jar Mayonnaise
    *   1 jar Pesto
    *   1 jar Hummus (for a veggie dip)
    *   **Allergy Note:** *Avoid watermelon.* Ensure no cross-contamination if needed.
    *   **Theme Prep:** Cut sandwiches into small rectangles or triangles. Use cookie cutters on bread rounds before filling. Pack in small "treasure chests" (containers).

2.  **"Pirate's Plunder" Fruit Skewers:**
    *   1 lb Grapes (Red, Green, or Mixed)
    *   1 pint Strawberries
    *   1 pint Blueberries
    *   1 pint Raspberries
    *   1 pint Blackberries (optional)
    *   Wooden Skewers (small, food-safe)
    *   **Allergy Note:** *Avoid watermelon.* Ensure berries are washed well.
    *   **Theme Prep:** Skewer grapes and berries. Pack in a small container or bag labeled "Plunder."

3.  **"Cannonball" Cheese & Crackers:**
    *   1 lb Cheese Cubes (Cheddar, Swiss, or Colby Jack)
    *   1 box Crackers (Simple square crackers, like Triscuits or basic saltines)
    *   **Theme Prep:** Pack cheese cubes in a small container. Pack crackers in a separate container or bag. Label as "Cannonballs."

4.  **"Pirate's Gold" Trail Mix:**
    *   1 bag Mixed Nuts (Almonds, Walnuts, Peanuts - *ensure allergy-safe*)
    *   1 bag Pretzels (Mini pretzels or sticks)
    *   1 bag Chocolate Chips (Mini or regular)
    *   1 bag Dried Fruit (Raisins, Cranberries, Blueberries)
    *   **Theme Prep:** Mix in a large "treasure chest" container for sharing.

**II. Main "Meals" (Simple & Portable):**

5.  **"Pirate's Feast" Mini Pizzas:**
    *   12-15 Mini Pizzas (Store-bought or homemade)
    *   1 cup Tomato Sauce (for spreading)
    *   1-2 cups Mozzarella Cheese (shredded)
    *   1 cup Pepperoni (optional)
    *   1 cup Sliced Bell Peppers (optional)
    *   **Allergy Note:** *Avoid watermelon.* Check pepperoni ingredients if needed.
    *   **Theme Prep:** Assemble mini pizzas just before serving. Pack toppings separately in small containers.

**III. Sides & "Ship's Provision":**

6.  **"Salted Sea" Chips:**
    *   1 large bag Potato Chips (Plain or lightly salted)
    *   1 large bag Tortilla Chips (Salty, not flavored)
    *   **Theme Prep:** Pack in a sturdy bag or container labeled "Salted Sea."

7.  **"Fresh Catch" Veggies:**
    *   1 bag Baby Carrots
    *   1 bag Celery Sticks
    *   1 bag Cherry Tomatoes
    *   **Allergy Note:** *Avoid watermelon.* Ensure tomatoes are washed well.
    *   **Theme Prep:** Pack in a container with a lid, maybe with a small flag on top.

**IV. Drinks & "Pirate's Brews":**

8.  **"Pirate Punch" (Non-Alcoholic):**
    *   1 gallon Lemonade
    *   1 gallon Orange Juice (or Apple Juice)
    *   1 gallon Sparkling Water (Lemon or Lime)
    *   1 gallon Unsweetened Iced Tea (optional)
    *   **Theme Prep:** Mix in a large cooler or jug. Serve in "treasure chests" or small cups.

9.  **"Crew's Water" Bottles:**
    *   24 Individual Juice Boxes (Various flavors - avoid watermelon)
    *   24 Small Plastic Water Bottles (Reusable or disposable)
    *   **Theme Prep:** Label juice boxes as "Crew's Juice" or "Pirate Punch."

**V. Dessert (Beyond Cake):**

10. **"Treasure Chest" Cookies:**
    *   2 dozen Pirate Ship Cookies (store-bought or homemade)
    *   1 bag Mini Chocolate Chips (for decorating)
    *   **Theme Prep:** Pack in a small decorated container or box labeled "Treasure Chest."

11. **"Cannonball" Brownies:**
    *   1 box Brownie Mix
    *   1 box Fudge Frosting
    *   1 bag Chocolate Chips
    *   **Theme Prep:** Bake brownies. Frost with fudge and sprinkle chocolate chips on top. Cut into small squares. Pack in a container.

**VI. Extras & Supplies:**

12. **Ice:** 2-3 large coolers full (for drinks, sandwiches, etc.)
13. **Coolers:** 2-3 large insulated coolers with ice packs
14. **Ice Packs:** 10-15
15. **Small Serving Spoons & Forks:** 10-12
16. **Napkins:** 20-30 (Theme fabric flags or paper with flags)
17. **Plasticware:** 10-12 Cups, 10-12 Plates, 10-12 Forks/Spoons
18. **Trash Bags:** 2-3 Large outdoor trash bags
19. **Allergy Labeling:** Clear labels for any packaged items with allergens.

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Portion Control:** Cut sandwiches/wraps into small, manageable pieces.
*   **Seal Everything:** Use containers, bags, and wraps to keep food fresh and contained.
*   **Allergy Vigilance:** Double-check all packaged items (chips, crackers, cookies) for allergen statements. Consider pre-packaged items with clear labeling or make simple things like sandwiches from scratch.
*   **Temperature:** Keep drinks and perishable items cold in coolers with plenty of ice.
*   **Theme Touch:** Incorporate pirate flags, map patterns, and simple decorations throughout the food setup.

Have a fantastic pirate-themed party at the beach! Your son is going to love the immersive experience.
