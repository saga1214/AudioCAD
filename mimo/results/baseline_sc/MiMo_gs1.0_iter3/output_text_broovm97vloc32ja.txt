That's an excellent question, and it's a very common situation for children's book authors. The short answer is yes, you absolutely can submit an entire completed project. In fact, it's often seen as a huge plus.

However, the process is different. You'll need to approach publishers directly, without an agent. You'll submit a package that includes your manuscript and your illustrations. You'll find publisher contact information on websites like the Association of American Publishers, and they often have specific guidelines on their websites for what they expect to see.

You'll also need to create a professional-looking PDF of your manuscript and your illustrations, and you'll address the editor directly, explaining why you think your book is a good fit for their list. So yes, you can absolutely submit your finished work, but you'll be going straight to the publisher, not an agent.
