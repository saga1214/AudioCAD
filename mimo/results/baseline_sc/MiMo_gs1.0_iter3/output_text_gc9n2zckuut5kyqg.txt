Absolutely! Here’s a budget-friendly dessert strategy that’s kid-friendly, healthy-ish, and diabetes-aware, with tips to keep costs down:

### 🍎 Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:
1. **Fruit-Based Treats** (Low-cost, high appeal):  
   - **Fresh Fruit Skewers**: Grapes, berries, melon chunks, pineapple (buy seasonal).  
   - **DIY Yogurt Parfaits**: Layer Greek yogurt (protein-rich), granola (buy in bulk), and seasonal fruit.  
   - **Fruit Salad**: Add a splash of honey or maple syrup for sweetness.  

2. **Healthy Swaps**:  
   - **Oatmeal Cookies**: Use whole-grain oats, mashed bananas (replaces butter/sugar), and dark chocolate chips (less sugar).  
   - **Frozen Yogurt Bark**: Spread Greek yogurt on parchment, top with berries and nuts, freeze, then break into pieces.  

3. **Kid-Friendly Fun**:  
   - **Mini Ice Cream Sandwiches**: Use low-sugar cookie bases and vanilla ice cream (buy tubs, not cones).  
   - **DIY Cookie Decorating Station**: Provide sugar-free frosting, sprinkles, and plain cookies (kids make their own).  

4. **Low-Cost Classics**:  
   - **Watermelon Wedges**: Buy pre-cut if needed.  
   - **Brownie Bites**: Use a mix (like Betty Crocker) + box chocolate chips (cheaper than homemade).  

### 💰 Cost-Saving Tips:
- **Buy in Bulk**: Seasonal produce, oats, yogurt, and frozen fruits are cheaper.  
- **DIY Stations**: Let guests assemble their own (e.g., yogurt parfaits) to reduce waste.  
- **Minimal Desserts**: Focus on 1-2 main desserts (e.g., fruit skewers + brownie bites) instead of a full spread.  
- **Use Existing Resources**: Ask local bakeries for sugar-free cookies or fruit baskets as donations.  
- **Water is Free**: Serve infused water (cucumber + lemon) alongside sugary drinks.  

### 🌟 Diabetes Awareness Tie-In:
- Label desserts with sugar content (e.g., "15g sugar" vs. "5g sugar").  
- Offer a "low-sugar" table with fruit, nuts, and sugar-free options.  
- Include a small informational card about choosing balanced desserts.  

**Example Budget Menu**:  
- **Main**: BBQ burgers/hot dogs (buy in bulk, use veggie skewers for vegetarians).  
- **Desserts**: Fruit skewers + oatmeal cookies + brownie bites.  
- **Drinks**: Water, lemonade, and iced tea (infused with berries).
