The vaccination campaign is already known to have strong opposition. But the sugar campaign, while also facing some resistance, might be less polarized. The sugar campaign's score, on the other hand, would improve because its resistance is more predictable and easier to manage through clear communication and education.

The vaccination campaign's effectiveness is directly tied to overcoming a much larger and more difficult hurdle of public distrust.
