That's a really interesting counterfactual. The Age of Discovery was a massive, complex event. The fall of Constantinople was a key trigger that amplified the desire for a new route, but it wasn't the sole cause.

However, the Portuguese were already pushing down the African coast before 1453, so the momentum was already building. The fall of Constantinople likely accelerated the efforts and provided a sense of urgency. So yes, it's possible the Age of Discovery would have happened around the same time, but the timing and intensity of Portuguese exploration might have been different.
