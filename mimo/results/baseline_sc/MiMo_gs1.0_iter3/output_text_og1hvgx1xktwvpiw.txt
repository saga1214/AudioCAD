Okay, let me clarify that for you. The original, classic Boulevardier recipe is actually equal parts: one ounce of whiskey, one ounce of sweet vermouth, and one ounce of Campari. The version with one and a quarter ounces of whiskey is a popular variation, but not the original. 

Now, for the Last Chance, that's an equal-parts cocktail too, but it uses different ingredients. You'll need three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. It’s a great choice, especially with the weather looking so nice. Enjoy your party!
