Okay, let's talk about that. The 'forever' part of the name is a bit of a misnomer. While these chemicals are extremely persistent and stay in the environment for a long time, your body does have a natural way to get rid of them. It's a process called detoxification. Basically, your liver processes them and your kidneys filter them out. So, while you can't magically make them disappear, your body is designed to handle them. Things like a healthy diet and exercise can definitely help support that process. A juice cleanse or a water fast won't do anything special; it's more about maintaining your overall health.

Now, for a quick list of things to avoid, here are five common sources to look out for. First, non-stick cookware, like Teflon. Second, grease-resistant packaging, like fast-food wrappers. Third, stain and water-resistant fabrics, such as carpets or outdoor clothes. Fourth, certain cosmetics and even some dental floss. And fifth, tap water, which is why a good water filter is so important.
