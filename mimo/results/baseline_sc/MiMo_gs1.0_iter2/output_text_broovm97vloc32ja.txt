Yes, you can absolutely submit an illustrated manuscript. In fact, many publishers actively seek out authors who can also do the artwork, which is often called an 'author-illustrator' submission. It's a huge advantage.

However, you need to be careful about the format. You'll want to submit a professional, single-page PDF file that clearly lays out your text and your illustrations. This is sometimes called a 'book sample'. Just make sure it's polished and ready for their review.

And yes, they will definitely consider it. In fact, it can be a very powerful selling point for your work.
