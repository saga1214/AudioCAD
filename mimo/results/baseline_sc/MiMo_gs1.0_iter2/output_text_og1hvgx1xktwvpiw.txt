Okay, let me clarify that for you. The original Boulevardier is made with equal parts of bourbon, sweet vermouth, and Campari. Your friend's version, with one and a quarter ounces of bourbon, is actually a variation called a "Boulevardier No. 2." The Last Word is a completely different drink made with gin, green Chartreuse, and maraschino. 

So, for the Last Word, the recipe is three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. All shaken and strained. Hope that helps you get your drink sorted out!
