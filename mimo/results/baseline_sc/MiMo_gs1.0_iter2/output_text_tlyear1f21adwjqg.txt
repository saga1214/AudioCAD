That's a tough one to answer with a single word, because it really depends on the location and what's already there. For instance, if you're near a large river, hydropower is often the most efficient choice. If you're in a sunny desert, solar might be better. And if you're in a windy area, wind power could be the top option. So, there isn't one single 'best' answer for everyone. The most efficient source is usually the one that fits the local geography best.
