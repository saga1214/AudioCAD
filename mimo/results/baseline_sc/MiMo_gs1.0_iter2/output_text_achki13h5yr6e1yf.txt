Of course. Let's break down the prices for the main dish, the side, and the total meal. For the main dish, the chicken cutlets: two pounds of chicken breasts will be between eight and fifteen dollars, a pound of butter is four to seven dollars, a half-gallon of milk is three to five dollars, a dozen eggs are three to seven dollars, and a pound of all-purpose flour is three to five dollars. For the garlic mashed potatoes side: a five-pound bag of potatoes is three to six dollars, and a head of garlic is about a dollar. And for the table, salt and pepper will be three to six dollars. This brings the total estimated cost for all ingredients for four people to between thirty-three and sixty dollars.
