You're getting very close to the core of the debate. It's a great point, but there's still a key distinction to be made.

The egg that contained the first chicken's DNA was laid by a bird that was not quite a chicken yet. It was a bird with the genetic potential to become the first chicken.

So, the egg was not the first chicken egg; it was the egg of a proto-chicken. The first true chicken hatched from it.

Therefore, the chicken still came first.
