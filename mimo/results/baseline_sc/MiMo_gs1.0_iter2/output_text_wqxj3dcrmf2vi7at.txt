Here's a quick recap of all the treat ideas for Princess, your brown-and-white corgi princess:

### 🍪 **Homemade Treats** (Healthy & Customizable)
1. **Pumpkin Bites**: Mashed canned pumpkin (plain) mixed with peanut butter or yogurt, rolled into small balls. Freeze for extra crunch!  
2. **Oat & Peanut Butter Cookies**: Roll oats + peanut butter into small balls, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 minutes. Cool before breaking into tiny pieces.  
3. **Carrot/Chicken Sticks**: Slice carrots or cooked chicken breast into thin sticks. Soak in water or broth for softness if needed.  

### 🛒 **Store-Bought Brands** (Convenient & Trusted)
1. **Blue Buffalo** (Grain-Free options, high-protein).  
2. **Wellness** (Grain-Free, no fillers).  
3. **Zuke’s** (Mini Nibbles – small, crunchy, grain-free).  
4. **Greenies** (Dental health treats – great for occasional chewers).  
5. **Merrick** (Grain-Free, natural ingredients).  

### 📌 **Key Tips for Princess**  
- **Safety First**: Avoid xylitol (in peanut butter/sweeteners), chocolate, or grapes.  
- **Portion Control**: Corgis are small! Stick to tiny pieces (size of a pea).  
- **Training**: Use treats for "sit," "quiet," or potty rewards. Pair with clear commands ("sit" or "up").  
- **Freeze for Fun**: Thaw homemade treats or freeze store-bought ones for a chewy reward.  

### 💡 Bonus Idea  
Try **freeze-dried blueberries** or **sweet potato chips**—crunchy, nutritious, and corgis adore them!
