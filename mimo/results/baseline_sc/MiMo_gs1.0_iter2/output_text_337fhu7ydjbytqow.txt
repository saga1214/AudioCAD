### **20-Mile Segment (Night Running)**  
*Focus: Warmth, visibility, and moisture management.*  
1. **Base Layer:**  
   - Moisture-wicking shirt (long-sleeve recommended).  
   - Moisture-wicking shorts or leggings.  
2. **Mid-Layer:**  
   - Fleece jacket or lightweight insulated vest.  
3. **Outer Layer:**  
   - Windproof/waterproof shell jacket.  
4. **Head & Hands:**  
   - Warm hat (fleece or balaclava).  
   - Gloves (windproof, preferably with insulation).  
   - Headlamp + **extra batteries** (or backup light).  
5. **Extras:**  
   - Buff/gaiter (for warmth or sun protection).  
   - Light insulated jacket (in case you slow down).  
   - Small running pack/vest (to carry layers/extra gear).  

---

### **12-Mile Segment (Daytime)**  
*Focus: Comfort, sun protection, and light warmth.*  
1. **Base Layer:**  
   - Moisture-wicking shirt (short-sleeve).  
   - Moisture-wicking shorts/leggings.  
2. **Mid-Layer:**  
   - Fleece or thin insulated jacket (lightweight).  
3. **Outer Layer:**  
   - Light rain jacket (for sudden showers).  
4. **Head & Hands:**  
   - Sun hat (upcycled baseball cap or wide-brimmed hat).  
   - Gloves (optional, but handy for cold hands).  
5. **Extras:**  
   - Buff/gaiter (for dust/sun protection).  
   - **No headlamp needed** (daytime visibility).  
   - Small running pack/vest (for extra layers/water).  

---

### **Shared Essentials (Both Segments)**  
- **Hydration:** 1L water bladder + hydration bladder tube.  
- **Layers:** Extra shirt + socks (in case you sweat heavily).  
- **Tools:** Trekking poles (if needed for hills).  
- **Safety:** Race bib, phone in a zippered pouch, ID.  
- **Foot Care:** Blister prevention (moleskin, tape).  

### **Key Notes**  
- **Pack/Vest:** Essential for carrying layers between segments.  
- **Weather Flexibility:** Adjust layers based on real-time conditions (e.g., add windbreaker if windy).  
- **Aid Stations:** Use them to resupply water/food but carry extras if trails are remote.
