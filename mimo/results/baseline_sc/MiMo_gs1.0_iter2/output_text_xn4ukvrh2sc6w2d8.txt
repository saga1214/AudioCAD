First, for the Old Fashioned. You'll need two ounces of bourbon, like a good Kentucky brand. Then, one sugar cube, or you can use half an ounce of simple syrup. For bitters, you'll want two to three dashes of Angostura, and one dash of orange bitters if you have them. For the garnish, you'll need an orange twist for the rim and to express the oils over the drink.

Next, the Paper Plane. You'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of Aperol, half an ounce of Amaro Nonino, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Shake all that with ice and strain it into a coupe glass. No garnish needed, but you can add a lemon twist if you like.

For the Whiskey Sour, you'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. If you're using the egg white option, you'll also add half an ounce of that. Shake everything with ice, strain into a rocks glass, and garnish with a cherry or an orange slice.

And for the French 75. You'll use an ounce of gin, half an ounce of lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. Shake those three with ice, then strain into a champagne flute and top it off with about three ounces of sparkling wine or prosecco. The garnish is usually a lemon twist.
