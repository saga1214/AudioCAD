First, the Old Fashioned. For that, you'll need two ounces of bourbon, one sugar cube, or half an ounce of simple syrup, and two to three dashes of Angostura bitters. Add the bitters and sugar to the sugar cube, muddle it until it's dissolved, then add the bourbon and stir it with ice. 

Next, the Paper Plane. This one's easy: two ounces of bourbon, one ounce of Aperol, half an ounce of Amaro Nonino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Just shake it with ice and strain into a coupe glass. 

For the Whiskey Sour, here's the classic with the egg white. You'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and one egg white. You'll do a dry shake first—so, just the bourbon, lemon juice, syrup, and egg white, all shaken without ice. Then, add ice and shake again hard, and serve it over fresh ice in a rocks glass. You can garnish it with an orange twist or a cherry. 

The Aviation is a great choice too. It's two ounces of gin, half an ounce of maraschino liqueur, a quarter-ounce of crème de violette, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Shake all that with ice and strain into a chilled cocktail glass. A cherry garnish is perfect. 

And for that third option you wanted, I'd recommend the French 75. You'll need an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and two ounces of dry champagne. Just shake the gin, lemon, and syrup first, strain it into a flute, and top with the champagne.
