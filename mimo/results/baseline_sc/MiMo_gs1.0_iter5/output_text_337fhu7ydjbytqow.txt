### **20-Mile Overnight Segment (Night Running)**
1. **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking long-sleeve shirt & leggings (lightweight, quick-dry).  
2. **Mid-Layer:** Fleece jacket or lightweight insulated vest (for warmth).  
3. **Outer Layer:** Windproof/waterproof jacket (e.g., rain shell).  
4. **Headwear:** Warm hat + buff/gaiter (for neck/wrists).  
5. **Hands:** Trail gloves + waterproof gloves (for grip/dryness).  
6. **Lighting:** **Bright headlamp** + spare batteries (critical for night).  
7. **Footwear:** Trail running shoes with grip (hilly terrain).  
8. **Extras:**  
   - Small daypack for layers/essentials.  
   - Lightweight insulated jacket (in case you slow down).  
   - Hand warmers (optional but helpful).  

---

### **12-Mile Daybreak Segment (Early Morning)**
1. **Base Layer:** Lightweight moisture-wicking shirt + shorts.  
2. **Mid-Layer:** Light fleece or breathable vest (for early chill).  
3. **Outer Layer:** Breathable windbreaker (no need for heavy rain gear).  
4. **Headwear:** Cap or lightweight sun hat (dawn sun can be strong).  
5. **Hands:** Gloves (if still chilly) or just a buff.  
6. **Lighting:** Headlamp or handheld light (if needed for low light).  
7. **Footwear:** Same trail shoes as earlier (damp ground possible).  
8. **Extras:**  
   - Small pack for layers if needed.  
   - Light jacket for the final miles as it warms up.  

---

### **Key Notes:**
- **Transition:** Swap layers as you switch segments (e.g., ditch the fleece after 20 miles, keep gloves/warm hat for the first half of the final segment).  
- **Aid Stations:** Use them to swap layers, gloves, or socks if needed.  
- **Weather:** If rain is likely, prioritize waterproof outer layers for the 20-mile segment.
