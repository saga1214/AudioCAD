Absolutely! Here’s a **budget-friendly, healthy, and kid-friendly dessert plan** that aligns with your diabetes awareness fundraiser goals:

### 🍎 Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas (Focus on Whole Foods & DIY)
1. **Fruit Skewers & Parfaits**  
   - **Skewers:** Alternate colorful fruit (berries, grapes, melon, pineapple) with a sprinkle of chia seeds or coconut flakes.  
   - **Parfaits:** Layer Greek yogurt with fresh fruit and a touch of oats (no sugar added). *Cost: Under $1/parfait.*

2. **Homemade Oatmeal Cookies**  
   - Use oats, mashed bananas or applesauce (instead of butter/sugar), and add-ins like chocolate chips, nuts, or seeds. Bake in batches ahead of time.  
   - *Tip:* Freeze extras and thaw as needed. *Cost: ~$0.75/cookie.*

3. **Energy Bites**  
   - Blend oats, nut butter, honey, and chia seeds. Roll into bite-sized balls. Add cocoa powder for chocolate-dipped treats.  
   - *Cost: ~$1.50/batch (makes 20 bites).*

4. **Frozen Yogurt Bark**  
   - Spread Greek yogurt on parchment, top with berries, honey, and crushed nuts. Freeze, then break into pieces.  
   - *Cost: ~$2/batch (serves 15).*

5. **"Dessert" Smoothie Bowls**  
   - Blend frozen bananas, spinach, and almond milk. Top with sliced fruit, granola, and a drizzle of nut butter.  
   - *Cost: ~$1.50/bowl.*

6. **Dark Chocolate-Dipped Fruit**  
   - Dip strawberries, orange segments, or banana chunks in melted dark chocolate (70%+ cocoa).  
   - *Tip:* Use bulk chocolate chips and melt with a double boiler. *Cost: ~$1.50/serving.*

### 💡 Cost-Saving Tips
- **Batch Cooking:** Make cookies/energy bites in large batches and store in airtight containers.  
- **Local Sourcing:** Buy seasonal fruits/veggies from farmers' markets (cheaper and fresher).  
- **DIY Decor:** Use edible flowers or citrus zest for garnish instead of expensive toppings.  
- **Partner with Local Bakeries:** Ask for donated sugar-free/low-sugar treats (many do this for community events!).  
- **Hydration Station:** Offer infused water (cucumber, lemon, mint) instead of sugary drinks.

### 📌 Key Considerations
- **Allergies:** Label desserts clearly (e.g., "Nut-Free Option").  
- **Presentation:** Use colorful plates and fun labels like "Berry Blast" or "Tropical Twist" to make healthy options appealing.  
- **Kid Engagement:** Let kids assemble their own fruit skewers or decorate yogurt parfaits.

This approach keeps desserts **affordable, nutritious, and fun** while supporting your diabetes awareness mission. By focusing on whole ingredients and prep-ahead strategies, you’ll minimize waste and costs! 🎉
