Of course. For your zone 10B climate, I'd suggest starting with some quick and easy options. Herbs are a great place to begin. You can get a basil plant, and it loves the heat. Mint is also super easy, but make sure you keep it in a container so it doesn't take over. You'll also want parsley, which is a little more hardy, and cilantro, which grows fast in the warm weather.

For leafy greens, you can try lettuce, spinach, and kale. They tolerate the heat a little better than some other greens, and they grow pretty fast, so you can get a few harvests in before the summer really kicks in.

Then, for something a bit more challenging but very rewarding, you could try tomatoes and peppers. They do well in the warmth. Just remember to give them plenty of sun and water. 

These choices are all pretty resilient in Florida's climate and should give you a good, healthy harvest. Happy growing!
