Here’s a clear, structured breakdown of a professional resume format, incorporating your specific concerns.

**Resume Structure & Formatting:**

1.  **Contact Information (Top):**
    *   **Name:** Centered or left-aligned. Bold, 14-16pt font.
    *   **Location:** City, State (e.g., San Francisco, CA).
    *   **Phone:** (123) 456-7890
    *   **Email:** your.email@example.com
    *   **LinkedIn URL:** (Optional but recommended - `linkedin.com/in/yourname`)
    *   **Portfolio URL:** (If applicable)
    *   **Optional:** Professional Photo (if industry standard, e.g., law, finance), Industry-Specific Links (e.g., GitHub, Behance).

2.  **Professional Summary (Above Experience):**
    *   2-4 sentences summarizing your career highlights, key skills, and career goals.
    *   Tailor this for each job application.
    *   *Example:* "Results-driven Marketing Manager with 10+ years of experience in digital campaign strategy and lead generation for SaaS companies. Proven ability to increase conversion rates by 25%+ through data-driven optimizations. Seeking to leverage expertise in growth hacking at [Company Name]."

3.  **Relevant Experience (Key for Career Changes):**
    *   **Format:** Job Title, Company Name, City, State | Month Year - Month Year (e.g., `Marketing Manager | Acme Inc. | Chicago, IL | 01/2018 - 12/2023`)
    *   **Bullet Points (3-5 per role):** Start with strong action verbs (e.g., "Spearheaded," "Increased," "Managed," "Developed"). Focus on achievements, not just duties. Quantify whenever possible (e.g., "Increased sales by 15%," "Managed a $500k budget").
    *   **Focus:** Prioritize roles and duties most relevant to the job you're applying for. You can omit very old, irrelevant roles.

4.  **Skills Section (Crucial & Strategic):**
    *   **Placement:** Below Experience, above Education. *Alternatively*, if skills are highly relevant, you can combine them with a "Summary" or "Profile" section.
    *   **Format:** Use clear categories if needed (e.g., **Technical Skills**, **Software**, **Languages**).
    *   **Content:** List hard skills (e.g., Python, Salesforce, Google Analytics, Project Management) and relevant soft skills (e.g., Leadership, Communication, Problem-Solving).
    *   **Tailor:** Mirror keywords from the job description.

5.  **Education (Top of Main Content):**
    *   **Degree:** Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (Bold)
    *   **University:** University of California, Berkeley
    *   **Location:** Berkeley, CA
    *   **Dates:** May 2010
    *   **Honors/Awards:** Graduated with Highest Honors (GPA: 3.9), Dean's List (4 Years), Scholarship Recipient (List specific if impactful)
    *   **Relevant Coursework:** (Optional, if highly relevant to target job)

6.  **Additional Experience / Work History (Optional but Recommended):**
    *   **Purpose:** For roles that are less relevant but provide good context (e.g., your 15-year gap period roles, part-time jobs from long ago).
    *   **Format:** Title, Company, Location | Dates (e.g., `Intern, Marketing Dept. | ABC Company | Boston, MA | 06/2008 - 08/2008`)
    *   **Content:** Keep it very brief (1-2 bullet points max). Focus on transferable skills if applicable.

7.  **Achievements & Awards (Optional Section):**
    *   **Placement:** Below Experience or combined with Education.
    *   **Content:** List significant non-work-related honors, publications, competitions, etc., that demonstrate exceptional ability. (e.g., "Winner, National Science Competition, 2009").

8.  **Certifications & Licenses:**
    *   **Placement:** Below Experience.
    *   **Content:** List relevant professional certifications (e.g., PMP, CPA, Google Ads Certification).

9.  **Projects (If Applicable):**
    *   **Placement:** Below Experience.
    *   **Content:** For students, recent grads, or roles requiring specific technical skills. Include Project Title, Description (brief), Technologies Used, and Link (if online).

**Key Principles:**

*   **Conciseness:** Aim for 1-2 pages max. Recruiters scan quickly.
*   **Clarity:** Use clear headings, bullet points, and consistent formatting.
*   **Tailoring:** Customize *every* resume for the specific job.
*   **Quantify:** Use numbers to demonstrate impact.
*   **Keywords:** Mirror the language from the job description.
*   **Proofread:** Absolutely no typos or grammatical errors.

This structure provides a clear path forward. You can build it step-by-step, focusing on the most relevant sections first. Good luck!
