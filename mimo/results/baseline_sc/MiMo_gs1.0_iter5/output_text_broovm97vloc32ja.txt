That's a great question, and it's a common point of confusion. Yes, if you are an author and an illustrator, you can absolutely submit a complete manuscript with your own illustrations to a publisher. However, the process is a bit different.

You'll typically submit your manuscript and your artwork to a publisher who specializes in something called 'illustrated books' or 'fully illustrated children's books'. The key difference is that these publishers are looking for the complete package, not just a story. You will need to research these specific publishers to see if they accept unsolicited manuscripts and artwork. Many will have guidelines for how to submit, so be sure to follow those exactly.
