Okay, let's break down the costs. For the cake, the main ingredients like flour, sugar, and butter are usually pretty affordable. A box mix might be under five dollars, and adding some frosting ingredients, you're probably looking at around ten to fifteen dollars total for the cake and frosting. 

For the wall repair, the drywall compound is maybe five to ten dollars, sandpaper is a dollar or two, and a putty knife is another five. The paint and molding will be the bigger costs here, probably around ten to twenty dollars combined. 

So, to be safe, I'd say you should bring at least thirty dollars with you to the store. That should cover everything for both the cake and the wall repair. If you find things are a bit more expensive, it's good to have a little extra, but thirty should be a solid starting point.
