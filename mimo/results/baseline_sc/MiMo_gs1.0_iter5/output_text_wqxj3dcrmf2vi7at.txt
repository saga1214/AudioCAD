Here are some treat ideas for Princess, the corgi princess, broken down into categories:

### 🏠 Homemade Treats (Easy & Healthy)
1. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**: Mix peanut butter (unsalted, no xylitol!) with rolled oats, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15-20 mins. Cool and break into small pieces.
2. **Freeze-Dried Chicken**: Bake chicken breasts until crispy, then freeze in small pieces (or buy pre-made from stores like Merrick).
3. **Sweet Potato Bites**: Steam and mash sweet potato, shape into small discs, freeze on a tray before bagging. Cut into quarters.

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands (Check Ingredients!)
- **Pupcakes**: Mini frozen treat cups (blueberries, oats, peanut butter).
- **Zuke's Mini Naturals**: Small kibble bites (look for "no artificial flavors").
- **Greenies Dental Treats**: For oral health (chewy, minty).
- **Earthborn Holistic Grilled Chicken Bites**: High-protein chew.

### 🌟 Tips for Princess
- **Portion Control**: Stick to 1-2 tbsp treats/day max.
- **Allergies**: Avoid xylitol (in peanut butter), dairy, and chocolate.
- **Training**: Use tiny pieces during commands (e.g., 1/8 of a cookie).
- **Potty Rewards**: High-value treats like chicken or cheese for outdoor success!

> "Corgis thrive on consistency—use the same treat size for every command to keep training fair!" 🐾
