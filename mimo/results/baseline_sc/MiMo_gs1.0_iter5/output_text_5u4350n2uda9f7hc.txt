That's a great question—and a very practical one! The good news is that **small succulents and snake plants are generally lightweight**, making them ideal for carrying up stairs. Here’s what to consider:

### ✅ **Weight & Portability:**
1. **Succulents:**  
   - Most stay small and lightweight, even in 4–6-inch pots.  
   - A full pot might weigh **3–7 pounds**—manageable for most people.  
   - Plastic nursery pots (common for succulents) are very light.

2. **Snake Plant (Sansevieria):**  
   - Mature plants are compact but can get wider (up to 12 inches across).  
   - A small pot (4–6 inches) weighs **5–10 pounds**. Larger pots are heavier, but you can get them small.  
   - **Tip:** Snake plants can be pruned to stay compact if needed!

### 📦 **Container Tips:**
- **Avoid heavy ceramic pots**—opt for lightweight plastic or terracotta (which is still manageable).  
- **Check pot weights** at the nursery before buying.  
- **Remove soil** before moving if possible (but don’t overwater to avoid root rot).

### 🌿 **Balcony Setup:**
- **On your table:** A small succulent pot (3–4 inches) will look great and won’t overcrowd the space.  
- **On the floor:** A snake plant will thrive there, staying low and stable.  
- **Wind resistance:** Snake plants are sturdy—succulents are delicate but low enough to avoid wind stress.

### 💡 **Final Advice:**
- **Buy small** so they stay lightweight.  
- **Water sparingly** to keep soil light.  
- **Repot later** if needed—both plants are easy to propagate if you want to share extras!

You can absolutely bring these home after your trip—just pack them in a reusable bag. Enjoy your NYC balcony oasis! 🌱✨
