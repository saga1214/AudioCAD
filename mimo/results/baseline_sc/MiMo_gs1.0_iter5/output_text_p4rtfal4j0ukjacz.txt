You're right, 450 milligrams is only 20 percent of the daily recommendation, so it's not excessive in a vacuum. However, when you consider that this is your *only* meal of the day, you're getting all that sodium in one go, which is a different situation.

It's generally better to get sodium from foods like milk or yogurt, rather than from sauces and tinned goods, because those can also bring in other additives. Since this is your only meal, it's not ideal to have such a high concentration of sodium in one serving, especially if you don't eat other salty foods later.

For a healthier version, you could try to reduce the sodium by using less soy sauce or oyster sauce, choosing a low-sodium version, or using other herbs and spices for flavor.
