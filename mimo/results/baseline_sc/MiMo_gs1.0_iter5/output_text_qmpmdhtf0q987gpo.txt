Okay, it sounds like you're making solid progress on the cabinet project, which is great. Since you've already taken care of the hardware removal and the sanding, the next step is to get everything prepped for the primer.

Here's what you should do: First, give all the sanded surfaces a quick wipe down with a tack cloth or a slightly damp rag to get rid of any dust. Then, you'll need to apply the primer. It's best to use a brush or roller for the edges and corners, and then use a paintbrush for the flat surfaces. You'll want to apply the primer in thin, even coats. After it dries, which usually takes about two to four hours, you'll need to lightly sand it one more time with some fine-grit sandpaper, around 220 grit. This step is really important because it helps the primer create a perfect, smooth surface for the final paint to stick to. Then, just wipe away any dust, and you're ready to move on to your new off-white paint color.
