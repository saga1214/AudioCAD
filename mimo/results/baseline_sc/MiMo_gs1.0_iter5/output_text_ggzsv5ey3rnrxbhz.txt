I understand the concern. While it's true that these chemicals are persistent, your body does have a way to get rid of them. It's a process called excretion, where your kidneys filter them out over time. So, things like drinking more water and eating a balanced diet can definitely help support your body's natural detoxification process.

As for a list of things to avoid, based on the most common sources, here's a quick rundown. The top five would be: first, non-stick cookware with Teflon; second, grease-proof packaging like pizza boxes; third, stain-resistant carpets and furniture; fourth, certain cosmetics and floss; and fifth, unfiltered tap water. But remember, the goal is to reduce your exposure, not to create a new list of anxieties. It's about making small, manageable changes.
