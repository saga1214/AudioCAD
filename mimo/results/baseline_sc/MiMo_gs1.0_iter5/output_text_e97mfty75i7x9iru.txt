Here's a complete recap of cough advice and tips for brain fog:

### For Your Cough:
1.  **Honey:** A spoonful (or in warm tea) can soothe irritation and reduce coughing.
2.  **Saltwater Gargle:** Mix warm water with 1/4 teaspoon salt. Gargle for 15-30 seconds to ease throat irritation.
3.  **Elevate Your Head:** Use extra pillows to sleep with your head raised, reducing coughing at night.
4.  **Steam & Humidity:** Take a steamy shower or use a cool-mist humidifier to loosen mucus and ease coughing.

### For Brain Fog (common with illness):
1.  **Hydration:** Drink plenty of fluids (water, broth, tea). Dehydration worsens brain fog.
2.  **Rest:** Prioritize sleep and downtime. Your brain needs energy to recover.
3.  **Gentle Movement:** Short walks or light stretching can improve circulation and alertness.
4.  **Caffeine (Moderate):** A small amount of coffee or tea *might* help focus, but don't overdo it.
5.  **Nutrition:** Eat easily digestible, nutritious foods when you can (e.g., soups, fruits, toast). Avoid heavy meals.
6.  **Deep Breathing & Mindfulness:** Simple breathing exercises or short meditation can help clear mental fog.
7.  **Cool Air:** Open a window for fresh air or use a fan to reduce stuffy room fogginess.
8.  **Patience:** Brain fog often lingers after the flu or cold resolves. Give your body time.

**Important:** If your cough is severe, persistent, or your brain fog worsens significantly, or if you have other concerning symptoms (like high fever, chest pain, difficulty breathing), please contact your doctor or healthcare provider. Rest and fluids are your best allies right now.
