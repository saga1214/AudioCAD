You're absolutely right to raise those critical safety and supervision concerns. While building a connection is the foundation, the environment and supervision are non-negotiable for safety and ethical practice.

Your first priority should be to report this to your BCBA immediately. You need to clearly document that there are no parents or appropriate supervisors present, and that the environment is cluttered and poses a safety risk, especially with the dogs running around.

Safety has to come first. You can't effectively teach or connect with a child if they're at risk of injury. Once those issues are addressed with your BCBA, then you can focus on building on your connection with him.
