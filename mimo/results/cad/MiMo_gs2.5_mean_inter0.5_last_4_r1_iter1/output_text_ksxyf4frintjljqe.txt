Can you get me a simple recipe to make a vanilla cake with white frosting?
Sure! For a simple vanilla cake, you’ll need butter, sugar, eggs, flour, baking powder, milk, and vanilla extract. Cream the butter and sugar, add eggs, then mix in the dry ingredients along with milk and vanilla. Pour into a cake pan and bake around 30 minutes at 350 degrees. For the frosting, just whip butter, powdered sugar, a little vanilla, and a splash of milk until it’s smooth and fluffy. Let the cake cool before frosting, and you’re all set!
