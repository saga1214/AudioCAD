Let me clarify that for you. The standard one and a quarter to one to one ratio is actually the Boulevardier. Your friend's equal parts version is also correct and very common, but not the 'classic'. The Last Word you mentioned is the one made with equal parts. Its ingredients are three-quarters of an ounce each of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. Stir and strain, garnish with a lime wedge. Hope that helps clear things up!
