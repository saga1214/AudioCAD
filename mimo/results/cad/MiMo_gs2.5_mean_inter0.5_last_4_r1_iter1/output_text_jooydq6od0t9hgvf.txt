Here are quick, elegant recipes for both drinks:

### 1. **Classic Vodka Collins** (Vodka Mixer)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz vodka (your leftover)  
- 4 oz fresh lemonade or lemon-lime soda (e.g., Sprite)  
- 2 oz sparkling water  
- 1 oz simple syrup  
- Ice  
- Lime wedge + mint sprig for garnish  

**Instructions**:  
Shake vodka, simple syrup, and lemonade with ice. Strain into a Collins glass filled with ice. Top with sparkling water. Gently stir. Garnish with lime and mint.  

---

### 2. **Argentinian Fernet-Coca** (Fernet-Based)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz Fernet-Branca (or substitute with other fernet if unavailable)  
- 4 oz Coca-Cola (or ginger beer for non-soda option)  
- ½ oz fresh lime juice  
- Ice  
- Lime wedge for garnish  

**Instructions**:  
Fill a rocks glass with ice. Add fernet, lime juice, and Coca-Cola. Stir gently. Garnish with a lime wedge.  

> **Note**: Fernet is intensely bitter—offer it as a *sipping cocktail*, not a mixer. Serve in small portions. For non-drinkers/allergy: Offer sparkling water with lime or fruit-based mocktails (e.g., virgin mojitos).

---

### Quick Tips:  
- **Soda Allergy**: Avoid soda in the vodka Collins (use lemonade/sparkling water instead).  
- **Formality**: Use chilled glassware and garnish both drinks.  
- **Balance**: The vodka mix is light and refreshing; the fernet-coca is bolder—offer both to let guests choose.
