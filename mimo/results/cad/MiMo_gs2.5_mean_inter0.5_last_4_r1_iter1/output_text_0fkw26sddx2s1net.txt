Of course. Here’s a shopping list for your husband’s allergy-friendly Asian-inspired meal, avoiding legumes and seafood. For the starter, cucumbers, miso paste, bonito flakes if you want the real deal, or you can just use kombu for a vegetarian miso, sesame oil, and soy sauce. For the main course, chicken teriyaki requires boneless, skinless chicken breasts, soy sauce, mirin, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, jasmine rice, some colorful stir-fry veggies like bell peppers, carrots, and snap peas, and some cooking oil. Finally, for dessert, sticky Thai or Vietnamese jasmine rice, fresh ripe mango, coconut milk, sugar, salt, matcha powder, heavy cream, and condensed milk for that homemade ice cream. Enjoy your romantic dinner.
