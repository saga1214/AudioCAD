### **Refresh Station Plan**
1. **Theme & Ambiance**  
   - **Decor**: Use a clean, bright color scheme (light blues/greens).  
   - **Setup**: A small table with a simple tablecloth, a vase of fresh eucalyptus or mint sprigs (easy to clean), and a handwritten "Thank You" banner.  
   - **Scent**: Light citrus or lavender sachets (non-perishable, easy to remove).  

2. **Drinks**  
   - **Infused Water**: Lemon-ginger, cucumber-mint, or berry-infused water.  
   - **Chilled Options**: Sparkling water, iced herbal teas (mint/green tea—since you tolerate it mildly?), and cold-brew coffee.  
   - **No Mint Alternative**: Skip mint-flavored drinks; use fruit-infused water instead.  

3. **Snacks**  
   - **Light & Energizing**:  
     - Fresh fruit (grapes, berries, apple slices).  
     Palette cleansers: Lemon drops, peach-flavored mints, or orange slices.  
   - **Allergy-Friendly**:  
     - Nut-free trail mix (dried cranberries, sunflower seeds, pretzels).  
     - Rice crackers with hummus.  

4. **Wellness Touches**  
   - **Chill Zone**: A small bench or folding chairs for 5-minute breaks.  
   - **Sensory Breaks**: A mini "calm kit" with a stress ball, lavender oil (on a card), and a poem about resilience.  

---

### **Key Logistics**  
- **Allergies**: Label all snacks clearly (e.g., "Nut-Free" tags). Avoid mint-flavored candies.  
- **First-Timers**:  
  - **Buddy System**: Pair new volunteers with a "Welcome Buddy" for a 10-minute orientation.  
  - **Onboarding Kit**: Include a map of the shelter, a schedule cheat sheet, and a "Ask Me Anything" bracelet.  
- **Badges**: **Print now**—personalized badges boost recognition and streamline shifts.
