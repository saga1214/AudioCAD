Here's a complete treat guide tailored for your little corgi princess, including repeats and new ideas:

### 🍪 Homemade Treats (Portions tiny!)
1. **Frozen Bites**  
   Mashed pumpkin/sweet potato patted into tiny discs, frozen solid. (Chewy, cooling, dental good)
2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**  
   Mix xylitol-free PB with rolled oats, spoon onto baking sheet, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12 mins. Cool.
3. **Cooked Chicken Sticks**  
   Thinly slice baked chicken breast into matchstick-sized pieces.
4. **Carrot "Curls"**  
   Peel and julienned carrots, steamed briefly until soft but firm.

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands (Check labels!)
- **Training Bites:** Blue Buffalo, Wellness Tiny Bites, Kikoriki
- **Wholesome Options:** Zuke’s Mini Naturals (xylitol-free), Earthborn Holistic
- **Specialty:** Greenies (dental), freeze-dried liver bits (like Healthy Edibles)

### 🐶 Training Tips
**Sit Command:**  
- Hold treat near her snout, lift it up and back over her head.  
- As her bottom touches the ground, say "Sit!" and reward immediately.  
- Practice 5x/day in short sessions.

**Potty Training:**  
- Take her out **immediately** after waking, meals, or playtime.  
- Use a consistent cue like "Go potty!"  
- Reward **overly** when she goes outside (10+ treats).  
- Clean accidents with enzymatic cleaner (e.g., Nature’s Miracle).

### 💖 Bonus Princess Treats
- **"Doggy Ice Cream"**: Blend frozen banana + cocoa powder, scoop into molds.  
- **Cheese "Crackers"**: Thinly slice cheddar or mozzarella, bake at 200°F (95°C) for 10 mins.  
- **Freeze-Dried Meat**: Liver or chicken bits (Life Savers brand) for crunchy rewards.

**Pro Tip:** Always introduce new treats gradually and supervise. Your corgi’s health and happiness come first! 🐾
