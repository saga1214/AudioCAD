Based on your situation and the core mechanics of TikTok's algorithm, **engaging content** is the **single most important factor** for success. Here's why it outweighs the others:

### Why Engaging Content is #1:
1. **Algorithm-Driven Discovery**: TikTok's algorithm *only* promotes videos that retain viewers. If people don’t stop scrolling, watch fully, or interact (likes/comments/shares), the algorithm won’t show it to more people.  
2. **Hook > Everything Else**: The first 3 seconds are critical. If your content doesn’t grab attention immediately, no technical skill (lighting/audio/consistency) matters.  
3. **Relatability Trumps Polish**: People engage with *real*, entertaining, or relatable content—not just high production. A raw, funny moment can outperform a polished tutorial.  
4. **Feedback Loop**: Engagement (watch time, shares, duets) directly signals to TikTok that your content deserves more exposure.  

### Why Other Factors Matter (But Are Secondary):
- **Following Guidelines**: Non-negotiable (avoiding restrictions). Without this, *no* engagement matters.  
- **Conciseness**: Shorter videos (<15 sec) often perform better *if* they’re engaging. Brevity alone doesn’t guarantee success.  
- **Consistency**: Helps algorithm familiarity, but quality engagement trumps frequency if videos flop.  
- **Description/Hashtags**: Helpful for context, but TikTok prioritizes *user behavior* over metadata.  

### Key Takeaway:
> **You can’t “be found” if people don’t stop to engage.**  
> Focus first on creating content that hooks viewers instantly and makes them interact. Technical improvements (lighting/audio) are *enablers*—not substitutes. If your first 3 seconds don’t captivate, TikTok won’t amplify it, no matter how perfect it looks.
