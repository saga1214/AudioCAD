That's a great goal! A pumpkin pie is a very easy one-pot recipe. You just mix canned pumpkin, sugar, spices, and an egg in a pie dish, and pour it into a pre-baked crust. You could even do a simple apple crisp topping for that.

For something a little different but still very easy, you could make a no-bake cheesecake. You just mix cream cheese, sugar, and eggs in a bowl and pour it into a crust, then chill it.
