Here's a breakdown of diesel van maintenance checks and spark plug replacement steps:

**Brake Check Essentials:**
1.  **Visual Inspection:** Park on a level surface. Lift the front wheels and check brake pads/shoes. Look for uneven wear, cracks, or grease contamination on pads. Inspect rotors for excessive grooves or warping.
2.  **Warning Signs:**
    *   **Squealing/Grinding:** New pads (squeal) or worn-out pads/shoes (grinding).
    *   **Soft/Mushy Pedal:** Fluid in the master cylinder (sign of a leak).
    *   **Pulling to One Side:** Uneven brake wear or hydraulic system issues.
    *   **Reduced Pedal Feel:** Low brake fluid level or air in the system.
    *   **Burning Smell:** Severe brake drag or overheating.

**Diesel Spark Plug Replacement (General Steps - Consult Your Manual First!):**

Diesel engines have robust but distinct plugs. Here's the process:

1.  **Tools Needed:**
    *   Correct spark plug socket (usually 13/16" or 19mm, often with a swivel joint).
    *   Ratchet with a flex-head or universal joint.
    *   Torque wrench (crucial!).
    *   New spark plugs (check your manual for exact type/specs).
    *   Penetrating oil (WD-40).
    *   Small brush (for cleaning).
    *   Gloves & safety glasses.

2.  **Process:**
    *   **Disconnect Battery:** Prevent accidental starts.
    *   **Remove Air Intake:** Often needs removal for access. Disconnect hoses/clamps.
    *   **Locate & Mark:** Identify the plug wires. Mark each wire's position relative to its connector/rotor.
    *   **Loosen & Remove:** Spray penetrating oil on the plug threads. Use the socket and ratchet to loosen (turn counter-clockwise). Remove the plug. *Be careful not to drop it.*
    *   **Clean Threads:** Inspect the engine block threads. Clean debris with a brush if needed.
    *   **Install New Plug:**
        *   Apply a *small* amount of anti-seize compound to the new plug threads (check manual).
        *   Hand-tighten the plug *into the threads* until snug. Do NOT cross-thread.
        *   Use the torque wrench to tighten to the specified value (usually 25-35 ft-lbs for diesel). *Over-torquing can destroy threads.*
    *   **Reconnect:** Reinstall the air intake, reconnect all hoses/clamps. Reconnect the battery.
    *   **Reinstall Wires:** Reconnect the spark plug wires in the correct sequence (refer to your manual or marks).

**Other Critical Pre-Trip Checks for Your Diesel Van:**

1.  **Engine Oil & Filter:** Check level (when cold) and condition (should be clean golden, not dark/greasy). Top up if needed. Consider a new filter.
2.  **Coolant Level:** Check the reservoir (not the overflow tank) when cold. Top up to the "COLD" marks with the correct type.
3.  **Tire Pressure & Condition:** Check pressures (including spare) when cold. Inspect tread depth and look for embedded objects/bulbs. Ensure good tread wear.
4.  **Fluids:** Top up wiper fluid, washer fluid (for screens), and power steering fluid (if applicable).
5.  **Battery:** Clean terminals if corroded. Ensure connections are tight.
6.  **Campervan-Specific:** Check propane tanks (fill if possible, ensure connections are tight), fridge operation, water system (if applicable), generator (if you have one), and awning/livestock equipment.
