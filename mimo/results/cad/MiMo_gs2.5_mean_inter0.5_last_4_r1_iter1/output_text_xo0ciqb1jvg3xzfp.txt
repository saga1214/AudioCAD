The traditional Thanksgiving story often presents the event as a purely happy occasion for both groups. However, from the Wampanoag perspective, it was a moment of survival and alliance, not of pure celebration, because they were facing immense hardship and were aware of the consequences that would follow.

For the Pilgrims, it was a celebration of survival and bounty. For the Wampanoag, it was a strategic partnership.

Neither story captures the full picture. The true story is more nuanced. The Wampanoag were cooperative, but they also understood the larger context of colonization and disease. The Pilgrims were grateful, but they also brought challenges.

So, the event was complex. It was a moment of cooperation, but it was also part of a much larger, and ultimately tragic, story for the Wampanoag people.
