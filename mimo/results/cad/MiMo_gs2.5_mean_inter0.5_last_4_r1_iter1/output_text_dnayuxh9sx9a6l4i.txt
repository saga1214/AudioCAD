Sure! Here are some potential dog breeds or hybrids that fit your criteria—easy to train, good with kids/dogs, low-chase tendencies, and generally chill, while leaning toward purebreds or known mixes:  

1. **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel** – Affectionate, gentle, and great with kids. They’re small, adaptable to apartments, and not prone to excessive barking when alone.  

2. **French Bulldog** – Low-energy, playful, and friendly. They’re small, quiet (though not completely silent!), and don’t chase often.  

3. **Bulldog (English Bulldog)** – Calm, loyal, and low-maintenance. They’re not high-energy and get along well with everyone.  

4. **Golden Retriever** (purebred) – If size restrictions allow, they’re famously friendly, trainable, and great with kids/dogs.  

5. **Labrador Retriever** (purebred) – If "not too big" still permits, Labs are outgoing, easygoing, and highly social.  

6. **Poodle (Standard, Miniature, or Toy)** – Highly intelligent, hypoallergenic, and great with kids. They’re low-to-moderate energy and trainable.  

7. **Cocker Spaniel** – Sweet-natured, affectionate, and good with families. Moderate energy levels.  

8. **Beagle** – Friendly and curious, but can be vocal (watch noise levels when alone). Social with other dogs.  

9. **Portuguese Water Dog** – If you want a mix, this breed is known for being hypoallergenic, smart, and low-shedding. They’re also very trainable.  

10. **Goldendoodle** (Golden Retriever + Poodle mix) – Often combines the friendliness of Goldens with the hypoallergenic traits of Poodles.  

**Next Steps:**  
- Check shelters/rescues for adult dogs matching these breeds or mixes.  
- Visit breed-specific rescues to meet potential candidates.  
- Confirm with your landlord that these sizes (under 25–30 lbs?) and temperaments meet their noise/alone-time rules.  
- Discuss training plans early—consistent socialization and exercise will help!
