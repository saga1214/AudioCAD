Re-evaluating with more factors, like the target audience, reveals a different picture. An Ancient Egypt exhibit is guaranteed to attract a large, diverse crowd, which is great for revenue. But it's a saturated market. An Ancient Mesopotamia exhibit is a unique opportunity to offer something truly special, but success depends heavily on the museum's audience and educational goals. For a general public museum, Egypt is likely the safer bet. But for a museum aiming for deeper historical insight, Mesopotamia might be the more impactful choice.
