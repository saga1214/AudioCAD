Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time for vibrant fall colors really depends on where you go. 

If you're looking for those classic, fiery reds and oranges, late September to November is ideal. That's when the Mapache maple trees are in full color in the highlands, and the mountains have these stunning fall foliage displays. 

For coastal inspiration, like Riviera Maya or Oaxaca, the autumn colors are a bit milder but still beautiful. You'll see the ceiba trees with their fiery reds and the agave plants turning golden. 

And if you want to see something really unique, you could visit the Copper Canyon in Chihuahua. The autumn foliage there is just... breathtaking, especially with the deep blues of the canyon. 

Oh, and a pro tip: Mexico City is also a great place to visit year-round. You can find amazing workshops there for watercolor classes and get inspiration from the city's own vibrant street art. 

So yeah, my advice is to aim for late October or early November for the peak colors and the best weather. You'll get plenty of inspiration for your watercolor techniques!
