**Nautical Napture (Nautical Napture) Party Shopping List**

**I. Themed Main Course (Easy & Packable):**

*   **Theming:** Cut tuna salad & cheese into small rectangles (like "Tuna Fleet Ships" or "Cheese Treasure Chests"). Arrange crackers around them on a platter as "ship decks" or "shoreline." Use a small ship cutout to serve.

**II. Themed Appetizers (Jewels & Gems):**
*   Fruits: 2 lbs. Grapes (Purple & Green), 2 lbs. Apple Slices (Toss in lemon juice to prevent browning), 2 lbs. Orange Slices (or Melon Slices if no watermelon allergy)
*   **Theming:** Thread grapes onto 12-inch skewers (1 kid per skewer) = "Sparkling Jewels" or "Pearl Necklace." Cut apples into wedges = "Treasure Chests." Cut oranges/melon into wedges = "Sea Gems." Serve on small platters labeled "Captain's Jewels."

**III. Themed Snacks (Portable & Fun):**
*   Mini Pretzels (Mini Rods or Twists) - *Theming: Pirate Ship Masts*
*   Mini Corn Chips or Tortilla Chips - *Theming: Treasure Map Pieces*
*   Goldfish Crackers (in the classic box) - *Theming: Pirate Gold*
*   **Packaging:** Put each snack type in its own small container or seal individually in snack bags labeled with a pirate name ("Captain's Gold," "First Mate's Chips").

**IV. Drinks (Spill-Proof & Easy):**
*   Individual Juice Boxes (Apple, Grape, Orange, Berry Blast) - *Theming: "Pirate Punch"* (Label with pirate names)
*   Individual Water Bottles (Small size) - *Theming: "Cursed Sea Water"* (Label with pirate names)
*   **Packaging:** Label boxes/bottles clearly. Provide small napkins and a trash can.

**V. Cake & Decor (Bonus Touch!):**

5.  **Theming Through Presentation:** Names like "Tuna Fleet Ships," "Captain's Jewels," and "Pirate Punch" enhance the pirate atmosphere without adding mess.
