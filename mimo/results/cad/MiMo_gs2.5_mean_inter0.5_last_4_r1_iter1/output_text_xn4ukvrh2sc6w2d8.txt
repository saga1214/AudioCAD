### **Old Fashioned**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz bourbon or r ry  
- 1 sugar cube (or ¾ oz simple syrup)  
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  
*Instructions:*  
1. Muddle sugar cube with ½ oz water in a mixing glass.  
2. Add bourbon, bitters, ice, and stir until chilled.  
3. Strain into a **rocks glass over ice**.  
4. Express orange twist over the drink and garnish.  
*Tip:* Use Kentucky bourbon (e.g., Maker’s Mark, Buffalo Trace) for authenticity.  

---

### **Paper Plane**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz gin (London Dry)  
- ¾ oz Aperol  
- ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
*Instructions:*  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a **coupe glass** (or martini glass).  
*Garnish:* Cherry or lemon twist.  
*Balance:* Bitter (Aperol/Nonino), sweet (Nonino/Aperol), tart (lemon).  

---

### **Whiskey Sour**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 1 egg white (optional)  
*Instructions:*  
1. **With egg white:** Dry shake (no ice) all ingredients → add ice → shake again → strain into coupe/rocks glass.  
2. **Without egg white:** Shake with ice → strain → omit egg white.  
*Garnish:* Cherry or lemon slice.  

---

### **Aviation**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz gin  
- ¾ oz maraschino liqueur (Luxardo)  
- ¼ oz crème de violette  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
*Instructions:*  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a **cocktail glass** ( stemmed).  
*Garnish:* Maraschino cherry.  

---

### **Suggested Gin Cocktail Add-On: French 75**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 2 oz Champagne or prosecco  
*Instructions:*  
1. Shake gin, lemon, and syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a **champagne flute**.  
3. Top with sparkling wine.  
*Fall Twist:* Garnish with rosemary sprig.

1. **Bitters:** Use Angostura for Old Fashioned/Paper Plane; orange twist adds aroma.
