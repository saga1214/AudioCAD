Yes, you can absolutely submit an illustrated manuscript. In fact, that's a very common and successful approach for children's books.

However, how you submit it depends on whether you're the illustrator. If you are, you have a couple of options.

First, you can submit it yourself directly to publishers who accept unsolicited manuscripts. This is a bit riskier, and you'll probably face a lot of rejection. Your second, and much more effective option, is to find an agent first. They will then help you submit your complete package, which includes both your text and your illustrations. This is usually the best way to go because agents have established relationships with editors.
