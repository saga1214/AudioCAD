Okay, let's break this down. Chronic skin redness has many possible causes. Besides rosacea and sun damage, things like eczema, contact dermatitis from irritants, or allergic reactions could be the culprit. It could also be related to underlying health issues or even genetics.

Vasculitis, as we've discussed, is a specific cause where the blood vessels themselves are inflamed. The size of the vessel affected determines the symptoms. For example, inflammation in large vessels can cause rashes and fever, while small vessels are more likely to cause skin color changes, rashes, and pain.

It's a complex topic, so it's great that you're trying to understand it. A doctor can run tests to figure out what's really going on with you. In the meantime, for hair and nails, collagen and iron are good things to look into. For your skin, calming inflammation from the inside out with things like omega-3s might be a good idea, but definitely talk to a doctor first.
