Here's a concise list of my top recommendations for cutting sheet metal, drywall fastening, and insulation—all available locally in Indianapolis:

### 1. **Sheet Metal Cutting Tools**
   - **Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel**  
     *Best for:* Thicker sheets or straight cuts. High power, fast cuts.  
     *Where to Buy:* **Menards** (best local selection), Home Depot, Lowe’s.
   - **Jigsaw with Abrasive Metal Blade**  
     *Best for:* Curves or thinner sheets. More maneuverable than grinders.  
     *Where to Buy:* Tractor Supply Co., Home Depot, Lowe’s.
   - **Aviation Snips**  
     *Best for:* Thin sheets (<16 gauge) and tight curves. Quick hand tool.  
     *Where to Buy:* Home Depot, Lowe’s, Tractor Supply Co.

### 2. **Drywall Fastening**
   - **1¼-Inch Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws**  
     *Why:* Superior holding power vs. nails, prevents nail pops.  
     *Where to Buy:* **Menards** (cheapest), Home Depot, Lowe’s.

### 3. Wood Framing (if applicable)
   - **Ring-Shank Nails**  
     *Why:* Gripping rings prevent backing out in studs.  
     *Where to Buy:* Menards, Home Depot.

### 4. Insulation
   - **Fiberglass Insulation Batt**  
     *Why:* Standard choice, easy to fit between studs.  
     *Where to Buy:* Menards, Home Depot, Lowe’s.

---

### **Best Indianapolis Store for All Supplies: Menards**  
*Why:*  
- Lowest prices (e.g., drywall screws ~$50 for 500 vs. $75 at Home Depot).  
- Full range of metal tools (cut-off wheels, jigsaw blades).  
- Carries fiberglass batts, aviation snips, and ring-shank nails.  
- Larger inventory than Home Depot/Lowe’s for specialty items.

### Alternatives:
- **Home Depot/Lowe’s:** Better for immediate needs (e.g., same-day drywall screw pickup).  
- **Tractor Supply Co.:** Good for aviation snips/jigsaws if Menards is sold out.  

For insulation, fiberglass batts are widely available at all three stores. Prioritize **Menards** for cost savings and selection! 🔧🔧
