That’s a great question! For quick dinners, I’d suggest focusing on **minimal-ingredient meals** that use pantry staples or easy-to-find ingredients. Here are a few ideas:

### 1. **One-Pan Roasted Veggies + Protein**  
   - Toss veggies (like broccoli, bell peppers, zucchini) with olive oil, salt, and pepper.  
   - Add a protein (chicken, tofu, or chickpeas) and roast together.  
   - Serve over rice or quinoa.  
   → *Why it works*: Minimal cleanup, customizable, and uses common ingredients.

### 2. **Stir-Fry**  
   - Sauté garlic/ginger in a pan, add sliced veggies (carrots, snap peas, mushrooms), and toss with a simple sauce (soy + honey or just soy + sesame oil).  
   - Top with rice noodles, tofu, or chicken.  
   → *Bonus*: You already have rice noodles! This uses spices you likely own.

### 3. **Soup or Stew**  
   - Blend canned beans/lentils with broth, carrots, onions, and spices (cumin, paprika).  
   - Heat and serve with crusty bread or rice.  
   → *Ideal for*: Using up leftover veggies or pantry staples.

### 4. **Pasta with Minimal Effort**  
   - Cook pasta, drain, and toss with olive oil, garlic, and a handful of spinach.  
   - Add leftover roasted chicken or canned tuna for protein.  
   → *Pro tip*: Keep pasta, canned beans, and frozen veggies stocked for emergencies!

**Key tip**: Keep staples like rice, pasta, canned beans, frozen veggies, and basic spices (salt, pepper, olive oil) on hand for these quick meals. This way, you won’t need to declutter new gadgets—just simple bowls and pans! 😊
