Sure! First, think about your lifestyle and living space—a high-rise with a dog park is a good clue. Next, consider breeds known for being chill, friendly, and trainable. Some options include Labrador Retriever, Golden Retriever, and Cavalier King Charles Spaniel. If you’re open to mixes, Cockapoo or Bichon-Shih Tzu mixes can be great too. All these breeds are generally good with kids, other dogs, and don’t tend to be too reactive to squirrels. Reputable shelters often have these types of dogs available. Just remember to meet the dog in person to see if there’s a good vibe. Don’t forget to ask about their landlord approval again just in case size still comes up.
