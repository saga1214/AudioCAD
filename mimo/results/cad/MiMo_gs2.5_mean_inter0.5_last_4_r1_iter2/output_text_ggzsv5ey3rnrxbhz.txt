I hear how worried you are, and it's completely understandable. Let's focus on what you can control.

When it comes to PFAS, your best bet is to focus on what you're exposed to, rather than trying to 'flush' them out. A juice cleanse won't work; there's no special detox for these chemicals. The best thing you can do is avoid them in the first place.

So, to give you a clear list, here are five products you should try to avoid to minimize your exposure:
First, non-stick cookware. Switch to stainless steel, cast iron, or glass.
Second, grease-resistant food packaging, like fast-food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags.
Third, products labeled 'stain-resistant' or 'waterproof', such as carpets and some outdoor clothing.
Fourth, cosmetics and dental floss that might contain PFAS.
And fifth, since tap water can be a source, using a certified water filter is a great precaution.
