Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids is a big group! Based on all our back-and-forth, here is a shopping list designed for minimal mess and grab-and-go convenience for your theme park adventure.

First, let's talk about drinks, where variety and hydration are key. You'll want lots of reusable water bottles for everyone, sugar-free sodas for the adults, low-sugar juice boxes for the kids, and milk or chocolate milk for the children who prefer that. Individual boxes of unsweetened applesauce are also a great, healthy backup snack. And don't forget plenty of ice to keep everything cold!

For snacks, which should be easy and less messy, think about cheese sticks, grapes, pretzels bags, trail mix, baby carrots with single hummus cups, and cut-up cucumbers and bell peppers with ranch dip cups.

For the main meal, you have a couple of solid options. You could go with ten to twelve individual calzones from a local pizza place. Just make sure to ask them to pack them separately in heat packs and boxes. Another great, very easy option is to pre-order about twenty sandwiches or wraps, also packed individually.

And for dessert, to keep it simple and clean, pre-made cupcakes or brownie bars are perfect. They're already in individual portions, so no plates or utensils needed.

Finally, for packing, use at least two large coolers. Use insulated bags inside the coolers to help with temperature, and pack everything in separate containers so groups can grab what they need. This setup should give you all the variety you want while keeping things neat and tidy for you and your friend group. Have an absolutely fantastic time!
