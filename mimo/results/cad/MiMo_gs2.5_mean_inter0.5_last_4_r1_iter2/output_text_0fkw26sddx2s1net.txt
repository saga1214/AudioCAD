Of course. Here’s a detailed shopping list for your husband's allergy-friendly Asian-inspired meal. I've broken it down into starter, main course, and dessert.

First, for the starter options. If you choose Miso Cucumber Salad, you'll need ginkgo or white miso paste, sliced cucumbers, rice vinegar, sugar, and sesame oil. Or, if you prefer a Cucumber Salad with Sesame Dressing, you'll need cucumbers, a mix of rice vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, toasted sesame oil, toasted sesame seeds, and some freshly grated ginger.

Next, for the main course Chicken Teriyaki meal. You'll need about a pound of boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs. For the teriyaki sauce, you'll need soy sauce, mirin, sake, brown sugar, and a little bit of grated ginger and garlic. For the jasmine rice, you need jasmine rice, water, and salt. And for the stir-fried vegetables, you'll need bell peppers, snap peas, carrots, and some oil, soy sauce, and ginger.

Finally, for the dessert. If you make Mango Sticky Rice, you'll need sticky glutinous rice, fresh ripe mangoes, coconut milk, sugar, and salt. If you decide to make Matcha Homemade Ice Cream, you'll need matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and a pinch of vanilla extract.

Just a couple of quick notes: make sure all legumes are left off the list, seafood is nowhere in sight, and matcha powder or good quality miso paste can really make a difference in the final taste. Enjoy your romantic evening!
