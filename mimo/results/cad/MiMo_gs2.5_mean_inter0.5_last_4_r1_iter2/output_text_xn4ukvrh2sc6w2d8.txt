Here are the classic recipes with precise ingredients and measurements for your reception — perfect instructions for your bartenders:

### **1. Old Fashioned**  
**Glass:** Rocks glass over ice  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey  
- 1 sugar cube (or ¾ oz simple syrup)  
- 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube with bitters in the glass.  
2. Add bourbon and ice. Stir well (20–30 seconds).  
3. Strain into a fresh glass over ice if desired.  
4. Express orange twist over the drink, then garnish.  

> *Optional (Classic Technique):* For silky texture, add 0.75 oz egg white. Dry-shake (no ice) first, then shake with ice.  

---

### **2. Paper Plane**  
**Glass:** Coupe glass  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz Aperol  
- ¾ oz Maraschino Liqueur (e.g., Luxardo)  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- Orange twist (optional garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a coupe glass.  
3. Garnish with lemon twist.  

---

### **3. Whiskey Sour**  
**Glass:** Rocks glass over ice  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- *Optional:* 0.75 oz egg white (for foam)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake bourbon, lemon juice, and syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a glass.  
3. *With egg white:* Dry-shake egg white first, then add other ingredients and shake with ice.  

---

### **4. French 75 (Recommended Gin Cocktail)**  
**Glass:** Champagne flute  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz lemon juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 1 oz Champagne or dry sparkling wine  
- Lemon twist (garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into flute.  
3. Top with Champagne.  
4. Express lemon twist over drink.
