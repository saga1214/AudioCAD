I’ll summarize everything I said for a flu diagnosis, the saying corrected, doctor’s reasoning no antibiotic, resting, fluids, old sayings debunked, honey, steam, pillows, and now, specifically for your cough and brain fog.

The old saying is corrected to feed a *cold*, starve a *feuver*. However, the best advice for any illness including the flu is to listen to your body. You should drink plenty of fluids and eat what you can to keep your strength up. Your doctor didn’t prescribe an antibiotic which is normal because the flu is a virus and antibiotics only fight bacterial infections so they wouldn’t help you at all. I hope you feel better soon.

The most important thing is to get as much rest as you can to let your body fight the virus. Also over-the-counter medicines can help with the fever and body aches. For a stuffy nose or cough a steamy shower or a humidifier can provide some relief. Just focus on taking it easy for a few days.

Regarding a cough, a spoonful of honey either by itself or in warm tea can really help soothe a cough. You can also try gargling with warm salt water. At night propping your head up with an extra pillow can make it easier to rest.

And for your brain fog, I’d suggest staying hydrated, getting enough rest, and eating a balanced diet. You might also want to try gentle exercise like a short walk and reducing screen time. If it persists, it’s always a good idea to check with your doctor.
