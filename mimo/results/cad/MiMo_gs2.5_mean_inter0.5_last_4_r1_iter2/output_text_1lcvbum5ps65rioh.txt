### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia
1. **General Rule**: U-turns are allowed when you have a clear view of both ways (150m/500ft), no traffic signals, no signs prohibiting them, and it won’t interfere with other vehicles.
2. **Prohibited Locations**:  
   - Intersections with traffic lights.  
   - Near curves, hills, or tunnels.  
   - Where visibility is obstructed (e.g., after blind corners).  
   - On highways or restricted roads (like one-way streets).  
3. **Penalties**:  
   - **Tickets**: $160 + $15 processing fee (total ~$175).  
   - **Insurance Impact**: Points on your license and higher premiums.  
   - **Risky Situations**: If you cause an accident, fines and legal costs could rise significantly.  

**Tip**: Use designated U-turn lanes or intersections where permitted. If unsure, wait for a safe gap in traffic.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
Here’s a classic recipe (serves 4):  

**Pantry Staples**:  
- Spaghetti (400g/14 oz)  
- Black pepper (freshly ground)  
- Garlic (2 cloves, minced)  
- Pancetta or guanciale (200g/7 oz, diced)  
- Parmesan cheese (100g/3.5 oz, grated)  

**Essential**:  
- Eggs (3 large eggs + 1 extra yolk)  
- Butter (1 tbsp, for richness)  
- Salt (to taste)  

**Optional**:  
- Red pepper flakes (for heat)  
- Fresh parsley (chopped)  

---

### Key Carbonara Tips  
1. **Egg Trick**: Whisk eggs with pepper, then toss hot pasta into the mix off-heat (add butter too) to prevent scrambling.  
2. **Pancetta Substitute**: Use thick-cut bacon (not smoked).  
3. **No Pasta Water?**: Add a splash if the sauce is too thick.
