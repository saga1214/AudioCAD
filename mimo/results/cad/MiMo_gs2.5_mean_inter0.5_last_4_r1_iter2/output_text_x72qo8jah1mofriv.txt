Okay, kosher dietary laws mean no mixing meat and dairy. Since you're serving weenies (which are meat) and sweet options, if there's any dairy in the sweets, that would be a problem. So, for the sweet option, you should avoid anything with dairy. Themed sweets like dark chocolate-covered nuts are usually fine. For drinks, Pinot Noir is a red wine, which is generally okay with meat. Just make sure there are no dairy-based garnishes. You might also want to have some separate utensils for the meat and dairy items to be safe. That way, everyone is comfortable.
