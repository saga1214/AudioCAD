Yes, you can absolutely submit a complete, self-created manuscript for a children's book. In fact, that's a very common and highly valued path. Many illustrators and authors are the same person. You just need to be very clear about it in your application.

Here’s how you'd do it. When you send your query letter, you'll also include a separate document called a 'sample packet.' This packet would contain a few pages of your finished manuscript text, along with a few of your best illustrations. You'll need to clearly state in the letter that you are the author and illustrator and that the book is a complete project, ready to be illustrated by you.

Publishers do this because it saves them a lot of time and money. They can see the full vision and know exactly what they're getting. It's a very strong selling point.
