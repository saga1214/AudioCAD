You're right, I see the confusion. Let me clarify. The most widely recognized original Boulevardier recipe uses the one-to-one-to-one ratio, meaning one and a quarter ounces of whiskey, one ounce of vermouth, and one ounce of Campari. Your friend was following the traditional recipe. The equal-parts version, three-quarters of an ounce each of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime, is for a completely different drink called the Last Word. If you want a Boulevardier, just stick with that one-to-one-to-one Campari recipe. If you want a Last Word, then use the three-quarters of an ounce proportions. Either way, you'll have a great cocktail.
