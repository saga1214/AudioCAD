First, about the left-brain versus right-brain idea. It's a simplified concept, and neuroscience shows that the brain works in a more integrated way. However, it's not entirely a myth. The left hemisphere often specializes in language, logic, and analytical tasks, while the right hemisphere is more involved in spatial reasoning, creativity, and emotional processing. But it's not a strict split; they constantly interact.

Another is graphic design or data visualization. You can turn abstract information into beautiful, clear visuals. It’s creative, analytical, and has a lot of applications. 

There’s also engineering or product design. It blends creativity and logic to solve real-world problems. 

And if you like building things, maybe something in the tech industry, like UX/UI design or even coding, where you can create functional, creative systems. 

These fields all let you build something tangible while still allowing for creativity and measurement.
