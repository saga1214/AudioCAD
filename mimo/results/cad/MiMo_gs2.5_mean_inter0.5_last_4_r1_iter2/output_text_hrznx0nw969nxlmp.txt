Staying at Secret Harbour Beach Resort puts you right in paradise! Here are pressure-free, scenic, and fun daytime activities around Secret Harbour and Cruz Bay on St. John:

1.  **Snorkel Right from Your Bay:** The resort's calm, protected bay is shallow and clear. Grab a snorkel gear from Secret Harbour or nearby shops (like Cruz Bay Gear Shop) and explore the vibrant coral gardens and fish right off the sand. No boats or pressure changes needed!
2.  **Walk to Coki Beach:** Just a short walk north from Secret Harbour leads to Coki Beach. Another beautiful, calm bay perfect for swimming and snorkeling, with great snorkel shops and restaurants right there.
3.  Morgan's Mango for Lunch & Views:** Grab a legendary mango smoothie or fresh seafood lunch at Morgan's Mango in Cruz Bay. Enjoy the views of Secret Harbour and Reef Bay Falls.
4.  **Explore Reef Bay Falls:** Take the easy, paved trail from Cruz Bay (about 1.5 miles one way). The walk through the rainforest leads to the beautiful, cascading Reef Bay Falls. It's a refreshing and scenic escape.
5.  **Sunset Sail & Snorkel Cruise:** Book a catamaran cruise departing from Cruz Bay (like the Coral Bay Catamaran or other operators). These offer gentle, open-ocean sailing with calm seas, fantastic snorkeling spots (pressure changes only during the sail itself), and stunning views. Look for options that include lunch.
6.  **Explore Coral Bay:** Take a short ferry ride from Cruz Bay to Coral Bay. Wander the colorful streets, visit the historic Fort Christian, and explore the unique, rugged coastline with excellent snorkeling spots like Salt Whistle Bay and Great Lagoon.
7.  **Kayak Reef Bay:** Rent kayaks from Cruz Bay or Coral Bay. Paddle silently through the calm waters of Reef Bay, exploring its hidden coves and potential marine life – a peaceful and active way to see the bay.
8.  **Explore Cruz Bay Shops & Boutiques:** Cruise Bay has a charming collection of shops, art galleries, and boutiques. Great for browsing local crafts, books, and souvenirs. Also home to the popular Cruz Bay Beach Club for a more lively atmosphere with games and drinks.
9.  **Visit The Cinnamon Tree:** While known for dinner, The Cinnamon Tree (in Cruz Bay) is famous for its incredible brunches and weekend breakfasts. Worth the trip for a special meal.
10. **Hike to Virgin Islands National Park:** Take a guided tour or hike (with care) to the park's highlights like Waterlemon Cay (for birding!) or the beautiful beaches within the park boundaries like Trunk Bay (though access can be tricky without a boat).

**Key Tips for Your Boat Life:**
*   **Always Check Weather:** Boating and snorkeling depend on calm seas and clear skies.
*   **Hydrate & Sunscreen:** St. John is sunny! Reapply sunscreen frequently, especially when near the water.
*   **Snorkel Gear:** Rent from shops near Secret Harbour, Coki, or Cruz Bay for the best deals.
*   **Explore by Boat:** Your chartered boat is the ultimate way to see secluded coves, hidden bays, and marine life your brother can comfortably access. Point it out to your captain for specific pressure-free snorkel spots.

Have an amazing week sailing the Virgin Islands! The boat offers incredible freedom to find exactly what you both enjoy.
