Here's a consolidated list of my best recommendations for cutting sheet metal and handling drywall, along with where to buy them locally in Indianapolis:

### Cutting Sheet Metal:
1.  **Thinner Sheets Curves:** Aviation Snips  
    *Buy at:* The Home Depot, Lowe's, Tractor Supply Co., Ace Hardware  
2.  **Thick Metal / Power / Straight Lines:** Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel  
    *Buy at:* The Home Depot, Lowe's, Tractor Supply Co., Ace Hardware  
3.  **Thick Metal Curves / Fine Detail:** Jigsaw with *Metal-Cutting Blade*  
    *Buy at:* The Home Depot, Lowe's, Tractor Supply Co., Ace Hardware  

### Drywall Fasteners:
1.  **Best Overall (Holding Power):** 1-1/4" Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws  
    *Buy at:* The Home Depot, Lowe's, Tractor Supply Co. *(Look for brands like "Great Stuff" or "Simpson Strong-Tie")*  
2.  *(Alternative if Nails)* Ring-Shank Nails *(Less ideal, prone to pops)*  

### Insulation (Behind Drywall):
1.  **Best Accessible/Standard:** Fiberglass Bat Insulation (Pre-Cut Rolls)  
    *Buy at:* The Home Depot, Lowe's  
2.  *(Alternative for Soundproofing/Fire)*: Mineral Wool Insulation  
    *Buy at:* The Home Depot, Lowe's  
3.  *(Alternative for Seal/Air Tightness)*: Spray Foam Insulation  
    *Buy at:* The Home Depot, Lowe's  

### Where to Buy in Indianapolis (Best Selection):
*   **Menards:** (Big Box Store - Often has the best tool selection *and* insulation alongside home improvement)  
*   **The Home Depot / Lowe's:** (Extensive tool & fastener selection, good insulation)  
*   **Tractor Supply Co. / Ace Hardware:** (Good for aviation snips/grinders/jigs, basic fasteners & insulation)  

**Key Takeaway:** For the widest selection of tools (especially metal cutters) and insulation, **Menards** is your best local stop. The Home Depot and Lowe's are excellent alternatives, particularly for drywall screws and standard insulation.
