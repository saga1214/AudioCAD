For candy tomorrow, grab classic Halloween favorites like Reese’s Cups, Hershey’s Kisses, Skittles, gummy worms, and maybe some sour patch kids. Those are usually a crowd-pleaser. You could also throw in some classic Halloween-themed candies like mini chocolate bars shaped like ghosts or pumpkins. If you want to save space, pre-packaged Halloween candy bags or goodie bags can be easy and fun. Just try to avoid candy that's too niche or might be allergenic—most people will be happy with the basics.
