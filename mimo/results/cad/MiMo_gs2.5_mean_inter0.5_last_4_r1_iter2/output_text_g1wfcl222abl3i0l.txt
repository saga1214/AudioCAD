**For the Glue Gun Issue:**

*   **Check the Bulb:** Is it a standard 65W bulb? Sometimes cheaper ones are lower wattage. Try a known good 65W bulb.
*   **Check the Cord:** Is the cord frayed? Try plugging it into a different outlet.
*   **Is it Hot?** Even if it feels hot, sometimes the glue inside doesn't melt if the bulb isn't truly working. Let it cool down completely (15-20 mins) and try again. If it *still* doesn't work, it's likely the bulb.
*   **Is it Old?** Glue guns stop working reliably after a year or two. If it's old, it might need replacing.
*   **Low Temp Option:** If it's a low-temp glue gun and that's what you need, a regular hot glue gun *might* work, but low-temp is definitely safer for delicate decorations like glass.
