To start an edible garden in St. Pete, I'd recommend herbs like basil and mint, lettuce/spinach for greens, radishes for something quick, tomatoes and peppers for fruit, and maybe even zucchini for a classic summer crop. Herbs are great 'cause they grow fast and are super useful. Lettuce and spinach are also easy and fast. Radishes are ready in like, three weeks. Tomatoes and peppers are forgiving and give you a ton of fruit. And zucchini is a classic summer favorite. Happy gardening!
