   - **Why**: Language immersion (English-friendly but rich in Japanese culture), cat cafés, ancient bookshops, and surreal landscapes.

     - Take a **language exchange** via HelloTalk or meetups.  
   - **Travel**: Flights from major US airports (e.g., $600–$800 round-trip off-season).

### **Key Tips for Your Trip**  
- **Safety**:  
  - Use trusted platforms (Hostelworld, Airbnb) for bookings.  
  - Travel during daylight, avoid isolated areas, and share plans with someone.  
  - Carry a power bank and offline maps.  
- **Budget**:  
  - Travel off-season (e.g., Japan in winter, Portugal in summer).  
  - Use student discounts (Skyscanner, Hostelworld).  
- **Writer’s Block Fix**:  
  - Keep a notebook for small scenes (e.g., "A stranger offers a cat a bite of their sandwich").  
  - Observe interactions at cafés or markets—real life fuels relatable fiction!
