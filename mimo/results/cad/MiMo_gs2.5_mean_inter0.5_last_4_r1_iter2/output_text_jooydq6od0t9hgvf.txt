### **Vodka Mixer**  
**Classic Cucumber-Mint Cooler**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz vodka  
  - 4 oz fresh cucumber juice (blend ½ cucumber + 2 oz water, strain)  
  - 6 oz sparkling water  
  - 5 fresh mint leaves  
  - 1 oz lime juice  
  - *Garnish*: Cucumber ribbon + mint sprig  
- **Why it works**: Light, refreshing, and soda-free. Vegetarian/vegan-friendly.  

### **Argentine-Style Fernet** (if you mean *Fernet-Branca* or similar)  
**Fernet-Branca "Mate"**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca (or Argentine-style herbal liqueur)  
  - 1.5 oz cold-brew iced tea (black tea)  
  - 1 oz dry sparkling wine or cider (soda allergy-safe)  
  - *Garnish*: Lemon twist  
- **Why it works**: Balances Fernet’s bitterness with tea’s smoothness. Serve chilled.  

### **Key Notes**  
1. **Soda Allergy**: Both recipes use sparkling water or cider (no carbonated sodas).  
2. **Formality**: Use chilled glassware and garnishes for elegance.  
3. **Omnivore/Veg Note**: Vodka is neutral—mixers work for all diets.
