Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! Here are a few top-rated ones:

First, there's Tokyo Joe's in Little Tokyo, known for their ramen. They have a rich, creamy broth and tender pork.

Next, if you're into pho, Pho 65 on North Oak Cliff is fantastic. Their broth is super flavorful and their noodles are fresh.

And for a more upscale experience, there's Miso Bar in Deep Ellum, which has great miso ramen and other Asian-inspired dishes.

As for protein bars, if you want something tasty and filling, I'd suggest Quest Bars—they're high in protein and have good macros. Or maybe RXBARs, which are simple ingredients and sweetened with dates. And of course, there's the classic Clif Bars, which are more natural and have some good protein.

Hope you enjoy your meal and snacks!
