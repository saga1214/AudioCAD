Alright, after all that, here’s a clear plan for three gifts under forty-five dollars—one for each of their hobbies, and all staying eco-friendly.

First, for hiking: an eco-water bottle. You could find a stainless steel one on Amazon or at REI for around twenty-five dollars.

Second, for knitting: the organic or recycled yarn. Brands like Lion Brand or Patons have eco-friendly options at craft stores or online for about fifteen dollars.

And third, for surfing: the home decor item. A piece of art or a hook made from recycled materials, maybe from Etsy or a local artist, should run you about ten dollars.

This mix gives you a practical item, something handmade, and a decorative touch. Let me know if you’d like any other ideas! Happy birthday to your friend!
