**From Wilmington, NC to Raleigh, NC by Car**

**Artsy Stops in Raleigh, NC**
1.  **North Carolina Museum of Art (NCMA):** (Highly Recommended) The premier art museum in the state. Features a vast collection of American art, European art, contemporary works, and a stunning outdoor sculpture park.
2.  **CAM Raleigh:** (Contemporary American Art Museum) Focuses on contemporary American art, with rotating exhibitions.
3.  **Artspace Downtown:** Offers artist studios, galleries, and performance spaces showcasing local artists.

**Raleigh Dining (Mid-Range & BBQ)**
1.  **The Pit:** Famous for its large, family-style smothered pork chop plates. Casual BBQ spot.
2.  **Clyde Cooper's Barbeque:** Known for its smoked meats, ribs, and mac & cheese. Offers a classic BBQ experience.

**Hair Product Stores in Raleigh (Type 4a Hair)**
