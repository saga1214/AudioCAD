   - Get a **JR Pass** (7-day option) for unlimited Shinkansen/trains between cities.

2. **Time Management**:  
   - Start sightseeing by 9 AM (avoid crowds).  
   - Workouts: Early morning (7–8 AM) or late evening (8–9 PM) to avoid disrupting family.

4. **Hotel Communication**:
