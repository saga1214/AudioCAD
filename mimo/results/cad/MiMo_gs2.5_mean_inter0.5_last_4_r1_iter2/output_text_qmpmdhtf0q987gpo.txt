Okay, you've done a lot of the prep work already. After sanding, the next crucial step is to give the cabinet boxes a good wipe down with a tack cloth. This gets rid of all the fine dust from the sanding, which is super important because that primer won't stick well if there's dust on it.

Then, before you start priming, you should definitely test your color. Do a small patch of primer on the cabinet, let it dry, and then paint over it with the off-white. This is the best way to be sure the color will actually cover the black and give you that 'wow' factor you want. Once you're happy with the test, you can go ahead and prime the whole cabinet.
