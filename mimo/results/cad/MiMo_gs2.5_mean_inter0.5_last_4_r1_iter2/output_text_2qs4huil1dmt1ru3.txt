Okay, breaking it down task by task. Drywall stuff can vary, but joint compound, primer, paint for a small spot, nails/adhesive and a replacement piece should probably run you around 20 to 35 dollars. Cake supplies depend on the recipe, but flour, sugar, butter, eggs, milk, extract and pans for a standard cake are usually around 15 to 25 dollars. The frosting items and decorations for the party are a smaller add-on, maybe 5 to 15 dollars. So, you’re probably looking at a range of 40 to 75 dollars total. I’d say bring around 50 to 60 dollars just to be safe.
