For your end-of-year celebration, a **hybrid approach** balances fun, inclusivity, and practicality—especially while pregnant! Here’s a tailored plan:

### 1. **Venue & Weather Plan**
   - **Rainy Day Option**: Host in the cafeteria (low effort, accessible). Use a projector/screen for a Brownie-themed slideshow or movie.
   - **Outdoor Celebration**: If possible, rent a pavilion at the park (check availability early). Have a **backup tent plan** for light rain.  
   → *Why?* Parks are cost-effective and let kids run around, but prioritize safety and logistics.

### 2. **Food Strategy**
   - **Dietary Inclusivity**:  
     - **Pizza**: Works for most. Offer a **dairy-free cheese option** for you and others.  
     - **Ice Cream/Sundaes**: Set up a "DIY Sundae Bar" with non-dairy ice cream (e.g., soy or coconut-based) and toppings.  
     - **Snack Mix**: Include gluten-free and nut-free options for allergies.  
   - **Parent Involvement**: Assign 2-3 parents to bring food (e.g., "Pizza Day" or "Sundae Day"). Rotate so no one feels burdened.

### 3. **Activities to Earn Badges**
   - **Quick Badge-Builders**:  
     - **Community Helper Badge**: Park clean-up (eco-badges) or helping at a food drive (service badges).  
     - **Crafty Mini-Workshops**: 15-minute badge-earning crafts (e.g., friendship bracelets for the "Friendship" badge).  
   - **Fun Games**:  
     - **Three-Legged Race** (if space allows)  
     - **Scavenger Hunt** (find items like "something green" or "a rock")  
     - **Pin the Tail on the Sash** (fun twist for the end-of-year theme)

### 4. **Parent Participation**
   - **Clear Roles**:  
     - **Snack Buddies**: Parents sign up weekly to bring snacks (e.g., "Dip Week," "Cookies Week").  
     - **Dues Fund**: Charge a **small annual fee** ($10–$20) covering badge patches, field trip supplies, and event materials.  
     - **Event Helpers**: Parents can volunteer for field trips/camping—offer incentives like "Troop Helper" badges.

### 5. **Sample Celebration Flow**
   - **1–2 PM**: Arrival + Sundae Bar (parent-brought)  
   - **2–3 PM**: Scavenger Hunt + Badge Crafting  
   - **3–4 PM**: Movie/Slideshow in cafeteria (if rain) or outdoor games  
   - **4–5 PM**: Sash ceremony + cake (parent-brought)  

### Key Tips:
- **Low-Pressure Camping**: Start with a **half-day trip** to a nearby campsite with pre-built cabins. Focus on s’mores and storytelling—avoid heavy hiking.  
- **Cookie Sale Integration**: Host a troop bake sale at school during the celebration to boost cookie sales goals.  
- **Your Well-being**: Delegate badge-earning tasks early—parents often love contributing!  

> 💡 **Bottom Line**: A **park BBQ with a rain plan** + **parent-buddy snack system** keeps it joyful and manageable. Focus on simple badge activities that let the girls shine—your job is to guide, not overdo it! You’ll be an amazing troop leader. 🍫✨
