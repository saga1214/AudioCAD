### 📁 For Paper Clutter:
1. **Go Digital First**: Scan important documents (receipts, contracts, notes) and store them in cloud folders.  
2. **The "One In, One Out" Rule**: For every new paper you bring in, remove one old one.  
3. **File Ruthlessly**: Keep only essentials in physical files (e.g., tax docs, warranties). Use labeled folders and a vertical file organizer.  

### 🔌 For Cable Chaos:
1. **Cable Management Kits**: Use velcro ties, clips, or under-desk trays to bundle cords.  
2. **Label Everything**: A small label maker (or sticky notes) prevents confusion.  
3. **Hide & Simplify**: Use a cable box to tuck everything behind your monitor or desk.
