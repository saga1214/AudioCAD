Here's a quick recap of your analogy for a child:

1.  **Heart:** The **gas station and engine**. It pumps the blood-cars all around your body.
2.  **Lungs:** Another **gas station**. The blood-cars stop here to get loaded up with fresh oxygen "fuel".
3.  **Arteries:** The **big highways**. Carry blood *away* from the heart towards your whole body.
4.  **Capillaries:** The **tiny streets**. Get super close to every house (cell) so the oxygen fuel can be dropped off.
5.  **Veins:** The **return roads**. Carry the blood-cars *back* to the heart and lungs to get refueled.
6.  **Kidneys:** **Car washes and checkpoints**. They filter the blood-cars, take out the bad stuff (like waste and extra water), and make the blood clean again.
7.  **Liver:** **A service station**. It sorts out the good stuff (like nutrients from food) and breaks down any harmful things found in the blood.
