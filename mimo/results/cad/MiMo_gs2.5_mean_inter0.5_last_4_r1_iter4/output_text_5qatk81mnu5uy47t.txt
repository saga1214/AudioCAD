### 🎯 Scuba Gear Recommendation
*   **For Beginners:** **Start with Short Fins.** They're easier to learn on, compact for travel, and less tiring for short dives. You can upgrade to long fins later if needed.
*   **For Strong Currents:** **Invest in Long Fins.** Essential for powerful propulsion in places like Cocos Island.
*   **Best Solution:** Pack **both sets!** Short fins for exploration/learning, long fins for specific adventures. Many rental shops also offer them.

### 🌟 Top Costa Rican Dive Sites (Tropical Paradise!)
1.  **Cocos Island (Pacific Side - Requires Boat Dive):**
    *   **Why:** World-famous for **big marine life** (sharks, rays, turtles, dolphins), dramatic cliffs, and strong currents.
    *   **Best For:** Experienced divers (long fins recommended here!), adventure, incredible wildlife.
    *   **Wildlife:** Great White Sharks, Hammerheads, Turtles, Dolphins, Sea Lions, Manta Rays.
    *   **Tip:** Requires a liveaboard dive boat (2-3 day trip). Book well in advance!

2.  **Tortuguero National Park (Caribbean Side - Boat/Dive Access):**
    *   **Why:** Unique **jungle-in-water** experience! Accessible only by boat/airplane/footbridge. Focus on **reefs, cays, and mangroves** teeming with life.
    *   **Best For:** Wildlife lovers, beginners (short fins ideal), exploring caves/coves.
    *   **Wildlife:** Turtles (nesting season!), Fish, Sharks, Dolphins, Manatees, Sea Turtles.
    *   **Tip:** Stay in a eco-lodge. Boat dives from Tortuguero are fantastic.

3.  **Cahuita National Park (Caribbean Coast - Beach Access):**
    *   **Why:** **Easy shore dives** right from the beach! Beautiful coral reefs, snorkeling, and diving.
    *   **Best For:** Beginners, families, quick dives before/after other adventures. Great for learning.
    *   **Wildlife:** Turtles (often!), Fish, rays, lobsters.
    *   **Tip:** Accessible by car/bus. Perfect for a base.

4.  **Playa Conchal & Santa Teresa (Pacific Coast - Beach/Boat Dives):**
    *   **Why:** Close to popular beaches. Good shore diving spots and boat trips to nearby islands (like Santa Rosa).
    *   **Best For:** Beach divers, combining land & sea adventures, good visibility.
    *   **Wildlife:** Turtles, fish, rays, occasional sharks.
    *   **Tip:** More developed area than Cocos/Tortuguero, good for relaxation too.

### 📋 Dream Itinerary Outline (Adjust based on trip length)
*   **Days 1-3:** Arrive in San José. Explore the city, visit coffee plantations. Fly to **Tortuguero**.
*   **Days 4-6:** Explore **Tortuguero National Park**. Take boat tours to dive sites, hike jungle trails. Perfect for short fin dives.
*   **Days 7-9:** Fly to **Manuel Antonio** or **Aguas Calientes**. Explore rainforests & beaches. Drive to **Cahuita** for easy shore dives.
*   **Days 10-12:** Fly to **Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic** (easy flight) or back to San José. Take a **liveaboard dive boat to Cocos Island** (2-3 day trip).
*   **Days 13-14:** Return from Cocos Island. Fly home.

### 🎒 Essential Tips for Your Trip
1.  **Book Early:** Especially for Cocos Island liveaboards, Tortuguero lodges, and flights between islands.
2.  **Dive Shops:** Research reputable shops near your dive sites. Get PADI/SSI certified if not already.
3.  **Water Temperature:** Expect warm water (26-30°C / 79-86°F) year-round. A rash guard and mask strap are essential.
4.  **Wildlife:** Costa Rica is famous for its biodiversity. Respect marine life (don't touch turtles/sharks!).
5.  **Sun Protection:** Reef-safe sunscreen, hat, and sunglasses are a must. UV is strong!
6.  **Travel:** Rent a car for flexibility on the mainland (Pacific/Caribbean). Book internal flights if needed.
7.  **Packing:** Wetsuit (if cold current dives planned), dive computer, tank, fins, snorkel, mask, fins, rash guard, towel, reef-safe sunscreen, water shoes, camera.
