Alright, here is a nautical-themed nautical birthday shopping list for seven seven-year-old pirates meeting at the beach. The goal is sand-free finger foods and drinks, plus avoiding watermelon for one child.

First, for the main course, I suggest mini pirate ship sliders. You'll need about 12 slider buns, 2 cups of cheese spread, 8 ounces of ham or turkey cut into small cubes, and 8 ounces of cheddar cheese that can be shredded or cubed. Also, grab a jar of pickles for relish to make it look like cannonball residue.

Next, for “buried treasure” fruit skewers, you'll need about 12 cups of grapes, one 2-pound container of pineapple chunks, one pound of watermelon—just kidding, skip that for the allergy—and about 12 apples, sliced into wedges. You can also get some strawberries or kiwi if you want extra fruit jewels.

For a snack course, let's do themed goldfish crackers and treasure chests. You'll need a 16-ounce box of goldfish crackers and maybe some themed crackers you can find at the store. You'll also need some sealed treasure chest snack bags to put the goldfish in. For a veggie course, get a 12-ounce container of cucumber sticks and a 12-ounce container of cheese cubes. You can label these “pirate jewels” or “sea treasure.”

For drinks, individual juice boxes are perfect. You can get about 24 small boxes of assorted flavors. For a non-watermelon punch option, you could make a batch of fruit punch and serve it in individual cups. For water, get six individual bottles for the guest of honor and juice boxes for the other kids.

For dessert, besides your cake, how about cookie “anchors”? You can make sugar cookies with an anchor icing design. Also, some“jewel” fruit leather shapes cut into pirate eyes or stars.

For packaging, you’ll want some small treasure chests to hold the sandwiches, and some sealed snack bags for the fruit and crackers to keep everything sand-free.
