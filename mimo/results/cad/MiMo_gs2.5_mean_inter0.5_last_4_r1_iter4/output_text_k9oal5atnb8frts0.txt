Of course. For a Retro Tech lunch theme, you could do a simple “Cereal Bowl” meal, inspired by the old computer mouse and keyboard shapes. Just serve a bowl of cereal with a spoon. For snacks, you could have pretzel sticks to look like old computer cables, and maybe some raisin “dots” for the old Microsoft Paint dots. Another idea is to make a “Pizza Slice” meal, where the slice looks like a floppy disk, served with a side of carrot sticks to look like old computer ports. These are easy and tie right into the theme.
