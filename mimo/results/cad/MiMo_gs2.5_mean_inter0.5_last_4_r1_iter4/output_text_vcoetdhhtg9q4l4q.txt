### 🥚 Quick & Minimal Ingredients:
1. **Eggs** (scrambled, boiled, or in omelets) + **1-2 seasonal veggies** (spinach, cherry tomatoes, or frozen peas).  
2. **Canned beans** (black beans, chickpeas) + **canned tomatoes** + **aromatics** (garlic, onion). *Example: Black bean tacos or chili.*  
3. **Frozen veggies** (broccoli, peppers) + **grains** (rice, quinoa) + **sauce** (soy, peanut, or curry paste).  
4. **Avocado** + **canned fish** (salmon, tuna) + **lemon juice**. *No cooking needed!*  
5. **Oats** (overnight oats with yogurt + berries) or **instant noodles** (simplified your kitchen clutter!).  

### 🔄 Key Principles:
- **Stick to 3-4 base ingredients per meal** (e.g., protein + veg + grain/sauce).  
- **Use pantry staples** (olive oil, salt, pepper, vinegar) instead of niche spices/gadgets.  
- **Batch-cook grains/beans** once a week for easy assembly.
