Okay, let's go back to the cabinets. Since you've already cleaned, degreased, light-sand, and set up a drop cloth to protect your counters, you're pretty much ready for the primer.

Before you apply the primer, I'd recommend you test your color. Paint a small piece of the door, let it dry, and then open it under different lighting in your kitchen. That will give you a really good idea of what the off-white will actually look like in your space.

Then, just apply your primer. Make sure to use a good quality brush or roller and apply it in smooth, even strokes. A good tip is to apply it in thin coats rather than one thick one, as it will give you a much better finish.
