Of course! Here are five fantastic road trip options from Vancouver that are perfect for your family, new baby, and soon-to-be four-legged friend.

First, there's the classic Vancouver-to-Toronto route. You'd drive through the US and see the beautiful Blue Ridge Mountains. A must-see is the Shenandoah National Park and the famous Skyline Drive. For food, you could try a famous Philly cheesesteak in Reading, Pennsylvania.

Second, you could head south to the San Francisco Bay Area. You'd drive through the stunning Redwood National Park. In San Francisco, you could go to Fisherman's Wharf, see the Alcatraz Island tour, and eat some fresh clam chowder in a sourdough bowl.

Third, for a trip to the mountains, you could go to Banff National Park in Alberta. The drive is through the Columbia Icefield, and the scenery is breathtaking, especially the turquoise lakes like Lake Louise. For activities, you could go on a scenic drive, see the Icefields Parkway, and maybe even hike or take a boat tour on Moraine Lake.

Fourth, if you want a coastal trip, you could drive to Victoria on Vancouver Island. You'd take a scenic ferry across the Strait of Georgia. In Victoria, you can explore the city's beautiful gardens, like the Butchart Gardens, and visit the famous Empress Hotel for a fancy afternoon tea.

And fifth, for a trip to the desert, you could drive to the Mojave National Preserve in California. You'd go through the Mojave Desert and see the stark beauty of Death Valley. There, you can explore the Badwater Basin, which is the lowest point in North America, and see the famous sand dunes at Mesquite Flat.

I hope you enjoy your adventures!
