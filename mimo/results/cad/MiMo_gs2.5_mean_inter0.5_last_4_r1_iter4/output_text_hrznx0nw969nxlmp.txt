Based on your boat trip vibe and brother's needs, here are fantastic pressure-free daytime activities around Secret Harbour Beach Resort or the Cruz Bay area on St. Thomas:

1.  **Snorkel Coki Beach:** Just a short walk from Secret Harbour, Coki Beach is a top spot. Its shallow, protected bay (only 3-4 feet deep near the shore) has incredible coral gardens teeming with fish. Perfect for your brother! Reserve a snorkel tour or simply paddle out from the beach.
2.  **Beachcombing at Secret Harbour:** Explore the rocky shore behind Secret Harbour. You'll find unique Virgin Islands treasures like sea glass (especially near Cruz Bay), interesting shells, and smooth stones. The tide pools here are also great for spotting small crabs and fish.
3.  **Kayak or Paddleboard in Coki Bay:** Rent kayaks or paddleboards from Coki Beach. Paddle through the calm, mangrove-lined channels of Coki Bay, discovering hidden coves and unique wildlife. The effort is minimal and the scenery is stunning.
4.  **Visit Cruz Bay Shops & Stroll:** Cruise Bay has a charming collection of shops, art galleries, and cafes right along the waterfront. Browse for local crafts, grab a snack, and enjoy the lively atmosphere without needing a car.
5.  **Explore the Cruz Bay Petroglyphs:** A short walk from the Secret Harbour area, these ancient rock carvings (petroglyphs) are a fascinating glimpse into the island's history. Easy walking trail.
6.  **Hike to Trunk Bay:** While a moderate hike, Trunk Bay is one of the most beautiful beaches on St. John. Its famous underwater snorkeling trail (shallow entry sections available) is world-class. Bring plenty of water and check trail conditions.
7.  **Visit the St. John Historical Society:** Located in Cruz Bay, this small museum offers fascinating insights into the island's ecology, history, and culture. A great educational stop.
8.  **Relax & Read on Secret Harbour Bay:** Simply soak up the sun, swim in the calm, shallow water, and enjoy the beautiful bay. Sometimes the best activity is just being on the beach!

**Key Considerations:**
*   **Shallow First:** Always prioritize activities with shallow entry points for your brother.
*   **Sun Protection:** Virgin Islands sun is strong. Reapply sunscreen, wear hats, and seek shade when needed.
*   **Hydration:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
*   **Boat Trips:** If you charter your boat, consider exploring nearby islands like Waterlemon Cay (famous for snorkeling) or a calm sail to the Baths on Virgin Gorda (very shallow entry).
