That's the spirit! Since you've got a coffee table ready and dislike overly sweet snacks, here are some savory and balanced vending machine ideas to fill it up:

### 🧀 **Savory Staples:**
- **Cheese Blocks:** A wedge of cheddar, mozzarella, or gouda (cut into slices).  
- **Nut Packs:** Almonds, walnuts, or mixed nuts (great for crunch).  
- **Veggie Sticks:** Carrots, celery, or bell peppers (dip them in hummus or ranch).  
- **Crackers:** Whole-grain or seed crackers (pair with cheese or nut butter).  

### 🍟 **Fun & Crunchy:**
- **Pretzels:** Mini pretzels or twists (classic salty-savory).  
- **Beet Chips:** A healthier twist (sweet but not sugary).  
- **Samosas:** If available (spiced, savory, and handheld).  
- **Popcorn:** Lightly salted (air-popped or microwave-style).  

### 🧂 **Small Bites & Snacks:**
- **Beef Jerky:** Protein-packed and savory.  
- **Olives or Pickles:** For tangy contrast.  
- **Sunflower Seeds:** Easy, no-mess snack.  
- **Seaweed Snacks:** Light, salty, and crispy.  

### 🍟 **Sweet with a Twist (if you indulge):**
- **Dark Chocolate Bars:** 70%+ cacao (bitter, not sugary).  
- **Caramel Pretzel Clusters:** Sweet-salty combo.  
- **Rice Krispie Treats:** Minis (mild sweetness, not overpowering).  

### 💡 **Tips for Display:**  
- **Tiered Setup:** Use small bowls or containers for dips (hummus, mustard) and veggies.  
- **Mix Textures:** Combine soft cheese, crunchy nuts, and chewy jerky.  
- **Keep it Fresh:** Store perishables (veggies, cheese) in a small container with a paper towel to absorb moisture.
