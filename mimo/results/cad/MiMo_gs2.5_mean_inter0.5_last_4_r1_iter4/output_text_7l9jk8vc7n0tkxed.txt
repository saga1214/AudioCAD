Okay, let's tackle this step-by-step. Stay calm and methodical – you've got the skills. Here's how to approach a severe deep clog with just your simple toolbox:

**Step 1: Visual Inspection**
*   **Check Under the Sink:** Look for signs of standing water, leaks, or debris *before* the drain. This helps pinpoint the blockage location (e.g., sink drain vs. main line).
*   **Check the Drain Pipe:** If possible, look into the drain opening. Is it completely filled? Are there visible chunks? This tells you how deep the blockage likely is.

**Step 2: Manual Dislodging (Using Hands)**
*   **Wear Gloves:** Protect your hands.
*   **Get Your Hands Wet:** Use a bucket or wet rag.
*   **Work the Plunger Again:** Do several strong plunges to loosen debris.
*   **Reach In:** Carefully insert your gloved hand into the drain opening. Feel around. Try to:
    *   Grab and pull out any visible debris you can reach.
    *   Use your fingers to manually push or dislodge the blockage further down if you feel resistance.

**Step 3: Hot Water Flush (If Safe)**
*   **Check for Leaks:** Ensure the area under the sink is secure.
*   **Pour Hot Water:** Fill a bucket with *very hot* water (not boiling, as it might crack pipes) and pour it down the drain. Repeat several times. The heat can soften the blockage.

**Step 4: Baking Soda & Vinegar (If You Have Them)**
*   **Pour Baking Soda:** Down the drain.
*   **Pour Vinegar:** Down the drain after the baking soda. It will fizz.
*   **Wait:** Let it sit for 15-30 minutes. This helps break down grease or organic matter.
*   **Flush:** Pour the hot water from Step 3 down the drain to rinse.

**Step 5: Drain Snake (Using Basic Tools)**
*   **Find a Wire Hanger:** Bend the hook end into a hook or a simple plier.
*   **Create a Snake:** Use your pliers to bend the hook end of the wire hanger into a simple hook or a short, stiff wire loop.
*   **Insert & Hook:** Carefully insert the hooked wire into the drain opening. Try to hook any debris you feel.
*   **Pull & Retrieve:** If you hook something, try to pull it out slowly and carefully.

**Step 6: Last Resort - Plunge & Hot Water Combo**
*   If the other steps fail, go back to the plunger.
*   Do multiple strong plunges.
*   Immediately pour a bucket of *very hot* water down the drain after each plunge. Repeat this cycle 5-10 times. The heat and pressure combo can sometimes work wonders on deep clogs.

**Critical Reminders:**

*   **Patience is Key:** Deep blockages take time. Don't force tools or rush.
*   **Avoid Chemical Cleaners:** With just your toolbox, you don't have safe, effective chemical drain cleaners. Stick to manual methods.
*   **Don't Damage Pipes:** Avoid using excessive force with pliers or wrenches on pipes unless absolutely necessary and you know what you're doing. Focus on dislodging the *blockage*, not the pipe.
*   **Call for Backup if Needed:** If you've tried all these methods and it's still completely blocked, it *might* be a main line issue. It's okay to call another plumber with better tools – you've done your best with what you had today.
