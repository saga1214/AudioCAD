I totally get it, flus make everything feel foggy. Let me break down the advice for you, and I'll also touch on that brain fog.

First, for the flu itself: drink plenty of fluids and eat what you can. Antibiotics won't help, that saying about feeding or starving is about comfort, not medicine, and your doctor was right. Rest is also key.

Now, for the cough specifically, here are a few things that can help: A spoonful of honey, gargling with warm salt water, and propping your head up with an extra pillow at night.

And for that brain fog... the main thing is hydration. Drink lots of fluids. Rest is also super important, because your brain needs energy to heal. You could also try some light stretching or a short walk when you feel up to it, as gentle movement can help. Just take it easy.
