#### **MAINS:**
3. **Miso-Glazed Eggplant with Kimchi Dipping Sauce**

4. Japanese-Style **Spicy Tuna & Avocado Bites**

   - *Unique*: Add **fish roe (ikura)** or **wasabi-marinated shrimp**.

6. **Tamarind-Spiced Nuts**
