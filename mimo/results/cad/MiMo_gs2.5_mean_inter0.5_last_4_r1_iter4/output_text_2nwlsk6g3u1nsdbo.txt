     - 2 tbsp olive oil  
     - 1 tbsp red pepper flakes  
     - 1 tsp sugar (balances acidity)

     - 1 cup heavy cream (for richness)  

### 3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**
   - **Ingredients**:
