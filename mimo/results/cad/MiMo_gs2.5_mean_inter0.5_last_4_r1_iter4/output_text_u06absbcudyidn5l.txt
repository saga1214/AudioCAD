Okay, let's break this down. Chronic redness has many possible causes. Besides rosacea and sun damage, other common ones include things like contact dermatitis, which is a reaction to something you touch, atopic dermatitis, also known as eczema, or even heat rash.

Now, about vasculitis. It's an autoimmune disorder, which means your immune system mistakenly attacks your own blood vessels. The size of the vessel that's inflamed determines the type. For example, large vessel vasculitis might affect big arteries, medium vessel types often affect small and medium arteries and veins, and small vessel types affect the tiniest capillaries and arterioles.

This inflammation can lead to a wide range of symptoms depending on which vessels are involved. Common ones include fever, fatigue, and rashes. Treatment usually involves immunosuppressants to calm the immune response.
