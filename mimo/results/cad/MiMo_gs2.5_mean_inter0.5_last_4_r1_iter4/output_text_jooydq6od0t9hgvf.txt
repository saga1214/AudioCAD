No problem! Here are quick, elegant vodka and mate recipes that fit your party’s needs—vegetarian-friendly, soda-free, and formal:

### 🥛 **Vodka Cocktail (Quick & Sophisticated)**
**"Spicy Citrus Vodka"**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz vodka  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 0.5 oz honey syrup (equal parts honey + hot water, cooled)  
  - 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - *Garnish*: Lemon twist  
- **Method**: Shake with ice, strain into a chilled coupe glass. Express lemon twist oil over drink.  
- **Why it works**: Refreshing, balanced, and uses minimal prep. Omnis and vegetarians both enjoy it.  

### 🌿 **Argentine Mate (Non-Alcoholic & Social)**
**Traditional Mate Setup**:  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - Loose-leaf yerba mate tea (or pre-steeped mate leaves)  
  - Hot water (195–205°F / 90–96°C)  
  - *Garnish*: Citrus slice or mint  
- **Method**:  
  1. Fill a *mate* gourd ⅔ full with yerba.  
  2. Pour hot water over leaves, then pour out immediately (steeping releases flavor).  
  3. Serve 6–8 oz in small glasses with a metal straw.  
- **Why it works**: A cultural, communal drink that’s naturally soda-free. Older guests may appreciate tradition; younger guests enjoy the ritual.  

### 🍸 **Bonus: Soda-Free Mocktail (For All)**
**Gin-Infused Sparkler**:  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz gin (or vodka for neutral taste)  
  - 0.5 oz fresh grapefruit juice  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
  - 2 oz sparkling water  
  - *Garnish*: Grapefruit wheel  
- **Method**: Shake gin, juices, and ice. Strain into a glass, top with sparkling water.
