Okay, so you and girlfriend are planning this West Coast to Nevada adventure by van—Joshua Tree into the desert, maybe some free BLM camping, and ending with a Vegas reset. That’s a killer road trip loop. If you want a "proper" Vegas experience after all that rough-and-tumble desert time, you’re right: a nice hotel and a hot shower are non-negotiable. But Vegas doesn’t have to be all glitz and gambling. Here’s how to tailor it for a couple fresh off an adventure:  

First, skip the mega-resorts if you’re budget-conscious or just want something quieter. Smaller boutique hotels like the **El Cortile** (historic, right on the Strip) or **The LINQ** (affordable, great pool) offer comfort without the crowds. For mid-range, **Excalibur** or **Luxor** have good amenities for under $150/night.  

For showers and relaxation, prioritize hotels with private bathrooms—no shared facilities. And if you’re *really* craving luxury, **Wynn** or **Encore** have insane spas (book a couples’ massage) and heated pools, but expect $300+/night.  

Beyond showers, make it a sensory reset:  
- **Food:** Hit a non-gambling spot like **Eataly** (Italian market/restaurant) or **Honey’s** (casual diner with great breakfast). Avoid overpriced Strip buffets.  
- **Vibe:** Walk the ** Fremont Street Experience** at night for free light shows and street performers—it’s old-school Vegas without the resort crowds.  
- **Quiet:** Rent a **Tesla** or **Lamborghini** for a few hours to cruise the Strip at sunset (it’s surprisingly cheap for a day).  

Key tip: Book your hotel *before* arriving in Vegas. Weekends and holidays can spike prices. And pack swimwear—you’ll want to soak in a pool after dusty desert hikes.  

This combo—desert adventure + free camping + Vegas indulgence—makes for a killer new relationship trip. Hope Joshua Tree lives up to the hype!
