Okay, so diesel camper van pre-trip maintenance. Brakes first. For wear, look for soft or spongy pedal feel, which could mean air in the system. Also listen for grinding or squealing noises when you brake. Check the brake pads themselves—they should have about 3/32 of an inch of thickness left; if it’s less, you need new ones. Also check the rotors for any warping or deep grooves. For spark plugs, they’re a bit different with diesel engines. They’re more durable but still need checking. You’d want to pull one out and look at the electrode gap—it should be about 0.030 to 0.034 inches, but always check the manual. Also, look for any oil contamination or carbon buildup. If you see oil, that’s a big problem, and you should probably have them inspected. If you’re not comfortable doing it yourself, a mechanic can do it for you. And don’t forget to check the diesel filter and the fuel system.
