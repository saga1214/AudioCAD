To summarize all the ingredient prices for four family dinners: The chicken cutlates require two pounds of chicken for eight to fifteen dollars; all-purpose flour for three to five dollars; twelve eggs for three to seven dollars; breadcrumbs for two to four dollars; vegetable oil for four to eight dollars; and salt and pepper for three to six dollars.

For the Garlic Mashed Potatoes, you'll need about five pounds of potatoes for three to six dollars; a head of garlic for one dollar; a pound of butter for four to seven dollars; and a half-gallon of milk for three to five dollars.
