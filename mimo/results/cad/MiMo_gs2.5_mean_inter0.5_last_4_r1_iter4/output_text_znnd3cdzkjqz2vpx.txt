Okay, you've got the essentials, but there are a few more things you'll probably need. Definitely grab some shims – those thin plastic or wood wedges are crucial for leveling the base cabinets under uneven floors. You'll also need a drill with the right screws for drywall and studs, and a stud finder with an electric mode to easily find the studs again. If Habitat included them, make sure to have any included hardware like brackets handy. A measuring tape is also key for final adjustments. Most importantly, please have your friends wear work gloves to protect their hands when handling the heavy cabinets. You've got a great plan, just double-check that you have a good grip on all the tools.
