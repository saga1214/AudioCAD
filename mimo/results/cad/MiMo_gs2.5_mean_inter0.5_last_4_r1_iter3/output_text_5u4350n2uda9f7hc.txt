You've raised a very important point—since you're carrying plants up five flights of stairs without an elevator, **lightweight and compact plants are essential**. Here’s a breakdown of the options we discussed, focusing on weight and suitability for your space:

### 1. **Succulents** (Perfect for Your Table)
   - **Weight**: Typically **very light**—most small succulents (e.g., Haworthia, Echeveria mini, or Gasteria) weigh **under 1–2 pounds** when potted. You can easily carry them in your hands.
   - **Space**: Ideal for tabletops—they’re low-profile and come in tiny pots. Look for compact varieties like:
     - **Haworthia cooperi**: Rosette-shaped, silver-green leaves.
     - **Haworthia fasciata**: Striped leaves, "spider plant" of succulents.
     - **Sedum dasyphyllum**: Tiny, succulent leaves with silver edges.
   - **City/Wind Tolerance**: Thrives in bright morning sun and handles NYC wind well due to sturdy stems.

### 2. **Bird’s Nest Snake Plant (Sansevieria trifasciata 'Bird’s Nest')**
   - **Weight**: Moderate—plants are **3–5 pounds** when potted. This is heavy for daily transport, but manageable for **one trip** when moving in/out.  
     *(Tip: Choose a small pot to minimize weight—e.g., a 4-inch nursery pot.)*
   - **Appearance & Placement**:  
     - **Leaves**: Wide, triangular, vertical green leaves with silver streaks (no wavy edges). Resembles a "mini palm" with a unique nest-like shape.  
     - **Placement**: Best on the **balcony floor** (sturdy and wind-resistant). Avoid the table unless using an extremely lightweight version.
   - **City/Wind Tolerance**: Extremely hardy—ignores wind, neglect, and low light. Perfect for NYC conditions.

### Key Considerations Before Buying:
- **Dwarf Varieties**: Prioritize **small-format plants** labeled "mini," "compact," or "dwarf" (e.g., "Haworthia mini" or "Sansevieria 'Bird's Nest'").
- **Pots**: Opt for **lightweight plastic pots** (not ceramic) to keep plants light. Ensure they have drainage holes.
- **Wind Protection**: While fifth-floor wind isn’t extreme, group plants together or place them against a wall for stability.
- **Temporary Setup**: Since you’re only renting, choose **non-invasive plants** (e.g., snake plants don’t spread, succulents stay compact).

### Final Recommendations:
1. **2 Succulents** (tabletop) + **1 Bird’s Nest Snake Plant** (floor).  
   - **Total Weight**: ~5–8 lbs (manageable for one trip).  
   - **Space**: Fits your table + floor perfectly.  
   - **Homey Factor**: Adds color (succulents) and structure (snake plant).  
   - **NYC Resilience**: Bright east sun, wind, and easy care.  

2. **Alternative Herbs**: Basil or mint in small pots (1–2 lbs) add greenery and scent. Place on the table—they’ll love the morning light!
