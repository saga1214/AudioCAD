Okay, great to hear you're making progress on the cabinets! Since you've already cleaned, degreased, light-sand, and gathered the primer, there are just a few final prep steps before you start painting.

First, give the sanded surface a quick wipe down with a tack cloth or a damp rag to remove all the dust the sanding created. 

Then, before you prime, you'll need to protect the floor and countertops with painter's tape and plastic sheeting. 

And make sure you have your paintbrushes or rollers ready to go. 

Oh, and if you're painting the doors separately, you'll want to make sure they're completely dry before you try to put the hardware back on.
