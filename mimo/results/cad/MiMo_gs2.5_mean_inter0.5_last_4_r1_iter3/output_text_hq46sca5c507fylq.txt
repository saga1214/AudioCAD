That's a great question! Based on your hands-on skills, interest in tailoring/restoring small items, and vintage pen hobby, here are some meaningful gift ideas:

**For Tailoring (or your New Hobby):**
1. **Quality Fabric Shears:** Since you specifically asked about shears, brands like **Gingher** or **Kai** make excellent professional-grade scissors. A good pair is an investment.
2. **Vintage Fabric Bundle:** Find a curated bundle of interesting vintage cottons or linens online. This supports your vintage pen hobby *and* gives you fabric to practice on.
3. **Specialized Pen Care Kit:** A nicer kit for your favorite Parker 51 (new cleaning supplies, spare nibs, ink vials) shows you're appreciated for what you already love.
4. **Tailoring Books/Workshop:** A book like "The Tailor's Shop" by Marlene Zieglinger or a local beginner's sewing workshop.
5. **Leatherworking Starter Kit:** If you explore leather, a basic kit with tools and scraps is very tactile and rewarding.

**For Work-with-Your-Hands Hobbies (Woodworking/Clay):**
1. **Woodworking:** High-quality **clamps** (bar clamps, pipe clamps) or a **hand plane**. A set of **pre-cut wood scraps** for practice.
2. **Clay:** A **potter's wheel** (if you're serious) or a **basic hand-building kit** with clay and tools. A **ceramic sculpture kit**.
3. **General:** **Sharpening stones** for tools (essential for woodworking/clay), or **high-quality sandpaper**.

**Experiential Gifts (Great for Hobbyists):**
1. **Local Class/Workshop:** Tailoring, leatherworking, pottery, woodworking – hands-on learning is incredibly valuable.
2. **Gift Card to a Hobby Store:** Lets you choose exactly what you need (sewing machine, wood, clay, leather).
3. **Subscription Box:** Tailored to your interests (e.g., "The Craft Box" for various crafts, "Pen Addict" for fountain pens).
4. **Time for Practice:** Offer to watch the kids/baby so they can dedicate a full day to a project.

**Tips for Choosing:**
*   **Listen to Your Interests:** Tailoring seems strong based on hands skill/vintage pens. Tailor suggestions if possible.
*   **Practical vs. Aspirational:** Do they need starter tools (like clamps) or something more specialized?
*   **Support Existing Passion:** A nice pen kit or vintage fabric bundle shows you appreciate what you already do.
*   **Experiences Often Win:** A class or workshop is a gift that keeps giving and builds skill.

**Recommendation:** Since tailoring seems like a strong fit, **quality fabric shears** (if they don't have great ones) or a **curated bundle of vintage-inspired cottons** would be deeply appreciated and practical. If they decide against tailoring, a **pottery wheel** or **woodworking clamps** would be excellent alternatives for hands-on creation. A **local workshop** is a fantastic, personalized option for any hobby!
