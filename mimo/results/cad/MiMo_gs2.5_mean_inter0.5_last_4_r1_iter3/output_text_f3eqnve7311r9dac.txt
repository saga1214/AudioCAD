That's a very smart observation. Older comics are definitely more scarce. A good starting point for Luke Cage collectors is *Captain America Comics* number one. It's his first appearance, but it's also the first appearance of Bucky Barnes and the first comic to feature the Captain America shield.

Here are nine other classic 1940s and 50s comics that are valuable collectibles. *All-Star Comics* number eight, which is the first appearance of the Justice League of America. *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, which is Batman's first cover appearance. *Tales of the Taboo* number one, featuring the first appearance of the Sandman. *Flash Comics* number one, which is the Flash's first cover. *Adventures of Superman* number one, Superman's first cover. *Warrior* number one, the first appearance of Green Lantern. *Weird Fantasy* number one, the first appearance of Doctor Strange. And finally, *The Spectacular Spider-Man* number one from 1963, which is the first appearance of the Green Goblin.

Just a couple of things to keep in mind: condition is extremely important. A comic in poor shape is worth very little. And you should always look for copies that have been professionally graded by a service like CGC. You can check the Heritage Auctions website for recent sales prices.
