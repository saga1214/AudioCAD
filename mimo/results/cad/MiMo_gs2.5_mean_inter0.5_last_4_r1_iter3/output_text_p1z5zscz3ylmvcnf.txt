Fourth, the Pontiac GTO, from 1964 to 1967. It's famous for being one of the first muscle cars and has a really loyal following. A really good one can be worth over 100,000. 

And fifth, the Ford Falcon, specifically the Mark II from 1969 to 1970. It's a bit of a hidden gem, but it's getting more popular. A really nice one can be worth around 80,000.
