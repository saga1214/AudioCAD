You are absolutely right to raise this. While connection is vital, the immediate safety of you and the client must be the top priority.

The single most important approach for those first 20 to 30 minutes now has to be establishing structure and safety.

Since elopement and aggression are challenges, you should start sessions with predictable routines to help him feel secure. This could be playing his music and then clearly moving on to the next part of your planned activity. You could also work on teaching simple, functional requests, like asking for a break or asking to stop a song. This can give him a way to communicate his needs without resorting to eloping or lashing out. Addressing the environmental hazards and supervisor issues is a critical next step for the overall safety and effectiveness of your sessions.
