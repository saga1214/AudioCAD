Here are five simple and comforting dinner ideas you can whip up in that kitchen.

First, you could do a quick and easy fajita bowl. Just grab some chicken sausage or shrimp, bell peppers and onions, and a can of black beans. Sauté the veggies, add the protein, and then serve it all over a bed of rice or quinoa.

Second, a creamy tomato basil pasta. You'll need pasta like penne, some canned tomatoes, garlic, fresh basil, and heavy cream or coconut milk. Just simmer the tomatoes and garlic, stir in the cream and basil, and toss it with the pasta.

Third, a classic pan-seared salmon with roasted veggies. Get a salmon fillet, slice some potatoes, and add asparagus or broccoli. Sear the salmon in a pan, then roast the veggies. You can drizzle the salmon with a little lemon-dill sauce.

Fourth, a hearty black bean and sweet potato skillet. Dice up a sweet potato and some onion, sauté them, add some canned black beans, cumin, and chili powder, and let it all simmer.

And fifth, a comforting chicken and vegetable stir-fry. Get some chicken breast, snap peas, carrots, and broccoli. Stir-fry everything in a little soy sauce, ginger, and garlic, and serve it over rice or noodles.
