For packing tips specific to your Northern California cold厌烦 and desire for California warmth: light layers are key (think sundresses, tank tops, flowy shirts), a quick-dry cover-up is essential, and flip-flops are your best friend for swimming and keeping toes warm. A foldable tote bag is handy for beach gear. A light windbreaker or shrug works for evening chill even in sunny spots.

Since you dislike heights, fantastic alternatives to a hot air balloon exist! Here are outdoor adventures that feel epic without the elevation:

1.  **Whale Watching & Marine Mammal Tours:** This is arguably the closest thing to a "wow" experience without heights. Take a large cruise ship or smaller boat tour around the Monterey Bay or near Santa Cruz. Spot humpbacks, blue whales, gray whales, sea lions, and harbor seals. It's magical and competitive on excitement! Companies like Monterey Bay Aquarium's Whale Watch or Half Moon Bay Whale Watch are excellent.
2.  **Scenic Train Rides:** Ride the California Zephyr from Emeryville to Lake Tahoe! It's a multi-day, luxurious train journey through stunning mountains and valleys. Or, for a shorter, more local adventure, the Coast Starlight offers beautiful views along the Central Coast. Both are thrilling and relaxing.
3.  SF Bay Cruise: As you mentioned, an Alcatraz cruise is perfect! The history is fascinating, and the views of the city skyline and bay are incredible. It's a great way to see the iconic landmarks without heights. Companies like Alcatraz Cruises offer various experiences.
4.  Hot Air Balloon Ride (Ground Level!): Some companies offer "ground level" balloon rides where you experience the launch and landing without getting in the basket. You might find these near Lake Tahoe or other scenic areas.
5.  Scenic Drive & Hike: Plan a full day trip! Drive the stunning Big Sur Coast Road (from San Luis Obispo to Carmel/Monterey), stopping at Bixby Bridge, McWay Falls, and Pfeiffer Beach. Pack a picnic lunch and do some easy coastal hiking. Or drive the Pinnacles National Park route for unique desert landscapes and hiking.

**For your daughter celebrating Celiac:** Focus on places with excellent gluten-free knowledge and dedicated facilities. Monterey and the Central Coast areas often excel here. Trish's Mini Donuts (Pier 39) is a must-visit treat. Ask about dedicated fryers and staff training when booking any tours or restaurants.

**Birthday Celebration in Alcatraz Area (Fisherman's Wharf):**
*   **Trish's Mini Donuts:** (Mentioned earlier) A guaranteed hit for a fun, sweet birthday treat.
*   **Fog Harbor Fish House:** (Mentioned earlier) Excellent seafood views, call ahead about GF needs.
*   **The Spinnaker:** Upscale seafood with beautiful harbor views. Their staff is usually very accommodating for Celiac.
*   **Scoma's:** Classic Fisherman's Wharf seafood spot. Known for being accommodating to dietary needs, including Celiac.

**Vacation Itinerary: 4-5 Days (Family SUV Friendly, Warm/Wet Focused, Healthy Budget)**

**Option 1: Central Coast Gem - Pismo Beach & Morro Bay**

*   **Why:** Guaranteed warm weather most of the year, beautiful beaches perfect for swimming, relaxed vibe, excellent dining, great for families.
*   **Stay:** Pismo Beach or Morro Bay. Look for a nice beachfront or ocean view condo/apartment for space and kitchen. *Example:* Pismo Beach Condos or Morro Bay Inn & Suites.
*   **Pack:** All your swim gear, light layers, cover-ups, sandals. A light sweater is optional.
*   **Swimming:** Pismo Beach, Morro Bay Harbor, Oceano Dunes State Beach nearby.
*   **Dining:** Abundant choices! Focus on fresh seafood and many places accommodate Celiac well. *Examples:* The Fish Shop (Pismo), The Seafood Company (Morro Bay), The Fish Market (Pismo - casual & GF options).
*   **Activities:**
    *   **Day 1:** Arrive, settle in, relax on Pismo Beach, swim, dinner at The Fish Shop.
    *   **Day 2:** Morning: Morro Bay Harbor tour (see sea lions, kayaking optional, great for Celiac). Afternoon: Explore Morro Rock State Natural Reserve (short hike to the sea cave). Evening: Dinner at The Seafood Company.
    *   **Day 3:** Morning: Visit Oceano Dunes State Beach (swimming, hiking, dunes). Afternoon: Explore Pismo Beach (walk the pier, shops). Evening: Dinner at The Fish Market.
    *   **Day 4:** Morning: Kayak tour in the Pismo Estuary or Pismo Dunes (rentals available). Afternoon: Relax/swim, maybe visit Pismo's famous mini donuts! Evening: Farewell dinner.
*   **Travel:** Easy drive from NorCal (approx. 2-2.5 hours from SF Bay Area).

**Option 2: Tropical Paradise - Laguna Beach**

*   **Why:** Stunning Southern California coastline, warmer than NorCal, beautiful beaches, hills, lots of shops and dining, very family-friendly.
*   **Stay:** Laguna Beach or nearby Heisler Park. Look for a beachfront condo or hotel with a pool. *Example:* The Beachfront Inn, Laguna Beach.
*   **Pack:** Same as Pismo - swim gear, cover-ups, light layers, cover-ups, sandals. A light jacket for evenings.
*   **Swimming:** Heisler Park Beach (right near downtown), Laguna Beach Cove, Victoria Beach.
*   **Dining:** Fantastic variety! Many restaurants are accommodating to Celiac. *Examples:* The Beachcomber Cafe (casual, great views), The Cliff Restaurant (upscale, GF options), Tacos El Gordo (lively, GF options), The Laguna Grill (downtown, accommodating).
*   **Activities:**
    *   **Day 1:** Arrive, check into Heisler Park area hotel/condo. Relax at the beach or pool. Dinner at The Beachcomber Cafe.
    *   **Day 2:** Morning: Hike up the Heisler Park cliffs for incredible views, then swim. Afternoon: Explore Main Beach, shops, galleries. Evening: Dinner at The Cliff.
    *   **Day 3:** Morning: Rent bikes and cycle the beautiful coastal path. Afternoon: Visit Crystal Cove State Park (swimming at Cove Beach, hiking). Evening: Dinner at Tacos El Gordo.
    *   **Day 4:** Morning: Relax/swim, maybe visit the Laguna Art Museum. Afternoon: Free time for shopping or more beach time. Evening: Farewell dinner at The Laguna Grill.
*   **Travel:** Also easy drive from NorCal (approx. 1.5 - 2 hours from SF Bay Area).

**Option 3: Coastal Charm & Wildlife - Santa Barbara**

*   **Why:** Upscale, beautiful beaches, warm weather, excellent dining, whale watching season (Dec-Apr), unique vibe.
*   **Stay:** Santa Barbara (downtown, State Street, or near Stearns Wharf). Look for a nice hotel with a pool or a condo. *Example:* Hotel Milo, The Westin Santa Barbara Mission Inn.
*   **Pack:** Same swim gear. A light sweater or cardigan is handy.
*   **Swimming:** Stearns Wharf Beach, East Beach, Butterfly Beach.
*   **Dining:** World-class dining! Many excellent options for Celiac. *Examples:* Boathouse at Waterfront (downtown waterfront, GF menu), Elx (upscale, GF options), The Lark (casual, great GF), Bouchon (upscale, GF options).
*   **Activities:**
    *   **Day 1:** Arrive, settle in. Walk State Street (shops, boutiques), dinner at Boathouse.
    *   **Day 2:** Morning: Stearns Wharf & Harbor tour (biking, walking, views). Afternoon: Butterfly Beach (swimming, hiking) or Visit the Santa Barbara Zoo. Evening: Dinner at The Lark.
    *   Whale Watching (if timing is right): **Crucial for Celiac:** MUST call the company (e.g., Santa Barbara Whale Watch) WELL IN ADVANCE to confirm their gluten-free protocols for the entire boat. This is non-negotiable for Celiac safety.
    *   **Day 4:** Morning: Relax/swim. Afternoon: Explore downtown or visit the Santa Barbara Mission. Evening: Farewell dinner at Elx or Bouchon.
*   **Travel:** About 3 hours from NorCal.

**Key Packing Reminders for Vacation Bag:**

*   **Swim Gear:** 2 bathing suits, cover-up, flip-flops/sandals, swimwear (rash guard if needed), sunscreen (reef-safe!), swim goggles, maybe a beach towel (quick-dry or foldable is great).
*   **Daytime:** Sundresses, shorts, tank tops, light pants, light jacket or shrug for evenings.
*   **Other:** Sunglasses, hat, reusable water bottle, travel-sized toiletries, medications, travel adapter, charger, small backpack or tote for beach/swim days.
*   **Celiac Safety:** Bring a small card explaining your diagnosis and what "gluten-free" means to you (if needed). Pack safe snacks like GF crackers, fruit, nuts (check labels!).
