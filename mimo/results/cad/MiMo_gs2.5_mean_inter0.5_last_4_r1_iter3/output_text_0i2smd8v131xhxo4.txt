Here's a structured approach to your questions, along with a tasty food suggestion:

**Switching to Adobe Audition:**
1.  **Start Small:** Import an Audacity project and try editing one track in Audition. Learn the timeline, multi-track workflow, and basic effects.
2.  **Leverage Tutorials:** Adobe offers official tutorials. YouTube channels like "Adobe Audition Tutorials" and "The Audio Guy" are excellent resources.
3.  **Key Advantages:** Focus on learning the timeline for precise edits, the multi-track setup for efficiency, and built-in AI tools like Auto-Refine for noise reduction. The learning curve is steeper, but the long-term efficiency gains are significant.

**Reliable AI Editing Tools:**
*   **Adobe Audition:** Its own **Auto-Refine** (for noise reduction) and **Neural Tune** (for pitch/timing correction) are powerful and integrated.
*   **iZotope RX:** Industry-standard for advanced audio repair. Its **De-click**, **De-reverb**, and **Dynamics Processing** modules use AI effectively. (Paid, but often bundled with subscriptions).
*   **Descript:** Great for transcription and editing via text (overdub editing). AI-powered transcription is reliable.
*   **Avoid:** Many free AI tools (like "AI Voice Remover" sites) lack nuance and quality for professional work. Stick with established DAW plugins or dedicated tools like RX.

**Balancing Recording, Editing & Marketing (Avoiding Burnout):**
1.  **Schedule Rigorously:** Block dedicated recording, editing, admin/marketing, and *rest* times. Treat work like appointments.
2.  **Batch Tasks:** Record multiple takes/session types in one block, then edit those blocks later. Do all client emails in one session.
3.  **Set Boundaries:** Define clear start/end times. Use "Do Not Disturb" modes. Communicate availability to clients.
4.  **Prioritize Self-Care:** **Hydrate** (room-temp water is best for voice), eat properly (see food suggestion!), take screen breaks, move your body. Vocal fatigue is real.
5.  **Automate Admin:** Use tools like Calendly for scheduling, Zapier for workflows.
