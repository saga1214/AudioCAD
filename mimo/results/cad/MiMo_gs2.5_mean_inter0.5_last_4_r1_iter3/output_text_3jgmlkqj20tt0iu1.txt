Initally, a face-to-face meeting can feel intimidating, which matches your earlier anxiety. A good way to start is to request a brief, scheduled meeting during one of your regular one-on-ones. You can frame it as a performance discussion, which feels less confrontational.

Another option is to send a professional email. You could write something like, 'Hi, I'd like to schedule some time to discuss my performance and career growth with you. Would you be available for a 15-minute meeting sometime next week?'

This gives you time to prepare and reduces the pressure of initiating an unscheduled, potentially awkward conversation.
