Here's a consolidated list of my top recommendations for sheet metal cutting, drywall fastening, and insulation, along with purchasing locations:

### Cutting Sheet Metal
1.  **Thinner Sheets & Curves:** Pair of Aviation Snips  
    *(Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards - best selection)*
2.  **Thicker Metal / Power:** Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel  
    *(Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards - best selection)*
3.  Alternative: Jigsaw with Quality Metal Cutting Blade  
    *(Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards, Tractor Supply Co.)*

### Drywall Fasteners
1.  **Best Overall:** 1-1/4" Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws  
    *(Coats, Durabolt, GRK brands at Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards - best selection)*
2.  **Alternative (if using nails):** Ring-Shank Nails  
    *(Ace Hardware, Tractor Supply Co., Menards - best selection for ring shanks)*

### Insulation
1.  **Standard Walls:** Fiberglass Bat Insulation (Pre-Cut Rolls)  
    *(Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards - best selection)*
2.  **Soundproofing / Fire Resistance:** Mineral Wool (Reefrock, Rockwool)  
    *(Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards, Ace Hardware, Building Supply stores)*
3.  **Best Air Sealing / Performance:** Spray Foam Insulation  
    *(Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards - best selection for DIY kits; Pros at local insulation companies)*

### Purchasing Locations (Indianapolis, IN - In-Store)
*   **Big Box Stores (Best Selection):**
    *   **Home Depot:** Extensive tool and material selection.
    *   **Lowe's:** Similar to Home Depot.
    *   **Menards:** Excellent for tools, fasteners, and insulation.
*   **Hardware Stores:**
    *   **Ace Hardware:** Good for drywall nails/screws and smaller tools.
    *   **Tractor Supply Co.:** Often carries aviation snips, jigsaws, and metal blades.

**Key Takeaways:**
*   **Snips/Jigsaw:** Aviation snips for thin/curved metal, grinder/cut-off wheel for thick metal.
*   **Drywall:** **Screws are strongly recommended** for best holding power. Fiberglass batts are the standard insulation choice.
*   **Insulation:** Choose based on priority: fiberglass for cost/ease, mineral wool for sound/fire, spray foam for air sealing.
