That's a great question. Since 15 minutes is too short for deep meditation, here are a few balanced options that would help you recharge without being jarring:

1.  **Short Walk (5-10 min):** As you suggested, this is excellent. Step away from your desk, get some fresh air (even if just around the office or outside), and focus on your surroundings. It combines gentle movement with sensory input, boosting alertness naturally. Pair it with your "Talking Timbuktu" playlist for a mini-immersion break.

2.  **Quick Mindfulness/Body Scan (5-10 min):** Instead of emptying your mind, focus gently inward. Sit comfortably, close your eyes if possible, and take 3-5 deep breaths. Then, briefly scan your body from head to toe, noticing any areas of tension (jaw, shoulders, hands) and consciously relaxing them. This is calming but doesn't require deep focus, making it less jarring than full meditation.

3.  **Light Stretching at Your Standing Desk:** Build on what you're already doing! Do a sequence of 10-15 minutes of gentle stretches: shoulder rolls, arm circles (both directions), overhead stretches (reaching arms high), torso twists, and maybe calf raises if you can step down briefly. Focus on releasing tension in your upper body and spine.

4.  **Hydrate & Snack Mindfully (5 min):** Grab a glass of water or herbal tea (avoiding heavy caffeine if it might affect your afternoon focus). While you drink, take a moment to be present – feel the temperature of the glass, notice the taste of the liquid, observe your breath. If you want a snack, choose something light and nutritious (fruit, nuts, yogurt) and eat it slowly and mindfully.

**Recommendation:** Since you mentioned it already helps you stay alert and rejuvenated, **combining a 5-minute walk with 5 minutes of mindful breathing or light stretching** seems like a perfect fit. It addresses both physical movement and mental calm in a short timeframe. Enjoy your break!
