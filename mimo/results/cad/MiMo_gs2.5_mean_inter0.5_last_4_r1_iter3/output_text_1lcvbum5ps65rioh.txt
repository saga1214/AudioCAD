### U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia:  
If you make an illegal U-turn in BC, you can expect:  
- **Fines**: $100–$160 (varies by city).  
- **Demerit Points**: 3 points on your license for unsafe U-turns.  
- **Ticket**: A traffic ticket for "Impeding Traffic" or "Improper Turn."  

**Key Rules to Remember**:  
- ✅ **Allowed**: At designated intersections with a clear view (≥150m) and no signs prohibiting it.  
- ❌ **Prohibited**: Near curves, hills, intersections, railway crossings, or if it blocks traffic.  
- 🚦 **Signal**: Always use your turn signal.  

Always check signs and be cautious—safety matters more than speed!

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients:  
Here’s a simple list for 4 servings:  
1. **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti (or bucatini/fettuccine).  
2. **Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 1 yolk (for richness).  
3. **Cheese**: 150g grated Pecorino Romano (or Parmesan).  
4. **Meat**: 200g diced pancetta or guanciale (cured pork cheek).  
5. **Garlic**: 1 clove, finely minced (optional).  
6. **Pepper**: Freshly ground black pepper (to taste).  
7. **Salt**: For pasta water.  

**Method Tip**:  
- Cook pasta in salted water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
- Toss hot pasta with egg/cheese mix off-heat—stir vigorously to create a creamy sauce. Add pancetta and pepper last.  

Enjoy your meal! 🍝
