### 1. **Color-Coding Icons**
   - **Memory vs. Mental Load**: Color-coding primarily reduces *mental load* by creating visual shortcuts. For example, blue for admin tasks or green for creative projects helps your brain quickly categorize things without scanning labels. It’s less about memory and more about making transitions between tasks smoother.
   - **Subtlety is Key**: Start with 2–3 colors max (e.g., blue, orange, gray). Too many colors can overwhelm. If icons get busy, add a *single* accent color for priority items.

### 2. **Playlist Rotation**
   - **Optimal Timing**: Switching every 45–60 minutes (e.g., lo-fi → nature sounds) can refresh focus without disrupting flow. If you’re deep in work, stick with one vibe for 90 minutes. For creative tasks, rotating every 30 minutes might spark ideas.
   - **Personal Preference Wins**: If one playlist energizes you and the other calms you, rotate based on *what you need* (e.g., nature sounds for post-meeting recovery, lo-fi for coding). Track your focus for a week to see what works.

### 3. **Physical Documents**
   - **Environment vs. Chunking**: Progress comes from **both**. A clean workspace reduces decision fatigue, but chunking tasks (e.g., "file invoices before lunch") builds momentum. Do it on a weekend if it feels like a chore—fresh mindset = better execution.
   - **Pro Tip**: Use a simple "In/Out Box" on your desk to capture documents daily. Process the box at set times (e.g., 9 AM, 5 PM).

### Dinner Suggestions (Mediterranean-inspired):
Here are 4 quick, satisfying options:

1. **Lemon-Garlic Shrimp with Quinoa & Roasted Veggies**  
   *Quick*: Sauté shrimp with garlic, lemon zest, and chili flakes. Toss with pre-cooked quinoa and roasted broccoli/zucchini. Serve with a dollop of tzatziki.

2. **One-Pot Greek Chickpea Curry**  
   *Comforting*: Sauté onion/cumin, add canned tomatoes, coconut milk, chickpeas, and spinach. Simmer 20 mins. Serve with couscous. (Uses pantry staples!)

3. **Mediterranean Sheet-Pan Chicken**  
   *Hands-off*: Toss chicken thighs with olive oil, oregano, lemon, and cherry tomatoes. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 25 mins. Add feta and olives before serving.

4. **Leftover Repurpose**  
   *Zero-waste*: Turn yesterday’s roasted veggies into a frittata or blend with Greek yogurt for a smoothie. If you’re craving carbs, add them to last night’s curry.
