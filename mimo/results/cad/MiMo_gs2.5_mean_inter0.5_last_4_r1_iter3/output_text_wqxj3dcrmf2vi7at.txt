Here’s a clear recap of corgi-friendly treats for "Princess":

### 🍪 Homemade Treats:
1. **Frozen Bites**: Freeze small portions of mashed **pumpkin** or **sweet potato** for a cool, fiber-rich snack.  
2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**: Mix xylitol-free peanut butter with oats, roll into small balls, and bake. *Always verify peanut butter safety!*  
3. **Cooked Protein Sticks**: Slice **plain chicken**, **turkey**, or **carrots** into thin, crunchy sticks.  

### 🛒 Popular Store-Bought Brands:
- **Blue Buffalo** (Life Protection Bits)  
- **Zuke’s** (Mini Life Parties)  
- ** Wellness** (Grain Free Mini Training Treats)  
- **Ziwi Peak** (New Zealand Green Lamb Lickies)  
- **Greenies** (Pet Dental Chews)  

### 📌 Key Tips:
- **Portion Control**: Corgis are compact—they need tiny treats!  
- **Safety First**: Avoid xylitol (in peanut butter), chocolate, grapes, and onions.  
- **Training Treats**: Use for high-value rewards during training sessions.
