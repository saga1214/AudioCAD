Here's a **clean, grab-and-go shopping list** designed for minimal cooler mess and maximum convenience for your theme park adventure with 10 dads, 10 kids, sugar-free sodas, calzones, and dessert:

### **DRINKS**  
*(Prevent spills with sealed bottles/cans)*  
- **Water bottles** (100+ for refills)  
- **Sugar-free sodas** (e.g., Sprite Zero, Coke Zero) in **individual cans** (10 cans per adult/kid = 20 cans)  
- **Low-sugar juice boxes** (e.g., apple or orange) for kids (10 boxes)  
- **Electrolyte drinks** (e.g., LMNT or Liquid IV) for hydration (10 packets)  
- **Ice packs** (5–6 large ones to keep everything cold)  

### **SNACKS**  
*(Zero-prep, single-serve)*  
- **Cheese sticks** (10 packs of 10 sticks)  
- **Baby carrots** (1 large bag)  
- **Single-serve hummus cups** (10)  
- **Pretzels** (mini or individual bags) (2 bags)  
- **Trail mix** (nut-free if needed) (2 bags)  
  *→ Optional: Cut-up cucumbers with single yogurt cups*  

### **MAIN MEAL**  
- **Mini calzones** (10–12 pre-made, from Italian deli/pizzeria)  
  *OR* **Pizza slices** (10 slices, no crust for less mess)  
- **Whole-wheat pitas/wrap containers** (10 individual wraps with fillings: turkey, hummus, veggies)  
- **Whole-grain crackers** (1 box)  
- **Single-serve guacamole or salsa cups** (optional for wraps)  

### **DESSERT**  
- **Cupcakes** (12, pre-decorated bachelor-themed) *OR* **Brownie bars** (1 box)  
- **Individual boxes** (for cupcakes/brownies to prevent drips)  
- **Small cookies** (e.g., oatmeal or chocolate chip) (1 bag)  

### **PRactical Extras**  
- **Reusable cooler bags** (2–3 large ones)  
- **Heavy-duty ziplock bags** (for extra snacks/snacks)  
- **Napkins** (100+)  
- **Biodegradable wet wipes**  
- **Allergy labels** (for calzone/wrap ingredients)  

---

### **Key Tips for Minimal Mess**  
1. **Pre-portion everything**: Use containers/bags so dads grab-and-go.  
2. **Dedicated soda cooler**: Separate soda-heavy cooler to avoid leaks.  
3. **Trash system**: Assign 2 dads as "mess monitors" with small trash bags.  
4. **Dessert timing**: Serve dessert mid-afternoon so it doesn’t get squashed.  

This setup balances nutrition, favorites (calzones/sodas), and park-friendly logistics. Enjoy the break for the moms and an epic theme park day! 🎢🎉
