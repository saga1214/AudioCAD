For winter chicks on concrete, the key is to create a thick, dry layer of bedding that will insulate them from the cold floor.

A good material is wood shavings, but use a smaller flake than before—about an inch to an inch and a half high. This provides insulation but is still rodent-resistant.

You can also use hardware cloth. Just line the bottom of their brooder with it, and then add about two inches of pine shavings on top. This will give them a solid surface to walk on without getting wet.

Whichever you choose, the most important thing is to manage moisture. Keep sweeping out wet, soiled bedding every day, and make sure you have great airflow. This will keep the chicks dry and warm.
