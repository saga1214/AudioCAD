The brain lateralization myth: It's oversimplified. While the left hemisphere often handles language and logical tasks, and the right handles spatial and creative tasks, it’s not like two separate computers. They constantly communicate through the corpus callosum. For example, a mathematician is creative and logical, which both sides of the brain contribute to. So, it’s not a myth, but it’s not a rigid, black-and-white division.

Or maybe data visualization, where you translate complex data into beautiful, understandable graphics. Another option is technical writing, which is creative and structured, but also has measurable outcomes.
