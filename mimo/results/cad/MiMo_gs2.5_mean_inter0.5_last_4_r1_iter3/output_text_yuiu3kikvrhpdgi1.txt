You got it! For your edible garden, I'd stick with the easy starters we talked about. Herbs like basil, mint, and parsley are your best friends—they sprout fast and are super resilient. Leafy greens like lettuce, spinach, and radishes are also fantastic because they're quick and you'll see a yield in just a couple of weeks. They're perfect for a beginner because they're not too demanding. Tomatoes and peppers are also great choices. They're forgiving, and once they get going, they produce a ton of delicious fruit. They're definitely worth the effort!
