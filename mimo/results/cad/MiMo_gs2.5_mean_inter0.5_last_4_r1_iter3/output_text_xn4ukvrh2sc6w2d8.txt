Here are the classic recipes with precise ingredients and measurements for your reception, formatted for your bartenders:

### **Old Fashioned**  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey  
  - 1 sugar cube (or ¾ oz simple syrup)  
  - 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (for garnish)  
- **Method:**  
  1. Muddle the sugar cube (or stir in simple syrup) in a mixing glass.  
  2. Add bitters, whiskey, and ice. Stir well (20–30 seconds).  
  3. Strain into a chilled rocks glass over fresh ice.  
  4. Express an orange twist over the drink and drop it in.  

---

### **Paper Plane**  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz gin  
  - ¾ oz Aperol  
  - ½ oz Amaro Nonino  
  - ½ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Method:**  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a **coupe glass** (chilled).  
  3. Garnish with a cherry.  

---

### **Whiskey Sour**  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - ½ oz simple syrup  
  - 1 egg white (optional, for texture)  
- **Method:**  
  1. **With egg white:** Combine bourbon, lemon juice, simple syrup, and egg white in a shaker. *Dry shake (no ice)* to emulsify. Add ice, shake again. Strain into a chilled rocks glass over ice. Garnish with cherry/orange slice.  
  2. **Without egg white:** Skip the egg white. Shake all ingredients with ice, strain into a coupe glass. Garnish with lemon twist.  

---

### **Aviation**  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz gin  
  - ¾ oz maraschino liqueur  
  - ¼ oz crème de violette  
  - ½ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Method:**  
  Shake with ice, strain into a **cocktail glass**. Garnish with maraschino cherry.  

---

### **Bonus Gin Cocktail Recommendation: French 75**  
- **Why it fits:** Elegant, vintage-inspired, and perfect for fall.  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 1.5 oz gin  
  - 0.75 oz lemon juice  
  - 0.75 oz simple syrup  
  - 3 oz chilled champagne or prosecco  
- **Method:** Shake gin, lemon, and syrup with ice. Strain into a flute. Top with champagne. Garnish with lemon twist.  

---

### **Finger Food Suggestions**  
Pair with autumnal, visually appealing bites:  
- **Spiced nuts** (candied pecans/walnuts)  
- **Mini quiches** (gorgonzola, cheddar, or chicken & herb)  
- **Gingersnaps or shortbread cookies**  
- **Mini sliders** with caramelized onions or fig jam  
- **Deviled eggs** (with a pinch of smoked paprika)

### **Key Tips for Bartenders**  
1. **Freshness is critical:** Use fresh lemon juice and proper ice.  
2. **Glassware:** Rocks glass for Old Fashioned, coupe for Paper Plane/French 75, coupe for Aviation.  
3. **Egg white:** Emphasize it’s optional but highly recommended for texture.  
4. **Bitters:** Use Angostura for Old Fashioned/Aviation.
