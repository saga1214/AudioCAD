Okay, diesel camper vans definitely need a pre-trip check. For brakes, you should inspect the pads and rotors for wear, listen for any grinding sounds, and check the brake fluid level. If the van has rear drum brakes, make sure the shoes are in good shape. As for spark plugs, they aren’t changed as often in diesel engines, but you can still check their condition. You’d need to remove the air intake to get to the plugs, and then use a wrench to turn them counter-clockwise while holding the engine block to prevent it from spinning. It’s a bit more involved than in a gas engine. Either way, a good inspection is key before a long trip.
