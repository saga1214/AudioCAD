Okay, this is a critical safety issue. I have to be very clear and firm here. The fact that your friend's car is the same model does not make the safety of the ramps or the situation itself any safer for you. The danger is external to the car model. Your driveway's slope is the problem. Ramps are designed for flat ground, and any angle, no matter how small you think it is, can cause the car to roll. It's not a matter of 'if' but 'when' it could happen. The risk is too great. Please, do not use the ramps on the sloped driveway. Find a completely flat, level spot. Your safety is more important than getting the paint finished or the oil changed.
