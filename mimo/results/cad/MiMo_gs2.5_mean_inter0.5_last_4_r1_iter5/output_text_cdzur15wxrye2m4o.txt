**No. Absolutely do not use the car ramps on a sloped driveway.** This is extremely dangerous and could result in serious injury or death.

Here's why you **must** find a level surface:

1.  **Rolling Hazard:** Even a "tiny slope" creates enough force for the car to roll. If the ramps are slightly angled, the car can roll *off* them or roll *onto* the blocks you've placed underneath. The force can be immense.
2.  **Ramps Aren't Rated for Slopes:** Car ramps are designed for level surfaces. Using them on a slope compromises their stability and safety features.
3.  **Loss of Control:** You will be underneath the car, completely vulnerable. If it rolls, you will be crushed.

**The only safe solution for painting and for changing the oil is to find a perfectly flat, level surface.**

*   **Painting:** Use the non-telescoping roller pole on a step ladder set up safely on the stairs if reaching the last few feet is manageable that way. Otherwise, borrow or buy a pole.
*   **Oil Change:** Use the ramps on a flat, level surface. Place jack stands securely under the car's frame (never just on the ramps) for safety.

**Please prioritize your safety.** Do not attempt to change the oil on your stairs wall wall on a slope. It's not worth the risk.
