Here's a breakdown addressing your questions, from technical setup to workflow and well-being:

### 1. **Mic/Acoustic Setup**  
- **Blue Yeti Tip:** Always speak into the side with the logo, 6-12 inches from your mouth. Angle it slightly toward your mouth (not straight on).  
- **Acoustic Quick-Fix:** Heavy blankets hung vertically absorb mid-to-high frequencies that cause harshness. Avoid hard surfaces (walls, desks) directly behind the mic.  
- **Interface Tweaks:** In your Scarlett Solo’s software (Focusrite Control), try reducing the "High-Pass Filter" (e.g., 80-100Hz) to cut low rumble without affecting voice warmth.  

### 2. **Software Switching (Audacity → Adobe Audition)**  
- **Transition Tips:**  
  - Export Audacity projects as WAV files first.  
  - Use Adobe’s free [Audition tutorials](https://helpx.adobe.com/audition/tutorials.html).  
  - Start with simple tasks (noise reduction, volume leveling) before complex edits.  
- **Time Savings:** Audition’s "Auto-Noise Reduction" and multi-track editing will save hours. The learning curve is steep but pays off.  

### 3. **Editing & Organization**  
- **AI Tools:**  
  - **Descript:** Transcribes audio, lets you edit by deleting words (great for mouth clicks), and auto-generates chapters.  
  - **iZotope RX:** Industry-standard for removing noise, clicks, and breaths.  
- **Organization Hack:** Use "Project" folders in your DAW for each client. Label files with `[Client]_[Date]_[Take]_Vocal`.  

### 4. **Vocal Health & Workflow Balance**  
- **Warm-Ups/Cool-Downs:**  
  - 5-min daily warm-ups: Humming, lip trills, tongue clicks.  
  - Post-session: Steam your voice or gargle warm salt water.  
- **Hydration:** Sip room-temperature water hourly. Avoid caffeine/alcohol 2 hours before recording.  
- **Burnout Prevention:**  
  - Set recording "blocks" (e.g., 9 AM–12 PM), then mandatory breaks.  
  - Use a **Pomodoro timer** (25 mins work/5 mins rest).  

### 5. **Demo Clip Memorability**  
- **Hook First:** Start with a bold line or unique vocal texture (e.g., a whisper, growl, or laugh) in the first 3 seconds.  
- **Show Range:** In 15 seconds, showcase 2-3 distinct emotions (e.g., panic → tenderness → determination).  
- **Punchy Mix:** Boost low-mids (150-300Hz) slightly for warmth, cut harsh highs (3-5kHz), and add subtle reverb.

Order from a place like **Sushi Go!** or **BaoBox** for:  
- **Flavor:** Sweet-savory teriyaki glaze, tender pork, pickled veggies.

- **Satiety:** Sticky rice + pork keeps you full.  
- **Pro Tip:** Ask for extra pickled cabbage on the side for crunch!
