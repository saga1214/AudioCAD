Okay, let me break down both analogies for you. 

First one was throwing a party: 
Milk is the big empty room, good bacteria are special guests, keeping it warm energizes them, sugar is party food, group hug is the thickening hug, tanginess comes from eating sugar.

Second one was about forming a net:
Milk is the disorganized crowd, bacteria are tiny team coaches, warmth energizes coaches, forming a net is holding hands, the net traps liquid, flavoring is like uniforms that give it an identity, and the net itself is a mesh made from milk proteins that link together to trap liquid.

Both analogies really focus on the bacteria causing the proteins to link up, which traps liquid and makes the yogurt thick. The flavoring is just a way to give it a specific taste while keeping that structure intact. Hope that helps clear things up!
