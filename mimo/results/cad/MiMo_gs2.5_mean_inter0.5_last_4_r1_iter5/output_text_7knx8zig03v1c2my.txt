### 🍬 Treat Recommendations:
- **Best Flavors:** Start with small, soft treats like peanut butter (xylitol-free!), chicken, hot dogs, cheese cubes, or freeze-dried meats. Avoid anything spicy or salty.
- **Size:** Use tiny pieces (pea-sized) since Galileo is only 3 pounds.
- **Pro Tip:** Use a mix of flavors to prevent boredom (e.g., chicken one day, cheese the next).

### 🎯 Clicker Training Explained:
- **What it is:** A simple clicker that acts as a "bridge" between the action and the reward.
- **How it works:**
  1. Show Galileo a command (e.g., "sit").
  2. The *instant* he sits, click the button (this marks the exact moment he did it).
  3. Immediately give a treat.
  4. Repeat 10–20 times a day. The clicker teaches him *exactly* what behavior earns rewards.
- **Why it works:** It’s precise, fast, and builds trust. Galileo learns faster because the clicker signals "YES, THAT’S IT!" before he even gets the treat.

### 🛠️ Training Tools to Buy:
1. **Clicker:** $5–$10 (Amazon or pet stores).
2. **Leash:** Adjustable retractable or standard.
3. **Collar:** Soft padded or harness (safer for small breeds).
4. **Treat Pouch:** For easy access during walks/training.
5. Papillons are lean, so avoid heavy harnesses.

### 🐕 Puppy School Basics:
Dog schools teach:
- **Obedience:** Sit, stay, come, leave it, loose-leash walking.
- **Socialization:** Controlled exposure to other dogs/people to reduce fear/aggression.
- **House Training:** Potty cues and crate discipline.
- **Safety Skills:** Reacting to commands in distracting environments.

### 🏠 Crate Training Breakdown:
- **Why it’s cozy, not cruel:** Crates mimic a den—dogs find enclosed spaces comforting. It’s like a "safe room."
- **How to make it cozy:**
  - Line it with a soft blanket (or bed).
  - Hide treats/toys inside (he’ll associate it with fun).
  - Never use it for punishment.
- **Key tip:** Gradually increase crate time (start with 5–10 minutes while you’re home).

### 🚨 If He Escapes to the Yard:
1. **Prevention:** Always use a leash in unsecured yards. Consider a small fence (e.g., chicken wire) around the yard.
2. **Recall Training:** Practice daily. Call his name + "Galileo, come!" with high-value treats. Run away and toss treats as he chases you (makes recall fun!).
3. **Emergency Plan:** If he bolts, stay calm. Call his name excitedly and run toward him *without chasing*. Use treats to lure him back.

### 🧼 Potty Training Tips:
- **Schedule:** Take him out every 30–60 minutes (especially after waking, eating, or playing).
- **Accidents:** Clean with enzymatic cleaner (e.g., Nature’s Miracle) to remove odors. Never punish—just clean and reset.
- **Reward:** Praise and treat *every time* he goes outside.

### 💡 Final Advice:
- **Patience:** Training takes weeks/months. Celebrate small wins!
- **Consistency:** Use the same commands (e.g., "sit" instead of "sit down").
- **Italian Touch:** Try Italian names like Dante or Enzo!
