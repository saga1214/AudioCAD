Okay, since you love romance novels and real life by the ocean, let's build on that.

One idea is a sunset catamaran cruise. You could charter a small one for just the two of you, with a picnic dinner and chilled local wine. The view of the sunset over the water is a classic romantic image.

Another idea, if you like seafood, is a private fishing charter. Many places offer half-day trips where you can catch your own dinner. You could then have it prepared at a seaside restaurant for a special meal that you cooked together.
