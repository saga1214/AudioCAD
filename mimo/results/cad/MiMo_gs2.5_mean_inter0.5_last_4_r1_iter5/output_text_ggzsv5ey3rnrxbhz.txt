Okay, it sounds like you're really worried about these "forever chemicals" and hypochondria is making this extra stressful for you. I get it. Let's focus on what you can actually do.

The five products you should really try to avoid to minimize exposure are:
First, non-stick cookware, like those with Teflon coatings.
Second, grease-resistant food packaging, like fast-food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags.
Third, anything labeled as stain- or waterproof, such as carpets and some clothing.
Fourth, certain cosmetics.
And fifth, since they can be in tap water, using a certified water filter.

PFAS stands for per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances. It's not just one single chemical, but a huge family of thousands of them. And yeah, bottled water can sometimes contain these chemicals, so a home water filter is a safer bet.

And to answer your other question—no, a juice cleanse or a water fast won't get rid of PFAS. Since they're called "forever chemicals," your body doesn't break them down easily. Exposure prevention is really the only way to manage it.
