If baking is what you're thinking of, you could try a quick recipe for a simple vanilla cake with that white frosting. You'd just need butter, sugar, eggs, flour, baking powder, milk, and some vanilla. Cream the butter and sugar together, then add the eggs, followed by the dry ingredients mixed with milk and vanilla. Pour that into a pan and bake for about 30 minutes at 350 degrees. For the frosting, just whip together some butter, powdered sugar, a bit of vanilla, and a splash of milk until it's nice and fluffy. Just make sure to let the cake cool down before you frost it. 

Chocolate in a pan... that's a good thought. Maybe a classic chocolate lava cake? You'd just mix flour, sugar, cocoa powder, eggs, and butter in a bowl, pour that into a greased ramekin, and bake at 375 degrees for about 10 to 12 minutes. It should have that gooey center. Or, if you want something richer, a chocolate mousse would be great. You'd just whip some heavy cream, melt some chocolate, and mix it all together in a bowl. You can serve it in glasses and top it with whipped cream and berries. Either one should be a hit.
