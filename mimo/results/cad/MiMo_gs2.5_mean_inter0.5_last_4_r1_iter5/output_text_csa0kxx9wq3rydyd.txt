Okay, let's get this all laid out for Thanksgiving. For appliances, you'll be using your main oven, a second oven, a slow cooker, an Instant Pot, a toaster oven, and a stovetop, though some of those will be used for smaller tasks. As for the cooking methods, you'll smoke meat, roast meat, roast vegetables, bake meat and sides, and bake a dessert. You'll also be grilling or frying the prime rib, making a salad fresh in a bowl, and preparing some items no-bake. This should give you a clear picture of what to do and what to get.
