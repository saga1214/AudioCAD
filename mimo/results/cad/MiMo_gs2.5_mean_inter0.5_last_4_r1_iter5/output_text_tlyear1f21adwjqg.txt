This is a tough question because efficiency can mean different things—like cost, availability, or how consistently it works. Solar energy is a strong overall choice because it's scalable and costs are always dropping. Hydropower is also highly efficient and reliable, especially where there are dams. Wind is a solid choice in windy places, but its output can be less consistent. If I absolutely had to pick just one, I'd still lean toward solar because of its broad availability and improving efficiency over time. However, the 'most efficient' choice really depends on the specific situation and location.
