That line of reasoning about genetic identity is interesting, but the evolutionary timeline still points to the chicken.

Here's how it works: for a mutation to change an egg into the egg of the first chicken, the genetic change had to happen in the organism that laid that egg. The egg itself doesn't contain the DNA that defines the whole species. The DNA is in the hen's reproductive system. Therefore, the hen with the new DNA had to exist first, and she was not yet a chicken. It was only after she hatched that she became the first chicken.
