Now, to answer your film student question: Can the eye see continuously? Well, it’s a bit of both. The eye is always processing visual information, but that processing isn’t perfectly continuous. Saccadic masking shows that during those rapid eye jumps, the brain can temporarily suppress or ignore some visual input to avoid blurring. So, the eye is always sampling the world, but the brain is stitching it all together to create a continuous experience. It’s not a perfect, seamless feed like a camera, but it’s close enough for us to navigate the world smoothly.
