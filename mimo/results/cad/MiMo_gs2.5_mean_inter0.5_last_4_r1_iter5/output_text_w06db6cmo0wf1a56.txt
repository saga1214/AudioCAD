Absolutely! Here's a practical, grab-and-go shopping list designed for your group of 10 dads, 10 kids, and minimal mess at the theme park, incorporating your calzone plan and soda request:

**Drinks (Focus on Grab & Go):**

1.  **Water Bottles (20 large reusable bottles):** The absolute essential for everyone all day.
2.  **Sugar-Free Sodas (10 cans/bottles per person = 100):** Choose flavors liked by adults and kids (e.g., root beer, cream soda, lemon-lime, strawberry). *Pack individual cans/bottles.*
3.  Pre-Packed Iced Tea (Lemon or Unflavored - 1-2 large boxes/packs): Single-serve boxes or bags work best.
4.  **Sports Drinks (1-2 large boxes/packs):** For extra hydration on a hot day (eatingsuch as Gatorade or Powerade).
5.  **Single-Serve Milk or Plant-Based Alternatives (10 single-serve packs):** For kids who prefer it (e.g., chocolate milk, almond milk cartons).
6.  *(Optional but Recommended)* Wet Wipes & Hand Sanitizer: For quick cleanups after snacks/drink.

**Snacks (Mess Minimized):**

7.  **Cheese Sticks (20 sticks):** A kid and dad favorite.
8.  **Grapes (2-3 large bags):** Easy to eat, nutritious.
9.  **Individual Pretzel Packs or Trail Mix Bags (20 single-serve bags):** Pretzels are classic kid/dad snack; trail mix is versatile.
10. **Baby Carrots (1 large bag):** A crunchy, healthy veggie option.
11. **Single-Serve Hummus Cups (10-12 cups):** Perfect pairing for carrots or pita chips (see below).
12. **Small Pita Chips (1-2 bags):** For dipping with hummus or carrots.
13. **Apple Slices (2-3 large bags):** Pack with a squeeze of lemon juice to prevent browning.
14. **Individual Granola Bars or Protein Bars (10-12 bars):** For a quick energy boost.
15. **Dried Fruit (Small bags - e.g., raisins, cranberries):** A small handful is a nice treat.

**Food (Main Meals - Calzone Focus):**

16. **Mini Calzones (Pre-Ordered!):** **This is key.** Order 10 mini calzones (or 10 small regular calzones) from your favorite pizzeria/gourmet pizza place. *Confirm they can deliver/pack them cold in disposable containers.* Ensure fillings include his favorites (pepperoni, mushrooms, onions, cheese, maybe pineapple if he likes it). Pre-ordered = minimal mess, hot food option.
17. **Sandwiches/Wraps (Pre-Made Individual Packets):** 10-12 pre-packaged sandwiches or wraps (e.g., turkey & cheese, ham & cheese, veggie). Easy to grab and eat.

**Dessert (Individual & Easy):**

18. **Mini Cupcakes (12 cupcakes):** Easy to handle, less messy than cake slices. Include frosting.
19. **OR Individual Brownie Packs (10-12 brownies):** Often easier to manage than cake slices.

**Cooler Organization Tip:**

*   **Group by Category:** Use different cooler compartments or pack similar items together.
    *   *Drinks Section:* Water bottles, sodas, juices, sports drinks, milk packs.
    *   *Snack Section:* Cheese, grapes, pretzels, trail mix, carrots/hummus, chips, fruit, granola bars.
    *   *Main Meal Section:* Calzones (in their containers), sandwiches/wraps.
    *   *Dessert Section:* Cupcakes or brownies.
*   **Label Coolers/Sections:** Label one cooler for "Group 1" (5 dads/kids), another for "Group 2," etc., or just label the main sections clearly.

**Key Advantages of This Plan:**

1.  **Grab & Go:** Everything is individually packaged or easily shareable in small containers.
2.  **Minimal Mess:** Avoids plates, utensils, and shared cakes/pies. Pre-ordered calzones come ready-to-eat.
3.  **Healthy Base:** Focuses on water, whole foods (grapes, cheese, carrots, hummus), and minimizes sugary options where possible (though sodas and desserts are included for celebration).
4.  **Meets Preferences:** Includes sugar-free sodas for adults/kids and the star-calzone request.
5.  **Portion Control:** Easy for everyone to take what they want without leftovers cluttering coolers.
6.  **Hydration Focus:** Emphasizes water.

**Remember:**

*   **Pre-Order Calzones:** This is crucial for freshness and minimizing park-day prep.
*   **Ice Packs:** Use plenty of high-quality ice packs to keep everything cold.
*   **Wet Bags:** Include a few large wet bags per cooler for spills (even with minimal mess, accidents happen!).
*   **Communicate:** Let all dads know the plan so they don't bring duplicates or extra mess.
