### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia (BC)
1. **General Rule**: U-turns are permitted unless prohibited by a sign or traffic control device (like a red light).  
2. **Visibility Requirement**: You must ensure you can see **at least 150 meters** in both directions (roughly 1.5 football fields).  
3. **Prohibited Locations**:  
   - Near curves, hills, or intersections.  
   - In blind spots (e.g., behind buildings or trees).  
   - Where it would interfere with traffic.  
4. **Penalties**:  
   - **$160 fine** (as of 2023).  
   - **2 demerit points** on your license.  
   - *Note*: Minor mistakes (e.g., short visibility) may result in warnings, but unsafe U-turns are always ticketed.  

**Pro Tip**: If unsure, pull over first or use a designated turn lane. Safety > fines!

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
For 4 servings, you’ll need:  
**Pasta**:  
- 400g spaghetti (or bucatini)  

**Protein**:  
- 200g guanciale (cured pork cheek; substitute pancetta if needed)  

**Eggs**:  
- 3 large eggs + 3 egg yolks  

**Cheese**:  
- 100g grated Pecorino Romano (or Parmigiano-Reggiano)  

**Seasoning**:  
- Freshly ground black pepper  
- Salt (for pasta water)  

**Optional**:  
- 50g grated Parmigiano-Reggiano (for extra richness)  

### Key Notes:  
- **No cream!** Authentic carbonara uses eggs and cheese.  
- **Cook guanciale** until crispy, then set aside.  
- **Toss pasta** with hot water, then mix with egg/cheese/pepper.  
- **Off-heat**, add guanciale and pepper to avoid scrambling eggs.  

Enjoy your meal! 🍝
