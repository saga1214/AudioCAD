For the vaccination campaign, the ethical debates and public resistance are a huge problem. If we assign a higher weight to public acceptance, its overall score would drop significantly, making it less favorable than the sugar campaign.

So, in that scenario, I would actually choose the campaign to reduce sugar consumption. While vaccination is medically important, the public resistance is a major, predictable, and difficult obstacle. The sugar campaign, while challenging for other reasons, avoids this intense controversy. It really highlights the difficult trade-offs national departments face when deciding on campaigns.
