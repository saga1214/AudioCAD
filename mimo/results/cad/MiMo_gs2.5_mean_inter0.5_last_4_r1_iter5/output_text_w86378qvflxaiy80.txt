    *   **Food Production:** Pollination (often by unique species), soil fertility maintenance, and pest control in agricultural landscapes depend heavily on hotspots.

2.  **Medicinal Resources:** The potential for discovering new medicines from unique plants, fungi, and animals found *only* in hotspots is immense. Extinction means losing potential cures forever. Protecting hotspots safeguards this biological heritage and future medical breakthroughs.

3.  **Foundation for Irreplaceability:** While the unique species themselves are irreplaceable (extinction means they vanish forever), their existence *is* the direct source of these critical ecosystem services and potential medicines. *   **Strategic Conservation:** It reinforces Norman Myers' original concept of strategic conservation – focusing limited resources on areas that deliver the highest conservation return for both biodiversity *and* human well-being.
