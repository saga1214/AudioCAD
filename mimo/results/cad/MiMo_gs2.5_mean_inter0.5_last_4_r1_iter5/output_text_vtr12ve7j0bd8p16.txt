### 🏔️ **Easy & Scenic (Low Effort, High Reward)**

2. **Canmore’s Bow Valley Parkway**  
   - **Adventure:** Walk or bike along the paved path (e.g., **Johnston Canyon Trail**—easy waterfall hike).  
   - **Why it’s great:** Waterfalls, wildflowers, and mountain backdrops. Low-key but photogenic.

### 🧗 **Moderate Hikes (Active & Rewarding)**  
3. **Devils’ Lounge (Canmore)**  
   - **Adventure:** A 3-hour hike through canyons, waterfalls, and emerald pools.  
   - **Why it’s great:** Feels like an adventure film—cool, shaded trails with epic photo ops.  

4. **Johnston Canyon to Moraine Lake (Banff)**  
   - **Adventure:** Walk to the iconic 1028 steps (easy) or hike further to the lake.  
   - **Why it’s great:** Iconic Canadian scenery. Pack a picnic for sunset!  

### 🚂 **Unique Experiences**  
5. **Icefields Parkway (Banff/Jasper)**  
   - **Adventure:** Drive the "Serenity Highway" (scenic route) + stop at **Peyto Lake** or **Athabasca Glacier**.  
   - **Why it’s great:** Minimal hiking, maximum awe. Stop for ice cream at **Bow River Ice Cream**.  

6. **Calgary Zoo Night Walk**  
   - **Adventure:** After dark, see animals up close with special guides.  
   - **Why it’s great:** Whimsical, low-energy, and intimate.  

### 🏕️ **Overnight Options (For a Mini-Getaway)**  
7. **Lake Louise or Moraine Lake (Banff)**

8. **Elk Island National Park (1.5 hrs east)**  
   - **Adventure:** Horseback riding through prairies or zip-lining at **Calgary Adventure Park**.  
   - **Why it’s great:** Wild horses, forests, and a mix of calm and thrill.
