Here are quick, elegant recipes for both cocktails—perfect for your vodka and Fernet!  

### 🍸 **1. Refreshing Vodka Cocktail (Vodka Mixer)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 1 oz raspberry syrup*  
- 1 oz ginger beer  
- Ice  
- *Raspberry syrup: Simmer 1 cup raspberries + ½ cup sugar in 1 cup water for 10 mins, strain, cool, and reduce to ½ cup syrup.  

**Instructions:**  
Shake vodka, lemon juice, and syrup with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Top with ginger beer. Garnish with a lemon twist and fresh raspberries.  

### 🥃 **2. Argentinian-Style Fernet**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 0.75 oz sweet vermouth  
- 0.5 oz honey syrup*  
- 0.25 oz fresh lime juice  
- 1 oz soda water  
- Ice  
- *Honey syrup: Mix 1 part honey + 1 part hot water until dissolved.  

**Instructions:**  
Shake Fernet, vermouth, honey syrup, and lime juice with ice. Strain into a rocks glass over ice. Top with soda water. Garnish with an orange peel twist.  

### 🎯 **Key Notes:**  
- **Soda-Allergic Guest:** Both drinks use non-soda bases (ginger beer/soda water). Label clearly!  
- **Formality:** Coupe/glassware + garnishes (twist/fruit) keep it chic.  
- **Age Mix:** Balanced sweetness/spice avoids overwhelming anyone.
