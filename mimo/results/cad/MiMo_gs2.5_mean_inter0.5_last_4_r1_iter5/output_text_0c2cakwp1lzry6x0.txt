Okay, so you want to install a TV, some hanging planters, and floating shelves. I can definitely give you some general tips on that. First, for the TV, you'll need to measure the wall and make sure it's structurally sound enough to support the weight. Then, you'll need the right kind of mounting hardware, like a wall bracket. For the hanging planters, you can just use hooks or mounting strips that are designed for lightweight items like that. For the floating shelves, you'll need strong anchors, like toggle bolts, to make sure they're secure. The most important thing is to always check the weight limits for whatever you're mounting and to follow the manufacturer's instructions.
