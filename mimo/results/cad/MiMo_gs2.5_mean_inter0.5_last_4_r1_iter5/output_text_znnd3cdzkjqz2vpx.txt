Just a couple more things to grab, and you're all set. I'd really recommend getting a drill with some wall anchors. Habitat cabinets usually come with them, but they're easy to lose. Anchors are way, way better than just screws in drywall because they give you so much more holding power.

Also, make sure you have a few shims on hand – those are the thin plastic wedges you use under cabinets to get them perfectly level.

And of course, safety first! So, work gloves are a good idea, and definitely have someone help you with the drill. You've got this!
