Okay, let's put it all together. Here's a clear, step-by-step breakdown of a standard resume format that should work really well for you.

First, at the very top, you'll have your name in about a fourteen-point font. To the right, or below your name if you prefer, you'll list your contact info vertically. Start with your email in about a nine-point font. On the next line, put your phone number. Below that, your city. It’s a good idea to add icons for the email and phone, but again, I'd suggest removing them just to be safe with those automated application tracking systems. A link to your LinkedIn profile would look great here too. So, the order is perfect what you were thinking.

Right below your name, create a short, powerful Professional Summary. This is just a few sentences that quickly summarize your most relevant experience and career goals.

Next, have a clear Education section. Here you'll list your degree, the school, and your expected graduation date. You can also add details like your honors or any scholarships you received right under that degree. If you have multiple degrees from the same college, just list them as separate entries.

After that, you'll have your Experience section. You can split this into two parts. First, a 'Relevant Experience' section for the older jobs that are most related to the new roles you want. For each job, list the job title, company, the dates you worked there, and then just one or two bullet points summarizing your key achievements. Then, have a separate 'Additional Work History' section for your more recent jobs. For these, just list the job title, company, and the dates. You don't need the detailed bullet points in this section.

It's also a good idea to have a dedicated Skills section, either right after your experience or as a separate part. List out your key skills, maybe even categorizing them, like 'Technical Skills' or 'Soft Skills'.

Finally, you can add an optional but helpful section for Certifications or Awards. Just list the name of the certificate or award and when you received it.

So, to recap the flow: it goes Name, then Contact Info, then a Professional Summary, followed by Education, then your Experience section split into Relevant and Additional Work History, and you can finish with a Skills section and an Awards section. This structure is clean, professional, and really highlights your most relevant qualifications.
