### On Color Coding & Mental Load
*   **Memory vs. Load:** Both! Color coding primarily aids **memory recall** (making categories instantly recognizable) and **reduces cognitive load** during task-switching. Your brain doesn't have to "label" the folder; the color does the work. Think of it as a visual shortcut.

### On Soundscapes & Timing
*   **Optimal Timing:** It's highly **personal preference**, but there *is* a general principle: **switch *before* your focus wanes.** If you notice ambient nature sounds starting to feel monotonous or distracting, a 1-2 minute switch to lo-fi (or vice-versa) can re-engage you. Avoid switching *during* deep work, but use it as a mini-reset if needed. Consistency can also be powerful.

### On Physical Documents & Progress
*   **Environment vs. Chunking:** It's a **combination**. A clean workspace *does* reduce friction and mental clutter, making starting easier. However, **breaking the task into small, manageable chunks** ("File emails from Q3," "Organize tax receipts") is often the *primary driver* of progress. The environment makes the *execution* smoother; chunking makes the *initiation* possible. Start with a small chunk in a tidy space.

### Mediterranean Dinner Suggestions (4 Ideas)
1.  **Simple & Fresh:**
    *   **Grilled Shrimp or Chicken Skewers:** Marinate shrimp or chicken in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, and a touch of chili flakes. Thread onto skewers, grill or pan-sear, and serve with a large Greek salad (cucumber, tomato, red onion, olives, feta) and a lemon-oregano vinaigrette.
2.  **Hearty & Comforting:**
    *   **Lentil Soup with Crusty Bread:** A warm, protein-packed bowl of Mediterranean-style lentil soup (chickpeas, carrots, celery, cumin, paprika) served with a baguette or pita for dipping. Add a side of hummus.
3.  **Light & Veggie-Rich:**
    *   **Roasted Vegetable & Tuna Salad:** Roast zucchini, bell peppers, eggplant, and red onion with olive oil, herbs, and salt. Toss with flaked canned tuna (in olive oil), a sprinkle of feta, olives, and a lemony dressing. Serve on greens or with toasted pita.
4.  **Cozy & Flavorful:**
    *   **Orzo Pasta with Spiced Chickpeas & Roasted Vegetables:** Sauté onions, garlic, and spices (like cumin, coriander, turmeric). Add rinsed orzo pasta and broth, simmering until tender. Stir in roasted vegetables (eggplant, tomatoes, peppers) and chickpeas. Finish with fresh parsley and lemon zest.
