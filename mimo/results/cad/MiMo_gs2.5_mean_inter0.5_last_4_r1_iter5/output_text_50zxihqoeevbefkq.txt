Absolutely! Here’s a **detailed shopping list** based on our menu plan for your New Year's Eve party (20 adults + 4 kids + your household):

---

### **Main Dish**
- **Ham**: 12–14 lbs (for 28–30 people)  
- *Optional*: Honey-baked glaze for extra flavor  
- *Optional*: Sweet potato pie or pecan pie for dessert  

---

### **Appetizers/Finger Foods** (Serves 28–30)
1. **Mini Sliders**:  
   - 8–10 lbs deli ham or turkey  
   - 4–6 lbs cheddar cheese  
   - 4–6 lbs butter lettuce  
   - 4–6 tbsp mayo  
   - 1–2 lbs buns  
2. **Classic Shrimp Cocktail**:  
   - 1 lb large shrimp (peeled & deveined)  
   - 1 bottle cocktail sauce (plus extra for dipping)  
3. **Pigs in a Blanket**:  
   - 1 lb sausage rolls (store-bought or homemade)  
   - 1 lb crescent roll dough  
   - 1–2 cups mustard  

---

### **Sides** (Serves 28–30)
1. **Scalloped Potatoes**:  
   - 4 lbs Yukon Gold potatoes (sliced)  
   - 2 cups shredded cheese (cheddar/mozzarella)  
   - 4 cups milk/cream  
   - 1 cup butter  
   - 1 tsp garlic powder  
2. **Roasted Asparagus**:  
   - 3–4 lbs asparagus  
   - 1 tbsp olive oil  
   - 1 lemon (sliced)  
   - 1 tsp salt  
3. **Roasted Carrots** (Clean Alternative):  
   - 2 lbs carrots (cut into sticks)  
   - 2 tbsp olive oil  
   - 1 tsp salt  
   - 1 tsp black pepper  
   - *No glaze to avoid sticky mess!*  

---

### **Drinks**
1. **Alcoholic**:  
   - 4–6 bottles champagne/ prosecco (for midnight toast)  
   - 2 bottles signature cocktail mix + ingredients (e.g., vodka, soda, fruit)  
2. **Non-Alcoholic**:  
   - 10+ 12oz bottles grape juice (for kids/guests who prefer it)  
   - Sparkling water + fruit cups  
   - Ice (1–2 bags)  
3. **Kids’ Special**:  
   - 4–6 kid-friendly cups (plastic flutes for grape juice toast!)  

---

### **Extras & Essentials**
- **Desserts**:  
  - Red velvet cake (or cupcakes)  
  - Whipped cream + red/green sprinkles  
- **Kid-Friendly**:  
  - Coloring sheets + crayons  
  - Noisemakers/balloon poppers  
- **Host Helpers**:  
  - Disposable containers (for leftovers)  
  - Trash bags + recycling bins  
  - Extra toilet paper + hand sanitizer  

---

### **Final Tips**
1. **Prep Ahead**:  
   - Scalloped potatoes and glazed carrots can be made 1 day ahead.  
   - Ham roasting takes 4–5 hours (start early!).  
2. **Kid Safety**:  
   - Keep hot dishes away from play areas.  
   - Use oven mitts for serving ham.  
3. **Music**:  
   - Create a 4–6 hour NYE playlist (add upbeat classics like "Auld Lang Syne" for countdowns).
