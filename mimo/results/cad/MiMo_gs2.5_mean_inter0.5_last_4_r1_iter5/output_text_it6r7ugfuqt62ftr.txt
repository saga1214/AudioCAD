Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimming, warm weather, diet goals, Celiac considerations, Alcatraz birthday cruise, and now a longer beach vacation for three! You've got a busy but exciting schedule planned. Let's tackle that 4-5 day beach getaway.

Since you're dieting to wear your bathing suit and your daughter has Celiac, places with warm weather, lots of swimming options, dedicated gluten-free dining, and scenic travel are key. Big Sur is perfect for this. It offers stunning coastline, incredible beaches perfect for swimming, luxury resorts with pools, world-class dining (including fantastic GF options), and breathtaking scenery.

Here’s a full itinerary for you, your husband, and your daughter, based on Big Sur:

**Destination:** Big Sur, California

**Why Big Sur?**
*   **Warm & Swimmy:** Coastal climate is mild, especially in summer. Many beaches have calm, warm waters perfect for swimming. Pools at resorts are also great.
*   **Gluten-Free Friendly:** Many high-end resorts and restaurants cater well to gluten-free needs. Dedicated GF bakeries exist.
*   **Luxury & Relaxation:** Offers a wide range of accommodations from boutique inns to luxury resorts.
*   **Stunning Scenery:** Unparalleled Pacific coastline views.
*   **Travel:** Easy drive from Northern California (approx. 5-6 hours).

**Accommodation Suggestions (Luxury Focus):**
1.  **The Post Ranch Inn (Pfeiffer Beach):** The quintessential Big Sur luxury experience. Multiple ocean-view rooms, private pools, spa, fine dining (including excellent GF options), beautiful grounds. A bit more remote, perfect for relaxation.
2.  **Nepenthe (Carmel Highlands - near Big Sur):** While technically in Carmel, it's a short drive to Big Sur beaches and offers iconic ocean views. Stylish, romantic, spa, excellent dining (check GF menu details).
3.  **Big Sur Lodge (Big Sur):** Larger lodge with a variety of rooms, multiple pools, spa, fine dining (ask about GF), central location near many attractions.
4.  **Point Lobos Lodge (Point Lobos):** Beautiful, modern rooms with ocean views, small private beach, excellent dining (known for strong GF offerings), quieter vibe.

**Detailed 4-Day Itinerary:**

*   **Day 1: Arrival & Settling In**
    *   **Morning:** Drive from Northern California to Big Sur. Stop at **Pfeiffer Beach** on the way for a quick swim and stretch on the unique purple sand. Arrive at your accommodation.
    *   **Afternoon:** Check-in, unpack, and immediately head to the **resort's pool** for a relaxing swim and lunch by the water.
    *   **Evening:** Enjoy a welcome dinner at the resort's restaurant. Discuss options beforehand to ensure a suitable gluten-free meal. Relaxed evening.

*   **Day 2: Big Sur Coast & Swimming**
    *   **Morning:** Rent an electric vehicle or car. Drive the iconic **Pacific Coast Highway (PCH)** south from Big Sur towards **Davenport**. Stop at **McWay Falls** ( viewpoints are walkable; swimming is possible but crowds are large, check tides). Stop at **Natural Bridges State Beach** for stunning views and potential tide pools/swimming if conditions are right.
    *   **Lunch:** Pack a picnic and find a scenic spot overlooking the ocean.
    *   **Afternoon:** Arrive in **Davenport**. Explore this charming, artsy town. Stop at **Davenport's Surf Shack** for ice cream (check if they have GF cones/waffles). Take a short walk to the **Davenport Pier** for great photos.
    *   **Swimming:** Head to **Davenport Beach**. This is one of the best beaches in Big Sur for swimming, with calm waters and a sandy shore. Relax and swim.
    *   **Evening:** Enjoy sunset drinks at the resort's bar. Dinner at the resort restaurant (GF option).

*   **Day 3: Ventana Big Sur & Swimming**
    *   **Morning:** Drive south towards **Ventana** area. Stop at **Nepenthe** for breakfast/lunch (if open and you want the iconic view, confirm GF options). Explore **Big Creek** for a short, beautiful hike through redwoods to a waterfall.
    *   **Afternoon:** Arrive in the Ventana area. Visit **McWay Falls** ( viewpoints are walkable; swimming is possible but crowds are large, check tides). Stop at **Natural Bridges State Beach** for stunning views and potential tide pools/swimming if conditions are right.
    *   **Swimming:** Visit **Pfeiffer Beach** again for swimming (the purple sand is unique!). Or, find a quieter spot like **Point Lobos State Natural Reserve** beaches (check access, swimming is possible but can be rocky).
    *   **Evening:** Relax at your resort. Enjoy the ocean views and perhaps a spa treatment. Dinner at the resort restaurant (GF option).

*   **Day 4: Point Lobos & Farewell Swim**
    *   **Morning:** Drive north towards **Point Lobos State Natural Reserve**. This is a must-see for wildlife (sea otters, seals, birds) and incredible coastal scenery. Do a short walk on the **Point Lobos Loop Trail** or **Lovers Point Trail** for breathtaking views.
    *   **Lunch:** Pack a picnic for the trails or enjoy lunch at the **Point Lobos Lodge Restaurant** (highly recommended, beautiful setting, excellent GF options).
    *   **Afternoon:** Return to Big Sur. Take a final relaxing swim at your resort's private beach or the nearby **Big Sur Beach**.
    *   **Evening:** Farewell dinner at the resort restaurant. Enjoy a special gluten-free meal together. Relax and enjoy the ocean views before your drive home.

**Transportation & Travel Tips:**
*   **Car:** Essential. A standard SUV or larger sedan is recommended for luggage and comfort. An electric vehicle is also a great option for the scenic drive.
*   **Getting Around:** Drive your rental car. While shuttles exist between some points, having your own car gives maximum flexibility.
*   **Packing:** Swimwear (pack more than one!), light clothing (dresses, shorts, tank tops), cover-ups, sunscreen (GF if needed), hats, sunglasses, light jacket for cooler evenings, swimsuits for daughter.
*   **Celiac Considerations:** Communicate your needs clearly to restaurants and resorts *when you make reservations*. Don't be afraid to ask questions. Trish's Mini Donuts (Pier 39) is a great GF treat if you stop by on your way up. The resorts themselves are generally very prepared.
*   **Budget:** With a healthy budget, focus on the resort experience – comfortable rooms, spa treatments, fine dining, and maybe a special excursion like a kayak tour (check for GF options/snacks).

This itinerary balances adventure, relaxation, swimming, scenery, and your daughter's dietary needs perfectly. Enjoy your Northern California beach getaway!
