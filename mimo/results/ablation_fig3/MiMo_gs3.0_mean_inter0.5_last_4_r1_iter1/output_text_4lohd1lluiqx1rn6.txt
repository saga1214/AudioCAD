Based on market presence and sedan lineup strength, the **top three car brands known for sedans** are typically:

1.  **Toyota:** Universally recognized for sedans, especially models like the Camry, Corolla, and Avalon (historically). Renowned for reliability and widespread availability.
2.  **Honda:** Another giant with iconic sedans like the Accord and Civic. Known for a strong balance of performance, efficiency, and practicality.
3.  **BMW:** While also famous for SUVs and performance cars, BMW is fundamentally a luxury sedan brand. The 5 Series and 7 Series are globally significant sedans, defining the brand's core identity.

**Honorable Mentions (Strong Sedan Lineups):**

*   **Hyundai:** The Elantra, Sonata, and now the Ioniq 6 sedan are major contenders.
*   **Lexus:** The ES and IS are flagship luxury sedans.
*   **Volkswagen:** The Passat is a major sedan in many markets.
*   **Mercedes-Benz:** The E-Class and C-Class are luxury sedan stalwarts.
*   **Ford:** The Fusion (now discontinued in North America) and Focus (also discontinued) were significant sedans historically.

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Focus:** These brands have historically and currently offer a wide range of sedan models across different segments (economy, mid-size, luxury).
*   **Market Shift:** The sedan market, especially in North America, has been declining compared to SUVs and crossovers. However, these brands remain dominant players in the sedan space globally.
*   **Performance vs. Practicality:** Toyota/Honda offer strong practicality and efficiency, while BMW (and Mercedes/VW/Lexus) lean towards performance and luxury.

So, while other brands have strong sedans, **Toyota, Honda, and BMW** consistently rank as the top three most associated with the sedan segment overall.
