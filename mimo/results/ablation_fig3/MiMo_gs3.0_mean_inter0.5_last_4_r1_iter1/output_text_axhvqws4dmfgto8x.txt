Alright, let's tackle stir-fry stir-up Steve!

The smoke alarm set-boiler incident is watermarked in history, but stir-fry is different. The biggest secret is prep work. Chop everything before you even turn on the heat. That means your onions, peppers, and meat.

Pro tip: brown your ground meat completely before adding any sauce. Don't just dump it in. Get it nice and crispy.

And here's your most important safety tip to avoid a repeat fire incident: use metal utensils only. Wood conducts heat and smoke, and metal does not. A wooden spoon is strictly forbidden territory in the stir-fry kingdom.

Once it's cooked, drain the noodles well and toss them with a tiny bit of oil to prevent sticking. Serve it up and enjoy
