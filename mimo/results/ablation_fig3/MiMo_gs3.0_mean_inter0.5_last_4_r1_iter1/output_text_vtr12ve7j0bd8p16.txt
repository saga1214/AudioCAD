Calgary is a fantastic base for exploring the Canadian Rockies. For an adventurous weekend trip, Banff is the classic choice, but it can get crowded. A great alternative is Lake Louise. It has stunning scenery and is usually less busy than Banff town itself, though the lake does freeze in the winter. If you're looking for something a bit more rugged and wild, you could head to Canmore, which is right on the edge of the Rockies and has a more off-the-beaten-path feel. For a truly unique experience, you could try the Icefields Parkway. It's one of the most spectacular drives in the world, leading to the Columbia Icefield. You can take a drive, walk on the glacier, and see the incredible Athabasca Glacier. If you're up for a real adventure, you could even take the Icefields Skywalk, which is a glass bridge that hangs over the glacier.
