### 1. Audio Setup Fixes
- **Mic Placement**: Speak side-on (logo/text facing you), 6-12 inches away. Angle ~45 degrees downward.
- **Acoustic Treatment**: Hang heavy blankets/moving blankets vertically. Place a soft rug under the mic stand to absorb reflections.
- ** Scarlett Solo Settings**: Use Blue Yeti's cardioid mode (top-down). In Audacity (or Scarlett software), gently boost low-mids (100-300Hz) and cut harsh highs (4-8kHz).

### 2. Vocal Endurance & Health
- **Warm-ups**: 5-min routine: lip trills → humming → tongue twisters.
- **Posture**: Sit upright, mic at eye level.
- **Breathing**: Use diaphragmatic breathing (belly expands on inhale).
- **Hydration**: Sip room-temperature water hourly. *Avoid cold water* – it constricts vocal cords.
- **Breaks**: 1-min breaks every 20 mins. Singing bowls or humming resets your voice.
