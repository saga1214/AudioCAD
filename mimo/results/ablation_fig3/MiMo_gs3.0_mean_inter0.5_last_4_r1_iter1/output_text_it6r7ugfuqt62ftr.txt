Post-birthday warm getaway itinerary for 3 adults (car needed):
Pismo Beach option - 4-night warm weather escape with beach access/swimming.

Pismo Beach 4-Day Plan:

Accommodation: 
Seaside Resort - Spacious suite with kitchenette for dietary control, ocean view. (Book early)

Travel logistics:
Fly into SFO/OAK, rent car (essential for this trip)
Drive ~3 hours to Pismo Beach

Daily activities:
Day 1: Arrival & Beach Relaxation
- Check into resort
- Walk along Pismo Beach
- Sunset at Butterfly Beach
- Dinner at Trish's Mini Donuts (verify GF status) or local seafood place with GF menu

Day 2: Ocean Adventures
- Morning: Paddleboarding at Oceano Dunes State Park (swimming after)
- Afternoon: Visit Morro Bay (Kayak to Morro Rock, see sea otters)
- Evening: Dinner at New York Bagel Co (has GF bagels/sandwiches)

Day 3: Waterfront Fun
- Morning: Rent jet skis or parasail
- Afternoon: Visit Avila Beach (lunch at Java Moon Cafe - GF options)
- Evening: Sunset sail or harbor cruise

Day 4: Nature & Farewell
- Morning: Visit Pismo State Park tide pools
- Afternoon: Relax on beach, pack
- Dinner: Farewell seafood feast at The Pismo牛排馆 (call ahead for GF prep)

Budget notes:
- Accommodation: $250-$400/night (mid-range)
- Food: $100-$150/day for family (GF dining can be pricier)
- Activities: $50-$100/day (rentals, tours)
- Car rental: ~$50/day + gas
- Total estimated: $2000-$3000 (flexible based on choices)

This balances your warm-weather needs, swimming, dietary requirements, and provides exciting outdoor experiences without extreme cold exposure. The beach setting allows easy maintenance of bathing suit readiness!
