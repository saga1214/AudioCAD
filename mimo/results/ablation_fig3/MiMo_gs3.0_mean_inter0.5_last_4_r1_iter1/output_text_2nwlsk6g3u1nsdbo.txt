You're so kind to do this for your friend! For the spicy lentil soup, the ingredients list would include:

1.  **Cooked Brown or Green Lentils**
2.  Vegetable Broth (about 6 cups)
3.  Canned Diced Tomatoes (with their juice)
4.  **Cayenne Pepper** or **Red Pepper Flakes** (to taste for heat)
5.  Onion, diced
6.  Carrots, diced (optional, for extra veggies)
7.  Celery, diced (optional, classic in lentil soup)
8.  Garlic, minced
9.  Bay Leaf (1)
10. Salt and Black Pepper to taste
11. Freshly Ground Black Pepper to taste (before serving)
12. Fresh Parsley or Cilantro, chopped (for garnish, optional)

For the shrimp dishes you asked about earlier:

1.  **Spicy Shrimp Scampi:**
    *   Large Shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails off), about 1 lb
    *   Linguine or Spaghetti
    *   Butter (4-6 tbsp)
    *   Olive Oil (1-2 tbsp)
    *   Garlic, minced (3-4 cloves)
    *   **Red Pepper Flakes** (to taste, for heat)
    *   White Wine (1/2 cup - optional, but recommended)
    *   Lemon Juice (1-2 tbsp)
    *   Lemon Zest (1 tsp)
    *   Fresh Parsley, chopped (for garnish)

2.  **Shrimp Fra Diavolo:**
    *   Large Shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails off), about 1 lb
    *   Crushed Tomatoes (1 can, 28 oz)
    *   Garlic, minced (4-5 cloves)
    *   **Red Pepper Flakes** (to taste, for heat - "diavolo" means devilish!)
    *   Olive Oil (1/3 cup)
    *   Dried Basil (1 tsp)
    *   Dried Oregano (1/2 tsp)
    *   Salt and Black Pepper to taste
    *   Pasta (like spaghetti or fettuccine) for serving
    *   Fresh Parsley, chopped (for garnish)

3.  Cajun Shrimp and Grits:
    *   Large Shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails off), about 1 lb
    *   Quick-Cooking Grits (1 cup)
    *   Water or Broth (4 cups for grits)
    *   **Cajun Seasoning** (to taste - check the salt content if you need to control salt)
    *   Butter (1/4 cup)
    *   Onion, diced
    *   Bell Pepper (any color), diced
    Any other veggies you like (e.g., mushrooms, celery), diced (optional)
    **Hot Sauce** or **Sriracha** (to taste, for extra heat)
    Salt and Black Pepper to taste
    Freshly Ground Black Pepper (to serve)
