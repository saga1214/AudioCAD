First, for the four doors, you'll need four door/window sensors, which will likely cost between sixty and two hundred dollars.

For the seven windows, you'll need seven more door/window sensors, costing between seventy and two hundred and ten dollars.

For one glass break sensor, that's one additional sensor, costing between thirty and one hundred dollars.

For an indoor siren, you'll need one of those, costing between thirty and seventy dollars.

Motion detectors are also highly recommended. For one motion detector, you're looking at a cost of between thirty and eighty dollars.

Next, the central control panel or hub will cost between one hundred and four hundred dollars.

If you get professional installation, there will be an installation fee, which can range from zero to five hundred dollars, depending on the company.

Finally, the monthly professional monitoring fee is typically between twenty and sixty dollars per month.

So, the total upfront cost can range from about four hundred if you install it yourself with basic equipment, to over a thousand with professional installation.
