That's a great attitude to have. Here are a few pointers for your email.

First, for the subject line, keep it neutral. Something like, "Meeting Request to Improve for Upcoming Exam" would work well.

In the email, start with a polite greeting and mention that you scored a 64 on the essay-style exam and that you want to do even better in your future work.

Then, transition to asking for his feedback. You could write, "As essay grading can seem subjective, I'd really appreciate any insight you could offer on what I could have improved upon in my submission. Understanding this feedback would help me prepare more effectively for the next test."

Finally, ask to meet privately. You could say, "Would you be available for a brief chat during your office hours or at another convenient time?"

This frames the whole thing as a proactive student seeking to learn, which should keep the interaction professional and low-pressure.
