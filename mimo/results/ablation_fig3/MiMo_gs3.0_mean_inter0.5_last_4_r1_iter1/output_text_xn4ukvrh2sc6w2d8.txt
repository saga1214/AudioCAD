Here are the classic recipes with precise ingredients and measurements for your Art Deco airport reception. Bartenders will need this for efficient batching:

### Old Fashioned
*   **Ingredients:**
    *   2 oz Bourbon Rye whiskey (e.g., Bulleit, Rittenhouse)
    *   1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup
    *   2 dashes Angostura bitters
    *   Orange twist (for garnish)
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Muddle the sugar cube (or add simple syrup) directly in an Old Fashioned glass.
    2.  Add whiskey and bitters.
    3.  Fill the glass with fresh ice.
    4.  Stir vigorously with a bar spoon until chilled and sugar is dissolved (~30 sec).
    5.  Discard ice.
    6.  Strain into a *new* rocks glass filled with fresh ice.
    7.  Express an orange twist over the drink (squeeze oils into foam), wipe rim (optional), and discard twist.

### Paper Plane
*   **Ingredients:**
    coupe glass
    *   2 oz Gin (London Dry, e.g., Beefeater, Tanqueray)
    *   ¾ oz Aperol
    *   ½ oz Amaro Nonino
    *   ½ oz Fresh Lemon Juice
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Shake gin, Aperol, Nonino, and lemon juice vigorously with ice for 10-15 seconds until well-chilled and slightly frothy.
    2.  Double-strain (using a Hawthorne strainer + fine-mesh strainer) into a prepared coupe glass filled with ice.
    3.  Garnish with an optional cherry or lemon twist.

### Whiskey Sour
*   **Ingredients:**
    *   2 oz Bourbon Rye whiskey
    *   ¾ oz Fresh Lemon Juice
    *   ½ oz Simple Syrup (adjust for egg white preference)
    *   1 egg white (optional, for texture)
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  **Option A (No Egg White):** Combine all ingredients in a shaker. Shake hard with ice for 15-20 seconds until chilled. Double-strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
    2.  **Option B (With Egg White):** Combine all ingredients *plus 1 egg white* in a shaker. **Dry Shake First:** Shake *without ice* 10-15 times until egg white emulsifies and forms a stiff foam. **Wet Shake:** Add ice, shake hard again for 15-20 seconds. Double-strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice. *(Note: Wet shake is traditional but harder for bartenders; dry shake is easier but slightly less silky).*

### Recommended Gin Cocktail: **French 75**
*   **Ingredients:**
    Ready-to-use champagne flute
    *   1.5 oz Gin (London Dry, e.g., Beefeater, Tanqueray)
    *   ¾ oz Lemon Juice
    *   ½ oz Maraschino Liqueur (e.g., Luxardo)
    *   ¼ oz Crème de Cassis (Blackcurrant)
    *   1-2 oz Sparkling Wine/Champagne
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Combine gin, lemon juice, maraschino, and crème de cassis in a shaker with ice.
    2.  Shake hard for 15-20 seconds until chilled.
    3.  Strain into a champagne flute filled with ice.
    4.  Top with sparkling wine/champagne to just below the rim.
    5.  Garnish with lemon twist (express oils, wipe rim).
