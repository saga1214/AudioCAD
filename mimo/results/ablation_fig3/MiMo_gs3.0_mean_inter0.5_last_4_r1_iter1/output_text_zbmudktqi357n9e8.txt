Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time to go would probably be during their fall season, which lines up with ours. I'd say September through November would be perfect. That's when you get all those incredible colors in the Mexican autumns too. But hey, if you're on a budget, late spring or early summer might also show off some beautiful landscapes with fewer crowds. Just make sure to explore rural areas, like Oaxaca or Chiapas, for the most vibrant autumn scenery. Happy painting! 😊🎨
