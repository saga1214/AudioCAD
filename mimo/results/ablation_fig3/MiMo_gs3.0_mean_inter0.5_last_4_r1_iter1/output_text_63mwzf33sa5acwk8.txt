### 💡 **Pro Tips for a Unique Vegas Experience**  
- **Stay Off the Strip**: Hotels like **The Cosmopolitan**, **Aloft**, or **The LINQ** offer better value, fewer crowds, and local vibes.  
- **Free Show Alternatives**:  
  - **Cirque du Soleil** (e.g., *O* at Bellagio) – stunning without Vegas excess.  
  - **Lucky’s** (high-energy cabaret show).  
- **Day Trip Detour**: Visit **Red Rock Canyon State Park** (1hr from Vegas) for hiking before your night in.  
- **Avoid Tourist Traps**: Skip the "free buffets" and overpriced slots. Focus on **craft cocktails** (Angel’s Share, The Chandelier) or **live music**.  

### 🚗 **Van Logistics**  
- **Parking**: Self-parking hotels ($30-50/night). Valet is cheaper but less convenient.  
- **Safety**: Park in well-lit lots. Use apps like SpotHero for discounted rates.
