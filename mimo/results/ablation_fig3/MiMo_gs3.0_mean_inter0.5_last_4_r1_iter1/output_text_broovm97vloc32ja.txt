That's an even better position to be in! If you have illustrations and a manuscript, you absolutely can submit a complete package. You'd just need to format it correctly. You'd use a standard manuscript font, like Times New Roman, and submit a clean, single-page PDF of just the text. Then, you would attach the high-quality image files of your illustrations as a separate file.

This is a huge plus for you because it demonstrates your full vision for the project right from the start. However, it's still crucial to find a publisher who is looking for illustrated picture books, not just text. This is why working with an agent is often the best route, as they can do that research for you and know which publishers are the right fit.
