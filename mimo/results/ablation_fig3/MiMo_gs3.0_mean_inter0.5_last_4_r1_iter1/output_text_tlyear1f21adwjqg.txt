Okay, efficiency can be measured in different ways—cost, scalability, availability, and so on. If we weigh all those factors, solar energy still seems to come out on top overall. Its costs keep falling, it can be used everywhere, and storage is getting better. However, hydropower is extremely reliable and efficient in its own right, especially where infrastructure already exists. Wind is also strong in windy areas. But if I absolutely had to pick just one to represent the single most efficient renewable source, I’d stick with solar, because of its broad applicability, improving cost, and scalability. But the best choice really depends on the specific location and needs.
