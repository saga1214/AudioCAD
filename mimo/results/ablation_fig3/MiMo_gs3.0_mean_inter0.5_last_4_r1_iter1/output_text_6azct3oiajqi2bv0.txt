### 🧵 Best No-Sew Solutions:
1. **Double-Sided Fabric Tape**  
   - *How to use*: Apply tape along the seam where fabric detached. Press firmly to adhere.  
   - *Why it works*: Invisible, holds weight, and won’t melt synthetic linings.  
   - *Where to buy*: Nordstrom Rack or Amazon (search "double-sided sewing tape").

2. **Non-Woven Sewing Adhesive**  
   - *How to use*: Apply a thin line of glue to the back of fabric, press for 1 minute, then clamp overnight.  
   - *Why it works*: Bonds fabric without heat/melting. Use sparingly!  
   - *Tip*: Test on a scrap first.

### ⚠️ Avoid These:
- **Hot glue guns** (can melt polyester/spandex linings).  
- **Strong adhesives** like E6000 (may stiffen fabric).

### 🔧 Quick Fixes (Temporary):
- **Safety pins**: Clasp detached fabric to the lining.  
- **Fabric glue dots** (like Aleene’s Tacky Glue): Press dots onto fabric, hold for 5 mins.  
- **Zip ties**: Loop through fabric loops or seams (not ideal but holds).
