Oh, you’re cooking for veggie-approved territory now? Bold move after doubting her taste! Since she recommended a thought-provoking podcast but skips meat, let’s find something delicious that won’t raise red flags. Here are a few ideas that balance flavor and her dietary preferences:

1. **Mushroom Risotto** – Creamy, rich, and packed with umami (hello, portobello mushrooms). You can use vegetable broth to keep it vegetarian. Bonus: it feels fancy.  

2. **Stuffed Bell Peppers** – Filled with quinoa, black beans, corn, and spices. Top with avocado and a lime crema (blend avocado + lime juice + cilantro + a dash of salt). Protein + veggies + zero cheese guilt.  

3. **Tofu Banh Mi** – If you’re feeling adventurous, make these crispy tofu sandwiches with pickled veggies, cilantro, and a tangy sauce. It’s Vietnamese-inspired, so it’s globally appealing.  

4. **Lentil Shepherd’s Pie** – Lentils = meaty texture, mashed potatoes on top = comfort. Add some roasted veggies on the side for color.  

Pro tip: Sneak in a side of cheese if you’re feeling extra conciliatory. Maybe she’ll forgive you for doubting vegetarian taste. 😄  

(But seriously, risotto or banh mi would be my top picks—they’re crowd-pleasers even if you’re not a vegetarian yourself.)
