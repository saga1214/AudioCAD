### **Tokyo (4 Days)**  
**Art-Focused Highlights:**

### **Osaka (2 Days)**  
**Art-Focused Highlights:**  
- **Day 5:** Osaka Castle (traditional patterns/colors) → Dotonbori (street art + typography inspiration)  
- **Day 6:** Morning workout → Kyoto day trip (see below)  

### **Kyoto (1 Day)**  
**Art-Focused Highlights:**  
- **Morning:** Fushimi Inari Shrine (stylized torii gates) + Kiyomizu-dera (woodblock print shops)  
- **Afternoon:** Nishiki Market (ceramic crafts) → Evening return to Osaka  

### **Nara (1 Day)**  
**Art-Focused Highlights:**  
- **Morning:** Todai-ji Temple (Buddhist sculpture + Tang Dynasty art) → Kasuga Taisha (lantern designs)  
- **Afternoon:** Nara Park (stone carvings) → Evening return to Osaka

### **Practical Tips for Your Art History Presentation**  
1. **Images/Design Research:**  
   - Osaka Castle’s crimson-and-gold palette, Kyoto’s muted woodblock colors, and Nara’s stone carvings provide ready-made slide design inspiration.  
   Japanese art places high value on **negative space, asymmetrical balance, and nature motifs** (cherry blossoms, bamboo).  

2. **Efficiency Hacks:**  
   - Use hotel Wi-Fi each evening for 20 mins: Add images to slide software (Canva/PowerPoint), note layouts from temples you visit.  
   Font ideas: Traditional *Kodachi Gothic* for calligraphy or modern *Noto Sans* for balance.  

3. **Check-Out & Transfers:**  
   - Japanese hotels typically require **10:00–11:00 AM check-out**.  
   - Most hotels offer **free shuttle buses** from major stations (e.g., Nara via JR Nara Line). If not, pre-book taxis via apps like Uber (English-friendly).  

4. **Workout Integration:**  
   - Morning workouts: Park yoga near temples (e.g., Nara’s Nijō Bridge, Kyoto’s Philosopher’s Path).  
   - Evening workouts: Hotel gyms or onsen relaxation sessions.

From Heian-era *Tale of Genji* scrolls to Hokusai’s *The Great Wave*, Japan’s art blends spirituality, nature, and innovation. ukiyo-e prints (like Hiroshige’s *Evening in Kyoto*) are especially visually striking—perfect for slide design examples!
