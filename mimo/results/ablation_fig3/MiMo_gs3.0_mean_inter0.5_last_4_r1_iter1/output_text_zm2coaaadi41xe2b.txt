Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! For ramen, check out Tonkatsu House or Ippudo. If you like pho, Pho 79 or Pho 32 are classics. For a mix of flavors, try Dim Sum Palace for dim sum and dumplings. And for a quick protein bar, I like RXBAR for simplicity or Quest for low-carb options.
