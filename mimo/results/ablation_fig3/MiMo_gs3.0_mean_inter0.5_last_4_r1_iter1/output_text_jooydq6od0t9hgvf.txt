### 🍷 **Argentine Vin de Matambre**  
*(A tangy, vinegary marinade for grilled meats—traditionally for *matambre* sausage)*  

#### **Alcoholic Version** (Wine-Based):  
- *Mix:* 1 cup red wine vinegar + 1 cup dry white wine + 3 minced garlic cloves + 1 tbsp dried oregano + 1 tsp salt + 1/2 tsp black pepper.  
- *Simmer:* 10 mins, then cool. Brush on grilled meat (or veggie skewers for vegetarians).  

#### **Non-Alcoholic Version** (Soda Allergy-Friendly):  
- *Mix:* 1 cup apple cider vinegar + 1 cup sparkling water + 3 garlic cloves + 1 tbsp dried oregano + 1 tsp salt + 1/2 tsp black pepper.  
- *Simmer:* 5 mins, then cool. Serve over grilled veggies or fruit skewers.  

- **Garnish:** Fresh oregano sprigs, olive slices, or lemon wedges.  
- *Why:* Light, tangy, and conversation-starting—fits your mixed-age group’s refined vibe.
