Absolutely! A dehydrator is a great appliance for making some healthy snacks. Some of the best things to dehydrate are fruits, vegetables, and herbs. For example, you could dehydrate apples, bananas, and strawberries to make fruit leather. You can also make fruit chips or vegetable chips from things like zucchini or sweet potato. And of course, you can make your own dried herbs and spices, like rosemary, thyme, or oregano.
