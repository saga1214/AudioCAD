The absolute best bedding for winter chicks is a thick layer of organic sawdust. It's very insulating and traps their body heat. To keep it dry, you should mix in a little bit of wood shavings. 

To avoid attracting rodents, make sure you keep the bedding clean and dry. You'll want to change it out regularly, and you can add a layer of clean wood shavings on top to help control any moisture and odors.
