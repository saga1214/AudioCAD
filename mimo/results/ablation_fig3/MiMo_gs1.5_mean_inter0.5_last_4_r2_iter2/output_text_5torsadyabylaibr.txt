A classic Thanksgiving dessert that's also relatively easy is a pumpkin pie. You can usually find a good canned pumpkin to use, and a store-bought crust will save you a lot of time.

For a main dish suggestion, a simple green bean casserole is a great choice. It's just green beans, cream of mushroom soup, and some fried onions on top. It's a very traditional and easy-to-make dish.
