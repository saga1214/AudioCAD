That's the spirit! Since you're not into sweet/chocolatey snacks, here are some savory and interesting vending machine ideas to fill that new coffee table:

### Savory Stars (Your Top Picks!)
1. **Beef Jerky or Biltong**: High-protein, chewy, and satisfyingly savory.  
2. **Cheese & Crackers**: Opt for flavored crackers (like everything bagel or parmesan) and sharp cheddar or gouda.  
3. **Pretzels**: Classic salted twists, mini pretzels, or pretzel rods.  
4. **Roasted Nuts**: Almonds, cashews, or peanuts (watch for added sugar/salt).  
5. **Veggie Sticks + Hummus**: Carrots, cucumbers, bell peppers, or radishes.  
6. **Sushi Rolls**: Some machines have pre-packaged rolls (e.g., California roll).  
7. **Tuna or Chicken Salad Packets**: With crackers or bread for dipping.  
8. **Spicy Tots or Fries**: Crispy, savory, and great for dipping in ranch or ketchup.  

### Sweet But Not Chocolatey (If You Want a Treat)
9. **Gummy Worms or Gummy Bears**: Tangy, chewy, and fun.  
10. **Licorice**: Black licorice, red vines, or chewy twists.  
11. **Rice Krispie Treats**: Marshmallowy and slightly sweet.  
12. **Popcorn**: Butter-flavored or cheese-flavored kernels.  

### Fun Combos
- **"Road Trip" Plate**: Pretzels + jerky + hummus + gummy bears.  
- **"Late-Night Movie" Setup**: Spicy tots + popcorn + cheese + nuts.  
- **"Indulgent Feast"**: Sushi + gummy worms + roasted almonds.  

### Pro Tips
- **Rotate Your Snacks**: Keep things fresh by swapping items every few days.  
- **Hydration Station**: Add a water bottle or sparkling water to your table!  
- **Watch the Salt**: Vending machine snacks can be salty—balance with fresh veggies or fruit.  

Enjoy your snack arsenal! Your coffee table is now the perfect centerpiece for snacks, projects, and maybe even that giant chocolate bar math later 😄.
