Great questions—let's break it down:

### 1. **Color-Coding Icons**  
   - **Memory vs. Mental Load**: Color-coding primarily **reduces mental load** by creating visual shortcuts. For example:  
     - 🔴 *Red folders* for "Urgent" tasks (quick recognition).  
     - 🟢 *Green folders* for "Reference" (calmer association).  
     - ⚫️ *Black folders* for "Archive" (neutral, low-stakes).  
   - **Avoid Overdoing It**: 3–4 colors max. Too many colors = cognitive overload. Start with a 2-color system (e.g., "Work" vs. "Personal") and expand if needed.

### 2. **Sound-Playing Strategy**  
   - **Personal Preference Matters Most**, but data suggests:  
     - Switching sounds **every 45–60 minutes** can refresh focus (e.g., lo-fi → rain sounds → ambient).  
     - For deep work, **consistent sound** (e.g., brown noise or café chatter) may reduce distractions better than frequent changes.  
     - **Try**: Lo-fi for 45 mins → nature sounds for 15 mins → repeat. Track your focus with apps like [Focus@Will](https://focusatwill.com/) to experiment.

### 3. **Physical Document Filing**  
   - **When to Tackle It**:  
     - **Weekends** = fresh mindset, but risk procrastination.  
     - **Quiet weekdays** = momentum from daily routines.  
   - **Key to Progress**:  
     - **Chunk it**: File 1 category/day (e.g., "invoices," "contracts").  
     - **Environment > Timing**: A clean workspace (even 10 mins of tidying) boosts motivation more than timing. Do it when energy dips (e.g., 3 PM slump).

### 4. **Timer-Based Breaks**  
   - **Pomodoro Technique (25 mins work / 5 mins break)** works for ~60% of people.  
   - **Gimmick or Genius?**: It’s **neither**—it’s a *framework*. If breaks feel rushed, try:  
     - 50/10 (90-min focus blocks) for deep work.  
     - 25/5 for routine tasks.  
   - **Pro Tip**: Breaks should be *unplugged* (no screens!). Stretch, hydrate, or gaze out a window.
