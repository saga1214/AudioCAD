### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia (General Guidelines)
1.  **Prohibited at Intersections:** You cannot legally make a U-turn at an intersection with a traffic control signal (traffic light, stop sign, or yield sign) unless a sign specifically authorizes it.
2.  **Visibility Requirement:** You must be able to see at least 150 meters (approximately 152 meters or 15 football fields) in both directions before and after the turn. This is the key "150-meter" rule you mentioned.
3.  **No Obstruction:** A U-turn is illegal if it interferes with traffic, occurs near curves, hills (where visibility is limited), or on railroad tracks.
4.  **No Signs = Often Prohibited:** If there is no sign *allowing* U-turns, it is generally illegal.
5.  **Penalties:**
    *   **First Offense:** Typically a ticket costing around **$100-$150** (exact amount can vary slightly).
    *   **Repeat Offenses:** Penalties increase, including higher fines and potential license points (which affect insurance premiums).
    *   **Dangerous Driving:** If the illegal U-turn is deemed dangerous, the fine could be much higher, potentially reaching $600.

**Key Takeaway:** When in doubt, **don't do the U-turn**. Look for signs allowing it, ensure you have ample visibility, and choose intersections where you can see clearly in both directions. Designated U-turn lanes or designated U-turn zones are the safest and easiest options.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients (Serves 2-3)
Here's a classic, authentic recipe focusing on the core ingredients:

1.  **Pasta:** 400g (14 oz) Dried Spaghetti (or Buckwheat Tagliatelle)
2.  **Pancetta or Guanciale:** 150g (5 oz) diced Pancetta (Italian cured pork belly) or Guanciale (cured pork cheek - traditional). *Bacon is a common substitute but flavor differs.*
3.  **Eggs:** 3 large Eggs + 1 large Egg Yolk (whisked together)
4.  **Cheese:** 100g (about 1 cup) finely grated Pecorino Romano cheese (traditionally used for its sharp, salty flavor) + 50g (about ½ cup) finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese (for a milder, nuttier note). *Pecorino is essential; Parmigiano is optional but recommended.*
5.  **Black Pepper:** Freshly ground black pepper to taste (generous amount!)
6.  **Salt:** For pasta water
7.  **(Optional but Recommended):** Reserved Pasta Water (a splash to adjust consistency)

**Key Technique Notes:**
*   **No Cream:** Authentic carbonara uses no cream.
*   **High Heat:** The sauce is emulsified by the heat from the pasta and starchy pasta water.
*   **Quick Mixing:** The eggs must be added off direct heat to prevent scrambling.

**Recipe Steps (Brief):**
1.  Cook pasta in well-salted water until *al dente*. Reserve at least 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
2.  In a large bowl, whisk together the 3 eggs, 1 egg yolk, grated Pecorino, Parmigiano (if using), and a *very* large amount of black pepper.
3.  In a separate pan, cook the diced pancetta/guanciale over medium heat until crispy. Remove from pan, leaving the rendered fat.
4.  **Crucial Step:** Turn off the heat under the pancetta pan. Add the hot, drained pasta to the pan with the pancetta and fat. Toss vigorously to coat.
5.  **Immediately** remove the pasta from the heat source. Add the egg/cheese mixture and a splash of pasta water. Toss *vigorously* and continuously (over the residual heat) until the sauce thickens and becomes creamy and glossy (it should not be scrambled).
6.  Serve immediately in warmed bowls, topped with extra grated Pecorino and more black pepper.
