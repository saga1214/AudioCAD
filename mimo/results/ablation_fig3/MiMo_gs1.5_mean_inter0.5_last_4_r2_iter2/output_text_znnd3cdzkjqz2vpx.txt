That's fantastic that you're taking the safer approach! You've got the basics down, but here are a few more things to have on hand to make the installation go smoothly.

First, you'll definitely need a stud finder that has a "screw mode" on it. This is a lifesaver because it shows you exactly where to place the screwdriver bit without having to drill into drywall. You'll also want some wall anchors, just in case you can't find studs in certain spots, and a utility knife to trim any excess shims or packaging. 

Oh, and a good pair of gloves will protect your hands from any splinters. Most importantly, please make sure you have someone holding a level against the cabinets while you're tightening the screws to make sure everything stays perfectly straight. 

You're all set for a safe and successful installation!
