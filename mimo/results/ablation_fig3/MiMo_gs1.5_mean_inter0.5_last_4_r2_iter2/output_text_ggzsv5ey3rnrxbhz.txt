While there's no magic 'detox' that will remove them all, your body does have a way of breaking them down, though it's a slow process. The most effective thing you can do is to dramatically reduce your exposure in the first place.

So, here's a list of things to avoid to minimize your intake. First, non-stick cookware, like those with Teflon coatings. Second, grease-resistant food packaging, such as fast-food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags. Third, products labeled as stain-resistant or waterproof, like carpets and certain clothing. Fourth, some cosmetics and even certain types of dental floss. And fifth, since they can be in tap water, using a certified water filter is a key step.

It's about reducing the new amount you're taking in, which helps your body manage what's already there.
