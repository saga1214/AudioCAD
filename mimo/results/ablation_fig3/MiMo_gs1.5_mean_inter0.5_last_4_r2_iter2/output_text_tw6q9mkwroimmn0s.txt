What a fantastic ocean-themed anniversary idea! Here are some delicious meal and dessert options tailored for your fiancée's dietary needs while celebrating your shared love of mango and spice:

**5 Ocean-Themed Meal Ideas:**

1.  **Spicy Shrimp & Mango Stir-Fry:** Toss shrimp (peeled & deveined) with colorful veggies (bell peppers, snap peas, carrots, broccoli). Add a vibrant sauce like a mix of lime juice, ginger, garlic, chili flakes, and a touch of honey. Finish with fresh mango chunks and cilantro. Serve over brown rice or cauliflower rice.
2.  **Scallop & Mango Salad with Citrus Vinaigrette:** Marinate sea scallops in olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon, garlic, and a pinch of chili powder. Grill or pan-sear. Serve atop a refreshing salad of mixed greens, cucumber, avocado, and mango. Drizzle with a light vinaigrette made from citrus juice, olive oil, honey, and a tiny bit of chili oil.
3.  **Mango-Coconut Grilled Fish:** Choose firm white fish like cod or mahi-mahi. Marinate in a mix of coconut milk, lime juice, ginger, garlic, turmeric, and a dash of fish sauce or soy sauce (check sugar content). Grill or bake until flaky. Serve with a fresh mango salsa (diced mango, red onion, cilantro, lime juice) and a side of steamed jasmine rice.
4.  **"Tuna Tartare" with Mango Salsa & Avocado:** Finely dice tuna (high-quality, skip the oil/sugar). Mix with finely diced red onion, cilantro, lime juice, a touch of olive oil, and a pinch of chili flakes. Serve on lettuce cups or crackers topped with a dollop of avocado and a spoonful of mango salsa.
5.  **Seared Scallops with Mango-Chili Glaze:** Sear sea scallops in a hot pan with butter or olive oil until golden and just cooked through. Prepare a glaze by simmering mango puree (blended fresh or canned) with a little honey, lime juice, and red chili paste or flakes until slightly thickened. Spoon generously over the scallops. Serve with roasted sweet potato wedges.

**5 Sweet Ocean-Themed Desserts:**

1.  **Mango & Coconut Key Lime Pie (Diabetic-Friendly):** Use a graham cracker crust (or nut crust for lower sugar). Fill with a light, tangy key lime curd (made with eggs, lime juice, zest, and light coconut milk) and layer generously with fresh mango chunks. Top with toasted coconut flakes.
2.  **Mango Salsa "Panna Cotta":** Make a creamy panna cotta using coconut milk, a small amount of sweetener (like erythritol or stevia), vanilla, and a touch of gelatin. Layer it with a vibrant mango salsa (similar to the salad but thicker). Chill until set.
3.  **Grilled Pineapple & Coconut Sorbet with Lime:** Serve a scoop of high-quality lime sorbet (often lower sugar than mango) topped with grilled pineapple slices (brushed with a little honey/lime mix) and toasted shredded coconut. The grilled pineapple adds a lovely caramelized note.
4.  **Tropical Fruit "Parfait" with Ginger Syrup:** Layer Greek yogurt (unsweetened) or coconut yogurt with a mix of fresh berries, mango chunks, and kiwi. Drizzle lightly with a simple ginger syrup (simmer ginger slices in a little water and honey/sweetener until slightly thickened). Top with toasted coconut.
5.  **Mango & Chili Chocolate Mousse:** Blend high-quality dark chocolate (70%+) with unsweetened cocoa powder, a small amount of sweetener, whipped cream (or coconut cream), and a *very* tiny pinch of cayenne or chili powder for a subtle warmth. Fold in blended fresh mango puree for sweetness and color. Chill and serve in glasses topped with a mango slice and mint.

**Tips for Your Ocean Night:**

*   **Decor:** Use blue and green tablecloths, seashells as napkin holders, blue candles, starfish accents, and maybe a small "treasure" bowl with chocolate coins or candies.
*   **Spice Level:** Adjust the spice in the meals based on how adventurous you both feel that night! Start mild and add heat gradually.
*   **Mango Magic:** Incorporating mango into both mains and desserts ties the theme together beautifully and plays to your shared love.
*   **Drinks:** Sparkling water with lime, a light white wine, or a simple mocktail like fresh limeade would be perfect.

Enjoy your incredibly romantic and delicious ocean-themed anniversary dinner! It sounds like a wonderful celebration of your love and shared tastes.
