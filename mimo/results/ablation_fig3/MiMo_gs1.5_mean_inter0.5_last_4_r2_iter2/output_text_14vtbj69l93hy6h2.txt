No problem! Every dog has different preferences—here are some enticing, vet-friendly treat options for Maisy, plus training tips:

### 🐶 Treat Ideas:
1. **Boiled chicken breast** (small cubes)  
   *Safe if vet-approved*—remove skin/fat.  
2. **Pumpkin puree** (100% pure, no spices)  
   Sweet, low-calorie, and dog-safe.  
3. **Dehydrated sweet potato chips**  
   Crunchy and mild—great for texture lovers.  
4. **Xylitol-free peanut butter**  
   *Tiny* amounts (1/4 tsp at a time) for high-value rewards.  
5. **Fish-shaped treats**  
   Often higher-value than plain shapes.  

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Size matters:** Cut treats into pea-sized pieces for quick rewards.  
- **Timing is key:** Reward the *exact* moment she performs the trick.  
- **Start simple:** "Sit" or "High Five" (touch nose to paw) are great starters.  
- **Short & sweet:** 2-3 training sessions/day (5 mins each) beats one long session.  
- **Praise + treats:** Use excited verbal cues ("Yes! Good girl!") to build enthusiasm.  

### 🛑 If treats still don’t work:
- Try **"snuffle mats"** with kibble hidden inside—keeps her focused.  
- **Chew toys** (like frozen Kongs) can be rewarding during training breaks.  

Remember: Consistency and patience are everything! Maisy’s eagerness to please will shine through soon. 🌟
