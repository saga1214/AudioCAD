Okay, so you want to surprise your daughter with a new gaming console. That's a great idea! Here are five of the most popular ones right now.

First, there's the Nintendo Switch. It's great for families because it's portable, so she can play on the go or on the TV, and it has a huge library of games that are perfect for two-player co-op.

Next is the PlayStation 5. It's considered the best for high-end single-player games with amazing graphics, and it has some fantastic multiplayer options.

Then there's the Xbox Series X or S. It's a great choice, especially if you're already in the Microsoft ecosystem, as it plays games from the entire Xbox library. It also has robust online multiplayer features.

Another one to consider is the Steam Deck. It's a powerful handheld PC that can play thousands of games from the Steam store, including many PC exclusives, making it super versatile.

Finally, there's the Nintendo Switch Lite. It's the smaller, lighter handheld-only version of the Switch, which is great if you mostly want to play on the go.

So, to quickly recap: The Nintendo Switch is probably the best all-around family choice. The PlayStation 5 is top-tier for single-player games. The Xbox is great for its backward compatibility. The Steam Deck is a powerhouse handheld PC, and the Switch Lite is the best for pure handheld gaming. You really can't go wrong with the Switch for a family-friendly experience!
