Of course! Let me give you a full rundown of ideas for your little princess. 

For homemade treats, you can make frozen pumpkin bites by mixing canned pumpkin with a little peanut butter and freezing it. Oat and peanut butter cookies are also great; just mix oats with peanut butter and bake until firm. Sweet potato sticks are easy, you just bake thin slices of sweet potato until tender and then freeze them. And for something simple, you can always offer small pieces of cooked chicken or carrot sticks. 

For store-bought brands, you could try Blue Buffalo, Wellness, Zuke's, Greenies for dental health, or Merrick. It's always a good idea to check the ingredient list for things like xylitol or soy, which can be harmful. 

And for training, remember to use small, soft treats during practice sessions. 

Enjoy giving your corgi princess all these delicious options!
