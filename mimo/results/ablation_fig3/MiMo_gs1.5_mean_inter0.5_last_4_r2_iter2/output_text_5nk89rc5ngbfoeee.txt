An end-of-year celebration at the park with a BBQ and outdoor activities is a fantastic idea—it’s memorable, inclusive, and reinforces the fun Brownies are known for. Here’s how to make it work smoothly and stress-free:

### 🎉 **End-of-Year Celebration Plan**
1. **Location & Setup**  
   - **Park BBQ**: Choose a shaded pavilion with picnic tables and easy parking. Bring a portable grill (rental or borrowed) and setup a simple "BBQ station" with pre-cooked burgers, hot dogs, or veggie skewers.  
   - **Rain Plan**: Have a backup indoor venue (school cafeteria, community center) and store gear (tents, chairs) in a waterproof container.

2. **Food & Snacks**  
   - **BBQ**: Keep it simple: pre-cooked proteins (no grilling stress while pregnant!), buns, chips, and sides like coleslaw. Offer dairy-free dips (vegan ranch) and chips/dip for the snack-loving girls.  
   - **Ice Cream Sundaes**: Add a post-meal ice cream bar with sundaes—satisfies the craving without heaviness.  
   - **Parent Contributions**: Ask parents to bring a dish to share (e.g., cookies, fruit salad) to spread the cost and effort.

3. **Activities**  
   - **Relay Races**: Classic Brownie fun! Set up:  
     - **Three-Legged Race** (team-building)  
     - **Wheelbarrow Race** (endurance)  
     - **Water Balloon Toss** (cooling off)  
     - **Scavenger Hunt** (for badges—e.g., "find a feather, a pinecone, a smooth rock").  
   - **Simple Crafts**: A "Remember Our First Year" bracelet station using yarn or pipe cleaners.  
   - **Trophies**: Award "Most Helpful," "Best Team Player," or "Biggest Explorer" badges (handmade or printed) for effort over winning.

4. **Involve Parents**  
   - Send a **volunteer sign-up sheet** for setup, activity supervision, or cleanup.  
   - Parents can join races or help with crafts—make it a family affair!

### 💡 **Key Tips for Success**
- **Budget**:  
  - Troop dues ($20–$50/year) can cover BBQ supplies, trophies, and crafts.  
  - Parents cover food costs via contributions or a small fee ($10–$15 per family).  
- **Pregnancy-Friendly**: Delegate setup/cleanup to parents. Pre-pack snacks for the girls.  
- **Flexibility**: If energy is low, pivot to a movie picnic or karaoke in the pavilion.  

### ✅ **Why This Works**
- **Fun + Achievement**: Combines teamwork (races), creativity (crafts), and celebration (BBQ).  
- **Inclusive**: Addresses dietary needs, activity preferences, and budget constraints.  
- **Memorable**: Builds camaraderie and leaves the girls excited for next year!  

You’ve got this! By starting small and leaning on parents, you’ll create a joyful tradition without burning out. 💕
