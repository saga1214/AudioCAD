Okay, she's finally getting a break from the chaos and wants to cook something to relax. That's a great idea. I'll give her some simple, comforting dinner ideas she could grab at a grocery store.

First, a quick pasta dish. She can get penne and a jar of marinara sauce. She can add some spinach for greens, maybe some pre-sliced chicken or sausage if she wants protein. It's quick and easy.

Second, a stir-fry. She could grab some rice noodles or rice, a bag of mixed veggies, and some tofu or chicken. The sauce is simple—soy sauce, garlic, ginger. It's fresh and healthy.

Third, a hearty soup. She can make a big pot with some canned beans, a can of diced tomatoes, and some frozen vegetables. Add some lentils for protein. It's warm and filling.

Fourth, a simple fajita bowl. Get some ground beef or chicken, peppers and onions, and a bag of tortilla chips. She can warm up the chips and put the beef and veggies on top with some cheese. It's customizable and fun.

Fifth, a grilled cheese and tomato soup. She can make the soup with canned tomatoes, onion, and garlic. It's super comforting and nostalgic. She can make a big grilled cheese sandwich to go with it.

I hope one of these sounds good and helps her unwind a little. She really deserves a relaxing vacation.
