That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour is perfect for unwinding. Here are some great daytime activities near your resort and around Red Hook:

1.  **Snorkel in Coki Beach:** Just a short walk from Secret Harbour, this beautiful bay is renowned for its calm, clear water teeming with colorful fish, turtles, and vibrant coral gardens. It's ideal for beginners.
2.  **Hike the Reef Trail:** A short, easy walk from Secret Harbour leads to this trail on the eastern side of the island. It offers stunning views of the turquoise waters and the Coki Beach area below.
3.  **Explore Cruz Bay:** Take a stroll through this charming village. Browse the unique boutiques, art galleries, and bookshops lining the main street. Grab a coffee or a light lunch at one of the many cafes.
4.  **Rent Bikes or Scooters:** Red Hook is a perfect area to explore by bike or scooter! Rent one and cruise along the waterfront paths, discovering hidden beaches, local restaurants, and the bustling atmosphere.
5.  **Relax on Other Local Beaches:**
    *   **The Reef Beach:** Another beautiful spot within a short walk from Secret Harbour, known for its clear water and gentle slope.
    *   **Laurita Bay:** A bit further, but easily accessible by bike or taxi. This protected bay is great for swimming and snorkeling, and it's home to the popular Laurita Bay Restaurant.
    *   **The Baths (Virgin Islands National Park):** While a short drive from Cruz Bay, this iconic attraction features hundreds of unique, often-enclosed rock formations, tide pools, and sparkling turquoise water. It's a must-see, and the water is very shallow and calm, ideal for your brother.
6.  **Visit the St. John Historical Society Museum:** Located in the historic Cruz Bay Hotel, this small museum provides fascinating insights into the island's rich history, from indigenous inhabitants to its development as a British colony and US territory.
7.  **Enjoy a Pina Colada or Local Food:** Red Hook has fantastic spots for a classic, refreshing pina colada (Duffy's, The Longboard, or even the Secret Harbour bar) or some delicious local food – think fresh seafood, Caribbean-style jerk chicken, or tropical fruit smoothies.
8.  **Visit the Cruz Bay Rum Distillery:** If you like rum, a tour of this local distillery is a unique cultural experience and a great way to cool off.

**Pro Tip:** The weather is usually best in the morning and early afternoon. If you're staying at Secret Harbour, you're perfectly positioned to head to Coki Beach or explore Red Hook right from your doorstep! Enjoy your week – boating and beaches on St. John are truly special.
