You're absolutely right to consider that. Both succulents and the bird's nest snake plant are very light. The succulents on your table will be in small pots, so they'll weigh just a few pounds at most. The snake plant on the floor would be in a small pot, maybe weighing one or two pounds when it's full of soil. They are definitely easy enough to carry up five flights of stairs, especially since you'll be taking them home at the end of your stay. You can just pack them in a box when you leave.
