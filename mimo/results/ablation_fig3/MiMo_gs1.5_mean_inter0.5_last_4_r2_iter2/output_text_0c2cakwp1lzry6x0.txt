Here's a step-by-step guide for installing those key items in your new apartment, keeping noise rules and landlord restrictions in mind:

**1. Mounting the TV:**
    *   **Plan & Measure:** Decide the *exact* location (center on the wall, eye-level for viewing). Measure the wall width between studs. Most mounts require anchors into *studs* for heavy TVs.
    *   **Find Studs:** Use a stud finder. Mark where each stud is located. If you don't find a stud, use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for the TV's weight (check manufacturer specs!).
    *   **Mark Mount Holes:** Use a level and pencil to mark the precise drilling points for the TV mount's mounting screws.
    *   **Install Mount:** Securely attach the TV mount bracket to the wall using the marked screw holes and appropriate anchors. Ensure it's level.
    *   **Install TV:** Mount the TV onto the bracket using the included hardware (usually screws or bolts). Tighten securely.
    *   **Key Tip:** Use a stud finder with a "TV Mounting" mode for easier installation. Ensure the wall is flat behind the TV if possible.

**2. Hanging Planters:**
    *   **Choose Planter & Hook:** Select planters and matching hooks/brackets. Ensure the hook's weight rating is significantly *higher* than the planter(s) + soil + water (it's heavy!).
    *   **Find Ceiling Joist:** Use a stud finder set to "Joist" mode. Find the exact center of the joist running above your wall. Mark it.
    *   **Mark Hook Hole:** Use a level and pencil to mark the exact center of the hook bracket's mounting holes directly above the joist center.
    *   **Install Hook:** Securely attach the hook bracket to the joist using long screws. Ensure it's level.
    *   **Hang Planter:** Place the planter into the hook. Ensure it sits snugly and the hook is holding weight without sagging.
    *   **Key Tip:** Place planters near windows for best light. Use heavy-duty hooks rated for *at least* 2-3 times the planter's weight to be safe. Double-check the hook is anchored securely into the joist.

**3. Installing Floating Shelves:**
    *   **Plan Layout:** Decide the shelf height, depth, and spacing. Sketch it out. A staggered arrangement can look great. Ensure spacing is even.
    *   **Measure & Mark:** Measure the exact spot for each shelf. Use a level to draw a faint pencil line where the top/bottom edge of the shelf will sit. Mark the *exact* center point of each shelf location.
    *   **Find Studs:** Use a stud finder to locate studs *above* your planned shelf locations. Mark where each stud is.
    *   **Mark Shelf Mounting Holes:** Use a level and pencil to mark the precise drilling points for the shelf bracket's mounting screws directly above the marked shelf center lines.
    *   **Install Brackets:** Securely attach the shelf brackets to the wall using the marked screw holes and appropriate anchors (often just into the drywall). Ensure brackets are level and flush.
    *   **Install Shelf:** Place the shelf onto the brackets. Check it's level and snug. You might need shims if it's slightly off.
    *   **Key Tip:** Use heavy-duty floating shelf brackets rated for the weight of the items you plan to put on the shelf. Ensure the shelf is level and the brackets are flush against the wall. Check for wobble.

**General Installation Tips:**

*   **Tools:** You'll likely need a power drill, screwdriver, level, tape measure, pencil, stud finder (with joist mode if possible), and potentially anchors.
*   **Safety First:** Always wear safety glasses when drilling. Use a stud finder to avoid hitting electrical wires or pipes.
*   **Weight Matters:** **This is crucial.** Never assume a hook or bracket is strong enough just because it looks sturdy. Always check the weight rating for the item you're hanging (TV, planter, shelf) and ensure the mounting hardware meets or exceeds it significantly. Heavy TVs need studs.
*   **Plan Ahead:** Measure twice, drill once. Mark everything clearly before you start drilling.
*   **Check Landlord Rules:** While decorating is allowed, ensure your drilling is within reason and doesn't cause damage. Using anchors into drywall is usually fine, but avoid drilling into load-bearing walls without permission.
*   **Dust & Debris:** Cover the wall area with painter's tape or cardboard to catch dust when drilling.

By following these specific steps and paying close attention to weight ratings and stud location, you can safely and effectively install your TV, planters, and shelves to create a beautiful and functional studio apartment! Good luck with your decorating project!
