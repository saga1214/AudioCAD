Before filming, here’s what you can do:

First, get permission from the place you’re performing, like the assisted living facility. Make sure they’re okay with you filming and posting it later.

Second, plan out your set. It doesn’t have to be fancy. You could use a table or a small stage. Just make sure it’s in a good spot with good lighting—natural light works best. You can even use a simple curtain or blanket to block out any distracting background noise.

Third, for your phone setup, prop it up on a tripod or a stack of books. You’ll want it to be stable. Then just make sure you turn on the recording feature on your phone.

Fourth, tell the audience that you’re being filmed. Kids and adults might act differently if they know they’re on camera, so just let them know.

Fifth, rehearse a little if you can. You’ll want to make sure your puppet moves and your voice are clear for the camera.

And finally, after the show, review the footage. See how it looks, and maybe ask a friend or family member for feedback.

That’s really it! Just keep it simple and have fun.
