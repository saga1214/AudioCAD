Here's a clear, segment-specific packing list based on your needs:

### **20-Mile Night Segment (Mid-30s, Hilly, Night Running)**
1.  **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking shirt & shorts (avoid cotton).
2.  **Mid-Layer:** Fleece jacket or lightweight insulated jacket (for warmth).
3.  **Outer Layer:** Windproof/water-resistant jacket (for cold, damp air).
4.  **Headwear:** Warm hat (to keep ears warm) + buff/neckerchief (for neck/wrists).
5.  **Hands:** Trail gloves or mittens (wind chill makes hands cold).
6.  **Lighting:** **Headlamp (essential!) + Extra Batteries** (handheld light is backup).
7.  **Footwear:** Sturdy trail running shoes with excellent grip.
8.  **Small Pack/Vest:** To carry layers as you warm up or cool down.
9.  **Socks:** Extra pair of wool/synthetic socks (change if sweaty).

### **12-Mile Final Segment (Likely Daytime, Still Hilly)**
1.  **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking shirt & shorts (keep dry).
2.  **Mid-Layer:** Light fleece or thin vest (for cooler morning temps).
3.  **Outer Layer:** Light rain jacket or windbreaker (weather can change).
4.  **Headwear:** Cap or sun hat (if sun is out).
5.  **Lighting:** **Headlamp (still useful for shade/low light)** + Extra Batteries.
6.  **Footwear:** Sturdy trail running shoes with grip.
7.  **Small Pack/Vest:** (Optional, but good for carrying a water bottle).
8.  **Sun Protection (Optional but Recommended):** Sunglasses (UV protection), sunscreen (if sun is out).

### **Items for Both Segments**
*   **Hydration:** Water bottle (or small hydration pack) - *Aid stations exist, but carry your own for convenience*.
*   **Layers:** Bring the base + mid-layer from the night segment. Add the light outer layer from the day segment if needed.
*   **Flexibility:** Pack layers separately so you can easily add/remove as needed at aid stations.

**Key Takeaways:**
*   **Night is Key:** Headlamp, warm hat, buff, insulated jacket, and extra batteries are non-negotiable for the 20-miles.
*   **Day is Lighter:** Focus on sun protection, a light outer layer, and a cap.
*   **Aid Stations Help:** You won't need *everything* at once, but having layers in your pack lets you swap as conditions change. The small pack/vest is crucial for carrying extras easily.
*   **Test Gear:** Try your layers on before the race to ensure comfort and fit.

This list gives you the right tools for each phase of pacing. Good luck supporting your husband – 32 miles is a huge commitment!
