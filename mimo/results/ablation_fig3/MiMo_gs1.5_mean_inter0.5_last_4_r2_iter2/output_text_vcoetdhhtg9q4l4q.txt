That's a great idea—home offices can get chaotic too! For quick dinner ingredients, here are a few simple, minimal-ingredient ideas:  

### 🍳 Quick Meal Ideas:  
1. **One-Pan Roasted Veggies + Protein**  
   - Toss veggies (like broccoli, bell peppers, zucchini) with olive oil, salt, and pepper.  
   - Add protein (chicken, tofu, or eggs) and roast together. Serve with a side of rice or bread.  

2. **Stir-Fry**  
   - Use pre-cut veggies (carrots, snap peas, mushrooms) + a simple sauce (soy sauce or peanut butter).  
   - Serve over rice noodles or instant rice.  

3. **Soup or Stew**  
   - Sauté onions, garlic, and canned tomatoes. Add broth, beans, and greens (like spinach or kale).  
   - Simmer 10–15 minutes. Easy and customizable.  

### 🛒 For Your Kitchen Declutter:  
- **Focus on staples**: Keep 3–4 spices (salt, pepper, garlic powder, maybe one others like cumin or paprika).  
- **Use what you have**: Repurpose rice noodles for stir-fries or soups instead of buying extras.  
- **Meal prep basics**: Cook a batch of rice or beans to reduce daily decisions.  

Minimalism in cooking is about *intentionality*—using fewer ingredients but more creativity. You’re already making progress by simplifying, so trust the process! 🌟
