Great question! Here's how to tune your instrument using both methods:

### 1. **Clip-On Electronic Tuner:**
   - **Attach it** to your instrument's headstock (like a guitar or ukulele).
   - **Pluck each string** one at a time.
   - **Turn the tuning peg** until the tuner's display shows the correct note (e.g., "A" for the 6th string on guitar).
   - **Tune to pitch**: The tuner will show how far off you are (e.g., "flat" or "sharp"). Adjust until the needle or light indicates perfect tuning.

### 2. **Tuner App on Phone:**
   - **Download an app** like GuitarTuna, Fender Tune, or Auto-Tuner.
   - **Hold the phone close** to the instrument strings (avoid background noise).
   - **Pluck each string** and listen for the app's audio feedback:
     - A **beep** means you're close to pitch.
     - A **buzz** indicates you're too sharp (tighten the string slightly).
     - A **hiss** means you're too flat (loosen the string slightly).
   - **Match the app's reference pitch** until the sound is stable.

### **Pro Tips:**
- **Tune up**: Start with the 6th string (lowest pitch) and move to the 1st string (highest).
- **Reference pitch**: Most tuners/apps default to **A440Hz** (standard tuning). Confirm this in settings.
- **Practice**: Tuning by ear takes time! Listen to the string's "ring" and adjust until it sounds clear and stable.
