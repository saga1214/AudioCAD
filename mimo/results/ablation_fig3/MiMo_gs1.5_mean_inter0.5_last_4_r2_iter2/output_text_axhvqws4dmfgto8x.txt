Okay, you're determined to tackle stir-fry. Let's make sure this noodle trip is a success!

The single most important thing to remember is to use a wooden or plastic spoon in your pot. Absolutely no wood, as that's what caused your smoke alarm adventure. Also, stir constantly so nothing sticks to the bottom.

Here’s a simple noodle method for stir-fry. First, bring a pot of water to a rolling boil. Then, add a good pinch of salt and a drizzle of oil to the water. This helps the noodles cook evenly and stay separate. After about two minutes, pour in your dry noodles. Let them cook for about five to seven minutes, stirring occasionally. Once they're tender but still a little firm, drain them really well in a colander. It's crucial to get all the water off, or your stir-fry will get soggy. Then, right back into the hot wok or pan they go!

The key is to use plenty of high heat.
