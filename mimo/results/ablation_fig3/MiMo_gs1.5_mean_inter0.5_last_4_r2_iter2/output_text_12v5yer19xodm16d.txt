Okay, you need a pick-me-up! Here are five drinks and five snacks that can help boost your energy. 

For drinks, I'd suggest:
First, an Iced Matcha Latte, which is rich in antioxidants and can give you a calm energy boost.
Second, Green Tea, which has caffeine and L-theanine to help you focus.
Third, Coconut Water, which is great for hydration and has natural electrolytes.
Fourth, a Cold Brew Coffee, which has less acidity and a smoother caffeine kick.
And fifth, a Turmeric Golden Milk Latte, which is soothing and anti-inflammatory.

For snacks, here are five ideas:
Number one, Almonds or Walnuts, which are packed with healthy fats and protein.
Number two, Dark Chocolate with at least 70 percent cocoa, which has caffeine and antioxidants.
Number three, Greek Yogurt with honey, for protein and quick energy.
Number four, an Apple with some peanut butter, for fiber and healthy fats.
And number five, some trail mix with nuts, seeds, and dried fruit, which is a balanced energy source.
