Okay, let me clarify that for you. The equal-parts version, with one ounce of each ingredient, is actually the original recipe. Your bartender friend likely just prefers that balance. The version with one and a quarter ounces of bourbon is also classic and very common. So, no confusion there. 

As for Last Chance, that’s an equal-parts drink too. It uses three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. Stir it well, strain into a glass, and garnish with a lime wheel. Let me know if you need anything else!
