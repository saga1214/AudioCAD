To buy a horse in Arizona, you do need to follow a few steps. You'll have to register the horse with the Arizona Department of Agriculture and get it microchipped. Depending on its age, it may also need to be vaccinated and dewormed. If you plan to own it on your own land, you'll also need to check with your local municipality about any specific regulations.
