Cooking can be good if you pick the right dish. Something quick, simple, and customizable would work well. A pasta dish, for example, can be adapted to her restrictions easily—you can use lactose-free cheese or just omit it. Something like a margherita pizza or a simple fettuccine Alfredo with gluten-free pasta if needed. Keep it to just the two of you, so it’s low-pressure. Focus on having fun with it rather than making a perfect meal, and maybe play some music in the background to make it more enjoyable.
