### 🍜 Asian Noodle Spots in Dallas:
1. **Yum Yum Noodle Bar** (Multiple locations)  
   - *Why*: Great ramen, pho, and stir-fried noodles. Consistent quality and flavorful broth.  
   - *Try*: Shoyu Ramen or Thai Green Curry Noodles.

2. **Noodle House** (Multiple locations)  
   - *Why*: Wide menu (Chinese, Thai, Korean). Their Pad See Ew and Beef & Broccoli are classics.  
   - *Tip*: Ask for extra chili oil!

3. **Pho 75** (Oak Cliff)  
   - *Why*: Authentic Vietnamese pho with chewy noodles. Perfect for a broth-focused meal.  
   - *Try*: Beef Pho Ga (chicken pho) or Spicy Beef.

4. **Ramen Lab** (Deep Ellum)  
   - *Why*: Japanese-style ramen with creative bowls. Their "Shoyu" or "Shio" broths are standout.  
   - *Note*: Small portions—great for sharing!

5. **Wok & Roll** (Multiple locations)  
   - *Why*: Casual spot for stir-fried noodles. Great for quick, customizable bowls.  
   - *Try*: Beef & Broccoli or Chicken Chow Fun.

### 🏋️ High-Protein Snack Bars:

   - *Flavors*: Double Chocolate, Peanut Butter, Cinnamon Roll.

2. **RXBAR**  
   - *Why*: Clean ingredients (just egg whites, dates, nuts). 10g protein, 180 calories.  
   - *Flavors*: Chocolate Sea Salt, Peanut Butter, Berry.

3. **Lärabar**  
   - *Why*: Natural, no added sugar. 5-8g protein, packed with fruit/nuts.  
   - *Flavors*: Chocolate Sea Salt, Peanut Butter, Cherry Almond.

4. **Clif Builder’s**  
   - *Why*: Balanced (carbs + protein). Good for post-workout or pre-recording energy.

5. **No Cow**  
   - *Why*: High protein (15-20g), low calorie. Tastes like candy.  
   - *Flavors*: Cookies & Cream, Peanut Butter Cup, Chocolate Peanut Butter.
