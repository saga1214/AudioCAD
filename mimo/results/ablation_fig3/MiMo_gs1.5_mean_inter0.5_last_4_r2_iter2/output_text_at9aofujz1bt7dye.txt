At minus ten degrees Celsius, the coefficient of friction for skate blades on ice is generally still quite low, but it might be a little higher than at the typical minus five to minus six degrees. Studies suggest it could be in the range of about 0.005 to 0.015. So, 0.005 is plausible, but it’s probably on the lower end of that range, especially if the ice is perfectly smooth and the blades are sharp.
