*   **Insert:** Feed the flexible cable into the drain opening. Try to get it as deep as possible.
*   **Twist & Pull:** Slowly and steadily twist the cable back and forth while pushing it in. Then, slowly pull the cable back out, keeping the twist. This breaks up or hooks the clog.
*   **Check & Repeat:** Pull the cable out. If you see debris on it, clean it off and try again. Continue until you feel significant resistance or see debris on the cable coming out.

**Step 3: Try a Drain Auger (Rigid Auger)**
*   **If the flexible snake fails:** Use a rigid auger (like a Zip-It or similar).
*   **Insert Carefully:** Feed the cable in, but **do NOT force it**. If it feels tight, stop.
*   **Twist & Push:** Slowly twist the cable clockwise while gently pushing it in. Look for a "bite" – a feeling of resistance or a change in direction.
*   **Retract:** Once you feel a bite, slowly twist the cable back out, pulling the clog out with it. **Stop immediately if you feel the cable hitting the pipe walls!**
*   **Check & Repeat:** Pull the cable out, look for debris, clean it, and try again.

**Step 4: Use Chemical Drain Cleaner (Last Resort)**
*   **Read Instructions:** Carefully read the product label first.
*   **Small Amount:** Pour a small amount (follow label directions) down the drain. **Avoid mixing chemicals!**
*   **Wait:** Let it sit for the recommended time (usually 30 mins to several hours). Do NOT flush with hot water immediately.
*   **Flush:** After the dwell time, slowly flush the drain with **plenty of hot water**. Check if it's clear.
