### For the Baby:
1. **Swaddle gently** – Use a receiving blanket to wrap the baby snugly (like a burrito). This mimics the womb and calms them.  
2. **Side position** – Place them on their back or side (never face-down) on a soft surface like a bed or couch.  
3. **Shush and swaddle** – Squeak a clean towel or use a white noise machine. Gentle swaying or rocking helps too.  
4. **Burp after feeding** – If they’ve eaten, burp them to relieve discomfort.  
5. **Offer a bottle** – If hands-free, a bottle can keep you focused while the baby sucks.
