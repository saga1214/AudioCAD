You got it! For St Pete, Zone 10B, especially during the fall and winter, you’ll want to focus on heat-tolerant, fast-growing stuff. Herbs like basil, mint, and parsley are perfect. Leafy greens like lettuce, spinach, and radishes are also a great bet.

If you're going for fruiting vegetables, tomatoes and peppers are fantastic choices. They actually do pretty well in your warm climate. Just be sure to get them started early in the fall, since they need some cooler nights to set fruit properly. 

Oh, and if you're feeling adventurous, you could also try okra or eggplant. They’re a bit more of a summer plant, but since your winters are so mild, you can probably get away with it. Just make sure to water them consistently and keep them in the shade if it gets really hot.

And remember, Florida’s humidity means you'll probably have to keep an eye out for pests like aphids or spider mites. Neem oil should take care of them. Hope that helps you get a fantastic edible garden started!
