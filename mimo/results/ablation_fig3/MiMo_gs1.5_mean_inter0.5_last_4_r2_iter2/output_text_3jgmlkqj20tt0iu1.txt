Here are two approaches. First, you could schedule a specific meeting for the purpose. Send an email to your manager with a clear subject line like, 'Meeting Request: Salary Review Discussion'. You can mention that you'd like to schedule some time to discuss your performance and career development, which frames the conversation professionally and gives you a dedicated, less-pressure slot. 

Alternatively, you could bring it up organically during a routine one-on-one meeting. You could say something like, 'I've really enjoyed the opportunities I've had over the past year and I've taken some steps to improve my time management. Based on my contributions and the expanded responsibilities I've taken on, I was hoping we could discuss my compensation moving forward.'
