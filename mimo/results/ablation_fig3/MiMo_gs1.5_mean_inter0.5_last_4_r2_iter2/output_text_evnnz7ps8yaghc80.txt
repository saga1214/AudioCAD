For your bean bags, which are denser and fall faster, aim for consistency and accuracy. Try practicing for 15 to 20 minutes each day. You can do this right after school or during a quiet evening.

For your scarves, which fall slowly, focus on patterns and rhythm. You could do this in the morning for a good warm-up, or maybe on the weekend for a relaxed session.

Here’s a sample weekly schedule you could follow:

Monday: 15 minutes of bean bag practice. Focus on smooth, relaxed throws.
Tuesday: 15 minutes of bean bag practice. Try to add one more throw.
Wednesday: 20 minutes of scarf practice. Focus on catching the slow, wide arc.
Thursday: 15 minutes of bean bag practice. Focus on catching high and low.
Friday: 15 minutes of bean bag practice. Try a simple pattern.
Saturday: 20 minutes of scarf practice. Try to juggle two scarves.
Sunday: Rest. Or, if you feel up to it, a short 10-minute bean bag session just for fun.
