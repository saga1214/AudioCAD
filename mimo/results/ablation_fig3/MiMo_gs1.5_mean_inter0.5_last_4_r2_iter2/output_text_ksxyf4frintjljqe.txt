Absolutely—here's a **Decadent Chocolate Hazelnut Pie** recipe that’s easy to make in one pan and will satisfy everyone’s sweet tooth!  

### **Ingredients** (for a 9-inch pie dish):  
- **1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk**  
- **3 large eggs**  
- **½ cup cocoa powder** (unsweetened)  
- **¼ cup melted butter**  
- **1 tsp vanilla extract**  
- **⅓ cup chocolate chips** (plus extra for topping)  
- **1 cup crushed graham crackers** (or Oreos for extra flavor)  
- **⅓ cup hazelnut butter** (or peanut butter if unavailable)  
- **Pinch of salt**  

### **Steps**:  
1. **Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)**. Grease a 9-inch pie dish and line the bottom with crushed crackers.  
2. **Mix the filling**: In a bowl, whisk eggs, cocoa powder, melted butter, vanilla, and salt. Stir in condensed milk, hazelnut butter, and chocolate chips.  
3. **Pour and bake**: Pour the batter into the crust, sprinkle extra chocolate chips on top, and bake for **25–30 minutes** until set but slightly jiggly.  
4. **Cool completely** before slicing (it firms up as it chills). Serve warm or chilled!  

### **Why It Works**:  
- **One-pan wonder**: No need for a separate crust—just crush crackers into a base!  
- **Crowd-pleaser**: Rich chocolate + nutty crunch = universally loved.  
- **Make-ahead**: Prep ahead and refrigerate; thaw 30 mins before serving.
