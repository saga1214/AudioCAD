Here's a balanced 9-day itinerary focusing on deep exploration of Tokyo, Osaka, Nara, and Kyoto, with practical logistics:

**Tokyo (5 Days)**

*   **Day 4:** **Work/Sightseeing Balance:** *Morning:* Nikko or Kamakura day trip (1hr by train). *Afternoon:* Return to Tokyo. *Late Afternoon:* Work on coursework (hotel/lounge).
*   **Day 5:** **Tsukiji & Modern Tokyo:** Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market (food), TeamLab Borderless/Planets (digital art - book ahead!). Afternoon: Odaiba (Seaside Park, Rainbow Bridge, shopping). Evening: Flight prep.

**Osaka (4 Days)**
*   **Day 6 (Travel):** Shinkansen to Osaka (45-60 mins). Check into hotel (Namba/Dotonbori area). Evening: **Dinner in Dotonbori** (street food, neon lights).
*   **Day 7:** **Osaka Castle & Umeda:** Morning: Osaka Castle Park (museum inside). Afternoon: Umeda Sky Building (Hiroshima View), shopping. Evening: **Kuromon Ichiba Market** (food), **Namba Parks** (observation deck).
*   **Day 8:** **Kyoto Day Trip (AM):** Shinkansen to Kyoto (30 mins). Morning: **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion), Ryoan-ji Rock Garden. Lunch: Pontocho Alley. Afternoon: Return to Osaka. Evening: Relax.
*   **Day 9:** **Nara Day Trip (AM):** Shinkansen to Nara (45 mins). Morning: **Todai-ji Temple** (Great Buddha), **Nara Park** (deer). Lunch: Local Nishin (herring) soba. Afternoon: Return to Osaka. Evening: Departure flight.

**Kyoto & Nara (Day Trips from Osaka):**

*   **Nara (Day 9 AM):** Easy access, hit the top attractions (Todai-ji, Nara Park). Deer are iconic!
