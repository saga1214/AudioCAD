Sure. French Bulldogs are popular, chill, and good with kids. They’re also easy to train but can be noisy, so check your landlord's rule. Golden Retrievers are friendly and trainable, but larger. Cocker Spaniels are small, gentle, and quiet. Bichon Frises are hypoallergenic and easygoing. Miniature Poodles are smart and playful. Labrador Retrievers are friendly and great with kids, but bigger. Shetland Sheepdogs are small, calm, and affectionate. Cavalier King Charles Spaniels are sweet and loyal. Finally, mixed breeds like a Labradoodle or Goldendoodle can be a good option too.
