Here's a simple, fabric-safe way to hang your jacket without damaging the lining:

1.  **Use a Standard Hanger:** A wooden or plastic (not wire) hanger is best.
2.  **Create a "Clothesline":** Fold the top of the jacket (the collar area) down over the top of the hanger, creating a loop. The loop should sit comfortably on the hanger's neck curve.
3.  **Secure the Loop:** Gently slide the jacket onto the hanger. The loop you created should now be securely pinned or held in place by the hanger's shoulder straps. The jacket hangs from this loop.
4.  **Support the Bottoms:** Let the bottom hem of the jacket rest naturally on the hanger's body. Avoid overstuffing the bottom part of the hanger – it should have a slight "S" curve, not be straight or bent.

**Why this works & protects the lining:**

*   **No Hooks/Pins:** You're not poking or scratching the lining with hooks or pins.
*   **Minimal Stress:** The weight is concentrated on the looped collar area, not stretching or pulling on the shoulders, arms, or hem.
*   **Support:** The bottom hem is supported by the hanger's body, preventing it from sagging and stretching.
*   **Avoids Wire Hangers:** Wire hangers can cut into fabric and damage delicate linings over time.

**Alternative (if you have a rubber band):**

*   Loop the jacket's top collar over a standard hanger.
*   Wrap a wide rubber band around the top of the hanger, just below the looped collar. This adds extra security to the loop and prevents it from slipping.

**Key Tips:**

*   **Lightweight First:** If your jacket is very bulky or heavy (like a down parka), this method might strain the hanger or the loop. For most fleece, wool, or synthetic jackets, it works well.
*   **Avoid Wire Hangers:** They are the worst for fabric damage.
*   **Don't Overcrowd:** Give the jacket space on the hanger.
*   **Temporary Fix:** This is great for Nordstrom Rack or REI while you wait for shipping to arrive!

This method keeps your jacket looking good and prevents the lining from snagging or stretching until your new purchases arrive!
