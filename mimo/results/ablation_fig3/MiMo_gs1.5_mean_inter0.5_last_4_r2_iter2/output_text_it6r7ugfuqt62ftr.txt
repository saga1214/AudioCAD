Okay, this is a big one! Since you have a flexible budget and want warm, swimmable weather plus dining options for a 12-year-old with Celiac, I have the perfect California getaway planned for you: **Big Sur and Pismo Beach**.

Here’s how I see it: You'll spend the first couple of days exploring the stunning coast of Big Sur, then head down to the warmer beaches of Pismo Beach for swimming and more relaxation. This way, you get both adventure and relaxation, plus the warm weather you're craving.

Let's start with accommodations. For your first two nights, I'd suggest the **Asilomar Conference Grounds** in Pacific Grove. It's a beautiful, historic spot right on the water, and they are very experienced with gluten-free dining. You can even take a short walk to the nearby **Lovers Point Park** for some beautiful ocean views.

Then, for your third and fourth nights, I'd recommend staying in **Big Sur**. The **Bixby Creek Inn** is a charming, boutique hotel with private balconies overlooking the ocean. It's a bit more secluded and peaceful, and it's right near the famous McWay Falls.

Now for the fun stuff—things to do! In Big Sur, you absolutely have to visit **McWay Falls**. It’s a short hike to a viewpoint of a 80-foot waterfall that just cascades right into the ocean. It's breathtaking! You can also walk the **Nepenthe overlook** for incredible panoramic views. And if you want a truly special experience, you could take a tour to visit **Hearst Castle**.

For a day trip from Pismo Beach, you could drive up to **San Simeon** to see the elephant seals, or visit **Avila Beach** for some more fun in the sun.

When it comes to eating, you'll be in great shape. Asilomar has fantastic restaurants. In Big Sur, you can't go wrong with Nepenthe, which has a great ocean-view terrace. In Pismo, there are lots of great seafood spots, like **The Fish House** at Pismo Beach.

Getting around is easy, as you'll be driving your own car. The main thing to remember is that Big Sur can be crowded, especially during the day, so it's a good idea to book your tours or popular restaurants ahead of time.

And for packing, remember your bathing suit, a light sweater for cooler evenings, a hat and sunglasses, sunscreen, and of course, a gluten-free travel kit with snacks and any necessary medication. 

This trip offers everything you're looking for: beautiful scenery, warm weather for swimming, exciting activities, and wonderful dining. I hope you all have an unforgettable vacation!
