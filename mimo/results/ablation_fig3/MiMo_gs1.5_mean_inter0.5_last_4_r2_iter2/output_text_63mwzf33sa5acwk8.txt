Okay, so you're thinking about combining a desert adventure with a Vegas pit stop. That's a solid plan. Here's how you can make the most of both. For the desert part, Joshua Tree is a great choice, but Nevada’s Black Rock Desert near Burning Man is another epic option if you want that raw, adventurous feel. Both are free for dispersed camping, which sounds like your thing. Just remember to respect the land—no trash, no fires unless it's allowed, and be self-sufficient. 

Now, for Vegas. If you're coming back from the desert, maybe you're tired but still want to vibe. A nice hotel with a hot tub is a good idea. The Wynn or Encore have amazing spas. Or if you're feeling more rugged, check out the Hard Rock or El Cortez—they have decent pools and are a bit more low-key. 

To have a 'proper' Vegas experience, think about what you both enjoy. Do you want to hit the casinos, see a show, or just relax? If you're into shows, Cirque du Soleil or a jazz club like Bluebird could be cool. For food, there are tons of options from fancy to cheap eats. And if you're up for it, a night out at a club like Omnia or Drai’s could be fun. 

The key is to balance the adventure with some relaxation. After a day of exploring the desert, a hot tub at a nice hotel sounds perfect. Vegas can be overwhelming, so maybe don't try to do everything at once. Pick a few things you're excited about and go from there.
