Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes for your vodka mix and Argentine-inspired Fernet drink—perfect for your dinner party!

### 1. **Vodka Sour** (Bright, balanced, & crowd-pleasing)  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz vodka (your leftover)  
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup (1:1 sugar:water)  
- ½ oz egg white (optional for silky texture)  
*Method:*  
Shake all ingredients with ice, strain into a chilled coupe glass. Garnish with lemon twist or a maraschino cherry.  
*Why it works:* Light, refreshing, and universally appealing. The egg white adds luxury (great for mixed age groups).

### 2. **Argentine-Inspired Fernet Smash** (Herbal, spicy, & sophisticated)  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz gin (or vodka if preferred)  
- ¾ oz fresh grapefruit juice  
- ¾ oz fresh lime juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 2–3 fresh mint leaves  
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
- ½ oz fernet brine (or substitute with ½ oz cherry liqueur + 2 dashes cherry bitters if you dislike fernet’s bitterness)  
*Method:*  
Muddle mint leaves lightly in a shaker. Add all ingredients and shake hard with ice. Strain into a rocks glass over a single large ice cube. Garnish with mint sprig and grapefruit wheel.  
*Why it works:* Gin’s botanicals balance fernet’s sharpness. Grapefruit/lime add brightness. Mint and bitters echo Argentine *pisco sour* vibes.

### Pro Tips:
- **Label drinks clearly** (e.g., "Vodka Sour" vs. "Fernet Smash") since guests may want to try both.  
- **Allergy-safe:** Both drinks exclude soda. Offer sparkling water with lemon as a non-alcoholic option.  
- **Speed:** Prep ingredients ahead—shake when guests arrive in 5 minutes.  
- **Fernet note:** If guests dislike bitter herbal notes, serve the fernet version *only* to those who’ve tried it before. The cherry liqueur sub is sweeter and safer.
