Here's a simple and tasty vegetarian "crab" sandwich recipe using artificial crab meat:

### **Artificial Crab Meat Sandwich**  
*(Serves 1)*  

**Ingredients:**  
- 1 tbsp olive oil or butter  
- ½ cup artificial crab meat (canned, drained)  
- 1 egg, fried sunny-side up (optional)  
- 1 slice whole-grain bread, toasted  
- ¼ avocado, sliced  
- 1 tbsp mayonnaise  
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard  
- Salt & pepper to taste  
- Fresh dill or parsley (optional)  

**Steps:**  
1. **Prep:** Toast bread. Fry egg in a pan until golden.  
2. **Mix filling:** In a bowl, combine crab meat, mayo, mustard, salt, and pepper. Fold gently.  
3. **Assemble:** Spread avocado on the toasted bread. Layer crab mixture, egg, and herbs.  
4. **Serve:** Cut in half and enjoy!  

**Tips:**  
- For extra flavor, add a squeeze of lemon or a pinch of Old Bay seasoning.  
- Replace egg with tofu scramble if needed.  
- Use high-quality artificial crab meat for best texture.  

This gives you that classic sandwich texture with a creamy, savory filling! 🥪
