That's a more nuanced point about genetics. Let's break it down.

The egg you're talking about was laid by a creature that was biologically a 'proto-chicken'. It had most of the chicken's DNA, but that one key protein for the shell was missing.

The mutation had to happen *after* the egg was laid to create the first true chicken. The egg itself didn't contain the full DNA for a chicken at that moment. It was the hatched creature that was the first chicken, which then produced the first true chicken egg.

So, the egg came first, but the creature that hatched from it was the first chicken. The egg was still first.
