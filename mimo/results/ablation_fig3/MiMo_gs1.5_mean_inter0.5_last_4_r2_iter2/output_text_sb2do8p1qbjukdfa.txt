### Appreciation Table Refresh Station Plan
**1. Drinks (Crisp & Cooling):**
   - **Infused Waters:** Lemon-cucumber, mint-berries, or orange-basil (no mint, as requested!). Keep chilled in a large pitcher.
   - **Chilled Herbal Teas:** Hibiscus, peppermint (or peach/strawberry if avoiding mint), or chamomile in a dispenser.
   - **Sparkling Options:** Light sparkling water with fruit slices or edible flowers.
   - **Simple Iced Coffee/Tea:** Pre-brewed and chilled with a splash of citrus juice.

**2. Snacks (Light & Energizing):**
   - Fresh fruit skewers (watermelon, grapes, berries).
   - Light rice crackers with avocado or hummus.
   - Mini muffins (blueberry, lemon, or banana-nut).
   - Fresh-cut veggies (carrots, bell peppers) with a yogurt dip.
   - Energy bites (oats, dates, coconut) with a hint of citrus zest.

**3. Decorations (Cheerful & Low-Maintenance):**
   - **Table Runner:** Light blue or mint-green (avoiding mint-themed colors) with a subtle pattern.
   - **Centerpiece:** A low vase with white daisies or eucalyptus sprigs (easy to remove/replace).
   - **Signs:** Handwritten chalkboard or cardstock tags like "Stay Refreshed!" or "Hydrate & Energize!" in playful fonts.
   - **Lighting:** A small string of fairy lights or a citronella candle (if outdoors) for ambiance.

**4. Bonus: Palate Cleansers & Breath Fresheners:**
   - **Candies:** Lime or passionfruit-flavored mints (if you tolerate fruit-flavored mint), or sugar-free lychee gelatin cubes.
   - **Palate Cleansers:** Small bowls of chilled grapes, cucumber slices, or rice crackers.
   - **Scents:** A light citrus or eucalyptus-scented diffuser (if indoors) or fresh-cut flowers for subtle fragrance.

### Creative Flavor/Scent Pairings (Bright & Refreshing)

- **Citrus + Floral:** Lemon water with edible lavender or rose petals.  
- **Chocolate + Citrus:** White chocolate-dipped strawberries with a lemon glaze.  
- **Chocolate + Fruit:** Dark chocolate truffles with raspberry or passionfruit filling.  
- **Fruit + Herb:** Watermelon skewers with a basil-infused honey drizzle.
