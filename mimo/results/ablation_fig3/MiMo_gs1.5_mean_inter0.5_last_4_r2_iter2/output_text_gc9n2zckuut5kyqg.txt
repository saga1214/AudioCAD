Absolutely! Here are some budget-friendly dessert ideas that are kid-friendly, diabetes-conscious, and easy to prepare:

### 🍓 **No-Bake & Fresh Fruit Focused:**
1. **Fruit Skewers:**  
   - Thread colorful fruits (strawberries, grapes, pineapple, melon, kiwi).  
   - *Cost tip:* Buy seasonal fruit in bulk.  
2. **Fruit Salad Cups:**  
   - Pre-cut fruit into small portions (use disposable cups or small bowls).  
   - Add a squeeze of lemon/lime to prevent browning.  
3. **Frozen Yogurt Pops:**  
   - Blend Greek yogurt with berries/kiwi, pour into popsicle molds, and freeze.  
   - *Budget hack:* Use store-bought yogurt for faster prep.  

### 🍪 **Simple Baked Goods:**
4. **Homemade Oatmeal Cookies:**  
   - Use oats, mashed banana/applesauce (replaces oil/butter), and minimal sugar.  
   - Cut into fun shapes (stars, hearts) for kids.  
5. **Yogurt Parfaits:**  
   - Layer Greek yogurt, granola, and fresh fruit in cups.  
   - *Budget tip:* Use whole-grain granola to save money.  

### 🍨 **Fun & Sweet Treats (Low-Sugar):**
6. **Mini Cheesecake Bites:**  
   - Make graham cracker crusts and fill with reduced-fat cream cheese + honey/maple syrup.  
7. **Rice Krispie Treats:**  
   - Use mini marshmallows and rice cereal. Add crushed nuts for texture.  
   - *Diabetes note:* Marshmallows add sugar—offer in moderation.  

### 💡 **Budget-Saving Tips:**
- **Batch Prep:** Make desserts ahead (cookies, parfaits) to save time.  
- **DIY Stations:** Set up a fruit skewer station where kids can assemble their own.  
- **Use Natural Sweeteners:** Honey, maple syrup, or mashed fruit reduce sugar reliance.  
- **Repurpose Leftovers:** Extra cookies/bars can become raffle prizes!
