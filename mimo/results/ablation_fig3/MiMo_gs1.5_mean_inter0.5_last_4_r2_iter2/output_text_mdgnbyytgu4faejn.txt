Okay, let's put it all together. Here's a simple, step-by-step breakdown of a standard resume format that's easy to follow.

First up is your contact information, which you've already got figured out. Your name in a larger font, like 14-point, then email, phone, city, and icons. Just remember to put this block directly below your name, not to the side.

Next is your professional summary. This is that short paragraph at the top where you briefly describe your most relevant skills and career goals.

After that comes your work experience. You should list each employer separately with your title, the company name, and the start and end dates for each role. Keep the descriptions short and to the point.

Then you have your education section. Here you'll list your degrees, the school's name, and any awards or honors you received for that degree.

If you have relevant certifications or licenses, you can create a separate section for that.

For your skills section, you should list your most relevant skills, maybe broken down into categories like technical skills or languages. This is a great place to tailor your resume for each job you apply for by matching the skills in the job description.

Finally, you can add an optional section for volunteer work or professional affiliations if they're relevant.

The most important thing is to keep it clean and easy to scan. Avoid using fancy designs or graphics, and make sure the formatting is consistent throughout. And of course, always proofread it carefully before you send it out!
