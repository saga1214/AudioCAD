That sounds like a fantastic plan! Here's a step-by-step guide to crafting an unforgettable Costa Rican scuba diving adventure, tailored to your preferences:

### 1. **Choose the Perfect Location**  
   - **Cocos Island**: Ideal for advanced divers. Famous for deep dives, dramatic cliffs, and abundant marine life (sharks, manta rays, turtles). Requires a boat trip from Santa Teresa or Puerto Viejo.  
   - **Puntarenas or Santa Teresa**: Gateway to the *Pacific side*. Offers diverse sites like **Sirena** (coral gardens, rays) and **Iguana** (reef breaks). Great for beginners to intermediates.  
   - **Narita or Playa Blanca (Guanacaste)**: For calm, shallow waters perfect for new divers. Sites like **Playa Naranjo** feature dramatic lava rock formations and schools of fish.  

### 2. **Pack Essentials**  
   - **Fins**: Stick with **short fins** for your first trip—easier to maneuver and less tiring.  
   - **Gear**: Bring your own BCD, regulator, and weights. Rent tanks if needed.  
   - **Extras**: Sun protection (lips/hands!), reef-safe sunscreen, dive computer, and a dry bag.  

### 3. **Top Dive Sites to Experience**  
   - **Cocos Island**: **Coral Garden** (shark encounters), **Fragile Coral Garden** (snorkeling).  
   - **Santa Teresa**: **Sirena** (manta rays), **Iguana** (reef dives).  
   - **Guanacaste**: **Playa Naranjo** (reef sharks), **Hornopilos** (caves, deep walls).  

### 4. **Book a Reputable Dive Operator**  
   - Look for eco-certified operators (e.g., PADI/SSI) with small-group tours. Operators like **Dive Cocos**, **Santa Teresa Divers**, or **Guanacaste Divers** are highly rated.  
   - **Pro Tip**: Opt for morning dives—calmer waters and cooler temps.  

### 5. **Wildlife & Unique Experiences**  
   - **Night Dives**: Spot bioluminescent plankton (in coves like **Malpelo** or **Cocos**) or nocturnal creatures.  
   - **Whale Sharks**: Year-round in Cocos (best Nov-Mar).  
   - **Sea Turtle Nesting**: Jan-Apr (Cocos/Guanacaste).  

### 6. **Logistics & Safety**  
   - **Visa**: Check entry requirements (U.S./Canadian citizens get 90-day visas-free).  
   - **Travel**: Fly into **Liberia (LIR)** or **San José (SJO)**. Rent a car or join guided tours.  
   - **Safety**: Choose operators with emergency response plans. Always check dive conditions (reef damage reports) via **Costa Rica Dive Report**.  

### 7. **Bonus: Beyond Diving**  
   - **Hiking**: Monteverde Cloud Forest or Arenal Volcano.  
   - **Adventure**: Zip-lining, white-water rafting, or hot springs.
