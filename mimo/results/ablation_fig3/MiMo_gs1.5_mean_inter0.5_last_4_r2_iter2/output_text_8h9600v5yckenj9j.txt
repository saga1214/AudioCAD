**Decorations (Quick & Impactful):**
1.  **Fog Machine Setup:** Build your DIY chiller tonight!
    *   **Steps:**
        *   Find a large, sturdy plastic container (like a storage tote or cooler).
        *   Cut a hole on one side for the fog machine nozzle to fit snugly.
        *   Cut a hole on the *opposite* side or near the top for fog to exit.
        *   Place wire racks or large trays inside to hold ice.
        *   Fill the ice area with ice cubes or **frozen water bottles**.
        *   Position the fog machine behind the container blowing towards the exit hole.
        *   Turn it on! The fog should now hang low near the ground around the container.
2.  **Lighting & Atmosphere:**
    *   **Purple/Orange Lights:** Your purple bulbs are a great start. Add battery-operated LED string lights (orange, purple, white) strung overhead, on fences, or wrapping around trees/porch railings.
    *   **Webbing:** Stretch large, wispy fake spider webs over doorways, windows, railings, or between trees/columns. Add smaller ones near the ground.
    *   **Ghosts/Bats:** Hang inflatable ghosts or plastic bats from trees, eaves, or lights.
    *   **Pumpkins/Gourds:** Place large plastic pumpkins or gourds near the entrance, on steps, or on tables. Add fake moss for texture.
    *   **Simple Signage:** Hang a sign on the door or garage: "Trick or Treat!" or "Enter the Spooky Zone!" in black paper cutouts.

**Food & Drink (Simple & Safe):**
1.  **Main Option (Pizza):** Stick with pizzas! Choose a few different styles (pepperoni, veggie, maybe a cheese) for variety. Pre-slice if possible.
2.  **Snack Stations (Focus):**

4.  **Drinks:** Basic is best.
    *   **Non-Alcoholic:** Bottled water, soda, juice boxes. Use dispensers or pitchers.
