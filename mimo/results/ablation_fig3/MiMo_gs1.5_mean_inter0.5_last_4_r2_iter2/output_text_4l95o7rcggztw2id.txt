Here’s a **pirate/nautical-themed, sand-friendly, allergy-conscious shopping list** for your son’s 7th birthday beach party, designed for easy handling and maximum immersion:

### **Pirate Party Shopping List**  
*(All items avoid watermelon & are easy to eat without sand)*  

#### **1. Appetizers & Snacks (Finger Foods)**  
- **Mini Sandwiches/Wraps (Cut into Shapes)**  
  - *Pirate Flag Shapes:* Ham & cheese, turkey & cream cheese, or veggie (no watermelon!).  
  - *Jelly Bean Eyes:* Add eyes for pirate cutouts.  
- **"Buried Treasure" Skewers**  
  - Grapes, cherry tomatoes, cheese cubes, goldfish crackers (arranged like treasure).  
- **"Pirate Jewels" Fruit Skewers**  
  - *Allergy-Friendly:* Banana, pineapple, blueberries, mango (skip watermelon!).  
- **Cracker Ships**  
  - Ritz crackers shaped like pirate ships.  
- **Pretzel Rods "Ship Masts"**  
  - Mini pretzel rods with cheese dip.  
- **Cheese "Gold" Cubes**  
  - Mozzarella or cheddar cubes.  

#### **2. Main Course (Sand-Safe & Portable)**  
- **Mini Fish-shaped Sandwiches**  
  - *Theme:* "Mermaid’s Catch" (tuna/chicken salad in bread cups).  
  - *Sandproof Tip:* Serve in mini containers or paper cups.  
- **Fish-shaped Crackers**  
  - *Allergy Note:* Ensure no watermelon garnish.  

#### **3. Sides (No-Spill & Themed)**  
- **"Cannonball" Fruit Cups**  
  - *Allergy Safe:* Pineapple, mango, berries (in clear cups labeled "Cannonballs").  
- **"Seagull Feathers" Toppers**  
  - Crispy roasted chickpeas or popcorn (in paper cones).  
- **"Treasure Chest" Cheese & Crackers**  
  - Mini containers of cheddar, crackers, and grapes.  

#### **4. Drinks (No Spills, Easy Open)**  
- **Individual Juice Boxes**  
  - *Theme:* Label as "Pirate Punch" or "Sea Water."  
- **Small Water Bottles**  
  - *Theme:* Wrap in flags or label with "Cannonballs."  
- **Pretzel Rods "Anchor" Treats**  
  - Dip pretzel rods in chocolate (cut into pirate ship shapes).  

#### **5. Allergy & Safety Notes**  
- **Avoid Watermelon Completely** – Use grapes, berries, pineapple, or mango instead.  
- **Label All Foods** – Include "Contains: [allergen]" and "Pirate Treasure!"  
- **Sandproof Packaging** – Use small containers, snack bags, or "treasure chest" boxes.  
- **No Loose Fillings** – Avoid salads, chips, or anything that could spill.  

#### **6. Extras for Immersion**  
- **Pirate-Themed Tableware:** Flag napkins, treasure chest plates.  
- **Activity Props:** Mini pirate hats, "X Marks the Spot" maps.  
- **Decor:** Jolly Roger flags, plastic treasure chests for food storage.  

---

### **Key Tips**  
- **Prep Ahead:** Cut sandwiches/wraps into shapes before the party.  
- **Portion Control:** Use small containers to prevent spills.  
- **Allergy Check:** Confirm the child’s full allergies (e.g., nuts, dairy) with parents.  

This list keeps everything **sand-free, thematic, and safe** while keeping your son’s pirate adventure alive! 🏴‍☠️🌊
