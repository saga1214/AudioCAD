Here's a clear, detailed list of classic recipes for your cocktails, including ingredients, measurements, and methods—perfect for your bartenders:

### **1. Old Fashioned**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Maker’s Mark)  
- 1 sugar cube (or ¾ oz simple syrup)  
- 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  

*Method:*  
1. Muddle the sugar cube with ½ oz water in a shaker until dissolved.  
2. Add bourbon, bitters, and ice.  
3. Shake 10–12 seconds.  
4. Strain into a chilled rocks glass over ice.  
5. Express an orange twist over the drink and drop it in.  

---

### **2. Paper Plane**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Bulleit)  
- ¾ oz Aperol  
- ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice  

*Method:*  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Shake hard until well-chilled (15–20 seconds).  
3. Strain into a coupe glass.  
4. Garnish with an orange twist or rosemary sprig.  

---

### **3. Whiskey Sour (with egg white option)**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 1 egg white (*optional*)  

*Method:*  
1. **Without egg white:** Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.  
2. **With egg white:**  
   - *Dry shake first:* Add egg white + all other ingredients to shaker. Shake **without ice** until frothy (15 seconds).  
   - *Add ice:* Shake with ice for 10–12 seconds.  
   - *Strain:* Pour into a coupe glass. Garnish with lemon twist.  

---

### **4. Aviation**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz gin (e.g., Hendrick’s)  
- ¾ oz maraschino liqueur (e.g., Luxardo)  
- ½ oz crème de violette  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  

*Method:*  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Shake until well-chilled (15–20 seconds).  
3. Strain into a coupe glass.  
4. Garnish with a maraschino cherry.  

---

### **5. French 75 (Recommended Gin Cocktail)**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz lemon juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 2 oz chilled Champagne  

*Method:*  
1. Shake gin, lemon, and syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a flute.  
3. Top with Champagne.  
4. Garnish with lemon twist.

### **Pro Tips for Bartenders:**  
- Use **fresh lemon juice** in all sour-based drinks (bottled dilutes flavor).  
- For the Paper Plane, **shake hard**—it’s a stirred drink, not a shaken one.  
- **Egg white technique:** Wipe the shaker rim dry after dry shaking to prevent splatter.
