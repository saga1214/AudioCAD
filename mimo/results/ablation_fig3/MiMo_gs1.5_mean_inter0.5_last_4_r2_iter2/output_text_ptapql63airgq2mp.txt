### Unique & Adventurous:
1. **Kimchi Seaweed Bites**:  
   - Mix crispy nori sheets with kimchi, sesame seeds, and a sprinkle of gochujang (chili paste). Serve with spicy mayo on the side.  
2. **Tamarind & Miso Glazed Nuts**:  
   - Roast cashews or almonds with tamarind paste, white miso, and a touch of honey for sweet-savory crunch.  
3. **Snow Monkey“Hot Spring” Snack Station**:  
   - Set up a small tray with:  
     - Dried squid or octopus jerky  
     - Wasabi peas  
     - Matcha-flavored mochi  
     - Mini mochi“ice cream” cups (store-bought or homemade)  
4. **DIY Tamagoyaki Station**:  
   - Let guests layer their own sushi rolls with tamagoyaki, cucumber, avocado, and spicy mayo.

   - Serve three dips:  
     - **Miso-Ginger**: White miso + grated ginger + sesame oil  
     - **Tamarind-Sriracha**: Sweet-tangy tamarind paste + sriracha  
     - **Black Bean & Garlic**: Bold, savory dip for veggies or crackers.
