That's a fantastic skill to have! If you can do the illustrations yourself, that's a huge advantage. It's very common for children's book authors to also be illustrators.

In that case, you might want to consider a hybrid approach. Instead of just an agent, you could try submitting directly to publishers. You can find these opportunities on websites like 'Weird Al's publishing blog' or by checking the submission guidelines on publisher websites. 

Another great option is to submit your complete manuscript and illustrations to an agent who specializes in children's books. They will know how to pitch it effectively to publishers. The most important thing is to make sure your illustrations are high-quality and perfectly match the story's text. Publishers are always looking for fresh voices and talented artists.
