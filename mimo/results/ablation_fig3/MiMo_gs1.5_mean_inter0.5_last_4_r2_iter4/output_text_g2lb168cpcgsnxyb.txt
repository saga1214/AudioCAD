You are absolutely right to be concerned about those critical environmental factors. They are just as important as the connection you've built.

While connecting with him is the goal, you must first ensure you can safely do so. The lack of supervision and the unsafe environment are major barriers to effective therapy.

Here are my recommendations. First, safety is the top priority. You need to advocate for a safe space. This could mean asking for sessions to happen in a less cluttered area, like a kitchen table, or even outdoors if possible. Second, you should definitely report the lack of supervision and the environmental hazards to the BCBA. Your safety and the client's safety are paramount. Third, you might suggest a modified version of Discrete Trial Training, but presented in a very low-pressure way. For example, you could pair a simple request with one of his music activities.
