That's a smart approach! Here are the safest methods to attach something to your jacket lining without tearing it, based on how delicate winter linings can be:

1.  **Fabric Glue (Best for Flat Items like Tags/Patches):**
    *   **Choose Wisely:** Use a glue specifically designed for fabric (like E6000 Fabric Adhesive, Aleene's Tacky Glue, or a strong craft glue). Avoid super glue or regular wood glue.
    *   **Apply Sparingly:** Put a *tiny* dot of glue on the fabric glue itself, not directly on the lining. Apply it to the back of the item you're attaching.
    *   **Press & Hold:** Press the item firmly onto the lining for several minutes (30-60 seconds is good). Hold it down with your fingers or a flat object (like a credit card) until it feels secure.
    *   **Test First:** Always test the glue on an *inside seam* or hem first to ensure it doesn't damage the lining or fabric.

2.  **Double-Sided Fabric Tape (Best for Flat Items like Patches):**
    *   **Look for the Right Tape:** Find tape specifically labeled for fabric or leather. Standard double-sided tape might not adhere well to winter fabrics.
    *   **Peel & Stick:** Peel the backing from one side of the tape, apply it firmly to the item you're attaching, and press the item onto the lining. Peel the backing from the other side and press down firmly.
    *   **Advantage:** Provides a strong, temporary bond without any glue residue.

3.  **Strong Fabric-Safe Double-Sided Tape (Good for Moderate Weight Items like Zippers/Pulls):**
    *   **Use Carefully:** Apply the tape to the item *before* attaching it. Ensure the tape is fully adhered. Press firmly onto the lining. *Best used on thicker tape designed for heavier fabrics.*
    *   **Avoid Thin Linings:** Don't use this on very delicate, thin linings.

4.  **Safety Pins (Temporary & Quick - Use with Caution):**
    *   **Position:** Place the pin *through the lining* and *through the item* you're attaching. The pin head should point *outwards* through the jacket shell.
    *   **Caution:** Only use this if the lining is thick enough to handle a pin. **Do not** poke directly into thin, delicate linings. It's a last resort for very temporary attachment.

5.  **Reinforce with Tape (If Attaching to a Seam/Edge):**
    *   If you need to attach something *to the edge* of the lining (like a label tag), carefully fold the edge under slightly if possible. Then, use strong double-sided tape or fabric glue *on the outside* of the jacket shell, just under the edge, to secure the item. This keeps the attachment hidden and minimizes stress on the lining itself.

**Key Tips for Success:**

*   **Handle the Lining Gently:** Winter linings (especially down or synthetic fills) are often very delicate. Avoid excessive pulling or stretching.
*   **Work on the Outside:** Whenever possible, apply glue or tape *on the outside* of the jacket shell, just below the hem or seam, and secure the item there. This puts the least stress on the lining.
*   **Avoid the Seam:** If possible, attach the item slightly below a seam, as seams are reinforced and more resilient.
*   **Less is More:** Use the smallest amount of adhesive or tape necessary. A tiny dot is better than a blob.
*   **Patience:** Allow plenty of drying/curing time before testing the attachment or wearing the jacket.

Using fabric glue or fabric-safe double-sided tape applied carefully to the outside edge is generally the most reliable method for permanent attachment without damaging the lining. Safety pins are good for a quick fix but aren't permanent.
