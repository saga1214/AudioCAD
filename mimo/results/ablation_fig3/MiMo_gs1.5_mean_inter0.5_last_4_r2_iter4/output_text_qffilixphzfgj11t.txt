Here are 5 safe and nutritious green vegetables you can offer your parrot, keeping Bruno's sensitivities in mind:

1. **Spinach** (wash well): Rich in vitamins A, C, and K. *Note: High in oxalates, so offer in moderation.*
2. **Kale** (especially curly/dinosaur): Excellent source of vitamins A, C, K, and calcium.
3. **Broccoli Sprouts** (young, tender): Very high in antioxidants and vitamins. *Avoid mature broccoli (which Bruno is sensitive to).*
4. **Cucumber** (seeds/pits removed): Hydrating, low-calorie, and rich in vitamin K.
5. **Bell Peppers** (any color): Packed with vitamin C, A, and antioxidants. Great for variety!

**Important Tips:**
* **Wash Thoroughly:** Always rinse greens under cold water to remove dirt/pesticides.
* **Chop Small:** Cut into bite-sized pieces for easier digestion.
* **Introduce Slowly:** Offer new veggies in small amounts first to check for tolerance.
* **Rotate Choices:** Provide a variety to ensure balanced nutrition.
* **Avoid Sensitive Items:** Skip apples, bananas, broccoli, and dark preferences (like mature spinach/kale if Bruno dislikes dark environments).

These greens provide essential vitamins, minerals, and fiber for a healthy parrot! 🌿
