Okay, got it. For a “Retro Tech” lunch, here are a couple of ideas. First, you could do a “Mod Lunch.” This would be inspired by the 1960s space race and the early tech boom. You could serve something like a retro-futuristic sandwich, maybe with mustard and pickles, along with some tangy coleslaw and a side of salted nuts. The whole vibe is clean, colorful, and optimistic. Another idea is a “Dial-Up Delight.” This is for the 1990s, when everyone was glued to their computers. You could have a nostalgic snack like a peanut butter and banana sandwich, some pretzels, and maybe a bag of gummy bears to represent the early internet. Both of these ideas are simple, fit the theme, and include snacks.
