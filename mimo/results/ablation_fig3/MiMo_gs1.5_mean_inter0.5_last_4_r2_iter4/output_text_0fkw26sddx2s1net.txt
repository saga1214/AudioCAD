Okay, let's see. You need a shopping list for an Asian-inspired meal, keeping your husband's legume allergy and dislike for seafood in mind. I've suggested miso soup or cucumber salad, chicken teriyaki, and matcha ice cream or mango sticky rice. For the starter, if you choose miso soup, you'll need dashi, miso paste, soy sauce, mirin, and a dash of sesame oil. For the cucumber salad, you'll need cucumbers, rice vinegar, sugar, salt, and sesame oil. 

For the main course, chicken teriyaki, you'll need boneless, skinless chicken breasts, soy sauce, mirin, sake or rice vinegar, sugar, garlic, ginger, and some butter for grilling. Steamed jasmine rice will need jasmine rice, water, a pinch of salt, and maybe some toasted sesame oil for serving. Stir-fried vegetables like broccoli, bell peppers, and carrots would be good. 

For dessert, if you're making homemade matcha ice cream, you'll need matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, vanilla extract, and maybe some vanilla ice cream for serving. If you choose mango sticky rice, you'll need sticky rice, fresh mango, coconut milk, sugar, and salt. 

I'd also suggest getting some fresh herbs like cilantro or Thai basil, and maybe some toasted sesame seeds or chopped peanuts for garnish. And don't forget cooking oil, garlic, ginger, and soy sauce for the main course. Hope this helps! Enjoy your meal.
