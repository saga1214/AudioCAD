### 🧊 For Frozen Hamburgers:
- **For the Vegan Friend**: **No**, frozen hamburgers are not okay. They contain animal products (beef, dairy, eggs) and often contain cross-contamination risks.  
- **For Others**: Most people will be fine, but ensure proper thawing:  
  - **Cooler with Ice Packs**: Essential if you want them to thaw safely (to ~40°F/4°C) before eating. Never leave them in the sun!  
  - **Thawing Time**: Allow 4–6 hours in the cooler. Cook immediately after thawing to avoid foodborne illness.  

### ✅ Better Alternatives:
1. **Vegan Burgers**: Buy plant-based patties (Beyond Meat, Impossible, or homemade).  
2. **Grilled Vegetarian Options**: Veggie burgers, portobello mushrooms, or tofu skewers.  
3. **Lactose-Free/Dairy-Free Buns**: For non-vegan friends.  
4. **Ice Packs**: Always bring extra for perishables.  

### 🌟 Pro Tips:
- **Ask Directly**: A simple text to your vegan friend: *"I’m planning burgers—would you prefer a vegan option? I can grab plant-based patties!"*  
- **Dietary Flexibility**: Policemen may have allergy restrictions; volunteers often prioritize waste reduction. Offering diverse options covers all bases.  
- **Backup Plan**: If vegan burgers aren’t possible, suggest a picnic with sandwiches, fruit, or salads as alternatives.  

### 💡 Bottom Line:
**No frozen meat burgers for the vegan friend.** Use a cooler for safe thawing, but prioritize inclusive options like vegan patties. Your effort to accommodate everyone shows great care—your friends will appreciate it! 🌱🍔
