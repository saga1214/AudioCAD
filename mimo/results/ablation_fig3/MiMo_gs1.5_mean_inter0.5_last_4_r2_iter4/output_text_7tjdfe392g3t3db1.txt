Using a tuner app is the easiest way to tune while waiting! Here's how to do it for common instruments:

### For **Guitar**:
1. **Strum an open string** (e.g., the 6th string).
2. **Listen to the app** – it will show if the string is sharp (too high) or flat (too low).
3. **Adjust**:
   - If **flat** → Turn the tuning peg **clockwise** (tighten).
   - If **sharp** → Turn the tuning peg **counterclockwise** (loosen).
4. **Repeat** until the app shows the string is perfectly in tune (e.g., tuned to "E").

### For **Ukulele**:
1. **Strum the 4th string** (G string).
2. **Follow the same steps** as above. Ukuleles typically tune to **G-C-E-A**.

### Tips:
- **Strum multiple strings** to tune all of them.
- If the app is unclear, **play a simple chord** (like G major on guitar or A minor on ukulele). The app will detect all strings when you strum.
- **Tuning takes practice** – don’t worry if it’s not perfect on the first try!

**Bonus**: Apps like GuitarTuna or Fender Tune are free and accurate. Just remember: **strum → listen → adjust**! 🎸
