No problem! For drinks, here are five that might help you feel more awake. First, there's green tea, which has caffeine but also L-theanine to help you focus. Next, a good quality coffee, which is a classic pick-me-up. You could also try a cold brew, which is smoother and has less acidity. Another option is matcha, which is similar to green tea but often has a longer-lasting effect. Lastly, a bit of dark chocolate can give you a little boost and is a nice treat.

For snacks, here are five ideas. First, nuts and seeds, like almonds or walnuts, for a protein and healthy fat boost. Second, yogurt with fruit and granola. Third, hard-boiled eggs, which are high in protein. Fourth, fresh fruit like an apple or banana. And fifth, veggies with hummus, which is great for fiber and protein.
