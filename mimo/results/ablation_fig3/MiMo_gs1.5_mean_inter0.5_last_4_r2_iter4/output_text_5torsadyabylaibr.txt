For Thanksgiving, a classic and impressive choice is a homemade apple pie. It's a bit more involved, but you can make it ahead of time, which is a huge plus.

For something much simpler, you could make a no-bake pumpkin cheesecake in a mug. It only requires a few ingredients and bakes in the microwave, so it's very quick.

And for a more casual dessert, a simple fruit salad is always a crowd-pleaser. Just wash, chop, and toss some seasonal fruit together with a little bit of honey or maple syrup.
