Absolutely, Lockport has a great museum that's perfect for this. It's called the Lockport Historical Society and Museum. It's located right there in the heart of the canal's history.

Inside, you'll find exhibits that show the canal's history, including the wartime spy theories and the engineering that made it all possible. The staff are also incredibly knowledgeable and can give you all the local color and stories you could want. It's a perfect place to get the full picture.
