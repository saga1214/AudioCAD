Okay, let me break down the costs for you. For a self-install system, you'd be looking at an upfront cost of around three hundred dollars. For a professional system, that could be over a thousand.

Monthly monitoring fees are typically between twenty and sixty dollars.

Now, for the itemized costs of the equipment itself: the control panel or hub is about one hundred to four hundred dollars. Each door or window sensor is fifteen to fifty dollars. Motion detectors are thirty to eighty dollars. A camera can cost anywhere from fifty to over two hundred dollars. Glass break sensors run between thirty and one hundred dollars, and an indoor siren is typically thirty to seventy dollars.

Keep in mind that professional installation adds a fee on top of these equipment costs.
