Here’s a comprehensive, low-mess shopping list for your theme park bachelor party, designed for grab-and-go convenience and minimal cleanup:

### **Drinks (Per Person = 1 Large & 1 Small)**
- **Water bottles** (20 large, 20 small)  
- **Sugar-free sodas** (20 cans/2L bottles per adult group)  
- **Juice boxes** (20, 12oz, 100% fruit-based)  
- **Electrolyte drinks** (10 boxes, for hot days)  
- **Ice packs** (for coolers)  

### **Snacks (Per Person = 1 Small Pack)**  
- **Cheese sticks** (10 packs of 3 sticks each)  
- **Grapes** (3 lbs, pre-washed)  
- **Pretzel sticks** (2 large bags, individual packs)  
- **Trail mix** (10 single-serve bags)  
- **Baby carrots** (2 lbs)  
- **Single-serve hummus cups** (10)  
- **Apple slices** (5 lbs, pre-cut)  
- **Granola bars** (10 boxes, 10 bars each)  

### **Main Meals (Pre-Made, Per Person)**  
- **Mini calzones** (20, from bakery/pizzeria)  
  *Fillings: Margherita, cheese, pepperoni, veggie*  
- **Wrap/sandwiches** (20 pre-made, individual wraps/sandwiches)  
  *Fillings: Turkey & cheese, hummus & veggies, chicken Caesar*  

### **Desserts (Per Person = 1)**  
- **Mini cupcakes** (10 boxes, 12 cupcakes each)  
- **Brownie bites** (10 individual packs)  

### **Extras & Essentials**  
- **Reusable cooler bags** (2-3 large insulated bags)  
- **Reusable containers** (for snacks/meals)  
- **Wet wipes & hand sanitizer** (10 travel-sized bottles)  
- **Sunscreen** (10 mini bottles)  
- **Ziplock bags** (for extra snacks)  

---

### **Tips for Success**  
1. **Prep Ahead**: Assemble snacks in containers, portion drinks, and pre-freeze calzones/wraps.  
2. **Label Coolers**: Mark with contents so groups can grab what they need.  
3. **Hydration Station**: Set up a cooler with water/sodas at a park bench for refills.  
4. **Kid-Friendly**: Include a "snack station" with fruit, cheese, and pretzels for easy access.  
5. **Cleanup**: Pack trash bags and wipes for on-the-go tidying.  

This plan balances nutrition, variety, and portability—keeping everyone energized and focused on fun! Enjoy the party! 🎢🎉
