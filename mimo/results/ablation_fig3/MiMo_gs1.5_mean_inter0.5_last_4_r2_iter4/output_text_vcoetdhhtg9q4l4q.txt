Absolutely! Here are some super quick dinner ingredient ideas that keep things simple and stress-free:

### 🍳 For Speed & Minimal Effort:
1. **Rice Noodle Bowls**:  
   Use one of your noodles + canned protein (tofu, chicken, or shrimp) + frozen veggies + soy sauce/tamari. *5 mins prep!*
2. **Miso Soup**:  
   Canned miso paste + instant noodles (discard noodles) + frozen fish/veggies + hot water. *2 mins!*
3. **Avocado Toast**:  
   Whole-grain bread + mashed avocado + canned beans (black or chickpeas) + chili flakes. *3 mins!*
4. **Canned Salmon Bowl**:  
   Canned salmon + microwaved sweet potato + steamed broccoli + olive oil. *4 mins!*

### 🛒 Smart Pantry Staples to Keep:
- **Canned Goods**: Beans, tomatoes, tuna, coconut milk.  
- **Frozen Veggies**: Peas, spinach, stir-fry mixes.  
- **Pre-Cooked Proteins**: Rotisserie chicken, hard-boiled eggs, tofu.  
- **Rice/Pasta**: Keep 1-2 go-tos (like your rice noodles!).
