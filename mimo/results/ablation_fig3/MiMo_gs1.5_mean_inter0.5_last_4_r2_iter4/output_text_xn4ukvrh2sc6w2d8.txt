Alright, here is a complete cocktail menu with all the recipes, ingredients, and measurements, perfect for your Art Deco airport reception in Kentucky. 

First, the Old Fashioned. You'll need two ounces of bourbon or rye, one sugar cube, or half an ounce of simple syrup. Then, two dashes of Angostura bitters, and an orange twist for garnish. To make it, muddle the sugar cube with a little bitters and water until it dissolves, add the bourbon, stir with ice, strain into a rocks glass over a new ice cube, and then express the oils from the orange peel over the top.

Next, the Paper Plane. For this, you'll use two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of Aperol, half an ounce of Amaro Nonino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice. You just shake that with ice, strain it into a coupe glass, and garnish with a lemon twist.

For the Whiskey Sour with egg white, you'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and one egg white. First, do a dry shake with all those ingredients to get a foam. Then add ice and shake again. Strain it into a rocks glass over ice and garnish with a cherry.

Then there's the Aviation. You'll need two ounces of gin, half an ounce of maraschino liqueur, a quarter-ounce of crème de violette, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Shake that with ice, strain it into a cocktail glass, and garnish with a maraschino cherry.

And for the third gin cocktail, the French 75. You'll mix one and a half ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. Dry shake that to emulsify the syrup, then add ice and shake again. Finally, strain it into a champagne flute and top with about three ounces of Prosecco or another sparkling wine. Garnish with a lemon twist.
