Of course! Some good options: First, Labrador Retrievers are friendly, trainable, and great with kids, though they do shed. Next, Golden Retrievers are also gentle, patient, and sociable with other dogs. Another one is the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel; they’re small, affectionate, and adapt well to apartment living. You could also consider a Basset Hound; they’re laid-back and low-energy. For a mix, a Labradoodle is popular, but be sure to check their temperament. A Cockapoo is another hybrid, combining a Cocker Spaniel with a Poodle, which is often small and playful. And finally, a French Bulldog is low-shedding, adaptable, and doesn’t need a lot of exercise.
