### 🌵 **Phase 1: Joshua Tree, CA (Adventure Start)**
*   **Drive:** ~3 hours from SoCal (LA/San Diego).
*   **Stay:** **Cottonwood Campground** (inside Joshua Tree NP). *Reserve ASAP!* (~$20/night, includes showers in nearby town).
*   **Vibe:** Iconic boulders, Joshua Trees, hidden canyons, incredible stargazing. Perfect for a first adventure.
*   **Tip:** Pack layers (desert nights are chilly!), download offline maps, and check weather.

### 🏜️ **Phase 2: Nevada Adventure (Desert to Mountains)**
*   **Drive:** From Joshua Tree to **Mesquite, NV** (~3 hours).
*   **Stay:** **Mesquite** has numerous RV/van-friendly campgrounds like **Desert View Campground** (~$25/night, basic). Or explore **BLM Land** near **Black Rock Canyon** (free dispersed camping - *research specific areas first*).
*   **Vibe:** Shift from iconic desert to high desert mountains. Mesquite offers casinos, food, and a quirky vibe. BLM land offers pure solitude.
*   **Adventure:** Hike **Valley of Fire SP** (1 hour from Mesquite) - stunning red rock formations. Or explore **Great Basin NP** (remote, high-altitude forests).

### 🏜️ **Phase 3: Nevada Wilderness (Remote & Scenic)**
*   **Drive:** From Mesquite towards **Ely, NV** (~2.5 hours).
*   **Stay:** **Ely** has campgrounds like **Ely Campground** (~$30/night). Or find dispersed spots near **Ely** or along **US-93**.
*   **Vibe:** High desert mountains, wide-open skies, potential for wildlife (bighorn sheep, mule deer).
*   **Adventure:** **Lone Mountain** (near Ely) - historic mining ghost town. **Mount Charleston** (near Las Vegas, but worth detouring) - alpine forests & lakes (check road conditions!).

### 🏖️ **Phase 4: Vegas Reset (Luxury & Fun)**
*   **Drive:** From Ely to **Las Vegas, NV** (~3.5 hours).
*   **Stay:** **Luxury:** Bellagio (fountains!), Aria (pool), Cosmopolitan (lively). **Mid-Range:** The LINQ (pool, games), Tropicana (location). **Budget:** Stratosphere (views, pool), or a nearby motel.
*   **Vibe:** Pure indulgence! Showrooms, incredible restaurants, pools, games, shopping, and nightlife.
*   **Essential Vegas Experience:**
    *   **A Night Out:** See a show (Cirque du Soleil, residencies), hit a club, or explore Fremont Street Experience (free, vintage Vegas vibe).
    *   **Pampering:** Book a spa day (many hotels offer packages).
    *   **Food & Drink:** Try diverse cuisines (from Michelin stars to late-night eats). Enjoy craft cocktails.
    *   **Pool Time:** Many hotels have stunning pools – perfect for relaxing after adventure.
    *   **Casino Fun:** Try your luck, but remember it's entertainment!

3.  **Respect Nature:** Pack out ALL trash, stay on trails, leave no trace (especially on BLM land).

5.  **Weather Prep:** Desert nights are cold (bring blankets/insulation). NV days can be hot. Vegas is warm year-round.
6.  **Flexibility:** Nevada's beauty is often found on scenic detours. Embrace the unexpected!
