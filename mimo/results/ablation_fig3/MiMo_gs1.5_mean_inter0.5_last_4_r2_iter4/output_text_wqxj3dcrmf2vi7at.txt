Here's a full recap of treat ideas for your adorable corgi princess, tailored to her coat color and safety:

### 🍪 **Homemade Treats**  
1. **Pumpkin or Sweet Potato Bites**:  
   - Mash canned pumpkin (not pie filling!) or steamed sweet potato, mold into small discs, and freeze.  
   - *Why she’ll love it*: Cooling, smelly, and healthy!  

2. **Peanut Butter & Oat Cookies**:  
   - Mix peanut butter (NO xylitol!) with rolled oats, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15 mins.  
   - *Tip*: Cut into tiny cubes—perfect for training!  

3. **Carrot or Zucchini Sticks**:  
   - Steam veggies until soft, then dehydrate (or air-dry) for chewy, crunchy snacks.  

### 🛒 **Store-Bought Brands**  
1. **Zuke’s Mini LifeStyle Treats**:  
   - Tiny, high-protein, and come in brown/white bags (like her coat!).  

2. **Blue Buffalo Home Style Grain-Free Bites**:  
   - Look for peanut butter or chicken flavors.  

3. **Greenies Dental Chews**:  
   - Small chew-sized for oral care (choose "small breed" sizes).  

4. **Merrick Beneful Bites**:  
   - Recipe-aided, grain-inclusive options (great for sensitive tummies).  

### 📌 **Key Tips**  
- **Portion Control**: Stick to 1-2 tbsp peanut butter per week.  
- **Safety First**: Avoid onions, grapes, chocolate, and raw chicken bones.  
- **Freeze Treats**: Pumpkin/sweet potato bites double as teething relief!  

Princess will adore these—especially the frozen pumpkin bites and Zuke’s mini treats! 🐾💖
