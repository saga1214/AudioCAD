That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour is right on a beautiful, protected bay, perfect for snorkeling and relaxing. Red Hook itself is the heart of the East End's action. Here are some great daytime activities right around there:

1.  **Snorkeling & Water Sports:** This is the obvious winner!
    *   **Coki Beach:** Just a short walk from Secret Harbour and Red Hook. It's one of the best beaches on St. Thomas, famous for its calm, clear water teeming with fish, rays, and sometimes even turtles. Perfect for snorkeling, paddleboarding, and kayaking right from the shore.
    *   **Secret Harbour Beach:** Right where you're staying! The calm bay is great for paddleboarding or just floating.

2.  **Explore Red Hook Village:** It's buzzing!
    *   **Duffy's Love Shack:** Grab a drink at this iconic spot (famous for happy hour) and soak up the Red Hook atmosphere.
    *   **The Longboard:** Another great bar and restaurant known for its patio, live music (as mentioned), and happy hour.
    *   **Morgan's Mango:** A classic dive bar with a huge menu, live music, and a fun, relaxed vibe.
    *   **Cruze Bay:** Take a short walk or drive into Cruz Bay, the main village just north of Red Hook. Browse local shops, art galleries, and find more restaurants and bars.

3.  **Visit the Red Hook Market (Sundays):** If you're there on a Sunday morning, the market is fantastic! It's a huge, vibrant outdoor market with fresh produce, local crafts, souvenirs, clothing, and incredible street food. It's a great way to experience local culture and find unique items.

4.  **Visit the Water Park:** For a more active family outing (if your brother is up for it!), drive up the hill to the **Red Hook Water Park**. It's a well-maintained facility with slides, a wave pool, and lazy river – perfect for cooling off and having fun.

5.  **Explore the Nature Trail:** For a quieter, more natural walk, head to the **Coki Beach Nature Trail**. It's a short, easy trail that leads down to Coki Beach and offers lovely views and a chance to spot wildlife like birds and lizards.

6.  **Take a Boat Charter (Beyond Snorkeling):** Since you're already chartering a boat, consider using it for more than just getting around! You could:
    *   **Explore nearby islands:** Visit St. John (like Trunk Bay or Coki Beach on St. John) or even Charlotte Amalie on St. Thomas.
    *   **Go fishing:** Many charters offer day trips for deep-sea fishing.
    *   **Relax and explore:** Just cruise around, soak in the views, and find hidden coves.

**Key things to remember for your trip:**

*   **Sun Protection:** The Virgin Islands sun is intense. Hats, rash guards, reef-safe sunscreen, and sunglasses are essential.
*   **Hydration:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
*   **Transportation:** Red Hook is walkable, but Cruz Bay is a short drive or taxi ride away. The Water Park is a short drive up the hill. Parking near Secret Harbour and Red Hook is usually available but can get busy.
*   **Easiness:** Red Hook and Secret Harbour offer a great mix of activity without needing to drive far. You can easily spend a full day exploring beaches, snacks, drinks, and the village vibe.

Enjoy your week! The combination of Secret Harbour's calm beauty and Red Hook's lively energy should make for an amazing trip.
