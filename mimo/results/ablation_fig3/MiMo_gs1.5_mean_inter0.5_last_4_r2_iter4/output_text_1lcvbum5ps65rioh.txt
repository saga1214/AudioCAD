### **U-Turn Rules in British Columbia**  
Here’s a clear breakdown to help you drive safely:  

1. **Signal First**: Always use your turn signal.  
2. **Visibility**: Must see **clearly in both directions** for at least 150 meters (about 1.5 football fields).  
3. **Prohibited Areas**:  
   - At traffic signals, stop signs, or yield signs **unless a sign allows it**.  
   - Near curves, hills, or where you can’t see far ahead.  
   - In bike lanes or crosswalks.  
   - On multi-lane roads without a median.  
4. **Penalties**:  
   - **Fines**: Up to **$171** (2024 BC standard).  
   - **Points**: 1–3 demerit points on your license (varies by violation).  
   - Repeat offenses may lead to a driving course or license suspension.  

**Tip**: Use designated U-turn lanes (if available) or intersections where signs permit.

### **Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients**  
For 4 servings, here’s a classic minimalist list (adjust for preferences):  

#### **Core Ingredients**:  
1. **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti or bucatini.  
2. **Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 1 extra yolk (for richness).  
3. **Cheese**:  
   - 150g **Pecorino Romano** (or Parmigiano-Reggiano if unavailable).  
   - 50g grated Parmesan.  
4. **Meat**:  
   - 150g **guanciale** (cured pork cheek, best) or pancetta (smoked bacon).  
5. **Aromatics**:  
   - 2 garlic cloves (minced).  
   - 1 tbsp black pepper (freshly cracked).  
6. **Extras**:  
   - 1 tsp red pepper flakes (optional for heat).  
   - Salt for pasta water.  

#### **Method**:  
1. Cook pasta in salted water until *al dente* (reserve 1 cup pasta water).  
2. Fry diced guanciale/pancetta in a pan until crispy. Add garlic and pepper flakes.  
3. Mix eggs, cheese, yolk, and black pepper in a bowl.  
4. Toss pasta with hot guanciale mixture off-heat (to avoid scrambling eggs). Add pasta water to emulsify.  

**Avoid**: Cream, onions, or mushrooms—traditional carbonara relies on eggs and cheese!
