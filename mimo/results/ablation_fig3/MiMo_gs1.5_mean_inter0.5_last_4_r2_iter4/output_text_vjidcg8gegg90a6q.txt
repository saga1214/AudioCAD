For winter chicks in their own area, the safest and best bedding is wood shavings, specifically the large flake kind. They provide great insulation and are easy to clean.

Avoid using pine shavings that are treated with a chemical called phenolic, as that can be irritating. Also, avoid straw or hay in that area, as you're worried about rodents.

To make it super easy, you can even use the rubber mats we talked about earlier. Just cover them with a thick layer of wood shavings. This gives them a warm, dry, and rodent-resistant floor to sleep on.
