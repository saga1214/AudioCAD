Okay, Austin has plenty of great casual spots for a relaxed dinner. I'd say you can't go wrong with places like Taco Deli, which is famous for its tacos, or Veracruz All Natural for amazing migas tacos. If you're in the mood for something a bit different, Home Slice Pizza is a classic. Or for a simple, cozy vibe, check out Odd Duck—they do creative Southern food. The main thing is to go where the locals are. That's usually where the best food is. Enjoy the food tour!
