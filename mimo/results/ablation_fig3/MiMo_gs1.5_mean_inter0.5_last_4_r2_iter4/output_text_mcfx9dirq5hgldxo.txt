That's a great plan. In Madrid, you have to try their tapas. 'Patatas bravas' are these delicious spicy potatoes, and 'jamón ibérico' is a world-famous cured ham.

For dinner, you should look for a 'restaurante tradicional'—that means a traditional restaurant—and ask for 'la menú del día', which is their daily special. And if you want dessert, 'churros con chocolate' are a Spanish classic. Enjoy your trip!
