You two sound like a dynamic fire sign duo! For an adventurous weekend getaway, Calgary is surrounded by fantastic options.

One of the most popular is Banff. It's a short drive and offers everything from stunning mountain scenery to world-class hiking and skiing. It's a classic for a reason.

Another great option is Canmore. It's a bit closer and has a more relaxed vibe, but it's still right in the heart of the Rockies and offers amazing hiking and wildlife viewing.

If you want something a bit more rugged and off-the-beaten-path, you could head to the Kananaskis Country. It has incredible hiking and a more wild, less touristy feel.

And for something completely different, there's the Ghost Rocks. It's an old mining town with a spooky, adventurous history. You could take a hike and explore the old mine shafts. It's a unique experience, but please be aware that exploring the mines is very dangerous and requires proper equipment, so it's not for just anyone.
