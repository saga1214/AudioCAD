Great point—staying on budget while keeping desserts appealing is key! Here are some **cost-effective, diabetes-friendly dessert ideas** that are also kid-friendly and easy to prepare in bulk:

### Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:
1. **Fresh Fruit Skewers**:  
   - Thread grapes, berries, melon, pineapple, and kiwi onto skewers.  
   - *Why it works*: Inexpensive, colorful, hydrating, and naturally sweet. Kids love picking their own!

2. **Homemade Frozen Yogurt Bark**:  
   - Spread Greek yogurt mixed with honey or maple syrup on a tray. Top with crushed nuts, berries, and dark chocolate chips (use sparingly). Freeze, then break into pieces.  
   - *Why it works*: Uses pantry staples, customizable, and portion-controlled.

3. **Fruit Pies or Bars**:  
   - Make low-sugar oatmeal or almond-based crusts. Fill with mashed seasonal fruit (e.g., apples, pears) and a touch of cinnamon.  
   - *Why it works*: Uses affordable seasonal produce and whole-grain crusts.

4. **DIY Ice Cream Sundae Bar**:  
   - Serve low-sugar ice cream (or sorbet) with toppings:  
     - Fresh fruit slices  
     - Unsweetened cocoa powder  
     - Chopped nuts  
     - A sprinkle of dark chocolate (70%+ cacao)  
   - *Why it works*: Lets kids customize while keeping sugar low.

5. **Oatmeal Cookies**:  
   - Bake in batches with oats, applesauce, cinnamon, and minimal sugar. Freeze extras to save on costs.  
   - *Why it works*: Uses cheap ingredients, high in fiber, and easy to scale.

### Budget-Saving Tips:
- **Batch Cooking**: Make desserts in large quantities (e.g., 20+ skewers at once).  
- **Seasonal Produce**: Buy fruits/vegetables when they’re in season—much cheaper!  
- **Ask for Donations**: Local bakeries, grocery stores, or restaurants might donate desserts for the cause.  
- **Minimal Sugar**: Skip syrups/jellies; rely on fruit’s natural sweetness.  
- **Simple Presentation**: Use paper cones for ice cream, bamboo skewers for fruit, or reusable jars for yogurt bark.

### Example Dessert Spread:
- **Main**: Fruit skewers + yogurt bark  
- **Side**: Small oatmeal cookies + dark chocolate chips  
- **Sundae Bar**: Low-sugar ice cream with toppings
