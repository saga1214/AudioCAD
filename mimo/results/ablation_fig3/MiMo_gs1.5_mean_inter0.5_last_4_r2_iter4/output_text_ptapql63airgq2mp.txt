### **Simple & Crowd-Pleasing Snacks**

   - Lightly toasted popcorn tossed with premium matcha powder.  
3. **Kimchi Seaweed Wraps**:  
   - Pre-portioned rice paper rolls with kimchi, avocado, and fresh herbs.

### **Unique & Exotic Choices**  
4. **Dried Seaweed & Kimchi Bento Station**:  
   - **Wakame or Dried Squid**: Crunchy, chewy, packed with oceanic umami.  
   - **Kimchi**: Offer small bowls of spicy-sweet fermented cabbage.  
5. **Tamarind "Candy Canes"**:  
   - Japanese-style tamarind candies (sour-sweet, sticky, and nostalgic).  
6. **Wasabi Pea & Shrimp Curls**:  
   - Savory-sweet shrimp snacks dusted with wasabi powder.  
7. **Mochi Taco Bar**:  
   - **Base**: Sweet red bean paste (anko) or savory mushroom paste.  
   - **Toppings**: Saffron powder, sesame, or grated daikon.  
8. **Snow Monkey Hot Cocoa**:  
   - Spiced cocoa with a hint of ginger and a pinch of wasabi for heat.

### **Interactive & Fun Elements**  
- **DIY Tare Bar**: Let guests assemble their own dipping sauces (soy, ponzu, yuzu).  
- **Umami Bomb Station**:  
  - Dried squid, anchovy crackers, and pickled radish.  
- **Gourmet Rice Crackers**:  
  - Offer matcha, wasabi, and salted yuzu-flavored crackers.

### **Why This Works**

- **Easy Prep**: Most components can be prepped ahead.
