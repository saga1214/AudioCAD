That's a great question! Dallas has a fantastic Asian food scene. Here are some top spots for Asian noodles, broken down by type and neighborhood:

**For Authentic & Flavorful Noodles:**

1.  **Little Elm (Downtown Dallas):** The heart of the Asian food scene. You'll find incredible options:
    *   **Szechuan Kitchen:** Famous for spicy Szechuan noodles (Dan Dan, Mapo Tofu).
    *   **Thai Basil:** Classic Thai noodle dishes (Pad See Ew, Tom Kha Gai).
    *   **Korean Noodle House:** Great for Ramyeon (Korean ramen), Bibimbap, and other Korean staples.
    *   **Many others:** Explore the entire area – there are dozens of great noodle spots.

2.  **Asian Market Area (Plano):** Near the Plano East Farmers Market, this area has a concentration of Asian eateries:
    *   **Yamashiro:** Japanese-inspired dishes, including good ramen and udon.
    *   **Lotus of Siam:** Award-winning Thai restaurant (though higher end, their Pad See Ew is legendary).
    *   **Sichuan Kitchen:** Another excellent Szechuan option.
    *   **Wok 'n Roll:** Great for casual ramen and stir-fries.

3.  **Greektown (Downtown Dallas):** While known for Greek, it also has a good mix:
    *   **Oriental Noodle House:** A classic, reliable spot for Chinese-American noodle dishes.
    *   **Tao:** Upscale Chinese with great noodles.

4.  **Korean Town (South Dallas - near Grand Ave):**
    *   **Tosokchon:** Famous for their BBQ, but also has great Korean noodles (Jjamppong, Myeokchon Ramyeon).
    *   **Other local spots:** Explore the area for more Korean noodle specialists.
