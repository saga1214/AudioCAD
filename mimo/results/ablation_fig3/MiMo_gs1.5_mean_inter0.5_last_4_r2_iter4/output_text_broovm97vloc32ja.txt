That's a fantastic question, and the answer is a resounding **yes**. If you can write and illustrate, you should absolutely consider submitting a self-published picture book. Many publishers actively seek out creators like you. Your illustrations are a huge asset. You can submit your entire manuscript and art to publishers through platforms like Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, or even directly to some literary agents. The process is very similar to a traditional submission, just with the added visual element.
