So you'll need to swap those out for something like beef or turkey brats. For the fruit and veggie platter, hummus is usually fine, so that's safe. The blood pudding is a red flag because it's made with blood, which isn't kosher. You'd need to find a dairy-based pudding or something else. If you're serving Pinot Noir, make sure it's kosher-certified, but that's a good call since it's not a dairy wine. Just swap the bacon for a different sausage and the blood pudding for something else, and you're good to go.
