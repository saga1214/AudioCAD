Don't worry, a finicky glue gun is the craft villain we all know and tolerate! Here's a quick troubleshooting guide while you wait for that hot glue to cooperate:

1.  **Check the Tip:** Is the tip clean? Sometimes a tiny bit of dried glue or debris can clog it. Try cleaning it with a toothpick or a pin.
2.  **Check the Temperature:** Is the cord plugged in securely? If it's a newer glue gun, make sure it's fully charged or that the heating element has had time to warm up completely (usually 10-15 mins). If it's an older one, it might need a gentle nudge – try warming it up again.
3.  **Check the Flow:** If it *looks* hot but *doesn't* flow, it might be too cold or clogged. Try:
    *   **Warming it up again:** Give it another full cycle.
    *   **Tapping the tip:** Gently tap the heated tip on the edge of a table or counter. Sometimes this dislodges a tiny clog.
    *   **Using a hairdryer:** If it's not working at all, blow some warm air (not hot) onto the tip for 30-60 seconds. This often gets it flowing.
4.  **Try a New Stick:** If it *looks* hot but *doesn't* stick, try a new glue stick. Old or low-quality sticks don't bond well.
5.  **Check the Surface:** Make sure the spot you're gluing to is clean, dry, and smooth. A little bit of dust or moisture can prevent sticking.
