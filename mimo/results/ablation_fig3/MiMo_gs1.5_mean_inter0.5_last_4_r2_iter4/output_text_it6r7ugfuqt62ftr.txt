Of course! Since you're coming from Northern California, I'd suggest a trip to Big Sur. It's perfect for warm weather swimming and a full family adventure.

Here’s a detailed four-day itinerary I've put together for you. I've focused on places with accommodations that can accommodate gluten-free needs.

On Day One, you'll arrive in Big Sur. I'd recommend staying at Ventana Big Sur. It's a beautiful luxury resort with private plunge pools, and they are very experienced with dietary restrictions. After you check in, you can rent bikes and ride along the scenic McWay Falls road. For dinner, Ventana has a fantastic restaurant on-site called Ventana Cafe, where you can just inform them about your daughter's Celiac needs.

For Day Two, you could take a hike at Pfeiffer Beach to see the famous purple sand. In the afternoon, you could explore Nepenthe Restaurant, which has amazing ocean views. For dinner, Nepenthe is also very accommodating for gluten-free meals.

On Day Three, you could visit the iconic Bixby Creek Bridge and the nearby McWay Falls. In the afternoon, you could take a short drive to the Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park to see the elephant seals. For dinner, I'd suggest the Point Sur Inn, which has a restaurant that's very experienced with Celiac needs.

Finally, on Day Four, you can take a scenic drive along the coast to San Simeon. You could visit the Grizzly Bear State Park and even see the wild bears. For your last dinner, the Point Sur Inn is a great choice again.

Just a couple of final tips: make sure to book your accommodations and reservations well in advance, especially for the peak season. And as always, it's a good idea to call ahead to confirm any restaurant's gluten-free policies. I hope you have a wonderful trip!
