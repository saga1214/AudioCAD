For a romantic coastal getaway, you could start with a private sunset cruise. Many places offer these, and the golden light on the water is perfect for creating a sense of intimacy.

If you're near a larger city, you might check for a harbor cruise with gourmet dining onboard. Some even offer themed menus, like a 'tasting menu at sea,' which would be a wonderful experience.

If you want something more adventurous, you could look into kayaking or paddleboarding tours, especially if they start or end with a beach picnic.
