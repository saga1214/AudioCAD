Yes, there's a good chance that for the Wampanoag people who were at the feast, the day was happy. The story of the harvest feast itself is probably true.

But the bigger issue is that the traditional Thanksgiving holiday, as we know it, was created much later. It was shaped by people who wanted to celebrate a time of survival and prosperity, completely ignoring the tragic consequences for Native Americans.

So, the story of the feast itself is probably true, but the story of Thanksgiving as a national holiday is a selective and incomplete one.
