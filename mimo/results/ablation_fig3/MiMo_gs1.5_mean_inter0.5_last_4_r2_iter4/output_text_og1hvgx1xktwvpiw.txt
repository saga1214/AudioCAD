Okay, I see what's confusing here. Let me clarify that. The one and a quarter to one to one ratio is actually the original and most common Boulevardier recipe. Your friend's equal-parts version is a popular variation. As for the Last Word, it's an entirely different drink made with three-quarters of an ounce of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. You can totally make a Last Word, it's a delicious choice. Just remember to shake it, not stir it, and add a lime wedge for garnish. Hope that helps clear things up!
