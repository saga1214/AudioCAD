That's a very smart observation. Older books are almost always rarer, which is a big plus. However, their condition is extremely important, and finding affordable copies in good shape can be a challenge. I can give you ten historically significant books from that era, but I must stress that you should only consider affordable copies, maybe in what's called 'low grade' or 'fair condition'. 

For Luke Cage specifically, you can find affordable copies of *All-Star Comics* number three, which features the first team-up of the JLA and the JSA, where the Hulk first appeared. You can also find *The Spectacular Spider-Man* number one, which introduced Luke as a guest star.

For other key issues under a hundred dollars, there's *Captain America Comics* number one, the first appearance of Cap. *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven is the first appearance of Batman. *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen is the first appearance of Spider-Man. *Tales of Suspense* number thirty-nine is the first appearance of the Hulk. *The Fantastic Four* number one is their debut. And *The Amazing Adventures of Spider-Man* number one is the first appearance of Doctor Strange. 

But please be very cautious. The condition of a 70-year-old book is critical. You should always buy from a reputable dealer who grades it, or if you're buying raw, buy from a trusted source and have it graded. And be very careful about fakes.
