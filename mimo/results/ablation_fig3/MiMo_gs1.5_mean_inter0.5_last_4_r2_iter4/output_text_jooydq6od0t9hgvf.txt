Here are quick, elegant recipes for both drinks—perfect for your dinner party:

### 1. **Vodka Sour (Simple & Refreshing)**  
*(Vegetarian-friendly, non-alcoholic option: use lemon-lime soda)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz simple syrup (or 1 tsp sugar)  
- Ice  
- Optional: 1 egg white (for silky texture)  

**Method:**  
1. Shake vodka, lemon juice, simple syrup, and egg white (if using) with ice.  
2. Strain into a chilled coupe or rocks glass.  
3. Garnish with a lemon twist or cherry.  

---

### 2. **Argentinian-Style Fernet (Herbal & Bitter)**  
*(Similar to Fernet-Branca—digestif-style)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz fernet (or Argentinian herbal liqueur like Fernet-Brancos)  
- 2 oz sparkling water (or soda water)  
- Ice  
- Lemon twist (optional garnish)  

**Method:**  
1. Pour fernet over ice in a highball glass.  
2. Top with sparkling water.  
3. Express a lemon twist over the drink and drop it in.  

---

### Quick Tips:  
- **Vodka Mixers:** Keep it simple with vodka + soda water + lime.  
- **Fernet Pairing:** Serve with aged cheese or spicy appetizers to balance its bitterness.  
- **Non-Alcoholic Option:** For fernet lovers, offer a herbal tea infusion (steep fernet in hot water, sweeten lightly).
