Absolutely! Here’s a comprehensive shopping list based on our menu plan, organized by category for easy reference. I've included quantities for **24 people** (20 adults + 4 kids) and noted any kid-friendly adjustments:

---

### **Appetizers & Finger Foods**  
- Mini slider buns (12–16)  
- Ground beef/pork (for sliders, ~1 lb)  
- Shrimp (1–1.5 lbs for shrimp cocktail)  
- Cherry tomatoes (1 pint)  
- Celery sticks (1 bunch)  
- Pigs in blanket: sausage links (1 lb) + cream cheese (1 block)  
- Tortilla chips (1 large bag)  
- Guacamole (1 tub)  
- Salsa (1–2 jars)  

### **Kid-Friendly Snacks**  
- Grape juice boxes (8–10 boxes)  
- Apple juice (for adults/kids, 1–2 gallons)  
- Baby carrots (1 bag)  
- Hummus cups (or make your own)  
- Goldfish crackers (1 bag)  
- Mini muffins (for dessert)  

### **Drinks**  
- Champagne/Prosecco (4–6 bottles)  
- Sparkling water (2–3 gallons)  
- Soda (2–3 12-packs: cola, lemon-lime)  
- Ice (10–12 bags)  
- Wine (optional: 1–2 bottles)  
- Cocktail ingredients (whiskey, vodka, etc., if serving)  

### **Main Dish: Ham**  
- Spiral-cut ham (12–14 lbs)  
- Honey mustard glaze (or honey + Dijon)  
- Cooking oil (for roasting)  
- Fresh herbs (rosemary/thyme, 1 bunch)  
- Baking sheet + aluminum foil  
- Leftover ham for sandwiches!  

### **Sides**  
- Scalloped potatoes: potatoes (5 lbs), butter, cream, flour  
- Roasted carrots (olive oil, salt): **no glaze** to avoid mess  
- Roasted asparagus (olive oil, lemon zest, salt)  
- Mixed greens for a simple salad (or bagged salad)  
- Mustard/dressing (for salad)  

### **Desserts**  
- Fruit platter (grapes, berries, melon)  
- Mini cupcakes (or homemade)  
- Whipped cream (1 tub)  
- Red velvet cake (optional)  

### **Extras**  
- Kids’ plates/cutlery (extra sets)  
- Balloons, streamers, or noisemakers  
- Party hats (for kids)  
- Napkins (plenty!)  
- Trash bags  
- Cleaning supplies (for spills)  

---

### **Pro Tips**  
1. **Prep Ahead**: Make scalloped potatoes, glaze ham, and chop veggies the day before.  
2. **Kid Zone**: Set up a small table with juice boxes, crackers, and carrots to keep little ones occupied.  
3. **Music**: Create a playlist (add some upbeat classics like "Auld Lang Syne" and modern hits).  
4. **Safety**: Keep a first-aid kit handy for minor splinters or scrapes.
