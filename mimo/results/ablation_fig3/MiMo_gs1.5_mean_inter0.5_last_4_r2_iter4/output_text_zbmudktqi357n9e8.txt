Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for inspiration! For watercolor, you’ll want the colors to be at their most vibrant. I’d say aim for late fall, like October to November. That’s when Mexico gets its most stunning autumn foliage—especially in places like Chiapas, Oaxaca, or the Yucatán Peninsula. The light is softer then, too, which is great for painting.

You could also think about coastal areas like Tulum or Cancún, where you can practice both river and ocean scenes together. The contrast between the bright blues of the water and the warm autumn colors of the landscape is just perfect for watercolor. 

And hey, if you’re into space scenes, you can even find some stargazing spots in Mexico. Places like the Copper Canyon or the Baja Peninsula offer dark skies. So you can get inspiration from both the autumn colors and the night sky. 

Just pack your paints, find a quiet spot, and start practicing. Mexico’s beauty will definitely help with your technique. Enjoy the trip and the painting!
