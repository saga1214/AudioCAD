That sounds like a fantastic theme! Here are 5 ocean-inspired mains and 5 sweet desserts, keeping your fiancée's dietary needs in mind:

**Mains:**
1. **Grilled Shrimp Skewers with Mango Salsa:** (Shellfish-free, diabetic-friendly)  
   *Toss large shrimp with olive oil, garlic, paprika, and lemon zest. Grill or pan-sear. Serve with a fresh salsa of diced mango, red onion, cilantro, lime juice, and a touch of honey.*  
   *(Spicy & sweet, uses mango!)*

2. **Pan-Seared Tuna Steak with Avocado-Lime Slaw:** (Shellfish-free, diabetic-friendly)  
   *Sear tuna steaks to medium-rare. Serve with a slaw made of shredded cabbage, avocado, lime juice, a little honey, and sesame seeds.*  
   *(Rich, lean protein, refreshing slaw.)*

3. **Spicy Mango & Coconut Curry with Firm Tofu:** (Shellfish-free, diabetic-friendly)  
   *Make a vibrant curry base with coconut milk, ginger, turmeric, red curry paste (adjust spice!), and coconut sugar. Simmer with cubed firm tofu, peas, and carrots. Serve over brown rice or cauliflower rice. Top with fresh mango slices.*  
   *(Spicy, sweet, uses mango! High protein & fiber.)*

4. **Baked Salmon with Lemon-Dill Cream Cheese & Roasted Vegetables:** (Shellfish-free)  
   *Bake salmon fillets with lemon slices and dill. Serve with roasted asparagus or zucchini. Offer a side of lemon-dill cream cheese (low-fat if preferred) for dipping.*  
   *(Classic, healthy, lean protein.)*

5. **"Crab" Cakes with Remoulade (Vegan Option):** (Shellfish-free, diabetic-friendly option)  
   *Make cakes with mashed black beans or chickpeas,老豆腐, breadcrumbs (or almond flour), chopped celery, onion, bell pepper, Old Bay seasoning, and flax egg. Pan-fry. Serve with a tangy remoulade sauce (use vegan mayo if needed).*  
   *(Texture like crab, flavorful, can be vegan-adapted.)*

**Desserts:**
1. **Mango-Coconut Mousse:** (Shellfish-free, diabetic-friendly)  
   *Blend ripe mango, coconut milk, a touch of honey, and gelatin. Chill and layer in glasses with toasted coconut flakes. Serve with fresh mango slices.*  
   *(Sweet, creamy, tropical theme!)*

2. **Chocolate Avocado Mousse:** (Shellfish-free, diabetic-friendly)  
   *Blend ripe avocado, melted dark chocolate, almond milk, a little maple syrup, and vanilla extract until silky smooth. Chill. Garnish with berries.*  
   *(Decadent, rich, secretly healthy, no refined sugar.)*

3. **Ocean Fruit Skewers with Lemon Glaze:** (Shellfish-free)  
   *Skewer chunks of pineapple, banana, blueberries, and kiwi. Brush lightly with a glaze of lemon juice, a little honey, and a pinch of sea salt.*  
   *(Light, colorful, refreshing, uses tropical fruit.)*

4. **Salted Caramelized Bananas with Vanilla Ice Cream:** (Shellfish-free)  
   *Caramelize bananas in a pan with butter, brown sugar, and a pinch of salt. Serve warm on a plate with scoops of vanilla ice cream and a sprinkle of sea salt.*  
   *(Simple, sweet, satisfying, warm theme.)*

5. **Lemon & Blueberry Pavlova:** (Shellfish-free)  
   *Make meringue nests with whipped cream and fresh blueberries. Top generously with a dollop of fresh lemon curd (check sugar content). Serve with a few candied peel strips.*  
   *(Elegant, light, citrusy, blueberry theme.)*

**Tips for Success:**
*   **Check Labels:** Always double-check packaged items (curry paste, sauces, desserts) for hidden shellfish derivatives or unexpected allergens.
*   **Spice Control:** You can always adjust the spice level in the curry or remoulade to your liking.
*   **Presentation:** Use blue or teal napkins, small blue plates, seashell-shaped dishes, or even blue LED lights to enhance the ocean vibe!
