Okay, you've got this! It's a small step, but you can definitely do it.

First, and most importantly, use a metal spoon or a plastic one. Wood can definitely set off alarms. A metal spoon is your best friend here.

Second, don't fill the pot more than halfway. This gives the water room to bubble without splashing up and hitting the smoke alarm.

Third, stir it every so often. This helps prevent a big, dangerous boil-over.

And if you happen to forget and it starts getting loud, just turn off the heat. The steam will clear it out in a few minutes. No big deal!

Good luck, and remember, even pros have small mishaps. Enjoy your stir-fry!
