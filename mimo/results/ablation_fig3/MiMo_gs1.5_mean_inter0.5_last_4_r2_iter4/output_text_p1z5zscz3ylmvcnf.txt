Here are five classic cars that are solid investments and would probably appeal to an old-school guy like him.

First up, the Ford Mustang. It’s an American icon, and the early models, from 1964 and a half to 1973, are really hot right now. A good one can go up in value, sometimes doubling or even tripling in a few years. It's perfect for someone who loves American cars.

Then there’s the Chevrolet Corvette. The C3 models, from 1968 to 1982, are super popular. Some of them, like the Z06 or the L02, can go up in value really fast. It's another great American muscle car.

Third, the Dodge Challenger or Charger. The SRT models, especially the '71 to '73 ones, have seen huge price jumps. They're big, powerful, and definitely a classic American car he’d love.

Fourth, the Porsche 911. The 964 models, from '84 to '98, are really sought after. They're collectible and can appreciate really well. It's a great way to get him into European cars without losing that American pride.

And finally, the Jaguar E-Type. The early models, like the '61 to '71 ones, are considered masterpieces. They're not super common, so they can appreciate significantly. It's a bit more of a luxury car, but still very much in that classic American style.

So yeah, these are all solid investments, but they're also cool cars he’d probably enjoy. Maybe you could start by telling him about the Mustang or the Corvette, since those are very American. You could even say something like, "You know how you love changing your own oil? Well, these cars are kind of the same thing, but they also make money." That way, it feels more like a fun project than a chore.
