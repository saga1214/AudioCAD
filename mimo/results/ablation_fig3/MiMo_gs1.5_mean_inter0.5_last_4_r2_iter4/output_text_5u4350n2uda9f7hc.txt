You're right to think about that. Succulents are very lightweight, so a few of those will be no problem at all.

The snake plant, though, can get heavier as it grows. A young one might weigh less than a kilogram, but a bigger one could be around five to ten kilograms. So, it might be a bit much for you to carry up five flights of stairs.

Since you plan to take the plants home, I'd suggest starting with a couple of small succulents for your table and maybe just one small snake plant for the floor. That way, you can see how you like them and it won't be too heavy to move around.
