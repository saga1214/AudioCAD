Here’s a step-by-step guide for installing your TV, planters, and shelves while respecting your apartment’s rules:

### 📺 1. TV Mount Installation
- **Tools Needed**: Stud finder, level, drill, screws (match mount weight), wall anchors (if no studs).
- **Steps**:
  1. **Locate Studs**: Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (wood framing) 16–24 inches apart.
  2. **Check Weight Limits**: Ensure the mount’s weight rating supports your TV.
  3. **Drill Pilot Holes**: Drill holes slightly smaller than the mount’s screws. If no stud, use heavy-duty anchors.
  4. **Secure Mount**: Attach the mount to the wall, then screw the TV onto it. Double-check alignment with a level.

### 🌿 2. Hanging Planters
- **Tools**: Drill, wall anchors, screws, measuring tape.
- **Steps**:
  1. **Choose Anchor Type**: Use masonry anchors for concrete walls or plastic anchors for drywall.
  2. **Mark Spacing**: Plan hanging points 12–18 inches apart to avoid overloading one spot.
  3. **Drill Holes**: Drill holes deep enough for anchors (typically ⅔ the anchor’s length).
  4. **Install Anchors**: Screw anchors into holes, then hang planters using S-hooks or chains.

### 🪢 3. Floating Shelves
- **Tools**: Laser level (highly recommended), level, drill, appropriate screws/anchors.
- **Steps**:
  1. **Plan Layout**: Arrange shelves top-to-bottom. Use a laser level for perfectly even spacing.
  2. **Mark Shelf Positions**: Measure from the floor, then mark shelf edges and shelf-to-shelf gaps.
  3. **Drill Pilot Holes**: Drill into studs (ideal) or use heavy-duty anchors for drywall.
  4. **Attach Shelves**: Screw shelves horizontally into studs or anchors. Test weight capacity before placing art/furniture.

### ⚠️ Critical Tips:
- **Landlord Rules**: Always confirm drilling permission first. Use low-impact anchors if noise is a concern.
- **Safety**: Never overload shelves—calculate weight limits (e.g., heavy art needs thicker shelves).
- **Efficiency**: Dedicate one Sunday to installations. Use a checklist to avoid forgetting steps.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: For floating shelves, add a small LED strip under each shelf for ambiance and to hide imperfections!

By following these steps, you’ll create a functional, stylish space without compromising your new home’s rules. Happy decorating! 🎨
