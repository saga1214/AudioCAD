Here’s a **nautical-themed, sand-friendly shopping list** designed for easy handling, allergy safety, and pirate immersion—complete with portion control to avoid messes:

---

### **Main Course: "Pirate's Plunder Plate"**  
*(Pre-portioned in sealed containers)*  
1. **Mini "Submarine" Sliders** (6 per child):  
   - 2 mini whole-wheat rolls  
   - Ham or turkey slices (allergy-safe)  
   - Cheese cubes or cream cheese  
   - *Theme:* Cut rolls to resemble ship hulls; label as "Buried Treasure Sandwiches."  
2. **"Cannonball" Cheese Cubes**:  
   - Block cheddar or mozzarella, cut into 2-inch cubes.  
3. **"Mermaid's Treasure" Fruit Skewers**:  
   - Orange wedges (round, easy to hold)  
   - Apple slices (soaked in lemon water to prevent browning)  
   - *Avoid:* Watermelon, grapes, berries.  

---

### **Side Dishes: "Pirate Ship Sides"**  
*(Individual portions in small containers)*  
1. **"Gold Coins" Cheese & Crackers**:  
   - Gouda or cheddar cheese sticks + Goldfish crackers.  
2. **"Cannonball" Veggie Cups**:  
   - Baby carrots, cucumber sticks, snap peas.  
3. **"Sea Salt" Potato Chips**:  
   - Mini bags of plain or lightly salted chips.  

---

### **Drinks: "Pirate Punch"**  
*(No spills, easy to open)*  
1. **Juice Boxes**:  
   - 2 per child (e.g., apple, orange, or berry—avoid watermelon blends).  
   - *Theme:* Label as "Pirate Punch" or "Mermaid's Tears."  
2. **Small Water Bottles**:  
   - Reusable bottles with fun pirate stickers.  

---

### **Themed Snacks & Extras**  
*(Fun, low-mess, allergy-safe)*  
1. **"Davy Jones' Locker" Trail Mix**:  
   - Pretzel rods, sunflower seeds (peanuts optional), chocolate chips.  
2. **"Treasure Chest" Cookies**:  
   - Store-bought pirate ship-shaped cookies.  
3. **Gummy Sharks/Ships**:  
   - Gummy fish or ship-shaped candies.  
4. **"Pirate's Gold" Pretzel Rods**:  
   - Mini pretzel rods in pirate-themed bags.  

---

### **Allergy-Safe Notes**  
- **Avoid**: Watermelon, grapes, berries, peanuts, and shellfish.  
- **Label Everything**: Clearly mark containers with "NO WATERMELON."  
- **Communicate**: Send a note to other parents about the allergy.  

---

### **Pirate Immersion Extras**  
- **Packaging**: Use cardboard "treasure chests" or bento boxes.  
- **Utensils**: Wooden cutlery shaped like swords or hooks.  
- **Activity Tie-In**: Serve with a "pirate map" menu or a treasure-hunt snack scavenger.  

---

### **Total Cost Estimate**  
- **Sandwiches/Sides**: $15–$20  
- **Fruit/Drinks**: $10–$15  
- **Themed Snacks**: $10–$15  
- **Containers/Labels**: $5–$10  

This setup keeps everything contained, themed, and allergy-safe—so your son stays in pirate mode while the kids dig for treasure (and snacks) without sand! 🏴‍☠️🌊
