Dallas has a great Asian food scene! For Asian noodle spots, you've got some solid options. There's H Mart, which is a huge Asian grocery store with food courts, so you can grab noodles there. You could also check out Yum Yum Express for fast Asian noodles, or try Saizerio for Italian-Japanese fusion, which is also great. For protein bars, I’d recommend checking out Quest, Optimum Nutrition, or Any4Performance. They’re all tasty and high in protein. Let me know if you want more details on any of those!
