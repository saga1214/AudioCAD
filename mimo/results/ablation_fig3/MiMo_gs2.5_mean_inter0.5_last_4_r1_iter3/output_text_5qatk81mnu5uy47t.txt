That's a fantastic goal! Planning a Costa Rica scuba diving trip around your dream trip and scuba passion is perfect. Here’s a framework to make it unforgettable, incorporating your preferences and experiences:

**1. Gear is Set (Short Fins First!):**  
*   **Recommendation Confirmed:** Short fins are ideal for beginners, ease of movement/calm waters, and compactness for travel. You'll find excellent rental shops in popular dive sites offering well-maintained short fins.
*   **Action:** Research rental shops in your planned dive areas (e.g., Tenerife Bank, Caño Island, Cenote Azul) to book in advance if needed. You can always upgrade later!

**2. Choosing the Perfect Dive Region (Mix of Tropical Wonders):**  
Costa Rica offers incredible diversity. Aim for at least 2-3 different regions for varied experiences:

*   **Guanacaste (North Coast - Tropical & Powerful):**
    *   **Tenerife Bank:** World-famous for **Great Turtles** (seasonal), **Whale Sharks** (seasonal - Jan-Mar), **Blue Sharks**, **Hammerheads**, and incredible **coral walls**. Tropical waters, strong currents possible. *Your past Honduran experience here will resonate.*
    *   **Playa Conchal / Santa Teresa:** Closer to San José, good for beginners, coral reefs, snorkeling, and relaxed vibe. Great for warm-up dives.
    *   **Puerto Viejo / Cahuita:** Offers reefs, wrecks, and sometimes manta rays. Good mix.

*   **Osa Peninsula (Southwest - Tropical & Remote):**
    *   **Caño Island (Sarapiquí):** An **unforgettable** experience. A protected marine park just offshore. Famous for **Sea Lions**, **Sea Turtles**, **Marlins**, **Tuna**, **Swordfish**, and incredible visibility. Requires a boat trip. *Pure tropical paradise.*
    *   **Drake Bay / Puerto Jiménez:** Accessible only by boat or plane. Dives here are often calmer, focusing on reefs and abundant marine life. Great for a secluded feel.

*   **Cenote Azul (Southwest - Unique Freshwater Twist):**
    *   **Cenote Azul:** While not ocean diving, this is a *must-see* for a different kind of wonder. A stunning, deep freshwater cenote with incredible stalactites, stalagmites, and crystal-clear water. Requires a guided descent. *Adds a unique, magical element.*

*   **Arenal Volcano Area (Central - Thermal Pools & Mountain Dives):**
    *   **Arenal Volcano Thermal Pools:** While not scuba, relaxing in natural hot springs *near* a volcano is a unique Costa Rican experience. *Great for unwinding after diving.*
    *   **Nearby Mountain Dives:** Some operators offer dives near Arenal, often focusing on reefs and potentially unique visibility. Less common than ocean, but adds variety.

**3. Crafting the Unforgettable Experience:**

*   **Mix It Up:** Combine easy reef dives (like Caño Island's outer reef) with more challenging wall dives (Tenerife Bank) and unique experiences (Cenote Azul).
*   **Focus on Wildlife:** Costa Rica's biodiversity shines. Target sites known for specific animals you love (turtles, sharks, rays, sea lions, iguanas). Research seasonal sightings.
*   **Prioritize Caño Island:** If possible, make this a highlight. The boat trip, the snorkeling/diving, and the sheer abundance of marine life is truly special.
*   **Consider a Live-Aboard:** For 3-5 days, a live-aboard is fantastic for dedicated diving, smaller groups, and accessing more remote sites like Tenerife Bank efficiently. Book with a reputable operator specializing in marine parks.
*   **Book Reputable Operators:** Research dive shops/liveaboards with good reviews, certified dive masters, recent equipment maintenance, and safety records. Look for those specializing in the sites you want.
*   **Plan for Relaxation:** Balance intense diving days with time to relax on the beach, enjoy local cuisine, or visit nearby rainforests. Costa Rica is about the *whole* experience.

**4. Logistics & Timeline:**

*   **When to Go:** The "dry season" (Dec-Apr) is peak for diving (warm water, good visibility, fewer crowds). Wet season (May-Nov) offers lush landscapes but can have rain and less visibility. Tenerife Bank has its best shark/turtle season (Dec-Mar).
*   **Getting There:** Fly into San José (SJO). From there, ground transport (rental car, bus, private transfer) gets you to Guanacaste ( Liberia - LIR), Osa (requires boat/plane), or Arenal.
*   **Sample Itinerary (Flexible):**
    *   **Days 1-3:** Arrive SJO, transfer to Guanacaste (e.g., Playa Conchal/Santa Teresa). Dive local reefs. Explore town.
    *   **Days 4-6:** Fly/boat to Osa Peninsula. Dive Caño Island (2-3 days). Explore Drake Bay/Rainforest.
    *   **Days 7-9:** Fly back to Guanacaste. Dive Tenerife Bank (2-3 days). Explore Liberia area.
    *   **Days 10-12:** Fly back to SJO. Depart.

**Key Advantages Short Fins Over Long Fins (For You Right Now):**

1.  **Easier Travel & Storage:** Lighter, foldable, less space-consuming.
2.  **Less Leg Strength Required:** Crucial for beginners building endurance.
3.  **Improved Maneuverability:** Easier to pivot, turn, and navigate tight spaces or calm reefs.
4.  **Less Fatigue on Long Swims:** Can be more comfortable for moderate distances in calm water.
5.  **Lower Profile:** Easier to travel through airports/cabins.
