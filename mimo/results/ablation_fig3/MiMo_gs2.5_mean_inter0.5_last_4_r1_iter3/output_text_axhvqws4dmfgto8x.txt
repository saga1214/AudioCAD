Stir-fry actually works great with noodles. The big secret is to cook them separately in a pot, then just toss them with your sauce at the end. That way, there's no risk of forgetting wood spoons in boiling pots!

Also, a pro tip: make sure to stir your noodles frequently and rinse them with cold water right after you drain them. That helps keep them from sticking together. You've got this!
