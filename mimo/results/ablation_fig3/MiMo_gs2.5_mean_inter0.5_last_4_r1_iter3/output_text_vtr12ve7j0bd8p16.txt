You two sound like a perfect pair. For a weekend adventure, Calgary is surrounded by some fantastic options.

If you're looking for something easy but beautiful, you could go to Canmore. It's about an hour and a half north, and you can take the scenic Bow Valley Parkway. It has amazing mountain views and a charming little downtown with lots of good restaurants and shops. Or, if you want something closer, you could drive about an hour south to Okotoks, which has a great downtown with a cool train museum and lots of restaurants.

If you're looking for more of a rugged, outdoor adventure, you could head to the Kananaskis country, about an hour and a half away. It's a world-class area for hiking and biking. A bit further, about two and a half hours, is Banff National Park. You could hike the famous Johnston Canyon trail, which is beautiful and relatively easy, or just soak in the stunning Lake Louise.

If you want something more relaxed, you could try the Strathmore Fruit Market, which is about an hour away, or head to the South Okanagan in BC for wine tasting.
