Okay, you’ve got a great retro tech theme planned for Dress-Up Day, and now you’re thinking about meals that match the vibe. Love that. Here are two lunch ideas that tie into the theme while being easy to eat and inclusive. First, for the main dish, you could do a “Moderna Taco Salad.” That’s a play on the word “modem.” For it, you'd serve a classic taco salad, but with a tech twist. For example, you could use black beans instead of ground beef for a more modern, sustainable feel, and maybe add some avocado slices for color, like data packets. On the side, you could have some tortilla chips and salsa, which look like little circuit boards. For snacks, a simple “Keyboard Snack Platter” would work well. Just arrange some pretzels, nuts, and maybe some fruit slices on a tray to look like a keyboard. You could even label the pretzels as “Enter Keys” or the nuts as “Shift Keys” for fun. A second idea is a “Vintage Pizza Party.” For this, you could make or order a retro-style pizza, maybe with neon-colored toppings like green olives and red pepper flakes to look like old-school RGB. For sides, you could have“Dial-Up Popcorn” – just regular popcorn, but serve it in a bowl that looks like a small speaker or a mini speakers to fit the tech theme. You could also have some“Floppy Disk” cookies, which are just round cookies with a little flag on top to look like a floppy disk. These ideas are casual, fun, and easy to eat while keeping the retro tech vibe going strong. Hope this helps you make the day a success!
