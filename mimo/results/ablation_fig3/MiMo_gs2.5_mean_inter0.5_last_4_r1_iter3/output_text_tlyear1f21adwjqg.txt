Okay, efficiency can mean different things here. If we're talking about cost efficiency over time, solar energy really comes out on top because its prices keep dropping and storage is getting better. For places with lots of rain or snow, hydro is fantastic because it's so reliable and efficient. But if we have to pick just one that works well almost everywhere, solar energy is the winner. It's scalable, getting cheaper, and you can put it on almost any surface, from rooftops to huge solar farms.
