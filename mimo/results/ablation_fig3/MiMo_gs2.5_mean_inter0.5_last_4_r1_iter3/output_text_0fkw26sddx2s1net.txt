Of course, here’s a detailed shopping list for your husband's allergy-friendly Asian-inspired meal. I'll break it down by course.

First, for the starter options. If you choose Miso Soup, you'll need miso paste, either white or red, and some soft silken tofu. For Cucumber Salad with Sesame Dressing, you'll need cucumbers, rice vinegar, sugar, salt, toasted sesame oil, and toasted sesame seeds.

Next, for the main course, Chicken Teriyaki with rice and veggies. You'll need boneless, skinless chicken breasts. For the teriyaki glaze, you'll need soy sauce, mirin, sake, and brown sugar, plus some grated ginger and minced garlic. For the jasmine rice, just jasmine rice and water. And for stir-fried veggies, pick some you both like, such as bok choy, bell peppers, carrots, or snap peas. You'll also need high-smoke cooking oil, and seasonings like soy sauce and a bit of sesame oil.

Finally, for the desserts. If you make Mango Sticky Rice, you'll need sticky jasmine rice, fresh ripe mangoes, full-fat coconut milk, and brown sugar. If you make Matcha Ice Cream, you'll need matcha powder, full-fat coconut cream, full-fat coconut milk, and brown sugar.

Just a couple of extra tips: make sure to check the labels on all your packaged goods to ensure they're legume-free. And for the matcha ice cream, stirring it every hour while it chills will give it a really smooth texture. Enjoy your date night!
