Great questions! Here's a breakdown:

### Treat Recommendations:
* **Best flavors:** Small pieces of **raw chicken**, **beef**, **cheese (mild like cheddar)**, **hot dog bits**, or **freeze-dried liver** are highly appealing. For non-food rewards, use **tug toys**, **chew toys**, or **high-value treats** (like chicken wings).
* **Tips:** Use tiny pieces so he can eat while training. Keep treats **small** and **high-value** (he can't ignore them). Consistency is key—always use the same treat for the same reward level.

### Clicker Training Explained:
Clicker training uses a **three-step process**:
1. **Clicker as a Marker:** When he does the right behavior (e.g., sits), click the clicker **instantly**.
2. **Immediate Reward:** Give a treat immediately after the click. The click = "You did it right!"
3. **Build Association:** Repeat this. Eventually, he’ll learn that **"click + treat = correct action."**  
   - Example: Hold treat near his nose, raise it over his head. When he sits, **click** → give treat. Repeat 10–20 times.  
   - After success, gradually add a verbal cue ("sit") before the click/treat.

**Why it works:** The click is objective—it marks the exact moment he does it right. This makes training faster and clearer than just using praise ("good boy!").

### Crate Training Tips (To Make It Cozy):
1. **Make it a positive space:** Feed meals in the crate, hide treats inside, and invite him in with toys/blankets.  
2. **Size:** It should be large enough for him to stand, turn, and lie down but not too big (so he doesn’t hide).  
3. **Comfort:** Add a soft bed, blanket, and maybe a chew toy.  
4. **Short sessions:** Start with 5–10 minutes, gradually increasing time.  
5. **Never use it for punishment:** It should feel like his safe den. If he panics, close the door but leave a treat inside to calm him.

### Recall Training (If He Escapes):
- **Always use a leash** in unsecured areas.  
- For recall: Call his name + "come!" with an excited tone. Reward heavily (treats/toys) when he comes. Practice in low-distraction areas first.  
- **Never chase:** This teaches him to run. Stand still, call calmly, and reward when he returns.

### Final Tip:
**Patience is key!** Puppies learn in stages. Celebrate small wins, and remember—consistent, positive training builds trust. Your new buddy Galileo will thrive with your care! 🐾
