That is a fantastic advantage to have. If you can both write and illustrate, you have a very valuable skill set. You can absolutely submit a fully illustrated manuscript to a publisher. However, the approach is a bit different. You need to understand that publishers will still hire their *own* illustrator. Your illustrations are more like a proof of concept to show them that you have a strong visual sense for the book.

So, you would submit your manuscript and your illustrations, but you would also include a note explaining that you are open to letting the publisher find a different illustrator if they prefer. This shows you're flexible while still proving your own artistic vision. They will often ask you to provide more sample art to review, so be prepared for that.
