### 🍽️ **Simpler & Crowd-Pleasing Options**
1. **Gourmet Flatbread Pizzas**  
   - Offer 3-4 toppings: Classic (mozzarella/basil), spicy (gochujang queso), umami (caramelized onion/fig jam), and a *unique* option (miso-ginger tofu or smoked salmon).  
   - *Why it works:* Build-your-own fun, minimal cleanup.

2. **Matcha Popcorn**  
   - Lightly dust with matcha powder + a pinch of sea salt.  
   - *Simple but unique:* Earthy green tea flavor that’s addictive.

3. **Mini Sliders with Kimchi Aioli**  
   - Use Hawaiian rolls for sweetness + spicy-sour kimchi mayo.  
   - *Foodie twist:* Unusual but approachable.

### 🌶️🔥 **Unique & Adventurous Choices**  
1. **Umami Explosion Platter**  
   - **Kimchi seaweed snacks** + **dried squid jerky** (soak in tamarind glaze).  
   - **Fish roe crackers** (takoyaki-style) + **yuzu-marinated olives**.  
   - *Label creatively:* "Kyoto Street Snacks" or "Tsukiji Market Bites."

2. **DIY Sweet & Sour Taco Station**  
   - Fillings: Grilled shrimp, pulled ramen noodles, or braised eggplant.  
   - Toppings: Pickled ginger, avocado crema, *sweet* tamarind glaze.  
   - *Unique twist:* Asian flavors in a taco format.

3. **Yuzu & Miso Glazed Eggplant**  
   - Lightly grilled with citrusy yuzu and salty-sweet miso.  
   - *Foodie frame hit:* Savory, tender, and globally inspired.

4. **Sweet & Savory Rice Cakes** (Gomae)  
   - Sweet red bean paste + savory crispy bacon bits.  
   - *Unique contrast:* Sweet/salty combo that’s surprisingly addictive.

### 🍬 **Sweet Finishers**  
- **Matcha Mochi** (soft, chewy, with red bean filling).  
- **Tiramisu-Style Bento** (mini desserts with coffee-soaked mochi).  
- **Tamarind Candy** (their friend’s import—serve alongside warm mochi for contrast).

### 💡 **Pro Tips for You (Tired Host!)**  
- **Prep ahead:** Make flatbread pizzas, yuzu glaze, and mochi ahead.  
- **Label everything:** "Spicy," "Umami," "Sweet" so guests can explore.  
- **Drinks:** Offer sparkling yuzu lemonade, matcha latte, and soju (optional).  
- **Movie pairing:** Choose a film with a Japanese setting (e.g., *Spirited Away*, *The Tale of the Princess Kaguya*) or a quirky indie like *Swiss Army Man* for fun vibes!
