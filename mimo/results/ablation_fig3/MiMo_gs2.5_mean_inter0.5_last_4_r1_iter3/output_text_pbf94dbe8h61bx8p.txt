Okay, you're looking for simple, meaningful Christmas gifts that your macramé-loving craft enthusiast and cookie-baking parent can get your family involved in, while accommodating different ages and avoiding potentially messy or controversial projects. Let's break down the options based on what you've shared:

**Crafts Building on Your Passion:**
1.  **Mini Plant Hanger Stations:** Since macramé plant hangers are your absolute favorite, make these the centerpiece! Set up stations:
    *   **Station 1 (2-5 yrs):** Pre-make small, simple plant hanger "frames" (e.g., basic knots). Kids decorate with colorful yarn, pom-poms, or beads.
    *   **Station 2 (6-10 yrs):** Assemble the hangers with you or older siblings, then personalize with paint or stickers.
    *   **Station 3 (10-15 yrs):** Learn basic knots with you to make small, simple hangers, maybe for tiny succulents or air plants.
    *   *Why it works:* Meaningful, uses your skill, scalable by age.

**Simplified Cookies (Minimal Rolling/Mess):**
1.  **No-Roll Sugar Cookies:** Bake a batch of simple sugar dough. Divide into balls. Kids:
    *   **2-5 yrs:** Decorate with sprinkles, sanding sugar, or pre-made cookie stencils.
    *   **6-10 yrs:** Pipe simple designs (stars, trees) with royal icing *after* baking.
    *   **10-15 yrs:** Handle icing details, stencils, or assembly (e.g., cookie house kits).
    *   *Why it works:* Festive, customizable stations, less rolling required.
2.  Meaningful Cookie Stations: Instead of baking, pre-make plain cookies. Set up stations for:
    *   Decorating with festive icing colors and sprinkles.
    *   Using cookie cutters to cut shapes from the plain dough.
    *   Creating themed cookie garlands or trees with the cut shapes.
    *   *Why it works:* Less kitchen chaos, still very creative.

**Other Meaningful & Simple Craft Ideas (Kid-Friendly):**
1.  **Fingerprint Art:** As mentioned, but expand:
    *   Create festive fingerprint trees, snowflakes, or reindeer.
    *   Use washable paint, cardstock, and markers.
    *   Kids can write a short holiday message on the back.
    *   Frame them or give them as individual cards.
    *   *Why it works:* Instantly personal, requires zero tools, minimal mess.
2.  Salt Dough Ornaments:
    *   Kids shape ornaments (snowmen, stars, trees) with cookie cutters.
    *   You bake them.
    *   Kids paint and decorate.
    *   *Why it works:* Durable, customizable, therapeutic feel.
3.  **Festive Felt Ornaments:**
    *   Pre-cut simple felt shapes (Santa, snowflake, tree).
    *   Kids add details with fabric markers, sequins, or embroidery floss.
    *   *Why it works:* Very tactile, easy to manage, no glue gun needed.

**For the Candy Globes & Pet Food:**
*   **Globe Alternative:** If the glue gun is the main pain point, try pre-assembled globes. Buy the plain plastic ones and just have kids add the candy and maybe glue on *one* element (like a hat) under your close supervision. Focus on the candy filling.
*   **Pet Food:** Stick with Walmart or compare prices on Chewy.com online. Check local discount grocery stores (like Aldi or Lidl) or warehouse clubs (Costco/Sam's Club) for value packs. Compare unit prices carefully online before buying.

**Putting it Together for Christmas:**

*   **Theme it:** Pick 1-2 main activities (e.g., Macramé Plant Hangers *and* No-Roll Sugar Cookies).
*   **Assign Roles:** Based on age/interest, assign specific tasks at each station (e.g., "You're the yarn decorator!", "You're the sprinkle master!").
*   **Prep Ahead:** You do the heavy lifting: pre-cut yarn for hangers, bake cookies, cut salt dough shapes, buy pet food in bulk, etc.
*   **Embrace the Chaos:** Focus on the joy of creating together. It's about the experience and the gift, not perfection.

**Recommendation:** Start with the **Sugar Cookie Decorating Station** (minimal prep, high fun, meaningful) combined with a simple **Fingerprint Art** activity for cards or ornaments. If you really want to lean into your macramé passion, the **Mini Plant Hanger Station** is fantastic, but requires more of your prep time. Choose based on your energy and what feels most doable!
