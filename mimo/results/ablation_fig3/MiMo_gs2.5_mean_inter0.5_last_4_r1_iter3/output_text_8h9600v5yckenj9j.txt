For candy that'll disappear fast at your Halloween party, focus on **Halloween classics and crowd-pleasers**—avoid niche flavors or bulk packs of unpopular items. Here’s a quick guide:

### Top-Selling Halloween Candy (Grab These):
1. **M&M's** (especially the Halloween-themed colors)  
2. **Reese's** (mini cups or cups)  
3. **Skittles** (or any bright, chewy candies)  
4. **Snickers** (full-size bars)  
5. **Gummies** (Starbursts, Haribo gummies)  
6. **Mini Chocolate Bars** (Milky Way, 3 Musketeers)  
7. **Candy Corn** (for fall vibes)  

### What to Avoid:
- **Artificial dye allergies already concern you—double-check labels!**  
- **Older candy** (like generic "Halloween candy bags"—always inspect for freshness).  
- **Overly sweet or niche flavors** (e.g., sour gummies, exotic chocolates).  

### Pro Tips:
- **Buy pre-bagged mini sizes**—easier to grab and less wasteful.  
- **Mix brands** (e.g., Mars, Hershey’s, Nestlé) to cover preferences.  
- **Add a fun twist**: Include a few novelty candies (e.g., sour strips or themed lollipops) for Instagrammable moments.  

### Food Recap:
- **Beyond pizza**: Mini sandwiches, veggie platters (dipped in ranch/hummus), fruit skewers, and popcorn are safe and crowd-friendly.  
- **Allergy-friendly**: Use paper/canopy napkins, plastic cutlery, and label drinks clearly (e.g., "orange juice—no dye").  

You’ve got this! Last-minute ≠ last-quality—Dollar Tree and grocery stores often have great Halloween candy deals today. Focus on ambiance (fog + lights!), allergy-safe snacks, and classic candy, and your party will be a hit. 🎃👻
