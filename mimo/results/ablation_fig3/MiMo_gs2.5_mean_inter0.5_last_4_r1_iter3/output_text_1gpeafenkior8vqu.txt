Here’s a **balanced 7-day Japan itinerary** covering Tokyo, Osaka, Nara, and Kyoto, optimized for sightseeing while leaving room for your coursework. I’ve included logistics, key attractions, and design-friendly ideas for your presentation:

### **Itinerary Overview**
- **Tokyo (4 days)**  
- **Osaka (2 days)**  
- **Nara (1 day)**  
- **Kyoto (1 day)**  
*(Travel via Shinkansen bullet train: Tokyo → Osaka ~2.5 hrs; Osaka → Kyoto ~30 mins)*

---

### **Detailed Schedule**
#### **Day 1: Tokyo Arrival**  
- **Morning:** Arrive in Tokyo, check into hotel (e.g., near Shinjuku/Shibuya).  
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Harajuku (Takeshita Street)** for quirky fashion and street art.  
- **Evening:** Dinner in **Shibuya** (try ramen at Ichiran).  
- **Workout:** Morning or evening session (hotel gyms are common).

#### **Day 2: Tokyo Art History (Design-Focused)**  
- **Morning:** **Ueno Park** (museums: Tokyo National Museum, Art Museum).  
- **Afternoon:** **Asakusa** (Senso-ji Temple, traditional paper lanterns).  
- **Evening:** **Odaiba** (futuristic art, teamLab digital exhibit).  
- *Presentation Tip:* Use temple architecture layouts or digital art visuals for slide design.

#### **Day 3: Tokyo Deep Dive**  
- **Morning:** **Shinjuku Gyoen** (Japanese gardens) + **Ghibli Museum** (if open).  
- **Afternoon:** **Shibuya** (scramble crossing, Hachiko statue) + **Harajuku** (designer boutiques).  
- **Evening:** **Shinjuku Kabukicho** (nightlife).  

#### **Day 4: Tokyo → Osaka (Travel Day)**  
- **Morning:** **Tsukiji Outer Market** (food + crafts).  
- **Afternoon:** Shinkansen to **Osaka** (book tickets early via JR Pass if applicable).  
- **Evening:** **Dotonbori** (street food, neon signs).  

#### **Day 5: Osaka & Nara**  
- **Morning:** **Osaka Castle** (woodblock print inspiration).  
- **Afternoon:** Day trip to **Nara** (Todai-ji Temple, deer park).  
- *Presentation Tip:* Ukiyo-e woodblock prints use **vibrant color schemes** (red, indigo, gold) — ideal for slide fonts (e.g., *Kodachi Gothic*).

#### **Day 6: Osaka → Kyoto**  
- **Morning:** Shinkansen to **Kyoto**.  
- **Afternoon:** **Kiyomizu-dera** (wooden architecture, nature balance).  
- **Evening:** **Gion District** (geisha spotting).  

#### **Day 7: Kyoto**  
- **Morning:** **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (torii gates, nature layout).  
- **Afternoon:** **Arashiyama Bamboo Forest** (minimalist balance).  
- **Evening:** Return to Tokyo/Osaka for flight home.

### **Key Visual Elements for Your Presentation Slides**

- **Layout:** Vertical compositions (like Japanese scrolls).  
- **Color Scheme:** Earthy tones (indigo, ochre) or seasonal pastels.  
- **Font:** Traditional scripts (e.g., *MingLiU*) or minimalist sans-serifs.
