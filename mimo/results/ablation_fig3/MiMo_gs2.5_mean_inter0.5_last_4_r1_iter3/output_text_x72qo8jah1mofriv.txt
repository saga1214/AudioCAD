Oh, that's a good point. For kosher dietary laws, the main thing to watch out for is cross-contamination. That means you need to be careful about using separate utensils or maybe even separate serving dishes if you can. The hummus and weenies in bacon you already mentioned are probably fine, as long as they're prepared without any non-kosher ingredients like pork fat or dairy. For the fruit platter, that's definitely safe. Theme sweets like dark chocolate are usually okay too, but you'd have to make sure the chocolate itself doesn't contain any dairy or non-kosher ingredients. So, really, it's just about keeping everything separate and using clean utensils to serve. That should cover the essentials.
