Here's a step-by-step guide for each installation, prioritizing safety, efficiency, and compliance with your landlord's rules (no late parties/loud drilling):

### 📺 1. TV Mount Installation
- **Prep Work**:  
  - Use a **stud finder** to locate wall studs (critical for heavy mounts).  
  - Measure the distance between studs for the mount’s required spacing.  
  - Mark wall positions with a level and pencil.  
- **Tools Needed**:  
  - Drill, wall anchors (if no studs), level, tape measure, screwdriver.  
- **Steps**:  
  1. **Secure Mount**: Attach the mount to the wall using screws into studs. If no studs, use **heavy-duty drywall anchors** (e.g., toggle bolts) rated for your TV’s weight.  
  2. **Mount TV**: Align the TV with the mount’s holes. Use a level to ensure it’s straight.  
  3. **Secure TV**: Attach the TV to the mount with screws (don’t overtighten).  
  4. **Cable Management**: Use cable clips to hide wires behind the TV.  

> ⚠️ **Safety Tip**: Wear safety glasses! Double-check weight limits for anchors.

### 🌿 2. Hanging Planters
- **Prep Work**:  
  - Check local codes for hanging weight limits (e.g., drywall can’t support >50 lbs without reinforcement).  
  - Measure planter weight + soil + plant (e.g., a 1-gallon pot + soil = ~15 lbs).  
- **Tools Needed**:  
  - Drill, small anchors (if needed), measuring tape, level.  
- **Steps**:  
  1. **Choose Location**: Hang near a stud or use **strong adhesive hooks** (e.g., 3M Command Strips rated for 20+ lbs).  
  2. **Install Hooks**:  
     - For drywall: Drill a small pilot hole, insert a small anchor, then screw in the hook.  
     - For studs: Drill a larger hole, insert a toggle bolt, then screw in the hook.  
  3. **Hang Planter**: Secure the planter to the hook. Ensure it’s level.  
  4. **Reinforce**: Use a secondary hook or L-bracket if weight exceeds capacity.  

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Use lightweight planters (ceramic > concrete) to reduce strain.

### 📚 3. Floating Shelves
- **Prep Work**:  
  - Measure shelf length and weight distribution.  
  - Use a level and pencil to mark shelf positions.  
- **Tools Needed**:  
  - Drill, wall anchors (if no studs), level, measuring tape.  
- **Steps**:  
  1. **Attach Shelf Backing**:  
     - If using a backing board, secure it to the wall first with anchors.  
  2. **Mount Shelf**:  
     - For studs: Drill holes, insert long screws into studs.  
     - For drywall: Use **heavy-duty anchors** (e.g., toggle bolts) and screws.  
  3. **Check Level**: Use a level app or bubble level. Adjust if needed.  
  4. **Add Decor**: Place books/art on the shelf (ensure total weight < shelf capacity).  

> ⚠️ **Critical**: Always test weight limits. For shelves >24", consult an engineer.

### 🛠️ **General Tips for Quiet, Efficient Installation**
1. **Noise Control**:  
   - Do installations on **weekends** (10 AM–4 PM) to avoid disturbing neighbors.  
   - Use **impact-resistant drill bits** to minimize noise.  
2. **Tool Prep**:  
   - Keep all tools (drill, anchors, screws) organized in a tray to avoid dropping noise.  
3. **Safety First**:  
   - Wear ear protection if drilling for long periods.  
   - Use a **laser level** for perfect alignment.  
4. **Landlord Compliance**:  
   - Take photos of installed items before moving in (to avoid "damage" claims).  
   - Use removable adhesives (e.g., Command Strips) for easy removal.  

> 💡 **Time-Saver**: Buy pre-drilled TV mounts or shelves to skip measuring.
