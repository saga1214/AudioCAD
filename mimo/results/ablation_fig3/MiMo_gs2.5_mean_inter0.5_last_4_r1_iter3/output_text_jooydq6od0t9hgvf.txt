Here are quick, elegant recipes for your vodka mix and Argentine-style Fernet-Branca:

### 🍸 1. Refreshing Vodka Soda with Mint (Vegetarian-Friendly)
* **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz vodka  
  - 4 oz sparkling water (non-soda!)  
  - 5 fresh mint leaves  
  - Lemon twist or wedge  
* **Steps:**  
  1. Muddle mint leaves gently in a glass.  
  2. Add vodka and top with sparkling water.  
  3. Stir lightly.  
  4. Garnish with mint and lemon twist.  
* *Why it works:* Light, crisp, and visually appealing—perfect for mixed age groups.

### 🇦🇷 2. Argentine-Style Fernet-Branca (Omnivore Classic)
* **Ingredients:**  
  - 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
  - 4 oz cola (or beer for a hoppy twist)  
  - Ice  
* **Steps:**  
  1. Fill a glass with ice.  
  2. Pour Fernet and cola (or beer) over ice.  
  3. Stir gently.  
* *Why it works:* Bold, bitter-sweet, and iconic in Argentina. *Note:* Fernet-Branca is vegetarian (no meat-derived ingredients), but beer contains hops—omit if strict vegetarian needed.

- **Non-alcoholic option:** Offer sparkling water with lime for the soda-allergic guest.
