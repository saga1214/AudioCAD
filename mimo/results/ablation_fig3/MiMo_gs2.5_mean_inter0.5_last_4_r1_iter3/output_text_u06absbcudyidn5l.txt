Okay, let's break this down. Chronic redness has many potential causes. It could be rosacea, sun damage over many years, genetics, contact dermatitis, or other conditions. Vasculitis is another possibility, where blood vessels are inflamed. This inflammation can be triggered by many things, including infections, autoimmune issues, or even certain medications. The symptoms of vasculitis can be very general, like fever, fatigue, and aches, or more specific, like skin rashes or sores. It happens when the immune system mistakenly attacks the blood vessels. Because these vessels are all over the body, the symptoms can vary a lot. The best way to diagnose it is usually a biopsy. Treatment often involves immunosuppressants, but a specialist, like a rheumatologist, can give you the most accurate information.
