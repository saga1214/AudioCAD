Of course, I can summarize all the prices for you. Chicken cutlates will run eight to fifteen dollars. All-purpose flour is three to five dollars. Twelve eggs cost three to seven dollars. Breadcrumbs are two to four dollars, and vegetable oil is four to eight dollars. Salt and pepper is three to six dollars. Moving on to sides, Garlic Mashed Potatoes will need three to six dollars for a five-pound bag of potatoes and one dollar for a head of garlic. For Creamy Garlic Parmesan Sauce, half a pound of butter is four to seven dollars, milk is three to five dollars, a container of sour cream is about two to four dollars, a quarter-pound of Parmesan cheese is one to three dollars, a small bunch of fresh parsley is about a dollar, a small onion is fifty cents, and a tablespoon of olive oil is around two dollars. Your dessert, Lemon Tart, will need two dollars for a can of lemon juice, four dollars for a box of tart shells, six dollars for cream cheese, three dollars for powdered sugar, five dollars for lemons, and two dollars for a can of whipped cream. So, the estimated total cost for four family members is between sixty and one hundred dollars.
