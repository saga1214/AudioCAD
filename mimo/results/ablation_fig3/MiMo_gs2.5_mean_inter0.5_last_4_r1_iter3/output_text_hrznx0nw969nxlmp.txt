If you're staying near Secret Harbour or boating freely, here are fantastic **pressure-free daytime activities** perfect for your week in the Virgin Islands:

### 🌊 **Water Adventures**
1. **Snorkeling at Coki Beach (St. John)**  
   - Right near Gallows Point Resort, this calm, shallow bay has vibrant coral gardens, parrotfish, and occasional rays. Secret Harbour’s bay is equally protected and ideal for beginners.
2. **Boat Tours to Reef Bay Caves (St. John)**  
   - A short boat ride to this secluded cove with ancient Arawak petroglyphs. Calm waters make it accessible for your brother.
3. **Magen's Bay Kayaking (St. Thomas)**  
   - Rent kayaks from Frenchtown docks and paddle to Frenchtown's waterfront bars (like The Longboard) for sunset drinks.

### 🏝️ **Land & Nature**
4. **Reef Bay Hike (St. John)**  
   - A gentle 1-mile trail through mangroves to a secluded beach with tide pools and nesting sea turtles (seasonal). End with a swim at Secret Harbour.
5. **Soggy Dollar Bar & Cane Field Crawl (St. Thomas)**  
   - Take a boat to Cruz Bay, then hike to Soggy Dollar Bar for their famous Painkiller. Walk to nearby Cane Field Crawl for rum distillery tours and orchards.

### 🚤 **Boat-Based Fun**
6. **Private Snorkel Trip to Trunk Bay Wall (St. John)**  
   - Rent a small boat for a guided snorkel to this iconic underwater wall teeming with barracuda, turtles, and tropical fish.
7. **Island Hopping to Waterlemon Cay**  
   - A quick 15-minute boat ride to this uninhabited island with pristine beaches—perfect for a secluded picnic.

### 🌿 **Relaxation & Culture**
8. **Laguna Cays Beach Club (St. Thomas)**  
   - Rent a cabana with cabana boys, snorkel gear, and service. Chill by the pool or float in the calm lagoon.
9. **Cruz Bay Farmers Market (Tuesdays/Saturdays)**  
   - Walk to the market for local crafts, fresh coconuts, and Caribbean snacks. Stroll shops in Cruz Bay afterward.

### ⚠️ **Key Tips**
- **Avoid pressure changes**: Stick to shallow snorkeling spots, calm bays, and flat-bottom boats for tours.
- **Rent a boat**: Companies like **Virgin Islands Water Sports** offer shallow-water excursions and private charters.
- **Turtle season**: If visiting in summer, morning walks on beaches like Reef Bay may reveal nesting turtles (check local guidelines).

Enjoy your boat-centric adventure—the Virgin Islands are a paradise for pressure-sensitive travelers! 🌴⛵️
