Sure, here's a simple checklist for your first self-filmed puppet show:

1. Get permission from the venue first—ask if they’re okay with you recording.
2. Set up your phone or camera before the show. Use a tripod if you can, to keep it steady.
3. Test the lighting. It’s better if it’s bright, so kids can see the puppets clearly.
4. Keep it simple. Focus on the puppet faces and actions, not a lot of background stuff.
5. After filming, review the footage to check the quality. Then edit if needed—cut out any mistakes or awkward pauses.
6. Upload it to your YouTube channel or whatever platform you choose. Remember to label it as kid-friendly.
7. Share it with friends or other volunteers to get feedback.

That’s it! Just start small and have fun with it.
