Absolutely! Here's a comprehensive shopping list based on our menu plan, organized by category for easy reference. Quantities are estimated for 24 people and include appetizers, sides, main dish, drinks, and extras.

### **Appetizers & Finger Foods**  
*(Serves 24+)*  
- Mini slider buns (2 loaves)  
- Ground beef or Italian sausage links (for pigs in a blanket)  
- Shrimp (for cocktail, ~2 lbs)  
- Coleslaw mix (2 large bags) or pre-made slaw  
- Pickles (for cocktail garnish)  
- Mini muffin tins (for baking sliders)  

### **Sides**  
*(Serves 24)*  
- **Scalloped Potatoes:**  
  - 3 lbs baby potatoes (peeled)  
  - 1 lb shredded cheddar cheese  
  - 1 cup sour cream  
  - 2 cups milk  
  - 1 tsp garlic powder  
  - Salt, pepper, and butter to taste  
- **Roasted Carrots (clean alternative!):**  
  - 3 lbs carrots (cut into sticks)  
  Kids’ carrots:  
  - 2–3 lbs baby carrots (optional)  
- **Roasted Asparagus:**  
  - 2 lbs asparagus  
  - 1/4 cup olive oil  
  - 1 lemon (juiced + zest)  
  - Salt, pepper, and garlic powder  

### **Main Dish**  
*(Serves 24)*  
- Ham (8–10 lbs, spiral-cut for ease)  
- 1 large onion (for glaze)  
- 2–3 cups brown sugar  
- 1 cup apple cider vinegar  
- 1/4 cup soy sauce  
- 2 tbsp mustard  
- 2 tbsp butter  

### **Drinks**  
*(For 24 guests)*  
- Champagne (12 bottles)  
- Kids’ Menu:  
  - 12 bottles grape juice (or apple juice)  
  Noisemakers/hats:  
  - 1 pack of kid-friendly noisemakers  
  - 2 packs festive party hats  
- Sparkling water (6 bottles)  
- Ice (20–30 lbs)  
- Juice boxes (24) for younger kids  
- Lemonade or iced tea (4 pitchers)  
- Coffee/tea (for adults)  

### **Extras**  
- Party napkins (24 packs)  
- Disposable plates/bowls (30 each)  
- Cups (30) + lids  
- Toothpicks (for sliders/pigs in blanket)  
- Toothpicks (for cocktail garnish)  
- Olive oil (for asparagus)  
- Lemon (for asparagus)  
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder (to taste)  
- Aluminum foil (for roasting)  
- Baking soda (for scalloped potatoes)  

### **Recipe Notes**  
1. **Scalloped Potatoes:** Layer potatoes in a 9x13 dish, top with cheese, sour cream, and milk. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 45–50 mins until bubbly.  
2. **Roasted Carrots:** Toss carrots with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 25–30 mins. *No sticky glaze—clean and easy!*  
3. **Asparagus:** Toss with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 12–15 mins.  
4. **Ham:** Slow-cook in a 300°F (150°C) oven. Baste with onion jam (simmer onion, sugar, vinegar, soy, and mustard).  

### **Tips**  
- Prep ahead: Make scalloped potatoes, chop veggies, and set up drink station.  
- Kids' Station: Set aside juice boxes, noisemakers, and coloring supplies near the kids.  
- Music: Curate a playlist (search "party hits 2023" on Spotify/YouTube).
