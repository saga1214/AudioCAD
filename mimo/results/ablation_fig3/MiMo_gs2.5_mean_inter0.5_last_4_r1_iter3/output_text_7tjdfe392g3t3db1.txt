To tune your instrument (likely the ukulele heard at the start), here's a step-by-step guide:

### 1. **Use a Tuner App or Device**  
   - **Apps**: Use free apps like *GuitarTuna*, *Fender Tune*, or *Tuner*. Open the app and point your phone’s microphone toward the instrument.  
   - **Clip-On Tuner**: Attach it to your instrument’s soundhole (e.g., for ukuleles or guitars). It vibrates or displays feedback when a string is out of tune.

### 2. **Tune to Standard Tuning**  
   Most instruments use specific tuning notes. For example:  
   - **Ukulele**: `G3-C4-G4-E5` (from thickest to thinnest string).  
   - **Guitar**: `E4-A4-D4-G4`.  
   *(Adjust based on your instrument)*.

### 3. **How to Use the Tuner**  
   - **Open Strings**: Pluck each string individually.  
   - **App/Clip-On Feedback**:  
     - **"Flat" (→ or —)**: String is too low. Turn the tuning peg *clockwise* (tighten) to raise pitch.  
     - **"Sharp" (↑ or ⬆️)**: String is too high. Turn peg *counter-clockwise* (loosen) to lower pitch.  
   - **Target Note**: Adjust until the tuner shows the correct note (e.g., `A4` for guitar’s 2nd string).

### 4. **Pro Tips**  
   - **Check Intonation**: Play a chord after tuning. If it sounds "off," recheck each string.  
   - **Visual Cues**: For clip-on tuners, watch the needle move toward the target note.  
   - **Avoid Over-Tightening**: Strings can snap if tightened too much. If a string breaks, replace it and retune.

### 5. **Practice**  
   - Tune after every session, as strings loosen over time.  
   - Use a metronome app to ensure rhythmic consistency.
