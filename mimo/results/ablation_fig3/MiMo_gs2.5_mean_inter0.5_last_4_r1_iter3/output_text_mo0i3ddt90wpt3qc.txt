You already mentioned SkinnyPop and Popchips, which are gluten-free. Another brand that you could look into is Siete. They have gluten-free snacks like their chickpea snacks and almond flour cookies.

For campfire-friendly gluten-free treats, rice cakes with natural peanut butter are a great option. And trail mix is always a classic that you can make yourself with nuts, seeds, and gluten-free dried fruit.
