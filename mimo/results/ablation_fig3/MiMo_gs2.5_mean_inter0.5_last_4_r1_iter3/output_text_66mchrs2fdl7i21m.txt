Of course, let's recap the travel times again to be clear. Driving from L.A. to Phoenix typically takes six hours without delays. A flight is much faster, with the actual flight time being about an hour, plus some time for getting to the airport. For your visit, both options are very feasible.

Regarding the horse, you will need a special Arizona horse permit to own one. However, if you're just considering buying a house with a large backyard, you don't need any special permits for that. But please keep in mind that owning a horse is a major financial and time commitment. Leasing one or starting with riding lessons at a stable could be a good way to explore the hobby first.
