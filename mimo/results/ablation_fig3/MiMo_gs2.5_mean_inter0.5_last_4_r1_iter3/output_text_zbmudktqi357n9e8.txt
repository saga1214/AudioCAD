Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time would really depend on when Mexico's autumn is. Their autumn is actually their springtime, from about March to May. That's when you'll see all the wildflowers and the green landscapes, which are great for vibrant painting.

If you're looking for that classic fall foliage vibe, though, I'd say late fall, around November to December, would be perfect. That's when you'll see the golden leaves and the cooler weather. Honestly, either time will give you amazing colors to work with. Just make sure to bring your watercolors and plenty of autumn colors!
