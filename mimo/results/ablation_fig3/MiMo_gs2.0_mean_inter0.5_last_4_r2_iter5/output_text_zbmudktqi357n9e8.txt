Alright, Mexico in autumn, that's a fantastic idea. If you're looking for inspiration, I'd say head to Mexico City between October and November. That’s their autumn, and it’s just stunning. You’ll find vibrant street art, beautiful autumn foliage in the city parks, and you can even paint the famous Zócalo or the Templo Mayor. 

Or, you could go to the Riviera Maya. The autumn colors there are incredible, and you can paint the jungle meets the coast, with those bright foliage colors against the ocean. Tulum is great for coastal scenes, and Playa del Carmen has some lovely market scenes where you can capture the energy and the colors. 

For more traditional autumn vibes, you could even head to Oaxaca. They have these amazing, warm autumns with incredible spices and markets. You could paint the Day of the Dead celebrations, the markets, or the countryside, which is just bursting with autumn colors. 

So yeah, Mexico is a great place for autumn inspiration. Just, you know, keep practicing your techniques while you're there, and you’ll bring back some amazing work. Good luck!
