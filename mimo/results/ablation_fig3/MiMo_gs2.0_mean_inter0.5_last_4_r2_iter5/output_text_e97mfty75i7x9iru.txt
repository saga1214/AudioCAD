For a cough, honey can help soothe it. You can also gargle with warm salt water, and propping your head up with an extra pillow can make it easier to rest.

And for brain fog, the flu can definitely cause it. Rest, staying hydrated, and eating well will all help. Some people also find that gentle exercise and taking a short break from screens can make a difference. But most importantly, just be patient with yourself, as it should clear up as you get better.
