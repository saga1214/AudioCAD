Hey there! Let’s give you that caffeine-free energy boost. For drinks, I'd suggest five things. First, green tea for a gentle caffeine kick. Second, a good cup of coffee for a more direct energy lift. Third, matcha, which is like green tea but more intense. Fourth, an espresso shot, a quick and strong option. And fifth, a smoothie for a healthy and energizing mix of fruits and greens.

As for snacks, here are five ideas to keep you going. First, an apple for a crunchy and healthy option. Second, a granola bar, which is easy and portable. Third, nuts for a protein and healthy fat boost. Fourth, yogurt with berries, a sweet and creamy treat. And fifth, a banana for a natural energy source. Hope that helps!
