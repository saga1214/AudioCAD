Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! If you're looking for authentic Asian noodles, I'd say check out these places in Dallas. 

First, there's Noodle House, which is a classic for ramen and other Asian noodle dishes. 

Second, there's Yum Yum Express, which is great for quick, flavorful noodles and dumplings. 

Third, if you want a more upscale experience, try Tonk2 for amazing ramen. 

For protein bars, I'd suggest looking into brands like Quest, RXBAR, or Clif Builders. They're all good options for energy and protein. Hope you enjoy your meal!
