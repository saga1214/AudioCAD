### 🍤 5 Meal Ideas (Mains)

   *Served with:* Jasmine rice or cauliflower rice.

2. **Spicy Tofu Stir-Fry with Mango Salsa**  
   *Her*: Diabetes-friendly stir-fry with tofu, bell peppers, and mango salsa.  
   *You*: Add shrimp or scallops to the stir-fry.  
   *Sauce*: Coconut aminos, ginger, and a pinch of cayenne.

3. **Mango-Shrimp Ceviche**  
   *You*: Fresh shrimp "cooked" in lime juice with mango, red onion, and jalapeños.  
   *Her*: Mango-based "ceviche" with avocado, cucumber, and a squeeze of lime (no seafood).

4. **Seafood Paella (Customizable)**  
   *You*: Paella with shrimp, mussels, and chorizo (for spice).  
   *Her*: Paella with extra peppers, artichokes, and *lots* of saffron-infused tomatoes/mango.

5. **Grilled Salmon with Mango-Chili Glaze**  
   *Both*: Salmon with a glaze of mango, lime, and a spicy honey touch.  
   *Her*: Lighter portion with extra mango and less glaze.

### 🍧 5 Dessert Ideas (Sweet & Ocean-Themed)
1. **Mango Sorbet with Sea Salt**  
   Fresh, diabetes-friendly, and visually stunning. Sprinkle with flaky sea salt for an "ocean" twist.

2. **Coconut Chia Pudding Parfaits**  
   *Her*: Layers of coconut chia pudding, mango puree, and fresh berries.  
   *You*: Add toasted coconut flakes and a hint of chili-lime zest.

3. **Dark Chocolate Sea Salt Truffles**  
   Rich, spice-free, and easy to portion. Use dark chocolate (70%+) and sea salt for contrast.

4. **Banana "Fish" Cake**  
   *Her*: Diabetes-friendly banana cake with dark chocolate chips. Decorate with blueberry "waves" and a silver drizzle.  
   *You*: Add mini chocolate "sharks" and a chili powder dusting.

5. **Blueberry-Lime Panna Cotta**  
   *Her*: Light, fruity, and low-glycemic. Top with fresh blueberries and a lime zest "ocean."  
   *You*: Add a spicy ginger swirl or a pinch of chili powder.

### 💡 Tips for Success
- **Spice Control**: Use chili flakes, ginger, or jalapeños sparingly—start mild and adjust to taste.  
- **Mango Magic**: Fresh mango is naturally sweet and diabetic-friendly in moderation.  
- **Shrimp Note**: Since shellfish is off-limits, shrimp is safe! Just avoid other seafood like crab or lobster.  
- **Presentation**: Use blue/green tablecloths, seashell accents, and "ocean" napkins for immersion!
