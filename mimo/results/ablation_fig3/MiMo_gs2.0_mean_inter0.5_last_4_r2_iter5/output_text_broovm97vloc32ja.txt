That's fantastic news! Having illustrations makes your picture book manuscript much more appealing. However, you should still approach it the same way as if it were a story alone: **first find an agent.**

Publishers almost never accept unsolicited manuscripts directly from an author-illustrator. They rely on their agents to vet submissions for them. So, your query letter will now need to be even more compelling because you're selling a complete, beautiful package.

You'll include a note with your query saying that you have the illustrations ready and are seeking an agent. When you're ready to send the full manuscript and images to the agent, you'll package them professionally. It's highly recommended to use a service called a 'password-protected PDF,' which keeps your artwork safe from being copied directly.
