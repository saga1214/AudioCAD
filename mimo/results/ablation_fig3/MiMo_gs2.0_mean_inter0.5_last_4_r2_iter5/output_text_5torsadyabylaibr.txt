For a classic Thanksgiving pie, a pumpkin pie is a must-try. It's a one-pot dessert that requires no baking. Another easy option is a simple fruit tart. You can buy a pre-made crust, make a quick filling, and assemble it.

For a main dish that's relatively easy but impressive, a honey-glazed ham is a great choice. It's a centerpiece dish that just needs to be glazed and baked.

And for a simple side dish, you could make a garlic green bean salad. It just involves steaming or boiling green beans, then tossing them with garlic and a little dressing. These are all one-pot recipes, so they should be perfect.
