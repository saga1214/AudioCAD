Of course. Here’s a shopping list for your meal plan. For starters, grab miso paste or dried wakame seaweed for the soup, or cucumbers and rice vinegar for the salad. For the main course, Chicken teriyaki is a great allergy-friendly choice. You'll need boneless, skinless chicken breasts, and for the teriyaki sauce, you'll need soy sauce, mirin, sake, and brown sugar. Also grab some jasmine rice, your favorite stir-fried veggies—broccoli or bell peppers are easy—and sesame oil. For dessert, matcha powder and heavy cream or condensed milk for ice cream, plus fresh mango and coconut milk and sugar for the sticky rice version. If you decide to make matcha ice cream, also pick up some cornstarch. That should cover everything. Enjoy your husband's special Asian-inspired dinner tonight!
