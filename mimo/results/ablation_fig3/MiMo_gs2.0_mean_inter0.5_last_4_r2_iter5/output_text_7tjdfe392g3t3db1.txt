You've got options! Here's a quick guide:

### For a Clip-On Electronic Tuner:
1. **Attach it** to your instrument or a stand.
2. **Pluck a string** one at a time.
3. **Follow the tuner's display**: It will show if a note is too sharp (red) or flat (green). Adjust until it hits the target note.

### For a Phone App (GuitarTuna, Fender Tune, etc.):
1. **Place your phone** close to the instrument’s soundhole or bridge.
2. **Strum a string** (or pluck individually).
3. **Listen to the app**: It will beep when the note is perfectly in tune, or buzz if it’s off. Keep adjusting until the sound is clear.

### Pro Tips:
- **For guitar/bass**: Use the app’s standard tuning notes (e.g., E, A, D, G, B, E for guitar).
- **For ukulele/piano**: Set the app to match your instrument’s tuning.
- **Double-check**: Use both an app *and* a clip tuner for precision!
