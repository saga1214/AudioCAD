Of course, here’s a quick summary of all the advice I've given you.
First, look into microchip feeders or timed ones to control how and when each cat eats.
Second, stretch your high-quality food by mixing it with fillers like rice if needed.
Third, seek out food banks, shelters, or charities for help with cat food.
Fourth, buy food in bulk to get better value per serving.
Fifth, prioritize prevention: keep their space clean, offer fresh water, and brush them.
Sixth, supplement their diet with safe, low-cost scraps when you can, like plain cooked meat or eggs.
Seventh, control their weight to avoid health problems.
Eighth, research community groups or online platforms for donations.
And finally, explain your situation to vets for advice, but focus on affordable care like vaccinations and spaying or neutering.
Hope that helps! Let me know if you need anything else.
