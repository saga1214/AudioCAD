Here are the three shrimp main courses I suggested earlier, with ingredients lists included:

1.  **Spicy Shrimp Scampi:**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
        *   8 oz pasta (like linguine or spaghetti)
        *   4 tbsp butter
        *   4 cloves garlic, minced
        *   1/4 cup dry white wine (like Pinot Grigio - optional but recommended)
        *   1/2 cup heavy cream
        *   1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
        *   1/4 cup fresh lemon zest
        *   1/4 cup finely chopped fresh parsley
        *   1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (for heat)
        *   Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
        *   1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

2.  **Shrimp Fra Diavolo:**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
        *   1 large onion, thinly sliced
        *   3-4 cloves garlic, minced
        *   1 large bell pepper (any color), thinly sliced
        *   1/2 cup red wine (optional)
        *   1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
        *   1/4 cup red wine vinegar
        *   1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
        *   1/4 cup chopped fresh basil
        *   1/4 cup chopped fresh oregano
        *   1/4 cup olive oil
        *   1/4 cup dry white wine (like Sauvignon Blanc - optional)
        *   1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
        *   1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese (optional)
        *   Red pepper flakes (amount to taste for spice)

3.  **Cajun Shrimp and Grits:**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
        *   1/2 cup chicken or vegetable broth
        *   1/2 cup heavy cream
        *   1/2 cup grated sharp cheddar cheese (plus more for topping)
        *   1/2 cup cooked quick-cooking grits (like Jiffy or Green Gourmet)
        *   2 tbsp butter
        *   1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
        *   1/4 cup chopped fresh chives
        *   1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese (optional)
        *   2 tbsp Cajun seasoning (for spice and flavor - see below)
        *   Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
        *   **Cajun Seasoning (approx. 1/2 cup for 1 lb shrimp):** Mix 1 tsp each paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, dried oregano, dried thyme, dried basil, dried mustard, salt, and freshly ground black pepper. Add 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust for heat) and 1/4 tsp white pepper. (Store leftover in a jar).
