Approaching this can feel intimidating, but there are a couple of good ways to start. One option is to request a brief meeting. You could send a short, professional email that says something like: "Hi, I'd like to schedule a brief meeting to discuss my progress and career development. Would you have 30 minutes available sometime next week?"

Another idea is to tie the conversation to a formal review. You could ask to have a 10-minute update meeting during your next performance review cycle to discuss your contributions and any potential growth opportunities.
