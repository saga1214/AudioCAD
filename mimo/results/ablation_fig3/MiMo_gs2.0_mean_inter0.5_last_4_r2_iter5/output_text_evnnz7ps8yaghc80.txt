It sounds like you're ready to build a solid plan. Here’s a sample weekly schedule you could follow.

On Monday, start with five minutes of beanbag patterns with one hand. Then, spend ten minutes on the transition from two to three beanbags. Finish with five minutes of scarf practice.

Tuesday, you could focus on using tennis balls. Try juggling those with just two in one hand to get used to their firm feel, and then attempt to add the third from the other hand.

Wednesday, switch back to beanbags and scarves. Spend ten minutes on beanbag patterns, and then ten minutes on slow patterns with the scarves.

Thursday, you could try a mix. Spend ten minutes on tennis ball patterns, and then ten minutes on beanbag patterns.

Friday is a great day to do a freeform session. Just follow your own curiosity, whether that's practicing three beanbags, scarves, or tennis balls.

Finally, on Saturday, do a relaxed, fun session with whatever objects you like best, and remember to celebrate your progress!

And one last tip: when stress does feel overwhelming, stop. Take a break. Juggling is about fun, not pressure. You've got this.
