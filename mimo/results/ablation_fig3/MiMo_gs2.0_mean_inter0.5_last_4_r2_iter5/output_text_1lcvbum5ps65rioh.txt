### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia, Canada  
In BC, U-turns are regulated by the *Motor Vehicle Act*. Key restrictions include:  
1. **At Intersections**: Prohibited unless a sign allows it.  
2. **Visibility**: You must see **150 meters** in both directions (≈1 football field + 50m).  
3. **Unsafe Locations**: Banned on curves, hills, near driveways, or where U-turns interfere with traffic.  
4. **Signs**: Always check for "NO U-TURN" signs.  

**Penalties**:  
- **Ticket**: $100–$200 + 3 demerit points.  
- **Repeat Offenses**: Higher fines and possible license suspension.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
**Traditional Ingredients** (serves 2):  
- **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti (or bucatini).  
- **Protein**: 200g guanciale (cured pork cheek) or pancetta/bacon.  
- **Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 1 egg yolk (whisked together).  
- **Cheese**: 100g Pecorino Romano (or Parmesan) + extra for grating.  
- **Seasoning**: Freshly ground black pepper, salt.  

**Essential Tools**:  
- Large pot for pasta, skillet/frying pan.  
- Tongs, mixing bowl, whisk.  

**Notes**:  
- Guanciale is traditional but hard to find—substitute pancetta or thick-cut bacon.  
- **Avoid cream**: Authentic carbonara is egg-based.  
- **Safety**: Cook eggs gently to prevent scrambling.  

---

### Quick Recipe Tips  
1. Cook pasta in **well-salted water** (like the sea).  
2. Render guanciale in a skillet, then remove from heat.  
3. Toss hot pasta with egg/cheese mixture *off-heat* to cook eggs gently.  
4. Stir in guanciale and pepper, then serve immediately.
