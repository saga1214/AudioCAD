That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour Beach Resort on St. John is perfect for your week—it's peaceful, has direct beach access, calm waters ideal for snorkeling right from shore, and is steps from Cruz Bay shops. Here are some great daytime activities right from your doorstep or nearby:

1.  **Snorkel the Reef (Right from the Beach!):** This is the biggest advantage! Secret Harbour has a shallow, protected bay where you can literally step into the water and see fish, turtles, and maybe even a parrotfish right from the shore. No boat needed!
2.  **Kayak Exploration:** Rent kayaks from the resort or nearby shops (like Cruz Bay Kayak) and paddle around the calm waters of Secret Harbour, exploring the little coves and reefs. It's a peaceful and active way to see the island up close.
3.  **Hike to Trunk Bay:** This is one of the most beautiful and easily accessible snorkel sites on St. John. The hike down is short and paved, leading to a stunning, clear lagoon with a famous underwater snorkeling tunnel (tunnels are optional and can be tight for some). The underwater scenery is fantastic.
4.  **Explore the Cruz Bay Area:** Wander the charming main street in Cruz Bay. Browse the unique shops (beaches, clothing, souvenirs), find fresh fruit at the market, grab a coffee or smoothie, and enjoy the local vibe. There are plenty of restaurants here too.
5.  **Hike to Waterlemon Cay (Slightly More Adventurous):** A short, easy hike (about 15-20 mins) from Cruz Bay leads to this beautiful, secluded cay with a lovely beach. It's a peaceful escape just steps from town.
6.  **Relax on Other Beautiful Shores:** Secret Harbour is lovely, but St. John has many more. Take a short taxi ride to **Cinnamon Bay** (famous for its white sand and underwater snorkeling) or **Lucky Strike** (known for its beautiful beach and snorkeling). The taxi ride is quick and easy.
7.  **Visit the Virgin Islands National Park:** St. John is part of the park. Hike the **Sage Mountain Trail** for incredible panoramic views of the island and the surrounding islands, or explore the **Cruz Bay Trail** which winds along the coast.
8.  **Visit the Cynic's Cove:** A short, easy walk from Cruz Bay leads to this stunning, crescent-shaped beach with turquoise water. It's often less crowded than Secret Harbour or Cinnamon Bay.

**Key things to remember for pressure sensitivity:**

*   **Avoid Sudden Changes:** Be mindful of going from high altitude (like a mountain trail) to low altitude (like a boat dive) or vice-versa quickly. Stick to coastal hikes or flat trails.
*   **Calm Waters Only:** Stick to the calm bays like Secret Harbour, Trunk Bay, Waterlemon Cay, or Cinnamon Bay (from the beach) for snorkeling or kayaking. Avoid rougher seas or boat trips that involve significant pressure changes.
