Of course! Let's go through homemade ideas again, plus a few more. For frozen treats, pumpkin and sweet potato bites or even small pieces of frozen blueberries work great. For baked cookies, peanut butter and oat cookies are a classic, but you can also try using plain yogurt or cottage cheese for a different flavor. For simple, crunchy snacks, try small carrot sticks, a few blue corn chips, or a few pieces of cooked chicken or turkey. 

Now for store-bought options, brands like Blue Buffalo, Wellness, and Zuke’s are all popular. You can also look at Greenies for dental health, or Merrick. Just be sure to read the labels to check for healthy ingredients. And remember, with all treats, portion control is key! Enjoy spoiling your little princess!
