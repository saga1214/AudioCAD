Okay, Northern California warm-weather swim vacation for three, with a gluten-free diet, a car too small, and a healthy budget. Got it. Palm Springs is the ideal choice. It's warm year-round, perfect for swimming, and very accessible.

Here’s a detailed four-day itinerary for you. I'll also add a fifth day if you'd like to stay longer.

On Day One, you'll arrive and settle in. You'll fly into John Wayne Airport in Orange County. From there, it's about a one-and-a-half-hour drive to Palm Springs. I'd recommend staying at the Miramonte Inn & Suites; it's affordable and has a pool. For dinner, The Palm Springs Baking Company is a great spot. They have a dedicated gluten-free menu and it has a lovely garden setting.

Day Two is all about pools and nature. In the morning, you can go to the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway. It’s a bit chilly at the top, so be sure to pack a light sweater. For lunch, the tramway has a restaurant with gluten-free options. In the afternoon, you can relax at the pool at the Hyatt Regency, which is a great spot for swimming. For dinner, try Elmer's Restaurant. It's a local favorite and is very good at handling gluten-free requests.

On Day Three, you can visit the Coachella Valley Botanical Garden. It's free, beautiful, and has a lovely café with gluten-free choices. In the afternoon, a great activity is the Palm Springs Lagoon, where you can rent a kayak or paddleboard. For dinner, the Tiki Bar & Restaurant is a fun, retro choice with gluten-free food and cocktails.

For Day Four, I suggest a trip to the Salton Sea. It's a unique area. You can visit the Crystal Cove Lagoon and the Decora Beach. For lunch, there's a place called Pappy's Hot Dogs that does gluten-free buns. In the afternoon, you can relax at a nearby resort pool. For dinner, back in Palm Springs, The Watercourse restaurant is excellent and has a dedicated gluten-free menu.

And if you have a fifth day, you could visit the nearby Temecula wine country. You can tour a winery like Bionic Winery, which is very friendly to gluten-free guests, and enjoy a nice lunch there.

For getting around, renting a car is necessary, but an Uber or Lyft is also a good option for shorter trips. I hope you have a wonderful vacation!
