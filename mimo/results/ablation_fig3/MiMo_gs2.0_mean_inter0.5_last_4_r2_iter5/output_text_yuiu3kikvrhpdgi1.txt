Of course! For Florida Zone 10B, especially during your cooler fall and winter months, I'd say start with radishes and lettuce. They grow really fast, like, you could get a harvest in about four to six weeks.

Herbs are also fantastic there. Basil, mint, parsley, and cilantro all do great in the heat. Tomatoes and peppers are also great choices. They're pretty forgiving and you'll get a ton of fruit from them.
