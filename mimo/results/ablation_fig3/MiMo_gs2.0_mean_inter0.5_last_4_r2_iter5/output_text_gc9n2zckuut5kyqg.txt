Absolutely! Here are some budget-friendly dessert ideas that are kid-friendly and diabetes-aware, while staying cost-effective:

### **Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas**  
1. **Homemade Fruit Pies or Crumbles**  
   - Use seasonal, inexpensive fruits (apples, berries, peaches).  
   - Make a simple oat/whole-wheat crust to save money.  
   - Serve in mini portions to reduce waste.  

2. **DIY Sundae Bar**  
   - Offer plain ice cream (or dairy-free alternatives) as a base.  
   - Provide toppings: fresh fruit slices, nuts, seeds, and a sprinkle of cinnamon.  
   - Kids can customize, so no extra costs for sugary syrups.  

3. **Mini Oatmeal Cookies or Energy Bites**  
   - Use rolled oats, mashed bananas, or applesauce for binding.  
   - Add dried fruit, nuts, or seeds for texture.  
   - Bake in small batches to save on oven time/electricity.  

4. **Frozen Yogurt Bark**  
   - Spread Greek yogurt on parchment paper.  
   - Top with berries, honey (sparingly), and a drizzle of fruit compote.  
   - Freeze, then break into pieces—healthy and cheap!  

5. **Banana "Nice Cream"**  
   - Blend frozen bananas until creamy.  
   - Serve in cones or bowls with a dash of cinnamon.  
   - Zero added sugar, minimal cost.  

6. **Rice Krispie Treats**  
   - Use mini marshmallows and rice cereal (bulk-bought).  
   - Add unsweetened shredded coconut or chocolate chips for fun variations.  

7. **Fruit Salad Jars**  
   - Layer seasonal fruits (watermelon, grapes, melon) in clear cups.  
   - Top with a dollop of Greek yogurt or whipped cream.  
   - No baking required—fresh and refreshing!  

### **Budget-Saving Tips**  
- **Buy in bulk**: Frozen fruits, oats, and nuts are often cheaper than fresh.  
- **Repurpose leftovers**: Extra BBQ buns can become mini pancakes or waffles.  
- **Volunteer help**: Assign family members to bake or prep desserts.  
- **Local donations**: Ask neighbors for homemade treats (cookies, pies) instead of buying.  

### **Example Menu**  
- **Main BBQ**: Burgers/hot dogs *plus* veggie skewers (bell peppers, zucchini).  
- **Desserts**: Fruit pies + rice krispie treats + banana nice cream.  
- **Drinks**: Water, herbal tea, sugar-free lemonade.
