For winter chicks integrated into the adult coop at five weeks old, you need bedding that's warm, clean, and safe. A brooder plate will heat a defined warm zone, so the bedding in that area should be soft and insulating. For the rest of the area, the rubber mats on the floor combined with sand you mentioned earlier will provide a good base.

So for the warm zone under the plate, your best choice is a thick layer of wool pellets, also known as woolens. They are incredibly warm, they don't attract rodents, and they're very easy to clean up. You could also use crumpled up paper towels or small cardboard strips, which the chicks can shred for insulation.

To top it all off, you can add a light layer of crumpled up newspaper over everything. This gives the chicks something to peck at and helps them create a nice, cozy pile to huddle in. Just make sure to replace it daily to keep it clean.
