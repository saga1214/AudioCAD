You've got all the essentials, but there are a few more things that will make the process much smoother and safer.

First, make sure you have a drill and a set of cabinet screws. Habitat restorios usually sell them, or you can find them at a hardware store. Using screws is much stronger than nails for this job. 

Also, you'll need something to act as shims to get the base cabinets perfectly level. You can use small pieces of wood or even old credit cards.

A stud finder is essential for finding those wall studs.

And most importantly, please have everyone wear safety glasses to protect their eyes from any dust or debris. 

You're all set to get started! Just remember to work safely.
