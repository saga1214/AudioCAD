Of course. I've put together a shopping list for you. It's broken down into drinks, appetizers, sides, main dishes, and other essentials.

First, for drinks: you'll need sparkling wine or champagne for the adults, plus sparkling cider for the kids and other non-alcoholic guests. Don't forget mixers, ice, and plenty of cups and straws. You'll also need juice, both for the signature cocktail and for the kids.

Next, for the appetizers: you'll need ground beef or pork for pigs in a blankrt, shrimp for the shrimp cocktail, slider meat and buns, a condiment spread, and any store-bought mini slider toppings you like.

For the sides: you'll need potatoes and butter for the scalloped potatoes, olive oil, salt, and asparagus for the roasted asparagus, and plain carrots and olive oil for the roasted carrot sides.

For the main dish: you'll need a ham, scalloped potatoes, and the roasted sides we just discussed.

For other party essentials, you should think about kid-friendly hats or noisemakers, a playlist for music, and decorations like balloons. Oh, and of course, plenty of paper towels and trash bags for cleanup.
