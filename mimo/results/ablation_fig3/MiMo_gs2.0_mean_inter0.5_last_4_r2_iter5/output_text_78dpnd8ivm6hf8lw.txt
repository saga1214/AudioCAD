Okay, let's recap the analogy for you. The heart is the gas station and the engine, pumping the blood. The lungs are the fresh fuel source where the cars get their oxygen. The veins are the return roads bringing blood back to the heart. The kidneys are the car wash and junkyard, cleaning the blood of waste. And the liver is the service station sorting nutrients and processing fuel. 

It's a whole team working together to keep the body's delivery system running smoothly!
