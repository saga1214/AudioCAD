Okay, since tacos seemed a bit too easy, you're ready for your first stir-fry adventure!

The wood spoon incident is definitely something to remember to avoid, so let's focus on the right tools. The key to great stir-fry noodles is to salt your water generously. This helps the noodles get a nice, even coating of starch, which keeps them from sticking together.

Also, make sure to rinse them very well after they're cooked. This gets rid of most of the excess starch. Finally, toss them with a little bit of oil right after rinsing.

When you add them to the stir-fry, just toss them in for the last minute or two and let them heat through. You shouldn't cook them for too long, or they'll get mushy. You're just warming them up in their own starch coating. You've got this!
