Alright, here’s a pirate nautical birthday party shopping list designed for seven seven-year-olds at the beach, keeping sand concerns and allergies in mind. It includes appetizers, themed mains, sides, drinks, and packaging to maintain immersion. The watermelon allergy is noted.

First, for nautical-themed appetizers and small main courses. For sandwiches and wraps, you could do mini slider bars cut into four pieces, maybe themed as 'Buried Treasure Sliders' by using flag-shaped cookie cutters on the buns. You can make them ham and cheese or turkey and cheese, just be sure to check for any other allergies. Another idea is themed rolls, which are small dinner rolls shaped like pirate ships or treasure chests.

Next, for other finger foods that are easy to handle, you could get veggie sticks like carrots, cucumbers, and bell peppers, calling them 'pirate jewels.' Also, goldfish crackers are great as 'gold coins.' Cheese cubes, either mozzarella or cheddar, can be called 'cheese treasure.' And for fruit skewers, you can use grapes or apple slices that have been pre-sliced to avoid watermelon. Just be sure to label any fruit differently.

For drinks, individual juice boxes work well. You could theme them by calling one 'Pirate Punch' and another 'Sea Water.' Small water bottles are also a good, spill-resistant option.

Now, for packaging and fun touches, you could use treasure chest snack containers to hold the food. Small portioned bags are great to seal everything up and keep it sand-free. And for labels, you can write fun pirate-themed names on the containers, like 'Captain’s Rations' or 'Treasure Chest.'

Finally, a few extras for the theme. Pirate hats or eye patches for the kids could be fun. A DIY treasure hunt with buried 'gold' like chocolate coins would be a great activity. And don't forget sunscreen, hats, and plenty of wet wipes for cleanup!

Let me know if you'd like any adjustments! Happy celebrating, Captain!
