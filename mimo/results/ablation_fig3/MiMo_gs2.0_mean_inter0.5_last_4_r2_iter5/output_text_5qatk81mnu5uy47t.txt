### 🌴 Core Dive Locations (Focus on Pacific Coast)
1. **Tenerife Island (Puntarenas)**  
   - **Why**: Costa Rica’s diving epicenter. Warm, clear Caribbean waters (tropical!) with stunning coral reefs, shipwrecks, and marine life.  
   - **Key Sites**:  
     - **Coral Garden**: Vibrant coral formations, schools of fish.  
     - **Shipwreck Exploration**: *La Cacique* (nearby) and WWII wrecks.  
     - **Maderas Reef**: Whale sharks (seasonal), manta rays, and turtles.  
   - **Fin Suggestion**: Start with **short fins** for calmer waters and maneuverability. Rent longer ones if currents intensify.  

2. **Ostional**  
   - **Why**: Remote Pacific coast gem with dramatic volcanic landscapes. Famous for **humpback whale migrations** (Dec–Apr) and nesting sea turtles.  
   - **Key Sites**:  
     - **Whale Shark Dives**: Year-round sightings.  
     - **Punta Maito**: Bioluminescent bays (snorkel at night!).  
     - **Coral Bays**: Healthy reefs for macro photography.  

3. **Manuel Antonio**  
   - **Why**: Mix of reefs, seamounts, and shallow bays. Perfect for beginners and advanced divers.  
   - **Key Sites**:  
     - **Dolphins & Sea Turtles**: Frequent sightings.  
     - **Barranco del Salado**: Rocky ledges with hammerheads.  

### 🏝️ Land Adventures to Complement Diving
- **Monteverde Cloud Forest**: Zip-lining, wildlife spotting.  
- **Arenal Volcano**: Hot springs, hiking.  
- **Tortuguero National Park**: Sea turtle conservation (nesting season: May–Nov).  
- **Waterfall Swims**: **Selvatura** or **Catarina Falls**.  

### 🧳 Practical Tips
- **Fin Choice**: Stick with **short fins** for now. They’re easier for beginners, packable, and ideal for most Costa Rican sites.  
- **Dive Operators**: Book with **Puntarenas Dive Center** or **Tenerife Dive** for expert guides.  
- **Season**: **November–April** (best visibility, warmest water). Whale sharks peak Dec–Mar.  
- **Logistics**: Fly into **San José**, rent a car, or use shuttles to Tenerife/Ostional.  

### 💡 Pro Tip to Resolve Your Debate
Test both fin types at a **local dive shop** before booking gear. Many offer rentals. Short fins = agility; long fins = endurance. Costa Rica’s varied sites suit both!
