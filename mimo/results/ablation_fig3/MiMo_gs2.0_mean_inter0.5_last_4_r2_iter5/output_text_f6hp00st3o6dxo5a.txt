That's still not quite right, because a mutation needs to happen in the DNA to create a new species.

Here’s how it really works: the creature laying the egg was very close to being a chicken, but it wasn't one yet. During the formation of that egg, a brand-new genetic mutation occurred in the DNA. The hatching process didn't *create* that mutation; the mutation already happened inside the egg.

Therefore, the first true chicken emerged from the egg laid by its predecessor. The chicken still came first.
