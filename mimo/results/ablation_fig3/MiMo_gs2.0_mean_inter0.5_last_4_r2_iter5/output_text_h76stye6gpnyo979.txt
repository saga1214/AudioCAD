Here’s a simple, step-by-step plan to bring your travel dreams and home savings together.

First, assign each trip to a specific savings fund. Bolivia salt flats can go into your apartment down payment fund, and Faroe Islands puffins can go into its own bucket list fund.

Next, calculate the cost for each trip, including flights, lodging, and food. Even a rough estimate helps you see how much you need to save each month.

Then, try to automate your savings by setting up small, regular transfers to each fund right after you get paid.

Finally, set small, weekly goals. For example, spend thirty minutes one evening researching just one of the trips, or maybe find a cheaper grocery bill to boost your savings. By breaking your ambitions into tiny, manageable pieces, you can make consistent progress without getting overwhelmed.
