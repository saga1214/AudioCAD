You're right, one-time high intake isn't harmful. However, the general daily recommendation of about 2,300 milligrams is the limit for the entire day, not a target to hit.

So, if your portion has 450 milligrams and that's your only meal, it's not necessarily 'too much' in a single sitting. But it does mean your single meal contains about 20 percent of your entire day's recommended sodium. 

For context, if you were to eat this every day for a week, that could add up to about 3,150 milligrams of sodium for the week, which is significantly over the recommended limit.

So to summarize: It's not harmful in a single meal, but it is a very high amount for a single meal, and if you ate it regularly, it would exceed your daily intake.
