Okay, so if you're thinking of ending a desert adventure with a Vegas trip, that’s a classic combo! You could totally do a night or two in Vegas after exploring Nevada. 

For a proper Vegas experience, you don’t have to blow a bunch of cash on expensive hotels or overpriced drinks. You could find a decent, mid-range hotel near the strip that offers free breakfast and maybe a pool. Then, instead of the typical Vegas crowds, check out some of the unique spots off the strip, like the Neon Boneyard for some quirky history or the Pinball Museum for a fun, cheap activity. 

If you both like live music, you could find a small, intimate venue instead of a big club. Or, if you’re into art, the Mob Museum is surprisingly cool and not too pricy. The key is to balance the classic Vegas stuff with more unique, offbeat experiences to make it your own adventure.
