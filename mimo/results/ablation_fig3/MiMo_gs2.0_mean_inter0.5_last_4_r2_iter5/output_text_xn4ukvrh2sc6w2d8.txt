Of course! Here’s a full list of the five cocktails with all the details for your bartenders.

First up, Old Fashioned: You’ll need two ounces of bourbon, one sugar cube, half an ounce of Angostura bitters, an orange twist for garnish, and some ice. You can either muddle the sugar cube with a tiny bit of water and the bitters, or just add a quarter-ounce of simple syrup if you want to skip that step.

Second, the Paper Plane: This one is equal parts, so that’s one ounce of bourbon, one ounce of Aperol, three-quarters of an ounce of Amaro Nonino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice.

Third, the Whiskey Sour. If you’re adding the raw egg white, you’ll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and one egg white. Remember to dry shake first. If you're skipping the egg white, it’s just two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and an optional cherry or orange slice for a garnish.

Fourth, the Aviation: You’ll need one and a half ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, a quarter-ounce of crème de violette, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice.

And for your fifth cocktail, I’d recommend the French 75. For that, you'll need three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and about two ounces of Champagne. You just shake the first three ingredients, strain them into a champagne flute, and top it off with the bubbly.
