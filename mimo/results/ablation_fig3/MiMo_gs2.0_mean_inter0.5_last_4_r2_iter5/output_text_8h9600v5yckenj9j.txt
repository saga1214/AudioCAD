### DIY Fog Chiller Instructions
1. **Gather Supplies**:  
   - Large clear plastic storage container (e.g., 5-gallon cooler or bin)  
   - Wire rack, baking sheet, or shelf  
   - Hole saw/drill (for entry/exit ports)  
   - Ice/frozen water bottles  
   - Fog machine & tubing  

2. **Cut Holes**:  
   - **Input**: Cut a hole on one side large enough for the fog machine nozzle + tubing to fit.  
   - **Output**: Cut a second hole on the *opposite* or elevated side.  

3. **Build the Cooler**:  
   - Place the wire rack inside the container.  
   - Pack ice or frozen water bottles around the rack (leave space for fog to flow).  
   - Position the fog machine behind the input hole, blowing fog into the cooler.  

4. **Test & Adjust**:  
   - As fog enters the cooler, it cools and becomes denser—forcing it out through the output hole.  
   - If fog disperses too fast, add more ice/bottles or close the output hole slightly with cardboard.

### Top Halloween Candy Picks (Avoid Leftovers!):
1. **Universal Favorites**:  
   - **Sour Patch Kids**  
   - **Reese's Cups/Peanut Butter Cups**  
   - **Skittles**  
   - **M&M's** (especially seasonal colors)  
   - **Gummy Worms/Chocolates**  

2. **Allergy-Friendly (Check Labels)**:  
   - **Dairy-free chocolate** (e.g., Enjoy Life brand)  
   - **Non-dye gummies** (e.g., Haribo "Natural" line)  
   - **Plain popcorn balls**  

3. **Avoid These (Common Pitfalls)**:  
   - **Black licorice** (hated by many!)  
   - **Old candy** (check expiration dates)  
   - **Overly sweet/generic brands** (like "Halloween candy bags" from bulk stores)  

**Pro Tip**: Buy 20-30% more candy than you think you need—guests will take it! Prioritize individually wrapped pieces for easy grabbing.
