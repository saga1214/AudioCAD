Sure. Let’s break it down task by task.

For drywall stuff, the joint compound small tub is usually under $10. Sandpaper or sponges are cheap, maybe $5. A 6-inch putty knife is around $5. Primer and paint depend on the size of the wall, but let’s say a small can is $15. Damaged molding, if any, is about $10 per foot. Nails or adhesive is under $10. So, the drywall repair budget is probably somewhere between $40 and $70.

For the cake, flour and sugar are both under $10. Baking powder is cheap, under $5. Two sticks of butter is about $4. Three eggs under $5. Milk under $5. Vanilla under $5. Frosting ingredients and powdered sugar are around $10. Pans depend on style, but under $10. All together, the cake supplies should be around $50.

So, if you add it all up, a good budget to have with you would be around $90 to $120. That should cover both tasks without you overspending. Let me know if you want to adjust anything!
