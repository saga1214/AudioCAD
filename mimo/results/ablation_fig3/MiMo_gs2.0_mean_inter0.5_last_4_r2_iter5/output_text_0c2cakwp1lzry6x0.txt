Here's a practical guide for installing your TV, planters, and shelves safely and efficiently:

### 📺 TV Mount Installation  
1. **Find studs**: Use a stud finder (or hammer test) to locate wall studs. Mounting to studs prevents cracking drywall.  
2. **Measure & mark**: Hold the bracket against the wall, mark screw holes, and use a level.  
3. **Secure bracket**: Attach the bracket to studs with provided screws.  
4. **Mount TV**: Align the TV’s mounting holes with the bracket and secure.  
5. **Optional**: Use a flexible *mounting arm* if you want swivel/tilt adjustments.  
**Safety tip**: For TVs over 55" or heavy art, hire a pro.  

### 🌿 Hanging Planters  
1. **Choose hardware**: Use *heavy-duty hooks* (e.g., S-hooks rated for 25+ lbs) or ceiling-rated hangers.  
2. **Locate anchors**: If no studs, drill into wall anchors (e.g., toggle bolts) rated for the planter’s weight.  
3. **Install hooks**: Screw hooks into anchors/studs.  
4. **Hang planters**: Attach planters to hooks.  
**Tip**: Test weight distribution first—don’t overload one side!  

### 🪚 Floating Shelves  
1. **Check weight limits**: Ensure shelves support your art/furniture.  
2. **Use anchors**: For drywall, install *toggle bolts* or *molly bolts* at shelf ends.  
3. **Measure & level**: Place shelves at eye-level, pre-drill holes to prevent cracking, then screw in.  
4. **Secure edges**: Add trim or LED lighting to highlight pieces.  
**Safety**: Don’t place shelves above head height—use a step stool!  

### 💡 Pro Tips  
- **Safety first**: Wear safety glasses and ear protection when drilling.  
- **Time-saving**: Buy pre-assembled shelves if possible.  
- **Organize**: Group similar items (e.g., small plants near a window, art by the sofa).  
- **Break it down**: Dedicate one Sunday to one item—don’t rush!  

Remember: **No drilling holes**—follow your landlord’s rules! If unsure, YouTube tutorials (e.g., "TV mount installation") or a local handyman can help. Enjoy your personalized space! 🎨✨
