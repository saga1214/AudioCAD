There are many potential causes for chronic redness. Rosacea is a very common one, especially for the face. Sun damage over many years can also cause persistent redness. Some medications or even allergies could be factors, and in some cases, it could be linked to vascular issues like vasculitis.

Now, about vasculitis itself. It's a condition where the walls of your blood vessels become inflamed. This inflammation can affect blood vessels of any size—large, medium, or small—which is why it can cause such widespread symptoms.

Common signs include fever, fatigue, and painful rashes that can appear anywhere on the body. Treatment usually involves medications that suppress the overactive immune system. Starting ibuprofen without talking to your doctor is not recommended, as they need to understand the underlying reason for the inflammation to treat it safely and effectively.
