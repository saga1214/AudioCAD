Okay, the camper van pre-trip maintenance question is a solid one—old diesel vehicles need extra care. Let’s break this down. For brakes, significant wear signs include visual changes like worn brake pads (metal wear indicators should be visible), uneven pedal feel, or a high-pitched squealing noise when braking. You should also check for excessive dust or fluid leaks under the van. If you’re not comfortable inspecting these things, a local mechanic can do a quick brake check before you leave. For spark plugs—this depends on your van’s age and model, but in many diesel engines, spark plugs aren’t user-serviceable unless it’s a very old or modified vehicle. If yours are accessible, you’ll need a special socket and a torque wrench to avoid damaging threads. Always refer to the owner’s manual for specs. If it’s not a common DIY task, just get a mechanic to handle it—diesel engines can be temperamental. The key is making sure both safety and performance are covered before you hit the road.
