Here are five fantastic road trips from Vancouver, each with a mix of beautiful scenery, great food, and unique activities:

First, you could head to Victoria and the Saanich Peninsula. The drive through the scenic Gulf Islands National Park Reserve is lovely. A must-see is Butchart National Park, with its beautiful gardens and historic chimneys. For a special meal, try the fine dining at a restaurant like Aire by Jean-Georges.

Second, you could explore the Okanagan Valley. The drive along the lakeshore is stunning. You can visit the galleries and vineyards in Penticton, like the Naramata Centre for the Arts. For a special experience, you could take a hot air balloon ride at sunrise or enjoy a gourmet meal at a winery.

Third, for a mountain adventure, you can drive to Whistler. The Blackcomb Gorge Skywalk is a breathtaking walk on a high cable bridge. You could also try a guided snowshoe tour in the winter. For a special meal, try the farm-to-table cuisine at Blackcomb Grill.

Fourth, you could visit the Fraser Valley. It's a short drive and great for farm experiences. You can visit the Capilano Suspension Bridge Park or the Stanley Park Farm. For a special meal, try a farm-to-table dinner at a restaurant like The Pig & Whistle.

Finally, for a coastal trip, you could go to the Sunshine Coast. You can drive the stunning Sea to Sky Highway. A beautiful spot is the Lighthouse Park on Galiano Island. For a special meal, try the fresh seafood at a restaurant like Sable Restaurant.

These trips should give you plenty of beautiful scenery, great food, and unique experiences to enjoy!
