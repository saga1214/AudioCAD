For “Retro Tech Day,” you could do a potluck-style lunch where everyone brings a snack and a main dish from that decade. For example, for the ‘80s, people could bring mini bagels and lox for the snacks, and for the main dish, maybe a tuna casserole. For the ‘90s, snacks could be pretzels and hummus, and the main could be a classic macaroni salad. That way, it ties the food right into the theme.
