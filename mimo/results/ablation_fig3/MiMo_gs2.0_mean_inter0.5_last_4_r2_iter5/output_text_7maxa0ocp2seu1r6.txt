**I. Core Measurements (Take for Every Fitting):**
1.  **Bust:** Across the fullest part.
2.  **Natural Waist:** The narrowest part of the torso, usually an inch or two below the bust.
3.  **Hips:** Across the fullest part of the hips, usually an inch or two below the waist.

**II. Essential Additional Measurements (For Fitted Garments):**
4.  **Inseam:** From the crotch down the inner thigh to the ankle (for pants/skirts).
5.  **Sleeve Length:** From the underarm point to the wrist bone.
6.  **Shoulder Width:** Across the shoulders (across the upper back for jackets/shirts).

**III. Important for Tailored Fits (Add as Needed):**
7.  **Thigh:** From the crotch down the front of the thigh to the top of the knee (for pants/skirts).
8.  **Neck:** For collars (measure around the neck at the base).
9.  **Bicep:** From shoulder point to the top of the bicep muscle (for sleeves).
10. **Across the Back (SAG):** From shoulder point to shoulder point across the upper back (critical for jackets/shirts to prevent pulling).

**IV. Crucial for Comfort (Especially on Tall Clients):**
11. **Front Rise:** From the crotch point up to the waistband (front).
12. **Back Rise:** From the crotch point up to the waistband (back) - *Essential for tall clients to prevent pulling when sitting or walking.*
