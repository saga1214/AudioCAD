Under older high school rules, if Red Team number nine was in an offside position when Green Team number ten kicked the ball for a goal kick, it wouldn't have been an offense because of the exception I mentioned earlier. However, those rules have since changed. In the current rulebook, an offside player can still be penalized if they interfere with play, even during a restart.
