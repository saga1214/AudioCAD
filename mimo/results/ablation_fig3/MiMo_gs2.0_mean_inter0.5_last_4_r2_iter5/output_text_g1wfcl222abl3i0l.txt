2. **Check the glue type**: Use only **hot glue sticks**—not liquid glue.  
3. **Let it cool slightly** (20–30 seconds) if it’s overheating.  
4. **Try a different glue stick**—sometimes old or dry ones don’t adhere well.  

### While You Wait:

- **Work in short bursts** (hold the stick to the tip for 3–5 seconds).
