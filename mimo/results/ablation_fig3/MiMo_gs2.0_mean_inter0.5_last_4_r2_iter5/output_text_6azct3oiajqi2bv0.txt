For shipping winter clothing without damaging the lining, here's a step-by-step method:  

1. **Use a Soft, Acid-Free Box**  
   - Avoid cardboard that could tear the fabric.  
   - Place a layer of **cotton jersey fabric** (like old t-shirts) or **bubble wrap** at the bottom.  

2. **Pack the Jacket Securely**  
   - Lay the jacket flat inside the box.  
   - Wrap it **gently** in **clean cotton cloth** (e.g., a pillowcase or scarf) to cushion and prevent friction.  
   - Avoid overstuffing – leave space for padding.  

3. **Protect the Lining**  
   - Cover the jacket with **acid-free tissue paper** (available at craft stores). This absorbs moisture and prevents the lining from sticking to the outer fabric.  
   - If using bubble wrap, wrap the *entire jacket* in it first, then place it in the box.  

4. **Secure the Box**  
   - Add **newspaper** or extra cloth around the jacket.  
   - Seal the box tightly with strong tape.  

5. **Label Clearly**  
   - Mark "Fragile – Handle with Care" and "Clothing – Avoid Sun/Moisture."  

**Pro Tip:**  
- Use **Velcro straps** instead of tape to avoid glue residue on the fabric.  
- For jackets with zippers, zip them up before packing to prevent snagging.  

This method minimizes friction and keeps the lining intact. Nordstrom Rack and REI both offer generous return policies if the item arrives damaged – always keep your receipt!
