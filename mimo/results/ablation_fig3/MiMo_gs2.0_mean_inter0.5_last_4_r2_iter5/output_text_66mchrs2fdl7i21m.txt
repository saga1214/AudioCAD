To fly from Los Angeles to Phoenix, the actual flight time is indeed about forty-five to fifty minutes. However, it's important to remember that you have to add in the time for getting to the airport, security, and boarding, so the total door-to-door time is usually closer to four to five hours.

Driving by car is faster in total travel time, as you mentioned, typically taking about five and a half to six hours door-to-door. So, for a last-minute trip, driving is generally the more time-efficient option.
