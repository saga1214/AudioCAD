Let's break down the potential gains/losses based on your scenario:  
**Investing $10,000 in a stock trading at $62.87/share**  

### 1. **10% Gain Scenario**  
- **Target selling price**: $62.87 × 1.10 = **$69.17/share**  
- **Gain per share**: $69.17 - $62.87 = **$6.30/share**  
- **Total gain**:  
  $6.30 × (10,000 / $62.87) ≈ **$99,986.13**  
  (You would sell **~159 shares** at $69.17/share).  

### 2. **10% Loss Scenario (Stop-Loss)**  
- **Stop-loss price**: $62.87 × 0.90 = **$56.58/share**  
- **Loss per share**: $62.87 - $56.58 = **$6.29/share**  
- **Total loss**:  
  $6.29 × (10,000 / $62.87) ≈ **$99,986.13**  
  (You would sell **~159 shares** at $56.58/share).  

### Key Notes:  
- **Leverage**: Futures magnify gains/losses (e.g., a 10% stock move in futures could mean a 10x gain/loss if leveraged).  
- **Risks**:  
  - Futures are **highly volatile** and require expertise.  
  - **Margin requirements** (borrowed funds) increase risk.  
  - Stop-losses may not guarantee losses won’t exceed 10% if gaps or rapid price moves occur.  
- **Fees**: Transaction costs (commissions, spreads) reduce net returns.  

### Advice:  
- **Stocks** are less risky for short-term goals but rarely hit 10% monthly returns.  
- **Futures** suit speculators but demand rigorous learning. Consider starting with paper trading.  
- **Diversify**: Spreading $10,000 across 5-10 stocks reduces risk.
