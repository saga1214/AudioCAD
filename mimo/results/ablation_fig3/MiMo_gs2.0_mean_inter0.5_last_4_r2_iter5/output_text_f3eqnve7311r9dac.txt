The 1940s and 50s market is highly specialized. You're right that supply and condition are everything, so only consider books that have been professionally graded and sealed by CGC. Finding a decent copy of an old book is expensive, but the right one can be a great long-term investment.

Here are ten historically significant books from that era. First, *Action Comics* number one, which launched Superman and is the most famous comic of all time. Second, *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, which introduced Batman. Third, *Superboy* number one is Superman's first solo adventure. Fourth, *The Flash Comics* number one is the first appearance of The Flash. Fifth, *Warrior* number one is the first comic to feature Green Lantern. Sixth, *Aquaman* number one from 1953 is his first solo book. Seventh, *The Brave and the Bold* number twenty-five is the first team-up of Batman and Superman. Eighth, *The Spectre* number one, which introduced this dark, supernatural hero. Ninth, *The Phantom* number one from 1939 is the first appearance of this classic pulp hero. And tenth, *Tales of the Black Panther* number one is the first appearance of T’Challa, who would later become Black Panther.

Just remember, because they are so old and rare, you should only buy these from a very reputable source or through a major auction house.
