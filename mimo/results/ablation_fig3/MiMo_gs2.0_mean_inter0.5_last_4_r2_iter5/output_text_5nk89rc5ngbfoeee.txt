For your end-of-year celebration, an **outdoor park barbecue** is a fantastic choice—it’s inclusive, memorable, and aligns with Brownies' spirit of community and adventure. Here’s a step-by-step plan to make it work smoothly:

### 1. **Location & Logistics**
   - **Park Venue**: Reserve a picnic area or open space early (contact the park manager). Ensure there’s shade, restrooms, and parking.
   - **Rain Plan**: Have a backup indoor venue (e.g., the cafeteria) and a tent rental on standby. This covers unpredictability without derailing the plan.

### 2. **Food & Snacks**
   - **Parent Partnership**: Assign a "Snack Crew" (2-3 parents) to organize food. Examples:
     - **Main**: BBQ (chicken, veggie burgers, gluten-free options) + pizza (order from a local pizzeria for safety).
     - **Sides**: Fruit skewers, veggie cups, and dairy-free chips (if needed).
     - **Desserts**: Ice cream sundaes (with toppings) or a sundae bar—let parents contribute toppings (sprinkles, caramel, etc.).
   - **Dietary Needs**: Send a pre-event survey for allergies/dairy restrictions. Label all food clearly.

### 3. **Activities & Games**
   - **Brownie-Themed Games**: 
     - **Three-Legged Race**: Classic and badge-relevant (Community Helpers badge).
     - **Wheelbarrow Race**: Fun for all ages—use cones for safety.
     - **S’mores Station**: Simple, cozy, and bonding.
   - **Craft Corner**: Set up a badge-making table (e.g., decorating friendship bracelets or nature crafts).

### 4. **Cost Management**
   - **Dues**: Charge a small end-of-year fee ($10–$15) covering:
     - Food for non-parent contributors.
     - Game supplies (cones, craft materials).
   - **Parents’ Responsibility**: Cover their own family’s food unless contributing to the BBQ. Clearly communicate this upfront.

### 5. **Timeline**
   - **Afternoon Schedule**:
     - 1:00 PM: Arrival & food setup.
     - 1:30 PM: Icebreaker (e.g., "Best Brownie Memory" stories).
     - 2:00 PM: Games + craft time.
     - 3:30 PM: S’mores + awards (e.g., "Most Helpful Troop Leader" for you!).
     - 4:00 PM: Group photo + cleanup.

### 6. **Your Pregnancy & Comfort**
   - **Delegate**: Ask a parent to oversee food setup/cleanup so you can relax.
   - **Seating**: Choose a shaded area with benches for easy access.
   - **Hydration**: Have water and electrolyte drinks available.

### Why This Works:
- **Inclusivity**: Games and food cater to all ages/dietary needs.
- **Memorable**: Outdoor adventures reinforce Brownie values.
- **Low-Stress**: Parent involvement shares the load, freeing you to enjoy the day.
