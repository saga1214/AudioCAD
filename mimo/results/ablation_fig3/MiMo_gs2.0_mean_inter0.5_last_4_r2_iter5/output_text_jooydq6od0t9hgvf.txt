Here are quick, elegant drink recipes for your vodka and Argentine Fernet—perfect for formal guests:

### 🥃 **1. Quick Vodka Mix (Refreshing & Customizable)**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1.5 oz vodka  
- 2 oz sparkling water or soda water  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- Ice  
- Garnish: Lime wedge or orange slice  

**How to**:  
Shake vodka, lime juice, and ice. Strain into a chilled glass (wine or rocks glass). Top with sparkling water. Garnish and serve.  
*Pro Tip*: Offer a small bowl of garnishes (citrus twists, mint) so guests can customize sweetness!

### 🥃 **2. Argentine-Style Fernet (Minty & Zesty)**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz Fernet-Branca (or Fernet-Concanaña)  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- 2 oz soda water  
- 4–5 fresh mint leaves  
- Ice  

**How to**:  
Muddle mint leaves lightly in a shaker. Add Fernet, lime juice, and ice. Shake well. Strain into a highball glass filled with ice. Top with soda water. Garnish with a mint sprig.  
*Why it works*: Light, herbaceous, and crowd-pleasing—no heavy spice!

### 🍸 **Key Considerations**:  
- **Allergy Safe**: Both recipes avoid soda entirely. Label drinks clearly if serving multiple options.  
- **Formality**: Use chilled glasses and elegant garnishes (e.g., citrus twists, fresh herbs).  
- **Variations**: For omnivore vodka fans, add a splash of cranberry juice; vegetarians can enjoy as-is.
