Here's a bartender-ready list with classic recipes and measurements for your cocktail reception. All quantities are for a single serving.

### **1. Art Deco Old Fashioned**
- **Ingredients:**  
  2 oz bourbon/rye (e.g., Buffalo Trace, Bulleit)  
  1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  4 dashes Angostura bitters  
  Orange twist  
- **Method:** Muddle sugar cube with ¾ oz water OR stir in simple syrup. Add bourbon and bitters. Stir with ice. Strain into a **rocks glass over fresh ice**. Express orange twist over drink (no garnish).  

### **2. Paper Plane**
- **Ingredients:**  
  1.5 oz gin (e.g., Hendrick’s, Bombay Sapphire)  
  0.75 oz Aperol  
  0.75 oz Amaro Nonino  
  0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Method:** Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a **coupe glass filled with ice**. Garnish with cherry OR orange twist.  

### **3. Kentucky Whiskey Sour**
- **Option A (with egg white):**  
  2 oz bourbon/rye  
  ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  ½ oz simple syrup  
  1 egg white  
- **Method:** *Dry shake* (egg white only) until frothy. Add ice, shake again. Strain into **rocks glass over ice**. Garnish with cherry/orange slice.  
- **Option B (no egg white):** Same ingredients, omit egg white. Stir with ice, strain over fresh ice.  

### **4 Aviation Cocktail**
- **Ingredients:**  
  1.5 oz gin (e.g., London Dry: Tanqueray, Beefeater)  
  ¾ oz maraschino liqueur (Luxardo)  
  ¼ oz crème de violette  
  ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Method:** Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a **cocktail glass**. Garnish with cherry.  

### **5 Third Gin Cocktail Recommendation: Gold Rush**
*(Art Deco gold theme + fall spice)*  
- **Ingredients:**  
  1.5 oz gin  
  0.75 oz honey syrup (equal honey:water)  
  0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
  0.5 oz ginger syrup (equal ginger syrup:water)  
- **Method:** Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a **coupe glass over ice**. Garnish with lemon twist OR candied ginger.
