Yes, you can definitely learn more. The best place to start is the Lockport Canal Museum. It's located right at the Lockport Locks and has exhibits, maps, and even a small boat ride through the locks. There's also a great book on the topic called "The Erie Canal and the War of 1812" that you could find at the Lockport Public Library.

If you'd prefer to learn more about the history of the Finger Lakes wineries we first mentioned, you could contact the Finger Lakes Wine Trail. They can provide maps and details on tours and tastings. Enjoy vacation!
