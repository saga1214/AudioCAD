Okay, little corgi princess needs snacks galore! Let's recap all the delicious homemade and smart store-bought ideas just for Princess:

**Homemade Homemade Homemade Homemade! (You control the ingredients!)**

1.  **Pumpkin Parfait Freezes:** Pure magic! Mash *ripe* pumpkin (like sugar pie pumpkin) or sweet potato until smooth. Spoon tiny, pea-sized mounds onto silicone molds, an ice cube tray, or a cookie cutter. Freeze solid (about 2-3 hours). Perfect cold chews and brain-teasers! *Bonus:* Add a tiny dot of plain peanut butter in the center before freezing (ensure pure peanut butter!). *Always check with xylitol!*
2.  **Oat PB Cookies (座右铭: Tiny portions!):** Mix 1 cup rolled oats, 1-2 tbsp pure peanut butter (NO xylitol!), 1/4 tsp vanilla (optional), and a splash of milk (or water) until it holds together. Roll into small balls or flatten into tiny discs on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 8-12 minutes until *very* dry and hard (they crisp up and last longer). Cool completely before breaking into tiny cracker-sized pieces. *Healthy-ish base, PB reward.*
3.  **Carrot or Chicken Spear:** Wash, peel, and *very finely* grate carrot or skinless cooked chicken breast. Thread tiny pieces onto short, safe wooden licks or lickable mats. Corgis love to gnaw on veggies and lean protein!
4.  **Simple Apple Chips:** Thinly slice apples (remove seeds!). Arrange on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake at 225°F (110°C) for 1.5 - 2.5 hours, flipping occasionally, until completely dry and brittle. Break into tiny pieces. *Hydrating and healthy.*

**Smart Store-Bought Choices (Look for simple, natural ingredients):**

1.  **Blue Buffalo:** Look for their Small Training Bites or Puplets line. Often grain-inclusive but good quality protein sources.
2.  **Wellness:** Their Grilled Chicken Bites or Sweet Potato Bites are generally wholesome choices.
3.  **Zuke's Mini Naturals:** These are incredibly popular with dogs. Tiny, round, and come in flavors like Chicken & Rice or Beef & Barley. Check for no xylitol.
4.  **Greenies for Dental:** While primarily dental chews, their smaller training shapes (like the "Mini" versions) are tasty treats too and good for teeth.
5.  **Merrick Training Bites:** Simple ingredients list, often grain-free options, good protein content.

**Key Reminders for Princess:**

*   **Xylitol is the Enemy!** This artificial sweetener is deadly to dogs. *Never* use peanut butter with xylitol, or any treats containing xylitol (many commercial baked goods do!). Read labels carefully.
*   **Portion Power:** Corgis are generally lean. Stick to *tiny* pieces for training rewards or small bites of cooked protein/veggies. Treats should be a small fraction of her daily diet.
*   **Whole Foods:** Homemade and treats like cooked chicken, carrot sticks, apple chips, and frozen bites are often healthier than heavily processed commercial treats.
*   **Praise Power:** Pair every treat with enthusiastic verbal praise ("Good girl!", "Yes!", "Wow!") to reinforce the good behavior or simply for being adorable!
*   Monitor Weight: Regular weigh-ins help ensure Princess stays the perfect little princess shape.

Enjoy spoilering your corgi princess with these treats! She's lucky to have you! 🐾💖
