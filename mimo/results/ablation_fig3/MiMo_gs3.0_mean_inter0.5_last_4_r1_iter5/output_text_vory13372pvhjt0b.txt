### 1. **The Core Innovation: Decentralized Trust**

### 2. **Unchangeable Transparency**

   - Prevents fraud or hidden manipulation.  
   - Enables auditability by anyone.  
   - Makes Bitcoin a **self-sovereign system**—users control their assets without relying on third parties.  
   - *This is what makes it revolutionary*: No other digital system offers this level of open, tamper-proof record-keeping.
