### 🚦 U-Turn Rules in BC
1. **General Rule**: U-turns are **not allowed** at:
   - Intersections with traffic signals (unless a sign permits it)
   - Near curves, hills, or tunnels
   - Where it would obstruct traffic
   - Near pedestrian crossings or driveways
2. **Visibility**: Must see **150m (165 yards)** in both directions. If you can’t, wait.
3. **Penalties**:
   - Ticket: **$160** (plus demerit points)
   - If it causes an accident: **Much higher fines + possible criminal charges**.
4. **Safe Alternatives**: Use designated U-turn lanes or intersections where permitted.

### 🍝 Spaghetti Carbonara Recipe
**Ingredients** (serves 2):
- **Pasta**: 200g spaghetti (or bucatini)
- **Eggs**: 2 large eggs + 1 extra yolk (for richness)
- **Cheese**: 100g Pecorino Romano (or Parmesan), finely grated
- **Cured Pork**: 150g guanciale (or pancetta/bacon), diced
- **Garlic**: 1 clove, minced (optional)
- **Pepper**: Freshly ground black pepper (generous amount!)
- **Parsley**: 2 tbsp chopped (optional garnish)

**Instructions**:
1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
2. In a bowl, whisk eggs, cheese, extra yolk, and pepper.
3. In a skillet, cook pork over medium heat until crispy. Add garlic (if using) in the last 30 seconds.
4. Turn off heat. Add drained pasta to the skillet with pork. Toss vigorously.
5. Off stove, pour egg mixture over pasta. Toss quickly (like scrambling eggs) to incorporate heat from residual pasta. Add pasta water 1-2 tbsp at a serve until silky.
6. Serve immediately with extra cheese and parsley. **Do not cook on heat**—it will scramble!

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **No cream**: Authentic Carbonara uses egg/cheese + pork fat.
- **Guanciale** is traditional cured pork cheek (found at Italian delis).
- **Serve immediately**—it waits for no one!
