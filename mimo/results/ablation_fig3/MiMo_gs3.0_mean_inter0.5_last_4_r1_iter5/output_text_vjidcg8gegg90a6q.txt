For winter chicks on concrete, the best solution is to use those rubber mats again, just like we discussed for the adult chickens. Cover them with a thick layer of large-flake pine shavings again. You can follow deep litter again, adding fresh shavings on top of the composting ones all winter. This provides natural heat while sand on top helps keep it clean and dry.

Avoid wood shavings because they can be dusty and dry out the chicks. Also, never use hay or straw, since rodents love that, nor should you use sand alone, because it doesn't absorb moisture well.

As a last resort, if you absolutely can't use shavings, you can use a single layer of clean, white paper towels. Just change them every day. But I really recommend sticking with the rubber mats and shavings for insulation and clean-up.
