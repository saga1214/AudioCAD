### Savory & Crunchy:
- **Vending Machine Picks:**  
  - *Chips/Crisps:* BBQ, sour cream & onion, or sea salt & vinegar (perfect for sharing!).  
  - *Pretzels:* Mini twists or sticks (lightweight and stackable!).  
  - *Beet Chips:* Savory and earthy (if your machine carries them).  
- **Grab & Go:**  
  - *Nuts:* Almonds, cashews, or peanuts (protein-packed and filling).  
  - *Trail Mix:* Unsweetened, with seeds or roasted chickpeas mixed in.  
  - *Seaweed Snacks:* Light, salty, and crispy.  

### Protein & Sustaining:
- *Rice Cakes* topped with everything bagel seasoning or avocado.  
- *Jerky* (beef, turkey, or mushroom—savory and chewy).  
- *Cheese Strips* or *mini cheddar cubes* (pair with crackers from the vending machine).  

### Sweet Exceptions (if you *occasionally* like non-chocolatey):  
- *Dark Chocolate* (70%+ cacao, bittersweet).  
- *Toffee Bits* (like Heath bars—salty-sweet but not sugary).  

### Setup Ideas for Your Table:  
1. **Tiered Display:** Use small bowls, dividers, or tiered trays to showcase snacks.  
2. **Label Creatively:** Write fun names like "Crunch Corner" or "Salty Summit."  
3. **Vending Machine Rotation:** Dedicate a shelf to snacks you grab from the machine daily.  
4. **Leftover Snack Math:** If you filled your drawer with 96 chocolate bars, now imagine fitting 288 Tootsie Rolls or 1,150 vending machine chips there!  

### Pro Tip:
