That's a great instinct. Older comics are generally much rarer, and their condition is a huge factor. The value is almost entirely in pristine examples, which are graded by CGC.

Here are ten significant comics from that era that you could potentially find for under five hundred dollars, but you'd have to find a lower-grade copy. The key is that the book itself is historically important.

First, *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen, Superman's first full appearance from 1938.

Second, *Action Comics* number one, 1938, Superman's first cover appearance.

Third, *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, which is Batman's debut in 1939.

Fourth, *Tales of the Teenage Mutant Hero Turtle*, which was the first appearance of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles in 1984.

Fifth, *Amazing Spider-Man* number three hundred, the first appearance of Venom from 1992.

Sixth, *Fantastic Four* number forty-eight, the first appearance of Silver Surfer from 1966.

Seventh, *Avengers* number one from 1963, the first official team-up of the Hulk and Captain America.

Eighth, *The Amazing Spider-Man* number fifty, which is the first appearance of the Punisher from 1976.

Ninth, *Savage She-Hulk* number one, her first appearance from 1979.

And tenth, *Thor* number three hundred fifty-five, which is the first appearance of Galactus from 1967.
