Okay, let's break this down.

First, the left-brain versus right-brain idea. It's a common concept, but it's mostly a myth. The brain works in networks, not hemispheres. The left side often handles language and logic, while the right side handles spatial and creative tasks, but both sides work together constantly. So, it’s not a strict split.

Now, for your career. You like structure, creativity, and measurable outcomes. You also don’t want to be stuck at a desk all day. Here are a few ideas: 
One, architecture or interior design. It’s creative, structured, and you can see tangible results. 
Another is graphic design or user experience design. It blends creativity with problem-solving and has measurable outcomes. 
Then there’s data visualization, which merges art with data analysis. 
Finally, consider teaching or mentoring. It’s structured, creative, and has a lasting impact.

It sounds like you’re looking for a field where you can build something solid and lasting while also being creative. These fields should fit that. Hope that helps!
