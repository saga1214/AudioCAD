Okay, looking for American sports cars under ninety K within the last five years with modern features, and leaning away from imports. V8, V10, or even V12 options... Got it.

Here are five top contenders for you:

1.  Chevrolet Corvette C8 Z06 (New): This is the ultimate power play. It's new, within your five-year preference, American, and packed with CarPlay. The V8 in this thing is insane—over 600 horsepower! It competes directly with actual supercars. Color like yellow or green will definitely scream mid-life-crisis-hero. Budget might touch the top, but CPO Corvettes can hit that sweet spot.
2.  Ford Mustang Shelby GT500 (Used/New): A newer Shelby model. Huge V8 engine, tons of power, modern features, American, not an import. It looks incredible and is a huge statement. Police attention risk for colorful paint applies just as much here. Colors like Grabber Blue or Bond Co. Orange are showstoppers.
3.  Chevrolet Corvette C8 Z51 (Used/New): A slightly more affordable Corvette than the Z06. Still has the V8, great tech, within your budget, new look, competitive feel. Neutral tones available, but a color one will grab more eyes.
4.  Dodge Challenger or Charger Hellcat (Used): American muscle, massive V8 power, tons of character. The Police Myth question? Flashes brightly here. Police *do* pull over muscle cars more often than neutral-toned ones. If mid-life crisis power is the goal, this is pure adrenaline. Newer Hellcats might be near the budget edge, but used ones are perfect.
5.  Ford Mustang Shelby GT350 (Used): A fantastic V8 supercar competitor. Higher performance than the GT500 in track focus, incredible sound, modern features. Very distinctive looks. Police Myth applies. Colorful paint would amplify the effect.

So, for maximum power and competitiveness, the Corvette Z06 is unbeatable. For the best mix of price and competition, the used Hellcat or GT350 are incredible. The Mustangs offer iconic American style and massive power. Choose the one that makes YOU young!
