Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes for your vodka mixer and Argentine-style Fernet, keeping everyone's preferences in mind:

### 🍸 Vodka Mixer (Soda-Free)
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup (or 1 tsp honey)  
- 6-8 fresh mint leaves  
- Sparkling water (soda-free, like club soda or mineral water)  
- Ice  
- Lime wheel or mint sprig for garnish  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle mint leaves with lime juice and simple syrup in a shaker.  
2. Add vodka and ice, then shake vigorously.  
3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
4. Top with sparkling water.  
5. Garnish with a lime wheel and mint sprig.  

---

### 🇦🇷 Argentine Fernet (Soda-Free)
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 6-8 fresh mint leaves  
- 2 oz club soda (soda-free)  
- Ice  
- Lime wheel or mint sprig for garnish  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle mint with lime juice and simple syrup.  
2. Add Fernet and ice, shake well.  
3. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.  
4. Top with club soda.  
5. Garnish with lime and mint.  

---

### Key Notes:  
- **No Soda:** Both use club soda/mineral water (caffeine-free) to honor the allergy.  
- **Elegant & Balanced:** Bright citrus + herbs keep it refined; simple syrup sweetens without cloying.  
- **Prep Ahead:** Mix the vodka base and Fernet base without sparkling water 1 hour ahead. Add soda just before serving.  
- **Adjust to Taste:** Add more syrup for sweetness or lime for tartness.
