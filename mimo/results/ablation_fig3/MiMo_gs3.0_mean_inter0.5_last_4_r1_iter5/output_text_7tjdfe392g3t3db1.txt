For tuning your acoustic guitar, here’s a step-by-step guide using either a **clip-on tuner** or a **tuner app**:

### 1. **Choose Your Tool**  
   - **Clip-on tuner**: Best for precision. Place it on the headstock (where the strings meet the neck).  
   - **Tuner app** (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune): Use your phone’s microphone. Calibrate it by playing an open string first.  

### 2. **Tuning Process**  
   - **Start with the 6th string (low E)**:  
     - Play it open (no fret pressed).  
     - Adjust the tuner’s dial until it shows **E4** (or the target note).  
     - If the tuner indicates "flat" (too low), tighten the string slightly.  
     - If "sharp" (too high), loosen it slightly.  
   - Repeat for all strings: **B (5th), G (4th), D (3rd), A (2nd), E (1st)**.  
   - **Fretted tuning**: For tighter tuning, press the string at the 5th fret (e.g., for B string).  

### 3. **Key Tips**  
   - **Tune up to pitch slowly** to avoid string breaks.  
   - **Retune often**: Acoustic guitars go slightly out of tune after playing.  
   - **Use the "sweet spot"**: Aim for the tuner’s indicator (e.g., a green dot) for accuracy.  
   - **Clean strings**: Wipe them with a microfiber cloth if they’re sticky or corroded.  

### 4. **Troubleshooting**  
   - **String breaks?** Check tension and avoid over-tightening.  
   - **Persistent issues?** Ensure the bridge isn’t loose or the saddle is worn.  
   - **Sound quality**: Tuning affects resonance. Test by strumming open chords.  

### 5. **Alternative: Ear Tuning**  
   If no tuner is available, use a reference pitch (e.g., a piano app) and compare notes by ear.  

Tuning takes practice! Start with open strings, and you’ll master it in no time. 🎸
