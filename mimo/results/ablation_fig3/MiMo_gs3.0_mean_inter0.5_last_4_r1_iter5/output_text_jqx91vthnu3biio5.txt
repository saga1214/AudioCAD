### Desk Orientation  
**Side-placing works best.** You get ample natural light and openness without staring at a wall **and** avoid direct glare/screen bouncing. It strikes the ideal balance.

### Monitor Placement  
Keep it perpendicular to the window light at **eye level**. This ensures visibility without needing to turn your head or squint.

### Standing Desk Verdict  
Worth it **if you commit to using it**. Mid-range chairs (like Steelcase Leap or Herman Miller Aeron) often offer 80% of ergonomic benefits at 60% of the cost. Studies show alternating positions reduces sedentary fatigue by 25-30%.

### Desktop Organization  
**Color-coding is highly effective for memory**, but **limit to 2-3 colors** to avoid visual noise. For example:  
- 🔵 Blue = Projects  
- 🟣 Red = URGENT  
- 🟠 Orange = Reference  
Test subtle palettes first—e.g., pastel backgrounds with accent colors.

### Background Sounds  
**Switching every 45-60 minutes** prevents auditory fatigue:  
- Lo-fi for focus  
- Rain/ocean for transitions  
- Ambient pads for unwinding  
Apps like *Noisli* or *Brain.fm* can automate this.

### Physical Documents  
**Tackle it on a weekend**—a fresh start avoids decision fatigue. Progress comes from:  
1. Clear workspace (environmental reset)  
2. Chunking into 30-minute sprints (task breakdown)  
3. Filing immediately after each session (momentum)
