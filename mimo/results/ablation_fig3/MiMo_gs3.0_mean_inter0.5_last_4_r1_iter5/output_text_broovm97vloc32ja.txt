Yes, absolutely. If you're an author-illustrator, that is a huge advantage. You can submit your complete manuscript and your illustrations together to publishers who publish children's books in that format. The process is generally the same, you just package it all together in your submission.

However, a very important thing to remember is that the publisher will hire their own separate illustrator for the final version of the book. You would only get to illustrate the book if you signed a contract that specifically states you are the illustrator. So, even with your own art, the publisher will likely commission a new artist to create the final illustrations.
