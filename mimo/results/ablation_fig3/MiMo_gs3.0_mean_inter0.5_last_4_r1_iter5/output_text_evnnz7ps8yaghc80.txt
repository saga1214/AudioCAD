It sounds like you're feeling stressed specifically when beanbags slip or you drop throws. A structured schedule can definitely help with that panic you mentioned earlier.

Here’s a weekly schedule you could try, focusing on about 15 to 30 minutes of practice each day. On Monday, start with a five-minute two-ball drill in one hand, then spend about ten minutes transitioning from two to three with beanbags, and finish with five minutes of scarf practice. On Tuesday, just focus on scarf practice to build that rhythm. Wednesday, you can take a rest day or do some gentle wrist stretches. On Thursday, go back to the first three-throw sequence with three balls. Friday could be another scarf day to reinforce the rhythm. On Saturday, you could mix it up and do five minutes of one-hand two-ball practice, ten minutes of tennis ball juggling for a different feel, and five minutes of scarf practice. Finally, on Sunday, take a full rest day to let your hands recover.

Remember, the key is to be patient and stress less about perfection. Celebrate every small win! Teaching your son will be worth all this effort.
