On a scale of 20 for in-game power, here's a re-ranking of the Pokémon we discussed, accounting for evolution and game mechanics across most titles:

- **Bulbasaur**: 12–15  
  Useful early game, but often falls off in later stages due to weak defensive stats. Fire and Flying weaknesses hurt.

- **Charizard**: 18–20  
  Offense, versatility, and type coverage (Fire/Flying) make it a top-tier pick in most games.IVs matter, but it’s consistently strong.

- **Squirtle**: 16–18  
  Balanced throughout the game. Water/Turtle typing resists many types, and it evolves into Wartortle/Blastoise, which are powerhouse defenders/offenders.

- **Gyarados**: 19–20  
  Magikarp’s evolution is one of the most powerful. Dragon/Fighting typing with high Attack and Speed makes it devastating.

- **Staryu**: 11–14  
  Pre-evolution: Fragile and slow. Evolves to Starmie (17–19), but Staryu itself is situational.

- **Caterpie**: 3–5  
  Weak stats and slow evolution (requires 25 levels). Evolves to Butterfree, which is agile but lacks power.

- **Magikarp**: 1–2  
  Lowest base stats of any Pokémon. Only useful for its "Gigantic Waterfall" gimmick in rare cases. Evolves to Gyarados.

### Key Factors:
- **Water-types** often rank higher due to resistances (Water, Grass, Fighting) and strong moves like Hydro Pump.
- **Charizard and Gyarados** dominate due to high offensive stats and coverage.
- **Early-game starters** cap out lower; Squirtle’s balance gives it an edge over Bulbasaur/Swadlow.
- **Pre-evolution stats** heavily impact ratings (e.g., Magikarp’s 1, Staryu’s 11–14).

This scale reflects raw gameplay impact, not popularity or nostalgia. Let me know if you’d like to add more Pokémon!
