Okay, you've got the essentials, but there are a few more things that will make this go a lot smoother and save you headaches. I'd strongly recommend renting a cordless drill and some wall anchors. Habitat stores almost always lend or sell these, and you'll need them because Habitat restoration store cabinets often don't have pre-drilled holes for studs.

Shims are also critical. You need ones specifically for cabinets, which are thicker and more flexible than regular ones. This will let you get every single cabinet perfectly level. And make sure you have at least two people with you when you're hanging the uppers.

Oh, and safety! A dust mask is a good idea since you'll be sanding and cutting. And definitely have a first-aid kit handy just in case.

You're all set once you grab those extra tools. Have fun with the project!
