You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is great, so I won't repeat brands. gluten-free camping snacks should also ideally be non-perishable and lightweight.

Rice cakes with almond butter are always a crowd-pleaser.

Popcorn made with coconut oil is another gluten-free, airy snack that works well.

Trail mix is also a good choice, where you can combine gluten-free nuts like almonds and cashews with seeds like pumpkin or sunflower seeds.
