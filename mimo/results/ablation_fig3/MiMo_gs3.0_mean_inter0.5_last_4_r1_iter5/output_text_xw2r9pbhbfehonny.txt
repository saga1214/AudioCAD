Sure, here’s a basic checklist for filming your own puppet show for your channel:

First, ask the facility if filming is allowed. If yes, get their permission in writing.

Next, set up your phone or camera on a tripod to keep it steady. Position it at an angle where both puppets and your performance are visible.

Before starting, make sure the puppets are ready—socks, props, everything. Test the camera settings to make sure the lighting and audio are clear.

During the show, keep your performance natural and engaging. Since it’s for kids, keep it short and interactive.

After filming, review the footage to check the quality. Edit any mistakes if needed, and add captions if possible.

Finally, upload it to your channel with a fun title and description, like “Melvin the Magnificent Fails Again!” or something similar.

That’s the basic idea. Good luck!
