Given your situation, the choice is actually quite clear.

The Manonadi competition is a perfect fit on every level. It asks for two contrasting arias, which you'll have—the Mozart fit glove you love, and the Puccini-turned-Latin American one you're excited about. You fit the age and level, and the travel is a bonus. It's the logical and achievable choice.

The Viva competition, on the other hand, has a major hurdle: the requirement for 'strong piano skills.' You mentioned this is a weakness for you. While the Latin American aria is a great fit culturally for the ethnomusicology part of the competition, the piano requirement is a deal-breaker. Trying to learn two new arias while also developing strong piano skills for a competition is likely too much.

Therefore, I would strongly recommend you enter the Manonadi competition. It aligns with your current voice assignments, travel plans, and vocal capabilities. You can always aim for the Viva competition in the future once you've had more time to develop your piano skills.
