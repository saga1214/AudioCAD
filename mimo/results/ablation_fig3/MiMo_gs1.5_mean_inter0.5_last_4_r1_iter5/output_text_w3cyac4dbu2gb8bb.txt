You're absolutely right to want that strong latte immediately! Here's how to solve both the coffee craving and the weather dilemma:

**For the Coffee:**
1.  **Use the Machine Method:** Brew coffee fresh from your machine for the richer flavor. Dissolve the instant coffee in just a *tiny* amount of hot water first to make a concentrated "base shot."
2.  **Warm the Milk Properly:** Heat the Cornish milk in the microwave for **45-60 seconds** (stirring halfway). This is faster than boiling and avoids curdling. It should be steaming hot, not boiling.
3.  **Combine Smoothly:** Pour the hot coffee base into a mug. Add the warm milk. Stir gently. The concentrated base will mix perfectly into the warm milk without clumping.

**For the Weather:**
1.  **Rain Jacket is Best:** Definitely wear your rain jacket. It's wind-resistant, keeps you dry, and is hands-free (so you can carry your latte safely). An umbrella *will* be useless in the wind – it'll flip inside out or blow away.
2.  **Timing is Key:** If the rain is heavy *right now*, wait 10-15 minutes. Check the forecast app. Often, heavy downpours come in waves. If it eases up slightly, that's your window.
3.  **Umbrella Backup:** If you *must* go *right now*, take the rain jacket *and* a compact, wind-resistant umbrella as backup. Tuck it securely in your bag.

**Summary Plan:**
*   Brew strong coffee fresh using your machine.
*   Heat Cornish milk for 45-60 seconds in the microwave.
*   Dissolve instant coffee in a little hot water first.
*   Pour coffee base into mug, add warm milk, stir.
*   Put on your rain jacket (and maybe a hat) if it's pouring.
*   Wait 10-15 minutes if possible, or go quickly with your rain jacket.

Enjoy that luxurious, strong Cornish latte! It's worth the tiny wait.
