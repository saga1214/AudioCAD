The term 'forever chemicals' really just means that these chemicals are very persistent in the environment and in the body. It doesn't mean they're permanent, though. Your body does have a way to process them, and you can definitely reduce your exposure.

To help your body get rid of them, it's good to focus on a few things. First, hydration is really important for your kidneys to filter things out. Second, eating foods that are rich in fiber, like fruits and vegetables, can help. And third, getting enough sleep helps your body's natural detox processes.

Okay, for a clear list of things to avoid, here are the top five:

First, non-stick cookware. You can switch to stainless steel or cast iron.
Second, grease-resistant food packaging, like fast-food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags.
Third, items labeled as stain or water-resistant, such as carpets, furniture, and outdoor clothing.
Fourth, certain cosmetics and dental flosses that contain these chemicals.
And fifth, be mindful of your tap water, as they can get into the supply. Using a certified water filter is a great way to reduce exposure.
