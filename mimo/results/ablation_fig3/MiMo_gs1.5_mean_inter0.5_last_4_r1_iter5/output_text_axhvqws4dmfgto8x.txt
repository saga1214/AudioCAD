Okay, let's break down how to boil noodles safely and perfectly for stir-fry. The key is to avoid wooden utensils and to drain the noodles properly.

First, use a metal spoon or a silicone one. No wood. Boil your water, add salt to taste, and then add the noodles. Stir them gently as you cook so they don't stick together.

Once they're done, drain them immediately in a colander. But here's the most important step: don't rinse them. Leaving a little starch on the noodles helps them stick together in the stir-fry. If you rinse them, they'll be slippery and won't hold up well. Just drain them well and set them aside. You can add a little oil to the colander if you're worried about sticking.

Then, when you're ready to cook the stir-fry, add the noodles back in at the end and toss them with the sauce. The heat will cook them through. You can also add a splash of water or broth to the stir-fry to help steam the noodles a bit.

So, the main things to remember are: use metal or silicone, don't rinse, and add the noodles back in at the end. You've got this!
