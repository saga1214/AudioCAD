Absolutely. Lockport is a city that's deeply proud of its canal heritage, and there are several great ways to learn more.

First, you could visit the Lockport Museum and the Canal Center. It's a great place to get a comprehensive overview of the canal's history. You can also see the famous "Flight of Five" locks up close, which are a major attraction.

If you want to get hands-on, there's the Lockport Museum's "Canal Days" program, where you can try operating the locks yourself. It's a really unique experience. There's also the Erie Canal Museum in Schenectady, which is a bit further away but has excellent exhibits on the canal's engineering and its impact on the region. 

For a scenic, more relaxed way to experience the canal, you could take a boat tour on the Lockport Waterfront. It's a great way to appreciate the history and the beautiful riverfront parks. You might even be able to spot the "Lockport Locks" sign, which is a fun little detail for any history enthusiast.
