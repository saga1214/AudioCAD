That sounds like a fantastic themed night! Here are 5 ocean-inspired mains and 5 sweet desserts that accommodate your fiancée's dietary needs (no shellfish, diabetes-friendly), while incorporating shrimp and mango, and keeping spicy food in mind:

**Ocean-Themed Mains:**

1.  **Grilled Shrimp Skewers with Mango-Habanero Salsa:** Toss large shrimp (peeled & deveined) with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and a pinch of cayenne. Thread onto skewers. Serve with a vibrant salsa of diced mango, diced red onion, jalapeño (or habanero for extra heat), cilantro, lime juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper. *Perfect for shrimp lovers!*
2.  **Coconut Curry with Shrimp & Tofu (for shellfish-free option):** Make a fragrant red or yellow coconut curry base using curry paste, coconut milk, ginger, garlic, and lemongrass. Sauté shrimp (or extra-firm tofu cubes if shellfish-free) and a mix of veggies (like bell peppers, snap peas, carrots). Serve over jasmine rice. *Rich, spicy, and diabetic-friendly.*
3.  **Baked Salmon with Mango-Jalapeño Glaze & Quinoa:** Coat salmon fillets in a glaze made from Greek yogurt (low-sugar), honey, lime juice, minced jalapeño, minced mango, and a touch of smoked paprika. Bake until flaky. Serve over quinoa tossed with roasted broccoli and cherry tomatoes. *Lean protein, colorful veggies.*
4.  **Shrimp Tacos with Mango Slaw & Spicy Avocado Crema:** Cook shrimp in a spicy tomato-corn salsa or a chipotle-tahini sauce. Serve in warm corn tortillas topped with a fresh mango slaw (shredded cabbage, carrots, mango, lime juice, cilantro). Drizzle with a creamy avocado sauce mixed with lime, cumin, and a pinch of chili powder. *Light, flavorful, and customizable spice level.*
5.  **Shrimp & Vegetable Stir-Fry with Cauliflower Rice:** Stir-fry shrimp quickly in a mix of soy sauce (low-sodium if preferred), ginger, garlic, and a touch of honey or maple syrup. Add a mix of colorful veggies (broccoli, snap peas, bell peppers, mushrooms). Serve over a bed of sautéed cauliflower rice. *Quick, light, and packed with flavor.*

**Ocean-Themed Sweet Desserts (Diabetic-Friendly):**

1.  **Mango & Coconut Chia Pudding Parfaits:** Layer chia pudding (made with unsweetened almond milk, chia seeds, a tiny bit of vanilla, and stevia/erythritol to taste) with fresh mango chunks and shredded coconut. Top with a sprinkle of toasted coconut. *High fiber, low sugar, refreshing.*
2.  **Grilled Mango with Honey-Lime Drizzle & Tajín:** Grill fresh mango slices until slightly caramelized. Serve with a drizzle of honey mixed with fresh lime juice and a sprinkle of Tajín seasoning (chili-lime salt). *Simple, elegant, and maximizes mango flavor.*
3.  **Vanilla Panna Cotta with Berry Compote:** Make a light, silky vanilla panna cotta (using low-sugar vanilla extract and sweetener of choice like stevia or monk fruit). Serve topped with a compote made from fresh berries (strawberries, raspberries, blueberries) simmered with just a touch of honey or sweetener and lemon juice. *Creamy, light, and berry-focused.*
4.  **Dark Chocolate Mousse with Raspberry Swirl:** Whip dark chocolate mousse (use high-cocoa chocolate and a low-sugar sweetener) until light and fluffy. Gently fold in a swirl of raspberry purée (simmered raspberries with a little water and sweetener). Serve in glasses. *Decadent yet mindful.*
5.  **Banana "Ocean" Cake:** Mash ripe bananas as the base. Add an egg, a touch of coconut oil, vanilla, cinnamon, and a low-sugar sweetener. Fold in chocolate chips (optional) and blueberries. Bake. Serve with a dollop of coconut whipped cream and a sprinkle of shredded coconut. *Uses natural sweetness from bananas.*

**Key Considerations for Your Theme Night:**

*   **Garnish:** Use blue/teal paper napkins, small seashells, starfish, or even blue and white striped tablecloth/canopy.
*   **Music:** Soft ocean waves or tropical-inspired music.
*   **Lighting:** Dim lighting with candles or blue/green LED lights.
*   **Check Labels:** Especially on pre-made sauces (curry paste, pesto, dressings) and sweeteners to ensure they align with diabetes management.
