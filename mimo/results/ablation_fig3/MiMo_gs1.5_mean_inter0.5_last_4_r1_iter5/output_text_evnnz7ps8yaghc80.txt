**Weekly Juggling Practice Schedule:**

*   **Monday: Bean Bags (Focus: Transition Drill)**
    *   **10 mins:** Warm-up: Juggle 2 bean bags (2s). Practice your chosen transition drill (either method we discussed) for 5-10 minutes. *Just focus on the throw sequence, not catching.*
    *   **5 mins:** "Catch-Only" Practice: Toss bean bags one at a time from each hand, focusing solely on a clean, soft catch with each hand. *No rhythm, just feel the weight and landing.*
    *   **5 mins:** Cool-down: Juggle 2s casually.

*   **Tuesday: Scarves (Focus: Rhythm & Pattern)**
    *   **10 mins:** Warm-up: Juggle 2 bean bags (2s) with good rhythm.
    *   **15 mins:** Scarf Practice: Use 1-2 scarves. Focus on letting them fall slowly and catching them by feel. Practice basic patterns like "cross under" or "over under." *Emphasize smooth catches and rhythm.*
    *   **5 mins:** Cool-down: Juggle 2s casually.

*   **Wednesday: Bean Bags (Focus: Catch-Only & Flow)**
    *   **10 mins:** Warm-up: Juggle 2s.
    *   **10 mins:** Catch-Only Practice: Toss bean bags one at a time, focusing intensely on a clean catch in each hand. *Build muscle memory and confidence.*
    *   **5 mins:** Cool-down: Juggle 2s.

*   **Thursday: Tennis Balls (Focus: Tactile Feel & Timing)**
    *   **10 mins:** Warm-up: Juggle 2 bean bags (2s).
    *   **15 mins:** Tennis Ball Practice: Juggle 3 tennis balls. Focus on the *feel* – they are harder and rounder. Practice your first few throws and catches. *Don't worry about rhythm yet, just get comfortable.*
    *   **5 mins:** Cool-down: Juggle 2s.

*   **Friday: Scarves (Focus: Pattern Refinement)**
    *   **10 mins:** Warm-up: Juggle 2s.
    *   **15 mins:** Scarf Practice: Work on a specific pattern you found tricky on Tuesday. Practice it slowly and deliberately.
    *   **5 mins:** Cool-down: Juggle 2s.

*   **Saturday: Catch-Up / Focus Day (Choose One):**
    *   **Option A:** Revisit your weakest point (e.g., the 2-to-3 transition drill, a tricky scarf pattern).
    *   **Option B:** Do a longer session (20-30 mins) mixing bean bags, scarves, and tennis balls.
    *   **Option C:** Rest! Listen to your body.

*   **Sunday: Rest or Light Fun:**
    *   **Rest:** Essential for recovery and mental reset.
    *   **OR:** Do a *very* short (5-10 min), completely stress-free juggle session with bean bags just for fun. No pressure.

**Key Principles for Success:**

1.  **Consistency Over Duration:** 10-15 focused minutes daily is FAR better than 1 hour once a week. Stick to the schedule!
2.  **Warm-up & Cool-down:** Always start with your dominant hand 2-ball juggling to get your timing right. End with casual juggling.
3.  **Focus on One Thing:** Each session has a clear mini-goal (e.g., "Today I focus on the first throw of the transition").
4.  **Embrace "Catch-Only" Days:** These are crucial for building confidence and muscle memory without the pressure of drops. They feel slow, but they work!
5.  **Listen to Your Body & Mind:** If you're feeling panicky or frustrated, STOP. Take a break. Practice relaxation (deep breaths) if needed. Come back tomorrow refreshed.
6.  **Celebrate Micro-Wins:** Did you get one extra throw? Catch one ball cleanly? That's progress! Acknowledge it.
7.  **Be Patient & Kind to Yourself:** Learning juggling 3 takes time. The stress you identified is real – managing it is part of the practice. If a session feels bad, just note it and move on to the next one.
8.  **Have Fun!** Remember, you're doing this for your son and for yourself. Enjoy the feeling of the objects in your hands and the rhythm.

This schedule gives you variety, builds specific skills, and prioritizes consistency. Stick with it for a few weeks, and you *will* see improvement in your ability to juggle 3 bean bags. You've got this! Good luck!
