### 3. **Dyslexia Types Explained**  
   - **Phonological Dyslexia**:  
     Difficulty decoding sounds in words (e.g., misreading "cat" as "kot").  
     *Example*: Struggling with homophones like "sea" vs. "see."  
   - **Surface Dyslexia**:  
     Reliance on phonetic decoding fails with irregular words (e.g., "yacht").  
     *Example*: Reading "w-a-t-e-r" as "wa-tuh."  
   - **Visual Dyslexia**:  
     Letter reversals (b/d confusion) or inconsistent word recognition.  
     *Example*: Seeing "dog" as "god."  

### 4. **Reasoning Styles: Deductive vs. Inductive**  
   - **Deductive (Top-down)**:  
     *Process*: General rule → Specific case.  
     *Example*: "All mammals breathe air. Whales are mammals. Thus, whales breathe air."  
   - **Inductive (Bottom-up)**:  
     *Process*: Specific observations → General rule.  
     *Example*: "I’ve seen 10 swans; they’re all white. Thus, all swans are white."  
   - **Abductive (Best Explanation)**:  
     *Process*: Incomplete data → Most likely inference.  
     *Example*: A detective concludes a suspect’s fingerprints match a stolen item.
