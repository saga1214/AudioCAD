Absolutely! Let's build on those umami-rich, adventurous flavors with more Japanese-inspired snacks that'll transport your guests. Here’s a curated list blending simple and unique options:

### **Simple & Easy Bases**
1. **Matcha Everything**:  
   - **Matcha Popcorn**: Toss popcorn with matcha powder + butter.  
   - **Matcha Mochi**: Soft, chewy mochi (buy pre-made or simple red bean paste mochi).  
   - **Matcha Chocodiles**: Freeze-dried matcha chocolate-covered pretzels.  

2. **Savory Snack Stations**:  
   - **Yakitori Skewers**: Marinated chicken (pre-cooked, just reheat).  
   - **Onigiri Station**: Rice balls with fillings like salmon, pickled plum, or peanut butter.  
   - **Edamame**: Lightly salted, steamed soybeans (with wasabi mayo dip).  

### **Unique & Adventurous Choices**  
3. **Umami Bombs**:  
   - **Kimchi Seaweed Snacks**: Crispy, spicy, and packed with tangy umami.  
   - **Dashi Popcorn**: Popcorn tossed with kombu dashi powder + nori flakes.  
   - **Miso-Marinated Edamame**: Toss steamed edamame in a light miso-ginger glaze.  

4. **Sweet & Savory Fusion**:  
   - **Tamarind-Glazed Dried Mango**: Sweet, sour, and sticky.  
   - **Spicy Wasabi Peanuts**: Crunchy, pungent, and addictive.  
   - **Black Sesame Nuts**: Toasted with a hint of salt and black sesame oil.  

5. **Interactive Stations**:  
   - **Miso-Glazed Eggplant Bites**: Pre-roasted eggplant chunks brushed with miso-ginger glaze.  
   - **Dashi-Spiced Tuna Crostini**: Cucumber slices topped with seasoned tuna and nori flakes.  
   - **Sake-Infused Popcorn**: Popcorn tossed with a light dashi-sake butter.  

### **Presentation Tips**  
- **Bento-Style Baskets**: Serve snacks in small bamboo containers or bento boxes.  
- **Garnish with Nori**: Edible seaweed strips add color and flavor.  
- **Dashi Broth Dip**: Offer a warm cup of dashi broth for mochi or edamame.  

This menu balances the familiar (popcorn, mochi) with the exotic (dashi popcorn, kimchi seaweed), while keeping prep simple. Your foodie friends will love the depth of flavor and cultural immersion!
