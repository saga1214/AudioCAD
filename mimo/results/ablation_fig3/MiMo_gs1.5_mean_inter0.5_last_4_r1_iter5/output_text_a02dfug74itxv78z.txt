Alright, based on your mid-life crisis needs for a powerful American sports car under ninety thousand dollars, with modern features and a preference against imports, here are five top recommendations that are competitive with supercars.

First, there's the Chevrolet Corvette C8 Z06. This is the American icon you're looking for. It has a V8 engine, makes 670 horsepower, and a top speed over 200 miles per hour. The key thing here is that it's American-made and has a modern tech package with CarPlay. You can find a C8 Corvette for under ninety thousand dollars.

Second is the Ford Mustang Shelby GT500. This is a high-performance V8 that makes 660 horsepower and has a top speed of around 180 miles per hour. It's a great choice if you want a bit more personality than a Corvette, and it also has modern features like CarPlay.

Third, you could look at a used Lamborghini Huracán. This is a true supercar competitor. It has a V10 engine with 610 horsepower. You can find one for under ninety thousand dollars, but you'll likely need to wait for it to come off a lease. The downside is it's an import and might be a bit higher maintenance, but the performance is insane.

Fourth is the Porsche 911 Turbo S. This is the supercar with the highest reliability. It has a flat-six engine that makes 640 horsepower, with a top speed of 195 miles per hour. The big plus is its legendary reliability and daily drivability, plus it has CarPlay. The main drawback is that it's a bit over your budget.

Finally, there's the Audi R8 V10 Plus. This is another true supercar competitor with a V10 engine. It makes 602 horsepower and has a top speed of 190 miles per hour. It's known for being a very user-friendly supercar and has CarPlay. The only potential issue is that it's an import and might have higher maintenance costs.

So, to summarize: if you want the best American option, go for the Corvette Z06 or Mustang GT500. If you want a true supercar, look at the used Huracán or R8. And if reliability is your top priority, the Porsche 911 Turbo S is the clear winner.
