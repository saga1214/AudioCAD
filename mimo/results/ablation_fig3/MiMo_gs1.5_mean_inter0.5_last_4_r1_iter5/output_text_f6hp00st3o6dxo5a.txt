That's a really interesting point about DNA and the egg, but the chicken still came first.

Here's the thing: DNA mutations happen *after* the egg is laid, not before. So the egg could only contain the DNA of the creature that laid it, not the DNA of a creature that didn't exist yet. The first chicken's DNA had to come from the creature that laid the egg, which wasn't a chicken.

So, the egg came first, and the chicken hatched from it.
