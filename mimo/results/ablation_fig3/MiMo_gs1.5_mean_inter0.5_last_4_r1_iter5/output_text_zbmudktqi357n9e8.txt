Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn inspiration! If you're aiming for those vibrant, warm colors, I'd suggest going in the late summer or early fall, specifically from September to November. That's when you'll find the peak of the seasonal changes, with the most intense colors in the foliage. 

Just a couple of tips: pack light layers for the heat, but don't forget a small umbrella or an artist's easel if you plan on painting outdoors. And definitely bring your watercolors and markers—Mexico’s landscapes will be a perfect canvas for your autumn scenes. Safe travels, and happy painting!
