That sounds like an amazing getaway! Since your partner's seasickness is a real constraint, you'll want to focus on romantic activities that avoid significant motion but still capture the essence of the ocean and fresh seafood. Here are some creative ideas:

1.  **The Calm Day Catamaran Cruise:** Instead of a crowded ferry, charter a private or small-group catamaran. They're much more stable than smaller boats due to their wide beam and shallow draft. You could:
    *   **Relax & Snorkel:** Float peacefully in calm, clear waters (check local conditions first).
    *   **Sunset Picnic:** Enjoy a gourmet picnic on deck as the sun dips below the horizon.
    *   **Island Hop:** Visit a nearby, stable island with a charming harbor or beach for a short walk.

2.  **Private Boat Charter (For a Specific Purpose):** Rent a small motorboat or sailboat *for a single, calm activity*:
    *   **Dolphin Watching Excursion:** Many operators offer short, guided trips specifically for spotting dolphins. They often use stable vessels and return promptly. Focus on the shared wonder, not the boat itself.
    *   **Private Seafood Market Visit:** Charter a boat to a small, local fishing village or a specific seafood market dock. Explore the market together, pick out your catch, and return to shore.

3.  **Land-Based Coastal Charm (Romantic & Seafood Focused):**
    *   **Sunset Stroll & Seafood Feast:** Find a perfect spot on a calm beach or pier for a sunset walk. Then, head to a highly-rated local seafood restaurant known for its fresh catches (maybe even one where you can see the boats).
    *   **Coastal Market & Cooking Class:** Visit a bustling morning or afternoon market together, selecting the freshest seafood and produce. Then, take a hands-on cooking class focusing on seafood dishes.
    *   **Private Dining on the Pier:** Many harbors offer private dining packages on the pier. Enjoy a gourmet seafood meal surrounded by the ocean view, completely motionless.

4.  **The "Adventure" with a Safety Net:**
    *   **Kayak & Picnic (Shallow Waters):** Rent tandem kayaks for a short paddle in very calm, protected waters (like a bay or lagoon). Focus on paddling together to a beautiful spot, then enjoy a packed picnic lunch. Pack plenty of water and snacks.
    *   **Beachcombing & Shell Collecting:** Explore a beautiful, accessible beach together. Collect shells, look for interesting driftwood, and simply enjoy the sand and sea air.

**Key Considerations for Success:**

*   **Prioritize Calm:** Choose times with minimal wind (calm mornings are often best). Avoid choppy seas entirely.
*   **Seating is Key:** Ensure any boat trip has comfortable, stable seating (like a deck chair or bench) with good support. Avoid standing.
*   **Focus on Shared Experience:** Frame it as an adventure *together* – the focus is on the shared moment, the stunning views, and the delicious food, not the motion itself.
*   **Manage Expectations:** Be honest with your partner. Frame it as "Let's try this calm activity *with* me" rather than "Let's go on a ferry." Offer alternatives if the chosen activity doesn't feel right.
*   **Seafood Focus:** Lean heavily into the shared love of seafood! A great seafood restaurant is almost always a romantic winner and avoids motion entirely.

The most romantic moments often come from shared focus and connection, not just the location. By focusing on calm settings, delicious food, and beautiful ocean views (without the risk of seasickness), you can create a truly special and memorable getaway! Have a wonderful trip!
