That's a fantastic idea! Travel can be incredibly inspiring for writing and a great way to step outside your comfort zone. Here are some destinations, ideas, and tips tailored to your passions, with a focus on safety and accessibility:

### 🌍 Top Travel Ideas for Inspiration & Connection

1. **Portugal (Lisbon/Algarve)**  
   - **Why**: Affordable, walkable, steeped in history, and rich in literature (think *The Alchemist* by Paulo Coelho!).  
   - **Activities**:  
     - Explore Lisbon’s historic neighborhoods (Belém, Alfama) and hidden bookshops.  
     - Hike coastal cliffs in the Algarve (great for fantasy/sci-fi landscapes).  
     - Practice your Italian? Focus on Portuguese—easier for Spanish later!  
   - **Accommodation**: Cozy hostels (like **Hostal Torel** in Lisbon) or pet-friendly guesthouses (check Airbnb filters).  
   - **Travel**: Fly into Lisbon (Porto might be cheaper). Trains connect major cities easily.  

2. **Japan (Tokyo/Osaka + Rural Journeys)**  
   - **Why**: A blend of futuristic tech and ancient traditions—perfect sci-fi/fantasy inspiration. Pet-friendly ryokans exist!  
   - **Activities**:  
     - Visit Shibuya Crossing or Kyoto’s bamboo forests.  
     - Learn Japanese at a local *juku* (language school).  
     - Take day trips to rural areas like Kanazawa for storytelling vibes.  
   - **Accommodation**: Booked.com has pet-friendly ryokans.  
   - **Travel**: Fly into Tokyo/Narita (budget airlines like Peach). Trains are safe and efficient.  

3. **Morocco (Marrakech/Tangier)**  
   - **Why**: Feels like stepping into a fantasy novel—vibrant markets, ancient architecture, and desert adventures.  
   - **Activities**:  
     - Wander Marrakech’s medina (try the "YSL" alley for photos!).  
     - Join a group tour for the Atlas Mountains (social + safe).  
     - Practice Arabic—connect with locals at tea shops.  
   - **Accommodation**: Riads (traditional inns) like **Riad Yasmine**.  
   - **Travel**: Fly into Casablanca/Marrakech. Use trains (SNCM) or buses (CTM).  

### ✈️ How to Make Travel Work for You

- **Budget Tips**:  
  - Use **Skyscanner** or **Google Flights** for flight deals (book 2-3 months ahead).  
  - Stay in hostels (Hostelworld) or Airbnb private rooms.  
  - Eat street food or cook in accommodations to save.  
- **Safety First**:  
  - Stick to tourist areas, use ride-shares (Uber/Grab), and share your itinerary with someone.  
  - Book group tours for adventure (e.g., hiking, city walks) to meet people safely.  
  - Avoid isolated places at night—stick to well-lit areas.  
- **Making Friends**:  
  - **Hostels**: Join the common area, attend free events (e.g., pub crawls).  
  - **Meetups**: Use **Meetup.com** for local travel groups.  
  - **Language Exchanges**: Apps like **Tandem** or **HelloTalk** connect you with locals.  

### ✍️ Turning Travel into Writing Fuel
- **Journal Prompts**:  
  - *"What’s the most magical/strange thing I saw today?"*  
  - *"How would a sci-fi character react to this scene?"*  
- **Backstory for Your Novel**:  
  - Use travel to flesh out characters (e.g., a language teacher from Morocco, a tech-savvy guide in Tokyo).  
  - Collect "worldbuilding" snippets: local folklore, architecture, or food traditions.  

### 🛡️ If You’re Still Nervous About Socializing
- **Start Small**: Join a **virtual book club** (via Discord or Meetup) before committing to in-person meetups.  
- **Themed Tours**: Look for "literary walking tours" (e.g., Prague’s Kafka tours) to meet like-minded people.  
- **Pet as a Bridge**: If you adopt a cat, pet-friendly travel spots will attract fellow pet lovers!  

Travel is a powerful tool to combat anxiety by immersing you in new, exciting environments—where creativity thrives. Remember: **You don’t need to "find" friends immediately. Just show up, observe, and let connections grow naturally.** Your novel will feed off these experiences! 🌟
