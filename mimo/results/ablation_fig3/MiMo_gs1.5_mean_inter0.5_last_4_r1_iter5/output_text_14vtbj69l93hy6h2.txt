That's a great question! Maltese often prefer sweeter or more aromatic treats, so cheese might not be her favorite. Here are some alternatives that tend to work well:

**High-Value Treat Recommendations:**
1. **Small Pieces of Apple:** Maltese love the natural sweetness of apple slices (no seeds!).  
2. **Peanut Butter (Xylitol-Free):** A tiny smear on your finger works wonders.  
3. **Hot Dog Bits:** If chicken is *only* avoided for allergies (not the dog itself), tiny bits of turkey or hot dog are popular.  
4. **Freeze-Dried Liver Bits:** These are concentrated, crunchy, and Maltese adore them. Look for plain liver, not bacon or other flavors.  
5. **Pumpkin (Pure):** A few drops of plain pumpkin puree can be a tasty reward.  

**For Liver Treats:**  
- **Freeze-Dried Liver:** The best option—rehydrates slightly when chewed.  
- **Dried Liver Chews:** Look for "bite-sized" or "small dog" options.  
- **Avoid:** Any liver treats with garlic, onion, or excessive salt.  

**Training Tips:**  
- **Start Small:** Use tiny pieces (1/4 to 1/2 of a treat) to avoid overfeeding.  
- **Pair with Praise:** Always say "Good girl!" or "Yes!" right when she performs the trick.  
- **Keep Sessions Short:** 5-10 minutes max—puppies have short attention spans!  
- **Grooming as a Reward:** After a successful trick, gently stroke her fur or give a tiny grooming brush as a "soft" reward. This reinforces grooming as a positive bonding activity.  

If Maisy isn’t motivated by any treat, try using a treat as a lure (e.g., moving your hand in a circle to teach "turn"). Consistency and patience are key—she’ll catch on quickly! 🐾
