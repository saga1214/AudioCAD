That's a thoughtful question! Here's how to handle frozen hamburgers for your diverse group:

1.  **For the Vegan Friend:** **Frozen hamburgers are NOT okay unless they are specifically vegan.** Most standard frozen beef/pork burgers are not vegan (contain meat, animal fats, binders like egg). You **must** bring plant-based burgers:
    *   **Brands:** Beyond Meat, Impossible, Gardein, Field Roast, Tofurky, or other veggie/bean-based patties.
    *   **Check Labels:** Always double-check the packaging for "Vegan" certification.

2.  **For Everyone Else:** Standard frozen beef/pork burgers are fine.

3.  **Cooler is Essential:** **Absolutely bring a cooler with ice packs.** Frozen burgers thaw quickly outside and can become unsafe. Ice packs are crucial for keeping them cold until you're ready to cook them.

**Recommendations:**

*   **Bring Both:** To be safe, bring *both* vegan and non-vegan frozen patties. This ensures everyone has a great option.
*   **Prep Ahead:** Unpack and pre-cook the burgers (or at least thaw them) before the picnic. Cooking directly from frozen takes much longer and is less reliable.
*   **Ice Packs:** Pack plenty of ice packs in the cooler. Keep the cooler closed as much as possible.
*   **Alternative:** If you're unsure about vegan options or want simpler prep, consider bringing **pre-made veggie/bean burgers** (like from a deli or frozen brand) instead of raw patties. These are often easier to handle and cook.
*   **Make it a Game:** Turn burger assembly into a fun group activity!

**In short:** Yes, frozen burgers are a great park picnic idea, but **only if you have vegan plant-based patties** and a **well-chilled cooler with ice packs.** Don't assume standard beef burgers are vegan-friendly.
