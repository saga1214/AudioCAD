Alright, let's talk about bedding for your winter chicks. The most common and effective choice is wood shavings. Make sure they are kiln-dried to be safe. You'll want a thick layer, at least three to four inches deep, so they can burrow in it for warmth. You can also mix in a little bit of wood pellet bedding, just be sure to remove any pellets that might get chewed on. This will help control moisture and add some ammonia-neutralizing power.

Another good option, especially if you're worried about dust, is a mix of pine pellets and organic compost. The compost will act as a natural heat source and deodorizer. Just be sure to add it in layers, like a blanket, to prevent it from getting too wet.

And just a reminder, absolutely avoid straw or hay, as they can attract rodents and don't insulate well. And definitely no loose dirt, as it'll get muddy and uncomfortable for them.
