Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items while respecting your new apartment's rules and avoiding distractions:

### 📺 1. TV Mount Installation
- **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, drill, screws, wall anchors (if mounting to drywall)
- **Steps:**
  1. **Mark Wall Studs:** Use a stud finder to locate wooden studs (every 16-24 inches). Mark with a pencil.
  2. **Level & Measure:** Place TV on a stand to mark mounting holes. Use a level to ensure it's straight.
  3. **Secure Mount:** Attach the mounting bracket to the wall *behind* the TV stand using screws into studs. For drywall without studs, use heavy-duty anchors.
  4. **Hang TV:** Lift TV stand by one edge at a time until it rests on the mount. Secure with remaining screws.
- **Landlord Tip:** Remove any tape/measurements before leaving. Clean drill marks with a cloth.

### 🌿 2. Hanging Planters
- **Tools Needed:** Wire hooks, drill, measuring tape, painter’s tape
- **Steps:**
  1. **Choose Location:** Pick a spot where plants will get light (e.g., near a window).
  2. **Mark Holes:** Use a pencil and level to mark hook positions. Place painter’s tape over marks to avoid scuffing walls.
  3. **Drill Pilot Holes:** Drill holes slightly smaller than hook shafts.
  4. **Install Hooks:** Screw hooks in securely. Test weight capacity.
  5. **Hanger Planters:** Slide planter pots over hooks (use plastic hangers if needed).
- **Noise Tip:** Drill during weekend mornings when neighbors are likely out.

### 🪢 3. Floating Shelves
- **Tools Needed:** Shelves, brackets, drill, screws, measuring tape
- **Steps:**
  1. **Measure Twice:** Measure shelf length and wall space. Mark placement points.
  2. **Drill Pilot Holes:** Drill holes at marked points. Use a level to keep shelves straight.
  3. **Attach Brackets:** Secure brackets to the wall with screws. Ensure brackets are level.
  4. **Place Shelves:** Slide shelves into brackets until snug.
- **Weight Note:** Avoid overloading shelves (max 5-7 lbs per shelf). Use brackets rated for your weight.

### ⚠️ Critical Safety Tips
- **Test Stability:** Give all installations a gentle nudge after completion.
- **Noise Control:** Use a battery-powered drill (quieter than corded).
- **Time Management:** Dedicate 2-3 focused 30-minute slots (e.g., 10 AM, 1 PM) to prevent fatigue.
- **Gym Connection:** Do your 5-minute leg stretches *before* starting installations to avoid stiffness.

### 📅 Recommended Schedule
- **Saturday AM:** Install TV mount (1-2 hours)
- **Saturday PM:** Hang planters (30 mins)
- **Sunday AM:** Install floating shelves (1 hour)
- **Sunday PM:** Decorate with art/furniture (flex time)

**Pro Tip:** Take photos of each step before screwing things in – it makes troubleshooting easier! Remember to hydrate during installations to combat fatigue. Your new space will look amazing while keeping your weekends balanced! 🎨✨
