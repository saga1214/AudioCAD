Yes, that's a great question, and you're right to think that way. You absolutely *can* submit your own illustrations.

In fact, many publishers actively seek out authors who can also do their own art. It's called 'author-illustrator' publishing, and it's a very valid path. The main thing to remember is that the publisher will own the artwork, but it's a common trade-off. You'll also likely need to sign a contract called an 'advance' agreement, which gives the publisher the rights to your work in exchange for an upfront payment.

So, yes, you should definitely consider submitting your work as a complete package. You could even start by looking for publishers or agents who specifically advertise that they represent 'author-illustrators'.
