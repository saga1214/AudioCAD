That's fantastic that you're all set and ready to go! You've got the basics covered, but here are a few more things you might want to grab to make the whole process smoother and safer.

First, a stud finder is really essential for making sure you hit those wall studs. You'll also want a good set of measuring tape to double-check your measurements. A level is great, but a carpenter's square is even better for making sure the cabinets are perfectly square as you put them together. A good drill is a must for screwing the cabinets into the wall.

Also, you'll want to grab some shims, which are basically thin wooden wedges, to make sure everything is perfectly level. You'll need some screws, and it's a good idea to have a few extra in case you need them. And definitely grab some safety glasses and a dust mask, since you'll be hammering and drilling.

And one last thing: have a plan for the countertop. You'll need to get the right kind of sealant for whatever material you're using, like a silicone for a granite or a different kind for a laminate. It's also a really good idea to have a helper for that part, since it's a bit tricky to get it just right. But you're doing an amazing job on this project!
