Okay, this is fun! For the 'Retro Tech' theme, two lunch ideas that tie into the era and are easy to eat. First, a 'Dial-Up Delight Plate.' This would feature a sandwich with mustard and pickles, which was a common lunch back then. For sides, you could have pretzel sticks and a bag of potato chips, maybe even the old-school crinkly ones. Second, a 'CRT Cathode Ray Tube Bowl.' This could be a simple soup and salad. The soup could be tomato or chicken noodle, and the salad could be a basic green salad with ranch dressing, just like the simple options you'd get from the cafeteria in the '90s. Both ideas are easy to eat and fit the theme without being too complicated.
