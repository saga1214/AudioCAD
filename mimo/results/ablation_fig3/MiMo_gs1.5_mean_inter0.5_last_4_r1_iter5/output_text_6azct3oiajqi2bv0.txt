That's a smart choice! Here's how to attach Velcro securely without tearing the lining:

1.  **Use Spray Adhesive:** This is your best bet. It creates a strong bond without stretching or puncturing the lining.
    *   **Apply:** Lightly spray a small amount onto the *back* of the Velcro strip (the hook side).
    *   **Press:** Immediately press the strip firmly onto the *inside* of the jacket's lining, right where you want it. Hold it for 10-15 seconds.
    *   **Why it works:** The adhesive clings without putting pressure on the fabric, and the Velcro itself becomes part of the lining's structure.

2.  **Use Fabric Glue (like Aleene's Tacky Glue or E6000):**
    *   **Apply:** Apply a thin, even line of glue along the *back* edge of the Velcro strip.
    *   **Press:** Press the strip firmly onto the lining. Hold it in place for several minutes until it starts to set.
    *   **Reinforce:** For extra security, you can apply a small dab of glue to the *front* of the Velcro strip (the soft side) right where it meets the main fabric of the jacket.
    *   **Low Heat Option:** You can also *carefully* use a low-heat iron (with a press cloth) to melt the glue into the Velcro backing. **Always test on a scrap first!**

3.  **Use Double-Sided Fabric Tape (like Wonder Under or Ultra-Fabric Adhesive Tape):**
    *   **Apply:** Cut a piece of the tape to fit the back of the Velcro strip. Press it down firmly.
    *   **Peel:** Once the adhesive is set (check the instructions, usually 1-2 minutes), carefully peel off the top paper.
    *   **Press:** Press the Velcro strip firmly onto the lining. The tape provides a strong bond without stretching.
    *   **Reinforce:** Apply a small dab of fabric glue to the front of the Velcro strip as well.

**Key Tips for Success:**

*   **Test First:** Always test your chosen method (adhesive, glue, tape) on a *scrap piece* of the jacket's lining fabric to ensure it doesn't damage or discolor it.
*   **Clean Surfaces:** Ensure both the Velcro back and the lining surface are clean and dry before applying any adhesive.
*   **Less is More:** Apply glue or spray adhesive thinly and evenly. Too much can seep out or cause issues.
*   **Hold Firmly:** Press and hold the Velcro strip firmly against the lining for the recommended time (usually 10-30 seconds for spray, longer for glue/tape) to ensure good adhesion.
*   **Reinforce Attachment Point:** If the Velcro is going near a stress point (like an elbow or shoulder), consider reinforcing the area with a small dab of glue or tape *underneath* the Velcro strip as well.

**Why NOT to use regular glue or staples?**

*   **Staples:** Will almost certainly puncture the lining.
*   **Regular Craft Glue (like school glue):** Isn't strong enough for this application and will likely peel off or sag over time, potentially *causing* the lining to tear.
*   **Hot Glue Gun:** Will melt the lining fabric.

**Best Overall Recommendation:** **Spray Adhesive** is generally the easiest, cleanest, and most reliable method for attaching Velcro securely to a lining without damage. **Fabric Glue or Double-Sided Fabric Tape** are also excellent alternatives if you don't have spray adhesive. **Avoid staples and regular craft glue.** Good luck!
