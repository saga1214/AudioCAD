Alright, staying on a budget for a family reunion fundraiser is totally doable. For desserts, you can focus on things that are simple to make but still feel special. For example, you could do a big batch of homemade cookies, like oatmeal or chocolate chip, and serve them with a small bowl of healthy toppings like nuts or yogurt. Fruit is always a good, cheap option, so you could make a fruit salad or have some banana or apple slices with a little nut butter. If you want something that feels a bit more special, you could set up a DIY sundae bar with a few different ice cream flavors and toppings. You could even make your own granola bars at home, which are often cheaper than buying them.
