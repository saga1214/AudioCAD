Okay, so the camper van needs a good pre-trip check. For brakes, you should listen for any grinding or squealing sounds. Also, check the brake fluid level and the condition of the pads and rotors. If they look worn down or there’s any significant damage, that’s a sign you need to replace them. As for spark plugs, it depends on whether they’re the standard copper ones or the longer-lasting platinum ones. If they’re copper, they probably need to be changed every 30,000 miles or so. The process is pretty straightforward: you’ll need a ratchet, a spark plug socket, and a new set of plugs. You just remove the old ones, check the gap on the new ones, and then install them. It’s a good idea to have a mechanic look at the engine bay and under the hood before you go. Just make sure to check the oil and the coolant levels too. It’s all about making sure the van is ready for the trip.
