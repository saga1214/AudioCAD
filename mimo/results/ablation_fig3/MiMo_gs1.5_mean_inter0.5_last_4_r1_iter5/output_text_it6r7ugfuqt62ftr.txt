What a wonderful plan! Since you're escaping Northern California's cool weather for a warm, active getaway with your daughter (who loves water and adventure!) and husband, I'd highly recommend **San Diego**. It offers:

*   **Year-round warm weather** (Dec-Apr is peak for warmest temps & best visibility)
*   **Abundant beaches** perfect for swimming
*   **World-class family attractions** (like the San Diego Zoo)
*   **Easy access to unique adventures** (whale watching, more balloon alternatives)
*   **Excellent gluten-free dining options**
*   **Plenty of activities** for all ages and interests

Here’s a detailed 4-day itinerary focusing on your priorities:

**Destination:** San Diego, California

**Accommodation Suggestion:** A beachfront condo or hotel in **La Jolla** or **Pacific Beach**. Many offer full kitchens (great for packing snacks/meals), ocean views, and easy beach access. Look for locations near La Jolla Cove or Pacific Beach for the best combo of scenery and amenities.

**Transportation:** **Rental SUV** (essential for carrying luggage and all your beach/swim gear). Book well in advance.

---

**Detailed Itinerary: San Diego Beach & Adventure Escape**

*   **Day 1: Arrival & Beach Bliss**
    *   **Morning:** Arrive, check into your accommodation. Settle in, unpack, and grab coffee/snacks.
    *   **Late Morning/Afternoon:** Head straight to **Pacific Beach** or **Mission Beach**. Set up your beach umbrella/chair. Swim, sunbathe, build sandcastles, play beach volleyball. Pack your swimsuit, towel, sunscreen, hat, and your waterproof bag with your favorite book/MP3 player.
    *   **Evening:** Enjoy a casual, fantastic **gluten-free dinner** at **Café 25** (Pacific Beach - known for creative GF dishes & beach vibe) or **The Fishery** (Pacific Beach - upscale but accommodating GF, excellent seafood). Walk along the boardwalk to catch the sunset.

*   **Day 2: Iconic San Diego Zoo & Lunch**
    *   **Morning:** Take a **rental car** to the **San Diego Zoo**. This is a MUST-DO for families and a true highlight. It's enormous, beautiful, and has something for everyone. Pack a picnic lunch (ensure GF) from your condo or a nearby market.
    *   **Afternoon:** After the zoo, head to **Balboa Park**. Explore the stunning architecture, gardens, museums (many have excellent gluten-free options like the Museum of Us or the San Diego Air & Space Museum), and the beautiful Japanese Friendship Garden (check GF policy, but often very accommodating).
    *   **Evening:** **Gluten-free dinner** at **Mango's Cantina** (Mission Valley - large, affordable, diverse Mexican & American, excellent GF menu) or **The Taco Stand** (Little Italy - fantastic Mexican, very friendly GF, check menu).

*   **Day 3: Adventure Day - Whale Watching & More**
    *   **Morning:** This is your daughter's special day! Book a **morning whale watching tour** departing from **San Diego Harbor**. Companies like **Crown Prince Cruises** or **SeaQuest Marine Park** offer tours specifically designed for families and are highly accommodating to dietary needs. This is the perfect "hot air balloon" alternative – thrilling, unique, and unforgettable. Pack your swimsuit, towel, and sunscreen (even on a boat!).
    *   **Late Morning/Afternoon:** After the tour, head back to the beach (Pacific or Coronado) for more swimming, exploring tide pools (check tide charts!), or visiting the **La Jolla Cove** sea lions (parking can be tricky, arrive early).
    *   **Evening:** **Gluten-free dinner** at **The Spinnaker** (La Jolla - upscale seafood, beautiful harbor views, excellent GF service) or **Ceviche & Tacos** (Little Italy - vibrant, fresh Mexican, very GF-friendly).

*   **Day 4: Relaxation & Departure**
    *   **Morning:** Enjoy a final lazy beach day. Swim, read, enjoy coffee on the sand, or take a gentle walk along the boardwalk.
    *   **Late Morning:** Depending on your flight time, you could squeeze in a visit to **Coronado Island** (take the free ferry from Coronado Beach or Pacific Beach). Stroll the famous beach, see the Hotel del Coronado, and enjoy the relaxed vibe. Pack up your beach bag and condo.
    *   **Afternoon:** Check out, return rental car, and head to San Diego International Airport (SAN) for your departure.

---

**Key Packing Tips (Especially for Swimming & GF Diet):**

1.  **Swimsuits:** Pack at least 2-3 swimsuits, a couple of cover-ups, a lightweight sarong, and a waterproof bag for phones/books.
2.  **Sun & Beach Gear:** High-SPF sunscreen (reef-safe if possible), wide-brimmed hat, sunglasses with UV protection, beach umbrella/tent, lightweight beach chairs, and a large beach bag.
3.  **Clothing:** Lightweight, breathable clothing for warm days. Pack one or two slightly cooler layers (lightweight cardigan or long-sleeve shirt) for evenings. Comfortable walking shoes.
4.  **Gluten-Free Essentials:** Bring your own **gluten-free snacks** (granola bars, nuts, fruit) for the flight and car ride. Pack your **own certified gluten-free snacks** for the beach. **Bring a small notebook or your phone** to jot down GF restaurant details if needed. **Call ahead** to confirm GF policies for any restaurant you plan to visit, especially smaller ones.
5.  **Other:** Reusable water bottle, medications, any personal toiletries, camera.

*   **GF Friendly:** Major city with a huge tourism industry and strong GF dining scene. Many top restaurants excel at GF.
*   **Family Fun:** Zoo, beaches, parks, museums – endless options for kids and adults.
*   **Convenient:** Easy access from Northern California via flight (short flight) or car (longer drive, ~5-6 hours).
