That's a great question! Since you're exploring hands-on hobbies like tailoring, vintage restoration, and potentially woodworking/clay, here are some thoughtful gift ideas that align with your interests:

### For Tailoring
1. **Precision Shears** (e.g., Gingher or Kai): Essential for clean cuts in fabric.  
2. **A High-Quality Awl**: For marking lines on fabric or leather.  
3. **Vintage Pen Restoration Book**: To deepen your existing hobby (e.g., *"The Parker 51: A History of the Iconic Pen"*).  
4. **Tailoring Tool Kit**: Includes thread, needles, pins, and a small measuring tape.  

### For Woodworking (if you explore it)
1. **Marking Gauge**: For precise lines on wood.  
2. **Clamp Set**: Crucial for joining pieces.  
3. **Woodworking Voucher**: For a local workshop or online course.  

### For Clay/Pottery
1. **Small Kiln Kit**: For firing handmade pieces at home.  
2. **Artist’s Clay Set**: High-quality clay in various colors.  
3. **Pottery Wheel Book**: For learning techniques.  

### General Gift Ideas
- **Skill Development**: A subscription to a craft magazine (*e.g., Tailor’s Magazine* or *Woodworking Magazine*).  
- **Experience Voucher**: Pottery studio class, tailoring workshop, or woodworking seminar.  
- **Custom Pen Restoration Kit**: If you stick with vintage pens.  

### Why These Work
- **Practical**: Tools support your hobbies without being overwhelming.  
- **Personal**: Gifts like pen restoration books or vintage-inspired tools tie into your passions.  
- **Flexibility**: Experiences (workshops) let you explore new skills affordably.
