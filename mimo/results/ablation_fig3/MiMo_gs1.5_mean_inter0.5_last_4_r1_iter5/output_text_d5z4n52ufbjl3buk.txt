First, start with a clear subject line, something professional like "Meeting Request: Exam Feedback."

Then, in the body, state your purpose right away. You could say, "I'm writing to request a brief meeting to discuss my recent exam score and learn how I might improve for future assignments."

Next, use your plan to frame it as a request for feedback. For instance, "As someone who has done well in my other exams, I'm eager to understand how my essay could have been improved to better meet the course expectations."
