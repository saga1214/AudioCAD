### 🌴 **Costa Rica Scuba Diving Itinerary: Best of the Pacific & Caribbean**
*(Ideal for mid-December to April)*

#### **1. West Coast: Cocos Island (Pacific Ocean)**
   - **Why it’s perfect**: Remote, wild, and teeming with marine life. Famous for **whale sharks, manta rays, turtles, and deep wrecks**.
   - **Top Dives**: 
     - **Cocos Island Shipwrecks** (e.g., *RMS Presidente*): Explore artificial reefs with schools of hammerheads.
     - **Tortuga Island** (protected reserve): Clear waters with tropical fish and reef sharks.
   - **Logistics**: Fly to **Tamarindo or Santa Teresa** (Pacific Coast). Stay at eco-lodges near the airport. Dive operators like **Dive Cocos** or **Ocean Raft** offer liveaboards (3-4 days).  
   - **Tip**: Use **short fins** here—they’re easier for current-heavy waters.

#### **2. South Pacific: Los Dominicos (Isabela Island, Galápagos)**
   - **Why it’s perfect**: If you loved Honduran wildlife, this is a **next-level experience**. Swim with sea lions, nurse sharks, and **humpback whales** (Dec–Apr).
   - **Top Dives**: 
     - **Los Dominicos** (cave-dive site): Crystal-clear water, turtles, and reef sharks.
     - **Coral Gardens**: Vibrant coral formations and schools of fish.
   - **Logistics**: Fly to **San José** → drive to **Puerto Viejo** (Caribbean) or **San José International Airport** (Galápagos). Book with **PADI-certified operators** (e.g., *Dive Galapagos*).  
   - **Tip**: Long fins ideal for strong currents but require leg strength.

#### **3. Caribbean Coast: Cahuita National Park (Puntarenas)**
   - **Why it’s perfect**: Easy access from San José. Gentle currents, **coral reefs, and abundant tropical fish**. Perfect for beginners.
   - **Top Dives**: 
     - **Cahuita Marine Park**: Colorful reefs with parrotfish, sea turtles, and rays.
     - **Playa Blanca**: Sandy bottom with snorkeling-friendly visibility.
   - **Logistics**: Stay in **Puntarenas**. Day trips from San José (1.5-hour drive). Book with **Dive Puntarenas**.  
   - **Tip**: Short fins ideal here—maneuverable in shallow waters.

#### **4. Caribbean Coast: Puerto Viejo (Dominical/Tamarindo)**
   - **Why it’s perfect**: Jungle-meets-ocean vibe. **Blue holes, wrecks, and freshwater springs**.
   - **Top Dives**: 
     - **Dominical Cenotes**: Freshwater springs with clear water and macro-life.
     - **Sarapiquí Bay**: Coral gardens and dolphin sightings.
   - **Logistics**: Stay in **eco-lodges** (e.g., *Casa de los Tres Monos*). Operators like **Dive Puerto Viejo** offer day trips.  
   - **Tip**: Short fins for easy access to shore dives.

### 🎒 **Key Planning Tips**
- **Fin Choice**: Stick with **short fins** for most sites (Cahuita, Puerto Viejo, Cocos) unless you’re targeting strong currents (Los Dominicos). Long fins will be heavy luggage!
- **Best Time**: **Dec–April** (dry season, calmest waters). Avoid May–Nov (rainy season, rough seas).
- **Permits**: Cocos Island requires a **tourist card** (cost: ~$100). Book operators in advance.
- **Wildlife Focus**: Prioritize **Cocos Island** (whale sharks/mantas) and **Los Dominicos** (reef sharks/turtles).
- **Local Experience**: Visit a **tortuga farm** (e.g., *Tortuga Island*), try **Pacuare River rafting**, or explore **Osa Peninsula** rainforests.

### 🎯 **Summary Itinerary**
- **Days 1–3**: San José → **Cahuita** (intro dives).  
- **Days 4–7**: **Puerto Viejo** → **Los Dominicos liveaboard** (Galápagos).  
- **Days 8–10**: **Cocos Island liveaboard** (shipwrecks/mantas).  
- **Days 11–13**: Return to San José.
