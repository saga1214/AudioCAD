### **U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia**  
If you make an illegal U-turn in BC, you can expect:  
- **Ticket:** $160 fine  
- **Demerit Points:** 3 points on your driver's license  
- **Safety Risk:** Illegal U-turns can cause collisions, so enforcement is strict.  

**Key Rules to Remember:**  
- Never U-turn at intersections with traffic lights or stop signs unless permitted by a sign.  
- Avoid U-turns on curves, hills, or blind spots.  
- Ensure a clear 150-meter visibility in both directions.  
- Always check signs—some areas explicitly prohibit U-turns.

### **Spaghetti Carbonara Recipe: Ingredients**  
Here’s a classic, authentic list (serves 4):  

#### **Aromatics**  
- 250g pancetta or guanciale (diced)  
- 2 garlic cloves (minced)  

#### **Pasta & Sauce**  
- 400g spaghetti  
- 3 large eggs (or 2 whole + 1 yolk for creaminess)  
- 150g Pecorino Romano cheese (grated)  
- 50g Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese (grated)  

#### **Seasoning & Garnish**  
- Salt (for pasta water)  
- Freshly ground black pepper  
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil (optional)  

#### **Equipment**  
- Large pot  
- Large skillet  

---

### **Key Tips for Success**  
1. **No Cream:** Authentic carbonara uses eggs, cheese, and pork fat.  
2. **Pasta Water:** Reserve 1 cup for silky sauce.  
3. **Temperature Control:** Mix eggs off-heat to avoid scrambling.  
4. **Cheese Balance:** Pecorino adds sharpness; Parmigiano mellowens it.
