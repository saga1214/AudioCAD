Here are quick, elegant drink recipes for your party—keeping the soda allergy in mind and focusing on versatility for vegetarians/omnivores:

### 🥂 1. **Simple Vodka Mix** (Refreshing & Universal)
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz vodka (your leftover bottle!)  
  - 1 oz fresh lemonade or lemon-lime soda  
  - 1 oz soda water (or sparkling water for no-soda guests)  
  - *Optional*: 0.5 oz simple syrup (if needed for sweetness)  
- **How to**: Shake or stir vodka, lemonade, and syrup (if using). Strain into a chilled glass over ice. Top with soda water. Garnish with a lemon wheel.  
- **Why it works**: Light, citrusy, and crowd-pleasing. No soda for the allergic guest—just swap soda water for soda-lime mix.

### 🇦🇷 2. **Argentine-Style Fernet** (Bold & Zesty)
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
  - 2 oz Clamato (tomato-clam juice mix—*key for authenticity*).  
  *No Clamato? Use 1.5 oz tomato juice + 0.5 oz lime juice.*  
  - 2 oz soda water  
- **How to**: Shake Fernet, lime, and Clamato (or tomato juice) with ice. Strain into a coupe or rocks glass. Top with soda. Garnish with an orange slice.  
- **Why it works**: Fiery, herbal, and tangy—perfect for mixed ages. Skip soda if needed; just use more soda water.

### 💡 Quick Tips:
- **No-Soda Option**: For both cocktails, replace soda-lime or soda water with **sparkling water** or **ginger beer** (non-alcoholic if preferred).  
- **Allergy Safe**: Both recipes use no soda.  
- **Formality**: Serve in chilled glasses with simple garnishes (lemon, lime, herb sprigs).  
- **Efficiency**: Prep bottles in advance—shake/stir when guests arrive.  

These drinks are quick, elegant, and cover all bases. Enjoy your dinner party! 🥂
