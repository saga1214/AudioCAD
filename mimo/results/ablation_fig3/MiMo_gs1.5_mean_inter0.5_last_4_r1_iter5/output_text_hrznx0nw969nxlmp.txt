You're in for a fantastic week! Secret Harbour is a wonderful choice, and the Red Hook area is indeed the heart of St. Thomas. Here are some great **daytime activities** to enjoy near Secret Harbour and Red Hook:

1.  **Snorkeling at Secret Harbour Beach:** This is your best bet right outside your door! The bay is incredibly calm, shallow, and protected, making it perfect for beginners and non-swimmers. You'll likely spot small fish, maybe some crabs, and even the occasional small conch or lobster. Grab a snorkel from the resort or pick one up locally.
2.  **Explore Cruz Bay:** Walk into the charming village of Cruz Bay just a short distance south of Secret Harbour. Browse the shops (like the Virgin Islands Ecological Services Center, the Red Hook Art Gallery, and various gift shops), grab a coffee or lunch at a cafe like **Coki Cafe** (right on the beach!), and soak in the local vibe.
3.  **Visit Coral World Ocean Park (Coki Beach):** Located just north of Secret Harbour, this is a must-visit for a controlled underwater experience. You can:
    *   **Walk Through the Tunnel:** A cool, air-conditioned underwater tunnel showcasing local marine life (tropical fish, sharks, rays, eels).
    *   **Go Aerial Tram:** Ride an observation tram above the reef for panoramic views.
    *   **Visit the Lagoon:** See a variety of colorful fish in a large, protected lagoon.
    *   **Snorkel or Dive:** Explore the beautiful coral gardens (snorkeling gear often available for rent).
4.  **Hike Reef Bay Trail:** A beautiful, relatively short (1.5-2 mile round trip) and easy hike leading to the historic **Reef Bay Plantation Ruins**. You'll walk through lush rainforest, past a freshwater stream, and enjoy stunning ocean views at the ruins and the beautiful **Reef Bay Beach** (which is also great for a swim/snorkel if you feel comfortable). It's a peaceful and scenic escape.
5.  **Relax at Coki Beach Park:** Just a short walk north of Secret Harbour is Coki Beach Park. It offers another beautiful, calm bay with great snorkeling (especially for fish), a playground for kids, and plenty of space to relax, swim, or have a picnic. It's a popular spot with a friendly atmosphere.
6.  **Sail Red Hook:** If you have your boat, consider taking a sail or a catamaran cruise around the beautiful anchorages of Red Hook (like the Little Moriches Bay area nearby) or even further afield. Many charter companies offer day sails – perfect for exploring without the pressure of flying.
7.  **Explore the Coki Beach Area:** Beyond the park, Coki Beach itself has shops, cafes, and restaurants. It's a great spot for a stroll, people-watching, and enjoying the beachfront atmosphere.
8.  **Visit the Virgin Islands National Park (St. John):** If you have a car or are willing to drive (about 45 mins from St. Thomas), St. John is stunning. Key sites include:
    *   **Cruz Bay Beach:** Famous for its calm water and vibrant coral gardens (snorkeling paradise).
    *   **Trunk Bay:** Known for its beautiful underwater snorkeling trail (great for beginners).
    *   **Sage Rock:** A protected area with excellent snorkeling and kayaking.
    *   **Laguna泻湖:** A secluded, beautiful bay perfect for swimming and sunbathing.
    *   **Virgin Islands National Park Visitor Center:** Learn about the island's ecology and history.

**Key Tips for Your Day:**

*   **Sun Protection:** The Caribbean sun is strong. Wear high-SPF sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, and cover up. Reapply often!
*   **Hydration:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
*   **Footwear:** Flip-flops are great for the beach, but sturdy sandals or water shoes are good for walking on the sand or coral gardens.
*   **Snorkel Gear:** You can rent gear locally in Cruz Bay or at Coral World, or bring your own if you have it.

Enjoy your relaxing and adventurous week in the Virgin Islands! It sounds like you'll have a wonderful boat trip experience.
