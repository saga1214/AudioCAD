Here's a curated list of **gluten-free snacks** perfect for camping and cycling fuel:

1. **Trail Mix**: Combine unsalted nuts (almonds, walnuts), seeds (pumpkin, sunflower), and gluten-free dried fruit (cranberries, apricots). *Tip: Avoid chocolate if you want to prevent melting.*
2. **Roasted Chickpeas**: Crunchy, high-protein, and portable. Look for pre-seasoned varieties or make your own.
3. **Nut Butter Packets**: Single-serve almond or peanut butter packets for quick energy. *Check labels for gluten-free certification.*
4. **Rice Cakes**: Top with nut butter or avocado (if refrigeration is available).
5. **Fresh Fruit**: Apples, bananas, oranges, or berries (if you have a cooler).
6. **Gluten-Free Granola Bars**: Brands like RXBAR, KIND, or Larabar (verify ingredients).
7. **Dark Chocolate**: 70%+ cocoa for antioxidants and quick energy (pack in a cooler).
8. **Hard-Boiled Eggs**: Pre-peeled for easy eating (keep refrigerated).
9. **Seaweed Snacks**: Lightweight, salty, and packed with minerals.
10. **Homemade Energy Balls**: Blend oats (certified GF), dates, nut butter, and chia seeds.

### Pro Tips:
- **Portion Control**: Pre-portion snacks into reusable containers to avoid overpacking.
- **Hydration**: Pair snacks with water! Add electrolyte tablets for long rides.
- **Label Check**: Always verify "gluten-free" labels (some facilities process wheat).
