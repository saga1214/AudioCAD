Here’s a **themed, sand-friendly shopping list** for your nautical birthday party, designed for 7 kids (including your son) at the beach, with pirate themes and allergy-safe options (no watermelon):

---

### **Nautical/Pirate Party Food & Drinks**  
*(All finger foods, easy to handle, and sand-resistant)*  

#### **Main Course (Pirate’s Feast)**  
- **Mini slider sandwiches** (cut into 2" pieces):  
  - 1 lb deli turkey or ham  
  - 1/2 lb cheese (cheddar, Swiss, or mozzarella)  
  - **Theme idea:** Wrap each slider in a tiny **pirate flag** (printable flags)  
- **"Buried Treasure" Cheese Cubes**:  
  - 2 blocks cheddar or mozzarella (cubed)  
  - Serve with **mini flag toothpicks**  
- **Veggie Sticks**:  
  - 1 bag baby carrots  
  - 1 bag cucumber sticks  
  - 1 bag bell pepper strips  
  - **Theme idea:** Arrange in a **"pirate treasure chest"** (plastic container)  

#### **Snacks (Pirate Ship Masts & Jewels)**  
- **Goldfish Crackers** (as "ship masts"):  
  - 3 boxes  
- **Pretzel Rods** (as "ship masts"):  
  - 2 boxes  
- **Grapes** (as "jewels"):  
  - 1 lb (allergy-friendly)  
- **Apple Slices**:  
  - 2 apples (dipped in lemon juice to prevent browning)  
  - **Theme idea:** Skewer with **mini pirate hats**  

#### **Drinks (Pirate Punch & Sea Water)**  
- **Individual Juice Boxes**:  
  - 8 boxes (strawberry, apple, or mixed)  
  - **Theme idea:** Label as **"Pirate Punch"** or **"Sea Water"**  
- **Small Water Bottles** (optional):  
  - 8 bottles (if kids prefer water)  
- **"Treasure Chest" Drink Station**:  
  - Use a **plastic treasure chest** as a drink holder  

#### **Allergy-Safe & Extras**  
- **Nuts-free** and **watermelon-free** options noted.  
- **Goldfish crackers** and **pretzel rods** are allergy-friendly (check labels).  
- **Lemon wedges** for apple slices to prevent browning.  

---

### **Themed Packaging & Decor**  
- **"Buried Treasure" Containers**:  
  - Small jars or containers for cheese cubes, crackers, and pretzels.  
- **Pirate Flag Toothpicks**:  
  - Print free templates online, then dip in hot glue (让孩子安全地拿着).  
- **Pirate Name Tags**:  
  - "Captain [Child's Name]" labels for each plate.  

---

### **Beach Setup Tips**  
1. **Sand-Free Zones**:  
   - Use a **plastic tablecloth** or **picnic blanket** to protect surfaces.  
   - Keep food in **sealed containers** (no open bowls).  
2. **Easy Cleanup**:  
   - Pack out trash bags and wet wipes for quick cleanup.  
3. **Themed Extras**:  
   - Serve food with a **"pirate map"** (printed map with food locations).  

This list keeps things **mess-free, allergy-conscious, and fully pirate-themed**—perfect for a 7-year-old’s nautical party! 🏴‍☠️✨
