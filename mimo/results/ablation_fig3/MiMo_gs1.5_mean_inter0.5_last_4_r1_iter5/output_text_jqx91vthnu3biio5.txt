Here are some delicious and practical dinner ideas to fuel your day — whether you're craving comfort, something quick, or a gourmet twist:

### 🍝 Quick & Comforting  
1. **Mediterranean Chickpea Salad Bowl**  
   - Toss mixed greens with chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, olives, feta, and a lemon-herb dressing. Top with roasted chickpeas for crunch.  
   *Why*: High-protein, healthy fats, and ready in 15 minutes.  

2. **Creamy Tomato Basil Pasta**  
   - Sauté garlic, add canned tomatoes and fresh basil, simmer into a sauce, then toss with al dente pasta and a splash of cream or coconut milk.  
   *Why*: Feels indulgent but is actually light.  

### 🥘 Hearty & Satisfying  
3. **Lentil Shepherd’s Pie**  
   - Brown lentils, carrots, peas, and herbs in gravy, top with mashed cauliflower or sweet potatoes, and bake until golden.  
   *Why*: Vegan, fiber-rich, and cozy.  

4. **Sheet-Pan Salmon & Roasted Veggies**  
   - Season salmon fillets with lemon, garlic, and dill. Toss broccoli, bell peppers, and sweet potatoes with olive oil. Roast together at 400°F (200°C) for 20 mins.  
   *Why*: Minimal cleanup, packed with omega-3s.  

### 🌱 Light & Fresh  
5. **Zucchini Noodles with Pesto & Shrimp**  
   - Spiralize zucchini, sauté with garlic, then add shrimp and fresh basil. Finish with toasted pine nuts.  
   *Why*: Low-carb, vibrant, and under 20 mins.  

6. **Mason Jar Salads**  
   - Layer greens, grilled chicken, quinoa, avocado, nuts, and vinaigrette in jars. Shake to mix before eating.  
   *Why*: Portable, no reheating needed.  

### 🍳 Bonus: 5-Minute Fix  
7. **Avocado & Egg Toast**  
   - Mash avocado on sourdough, top with a fried/poached egg, chili flakes, and microgreens.  
   *Why*: Ultimate protein + healthy fats combo.
