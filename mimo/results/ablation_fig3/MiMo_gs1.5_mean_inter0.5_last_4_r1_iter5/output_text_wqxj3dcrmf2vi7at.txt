Of course! Let's go over some more ideas for your corgi princess, Princess. 

For homemade treats, you could make peanut butter oat cookies by mixing peanut butter and oats and baking them into little shapes. Mashed sweet potato cut into tiny pieces freezes well or can be baked until firm for a crunchy snack. And while not a treat, small pieces of cooked chicken are a great protein boost.

For store-bought options, brands like Blue Buffalo, Wellness, Zuke's, Greenies, and Merrick offer a lot of choices. Just be sure to check the ingredients for something wholesome.

And remember, portion control is important, so always give her tiny pieces!
