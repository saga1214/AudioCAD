That's a really important question. While all the reasons are critical, I'd say the most fundamental aspect is that hotspots are the only places where you can find those unique species. Their existence is irreplaceable.

Think of it this way: we can't just create a new habitat somewhere else to replace a rainforest that's been destroyed. That specific ecosystem and all the life within it are gone forever. So, by preserving a hotspot, we are literally preserving the only place in the entire world where those species can live. It's about preventing a unique, and often irreplaceable, form of life from vanishing forever.
