**Important Considerations:**

*   **Introduce Slowly:** Add new veggies one at a time in very small amounts to monitor for any digestive upset (diarrhea, lethargy).
*   **Fresh is Best:** Wash thoroughly. Avoid pre-cut or pre-packaged veggies unless you know the source.
*   **Preparation:** Cooking (steaming, lightly baking, or microwaving) can make some veggies more digestible and mellow strong flavors. Always chop into small, bird-sized pieces.
*   **Variety:** Rotate different green veggies regularly for maximum nutritional benefit.
*   **Moderation:** Treat veggies as a supplement to their primary diet (pellets/seed blend), not the main food source.
*   **Avoid:** Peas (high sugar), corn, onions, garlic, leeks, potatoes, tomatoes (especially green parts), and any moldy vegetables. *Always be cautious with new foods.*
