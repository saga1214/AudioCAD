1.  **Prevention Over Reaction:** Access control aims to *prevent* unauthorized entry *before* it happens. If guards or screening fail, the next layers (alarms, cameras, secure displays) become crucial for *detecting* and *responding* to a breach.  
2.  **Deterrence & Awareness:** Visible security personnel and bag checks signal that the museum takes security seriously, deterring opportunistic theft.  
3.  **Scope Limitation:** Even if a guard makes a mistake (e.g., lets someone in), the access control layer restricts their movement to specific zones, buying time for other systems to activate.  
4.  **Strategic First Point:** The entrance is the only place where you can *directly* control who enters and what they bring in. All other security layers protect *after* entry.
