Here's a quick guide to crowd-pleasing candy and efficient party prep:

### 🎃 Candy Strategy (Avoid Waste!)
1. **Classic Favorites**:  
   - **M&M's, Skittles, Hershey's Kisses**: Universally loved, bright colors, easy to eat.  
   - **Mini Chocolate Bars**: Snickers, Milky Way, Twix (small packs reduce waste).  
   - **Gummies**: Starbursts, Haribo gummies (check for artificial dye-free options).  
   - **Balls/Candy Cores**: Tootsie Rolls, Lifesavers (cheap, low-risk for leftovers).  

2. **Themed Treats**:  
   - **Pumpkin-shaped chocolates** or **ghost candies** (add Halloween flair).  
   - **"Monster" candy jars** (black bags with candy labels like "Zombie Juice").  

3. **Avoid**:  
   - Overly niche flavors (e.g., sour candy if kids attend).  
   - Large bags (e.g., full-size candy bars—offer mini versions instead).  
   - Expensive items (e.g., gourmet chocolates) unless you know guests’ tastes.  

4. **Allergy Tip**:  
   - Label clearly: **"Artificial Dye-Free"** or **"Gluten-Free"** if needed.  
   - Use **separate bowls** for allergen-safe candies to prevent cross-contamination.  

### 🛒 Last-Minute Party Essentials
- **Decor**:  
  - **Inflatables**: Bats, ghosts, or witches (Target/Walmart).  
  - **LED Lights**: String lights + orange/red bulbs.  
  - **DIY Props**: Hang paper cutouts of tombstones or spooky trees.  
- **Food**:  
  - **Pizza**: Order extra for leftovers.  
  - **Allergy-Friendly**: Veggie platter, fruit skewers, popcorn.  
  - **Drinks**: Lemonade + water in pitchers (add candy canes for fun).  
- **Fog Machine Hack**:  
  - **DIY Fog Chiller**: Place a cooler with **ice + water bottles** behind the fog machine. Fog will flow through it and pool lower.  

### 🕵️‍♂️ Pro Tip
Check expiration dates on candy *before* buying—last-minute parties can lead to leftovers! Aim for **$5–$10 per person** for candy to avoid waste.
