You two sound like you'd have a great time on an adventure. The good news is Calgary is surrounded by incredible outdoor spots. For a weekend trip, you have a ton of options.

If you're looking for something with a bit of a challenge, you could hike up the Plain of Six Glaciers in Banff. It's a tough one, but the views from the top are breathtaking. Or, for a more accessible but still beautiful hike, Johnston Canyon in Banff is great. It has a bridge that goes right over a waterfall, so it's pretty impressive.

If you'd rather be on the water, you could rent a canoe or kayak on the Kananaskis Lake. It's a stunningly beautiful spot. Or, for something a bit more relaxed, you could take a cruise on the Bow River.

If you want to see wildlife, you could drive out to the Elk Valley. You might spot elk, bighorn sheep, or even a grizzly bear. Or, you could drive the Icefields Parkway to see the glaciers and wildlife there.

For a more low-key adventure, you could take a day trip to the Calgary Zoo. It's a great way to see animals and enjoy the outdoors without a lot of effort.

Whatever you choose, I'm sure it'll be a great time. Just be sure to pack layers, because the weather can change quickly.
