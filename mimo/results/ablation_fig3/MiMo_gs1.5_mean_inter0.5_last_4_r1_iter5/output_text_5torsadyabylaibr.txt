I'm happy to help. For a classic Thanksgiving pie, you could make a simple pumpkin pie. It's a very traditional and beloved dessert. It requires a pie crust that you can buy from the store, and the filling is just canned pumpkin, eggs, sugar, and spices. It's a great option, especially if you're not a confident baker.

For a completely hands-off option, there's always a fruit pie. You can buy a pre-made crust, and then use canned fruit like peaches or apples to fill it. It's very simple and looks impressive.

And if you're feeling a little more adventurous, a sweet potato pie is another wonderful Thanksgiving classic. It has a very rich flavor and is delicious. All of these are very manageable for a beginner.
