### 🔧 **Glue Gun Help:**
1. **Check the Temperature:** If it’s hot but not melting, it might be overheating or the glue is old/dry. Try letting it cool for 15-20 minutes, then test on scrap paper.
2. **Use the Right Glue:** Ensure it’s a **hot glue stick** (not liquid glue). If it’s not working, clean the nozzle with a damp cloth and try a new stick.
3. **Ambient Temperature:** Cold rooms can make glue sluggish. Keep your workspace warm (60°F+ is ideal).

### 🎃 **Spooky Decoration Boosters:**
- **Blood Drops:** Add tiny, glossy red dots (use red gelatin or fake blood) around the base/masks for a macabre touch.
- **Faux Moss & Roots:** Wrap the base with dark green faux moss and tuck in twisted "roots" (plastic vine or black wire) for a graveyard vibe.
- **Miniature Skeletons:** Glue tiny skeleton hands, skulls, or bones around the globes.
- **Eerie Shimmer:** Sprinkle **black or silver glitter** into the fairy lights for a ghostly glow.
- **Mask Details:** Attach **tiny faux tears** (clear plastic "tears" glued to masks) for a haunting masquerade twist.
- **Scent:** Add a drop of **blackberry/clove essential oil** around the base for a mysterious aroma.

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Use a glue gun to attach black tulle or organza around the base—it mimics "dressed" cobwebs and adds depth without bulk!
