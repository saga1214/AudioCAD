Okay, let's quickly review your prep steps. You've removed the hardware and sanded the surfaces. That's great. The next most critical step before applying primer is to apply a protective coat. You'll want to use painter's tape to protect the areas you don't want to get paint on, like the floors, countertops, and any trim around the cabinets.

After you've taped everything off, use a drop cloth or plastic sheet to cover the floor and any other surfaces that could get paint on them. You don't want to be sanding that stuff in later.

Once you're all taped and covered up, you can go ahead and apply your primer.
