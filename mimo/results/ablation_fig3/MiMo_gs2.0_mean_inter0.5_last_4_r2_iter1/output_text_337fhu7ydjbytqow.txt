### **For the 20-Mile Daytime Segment (Mid-30s, Hilly, Daylight)**
1.  **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking synthetic top & bottom (e.g., polyester or merino wool blend).  
2.  **Mid-Layer:** Lightweight fleece or windbreaker (for cooler moments or wind).  
3.  **Outer Layer:** Wind-resistant or light water-resistant jacket (e.g., rain shell or lightweight rain jacket).  
4.  **Headwear:** Cap (for sun) or lightweight beanie (if expecting afternoon cloud cover).  
5.  **Handwear:** Running gloves (optional, but good for grip/hills).  
6.  **Footwear:** Trail running shoes with aggressive tread/grip (essential for hills).  
7.  **Lighting:** Headlamp or handheld light + **spare batteries**.  
8.  **Pack/Vest:** Small running pack or vest to carry layers as you warm up/cool down.  
9.  **Buff/Gaiter:** Quick-dry neck cover (useful for sun, wind, or sweat management).  

### **For the Final 12-Mile Night Segment (Mid-30s, Likely Colder & Windy)**
1.  **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking top & bottom (synthetic/merino wool).  
2.  **Mid-Layer:** **Essential:** Fleece jacket or lightweight insulated jacket (e.g., vest or puffy).  
3.  **Outer Layer:** **Essential:** Windproof/waterproof jacket (e.g., hardshell or rain jacket).  
4.  **Headwear:** **Essential:** Warm beanie or hat (wind chill makes extremities cold fast).  
5.  **Handwear:** **Essential:** Gloves (fingerless or full-fingered).  
6.  **Footwear:** Same trail shoes with grip.  
7.  **Lighting:** Headlamp or handheld light + **spare batteries** (critical for night).  
8.  **Pack/Vest:** Small pack or vest (to stash/remove layers if needed).  
9.  **Buff/Gaiter:** **Essential:** Light insulation layer (can wrap around neck for warmth).  

### **Key Adjustments for Night:**
- **Layering is critical:** You’ll need the insulated jacket + buff/gaiter at night, even if you didn’t need it during the day.  
- **Lighting:** Headlamp is non-negotiable. Backup batteries are a must.  
- **Extremities:** Gloves and a warm hat prevent heat loss significantly.  
- **Wind Protection:** Windproof outer layer is essential at night.  

### **Pro Tips:**
- **Pack Smart:** Use a vest/pack to carry layers easily. Swap mid-run if needed.  
- **Test Layers:** Try your gear beforehand to ensure a good fit.  
- **Aid Station Prep:** Know exactly where stations are to plan layer swaps.  
- **Comfort First:** Prioritize what keeps you warm/dry and visible without hindering pace.
