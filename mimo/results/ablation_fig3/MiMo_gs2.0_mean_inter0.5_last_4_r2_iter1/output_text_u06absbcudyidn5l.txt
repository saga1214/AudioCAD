Okay, let's break this down. Chronic redness, or erythema, across your lifetime can stem from many places. The most important thing to know is that vasculitis is inflammation of the blood vessels, often autoimmune, where the body's defenses mistakenly attack them. This can affect vessels of any size.

Symptoms vary widely depending on where the inflammation is. You might feel generally unwell with fever and fatigue, or you could see rashes, sores, or even vision problems. Hair and nails can definitely be affected; you might notice them becoming brittle or slow to grow.

Besides vasculitis, other common causes of persistent redness include rosacea, sun damage, eczema, allergies, or even some medications. To get to the bottom of it, a doctor would need to do some exams and tests, possibly including a biopsy for confirmation.

For hair and nails, supplements like collagen peptides, keratin, iron, and zinc can be beneficial. For inflammation, ibuprofen can provide temporary relief but absolutely should not be started as a regular regimen without a doctor's approval, especially when a potential systemic condition like vasculitis is on the table. They can determine the cause and create a safe, comprehensive plan for you.
