That's thoughtful of your family! Here are some meaningful gift ideas tailored to hands-on hobbies, balancing practicality and sentiment:

### For Tailoring
1. **Specialty Thread Set** (e.g., French cotton or leather thread) – Essential for quality work.  
2. **Vintage-Inspired Accessories** – A Parker 51-themed button set or pocket square to tie back to your pen hobby.  
3. **Fabric Swatch Book** – Curated swatches for inspiration.  
4. **Handmade Leather Lapel Pins** – A subtle nod to your pen restoration skills.  

### If Exploring Woodworking
1. **Pre-Owned Hand Plane** – A classic tool that elevates projects.  
2. **Workshop Apron** – For protection and style.  
3. **Woodworking Plan Book** (e.g., *The Anarchist’s Workshop* by David Clary) – Fun, approachable projects.  

### For Clay/Pottery
1. **Air-Dry Clay Kit** – Low-cost, low-commitment for experimentation.  
2. **Stylus Set** – For sculpting precision.  
3. **Mini Pottery Wheel** – If you want to dive deeper.  

### Universal Gifts
- **"Craft Co-Op Hours"**: Have family members commit to a set number of hours helping you work on a project (e.g., hemming a shirt, shaping a mug).  
- **Subscription Box**: *The Stitchery Box* (tailoring) or *Woodworking Tool of the Month* for gradual upgrades.  

**Key Tip**: Prioritize gifts that support *your specific skills* (like thread for tailoring) over generic tools. A heartfelt note explaining *why* you chose it ("I know how much you enjoy small, detailed work—this thread will help you perfect your stitches!") adds emotional weight.  

Your family’s curiosity about your hobbies is a great sign—they’re invested in *you*. Tailoring seems like a natural fit, and these gifts could nurture that passion! 🧵✨
