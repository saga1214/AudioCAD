Here’s a weekly practice plan to help you make steady progress without stress:

Monday: Start with beanbags. Practice one-handed two-ball juggling for 15 minutes to reinforce rhythm. Then, attempt transitions from two to three beanbags for 10 minutes.

Tuesday: Use tennis balls. Focus on slow, deliberate catches since they are firm. Practice this for 15 minutes.

Wednesday: Juggle scarves. Since they fall slowly, spend 15 minutes visualizing and practicing a simple pattern, focusing on the timing between throws.

Thursday: Go back to beanbags and try the first three-throw sequence again for 15 minutes.

Friday: Use scarves for 15 minutes. Try juggling two scarves with one hand to build up hand independence.

Saturday: Practice with tennis balls for 15 minutes, focusing on a relaxed grip.

Sunday: Rest. This day is for family juggling time or simply enjoying the activity stress-free.

This schedule mixes up the objects, gives your hands different sensations, and gradually builds up to your goal of teaching your son. Remember, patience and consistency are key.
