Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! Here are a few top picks:

First, there's Ramen Stop. They have amazing tonkotsu and shoyu ramen, and the broth is rich and flavorful. It’s a must-visit for ramen lovers.

Next is Noodle House. They serve a variety of styles like chow mein, Pad See Ew, and Poon Choi, and their dishes are bold and satisfying.

And you should check out Pho Saigon. They have incredible pho, but their noodle dishes like bun bo hue and beef noodle soup are also fantastic.

As for protein bars, if you want something tasty but healthy, here are a few good ones:

There are the classic Quest bars, which are high protein, low sugar, and come in a lot of flavors.

Then there are Optimum Nutrition, which are a great balance of protein and taste.

And finally, there are the popular RXBARs, which are made with simple ingredients and are great for energy.
