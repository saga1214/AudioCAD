Of course. Let me break it down for both the chicken cutlets and the garlic mashed potatoes.

For the main course, the chicken cutlets:
First, two pounds of boneless, skinless chicken breasts will cost between eight and fifteen dollars.
All-purpose flour is three to five dollars for a bag.
Dozen eggs are three to seven dollars.
A container of breadcrumbs is two to four dollars.
And a one-gallon bottle of vegetable oil will be four to eight dollars.

For the side, the garlic mashed potatoes:
A five-pound bag of potatoes is about three to six dollars.
A head of garlic is about a dollar.
A pound of butter is four to seven dollars.
And a half-gallon of milk is three to five dollars.
