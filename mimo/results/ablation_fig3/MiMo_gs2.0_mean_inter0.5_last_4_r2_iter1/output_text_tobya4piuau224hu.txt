Got it. For wedding speeches, a good guideline is to write about one and a half times the standard word count. So for five minutes, that would be around 1,050 words. As for your structure, a simple four-part flow usually works best. You could start with how long you've known her and a short, funny high school memory. Then, talk about watching their relationship grow. After that, express your happiness for them. And finally, end with a simple toast.
