Absolutely! Here’s a step-by-step guide for installing your TV mount, hanging planters, and floating shelves, keeping noise restrictions and landlord rules in mind:

### 📺 **TV Mount Installation**
1. **Measure & Plan**:  
   - Measure your TV’s weight and dimensions.  
   - Mark the desired mounting height (center of screen at eye level when seated).  
   - Use a level to ensure perfect alignment.

2. **Wall Prep**:  
   - **Check for Studs**: Use a stud finder to locate studs (critical for heavy TVs).  
   - **Secure Anchors**: If no studs, drill pilot holes and use heavy-duty anchors (e.g., toggle bolts or molly bolts) rated for your TV’s weight.  
   - **Mark Mount Holes**: Transfer the mount’s screw holes to the wall.

3. **Install Mount**:  
   - Attach the mount to the wall with anchors and screws.  
   - Secure the TV to the mount using its included hardware.  
   - *Tip*: Use a TV wall mount guide for model-specific instructions.

### 🌿 **Hanging Planters Installation**
1. **Choose Mounting Method**:  
   - **Adhesive Hooks**: For lightweight planters (up to 10 lbs), use high-strength adhesive hooks (e.g., 3M Command™ strips).  
   - **Drill-Free Hooks**: Use wall anchors (like plastic or toggle anchors) for heavier planters.  
   - **Avoid Drilling**: Command™ hooks or removable adhesive strips minimize noise.

2. **Install**:  
   - Clean the wall surface with rubbing alcohol before applying adhesives.  
   - Press hooks/strips firmly for 30 seconds. Test weight before adding plants.  
   - Hang planters from hooks using sturdy hangers or chains.

### 📚 **Floating Shelf Installation**
1. **Locate Studs**:  
   - Use a stud finder to pinpoint studs (required for heavy items like books/furniture).  
   - If no studs, drill into drywall and use heavy-duty anchors (e.g., toggle bolts).

2. **Measure & Mark**:  
   - Measure shelf length and mark placement on the wall (spaced evenly).  
   - Use a level to ensure horizontal alignment.

3. **Install**:  
   - Drill pilot holes at your marks.  
   - Attach shelf brackets to the wall with anchors/screws.  
   - Secure the shelf to the brackets.  
   - *Tip*: For glass shelves, use specialized brackets or adhesive mounts.

### 🛠️ **Essential Tools & Safety**
- **Tools**: Stud finder, level, drill, measuring tape, screwdriver.  
- **Anchors**: Plastic anchors (lightweight), toggle bolts (heavyweight), or molly bolts (drywall).  
- **Safety**: Wear safety glasses. Avoid overloading mounts/shelves (check weight limits!).  
- **Noise Control**: Use a cordless drill to minimize noise. Work during daytime to avoid disturbing neighbors.

### 💡 **Pro Tips**
- **Label Hardware**: Take photos as you work to avoid confusion.  
- **Test First**: Install hardware on a spare wall section to test stability.  
- **Decorate Gradually**: Tackle one item per Sunday session to avoid fatigue.  

By following these steps, you’ll create a functional, stylish space without breaking rules! 🎨✨
