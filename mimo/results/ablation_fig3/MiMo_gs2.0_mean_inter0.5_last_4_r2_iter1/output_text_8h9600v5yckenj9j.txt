For candy, you’ll definitely want to get the classics that kids and adults love. Think about the big Halloween staples like Reese’s Cups, Hershey’s Kisses, Skittles, and gummy candies like gummy bears or worms. You could also grab some classic Halloween candies like candy corn, but maybe mix in some chocolate bars like Snickers or Milky Way for variety. If you want something with a bit of a thrill, sour candies like Sour Patch Kids are always a hit. And of course, you can't go wrong with classic M&Ms. Just buy a bunch of different bags and mix them up to avoid having any leftovers.
