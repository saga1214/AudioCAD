For activities on the water, a sunset sail is always romantic. You could suggest a private charter to ensure they have the boat to themselves.

For something more hands-on, a catamaran sailing lesson could be a great way for them to connect, maybe with some delicious local seafood for a picnic afterward.

And for a more relaxed evening, a private dockside dinner after a day of kayaking or paddleboarding would be a wonderful, memorable way to end the day.
