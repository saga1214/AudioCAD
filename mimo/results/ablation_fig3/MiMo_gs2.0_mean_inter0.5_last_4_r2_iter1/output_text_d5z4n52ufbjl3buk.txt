That's a very wise way to approach it. You want to be clear but professional.

Here’s a suggested structure for your email that balances your needs with respect.

First, for the subject line, you could write something like: "Following Up on Exam Feedback - Student so-and-so".

Then, in the body of the email, you could start with a polite greeting and mention that you scored a 64 on the recent essay exam and you'd appreciate any feedback to help you improve for future assignments.

Next, you could mention that because the exam was essay-based, you were wondering if you could understand what contributed to the score. Frame it as a desire to learn, not as a challenge to his judgment.

Finally, ask if he has 10 to 15 minutes during his office hours sometime this week to discuss this briefly. You could also offer to suggest alternative times if needed.

This way, you're being very direct about wanting feedback, but you're doing it in a way that focuses on your growth as a student, which should minimize his ego.
