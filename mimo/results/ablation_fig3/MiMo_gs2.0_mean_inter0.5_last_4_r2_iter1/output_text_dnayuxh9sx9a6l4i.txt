Got some options for dog care! First, Labrador Retrievers—they’re friendly, trainable, and great with kids, but big. Next, French Bulldogs: smaller, less shedding, and chill. Another idea is a Cocker Spaniel, which can be sweet and social but might bark more. You could also look into Bluetick Coonhounds; they’re smaller, not too noisy alone, and social. Another chill dog: the Basset Hound—low energy, gets along with others. Golden Retrievers are also big but fit the criteria, so size is a factor. Lastly, consider mixes like a French Bulldog and Pug mix, or a Cocker Spaniel and Lab mix—these could blend your purebred and rescue goals.
