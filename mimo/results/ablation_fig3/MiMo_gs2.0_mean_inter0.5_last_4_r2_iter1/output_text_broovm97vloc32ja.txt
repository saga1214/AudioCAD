That's a great question. While publishers do accept manuscripts with illustrations, it's generally not the recommended first step. The main reason is that illustrations are the author's and the illustrator's separate art. The publisher's art director will then create their *own* unique illustrations for the book, based on the text. This ensures a consistent style for the series and gives the publisher full creative control.

However, if you are the illustrator as well, there are ways to leverage that skill. You could submit a 'manuscript-only' query, which is just the text and query. Once an agent sells it to a publisher, they will then ask for your illustrations. Another option is to find a publisher who specifically seeks out 'author-illustrator' pairings, which are quite popular these days. They often have guidelines for these types of submissions.
