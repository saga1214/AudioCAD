Here's a full recap of tasty treats for your brown-and-white princess:

### 🧁 Homemade Delights
1. **Frozen Bites**:  
   - Mashed **pumpkin** or **sweet potato** patted into small, flat discs and frozen solid.  
   - *Bonus*: Add banana or blueberry for extra flavor!  
2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**:  
   - Mix peanut butter (NO xylitol!) with rolled oats.  
   - Roll into small balls, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10–12 minutes.  
3. **Healthy Chews**:  
   - Small **cooked chicken** or **carrot sticks** (steamed first for softness).  
   - **Freeze-dried liver** or **zucchini** (cut into rings).

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands
- **Training Treats**:  
  - **Zuke’s Mini Naturals** (small, grain-free).  
  - **Greenies Dog Bites** (dental + tasty).  
- **Snacks**:  
  - **Blue Buffalo** Training Bites.  
  - **Whisk** Protein Bites (high-protein).  

### 🎨 Collar Color Ideas
For a **brown-and-white coat**, colors that **POP**:  
- **Teal/Turquoise**: Contrasts beautifully.  
- **Pink/Rose Gold**: Soft and feminine.  
- **Royal Blue**: Vibrant against warm brown.  
- **Black/Leather**: Classic and sleek.  

### 📌 Key Tips
- **Safety First**: Avoid xylitol (in peanut butter, gum, etc.), chocolate, grapes, and onions.  
- **Portion Control**: Corgis are compact—stick to tiny pieces!  
- **Training**: Use treats during "sit" commands (pair with praise!).  
- **Potty Training**: Take her outside on a schedule. Reward successes *every time*!
