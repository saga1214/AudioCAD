Okay, since you want low-carb and high-protein, I've got a few great restaurant ideas for you. If you're in the mood for Italian, you could check out Olive Garden. They have great options like grilled salmon with a lemon-herb sauce, or you could even get chicken Parmesan. If you're feeling more like a steakhouse, Outback has a good sirloin with garlic butter. For something quick and easy, maybe a Chipotle burrito bowl, just make sure you get it with double meat and no beans. And for a burger joint, Five Guys is a solid choice, just get it with no bun and extra bacon. Enjoy your meal!
