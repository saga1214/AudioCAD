For your stir-fry noodles, here's the secret: don't overcook them. Stir-fry noodles are meant to be chewy. The key is to cook them just until they are 'al dente,' which means they are tender but still have a firm bite. You can check them about a minute before they're done. Then, as soon as you drain them, immediately put them back into the hot wok or large pan you used for the meat.

The residual heat from the pan will finish cooking them perfectly and keep them at that ideal, chewy texture.
