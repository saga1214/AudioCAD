Here are five fantastic road trip ideas from Vancouver. First, you could head to Victoria and the Gulf Islands for some beautiful beaches and a charming city. Second, the Vancouver Island West Coast is stunning, with places like Tofino for surfing and hiking. Third, for a classic drive, you could go to the Okanagan Valley to see the lakes and the famous wineries. Fourth, if you want mountains and icefields, the Fraser and Kootenay parks are breathtaking. And fifth, for a quick and easy trip, you could explore the islands in the Salish Sea.

Each trip offers something different, from beaches and wine to mountains and whale watching. You can't go wrong with any of them!
