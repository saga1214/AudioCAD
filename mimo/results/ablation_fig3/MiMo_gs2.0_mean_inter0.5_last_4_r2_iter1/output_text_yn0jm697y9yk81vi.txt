Based on your project needs and Indianapolis availability, here's a consolidated list of my best recommendations:

### Cutting Tools
1. **Aviation Snips**  
   *Best for:* Curves/thinner sheets (< 16 gauge)  
   *Where to buy:* The Home Depot, Lowe's  
2. **Angle Grinder with Cut-off Wheel**  
   *Best for:* Thicker sheets or straight cuts  
   *Where to buy:* Tractor Supply Company, Ace Hardware  
3. **Jigsaw w/Metal Cutting Blade**  
   *Best for:* Curves/thinner sheets  
   *Where to buy:* Menards, Tractor Supply Company  

### Drywall Hardware
4. **1-1/4" Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws**  
   *Why better:* Prevents nail pops, self-drilling, holds better in wood  
   *Where to buy:* All big-box stores (Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards, Tractor Supply, Ace)  

### Insulation
5. **Fiberglass Batts (Pre-cut for studs)**  
   *Best for:* Standard walls  
   *Where to buy:* Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards  
6. **Mineral Wool (Rigid or Rolls)**  
   *Best for:* Soundproofing/fireproofing  
   *Where to buy:* Tractor Supply Company, Ace Hardware  

### Indianapolis Store Priorities
- **Best Overall Selection:** **The Home Depot**  
  (Comprehensive tool/material inventory in one place)  
- **Alternative Options:**  
  - **Lowe's or Menards** (Competitive pricing, especially for insulation)
