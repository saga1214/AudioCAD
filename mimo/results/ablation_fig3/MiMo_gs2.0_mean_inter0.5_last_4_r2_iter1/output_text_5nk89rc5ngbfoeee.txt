For an end-of-year celebration, a **park BBQ** is a fantastic idea—it’s fun, inclusive, and easy to scale. Here’s how to make it work smoothly:  

### **Logistics & Setup**  
- **Venue**: Choose a park with picnic tables/fields (weather permitting). Have a backup plan (e.g., cafeteria if rain) and a tent for shade.  
- **Food**:  
  - **Parents help**: Assign shifts for grilling (pizza, hot dogs, veggie burgers), setting up a **sundaes bar** (ice cream + toppings), or preparing **ice cream sundaes** (if some parents provide frozen treats).  
  - **Kid-friendly options**: Include pizza, chips, fruit skewers, and veggie sticks. Avoid dairy-heavy snacks since you need to avoid it.  
- **Cost**: Parents cover the cost—ask for a small fee ($10–$15 per family) to cover supplies.  

### **Activities to Keep It Fun**  
- **Classic Brownie Games**: Set up a **wheelbarrow race**, three-legged race, or sack races. Use cones/tarps for obstacle courses.  
- **Badge Celebration**: Display earned badges on a banner. Have girls share favorite memories.  
- **Ice Cream Sundaes**: Let kids customize sundaes with sprinkles, candy, or fruit.  
- **Simple Crafts**: Provide paper plates to decorate with paint or markers.  

### **Why This Works**  
- **Low Effort**: Parents handle food/cleanup—focus on supervision.  
- **High Energy**: Active games keep kids engaged.  
- **Nostalgia**: Taps into your fond memories of Brownie parties.  

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Send a sign-up sheet to parents 2 weeks ahead for food duties. Include a note: *"Let’s make this a memorable celebration—your help ensures everyone has fun!"*  

You’ll do great—your enthusiasm and planning will set a great example for your troop! 🎉
