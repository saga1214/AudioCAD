Okay, pirates at the beach! You need sand-friendly, themed, allergy-safe nautical food. Here's a full menu plan based on our chat.

For the main course, mini slider bars cut into small rolls are perfect. Theme them as “buried treasure sandwiches” using small treasure chest containers. To avoid watermelon, you can use ham, cheese, and tomato, but check the allergy first. Serve them with themed toothpicks.

For a fruit option, grapes or apple slices labeled as pirate jewels are a good choice.

As a fun side, you could pack pirate ship masts, which are just pretzel rods.

For drinks, themed juice boxes named things like pirate punch or sea water will work great.

This plan should keep everything neat and on-theme for your son!
