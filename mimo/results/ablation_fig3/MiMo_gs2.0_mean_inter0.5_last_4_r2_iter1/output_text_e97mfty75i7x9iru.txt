I understand. Let me break down the advice for you. 

First, the saying is "feed a cold, starve a fever."

Then, for general advice when you're sick: drink plenty of fluids and eat what you can to keep your strength up. Antibiotics aren't prescribed for the flu because it's a virus.

For the flu specifically, rest is key. Over-the-counter medicines can help with the fever and body aches, and steam from a shower or humidifier can help with a cough.

And for your cough specifically: a spoonful of honey, gargling with warm salt water, and propping your head up with an extra pillow at night can all help.

And for the brain fog you mentioned: it can often be helped by staying hydrated, getting plenty of rest, and maybe some gentle exercise when you feel up to it. But most importantly, listen to your body and don't push yourself too hard.
