### 1. **Color-Coding Icons/Folders**  
   - **Memory vs. Mental Load**: Color-coding primarily aids **visual memory** (e.g., red = urgent tasks, blue = creative projects). It reduces "mental load" by creating instant visual cues—so you don’t need to actively recall folder names.
