Okay, so you want to surprise your daughter with a new console for some good old-fashioned video gaming. That's a really sweet idea.

Based on what's popular right now, here are five great gaming consoles you could get her.

First, there's the Nintendo Switch. It's super versatile because it can play games on the TV or on the go. It's perfect for family games like Mario Kart, Zelda, and Animal Crossing.

Next, you have the PlayStation 5. It's a great choice for more action-adventure games and racing games, like the Gran Turismo or the new God of War series.

Then there's the Xbox Series X or S. It's another strong choice with great racing games like Forza and popular adventure games like Halo and The Elder Scrolls.

There's also the Nintendo Switch Lite. It's a smaller, handheld-only version of the Switch, which is great for playing on the go.

And finally, there's the Nintendo 3DS. It's a bit older but still has a lot of classic games, like Pokémon and Animal Crossing.

These are all great options for playing together, so you can't go wrong with any of them.
