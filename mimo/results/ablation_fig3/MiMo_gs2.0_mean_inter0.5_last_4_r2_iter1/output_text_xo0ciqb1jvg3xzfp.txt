That's a good question. It was happy for the Pilgrims in a short-term way, but not for the Wampanoag in the same way.

For the Pilgrims, it was a celebration of survival and a new political alliance.

For some of the Wampanoag, it was a diplomatic event. But for many others, it meant the arrival of people who brought disease and would eventually take their land. So it wasn't a universally happy moment for them.

The key is to remember it was just one event in a much larger story of both cooperation and conflict.
