Okay Will, here is a list of 12 90s movies perfect for your party night, all from the 1990s.

1. *The Matrix* (1999) - For sci-fi fans.
2. *Toy Story* (1995) - The first animated feature to really break through.
3. *The Shawshank Redemption* (1994) - A classic drama.
4. *Fight Club* (1999) - A cult favorite for its unique style.
5. *The Big Lebowski* (1998) - A quirky, hilarious comedy.
6. *Good Will Hunting* (1997) - A really moving story.
7. *The Silence of the Lambs* (1991) - An intense thriller.
8. *Star Wars: Episode I - The Phantom Menace* (1999) - A big sci-fi spectacle.
9. *The Crow* (1994) - A dark, stylish fantasy.
10. *Singles* (1992) - A romantic comedy.
11. *Thelma & Louise* (1991) - A dynamic female-led drama.
12. *Donnie Darko* (2001) - This one is from the very early 2000s, but it has a huge 90s vibe and is often considered a late 90s film. It's a great choice if you want something a bit more complex.

Enjoy your themed night!
