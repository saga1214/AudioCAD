Alright, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time for that would be, uh, from late October to early December. That’s their autumn season, and it’s when you get those peak colors in the forests around places like Chiapas and Oaxaca. The weather is mild, perfect for painting.

If you’re really set on Mexico City, you could go anytime from spring to fall. It’s always a good time for city scenes. But for those classic autumn colors, definitely aim for late October to November. That’s when you’ll see the best light and the most vibrant foliage. So, if you can swing it, October is the ideal time. You’ll get the best of both worlds—autumn colors and great light for painting. Enjoy your trip and your new techniques!
