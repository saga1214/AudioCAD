Great question! The best tool depends on your instrument, but here are the top options for tuning strings while waiting:

### 1. **Clip-On Tuner** (Best for Precision)
   - **How to use**: Clip it near the bridge of your instrument (guitar, ukulele, etc.).  
   - **Pros**: Electronic sensors detect vibrations accurately.  
   - **Cons**: Requires batteries.  
   - **Tip**: Place it close to the bridge but *not* touching strings to avoid interference.

### 2. **Tuner App** (Most Accessible)
   - **Apps to try**:  
     - GuitarTuna  
     - Fender Tune  
     - Tuner Lite  
   - **How to use**:  
     1. Place your phone near the strings.  
     2. Press "Start Tuning."  
     3. Play a string (e.g., the 6th string on guitar).  
     4. Adjust until the app shows **green** (perfect pitch).  
   - **Tip**: Mute other strings to avoid noise.

### 3. **Electronic Tuner** (Handheld)
   - **How to use**: Place the sensor near strings and press buttons for each string.  
   - **Best for**: Guitarists/ukulele players who prefer tactile feedback.

### What to Do While Tuning:
- **Start with the thickest string** (6th on guitar) and work down to the thinnest (1st).  
- **Tune *up* to the note** (e.g., if low, gently tighten the string).  
- **Listen for a "clean" bell-like tone**—avoid buzzing or flatness.  

### Quick Troubleshooting:
- **App shows red?** The string is too low—tighten it slightly.  
- **App is noisy?** Move away from humming appliances (fridges, fans).  
- **Strings won’t stay in tune?** Check for loose tuning pegs or worn strings.
