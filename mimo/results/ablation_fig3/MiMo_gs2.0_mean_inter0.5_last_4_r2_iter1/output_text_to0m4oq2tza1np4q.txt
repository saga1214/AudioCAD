Absolutely! Pickles and pickle juice are versatile for healthy cooking. Here are a few ideas tailored to your goals:

### 1. **Pickle Juice Vinaigrette**  
   - Mix **3 tbsp pickle juice**, **1 tbsp olive oil**, **1 tsp Dijon mustard**, **1 tsp honey**, **salt**, and **pepper**.  
   - *Great for:* Drizzling over the salmon after cooking, or tossing with spinach and cherry tomatoes for a salad.

### 2. **Pickle Relish Dip**  
   - Blend **½ cup pickle juice**, **¼ cup Greek yogurt**, **1 tbsp lemon zest**, and **1 minced garlic clove**.  
   - *Great for:* A refreshing dip with cucumber slices, bell peppers, or your leftover spinach/tomato salad.

### 3. **Pickled Veggie Snacks**  
   - Thinly slice cucumbers, carrots, or radishes. Quick-pickle them in a jar with **1:3 pickle juice to water**, **salt**, and **dill**. Refrigerate for 30 mins.  
   - *Great for:* Low-sodium, crunchy snacks post-run!

### 4. **Pickled Salmon Bowl**  
   - After marinating the salmon, let it sit in the pickle juice for 10 minutes before grilling. Serve atop greens with cherry tomatoes and a dollop of Greek yogurt.  
   - *Bonus:* The juice adds tang without extra salt!

### ⚠️ Salt Note:  
Since you’re sodium-conscious, use pickle juice sparingly (it’s salty!) and rinse pickles before adding to salads if needed. Focus on **olive oil, lemon, herbs, and garlic** in marinades/dressings for heart-healthy flavor.
