Let’s estimate the drywall items first. A tub of drywall compound is about five to ten dollars. Sandpaper or sponges are around five. A six-inch putty knife is about eight to twelve dollars. Primer and paint depend on the color, but let’s say ten to fifteen. Nails or adhesive, around five. And if a replacement molding piece, maybe ten to twenty dollars. So for the drywall task, let’s budget about forty to seventy dollars.

For the cake, flour, sugar, baking powder, butter, eggs, milk, extract, frosting ingredients, pans, and decorations, that’s probably going to be around thirty to fifty dollars.

So, let’s say you bring about seventy to seventy-five for drywall and thirty to fifty for cake. That means you’re looking at a total of about one hundred to one hundred and twenty-five dollars. That should be a good starting point for your trip. Let me know if you want any adjustments!
