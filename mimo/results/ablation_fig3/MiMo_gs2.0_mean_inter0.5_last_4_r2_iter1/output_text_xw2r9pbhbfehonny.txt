Sure, before you film your first YouTube-style performance, here’s a quick checklist.

First, get permission from the facility, like Sunnyside. They might not mind volunteer performances, but they may not be happy about recording or posting it publicly.

Next, just set up your phone to record in high quality. Make sure you have good lighting, so a table or something bright to shine on your puppets. Keep it simple at first—just focus on the performance itself.

After, think about editing. You don’t have to do anything fancy, but you can trim the beginning and end, maybe add some fun text or simple music. There are apps like CapCut or iMovie that are easy to use.

Then, create a kid-friendly title and thumbnail—something colorful and fun that’ll make people want to watch.

Finally, just have fun with it. You’ve got the talent, so just film it, share it, and see what happens. Good luck.
