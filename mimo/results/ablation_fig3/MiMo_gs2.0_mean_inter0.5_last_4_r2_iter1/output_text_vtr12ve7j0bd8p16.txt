Leo and Sagittarius is indeed a classic power couple in astrology. Your shared fire sign energy means you're both natural-born adventurers. For a weekend trip near Calgary, you have some great options.

One of the most popular and romantic choices is driving out to Banff or Lake Louise. It's a bit of a drive, maybe two and a half hours, but the scenery is incredible. You could take a scenic drive, have a nice dinner, and then stay in a nice lodge or even a cozy cabin in the woods.

If you want something closer to the city, you could try the Kananaskis Country area. It's often called Little Banff and has beautiful hiking trails, the Kananaskis River, and the famous Kananaskis Lake. You could rent kayaks or paddleboards there.

Another idea is the Elk Valley, which is a bit further east. It's a quieter, more rustic area with charming towns like Banff and Canmore, plus amazing hiking and a chance to see wildlife.

And if you're really feeling adventurous, you could try a hot air balloon ride over the Calgary foothills. It's a unique and memorable experience, especially at sunrise.
