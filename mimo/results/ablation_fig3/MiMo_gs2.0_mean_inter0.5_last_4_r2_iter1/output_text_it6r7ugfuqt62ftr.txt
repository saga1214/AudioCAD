Okay, since Northern California warming up is your goal and swimming is a priority, I have a perfect four-to-five day vacation plan for you. It's in Southern California, specifically centered around San Diego, which has consistently mild weather and plenty of places to swim year-round.

Here’s a day-by-day breakdown:

**Day 1: San Diego Arrival & Pacific Beach**
*   **Stay:** I'd recommend The Westin Gaslamp Union Square. It's centrally located, has a full-service restaurant, and is just a short walk to the beach.
*   **Eat:** For dinner, go to **Born & Raised**, which is a high-end restaurant with a fantastic gluten-free menu that they prepare with care.
*   **Do:** You can check into your hotel, then head down to Pacific Beach for some time in the sun and a swim at Mission Beach. For the evening, you can take a walk on the boardwalk and enjoy some street food, like from the Fish Market for gluten-free options.

**Day 2: San Diego & Coronado**
*   **Travel:** Take the free trolley or an Uber to the Coronado Bridge, then walk across it for amazing views of the bay. From there, it's a short walk to Hotel del Coronado. You can take a guided tour or just relax by the pool.
*   **Eat:** For lunch, try **The Taco Stand** on Coronado Island for fantastic, safe gluten-free tacos.
*   **Do:** After lunch, you can take a short walk to Coronado Beach and swim, or relax by the Hotel del's famous pool. In the evening, you can head back to the Gaslamp area for dinner. A great upscale option is **Ironside Seafood Kitchen**.

**Day 3: Big Sur Scenic Drive**
*   **Travel:** Drive south on Highway 1. It's a scenic four to five-hour drive, so plan for a late morning departure. Pack a picnic lunch.
*   **Stay:** I'd suggest an overnight in **Big Sur**. A great choice is **Nepenthe**, which has stunning ocean views, a full restaurant with a dedicated gluten-free menu, and is perfect for celebrating your daughter's birthday. You can even arrange a gluten-free cake.
*   **Eat:** For dinner, you can enjoy a special meal at Nepenthe. For breakfast, the hotel's restaurant also has safe gluten-free options.
*   **Do:** Spend the afternoon exploring Big Sur. You can walk along the Nepenthe Cliffs Trail, visit McWay Falls, and drive the scenic road.

**Day 4: Monterey & Pescadero**
*   **Travel:** Drive south back towards Monterey, which takes about two and a half hours.
*   **Stay:** For the night, I recommend **The Inn at Pescadero**. It's a beautiful, secluded spot with a lovely restaurant called **The Inn Restaurant**, which has a dedicated gluten-free chef and an incredible ambiance.
*   **Eat:** You can have a lovely dinner at The Inn Restaurant. For breakfast the next morning, they also have excellent gluten-free options.
*   **Do:** Spend the afternoon exploring Pescadero. You can walk the coastal trails at **Tomales Point** or visit the **Pigeon Point Lighthouse**. It's a great spot for a walk and photos.

**Day 5: Return to San Francisco**
*   **Travel:** Drive back towards San Francisco, which takes about three and a half hours. You can stop in Santa Cruz for a quick swim at Natural Bridges State Beach if you have time and energy.
*   **Stay:** Back in San Francisco, you could stay at the **Four Seasons** in Union Square for a luxurious final night.
*   **Eat:** For your last dinner, you could go to **Coi** in San Francisco, which is a Michelin-starred restaurant with excellent gluten-free options that they handle with care.
*   **Do:** In the morning, you can do a final walk around the city, visit a museum like the de Young or the California Academy of Sciences, or grab a coffee and pastry at a dedicated gluten-free bakery like **Glutino's**.

**Key things to remember for packing:**

*   **Bathing Suit:** Definitely pack your favorite one!
*   **Cover-Up:** A versatile sarong or wrap is great for being quick and easy.
*   **Light Clothing:** Pack lots of light, comfortable clothes like shorts, tank tops, sundresses, and flip-flops or sandals.
*   **Light Jacket:** A light sweater or windbreaker is perfect for cooler evenings, even in the south.
*   **Sunscreen, Sunglasses, and a Hat:** Essential for sun protection.
*   **Gluten-Free Documentation:** Since your daughter has Celiac, it's a good idea to have a doctor's note or a card from a support group like the Celiac Disease Foundation.
*   **Reusable Water Bottle:** Very helpful for staying hydrated.

This itinerary gives you a mix of relaxation, adventure, incredible scenery, and world-class dining, all while focusing on warm weather and your daughter's dietary needs. I hope you all have an amazing trip!
