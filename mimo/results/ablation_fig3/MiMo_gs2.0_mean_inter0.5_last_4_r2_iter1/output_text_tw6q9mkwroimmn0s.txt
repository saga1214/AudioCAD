That sounds like a fantastic theme! Here are 5 ocean-inspired meals (considering shellfish allergy, diabetes-friendly options, love for mango, and spice) and 5 sweet desserts:

### 🍤 Ocean-Themed Mains
1. **Spicy Mango & Blackened Shrimp Stir-Fry**  
   *(Substitute shrimp with **tofu** for shellfish-free)*  
   Toss shrimp/tofu with bell peppers, snap peas, and a sauce of mango puree, lime, ginger, and chili flakes. Serve over brown rice. *Diabetes-friendly: Low-carb, high-protein.*

2. **Coconut Curry with Tuna & Mango**  
   Simmer tuna steaks in a rich coconut-mango curry with spinach and potatoes. Top with a chili-lime dusting. *Diabetes-friendly: Lean protein, fiber-rich veggies.*

3. **Mango-Glazed Salmon with Mango Salsa**  
   Grill salmon with a mango-honey glaze. Serve with a fresh salsa of diced mango, red onion, cilantro, and jalapeño. *Diabetes-friendly: Omega-3s, low-GI mango.*

4. **Spicy Tuna & Avocado Ceviche (Shellfish-Free)**  
   Mix fresh tuna with lime juice, diced mango, avocado, red onion, and cilantro. Add a pinch of cayenne. *Diabetes-friendly: Healthy fats, moderate carbs.*

5. **Mango-Coconut Chia Pudding Parfait**  
   *(Dessert!) Layer chia pudding (made with coconut milk) with mango puree and toasted coconut flakes. Add a dusting of cinnamon or chili powder for spice.*  
   *Diabetes-friendly: High fiber, low glycemic.*

### 🍹 Ocean-Themed Desserts
1. **Mango & Coconut Cheesecake**  
   Use a graham cracker crust, Greek yogurt filling, and fresh mango topping. *Diabetes-friendly: Low-sugar, high-protein.*

2. **Spicy Dark Chocolate & Sea Salt Tart**  
   Dark chocolate base with sea salt, topped with mango compote. *Diabetes-friendly: Low-GI chocolate, fiber-rich mango.*

3. **Mango Sorbet with Lime Zest**  
   Fresh, creamy, and subtly spicy with lime. Serve with a sprinkle of coconut flakes. *Diabetes-friendly: Low-glycemic sorbet.*

4. **Mango Sticky Rice with Coconut Milk**  
   Warm rice dipped in coconut milk, topped with fresh mango and a pinch of chili powder. *Diabetes-friendly: Low-GI rice, healthy fats.*

5. **Grilled Pineapple "Shrimp" Skewers**  
   Marinate pineapple chunks in lime, honey, and chili powder. Grill and serve with coconut yogurt dip. *Diabetes-friendly: Low-sugar fruit, protein-rich dip.*

### 💡 Tips:
- **Spice Control:** Adjust chili levels to your preference.  
- **Diabetes Focus:** Prioritize lean proteins, fiber, and low-GI carbs (mango, quinoa, brown rice).  
- **Theme Touch:** Use blue/green decor, ocean-inspired tableware, and serve drinks like coconut water or cucumber-mint mocktails.  

Enjoy your romantic ocean-themed feast! 🌊✨
