Okay, you want to expand on that crisp, refreshing appreciation table theme. Let's build on it. For the drinks, besides infused water and tea, you could also do sparkling water with a splash of fruit juice or maybe some chilled coconut water for a real hydrating touch. For snacks, think light and fresh again—maybe some veggie sticks with hummus, yogurt parfaits, or even small chia seed pudding cups.

For the decorations, to keep that bright and easy-clean feeling, a linen tablecloth in a light color like blue or white would be perfect. You could hang some little balloons or ribbons in a fun color. And for thank-you notes, maybe use dry-erase markers on a whiteboard for some fun, temporary messages.

Breath mints are a great idea for a quick refresh. Something like mint or citrus-flavored would fit the theme. Palet cleansers could be simple citrus-flavored gum or even just a little bowl of dried citrus slices.

And for the badges, definitely print new ones for appreciation weekend! It makes everyone feel special and organized. You could even add a fun little dog paw print or a heart on them.

For scent pairings that go with citrus and chocolate, maybe some lavender or eucalyptus essential oil in a small diffuser, or even just a basket of fresh oranges. That citrus smell is super uplifting!

So basically: light drinks, fresh snacks, simple decorations, breath mints, new badges, and a little fresh scent. How does that all sound?
