You've got most of it, but you're missing a few key things. 

The most important thing you're missing is wall anchors. Habitat restore cabinets usually come with these plastic anchors to screw into the wall studs. You absolutely need them for safety, because just screwing directly into wood without a stud isn't secure at all.

You'll also want a measuring tape to make sure the distance between cabinets is right, and a pencil to mark their positions. And make sure you have a couple of clamps, like C-clamps, to hold adjacent cabinets flush while you're screwing them together.

So, to recap, you'll need: wall anchors, a measuring tape, a pencil, clamps, and a drill or screwdriver that can drive those anchors. You should be all set then!
