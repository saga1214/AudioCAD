Alright, budget is a key factor. For drinks, water is obviously the best, but you could set up a lemonade or fruit-infused water station. That's pretty cheap to make. For snacks, you could have trail mix made with unsalted nuts and dried fruit, or rice cakes with natural nut butter. Homemade energy bites are another great, budget-friendly option. You could also do small portions of granola bars or even make your own ice cream sundaes with a few toppings to keep it fun.
