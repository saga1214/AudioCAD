Regarding the brain hemispheres: The left-brain/creative and right-brain/logical dichotomy is a myth. The brain is highly interconnected, and creativity and logic aren't strictly separate. But, some people do tend to rely more on certain functions. If you like both structure and creativity, you might have a balanced brain. 

For tinnitus, it’s almost always physiological. Try protecting your hearing and consulting a specialist if it persists.

Now, for a career that balances objective measurement and creativity, consider these paths: 
First, UX/UI design. It’s about structuring user experiences based on measurable data, but it’s also very creative. 
Second, technical illustration or data visualization, which turns complex data into beautiful, understandable visuals. 
Third, architecture or engineering, which blends creativity with concrete, measurable structures. 
And fourth, scientific illustration, which merges art with scientific accuracy. 

These fields offer structure and creativity, and you won’t be stuck at a desk all day. Grab that second cup and let those ideas swirl around in your mind!
