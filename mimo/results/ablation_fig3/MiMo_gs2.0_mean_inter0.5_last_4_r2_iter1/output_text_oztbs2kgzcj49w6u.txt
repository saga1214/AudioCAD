Ah, that spy story really did pique your interest, didn't it? I can certainly help you find ways to explore Lockport's deep connection to the Erie Canal.

The best place to start would be the Lockport Historical Society. They have a wealth of local history, including photos and documents that would confirm that interesting World War Two story. They also have the Lockport Canal Museum, which is right inside the locks themselves and gives you a hands-on understanding of how they worked.

For a more immersive experience, you could take a boat tour on the Lockport Water Taxi. They offer special tours that go right through the locks, and their guides are great at telling all the stories, including the spy rumors. You could also visit the Buffalo and Erie Canal Museum, which is also in Lockport, and see the replica of a canal boat that was used to carry timber and stone for the locks.
