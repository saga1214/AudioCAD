Okay! Here’s a full list of your cocktail recipes with all the measurements and instructions. I've also added finger food ideas to tie into Kentucky fall!

First, Old Fashioned: You'll need two ounces of bourbon or rye, one sugar cube or half an ounce of simple syrup, three dashes of Angostura bitters, ice, and an orange twist for garnish. The instructions are to muddle the sugar cube with a little bitters, add the rest of the bourbon and ice, stir it well, and then serve in a rocks glass with the cherry or orange peel.

Paper Plane: Equal parts bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and fresh lemon juice. So, one ounce each of bourbon, Aperol, and Nonino, plus one ounce of fresh lemon juice. The instructions are to shake all that with ice, strain it into a coupe glass, and garnish with a cherry.

For the Whiskey Sour with or without egg white: You'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup. For the egg white option, you just add one egg white to the shaker. The dry shake I mentioned first, then add the ice, shake again, and strain into a rocks glass. Garnish with a cherry or orange slice.

And for the third gin cocktail, I recommend the French 75. For that, you'll need one and a half ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and about three ounces of sparkling wine. You dry shake the gin, lemon, and syrup, then add ice and shake again. Top it with the sparkling wine and garnish with a lemon twist.

For finger foods, I’d suggest some charcuterie with cheeses and crackers, mini pumpkin or sausage rolls since it’s fall, and maybe some bourbon-spiced nuts or brown butter popcorn to tie into Kentucky. You could also do mini sliders with pickles and bacon.
