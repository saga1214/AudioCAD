Strategy idea number one is to request a formal performance review meeting. You can frame it as a chance to discuss your progress and contributions, which makes the conversation more professional.

Strategy idea number two is to bring it up informally during a regular face-to-face meeting. You could say something to the effect of, 'I’ve been reflecting on my contributions and growth since joining the team, and I wanted to check in on how my performance aligns with expectations moving forward.' This keeps the conversation more conversational and less confrontational.
