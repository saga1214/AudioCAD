Okay, so you're planning a road trip from Joshua Tree down to Nevada, maybe combining some desert camping with a little Vegas at the end. That’s a cool plan! If you want a proper Vegas experience after camping, here’s what I’d suggest. 

First, book a nice hotel—maybe something with a pool, like the Bellagio or the Venetian, to relax after a day in the van. A hot shower is definitely in order. Then, you could hit up a few classic Vegas spots: see a show, maybe at Cirque du Soleil or a comedy club; grab dinner at a fancy restaurant; and if you’re both into it, hit the casino floor for some low-stakes fun. Vegas is all about the energy, so just follow your vibe.
