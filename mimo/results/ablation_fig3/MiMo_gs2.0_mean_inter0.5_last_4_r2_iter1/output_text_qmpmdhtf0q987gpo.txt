Okay, sounds like you're fully prepped and ready to prime. Before you do that, just double-check that the sanded cabinets are completely clean and dust-free. A little bit of dust on the primer will make it peel or chip later. Also, make sure your primer is the right kind for painted surfaces and that you're working in a well-ventilated area. Once that primer is on, you're basically ready for paint. Good luck dealing with neighbors *and* getting your kitchen painted!
