Right, let’s circle back to those exotic movie night snacks since you’re feeling inspired now. Building on your love for umami and unique flavors, here are some more adventurous options. You could do a takoyaki station—those little octopus balls from Osaka are crispy outside and molten inside, perfect for dipping. Or a yuzu-flavored popcorn mix for a citrusy twist.

For something with a bit of an edge, try wasabi peas or spicy eggplant chips. Another great idea is a mini omurice bar where guests can customize their fried rice bowls with omelets, veggies, and sauces. 

And since you liked sweet-sour Japanese snacks, you could serve some sakura mochi, which are chewy rice cakes with a cherry flavor, or even some matcha-flavored melon pan for a sweet bread that looks like a melon. This menu will definitely impress your foodie friends.
