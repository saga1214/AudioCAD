### 🎣 Snowshoeing  
- **Gear Needed**:  
  - Snowshoes (rent or buy used first).  
  - Waterproof boots with crampons or microspikes for grip.  
  - Warm layers, gloves, and a hat.  
  - A map or GPS (snowshoes can cover you in unfamiliar areas).  
- **Tip**: Start on flat, groomed trails (like parks or ski resorts) to practice balance.  

### ⛷️ Cross-Country Skiing  
- **Gear**:  
  - Skis, poles, and boots (rental shops offer gear + lessons).  
  - Lightweight layers (it’s surprisingly warm!).  
- **Tip**: Try "classic" (traditional) or "Nordic" (scrambling) styles—both are great for fitness.  

### 🚴 Winter Hiking  
- **Gear**:  
  - Sturdy boots, microspikes/crampons.  
  - Snow goggles (to reduce glare).  
  - Trekking poles for stability.  
- **Tip**: Stick to marked trails and check avalanche forecasts if hiking in mountains.  

### 🛷 Snowmobiling  
- **Gear**:  
  - Helmet, goggles, gloves, and waterproof pants.  
  - A trail map (or app like **SnowMobiler**).  
- **Tip**: Start on groomed trails—don’t venture off-road alone until you’re experienced.  

### ❄️ Ice Skating  
- **Gear**:  
  - Ice skates (rent at rinks or outdoor rinks).  
  - Warm layers and a hat.  
- **Tip**: Wear water-repellent gloves—ice water is shockingly cold!

- **Gear**:  
  - Helmet, goggles, gloves, and waterproof pants.  
  - A trail map (or app like **SnowMobiler**).  
- **Tip**: Start on groomed trails—don’t venture off-road alone until you’re experienced.  

### ❄️ Winter Camping  
- **Gear**:  
  - Insulated sleeping bag (rated for below freezing).  
  - Tent with a windbreak.  
  - Hot water bottle or hand-warmer.  
- **Tip**: Check local regulations—some areas require permits for winter camping.
