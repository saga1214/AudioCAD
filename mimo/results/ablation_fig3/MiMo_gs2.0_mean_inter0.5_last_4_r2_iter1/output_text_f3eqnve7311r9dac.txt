Yes, older comics are often more scarce because of the lower printing numbers and poor preservation. For Luke Cage collectors, here are ten significant books from the 1940s and 50s that are within your five-hundred-dollar budget.

For Luke and Defenders related books, you can get a copy of *Hero for Hire* number one and *Power Man and Iron Fist* number fifty fairly graded. You can also find *The Defenders* number one from 1964. A later key issue, *New Avengers* number one from 2012, is also a smart buy.

For other solid investments under five hundred dollars, you have *Amazing Spider-Man* three hundred, mentioned before. Also, *Savage She-Hulk* number one. For a modern choice, *Ultimate Fallout* number four introduced Miles Morales and is always in high demand. And one more to consider is *Ms. Marvel* number one from 2014, which is the first issue for Kamala Khan. These are all excellent, affordable key issues.
