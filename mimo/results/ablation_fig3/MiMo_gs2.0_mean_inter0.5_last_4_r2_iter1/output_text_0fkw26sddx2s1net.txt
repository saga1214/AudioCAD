Of course! Here’s a shopping list I put together. For the starter, miso soup will need miso paste, a little bit of mirin, and kombu. Cucumber salad will need cucumbers, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and soy sauce. 

For the main course, chicken teriyaki requires chicken breasts, mirin, sake, sugar, and soy sauce. For the jasmine rice, just jasmine rice and water. And for the stir-fry, bell peppers, snap peas, and carrots, plus some vegetable oil and soy sauce.

For dessert, matcha ice cream needs matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and salt. Mango sticky rice requires sticky rice, coconut milk, sugar, salt, fresh mango, and more coconut milk for the sauce.

Oh, and don't forget a small container for the kombu to keep it fresh. Hope you both enjoy your Asian meal tonight.
