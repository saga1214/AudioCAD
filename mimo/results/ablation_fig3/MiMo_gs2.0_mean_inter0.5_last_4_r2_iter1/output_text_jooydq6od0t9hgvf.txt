Here are quick, elegant drink ideas for your dinner party:

### 1. **Vodka Mixer (Refreshing & Light)**
   - **Recipe:** Mix 2 parts vodka with 1 part sparkling water (or soda alternative like club soda) + 1 splash of fresh lime juice.  
   - **Garnish:** Lime wheel or cucumber ribbon.  
   - **Why:** Light, hydrating, and soda-free. Easy to scale up.

### 2. **Argentine-Style Vincho (Wine-Based)**
   - **Recipe:** In a glass, add 2 oz dry white wine (e.g., Sauvignon Blanc or Torrontés), 0.5 oz vermouth, and 2 dashes bitters (like Angostura). Stir lightly.  
   - **Garnish:** Lemon twist or mint sprig.  
   - **Why:** Sophisticated, balanced, and avoids carbonation. Dry white wine works for most palates.

### Bonus Quick Option:
   - **Classic Gin & Tonic (if vodka isn’t used):** High-quality gin + premium tonic (ensure no artificial sweeteners) + lemon wedge.  
   - **Non-Alcoholic:** Sparkling water with fresh berries or a splash of ginger beer (soda-free).

### Key Considerations:
   - **Allergy:** Both recipes exclude soda.  
   - **Formality:** Elegant glassware (coupe or rocks glasses) elevates the look.  
   - **Mix of Ages:** Keep flavors light and avoid overly bitter notes.  

These are easy to prep ahead and require minimal effort during the party. Cheers! 🍸
