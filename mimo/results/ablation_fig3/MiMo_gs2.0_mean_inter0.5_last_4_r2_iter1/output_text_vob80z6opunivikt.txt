That's fantastic! With your newly assembled coffee table ready, here are some snack ideas from a vending machine that balance savory, sweet (if you're open to it), and fun display options:

### 🍿 Savory & Crunchy (Best for non-sweet lovers):  
- **Pretzel Rods or Twists** – Salty, chewy, and satisfying.  
- **Potato Chips** (e.g., Lay’s, Ruffles, or flavored like BBQ or sour cream & onion).  
- **Corn Nuts** – Crunchy, slightly sweet, and nostalgic.  
- **Rice Krispie Treats** (if you want a *very* mild sweetness – they’re more marshmallowy than chocolatey).  
- **Popcorn** – Air-popped or caramel-flavored (less sweet than candy).  

### 🍬 Sweet (If you’re feeling adventurous):  
- **Gummy Bears/Worms** – Fun colors, but avoid if you dislike overly chewy candy.  
- **Chocolate-Covered Almonds** – Salty-sweet combo (milk or dark chocolate).  
- **Fruit Gummies** (e.g., fruit-flavored) – Less sweet than chocolate, more fruity.  
- **Snickers or Twix** – For a classic candy bar fix (but these are *very* sweet).  

### 🧊 Drinks & Extras:  
- **Soda or Sparkling Water** – Great for pairing with salty snacks.  
- **Gummy Candy** (e.g., sour gummies or jelly beans) – If you’re okay with fruity sweetness.  
- **Hard Candy** (like licorice or peppermint) – For a sweet-but-not-chocolatey option.  

### 💡 Pro Tip:  
Mix textures for a fun display! Stack pretzel rods in a bowl, arrange chips in a pyramid, and tuck gummies into the corners. Since your table has shelves/drawers, you could even use the drawer for chips and pretzels, and the top shelves for candy.  

**Final Thought:** If savory is your priority, go for pretzels, chips, and popcorn—easy to grab and zero chocolate! 🎉 Enjoy your snack-filled table!
