### U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia:
- **Fines**: Approximately **$160** plus a 11% surcharge (total ~$178) for an improper U-turn.  
- **Crucial Warning**: Improper U-turns can lead to **serious accidents**. Always prioritize safety over rules. If you make a mistake, safely pull over and reverse if possible.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients (Serves 4):  
**Pasta**: 400g spaghetti (or bucatini/fettuccine)  
**Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 1 egg yolk (room temperature)  
**Cheese**: 100g grated Pecorino Romano (or Parmigiano-Reggiano)  
**Protein**: 200g guanciale (or pancetta/bacon), diced  
**Aromatics**: 2 cloves garlic, minced  
**Salt**: For pasta water + finishing  
**Black Pepper**: Freshly cracked (to taste)  
**Optional**: Red pepper flakes for heat  

---

### Key Tips for Carbonara:  
1. **Don’t Overcook Pasta**: Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.  
2. **Eggs First**: Whisk eggs + cheese + pepper *before* adding to pasta.  
3. **Heat Control**: Cook guanciale in a cold pan first, then medium heat. Remove from heat before mixing eggs to prevent scrambling.  
4. **Pancetta Substitute**: Use 150g pancetta + 50g butter for extra richness.
