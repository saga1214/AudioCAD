Okay, let me quickly recap both analogies for you.

In the first one: Milk was the room, bacteria were guests, sugar was food, and the group hug was what thickened it.

In the second one: Milk was a disorganized crowd, bacteria coaches formed a tight net, and flavoring was like uniforms.

But honestly, the simplest and most accurate way to put it is this: The good bacteria you add act like tiny sculptors. They cause the milk's proteins to link together, forming a fine net that traps the liquid. That net is what turns runny milk into thick yogurt. The heat just speeds up their sculpting process.
