Here are some treat recommendations and an explanation of clicker training:

### Treat Recommendations:
- **Best Flavors:**  
  - **Bacon or Chicken:** Universally loved by dogs (look for plain, no salt or additives).  
  - **Cheese:** Small cubes of mild cheddar or mozzarella (avoid sharp/aged varieties).  
  - **Commercial Pup Treats:** Choose soft, bite-sized options like Zukes, freeze-dried liver, or training chews.  
- **Tips:**  
  - Use tiny pieces (about the size of a pea) to avoid overfeeding.  
  - Freeze treats for longer-lasting engagement.  
  - Rotate flavors to keep training exciting.  

### Clicker Training Explained:  
A clicker is a simple handheld device that makes a sharp "click" sound. It works as a **"bridge signal"** to mark the exact moment your dog does something right. Here’s how it works:  

1. **Pair the Click with a Reward:**  
   - Click *immediately* when your puppy performs the desired behavior (e.g., sits).  
   - Before clicking, toss a high-value treat into their mouth. Repeat 10–15 times.  
   - Your puppy learns: *Click = Treat is coming!*  

2. **Add the Command:**  
   - Say the command ("Sit"), then click *as soon as* they sit.  
   - Deliver the treat. Repeat daily.  

3. **Gradual Progression:**  
   - Slowly add pauses between clicks and treats (e.g., click → wait 1 second → treat).  
   - Eventually, the click alone becomes the reward. Your dog will perform the behavior just for the click!  

**Why it works:** The click is precise and consistent, helping your puppy understand *exactly* what you want. It’s like a camera shutter sound—clear and exciting!  

**Start with simple tricks** (e.g., "look at me," "touch nose to hand") before complex commands. Practice 5–10 minutes daily. If your puppy ignores the click, reset and try again—no frustration!
