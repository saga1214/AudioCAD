Great point—budget-friendly desserts can still be delicious, fun, and diabetes-conscious! Here are some ideas that balance cost, nutrition, and appeal to kids:

### Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:
1. **Fruit Skewers or Salad**  
   - **Cost-effective**: Use seasonal, affordable fruits like bananas, berries, grapes, and melon.  
   - **Kid-friendly**: Add a drizzle of honey or yogurt for extra fun.  
   - **Bonus**: No cooking required!

2. **Homemade Banana Bread or Oatmeal Cookies**  
   - **Budget tip**: Use pantry staples—bananas, oats, flour, and eggs. Skip expensive chocolate chips.  
   - **Healthier twist**: Add nuts or seeds for texture, or use whole-wheat flour.  
   - **Kid appeal**: Let kids decorate cookies with natural food coloring or sprinkles.

3. **Frozen Yogurt Pops**  
   - **Easy & cheap**: Blend Greek yogurt with frozen fruit (mango, berries) and pour into popsicle molds.  
   - **DIY fun**: Kids can add their own toppings like granola or honey.

4. **Yogurt Parfaits**  
   - **Layered simplicity**: Use store-bought granola and fresh fruit to mix with Greek yogurt.  
   - **Cost-saving**: Buy larger tubs of yogurt and split them.

5. **Mini Fruit Cups**  
   - **No cooking**: Mix diced seasonal fruit (peaches, pineapple) with a squeeze of lemon and a sprinkle of cinnamon. Serve in small cups.

6. **DIY Ice Cream Sundae Bar**  
   - **Budget-friendly setup**: Buy a small tub of vanilla ice cream ($3–$5).  
   - **Healthy toppings**: Offer bowls of fresh fruit, crushed nuts, and a dash of cinnamon instead of sugary syrups.  
   - **Kid engagement**: Let them build their own mini-ice cream creations.

### Beverage Tips:
- **Water station**: Use large dispensers with infused water (cucumber, lemon, mint).  
- **Sugar-free options**: Offer herbal teas, sparkling water, or infused lemonade (no added sugar).  
- **Homemade lemonade**: Dilute store-bought with water and add a splash of honey if needed.

### Pro Budget Hacks:
- **Buy in bulk**: Purchase seasonal fruits/vegetables in large quantities.  
- **DIY snacks**: Make popcorn from scratch (air-popped) instead of buying pre-packaged.  
- **Barbecue smart**: Use ground turkey instead of beef for burgers/hot dogs.  
- **Community help**: Ask family members to bring dishes (e.g., casseroles, desserts) to share the load.

### Final Thought:
Focus on **portion control** (smaller servings) and **variety**—kids won’t feel deprived if they can try a little of everything! Your awareness message shines by offering *healthy choices without guilt*—and it’s more budget-friendly than you’d think.
