Great questions! Here's a breakdown of treats and clicker training for your new pup:

### Treat Recommendations:
1. **High-Value Treats** (for training):  
   - **Small & Soft**: Freeze-dried liver, chicken, or beef bites (easy to swallow).  
   - **Novelty**: Cheese cubes, cooked eggs, or hot dog bits (dogs go wild for these!).  
   - **Science Twist**: Try "kibble treats" (like freeze-dried science diet kibble) or themed toys (e.g., "cosmic" or "galaxy" shaped treats).  
   - **Italian Flavors**: Mini salami slices or sun-dried tomato bits (for roots-inspired treats!).  

2. **Low-Value Treats** (for everyday rewards):  
   - Common kibble, peanut butter (xylitol-free!), or soft cheese cubes.  

**Tip**: Always use tiny pieces—puppies have tiny mouths!  

### Clicker Training Explained:
Clicker training uses **three simple steps**:  
1. **Click**: Press the clicker to mark the exact moment Galileo does the right thing (e.g., sits).  
2. **Treat**: Immediately after clicking, give a high-value treat.  
3. **Repeat**: Practice until he connects the click + treat with the action.  

**How it works**:  
- The click acts like a "camera shutter"—it tells Galileo, "I saw that! Now get a reward!"  
- The treat confirms the behavior.  
- Over time, he learns that *when I sit, I hear a click and get a treat*.  

**Example for "Sit"**:  
- Hold a treat near Galileo’s nose → move it upward → when his bottom touches the ground → **CLICK** → treat.  
- Repeat 10–15 times daily.  

**Why it’s great**:  
- No guessing games—clickers make timing precise.  
- Builds trust and excitement for training!  

### Final Tips:
- **Consistency**: Use the same clicker and treats for every session.  
- **Short Sessions**: Keep training fun (2–5 minutes max).  
- **Patience**: Galileo is young—progress takes time!  

With clicker training, you’ll see quick results, and your puppy will associate training with joy. 🐾
