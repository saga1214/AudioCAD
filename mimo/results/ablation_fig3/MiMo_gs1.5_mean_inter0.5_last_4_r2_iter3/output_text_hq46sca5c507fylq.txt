That's a great question! Here are some meaningful gift ideas tailored to hobbies like tailoring, woodworking, or claywork—focusing on quality, practicality, and inspiration:

### For Tailoring:
1. **Vintage Sewing Machine** (e.g., a classic Janome or Singer): A beautiful, functional heirloom.  
2. **Linen or Wool Fabric Bundle**: High-quality, pre-cut fabric for practice projects.  
3. **Handmade Button or Embroidery Kit**: A small, intricate kit for personalizing garments.  
4. **Tailor’s Hammer & Awl Set**: Essential tools for precise alterations.  
5. **Vintage Fountain Pen Restorer’s Kit**: A nod to your existing passion!  

### For Woodworking:
1. **Clamp Set**: A set of versatile bar clamps (e.g., Irwin or BenchDog).  
2. **Pre-cut Wood Blocks**: For practice or small projects (e.g., charcuterie boards, coasters).  
3. **Woodworking Plan Book**: A guide to beginner projects (e.g., "The Complete Woodworker").  
4. **Sanding Blocks or Abrasive Pads**: For smoothing finished pieces.  
5. **Specialized Tool**: Like a router bit set or a hand plane (if they don’t have one).  

### For Clay/Pottery:
1. **Potter’s Wheel Kit**: A beginner-friendly electric wheel (e.g., Schindler or Lekko).  
2. **Glaze Samples Kit**: Lets them experiment with colors/textures.  
3. **Clay Storage Container**: To keep clay from drying out.  
4. **Sculpting Tools Set**: Wooden or metal tools for modeling.  
5. **Personalized Molds**: For creating custom items (e.g., jewelry molds).  

### Universal Gift Ideas:
- **Subscription Boxes**: Monthly supplies for their chosen hobby (e.g., "The Sewing Box" or "Woodworking Kit").  
- **Personalized Workbench/Studio Upgrade**: A custom apron, engraved tool holder, or workshop mat.  
- **Experiences**: A pottery class, woodworking workshop, or tailoring workshop.  

### Budget-Friendly Options:
- **Project Kit**: A pre-designed kit for a specific project (e.g., "Make a Wooden Watchbox").  
- **Quality Supplies**: A premium needle set (tailoring), sandpaper (woodworking), or clay (pottery).  
- **Artisan Gift Basket**: Curated tools/materials from local makers.  

**Key Tip**: If your family is unsure, ask them what *one tool or material* they’d love to have to deepen their hobby. This ensures the gift is both useful and exciting!
