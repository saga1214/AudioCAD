### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia  
In BC, U-turns are **legal** in most areas but subject to these rules:  
1. **No Signalized Intersections**: Prohibited at traffic lights unless a sign allows it.  
2. **Visibility**: Must see **150m+** in both directions.  
3. **Restricted Zones**: Not allowed on curves, hills, near driveways, or where it interferes with traffic.  
4. **Signage**: Always check signs (e.g., "U-Turn Not Permitted").  
**Penalty**: Ticketed for $160 + demerit points.  

**Pro Tip**: Use designated U-turn lanes or uncontrolled intersections where visibility is clear.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
For **4 servings**, here’s what you’ll need:  
#### Essentials:  
- **400g spaghetti**  
- **150g guanciale** (or pancetta), diced  
- **4 large eggs** (3 whole + 1 yolk)  
- **100g Pecorino Romano cheese**, grated  
- **Freshly ground black pepper** (lots!)  

#### Optional (Traditional):  
- **50g Parmigiano Reggiano** (for extra creaminess)  
- **1 tsp garlic** (optional, but not classic)  

#### Cooking Steps:  
1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water (al dente). Reserve 2 cups pasta water.  
2. Fry guanciale until crispy; drain.  
3. Whisk eggs, cheese, yolk, and pepper.  
4. Toss hot pasta with guanciale, then mix in egg sauce off-heat (add pasta water as needed).  
5. Serve immediately!  

**Key Tip**: Avoid cream—traditional carbonara relies on emulsified egg/pasta water.
