Okay, it sounds like you're ready for the primer step, but let's make sure everything is set for the best results. After you've sanded, the next crucial step is to wipe down the cabinets with a tack cloth or a damp cloth to get rid of all the dust. This is super important because any dust on the primer will just scratch off and create a bad finish later.

Once they're dust-free, go ahead and apply that primer. And since you're going from black to off-white, you'll definitely need at least two full coats. After the primer is completely dry, you can start painting with your off-white color. You'll probably want two to three coats of that paint to get a nice, smooth, opaque finish.

And a final tip: when you put the hardware back on, make sure to clean the spots where the handles were. Just wipe them down with a little rubbing alcohol to remove any dust or grease, so the new hardware sticks perfectly. Good luck with both the project and your neighbor!
