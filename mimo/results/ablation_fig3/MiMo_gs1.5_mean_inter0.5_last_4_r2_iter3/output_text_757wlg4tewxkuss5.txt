**Recommendations for Success:**

*   **Get Plant-Based Burgers:** **This is essential.** Look for frozen plant-based patties specifically labeled as vegan (like Beyond Meat, Impossible, Gardein, Lightlife, or a local brand). Check the ingredients if you know any of your friends are strict vegans (some might avoid certain soy or gluten-containing products, though most are fine).
*   **Bring a Cooler with Ice Packs:** **Highly Recommended.** Frozen burgers need to stay frozen until cooking. A cooler with ice packs is the best way to transport and hold them frozen. This is crucial for food safety.
*   **Consider Labeling:** If you bring multiple plant-based burgers, label them clearly as "Vegan" so your vegan friend knows exactly what to take.
*   **Portion Size:** Frozen burgers often expand when thawed. Plan to have enough for everyone, plus potential leftovers.
*   **Cross-Contamination:** If you're cooking the burgers, have a separate plate or foil for the vegan ones to prevent any meat juices from transferring.
