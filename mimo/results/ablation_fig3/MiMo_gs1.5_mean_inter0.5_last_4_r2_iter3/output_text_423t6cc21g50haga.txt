### **1. Kyoto, Japan (Language + Culture + Scenic Beauty)**  
- **Why?** Perfect for language immersion (many locals speak English in tourist areas, but practice Japanese). Rich in history, stunning temples, and quiet bookshops.  
- **Activities:**  
  - Visit **Kyoto National Museum** (fantastic for fantasy/sci-fi fans).  
  - Learn calligraphy or tea ceremony.  
  - Stroll through **Arashiyama Bamboo Forest** (great for writing inspiration!).  
- **Accommodation:**  
  - **Ryokan (traditional inns)** like *Gion Hatanaka* (pet-friendly!).  
  - Budget: *Piece Hostel Kyoto* (pet-friendly, social atmosphere).  
- **Getting There:**  
  - Fly into **Kansai International Airport (KIX)** → 75-min train to Kyoto.  
  - Book flights via Skyscanner/Kayak.

### **2.里斯本, 葡萄牙 (Bookish + Language + Coastal Charm)**  
- **Why?** Lively, book-filled cafés, and a gateway to Atlantic beaches. Portuguese is easier to learn than Italian (more English-friendly).  
- **Activities:**  
  - Explore **Livraria Bertrand** (world’s oldest bookstore).  
  - Take a **tram tour** for iconic city views.  
  - Visit **Sintra** (fairytale palaces perfect for fantasy writing!).  
- **Accommodation:**  
  - **Boutique hotels** like *Hotel Santa Luzia* (pet-friendly!).  
  - Budget: *Hostal das Palácias* (social, central).  
- **Getting There:**  
  - Fly into **Lisbon Airport (LIS)** → 10-min train to city.

### **3. 布拉格, 捷克 (Fantasy + Architecture + Pet-Friendly)**

### **Tips for Making Friends While Traveling:**  
- **Join group tours** (e.g., **Intrepid Travel** or **G Adventures**) for shared experiences.  
- **Attend local events**: Bookshops often host readings; cafes have trivia nights.  
- **Use apps** like **Meetup** or **Bumble BFF** to find travelers in your area.  
- **Volunteer**: Help at animal shelters or libraries (e.g., **Workaway**).

- **Try "writing walks"**: Wander aimlessly with a notebook—let the world fuel your ideas.
