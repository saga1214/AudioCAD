Alright, let's break this down. For the drywall stuff, the drywall compound is usually around 10 to 20 bucks. Sandpaper and a putty knife can be 10 to 15. Primer and paint, if you need it, maybe 20 to 30. If you're buying replacement molding, that's the most variable part, could be 20 to 50 depending on what you get. Nails or adhesive is cheap, like 5 to 10. So for the wall repair, I'd budget around 65 to 125 dollars.

For the cake, flour, sugar, baking powder, butter, eggs, milk, vanilla... that's all pretty cheap. Frosting and decorating stuff is another 10 to 20. Pans are maybe 10 to 20. All in, the cake should be under 50 bucks.

So, to be safe, I'd say bring around 130 to 150 dollars. That should cover both tasks without any surprises.
