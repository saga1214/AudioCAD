Of course! For St. Pete, Zone 10B, I’d really recommend focusing on plants that love the warmth. Herbs like basil, mint, and parsley are fantastic. Leafy greens like lettuce and spinach are also great, but you’ll want to make sure they have some shade during the hottest parts of the year. Radishes are a good one because they grow super fast.

Then you have your fruiting plants. Tomatoes and peppers are perfect for Florida. They thrive in the heat. You can also try okra and sweet potatoes, which are both native to warm climates and do really well there. Eggplant is another one that loves the heat. 

Just remember, the key is to stagger your plantings. Since you don’t really have a traditional off-season, you can plant things in succession to keep a steady harvest going all year round. That’s really the trick to success in a climate like yours.
