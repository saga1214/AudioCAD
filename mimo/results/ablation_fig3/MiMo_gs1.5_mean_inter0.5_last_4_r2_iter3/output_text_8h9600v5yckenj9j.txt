Here's a quick, crowd-pleasing candy strategy to minimize waste and maximize fun:

### 🎃 Best Candy for Halloween Parties
1. **Classic Snacks (Crowd-Favorites):**
   * **M&M's & Skittles:** Universally loved, colorful, and easy to eat.
   * **Reese's Cups/Puffs:** Peanut butter chocolate combo is a winner.
   * **Snickers:** The full-size candy bar classic.
   * **Butterfingers:** Always a hit.
   * **Gummy Bears/Worms:** A staple for younger kids.

2. **Fun & Festive Options:**
   * **Mini Candy Bars:** (Milky Way, 3 Musketeers, Snickers Mini) - Great portion control.
   * **Sour Patch Kids:** Surprising sweet-and-sour kick.
   * **Candy Apples:** Look festive and delicious (often pre-made).
   * **Themed Treats:** (e.g., "ghost" chocolate drops, bat-shaped cookies).

3. **Kid-Approved Staples:**
   * **Gummy Candy:** Gummy bears, worms, frogs, or eyes.
   * **Chocolate Coins:** Fun novelty.
   * **Lollipops:** Easy to hand out.

### 📦 Pro Tips to Avoid Leftovers
- **Buy Variety Packs:** Mix a few different popular candies (e.g., M&M's + Skittles + Reese's Mini).
- **Portion Control:** Use small bags or cellophane wrappers to give out individual pieces instead of full candy bars. Guests appreciate this!
- **Bundling:** Bundle 3-5 small candies into a mini-bag for each guest – adds fun and reduces waste.
- **Themed Bags:** Use Halloween-themed cellophane bags or tissue paper for extra flair.
- **Check Expiry Dates:** Grab candy near the back of shelves for fresher stock.
- **Avoid "Bummy" Candy:** Skip overly niche or potentially disliked items (e.g., hard candies like Tootsie Rolls unless you know kids love them).
