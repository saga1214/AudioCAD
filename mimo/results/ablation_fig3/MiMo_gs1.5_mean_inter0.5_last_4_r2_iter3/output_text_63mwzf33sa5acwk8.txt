That's a great plan! After a desert adventure, a Vegas pit stop sounds perfect. Here’s how to make it happen:

### 🏨 **Hotel Options**
1. **Luxury Resort & Pool:**  
   - **Resorts World** (free pool, spa, buffet)  
   - **Wynn/Encore** (stunning pool, high-end vibe)  
   - **Aria** (great pool, free shuttles)  
   *Cost:* $200–$600+/night (weekdays = cheaper)  

2. **Budget-Friendly:**  
   - **Stratosphere** ($99–$150) – Skyline views, free pool.  
   - **Downtown Plaza** ($70–$120) – Near Fremont Street.  

3. **Van/RV-Friendly:**  
   - **Elko, NV** (2.5 hrs from Joshua Tree):  
     - **Elko Inn & Suites** ($80–$120) – Pet-friendly, van parking.  
     - **Camping at Great Basin National Park** ($10/night) – Stunning alpine desert.  

### 🎭 **Vegas Experience Tips**
- **Skip the Strip:**  
  - **Fremont Street (Downtown):** Free light shows, low-energy vibe, cheap drinks ($5–$10).  
  - **Area 15:** Art museum + immersive experiences (e.g., *Meow Wolf*).  
- **Shows:**  
  - Cirque du Soleil (*O* or *Mystère*) – Stunning, ~$80–$150.  
  - Absinthe – Hilarious, ~$75.  
- **Food:**  
  - **Gordon Ramsay’s Hell’s Kitchen** – Classic Vegas dining (~$100/entrée).  
  - **Food Trucks:** Striking distance in Downtown.  

### 🚗 **Route Planning**
- **Joshua Tree → Vegas:**  
  - **Distance:** ~220 miles (3.5–4 hrs).  
  - **Best Route:** I-10 E → I-15 N (scenic drive through Mojave).  
  - **Stop:** **Pioneertown, CA** (Old West film set) or **Palm Springs** (30-min detour).  

### 💡 **Pro Tips**
- **Book Early:** Hotels fill fast in summer.  
- **Van Parking:** Confirm if your hotel allows van parking (some charge extra).  
- **Vegas Pass:** Save on entry to casinos/shows (e.g., *Vegas Pass* for 3 free entries).  

### 🌟 **Unique Add-On**
- **Red Rock Canyon State Park** (30 mins from Vegas):  
  - Hike, scenic drive, picnic spot ($10/vehicle).  

This mix of adventure + relaxation is perfect for new relationships. Prioritize flexibility—Vegas is about vibes, not ticking boxes. Have an incredible trip! 🌵🚗✨
