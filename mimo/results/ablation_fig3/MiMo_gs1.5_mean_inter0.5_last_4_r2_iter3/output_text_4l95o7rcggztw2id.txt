Here's a complete pirate-themed shopping list designed for beach convenience, sand avoidance, and allergy safety, with themed names to keep your son immersed:

**Main Food (Portion: ~15-20 Servings)**
*   **Mini "Buried Treasure" Sandwiches (Theme: Pirate's Lunch):**  
    *   1 lb Ham or Turkey (diced small)  
    *   1 lb Cheese (Cheddar, Swiss, or Pepper Jack, diced small)  
    *   1 loaf Whole Wheat or Pita Bread (cut into 12-14 mini squares)  
    *   1 cup Butter (for sealing)  
    *   *Assembly:* Mix ham/cheese, spread butter on bread, press together, seal edges with butter. Serve in small containers. *Allergy Note: Ensure cheese is watermelon-free.*
*   **Mini "Cheese Crust" Sliders (Theme: Ship's Rations):**  
    *   8-10 small Hamburger Buns  
    *   1 lb Ground Beef or Turkey  
    *   1/2 cup Cheese Sauce (store-bought or homemade)  
    *   Salt, Pepper, Onion Powder  
    *   *Assembly:* Brown meat, mix with cheese sauce and seasonings. Fill buns, wrap tightly in foil. *Portion:* ~8-10 sliders (adjust beef for more).
*   **Veggie "Jewels" & Cheese "Gold" (Theme: Pirate's Bounty):**  
    *   1 bunch Celery (cut into bite-sized sticks)  
    *   1 bag Baby Carrots (halved if large)  
    *   1 lb Cheese Cubes (Cheddar, Mozzarella, or watermelon-free blend)  
    *   1 bag Goldfish Crackers (or similar "pirate coins")  
    *   *Portion:* ~2 cups veggies, ~1 bag cheese cubes, ~1 bag Goldfish.

**Fruit (Theme: Island Fruit)**
*   **"Jewel" Grapes (Theme: Mermaid's Treasure):**  
    *   1 lb Grapes (red, green, or mixed)  
    *   *Portion:* Wash, dry, and halve/grape if large. *Allergy Note: Avoid watermelon.*
*   **"Cursed" Apple Slices (Theme: Forbidden Fruit):**  
    *   2-3 medium Apples (like Honeycrisp or Fuji)  
    *   1 Lemon (for preventing browning)  
    *   *Portion:* Wash, peel, core, and slice into thin rounds. Toss with lemon juice. *Optional:* Themed cookie cutter shapes.
*   **"Sea Berry" Skewers (Theme: Ocean Gems):**  
    *   1 bag Mixed Berries (Strawberries, Blueberries, Raspberries - *ensure no watermelon*)  
    *   1 small Bag Marshmallows (optional "frosting")  
    *   *Portion:* Thread 3-4 berries and a marshmallow onto a small skewer. *Allergy Note: Check berry labels.*

**Snacks (Theme: Pirate's Cache)**
*   **"Ship Mast" Pretzel Rods:**  
    *   1 large Bag Pretzel Rods  
    *   *Portion:* ~12-15 rods.
*   **"Pirate Coins" Goldfish Crackers:**  
    *   1 large Bag Goldfish Crackers  
    *   *Portion:* ~1 bag.

**Drinks (Portion: ~7-8 Servings)**
*   **"Pirate Punch" Juice Boxes:**  
    *   8-10 Individual Juice Boxes (Apple, Orange, Grape, or Mixed Berry)  
    *   *Theme:* Label with pirate names or "X Marks the Spot!"
*   **"Sea Water" Bottled Water:**  
    *   8-10 Small Bottled Waters (or reusable bottles with pirate labels)  
    *   *Optional:* Add a splash of blue food coloring for "ocean water."
*   **"Cursed Lemonade" (Optional Fun):**  
    *   1 Lemon  
    *   1/4 cup Sugar  
    *   1 cup Water  
    *   *Prep:* Make simple lemonade, freeze in small cups as "frozen treasure."

**Packaging & Extras (Crucial for Sand Avoidance!)**
*   **"Pirate Treasure Chests":** Small cardboard boxes or reusable containers for main foods.
*   **Small Individual Containers:** For sandwiches, cheese cubes, crackers.
*   **Snack Bags:** For grapes, berries, pretzel rods.
*   **Tongs:** For serving skewers and pretzels.
*   **Lemon Wedges:** For water bottles if desired.
*   **Pirate-Themed Napkins:** For easy cleanup.
*   **Allergy Label:** Clearly mark any food containing watermelon or other allergens.

**Key Considerations:**

1.  **Portion Size:** Plan for 1.5-2 servings per food item to account for kids' appetites and potential leftovers.
2.  **Temperature:** Sandwiches and sliders can be served warm or at room temp. Other items are best chilled. Bring ice packs in cooler.
3.  **Labeling:** Clearly mark food containers with contents, especially allergens. "Watermelon-Free" is essential for the affected child.
4.  **Theme Consistency:** Use pirate names for everything ("Mermaid's Treasure," "Pirate Punch," "Cursed Apple Slices").
5.  **Mess Control:** Pack everything sealed. Use containers and bags. Have wet wipes and a small trash can ready.

This list provides a balanced, themed, and practical menu that minimizes sand contact while keeping the pirate adventure alive! Enjoy the party!
