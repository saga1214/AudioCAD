You know, upon deeper reflection, I'd have to change my answer. I think Mesopotamia is actually the better choice. Here's why: while Ancient Egypt is visually spectacular, Mesopotamia represents the dawn of human civilization, like the invention of writing and cities. That has a much deeper educational and cultural impact. The artifacts, like cuneiform tablets, are unique and powerful. And the storytelling potential, once explained well, is incredibly rich. So, for a museum aiming for real depth and impact, I believe Mesopotamia is the superior option.
