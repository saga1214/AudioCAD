Here are quick, elegant recipes for your two drink requests—perfect for a formal yet relaxed dinner party:

### 1. **Vodka Mixer (Refreshing & Light)**  
**Recipe:**  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 1.5 oz vodka  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 1 oz ginger beer (or sparkling water for drier option)  
  - Ice  
  - *Garnish:* Lemon wheel or cucumber ribbon  

**How to Make:**  
Shake vodka, lemon juice, and ice. Strain into a glass filled with ice. Top with ginger beer. Stir gently. Garnish and serve.  
*Tip: Use cucumber instead of lemon for a more spa-like vibe!*

---

### 2. **Argentine-Style Fernet (Bitter & Herbal)**  
**Recipe:**  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 0.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
  - 2 oz soda water (or club soda)  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
  - *Garnish:* Fresh mint sprig  

**How to Make:**  
Fill a highball glass with ice. Add Fernet, lime juice, and soda water. Stir lightly. Garnish with mint.  
*Authentic Twist:* Add a splash of cola or orange soda for sweetness (optional).  

---

### Quick Tips:  
- **Non-Alcoholic Option:** Serve sparkling water with lime/mint alongside alcoholic drinks.  
- **Prep Ahead:** Mix the vodka base and Fernet bases in pitchers; just top with soda water when serving.  
- **Allergy Check:** Both recipes avoid nuts/dairy. For the vodka mix, use ginger beer (check labels for soda allergies).
