Of course. Let's clear that up. The original, more spirit-forward Boulevardier is the one with one and a quarter ounces of bourbon, one ounce of vermouth, and one ounce of Campari. The equal-parts Boulevardier is made with one ounce of each of those three ingredients. 

Now, for the Last Word, that's an entirely different drink. It's made with equal parts of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. Each of those is three-quarters of an ounce. 

So, to recap: The Boulevardier is whiskey, vermouth, Campari. The Last Word is gin, Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime. Hope that helps you choose!
