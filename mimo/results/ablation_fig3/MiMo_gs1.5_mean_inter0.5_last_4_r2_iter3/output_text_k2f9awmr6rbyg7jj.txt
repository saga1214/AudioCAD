Here's a simple recipe for a breakfast sandwich using artificial crab meat (surimi):

### Artificial Crab Breakfast Sandwich
**Ingredients:**
*   2 slices of bread (English muffins, whole wheat, or bagels work well)
*   2 slices of cheese (American, cheddar, or Swiss)
*   2-3 slices of artificial crab meat (surimi)
*   2-3 eggs (fried, scrambled, or poached)
*   Optional: Avocado slices, hot sauce, mustard, lettuce, tomato, sprouts

**Instructions:**

1.  **Prepare Eggs:** Cook your eggs however you like (fried, scrambled, or poached). Set aside.
2.  **Cook Crab:** Gently warm the slices of artificial crab meat in a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat for 1-2 minutes per side, just to heat through. Don't overcook as it can become rubbery.
3.  **Cook Cheese (Optional):** If using cheese slices, melt them slightly on the bread as you toast it.
4.  **Assemble:**
    *   Lightly toast the bread slices (optional but recommended).
    *   Place the cooked egg(s) on one slice of bread.
    *   Add the warmed artificial crab meat on top of the egg(s).
    *   Add the melted cheese slice (if using) on top of the crab.
    *   Top with the second slice of bread.
5.  **Add Toppings (Optional):** Add avocado, hot sauce, mustard, lettuce, tomato, or sprouts if desired.
6.  **Serve:** Cut in half and enjoy your breakfast sandwich!

**Key Notes:**

*   **Artificial Crab:** This is a processed seafood product. It's not "fake" crab, but it's made from fishery by-products (like the trimmings from catching real crab). It has a mild, sweet, flaky texture similar to crab.
*   **Flavor:** It pairs well with eggs, cheese, and avocado. A touch of hot sauce or mustard can add nice contrast.
*   **Vegetarian Option:** If you want a fully vegetarian version, simply omit the eggs and cheese. You could add a slice of avocado or a smear of hummus instead.

Enjoy your breakfast sandwich!
