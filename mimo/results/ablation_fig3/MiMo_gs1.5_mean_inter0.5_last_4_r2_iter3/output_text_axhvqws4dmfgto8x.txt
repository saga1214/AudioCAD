Okay, let's tackle this noodle problem! First and foremost, NO WOODEN SPOONS! Use a metal one. Seriously, that smoke alarm story is burned into my memory.

Here's the game plan for perfect stir-fry noodles:

First, salt your water generously, like a cup per gallon. This makes the noodles chewier.

Next, stir constantly, but don't stir them until they are completely done. You want them to be just right, not mushy.

Most importantly, drain them immediately after cooking. As soon as they're done, pour them into a strainer and shake it well. This is the key to getting that perfect texture.

Then, toss them with a little oil to keep them from sticking together.

And here's the trick to prevent sticking when you add them back to the wok: let the hot noodles sit for just a minute or two. This lets any extra water evaporate. Then, when you toss them in, they won't turn into a sticky mess.

You've got this! Just remember: no wood, salt the water, drain fast, and let them cool for a minute before adding them back.
