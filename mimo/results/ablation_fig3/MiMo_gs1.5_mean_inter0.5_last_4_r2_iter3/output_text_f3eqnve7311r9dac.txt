For Wonder Woman, *All-Star Comics* number eight is the first appearance of her in 1942. A high-grade copy sold for over one and a half million in 2024.

*Warrior Angel* number one from 1945 is the first appearance of Captain America. It's a rare and valuable book, selling for around two hundred thousand dollars.

*The Spectre* number one from 1952 is the first appearance of that character, with a good copy worth about fifty thousand dollars.

*The Silver Surfer* number one, from 1966, is the first appearance of that character and can sell for around thirty thousand.

*Fantastic Four* number one, from 1961, is the first appearance of the Fantastic Four. A good copy can be worth around fifty thousand dollars.
