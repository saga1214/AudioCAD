Okay, you mentioned burning the candle at both ends, and yes, there are a few other idioms with similar meanings. For example, there's “up to your elbows in work,” which means being extremely busy. Another is “running on empty,” which describes feeling completely drained. And of course, “working around the clock,” which means you're working non-stop with no breaks. They all basically describe overcommitting and exhausting yourself.
