That's frustrating! Even though the glue gun feels hot, sometimes the glue itself isn't flowing properly. Here are the most common reasons and fixes:

1.  **Glue is Old or Hardened:** Glue sticks have a shelf life. If it's been sitting for a while (even months), the glue can dry out and harden inside the nozzle.
    *   **Fix:** Try a brand new glue stick. If the gun *still* doesn't work, the glue might be permanently clogged.

2.  **Glue is Too Thick for Your Climate:** If it's very hot and humid, or extremely cold and dry, the glue can become too thick or too runny.
    *   **Fix:** Try a different glue stick. Some brands work better in specific temperatures. If possible, try a different glue gun if you have one.

3.  **Nozzle is Clogged:** Tiny bits of dried glue or debris can block the tip.
    *   **Fix:** Carefully poke the tip with a pin or toothpick (be gentle!). Sometimes heating it for a few seconds with a lighter or hairdryer (hold it far away!) can melt the clog out. Clean the nozzle with rubbing alcohol on a cotton swab if it's sticky.

4.  **Glue Stick Not Seated Properly:** Sometimes the stick just isn't pushed in far enough or isn't aligned correctly.
    *   **Fix:** Unscrew the tip, pull out the old stick, and push a *brand new* stick in firmly and straight until it clicks.

5.  **Cord Not Plugged In (Seriously!):** It's the simplest oversight!
    *   **Fix:** Double-check the power cord is firmly plugged into the wall outlet and the gun itself.

6.  **Glue Gun is New?** Sometimes new guns need a "break-in" period or just need the first stick run through.
    *   **Fix:** Run a new stick through the gun if it's brand new.

**While you troubleshoot the glue gun, here are some alternative ways to attach things:**

*   **Double-Sided Tape:** Great for masks, small flower stems, or lace. Strong hold for lightweight items.
*   **Hot Glue Sticks (Different Type):** Try a different brand or type (like "super glue" or "tacky glue" sticks) if the original isn't working.
*   **Small Drops of Glue:** Use a fine-tip glue pen or a toothpick dipped in strong craft glue (like E6000 or tacky glue) for tiny additions like spiders or moss.
*   **Wire:** Bend thin craft wire around stems or bases for a more secure hold.
*   **Screws & Washers:** If you're feeling ambitious and the globes have a base you can access, small screws and washers can hold heavier items securely.
