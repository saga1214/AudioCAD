Oh, okay, kosher means no pork, shellfish, and no mixing of meat and dairy. So, for your snacks, you'll want to avoid bacon, which you mentioned. You could switch that out for something like plant-based sausage or turkey bacon. For the sweet treats, just make sure any desserts you bring don't contain dairy, so maybe opt for a fruit-based dessert or a fruit salad. You can also offer a variety of fruits and veggies, and make sure any dips like hummus are made with vegetable oil, not fish oil or anything else that might be an issue. And just a general tip, it's always good to double-check labels on packaged stuff like cookies or bread to make sure they're kosher-certified.
