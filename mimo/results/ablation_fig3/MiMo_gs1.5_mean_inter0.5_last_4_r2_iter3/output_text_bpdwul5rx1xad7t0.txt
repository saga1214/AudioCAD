Here are five fantastic road trip options from Vancouver, covering a mix of nature, culture, and culinary experiences.

First, for a coastal drive, you can head to the Sea to Sky Highway to Whistler. The scenery is breathtaking, and Whistler offers great hiking and skiing year-round. For food, you can try the upscale dining at席间, which has Michelin recognition, or a classic spot called The Lumiere Restaurant.

Second, for a bit of history and drama, you can drive to Victoria and the Vancouver Island Wineries. You can explore the historic inner harbour and the Fairmont Empress Hotel, and then visit many wineries in the Oak Bay area. For a fine dining experience, try the restaurant Oceana.

Third, for a dramatic mountain drive, you can take the Icefields Parkway to Banff. The views of the Canadian Rockies are incredible. In Banff, you can visit the Banff Gondola and try some world-class dining at the Banff Springs Hotel.

Fourth, for a relaxed coastal escape, you can drive to the Sunshine Coast. You can visit the charming village of Semiahmup and the unique spot called the Horn of God, which is a natural rock arch. For a special meal, try the restaurant Lighthouse.

Finally, for a quirky and artistic trip, you can drive to the Okanagan Valley. You can visit the quirky town of Penticton and the Kelowna Wine Country. You can also find unique activities like a vineyard tasting or even a hot air balloon ride. For fine dining, there's a restaurant called The Keg Steakhouse & Bar.

Each of these trips offers a different way to see the beauty of British Columbia!
