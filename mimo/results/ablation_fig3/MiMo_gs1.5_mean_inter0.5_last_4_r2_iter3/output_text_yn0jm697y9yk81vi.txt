Based on our conversation, here's a consolidated list of my top recommendations for your sheet metal and drywall projects, along with where to find them locally in Indianapolis:

### Cutting Tools
1.  **For Thin Metal/Curves:** Aviation Snips  
    *   *Why:* Ideal for precise curved cuts on thinner sheets.
2.  **For Thicker Metal/Power Cutting:** Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel  
    *   *Why:* Provides the power and speed needed for thicker metal.
3.  **Alternative for Metal:** Jigsaw with Metal-Cutting Blade  
    *   *Why:* Good for intricate cuts or where an angle grinder isn't practical.

### Drywall Fasteners
4.  **Best Overall:** Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws (1-1/4" for 5/8" drywall)  
    *   *Why:* Superior holding power, prevents nail pops, easy to set with a screw gun, creates a perfect dimple.
5.  **Alternative (if using nails):** Ring-Shank Nails  
    *   *Why:* Best nails for wood studs; the rings provide excellent grip and holding power.

### Insulation
6.  **Best Standard Insulation:** Fiberglass batt insulation (pre-cut rolls)  
    *   *Why:* Most accessible, cost-effective, and commonly used for standard wall cavities.
7.  **Alternative for Soundproofing/Fire Resistance:** Mineral Wool  
    *   *Why:* Excellent sound absorption and fire resistance.
8.  **Alternative for Maximum Air Sealing:** Spray Foam (professionally installed)  
    *   *Why:* Creates an airtight seal; best performance but requires pro installation.
