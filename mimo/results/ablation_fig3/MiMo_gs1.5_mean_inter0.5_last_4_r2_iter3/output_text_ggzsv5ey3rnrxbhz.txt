Okay, you're right, the name 'forever chemicals' can sound really scary. But here's the thing: your body is actually pretty good at getting rid of them on its own. It's a process that just takes a little bit of time.

While there's no magic juice cleanse that works for this, you can definitely help your body by staying hydrated. That helps everything move through your system. And eating a balanced diet with lots of fiber from fruits and veggies can help support your body's natural detox processes.

As for a simple list of things to avoid, here are five common sources:

First, non-stick cookware. You can switch to stainless steel or cast iron instead.
Second, grease-resistant food packaging, like on fast-food wrappers.
Third, products labeled as stain-resistant or waterproof, such as carpets and outdoor gear.
Fourth, some cosmetics and even certain types of dental floss.
And fifth, since these chemicals can get into tap water, using a certified water filter can really help.
