That's a very common and valid question. While it's absolutely possible to be the author and illustrator of your own children's book, it's important to understand that publishers are usually looking to acquire the *text* from an author and pair it with *new* illustrations from a separate illustrator. They are less likely to buy an entire package that's already assembled.

However, there are ways to approach this. You could first submit the text of your story to literary magazines to gain recognition. Once the story is published, you can then use it to attract an agent or publisher who is specifically looking for someone who can also do the illustrations. Another option is to find a publisher who specializes in self-published authors, which is a different path than going through a traditional agent.
