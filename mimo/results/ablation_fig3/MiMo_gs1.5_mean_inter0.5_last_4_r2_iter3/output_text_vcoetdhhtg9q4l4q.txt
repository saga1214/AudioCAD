That's a great goal! Keeping ingredients simple is key for faster, stress-free meals. Here are some pantry staples that work for quick dinners without needing a million spices:

### 🥚 **Pantry Staples for Speed:**
1. **Grains & Starches:**  
   - Rice (white, brown, or instant)  
   - Pasta (any shape)  
   - Quinoa or couscous  
   - Tofu or canned beans (black, chickpeas, etc.)  

2. **Proteins:**  
   - Eggs (scrambled, boiled, or fried)  
   - Frozen shrimp or chicken breast  
   - Canned tuna or salmon  

3. **Veggies:**  
   - Frozen mixed veggies (peas, carrots, corn)  
   - Canned tomatoes (diced, crushed, or paste)  
   - Spinach or pre-chopped bagged greens  

4. **Sauces & Seasonings:**  
   - Soy sauce, olive oil, garlic powder  
   - Pre-made stir-fry sauces (like Teriyaki or peanut)  
   - Hummus or tahini for quick dips/dressings  

### 🍳 **Easy Meal Ideas with These Ingredients:**
- **Stir-Fry:** Sauté frozen veggies + tofu/chicken + 1 tbsp soy sauce + pre-made sauce. Serve over rice.  
- **One-Pot Pasta:** Cook pasta, add canned tomatoes, spinach, and canned beans. Season with garlic powder and herbs.  
- **Breakfast-for-Dinner:** Scrambled eggs with frozen spinach and canned tomatoes.  
- **Sheet Pan Meal:** Toss pre-cut veggies + canned chickpeas + olive oil + spices. Roast at 400°F until tender.  

### 💡 **Key Tip:**  
**Prep once, eat often!** Chop veggies in batches on weekends, or cook a big pot of rice/quinoa to use for 3+ meals. This cuts down on daily decisions and cleanup.  

Minimalism in cooking isn’t about deprivation—it’s about freeing up time for what matters. You’ve got this! 🌱
