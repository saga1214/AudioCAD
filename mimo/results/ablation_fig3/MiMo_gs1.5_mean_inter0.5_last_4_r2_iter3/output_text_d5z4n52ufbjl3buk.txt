### **Email Structure & Key Points**
1. **Subject Line**:  
   `Request for Feedback on [Course Name] Exam [Date]`

2. **Opening (Respectful Tone)**:  
   > "Dear Professor [Name],  
   > Thank you for the opportunity to engage deeply with this course material. I’ve learned a lot from your class and appreciate your dedication to challenging us."

3. **State Your Purpose (Focus on Growth)**:  
   > "I scored a 64 on the recent exam and would sincerely appreciate your guidance on how I could improve for future assignments. Specifically, I’d like to understand what I might have missed in my essay that could lead to a higher score."

4. **Be Specific About the Exam (Without Accusation)**:  
   > "Since the exam was essay-based and subjective, I’m seeking clarity on the grading criteria you emphasized. For example, were there specific points, structure, or analysis expectations I didn’t fully address?"

5. **Close Professionally**:  
   > "Thank you for your time and insight. I’m committed to improving and value your feedback. Please let me know a convenient time to discuss further."  
   > *Sign off with "Best regards," and your full name.*

### **Why This Works**
- **Disarms Defensiveness**: Framing it as a desire to *improve* (not challenge) reduces ego triggers.

- **Specificity**: Mentioning "grading criteria" and "essay expectations" directs the conversation productively.  
- **Professionalism**: A formal structure shows respect and keeps the interaction focused.
