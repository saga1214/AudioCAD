Of course! Here’s a simple checklist for filming your next show.

First, plan the performance. Pick a short, fun routine that’s under ten minutes. Think of a funny skit with your characters, Melvin and Sloppy Snake, and maybe a simple magic trick or joke.

Next, get someone to film. You’ll want them to be close to you, maybe at eye level, so the audience can see the puppets clearly. It helps if they can hold the camera steady, or maybe they can use a tripod or a stand.

Then, just before you start, do a quick sound check. Make sure the microphone is working or that the room is quiet enough.

And finally, after the show, edit and upload. You can use free apps on your phone to trim the best parts, add a little title or text, and then upload it to YouTube with a fun title and a kid-friendly label.

That’s pretty much it. Start simple, have fun, and see how it goes.
