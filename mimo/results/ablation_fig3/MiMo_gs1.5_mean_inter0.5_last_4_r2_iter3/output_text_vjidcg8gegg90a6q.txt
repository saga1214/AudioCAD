For winter chicks, the best bedding is wood shavings. They are great insulation and are easy to clean up. Avoid using pine shavings that are treated with a chemical called phenol, as that can irritate the chicks. A deep litter method also works well here. Just add fresh shavings daily, and the composting underneath the chicks will generate natural warmth. Sand is another excellent, rodent-resistant option that helps keep the coop dry. Just remember to fluff the bedding daily to prevent it from getting too damp.
