Hey, it sounds like you're really missing what she brought to the table every year. A chocolate dessert is a nice way to remember her. A really easy one is a chocolate mug cake. You just need flour, cocoa powder, sugar, milk, and a little oil or butter. Mix it all in a mug and microwave it for about a minute. It's super quick and everyone loves it. Or, you could try a chocolate avocado mousse, which is healthier but still really good. Just blend avocado with melted chocolate and a little sweetener. You could serve it in cups, so it feels a little more special. Both are good options, and it's okay to do something simple this year.
