For an end-of-year celebration, focus on simplicity, inclusivity, and low stress—especially since you're pregnant! Here are a few ideas tailored to your situation:

### 🎉 Top Suggestions:
1. **Park Picnic/BBQ Potluck**  
   - **Why it works:** Casual, budget-friendly, and parents can contribute food.  
   - **Logistics:**  
     - Assign parents to bring specific items (e.g., "Sundae Bar toppings," "Pizza toppings," or "Sack race supplies").  
     - Pack easy snacks like chips/dip (dairy-free options available), fruit, sandwiches, and ice cream sundaes.  
     - Play games: **Sack races, relay races, or badge-themed crafts** (e.g., decorating a "trophy" for achievements).  

2. **Cafeteria Party**  
   - **Why it works:** No setup/logistics stress!  
   - **How to make it special:**  
     - Add a **themed sundae bar** (ice cream, sprinkles, candies) or **DIY pizza station** with toppings.  
     - Include **"trophy" awards** (e.g., "Best Helper," "Most Creative Badge Project").  

3. **Outdoor Movie Night**  
   - **Why it works:** Relaxing and nostalgic.  
   - **Tips:**  
     - Parents can bring a projector, screen, blankets, and snacks.  
     - Serve popcorn (dairy-free), watermelon, or frozen yogurt cups.  

### 💡 Key Considerations:
- **Food:**  
  - **Dairy-free chips/dip** (vegan ranch, hummus) and **fruit skewers** are easy crowd-pleasers.  
  - For pizza: Use dairy-free cheese or offer a separate vegan pizza.  
  - **Sundae bar** is a hit—just use dairy-free ice cream.  

- **Activities:**  
  - **Low-cost classics:** Sack races, three-legged races, or "Pin the Tail on the Tiger" (if you have a tiger badge).  
  - **Creative options:** Decorate a "trophy" cup, write thank-you notes to each other, or make friendship bracelets.  

- **Parents' Role:**  
  - **Involve them early**—ask for food/drink donations or help with setup/cleanup.  
  - **Keep dues reasonable** (e.g., $10–$20 for year-end party + supplies).  

### 🌟 Pro Tip:
End with a **group photo** (trophy or badge-themed) to commemorate the year! You’ve got this—your enthusiasm will make it memorable for everyone. 😊
