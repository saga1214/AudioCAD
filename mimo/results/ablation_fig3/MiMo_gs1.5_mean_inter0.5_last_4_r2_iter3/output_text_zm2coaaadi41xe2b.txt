Dallas has a ton of great Asian noodle spots! For authentic and delicious options, I'd recommend these places. First, there's Wonton Noodle House in Little Elm, which is known for its handmade noodles and flavorful broth. Another great place is Pho 79 in Plano, which serves up amazing Vietnamese pho. If you're looking for Chinese-inspired noodles, check out Din Tai Fung in the Dallas Galleria. And for a more casual vibe, check out Yummy Yum Noodle House in Richardson. As for protein bars, it depends on what you're looking for. If you want something high in protein, look for brands like Quest or Optimum Nutrition. If you prefer a more natural option, Clif Bars or RXBAR are good choices. Let me know if you want more recommendations!
