Yes, there are some excellent places to learn more about the history.

The best place to start is the Lockport Locks Museum, which is located right next to the "Flight of Five" locks themselves. You can see the locks in action and learn all about their history. You can also explore the area around the canal with the historic canal towpaths, which are now beautiful walking trails. The city even has a canal-themed festival every year called "Canal Days" in the summer.
