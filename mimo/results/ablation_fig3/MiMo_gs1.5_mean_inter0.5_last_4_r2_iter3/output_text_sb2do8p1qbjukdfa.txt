Okay, this is getting really fun! Let's break it down. For the refreshment table, I think I have a few ideas. 

For drinks, besides the infused water and teas, how about chilled coconut water? It's super hydrating and light. Or maybe sparkling water with a splash of fruit juice. For snacks, you could do mini fruit skewers with yogurt dip, or maybe some rice cakes with avocado or nut butter. And for that extra palate cleanser you mentioned, little mint leaves or even a small bowl of chilled grapes could work really well.

As for decorations, maybe a simple tablecloth with a subtle pattern, like a wavy or leaf design to keep with the theme. You could hang a few small paper fans for a breezy feel. For scents, a small bowl of cinnamon sticks or citrus peels can give a subtle, fresh aroma without being overwhelming.

And for the badge question... I'd definitely recommend printing new badges for appreciation weekend. It makes people feel special and recognized. You can just do a simple, clean design that fits the refresh theme, maybe with a water droplet or leaf motif.

So, to recap: for refreshments, think light and hydrating. For decorations, keep it cheerful and easy to clean. And for badges, new ones for the weekend are a great idea. Does this sound like a good start?
