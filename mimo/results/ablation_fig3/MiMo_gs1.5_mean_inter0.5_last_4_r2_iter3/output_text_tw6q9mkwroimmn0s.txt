That sounds like a fantastic theme! Here are some ocean-inspired meal and dessert ideas, keeping your fiancée's dietary needs in mind:

**Ocean-Themed Mains (5 Ideas):**

1.  **Spicy Coconut Curry with Tuna & Mango:** A vibrant, creamy curry featuring tuna (safe for shellfish allergy), mango chunks, and seasonal veggies like bell peppers and zucchini. Serve over brown rice (diabetic-friendly). *Uses both mango and spicy elements!*
2.  **Mediterranean Shrimp & Vegetable Skewers (or Scallop Skewers):** Marinated shrimp or scallops (safe!) with zucchini, bell peppers, red onion, and cherry tomatoes on skewers, grilled or broiled. Drizzle with a lemon-tahini sauce. *Light, fresh, and visually appealing.*
3.  **Tuna Poke Bowl with Avocado & Edamame:** A fresh, no-cook option. Top cooked rice with flaked tuna (safe!), creamy avocado, edamame, cucumber, scallions, sesame seeds, and a touch of soy sauce or ginger-sesame dressing. *Quick, healthy, and visually interesting.*
4.  **Pan-Seared Salmon with Mango Salsa & Quinoa:** Perfectly cooked salmon fillet topped with a fresh salsa of diced mango, red onion, cilantro, lime juice, and a pinch of chili flakes. Serve with quinoa for a complete meal. *Rich in omega-3s, uses mango, can be spicy.*
5.  **"Crab" Cakes with Remoulade (Use Shrimp or Crab-Style Mix):** Make flavorful cakes using well-drained canned white beans or a crab substitute mix (ensure shellfish-free!), mixed with finely chopped celery, onion, bell pepper, herbs (dill, parsley), and Old Bay seasoning. Pan-fry until golden. Serve with a tangy remoulade sauce. *Classic seafood dish, adaptable.*

**Ocean-Themed Desserts (5 Sweet Ideas):**

1.  **Coconut Shrimp "Cakes" with Honey-Lime Glaze:** Lightly breaded and pan-fried shrimp (or use large macadamia nuts for a nutty version) coated in a sweet-tart honey and lime glaze. Serve with fresh mango chunks on the side. *Playful, uses coconut (often safe for shellfish allergy), sweet & tangy.*
2.  **Mango Passionfruit Panna Cotta with Berry Coulis:** Silky vanilla panna cotta layered with vibrant mango and passionfruit puree. Serve topped with a light berry coulis (strained berry sauce) and fresh mint. *Incredibly refreshing, uses mango, naturally diabetic-friendly.*
3.  **Chocolate "Sea Wave" Cake:** A rich chocolate cake decorated with chocolate ganache piped to look like ocean waves. Serve with a side of fresh raspberries or blueberries for a pop of color. *Decadent, visually thematic.*
4.  **Ocean Fruit Tart with Sea Salt Finish:** A buttery shortcrust pastry filled with sweetened ricotta or cream cheese mixture. Top with a vibrant fruit curd (mango, passionfruit, or berry) and arrange fresh fruit (kiwi, blueberries, raspberries) to resemble waves or coral. Sprinkle with flaky sea salt. *Beautiful presentation, uses fruit flavors.*
5.  **Banana "Fishtails" with Chocolate Drizzle:** Slice bananas lengthwise, roll in melted chocolate (dark or milk), and shape the ends into little "fishtails." Chill to set. Serve with a sprinkle of sea salt or chopped nuts. *Fun, playful, simple.*

**Tips for Your Ocean Night:**

*   **Allergy Safety:** Double-check every ingredient, especially for shared items like sauces, dressings, or shared cookware. Be vigilant about cross-contamination.
*   **Mango Magic:** Incorporate mango into multiple dishes (curry, salsa, panna cotta, dessert glaze) for a cohesive theme.
*   **Spice Control:** Offer spicy options but also have milder versions available.
*   **Presentation:** Use blue/green tablecloths, blue/green napkins, seashell decorations, starfish, or driftwood accents. Maybe play ocean sounds softly.
*   **Music:** Curate a playlist with ocean waves and some calming or upbeat music.
