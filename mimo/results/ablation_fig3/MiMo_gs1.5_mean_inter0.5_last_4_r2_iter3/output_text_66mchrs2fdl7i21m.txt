To confirm, driving from L.A. to Phoenix takes about five and a half to six hours. Flying is much faster, with a direct flight taking about an hour.

Regarding owning a horse, you do need to check with your local county for any specific permits or regulations. However, you don't need a special state permit just to buy and own one. It's very important to remember that it's a major financial commitment. The costs include the purchase price, boarding, feed, and regular vet care. Leasing a horse is a great alternative, and there are plenty of lessons available if your granddaughter wants to try it out.
