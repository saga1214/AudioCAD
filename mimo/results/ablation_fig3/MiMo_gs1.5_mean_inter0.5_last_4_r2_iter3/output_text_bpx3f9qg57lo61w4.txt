Okay, this is a fun challenge! Your friend is clearly passionate about all these diverse hobbies while being eco-conscious—that's such a thoughtful person. Let's build a gift mix that feels personal and stays under $45. 

First, since you've already ruled out the obvious hiking, fishing and knitting gear, I'll focus on adjacent items that celebrate their values. For hiking, I'm thinking reusable essentials that make adventures better for nature. For knitting, something that elevates their craft sustainably. And for surfing, home decor that nods to their tournaments without duplicating sponsor haul items. 

Here’s a curated list with clear eco-benefits and personal touches:

**Hiking (reusable essentials)**  
1. **Solar-powered headlamp** ($35-40)  
   - Why it works: Keeps hands free for climbing while charging via sunlight—perfect for dawn/dusk hikes. Brands like BioLite have stylish designs.  
   - Eco-benefit: Replaces disposable batteries and reduces waste.  

2. **Bamboo fiber trekking poles** ($40)  
   - Why it works: Lighter than aluminum and biodegradable. Great for stability on rough trails.  
   - Eco-benefit: Made from renewable bamboo (no mining).  

**Knitting (sustainable accessories)**  
1. **Organic cotton stitch markers** ($12)  
   - Why it works: Tiny but thoughtful—helps track progress in patterns. Look for sets with natural stones.  
   - Eco-benefit: GOTS-certified cotton means no toxic dyes.  

2. **Recycled wool knitting bag** ($30)  
   - Why it works: A soft, insulated pouch for projects on the go. Choose one with a hemp lining.  
   - Eco-benefit: Repurposes plastic bottles into wool.  

**Surfing (home decor)**  
1. **Recycled ocean plastic surfboard hook** ($25)  
   - Why it works: Hangs boards on walls without scratching. Made from beach cleanup materials.  
   - Eco-benefit: Gives plastic waste a new life.  

2. **Handmade driftwood keychain** ($15)  
   - Why it works: A subtle nod to coastal adventures. Find local artisans who use reclaimed wood.  
   - Eco-benefit: Celebrates nature without exploiting it.  

**Bonus thought**: Wrap gifts in reusable fabric bags (like those from reusable produce bags) to double down on the eco-theme!  

This mix hits each hobby with a unique twist—no duplicates, all under budget, and packed with meaning. Your friend will feel seen for their whole lifestyle, not just one hobby. Happy birthday planning! 🎁🌿
