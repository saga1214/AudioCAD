Of course. Based on our conversation, here is a shopping list for your New Year's Eve party. I've broken it down into categories to make it easier to follow.

First, for drinks: you'll need sparkling wine for the champagne, sparkling cider for the kids, and I'd also suggest some non-alcoholic sparkling juice and water. You'll also need a bottle of wine, maybe a red or white.

Next, for the main course: you'll need a large ham, about 14 to 16 pounds for your group. You'll also need potatoes, like russet or Yukon gold, and butter for the scalloped potatoes. For the roasted carrots, you'll need carrots, olive oil, salt, and pepper.

For the other sides: you'll need asparagus, olive oil, lemon, and salt for the roasted asparagus, and green beans for the green bean side dish, which you can do with or without bacon.

For appetizers, you'll need ground beef or Italian sausage for the sliders, slider buns, shredded cheese, and condiments. For the pigs in a blanket, you'll need sausage rolls and cream cheese. For the shrimp cocktail, you'll need shrimp and cocktail sauce. And you'll need puff pastry for the mini quiches.

For kids' food and drinks, you'll need grape juice and snacks like pretzels or crackers.

As for your own party supplies, you'll want to grab a signature cocktail ingredients, ice, cups, plates, napkins, utensils, and of course, those kid-safe plastic flutes for the toast.

I'd also add a few other things to consider, like having plenty of trash and recycling bins, and maybe some wet wipes or a small first-aid kit for cleanup. Enjoy your party!
