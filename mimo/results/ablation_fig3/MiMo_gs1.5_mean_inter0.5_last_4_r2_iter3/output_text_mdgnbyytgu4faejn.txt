Okay, let's put it all together. Here's a standard and effective format you can follow:

First, at the very top, you have your name in about a 14 to 16-point font. Right below that, on a separate line, is your contact info: email, phone number, city. You can use small icons, but remember they might cause ATS issues, so it's safer to skip them. You can also add a link to your LinkedIn profile.

Next, you'll have a professional summary. This is a short paragraph, maybe three to five sentences, that highlights your most relevant skills and career goals.

After that, list your experience. This is your main section. You'll want to list your most recent and relevant jobs first. For each job, include the company name, your job title, and the dates you worked there. Then, use bullet points to describe two or three key achievements. Just keep it concise and focused on what you accomplished.

Then comes your skills section. It's very important. You should tailor this to the job you're applying for. List key skills, maybe starting with hard skills like software or technical abilities, and then follow up with soft skills like communication or leadership.

After that, you'll have your education. List your degree, the school, and your graduation date. Right there, you can also add any honors or awards you received, like 'Graduated with Highest Honors' or the name of a scholarship.

If you have a lot of experience, you can add a section for additional work history, but just list it very briefly.

Finally, you can have an optional 'Other' section for things like certifications or volunteer work.

This structure is clean, professional, and makes it easy for a recruiter to find what they need. Just remember to tailor your summary and skills section for each job you apply to.
