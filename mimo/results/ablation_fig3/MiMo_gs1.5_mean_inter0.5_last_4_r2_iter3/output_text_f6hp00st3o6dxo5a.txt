That's an interesting twist. If we focus on the genetic content of the egg, we could argue that the egg contained the blueprint for the first chicken.

However, a genetic blueprint isn't enough. The egg also needs to be produced by an organism that is already part of the chicken family. Since the hen must possess the chicken protein to form the shell, the hen has to be a chicken, or at least a very close ancestor. Therefore, the first chicken still had to come from the egg of a non-chicken.
