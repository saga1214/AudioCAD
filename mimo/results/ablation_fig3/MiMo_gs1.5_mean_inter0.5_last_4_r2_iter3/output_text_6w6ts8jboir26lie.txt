Okay, you're on the mend, which is great! I'm really glad to hear that. Taking control of your own meal is a wonderful idea. 

Here are five simple and satisfying things you can whip up from the grocery store:

First, a classic one-pot pasta. Just get some pasta, your favorite sauce, and some veggies. It's quick, comforting, and easy. 

Second, a stir-fry. Grab some rice noodles, a protein like chicken or tofu, and whatever veggies you like. It's super fast and healthy.

Third, a hearty omelet or a frittata. You can load it with cheese, veggies, and meat for a quick, protein-packed meal.

Fourth, you could try a simple sheet pan dinner. Toss some chicken and veggies on a pan, drizzle with oil and seasonings, and bake it. It's minimal cleanup, which is nice.

And fifth, a cozy bowl of soup. You can make a quick version with broth, beans, and veggies, or even buy a good quality soup from the store and heat it up. 

Whatever you choose, I hope it feels like a nice little break. Just take it easy.
