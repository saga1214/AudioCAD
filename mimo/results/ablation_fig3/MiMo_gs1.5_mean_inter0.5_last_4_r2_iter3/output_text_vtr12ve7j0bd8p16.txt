I'm sorry, I don't have a personal life, but based on my knowledge of Calgary, there are plenty of great adventures nearby. You could head to Banff National Park for hiking and the famous Lake Louise. Just a little further is the Kananaskis Country area, which has amazing trails and a great lake called Kananaskis Lake. For something closer, you could explore the Bow River Pathways for a scenic bike ride, or visit Fish Creek Provincial Park. If you're up for a drive, the Canmore Dinosaur Museum is a fun trip. For a really spontaneous adventure, you could even take a day trip to the Kicking Horse Mountain Bike Park in Canmore.
