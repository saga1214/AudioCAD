Here’s a **balanced 7-day itinerary** for deep exploration of Tokyo, Osaka, Nara, and Kyoto, optimized for sightseeing efficiency while allowing time for your coursework. Adjust based on your pace:

---

### **Tokyo (4 Days)**  
**Day 1: Arrival & Iconic Landmarks**  
- Morning: Arrive in Tokyo, check into hotel (stay near Shinjuku/Shibuya).  
- Afternoon: **Shibuya Scramble Crossing**, Hachiko Statue, Shibuya Sky (panoramic views).  
- Evening: **Shinjuku** (Lunch at Omoide Yokocho, explore Golden Gai).  
- *Workout*: Evening gym session.  

**Day 2: Tradition & Nature**  
- Morning: **Asakusa** (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Street).  
- Afternoon: **Ueno Park** (Museums: Tokyo National Museum + Tokyo Museum of Art for Art History research).  
- Evening: **Akihabara** (tech/gaming culture).  
- *Workout*: Morning or afternoon gym.  

**Day 3: Modern Tokyo & Culture**  
- Morning: **Harajuku** (Takeshita Street, Meiji Shrine).  
- Afternoon: **Shibuya** (Shibuya 109, Hachiko Park).  
- Evening: **Roppongi** (nightlife, Mori Art Museum).  
- *Workout*: Morning or afternoon gym.  

**Day 4: Day Trip to Nikko or Kamakura**  
- **Option A (Nikko)**: UNESCO sites (Toshogu Shrine, Kegon Falls).  
- **Option B (Kamakura)**: Great Buddha, Tsurugaoka Shrine, bamboo forest.  
- Evening: Return to Tokyo, dinner.  
- *Workout*: Morning or afternoon gym.  

---

### **Osaka (3 Days)**  
**Day 5: Arrival & Osaka Castle**  
- Morning: Train to Osaka, check into hotel (stay near Namba).  
- Afternoon: **Osaka Castle** (museum inside), Kuromon Market (food).  
- Evening: **Dotonbori** (street food, neon lights).  
- *Workout*: Morning or afternoon gym.  

**Day 6: Kyoto Day Trip (Full Day)**  
- Train to Kyoto (45 min).  
- Morning: **Kiyomizu-dera Temple**, Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka streets.  
- Afternoon: **Ginkaku-ji** (Silver Pavilion), Ryoan-ji (Zen rock garden).  
- Evening: Return to Osaka.  
- *Workout*: Morning or afternoon gym.  

**Day 7: Nara Day Trip (Full Day)**  
- Train to Nara (30 min).  
- Morning: **Todai-ji Temple** (Great Buddha), Nara Park (deer).  
- Afternoon: **Kasuga Taisha Shrine**, return to Osaka.  
- Evening: **Universal Studios Japan** or dinner in Namba.  
- *Workout*: Morning or afternoon gym.  

---

### **Kyoto (2 Days – Stay Overnight)**  
**Day 8: Kyoto Highlights**  
- Morning: **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (early to avoid crowds).  
- Afternoon: **Arashiyama** (Bamboo Grove, Tenryu-ji Temple).  
- Evening: Stay in Gion district.  
- *Workout*: Morning or afternoon gym.  

**Day 9: Kyoto’s Culture**  
- Morning: **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion), Ryoan-ji.  
- Afternoon: **Gion** (geisha district), Nishiki Market.  
- Evening: Return to Osaka.  
- *Workout*: Morning or afternoon gym.
