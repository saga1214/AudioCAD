Okay, let's break this down. Vasculitis is inflammation of the blood vessels. This inflammation can affect vessels of any size, and the symptoms depend on which vessels are involved. For example, large vessel issues might cause joint pain, while small vessel problems can lead to skin rashes. It's a complex topic, so it's great that you're seeking clarity.

As for other potential causes of chronic redness, besides vasculitis, things like rosacea, sun damage, allergies, or even genetics could be at play. A proper diagnosis is the key to finding the right solution.
