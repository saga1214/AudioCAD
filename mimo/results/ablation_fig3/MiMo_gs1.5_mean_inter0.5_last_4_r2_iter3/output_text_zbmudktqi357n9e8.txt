Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn inspiration! The season there is just beautiful. You've got the classic fall colors in the north and central states, and the southern areas have these incredible, lush landscapes. 

So, if you're heading to Mexico for autumn inspiration, I'd say spring is your best bet. That's from about March to May. The weather is warm and dry, perfect for outdoor painting. You can find amazing autumn-like scenes in the northern states like Chihuahua or Nuevo León, where the golden grasslands and mesas look just like fall back home. Or you could go to the southern Yucatán Peninsula, where the tropical foliage gives off that rich, earthy vibe. 

Just a couple of quick tips: spring is peak tourist season, so book your place early. And definitely pack your paints and brushes because you're going to be creating a lot. Have an amazing time!
