### 🌟 **Top Diving Spots in Costa Rica**
1. **Pacific Coast (Best for Whale Sharks & Thrill Dives)**  
   - **Cocos Island** (2.5-hour boat ride):  
     - **Must-see**: Whale sharks (season: Jan–Apr), manta rays, giant Pacific rays, and sperm whales.  
     - **Dive Sites**: *Punta Mina*, *Rancho Matapalo*.  
   - **Ostional** (Golfo Dulce):  
     - **Best for**: Clear, calm waters, tropical fish, and coral reefs.  
     - **Dive Sites**: *Punta Mina*, *Playa Maderas*.  

2. **Caribbean Coast (Best for Reefs & Shipwrecks)**  
   - **Tortuguero** (Palm Island):  
     - **Must-see**: Coral reefs, turtles, and the *Tortuga wreck* (a WWII-era ship).  
     - **Dive Sites**: *Palm Island*, *Isla Blanca*.  
   - **Cahuita** (National Park):  
     - **Best for**: Easy access, vibrant reefs, and marine mammals.  
     - **Dive Sites**: *Cahuita Reef*, *Punta Pez*.  

3. **Golfo Dulce** (Best for Clear Water & Night Dives)  
   - **Quepos** (Pacuare River):  
     - **Best for**: Night dives to spot bioluminescence and nocturnal species.

- **Days 1–3: Caribbean Coast**

  - Return to **Ostional** for calm, reef-focused dives.  
- **Days 7–9: Return to Caribbean**
