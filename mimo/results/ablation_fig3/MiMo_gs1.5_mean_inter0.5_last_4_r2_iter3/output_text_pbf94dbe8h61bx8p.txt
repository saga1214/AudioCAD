Okay, so you want simple, meaningful gifts where everyone, from ages 2 to 15, can get involved, and you want to minimize supervision. Let me think of a few more ideas that fit that.

First, there's always a simple paper chain. The kids can decorate individual links with drawings or stickers, and you can tie them together to make a chain. It’s easy, customizable, and you can hang it up as a decoration while they work on it.

Another idea is making seed bombs. You just mix clay, soil, and wildflower seeds, roll them into balls, and let them dry. You can then bury them in a garden bed, and they’ll grow into flowers. It’s eco-friendly and fun.

For macramé, since you love it, you could adapt it for the little ones. For example, you could make a simple macramé plant hanger and let the kids decorate it with beads or small trinkets. Or you could make a macramé gift tag for presents.

For the older kids, you could do a collaborative art project, like a big holiday-themed mural on a poster board. Everyone can contribute their own drawings or designs.

The key is to pick something that’s simple enough that the kids can do independently but still feel meaningful when they give it as a gift. Does one of those sound like a good starting point?
