That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour Beach Resort puts you right in the heart of Coki Beach on St. John, which is arguably the most vibrant and beautiful beach area on the island. Here are some great daytime activities for you and your brother:

1.  **Explore Reef Bay:** Just a short walk from Secret Harbour, this stunning, secluded bay is perfect for a peaceful walk along the beach path. It features ancient petroglyphs and is a great spot for a quiet swim and snorkel in the calmer waters.
2.  **Snorkel at Coki Beach:** This is the main attraction! The reef off Coki Beach is teeming with life, especially during the summer months. You'll see parrotfish, surgeonfish, sea turtles (often resting on the sandy bottom), and maybe even a small reef shark or rays. Grab a snorkel mask and fins – it's incredible. (Keep it peaceful for the marine life).
3.  **Kayak or Stand-Up Paddleboard (SUP):** Rent kayaks or SUPs right at Coki Beach from local vendors. Paddle out to the reef or along the coast for a different perspective. It's a great way to explore the coastline and get up close to the underwater world.
4.  **Hike the Virgin Islands National Park Trails:** St. John's trails are well-maintained and offer stunning views without strenuous climbs. The most popular and accessible trail is the **Aerial Tramway** (a short shuttle ride from Cruz Bay), which takes you up to **Honeymoon Beach** (a beautiful, quiet beach with great snorkeling) and **Sage Mountain** (panoramic views of Cruz Bay and the island). Another easy trail is the **Cinnamon Bay Trail**, leading to the famous Cinnamon Bay and Anegrae Beach (great for swimming and snorkeling).
5.  **Relax on the Beach:** Sometimes the best activity is simply finding a shady spot under a palm tree, reading a book, listening to the waves, and soaking up the Caribbean atmosphere. The hammocks at Secret Harbour are perfect for this!
6.  **Visit the Soggy Dollar Bar (For the Experience!):** While primarily known for sunset drinks (and their famous Painkiller), Soggy Dollar is also a fantastic spot for lunch or an afternoon drink. It's a bit of a trek from Secret Harbour (about 15-20 mins), but it's a unique, rustic vibe. They serve delicious food and have a lovely pool. *Note: This is more of a destination for a treat than a quick stop.*
7.  **Explore Cruz Bay:** Take a stroll into the charming Cruz Bay village. Browse the unique shops, find the post office, grab a fresh coconut, and enjoy the lively atmosphere. It's a great way to feel connected to the local community.

**Key Tips for Your Daytime Adventures:**

*   **Sun Protection:** St. John has intense sun. Wear hats, sunglasses, and reef-safe sunscreen.
*   **Water Temperature:** The water is usually warm, but if you have sensitive ears, stick to shallower areas or the sandy bottom for snorkeling. Avoid diving or submerging your head too deeply.
*   **Hydration & Snacks:** Bring plenty of water and snacks.
*   **Respect Wildlife:** Observe marine life and birds from a distance. Never touch or feed them.
*   **Island Time:** Embrace the relaxed pace. Don't try to cram everything in; enjoy the moments.

Staying at Secret Harbour means you're perfectly positioned for easy access to Coki Beach and its fantastic snorkeling, combined with the charm of Reef Bay and the village of Cruz Bay. Have an amazing trip!
