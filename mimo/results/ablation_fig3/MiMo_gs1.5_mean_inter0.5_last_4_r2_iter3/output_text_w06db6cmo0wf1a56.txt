---

### **Drinks**  
*(All in single-serve packaging)*  
- **Water bottles** (20 large reusable bottles)  
- **Sugar-free sodas** (e.g., Coke Zero, Sprite Zero) – 10 cans per adult  
- **Low-sugar juice boxes** (for kids) – 10 boxes  
- **Electrolyte drink packs** (e.g., Liquid I.V. or Nuun) – for hydration  
- **Ice packs** (to keep drinks cold)  

---

### **Snacks**  
*(No utensils needed, easy to grab-and-go)*  
- **Cheese sticks** (10–12 per family)  
- **Baby carrots** (10 bags)  
- **Single-serve hummus cups** (10)  
- **Trail mix** (nuts, dried fruit, dark chocolate chips) – 10 individual bags  
- **Pretzels or rice crackers** (10 bags)  
- **Granola bars** (healthy, no-mess) – 10 boxes  

---

### **Main Meals**  
*(Pre-made, no reheating needed)*  
- **Mini calzones** (from a pizza place) – 10 boxes (10 calzones total)  
  → *Ask for plain cheese or veggie options to keep it simple.*  
- **Sandwich/wrap boxes** (pre-cut & wrapped) – 10 boxes  
  → *Include turkey, ham, veggie, or PB&J options.*  
- **Pita chips or whole-grain crackers** (10 boxes)  
- **Yogurt cups** (Greek or regular) – 10 cups  

---

### **Dessert**  
*(No utensils required)*  
- **Brownie cups or individual brownie packs** (10 boxes)  
  → *Easy, mess-free, and universally loved!*  
- **Mini cupcakes** (from a bakery) – 10 boxes  
  → *Or opt for a single **red velvet or chocolate cake** cut into 10 slices (if you prefer cake over pie).*  

---

### **Extras & Essentials**  
- **Reusable cooler bags** (2 large ones)  
- **Napkins** (10 rolls)  
- **Utensils** (10 sets: forks/spoons, plus 10 kids’ plastic sets)  
- **Sunscreen & lip balm** (for kids/adults)  
- **Small trash bags** (for easy cleanup)  

---

### **Key Tips for Success**  
1. **Calzone Compromise**: If you’re set on real calzones, order **mini versions** from a local pizza place. They’re faster, easier to eat, and less messy than full-size ones.  
2. **Dietary Notes**: Label containers if some friends have allergies (e.g., "No nuts").  
3. **Cooler Organization**:  
   - **Top shelf**: Drinks (keep cold with ice).  
   - **Middle shelf**: Sandwiches/wraps.  
   - **Bottom shelf**: Calzones and desserts (keep warm with a cooler bag).  
4. **Kid-Friendly Extras**: Add **fruit leather, cheese cubes, or mini muffins** for picky eaters.
