Here's a step-by-step guide for each installation, keeping noise and safety in mind:

### 📺 1. TV Mount Installation  
**Tools Needed:** Tape measure, level, stud finder, drill, wall anchors (if no studs), screwdriver.  
**Steps:**  
- **Locate studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (critical for heavy TVs).  
- **Measure & plan:** Decide TV height (center at eye level when seated).  
- **Mark drill points:** Use a pencil to mark screw holes.  
- **Drill pilot holes:** Drill slightly smaller holes than your screws.  
- **Install mounts:** Attach mounting plate to TV *first*, then screw into wall (use anchors if no studs).  
**Tip:** Check weight limits! Use a VESA-rated mount matching your TV’s weight.

### 🌿 2. Hanging Planters  
**Tools Needed:** Suction cups (for lightweight plants), strong fishing line (for heavier plants), measuring tape, level.  
**Steps:**  
- **Choose secure method:**  
  - *Lightweight:* Suction cups (test suction strength first).  
  - *Heavy:* Use fishing line tied to ceiling hooks (avoid nails).  
- **Hang securely:** Ensure plants won’t swing or fall.  
- **Test weight:** Place a bag of water in the planter to simulate full weight.  
**Safety Note:** Never hang lamps or weighted items this way!

### 🔧 3. Floating Shelves  
**Tools Needed:** Measuring tape, level, drill, wall anchors, L-brackets (optional for stability).  
**Steps:**  
- **Measure twice:** Mark shelf top and bottom positions (e.g., 18" apart for small shelves).  
- **Drill pilot holes:** Drill into studs *or* secure anchors in drywall.  
- **Install brackets:** Attach L-brackets to wall first, then screw shelves to brackets.  
**Critical:**  
- Don’t overhang the wall edge.  
- Use L-brackets if shelves exceed 24" or hold >20 lbs.  
- Check shelf stability after installation!

### 🛠️ General Tips  
- **Drill noise:** Do this early when neighbors aren’t home. Use a cordless drill to minimize noise.  
- **Safety First:** Wear safety glasses. Use a step stool for high walls.  
- **Landlord Rules:** Double-check your lease—some require permits for wall modifications.  
- **Test Everything:** After installation, gently test weight limits (e.g., place a book on the shelf).
