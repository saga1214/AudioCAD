Okay, so for a high-performance American sports car under ninety thousand dollars, here are a few great options that fit what you're looking for.

First, the Chevrolet Corvette, specifically the Z06 or ZR1. It's got over 490 horsepower, a mid-engine setup, and modern tech like CarPlay. You can find one used from within the last five years.

Next, the Ford Mustang Shelby GT500. It has a super powerful V8, over 600 horsepower, and is way more practical for daily driving. It also has all the latest tech.

A third option is the Dodge Challenger or Charger, with the SRT Hellcat models. These have an insane 717 to 807 horsepower from a V8, and they're pretty fun to drive in the rain, though they are a bit heavier.

Fourth, you could look at a used American muscle car with a V10, like a Ford Mustang Shelby GT350. It's got about 525 horsepower and is known for being incredibly reliable for a supercar.

And finally, for that ultimate thrill, there's the Dodge Challenger SRT Demon. It has a V8 with 840 horsepower and is basically designed to be a mid-life crisis car. It's an absolute beast, but it's really loud and attention-grabbing, which ties back to your paint color question.

So yeah, for maximum power and a thrill, the Dodge Hellcat models are probably your best bet. For a more balanced, daily-friendly choice, the Corvette Z06 or the Mustang GT500 are fantastic. And if you want something that feels a bit more like a supercar, the Ford GT350 is a great V10 option.
