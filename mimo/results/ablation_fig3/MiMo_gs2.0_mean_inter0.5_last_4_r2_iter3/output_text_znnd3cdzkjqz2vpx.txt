Okay, you've got all the essentials, but there are a few more things you should grab to make this go smoothly and safely.

First, you'll definitely need a power drill and some wall anchors. Habitat Restores usually sell them, so you can check if they have any. You'll need them for the base cabinets if you can't find studs, and for hanging things like the upper cabinets' backsplash or the countertop later.

Also, grab a couple of large clamps. They'll be super helpful for holding cabinets steady while you're screwing them in.

And make sure you have a box of shims handy. They're little pieces of wood for adjusting the cabinets so everything is perfectly level and square.

You might also want a pencil and a tape measure just in case you need to double-check any measurements while you're working.

Oh, and lastly, please make sure you have at least one friend who can act as your spotter to hand you tools or help steady things. It makes a huge difference. You've got this!
