Ooh, opening night for a play is a huge moment! A thoughtful gift would mean a lot. Since she’s put in so much work, maybe a personalized thank-you note with your own words of encouragement. Or a small treat, like her favorite chocolates or ice cream, for an instant reward. If she’s into fashion, a cute play-related accessory like a little theater mask pin or a playbill frame could be sweet. Or, you could even surprise her with a short, fun activity afterward, like a quick dessert run or a ride on a hoverboard, just to make the night extra special! The key is something that shows you truly appreciate her effort.
