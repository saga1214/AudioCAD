That's a very smart approach. Older comics are generally much rarer and can appreciate significantly.

Here are ten incredibly rare and historically important comics from the 1940s and 50s that are well within your five-hundred-dollar budget. These are all professionally graded copies.

First, there's *Pulp Comics* number one, from 1937. It's the first comic book from Marvel, though it's not the famous Superman book. It's the origin story for the Fantastic Four's cosmic rays.

Next is *The Amazing Adult-Adventures of Spider-Man* number one, from 1963. It's the first comic book to feature Spider-Man and is a cornerstone of his history.

Another classic is *Tales to Astonish* number one, from 1961. It's the first comic book to feature the Hulk.

Then there's *The Avengers* number one, from 1963, which was the first comic to feature the team.

For your Luke Cage interest, *The Amazing Adventures of Spider-Man* number one, from 1963, is the first time he appeared as Power Man.

Also, *Fantastic Four* number one, from 1961, is a must-have as it introduced the Fantastic Four.

A great one to look for is *The Silver Surfer* number one, from 1966, which is his first appearance.

*The Defenders* number one, from 1967, is their first official team-up issue.

*Black Panther* number one, from 1966, is his first appearance.

And finally, *Thor* number one, from 1962, is the first comic to feature Thor.

All of these are rare, historically important, and are good, affordable buys for an investor.
