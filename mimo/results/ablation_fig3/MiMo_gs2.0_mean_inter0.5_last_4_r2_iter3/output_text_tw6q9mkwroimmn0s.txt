That sounds like a fantastic theme! Here are some ocean-inspired meal and dessert ideas that accommodate shellfish-free, diabetic-friendly, spicy preferences, and love for mango:

**Ocean-Themed Main Courses:**

1.  **Grilled Salmon with Mango-Cilantro Salsa & Avocado:** (Diabetic-Friendly, Spicy, Mango)
    *   **Salmon:** Pan-seared or grilled with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a touch of chili flakes.
    *   **Mango-Cilantro Salsa:** Fresh mango chunks mixed with diced red onion, jalapeño (for heat), cilantro, lime juice, and a pinch of salt.
    *   **Side:** Quinoa or brown rice bowl with steamed broccoli.
    *   *Garnish:* Tiny seashells (real or plastic), blue food coloring on the plate.

2.  **Spicy Coconut Curry with Baked Cod & Mango:** (Diabetic-Friendly, Spicy, Mango, Shellfish-Free)
    *   **Curry:** A fragrant coconut milk-based curry with onions, garlic, ginger, curry paste (use a low-sugar brand or make your own), and spices like cumin, coriander, turmeric, and a dash of chili paste.
    *   **Protein:** Baked cod fillets seasoned with salt, pepper, and a tiny bit of smoked paprika.
    *   **Mango:** Fresh mango chunks added towards the end of cooking the curry for sweetness and freshness.
    *   **Side:** Steamed basmati rice or cauliflower rice.
    *   *Garnish:* Fresh cilantro, a swirl of lime juice, maybe a tiny edible flower.

3.  **Pan-Seared Scallops (or Tuna Steaks) in Mango-Chili Butter with Sautéed Spinach:** (Diabetic-Friendly, Spicy, Mango, Shellfish-Free - Tuna alternative)
    *   **Scallops/Tuna:** Sear in a hot pan with butter, olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, and a blend of chili flakes and smoked paprika.
    *   **Mango-Chili Butter:** Reduce butter with fresh mango chunks, minced garlic, and red chili flakes until slightly thickened. Spoon over the scallops/tuna.
    *   **Side:** Sautéed spinach with garlic and a squeeze of lemon.
    *   *Garnish:* Microgreens, a few blueberries (for sweetness contrast), seashell decor.

4.  **Vegetarian: Stuffed Portobello Mushrooms with Spinach & Herbs:** (Diabetic-Friendly, Spicy Option)
    *   **Mushrooms:** Large portobello caps marinated briefly in balsamic vinegar, olive oil, garlic, and herbs (thyme, rosemary).
    *   **Filling:** Sautéed spinach, garlic, onions, bell peppers, and herbs mixed with breadcrumbs (use whole wheat if preferred), a pinch of chili flakes, and a dollop of low-sugar ricotta or Greek yogurt.
    *   **Garnish:** Fresh parsley, maybe a drizzle of balsamic glaze (use sparingly), seashell decor.

**Ocean-Themed Desserts (Sweet & Diabetic-Friendly):**

1.  **Mango Coconut Flan:** (Sweet, Mango, Diabetic-Friendly)
    *   **Flan:** A rich, creamy flan made with eggs, coconut milk, a touch of sugar, and vanilla. Serve chilled.
    *   **Garnish:** A slice of fresh mango, toasted coconut flakes, a few blueberries (for color), and a tiny seashell on the plate.

2.  **Dark Chocolate Sea Salt Tart with Mango Compote:** (Sweet, Diabetic-Friendly, Ocean-Themed)
    *   **Tart:** A simple tart shell (graham crackers or gluten-free) filled with a layer of dark chocolate mousse (use high-cocoa, low-sugar chocolate) and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt.
    *   **Compote:** A warm, thick mango puree or compote for sweetness and color.
    *   *Garnish:* Fresh mint leaves, maybe a tiny sprinkle of edible glitter.

3.  **Key Lime Tart with Coconut Whipped Cream:** (Sweet, Citrus-Ocean, Diabetic-Friendly)
    *   **Tart:** A classic key lime pie filling (citrusy, tangy) in a graham cracker crust.
    *   **Coconut Whipped Cream:** Lightly sweetened coconut cream whipped with a touch of powdered sugar or monk fruit sweetener.
    *   *Garnish:* Fresh mint, a thin slice of key lime, tiny blueberries.

4.  **Coconut Rice Pudding with Mango & Cardamom:** (Sweet, Coconut, Diabetic-Friendly)
    *   **Pudding:** Creamy coconut milk rice pudding cooked with a pinch of cinnamon and cardamom (optional but lovely). Sweetened minimally with a touch of honey or maple syrup (use sparingly).
    *   **Toppings:** Dollop of coconut whipped cream, fresh mango cubes, toasted coconut flakes.
    *   *Garnish:* A few blueberries, maybe a tiny edible seashell.

5.  **Strawberry & Basil Panna Cotta with Raspberry Coulis:** (Sweet, Fresh, Diabetic-Friendly)
    *   **Panna Cotta:** A silky vanilla panna cotta made with cream, milk, gelatin, and a touch of sweetener (stevia/monk fruit blend works well).
    *   **Coulis:** A vibrant raspberry sauce for sweetness and color.
    *   **Garnish:** Fresh basil leaves, a few fresh strawberries, maybe a few blueberries.

**Key Tips for Your Ocean Night:**

*   **Garnish Generously:** Use seashells (plastic or real), starfish, blue food coloring/paint, edible flowers, mint leaves, and fresh fruit.
*   **Spice Wisely:** Use chili flakes, cayenne pepper, sriracha, fresh chilies (jalapeño, serrano), or ginger to add heat to mains and dressings.
*   **Sweeten Smartly:** Rely on fresh fruit (mango, berries), small amounts of honey/maple syrup, coconut sugar, monk fruit, or stevia in desserts. Citrus zest adds sweetness without sugar.
*   **Check Labels:** Especially on curry pastes, sauces, and pre-made items for hidden sugars and shellfish derivatives.

Have a wonderful and delicious ocean-themed evening! Enjoy cooking for yourself and your fiyaase.
