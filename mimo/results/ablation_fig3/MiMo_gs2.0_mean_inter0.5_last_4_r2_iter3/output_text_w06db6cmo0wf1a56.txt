Absolutely! Here's a practical, low-mess shopping list tailored for your 10 dads, 10 kids, and theme park day, based on your requests:

### 🥤 DRINKS (Pre-filled bottles/boxes for grab-and-go)
*   **Water:** 20 large water bottles (1L or 2L)
*   **Sugar-Free Sodas:** 10-12 cans or 10-12 boxes (divided into 10 groups for dads/kids)
*   **Low-Sugar Juice Boxes:** 10-12 boxes (for kids who prefer juice)
*   **Sports Drinks (Optional):** 10 boxes (for very active kids or hot weather)

### 🥪 SNACKS (Individual portions)
*   **Cheese Sticks:** 20 sticks
*   **Grapes:** 1 large bunch (pre-portioned into 10 small bags)
*   **Pretzels:** 1 large bag, divided into 10 individual snack-size bags
*   **Trail Mix:** 1 large bag, divided into 10 individual bags
*   **Baby Carrots:** 1 large bag, divided into 10 individual bags
*   **Single-Serve Hummus Cups:** 10 cups
*   **Veggie Sticks (Optional):** Cucumber slices or bell pepper strips (in 10 individual bags)
*   **Ranch Dip Cups (Optional):** For veggies/chips

### 🍕 MAIN MEAL (Pre-ordered & easy to eat)
*   **Real Calzones (Key Request!):**  
    *   **Option 1 (Best Mess Control):** Order 10 mini calzones from a local pizzeria. They're bite-sized, individually wrapped, and easy for everyone.  
    *   **Option 2 (Traditional):** Order 10 standard calzones. *Crucial:* Ask the pizzeria to pack them *separately* in 10 individual boxes (no sharing boxes!).  
    *   *Content Ideas:* Pepperoni & cheese, sausage & mushroom, veggie & cheese. Include a couple of different fillings.
*   **OR Pizza:** 1 large cheese pizza and 1 large pepperoni pizza (cut into 10 slices each, pre-packaged in 10 individual boxes). *Cheaper/easier alternative to calzones.*

### 🍰 DESSERT (Grab-and-go treats)
*   **Cupcakes:** 10 cupcakes (plain or simple flavor like vanilla, chocolate, or carrot cake) OR
*   **Brownie Cups:** 10 individual brownie cups (pre-packaged from a bakery or grocery store). *Brownies are often a huge hit with both kids and dads!*

### 🧊 PRACTICAL Additions for Coolers
*   **Ice Packs/Ice Bags:** Generous amount (at least 3-4 large ones).
*   **Coolers:** You'll need at least 2 large coolers (one for drinks/snacks, one for main meal/dessert).
*   **Insulated Drink Bottles/Thermoses:** For dads who prefer hot coffee/tea (optional but nice).
*   **Wet Wipes & Hand Sanitizer:** Essential for spills and quick cleanups.
*   **Trash Bags:** Small ziplock bags for easy litter disposal.
*   **Rope/Ties:** To secure coolers shut.

**Key Tips for Success:**

1.  **Order Calzones/Pizza Early:** Give the pizzeria plenty of notice (at least 1-2 days) so they can pack them perfectly.
2.  **Pack Smart:** Use clear containers or ziplock bags for snacks so everyone can see and grab what they want. Group similar items together.
3.  **Assign Coolers:** Designate one main cooler for drinks/snacks and another for the calzones/pizza and dessert. This makes grabbing easier.
4.  **Label:** Put small labels on cooler groups if needed (e.g., "Drinks & Snacks," "Main Meal").
5.  **Check Allergies:** Quickly confirm if any kids have dietary restrictions (nuts, dairy, etc.) before buying snacks/trail mix.

This plan gives everyone choices (soda, calzone/pizza, cupcakes/brownies) while keeping things organized, mess-free, and focused on enjoying the theme park! Have an absolutely fantastic bachelor party! 🎢🎉
