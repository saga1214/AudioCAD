Here's a quick recap of the analogy for kids:

- **Heart**: The gas station and engine. Pumps the blood-cars everywhere!
- **Lungs**: The refueling stop. Blood-cars pick up fresh oxygen fuel here.
- **Capillaries**: Tiny streets. Deliver oxygen fuel to every cell.
- **Veins**: Return roads. Carry blood-cars back to the heart/lungs to refuel.
- **Kidneys**: Car washes. Filter out junk and extra water from the blood.
- **Liver**: Service station. Processes nutrients and breaks down harmful stuff.
