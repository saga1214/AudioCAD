Initally, you could schedule a brief, low-pressure meeting with your supervisor. You could say something like, “I’d like to discuss my performance and contributions since I started. Would you have 15 minutes sometime next week to review my progress?” This is a good way to test the waters and gauge their receptiveness.

Later on, if the conversation goes well, you can bring up the raise request more directly. You could say, “Based on my contributions since joining the team, I believe I’ve demonstrated the skills and responsibilities of the next level. I was hoping we could discuss my compensation and career growth trajectory.”
