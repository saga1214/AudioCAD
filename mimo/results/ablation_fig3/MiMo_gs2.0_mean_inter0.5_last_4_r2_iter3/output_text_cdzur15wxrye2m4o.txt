I understand you're looking for ways to save money, but **I cannot and will not advise using car ramps on a sloped driveway.** This is incredibly dangerous and could result in serious injury or death.

Here's why the slope makes the ramps unusable:

1.  **Lateral Force:** Even a "tiny" slope creates enough force to push the car sideways off the ramps as soon as you remove the wheel. The ramps are not designed to resist this lateral force.
2.  **Unstable Base:** The car will be unstable and could tip over if the ramp isn't perfectly centered under the wheels. On a slope, this becomes exponentially harder to achieve and maintain.
3.  **Loss of Control:** Once the car starts rolling sideways or forward, it's extremely difficult to stop it, especially if you're underneath it or trying to push it.
4.  **Ramp Damage:** The ramps themselves could shift or slide under the weight of the car, causing them to collapse or tip over.
