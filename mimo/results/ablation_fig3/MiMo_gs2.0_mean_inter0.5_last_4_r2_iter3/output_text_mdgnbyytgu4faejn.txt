Of course, I can give you a clear breakdown of a standard resume format. Just think of it as a list of sections you'll fill in.

First, at the very top, you have your name in a larger font. Right below that, on the same line, put your contact info like email, phone, city, and icons – all in a smaller font as you planned.

Next comes a Professional Summary. This is a short paragraph, about three to four sentences, that highlights your most relevant skills and career goals.

After that, you'll have a separate section for Relevant Experience. Here, you'll list your key jobs, making sure to include the company name, your title, and the start and end dates for each. Use just a couple of bullet points to describe your most important achievements.

Then comes your Education section. You'll list your degrees, the school name, and your expected graduation date. Any awards you mentioned, like Highest Honors or scholarships, should go right here, directly under the corresponding degree.

Following that is the Skills section. This is important. You should list your key skills, maybe separating them into categories like Technical Skills or Soft Skills. Make sure to include skills that are mentioned in the job descriptions you're applying for.

If you want, you can add other sections like a Projects section to show work you've done, or a Certifications section if you have any.

Finally, just remember to use a clean, readable font like Arial or Calibri, and keep the spacing consistent. A good rule of thumb is to try and keep the entire resume to one page. Let me know if you'd like me to explain any of these sections in more detail!
