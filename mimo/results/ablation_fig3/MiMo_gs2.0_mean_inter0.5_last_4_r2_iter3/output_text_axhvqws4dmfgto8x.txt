Don't worry about the smoke alarm story anymore! Stir-frying is actually even safer for that.

The most important thing with stir-fried noodles is to make sure the pot is completely covered in boiling water. That's your smoke alarm shield right there.

Then, just use metal or bamboo chopsticks to stir them gently so they don't clump together. And here's a pro-tip: save a cup of the pasta water before you drain it. If your noodles seem a bit dry after draining, you can add some of that starchy water back in to make them glossy and delicious. You've got this!
