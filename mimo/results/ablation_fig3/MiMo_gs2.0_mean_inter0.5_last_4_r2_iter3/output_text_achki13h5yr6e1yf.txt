Of course, I can put all the ingredient prices together for you. Chicken cutlets will cost eight to fifteen dollars for two pounds. All-purpose flour is three to five dollars. Eggs are three to seven dollars for a dozen. Breadcrumbs run two to four dollars, and vegetable oil will be four to eight dollars. If needed, salt and pepper cost three to six dollars.

For the garlic mashed potatoes, a five-pound bag of potatoes is three to six dollars. Garlic is about a dollar. A pound of butter is four to seven dollars, and a half-gallon of milk is three to five dollars.
