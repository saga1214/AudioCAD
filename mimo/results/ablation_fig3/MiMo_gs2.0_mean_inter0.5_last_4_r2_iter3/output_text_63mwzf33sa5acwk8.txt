Okay, so after a desert adventure, you're thinking of ending your trip with a Vegas vibe. That's a fun way to cap it off. If you're driving the van down, you could stop in Vegas, check into a nice hotel, and just soak in the energy of the city. 

For a proper Vegas experience, I'd say hit up a classic casino, maybe try a live show like a magic act or a Cirque du Soleil performance. Definitely get a good meal at a restaurant with a view of the Strip. If you're both into nightlife, you could hit a club, but if you're more about relaxation, a spa day or even just people-watching at a rooftop bar could be great. The key is to do what feels right for you two.
