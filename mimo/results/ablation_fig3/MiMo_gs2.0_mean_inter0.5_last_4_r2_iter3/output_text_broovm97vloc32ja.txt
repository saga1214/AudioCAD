Yes, that's a fantastic option. If you're an author-illustrator, many publishers and agents are very interested in that. For agents, some represent both the words and the pictures, while others specialize in one or the other. You would need to find an agent who represents picture books and is open to or actively seeks out author-illustrators.

Once you have an agent, they would then help you find a publisher. Publishers are also very open to completed manuscripts with artwork, especially for children's books. They love to see the finished product because it helps them visualize the book and assess the quality of the art. Your agent will then negotiate a contract, which will cover things like your advance payment and the royalty rates you'll earn from sales.
