Alright, here is a full list of your cocktail recipes, all with exact measurements, perfect for your bartenders. I've also added finger food ideas to tie into Kentucky fall flavors.

First, for Old Fashioned: You'll need two ounces of bourbon or rye, one sugar cube, or you can use half an ounce of simple syrup. Add two to three dashes of Angostura bitters, and a little water to dissolve the sugar. To make it, stir the sugar, bitters, and water together in a shaker until it's dissolved. Add two ice cubes, stir again until it's chilled, and then serve it in a rocks glass.

Next, Paper Plane: Equal parts bourbon, Aperol, Nonino Amaro, and lemon juice. So that's one and a half ounces of bourbon, one and a half ounces of Aperol, one and a half ounces of Nonino Amaro, and one and a half ounces of fresh lemon juice. You'll shake all that with ice, then strain it into a coupe glass.

For finger food, I’d suggest things like mini deviled eggs, smoked gouda and fig skewers, or some buttermilk biscuits with honey butter.

And for the Whiskey Sour with or without the egg white: For the egg white version, you'll use two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and one egg white. Dry shake everything in the shaker, then add ice and shake again, then serve over ice. For the classic sour, just leave out the egg white.

And finally, the French 75 for the gin cocktail: One ounce of gin, half an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and top it with one and a half ounces of champagne. Shake the first three ingredients without ice, then add ice and shake again, and strain into a flute.
