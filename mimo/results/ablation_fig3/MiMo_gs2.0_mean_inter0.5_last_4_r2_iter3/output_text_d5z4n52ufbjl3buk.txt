Here are a few pointers for your email. You should start with a clear subject line, like "Meeting Request to Discuss Exam Feedback."

In the body, begin with a polite opening. You could say something like, "I hope this email finds you well. I scored a 64 on the recent essay exam and was hoping to better understand how my performance aligned with the grading criteria to improve for future assignments."

This keeps the focus on your growth.

Then, you could add a sentence or two about how subjective essay exams can feel, but phrase it as a question, for instance, "I'm curious if there were specific areas in my essay that you felt fell short."

Finally, propose a meeting. You could say, "Would you be available for a brief 15-minute chat during your office hours or at another time convenient for you?"

This way, you're respectful, focused on learning, and give him an easy way to say yes. Good luck.
