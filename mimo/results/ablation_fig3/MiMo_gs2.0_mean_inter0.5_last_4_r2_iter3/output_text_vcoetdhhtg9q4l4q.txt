Absolutely! When you want speed and simplicity, focus on **prepped ingredients, versatile bases, and minimal-cook methods**. Here are some efficient ideas:

### 🥗 Quick Ingredient Swaps:
- **Pre-cut veggies** (frozen or bagged): Save chopping time.  
- **Pre-cooked grains**: Canned beans, instant rice, or pre-cooked quinoa.  
- **Frozen proteins**: Shrimp, tofu, or pre-baked chicken.  
- **Pantry staples**: Soy sauce, curry paste, or pesto for instant flavor.  

### 🍳 Speedy Meal Ideas:
1. **Stir-fry in 10 mins**:  
   - Base: Rice noodles (one type!) or pre-cooked rice.  
   - Veggies: Frozen stir-fry mix or pre-chopped peppers/cabbage.  
   - Protein: Tofu or frozen shrimp.  
   - Sauce: 1 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tsp sesame oil.  

2. **One-pan roasted veggies + protein**:  
   - Toss broccoli, bell peppers, and chickpeas with olive oil and spices.  
   - Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 20 mins.  
   - Serve over pre-cooked quinoa or microwaved noodles.  

3. **Pasta or rice bowls**:  
   - Cook pasta or microwave rice noodles.  
   - Top with canned tuna, canned beans, or leftover roasted veggies.  
   - Add a drizzle of olive oil and herbs.  

### 🛒 Smart Shopping Tips:
- **Buy duplicates you actually use**: Keep only 1 type of each staple (rice, pasta, spices).  
- **Frozen > fresh**: For veggies/proteins, frozen often means less waste and faster cooking.  
- **Seasonal produce**: Buy 1–2 seasonal items weekly to avoid overstocking.  

Minimalism in cooking isn’t about scarcity—it’s about **intentionality**. Stick to 3–4 versatile ingredients per meal, and you’ll spend less time decluttering your kitchen and more time enjoying food. You’ve got this! 💪  

*(P.S. If your office is cluttered, start small: Clear just one surface today!)*
