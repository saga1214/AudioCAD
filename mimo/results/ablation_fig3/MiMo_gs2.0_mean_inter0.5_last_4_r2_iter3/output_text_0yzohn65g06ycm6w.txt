    *   **Seek Local Help:** Search for **pet food banks or shelters** offering supplies.
    *   **Buy in Bulk:** Purchase larger bags of quality food for a lower cost *per serving*.
    *   **Stretch Meals:** Occasionally mix small amounts of higher-quality food with cheap fillers (like plain cooked rice) – *check with a vet first*.
    *   **Prioritize Essential Vet Care:** Focus on low-cost essentials: **spaying/neutering** and **vaccinations**. Explain your situation to clinics for advice.
    *   **Supplement Safely:** Offer safe, plain **cooked proteins** (chicken, fish) when possible.

3.  **Keeping Cats Healthy on a Budget:**
    *   **Prevent Illness:** Maintain **clean environments**, **clean food/water bowls**, and provide fresh water.
    *   **Basic Grooming:** **Brush cats** regularly to reduce hairballs and skin issues.
    *   **Supplement Nutrition:** Use **plain cooked meat/fish** as occasional supplements (avoid onions/garlic/salt).
    *   **Promote Activity:** Use **homemade toys** (string, crumpled paper) to encourage play and maintain weight.
    *   **Monitor Closely:** **Observe cats daily** for early signs of illness (lethargy, appetite changes, litter box issues). Catching problems early is crucial.

**Key Principle:** Do the absolute best you can with what you have, prioritize prevention, seek community support, and be vigilant about early signs of illness.
