First, about the brain's hemispheres. The left-brain, right-brain idea is mostly a myth. While the brain's hemispheres do specialize in different tasks—like the left for language and the right for spatial skills—they work together constantly. It’s not like one side is purely creative and the other is purely logical. The brain is more integrated than that. 

Now, for careers that blend structure and creativity, design fields are a great fit. User experience design, for example, involves measuring user behavior (objective) while also being deeply creative. Architecture is another one—it’s about building solid, lasting structures but also requires creativity and problem-solving. Graphic design or data visualization can also merge measurable facts with artistic expression.

As for tinnitus, it’s almost always physiological. Think hearing damage, ear conditions, or even stress aggravating physical symptoms. It’s not usually post-traumatic, unless the trauma directly led to noise exposure.
