Sure, here's a simple checklist to get you ready for filming your first YouTube-style puppet show.

Before filming:
1. Permission: Make sure the place you're performing, like the assisted living, has given you permission to record and post the video. That’s important for legal and safety reasons.

2. Setup: Bring your sock puppets, a script or outline, and maybe a simple tripod or stand for your phone to keep it steady.

3. Lighting: Film in a well-lit area, like a brightly lit room or a living room. Bad lighting makes everything look worse.

4. Practice: Run through the show once or twice so it feels natural, especially since it might be a little weird to know you’re being recorded.

5. Engagement: Remember to smile and interact with the audience, even if it’s just the camera. Kids connect with energy.

That’s pretty much it. Start simple, be yourself, and have fun. You’ve got this.
