Leo and Sagittarius pairing up for an adventure sounds perfect! For a weekend trip, you could head to Canmore. You could go for a hike to Johnston Canyon, maybe have a picnic dinner at the Banff Springs Lake. Or you could drive about an hour and a half east to the Kananaskis country, which is stunning.
