### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia, Canada  
In BC, U-turns are generally permitted **except** where:  
- 🛑 **Traffic signals control intersections** (unless signed as allowed).  
- 🛑 You interfere with other traffic, curves, hills, or blind spots.  
- 🛑 Visibility < 150m in both directions (roughly **1.5 football fields**).  
- 🛑 Signs explicitly prohibit U-turns (e.g., "NO U-TURN" signs).  

**Penalties for Violations**:  
- **Fine**: $110–$160 (plus demerit points if applicable).  
- **Insurance**: Possible rate hikes for reckless driving.  

**Pro Tip**: Use designated U-turn lanes at busy intersections or pull over safely if unsure.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
Here’s what you’ll need (serves 2):  
| **Ingredient**         | **Quantity**          | **Notes**                                  |  
|------------------------|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------|  
| Spaghetti              | 200g (dry)            | Cook al dente!                             |  
| Guanciale/Pancetta    | 100g (diced)          | *Essential for flavor; guanciale is traditional (cured pork cheek)* |  
| Eggs                   | 2 large               | *Room temperature*                         |  
| Pecorino Romano cheese | 50g (grated)          | *Salty, hard cheese (mix with Parmigiano if too strong)* |  
| Black pepper           | 1 tsp                 | Freshly cracked                            |  
| Garlic                 | 1 clove (optional)    | Minced for depth                          |  
| White wine vinegar     | 1 tbsp                | *For pasta water (adds brightness)*        |  
| Olive oil              | 2 tbsp                | *To finish pasta*                         |  

**Key Technique**:  
- Whisk eggs, cheese, and pepper together.  
- Toss hot pasta with *reserved pasta water* (1 cup) to create a silky sauce.  
- Off heat, mix in eggs, cheese, and guanciale. **NO BOILING EGG SAUCE!**  

Enjoy your authentic carbonara! 🍝
