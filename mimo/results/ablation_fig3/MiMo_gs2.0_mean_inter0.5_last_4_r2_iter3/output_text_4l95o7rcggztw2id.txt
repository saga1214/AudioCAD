Okay, pirate-themed nautical beach party food list for seven kids total, including your son, so I'll make it for eight to be safe. All finger-friendly to avoid sand, allergy-conscious excluding watermelon, and fully themed. Here’s a full menu with packaging ideas.

For the main course, called "Pirate’s Plunder Plates": You'll need mini submarine sandwiches, cut into circles. Think ham and cheese or turkey and cheese. Theme them by using round cookie cutters before baking the bread and packing them in small treasure chest containers. You'll also want small rolls that look like“buried treasure.” Theme them by toasting them to look like masts and putting butter on top for“sails.” Pack them in individual boxes. Lastly, mini cheese quesadillas, cut into wedges. Theme them as“treasure coins.” You'll need cheese, tortillas, and chicken or black beans for protein.

For“Pirate Jewels” Fruit & Veggie Skewers: Use grapes, apple slices that have been soaked in lemon juice, and cheese cubes. Theme them by dipping them in goldfish cracker“dust,” which is just crushed goldfish crackers mixed with a little milk or water. Pack these in sealed bags labeled“Jewels of the Sea.”

For the“Pirate’s Snack Chest” snack mix: Combine pretzel rods as“ship masts,” with crackers as“cargo,” goldfish crackers as“gold coins,” and veggie sticks as“mermaids’ tails.” Theme it by putting it all in a small pirate-themed treasure chest container.

For drinks, labeled“Pirate Punch Boxes”: Use individual juice boxes or small water bottles. Pre-label them with fun names like“Treasure Cove Punch” or“Sea Monster Surprise.” You can also make a special“Pirate Punch” in a separate cooler using cranberry and berry juices mixed with a little lemonade, and serve it in small cups if you want to step it up.

For dessert,“Cannonball Cupcakes”: Theme them as pirate ships with icing“sails.” You can find pre-made ones or make them yourself. Pack them in small boxes. As an optional“treasure” addition, you could include mini chocolate chips hidden in the batter.

For allergy safety, make sure to label all watermelon-containing items clearly. It might be helpful to have a separate sign explaining the allergen-free options. And of course, pack wet wipes handy just in case of minor spills!

This plan should give you a full, themed, sand-free meal that the kids will love, especially while they're dressed as pirates!
