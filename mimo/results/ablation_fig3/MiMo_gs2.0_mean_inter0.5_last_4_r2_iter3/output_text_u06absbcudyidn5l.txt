Okay, let's break this down. Chronic redness, or erythema, across your lifetime can stem from several places. Rosacea is a big one, causing those redness spells, often on the face. Long-term sun damage is another major cause, as UV rays can really break down the skin. Genetics can also play a role, sometimes leading to conditions like chronic urticaria, which are hives, or even something like fibrous dysplasia, though that's more specific to bones.

Now, about vasculitis. It's a condition where your immune system mistakenly attacks your blood vessels. Because these vessels are everywhere, the symptoms can be too. You might feel generally unwell, with fever and fatigue. Skin symptoms are common, like rashes, purple spots, or sores. Vasculitis can affect vessels of any size—large, medium, or small—which is why the symptoms can vary so much.
