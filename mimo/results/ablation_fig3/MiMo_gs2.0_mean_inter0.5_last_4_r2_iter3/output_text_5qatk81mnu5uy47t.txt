### 🌟 Top Costa Rica Dive Destinations

2. **Tortuguero (Caribbean Coast)**  
   - **Why**: Remote, lush canals, great for nurse sharks, sea turtles (nesting season: June–November), and reef walls.  
   - **Best for**: Eco-diving, wildlife, serene environment.  
   - **Key Site**: **Gorgona Wall** (deep walls for advanced divers).  

3. **Puerto Viejo (Caribbean)**  
   - **Why**: Vibrant coral reefs, tropical fish, and opportunities to dive near **Island Beach**.  
   - **Best for**: Mixed experience levels, colorful reefs.  

### 🎯 Trip Timeline (7-10 Days)
- **Days 1-3**: Arrive in San José → Transfer to **Cabo Blanco/Playa Nicuesa** (1.5 hrs).  
- **Days 4-5**: Fly to **Tortuguero** (1 hr) → Explore canals, dive the Gorgona Wall.  
- **Days 6-7**: Return to **Puerto Viejo** → Dive Island Beach or **Cocora Reef**.  
- **Day 8**: Fly home.  

### 🧳 Must-Pack Gear
- **Fins**: Start with **short fins** (maneuverable, compact).
