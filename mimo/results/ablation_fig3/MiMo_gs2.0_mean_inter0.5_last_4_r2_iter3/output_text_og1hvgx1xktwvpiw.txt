Of course, let me clarify. The equal-parts version is actually the original Boulevardier. So, three-quarters of an ounce each of bourbon, one ounce of sweet vermouth, and one ounce of Campari stirred over ice. Later, a variation was created with one and a quarter ounces of bourbon, one ounce of vermouth, and one ounce of Campari. Your friend was using that variation. 

Now, for the Last Word, since you mentioned it: that's three-quarters of an ounce each of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. Stir those over ice and serve in a coupe glass with a lime twist. Hope that helps! Enjoy your party.
