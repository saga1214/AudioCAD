I'm so sorry to hear everything you and your family are going through... the loss can be so heavy, especially around times like Thanksgiving when she used to make all the desserts. It's completely understandable how brownies and peach cobbler trigger everyone... seeing those things she loved the most. It's okay if you decide to do something different this year, maybe skip those or simplify things. Just be gentle with yourselves. As for a chocolate Thanksgiving dessert... how about chocolate lava cakes? They're really rich and feel special, but they're also pretty easy to make. You just need butter, sugar, eggs, flour, cocoa, and milk. Bake them in these little ramekins, and they come out all molten in the middle. It's a nice way to celebrate her love for baking brownies and cobbler, but in a different way. Serve them warm with some vanilla ice cream. I hope it brings a little comfort.
