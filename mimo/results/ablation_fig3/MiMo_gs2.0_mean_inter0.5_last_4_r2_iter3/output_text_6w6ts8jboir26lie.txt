Okay, she's on the mend and wants to cook. That's a great idea to take control and do something nice for herself.

Here are five simple, comforting dinner ideas you could whip up:

First, a classic Margherita pizza. You can get a pre-made crust, and just top it with fresh mozzarella, some basil, and a drizzle of olive oil.

Second, a hearty vegetable stir-fry. You can grab some tofu, bell peppers, broccoli, and snap peas, and cook it all up with a simple garlic-ginger sauce.

Third, a cozy chicken and rice casserole. You can just mix shredded chicken with rice, some broth, and maybe a can of cream of mushroom soup for a comforting meal.

Fourth, a simple grilled cheese sandwich with tomato soup. It's a classic comfort food combination.

And fifth, a fresh salmon with roasted asparagus. You can get some salmon fillets and just season them with lemon and herbs, then roast some asparagus on the side.

Enjoy your well-deserved vacation, even if it's a bit unconventional!
