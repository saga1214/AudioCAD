Here's a step-by-step guide to safely install your TV, hanging planters, and floating shelves while respecting your apartment rules:

### 📺 TV Mounting
1. **Measure & Plan**:  
   - Use a laser level to mark wall studs at desired height (at least 42" from floor).  
   - Check TV weight limits on the mount – choose a sturdy "full-motion" or "flat" mount rated for your TV’s weight.  
   *Tip: For heavy TVs, use 3" anchors if no studs are available.*

2. **Tools Needed**:  
   - Stud finder (with laser)  
   - Level  
   - Drill  
   - Wall anchors (if needed)  
   - Screws + wall plate  

3. **Installation**:  
   - Attach the mount’s top plate to studs using long screws (or anchors).  
   - Secure the mount to the plate.  
   - Attach TV to mount with provided screws.  
   *Always test stability before mounting the TV!*

---

### 🌿 Hanging Planters
1. **Weight Calculation**:  
   - Measure soil weight + pot weight (wet + dry).  
   - Use adhesive hooks rated for **100% of this weight**.  

2. **Tools Needed**:  
   - Adhesive hooks (e.g., Command™ Picture Hanging Strips)  
   - Level  
   - Drill (optional for heavier plants)  

3. **Installation**:  
   - Clean walls first (removable adhesives need smooth surfaces).  
   - Apply adhesive strips/hooks to wall, press firmly, wait 24 hours.  
   - Hang planters at eye level (about 5-6 ft from floor).  
   *Safety Tip: For heavy plants (e.g., hanging baskets), use wall anchors.*

---

### 📚 Floating Shelves
1. **Prep**:  
   - Clean walls with glass cleaner to remove dust/oils.  
   - Use a stud finder to locate studs (or use wall anchors for drywall).  

2. **Tools Needed**:  
   - Shelves (MDF, wood, or metal)  
   - Level  
   - Wall anchors (if no studs)  
   - Screws  
   - Drill  

3. **Installation**:  
   - Mark shelf position 1-2" above desired height.  
   - Attach brackets to wall at top/bottom of shelf.  
   - Secure shelf to brackets.  
   *Load Tip: Place heavier items at the bottom and lighter ones higher.*

---

### 🛠️ Pro Tips
- **Avoid Drilling**: Use removable adhesives for lightweight decor (e.g., frames, hooks).  
- **Safety First**:  
  - Wear safety glasses when drilling.  
  - Never overload shelves – follow manufacturer weight limits.  
- **Decor Ideas**:  
  - Layer lighting: Use LED strip lights under shelves or in planters.  
  - Group art by height for depth.  
  - Use baskets for storage to keep decor organized.

**Time-Saving Hack**: Label all hardware/assembly parts before starting! 📏✨
