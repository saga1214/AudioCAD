Okay, let's build a Fleur-inspired look using jeans! This is totally doable and unique within the Harry Potter theme.

**The Core Idea:** Instead of a full French uniform, lean into the *elegantBeauxbatons student* vibe using modern pieces. Think "French chic meets wizardry."

**Here's a breakdown of how to make jeans work:**

1.  **The Jeans:** You're right, jeans are the foundation. Go for a **classic dark wash, straight-leg or slightly wide-leg jeans**. They instantly feel more modern and less "Hogwarts student" than a skirt or uniform. Ensure they fit well – they are your statement piece.
2.  **The Top (Key Color Contrast):** This is where you create the magic pop.
    *   **Option A (Best Match):** A **vibrant, jewel-toned top**. Think **emerald green, royal blue, deep purple, or even a rich burgundy**. This creates a stunning contrast with dark jeans and feels very magical and luxurious, echoing Fleur's signature blue but making it your own. A simple, well-fitting turtleneck, boatneck, or crewneck sweater works perfectly.
    *   **Option B (Easy & Stylish):** A **bold printed top**. Something with a **small floral print, subtle geometric pattern, or even a vintage-inspired plaid** in a dark color (black, navy, deep green) would look great against dark jeans. Keep the neckline relatively simple (like a V-neck or crewneck) so the jeans remain the focal point.
    *   **Option C (Modern Twist):** A **slightly cropped, fitted knit top** (like a ribbed tank or a thin turtleneck) in a solid color (white, cream, pale pink, or a muted jewel tone) paired with the dark jeans. This adds a modern, slightly edgy feel while keeping the elegant base.
3.  **The Headpiece (The Beret Hack):** You don't need a full wig! A **beret** is Fleur's signature.
    *   **Find a Beret:** Look for a **dark blue, navy, or even black beret** at a craft store, costume shop, or online. They are surprisingly common and affordable.
    *   **Style it Simply:** You don't need a French wig! A sleek, dark brown or black wig cap (or even just very sleek dark hair) will work. The beret itself adds the character instantly.
    *   **Denim Headband/Cap Alternative (If Beret is Too Much):** If a beret feels too "costumey," try a **simple, thin headband** in a dark color (black, navy, deep red) or even a **small denim cap** (like a baseball cap with the brim pulled up and flipped slightly). This is less Fleur, but leans into the "modern magical" feel you mentioned with the jeans. A **simple dark headband** worn low on the forehead also adds a subtle touch.
4.  **Accessories (Magic Touch):**
    *   **Jewelry:** Fleur is elegant. Think **delicate gold hoops, a simple gold chain necklace, or a small gold ring**. Avoid anything too sparkly or cartoonish.
    *   **Bag:** A **structured satchel** in a dark color (black, brown, navy) or even a **mini crossbody bag** would fit the vibe better than a backpack.
    *   **"Wand" (Optional):** Carry a **simple, elegant-looking wand** (avoid the super long, flashy ones). Hold it casually.
    *   **"Spellbook" (Optional):** A **small, leather-bound notebook** or even just a **simple book** could double as a prop.
5.  **Shoes:** Keep it simple and stylish.
    *   **Ankle Boots:** Dark brown or black **ankle boots** (knee-high or mid-calf) are perfect. They add height and polish.
    *   **Clean Sneakers:** If you prefer sneakers, go for **clean, white, or black low-tops** with minimal branding. They keep it casual but still work with the jeans and top.
    *   **Barefoot (If Party Allows):** If it's a super casual party and you can pull it off, bare feet are an option, especially with the jeans and top.

**Putting it Together: The Fleur Denim Look**

*   **Bottom:** Dark Wash Jeans (Straight-leg or Wide-leg)
*   **Top:** Emerald Green Turtleneck Sweater OR Royal Blue Boatneck OR Deep Purple Cropped Knit
