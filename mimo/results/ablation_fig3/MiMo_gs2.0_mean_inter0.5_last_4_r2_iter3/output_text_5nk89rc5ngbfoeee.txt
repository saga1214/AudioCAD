For an end-of-year celebration, a **park barbecue** sounds fantastic—it’s active, inclusive, and lets you showcase the fun Brownies built this year! Here’s how to make it work smoothly:

### 🎉 Event Plan:
1. **Location**:  
   - Choose a nearby park with picnic tables, shade, and space for games.  
   - **Weather Backup**: Rent a tent or book a rain date at the school cafeteria (your usual meeting spot).

2. **Activities**:  
   - **Classic Games**: Wheelbarrow races, sack races, or a "Three-Legged Race" (assign partners to parents for extra fun!).  
   - **Themed Challenges**: "Cookie Sales Relay" (deliver pretend cookies) or badge-related scavenger hunts.  
   - **Relaxation**: Add a "Sundaes Bar" or pizza station—let parents handle grilling while you oversee snacks.

3. **Food & Logistics**:  
   - **Parent Coordination**:  
     - Assign 2-3 parents as "Barbecue Crew" (grill, set up chairs).  
     - Others can bring side dishes (e.g., veggie platters for dairy-free options).  
   - **Snacks**:  
     - Chips, dip, ice cream sundaes (offer dairy-free alternatives like coconut milk).  
     - Keep it simple: Pre-packable options (mini pizzas, fruit cups) reduce cleanup.  
   - **Cost**:  
     - **Dues**: Cover games/activities (e.g., $10-15/season).  
     - **Parents**: Split grill/food costs (e.g., $20-30/parent).  
     - **You**: Focus on planning—no need to cover costs while pregnant!

4. **Safety & Inclusivity**:  
   - **Dietary Tags**: Use color-coded wristbands (e.g., green = dairy-free).  
   - **Space**: Designate quiet zones for kids who need breaks.  
   - **Badges**: Award badges during the event (e.g., "Community Helper" for field trips).

### 💡 Why This Works:
- **Energy**: Outdoor games burn calories and build teamwork.  
- **Affordability**: Parents share responsibilities—less stress for you.  
- **Nostalgia**: Channels her love for parties (chips/dip) into a shared celebration.
