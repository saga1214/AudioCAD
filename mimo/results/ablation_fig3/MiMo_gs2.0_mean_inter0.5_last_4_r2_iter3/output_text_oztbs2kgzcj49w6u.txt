Absolutely, Lockport has preserved its deep connection to the Erie Canal beautifully. It's a great place to explore.

You could start at the excellent Lockport Maritime Museum, which is housed in a restored 1870s canal warehouse. It has a great collection of artifacts, including the historic "Lockport Locks" plaque that confirms the spy story, and even a working replica of the canal's original steamboat. They also offer boat tours of the locks.

You'll also find many preserved historic sites along Route 31, like the Old Erie Canal Village with its restored mills and homes, and the beautiful canal-side park at the Lockport Locks. And for a really unique view, you can drive up to the top of the Niagara Escarpment, which has a great overlook looking down at the locks. It's a perfect vacation detour.
