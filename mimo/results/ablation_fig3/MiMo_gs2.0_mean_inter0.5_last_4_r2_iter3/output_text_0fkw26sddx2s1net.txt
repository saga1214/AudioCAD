Alright, here is a detailed shopping list for your husband's Asian-inspired meal. It excludes legumes and seafood, and includes the matcha ice cream option. Let's break it down by course.

First, for the starter options: Miso Soup and Cucumber Salad. For that, you'll need miso paste, and if you want to add tofu, be sure it's certified legume-free, as some tofu is made from soybeans. You'll also need a dashi stock, which you can make from kombu and bonito flakes, or just buy it pre-made. For the cucumber salad, you'll need cucumbers, rice vinegar, sesame oil, soy sauce, and some toasted sesame seeds.

Next, for the main course: Chicken Teriyaki with Jasmine Rice and Stir-Fried Veggies. You'll need boneless, skinless chicken breasts, jasmine rice, any veggies you like such as bell peppers, broccoli, and carrots, cooking oil, soy sauce, and a ginger-garlic paste. For the teriyaki sauce, you'll need soy sauce, mirin, sake, brown sugar, and a bit of cornstarch.

And finally, for the dessert, here's your simple homemade Matcha Ice Cream. You'll need matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and a little vanilla extract if you want to add it.

Just a couple of extra tips: check the labels on soy sauce and mirin to make sure they're legume-free, as some might have additives. And make sure your matcha powder is high-quality for the best flavor. Enjoy your romantic dinner!
