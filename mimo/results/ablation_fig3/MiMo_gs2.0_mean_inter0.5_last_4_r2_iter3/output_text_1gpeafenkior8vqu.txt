Here’s a **7-day sample itinerary** balancing Tokyo’s energy with Osaka’s culinary and cultural charm, including day trips to Kyoto and Nara. This assumes 4 days in Tokyo, 3 in Osaka, and dedicated travel time to Kyoto/Nara.

---

### **Days 1–4: Tokyo (Deep Exploration)**
**Day 1: Arrival & Shinjuku**  
- **Morning:** Check into hotel (confirm 10 AM check-in).  
- **Late Afternoon:** Explore **Shinjuku Gyoen Garden** (peaceful respite).  
- **Evening:** **Shinjuku Station** (shopping, dining, nightlife).  
- *Workout:* Morning or early evening at hotel gym.  

**Day 2: Imperial History & Asakusa**  
- **Morning:** **Tokyo Imperial Palace East Gardens** (opens 9 AM).  
- **Afternoon:** **Asakusa** (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Market).  
- **Evening:** **Sumida River Cruise** to Odaiba (night views).  

**Day 3: Modern Tokyo & Shibuya**  
- **Morning:** **Meiji Shrine** (Hamarikyu Gardens nearby).  
- **Afternoon:** **Shibuya** (Scramble Crossing, Hachiko Statue, Shibuya Sky).  
- **Evening:** **Omoide Yokocho** (tiny alley bars).  

**Day 4: Art & Nature**  
- **Morning:** **Ueno Park** (Tokyo National Museum, Shinobazu Pond).  
- **Afternoon:** **Ghibli Museum** (book tickets in advance!) or **teamLab Planets**.  
- **Evening:** **Roppongi Hills** (art galleries, Tokyo City View).  

---

### **Day 5: Travel to Kyoto (Morning Train)**  
- **Morning:** **JR Shinkansen** (1.5 hours from Tokyo Station).  
- **Check-in:** Kyoto Hotel (Lunch in Gion).  
- **Afternoon:** **Kiyomizu-dera Temple** (panoramic city views).  
- **Evening:** **Pontocho Alley** (sushi/tapas).  

---

### **Day 6: Kyoto & Nara Day Trip**  
- **Morning:** **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (opens 6 AM; arrive by 7 AM for solitude).  
- **Afternoon:** **Nara** (1-hour train from Kyoto). **Todai Temple** (Great Buddha) & **Nara Park** (deer).  
- **Evening:** Return to Kyoto. **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion).  

---

### **Day 7: Osaka & Departure**  
**Travel to Osaka:**  
- **Morning:** **Haruka Express** (30 mins from Kyoto Station).  
- **Check-in:** Osaka Hotel (Lunch in Dotonbori).  

**Osaka Highlights:**  
- **Afternoon:** **Osaka Castle** (museum inside).  
- **Evening:** **Dotonbori** (takoyaki, neon signs, night market).  

**Day 8: Departure**  
- **Morning:** **Kuromon Market** (seafood breakfast).  
- **Afternoon:** **Universal Studios Japan** (if time allows) or souvenir shopping.
