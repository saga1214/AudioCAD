Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes using your leftover vodka and Argentine-style Fernet, designed for formality and mixed preferences:

### 1. **Vodka Cucumber-Mint Fizz** (Refreshing & Light)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1.5 oz vodka  
- 4 cucumber slices (thin)  
- 5 fresh mint leaves  
- 0.5 oz lime juice  
- 1 oz soda water  
- Ice  
- *Optional garnish*: Mint sprig  

**Steps**:  
1. Muddle cucumber and mint in a shaker.  
2. Add vodka, lime juice, and ice. Shake well.  
3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
4. Top with soda water. Garnish with mint.  

### 2. **Fernet Argentino Sparkler** (Bold & Herbal)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 0.5 oz Fernet-Branca (or Argentine Fernet)  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- 2 oz soda water  
- Ice  
- *Optional garnish*: Lime wheel  

**Steps**:  
1. Shake Fernet, lime juice, and ice vigorously.  
2. Strain into a rocks glass filled with ice.  
3. Top with soda water. Garnish with lime.  

---

### **Key Notes**:  
- **Soda Allergy**: Both recipes use *sparkling water* (not soda).  
- **Vegetarian/Omnivore Friendly**: No dietary conflicts.  
- **Formality**: Use chilled glasses (coupe/rocks).  
- **For Non-Drinkers**: Offer sparkling water, wine, or infused water (e.g., cucumber-lime).
