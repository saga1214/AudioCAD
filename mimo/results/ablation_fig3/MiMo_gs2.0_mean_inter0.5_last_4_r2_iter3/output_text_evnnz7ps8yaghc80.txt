Here’s a balanced weekly schedule to help you practice effectively without stress:

- On Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, dedicate 20 minutes to practicing one-hand two-ball juggles to refine your rhythm.
- On Tuesday and Thursday, practice transitioning from two to three beanbags for 20 minutes.
- On Saturday, try juggling tennis balls for 15 minutes.
- On Sunday, practice with scarves for 15 minutes.

Remember to start with short sessions and gradually increase the time as you feel more comfortable. The most important thing is to enjoy the process!
