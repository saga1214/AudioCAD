For a romantic getaway, you could start with a catamaran charter for a private sail along the coast.

For dining, instead of a big seafood feast, you could book a private dinner on a small boat that sails under the stars. Imagine a spread of fresh oysters and grilled lobster.

To make it more special, you could visit a secluded spot like a hidden cove that you can only get to by boat, and maybe take a refreshing dip together.
