### 🧵 Non-Destructive Attachment Methods:
1. **Fabric-Safe Tapes**  
   Use removable double-sided tape or adhesive hooks (like Velcro dots). Avoid heat-activated tape—it can melt synthetic fabrics.  
   *Example:* Stick a name tag or patch with fabric glue or double-sided tape.

2. **Clothespins or Hair Ties**  
   Clip tags or small items *temporarily* with wide clothespins. Hair ties work for lightweight additions (e.g., a hat loop).

3. **Button or Hook & Loop**  
   Sew a button onto a seam allowance (inside the jacket) and attach your item with a hook & loop strap.  
   *Avoid:* Sewing directly onto the outer fabric—it might snag.

4. **Elastic or Ribbon**  
   Attach ribbon loops to the inside hem or pocket. Tuck items in securely without piercing the lining.

5. **Safety Pins (Use with Caution)**  
   Only use if the lining is thick (e.g., fleece). Point pins *away* from the fabric to avoid punctures.  
   *Never* use on delicate materials like down or thermal linings.

### ⚠️ What to Avoid:
- **Needles/Sewing** (if you’re not confident): Can damage seams or insulation.
- **Hot Glue/Hair Dryers**: Risk melting synthetic fibers.
- **Sharp Objects**: Puncture the lining and reduce warmth.

### 💡 Pro Tip:
If you’re adding patches or tags, buy **removable adhesive patches** from craft stores (like Michaels) or online. They’re designed for fabric and peel off cleanly.
