Here's a concise list of recommended items and where to find them locally in Indianapolis:

### **Tools & Materials**
1. **Aviation Snips**  
   *Best for:* Thinner sheet metal curves.  
   *Buy at:* The Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards.  

2. **Angle Grinder w/Cut-Off Wheel**  
   *Best for:* Thicker metal or straight cuts.  
   *Buy at:* Same big-box stores (Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards).  

3. **Jigsaw w/Metal Blade**  
   *Best for:* Curved cuts in thicker metal.  
   *Buy at:* Home Depot, Lowe's, Ace Hardware.  

4. **Ring-Shank Drywall Nails**  
   *Best for:* Wood studs (secure grip).  
   *Buy at:* Tractor Supply Co., Ace Hardware.  

5. **1-1/4" Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws**  
   *Why better:* Prevents nail pops, holds firmly in studs.  
   *Buy at:* All big-box stores (Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards).  

6. **Fiberglass Insulation Batts**  
   *Best for:* Most residential walls (easy to install).  
   *Buy at:* Home Depot, Lowe's.  

7. **Mineral Wool Insulation**  
   *Best for:* Soundproofing/fire resistance.  
   *Buy at:* Home Depot, Tractor Supply Co.  

8. **Spray Foam Insulation**  
   *Best for:* Airtight sealing (premium option).  
   *Buy at:* Home Depot, Lowe's.
