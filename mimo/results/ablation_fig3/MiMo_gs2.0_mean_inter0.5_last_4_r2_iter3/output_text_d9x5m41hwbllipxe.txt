Based on your home's details (4 doors, 7 windows, ~1500 sq ft) and interest in a professional system, here’s an itemized cost breakdown for security equipment:

### **Core Equipment Costs**  
1. **Control Panel/Hub**:  
   - Self-install: $100–$400  
   - Professional installation: $200–$800 (includes labor)  

2. **Door/Window Sensors**:  
   - **4 doors + 7 windows** = 11 sensors  
   - Cost: $15–$50 per sensor  
   - **Total**: $165–$550  

3. **Motion Detectors**:  
   - **2–3 detectors** (one per room)  
   - Cost: $30–$80 each  
   - **Total**: $60–$240  

4. **Glass Break Sensor**:  
   - **1–2 sensors** (for vulnerable areas)  
   - Cost: $30–$100 each  
   - **Total**: $60–$200  

5. **Indoor Siren**:  
   - **1 siren** (to deter intruders)  
   - Cost: $30–$70  
   - **Total**: $30–$70  

6. **Cameras** (Optional but recommended):  
   - **1–2 indoor/outdoor cameras**  
   - Cost: $50–$200 each  
   - **Total**: $50–$400  

---

### **Professional Monitoring Fees**  
- **Monthly Cost**: $20–$60 (without glass break sensors)  
- **With Glass Break Sensors**: $30–$80 (due to higher-risk features)  
- **Contract Term**: 5 years is common (as you noted). Over 5 years, this adds **$1,200–$3,600** to your total cost.  

---

### **Total Estimated Costs**  
| **Option**               | Upfront Cost | Monthly Cost | 5-Year Total (with Monitoring) |  
|--------------------------|--------------|--------------|--------------------------------|  
| **DIY System**           | $300–$800    | $20–$60      | $1,200–$3,600                  |  
| **Professional System**  | $800–$2,000  | $30–$80      | $1,800–$4,800                  |  

---

### **Key Recommendations**  
1. **Prioritize Professional Monitoring**: Even DIY systems require a monitoring hub (e.g., SimpliSafe, ADT) for emergency response.  
2. **Glass Break Sensors**: Highly recommended for break-in-prone areas—they add ~$20–$40/month but offer critical deterrence.  
3. **Contract Caution**: Always compare "total lifetime cost" (equipment + 5-year fees) before signing.  
4. **Insurance Discounts**: Many insurers offer 5–20% discounts for verified systems.  

For exact quotes, consult local installers or brands like **Ring**, **SimpliSafe**, or **ADT**. They’ll customize based on your door/window types and smart-home integration (e.g., Alexa/Google Assistant).
