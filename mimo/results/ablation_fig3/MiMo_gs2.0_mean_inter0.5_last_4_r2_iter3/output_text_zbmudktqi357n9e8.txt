Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn inspiration! I'd say late September through November is ideal. That’s their autumn season, when you get those vibrant fall colors in the highlands and the coasts. The weather is usually sunny but not too hot, perfect for painting outside. 

Specifically, you should definitely check out Lake Pátzcuaro in Michoacán. The mountains are covered in these amazing fall colors, and the lakeside towns are just so picturesque. Tulum is another great option, especially for coastal scenes with those autumn hues. 

So yeah, plan your trip around late September to November, and you’ll have plenty of inspiration and beautiful weather for your watercolor practice. Happy painting!
