That still doesn't quite work from an evolutionary standpoint.

The important thing to remember is that an egg contains DNA from *two* parents, one from the mother and one from the father. For the hatched creature to become the first true chicken, a specific chicken-defining gene had to be in its DNA at the moment the egg was laid. That gene came from the father, who was already a chicken or had the right genetics.

Therefore, the egg, laid by a non-chicken, still only contained about half of the DNA needed to create a chicken. The other half came from the father.
