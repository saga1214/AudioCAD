Okay, so you're asking about juice cleanses and water fasts to get rid of PFAS. I understand the concern, but unfortunately, there's no specific treatment to remove them from your body. They are very persistent, which is why they have that name. Your body does have natural ways to process chemicals, but it's not a quick fix like a cleanse. The best approach is really to focus on reducing your ongoing exposure through lifestyle changes.

So, to help you create that list, here are five key things to avoid to minimize your intake.

First, non-stick cookware. It's much better to switch to stainless steel, cast iron, or ceramic.

Second, grease-resistant food packaging. Try to avoid wrappers from fast food, pizza boxes, and microwave popcorn bags.

Third, products labeled as stain or water-resistant, like carpets, furniture, and some outdoor gear.

Fourth, certain cosmetics and even some types of dental floss. Look for brands that are labeled PFAS-free.

And fifth, since tap water can be a source, using a certified water filter is a great way to reduce exposure. Remember, activated carbon filters retain minerals.
