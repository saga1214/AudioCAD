That's awesome! Tapping trees and snowshoeing are both fantastic winter activities. Here’s a quick gear guide for snowshoeing and other fun winter ideas:

### **Snowshoeing Gear Essentials**  
1. **Snowshoes**:  
   - **Beginner-friendly**: Look for lightweight, frame-style snowshoes (e.g., MSR Lightning Ascents or Black Diamond).  
   - **Size**: Ensure your shoe fits snugly over your boots (check manufacturer sizing charts).  
   - **Rent first**: Try them at outdoor shops before buying—feels different than hiking boots!  

2. **Boots**:  
   - Sturdy hiking boots with aggressive tread (e.g., Salomon Quest 4).  
   - Waterproofing is key for wet snow.  

3. **Poles**:  
   - Adjustable trekking poles (e.g., Black Diamond Alpine Carbon) for stability.  

4. **Safety Gear**:  
   - Map/compass (snowshoe trails can drift off course).  
   - Warm layers (base layer + fleece + waterproof shell).  
   - Headlamp (snow reduces visibility).  

5. **Extras**:  
   - Ice axe (optional for steep terrain).  
   - Trekking poles with crampons for icy patches.  

---

### **Other Winter Activities**  
- **Cross-Country Skiing**: Low-impact, scenic, and great for trails. Try rental gear at local shops.  
- **Snowmobiling**: Rent a sled (e.g., Polaris Ranger) for trails or groomed groomed trails.  
- **Winter Hiking**: Explore local trails with snowshoes or microspikes.  
- **Sledging**: Build a simple sled with plywood and handles!  
- **Ice Climbing**: Requires gear (ice axes, crampons) and a guided tour.
