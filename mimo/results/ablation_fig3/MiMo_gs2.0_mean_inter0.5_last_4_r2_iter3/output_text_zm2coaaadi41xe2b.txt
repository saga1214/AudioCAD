Okay, Dallas Asian noodle spots—got it. If you're craving authentic flavors, I'd say check out Noodle House for classic Szechuan-style dishes, or if you like pho, Pho 75 is a solid choice. For a more upscale experience, there's also Tien House, which serves great Cantonese and dim sum. 

And for protein bars, if you want something high-protein and tasty, look into Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard or MyProtein. They're both popular for their macros and flavor. If you prefer a bit more variety, Legion Sports Food has some really good options too. Hope you enjoy your meal and your upgrades!
