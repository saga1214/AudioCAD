For a relaxed Austin dinner, here are some top casual spots that locals love—perfect for unwinding after a day of exploring:

### 🍻 **Top Casual Picks in Austin**
1. **Terry Black’s Barbecue**  
   - *Why*: Iconic slow-smoked BBQ (try the brisket or ribs) with a laid-back vibe. No-frills, just great food.  
   - *Location*: 1001 Barton Springs Rd.  

2. **Veracruz All Natural**  
   - *Why*: A hole-in-the-wall spot famous for tacos al pastor, handmade tortillas, and a vibrant street-art scene.  
   - *Location*: 604 W Cesar Chavez St.  

3. **De La Carreta**  
   - *Why*: Authentic Cuban sandwiches, plantains, and a friendly, communal atmosphere.  
   - *Location*: 2020 S Congress Ave.  

4. **Home Slice Pizza**  
   - *Why*: Neighborhood pizzeria with wood-fired, Neapolitan-style pizzas. Go for the Margherita or pepperoni.  
   - *Location*: Multiple spots (e.g., 1002 E 12th St.).  

5. **The Salt Lick**  
   - *Why*: Historic BBQ joint (since 1986!) with open-air dining and a rustic, rustic charm.  
   - *Location*: 1511 S Congress Ave.  

### 📍 **Neighborhood Vibes**  
- **East Austin**: Dive bars like **The Continental Club** (music + tacos) or **Pecan Lodge** (BBQ with local flair).  
- **Downtown**: **Odd Duck** (farm-to-table, creative dishes) or **Loro** (global street food in a garden setting).  

### 🍴 **Greek Food in Austin**  
Since you’re a tzatziki fanatic, don’t miss:  
- **Therapee** (modern Greek, upscale but casual)  
- **Sokyo** (small plates, olive oil ice cream!)  
- **Mamakos** (artisanal pastries + wine)  

### 💡 **Pro Tip**  
Check reservations on **Resy** or **OpenTable** for popular spots—especially on weekends!
