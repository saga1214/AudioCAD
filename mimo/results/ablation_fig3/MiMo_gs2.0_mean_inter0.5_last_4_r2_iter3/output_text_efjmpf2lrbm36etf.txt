Okay, this anniversary planning journey has taken quite a turn—from restrictions to creativity to packaging design. I love it. For last-minute indulgence, you’re absolutely right: bright, vibrant drinks and a focused menu can transform constraints into charm. 

Here’s a quick concept for you: Find a cozy wine bar or a small cocktail lounge with early closing times—many do. Ask for their “short and sweet” menu, often featuring seasonal small plates like bruschetta, grilled shrimp skewers, or truffle arancini. These are quick to eat and feel special. 

For drinks, go for anything citrus-forward but avoid if your wife can’t have it! A margarita made with a non-citrus spirit base like rum or tequila with lime is a classic. Or, a non-citrus cocktail like a bourbon smash or a gin fizz with berries and mint. If you want something truly celebratory, ask for a custom spritz with local non-citrus juices—think peach or pear. 

The key is to lean into the spontaneity. Tell the staff it’s an anniversary, and they’ll often make something extra thoughtful. Keep it intimate—maybe even skip the restaurant research altogether and let them guide you. The effort you’ve put into this anniversary note and meal planning already shows deep care, so trust that energy.
