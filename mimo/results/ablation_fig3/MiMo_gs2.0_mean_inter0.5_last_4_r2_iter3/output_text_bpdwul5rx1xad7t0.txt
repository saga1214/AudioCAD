That sounds like an amazing plan for your first road trip! Here are five fantastic options for you and your new family.

First, for a short trip, you can drive about an hour and a half to the Okanagan Lake area. You can enjoy the stunning blue waters, visit charming wineries, and even stay in a luxury lakeside resort for a fancy dinner.

For a longer trip, about five hours, you can drive to Vancouver Island. You'll love the scenic drive along the coast, hike in the rainforests, and visit the famous Butchart Gardens.

Next, for a classic adventure, you can head to the Fraser Canyon, which is about a four-hour drive. You can explore historic gold rush towns and visit a beautiful hot spring resort.

If you want to explore the coast, you could drive to the Saltspring Island area, about four and a half hours away. It's known for its beautiful beaches, charming little towns, and lots of gourmet food options.

Finally, for a trip to the mountains, you can drive about three and a half hours to Pemberton. There you can go hiking through the rainforest, visit a vineyard, and stay in a cozy cabin.

All of these trips offer a great mix of scenery, activities, and wonderful places to eat, making them perfect for your new family!
