For candy, the classic Halloween favorites are always chocolate bars like Snickers, Milky Way, and 3 Musketeers, plus gummies like Starbursts and Haribo gummies. You could also get those individually wrapped candies like Skittles, M&M's, or Reese's Pieces. For something spooky, you could get those mini chocolate bars with candy eyes or fun-sized versions of everything. If you want something a little different, sour patch kids or gummies are always good options too. Just try to buy in bulk or in those Halloween-themed bags to make it fun for guests. The more classic and recognizable, the better, so that no one feels left out.
