Here's a comprehensive list of treats and tips for your adorable corgi princess, Princess:

### 🍦 Homemade Treats
1. **Frozen Bites**:  
   - Mashed **pumpkin** or **sweet potato** in ice cube trays (freeze overnight).  
   - Blend **steamed carrots** or **zucchini** with yogurt for soft, chilled bites.  
2. **Peanut Butter Cookies**:  
   - Mix peanut butter (xylitol-free!) with oats, shape into small discs, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10 minutes.  
3. **Yogurt & Fruit**:  
   - Freeze plain Greek yogurt with mashed **blueberries** or **banana** for tiny, protein-rich snacks.  
4. **Carrot Sticks**:  
   - Cut raw carrots into thin sticks for crunchy, low-calorie chews.  

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands
- **Budget-friendly**: Zuke’s Mini Naturals, Wellness Treats.  
- **Specialty**: Blue Buffalo Life Protection Bits, Greenies Mini Dental Treats.  
- **Human-like flavors**: Merrick Healthy Grains Training Treats (grain-free options available).  

### 🐾 Training Tips
- **Sit Command**: Pair a raised treat over her head with the word "sit." Reward instantly when she lowers her rear.  
- **Potty Training**:  
  - Take her outside **immediately** after meals, naps, or waking.  
  - Use a **consistent phrase** ("Go potty!") and celebrate heavily when successful.  
  - For accidents, clean with an enzymatic cleaner (e.g., Nature’s Miracle) to erase scent.  
- **Barking at Felix**:  
  - Redirect with a **chew toy** or command like "look at me."  
  - Reward quiet behavior with treats and praise.  
  - Ignore barking entirely—no eye contact or attention.  

### 🎀 Collar Color Ideas
- **Teal** or **turquoise**: Stands out beautifully against brown fur.  
- **Pink** or **coral**: Sweet and playful.  
- **Royal blue**: Elegant contrast.  
*Pro Tip:* Add a charm matching her favorite toy (e.g., a bone-shaped clasp)!  

### ⚠️ Safety Notes
- **Homemade treats**: Keep portions tiny (1-2 per feeding). Avoid grapes, onions, or chocolate.  
- **Store-bought**: Check for artificial additives—opt for "grain-free" or "natural" labels.  

Princess will love these treats, stay patient with training, and look fabulous in her new collar! 💖
