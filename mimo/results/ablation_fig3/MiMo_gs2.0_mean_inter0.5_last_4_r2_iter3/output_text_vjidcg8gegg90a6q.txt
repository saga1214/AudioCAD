For winter chicks integrated into the adult coop, you need bedding that provides insulation, is safe, and doesn't attract pests. Since rodents are a concern, avoid anything loose like straw or wood shavings. 

Your absolute best option is to use a thick layer, about two to three inches, of clean wood pellets. They are very dense and insulating, they don't break down and heat up, and they are extremely difficult for rodents to chew through. 

An excellent alternative is to use a commercial, high-quality chick starter feed. You'll want to use the unmedicated variety. You just need to spread a very thick layer, maybe four to six inches deep, on the plate. The chicks will naturally huddle inside it for warmth, and it will compost down into a warm, nutrient-rich fertilizer for the rest of your flock.
