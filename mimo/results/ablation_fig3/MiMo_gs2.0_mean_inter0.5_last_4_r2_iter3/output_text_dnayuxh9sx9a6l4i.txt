Sure! First, Golden Retrievers are friendly, trainable, and great with kids, though larger—verify landlord size rules. Second, Labrador Retrievers are similar: sociable, easygoing, and good with other dogs. If size is a concern, they might qualify as 'not too big.' Third, Basset Hounds are calm, low-energy, and affectionate, though they can be noisy when alone—so check the 'too noisy' rule. Hybrid breeds could include Goldendoodles—they're popular for being hypoallergenic and friendly—or Labradoodles, which are playful but can be high-energy. Lastly, Cavalier King Charles Spaniels are small, loving, and calm, perfect for apartment living, but verify shedding concerns if either of you dislikes hair. Research each breed’s needs, temperament, and shedding to find the best fit for your high-rise life and schedules.
