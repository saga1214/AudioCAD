Based on **fuel efficiency, comfort, and practicality** for sedans, here's the top three list, with BMW often not making the cut:

1.  **Toyota:** Renowned for exceptional reliability, excellent comfort across price points, and outstanding fuel efficiency (especially in hybrids like the Prius and Camry Hybrid). Known for low ownership costs and long-term durability.
2.  **Honda:** Praised for a great blend of efficiency, comfort, and practicality. Offers smooth rides, reliable engines, and spacious interiors in models like the Accord and Civic. Strong value proposition.
3.  **Hyundai:** Often overlooked, Hyundai (and Kia) offer tremendous value, competitive fuel efficiency (especially in the Elantra and Sonata), and very good comfort and practicality. Their "Value Package" is frequently a standout feature.

**Why BMW often isn't in this top 3 for *these* criteria:**

*   **Fuel Efficiency:** BMW sedans typically offer good *performance* but not always the *absolute best* fuel economy compared to Toyota, Honda, or Hyundai. Their engines are powerful but less focused on efficiency.
*   **Comfort & Practicality:** While BMW provides a premium, sporty *feel* and solid ride comfort, their sedans are often less spacious inside (especially in the rear) compared to competitors like the Accord or Sonata. Practicality (like cargo space and rear seat room) is generally not their primary focus.
*   **Cost:** BMW sedans come with higher purchase prices and significantly higher maintenance/repair costs over time.

**In summary:** For the *best overall combination* of **fuel efficiency, comfort, and practicality** in a sedan, **Toyota, Honda, and Hyundai (Kia)** are typically the top contenders. BMW excels in performance, handling, and luxury, but not in the specific criteria of efficiency and practicality.
