That's a thoughtful question! Here's a breakdown to help you decide:

### 🥩 For Vegans:
- **Regular frozen burgers** are **not vegan** (most contain animal products like eggs, dairy, or meat fillings).
- **Vegan frozen burgers** exist (e.g., Beyond Meat, Impossible, or brands like Lightlife). **Always check labels** for hidden animal products.

### 🌡️ For Everyone:
- **Food Safety**: Frozen food **must stay at 40°F (4°C) or below** until eaten to prevent bacterial growth. A cooler with ice packs is essential.  
- **Time Factor**: If burgers thawed, they’ll spoil faster. Serve them **immediately after thawing** (use a cooler with ice).

### 💡 Suggestions:
1. **Opt for vegan burgers**: Bring plant-based frozen patties (e.g., Beyond Meat Beefless Patties) and label them clearly.  
2. **Prevent thawing**: Pack frozen burgers in a cooler with ice packs.  
3. **Alternative options**: Vegan hot dogs or veggie burgers are also quick and safe.
