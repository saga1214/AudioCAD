That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour Resort is right on a beautiful, calm bay, and Red Hook is just a short walk away with tons of energy. Here are some great daytime activities right near Secret Harbour and Red Hook:

1.  **Snorkeling at Coki Beach (Right Nearby!):** This is the absolute closest and most convenient spot. The reef is vibrant, teeming with fish, and the water is usually calm. You can easily walk down or rent gear nearby. Perfect for a quick, refreshing swim and snorkel.
2.  **Kayaking/Paddleboarding at Coki Beach or the Adjacent Park:** The Secret Harbour Resort is literally steps from the entrance to Coki Beach State Park. They have kayak and paddleboard rentals. Paddling out on the calm bay offers a unique perspective – you might spot sea turtles or other marine life from the water!
3.  **Explore the Red Hook Market & Shops (Short Walk):** Wander along the waterfront promenade. It's always lively, with shops selling souvenirs, local crafts, and art. Grab some fresh fruit or a local treat from the vendors. It's a great way to soak up the atmosphere.
4.  **Hike to the St. Thomas Summit Trail (Slightly Further, but Worth It):** A scenic, moderate hike (about 1-1.5 hours round trip from Red Hook) offering breathtaking views of the Virgin Islands, especially the Baths in Virgin Gorda. Great exercise and stunning photo ops. Check the trail conditions before you go.
5.  **Visit the Red Hook Pirate Museum:** Learn about the area's colorful pirate history in an interactive, engaging museum located right on the waterfront in Red Hook. It's a fun, informative stop.
6.  **Relax & Swim at Secret Harbour Beach:** Simply enjoy the resort's own private beach! It's peaceful, beautiful, and perfect for swimming in the calm bay waters right off the deck.
7.  **Visit the Red Hook Waterfront Park:** A small but pleasant park with benches, a playground, and more waterfront views. Good spot for a picnic lunch.
8.  **Explore the Red Hook Waterfront Promenade:** Just walk! Stroll along the boardwalk, admire the sailboats, watch the traffic, and soak in the vibrant local vibe. Stop at a cafe for a drink or snack.

**Pro Tip:** If the weather isn't perfect for snorkeling or hiking, the bustling Red Hook market and shops are always a great, low-pressure alternative to enjoy the local culture.
