The left brain versus right brain idea is largely a myth. While some functions are more dominant in one hemisphere, the brain is highly interconnected. Creativity and logic are both processed across the brain, not neatly separated. However, people do tend to have different cognitive styles. Some might be more analytical, while others are more intuitive. But it’s not a strict left versus right thing.

Second, data visualization or infographics. It blends creativity with concrete data, making complex information understandable. 

Third, scientific illustration or technical drawing. It’s a mix of art and science, creating precise and beautiful representations of factual information.
