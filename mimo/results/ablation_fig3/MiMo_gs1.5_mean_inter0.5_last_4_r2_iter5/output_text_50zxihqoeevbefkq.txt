Of course, here is a shopping list for your New Year's Eve party. I've organized it into categories to make it easier for you.

First, for drinks: you'll need champagne, sparkling grape juice, wine for the adults, and plenty of soda water and ice. Don't forget your signature cocktail ingredients and any mixers you might use.

For appetizers and main dishes: you'll need ground beef or pork for the sliders, shrimp for the cocktail, and pre-made pigs in a blanket. For the main course ham, you'll need a good quality ham, the scalloped potatoes, roasted asparagus, and olive oil and lemon for that simple carrot side dish.

For kid-friendly items, make sure you have plenty of grape juice and maybe some fun plastic cups and noisemakers.

For food safety and cleanup, stock up on paper towels, napkins, and a big cooler with ice. And of course, all the basic pantry staples like butter, salt, pepper, oil, and sugar will be essential.

Finally, for the party atmosphere, you'll need decorations like balloons and a playlist for your music.
