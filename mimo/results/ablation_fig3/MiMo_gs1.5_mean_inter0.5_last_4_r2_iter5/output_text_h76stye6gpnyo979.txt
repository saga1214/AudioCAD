Okay, you're juggling some amazing dreams—the Faroe Islands trip, Bolivia's salt flats—and real-life goals like saving for an apartment and home renovation.

Here’s a way to organize your ambitions so they feel more doable, kind of like how you figured out your bookshelf.

First, you'd want to match each dream to a savings category. You could have a 'Dream Travel' fund for the Faroes, Bolivia, and Japan. Then a separate 'Home Base' fund for the apartment down payment and the renovation. This helps you see where the money is going.

Next, you'd break each trip down into tiny, actionable tasks. For the Faroes puffins, you could start with a 30-minute search for the best times to visit. Then, maybe compare two or three budget airlines. And finally, just add the trip to your calendar for next year. You can do these little things on your phone while you're waiting for a meeting to end.

For Bolivia, you could start by looking up the best time to visit the salt flats, or maybe add the place to your travel bucket list app.

Then, for your financial goals, you could set up an automatic transfer to your 'Dream Travel' account right after you get paid. For the 'Home Base' fund, maybe round up your daily coffee purchases and transfer the spare change. And for your work, you could block out a specific time on your calendar for planning, just like you did with reorganizing your books.

The key is to focus on one big thing at a time. For the next six months, really focus on the Faroes puffins, and then shift your attention to Bolivia. This way, you're making steady progress on your dreams without letting work and life get in the way.
