Absolutely, there are some great things to do in Lockport that focus on its canal heritage. The most famous is the Erie Canal Museum, which is located right next to the locks. It has a fantastic, hands-on exhibit called the "Canal Connection" that shows exactly how the canal works.

Beyond that, the city is full of history. You can visit the historic canal houses and the beautiful canalfront parks. If you're really into the World War Two spy story, you might even find some memorabilia at local museums or historical societies. The whole area has a wonderful atmosphere that's built around the legacy of the canal.
