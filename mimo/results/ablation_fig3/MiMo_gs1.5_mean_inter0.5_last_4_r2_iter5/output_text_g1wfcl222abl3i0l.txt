**Glue Gun Troubleshooting Tips:**

2. **Ensure it's fully heated:** Wait until the cord stops flashing and the tip is bubbling. Sometimes it needs 2-3 minutes.

4. **Clean the tip:** Wipe the nozzle with a damp cloth if it looks dry or clogged.
