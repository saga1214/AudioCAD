You're absolutely right to bring that up. Public acceptance is the most volatile factor. If we give public acceptance a much higher weight, the vaccination campaign scores drop significantly. The sugar campaign, while difficult, isn't nearly as politically divisive.

So, my final recommendation would be the sugar reduction campaign. It has the medical feasibility of the vaccination campaign but without the explosive political controversy.
