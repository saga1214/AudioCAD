If you're looking for a weekend adventure, you've got some fantastic options nearby.

For a quick escape, you could drive about an hour east to the Banff Gondola. It's not quite the Rockies yet, but the views from the summit are stunning, and you can have a picnic or grab a drink at the top. It's a bit more relaxed than a full-on hiking trip.

If you want something more active, you could head to Canmore and rent bikes to explore the Peter Lougheed Park trails. It's a beautiful, flat ride with great scenery.

For a real road trip vibe, you could drive up to Jasper, which is about a three-hour drive. You can hike in Maligne Canyon, take a boat cruise on Maligne Lake, or visit the famous Columbia Icefield. It's a bit further, but it's a classic Alberta adventure.

And if you want something a bit more off-the-beaten-path, the Kananaskis Country area is incredible. It's just west of Calgary and has everything from scenic drives to hiking trails like the Kananaskis Lakes loop, which is a beautiful, moderate hike.

Whichever you choose, it sounds like you're off to a great start!
