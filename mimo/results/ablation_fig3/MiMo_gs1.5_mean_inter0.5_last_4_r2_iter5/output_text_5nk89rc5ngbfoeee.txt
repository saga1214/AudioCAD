For an end-of-year celebration, a **park BBQ with sundae bar** is a fantastic choice! It hits all the right notes: fun, active, inclusive, and memorable. Here’s why it works and how to make it smooth:

1.  **Location:** A park is perfect. It feels special (not just the usual cafeteria), provides space for games, and the BBQ vibe is inherently celebratory. Ensure you have a shaded area and a good picnic table or portable stove setup.
2.  **Food:**
    *   **BBQ:** Keep it simple but delicious! Pre-cook burgers/hot dogs or chicken wings. Let the girls help with simple tasks like grilling (supervised!), setting out condiments, or serving.
    *   **Sundae Bar:** This is the star! Set up a station with:
        *   Vanilla/Chocolate Ice Cream (check dairy-free options if needed).
        *   Syrups (chocolate, caramel, strawberry).
        *   Toppings: Sprinkles, mini M&Ms, chopped nuts, whipped cream, maraschino cherries.
        *   Have bowls prepped with toppings and ice cream scoops ready.
    *   **Snacks:** Offer a mix: chips, pretzels, fruit cups, or watermelon slices alongside the BBQ and sundaes. This caters to different tastes and keeps energy up.
3.  **Activities:** Build in fun, low-pressure games:
    *   **Three-Legged Race:** Classic and hilarious for Brownies!
    *   **Sack Race:** Always a crowd-pleaser.
    *   **Obstacle Course:** Use cones, hula hoops, pool noodles, and a jump rope. Let the girls set it up themselves!
    *   **Brownie Badge Showcase:** Have each girl briefly (1-2 min) share one badge they earned that year. It's a great way to celebrate their achievements.
    *   **Group Photo:** Capture the year with a fun group shot.
4.  **Logistics & Cost:**
    *   **Parents:** Ask a few parents to help with setup (parking, tables, grills), food prep (if possible), supervision during games, and cleanup. A simple sign-up sheet works wonders.
    *   **Food Cost:** Keep the BBQ cost low by focusing on simple proteins and letting parents/girls help. The sundae bar ingredients (ice cream, toppings) can be done affordably. Consider a small "BBQ Fund" collected earlier in the year or split the BBQ cost among parents.
    *   **Your Role:** Focus on being the hostess and cheerleader! Delegate tasks: "Can you help set up the sundae bar?" or "Who wants to help flip burgers?" Your pregnancy means you don't need to do everything physically.
5.  **Weather Backup:** Have a simple plan (like the cafeteria or a local park pavilion) if rain is possible, but usually, a park BBQ is resilient enough.

**Why this works for YOU:**

*   **Manageable:** Focuses on a few key elements (BBQ, sundae bar, games) rather than a huge production.
*   **Low-Pressure:** Games are simple and don't require special equipment.
*   **Celebratory:** The BBQ and sundaes feel like a proper party.
*   **Inclusive:** Caters to different food preferences (dairy-free option, variety of snacks).
*   **Parent Support:** Makes it easier for you by involving others.

**Key Takeaway:** A park BBQ with a sundae bar is a classic, fun, and achievable end-of-year celebration. It leverages the girls' energy, involves parents, celebrates their badge-earning, and provides delicious treats. Focus on the fun vibe and delegate – you've got this!
