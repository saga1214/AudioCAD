Got it. For Asian noodle places in Dallas, you’ve got some great options. I'd recommend these:

First, there's **Noodle House**. They have a huge variety of noodle dishes, like the famous Hot and Sour Soup and Beef and Broccoli. It's a classic spot.

Then, there's **Saizerio**. They have a really good ramen and tonkotsu, and the service is fast. It’s a bit more upscale but still casual.

Another great place is **Yamashiro**. If you want something more high-end, they do amazing teppanyaki and sushi, and their miso ramen is a standout dish.

And lastly, **Kung Fu Sushi**. It’s more casual, but their noodle rolls and spicy noodles are great, and it’s a fun, lively atmosphere.

As for protein bars, if you want something high in protein and low in sugar, I'd suggest these:

- **Quest Bars**: They’re high in protein, low in carbs, and have a good texture.
- **RXBAR**: They use whole ingredients like dates and egg whites for natural energy.
- **Lärabar**: A bit softer, but they have good options with nuts, seeds, and dried fruit.
- **Clif Builder’s**: A classic choice, with a good balance of protein and carbs.
