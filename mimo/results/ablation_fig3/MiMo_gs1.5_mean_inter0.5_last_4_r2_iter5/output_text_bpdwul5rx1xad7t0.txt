Of course! Vancouver is a fantastic base for road trips. Here are five great options for you and your growing family.

First, for a beautiful coastal trip, you could drive to Tofino on Vancouver Island. You can watch whales from the water, hike among ancient trees, and even go whale watching. It's a bit of a drive, about seven and a half hours, but it's a wonderful adventure.

Next, for a scenic and relaxed trip, you could head to the Okanagan Valley, which is only about an hour and a half from Vancouver. You can enjoy swimming in the lakes, exploring wineries, and trying local food. It's a great choice if you're ready for a getaway soon.

If you want to see mountains, a trip to Whistler Blackcomb is a must. You can go skiing in the winter or hiking in the summer, and it's only about a two-hour drive.

For a more rugged and wild experience, you could explore the Pacific Rim National Park on Vancouver Island. The trails are beautiful and perfect for your new Golden Retriever.

Finally, if you want to see mountains and coastlines all in one, you could drive up to the Fraser Valley. It's a short two-hour drive and you can enjoy wine tasting and farm visits.

All of these are great for families and offer a mix of beautiful scenery and fun activities.
