Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your TV mount, hanging planters, and floating shelves while respecting your apartment rules:

### 📺 1. **TV Mount Installation**
   - **Check Wall Type:** Use a stud finder to locate studs (critical for heavy mounts). If no studs, use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for your TV's weight.
   - **Measure Twice:** Ensure the mount aligns perfectly with your TV's VESA pattern (measured in millimeters).
   - **Secure Mount:** Attach the wall bracket to the studs or anchors, then secure the TV to the bracket.
   - **Power Safety:** Plug the TV into a surge-protected power strip. Avoid drilling near electrical outlets.
   - **Tools Needed:** Power drill, stud finder, level, screwdriver.

### 🌿 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **No-Drill Options:** Use:
     - **Suction cups** (for lightweight pots; test suction strength first).
     - **Adhesive hooks** (e.g., Command™ strips; ideal for lightweight plants).
     - **Wall-mounted planters** (ensure they’re rated for weight).
   - **Drill-Allowed Areas:** If permitted, use wall anchors in drywall. Avoid load-bearing walls.
   - **Placement:** Hang near windows for light. Ensure pots are securely attached and won’t tip.

### 📚 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Drywall Anchors:** For shelves under 15 lbs, use toggle bolts or heavy-duty anchors.
   - **Stud Mounting:** For heavier shelves (or multiple shelves), anchor to studs.
   - **Level Carefully:** Use a spirit level to prevent wobbly shelves.
   - **Weight Limits:** Check manufacturer specs—some shelves may need a second bracket on the back for stability.
   - **Tools Needed:** Drill, level, measuring tape, anchor kit.

### 🛠️ **Before You Start**
- **Landlord Rules:** Double-check drilling permissions. Use adhesive solutions where allowed.
- **Safety First:** Wear safety glasses. Avoid unstable walls (e.g., near windows or plumbing).
- **Workspace Prep:** Clear the area. Use drop cloths to protect floors.
- **Work Breaks:** Set timers (e.g., 25 mins work, 5 mins stretch) to avoid stiffness.

### 💡 **Pro Tips**
- **Label Parts:** Keep screws/tips organized for easy reassembly.
- **Test Fit:** Dry-fit shelves before drilling to confirm placement.
- **Lighting:** Add LED strip lights under shelves for ambiance.

By following these steps, you’ll create a functional and stylish space without violating rules. Prioritize safety and measure meticulously—your new studio will feel like home in no time! 🏡✨
