You're right to consider the big picture. Given everything you've described, there are two critical things to address right away.

First, and most importantly for his safety and your effectiveness, is the environment. The clutter and unpredictable dogs are serious safety hazards. You should discuss this immediately with your supervisor. The priority is to make the space safe. This could mean sessioning in a different room, setting up a clear, safe play area, or even suggesting that someone is present to manage the dogs during your sessions.

Second, on a practical level, since you can't change the environment overnight, you can work within it. Use music and sensory activities as your main entry points. Keep your sessions short and fun at first, and try to end on a positive note, even if you only made a tiny bit of progress. Building that trust is everything.
