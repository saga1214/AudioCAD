For the Pinot Noir, it should be stored at about 55 Fahrenheit and served slightly below room temp, around 55 to 60. The Pinot Grigio is a white, so it should be stored around 50 to 55 Fahrenheit and served chilled, at about 45 to 50. Champagne should be stored and served between 45 and 50 Fahrenheit. For cold weather, the Pinot Noir and the Italian Chianti are perfect, served slightly below room temperature to highlight their warmth. Mulled wine is also a classic for cold evenings, served hot.
