Here are some **crowd-pleaser candy options** that are universally loved at Halloween parties, with minimal risk of leftovers:

### 🎃 Top Tier Favorites (Safe & Reliable)
1. **Chocolate Bars**  
   - Milky Way, Snickers, Twix, 3 Musketeers  
   *(Everyone loves these classics!)*  
2. **Gummies**  
   - Starbursts, Sour Patch Kids, Haribo gummies  
   *(Sweet/sour combo = instant hits)*  
3. **Chewy Treats**  
   - Reese’s Cups/Peanut Butter Cups, 3 Musketeers  
   *(Soft textures win over picky eaters)*  
4. **Sour Candy**  
   - Skittles, Nerds, Warheads  
   *(Fun, nostalgic, and easy to eat)*  

### 🍬 Smart Strategy to Reduce Leftovers
- **Buy in Small Portion Sizes**:  
  Use small bags/bowls instead of large bags. Guests will take just a handful.  
- **Mix High & Low Demand**:  
  Include 1–2 "guilty pleasure" candies (e.g., Albanese Gummy Bears) alongside classics.  
- **Avoid Niche Flavors**:  
  Skip regional brands or overly sweet options (e.g., tropical Skittles).  
- **Check Expiration Dates**:  
  Buy today if possible—candy left in bowls often lasts 1–2 days.  

### ⚠️ Allergy-Friendly Tips
- **Natural Dyes**: Choose candies with natural colors (e.g., Skittles, gummies).  
- **Label Clearly**: If offering multiple candies, mark allergens (e.g., "No artificial dyes" or "Nut-free").  

### 💡 Pro Tip
**Pre-portion into small bags** as guests arrive. This encourages sharing and reduces bulk candy waste.  

With these, you’ll have a festive spread that’s fun, inclusive, and waste-free! 🎭
