Got it. For a 'Retro Tech' theme, you could have a meal called “Circuit Board Sandwiches.” The idea is to use mini circuit board-shaped cookie cutters on bread and fill them with turkey and cheese. For a snack, you could do “Dial-Up Cookies,” which are little cookies decorated to look like old computer icons. Another option is to serve a “Floppy Disk” salad in those plastic disk-shaped containers. Both are tech-themed but easy to eat.
