Of course! Let me recap the best plants for Florida Zone 10B that are also beginner-friendly. Herbs are a great place to start, like basil, mint, and parsley. Leafy greens are also fantastic choices—think lettuce, spinach, and kale. Radishes are another good one because they grow fast. 

For fruiting vegetables, tomatoes and peppers are ideal because they handle the heat pretty well. You could also try okra, which is a classic Southern crop that loves the warmth. Eggplant is another one that does well in your climate. 

Just remember to water them well during the heat waves and use shade cloth if you need to. Happy gardening!
