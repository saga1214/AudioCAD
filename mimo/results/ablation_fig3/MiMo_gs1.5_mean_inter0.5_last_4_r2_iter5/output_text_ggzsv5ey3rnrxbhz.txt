Eating a balanced diet with plenty of fiber can help your body get rid of them more efficiently, but it's not a quick fix.

Now, for your list of things to avoid. Based on what we've discussed, here are the top five:

Number one: Non-stick cookware, you know, like Teflon pans. It's much better to use stainless steel or cast iron.

Second: Grease-resistant packaging, like the paper on fast-food wrappers or microwave popcorn bags.

Third: Products labeled as stain-resistant or waterproof, such as carpets, furniture, and outdoor gear.

Fourth: Personal care products and some cosmetics, which can be found in things like shampoo.

And fifth: Be sure to filter your drinking water to avoid tap water and bottled water, which can both contain PFAS.
