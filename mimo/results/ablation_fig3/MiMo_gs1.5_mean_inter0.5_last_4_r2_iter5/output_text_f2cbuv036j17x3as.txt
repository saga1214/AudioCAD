Okay, so you're taking your diesel camper van on a long road trip and want to make sure it's safe. That's a smart move. For brakes, the most obvious sign of wear is when your vehicle starts pulling to one side, or if you hear a grinding noise when you brake. You should also check for any visible rust or unevenness in the pads and rotors. If you're not comfortable doing this yourself, it’s always best to have a professional mechanic give it a quick inspection before you go. For spark plugs, they are part of the engine’s ignition system and can wear out over time. If you're changing them yourself, you'll need to first disconnect the battery to avoid any electrical issues. Then, you'll have to remove the old plugs, which might involve some tools like a ratchet and socket. It’s a bit technical, so if you're not used to working on diesel engines, it might be safer to take it to a mechanic to handle that. Either way, getting the brakes and spark plugs checked and possibly replaced can give you peace of mind for the trip.
