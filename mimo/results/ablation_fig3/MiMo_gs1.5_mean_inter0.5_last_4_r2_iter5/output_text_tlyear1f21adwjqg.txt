This is a great question, but 'efficiency' can mean different things. If we're talking about pure energy conversion, hydropower is often the most efficient because it's so reliable and converts a high percentage of water's energy. But if we're talking about overall efficiency, including cost, scalability, and ease of use, solar energy usually comes out on top. It’s becoming more and more affordable, works almost everywhere, and is very easy to scale up or down. So, it really depends on what you value most: the highest conversion rate or the most practical, widespread solution.
