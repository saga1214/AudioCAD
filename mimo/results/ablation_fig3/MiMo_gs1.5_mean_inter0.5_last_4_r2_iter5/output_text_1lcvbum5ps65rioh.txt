### **U-Turn Rules in British Columbia:**
1. **General Restrictions**:  
   - **Not allowed at signalized intersections** unless a sign permits it.  
   - **Prohibited** on curves, near hills/obstructions, or where you can't see at least 150 meters in both directions.  
   - **Illegal** if it blocks traffic flow.  

2. **Penalties**:  
   - **Fine**: Up to $168 (as of 2023).  
   - **License points**: 1 point under BC Traffic Safety Act.  

3. **Quick Tips**:  
   - **Check signs**: Look for "U-Turn Permitted" or "No U-Turn" signs.  
   - **Visibility test**: If you need to pause to check traffic, wait for a clear gap.  
   - **Designated spots**: Use wide, straight roads or intersections where U-turns are legal.

### **Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients**:  
*(Authentic Roman-style, no cream)*  
**For 4 servings**:  
- **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti or bucatini  
- **Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 1 egg yolk (room temperature)  
- **Cheese**: 100g Pecorino Romano (grated), plus extra for serving  
- **Pancetta/Bacon**: 150g diced (pancetta preferred)  
- **Black pepper**: Freshly ground to taste  
- **Optional**: Pinch of salt (if pancetta isn’t salty)  

**Key Notes**:  
- **No cream**: Authentic carbonara relies on eggs, cheese, and pasta water for creaminess.  
- **Tempering**: Whisk eggs/cheese/pepper together. Slowly add hot pasta water to eggs off-heat to avoid scrambling.
