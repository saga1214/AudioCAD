That's a more nuanced take, but it still points to the chicken.

Here's the thing: a mutation happens in the DNA of an egg that's being formed. This mutation is what changes the creature hatched from it from a 'chicken' to a 'chicken' for the first time. The egg itself was produced by a bird that was already a chicken, or at least very close to it.

So, the first true chicken hatched *from* the egg, but the egg was made by a creature that was already in the process of becoming a chicken. The chicken hatched from the egg first.
