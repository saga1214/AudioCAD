### **Snacks (Sand-Safe & Themed)**

    *   1 bag strawberries (hulled & halved)
    *   *Theme Tip:* Thread onto small wooden skewers (cut in half for kids).
3.  **"Gold" Pirate Ship Masts:**
    *   1 bag Goldfish crackers
    *   *Theme Tip:* Pack in a mini treasure chest container or clear bag decorated like a ship.
4.  **"Mermaid’s Kiss" Fruit Cups:**
    *   1 container pineapple chunks
    *   1 container mandarin orange segments
    *   1 container peach cups
    *   *Theme Tip:* Label the containers with fun pirate names (e.g., "Mermaid’s Kiss," "Treasure Chest Fruit").
5.  **"Cannonball" Cheese Cubes:**
    *   1 large block Swiss or cheddar cheese (pre-cut into small cubes)
    *   *Theme Tip:* Pack in a small container labeled "Cannonballs!"
6.  **"Treasure Chest" Veggie Cups:**
    *   1 bag baby carrots
    *   1 bag cucumber sticks
    *   1 bag bell pepper strips
    *   Ranch or hummus dip (small cup)
    *   *Theme Tip:* Pack veggies upright in a small container, dip in a separate mini cup.
