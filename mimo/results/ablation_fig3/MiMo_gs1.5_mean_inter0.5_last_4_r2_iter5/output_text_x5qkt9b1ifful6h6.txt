Based on current scientific consensus, **ultra-processed foods (UPFs)** are widely considered the **most detrimental category** of food when consumed in excess, though they often *contain* other "worst" ingredients like artificial trans fats, sugar, and sodium. Here’s why:

### Key Reasons UPFs Are the Biggest Culprit:
1. **Nutrient Void, Calorie-Dense**:  
   UPFs are stripped of fiber, vitamins, and minerals while being packed with added sugars, unhealthy fats, salt, and artificial additives. This leads to "empty calories" and chronic overconsumption.

2. **Metabolic Harm**:  
   High sugar/fat/salt combinations disrupt blood sugar, increase inflammation, and promote obesity, diabetes, and cardiovascular disease. Studies link UPF-heavy diets to a **20–40% higher risk of premature death**.

3. **Additive Toxicity**:  
   They often contain preservatives (e.g., emulsifiers, artificial colors) that may harm gut health and trigger inflammation. For example, **trans fats** (now banned in many countries) are almost always *part* of UPFs.

4. **Behavioral Effects**:  
   UPFs are engineered to override hunger signals, encouraging overeating and contributing to weight gain.

### Why Trans Fats Aren’t the *Only* Worst Choice:
While artificial trans fats are uniquely toxic (raising LDL cholesterol while lowering HDL), **UPFs are the most common vehicle for them**. Modern UPFs also frequently contain:
- **Added sugars** (linked to diabetes/fatty liver)
- **Refined vegetable oils** (e.g., soybean/canola)
- **Excessive sodium** (linked to hypertension)

### Bottom Line:
- **Worst single ingredient**: Artificial trans fats (due to direct cholesterol impact).  
- **Worst *category* of food**: Ultra-processed foods (due to combined risks of multiple toxins + nutrient loss).  

For your class: Emphasize that **reducing UPFs** (like sugary drinks, packaged snacks, frozen meals) is the most practical step for improving health. Moderation matters, but minimizing these foods is key.
