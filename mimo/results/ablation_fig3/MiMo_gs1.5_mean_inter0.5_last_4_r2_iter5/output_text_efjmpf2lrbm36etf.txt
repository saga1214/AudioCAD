Of course, here are a few other idioms similar to burning the candle at both ends. First, there's “catching two buses at the same station,” which means to be too busy to focus on anything. Second is “robbing Peter to pay Paul,” which means you're sacrificing one thing for another, like spending money you don't have. Lastly, “putting in one’s hours” means working for a very long time.

Now, for your anniversary dinner plans, a few spontaneous and indulgent ideas. For drinks, you could try a Bellini, which is peach puree with prosecco, or a Aperol Spritz for something bright and refreshing. For a menu, look for places that have small plates or a nice charcuterie board, so you can share. A cozy wine bar or a rooftop lounge could work well. The key is to pick something that feels special, even if it's quick, like a place known for its cocktails or a local favorite that your wife enjoys. The goal is to make it fun and memorable.
