Okay, you're moving past the worst of it and ready to cook. That's great. Here are five simple and satisfying dinner ideas you can whip up with some basic ingredients. First, a big, comforting Pasta Primavera. You can get any kind of pasta, some veggies like asparagus, zucchini, and cherry tomatoes, and a jar of pesto. Just cook the pasta, sauté the veggies, and toss it all together with the pesto. Second, a hearty Chicken Stir-Fry. Grab some chicken breast, broccoli, bell peppers, and snap peas. Use a bottle of stir-fry sauce and a bit of soy sauce. Third, a classic Spaghetti Aglio e Olio. This one is super simple. You just need spaghetti, olive oil, some garlic cloves, red pepper flakes, and parsley. Fourth, a Creamy Tomato Basil Soup. Just grab a can of crushed tomatoes, some vegetable broth, a handful of fresh basil, and some cream or heavy cream. You can even toast some bread to go with it. And fifth, a quick and delicious Fajita Bowl. Get some ground beef or turkey, some peppers and onions, and a jar of fajita seasoning. Cook it all up, serve it in a bowl with rice and some toppings like avocado and sour cream. I hope that helps you relax and enjoy your vacation a little bit.
