Alright, here is a full list of recipes for your cocktails, with all the measurements, just like you asked.

First, the Old Fashioned: You'll need two ounces of bourbon or rye, one sugar cube, or one teaspoon of simple syrup, three to four dashes of Angostura bitters, and an orange twist for the garnish. To make it, you'll muddle the sugar cube with bitters, add the bourbon and ice, stir it well, and then garnish with the orange twist.

Next, the Paper Plane: For this one, you'll need two ounces of bourbon, one ounce of Aperol, three-quarters of an ounce of Amaro Nonino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Just shake all the ingredients with ice and strain into a coupe glass.

For the Whiskey Sour, with or without the egg white: If you're making it with the egg white, you'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and one fresh egg white. You'll dry shake everything first, then shake with ice, and strain into a rocks glass. If you prefer it without the egg white, just skip the egg white and stir the other ingredients over ice in a rocks glass.

Then there's the Aviation: You'll need two ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, a quarter-ounce of crème de violette, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Shake it with ice and strain into a cocktail glass.

And for the optional third gin cocktail, the French 75: You'll need one and a half ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and you'll top it with about two ounces of Champagne or Prosecco. You can either shake the first three ingredients and strain into a flute, then top with the champagne, or just shake everything together and serve it over ice.
