That's a great next step! For faster dinners, focus on versatile ingredients that minimize prep and cleanup. Here are some ideas:

1.  **Pantry Staples:** Rice (white, brown, or instant), pasta, lentils, canned beans (black, chickpeas, kidney), canned tomatoes (diced, crushed, paste), canned tuna/salmon, broth (vegetable or chicken).
2.  **Proteins:** Eggs (amazingly fast!), frozen shrimp, pre-cooked chicken (rotisserie or shredded), tofu (pre-baked or marinated), canned fish.
3.  **Vegetables:** Frozen mixed veggies (peas, carrots, corn), frozen stir-fry mixes, pre-washed salad greens, bagged spinach, mushrooms, bell peppers (often pre-sliced).
4.  **Flavor Boosters:** Soy sauce, sesame oil, basic spices (salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder), hot sauce, pre-made sauces (teriyaki, pesto, curry paste - check labels!), instant soups.

**Example Quick Dinners Using These:**

*   **Pantry Power Bowl:** Cook rice or pasta. Sauté frozen stir-fry veggies (or fresh broccoli/carrots) in a little oil. Add canned beans (drained/rinsed) and pre-cooked chicken. Toss with soy sauce, sesame oil, and maybe a spoonful of curry paste or teriyaki sauce. Top with pre-washed greens if you have them.
*   **Egg & Veggie Scramble:** Whisk eggs with salt, pepper, and maybe a splash of milk. Sauté chopped frozen veggies or spinach in a pan until warm. Pour in eggs, scramble until cooked. Serve over leftover rice or toast.
*   **Tuna Pasta Salad:** Mix canned tuna (drained), a can of drained/rinsed chickpeas or black beans, halved cherry tomatoes, chopped cucumber, and a bit of pre-washed greens. Dress simply with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and maybe a dash of mustard.
*   **Speedy Stir-Fry:** Use frozen stir-fry veggies. Heat oil in a pan. Add pre-cooked chicken or tofu cubes, stir-fry for a few minutes. Add frozen veggies, cook until heated through. Stir in a spoonful of soy sauce or teriyaki sauce. Serve over instant rice or noodles (like those five types you have!).

**Key Tips for Speed:**

*   **Batch Cook:** Make a big pot of rice, quinoa, or beans on the weekend.
*   **Prep Ahead:** Wash/chop veggies for the week, cook grains in bulk.
*   **Frozen is Fine:** Frozen veggies are just as nutritious and often pre-cooked, saving water and time.
*   **Pre-Cooked Proteins:** Rotisserie chicken, pre-cooked lentils, or canned beans are lifesavers.
*   **Minimal Sauces:** Use pre-made sauces sparingly to add flavor without extra cooking.

By focusing on these core ingredients, you can throw together satisfying meals in under 20 minutes!
