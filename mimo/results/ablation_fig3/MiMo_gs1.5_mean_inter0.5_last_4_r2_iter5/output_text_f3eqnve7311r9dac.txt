Yes, that's a great point. Older comics are generally rarer and can be more valuable. Here are ten notable books from the 1940s and 1950s that fit your criteria.

First, there's *The Flash* number one, the first appearance of The Flash from 1940. *Aquaman* number one, which introduced Aquaman in 1943, is another. *Green Lantern* number one, from 1956, is the first appearance of GL. *The Atom* number one, from 1945, is also a key Silver Age book. A classic from the Golden Age is *The Spectre* number one, from 1952.

For a more modern classic, *Black Panther* number one from 1966 is a must-have. *She-Hulk* number one from 1979 is another great key. A book that was printed in a very small number is *Marvel Super-Heroes* number one, from 1968. A major Silver Age key is *Thor* number one, from 1966. And finally, *Captain Marvel* number one, from 1967, is a very rare and valuable book. Just remember, finding high-grade copies of these will require patience and a good eye.
