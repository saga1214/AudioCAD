You've got most of the essentials! To make sure everything goes smoothly, I'd suggest grabbing a few more things. A drill with some screws is much easier and faster than hammering nails. You'll also need some shims to adjust the cabinets so they're perfectly level. A measuring tape is handy for checking spacing, and a pencil for marking screw holes. You'll also need a stud finder to locate the studs for the upper cabinets. If you have a box of old cabinet doors, that would be a great idea to use for clamping the uppers together. And finally, don't forget to wear safety glasses and work gloves to protect yourself from any splinters or debris. You're all set!
