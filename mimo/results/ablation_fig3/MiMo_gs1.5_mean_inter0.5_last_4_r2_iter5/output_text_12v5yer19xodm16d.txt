Okay, you need a pick-me-up. I hear you. I've got five drink ideas and five snack ideas to help you out. 

For drinks, you could try a cold brew coffee for a caffeine boost. Or maybe an energy drink, but watch the sugar. Green tea is a good option too, with its natural caffeine and antioxidants. A spicy hot chocolate can also give you a warm, energizing drink. And if you're not a fan of coffee, you could try a matcha latte.

For snacks, try some dark chocolate—it's rich in antioxidants and has caffeine. Nuts and seeds are great for a healthy fat and protein boost. Greek yogurt is high in protein, which helps with energy. An apple with peanut butter is a good mix of fiber and healthy fats. And of course, an energy bar is a classic for a quick fix.

Hope one of those helps you feel a little more awake. Let me know if you need anything else!
