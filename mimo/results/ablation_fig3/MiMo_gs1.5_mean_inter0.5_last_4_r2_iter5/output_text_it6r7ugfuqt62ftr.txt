Okay, based on your needs for warm weather, swimming, your diet, and a family vacation, I've put together a detailed itinerary for you. I'm thinking of a trip to Southern California, specifically focusing on San Diego and the nearby Coronado Island. It's perfect because it has warm ocean water, world-class beaches, plenty of pools, and lots of great food.

Let's start with where to stay. I'd really recommend a place called The Beachcomber Hotel on Coronado Island. It's a great choice because it's literally steps from the beach, very family-friendly, and has a lovely pool. It's also super convenient since you can drive directly onto the island. A mid-range hotel like this would probably run you around 300 to 400 dollars a night.

For the trip itself, I suggest renting a large SUV or a minivan. That way, you'll have plenty of space for everyone and all your gear. It'll only cost about 50 to 70 dollars a day.

Here’s a possible four-day plan. On Day One, you could arrive and settle in at The Beachcomber. In the afternoon, you can explore Coronado Beach and maybe rent some beach chairs. For dinner, you could go to The Crown Room on Coronado for a classic, upscale Californian menu. They can definitely accommodate gluten-free needs.

On Day Two, you could drive to La Jolla. You can visit La Jolla Cove to see the sea lions and take a swim at La Jolla Shores. For lunch, there's a place called The Cliff Restaurant with ocean views. In the afternoon, you could take a boat tour to see the sea caves. For dinner, The Fishery is a fantastic choice for fresh seafood.

For Day Three, you can head to San Diego. In the morning, visit Balboa Park, which has museums and gardens. For lunch, you could try Café 2228, which has a great gluten-free menu. In the afternoon, you could go to Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve for a hike with ocean views. For dinner, I'd suggest The Taco Stand for some authentic tacos.

On your last full day, Day Four, you could take a trip to Solana Beach. You can walk along the beach, visit A New Day Market for a gluten-free smoothie, and then take a tour of the Mission Beach Boardwalk. For dinner, you could go to Mission Beach Bar & Grill for some casual seafood.

Now, for packing, since you want to wear your bathing suit, make sure to bring two or three swimsuits, a couple of cover-ups, and a good swimsuit cover. For the warm weather, pack light clothes like shorts, t-shirts, and sundresses, along with a light sweater for cooler evenings. For sunscreen, I'd recommend a high-SPF, mineral-based one that's also gluten-free, and bring plenty of it. And don't forget a wide-brimmed hat and sunglasses.

As for dining, remember to always mention your daughter's Celiac diagnosis and that she needs a gluten-free menu. Most places in these areas are very used to handling it. Just be sure to call ahead to confirm.

Overall, this trip offers warm weather, great swimming, and a fantastic mix of relaxation and adventure for your family. Enjoy your vacation!
