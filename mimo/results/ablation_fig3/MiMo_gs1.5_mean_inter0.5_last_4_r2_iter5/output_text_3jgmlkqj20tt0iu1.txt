Here are two ideas. First, you can schedule a dedicated meeting for the purpose. Send your boss a brief email requesting a meeting specifically to discuss your career growth and compensation. You could say something like, "Hi, I'd like to schedule a time to discuss my performance and career path. Would you have 30 minutes available next week?" This shows you're professional and prepared, and it also gives you time to prepare your talking points beforehand.

Second, you could bring it up during a formal performance review meeting. If your company has a scheduled annual or semi-annual review, that's a very natural and appropriate time to have the conversation. You can frame it as a discussion about your contributions and your future goals within the company, and then ask if compensation adjustments are part of the company's plan for high performers.
