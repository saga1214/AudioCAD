That’s a thoughtful gesture! Since your sister is a vegetarian, I’d suggest focusing on flavorful, hearty dishes that don’t feel like a compromise. Here are a few ideas:  

- **Main Course:** Mushroom risotto with truffle oil and Parmesan, stuffed bell peppers with quinoa, black beans, and sweet potatoes, or a lentil shepherd’s pie with a creamy mashed potato topping.  
- **Side Dishes:** Roasted Brussels sprouts with balsamic glaze, garlic sautéed spinach, or a simple arugula salad with lemon vinaigrette.  
- **Dessert:** Chocolate avocado mousse, a berry tart, or a flourless chocolate cake.  

Pick what sounds best to you—she’ll appreciate the effort! Happy planning!
