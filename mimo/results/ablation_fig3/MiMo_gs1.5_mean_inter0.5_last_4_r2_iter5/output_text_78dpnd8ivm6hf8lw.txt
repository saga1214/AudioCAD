### Putting it all together:
1. **Heart (Gas Station)**  
   Pumps cars (blood) and refuels them with oxygen from the lungs.

2. **Lungs (Fuel Source)**  
   Provide the "fuel" (oxygen) that powers the blood cars.

3. **Highways (Arteries)**  
   Big roads that carry blood away from the heart at high speed.

4. **Small Streets (Capillaries)**  
   Tiny roads where blood cars deliver oxygen to cells and pick up waste.

5. **Return Roads (Veins)**  
   Take blood cars back to the heart after they’ve delivered oxygen.

6. **Kidneys (Car Wash)**  
   Filter waste and excess water from the blood, turning it into urine.

7. **Liver (Service Station)**  
   Processes nutrients, detoxifies harmful substances, and stores energy.
