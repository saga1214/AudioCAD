You're right to think about weight. Succulents are very lightweight. The bird's nest snake plant is also relatively light, but it is a bit sturdier and taller than the succulents, so it might feel a little heavier to carry.

So, for your table, you could choose a few small succulents. They'll be very easy to carry up five flights of stairs. You could pick three or four small pots and just put them in a single bag.

For the snake plant, since it might be a little heavier, you could consider a smaller version, like a 'Golden Bird's Nest' variety, which is a bit more compact. Even so, it's still manageable to carry up five flights of stairs. At the end of your stay, you can easily take both types of plants home with you.
