Here’s a plan for your appreciation table refresh station, aligned with your crisp, energizing vibe—plus badge advice and flavor ideas:

### Refresh Station Plan
1. **Drinks**:  
   - **Infused Waters**: Lemon-ginger, cucumber-mint, or berry-basil.  
   - **Herbal Teas**: Mint, chamomile, or hibiscus (iced or hot).  
   - **Light Options**: Coconut water, sparkling water, or coconut milk in cartons.  

2. **Snacks**:  
   - Fresh fruit (berries, grapes, apple slices).  
   - Light crackers, rice cakes, or veggie sticks with hummus.  
   - Energy bites (oats + dates + cocoa powder).  
   - *Avoid nuts* (due to allergies).  

3. **Decorations**:  
   - **Tablecloth**: Light blue or mint-green linen.  
   - **Centerpiece**: Small vase with white daisies or eucalyptus.  
   - **Signs**: Handmade chalkboard notes ("Stay Refreshed!") or dried flower garlands.  
   - **Easy Cleanup**: Use washable materials; store items in clear jars/bottles.  

4. **Palate Cleansers**:  
   - Breath mints (lemon or vanilla).  
   - Coconut water popsicles or cucumber sorbet.  
   - Bite-sized dark chocolate truffles (mild cocoa, no mint).

For that **bright, refreshing theme**:

- **Chocolate Alternatives**:  
  - Coconut + vanilla (mild, tropical).  
  - Dark chocolate + orange zest (bold but not minty).  
  - Matcha + white chocolate (subtle earthy sweetness).
