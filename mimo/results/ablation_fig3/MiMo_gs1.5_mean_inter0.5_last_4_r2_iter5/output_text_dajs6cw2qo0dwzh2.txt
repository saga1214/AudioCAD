That's a fascinating question. I think the timing is likely the same, but the *character* of the Age of Discovery would have been very different. The event in Constantinople would have given the whole quest a much more urgent and dramatic political motivation.

For example, the intense rivalry between Spain and Portugal, which fueled so much exploration, was partly driven by the desire to establish Christianity. The threat of the Ottomans would have made that a much more pressing goal. So, while the exploration might have happened anyway, the specific motivations, the speed of the race, and the intensity of the competition would have been completely different.
