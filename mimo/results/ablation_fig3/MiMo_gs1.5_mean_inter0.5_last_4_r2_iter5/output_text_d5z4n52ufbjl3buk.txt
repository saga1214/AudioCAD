That's a great goal—expressing respect while being direct. Here’s how you could structure it.

First, start with a polite subject line. For example: 'Meeting Request to Discuss Exam Feedback'.

In the body, state your purpose clearly and positively. You could write something like, 'I'm writing to request a brief meeting to discuss my recent exam score and to seek your guidance on how I can improve my essay writing for your class.'

Then, add a specific request. For instance, 'I'm particularly interested in understanding how I could have addressed the content more effectively based on your grading criteria.'

Finally, propose a time. You could say, 'Would you be available for a 15-minute chat during your office hours or at another convenient time for you?' And be sure to thank him for his time and insight. 

This approach keeps the conversation professional and focused on growth.
