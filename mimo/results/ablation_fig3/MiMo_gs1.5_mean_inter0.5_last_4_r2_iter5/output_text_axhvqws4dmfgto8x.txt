Alright, let's get you past that smoke alarm. Stir-fry is a good choice, and we'll make sure the noodles are perfect.

First, and this is the most important part: use a metal spoon. Wood will never set off your smoke alarm again. A metal spoon is what you need for boiling water.

Second, here's a pro-tip for perfect noodles. When you pour the boiling water into the pot, just pour it in. Wait about a minute, then take a deep breath and stir the noodles vigorously. This is called 'breaking up' the noodles. It makes sure they cook evenly and don't clump together.

Then just cook them for the time on the package, drain them well, and you're good to go. No more smoke alarms!
