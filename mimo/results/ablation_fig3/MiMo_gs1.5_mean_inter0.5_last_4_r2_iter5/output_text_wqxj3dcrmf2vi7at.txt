Here's a complete recap of delicious treats for Princess, your brown-and-white corgi princess:

### 🐾 **Homemade Treats**  
1. **Frozen Bites**:  
   - Mashed pumpkin or sweet potato shaped into small patties, frozen solid.  
   - *Bonus*: Add a dash of peanut butter for extra richness!  

2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**:  
   - Mix peanut butter (xylitol-free!) with rolled oats.  
   - Roll into balls, flatten, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15-20 minutes. Cool before breaking into pieces.  

3. **Chicken/Carrot Sticks**:  
   - Cooked chicken breast or steamed carrot sticks cut into thin strips.  

### 🛒 **Store-Bought Brands** (Look for these):  
1. **Blue Buffalo** (Grain-Free Bites)  
2. **Wellness** (Natural Grain Free Treats)  
3. **Zuke’s** (Mini Nibbles)  
4. **Greenies** (Dental Chews)  
5. **Merrick** (Grain-Free Recipes)  

### 📌 **Key Tips**:  
- **Portion Control**: Corgis are prone to obesity—stick to small pieces (1-2 per day).  
- **Training Treats**: Use tiny pieces (pea-sized) for commands like "sit" or "potty."  
- **Potty Training**: Reward **immediately** after she goes outside!  
- **Safety First**: Avoid chocolate, onions, grapes, xylitol, or raw dough.  

### 💖 **Color Idea for Collar**:  
Teal/turquoise or soft pink will **pop beautifully** against her brown and white coat—making her look like a regal corgi queen!  

Princess deserves the best—happy snacking, little princess! 🐾✨
