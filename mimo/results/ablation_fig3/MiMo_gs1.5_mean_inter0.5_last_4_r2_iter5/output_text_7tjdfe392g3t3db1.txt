To tune your instrument (like a guitar), here's how to use either a physical tuner or a phone app:

### 1. **Clip-On Tuner (Best for Guitar/Ukulele):**
   - **Attach it** to the headstock (where the strings meet the instrument).
   - **Pluck a string** and let it ring out.
   - **Watch the display:** The tuner shows pitch (like "A," "E," etc.) and a visual indicator (green = perfect, yellow = adjust slightly, red = too far off).
   - **Adjust the tuning peg:** Turn the peg clockwise to tighten (raise pitch) or counterclockwise to loosen (lower pitch) until the tuner shows green.

### 2. **Phone App (Free & Portable):**
   - **Open an app** like "GuitarTuna," "Fender Tune," or "Tuner."
   - **Press the tuner button** on the app.
   - **Pluck a string** and let it ring.
   - **Follow the app's guidance:** It’ll show which string to tune (e.g., "A string: tune higher/lower") and how much.
   - **Use the fine-tuning knob** on your instrument to adjust until the app confirms "tuned."

### **How Tuners Work:**
   - They detect the **frequency** of the vibrating string (measured in Hertz).
   - Compare it to a **reference pitch** (e.g., the note "A" at 440 Hz).
   - Display feedback to help you match the pitch precisely.

### **Tips for Success:**
   - **Start with the 6th string (low E)** and work up to the 1st (high E).
   - **Pluck strings gently** for consistent vibrations.
   - **Tune in pairs** (e.g., 1st-2nd strings together, 3rd-4th, etc.) for evenness.
   - **Tune often**—temperature changes affect tuning!
