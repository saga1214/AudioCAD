Yes, you can absolutely submit a complete package, and that's often a much stronger approach. If you've illustrated your own poems, you can submit the final text and artwork together to publishers or agents who specifically represent illustrated books, like children's books or poetry books.

However, there is a risk to consider. If you submit the artwork with your manuscript, the publisher would technically own both the text and the art. This means you wouldn't be able to sell the illustrations to anyone else, like for a personal website or a different book.

So, if you have a personal passion for the illustrations, you might consider self-publishing the book yourself. That way, you maintain full control and can sell the book and the artwork independently.
