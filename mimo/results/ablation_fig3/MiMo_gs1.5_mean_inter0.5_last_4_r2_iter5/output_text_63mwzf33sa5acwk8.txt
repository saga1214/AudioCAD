Okay, so after your desert adventure, heading into Vegas for a change of pace sounds perfect. For a proper Vegas experience, you could splurge on a nice hotel room with a pool, maybe somewhere with a great view. A hot shower is definitely a must after a long drive. You could also check out a classic Vegas show, like Cirque du Soleil or a big-name performer. If you're into nightlife, some clubs have great music and a fun vibe. And of course, you can always hit up a casino for a few games or just enjoy the atmosphere. Just remember to pace yourselves—it's easy to get overbooked!
