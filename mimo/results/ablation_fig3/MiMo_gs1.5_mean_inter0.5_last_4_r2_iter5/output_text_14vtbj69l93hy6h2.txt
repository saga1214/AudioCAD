No worries, it's common for dogs to be picky. If cheese wasn't her thing, maybe try some tiny peanut butter treats or cooked chicken bits instead. The key is to find something she really loves, so she associates training with something delicious. For freeze-dried liver, plain, unsalted liver pieces or liver wafers are usually great.
