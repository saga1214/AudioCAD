Here's your optimized packing list split by segment, keeping your conditions and pacing duties in mind:

### **Day Segment (20 Miles, Daytime - Mid 30s, Hilly)**
1. **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking top & bottom (synthetic or merino wool).  
2. **Mid-Layer:** Fleece jacket or lightweight puffy (for cool downshifts).  
3. **Outer Layer:** Wind/water-resistant shell (e.g., rain jacket or windbreaker).  
4. **Headwear:** Light-brimmed hat or cap (breathable).  
5. **Hands:** Gloves (lightweight for day, thicker for night if needed).  
6. **Footwear:** Trail shoes with aggressive tread (for hills).  
7. **Visibility:** Reflective gear or light (daytime visibility near roads).  
8. **Small Pack/Vest:** To carry extra layers at aid stations.  
9. **Extras:** Buff/skin-tight for neck/wrists, sunglasses, sunscreen.  

### **Night Segment (12 Miles, Nighttime)**
1. **Base Layer:** Same as day (keep dry).  
2. **Mid-Layer:** Insulated puffy or fleece (critical for warmth).  
3. **Outer Layer:** Windproof/waterproof shell (e.g., softshell or hardshell jacket).  
4. **Headwear:** Warm hat or balaclava (to retain heat).  
5. **Hands:** Gloves (insulated or mittens).  
6. **Lighting:** **Headlamp + spare batteries** (mandatory). Consider a handheld light too.  
7. **Visibility:** Reflective strips or vest (for low light).  
8. **Footwear:** Same trail shoes (tread for hills/dew).  
9. **Extras:** Buff/gaiter (for face/wrists), extra layers (if pack is full).  

### **Key Considerations for Both Segments**
- **Layer Flexibility:** Pack items that can be added/subtracted (e.g., puffy over fleece).  
- **Aid Station Access:** Use stations to swap layers or refuel.  
- **Weather Buffer:** Add a thin windbreaker to outer layer if it’s breezy.  
- **Practice:** Test gear *before* race day to avoid surprises.  

**Pro Tip:** For night, prioritize warmth and visibility. If it’s damp, pack a light rain layer. You’ve got this! 🌟
