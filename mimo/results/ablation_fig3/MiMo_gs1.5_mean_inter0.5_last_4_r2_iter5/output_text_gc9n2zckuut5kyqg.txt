Great point—sticking to a budget is key! Here are **budget-friendly, diabetes-aware desserts** that are kid-friendly and easy to prepare:

### 🍓 **Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:**
1. **Fruit Skewers or Salad Bowls**  
   - **Why it works:** Fresh fruit is inexpensive and naturally sweet.  
   - **Tip:** Add yogurt or a drizzle of honey for extra richness.  
   - **Cost:** ~$0.50–$1.00 per person.

2. **Homemade Banana Bread or Muffins**  
   - **Why it works:** Uses pantry staples (bananas, oats, eggs).  
   - **Tip:** Reduce sugar by 25% and use mashed banana instead.  
   - **Cost:** ~$1.50–$2.00 per serving.

3. **Frozen Yogurt Pops or Cups**  
   - **Why it works:** Yogurt is affordable and customizable.  
   - **Tip:** Layer yogurt, berries, and granola in cups.  
   - **Cost:** ~$1.00–$1.50 per person.

4. **Mini Cheesecake Parfaits**  
   - **Why it works:** Uses store-bought graham crackers and cream cheese.  
   - **Tip:** Layer with Greek yogurt and fresh berries.  
   - **Cost:** ~$1.25 per person.

5. **Dark Chocolate-Dipped Fruit**  
   - **Why it works:** Dark chocolate (70%+ cacao) has less sugar.  
   - **Tip:** Dip strawberries or orange slices for a sweet treat.  
   - **Cost:** ~$2.00 per person.

### 💡 **Budget-Saving Tips:**
- **Batch Baking:** Make desserts in large quantities (e.g., 1 loaf of banana bread instead of 6 muffins).  
- **Local Donations:** Ask family members to contribute ingredients (flour, oats, yogurt).  
- **Label Clearly:** Use signs like "Low-Sugar" or "High-Fiber" to educate guests.  
- **DIY Stations:** Let kids decorate their own yogurt parfaits or fruit skewers.  

### 🍳 **Food Pairing:**
- **Main:** BBQ burgers/hot dogs (use lean ground turkey for a healthier twist).  
- **Sides:** Grilled veggies, bean salads, and whole-grain buns.  
- **Beverages:** Infused water (cucumber + lemon), herbal tea, and sparkling water.
