For winter chicks, the best and safest bedding is wood shavings. Specifically, I recommend pine or aspen shavings that are labeled for poultry. They provide excellent insulation and are easy to manage.

Avoid using straw or hay because they can get damp very easily and become a slipping hazard. Also, avoid cedar shavings, as the essential oils can irritate the chicks' respiratory systems.

For cleanliness, you can use a deep litter method with a thick layer of the shavings, or you can do spot cleaning as you go. Just make sure to leave a dry area for them to huddle in.
