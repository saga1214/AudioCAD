Alright, here’s a detailed shopping list for your Asian-inspired meal, keeping legumes and seafood out. Let's break it down course by course.

First, for the starter, the miso soup or cucumber salad. If you go with miso soup, you'll need miso paste, either white or red, some dashi or vegetable broth, sliced shiitake mushrooms, and maybe some wakame seaweed. If you choose the cucumber salad, you'll need cucumbers, rice vinegar, sugar, sesame oil, salt, and some toasted sesame seeds for garnish.

Next, for the main course, chicken teriyaki. You'll need boneless, skinless chicken breasts, soy sauce, mirin, sake, or even just a bit of sugar or honey for the glaze, some minced garlic, and ginger. For the side, you'll want jasmine rice, and a mix of stir-fried veggies like bell peppers, carrots, and snap peas. You could also grab some sliced mushrooms for that.

Finally, for dessert, if you're making matcha ice cream, you'll need fresh matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and maybe a splash of vanilla extract. For the mango sticky rice, you'll need sticky jasmine rice, fresh mangoes, coconut milk, sugar, and salt.

Don't forget the condiments and seasonings you'll need for the whole meal: soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, mirin, sake, sugar, salt, and black pepper.
