Got it, quick and easy. For the vodka, a simple Vodka Soda is always a safe and classy choice. Just mix vodka with soda water, add a lime wedge or a slice for a little citrus. You could even add a dash of simple syrup if you want it a little sweeter. For the Argentinian-style Fernet, a classic choice is a Fernet and Cola. It’s a bold mix, so you might want to suggest a one-to-two ratio of Fernet to cola. You can serve it over ice with a lemon twist if you want to make it look a little fancier. Both are easy to make and should work well for the drinkers in your group. Hope that helps!
