Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn inspiration! If you're going in the fall, say, September to November, you'll definitely want to go during their dry season. That's roughly October to November, when the weather is just right—warm but not too hot. You can explore coastal areas like Tulum or Cancún for those beautiful autumn waters, or head inland to Oaxaca for its incredible fall foliage and local markets. 

You could even paint in a studio or just sketch outdoors. The light is softer and more vibrant during this time, which is great for capturing autumn scenes. Just remember to pack your watercolors and a sketchbook—you never know what beautiful colors you’ll find! Enjoy your trip and your new techniques!
