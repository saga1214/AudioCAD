Don't worry about setting off the alarm this time!

Stir-fry uses almost no boiling at all, so you're in the clear on that front. For the noodles, you'll want to use a large pot and just add plenty of water. You can test if it's ready by putting a pinch in and seeing if it sinks. Then, stir them constantly until they float to the top.

The most important part is to drain them very well. Let them sit in the strainer for a minute or two before you add them to the stir-fry. That will prevent them from getting mushy.

And hey, if disaster does strike, just remember setting off the alarm is tradition at this point
