Okay, let me recap all the key points for you. Driving from L.A. to Phoenix is about six hours without traffic. A flight is much faster, typically just over an hour.

Phoenix is famous for sunny outdoor activities like hiking and golfing. Culturally, it has several notable museums.

Population-wise, it is definitely growing.

Average year-round temperature in Phoenix is about 75 degrees, compared to L.A.'s cooler average of 64. Public schools rank in the lower half nationally, and property taxes are generally lower.

Politically, Phoenix as a city leans Democratic, while many suburbs are more conservative.

Finally, regarding horses, yes, it has a strong ranching culture. Horseback riding is very easy to find, and while owning a horse is expensive, a good way to start is with lessons or leasing.
