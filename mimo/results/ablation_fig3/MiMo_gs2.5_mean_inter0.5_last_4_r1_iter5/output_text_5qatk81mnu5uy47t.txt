### 🌴 Top Costa Rican Dive Spots for You
1. **Cocos Island**  
   - **Why you’ll love it:** Famous for **tiger sharks, sea lions, manta rays**, and deep dives into volcanic caves.  
   - **Best for:** Adventurous divers (though some sites require experience).  
   - **Tip:** Stay in **Santa Catalina** (charming village, easy access).

2. **Tortuguero National Park**  
   - **Why you’ll love it:** Gentle, warm-water reefs with **sea turtles** (nesting season: June–November) and **manatees**.  
   - **Best for:** Beginners, calm waters, eco-tourism.  
   - **Tip:** Boat from Tortuguero to **Drake Bay** for incredible biodiversity.

3. **Puerto Viejo de Talamanca**  
   - **Why you’ll love it:** **Coral reefs, humpback whales** (July–October), and river adventures.  
   - **Best for:** Scenic diving + cultural immersion (beachfront stays).

4. **Playa Conchal (Manuel Antonio)**  
   - **Why you’ll love it:** Close to San José, **easy shore dives**, snorkeling with sea lions, and pristine beaches.  
   - **Best for:** First-time divers wanting convenience.

### 📋 Sample 10-Day Itinerary
- **Days 1–3:** Arrive in San José → Drive to **Puerto Viejo** (5–6 hrs).  
- **Days 4–6:** Dive at **Tortuguero** (boat tours to offshore reefs).  
- **Days 7–9:** Fly to **Cocos Island** (1-hour flight). Explore deep dives and marine mammals.  
- **Days 10–11:** Return to mainland → Relax in **Conchal** or **Manuel Antonio**.

### 💡 Pro Tips for Beginners
- **Dive Shops:** Book with **Costa Rica Dive Center** (Tortuguero) or **Cocos Island Dive** (Cocos). They offer guided dives for all levels.  
- **Gear:** Your short fins will work perfectly. Bring reef-safe sunscreen and a dry bag.  
- **Safety:** Stick to **PADI-certified operators** and dive max 4 sites/day to avoid fatigue.  
- **Logistics:** Rent a car for mainland exploration; use boats/tours for island/island dives.  

### 🌟 Why Costa Rica Fits Your Style
- **Marine Life:** Similar to Belize/Honduras—turtles, rays, sharks, and vibrant coral.  
- **Accessibility:** Calmer sites (like Tortuguero) balance adventure with ease.  
- **Beyond Diving:** Zip-lining, wildlife sanctuaries, and beach time round out your dream trip.
