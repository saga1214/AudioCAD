For wedding speeches, the standard is about 750 words for five minutes. If you tend to talk faster when nervous, you could draft closer to 1,125 words. The key is to practice the speech out loud to get a feel for the pacing. You can even record yourself to check how it sounds. To avoid rambling, just focus on telling the key stories about your friendship and watching her relationship grow with her fiancé. And don't forget to leave room for natural pauses when the audience reacts. You can do this!
