That’s a great parallel! Decluttering your home office can feel just as overwhelming as your kitchen, especially with papers and tangled cables. Here’s how to tackle it:

### 📦 For Home Office Decluttering:
1. **Paper Triage**:  
   - **Keep**: Important documents (taxes, warranties).  
   - **Shred**: Old bills, drafts.  
   - **Recycle**: Anything else.  
   - Use a scanner app (like Adobe Scan) to digitize and shred paper immediately.  

2. **Cable Chaos**:  
   - **Label everything** with a sticky note.  
   - Use cable sleeves or Velcro ties to bundle cords.  
   - For charging, try a **multi-port USB hub** to reduce outlets.  

3. **Digital Declutter**:  
   - Unsubscribe from non-essential emails.  
   - Organize files into clear folders (e.g., "Work," "Personal").  
   - Delete old downloads—keep only essentials.  

4. **Small Steps**:  
   - Start with *one drawer* (e.g., desk supplies).  
   - Next: *one file cabinet* (pay stubs only).  
   - Progress builds momentum!  

### 🍳 For Fast Dinner Ingredients:
Since you’re simplifying your kitchen, focus on **pantry staples** that require minimal prep:  
- **Rice/noodles**: Stick with your top 1–2 favorites!  
- **Proteins**: Eggs, canned beans, tofu, or pre-cooked grains.  
- **Veggies**: Frozen stir-fry mixes, spinach, or pre-chopped onions.  
- **Sauces**: A versatile base like soy sauce, sesame oil, or curry paste.  

**Quick Meal Idea**:  
> **15-Minute Stir-Fry**:  
> Sauté garlic + ginger in a pan.  
> Add frozen veggies + protein (e.g., tofu).  
> Toss with 1 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tsp sesame oil.  
> Serve over rice or your rice noodles!  

### 💡 Key Insight:
Decluttering *any* space starts with **identifying what you actually use**. Your five rice noodle types are a perfect example—keep your favorite, donate the rest. This mindset applies to office supplies, cables, and even digital files.  

You’re doing great by tackling this step by step! Focus on one area at a time, and celebrate small wins. 🎉
