Okay, this is a great plan. You're taking control by preparing in advance.

Here are some key pointers for your email. First, start with a clear subject line, something like: "Meeting Request: Seeking Exam Feedback".

Then, in the body of the email, state your purpose right away. You could say, "I scored a 64 on my latest exam and would like to learn how I could improve on essays for this class to do better on future assessments."

Next, make it clear you're asking for constructive feedback, not challenging the grade. You could add, "I'm particularly interested in understanding how your subjective grading criteria were applied in this case to help me better align with them."

Finally, ask for a specific time to meet, and be sure to mention that his office hours conflict with your class schedule. You could say, "Could you please let me know if another time would work for you?"

This approach keeps it professional, focuses on your growth, and avoids triggering his ego. Good luck!
