Okay, you want some ocean-themed dinner and dessert ideas, keeping diabetes and shellfish allergies in mind. Let's see...

For dinner, since mango and spice are enjoyed, Spicy Tuna and Mango Sushi Rolls would be a fresh island vibe. For something heartier, a Mango-Coconut Shrimp Stir-Fry is flavorful, just make sure to check the stir-fry sauce for shellfish. A Seaweed Salad with Sesame Oil is light and crunchy. A Tropical Fish Ceviche with mango salsa would be bright and citrusy. And finally, a Spicy Miso Soup with Tofu gives a nice, warm finish.

For desserts, how about a Mango and Coconut Chia Pudding? It's sweet and tropical. A Dark Chocolate Sea Salt Cheesecake would be rich and spicy. A Lemon and Blueberry Panna Cotta with a berry compote is light and refreshing. A Pineapple Upside-Down Cake is always a crowd-pleaser. And for a fun, sweet touch, a Dark Chocolate-Dipped Strawberries with a drizzle of white chocolate would be perfect.

Have fun with your ocean night!
