Here's a recap of homemade and store-bought ideas for your little corgi princess, along with training tips and a color suggestion:

### 🍪 Homemade Treats:
1. **Frozen Bites**:  
   - Mash **pumpkin** or **sweet potato**, shape into small balls, and freeze.  
   - *Bonus*: Add a tiny bit of plain yogurt for extra flavor (no xylitol!).  
2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**:  
   - Mix peanut butter (check for xylitol!), oats, and an egg (optional for binding).  
   - Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15 mins. Cool before breaking into tiny pieces.  
3. **Carrot/Apple Sticks**:  
   - Cut raw carrots or steamed apples into thin sticks.  
   - *Tip*: Dip in peanut butter for extra appeal!  
4. **Cooked Chicken**:  
   - Plain, boiled chicken breast, diced into pea-sized pieces.  

> **Always**: Start with tiny portions (1-2 treats/day) to keep her fit!

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands:
- **Training Treats**: Zuke’s Mini Naturals, Wellness Treats, Blue Buffalo Homestyle Bites.  
- **Dental**: Greenies (small size).  
- **UK/US Brands**: Long Life (UK), Fromm (US).  
- *Avoid*: Anything with xylitol, salt, or garlic.  

### 🐾 Training Tips:
- **Potty Training**:  
  - Take her out on a schedule (after waking, meals, play).  
  - Reward instantly with praise + a tiny treat when she goes outside.  
  - Clean accidents thoroughly ( enzymatic cleaner removes odor).  
- **Quiet Command** (for barking at Felix):  
  - When barking starts, hold up a treat and say “Quiet.” When she stops, reward her!  

### 🎨 Collar Color Idea:  
For a **brown and white** princess, **teal/turquoise** or **soft pink** would make her eyes sparkle! Royal blue or even gold would also pop against her coat.  

Stay consistent with training, and your corgi princess will shine like royalty! ✨🐾
