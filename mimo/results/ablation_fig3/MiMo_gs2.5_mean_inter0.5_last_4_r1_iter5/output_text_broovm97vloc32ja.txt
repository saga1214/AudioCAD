That's a fantastic skill to have! In fact, being an artist who writes is a huge advantage in the children's book world.

Here's the best way to approach it. First, you'll write your query letter, just like we discussed. But in that letter, you'll specifically mention that you are an artist and that you have attached illustrations for your book.

Then, you'll submit your manuscript along with your illustrations. Publishers and agents love it when an author can also do the art because it shows the story and art are perfectly matched. Just make sure you use the same illustrations for every agent you query, so you can be consistent.

The only thing to remember is to follow their specific guidelines. Some agents might ask for a few sample illustrations instead of the whole book. If that happens, just follow their instructions. But for the most part, they will be very interested in seeing the complete package.
