   - **Stay:**  
     - **Hostels:** *Generator Bologna* (social, book-friendly) or *The Yellow Book Hotel* (Tuscany, literary theme).  
     - **Unique Stays:** Agriturismos in Tuscany (farm stays with cat-friendly spaces!).  
   - **Do:**  
     - Bookshops like *Libreria Comunista* (Bari) or *Casa del Libro* (Rome).  
     - Fantasy-themed tours (e.g., *Tolino Fantasy Tours* in Rome).  
     - Volunteer at cat sanctuaries (e.g., *NGO Feline* in Milan).  
   - **Get There:** Fly into Florence/Rome. Trains are cheap/easy (Italo or Trenitalia).  

### 2. **Portugal (Affordable Language + Bookish Vibes)**  
   - **Why?** Easy to travel affordably, Lisbon has a sci-fi/fantasy scene, and the Algarve is stunning.  
   - **Where?**  
     - **Lisbon:** Literary cafés (Café de Lis), futuristic architecture (Miradouro da Senhora do Monte).  
     - **Algarve:** Gothic cathedrals, coastal hikes (Praia da Marinha).  
   - **Stay:**  
     - **Hostels:** *The Yellow Book Hotel* (Lisbon, book-themed) or *Tascas do Bodegas* (Algarve).  
     - **Budget Tip:** Use *Hostelworld* for deals; student discounts exist.  
   - **Do:**  
     - Visit *Livraria Bertrand* (Lisbon’s oldest bookstore) or *Casa da Letra* (literary museum).  
     - Attend *Fantasia* (Lisbon’s fantasy convention) if timed right.  
     - Learn Portuguese with *Interlingua* (language exchange groups).  
   - **Get There:** Fly into Lisbon. Trains to Algarve are 1.5 hours.  

### 3. **Japan (Fantasy + Modern City Contrast)**  
   - **Why?** A surreal blend of sci-fi (neon-lit cities) and fantasy (temples, anime culture).  
   - **Where?**

   - **Stay:**  
     - **Hostels:** *Sakura Hostel* (Kyoto, cat-friendly!) or *Machi Hostel* (Tokyo).

     - Explore *Kawasaki* for manga/anime shops.  
     - Join *Tofu Tour* (Kyoto’s food culture).  
   - **Get There:** Fly into Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND). Trains: *JR Pass* (¥15,000/7 days covers most travel).
