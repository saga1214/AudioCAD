Here’s a detailed, **sand-free, pirate-themed shopping list** for your nautical birthday party, designed for 8 kids (your son + 7 guests), beach-friendly ease, and including a watermelon allergy alert:

### **Appetizers & Snacks**  
*(All pre-portioned to avoid spills)*  
- **Mini slider cups (8)**:  
  - 16 mini whole-wheat slider buns  
  - 1 lb pre-sliced turkey or ham  
  - 1 lb cheddar cheese  
  - 8 mini dill pickles  
  - 1 can cream cheese (for "pirate treasure" topping)  
- **"Treasure Chest" Cheese Cubes (8):**  
  - 1 lb pre-cut cheddar or mozzarella cubes  
- **Veggie Cups (8):**  
  - 1 bag pre-cut carrots, celery, and cucumber sticks  
  - 1 pint hummus (single-serve cups)  
- **Goldfish Crackers (8):**  
  - 2 bags (labeled "Captain’s Treasure" with pirate stickers)  
- **Pirate Ship Pretzels (8):**  
  - 12 mini pretzel rods (cut into 2-inch pieces)  

### **Fruits**  
*(All allergy-safe, pre-cut, and sealed)*  
- **Grapes (8 cups):** Pre-pitted and halved  
- **Apple Slices (8 cups):** Tossed in lemon juice to prevent browning  
- **"Jewel" Berries (8 cups):**  
  - Blueberries, raspberries, and strawberries (no watermelon!)  
  - *Label with pirate-themed stickers*  

### **Drinks**  
*(Individual packaging = zero spills)*  
- **Juice Boxes (8):**  
  - 12 boxes apple, berry, and tropical flavors  
  - *Decorate with "Pirate Punch" labels*  
- **Water Bottles (8):**  
  - 8 reusable bottles (or single-use plastic) with nautical lids  
- **Sparkling Water (optional):**  
  - 8 cans (for adults only—label "Captain’s Reserve")  

### **Desserts**  
- **Mini Cupcakes (8):**  
  - Vanilla or chocolate (no watermelon toppings)  
  - *Decorate with:*  
    - Blue edible glitter ("sea foam")  
    - Gold sprinkles ("gold coins")  
    - Mini chocolate chips ("pirate eyes")  
- **Pirate Treasure Cups (8):**  
  - Yogurt parfaits: Greek yogurt + granola + berry "jewels"  
  - *Use mini cups with pirate flag lids*  

### **Allergy-Safe Notes**  
- **Watermelon exclusion:** Use strawberries/blueberries instead.  
- **Label everything:** Clearly mark "NO WATERMELON" on fruit cups and juice boxes.  
- **Separate containers:** Store allergy-safe foods in labeled treasure chests.  

### **Thematic Packaging**  
- **"Pirate Chest" Trays:** Stack containers in wooden chests.  
- **Nautical Napkins:** Blue/white striped with anchor designs.  
- **Pirate Hats:** Optional for kids to wear!  

### **Sand-Friendly Extras**  
- **Individual Wet Wipes:** For quick cleanups.  
- **Portable Trash Bins:** Labeled "Doubloons" (gold foil).
