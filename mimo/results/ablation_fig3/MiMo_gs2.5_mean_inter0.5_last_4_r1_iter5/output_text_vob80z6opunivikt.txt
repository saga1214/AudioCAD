That coffee table is calling your snacks! Since you don’t mind non-sweet options, here are some ideas to fill it up:

### **Savory & Crunchy**  
- **Roasted chickpeas** or **puffed quinoa** (nutty, protein-packed).  
- **Spiced nuts** (e.g., chili-lime or maple-glazed).  
- **Seaweed snacks** or **kombu chips** (umami-rich).  
- **Crackers** with cheese slices or hummus.  
- **Air-popped popcorn** (with truffle salt or parmesan).  

### **Slightly Sweet/Salty**  
- **Dark chocolate-covered almonds** (70% cocoa, not too sugary).  
- **Candied nuts** (like maple pecans or coconut macadamias).  
- **Fruit leather** (tart cherry or pineapple).  
- **Gourmet popcorn** (cheddar, caramel, or everything bagel seasoning).  

### **Unique Vending Machine Picks**  
- **Wasabi peas** or **mango chili jerky** (spicy kick).  
- **Matcha-flavored rice crackers** (earthy and refreshing).  
- **Lemon olive oil crisps** (bright and savory).  
- **Dried mango or pineapple** (tangy, not sugary).  

### **Fun Display Ideas**  
- Arrange snacks in small bowls or on tiered trays to showcase them.  
- Add a mini cheeseboard with grapes, grapes, and crackers.  
- Use the coffee table as a "snack bar" with labeled sections (e.g., "Sweet & Salty," "Adventure Zone").
