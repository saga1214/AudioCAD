That's a very smart observation. Older books are inherently rarer, which makes them more valuable. Here are ten historically significant comics from that era that are still within a reasonable investment budget. For example, there's *Captain America Comics* number one, the first appearance of Cap, which sold for three million in 2021. Another is *Tales of Suspense* number thirty-nine, the last issue featuring Captain America before he was killed. You could also look at *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen, the first Spider-Man. And since you're a Power Man fan, *Champ Comics* number six is his first appearance. A few others to consider are *The Fantastic Four* number one, the first appearance of the Fantastic Four; *The Amazing Adventures of Spider-Man* number one, the first comic to feature the famous cover by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko; *The Avengers* number one, the first comic to introduce the Avengers; *Thor* number one, the first appearance of Thor; and *The Incredible Hulk* number one from 1962. These are all historically important books that have seen significant price increases over the years.
