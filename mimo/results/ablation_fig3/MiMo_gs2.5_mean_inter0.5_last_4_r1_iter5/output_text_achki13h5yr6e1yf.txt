Of course. To recap all the ingredients and their total price ranges for four family members:

For the Chicken Entree, two pounds of boneless skinless chicken breasts will be eight to fifteen dollars; All-purpose flour is three to five dollars; twelve eggs are three to seven dollars; a container of breadcrumbs is two to four dollars; and a quart or more of vegetable oil will be four to eight dollars.

For the Garlic Mashed Potatoes, a five-pound bag of potatoes will be three to six dollars; a head of garlic is about one dollar; a pound of butter is four to seven dollars; and a half-gallon of milk is three to five dollars.

So, for the main and the side combined, your total estimated cost will be somewhere between thirty and sixty-five dollars.
