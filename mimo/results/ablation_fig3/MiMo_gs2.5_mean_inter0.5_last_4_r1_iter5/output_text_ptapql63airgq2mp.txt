### **Simple (Easy to Prep, Crowd-Pleasing)**

3. **Miso-Glazed Eggplant Bites**:  
   - Roasted eggplant cut into wedges, brushed with white miso + mirin glaze. Serve with sesame seeds.

### **Unique (Exotic & Flavor-Forward)**  
1. **Seaweed & Kimchi Dip Station**:  
   - **Base**: Kimchi paste + sesame oil + rice vinegar.  
   - **Dippers**: Thinly sliced nori, cucumber ribbons, roasted rice cakes, and crispy wonton strips.  
   - *Bonus*: Add a spicy tamarind dipping sauce (mix tamarind paste + fish sauce + lime).  

2. **"Umami Bomb" Tacos**:  
   - DIY station with:  
     - *Protein*: Crispy tempura shrimp or pan-seared scallops.  
     - *Unique Toppings*: Fermented black garlic mayo, crispy lotus root chips, and pickled wasabi ginger.  

3. **Sweet & Sour "Snow Monkey" Snacks**:  
   - **Tamarind-Glazed Dried Squid**: Lightly fried squid strips coated in sweet-sour tamarind glaze.  
   - **Lychee & Mango Skewers**: Fresh fruit skewers drizzled with coconut cream + tamarind syrup.  

4. **Matcha-Everything Upgrade**:  
   - **Matcha Nachos**: Sweet potato tortilla chips + white cheddar + matcha dust.  
   - **Matcha Cheesecake Bites**: Mini baked cheesecakes with matcha frosting.  

5. **Kimchi Seaweed Ceviche**:  
   - Quick-marinated white fish (like flounder) with kimchi, nori flakes, and sesame oil. *Bright, tangy, and refreshing.*

### **Logistical Tips for 15 Guests**

- **Cold Items**: Keep ceviche, sushi tacos, and fruit skewers chilled in a cooler.  
- **Drinks Pairing**:
