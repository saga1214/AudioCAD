**Step 1: Confirm the Blockage Location**
*   **Check the U-Bend:** This is usually the culprit. Safely remove the drain cover and the U-bend assembly (it's usually held by two or four slip nuts under the sink). Inspect the pipes inside the U-bend for hair, grease, food debris, or a solid object. Clear any visible blockages with pliers or tongs.

**Step 2: Use a Drain Snake (Auger)**
*   **Insert Carefully:** Feed the snake head into the drain, pointing it towards the blockage. If you can feel resistance, you've likely hit it.
*   **Engage & Turn:** Apply gentle downward pressure while slowly rotating the handle clockwise. This "engages" the head and starts breaking up the clog.
*   **Drive Through:** Keep pushing and turning until you feel the head pass through the blockage and come out the other side. The resistance should lessen significantly.
*   **Clear Debris:** Once the head clears, pull it back out. You should see debris wrapped around it. Wipe it clean and repeat if necessary. Sometimes you might need to push the head further down the pipe if the debris is deep.

**Step 3: Apply Enzyme-Based Drain Cleaner (If Tools Aren't Sufficient)**
*   **Use ONLY Enzyme/Bio-Cleaners:** Avoid harsh chemical cleaners like Drano or Liquid Plumber, as they can damage pipes over time and are often ineffective on severe blockages. Choose a product specifically designed for enzymes or bacteria that break down organic matter.
*   **Follow Instructions:** Pour the correct amount directly into the drain, according to the product's directions.
*   **Be Patient:** These cleaners work slowly. Don't pour boiling water or other cleaners on top of them. Give it several hours (or overnight if the instructions say so).

**Step 4: Boiling Water (Use with Caution)**
*   **Only for Grease Clogs:** This is *only* effective for grease clogs that might be partially blocking the pipe *after* you've tried a snake or if the cleaner is working slowly. Pour a full kettle of boiling water slowly down the drain in stages (don't pour it all at once!). **Warning:** Never use this on PVC pipes, as the heat can melt them. Also, avoid using this on wax rings or on drain covers.

**Step 5: Call a Professional (If Needed)**
*   **If the Above Fails:** If the blockage is very deep, mineral-based (like limescale), or caused by a solid object (like a toy), you might need:
    *   **A Hand Auger:** A more powerful manual auger designed for deeper clogs.
    *   **A Power Drain Snake:** A much longer and more powerful electric auger.
    *   **A Plumber:** For a camera inspection to locate the exact blockage or for more complex repairs.

**Key Tips While Working:**
*   **Protect Yourself:** Wear gloves and eye protection, especially when removing the U-bend or using chemicals.
*   **Protect Pipes:** Be gentle when inserting the snake or auger to avoid scratching or damaging the pipe walls.
*   **Patience is Key:** Don't force the snake or auger aggressively. Work it slowly and steadily.
*   **Clear Debris:** As you clear the pipe, dispose of the waste properly (e.g., flush it down another drain if possible).
