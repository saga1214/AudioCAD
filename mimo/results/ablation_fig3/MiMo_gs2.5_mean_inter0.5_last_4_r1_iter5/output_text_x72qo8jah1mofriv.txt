Okay, for a kosher book club, you need to make sure everything is certified. That means checking ingredients for any non-kosher stuff like pork, shellfish, or certain fats. So for your healthy snack, veggie platters or fruit skewers are great because those are naturally fine. Your weenies in bacon, though, would need to be kosher-certified bacon. For sweets, theme it up—dark chocolate is usually fine if it's certified, and themed cookies would also work. For wine, you'll need to look for kosher-certified brands. Just check the labels on everything to make sure it has the proper certification. That way, everyone's covered.
