For candy tomorrow, you'll definitely want to grab the classics: Reese's Cups, Hershey's Kisses, Skittles, gummy worms, and maybe some mini chocolate bars. Those are always safe bets. You might also want to think about getting some Halloween-themed candy bags or boxes to display them in, which looks more festive. A few years ago, I threw a party and got a bunch of themed candy, and the guests loved it. Just make sure to check for allergens again on the candy labels, especially artificial dyes. And don't forget to set out a bowl of candy so people can grab what they want!
