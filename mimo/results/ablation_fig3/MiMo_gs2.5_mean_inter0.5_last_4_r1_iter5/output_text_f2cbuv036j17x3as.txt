Alright, diesel van pre-trip checklist. For brakes, the biggest thing is to listen for a grinding or squealing sound when you hit the pedal. Also, if your van feels like it’s dragging or you’re seeing a lot of dust from the wheels, that’s a red flag. You should also check the brake fluid level—if it’s low, that’s a sign of wear. For spark plugs, diesel engines use high-torque, long-life ones, but they still need to be replaced eventually, usually around every 100,000 miles. You’d need to remove the cylinder head to get to them, so it’s not a quick DIY job. A mechanic should handle it. Before you leave, just make sure your camper’s water system is disconnected from the main tank and you have fresh water for the trip. And always check your tires and fluids—oil, coolant, and transmission fluid—before you go. Safe travels!
