For an end-of-year celebration, a hybrid approach works best—balancing simplicity, inclusivity, and a sense of achievement. Here’s a practical plan tailored to your situation:

### 1. **Location Flexibility**
   - **Rain Plan:** Host the main event in the cafeteria (low-cost, familiar space). Use picnic tables for races if weather permits.
   - **Outdoor Option:** If weather cooperates, reserve a local park with a pavilion (many parks allow troop rentals for minimal fees). This adds adventure without requiring camping skills.

### 2. **Activities**
   - **Quick Games:** Focus on 30-minute activities like relay races, sack races, or a scavenger hunt near the park/cafeteria. These require minimal setup and badges.
   - **Sundae Bar:** A crowd-pleaser! Provide:
     - *Base:* Mini ice cream cones (dairy-free vanilla for you!).
     - *Toppings:* Cupcakes, sprinkles, fruit, and chocolate sauce. Parents can chaperone to avoid dietary issues.
   - **Award Ceremony:** Hand out badges in a short ceremony (5 mins). For a touch of fun, have girls present patches to each other.

### 3. **Food & Logistics**
   - **Snack System:** Rotate responsibility among parents—assign 2-3 per meeting to bring snacks. This shares the load.
   - **Dinner Option:** For a larger celebration, opt for a pizza party (many girls love it!) or a low-key BBQ. Use a potluck: parents bring sides, troop provides pizza/BBQ.
   - **Budget:** Charge a small "troop fee" ($10-15) to cover snacks and event supplies. Clearly state parents pay for sashes/patches separately.

### 4. **Weather Contingency**
   - If outdoors, have a backup tent or move to the cafeteria. A "rainy day" plan (like indoor karaoke or a talent show) keeps morale high.

### 5. **Camping Lite**
   - Skip full camping for your first year. Instead, host a "sleepover picnic" at a friend’s backyard or a cabin rental (if affordable). Focus on simple crafts and stargazing—no heavy gear required.

### Key Takeaway:
Prioritize **low-cost, high-inclusion activities** that celebrate achievements without overwhelming logistics. Your hybrid approach (cafeteria + park) ensures flexibility while making everyone feel special—especially with cookie sales and field trips already on your plate. You’ve got this! 🍦🎉
