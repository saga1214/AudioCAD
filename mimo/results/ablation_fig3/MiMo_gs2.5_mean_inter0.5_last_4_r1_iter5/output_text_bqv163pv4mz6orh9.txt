### 🌟 Personalized Keepsakes
1. **Custom Jewelry**: A bracelet, necklace, or keychain with her initials, name, or the play's title engraved.  
2. **Photo Frame**: A small frame with a photo of her rehearsing or a fun family pic.  
3. **Personalized Book**: A children's book with her name in the title (e.g., *"The Adventures of [Her Name]"*).

### 🎁 Practical & Fun Rewards
1. **Gift Card**: To her favorite ice cream shop, toy store, or a café she likes.  
2. **Experience Tickets**: Movie tickets, a museum pass, or tickets to a concert.  
3. **Fun Activity**: A spa day (if she’s into that), a game night, or a pizza-and-movie night *at your home*.

### 💌 Sentimental Touches
1. **Handwritten Note**: Write a heartfelt letter about how brave and talented she was.  
2. **"Proud Mom/Dad" Jar**: Fill a jar with small notes praising her specific moments on stage (e.g., *"You sounded amazing in Act 2!"*).  
3. **Custom Art**: Commission a simple illustration of her in costume or a silhouette of her on stage.

### 💡 Quick & Easy Ideas
- **A "Proud Parent" T-shirt** (if she’s into quirky gifts).  
- **A small plant** (symbolizing growth and pride).  
- **A cozy blanket** for post-show cuddles.

### 🎭 Pro Tip:
Capture the moment! Take a photo backstage with her castmates and frame it. Kids love seeing their hard work immortalized.

**Remember**: Even a small token with a genuine "I saw you work so hard for months, and I’m so proud!" will mean the world. Your daughter will remember the effort behind the gift more than the cost. 🌈✨
