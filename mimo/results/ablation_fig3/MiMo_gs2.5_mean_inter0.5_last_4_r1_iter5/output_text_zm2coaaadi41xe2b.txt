Dallas has a lot of great Asian noodle spots! For authentic and delicious options, I'd suggest these places:

First, there's Toritsuya, a Japanese ramen shop in Little Tokyo. They serve rich, flavorful ramens, and their shoyu or tonkotsu are particularly good.

Second is Pho Saigon, a Vietnamese spot with amazing pho. Their noodle broth is so aromatic and rich.

Third is the Golden Palace, which is a Korean-Mexican fusion restaurant. They have great jjapchae, which are sweet potato noodles, and other Korean dishes.

For protein bars, I'd recommend these:

One, RXBAR. They’re simple, with clean ingredients and good protein content.

Two, Quest Nutrition. These are low-carb and high-protein, so they're great for energy.

Three, Clif Builders. They're a bit sweeter but are very balanced and filling.
