Okay, so you and girlfriend are planning this West Coast to desert Nevada adventure in a van, then maybe hitting Vegas for the grand finale. Smart move combining them geographically. Joshua Tree/Nevada combo gives you distinct landscapes before the city reset.

Since new relationship, Joshua Tree's remoteness is perfect for connection – stargazing without distractions, hiking side-by-side. Vegas afterwards can be the celebratory "we made it!" experience. But key is tailoring each leg to your dating vibe.

Joshua Tree Practicalities:
- Drive time: 3-5hrs from major CA metros depending on your start point. Pack water, snacks, sun protection. Desert gets hot fast.
- Camping: Yes, official sites ($20-30/night) like Jumbo Rocks or Black Rock Canyon. Or free dispersed BLM spots outside the park (check BLM.gov for access points). Restrooms are limited – pack a small toilet bag.
- Unique touches: Sunrise at Keys View, geocaching, visiting the "actual" Joshua Trees exhibit at the visitor center. Download offline maps – service is spotty.

Nevada Transition:
- From Joshua Tree, head northeast on I-15. Stop in St. George, UT for lunch if needed (great BBQ spots).
- Red Rock Canyon: $10/night camping, stunning red rocks just 30min from Vegas. Or Valley of Fire ($15/night) for more solitude.
- BLM Note: In NV, dispersed camping is legal on BLM land but requires permits during peak season (May-Oct). Check Recreation.gov. Areas like the Midos or Tonopah Flats are remote gems.

Vegas Aftermath:
- Skip tourist traps. Book a boutique hotel (The Cosmopolitan's rooftop is romantic, or The Ace Hotel for indie vibe).
- "Proper" Vegas: A fancy cocktail at Atelier Crenn (tasting menu only!), then walk the Fremont Street Experience for free street performances. Rent bikes to explore the Strip at sunset.
- Avoid: Overpriced shows unless it's your absolute dream. Focus on intimate moments – maybe a private pool cabana or a cooking class.

Adventure Flow:
Day 1-3: Joshua Tree van adventure (camping/hiking)
Day 4-5: Drive to Red Rock/Valley of Fire (camping)
Day 6: Drive to Vegas, check into hotel
Day 7: Vegas relaxation/exploration

Budget Estimate:
- Gas: $300-500 depending on distance driven
- Campsites: $40-60 total
- Vegas: $200-400 per night for mid-range hotel + activities

This sequence balances adventure with luxury, new relationship intimacy with city fun. The van becomes your mobile basecamp – make it comfy with blankets, music, and maybe a portable speaker for desert vibes. Enjoy the adventure!
