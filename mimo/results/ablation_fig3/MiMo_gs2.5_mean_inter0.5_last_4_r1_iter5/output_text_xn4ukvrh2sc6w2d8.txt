Here’s a clear, bartender-friendly recipe list with precise measurements for your Art Deco-themed cocktail reception in Kentucky:

### 1. **Old Fashioned**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey  
- 1 sugar cube (or ¾ oz simple syrup)  
- 3–4 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  
*Instructions:*  
1. Muddle sugar cube with bitters in a mixing glass.  
2. Add bourbon and ice. Stir well until chilled.  
3. Strain into a chilled rocks glass over fresh ice.  
4. Express an orange twist over the drink and drop it in.  

---

### 2. **Paper Plane**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 1.5 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz Aperol  
- ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
*Instructions:*  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a coupe glass over crushed ice.  

---

### 3. **Whiskey Sour**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- *Optional:* ½ egg white  
*Instructions:*  
- **With egg white:** Dry shake (without ice) all ingredients in a shaker to emulsify. Add ice, shake again, and strain into a rocks glass. Garnish with a cherry or orange slice.  
- **Without egg white:** Shake with ice and strain as usual.  

---

### 4. **Aviation**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz maraschino liqueur  
- ½ oz crème de violette  
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice  
- 2 dashes maraschino cherry (for garnish)  
*Instructions:*  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
3. Garnish with a maraschino cherry.  

---

### 5. **Recommended Additional Gin Cocktail: French 75**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 3 oz chilled sparkling wine (Champagne or prosecco)  
*Instructions:*  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a champagne flute.  
3. Top with sparkling wine.  

---
