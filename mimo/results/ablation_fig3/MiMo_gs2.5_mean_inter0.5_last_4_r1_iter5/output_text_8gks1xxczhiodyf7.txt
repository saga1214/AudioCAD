Okay, easy access to processed, calorie-dense foods still feels like the top driver to me. It’s such a big part of modern life—cheap, everywhere, and designed to make you eat more. But I get why other factors matter too. Physical inactivity plays a huge role, and stress or sleep issues can mess with hunger signals. Genetics can make it harder for some people. But the food system makes processed stuff so easy to overconsume, I think that’s the biggest piece of the puzzle.
