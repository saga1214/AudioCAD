Okay, Mexico... Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration, especially if you go in the fall. The colors will be at their peak! If you're going in September or October, you'll see those incredible shades of red, orange, and yellow from all the leaves and flowers. The landscapes, like the mountains and deserts, will also give you a ton of variety. 

But, uh, if you're going for the winter, around December to February, you'll see the opposite. It's their spring, so you'll get more green, but you could still find some nice autumn-like colors in the highland areas. If you're going in the summer, from May to August, it's their peak summer, so everything is lush and green. That could also be a good time if you're more into the spring and summer vibes. But yeah, for that classic, vibrant autumn palette you love, definitely aim for September or October. That's when the colors are going to be at their most dramatic. Enjoy your trip!
