Sure, here’s a quick checklist for when you film your own puppet show. 

First, make sure you have permission to record, especially at places like assisted living facilities. 

Next, set up your camera or phone beforehand. You’ll want good lighting and a stable shot, so maybe prop it up against something. 

Then, during the show, try to keep your performance natural and engaging. Since it’s for older adults this time, Melvin the Magnificent might be fun to try. 

After the show, save the video and edit it if you want. You can add captions or music. 

Finally, upload it to your own channel and share it with friends or family who might help promote it. 

That’s the basic rundown. Good luck!
