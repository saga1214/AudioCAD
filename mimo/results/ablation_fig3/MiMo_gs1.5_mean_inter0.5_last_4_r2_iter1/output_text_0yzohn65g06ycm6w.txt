Here’s a concise summary of solutions for your fussy eaters and budget-friendly care:

### **Feeding Solutions**
1. **Controlled Access**  
   - Use **microchip feeders** or **timed dispensers** to ensure portions are fair.  
   - Separate fussy cats in **their own rooms** during feeding times.  

2. **Budget Food Hacks**  
   - Buy **quality food in bulk** (cheaper per serving).  
   - Mix small amounts of quality food with **fillers like plain cooked rice** (stretch meals affordably; consult a vet first).  

### **Low-Cost Health Maintenance**
3. **Free/Cheap Resources**  
   - Seek **local pet food banks** or shelters offering supplies.  
   - Join community groups for **donations or swaps**.  

4. **Preventive Care**  
   - Prioritize **low-cost vet services** (spaying, neutering, vaccines).  
   - Maintain **basic hygiene**: Clean bowls daily, offer fresh water.  
   - **Grooming**: Brush regularly to reduce hairballs/skin issues.  
   - **Exercise**: Use DIY toys (string, crumpled paper) for health.  

5. **Monitor at Home**  
   - Check for **weight changes, lethargy, or appetite shifts**.  
   - Catch early issues to avoid costly vet visits.  

### **Key Takeaway**  
Focus on **controlled feeding**, **preventive care**, and **community resources** to keep cats healthy while stretching funds. Prioritize bulk buying, hygiene, and early observation to minimize vet costs. 🐾
