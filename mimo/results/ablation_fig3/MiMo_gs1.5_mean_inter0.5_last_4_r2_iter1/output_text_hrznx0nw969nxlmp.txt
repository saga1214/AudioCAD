That's a fantastic choice! Here are some great daytime activities tailored to your boat-based trip and location:

**At Secret Harbour Beach Resort:**
1. **Snorkel the Resort's Private Bay:** The calm, protected waters right off their beach are ideal for beginner snorkeling—look for parrotfish, sea urchins, and maybe a small octopus.
2. **Kayak Exploration:** Rent kayaks (available on-site) and paddle to nearby **Cinnamon Bay**, stopping at the iconic **Cinnamon Bay Beach** for a swim and lunch. The trail is flat and scenic.
3. **Hike to Cinnamon Bay:** Take the easy, paved trail from Secret Harbour to Cinnamon Bay (about 15 mins). The climb is gentle, and the payoff is stunning turquoise water and a secluded beach—perfect for a picnic.

**Around Red Hook (East End):**
1. **Snorkel at The Baths (St. John):** A short boat ride (10-15 mins) to this iconic spot. Explore the tidal pools and snorkel in calm, shallow waters teeming with fish and crabs. *No diving needed!*
2. **Visit The Reef Cafe:** A top-rated spot right in Red Hook with ocean views. Order fresh seafood (try the lobster roll!) and enjoy live music on sunny days.
3. **Explore Cruz Bay:** Stroll the main street for local crafts, rum shops, and the **Red Hook Food Truck Park** (great for lunch with a view of Cruz Bay).
4. **Kayak or Paddleboard:** Rent gear from Red Hook Outfitters for a peaceful paddle along the mangroves and out to the open bay.

**Bonus Tip:** Check the tides! Calm, flat seas are best for snorkeling and kayaking. The Baths and Cinnamon Bay are usually safest during high tide. Have an amazing week—your boat trip sounds like the perfect adventure! 🌴🚤
