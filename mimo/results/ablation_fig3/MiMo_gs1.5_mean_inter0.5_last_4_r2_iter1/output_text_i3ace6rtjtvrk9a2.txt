### 2. **Brain's Role (Perceptual Processor)**
   - The visual cortex integrates signals from the eye and constructs a continuous percept.  
   - **Saccadic Masking Mechanism:** During saccades, the brain:  
     - Temporarily suppresses motion perception to prevent disorientation.  
     - Fills in visual gaps between saccades using prior/expected data.  
     - Creates the illusion of seamless motion despite the eye's rapid "jumps."  
   - **Perceptual Threshold:** Humans perceive motion as continuous at **>16 fps**, but **60 fps** feels "real-time" due to reduced motion blur.  

### 3. **Key Distinction: Eye vs. Brain**
   - **Eye:** Detects discrete events (e.g., flicker at 60 Hz, motion during saccades).  
   - **Brain:** *Constructs* continuity by smoothing gaps, suppressing blur, and integrating context.  
   - **Critical Point:** You do **not** "see continuously" because saccadic masking ensures you *never consciously perceive* the blur of eye movements.  

### 4. **Film vs. Reality Analogy**
