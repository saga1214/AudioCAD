Okay, let's see. You want to get your daughter a console to play with her. That's a great idea! 

Right now, the big three are the PlayStation 5, the Xbox Series X or S, and the Nintendo Switch. The Switch is really unique because you can play it on the TV or just on the go. Then there's the Steam Deck, which is more like a powerful laptop for gaming, and the Asus ROG Ally, which is similar.

So those would be five popular options. The Switch is probably the best bet if you want something she can take with her too. The PS5 and Xbox are more for playing at home with a big screen. Hope that helps you surprise her!
