What a wonderful way to celebrate her hard work! Here are some thoughtful gift ideas, ranging from quick to more involved:

### 🌟 Quick & Sweet (Under $20)
- **Custom Playbill:** Order a framed print of the playbill. It’s a permanent keepsake of her performance.
- **Starlight Cake:** Order a small, glittery cake from a local bakery (many offer "stage star" designs).
- **Theater-Themed Treats:** A box of gourmet chocolates or gourmet popcorn in a fun box.

### 🎭 Personalized Keepsake ($20-$50)
- **Personalized Playbill:** Add her name/role to the playbill print (e.g., "Role: Princess Elara" + her name).
- **Custom Jewelry:** A charm necklace with a theater mask or star symbol (like "Star of the Show").
- **Photo Frame:** Print a candid pic of her rehearsing and frame it with a handwritten note.

### 🎉 Experience-Based ($50+)
- **Post-Show Dinner:** If the food tour is next Thursday, invite her to join you! Turn it into a celebration.
- **VIP Box Tickets:** Buy a ticket for her (or both of you) to see a future show in a special section.
- **"Star" Gift Basket:** Assemble a basket with bath bombs, a cozy robe, a scented candle, and a thank-you note.

### 💡 Thoughtful Touches
- **Handwritten Note:** Write a heartfelt message about her dedication and pride. Kids remember that more than anything!
- **"Encore" Gift Card:** A Starbucks or dessert place card for after future shows.
