Of course. Here is a complete breakdown of the standard format for a resume. Just follow these sections and you'll be all set.

First, at the very top, you'll have your name in about a 14-point font. Directly below that, on a separate line, you'll put your contact information: your email, phone number, city, and a link to your LinkedIn profile.

Next, you'll have a short, powerful Professional Summary. This is a brief paragraph that highlights your most relevant skills and career goals.

After that, list your Relevant Experience. This is where you'll put the jobs that are most pertinent to the new roles you're targeting. For each job, list the company, your title, and the dates you worked there, followed by a few bullet points that describe your key responsibilities and achievements.

Then comes Education. Here you'll list your degrees, starting with the most recent. For each one, include the school name, the degree, your expected graduation date, and any academic honors or awards you received.

After Education, you can have a Skills section. This is a great place to list your key skills, like software you're proficient in or languages you speak. You can even organize them into categories if you want.

If you have significant volunteer work, you can add a Volunteer Experience section.

Finally, you can add a Professional Development section. This is where you can list any relevant certifications or online courses you've completed.

Just remember to use a clean, simple layout and keep it to one or two pages. This structure should help you create a professional and effective resume.
