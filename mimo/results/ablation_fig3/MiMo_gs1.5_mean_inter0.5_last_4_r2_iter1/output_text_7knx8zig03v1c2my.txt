Great questions! Here's a breakdown of treats and clicker training:

### Treat Recommendations:
1. **Flavors:**  
   - **Chicken:** High-value for most dogs (your puppy loves chicken!).  
   - **Beef:** Also popular and flavorful.  
   - **Carrot Sticks:** Low-calorie, crunchy, and hydrating.  
   - **Blueberries:** Sweet, healthy, and safe in moderation.  
   - **Freeze-Dried Liver:** Small, chewy, and packed with flavor.  
   *Tip:* Cut treats into tiny pieces (pea-sized) for easy eating while training.

2. **Key Considerations:**  
   - Use **low-calorie treats** (10% of daily calories) if your puppy is prone to weight gain.  
   - **Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions, or xylitol**—they’re toxic to dogs.  
   - **Rotate flavors** to keep training exciting!  

---

### Clicker Training Explained:  
Clicker training uses a **three-step process** to teach reliably:  

1. **The Clicker:**  
   - A small, handheld device that makes a sharp "click" sound.  
   - It bridges the gap between your puppy’s action and the reward.  

2. **How It Works:**  
   - **Step 1:** Let your puppy do something you like (e.g., sit, wag tail).  
   - **Step 2:** When they do it, **click** immediately (the click = "YES, I want that!").  
   - **Step 3:** Give a treat within 1–2 seconds after the click.  
   - Repeat 10–15 times per session.  

3. **Why It Works:**  
   - The click **marks the exact moment** your puppy does something right.  
   - The treat follows the click, so your puppy learns the click = reward.  
   - Over time, they’ll associate the click with good things!  

**Example:**  
> You hold a treat near your puppy’s nose. When they sit, you click → give treat. Repeat until they sit on command!  

---

### Bonus: Training Tips  
- **Short Sessions:** 5–10 minutes, 2–3 times daily. Puppy brains tire fast!  
- **Positive Reinforcement Only:** Never punish—reward every small step.  
- **End on a Win:** Stop when your puppy succeeds to keep them motivated.
