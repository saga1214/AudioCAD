Okay, let's break this down.

First, about the brain hemisphere idea. It's largely a myth. While each hemisphere does specialize in certain functions—like the left side for language and the right for spatial tasks—they work together all the time. Creativity and logic aren't locked into one side; they're more like a spectrum that we all exist on. So, it’s not an either/or thing.

Now, for careers that blend structure and creativity. Architecture is a great fit. It’s about building solid, lasting structures while also being creative with design. Another idea is technical illustration or data visualization—turning complex, measurable data into beautiful, creative visuals. You could also consider UX design, which is about structuring digital experiences while being creative in how you solve problems.

As for tinnitus, it’s almost always physical. It’s caused by damage to the auditory system, often from noise exposure or aging. Stress can make it feel worse, but it’s not post-traumatic in the way you might be thinking of it.
