That depends on what instrument you're tuning! Here's how to tune common stringed instruments using a tuner:

### For Guitar
1.  **Turn on the tuner** (clip-on or app).
2.  **Pluck a string** (usually the low E string first).
3.  **Watch the needle/app display:**
    *   If it points **left** of center (flat), **turn the tuning peg clockwise** (tighten).
    *   If it points **right** of center (sharp), **turn the tuning peg counter-clockwise** (loosen).
4.  **Pluck again** until the needle/app is centered (perfect pitch).
5.  **Repeat** for all strings, usually starting with the low E and moving up.

### For Violin
1.  **Position the tuner** near the bridge.
2.  **Draw the bow** on the string (or pluck it).
3.  **Watch the needle/app display:** Adjust pegs similarly (tighten/loosen).
4.  **Use a reference pitch:** Many tuners have a built-in reference note (like A440), or use an online tuner app.

### For Ukulele
1.  **Position the tuner** on the headstock.
2.  **Pluck a string** (usually the G string first).
3.  **Watch the needle/app display:** Adjust pegs similarly.
4.  **Tune to standard tuning:** G-C-E-A (from thickest to thinnest).

### For Banjo
1.  **Position the tuner** on the headstock.
2.  **Pluck a string** (usually the D string first).
3.  **Watch the needle/app display:** Adjust pegs similarly.
4.  **Tune to standard banjo tuning:** D-G-D-G-B-D (from thickest to thinnest).

### General Tips
*   **Be Patient:** Tuning takes practice. Adjust slowly.
*   **Listen:** A good tuner app also has an ear training mode – listen to the reference note and try to match your string.
*   **Check periodically:** Temperatures and humidity can affect tuning.
