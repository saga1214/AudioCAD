Here's a **nautical/pirate-themed, sand-friendly shopping list** for a full course beach party, including allergy-friendly options and immersive details:

---

### **Appetizers (Themed as "Pirate Treasure")**
1. **Mini Buried Treasure Sandwiches**:  
   - Mini slider rolls (whole wheat or white)  
   - Fillings: Cheese, ham, or turkey  
   - *Presentation*: Cut into 1" cubes, pack in mini treasure chest containers.  
2. **Jewel Fruit Skewers**:  
   - Grapes (no watermelon allergy), pineapple chunks, berries  
   - *Presentation*: Thread onto toothpicks; label as "Pirate Jewels."  
3. **Dipped "Gold" Veggies**:  
   - Baby carrots, cucumber slices, bell pepper strips  
   - *Presentation*: Pack in small bento boxes with goldfish crackers ("gold dust").  

---

### **Main Course (Themed as "Pirate Ship" Meals)**  
1. **Pirate Ship Chicken**:  
   - Mini grilled chicken breasts or tenders  
   - *Presentation*: Cut into "ship" shapes; pack in individual foil boats.  
2. **Captain’s Cheese Platter**:  
   - Mini cheddar squares, mozzarella balls ("buccaneer pearls")  
   - *Presentation*: Arrange on a "ship deck" cardboard base.  
3. **Mermaid’s Fish Tacos**:  
   - Flaked white fish (cod or tilapia) in lettuce cups  
   - *Presentation*: Pack in "treasure chest" containers with pirate-themed stickers.  

---

### **Sides (Sand-Safe & Themed)**  
1. **Treasure Chest Fries**:  
   - Mini sweet potato or regular fries  
   - *Presentation*: Pack in small cardboard "chest" containers.  
2. **Cannonball Corn**:  
   - Popcorn in mini cups, decorated with pirate stickers  
   - *Presentation*: Label as "cannonballs."  
3. **Pirate’s Punch**:  
   - Lemonade or fruit punch in juice boxes  
   - *Presentation*: Add glitter (food-safe), label "Sea Water."  

---

### **Allergy-Safe & Extras**  
- **Watermelon Alternative**: Use grapes, pineapple, or apple slices.  
- **Dessert**: Themed cupcakes (no watermelon filling) or pirate cookie cutters.  
- **Packaging**:  
  - Small bento boxes, snack bags, or cardboard "chest" containers.  
  - Label all items with pirate names (e.g., "Captain’s Snack," "First Mate Fruit").  

---

### **Shopping List Summary**  
| **Category**       | **Items**                                                                 |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Proteins**        | Mini slider rolls, chicken breasts, cheese, mozzarella, fish fillets      |
| **Fruits**          | Grapes, pineapple, berries, apples (cut into wedges)                     |
| **Veggies**         | Baby carrots, cucumber, bell peppers                                      |
| **Snacks**          | Goldfish crackers, popcorn, pretzel rods (themed as "ship masts")         |
| **Drinks**          | Juice boxes (lemonade/punch), water bottles                              |
| **Packaging**       | Mini treasure chest containers, bento boxes, snack bags, cardboard bases  |
| **Extras**          | Pirate stickers, glitter, labels, toothpicks                               |

---

### **Sand-Proofing Tips**  
1. **Double-Bag Everything**: Use snack bags inside containers.  
2. **No Crumbs**: Avoid dry crackers; opt for sealed dips or veggies.  
3. **Drinks**: Juice boxes > spill-prone bottles.  
4. **Theme as Defense**: Kids will eagerly eat "pirate jewels" or "treasure" to stay in character!  

This plan ensures **mess-free eating**, strong pirate immersion, and safety for allergies. Have a fantastic party! 🏴‍☠️🌊
