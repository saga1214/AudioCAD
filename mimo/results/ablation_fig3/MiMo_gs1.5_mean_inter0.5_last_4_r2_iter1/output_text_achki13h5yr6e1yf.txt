To break it down for Chicken Cutlets: two pounds of chicken breasts will cost between eight and fifteen dollars; all-purpose flour is three to five dollars; a dozen eggs are three to seven dollars; breadcrumbs are two to four dollars; and vegetable oil is four to eight dollars.
