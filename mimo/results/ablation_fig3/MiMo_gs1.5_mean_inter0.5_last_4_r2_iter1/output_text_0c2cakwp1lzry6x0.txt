Here's a specific guide to installing your items safely and effectively:

**1. TV Mounting:**
    *   **Measure Twice, Drill Once:** Precisely measure the wall location, TV size, and desired height (eye level is ~42-48 inches from floor). Consider the distance to your main seating area.
    *   **Know Your Wall:** Use a stud finder. Mounting directly into a stud provides the strongest hold. If no stud is available, use appropriate drywall anchors rated for your TV's weight.
    *   **Choose the Right Mount:** Select a mount rated for the *exact* weight of your TV (check specs!). Full-motion mounts offer flexibility, VESA wall mounts are universal, and simple tilting mounts are budget-friendly.
    *   **Use a Laser Level:** Essential for perfect vertical and horizontal alignment. Place the laser level on the wall, align the TV bracket's holes with the laser dot.
    *   **Secure the Mount:** Attach the wall plate securely to the wall using the correct screws/anchors. Attach the TV bracket to the plate. *Always unplug the TV before mounting or handling cables.*

**2. Hanging Planters:**
    *   **Weight is Key:** Check the *actual* weight of each planter (pot + soil + plant). Add 20-30% for water weight.
    *   **Choose the Right Hardware:** Use hooks or brackets rated for the *total* weight of the planter(s). For medium/heavy planters, use S-hooks or heavy-duty wall brackets. For lightweight ones, standard hooks work.
    *   **Anchor into Studs:** Whenever possible, drill into a wall stud. If hanging near a window, ensure the window frame is solid and rated for hanging weight.
    *   **Positioning:** Hang near windows for light but ensure they don't block airflow or access. Consider height for optimal light exposure and visibility.
    *   **Protect the Wall:** Place a small, thin plastic bag or tray under the planter base to catch any potential water drips.

**3. Floating Shelves:**
    *   **Measure & Mark:** Measure the exact spot on the wall. Use a pencil to mark the top and bottom edges. Use a level to ensure marks are perfectly horizontal.
    *   **Find Studs (Crucial):** Locate studs. Shelves should be mounted into studs whenever possible. If the shelf is long or heavy, you'll need multiple studs.
    *   **Shelf Support:** Use appropriate shelf brackets or cleats. For heavy items, use L-brackets or heavy-duty cleats. Ensure brackets have weight ratings exceeding the shelf's *combined* load.
    *   **Level is Essential:** Place the shelf on the wall and use a level to ensure it's perfectly level. Adjust slightly if needed.
    *   **Secure Brackets/Cleats:** Drive screws firmly into the marked stud locations. Ensure brackets/cleats are flush with the wall for a clean look.
    *   **Install Shelf:** Carefully place the shelf onto the brackets/cleats and secure it fully with screws. Double-check it's level and stable.

**General Safety & Tips:**

*   **Safety First:** Wear safety glasses and a dust mask when drilling. Use a stud finder with承重 capability.
*   **Plan Your Order:** Install shelves first, as they provide a base for hanging items like planters or smaller shelves. Mount the TV last, as it's often the largest/heaviest.
*   **Check Capacity:** Always double-check the weight limits of *all* hardware (mounts, brackets, hooks, anchors).
*   **Use Anchors:** If mounting into drywall without a stud, use appropriate wall anchors rated for the weight.
*   **Clean Up:** Use painter's tape to protect the wall. Have rags ready for dust. Remove furniture from the area if possible.
*   **Consider Landlord Rules:** While decorating, avoid noisy power tools late at night. Stick to the rules!
*   **Work Weekend:** Schedule this for a weekend when you have time and can dedicate focus.
