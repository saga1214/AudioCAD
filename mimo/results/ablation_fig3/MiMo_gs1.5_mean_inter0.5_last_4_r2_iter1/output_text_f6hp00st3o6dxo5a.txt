That's an interesting angle focusing on DNA, but it still leads back to the chicken-first conclusion.

Here’s why: The egg that contained the chicken's DNA was laid by an organism that was not quite a chicken. The mutation that defined it as a chicken only occurred after the egg was laid and the chick hatched from it. So, the egg itself wasn't a 'chicken egg' when it was laid; it only became one after the first true chicken emerged. The chicken was still first.
