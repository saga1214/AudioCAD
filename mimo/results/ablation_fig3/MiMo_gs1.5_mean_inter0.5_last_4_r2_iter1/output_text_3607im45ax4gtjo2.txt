Dealing with a difficult person—especially in a neighborhood—can be incredibly draining, but these strategies might help you manage the stress and protect your peace:

### 1. **Prioritize Your Well-being & Boundaries**
   - **Limit Exposure**: Minimize direct interactions if possible. A brief, polite acknowledgment is enough (e.g., "Good morning" or a nod).
   - **Set Clear Boundaries**: If she approaches, stay calm and say, "I understand you have concerns, but we’re handling this differently." Then disengage.
   - **Protect Your Kids**: If safety is a worry, discuss rules with them (e.g., "If she calls, we’ll handle it—we won’t argue with her").

### 2. **Document Everything**
   - Keep a detailed log of incidents: dates, times, what happened, and witnesses. This is crucial if authorities (police, CPS) need evidence later.

### 3. **Seek Support**
   - **Talk to Others**: Connect with neighbors who’ve experienced similar issues. A shared perspective can reduce isolation.
   - **Professional Help**: If stress feels overwhelming, therapy can help process emotions. Apps like BetterHelp or local clinics offer accessible options.

### 4. **Know Your Rights & Options**
   - **Legal Advice**: Consult a lawyer if CPS/police involvement escalates. They can advise on harassment laws or restraining orders.
   - **Community Resources**: Contact local non-profits (e.g., neighborhood watch groups) or city housing authorities if she’s violating ordinances.

### 5. **Focus on What You Can Control**
   - Redirect energy toward positive routines—like your kids’ school events or Halloween prep. Small joys (like listening to Forrest Frank) build resilience.
   - **Practice Stress Reduction**: Deep breathing, short walks, or mindfulness apps (like Headspace) can help reset during tense moments.

### 6. **Escalate Safely**
   - If threats or safety concerns arise, contact authorities immediately. CPS/police have protocols for handling such situations.

> 💡 **Key Insight**: You can’t control the neighbor’s behavior, but you *can* control how you respond. Consistency, documentation, and support create a shield against her actions.
