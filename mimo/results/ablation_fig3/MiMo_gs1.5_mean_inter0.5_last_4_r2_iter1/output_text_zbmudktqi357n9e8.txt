Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for watercolor inspiration! The autumn foliage there is just stunning. I'd definitely suggest going in the fall, from about September to November. That’s when you’ll see the most vibrant colors in the foliage, especially in the highlands or coastal areas. 

Also, the weather in September and October is pretty mild, which is ideal for painting outside. You might even find some waterfalls or lakes that would be great for practicing rivers and oceans. Just pack your paints, find a quiet spot with a view, and you’ll be good to go. Enjoy the trip!
