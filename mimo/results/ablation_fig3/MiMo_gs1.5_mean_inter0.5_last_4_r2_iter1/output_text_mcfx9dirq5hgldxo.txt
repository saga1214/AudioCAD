Since your layover is in Philadelphia, you should definitely try some local Philly classics.

First, you have to try a Philly Cheesesteak. You could go to a famous spot like Pat's or Geno's, or find a local place.

You should also try soft pretzels, maybe with some mustard or cheese dip. And of course, there's classic Philly Roast Pork, which is a tender, juicy pork sandwich on a long roll. 

If you're looking for something quicker, a Philly Sub is a great option, or maybe some classic Philly Pizza from a pizzeria. Enjoy your trip!
