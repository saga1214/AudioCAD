Okay, so you want to make sure your diesel camper van is safe and ready for a long road trip. That's smart. For the brakes, the most important thing is to get them inspected by a professional mechanic. They'll check for wear on the pads and rotors, and any other issues. If you're doing a DIY check, you can look for signs like a grinding noise when you brake, or if your brake fluid looks dark or smells burnt. If the van pulls to one side when you brake, that's a red flag. For spark plugs, it's really not something you'd want to tackle yourself, especially on an old diesel. They're under a lot of compression and the van is already old, so it's best to let a mechanic handle it. They'll replace them if they're worn out, which usually happens every 80,000 to 100,000 miles. Just make sure you get a thorough check before you hit the road.
