Okay, let's see what you'll need. For appliances, you'll definitely need an oven for the smoked turkey, the prime rib, mac and cheese, root veggies, and stuffing. A toaster oven is also needed for the asparagus. You'll use an Instant Pot for the cranberry sauce. And since you have double ovens, you could probably use one for the pumpkin cheesecake or the fruit tart if you decide to make them. You'll also need a stovetop for the wild rice salad, cranberry sauce, and fruit tart. As for the cooking methods, you'll be doing smoking, roasting, baking, stovetop sautéing, slow cooking, and making no-bake desserts. You'll also be assembling salads.
