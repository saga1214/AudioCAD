That's great to hear you're getting some relief. Cooking can be the perfect way to unwind. Here are a few simple, delicious dinner ideas you could whip up with ingredients you can grab at the store:

1.  **Easy Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta:** Sauté shrimp in a pan with minced garlic, butter, and a splash of white wine or lemon juice. Toss with cooked pasta (like linguine or fettuccine) and some fresh parsley. Simple, quick, and feels special.
2.  **One-Pan Chicken & Veggie Stir-Fry:** Cook chicken breast strips in a hot pan. Push the chicken aside, add sliced bell peppers, broccoli florets, and snap peas. Toss everything together with a simple sauce made from soy sauce, ginger, garlic, a touch of honey, and a little sesame oil. Serve over instant brown rice or noodles.
3.  **Loaded Baked Potatoes:** Bake a couple of russet potatoes. Split them open, fluff the insides, and top with things you love: maybe leftover chili, black beans, cheese, salsa, sour cream, and avocado. Super comforting and customizable.
4.  **Tuna or Chicken Salad Sandwiches:** Mix canned tuna (or chicken) with mayo, a little Dijon mustard, diced celery, red onion, salt, and pepper. Serve on bread with lettuce, tomato, and maybe some mustard. Pair with a simple side salad or crackers.
5.  **Simple Fajita Bowls:** Sauté sliced bell peppers and onions in a pan with taco seasoning. Add cooked chicken or shrimp. Serve over rice and top with salsa, sour cream, shredded cheese, and chopped cilantro. Very fresh and easy.
