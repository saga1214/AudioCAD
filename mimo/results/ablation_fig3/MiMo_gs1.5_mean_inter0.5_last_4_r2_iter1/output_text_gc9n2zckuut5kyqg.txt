Great point about budget constraints! Here are some **cost-effective, diabetes-friendly dessert ideas** that are still appealing to kids and align with your awareness goal:

### Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:
1. **Fresh Fruit Skewers/Cups**  
   - **Why it works:** Super cheap, healthy, and colorful.  
   - **Budget tip:** Use seasonal fruits (watermelon, berries, grapes, pineapple).  
   - **Kid appeal:** Offer small cups with yogurt dip on the side.

2. **Homemade Oatmeal Cookies**  
   - **Why it works:** Uses pantry staples (oats, flour, sugar).  
   - **Health twist:** Swap 1 cup sugar for applesauce or mashed banana.  
   - **Budget tip:** Make a big batch—kids love mini cookies!

3. **Frozen Yogurt Pops**  
   - **Why it works:** Yogurt is affordable, and kids love popsicles.  
   - **DIY:** Blend yogurt + berries, pour into molds, freeze.  
   - **Bonus:** Add a sprinkle of granola on top.

4. **DIY Rice Krispie Treats (Healthier Version)**  
   - **Why it works:** Cheap base ingredient (rice cereal).  
   - **Health twist:** Use low-sugar marshmallows or honey instead of syrup.  
   - **Fun factor:** Let kids decorate with melted chocolate drizzle.

5. **"Healthy" Chocolate-Dipped Fruit**  
   - **Why it works:** Chocolate is a kid favorite!  
   - **Budget version:** Use dark chocolate (70% cacao) + banana slices or strawberries.  
   - **Tip:** Pre-dip and freeze before serving to save time.

### Budget-Friendly Drink Ideas:
- **Sparkling Water + Fruit Slices** (lemons, oranges, berries).  
- **Sugar-Free Lemonade** (use stevia/monk fruit sweetener).  
- **Homemade Iced Tea** (infuse with mint or berries).  

### Pro Tips to Save Money:
1. **Ask for Donations:** Local bakeries, farmers' markets, or grocery stores might donate desserts or ingredients.  
2. **Bulk Batches:** Make large quantities of simple items (e.g., cookies, fruit cups).  
3. **DIY Decorations:** Use cookie cutters for fun shapes (no extra cost!).  
4. **Label Everything:** Clearly mark high-sugar items (e.g., "Limited Sugar" or "No Added Sugar") to reinforce awareness.
