Okay, let's make sure you don't set off that smoke alarm again! Here are a few key tips for perfect noodles.

First, use metal utensils. Definitely no more wood! A sturdy metal spoon or a plastic one is your best friend.

Second, don't overcrowd the pot. If your pot is too small, the water will get too hot and can start to boil over. Use a pot that's a bit bigger than you think you need.

Third, make sure the water is boiling hard before you add the noodles. Then, as soon as they're in, stir them right away. This prevents them from sticking together and gives you control.

Finally, drain the noodles right when they're done, and toss them with a little bit of oil right away. This stops them from getting mushy. You can add them to your stir-fry when it's almost finished.
