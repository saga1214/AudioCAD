That's the perfect combo—pickles add tang and crunch! Here are a few delicious, health-conscious ways to use them:

### 🥒 Quick-Pickled Vegetables (Customizable!)
- **How**: Thinly slice cucumbers, carrots, or radishes. Toss in a jar with:  
  - 1 cup **pickle juice** (or water + vinegar)  
  - 1 tbsp **maple syrup** or honey  
  - 1 tsp **mustard seeds**  
  - 1 garlic clove (sliced)  
  - 1/2 tsp **black peppercorns**  
  - Fresh dill or thyme.  
- **Steep 30 mins** in the fridge. Great for salads, sandwiches, or as a crunchy snack!

### 🔥 Pickle Juice Salmon Glaze
- **How**: Simmer 1/4 cup **pickle juice** + 1 tbsp **olive oil** + 1 tsp **honey** + minced garlic until slightly thickened. Brush over your salmon while grilling or baking for a tangy, salty-sweet finish!

### 🥤 Post-Run Recovery Drink
- **How**: Mix 2 oz **pickle juice** with water, a pinch of salt, and a splash of lemonade. The sodium and potassium help replenish electrolytes after your run!

### 🥗 Pickle & Herb Salad Topper
- **How**: Top your salmon or grain bowls with dill pickles and a sprinkle of fresh dill or mint. The acidity brightens rich foods like salmon.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: If using jarred pickles, check labels—many are high in sodium. Quick-pickling lets you control salt! For bloating concerns, try cucumber or radish pickles (lower sodium than dill pickles).
