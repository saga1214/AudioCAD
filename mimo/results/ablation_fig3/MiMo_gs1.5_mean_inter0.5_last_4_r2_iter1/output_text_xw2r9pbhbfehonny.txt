Alright, here's a simple checklist for your first YouTube recording.

Before filming, first, you need to get permission from the venue, especially since it’s an assisted living facility. Make sure they’re okay with you filming and posting it online.

Next, plan your setup. You can use your phone, but a tripod or a small stabilizer will make the video look smoother. If you can, try to film from a slight angle so people can see the puppets and the audience. You can also ask someone to film for you if you don’t want to multitask.

Then, just prepare like you normally would for a show. Do your routine, be natural, and try to make it engaging. Kids love energy, so just have fun with it.

After the show, you can edit the video. You can trim the best parts, add some simple text or sound effects if you want. Then just upload it to YouTube, label it as kid-friendly, and share it with friends or fans to spread the word.

So, just plan ahead, get permission, film, and post. You’ve got this.
