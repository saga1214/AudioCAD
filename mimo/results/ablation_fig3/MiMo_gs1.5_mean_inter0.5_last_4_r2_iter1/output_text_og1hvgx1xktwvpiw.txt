Okay, let me clarify this for you. The classic Boulevardier is actually the one with equal parts. So that's one ounce of bourbon, one ounce of sweet vermouth, and one ounce of Campari. The version with one and a quarter ounces of bourbon is actually a common variation, sometimes called a 'Boulevardier Negroni'. So your friend's recipe is a popular way to make it. Now, for the Last Word, that's a completely different drink. It's also equal parts: three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. You just shake all that up and strain it into a glass with ice. Hope that helps you figure it out at the store!
