Of course! Let's recap the best choices for St. Pete, Zone 10B. Herbs are your best bet, so grab a basil plant—it's easy and loves the sun. Mint and parsley are also fantastic and fast-growing. For leafy greens, lettuce and spinach are quick. Radishes are another great one because they grow super fast.

Then you've got your fruiting plants. Tomatoes and peppers are both very forgiving and will give you a ton of harvest. They just need a little support, so you might want to grab some tomato cages or stakes. 

These are all low-maintenance and will do great in your reversed climate. Happy gardening!
