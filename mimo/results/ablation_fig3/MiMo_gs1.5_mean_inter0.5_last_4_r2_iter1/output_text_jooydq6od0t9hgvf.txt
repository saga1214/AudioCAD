Here are quick, elegant drink ideas for your vodka mix and Argentine vin de vino:

### 1. **Vodka Mix (Vodka Spritzer)**  
**Recipe:**  
- Fill a glass with ice.  
- Add 2 oz vodka.  
- Top with 4 oz **sparkling water** (allergy-safe, no soda).  
- Add a splash of fresh lime juice.  
- **Garnish:** Thin cucumber slice + fresh mint sprig.  

**Why it works:** Light, refreshing, and visually appealing. The sparkling water keeps it fizzy without soda, while the cucumber/mint adds sophistication.

---

### 2. **Argentine-Style Vin de Vino (Malbec)**  
**Serving:**  
- Serve a **Malbec** (e.g., Catena or Pichincha) in proper wine glasses.  
- Pair with roasted chicken/fish or risotto.  
- **Bonus:** Offer a small cheese plate (brie, manchego) to savor the wine.  

**Why it works:** Malbec is rich, fruity, and deeply satisfying—perfect for a formal gathering. It’s universally loved and avoids soda entirely.
