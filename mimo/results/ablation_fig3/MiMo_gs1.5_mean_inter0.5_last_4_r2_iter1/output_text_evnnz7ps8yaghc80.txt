That's a smart plan. A structured schedule will definitely help. Here’s a balanced weekly plan that mixes different equipment and focuses on consistency.

I suggest practicing for about 15 to 25 minutes each day. Here’s a sample schedule you can follow:

On Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday, you could start with 5 minutes of one-handed two-ball juggling with beanbags to warm up. Then, spend about 10 minutes practicing the transition to three balls. After that, try 5 minutes with scarves to work on slow, deliberate throws and catches.

Thursday and Friday would be good for using tennis balls. Spend about 10 minutes juggling three tennis balls with beanbags and focus on your catches. Then, try 5 minutes with scarves, but move a bit faster.

On Saturday, you could do a short 10-minute session with scarves, practicing a specific pattern. Then, spend about 10 minutes juggling with beanbags, and really focus on your rhythm.

Finally, on Sunday, just take a rest day. No juggling at all. It's really important to let your body recover and keep your mind fresh.

The key is to be consistent and patient with yourself.
