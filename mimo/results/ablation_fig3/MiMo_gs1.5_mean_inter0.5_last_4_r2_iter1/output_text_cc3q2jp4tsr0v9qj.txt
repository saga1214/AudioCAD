That's a great first step! Now that your house is documented, it's time to unpack strategically.

I'd suggest starting with the essentials first. Things like toilet paper, soap, towels, and a shower curtain are probably top of your list. Make sure your kitchen has enough for a basic meal, and get your beds put together and in the rooms.

Also, make sure you direct the movers so the big furniture, like the sofa and the dining table, ends up in the correct rooms. This will save you a lot of work later on.

And remember, plan on getting takeout for dinner. You've earned it.
