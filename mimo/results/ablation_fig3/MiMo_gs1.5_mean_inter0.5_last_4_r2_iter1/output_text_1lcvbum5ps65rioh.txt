### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia:  
In BC, **U-turns are legal** in most areas, but key restrictions apply:  
1. **No signals/control lights**: You can’t turn around at intersections with traffic signals unless a sign explicitly allows it.  
2. **Visibility**: You must have a clear 150-meter view in both directions.  
3. **Prohibited zones**: Avoid U-turns on curves, hills, near driveways, or where they obstruct traffic.  
4. **No crosswalks**: Never U-turn when pedestrians are crossing.  
**Penalties**: Illegal U-turns can result in a **$150 fine** and **2 demerit points** on your license.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients:  
For **4 servings**, you’ll need:  
| **Ingredient**         | **Quantity**          | **Notes**                                  |  
|-------------------------|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------|  
| Spaghetti               | 400g (14 oz)          | Dry pasta                              |  
| Guanciale (or pancetta)| 200g (7 oz), diced    | *Guanciale* is cured pork cheek (best)    |  
| Eggs                    | 3 large               | Use room-temperature eggs                 |  
| Parmigiano Reggiano     | 100g (1/2 cup), grated| Freshly grated is ideal                    |  
| Black pepper            | 1-2 tsp               | Freshly cracked                           |  
| Salt                    | To taste              | For pasta water                           |  

**Optional**: Red pepper flakes for heat.  

---

### Quick Recipe Steps:  
1. **Cook pasta** in salted boiling water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
2. **Sauté guanciale** in a pan until crispy. Remove from heat.  
3. **Whisk eggs**, cheese, pepper, and 1 tbsp pasta water in a bowl.  
4. **Combine**: Toss hot pasta with guanciale. Remove from heat.  
5. **Mix eggs in**: Slowly pour the egg mixture over pasta while stirring vigorously. Add reserved pasta water as needed for creaminess. Serve immediately!  

**Tip**: Add pasta water gradually to create a silky sauce without scrambling the eggs. Enjoy your meal! 🍝
