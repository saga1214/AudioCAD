That pigeon puzzle is a great way to think about organizing things! Here’s a plan that could help you balance your ambitious dreams with your financial goals.

First, create a single savings list. You could have columns for the goal, the target amount, and the timeline. For Bolivia, maybe the down payment, and for the Faroe Islands.

Next, break each goal down into small, manageable monthly contributions. Even a small amount adds up over time. Then, set a monthly reminder to review your progress.

Finally, try to integrate these goals into your daily life. For instance, while you're hiking, you could listen to a podcast about Bolivia. Or while you're working, you could add a small amount to your savings app.

This way, your big dreams and financial goals can coexist, just like the pigeons on different rooftops!
