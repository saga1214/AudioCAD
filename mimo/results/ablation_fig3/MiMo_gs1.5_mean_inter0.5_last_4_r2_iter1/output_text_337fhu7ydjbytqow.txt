### **20-Mile Day Segment Packing List**  
*(Focus: Layering, breathability, aid station access)*  
1. **Base Layer**:  
   - Moisture-wicking top & shorts (avoid cotton).  
   - Lightweight leggings/tights (if needed).  
2. **Mid-Layer**:  
   - Fleece or light synthetic jacket (adjustable for warmth).  
3. **Outer Layer**:  
   - Windproof/water-resistant jacket (packable for compactness).  
4. **Hands/Head**:  
   - Gloves (lightweight for warmth/sun).  
   - Sun hat or cap (for daytime).  
   - Buff/gaiter (for face protection or extra warmth).  
5. **Footwear**:  
   - Trail shoes with grip (sturdy but breathable).  
6. **Accessories**:  
   - Small running vest/bag (to carry layers as needed).  
   - Headlamp (handheld, with extra batteries).  
   - Refillable water bottle (use aid stations).  
   - Personal items (ID, phone, cash).  

---

### **12-Mile Night Segment Packing List**  
*(Focus: Insulation, visibility, warmth)*  
1. **Base Layer**:  
   - Moisture-wicking top & shorts (same as day).  
2. **Mid-Layer**:  
   - **Skip or replace** – Insulation is critical here.  
3. **Outer Layer**:  
   - **Light insulated jacket** (e.g., 50-70°F rated) or fleece.  
4. **Hands/Head**:  
   - **Warm gloves** (insulated).  
   - **Warm hat** (fleece-lined).  
   - Buff/gaiter (for neck/warmth).  
5. **Footwear**:  
   - Same trail shoes (add **wool socks** if not already wearing).  
6. **Accessories**:  
   - **Bright reflective vest** (for safety).  
   - **Strong headlamp** (100+ lumens) + **extra batteries**.  
   - Zip-off arm/leg covers (if available).  
   - **Extra warm layer** (e.g., puffer vest) – *store in pack until needed*.  
   - **Personal items** (phone, ID, cash).  

---

### **Key Tips**  
- **Transition Strategy**: Pack the night layer in your vest/bag during the day segment. Remove gloves/hat as you warm up, add them back when cooling.  
- **Visibility**: Reflective gear is non-negotiable for nighttime.  
- **Light**: Test your headlamp *before* race day.  
- **Aid Stations**: Use them for hydration/snacks – don’t carry extra weight if not needed.
