I understand your confusion. You're right that 450 milligrams is only 20 percent of the general daily recommendation. 

However, the issue is that your entire meal is just one single portion. In the context of a single meal, especially if it's your only meal for the day, that amount of sodium can contribute to feeling bloated or water retention, which is why I'd consider it a lot for one sitting. 

If you spread that sodium intake out over multiple meals throughout the day, it would be much more manageable.
