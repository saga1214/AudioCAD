### 5 Ocean-Themed Meals
1. **Spicy Mango & Coconut Shrimp Stir-Fry (Tofu Option)**

   *Why it works:* Uses mango, spicy, low-carb option, and avoids shellfish if needed.

2. **Miso-Glazed Salmon with Mango Salsa**  
   *Grilled or baked salmon topped with a sweet-savory miso glaze, paired with fresh mango-pineapple salsa.*  
   *Why it works:* High-protein, low-glycemic, and leverages your shared love for mango.

3. **Tropical Tofu Tacos with Mango-Avocado Slaw**  
   *Crispy baked tofu in corn tortillas with spicy avocado-lime slaw, pickled veggies, and mango salsa.*  
   *Why it works:* Spicy, customizable, low-carb, and uses mango.

4. **Lemon-Herb Grilled Shrimp or Tofu Skewers with Mango Salsa**  
   *Marinated skewers with herbs, lemon, and chili, served with a fresh mango-avocado relish.*  
   *Why it works:* Light, spicy, and easy to share. *Skip shrimp for shellfish-free.*

5. **Seafood Paella (Shellfish-Free)**  
   *Paella-style rice with mussels/clams, chorizo (optional), and a vibrant tomato-mango broth.*  
   *Why it works:* Classic ocean vibe, uses mango for sweetness, and chorizo adds spice.

### 5 Ocean-Themed Desserts
1. **Mango Sorbet with Lime Zest & Sea Salt**  
   *Refreshing, naturally sweet, and light—perfect for a tropical finish.*

2. **Coconut-Chia Pudding Parfaits**  
   *Layer chia pudding (coconut milk base) with mango puree and toasted coconut flakes. Add a drizzle of dark chocolate for indulgence.*

3. **Dark Chocolate "Sea Salt" Tart with Pineapple**  
   *A rich dark chocolate crust filled with caramel or cream, topped with pineapple chunks and sea salt.*

4. **Spiced Apple-Cranberry "Crumble" with Sea Salt**  
   *Warm spiced apples and cranberries baked with oats, served with coconut whipped cream and a sprinkle of sea salt.*

5. **Pineapple Upside-Down Cake with Sea Salt**  
   *Traditional cake, but use reduced-sugar pineapple and a pinch of sea salt for a gourmet twist.*

### Key Considerations:
- **Allergies:** For shellfish, use tofu, mushrooms, or extra shellfish-free seafood (like mussels/clams).  
- **Diabetes:** Focus on portion control, lean proteins, and low-glycemic carbs (tofu, cauliflower rice, vegetables).  
- **Spice Level:** Adjust chili levels based on your preferences—add extra heat or keep it mild.  
- **Theme Touches:** Garnish with edible seaweed, starfish-shaped desserts, or ocean-blue accents!
