Absolutely! Here’s a practical, mess-minimizing shopping list based on your group size (10 dads + 10 kids) and theme park goals. I’ve prioritized grab-and-go portions, minimal prep, and accommodating both calzone lovers and sugar-free sodas:

### 🥤 **Drinks** (Pre-Cool with Ice Packs!)
- **Water**: 20 large water bottles (1L each)  
- **Sugar-Free Sodas**: 10 cans each of your favorite flavors (e.g., root beer, cream soda, cola)  
- **Juice Boxes**: 15 boxes (6oz each, e.g., apple, orange)  
- **Milk**: 10 cartons (low-fat)  
- **Electrolyte Drinks**: 10 boxes (for dads post-excitement!)  
- *Extras*: Ice packs, reusable water bottles (if needed)

### 🥪 **Snacks** (Pre-Packaged Individually)
- **Cheese Sticks**: 10 packages (2 per pack)  
- **Grapes**: 3 lbs (pre-washed)  
- **Pretzels**: 2 large bags (mini or bite-sized)  
- **Trail Mix**: 2 bags (no chocolate for heat sensitivity)  
- **Baby Carrots**: 3 lbs (with 10 single-serve hummus cups)  
- **Apples**: 10 (pre-sliced with 10 lemon slices to prevent browning)  
- **Bananas**: 10 (easy-peel)  
- **Crackers**: 10 boxes (Ritz or saltines)  
- **Nuts**: 2 bags (almonds or trail mix)  

### 🍕 **Main Meals** (Pre-Portioned & Portable)
- **Calzones**: 15 from a local pizzeria (order 5-6 different fillings: pepperoni, cheese, veggie, etc.).  
  *Tip: Ask for individual boxes with napkins/utensils.*  
- **Sandwiches/Wraps**: 10 pre-made wraps (turkey/cheese, veggie, PB&J) if calzones aren’t feasible.  
- **Extras**: 10 small containers of chips (if calzones aren’t enough carbs!).

### 🍰 **Desserts** (Grab-and-Go Friendly)
- **Cupcakes**: 15 (cake or cookies—ask for no sprinkles to avoid mess)  
- **Brownie Cups**: 15 (pre-packaged from bakery)  
- **Cookies**: 10 (Oreos or granola bars for quick bites)  
- *Optional*: 5 mini chocolate bars for a "surprise" treat!

### 🧺 **Packaging & Extras**
- **Coolers**: 2 large insulated ones with ice packs  
- **Utensils**: 10 sets of disposable forks/spoons + 10 napkins  
- **Wipes**: 1 large pack of wet wipes (for spills!)  
- **Trash Bags**: 2 heavy-duty bags  
- **Lids/Seals**: For calzones/sandwiches to prevent spills  

### 💡 **Key Tips**
1. **Label Containers**: Write "DRINKS," "SNACKS," "MAIN," "DESSERT" on coolers.  
2. **Calzone Hack**: If ordering calzones, ask for *plain cheese* as a backup option—some kids might not like the fillings.  
3. **Hydration Station**: Set up a cooler with water/sodas first so drinks stay cold longer.  
4. **Dietary Note**: If any allergies exist (e.g., nut-free), swap trail mix for sunflower seeds.  

This plan keeps things fresh, organized, and minimizes cleanup while ensuring everyone gets their favorite foods! Have an epic bachelor party! 🎢🎉
