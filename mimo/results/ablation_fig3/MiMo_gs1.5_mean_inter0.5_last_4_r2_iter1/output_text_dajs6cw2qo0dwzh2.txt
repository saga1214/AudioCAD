That's an insightful point, and it gets to the heart of what really drove the Age of Discovery. The main drivers were definitely the technological innovations in shipbuilding and navigation, plus the intense rivalry between European states.

The fall of Constantinople was a huge catalyst. It didn't start the process, but it massively accelerated it and gave a huge competitive advantage to Portugal, who had the lead on the African route. It also created a sense of urgency and glory that fueled the whole era. So while the process was already underway, that event was a major catalyst and accelerator.
