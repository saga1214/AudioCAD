Here are five great road trip options from Vancouver that are perfect for a new family with a baby and a puppy.

First, for a classic coastal drive, you could head to Tofino on Vancouver Island. You can take the scenic route along the Pacific Rim Highway, stopping at spots like the Cape Perpetua tide pools. You'll love the stunning beaches and the fresh seafood at the town's restaurants.

Second, for some mountains, you could drive to Whistler. It's a great place to explore, with activities like the Peak 2 Peak Gondola or the Blackcomb Glacier. You can find a lovely dinner at a restaurant like Revel or Chateau Luxxe.

Third, for a bit of history, you could drive to Victoria and the Okanagan Valley. You could visit the Butchart Gardens, take a winery tour, or swim in the Okanagan Lake.

Fourth, for a more rugged adventure, you could head to Yoho National Park. You can hike the Emerald Lake Trail and visit the famous canyon at Takakkaw Falls.

Finally, for a quieter trip, you could drive to the Fraser Valley. There you can visit the Lilac Farm winery, see the heritage homes at Harrison Hot Springs, and enjoy a nice meal at a restaurant like The Farmhouse Kitchen.

All of these destinations have great dining and plenty of beautiful things to see!
