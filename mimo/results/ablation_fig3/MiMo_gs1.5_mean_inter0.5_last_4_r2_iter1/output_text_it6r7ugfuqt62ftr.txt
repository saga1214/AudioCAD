Okay, I've got a detailed plan for your Northern California family vacation. Since you're escaping the cold and want warm weather where swimming is possible, and you're diet-conscious, I've focused on that. I suggest a trip to the Central Coast, specifically Morro Bay and Pismo Beach. It's a perfect fit—it's consistently warm, great for swimming, and has a relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere.

For your accommodation, I'd recommend the Pismo Beach Inn. It's a lovely beachfront motel, which means you'll have easy access to the beach, and it's conveniently located near restaurants and shops.

Now, for your itinerary, here’s a suggested four-day plan.

On day one, you'll arrive and settle in. You can check into your hotel and just relax. For dinner, I'd suggest The Sea Shack in Morro Bay. It's a casual spot with fresh seafood, and they're known for being able to accommodate gluten-free diets.

On day two, you can spend the morning exploring Morro Bay. You could take an eco-tour on a kayak or a stand-up paddleboard to paddle around Morro Rock. It's a fantastic way to see marine life. In the afternoon, you could take a walk on the Morro Bay State Park beach or visit the Morro Bay Lighthouse. For lunch, The Fisherman's Wharf has a nice café with a variety of options, including gluten-free.

For your third day, you can head over to Pismo Beach. In the morning, you could rent bikes and cycle along the Pismo Dunes State Park. It's a beautiful, wide beach perfect for biking. In the afternoon, you can take a walk on the Pismo Beach Pier and maybe stop at Trish's Mini Donuts again, as they have that great gluten-free version. For dinner, I'd suggest The Pismo Beach Pier Restaurant. It’s right on the pier, has a lovely view, and they can definitely accommodate gluten-free requests.

On your fourth day, you can take some time for more beach activities or just relaxing before heading home. For a final meal, you could go back to Fog Harbor Fish House in Fisherman's Wharf, the place we discussed for her birthday, since it's in your travel path.

As for getting around, renting a large SUV or minivan is the best option for your family. You can easily rent one at an airport like San Luis Obispo or Santa Maria. Driving times are pretty short, maybe an hour and a half to two hours each way from your hotel.

For packing, remember your bathing suits, a good cover-up, sunscreen, sunglasses, hats, and of course, any necessary medical supplies for your daughter's Celiac disease. A light jacket is a good idea for cooler evenings, but you won't need anything heavy.

This trip offers that warm weather, plenty of swimming, and a mix of adventure and relaxation, all while being very mindful of your daughter's dietary needs. I hope you have a wonderful family getaway!
