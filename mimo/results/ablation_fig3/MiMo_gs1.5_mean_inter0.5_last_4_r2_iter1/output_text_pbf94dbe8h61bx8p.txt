Okay, you want simple, meaningful, and age-appropriate gift ideas that involve your kids without being too much work. I've got a few more ideas that fit what you're looking for.

One is paper plate animals. The kids can paint or cut out shapes from paper plates, and then you can add googly eyes or draw faces. It's simple, customizable, and works for any age. 

Another idea is a 'gratitude jar.' The kids can write or draw things they're thankful for on little pieces of paper, and you can roll them up to put in a jar. They can add to it all year, and then give it to family or friends as a gift. It's meaningful and not tied to any religion. 

For the pine cone bird feeders, if the peanut butter worries you, you could use honey instead. Or, you could skip the sticky part and just roll the pine cone directly in the birdseed, maybe tying it with a ribbon. 

These ideas are all easy, not too messy, and let your kids get creative while making something thoughtful. Would one of these work for you?
