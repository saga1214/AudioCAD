For a 'Retro Tech Day' lunch, you could serve a “Mainframe Sandwich.” This would be a hearty sandwich with turkey, ham, and Swiss cheese to look like a computer motherboard, served with a side of “Dial-Up Chips,” which are just pretzel rods with M&M's. Another idea is a “Floppy Disk Bowl,” where you put a salad in a clear container to look like a floppy disk, and serve it with some “RAM Sticks,” which are just celery sticks with peanut butter. Both are easy to theme and easy to eat.
