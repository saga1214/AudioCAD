Okay, it sounds like you're ready to prime! Before you start, just a few last checks. Make sure the sanding dust is completely gone and the cabinet is totally dry. Then, lightly wipe it down with a tack cloth or a damp rag to get any remaining grit off. This is really important to prevent the primer from getting all bubbly.

Also, just double-check that your primer is actually for painting over dark colors. A tinted primer, maybe a little grayish for the black-to-white transition, would work best. Once it's all prepped, you can apply that primer and then your off-white paint. Good luck with both the project and your neighbor!
