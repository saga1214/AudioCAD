Absolutely, there are several excellent ways to explore that history firsthand.

The most direct way is to visit the Lockport Canal and Erie Canal Museum. It's a great spot to learn all about the locks and their military significance. You could also check out the Lockport Historical Society's archives for more detailed documents and photos. And of course, the city's own website, lockportny.gov, has a wealth of information on the canal's history and the city's role in it. It's a really rich history worth exploring!
