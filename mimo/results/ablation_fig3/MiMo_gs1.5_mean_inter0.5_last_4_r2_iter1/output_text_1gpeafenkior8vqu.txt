Here's a **detailed 8-day itinerary** balancing Tokyo exploration, Osaka highlights, and day trips to Kyoto and Nara, while accommodating your holiday pace and coursework deadlines:

### **Tokyo (4 Days)**
**Day 1: Arrival & Asakusa**  
- Arrive at Narita/Haneda Airport  
- Check into hotel (e.g., Shinjuku/Skytree area for transit)  
- Evening: **Senso-ji Temple** (Tokyo's oldest temple), Nakamise Street snacks  

**Day 2: Modern Tokyo & Culture**  
- Morning: **Meiji Shrine** (Harajuku), **Yanaka Ginza** (old Tokyo vibe)  
- Afternoon: **Shibuya** (Scramble Crossing, Hachiko Statue)  
- Evening: **Shibuya Sky** sunset views  

**Day 3: Imperial & Financial Districts**  
- Morning: **Imperial Palace East Gardens** (free), **Tokyo Station** (architecture)  
- Afternoon: **Akihabara** (tech/anime culture), **Ueno Park** (museums)  
- Evening: **Roppongi** (nightlife/views)  

**Day 4: Day Trip to Nikko or Kamakura**  
- *Option 1 (History):* **Nikko** (Toshogu Shrine, UNESCO site)  
- *Option 2 (Beaches/Temples):* **Kamakura** (Great Buddha, bamboo forest)  
- Return to Tokyo for dinner  

---

### **Osaka (3 Days + Day Trips)**
**Day 5: Osaka Castle & Dotonbori**  
- Morning: **Osaka Castle** (museum inside), Nakanoshima Park  
- Afternoon: **Dotonbori** (street food, neon signs), **Shinsekai** (retro district)  
- Evening: **Kuromon Market** for dinner  

**Day 6: Kyoto Day Trip (Full Day)**  
- **Shinkansen** to Kyoto (45 mins)  
- Morning: **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion), **Ryoan-ji** (Zen rock garden)  
- Afternoon: **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (early visit to avoid crowds)  
- Evening: Return to Osaka  

**Day 7: Nara Day Trip (Full Day)**  
- **Shinkansen** to Nara (30 mins)  
- Morning: **Todai-ji Temple** (Great Buddha), Nara Park (deer)  
- Afternoon: **Kasuga Shrine**, return to Osaka  

**Day 8: Departure**  
- Morning: **Umeda Sky Building** ( Osaka's skyline), **Osaka Aquarium**  
- Check out, fly home
