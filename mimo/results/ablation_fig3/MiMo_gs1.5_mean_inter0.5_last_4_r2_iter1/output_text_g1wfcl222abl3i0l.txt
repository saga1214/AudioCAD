That frustration is totally understandable—glue guns can be the ultimate party poopers! Here's what to try:

### 🔧 Quick Fixes for Your Glue Gun:
1. **Check the Glue Stick**:  
   → Make sure you're using **hot glue sticks** (not cold glue or craft glue). They need to be *specifically* rated for hot glue guns.  
   → If the stick is old/dried out, it might not activate. Try a new one.

2. **Nozzle Maintenance**:  
   → Wipe the nozzle with a damp cloth to remove dust/clogs.  
   → If glue is stuck inside, heat it gently with a hairdryer for 30 seconds to liquefy it.

3. **Temperature Check**:  
   → Most glue guns work best at **185–200°F (85–93°C)**. If yours is too cool, the glue won’t melt properly.  
   → Use a thermometer if possible! If it’s too hot, reduce the setting.

4. **Test on Scrap Paper**:  
   → Stick a glue dot onto scrap paper. If it melts smoothly and holds, it’s working. If not, recalibrate the gun.

### 🌟 If the Glue Gun Still Fails:
- **Alternative Adhesives**:  
  Try **strong craft glue** (like E6000) for delicate elements (masks, lace) and **double-sided tape** for flowers/moss.  
- **Epoxy Putty**:  
  For heavier items (cobwebs/spiders), use a black or dark-colored epoxy putty. It dries hard and spooky!
