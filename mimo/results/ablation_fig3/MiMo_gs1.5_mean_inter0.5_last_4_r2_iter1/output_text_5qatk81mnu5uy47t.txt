That sounds like a fantastic goal! Costa Rica is an **excellent choice** for scuba divers, offering warm tropical waters, diverse marine life, and unique dive sites. Here’s a plan to make it unforgettable:

### 🌴 **Costa Rica Dive Trip Itinerary (5-7 Days)**
1. **Base: Puntarenas or Tamarindo**  
   - **Why?** Closest point to the Pacific and Caribbean coasts. Puntarenas offers boat-based dives, while Tamarindo has convenient shore access and a lively vibe.  
   - **Best Time:** December–April (dry season, warmest waters).  

2. **Key Dive Sites:**  
   - **Cocos Island (Caribbean side):**  
     - **Highlights:** Whale sharks (May–Oct), manta rays, sea turtles, and shipwrecks.  
     - **Access:** Fly to Puerto Viejo (1-hour flight from San José) or take a boat (4–5 hours).  
   - **Caño Island (Pacific side):**  
     - **Highlights:** Snorkeling with dolphins, sea lions, and vibrant coral gardens (accessible by boat from Sirena Beach, Guanacaste).  
   - **Tortuguero (Pacific):**  
     - **Highlights:** Mangrove forests, sea turtles (nesting season: June–August), and easy shore diving.  
   - **Tamarindo (Pacific):**  
     - **Highlights:** Easy shore dives near reefs and wreck sites; great for beginners.  

3. **Gear Recommendation:**  
   - **Fins:** Start with **short fins** for maneuverability and travel ease. Rent long fins if you plan deeper dives.  
   - **Other:** Rent a dive computer and mask/fins if you’re new to the region.  

4. **Unique Experiences:**  
   - **Night Dives:** Spot bioluminescent plankton or nocturnal creatures.  
   - **Wildlife:** Kayak with dolphins, snorkel with sea turtles, or spot monkeys in Costa Rica’s rainforests.  
   - **Food:** Try *casado* (Costa Rican lunch) or fresh seafood by the beach.  

5. **Logistics:**  
   - **Dive Shops:** Look for PADI/SSI-certified shops in Puntarenas (e.g., **Dive Puntarenas**) or Tamarindo (e.g., **Tamarindo Divers**).  
   - **Accommodation:** Stay near the coast (e.g., **Hotel Tamarindo** or **El Pescador Lodge** in Puntarenas).  
   - **Flights:** Fly into San José (SJO), then rent a car or take shuttles to coastal bases.  

### 💡 **Pro Tips**  
- **Certification:** If you’re certified, book multi-day courses (e.g., Advanced Open Water).  
- **Wildlife:** Visit Tortuguero National Park for sea turtle conservation tours.  
- **Budget:** ~$1,500–$2,500 per person (flights not included) for 5 days.  

### 🌟 **Why This Works**  
Costa Rica blends adventure, wildlife, and culture seamlessly. From mantas at Cocos Island to kayaking with dolphins, you’ll experience the *magical realism* of the jungle meeting the ocean—a dream trip for scuba lovers!
