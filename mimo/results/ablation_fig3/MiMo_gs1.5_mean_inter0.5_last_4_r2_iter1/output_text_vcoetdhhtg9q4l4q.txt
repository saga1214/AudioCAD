That's a great approach! Here are some versatile, time-saving ingredients that work for quick dinners and minimize clutter:

### 🥬 Pantry Staples (Minimal Storage)
1. **Rice or pasta** – Cooks in minutes, freezes well, and pairs with almost any protein/veg.  
2. **Canned beans** (black, chickpeas, lentils) – Ready in seconds, high in protein, and shelf-stable.  
3. **Canned tomatoes** or **tomato paste** – For quick sauces, soups, or stews.  
4. **Oats** or **quinoa** – Nutrient-packed, cooks in 15 mins, and great for bowls.  

### 🥕 Produce (Focus on Storage)
1. **Carrots, onions, garlic** – Cut ahead or freeze for later use.  
2. **Spinach or kale** – Wilted in minutes; store in freezer bags.  
3. **Cabbage or bell peppers** – Lasts weeks; slice and freeze for stir-fries.  
4. **Frozen veggies** (broccoli, peas, corn) – Skip washing/chopping; add directly to meals.  

### 🍳 Proteins (Quick to Prep)
1. **Eggs** – Scrambled, fried, or boiled in <5 mins.  
2. **Canned tuna/salmon** – Mix with mayo, lemon, and veggies for a salad.  
3. **Frozen shrimp** – Sear in 3 mins for tacos or pasta.  
4. **Pre-cooked tofu/tempeh** – Pan-fry or bake for stir-fries.  

### 🧂 Flavor Boosters (No Extra Bowls)
- **Soy sauce, olive oil, lemon juice** – For dressings, marinades, or finishing touches.  
- **Pre-ground spices** (cumin, paprika, chili flakes) – Store in small jars.  
- **Nutritional yeast** – Adds cheesy flavor to plant-based meals.  

### 💡 Minimalist Meal Ideas
- **Stir-fry**: Frozen veggies + canned tofu + soy sauce over rice.  
- **Soup**: Canned beans + frozen spinach + broth + pre-chopped onions.  
- **Breakfast-for-dinner**: Scrambled eggs + frozen peas + canned tomatoes.  

**Pro Tip**: Prep a "clutter-free base" on weekends—cook grains, roast veggies, or hard-boil eggs. Store portions in clear containers for grab-and-go meals. This cuts down on daily decisions and cleanup!
