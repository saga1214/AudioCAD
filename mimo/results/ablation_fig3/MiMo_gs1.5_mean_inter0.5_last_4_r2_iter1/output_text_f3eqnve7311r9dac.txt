That's a great instinct. Older comics are generally much rarer and more collectible. Here are ten iconic books from the 1940s and 50s that are great investments.

First, *Captain America Comics* number one, the first appearance of Captain America. Then *Tales of Suspense* number thirty-nine, which is Captain America's first team-up with Thor. For a classic horror book, *Tales from the Crypt* number one, the first issue, is a great choice. *The Spectre* number one is also highly collectible. For a great horror key, *Weird Fantasy* number one is a must. Then there's *The Silver Surfer* number one, which is the first appearance of the character. *Fantastic Four* number one is a cornerstone of Marvel history. *The Amazing Spider-Man* number one is, of course, the most famous first issue of them all. *Conan the Barbarian* number one is a major classic from Marvel. And finally, *The Incredible Hulk* number one, the first appearance of the Hulk. You can find copies of these in the mid-grade range, which fits your budget. Just remember, condition is everything for these old books.
