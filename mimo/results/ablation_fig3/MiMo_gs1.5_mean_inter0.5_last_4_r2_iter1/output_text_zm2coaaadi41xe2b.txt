Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! For ramen, you could try Tonkatsu House for their thick-cut pork or Tokyo Ramen Street for their tonkotsu. If you want pho, Pho 79 is a classic. For something more casual, Noodle House has amazing xiao long bao and hand-pulled noodles. 

For protein bars, it really depends on what you need. If you want something high in protein and low in sugar, look at Quest or Optimum Nutrition. If you prefer a more natural, whole-food option, Clif Builders Bars are decent. But if you want something with a good balance of taste and macros, RXBAR is a good choice. 

Enjoy your meal and snacks!
