You're right to think about the weight. Succulents are very lightweight. A small pot of soil and the plant itself will probably weigh less than five pounds. You could easily carry that up five flights of stairs.

The bird's nest snake plant is a bit sturdier, but it's also not very heavy. A small plant in a small pot, which is exactly what you'd want for your balcony space, will likely weigh less than ten pounds. It's definitely manageable to carry up five flights of stairs, especially if you use a small bag or tote to hold it. Since you're only renting, you could buy smaller versions of both that would be very easy to move.
