That's a fantastic skill to have. Yes, absolutely, you can submit an entire package with your own illustrations. This is often called a 'slush pile' submission, where you send the complete manuscript and art together to a publisher.

However, there are a few very important things to keep in mind. First, publishers are always looking for a fresh, unique voice. So your art style and your story need to be strong and stand out. Second, the quality of your artwork has to be professional-level. Publishers expect a very high standard. And third, it's a good idea to check their submission guidelines first, as some publishers might prefer to work with a writer-illustrator pair rather than accepting a full package.

If you do decide to go that route, you can submit the package directly to the children's book department of a publisher, or you can find a children's book agent who specializes in writer-illustrators and submit it to them.
