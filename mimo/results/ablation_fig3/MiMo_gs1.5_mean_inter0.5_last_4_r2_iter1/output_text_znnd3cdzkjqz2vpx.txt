You've got the essentials, but I'd suggest a few more things to make the job go smoother and safer. You'll definitely want a measuring tape to double-check your levels and measurements, and a pencil for marking. You'll also need a drill and some wall anchors, as Habitat restore cabinets usually come with those. Oh, and don't forget safety glasses and work gloves! You'll need them to protect your eyes from dust and your hands from splinters. You could also bring a small trash bag for any old hardware you find or for shavings. That's a great start, though! You're all set for the main event.
