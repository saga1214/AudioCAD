Okay, so you're feeling anxious but also really want to travel. That’s a great idea—new places can be a huge boost. Let’s brainstorm some destinations that might feel inspiring and manageable for you.

First, Japan is a fantastic choice. It’s safe, beautiful, and full of literary history. You could visit Kyoto for its temples and tea houses, or Tokyo for its modern vibe. Staying in a traditional ryokan could be a fun and immersive experience. You can get there by flying on a major airline, and the transit is usually smooth.

Another idea is Italy. You mentioned practicing Italian there before, so you could return to places you know or explore new regions. Venice is magical, and Sicily is full of history. You can stay in a cozy bed and breakfast or a hostel. Flights are common, and trains make travel easy and scenic.

If you want something more adventurous, Costa Rica is perfect. It’s got nature, wildlife, and eco-friendly accommodations. You can zip-line through rainforests, relax on beaches, or hike volcanoes. Getting there involves a flight, and the country is very tourist-friendly.

For a quieter escape, Iceland is stunning. You can see waterfalls, glaciers, and even the Northern Lights. Staying in a guesthouse or a cozy cabin could be great. Flights connect well from the US, and tours are available if you’d prefer a guided trip.

Lastly, South Korea is a great blend of tradition and modernity. You can explore Seoul’s history, eat delicious street food, and stay in a stylish hostel. It’s easy to get to from the US, and the transit system is efficient.

Remember, you can plan these trips at your own pace. Researching and saving up can make it less overwhelming. And when you’re ready, the world is out there waiting for you. It can be a wonderful escape and a source of inspiration for your novel.
