Of course! Vancouver to the interior is a beautiful drive.

1.  **Whistler:** You have to go! It's a short drive up the Sea to Sky Highway. She'll love dog-friendly hikes like the Blackcomb Glacier Trail, and you can hit the slopes in winter or go for a bike in summer.
2.  **Nanaimo and the Coast:** The drive along the coast is stunning. Stop at a place called Tofo Beach to watch the sea lions. In Nanaimo, dog cafes are popular, and you can enjoy a nice dinner at a place like The Elephant Room.
3.  **Tofino and the West Coast:** This is for dramatic rainforest and ocean views. She'll adore playing in the mud and seaweed at Uplands Beach. For you, try dining at the Acorn House for a beautiful seafood meal.
4.  She'll love playing in the snow at Kicking Horse Meadows near Golden. From there, you can drive into Banff. The turquoise lakes are unreal. Dog-friendly activities include hiking Johnston Canyon and visiting the Banff Zoo.
5.  For a quick getaway, drive to Penticton. It's a short trip. She'll run freely along Lake Roslyn. You can enjoy wine tastings and a fine dinner at a restaurant like the Fairmont's lift-ski lodge.

All of these are wonderful places for Golden to explore with you!
