Let's talk about throw-ins. US high school and college soccer both follow the same core offside principles we've discussed.

However, there is a key difference. In college soccer, an attacker is not offside receiving the ball on a corner kick. But, in both high school and college rules, they ARE offside receiving it on a goal kick.

For example: Player Red 9 is in an offside position during a Red Team's goal kick. If the kick is played directly to him, it is an offside offense.

So, while the offside test itself doesn't change, a restart like a goal kick is treated as an offside offense in both levels, whereas a corner kick is not.
