Okay, she's on the mend and wants to cook. That sounds like a wonderful way to reclaim her vacation.

Of course! Here are five relaxing and easy dinner ideas.

First, Shrimp and Corn Skewers. You just get some shrimp and corn on the cob, marinate them in olive oil, lemon juice, and herbs, and grill them. Quick and fresh.

Second, a Pasta with a Creamy Mushroom Sauce. You can use any pasta, sauté some mushrooms, add heavy cream, garlic, and thyme, and mix it all together. Very comforting.

Third, a Simple Grilled Salmon with Roasted Vegetables. Grill some salmon, and roast some veggies like asparagus or zucchini. Easy to do.

Fourth, a Tomato and Avocado Salad. Just slice up a tomato and an avocado, add some cucumber, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Super fresh and light.

And fifth, Grilled Cheese and Tomato Soup. A classic comfort food combo. Just melt some good cheese in bread and have it with some tomato soup. Enjoy your well-deserved vacation!
