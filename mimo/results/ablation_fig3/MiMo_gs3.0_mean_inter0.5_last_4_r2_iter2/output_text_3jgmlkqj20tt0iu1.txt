Initally nervous is pretty common during these conversations. One preparation step is to request a formal meeting time well in advance through email. You can frame it as a professional request to discuss your performance and career growth, rather than a last-minute, urgent issue.

Another idea is to do some 'rehearsal'. Practice what you want to say with a trusted mentor or even just by talking through the key points out loud. This can really help to reduce the anxiety and make you feel more prepared.
