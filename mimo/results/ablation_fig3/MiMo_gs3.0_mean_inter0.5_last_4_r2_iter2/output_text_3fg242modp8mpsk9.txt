For activities, small experiences often make for the best memories. Chartering a small boat for a few hours for some quiet fishing could be romantic.

You could also combine something you both love, like a private kayak tour to a favorite seafood restaurant that's hard to get to by land.

And since your real-life partner dislikes seasickness, it's smart to prioritize stable vessels, like catamarans, or even sunset cruises from the shore where motion is minimal.
