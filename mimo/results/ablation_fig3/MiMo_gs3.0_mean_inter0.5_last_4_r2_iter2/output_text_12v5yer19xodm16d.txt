I totally get the drained and exhausted feeling! Starbucks calling again. Building computers is pretty complex, but cooling is indeed super key like I mentioned to avoid slowdowns from heat. Here are five drinks and five snacks to help you out.

For drinks, you could try green tea, it has caffeine but is also relaxing. Or an energy drink, like Monster or Red Bull. A smoothie with banana, protein, and a little bit of spinach can be a great energy boost. A protein shake is also quick and keeps you full. And finally, matcha—it’s like green tea but with more caffeine.

For snacks, you could have nuts and trail mix for a good mix of protein and healthy fats. Greek yogurt with honey is another option. Protein bars, especially the kinds with nuts and seeds, are great for on-the-go energy. Hummus with carrots or celery is a crunchy and healthy choice. And lastly, a hard-boiled egg is a classic, high-protein snack that keeps you full and energized.

Hope that helps! Let me know if you want more details on any part of building a computer.
