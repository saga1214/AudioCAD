### **Key Design Tips for Art History Presentation**  
1. **Images**: Ukiyo-e prints, kimono textiles, pottery, or contemporary Japanese art.  
2. **Layout**: Use asymmetrical balance (common in Japanese design).  
3. **Color Scheme**: Indigo, gold, white, and earth tones (e.g., Japanese tea ceremony palettes).  
4. **Font**: Elegant calligraphy (e.g., "Miyabi" font) or minimalist sans-serif (modern contrast).
