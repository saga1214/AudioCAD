Breeds to consider: First, Labrador Retrievers—they’re friendly, trainable, and love people but shed a good amount. Another friendly, easygoing, and less-shedding dog is a Cocker Spaniel. Next, Goldendoodles, which are mixed-breed “labradoodles,” often have a great temperament and are less likely to shed a ton. Another chill option is Basset Hounds—they're laid-back but can be vocal when left alone. Another low-shedding breed-friendly mixed option is a Cavalier King Charles Spaniel.
