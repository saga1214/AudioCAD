Now, about that stubborn glue gun—it's so frustrating when it won't work even when "hot"! Try these quick fixes:  
1. **Check the heating element**: If it's a glue stick-based model, peel the plastic cover and gently nudge the heating coil with a pencil to warm it up.  
2. **Add a little heat**: Blow gently on the tip *while* pressing the trigger—sometimes it needs an extra push of air to activate.  
3. **Try room-temperature glue**: If your glue sticks are brand-new, they can be too cool. Microwave them for 5 seconds (in a sealed bag) to warm the liquid inside.  

If all else fails, temporarily tape elements in place with painter’s tape or double-sided tape until your glue gun heats up. Hang in there—your spooky globes are worth the wait! 🔥👻
