Yes, numerically, scoring it that way tips the scale to *Frankenstein* with a total of twenty-two versus twenty-one. Its perfect cultural-relevance score was the key.

However, scores are simplified. From just a filmmaking perspective, many directors might argue that *The Count* is an easier project. Condensing a great action-revenge story is a well-known formula. *Frankenstein's* slower sections are harder to translate into modern pacing.

So, it really depends. If you want themes tied directly to futuristic dilemmas, then based on the scores, go with *Frankenstain*. If you want a guaranteed action-adventure epic, then *The Count* might be the safer and more straightforward choice. Both are fantastic novels either way.
