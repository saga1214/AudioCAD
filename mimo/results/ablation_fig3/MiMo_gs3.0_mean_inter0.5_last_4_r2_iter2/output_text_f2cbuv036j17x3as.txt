For diesel camper vans, maintenance before a long trip is really important. For brakes, a good first step is to have a mechanic visually inspect them to look for things like uneven wear on the pads or rotors, or if there’s excessive dust or oil contamination, which can be a sign of problems. You might also hear a grinding noise or feel a vibration, which could indicate worn-out parts. If the van is older, it’s probably a good idea to replace them rather than just have them inspected. For spark plugs, on a diesel engine, the process is a bit different. You’ll need a special 17/19 millimeter socket and a torque wrench. You’ll have to take off the valve covers and then carefully remove each plug and replace it with a new one. It’s a pretty involved job, so if you’re not comfortable, it’s best to have a mechanic do it. Diesel engines also have high compression, so tightening them too much can damage the engine. Either way, it’s a good idea to make sure the cooling system is checked and fluids are topped up too. Safe travels!
