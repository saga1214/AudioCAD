For winter chicks integrated into the adult coop later five-plus weeks, the best bedding choice is wood shavings. Specifically look for large-flake, kiln-dried pine shavings. They provide excellent, consistent insulation and are dry enough that rodents won't be attracted to them. You'll just need to manage it regularly by adding fresh shavings on top of the composting layer we talked about earlier. This deep litter approach generates natural warmth and is a very safe and effective strategy for that age.
