Yep, quick recap for Florida Zone 10B in reverse seasons: Herbs—definitely try basil, mint, and parsley. Fast greens like lettuce, spinach, radishes. And fruiting plants like easy-going tomatoes and peppers.

Herbs are a great start 'cause they’re resilient to heat. Radishes and lettuce can handle that humidity pretty well. Tomatoes and peppers might need that row cover sometimes, but generally, your warm climate is a huge advantage. Just make sure that drainage I mentioned is on point. Enjoy your garden!
