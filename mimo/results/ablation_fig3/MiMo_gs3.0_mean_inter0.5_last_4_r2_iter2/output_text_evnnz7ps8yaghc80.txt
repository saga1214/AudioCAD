A structured plan is a wonderful idea. Here is a sample weekly schedule you can adjust to fit your routine.

For Monday and Thursday, you could do tennis balls in one session for 15 minutes.

For Tuesday and Friday, practice one-handed two-ball beanbags for 15 minutes, then tennis balls for another 15 minutes.

For Wednesday, use scarves to focus on pattern for 20 minutes.

For Saturday, dedicate about 30 minutes to transitioning with beanbags.

And for Sunday, take a rest day or just do a few casual throws without catching.
