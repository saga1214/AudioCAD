Buying original vintage comics from the forties and fifties is a different game.

Before you buy anything, it's critical to have the comic professionally graded by a company like CGC. The condition of a vintage comic is everything.

Also, be prepared for the cost. You'll need a high budget for shipping, insurance, and the grading fee itself. A single high-grade vintage comic can easily cost between five and ten thousand dollars. 

With that said, here are ten iconic books from that era that are major investments. *Action Comics* number one, Superman's first full appearance, sold in 2024 for six million. *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen, Spider-Man's debut, recently sold for three point six million. *Captain America Comics* number one from '41 sold for just over three million. *Tales to Astonish* number four is the first full appearance of the Hulk. *The Fantastic Four* number one introduced them and is always in demand. A key for your Luke interest is his very first solo book from 1972, *Hero for Hire* number one, especially in high-grade. *The Defenders* number one is his first official team-up issue. *New Avengers* number one brought your guy back prominently in modern comics. *Savage She-Hungle* number one is Kamala Khan's Ms. Marvel debut. And Miles Morales' first appearance in *Ultimate Fallout* number four is always a smart, affordable buy. Remember, the price under 5 hundred dollars would be for a mid-grade copy. The value is always determined by the comic's condition, so focus on professionally graded books.
