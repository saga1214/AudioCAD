</Acoustic guitars usually tune to what's called Standard Tuning. That means four open strings tuned to the notes E, A, D, and G, from the thickest to the thinnest. 

Using an app is pretty simple: place your phone near the instrument, strum each string one by target note, and follow the green or red indicator until it's perfectly in tune. If you use a clip-on, you just point it at the string you're playing.
