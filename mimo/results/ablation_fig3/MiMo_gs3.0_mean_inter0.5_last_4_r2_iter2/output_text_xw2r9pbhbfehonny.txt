Yeah, before filming your first YouTube video for kids as a sock puppeteer, here’s a quick checklist:

First, permission. If it’s at a volunteer spot like an assisted living, make sure you can record and post. Hospitals and schools may have stricter rules.

For equipment, just use your phone—it’ll be fine at first. If you can, ask someone to film you while you perform, or set your phone on a tripod if you have one.

Plan your skit. Pick your favorite short performance or maybe a new five-minute show for the camera. Keep it simple and engaging.

Think about editing. You don’t have to at first, but apps like CapCut or iMovie can trim the best parts and add some simple titles, like, you know, "Melvin the Magnificent!"

Then title and tag your videos. Give them fun names and add tags like, like, puppetry or kids channel Melvin, or whatever fits.

And share it. Once you have a few up, share the channel with friends and social media where kids can find it safely.

Start small, film Friday’s show, and see how it goes. You’ve got the talent, so it should be a lot of fun.
