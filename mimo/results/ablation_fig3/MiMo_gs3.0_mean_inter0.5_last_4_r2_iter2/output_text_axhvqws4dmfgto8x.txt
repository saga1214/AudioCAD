Here’s the trick for stir-fried noodles: Use plenty of water, filling your pot about three-quarters of the way. Then, cover the pot and bring the water to a rolling boil first. Let it bubble hard for a minute or two.

Then, and this is the most important part, drain the noodles completely. Stir-fry cooks very quickly, so if your noodles are still wet, your stir-fry will get soggy. A quick rinse with cold water right after draining is also a great tip to stop the cooking and keep them from sticking together.
