Alright, let's compare a mushroom-based leather, like the brand MycoWorks is known for, against Piñatex and cactus leather.

In terms of popularity, which I'm scoring on a zero to one hundred scale, mushroom leather is the most controversial. It's brand new and unknown, so I'd give it a popularity score of about 30.

Manufacturing cost is very different. Mushroom leather is currently very expensive to produce, maybe 120 to 150 dollars a bag. This is higher than both the other options.

However, because of its high cost, you can set a much higher selling price for a mushroom leather bag, somewhere in the 600 to 750 dollar range.
