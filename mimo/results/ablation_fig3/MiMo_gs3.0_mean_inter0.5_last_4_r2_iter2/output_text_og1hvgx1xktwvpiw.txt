Okay, I can see the confusion here. Let me clarify those two cocktails for you so you have the proportions straight. A classic Boulevardier traditionally starts with more bourbon—it's one and a quarter ounces, one ounce vermouth, and one ounce Campari. Your friend's equal-parts version, which is also classic, uses one ounce of bourbon, vermouth, and Campari. Now, for the Last Word, if you decide to make that instead, it's equal parts of three-quarters of an ounce each of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. Stir them well, strain, and garnish with an orange twist. Both Boulevardiers and the Last Word are fantastic, so you can't go wrong. Enjoy your party!
