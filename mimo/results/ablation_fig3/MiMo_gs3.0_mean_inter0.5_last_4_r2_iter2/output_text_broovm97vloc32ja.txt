That is absolutely possible. If you write and illustrate your own children's book, this is called a 'hybrid' book and it's a very valuable skill. You can absolutely submit a complete manuscript and your own artwork to a publisher or an agent. However, you should know that publishers **do not pay** the illustrator. They hire a new illustrator for every book, which is why publishers rarely buy hybrid books. Your best path is to submit your complete book to literary agents. If an agent who represents picture books likes your story *and* your art, they will accept you as a client and submit it to their publisher contacts. Just be prepared, as most agents will pass on hybrid books unless the story is exceptionally strong.
