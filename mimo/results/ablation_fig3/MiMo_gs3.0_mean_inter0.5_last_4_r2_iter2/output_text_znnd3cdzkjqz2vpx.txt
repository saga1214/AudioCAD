That's fantastic that you reconsidered standing on the cabinets for everyone's safety!

You've pretty much got all the essentials there. Just to be sure, I'd also suggest having a few shims on hand, which are just those little flat pieces of wood for getting cabinets perfectly level. 

You'll also need some cabinet screws, usually the kind that come with them are coarse and self-drilling. A drill or electric screwdriver will make that part go much faster than a manual screwdriver. And of course, a tape measure to double-check spacing between cabinets or for countertop thickness.

One last thing: make sure you have a safe place to put the upper cabinets before you take them out of the box, like on the floor or a stable table. You don't want them falling if the box starts to fall over.

With that step stool and the basic tools, you should be all set to get those Habitat finds installed. Enjoy that savings and your newly remodeled kitchen!
