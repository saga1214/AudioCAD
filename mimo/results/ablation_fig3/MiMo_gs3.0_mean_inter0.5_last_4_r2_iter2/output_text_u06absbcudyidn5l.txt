General causes for chronic facial redness do include rosacea, sun damage, very sensitive skin, or circulation issues.

Symptoms of vasculitis, which involves blood vessel inflammation, can be vague at first—like fatigue and fever. Skin signs could be rashes, blisters, or just persistent redness.
