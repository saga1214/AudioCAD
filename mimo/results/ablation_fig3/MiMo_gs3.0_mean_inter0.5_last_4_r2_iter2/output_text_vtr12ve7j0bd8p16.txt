Leo and Sagittarius pairing up for fire-side adventures? That sounds perfect. For a weekend trip near Calgary, one fantastic option is to drive about an hour and a half south to the Kananaskis Valley. It's stunning mountain scenery with lots to do, from hiking to scenic drives. Another great choice, also about an hour and a half away, is Banff National Park, though it's very popular, so you might want to book something in advance. If you both love the water, you could even drive down to Okotoks and ride the Old Man Railway, which is a vintage steam train ride that goes over a scenic canyon.
