Okay, this is a lot of information to organize. Let me break it down for Thanksgiving. For smoking, you need a smoker. For roasting, an oven is required. For roasting small dishes, a toaster oven works. For stovetop roasting, you'll need a stovetop. For boiling, like for wild rice salad mac and cheese, you need a pot and stove. For baking, most desserts require double ovens, and the apple crisp needs a slow cooker. For pressure cooking, stuffing and cranberry sauce need an Instant Pot. A mixer might be needed for the tart or mac and cheese. For salads that need chilling, a fridge is a must.
