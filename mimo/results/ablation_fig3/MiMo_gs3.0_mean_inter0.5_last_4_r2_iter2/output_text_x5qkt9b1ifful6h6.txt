Based on current scientific consensus, **ultra-processed foods (UPFs)** are widely considered the *one type of food that would be the worst for your health if eaten in excess**. Here's why:

1. **Combination of Harmful Ingredients**:  
   UPFs are engineered to combine multiple risk factors:  
   - **Added sugars** (linked to diabetes, obesity, heart disease).  
   - **Unhealthy fats** (like trans fats or saturated fats).  
   - **Excessive sodium** (causing hypertension and kidney damage).  
   - **Lack of fiber/nutrients** while being high in calories.  

2. **Synergistic Harm**:  
   Unlike isolated foods (e.g., sugar *or* trans fats), UPFs deliver multiple toxins simultaneously. This synergy amplifies risks like inflammation, metabolic dysfunction, and chronic disease.

3. **Obesogenic Design**:  
   UPFs are hyper-palatable (designed to overstimulate cravings), encouraging overconsumption without satiety. This drives weight gain and insulin resistance.

4. **Nutrient Deprivation**:  
   stripped of protective nutrients (e.g., fiber, vitamins, minerals) while being calorie-dense. This disrupts gut health and cellular repair.

5. **Pervasive Health Impact**:  
   Linked to **heart disease, type 2 diabetes, fatty liver, obesity, and premature mortality**—outpacing the risks from single-food excesses.

### Why Not Other Contenders?
- **Trans Fats**: While uniquely harmful, modern regulations have reduced their prevalence (e.g., banned in the US/EU).  
- **Sugar/Salt**: Harmful but often found *within* UPFs (e.g., sugary cereals, processed snacks).  
- **Processed Cheeses**: A subset of UPFs, but not the most pervasive category.  

### Key Takeaway for Your Class:
UPFs represent a **systemic threat** due to their accessibility, marketing, and engineered composition. Moderation of whole, minimally processed foods (fruits, veggies, lean proteins) remains the cornerstone of health. Always check labels—avoid ingredients you can’t recognize! 🥦🍠
