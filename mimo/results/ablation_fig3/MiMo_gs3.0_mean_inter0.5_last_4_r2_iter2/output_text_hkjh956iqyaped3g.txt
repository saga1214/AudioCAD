Let's break this fifteen-minute break into two parts to make it more effective.

First, for the first five minutes, focus on movement and grounding. Get up and walk briskly around the room or even outside if you can. Pair that with deep breathing—maybe inhale for four counts, hold for two, and exhale slowly for six. This combination is great for clearing your head.

Then, for the last ten minutes, I'd suggest a mindfulness exercise. You can find many guided ten-minute meditations on apps like Insight Timer or even on YouTube. The key is to just focus on your breath for a period. It's a great way to end your day by creating a little mental space before finishing your work.
