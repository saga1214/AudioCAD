Here's a practical guide to installing your key items while respecting your landlord's rules:

### 1. **Mounting a TV**
   - **Find studs**: Use a stud finder (or tap-test method) to locate wall studs. Mark them with a pencil.  
   - **Install the bracket**:  
     - Align the bracket with the marked studs.  
     - Use drywall anchors *only if no studs are present* (e.g., for lightweight mounts).  
     - Secure with screws—tighten firmly to avoid vibrations.  
   - **Attach the TV**: Ensure the VESA pattern matches the mount.  
   - **Safety tip**: Avoid drilling near electrical wires or pipes. Use a stud finder with wire detection.  

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Lightweight options**:  
     - Use adhesive hooks (e.g., Command™ strips) for small planters.  
     - Hang pots from ceiling hooks or ceiling-mounted racks (ensure ceiling supports weight limits).  
   - **Heavy pots**:  
     - Drill a small hole into the wall, insert a toggle bolt, and screw in a hook.  
     - Use a pot stand on the floor instead—no drilling needed.  
   - **Safety**: Avoid hanging pots directly over walkways. Use saucers to catch water.  

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Install a cleat system**:  
     - Attach a horizontal "cleat" to the wall at your desired height.  
     - Slide the shelf onto the cleat and secure with small nails or screws.  
   - **No-drill alternative**: Use strong adhesive shelf brackets (e.g., 3M Vibration Isolator).  
   - **Weight limits**: Test shelves with small items first. Avoid overloading.  

### General Tips:
- **Use tools wisely**: Rent a drill if needed, but **avoid power tools** (like sanders) that could violate noise rules.  
- **Safety first**: Wear safety glasses and gloves.  
- **Plan layouts**: Sketch decor zones (art walls, furniture placement) before buying pieces.  
- **Lighting**: Use LED strip lights under shelves or small lamps for ambiance.  

> 💡 **Decorating Strategy**: Start with large art/furniture, then add shelves and plants. Group art by color or theme for cohesion. Keep decor open and uncluttered to maintain a studio vibe!
