Okay, so if you and girlfriend drive down to Nevada after exploring BLM land or Burning- wait not Burning Man unless it's event time. Let's say post-adventure, heading to Vegas. Vegas is all about variety. You could hit up a classic casino for the atmosphere, but don’t forget to walk the Strip. It’s like a city within a city. 

For a proper Vegas experience, definitely try to get a cocktail at a cool speakeasy or a nice dinner with a view. Some places have amazing rooftop bars. If you want to be wild, there are clubs and live shows. If you want to be classy, there are fine dining options and even a pool party at the Bellagio fountains. It really depends on the vibe you two are after.
