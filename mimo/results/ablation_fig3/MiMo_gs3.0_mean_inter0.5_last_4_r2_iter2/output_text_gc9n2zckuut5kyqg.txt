Absolutely! Here are some budget-friendly dessert ideas that are both kid-appealing and align with increasing diabetes awareness:

### **Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:**
1. **Fresh Fruit Platters & Skewers**  
   - Arrange seasonal fruits (berries, grapes, melon, bananas) in colorful platters.  
   - Use toothpicks for skewers – kids love this, and it’s low-cost.  

2. **DIY Frozen Yogurt Bar**  
   - Serve plain Greek yogurt (protein-rich and affordable) with toppings like:  
     - Fresh berries  
     - Chopped nuts  
     - A drizzle of honey *sparingly*  
     - A sprinkle of cinnamon  

3. **Homemade Popsicles**  
   - Blend frozen fruit (e.g., pineapple, mango, or banana) with water or coconut milk and pour into molds. Freeze overnight.  
   - Kids can decorate with a few berries or a small sprinkle of granola.  

4. Barb**e Quekies** (Kid Twist)  
   - Cut small oatmeal cookies or rice cakes into shapes (stars, animals) for kids to dip in yogurt or sprinkle with seeds.  

5. **Mini Whole-Grain Cookies**  
   - Bake simple cookies with oats, whole-wheat flour, and minimal sugar (e.g., chocolate-chip or peanut butter). Use bulk ingredients to save money.  

6. **Fruit Salad Cups**  
   - Pre-portion small cups of mixed fruit (melon, pineapple, grapes). Add a squeeze of lime for freshness.  

### **Budget Tips for Food in General:**  
- **BBQ Smartly:** Buy in bulk for burgers/hot dogs, but limit processed toppings (e.g., offer mustard/ketchup *and* avocado slices or salsa).  
- **DIY Beverages:**  
  - Bulk water + infused water (cucumber/mint) is free.  
  - Sugar-free lemonade or iced tea (use concentrate to save).  
  - Offer watermelon slices or orange wedges as thirst-quenching "beverages."  

### **Awareness Tie-In:**  
Label dishes with small signs (e.g., "Low-Sugar Fruit Platter" or "Protein-Packed Yogurt"). This subtly educates without being preachy.
