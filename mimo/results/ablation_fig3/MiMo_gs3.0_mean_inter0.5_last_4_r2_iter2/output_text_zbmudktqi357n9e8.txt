Alright, Mexico is an *excellent* choice for autumn scene inspiration! The peak of their fall foliage, which they call "temporada de hojas doradas," is typically from late September to November. The colors are just stunning in regions like Chiapas, Oaxaca, and the Riviera Maya. If you can swing it, late October or early November is ideal—you’ll see the most vibrant colors without the peak crowds of spring.

And watercolors? Mexico’s landscapes are practically begging for your brush. The fiery oranges, burnt siennas, and deep reds of the foliage, the turquoise waters of the coast, and the rustic villages... it’s all perfect watercolor material. Practice wet-on-wet for those soft, blended leaves, layering for rich skies, and splattering for water effects. Mexico’s autumn will give you endless techniques to develop.

Just pack your paints, find a quiet spot to observe the light, and let the colors inspire you. You’ve got this!
