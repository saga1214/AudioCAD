Minimalizing meals is perfect for busy declutterers—it’s all about fewer ingredients, less cleanup, and more time. Here are some super simple dinner ideas that align with your simplification journey:

### 🍳 Quick & Minimalist Meals:
1. **One-Pan Roasted Veggies + Protein**  
   Toss seasonal veggies (like broccoli, bell peppers, zucchini) with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Add protein (chicken, tofu, or chickpeas) to the same pan. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 20–25 mins. *Minimalist win*: One pot, one pan, minimal spices.

2. **Stir-Fry with Repurposed Ingredients**  
   Use leftover rice (one of your five molds 😉), frozen veggies, and a simple sauce (soy sauce + ginger + garlic). Cook in 10 mins. *Bonus*: No extra gadgets needed—just a pan and basic tools.

3. **Hearty Stew or Soup**  
   Sauté onions, carrots, and celery. Add canned beans, broth, and a handful of spinach or frozen peas. Simmer 15 mins. Serve with whole-grain bread. *Ideal for spices/gadget overload*: Use pantry staples you *actually* use.

### 💡 Key Minimalist Cooking Principles:
- **Repurpose, Don’t Collect**: Use what you *already own*. If you have three types of soy sauce, just pick one favorite.  
- **Batch Prep**: Cook a big pot of grains (rice/quinoa) on Sunday for quick meals all week.  
- **Season Simply**: Master 2–3 flavor bases (e.g., garlic + ginger + chili flakes) instead of hunting for niche spices.  

### 💬 For Your Home Office Clutter:
Since you asked! For papers:  
- Go digital (scans + cloud storage).  
- For cables: unplug non-essentials, use cable organizers, and recycle extras.
