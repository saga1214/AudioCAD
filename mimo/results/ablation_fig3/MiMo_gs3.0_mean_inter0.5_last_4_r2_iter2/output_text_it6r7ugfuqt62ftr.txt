Here’s a rough four-day itinerary for you and your family.

For where to stay, I'd suggest The Westin Bonaventure Hotel and Resort. It's a luxury spot with great pools, including a heated one perfect for swimming, and it handles dietary needs well.

On Day One, you'll arrive, check into the hotel, and maybe grab a casual lunch at the resort. In the evening, you can rent bikes and ride around the Coachella Valley.

For Day Two, you could take a tour to the Salton Sea. It's a unique, otherworldly place where you can actually go saltwater swimming. For lunch, a great gluten-free-friendly spot is SaltLife Salton Sea. You'll spend the afternoon exploring, and then for dinner back at the resort, The Bistro has a dedicated gluten-free menu.

On Day Three, you could visit the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway for incredible views and a ride up Mount San Jacinto. For lunch, the tramway has a café with options. Afterwards, you can relax by the resort's pools.

For Day Four, you could tour the Pioneertown, which is a classic Old West movie set. A fun lunch spot there is Pappy and Harriet's. In the afternoon, you can explore the historic area, and then return to the resort for a final swim and a special farewell dinner.

Getting around is easy, as there's a lot to do right near the hotel, and ride-shares are plentiful.

Enjoy your wonderful family vacation
