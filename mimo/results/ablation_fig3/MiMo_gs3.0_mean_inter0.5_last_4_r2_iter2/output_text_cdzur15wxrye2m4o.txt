### Steps to Change Your Own Oil (Drain from Bottom Method):
1. **Warm up the engine** (10–15 minutes of light driving) to make draining easier.  
2. **Drain old oil**:  
   - Park on a **level surface**.  
   - Place an **oil drain pan** under the engine.  
   - Remove the **drain plug** from the oil pan (located at the bottom of the engine) and let oil drain completely.  
3. **Replace the oil filter**:  
   - Remove the old filter.  
   - Lubricate the rubber seal of the new filter with a little new oil.  
   - Screw it on by hand until snug (don’t overtighten).  
4. **Refill with new oil**:  
   - Add the **exact type/amount** specified for your engine (check your owner’s manual).  
   - Start the engine and run it for a minute to circulate the oil.  
5. **Check oil level**:  
   - Turn off the engine, wait 2–3 minutes, then check the dipstick. Top up if needed.  

### Critical Reminders:
- **Safety first**: Ensure the car is **level and stable** when working under it—no slopes, no uneven ground.  
- **Tools**: You’ll need a drain pan, new oil/filter, wrenches, and an oil filter wrench. Your friend’s ramps should work if they fit your stairwell car, but **only use them on level ground**.  
- **Oil/FILTER specs**: Same make/model doesn’t guarantee compatibility. **Always verify the exact specs in your owner’s manual**.
