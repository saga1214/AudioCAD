Absolutely, there are several great ways to explore Lockport's incredible history.

For a more in-depth look, you could check out the Lockport Museum of History & Industry, or MOHI for short. It's located right inside the locks and does a great job of telling the entire story of the canal's construction and its lasting impact on the city.
