Dallas has some fantastic Asian noodle spots! For good Asian noodles in Dallas, you might try these places. First, there's Pho 79 in Little Elm, which is known for its pho, but they also have amazing noodle dishes. Second, Noodle House in Richardson has really flavorful担担面, or Dan Dan Noodles. And third, there's Din Tai Fung, which is a famous restaurant that has xiao long bao and other noodle dishes.
