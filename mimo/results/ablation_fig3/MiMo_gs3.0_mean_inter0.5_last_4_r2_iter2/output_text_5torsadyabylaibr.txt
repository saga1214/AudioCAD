A classic and impressive choice for Thanksgiving is a simple pumpkin pie. You only really need an oven.

Another easy dessert is a no-bake cheesecake. It just requires a single pot on the stovetop, so again, no oven is needed.

And for a lighter option, a simple apple crisp is wonderful. It's just sliced apples topped with a simple oat and butter crust, and it bakes in one pot.
