That's fantastic that you're all prepped and ready to go! You've basically got everything you need for the installation itself.

But, just to be safe, I'd suggest a few extra things you might not have thought of. Shims are super important for making sure everything is level and perfectly straight. You'll also need a drill and a set of screws, probably one and a quarter-inch ones, which will be much easier to work with than nails. And definitely don't forget safety glasses and work gloves to protect your hands.

Oh, and another thing: Habitat cabinets often come with little pre-drilled holes or guides. Just double-check the instructions to see if you need to drill any pilot holes before you start fastening.
