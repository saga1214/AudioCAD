Calgary is a fantastic base for adventures. For a romantic weekend getaway, you're spoiled for choice.

For a classic mountain adventure, you can be at the Banff Gondola in less than an hour. You can ride up to Sulphur Mountain for breathtaking views, or hike in the stunning Banff Gorge. Just a little further, the Icefields Parkway is one of the most spectacular drives in the world, leading to the Columbia Icefield and a trip on the Icefields Parkway. It's an unforgettable adventure.

If you prefer the coast, you can drive about two and a half hours to the Kicking Horse Canyon for some hiking, or head about an hour and a half east to the famous Elk and Grizzly Country in Wood Buffalo National Park. For a more relaxed, scenic drive, the Canal District along the Bow River is beautiful, especially at sunset. You could also take a short detour to the Kananaskis Valley, which is known as the 'Banff of the East' and has fantastic hiking and views. It's a wonderful spot for a romantic day trip or a longer weekend.
