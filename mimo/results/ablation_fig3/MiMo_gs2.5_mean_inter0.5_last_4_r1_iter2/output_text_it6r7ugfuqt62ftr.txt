You're clearly planning multiple trips back-to-back! Northern California has plenty of warm, beachy, and pool destinations perfect for your goals.

Since you dislike cold but Northern California winters can be chilly, especially without the ocean's warmth, I suggest heading south. The Central Coast or even a quick trip down to Orange or San Diego County offers reliably warm weather perfect for swimming year-round.

For a 4-5 day trip in an SUV, I have two fantastic options. They both offer warm weather, swimming, and are within a reasonable drive.

Option 1 is Santa Barbara. It's about a 3.5 to 4-hour drive from Northern California. You'll find plenty of beaches perfect for swimming, like East Beach or Butterfly Beach. The Santa Barbara Pier has public showers and lockers, making it easy to jump in. There are also great pool options at resorts, especially in the Montecito area. The weather is reliably mild and warm. For places to eat, Boathouse at Hendry's Beach is excellent for seafood with ocean views and is very accommodating to gluten-free needs. You could also try Gjelina for upscale Californian food.

Option 2 is Laguna Beach in Orange County. This is about a 4-hour drive. It's known for its stunning coastline and beaches, like Heisler Park beach, which has lifeguards and is great for swimming. Many resorts, like the Beach Boys Bungalows, have pools. The weather is consistently warm. For dining, The Beachcomber at Heisler Park is a classic beachfront spot with good GF options. You could also try Farmhouse Kitchen in nearby Laguna Niguel for a farm-to-table experience.

Both locations are beautiful, have warm weather guaranteed for swimming, and offer plenty of activities beyond the beach, like hiking or exploring local art galleries. Since you have a healthy budget, you can stay at nice beachfront resorts or charming B&Bs in either location.

I hope this helps you plan a wonderful vacation. Enjoy your time away!
