To attach Velcro securely without damaging your jacket's lining, here are the best methods, ordered by effectiveness:

### 1. **Iron-On Adhesive (Best for Beginners)**
   - **How to Use:** Cut Velcro strips to size. Press the *hook side* onto the jacket first for 15-20 seconds with medium heat (no steam). Peel off backing, then press the *loop side* onto the jacket for another 15-20 seconds.
   - **Pros:** No sewing needed, holds well, and is durable.
   - **Cons:** Requires an iron and careful heat application.

### 2. **Double-Sided Fabric Tape (Lowest Risk)**
   - **How to Use:** Apply tape directly to the Velcro backing, press firmly, and smooth out wrinkles.
   - **Pros:** No heat/heat-sensitive materials needed; works on delicate linings.
   - **Cons:** Less durable for heavy use; may need reapplication over time.

### 3. **Fabric Glue (For Flexible Linings)**
   - **How to Use:** Apply a small dot of fabric glue to the Velcro backing, press firmly, and hold for 30 seconds.
   - **Pros:** Quick and inexpensive; works for small repairs.
   - **Cons:** Not ideal for high-stress areas; may require reinforcement.

### ⚠️ **Avoid:**
   - **Staples:** Will tear the lining and damage the jacket permanently.
   - **Hot Glue:** Melts synthetic fabrics and damages linings.

### Pro Tips:
- **Test First:** Always try the method on an inconspicuous area (like an inner seam).
- **Reinforce:** For heavy-duty use, add a piece of leather or vinyl behind the Velcro for extra grip.
- **Placement:** Attach Velcro to a sturdy part of the jacket (e.g., cuffs, bottom hem) to avoid stress points.
