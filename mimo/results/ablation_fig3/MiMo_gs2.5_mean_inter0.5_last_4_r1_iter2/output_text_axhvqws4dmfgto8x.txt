Alright, let's tackle this stir-fry noodle question with care. Setting off smoke alarms purely boiling pasta with wood spoons is a legendary mistake, so we'll avoid that at all costs. Stir-fry with noodles is a great first real attempt—it's fast, forgiving, and customizable.

The absolute critical step for perfect noodles is **salted water**. When you get your pot of water boiling, add a generous amount of salt. It makes the noodles taste better and helps them hold together. Don't forget to **stir them constantly** while they cook to prevent sticking.

Once they're done, **drain them immediately**. Stir-fry water is like lava, it will make your stir-fry soggy if you dump it in too soon.

Here's a pro-tip: **save a cup of that hot noodle water**. When you add your noodles back to the hot pan with sauce, a splash of this starchy water helps the sauce cling perfectly to the noodles. It’s a game-changer!

Most importantly, **no wood in the pot!** Stick to metal or sturdy plastic spoons. You've got this—just watch the noodles, drain them fast, and save that water. It'll be a huge success
