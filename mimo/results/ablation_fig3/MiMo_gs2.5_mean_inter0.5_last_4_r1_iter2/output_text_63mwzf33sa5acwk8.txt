Okay, so you're thinking about combining a desert adventure with a Vegas trip in your van. That’s a fun idea. If you drive down to Joshua Tree, then head to Vegas, the drive is manageable. You could stop in some quirky small towns along the way, like Pioneertown or maybe even the Mojave Desert area. 

For a Vegas experience, it’s all about what you two want. If you’re into nightlife, there are clubs and shows, but if you’re more into a low-key vibe, you could find a nice hotel with a pool, maybe somewhere like The Venetian or the Bellagio for a fancy night. You could also check out the Strip from a different angle—some hotels have great views of the fountains. 

Just a heads up, Vegas can be expensive, so plan your budget for food, drinks, and activities. You can also find cheaper spots off the Strip if you don’t mind being a bit farther out.
