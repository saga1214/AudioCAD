That line of reasoning still doesn't quite work.

See, while it's true that a genetic mutation in the egg led to the first chicken, the egg itself wasn't 'chicken' until the creature that hatched from it was. The egg from a non-chicken parent would hatch into a chick, but it wouldn't be the *first* true chicken. The first chicken had to hatch from the egg laid by that creature that was one evolutionary step away. So, the chicken still hatched first.
