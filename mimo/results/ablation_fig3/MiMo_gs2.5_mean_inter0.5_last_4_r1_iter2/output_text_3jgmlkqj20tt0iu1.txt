Approaching this can feel a little intimidating, but there are a couple of common ways to do it.

One option is to ask to schedule a meeting during your boss's performance review period. You could say something like, 'Hi, I'd like to schedule a time to discuss my performance and contributions since I started.' This puts it in a more natural context and can feel less like a surprise request.

A second option is to bring it up in a face-to-face meeting where you're already discussing your work. For instance, if you're talking about your recent assignments and reviews, you could say, 'Based on the positive feedback I've been receiving, I'd like to discuss my role and compensation moving forward.' This links the raise directly to your demonstrated success.
