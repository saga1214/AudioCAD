### **Costa Rica Dive Highlights**  
1. **Tortuguero National Park**  
   - **Why**: Warm, clear waters with abundant marine life.  
   - **Dive Sites**:  
     - *Cocles Cut*: Snorkeling with turtles, rays, and tropical fish.  
     - *La Punta*: Strong currents (advanced divers), whale sharks in season.  
   - **Wildlife**: Sea turtles (nesting season: May–Nov), manatees, manta rays.  
   - **Logistics**: Accessible by boat from Puerto Viejo. Pack short fins for ease.  

2. **Cocos Island**  
   - **Why**: Remote, dramatic reefs with deep walls and big waves.  
   - **Dive Sites**:  
     - *La Pintada*: Marine iguanas, giant sea turtles.  
     - *La Fortuna*: Thrilling drift dives with reef sharks.  
   - **Wildlife**: Dolphins, hammerheads, and endemic species like the Cocos parrotfish.  
   - **Logistics**: Fly to Limón, then ferry to the island (4-hour boat ride).  

3. **Aguas Claras**  
   - **Why**: Gentle currents and shallow coral gardens.  
   - **Dive Sites**:  
     - *Cerro Negro*: Colorful coral formations.  
     - *Monteverde*: Snorkel-friendly for beginners.  
   - **Wildlife**: Stingrays, schools of fish, and occasional dolphins.  

**Pro Tip**: Visit **May–November** for optimal visibility and wildlife. Avoid rainy season (Dec–Apr) if possible.  

---

### **Bonus Tropical Alternatives**  
1. **Galápagos Islands (Ecuador)**  
   - **Why**: Unique wildlife encounters (sea lions, penguins, turtles).  
   - **Dive Sites**:  
     - *Los Rios*: Giant tortoises in shallow waters.  
     - *Los Tuneles*: Swim-throughs with hammerheads.  
   - **Best Time**: June–November (dry season).  

2. **Bonaire (Caribbean)**  
   - **Why**: World-class shore diving with calm, shallow reefs.  
   - **Dive Sites**:  
     - *Shoemaker’s Reef*: Coral gardens teeming with fish.  
     - *The Wall*: Deep drop-offs for advanced divers.  
   - **Bonus**: Snorkeling with snorkel schools (baby sharks!).  

3. Finns Comparison Recap:  
   - **Short fins**: Best for calm waters, shore diving, or beginners (maneuverable, compact).  
   - **Long fins**: Ideal for strong currents, long-distance, or powerful swimmers.
