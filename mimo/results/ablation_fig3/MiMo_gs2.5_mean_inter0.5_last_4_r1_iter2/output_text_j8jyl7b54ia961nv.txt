Yes, if public acceptance and ethical controversy are the absolute top priorities, then the sugar campaign might be the safer bet. Misinformation and freedom issues around vaccines are a huge challenge. However, vaccines are a more efficient tool for preventing immediate, life-threatening diseases. So, it's really a trade-off: is it more important to protect people from preventable diseases quickly, or to promote healthy lifestyles with less immediate backlash? Both are important national health goals, but the vaccine campaign has a higher risk of failure if public support isn't strong.
