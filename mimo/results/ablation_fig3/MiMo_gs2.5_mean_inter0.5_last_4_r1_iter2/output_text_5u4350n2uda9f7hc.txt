You've raised an excellent point—weight is crucial when carrying plants up five flights of stairs! Here’s a breakdown of your options, focusing on size, weight, and suitability for your space:

### **1. Bird's Nest Snake Plant (Sansevieria hahnii)**  
- **Appearance**: Compact, with upright, pointed leaves growing in a tight spiral (like a nest), often with silver-green stripes.  
- **Light & Wind**: Thrives in bright indirect light (perfect for your east balcony) and tolerates city wind/wind gusts.  
- **Size**: Stays small (6–12 inches tall) and narrow. Pots are lightweight (1–3 lbs max).  
- **Water Needs**: Extremely drought-tolerant—water only when soil is *completely dry* (every 3–4 weeks).  
- **Transport**: Very easy to carry home.  

### **2. Small Succulents (for your table)**  
- **Options**:  
  - **Echeveria** (rosette-shaped, pink/green)  
  - **Haworthia** (tiny, striped, "zoohoid" type)  
  - **Sedum** (small, trailing, or bushy)  
- **Light & Wind**: Love bright morning sun. Wind won’t harm them—they’re resilient.  
- **Size**: Grow slowly (1–2 inches wide). Pots are shallow and lightweight.  
- **Water Needs**: Water sparingly (every 2–3 weeks). Overwatering is the biggest risk.  
- **Transport**: Tiny pots (under 1 lb) fit in a bag.  

### **3. Herbs (Bonus Options)**  
- **Basil** or **Mint**: Compact, love morning sun, and thrive in NYC’s humidity.  
- **Size**: Stay low (6–12 inches). Lightweight pots.

### **Top Picks for Your Balcony**  
1. **One Bird’s Nest Snake Plant** (on floor): Sturdy, air-purifying, and low-maintenance.  
2. **One Succulent** (e.g., Echeveria) on your table: Colorful, compact, and sun-loving.  
3. **Optional Herb** (Basil): Adds freshness and color.  

### **Pro Tips**  
- **Avoid**: Large, heavy plants (e.g., Monstera, Fiddle Leaf Fig).  
- **Shop Smart**: Buy from **nurseries or plant swaps**—smaller pots are cheaper and easier to transport.  
- **City-Specific Care**: NYC apartments are dry. Mist succulents lightly if soil feels *too* dry (but still water sparingly!).  

These choices will give you color, life, and zero stress—plus they’ll travel home with you beautifully! 🌿
