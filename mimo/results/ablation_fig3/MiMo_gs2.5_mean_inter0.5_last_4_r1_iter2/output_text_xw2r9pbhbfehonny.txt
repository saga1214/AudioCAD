Sure, here’s a quick checklist for filming your own puppet show before posting online:

First, get permission from the venue, especially for assisted living places. Second, bring a tripod or stabilizer for steady shots. Third, think about angles, like maybe a wide shot of you performing and a close-up of the puppet. Fourth, test the lighting before you start. Fifth, record audio clearly, maybe using a lavalier mic or a directional mic. Sixth, label the video as "for kids" on YouTube. Seventh, edit it later, if you want, to make it snappier. Eighth, post it regularly to grow an audience. Ninth, make sure your channel has a friendly, kid-friendly vibe. And tenth, stay consistent with posting and engaging with viewers.

Start simple, and you can improve as you go. Good luck!
