No problem! Let's reconstruct everything clearly for you. Here's a complete, categorized list of measurements, starting with the basics and building up:

### **I. Core Body Measurements (Take for EVERY fitting)**  
1. **Bust**: Across the fullest part.  
2. **Natural Waist**: Tightest part of the torso, not the belly button.  
3. **Hips**: Widest part, 8–10 inches below the natural waist.  

### **II. Essential Measurements (Take for more fitted garments)**  
4. **Shoulder Width**: Across shoulders for jackets/shirts to prevent pulling.  
5. **Neck**: For collars/cuffs.  
6. **Bicep**: At the widest part for sleeve comfort.  

### **III. Garment-Specific Measurements (Add as needed)**  
7. **Inseam** (for pants): From crotch to ankle.  
8. **Sleeve Length**: From shoulder point to wrist.  
9. **Thigh**: Between knee and hip (for tailored pants/skirts).  
10. **Back Rise**: Distance from crotch to waistband (critical for tall clients!).  
11. **Rise**: Front rise (distance from crotch to waistband).

### **Key Tips for Your Neighbor’s Pants**  
- **Back Rise**: Measure this! His 6'4" height means a longer back rise (likely 14–16 inches) to prevent sagging.

- **Test Fit**: Have him try the pants on with his shoes to finalize the hem length!
