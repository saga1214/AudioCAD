This is a tough question because "efficient" can mean different things. If you're talking about the highest energy output per square meter, it might be solar. If it's about cost efficiency, solar is leading. But for reliability and how much energy you get per kilowatt-hour over its lifetime, hydropower often comes out on top. So, it really depends on what you prioritize. Solar is great for scalability and cost, wind is good where the wind is strong, and hydropower is a rock-solid, reliable source where it's available. If I absolutely had to pick just one and consider everything, I think solar energy still takes the lead because it’s so versatile, scalable, and continuously gets better.
