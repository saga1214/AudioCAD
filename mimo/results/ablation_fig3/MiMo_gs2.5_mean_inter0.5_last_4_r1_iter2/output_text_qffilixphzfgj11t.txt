Bruno Mars-free zone and broccoli skip noted. For Bruno the parrot, here are five safe green vegetables. You can give him steamed or raw spinach, romaine lettuce, and Swiss chard. Kale is great, just make sure to remove the tough stems first. And steamed broccoli is fine, as long as Bruno isn't sensitive to it, which he is.

So, spinach, romaine lettuce, Swiss chard, kale, and steamed broccoli are all good options.
