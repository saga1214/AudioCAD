Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration, especially since their autumn is actually our spring. The colors are just... wow. 

For the best autumn colors, you should go in late September through November. That's when you'll see the most vibrant foliage in places like Chiapas or the Yucatán Peninsula. The colors are just... incredible. 

For ocean scenes, maybe think about the Riviera Maya or the Pacific coast. You can paint those turquoise waters, the palm trees... 

And for rivers, you could check out places like the Río Lagartos or the Yucatán River. The reflections and the movement of the water are just... so inspiring. 

Oh, and a pro tip: try to visit some local markets. You can find the most incredible pigments for your autumn palette. Just pack a small sketchbook and a watercolor set. You're going to come back with so much inspiration, it's going to be amazing. Just... take your time and enjoy the process. The inspiration will come.
