That sounds like a fantastic ocean-themed date night! Here are 5 meal ideas for you and your fiancée, keeping dietary needs in mind, plus 5 sweet desserts:

**5 Ocean-Themed Mains:**

1.  **Spicy Mango & Tuna Coconut Curry:** A vibrant, creamy curry packed with protein. Use high-quality tuna (like albacore) instead of shellfish. Stir in fresh mango chunks towards the end for sweetness and heat. Serve with steamed rice. *Diabetes Note:* Use light coconut milk and lean tuna.
2.  **Mango "Fish" Tacos:** Use firm white fish (like cod or tilapia) or even large shrimp (if you confirm she can have cooked shrimp safely). Marinate briefly in lime, chili powder, and cumin. Sear quickly. Serve in warm corn tortillas with a spicy mango salsa (diced mango, red onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice), shredded cabbage, and a touch of avocado.
3.  **Spicy Mango & Shrimp Skewers:** Thread large shrimp (shell-on but peeled) and chunks of firm mango onto skewers. Brush with a simple glaze of lime juice, olive oil, minced garlic, and a pinch of chili flakes. Grill or broil until shrimp is cooked through and slightly caramelized. Serve with a side of mango chutney or a cooling cucumber salad.
4.  **Mango Shrimp Stir-Fry with Tofu:** Use shrimp and firm tofu (pressed and cubed) for protein. Stir-fry tofu and shrimp with garlic, ginger, soy sauce/tamari, and a touch of chili paste. Add broccoli florets and bell peppers. Stir in a generous amount of diced fresh mango right at the end. Serve over a small portion of brown rice or cauliflower rice. *Diabetes Note:* Focus on lean protein, veg, and low-GI carbs.
5.  **Spicy Coconut Curry Fish with Mango Puree:** Sear white fish fillets (like snapper or cod) in a pan. Remove and set aside. Sauté onions, garlic, and ginger. Add curry paste (ensure no shellfish), coconut milk (light), and a splash of lime juice. Simmer. Return fish to the sauce. Serve over rice and drizzle with a smooth mango puree (blend ripe mango with a little lime juice and salt). Garnish with cilantro.

**5 Sweet Ocean-Themed Desserts:**

1.  **Mango Coconut Rice Pudding:** Simmer rice with coconut milk (light or full-fat, check sugar), vanilla, and a pinch of salt until creamy. Stir in diced fresh mango and a touch of honey or maple syrup if needed (check labels). Serve chilled. *Diabetes Note:* Focus on the natural sweetness of the mango and coconut milk; minimal added sugar.
2.  **Spiced Mango Sorbet:** Blend ripe mango chunks with a little lime juice, a pinch of cinnamon or cardamom, and a tiny drizzle of honey/maple syrup if necessary (optional for extra sweetness). Freeze, scraping with a fork every 30 mins until firm. Serve in small glasses. *Diabetes Note:* Naturally sweet; minimal added sugar.
3.  **Coconut Rice Pudding with Mango:** Similar to the rice pudding above, but use a mix of long-grain rice and maybe a small amount of cooked coconut rice (cooked with extra coconut milk). Stir in mango chunks and puree at the end. Serve warm or chilled.
4.  **Mango "Fish" Cake Bites:** Mash ripe mango with a little coconut, shredded coconut, an egg (or flax egg), and spices like ginger and chili powder. Form into small cakes. Pan-fry or bake until golden. Serve with a dollop of yogurt or coconut cream. *Diabetes Note:* Naturally sweet, minimal added sugar.
5.  **Coconut Rice Pudding with Mango Puree:** Similar concept to the first rice pudding, but serve it warm or at room temperature. Layer slightly warm coconut rice pudding with a smooth mango puree. Garnish with toasted coconut flakes. *Diabetes Note:* Focus on natural sweetness; minimal added sugar.

**Tips for Success:**

*   **Read Labels:** Always check labels on pre-made items (curry paste, sauces, coconut milk, tofu, etc.) for hidden shellfish derivatives or added sugars/sodium.
*   **Spice Control:** Have your favorite chili flakes, hot sauce, or fresh chilies ready to adjust the heat level for both of you.
*   **Mango Freshness:** Use ripe but firm mangoes for best flavor and texture in cooking and desserts.
*   **Presentation:** Use ocean-themed plates, napkins, or simple garnishes like edible flowers, fresh herbs (cilantro, mint), or a sprinkle of toasted coconut to enhance the theme.
*   **Enjoy Together!** The most important ingredient is sharing the meal and the fun atmosphere.

Have a wonderful and romantic ocean-themed date night!
