Yes, vintage comics from the forties and fifties are often more valuable precisely because of their rarity.

Here are ten notable examples from that era that are considered important keys. Be aware, the prices for any of these will be well above five thousand dollars.

First, there's *Action Comics* number one from 1938, the debut of Superman. It's arguably the most famous comic ever and recently sold for six million dollars.
Second, *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, which first introduced Batman in 1939.
Third, *Warrior* number one from 1937, which is the first comic appearance of Captain America.
Fourth, *The Flash Comics* number one, debuting The Flash in 1939.
Fifth, *Tales of the Fantastic* number forty-five, which is the first appearance of Doctor Strange in 1963.
Sixth, *Strange Tales* number one hundred and ten, which was the first appearance of the character Nick Fury, Agent of S.H.I.E.L.D.
Seventh, *Fantastic Four* number one, from 1961, which introduced the Fantastic Four and Galactus.
Eighth, *Silver Surfer* number one, debuting the Surfer in 1966.
Ninth, *Thor* number one, which first appeared in 1962.
And tenth, *The Amazing Spider-Man* number thirty-three, which was the first cover appearance of the villain Kingpin.

Again, these are major cornerstone issues, so they are very rare and command very high prices.
