Of course. Let's go over the wines we've discussed and their temperatures. For high-end Pinot Noir, you store it at cellar temperature, which is about 55 degrees Fahrenheit, and serve it slightly below room temp. For Japanese Hon-Mirin, you store it at 60 to 68 degrees Fahrenheit and serve or taste it at room temp. Then for Italian wines, we looked at Chianti, which you store at 55 Fahrenheit and serve a bit below room temp. For Pinot Grigio, you store it at 50 to 55 Fahrenheit and serve it chilled.

So, to answer your question about which is best for hot weather, I'd say Pinot Grigio is your best bet because it's served chilled. For cold weather, a Chianti would be great, as it's a bold red. The Pinot Noir is also a good choice for cooler weather, but it can be served a little warmer. As for the Hon-Mirin, it doesn't really have serving temperatures since you cook with it most of the time, room temp is fine if you taste it. Hope that helps you plan for the party. Cheers!
