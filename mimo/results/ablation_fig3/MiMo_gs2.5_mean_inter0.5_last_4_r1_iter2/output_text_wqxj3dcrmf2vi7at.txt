Of course! Let's go through homemade ideas again. For frozen bites, pumpkin or sweet potato mashed with a tiny bit of water works great. For cookies, mix peanut butter without xylitol with rolled oats, flatten them, and bake until firm, then freeze for extra chilling. Cooked chicken or steamed carrot sticks are also healthy options.

Moving to store-bought brands, popular choices include Blue Buffalo, Wellness Treats, Zuke's Mini Naturals, Greenies dental chews, and Merrick Healthy Puppy or Adult Treats. Look for brands with clean, natural ingredients.

Training is also rewarding! To teach "sit": use a treat, lure her into the sitting position, say "sit," and reward her. For potty training, take her out on a regular schedule, reward heavily when she goes outside, and clean up accidents promptly to remove the scent. For managing barking at Felix, teach a "quiet" command with a reward, and redirect her to a toy or a different command.
