That sounds like a wonderful plan! With a two-bedroom apartment and a baby on the way, I'd suggest sticking to scenic drives that are close to Vancouver, maybe within a two to three-hour drive. This way you can get back home easily and not have to worry about long journeys.

Here are five fantastic options for you:

1.  **Tofino on Vancouver Island:** You'll see some truly stunning rainforests and beaches. A must-do is a guided kayak trip to see the sea otters at Long Beach. You'll also want to visit the wild Pacific Rim National Park.

2.  **Nanaimo and the Saanich Peninsula:** You can visit the famous Butchart Gardens, which are beautiful. Then head to the Cowichan Valley for some lovely wineries and a chance to see some of the local First Nations art.

3.  **Pemberton and the Fraser Canyon:** This is where you'll find the famous Lillooet Pass, which has some of the most dramatic mountain scenery in the province. You could go for a hike on the Lillooet River Trail and maybe even try some fly-fishing.

4.  **Whistler:** This is a classic for a reason, with its amazing skiing in the winter and hiking in the summer. You can also visit the Whistler Village for some great shopping and dining. A fun activity is the Blackcomb Gondola ride, which gives you incredible views of the whole valley.

5.  **Kamloops and the Okanagan Lake:** This area is known for its hot springs and wine country. You can relax at the Spawen Springs, which are right on the lake, and then visit some of the wineries in the Okanagan Valley.

For any of these trips, I'd recommend making a list of the restaurants you want to try beforehand, especially if you're planning to visit during peak season. Enjoy your exploring!
