    *   **Ingredients List (Key Components):**

        *   Olive oil
        *   Butter
        *   Salt and black pepper
        *   Fresh parsley (chopped, optional garnish)

3.  **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**
    *   **Description:** Shrimp sautéed in Cajun seasoning and served over creamy, spicy grits (cornmeal porridge).
    *   **Ingredients List (Key Components):**

        *   Grits (quick-cooking or stone-ground)

        *   Butter or olive oil
