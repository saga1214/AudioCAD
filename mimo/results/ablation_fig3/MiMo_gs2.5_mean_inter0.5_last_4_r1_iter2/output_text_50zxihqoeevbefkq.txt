Okay, host confirming everything looks good! Let me put together a comprehensive shopping list for 24 total guests—that's twenty-four adults plus your four kids.

For the appetizers and finger foods, I'm thinking mini slider buns, ground beef or pork, ketchup and mustard, mayo, pickles, and cheese slices. You'll also need shrimp, cocktail sauce, cooking oil, pigs in blanket which come pre-coated, a cream cheese tube, and sausage links for those.

Drinks will include champagne, either wine coolers or vodka for your signature cocktail, sparkling cider for the kids, lemonade or iced tea, and plenty of water bottles. And since you liked the idea, those kid-safe plastic flutes are a great touch.

For the signature kids' drink, you'll need grape juice, plus any sodas or popsicles you might want for snacks.

For the main course, you'll need a good-sized ham, about 14 to 16 pounds should work. Also, two pounds of butter for the scalloped potatoes, about 5 to 6 pounds of potatoes, and some garlic powder, salt, and pepper. For the roasted carrots, you'll need about 4 to 5 pounds, and for the asparagus, a good 2 to 3 pounds. Olive oil and lemon juice are also on the list.

Deeper in the pantry, you'll probably need flour, baking powder, sugar, salt, pepper, garlic powder, some cooking oil, and maybe some aluminum foil or a roasting pan.

Decorations-wise, balloons, festive hats noisemakers, and maybe a few extra plastic flutes would be good.

And of course, for essentials, you'll need plenty of paper plates, napkins, plastic cups, cutlery, and trash bags.

Let me know if you want recipes for anything specific or need any other adjustments! Happy planning, and I hope your party is amazing!
