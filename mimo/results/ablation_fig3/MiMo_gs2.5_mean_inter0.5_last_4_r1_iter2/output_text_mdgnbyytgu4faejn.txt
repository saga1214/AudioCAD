Okay, let's break this down into a clear, step-by-step format that's easy to follow.

First is your Header. Center your bold name in about a fourteen-point font. To the right of the name, aligned underneath it, list your email, phone number, city, and any icons. Nine-point font works fine here.

Next, you have your Contact Information Order. Name first, then directly below it, email, then phone number, and finally your city. Having it directly below your name is traditional and easier to scan than having it to the side.

Then comes your Professional Summary. This is that short paragraph at the very top where you highlight your most relevant skills from your past experience and state your career goals. It addresses the gap.

After that, your Experience section. Your "Relevant Experience" should be first, where you list your older roles that are most relevant to the jobs you want now, including dates. This is where you can use concise bullet points. Following that, you have your "Additional Work History" section, where you list the rest of your jobs with just the title, company, and dates.

Next is Education. Here, you'll list each degree you earned from the same school. Under each degree, you can add a line for any honors or awards you received from that school, like 'Highest Honors' or a scholarship.

Then, you have a Skills Section. This is highly recommended. List your most relevant skills, maybe grouped into categories like Technical Skills or Languages, right after your work experience.

Finally, you can add optional sections like Certifications, Awards, or Volunteer Work if they're relevant to the job.
