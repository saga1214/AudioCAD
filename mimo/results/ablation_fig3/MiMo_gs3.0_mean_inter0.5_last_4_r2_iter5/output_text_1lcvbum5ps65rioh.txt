BC Traffic Laws state that violating U-turn rules is an infraction, similar to a speeding ticket. You could get a ticket with points on your license, depending on how serious the violation is. So, it's definitely worth paying attention to the rules to avoid fines. Now, for your spaghetti carbonara, here's what you'll need: spaghetti pasta, guanciale or pancetta, eggs, grated Pecorino Romano cheese, freshly ground black pepper, garlic, and some olive oil. You could also have some parsley on hand for a garnish. Just make sure the eggs and cheese are at room temperature.
