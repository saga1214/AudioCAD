That's fantastic! Since you don't like overly sweet snacks, here are some balanced vending machine ideas to fill your new coffee table:

### Savory & Crunchy:
1. **Chips/Crisps** (Classic flavors like salt & vinegar, BBQ, or jalapeño)  
2. **Pretzels** (Mini twists or sticks—savory with a salty kick)  
3. **Beetroot Chips** (Unexpectedly savory and earthy)  
4. **Roasted Nuts** (Peanuts, almonds, or cashews—look for unsalted or lightly salted)  
5. **Rice Crackers** (Light, crispy, and neutral-flavored)  

### Sweet (But Not Chocolaty):  
6. **Cookies** (Oatmeal, chocolate chip, or shortbread—avoid chocolate-dusted ones)  
7. **Gummy Bears/Celeries** (Fruity and chewy but not overly sweet)  
8. **Fruit Snacks** (Like mini mandarins or dried mango—check for no added sugar)  
9. **Rice Krispie Treats** (Sweet but more of a cereal-candy hybrid)  

### Fun & Unique:  
10. **Cheese Puffs** (For a cheesy, airy crunch)  
11. **Spiced Popcorn** (Butter-flavored or chili-lime)  
12. **Malt Balls** (Sweet, chewy, and nostalgic—like a candy-coated malt ball)  

### Pro Tips:  
- **Mix textures**: Pair crunchy (chips) with chewy (gummies) or soft (cookies).  
- **Balance flavors**: Add a savory item (pretzels) and a mildly sweet one (fruit snacks) to avoid monotony.  
- **Check labels**: Vending machines sometimes have hidden sugars—opt for "no added sugar" or "natural fruit flavors" where possible.
