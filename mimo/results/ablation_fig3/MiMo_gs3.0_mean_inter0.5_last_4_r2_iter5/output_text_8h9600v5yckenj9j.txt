For Halloween candy, you definitely want to grab the classics that almost everyone loves. The best options are things like chocolate bars, specifically Reese’s Cups, Snickers, 3 Musketeers, and Milky Way bars. Mini chocolate chips are also great. Gummies are a hit too—Sour Patch Kids, gummy worms, and gummy bears are always popular. You could also get some lollipops or hard candies like Taffy Pulls, Blow Pops, or classic candy canes. For something spooky, you can get the individually wrapped Halloween-themed candies like the pumpkins or ghost-shaped ones. Just make sure whatever you buy is allergen-friendly, free of artificial dye as you mentioned was needed, and you can always supplement with a bowl of candy canes or M&Ms that people can help themselves to!
