There are definitely a few great ways to explore Lockport's rich canal history.

Your best bet would be to visit the National Museum of the Great Lakes, which is located right in Lockport. It has extensive exhibits on the city's entire history centered around the Erie Canal. They also have a great audio guide that covers that spy rumor in detail.

If you're more of a hands-on learner, you could take a boat tour of the locks themselves. The Lockport Locks Association often offers tours where you can actually watch a boat being lifted over the escarpment, which gives you a real sense of how it works. And of course, just driving around the city, you can see many old canal-side buildings that have been converted into museums or shops, which is a great way to get a feel for the town's heritage.
