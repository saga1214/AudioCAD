### **Tokyo (4 Days + 2 Flex Days)**  
**Day 1: Arrival & Modern Landmarks**  
- **Morning**: Arrive at Narita/Haneda Airport → Hotel check-in (Shinjuku/Shibuya recommended)  
- **Afternoon**: Explore **Shibuya** (Scramble Crossing, Hachiko Statue)  
- **Evening**: Dinner at **Omoide Yokocho** (Shinjuku)  
- *Academic Prep*: Browse **teamLab Planets** (digital art museum) for slide design inspiration  

**Day 2: Imperial Culture & Art**  
- **Morning**: **Asakusa** (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Market)  
- **Afternoon**: **Ueno Park** (Tokyo National Museum of Art – great for coursework literacy content research)  
- **Evening**: **Ginza** (luxury shopping, design font/color scheme studies for slides)  

**Day 3: Nature & Day Trip Prep**  
- **Morning**: **Meiji Shrine** (Yoyogi Park)  
- **Afternoon**: **Shinjuku Gyoen Garden**  
- **Evening**: Explore **Golden Gai** (tiny bars)  
- *Design Prep*: Collect images/fonts from museums like **Mori Art Museum**  

**Day 4: Day Trip to Nikko** *(Rich Art History Focus)*  
- **Morning**: Shinkansen to Nikko (1.5 hrs)  
- **Highlights**: **Toshogu Shrine** (intricate雕刻), **Kegon Falls**, **Kegon Temple** (calligraphy influence)  
- **Evening**: Return to Tokyo → Dinner at **Izakaya** (try edamame art!*)  
- *Presentation Link*: Hokusuia used Nikko themes in ukiyo-e prints.  

**Days 5-6: Flex Days for Design & Workout**  
- **Morning**: Gym session  
- **Afternoon**:  
  - **Option A**: **Edo-Tokyo Museum** (traditional Japanese art/design)  
  ceramics studio in **Kiyosumi**  
  - **Option B**: **Akihabara** (calligraphy shops, anime art galleries)  
- **Evening**: Free time

### **Osaka + Kyoto/Nara (3 Days)**  
**Day 7: Kyoto & Nara**  
- **Morning**: Shinkansen to Kyoto (30 mins) → **Osaka Castle** (Kimono patterns!)  
- **Afternoon**: **Dotonbori** (street art, food culture) → **Shinkansen to Nara** (40 mins)  
- **Highlights**: **Todai-ji Temple** (Great Buddha’s ukiyo-e influence), **Nara Park** (deer)  
- **Evening**: Return to Osaka → **Universal Studios** (optional)  

**Day 8: Osaka Art & Departure**  
- **Morning**: **Osaka Museum of Art** (historical ceramics/kimono exhibits)  
- **Afternoon**: **Shitenno-ji Temple** (world’s oldest Buddhist temple) → Airport transfer  
- *Academic Tip*: Photograph **Osaka’s color schemes** (red lanterns, blue river) for slide palette.
