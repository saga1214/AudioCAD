Okay, Belize sounds like your home turf for amazing dives. Building on that, Costa Rica also fits right into the tropical diving zone, though the environments are very different. Costa Rica has Pacific coasts with volcanic rock formations and coral reefs, and Caribbean coasts similar to Belize with lots of marine life. So your debate last night becomes simpler: short fins for exploring Costa Rica's varied, often calmer Pacific sites and maneuvering in caves or rivers. Long fins are perfect if you plan many strong-current Caribbean dives or long out-to-cays trips.

Here’s a plan focusing on maximizing scuba diving in both locations:

1.  **Costa Rica Route (Pacific Coast Focus):**
    *   **Location:** Guanacaste Province (Pacific side).
    *   **Why:** Best Pacific diving in CR, known for volcanic rock formations, lava tubes, wreck dives, and decent coral health. Strong currents are less common than the Caribbean side.
    *   **Key Sites:** Playa Conchal (near Limón - coral gardens), Caño Island (resort only, incredible visibility, reef sharks, rays), Puntarenas (diver town), Santa Teresa (hippie vibe, good diving).
    *   **Fins Recommendation:** Start with **short fins** here. You'll appreciate the agility in lava tunnels, shallow reefs, and river/lake dives.
