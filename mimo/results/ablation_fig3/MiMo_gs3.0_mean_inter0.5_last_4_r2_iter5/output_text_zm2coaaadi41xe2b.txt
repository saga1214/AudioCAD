Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! Here are a few great places to check out:

First, there's Baolong Noodle in Little Elm. They’re known for their hand-pulled noodles and amazing Peking duck.

Next, there’s Kyochan Ramen in Richardson, which is a must for their spicy miso ramen.

Then there’s The Rice Boat in Plano, which serves up really flavorful Vietnamese pho and other noodle dishes.

And if you like Szechuan flavors, Lao Ban in Garland is a great choice.

As for protein bars, it depends on what you like. If you want something high in protein, consider Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard or GNC’s Core Power. If you prefer a more natural option, you could try RXBAR or Quest Nutrition. And if you like fruity flavors, look into Clif Builders or Recovery Bars.
