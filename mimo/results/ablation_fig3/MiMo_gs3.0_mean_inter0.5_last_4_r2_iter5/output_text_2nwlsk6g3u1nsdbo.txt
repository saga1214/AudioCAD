Here are the shrimp main courses you asked for earlier, with ingredients lists:

1.  **Spicy Shrimp Scampi:**
    *   **Ingredients:** 1 lb large shrimp (peeled & deveined), 4 tbsp olive oil, 4 cloves garlic (minced), 1/2 onion (finely chopped), 1/2 bell pepper (any color, diced), 1/2 cup heavy cream or full-fat coconut milk (for richness), 1/4 cup dry white wine or chicken broth (optional), 1 tsp dried oregano, 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat), salt and pepper to taste, fresh parsley (chopped, for garnish).

2.  **Spicy Shrimp Fra Diavolo:**
    *   **Ingredients:** 1 lb large shrimp (peeled & deveined), 1/2 cup olive oil, 4 cloves garlic (minced), 1/2 onion (finely chopped), 1/2 bell pepper (diced), 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes, 1/4 cup dry white wine or chicken broth, 1/4 cup red wine (optional), 1-2 tbsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat), 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp sugar (optional, to balance acidity), salt and pepper to taste, fresh parsley (chopped, for garnish).

3.  **Spicy Shrimp and Grits (Cajun Style):**
    *   **Ingredients:** 1 lb large shrimp (peeled & deveined), 1/4 cup olive oil or butter, 4 cloves garlic (minced), 1/2 onion (finely chopped), 1/2 bell pepper (diced), 1 tsp Cajun seasoning (adjust for heat), 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust for heat), 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp dried thyme, 1/4 cup chicken broth, 1 (8 oz) package quick-cooking whole wheat grits (or stone-ground grits if you have time), salt and pepper to taste, chopped green onions (for garnish).
