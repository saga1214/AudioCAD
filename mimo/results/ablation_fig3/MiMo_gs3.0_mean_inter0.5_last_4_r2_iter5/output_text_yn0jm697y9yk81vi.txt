Here's a consolidated list of your best recommendations, including where to buy them locally in Indianapolis without going online:

### Cutting Sheet Metal:
1. **Pair of Aviation Snips** (aviation snops)  
   - **For**: Thinner sheets + curves. Best for precision and cleanup.  
   - **Where to Buy**: The Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards, Tractor Supply Co., local Ace Hardware.  

2. **Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel**  
   - **For**: Thicker metal or power cutting. Versatile for straight/curved cuts.  
   - **Where to Buy**: The Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards, Tractor Supply Co.  

3. **Jigsaw with Metal-Cutting Blade**  
   - **For**: Thinner sheets, curves, or intricate cuts.  
   - **Where to Buy**: Home improvement stores (The Home Depot, Lowe’s, Menards), Tractor Supply Co.  

---

### Drywall Fasteners:
4. **1¼-Inch Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws**  
   - **Why Better**: Superior holding power vs. nails (less nail pops), self-drilling, and prevents backing out. Ideal for wood studs. Ring-shank nails are the metal equivalent for nails.  
   - **Where to Buy**: All recommended stores (Home Depot, Lowe’s, Ace Hardware, Tractor Supply Co.).  

---

### Insulation:
5. **Fiberglass Batts (Pre-Cut Rolls)**  
   - **For**: Most walls. Easy to fit between studs, moderate R-value.  
   - **Where to Buy**: The Home Depot, Lowe’s, Menards.  

6. **Mineral Wool (Rock Insulation)**  
   - **For**: Soundproofing, fire resistance, and superior moisture control.  
   - **Where to Buy**: Tractor Supply Co., The Home Depot, Lowe’s.  

7. **Spray Foam Insulation**  
   - **For**: Maximum air sealing + insulation. Best for irregular spaces or new builds.  
   - **Where to Buy**: The Home Depot, Lowe’s (professional installation recommended).  

---

### 🛒 **Best Store for All Supplies (Indianapolis Physical Stores)**:  
**The Home Depot** (Multiple locations in Indianapolis)  
- **Why**: Largest selection of all items listed (metal tools, drywall screws, insulation, nails). Staff can assist with product specs and installation tips.  

### Alternative Stores:  
- **Menards**: Great for grinder cut-off wheels and metal tools (often cheaper).  
- **Lowe’s**: Similar to Home Depot for drywall/insulation.  
- **Tractor Supply Co/Ace Hardware**: Ring-shank nails, mineral wool, and jigsaw blades.  

**Pro Tip**: For sheet metal tools, visit Home Depot’s metal section first. If Aviation snips aren’t available, angle grinders/jigsaws are more common. For drywall, fiberglass batts are the standard choice—opt for mineral wool if soundproofing is a priority.
