Yes, publishers **love** it when an author illustrator submits a complete package. In fact, it significantly increases your chances of success. The standard practice is for the author to write the words and the illustrator to create the pictures. The author would provide the query letter I mentioned, along with their manuscript and their illustrations. It's also common to provide a few sample spreads to showcase your style.

However, there are two very important things to keep in mind. First, you still must work with an agent. Publishers almost exclusively only work with agents. Second, you must clearly state in your query that you are the illustrator of the work and that you are seeking an agent to help you find a publisher. They will then help you decide if your specific skill set is what they represent.
