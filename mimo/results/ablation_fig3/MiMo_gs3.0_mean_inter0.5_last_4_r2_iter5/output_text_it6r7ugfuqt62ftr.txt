Of Northern California, Palm Springs immediately comes to mind for swimming warmth and diet-friendly swimming suits.

Here’s a full four to five day itinerary for Palm Springs.

For places to stay, you have great options. The Hotel Palmore is a fantastic mid-range choice, right on the main drag and with a great pool. If you're looking for something more luxurious, The Abbey is a historic resort with a beautiful pool and spa.

On your first day, you can arrive and settle in, then head to the pool for some relaxing time. For dinner, I'd suggest The Lazy Dog Restaurant and Bar. It's very accommodating for dietary needs and has a fun, casual atmosphere.

Day two is all about swimming! You absolutely must visit the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway. While the main ride is up high, the lower tram stop has a pool and hot tub, and the tram itself offers incredible views. Afterwards, you could explore the nearby Palm Springs State Park, which has beautiful hiking trails.

On day three, you could visit the vibrant downtown area of Palm Springs. It has great shops and restaurants. For lunch, you could try the iconic Salty Dog Cafe. Then, you could take a short drive to the nearby Coachella Valley to see the Salton Sea. It's a unique and surreal landscape, and there are places like Elmer's Restaurant where you can eat.

For your fourth day, you could take a day trip to the nearby Temecula Valley, which is Northern California but has a much warmer microclimate. You can visit the Temecula Winery for wine tasting, and they handle dietary needs well. For lunch, The Goat Park Kitchen is a lovely spot with a beautiful outdoor setting.

On your final day, you can enjoy a last swim at your hotel pool before heading home.

This itinerary gives you plenty of warm weather, swimming, and plenty of great food options! Have a wonderful trip
