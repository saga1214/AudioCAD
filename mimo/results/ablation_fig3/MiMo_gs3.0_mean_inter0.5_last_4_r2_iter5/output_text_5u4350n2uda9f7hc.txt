Both succulents and the bird's nest snake plant come in very small and lightweight pots. A single succulent pot, for instance, might weigh less than three pounds, and the snake plant is even lighter, probably around two pounds. They come with small pots, so you can easily carry them up five flights of stairs.

Since you'd like to bring them home with you, that's no problem at all. They'll be light enough for you to transport back.
