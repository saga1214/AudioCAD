Here's a full list of treats perfect for your corgi princess:  
1. **Frozen Bites**: Mashed pumpkin/sweet potato cut into rounds, frozen solid.  
2. **Peanut Butter Cookies**: Roll peanut butter (free xylitol!) in oats, bake until firm.  
3. **Cooked Chicken Carrots**: Thin carrot sticks or small pieces of baked chicken.  
4. **Carrot Sticks**: Plain or lightly steamed for extra softness.  
5. **Cheese Puffs**: Stuff mini cheddar buns with cream cheese, bake.  
6. **Rice Cakes**: Lightly toasted with a smear of peanut butter.  
7. **Mini Muffins**: Bake with dog-safe ingredients like blueberries or pumpkin.  
**Store-Bought Brands**: Blue Buffalo, Wellness, Zuke's Nibbles, Greenies Dental Chews, Merrick Training Bites, Royal Canin Small Training Treats.  
**Key Tips**:  
- **Portion Control**: Corgis can gain weight, so limit treats to 10% of her daily calories.  
- **Safety First**: Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions, xylitol, or garlic.  
- **Training**: Pair treats with commands like "sit" or "stay" for learning.  
Princess will adore these, but always supervise new treats to ensure she tolerates them well! 🐾
