### 🌟 **The Vegas Luxury Reset**

     - **Bellagio** (Fiori Lavasi Spa: rooftop garden access + soothing cedarswood massages).

2. **Dinner & Atmosphere**

     - **Joël Robuchon** (Michelin-starred French in MGM Grand—elegance + adventure contrast).

   - **Unique Vibes**:  
     - **Nobu** (fusion sushi + ocean views).  
     - **Eataly** (Italian market with rooftop terrace at The Cosmopolitan).

### 🗺️ **Route Suggestion: Desert → Nevada → Vegas**
- **Day 1–2**: **Joshua Tree**  
  - Drive to Anza-Borrego SP (1.5 hrs east) for stargazing (best in January).  
  - **Unique Stop**: Hidden Valley Trail (quiet, less crowded than Barker Dam).  
- **Day 3–4**: **Nevada Adventures**  
  - **Option A** (Art/Culture): Valley of Fire SP → Red Rock Canyon (sculptures + canyon hikes).  
  - **Option B** (Desert Wild): Black Rock Canyon (free BLM camping—check [BLM.gov](https://www.blm.gov) for alerts).  
- **Day 5–6**: **Vegas Luxury**  
  - Spa → Michelin dining → Cirque show → intimate bar.  

### ⚠️ **Key Nevada Notes**  
- **BLM Camping**: Free but *strictly* dispersed (no fires near structures, pack out trash).  
- **Safety**: Carry a satellite communicator (e.g., Garmin inReach) in desert areas.  
- **BLM Access**: Use apps like [BLM.gov](https://www.blm.gov) or [iOverlander](https://ioverlander.com) for legal spots.
