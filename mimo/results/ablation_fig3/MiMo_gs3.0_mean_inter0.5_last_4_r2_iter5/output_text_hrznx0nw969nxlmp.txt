That's a fantastic area for daytime adventures! Here are some great options near Secret Harbour and Cruz Bay on St. John:

### Beaches & Nature
1. **Snorkel at Secret Harbour Bay**: Right from your resort—calm, clear water with snorkeling spots just off the shore.  
2. **Anegada Trail**: Hike this coastal path from Cruz Dock to Anegada (a short ferry ride away). Stop at **Cinnamon Bay** for iconic turquoise waters and hidden coves.  
3. **Trunk Bay**: Accessible via a short walk from Cruz Bay. Famous for its underwater snorkeling trail with vibrant coral gardens.  

### Shopping & Culture
4. **Frenchtown Market**: Explore this historic village center every Tuesday and Saturday for crafts, local art, and food stalls.  
5. **Cruz Bay Shops**: Browse local boutiques like **Sundial Books** or **The Virgin Islands Museum** for souvenirs and history.  

### Food & Local Life
6. **Cruz Bay Fish Fry**: Check the calendar for Friday/Saturday evening fish fries (like **The Longboard**)—live music, food, and a lively local vibe.  
7. **Cruz Bay Café**: Grab a coffee or smoothie at this popular spot near Gallows Point for people-watching.  

### Boat-Optional Activities
8. **Kayak Tour**: Rent kayaks in Cruz Bay to paddle along mangroves or to nearby bays like **Salt River Bay**.  
9. **Kayak to St. Thomas**: Paddle across the shallow waters to Charlotte Amalie (about 45 mins)—great for a quick cultural shift!  

### Pro Tips:
- **Check Tides**: Secret Harbour is calmest at low tide.  
- **Snorkel Gear**: Rentals available at **Coral Bay Dive Shop** or **Red Hook Dive Center**.  
- **Pressure Safety**: Confirm boat stability systems before tours. Your brother can likely join shallow, stable tours (e.g., mangrove kayaking).
