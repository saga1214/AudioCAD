Sounds perfect! Pirates eat “hard stuff,” so pretzel rods can be masts. Sandwich rolls buried in containers work. Grapes instead of watermelon keep allergies safe.

Here’s a nautical and sand-proof food and drink shopping list broken down into a few course ideas.

For the first course, which can be breakfast-style, you could make mini slider sandwiches cut in half—theme them buried treasure—and pack them in treasure chest snack containers. Also, pack apple slices labeled as pirate jewels and grapes also called pirate jewels. Skip the watermelon.

For a snack course, goldfish crackers themed as treasure coins are great, along with veggie cubes or sticks you can dip in cheese cubes, calling that the “pirate’s dip.” Pirate ship masts would be the pretzel rods.

For drinks, individual juice boxes themed creatively with fun names and labels. Small water bottles also work.

Pack everything individually to seal it from the sand issue. Your son wearing his outfit will feel like a real pirate birthday king at the beach!
