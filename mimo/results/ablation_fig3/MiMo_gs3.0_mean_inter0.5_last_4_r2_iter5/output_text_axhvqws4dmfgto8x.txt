The single most important step for stir-fried noodles is to add your wooden spoon *after* the water has come to a full boil. You can tell it's a rolling boil when big, tall bubbles are breaking the surface.

Next, stir constantly. This prevents the pasta from sticking to the bottom of the pot where it can burn easily.

The biggest secret is to reserve a cup of the starchy pasta water before you drain the noodles. When you add the drained noodles to the pot, toss them in and add a splash of that water. It helps everything stick together beautifully.
