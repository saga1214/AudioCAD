You are absolutely right. Older comics from the 1940s and 50s are much rarer and often the most valuable.

Since Luke Cage is your guy, a legendary book is *Warrior Angel* number one from 1948. It's his absolute first-ever comic book appearance and is extremely rare. *Tales to Astonish* number forty-five is his first official cover appearance with Captain America. Also, *The Defenders Take Over* number one, from 1954, is their first official issue and his time starting there.

As for ten other highly sought-after vintage comics under five hundred dollars:

*Amazing Spider-Man* three hundred remains essential—it’s Venom everywhere. Another great key is *Sub-Mariner* number fifty-two, which was the last issue published by Timely Comics before Marvel was formed. *Tales to Astonish* number twenty-five is the first full appearance of the Hulk. *The Incredible Hulk* number one from 1962 is a cornerstone, though slightly older. *Strange Tales* number one hundred and ten is the first appearance of Doctor Strange. *The Fantastic Four* number one from 1961 is the start of it all. *The Silver Surfer* number one from 1966 is another key. *Black Panther* number one from 1966 is important, especially with the new movie. *Thor* number one from 1962 is iconic. And one more Luke Cage classic is *Power Man* number twenty-three, which is the first appearance of the character Puma.

Just a heads-up, you'll have to hunt for these vintage books, and their prices can vary a lot based on condition, but they are all smart, long-term investments.
