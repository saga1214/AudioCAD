Just to double-check, you've got a few other things you'll need. You'll definitely want a power drill and a set of screwdrivers, not just a hammer. Habitat cabinets usually have pre-drilled holes, so a drill is essential.

You'll also need some long shims, which are basically thin wooden blocks to get cabinets level. And a good pair of work gloves to protect your hands.

You might also consider a stud finder that beeps, which Sarah mentioned. It makes locating those wall studs even faster and easier.

You've got all the basics down, though. Good luck with the installation!
