Breaking it down task by task. Drywall supplies like compound, sandpaper, nail adhesive, and if molding is damaged, should probably run you between 20 and 40 dollars, depending on style. Cake ingredients all-purpose, sugar, baking powder, butter, eggs, milk, extract, sugar for frost, cocoa if needed, pans... that should be around 20 to 30 dollars. Decorations like balloons or printables for the card, maybe 5 to 15. All in, you're probably looking at a range of 45 to 85 dollars. If you’re buying name-brand primer or fancy pans, it could be a little more, but that’s a fair starting point.

I’d bring at least 50 to 60 dollars just to be safe!
