For activities, small experiences often make the best memories. Chartering a small boat for half a day so you can anchor in a quiet cove for a picnic would be great.

You could also combine what you love. Taking a cooking class focused on fresh seafood right on the docks connects your passion for food with the ocean setting.

Another idea is to combine a land and sea adventure, like renting bikes to ride along a scenic coastal path that leads right to a hidden beach.
