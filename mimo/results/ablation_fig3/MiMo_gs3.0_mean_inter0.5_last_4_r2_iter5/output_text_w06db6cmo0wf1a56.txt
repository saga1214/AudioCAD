Here’s a practical, low-mess shopping list for your theme park adventure, designed for grab-and-go freshness in coolers. Quantities are based on **10 dadds, 10 kids, plus drinks/snacks/lunch/dessert**:

---

### **DRINKS**  
*(Focus on hydration + minimal cleanup)*  
- **Water**: 30 large water bottles (2L each)  
- **Sugar-free Sodas**: 30 individual boxes (e.g., Coke Zero, Sprite Zero)  
- **Low-sugar Juice Boxes**: 15 boxes (for picky kids)  
- **Infused Water**: 5 bottles (add berries/cucumber for flavor)  
- **Ice Packs**: 3–4 large ones (to keep drinks cold)  

---

### **SNACKS**  
*(No plates/forks needed; mess-free grab-and-go)*  
- Cheese Sticks: 20 (10 dadds + 10 kids)  
- Baby Carrots: 1 large bag (pre-cut)  
- Hummus Cups: 10 individual cups  
- Trail Mix: 10 small bags (nuts, dried fruit, yogurt drops)  
- Fruit: 20 grapes or apple slices (pre-washed)  
- Energy Bars: 10 individually wrapped (e.g., KIND bars)  
- Pretzels: 10 small bags  

---

### **LUNCH**  
*(Pre-made portions = zero assembly required)*  
- **Calzones**: 10–12 mini calzones from local pizzeria or frozen store-bought (ensure reheatable).  
- **Wrap Bar**:  
  - Tortillas: 10–15  
  - Fillings: Hummus, turkey breast, spinach, and feta (pre-sliced)  
  - Toppings: Olives, cherry tomatoes, cucumbers (small-diced)  
- **Side**: 10 whole-grain pitas with single hummus cups  

---

### **DESSERT**  
*(Celebratory + easy sharing)*  
- **Mini Celebration Calzones**: 10 (fill with ricotta + berries or Nutella)  
- **Cupcakes**: 12–15 (grab-and-eat, no knife needed)  
  *Or*  
- **Pie Slices**: 10 slices of pre-sliced lemon meringue or key lime (no serving plate)  

---

### **ESSENTIALS**  
- **Coolers**: 2–3 large insulated ones + ice packs  
- **Utensils**: 10 reusable cups, 10 spatulas for drinks  
- **Extras**: Wet wipes, hand sanitizer, trash bags, napkins  

---

### **WHY THIS WORKS**  
- **Minimal Waste**: All items are pre-portioned; no plates/forks needed for most foods.  
- **Kid-Friendly**: Cheese, fruit, and mini calzones appeal to all kids.  
- **Adult Flexibility**: Sugar-free sodas + mini calzones cater to the bachelor’s love.  
 pack everything tightly in coolers, label containers, and assign 2–3 dads as "refrigerator monitors" to rotate ice packs. Prioritize foods that don’t need refrigeration after opening (like trail mix) to keep coolers fresh longer.
