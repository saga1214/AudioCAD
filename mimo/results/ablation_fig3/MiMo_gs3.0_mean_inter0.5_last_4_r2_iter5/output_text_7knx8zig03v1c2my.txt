That's great you're thinking carefully about your puppy's comfort and safety! Here's a breakdown:

### Treat Recommendations
*   **Best Flavors:** Look for **high-value, small, and digestible** options:
    *   **Chicken:** Especially freeze-dried or dehydrated (tiny pieces). Very popular.
    *   **Beef:** Similar to chicken, often freeze-dried. Great for training.
    *   ** Salmon:** Many dogs love it, and it's good for skin/coat.
    *   Paprika-coated kibble: Small pieces, low-calorie, often high-value.
    *   Cheese (small cubes): Like cheddar or mozzarella.
    Crate cozy ideas? **Snuggle beds:** Place a soft blanket or fleece "snuggle pad" inside the crate before he goes in. Start with the door open, let him choose to enter, and place treats inside. Gradually close the door for short periods with treats inside, praising him when he enters on his own. Never use it for punishment – only a happy retreat.
Potty mishaps? Accidents happen! **Humane punishments? NO.** Chasing or yelling confuses dogs. **Clean up calmly** with an enzymatic cleaner to remove smell. **Increase frequency** of potty breaks (after waking, eating, playing). **Celebrate RIGHT** when he goes outside. If escapes without fencing? **Call calmly with EXCITEMENT and TREATS/TOYS:** crouch low ("come! come! come!"), use happy tone, wave arms. Don't chase. Having a solid recall command ("Come!") trained makes recovery much easier. Practice recalls in safe, enclosed spaces first.
Clicker training? A **clicker** marks the exact moment he does the *right* thing (like sitting). You click *instantly* when he sits, then immediately give a treat. The click tells him "THAT was the behavior I wanted!" followed by the reward. Practice 5-10 minutes daily.
