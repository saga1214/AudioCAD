Okay, diesel camper vans definitely need a good pre-trip check. For brakes, a couple of easy signs of wear are if you hear any grinding or squeaking when you stop, or if the pedal feels soft. You can also check the tire pressure and tread depth, and look for any oil leaks on the ground. As for spark plugs on a diesel diesel, they’re a bit different—usually, they last longer than in a gas engine, often 100,000 miles or more. If they’re older, you can replace them yourself with a socket and wrench, but if you're not comfortable, a mechanic can do it in about an hour. Either way, it’s good to have someone take a look at things before you hit the road, just to be safe.
