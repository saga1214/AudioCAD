Yeah, before filming your first YouTube video as a volunteer puppeteer, just keep a few things in mind.

Before the show, ask for permission from the venue— hospitals, schools, nursing homes—to record and share the video. That’s a must.

Set up your phone or camera before you start the performance. Make sure it’s stable, maybe on a tripod, so it doesn’t shake. You can also ask an audience member or a friend to help film for you.

While you're performing, just do what you normally do—be natural, energetic, and have fun. Kids respond best to that.

After the show, transfer the video to your phone and check the audio and video quality. Then upload it to kid-friendly platforms with titles and descriptions that appeal to kids, like something catchy like Puppetry with Melvin or sock puppet magic skits.

Start small, be consistent, and have fun. You’ve got years of great performances, so you’ll do great.
