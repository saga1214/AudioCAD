Okay, so you want to get a new video game console to play with your daughter. That's a great idea.

Here are five of the most popular consoles right now:

First, there's the Nintendo Switch. It's great because you can play it on a TV or take it on the go, and it has a huge variety of games, especially for racing and adventure.

Second is the PlayStation 5. This is a very powerful console with amazing graphics and a lot of great single-player games.

Third, there's the Xbox Series X or S. It's another top-tier console with a strong online service and a big library of games.

Fourth is the Steam Deck. It's a little handheld computer that plays games from the Steam store, which has thousands of options.

And fifth, there are some really cool retro-style consoles like the Nintendo Switch Lite or the PlayStation Mini, which are great for bringing back that classic gaming feeling you were reminiscing about earlier.

For you and your daughter to play together, the Nintendo Switch is probably a great choice since it's designed for multiplayer fun. The PS5 or Xbox would also be excellent for a shared experience.
