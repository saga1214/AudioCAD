A hybrid approach—like a **park BBQ with parent help**—works great for end-of-year celebrations. Here’s how to make it fun, inclusive, and manageable:

### **1. Location & Setup**
- **Park BBQ**: Choose a nearby park with picnic tables/grill areas. Easy for parents to handle food prep while you focus on activities.
- **Cafeteria Alternative**: If budget/logistics are tight, a cafeteria party with decorations (streamers, Brownie-themed crafts) keeps it simple.

### **2. Food & Snacks**
- **Mix it up**: Offer sundaes (add dairy-free dip), pizza, and simple snacks like popcorn or veggie trays.  
- **Parent Help**: Assign parents to bring specific items (e.g., one brings drinks, another brings games) to share costs.

### **3. Activities**
- **Badge-Themed Games**: Host a **"Badge Olympics"** with low-effort, badge-earning activities:
  - **Nature Race**: Collect 10 pinecones, 5 smooth rocks, and 3 leaves (Community Helper Badge).
  - **Storytelling Relay**: Kids share fun facts about animals (Animals Badge).
  - **Sack Race or Wheelbarrow Race**: Classic, energetic fun (Outdoor Skills Badge).
- **Keep it Short**: 1–2 games max to avoid fatigue.

### **4. Budget & Costs**
- **Celebration Fee**: Charge $5–10/daughter to cover BBQ supplies (coolers, tables) or small prizes.  
- **Parent Contributions**: Ask parents to donate food/drinks to offset costs.

### **5. Safety & Logistics**
- **Pregnancy-Friendly**: Park BBQ lets you sit/stand as needed. Avoid heavy lifting.  
- **Weather Backup**: Plan for rain (tents in cafeteria) and keep snacks portable.

### **Why This Works**
- **Memorable**: Outdoor BBQ feels like a "trophy" moment.  
- **Low-Pressure**: Parents help with food/games, easing your seven-month-pregnant workload.  
- **Badge-Focused**: Games tie directly to achievements you’ve earned all year.  

Start small, celebrate their growth, and you’ll crush troop leader duty—even seven months pregnant! 😊
