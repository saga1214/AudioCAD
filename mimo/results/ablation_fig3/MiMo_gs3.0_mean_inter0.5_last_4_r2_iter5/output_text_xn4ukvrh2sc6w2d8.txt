Sure, I can put a full list together for you.

First up, Old Fashioned: You’ll need two ounces of bourbon like Maker’s Mark, one sugar cube or half an ounce of simple syrup, three or four dashes of Angostura bitters, ice, and an orange twist for garnish.

Paper Plane: Equal parts. So, one ounce of bourbon like Bulleit, half an ounce of Aperol, half an ounce of Nonino’s Amaro, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice.

Whiskey Sour: With or without the egg white. With it: one egg white, one and a half ounces of bourbon, half an ounce of lemon juice, and half anoint of simple syrup. Dry shake it first, add ice, shake again, and strain. Without it: just one and a a half ounces of bourbon, half an ounce of lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup.

And for the French 75: One and a half ounces of gin like Hendrick’s, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and you’ll top it with Champagne or Prosecco.

As for finger foods in Kentucky fall, simple things like spiced nuts, deviled eggs, or charcuterie with some aged cheddar would probably work great!
