</Acoustic guitars usually tune to the same set of notes: E, A, D, G, B, and then E again.

With either tool, you strum a string gently. The device app or clip will show when a note is sharp, which means it's a little high, or flat, which means it's a little low.

Then you just turn the tuning peg on your headstock. If the tuner says 'sharp,' you loosen the peg a tiny bit. If it says 'flat,' you tighten it. Keep making small adjustments until the tuner shows that note is perfectly in tune. Just repeat that for each string.
