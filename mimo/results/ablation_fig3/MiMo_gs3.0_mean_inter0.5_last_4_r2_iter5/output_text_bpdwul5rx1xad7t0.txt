Here are five fantastic road trips from Vancouver, each with a different kind of beauty.

First, there's the classic Sea to Sky Highway to Whistler. You'll get stunning ocean views and a chance to ride the gondola.

Second, you could head up to the Fraser Canyon to Lillooet. It's a very scenic drive and you can visit a historic gold rush town.

Third, for a quieter trip, drive to the Okanagan Valley. It's famous for its lakes and especially for its award-winning wineries. You can take a tour and enjoy a glass of wine.

Fourth, a very scenic route is the Coquouls Highway to Pender Harbour on Vancouver Island. It's surrounded by rainforests and you can visit a charming little village.

And fifth, you could take the scenic Highway 7 to the town of Osoyoos, which is on the US border and has a warm climate and a great winery.

All of these trips have wonderful, upscale dining options and beautiful scenery to enjoy.
