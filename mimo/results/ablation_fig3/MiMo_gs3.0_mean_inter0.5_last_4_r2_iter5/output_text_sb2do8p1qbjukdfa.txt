Okay, you want flavors and scents that are crisp, refreshing, pair well with citrus, and are light. Got it. Here are a few ideas.

For drinks beyond infused water and tea, maybe sparkling lemonade or some chilled coconut water.

Snacks could be things like sliced melon, grapes, or even chilled mochi—it's light and has a nice texture.

Palate cleansers... yeah, small mints or maybe those little popsicles that are cooling could work. Or even just a small bowl of fresh mint leaves, which adds a fresh scent.
