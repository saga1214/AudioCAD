You're right, it can be a little confusing. Let me clarify. Using bourbon rye whiskey gives you the one-and-a-quarter-to-one-to-one Boulevardier with richer whiskey notes. Using bourbon or rye plus equal parts of vermouth and Campari is also classic—that one creates a more balanced BoulevardIER. And for your Last Word, which is also great, you'd use three-quarters of an ounce of each: gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime. Just stir, strain, orange twist, and enjoy either one!
