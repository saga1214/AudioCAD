### 3. **Realistic Risk with Futures**  
Futures are contracts to buy/sell an asset (e.g., oil, gold, stock indices, currencies) at a predetermined price on a future date. Key traits:  
- **Leverage**: You only need a fraction of the asset’s value (e.g., deposit $2,000 to control $20,000 in oil futures). Gains/losses are amplified.  
- **Volatility**: Prices can swing wildly in days.  
- **Margin Requirements**: Brokers hold collateral, adding risk.  

**Example**:  
If you buy a $100/share futures contract (deposit $2,000 for $20,0 aiming for $110):  
- **Gain**: $10 → $2,200 profit → **110% return** (but you lose $2,000 margin).  
- **Loss**: $100 → $90 → $1,800 loss → **90% loss** of your margin.
