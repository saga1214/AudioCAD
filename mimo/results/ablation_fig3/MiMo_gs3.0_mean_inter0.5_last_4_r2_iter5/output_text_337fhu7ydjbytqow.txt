Here's your final, segment-specific packing list to keep you functional and comfortable through both sections:

### **For the 20-Mile Daytime Segment (Tomorrow Night, Aid Stations Available):**
1. **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking top and bottom to keep sweat off skin.  
2. **Mid-Layer:** Fleece or lightweight puffy vest/jacket for light warmth (removable).  
3. **Outer Layer:** Windproof/water-resistant shell (e.g., lightweight rain jacket).  
4. **Hands/Head:** Gloves + warm hat (fingerless gloves work well).  
5. **Footwear:** Aggressive-grip trail shoes/running shoes (broken-in!).  
6. **Light:** Headlamp or handheld light + **EXTRA BATTERIES** (critical!).  
7. **Transport:** Small running vest/hip pack to carry/remove layers as needed.  

### **For the Final 12-Mile Night Segment (No Aid Stations Nearby):**
1. **Base Layer:** Same as above (no changes).  
2. **Mid-Layer:** **Thicker insulation** (e.g., puffy jacket or fleece) – *essential for warmth*.  
3. **Outer Layer:** Stick with windshell or upgrade to a waterproof jacket.  
4. **Hands/Head:** Gloves + warm hat + buff/gaiter (extra warmth).  
5. **Footwear:** Same grip-focused shoes.  
6. **Light:** Headlamp/handheld + **BACKUP LIGHT/BATTERIES** (night running = colder + less visibility).  
7. **Insulation:** Lightweight insulated jacket (e.g., 50-70°F range) – *non-negotiable for nighttime*.  
8. **Transport:** Same vest/pack for layer swaps.  

### **Key Extras for Both Segments:**
- **Socks:** Extra pair (if not already packed).  
- **Layers Flexibility:** Bring mid/outer layers that zip/unzip or are packable (easier to adjust).  
- **Battery Check:** Test your light before leaving.  
- **Weather Check:** Mid-30s can dip to freezing at night – prioritize warmth for the last stretch.  

**Why this works:**  
- **Day 20 miles:** Minimize bulk (aid stations let you resupply; focus on mobility).  
- **Night 12 miles:** Prioritize warmth/visibility (aid stations may be scarce or cold).  
The vest/pack lets you swap layers seamlessly during aid stops. Stay light but prepared! 🏃‍♀️💨
