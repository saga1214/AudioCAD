It sounds like balancing big ambitions with life demands really comes down to smart, small planning.

Here’s a simple approach you could try.

First, assign each passion-trip a 'Priority Level,' for instance, Bolivia might be Priority 1 and Ferough Islands might be Priority 2 right now.

Then, create a specific monthly savings target directly linked to that trip, and automate the transfers.

For work demands like meetings piling up, try to negotiate shorter or fewer meetings where you can.

Finally, schedule just ten or fifteen minutes each week to work directly on one part of your ambition, like booking a Faroe Islands tour.
