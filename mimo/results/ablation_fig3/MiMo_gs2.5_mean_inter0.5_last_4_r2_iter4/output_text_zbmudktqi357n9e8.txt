Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The timing will really depend on what you’re looking for. 

If you want peak fall colors, you should go in late October through November. That’s when Mexico’s autumn foliage is most vibrant, especially in the highlands like Pátzcuaro or the Yucatán Peninsula.

If you’re more interested in the beach vibe but still want some fall energy, then early November could be a good balance. You’ll still get some greenery and warmth without the peak crowds.

Either way, you’ll have plenty of color to work with and great inspiration for your paintings. Safe travels, and happy painting!
