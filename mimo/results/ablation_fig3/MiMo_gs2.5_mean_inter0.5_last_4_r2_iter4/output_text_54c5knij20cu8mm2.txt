Alright, you've got some conflicting desires—objective measurement versus creativity, structure versus fluidity. Let’s break this down.

The left-brain versus right-brain idea is mostly a myth. The brain doesn’t split functions so neatly. However, people who are highly creative often use different cognitive strategies. For example, a creative person might use inductive reasoning—drawing general ideas from specific observations—while someone focused on concrete answers might rely more on deductive reasoning. But it’s not a fixed left versus right thing. Everyone uses both.

Now for careers: If you want something that’s objective but creative and not desk-bound, here are a few ideas. Architecture or industrial design can be very structured and measured, but also highly creative. Graphic design or data visualization blends creativity with concrete data. Even something like scientific illustration can appeal to both sides. You could also consider filmmaking or animation, where you structure narratives but measure outcomes through engagement or impact.
