Alright, here’s a full cocktail menu for your Art Deco airport reception in Kentucky fall theme, with all the classic recipes and directions laid out.

First, Old Fashioned: You'll need two ounces of bourbon or rye, one sugar cube—or if you prefer, half an ounce of simple syrup. Add two to three dashes of Angostura bitters, stir it all together until the sugar is dissolved, and serve it over ice in a rocks glass. You can garnish it with an orange twist or an orange slice.

Paper Plane: Equal parts. That’s two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce Aperol, half an ounce Amaro Nonino, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. You're going to shake that with ice, strain it into a coupe glass, and that's it.

Next, Whiskey Sour. If you decide to use the raw egg white, here’s how: Add one raw egg white, half an ounce of lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup to your shaker. First, a dry shake—that's without ice—to foam the egg white. Then add the ice, shake again, and strain into a rocks glass. Garnish with a cherry or orange slice. If you leave the egg white out, just shake the bourbon, lemon, and syrup with ice and strain it into a rocks glass.

Then, the French 75. You'll take one ounce of gin, half an ounce of lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. Dry shake those three first, add ice and shake again, then strain into a champagne flute and top it with champagne or prosecco.

As a bonus fourth option, since you're at an Art Deco location, I’d also recommend a Gin Rickey. For that, you'll need two ounces of gin, half an ounce Aperol, three-quarters of an ounce fresh lime juice, and two dashes of Angostura bitters. Shake that with ice and serve it over ice in a rocks glass.
