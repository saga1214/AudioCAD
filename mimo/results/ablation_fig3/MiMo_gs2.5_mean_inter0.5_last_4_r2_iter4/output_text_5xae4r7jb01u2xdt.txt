Under high school rules, red team goalkeeper number one kicks the ball outside the goal area for a goal kick. The ball is then played directly to blue team player number seven, who scores.

No offside offense.

This is one of the standard restart exceptions we mentioned, where a player cannot be offside when receiving the ball directly from a goal kick. High school and college rules are equal here.

Now, if we were talking about F A A professional soccer rules, they have a key difference. In that rulebook, a goalkeeper can only play the ball outside their goal area with a hand if it's a deliberate attempt to play the ball. If the ball just deflects or rebounds off the keeper's hand or body, the offside rule is still in effect. So, in that specific case, the blue player might have been called for offside.
