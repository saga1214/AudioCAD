For a classic Thanksgiving pie, a simple pumpkin pie is a great choice. It just requires eggs, sugar, pumpkin puree, and spices.

If you want to avoid baking the pie at home first, a no-bake pie is another fantastic option. Many recipes use a pre-made crust and a simple custard filling that you just assemble and transport.

Both are relatively easy to make with limited kitchen supplies and are sure to make you a 'legend' at the family gathering.
