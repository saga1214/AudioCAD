Let’s break down the drywall items first. Joint compound, sandpaper/sponge, nails/adhesive caulk gun should probably run you around $15 to $25. Primer and paint, depending on the size canal cost around $20 to $30. A replacement piece of molding, if you need it, could be another $15.

Cake items are generally cheaper. All-purpose flour, sugar, baking powder, butter, eggs, milk, vanilla, powdered sugar for frosting, all that should be under $20.

Decorations like balloons or a simple set might be around $10.

So, let’s add it all up. Drywall: $40 to $55. Cake: $20. Decorations: $10. That brings the total to somewhere between $70 and $90. I’d say it’s a good idea to have about $85 on hand to be safe for any extra items you might need.
