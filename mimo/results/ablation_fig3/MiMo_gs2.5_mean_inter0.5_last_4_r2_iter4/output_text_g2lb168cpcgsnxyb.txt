While building his trust is still the foundation, the single most important approach you need to implement early on is safety and managing his antecedents—that is, what happens before the behavior.

With elopement, aggression, dogs running around, and a cluttered space, you need to create structure right away.

For safety, try to establish a clear session space if you can, control the dogs, and have a safe 'calm down' corner. To prevent elopement, make it clear when the session starts and ends, and never leave him alone.

By managing these environmental factors at the beginning of your session, you can proactively reduce the triggers for his challenging behaviors.
