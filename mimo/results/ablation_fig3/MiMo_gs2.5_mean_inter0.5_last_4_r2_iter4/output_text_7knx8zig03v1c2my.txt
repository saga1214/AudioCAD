### 🍬 Treat Recommendations:
- **Best flavors**: Liver, chicken, beef, or cheese (especially if Italian roots lean in—try mild cheddar sticks!). Freeze-dried options are great for training.
- **Size**: Small pieces for Galileo’s tiny mouth (3 lbs).
- **Tip**: Use high-value treats (like cheese or hot dogs) for difficult tasks.

### 🎯 Clicker Training Explained:
1. **The clicker**: Acts like a "camera shutter" to mark the exact moment Galileo does what you want.
2. **How it works**:
   - **Step 1**: Click when he sits (even imperfectly).
   - **Step 2**: Immediately give a treat.
   - **Step 3**: Repeat until he learns to sit on cue.
3. **Why it works**: The click predicts the treat, creating a strong association between the behavior and reward.

> 💡 **Science nerd alternative**: Use a verbal marker like "Yes!" instead of a clicker—same principle!

### Recall Training if He Escapes:
Stay calm and use **excited calls + high-value rewards**:
1. Crouch down, open arms wide, and call: "Galileo! Come, come, come!"
2. If he hesitates, toss treats/chicken bits toward the house.
3. **Never chase**—it turns it into a game. Always reward when he returns.

Training takes patience, but Galileo’s energy is an advantage! With science names, cozy crates, and positive reinforcement, you’ll build a strong bond. 🐾
