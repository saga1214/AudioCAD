That idea about the pigeons is interesting—it's all about smartly distributing resources.

You could apply a similar idea to your ambitions by categorizing each goal by its timeline.

For 'short-term' savings, like home renovation, focus on small, automatic monthly contributions whenever you get paid. Pair this with cutting out a few small, recurring expenses.

For trips that are a bit further off, like Bolivia or the Faroes, start by doing just 30 to 60 minutes of detailed research each week. Then, create a simple spreadsheet to estimate the total cost and see how much you'd need to save each month.

Finally, always schedule short blocks of time for planning—maybe right after work meetings end—so you can tackle these big projects without feeling overwhelmed by work exhaustion
