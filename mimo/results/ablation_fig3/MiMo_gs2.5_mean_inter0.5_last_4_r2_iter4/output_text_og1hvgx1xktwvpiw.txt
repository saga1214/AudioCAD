You're right, I see where the confusion came from. Let me clarify. The truly original Boulevardier started with bourbon/rye, one ounce of sweet vermouth, and one ounce of Campari. That's your non-equal-parts ratio. The equal-parts version, mixing one ounce of each, is a very popular and classic variation that came later. 

As for the Last Word you mentioned, that's an excellent choice. Its proportions are three-quarters of an ounce for each of these three ingredients: three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. Then you just stir and garnish with a lemon twist. Hope that helps you decide!
