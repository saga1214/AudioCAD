Absolutely, Lockport has several excellent resources for visitors.

The main hub is the Canal Museum, which is located right next to the Locks. It has a great exhibit on Japanese espionage specifically and covers everything from the construction days to the canal's role in the modern economy. You can also walk right over to the "Flight of Five" locks to see them for yourself. And if you want to see the locks from above, the Lockport Tower offers a great aerial view.
