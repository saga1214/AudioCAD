A structured plan is definitely the best way to build skill without burning out. Here’s a sample weekly schedule.

You could start each day with about five minutes of beanbag one-hand drills with your dominant hand. Then, warm up with tennis balls for another five minutes, practicing a simple pattern. After that, spend about ten minutes with scarves to slow things down and perfect your pattern focus. Finally, dedicate about ten minutes to transitioning with three beanbags.

Repeat that for four days of the week. On the other two days, you can mix things up. For example, you could do a day focused on music, or a day where you just practice catching tennis balls. Just remember to be patient and not stress. Every failed throw is just a step closer to catching. You've promised your son, which is a wonderful motivator, and you will figure this out.
