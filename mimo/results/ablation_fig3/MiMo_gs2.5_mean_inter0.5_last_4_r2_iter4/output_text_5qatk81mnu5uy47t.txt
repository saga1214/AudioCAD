### 🌴 **Your Ideal Costa Rican Itinerary**

2. **Top Tropical Dive Sites**:  
   - **Caribbean Coast**:  
     - **Cahuita National Park**: Calm, biodiverse reefs (turtles, rays). Ideal for beginners.  
     - **Playa Conchal**: Beachfront dives with coral gardens and whale sharks (Dec-Apr).  
     - **Puntarenas**: Famous for **shipwrecks** (like the *SS Presidente*).  
   - **Pacific Coast**:  
     - **Tortuguero**: Mangrove forests, sea turtles ( nesting season: July-Aug).  
     - **Malpelo Island** (advanced): Remote, big-coral walls & sharks (book a liveaboard).  

3. **Unique Additions**:  
   - **Cenotes**: Visit **Cenote Azul** (Guanacaste) for crystal-clear freshwater diving with stalactites.  
   - **Whale Sharks**: Join a boat tour (Dec-Apr) for sightings near Playa Conchal.

- **Certification**: If new to Costa Rica, book a PADI course at **Dive Costa Rica** (San José).  
- **Gear**: Pack short fins + a backup mask/snorkel. Use a dive computer.  
- **Local Guides**: Hire certified guides (e.g., **Costa Rica Dive Center**) for safety and insights.
