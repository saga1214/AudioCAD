This is fantastic! Illustrating your own picture book is a huge advantage. However, publishers are typically looking to pair an **original** writer with an **original** illustrator. They don't usually publish a writer's own illustrations unless it's a very specific niche, like a self-published author.

So, the best approach would be to treat it like two separate projects. First, you'll write the manuscript and query literary agents or publishers who specialize in picture books. Second, you'll develop your illustrations and find a publisher or a small press that specifically looks for illustrated manuscripts. You can also query children's book publishers directly, since many accept manuscripts and illustrations from single illustrators who are also writers.
