Alright, since Starbucks keeps tempting you, here are five alternative drinks and five snacks. 

For drinks, you could try a matcha latte, which gives you energy without too much caffeine. A turmeric latte is good for inflammation. A green tea with lemon is simple and refreshing. A chia pudding is a healthy, creamy option. And a warm ginger tea is soothing and good for digestion.

For snacks, you could have some almond butter with banana, or avocado toast. Dark chocolate with nuts is another good one. Edamame with sea salt is a crunchy and protein-rich choice. Hummus with veggies is a fresh and healthy mix. And Greek yogurt with berries is light and full of protein.

There you go, hopefully that helps with your energy needs!
