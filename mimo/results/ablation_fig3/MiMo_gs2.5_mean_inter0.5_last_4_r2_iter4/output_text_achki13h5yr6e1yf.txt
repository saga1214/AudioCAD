Chicken cutlets require two pounds of chicken breasts for eight to fifteen dollars; all-purpose flour at three to five dollars; twelve eggs for three to seven dollars; breadcrumbs at two to four dollars; and vegetable oil at four to eight dollars. Salt and pepper is three to six dollars if needed.

For garlic mashed potatoes, you'll need about five pounds of potatoes for three to six dollars; a head of garlic for a dollar; a pound of butter for four to seven dollars; and a half-gallon of milk for three to five dollars. In total, you should expect to spend between forty and seventy dollars for everyone.
