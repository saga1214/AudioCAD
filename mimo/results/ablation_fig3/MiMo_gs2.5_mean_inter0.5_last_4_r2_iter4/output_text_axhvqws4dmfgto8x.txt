Stir-fried noodles are even simpler than tacos. The absolute key is cold water. You have to add cold water to the pot, never wood spoons!

Also, stir-frying itself is forgiving because you can dump everything in the pan and stir it around. You can't really burn most ingredients, so if something browns too quickly, you just add more liquid.

My best tip is to buy the pre-cooked noodles and just have to heat them up at the end. You can't go wrong with that
