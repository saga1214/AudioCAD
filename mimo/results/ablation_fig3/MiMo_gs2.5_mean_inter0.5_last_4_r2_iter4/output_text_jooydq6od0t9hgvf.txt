For the vodka, you could do a simple mix with fresh lime juice, a touch of simple syrup, and maybe muddle a few berries in the glass before adding ice. That’s refreshing and quick. For the Argentine Fernet, you can do a Fernet and soda water with a lime wedge. If you want to jazz it up, add a dash of bitters. Both are light but still refined enough for your formal vibe.
