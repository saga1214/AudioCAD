Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots. If you're craving good Asian noodles in Dallas, Texas, I'd suggest checking out a few places. For ramen, you could try Tonkatsu House or Ichiran. If you're looking for a more casual spot, there's also Lucky Boy, which is a local favorite. For something more upscale, check out Sushi Gen. As for protein bars, it depends on your taste. You could try Quest, which are high protein and low sugar. Or maybe RXBAR, which are made with whole food ingredients. Or if you like chocolatey ones, think about Clif Bars or RXBAR. Hope you enjoy your meal!
