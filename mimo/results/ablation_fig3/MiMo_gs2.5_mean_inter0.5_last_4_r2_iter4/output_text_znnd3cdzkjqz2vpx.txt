You've got all the essentials down, but you're missing a few crucial things.

First, you'll definitely need a power drill and some wall anchors. Habitat Restores usually include these, but it's good to have them ready. Stud finders are also essential for hanging those upper cabinets first.

For tools, a stud finder is key. A level is absolutely necessary for everything. You'll need a power drill with the right bits for screws and anchors. A hammer or mallet is for tapping things into place. And a measuring tape will be super helpful for making sure everything lines up perfectly.

Most importantly, make sure you have a safety first-aid kit handy just in case of any little accidents. And please, wear safety glasses to protect your eyes from any dust or debris.
