Got those traits in mind? Fine. Breeds to consider: First, Labrador Retrievers are friendly, trainable, and great with kids and other dogs. Next, Golden Retrievers are similar—they’re gentle, social, and easygoing. Another option is Cavalier King Charles Spaniels, who are small, affectionate, and less likely to chase squirrels. Another one is the Basset Hound; they’re laid-back and chill. Also, Boxers are playful but loyal and family-oriented. If purebreds aren’t the goal, mixed-breed dogs at a local shelter might have similar traits.
