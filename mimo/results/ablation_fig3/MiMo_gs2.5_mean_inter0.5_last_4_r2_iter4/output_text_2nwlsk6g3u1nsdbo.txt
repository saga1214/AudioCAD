Here are the three spicy shrimp dishes you asked for earlier:

1.  **Spicy Shrimp Scampi:**
    *   **Ingredients:** 1 lb large shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails on or off), 8 oz linguine or spaghetti, 4 tbsp butter, 4 tbsp olive oil, 4-6 garlic cloves (minced), 1/2 cup dry white wine (optional), 1 cup heavy cream (or half-and-half for lighter), 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice, 1/4 cup fresh lemon zest (for garnish), 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley, Salt and pepper to taste.

2.  **Shrimp Fra Diavolo:**
    *   **Ingredients:** 1 lb large shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails on or off), 8 oz linguine or spaghetti, 4 tbsp olive oil, 4 tbsp butter, 4-6 garlic cloves (minced), 1/2 cup dry white wine (optional), 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes, 1/4 cup red pepper flakes (adjust to desired heat), 1 tsp dried oregano, Salt and pepper to taste, 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley (for garnish).

3.  **Cajun Shrimp and Grits:**
    *   **Ingredients:** 1 lb large shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails on), 1 cup quick-cooking grits (or stone-ground), 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth, 2 tbsp butter, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1/2 medium onion (diced), 1 bell pepper (any color, diced), 2 cloves garlic (minced), 2-3 tbsp Cajun seasoning (adjust heat), 1/2 cup heavy cream (optional, for richness), Salt and pepper to taste, Fresh chopped parsley or green onions (for garnish).

**Tips for Takeout:**
*   Cook shrimp dishes partially, cool completely, then transport. Grits might need reheating slightly when they arrive.
*   Mac and Cheese or Scampi are easiest to transport fully cooked.
*   Include any garnishes separately (like extra Cajun seasoning, lemon wedges, parsley).
