Okay, so you want some classic cars that are great investments, especially in Arizona? I can definitely give you a few. Here are some solid options that tend to appreciate well.

First, there's the Ford Mustang. It’s an American icon, a classic muscle car. People love them. The first-generation '65 to '66 models are super desirable, especially if it's a 'hemi' version. The second and third generations, from '70 to '79, are also popular and can be great investments if you get a good one.

Next, the Chevrolet Camaro. It's like the Mustang's rival. The first-gen '67 to '72 models are highly sought after. The second and third gens, from '73 to '82, are also good choices, especially the SS models, because they're more affordable to buy.

Then there's the Dodge Charger. A true muscle car legend. The first and second gens, from '66 to '78, are the ones to look for, especially the 'hemi' models. The third gen, from '79 to '84, is a bit less valuable, but still a cool car.

Another one is the Pontiac GTO. It's known for its powerful engine, the 'hemi' V8. The first and second gens, from '64 to '69, are highly collectible. The third gen, from '70 to '74, is less valuable, but still a good option.

Finally, there's the Maserati Ghibli. It's a luxury sports car from Italy. The original one, from 1974 to 1998, is a great investment if you can find one in good shape. They're rare and hold their value really well.

So, those are five solid options. The key is to find one in good condition and maybe even get it restored, because that really increases the value. And of course, Arizona is perfect for classic cars since they don't rust away in the desert. Just make sure to do your research on the market before you buy.
