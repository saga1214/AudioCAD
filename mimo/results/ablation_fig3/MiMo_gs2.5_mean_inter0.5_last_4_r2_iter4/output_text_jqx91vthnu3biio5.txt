### 🧠 Quick Tips for Your Productivity Experimentation:
- **Color-coding icons/files:** Start with 2 colors max (e.g., blue = work, green = personal). It boosts visual recall *without* clutter if colors aren’t overloaded.  
- **Sound-switching:** Try every **75–90 minutes** instead of hourly—long enough to maintain flow but refreshing. If ambient nature sounds feel too jarring, stick with one lo-fi loop.  
- **Physical documents:** Weekend mindset > today. Decluttering feels rewarding fresh-start energy. Set a 60-minute timer for focused filing!  
- **Break timers:** Pomodoro (25-min work/5-min break) boosts focus for many—but experiment: some thrive with longer breaks (e.g., 50/10). Move your body during breaks!
