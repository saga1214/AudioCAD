Got Bruno Mars handled and darkness avoided. Apples, bananas, and broccoli sensitivity noted and skipped.

Now for safe green veggies:

Kale is a great source of vitamins A and K.

Collard greens are a good, nutrient-rich choice.

Swiss chard is another one packed with minerals and fiber.

Turnip greens are nutritious and Bruno should be able to handle them.

Mustard greens are also a good option, just remember to remove any tough stems.

So, five safe green options for your parrot are: Kale, Collard Greens, Swiss Chard, Turnip Greens, and Mustard Greens.
