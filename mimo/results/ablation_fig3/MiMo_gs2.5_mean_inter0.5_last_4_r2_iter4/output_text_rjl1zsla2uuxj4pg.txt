Here's a quick reminder of the essential items we discussed, plus a few additions based on your progress:

### Cat Bed (Yes, you do need one! 🛏️)
- **Choose based on your cat's personality**:  
  - **Raised beds**: Great for older cats or those who need extra support.  
  - **Plush, soft beds**: Ideal for kittens or cats who love curling up.  
  - **Orthopedic options**: For senior cats with joint issues.  
- **Tip**: Place it in a quiet, low-traffic corner so your cat feels safe.

### Other Essentials You Might Need:
1. **Food Bowls & Water Bowls**:  
   - Use **wide, shallow bowls** (avoid deep ones—cats hate dipping their paws).  
   - Stainless steel or ceramic are best (avoid plastic, which can harbor bacteria).  
   - Consider an **automatic water fountain** for hydration.  

2. **Scratching Posts**:  
   - **Crucial for dental health and claw maintenance**.  
   - Offer vertical posts (for climbing) and horizontal pads (for lounging).  

3. **Grooming Tools**:  
   - **Brush**: Slicker brush or rubber grooming glove (2–3 times/week).  
   - **Nail clippers**: For regular trims.  
   - **Wet/dry food**: Transition slowly if switching from dry-only.  

4. **Safety & Enrichment**:  
   - **Window perches** (cats love bird-watching!).  
   - **Hiding spots** (cardboard boxes or tunnels reduce stress).  
   - **Cat-proofing**: Secure wires, toxic plants (e.g., lilies), and small objects.  

### Quick Safety Tip:
Always check that any new bed or toy is **non-toxic** and **chew-resistant**—especially if your cat is a nibbler!
