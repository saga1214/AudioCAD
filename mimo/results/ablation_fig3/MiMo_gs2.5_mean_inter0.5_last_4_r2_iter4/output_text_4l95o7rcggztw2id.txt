Alright, pirate nautical beach party food—sand-free friendly and allergy-safe! Got it. Here’s a themed snack attack shopping list for seven kids total (host son + guests), avoiding watermelon per allergy need. Everything easy-handling = no sandy disasters.

First, for themed main items, called 'Buried Treasure Sliders', you'll need about 12 mini slider muffin tin-style rolls. You can fill them with whatever his sandwich of choice is, like PB&J pirate flag sandwiches, ham and cheese, or turkey and cheese. Just be sure to cut them before serving into small pirate-sized portions. For themed fruit jewels, you'll want grapes, apple slices—maybe cut into ship masts—and goldfish crackers. Pack these in pirate chest snack containers or small bento boxes labeled as 'Pirate Jewels'. For veggie morsels, get carrot sticks and cucumber sticks, and pack those in individual containers themed as 'Pirate Ship Masts'. For other easy pirate snacks, you could get pretzel rods, themed as ship masts, and mini fruit snacks. For drinks, individual juice boxes you can name 'Pirate Punch' or 'Sea Water', and small water bottles. And of course, his cake!

Here's a quick rundown of why this works: Mini sliders stay put. Grapes and crackers are sand-proof. Juice boxes seal spills. The pirate theme runs through the names and packaging. Goldfish crackers are allergy-safe, just be sure to check the label for watermelon. And snack containers seal everything away from the sand. Have a blast with the pirate outfit!
