You are absolutely right that older comics from the 1940s and 50s are generally far rarer.

Here are ten essential, high-value books from that era, all under the five-hundred-dollar price point.

Number one is *Action Comics* number one again, but for affordability, you're looking at a 'Good' to 'Very Good' grade copy, which can be found in your range. Superman's first full appearance.
Number two is *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen from 1959. Spidey's first appearance, though finding a cheap copy in good condition can be tough.
Number three is *Fantastic Four* number one from 1961, Reed Richards' and Susan Storm's debut, and it's still a strong key.
Number four is *The Incredible Hulk* number one, from 1962. It's Stan Lee and Jack Kirby's first appearance of the Hulk.
Number five is *Captain Marvel* number one, from 1940. This is Shazam's first appearance.
Number six is *The Amazing Spider-Man* number fifty from 1966. It’s the first team-up of Spider-Man and Hulk.
Number seven is *Fantastic Four* number forty-nine from 1966. This is the first appearance of Galactus, who is a huge cosmic character.
Number eight is *The Avengers* number one, from 1963. This is the first official team-up issue.
Number nine is *Captain America Comics* number one again, from 1941, but you'd be looking at a lower-grade copy for it to be in that range.
And number ten is *The Silver Surfer* number one, from 1966, which is the first appearance of the character.
