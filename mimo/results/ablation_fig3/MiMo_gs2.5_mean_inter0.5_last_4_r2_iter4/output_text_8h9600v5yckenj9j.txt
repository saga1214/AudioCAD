For Halloween candy, you can't go wrong with the classics. Things like Reese’s Cups, Snickers bars, Hershey’s Kisses, and 3 Musketeers are always popular. You could also grab some gummy worms, gummy bears, or ghost-shaped candy. And of course, you can't forget the old-school favorites like candy corn and fun-size Milky Way bars. Buying the small, individual packs is a good idea because guests can take a few without taking too much. If you want to be extra spooky, you could get some Halloween-themed versions of these candies. Just make sure to check the allergens again, as some candies might have nuts or artificial dyes you mentioned earlier.
