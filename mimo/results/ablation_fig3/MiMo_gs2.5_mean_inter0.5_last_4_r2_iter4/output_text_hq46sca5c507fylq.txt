### For Woodworking:
1. **Entry-Level Tool Kit**: A basic set (e.g., hand plane, chisel, mallet) from brands like Veritas or Stanley.  
2. **Wooden Project Kit**: Pre-cut wood for a beginner-friendly project (e.g., cutting board, birdhouse).  
3. **Woodworking Plans**: A beautiful, step-by-step book for a project they’d love (e.g., stool, shelf).  
4. **Safety Gear**: A quality respirator mask or sanding gloves.  
5. **Leatherworking Tools**: If they lean toward leather crafts (e.g., stamps, veg-tan leather scraps).  

### For Clay/Pottery/Sculpting:
1. **Pottery Kit**: Air-dry clay or stoneware clay with basic tools (wire tools, rib tools).  
2. **Sculpting Clay**: High-quality polymer clay (e.g., Sculpey) for miniatures or figurines.  
3. **Personalized Clay Mold**: A custom mold (e.g., for jewelry or decorative items).  
4. **Glaze or Pigment Set**: For hand-painted pottery or sculpting.  
5. **Workshop Voucher**: A local class to learn wheel-throwing or advanced techniques.  

### Universal Thoughtful Ideas:
- **Custom Tools**: Engraved scissors, chisels, or shears with their initials.  
- **Experience Gift**: A weekend workshop for pottery, woodworking, or leathercraft.  
- **Curated Project Box**: A themed kit (e.g., "Beginner Tailoring" with fabric, buttons, and a pattern).  
- **Quality Journals**: For sketching ideas or documenting projects.  

**Key Tip**: Avoid overly expensive tools early on (unless they’re a pro!). Focus on **quality materials** or **experience-based gifts**—these often mean more than gadgets. Since they enjoy vintage pens, gifts that echo that nostalgia (like vintage-inspired supplies) could resonate deeply.
