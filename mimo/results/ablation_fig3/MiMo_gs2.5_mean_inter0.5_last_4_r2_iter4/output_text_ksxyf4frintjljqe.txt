Now, for your Thanksgiving dessert idea... a chocolate idea that's easy in one pan sounds like a wonderful alternative. How about chocolate pudding pie? It's rich and comforting. For the filling, you'd just need cocoa powder, sugar, milk, eggs, butter, and a pinch of salt. Pour that over some melted butter that's in the bottom of a pie crust, and bake it at 350 for about 40 minutes. Once it's cooled, you can top it with whipped cream or some chocolate shavings. It’s a comforting treat that won't bring back those painful memories.
