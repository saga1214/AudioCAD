For your old diesel camper van, pre-trip maintenance is crucial. Here's a breakdown of key checks and steps:

### **Brake Inspection**
- **Visual Inspection**: Look for uneven pad wear (one side much thinner than the other), excessive dust (dark blue/green indicates rust), or cracks on rotors. Squealing when cold that disappears when warm is normal.
- **Signs of Wear**: Grooves on rotors, cracks, or material loss beyond 3mm requires replacement. Uneven wear suggests misalignment or failing components.
- **Action**: Use a mechanic’s mirror to inspect pads. For rotors, check thickness with a micrometer (replace if < 0.50 inches).

### **Spark Plug Replacement (Diesel Engines)**
1. **Tools Needed**: 19mm socket, torque wrench, spark plug socket with rubber tip, valve cover removal tools.
2. **Steps**:
   - Park on a level surface, let engine cool.
   - Remove valve cover bolts and lift cover.
   - Inspect plugs: Look for fouling, excessive wear, or oil residue.
   - Remove plugs using a spark plug socket (gently, no prying).
   - Clean threads with a brush and apply dielectric grease.
   - Install new plugs (torque to spec—usually 25-35 ft-lbs for diesel).
   - Reinstall valve cover and bolts.

### **Other Critical Diesel Checks**
- **Oil & Filters**: Check level/condition (diesel is prone to sludge). Replace oil every 5k miles and fuel filter every 15k miles.
- **Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF)**: If equipped, ensure it’s not clogged (monitor via OBD-II scanner).
- **Fluids**: Brake fluid (absorb moisture), coolant, and transmission fluid.
- **Tires**: Pressure, tread depth, and alignment.
- **Battery**: Clean terminals and check voltage (12.6V+).

### **When to Seek Professional Help**
- If brakes have uneven wear, grooves, or noise.
- If spark plugs show severe carbon buildup or oil contamination.
- If the van struggles at hills or idles roughly.

**Pro Tip**: Use a mechanic’s stethoscope to listen for unusual noises in the engine bay. Diesel engines are sensitive to wear—don’t skip these checks! Safe travels! 🚐💨
