Here’s your final, split packing list optimized for both sections, prioritizing warmth, versatility, and hill-friendly gear. No nutrition, just **clothing gear focus**:

### **Daytime Pacing (20 Miles)**
1. **Base Layer:**  
   - Moisture-wicking top (synthetic or merino wool, long-sleeve)  
   - Tights/shorts (convertible leggings or shorts with built-in liner)  
   *Why:* Manage sweat to avoid chills on downhills.  

2. **Mid-Layer:**  
   - Fleece jacket or light puffer vest (adjustable hood)  
   *Why:* Add warmth hilly sections demand; stashable at aid stations.  

3. **Outer Layer:**  
   - Windproof/water-resistant jacket (e.g., rain shell)  
   *Why:* Hilly + mid-30s = unpredictable wind.  

4. **Hands/Head:**  
   - Gloves (lightweight, grip-enhanced)  
   - Warm hat (fleece-lined)  
   *Why:* Extremes lose heat fast on trails.  

5. **Layers (for aid stations):**  
   - Extra base layer top  
   - Buff/gaiter (for neck/wrists)  
   *Why:* Swap in/out as you warm up/down between aid stations.  

6. **Shoes:**  
   - Trail shoes with aggressive tread (for hills)  
   - Socks (merino wool, moisture-wicking)  

---

### **Final 12-Miles (Night Pacing)**
1. **Base Layer:**  
   - Same as daytime (moisture-wicking top + convertible tights)  

2. **Mid-Layer:**  
   - Fleece jacket (retained from daytime)  

3. **Outer Layer:**  
   - **Waterproof shell jacket** (replace windproof from day)  
   *Why:* Night rain is likely; prioritize waterproofing.  

4. **Warmth Additions:**  
   - Fleece hat (with ear flaps)  
   - Fleece-lined gloves (or add insulating layer)  
   - Buff/gaiter (for neck)  
   *Why:* Wind chill + darkness = rapid cooling.  

5. **Lighting:**  
   - Headlamp + **spare batteries**  
   - Handheld light (if headlamp isn’t enough)  
   *Why:* Night aid stations may not have spare batteries.  

6. **Shoes/Socks:**  
   - Same as daytime (ensure socks are dry from day segment)  

---

### **Key Extras for Both Sections**
- **Small running pack/vest:** To carry layers between aid stations.  
- **Trekking poles (optional):** Steep hills = stability.  
- **Weather backup:** Check race forecast; add a thin beanie if temps drop unexpectedly.  

### **Pro Tips**
- **Test layers before the race!** Run in similar conditions to avoid surprises.  
- **Aid stations = your friend:** Use them to swap layers, gloves, or batteries.  
- **Night priority:** Warm hat/gloves + waterproof shell > all else.
