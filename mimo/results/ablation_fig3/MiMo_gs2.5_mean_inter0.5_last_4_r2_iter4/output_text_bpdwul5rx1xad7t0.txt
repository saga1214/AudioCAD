Here are five fantastic road trips from Vancouver for a new family. 

First, you could drive to Victoria. It's a short drive where you can explore the beautiful gardens at Butchart National Park. 

Second, head to the Okanagan Lake area. You can visit the Nilligchi Bear Sanctuary to see bears up close, and enjoy a fancy dinner at a place like the Fairmont Okanagan Hotel. 

Third, take a trip to Whistler. You can enjoy the scenery, and even if you can't lift heavy things, your wife might love the gentle chairlift ride up Blackcomb Mountain for a beautiful view. 

Fourth, you could go to the island to see Vancouver Island's rainforest. There are off-leash beaches like Johnston's Landing you can visit with your Golden girl. 

And fifth, for a bit of adventure, drive to the West Coast of Vancouver Island to Tofino. She'll love running on Aylard Cove Beach, and you can enjoy a lovely seafood dinner at a restaurant in Ume Bay. 

Each of these trips offers something unique and beautiful. Enjoy your explorations!
