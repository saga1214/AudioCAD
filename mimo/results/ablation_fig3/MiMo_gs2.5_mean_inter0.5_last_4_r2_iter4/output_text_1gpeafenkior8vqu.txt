### **Key Tips**  
1. **Workout Integration**:  
   - Use hotel gyms or morning runs in parks (e.g., Shinjuku’s Yoyogi Park, Osaka’s Vantone).  
   - Aim for 7–8 AM workouts to avoid sightseeing fatigue.  

2. **Presentation Prep During Trip**:  
   - **Images**: Visit calligraphy studios (Tokyo), ukiyo-e museums (Osaka City Museum), and ceramics shops (Kiyomizu-dera).  
   - **Layout Inspiration**: Osaka’s neon-lit streets (Dotonbori) and Kyoto’s balanced garden designs offer color/font ideas.  
   - **Daily 20-Minute Blocks**: Use downtime (e.g., between temple visits) to sketch slide concepts or save photos.  

3. **Transport**:  
   - **JR Pass**: Activate a 7-day pass for Shinkansen travel (Osaka-Kyoto-Nara).  
   - **Osaka Pass**: Covers buses/trams + 100+ attractions.  

4. **Budget Note**:  
   - Taxis in Osaka cost ~¥1,500–¥3,000 per ride; use buses for short trips.  
   - Tokyo’s public transport is efficient (¥230–¥310 per train ride).
