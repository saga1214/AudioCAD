That’s a great question! For quick dinners, focus on **versatile, pantry-friendly ingredients** that require minimal prep. Here are some ideas:

### 🥚 **Simple & Speedy Ingredients:**
1. **Eggs**: Scrambled, boiled, or in an omelet (takes 5–10 minutes).  
2. **Rice/Pasta**: Use leftover rice or cook a small batch. Pair with canned beans, frozen veggies, or a simple stir-fry sauce.  
3. **Canned Goods**: Tomatoes, beans, tuna, or coconut milk for soups/stews (no cooking needed if using canned).  
4. **Frozen Veggies**: Pre-chopped and ready to thaw or steam (e.g., stir-fry or microwave).  
5. **Sauces/Salsas**: Store-bought stir-fry sauce, salsa, or pesto to add flavor instantly.  

### 🍜 **Easy Meal Ideas:**
- **Stir-fry**: Sauté frozen veggies + canned chicken/tuna + soy sauce over rice.  
- **Miso-Ginger Soup**: Simmer canned broth + miso paste + frozen fish/veggies.  
- **Tuna Salad**: Mix canned tuna with Greek yogurt (instead of mayo) + diced celery + lemon juice.  
- **Omelet**: Whisk eggs + spinach + cheese → cook in 10 minutes.  

### 💡 **Key Tip:**  
**Prep staples ahead!** Wash/chop veggies on Sundays, portion grains, or keep canned goods visible. This cuts cooking time in half.  

Minimalism in cooking = fewer steps, fewer ingredients, more intention. You’ve got this! 😊
