Absolutely! Since warm water and swimming are your priorities in Northern California, I'd suggest Big Sur. It's beautiful in the cooler months and offers everything you're looking for.

Here’s a full itinerary I've put together for a four to five-day trip for you and your family.

For your first two nights, I'd recommend staying in Carmel-by-the-Sea. It's a charming town, and it's gluten-free friendly.

For places to eat, you can't go wrong with Nepenthe. It's that famous restaurant with incredible cliffside views. They handle gluten-free food very well and have a beautiful atmosphere, which would be a perfect celebration dinner. For a more casual lunch, the Big Sur Bakery in Dinosaur Canyon is a must-visit; they are experts at gluten-free pastries and sandwiches.

Now, for the main attractions in the Big Sur area, here are two things you should definitely do.

First, you have to drive the entire Pacific Coast Highway, which is Highway 1. You should definitely stop at a few key spots, like the iconic Bixby Bridge and McWay Falls, where a huge waterfall flows right into the ocean. You can also stop at Pfeiffer Beach for its famous purple sand. Just remember to pack a car that has enough space for the three of you.

Second, I highly recommend a kayak tour. You can find operators like Paddle Big Sur that offer tours right from Davenport Beach. They're great because they handle gluten-free needs and you get to see the coastline up close. You might even spot some sea otters!

For your third and fourth nights, you could stay in Big Sur. The lodging there is a bit more rustic, so I'd suggest looking for a cabin or an Airbnb with a kitchen, which would be really convenient for your daughter's dietary needs.

For another special meal, you could try the Big Sur Lodge Restaurant. It's located right in the heart of Big Sur and is also very accommodating for gluten-free guests, offering a wonderful dining experience.

Finally, for your fifth day, you'll want to head back home. On the way, you could stop in Santa Barbara for a few hours. It's a lovely coastal city, and it gets warmer there in the fall. You can grab a quick, friendly gluten-free lunch at a place like The Cook Shop and enjoy the beach before heading home.

Overall, this itinerary should give you warm weather, plenty of swimming opportunities, and all the accommodations and dining you'll need. I hope you have a wonderful vacation!
