Absolutely! Here are some **budget-friendly dessert ideas** that are kid-friendly, healthy-ish, and reinforce diabetes awareness—all while keeping costs low:

### 1. **Frozen Yogurt Bites**  
   - **How**: Layer plain Greek yogurt, honey (sparingly), and fresh berries in small muffin tins. Freeze for 2 hours.  
   - **Budget Tip**: Greek yogurt is cheap in bulk, and berries are seasonal/affordable.  
   - **Awareness**: Label them as "Low-Sugar Treats" or "Protein Popsicles."

### 2. **Oatmeal Cookies**  
   - **How**: Use rolled oats, mashed bananas (natural sweetness), a touch of honey, and nuts/seeds.  
   - **Budget Tip**: Oats are dirt-cheap—skip butter/eggs if possible.  
   - **Awareness**: Highlight they’re "high-fiber, low-sugar cookies."

### 3. **Fruit Skewers & Popsicles**  
   - **How**: Thread grapes, melon, berries, and pineapple onto skewers. For popsicles, blend fruit + yogurt or water and pour into molds.  
   - **Budget Tip**: Use seasonal fruit (watermelon, berries) when prices drop.  
   - **Awareness**: Serve with a small sign: "Fresh Fruit = Natural Sugar!"

### 4. **Mini "Dessert" Bowls**  
   - **How**: Offer small portions of:  
     - Yogurt + granola + honey (1 tbsp)  
     - Banana "nice cream" (frozen bananas blended)  
     - Fruit salad with a sprinkle of nuts.  
   - **Budget Tip**: Buy granola in bulk (or make your own). Bananas are a budget superstar.  

### 5. **Homemade Rice Krispie Treats (Sugar-Simplified)**  
   - **How**: Reduce sugar by 20% and add mashed cauliflower (unnoticeable!) for fiber.  
   - **Budget Tip**: Rice cereal is cheap, and sugar reduction cuts costs significantly.  

### 6. **DIY Dessert Bar**  
   - **How**: Set up a table with:  
     - Base: Plain yogurt, oatmeal, or fruit salad.  
     - Toppings: Fresh fruit, nuts, seeds, or *minimal* honey.  
   - **Budget Tip**: Lets guests customize—reduces waste and costs.  

### Cost-Saving Tips:  
- **Buy in bulk**: Yogurt, oats, and seasonal fruit are often cheaper this way.  
- **Use pantry staples**: Bananas, oats, and yogurt are affordable year-round.  
- **DIY where possible**: Skip store-bought treats; make your own.  
- **Volunteer help**: Ask family to help prep desserts (e.g., chopping fruit).  

### Final Thought:  
Balance fun and awareness! Label desserts with sugar content (e.g., "1 tbsp honey per cookie") to subtly educate. Kids will love interactive options like skewers or popsicles, while healthier choices align with your cause.
