Of course! For St Pete, Zone 10B, especially for the reversed growing seasons you mentioned, fast-growing things are your best friends.

Herbs are a great place to start: basil, mint, parsley. They're all easy and love the warmth.

Leafy greens work great there too: lettuce, spinach, and radishes. They're quick, and radishes especially are a super fast crop.

And for something fruitier, tomatoes and peppers are fantastic in Florida. They're pretty forgiving and they taste incredible. 

Those five things should give you a fantastic, beginner-friendly edible garden to get you started.
