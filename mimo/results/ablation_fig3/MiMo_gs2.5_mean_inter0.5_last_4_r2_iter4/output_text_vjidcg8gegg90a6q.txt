For winter chicks integrated into the adult coop later on, the deep litter approach we talked about with pine shavings is the perfect bedding. Five-week-old chicks need a consistent floor temperature, and pine shavings provide that insulation safely. Sand is also an option, but it's a bit less comfortable for their tiny feet. Pine shavings are also rodent-resistant, which fits your coop on concrete. Just remember to manage the deep litter method by constantly adding fresh ones on top of the composting ones below to generate that natural warmth.
