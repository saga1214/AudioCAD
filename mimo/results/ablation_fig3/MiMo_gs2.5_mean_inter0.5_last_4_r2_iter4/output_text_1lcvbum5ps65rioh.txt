### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia, Canada
In BC, U-turns are governed by the *Motor Vehicle Act*. Key rules:
1. **Signal Required**: Always use your turn signal.
2. **Intersection Restrictions**: Prohibited at traffic signals unless posted as allowed.
3. **Visibility**: You must have a clear 150-meter view in both directions (≈1.5 football fields).
4. **Hills/Curves**: Prohibited on curves, near top-of-hill areas, or where visibility is obstructed.
5. **No Interference**: Never block traffic flow or pedestrian paths.
6. **No Signs = Illegal**: If a sign prohibits U-turns, you cannot do one there.

**Penalties**: A $160 fine + 3 demerit points under *Traffic Act* s.158.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients
For a classic (pork-free) recipe, you’ll need:
#### Essentials:
- **400g spaghetti**  
- **150g guanciale** (cured pork cheek; substitute pancetta or prosciutto if needed)  
- **3 large eggs** + **3 egg yolks**  
- **100g Pecorino Romano** (hard sheep’s cheese; Parmesan works)  
- **Freshly ground black pepper**  

#### Optional but Traditional:
- **Red pepper flakes** (for heat)  
- **Chives** (for garnish)  

#### Key Notes:
- **No cream or onions**: Authentic carbonara relies on emulsified eggs/cheese.  
- **Pancetta/Prosciutto**: Use if avoiding pork (but check your religious guidelines).  
- **Cheese**: Pecorino is essential for flavor.  

### Cooking Tips:
1. Render guanciale in a skillet until crispy, then remove.  
2. Cook pasta in salted water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
3. Whisk eggs, yolks, cheese, and pepper. Toss hot pasta with guanciale, then mix in egg mixture off-heat (add pasta water to create a creamy sauce).  
4. Serve immediately with extra cheese and pepper.
