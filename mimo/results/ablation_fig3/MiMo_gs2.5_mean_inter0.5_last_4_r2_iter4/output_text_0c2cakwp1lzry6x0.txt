Here's a step-by-step guide for each installation, prioritizing safety and efficiency:

### 📺 1. **Mounting a TV**
- **Tools Needed**: Level, stud finder, drill, TV mount, screws, wall anchors (if no studs).
- **Steps**:
  1. **Find Studs**: Use a stud finder to locate wall studs (spaced 16" apart). If no studs, use heavy-duty anchors.
  2. **Measure Twice**: Hold the TV mount against the wall. Use a level to ensure it’s perfectly horizontal. Mark screw holes.
  3. **Install Mount**: Drill pilot holes, insert anchors if needed, and screw the mount into studs/anchors.
  4. **Secure TV**: Mount the TV to the bracket using included screws. Avoid overtightening.

### 🌿 2. **Hanging Planters**
- **Tools Needed**: Drill, appropriate hooks/screws (e.g., toggle bolts for heavy pots), measuring tape.
- **Steps**:
  1. **Calculate Weight**: Add pot + soil + plant weight. Choose hooks rated for 1.5x this weight.
  2. **Mark Placement**: Hang pots at eye-level (1.7m) or slightly higher. Ensure they won’t block light/ventilation.
  3. **Install Hooks**: Drill holes above pot positions. Use toggle bolts or molly bolts for drywall.
  4. **Hang Planters**: Slide hooks into the planter’s hanger, ensuring weight is fully supported.

### 🪵 3. **Installing Floating Shelves**
- **Tools Needed**: Drill, level, shims (for uneven walls), appropriate shelf brackets.
- **Steps**:
  1. **Measure & Mark**: Place shelves where desired. Mark top/bottom positions using a level. Add 1" overhang if aesthetic.
  2. **Check Wall Strength**: Use a stud finder. For drywall, use toggle bolts or anchors rated for shelf weight.
  3. **Install Brackets**: Drill pilot holes. If studs are present, screw brackets in. For drywall, use anchors.
  4. **Test Stability**: Place a heavy object on the shelf. Adjust shims if needed for levelness.

### ⚠️ **Critical Tips**
- **Safety First**: Wear safety glasses. Never hang heavy items (like TVs) on drywall alone.
- **Time Management**: Dedicate one Sunday block per project (e.g., 2 hours for TV/shelves, 1 hour for planters).
- **Avoid Noise**: Do installations early Sunday morning to comply with landlord rules.
- **Post-Installation**: Use rugs to dampen footsteps, and keep a white noise machine for focus.

### 🎨 **Decorating Pro Tips**
- **Art Layout**: Create a gallery wall with consistent spacing. Start with one focal piece, then arrange others around it.
- **Furniture Placement**: Anchor shelves with sturdy pieces (e.g., a sideboard). Keep pathways clear.
- **Lighting**: Add LED strip lights under shelves or a floor lamp for ambiance.
