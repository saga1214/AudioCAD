Alright, let's summarize all the homemade and store-bought ideas for your brown and white princess. For homemade treats, you can make frozen bites from pumpkin or sweet potato, bake peanut butter and oat cookies (with no xylitol), or give small pieces of cooked chicken or carrot sticks. For store-bought brands, you could try Blue Buffalo, Wellness, Zuke's, Greenies, or Merrick. Just remember to keep the portions tiny!
