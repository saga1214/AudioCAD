For an end-of-year celebration, an **indoor BBQ in the cafeteria** strikes a great balance between fun and practicality. Here’s why and how to make it work:

1. **Location & Setup**:  
   - Use cafeteria tables for seating and set up a simple BBQ station (e.g., hot dogs, veggie burgers, or pizza) to minimize cleanup.  
   - If space allows, create a "sundae bar" with parents bringing toppings (chocolate, sprinkles, whipped cream) for a dairy-free option if chips/dip aren’t available.  

2. **Activities**:  
   - **Low-Cost Park Games**: Set up a mini version of three-legged races or sack races using chairs or tables. For safety, avoid muddy/wet conditions.  
   - **Badge Spotlight**: Have girls proudly display their sashes and badges, then share one favorite memory from the year (e.g., "My best pizza night!" or "Learning fire station tricks").  

3. **Logistics**:  
   - **Parents Help**: Assign a few parents to bring BBQ supplies (grill, charcoal) or snacks (ice cream cones, pizza). This shares the load.  
   - **Budget**: Charge a small $5–$10 "celebration fee" to cover food costs. Parents can Venmo you or drop cash.  

4. **Pregnancy-Friendly**:  
   - Skip heavy cleanup—parents can help pack up.  
   - If energy is low, pivot to a movie night with a Brownie-themed film (e.g., *Girlboss*).  

**Pro Tip**: End with a group photo! It’ll be a cherished keepsake for everyone. You’ve got this—starting simple ensures fun for the girls and sanity for you! 🎉
