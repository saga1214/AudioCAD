Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids does mean you'll need a lot of food. I can definitely help you put together a shopping list that focuses on healthy options but also includes the sugar-free sodas and desserts you were thinking about.

Let's start with drinks. You'll want plenty of reusable water bottles for everyone. For low-mess kid options, grab low-sugar juice boxes. For the adults, sugar-free sodas in individual cans or bottles will be perfect.

For snacks, single-serve options are key. Things like cheese sticks, grapes, pretzels, trail mix, baby carrots with pre-packaged hummus cups, and cut fruit like apple slices that you can toss in lemon juice to keep them fresh should work great.

For the main meal, since calzones seemed a bit uncertain, how about focusing on wraps and sandwiches packed individually?

And for dessert, cupcakes or brownies would be much cleaner and easier to handle at the park than cutting into a whole cake or pie.

So, a good plan would be to fill several coolers with all these items, clearly labeled. That way, everyone can just grab what they want and move around without any fuss. Hope you all have a blast!
