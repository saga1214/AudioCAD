If you're combining a desert trip with a Vegas finish, that's a solid plan. 

For a proper Vegas experience, you could either splurge on a nice hotel with a pool and spa for those hot showers and maybe a cocktail, or go for the classic Vegas vibe with a cheap hotel and explore the Strip. 

Some people love the big resorts with themed pools and shows, while others prefer the more gritty, old-school Vegas feel in areas like the Strip or Fremont Street. It really depends on what she’s into. Either way, you can't go wrong with some good food, maybe a fun show, and definitely a few drinks. Vegas is all about the energy and the experience.
