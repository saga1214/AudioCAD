The absolute best winter bedding for five-plus-week-old winter chicks on top of those brooder plates is wood shavings. Specifically, I'd look for large-flake shavings, similar to what I mentioned for chickens earlier—they provide fantastic insulation without attracting rodents. You should avoid sand for the chicks' area because it holds moisture really well and can get very cold overnight.

Also, a quick tip: make sure the legs on their plate go up high enough, at least six to eight inches, so the chicks can always hide underneath where it's warmest.
