After considering all the information—the unique species, the untapped scientific potential, and especially the critical role in providing essential "ecosystem services"—I believe the most important aspect of preserving biodiversity hotspots is their function as the planet's primary reservoir for irreplaceable genetic and ecological knowledge.

These unique species and healthy ecosystems hold genetic information that could lead to future medicines, agricultural improvements, and solutions to climate change.
