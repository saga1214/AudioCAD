Ah, diesel camper vans definitely need a good pre-trip check. For brakes, a simple way to tell if they’re worn is if you hear a grinding or squealing noise when you press them. If the pads or rotors look thin, that’s a sign. For spark plugs on your old diesel van, they usually last longer than gas engines—often 80,000 to 100,000 miles. Replacing them involves taking off the air filter box and some hoses, then using a special socket to turn them out. You’d need to get the right replacement plugs and a new gasket. It’s a bit of work but doable if you’re handy. If not, a mechanic can handle it. Just make sure everything’s safe for the family trip!
