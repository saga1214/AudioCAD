Here's a step-by-step guide to decorate efficiently and safely install your items on Sundays:

### 🛠️ Installation Tips by Item
1. **Mounting the TV**  
   - **Measure Twice**: Use a level to mark center points.  
   - **Secure Anchors**: For drywall, use toggle bolts rated for your TV’s weight. For concrete, drill into rebar or use concrete anchors.  
   - **Safety First**: Ensure wall studs are hit for support. Use a stud finder with laser level for precision.  
   - **Cord Management**: Plan where cables will exit (e.g., behind furniture or under carpets).

2. **Floating Shelves**  
   - **Weight Distribution**: Place heavier art pieces lower.  
   - **Hardware**: Use strong L-brackets or cleats rated for the load. For drywall, add toggle bolts.  
   - **Alignment**: Mark shelf positions with a level, then drill pilot holes.  
   - **Anti-Tip Brackets**: Secure shelves to wall studs to prevent falling art.

3. **Hanging Plants**  
   - **Wire Strength**: Use thick, UV-resistant wire or macramé hangers.  
   - **Weight Check**: Ensure hooks can support the plant + soil.  
   - **Placement**: Hang near windows for light but avoid direct sun. Use self-watering pots if needed.

### 🎨 Decorating Strategy
- **Start with Zones**: Create a functional area first (e.g., a workspace near your desk, a lounge area by decoration Sundays).  
- **Art Layout**: Group pieces by color or theme. Hang at eye-level (57-67 inches from the floor).  
- **Furniture Flow**: Anchor pieces against walls for spaciousness. Use rugs to define areas.  
- **Lighting**: Layer lighting: overhead (pendant lamp), task (desk lamp), and accent (track light for art).  

### ⚠️ Safety & Efficiency
- **Tool Prep**: Gather all hardware/hardware *before* starting each task.  
- **Noise Control**: Install during weekday afternoons if friends are nearby ( landlord rules!).  
- **Time Boxing**: Dedicate 2-3 hours per Sunday for installation/decorating.  

### 💡 Pro Tip
Take photos of your progress! It’ll motivate you and help visualize the final look. Remember: **cohesive color schemes and intentional zones** make spaces feel polished without clutter.
