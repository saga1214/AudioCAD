Oh, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! You’ve got vibrant foliage in places like Chiapas or the Yucatán, and just... stunning sunsets and landscapes. If you can swing it, going during Mexico’s actual autumn, which is around September to November, would be ideal. The colors are just at their peak. 

If that’s not possible, just any time in the summer or early fall should still give you plenty to paint. The key is to observe closely—note the light, the colors, the textures. Even if you don’t paint while you’re there, soaking in that inspiration will translate back to your studio. Safe travels, and happy painting!
