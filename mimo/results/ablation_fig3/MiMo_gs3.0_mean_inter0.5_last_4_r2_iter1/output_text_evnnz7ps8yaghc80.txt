It's wonderful that you're planning your practice. Here’s a possible weekly schedule to keep you on track.

For the first five days of the week, dedicate about 15 to 20 minutes each day to using tennis balls after mastering scarves. Focus on getting clean catches.

On the sixth day, use scarves for about 10 to 15 minutes to slow everything down and perfect your pattern memory.

Finally, on the seventh day, combine all the tools. Spend 10 minutes with bean bags in one hand to warm up, 10 minutes with tennis balls, and 5 minutes with scarves.
