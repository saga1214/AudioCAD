### Key Risks with Futures
1. **Leverage Magnifies Losses:** A 10% loss in futures can become a 20-33% loss.  
2. **No Asset Ownership:** You’re trading a contract, not the underlying asset.  
3. **Complexity:** Requires deep market knowledge (e.g., understanding basis spreads, margin requirements).  
4. **Short-Selling Risk:** If you go short (betting prices *fall*), you face unlimited losses if prices rise.
