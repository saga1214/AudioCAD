Alright, let's go over the appliances and cooking methods. For smoking, you'll need a smoker. For roasting, an oven. For baking, you'll need an oven for the no-bake pumpkin cheesecake, the fruit tart, and the apple crisp. For roasting in a smaller appliance, a toaster oven was suggested for the prime rib roast, roasted asparagus, and stuffing. For side dishes, you'll need a stovetop or slow cooker for wild rice, root vegetables, mashed potatoes, mac and cheese, Brussels sprouts salad, bread salad, asparagus, stuffing, cranberry sauce, and apple crisp. The Instant Pot was needed for cranberry sauce and stuffing.
