The wood spoon incident is understandable. For stir-frying noodles, here's the key step: use metal or plastic utensils in boiling water pots, never anything wooden.

More importantly for stir-fry, timing is everything. You need to cook the ingredients as you add them. Start with a hot pan with a little oil. Brown the ground meat and drain most of the fat from the pan. Then add your stir-fried veggies and cook for a minute or two. Finally, add your cooked noodles, a little sauce or soy sauce, and toss it all together very quickly over high heat.
