For Halloween candy, some of the most popular and crowd-pleasing options are classic chocolate bars like Hershey's Kisses, Skittles, gummies, and of cos, Reese's Peanut Butter Cups. Mini candy bars like Snickers, Twix, and Milky Way are also great choices. You could also get some Halloween-themed candy bags or boxes that are already filled. And since allergies were a concern earlier, it's always good to have some safe options like plain peanuts or sunflower seeds available for guests.
