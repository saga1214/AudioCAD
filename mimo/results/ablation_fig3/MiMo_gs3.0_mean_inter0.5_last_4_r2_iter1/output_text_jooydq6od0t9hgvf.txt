For a refined formal crowd, keep the drinks classy but simple. For the vodka, you could mix it with fresh juice like grapefruit or a splash of soda with some fresh berries and mint. For the Argentinian-style vinho (vin tinto), which is red wine, you could serve a nice Malbec since it’s smooth and well-liked. Offer a sparkling water option for your allergy guest alongside everything else. That should keep it sophisticated and fun. Enjoy the party!
