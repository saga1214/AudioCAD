Bruno Mars-free zone means broccoli is a no-go based on sensitivities.

So, sticking to green vegetables Bruno can likely eat, you could offer things like:

Kale is a great one, just be sure to remove the tough stems. Collard greens are another good option.

Swiss chard is also a possibility. Cucumber is another one birds can have, and steamed green peas are usually fine in moderation.

Just remember Bruno avoids apples, bananas, broccoli, carrots, and mango.
