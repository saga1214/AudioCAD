Don't worry, it can be a bit confusing! Let me clarify. The most widely recognized original Boulevardier ratio is one and a quarter bourbon to one ounce vermouth/Campari. However, an equal-parts version, with one ounce of each spirit, is so traditional and popular that many people consider it the 'classic' ratio too. So both are correct classic definitions. Now, if you want to make a Boulevardette instead, which is often made with equal parts but using gin, the proportions are three-quarters of an ounce each for: one part gin, one part green Chartreuse, one part maraschino liqueur, and one part fresh lime juice. Stir and strain, no garnish for that one. Hope that helps you out in the store!
