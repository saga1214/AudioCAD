Leo and Sagittarius pairing up outdoors sounds ideal! For a quick weekend trip nearby, you have some great fire-friendly activities.

You could drive about an hour east to Fish Creek Provincial Park for hiking and exploring. Or, about an hour west, you could rent bikes and ride along the scenic Bow River Trail. For something more dramatic, you could take a drive down to the Canmore area to see the famous Bow Valley Canyon. Closer to Calgary, you're right on the edge of the Rockies, so you could take an easy walk along the St. Patrick's Skywalk or drive up to Lake Louise for some stunning mountain views. If you're both active, you could also look into mountain biking trails in the Canmore area or at Glenmore Reservoir.
