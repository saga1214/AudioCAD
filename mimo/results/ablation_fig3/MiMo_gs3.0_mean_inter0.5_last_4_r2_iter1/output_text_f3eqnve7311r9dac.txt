Just to give you an idea, in 2024 a nearly perfect copy of *Action Comics* number one sold for six million dollars.

Similarly, a pristine copy of *Amazing Fantasy* fifteen from 19 Ifteen sold in 2021 for three point six million, and *Captain- a number one from 194 sold at auction in 2022 for just over three million dollars.
