Alright your pirate nautical beach birthday sounds awesome for seven kids including your son wearing his outfit. Sand-friendly finger foods themed as treasure are key and avoiding watermelon allergy is also important Here’s a shopping list idea.

First, for "Buried Treasure Sliders," plan for about 24 mini rolls or sliders per child. This includes options like turkey and cheese or pirate meatloaf sandwiches cut small and thematic.

For snack containers dressed as pirate treasure chests, you'll need 6 to 8 of them to hold things like cheese cubes labeled “gold coins,” themed crackers labeled “ship masts,” themed veggie sticks labeled “jewels,” and grapes or apple slices also labeled as “pirate jewels.” Skip the watermelon and include grapes as an allergy-safe fruit alternative.

For drinks, theme juice boxes by calling one "pirate punch" or "sea water." Individual boxes per child is best to avoid sand.

Finally, for packaging, use things like snack-size treasure chests for snacks and mini pirate ship-shaped cookies or flags for extra theme decoration.
