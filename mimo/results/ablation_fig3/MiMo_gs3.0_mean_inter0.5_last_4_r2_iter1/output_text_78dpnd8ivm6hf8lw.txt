Here's the quick recap of your child-friendly circulatory system analogy:

*   **Heart:** The **gas station and engine**. Pumps the cars all around and powers the whole system.
*   **Lungs:** The **main refueling station**. Blood cars stop here first to pick up fresh oxygen fuel.
*   **Arteries:** The **big highways**. Take blood away from the heart super fast to deliver fuel.
*   **Capillaries:** The **tiny neighborhood streets**. Get blood *right up to* every single cell/house to deliver the oxygen.
*   **Veins:** The **return lanes**. Take the blood cars back to the heart/gas station after they've delivered their fuel.
*   **Kidneys:** A **special service station/checkpoint**. Clean the blood of junk and extra water as it passes through.
*   **Liver:** An **all-in-one sorting service station**. Processes useful fuel/nutrients picked up, figures out storage/send, breaks down potentially harmful stuff. Keeps fuel balanced.

This analogy gives kids a simple map: cars (blood) drive on roads (vessels) from the heart (engine/gas) to deliver fuel (oxygen) everywhere, then come back for clean fuel and a system check!
