Initally nervous is pretty normal. One preparation step you can take is to request a formal performance review meeting. You can frame it in a positive way, by saying something like, “I’d like to discuss my performance and contributions since I’ve been performing well.”

As for in-the-moment strategies, first, it helps to prepare a concise, data-driven summary of your key accomplishments and their impact. Focusing on facts rather than just feelings can ground the conversation. Second, you can practice what's called “power posing.” Before the meeting, spend a minute or two standing tall with your shoulders back in a private space. Research suggests this can actually help reduce stress and increase feelings of confidence.
