Here are five fantastic road trip ideas from Vancouver.

First, just a couple of hours away is Whistler. It's perfect for hiking in the mountains, and the town is charming. In the summer, you can try white-water rafting.

Second, you can head to Victoria. It's the capital city, and you can tour the famous Fairmont Empress hotel. You can also visit the Butchart Gardens, which are stunning.

Third, for a trip to the US, you can drive to Seattle. You can visit the famous Pike Place Market and even take a ferry to Bainbridge Island.

Fourth, you can head south to the Okanagan Lake area. It's famous for its wineries, and you can tour the beautiful vineyards.

And fifth, for a more adventurous trip, you can drive to the Fraser Canyon. There are great hiking trails and you can even try a gold panning adventure.

Each of these trips has wonderful food and activities for your first baby and dog! Enjoy your adventures.
