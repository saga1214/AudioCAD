Got distracted by monkeys—no worries, they are pretty mesmerizing! Let’s dive back into unique movie night snacks inspired by Japan. You could set up a takoyaki station—those little savory balls filled with octopus and tempura bits are crispy on the outside and gooey inside. Another fun idea is yuzu-marinated octopus bites, which are sweet and spicy. 

For something more interactive, a mochi-making station where guests can try pounding the dough and fill it with red bean paste feels traditional and fun. You could also do a small omakase sushi or sashimi bar, but that might be too much prep. 

A unique twist on charcuterie could be a Japanese-style bento box bar with tamagoyaki, onigiri, and pickled veggies. Does this mix of textures and flavors sound exciting enough for 15 foody frames?
