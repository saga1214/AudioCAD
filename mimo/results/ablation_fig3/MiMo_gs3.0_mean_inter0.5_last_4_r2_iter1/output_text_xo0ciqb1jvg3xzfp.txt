That's an excellent question. Was it a happy occasion for both Pilgrims and Wampanoag families? The answer is almost certainly no.

For the Pilgrims, it was likely a moment of relief and celebration after barely surviving.

For some Wampanoagers, especially local hunters who provided the five deer, it could have felt like an honor.

However, for many Wampanoag people, especially those who had lost so many family members to European diseases, the event would have been a painful reminder of the tragedy and the unknown threat the settlers brought.

So, it was a moment of cooperation, but not a single, shared feeling of happiness for both groups.
