Here's a clear recipe list for your event, all laid out for your bartenders.
Old Fashioned: Two ounces of bourbon like Maker’s Mark, one sugar cube or half an ounce of simple syrup, three or four dashes of Angostura bitters, ice, and an orange twist for a garnish.
Paper Plane: Equal parts, so two ounces of bourbon, half an ounce of Marzipol, half an ounce Nonino Amaro, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice.
Whiskey Sour: An ounce of bourbon, half an ounce of fresh lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. If you decide to use raw egg white, dry shake it first for about 15 seconds for foam, add ice and shake again, then strain. You can garnish with a cherry or orange slice.
French 75: An ounce of gin like Tanqueray, half an ounce of fresh lemon juice, half an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and half an ounce of simple syrup. Dry shake, add ice, shake again, and top it off with champagne.
