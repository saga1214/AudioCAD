Sure, here’s a quick checklist for your first self-recorded kid-friendly channel.

Before filming any show, you want to make sure it’s kid-friendly, short and visually engaging.

So first, permission—make sure wherever you’re performing, whether it’s an assisted living or foster home, that you’ve got permission to videotape and post it online.

Then, bring good lighting if you can, natural light works great, but a simple lamp or two can make a big difference.

For recording, ask someone to film from a different angle or use your phone in landscape mode for better video quality.

After filming, you can edit if you want—trim the beginning and end, add some simple captions for puppets’ names or gags, and then upload directly to YouTube labeling it as kid-friendly.

Start small, film what you do best, post consistently, and it’ll grow. You’ll do great.
