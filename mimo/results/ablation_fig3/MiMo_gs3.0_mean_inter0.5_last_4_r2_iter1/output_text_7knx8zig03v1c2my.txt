### **Clicker Training Explained**  
Clicker training uses a **three-step process**:  
1. **Clicker as a Marker**: When your puppy does something you want (e.g., sits), press the clicker **immediately** to "mark" that exact moment.  
2. **Reward**: Give a treat **within 1-2 seconds** after the click. The puppy learns: "Click + Treat = Good behavior!"  
3. **Repetition**: Repeat this 10-15 times daily. Over time, the clicker alone becomes a signal for a reward.  

**Example**:  
- Puppy sits → You click → Give treat.  
- Repeat until he sits on cue ("sit") instantly.  

**Why it works**: Clickers bridge the gap between behavior and reward with precision, speeding up learning.  

---

**Pro Tip**: Pair clicker training with verbal cues (e.g., "sit" after clicking) to build stronger communication! 🐶✨
