That's awesome—congrats on the coffee table! Since you don't like sweet snacks, vending machine options can be tricky, but here are some savory or neutral choices to fuel your project or snack break:

### 🧀 Savory & Crunchy Picks:
1. **Cheese Sticks or Slices** – Classic savory, protein-packed (many machines carry pre-packaged options).  
2. **Pretzels** – Classic salty crunch (try mini pretzel bites or twists).  
3. **Popcorn** – Light, airy, and neutral (ask if they have plain or lightly salted).  
4. **Nuts** – Almonds, peanuts, or mixed nuts (check for roasted/salted varieties).  
5. **Crackers** – Plain rice crackers, water crackers, or savory-flavored varieties.  

### 🧂 Savory Snacks (if available):
- **Beef Jerky** – Protein-rich and savory (look for low-sugar brands).  
- **Roasted Chickpeas** – Crunchy, savory, and satisfying (if your machine stocks them).  
- **Seaweed Snacks** – Light, salty, and refreshing.  

### 🧈 Neutral/Umami Options:
- **Hummus or Guacamole Cups** – Pair with crackers if available.  
- **Pickles or Pickle Spears** – Tangy and salty (always a wildcard!).  
- **Rice Cakes** – Plain or lightly salted (top with leftover hummus if you have it!).  

### Pro Tip:
Scan the vending machine for **"salty," "savory," or "protein" labels**—many machines now categorize snacks this way. If it’s all sugary candy, consider grabbing a **cup of coffee or tea** to balance it out!
