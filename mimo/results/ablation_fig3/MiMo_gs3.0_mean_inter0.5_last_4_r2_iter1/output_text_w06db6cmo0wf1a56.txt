Here’s a **mess-minimizing meal and drink plan** for your bachelor party, packed in coolers for grab-and-go convenience. It balances health, variety, and preferences (real calzones, sugar-free sodas, desserts):

---

### **Drinks** (Pack plenty of water alongside!):
1. **Water**: 20+ reusable water bottles for kids/adults (easy refills at park too).  
2. **Sugar-Free Sodas**: 10-12 cans/boxes for dads (e.g., Sprite Zero, Coke Zero). Kids can share with water/juice.  
3. **Low-Mess Juices**: 10 juice boxes for picky kids.  
4. Optional: Infused water (cucumber/mint in large jug) for extra flair.  

---

### **Snacks** (Mess-free single-serve packs):  
1. Cheese sticks (10 dads × 2 sticks each).  
2. Baby carrots + 5 single-serve hummus cups.  
3. Individual pretzels/trail mix bags (10 bags). Grapes (1 bag).  
4. Granola bars or fruit snacks (10 packs).  

---

### **Meal**: Real Calzones!  
- **Option 1**: **Pre-made frozen calzones** (e.g., Trader Joe’s, Whole Foods). Thaw overnight in coolers. Easy, clean, reheats well.  
- **Option 2**: **Order ahead from a pizzeria**. Specify:  
  - 10 large calzones (mix fillings: cheese, pepperoni, veggie).  
  - Wrap tightly in foil; pack in cooler.  
- *Why not fresh?*: Cutting messy calzones at the park is hard. Pre-ordered/frozen avoids this.  

---

### **Dessert** (Celebratory & grabbable):  
- **Cupcakes**: Celebration-themed (12-15 to share). Already individual—grab-and-go clean.  
- **Brownie trays**: Pre-cut into small squares (10-12 pieces). Low-mess, easy pack.  
- *Pie not recommended*: Requires plates/forks/cutting, messy for movement.  

---

### **Cooler Packing Tips**:  
1. **Layer**: Drinks at bottom (keep cold), snacks/meals on top.  
2. **Label**: Cooler #1 = Calzones/Meals; Cooler #2 = Drinks/Snacks/Dessert.  
3. **Ice**: Use ice packs + bags of ice (rotate coolers if needed).  
4. **Extras**: Wet wipes, hand sanitizer, trash bags (for quick cleanup).  

This plan covers all bases—real calzones for the groom, soda options, healthy basics, and easy desserts—so you guys can fully enjoy the park! Have an amazing time! 🎢🎉
