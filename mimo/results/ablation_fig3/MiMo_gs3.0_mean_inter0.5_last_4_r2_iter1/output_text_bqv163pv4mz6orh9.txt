Ooh, opening night for a play is huge! A thoughtful gift would be perfect. Some good ideas would be a personalized thank-you note with your message, or maybe a small treat like a fancy cake or her favorite chocolates. You could also get a cute play-related item, like a little playbill frame or a drama-themed notebook. If she likes fashion, a small accessory like a fun hat or scarf could be sweet. Or, if she likes to relax, a cozy blanket or some scented candles are always a nice touch. You could even surprise her with tickets to another show or a fun outing afterward!
