Okay, where to begin?

Let’s start with the brain hemispheres. The right-left brain idea is mostly a myth. Real neuroscience shows that both sides work together. Creativity and structure aren’t locked into one lobe or the other. It’s more about how the brain integrates both functions. So, you can be both creative and objective—it’s about finding fields that let you do both.

Now for tinnitus again: mostly physiological, as I said. Check if you’ve protected your ears from loud noises, hydrated enough, or if it’s stress-aggravated. 

Now for your career dilemma. You want concrete answers but also creativity, and you like building things. Here are a few ideas:

First, graphic design or data visualization. It’s measurable because it’s about user needs and data, but also very creative.

Second, architecture. It’s about structuring and building things, but also artistic. You can measure functionality while expressing creativity.

Third, technical illustration or scientific visualization. It’s about translating complex, measurable information into creative visuals.

And fourth, consider computational creativity. It’s about using algorithms and logic to create art or music, merging your love for measurement and flow.

Grab that second cup and dream big!
