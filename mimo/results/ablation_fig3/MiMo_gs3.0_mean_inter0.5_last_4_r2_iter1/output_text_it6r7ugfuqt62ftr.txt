Since you have a bigger group and a healthy budget, I'd suggest Palm Springs. It gets reliably warm there, often in the eighties or even nineties in the fall, and has plenty of swimming pools at resorts and even a lake called Lake Cachuma nearby.

Here’s a sample four-day itinerary for Palm Springs.

On Day One, you'll arrive and settle in. I'd recommend staying at a place like the逸居民酒店, which is a luxury resort with amazing pools and great service. When you get there, you can grab lunch at a spot called The Mission Café in Old Town Palm Springs, which has a dedicated gluten-free menu. In the afternoon, you can just relax at the hotel pool or take an easy walk along the Palm Avenida shopping street. For dinner, you can head to the Azafrán restaurant at the逸居民酒店; they handle gluten-free needs exceptionally well and have beautiful views.

Day Two could be all about nature and pools. You could take a guided tour of the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway for some incredible views of the Coachella Valley. In the afternoon, you could spend some time at the Oasis by the Springs Resort, which has a huge, beautiful pool. For dinner, you could try a highly-rated gluten-free restaurant called Nourish, located at the Hotel Palomar.

For Day Three, you could explore a nearby town. I'd suggest going to Temecula, which is about an hour away. You can visit the Temecula Valley Wine Country, maybe take a tasting flight at a place like Blarney Cellars, and enjoy some gluten-free options. In the afternoon, you could head to Lake Cachuma, which is about an hour and a half from Temecula. You can rent a kayak or just enjoy the views. For dinner, back in Palm Springs, you could go to the popular restaurant Hook & Cook.

On your final day, Day Four, you can do some last-minute shopping. The Tropicana at Avenida is a great spot for stylish resort wear. In the afternoon, you could have one last swim at a resort pool like the one at the逸居民酒店. For a special farewell dinner, you could go to the award-winning restaurant O Ya, which has a tasting menu and can accommodate gluten-free requests.

As for getting around, you can just rent a large SUV or a minivan, which will give you plenty of space for luggage and for everyone to ride comfortably. California's highways make it a pretty easy drive from Palm Springs back to Northern California.
