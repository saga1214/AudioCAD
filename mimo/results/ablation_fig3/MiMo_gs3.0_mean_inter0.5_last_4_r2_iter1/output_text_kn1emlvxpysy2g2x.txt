Of yours, high-end Pinot Noir should be both stored and served at cellar temperature, which is about 55 degrees Fahrenheit, or 13 Celsius.

Japanese Hon-Mirin isn’t sipped straight very often, it’s mostly for cooking, so room temp works at serving, around 68 to 75 degrees Fahrenheit, or 20 to 24 Celsius. Hon-Mirin should be stored cool, around 60 to 68 degrees Fahrenheit, or 16 to 20 Celsius.

Pinot Grigio should be stored chilled, around 45 to 50 degrees Fahrenheit, or 4 to 10 Celsius, and served just as cold.

Mulled red wine should be warmed significantly for serving until it reaches 140 to 160 degrees Fahrenheit, or 60 to 70 Celsius, but stored red at normal cellar temps of 55 Fahrenheit, or 13 Celsius first.

For hot weather, I’d say Sauvignon Grigolio or Champagne is best served cold. For cold weather, Pinot Noir is the classic choice. Hope this helps you plan the party! Cheers!
