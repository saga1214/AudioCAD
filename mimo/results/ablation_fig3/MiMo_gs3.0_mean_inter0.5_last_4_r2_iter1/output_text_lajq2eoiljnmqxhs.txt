Cooking could work if you pick something simple and stress-free. Keep it easy—maybe a quick stir-fry using safe ingredients she tolerates, like breaded meats if she eats those, rice, and plenty of alternative veggies like carrots or bell peppers. Or you could try making a build-your-own taco or wrap bar, where you can both customize plates without forcing agreement on toppings. The focus can be on doing something creative together, which might help rebuild some sisterly chemistry naturally.
