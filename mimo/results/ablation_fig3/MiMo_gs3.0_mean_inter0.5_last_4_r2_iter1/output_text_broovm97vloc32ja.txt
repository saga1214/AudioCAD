Yes, publishers **highly** value authors who can also illustrate their books. In fact, many famous authors, like Mo Willems, write and draw their own picture books. However, if you're not an illustrator by profession, that's perfectly fine too. The publisher will hire their own illustrator.

If you do have illustrations, the best thing to do is mention in your query letter that you have created some sample artwork for your story. Then, when an interested agent acquires your book, they will share your story with the publisher, who will then commission their own professional illustrator to do the final art. You won't be paid for the illustrations unless you specifically offer to do the full book yourself, which is a rare scenario.
