Here’s a way you could structure the email:

First, state your purpose clearly and professionally. You could write: "I hope you're doing well. I scored a 64 on our recent essay-style exam and was wondering if, at your convenience, I could get some feedback on what I could improve on to perform better in future assessments?"

Next, add a sentence to acknowledge the subjective nature you mentioned without challenging him directly. For example: "As the exam focused on written analysis, I found the content to be strong, but I'm eager to understand how your grading criteria led to that score."

Finally, close it by reinforcing that your focus is on your own growth, not disputing the grade. You could say: "My goal is to strengthen my skills for this course and future work. Thank you for your time and guidance."

Stick to that script, and you should have a much better chance of a constructive conversation.
