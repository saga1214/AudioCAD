Absolutely. Lockport's identity is deeply tied to its history as the heart of the Erie Canal.

For a great overview, you should definitely visit the Lockport Canal & Wharf Museum. It's housed in an old warehouse and does an excellent job of explaining the city's economic boom and the lock's engineering. 

You can also walk along the canal and see the modern locks in operation; it's pretty impressive to watch the boats get lifted up. And of course, the "Flight of Five" locks near the Niagara Escarpment are a major attraction and the direct subject of that spy rumor you asked about. A good way to see everything is to rent a bike and just ride the canal path, which is called the Canal Edge Trail.
