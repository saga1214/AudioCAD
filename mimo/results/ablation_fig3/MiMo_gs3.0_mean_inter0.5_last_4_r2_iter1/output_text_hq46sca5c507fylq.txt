That's thoughtful of you! Here are some meaningful gift ideas tailored to hands-on hobbies, depending on which path you decide to take:

### For Tailoring:
1. **Specialized Tools:**  
   - High-quality sewing shears (e.g., Gingher) for precise cuts.  
   - A tailor’s ham or a pattern weights set.  
2. **Vintage-Inspired Supplies:**  
   - Parker 51-themed thread or pen repair kits (tying back to your existing skill!).  
   - A vintage fabric sample book or a unique linen/cotton bundle.  
3. **Learning Resources:**  
   - A book like *The Tailor’s Art* by David East.  
   - A pattern drafting course or fabric swatch kit.  

### For Woodworking:
1. **Essential Starter Tools:**  
   - A quality hand saw or a cordless drill kit.  
   - Clamps (even basic spring clamps are useful).  
2. Clay Work Gifts:  
   - A stoneware wheel kit (budget-friendly options exist).  
   - Natural clay, leather-hard pottery, or sculpting tools.  
   - A book on pottery or wood carving.  

### For General Hands-On Hobbies:
- **Project Starter Kits:**  
  A tote bag template + fabric for sewing, a mini wood carving set, or a sculpting clay pack.  
- **Skill-Building Tools:**  
  A seam ripper, a small mallet for woodworking, or a sculpting brush set.  
- **Personalized Touches:**  
  Engraved tools (e.g., a chisel with your initials) or a custom workshop apron.  

### Key Tips:
- **Avoid Overly Expensive Items Early On:** Stick to mid-range tools/books unless you’re committed.  
- **Quality Over Quantity:** One excellent tool (like shears/clamps) can last a lifetime.  
- **Encourage Creativity:** Gift kits for small projects (e.g., "make a tote bag" kits) to build confidence.
