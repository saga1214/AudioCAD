Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! I’d say check out **Yamashiro** for high-end Japanese ramen, **Nong’s Noodle Bar** for a more casual but delicious Sichuan-style vibe, or **Shabu Shabu** for a great hot pot experience. If you’re craving something quick, **Mitsuwa** (the Japanese grocery market) has amazing bento boxes and noodles for a grab-and-go meal. 

For protein bars, it depends on your taste—**Quest** bars are high-protein and low-sugar, **Lärabar** is more natural and nut-based, and **RXBAR** is simple with just a few ingredients. If you want something sweet, **Clif Bars** or **Kind Bars** are solid too. Let me know if you want more recommendations!
