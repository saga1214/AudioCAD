You know, Ancient Egypt definitely dominates pop culture. But if the museum's goal isn't just to entertain but to, say, broaden cultural horizons, Mesopotamia is actually the better choice. Its educational importance scores the highest, artifact variety is strong if loans can be secured, and storytelling around writing and cities is incredibly rich, it just needs better framing for the public.

Given that, and if artifact availability is a major hurdle, I'd even suggest a compromise. A hybrid exhibit, maybe focusing on 'Nile versus Tigris,' could leverage Egypt's visual power to introduce Mesopotamia's history in a really engaging way. So, while numbers technically favor Egypt, my final recommendation would actually be for Mesopotata—provided the museum is willing to do the educational legwork.
