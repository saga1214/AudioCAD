I understand your point. Your portion isn't inherently 'too much' in isolation.

However, when it makes up your entire day's calories and other nutrients, it contributes to a high sodium load for the whole meal. Chronic high sodium intake is linked to increased blood pressure and cardiovascular risks. So, even if it's your only meal, having such a high sodium portion daily isn't considered ideal for long-term health.

If you want to lower it next time, you could replace tinned tomatoes with about 200 grams of fresh cherry tomatoes without salt, use low-sodium oyster and soy sauces, or add more vegetables to dilute the sodium per calorie.
