Okay, kosher dietary laws. The main thing to know is that it prohibits mixing meat and dairy, and wine is a dairy product. So, Tony can't eat any dairy-based snacks. Hummus you mentioned earlier is out, fruit platters are great because they’re fruit. Munees are meat, so that’s fine. Chocolate cookies, as long as they're made with dairy-free chocolate, would be okay. For theme food like the blood pudding, you’d just need to make sure there's no dairy in the sauce or anything. For drinks, Tony can't have the white or red wine, but you could bring a kosher-certified non-alcoholic option, like sparkling grape juice. Or just switch to tea or other non-kosher beverages. The key is just to have some dairy-free options for him and avoid serving anything with dairy. That way everyone is included.
