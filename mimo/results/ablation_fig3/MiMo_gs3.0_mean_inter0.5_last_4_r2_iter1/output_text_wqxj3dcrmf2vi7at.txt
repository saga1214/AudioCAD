What a delightful little princess! Here's a **recap with ideas grouped by type**, tailored for brown/white beauty:

### 🧊 Homemade Freezable Fun
1. **Pumpkin Bites**: Mashed canned pumpkin mixed with a tiny bit of Greek yogurt. Spoon onto silicone molds or ice cube trays. Freeze solid – she'll love the chill!
2. **Sweet Potato Bites**: Grate sweet potato, mix with a touch of oats or pumpkin puree, form into small discs, and freeze.
3. **Yogurt & Blueberry Bites**: Mix plain Greek yogurt, mashed blueberries, and a pinch of oats. Freeze on a baking sheet, then transfer to a bag.

### 🍪 Baked Goodness (Peanut Butter Free Option Too!)
4. **Peanut Butter & Oat Cookies**: Mix PB (XYLitol-free!) with oats, shape into balls, flatten, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 mins. Cool completely.
5. **Fish & Oatmeal Balls**: Finely chop cooked salmon, mix with oats, roll into small balls, and bake briefly.
6. *(PB-Free Option)* **Banana & Oat Cookies**: Mash banana, mix with oats, roll into balls, flatten, and bake.

### 🐟 Simple Pieces & Veggies
7. **Cooked Chicken**: Plain, boiled chicken breast, cut into tiny cubes.
8. **Cooked Carrot Sticks**: Steam carrot sticks until soft, then cut into thin matchsticks (ensure they are cooked soft enough to be safe!).

### 🛒 Store-Bought Picks (Check Labels!)
*   **Training Treats**: Zuke's Mini Nibbles, Greenies Dog Bites (small size), Sweet 'n Salty training chews.
*   **Fun Shapes/Flavors**: From brands like Wellness, Blue Buffalo, or Merrick. Look for small, soft, low-calorie options.
*   **Dental/Whisker**: KONG Wobbler Pup Pals or similar – often come in fun shapes/sizes.

### 🎨 Collar Color Magic for Brown & White
*   **POP**: **Teal, Turquoise, or Royal Blue** – Creates a beautiful contrast.
*   **Elegant**: **Soft Blush Pink, Gold Foil, or Satin Black** – Adds a touch of glamour.
*   **Classic & Bold**: **Ivory Cream or Light Silver** – Complements without clashing.
*   **Fun & Playful**: **Pastel Mint Green or Lavender** – A unique pop!

**Key Tips for Princess:**
*   **Portion Control:** Corgis are adorable but can be prone to weight gain. Stick to tiny pieces!
*   **Consistency:** Use the same treats and commands for training.
*   **Potty Training:** Be patient! Reward *only* successes. Clean accidents thoroughly with enzymatic cleaner.
*   **Collar Fit:** It should be snug enough that she can't slip out, but with a little room to move her head. Add a safety release buckle!
