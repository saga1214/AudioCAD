Here's a complete shopping list for a sand-free, pirate-themed nautical birthday party at the beach, designed for seven-year-olds including one with a watermelon allergy:

### **Appetizers & Snacks**  
*(Themed as "Pirate Supplies" or "Treasure")*  
- **"Mermaid Coin" Goldfish Crackers** (boxed)  
- **"Cannonball" Mini Sandwiches** (cut into small rectangles)  
  - Bread: 1 loaf  
  - Cheese cubes or cream cheese spread  
  - Turkey or ham slices  
  - *Avoid mustard/pickles to prevent drips*  
- **"Ship Wheel" Cheese Wedges** (mini cheddar or mozzarella)  
- **Grapes** (instead of watermelon for allergy safety)  
- **Pineapple chunks**  
- **Strawberries** (halved)  
- **Pretzel rods** (as "ship masts")  
- **Popcorn** (in bags labeled "Mermaid Tail Treasure")  

### **Main Course**  
*(Easy-to-handle "Dinner at Sea")*  
- **Mini Pirate Ship Sliders** (cut into small portions):  
  - 8-10 slider buns  
  - Fillings: Turkey-ham combo, cheese, or fruit spreads  
- **"Doubloon" Pasta Salad** (in small jars):  
  - Rotini pasta (shaped like coins)  
  - Cherry tomatoes (halved)  
  - Mozzarella balls  
  - Parmesan shavings  
  - Olive oil/vinegar dressing  
- **"Cannonball" Deviled Eggs** (in mini cups)  

### **Sides**  
*(Light, no-sand-friendly options)*  
- **"Pirate Gold" Popcorn** (in bags)  
- **"Mermaid Tail" Cucumber Slices** (dipped in yogurt ranch)  
- **Apple slices** (tossed in lemon juice to prevent browning)  
- **Cheese cubes** (with crackers for dipping)  

### **Drinks**  
*(Individual, spill-proof "Pirate Punch")*  
- **Juice boxes** (strawberry, apple, or grape—*avoid watermelon*)  
- **Sparkling water** (in cans or sealed bottles)  
- **Iced tea** (in individual cups)  
- **Themed labels**: Write "PIRATE PUNCH" or "SEA BREEZE" on paper tags  

### **Allergy Note**  
- **Double-check labels** for watermelon derivatives (e.g.,西瓜粉).  
- Use **clearly labeled containers** (e.g., "NO WATERMELON") to avoid cross-contamination.  

### **Beach-Friendly Tips**  
- Serve in **small plates/bowls** with **napkins** (call them "parasols").  
- Use **paper cones or cups** for popcorn/deviled eggs.  
- Pack **wet wipes** for quick cleanups.  

This setup keeps everything bite-sized, sealed, and sand-resistant while maximizing pirate fun! 🏴‍☠️⚓
