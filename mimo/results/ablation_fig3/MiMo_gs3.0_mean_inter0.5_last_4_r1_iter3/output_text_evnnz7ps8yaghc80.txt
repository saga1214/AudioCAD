Here’s a stress-friendly,儿子承诺驱动的 jugglin' schedule designed for panic-to-proficiency transformation:

周一：两球 beanbags only (Dominant Hand Drill)
Focus: Refine one-handed two-ball rhythm.
Do: 15 minutes of ONLY one-handed two-ball beanbag practice. Toss beanbags until your wrist movement is relaxed and smooth. Add music if helpful!

周二：过渡特训三球初探
Method 1 Focus:
Hold two beans LHand + one R.
THROWS ONLY: R -> Peak -> L -> Peak -> Last Bean.
Do: 10 mins throws ONLY. Catch is bonus. Reset balls if they drop. Celebrate every successful sequence!

周三：方法二过渡 | One-Hand Drill Mastery
Choose Method 2 Day OR Master One-Hand Two Beans RHand.
Do: If Method 1 Day: More first-three-throw practice.
If Mastering Day: Juggle two beans ONLY in RIGHT hand until effortless. Feel that rhythm deeply.

周四： scarf exploration & rhythm game
Do: 15 mins scarf play.
1. Toss scarves gently. Notice how slow they fall? Focus on spacing throws.
2. Juggle two scarves one-handed to master beanbag rhythm.
3. Attempt adding third scarf ball-throws.

周五：混合挑战日
Do: Rotate beanbags and scarves in 10-min blocks.
Example: 10 mins bean three attempts -> 10 mins scarves (two & three practice) -> 10 mins bean

周六：儿子教学预演
Do: 20 mins full三球 beanbags practice with catching focus.
Goal: Get ONE clean cascade for him (patience!). Show scarf tricks too!

周日：休息或趣味练习
Do: No pressure! Maybe watch a juggling tutorial, toss beans mindfully, or juggle scarves for fun. Recovery is key!

**Key Schedule Tips:**
- **Short & Sweet:** Sessions are 10-20 mins max. Less stress = faster learning.
- **Scarf Advantage:** Tuesdays/Thursdays let scarves teach timing without the panic of drops.
- **Progress Tracking:** Mark a checkmark on your calendar for every practice day. Visual progress crushes panic!
- **Stress Busters:** If frustrated: STOP. Do 1 minute of beanbag tosses with ZERO catches. Breathe. Try again.

You promised joy—you *can* deliver. That transition stress fades when you stop chasing catches and start dancing with the rhythm. By Friday, you'll juggle three.
