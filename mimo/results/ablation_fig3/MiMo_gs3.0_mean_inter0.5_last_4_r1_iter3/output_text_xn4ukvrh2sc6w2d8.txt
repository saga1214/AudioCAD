Here's a complete, bartender-ready list with classic recipes, measurements, and key details for your Art Deco cocktail reception:

### **Old Fashioned**
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey  
  - 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (for garnish + expressed oils)  
- **Egg White Option**:  
  - 0.5 oz pasteurized egg white (add before ice; *optional*).  
- **Method**:  
  1. Rub orange peel on glass rim. Add sugar cube or simple syrup.  
  2. Muddle sugar cube (or stir simple syrup) with bitters.  
  Rocks glass over ice.  
  3. Stir 15–20 seconds. Garnish with Otw peel.  
  Cherry optional.  

---

### **Paper Plane**
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  - ¾ oz Maraschino liqueur  
  coupe  
  - ¼ oz crème de violette  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice (LJ)  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into chilled aviation coupe glass.  
  3. Garnish with cherry (optional).  

---

### **Whiskey Sour**
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  fresh lemon juice (LJ)  
  coupe  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice (LJ)  
  - ½ oz simple syrup  
- **Egg White Version**:  
  - 0.5 oz pasteurized egg white (dry shake first).  
- **Method**:  
  1. *Dry shake*: Add bourbon, LJ, simple syrup, and egg white (if using) to shaker. Shake without ice.  
  2. *With ice shake*: Add ice, shake vigorously.  
  3. Strain into coupe glass over ice.  
  4. Garnish with lemon twist or cherry.  

---

### **Recommended Gin Cocktail: French 75**
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz London Dry Gin  
  coupe  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice (LJ)  
  - ½ oz simple syrup  
  - 0.25 oz crème de violette  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake gin, LJ, simple syrup, and cr. violette with ice.  
  2. Strain into chilled French 75 coupe.  
  3. Top with 2 oz chilled sparkling wine (Champagne or prosecco).  
  4. Garnish with lemon twist.  

---
