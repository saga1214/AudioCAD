Here are quick, elegant recipes for both cocktails—keeping dietary restrictions and soda allergy in mind:

### 🥛 1. **Simple Vodka Mixer**  
*(Non-alcoholic or with alcohol—adjust to taste)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka (or omit for mocktail)  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup (or 1 tsp sugar + 1 oz water, heated)  
- Top with **sparkling water** (not soda!) or soda-free tonic  
- Garnish: Lemon wheel + mint sprig  

**Why it works:** Refreshing, versatile, and soda-free. Customize with fruit (berries, cucumber) if you have time.

---

### 🌿 2. **Argentine-Style Fernet-Morin**  
*(Non-alcoholic herbal drink)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz **Fernet-Branca** (or substitute with Argentine Fernet-Morin if available)  
- 4–6 oz **sparkling water** (soda-free)  
- 1–2 mint leaves  
- Ice  
- Optional: Lime wedge  

**Instructions:**  
1. Fill a glass with ice.  
2. Add Fernet and sparkling water. Stir gently.  
3. Squeeze mint leaves over the drink (releases oils) + garnish with mint/more ice.  

**Why it works:** Light, aromatic, and aligns with the soda allergy. The mint enhances the herbal notes.

---

### 🧾 Key Tips:  
- **Label drinks:** Clearly mark alcoholic vs. non-alcoholic to avoid confusion.  
- **Mocktail twist:** For non-drinkers, offer the vodka-mixer without vodka, or a Fernet-mocktail with soda + mint.  
- **Balance:** Pair with desserts like chocolate mousse (for omnivores) or berry tart (vegetarian-friendly).
