### Step-by-Step Fix Plan:
#### 1. **Plunger (Your Best First Tool)**  
   - Use a **flat plunger** (if available). Seal it tightly over the drain.  
   - Pump **20+ strong, steady motions** straight down and up.  
   - *Why?* Pressure can dislodge deep clogs without tools.

#### 2. **Basic Drain Snake (If in Your Toolbox)**  
   - Insert the snake tip deep into the drain.  
   - Twist it slowly to catch the clog. Pull it out steadily.  
   - *Tip*: If it’s stuck, wiggle it gently—don’t force it (risk breaking pipe).

#### 3. **Hot Water + Dish Soap**  
   - Pour **boiling water** (carefully!) down the drain.  
   - Follow with **1 cup dish soap**. Let it sit 5 minutes.  
   - Flush with another pour of boiling water.  
   - *Why?* Soap softens grease; heat melts debris.

#### 4. **Baking Soda & Vinegar (If You Have Supplies)**  
   - Mix **½ cup baking soda** + **½ cup vinegar**.  
   - Pour down the drain. Let it fizz for 15 minutes.  
   - Follow with **2 cups boiling water**.  
   - *Caution*: Avoid this if the drain is already septic (can backfire).

#### 5. **Manual Removal (Last Resort)**  
   - If visible, use pliers to gently scrape stuck debris.  
   - Never use a knife or sharp tools—risk cutting pipes.
