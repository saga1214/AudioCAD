Of course! With your two-bed apartment and first baby on the way, I'd suggest starting with some shorter trips. Here are five fantastic day trips from Vancouver that are perfect for new parents and soon-to-be parents.

First, there's the Whistler Blackcomb area. It's famous for skiing, but in the summer, it's a beautiful mountain town for hiking and biking.

Second, you can visit Victoria, the capital of British Columbia. It's a charming city with lovely gardens and a historic inner harbour.

Third, on Vancouver Island, is Tofino. It's on the wild Pacific coast with stunning beaches and great whale watching.

Fourth, the Okanagan Lake area is a great choice. It's a sunny inland valley with beautiful lakes and wineries.

And fifth, you could explore the Fraser Canyon region, which has historic towns like Kamloops and the Kettle Valley Regional Park. Enjoy your adventures!
