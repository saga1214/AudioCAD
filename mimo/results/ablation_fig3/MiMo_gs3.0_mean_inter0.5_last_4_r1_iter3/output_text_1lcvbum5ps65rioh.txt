### U-Turn Safety in British Columbia
**Key Rules:**  
1. **No Signal Required** if no signs/lines prohibit U-turns.  
2. **Visibility Minimum:** Must see **150m** in both directions (roughly 1.5 football fields).  
3. **Prohibited Areas:**  
   - Near intersections, curves, hills, or driveways.  
   - On highways (except designated turnarounds).  
4. **Penalties:**  
   - **$60-$300 fine** + 3 demerit points for illegal U-turns.  
   - **More severe** if causing danger (e.g., near intersections).

**Easy Safety Check:**  
- **Stop fully** before turning.  
- **Look left-right-left** (150m = ~10 seconds of clear visibility).  
- **Use mirrors** to check blind spots.  
- **Signal early** if legal.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients
**For 4 servings:**  
| **Category**       | **Ingredients**                                                                 |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Pasta**          | 400g dried spaghetti (or bucatini)                                              |
| **Sauce Base**     | 3 large eggs + 3 egg yolks<br>150g grated **Pancetta** (or thick-cut bacon)    |
| **Cheese**         | 150g grated **Pecorino Romano** (or Parmesan blend)                             |
| **Seasoning**      | Freshly ground black pepper (optional)                                          |
| **Extras**         | 1 tbsp olive oil (for pancetta)                                                 |

**Key Notes:**  
- Use **pancetta** (Italian cured pork belly) for authenticity.  
- **Never add cream** – classic carbonara is egg-based.  
- **Temper eggs** by slowly mixing in hot pasta water to avoid scrambling.
