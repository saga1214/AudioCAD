Based on your potluck-style gathering menu suggestions, here's a breakdown of the appliances and cooking methods you'll need:

### Appliances:
1. **Smoker**: For smoking the turkey.
2. **Oven (x2)**: One for the smoked turkey (if smoked), roasted prime rib, stuffing, mac and cheese, or roasted root vegetables. The second oven can handle desserts like the fruit tart or cranberry sauce.
3. **Instant Pot**: For making cranberry sauce (or as a backup for cranberry sauce on the stovetop).
4. **Toaster Oven**: For roasting Brussels sprouts salad or preheating space for smaller dishes.
5. **Stovetop**: For preparing salads (shredding Brussels sprouts, pears, nuts), chopping vegetables, and reheating food.

### Cooking Methods:
1. **Smoke**: Whole turkey (for smoked main course).
2. **Roast**: Prime rib or whole turkey (for roasted main course).
3. No-Bake: Pumpkin cheesecake, icebox cake, or apple crisp.
4. Roast (in oven or toaster oven): Brussels sprouts salad, root vegetables.
5. Bake: Mashed potatoes, mac and cheese, stuffing, fruit tart.
6. Instant Pot: Cranberry sauce (or stovetop alternative).
7. Prepare fresh: Salads (Brussels sprouts, wild rice, pear and Gorgonzola).

### Key Tips:
- **Streamline with Timing**: Smoke the turkey ahead, roast the prime rib last-minute, and bake sides during oven downtime.
- **Delegate where you can**: Ask guests to cover cranberry sauce, dessert components, or drinks to lighten your load.
- **No-Bake Saver**: Make pumpkin cheesecake, icebox cake, or apple crisp ahead to minimize Thanksgiving Day stress.
