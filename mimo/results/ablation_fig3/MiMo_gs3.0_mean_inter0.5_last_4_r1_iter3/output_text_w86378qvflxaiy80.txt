That's a really important question. While the ecosystem services and medicine are definitely huge factors, the most fundamental aspect of a hotspot is its irreplaceable uniqueness.

Norman Myers designed the term this way for a reason. The requirement of those endemic plant species—that "nowhere else" quality—is the core of the whole concept. It highlights that we're not just talking about losing one species, but an entire library of genetic information that has never been read and can never be replicated anywhere else on Earth.

So, while protecting those vital services is critical, the term was coined to make us all aware of the irreversible loss of unique life and the genetic potential that comes with it. That's the root of the whole movement.
