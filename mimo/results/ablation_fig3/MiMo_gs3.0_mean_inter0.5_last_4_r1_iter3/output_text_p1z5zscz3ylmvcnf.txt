Second, the 1969 Ford Mustang Boss 400. That one’s a classic muscle car with a lot of history and demand. 

Third, a 1965-1967 Pontiac GTO. The GTO models, especially the Judge, are highly sought after. 

Fourth, a 1963 or 1964 Jaguar E-Type. It’s a British car, but he might overlook it since the styling is so iconic. They’ve been climbing in value. 

And fifth, the 1970 Dodge Challenger SRT/406. Another muscle car that’s become a collector’s item. 

For him, I’d suggest focusing on ones that are easy to maintain, like the Corvette or Mustang, since he changes his own oil. But if he’s up for a little more work, the GTO or Challenger could be great long-term plays. The Jaguar might be a stretch pride-wise, but it’s worth mentioning.
