Okay, efficiency can be measured in different ways—cost, scalability, availability, and reliability. Solar energy still fits best as the single most efficient overall renewable. Its costs keep dropping, it’s easy to scale from rooftops to huge farms, and storage is getting better. But hydropower is a very close second, especially where existing dams are used. It's incredibly reliable and efficient. Wind power is also strong in windy regions. So, if I absolutely had to pick just one, I’d stick with solar because of its broad availability and how quickly it's becoming more cost-effective. But in specific situations, like by a large dam, hydropower might be the top choice.
