Okay, you need a card for your mom too. Given earlier guilt over calling her overpowering but good rapport overall, balance warmth with sensitivity is key. Avoid overly sentimental language but show appreciation and love. A tone of fond familiarity works best. Here are a couple of options:

Option 1 (Simple & Direct):
"Mom,
Hope you're having a wonderful Christmas! Thanks for all you do. Wishing you a great holiday and a fantastic new year.
Love,
[Your Name]"

Option 2 (Slightly Warmer):
"Hi Mom,
Sending you lots of Christmas cheer! Really appreciate you and hope you're having a lovely holiday. Here's to more great memories in the new year.
Love you,
[Your Name]"

Both options:
*   Use familiar language ("Mom," "Hi Mom")
*   Express appreciation ("Thanks for all you do," "Really appreciate you")
*   Wish her well ("Hope you're having a wonderful Christmas," "Wishing you a great holiday," "Hope you're having a lovely holiday")
*   Look forward to the future ("Here's to more great memories," "Love you")
*   Avoid heavy sentimentality while clearly conveying love and care.
*   Keep it concise and warm.

Choose the one that feels most natural for your relationship. They both convey the right message of appreciation and love without being overly emotional.
