### Treat Recommendations:
*   **Best Flavors:** Small, soft, low-calorie treats are ideal. Look for:
    *   **Chicken:** Universally loved by puppies (your pup prefers it anyway!).
    *   **Beef or Turkey:** Similar to chicken, high-value and tasty.
    *   **Fish:** Mild flavor, often well-accepted.
    *   **Carrot Sticks or Green Beans:** Low-calorie veggies (ensure they're organic and washed).
    *   **Commercial Puppy Training Treats:** Designed to be small, soft, and easy to swallow (check ingredients).
    *   *Avoid:* Large, hard, or crunchy treats that could be choking hazards. Skip anything with xylitol (a dangerous sweetener).

### Clicker Training Explained (How it Works):
Clicker training uses a "marking" tool – the clicker – to precisely communicate to your puppy *exactly* the behavior you want them to repeat. It's a highly effective method based on positive reinforcement.

1.  **Mark the Behavior:** The moment your puppy does the *exact* thing you want (e.g., sits), you *click* the clicker. This sound is completely neutral to the puppy at first but becomes associated with a reward.
2.  **Reward Immediately:** The instant you click, you give them a small, high-value treat. The click becomes a signal meaning "That's the behavior I want! Get a treat!"
3.  **Repeat:** Practice many times in short sessions (5-10 minutes). Click for the *exact* sit position, then treat.
4.  **Build Duration & Distractions:** Once your puppy reliably sits on cue (after a click), you can add:
    *   **Longer Holds:** Click after they hold the sit for 2 seconds, then treat.
    *   **Adding Verbal Cue:** After clicking/treating for a few sessions, start saying "Sit" *just before* you click for the first treat. Gradually add the cue before each click/treat.
    *   **Adding Distractions:** Practice in quieter environments, then gradually move to busier spots (like your living room, then the backyard).

**Why it works so well:**
*   **Precision:** The click marks the *exact* moment the behavior happens, so the puppy knows *what* to repeat.
*   **Clarity:** The sound is distinct and easy for the puppy to learn.
*   **Positive Association:** It creates a strong link between the desired behavior and an immediate reward.

**Key Clicker Training Tips:**
*   **Be Consistent:** Always click for the same behavior in the same way.
*   **Use High-Value Treats:** Especially when starting out.
*   **Click *Then* Treat:** The click happens first, then the treat follows immediately.
*   **Start Simple:** Focus on one behavior (like sit) in a quiet environment before adding complexity.
*   **End on a Success:** Finish training sessions when your puppy is still enthusiastic.

For teaching "sit": Click -> Give Treat -> Say "Sit" -> Repeat. Practice until your puppy reliably sits on cue for the click. Then gradually add the verbal cue before the click/treat.
