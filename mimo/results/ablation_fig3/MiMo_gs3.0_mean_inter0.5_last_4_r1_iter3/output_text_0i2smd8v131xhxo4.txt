### Studio & Mic Tips
- **Harsh Audio Fix**: Speak into Yeti’s side (with logo), keep it 6–8 inches from your mouth, and angle slightly off-axis. Harshness often comes from off-axis placement or reflections.

### Vocal Endurance
- **Warmups**: Daily 10-minute routine: lip trills, humming, tongue twisters, and vocal sirens (sliding pitch).
- **Breathing**: Practice diaphragmatic breathing (belly breathing) to support phrases.
- **Hydration**: Sip room-temperature water hourly. Cold water constricts vocal cords.
- **Breaks**: Rest 5 mins every 20–30 minutes. Drink water, stretch, and avoid caffeine/alcohol.

### DAW & Editing
- **Adobe Audition vs. Audacity**: Audition is faster for multitrack editing, noise reduction, and automation (e.g., volume keyframing). The learning curve is steep but worthwhile for complex projects.
- **Plugins**: 
  - **Mouth Clicks**: iZotope RX 10 (De-click) or Waves Clarity Vx.
  - **Background Noise**: RX 10 (Denoise) or Audition’s Noise Reduction dialog.
- **AI Tools**: 
  - **Trim Silence**: Descript (auto-trims gaps).
  ️ **Labeling**: Otter.ai (transcription) or Descript’s "Edit in Studio" for easy edits.

### Workflow & Organization
- **Project Management**: Use cloud storage (Google Drive/Dropbox) with client-specific folders. Label files: `ClientName_Date_ScriptVersion.wav`.
- **Time-Saving**: Batch-edit similar clips. Use shortcuts (e.g., Audition’s "S" for selection, "C" for cut).

### Demo Clip Tips
- **Hook**: Start with a 3-second high-energy character moment.
- **Show Range**: Include 2–3 distinct voices (e.g., villain, child, narrator).
- **Keep It Short**: 45–60 seconds max. End with a "call to action" (e.g., "Hire me for [genre]!").

### Switching to Audition
- **Transition Plan**: 
  1. Export Audacity projects as WAV.
  2. Import into Audition’s "Multitrack Editor."
  3. Practice basics: volume automation, noise reduction, and rendering.
  4. Use YouTube tutorials (e.g., "Audition for Beginners" by Sweetwater).

### AI Tools Deep Dive
- **Descript**: AI-powered editing (e.g., "Search and Replace" finds spoken words).
- **Adobe Sensei**: Auto-normalizes volume, removes breaths.
- **Avoid Over-Reliance**: AI can’t replace nuanced mixing—use it for efficiency, not creativity.

### Vocal Health Habits
- **Meal Suggestion**: Order a **Green Curry Rice Bowl** from your Thai spot: 
  - *Why it fits*: Quick, flavorful, portable, and healthy (coconut aminos, veggies). Eat between takes to recharge.
  - *Bonus*: Pair with ginger tea (anti-inflammatory) post-session.

### Pro Balance Tip
- **Time Blocking**: Schedule 2-hour recording blocks → 1-hour editing → 30 mins marketing (social posts, emails). Use calendar apps to enforce breaks.
