For Asian noodles in Dallas, check out these spots:

First, there's Little Pho, a Vietnamese spot. They have amazing pho and noodle dishes.

Second, Sushi Dallas is great for sushi and other Japanese noodle dishes.

Third, for Chinese noodles, try Peking House. They’ve got great dumplings and noodle soups.

For protein bars, I’d recommend these:

First, Quest Bars. They’re high-protein, low-carb, and taste good.

Second, Optimum Nutrition. They’re classic and filling.

Third, Clif Builders. They’re good for post-workout recovery.

Hope you enjoy your meal!
