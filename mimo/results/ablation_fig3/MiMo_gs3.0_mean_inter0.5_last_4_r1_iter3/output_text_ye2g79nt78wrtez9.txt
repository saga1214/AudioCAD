Re-evaluating everything, especially with the artifact availability weighted so heavily, I'd have to say Ancient MezPaz is the superior choice. The difficulty in getting top Egypt artifacts like Tutankhamun's mask or the Rosetta Stone is a massive practical hurdle for most museums. Mesopotamia's artifacts, while less famous, are far more widely distributed. This makes a Mesopotamia exhibit not only more unique and culturally significant but also much more feasible to actually put on. The story of writing and early cities is just as profound and more accessible to an audience familiar with modern technology. So, for a museum that wants a powerful, well-told, and achievable story, Ancient MezPaz is the better bet.
