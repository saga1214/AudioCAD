Here’s a quick recap of homemade and store-bought ideas for your little corgi princess, formatted for easy reading:

### Homemade Treats  
1. **Frozen Bites**  
   - Mashed **pumpkin** or roasted/sliced **sweet potato**, formed into small balls, frozen solid.  
   - *Bonus:* Adds hydration and satisfies their sweet tooth!  

2. **Peanut Butter & Oat Cookies**  
   - Mix peanut butter (check for **NO xylitol**), oats, and a pinch of honey/maple syrup.  
   - Roll into balls, flatten, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10–12 minutes. Cool before cutting into pieces.  

3. **Simple Sticks/Chews**  
   - **Carrot sticks:** Wash, cut into thin sticks, and freeze for 30 minutes to chill.  
   - **Cooked chicken:** Freeze in small cubes for a healthy, protein-packed snack.  

### Store-Bought Brands  
- **Training Treats:** Zuke’s Mini Naturals, Wellness Train Pawsible Treats.  
- **Healthier Options:** Blue Buffalo Natural Training Bites, Merrick Healthy Puppy Bites (works for all ages).  
- **Odor-Control:** Greenies Pup Chews (chewable biscuits).  

### Training Tips  
- Pair potty training with scheduled outdoor breaks and immediate rewards.  
- Use a consistent cue (e.g., “go potty”) and a fun reward like a tiny treat.  

Your corgi princess will thrive with these tasty, healthy options! 🐾💕
