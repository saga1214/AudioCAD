Here's how to install each item safely and efficiently, keeping your landlord rules in mind:

### 1. **Mounting a TV**
   - **Tools Needed:** Level, stud finder, drill, wall anchors (if no studs), screwdriver, TV mount.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (wood beams behind drywall). This avoids damaging walls and complies with "no drilling holes" rules if you use drywall anchors instead.
     2. **Measure & Align:** Hold the TV mount against the wall, align it with stud marks, and use a level.
     3. **Drill Pilot Holes:** Drill holes slightly smaller than your screws into studs.
     4. **Install Mount:** Secure the mount to studs with screws. If no studs are available, use heavy-duty drywall anchors (quiet and compliant).
     5. **Attach TV:** Secure the TV to the mount with provided screws.
   - **Tip:** Place the TV where you’ll watch most (e.g., near your favorite chair). Use a **mounting arm** for adjustability.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Tools Needed:** Drill, wall anchors, wire/hook, measuring tape.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Choose Placement:** Pick a spot above a shelf or near a window (for light). Avoid high-traffic areas.
     2. **Measure & Mark:** Hold the planter against the wall, measure from the floor, and mark the hanging point.
     3. **Drill Hole:** Drill a hole slightly larger than your hook/wire.
     4. **Install Anchor:** Insert a plastic or metal anchor into the hole.
     5. **Hang Planter:** Secure a hook or S-hooks to the anchor. Hang the planter by its hanger.
   - **Low-Noise Alternative:** Use **staples** or **clear adhesive hooks** (no drilling). Ideal for pre-existing nails or molding.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Tools Needed:** Level, drill, wall anchors, shelf brackets, measuring tape.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Measure & Plan:** Decide shelf height/length. Use a level to ensure it’s straight.
     2. **Mark Bracket Positions:** Hold a bracket against the wall, align with stud marks, and mark screw holes.
     3. **Drill Pilot Holes:** Drill holes for screws into studs.
     4. **Attach Brackets:** Secure brackets to studs with screws.
     5. **Install Shelf:** Place the shelf onto the brackets and tighten screws.
   - **Stud-Free Solution:** Use **heavy-duty drywall anchors** or **L-brackets** for extra support (hold up to 50 lbs each).

### ⚠️ **Key Rules-Compliant Tips:**
- **Avoid Loud Noise:** Do installations during the day (e.g., Sunday afternoons). Use a **drill with a quiet mode**.
- **Minimal Drilling:** Use anchors/hooks where possible. If you must drill, work quickly and clean up debris.
- **Safety First:** Wear safety goggles. Test weight limits (e.g., shelves should hold 30-50 lbs).
