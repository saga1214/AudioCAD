That line of thought about genetic identity is interesting, but the scientific consensus still points to the chicken coming first.

Here's why: for a mutation to happen in the egg *before* it hatches, the egg had to have been produced by a hen. The eggshell-forming protein I mentioned earlier is exclusive to hens. So, the egg had to exist *as a chicken's egg* before the first true chicken could emerge from it.

In short, the first chicken had to hatch from a chicken's egg, not the other way around.
