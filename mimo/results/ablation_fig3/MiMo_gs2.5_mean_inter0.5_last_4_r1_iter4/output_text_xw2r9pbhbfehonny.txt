Sure, here's a quick checklist for filming your own puppet show:

First, get permission. Ask the facility ahead of time if you’re allowed to record and if there are any rules about it.

Next, set up your phone or camera. Place it where it’ll get good angles, maybe on a tripod, and make sure it’s stable so nothing gets shaky.

Then, test the lighting. You want it bright enough to see the puppets clearly, but not too harsh.

After that, prepare your script or notes. Since you do some improv, having a loose plan will help keep things on track.

Finally, just be yourself and have fun. The audience should feel your energy. 

That’s it, just keep it simple and enjoy the show.
