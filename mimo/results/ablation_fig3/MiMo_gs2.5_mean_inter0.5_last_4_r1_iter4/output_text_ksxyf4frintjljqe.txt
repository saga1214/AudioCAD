I'm truly sorry to hear about your family's loss. Losing someone who brought so much joy through their desserts, especially around a difficult holiday... it's incredibly hard. Browning brownies... peach cobbler... those smells and tastes will trigger so much grief for your brother and the whole family. Maybe this year, you can find a way to honor her memory without those specific triggers. Perhaps sharing favorite stories, or even creating a new ritual together. Skipping those desserts or doing something simpler is OK—if it helps the most right now. Just be gentle with yourselves.

But yes, for a chocolate dessert that goes in a pan... fudge brownies are always a crowd-pleaser. They're easy to make—just melt some chocolate and butter, mix in sugar and eggs, and pour it into a greased pan. Bake it at 350 degrees for about 20 minutes. They come out really fudgy and delicious. Everyone will enjoy them, without touching those triggers.
