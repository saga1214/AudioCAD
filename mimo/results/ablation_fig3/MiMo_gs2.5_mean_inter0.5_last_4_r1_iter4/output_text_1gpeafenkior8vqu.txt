**Design Aspect Tips (For Slates - Images/Layout/Color/font):**
*   **Images:** Prioritize high-resolution photos you took in Japan. Supplement with museum online collections (ensure copyright!). Focus on ukiyo-e prints, Buddhist sculptures, traditional ink paintings, kimono patterns, or modern interpretations.
*   **Layout:** Emulate Japanese composition principles - asymmetry, balance, use of negative space (ma), flowing lines inspired by nature. Avoid overcrowding.
*   **Color Scheme:** Use a harmonious palette. Options:
    *   Monochromatic (Black, White, Grey - Sumi-e).
    *   Earth Tones (Indigo, Brown, Cream - Ukiyo-e).
    *   Vibrant (Indigo, Red, White - Traditional Kimono).
    *   Minimalist (2-3 key colors).
*   **Font:** Use clean, modern sans-serifs for readability. For headings, consider fonts with subtle East Asian influences if appropriate and used sparingly. Ensure excellent contrast.
