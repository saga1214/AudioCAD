Here's a safe way to attach Velcro securely without damaging your jacket lining, using common household items:

**Best Temporary Methods (No Sewing Needed):**

1.  **Safety Pins (Best Option):**
    *   **Why:** They provide strong, adjustable hold without adhesive residue.
    *   **How:**
        *   Cut the Velcro loop (the soft, fuzzy side) to the desired length.
        *   Slide one end of a safety pin through the loop.
        *   Press the pin through the jacket fabric *twice* (once through the outer fabric, once through the lining) near where you want the pin.
        *   Press the remaining Velcro loop firmly against the jacket surface. The pin acts like a small anchor holding the loop in place. You can adjust the pin position easily.

2.  **Double-Sided Tape (Strong & Clean):**
    *   **Why:** Creates a strong, clean bond without residue (if using good quality tape).
    *   **How:**
        *   Cut the Velcro loop to the desired length.
        *   Apply a small strip of strong double-sided tape (like painter's tape or heavy-duty tape) to the back of the loop.
        *   Press the loop firmly onto the jacket surface. The tape holds it securely.
        *   **Tip:** Test the tape on an inconspicuous area first to ensure it won't damage the fabric or lining.

3.  ️ **Staple Gun (Use with Caution):**
    *   **Why:** Provides a very strong, permanent hold quickly.
    *   **How:**
        *   Cut the Velcro loop to the desired length.
        *   **Crucial:** Use a **light-duty staple gun** (like a standard brad nailer). Heavy-duty staples will likely tear the lining.
        *   Hold the loop perpendicular to the fabric.
        *   Apply a light staple *through* the loop *and* the outer fabric, just below the surface. Don't staple deep into the lining.
        *   **Warning:** This is the riskiest method. Use VERY sparingly, only on thick areas if possible, and test first. Avoid near seams or delicate areas.

**What NOT to Do:**

*   **Hot Glue Gun:** Can melt or damage fabric and lining.
*   **Strong Adhesive Glue:** Can leave a permanent residue that's hard to remove, especially from synthetic fabrics and linings.
*   **Stapling Deeply:** Will almost certainly puncture and damage the lining.

**Recommendation:**

*   **Start with Safety Pins:** They are the safest, most adjustable, and least likely to cause damage. Perfect for a temporary fix while you wait for new clothes from Nordstrom Rack.
*   **Double-Sided Tape:** A great clean option if you're confident the tape won't damage the fabric. Ensure it's strong and applied cleanly.
*   **Staples:** Only if you're very careful and use light-duty staples sparingly. Not ideal for delicate fabrics.

**For a Permanent Fix (If you want to sew later):**

*   You can easily sew the Velcro loop onto the jacket lining itself. Simply use a needle and thread or a sewing machine. This is the most durable solution.

**Key Takeaway:** Safety pins are your safest bet for a quick, damage-free attachment right now. Double-sided tape is also excellent if you can find the right kind. Avoid staples and adhesives unless you're very careful. Good luck with your jacket repair and your Nordstrom Rack order!
