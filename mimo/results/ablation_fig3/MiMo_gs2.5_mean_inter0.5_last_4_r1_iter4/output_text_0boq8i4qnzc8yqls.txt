Here’s a balanced, warm card for your mom that acknowledges your relationship gently while keeping it heartfelt:  

**“Dear Mom,**  
Thanks for planning our New Year’s time together! I’m looking forward to visiting—short gets quality, right? Wishing you a joyful, peaceful holiday season. Love you always.  

**With warmth,**  
[Your Name]”  

### Why this works:  
- **Addresses the visit length subtly**: Phrases like “short gets quality” reframe your earlier comment positively, focusing on connection over duration.  
- **Acknowledges planning**: Starting with “Thanks for planning” shows appreciation, softening any residual tension.  
- **Warm closing**: “Love you always” feels sincere without being overly emotional.  
- **Neutral tone**: Avoids over-apologizing or overpromising, keeping it light and genuine.  

If you’d like a slightly more personal touch, add a brief specific memory:  
*“Can’t wait to catch up over [her favorite coffee/dinner]!”*  

This approach keeps it loving, respectful, and low-pressure—perfect for easing any guilt while setting a comfortable dynamic. Wishing you a wonderful visit! 🌟
