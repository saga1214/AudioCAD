For the brooder plate, the best bedding is wood shavings, again using the deep litter approach. Just continuously add fresh ones on top of the composting layer below to generate natural warmth.

To keep rodents out, you could also use hardware cloth or a fine mesh over the brooder area. And it's very important to keep the floor dry. If you're using sand as bedding inside the main coop, you can use it in the brooder too, but make sure you have good drainage to keep it from getting too damp.

So, to sum up, I'd recommend using pine shavings with the deep litter method for warmth, and sand for rodent resistance. Then, for your winter chicks, use a safe brooder plate with one of those two bedding types. That should get you through.
