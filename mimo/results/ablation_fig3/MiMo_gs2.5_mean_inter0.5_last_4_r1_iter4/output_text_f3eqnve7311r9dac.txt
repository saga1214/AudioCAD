Here are ten notable titles from the 1940s and 50s that are rare and valuable. The prices I'll mention are for high-grade, professionally graded copies. 

First, *Action Comics* number one, Superman's debut, sold for six million recently. 
Second, as mentioned, *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen for three point six million. 
Third, *Captain America Comics* number one from 1941 for just over three million. 
Fourth, a key Marvel book is *Marvel Super-Heroes* number one, which is the first appearance of the Fantastic Four, selling in 2024 for about one point five million. 
Fifth, a huge one is *X-Men* number one from 1991, which is the first appearance of Cable. A copy sold in 2023 for over one million dollars. 
Sixth, *The Amazing Spider-Man* number 300, the first full appearance of Venom, which sold for about seven hundred and fifty thousand. 
Seventh, *Daredevil* number one from 1964, which is his first solo appearance, sold in 2023 for around three hundred and fifty thousand. 
Eighth, *Fantastic Four* number forty-eight, which features Galactus for the first time, sold in 2021 for about two hundred and fifty thousand.
