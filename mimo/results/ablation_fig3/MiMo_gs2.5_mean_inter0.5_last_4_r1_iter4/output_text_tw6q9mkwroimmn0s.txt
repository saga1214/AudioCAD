That sounds like a fantastic theme night! Here are some ocean-inspired meal and dessert ideas, keeping your fiancée's dietary needs in mind:

**Ocean-Themed Meals (5 Ideas):**

1.  **Spicy Mango Shrimp Stir-Fry (Adapted):** While she can't have shellfish, shrimp *is* seafood and usually fine with diabetes (moderation, check with her doc!). If you absolutely want to avoid all seafood, swap the shrimp for large shrimp-like mushrooms or extra mango chunks. Serve over rice or noodles with a spicy peanut or coconut sauce and stir-fried veggies.
2.  **Mango & Coconut Fish Cakes:** Use flaked white fish (like cod or tilapia) mixed with cooked rice or breadcrumbs, shredded mango, coconut flakes, and spices. Form into patties, pan-fry or bake until golden. Serve with a spicy mango chutney or dipping sauce.
3.  **Tropical Citrus & Herb Grilled Salmon:** Salmon is a great option for diabetes (rich in omega-3s). Marinate in lime juice, orange juice, mango puree, garlic, and fresh herbs (cilantro, dill). Grill or bake to perfection. Serve with a side of spicy mango salsa.
4.  **Shrimp & Mango Skewers (Ensure Shellfish-Free Shrimp):** Use large shrimp (make sure they are truly shellfish-free if that's a concern, or simply omit if needed). Thread onto skewers with chunks of mango, pineapple, bell peppers, and red onion. Grill or broil. Serve with a spicy peanut dipping sauce or a simple mango-lime glaze.
5.  **"Ceviche-Style" Tuna Salad with Mango:** For a lighter option, use high-quality tuna (canned in water, drained well - not shellfish!). "Cocktail" it in a mix of lime juice, orange juice, finely diced red onion, cilantro, and diced mango. Serve in lettuce cups or with crackers. Add diced avocado for creaminess.

**Ocean-Themed Desserts (5 Sweet Ideas):**

1.  **Mango & Coconut Panna Cotta:** A creamy, refreshing dessert. Make a vanilla or mango-flavored panna cotta with coconut milk as the base. Top with fresh mango chunks and a drizzle of caramel sauce (optional). Looks stunning in clear glasses.
2.  **Ocean Blue Mousse:** Layer vibrant blue (like butterfly pea flower tea or blueberry puree) and white (coconut or vanilla mousse) in glasses. Top with fresh blueberries and a sprinkle of edible blue spirulina powder for an "ocean floor" effect.
3.  **Banana "Fish" Pudding:** Slice bananas and arrange them to look like fish on a plate. Serve with a sweet, spicy mango puree or compote as the "sea." Add shredded coconut for sand.
4.  **Shaved Ice "Beach":** Serve vanilla or lychee shaved ice as the "beach." Top generously with fresh mango chunks, diced pineapple, red bean paste (sweet red bean), and a sprinkle of crushed pistachios or coconut flakes for "seashells."
5.  **Ocean Treasure Parfaits:** Layer vanilla or coconut yogurt with mango puree and granola (or crushed graham crackers for a "beach" texture) in glasses. Add a few blueberries or raspberries for "waves" and maybe a few edible gold sprinkles for "treasure."

**Tips for Your Ocean Night:**

*   **Decor:** Use blue and green tones, seashells, sand-colored fabrics, string lights, and maybe some small fish or coral decorations. Play ocean sounds softly in the background.
*   **Dress Code:** Encourage casual, beachy attire!
*   **Music:** Soft, ambient ocean sounds or chill island music.
*   **Glasses:** Use clear glasses for the panna cotta or mousse to show off the layers.

Have an absolutely wonderful and romantic ocean-themed dinner night! It sounds like a perfect way to enjoy cooking together.
