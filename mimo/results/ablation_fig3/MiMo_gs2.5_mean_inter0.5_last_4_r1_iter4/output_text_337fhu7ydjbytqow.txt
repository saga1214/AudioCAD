### **For the 20-Mile Daytime Segment (Tomorrow Night)**  
*(Mid-30s, Hilly, Aid Stations Available)*  
1. **Base Layer**:  
   - Moisture-wicking shirt (long-sleeve for sun protection + warmth).  
   - Moisture-wicking shorts or leggings (with liner if needed).  
2. **Mid-Layer**:  
   - Fleece jacket or light puffy vest (removable for hills).  
   - Windbreaker or rain shell (if rain is possible).  
3. **Outer Layer**:  
   - Light windbreaker or rain jacket (water-resistant).  
4. **Hands/Head**:  
   - Gloves (lightweight for day, switch to insulated later).  
   - Cap or hat (sun protection).  
   - Headlamp/handheld light (even during day – races can get dark fast).  
5. **Extras**:  
   - Lightweight pack or vest (carry layers/essentials).  
   - Buff or neck gaiter (multi-use: warmth, sweat absorption, sun shield).  
   - Socks (quick-dry, trail-specific).

### **For the Final 12-Mile Night Segment**  
*(Run After Dark, Likely Colder)*  
1. **Base Layer**:  
   - Moisture-wicking long-sleeve shirt (thermal if possible).  
   - Thermal leggings or tights (add warmth).

   - Fleece jacket or lightweight puffy.  
3. **Outer Layer**:  
   - Insulated jacket or fleece (e.g., 50°F-rated) + windbreaker.  
4. **Hands/Head**:  
   - Insulated gloves or mittens.  
   - Warm hat (fleece-lined or balaclava for extreme cold).  
   headlamp/handheld light (with **extra batteries** – critical!).  
5. **Extras**:  
   - Lightweight pack (carry extra layers if needed).

### **Key Tips**:  
- **Layers > Bulk**: Choose items you can easily add/remove. Aid stations have nutrition – use them to swap layers.  
- **Battery Backup**: Carry spare batteries for your headlamp – races drain them fast.  
- **Foot Protection**: Trail shoes with grip + extra socks (chafing risk at night).  
- **Visibility**: Reflective gear or lights if aid stations are sparse.
