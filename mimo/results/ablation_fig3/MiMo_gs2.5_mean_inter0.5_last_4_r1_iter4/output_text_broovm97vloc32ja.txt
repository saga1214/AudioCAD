That's a fantastic question, and you're right to ask. For a children's book, if you're the illustrator, the approach is a bit different. You can absolutely submit your own artwork. In fact, it's a strong advantage.

Here’s what you should do: first, get your manuscript perfectly polished. Then, you'll need to create a few sample illustrations to go with the text. Publishers love to see this because it shows your vision for the book. You can find a template online for what's called a "book sample" or a "book dummy."

Once you have that complete package, you can query publishers directly. There are specific websites that list publishers who are open to submissions from self-illustrated authors. You can also look into contests and grants for children's book illustrators as another avenue.
