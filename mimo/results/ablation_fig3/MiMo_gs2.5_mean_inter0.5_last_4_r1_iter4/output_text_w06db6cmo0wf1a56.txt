Absolutely! Here's a well-balanced, low-mess shopping list designed for 10 dads, 10 kids, sugar-free sodas, mini gourmet calzones, and grab-and-go desserts—all packed in coolers for maximum convenience:

---

### **DRINKS**  
*(Pre-filled 24-oz water bottles, 12 sugar-free sodas per group)*  
- **Water bottles (40 total)**: Reusable bottles for adults/kids.  
- **Sugar-free sodas (20 cans)**: Pick your favorites (e.g., Zevia, Olipop, or zero-sugar Coke/Dr. Pepper).  
- **Low-sugar juice boxes (15 boxes)**: For picky kids (e.g., apple or cranberry).  
- **Electrolyte packets (10 individual packs)**: For hot-day hydration (e.g., LMNT or Nuun).  

---

### **SNACKS**  
*(All single-serve, no wrappers)*  
- **Cheese sticks (20 sticks)**: Mixed cheddar/mozzarella.  
- **Grapes (10 lbs)**: Pre-washed, easy for kids.  
- **Single-serve pretzels/trail mix (10 bags)**: Individual microwave bags.  
- **Baby carrots (10 lbs)**: With **10 single-serve hummus cups**.  

---

### **MAIN MEAL**  
- **Mini gourmet calzones (40 pieces)**:  
  - *Options*: Cheese, pepperoni, veggie, or chicken-mozzarella.  
  - *Where to get*: Order from a pizzeria/gourmet deli (ask for "mini" sizes—they’re crispy, handheld, and ready in 20 mins).  
  - *Tip*: Pack in airtight containers to prevent sogginess.  

---

### **DESSERT**  
*(Minimal cleanup)*  
- **Mini cupcakes (40 pieces)**: Frosting-free or sprinkle-only (easier to eat).  
- **Brownie bites (20 boxes)**: Single-serving packs.  
- **Dessert optional**: Add **10 mini ice cream sandwiches** if budget allows.  

---

### **COOLER ESSENTIALS**  
- **Insulated coolers (2-3 large ones)**: With ice packs.  
- **Leak-proof containers**: For calzones, hummus, etc.  
- **Paper towels & wet wipes**: For quick cleanups.  

---

### **WHY THIS WORKS**  
1. **Minimal Mess**: No plates, utensils, or shared desserts. Everything is grab-and-go.  
2. **Diet Flexibility**: Options for picky eaters (grapes, cheese, mini calzones).  
3. **Hydration Balance**: Water + sodas cover all bases.  
4. **Time-Saver**: Pre-ordered calzones and packaged snacks = zero last-minute stress.  

**Pro Tip**: Assign 2 dads as "cooler tenders" to monitor ice levels and redistribute food as needed!
