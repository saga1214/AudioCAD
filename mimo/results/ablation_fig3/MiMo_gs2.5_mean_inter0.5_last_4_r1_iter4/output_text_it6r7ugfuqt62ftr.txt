Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimming, warm weather, diet goals, celiac considerations, birthday adventures, cruise plans, and now a full family vacation itinerary! You're really covering California well. 

Since you're dieting to wear your bathing suit and dislike cold, I'll focus on year-round warmth and plenty of swim opportunities. With a healthy budget and a family of three, we can do something truly special. 

Given your Northern location and birthday adventure request, I'd suggest heading south to either **Tahiti Bay** in Dana Point (which has exceptional gluten-free dining and a huge pool) or **Seabridge Resort** in Laguna Beach (with its famous cliffside pool and spa). Both are about a 3.5-hour drive from you in NCal.

For packing, remember beyond the two swimsuits and cover-up: quick-dry rash guards for sun protection, flip-flops or water shoes, a compact towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, a light sweater, a reusable water bottle, and any special snacks you might need. A waterproof phone pouch is also super handy.

Now, for the vacation plan! Here’s a 4-day itinerary for Laguna Beach:

**Day 1: Arrival & Relaxation**
*   **Travel:** Pick up a larger rental car (SUV or minivan). Drive south on I-5 to Laguna Beach, about 3.5 hours. Stop for lunch in Orange or Santa Ana if needed.
*   **Stay:** Check into your hotel/resort. Unpack and settle in.
*   **Dinner:** Enjoy a relaxed meal at the resort or nearby. If gluten-free, ask your server about options.
*   **Evening:** Spend your first evening unwinding at your resort's amenities. Maybe a spa treatment for mom and dad!

**Day 2: Adventure & Views**
*   **Morning:** Rent bikes or take a guided tour to explore the stunning coastal bike paths. Stop at tide pools at First Beach.
*   **Lunch:** Picnic at Cowell's Beach or find a casual spot near the beach.
*   **Afternoon:** Hike up to the cliff-top trails like the Upper Beach Trail for breathtaking views. You can see the sea caves from above.
*   **Dinner:** Celebrate at a special restaurant like **Laguna Grill** (known for accommodating dietary needs and ocean views) or **The Lobster** for a seafood feast.
*   **Evening:** Stroll along Main Beach, listen to the waves, and enjoy the ocean breeze.

**Day 3: Water Fun & Culture**
*   **Morning:** Head to a fantastic spot like **Crystal Cove State Park**. Snorkel in the clear, warm waters (check conditions first!), swim in the designated areas, and explore the tide pools. You can rent gear nearby.
*   **Lunch:** Grab a quick bite at a snack bar near the beach or enjoy a picnic.
*   **Afternoon:** Visit the **Laguna Art Gallery** or the **Laguna Beach海洋艺术博物馆** (Marine Arts Gallery) to see local artists. Or, explore the charming shops on Coast Highway.
*   **Dinner:** Try something different! **Hacienda** offers Mexican cuisine that can be modified gluten-free, or **Sushi Beach** for fresh sushi with careful preparation requests.
*   **Evening:** Enjoy a relaxing night in or perhaps a sunset cruise tour if you're up for it.

**Day 4: Farewell & Shopping**
*   **Morning:** Enjoy one last swim or spa time at your resort. Maybe a final breakfast at a favorite café.
*   **Lunch:** Find a great seafood spot or a fun lunch spot like **The Beachcomber** in Laguna Beach.
*   **Afternoon:** Do some souvenir shopping along Coast Highway. Laguna Beach has fantastic boutiques.
*   **Travel:** Drive back to I-5 heading north towards Orange County or I-5 North directly. Stop for a final Southern California lunch if desired.

**Key Packing Tips for the Trip:**
*   **Swimsuits:** Pack your favorite bathing suits and a couple of different cover-ups.
*   **Casual Wear:** For the beach and exploring, think shorts, t-shirts, tank tops, sundresses, and casual pants.
*   **Footwear:** Flip-flops, sandals, water shoes, and comfortable walking shoes for exploring.
*   **Sun & Swim Gear:** High-SPF sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, a reusable water bottle, and a compact towel. A waterproof phone case is essential.
*   **Celiac Safety:** Bring your diagnosis card or a note from a doctor. Research restaurants ahead of time or call to confirm they can safely accommodate gluten-free requests. Trish's Mini Donuts are a must-visit treat!

Have an absolutely wonderful trip! Laguna Beach is perfect for warm weather, swimming, and making great memories.
