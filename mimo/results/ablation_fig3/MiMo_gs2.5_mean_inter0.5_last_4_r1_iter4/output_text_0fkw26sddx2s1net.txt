Of course. Here’s a detailed shopping list, organized by course. 

For the starter: Cucumbers; miso paste, either white or red; dashi, or you can just use 1 teaspoon of kombu seaweed for the soup base; and sesame oil and soy sauce.

For the main course, Chicken Teriyaki: You'll need boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts. For the teriyaki sauce, you'll need soy sauce, mirin, sake, or dry sherry, and brown sugar or honey. For the veggies, broccoli florets, snap peas, and carrots, sliced thinly. And of course, steamed jasmine rice and cooking oil.

For dessert options: If you're making mango sticky rice, get sticky jasmine rice, fresh ripe mangoes, coconut milk, sugar, and salt. If you decide to make the homemade matcha ice cream, you'll need matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and vanilla extract.
