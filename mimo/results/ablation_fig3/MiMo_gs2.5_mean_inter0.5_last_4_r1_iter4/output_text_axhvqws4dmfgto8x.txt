The single most important step for stir-frying is not to overcrowd the pan. You want to brown the meat quickly over high heat. A good stir-fry also has a sauce, so you can pour it over the meat at the end.

As for the noodles, just make sure to use plastic utensils, and drain them well. The secret to perfect noodles is to rinse them with cold water right after draining. This stops the cooking process. Then, you can toss them in the stir-fry sauce. You've got this!
