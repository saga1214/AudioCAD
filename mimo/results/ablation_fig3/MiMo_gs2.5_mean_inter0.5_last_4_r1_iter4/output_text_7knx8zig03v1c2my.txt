### Clicker Training Explained:
**How It Works:**  
1. **Clicker as a Marker:**  
   - Hold the clicker in your hand. When Galileo does something you want (e.g., sits), *click* immediately.  
   - The click = "That’s exactly right!" in dog language.  

2. **Reward Timing:**  
   - Within 1-2 seconds of clicking, give a treat.  
   - Over time, Galileo learns: "Click → Treat = Sit = Good!"  

3. **Build Complexity:**  
   - Start with simple commands (sit, paw), then add steps (e.g., "sit, then stay").  
   - Phase out the clicker by replacing clicks with verbal praise ("Good boy!") once he’s mastered.  

**Why It Works:**  
- Creates instant clarity between behavior and reward.  
- Builds trust and communication.  
- Works faster than verbal-only training for new behaviors.  

**Tips:**  
- Practice 5-10 minutes daily.  
- Use the same clicker sound for consistency.  
- Pair with verbal cues ("Good sit!") eventually.  

**Example Session:**  
1. Hold clicker. Say, "Sit!"  
2. Galileo sits → *CLICK* → give treat.  
3. Repeat 5-10 times. Gradually add a pause between clicks/treats.  

Clicker training turns learning into a fun game—perfect for a new puppy like Galileo! 🐾
