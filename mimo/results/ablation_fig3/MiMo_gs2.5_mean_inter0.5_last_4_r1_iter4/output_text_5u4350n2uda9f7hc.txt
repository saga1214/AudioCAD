For a temporary Airbnb stay without an elevator, keeping plants lightweight is definitely the priority.

Small succulents in little pots will be very light, so they're easy to carry up five flights of stairs.

The bird's nest snake plant is also sturdy and lightweight, so you should have no problem moving it around.

Since you plan to take them home, choosing something compact and light is a great idea. They'll feel just as homely without being a hassle to transport.
