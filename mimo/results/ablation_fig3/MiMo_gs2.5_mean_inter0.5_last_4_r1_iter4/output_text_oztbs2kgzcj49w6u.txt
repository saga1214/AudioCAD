Absolutely. The Lockport area has several great places to learn all about the canal's history. The most important one is the Lockport Canal Museum. It's located right at the site of the locks themselves, so you can see them as you explore the museum's exhibits.

Another fantastic spot is the Erie Canal Museum in Youngstown, which is also along your route. It's considered one of the best museums in the entire state for understanding the canal's history and impact.

And since you're up for a longer drive, you should definitely stop by the Buffalo and Erie Canal Village. It's a beautifully restored historic district where you can walk through preserved buildings from the canal's heyday. It's a truly immersive experience.

So, if you're interested in history and architecture, this trip could be just as interesting as relaxing at a winery!
