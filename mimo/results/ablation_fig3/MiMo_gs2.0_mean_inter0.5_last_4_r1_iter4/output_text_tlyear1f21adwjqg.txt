Okay, efficiency can mean different things—like cost, how much energy you get per square meter, or how much you can scale it up. If we're talking about lifetime cost and overall practicality, solar energy usually takes the top spot. It's getting cheaper, you can use it everywhere, and storage is improving. But it really depends on the situation. For instance, in places with lots of rainfall and existing dams, hydropower might be more efficient. And in windy areas, wind turbines could outperform solar. So there's no single best answer—it's about matching the best source to the local conditions.
