That's a fantastic theme choice! Here are two themed lunch ideas for your "Retro Tech" dress-up days, with snacks and easy-to-adapt options:

### 🖥️ Theme 1: Vintage Computer Hardware (Floppy Disks, CRT Monitors, Cassette Tapes)
**Snacks:**  
- **"Data Packet" Crackers:** Mini whole-wheat crackers with:  
  - *Option A:* "Binary Code" Hummus (black bean + olive tapenade swirls)  
  - *Option B:* "Floppy Disk" Pickles (mini cucumber spears in brine)  
- **"Memory Chip" Almonds:** Marinated almonds in tech-inspired colors (blue/green)  

**Main Dish:**  
- **"Cassette Tape" Sandwich:**  
  - *Bread:* Toasted sourdough "tape"  
  - *Filling:* Turkey/chicken salad with cranberry "data bits"  
  - *Tape:* Sliced pickles or cucumber "tapes"  
  - *Spice:* "Static" paprika dust  

**Dessert:**  
- **"Floppy Sweet" Chocolate:** Mini chocolate chip cookies with a "read-only" (edible gold leaf) center  

---

### 📱 Theme 2: Early Mobile Tech (Blackberry, Flip Phones, Palm Pilots)
**Snacks:**  
- **"Battery" Energy Bites:** Dates + oats + dark chocolate (shaped like batteries)  
- **"Antenna" Pretzel Rods:** Pretzels with cheese dip "signal boosters"  

**Main Dish:**  
- **"Feature Phone" Wraps:**  
  - *Tortilla:* Spinach + cheese "phone screen"  
  - *Filling:* Black bean "tech specs" + avocado "OS"  
  - *Tape:* Black olives "keyboard strips"  
  - *Sauce:* "Text Message" salsa (tomato + cilantro)  

**Dessert:**  
- **"Polymer Shell" Pudding:** Vanilla pudding in clear cups with a "screen protector" (edible glitter)  

---

### 💡 Pro Tips:  
1. **Dietary Swaps:**  
   - Vegan: Use hummus/veggie sausage instead of meat  
   - Gluten-Free: Rice crackers, corn tortillas, GF cookies  
   - Nut-Free: Sunflower seed "chips" or roasted chickpeas  

2. **Theming Fun:**  
   - Print tiny retro tech icons (floppy, cassette, flip phone) to glue onto snacks  
   - Use tech-themed tableware: "RAM" bowls, "CPU" coasters  

3. **Prep Ahead:**  
   - Batch-cook fillings for wraps/sandwiches  
   - Pre-portion snacks in "tech accessories" bags (mini containers = "hard drives")  

These themes let everyone participate creatively while keeping meals practical! Your "Retro Tech" approach is a perfect way to boost morale and celebrate company history. 🎉💻
