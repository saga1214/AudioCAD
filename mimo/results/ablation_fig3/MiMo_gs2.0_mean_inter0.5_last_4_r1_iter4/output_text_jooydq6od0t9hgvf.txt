Here are quick, elegant drink recommendations for your party—keeping formality, mixed diets, and that soda allergy in mind:

### For the Vodka Mixer (Quick & Sophisticated):
1. **Kir Royale Vodka Style**  
   *Mix:* 1.5 oz vodka + 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice + 0.5 oz simple syrup.  
   *Top:* 3 oz prosecco or dry sparkling wine (not soda!).  
   *Garnish:* Lemon twist.  
   *Why it works:* Light, bubbly, and universally appealing. Uses minimal prep.

2. **Vodka Mojito Light**  
   *Mix:* 2 oz vodka + 4-5 muddled mint leaves + 0.5 oz lime juice + 0.5 oz simple syrup.  
   *Top:* 4-6 oz sparkling water (not tonic/soda).  
   *Garnish:* Mint sprig.  
   *Why it works:* Refreshing and fizzy, but avoids soda.

### For the Argentine Fernet (Bitter & Herbal Twist):
1. **Fernet Beer Cocktail** (Classic Argentine Style)  
   *Mix:* 2 oz Fernet-Branca + 4 oz crisp lager or pilsner (e.g., Corona, Budweiser).  
   *Garnish:* Lime wedge.  
   *Why it works:* Bitterness is balanced by beer’s carbonation and malt. Quick and iconic.

2. **Fernet Sparkler** (Simpler Alternative)  
   *Mix:* 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca + 0.5 oz fresh grapefruit juice + 0.5 oz simple syrup.  
   *Top:* 4 oz prosecco or ginger beer (check label for "soda" warnings—use *sparkling ginger ale* if needed).  
   *Garnish:* Grapefruit twist.  
   *Why it works:* Adds fruitiness to offset bitterness. Use ginger ale instead of soda to stay safe.

### Key Notes:
- **Soda Allergy:** Always use **sparkling water, prosecco, or beer** instead of soda/tonic.  
- **Formality:** Serve drinks in chilled glasses (coupos, wine glasses).  
- **Efficiency:** Prep cocktails in batches ahead of time.  
- **Flexibility:** Offer non-alcoholic options like sparkling water with lemon or a fruit-infused mocktail (e.g., cucumber-mint water).
