For an end-of-year celebration, focus on simplicity, fun, and sharing the load. Here's a breakdown of options:

**Indoor Option (Cafeteria):**
*   **Pros:** Easy setup (no weather worries), familiar space, minimal cleanup, manageable for one person.
*   **Cons:** Might feel less special.
*   **How to Make it Great:** Decorate simply (streamers, Brownie flags), set up a "Sash Display" table with photos and badges, have a sundae bar (ice cream, toppings), or a pizza station. Play Brownie-themed music. Parents can help set up/take down. Cost is minimal.

**Outdoor Option (Park Picnic/BBQ):**
*   **Pros:** More festive, nature connection, potential for active games (like the races you mentioned!).
*   **Cons:** Weather-dependent, requires more planning/cleanup, potential for mess.
*   **How to Make it Great:** Keep it simple! Rent a large picnic table or set up a canopy/tarp. **Rotate snack duties among parents** (e.g., 2 parents bring food, 2 bring drinks, 2 bring games/supplies). A sundae bar or pizza works well. Play Brownie songs. Focus on celebrating achievements (show badges/sashes).

**Hybrid Idea: "Sash & Sundae" Party**
*   **Where:** Cafeteria or simple park setup (like a picnic table).
*   **What:** Celebrate by formally passing out sashes (if not done earlier) and badges. Have a sundae bar (ice cream, hot fudge, sprinkles, whipped cream, candy). Maybe a quick group photo with sashes. Keep it short and sweet (1.5-2 hours max).

**Key Tips for Success:**

1.  **Involve Parents Early:** Clearly communicate the end-of-year plans (date, time, location, expected cost if any). **Ask parents to sign up for specific duties** (bringing a food item, drinks, games, supplies, or helping with setup/cleanup). This shares the load.
2.  **Keep Food Manageable:** A sundae bar or pizza station is easier than a full BBQ. Offer a mix of popular kid-friendly options.
3.  **Focus on Celebration:** The main goal is celebrating the girls' achievements and fun they had! Avoid making it feel like another activity.
4.  **Logistics:** Choose a date that doesn't conflict with school events. Ensure the location has restrooms (crucial!). Have a rain plan if outdoors.
5.  **Your Well-being:** Don't overcommit. Your health comes first. Delegate tasks and enjoy the celebration too!

**Recommendation:** Given your pregnancy and the goal of fun, a well-delegated **indoors cafeteria party with a sundae bar** is often the easiest and most successful. If you crave the park vibe, a **park picnic with parents rotating food duties** is a great middle ground. Whichever you choose, **clear communication and parent involvement** are key to making it fun and manageable! You've got this!
