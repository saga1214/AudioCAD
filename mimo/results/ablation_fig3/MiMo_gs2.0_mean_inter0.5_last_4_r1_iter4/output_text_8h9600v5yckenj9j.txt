### Smart Candy Tips:
- **Avoid niche flavors** (e.g., specific fruit flavors or exotic chocolates).  
- **Buy in bulk** at stores like Costco, Dollar Tree, or Amazon (search "Halloween candy bags").  
- **Pre-portion in small bags** (2-4 oz) to control costs and prevent messes.  
- **Add themed touches** like orange wrappers or black bags for Halloween flair.  

### Leftover Prevention:
- **Rotate stock**—check candy levels mid-party.  
- **Offer alternatives** like candy apples or caramel apples if some items sell out.  
- **Don’t overbuy**—aim for 1 bag per 5-10 guests.  

### Final Pro Tip:
