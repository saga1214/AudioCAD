Fourth, a classic shrimp and vegetable skewers. Get some shrimp, cherry tomatoes, and bamboo skewers. You can grill them on the smoker grill—now clean—after cleaning it, or just pan-fry them. Brush them with a little olive oil, lemon juice, and garlic.

And fifth, for something super simple, you can do a loaded baked potato. Get a can of potatoes, some shredded cheddar, a can of black beans, and maybe some salsa and green onions. Just bake the potatoes and top them when they're hot.
