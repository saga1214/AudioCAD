Absolutely! Here's a **grab-and-go friendly shopping list** designed for minimal mess at the theme park, based on your group size (10 dads, 10 kids) and preferences. I've prioritized individual portions, easy-to-carry items, and included options for both healthier choices and indulgences (like sodas and calzones):

---

### **DRINKS** (Pack in 12-pack cases)
*   **Water Bottles:** 20 large reusable water bottles (1 per person) + 10 extra bottles (for refills).
*   **Sugar-Free Sodas:** 10 12-pack cases (Dad favorites like Coke Zero, Pepsi Zero, etc.).
*   **Low-Sugar Juice Boxes:** 10 12-pack cases (Apple, Grape, or Mixed Fruit - for kids).
*   **Electrolyte Drink Pouches:** 20 individual pouches (for hydration in heat).
*   **Ice Packs:** 3-4 large coolers + plenty of ice packs.

---

### **SNACKS** (Pack in single-serve bags/containers)
*   **Cheese Sticks:** 20 sticks (divided among kids & dads).
*   **Individual Hummus Cups:** 20 cups (with veggie sticks inside or separate).
*     **Baby Carrots:** 5 pre-washed bags (great for hummus).
*   **Trail Mix:** 10 individual bags (nuts, seeds, dried fruit).
*   **Pretzels:** 10 bags (small individual packs or 1 large bag cut into pieces).
*   **Grapes:** 5 pre-washed bags (easy for kids).
*   **Rice Cakes:** 10 individual packs (with single-serve nut butter or cream cheese spread).
*   **Granola Bars:** 10 boxes of healthy options (like KIND, RXBAR).

---

### **MAIN MEAL** (Pre-packaged for easy grabbing)
*   **Mini Calzones (Pre-Order):** 10 boxes (approx. 2 mini calzones per box, so 20 total).  
    *   *Choose 2-3 flavors:* Margherita, Pepperoni, Meatball Marinara.
*   **Sandwiches/Wraps (Pre-made & Individual):**  
    *   10 Turkey & Avocado Wraps  
    *   10 Chicken Caesar Wraps  
    *   10 Ham & Swiss Sandwiches (cut in half if large)
*   **Pita Chips:** 5 bags (for dipping).
*   **Dip Cups:** 10 cups of single-serve hummus or tzatziki.

---

### **DESSERT** (Grab-and-go celebration)
*   **Cupcakes:** 20 cupcakes (pre-order from a bakery - offer 2 flavors if possible, e.g., Chocolate & Vanilla).
*   **Small Cookies:** 20 cookies (like Oreos or chocolate chip - easy for kids).
*   **OR Pie:** If you prefer pie, get a large individual slice/pie piece pre-cut and wrapped in foil.

---

### **ESSENTIALS FOR MINIMAL MESS**
*   **Coolers:** 3-4 large insulated coolers (with ice packs).
*   **Lids/Sealable Bags:** To secure coolers and organize snacks.
*   **Wet Wipes & Hand Sanitizer:** 2 large packs (crucial for park messes!).
*   **Trash Bags:** 5-6 heavy-duty ziplock bags (for quick trash disposal).
*   **Reusable Bags:** For carrying cooler contents.
*   **Optional:** Bug spray, sunscreen, small first-aid kit.

---

### **KEY STRATEGIES FOR MESS-FREE MOVEMENT**
1.  **Pre-Portion Everything:** Don't buy loose items. Use containers, bags, and pre-made meals.
2.  **Label Coolers:** Clearly mark each cooler's contents (e.g., "Drinks," "Snacks," "Calzones").
3.  **Assign Cooler Helpers:** Recruit 2-3 dads to manage coolers throughout the day.
4.  **Trash on the Go:** Use the small trash bags to collect waste immediately inside the park.
5.  **Avoid Plates/Forks:** Stick to hands, pouches, cups, and wraps.

This plan gives everyone choices (healthy snacks, juice boxes, sodas, indulgent calzones, cupcakes) while keeping things organized and easy for the dads to manage with the kids. Have an absolutely fantastic bachelor party!
