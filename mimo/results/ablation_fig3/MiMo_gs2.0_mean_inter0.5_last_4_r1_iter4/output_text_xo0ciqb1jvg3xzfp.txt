No, it wasn't a universally happy occasion for everyone involved.

For the Pilgrims, it was a joyful celebration of survival and abundance.

For the Wampanoag, while they did participate, it was part of a political alliance. The story of the harvest feast doesn't acknowledge the great suffering that followed, like the loss of their ancestors to disease and the eventual loss of their land.

So, it's important to remember it was a moment of cooperation, but one that came within a much larger and more tragic historical context.
