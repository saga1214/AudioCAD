   - **Why it fits**: Builds on your curry experiment.  
   - **How**: Sauté shrimp/tofu with snap peas, bell peppers, and carrots. Toss in pre-cooked jasmine rice. Finish with coconut milk, lime, and cilantro.

### 🍝 3. One-Pot Creamy Pasta  
   - **Why it fits**: Comfort food for post-workout brain reset.  
   - **How**: Cook pasta, then sauté garlic in olive oil. Add heavy cream, spinach, sun-dried tomatoes, and parmesan. Toss with pasta and top with chili flakes.  
   - **Pro tip**: Use a non-stick pot to avoid cleanup fatigue.

### 🥪 4. Avocado & Hummus Wrap  
   - **Why it fits**: Zero-cook option for chaotic days.  
   - **How**: Whole-grain wrap + mashed avocado + hummus + shredded carrots + spinach + everything bagel seasoning. Roll tightly.  
   - **Add-on**: Serve with cherry tomatoes and a glass of cold sparkling water.
