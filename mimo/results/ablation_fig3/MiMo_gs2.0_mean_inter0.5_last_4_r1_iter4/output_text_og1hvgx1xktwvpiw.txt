Of course, let me clarify. The original Boulevardier is one and a quarter ounces of whiskey to one ounce each of vermouth and Campari. Your friend's recipe, and the one I also called valid, is equal parts of all three. 

Now, for the Last Word you asked about, that's also equal parts, but it uses different ingredients. You'll need three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. Stir that one well and strain into a glass too. 

So to recap: Boulevardier can be made with either a whiskey-heavy recipe or an equal-parts recipe. The Last Word is strictly equal-parts with gin and those other ingredients. Hope that helps you pick!
