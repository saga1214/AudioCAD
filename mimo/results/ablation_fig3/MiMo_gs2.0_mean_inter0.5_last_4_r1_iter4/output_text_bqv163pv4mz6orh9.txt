That's a great idea! Here are a few thoughtful gift options, ranging from quick and sweet to slightly more involved, that would make your daughter feel truly proud of her hard work:

1.  **A Small, Sweet Indulgence:**
    *   **Why it works:** Something delicious and immediate.
    *   **Options:** A box of her favorite chocolates, gourmet cookies, or even a small jar of fancy popcorn. You could even pick up a small, high-quality fruit basket.
    *   **Best for:** If you want something quick, affordable, and universally appreciated right after the show. Pair it with a heartfelt note.

2.  **A Personalized Keepsake:**
    *   **Why it works:** Shows thoughtfulness and commemorates her achievement.
    *   **Options:**
        *   **Custom Photo Frame:** Find a pre-made frame online or at a store. Print a picture of her (even a recent one) or a fun graphic like "Opening Night Star!" inside. Add a personalized message inside the frame.
        *   **Personalized Keychain or Jewelry:** Something simple like "Opening Night 2024" or her name and the play's title engraved on a charm or a small bracelet.
    *   **Best for:** If you have a little more time (or can order online quickly) and want something she can keep for years. The photo frame is especially nice.

3.  **A Thoughtful Experience:**
    *   **Why it works:** Creates a special memory.
    *   **Options:**
        *   **Tickets to a Future Show:** Buy tickets for a play, concert, or movie she'd love to see together soon after the play. Frame the tickets as a "Thank You for Opening Night" gift.
        *   **A Fun Activity:** Tickets for an amusement park ride she loves, a trampoline park, or a fun class (like pottery or painting) you can do together.
    *   **Best for:** If you want to plan something special for her soon. Frame the tickets as the main gift.

4.  **Combine with a Heartfelt Note:**
    *   **Why it works:** The message itself is powerful. Pair it with one of the above!
    *   **Write something like:** "Saw you shine tonight! So incredibly proud of all your hard work and dedication. You were amazing! Love, Mom/Dad."
    *   **Best for:** *Any* of the gift options above. The note makes the gift even more meaningful.

**Recommendation based on your situation:**

*   **For Speed & Simplicity:** Go with the **small box of her favorite chocolates or cookies** paired with a **heartfelt note**. This is easy, quick, and delicious.
*   **For Thoughtfulness & Lasting:** Go with a **pre-made personalized photo frame** with a **custom message** inside, paired with a **heartfelt note**. This is the most "proud parent" gift.
*   **If you have a little more time/budget:** The **personalized keychain** or **experience tickets** are great choices.

**Key things to remember:**

*   **Personalize it:** If you know her favorite chocolate, cookie, or what kind of jewelry she likes, mention it.
*   **Handwritten Note:** This is the most important part! Tell her specifically what you saw that made you proud (e.g., "the way you held your own on stage," "your confidence," "how much you loved it").
*   **Timing:** Give it to her *after* the show, when she's still feeling the excitement and adrenaline.

No matter what you choose, the fact that you're thinking of a special gift to acknowledge her months of hard work and her big performance on opening night will mean the world to her. Enjoy the play!
