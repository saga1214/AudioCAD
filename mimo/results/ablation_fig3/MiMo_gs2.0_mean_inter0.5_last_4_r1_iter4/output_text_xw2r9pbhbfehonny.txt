Sure, here’s a quick checklist for your next self-filmed performance:

First, plan ahead. Pick one of your shorter, funer skits—maybe a Melvin magic trick or a Sloppy Snake story.

Second, set up your phone. Put it on a tripod or ask someone to hold it steady. Make sure the lighting is good, like near a window or with some soft lights on.

Third, test the audio. You can use a lavalier mic or just speak clearly into the phone. Background noise can be a problem.

Fourth, dress up as you always do. Kids love your energy, and your puppets are already great.

Finally, after the show, check the footage. See what works and what doesn’t, and you can edit it a little later.

That’s really all there is to it. Just have fun and be yourself.
