Okay, you're right, there are a lot of similar idioms. For example, "wearing a candle at both ends" means the same thing as burning it, just a slightly different phrasing. Then there's "living like a candle," which suggests living life intensely or quickly. "Letting the candle burn out" means to allow something to completely exhaust itself. "Candle at both ends" just emphasizes the idea of overextending yourself. And "burning the candle down" means to use something up completely and quickly.
