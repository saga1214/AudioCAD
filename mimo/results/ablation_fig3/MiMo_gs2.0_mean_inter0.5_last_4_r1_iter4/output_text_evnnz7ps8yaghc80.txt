It's wonderful that you've committed to a practice schedule! Having structure will definitely help. Here’s a balanced weekly plan you can follow.

For Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Sunday, I suggest a session of about 30 to 45 minutes. Start by warming up with one-handed two-ball juggling for about five minutes. Then, spend 15 minutes focusing on transitioning from two to three beanbags, concentrating only on your dominant hand. After that, cool down for five minutes with some slow-motion throws with the scarves, just to get a feel for the rhythm.

For Tuesday and Thursday, you can do a shorter session of about 20 to 30 minutes. In this one, you could spend about ten minutes practicing tennis ball juggling to improve your catches, and then the last ten minutes can be dedicated to experimenting with patterns with the scarves.

Saturday would be a great day for a longer session of about an hour. You can use this time to mix everything together: start with your one-handed two-ball juggling, then move to three beanbag juggling, and finish with some creative scarf patterns.

Remember, consistency is more important than duration. It's better to have a little practice every day than a long session once in a while. Be patient with yourself, and don't forget to celebrate your small victories. You've got this!
