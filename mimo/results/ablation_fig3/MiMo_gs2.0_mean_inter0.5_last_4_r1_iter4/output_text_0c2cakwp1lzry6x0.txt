Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your TV mount, hanging planters, and floating shelves, keeping your landlord's rules and new apartment's features in mind:

### 📺 1. TV Mount Installation
*   **Safety First:** Turn off power at the breaker if using a stud finder with power detection. Use a stud finder with a laser level for accuracy.
*   **Plan:** Measure the wall, mark the exact spot for the mount center (consider TV size/weight). Use painter's tape to mark drill points.
*   **Drill Carefully:** Drill pilot holes slightly smaller than your anchor/hammer hole. Drill slowly to avoid cracking drywall. Use appropriate anchors (plastic or metal) for your wall type.
*   **Secure Mount:** Attach the wall plate securely to the anchors/studs. Double-check it's level before mounting the TV.
*   **Tip:** If the wall is purely drywall (no studs), use heavy-duty toggle bolts rated for your TV's weight.

### 🌿 2. Hanging Planters
*   **Choose the Spot:** Pick a location above a table or counter where plants won't get in the way. Ensure it's structurally sound (stud or thick drywall).
*   **Select Hangers:** Use sturdy hooks (e.g., S-hooks, decorative wall-mounted hooks, or a dedicated planter hanger). Match the weight of the planter + soil + water.
*   **Install Hooks:** Drill pilot holes. Use appropriate anchors (plastic or metal toggle bolts) if needed. Ensure hooks are level and securely attached to studs or thick drywall.
*   **Hang Planters:** Carefully lift each planter and hook it securely onto the hook. Avoid overfilling with water.
*   **Tip:** Use lightweight materials for planters (ceramic, plastic) to reduce stress. Check hooks regularly for rust or wear.

### 🪢 3. Floating Shelves
*   **Identify Studs:** Use a stud finder. Shelves need to be anchored into studs for safety, especially for heavier items. If no studs are found, use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for the shelf's weight.
*   **Measure & Mark:** Mark the exact spot for the top and bottom of the shelf. Use a level to ensure it's perfectly horizontal.
*   **Drill Pilot Holes:** Drill holes slightly smaller than your shelf brackets or shelf pins.
*   **Install Brackets/Pins:** Securely attach shelf brackets to the wall studs or anchors. If using shelf pins, place them on the wall and then position the shelf.
*   **Place Shelf:** Carefully lower the shelf onto the brackets or pins. Ensure it's level and secure.
*   **Tip:** For long shelves, use multiple brackets for support. Avoid overloading shelves.

### 🔑 Key Considerations for Your New Apartment
*   **Noise:** Keep installers quiet. Avoid power tools on shared walls/ceilings if possible. Do heavy work during the day.
*   **No Holes?** For drywall areas without studs, use high-quality toggle bolts or heavy-duty anchors rated for the weight. Check if your landlord requires permits for wall modifications.
*   **Workspace:** Dedicate a specific Sunday morning (e.g., 9 AM - 1 PM) for installation. Clear the area you're working in.
*   **Safety:** Wear safety glasses and gloves. Ensure good lighting. Don't overreach while hanging heavy items.

**Before you start, measure each item carefully and ensure your tools are ready.** Breaking the installation into manageable chunks (e.g., install TV mount one Sunday, shelves another) will keep things less overwhelming. Good luck transforming your space! 🏡✨
