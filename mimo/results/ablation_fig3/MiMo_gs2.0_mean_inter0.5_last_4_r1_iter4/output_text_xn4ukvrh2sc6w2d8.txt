### **1. Old Fashioned**
**Ingredients:**
*   2 oz bourbon rye (e.g., Wild Turkey 101, Bulleit)
*   1 sugar cube or ¾ oz simple syrup
*   2-3 dashes Angostura bitters
*   Orange twist (for garnish)
*   Ice

**Instructions:**
1.  Place sugar cube (or simple syrup) in a mixing glass. Add bitters.
2.  Muddle gently with a bar spoon.
3.  Add 2 oz bourbon/rye.
4.  Add ice to mixing glass, stir vigorously for 20-30 seconds until chilled and properly diluted.
5.  Strain into a **rocks glass over one large ice cube**.
6.  Express an orange twist over the drink (squeeze oil into foam), wipe the rim, and garnish.

### **2. Paper Plane**
**Ingredients:**
*   2 oz gin (e.g., Hendrick's, Bombay Sapphire)
*   ¾ oz Aperol
*   ¾ oz Amaro Nonino (e.g., Fernet-Branca, Luxardo)
*   ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
*   Orange twist (optional garnish)

**Instructions:**
1.  Combine all ingredients in a shaker.
2.  Add ice and shake vigorously for 10-15 seconds until well-chilled.
3.  Strain into a **coupe glass** filled with ice (or a single large ice cube).
4.  Garnish with an orange twist (optional).

### **3. Whiskey Sour (with Egg White Option)**
**Ingredients:**
*   2 oz bourbon (e.g., Maker's Mark, Four Roses)
*   ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
*   ½ oz simple syrup
*   1 egg white (optional)
*   Cherry or Orange slice (garnish)

**Instructions:**
1.  **Option A (No Egg White):** Combine all ingredients in a shaker. Add ice, shake hard for 10-15 seconds. Strain into a **rocks glass over ice**. Garnish.
2.  **Option B (With Egg White - Classic Texture):**
    *   Combine bourbon, lemon juice, simple syrup, and **1 egg white** in a shaker.
    *   **Dry Shake:** Add ice and shake **without ice** for 10-15 seconds. This aerates the egg white.
    *   Add ice again, shake hard for another 10-15 seconds.
    *   Strain into a **rocks glass over ice**. Garnish.

### **4. Aviation**
**Ingredients:**
*   2 oz gin (e.g., Tanqueray No. TEN, London Dry)
*   ¾ oz maraschino liqueur (e.g., Luxardo Maraschino)
*   ¼ oz crème de violette (e.g., St. George Violette)
*   ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
*   Cherry (garnish)

**Instructions:**
1.  Combine all ingredients in a shaker.
2.  Add ice and shake vigorously for 10-15 seconds.
3.  Strain into a **cocktail glass** (chilled coupe or Nick & Nora glass).
4.  Garnish with a cherry.

### **Pro Tips for Bartenders**
*   **Ice is Key:** Use a high-quality ice machine for all drinks to ensure clarity and slow dilution.
*   **Fresh Juice:** Make lemon juice fresh daily. Pre-mix simple syrup.
*   **Glassware:** Have plenty of chilled coupe glasses, rocks glasses, and cocktail glasses ready.
*   **Bitters:** Keep Angostura bitters easily accessible for topping Old Fashioneds.
*   **Egg White Safety:** Use pasteurized eggs if concerned about raw eggs. Wash hands thoroughly after handling.
