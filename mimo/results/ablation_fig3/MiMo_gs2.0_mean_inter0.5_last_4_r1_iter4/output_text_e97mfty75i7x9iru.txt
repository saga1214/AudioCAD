I understand the flu can make it hard to think clearly. Here's a summary of everything you need to know.

The saying is feed a cold, starve a fever.

Antibiotics are unnecessary because the flu is a virus.

Your doctor didn't prescribe them for that exact reason—they won't help a viral infection.

The core advice is to rest, drink plenty of fluids, and eat what you can.

For the flu specifically, over-the-counter meds can help with fever and aches.

For a cough, honey in warm tea, salt water gargling, and elevating your head can provide relief.

And if your symptoms are severe or last more than a week, it's important to contact your doctor.
