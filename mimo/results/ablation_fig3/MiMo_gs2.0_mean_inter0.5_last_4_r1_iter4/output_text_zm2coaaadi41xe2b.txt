For Asian noodles in Dallas, you could try Yum Yum Express, which is great for fast, tasty options. There's also The Rice Boat, which is more of a sit-down place with really authentic flavors. If you want a bit more variety, check out Pho 79 for some amazing pho and noodle dishes. As for protein bars, Quest is great if you want low sugar and high protein. Optimum Nutrition has a solid taste and protein content. And if you want something a little sweeter, think about Clif Bars or RXBAR for a balanced snack. Hope you enjoy your meal and your upgrades!
