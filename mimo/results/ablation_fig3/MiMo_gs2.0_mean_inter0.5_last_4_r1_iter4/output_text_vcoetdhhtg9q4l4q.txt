That’s a great question! For quick, easy dinners, I’d suggest focusing on **pantry staples** and **minimal prep**:  
- **Sheet-pan meals**: Toss veggies (like broccoli, bell peppers, carrots) and a protein (chicken, tofu, or chickpeas) with olive oil, salt, and spices. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 20-25 minutes.  
- **Canned bean bowls**: Mix canned lentils or black beans with pre-cooked rice, avocado, salsa, and a squeeze of lime.  
- **Pasta with jarred sauce**: Use a simple marinara or pesto, add cherry tomatoes and spinach while cooking, and top with cheese or nuts.  
- **Omelets/frittatas**: Whisk eggs with a handful of spinach, mushrooms, or leftover cooked veggies. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 20 minutes.  

**Pro tip**: Keep frozen shrimp, pre-cut veggies, and jarred sauces on hand for even faster assembly. No complicated cooking—just chop, toss, and roast! 😊
