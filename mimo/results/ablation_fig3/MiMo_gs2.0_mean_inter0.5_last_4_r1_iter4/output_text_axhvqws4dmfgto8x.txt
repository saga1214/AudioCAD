You're on the right track! Stir-fry noodles are actually a great next step—they're forgiving, quick, and let you practice high-heat cooking. Here’s how to nail them without a smoke alarm (or another spoon incident!):

### Key Tips for Perfect Stir-Fry Noodles:
1. **Choose the Right Noodles**:  
   Use **dry, straight noodles** (like spaghetti, lo mein, or udon). Avoid rice noodles—they turn mushy if overcooked.  
   *Pro tip*: Cook them **1 minute less** than package instructions. They’ll finish cooking in the stir-fry.

2. **Salt the Water (But Don’t Overdo It)**:  
   Add a pinch of salt to the boiling water (like pasta), but rinse them *lightly* afterward to prevent sticking.

3. **Drain THOROUGHLY**:  
   Drain in a colander, then **shake the noodles** vigorously to remove excess water. Soggy noodles = sticky stir-fry.

4. **NO WOOD SPOONS IN BOILING WATER**:  
   🔥 **CRITICAL**: Use a **metal spoon or tongs** when tossing noodles. No more smoke alarms!

5. **Stir-Fry Hot & Fast**:  
   - Heat your wok/pan until it’s smoking hot.  
   - Add oil, then noodles. Toss constantly with tongs (not a spoon) for 1–2 minutes until lightly charred.  
   - Add sauce and veggies, then toss again.  
   *High heat = crispy noodles, not steam clouds.*

6. **Sauce Wisely**:  
   Start with a light sauce (e.g., soy + sesame oil + chili paste). Too much liquid = soggy noodles.

### Why This Works for You:
- **Controlled heat**: Noodles brown quickly, so you’re less likely to overcook them.  
- **Less chopping**: Focus on tossing noodles vs. prepping veggies like stir-fry.  
- **Less room for error**: No wood in pots = no smoke. Tongs are your new best friend!

If you’re still nervous, practice with **canned raman noodles** first—they’re ultra-forgiving. You’ve got this! 🍜🔥
