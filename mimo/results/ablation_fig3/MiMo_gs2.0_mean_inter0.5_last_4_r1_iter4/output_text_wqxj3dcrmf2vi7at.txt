Here's a full list of corgi princess treats, categorized for easy reference:

**Homemade Treats:**
1.  **Frozen Bites:** Mashed pumpkin or sweet potato frozen in ice cube trays.
2.  **Peanut Butter & Oat Cookies:** Roll peanut butter (xylitol-free!) in oat flour or oats, flatten, and bake until firm.
3.  **Cooked Chicken:** Small, bite-sized pieces of plain, cooked chicken breast.
4.  **Carrot Sticks:** Thinly sliced, steamed carrot sticks.
5.  **Banana Bites:** Small pieces of ripe banana.
6.  **Sweet Potato Bites:** Small, baked sweet potato cubes.
7.  **Cheese Bites:** Very small cubes of plain, mild cheese (like cheddar or mozzarella).

**Store-Bought Treats:**
1.  **Training Pouches:** Brands like Blue Buffalo, Wellness, or Zuke's offer soft, savory training treats.
2.  **Grain-Free Bites:** Look for options like Zuke's Minis, Healthy Essential training treats, or Simple Answer.
3.  **Pumpkin or Sweet Potato Bites:** Many brands offer these frozen or dehydrated.
4.  **Greenies Dog Dental Chews:** Smaller sizes or the "Mini Greenies" are good for dental health and training.
5.  **Merrick or Wellness Training Treats:** Known for wholesome ingredients.
6.  **Dehydrated Meat Sticks:** Look for low-sodium, single-protein options (like chicken or beef).

**Key Tips:**
*   **Safety First:** Always check ingredients (NO XYLITOL, CHOCOLATE, ONions, Garlic, Grapes, Raisins).
*   **Portion Control:** Treats should only be 10-15% of her daily calorie intake.
*   **Start Small:** Introduce new foods in tiny amounts to avoid stomach upset.
*   **Pair with Training:** Use treats strategically for commands and rewards.
*   **Praise & Affection:** Combine treats with enthusiastic praise and petting!
