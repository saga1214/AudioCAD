For winter chicks in an enclosed area, the best bedding is wood shavings. They provide good insulation and are rodent-resistant. For a concrete floor, make sure to use a thick layer, at least two inches, to create a warm barrier between the floor and the chicks. You can manage it using the deep litter method we talked about earlier.

Avoid using straw because it does attract rodents. Also, stay away from pine needles, which can be irritating, and avoid wood chips that might splinter. A good quality wood shavings is your best bet.
