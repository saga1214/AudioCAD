To tune your guitar (or other stringed instrument) while waiting for your hair dye, here are the best tools and methods:

### 1. **Clip-On Tuner (Most Recommended)**
   - **What it is**: A small electronic tuner that clips onto your instrument's headstock or soundhole.
   - **How to use**: 
     - Turn it on and let it warm up.
     - Play a string (start with the 6th string, thickest one).
     - The tuner will show the pitch (e.g., "A" for the 6th string).
     - Adjust the tuning peg until the needle/LED stays centered on the target note.
   - **Pros**: Precise, portable, and works in noisy environments.

### 2. **Smartphone Tuner App**
   - **Apps to try**: GuitarTuna, Fender Tune, or Tuner Pro.
   - **How to use**: 
     - Open the app and place your phone near the instrument.
     - Pluck a string (start with the 6th string).
     - The app will display the pitch and show how far off it is.
     - Adjust the tuning peg until the note matches the target frequency (e.g., "A440" for the 6th string).
   - **Pros**: Free, convenient, and works anywhere.

### 3. **Electronic Tuner (Plug-in Type)**
   - **What it is**: A tuner with a built-in microphone that plugs into the instrument’s audio jack.
   - **How to use**: 
     - Plug it into the soundhole.
     - Play a string and adjust until the tuner’s display shows the correct note.
   - **Pros**: Good for acoustic guitars without a soundhole (e.g., ukuleles).

### **Key Tips for Tuning**:
- **Start with the 6th string (lowest)**: Tune it to **A440 Hz** first.
- **Tune Up**: If the tuner shows a note too low, tighten the string (turn the peg clockwise).
- **Tune Down**: If too high, loosen the string (turn counterclockwise).
- **Check Intonation**: After tuning one string, check adjacent strings for drift.

### **Common Tuning Notes**:
| String | Note | Frequency (Hz) |
|--------|------|----------------|

| 4th    | G    | 988            |

Tuning takes practice! Start with the 6th string, then work your way up. Happy tuning! 🎸
