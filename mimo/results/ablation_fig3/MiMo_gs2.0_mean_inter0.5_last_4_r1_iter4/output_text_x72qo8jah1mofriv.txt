Okay, so Tony is kosher, which means he won't eat anything that isn't kosher. Your healthy snack sounds fine, probably no issues there. The sweet option is where you'll need to be careful. M&Ms in bacon sound like a great theme, but bacon isn't kosher. So you'll just need to swap that out for something sweet that is. You could do cookies, since many are kosher, just check the label. Or maybe some fruit with chocolate, like strawberries dipped in dark chocolate. That way, you can keep the fun, gothic vibe of Frankenstein while making sure everyone has a good time.
