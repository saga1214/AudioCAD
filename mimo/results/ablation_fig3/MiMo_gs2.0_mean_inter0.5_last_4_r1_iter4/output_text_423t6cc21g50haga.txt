That’s a fantastic mindset shift! Travel can be incredibly healing and inspiring. Here are a few ideas tailored to your passions—scifi/fantasy, languages, pets (if you go pet-free), and modern fiction—plus practical logistics:

### 🌍 **Top Travel Ideas**  
1. **Japan**  
   - **Why**: Blend of futuristic cities (Tokyo), ancient history (Kyoto), and language immersion.  
   - **Stay**: Hostels like **Piece Hostel** (Tokyo) or **The Wing** (Kyoto)—great for meeting people.  
   - **Do**: Visit Akihabara (scifi arcades), cat cafés, language exchanges (Meetup/Tandem app), and hike Mount Fuji.  
   - **Get There**: Fly via ANA (safe, efficient). Budget flights from NYC (~$600 round-trip).  

2. **Portugal (Lisbon + Algarve)**  
   - **Why**: Affordable, friendly locals, coastal beauty, and a creative vibe.  
   - **Stay**: **Mooinho** (Lisbon) or **Casa de São Lourenço** (Algarve).  
   - **Do**: Explore Lisbon’s literary cafés, surf in Nazaré, learn Portuguese (apps like HelloTalk), and adopt a rescue cat (many shelters there).  
   - **Get There**: Fly into Lisbon, train to Algarve (~2 hrs).  

3. **Costa Rica**  
   - **Why**: Nature, wildlife, and adventure—perfect for pet-free inspiration.  
   - **Stay**: Eco-lodges like **Lapa Ríos** (sustainable, cat-friendly if you go).  
   - **Do**: Zip-lining in Monteverde, cloud forest hikes, and surfing in Tamarindo.  
   - **Get There**: Direct flights from major airports (~$500).  

### ✈️ **How to Make It Happen**  
- **Budget**: Use Skyscanner for deals; stay in hostels or Airbnb kitchens to save.  
- **Safety**: Book through platforms like **Hostelworld** or **Booking.com** (read reviews!). Avoid remote areas alone.  
- **Language**: Apps like **Tandem** or **HelloTalk** connect you with locals to practice Italian/Mandarin.  

### ✍️ **Writing Inspiration**  
- **On the Go**: Keep a journal or voice notes app handy. Capture cat cafés, language quirks, or sci-fi cityscapes.  
- **Back Home**: Turn travel snippets into modern fiction scenes—like a café in Lisbon where languages collide, or a cat with mysterious powers in Japan.  

### 💬 **Making Friends While Traveling**  
- **Hostel Bars**: Join pub crawls or language exchange nights.  
- **Group Tours**: Try **Intrepid Travel** (small-group adventures).  
- **Volunteer**: **Workaway** lets you stay for free in exchange for work (e.g., cat-sitting at a shelter!).
