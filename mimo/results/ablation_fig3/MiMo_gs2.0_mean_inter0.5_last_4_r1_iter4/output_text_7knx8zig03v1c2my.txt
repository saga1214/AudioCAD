Here are some treat recommendations and an explanation of clicker training:

### Treat Recommendations:
- **Best flavors**: Chicken, beef, or salmon (their puppy already likes chicken!). Look for small, soft, low-calorie options like freeze-dried treats or training pellets.
- **Size**: Tiny bites (e.g., ¼ of a small treat) since Cosmo is only 3 pounds.
- **Avoid**: Chocolate, grapes, or onions—these are toxic to dogs.

### Clicker Training Explained:
**How it works**:  
1. **Clicker as a marker**: The clicker makes a sharp, consistent sound ("click") that signals *exactly* when Cosmo does the right thing.  
2. **Immediate reward**: Within 1 second of the click, give a treat.  
3. **Repetition**: Over time, Cosmo learns that "click + treat" = good behavior.  

**Example for "sit"**:  
- Hold treat near Cosmo’s nose → raise toward his head.  
- When his bottom touches the floor → **click** → give treat.  
- Repeat 10+ times daily. Soon, he’ll sit on cue!  

**Why it works**:  
- The click precisely marks the desired behavior.  
- Builds trust and focus.  
- Works for all training (e.g., recall, "stay," or crate training).  

### Crate Training Tips:  
- **Make it cozy**: Line the crate with soft blankets, add a favorite toy, and hide treats inside.  
- **Positive association**: Feed meals in the crate, reward calm behavior.  
- **Never use it as punishment**—it should feel like a safe den.  

### Recall Training (if Cosmo escapes):  
- **Use a happy voice**: Call his name + "come!" with treats.  
- **Reward immediately**: If he returns, shower him with praise and treats.  
- **Practice often**: Start in quiet areas, gradually increase distance.  

Clicker training and crate setup will make Cosmo a well-behaved, confident companion. Happy training! 🐾
