You can absolutely submit an illustrated manuscript, and that's often a big advantage. The process just depends on whether you want to self-publish or traditionally publish.

If you want to traditionally publish, you'll need to find an agent first. You can do this by submitting your query letter and manuscript. If an agent accepts you, they will then submit the whole package—the text and your illustrations—to their publisher contacts. The publisher will then decide if they want to publish it.

If you want to self-publish, you can go directly to a publisher that accepts illustrated manuscripts, like a hybrid publisher or a traditional one that works with self-publishing platforms like Amazon KDP. In that case, you would submit your manuscript and your illustrations together. Just be sure to check the publisher's specific guidelines to see if they prefer certain styles of artwork.
