Okay, so you're asking about diesel camper van maintenance before a Midwest road trip. Let's break this down. Brakes are critical for safety. One sign of significant wear is a soft or spongy brake pedal. If you press the brake pedal and it feels mushy, that could mean air in the system or worn brake pads. Another sign is a burning smell when you brake, which could indicate overheating. For spark plugs, they usually last about 60,000 to 100,000 miles on a diesel, but you'd need a mechanic to change them. It's not a DIY job unless you're experienced. So, getting a professional inspection before the trip is a good idea. They can check the brakes, spark plugs, and other critical systems. That way, you can hit the road with peace of mind.
