### **Food & Drinks**
**Appetizers/Finger Foods:**
- 1 lb shrimp (for cocktail)
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 package (16-20) slider buns
- 1 package (12-16) pigs in a blanket/puff pastry
- 1 bag cheese sticks or mozzarella balls
- Fresh baguette or rolls (for dips)

**Main Course:**
- 1 large ham (12-14 lbs) – *for 24 servings*
- 1 (36-serving) box scalloped potatoes (or make from scratch: potatoes, cream, butter, salt/pepper)

**Sides:**
- 24 large asparagus spears (fresh or frozen)
- 1 lb carrots (for roasted carrots)
- Olive oil, salt, and pepper (for carrots)
- Fresh lemon (for asparagus)

**Kids' Drinks:**
- 24 boxes of grape juice (or bottles)

**Adult Drinks:**
- 8 bottles champagne/sparkling wine (for midnight toast)
- 1 bottle non-alcoholic champagne (for kids/guests who don’t drink)
- **Signature Mocktail Ingredients:**  
  - 2 cups cranberry juice  
  - 1 cup orange juice  
  - 1 cup soda water  
  - 1 cup fresh pomegranate seeds or diced apples (optional)  
  - Garnish: citrus slices, mint

**Other:**
- 1 bag tortilla chips (for dip)
- 1 jar salsa/hummus/dip (for chips)

---

### **Party Supplies**
**Decorations:**
- Balloons (various sizes)
- Festive tablecloths or napkins
- Noisemakers or party hats (for kids)

**Utensils & Serving:**
- Extra paper plates, cups, and napkins (24+ sets)
- Serving platters, bowls, and tongs
- Kid flutes or cups (for grape juice)
- Cocktail shaker, strainer, and muddler (for mocktail)

**Snacks for Kids:**
- Small candy bags (for after dinner)
- Play kits (optional: coloring books, crayons)

---

### **Tips**
1. **Prep Ahead:**  
   - Marinate ham (24-48 hrs in citrus juice + spices).  
   - Roast carrots asparagus early; reheat.  
   - Make mocktail mix ahead; store in pitcher.
2. **Leftovers:**  
   - Ham and scalloped potatoes freeze well.  
   - Pack leftovers in containers for guests to take home.
3. **Kid-Friendly:**  
   - Label food/drink clearly (e.g., "Kid Juice" in flutes).  
   - Set up a "helper station" for kids to assemble sliders or decorate cookies.
