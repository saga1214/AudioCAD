**Allergy Considerations:**

*   **Watermelon-Free:** All listed fruits (strawberries, blueberries, grapes, pineapple, kiwi, mango) are safe. Double-check labels on packaged items (crackers, cheese, yogurt, hummus) for any hidden watermelon or other allergens.
*   **Label Clearly:** Use waterproof labels on all containers, clearly stating ingredients, especially highlighting the watermelon-free status and any other major allergens present (e.g., nuts, dairy if using cheese dip).

*   **Cooler:** Keep drinks cold and food fresh.
*   **Trash Bags:** Easy cleanup!
