Here's the complete analogy of the human circulatory system for a child, using your car and refueling idea:

### 🚗 The Blood System = Tiny Cars on Body Roads
1. **Heart = Gas Station & Engine**  
   → Pumps cars (blood) everywhere.  
   → Refuels cars with fresh oxygen at the lungs.

2. **Lungs = Oxygen Refueling Station**  
   → Where blood cars stop for "fresh fuel" (oxygen).

3. **Arteries = Big Highways**  
   → Wide, strong roads carrying blood *away* from the heart.

4. **Capillaries = Tiny Streets**  
   → Super small roads where blood cars *deliver oxygen* to muscles/cells.

5. **Veins = Return Roads**  
   → Take oxygen-poor blood *back* to the heart/lungs for refueling.

6. **Kidneys = Car Washes**  
   → Filter dirty fuel (waste/water) out of blood → turns it into pee.

7. **Liver = Fuel Processor**  
   → Sorts nutrients (from food), cleans harmful stuff, and stores energy.

### 🔄 How It All Works Together:
- **Heart** pumps cars → **Arteries** take them fast → **Capillaries** deliver fuel → **Veins** return cars → **Lungs** refuel them → Repeat!  
- **Kidneys** clean the cars' "fuel" → **Liver** processes the "fuel" → Body stays powered and clean!  

This keeps the whole system simple, visual, and fun for kids! 🌟
