### U-Turn Rules & Penalties in BC
1. **General Rule**: U-turns are allowed **except** where prohibited by signs, traffic control devices (like stop signs or signals), or where it would interfere with traffic.
2. **Visibility Requirement**: You must ensure a clear **150-meter view** in both directions before executing a U-turn. This is challenging to judge visually.
3. **Prohibited Locations**:  
   - Near intersections, curves, hills, or tunnels  
   - Where traffic flow is obstructed  
   - On multi-lane highways (except designated areas)  
   - Near pedestrian crossings or intersections  
4. **Penalties**:  
   - **Fines**: $160 (2024) + 3 demerit points  
   - If done at an intersection with a signal: $250 + 4 demerit points  
   - Repeat offenses may lead to license suspension.

**Tip**: Use intersections with clear visibility (e.g., 4-way stops) or designated U-turn lanes. If unsure, wait for a safe gap or find a legal turn lane.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients
Here’s a classic recipe for 4 servings:

| **Category**       | **Ingredients**                                                                 |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Pasta**           | 400g spaghetti (or bucatini/fettuccine)                                         |
| **Protein**         | 200g pancetta (diced) or guanciale (traditional)                                |
| **Eggs**            | 3 large eggs + 1 yolk (whisked together)                                        |
| **Cheese**          | 100g Pecorino Romano (grated) + 50g Parmigiano-Reggiano (optional)               |
| **Aromatics**       | 2 cloves garlic (minced)                                                        |
| **Seasoning**       | Freshly ground black pepper (generous)                                          |
| **Optional**        | 1 tbsp parsley (chopped), red pepper flakes for heat                             |

**Key Notes**:  
- **No cream**: Authentic carbonara uses only eggs, cheese, and pasta water.  
- **Cook pancetta** until crisp (save drippings for seasoning pasta).  
- **Reserve 1 cup pasta water** before draining to adjust sauce consistency.  
- **Combine gently** over low heat to avoid scrambling eggs.
