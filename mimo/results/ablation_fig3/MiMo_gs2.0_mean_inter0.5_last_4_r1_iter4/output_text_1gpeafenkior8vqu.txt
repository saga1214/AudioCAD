Here’s a **balanced 10-day Japan itinerary** combining deep exploration of Tokyo/Osaka/Kyoto/Nara with time for your Art History coursework. I’ve prioritized efficiency, cultural immersion, and visual research opportunities for your slides:

---

### **Tokyo (4 Days)**  
**Day 1:**  
- *Morning:* Check out, travel to Tokyo (Shinkansen ~2.5 hrs).  
- *Afternoon:* Explore **Asakusa** (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Street).  
- *Evening:* Sumida River cruise (scenic views).  
- *Work:* 1-hour workout. *Presentation:* Brainstorm ukiyo-e topics; sketch layout ideas.  

**Day 2:**  
- *Morning:* **Ueno Park** (Tokyo National Museum for art history literacy content).  
- *Afternoon:* **Shibuya** (crosswalk, Hachiko Statue – great for design imagery).  
- *Evening:* Shinjuku (Golden Gai for unique typography inspiration).  
- *Work:* Design color scheme (cool blues/whites for modern Tokyo).  

**Day 3:**  
- *Morning:* **Edo-Tokyo Museum** (ancient Japanese art history).  
- *Afternoon:* **teamLab Borderless** (digital art – ideal for slide visuals).  
- *Evening:* Akihabara (pop culture art, font/style research).  
- *Work:* Workout + font selection (clean sans-serif for readability).  

**Day 4:**  
- *Morning:* **Meiji Shrine** (Shinto art, layout inspiration).  
- *Afternoon:* **Ginza** (ceramics/calligraphy shops for images).  
- *Evening:* Depart for Osaka (~30-min train).  
- *Work:* Compile literacy content; outline presentation structure.  

---

### **Osaka (3 Days) + Day Trip**  
**Day 5:**  
- *Morning:* **Osaka Castle** (woodcarving, color scheme red/gold).  
- *Afternoon:* **Dotonbori** (street art, typography for signage).  
- *Evening:* Kuromon Market (food + downtime).  
- *Work:* Workout + incorporate Osaka visuals.  

**Day 6:**  
- *Morning:* Train to **Kyoto** (~30-min ride).  
- *Afternoon:* **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (torii gates – dramatic color/design).  
- *Evening:* Return to Osaka.  
- *Work:* Review slide layout (asymmetry for Inari gates).  

**Day 7:**  
- *Morning:* **Kiyomizu-dera Temple** (bamboo forest – nature theme).  
- *Afternoon:* **Nara** (day trip; Todai-ji Temple’s giant Buddha, deer – unique imagery).  
- *Evening:* Return to Osaka.  
- *Work:* Finalize images; start slide design.  

---

### **Kyoto (3 Days)**  
**Day 8:** Heian-era art focus:  
- *Morning:* **Nijo Castle** (tapered gate design – font inspiration).  
- *Afternoon:* **Kinkaku-ji** (gold leaf – luxury color scheme).  
- *Evening:* Gion district (geisha art, subtle elegance).  

**Day 9:**  
- *Morning:* **Ginkaku-ji** (stone garden – minimalist layout).  
- *Afternoon:* **Imperial Palace** (courtly art history).  
- *Evening:* Return to Osaka.  

**Day 10:**  
- *Morning:* **Osaka Museum of Housing & Living** (traditional crafts).  
- *Afternoon:* Check out hotel, travel home.  
- *Work:* Final slide edits; submit coursework.  

---

### **Key Design Tips for Your Slides:**  
1. **Images:** Ukiyo-e prints (Edo period), kimono patterns, temple architecture.  
2. **Layout:** Vertical flow for historical timelines; asymmetry for Japanese aesthetics.  
3. **Color:**  
   - *Japanese art:* Indigo, washi paper white, gold accents.  
   - *Modern Tokyo:* Blues/grays for contrast.  
4. **Fonts:** Traditional calligraphy-inspired scripts (literacy slides) + clean sans-serifs (body text).  

### **Hotel Tips:**  
- **Pick-up Service:** Larger hotels (like The Ritz Osaka) often offer free transfers. Email them directly for confirmation.  
- **Check-out:** Typically **10:00 AM** (confirm with your Osaka hotel).  

### **Workout Integration:**  
- Morning: Gym/running before sightseeing.  
- Evening: 1-hour workout after dinner.
