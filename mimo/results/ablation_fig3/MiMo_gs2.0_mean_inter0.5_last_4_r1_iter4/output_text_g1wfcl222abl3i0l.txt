### 🌑 Spooky Additions for Maximum Chills:
1. **Miniature "Bodkin" Projections**:  
   Use a black lacquer or matte spray to weather the glass globes, then add tiny faux metal spikes (like a "bodkin" from folklore) around the pumpkins. Pair with a flickering LED tea light inside for a ghostly glow.  

2. **"Cursed" Moss & Roots**:  
   Drape faux black moss or dried ferns around the base, and tuck in miniature twisted roots made from wire + dark clay. Moss hides cobwebs beautifully!  

3. **Dramatic Shadows**:  
   Place a small black mirror or mirrored tile at the base. The fairy lights will cast haunting reflections, making it look like ghosts are flickering inside the globes.  

4. **"Blood" Splatters**:  
   Use hot glue to attach tiny drops of red resin (or fake blood) near the pumpkins. It adds a sinister, "accidental" vibe!  

5. **Masquerade Horror Twist**:  
   If you go with the masks, try cracked porcelain or "decayed" lace masks. Pair them with a single black rose—it’s gothic, elegant, and unsettling.  

### 🔥 Quick Glue Gun Hack:
If it’s overheating, try:  
- **Cooling it down**: Place it in the freezer for 10 minutes.  
- **Lowering the temp**: Use a lower setting if your gun has one.  
- **Less is more**: Apply glue sparingly to avoid melting the globes.
