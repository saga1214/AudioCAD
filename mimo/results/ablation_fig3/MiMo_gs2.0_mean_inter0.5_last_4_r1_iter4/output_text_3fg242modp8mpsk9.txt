Okay, you could start with a sunset sail. Many charter companies offer private sunset cruises, which are perfect for couples.

If you want something more adventurous, you could look into a kayak or stand-up paddleboard tour along a quiet shore or a mangrove channel. It's a great way to explore the coastline up close.

For something truly special, you might consider a cooking class at sea. Some catamarans offer private chef classes where you can learn to make fresh seafood dishes while cruising.

And when you get back to shore, a nice seafood dinner at a cliffside restaurant or a beachfront tiki bar would be a perfect, romantic end to the day.
