Let's focus on the key products to avoid to minimize your exposure. First, non-stick cookware is a big one; switching to stainless steel or cast iron is a great step. Second, try to avoid grease-resistant packaging, like what comes with fast food or microwave popcorn. Third, steer clear of anything labeled stain-resistant or waterproof, such as carpets and some clothing. Fourth, check your cosmetics, as some products can contain these chemicals. And fifth, since tap water can be a source, using a certified water filter is a very effective measure.
