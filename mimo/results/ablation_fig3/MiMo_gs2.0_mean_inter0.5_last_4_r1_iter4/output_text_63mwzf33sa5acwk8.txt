Ah, the classic Vegas loop! If you’re camping in the desert and then want to head into Vegas, you can totally do that. You’d just drive northeast from Nevada towards Las Vegas. It’s about a 30-minute drive from Red Rock Canyon. 

For a proper Vegas experience, it really depends on what you two are into. You could splurge on a fancy hotel with a pool, or go for something more unique like a themed hotel. Vegas has everything from high-end to quirky. And of course, you can’t miss the shows, the food, or maybe just walking the Strip at night. If you’re adventurous, there are also plenty of day trips from Vegas, like to the Grand Canyon or the Hoover Dam. Just remember, Vegas is expensive, so plan your budget.
