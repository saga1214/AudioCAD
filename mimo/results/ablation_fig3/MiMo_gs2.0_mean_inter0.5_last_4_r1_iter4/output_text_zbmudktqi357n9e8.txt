Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn inspiration! The best time to go would definitely be late September through November, which is their *verano秋* season. That's when you get the most vibrant fall colors—especially in the highlands, like around San Miguel de Allende or Pátzcuaro. The Oaxacan Valley also has amazing foliage during that time. 

If you're specifically looking for those intense autumn colors, late October to early November is probably the sweet spot. You'll get those bright reds, oranges, and yellows from the maples and other deciduous trees, especially in the mountainous regions. The beaches, like Tulum or Cancún, will still have plenty of color from tropical plants, but the real show is in the highlands. 

Oh, and a little tip: the markets there are amazing for picking up local colors and inspiration for your palette. So yeah, late October to early November for the best balance of warm weather and gorgeous scenery. That sounds like a perfect trip to get inspired for your art!
