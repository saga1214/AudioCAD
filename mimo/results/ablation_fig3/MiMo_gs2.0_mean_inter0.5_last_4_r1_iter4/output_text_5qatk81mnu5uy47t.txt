**Your Dream Trip: Costa Rica Focus**

1.  **Top Dive Sites (Tropical Paradise):**
    *   **Cocos Island:** (The pinnacle!) Famous for incredible marine life (sharks, manta rays, dolphins), dramatic drop-offs, and big-wave diving (for experienced divers). Requires a boat trip from Santa Teresa or Puerto Jiménez. *Perfect if you want a true adventure.*
    *   **Tortuguero National Park:** Accessible only by boat or plane. Focuses on incredible biodiversity *on land and in water*. Great for spotting turtles (nesting season June-August), manatees, and diverse fish near mangrove channels. Calmer waters. *Perfect if you love jungle/tortoise encounters.*
    *   **Drake Bay (Ostional):** The northernmost point of Costa Rica. Pristine, cold (but still tropical!), and known for incredible marine life diversity, including whale sharks (May-Oct), manta rays, and abundant fish. Requires a boat trip from Drake Bay.
    *   **Cantilloes (Manuel Antonio):** Near the popular beach town. Offers good visibility and a variety of dive sites suitable for beginners and intermediates, including coral gardens and reef walls. Easy access from Manuel Antonio.
    *   **Isla del Drago (San José del Cabra):** Close to San José airport. Features stunning volcanic rock formations, deep walls, and good visibility. Offers a mix of easier reefs and more challenging drift dives. Great for a quick, accessible trip.

2.  **Why Costa Rica is Perfect for Your Debate:**
    *   Costa Rican reefs vary! Cantilloes might favor short fins for maneuverability in coral gardens. Drake Bay/Tortuguero/Cocos might favor the extra power of long fins for currents or deeper walls. You'll get a practical feel for both!

3.  **Planning Tips:**
    *   **Booking:** Use reputable dive operators (like PADI or RAID centers) based near your base (Manuel Antonio, Drake Bay, Santa Teresa, Tortuguero). They handle gear, boats, and guides.

    *   **Season:** Dry season (Dec-Apr) offers the best visibility. Whale shark season peaks in Drake Bay (May-Oct), especially June-August for nesting sea turtles.
    *   **Combine with Nature:** Costa Rica is more than diving! Add a few days of hiking, wildlife spotting, or visiting a hot spring.
