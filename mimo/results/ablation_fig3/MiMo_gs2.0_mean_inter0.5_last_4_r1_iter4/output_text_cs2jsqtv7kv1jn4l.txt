Here are five popular choices:

First, there's the Nintendo Switch. It's really popular because you can play games both at home on the TV and on the go with a portable screen. It has a huge variety of games, from Mario and Zelda to Pokémon, and it's great for playing together in multiplayer mode.

Second is the PlayStation 5. This one is known for having amazing graphics and fast loading times, which makes it perfect for immersive single-player games like Spider-Man or God of War. It also has great multiplayer online options.

Third is the Xbox Series X or S. It's very competitive with the PlayStation 5 and has a big library of games, including many from Microsoft. It's also great for playing with friends online through Xbox Live.

Fourth is the Nintendo Switch Lite. This is a smaller, handheld-only version of the Switch, which is perfect if you want something more portable, though it doesn't have the same power as the full console.

And fifth is the Steam Deck. This is a bit different because it's a handheld PC, so you can play thousands of games from PC, including Steam games, right from your hands. It's very powerful but has a steeper learning curve than the others.
