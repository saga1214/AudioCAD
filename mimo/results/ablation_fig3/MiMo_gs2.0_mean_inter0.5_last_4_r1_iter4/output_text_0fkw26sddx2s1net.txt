Of course. Here’s a shopping list for your Asian-inspired meal, keeping legume allergies and seafood avoidance in mind. Let's break it down.

First, for the starter options. If you choose Miso Soup, you'll need miso paste, either white or red, a package of nori seaweed, and some sliced green onions. For Cucumber Salad, get some English or Persian cucumbers, sesame oil, rice vinegar, sugar, and a little soy sauce.

Next, for the main course, Chicken Teriyaki. You'll need boneless, skinless chicken breasts, soy sauce, mirin—which is a sweet rice wine you might need to substitute with a mix of sugar and rice vinegar if you can't find it—rice vinegar, garlic, ginger, and some sugar. For steamed jasmine rice, jasmine rice itself and some water. For the stir-fried vegetables, you can use bell peppers, snap peas, shredded carrots, and some sliced mushrooms. And finally, some sesame oil for stir-frying.

Lastly, for the dessert. If you're making Mango Sticky Rice, you'll need sticky jasmine rice, fresh ripe mangoes, coconut milk, and sugar. If you go for the homemade Matcha Ice Cream, you'll need matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and a pinch of salt.

Let me know if you’d like any substitutions for mirin or other items. Enjoy your husband’s dinner!
