Of course! Vancouver is a fantastic base for road trips.

Here are five great options. First, you could head to the Okanagan Valley, about a 3-hour drive. You can visit wine country, and I'd recommend trying the Okanagan Lake Winery. You can also go to the Shuswap Lake area, which is perfect for kayaking and hiking. Another option is the Fraser Canyon, which has amazing berry picking and the Chilcotin Gold Mine tour. You could also drive to the Salish Sea Islands for unique birdwatching and kayaking. Finally, for a bit of a longer trip, you can go to the Kootenay region for its stunning hot springs and hiking. Enjoy your adventures!
