### 🍤 5 Main Dishes (Shellfish-Free, Diabetes-Friendly)
1. **Spicy Coconut Curry with Tofu & Veggies**  
   *Why it works:* High in protein (tofu), low-carb veggies, and no added sugar. Serve with cauliflower rice instead of white rice.  
   *Add ocean flair:* Top with fresh mango strips and a sprinkle of chili-lime seasoning.  

2. **Mango-Glazed Grilled Salmon**  
   *Why it works:* Rich in omega-3s (great for diabetes), paired with grilled asparagus and quinoa.  
   *Ocean touch:* Use mango puree as the glaze—sweet, spicy, and visually vibrant.  

3. **Spicy Shrimp-Free "Shrimp" Tempura with Mango Salsa**  
   *Why it works:* Light, crispy tempura made with tofu or cauliflower. Salsa adds freshness without shellfish.  
   *Sweet twist:* Serve with a side of mango-lime rice.  

4. **Spicy Tomato & Tuna Stew with Avocado**  
   *Why it works:* Tuna is lean protein; tomatoes add flavor without sugar. Avocado adds healthy fats.  
   *Ocean theme:* Garnish with sea salt and fresh cilantro.  

5. **Thai-Inspired Stir-Fry with Tofu & Bell Peppers**  
   *Why it works:* Low-carb, high-protein stir-fry with minimal sugar. Use tamari (not soy sauce) for gluten-free.  
   *Sweet-spicy combo:* Finish with a splash of mango puree and chili flakes.  

### 🍮 5 Desserts (Sweet & Shellfish-Free)
1. **Coconut-Chocolate Mousse**  
   *Why it works:* Naturally sweetened with dates, rich in healthy fats.  
   *Ocean vibe:* Top with toasted coconut flakes.  

2. **Spicy Mango Sorbet (Small Portion)**  
   *Why it works:* Low-glycemic sorbet (use ripe mangoes).  
   *Diabetes note:* Serve as a small dessert or palate cleanser.  

3. **Passionfruit Panna Cotta**  
   *Why it works:* Light, tangy, and rich in antioxidants. Use low-sugar gelatin.  
   *Sweetness:* Sweeten with monk fruit or stevia.  

4. **Chocolate-Dipped Strawberries with Sea Salt**  
   *Why it works:* Classic, elegant, and no added sugar needed.  
   *Ocean touch:* Roll in crushed freeze-dried mango for color.  

5. **Spicy Orange & Ginger Sorbet**  
   *Why it works:* Citrus is naturally sweet and low-glycemic.  
   *Flavor boost:* Add a pinch of ginger for warmth.  

### 🎯 Key Tips for Success  
- **Allergy Safety:** Double-check all packaged ingredients (soy sauce, broths, etc.) for hidden shellfish.  
- **Diabetes Focus:** Prioritize lean proteins, veggies, healthy fats, and minimal added sugars. Portion control is key!  
- **Spice Smart:** Use chili flakes, ginger, or cayenne for heat without added sugar.  
- **Presentation:** Garnish with lime wedges, edible flowers, or a light sprinkle of smoked paprika for an "ocean sunset" effect.
