That's a great question! Here are some thoughtful gift ideas tailored to your hobbies and interests, ranging from beginner-friendly to more specialized:

### For Tailoring (Since it seems like the frontrunner):
1. **Quality Fabric Swatches**: A sampler pack of mid-weight cottons (like twill or poplin) to experiment with.  
2. **Specialized Sewing Needles**: A set of sharp needles (e.g., Schmetz or Gutterman) in sizes 70/10 or 90/14 for precision work.  
3. **Sewing Machine Upgrade**: If you don’t have one, a reliable entry-level machine (e.g., Janome HD3000) or a **sewing machine accessory kit** (presser feet, thimble, etc.).  
4. **Vintage Pen Display Case**: To showcase your Parker 51 and other pens—tailoring could even inspire you to build one!  
5. **Tailoring Journal**: A small notebook to sketch fabric ideas or track projects.  

### For Woodworking (If you explore it):
1. **Hand Plan**: A great starting tool (e.g., a #5 or #4 bench plane).  
2. **Sanding Block Set**: Pre-cut blocks in various grits for smooth finishes.  
3. **Woodworking Workbench**: A portable mini-workbench for small projects.  
4. **Stain or Finish Kit**: Pre-mixed finishes (like Minwax) for easy application.  

### For Clay Work (If you try it):
1. **Sculpting Tools Kit**: Basic tools (needle, ribs, wire cutter) in wood or metal.  
2. **Air-Dry Clay**: A beginner-friendly, non-toxic option for practice.  
3. **Pottery Wheel Starter Kit**: If you commit, a small electric wheel (e.g., Dharma) is ideal.  

### General Gift Ideas:
- **Subscription Box**: A monthly box like *The Tailor’s Guide* or *Woodworking Monthly*.  
- **Vintage Fountain Pen Supply Kit**: Ink cartridges, cleaning tools, or a new nib.  
- **Skill Development**: Online courses (Skillshare, Domestika) for tailoring, woodworking, or pen restoration.  

### Key Considerations:
- **Practicality**: Since you like precision, gifts that improve your workflow (e.g., shears, needles, or a better machine) will likely be appreciated.  
- **Personal Touch**: A gift related to your favorite Parker 51 (e.g., a custom pen case or engraved nib) could be extra meaningful.  
- **Flexibility**: If you’re still exploring, a gift card to a craft store (Joann, Hobby Lobby, or a local shop) gives you freedom to choose.
