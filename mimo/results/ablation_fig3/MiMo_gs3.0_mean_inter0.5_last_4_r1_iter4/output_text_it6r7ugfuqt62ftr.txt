Based on your Northern California location, dislike of cold, diet goals wanting you able to wear your bathing suit, and desire for warm weather swimming—all while traveling with family—I've put together a detailed 4-5 day vacation itinerary for two main warm destinations. Since your car is too small, renting a minivan or SUV is highly recommended for group travel.

Here are two excellent options:

### Option 1: Laguna Beach, Orange County (Warmer Coastal Escape)
*   **Why?** Consistently mild (60-75°F), stunning beaches, ocean pools, lots of casual dining, great for families.
*   **Best Time:** Year-round, but avoid peak summer crowds if possible.
*   **Travel:** Rent a minivan/SUV from North County Auto Rentals or similar.
*   **Accommodation:** Consider a condo with a pool (e.g., Beachcomber Resort) or a hotel like The Beachcomber Hotel & Spa (pool, restaurant).
*   **Pack:** Bathing suits, swimwear, cover-ups, swimsuits, rash guards (for sun protection), flip-flops/sandals, light beachwear (shorts, tank tops, sundresses), a light jacket for evenings, sunglasses, hat, high-SPF sunscreen, reusable water bottle, GF snacks if desired.

**4-Day Itinerary:**

*   **Day 1: Arrival & Settling In**
    *   Arrive, check into accommodation.
    *   Relax by the pool or beach.
    *   **Dinner:** Casual beachside spot like **The Beachcomber Cafe** (great GF options, ocean views) or **Tacos El Gordo** (famous for tacos, many GF options).
*   **Day 2: Beach & Exploration**
    *   **Morning:** Swim and relax at **Heisler Park Beach** or explore tide pools at **Main Beach**. Rent kayaks/paddleboards.
    *   **Afternoon:** Hike part of the **Laguna Coast Path** (easy, scenic). Visit **Heisler Park** (art galleries, shops, playground).
    *   **Evening:** Dinner at **Baja Beach Cafe** (oceanfront, good GF choices) or **The Surfing Pig** (casual, check GF menu).
*   **Day 3: Nature & Shopping**
    *   **Morning:** Explore **Crystal Cove State Park** (hike to tide pools, historic cabins, ocean views).
    *   **Afternoon:** Browse unique shops on **Main Beach** or **South Coast Plaza** (luxury shopping, but also casual boutiques).
    *   **Evening:** Dinner at **Café Gratitude** (creative, healthy menu, many GF options) or **Puesto** (upscale Mexican, excellent GF).
*   **Day 4: Leisure & Departure**
    *   **Morning:** Relax by the pool, take a leisurely walk along the coast, or visit the **Laguna Beach Art Center**.
    *   **Lunch:** Pack a picnic or grab sandwiches at **The Beachcomber Cafe**.
    *   **Afternoon:** Depart for Northern California.

### Option 2: Carmel Valley, Monterey County (Inland Warmth & Luxury)
*   **Why?** Warmer (65-80°F), luxury resorts with pools, gourmet dining, scenic drives, art galleries, spa options. Excellent for celebrating a 12th birthday.
*   **Best Time:** Spring (April-June) or Fall (Sept-Oct) for best weather.
*   **Travel:** Rent a minivan/SUV.
*   **Accommodation:** Consider a luxury hotel with a pool (e.g., **Carmel Valley Ranch**, **The Lodge at Palms Cove**) or a condo. Many cater well to families and dietary needs.
*   **Pack:** Same as Laguna Beach, plus maybe a light sweater for cooler evenings.

**4-Day Itinerary:**

*   **Day 1: Arrival & Welcome**
    *   Arrive, check into accommodation.
    *   Relax by the resort pool.
    *   **Dinner:** Enjoy the resort's restaurant or try **The Farm at San Simeon** (farm-to-table, excellent GF options) or **Carmel Valley Bistro** (French-inspired, check GF menu).
*   **Day 2: Scenic Drive & Wine**
    *   **Morning:** Drive the stunning **17-Mile Drive** (stunning coastal views, Pebble Beach).
    *   **Afternoon:** Visit **Carmel Beach** (different vibe than Laguna), explore **Carmel Village** (shops, art galleries), maybe a stop at a **winery** (like **Chateau Montelena** - check tours/GF needs).
    *   **Evening:** Dinner at **Mesa** (modern Californian, excellent GF menu) or **Oenotri** (wine bar/restaurant, check GF).
*   **Day 3: Spa Day & Pool Fun**
    *   **Morning:** Enjoy a spa treatment (many offer GF options) or simply relax by the pool.
    *   **Afternoon:** Explore **Monterey** (easy drive) - visit the world-famous **Monterey Bay Aquarium**, walk the **Lighthouse Point** boardwalk, or explore Cannery Row. *Note: Aquarium can be a full day.*
    *   **Evening:** Casual dinner at **T烹** (modern Asian fusion, great GF menu) or **The Bench** (casual, check GF).
*   **Day 4: Leisure & Departure**
    *   **Morning:** Leisurely breakfast, final pool time, maybe browse the resort spa shop.
    *   **Lunch:** Grab sandwiches/wraps at the resort or a local deli.
    *   **Afternoon:** Depart for Northern California.

*   **Minivan:** Essential for group travel.
*   **Bathing Suits:** Pack at least two and a cover-up.
*   **Beach Essentials:** Swimwear, flip-flops/sandals, rash guards, sunglasses, hat, high-SPF sunscreen, water bottle.
*   **Casual Wear:** Light cover-ups (towels, sarongs), shorts, tank tops, sundresses, T-shirts.
*   **Layers:** A light jacket or sarong for cooler evenings (especially Carmel Valley).
*   **GF Snacks:** If you prefer, pack some GF snacks for the road.
*   **Reusable Bags:** For groceries or souvenirs.
