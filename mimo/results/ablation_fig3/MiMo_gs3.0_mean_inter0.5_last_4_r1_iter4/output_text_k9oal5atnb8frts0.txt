For a 'Retro Tech' theme, you could do a 'Vintage Computer Repair Lunch.' Serve a hearty sandwich or wrap to symbolize the 'hardware' of the computer, and some fruit salad or a fruit cup for the 'software.' Alongside that, have some pretzels and a bag of mixed nuts as the 'cables' or 'wires.' Another idea is a 'Mainframe Power Lunch.' For this, you could serve a big bowl of chili with a side of cornbread, which represents the 'mainframe.' For 'input/output,' serve some carrot sticks with hummus. And for a fun snack, you could have mini muffins that look like old floppy disks. These ideas tie the theme to food without being too complicated.
