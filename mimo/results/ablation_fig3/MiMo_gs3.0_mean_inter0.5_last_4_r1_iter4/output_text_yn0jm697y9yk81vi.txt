Here’s a consolidated list of my top recommendations for sheet metal cutting, drywall fastening, and insulation—all with local purchasing options in **Indianapolis, IN**:

### **Tools for Cutting Sheet Metal**  
1. **Aviation Snips** (Thinner sheets curves):  
   - **Where to Buy**: The Home Depot, Lowe’s, Tractor Supply Co., Ace Hardware.  
2. **Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel** (Thicker metal/power):  
   - **Where to Buy**: All major stores (Home Depot, Lowe’s, Menards).  
3. **Jigsaw Metal-Cutting Blade** (Curves/slim profiles):  
   - **Where to Buy**: Menards or Home Depot (specialty blade section).  

### **Drywall Fasteners**  
1. **Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws** (1 ¼" length):  
   - **Why**: Superior holding power vs. nails; prevents pops/cracks.  
   - **Where to Buy**: Menards, Home Depot, Lowe’s (bulk packs).  
   - *Safety Note*: Use **ring-shank nails** if you must use nails.  

### **Insulation Options**  
1. **Fiberglass Woven batts** (Pre-cut rolls for studs):  
   - **Where to Buy**: Menards, Home Depot (standard choice).  
2. **Mineral Wool** (Soundproofing/fire resistance):  
   - **Where to Buy**: Menards (specialty section) or online (Amazon, Grainger).  
3. **Spray Foam** (Best air sealing):  
   - **Where to Buy**: Menards (open-cell/closed-cell) or local suppliers (e.g., **Fiberglass Supply Co.** in IN).  

### **Key Store Recommendations for Indianapolis**  
- **Menards** (Best selection for tools, screws, and insulation):  
  - Locations: **11111 E 10th St, IN** (Westside) or **1001 N Spring Ave, IN** (Southside).  
- **Home Depot/Lowe’s** (Aviation snips, jigsaw blades, drywall screws).  
- **Ace Hardware** (Aviation snips, basic screws).  

### **Safety Reminder**  
- **Angle Grinders/Jigsaws**: Wear **cut-resistant gloves**, **eye protection**, and a **respirator** (metal dust).  
- **Spray Foam**: Use **ventilation**, a **respirator**, and **PPE** (chemical-resistant clothing).  

For insulation, prioritize **fiberglass batts** unless soundproofing/fireproofing is critical. All items can be sourced locally at Menards or Home Depot/Lowe’s.
