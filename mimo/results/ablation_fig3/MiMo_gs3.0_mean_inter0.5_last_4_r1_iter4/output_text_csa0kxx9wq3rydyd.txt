Alright, let's break down what you'll need. For smoking, you'll need a smoker. For roasting, your main oven. For methods that need the oven, you have your two oven ovens, which will be used for smoking the turkey, baking the mac and cheese, roasting the root vegetables, and toasting the Brussels sprouts. For the toaster oven, you'll use it to roast the asparagus. For the Instant Pot, the cranberry sauce will be made there. And for no-bake desserts, you'll need double ovens for the pumpkin cheesecake and icebox cake, plus a slow cooker for the apple crisp. For stovetop items, you'll need a stove for the wild rice salad and the fruit tart. That's everything for Thanksgiving! Happy hosting!
