You can definitely learn more. A great place to start is the Lockport Canal Museum, which is located right in the basement of the historic Lockport City Hall. It provides a deep dive into the history of the canal and the city's connection to it.

For a more hands-on experience, you can visit the Erie Canal Museum in Youngstown, which is also in the Finger Lakes region, very close to Rochester. There, you can actually see working models of canal boats and learn all about the technology that made the whole system possible. It's a perfect vacation activity that connects your summer break right back to your hometown history.
