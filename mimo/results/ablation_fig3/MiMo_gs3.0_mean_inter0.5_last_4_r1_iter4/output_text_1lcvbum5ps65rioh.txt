### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia (BC)
1. **General Rule**: U-turns are legal unless prohibited by a sign or specific conditions.
2. **Key Restrictions**:
   - **Not at Intersections**: You cannot make a U-turn at a traffic light, stop sign, or controlled intersection.
   - **Visibility**: You must ensure you can see **150 meters (approx. 490 feet)** in both directions with no obstructions (hills, curves, etc.).
   - **No Cross Traffic**: Avoid U-turns if they block traffic or pedestrians.
   - **Specific Locations**: Prohibited on highways, near driveways, or in business districts without signage.
3. **Penalties**:
   - **First Offense**: ~$150 fine + 2 demerit points.
   - **Repeat Offenses**: Higher fines and potential license suspension.
   - **Serious Violations** (e.g., causing an accident): Jail time or吊销驾照 (driver's license revocation).

**Pro Tip**: Use designated U-turn lanes (if available) or wait for a clear, safe gap in traffic. Apps like Google Maps can alert you to restricted zones.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients
**For 4 servings**:  
| **Ingredient** | **Amount** | **Notes** |
|----------------|------------|-----------|
| Spaghetti       | 400g       | Dried pasta |
| Guanciale/Pancetta | 150g (diced) | *Guanciale = cured pork jowl (essential for authentic flavor). Substitute with pancetta (Italian bacon) if unavailable.* |
| Eggs            | 3 large   | *Room temperature* |
| Pecorino Romano | 100g (grated) | *Sharp Italian sheep’s cheese. Substitute with Parmesan if needed.* |
| Black Pepper   | 1 tsp + more | Freshly cracked |
| Salt            | To taste  | |
| Pasta Water    | 200ml     | Reserved for sauce |

**Optional**:  
- Garlic (minced, but traditional carbonara excludes it).  
- Red pepper flakes for heat.  

**Key Tips**:  
1. **No Cream**: Authentic carbonara uses eggs and cheese only.  
2. **Off-Heat Mixing**: Combine eggs, cheese, and pepper in a bowl. Toss pasta with hot water, then mix with guanciale and egg mixture off direct heat to avoid scrambling.  
3. **Guanciale**: Buy from Italian delis (e.g., Whole Foods, Italian markets). If unavailable, use pancetta.
