While the connection you've built is vital, the single most important approach within 20 to 30 minutes now includes two things.

First, establishing Predictability and Safety Routines. Music is great for connection, but try to pair it with a clear signal that 'school time' is starting. For example, you could take off your shoes before playing his song. This subtle cue can help reduce elopement because he starts to understand what's about to happen.

Second, and this is critical, is Environmental Management. You should absolutely report the lack of supervision and the environmental hazards to his BCBA. Until that's addressed, safety has to be your top priority. This might mean you need to modify your goals to first teach safety skills, like how dogs move and how to avoid clutter safely.
