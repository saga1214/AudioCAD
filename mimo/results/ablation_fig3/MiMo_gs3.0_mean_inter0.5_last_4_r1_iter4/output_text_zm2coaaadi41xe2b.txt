Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! 

For Asian noodles, you could check out:
First, there's Sushi Express for quick, affordable sushi and ramen.
Next, there's Daikokuya, which is a great spot for authentic ramen.
Also, there's Pho 79, which serves amazing Vietnamese pho.
And finally, there's Tokyo Joe, a classic spot for Japanese ramen and noodles.

For protein bars, I’d recommend:
First, Quest Nutrition, which are high-protein and low-sugar.
Next, Optimum Nutrition, which are classic and tasty.
Also, The Paleo Bar, which is great for a clean, nutrient-dense bar.
And finally, Clif Builders, which are high in protein and carbs for energy.

Enjoy your meal!
