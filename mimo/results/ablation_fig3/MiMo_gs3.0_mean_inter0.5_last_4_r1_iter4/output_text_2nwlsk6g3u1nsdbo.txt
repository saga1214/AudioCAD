You're welcome! Here's a quick recap of the three shrimp main courses I suggested earlier:  

### 1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined  
- 8 oz pasta (e.g., linguine)  
- 4 tbsp butter  
- 4 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped  
- 2 tbsp lemon juice  
- 1/2 cup heavy cream  
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat)  
- Salt and pepper to taste  

### 2. **Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined  
- Crushed tomatoes (1 can, ~28 oz)  
- 4 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1/4 cup red wine (optional)  
- 1/4 cup butter  
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese  
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley  
- 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes  
- Salt and pepper to taste  

### 3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined  
- 1 cup quick-cooking grits  
- 2 cups chicken or vegetable broth  
- 1/2 tsp Cajun seasoning  
- 1/4 cup chopped green onions  
- 1/4 cup grated cheddar cheese  
- 1/4 cup heavy cream  
- 2 tbsp butter  
- Salt and pepper to taste  

**Tip**: All of these reheate well when brought as meals! Let me know if you'd like more details on any of them. ❤️
