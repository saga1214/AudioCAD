You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is a great gluten-free start.

You could consider rice cakes with gluten-free nut butter for a simple and satisfying snack.

Trail mix is also always a campsite favorite and is very customizable. You could make your own gluten-free mix with nuts, seeds, and gluten-free dried fruit. 

Popcorn can often be made gluten-free as well, just make sure to double-check the labels on the pre-packaged types you buy.
