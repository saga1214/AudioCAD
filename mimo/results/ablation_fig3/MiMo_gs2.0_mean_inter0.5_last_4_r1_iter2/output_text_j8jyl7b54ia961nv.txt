Yes, considering the significant weight of public resistance, my opinion would change. I would still lean towards the vaccination campaign, but I would need to design it with much more care. A vaccination campaign can save lives now, but a sugar campaign is for long-term prevention. Given the ethical controversies around forced vaccination, a national campaign to reduce sugar feels like a more ethically sound and less polarizing choice. It promotes health without directly infringing on deeply held beliefs, which could make it easier to get broad support from the population.
