That sounds like a wonderful plan! Combining your love for oceans and seafood with a romantic getaway is a fantastic idea. Here are some creative water-based activities perfect for a coastal weekend:

1.  **Sunrise Kayaking & Picnic:** Rent private kayaks and paddle silently out to a secluded cove or sandbar at sunrise. Enjoy the stunning light, the sound of the waves, and the feeling of exploration. Pack a simple picnic with fresh local seafood (like oysters, clams, or grilled fish) and enjoy it right there on the water. It's peaceful, intimate, and incredibly romantic.
2.  **Sailboat Cooking Class:** Many coastal towns offer short sailing or cooking classes on small sailboats. Learn the basics of sailing while you catch your own seafood (often included!) or choose from a market selection. Then, work together to prepare your catch or a delicious local dish on the boat before enjoying a gourmet meal as you sail under the stars. Combines adventure, skill-building, and a gourmet experience.
3.  **Private Sunset Sail with a Seafood Feast:** Charter a small private sailboat for a sunset cruise. Many charter companies offer this as a package, often including a delicious seafood dinner prepared onboard as you sail back towards the harbor under the first stars. It's pure romance and relaxation.
4.  **"Shore-to-Boat" Seafood Adventure:** If you have a small boat (or rent one), plan a day trip. Start by exploring different coastal areas by boat, then anchor in a protected bay. Swim or kayak ashore to explore tide pools or a quiet beach. Return to your boat for lunch – fresh-caught fish (if you're willing and able) or a packed seafood lunch you grab from a market before you left. It's active, adventurous, and ends with a private meal.
5.  **Ferry Hopping & Seafood Exploration:** Use the ferry itself as your transport! Take an early trip to a nearby island or smaller town known for its unique seafood (like oyster shacks, clam bars, or fresh fish markets). Explore the island by foot, then return on the ferry. Dedicate the afternoon to sampling the local specialties – maybe oysters on the half-shell, a freshly baked clam bake, or some local catch of the day. It's a delicious journey.
6.  **Boat-Based Wine & Seafood Tasting:** Some areas offer boat trips specifically focused on wine and seafood pairings. They might visit local vineyards along the coast or anchor in scenic spots while a sommelier or knowledgeable host presents a tasting menu of local seafood paired with wines. A unique and sophisticated experience.

**Key things to consider for your trip:**

*   **Accommodation:** Stay in a charming waterfront inn, a cozy seaside cottage, or even a small houseboat if you prefer something unique.
*   **Food:** Research the best local seafood spots – from high-end restaurants to beloved oyster bars and clam shacks. Don't forget to try local specialties.
*   **Weather & Conditions:** Check forecasts and be realistic about wind and wave conditions for your chosen activities. Some activities (like kayaking or sailing) are much more enjoyable in calm conditions.

These activities focus on shared experiences, intimacy, and combining your passions for the water and delicious seafood – the perfect recipe for a romantic getaway straight out of your novel's inspiration! Enjoy your trip!
