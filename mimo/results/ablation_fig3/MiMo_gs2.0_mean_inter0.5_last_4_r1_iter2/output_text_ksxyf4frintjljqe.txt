I’m so sorry you’re all navigating such grief this Thanksgiving. Losing someone who brought so much joy through baking, especially desserts they loved, makes the holidays even harder. Brownies and peach cobbler... those must trigger painful memories for your brother. Maybe this year you can find a way to honor her memory without those triggers, like sharing favorite stories or creating a new ritual together. And it’s OK if you skip those desserts or do something simpler—whatever helps the most right now. Just be gentle with yourselves.

As for your other questions... hiking in Indiana! Turkey Run, Brown County, and Shades are all great options. They offer different kinds of scenery, from sandstone canyons to forested trails. They could be a good place to start.

For a 10th birthday party, a sports theme, superheroes, or video games are usually popular with boys that age. It really depends on his interests, but those are good starting points.

And that 15-minute workout? A quick warm-up, a few sets of squats and push-ups, a core circuit, and a stretch can be really effective. Each move lasts about 30 seconds to a minute, so you can get a full routine in a short time.

That vanilla cake recipe... you just need butter, sugar, eggs, flour, baking powder, milk, and vanilla. For the frosting, it’s just butter, powdered sugar, vanilla, and a little milk. Spread it on the cooled cake, and you’ve got a simple dessert.

I hope you and your family can find some peace this Thanksgiving.
