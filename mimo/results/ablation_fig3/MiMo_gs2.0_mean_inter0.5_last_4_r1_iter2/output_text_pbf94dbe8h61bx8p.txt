Okay, you want simple, meaningful gifts that are appropriate for all ages, and you want your kids to help. Got it. Since macramé seems to be your passion, you could guide the older kids on how to make mini plant hangers for you. They could cut the cord, tie knots, and you handle the final touches. That way, it’s still your craft, but they’re involved.

For the younger ones, like the two-year-old, something like paper garlands would work great. You just cut out shapes or draw them, and they can help glue them to popsicle sticks. Super easy, minimal mess, and they can decorate them however they want. 

And if you want to tie it all together, you could do a family gift-wrapping station. Have everyone help wrap the finished items. You could even make it a game to see who wraps the neatest. That way, everyone’s hands are in the mix, and it’s a fun activity.

The key is just letting them do their part without too much pressure, you know? Does that sound like a good way to go?
