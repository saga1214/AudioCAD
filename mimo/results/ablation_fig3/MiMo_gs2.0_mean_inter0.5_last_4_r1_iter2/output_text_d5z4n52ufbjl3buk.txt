Okay, I'll give you some pointers for your email. The most important thing is to keep the tone professional, respectful, and focused on your desire to learn.

Start by clearly stating your purpose. You could write something like: "I'm writing to request feedback on my recent exam score to understand how I could improve my essay writing for this course."

Then, express your appreciation. Add a sentence like: "I found your lectures very insightful, and I'm committed to doing well in your class."

Next, make a specific request. For example: "If you have time, I would be grateful to hear what stood out to you in my work, or what areas I could focus on for future assignments."

Finally, offer a way to meet. You could say: "Would you be available for a brief chat at your convenience? Thank you for your time and guidance."

This approach frames the whole thing as a request for help, which is much less likely to trigger his ego than a direct challenge.
