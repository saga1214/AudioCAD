Here’s a **pirate-themed, sand-friendly shopping list** for your nautical birthday party, designed for easy handling at the beach while keeping your son immersed in the pirate theme:

---

### **Appetizers & Main Dishes**
1. **Mini Pirate Ship Sandwiches**  
   - *Ingredients:* Bread, cheese slices, turkey/ham, butter (for sealing).  
   - *Theme Twist:* Cut into small "ships" or wrap in paper sails.  
2. **"Buried Treasure" Cheese & Fruit Cups**  
   - *Ingredients:* Mini cheddar cubes, grapes, berries, melon balls.  
   - *Theme Twist:* Arrange in a plastic "treasure chest" container.  
3. **Pirate Goldfish Crackers**  
   - *Brand:* Goldfish crackers (pack in a pirate-themed snack bag).  
4. **Jelly "Pearls"**  
   - *Ingredients:* Strawberry or grape jelly cups (sealable).  
   - *Theme Twist:* Serve in mini mason jars labeled "Pearls of the Deep."  
5. **Veggie "Cannonballs"**  
   - *Ingredients:* Carrot sticks, cucumber slices, bell pepper strips.  
   - *Theme Twist:* Serve in a cardboard "ship" container.  

---

### **Desserts**
1. **Treasure Chest Cupcakes**  
   - *Ingredients:* Cupcakes (vanilla or chocolate), buttercream frosting, sprinkles.  
   - *Theme Twist:* Top with pirate flags, treasure coins, or mini chocolate chips.  
2. **Graham Cracker "Shipwrecks"**  
   - *Ingredients:* Graham crackers, chocolate chips, marshmallows.  
   - *Assembly:* Stack crackers, add chocolate chips as "cargo," and marshmallows as "sails."  
3. **Sand Dollar Cookies**  
   - *Ingredients:* Store-bought or homemade cookies shaped like sand dollars.  

---

### **Drinks**
1. **"Pirate Punch" Juice Boxes**  
   - *Ingredients:* Individual 6-oz juice boxes (apple, orange, or berry).  
   - *Theme Twist:* Label with pirate names ("Captain Blackbeard Punch").  
2. **Water Bottles in "Ship" Cases**  
   - *Ingredients:* Reusable water bottles (blue/green colors), ice.  
   - *Theme Twist:* Decorate with paper sails or flags.  
3. **"Dew Drop" Lemonade Cups**  
   - *Ingredients:* Lemonade cups with lids (straw included).  

---

### **Sand-Safe Packaging & Extras**
- **Portion Control:** Use small containers or themed snack bags (e.g., pirate parrot bags).  
- **Allergy Note:** Double-check labels—**exclude watermelon** and substitute with grapes/strawberries.  
- **Theme Decor:** Include pirate parrot napkins, treasure maps for food labels, and a "pirate flag" ice bucket.  
- **Practical Tip:** Pack wet wipes and small brooms for quick sand cleanup!  

---

### **Why This Works**
- **Sand-Resistant:** All foods are bite-sized, sealed, or easy to grip.  
- **Pirate Immersion:** Every item ties into the theme (ships, treasure, flags).  
- **Kid-Friendly:** No choking hazards, easy to eat while running around.
