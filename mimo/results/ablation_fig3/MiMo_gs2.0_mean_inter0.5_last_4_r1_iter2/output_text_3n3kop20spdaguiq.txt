Of course. A dehydrator is a fantastic kitchen appliance that can create many different snacks. Here are some of the best things to dehydrate. First, fruits are the easiest to start with. You can dehydrate apples, bananas, mangoes, and peaches. You can also make dried strawberries and pineapple. Another great option is making fruit leather. You can take all your dried fruit, blend it with a little bit of honey or sugar, and then pour it onto a dehydrator tray. It will come out in nice, chewy sheets. You can also make dried fruit and nut clusters. Just take some dried fruit, chopped nuts, and coconut flakes, mix them together, press them into a tray, and then dehydrate it. Another popular snack is jerky. You can make your own beef, turkey, or even salmon jerky. You just need to marinate the meat, slice it thinly, and then dehydrate it. You can also make dried vegetables like carrots, onions, and zucchini. Just remember to cut them into thin strips or half-moons and dehydrate them. For a sweet treat, you can make date nuggets. You just take a date, put it in a bag with some chopped nuts and coconut, and then dehydrate it until it's chewy. You can even make your own dried herbs and spices like basil, oregano, and thyme. Just remember to dehydrate them at a lower temperature than you would for fruits or meats. Finally, you can make fruit gummies. You just take some fruit juice, a little bit of gelatin, and some fruit puree, mix them together, pour it into a tray, and dehydrate it.
