Okay, efficiency can mean different things. If we're talking about the highest energy output per dollar or per square meter, solar is leading the pack. It's very efficient at capturing sunlight. But if you mean the most reliable and consistent source, then hydropower takes the crown because it's always on. Wind power is somewhere in the middle—it's efficient in windy areas but can be intermittent. So, it really depends on how you define 'efficient' and what the specific needs of the area are.
