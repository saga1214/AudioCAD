### 🥣 Quick & Simple Ingredients:
1. **Pantry Staples**:  
   - **Rice/pasta** + canned beans/lentils (cook once, reuse for bowls/stir-fries).  
   - **Frozen veggies** (peppers, spinach, broccoli) — no chopping needed.  
   - **Canned tomatoes** or **coconut milk** for quick sauces.  

2. **Proteins**:  
   - **Eggs** (scrambled, boiled, or fried).  
   - **Pre-cooked tofu/chicken** (from the freezer section).  
   - **Tuna/salmon** (packed in water; add to salads or wraps).  

3. **Seasonings**:  
   - **Soy sauce**, **garlic powder**, **spice blends** (like curry or taco seasoning).  
   - Fresh herbs (cilantro, parsley) for quick garnish.  

### 🍳 Sample Meals (15-20 mins):
- **One-Pan Stir-Fry**:  
  Sauté frozen veggies + pre-cooked rice + tofu/chicken + soy-garlic sauce.  
- **Pasta Primavera**:  
  Toss cooked pasta with frozen mixed veggies + jarred pesto + cherry tomatoes.  
- **Breakfast-for-Dinner**:  
  Scrambled eggs + avocado + whole-grain toast.  

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Batch cook grains** (rice/quinoa) at the start of the week.  
- **Use pre-cut veggies** (salad kits) to skip prep.  
- **Keep a "clean-out" jar** in the fridge for using up leftovers creatively.  

> ⚠️ **Declutter Reminder**: If you’re simplifying, donate unused spices/gadgets now! Fewer ingredients = fewer decisions = faster meals.
