Okay, this is a big one, packing a whole Northern California beach vacation! You have a healthy budget, so we can definitely make this a really special trip for you and your family. Since swimming is the priority, I'm thinking the Big Sur area is perfect. It has warm ocean temperatures, lots of swimming spots, and just incredible scenery.

Here’s a detailed plan for a four or five-day trip. The best base for this would be to rent a house or an condo near Cambria or Morro Bay. That way, you have plenty of space and can all stay together.

For your first day, you could arrive in Cambria in the afternoon. I'd suggest checking into your rental and then heading to Morro Bay for dinner. A great place is The Shell House Restaurant; it's right on the water and has a lovely atmosphere. For an early start, you could go to the Madonna Inn for breakfast the next morning.

Day two is all about the coast! You can drive the scenic Pacific Coast Highway, stopping at places like Pfeiffer Beach for its famous purple sand and Big Creek Regional Park for some great swimming. For lunch, stop in San Simeon to try the famous clam chowder in a bread bowl at a place called Simeon's Restaurant. In the afternoon, you can visit Hearst Castle, which is a must-see. You'll need to book tickets for that ahead of time. You could have a nice dinner at the historic Casa Blanca Inn in San Simeon.

On day three, you can continue exploring. Stop at Davenport to see the historic Davenport Beach Hotel, and then maybe swim at Natural Bridges State Beach. For lunch, you could grab a classic California fast food burger at a place called The Wayfarers Restaurant. Later, you could visit the world-famous Hearst Castle again, or explore the beautiful Pfeiffer Beach more. For dinner, consider the Noyo Harbor Restaurant in Año Nuevo, which is right by the sea.

For day four, you could head to the Santa Cruz area. Stop at the Santa Cruz Wharf for lunch and maybe a ride on the historic carousel. In the afternoon, you can visit Steveston Village, which has great seafood, and then walk along Main Beach. For dinner, the Fisherman's Grotto is a fantastic choice with amazing ocean views.

On your last day, you could take a scenic drive down to Monterey. You could visit the world-renowned Monterey Bay Aquarium for lunch and then walk along the Cannery Row. A great dinner spot there is the Sardine Factory Restaurant. Afterwards, you can drive back home.

Just a few final tips: renting a large SUV or minivan would be your best bet for space. And since your daughter has Celiac, it's a good idea to call ahead to your rental properties and restaurants to make sure they can accommodate her needs. Have an amazing time!
