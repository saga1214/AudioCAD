Absolutely, for a Thanksgiving pie, a classic pumpkin pie is always a safe and delicious bet. It requires very little cooking equipment and just uses a simple can of pumpkin and a pie crust.

If you want to try something a bit different, you could make a sweet potato pie. It has a very similar taste and texture, but it's made with mashed sweet potatoes instead of pumpkin. It's a wonderful alternative that's still very traditional.
