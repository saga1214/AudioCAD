### **Simple (Easy to Prepare)**
1. **Matcha Popcorn**: Sweetened with white chocolate or savory with furikake seasoning.  
2. **Takoyaki Molds**: Pre-made batter for mini octopus balls (fun to cook while guests arrive!).  
3. **Yuzu-Marinated Fruit Skewers**: Citrusy yuzu juice (lemon-lime) with grapes, melon, and kiwi.  
4. **Dashi Soup Noodles**: Quick-cooking instant ramen topped with dashi broth, nori, and green onions.  
5. **Dried Seaweed Cups**: Nori sheets filled with sesame seeds, wasabi, or a mix of sweet potato chips.

---

### **Unique & Adventurous**
1. **Kimchi Seaweed Dip**: Fermented kimchi blended with nori seaweed—serve with tempura-style veggies or crispy wontons.  
2. **Takuan Pickles**: Pickled daikon radish with a sweet-salty kick (add a drizzle of yuzu marmalade for flair).  
2. **Yuzu-Glazed Sweet Potato Bites**: Roasted cubes glazed with yuzu ponzu and honey.  
3. **Mochi Skewers**: Chewy mochi balls skewered with salmon roe or grilled shrimp.  
4. **"Yakitori" Skewers**: Pre-marinated chicken thighs/tips (thawed from frozen) grilled in a wok.  
5. **Sweet & Spicy Tamarind Balls**: *Mochi* filled with tamarind paste (like Japanese *daifuku*).  

---

### **Interactive Stations**
- **DIY Matcha Lab**: Offer matcha powder, vanilla, sugar, and milk for guests to customize their own lattes.  
- **Tempura Bar**: Lightly battered veggies (bell peppers, zucchini, shrimp) fried in hot oil (use a deep fryer or double boiler).  
- **Sake & Umami Bar**: Small cups of sake, soy sauce, and wasabi for dipping snacks.  

---

### **Key Tips**  
- **Balance Flavors**: Pair spicy (kimchi, wasabi) with sweet (yuzu fruit, honey glaze).  
- **Texture Play**: Crunchy (tempura), chewy (mochi), crispy (seaweed cups), soft (popcorn).  
- **Presentation**: Serve on bamboo trays, washi paper, or in bento-style boxes. Garnish with edible flowers or nori flakes.
