Dallas has a great Asian food scene! For noodles, I’d recommend Yum Yum Noodle Bar for a more casual vibe, or The Rice Boat for a fancier, upscale experience. If you're looking for something with a bit of spice, try Pho Saigon for their pho-style noodles. For a more traditional Japanese option, check out Sushi Dai for fresh sushi and ramen.

As for protein bars, it depends on what you need. If you want something with a good balance of macros, Quest Bars are great. For a higher protein option, look into Optimum Nutrition or MyProtein. If you prefer a more natural, less sweet taste, think about Clif Bars or RXBAR. There’s also GoMacro for plant-based protein bars. Just make sure to read the labels to see what works best for your energy and digestion.
