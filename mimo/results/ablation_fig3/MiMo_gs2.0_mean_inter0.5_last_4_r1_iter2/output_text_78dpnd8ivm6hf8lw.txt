Based on our car analogy for the human circulatory system, here's a quick recap of the key organs:

1. **Heart** → **Gas Station & Engine**:  
   The heart is the power plant that pumps the blood-cars. It also acts as the gas station where the lungs deliver fresh oxygen "fuel" to the blood.

2. **Lungs** → **Fuel Refinery**:  
   The lungs provide the oxygen "fuel" that the blood-cars pick up at the heart's gas station. They turn air into breathable energy.

3. **Kidneys** → **Car Wash & Drain**:  
   The kidneys are like super-efficient car washes. They filter out waste ("junk") and extra water from the blood-cars, cleaning them up. The waste drains away as urine.

4. **Liver** → **All-in-One Service Station**:  
   The liver acts as a sorting and processing center. It breaks down toxins ("harmful stuff"), stores useful nutrients ("energy reserves"), and prepares everything to keep the blood-cars running smoothly.

**Simple Summary**:  
- **Heart**: Pumps the cars.  
- **Lungs**: Fill cars with oxygen fuel.  
- **Arteries**: Big highways carrying blood *away* from the heart.  
- **Capillaries**: Tiny roads where fuel/waste get exchanged.  
- **Veins**: Return roads taking blood *back* to the heart.  
- **Kidneys**: Clean the cars by washing out waste.  
- **Liver**: Repairs and refuels the cars with nutrients.  

This keeps the whole system fun, logical, and easy for kids to visualize! 🚗💨
