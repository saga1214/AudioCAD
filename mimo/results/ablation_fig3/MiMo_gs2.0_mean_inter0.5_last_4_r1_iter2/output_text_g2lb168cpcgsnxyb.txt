You're raising some very important and valid safety concerns.

While building that connection is incredibly important, given the aggressive elopement, the aggression itself, and the unsafe environment, the single most important approach in those first 20 to 30 minutes now becomes establishing safety and structure.

This means you should inform your supervisor immediately. You need to clearly document the lack of supervision and the environmental hazards. This is critical for both the client's safety and for making sure your services are covered properly.

At the same time, you can start with safety. This might mean using a visual cue to ask him to stay in the room, or using music to calm him before any structured work begins. The goal is to create a predictable and safe space before you even think about teaching.
