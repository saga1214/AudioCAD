Okay, to help you avoid these forever chemicals, let's focus on the top products to steer clear of.

First, definitely non-stick cookware. Try switching to stainless steel or cast iron instead.

Also, avoid grease-resistant food packaging, like what you find on fast-food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags.

Another one is anything labeled stain-resistant or waterproof, such as carpets, furniture, and some outdoor clothing.

You might also check certain cosmetics.

Since these can get into tap water, using a certified water filter can really help.
