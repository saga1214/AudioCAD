Okay, so you're at the final stage before priming. Let me double-check your work to make sure you're all set. 

First, make sure all the doors are completely sanded down and there are no rough spots or dust left. A quick wipe with a tack cloth will help.

Next, just do a quick visual check to make sure all the screws and hinges are back in place and nothing's loose.

And most importantly, make sure the whole piece is bone dry before you start applying the primer. Any moisture will ruin it. 

Once you've done that, you're ready to prime. Just remember to use a primer specifically for painting over dark colors. You'll probably need two coats. Good luck!
