**Regarding the glue gun:** Don't worry, it happens! Here are some quick troubleshooting tips while you wait for it to heat up:

1.  **Check the Cord:** Is the cord plugged firmly into the wall outlet? Try plugging it into a different outlet to rule out a bad wall socket.
2.  **Check the Plug:** Ensure the plug fits securely into the wall socket. Sometimes they can be slightly loose.
3.  **Check the Tip:** Look at the tip. Is it clogged with dried glue? A very fine needle (like from a sewing kit) or a toothpick dipped in rubbing alcohol can gently poke out any blockages. Don't force it.
4.  **Wait Patiently:** Glue guns often need a few minutes of steady heat to fully melt and flow. Don't give up after 30 seconds. Check back every minute or so.
5.  **Try a Different Tip (if possible):** Sometimes a wider tip melts faster than a very fine one.
6.  **Check the Power Source:** Is the room cold? Cold air can sometimes affect how quickly the glue gun heats up. Moving it to a warmer spot might help.
