Well, considering the practical realities, I'd have to say no, Mesopotamia is the better choice. The key issue is artifact availability. Major Egyptian artifacts are almost always loaned out or protected, making an exhibit with a sarcophagi difficult. Mesopotamian artifacts, while still significant, are often more accessible. This means an Egypt exhibit would likely just feature a few famous items, whereas a Mesopotamia exhibit could tell a much richer and more complete story about the dawn of civilization, making it a more compelling and achievable project.
