Here are quick, elegant recipes for both cocktails—perfect for your party’s vibe:

### 1. **Vodka Cocktail: "Cucumber-Mint Mule"**  
*(Refreshing, non-carbonated-friendly, works for all diets)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- ¾ oz ginger syrup (simmer 1 cup sugar + 1 cup water + 1 cup sliced ginger for 10 mins; strain)  
- 4-5 mint leaves + ½ cucumber (thinly sliced)  
- Top with **ginger beer** (soda allergy safe)  
- Garnish: Lime wheel, mint sprig  

**Quick Method:**  
Muddle mint + cucumber in a shaker. Add vodka, lime, ginger syrup, and ice. Shake well. Strain into a copper mug or highball glass. Top with ginger beer. Serve immediately.  

---

### 2. **Argentinian-Style Fernet: "Fernet & Cola with Citrus Twist"**  
*(Bold, aromatic—traditional pairing with soda)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 4 oz cola (or any cola brand)  
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz orange juice (optional, for brightness)  
- Garnish: Lemon twist  

**Quick Method:**  
Shake Fernet, cola, lemon juice, and orange juice (if using) with ice. Strain into a chilled rocks glass. Express a lemon twist over the drink and drop it in.  

---

### Pro Tips:  
- **Soda Allergy?** Both cocktails use ginger beer (non-soda) and cola (not carbonated like soda).  
- **Mocktail Option:** For non-drinkers, serve the **Cucumber-Mint Mule** without vodka, or a **Fernet mocktail** with soda water, lime, and mint.  
- **Formality:** Use premium ginger beer (e.g., Fever-Tree) and chilled copper mugs for the Vodka drink. For Fernet, serve in a crystal rocks glass.
