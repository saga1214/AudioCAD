To tune your stringed instrument (like a guitar, ukulele, or mandolin) while waiting, here's how to do it:  

### 1. **Use a Tuner App**  
   - **Download an app** like *GuitarTuna*, *Fender Tune*, or *Tuner*.  
   - **Place your phone near the instrument** (or clip it to a strap).  
   - **Pluck a string** and follow the app’s visual/audio cues to adjust the tuning.  

### 2. **Use an Electronic Tuner**  
   - **Clip the tuner** to your instrument.  
   - **Pluck a string**—the tuner will show the note’s pitch (e.g., "A," "E," "G").  
   - **Adjust the tuning peg** until the tuner flashes green (or shows the correct note).  

### 3. **Standard Tuning Reference**  
   - **Guitar (Standard Tuning):** E-A-D-G-B-E  
   - **Ukulele:** G-C-E-A  
   - **Mandolin:** G-D-G-D (tuned in octaves)  

### 4. **Key Tips**  
   - Start with the **thickest string** (low E on guitar) and work to the thinnest.  
   - **Turn the pegs slowly**—small adjustments make a big difference.  
   - **Listen for pitch changes**: If the tuner’s note gets sharper (higher pitch), tighten the string; if flatter (lower pitch), loosen it.  
   - **Retune often**: Tuning can drift as you wait.  

### 5. **Pro Tip**  
   If you’re unsure about the tuning order, search for "[your instrument] tuning chart" online for a quick reference!  

While you tune, you can also practice a simple scale or warm-up to pass the time. 🎸
