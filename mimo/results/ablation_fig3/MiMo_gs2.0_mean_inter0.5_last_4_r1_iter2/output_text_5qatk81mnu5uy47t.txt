Absolutely! Based on your scuba diving passion, dream trip to Costa Rica, past experiences in Honduras and Belize, and your debate about fins, here’s a tailored plan for an unforgettable Costa Rican dive adventure:

### **1. Fin Choice Recommendation: Start with Short Fins**
   - **Why?** As beginners, short fins are easier to maneuver, less tiring, and more travel-friendly. They’re ideal for calm waters, coral reefs, and exploring shipwrecks. Long fins require more leg strength and are better for strong currents or long-distance swimming.
   - **Costa Rica Tip:** Most dive sites here have calm, protected waters—short fins will work perfectly for 90% of sites.

### **2. Best Dive Locations in Costa Rica:**
   - **Pacific Coast (Best for Wrecks & Big Fish):**
     - **Cocos Island:** Famous for shipwrecks (like the *Captain Cook*), mantas, sharks, and hammerheads. Requires a boat trip (1.5–2 hours from Santa Teresa).
     - **Puerto Viejo:** Calmer waters with coral gardens, turtles, and reef sharks.
   - **Caribbean Coast (Best for Reefs & Coral):**
     - **Tortuguero:** Mangrove forests, sea turtles, and shallow reefs. Easy access by boat or kayak.
     - **Drake Bay:** Remote, pristine reefs with abundant marine life (seahorses, rays, and tropical fish).
   - **Golfo Dulce (Best for Night Dives & Whale Sharks):**
     - **Cahuita:** Protected marine park with vibrant coral, lobster, and reef sharks. Easy access from Limón.

### **3. Itinerary Suggestion (5–7 Days):**
   - **Days 1–2:** Arrive in **Limón** (Caribbean) or **Santa Teresa** (Pacific). Acclimate and take a freediving/snorkeling day.
   - **Days 3–4:** Boat dive to **Cahuita** (Caribbean) or **Cocos Island** (Pacific). Focus on wrecks, reefs, and marine biodiversity.
   - **Day 5:** **Night Dive** at **Cahuita** (spotting bioluminescent plankton or nocturnal creatures).
   - **Day 6:** Explore **Tortuguero** (Caribbean) or **Uvita** (Pacific) for turtle encounters.
   - **Day 7:** Relax in **Manuel Antonio** (beaches + snorkeling) or **Arenal** (volcanic landscapes).

### **4. Must-See Species & Sites:**
   - **Tortuguero (Caribbean):** Sea turtles (nesting season: June–November), spotted eagle rays.
   - **Cahuita (Caribbean):** Vibrant coral reefs, giant lobster, reef sharks.
   - **Cocos Island (Pacific):** Whale sharks (seasonal), hammerheads, pilot whales.
   - **Golfo Dulce (Pacific):** Night dives with bioluminescent plankton.

### **5. Pro Tips:**
   - **Guides:** Hire certified guides (PADI/SSI) for safety and local insights.
   - **Gear:** Pack short fins, a dive computer, reef-safe sunscreen, and a waterproof camera.
   - **Best Time:** Dry season (Dec–April) for calm waters and visibility.
   - **Bonus:** Combine diving with rainforest hikes (e.g., Corcovado National Park).

### **6. Alternative Idea: Tenerife, Canary Islands (Europe)**
   - If you want warm tropical waters without long travel, Tenerife offers:
     - **Cinco Viejas Lagoon:** Crystal-clear waters, sea turtles, and volcanic landscapes.
     - **Los Gigantes:** Dramatic cliffs, whale sharks, and coral reefs.
   - *Why?* Similar biodiversity to Belize/Honduras, but with European convenience.

### **Final Advice:**
Costa Rica’s diversity (reefs, wrecks, turtles, and landscapes) makes it a dream. Prioritize **short fins** for comfort, and focus on **Cahuita/Tortuguero** for Caribbean beauty or **Cocos Island** for Pacific adventure.
