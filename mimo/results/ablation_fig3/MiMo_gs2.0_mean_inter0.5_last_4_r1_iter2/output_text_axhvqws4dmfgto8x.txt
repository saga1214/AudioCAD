For the stir-fry, the noodle part is actually the easiest step. Just make sure you use metal utensils this time. The real key to great stir-fry noodles is the water-to-noodle ratio. Too much water and your noodles will be soggy, but too little and they'll be overcooked.

Here’s the trick: boil plenty of water, add the noodles, and stir them constantly. You want to cook them until they are just done, not mushy. Then, and this is important, drain them thoroughly. A colander works great for this. After draining, add a little oil to the colander to keep them from sticking together.

Then you just toss them back into the wok with your stir-fry sauce. You've got this, and definitely no wood in the pot!
