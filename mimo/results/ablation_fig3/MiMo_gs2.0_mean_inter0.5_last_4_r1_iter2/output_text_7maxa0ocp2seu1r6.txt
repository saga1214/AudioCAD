No problem! Here's a clear, categorized list of the essential measurements to take at every fitting, along with the additional ones for more fitted garments:

### **Core Body Measurements (Every Fitting)**
1.  **Bust:** Measure around the fullest part, under the arms.
2.  **Natural Waist:** Measure around the smallest part of the waist (not the hips!), usually just above the belly button.
3.  **Hips:** Measure around the fullest part of the hips, usually above the thickest part of the thighs.

### **Essential Measurements (For Most Garments)**
4.  **Inseam:** Measure from the inside of the thigh crease to the ankle bone (for pants/shorts).
5.  **Sleeve Length:** Measure from the center back neck point down the shoulder to the wrist bone (for tops, blouses, jackets).
6.  **Shoulder Width:** Measure across the shoulders, from the edge of one shoulder point to the edge of the other (crucial for jackets, shirts, blazers).

### **Key Additional Measurements (For Better Fitted Garments)**
7.  **Neck:** Measure around the neck at the base of the jaw, where the collar would sit.
8.  **Bicep:** Measure around the fullest part of the bicep muscle, about 2-4 inches below the shoulder point.
9.  **Upper Back (Shoulder to Shoulder):** Measure across the upper back, from the edge of one shoulder point to the edge of the other (for jackets, blazers, well-fitting shirts).
10. **Front Rise:** Measure from the natural waist point up to the crotch point (front of the pants).
11. **Back Rise:** Measure from the natural waist point down to the crotch point (back of the pants). *(Critical for tall clients like your neighbor!)*
12. **Hip Width:** Measure around the fullest part of the hips (often overlaps with hip measurement, but confirming can help).
13. **Thigh:** Measure around the fullest part of the thigh, about 4-6 inches below the hip point (essential for tailored pants/skirts).

### **For Your Neighbor's Pants**
*   **Keep the Inseam at 37 inches.** Have him try them on *with the shoes he'll wear* to pin the hem exactly where he wants it (likely slightly shorter than his ankle/heel measurement).
*   **Focus on the Back Rise:** Ensure it's long enough (a few inches above the crotch point) to prevent pulling down on his tall frame.
*   **Add Ease to Hips & Thighs:** Give him extra room in these areas for comfort when sitting, especially with sneakers on.

You've got this! Taking these measurements systematically will make your tailoring consistently great.
