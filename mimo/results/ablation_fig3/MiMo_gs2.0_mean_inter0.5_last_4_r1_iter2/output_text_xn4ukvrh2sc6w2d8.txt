Alright, here are four classic cocktail recipes with all the ingredients and measurements for your Art Deco airport reception in Kentucky.

First, the Old Fashioned: You'll need two ounces of bourbon or rye, one sugar cube, or half an ounce of simple syrup, three to four dashes of Angostura bitters, and an orange twist for garnish. You can also add an orange peel or a cherry. To make it, muddle the sugar cube with a few dashes of bitters in a mixing glass. Add the bourbon, fill the glass with ice, and stir it down until it's chilled. Strain it into a rocks glass over fresh ice and garnish with that orange twist.

Next, the Paper Plane: You'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of Aperol, half an ounce of Amaro Nonino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Shake it with ice, strain it into a coupe glass, and garnish with an orange peel or a cherry.

Then, the Whiskey Sour. For this, you'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. If you're using a raw egg white, you'll also need one egg white. You'll add the egg white, bourbon, lemon juice, and syrup to a shaker, dry shake it without ice to get it frothy, then shake with ice, and strain into a rocks glass. For garnish, use a cherry or an orange slice.

And for the French 75, you'll need an ounce of gin, half an ounce of fresh lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. Shake those three ingredients with ice, strain them into a champagne flute, and top it off with about two ounces of dry sparkling wine like Prosecco or Champagne. You can garnish it with a lemon twist or a maraschino cherry.
