Okay, you want a full breakdown of the resume format. Let's go through it step by step. 

First, at the very top, you have your name in about a 14 or 16-point font. Right below that, on the same line, you put your contact info: email, phone number, city, and state. It's better to use icons sparingly or not at all, and you should include a link to your LinkedIn profile. 

Next, you'll have a professional summary. This is a short paragraph, about three to five sentences, that highlights your most relevant skills and career goals. 

After that, you can have an optional skills section. Here, you'll list your key skills, maybe five to seven per job description, and try to use the same keywords as the job posting. 

Then comes the experience section. This is where you'll list your most relevant jobs first, followed by older ones in your "Additional Work History" section. For each job, you'll have the company name, your title, the dates you worked there, and then two to four bullet points describing your main accomplishments. 

If you have a lot of relevant older experience, it's a good idea to create a separate section for it, listing the jobs in reverse chronological order but without the bullet points. 

Your education section should come next, listing your highest degree first. You can include details like your graduation date and any honors or awards you received, such as a scholarship or graduating with highest honors. 

Then, you can have an optional section for other achievements, like publications or professional certifications. 

Finally, you can add an optional certifications section, where you list the name of the certifying organization and the date you received it. 

Just remember to keep it clean, use consistent formatting, and always tailor it to the specific job you're applying for. And don't forget to proofread it carefully before sending!
