Here's a simple recipe for a sandwich with artificial crab meat, keeping it vegetarian-friendly:

### Artificial Crab Meat Sandwich
**Ingredients:**
- 2 slices of bread (whole wheat, sourdough, or your choice)
- 2-3 tbsp mayonnaise or vegan mayo
- 1-2 tbsp artificial crab meat (canned or pre-cooked)
- 1-2 tbsp finely chopped celery
- 1-2 tbsp finely chopped pickles or relish (optional)
- 1-2 slices lettuce
- 1-2 slices tomato
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: sliced avocado, sprouts, or a drizzle of sriracha

**Instructions:**
1. **Prepare the filling:** Mix mayonnaise with chopped celery and pickles (if using) in a small bowl. Add the artificial crab meat and gently fold to combine.
2. **Assemble the sandwich:** Spread the crab mixture evenly on one slice of bread. Top with lettuce, tomato, and any extras.
3. **Add the second slice:** Place the second slice of bread on top and press lightly. Sprinkle with salt and pepper.
4. **Serve:** Cut in half and enjoy!

**Tips:**
- For extra flavor, lightly pan-fry the crab mixture before assembling.
- Use vegan mayo and plant-based bread for a fully vegan version.
- Serve with cucumber slices or a side salad.
