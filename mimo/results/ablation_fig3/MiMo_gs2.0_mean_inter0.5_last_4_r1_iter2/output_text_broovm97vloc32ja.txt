Yes, you can absolutely submit a completed project with both text and illustrations. In fact, for a children's book, that's often a huge advantage. The most important thing is to research publishers who are open to this. You can find them by looking at the submission guidelines on sites like Submittable or the Children's Book Board website. When you submit, you'll need to include a few things. First, a cover image showing the book's front and back. Second, a few interior spreads so they can see how your text and art work together. And finally, you'll submit the full manuscript, which includes your text and the specific rhyme scheme you mentioned. Good luck with your project.
