Okay, you've got the essentials down, but a few more things will make the job smoother and safer. I'd highly recommend having a stud finder that can also locate electrical wires. Habitat cabinets are heavy, and hitting a wire would be a disaster. Also, a measuring tape is crucial for spacing between cabinets and ensuring they're perfectly level. A drill with the right screws for drywall or wood is much faster and easier than using a hammer and nails. And most importantly, please have safety glasses on at all times, especially when you're lifting and holding the upper cabinets. You've got a good plan now, just be safe and enjoy the savings!
