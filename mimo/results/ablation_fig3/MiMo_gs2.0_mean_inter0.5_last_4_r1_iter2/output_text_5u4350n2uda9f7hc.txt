You've raised an excellent point about weight and portability! Since you're carrying plants up five flights of stairs daily, let's focus on plants that are **compact, lightweight, and easy to transport**:

### 🌿 Succulents (for your table)
*   **Weight:** Typically **very light**. Pots are small, soil is minimal, and the plants themselves are small and dry. A single succulent pot usually weighs **under 5 pounds** (2.3 kg), easily manageable in one hand.
*   **Transport:** Perfect for carrying up stairs. Choose small varieties like:
    *   **Haworthia:** Compact, rosette-shaped, silver/green variegation.
    *   **Echeveria:** Rosette-forming, comes in many colors (blue, pink, purple).
    *   **Sedum 'Autumn Joy' or 'Black Stonecrop':** Low-growing, succulent leaves, good for ground-level.
*   **Why they work:** Thrive in bright morning sun (east-facing), need very little water (ideal for short stays), and are perfect for small spaces.

### 🌱 Bird's Nest Snake Plant (for the floor)
*   **Weight:** Moderate. A small plant in a standard 4-6 inch pot might weigh **5-10 pounds** (2.3-4.5 kg). While heavier than a succulent, it's still **easily manageable in one hand** for a short stair climb. Look for lightweight plastic or unglazed terracotta pots.
*   **Transport:** Fits easily on your hands. The plant itself is sturdy and won't tip over.
*   **Appearance & Placement:** As described, it has **tall, upright, sword-like leaves growing in a tight, circular cluster from the soil**, resembling a bird's nest. It stays low and compact (usually 12-24 inches tall), making it ideal for the floor space.
*   **Why it works:** Loves bright indirect light (including morning sun), is extremely tolerant of NYC conditions (wind, city pollution), and is very low-maintenance during your month-long stay.

### Key Considerations for Transport & Setup

1.  **Pot Choice is Crucial:** Opt for **lightweight pots** (plastic, unglazed terracotta, or lightweight glazed ceramic) when buying. Avoid heavy ceramic or large glazed pots.
2.  **Size Matters:** Stick to **small plants** in **small pots** (4-6 inch diameter is plenty). You want something that fits easily on your small table or the floor.
3.  **Dry Soil is Best:** **Water them well** before you leave for your on-site, but **let the soil dry out completely** before your return. Wet soil adds unnecessary weight and risk of root rot during transport. You can lightly mist leaves during your stay if needed.
4.  **Sturdy Pots:** Ensure the pot has a base that won't slip on the balcony surface (e.g., a saucer or textured bottom).
5.  **Plan Ahead:** Pack the plants separately from your main luggage. Place them on the floor of your car if possible.

### Recommendation Summary

*   **For the Table:** Get **1-2 small succulents** (like Haworthia or Echeveria) in lightweight pots. They'll add color and texture.
*   **For the Floor:** Get **one Bird's Nest Snake Plant** in a lightweight pot. It provides structure and a unique look.
*   **Total Weight:** You should be able to carry both plants easily up five flights. The succulents are very light; the snake plant is manageable in one hand.

These choices perfectly match your east-facing balcony light, NYC conditions, small space, and the need for easy transport. They'll make your Airbnb feel much cozier! 🌸
