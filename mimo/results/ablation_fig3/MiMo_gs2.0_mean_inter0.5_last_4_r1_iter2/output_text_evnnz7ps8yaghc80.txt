Here’s a balanced weekly practice schedule designed to build skills efficiently while preventing stress buildup. It incorporates both beanbags and scarves, with flexibility for rest days.

**Weekly Practice Schedule:**

*   **Monday:** **Focus: Two-Beanbag Mastery & Three-Beanbag Transition**
    *   *Warm-up:* 5 minutes of two-beanbag juggling in one dominant hand.
    *   *Transition Practice:* 15 minutes practicing the "first three throws" method we discussed.
    *   *Music Session:* 10 minutes juggling two or three beanbags to music with your dominant hand.

*   **Tuesday:** **Focus: Rest or Active Recovery**
    *   *Option 1 (Active):* Light stretching or a short walk focusing on hand-eye coordination.
    *   *Option 2 (Rest):* Complete rest. Stress reduction day! Do an activity you enjoy that isn't learning-related.

*   **Wednesday:** **Focus: Three-Beanbag Consistency**
    *   *Warm-up:* 10 minutes juggling two beanbags in one hand.
    *   *Practice:* 20 minutes focused on juggling three beanbags. Aim for clean throws, consistent rhythm, and longer sequences. Don't panic if you drop! Just restart calmly.
    *   *Cool-down:* 5 minutes of gentle wrist circles.

*   **Thursday:** **Focus: Scarf Practice & Rhythm**
    *   *Warm-up:* 10 minutes of slow, deliberate two-beanbag juggling.
    *   *Scarf Practice:* 20 minutes working with one or two scarves. Focus on the slow-motion feel, the pattern, and gentle catches. Experiment with different rhythms.
    *   *Optional:* 10 minutes of beanbag juggling to music.

*   **Friday:** **Focus: Mixed Practice & Review**
    *   *Warm-up:* 10 minutes one-hand two-beanbag juggling.

*   **Sunday:** **Focus: Complete Rest & Family Time**
    *   Take a full rest day. Focus on fulfilling your promise to your son by observing practice or talking about your journey! This reduces pressure and builds excitement.

**Key Tips for Success:**

1.  **Short & Consistent:** Practice in short, focused bursts (like the 15-20 mins on weekdays) is far more effective than long, frustrated sessions.
2.  **Lower Expectations:** Your goal each session is "not to panic" and "to catch once." Celebrate tiny wins!
3.  **Listen to Stress:** If you feel panic rising mid-session, STOP. Do a different activity for 5 minutes, take deep breaths, or skip the session. Come back fresh tomorrow.
4.  **Be Kind to Yourself:** Learning three things is hard. You mastered two, that's a huge step. The transition takes time for almost everyone.
5.  **Focus on Fun:** Remember why you started – to teach your son! Connecting practice to that positive goal can boost motivation.
6.  **Modify:** This is a template! If you need more transition time on Tuesday, add it. If Saturday feels too long, shorten it. Do what works for you.

This schedule builds skills systematically, incorporates different tools for varied practice, and prioritizes managing stress – exactly what you identified as a hurdle. Trust the process, be patient, and enjoy the journey! You absolutely can do this.
