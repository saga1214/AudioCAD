That vegan friend might want to avoid meat. Police work can be irregular schedules, software devs might eat anything for convenience. Food pantry work might mean they're very mindful of food waste or serving others. My best advice is to keep it simple and safe. Stick to the vegan option to be sure. You could do a cheeseburger for the developer, a veggie burger for the vegan, and a regular hamburger for the others. That covers all the bases without any awkwardness.
