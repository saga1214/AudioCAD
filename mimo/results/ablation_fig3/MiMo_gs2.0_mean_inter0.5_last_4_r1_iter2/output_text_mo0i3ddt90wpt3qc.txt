You already mentioned SkinnyPop and Popchips, which are gluten-free. Along those lines, rice cakes are a simple gluten-free snack that you can even top with gluten-free nut butter.

Another great option is trail mix, which you can make by combining gluten-free nuts like almonds and cashews with gluten-free seeds like pumpkin and sunflower seeds. 

And for something sweet and safe, gluten-free energy bars from a brand like Enjoy Life would work perfectly.
