That's wonderful! Combining Calgary's adventure spirit with nearby escapes creates perfect weekend getaways. Here are some fantastic options, balancing romance, scenery, and accessibility while avoiding triggers like crowds, tight spaces, or water:

### 🌲 Iconic Mountain Escapes (1-2 hours from Calgary)
1. **Banff National Park**  
   - **Lake Louise**: Romantic lakeside dining at the Fairmont Chateau, sunset walks on the lakeshore, and the iconic gondola ride (open air, scenic views).  
   - **Johnston Canyon**: Easy, paved hike to waterfalls (stunning views, less crowded than Moraine Lake).  
   - **Banff Gondola**: Sweeping views of the Bow Valley—romantic and low-effort adventure.

2. **Moraine Lake**:  
   - Turquoise waters and epic vistas. *Note: Shuttle system (book ahead) and timed entry permits required.*  
   - **Why it’s great**: Serene, photogenic, and feels like a private paradise. Ideal for a quiet, intimate picnic.

### 🍂 Hidden Gems & Scenic Drives
3. **Kananaskis Valley (West of Calgary)**:  
   - **Kananaskis Lakes**: Crystal-clear turquoise waters, great for swimming, kayaking (if she’s comfortable), or picnics.  
   - **Maligne Canyon**: Dramatic red rock formations—easy trails and overlooks.  
   - **Bonus**: Stop at **Strathmore Winery** for wine tasting en route.

4. **Canmore**:  
   - **Prairie Mountain Geopark**: Unique geology, easy trails, and cozy mountain lodges.  
   - **Rocky Mountain Gardens**: Stunning floral displays (seasonal).  
   - **Music Note**: Canmore often has live music at local pubs!

### 🌟 Unique & Romantic Experiences
5. **Emerald Lake (Banff)**:  
   - A short drive from Lake Louise. Hike to the top for emerald waters and alpine views.  
   - **Romantic Touch**: Pack a picnic with local cheeses and wine.

6. **Stargazing in the Dark Sky Preserve (Canmore/Banff)**:  
   - **Why it’s great**: Magical for couples. Check out events at **Dark Sky Festival** or guided tours.  
   - *Note: Avoid crowded summer events if hydrophobia is a concern—opt for early evening or private tours.*

### ⚠️ Key Considerations
- **Book Ahead**: Banff/Moraine Lake require reservations (parking, gondola, tours).  
- **Avoid Crowds**: Weekdays or early mornings are quieter.  
- **Accessibility**: Banff Gondola, Johnston Canyon, and Moraine Lake’s boardwalk are wheelchair-friendly.  
- **Phobia-Sensitive**: Stick to open spaces, avoid tunnels/caves, and ensure trails aren’t crowded.
