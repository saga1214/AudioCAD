Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time would really depend on the region you're visiting. For the most vibrant fall colors, you’d want to head to the southern states like Chiapas or Oaxaca around October or November. That’s when the foliage is at its peak. 

But, if you’re also interested in watercolor techniques, maybe a coastal area like Cancun or Tulum could be good. You could paint the ocean and the local architecture, plus you'd get some beautiful autumn hues from the tropical plants. Just, you know, keep in mind the weather might be a bit different there. 

So yeah, either way, Mexico is a great place to get that inspiration and practice your new techniques!
