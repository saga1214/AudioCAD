That sounds like an awesome winter adventure! Here are some great winter activities to try, building on your interests:  

### 🎣 **Ice Fishing Upgrades**  
- **Fish finders**: Help locate fish under the ice.  
- **Warm gear**: Insulated boots, a thermal sleeping pad, and a heated vest keep you comfortable.  
- **Try different techniques**: Drilling multiple holes, using sonar, or targeting specific fish like walleye or crappies.  

### 🚶 **Snowshoeing Essentials**  
- **Rent or buy**: Start with beginner-friendly frames and lightweight boots (e.g., Merrell or Altra).  
- **Safety gear**: Bright colors, a headlamp, and a map/GPS (snow can obscure landmarks).  
- **Local trails**: Check parks or groomed trails near you—many have rentals!  

### ❄️ **Other Winter Adventures**  
1. **Cross-country skiing**: Low-impact and great for trails.  
2. **Dog sledding**: Try a guided mush tour (Wisconsin has great outfitters!).  
3. **Winter hiking**: Explore snowy forests with microspikes for traction.  
4. **Ice climbing**: Requires training, but local clubs often host clinics.  
5. **Snowboarding/Skiing**: If you have access to hills, or try indoor slopes!  

### 🍁 **Maple Syrup Tapping (Wisconsin-style!)**  
- **Tools**: Use a tap (spile) that fits your tree’s diameter (e.g., 1.5" for smaller trees).  
- **Timing**: Tap in late winter when temps dip below freezing at night.  
- **Collection**: Hang a bucket below the tap—watch the sap drip!  
- **Boil it down**: Simmer sap in a large pot until syrupy (about 1:3 sap to syrup).  

### 💡 **Pro Tip**  
Bundle in layers! Moisture-wicking base layers + insulated mid-layers + wind/waterproof outer shell = stay warm and dry.
