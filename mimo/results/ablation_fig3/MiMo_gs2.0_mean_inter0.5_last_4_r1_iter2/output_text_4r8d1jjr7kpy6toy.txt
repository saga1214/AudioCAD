Okay, so you're craving barbecue, which we already established, but for dinner tonight, you want restaurant suggestions that fit your high-protein, lower-carb lifestyle. Since you mentioned craving barbecue earlier, a good place to start would be a barbecue restaurant. Many of them have grilled meats like chicken or steak, which are high in protein and you can easily get them with side salads or steamed veggies. That would be a great fit for your diet. 

Another option could be a steakhouse. They typically have lean cuts of beef, which are super high in protein, and you can order with minimal carbs, like just a side salad or some roasted potatoes with the skin on. 

If you're in the mood for Italian, you could go to an Italian place that offers grilled chicken or a bolognese, which is usually made with lean ground beef, and ask for it to be served with a side of zucchini noodles or just a simple green salad. 

A sushi place could also work, especially if you get rolls with tuna, salmon, or shrimp. You can always ask for no rice or extra veggies to keep it low-carb. 

Finally, a Thai or Vietnamese restaurant might have options like grilled chicken or tofu curry, or even a stir-fry with lots of veggies and lean protein. You'd just have to be careful with the sauces, but you can usually ask for them to be made without sugar or soy sauce to keep it low-carb. 

Just remember to communicate with the restaurant staff about your dietary preferences so they can accommodate you. Enjoy your dinner!
