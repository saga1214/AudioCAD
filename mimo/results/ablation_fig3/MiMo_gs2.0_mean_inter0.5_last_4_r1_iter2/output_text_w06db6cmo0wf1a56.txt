Here's a practical, low-mess shopping list based on your group size (10 dads + 10 kids) and theme park goals, focusing on grab-and-go items for coolers:

### Drinks (Mix & Match)
*   **Water Bottles:** 20 Large bottles (1 per person + extras)
*   **Sugar-Free Sodas:** 15 cans (divided among dads/kids - check labels for low sugar)
*   **Low-Sugar Juice Boxes:** 15 boxes (for kids who prefer juice)
*   **Sparkling Water (Sugar-Free):** 15 cans or 12-pack (great adult/kid option)
*   **Ice Packs:** Several large ones to keep everything cold
*   **Optional:** Sports drinks (small boxes/bottles) for extra hydration

### Snacks (Quick, Minimal Prep)
*   **Cheese Sticks:** 20 individual packs (divided evenly)
*   **Single-Serve Hummus Cups:** 10 cups (pair with veggies)
*   **Baby Carrots:** 1 large bag (easy for kids to eat)
*   **Individual Pretzel Snacks:** 10 small bags (Pretzels, Cheese, Cheddar)
*   **Trail Mix:** 10 individual bags (no nuts if needed)
*   **Grapes:** 1 large container (pre-washed & ready)
*   **Apple Slices:** 1 large container (tossed in lemon juice to prevent browning)
*   **Granola Bars:** 10-15 individual packs (various kid/adult options)
*   **Energy/Gum Chews:** 10 packs (quick pick-me-up)

### Main Meals (Portable & Satisfying)
*   **Calzones (Pre-Made):** 10-12 (Look for: Chicken & Cheese, Veggie, Pepperoni - check labels for less oil/salt if desired). *Order from a pizza place specializing in calzones or a local bakery.*
*   **Sandwiches/Wraps:** 10-12 (Pre-made individual wraps/sandwiches - Turkey & Cheese, Ham & Swiss, Veggie Hummus). *Include cutlery!*
*   **Optional Main:** Hummus & Veggie Cups (if not enough calzones/wraps)
*   **Optional Main:** Mini Pizzas (if calzones aren't ideal - order from a pizza place)

### Dessert (Minimal Cleanup)
*   **Cupcakes:** 10-12 (Check for nut-free options if needed). *Pre-cut or have easy-to-grab handles.*
*   **OR Individual Pies:** 4 small individual pies (like Lemon Meringue or Apple Pie) - *ensure easy to eat one-handed.*
*   **OR Individual Brownie Cups:** 10-12 (Easy grab, no knife needed).

### Organization Tips
1.  **Label Coolers:** Use tape and markers to label sections (e.g., "Drinks," "Snacks," "Main," "Dessert").
2.  **Ice Management:** Pack ice packs *with* food containers, not loose. Use a cooler liner if possible.
3.  **Portion Control:** Pre-portion snacks into individual bags/containers. Cut sandwiches/wraps in half if needed.
4.  **Utensils:** Include a small bag of forks and napkins per person.
5.  **Allergy Note:** If any known allergies (dairy, nuts, gluten), mark those items clearly or leave them out.
6.  **Extras:** Pack a few bags of wet wipes and hand sanitizer for everyone!

This list gives you a great variety, minimizes cleanup, and caters to both dads and kids with the calzones and sugar-free sodas. Have an absolutely fantastic party!
