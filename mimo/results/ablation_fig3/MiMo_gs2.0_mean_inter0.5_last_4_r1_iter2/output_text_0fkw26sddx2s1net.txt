Alright, here is a detailed shopping list for your husband’s allergy-friendly Asian-inspired dinner. It excludes legumes and seafood and includes homemade dessert options. Let's break it down.

First, for the starter. You'll need miso paste, preferably dashi-free or vegetarian miso if you can find it. For cucumber salad, grab one English cucumber, some rice vinegar, sesame oil, soy sauce, sugar, and a pinch of salt. You'll also need a small head of garlic and a couple of green onions.

Next, for the main course, Chicken Teriyaki. You'll need about a pound of boneless, skinless chicken breasts, soy sauce, mirin, sake, and brown sugar for the glaze. For stir-fried vegetables, you can use broccoli, bell peppers, snap peas, and carrots. You'll also need about a tablespoon of sesame oil, minced garlic, ginger, and some vegetable oil. And of course, jasmine rice—about a bag of pre-cooked or instant rice works just fine.

Finally, for dessert, if you're making the homemade Mango Sticky Rice, you'll need four ripe but firm mangoes, some coconut milk, sugar, and salt. For the Matcha Ice Cream, you'll need about 100 grams of unsweetened coconut cream, 50 grams of condensed milk, one tablespoon of matcha powder, and a teaspoon of vanilla extract. And for toppings, maybe some toasted coconut flakes or extra matcha powder.

A few extra things you might need are cooking oil, salt, pepper, and maybe some chopsticks for serving. Enjoy your special meal!
