Okay, for the chicks, let's focus on a bedding that's rodent-proof and safe. My top recommendation is to use a thick layer of wood shavings that are specifically labeled for pets. They are less likely to attract rodents and provide good insulation.

Another great alternative is to use pine cones. You can just toss a few in their little area to help absorb moisture and provide a bit of insulation. It's a natural option that doesn't attract pests.
