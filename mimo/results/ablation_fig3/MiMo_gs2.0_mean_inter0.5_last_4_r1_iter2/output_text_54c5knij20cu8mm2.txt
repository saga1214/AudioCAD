Okay, let's break this down.

First, about the left-brain versus right-brain idea. It's a myth, but it's persistent. The brain doesn't really split tasks that way. Both sides work together, and creativity and logic happen in both hemispheres. But, some people do tend to have slightly different emphases—some are more analytical, others more intuitive. It's not a hard rule, though. 

Now, for careers that are creative, objective, and have lasting value, here are a few ideas. You could look into architecture or engineering design, which are very structured but also creative. Or maybe data visualization, which turns complex data into beautiful, understandable art. Another option is UX/UI design, which is all about making digital products both functional and delightful to use. There's also scientific illustration, where you'd combine scientific knowledge with artistic skill. 

As for that buzzing in your ear, tinnitus is usually physiological, like from noise exposure or aging. But it can also be influenced by stress. If it’s persistent, it’s a good idea to see a specialist to rule out other issues. 

And regarding your writing quirks, it’s likely a combination of factors. Maybe it’s a motor skill thing, or just a habit. If it’s causing problems, occupational therapy could help. 

I hope that gives you some ideas to think about.
