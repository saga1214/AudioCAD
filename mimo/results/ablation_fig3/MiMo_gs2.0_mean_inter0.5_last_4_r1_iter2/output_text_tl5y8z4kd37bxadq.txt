Great question! For a laid-back Austin dinner, I’d recommend **Tarrytown** (off South Congress). It’s got that cozy, unpretentious vibe with Southern-inspired dishes, friendly service, and a small-batch cocktail menu. Perfect if you’re craving comfort food without the fuss.  

**Step-by-step breakdown for your first night:**  
1. **Timing**: Aim for 7:00–7:30 PM (avoid weekend crowds).  
2. **Dress**: Casual—jeans + tee are fine.  
3. **Menu**: Try the smoked brisket tacos or pecan-crusted chicken.  
4. **Ambiance**: Outdoor patio with live acoustic music (weekends).  

**Bonus tip**: Walk next door to **Home Slice Pizza** for post-dinner slices—affordable and iconic!
