Okay, so you're thinking about combining a van adventure with a Vegas trip. That's a smart move—it gives you the best of both worlds: adventure time in the desert, then a proper city escape. Here’s how you could structure it to make the most of both.

First, the van adventure in Joshua Tree. Spend a few days there—maybe two to three—exploring the park, hiking, and just soaking in the desert vibes. You can find free dispersed camping on BLM land, which is perfect for van life. The key is to embrace the solitude and nature. Then, when you’re ready to switch things up, you can drive the van down to Vegas. It’s about a three-hour drive, so it’s not too far.

Once you get to Vegas, you can park the van somewhere like the Strip, or maybe off-Strip if you want to save money. Then, you can enjoy the city’s offerings. If you want a proper hotel experience, you can book a room at a nice casino—maybe the Bellagio, the Venetian, or the Wynn. They all have great pools and spas. You can definitely get a nice hot shower and relax after a few days of van camping.

Beyond the hotel, you can still explore Vegas in a unique way. Check out the art installations at the Neon Museum, or take a day trip to Red Rock Canyon, which is just outside the city. If you’re into music, you can catch a show at the MGM Grand or the Cosmopolitan. The key is to balance the adventure time with the city time, so you get the best of both worlds. It’s all about making the most of your time in each place before you head back to the van for another adventure.
