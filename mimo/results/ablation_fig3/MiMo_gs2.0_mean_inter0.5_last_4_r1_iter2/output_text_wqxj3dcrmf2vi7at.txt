Here's a full recap of treat ideas tailored for Princess the corgi princess, plus training tips:

### 🧁 Homemade Treats (All dog-safe)
1. **Pumpkin/Sweet Potato Bites**: Mashed with a splash of water, baked flat, then frozen in a silicone tray.  
2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**: Mix PB (no xylitol!) with rolled oats, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15 mins.  
3. **Carrot Sticks**: Raw, steamed, or lightly steamed carrot slices (peeled if raw).  
4. **Apple Chunks**: Core/slice apples, de-seed, and freeze into small cubes.  
5. **Zucchini Bites**: Grated zucchini mixed with egg, baked into small rounds.  

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands (Check Ingredients!)  
- **Natural**: Blue Buffalo, Wellness, Zuke’s Nibbles  
- **Training**: Greenies (for dental health), freeze-dried liver from Healthy Edibles  
- **Novelty**: Doggy Biscuits by The Honest Kitchen (single-ingredient)  

### 🐾 Training Tips for Princess  
- **Sit Command**: Use a high-value treat (peanut butter!) near her nose → raise toward her "sit" → reward.  
- **Potty Training**:  
  - Take her out *after* waking, meals, play, or naps.  
  - Say "go potty" consistently. Reward *immediately* when she goes outside.  
  - Clean accidents with enzymatic cleaner (no scents left!).  
- **Barking at Felix**:  
  - Teach "quiet" with a treat lure → pause → reward when she stops.  
  - Redirect with a toy or command like "leave it."  

### 💖 Collar Colors for Brown & White  
- **Bold**: Turquoise, bright pink, or royal blue (contrasts beautifully).  
- **Earthy**: Sage green, mustard yellow, or terracotta.  
- **Classic**: Black or charcoal (dramatic against her coat).  

**Pro Tip**: Always introduce new treats gradually and watch for allergies! Princess’s tiny tummy comes first. 🐾💕
