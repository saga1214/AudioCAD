Based on your need to stick something to a jacket lining without damage, here are the best methods:

1.  **Fabric Glue (Best Option):**
    *   **Why:** Specifically designed for textiles. It bonds well to fabrics and linings without damaging synthetic materials common in jackets.
    *   **How:** Apply a small dot or line of fabric glue directly to the *back* of the item you want to stick (e.g., an ID badge, temporary ID, small tag). Press firmly onto the lining of the jacket. Hold for 30-60 seconds. Avoid over-application to prevent seepage.

2.  **Double-Sided Fabric Tape:**
    *   **Why:** Provides a strong, temporary, and clean bond. Reusable and removable.
    *   **How:** Cut a piece slightly larger than the item. Stick the *sticky side down* onto the lining of the jacket. Peel off the backing. Press the item firmly onto the sticky side. For temporary ID, this is often ideal.

3.  **Removable Adhesive Tape (Fabric-Safe):**
    *   **Why:** Strong hold, easy to remove, and usually residue-free.
    *   **How:** Use a high-quality fabric-safe adhesive tape (like some types of double-sided tape or specific "fabric glue tape"). Apply to the item, then press firmly onto the lining. Test removal carefully first.

**Methods to AVOID:**

*   **Regular Tape (Scotch Tape, Duct Tape, etc.):** Can tear the fabric, leave residue, or damage the lining's texture and appearance.
*   **Super Glue (Cyanoacrylate):** Can soak into and potentially damage synthetic linings, causing stiffening or discoloration.
*   **Hair Gel/Spray:** Can ruin the lining's texture, cause stiffening, and leave residue. Not a reliable adhesive for this purpose.
*   **Permanent Liquid Glue (like Aleene's):** High risk of soaking in and damaging the lining, causing discoloration or stiffening.

**Key Tips:**

*   **Test First:** Always test your chosen adhesive (glue or tape) on an inconspicuous area of the jacket lining first to ensure it doesn't damage the color or texture.
*   **Less is More:** Apply glue or tape in small amounts to avoid seepage or residue.
*   **Press Firmly:** Ensure good contact between the item and the lining for a strong bond.
*   **Consider Placement:** Stick items to the *lining* itself, not over the outer fabric where they might be visible or snagged.

Using **fabric glue** or **double-sided fabric tape** is your safest bet for securely attaching an item to your jacket lining without compromising its integrity.
