Okay, this is a sudden and serious situation. I'm very sorry to hear about your aunt. First, take a breath. The most critical step right now is to confirm the details. You need to know the hospital's name, her room number, and the contact information for her doctor or nurse. You can usually find this on an emergency card if she was taken by ambulance. If you don't have that, call 911 and ask for help finding the hospital. Once you know the location, you can contact the hospital's visitor services to ask about their visitation policies, especially for non-family members. They'll tell you if you need to register or show an ID. After that, you can start making the actual reservations, either online through the hospital's website or by calling their visitor information line. Just focus on those first steps.
