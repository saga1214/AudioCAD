Okay, kosher dietary laws are pretty specific. The main thing to know is that they require separating meat and dairy. So, for the book club, you'd want to keep the two food groups completely separate. That means having a dedicated plate for the meat items, like the blood pudding, and a different set of plates for the dairy items. Also, make sure any utensils or serving spoons used for meat aren't touching anything used for dairy. So, maybe have two different serving bowls and two different sets of tongs. For the snacks, the hummus is dairy, so it needs its own plate. The weenies in bacon are meat, so that's a separate plate. And the fruit platter and any cookies would be in a third plate. The key is just making sure everything is kept apart and no one's utensils get mixed up. That should be fine.
