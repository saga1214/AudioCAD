To recap the total cost for four family members: for the Chicken Cutlets, chicken breasts will be eight to fifteen dollars, all-purpose flour three to five dollars, twelve eggs three to seven dollars, breadcrumbs two to four dollars, vegetable oil four to eight dollars, and salt and pepper three to six dollars.

For the Garlic Mashed Potatoes, a five-pound bag of potatoes is three to six dollars, and a head of garlic is about one dollar.
