### U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia  
If you make an illegal U-turn in BC, penalties include:  
- **Tickets:** 100 to 1,000+ dollars (depending on violation severity).  
- **Demerit Points:** 1–3 points on your license for dangerous U-turns (e.g., near intersections, curves, or pedestrians).  
- **Insurance Impact:** Repeated offenses may raise premiums.  
- **Warning:** First-time minor violations (e.g., in a no-U-turn zone) might result in a warning.  

**Key Rules:**  
- Never U-turn at red lights, stop signs, or where prohibited by signs.  
- Ensure full visibility (150+ meters) in both directions.  
- Avoid blind curves, hills, or busy roads.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Recipe (Ingredients)  
**For the Sauce:**  
- 4 large eggs  
- ½ cup Pecorino Romano cheese (grated)  
- ½ cup Parmigiano Reggiano cheese (grated)  
- 1 tsp black pepper  
- Salt (to taste)  

**For Cooking:**  
- 225g pancetta or guanciale (diced)  
- 400g spaghetti  
- 2 garlic cloves (minced, optional)  

**Steps:**  
1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
2. Fry pancetta in a pan until crispy. Add garlic if using.  
3. Whisk eggs, cheeses, pepper, and salt.  
4. Turn off heat. Toss pasta with pancetta and pasta water. Off heat, mix in egg mixture.  
5. Serve immediately!  

**Tip:** Don’t cook eggs with heat—residual warmth cooks them gently.  

Enjoy your meal! 🍝
