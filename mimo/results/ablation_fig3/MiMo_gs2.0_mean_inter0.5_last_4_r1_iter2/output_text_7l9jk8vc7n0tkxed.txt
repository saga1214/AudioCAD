### Step 1: Disconnect the Trap
*   **Action:** Locate the U-shaped pipe (the trap) under the sink or fixture. It connects to the drain pipe below.
*   **Tools Needed:** Pliers (needle-nose or regular).
*   **How:** Use the pliers to grip the slip nut (the threaded fitting) on the trap. Turn it counter-clockwise (lefty-loosey) until it's loose. Carefully pull the trap assembly straight up and out of the drain opening. Place it aside.

### Step 2: Inspect the Trap & Attempt Removal
*   **Action:** Look *inside* the trap for any obvious debris (hair, soap scum, etc.). Use pliers to grab any large chunks.
*   **Action:** Try to pull the blockage out with a wire hanger or a drain snake (if you have one).
    *   Straighten the hanger so the hook points down.
    *   Lower it into the drain opening, hooking into the blockage.
    *   Pull firmly upwards. If it's deep, it might snag and come out partially or break apart. Be patient.

### Step 3: Attempt to Loosen the Drain Pipe (If accessible)
*   **Action:** If the drain pipe below the trap is accessible (like in a crawlspace or basement), locate where it connects to the wall or floor.
*   **Tools Needed:** Pipe wrench.
*   **How:** Place the pipe wrench securely around the drain pipe fitting. Turn it clockwise (righty-tighty) to tighten it, then counter-clockwise (lefty-loosey) to loosen it. Use a screwdriver to hold the nut steady if needed. Once loose, carefully pull the pipe out a few inches. Sometimes this motion shifts the clog. Reinsert it and see if water flows better.

### Step 4: Check the P-Trap (If still connected)
*   **Action:** If the trap is still connected (or you found it's the problem), carefully disconnect it using the same slip nut method as Step 1.
*   **Action:** Use pliers to remove any debris stuck in the trap's U-bend.

*   **Action:** If the blockage is gone, reconnect everything securely (use pliers for slip nuts).
*   **Action:** If the blockage remains, try plunging *again* but *after* disconnecting the P-trap. This creates a better seal. Use a strong, steady push-pull motion.
