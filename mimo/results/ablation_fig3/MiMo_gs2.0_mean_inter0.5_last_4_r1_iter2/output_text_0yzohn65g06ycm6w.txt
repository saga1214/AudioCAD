Here's a concise summary of the advice:

1.  **Control Feeding:** Use microchip feeders or timed feeders to prevent greedy cats from eating everything immediately, ensuring fussy ones get fed. Isolate fussy cats briefly if possible.
2.  **Seek Support:** Look for local pet food banks, shelters, or charities offering pet food assistance.
3.  **Stretch Quality Food:**
    *   Buy quality food in bulk for better value.
    *   Mix small amounts of quality food with cheap fillers like plain cooked rice or pasta (vet-approved if possible).
4.  **Prevent Problems:** Maintain clean environments, bowls, and water. Brush cats to reduce hairballs/skin issues. Monitor weight to prevent obesity.
5.  **Care for Yourself:** Focus on basic self-care and community resources, as your well-being impacts your cats' care. Supplement with safe, low-cost table scraps (plain cooked chicken/fish) when possible.
6.  **Early Vigilance:** Watch closely for early signs of illness. While full vet visits are costly, catching issues early allows for simpler, potentially cheaper interventions later.
