Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your TV, planters, and shelves, keeping your rules and schedule in mind:

### 📺 **TV Mount Installation**
1. **Prep Work:**  
   - Measure your TV’s weight and dimensions.  
   - Use a **stud finder** to locate wall studs (critical for heavy mounts).  
   - If no studs, use **heavy-duty drywall anchors** rated for your TV’s weight.  

2. **Mounting:**  
   - Hold the bracket against the wall at your desired height (use a **level**!).  
   - Mark screw holes, then drill pilot holes slightly smaller than the screws.  
   - Attach the bracket to studs or anchors, then secure the TV with included screws.  

3. **Tools Needed:**  
   - Drill, stud finder, level, screws, wall anchors (if needed).  
   - *Tip: Do this Sunday morning before noise-sensitive hours.*

### 🌿 **Hanging Planters**
1. **Lightweight Options (Macramé, Small Pots):**  
   - Use **S-hooks or picture wire** to hang from ceiling beams or studs.  
   - Ensure hooks are rated for the planter’s weight.  

2. **Heavier Pots (terracotta, large plants):**  
   - Install a **wall-mounted planter holder** (secure to studs).  
   - Or drill a hole in the wall for a **hanging pot pocket** (use waterproof anchors).  

3. **Tools Needed:**  
   - Drill, anchors (for heavy pots), hooks/wires, measuring tape.  
   - *Tip: Hang during gym hours to avoid noise conflicts.*

### 📚 **Floating Shelves**
1. **Alignment First:**  
   - Mark shelf positions with a **pencil** and **level**. Ensure even spacing.  

2. **Installation:**  
   - Drill pilot holes at marked points.  
   - Attach **L-brackets** or use **floating shelf hardware** (like French cleats).  
   - For extra stability, screw into studs at the back.  

3. **Tools Needed:**  
   - Drill, L-brackets/French cleats, screws, level, stud finder.  
   - *Tip: Install after gym sessions to avoid stiffness while focusing.*

### ⚠️ **Key Reminders**
- **Noise Rules:** Do all drilling/hanging on weekends (avoid late nights).  
- **Gym Timing:** Prep tools before workouts to avoid distractions.  
- **Safety:** Always wear safety glasses and use a stud finder!  

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Dedicate 2–3 hours on Sunday to install everything at once. Work in this new space during the week, and by weekends, you’ll have both a functional and cozy home!
