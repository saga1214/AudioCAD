Okay, Northern California warm weather vacation with swimming opportunities, packing help previously given, and now a full itinerary request for a family of three, including a 12-year-old with Celiac disease, for 4-5 days. The budget is healthy. Let's break this down.

First, the key requirements are warm weather for swimming, dining accommodating Celiac, and travel for a family of three. Since they want to compete with a hot air balloon, we need to focus on truly spectacular outdoor experiences.

Considering the timing, which is after her birthday and likely in October or November, coastal areas are the way to go. Carmel-by-the-Sea comes to mind immediately. It's known for warmth, beaches, and a charming, upscale vibe. It's about a 2.5 to 3-hour drive from Northern California, so it's feasible for a weekend trip. The weather there is typically mild but can be warm enough for swimming in the ocean, especially if you go early in the morning or late in the afternoon.

For the itinerary, a 4-day plan seems right. Day one could be arrival and settling in. Day two would be the main adventure day, focusing on the Big Sur coast, which is breathtaking and offers hiking with ocean views. Day three could be more relaxed, exploring Carmel itself. Day four would be for departure.

Accommodation-wise, Carmel has a range of options. For a family with a healthy budget, a nice hotel like the Carmel River Inn or the Hotel Carmel would be perfect. They offer pools, which are great for swimming, and are centrally located. I'd recommend booking a room with a balcony or ocean view if possible. For dining, Carmel is excellent for Celiac-friendly restaurants. Places like The Del Mar in Carmel Valley are highly rated for their gluten-free options. There's also a great dedicated gluten-free restaurant called The Big Sur Bakery & Café.

For the adventure, the Big Sur coast is the star. The drive itself is an event, with stops at Bixby Bridge and McWay Falls. For swimming, Carmel Beach is the main spot, and you can also find tide pools. The Big Sur Coast has tide pools too, but they're more rocky. A scenic drive along the Pacific Coast Highway is a must-do activity.

Packing tips would include swimwear, a light cover-up, sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, and light layers for the evenings. A reusable water bottle is also a good idea.

This plan seems to cover everything: warm weather for swimming, spectacular outdoor adventures, accommodation, dining, and travel logistics for the family. It should be a wonderful getaway.
