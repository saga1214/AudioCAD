That's a great question about balancing the creative grind with self-care. Here's how pros handle it, and some quick, flavorful food ideas for your craving:

**Balancing Recording, Editing & Marketing:**
1.  **Time Blocking:** Schedule dedicated blocks for recording, editing, and admin/marketing. Don't let one bleed into the other uncontrollfully.
2.  **The "Record-Edit-Market" Loop:** Record when your voice is fresh. Edit immediately after (while the performance is still in your head). Schedule specific marketing time (social media, outreach, admin) *away* from the mic.
3.  **Batch Processing:** Record multiple takes or projects in one session if possible. Edit several projects together.
4.  **Prioritize:** Focus on the project with the tightest deadline first. Don't let "perfect" editing delay marketing.
5.  **Set Boundaries:** Protect your vocal rest time (like meals!) and personal time. Learn to say "no" or "not now" to non-urgent requests.
6.  **Automate & Systematize:** Use templates for editing, pre-written social posts, checklists. Reduce decision fatigue.

**For the Harsh Audio & Workflow:**

*   **Switching to Audition:** Yes, it's worth it! The learning curve is steeper than Audacity, but the speed, processing power, and effects (like the excellent noise reduction and mouth click removal tools) are significant advantages for voice work. Look for tutorials specifically for voice actors.
*   **Reliable AI Tools:**
    *   **Silence Trimming:** Audition's "Noise Reduction" (with high quality) and "Cleanup" tools work well. iZotope RX 10's "VoiceIsolate" and "DeClick" are industry standards (paid, but powerful).
    *   **Labeling:** While no single AI tool does *everything* perfectly yet, tools like Descript (especially with AI features) and Audacity's "Segmenter" plugin (paid) can help auto-label sections. Often, manual labeling is still faster and more accurate.
*   **Plugins for Mouth Clicks/Noise:** **iZotope RX** is the gold standard. Its "DeClick" and "Denoise" modules are exceptionally effective for voice. Audition's built-in tools are very capable too.

Here are some quick, flavorful bets from a place like a local Chinese, Thai, or Japanese restaurant chain:

7.  **Korean Fried Chicken (Bokkeumbap):** (Korean-American) Crispy, spicy, or sweet and spicy fried chicken served over rice. Ultimate comfort food.
