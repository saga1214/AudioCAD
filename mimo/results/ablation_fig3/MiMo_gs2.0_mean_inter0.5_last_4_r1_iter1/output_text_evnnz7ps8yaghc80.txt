That's a fantastic idea. A good, structured plan is key to making progress without burning out. Here is a balanced weekly schedule to help you.

On Monday, start with a five-minute warm-up, just throwing two beanbags back and forth. Then, spend about fifteen minutes on three-ball drills, like we talked about. Follow that with ten minutes of scarf practice. Finally, cool down with five minutes of two-ball beanbag juggling.

Tuesday, you could do some light juggling with beanbags for ten minutes, and then spend fifteen minutes on your one-hand two-ball drills. After that, ten minutes with scarves, and cool down with five minutes of two-ball juggling.

Wednesday is a rest day. Just do some gentle stretching or watch some juggling tutorials.

Thursday, you can do a ten-minute warm-up with beanbags, then fifteen minutes of scarves to focus on slow, controlled throws. Then, ten minutes on three-ball drills, and cool down with five minutes of two-ball beanbag juggling.

For Friday, you could do ten minutes of one-hand two-ball drills, then fifteen minutes of beanbag three-ball practice. Follow that with ten minutes of scarves, and finish with a five-minute warm-up.

Saturday is for putting it all together. Spend twenty minutes on your three-ball beanbag juggling, using both drills and scarves if you need to. Then, ten minutes of beanbag warm-up, and cool down with ten minutes of two-ball beanbag juggling.

Finally, Sunday is another rest day.
