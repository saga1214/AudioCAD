Okay, you need a pick-me-up! Let's get you energized. For drinks, here are five ideas. First, a strong cup of black coffee for an instant caffeine boost. Second, green tea, which has caffeine but also L-theanine for a smoother energy. Third, a cold glass of lemonade for hydration and a bit of a zing. Fourth, a smoothie, which gives you vitamins, protein, and energy from fruits and greens. And fifth, a cup of hot chocolate with a little extra sugar for a cozy but effective energy lift.

As for snacks, here are five to keep you going. First, some nuts or trail mix for healthy fats and protein. Second, yogurt with honey and berries for a nice mix of protein and carbs. Third, a banana or an apple for quick, natural energy. Fourth, some dark chocolate, like 85 percent cacao, for antioxidants and a little caffeine. And fifth, hard-boiled eggs for a solid protein punch.
