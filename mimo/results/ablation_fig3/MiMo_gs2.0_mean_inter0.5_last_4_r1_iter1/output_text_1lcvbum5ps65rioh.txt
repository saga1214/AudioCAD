### U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia:
If you make an unauthorized U-turn in BC, the penalties can include:
1. **Ticket**: A fine of up to **$100** (plus court costs).
2. **Demerit Points**: Up to **3 demerit points** on your driver's license for careless driving (if the U-turn obstructed traffic).
3. **Reckless Driving**: If deemed dangerous, penalties increase to a **$1,600 fine** and **3 demerit points**.

**Key reminders**: Always check for signs, ensure visibility, and avoid U-turns at signals, curves, or near intersections unless explicitly permitted.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients:
Here’s your shopping list for **4 servings**:

#### **Core Ingredients**:
1. **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti (or bucatini).
2. **Eggs**: 4 large eggs + 1 extra yolk (for creaminess).
3. **Cheeses**:  
   - 1 cup grated **Pecorino Romano** (essential for authentic flavor).  
   - ½ cup grated **Parmigiano-Reggiano** (for balance).  
4. **Meat**:  
   - 200g diced **pancetta** (or guanciale for traditional taste).  
   - *Substitute*: Cooked bacon or thick-cut ham.  
5. **Aromatics**:  
   - 2 cloves garlic, finely minced.  
   - 1 tsp black pepper (freshly ground).  
6. **Finishing Touches**:  
   - 1 tbsp lemon juice (brightens flavor).  
   - 1 tbsp olive oil (optional, for richness).  

#### **Seasoning**:
- Salt (for pasta water).

#### **Equipment**:
- Large pot, large skillet/frying pan, mixing bowl, tongs, whisk.

---

### Quick Tips:
- **Cheese Choice**: Pecorino is salty/strong; use less if your pancetta is salty.  
- **Egg Safety**: Cook the eggs in a pan over low heat for 2–3 minutes to set.  
- **Worst-Case Scenario**: If caught making an illegal U-turn, the officer will issue a ticket on the spot. Always prioritize safety!
