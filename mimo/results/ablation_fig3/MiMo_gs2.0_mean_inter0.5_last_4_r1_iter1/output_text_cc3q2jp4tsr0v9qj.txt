### 1️⃣ **Open Your "Essentials" Box**  
   - **Toiletries:** Soap, hand soap, toilet paper.  
   - **Phone Chargers:** Power up devices.  
   - **Snacks/Drinks:** Fuel the move!  
   - **Water:** Stock up on bottled water (find shut-offs ASAP).

### 2️⃣ **Set Up Beds Immediately**  
   - Tackle sofas/chairs first.  
   - Prioritize sleep comfort—you’ll both be exhausted!

### 3️⃣ **Turn On Utilities**  
   - **Water:** Open the main shut-off valve (if needed). Test faucets/shower.  
   - **Power:** Plug in appliances (fridge, AC). Test outlets.  
   - **Gas:** If applicable, check detectors and pilot lights.

### 4️⃣ **File HOA Documentation**  
   - Submit your registration paperwork.  
   - Note rules: Yard maintenance, exterior changes, parking, trash pickup times.

### 5️⃣ **Unpack Strategically**  
   - **Kitchen First:** Plates, utensils, coffee maker.  
   - **Bathroom:** Toiletries, towels, shower essentials.  
   - **Chairs/Tables:** Set up before eating dinner.

### 6️⃣ **Schedule Internet Setup**  
   - Call providers to install service.  
   - For satellite dishes: Confirm HOA approval *before* installation.

### 7️⃣ **Clean Up Movers’ Debris**  
   - Remove packing tape, cardboard, and trash.  
   - Sweep/vacuum high-traffic areas.

### 8️⃣ **Order Takeout or Groceries**  
   - Celebrate with food! No cooking fatigue tonight.  
   - If cooking tomorrow, stock basics: pasta, eggs, bread.

### 9️⃣ **Set Up Safety Systems**  
   - Install smoke/CO detectors.  
   - Label trash/recycling bins.

### 🔟 **Relax & Celebrate!**  
   - Watch a movie, play a game, or enjoy a bath.  
   - Sleep in your new bed—no chores tomorrow!

### Pro Tips:  
- **Document Everything:** Take photos of damage (HOA rules apply).  
- **Store Manuals:** Keep appliance manuals handy for setup.  
- **Label Boxes:** "Kitchen," "Bedroom," etc., for faster unpacking.
