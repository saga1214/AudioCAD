Okay, for a retro tech theme lunch, here are two ideas. First, you could do a 'Mainframe Memory Bowl'. The base of the meal would be a hearty lentil soup, which is like the 'mainframe' of the meal. For sides, you could have some roasted chickpeas as a crunchy side, which represent 'RAM', and some whole-grain crackers as 'hard drives'. For snacks, pretzel bites with mustard could be 'boot-up strips', and trail mix could be 'data packets'. A second idea is a 'Vintage Vending Machine Platter'. The main dish could be a quinoa salad with a lemon-tahini dressing, which is like the 'vending machine contents'. For sides, you could have some veggie sticks with hummus, acting as the 'vending machine nozzle', and some fruit slices for a 'healthy option' label. For snacks, popcorn kernels could be 'classic vending machine snacks', and some cheese cubes could be 'premium snacks'. Both ideas keep it simple but tie back to the tech theme.
