Okay, first, let's look at the phrases similar to "burning the candle at both ends." There's "cramming in too much," which means trying to do too many things in a short amount of time. Then there's "racing against the clock," which is about feeling rushed because of time pressure. And "running on empty" describes being completely exhausted from overworking. Another one is "living hand-to-mouth," which means barely getting by financially or in terms of energy. Lastly, "stretching thin" means being overextended and unable to handle everything.
