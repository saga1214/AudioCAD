### 🍜 Asian Noodle Spots in Dallas (Check Menus for Soy Content!)
1.  **Yummy World:** Famous for customizable ramen. You can easily swap out the broth base (many veggie options) and avoid tofu/soy sauce by asking the chef. Great for controlling soy intake.
2.  **Sushi Tokyo:** While primarily sushi, their *donburi* (rice bowls) and *gyudon* (beef bowls) often feature udon or soba noodles. Ask specifically for no soy sauce, tamari, or tofu when ordering. Their veggie options are usually soy-free.
3.  **Pho 79:** Pho broth is traditionally soy-based (fish sauce + soy sauce), but many places offer "hoisin-free" or "fish sauce-free" broth options upon request. *Crucially:* **Ask if the broth is made with soy sauce or contains tofu.** Some places might be able to make it for you without soy, but it's not guaranteed. Verify carefully.
4.  **Mama's Kitchen (Makro):** Offers various Asian noodle dishes. Ask specifically about soy sauce, tofu, and soy lecithin in any noodle dish you choose.
5.  **Yum Yum Noodle House:** Another customizable spot. Clearly state you need no soy sauce, tamari, tofu, or any soy derivatives. They're accustomed to requests.

**Pro Tip:** Always call ahead or check the restaurant's website/menu online *before* you go. Politely explain your soy intolerance and ask how they can prepare your meal safely. Many places can accommodate, but communication is key!

### 🥜 Soy-Free Protein Bar Recommendations
Look for bars labeled "Soy-Free" or "Soy-Free Protein Bar." *Always double-check the ingredients list for soy lecithin, soy protein isolate, soy protein concentrate, tofu, or soy flour.*

1.  **RXBAR:** Simple ingredients (dates, nuts, egg white, protein). Most flavors are soy-free (check specific flavor ingredient lists).
2.  **Clif Bars (Specifically):** The original Clif Bars and most Clif Bars Protein Bars are soy-free. *Avoid* Clif Builders (contains soy protein isolate).
3.  **Quest Nutrition:** Their Bilt Berry and Chocolate Peanut Butter Protein Bars are soy-free. *Avoid* their Cookies & Cream (contains soy lecithin).
4.  **Imagine Bars:** Most flavors are soy-free (check individual labels). Known for lower sugar.
5.  **Lärabar:** Many flavors are soy-free (check label - often contains nut butters, dates, oats).
6.  **NOVA Bar:** Simple ingredients (dates, nuts, coconut oil, vanilla). Soy-free.
7.  **RXBAR Mini:** Smaller, soy-free option.
8.  **Beeyondbar:** Soy-free options available (check specific flavor).

**Key Tip:** **ALWAYS verify the ingredients list on the specific package you buy!** Formulations can change. Look for bars with minimal ingredients and clear "Soy-Free" labeling if possible. For convenience, stores like Target, Whole Foods, Sprouts, and Walmart usually carry several of these brands.
