Of course! With a new Toyota SUV and a new baby, here are five fantastic road trip ideas for you.

First, you could head to Whistler. It's a classic, about a three-hour drive. You can ski in the winter, hike in the summer, and of course, go to the famous Whistler Village for great food and shopping.

Second, a day trip to the Vancouver Island Wine Country near Victoria is perfect. You can tour the wineries, and since you have beds sorted at home, you can even find a comfortable place to stay overnight and really enjoy it.

Third, there's Okanagan Lake. It's known for its stunning lakeside scenery and world-class wineries. It's a great spot for swimming and relaxing.

Fourth, you could take a trip to the Fraser Canyon. It's about four hours from Vancouver and features some incredible old-growth forests and historic towns.

And fifth, for a longer trip, you could drive to Banff National Park in Alberta. The drive itself is spectacular, and the national park offers everything you'd want: hiking, lakes, and a beautiful mountain landscape.

These are all great options to start with, and you can take them at your own pace, especially with the new baby.
