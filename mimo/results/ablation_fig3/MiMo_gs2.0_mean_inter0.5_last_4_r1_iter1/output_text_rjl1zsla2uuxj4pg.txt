You've got the essentials covered! Here's a quick recap of what you'll likely need for a new cat:

1.  **Scratching Post:** **Essential.** This is non-negotiable for protecting your furniture and keeping your cat's claws healthy and sharp.
2.  **Food & Water Bowls:** Stainless steel or ceramic are best for durability and preventing bacteria. Get both dry and wet food bowls.
3.  **Cat Bed:** Optional but recommended. Many cats appreciate a cozy spot to rest, nap, and hide.
4.  **Carrier:** **Essential.** A sturdy, secure carrier is needed for travel to the vet and beyond. Get one that fits the cat snugly as a kitten.
5.  **Litter Box:** You already have this, great!
6.  **Litter:** Get a type your cat likes (some prefer clumping, some prefer non-clumping).
7.  **Cat Tree/Scratching Post Combo:** Can serve multiple purposes at once.
8.  **Cat Toys:** Interactive toys (wands with feathers, balls) are great for bonding and exercise. Some cats love puzzle feeders.
9.  **Grooming Supplies:** Cat-safe nail clippers, cat-safe brush or comb (depending on coat length), and maybe a toothbrush/toothpaste.

**Regarding breeds again:** The **American Shorthair** is a fantastic all-around choice, friendly and adaptable. The **British Shorthair** is calmer with a luxurious coat. The **Siamese** is more vocal, social, and active. Consider your lifestyle and preferences when choosing!

You're well on your way to being a great cat parent! Enjoy the process!
