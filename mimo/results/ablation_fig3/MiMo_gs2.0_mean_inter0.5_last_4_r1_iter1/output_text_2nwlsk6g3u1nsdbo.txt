Great question! Here's a quick recap of the three shrimp main courses I suggested:  

1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
   * **Ingredients**:  
     - 1 lb large shrimp, peeled & deveined  
     - 8 oz linguine  
     - 4 cloves garlic, minced  
     - ½ cup butter  
     - ½ cup dry white wine  
     - ½ cup lemon juice  
     - 1 tsp red pepper flakes  
     - Salt & pepper to taste  
     - Fresh parsley (optional, for garnish)  

2. **Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
   * **Ingredients**:  
     - 1 lb shrimp, peeled & deveined  
     - 2 cups crushed tomatoes  
     - 3 cloves garlic, minced  
     - ¼ cup olive oil  
     - 2 tbsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat)  
     - ¼ cup fresh basil, chopped  
     - 1 cup heavy cream (optional, for richness)  
     - Salt & pepper to taste  

3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
   * **Ingredients**:  
     - 1 lb shrimp, peeled & deveined  
     - 1 cup quick-cooking grits  
     - 2 cups chicken or vegetable broth  
     - 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning  
     - 1 bell pepper, diced  
     - 1 cup spinach, chopped  
     - ¼ cup grated Parmesan  
     - Hot sauce (optional)
