That's an excellent point, and it's a very common question. The answer is yes, you absolutely can submit an entire project with illustrations, and it can be a huge advantage.

However, the approach depends on your goals. If you want to publish as a traditional author-illustrator, meaning you want to be the one who both writes the book and draws the pictures, then you'll need to target publishers who specifically look for that kind of package. You'll have to find agents or publishers who represent author-illustrators and send them your complete manuscript along with your illustrations.

If you're not sure if you want to do both, you could submit just the text, and the publisher would then hire an illustrator. But since you have the art, the most direct path is to target those author-illustrator opportunities.
