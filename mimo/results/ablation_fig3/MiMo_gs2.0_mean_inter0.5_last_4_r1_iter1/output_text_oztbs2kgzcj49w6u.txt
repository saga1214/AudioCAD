You're in luck, as there are several great places to learn more about the canal's history in Lockport.

The absolute best place is the Lockport Canal Museum. It's located right there at the Lockport Locks and it has exhibits, a working demonstration of how the locks work, and even a small boat you can ride through to see it all in action.

If you'd rather learn about the canal's history in a more relaxed setting, the Erie Canal Heritage Center in Waterford is another great option. They have a museum, a large boat, and a nice, easy walking path along the canal.
