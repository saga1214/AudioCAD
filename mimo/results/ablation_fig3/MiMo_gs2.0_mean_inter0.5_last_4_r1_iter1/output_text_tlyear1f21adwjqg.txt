Hmm, this is a tough one because each source has its own strengths. Solar is great for scalability and falling costs. Wind is reliable in certain areas, and hydropower is incredibly dependable with its existing infrastructure. If I absolutely had to pick just one overall, I'd still lean towards solar energy. It's available almost everywhere, easy to scale from rooftop panels to huge farms, and the cost is dropping fast. Storage solutions are also getting better, which helps with its intermittency. So while the others are strong, solar's versatility and cost-effectiveness give it the edge for me.
