Okay, let me re-read your cabinet prep steps to make sure I didn't miss anything. You mentioned removing hardware and sanding, which is perfect. Before you apply that primer, the very last step is to give the sanded surface a quick wipe with a tack cloth. This gets rid of all the dust the sanding created, which will make sure the primer sticks perfectly.

After that, you're all set to go. Just apply the primer according to the instructions, let it dry completely, and then you can start painting. Good luck with both the project and your new neighbor.
