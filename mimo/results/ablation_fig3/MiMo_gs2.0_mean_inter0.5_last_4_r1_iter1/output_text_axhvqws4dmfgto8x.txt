Don't worry about setting off the alarm this time – stir-fry doesn't involve boiling!

The most important thing for stir-fry is to get your pan really hot before you add the meat. You want to hear that sizzle. Also, try to cook the meat in batches if your pan isn't huge, so nothing gets steamed.

The biggest secret to good stir-fry noodles is to cook them al dente, which means a little bit firm, and then save about half a cup of the starchy pasta water before you drain them. You'll need it to make the sauce.

Then, when you add the noodles back to the pan with the sauce, toss them around really well to make sure they're coated and not just sitting in a puddle of sauce. You got this!
