**For a working glue gun on a hot day:**

1.  **Reseat the Cartridge:** Sometimes the plastic tip gets loose when hot. Turn it off, let it cool slightly, and gently pull out the hot glue cartridge. Check the tip for cracks or damage. Re-seat the cartridge firmly.
2.  **Check the Tip:** Is the tip cracked? A cracked tip won't hold heat properly. If cracked, you'll need a new tip.
3.  **Give it Time (Briefly):** Sometimes it just needs a tiny moment to fully melt. Try a small test dot on scrap paper. If it doesn't melt within 10-15 seconds, it might be faulty.
4.  **Try a Different Brand/Type:** Some brands or types (especially cheap ones) are notoriously finicky on hot days. If it's a new cartridge, try a different brand or type known for reliability.
5.  **Use a Hairdryer (Gently!):** If reseating and checking don't work, *carefully* warm the tip with a hairdryer on low setting for 10-15 seconds. This can sometimes help the plastic expand slightly and seal better. **Be extremely cautious not to overheat it.**
6.  **Try Hot Water (Extreme Caution):** As a last resort, *very briefly* dip the *entire* tip (not the cord!) into boiling water for 5-10 seconds. This melts the plastic inside the tip. **This can damage the tip permanently.** Let it cool completely before trying. **Use this method sparingly.**
7.  **Consider Hot Glue Alternative:** If it's consistently unreliable, especially for these delicate decorations, consider using a strong craft glue (like E6000 or Tacky Glue) applied with a small dotting tool or toothpick for precision. It gives you more control and time to work.

**While you're waiting for it to work (or if you need alternatives):**

*   **Strong Craft Glue:** As mentioned, dots or lines of glue applied carefully with a toothpick or fine brush work well for attaching masks, spiderwebs, or securing lace netting.
*   **Double-Sided Tape:** Great for attaching masks or securing lace netting in place. Use small pieces.
*   **Mini Velcro Strips:** Perfect for attaching masks or even securing the base of spiderwebs if you want something reusable.
*   **Hot Fusible Tape (Iron-On):** Works well on fabrics like lace or netting. You'd need an iron and patience to let it set.
