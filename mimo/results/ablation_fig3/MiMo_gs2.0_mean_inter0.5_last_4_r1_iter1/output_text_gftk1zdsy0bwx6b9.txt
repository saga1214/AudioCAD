Here’s how you could organize it: You can give Sal and Greg the gingerbread houses along with the Oatmeal and Chocolate Chip cookies in the frilly tin. Emily and Diane could get the houses with the Gingerbread and Snickerdoodle cookies in the Christmas tin.
