Of course. Here’s a shopping list for the Asian-inspired meal you two will enjoy, keeping legumin-free and avoiding seafood. I'll break it down for you.

First, for the starter: Miso Cucumber Salad or Sesame Cucumber Salad. For this, you'll need English or Japanese cucumbers, mirin, rice vinegar, sugar, and white miso paste. You'll also need a little soy sauce and sesame oil. For the sesame version, grab toasted sesame seeds, and for the miso soup option, you'll need some dashi granules or a kombu tea substitute.

Next, for the main course, Chicken Teriyaki with Jasmine Rice and Stir-Fried Veggies. You'll need boneless, skinless chicken breasts. Remember, he can't have legumes, so use chicken. For the teriyaki sauce, you can use store-bought or make your own with soy sauce, mirin, sake, and a bit of sugar. You'll also need steamed jasmine rice. For the stir-fried veggies, you can pick any he likes, like bell peppers, carrots, snap peas, or mushrooms. You'll need cooking oil, soy sauce, and a bit of sesame oil for flavor. A touch of ginger and garlic powder can add some nice aroma.

Finally, for dessert, I suggest Mango Sticky Rice with Sweetened Coconut. You'll need sweet, ripe mangoes. For the sticky rice, you can find pre-cooked or frozen bags in the Asian grocery section. For the sauce, you'll need full-fat coconut milk, a little sugar, and salt. And if you want to get fancy, you can pick up some matcha powder for that homemade ice cream, but it's optional.

Just a few extra things to keep in mind: make sure the miso paste is legume-free, as some brands use soy flour, and double-check that the chicken teriyaki sauce doesn't have any bean sprouts or other hidden legumes. Enjoy your romantic dinner!
