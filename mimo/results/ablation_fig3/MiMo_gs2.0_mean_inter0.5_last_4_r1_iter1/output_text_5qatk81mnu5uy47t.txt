Absolutely! Planning an unforgettable Costa Rica scuba diving trip requires balancing your experience level, interests, and Costa Rica’s incredible diversity. Here’s a tailored roadmap to help you:

### 1. **Pre-Trip Essentials**
   - **Certification & Training**: Ensure you’re certified (PADI/SSI) and comfortable with deep dives (Cisterns in Costa Rica can reach 40m/130ft). If you’re new to strong currents, consider a "Rescue Diver" course for added confidence.
   - **Gear**: For beginners, **short fins** are ideal (easier to learn and less tiring). Pack reef-safe sunscreen, a dive computer, and a dive bag.
   - **Health**: Consult a doctor if you have heart conditions or other pre-existing issues.

### 2. **Costa Rica’s Best Diving Hotspots**
   Costa Rica has **three distinct zones**:
   - **Tortuguero (North)**:  
     - *Focus*: Calm, clear waters, mangrove forests, and **turtles** (hatchlings in July–August).  
     - *Best for*: Beginners, snorkeling, and serene dives.  
     - *Key Sites*: Cocos Island (dolphin encounters), Playa Naranjo (reef sharks).
   - **Cabo Blanco (Central Pacific)**:  
     - *Focus*: Coral reefs, **tuna**, **sea lions**, and **reef sharks**.  
     - *Best for*: Intermediate divers; strong currents but manageable.  
     - *Key Sites*: Playa Blanca (barracuda), Punta Islas (blacktip reef sharks).
   - **Córdoba (West Coast)**:  
     - *Focus*: **Whale sharks** (Dec–Feb), **sea turtles**, and **reef sharks**.  
     - *Best for*: Advanced divers; strong currents and deep dives.  
     - *Key Sites*: Playa Blanca (whale sharks), Playa Naranjo (reef sharks).

### 3. **Itinerary Suggestion**
   - **Day 1–3: Tortuguero**  
     - Stay in eco-lodges, kayak to dive sites, and dive early to avoid crowds.  
     - *Dive*: Playa Naranjo (reef sharks, turtles) + Cocos Island (dolphin pods).  
   - **Day 4–6: Cabo Blanco**  
     - Stay in Tamarindo or Playa Blanca. Dive with local operators (e.g., PADI centers).  
     - *Dive*: Playa Blanca (barracuda, rays) + Punta Islas (blacktip reef sharks).  
   - **Day 7–9: Córdoba**  
     - Stay in Puerto Viejo. Dive with operators like "Dive Paradise."  
     - *Dive*: Playa Blanca (whale sharks) + Playa Naranjo (reef sharks).  

### 4. **Wildlife & Experiences**
   - **Whale Sharks**: Best at **Córdoba** (Dec–Feb) and **Cocos Island** (year-round).  
   - **Turtles**: Common in **Tortuguero** (hatchlings in summer) and **Córdoba**.  
   - **Blue Hole**: Dine near **Tortuguero** at sunset (no diving required).  
   - **Night Dives**: Spot bioluminescent plankton at **Córdoba** (e.g., Punta Uva).

### 5. **Logistics & Tips**
   - **Accommodation**: Eco-lodges in Tortuguero, beachfronts in Cabo Blanco, or jungle resorts in Puerto Viejo.  
   - **Transport**: Fly into San José, then drive to Tortuguero (boat/boat combo), Cabo Blanco (ferry), or Córdoba (road trip).  
   - **Budget**: $1,500–$2,500/person for 10 days (flights, diving, accommodation).  
   - **Sustainability**: Choose eco-certified operators; avoid touching wildlife.

### 6. **Pro Tips**
   - **Avoid Peak Crowds**: Visit in **dry season (Dec–Apr)** for best visibility, but **Jan–Feb** is peak whale shark season.  
   - **Weather**: Rainy season (May–Oct) means fewer crowds but rougher seas.  
   - **Local Culture**: Visit a coffee plantation in Tortuguero or a chocolate factory in Tamarindo.

### Final Advice:
Costa Rica’s diving is unmatched for its biodiversity. If you prioritize **calm waters and turtles**, start with **Tortuguero**. For **advanced divers seeking sharks and currents**, focus on **Córdoba**. Pack light—focus on gear and a positive mindset!
