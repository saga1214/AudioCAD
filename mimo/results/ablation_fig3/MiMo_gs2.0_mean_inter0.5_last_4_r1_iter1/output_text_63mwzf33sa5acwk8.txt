That’s a great plan! You can totally blend adventure with a classic Vegas vibe. Here’s how to make it work:

### **1. Adventure Loop First:**
   - **Joshua Tree NP:** Stay at **Jumbo Rocks Campground** (rock formations for drama) or **Hidden Valley** (hiking vibes). Cost: ~$20–$30/night. Drive time: ~3 hours from SoCal.
   - **NV Desert Exploration:** Head to **Valley of Fire State Park** (red rocks, petroglyphs). Camp at **Valley of Fire Campground** (~$20/night). Or try **free BLM land** near **Pleasant Valley** (off-grid desert camping).

### **2. Vegas Reset:**
   - **Skip the Strip:** Book a **boutique hotel** (e.g., **The Cosmopolitan** or **Aloft**) for skyline views, or find a **private cabana** at a pool bar (e.g., **Drai’s** or **Omnia**) for sunset cocktails.
   - **Budget Hack:** Stay at a **Bellagio** or **MGM Grand** but use their **free pool loungers** (no room needed). Or book a **cheap Airbnb** near the Strip for a private space.

### **3. Vegas Experience Highlights:**
   - **Food:** Try **Gordon Ramsay’s Hell’s Kitchen** (splurge) or **Secret Pizza** (local favorite).
   - **Shows:** Catch a **high-energy Cirque du Soleil** (O or KÀ) or an intimate **magic show** at **The Absinthe**.
   - **Nightlife:** Rent a **private cabana** for sunset at **Encore Beach Club** or hit **The Park at The Cosmopolitan** for chic poolside drinks.
   - **Uniqueness:** Rent bikes to explore the **Fremont Street Experience** (free, artsy, vintage Vegas) or take a **helicopter tour** over the Strip.

### **4. Practical Van Tips:**
   - **NV State Parks Pass:** Get a **$30 annual pass** for unlimited camping at state parks (Valley of Fire included).
   - **BLM Land Rules:** Respect "No Overnight" zones. Pack out all trash and carry water.
   - **Vegas Charging:** Find a **van-friendly spot** like **Pilot Travel Centers** (Level 2 chargers) or **Free Campground** apps for overnight parking.

### **Route Example:**
   - Day 1: Drive to Joshua Tree → Hike Hidden Valley → Camp under the stars.
   - Day 2: Explore Jumbo Rocks → Drive 2.5hrs to Valley of Fire → Sunset at **Fire Wave**.
   - Day 3: Drive to Vegas → Check into boutique hotel → Pool day.
   - Day 4: Fremont Street → Show at Bellagio → Helicopter tour.
   - Day 5: Drive home via scenic route (e.g., **Pioneertown** stop).

This mix of wild deserts, unique NV landscapes, and Vegas glamour should make it unforgettable. Just pack layers (desert nights get cold!) and remember: Vegas is about the *experience*—not the spending! 🚐✨
