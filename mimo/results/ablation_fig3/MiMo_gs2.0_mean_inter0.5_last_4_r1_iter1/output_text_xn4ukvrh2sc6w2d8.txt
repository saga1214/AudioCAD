Here's a detailed bartender list with exact measurements and prep instructions for all your themed cocktails:

### **Old Fashioned**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Buffalo Trace)  
  - 1 sugar cube *or* ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (for garnish)  
- **Method**:  
  1. Muddle sugar cube with ¾ oz water in a mixing glass.  
  2. Add bourbon, bitters, and ice. Stir 30 sec until chilled.  
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.  
  4. Express orange twist over drink (no muddle), wipe rim, and garnish.  
- *Garnish*: Orange twist  

### **Paper Plane**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Bulleit)  
  - ¾ oz Aperol  
  - ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice 10-12 times (hard shake).  
  2. Strain into a coupe glass over fresh ice.  
  3. Express orange twist over drink and garnish.  
- *Garnish*: Orange twist  

### **Whiskey Sour**  
#### *Version 1: With Egg White*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - ½ oz simple syrup  
  - ½ egg white  
- **Method**:  
  1. Dry shake (no ice) all ingredients 15 sec until frothy.  
  2. Add ice, shake again 10-12 sec.  
  3. Strain into coupe glass over ice.  
  4. Garnish with cherry and lemon peel.  
- *Garnish*: Cherry + lemon peel  

#### *Version 2: Without Egg White*  
- **Follow same method as above, omit egg white.**  
- *Garnish*: Cherry  

### **Aviation**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz gin (e.g., Hendrick’s)  
  - ¾ oz maraschino liqueur (e.g., Luxardo)  
  - ½ oz crème de violette  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice 10-12 times.  
  2. Strain into a coupé glass over ice.  
  3. Express maraschino cherry over drink and garnish.  
- *Garnish*: Maraschino cherry  

### **French 75 (Gin Cocktail)**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz gin (e.g., Tanqueray)  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 3 oz dry champagne  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake gin, lemon, and syrup with ice 8-10 sec.  
  2. Strain into flute over ice.  
  3. Top with champagne.  
- *Garnish*: Lemon twist
