For an end-of-year celebration, a **low-key park picnic with optional activities** strikes the perfect balance between fun, budget, and flexibility. Here’s how to make it work:

### 🎉 Event Structure:
1. **Location**:  
   - **Park Picnic**: Choose a quiet spot near home (avoids travel stress).  
   - **Weather Backup**: Have a rain plan (e.g., a local community center or her school cafeteria).  

2. **Food & Snacks**:  
   - **Potluck Style**: Assign parents to bring specific dishes (e.g., "ice cream sundaes," "pizza toppings," "snack mixes").  
   - **Budget-Friendly**: Use a simple cake or cupcakes as the "official" treat.  
   - **Dietary Notes**: Clearly share dairy-free/gluten-free needs in advance.  

3. **Activities (Keep it Simple!)**:  
   - **Free Play**: Kids can run, play tag, or use the park’s playground.  
   - **Low-Effort Games**:  
     - **Three-Legged Race**: Use her favorite activity!  
     - **Scavenger Hunt**: Create a list of park items to find.  
     - **Decorate Cookies/Cupcakes**: Quick and messy-friendly.  
   - **Parent Help**: Ask parents to organize one game each (e.g., "Mom/Dad Teams Wheelbarrow Race").  

4. **Budget & Logistics**:  
   - **Dues Use**: Allocate a small portion (e.g., $10–$15 per troop) for a cake or activity supplies.  
   - **Shared Costs**: Parents cover potluck dishes; she handles permits if needed (most parks are free).  
   - **No Stress**: Skip complex setups—focus on safety and smiles!  

### 💡 Why This Works:
- **Inclusive**: Lets ice cream/pizza lovers and low-energy kids coexist.  
- **Affordable**: Potluck minimizes her spending.  
- **Flexible**: Parents handle logistics; she can relax while pregnant.  

**Final Tip**: End with a group photo or a "trophy" for participation—celebrating effort over perfection! You’ve got this, and your daughters will love your leadership. 🌟
