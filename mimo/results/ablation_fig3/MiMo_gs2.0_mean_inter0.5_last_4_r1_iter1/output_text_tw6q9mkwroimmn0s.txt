**Ocean-Themed Main Courses:**

1.  **Herb-Crusted Scallops with Lemon-Herb Quinoa:** (Elegant & Mild)
    *   **Theme:** Scallops evoke the ocean floor. Use fresh dill, parsley, chives, lemon zest, and garlic in the crust and quinoa.
    *   **Considerations:** Scallops are mild and delicious. Quinoa is a great low-glycemic grain base. Serve with a light lemon-dill sauce.
2.  **Spicy Coconut Curry with Tofu & Vegetables:** (Spicy & Tropical)
    *   **Theme:** Coconut milk instantly brings an island/ocean vibe. Use vibrant green and yellow veggies.
    *   **Considerations:** Tofu is a great high-protein, low-carb option. Keep the curry flavorful but spicy (adjust heat level), and use low-sugar coconut milk. Serve with cauliflower rice or a large green salad.
3.  **Grilled Salmon with Mango-Jalapeño Salsa:** (Classic & Spicy)
    *   **Theme:** Salmon is quintessentially ocean. The vibrant salsa adds color and a tropical twist.
    *   **Considerations:** Salmon is rich in omega-3s. The mango provides sweetness and fiber, the jalapeño adds spice. Use the salsa sparingly due to sugar content. Serve with roasted asparagus or steamed green beans.
4.  **"Lobster" Ravioli with Brown Butter Sage & Cauliflower Puree:** (Elegant & Indulgent)
    *   **Theme:** Lobster is the ocean's crown jewel! Use a rich cheese filling (like ricotta, parmesan, spinach) and make the pasta look like mini "lobsters" if desired.
    *   **Considerations:** This is rich. Serve with the low-carb cauliflower puree. Brown butter and sage add incredible flavor without needing much sugar. Portion control is key.
5.  **Spicy Shrimp Skewers with Mediterranean Quinoa Salad:** (Bright & Flavorful)
    *   **Theme:** Shrimp are ocean dwellers. Skewers suggest a beach BBQ vibe.
    *   **Considerations:** Use large shrimp (not shellfish, but still ocean-themed!). Marinate in lemon, garlic, olive oil, and a touch of chili flakes. Serve over a colorful quinoa salad with chopped cucumber, cherry tomatoes, Kalamata olives, feta (optional), and a lemon-herb vinaigrette.

**Sweet Ocean-Themed Desserts:**

1.  **Mango Panna Cotta with Fresh Berries & Lime Zest:** (Elegant & Tropical)
    *   **Theme:** Mango is central. Panna cotta is smooth and elegant. Berries add color and freshness.
    *   **Considerations:** Panna cotta is naturally lower in sugar than many desserts. Use full-fat coconut milk for richness. Sweeten with a touch of honey or maple syrup if needed, or rely on ripe mango. Top with fresh berries for fiber and nutrients.
2.  **Dark Chocolate Sea Salt Caramel Pots de Crème:** (Rich & Decadent)
    *   **Theme:** Chocolate is like the deep ocean. Sea salt is the obvious ocean connection.
    *   **Considerations:** Rich and satisfying. Use high-quality dark chocolate (70%+). Make the caramel sauce using a little honey or maple syrup instead of refined sugar. Serve with a sprinkle of flaky sea salt.
3.  **Ocean Berry Parfait:** (Light & Refreshing)
    *   **Theme:** Layers of color resemble ocean depths. Use blueberries, raspberries, and blackberries.
    *   **Considerations:** Naturally low-glycemic and high in antioxidants/fiber. Layer with plain Greek yogurt (high protein, low sugar) or a dollop of thick coconut yogurt. Add a sprinkle of chopped toasted coconut or chia seeds for texture.
4.  **Coconut Chia Pudding with Mango & Lime:** (Healthy & Tropical)
    *   **Theme:** Coconut is the quintessential tropical/indirect ocean ingredient. Mango adds sweetness and color.
    *   **Considerations:** Chia seeds are packed with fiber and omega-3s. Use full-fat coconut milk. Sweeten with fresh mango chunks and a squeeze of lime juice. Top with toasted coconut flakes. Very satisfying and portion-controlled.
5.  **Simple Vanilla Cake with Ocean Sprinkles & Lemon Glaze:** (Classic & Fun)
    *   **Theme:** The "Ocean Sprinkles" (blue and white nonpareils or sanding sugar) instantly add the theme.
    *   **Considerations:** Use a good quality vanilla cake recipe. Sweeten with sugar or a sugar substitute like erythritol. Make a light lemon glaze (lemon juice, powdered sugar substitute, maybe a touch of water) for brightness without heavy sweetness. Keep portions reasonable.
