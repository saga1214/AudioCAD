I see your point, and you're right that 20 percent of the daily limit in a single meal isn't excessive. However, the total sodium for the entire day is what really matters.

If this meal has 450 milligrams of sodium and it's your only meal, you'd still need to account for the sodium from the noodles and the original full recipe. That could easily push your total sodium for the day to over 1,500 milligrams. For most adults, the recommendation is to stay under 2,300 milligrams per day, so that would be quite high.

In short, it's not too much for one meal, but it could be too much for an entire day's intake if it's your only meal.
