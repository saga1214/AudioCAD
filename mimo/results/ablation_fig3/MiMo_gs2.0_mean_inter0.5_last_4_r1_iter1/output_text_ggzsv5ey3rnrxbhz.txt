Okay, so to reduce your exposure, here are the top five products to avoid. First, non-stick cookware, because they often contain PFAS. Second, grease-resistant food packaging, like those wrappers and microwave popcorn bags. Third, anything with stain or water resistance, such as carpets and outdoor gear. Fourth, some cosmetics and even certain types of dental floss. And fifth, since they're in tap water, using a certified water filter is a big help.
