For dinner in Madrid, tapas are the place to be. You should try patatas bravas, which are crispy potatoes with a spicy sauce. There's also fabada, a rich bean stew, and jamón ibérico, which is a fancy cured ham.

But if you're in Philadelphia, you'll find Spanish food in the Mission District, at a place called El Merkury. And for breakfast, you can't go wrong with churros con chocolate. Hope you have a great trip!
