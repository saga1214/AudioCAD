Here are five simple dinner ideas you could whip up from the grocery store. 

First, you could do a quick and easy fajita bowl. Just get some chicken breast, bell peppers, and onions. You can sauté them with a little cumin and chili powder, and serve it all over some pre-cooked rice. Top it with avocado and salsa.

Another idea is a classic tuna melt. You can grab a can of tuna, some mayo, salt, pepper, and shredded cheddar cheese. Just mix the tuna with the mayo in a bowl, spread it on some bread, and melt the cheese on top in a pan.

For a third option, you could make a pasta dish. Grab some pasta, jarred marinara sauce, and a block of either pepperoni or mozzarella. You cook the pasta, drain it, and then just toss it all together with the sauce and the meat or cheese.

Fourth, you could make a simple grilled cheese with tomato soup. You can make a really good grilled cheese sandwich with sourdough, cheddar, and maybe some pesto. Then, you can just heat up some canned tomato soup to have with it.

And finally, for a fifth idea, you could do a stir-fry. Grab some chicken, broccoli, snap peas, and bell peppers. You'll need some soy sauce and a bit of oil. Just stir-fry the chicken, then add the veggies, and pour the soy sauce over it all to finish. Serve it over instant rice.
