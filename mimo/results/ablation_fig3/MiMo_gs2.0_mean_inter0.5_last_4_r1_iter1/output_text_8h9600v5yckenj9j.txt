For candy, you can't go wrong with classics like Reese's Cups, Snickers, and Hershey's Kisses. You might also want to get some sour candies, like Sour Patch Kids, or gummies like Starbursts. If you want something themed but still popular, you could look for pumpkin-shaped chocolates or ghost-shaped candy. Just make sure to check for allergens, especially nuts, since some candies like peanut butter cups have peanuts. You can also get individually wrapped candies to avoid spills, and you could even make a 'trick or treat basket' for guests to pick their favorites. It's always good to have a mix of sweet, sour, and chocolatey options so there’s something for everyone.
