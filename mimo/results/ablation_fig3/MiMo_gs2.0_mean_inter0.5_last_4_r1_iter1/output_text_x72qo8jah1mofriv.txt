Okay, kosher dietary laws. The main thing is no cross-contamination. So, you'll need to be careful about separating any ingredients that might have been handled by non-kosher items. For example, if you're making the blood pudding, you'd need to make sure the oats and spices are certified as kosher. For the healthy snacks like fruit or veggies with hummus, the hummus itself needs to be kosher. Mini wieners are usually fine, but the bacon might be a problem. You'd need to find a kosher-certified bacon or substitute it with something like a kosher sausage. You could also offer a fully kosher snack platter—things like kosher beef jerky, hummus, and maybe some fruit. The wine you suggested, Pinot Noir, should be fine if it's kosher-certified. Just double-check the labels on everything to make sure.
