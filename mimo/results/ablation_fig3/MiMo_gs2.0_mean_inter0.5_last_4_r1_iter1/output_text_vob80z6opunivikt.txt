That's the best feeling—when everything clicks into place! Since you're avoiding sweet/chocolatey snacks, here are some savory vending machine ideas to fill your coffee table:  

### 🍟 **Salty & Crunchy Options**  
- **Potato Chips**: Classic options (Ruffles, Lay’s) or flavored twists (spicy, sour cream & onion).  
- **Pretzels**: Soft pretzel bites, pretzel twists, or mini pretzel rods.  
- **Rice Crackers**: Light and crispy (e.g., Triscuits, Wasabi Pea crackers).  
- **Beet Chips**: Savory veggie crisps (if available!).  

### 🥜 **Nutty & Protein-Packed**  
- **Mixed Nuts**: Trail mix, pistachios, or almonds (check for no chocolate coatings).  
- **Soy Nuts**: Smoky or spicy roasted soybeans.  
- **Peanut Butter Cups (salty)**: Some brands offer PB cups with a savory twist.  

### 🥒 **Fresh & Light**  
- **Veggie Sticks**: Carrot, cucumber, or bell pepper sticks with ranch dip cups.  
- **Olives or Pickles**: Single-serve packs for a tangy kick.  

### 🍞 **Bread & Savory Bites**  
- **Mini Meatballs**: Served with tomato sauce or marinara.  
- **Pretzel Bites**: Often found near chips—perfect for dipping.  
- **Savory Scones**: Some machines have cheese or herb-flavored scones.  

### 🧂 **Extra Savory Touches**  
- **Spice Blends**: Single-serve packets of everything bagel seasoning or chili powder for chips.  
- **Flavored Mustard or Hot Sauce**: For dipping pretzels or veggies.  

### 💡 **Pro Tip**:  
If the vending machine has a **"snack sampler" bag**, it might include savory options like popcorn, cheese puffs, or flavored nuts.
