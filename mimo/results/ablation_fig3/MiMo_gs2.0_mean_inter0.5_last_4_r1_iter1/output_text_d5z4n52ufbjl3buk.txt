For the subject line, something neutral like 'Course Name, Exam Discussion' would work well.

In the email, you can start with a polite greeting and mention that you scored a 64 on the recent essay exam and would like to understand how the score was determined to improve on future work.

Then, you can state that subjective exams can be hard to understand, but you're specifically curious about what aspects of your content could have been improved.

Finally, you can ask if he has time for a brief meeting to discuss this. It's also a good idea to mention that his office hours might conflict with your class schedule, and ask if another time would be convenient.
