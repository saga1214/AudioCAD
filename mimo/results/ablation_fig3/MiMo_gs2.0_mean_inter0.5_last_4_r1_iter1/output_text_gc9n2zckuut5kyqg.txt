That's a great point—keeping it budget-friendly while still being delicious and fun! Here are some dessert ideas that fit your goals and cost:  

### 🍓 Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:  
1. **Fruit Skewers or Parfaits**:  
   - Thread colorful fruits (grapes, berries, melon, pineapple) on skewers.  
   - Layer yogurt, granola, and fresh fruit in cups (great for kids!).  
   - *Cost Tip:* Use seasonal fruits (like summer berries or frozen berries) to save money.  

2. **Simple Baked Goods**:  
   - **Oatmeal Cookies**: Mix oats, mashed banana or applesauce (no added sugar), and cinnamon.  
   - **Muffins**: Use whole-grain flour and add mashed fruit or applesauce for moisture.  
   - *Cost Tip:* Bake in batches—store-bought cookies are expensive!  

3. **Frozen Treats**:  
   - **Fruit Popsicles**: Blend fruit puree (e.g., strawberries, mango) with a splash of juice, then freeze in molds.  
   - **Ice Cream Sandwiches**: Use store-bought cookies with a scoop of yogurt or frozen yogurt.  

4. **DIY Dessert Bar**:  
   - Set up a station with:  
     - **Dips**: Yogurt, nut butter, or chocolate (dark, sugar-free options align with diabetes awareness).  
     - **Toppings**: Granola, coconut flakes, and fresh fruit.  
   - *Cost Tip:* Buy in bulk and let family members bring toppings!  

5. **Kid-Friendly Classics**:  
   - Mini cupcakes (use cake mix for speed, add mashed banana for sweetness).  
   - Rice krispie treats with fruit glaze (instead of candy).  

### 💡 Cost-Saving Tips:  
- **Ask for Contributions**: Let family members donate desserts or baking ingredients.  
- **Use Pantry Staples**: Canned fruit (in juice, not syrup), oats, yogurt, and frozen fruit are cheap and versatile.  
- **Bulk Buy**: Purchase in larger quantities (e.g., frozen berries, whole-grain flour).  
- **Minimize Waste**: Prep desserts ahead and store extras for raffle prizes!  

### 🌟 Alignment with Diabetes Awareness:  
- Label desserts with sugar content or "whole-grain" labels.  
- Offer water/tea alongside sugary drinks.  
- Include a small table with diabetes resources (flyers, brochures).
