Here’s the quick recap of your body’s systems using your car analogy:

- **Heart**: The gas station and engine that pumps blood-cars all around your body.  
- **Lungs**: The refueling stops where blood-cars pick up fresh oxygen "fuel."  
- **Capillaries**: Tiny streets where blood-cars drop off oxygen to your cells.  
- **Veins**: The return roads that carry blood-cars back to the heart/lungs.  
- **Kidneys**: Car washes that filter out waste and create urine.  
- **Liver**: A service station that processes nutrients and breaks down harmful stuff.  

Your blood-cars keep this whole delivery system running smoothly! 🚗💨
