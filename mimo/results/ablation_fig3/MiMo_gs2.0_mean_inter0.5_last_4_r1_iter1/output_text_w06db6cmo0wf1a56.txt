Absolutely! Here’s a practical, low-mess shopping list based on your needs—packed for coolers with grab-and-go portions. All quantities are for **10 people/kids**:

---

### **DRINKS**  
*(Pre-chill water bottles/soda boxes)*  
- **Water bottles**: 20 large bottles (1.5L each)  
- **Sugar-free sodas**: 10 boxes (e.g., Sprite Zero, Coke Zero)  
- **Low-sugar juice boxes**: 10 boxes (for kids)  
- **Infused water**: 5 pitcher-sized containers (add fruit like cucumber, lemon, or berries for flavor)  
- **Ice packs**: 3–4 (to keep everything cold)  

---

### **SNACKS**  
*(Single-serve, no wrappers)*  
- Cheese sticks: 20  
- Grapes: 3 lbs (pre-washed)  
- Baby carrots: 4 lbs (with 4 single-serve hummus cups)  
- Trail mix: 5 bags (individual)  
- Pretzels: 5 bags (mini or individual)  
- Fruit leather pouches: 10  
- Hummus cups: 4 (as mentioned)  
- Protein bars: 10 (optional, e.g., KIND or Clif Bars)  

---

### **MAIN MEALS**  
*(Pre-assembled, easy to eat)*  
- **Mini calzones**: 12 (smaller portions = less mess)  
- **Sandwiches/wraps**: 10 pre-cut sandwiches (e.g., turkey & cheese, hummus & veggies) + wraps (5)  
- **Whole calzones**: 6 (if you want larger ones—pack 1 per dad)  
- **Extra napkins**: 2 rolls per cooler  
- **Trash bags**: 2 per cooler  

---

### **DESSERT**  
*(Grab-and-go, no utensils)*  
- **Sugar-free cupcakes**: 12 (with sugar-free frosting)  
- **Mini brownies**: 10 boxes (individual packs)  
- **Granola bars**: 10 (optional, e.g., RXBAR)  

---

### **KEY LOGISTICS**  
1. **Coolers**: Use 3–4 coolers (one per family unit).  
2. **Labeling**: Mark coolers by dad’s name so kids can grab their snacks.  
3. **Extras**: Pack wet wipes, hand sanitizer, sunscreen, and a small first-aid kit.  
4. **Sweeteners**: Add honey sticks or sugar-free syrup for calzone filling if needed.  

---

### **WHY THIS WORKS**  
- **Minimal mess**: Everything is pre-portioned, sealed, or easy to eat with hands.  
- **Balanced**: Calzones for the groom’s love, mini-calzones/snacks for kids, and sugar-free sodas for adults.  
- **Hydration focus**: Water is prioritized + soda as a treat.  
- **Portability**: No plates, utensils, or large containers to manage.
