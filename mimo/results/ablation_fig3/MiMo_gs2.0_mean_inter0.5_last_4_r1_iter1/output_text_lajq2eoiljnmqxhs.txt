Cooking can be good because it's interactive. To account for dietary issues, you could pick a lactose-free recipe that uses veggies she does like, or at least avoids seafood and poultry. Something simple and quick, like a stir-fry or a pasta dish without cheese, would work. It keeps the interaction focused on a shared task rather than a meal she might dislike. Start with that, and maybe see how it goes before planning anything more involved.
