### **Packaging & Presentation**  
- **"Treasure Chest" Snack Boxes**: Seal sandwiches/cookies in small containers.  
- **Themed Containers**: Use mini mason jars for fruit skewers, or cardboard pirate ship cutouts as placemats.  
- **Labels**: Write "Pirate’s Loot" or "Captain’s Treats" on containers.  

---

### **Allergy-Friendly Swaps**  
- **Instead of watermelon**: Pineapple chunks, grapes, or apple slices.  
- **Gluten-free**: Use GF cookies or pretzels.  
- **Nut-free**: Skip peanut butter; use sunflower seed butter for hot dogs.  

---

### **Optional Extras**  
- **Ice Bucket**: Label as "Frozen Treasure" for drinks.  
- **Pirate Flag**: Hang above the food table for immersion.  
- **Sandcastle Kits**: Include cookies as "treasure" inside.  

---

### **Key Tips to Avoid Sand**  
1. **Individual Portions**: Juice boxes, sealed containers, and skewers prevent spills.  
2. **No Utensils Needed**: All items are grab-and-go.  
3. **Shade Setup**: Use a canopy or tent to keep food/drink dry and away from sand.
