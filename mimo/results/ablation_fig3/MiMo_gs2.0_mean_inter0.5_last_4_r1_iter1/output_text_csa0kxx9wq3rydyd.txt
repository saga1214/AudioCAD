Okay, so you'll need a few things to get everything done. You'll definitely need an oven for smoking the turkey, roasting the root vegetables and stuffing, and baking the mac and cheese. A slow cooker is good for the apple crisp. You'll need a stovetop for the Instant Pot cranberry sauce, and an Instant Pot itself. A toaster oven is perfect for the roasted asparagus. And you'll need a regular pot to make the wild rice salad. So, the cooking methods are smoking, roasting, roasting again, baking, boiling, pressure cooking, air frying, and making a salad. Hope that helps you get everything ready for Thanksgiving!
