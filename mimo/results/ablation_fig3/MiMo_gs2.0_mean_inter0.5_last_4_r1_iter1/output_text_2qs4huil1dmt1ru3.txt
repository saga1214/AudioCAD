Sure, let's break it down task by task. For drywall stuff, the compound is usually five to ten bucks. Sandpaper or sponge is a couple of dollars. A six-inch putty knife is around three to five. Primer and paint depend on the brand, but a small amount should be ten to twenty dollars. Molding, nails, and adhesive could be another ten to fifteen. So drywall total is probably around thirty to fifty dollars.

Cake ingredients are much cheaper. Two cups of flour is about three dollars, sugar two, baking powder less than two, butter about five, eggs three, milk three, vanilla two, frosting stuff another five or so. So the cake shopping is around twenty to twenty-five dollars.

Putting it all together, you’re looking at a total of fifty to seventy-five dollars. I’d say it’s safe to bring around eighty dollars to cover any unexpected things you might find. You can always check the prices at the store, but that should be a good starting point.
