Okay, host here with a comprehensive shopping list for your 24-guest New Year's Eve party. I've organized it into food, drinks, and essentials to make it easier for you to tackle.

First, for food:
Appetizers will need ground pork or kielbasa for sliders, shrimp for the cocktail, ground sausage for pigs in a blanket, butter, garlic, and puff pastry. For assembly, you'll need slider buns, cocktail sauce, cocktail shrimp, and toothpicks.
Main dish for the ham will require a ham, butter, cloves, and some chopped fresh parsley.
For the sides, you'll need potatoes for the scalloped potatoes, carrots and olive oil for the roasted carrots, and asparagus for the roasted asparagus. You'll also need olive oil, lemon juice, and salt for the asparagus.
For dessert, grab a box of cookies and some frosting for decorating.
For drinks, you'll need champagne, grape juice, soda water, ice, and a signature cocktail of your choice, along with its ingredients like vodka, simple syrup, and fruit juice. And don't forget plenty of sparkling cider for the kids' toast flutes.

Next, for drinks and beverages:
You'll need champagne, grape juice, soda water, ice, and any other beverages like soda or beer. Also, consider grabbing some sparkling cider flutes for the kids and non-alcoholic mixers if you're making the signature cocktail.

And for essentials:
For decorations, grab balloons, festive hats, and noisemakers. For serving, you'll need plenty of plates, cups, utensils, and napkins. For cooking, make sure you have oven mitts and a cutting board. And for cleanup, plastic bags and trash cans will be your best friends.
