Here’s a balanced 9-day itinerary covering Tokyo, Osaka, Nara, and Kyoto, designed for deep exploration while accounting for your holiday schedule and coursework needs:

### **Tokyo (4 Days)**
- **Day 1**: Arrival + Asakusa (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Street)  
  *Evening*: Akihabara (electronics/culture)  
- **Day 2**: Shibuya (Shibuya Crossing, Hachiko Statue) + Harajuku (Takeshita Street)  
  *Afternoon*: Meiji Shrine (Shinjuku Gyoen)  
- **Day 3**: Day trip to Nikko (Toshogu Shrine, Kegon Falls)  
  *Evening*: Shibuya Sky sunset views  
- **Day 4**: Ueno Park (museums, temples) + Asakusa revisit  
  *Workout*: Morning gym session at hotel  

### **Osaka (2 Days)**  
- **Day 5**: Osaka Castle + Dotonbori (street food, neon signs)  
  *Evening*: Shinsekai (retro district)  
- **Day 6**: Nara Day Trip (Todai-ji Temple, Nara Park deer)  
  *Evening*: Return to Osaka, explore Kuromon Market  

### **Kyoto (2 Days)**  
- **Day 7**: Kyoto (Fushimi Inari Shrine, Gion district)  
  *Afternoon*: Arashiyama (bamboo forest, monkey park)  
- **Day 8**: Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) + Ryoan-ji (rock garden)  
  *Evening*: Return to Osaka  

### **Key Tips**  
1. **Work/Coursework Integration**:

2. **Travel Logistics**:  
   - **Tokyo → Osaka**: Shinkansen (2.5 hours). Book via JR Pass if staying >7 days.

3. **Art History Focus**:

4. **Taxi Costs in Osaka**:  
   - Short trips: ¥500–1,500 (e.g., to Nara).  
   - Long trips: ¥3,000+ (e.g., to Kyoto). Use apps like **ETC** or **Bee App** for cashless payments.
