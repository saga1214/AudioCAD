### **Vodka Mixer** (Soda-Free & Refreshing)
1. **Gin & Vodka Tonic**  
   - 2 oz vodka + 2 oz gin + 1 oz tonic water + ice  
   - *Why it works:* Classic, balanced, and crowd-pleasing. Gin adds botanical depth without overpowering the vodka. Garnish with cucumber ribbon or lime wheel.  
   - *Time save:* Prep tonic water in advance.

2. **Cucumber-Mint Vodka Spritzer**  
   - Muddle 2 cucumber slices + 5 mint leaves  
   - Add 2 oz vodka, 1 oz lime juice, 3 oz sparkling water (if *strictly* no soda, use plain water + ice)  
   - *Why it works:* Light, refreshing, and visually appealing. No soda needed—sparkling water keeps it soda-free.

---

### **Argentinian-Style Fernet** (Soda-Free)
**Fernet & Beer Shot** (The Classic):  
- 1 oz Fernet-Branca + 2 oz light beer (e.g., Stella Artois, Corona)  
- *Why it works:* Mimics the Argentine *Fernet y Cerveza* but skips soda. The beer mellows Fernet’s bitterness, creating a bold yet approachable shot. Garnish with a lime wedge.  

**Fernet & Soda Water** (Lighter Option):  
- 1 oz Fernet-Branca + 3 oz soda water + 1 oz lime juice  
- *Why it works:* Keeps the herbal kick but dilutes it with bubbles. Add a twist of lime.

- **Allergy Safe:** Both vodka and Fernet recipes avoid soda entirely.
