Okay, this is a great opportunity to create a clear, step-by-step guide for you. Here’s a standard resume format that's both professional and easy to create.

First, at the very top, you'll have your name in about a fourteen-point font. Right below that, in a nine-point font, you can have a horizontal line to separate it from the contact info. Then, list your contact details in this order: your email, then your phone number, and finally your city. You can use simple periods or small icons for these, but definitely avoid the larger icons.

Next, right under your name, is your professional summary. This is a short paragraph, about three to five sentences, where you highlight your most relevant skills and career goals. This is the perfect place to address any potential gaps in your work history.

After the summary, you'll have your Experience section. This is where you list your jobs. For each job, include the company name, your job title, the start and end dates, and then a short bullet point for each key accomplishment. If you have multiple roles at the same company, just list them as separate entries with the company name only once at the top.

Then comes your Education section. Here, you'll list your degree, the school's name, your expected graduation date, and any honors you received, like 'Summa Cum Laude' or the name of a scholarship. You can also list any other degrees from that school if you want.

If you have significant work experience that doesn't quite fit under your main experience section, you can create a 'Additional Experience' section, following the same format.

After that, you'll have a Skills section. This is very important. List your most relevant skills, and try to match them to the job description. You can group them into categories like 'Technical Skills' or 'Languages'.

Finally, you can add an optional 'Other' section for things like awards, certifications, or volunteer work. Just keep it brief and only include the most impressive items.

Remember to use consistent formatting throughout, like a standard font and a clean one-inch margin. And before you send it, make sure to proofread it very carefully.
