Here are 5 safe green vegetables for your parrot (remembering Bruno avoids broccoli):

1.  **Spinach:** Rich in vitamins A, C, and K, iron, and fiber. *Chop finely* to avoid choking.
2.  **Kale:** Packed with vitamins A, C, K, calcium, and antioxidants. *Remove tough stems* and chop leaves.
3.  **Cucumber:** Hydrating, low-calorie, and easy to digest. *Wash thoroughly* and offer slices or chunks.
4.  **Swiss Chard:** Similar to spinach, high in vitamins, minerals, and fiber. *Wash well* and chop stems/leaves.
5.  **Romaine Lettuce:** Safe, crunchy, and provides hydration and some vitamins/minerals. A great starter veggie.

**Important Notes:**

*   **Always Wash:** Thoroughly wash all vegetables before feeding to remove dirt, pesticides, or bacteria.
*   **Introduce Gradually:** Offer new veggies one at a time, in small amounts, to monitor for any digestive upset.
*   **Avoid Choking Hazards:** Chop or shred larger vegetables like chard and spinach.
*   **Not Broccoli:** As you mentioned, Bruno avoids broccoli due to his sensitivity.
*   **Fruits are Different:** Apples (with skin removed and seeds removed) and bananas are safe fruits, but these are vegetables.
