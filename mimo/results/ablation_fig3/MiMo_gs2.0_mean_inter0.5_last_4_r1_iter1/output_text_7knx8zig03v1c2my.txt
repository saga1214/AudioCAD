### 🍬 **Treat Recommendations**  
The best treats are high-value rewards (things your puppy *really* loves). Consider these flavors/flavors:  
- **Soft & Melt-in-Mouth:** Peanut butter (xylitol-free!), cheese, cooked chicken, or yogurt.  
- **Harder Treats:** Freeze-dried liver, chicken, or beef bits (great for training rewards).  
- **Commercial Treats:** Puppy-specific training treats (like "Pup-Peroni" or "Ziwi Peak").  
- **Italian Flavors:** Sunflower seeds, olives, or even small pieces of bread with olive oil (if he likes them).  

**Pro Tip:** Use tiny pieces (pea-sized) for training to avoid overfeeding. Always pair treats with praise and pets!

---

### 🎯 **Clicker Training Explained**  
Clicker training uses a 3-step process:  
1. **The Clicker:** A simple, handheld clicker (or even a pen cap).  
2. **The Marker:** When he does something right (e.g., sits), *click* immediately. The click = "YES, YOU DID IT!"  
3. **The Reward:** Give a treat *right after* the click. Over time, he learns that "click" = "reward is coming!"  

**How It Works:**  
- Click → Treat. Repeat until he understands the command.  
- Gradually add distractions (e.g., walk outside before rewarding).  
- Phase out the clicker after mastery: Use a verbal cue ("Good sit!") and just praise/treat.  

**Why It Works:** The click is an objective marker—dogs understand it better than verbal praise alone.  

---

### 🛠️ **Training Tools Recap**  
| **Tool**          | **Purpose**                                                                 |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Treats**        | Positive reinforcement (see above).                                        |
| **Clicker**       | Marks the exact moment he does the right thing.                             |
| **Leash/Harness** | Practice recall, heel, and off-leash skills.                               |
| **Chew Toys**     | Tease anxiety (e.g., Kong stuffed with peanut butter).                     |

**Key Tip:** Keep training sessions short (5-10 mins), fun, and positive! 🐶✨
