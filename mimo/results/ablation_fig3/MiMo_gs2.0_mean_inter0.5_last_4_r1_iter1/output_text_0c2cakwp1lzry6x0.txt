Absolutely! Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your TV mount, hanging planters, and floating shelves, keeping safety and practicality in mind:

### 📺 1. **TV Mount Installation**
   * **Tools Needed:** Level, stud finder, drill, appropriate drill bits (for wall type), screws, wall anchors (if needed), TV mount hardware.
   * **Steps:**
     1. **Check Weight & VESA:** Confirm your mount's weight limit and VESA rating (measured in mm) match your TV's specs. This tells you how thick your wall is and which screws are needed.
     2. **Find Studs:** Use a stud finder. Mounts require heavy-duty screws driven into studs (wood or metal framing) for support. If no studs are available, use heavy-duty toggle bolts or molly bolts rated for your mount's weight.
     3. **Mark & Level:** Hold the mount bracket against the wall, use a level to ensure it's perfectly horizontal, and mark screw hole positions.
     4. **Drill Pilot Holes:** Drill holes slightly smaller than your screws or anchors.
     5. **Install Anchors/Screws:** Drive anchors or screws into the holes. Ensure they're tight and flush.
     6. **Attach Mount:** Secure the mount bracket to the wall using the screws/anchors.
     7. **Mount TV:** Align the TV's mounting holes with the mount and secure it firmly.
     8. **Test:** Ensure the TV is level and the mount feels solid.

### 🌿 2. **Hanging Planters**
   * **Tools Needed:** Drill, appropriate drill bits (for wall type), wall anchors (if needed), hook, wire, hanging basket.
   * **Steps:**
     1. **Choose Location:** Pick a spot above a table or shelf where the planter will hang safely.
     2. **Find Anchor Point:** Use a stud finder or drill a small pilot hole to test if you hit a ceiling joist (strongest option). If not, use heavy-duty toggle bolts or molly bolts rated for the planter's weight + soil/water.
     3. **Mark & Drill:** Mark the hook position, drill a hole slightly larger than the anchor, and tap the anchor in.
     4. **Install Hook:** Secure the hook firmly into the anchor with a screw.
     5. **Hang Planter:** Thread a wire through the planter's hanger, loop it over the hook, and secure the top. Ensure it's stable and won't swing dangerously.
     6. **Water Access:** Plan to water plants carefully to avoid dripping onto furniture below.

### 🪜 3. **Floating Shelves**
   * **Tools Needed:** Level, stud finder, drill, appropriate drill bits (for wall type), wall anchors (if needed), shelf bracket hardware.
   * **Steps:**
     1. **Measure Twice:** Decide shelf length and position. Measure from the top of the wall below to the desired shelf height. Use a level to ensure the line is straight.
     2. **Find Studs:** Locate studs along the measurement line. Mounting into studs is ideal for heavy shelves.
     3. **Mark & Level:** Mark stud positions and shelf edges. Hold a level against the marks to ensure they are level.
     4. **Drill Pilot Holes:** Drill holes slightly smaller than your shelf bracket screws or anchors.
     5. **Install Anchors/Screws:** Drive anchors or screws into the holes. Ensure they're tight and flush.
     6. **Attach Shelf Brackets:** Secure the brackets to the wall using the screws/anchors. Adjust so the shelf sits level.
     7. **Place Shelf:** Slide the shelf into the brackets and secure it firmly with the provided screws or clips.
     8. **Test:** Gently push on the shelf to ensure it's solid and doesn't wobble.

### 🛠️ **Key Tips for All Installations**
* **Know Your Walls:** Use a stud finder to identify types (drywall, plaster, brick, concrete). Concrete requires special anchors.
* **Weight Matters:** Always use anchors rated for the *total* weight (TV + mount, planter + soil + water, shelf + items).
* **Level is Essential:** Use a level constantly. A crooked mount, shelf, or planter looks bad and can be unstable.
* **Safety First:** Wear safety glasses. Use a step stool or ladder securely placed.
* **Read Instructions:** TV mounts and shelf brackets come with specific hardware guides. Follow them!
* **Plan Ahead:** Measure and mark carefully before drilling. Drywall anchors are much harder to remove cleanly than drilling into studs.

**Once installed, your space will look amazing!** Just take breaks during installation to stretch and avoid stiffness. Happy decorating! 🎨
