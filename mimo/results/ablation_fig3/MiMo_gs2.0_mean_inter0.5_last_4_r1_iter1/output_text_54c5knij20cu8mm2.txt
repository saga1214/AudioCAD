### 2. Debunking the Left/Right Brain Dichotomy  
- **It’s largely a myth**: Neuroimaging shows that *all* cognitive tasks involve *both* hemispheres simultaneously.  
- **What’s true**:  
  - The *right* hemisphere often handles *holistic* tasks (spatial reasoning, creativity).  
  - The *left* hemisphere often handles *linear* tasks (language, logic).  
- **Key nuance**: These are *tendencies*, not rigid rules. Most tasks require integration of both.  

### 3. Addressing Your Tinnitus  
- **Physiological causes** are most common:  
  - Noise exposure (e.g., concerts, machinery).  
  - Age-related hearing loss.  
  - Earwax blockages.  
  - Temporomandibular disorder (jaw issues).  
- **Psychological factors** (e.g., stress) can *amplify* tinnitus but rarely cause it.  
- **Action steps**:  
  1. Rule out physical causes (audiologist check).  
  2. Use white noise apps or masking sounds.  
  3. Stress reduction (mindfulness, therapy).
