That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour Beach Resort offers privacy, direct beach access, and stunning views. Here are some great **pressure-change-free** activities for your day in St. Thomas:

### 🌊 **Snorkeling & Marine Life**
1. **Coki Beach Trail (St. John)**:  
   A short walk from Secret Harbour, this easy, flat trail leads to a protected reef teeming with tropical fish, sea turtles, and soft corals. The water is calm and shallow—perfect for your brother.  
2. **Trunk Bay (St. Thomas)**:  
   A 30-minute boat ride away, this is one of the Caribbean’s top snorkeling spots. The underwater observatory offers glass-bottom viewing, and the reef is shallow, vibrant, and accessible.  
3. **Sapphire Spring (St. Thomas)**:  
   A natural spring with crystal-clear, shallow water—ideal for beginners. You’ll spot parrotfish, grunts, and possibly a sea turtle.

### 🚤 **Boating & Kayaking**
- **Kayak at Coki Beach**: Rent kayaks at Secret Harbour and paddle to nearby coves. Gentle waves and flat terrain make it safe.  
- **Glass-Bottom Boat Tours**: Companies like **Virgin Islands National Seashore Cruises** offer shallow, scenic cruises (no pressure changes!) to Maho Bay or Salt River Bay.

### 🌅 **Beach & Relaxation**
- **Secret Harbour’s Beach**: Spend the day lounging on powdery white sand. The resort’s pool and cabanas offer shade.  
- **Trunk Bay Beach (St. Thomas)**: Famous for its turquoise water and underwater snorkeling without wetsuits.  
- **Coki Beach (St. John)**: Less crowded, with a laid-back vibe and great snorkeling right off the shore.

### 🍽️ **Daytime Food & Drink**
- **Duffy’s Love Shack (Red Hook)**: Try their famous happy hour (4-7 PM) for live music, tacos, and craft cocktails.  
- **Coco’s (Cruz Bay)**: A casual spot with fresh seafood tacos, ceviche, and local brews.  
- **Red Hook Food Market**: Grab a snack and explore the vibrant market scene.

### 🌅 **Sunset & Evening**
- **The Longboard (Red Hook)**: Legendary for sunset views and live bands. Their happy hour is lively and affordable.  
- **Duffy’s Love Shack**: If you missed happy hour, their dinner service is also great for a relaxed vibe.

### ⚠️ **Key Tips for Your Brother**
- **Avoid Depth Changes**: Stick to shallow areas (under 5 feet deep) and gradual entry points.  
- **Hydrate & Cool Down**: Virgin Islands heat can be intense—use reef-safe sunscreen and drink plenty of water.

**Pro Tip**: Rent a car for day trips! You can drive to **Salt River Bay National Park** (with its easy trails and shallow streams) or **Cruz Bay** for local shopping and coffee.
