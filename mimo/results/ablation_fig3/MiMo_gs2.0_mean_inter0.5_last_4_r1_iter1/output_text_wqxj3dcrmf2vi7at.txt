### 🧊 **Homemade Homemade Treats**
1. **Freeze-Dried Pumpkin Bites**  
   *Mix mashed pumpkin with a tiny bit of peanut butter, roll into small balls, freeze for 2+ hours.*  
2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**  
   *Mix 1 cup rolled oats + ½ cup xylitol-free peanut butter. Roll into balls, flatten, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15 mins. Cool completely.*  
3. **Sweet Potato Chews**  
   *Slice sweet potatoes thinly, bake until tender (45-60 mins). Cut into tiny strips or cubes.*  
4. **Yogurt Drops**  
   *Mix Greek yogurt + a pinch of oats or peanut butter. Drop spoonfuls on parchment, freeze.*  
5. **Cheese Cubes**  
   *Grate mild cheddar or mozzarella, freeze into small cubes (avoid aged cheeses).*

### 📌 **Key Safety Tips**  
- **Portion Control:** Treats should be <10% of daily calories.  
- **Avoid:** Xylitol (in peanut butter), grapes, raisins, onions, chocolate, bones.  
- **Size:** Always cut homemade treats into pea-sized pieces.  
- **Freeze for Fun:** Many homemade options freeze well for teething or hot days!
