Here's a step-by-step guide to install your TV, hanging planters, and floating shelves—prioritizing safety, efficiency, and landlord-compliance (no loud drilling!):

### 🛠️ 1. **Mounting the TV**  
- **Use a VESA wall mount** (compatible with your TV’s weight).  
- **Non-drilling alternative**: Command Strips (e.g., Command™ TV Mount). Test weight limits first—ideal for lightweight TVs under 33 lbs.  
- **If drilling is allowed**:  
  - Use a stud finder to locate wall anchors.  
  - Secure the mount with anchors and screws.  
  - Attach the TV to the mount.  
- **Cord management**: Use cable ties or a cord organizer to run wires behind furniture.  

### 🌿 2. **Hanging Planters**  
- **Adhesive hooks**: Command™ Picture Hanging Hooks (rated for 10–20 lbs). Test adhesion on a scrap wall area first.  
- **Suction cups**: For non-porous surfaces (glass, tile). Ensure a tight seal.  
- **Lightweight option**: Hang plants on a floating shelf (see below) or use a macramé hanger on a wall hook.  
- **Safety tip**: Avoid overloading—choose 1–2 plants per planter to prevent tipping.  

### 📊 3. **Installing Floating Shelves**  
- **No-drill method**: Use **heavy-duty adhesive strips** (e.g., 3M™ Command Shelf Strips rated for 20–40 lbs).  
  - Clean the wall surface with rubbing alcohol first.  
  - Press firmly for 30 seconds. Test weight before adding art.  
- **If drilling is permitted**:  
  - Use wall anchors (e.g., plastic or toggle bolts) for heavy items.  
  - Place shelves horizontally for stability.  
  - Anchor at both ends for support.  
- **Decorate**: Place art pieces, plants, or small decor on shelves—balance weight distribution.  

### ⚠️ Safety Tips  
- **Weight limits**: Always check adhesive strip/planch hook capacities.  
- **Wall type**: Adhesives work best on drywall; avoid porous surfaces.  
- **Tools**: Use a step stool for high shelves—no ladders!  

### 🎨 Pro Decoration Tips  
- **Theme**: Choose a color palette (e.g., neutral with one bold accent) to unify pieces.  
- **Art grouping**: Cluster similar art (e.g., by size or color) on one wall for impact.  
- **Lighting**: Add LED strip lights under shelves or a floor lamp for ambiance.
