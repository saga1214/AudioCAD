Alright, let's reorganize all those delicious homemade and store-bought treat ideas for your brown-and-white princess, Princess! Home-baked options are great for knowing exactly what's in them. For homemade, think mashed pumpkin frozen in tiny bites, peanut butter and oat cookies (double-checking for that dangerous sweetener xylitol!), cooked plain chicken, sweet potato sticks, or even small carrot sticks. They're simple, healthy-ish, portion-controlled snacks.

For grab-and-go store-bought, brands like Blue Buffalo, Wellness, and Zuke's offer a variety of treats. Merrick is another good one. Look for brands like Greenies for dental health or others focused on whole ingredients. Just scan the labels for wholesome ingredients.

Teaching "sit" is a classic start! You lure her nose with a treat, move it slowly up and over her head – she'll naturally sit to follow it. Say "sit!" the moment her bottom touches the floor, give the treat, and shower her with praise! Consistency is everything.

Potty training takes patience. Stick to a schedule: take her out first thing, after eating, after playing, and before bed. Reward heavily with praise and tiny treats every single time she goes outside. Accidents happen; clean them thoroughly to remove scent cues. Punishment only teaches fear.

If Princess barks at Felix, try to redirect her attention before it starts – maybe with a favorite toy or a different command like "sit." Or teach a "quiet" command: lure her nose up, say "quiet," reward the moment she stops, then release her to get the treat. Stay calm and consistent.

For collars on brown and white Princess, colors that make a splash are teal/turquoise or soft pink or royal blue!
