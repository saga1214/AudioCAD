Sure, for drinks, you could try green tea, which has antioxidants. Or a turmeric latte for its anti-inflammatory benefits. There's also golden milk, which is similar and good for digestion. For a fruity option, a smoothie is always great. And for something refreshing, cucumber water is perfect.

For snacks, I'd suggest hummus with veggies, like carrots or cucumbers. Or avocado toast on whole grain bread. You could also have a trail mix with nuts and dried fruit. Greek yogurt with honey is another good one. And finally, some roasted chickpeas are a crunchy and protein-packed choice.

These should give you some energy and keep you full!
