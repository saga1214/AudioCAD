Here's a comprehensive guide to making a house baby-proof, focusing on **core safety principles** and **actionable steps**:

### 🛡️ **Core Safety Principles**
1. **The "One-Year Rule"**: Remove or secure anything that could pose a risk to a **1-year-old** (e.g., sharp edges, chemicals, small parts).
2. **Create "Baby Zones"**: Secure areas where baby spends time (e.g., nursery, playroom).
3. **Check Regularly**: Baby-proofing evolves as baby grows (revisit every 2-3 months).

### 🔒 **Critical Hazards to Address**
| **Category**       | **Hazards**                                                                 | **Solutions**                                                                 |
|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Falls**           | Stairs, high furniture, window falls, uneven flooring                       | Safety gates, padding furniture, window locks, secure rugs with non-slip pads |
| **Choking**         | Small objects (coins, batteries), cords, food (choking hazards), trash bins | Childproof containers, secure cords, cut food into small pieces              |
| **Chemicals**       | Cleaning supplies, medications, paint, pesticides, electronics batteries    | Lock up in cabinets, store out of reach, use non-toxic products              |
| **Fire/Smoke**      | Space heaters, unattended cooking, smoke detectors                         | Install smoke alarms, keep heaters away from crib, never leave cooking unattended |
| **Electrical**      | Power strips, outlets, appliances, chargers                                | Outlet covers, cord organizers, unplug unused chargers                      |
| **Strangulation**   | Curtains, cords, air fresheners, strollers, toy parts                       | Secure window blinds, hang items out of reach, avoid dangling cords           |

### 🧹 **Step-by-Step Checklist**
1. **楼梯 & 窗户 (Stairs & Windows)**  
   - Install **top and bottom safety gates** on stairs.  
   - Use **window locks** (reinforced ones for second floors).  
   - Remove window screens if broken.  

2. **家具 (Furniture)**  
   - Anchor **all furniture** to walls (bookcases, TVs, dressers).  
   - Use **furniture pads** on sharp edges.  
   - Keep cribs/rockers in safe, flat areas.  

3. **储藏室 (Storage Areas)**  
   - **Lock cabinets** with chemicals, medications, and cleaning supplies.  
   - Use **childproof containers** for small items (batteries, toys, jewelry).  
   - Secure trash cans with lids.  

4. **厨房 (Kitchen)**  
   - Install **appliance guards** on knives, scissors, and blenders.  
   - Lock pantry doors with **childproof latches**.  
   - Never leave hot liquids unattended.  

5. **浴室 (Bathroom)**  
   - Use **grab bars** for stability.  
   - Install **shower curtains with clips** to prevent tearing.  
   - Keep medicine cabinet locked.  

6. **电器 (Electronics)**  
   - **Unplug chargers** when not in use.  
   - Use **outlet covers** (ensure they’re compliant with safety standards).  
   - Keep cords **out of reach** with cord organizers.  

### 💡 **Pro Tips**
- **Test everything**: Ensure gates are sturdy and latches are child-resistant.  
- **Avoid baby gates as barriers**: They shouldn’t block exits or create unsafe spaces.  
- **Childproofing isn’t one-time**: Revisit every 2-3 months as baby crawls, walks, or crawls on furniture.  
- **Don’t forget the "hidden" hazards**:  
  - **Air fresheners** (remove from crib area).  
  - **Plastic toys** with small parts (replace if cracked).  
  - **Car seat straps** (store safely to prevent choking).  

### 🚨 **Critical Reminders**
- **Never baby-proof at the expense of adult access**: Ensure gates don’t block exits.  
- **Supervision is irreplaceable**: No device can fully replace watching your child.  
- **Prioritize high-risk areas**: Focus first on stairs, windows, and small objects.
