### 1. **Decentralization (The Trust Mechanism)**  
   - Bitcoin's blockchain is maintained by a global network of nodes and miners, eliminating reliance on any central authority.  
   - This ensures no single entity can alter transaction history or manipulate supply—making the ledger **immutable and transparent**.

### 2. **Fixed Supply (The Economic Foundation)**

- **Scarcity validates decentralization**: The fixed supply incentivizes network participation (via block rewards) and ensures long-term security.  

### The Result: **"Hard Money"**  
Bitcoin’s true innovation is creating **digital scarcity**—a scarce, censorship-resistant asset where supply rules are enforced by **mathematics, not humans**.
