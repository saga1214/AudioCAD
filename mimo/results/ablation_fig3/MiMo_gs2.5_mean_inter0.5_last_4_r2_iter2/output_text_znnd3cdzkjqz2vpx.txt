Just a few final things to check before you start:

First, make sure you have some strong screwdrivers, both Phillips and flathead, because Habitats restore cabinets often come with older hardware.

Second, you'll need some long screws, maybe two and a half to three inches, to secure the cabinets into the studs. A hammer or a mallet is also helpful for tapping things into place.

And lastly, a stud finder that can show you where the studs are from behind the wall can be really useful.
