### **Daytime Segment (20 miles, daylight/early evening)**
1. **Base Layer**: Moisture-wicking shirt & shorts (avoid cotton).  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece or light puffy jacket (removeable).  
3. **Outer Layer**: Wind/water-resistant shell (e.g., rain jacket or breathable vest).  
4. **Hands/Head**: Gloves (for grip/warmth), warm hat (to retain heat).  
5. **Footwear**: Sturdy trail shoes with aggressive grip for hills.  
6. **Lighting**: Headlamp (with **extra batteries**) + handheld light (optional).  
7. **Storage**: Compact running vest or small pack (to stash/removeable layers).  
8. Aid stations will handle nutrition/water; bring a handheld bottle if you prefer.

### **Night Segment (Final 12 miles, darkness)**
1. **Base Layer**: Same as daytime (wicking top/shorts).  
2. **Mid-Layer**: **Essential**—fleece or insulated puffy (add if he sheds his daytime layers).  
3. **Outer Layer**: Wind-resistant shell (may need it if damp).  
4. **Hands/Head**: Gloves + hat + buff/gaiter (wrap neck/hands if cold).  
5. **Footwear**: Same shoes (hills still present).  
6. **Lighting**: Headlamp (batteries charged!) + handheld light (add light cover if needed).  
7. **Storage**: Vest/pack (critical for layer management).  
8. **Extra Warmth**: Light insulated jacket (e.g., 50°F-rated) if temps drop significantly.  

### **Key Cross-Segment Essentials**
- **Battery Backup**: Keep spare batteries for lights (night pacing = low visibility).  
- **Buff/Gaiter**: Versatile for warmth, sweat absorption, or face protection.  
- **Simplicity**: Avoid bulk—wear removable layers and use the vest for storage.
