</Acoustic guitars usually tune to a sequence of four main notes, going from the thickest string to the thinnest. That sequence is E, A, D, G, B, and then E again. So you want to tune each of those strings to that exact pitch.

With whichever tool you choose, you strum a string and watch or listen for feedback. When the note gets closer to its target pitch, the indicator on the app or tuner will start to sound better, usually going from red to green or a pleasant chime. You just keep adjusting the tuning peg on the headstock until it's perfect.
