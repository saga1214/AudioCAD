Here's a clear, list-style recipe for each cocktail you're hosting, with measurements and any special notes like the dry shaking for egg white if you decide to use it.

### Old Fashioned  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz bourbon or rye  
  - 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup *(1:1 sugar/water mix)*  
  - 3–4 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist for garnish  
- **Instructions:**  
  1. Muddle the sugar cube with a splash of bitters and water in the bottom of a mixing glass until dissolved.  
  2. Add bourbon, fill with ice, and stir well until chilled.  
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.  
  4. Express an orange twist over the drink (squeezing oils into the air) and rub it around the rim before dropping it in.

### Paper Plane  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz bourbon *(e.g., Buffalo Trace)*  
  - ¾ oz Aperol  
  - ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Instructions:**  
  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker.  
  2. Add ice and shake vigorously for 15 seconds until well-chilled.  
  3. Strain into a **coupe glass** (chilled is best).  

---

### Whiskey Sour *(with/without egg white)*  
**Classic (without egg white):**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- Cherry or orange slice for garnish  
- **Instructions:** Shake with ice, strain into a rocks glass.  

**With Egg White (traditional but optional):**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup *(reduces sugar for balance)*  
- 1 fresh egg white  
- **Instructions:**  
  1. **Dry shake:** Add bourbon, lemon juice, simple syrup, and egg white to a shaker. Shake **without ice** until frothy (~15 seconds).  
  2. **Add ice:** Fill shaker with ice, shake again for 10–12 seconds until very cold.  
  3. Strain into a coupe or rocks glass over ice.  
  - *Tip: Use pasteurized eggs if concerned about safety.*  

---

### Aviation  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz gin *(e.g., London dry gin)*  
  - ¾ oz maraschino liqueur *(Luxardo preferred)*  
  - ¼ oz crème de violette  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Instructions:** Shake all ingredients with ice, strain into a **cocktail glass**, and garnish with a maraschino cherry.
