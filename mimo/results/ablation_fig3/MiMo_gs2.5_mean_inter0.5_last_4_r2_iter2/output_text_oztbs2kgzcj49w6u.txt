That spy story can definitely pique a traveler's curiosity! 

For a great introduction to Lockport's deep connection with the Erie Canal, the best place to start is the Lockport Canal Museum. It's located right at the site of the locks and gives a wonderful overview of the entire system's history.

Another wonderful spot is the Lockport Historical Society, which is just a short walk from the museum. They specialize in the city's history and have a fantastic collection of old photos and documents that bring the story of the canal to life. They also have a lovely, quiet park in their building.
