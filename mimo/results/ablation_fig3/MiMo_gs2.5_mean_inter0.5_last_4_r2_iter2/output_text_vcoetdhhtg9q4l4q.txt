For a quick dinner, I'd suggest a **stir-fry**—it’s fast, uses minimal ingredients, and matches your Japanese food love! Here’s a simple version:

### Quick Stir-Fry Recipe:
1. **Protein & Veggies**:  
   - 100g chicken/tofu/shrimp (pre-cooked or pan-seared quickly).  
   - 1 cup mixed veggies (e.g., bell peppers, broccoli, carrots—pre-cut or frozen).  
   - 1–2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, splash of sesame oil.  

2. **Cook It**:  
   - Heat oil in a pan. Sauté ginger-garlic paste for 30 seconds.  
   - Add protein, cook 2 mins. Toss in veggies, stir-fry 3–4 mins until crisp-tender.  
   - Add sauces, stir, and serve over rice (or your rice noodle!) or greens.  

### Why It Fits Your Decluttering:  
- Uses **fewer spices** (just soy, sesame oil, basics).  
- Requires **minimal gadgets** (one pan + chopsticks/spatula).  
- No fancy tools—just chop, stir, and enjoy!
