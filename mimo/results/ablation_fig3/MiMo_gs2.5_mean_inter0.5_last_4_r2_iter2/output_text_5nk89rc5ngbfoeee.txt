For your end-of-year celebration, a **park BBQ/sunada/pizza party** is a fantastic idea—it’s fun, flexible, and lets you cater to the girls' preferences. Here’s how to make it work smoothly:

### **Logistics & Cost-Saving Tips**
1. **Parent Coordination**: 
   - **Ask parents to sign up for shifts** (e.g., 2 parents bring food, 1 sets up games). This shares the workload.  
   - **Dues Structure**: Charge a small annual fee ($10–$20) for supplies (badge materials, BBQ coals, etc.). Clearly state parents cover sashes/patches.  

2. **Food Strategy**:  
   - **Sunada bar** (homemade ice cream sundae station) is affordable, interactive, and loved by all ages.  
   - **Pizza** works for larger groups—order from a local pizzeria or have parents split the cost.  
   - **Snack Mixers**: Include simple games like a three-legged race or sack race to keep energy high.  

3. **Safety & Practicality**:  
   - Choose a **local park** with restrooms/shade.  
   - **Weather Backup**: Have a rain plan (e.g., cafeteria or community hall).  
   - **Pregnancy Prep**: Bring a cooler for your snacks (dairy-free options like fruit cups or hummus).  

### **Badge Integration**
Tie field trips to badges early:  
- **Community Helper Trip**: Visit fire/police stations → *Community Helpers Badge*.  
- **Nature Walk** → *Outdoor Adventure Badge*.  
- **Camping Trip**: Start with a one-night trip to a nearby campsite. Focus on basics (tents, cooking) → *Camping Badge*.  

### **Why This Works**
- **Low Pressure**: Parents sharing responsibilities eases your pregnancy burden.  
- **Bonding**: Shared food and games build troop camaraderie.  
- **Scalable**: BBQs/sunadas are budget-friendly and scalable for bigger groups.  

**Bottom Line**: Go with the park BBQ! It’s festive, inclusive, and sets a joyful tone for your first year. You’ll do great—Brownies thrive on fun, not perfection.
