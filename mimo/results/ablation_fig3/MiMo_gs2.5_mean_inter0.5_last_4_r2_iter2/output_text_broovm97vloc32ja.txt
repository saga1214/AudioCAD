That's fantastic! Self-publishing your own illustrated picture book is a very viable and powerful option. In fact, many children's books are self-published by the author-illustrator. The process is to create a complete, polished manuscript and a set of illustrations. Then, you can use a professional self-publishing platform like Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, or even hire a specialized publisher who focuses on children's books.

There is a significant upfront investment of time and money, but it gives you complete creative and financial control. The platform will then handle the printing and distribution, making it possible to get your book into physical bookstores, libraries, and online retailers.
