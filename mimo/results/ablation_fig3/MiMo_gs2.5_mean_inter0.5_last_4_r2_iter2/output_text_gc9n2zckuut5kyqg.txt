Absolutely—here’s a **budget-friendly dessert plan** that’s kid-friendly, awareness-conscious, and cost-effective:

### 🍎 Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:
1. **Fruit Skewers & Salad**:  
   - Skewer grapes, berries, pineapple, and melon chunks. Kids love them!  
   - Serve with a small bowl of yogurt dip (Greek or low-sugar) for dipping.  
   - *Cost*: $0.50–$1.00 per person.  

2. **Homemade Popsicles**:  
   - Blend real fruit (mango, berries) with a splash of milk or coconut water. Freeze in molds.  
   - *Cost*: ~$0.30–$0.50 per popsicle.  

3. **Mini Oatmeal Cookies**:  
   - Make a batch of low-sugar oatmeal cookies (use applesauce instead of butter/oil).  
   - *Cost*: ~$0.40 per batch (makes ~20 cookies).  

4. **Fruit Kabobs**:  
   - Skewer melon, banana, and berries. Add nuts/seeds for crunch (optional).  
   - *Cost*: ~$0.40 per person.  

5. **Watermelon Slices with Yogurt Dip**:  
   - Cut watermelon into fun shapes (stars, animals). Serve with a dollop of yogurt.  
   - *Cost*: ~$0.60 per person.  

### 💡 Budget-Saving Tips:
- **Repurpose Barbecue**: Grill veggies (zucchini, bell peppers) alongside burgers to add fiber/nutrients.  
- **DIY Drink Stations**: Set up a water station with infused water (cucumber/mint) and self-serve lemonade.  
- **Community Donations**: Ask local bakeries for leftover cookies or fruit stands for surplus fruit.  
- **Activity Cost Control**: Use free scavenger hunt apps (like GooseChase) to organize clues.  

### 🌟 Awareness Integration:
- Label all desserts with icons (e.g., "Low-Sugar" or "High-Fiber").  
- Include a **DIY fruit parfait station** where kids can layer yogurt and fruit—subtle education.
