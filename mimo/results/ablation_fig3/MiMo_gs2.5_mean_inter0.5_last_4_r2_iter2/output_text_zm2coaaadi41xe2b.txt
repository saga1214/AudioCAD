Okay, Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! If you're craving authentic Asian noodles in Dallas, here are a few top recommendations:

First, there's a place called **Tokyo Joe's** in Little Tokyo. It's known for its solid ramen and udon. They have a location on Elm Street near Main. 

Another option is **Yamashiro**, which is a bit upscale but worth it for their tonkotsu ramen. It's near the Galleria.

If you want something more casual, **Pho 79** on North Central has amazing pho, which is a type of noodle soup. 

And if you're near Uptown, **Bamboo Sushi & Ramen Bar** is a good choice for their miso ramen.

For protein bars, I'd recommend looking for brands with clean ingredients and good macros. Some top picks are:

- **Quest Protein Bars**—they're high in protein and low in sugar.
- **RXBAR**—simple ingredients and a good balance of protein and fiber.
- **Lärabar**—softer texture, made with whole food ingredients.
- And **Clif Builder's**—they're a classic with decent protein and carbs.
