Absolutely! Here's a comprehensive shopping list based on our plan, designed for 24 guests (24 plates/glasses):

### **Appetizers/Finger Foods (24 servings)**
1. **Mini Sliders (Ham or Cheese):** 2 lbs ground beef/pork or pre-cooked patties  
2. **Shrimp Cocktail:**  
   - 2 lbs shrimp (thawed if frozen)  
  3. **Pigs in a Blanket:** 50 pre-made sausage rolls (or make your own: 1 lb sausage, 50 phyllo cups)  
4. **Sliders Toppings:**  
   - BBQ sauce, mustard, mayo  
   - Lettuce, tomato, pickles (for sliders)  
5. **Shrimp Dip (optional kid-friendly):** 1 tub cream cheese, 1 cup mayo, 1/2 cup horseradish, 1/4 cup chopped celery  

### **Main Dish (Serves 24)**
6. **Ham:** 10–12 lbs (thawed if frozen)  
7. **Creamy Scalloped Potatoes:**  
   - 3 lbs potatoes (peeled, cubed)  
   - 1/2 cup butter  
   - 1 cup milk  
   - 1/2 cup grated Parmesan  
   - Salt/pepper to taste  
8. **Roasted Asparagus:**  
   - 1 bunch asparagus (or 12 oz fresh)  
   - 3 tbsp olive oil  
   - Salt/pepper  
9. **Clean Kid-Friendly Veg (Alternative to Glazed Carrots):**  
   - 2 lbs carrots (cut into sticks)  
   - 3 tbsp olive oil  
   - Salt/pepper (skip glaze!)  

### **Drinks (24 servings)**  
10. **Champagne for Toast:**  
    - 2 bottles (e.g., 750ml each)  
11. **Signature NYE Cocktail:** Choose one example:  
    - *Cosmopolitan:* 1 bottle vodka, 1 bottle triple sec, 1 bottle cranberry juice, 1/2 cup lime juice, club soda  
    - *Margarita:* 1 bottle tequila, 1 bottle lime juice, 1 bottle triple sec, salt rim  
12. **Grape Juice (Clean Kids' Drink):** 4–6 bottles (24 oz each)  
13. **Non-Alc Sparkling Options:**  
    - Club soda, lemonade, or fruit punch  
14. **Water:** 3–4 gallons  

### **Desserts/Snacks**  
15. **New Year’s Treats:**  
    - 12 cookies (e.g., sugar cookies, decorated)  
    - 12 mini cupcakes  
    - 1 lb chocolate chips (for DIY s’mores if you have a fire pit)  
16. **Fresh Fruit:** 1 watermelon, 1 pineapple (for grazing)  

### **Kid-Specific Essentials**  
17. **Noisemakers/Hats:** Party store (for festive participation)  
18. **Plastic Grape Juice Cups:** Reusable or disposable (cleanest option)  

### **Supplies**  
19. **Dinnerware:**  
    - 24 plates, 24 glasses, 24 napkins  
    - 24 serving platters/boards  
20. **Utensils:** Extra forks/spoons/knives (if needed)
