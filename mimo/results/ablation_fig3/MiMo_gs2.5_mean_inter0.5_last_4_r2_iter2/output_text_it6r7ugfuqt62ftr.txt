Absolutely! Since Northern California warming up, I'd recommend a trip to Southern California, specifically to Laguna Beach. It has warm ocean water for swimming, plenty of outdoor activities, and is perfect for a family.

Here’s a detailed four-day plan for you, your husband, and your daughter.

For your accommodation, I'd suggest The Beach Bohemian Resort in Laguna Beach. It's a great spot for families, with pools, a spa, and it's right on the beach.

On Day One, you can drive down to Laguna Beach, which is about a three-and-a-half-hour trip from Northern California. After checking in, you can take a walk along Coast Highway and visit the Laguna Beach Art Gallery. For dinner, a wonderful place nearby is The Beachcomber Cafe; they're excellent at handling gluten-free diets.

On Day Two, you can swim at Main Beach in Laguna Beach, which has calm, warm water. In the afternoon, explore Heisler Park, which has a playground and tide pools. For dinner, you could go to the famous Ocean Mesa right on the beach.

On Day Three, you could take a trip to Crystal Cove State Park, which is about a 25-minute drive away. You can swim at the cove there and walk along the historic beachfront. In the afternoon, you can visit the Crystal Cove Cafe for a gluten-free lunch. Then, you can drive about 40 minutes to the Mission San Juan Capistrano, which is a beautiful and historic place.

For your last day, on Day Four, you can take a trip to the beautiful Mission San Simeon, which is about an hour and a half away. It's a bit cooler there, so you might want to pack a sweater. You can also visit the elephant park there for some fun photo ops. For a special birthday lunch, you could try the Mission San Simeon Inn, which has a dedicated gluten-free menu.

To get around, renting a car is definitely the best way, as Laguna Beach is a bit spread out.

And a final thought on packing: remember your bathing suit, cover-up, and a light sweater. A reusable water bottle and sunscreen will also be key.

Enjoy your warm and relaxing Southern California getaway!
