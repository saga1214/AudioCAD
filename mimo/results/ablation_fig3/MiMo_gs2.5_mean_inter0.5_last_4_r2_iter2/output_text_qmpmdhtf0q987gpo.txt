Okay, sounds like you're back on task with your cabinets. Before you apply that primer, here are two crucial final prep steps. First, ensure the cabinet surfaces are completely dry after sanding. Any moisture will ruin the primer. Second, and this is very important for your color change, lightly sand the cabinets right before you apply the primer. This creates a tiny bit of a 'tooth' on the surface, which will make the primer grab on much better and prevent any peeling later. After you've sanded the primer surface, wipe away all the dust with a tack cloth. Then you'll be perfectly ready for the primer
