For stir-fry noodles, the single most important tip is to use wooden or plastic spoons and tongs from now on. Absolutely no wood allowed in hot pots.

Another key step is to add just a tiny pinch of salt to the boiling water. It doesn't make the noodles taste salty, but it helps them cook up nice and firm.

Finally, try to rinse the cooked noodles in a colander with cold water right after you drain them. This stops them from sticking together. You can then just add them to your stir-fry at the very end.
