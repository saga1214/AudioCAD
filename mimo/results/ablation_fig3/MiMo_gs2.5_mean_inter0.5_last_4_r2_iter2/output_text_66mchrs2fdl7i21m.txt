To fly from Los Angeles to Phoenix is very efficient, taking about an hour from start to finish. Driving by car is a different story, as mentioned previously, it typically takes about six hours without major traffic.

Regarding horse ownership, if you buy a horse to keep in your personal backyard, you usually don't need a special permit. Arizona is very horse-friendly, and the Phoenix area is a major destination for ranches and stables offering all kinds of equestrian activities.
