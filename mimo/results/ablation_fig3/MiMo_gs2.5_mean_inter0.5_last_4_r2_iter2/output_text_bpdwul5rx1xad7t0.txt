That's a fantastic plan! Vancouver is a great base for road trips.

Here are five beautiful places nearby:

First, you have Whistler, which is about a two-hour drive. It's perfect for hiking in the summer and skiing in the winter. You can also try white-water rafting on the river.

Second, there's Vancouver Island. You can drive the scenic coastal route along the ocean or take the ferry to the stunning Uplands Beach on the inside passage.

Third, you can head to the Okanagan Valley for wine tasting. The drive through the mountains is gorgeous, and you can enjoy a scenic floatplane flight over the lakes.

Fourth, check out the Fraser Valley. It's great for farm visits, picking fresh berries, and enjoying local craft breweries.

And fifth, for a quick trip, you can visit the Gulf Islands. You can take a ferry to Salt Spring Island, which is lovely for biking and exploring its little villages.

For the best food, make sure to try some of the local seafood, like Dungeness crab. And for a truly special experience, look for a farm-to-table restaurant where you can learn about the local ingredients. You'll find all of these are just a short drive from the city!
