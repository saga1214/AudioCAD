For the work gathering taco and esquites setup, corn tortillas will satisfy the plant-based person if checked for plant-based ingredients. Flour tortillas also work if plant-based. Yuca is plant-based and gluten-free. Your modified esquites with lime, chili, and herbs should work fine without the dairy. And Chipotle chicken and yuca as fillings cover most restrictions.

As for a potato side dish, you could do a simple roasted or baked potato salad. That’s a crowd-pleaser, and it’s neutral enough for picky eaters. It’s hearty, kid-friendly, and everyone can eat it.
