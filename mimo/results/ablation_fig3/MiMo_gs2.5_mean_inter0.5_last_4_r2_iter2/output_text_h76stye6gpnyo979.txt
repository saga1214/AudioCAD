It sounds like juggling big dreams with daily responsibilities requires smart and patient planning.

Here’s a simple way to tackle it. First, match each ambition to a savings account or a 'fund' you create. Bolivia salt flats would go into apartment savings, Faroe Islands puffins could be their own bucket-list fund, home renovation gets its own savings, and fermented cooking trends could be a hobby fund.

Then, for each goal, create very small, specific tasks for weekly or monthly to-do lists. For Bolivia, you could spend 15 minutes this week comparing flight prices. For the Faroes, you could spend 10 minutes researching puffin nesting seasons tonight. For fermented foods, you could buy one small item to try this weekend.

By turning each big ambition into a tiny, manageable action on your regular to-do list, you can steadily make progress on all of them, even when work is exhausting.
