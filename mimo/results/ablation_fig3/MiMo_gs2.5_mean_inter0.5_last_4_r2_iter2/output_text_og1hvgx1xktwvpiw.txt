Of course, let's clarify. The most widely recognized Boulevardier recipe is one and a quarter bourbon to one ounce vermouth and Campari each. However, an equally popular and classic variation is using equal parts of all three. Now, for the Last Word, that's an entirely different but delicious cocktail. It's made with equal parts of three things: gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. Each of those is three-quarters of an ounce. You stir it over ice and garnish with a lime wedge. Enjoy whichever one you pick!
