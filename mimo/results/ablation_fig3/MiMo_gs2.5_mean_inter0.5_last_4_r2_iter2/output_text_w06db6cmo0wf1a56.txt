Okay, planning for ten dads, ten kids, sugar-free sodas, real calzones if possible, cupcakes/brownies for dessert... Let me think this through.
For drinks, you'll need waters, low-sugar juice boxes for kids, sugar-free sodas for adults, and maybe some sports drinks in case anyone needs them for extra hydration. Having plenty of reusable water bottles is key for refills later.
For snacks, single-serve things are definitely the way to minimize mess. Think cheese sticks, grapes, pretzels in bags, trail mix, baby carrots with single hummus cups, and hard-boiled eggs which are a great protein snack for the dads.
For the main meal, since real calzones are what everyone wants, your best bet is to pre-order them from a pizza place. You can get ten large ones and cut them into ten individual portions to put in the coolers, so it's like having real calzones without the mess of cutting on-site.
And for dessert, cupcakes are the cleanest and easiest individual portion option to grab and go. They capture that cake feel you were looking for.

So, based on all that, here’s a rough shopping list you can use. Just remember to check with everyone to see about any specific allergies before you buy everything! Have an amazing day.
