Of course, a plan can make a big difference. Here’s a simple weekly schedule to guide you.

On Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, plan to practice for about fifteen to twenty minutes. You can start with music if you'd like. Focus on your one-handed two-ball juggling until it feels easy, then try to transition to three by practicing those first three throws I mentioned.

On Tuesday and Thursday, you could do a similar session without music and dedicate yourself to perfecting that two-to-three transition.

Finally, on Saturday, switch things up and practice with the slow-falling scarves for about ten to fifteen minutes. This will really help you concentrate on patterns and timing. Then, on Sunday, take a complete rest.

Remember to be patient with yourself. Panic is stress getting in the way, as you said, so try to turn this into a relaxing activity. Celebrate every small win!
