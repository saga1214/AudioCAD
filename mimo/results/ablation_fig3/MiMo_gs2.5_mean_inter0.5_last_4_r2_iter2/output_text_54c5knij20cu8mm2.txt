First, about tinnitus being physiological versus psychological. While most causes are physical, like hearing damage or stress can sometimes make it worse or even trigger it, it’s rare for it to be the root cause. Think of it like how stress can make a headache worse—it doesn’t cause the headache itself.

Now, about structuring things in a "solid and indestructible" way. That sounds like you enjoy fields where there’s a clear framework or methodology. In psychology, that would be behavioral analysis or cognitive modeling. But if you want even more structure and measurement, fields like engineering, architecture, or even graphic design could be a better fit. You get to be creative while still working within measurable constraints.

Let’s tackle the left vs. right brain. It’s largely a myth. The brain doesn’t neatly split functions between hemispheres. Creativity and logic are processed in overlapping networks. But, the idea does stick around because it’s a useful shorthand for describing different ways of thinking. You can think of it like this: the left brain is the detail-oriented builder, and the right brain is the visionary artist. But they work together constantly.

For careers that blend objective measurement with creativity, consider: 
First, Information Design or Data Visualization. It’s about turning concrete data into visually compelling stories. 
Second, UX/UI Design. It’s about building usable, measurable digital products but with a creative flair. 
Third, Technical Illustration. It’s a mix of scientific accuracy and artistic skill.
