For a week of boat-based fun on Secret Harbour Beach Resort (or Gallows Point Resort), here are pressure-change-free daytime activities right nearby:

### Beach & Water Activities
1. **Snorkeling at Secret Harbour Bay**  
   Right from your resort: Calm, clear water with vibrant coral reefs and fish. No boat needed!
2. **Kayaking at Coki Beach** (5-min boat ride)  
   Gentle paddling along mangroves and reefs. Rentals available.
3. **Kayak/Paddleboard Rentals**  
   Secret Harbour offers rentals for kayaking or stand-up paddleboarding in sheltered bays.

### Nature & Exploration
4. **Reef Bay Trail** (15-min drive from Cruz Bay)  
   Easy paved path to a historic plantation with historic pools and lush rainforest. Great for walking.
5. **Virgin Islands National Park**  
   Visit Trunk Bay (boat ride) or Magens Bay (shallow, shallow entry) for shallow-water snorkeling.

### Local Spots
6. **Cruz Bay Shops & Restaurants**  
   2-min walk from Gallows Resort: Grab lunch/dinner at **Ocean 362** (seafood/sunset views) or **Cafe La Terre** (French-Caribbean cuisine).
7. **Duffy's Longboard** (Cruz Bay)  
   Popular happy hour spot with tacos (vegetarian options available!) and live music on weekends.
8. **Secret Harbour Beach Resort's Restaurant**  
   Enjoy beachfront dining with your property.

### Pro Tips
- **Avoid windy days** for kayaking/paddleboarding—Secret Harbour’s calm bay is best on calm days.
- **Water stability**: Shallow snorkeling spots (like Reef Bay or Magens Bay) are ideal for your brother.
- **Red Hook alternatives**: If exploring Red Hook, stick to its **beach bars** (like The Longboard) for live music—no hiking needed!
