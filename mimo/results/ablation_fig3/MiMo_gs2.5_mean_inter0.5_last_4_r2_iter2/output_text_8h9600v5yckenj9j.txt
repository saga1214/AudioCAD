For Halloween candy, the safest bet is always classic, nostalgic favorites. The top crowd-pleasers are things like Reese’s Cups, Reese’s Peanut Butter Cups, Hershey’s Kisses, Twix bars, Kit Kats, Snickers, and any brand of M&M’s. Gummy worms, gummy bears, and sour candy are also always popular. You could also grab some mini chocolate bars, like Skittles or Starbursts, for variety. Just make sure to check for allergens—some people might have issues with nuts or dairy in things like Milky Way bars. It's a good idea to mix a variety of these classic candies to make sure everyone finds something they like.
