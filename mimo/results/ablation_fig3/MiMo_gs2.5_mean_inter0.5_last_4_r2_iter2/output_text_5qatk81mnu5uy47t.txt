### **Trip Recommendation: Costa Rica's Pacific Coast (Osa Peninsula & Manuel Antonio)**
*   **Why it fits:** Offers powerful currents, diverse marine life, incredible shore diving, and stunning biodiversity – matching your Honduras/Belize experiences. Pacific waters are **tropical** (warm, clear, year-round).  
*   **Advantage of Short Fins:** Ideal for shore diving, maneuvering in rocky/coral areas, and travel convenience. Less leg strength required. Best for beginners or varied conditions.  
*   **Fin Suggestion:** Invest in **high-quality short fins** (e.g., Fins+ or ScubaPro). They’ll serve you well for calm days and light currents.

### **Unforgettable Experience Highlights**
1.  **Sirena Beach (Osa Peninsula):**  
    *   **Why:** One of the best shore dive sites in Costa Rica. Home to massive **tiger sharks** (often relaxed), **sea turtles**, **hammerheads**, **reef sharks**, **reef rays**, and abundant coral. Strong currents make it thrilling.  
    *   **Perfect for:** Powerful fin kicking (short fins handle it well with technique).  
2.  **Cocles Bay (Manuel Antonio):**  
    *   **Why:** Crystal-clear water with vibrant **coral gardens**, **reef sharks**, **tiger sharks**, **sea turtles**, and **manta rays** (seasonal). Great visibility and calm-to-light currents.  
    *   **Perfect for:** Beginners to intermediates. Short fins ideal for easy navigation.  
3.  **San Isidro Shipwreck (Pacific):**  
    *   **Why:** A **historical wreck** teeming with life – **turtles**, **sharks**, **lobsters**, schools of fish, and coral growth. A must-see for wreck divers.  
    *   **Perfect for:** Short fins due to tight navigation around the wreck.  
4.  **Dona María (Osa Peninsula):**  
    *   **Why:** Famous for **whale sharks** (seasonal, Jan-Jun) and **tiger sharks** in shallow, protected bays. Stunning scenery.  
    *   **Perfect for:** Short fins – easy shore entry and maneuvering.  

### **Trip Logistics & Tips**
*   **When to Go:** Year-round, but **Dec-Apr** offers best conditions (warmer water, fewer clouds, high season). Avoid peak crowds if possible.  
*   **Where to Stay:**  
    *   **Osa Peninsula:** **Laguna National Park Lodge** (eco-luxury, direct beach access). **Dona María Lodge** (close to Dona María dive).  
    *   **Manuel Antonio:** **Hotel Santa Teresa** (beachfront, good dive shop) or **Paradise Cove** (private beach, excellent service).  
*   **Dive Shops:** Choose reputable local operators (e.g., **Dive Osa**, **Sirena Diving**, **Paradise Diving**). Ask about guide experience and boat safety.  
*   **Packing Essentials:**  
    *   **Drysuit** (for cooler mornings/wet entries) or **wetsuit** (7/3mm recommended).  
    *   **Short fins** (your chosen type), snorkel, mask, fins, weights, regulator, BCD.  
    *   **Reef-safe sunscreen**, hat, rash guard, dive computer, camera.  
    *   **Cash in Costa Rica** (small bills).  
*   **Beyond Diving:** Explore **Corcovado National Park** (Osa), **Manuel Antonio National Park**, or enjoy **surfing** in Santa Teresa (Manuel Antonio area).
