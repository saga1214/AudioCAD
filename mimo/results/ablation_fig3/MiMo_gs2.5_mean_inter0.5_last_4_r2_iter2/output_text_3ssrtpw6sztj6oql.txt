You've hit on a crucial point: while **controlling access** (guards/bag checks) is functionally the *first operational layer*, modern security recognizes that **secure displays and mounting systems** represent the **most critical, final first line of defense** against actual theft. Here's why:

1.  **Direct Barrier to the Art:** They are the *immediate physical obstacle* between the artwork and an intruder. Reinforced glass, thick plexiglass, or steel frames make smashing a case incredibly difficult and noisy, triggering specific alarms.
2.  ** buys Critical Time:** Even if an intruder bypasses guards and sensors, defeating a secure display buys time. They need tools, knowledge, and time to weaken or remove the case/structure, during which alarms, surveillance, and response teams are activated.
3.  **Reduces Reliance on Human Vigilance:** While guards are essential at entry points, the physical security of the artwork itself is less dependent on a guard spotting an intruder *inside* the building. The display system acts as a passive, engineered barrier.
4.  **Deterrence:** The visible presence of advanced security systems (like thick, seemingly indestructible cases or complex magnetic mounts) inherently deters potential thieves who know the challenge is too great.
5.  **Specific Alarm Triggers:** Many secure displays are designed to trigger alarms when tampered with. A pedestal sensor detects unauthorized weight or tool use; a case might have pressure sensors or glass break sensors.

**In summary:**

*   **Functional First Line:** Managing entry points (guards, screening) to *prevent* unauthorized access.
*   **Critical Art-Specific First Line:** **Reinforced display cases, robust mounting systems, secure pedestals, and their integrated alarms.** This is the *last reliable barrier* designed specifically to protect the artwork itself, regardless of how an intruder got inside. It's the engineered "first line" that directly confronts the threat to the collection.

For modern art museums, where irreplaceable, high-value items are the core asset, this physical hardening of the artwork is paramount. It's the ultimate failsafe layer ensuring that even if the perimeter fails, the art remains protected as long as possible.
