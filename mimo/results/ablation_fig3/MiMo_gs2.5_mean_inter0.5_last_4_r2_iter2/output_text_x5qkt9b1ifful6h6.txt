1.  **Combination of Harmful Components:** They are rarely *just* high in sugar, salt, or unhealthy fat. 2.  **Engineered Overconsumption:** They are specifically designed to be hyper-palatable and addictive, tricking your brain into eating far more calories than you need, contributing significantly to obesity.

3.  **Nutrient Deficiency:** They are stripped of essential nutrients like fiber, vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants found in whole foods.

4.  **Synergistic Harm:** The *combination* of these factors – high calorie density, low satiety (fiber), high fat/sugar/salt, and artificial additives – creates a synergistic effect that independently harms health. This leads to:
    *   **Weight Gain & Obesity:** Due to excess calories and poor satiety.
    *   **Type 2 Diabetes:** Due to blood sugar spikes and insulin resistance.
    *   **Cardiovascular Disease:** Due to inflammation, high blood pressure, and dyslipidemia.
    *   **Fatty Liver Disease:** Due to excessive fructose (sugar) intake.
    *   **Inflammation:** Additives and unhealthy fats trigger chronic low-grade inflammation, a root cause of many chronic diseases.
    *   **Certain Cancers:** Strong links exist between high ultra-processed food intake and increased cancer risk.

**Evidence:** Major health organizations (WHO, American Heart Association, American Cancer Society) and extensive epidemiological studies consistently link high ultra-processed food intake to significantly increased risks of all the chronic diseases mentioned above.

**In summary:** While excess sugar, sodium, trans fats, and processed foods are all very harmful individually, **ultra-processed foods represent the most concentrated form of these risks.** Their combination of harmful ingredients, engineered overconsumption, and nutrient void make them the category most detrimental to overall health when eaten in excess. Moderation and prioritizing whole, unprocessed foods are key for health.
