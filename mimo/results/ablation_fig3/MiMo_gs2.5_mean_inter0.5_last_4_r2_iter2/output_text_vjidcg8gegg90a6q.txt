For winter chicks integrated into your adult coop later on, the deep litter approach we talked about earlier is the best bedding choice. Five weeks old means they can handle composting heat safely. Just use large Flake pine shavings on top of the rubber mats you liked. As they compost, they'll naturally generate warmth for both the chicks and the adult flock. Sand is also a good option, but the deep litter method is more energy-efficient and provides that valuable natural heat.
