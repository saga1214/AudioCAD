Let’s start with drywall supplies. Joint compound, around 10 to 15. Sandpaper/sponge, about 5. Putty knife, 8 to 12. Primer and paint for a small stain, 15 to 25. Nails/adhesive/caulk gun, around 10. If you need a piece of molding, that could be anywhere from 15 to 30 depending on the style. So, let’s say 68 to 112 for the repair.

Cake supplies add up more. Two cups of AP flour, $3. Cup-and-a-half of sugar, $4. Baking powder, under $2. Stick of butter, $4. Three large eggs, around $3. Cup of milk, $2. Vanilla extract, $3. Frost ingredients, another 5 to 10. Pan, depending on material, could be 5 to 10. All told, let’s say 27 to 39 for the cake.

So, budget around 95 to 151 total for both tasks. If you buy generic brands, you can probably get it under 100.
