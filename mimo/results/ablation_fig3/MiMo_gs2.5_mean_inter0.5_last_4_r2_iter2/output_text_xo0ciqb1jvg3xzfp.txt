Was it a happy occasion for both Pilgrims and Wampanoag? Probably not, and it's highly unlikely.

For the Wampanoag, even if they were happy at that moment, the arrival of the Pilgrims marked the beginning of a devastating series of events for their people.

For the Pilgrims, the feast was a celebration of survival, but it was also a strategic event to secure an alliance for their own survival.

So again, combining both stories gives the clearest picture of a single diplomatic meal happening within a much longer and more tragic historical context.
