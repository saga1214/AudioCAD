That sounds like a fantastic themed date night! Here are some ocean-inspired meal and dessert ideas that are diabetic-friendly (where noted) and can pack a spicy mango punch:

### 🍽️ Ocean-Themed Mains (3 Options)
1. **Spicy Coconut Shrimp Scramble**  
   *Shrimp sautéed in coconut milk with chili flakes, served with avocado toast and seaweed salad.*  
   *(Diabetic-friendly: Use avocado instead of sour cream, control shrimp portion.)*

2. **Mango-Coconut Curry with Tuna & Tofu**  
   *Tuna and firm tofu simmered in a spicy mango-coconut curry broth, served with zucchini noodles.*  
   *(Diabetic-friendly: Use low-sugar curry paste, skip added sugar.)*

3. **"Diver's Delight" Fish Tacos**  
   *Pan-seared white fish (like cod) in lime-cilantro sauce, topped with mango salsa and pickled red onions, in corn tortillas.*  
   *(Diabetic-friendly: Corn tortillas > flour, skip creamy toppings.)*

### 🍰 Ocean-Themed Desserts (5 Sweet Options)
1. **Mango Sorbet with Coconut "Coral"**  
   *Fresh mango sorbet topped with toasted coconut flakes shaped like "coral."*  
   *(Diabetic-friendly: Natural sugar in mango, no added sugar needed.)*

2. **Diabetic-Friendly Chocolate-Dipped Shrimp**  
   *Cacao-dipped shrimp (ensure no shellfish cross-contamination), with a drizzle of sugar-free caramel.*  
   *(Use shrimp from a separate batch if strict on shellfish.)*

3. **Seaweed & Mango Parfait**  
   *Layers of vanilla yogurt, mango puree, and nori seaweed flakes for a "ocean breeze" flavor.*  
   *(Diabetic-friendly: Full-fat Greek yogurt for protein/fiber.)*

4. **Coconut Pina Colada Pudding**  
   *Lightened coconut milk pudding with mango chunks, topped with a splash of diet pineapple juice.*  
   *(Diabetic-friendly: Use full-fat coconut milk, no ice cream.)*

5. **"Lagoon" Fruit Skewers**  
   *Skewers of pineapple, mango, blueberries, and kiwi, drizzled with a splash of sparkling water and lime.*  
   *(Diabetic-friendly: All natural sugars, no added sugar.)*

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Spice Control:** Adjust chili levels to your taste.  
- **Diabetic Swaps:** Use avocado, Greek yogurt, and natural fruit sugars instead of refined sugars.  
- **Theme Touch:** Decorate with blue lights, starfish centerpieces, and a "coral reef" of fruit skewers!

Enjoy your culinary adventure under the sea! 🌊✨
