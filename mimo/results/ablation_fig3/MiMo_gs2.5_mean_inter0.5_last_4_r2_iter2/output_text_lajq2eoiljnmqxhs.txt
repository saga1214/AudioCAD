Cooking could work if you pick a simple recipe. Something quick, like a stir-fry or a pasta dish, where there’s more action—chopping and stirring—than conversation is needed. This can keep things from getting too quiet. Choose a dish neither sister dislikes too much, focusing on safe ingredients like non-lactose ones such as almond milk or butter. Keep it short and fun, maybe even make it a friendly, low-pressure competition.
