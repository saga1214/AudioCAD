Let's quickly run through the whole system we built with the car analogy. The heart is the gas station and engine, pumping the cars. The lungs are the refueling point itself, where the cars get that fresh oxygen fuel. 

Then you've got your main highways, the big arteries, carrying blood away. The really tiny roads, the capillaries, are where the blood drops off its fuel to your cells. And to get back, you have the return roads, the veins, that bring the blood back to the heart. 

Down in the cleanup department, you've got the kidneys as the car washes, filtering out all the waste. And the liver is that service station sorting and processing everything to keep us balanced. 

So yeah, it's all these different parts working together, like a team of little cars on a super important road trip inside you! It all just works together to keep you powered up and healthy.
