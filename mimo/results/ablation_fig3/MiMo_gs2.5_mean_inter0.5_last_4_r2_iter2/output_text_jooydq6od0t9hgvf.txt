Here are quick yet elegant drink recommendations for your signature cocktails:

### 1. **Vodka Collins** (Classic & Refreshing)  
- **Base**: 1.5 oz vodka  
- **Mixers**:  
  - 3 oz fresh lemonade or grapefruit juice  
  - 2 oz sparkling water (instead of soda!)  
- **Garnish**: Lemon twist or cucumber ribbons  
- *Why it works*: Light, refreshing, and soda-free. Easily adaptable for non-drinkers (just sparkling water + juice).

### 2. **Argentine-Inspired "Fernet Light"** (Herbal & Bold)  
- **Base**: 1 oz gin (milder than vodka, smoother with herbal notes)  
- **Mixers**:  
  - 0.5 oz Fernet-Branca (herbal amaro)  
  - 1 oz soda water  
  - 0.5 oz simple syrup  
- **Garnish**: Orange slice + lemon wedge  
- *Why it works*: A gentler take on the classic Argentine Fernet-MRE. The gin softens the bitterness, making it approachable for mixed age/diet preferences.  
- **Non-Alcoholic Option**: Sparkling water + Fernet syrup (mix 1 oz Fernet with 1 oz water, then top with soda).

### 3. **Signature Mocktail Bar**  
- Offer infused waters (e.g., cucumber-mint or berry-ginger)  
- Mixers: Sparkling water + fresh juices (orange, blood orange, or pomegranate)  
- Garnish with edible flowers or citrus twists  

### Key Notes:  
- **Label Clearly**: Use small signs for alcohol/non-alcoholic versions.  
- **Allergy Alert**: Fernet-Branca is safe for the soda-allergic guest.  
- **Formality**: Serve in chilled glasses with elegant garnishes.  

Enjoy your party! 🍸✨
