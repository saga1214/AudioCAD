That's a wonderful idea. The gingerbread house kit adds a lot of fun and sentiment to the cookies.

As for the Pringles can method, packing tightly with cushioning works well. Another option is to double-wrap the cookies in parchment paper, pair them back-to-back, and place them in a sturdy cardboard box filled with crumpled newspaper.

And giving mini gingerbread cookies to the friends along with the house kits is a great idea. It gives everyone something extra to enjoy and share.
