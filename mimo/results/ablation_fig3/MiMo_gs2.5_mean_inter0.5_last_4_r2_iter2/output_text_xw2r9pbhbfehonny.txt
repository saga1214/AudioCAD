Okay, puppeteer, so you're starting your YouTube kid channel. Here’s a quick checklist before filming:

1. Permission: Make sure you have permission from the venue, like that assisted living, before recording. You don’t want any legal issues.

2. Setup: Keep it simple. Film in a well-lit area, maybe use a tripod or ask someone to hold the phone steady. Focus on your performance and the puppets.

3. Short Skits: Pick your best characters or favorite routines. Edit them down to five to ten minutes—short and snappy.

4. Kid-Friendly Label: When you upload on YouTube, make sure it’s labeled as kid-friendly to keep settings safe.

5. Share: Start small. Share your channel with people already fans—like that influencer family—and stick with posting regularly.

You’ll do great. Just film what you do best, post consistently, and make it fun and engaging. Nothing hurts to try.
