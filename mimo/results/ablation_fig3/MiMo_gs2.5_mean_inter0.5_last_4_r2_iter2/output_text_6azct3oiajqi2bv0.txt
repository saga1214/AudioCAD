To stick fabric securely without damaging winter clothing linings, here's the best method:

### Best Solution: Use Double-Sided Fabric Tape
1. **Why it works**:  
   - Won’t damage synthetics (common in winter jackets/fleeces).  
   - Provides strong hold without glue residue or stitching.  
   - Removable if needed (unlike glue).

2. **Steps**:  
   - Clean the fabric surface thoroughly.  
   - Cut tape slightly larger than the sticky area.  
   - Press firmly for 30 seconds.  
   - Trim excess tape after sticking.

### Alternatives (if unavailable):
- **Stitching**: Use a needle/thread for small tears (test on scrap fabric first).  
- **Temporary Fix**: Safety pins *inside* the lining (avoid stitching visible seams).  
- **Avoid**: Glue guns (they melt synthetic fabrics) or regular tape (rips easily).
