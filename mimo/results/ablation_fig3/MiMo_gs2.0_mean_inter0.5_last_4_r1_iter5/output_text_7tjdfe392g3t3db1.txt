To tune your instrument using a tuner app, follow these steps:  

### 1. **Choose the Right App**  
   - Use apps like **GuitarTuna**, **Fender Tune**, or **Tuner Lite** (free, no ads).  
   - Ensure it supports your instrument (guitar, bass, ukulele, violin, etc.).  

### 2. **Prepare Your Instrument**  
   - **Plug in an electric instrument** (e.g., electric guitar) if needed.  
   - **Strum a string** (or play a note) to send a vibration to the app.  

### 3. **Identify the Correct Pitch**  
   - **For acoustic instruments:**  
     - Place the tuner near the soundhole or bridge.  
     - The app will display a **needle/meter** (red = sharp, blue = flat).  
     - Adjust the tuning pegs until the needle centers (green).  
   - **For electric instruments:**  
     - The app will show a **frequency waveform** (e.g., 261.6 Hz for A4).  
     - Tune to the reference pitch (e.g., A4 = 440 Hz).  

### 4. **Tuning Sequence**  
   - **Guitars/Basses:** Start with the **low E string**, then move up to the **high E string**.  
   - **Ukuleles:** Tune from **low G to high G**.  
   - **Violins:** Use the **A string as a reference** (A440), then tune other strings relative to it.  

### 5. **Double-Check**  
   - Play a chord or melody to ensure all strings are harmonically aligned.  
   - Retune after playing (strings stretch and go slightly out of tune).  

### ⚠️ **Important Notes**  
- **Reference Pitch:** Most apps default to **A440 Hz** (concert A = 440 Hz).  
- **Intonation:** Some apps offer "tuning guides" for fretted notes.  
- **Accuracy:** Place the tuner close to the sound source for best results.  

### 🎸 **Example Workflow (Acoustic Guitar)**  
1. Open **GuitarTuna** → Select "Acoustic."  
2. Strum the **6th string (low E)**.  
3. Adjust tuning peg until the needle centers.  
4. Repeat for all 6 strings (low E to high E).
