### Best for Hot Weather: **Pinot Grigio**  
- **Why?** Its chilled serving temperature (45–50°F / 7–10°C) provides a crisp, refreshing contrast to warm days.  
- **Tip:** Store in an insulated cooler with ice packs on the deck to maintain chill.

### Best for Cold Weather: **Pinot Noir**  
- **Why?** Served slightly below cellar temp (55–60°F / 13–16°C), its structure and acidity feel comforting without heaviness.  
- **Tip:** Use a wine bucket with ice to keep it cool while serving outdoors.

### Honorable Mentions  
- **Italian Chianti:** Good for cool evenings (60–65°F / 16–18°C). Its medium body holds up better in mild weather than Pinot Grigio but isn’t ideal for extreme heat.  
- **Hon-Mirin:** Not for temperature play—room temp (68–75°F / 20–24°C) is ideal for tasting or cooking.

### Key Takeaway  
For **hot weather**, prioritize whites like Pinot Grigio or rosé (if available). For **cold weather**, opt for cooler-served reds like Pinot Noir. Chianti is a versatile "middle ground" for mild evenings.
