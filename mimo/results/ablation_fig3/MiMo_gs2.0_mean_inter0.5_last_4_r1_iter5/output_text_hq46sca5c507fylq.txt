### For Woodworking (if you explore it):
1. **Hand Tools**: A quality mallet, marking gauge, or chisels.  
2. **Wood Samples**: A curated selection of exotic or beginner-friendly woods.  
3. **Workshop Essentials**: Clamps, sanding blocks, or a small bench vise.  
4. **Project Book**: Step-by-step guides for beginners.  

### For Clay/Pottery (if you explore it):
1. **Pottery Kit**: Hand-building tools + a small pottery wheel (or a local studio membership).  
2. **Clay Sets**: Air-dry or polymer clay in useful colors.  
3. **Glazing Supplies**: Unique glazes or underglazes for personalization.  
4. **Molds/Tools**: Molds for creating textured items or carving tools.  

### General Craft-Themed Gifts:
- **Subscription Boxes**: Like "The Craft Box" or "Cratejoy" for monthly craft supplies.  
- **Workshop Tickets**: A local class on sewing, woodworking, or pottery.  
- **Personalized Tools**: Engraved sewing needles, wood carving tools, or clay stamps.  

### Affordable & Thoughtful Options:
- **Vintage Fountain Pen Set**: Parker 51 + matching ink bottle.  
- **Fabric Scrap Bundle**: Small, colorful pre-cut fabrics for practice.  
- **"Hobby Starter" Jar**: Fill it with craft supplies (sewing pins, buttons, needles) + a fun project idea.
