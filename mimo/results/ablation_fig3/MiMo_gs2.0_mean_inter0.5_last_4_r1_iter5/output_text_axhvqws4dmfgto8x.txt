Alright, let's tackle this stir-fry noodle question. Setting off the smoke alarm with wooden spoons is a classic beginner's tale, so we can skip that for the stir-fry!

Here are the keys to perfect noodles. First, and most importantly, use a metal spoon or spatula. No wood!

Second, add a pinch of salt to the water. It really improves the flavor and helps the noodles hold together.

Third, don't add oil until after you've added the noodles. Stir them in, then add oil. This prevents sticking.

Finally, drain the noodles well and rinse them with cold water. This stops the cooking process and makes them easier to toss in your stir-fry.
