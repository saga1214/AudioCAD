**Cocktails to Elevate the Night:**

1.  **Lyrebird Cocktail:** (Perfect non-citrus, visually stunning)
    *   **What it is:** A vibrant, layered cocktail with lychee, rose, ginger, and a hint of chili. The colors are beautiful (pink, light green, yellow).
    *   **Why it fits:** Indulgent, unique, visually impressive, herbaceous/flower notes, no citrus. Feels special and celebratory.
2.  **Sparkling Blood Orange Sipper:** (Elevated non-citrus "orange")
    *   **What it is:** Blood orange juice (not citrus juice, technically different!) muddled with star anise and orange bitters, topped with prosecco or sparkling wine. Garnish with a star anise pod.
    *   **Why it fits:** The "orange" in the name is misleading – it's the fruit, not the juice category. Sophisticated, festive, refreshing, no actual citrus juice.
3.  **Lavender Honey Basil Smash:** (Herbaceous & Refreshing)
    *   **What it is:** Muddled fresh basil, lavender honey syrup, lemon juice (small amount for balance, not dominance), topped with soda water. Garnish with basil.
    *   **Why it fits:** Herbaceous and floral, refreshing without being tart, unique, no citrus juice focus. The honey balances any acidity.
4.  **Smoked Old Fashioned:** (Classic & Indulgent)
    *   **What it is:** Bourbon or rye whiskey, muddled orange peel (not juice!), bitters, a dash of bitters, and a unique smoked element (like a smoked sugar cube or actual smoking).
    *   **Why it fits:** Sophisticated, classic, warming yet smoky/indulgent. The orange peel adds aroma without juice.
