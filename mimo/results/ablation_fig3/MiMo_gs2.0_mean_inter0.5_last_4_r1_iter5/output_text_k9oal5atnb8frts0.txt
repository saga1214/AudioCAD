For a 'Retro Tech' lunch theme, you could do a 'Dial-Up Delight Platter.' This would include a simple trail mix with pretzels, peanuts, and M&Ms to represent the 'dial-up' sound. For the main course, a classic tuna or chicken salad sandwich on a brown bag, because brown bags are nostalgic. You could also include a fruit cup and maybe some carrot sticks for a healthy touch. For drinks, you could do a retro soda or just water. That should work well.
