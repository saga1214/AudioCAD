### **Key Tips for Coursework Prep**  
1. **Visual Research:**  
   - Visit teamLab (Tokyo) and teamLab Borderless (Osaka) for digital art inspiration.  
   - Photograph ukiyo-e prints at museums (e.g., Tokyo National Museum).  
2. **Design Aspects:**  
   - **Images:** Traditional woodblock prints, kimono patterns, Zen gardens.  
   - **Layout:** Vertical scrolling like Japanese scrolls; negative space.  
   - **Color Scheme:** Indigo, gold, white (kimono motifs); seasonal palettes.  
   - **Font:** Handwritten calligraphy or minimalist sans-serif.  
3. **Time Management:**  
   - Dedicate 2–3 hours/day during sightseeing for research.  
   - Use hotel time for slide editing.  

### **Travel Logistics**  
- **Transport:** Get a 14-day JR Pass (if traveling between Tokyo/Osaka/Kyoto/Nara frequently).  
- **Check-out:** Typically 10 AM (confirm with hotels).  
- **Taxis:** Use apps like Uber; costs vary (e.g., Asakusa to Shinjuku ≈ ¥3,500).  
- **Communication:** Use Google Translate’s camera mode for signs.
