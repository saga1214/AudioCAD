Since you're staying at Secret Harbour over on St. Thomas, Red Hook is the perfect base for fun daytime adventures! Here are some great activities:

1.  **Dive & Snorkel:** Red Hook is world-famous for its vibrant coral reefs teeming with marine life. Secret Harbour Beach Resort has calm bays ideal for snorkeling right off the shore. For deeper exploration, head to popular spots like **Coki Beach** (just a short walk from Secret Harbour) or **Cruz Bay** (the main town hub) for world-class dive shops offering guided dives and snorkel tours. You might see parrotfish, rays, and even sea turtles!
2.  **Explore Coki Trail:** This easy, scenic trail winds along the coast from Secret Harbour up to Coki Beach. You'll get beautiful ocean views, pass the famous Coki Beach Cafe (great for breakfast!), and enjoy the lush tropical surroundings.
3.  **Visit Cruz Bay:** The bustling main town hub is a short walk or bike ride from Secret Harbour. Explore the colorful shops, browse the local market, grab a coffee, or stop by **The Longboard** for lunch (remember the great vegetarian options!).
4.  **Relax on the Beach:** Simply unwind on the calm, beautiful beaches of Secret Harbour or Coki Beach. Read a book, listen to the waves, and soak up the Caribbean sunshine (don't forget sunscreen!).
5.  **Kayak or Stand-Up Paddleboard:** Many Red Hook tours and shops offer guided kayak or SUP (stand-up paddleboard) adventures, often exploring mangrove channels or heading out to sea. These are usually low-pressure and fantastic ways to explore the coastline.
6.  **Visit Waterlemon Cay:** This small, uninhabited island is easily accessible by boat from Cruz Bay or Red Hook. It's a beautiful spot for a short hike through the jungle to a secluded beach, offering a peaceful escape and great photo ops.

**Key Tip:** Red Hook has a lively, relaxed vibe. Grab a drink or a meal at a beachfront bar like **The Longboard** (especially for happy hour!) or **Coki Beach Cafe** during the day to soak up the atmosphere. Enjoy your week sailing and exploring!
