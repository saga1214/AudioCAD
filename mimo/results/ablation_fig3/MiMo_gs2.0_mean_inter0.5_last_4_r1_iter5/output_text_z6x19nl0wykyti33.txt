Okay, so you have blonde hair and jeans, which are great starting points! Let's think of characters that would pair well with jeans and still feel like they fit the Harry Potter vibe.

One cool option is to go as a Ravenclaw student. You could pair jeans with a smart-looking top like a sweater or blouse, maybe a scarf or a brooch that looks like a Ravenclaw emblem. Ravenclaw is all about intelligence and style, so jeans could actually make you look pretty modern and cool.

Another idea is to go as a Hufflepuff. Hufflepuffs are known for being kind and grounded, so you could wear jeans with a cozy sweater or a hoodie. Maybe even add a scarf or a beanie to give it that earthy, warm feel.

Or, if you want something more unique, you could go as a Gringotts bank employee. You could wear jeans with a long, dark coat or a skirt, and maybe add some gold accessories like a necklace or earrings that look like gold coins.

These are all pretty easy to pull together and would definitely stand out at a party while still feeling like they fit the Harry Potter theme.
