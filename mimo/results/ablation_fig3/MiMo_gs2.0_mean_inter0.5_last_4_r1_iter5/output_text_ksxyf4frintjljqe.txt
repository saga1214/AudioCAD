And for the vanilla cake, baking something sweet could be a nice way to fill a hard time. A simple vanilla cake recipe is easy to find. You’ll need butter, sugar, eggs, flour, baking powder, milk, and vanilla extract. Cream the butter and sugar, add eggs, then mix in the dry ingredients along with milk and vanilla. Pour into a cake pan and bake at 350 degrees for about 30 minutes. For the frosting, just whip butter, powdered sugar, a little vanilla, and a splash of milk until it’s smooth and fluffy. Let the cake cool before frosting, and you’ve got a delicious treat.
