That sounds like a fantastic theme! Here are 5 ocean-inspired meals and 5 sweet desserts that accommodate your fiancée’s dietary needs (no shellfish, diabetes-friendly where possible, and plenty of spicy/mango options):

### 🌊 Ocean-Themed Meals
1. **Grilled Shrimp & Mango Skewers**  
   - Toss shrimp with lemon, garlic, and chili flakes; skewer with cubed mango. Serve with a light coconut-lime dipping sauce.  
   *Why it works:* Shellfish-free, mango, spicy, and low-glycemic.

2. **Spicy Coconut Curry with Tuna & Vegetables**  
   - Sauté shrimp or firm white fish (not shellfish!) in a coconut milk-based curry with ginger, turmeric, and chili. Add diced mango for sweetness. Serve with cauliflower rice.  
   *Why it works:* No shellfish, uses lean protein, and customizable spice level.

3. **Mango Tuna Salad with Coconut Chips**  
   - Mix canned tuna with diced mango, lime juice, cilantro, and a pinch of cayenne. Top with toasted coconut flakes.  
   *Why it works:* No shellfish, sweet-spicy combo, and quick to prepare.

4. **Shrimp Tacos with Mango Salsa**  
   - Marinate shrimp in smoked paprika and lime; grill and serve in corn tortillas with mango-pineapple salsa (diced mango, tomato, jalapeño, cilantro).  
   *Why it works:* Light, spicy, mango-forward, and low-carb if using corn tortillas.

5. **"Ocean" Paella with Shrimp & Squid**  
   - Use a shellfish-free seafood paella recipe (shrimp, squid, mussels if allowed, or extra shrimp). Add diced mango near the end for sweetness.  
   *Why it works:* Feels celebratory, uses seafood instead of shellfish, and customizable spice.

### 🍹 Ocean-Themed Desserts
1. **Mango Flan with Lime Zest**  
   - Classic Mexican flan (soft, creamy custard) topped with diced mango and a hint of chili-lime seasoning.  
   *Why it works:* Low-sugar option (skip added sugar), rich in calcium, and sweet-spicy.

2. **Coconut-Chia Pudding Parfaits**  
   - Layer chia seeds in coconut milk with mango puree and a sprinkle of shredded coconut. Top with toasted coconut flakes.  
   *Why it works:* High fiber, low-glycemic, and tropical-themed.

3. **Pina Colada Floats**  
   - Blend frozen mango with coconut milk and a touch of vanilla. Serve in glasses topped with a splash of pineapple juice and a sprinkle of crushed ice.  
   *Why it works:* No alcohol, sweet-coconut-mango fusion.

4. **Chocolate-Covered Strawberries with Sea Salt**  
   - Dip strawberries in dark chocolate (70%+ cacao), sprinkle with flaky sea salt, and add a drizzle of caramel-free coconut syrup.  
   *Why it works:* Elegant, low-sugar strawberries, and balanced sweet-salty-spicy.

5. **Spicy Mango Sorbet with Lime**  
   - Homemade sorbet with mango, lime juice, and a pinch of cayenne. Serve in a chilled glass with a side of coconut whipped cream.  
   *Why it works:* No added sugar (if using ripe mango), refreshing, and vibrant.

### 💡 Tips for Your Theme
- **Decor:** Use blue/green lights, seashell centerpieces, and ocean-printed tablecloths.  
- **Spice Control:** Keep chili flakes, hot sauce, or fresh jalapeños at the table for adjustable heat.  
- **Diabetes Note:** Focus on lean proteins, veggies, whole grains, and natural sweetness (mango, coconut). Avoid sugary sauces/syrups.
