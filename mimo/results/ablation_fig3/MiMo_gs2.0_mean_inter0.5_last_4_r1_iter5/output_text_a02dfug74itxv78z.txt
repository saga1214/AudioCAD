Alright, based on your midlife crisis power craving under ninety K, American preference, recent sports cars, CarPlay, avoiding imports, and the police color myth clarification, here are five contenders with V8s, V10s, or V12s. Remember the paint color thing? Flashy cars just get *more looks*, cops pulling over colorful ones specifically? Unproven. Neutral tones help you blend in. Now, for the cars...

1.  **Chevrolet Corvette (C8 Z06 - Newer than 5 yrs, but base Stingray fits):** The American king. Base C8 Stingray starts around seventy grand, easily under 90. Loaded with tech like CarPlay. The Z06 is a V8 masterpiece with insane performance. It's competitive with some supercars lap-for-lap. Pure young feeling power. Color options are vibrant, but expect attention regardless. Reliability? Porsche level? No, but excellent for its class.

2.  **Ford Mustang Shelby GT350 / GT500 (Newer models):** American muscle meets track focus. GT350 is a flat-nosed V8 beast around eighty-five K. GT500 is the V8 twin-turbo powerhouse pushing eighty-eight K. Both have modern tech, CarPlay included. They scream performance and youth. Cops pulling you over *just* for bright paint? Unlikely. But a Shelby GT350 in a bold color? It'll definitely get noticed driving fast. Reliability is generally very good for Ford's high-performance stuff.

3.  **Audi R8 V10 (Specifically the 2019-2020 models):** This is your V10 supercar under 90K. The V10 engine is legendary for its reliability and smooth power. It has CarPlay, modern tech, and stunning supercar looks. Drives like a dream. A bright R8? It will absolutely stand out, potentially drawing more attention than a neutral one driving aggressively. Porsche reliability? It's Audi, so very high, but maybe not quite at Porsche 911 levels. Still, exceptional.

4.  **Porsche 911 Turbo S (2018-2019 models):** The V8 *or* V12? The Turbo S is a V8 twin-turbo powerhouse. It's pure Porsche engineering reliability. It has CarPlay, modern tech, and competes fiercely with supercars on track and road. It feels incredibly young and capable. A bright Turbo S? It's a Porsche, so it gets looks, but it's less "flashy showstopper" than a wild R8. V10? Not in the Turbo S. V12? That's in the GT2 RS, which is way over budget and not recent enough.

5.  **Audi S8 (2018-2019 models):** If you want massive V8 power and comfort with Porsche-level reliability, this is it. It has a V8 twin-turbo, CarPlay, and modern tech. It's not quite as razor-sharp as the R8, but it's a performance sedan that feels incredibly capable and young. A bright S8? It's sporty, but less overtly flashy than an R8. Reliability is excellent. V10? No. V12? No. But the V8 is superb.

So there you go: Corvette for raw American V8 power, Mustang for muscle with a Shelby badge, R8 for the legendary V10 supercar experience, 911 Turbo S for Porsche perfection, and S8 for V8 luxury performance. Pick your poison!
