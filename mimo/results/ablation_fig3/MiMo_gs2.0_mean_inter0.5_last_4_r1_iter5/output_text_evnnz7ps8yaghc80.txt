### Weekly Practice Schedule (5 Days/Week)
*(Each session: 15–20 minutes max)*  
**Monday:**  
- Warm-up: 5 mins of two-ball beanbag juggling in one hand (focus on rhythm).  
- Transition Practice: 10 mins tossing just the **first three throws** (dominant hand → peak → other hand → peak → last ball). Don’t focus on catching yet.  
- Cool-down: 5 mins of catching beanbags gently in one hand.  

**Tuesday:**  
- Warm-up: 5 mins scarves (slow, deliberate throws focusing on pattern).  
- Rhythm Work: 10 mins juggling to music (use a steady-tempo song like "Happy" by Pharrell).  
- Cool-down: 5 mins two-ball one-handed juggling (reinforce muscle memory).  

**Wednesday:** Rest or light activity (e.g., stretch, watch a juggling tutorial).  

**Thursday:**  
- Warm-up: 5 mins scarves (practice "criss-cross" patterns).  
- Catch Practice: 10 mins juggling beanbags and focusing **only on soft, controlled catches**.  
- Cool-down: 5 mins two-ball one-handed juggling (build confidence).  

**Friday:**  
- Warm-up: 5 mins beanbag two-ball throws (practice throwing from both hands).  
- Three-Ball Focus: 10 mins adding the third beanbag **one throw at a time**.  
  - Try: Toss beanbag 1 → catch → Toss beanbag 2 → catch → Toss beanbag 3 → catch → Repeat.  
- Cool-down: 5 mins scarves (explore new patterns).  

**Saturday:** Rest or playful juggling (e.g., with family).  

**Sunday:** Rest.

### Key Tips to Reduce Stress & Speed Up Learning  
1. **Embrace "Failure" as Data**:  
   - If a throw is off, pause and note *what* went wrong (e.g., "I threw too high"). Reset and try again. No panic!  

2. **Short, Frequent > Long, Frustrating Sessions**:  
   - 15 minutes daily is better than 1 hour once a week. Consistency builds skill faster.  

3. **Use Scarves to Reset**:  
   - When beanbags frustrate you, switch to scarves. Their slow fall helps you **see the pattern**, not just react.  

4. **Stress-Busting Tricks**:  
   - **Breathe**: If you panic, pause and take 3 deep breaths.  
   - **Progress Tracking**: Mark an "X" on a calendar for each session. Visual progress reduces anxiety.  

5. **Remember Your Two-Ball Win**:  
   - You’ve already proven you can juggle! The three-ball jump is just a new skill—**not a failure**.
