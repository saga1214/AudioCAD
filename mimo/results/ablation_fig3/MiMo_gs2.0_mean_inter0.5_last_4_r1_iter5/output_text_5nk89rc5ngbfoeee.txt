For an end-of-year celebration, a **hybrid "Park Party in the Cafeteria"** is a fantastic idea! It combines the best of both worlds: outdoor fun with a rain-safe backup. Here’s how to make it work:

### 🎉 Event Plan:
1. **Theme:** "Park Picnic Extravaganza"  
   - **Decor:** Brownie flags, streamers, and picnic blankets (parents can bring lawn chairs for an outdoor feel).  
   - **Activities:**  
     - **Sundae/Salad Bar:** Ice cream (dairy-free options!), whipped cream, sprinkles, and fruit salad.  
     - **Mini-Competitions:** 3-legged races, sack races, or a "Pin the Badge on the Bear" game.  
     - **Craft Corner:** Decorating sashes or making friendship bracelets.  

2. **Location:**  
   - **Primary:** School cafeteria (easy setup, covered).  
   - **Backup:** If weather is good, move outside to the park with tents/shade.  

3. **Food:**  
   - **Simple & Inclusive:** Pizza (vegetarian options), fruit cups, and a sundae bar.  
   - **Dietary Note:** Label clearly (dairy-free, nut-free) and keep snacks prepped by you.  

4. **Parent Involvement:**  
   - **Snack Duty:** Assign parents to bring 1–2 items (e.g., chips, cookies, drinks).  
   - **Setup Help:** Parents can bring lawn chairs, games, or decorations.  

### 💰 Cost & Logistics:
- **Patches/Sashes:**  
  - Charge a **small dues fee** ($10–$20) to cover patches/sashes. Parents can buy sashes at troop meetings or provide their own (with troop branding).  
- **Snacks:**  
  - **You provide:** Main items (pizza, ice cream).  
  - **Parents provide:** Extras (e.g., chips, cookies).  
- **Weather Plan:** Always have an indoor backup—no stress if it rains!  

### ✅ Why This Works:
- **Fun & Low-Pressure:** Combines games, crafts, and food without overcommitting.  
- **Inclusive:** Fits dietary needs, activity preferences, and weather.  
- **Affordable:** Dues cover essentials; parents help with extras.  

**You’ll be great!** Start small, lean on parents for support, and focus on celebrating your daughters’ achievements. This end-of-year event will build troop spirit while keeping things manageable. 🌟
