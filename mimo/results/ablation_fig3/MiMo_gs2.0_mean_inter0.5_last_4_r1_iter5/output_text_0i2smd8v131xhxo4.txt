Here's a breakdown addressing your questions, moving from technical setup to workflow and well-being:

### 1. **Harsh Audio Fixes**
   - **Mic Placement**: Yes, speak into Yeti's side with the logo, not the top. Keep it 6–12 inches from your mouth, slightly off-axis (not directly in front). Experiment with angles.
   - **Acoustic Treatment**: Heavy blankets/duvets on walls/ceilings work well. Add a portable vocal booth (like a DIY closet setup) or a reflection filter around your mic if reflections persist. Yeti is sensitive to room noise.

### 2. **Vocal Endurance & Health**
   - **Warm-ups**: Do 5 minutes daily: lip trills, humming, sirens (glissando between notes), and tongue twisters.
   - **Hydration**: Sip room-temperature water hourly. Avoid caffeine/alcohol 4 hours before recording.
   - **Breathing**: Practice diaphragmatic breathing (inhale deeply, exhale slowly). Record in short segments (3–5 mins) with 10-minute breaks.
   - **Posture**: Sit upright, feet flat, shoulders relaxed. Use a mic stand, not a headset.
   - **Audiobook Tip**: Narrators use "energy markers" in scripts and record in 30–45 minute blocks with vocal exercises.

### 3. **Editing Workflow & Tools**
   - **Adobe Audition vs. Audacity**:  
     - *Pros*: Faster rendering, advanced noise reduction (e.g., "Voice Isolation" AI), multitrack editing, and spectral repair tools.  
     - *Learning Curve*: Steeper than Audacity, but tutorials (Adobe’s YouTube channel) help.  
     - *Save Time*: Batch processing for noise reduction/export.
   - **Mouth Noise Tools**:  
     - **iZotope RX**: "Denoise" module (AI-powered) for mouth clicks; "De-Click" for sharp noises.  
     - **Free Alternatives**: Audacity’s "Noise Reduction" (hold space to sample noise).  
   - **Organization**: Use a template session with labeled tracks (e.g., "Raw," "Noise Reduced," "Delivery"). Cloud storage (Google Drive) for client files.

### 4. **Balancing Work & Well-being**
   - **Time Allocation**:  
     - 60% recording/editing, 20% marketing (social media, outreach), 10% admin, 10% rest.  
     - Use a timer: 25 mins work, 5-min break (Pomodoro technique).
   - **Avoid Burnout**:  
     - Set recording hours (e.g., 9 AM–1 PM).  
     - Track calories/nutrients (apps like Cronometer) to avoid fatigue.  
     - Stretch hourly to prevent voice strain.

### 5. **Demo Tips**
   - **Memorable Hook**: Start with 1–2 seconds of high-energy dialogue (e.g., "You won’t believe what happened next...").  
   - **Character Variety**: Show 3 distinct voices (e.g., warm narrator, gruff villain, youthful hero).  
   - **Production**: Use subtle EQ (cut harsh 3–5 kHz), light reverb, and noise reduction. Keep it under 60 seconds.

### 6. **Restaurant Meal Recommendation**
   For **quick, flavorful, and energizing** food from a Thai-Japanese fusion spot:  
   - **Order**:  
     - **Pad See Ew** (stir-fried noodles with tofu, veggies, and sweet soy sauce).  
     - **Add**: Extra tofu and a side of pickled vegetables for crunch.  
   - **Why**: Balanced protein + carbs + veggies. High in iron (tofu) and B vitamins (noodles) for sustained energy.  
   - **Delivery Tip**: Ask for "extra sauce on the side" to control sodium.

### Final Tip
Track your progress weekly. Audio quality, vocal health, and workflow efficiency improve with iteration. Celebrate small wins! 🎤
