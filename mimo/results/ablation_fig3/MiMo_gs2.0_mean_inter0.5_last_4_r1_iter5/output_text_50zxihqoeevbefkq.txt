Okay, host here! New Year's Eve party for 24 people including 4 kids. Let's get your shopping list organized by category.

First, for appetizers and snacks, you'll need ground beef or Italian sausage for the sliders, bun fillings, mustard, and pickles. For the shrimp cocktail, get about a pound of shrimp, some cocktail sauce, and extra lemon wedges. For pigs in a blanket, you'll need sausage rolls, cream cheese, and mini phyllo cups.

For drinks, you'll need champagne for the adults, a base spirit and mixers for your chosen cocktail, and definitely the grape juice you decided on for the kids.

As for the main course, you'll want a good-sized ham, say about 14 to 16 pounds. For the sides, get a large bag of potatoes, butter, and cream of mushroom soup for the scalloped potatoes. For the clean roasted carrots, you'll need about two pounds, olive oil, salt, and pepper. And for the asparagus, get a pound, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

Don't forget the essentials: a signature cocktail mixers, sparkling water, coffee, tea, plenty of water bottles, ice, and cups for everyone.

For decorations, grab some balloons and kid hats or noisemakers. And for serving, make sure you have plenty of plates, napkins, and utensils.
