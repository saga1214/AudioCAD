1. **Mediterranean Chickpea Bowls**:  
   Toss canned chickpeas with cherry tomatoes, cucumber, olives, red onion, and feta. Drizzle with lemon-tahini dressing and a sprinkle of fresh herbs. Serve with whole-grain pita. *Why you’ll like it*: Echoes your earlier snack craving, protein-packed, and super fast.

2. **Sheet-Pan Salmon & Roasted Veggies**:  
   Toss broccoli, bell peppers, and sweet potatoes with olive oil, garlic, and rosemary. Top salmon fillets with a squeeze of lemon before roasting at 400°F for 15–20 mins. *Why you’ll like it*: Minimal cleanup, balanced flavors, and feels indulgent without being fussy.

3. **Hearty Lentil Soup**:  
   Sauté onions, carrots, and celery with cumin and paprika. Add lentils, veggie broth, and canned tomatoes. Simmer 20 mins, then blend partially for creaminess. Top with a swirl of pesto or yogurt. *Why you’ll like it*: Comforting, budget-friendly (lentils are cheap!), and ready in under 30 mins.

4. **Quick Tofu Stir-Fry**:  
   Press firm tofu, cube it, and pan-fry until crispy. Stir-fry frozen stir-fry veggies with garlic, ginger, and soy sauce. Add tofu back in, toss with sesame oil and a splash of rice vinegar. Serve over instant brown rice. *Why you’ll like it*: Plant-based, customizable, and uses pantry staples.
