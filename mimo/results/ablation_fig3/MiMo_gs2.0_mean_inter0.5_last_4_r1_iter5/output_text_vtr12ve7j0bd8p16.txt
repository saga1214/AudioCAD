Yes, Calgary is a fantastic base for exploring. For a weekend trip, you could head to Banff. It's just an hour and a half drive to the town of Canmore, and from there, you can hike in the Bow Valley, visit the Icefields Parkway, or take a scenic drive along the Columbia Icefield. A bit further out, there's the Kicking Horse Mountain Bike Park for an adrenaline rush. Or you could take a road trip along the Kananaskis River. If you're feeling more adventurous, you could even try a rock climbing trip in Canmore or a day hike up Mount Rundle near the city. There are also many great wine and beer tours in the area.
