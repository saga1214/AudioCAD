Here's a **separate packing list for each segment**, prioritizing temperature management, safety, and adaptability since conditions change significantly between day and night:

### 🌤️ **20-Mile Segment (Daytime: Mid-30s, Hilly, Aid Stations Available)**
Focus: **Moisture-wicking, sun protection, and light warmth.**  
Bring layers you can shed easily at aid stations.

| **Category**       | **Items**                                                                 |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Core Clothing**  | - Moisture-wicking running shirt (short or long sleeve) <br> - Lightweight, breathable shorts with liner (or tights for cooler moments) <br> - Fleece or windbreaker jacket (lightweight, packable) |
| **Hands/Head**     | - Lightweight gloves (for grip & sun) <br> - Cap or sun hat (UPF-rated) <br> - Buff/gaiter (for sun/heat) |
| **Footwear**       | - Trail running shoes with good grip <br> - Extra socks (merino wool) |
| **Extras**         | - Small waist pack (for keys/water) <br> - Trekking pole (for hills) <br> - Sunglasses (if sunny) |

### 🌌 **12-Mile Segment (Nighttime: Mid-30s, Aid Stations + Final Push)**
Focus: **Warmth, visibility, and safety.**  
Layer up aggressively—night temps drop fast, and fatigue increases risk.

| **Category**       | **Items**                                                                 |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Core Clothing**  | - **Base Layer:** Merino wool top/bottom (moisture-wicking + warmth) <br> - **Mid-Layer:** Fleece jacket or light puffer <br> - **Outer Layer:** Windproof/waterproof shell (essential) |
| **Hands/Head**     | - **Warm Gloves** (puffy or insulated) <br> - **Warm Hat** (balaclava or beanie) <br> - **Buff/Gaiter** (neck/wrists) <br> - **Headlamp/HANDHELD LIGHT** (with extra batteries) |
| **Footwear**       | - Trail shoes with grip (check for wear) <br> - Extra socks (wool) |
| **Extras**         | - Small pack or vest (for layers/light) <br> - Trekking pole (stability) <br> - Extra batteries (headlamp) |

### 🔑 **Key Considerations for Both Segments**
1. **Layer System:** Wear base + mid + outer layers. Add/remove as needed.  
2. **Visibility:** Reflective gear or bright colors for night.  
3. **Trekking Pole:** Crucial for hills—reduces strain on arms/legs.  
4. **Test Gear:** Try everything during training to avoid chafing/rashes.  
5. **Aid Station Strategy:** Shed layers *before* arriving at stations to avoid sweating in warmth.  

### 💡 **Pro Tip**
Bring a **lightweight puffy jacket** (e.g., Nano Puff style) for the 12-mile segment. If it’s dry, you can use it as an insulating mid-layer; if wet, it’s still packable. Gloves are non-negotiable at night—cold hands drain energy fast.
