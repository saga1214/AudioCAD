Okay, let's break down chronic skin redness, or erythema. There are many potential causes over a lifetime. Common ones include rosacea, long-term sun damage, genetics, contact dermatitis from irritants or allergens, and infections like Lyme disease or fungal infections. For the vasculitis part, it's an autoimmune condition where the immune system mistakenly attacks blood vessels. This inflammation can affect vessels of any size—large, medium, or small. Symptoms can vary widely depending on where the inflammation is, but often include fever, fatigue, and skin rashes. The cause is the autoimmune attack itself. If this inflammation isn't treated, it can lead to serious complications, like the vessels becoming narrowed, which restricts blood flow to organs and can cause damage.
