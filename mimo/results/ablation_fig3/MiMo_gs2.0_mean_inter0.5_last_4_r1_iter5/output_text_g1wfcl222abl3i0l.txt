I totally get that excitement! And that frustration with the glue gun! Here's a plan to tackle both:

**Glue Gun Troubleshooting:**
1.  **Check the Bulb:** Is it the right wattage for your glue gun? Usually 40-60 watts. Is it screwed in *tight*?
2.  **Check the Cord/Power Source:** Ensure the cord isn't frayed or plugged in securely. Try a different outlet if possible.
3.  **Warm-Up Time:** Glue guns need a few minutes to fully heat up after being turned on. Don't give up after 1 minute!
4.  **Clean the Tip:** Sometimes residue clogs the tip. Wipe it clean with a dry cloth or tissue.
5.  **Use the Right Glue:** Make sure you're using the correct glue for your glue gun (hot glue).
6.  **Heat the Tip:** Gently rub the hot tip on the globe or pumpkin base to help the glue flow better.
7.  **Try a Different Tip:** If possible, try a slightly larger or different shaped tip.

**Spooky Globe Ideas (Building on Yours!):**

Your ideas (cobwebs, lace, masks, dark flowers, glitter) are fantastic! Here are some additions to make them *really* creepy:

1.  **Faceless Masks or Skeleton Hands:** Instead of masquerade masks, use smaller, simple white or grey plastic skeleton hands or creepy, expressionless masks (think "dead" not fancy masquerade). Glue them around the base.
2.  **Miniature Bones:** Scatter small plastic or resin bones (like vertebrae or femurs) around the base of the globe. Glittered bones look extra spooky!
3.  **Fake Blood Drips:** Use clear-drying glue or a small amount of acrylic blood paint to create dark red "drips" or streaks on the base or pumpkin stem. Let it dry slightly before adding glitter for a wet look.
4.  **Eerie Moss & Lichen:** Your moss idea is great! Add patches of deep green or greyish lichen (available in craft stores).
5.  **Tiny "Worms":** Use black or dark green craft wire twisted into worm shapes, or small plastic worms. Glue them near the base or stems.
6.  **Finger Bones:** Glue small, articulated plastic finger bones pointing out from the base.
7.  **Miniature "Gothic" Ornaments:** Add tiny plastic spiders, bats, or even miniature black pumpkins.
8.  **Deepening the Darkness:** Use black acrylic paint to dry-brush very lightly on the *outside* of the pumpkin stem or base for a weathered, aged look. Be subtle!
9.  **Glitter Enhancement:** Your glitter idea is spot-on! Use **deep purple, black, or even a subtle "graveyard" green** glitter. Apply it to:
    *   The *stems* (make them look like gnarled, decaying wood).
    *   The *bases* (like dust or cobwebs).
    *   The *edges* of the masks or flowers (like dust or highlights).
    *   The *bones* (like grime or highlights).
10. **Miniature "Coffin" Base:** Glue a tiny black plastic coffin (or make one from cardstock) to the base of the globe so it looks like the pumpkin is sitting on it.
