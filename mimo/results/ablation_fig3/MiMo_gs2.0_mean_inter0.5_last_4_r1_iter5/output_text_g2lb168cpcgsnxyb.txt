You've identified some really critical safety and ethical issues here.

While building that connection you have is vital, the absolute most important thing for those first 20 to 30 minutes is his immediate safety and reducing environmental triggers.

Given the dogs running around and the clutter, your first priority should be to set up a small, safe, and predictable space within the room. You might need to block off an area to keep the dogs out and make sure there are no cluttered obstacles. 

Once that space is safe, you can use music and other sensory activities to engage him. It's also crucial that you report these safety concerns to the BCBA right away. Ensuring the environment is appropriate for therapy is a fundamental part of your ethical responsibility.
