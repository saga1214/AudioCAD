Here's a **practical, mess-minimized shopping list** for your theme park bachelor party, designed for 10 dads and 10 kids (10 coolers worth):

### 🥤 DRINKS (Pack in large bottles/bags)
*   **Water Bottles:** 30 large reusable bottles (10 for adults, 10 for kids + extras)
*   **Sugar-Free Sodas:** 30 cans (e.g., Sprite Zero, Coke Zero, Pepsi Zero) - *for adults*
*   **Low-Sugar Juice Boxes:** 30 boxes (Apple, Orange, Grape) - *for kids*
*   **Sparkling Water (Sugar-Free):** 15 cans or 15 bottles (adds fun fizz without sugar)
*   **Sports Drinks (Optional):** 10 boxes/packets (for active kids/adults)
*   **Ice Packs:** 3-4 (Keep everything cold!)

### 🥪 SNACKS (Single-Serve or Minimal Prep)
*   **Cheese Sticks:** 20-30 sticks (variety of flavors if possible)
*   **Grapes:** 2-3 lbs (easy, healthy, kid favorite)
*   **Individual Hummus Cups:** 10-15 cups (with baby carrots or pita chips)
*   **Pretzel Snacks:** 10-15 small bags (mini twists or sticks)
*   **Trail Mix:** 2-3 large bags (individual snack bags for cooler)
*   **Baby Carrots:** 2-3 lbs (pair with hummus cups)
*   **Apple Slices:** 3-4 lbs (sprinkle with lemon juice to prevent browning)
*   **Granola Bars:** 10-15 boxes (look for lower-sugar options)
*   **Protein Bars:** 10-15 boxes (for energy boost)

### 🍔 LUNCH (Grab-and-Go Main Dishes)
*   **Wrap Bar (Pre-Made):**
    *   **10-12 Whole Wheat Wraps:** (Use as base)
    *   **Filling Options (Prepped Separately & Pack in Cooler):**
        *   **Chicken Salad:** 4 cups (mix with mayo, celery, onion)
        *   **Tuna Salad:** 4 cups (mix with mayo, celery)
        *   **Hummus & Veggie:** 2 cups (spinach, cucumber, tomato)
        *   **Turkey & Cheese:** 4 cups (sliced turkey, cheddar, mustard)
        *   **Black Bean & Corn:** 2 cups (mix with salsa, avocado)
    *   *Wrap each lunch individually with a small napkin.*
*   **OR Sandwiches (Pre-Made):**
    *   **10-12 Sandwiches:** (PB&J, Turkey & Cheese, Ham & Cheese, Veggie - pack cut sides down)
    *   **10-12 Wrap Sandwiches:** (Similar to above, pre-filled)
*   **Optional Main:** **Mini Calzones (Pre-Order from Pizza Place):** Order 10-12 mini calzones (e.g., cheese, pepperoni, veggie). *Ensure they are individually boxed/packaged.* (This is the "real" calzone option!)

### 🍰 DESSERT (Minimal Cleanup)
*   **Option 1 (Cake Feel):** **24 Cupcakes** (with frosting, pre-pack in boxes of 6) - *Choose less sugary frosting if possible*
*   **Option 2 (Pie Feel):** **1 Large Individual Pudding Cups (Chocolate or Vanilla)** OR **1 Large Individual Key Lime Pie** - *Much easier than cutting a whole pie*
*   **Option 3 (Simpler):** **1 Large Box of Brownie Bites** (12-16) - *Pack in individual boxes*

### 🧺 COOLER ORGANIZATION TIPS
1.  **Liquids First:** Put drinks and ice packs at the bottom.
2.  **Dry Snacks First:** Place chips, pretzels, granola bars, cheese sticks, etc., in the middle.
3.  **Prepped Lunches:** Wrap sandwiches/wraps tightly and place near the top.
4.  **Desserts Last:** Keep cupcakes/pies/puddings/brownies in their own cooler or top layer.
5.  **Label Coolers:** Clearly mark each cooler's contents (e.g., "Drinks," "Snacks," "Lunches").
6.  **Single-Serve Everything:** Use individually wrapped items whenever possible.
7.  **Trash Bags:** Pack multiple small zip-top bags for easy trash disposal.

**This plan gives you:**  
✅ **Healthy & Balanced Options:** Water, fruits, veggies, hummus.  
✅ **Kid & Dad Favorites:** Cheese, soda, mini calzones, cupcakes.  
✅ **Minimal Mess:** Grab-and-go packaging, no plates needed.  
✅ **Cooler-Friendly:** Everything stays fresh and separate.
