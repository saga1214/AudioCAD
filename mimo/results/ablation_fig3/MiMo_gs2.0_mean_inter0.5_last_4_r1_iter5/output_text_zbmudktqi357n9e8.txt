Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scenes! The best time to go would be between September and November, which is their fall season. That's when you get those incredible warm colors—golden leaves, bright orange trees, and all that. 

For the most vibrant autumn colors, I'd say October is probably your sweet spot. That's when the foliage is at its peak. You could go to places like the Yucatán Peninsula or the Pacific coast for those classic autumn vibes. 

And hey, don't forget to capture those water scenes too! The Caribbean Sea is stunning in the fall, so you can practice your ocean and river techniques right there. Just pack your watercolors and markers and get ready to create!
