1.  **The Algorithm's Fuel:** TikTok's algorithm (what shows your video in FYP) primarily rewards **high engagement** – likes, comments, shares, saves, and especially watch time (especially completion rate). If people aren't interacting, the algorithm won't push it to more users.
2.  **The Hook is King:** As stressed earlier, grabbing attention *immediately* in the first 1-3 seconds is critical. Engaging content does this effectively. If viewers don't stop scrolling, your video fails at the most fundamental step.
3.  **Guidelines Are Necessary, But Not Sufficient:** Following guidelines (your #1 factor) is absolutely essential to *not get your video taken down* (making it invisible). However, even if it's compliant, if the content itself isn't engaging, it won't get pushed by the algorithm.

This is what the algorithm responds to and amplifies. Everything else (consistency, conciseness, description, hashtags) supports making your content *more* engaging or helps the algorithm find it, but engagement is the core driver of exposure.
