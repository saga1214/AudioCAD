Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your TV, planters, and shelves while staying landlord-compliant and mindful of your energy:

### 📺 1. TV Mount Installation
- **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, drill, screws, wall anchors (if not in studs), tape measure
- **Steps:**
  1. **Find studs:** Use a stud finder to locate solid wood or drywall anchors.
  2. **Mark placement:** Measure 36-48" above your stand, align with the TV center.
  3. **Test mount weight:** Ensure your mount can support your TV's weight (check manufacturer specs).
  4. **Secure anchors:** If mounting to drywall, drill pilot holes and insert anchors.
  5. **Install mount:** Attach the mount to the wall, then secure the TV.

### 🌿 2. Hanging Planters
- **Tools:** Drill, measuring tape, small nails/screws, wall anchors (if needed)
- **Steps:**
  1. **Choose height:** Install 5-6" above the area where plants will drip.
  2. **Mark points:** Use painter's tape to mark screw locations.
  3. **Install hooks:** Drill holes, insert screws, then hang planters.
  4. **Weight check:** Ensure hooks can support the planter + soil weight.

### 🪚 3. Floating Shelves
- **Tools:** Level, drill, shims (for uneven walls), wall anchors
- **Steps:**
  1. **Measure twice:** Space shelves 12-24" apart. Use a level to keep them even.
  2. **Locate studs:** Anchor to studs whenever possible for support.
  3. **Install brackets:** Drill pilot holes, insert anchors, attach brackets.
  4. **Place shelves:** Slide shelves onto brackets and secure with screws.

### ⚠️ Critical Safety Tips
- **Check weight limits:** Never exceed manufacturer load limits.
- **Use anchors:** Always use anchors in drywall (even if near studs).
- **Clean up:** Store tools/drills in a designated "work zone" to avoid clutter distractions.
- **Renter-friendly:** Use removable anchors if you won’t be there long-term.

### 📅 Sunday Decorating Strategy
1. **Time-block:** Dedicate 2-hour windows (e.g., 10 AM & 2 PM).
2. **Prep first:** Lay out all tools/furniture before starting.
3. **Focus on one area:** Install shelves first to create functional display space.
4. **Break tasks:** Mount TV before adding decor to avoid misalignment.

> 💡 **Focus Preservation Tip:** Set phone to "Do Not Disturb" during work hours. Use noise-canceling headphones if decorating noises distract you later in the week.

Remember to test all installations before leaving the room for the day. This creates a professional, functional workspace that respects your new rules while supporting your productivity!
