Here’s a **nautical-themed shopping list** designed for beach comfort, allergy safety (avoiding watermelon), and fun pirate flair—perfect for your 7-year-old’s pirate party with 7 kids total!

---

### **Appetizers & Snacks ("Pirate Treasure")**
1. **Mini slider rolls**  
   - Cut into 2" pieces. Theme: "Buried Treasure Sandwiches"  
   *Fillings:* Cheese, ham, or veggie cream cheese (allergy-friendly).  
2. **Fruit "Jewels" Skewers**  
   - Grapes, pineapple chunks, melon balls (skip watermelon!).  
   - *Theme:* "Pirate Jewels" or "Mermaid’s Gemstones."  
3. **Cheese Cubes**  
   - Pre-cut cheddar or mozzarella. Theme: "Gold Coins."  
4. **Pretzel Rods**  
   - "Pirate Ship Masts" or "Cannonballs."  
5. **Crackers**  
   - Themed boxes (e.g., "Treasure Chest" crackers).  
   - *Optional:* Smear with cream cheese + mini pepperoni ("cannon fire").  

### **Main Course ("The Pirate Ship")**  
6. **Mini Sandwiches or Wraps**  
   - Cut into ship shapes (use cookie cutters!).  
   *Fillings:* Turkey & cheese, cucumber & cream cheese.  
7. **Veggie Sticks & Dips**  
   - Bell peppers, cucumber, carrots.  
   - *Dips:* Hummus ("Cannonball Dip") or ranch ("Sea Fog Dip").  
   Pack in small cups to avoid spills!  

### **Drinks ("Sea Water" & Rum Punch for Grown-ups)**  
8. **Individual Juice Boxes**  
   - Theme: "Pirate Punch" (blueberry/purple juice).  
9. **Small Water Bottles**  
   - Clear "sea water" bottles with pirate stickers.  
10. **Allergy Note:**  
    - *Skip watermelon entirely.* Use melon *if safe*, but label clearly.  

### **Dessert ("Pirate Loot")**  
11. **Pirate Gold Crackers**  
    - Themed boxes (e.g., "Captain’s Treasure").  
12. **Fruit "Jewels"**  
    - Skewers with berries and pineapple.  
13. **Mini Chocolate Chips**  
    - "Cannonballs" or "Gold Nuggets."  

### **Packaging & Extras**  
14. **Themed Treasure Boxes**  
    - Small containers for snacks (label with pirate names).  
15. **Individual Napkins**  
    - "X Marks the Spot" or pirate flag designs.  
16. **Wet Wipes & Trash Bags**  
    - *Essential for beach cleanup!*  

---

### **Why This Works**  
- **Sand-Free:** All items are sealed, bite-sized, or in spill-proof packaging.  
- **Allergy-Safe:** No watermelon—use melon *only if confirmed safe*.  
- **Theme-Driven:** Everything ties into pirates, treasure, and the sea.  
- **Beach-Friendly:** No utensils needed—kids can eat hands-free!  

**Pro Tip:** Set up a "treasure table" with bowls for each snack item. Kids can grab "loot" like a pirate!  

Enjoy the party—your son will feel like a real pirate captain! 🏴‍☠️⚓
