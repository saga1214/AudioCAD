That's a fantastic skill to have! In fact, being able to create your own illustrations is a huge advantage for a children's book.

Here's how you could approach it: You can submit your completed manuscript and illustrations together to publishers who are looking for an 'illustrated manuscript.' This is a very common format, especially for picture books. Many publishers prefer it because it shows you have a complete vision for the book.

However, there's a very important thing to know: most publishers will still hire their own separate illustrator for the final art, even if you provide your own. The reason is that they have their own large in-house art teams and prefer to have complete control over the final look of their books.

But that's not a reason not to submit! Your illustrations are still valuable. They will show publishers that you can write and are a capable creator. If a publisher loves your book and your art, they will either decide to use your illustrations as they are, or they'll work with you to make some changes. They might even hire you to continue illustrating other books for them. It's definitely a very positive thing to do.
