Okay, standardizing everything to a scale of 100 makes for an easier comparison. Grand Theft Auto 5 stays an 8 out of 8, which becomes 800. Call of Duty and League of Legends were both sevens, so they become 700 each. Warcraft was a 20, which becomes 2000. StarCraft a 19 becomes 1900, and Hearthstone a 18 becomes 1800. And those poorly-rated ones, like Big Rigs and Superman 64, both get a 1, which is 100. Ride to Hell: Retribution is also a 1, so that's 100 as well.
