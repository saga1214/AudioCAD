Here are the classic recipes with precise ingredients and measurements for your Art Deco airport cocktail reception in Kentucky. I've also included a recommended gin cocktail and a pro tip for your bartenders:

### 1. **Old Fashioned**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon rye (e.g., Buffalo Trace or Maker’s Mark)  
  - 1 sugar cube or ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (for garnish)  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Muddle sugar cube with bitters in a mixing glass.  
  2. Add bourbon, ice, and stir until chilled (20–30 sec).  
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.  
  4. Express orange twist over drink and garnish.  

---

### 2. **Paper Plane**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz bourbon (e.g., Woodford Reserve)  
  - 0.75 oz Aperol  
  - 0.5 oz Amaro Nonino  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a coupe glass.  
  3. Garnish with lemon twist or orange slice.  

---

### 3. **Whiskey Sour**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 0.75 oz simple syrup  
  - **Optional**: 0.25 oz egg white (for texture)  
- **Instructions**:  
  - **With Egg White**:  
    1. Dry shake (without ice) all ingredients + egg white.  
    2. Add ice, shake again, strain into chilled glass.  
  - **Without Egg White**: Skip dry shake.  
  - Garnish with cherry or orange slice.  

---

### 4. **Aviation**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz gin (e.g., Hendrick’s or Bombay Sapphire)  
  - 0.75 oz maraschino liqueur (Luxardo)  
  - 0.25 oz crème de violette  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a coupe glass.  
  3. Garnish with maraschino cherry.  

---

### 5. **Gin Cocktail Recommendation: French 75**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz gin  
  - 0.75 oz lemon juice  
  - 0.5 oz simple syrup  
  - Top with 1.5 oz champagne or prosecco  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice.  
  2. Strain into champagne flute.  
  3. Top with sparkling wine.  
  4. Garnish with lemon wheel.

### **Bartender Pro Tips**  
1. **Glassware**: Use vintage-style rocks glasses for Old Fashioneds and coupes for Paper Planes/Aviations.  
2. **Egg White Safety**: Use pasteurized eggs if concerned about raw eggs.  
3. **Kentucky Twist**: Add a pinch of cinnamon to the Old Fashioned sugar cube for fall warmth.  
4. **Ice**: Use large cubes for Paper Planes/French 75 to prevent dilution.
