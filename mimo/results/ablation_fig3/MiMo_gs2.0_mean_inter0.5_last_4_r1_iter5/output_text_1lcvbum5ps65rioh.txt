### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia
1. **General Rule**: U-turns are permitted unless prohibited by a sign or traffic control device (like a stop sign or traffic light).
2. **Key Restrictions**:
   - **At Intersections**: Prohibited unless:
     - You signal clearly.
     - You yield to pedestrians, vehicles, and traffic signals.
     - You can see both ways **150 meters** in the direction you’re turning.
   - **No Obstruction**: Never block traffic or pedestrians.
   - **Specific Locations**: Prohibited on curves, near hills/obstructions, or in bus lanes.
   - **Speed**: Must be at a "reasonable speed" (no reckless maneuvers).
3. **Penalties**:
   - **Basic Error**: $155 fine + 3 points (e.g., failing to signal or yielding).
   - **Serious Violation**: Higher fines (up to $600) + 6 points (e.g., blocking traffic or ignoring visibility rules).
   - **Dangerous Driving**: Up to 12 points + possible license suspension.

**Pro Tip**: Use apps like Google Maps for real-time guidance, or check signs for "U-Turn Permitted" markers. If unsure, pull over safely first.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
**For 4 servings** (authentic Roman-style, no cream):  
- **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti (or bucatini)  
- **Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 2 egg yolks (separated)  
- **Cheese**: 100g Pecorino Romano (or Parmigiano-Reggiano) + extra for grating  
- **Protein**: 150g pancetta or guanciale (diced)  
- **Aromatics**: 1 small garlic clove (minced), 1 tbsp fresh parsley (chopped)  
- **Seasoning**: Salt, black pepper, red pepper flakes (optional)  
- **Liquid**: 2 tbsp pasta water (reserved)  

**Key Notes**:  
- **No cream**: Authentic carbonara relies on eggs and cheese.  
- **Prep**: Cook pasta *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.  
- **Technique**: Toss pasta with pancetta fat, then mix in eggs/cheese off-heat to avoid scrambling.
