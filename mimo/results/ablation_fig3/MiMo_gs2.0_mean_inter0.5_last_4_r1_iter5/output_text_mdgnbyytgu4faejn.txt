Okay, let's break this down. First, the contact info order. Moving it below your name creates a cleaner header. Stick with icons, nine-point font is fine. Order: Name largest font, then Email, Phone, City all right below in descending order of importance. It's preferred and scans better. No icons due to ATS risk. Experience strategy: Create a "Relevant Experience" section for older, ideal roles. List Job Title, Company, Dates. Then a smaller "Additional Work History" for less relevant jobs, also with just Job Title, Company, Dates. Include dates for all jobs. Gap handling: Use a strong professional summary to frame your relevant skills and goals. Education: Include in-progress degrees with "Expected" and date. List all degrees from the same school together. Skills: Very important. Create a separate "Skills" section. Tailor it to the job description. Awards: Place within the Education section for academic achievements. Now, the full format. Here’s a quick rundown: Start with Contact Info. Then a Professional Summary. Follow with Relevant Experience. Then Education. Then Additional Work History. Then Skills. Finally, any Certifications or Awards can go last. This structure is clean, traditional, and tailored for changing career paths.
