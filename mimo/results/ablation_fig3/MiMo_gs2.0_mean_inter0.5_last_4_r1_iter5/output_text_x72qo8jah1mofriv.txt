Okay, kosher dietary laws mean no pork, shellfish, or cross-contamination with non-kosher ingredients. So, based on what you already brought... the mini weenies in bacon are definitely out, as bacon is pork. So you'd need to swap those out for something like veggie-based sausages or just regular veggie dogs. The hummus from fruit and any dark chocolate would be fine, since those don't have kosher restrictions. So really, the only thing to change is the snack you brought.
