Regarding the brain's hemispheres: The right-left brain dichotomy is largely a myth. It oversimplifies how the brain works. While there are some generalizations, like the right hemisphere often being associated with creativity and the left with logic, these functions are distributed across the whole brain and are highly interconnected. It's like saying the heart is only on the left side and does all the work—that's not how the body functions. The brain is a network, and both sides contribute to everything. 

For your career, since you like structure and creativity but don’t want to be stuck at a desk all day, here are a few ideas that blend objectivity with creative impact. 

One is architecture or urban design. It’s all about building things that are both functional and beautiful, and the results are physical, lasting structures. 

Another is data visualization. It takes raw, measurable data and turns it into creative, understandable graphics. It’s objective but also artistic. 

You could also consider scientific illustration or technical writing. These fields require accuracy and structure but also creativity in how you present information. 

And finally, product design. It’s about creating objects that solve real problems, so it’s practical and measurable, but also involves a lot of creativity and aesthetics. 

All of these have a lasting impact on the world, whether it's through buildings, data, or products that people use every day.
