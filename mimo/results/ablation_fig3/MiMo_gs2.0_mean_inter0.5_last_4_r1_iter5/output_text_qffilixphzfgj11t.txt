Here are five safe green vegetables for your parrot (like Bruno Mars-loving Bruno!), avoiding apples, bananas, broccoli, and Bruno's darkness preference:

1.  **Spinach:** Rich in vitamins A, C, and K, plus iron and calcium. *Note:* Wash thoroughly and offer in moderation (high oxalates can bind calcium).
2.  **Kale (especially curly or Lacinato/Dinosaur):** Excellent source of vitamins A, C, K, and calcium. Chop finely for easier digestion. *Note:* Wash well; stems can be tough.
3.  **Collard Greens:** Packed with vitamins A, C, K, calcium, and fiber. Blanch briefly or steam to soften stems.
4.  **Green Peas (shelled):** Good source of protein, vitamins A, C, and K, and fiber. Remove seeds if large.
5.  **Bell Peppers (Green):** High in vitamins A, C, and B6, plus antioxidants and fiber. Offer fresh or steamed.

**Important Tips:**

*   **Wash Thoroughly:** Always rinse greens well under cool water to remove dirt, pesticides, or wax.
*   **Introduce Slowly:** Add new veggies one at a time to check for tolerance.
*   **Portion Control:** Offer a small amount daily (e.g., 1-2 tablespoons per medium-sized bird) as part of a varied diet.
*   **Preparation:** Most greens are best fresh. Blanching or steaming can soften tough stems and improve digestibility.
*   **Avoid:** Cruciferous veggies like broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage (can cause gas), and raw beans/legumes. Avoid iceberg lettuce (low nutritional value).

These greens provide essential vitamins, minerals, and fiber for Bruno's health!
