Organizing multiple goals is a bit like planning where the pigeons will sit! You need a clear, logical plan.

Here’s how you could approach it:

First, match each trip to a savings goal. Bolivia salt flats would go with your apartment down payment, and the Faroe Islands with your home renovation fund. That's step one—grouping them.

Next, for each trip, estimate how much it would cost. Then, divide that by the number of months you want to start saving. That gives you your monthly target. You can then set up automatic transfers to your special savings account.

Finally, just keep track of your progress. Seeing that your fund grows is incredibly motivating. This way, you can plan for your big adventures without derailing your financial stability.
