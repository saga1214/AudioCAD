That sounds like a fantastic plan! Here are five great road trip destinations from Vancouver, all with beautiful scenery and lots of activities for a new family.

First, there's the drive to Victoria. It's a short three-hour trip and you can see the iconic Victoria Harbour and the Fairmont Empress Hotel. You can take a scenic drive through the Butchart Gardens and even visit the Royal BC Museum. For a special dining experience, check out the chef's table at the Fairmont's restaurant.

Second, head to the Okanagan Lake area. It's about a four-hour drive. You can visit the beautiful Lake Okanagan and explore the Kettle Valley Parkway for stunning views. For a memorable meal, the restaurant at the Delta Lake Okanagan Resort is a great choice.

Third, you could drive to Whistler. It's about a four-and-a-half-hour trip. Even in the off-season, the scenery is breathtaking. You can go on a scenic drive along the Sea to Sky Highway and visit the Whistler Village for some beautiful crafts. The Lost Lagoon is a lovely spot for a walk.

Fourth, consider a trip to Fraser Canyon. It's a bit longer, about a five-hour drive. You can explore the Fraser Canyon Heritage Park and visit the Spatsizi Plateau Provincial Park. For a nice meal, the restaurant at the Fraser Canyon Inn is known for its local ingredients.

And fifth, you could drive to Nanaimo. It's a four-hour trip and you can visit the famous Lillooet Falls. You can also take a scenic drive along the Nanaimo Harbour and explore the Royal Canadian Air Force Museum. For a special dining experience, the restaurant at the Nanaimo Yacht Club is known for its seafood.

Happy travels!
