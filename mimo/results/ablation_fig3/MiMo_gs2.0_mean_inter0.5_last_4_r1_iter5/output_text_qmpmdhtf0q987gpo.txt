Okay, let's see where you are in the process. You've got the doors and hardware off, degreased, light sanded, and primed. That's a great start. Before you apply the primer, you'll want to make sure the whole cabinet frame is completely dust-free. A tack cloth or even a slightly damp rag wiped down will do the trick. This is super important for the primer to adhere properly.

Also, just make sure the primer itself is fully dry before you move on to the paint. You should be all set then! Good luck with your first home renovation project.
