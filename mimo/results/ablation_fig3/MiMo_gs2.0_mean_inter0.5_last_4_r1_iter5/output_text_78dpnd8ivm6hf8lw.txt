Here's a quick recap of your child-friendly circulatory system analogy:

1.  **Heart:** The gas station and engine. It pumps the blood-cars.
2.  **Lungs:** The fresh fuel station. Blood-cars stop here to pick up oxygen.
3.  **Arteries:** The big highways. Carry blood away from the heart with fresh oxygen.
4.  **Capillaries:** The tiny neighborhood streets. Where blood-cars stop to deliver oxygen to cells and pick up waste.
5.  **Veins:** The return roads. Carry blood-cars back to the heart after they've dropped off oxygen and picked up waste.
6.  **Kidneys:** The car washes/checkpoints. Clean the blood-cars by removing waste and extra water (making urine).
7.  **Liver:** The all-in-one service station. Processes nutrients, breaks down harmful stuff, stores important things.
