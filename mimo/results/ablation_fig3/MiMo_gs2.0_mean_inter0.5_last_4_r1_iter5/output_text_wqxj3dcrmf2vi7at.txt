Here's a comprehensive list of treats for your little corgi princess, categorized for easy reference:

### 🏠 Homemade Treats
1. **Frozen Pumpkin Bites**:  
   Mix mashed pumpkin with xylol-free peanut butter, press into a silicone mold, and freeze.  
   *Why she loves it*: Cool, sweet, and keeps her busy.

2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**:  
   Blend oats + xylol-free peanut butter + a pinch of flour, roll into balls, and bake until firm.  
   *Tip*: Cut into tiny cubes for training.

3. **Carrot Sticks**:  
   Steam carrots until soft, then slice into sticks.  
   *Bonus*: Healthy and great for dental health.

4. **Zucchini "Bones"**:  
   Bake zucchini strips at 350°F (175°C) for 20 mins until crisp.  
   *Perfect for*: Chewing between meals.

### 🛒 Store-Bought Treats
1. **Blue Buffalo Life Protection Bits**:  
   Small, crunchy kibble bites in flavors like chicken or salmon.  
   *Best for*: Training reinforcement.

2. **Whimzees Natural Dog Chews**:  
   Silicone chew toys infused with flavors like apple or vanilla.  
   *Ideal for*: Long-lasting engagement.

3. **Zuke's Mini Training Treats**:  
   Small, bite-sized biscuits in peanut butter or sweet potato flavors.  
   *Why try it*: Affordable and widely available.

4. **Merrick Backcountry Freeze-Dried**:  
   High-protein dehydrated meals in flavors like turkey & blueberry.  
   *Note*: Rehydrate with warm water for a mini meal!

### 🎖️ Training Tips for Princess
- **Sit Command**: Use a treat as a lure—move it over her head until she sits, then reward.  
- **Potty Training**: Take her outside on a consistent schedule (every 1-2 hours). Celebrate successes with praise + a tiny treat.  
- **Barking at Felix**: Teach a "quiet" command (reward silence) or redirect with a tug toy.

### 💖 Collar Color Ideas for Brown & White
- **Teal/Turquoise**: Pops against her coat!  
- **Royal Blue**: Elegant and eye-catching.  
- **Soft Pink**: Sweet and feminine.  
- **Gold/Silver**: Adds a regal touch.  

### 🚫 Safety Reminders
- **Never feed**: Chocolate, grapes, onions, xylitol, or alcohol.  
- **Portion control**: Treats should be <10% of daily calories.  
- **Freeze wisely**: Use ice cube trays for frozen treats to keep them cool longer.
