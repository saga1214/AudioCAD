That's wonderful! Here are some thoughtful gift ideas that will make your daughter feel truly proud and appreciated after her big performance:

**Easy & Practical:**

1.  **A Special Treat from the Food Tour:** Order a dessert or a favorite snack from the food tour itself! Tell her, "I got you the best chocolate lava cake from Thursday's tour to celebrate!" (Just make sure it's something you can get ahead of time or order for delivery).
2.  **A Nice Gift Card:** A card to her favorite store, a bookstore, or even a fun activity place like a trampoline park or a museum. "Go buy that book you wanted!" or "Go have some fun!"
3.  **A Custom Keychain or Charm:** Find something simple and personalized online or at a craft store. A charm with her initials, a small plastic keychain with a fun phrase like "Theater Star" or "Opening Night Hero," or even a mini figurine of her character.
4.  **A "Golden Ticket" Style Card:** Write a heartfelt note on nice cardstock. Inside, write something like: "You worked so hard for months! This is your golden ticket to celebrate YOU. Use it to get ice cream, a new book, or just relax. You did amazing!" (Then add a small amount of cash inside).
5.  **A Small Plant or Succulent:** A cute, easy-to-care-for plant symbolizes growth and success. "This is for all the growth you showed on stage!"

**Sentimental & Keepsake:**

6.  **A Personalized Photo Frame:** Get a frame with her name engraved or a simple "Star" or "Play" symbol. Fill it with a photo of her rehearsing or a favorite picture of her smiling. "This captures the hard work leading up to opening night!"
7.  **A Custom Playbill Frame:** If you have a program or playbill, get it framed nicely. This is a perfect keepsake of her big moment.
8.  **A Simple Jewelry Piece:** A delicate bracelet, necklace, or earrings with a charm that means something special (a star, a theater mask, her birthstone, her initials). "Something to remember your first big show."
9.  **A "Thank You" Jar:** Fill a jar with small notes. Each note could say something specific you admired: "Loved how you sounded in Act 2!", "Your dancing was amazing!", "I loved your smile when you came out!", "You were so brave!" She can pull one out whenever she needs a boost.

**For the Experience:**

10. **Tickets to a Future Show:** If she loves performing, maybe she'd love to see another play, concert, or even a local community show? "Let's go see another show together soon!"
11. **A Fun Activity Voucher:** "You earned this! Let's go to the trampoline park, the science museum, or the zoo next weekend!"

**Tips for Giving:**

*   **Timing:** Give the gift to her *after* the show, ideally soon after she's had a moment to process (but before you head to your food tour if you're both going). A heartfelt hug and a specific compliment ("You were so brave and funny when you came out on stage!") are priceless too.
*   **Presentation:** Wrap it nicely if it's a physical item. Even a simple gift bag with the chocolate cake or ice cream works wonders!
*   **Focus on Effort:** Emphasize how proud you are of the *months* of hard work she put in. "I'm so proud of how hard you practiced!" is more meaningful than just "You did great!"

**Remember:** The most important thing is your genuine pride and support. Pairing a small, thoughtful gift with a heartfelt "I am so incredibly proud of you" will mean the world to her. Enjoy your food tour and massage!
