### 🐾 Treat Recommendations:
* **Best Options:** Small, soft, and smelly treats work best (like chicken, cheese, hot dog bits, peanut butter, or freeze-dried liver). Avoid anything too large or hard.
* **Safety First:** Never give chocolate, grapes, onions, or xylitol-containing treats.
* **Training Treats:** Use low-value treats during training (e.g., kibble) and higher-value rewards (like chicken) for harder tasks.
* **Italian Roots Idea:** Try small pieces of cooked chicken, turkey, or cheese (in moderation).

### 🔍 Clicker Training Explained:
Clicker training uses a **three-step process** to teach reliably:
1. **Clicker as Marker:** The clicker makes a sharp, consistent sound ("click!") to *mark* the exact moment your puppy does something right.
2. **Reward Immediately:** Right after the click, give a treat. The puppy learns that "click + treat" = good behavior.
3. **Build Commands:** Practice short sessions (5-10 mins). Click when they sit, give a treat, repeat. Gradually add distractions.

**Example:**  
You hold a treat near your puppy’s nose. As he lowers his head, click! Immediately give the treat. Repeat until he starts sitting on cue.

### 🛠️ Training Tools to Consider:
| **Tool**          | **Purpose**                                                                 |

| **Leash**         | Essential for walks and recall practice. Choose a lightweight, adjustable one. |
| **Training Collar** | Harnesses are safer for necks; collars work for short distances (e.g., puppy-proofing). |
| **Clicker**       | Reinforces precise timing of rewards. Start with 1-2 clicks per session.    |
| **Treat Dispenser** | Keeps training treats fresh and ready.                                      |

### 🌟 Pro Tips:
1. **Short & Fun:** Keep sessions brief (3-5 mins) to avoid frustration.
2. **Praise + Toss:** Use a happy tone ("Good boy!") and toss treats in a circle so he looks at you.
3. **Practice Daily:** Even 2-3 short sessions build trust and skills faster.
4. **Consistency:** Use the same command words (e.g., "sit," "stay") and rewards every time.

**For Recall Training:** If your puppy escapes, practice recall indoors first. Call his name + "Galileo, come!" with a treat in your hand. Reward heavily when he comes. Gradually increase distance and add distractions outdoors.
