### **Art & Culture in Raleigh**
1. **North Carolina Museum of Art**

2. **CAM Raleigh**  
   - Focuses on cutting-edge contemporary art installations.  
3. **Artspace Raleigh**  
   - Mixed-use arts district with artist studios, galleries, and creative spaces downtown.  

### **Art & Culture in Florence, SC**
1. **Florence County Museum**  
   - Historical and regional art exhibits with local artifacts.  
2. **Lynda English Studio Gallery**  
   - Showcases local artists’ works (paintings, sculptures, textiles).

1. **The Pit**  
   - Popular for mid-range barbecue (classic smoked meats, sides).  
2. **Clyde Cooper’s Barbeque**  
   - Old-school, casual spot for Southern BBQ.
