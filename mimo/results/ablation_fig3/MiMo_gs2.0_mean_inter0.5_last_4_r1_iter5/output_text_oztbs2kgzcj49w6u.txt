Lockport is called the "City of Locks" for a very good reason. The Erie Canal is the heart of the town's identity and history. The best place to start your exploration is the Lockport Canal Museum. It's located right at the canal's head of navigation and gives you a great overview of the canal's history, engineering, and even has working model locks you can see in action. The museum also has a replica of a historic canal boat called the "Canal Queen" that you can walk through. For a more hands-on experience, you could visit the nearby Erie Canal Heritage Center, which has interactive exhibits and a replica of a canal boat called the "Water Witch." If you prefer to see the locks in person, you can walk along the canal's towpath, which runs right alongside them. It's a great way to see the scale of the engineering. And finally, Lockport is home to the very first canal boat ever to be pulled up the Niagara Escarpment, which is now on display at the Lockport Historical Society.
