The term 'forever chemicals' is a bit of a misnomer. Your body can eliminate them, but it's a slow process. There isn't a magic cure like a juice cleanse; it's really more about prevention and reducing your intake.

Okay, to get you a clear list of things to avoid, here are the top five:

First, non-stick cookware, like pans with a Teflon coating.

Second, grease-resistant packaging, such as fast-food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags.

Third, stain- and water-resistant fabrics and home goods, like carpets and outdoor clothing.

Fourth, certain cosmetics and even some types of dental floss.

And fifth, since they can get into tap water, using a certified water filter is a great way to reduce exposure.
