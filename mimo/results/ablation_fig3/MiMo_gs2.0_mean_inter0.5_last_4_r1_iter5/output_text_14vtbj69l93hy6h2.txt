That's totally normal! Every dog has different tastes. Here are some tasty, small treat options to try with Maisy:

### 🧀 Treat Alternatives:
1. **Plain Boiled Chicken**  
   - Shred into tiny bits (1/8" pieces).  
   - Highly enticing for Maltese!  
2. **Peanut Butter**  
   - Use *xylitol-free* peanut butter (no sugar).  
   - Offer a pea-sized smear on your finger.  
3. **Fish Treats**  
   - Salmon or tuna bites (check for additives).  
4. **Freeze-Dried Liver**  
   - Try "training liver" bits from brands like Royal Canin or Blue Buffalo.  
5. **Cheese Crumbles**  
   - Mild cheddar or cottage cheese (small pieces).  

### 🐶 Training Tips:
- **Size Matters:** Treats should be smaller than your fingertip.  
- **Timing:** Reward the *instant* she performs the trick.  
- **Patience:** Short sessions (5 mins) > long, stressful ones.  
- **Environment:** Train in a quiet area with minimal distractions.  

### 💡 Pro Tip:  
If Maisy ignores treats, try **positive reinforcement** first:  
- Praise enthusiastically ("Good girl!").  
- Use a clicker (click → treat → praise).  
- Gradually phase out treats by rewarding only when she *volunteers* the behavior.  

> "Training should feel like play, not a test. Maisy’s quirks will guide you—what works for one dog might not work for another. Trust her cues!"
