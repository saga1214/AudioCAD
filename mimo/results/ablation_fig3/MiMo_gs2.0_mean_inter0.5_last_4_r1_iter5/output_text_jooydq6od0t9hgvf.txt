Here are quick, elegant drink recipes for both cocktails — ready in under 5 minutes:

### 1. **Vodka "Siesta" (Refreshing Vodka Mixer)**
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz fresh lemonade (or lemon juice + simple syrup)  
- 1 oz sparkling water  
- Ice + lemon wedge  
**Method:**  
Shake vodka + lemonade with ice. Strain into a chilled glass. Top with sparkling water. Garnish with lemon wedge.  
*Why it works:* Light, fizzy, and citrusy — soda-free and crowd-pleasing.

### 2. **"Argentine Fernet" (DIY Fernet-Branca Style)**
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz Fernet-Branca (or substitute with ginger beer for spice)  
- 1 oz soda water  
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice  
- Ice + lemon twist  
**Method:**  
Shake Fernet + lemon juice with ice. Strain into a coupe glass. Top with soda water. Express lemon twist over drink.  
*Why it works:* Nailed the Argentine vibe — herbal, spicy, and effervescent. Ginger beer adds depth if Fernet is unavailable.

### Key Tips:
- **Soda Allergy?** Both recipes use sparkling water/soda water — no soda or artificial sweeteners.  
- **Formality:** Serve in chilled glasses with simple garnishes (lemon wedge, twist).  
- **Age Mix:** These are approachable for all generations — no games to learn.
