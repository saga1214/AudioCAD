### 🌊 **Top Dive Sites in Costa Rica**
1. **Cabo Blanco Marine Park (West Coast)**  
   - **Why**: Crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral reefs, and abundant marine life (turtles, rays, dolphins).  
   - **Must-see**: The "Sardine Run" (seasonal) where thousands of sardines flee from predators.  
   - **Best for**: Beginners & intermediates; easy access from Limón.

2. **Tortuguero National Park**  
   - **Why**: Mangrove forests, sea turtles (nesting season: June–August), and the "Sea Turtle Conservancy."  
   - **Unique Experience**: Night dives to see hatchlings enter the ocean.  
   - **Bonus**: Canoe through rivers to remote dive sites.

3. **Cahuita National Park**  
   - **Why**: Coral reefs teeming with tropical fish, octopuses, and reef sharks.  
   - **Highlights**: The "Coral Garden" and "Lighthouse Reef."

4. **Arenal Volcano Area**  
   - **Why**: Thermal vents and unique volcanic ecosystems.  
   - **Site**: **Arenal 1968** (steep walls, strong currents for advanced divers).

5. **Isabela & San Isidro (Ostional)**  
   - **Why**: Sea turtle nesting beaches (May–November) and easy shore dives.  
   - **Bonus**: Nearby **Cenote Actun Ha** for freshwater cave diving.

### 🧭 **Trip Itinerary (10 Days)**  
- **Days 1–3**: **Limón & Cabo Blanco**  
  - Dive sites: Cabo Blanco, Puerto Viejo.  
  - Stay: Eco-lodge near the park.  
- **Days 4–5**: **Tortuguero**  
  - Boat tour to mangrove dives, turtle conservation visit.  
- **Days 6–7**: **Cahuita**  
  - Coral reef snorkeling/diving, national park visit.  
- **Days 8–9**: **Arenal**  
  - Advanced dives at Arenal 1968, thermal vent exploration.  
- **Day 10**: **Ostional**  
  - Turtle nesting tours (if in season), Isabela Island.  

### 🧤 **Gear & Logistics**  
- **Fins**: Start with **short fins** (maneuverable, compact). Upgrade to long fins if you dive strong currents (e.g., Arenal 1968).  
- **Booking**: Use **Costa Rica Dive** or **Coral Reef Adventures** for guided tours.  
- **PADI Centers**:  
  - **Cabo Blanco** (Limón)  
  - **Tortuguero Dive Center**  
  - **Arenal Dive Center**  

### 💡 **Pro Tips**  
- **Best Time**: **Dry Season (November–April)** for calm waters.  
- **Marine Life**: Humpback whales (Jan–Mar), sea turtles (year-round), manta rays (Cabo Blanco).  
- **Travel**: Rent a car for flexibility or use shuttles between sites.  
- **Sustainability**: Choose eco-certified operators (e.g., **Rainforest Alliance**).
