Okay, since Northern California cold is setting in and swimming is high on your list, while your budget is healthy, I'd suggest a trip to either Palm Springs or Carmel-by-the-Sea. Both are great year-round, but Palm Springs is definitely the winner for guaranteed warm weather and swimming. Carmel is beautiful, but the ocean's water is cool.

Let's go with Palm Springs. It's about a four to four-and-a-half-hour drive from Northern California, and it's perfect for a four-to-five-day trip in the middle of November. The weather will be warm, usually in the seventies and eighties, and there are plenty of pools and even some warm ocean spots.

Here’s a rough itinerary for you.

Day one is your arrival day. You'll drive into Palm Springs, which is about four hours. When you get there, you can check into your hotel. I'd suggest the Hotel Palmore for a luxury experience, or the Hotel Palmdale for a more boutique feel. After you settle in, you can grab some dinner at a place called Elmer's Restaurant. It's a classic spot for a great salad bar and casual Southern California food. For travel, a rental car is a must, and you can easily book one through a service like Enterprise.

Day two is all about luxury and pools. In the morning, you could take a tour of the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway for some incredible views. For lunch, a great gluten-free spot is The Spa at the Ace Hotel. In the afternoon, it's pool time! You could check out the pools at the Hyatt Regency or the El Paseo Hotel, which has a stunning pool area. For dinner, you could splurge at Nobu Palm Springs for some amazing sushi.

Day three is for more adventure. You could start by visiting the Palm Springs Art Museum. Then for lunch, there's a fun spot called The French Laundry, which is famous for its food. In the afternoon, you could take a trip to the nearby Coachella Valley to see some of the beautiful Spanish-style architecture. For dinner, I'd suggest the Indian restaurant, The Mess Hall, which is fantastic and has gluten-free options.

Then in the afternoon, you could visit the Palm Springs State Park. For your last night, a wonderful dinner at the upscale restaurant Bouchon would be a perfect way to end your trip.

And for day five, your departure day, you can have a final breakfast at a place called The Breakfast Club, then just drive home.

As for packing, since you want that bathing suit visible, pack your swimsuits, a cover-up, sunglasses, and a hat. A light sweater is still a good idea for the evenings. And since your daughter is gluten-free, Trish's Mini Donuts in Palm Springs is a must-visit treat. 

I hope you have a wonderful vacation!
