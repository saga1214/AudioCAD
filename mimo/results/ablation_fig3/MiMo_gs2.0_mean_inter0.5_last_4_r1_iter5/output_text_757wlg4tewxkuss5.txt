That's a thoughtful question! Here's how to handle it:

1. **For the Vegan Friend:**  
   Standard frozen hamburgers are **not vegan** (they contain animal products like meat, dairy, or eggs). **Solution:**  
   → Bring **plant-based frozen burgers** (e.g., Beyond Meat, Impossible, or other vegan brands).  
   → Clearly label them so everyone knows what they are.  

2. **For Food Safety & Inclusivity:**  
   → **Use a cooler with ice packs** for perishable items (including the burgers), even if it's sunny.  
   → If possible, pack burgers in sealed containers to avoid cross-contamination.  

3. **Bonus Tip for Your Diverse Group:**  
   → Offer **non-meat alternatives** like veggie burgers or tofu skewers for everyone (including non-vegans).  
   → The food pantry volunteer might appreciate if you donate extra burgers afterward!
