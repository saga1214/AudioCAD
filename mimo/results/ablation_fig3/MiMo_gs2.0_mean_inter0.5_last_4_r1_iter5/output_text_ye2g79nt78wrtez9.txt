Yeah, after thinking it through, I'd say Ancient Egypt is the most viable choice. It's just more accessible to a general audience. Even though the artifacts aren't always available, the stories of the pharaohs and pyramids are so well-known that you can still create a powerful exhibit. The Mesopotamia exhibit, while historically incredibly important, risks being too niche for a broad audience. The goal is to educate and engage as many people as possible, and Egypt's familiarity is a big advantage there.
