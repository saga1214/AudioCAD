Okay, this is a delicate balance. You need to be professional and respectful, but also firm and clear about your concerns. Here's a good approach.

First, use a professional subject line, like "Meeting Request: Exam Feedback - [Your Name]". In the email, you can start by saying something like, "I hope this email finds you well. I scored a 64 on my latest exam and wanted to learn more about the grading criteria to improve on essays in the future."

Then, you can add a specific question. For example, "I was wondering if you could share more about how the subjective content was evaluated for this particular exam?"

Finally, ask to meet, saying something like, "If you have some time available, I'd be grateful for the opportunity to discuss this further. Please let me know what time might work for you."

This keeps the focus on your growth and avoids any accusation.
