That's impossible to say for sure for both groups that day. For the Pilgrims, it was likely a moment of genuine celebration. For the Wampanoag, while they were invited guests and part of a political deal, the perspective of many Native people includes remembering their ancestors as survivors of a profound and devastating historical trauma.

So, you can't assume it was a "happy occasion" for everyone in the same way. Acknowledging this complexity in your children's play would be very meaningful.
