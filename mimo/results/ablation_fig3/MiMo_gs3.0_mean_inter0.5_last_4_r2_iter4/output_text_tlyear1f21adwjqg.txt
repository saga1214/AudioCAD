This is a tough final choice, since efficiency can mean different things—like cost, scalability, consistency, or availability. Solar leads overall because it's everywhere, scalable from rooftop to massive farms, and getting cheaper. But hydropower is a close second, especially where pre-existence infrastructure is available. Wind is also great in specific, windy locations. So if I absolutely had to pick just one and look at the whole picture, I’d still stick with solar energy.
