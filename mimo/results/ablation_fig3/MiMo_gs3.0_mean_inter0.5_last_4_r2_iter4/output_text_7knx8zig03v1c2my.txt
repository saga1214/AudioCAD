### Making a Crate Cozy vs. Mean  
Crates are **den-like**, helping dogs feel secure—not caged. To cozy it up:  
- **Comfort items**: Add a soft blanket, bed, or even his favorite toy inside.  
- **Positive association**: Feed meals in the crate (with the door open), hide treats, or place a water bowl nearby.  
- **Never use for punishment**: Only close the door when he’s calm (e.g., overnight).  
Without a fence, always leash him in the yard. If he escapes:  
Stay C**alm**, crouch low, call excitedly with treats/toys ("Galileo, HOME!"), and run toward him (chasing can turn into a game).

---

### Treat Recommendations  
Best flavors/flours for training:  
- **Soft, small bites**: Cheese cubes, cooked chicken, peanut butter (no xylitol), or freeze-dried liver.  
- **Commercial treats**: Look for low-sugar, high-protein options (e.g., "Pupcakes" or "Ziwi Peak"). Italian-inspired? Try truffle-infused treats!  

---

### Clicker Training Explained  
A clicker is a **sound marker** teaching Galileo what behavior earns a reward:  
1. **Step 1**: Click when he does something right (e.g., sits).  
2. **Step 2**: Immediately give a treat.  
3. **Step 3**: Repeat! The click = "Yes! Do it again!"  
Over time, he learns to perform the action to hear the click/treat.
