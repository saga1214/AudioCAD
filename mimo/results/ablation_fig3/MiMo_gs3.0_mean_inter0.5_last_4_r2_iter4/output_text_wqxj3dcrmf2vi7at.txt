Here’s a full recap of yummy homemade and store-bought ideas for your brown-and-white corgi princess, organized by type:

**Homemade Homemade Treats:**  
1. **Frozen Bites:**  
   - Mix mashed pumpkin/sweet potato with a tiny splash of applesauce, pack into silicone molds, freeze.  
   - *Bonus:* Peppermint extract (tiny drop!) for a fun flavor.  

2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies:**  
   - Mix ¼ cup peanut butter (检查无 xylitol!) + ½ cup rolled oats. Roll into balls, flatten with a cookie press. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 mins. Cool completely.  

3. **Chicken/Carrot Sticks:**  
   - Cut cooked chicken breast or sweet potato/carrot into thin matchstick shapes.  

**Easy Store-Bought Brands:**  
- **Blue Buffalo** (Brownie Bites, Zinger Chews)  
- **Zuke’s** (Mini Zuke’s, Small World)  
- **Merrick** (训练零食, Tiny Bites)  
- ** Wellness** (训练零食, 无谷物)  
- **Greenies** (牙齿护理零食)  

**Training Tips:**  
For commands like "sit," pair with treats: Hold treat near nose, lift high while saying "sit." Reward instantly when her bottom touches the floor!  

**Training Troubles:**  
For potty training:  
- Take her outside on a schedule (every 1-2 hours, after meals).  
- Use an enzymatic cleaner for accidents to remove all odors.  
- Reward heavily when she goes outside!  

**Collar Color Picks:**  
Teal/turquoise, soft pink, or royal blue pop beautifully against brown and white coats! Choose something that makes her look even more precious.  

Always prioritize safety: Portion control is key, avoid xylitol, and supervise new treats! Happy snacking, Princess! 🐾💖
