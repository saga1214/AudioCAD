**The Route Blueprint** (West Coast → Joshua Tree → Nevada → Vegas return)
1. **Day 1: Desert Immersion (Joshua Tree, CA)**  
   - Drive: 3hrs from SoCal. Pack layers (desert nights are chilly!).  
   - Free dispersed camping: **Skull Rock Campground** (no reservations, first-come) or **Barker Dam** (official $20/night). Stargaze at Salvation Mountain.  
   - Adventure: Hike Hidden Valley Trail (rock formations), explore Cholla Cactus Garden at sunset.  
   - *Why unique?* Moonlight boulders, petroglyphs, and that surreal silence.  

2. **Day 2: Nevada Desert Expansion**  
   Drive 1.5hrs to **Valley of Fire, NV** ($20/night official campgrounds or BLM free sites near **Pleasant Valley**).  
   - Must-sees: Fire Wave arches, ancient petroglyphs, Crystal Cave (unofficial).  
   - Nevada twist: Stop at a quirky small town like **Pioche** (saloon vibes) or **Ely** (old mining ghost town).  

3. **Day 3: Black Rock外围探秘 → Vegas Luxury**  
   - *Optional Burn adjacent:* Visit **Black Rock堆积区** near Germer Crater (burn remnants scattered here legally). NO central粉艺城 access without ticket.

     - **Luxury:** Bellagio fountain show + spa (book ahead).  
     - **Budget:** Free pool at Fremont Street (Old Vegas).  
     - *Pro tip:* Get a room near the Strip but outside the main casino district for quiet nights.  

**Budget Breakdown**  
- Gas: $400-600 total (depending on route).  
- Camping: $60-100 total (mix free/official).  
- Vegas: $200-400 (hotel + 1-2 activities).  

**Adventure Non-Negotiables**

- Solar charger for phones.  
- Emergency kit (water, first aid, blanket).
