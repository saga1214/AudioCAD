You're right, I see where the confusion came from. Let me clarify. Equaling bourbon/vermouth/Campari is actually considered the original, traditional Boulevardier from way back. Later on, the one-and-a-half-ounce bourbon-to-one-ounce-sweet-vo version became more popular. So, to answer your question, for a classic Boulevardier, use one ounce of each ingredient: bourbon, sweet vermouth, and Campari. As for your other question, the Last Word is an entirely different cocktail. It's made with equal parts of gin at three-quarters of an ounce, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice, all stirred together for a bright, herbal drink. Hope that helps!
