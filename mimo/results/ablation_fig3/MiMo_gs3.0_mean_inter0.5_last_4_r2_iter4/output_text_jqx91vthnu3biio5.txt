### Office Tweaks Recap & Refinements
1. **Desk Orientation**: Window-facing wins for light/vibe *if* glare/glare is truly minimal. Side placement strikes the ideal balance.  
2. **Monitor Placement**: Centered at eye level, angled to avoid glare from the window.  
3. **Standing Desk/Walking Pad**: Great for bursts of movement during meetings or tasks. Real studies (like those from the *Journal of Occupational Ergonomics*) show alternating positions reduces fatigue and improves focus by 10–15%.  
4. **Lighting**: Avoid overhead lighting. Use cool-white LEDs as primary + warm desk lamps for ambiance.  
5. **Chair**: Ergonomics are worth the investment if you sit long-term. Prioritize lumbar support and adjustability.

### Dinner Ideas (Affordable Mediterranean/Mediterranean-Inspired)
1. **Lemon-Herb Tuna Salad with Whole-Grain Crackers & Veggies**  
   - Mix canned tuna (in water), diced cucumber, cherry tomatoes, Kalamata olives, lemon juice, olive oil, oregano, and a pinch of salt. Serve with whole-grain crackers, carrot sticks, and bell peppers.  
   - *Why it fits*: Quick, healthy, uses pantry staples, and has that Mediterranean zest.  

2. **Simple Pasta with White Beans, Spinach & Garlic**  
   - Cook pasta (penne or fusilli). Sauté minced garlic in olive oil, add canned white beans, fresh spinach, and a splash of pasta water. Toss with cooked pasta, lemon zest, and red pepper flakes.  
   - *Why it fits*: Comforting, protein-rich, and minimal prep.  

3. **Chickpea & Avocado Salad Wraps**  
   - Mash chickpeas with diced avocado, red onion, parsley, lemon juice, and olive oil. Spread on whole-wheat tortillas with hummus and shredded carrots. Top with feta or olives.  
   - *Why it fits*: Plant-based, vibrant flavors, and uses common ingredients.  

4. **Salmon with Roasted Lemon-Dill Asparagus**  
   - Season salmon fillet with salt, pepper, and lemon-dill butter. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 12–15 mins. Serve with asparagus tossed in olive oil, salt, pepper, and lemon zest.  
   - *Why it fits*: Lean protein, under 15 mins active time, and feels special without "fortunes."
