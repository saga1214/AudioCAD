That is a **huge** advantage. Self-published children's books almost always require an author-illustrator because publishers are often looking for a single person to handle both. If you can illustrate your own book, you should absolutely consider self-publishing.

Here’s how it works. First, you'll work with a professional to design the book's interior layout. Second, you'll choose a print-on-demand service, like Amazon KDP or IngramSpark, which will handle printing and shipping. Third, you'll set the price for the book. And fourth, you'll promote it online and in local stores.

This path gives you full creative control and one hundred percent of the profits. The risk of theft disappears completely since you publish it yourself.
