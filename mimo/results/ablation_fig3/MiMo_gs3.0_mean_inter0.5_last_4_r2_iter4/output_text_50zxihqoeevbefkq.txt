Okay, hosting 24 guests including 4 kids requires serious planning, but I've got you covered! Based on our menu discussions, here's a comprehensive shopping list organized by category. I've included kid-friendly touches throughout to make everyone feel included.

**Drinks**
Champagne (one bottle per 10 adults minimum - 3 recommended) or sparkling wine
Grape juice (4-5 bottles)
Kid signature cocktail (e.g., Sparkle Punch: juice, soda, maybe fruit slices)
Ice (lots!)
Sparkling water
Soda water
Kombucha or other non-alcoholic options if needed

**Kid Involvement & Mess Prevention**
- Glazed carrots alternative: Roasted carrots w/o glaze (olive oil/salt roasted)
- Provide kid flutes for grape juice during toast
- Consider: 
  * Mini cotton balls with pipe cleaners for simple crafts
  * "Sugar scrub" playdough with glitter
  * Simple games like pin the tail on the donkey

**Produce**
Onions (for sliders/potatoes/asparagus)
Carrots (for roasted version)
Celery (for shrimp cocktail/sliders)
Garlic & ginger (for作为滑毯猪肺的馅料)
Mushrooms (for pigs in a blanket/asparagus)
Lemon (for asparagus)
Fresh herbs (parsley/dill for garnish)
Optional: colorful veggies for signature cocktail garnish

**Proteins & Dairy**
Pork loin ham (12-14 lbs for 24 people - check weight per pound online)
Ground beef/pork for sliders/pigs in blanket
Butter for baking flutes/cornbread (optional side)
Eggs (for deviled eggs appetizer?)

**Frozen**
Mini slider buns/pigs in a blanket rolls
Asparagus tips (fresh hard to find year-round)

**Pantry Staples**
Sliders/pigs in a blanket meatballs
Mini slider patties
Pigs in a blanket sausage
Mini corn muffin liners (for ham)
Butter
Olive oil
Salt & pepper
Potato flakes (for scalloped potatoes)
Potato granules (for scalloped potatoes)
Cornstarch (for scalloped potatoes)
Sugar (for signature cocktail)
Grape jelly (for signature cocktail)
Kid cocktail mixers (juice/soda)
Flour (for cornbread)
Baking powder (for cornbread)
Kid hat noisemakers/balons (decoration)

**Bakery**
Ham cornbread mix (or make your own)
Mini slider buns
Pigs in a blanket rolls
Kid cocktail cups

**Equipment Check**
- Sliders grill/oven
- Roasting pan for ham
- Potato gratin dish
- Asparagus pan
- Shrimp cocktail platter
- Pigs in a blanket tray
- Kid flutes & juice
- Activity supplies (cotton balls, pipe cleaners)
- Decorations (balloons, festive hats/noisemakers)

**Timing Tips**
- Roast ham 4-6 hours before serving (reheat last 30 mins)
- Make scalloped potatoes ahead
- Prep sliders/pigs in a blanket fillings 1 day ahead
- Assemble sliders/pigs in a blanket day-of
- Grill appetizers 1-2 hours before party

This plan keeps mess potential low while still celebrating traditions. The roasted carrots won't stain clothes, kid flutes add festive fun, and cornbread/corn muffins provide hearty comfort food. Most prep can be done days ahead to reduce stress! Enjoy your elegant yet fun NYE party!
