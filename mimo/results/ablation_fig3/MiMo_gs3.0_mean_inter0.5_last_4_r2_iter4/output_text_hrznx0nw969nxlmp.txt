That's a fantastic spot to be! Secret Harbour Beach Resort is perfect because Secret Harbour Bay is calm, beautiful, and right on their doorstep. Here are some great daytime activities near your resort in Secret John:

1.  **Snorkel the Secret John Snorkel Trail:** This is the absolute best part! Just steps from Secret Harbour, you can snorkel directly from shore along a trail that winds through the bay. You'll see vibrant coral, schools of fish, eels, and maybe even a turtle! Calm conditions make it pressure-free.
2.  **Explore Anemone Wall:** Walk about 5-7 minutes down the path from Secret Harbour towards Cinnamon Bay. You'll find "Anemone Wall," a stunning snorkel spot famous for its colorful anemones and other marine life. The walk is flat and easy.
3.  **Visit Reef Bay:** Take the short walk down to Reef Bay. You can:
    *   **Snorkel the Reef Bay Snorkel Trail:** Another shore snorkel spot with interesting rock formations and coral.
    *   **Kayak:** Rent kayaks from shops near Gallows Point/resort and paddle around Reef Bay, exploring the lighthouse ruins and the bay's calmer areas.
    *   Cruz Bay Lighthouse: A short walk to the top for great views over Cruz Bay and the surrounding islands.
4.  **Kayak Coral Bay:** Another great shore launch spot is Coral Bay, just north of Secret Harbour. Paddle through the mangroves and explore the calmer waters of Coral Bay.
5.  **Explore Coral Bay:** Walk down to Coral Bay (a short distance from Secret Harbour). Enjoy the colorful shops, restaurants, and historic Fort Christian. It's a lovely place for a relaxed stroll.
6.  Maho Bay (If Conditions are Perfect & Calm): *Only if* you have a calm, flat sea day and your boat is anchored in the bay (not the open ocean). Maho Bay is legendary for its calm, shallow waters teeming with fish and turtles. Snorkeling here is incredible. **Crucial:** Only suggest if conditions are genuinely calm and pressure changes are minimal – otherwise, Maho Bay's open water can have more pressure variation. Your captain will know best.
7.  **Relax & Explore Cruz Bay:** Gallows Point Resort puts you steps from Cruz Bay shops, restaurants, and cafes. Spend a day browsing boutiques, grabbing ice cream, or just soaking up the village atmosphere.

**Key things to remember for your trip:**

*   **Ask Your Captain:** They know St. John best! They can suggest the best spots based on weather, tide, and what's accessible from your boat.
*   **Pressure Sensitivity:** Stick to calm, sheltered bays like Secret Harbour, Reef Bay, Coral Bay, and Maho Bay (only if calm). Avoid open ocean conditions or areas with strong currents/waves where pressure changes might be more noticeable *in the water*.
*   **Rentals:** Shops in Cruz Bay rent snorkel gear, kayaks, paddleboards, and sometimes even small dinghies for exploring coves.

Enjoy your week sailing the beautiful bays of St. John!
