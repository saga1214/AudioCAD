For Halloween candy, it's always a good idea to get the classic favorites that pretty much everyone loves. Reeses, Snickers, Milky Way, and 3 Musketeers are always popular. But if you want some Halloween-themed candy, you could grab some of the plastic, novelty-sized ones like a large box of Hershey's Kisses or some gummies shaped like pumpkins or ghosts. You could also get some of the individually wrapped candy bags or bars, like Twix, Kit Kats, or Skittles, because they’re easy for guests to grab and take home. If you want something healthier, you could also set out some fruit snacks or yogurt-covered raisins, but the main thing is just to get the classics. Don’t worry, guests will love it!
