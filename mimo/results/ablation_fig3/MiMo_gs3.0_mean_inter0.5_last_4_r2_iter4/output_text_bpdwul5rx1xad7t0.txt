Here are five fantastic road trip ideas from Vancouver!

First, for a classic ocean trip, head to the San Juan Islands. You can take a ferry to San Juan Island and rent a car. A must-do is a whale-watching tour. For food, you absolutely have to try the oysters at Oyster Society.

Second, for some mountain scenery, you can drive to Whistler. The drive up the Sea-to-Sky Highway is spectacular. Once you're there, you can hike a beautiful alpine lake like Green Lake. For dinner, the Whistler Village has many great options.

Third, if you want a quick escape, drive to the Okanagan Valley. You'll find amazing vineyards and wineries. You can tour them and even take a wine-tasting cruise on the lakes. For food, the local farmers' markets are a wonderful place to find fresh fruit.

Fourth, for a trip that's more about nature, you can drive to Banff in Alberta. The drive through the Rocky Mountains is breathtaking, with the famous Icefields Parkway. You'll see incredible waterfalls and wildlife. For food, Banff has some world-class restaurants.

And fifth, for a trip with a bit of history, you could drive to Victoria and then continue to the Vancouver Island wineries. You can tour some of the island's vineyards, which are known for their Pinot Noir. For a special meal, check out the fine dining options in Victoria's inner harbour.

Enjoy your explorations!
