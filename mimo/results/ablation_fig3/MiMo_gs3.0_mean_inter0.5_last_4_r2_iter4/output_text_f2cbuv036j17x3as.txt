For diesel camper vans, pre-trip maintenance is really important. For brakes, some signs of significant wear would be if you hear a grinding noise when you brake, or if the brake pedal feels like it’s going all the way down before stopping. You could also look for dust or shiny spots on the rotors, which can indicate wear. If you’re comfortable, you can check by removing the wheel and looking at the pads themselves—if they’re below a certain thickness, they might need replacing. As for spark plugs, in a diesel engine, they’re not quite like a gasoline engine’s. You can’t just pull them out with a normal socket. You’d typically need a special tool, like a spark plug socket with a long extension, and a torque wrench to tighten them properly. It’s a process that requires some skill, so if you’re not used to it, it might be safer to have a mechanic check them or replace them for you, especially since diesel engines are a bit different. Either way, a good pre-trip inspection is always a good idea!
