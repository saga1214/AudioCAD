Absolutely! Southern California in the winter is perfect for swimming and warm weather. Since you'll be traveling as a family of three, have a bigger budget, and want warm swims, Big Sur is an incredible choice. It combines stunning scenery with fantastic spots to relax.

Here’s a detailed four-day itinerary for you.

On Day One, you'll arrive in Carmel-by-the-Sea. I'd suggest you fly into San Francisco and rent an SUV to navigate the coastal roads. Drive down to Carmel, check into a nice hotel like the historic Hotel Carmel Villas, and then have a lovely dinner at Nepenthe, a restaurant with breathtaking cliffside views.

For Day Two, you'll drive the famous 17-Mile Drive, which goes through Pebble Beach. You can swim or snorkel at the private Spyglass Inn Beach. For lunch, stop at a spot called Natural Elements. Afterwards, explore the beautiful Bixby Creek Bridge and then head to Santa Barbara. You can stay at the historic Hotel Milo and enjoy a nice seafood dinner at Boathouse Restaurant right on the waterfront.

On Day Three, you'll continue south into Malibu. You can swim at either El Matador Beach, which has amazing rock formations, or Zuma Beach. For lunch, grab a famous smoothie from the Malibu Farm Pier Cafe. In the afternoon, you could take a tour of the Getty Center, which has incredible art and views. For dinner, you could go all out at Nobu Malibu.

Finally, on Day Four, you can relax in Malibu before flying home from LAX. You could have a final swim at Surfrider Beach and a great brunch at the Ivy. 

Just a few key tips: renting an SUV is a must. Also, Alisal Beach has warm year-round water, and Catalina Island is another fantastic warm-water getaway if you prefer a more relaxed island vibe. But I think this plan will give you all that adventure and warmth you're looking for.
