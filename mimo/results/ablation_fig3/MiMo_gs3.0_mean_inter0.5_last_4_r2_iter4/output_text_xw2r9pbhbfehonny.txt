First, check YouTube’s kid-friend labels—it requires extra safety settings once you have 1,000 subscribers. Until then, just label each video manually.

Before filming, ask the venue if you’re allowed to record volunteer events. Nursing homes or hospitals might have their own rules.

Set up your phone or camera on a tripod or have someone hold it steady. Kids lose interest with shaky or off-angle shots.

Puppet characters should be clear and visible. Maybe focus on your fun-running gags, like Melvin’s bad magic tricks, to keep it engaging.

Keep skits short—five to eight minutes is ideal. Edit lightly if you want, but kids usually respond better to raw, energetic performances.
