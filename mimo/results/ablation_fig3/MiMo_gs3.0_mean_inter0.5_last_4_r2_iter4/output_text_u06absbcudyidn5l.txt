General causes for chronic facial redness include rosacea, sun damage over your lifetime, genetics, and contact dermatitis.

Symptoms of vasculitis can include fever, fatigue, rashes, or purple spots, because the inflammation happens in the blood vessels near the skin's surface.

Diagnosis usually involves a physical exam and blood tests, with a biopsy being the gold standard. Vasculitis can affect all ages. There are many types, which are generally grouped by the size of the vessel they target—large, medium, or small. Because it can affect any organ, symptoms vary widely. Treatment often involves calming the immune system with medications like steroids. Ibuprofen alone is not a long-term solution for underlying inflammation; your doctor can help create a safe regimen and treatment plan.
