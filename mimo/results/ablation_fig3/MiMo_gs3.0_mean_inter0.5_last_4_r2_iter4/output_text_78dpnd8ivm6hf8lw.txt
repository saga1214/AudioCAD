Here's a quick summary using your car analogy:

*   **Heart:** The **Gas Station & Engine** - Pumps cars all around.
*   **Lungs:** The **Refueling Spot** - Where cars stop for fresh oxygen "fuel".
*   **Main Arteries:** The **Big Highways** - The first roads the heart sends blood onto.
*   **Capillaries:** The **Tiny Streets** - Get close to every "house" (cell) to drop off oxygen fuel.
*   **Veins:** The **Return Roads** - Carry the empty cars back to the heart/lungs to refuel.
*   **Kidneys:** The **Car Wash & Recycling Plant** - Clean out waste/junk and filter old fuel.
*   **Liver:** The **All-in-One Service Station** - Processes useful fuel/store what to send where/break down harmful stuff.

Together, these parts keep delivering energy fuel, cleaning out waste, and balancing the system!
