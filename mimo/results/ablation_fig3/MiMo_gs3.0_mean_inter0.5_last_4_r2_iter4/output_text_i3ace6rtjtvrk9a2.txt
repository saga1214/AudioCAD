Saccades are the rapid jumps the eyes make between focal points. Saccadic masking means the brain suppresses blur during these movements. So even if the eyes sense lots of movement or details, the brain edits information before conscious awareness. Pronunciation of "saccadic" is "sa-KAH-dik." S is silent.
