Okay, that sounds like a fun theme! For the meal, I'd suggest Mango Shrimp Stir-Fry—spicy and mango fits you both. Pair that with Diabetes-Friendly Coconut Fish, using a light coconut milk sauce herbed with lime. For a carb option, Spicy Tamarind Glazed Tofu with a side of Mango Salsa.
