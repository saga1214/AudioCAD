Both adding gingerbread kits and extending deliveries are lovely ideas.

For your folks who love Snickerdoodles, including the kits would be a wonderful surprise. It shows you put a lot of thought into their gift.

As for sending more cookies, ginger is a classic cookie flavor, so gingerbread fits in well. Chocolate chip pairs nicely with it. You could pack two gingerbread cookies and two chocolate chip cookies together in Pringles can number one.

For Diane, who likes cinnamon, the gingerbread fits right in. You could pair it with two Snickerdoodle cookies in the snowflake-decal can.

And for Greg, who loves Snickerdoodles, you could pair two Snickerdoodles with two gingerbread cookies in the black can. It gives him a new flavor and keeps Diane from getting black.

Just remember to cushion everything well no matter which tins or alternative containers you use.
