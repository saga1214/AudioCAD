### Professional vs. DIY Key Differences
1. **Installation**: Professional includes expertise, wiring, and compliance with local codes.  
2. **Equipment Quality**: Professional systems often use higher-grade sensors and panels.  
3. **Warranty**: Typically longer (5–10 years) with professional setups.  
4. **Integration**: Professional systems may connect to smart home devices or smart locks.

### Tips to Reduce Costs
- **DIY Kits**: Save $700+ upfront, but handle installation yourself.  
- **Bundle Discounts**: Some companies offer discounts for multiple sensors/sensors.  
- **Verify玻璃break Sensor Placement**: Install near large windows or sliding doors.  
- **Ask About Contracts**: Negotiate shorter terms or look for providers without contracts.
