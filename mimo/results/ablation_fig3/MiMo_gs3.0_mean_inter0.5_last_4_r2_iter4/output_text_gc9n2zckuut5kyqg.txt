### 💧 Budget-Friendly Drinks:
- **Water Stations**: Fill large dispensers with infused water (cucumber + lemon) and offer reusable cups.  
- **Sugar-Free Teas**: Buy tea bags in bulk (hibiscus, mint, or chamomile).  
- **Homemade Smoothies**: Blend frozen fruit + spinach (kids won’t notice!) + almond milk.

### 💡 Money-Saving Tips:
- **Barbecue Smartly**:

- **Dessert Station**:  
  - Pre-make cookies/popsicles at home to avoid barbecuing costs.  
- **Volunteer Help**:
