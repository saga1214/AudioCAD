You can start your exploration right at the source in Lockport by visiting the Buffalo and Erie Canal Museum. It's the world's oldest operating canal museum and has a great exhibit on espionage there specifically about Japanese study.

You could also take a boat tour of the locks, which gives you a very clear demonstration of the engineering. Along Route 31 back to Rochester, you can look for markers for other key spots like the "Bargemen's Monument" and the "Coffin Ship."
