Here's a step-by-step guide for installing key items while respecting your landlord's rules:

### 📺 1. Mounting a TV
- **Tools Needed:** Drill (if not pre-drilled), level, TV mount, screws, stud finder
- **Steps:**
  - Find studs or use drywall anchors (prevents damage)
  - Mark center point on wall
  - Hold mount against wall, use bubble level
  - Drill pilot holes, insert screws
  - Attach TV to mount, secure to wall
  - **Tip:** Use a TV stand instead of wall-mounting if furniture is heavy

### 🌿 2. Hanging Planters
- **Lightweight Items (vases, small plants):**
  - Use **3M Command Strips** or **Velcro Hooks** (no drilling)
  - Hang at eye level (54"-60" from floor)
- **Medium-Weight Items (potted plants):**
  - Find studs with **stud finder**
  - Use **Simpson Strong-Tie hooks** (rated for 25+ lbs)
  - Install hooks 60" above floor
- **Heavy Items (large planters):**
  - Use **heavy-duty ceiling hooks** rated for 50+ lbs
  - Reinforce with **ceiling joist anchors**

### 🪢 3. Floating Shelves
- **Tools Needed:** Tape measure, level, drill, wall anchors
- **Steps:**
  - Measure spacing between shelves (12"-24" apart ideal)
  - Mark shelf positions (centered on wall studs)
  - Drill pilot holes at mark
  - Insert **toggle bolts** or **heavy-duty anchors**
  - Attach shelf brackets with screws
  - **Weight Tip:** Place heavier art pieces lower

### ⚠️ Critical Safety Notes:
- Always check weight ratings (TVs = 100-200 lbs, shelves = 25-50 lbs per bracket)
- Use **ladder stabilizers** or spotter for heavy items
- For furniture drilling, use **furniture-grade anchors** (e.g., wedge anchors)
- Test installations with a **weight test** before committing

### 💡 Time-Saving Decor Sundays:
- Work in 90-minute blocks (e.g., 9-10:30 AM: TV, 10:30-12:00 PM: shelves)
- Keep tools organized in a **rolling cart** nearby
- Decorate while furniture is in place (hang art near matching pieces)
- Use **LED strip lights** to highlight artwork/shelves

> "Start with permanent installations first—TV, shelves, major hardware. Then add decor with adhesives or removable hooks. Always mark drill points with tape so you can reposition if needed."

Remember: If you hit a stud, anchor everything to it! This maximizes stability without drilling into drywall. Happy decorating—and protecting your legs' flexibility with those stretches! 💪🎨
