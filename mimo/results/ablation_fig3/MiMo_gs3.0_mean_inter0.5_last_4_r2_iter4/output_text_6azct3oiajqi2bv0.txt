That's a good call sticking with Nordstrom Rack for accessibility. To avoid damaging synthetics or waterproof linings, try these steps:  
1. **Use a Fabric-Specific Glue** like E6000 or Aleene’s Tacky Glue (test on scrap first).  
2. **Prep the Fabric**: Clean the area, remove loose fibers, and iron on a low-heat setting if needed.  
3. **Apply Sparingly**: Put a thin layer on both surfaces, then press firmly for 1–2 minutes.  
4. **Avoid Heat**: Never use hot glue or iron directly on the fabric—it’ll melt the lining.  

For jackets/fleeces, focus on seams or high-stress areas (elbows, cuffs). If the lining tears significantly, consider patching *outside* the jacket or consulting a local tailoring shop like **Fusion Sewing Studio** on Arden Way—they offer affordable repairs.
