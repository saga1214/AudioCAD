Here’s a simple four-step plan you can use to juggle ambitious life goals.

First, assign each passion and savings goal to a category, like 'Trip Fund' or 'Apartment Down Payment Fund'.

Second, assign small, fixed monthly amounts to each fund from your paycheck. You could even automate the transfers so you don't have to think about it.

Third, break one trip research task into tiny weekly research sessions of about 15 to 20 minutes each. This makes it feel much less overwhelming.

And fourth, every month, review your small progress reports to see how you're doing.

This kind of organized, small-step approach helps you make steady progress on everything without the exhaustion.
