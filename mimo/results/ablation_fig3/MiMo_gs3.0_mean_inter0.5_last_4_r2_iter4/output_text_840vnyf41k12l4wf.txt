   *Plastic-based material derived from polyurethane. It mimics the look and feel of animal leather at a lower cost, making it a widely accessible vegan alternative since the market launch in the **1960s**.*  

2. **Piñatex**

4. **Mushroom Leather**  
   *Grown from mycelium (mushroom roots), this material is cultivated into leather-like sheets. It’s fully biodegradable, carbon-negative, and developed from waste byproducts since its emergence in **2018–2020**.*
