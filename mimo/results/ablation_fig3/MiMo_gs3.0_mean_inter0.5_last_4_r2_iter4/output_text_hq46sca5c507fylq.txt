That's thoughtful of you to consider meaningful gifts! Here are some ideas tailored to hands-on hobbies, balancing usefulness and personal touch:

### For Tailoring (Leverages hands-on restorations):
1. **Premium Fabric Shears**: A sharp pair of **Gingher or Kai shears** (like your pen tools) for precision cutting.  
2. **Vintage-Inspired Thread Set**: High-quality cotton or linen thread in classic colors.  
3. **Parker 51-Themed Gift**: A vintage Parker parts kit, a pen case, or a matching thread set.  
4. **Pattern Book**: A book on classic menswear or vintage-inspired sewing patterns.  

### For Woodworking (Addresses cost concerns):
1. **Basic Starter Kit**: A quality saw (e.g., handsaw), chisels, and clamps (start with 1–2 essential tools).  
2. **Pre-cut Wood Blocks**: For practicing joinery or small projects.  
3. **Wood Finish Set**: Sandpaper + sealant for finishing.  
4. **DIY Workshop**: A small project kit (e.g., wooden stool or plant stand) to reduce tool-buying upfront.  

### For Clay/Pottery:
1. **Clay Studio Kit**: Basic air-dry or polymer clay + sculpting tools.  
2. **Sculpting Kit**: A beginner pottery wheel + small clay set (cheaper entry than kilns).  
3. **Mold Set**: For casting or creating decorative pieces.  

### Universal Gift Ideas:
- **Hobby-Specific Journal**: To sketch designs or track progress.  
- **Subscription Box**: Monthly supplies for your chosen hobby (e.g., "Tailor's Tool Box" or "Woodworking Monthly").  
- **Experience Voucher**: A local workshop class (e.g., "Intro to Tailoring" or "Woodworking 101").  

### Key Advice:
- **Avoid Overly Expensive Gear Early On**: Tools/kilns add up fast—start with consumables (fabric, wood, clay) or small tools.  
- **Personalize**: A gift tied to your Parker 51 or vintage hobby shows they *really* know you.  
- **Quality Over Quantity**: One well-chosen tool or material can inspire months of work.
