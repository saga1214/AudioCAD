### Phase 1: Daytime (Hilly, Aid Stations Available)
Keep it light but adaptable. You can grab hot food/water at aid stations.  
- **Outer Layer:** Wind/water-resistant shell (e.g., rain jacket or windbreaker)  
- **Mid-Layer:** Fleece or light puffy jacket (remove if sweating at aid stations)  
- **Base Layer:** Synthetic or merino wool top/bottom  
- **Extras:** Warm hat (if sun goes behind clouds), knee/ankle sleeves (optional), sunscreen  
- **Safety:** Whistle (for emergencies), small first-aid kit  

---

### Phase 2: Night (Critical for Warmth & Visibility)
Prioritize warmth, visibility, and warmth for extremities.  
- **Outer Layer:** Same wind shell as Phase 1 (add reflective strips if possible)  
- **Mid-Layer:** Insulated jacket (down/synthetic fill)  
- **Base Layer:** As above  
- **Warmth Essentials:** Warm hat (fleece or balaclava if cold), gloves (wool/thermal)  
- **Visibility:** Reflective vest or bright jacket, headlamp (always carry batteries!)  
- **Extras:** Knee sleeves, thermal socks (change if feet get cold), knee brace (if he/you need support)
