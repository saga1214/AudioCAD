Of course! Here’s a full list of the four cocktails you decided on, with all the measurements and directions, ready for your bartenders.

First, Old Fashioned: You’ll need two ounces of bourbon or rha, one sugar cube, or you can use three-quarters of an ounce of simple syrup. Then add two dashes of Angostura bitters. Just stir all that over ice in a rocks glass until it's chilled, and finish it off with an orange twist.

Second Paper Plane: Equal parts, so that’s one ounce of bourbon, half an ounce of Aperol, half an ounce Amaro Nonino liqueur, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. You just need to shake those four ingredients with ice, strain them into a coupé glass, no garnish needed.

Third, Whiskey Sour with egg white if you're using it. Add one egg white, three-quarters of an ounce of simple syrup, half an ounce of fresh lemon juice, and one ounce of bourbon. Like I said, dry shake first for foam, add the ice and shake again, then strain into a rocks glass. You can garnish it with a cherry or an orange slice.

And your fourth cocktail, the French 75: You'll take one and a half ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and then top it with two ounces of champagne or prosecco. Stir the first three ingredients with ice, then strain into a coupé flute and top with the sparkling wine.
