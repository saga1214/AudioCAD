Don't worry about setting off the alarm this time!

Stir-fries use much lower temperatures for browning ground meat anyway. For the noodles, the most important trick is to use wooden noodles next time. Metal pots get very hot, so a wooden spoon slipping into one is perfect smoke-alarm fuel.

If you absolutely have to use wood this week, just be extremely careful and use a plastic spoon. But seriously, get some ramen or bucatini noodles.
