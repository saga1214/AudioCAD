Based on your criteria (fuel efficiency, comfort, **and** practicality), BMW typically **does not make the absolute top 3**, though it excels in different areas. Here's a breakdown:

1.  **Toyota, Honda, Hyundai:** These brands consistently rank **higher** for the *combination* of fuel efficiency, comfort, and practicality in their mainstream sedans (like Corolla, Camry, Accord, Elantra, Sonata).
    *   **Toyota/Honda:** Renowned for exceptional reliability, strong fuel economy (especially hybrids), good comfort, and practical interior space at a competitive price.
    *   **Hyundai:** Offers excellent value, strong warranty, good fuel economy, and surprisingly comfortable/practical sedans (like the Sonata, Elantra).

2.  **BMW:** **Strengths:** Performance, handling, luxury feel, premium comfort, brand prestige.
    *   **Weaknesses (for your criteria):** Lower fuel efficiency compared to Toyota/Honda/Hyundai (especially non-plug-in hybrids), higher purchase price, higher maintenance/repair costs, less "everyday" practicality (e.g., trunk space, ride stiffness on rough roads).
    *   **Hybrids:** BMW's plug-in hybrids (like the 5 Series PHEV) improve efficiency significantly, but they are still less efficient than comparable Toyota/Honda/Hyundai hybrids.

3.  Lexus (often grouped with Toyota): Known for Toyota's reliability and engineering, plus enhanced luxury and refinement. Their sedans (ES, IS) offer excellent comfort, good reliability, and decent fuel efficiency, making them strong contenders in the luxury/practicality segment.

**Conclusion:**

*   If **fuel efficiency, reliability, and practicality** are your *top priorities*, **Toyota, Honda, and Hyundai** are most likely your top 3 choices.
*   If **luxury, performance, and premium feel** are your top priorities, **BMW** (and Mercedes-Benz, Audi) excel, but sacrifice some efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and "everyday" practicality compared to the top 3 above.
*   **Lexus** often ranks highly for a blend of Toyota's reliability, luxury features, and decent efficiency.

**So, for the specific combination of fuel efficiency, comfort, AND practicality, Toyota, Honda, and Hyundai are generally the top 3 brands known for sedans.** BMW is a top brand, but for different strengths.
