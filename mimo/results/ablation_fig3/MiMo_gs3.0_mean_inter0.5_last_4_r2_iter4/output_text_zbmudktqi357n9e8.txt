If you’re heading to Mexico for inspiration, autumn there is... well, it’s peak foliage season, especially in the highlands like Chiapas or the Yucatán Peninsula. The colors will be incredible for your autumn scenes. 

But space? Mexico has some amazing observatories and stargazing spots, especially around Baja California or near Chihuahua. Autumn there is also a good dry season for stargazing. 

Both scenes would be inspiring, but for water scenes, a river-filled area like the Río Grande or the coast near Cancún could be perfect. Take your paints, observe the colors and movements, and practice those wet-on-wet and layering techniques you’ve been working on. Mexico will definitely help sharpen your skills—enjoy the trip and the art!
