Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! Here are a few great options:

First, there's Ramen Stop in Little Elm. They have incredible tonkotsu and shoyu ramen. It's a bit of a drive from downtown but totally worth it.

Then there's Noodle House in Richardson. It’s more of a fusion place, but their担担面 and 辣子鸡 are amazing.

And for a more upscale experience, check out Daigo Ramen in Plano. Their spicy miso ramen is a must-try.

As for protein bars, it depends on what you’re looking for. If you want something high in protein and low in sugar, I’d recommend Optimum Nutrition or MyProtein. If you prefer a more natural, plant-based option, there’s Clif Builders or RXBAR. Just make sure to check the labels to see what works best for you.
