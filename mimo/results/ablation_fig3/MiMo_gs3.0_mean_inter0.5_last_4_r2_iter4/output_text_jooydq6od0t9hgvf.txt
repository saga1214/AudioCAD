No problem—here’s a quick, elegant vodka and Fernet recipe for your guests, keeping it refreshing and crowd-pleasing:

### **1. Classic Vodka Soda (Soda-Free Option)**  
**Mixers:**  
- **For Omnivores/Vegetarians:** Gin or vodka + fresh lime juice + soda water (or sparkling water if no soda allergy conflict). Garnish with a lime wheel.  
- **Effortless Twist:** Add a splash of elderflower liqueur (St. Germain) for floral notes.  

### **2. Argentine Fernet (Quick & Refreshing)**  
**Recipe (Per Serve):**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz Fernet-Branca (or Argentine-style bitter aperitivo)  
- 4 oz sparkling water (soda-free!)  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- *Garnish:* Lime wheel or mint sprig  

**How to Serve:**  
Shake vodka, Fernet, lime juice, and ice briefly. Strain into a chilled glass, top with sparkling water, stir gently, and garnish.  

### **Key Tips:**  
- **Avoid Sulfites?** Skip artificial sweeteners/soda if allergy extends beyond carbonation.  
- **Game-Changer:** Add cucumber slices to the glass for extra sophistication.  
- **Mocktail Option:** For non-drinkers, pair with your fruit tart sorbet or chocolate mousse.  

This keeps drinks light, sophisticated, and allergy-safe—no soda, no stress! 🥂
