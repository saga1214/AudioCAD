Both succulents and the bird's nest snake plant are very lightweight. The succulents sitting on your table will be tiny. The bird's nest plant itself is small and even lighter because the soil will have dried out during your work month. You can easily carry both of them up five flights of stairs.

They'll do great overall on your east balcony—the bright morning sun is ideal for both. Succulents barely need watering, and the snake plant is practically indestructible. Since New York wind might stress other, taller plants, their sturdiness is a real plus for you. They'll make your Airbnb feel much more like a home.
