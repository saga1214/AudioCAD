That's a great way to approach it. You want to be professional and strategic.

Here’s a framework you could use in your email. Start with a subject line like: Meeting Request to Improve for Future Assessments.

In the body, you could write something like this: "Dear Professor [Last Name], I scored a 64 on the recent essay-style exam and wanted to understand what I could have improved on to perform better in the future. My other grades in your class have been excellent, and I've found essay grading can be subjective. If you have a moment, could we briefly meet to discuss your feedback? Sincerely, [Your Name]."

This focuses on your growth and doesn't challenge the grade directly. Good luck with scholarship applications—you absolutely should apply.
