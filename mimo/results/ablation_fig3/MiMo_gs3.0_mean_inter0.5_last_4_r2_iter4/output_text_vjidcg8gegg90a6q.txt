For winter chicks integrated into your adult coop at five-plus weeks old, their bedding needs to be durable, energy-efficient, and safe near whatever heater you choose. 

The best choice by far is a deep-bed mix. You can combine large-flake pine shavings on top of the rubber mats you were planning to use with a layer of compost underneath it. As that bottom layer composts this winter, it will naturally generate a significant amount of steady, underlying heat. This method is excellent insulation, discourages rodents, and provides heat passively.

If you need something a bit drier and cleaner, you can use a thick layer of sand. It's also resistant to rodent nesting and helps wick away moisture.

Whichever method you choose, just remember to seal cracks to block drafts but maintain high-level ventilation to prevent frostbite. This combination will keep both your adult flock and your winter chicks very warm and comfortable all season.
