Okay, so you’re asking about diesel camper van maintenance before a Midwest road trip. That’s smart. For brakes, a few key things to check: look for excessive wear, which might show up as uneven pad thickness or glazed rotors. If you hear a grinding noise when braking or see blue spots on the pads, that could mean they’re worn out. Also, check for any leaks in the brake fluid or any unusual smells. For spark plugs, that’s a bit more involved. Diesel engines usually have high compression and need special copper or platinum spark plugs. You’ll need a socket that fits the long reach of diesel plugs, a torque wrench, and a gap gauge. The process is basically unscrewing the old ones, cleaning the threads, installing new ones with the correct gap, and tightening them with the right torque. It’s not a quick job, but a good way to avoid unexpected breakdowns. It’s always better to get a professional to check both of these before you hit the road.
