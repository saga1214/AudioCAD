For the main course, chicken cutlets, chicken breasts will be eight to fifteen dollars for two pounds; all-purpose flour is three to five dollars; eggs are three to seven dollars for a dozen; breadcrumbs are two to four dollars; vegetable oil is four to eight dollars; and salt and pepper is three to six dollars.

Now for the side, garlic mashed potatoes: russet potatoes will be three to six dollars for a five-pound bag; garlic is about a dollar; butter is four to seven dollars for a pound; and milk is three to five dollars for a half-gallon. 

Just so you have an idea, the estimated total for chicken alone is between twenty-two and thirty-eight dollars, and for the potatoes it's about six to ten dollars. This doesn't include other costs like oil beyond frying, condiments, or serving dishes.
