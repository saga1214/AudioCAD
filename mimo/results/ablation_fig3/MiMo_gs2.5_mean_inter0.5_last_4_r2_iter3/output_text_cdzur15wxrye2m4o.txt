Okay, this is where I need to be very careful. While the steps *are* mechanically identical, using car ramps introduces a safety factor independent of the car model. The size of the ramp and the weight of the car are the critical things. 

So, yes, since it's the same car, your engine block, oil pan, drain plug, and filter should fit perfectly. But the ramps... the safety of the ramps depends on the car's weight and how you use them. If her friend's ramps fit her car safely, they will almost certainly fit yours mechanically. But I have to add a safety note: always make sure the ramps are rated for the weight of your specific car and that they are set up securely on a level surface. Don't assume fitment alone means safety.
