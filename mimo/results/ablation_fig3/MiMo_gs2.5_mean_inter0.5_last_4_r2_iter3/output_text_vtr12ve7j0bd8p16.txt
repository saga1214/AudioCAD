Leo and Sagittarius pairing up for a fire-filled adventure sounds wonderful. For a weekend trip in Calgary, you have some great options.

One idea is to head to the Kananaskis Valley. You could hike up a beautiful trail, maybe drive the scenic Kananaskis Lake Road, and then enjoy a casual campfire dinner. It's a classic, rugged Canadian mountain experience.

Another option, if she's interested in history, is to visit Old Man's Cave and the Balcony House. It's a bit of a drive, but it's a fascinating ancient rock shelter that has a very romantic, mysterious feel.

For a completely different kind of adventure, you could try ice climbing at the Icefields Parkway. It's a unique experience and fits that Sagittarian Leo adventurous spirit perfectly.

And if you want something really special and romantic, you could book a private glamping cabin in the Rockies. It's more luxurious than camping but still feels incredibly rustic and intimate, like a modern log cabin in the wild.
