For a deeper dive into Lockport's rich canal history, the most important place to start is the Lockport Canal Museum. It's located right next to the locks themselves, so you can see the historical exhibits and then literally walk right out the door to see the working machinery. It gives you a really tangible sense of the city's entire identity, which was built on the Erie Canal.
