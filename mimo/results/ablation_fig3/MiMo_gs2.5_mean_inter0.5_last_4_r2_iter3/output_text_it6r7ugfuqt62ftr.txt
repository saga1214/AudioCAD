Absolutely! Since warm water swimming is your goal, Big Sur or Leggett in Mendocino County are perfect choices this time of year. They stay mild and often have tide pools and secluded beaches.

Given you'll need an SUV and have a healthy budget, I'll suggest an itinerary for Big Sur. It's a four-day trip that includes a couple of great destinations, places to stay and eat, and travel logistics.

On Day One, you'll arrive in Monterey. I'd suggest staying in a lovely boutique hotel called Point Lobos Lodge, which is right near the famous Point Lobos State Park. For dinner, the lodge's own Point Lobos Lodge Restaurant is incredible, but you should definitely call ahead to confirm they can handle her Celiac needs.

Day Two is for exploring Point Lobos State Park. You can take a guided kayak tour from the lodge to see sea caves and otters. In the afternoon, you can drive along the scenic road to see more wildlife and beautiful views. For lunch, a great spot is the Point Lobos Inn Restaurant, which is also nearby and accommodating.

On Day Three, you can head towards the central coast, stopping at Pfeiffer Beach to see the famous purple sand. You could also take a tour of Hearst Castle, which is a must-see. For dinner, the historic and accommodating El Capitan Canyon is a great choice.

For Day Four, you can take a scenic drive along the Big Sur Coast. A must-see is McWay Falls, where you can hike down to the beach and even swim in the water right in front of the waterfall. You can also find tide pools at Pfeiffer Beach. For a special lunch, there's a wonderful farm-to-table place called Nepenthe, though I'd recommend making reservations.

For your return trip home, you can drive back along Highway 1. If you have time, you could stop in Carmel-by-the-Sea for a nice dinner at a restaurant like The White House.

Just a few final tips: The Alcat-raz cruise dinner idea is still a perfect lead-in to this trip. And Trish's Mini Donuts make an amazing souvenir. Most importantly, always, always double-check with restaurants that they can handle Celiac needs safely. I hope you have an unforgettable family vacation!
