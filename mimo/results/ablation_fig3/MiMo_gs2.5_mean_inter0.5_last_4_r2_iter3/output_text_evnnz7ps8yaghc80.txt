A structured schedule is a wonderful idea. Here’s a sample routine that mixes different tools and keeps stress low.

For two or three days a week, start with music. Warm up for five minutes juggling your tennis balls in one hand, then spend about fifteen minutes practicing the first three-ball throws we talked about. Cool down with five minutes with scarves.

For the other two or three days, focus more on pattern. Start with ten minutes juggling beanbags or tennis balls to warm up. Then, for ten minutes, really concentrate on catching each object cleanly. Finish with ten minutes using scarves to slowly practice a three-ball cascade pattern.

On your off days, feel free to just relax or even show son what you've learned, even if it's just practicing one hand!
