The wooden spoon disaster won't be a problem. Here are a few key tips for perfect stir-fry noodles:

First, and most importantly, make sure your pot is metal. No wood, bamboo, or plastic.

Second, add your noodles to the water while it's already at a full, rolling boil. This helps them cook evenly and quickly.

Third, stir them often so they don't stick together.

Finally, as soon as they are done, drain them very well. Stir-fry is a very wet dish, so having dry noodles is crucial.
