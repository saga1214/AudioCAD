For Asian noodles in Dallas, I’d recommend checking out spots like Tokyo Joe in Richardson, which has great ramen, or Saizeriya, a budget-friendly Italian-Asian fusion place that’s decent for quick bites. If you want authentic, try Noodle House for Thai-style noodles. 

For protein bars, it depends on your taste preference. Quest has high protein with minimal sugar, Optimum Nutrition has a classic taste, and RXBAR is simple with just a few ingredients. If you want something chewy, Clif Bars or GoMacro are good too.
