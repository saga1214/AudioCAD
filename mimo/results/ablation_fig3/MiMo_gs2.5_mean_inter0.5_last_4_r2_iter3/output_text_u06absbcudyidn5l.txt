Common causes include rosacea, sun damage, certain skin conditions like contact dermatitis, or even just flushing. Genetics plays a huge part.

To explain vasculitis a bit more, it's simply when the walls of your blood vessels become inflamed. This inflammation can happen in small, medium, or large vessels. Because the vessels are swollen, they're less elastic and can restrict blood flow, which shows up as redness or rashes. If it's not treated, this can lead to long-term damage.

A dermatologist can run tests and look at you to figure out if it's one of these conditions or something else. They're the best person to help you address both the root cause and the physical symptoms you're seeing.
