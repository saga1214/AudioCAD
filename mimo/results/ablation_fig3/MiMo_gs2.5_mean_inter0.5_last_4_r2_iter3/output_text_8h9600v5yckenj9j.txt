For Halloween candy, the crowd favorites are usually things like mini chocolate bars, like Snickers, Twix, or Milky Way, and also Reese’s Cups. Gummy worms, gummy bears, and regular jelly beans are always a hit. You could also get some classic Halloween candies like candy corn, butterscotch pumpkins, and those little chocolate eggs. Any of those are sure to be popular and will be gone by the end of the party, so you won’t have much left over.
