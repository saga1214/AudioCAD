Okay, before you start priming, just take a moment to do a final check. Make sure all the doors and hardware are removed and packed away neatly.

Then, do a quick once-over to ensure there's no visible grease or dirt left on the cabinets. If you missed any spots, do a final degreaser wipe-down.

After they're completely clean and dry to the touch, a final light sanding should be done, especially over any areas where the black paint might have been sanded through. This just ensures the surface is perfectly smooth for the primer.

And one last thing: before you put that primer on, use painter's tape to mask off any areas you don't want painted, like the walls or any trim around the cabinets. That will keep them looking clean.
