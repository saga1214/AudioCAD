That's fantastic news! Illustrations are a huge asset for a children's picture book. Reputable publishers **do** love it when the text and art are combined. However, the agent you first need to find must represent agents who specifically look for or accept manuscripts that include artwork.

So, your first step is to research. You can use resources like the Society of Children's Book Writers and Illustrators, or SCBWI, to find agents who are open to receiving complete manuscripts with art attached.

When you query them, you'll submit the query letter plus a digital portfolio of your illustrations. Once they accept you, they'll help find a publisher who is looking for that exact combination of a great story and high-quality art. This makes your project much more appealing to them.
