What a fantastic plan! Here’s a **mess-minimizing, grab-and-go shopping list** designed for 10 dads, 10 kids, and coolers at the theme park, balancing health, variety, and minimal cleanup:

### 🥤 DRINKS (Pack Cases of)
*   **Water Bottles:** 10 cases (40 bottles) - Essential hydration.
*   **Low-Sugar Juice Boxes:** 1 case (24 boxes) - For kids (or adults who prefer juice).
*   **Sugar-Free Sodas:** 2 cases (24 cans) - Variety (e.g., root beer, cola, lemon-lime).
*   **Sparkling Water:** 1 case (24 cans/bottles) - Healthier alternative to soda.
*   *(Optional: Ice Packs - Lots of them!)*

### 🍪 SNACKS (Pack Individual Servings)
*   **Cheese Sticks:** 2 large packs (10-12 sticks per pack).
*   **Grapes:** 3 large bags.
*   **Baby Carrots:** 3 large bags.
*   **Individual Trail Mix Packs:** 10 packs (look for low-sugar options).
*   **Mini Pretzels:** 2 large bags (or single-serve packs).
*   **Fresh Apple Slices:** 2 large bags (toss with lemon juice to prevent browning).
*   *Hard-boiled Eggs:* 10-12 (great protein, eat on the go).

### 🍕 FOOD (Main Meals - Pack in Coolers with Ice)
*   **Frozen Mini Calzones:** **THE STAR!** (e.g., Trader Joe's, Whole Foods, or similar). Get 3 packs (30-40 mini calzones). *Why Frozen?* They stay cold in the cooler, thaw slightly during the day, and are easy to grab. Fillings like pizza, cheese, or chicken are crowd-pleasers. *Check labels for veggie options if needed.*
*   **OR Homemade Option:** If you want restaurant-style, make 10 calzones at home. Freeze them *before* the party, pack in a dedicated cooler with ice packs. Thaw slightly in coolers or pack with a *small* cooler dedicated to them.

### 🍰 DESSERT (Grab-and-Go)
*   **Cupcakes:** 2 dozen (various flavors). Easy peasy, no knife needed!
*   **OR Brownie Box:** A large box (e.g., 24-36 brownies). Split easily.
*   *(Skip the pie/cake cutting issue - cupcakes/brownies win for mess-free fun!)*

### 🧺 ESSENTIALS FOR MINIMAL MESS
*   **Coolers & Ice Packs:** Multiple coolers (one for drinks/snacks, one for frozen food, maybe one for dessert).
*   **Reusable Insulated Bags:** For non-perishable snacks/drinks.
*   **Trash Bags:** Small, zip-top bags for easy litter disposal.
*   **Wet Wipes & Hand Sanitizer:** For quick cleanups.
*   **Paper Towels/Rags:** For spills (use sparingly!).

**Key Tips for Success:**
1.  **Pre-Sort & Portion:** Pack everything into clearly labeled cooler bags *before* heading out.
2.  **Assign Coolers:** Give each dad/family a cooler bag to manage.
3.  **Hydration Station:** Keep water/juice/soda easily accessible near the park's water fountains.
4.  **Wipe It Down:** Use wet wipes frequently, especially after eating.
5.  **Trash Talk:** Encourage everyone to use the designated trash bags.

This plan gives everyone choices (juice vs. water, soda vs. sparkling water), satisfies the calzone craving cleanly, includes healthy staples, offers substantial meals and dessert, and prioritizes easy cleanup. Have an absolutely amazing bachelor party! 🎢🎉
