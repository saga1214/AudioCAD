That's a smart budget choice! For applying duct tape without damaging the lining, here's the best way to do it:

1.  **Clean the Area:** Wipe the torn edges with a slightly damp cloth (avoiding the lining) to remove any dirt or debris. Let it dry completely.
2.  **Scrape Off Residue (Optional but Recommended):** If the fabric is prone to sticking, gently scrape off any existing residue from the jacket's outer material with a plastic card or credit card before applying.
3.  **Use Thin Strips:** Cut the duct tape into very thin strips (about 1/4 inch wide or less). Thick strips are more likely to leave residue and damage the fabric.
4.  **Apply Parallel to Tears:** Place the strip *parallel* to the direction of the tear. This helps reinforce the tear line without adding bulk perpendicular to the fabric.
5.  **Press Firmly:** Smooth the tape down firmly over the tear, pressing out any air bubbles. Use a soft cloth or your fingers.
6.  **Consider Reinforcement (Optional):** For extra hold, you can place a thin strip of clear packing tape *over* the duct tape strip once it's down. This helps prevent the duct tape from peeling off the outer fabric.

**Important Considerations:**

*   **Test First:** If you're unsure, test a small piece of tape on an inconspicuous seam or hem first.
*   **Residue Risk:** Duct tape can sometimes leave a sticky residue, especially if the fabric is slick or the tape is old. The scraping step helps minimize this.
*   **Temporary Fix:** This is a strong temporary repair. If the jacket is old or the tear is large, consider replacing it when you shop at Nordstrom Rack online.
*   **Avoid the Lining:** Be very careful not to let the tape touch the inner lining or insulation directly, as it could melt or stick to it.

This method gives you the strength you need while minimizing damage to the outer fabric!
