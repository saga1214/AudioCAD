You're right to double-check, it can be confusing! Let me clarify. The most traditional Boulevardier uses one and a quarter bourbon to one part vermouth and Campari. The equal-parts version, one ounce of each, is also traditional and very popular. Now, for the Last Word you mentioned, that's an equal-parts drink made with gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime. Each of those ingredients is three-quarters of an ounce. Both cocktails are fantastic choices!
