For winter chicks integrated into your adult coop at five-plus weeks old, the best bedding is definitely a thick layer of pine-flake shavings. They provide excellent thermal insulation on concrete and are rodent-resistant if managed correctly.

Use what's known as the deep litter principle again. Just continuously add fresh shavings on top of the old ones. As that bottom layer composts, it will naturally generate warmth for your chicks. Make sure to seal cracks to block drafts but maintain high-up ventilation to prevent frostbite and moisture buildup. Avoid straw, as you correctly noted, because it does attract rodents on a concrete floor. Pine shavings are a much safer alternative.
