   → Switch to stainless steel, cast iron, or ceramic alternatives.

4. **Detergents & Cleaners** (some laundry detergents, floor polishes)  
   → Choose eco-friendly brands labeled "PFAS-free."  
5. **Tap & Bottled Water**  
   → Use an **activated carbon filter** on your tap (pitcher-style or faucet-mounted). *Note: Carbon filters retain minerals.*

### Key Clarifications:
- **Shampoo/Cosmetics**: Yes, found in *some* products. Check labels for "PFAS" or avoid brands using PFAS for stain/water resistance.

- **Water Debate**: Pitcher/faucet carbon filters **keep essential minerals**—intensive RO systems remove them. pitcher filters are safer.  
- **Cleanses**: No scientific proof juice cleanses remove PFAS. Focus on reducing new exposure.  

### Why This Works:
PFAS build up slowly over years. By avoiding the top 5 sources above, you reduce your body’s burden significantly. For your kids:  
- Wash toys/clothes in a **PFAS-free detergent**.  
- Avoid waterproof car seats/strollers (check for PFAS).  

### Final Advice:
- **Certify your filters**: Look for NSF/ANSI standards 53 or 57 for PFAS reduction.
