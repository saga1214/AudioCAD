Okay, so Mexico sounds like a fantastic place to get inspiration for autumn scenes! If you're aiming for those vibrant watercolor autumn colors, I’d say late fall or early winter would be ideal. That’s when the foliage turns really bright there. 

If you’re going for rivers though, coastal Mexico in the spring might be lovely—showing those reflections of palm trees or blue skies on the water. Either way, Mexico’s landscapes are just... perfect for practicing all those watercolor techniques we’ve been talking about. You’ll get tons of inspiration and maybe even see some techniques in real life before you paint them. Have an amazing time!
