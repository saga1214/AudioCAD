For something a bit more unique, there’s the Pontiac GTO. It was kind of the first muscle car, and the original models have become really collectible.

And finally, a Mercedes-Benz 300SL 'Gullwing'. It's not American, but it's one of the most valuable classic cars in the world and has appreciated like crazy. It might be a bit out of his pride-driven style, but it's a massive appreciation play.
