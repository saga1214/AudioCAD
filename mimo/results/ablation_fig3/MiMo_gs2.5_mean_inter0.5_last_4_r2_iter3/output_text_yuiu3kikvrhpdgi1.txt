Of course! For Florida Zone 10B, especially during your reversed 'fallen/winter' seasons, quick and easy wins are your best bet.

Herbs are a great place to start: basil, mint, and parsley are classics—they grow fast and are super forgiving. Leafy greens like lettuce, spinach, and radishes are also fantastic because they mature quickly. Radishes can be ready in like, three weeks, which is a nice confidence boost.

If you’ve got more space, tomatoes and peppers are definitely the way to go. They’re pretty resilient and give you a huge return. 

Basically, start with these. They’re the easiest for you to get going in St. Pete, Florida. Then you can branch out from there. Happy gardening!
