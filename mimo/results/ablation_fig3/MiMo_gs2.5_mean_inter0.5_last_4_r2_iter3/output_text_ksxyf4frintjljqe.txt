I'm truly sorry to hear about your family's loss, especially around Thanksgiving traditions she created, like baking brownies and peach cobbler. It's completely understandable how seeing those desserts could be so difficult for your brother and the whole family. Maybe this year you can find a way to honor her memory in a way that feels right... sharing favorite stories or creating a new ritual together feels important. And it’s OK if you skip those desserts or do something simpler—whatever helps the most right now. Just be gentle with yourselves.

Now, for your dessert idea... How about a chocolate mousse? It’s rich and comforting, but it doesn’t trigger anyone in the same way. You just layer some melted chocolate with whipped cream in a pan, bake it for a bit, and then let it cool. Another idea is a chocolate tart. You can use a pre-made crust, fill it with a chocolate ganache, and it's a really elegant option that everyone will enjoy. Both are comforting without being too heavy on the memories.
