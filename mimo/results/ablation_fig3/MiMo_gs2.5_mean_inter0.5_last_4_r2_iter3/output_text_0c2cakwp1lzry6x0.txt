Here's a noise-friendly, step-by-step guide for installing your key decor items while respecting your landlord's rules:

### 1. **Mounting the TV (No Drilling Required!)**
   - **Adhesive Mounts**: Use heavy-duty **TV wall mounts** with strong adhesive strips.  
     *Steps*:  
     - Clean the wall surface with rubbing alcohol.  
     - Apply the adhesive strips *over* the mounting plate.  
     - Press the plate firmly onto the wall for 30 seconds.  
     - Test stability before hanging the TV.  
   - **Screw-in Mounts (If Allowed)**: Rent a drill (if permitted) and use **short screws**.  
     *Steps*:  
     - Mark screw holes with a pencil.  
     - Pre-drill small pilot holes to prevent cracks.  
     - Secure the mount plate to studs or use **heavy-duty anchors** for drywall.  

### 2. **Hanging Planters (Noise-Free Options)**
   - **Adhesive Hooks**: Ideal for lightweight planters.  
     *Steps*:  
     - Use **3M Command Hooks** rated for your planter’s weight.  
     - Press firmly for 30 seconds—no tools needed.  
     - Test weight limit (usually 2–3 lbs).  
   - **Screw Hooks (If Permitted)**:  
     - Use **small, short screws** and pilot holes.  
     - Hang planter directly from the hook.  
   - **Floating Planters**: Place on shelves or use **magnetic wall mounts** (no drilling).

### 3. **Installing Floating Shelves**
   - **Anchor to Studs (Best Option)**:  
     *Steps*:  
     - Use a **stud finder** to locate studs.  
     - Mark shelf position and shelf depth.  
     - Secure with **2.5" screws** into studs.  
   - **Drywall Anchors (If No Studs)**:  
     - Rent a drill and use **toggle bolts** or **plastic anchors**.  
     - Ensure shelves are evenly spaced and level.  
     - **Tip**: Place shelves *above* eye level to avoid frequent adjustments.  

### General Tips for Noise-Free Installation:
- **Work in Short Bursts**: 15–20 minutes between stretches to avoid stiffness.  
- **Prep Tools**: Have all screws, anchors, and adhesives ready *before* starting.  
- **Protect Walls**: Use painter’s tape to hide marks.  
- **Safety First**: Wear safety glasses when drilling.  

### Post-Installation Focus Boosters:
- **Decorate While Working**: Set up a "decorating station" away from your workspace.  
- **Time Blocks**: Dedicate 2-hour Sunday slots (e.g., 10 AM: TV, 12 PM: Shelves, 2 PM: Planters).  
- **Stretch Breaks**: After every 20 minutes, do 1 leg stretch (calf raise, seated hamstring stretch).  

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Start with your heaviest art piece first—this anchors the space. Then layer lighter decor around it. Use natural light to highlight artwork and avoid overwhelming spaces with clutter.
