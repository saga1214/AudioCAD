Here’s your final, segment-specific packing list to keep you functional, comfortable, and supportive for both sections:

### **For the Day/Night 20-Mile Segment (Until Sunset/Dusk):**  
1. **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking synthetic top and bottom (e.g., Patagonia Capilene, Baleaf).  
2. **Mid-Layer:** Fleece or light puffy vest (e.g., REI Co-op Fleece Jacket).  
3. **Outer Layer:** Wind-resistant rain shell (e.g., Salomon Speedskin or Columbia OutDry).  
4. **Headlamp/Handheld Light:** With spare batteries + headlamp strap.  
5. **Gloves:** Lightweight trail gloves (e.g., Chaco).  
6. **Hat:** Warm beanie or cap.  
7. **Buff/Gaiter:** For warmth or face protection.  
8. **Trail Shoes:** Sturdy, grippy road/trail shoes (e.g., Brooks Ghost + trail inserts).  
9. *Optional:* Light pack/vest (for quick layer swaps).  

### **For the Final 12-Mile Night Segment:**  
1. **Base Layer:** Same as Day segment.  
2. **Mid-Layer:** Fleece or puffy (add insulation for cooler temps).  
3. **Outer Layer:** **Wind/Water-Resistant Insulated Jacket** (e.g., Columbia OutDry Thermal + Windstopper).  
4. **Headlamp/Handheld Light:** **With spare batteries + headlamp.**  
5. **Gloves:** Insulated trail gloves (e.g., Chaco Wool).  
6. **Hat:** Warm beanie + wind-resistant cap (e.g., Hestra).  
7. **Buff/Gaiter:** For neck/wrists.  
8. **Light Insulated Jacket:** For immediate use (critical for night).  
9. **Small Pack/Vest:** **Essential** for stashing husband’s gear + your layers.  
10. *Extra:* Extra batteries, buff for added warmth.  

### **Key Differentiators for Night:**  
- **Insulated Outer Layer:** Wind resistance + warmth combo prevents chills.  
- **Extra Batteries:** Headlamps die fast in cold/humidity.  
- **Pack/Vest:** Carry layers to husband at aid stations (e.g., remove his sweaty top, add yours).  

### **Pro Tips:**  
- **Test Layers:** Wear your mid-layer during pacing to gauge warmth.  
- **Pack Light:** Aid stations have extras—don’t overpack.  
- **Safety:** Tell someone your route and expected return time.
