Here's a quick recap of the analogy:

- **Heart**: Gas station + engine (pumps cars/blood).
- **Lungs**: Refueling pit stops (give blood cars oxygen).
- **Arteries**: Big highways (carry blood away from the heart).
- **Capillaries**: Tiny side-streets (deliver oxygen to cells).
- **Veins**: Return roads (bring blood back to the heart).
- **Kidneys**: Waste plant (filter blood and make pee).
- **Liver**: Service station (processes fuel/junk).

All working together to power your body! 🚗💨
