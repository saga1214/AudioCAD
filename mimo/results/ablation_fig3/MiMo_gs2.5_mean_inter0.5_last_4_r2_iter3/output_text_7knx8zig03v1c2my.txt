### 🎯 Clicker Training Explained  
A clicker is a tool to **mark the exact moment your puppy does something right**. It works like this:  
1. **Train the clicker:** Click → give a treat. Repeat 10+ times. Puppy learns "click = treat."  
2. **Add the behavior:** Puppy sits? **Click** → immediately give a treat.  
3. **Build the chain:**  
   - Puppy sits → **Click** → treat.  
   - Puppy sits and looks at you → **Click** → treat.  
   - Repeat, adding complexity.  

**Why it works:** The click is precise and exciting! It teaches your puppy *exactly* what to repeat.
