Cooking could work if you pick something simple and stress-free. Something quick and non-traditional might help since dietary issues could still be a landmine. How about a vegetarian pasta or a stir-fry with lots of veggies she might actually eat, like bell peppers or corn? Keep the steps easy, maybe a one-pot meal with minimal prep. The focus can be on having fun in the kitchen rather than performance, and you’ll both have hands busy, which can help.
