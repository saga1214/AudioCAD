That's a fantastic idea—pickle juice makes salmon tender and flavorful! Here are a few ways to use them:

### 🐟 **Pickle Juice Salmon Marinade**  
Mix:  
- ¼ cup pickle juice  
- 2 tbsp olive oil  
- 1 garlic clove, minced  
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup  
- Fresh dill or thyme  
- Salt & pepper to taste  
Let the salmon sit for **20–30 minutes** before baking, grilling, or pan-searing.  

### 🥒 **Other Pickle Ideas**  
1. **Spicy Pickle Dip**:  
   Blend pickle juice, Greek yogurt, a splash of hot sauce, and a pinch of cayenne. Serve with veggies or crackers.  
2. **Cucumber Salad**:  
   Toss sliced cucumbers, red onion, and fresh dill with **1 tbsp pickle juice + 1 tsp olive oil + lemon zest**. Chill 15 mins.  
3. **Pickle Juice "Garnish"**:  
   Drizzle a few drops into pan sauces for salmon or grilled chicken.  

### ⚠️ Quick Note:  
Store-bought pickle juice can be high in sodium. If possible, use homemade pickles (or rinse store-bought ones briefly to reduce salt).  

Enjoy your meal—it’s clever, healthy, and full of flavor! 🥗✨
