Got one for you. Let's say the Red Team is attacking, and Red Player 7 is in an offside position when Player 11 plays a through ball to them. Now, imagine Red Player 7 is about to receive the ball off the pass, but right then, Red Player 7 bumps into Blue Defender number 6, causing the ball to deflect off the defender instead.

Here's the difference in how that's called. According to modern college high school rules, since Red Player 7 was in an offside position and deliberately interfered with the play by bumping into the defender, Player 6 reset the situation. That makes the attacker no longer offside, and the play should continue.

However, under older, US pre-2004 rules, a defender's touch would only reset the situation if it was an involuntary rebound. A deliberate touch, like being bumped into, would not reset it. So under the old rules, it would still be an offside offense.
