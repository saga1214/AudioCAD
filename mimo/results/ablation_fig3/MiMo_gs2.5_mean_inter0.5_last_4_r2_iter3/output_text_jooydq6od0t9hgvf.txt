### 🥫 Vodka Cocktail (Quick & Elegant):  
**"Berry-Mint Sparkler"**  
- **Ingredients**: 2 oz vodka, 1 oz fresh lemon juice, 3 oz mixed berries (muddled), 4 fresh mint leaves, 2 oz soda water (soy sauce-free for allergen safety), ice.  
- **Garnish**: Skewered berries + mint sprig.  
- **Why it works**: Light, refreshing, and soda-free. Works for all diets!  

### 🇦🇷 Fernet Argentine-Style (Classic & Bold):  
**"Fernet-Vodka Tonic"**  
- **Ingredients**: 1.5 oz vodka, 1 oz Fernet-Bitters, 1 oz tonic water, 2 oz soda water, cucumber slice (optional garnish).  
- **Shake**: Combine vodka + Fernet in a shaker with ice. Strain into glass. Top with tonic + soda water. Add cucumber garnish.  
- **Why it works**: Authentic Argentine vibe, balanced bitterness, soda-free (use soda water only).
