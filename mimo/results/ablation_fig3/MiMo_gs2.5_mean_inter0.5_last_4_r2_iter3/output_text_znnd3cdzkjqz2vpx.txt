Okay, you've got a great plan now, and you have all the basics covered.

But just to be sure, you'll definitely want a power drill with the right screws for wood. Habitat cabinets almost always come with pre-drilled holes, so hammering nails is generally not needed.

You'll also want a measuring tape to double-check the spacing between cabinets and for countertop length. Shims are also crucial for getting everything perfectly level and square before it's countersunk.

And please, make sure you have safety glasses on while you're working. It's much safer to have the countertop on *before* you stand on the cabinets anyway, as it adds a lot of stability to the base units. But for now, with the step stool, you should be good to go! Have fun with the project!
