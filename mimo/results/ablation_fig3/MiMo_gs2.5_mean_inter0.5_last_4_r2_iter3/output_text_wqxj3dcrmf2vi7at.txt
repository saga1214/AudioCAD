**Homemade Homemade Delights (Brown & White Coat Friendly):**
*   **Pumpkin/Occots Bites:** Mashed canned pumpkin (puree, *no spices*) mixed with a tiny bit of peanut butter (certified xyl醇-free!) or milled oats. Spooned onto a baking sheet, flattened slightly, and baked at 350°F (175°C) for 15-20 mins until firm. Freeze for frozen chews! *Why she'll love it:* Warm, sweet根茎植物味道，冷却冷冻后是美味的零食。
*   **Peanut Butter & Oat Cookies:** Mix 1-2 tbsp xyl醇-free peanut butter with 1/4 cup rolled oats (not instant). Roll into small balls or flatten into cookies. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 minutes until lightly golden. Cool completely. *Why she'll love it:* Classic corgi favorite texture! Oats add fiber.
*   **Chicken Skewers/Fingers:** Thread thin slices of plain, cooked chicken breast onto short skewers or wooden sticks. *Why she'll love it:* Lean protein, quick to prepare, great for teething.

**Easy Store-Bought Options (Check Ingredients!):**
*   **Brand Focus:** Look for brands emphasizing whole ingredients.
    *   **Blue Buffalo Basic Training Treats:** Small, crunchy, widely available. (Check size portion!).
    *   **Wellness Training Treats:** Often come in bags of various sizes/shapes, good texture.
    *   **Zuke's Mini Nibbles:** Small, meaty, widely available.
    *   **Greenies Mini Dental Treats:** If dental health is a concern, these are good low-cal options.
    *   **Merrick Hot Dog Bites:** Shaped like tiny hot dogs, often brown/orange but brown corgis sometimes carry them well. Check ingredients.
*   **Key Tip:** Always read the ingredient list! Avoid mystery fillers, artificial colors, and crucially, **Xylitol** (a common sweetener deadly to dogs).

**Recap of Training Tips (Sit Command):**
1.  **Get Attention:** Hold a treat close to her nose.
2.  **Raise & Shape:** Slowly raise the treat straight over her head. Her bottom will naturally lower to follow.
3.  **Name It:** The *instant* her bottom touches the ground, clearly say "Sit!" in a happy, encouraging tone.
4.  **Reward:** Immediately give her the treat. This links the word, the action, and the reward.
5.  **Repeat:** Practice short sessions several times a day in different locations. Keep it positive! Consistency is key – practice daily, even if just a few times.

**Collar Color Suggestion Recap:Teal/Turquoise, Soft Pink, Royal Blue.** These vibrant colors will look stunning against her brown and white fur!

**Putting it all together for Princess:**
*   **Treat Variety:** Mix homemade frozen bites and baked cookies with a few controlled portions of vet-approved store-bought options.
*   **Training Consistency:** Short, frequent "sit" practice sessions using the shaping method will build a strong foundation.
*   **Potty Patience:** Regular schedule, immediate praise/reward outside, and accident-free cleaning are essential.
*   **Barking Management:** Use the "quiet" command with treats/toys as a preventative tool, focusing on redirecting her attention to you or an appropriate chew.
*   **Style Princess:** A bright teal or turquoise collar will make her even more eye-catching!
