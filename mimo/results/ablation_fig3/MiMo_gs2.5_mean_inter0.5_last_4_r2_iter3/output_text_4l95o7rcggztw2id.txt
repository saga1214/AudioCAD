Alright here is a pirate nautical birthday shopping list with sand-friendly finger foods, allergy accommodations, theming and full coverage. 

First, for appetizers or “buried treasure”, you could get six 8-piece slider packs cut into mini rolls. Theme them by putting fillings like ham and cheese or PB&J inside and cut into shapes if you can find them. You can label them as 'Captain’s Rations' or 'Pirate Gold'. Also, get veggie sticks, cheese cubes or goldfish crackers all themed as 'Jewels' or 'Treasure'. To avoid watermelon, grapes or apple slices themed as pirate gems are great alternatives.

For the main course, mini pirate ship pizzas are a fantastic, mess-proof option where the pizza is shaped like a ship. Individual deviled eggs themed as 'eggplants' are another fun choice.

For snacks, get pretzel rods themed as pirate ship masts. You could also do popcorn in a 'pirate chest' container. Packing everything sealed snack bags or in treasure chest containers will prevent sand contact.

For drinks, themed juice boxes you can name 'pirate punch' or 'sea water', along with individual water bottles.

As a dessert besides your cake, you made pirate cookies. As an optional treat, you could get nautical cupcakes that are pirate-themed.

For extras, have plenty of napkins, maybe pirate-themed ones, plenty of trash bags, and serving utensils.
