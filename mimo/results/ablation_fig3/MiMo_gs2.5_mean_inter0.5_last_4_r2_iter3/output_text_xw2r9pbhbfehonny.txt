Of course. Here’s a quick checklist for filming one of your volunteer shows yourself:

Before you start, ask the venue if recording is okay—especially for nursing homes or hospitals. Set up your phone or a simple tripod, maybe on a table or a stand, somewhere it’s visible but not too distracting.

Then just focus on your show and be natural. Kids connect with authenticity.

After filming, you can trim the best parts—cut out any boring parts or mistakes—and edit lightly with simple effects or music if you like.

Then upload it to YouTube with kid-friendly labels and a fun title or description.

Start small like this, and you can learn as you go. It’s your work, and kids will love seeing more of it.
