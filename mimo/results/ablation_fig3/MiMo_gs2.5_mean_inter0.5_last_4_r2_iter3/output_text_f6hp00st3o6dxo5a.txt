Even though a small genetic change in the egg led to the first true chicken, the creature that originally laid the egg wasn't considered a chicken because of that missing protein I mentioned earlier. So, the 'first chicken' hatched out of an 'egg of an almost-chicken'. Therefore, the first chicken still appeared first relative to that initial egg.
