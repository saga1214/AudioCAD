Both succulents and the bird's nest snake plant are quite lightweight, so they will be easy to carry up five flights of stairs. You should definitely take them home at the end of your stay.

Small succulents usually come in little pots and weigh less than two or three pounds. The bird's nest snake plant also stays compact and small, usually in a pot that weighs between three and five pounds. You could easily carry both in one hand. Just make sure to bring some pots with you so you can transport them when you leave New York.
