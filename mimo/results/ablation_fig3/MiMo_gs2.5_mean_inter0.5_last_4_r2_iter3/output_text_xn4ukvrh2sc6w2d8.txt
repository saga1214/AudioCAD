First, Old Fashioned: You’ll need two ounces of bourbon or rye, one sugar cube, or half an ounce of simple syrup, three or four dashes of Angostura bitters, ice, and an orange twist for a garnish. To make it, muddle the sugar cube with a little simple syrup and the bitters, then add the bourbon and ice, and stir it well.

Paper Plane: Equal parts bourbon, so two ounces, Aperol, which is one ounce, Amaro Nonino at three-quarters of an ounce, and fresh lemon juice at three-quarters of an ounce. You’ll also need ice and a cherry or orange peel for a garnish. Just shake all the ingredients with ice and strain them into a coupe glass.

For the optional egg white Whiskey Sour, here’s how to do dry shake with it: You’ll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce for lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and one fresh egg white. Put all of that into the shaker and shake it first without ice until it’s frothy. Then, add the ice, shake again, and strain into a rocks glass over ice. You can garnish it with a lemon twist or a cherry.

And for the French 75: Use one and a half ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and about two ounces of Champagne or another bubbly. Dry shake the gin, lemon juice, and syrup without ice to foam it up. Then add ice, shake again, and strain into a champagne flute. Top it off with the bubbly and garnish with a lemon twist.
