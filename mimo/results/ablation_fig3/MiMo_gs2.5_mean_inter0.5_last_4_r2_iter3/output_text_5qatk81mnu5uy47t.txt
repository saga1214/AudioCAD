Okay, Belize sounds like the obvious choice based on your past experiences there, and Costa Rica is tropical but isn't scuba diving focused like Belize or Honduras. Belize checks all the scuba boxes you love. Let's build a trip around that Blue Hole wonder and more tropical diving goodness.

First, for short fins versus long fins. Since you're debating and are beginners investing in gear, short fins are still the smarter move. They're compact, maneuverable, and travel well, which is perfect for a dream trip. Long fins add power but require leg strength you might not have yet. So let's lock in short fins for Costa Rica.

Now for Costa Rica itself. While it has some reefs, it's not as renowned for deep diving as Belize. The best spots are around the Nicoya Peninsula for more relaxed shore diving, or you could fly into Puerto Viejo for some good coastal dives. But honestly, since you've already experienced Belize's incredible sites, Costa Rica might not live up to that Blue Hole magic.

This brings me to a better alternative: Curaçao. It's in the Caribbean, tropical waters, warm all year, and has world-class diving right off the shore. Think stunning reefs, shipwrecks like the Antilla, and even potential for whale sharks. It's a beginner-friendly destination with great infrastructure. Plus, the island is compact, making it easy to explore.

So here's a quick plan: Fly into Willemstad, rent a car, and dive at places like Playa Lagun, Sint Anna Bank, and the wreck sites. Focus on shore diving to master your short fins. This gives you warm water, incredible marine life, and a fantastic tropical experience without the cold of temperate zones. It sounds like a perfect fit for your scuba diving dream trip!
