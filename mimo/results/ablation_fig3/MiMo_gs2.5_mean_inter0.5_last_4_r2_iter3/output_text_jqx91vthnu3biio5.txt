### 1. **Color-Coding Icons: Memory vs. Mental Load**
   - **Memory Boost**: Colors can act as visual shortcuts, speeding up recall (e.g., red for urgent tasks, blue for archives).  
   - **Mental Load Risk**: Too many colors or mismatched hues can create "noise," making your desktop feel busy. Start with **2–3 colors max** (e.g., green = active projects, purple = reference). Tools like **Rainy Look** (Windows) or **Amethyst** (macOS) let you apply themes globally.  
   *→ Test it! If icons blend together after 10 minutes, simplify.*

### 2. **Playlist Rotation vs. Consistency**

   - **Lo-Fi Continuity**: Ideal if you prefer steady background noise without surprises.  
   *→ Tip: Use **Spotify’s "Focus" playlist** or **Noisli** (browser extension) for seamless transitions. Start with 1 hour of each sound before rotating.*
