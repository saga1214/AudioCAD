On natural lake ice, it’s higher, maybe 0.05 to 0.1. And here’s the tricky part: when rink ice gets extra cold to minus ten degrees, friction can actually increase a little, maybe to around 0.005 to 0.01, because less of that helpful water layer forms between the blade and the ice.
