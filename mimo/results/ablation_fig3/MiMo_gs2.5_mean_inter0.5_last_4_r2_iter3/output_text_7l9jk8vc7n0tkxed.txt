**First, safety:**  
Turn off the water supply to that pipe. Make sure it's empty.

**Step 1: Hand-Threading (Best with simple tools)**  
- Grab your hand auger or wire pipe explorer (if you have one).  
- Feed it in slowly, rotating it clockwise.  
- When you feel resistance, stop. Gently push forward and turn it the other way to cut or loosen the blockage.  
- Work in short bursts—don't force it. If you hear a crack, stop immediately to avoid breaking the pipe.

**Step 2: If hand-threading fails, try a small auger**  
- If your toolbox has a mini drain snake:  
  - Feed it in gently, rotating it slowly.  
  - When you hit the blockage, push forward lightly while turning to break it up.  
  - Don’t crank hard—let the tool do the work.

**Step 3: Last resort—boiling water**  
- Pour 2–3 cups of boiling water down the drain.  
- Wait 30 seconds, then pour a cup of *ice-cold* water.  
- Repeat 2–3 times. The thermal shock can sometimes dislodge the blockage.
