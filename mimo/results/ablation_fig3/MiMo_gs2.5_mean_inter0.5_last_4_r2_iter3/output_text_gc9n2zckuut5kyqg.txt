Absolutely—budget-friendly and health-conscious desserts can be both fun and cost-effective! Here’s a plan that keeps costs low while keeping kids happy and aligning with your goals:

### **Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:**
1. **DIY Fruit Skewers or "Build-Your-Own" Parfaits**  
   - **Cost:** Very low (fresh fruit is affordable).  
   - **How:** Offer a station with grapes, berries, melon, pineapple, and a yogurt dip. Kids can assemble their own skewers or layer them in cups.  
   - **Bonus:** Uses seasonal produce and encourages active participation.

2. **Mini Fruit Jellies or Popsicles**  
   - **Cost:** Low (uses frozen fruit or fresh fruit scraps).  
   - **How:** Blend fruit (like mango or berries) with a splash of water, pour into silicone molds, and freeze. Kids love colorful, bite-sized treats.  

3. **Oatmeal Cookie Bites**  
   - **Cost:** Low (uses pantry staples like oats, peanut butter, and honey).  
   - **How:** Make small cookie dough balls, flatten slightly, and bake. Serve with a dollop of sugar-free yogurt for dipping.  

4. **"Healthy" Cupcake Swap**  
   - **Cost:** Low (use banana or zucchini bread as a base—it’s cheaper and requires less frosting).  
   - **How:** Top with a few berries or a drizzle of honey instead of buttercream. Label them "Family Fun Swaps" to tie into awareness.  

5. **DIY Ice Cream Sundae Bar**  
   - **Cost:** Low (use sugar-free vanilla ice cream and toppings like crushed nuts, fruit, or cocoa powder).  
   - **How:** Let kids customize sundaes—offer a side of fruit for a healthier alternative.  

---

### **Money-Saving Tips for All Food:**
- **Barbecue Smart:** Use charcoal cookers (cheaper than propane) and focus on veggie burgers or chicken breasts instead of beef burgers.  
- **Bulk Batching:** Make large batches of sides (like potato salad or pasta salad) and portion them into family-style containers.  
- **DIY Stations:** Let guests assemble their own trail mix, yogurt parfaits, or fruit skewers—reduces waste and costs.  
- **Ask for Donations:** Reach out to local bakeries, farmers, or grocery stores for dessert donations (e.g., cupcakes, cookies) in exchange for promoting their brand.  

---

### **Final Notes:**  
- **Label Everything:** Use signs to highlight how each item supports diabetes awareness (e.g., "Fresh fruit for natural sugars!").  
- **Balance Sweet & Savory:** Keep simple salty snacks (veggie sticks with hummus) alongside desserts to avoid sugar overload.  
- **Kids’ Corner:** Dedicate a small area with colorful, easy-to-grab desserts to keep little ones happy without breaking the bank.
