Here’s a detailed 8-day itinerary balancing deep exploration of Tokyo, Osaka, Nara, and Kyoto with your coursework needs:

### **Days 1–4: Tokyo Immersion**  
- **Day 1**:  
  - *Morning*: Check out (10 AM), airport transfer (taxi ~¥3,000–5,000).  
  - *Workout*: Gym at hotel or nearby park (7:00–8:00 AM).  
  - *Sightseeing*: Akihabara (tech calligraphy galleries), Shibuya Scramble Crossing, Meiji Shrine.  
  - *Evening*: Harajuku nightlife.  
- **Day 2**:  
  - *Workout*: Morning run in Shinjuku Gyoen.  
  - *Sightseeing*: Senso-ji Temple (Edo-era art), Nakamise Market, Ueno Park (sculpture gardens).  
- **Day 3**:  
  - *Workout*: Gym session.  
  - *Sightseeing*: Asakusa (traditional art workshops), Sumida River cruise (views of Edo castle ruins).  
  - *Night*: TeamLab Planets digital art museum (great for presentation visuals!).  
- **Day 4**:  
  - *Workout*: Morning run in Yoyogi Park.  
  - *Day Trip*: Nikko (UNESCO Buddhist carvings, Kegon Falls).  
  - *Evening*: Return to Tokyo; research Nikko’s woodblock prints for presentation.  

### **Day 5: Tokyo → Kyoto (Shinkansen, ~2.5 hours)**  
- *Morning*: Travel to Kyoto; check into hotel (¥10,000–15,000/night).  
- *Workout*: Hotel gym (7:00–8:00 AM).  
- *Sightseeing*: Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion), Ryoan-ji Zen rock garden.  
- *Evening*: Gion district (geisha culture).  

### **Day 6: Kyoto & Osaka**  
- *Morning*: Day trip to Kyoto: Fushimi Inari Shrine (tunnels + torii gates).  
- *Afternoon*: Travel to Osaka (Shinkansen, 30 mins); check into hotel (¥8,000–12,000/night).  
- *Workout*: Gym (7:00–8:00 AM).  
- *Sightseeing*: Osaka Castle (ukiyo-e prints), Dotonbori (street art/kimono patterns).  
- *Night*: Kuromon Market (food + souvenir shopping).  

### **Day 7: Osaka → Nara → Kyoto**  
- *Morning*: Day trip to Nara: Todaiji Temple (Great Buddha, woodblock prints).  
- *Workout*: Morning run in Nara Park.  
- *Sightseeing*: Nara Park (deer + ancient sculptures).  
- *Evening*: Return to Kyoto; research Nara’s Buddhist art for slides.  

### **Day 8: Kyoto → Osaka → Departure** Universal Studios optional.  
- *Morning*: Travel to airport (Shinkansen, 30 mins).  
- *Workout*: Gym (7:00–8:0am).  
- *Sightseeing*: Kyoto Imperial Palace (free entry).  
- *Departure*: Submit coursework during layover.
