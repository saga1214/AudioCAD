Acoustic guitars usually tune to a sequence of four main notes: E, A, D, and E, going from the thickest string to the thinn-thinnest.

With a clip-on tuner, you just attach it to the headstock where the strings meet. With an app, you turn on the tuner, attach your phone securely, and then strum a string. The app will show you if the note is too high or too low. You just keep strumming that string and making tiny adjustments until the tuner's needle or line is perfectly centered. Just repeat that for each of the four strings.
