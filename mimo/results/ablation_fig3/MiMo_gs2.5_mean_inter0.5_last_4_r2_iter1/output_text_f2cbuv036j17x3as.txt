For diesel camper vans, pre-trip maintenance is really important. For brakes, a few signs of significant wear would be a high-pitched squeaking noise when you brake, or if the brake pedal feels like it’s going all the way to the floor—that usually means the pads are worn out. Another thing is if your vehicle is pulling to one side when braking, that could indicate an issue with the rotors or calipers. To check them, you’d want to inspect the pads and rotors visually—some mechanics can do this for you if you're not comfortable doing it yourself. As for spark plugs, they can wear out over time and affect performance, but changing them is a bit more involved—you’d need to remove the diesel engine’s air intake system, get to the spark plugs, and use a special tool to remove them. If you’re not experienced, a mechanic is probably the best bet. It’s always good to get a professional inspection before a big trip, especially for an older diesel van.
