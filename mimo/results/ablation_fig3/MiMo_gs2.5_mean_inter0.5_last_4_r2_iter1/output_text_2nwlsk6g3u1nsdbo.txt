### Shrimp Main Course Ideas:
1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
   **Ingredients**:  
   - 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled/deveined  
   - 8 oz pasta ( linguine, fettuccine, or spaghetti)  
   - 4 cloves garlic, minced  
   - 4 tbsp butter  
   - ½ cup dry white wine (optional)  
   - ½ cup fresh lemon juice  
   - ¼ cup chopped parsley  
   - 1–2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat)  
   - Salt and black pepper to taste  

2. **Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
   **Ingredients**:  
   - 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled  
   - 1 large onion, sliced  
   - 4 bell peppers, sliced  
   - 3 cloves garlic, minced  
   - 2 tbsp olive oil  
   - 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes  
   - 2–3 tbsp red pepper flakes  
   - 1 tsp dried oregano  
   - Salt and pepper to taste  
   - Fresh basil for garnish  

3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
   **Ingredients**:  
   - 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled  
   - 1 cup quick-cooking grits  
   - 2 cups vegetable or chicken broth  
   - 2 tbsp butter  
   - 1 small onion, diced  
   - 3 cloves garlic, minced  
   - 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning (for spice + salt)  
   - ½ cup chopped green onions  
   - ¼ cup chopped bell peppers  

### Spicy Lentil Soup (Recap):  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 cup dried lentils, rinsed  
- 4 cups vegetable broth  
- 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes  
- 2–3 tsp cayenne pepper OR 2 tbsp red pepper flakes  
- 1 onion, diced  
- 2 carrots, diced  
- 3 cloves garlic, minced  
- Salt and pepper to taste  
- Optional: Fresh cilantro for garnish  

### Tips for Taking:  
- **Portion**: Split large dishes like Jambalaya or Fra Diavolo into 3 even servings.  
- **Spice Control**: Include a note like, "Adjust heat to your preference!" since illness can affect tolerance.  
- **Labeling**: Mark dishes and include an ingredients list as requested (all provided above!).
