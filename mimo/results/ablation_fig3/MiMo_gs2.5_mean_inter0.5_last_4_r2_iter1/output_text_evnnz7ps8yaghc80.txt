Of course. A weekly plan can definitely help. I suggest a routine of about three 15-minute sessions each week.

You could start each session with five minutes on one-handed two-ball juggling with your dominant hand using the beanbags. This will help reinforce your two-ball skills.

Then, spend about five minutes with tennis balls to adapt to their firm feel and round shape.

Finally, dedicate the last five minutes to scarves to slow everything down and really focus on the pattern.
