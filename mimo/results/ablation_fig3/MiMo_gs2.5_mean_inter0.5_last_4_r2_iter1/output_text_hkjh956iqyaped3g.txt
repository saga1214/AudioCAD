For a 15-minute break, especially as your last one, I'd suggest combining a little gentle movement with some mindful rest.

You could start with just two or three minutes at your standing desk. Do some more arm lifts and foot movements, but this time, try to focus on your breath as you do it.

Then, for the next five to eight minutes, find a comfortable spot to sit or stand. Close your eyes and just listen deeply to Toumani Diabáté's album we talked about. Don't try to focus on the lyrics or the instruments, just let the music wash over you. This is a form of meditation and is much gentler than a deep session, but it's very effective for re-energizing.

Finally, spend the last couple of minutes just stretching your hands and wrists gently, maybe taking a sip of water.
