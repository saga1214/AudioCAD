That's a fantastic pairing! Here are a few delicious and heart-healthy ways to use pickles and pickle juice with your salmon:

### For the Salmon Itself:
1. **Pickle-Infused Glaze**:  
   Mix pickle juice with a splash of olive oil, a teaspoon of honey, a minced garlic clove, and a pinch of black pepper. Brush this lightly over the salmon while grilling or pan-searing. The tang cuts through the richness beautifully.  
   *Tip: Reduce sodium by diluting the pickle juice with water or apple cider vinegar (1:1 ratio).*

2. **Pickle & Herb Butter**:  
   Soften butter, stir in finely chopped pickles, fresh dill or parsley, lemon zest, and a touch of garlic powder. Top the salmon with this butter before baking or broiling.

### Pickle Juice Ideas (Beyond the Salmon):
3. **Quick Pickled Veggies**:  
   Thin pickle juice with water or vinegar (1:1), then add thinly sliced cucumber, bell peppers, or red onion. Let sit for 15+ minutes for a bright, crunchy side dish.

4. **Pickle Juice Salad Dressing**:  
   Whisk pickle juice with a little olive oil, Dijon mustard, apple cider vinegar, and a squeeze of lemon. Toss with mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, and your balcony-grown spinach or herbs.  
   *Note: Use sparingly for sodium control.*

5. **Pickle Juice "Mocktail"**:  
   Chill pickle juice, mix with sparkling water, a splash of lemonade, and fresh mint. A refreshing post-run drink!

### Key Tip for Bloating Awareness:
Since pickle juice is high in sodium, **always dilute it** (e.g., 50% water, 50% juice) or use it as a *small* flavor enhancer rather than a base. Pair it with plenty of fresh veggies from your garden to balance hydration.
