Fire signs sharing passion often seek thrilling activities. Sagittarians typically enjoy travel, freedom, and new experiences, while Leos love to be the center of attention and thrive on grand gestures. Calgary is surrounded by fantastic options.

For a scenic drive and some hiking, you could head to Canmore and the Bow Valley Parkway. You can find great trails like the one at Johnston Canyon.

If you're up for a drive to the coast, you could go to Kananaskis Country. The drive itself is beautiful, and you can hike to the Kananaskis Lake Overlook for a panoramic view.

Another idea is to drive to the town of Banff. You can take a nice evening walk along the Bow River path and then find a nice spot in Banff to watch the sunset over the mountains. The drive back to Calgary along the Icefields Parkway is also spectacular.

A truly Leo-approved adventure would be to book a scenic flight over the Rockies. It's a bit more of an investment, but it's a spectacular way to see the landscape from above.

If you'd rather stay closer to the city, you could drive to the Elbow River for some kayaking or canoeing. Or, for a more relaxed day, you could take a scenic drive to the town of Okotoks and visit the High Level Bridge, which is a unique, old concrete bridge with great views.
