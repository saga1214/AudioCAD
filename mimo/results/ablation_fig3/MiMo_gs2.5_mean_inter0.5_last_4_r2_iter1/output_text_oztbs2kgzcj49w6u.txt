Absolutely, there are several great ways to explore Lockport's history.

First, you should definitely visit the National Canal Museum in Lockport. It's housed in the former Lockport Waterworks building and has exhibits on the history and engineering of the entire canal system, including those military espionage theories.

You could also drive the Buffalo andNiagara Heritage Trail. It's a scenic route that follows the path of the old Erie Canal, and you can see many historic sites along the way, including parts of the locks themselves. Just be sure to check their official website for any current hours or any restrictions on driving through the historic district.
