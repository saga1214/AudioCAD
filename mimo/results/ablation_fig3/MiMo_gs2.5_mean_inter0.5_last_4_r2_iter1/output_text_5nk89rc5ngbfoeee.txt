Here’s a practical plan for your end-of-year celebration that balances fun, inclusivity, and your needs:  

### **Option 1: Hybrid Celebration (Recommended)**  
**Location:** Park + Cafeteria  
- **Afternoon (Park):** Host a **mini field trip event** like a nature scavenger hunt or community badge activities. Set up simple stations (e.g., bird-watching, seed planting). End with a **picnic BBQ** (dairy-free salsas, veggie burgers, ice cream sundaes with dairy-free toppings).  
- **Evening (Cafeteria):** Follow up with a **low-key movie night** or craft party. Parents bring snacks (pizza, sundaes, etc.), easing your planning load.  

### **Option 2: Park-Only Party**  
- **Setup:** Use the cafeteria during the day for crafts/badge workshops, then move to the park for a **"Brownie Olympics"** (wheelbarrow races, sack races, water balloon toss).  
- **Food:** Parents bring BBQ supplies (rotating sign-ups). You provide a **dairy-free snack station** (chips, fruit, ice cream alternatives).  

### **Key Tips for Success**  
1. **Food Flexibility:**  
   - Offer **dairy-free ice cream sundaes** (store-bought or homemade with coconut milk).  
   - Pizza is fine—just confirm gluten-free options if needed.  
   - Rotate snacks: Parents bring desserts (e.g., one family brings sundaes, another brings pizza).  

2. **Manage Expectations:**  
   - Keep weather-dependent activities (like camping until later) simple your first year. Start with a **one-night cabin stay** at a nearby camp.  
   - Cookie sales: Assign regional teams (e.g., "East Block" sells at grocery stores) to spread logistics.  

3. **Budget & Roles:**  
   - **Dues:** Charge a small fee ($15–$20/season) for sashes, patches, and activity supplies. Parents cover sashes ($30–$50/person).  
   - **Snacks:** Create a **"Snack Schedule"** (Google Doc) where parents volunteer to bring one event per quarter.  

### **Why This Works**  
- **Low-Pressure Start:** Park events are easy to manage while pregnant; cafeteria meetings reduce setup stress.  
- ** badge Alignment:** Scavenger hunts earn Community Helper badges; crafts cover Arts badge.  
- **Inclusivity:** Food options cater to allergies/dietary needs (yours included).
