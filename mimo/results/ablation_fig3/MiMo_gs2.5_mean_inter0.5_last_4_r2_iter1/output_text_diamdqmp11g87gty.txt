### Snowshoeing Gear:
1. **Snowshoes**: Rent or buy beginner-friendly models (28-30 inches wide). Look for lightweight frames with deep footprints.
2. **Footwear**: Sturdy waterproof boots (like hiking boots) with good ankle support.
3. **Clothing**: Moisture-wicking base layers, insulating mid-layers (fleece/down), and a waterproof/windproof outer shell. Don’t forget gloves, a hat, and warm layers underneath snowshoes!
4. **Safety**: Trekking poles help with balance. Carry a map, compass, or GPS, and tell someone your route.

### Other Winter Activities:
- **Cross-Country Skiing**: Low-impact, scenic, and great for fitness (rent skis in most towns).
- **Sledding/Tubing**: Hit hills or designated sledding areas (always wear a helmet!).
- **Winter Hiking**: Explore trails with microspikes for traction (check avalanche risk in mountains).
- **Ice Skating**: Public rinks or frozen lakes (if safe—check ice thickness!).
- **Winter Photography**: Capture frost, snowscapes, and wildlife in their winter coats.
- **Fat Biking**: Ride on snowy trails with wide, knobby tires (requires studded tires for ice).
- **Snowmobiling**: Rentals available in many regions (wear a helmet and safety gear).
- **Winter Camping**: If you’re experienced, try a backcountry trip with proper insulation and gear.

### Pro Tips:
- **Layer Up**: Temperature swings between day/night make layering key.
- **Hydrate & Snack**: Cold air is dehydrating—drink water and pack high-energy snacks.
- **Safety First**: Check weather, trail conditions, and local advisories (e.g., avalanche forecasts).
