Here are five fantastic road trip ideas from Vancouver, perfect for a new family with a growing interest in exploring:

1.  **Whistler:** About 90 minutes away. You'll ski in the winter or hike in the summer. Stop at the famous Village Sausage House for a meal.
2.  **Tofino & the Coast:** A scenic 5-hour drive. Hike the rainforest and visit the wild Tofino beaches. Stay in a cozy cabin.
3.  **Kelowna:** A 4-hour drive. It's a beautiful wine region. Visit a winery and enjoy the views.
4.  **Nanaimo & the Saanich Peninsula:** A 2-hour drive. See the lighthouse and visit the charming town. Try the local smoked salmon.
5.  **Kamloops & the Kootenays:** About 4.5 hours. Visit Banff or Jasper on the way. There are great lakes and mountains to see.

Have a wonderful time exploring!
