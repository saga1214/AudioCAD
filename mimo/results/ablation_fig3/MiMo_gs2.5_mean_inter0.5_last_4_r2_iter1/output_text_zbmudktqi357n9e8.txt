Okay, Mexico is an *amazing* choice for autumn scene inspiration and watercolor practice. The best time for fall colors really depends on where you go, but here are a few suggestions:

In **Oaxaca**, the autumn foliage is vibrant from about mid-October to November. The city’s colonial streets and surrounding hills are just perfect for watercolor studies.

Then there’s **Jocotitlán**, which is known for its incredible fall foliage, especially in October. The rolling hills are just covered in these bright reds and oranges—perfect for practicing your autumn color mixes.

And of course, **Pátzcuaro** in Michoacán has these stunning autumnal views, especially around October and November. The lake and surrounding mountains are just begging for watercolor sketches.

If you’re also interested in rivers, areas like **San Miguel de Allende** or **Zapopan** have lovely riverside settings. And for space-like scenes, maybe the high-altitude landscapes around **Taxco** could give you some unique inspiration.

Mexico’s diverse landscapes and amazing fall colors will definitely give you a ton of material to practice your techniques. Safe travels, and happy painting!
