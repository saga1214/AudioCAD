Okay, it sounds like you've completed the most intensive parts of the prep! Since you've already cleaned, degreased, light-sand, and primed the surface, you're really at the final step before painting. 

Before you apply that last coat, make sure the primer is completely dry to the touch—usually, that's a good 24 hours. 

Then, you can start painting. Given you're going from a dark color to a light one, using a good quality paint in a color slightly brighter than your final off-white might help with coverage. And definitely apply two coats for a smooth, professional finish. While you're at it, you can also paint the cabinet frames and the backs of the doors for a more cohesive look.
