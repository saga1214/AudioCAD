Here's a complete list of corgi-friendly treats, tailored for your brown-and-white princess:

**Homemade Treats:**
1.  **Frozen Bites:** Mashed pumpkin, sweet potato, or zucchini mixed with a tiny bit of peanut butter (NO xylitol!), frozen solid.
2.  **Peanut Butter Cookies:** Roll peanut butter (check label!) into balls, flatten slightly, and bake until firm.
3.  **Carrot Sticks:** Simple, crunchy, and healthy.
4.  **Steamed Veggie Chunks:** Cut small steamed carrots, green beans, or zucchini into tiny, easy-to-chew pieces.
5.  **Peanut Butter Kibble Balls:** Mix plain kibble with a small amount of peanut butter, roll into balls, and freeze.
6.  **Yogurt Cubes:** Plain, unsweetened yogurt (dairy or vet-approved soy-based for sensitive dogs), frozen solid. (Ensure no added sugar or Xylitol).

**Store-Bought Brands (Check Ingredients!):**
1.  **Zuke's Mini Training Morsels:** Tiny, widely available, generally good quality.
2.  ** Wellness Train 'n Tasty Mini Biscuits:** Often found in nice colors matching coat.
