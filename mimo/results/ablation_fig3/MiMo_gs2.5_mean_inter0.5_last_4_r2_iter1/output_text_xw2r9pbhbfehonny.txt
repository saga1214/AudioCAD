Sure, here's a quick checklist for your first YouTube kid-show recording:

Before filming, plan out which skits or characters you’ll feature. Pick short, high-energy bits.

Then, set up your phone or camera before you start your performance, so you don’t have to worry about it while you’re on.

Make sure the audience is kids who’ll enjoy it, like foster homes or schools, if you can.

After the show, transfer the video to your phone or computer, edit it a little if you want—maybe add some captions or simple graphics.

Then, create a kid-friendly thumbnail for the video—something colorful and fun.

Finally, upload it to your new channel and share it with friends and family to start spreading the word.

That’s really all there is to it. Just start small and have fun with it.
