That's a smart call if Nordstrom Rack feels more accessible right now! For ironing winter clothing linings safely, here's what to do:

1.  **Identify the Fabric:** Check the jacket/fleece label. Is it cotton, polyester, wool, or a synthetic blend (like thermal fleece)? This determines how you handle it.
2.  **Use the Right Setting:**
    *   **Cotton/Linen:** Medium or high heat.
    *   **Wool:** Low heat.
    *   **Polyester/Fleece/Synthetic:** Medium or low heat. **Always check the care label first.**
3.  **Use Steam Sparingly:** Most winter linings are synthetic and sensitive to steam. **Turn off the steam function** on your iron unless the care label specifically says it's okay.
4.  **Press, Don't Iron:** Instead of sliding the iron across the fabric, place the iron directly on the lining and press down firmly for a few seconds. This transfers the heat without dragging the fabric.
5.  outer fabric facing down: If you're ironing the *outer* fabric and worry about damaging the lining underneath, place a **thin cotton cloth or press cloth** between the iron and the lining. This protects the lining from direct heat and scratches.
6.  **Avoid Direct Contact:** Never let the iron tip touch the lining directly, especially if it's thin or prone to melting. Keep the iron slightly lifted off the fabric while pressing.
7.  **Iron in Sections:** Work on small sections at a time, moving slowly to distribute heat evenly.

**Key Takeaway:** Low/medium heat, no steam (unless labeled safe), press firmly, and use a press cloth if ironing the outer fabric. If you're nervous, just air fluff or steam lightly from a distance with a garment steamer instead. Happy shopping at Nordstrom Rack!
