Absolutely! For budget-friendly desserts that are both fun and diabetes-conscious, here are some ideas:  

### 🍓 Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:
1. **Fresh Fruit Skewers**  
   - Thread grapes, berries, pineapple chunks, and melon onto skewers.  
   - *Why?* Kids love them, and it’s mostly natural sweetness with no added sugar.  

2. **Mini Oatmeal Cookies**  
   - Bake small batches using store-bought cookie mix (add oats for texture) and a touch of cinnamon.  
   - *Tip:* Offer sugar-free chocolate chips if you have a budget for 1–2 extra items.  

3. **Homemade Popsicles**  
   - Blend fruit (like strawberries, mango, or berries) with a splash of juice, pour into molds, and freeze.  
   - *Bonus:* Label them "Diabetes-Friendly" to reinforce awareness subtly.  

4. **Fruit Salad Bar**  
   - Set out bowls of seasonal fruits (melon, berries, apples, bananas) and let guests assemble their own.  
   - *Budget hack:* Use seasonal produce (e.g., watermelon in summer, apples in fall).  

5. **Yogurt Parfaits**  
   - Layer plain Greek yogurt with granola and fresh fruit in cups.  
   - *Why?* Protein-rich, filling, and naturally sweetened.  

### 💡 Budget-Saving Tips:
- **Ask for Donations:** Reach out to relatives for baked goods (cookies, pies, cakes).  
- **Bulk Staples:** Buy oats, flour, and seasonal fruit in bulk.  
- **DIY Decorations:** Let kids decorate cookies with edible flowers or sprinkles (minimal cost).  
- **Water Stations:** Use a large cooler for infused water (lemon, cucumber, berries).  

### 🧑‍🍳 Execution Plan:
1. **Barbecue Combo:** Grilled burgers/hotdogs + fruit skewers + yogurt parfaits for a balanced spread.  
2. **Dessert Station:** Set up a small table with oatmeal cookies, fruit salad, and popsicles.  
3. **Labels Matter:** Use chalkboard signs to note "Low-Sugar" or "High-Fiber" options.  

This keeps costs down while ensuring every dessert aligns with increasing diabetes awareness. Let me know if you need help sourcing supplies or organizing volunteers! 😊
