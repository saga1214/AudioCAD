Just to double-check your list, I'd suggest adding a few more things. You'll definitely want a measuring tape to make sure everything is the right size. You'll also need a saw, like a circular saw or a jigsaw, if you plan to cut any of the cabinet parts. A drill and some wall anchors will be needed if you find no studs in the wall. And you'll need countertop material, plus the hardware for installing the counters, like screws or brackets.

A stud finder is already essential since Habitat cabinets usually don't come with wall anchors. And if you have any questions about how to do something, it's always a good idea to have a contractor's license verification card handy in case a professional comes to check on you. 

Beyond tools, you'll need a painter's tape to protect the walls, some painter's cloths to cover the floor, and maybe some masking paper for dust. You might also need some caulk or silicone for sealing gaps later on. 

You're all set with the basics, but these extras will make the whole process go much smoother. Have a great time with Sarah!
