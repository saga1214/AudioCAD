That still misses a key biological detail.

Even though small genetic changes can happen in an egg while it's developing, the defining protein I mentioned earlier—the one and only ingredient that makes an egg count as a 'chicken's egg'—has to come from a hen's ovary. 

Therefore, you still need a chicken to produce that protein and lay a true chicken egg. The creature laying that first chicken egg had enough chicken-like DNA that it was already classified as a chicken, even if it wasn't the absolute first of its kind from scratch.
