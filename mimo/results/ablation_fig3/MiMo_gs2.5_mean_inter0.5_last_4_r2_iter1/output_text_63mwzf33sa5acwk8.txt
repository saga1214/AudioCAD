Joshua Tree locked your attention—state's CA, not metaphorical tree but Joshua Trees *everywhere*. Quirky park vibes, stargazing potential. West coast to JT basecamp: ~3hrs from SoCal, longer depending on origin.  
**Camping logistics:**  
- **Inside JT NP:** Scenic campgrounds (like Hidden Valley/Joshua Tree) $20-$30/night, *book NOW* (reservations essential, fill fast).  
- **Free BLM option:** Yes! Bureau of Land Management land *outside* the park lets you camp for free (no hookups, pack out trash). Search "BLM campsites near Joshua Tree" for GPS coordinates. Ideal for privacy/adventure.  

Joshua fascination led to Nevada cross-state thinking—your new relationship fascination with that state. Drive from JT to NE Nevada border: **~2.5-3 hours**. Las Vegas is northeast, ~3 hours total. Nevada desert camping options:  

1. **Valley of Fire SP (NE of Vegas):** Stunning petrified wood formations. Campgrounds $20-$30/night. ~1hr from Strip.  
2. **Red Rock Canyon NP (near Vegas):** Dramatic cliffs, hiking trails. Campgrounds $20/night. ~30 mins from Strip.  
3. **Free BLM Land:** Abundant further north (towards Black Rock Playa/Burning Man area). Scenic but remote. Search "BLM camping Nevada" + your desired region.  

 Burning Man connection: Yes, held annually in **Black Rock Desert** (northeast Nevada, ~4hrs from Vegas).
