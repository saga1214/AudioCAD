Of course, here is a clear, step-by-step breakdown of a standard professional resume format that you can follow.

First, let's talk about the contact information layout. Start with a bolded, larger font, about 14-point, for your full name. Right below and aligned to the right, using a smaller nine-point font, you can have icons followed by your email in parentheses, a hyphen, your phone number, another hyphen, and finally your city. Placing this all below your name is much cleaner than having it to the side.

Second, the experience section. The "Relevant Experience" is your top priority. Here, you'll detail your most relevant jobs, including the job title, company name, and dates. Use concise bullet points to highlight your key achievements. Then, in the "Additional Work History" section, you can list your other roles, again with the job title, company, and dates, but you can leave out the detailed bullet points for these.

Third, your education section. List your highest degree first, including the school name and the expected graduation month and year. Within this section, add details right underneath, like if you "Graduated with Highest Honards" or received a scholarship.

Fourth, your skills section. This should be tailored to the job description. You can list your technical skills, soft skills, and any languages you speak. Just keep it relevant and to the point.

And optionally, you can add a Professional Summary, Awards and Honors section similar to your education achievements, or a Certifications section at the end.

Here’s a quick example of how it would look. At the top, you have your name. Below that, your contact info. Then a Professional Summary. After that, the Relevant Experience section with your most important job. Following that is the Additional Work History. Then the Education section, followed by a Skills section, and any other optional sections at the end.

The most important thing is to tailor this structure for every job you apply for, emphasizing the most relevant information. Does this help clarify the structure for you?
