Initally nervous is very common in these conversations. One preparation step you can take is to request a formal meeting by email. You could frame it as a 'performance review' and then, once you're in the meeting, transition the conversation naturally by saying something like, “Based on my first year, I’d like to discuss my performance and contributions.”

Another idea is to use the annual company performance review meeting as the natural and expected setting for this conversation, since it's part of the company's standard process.
