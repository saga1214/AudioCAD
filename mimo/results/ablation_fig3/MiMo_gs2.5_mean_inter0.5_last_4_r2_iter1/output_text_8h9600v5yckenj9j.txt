For Halloween, classic crowd-pleasers are always a safe bet. Things like Skittles, Sour Patch Kids, gummy worms or bears, M&M's in spooky colors, and classic chocolate bars like Snickers or Milky Way are usually a hit. If you can find them, mini candy bars are great because guests can grab a few without feeling like they’re taking too much. You could also get some glow sticks, which are always fun at parties. Just try to avoid the weird Halloween-themed candies that nobody really likes, or the super cheap, old-looking ones. Stick with the good stuff, and you’ll have plenty left over to eat on Halloween night!
