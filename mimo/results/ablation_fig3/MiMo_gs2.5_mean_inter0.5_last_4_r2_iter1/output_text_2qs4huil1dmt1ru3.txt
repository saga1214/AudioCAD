Let’s estimate both tasks.

Drywall items: Joint compound small tub, sandpaper/sponge, putty knife 6-inch, nails/adhesive, primer/paint for a small area, maybe replacement molding. Let’s say $15 to $30 depending on paint quality.
Cake items: AP flour, sugar, baking powder, butter sticks x2, eggs, milk cup, vanilla tsp, powdered sugar for frosting, cocoa if chocolate, pans. That’s probably around $10 to $20.

Total, I’d estimate around $25 to $50. It depends on paint and the size of the cake pans. If you’re buying paint by the gallon, it could go up. But for small repairs and a standard cake, $30 to $40 should be a safe budget. You can always double-check prices at the store, but that’s a rough starting point.
