Of course! Here’s a full list of your four Kentucky fall-style Art Deco airport reception cocktails with all the details for your bartenders.

First up is the Old Fashioned: You’ll need two ounces of bourbon rye, one sugar cube or three-quarters of an ounce of simple syrup, three or four dashes of Angostura bitters, ice, and an optional lemon twist or cherry. Just drop the sugar cube in the glass, muddle it with bitters, add the rest, stir with ice, and serve in a rocks glass over fresh ice.

Next, the Paper Plane: equal parts, so that’s one ounce of bourbon, three-quarters of an Aperol, about half an ounce of Nonino Amaro, and three-quarters of fresh lemon juice. Shake it with ice, strain into a coupé glass, and maybe garnish with a lemon twist or a cherry.

Then the Whiskey Sour without the raw egg white: you'll take one and a half ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and ice. Dry shake all those ingredients together to foam up the egg white, add the ice, shake again, and strain into a rocks glass. You can garnish it with a lemon twist or a cherry.

And finally, the French 75: you'll mix one and a half ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and ice. Dry shake those, then strain into a champagne flute and top it off with about two ounces of champagne or prosecco. Garnish with a lemon twist.

Just a couple of final tips: If your bartenders are hesitant about raw eggs, you can just leave that step out. And since finger foods are needed, maybe pair some of these with smoky or sweet bites, like deviled eggs, smoked salmon blinis, or caramelized pork sliders.
