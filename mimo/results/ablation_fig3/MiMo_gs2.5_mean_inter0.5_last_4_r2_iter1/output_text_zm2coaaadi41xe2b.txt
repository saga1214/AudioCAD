Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! For ramen, check out Tonk61, which is great for tonkotsu, or Yama for their shoyu ramen. If you're into pho, Pho 99 is a classic. For a more casual vibe, there's Little Sushi for their miso ramen. And if you want something with a bit of spice, Sapporo has solid miso and shoyu ramen. 

For protein bars, it depends on what you need! If you want something high-protein and tasty, Quest Bars are great—low sugar, high protein. Optimum Nutrition has solid options too, like their Double Chocolate or Peanut Butter Crunch. If you prefer something more natural, look into Clif Bars or RX Bars. And if you're into jerky, Beef Jerky Sticks are always a good snack. Hope you enjoy your meal!
