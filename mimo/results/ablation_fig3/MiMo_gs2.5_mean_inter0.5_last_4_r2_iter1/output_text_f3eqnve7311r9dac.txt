That's a very smart way to think about it. Older comics are definitely rarer, and their condition is what makes them valuable. For truly foundational and valuable books from that era, you could look at *Tales of Suspense* number ninety, which is Captain America's first appearance alongside Hulk number one. *Whiz Comics* number fifteen is the first appearance of the Silver Age Flash. *Adventure Comics* number two hundred and fifty-two is the first full appearance of the New Teen Titans. You can also find affordable keys like the first appearance of the Creeper in *Detective Comics*. For Luke-related older books, there's an early Golden Age team-up in *The Defenders* number one from 1954. Other great buys under a thousand include *Captain Marvel* number one, the first appearance of Shazam, and *The Flash* number one from 1956. Just remember, finding these in good condition will cost more, and you'll need to budget for professional grading to maximize the investment.
