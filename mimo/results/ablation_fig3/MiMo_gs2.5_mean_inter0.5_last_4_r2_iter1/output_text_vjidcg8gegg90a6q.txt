For winter chicks integrated into your adult coop, the broder plate makes bedding choices a bit simpler. The main thing is to use a deep layer of a bedding that won't catch fire near the plate and repels dust.

My top recommendation would be to continue with large-flake pine shavings, just like the deep litter you planned for the adults. A thick layer (about four to six inches) will provide insulation and is safe with the heater right next to it.

An alternative is to use wood pellets. They are excellent insulators and are very rodent-resistant, which matches your concerns.

Whichever you choose, just make sure to clean up any wet or soiled bedding daily to keep moisture down.
