Of Northern California, I think Big Sur and Monterey would be absolutely perfect for swimming warmth and outdoor adventure.

Here’s a possible itinerary for about five days.

On day one, you could arrive in Carmel-by-the-Sea and stay at a nice place like the Inn at La Hacienda. For dinner, The Sardine Factory is fantastic and handles gluten-free requests well.

Day two could be a full day trip to Pfeiffer Beach. You can swim and see the famous pink sand. For lunch, you could grab a snack at the nearby Dinosaur Bar-B-Que, which has gluten-free options. In the afternoon, you could explore McWay Falls and visit Pfeiffer Canyon. For dinner, you could return to Carmel and try El Camino Real Inn Restaurant.

On day three, you could head to Davenport and swim at Natural Bridges State Beach, which is known for warm, sheltered water. You could have a picnic lunch at the nearby Davenport Inn. In the afternoon, you can drive along the scenic Route 1 to see the famous Bixby Creek Bridge. For dinner, you could try The Davenport, which is a historic inn with a great restaurant.

Day four would be a trip to Monterey and the Monterey Bay Aquarium. You can swim or snorkel at the beautiful Piedras Blancas State Beach. For lunch, you could have fish tacos at the popular Fisherman's Grotto. In the afternoon, you can explore the aquarium and then visit the Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctuary. For dinner, you could go to The Sardine Factory again in Monterey.

On your last day, you could visit Pfeiffer Beach one more time and then head home.

As a little bonus, if you really want to guarantee warm water, you could stop at the Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Beach in Big Sur on your way out. And Trish's Mini Donuts is worth another go in Monterey before you leave!

I hope you all have a wonderful and relaxing trip
