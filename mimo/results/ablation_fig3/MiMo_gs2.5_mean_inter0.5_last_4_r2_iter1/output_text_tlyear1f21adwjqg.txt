That's a tough choice, since "efficiency" can mean different things. Solar energy is great because it's scalable and getting cheaper. Wind power is very efficient in the right locations. Hydropower is super reliable and efficient once built. But if I absolutely had to pick just one for the single most efficient renewable energy source overall, I'd still lean towards solar. The reason is that its combination of falling costs, wide availability, and the ability to scale from small rooftop panels to huge farms makes it the most versatile and efficient option for most situations.
