Based on our planning for 20 adults + 4 kids, here's a comprehensive shopping list organized by category. I've included quantities for 24 people and prioritized mess-minimizing kid-friendly alternatives per your preferences.

### **Produce**
- Carrots (2–3 medium, *not glazed*) rosted simply with salt/oil  
- Asparagus (1 bunch)  
- Potatoes (2 lbs) for scalloped potatoes  
- Lemons (2)  
- Other fresh veggies (e.g., broccoli for sides)  

### **Proteins & Appetizers**  
- Ham (1 pre-cooked, 8–10 lb) for main course  
- Shrimp (1 lb) for shrimp cocktail  
- Sausage/kielbasa (1 lb) for pigs in a blanket  
- Pepperoni (for mini slider meat)  
- Cheese/bechamel sauce (for sliders/pigs in a blanket)  

### **Dairy & Eggs**  
- Cream cheese (1 block) for scalloped potatoes  
- Butter (1 stick) for potatoes  
- Eggs (6–8 for potential deviled eggs)  

### **Drinks**  
- Champagne (3 bottles)  
- Signature cocktail ingredients (vodka, mixers, garnishes)  
- Sparkling cider (1 bottle)  
- **Grape juice (4–5 bottles)** for kids  
- Water (large jug)  
- Ice (20–30 lbs)  

### **Sides**  
- **Clean carrot alternative:** Olive oil + salt-roasted carrots  
- Scalloped potatoes (cream cheese, butter, milk)  
- Roasted asparagus (olive oil, lemon zest)  

### **Kid-Friendly Focus**  
- Mini slider buns (24)  
- Pigs in a blanket (sausage rolls)  
- "Flutes" of grape juice (mess-free sweetness alternative)  

### **Decor & Atmosphere**  
- Balloons (festive colors)  
- Festive hats/noisemakers  
- Plastic flutes (for juice)  

### **Extras**  
- Appetizer plates/napkins  
- Food storage containers  
- Cleaning supplies (sticky-spot remover)  

### **Quantity Notes**  
- Appetizers: Sliders, pigs in a blanket, and chips will feed the crowd.  
- Carrots: Roasting avoids sticky glaze mess.  
- Drinks: Extra juice for refills; champagne for toast.
