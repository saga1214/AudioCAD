That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour Beach Resort on St. John is truly special with its protected, calm bay perfect for swimming and snorkeling right off the shore. Here are some great daytime activities nearby and around Cruz Bay:

1.  **Snorkeling at Reef Bay:** Just a short walk south from Secret Harbour, this tiny bay is incredibly shallow and calm. You can often spot small fish, rays, and sometimes even sea turtles right in the clear water. It's a pressure-free, easy snorkel spot.
2.  **Explore Cruz Bay & the Shops:** Gallows Point Resort and Secret Harbour are steps away from the charming little town of Cruz Bay. Stroll along the waterfront, browse the local shops (like The Waterman and the Virgin Islands Trading Company for souvenirs), and enjoy the lively atmosphere.
3.  **Snorkel at Reef Bay (Deeper Exploration):** While the bay itself is shallow, the reef just offshore (especially near the resort's dock) can offer a bit more depth and better visibility for snorkeling. A short walk south leads you there.
4.  **Kayaking on the Mangroves:** Several operators (like Gallows Point or Cruz Bay Kayaks) offer guided kayak tours through the stunning mangrove forests. It's a peaceful, low-pressure way to explore the islands, often with birdwatching included! You'll see crabs, fish, and maybe even dolphins or manatees.
5.  **Hike the Reef Bay Trail:** While not a strenuous hike, this beautiful trail starts near Secret Harbour and leads down to the beautiful Reef Bay beach (where you can swim). It's a nice way to get away from the resort area and enjoy the island scenery. Be mindful of the heat and sun.
6.  **Boat Trip to Coki Point:** A short boat ride (often offered from Cruz Bay or Magens Bay) takes you to Coki Point, a beautiful spot with a small beach and great snorkeling over a submerged reef. The boat ride itself is a fun pressure-free adventure!
7.  **Explore Trunk Bay (St. John):** If you rent a boat or take a water taxi, a visit to Trunk Bay is a must. It's one of the Virgin Islands National Park beaches, famous for its stunning underwater snorkeling trail with plaques describing the marine life. The bay itself is incredibly beautiful.
8.  **Snorkel at Maho's Reef (Red Hook, St. Thomas):** If you switch to St. Thomas, Maho's Reef is one of the best shore snorkeling spots in the US Virgin Islands, especially for seeing parrotfish, surgeonfish, and often sea turtles. It's very accessible from shore.

**Key things to remember for your trip:**

*   **Sun Protection:** Virgin Islands sun is strong! Always wear reef-safe sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, and cover up. Staying hydrated is crucial.
*   **Bug Spray:** Use good insect repellent, especially in the morning, dusk, and after dark. Mosquitoes can be an issue.
*   **Boat Access:** Since you're chartering, make the most of it! Explore different bays, check out hidden coves, and look for marine life. Your brother can safely enjoy the pressure-free experience of being on the water.
*   **Birdwatching:** Keep your binoculars handy! Besides seabirds near reefs, you might spot the iconic Bananaquit flying overhead or other tropical birds in the island vegetation.

Have an absolutely amazing week boating, swimming, snorkeling, and exploring the beautiful Virgin Islands with your brother!
