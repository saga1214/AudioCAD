Okay, your daughter's big play night! A thoughtful gift will go a long way. Here are a few ideas. First, a personalized item, like a custom keychain or a framed art piece with her name on it. Another idea is a sweet treat, like a cake or cupcakes from her favorite bakery, or maybe a fancy chocolate box. You could also get her a small keepsake, like a mini trophy or a memory book with notes from friends and family. If she likes to relax, a cozy item like a soft hoodie or a blanket could be nice. And hey, if running appeals to her, you could even get her a water bottle or a sports bra with her favorite running emoji on it!
