Here's a practical installation guide respecting your landlord rules while making your space beautiful:

### 1. **Mounting the TV**
   - **Tools Needed**: Level, drill, stud finder, TV mount, screws, wall anchors (if no studs).
   - **Steps**:
     1. Mark the center of your TV and use a stud finder to locate wall studs (or rent an anchor kit if no studs).
     2. Hold the mount on the wall with the level to ensure it's straight.
     3. Drill pilot holes slightly smaller than the screws/anchor bolts.
     4. Secure the mount to the wall, then attach the TV.
   - **Tip**: Avoid drilling late hours. Use a remote control mount to minimize noise.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Tools**: Drill, wire, hook, measuring tape.
   - **Steps**:
     1. Choose sturdy hooks or planter hangers rated for your planter’s weight.
     2. Mark hook placement on the wall (use a level!).
     3. Drill holes slightly larger than the hook’s diameter.
     4. Secure hooks with screws or thick wire.
     5. Hang the planter.
   - **Noise Tip**: Use a hammer drill (quieter than standard drills) and work during daytime.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Tools**: Drill, level, shelf brackets, screws, measuring tape.
   - **Steps**:
     1. Measure shelf spacing and mark positions (use a level).
     2. Drill pilot holes for brackets.
     3. Secure brackets to the wall with screws.
     4. Attach the shelf to the brackets.
   - **Tip**: Place heavier art pieces at shelf height (eye-level) for balance.

### General Decoration Tips:
- **Color Scheme**: Pull colors from your furniture/art for a cohesive palette.
- **Art Grouping**: Cluster similar-sized pieces together (e.g., 3 medium frames + 1 large gallery wall).
- **Lighting**: Layer lighting: 
  - Task lights near workspaces/art stations.
  - Warm bulbs (2700K) for cozy ambiance.
  - String lights for a studio vibe.
- **Keep it Open**: Avoid overcrowding—leave breathing room!

### Sunday Workflow:
1. **Morning**: Install heavy/delicate items (TV, shelves).
2. **Afternoon**: Hang art and plants.
3. **Evening**: Soft-decor touches (throws, plants, candles).

> ⚠️ **Noise Management**: If you *must* drill late, wear ear protection, use a battery drill (quieter), and keep it brief. Otherwise, prioritize daytime installations.

With these tips, you’ll transform your space without breaking the rules—and keep your legs limber! 🛠️✨
