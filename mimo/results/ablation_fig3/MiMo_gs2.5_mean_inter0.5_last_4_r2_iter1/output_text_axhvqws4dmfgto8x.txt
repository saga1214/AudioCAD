Don't worry, setting off the smoke alarm once doesn't make you terrible—most home cooks set theirs off!

For stir-fry noodles, here's the trick: make sure you're using metal tongs this time, no wood allowed! The most critical step is to drain the noodles right after they are cooked. You should leave them in the strainer to let any extra water out.

Then, you need to toss them with your sauce and toppings right away, while the noodles are still hot. If you leave them sitting, they will get soggy.
