That's fantastic! Illustrations are a huge plus. However, when you query with a children's book agent, **you must submit only the text** in your query. Do not include the illustrations. The agent's job is to find you an illustrator.

But—and this is a big 'but'—you must make sure the story itself is strong enough to stand on its own. Publishers will want to see illustrations, but they won't accept a manuscript that includes drawings. The agent's success depends on their ability to sell *both* the text and the art separately. So, your illustrations are a valuable asset, but they are part of the package the agent will need to sell later.
