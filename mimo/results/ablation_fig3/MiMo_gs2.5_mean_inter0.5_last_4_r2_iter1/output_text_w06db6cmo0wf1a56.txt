Okay, planning for ten dads, ten kids, sugar-free sodas, real calzones if possible, cupcakes or brownies for dessert—all clean grab-and-go options. Let me compile a shopping list for that.

Drinks: You'll definitely need water bottles for everyone. For the kids, grab low-sugar juice boxes. For the adults, sugar-free sodas in individual cans or bottles, plus plenty of extra water. Also, it's always good to have some sports drinks for the kids in case they get thirsty after a lot of running around.

Snacks: Cheese sticks are a big hit. Baby carrots are great with pre-packaged single-serve cups of hummus. Individual pretzels or trail mix bags are mess-free. Fresh grapes are also easy and healthy. Don't forget granola bars for a quick energy boost for everyone.

For the main meal: Since you really want real calzones, your best bet is to check pizzerias or Italian restaurants ahead of time. You can usually order a dozen or so mini calzones for pickup. Just make sure to pack them in insulated bags with ice packs to keep them cold and fresh.

Dessert: Cupcakes would be perfect because they're already individual. Or, for a simpler option, packs of brownies.

Just remember to fill plenty of large coolers and use ice packs. This setup should let everyone grab what they need and enjoy the theme park mess-free. Have an amazing time!
