Of course! For Florida Zone 10B, especially during the reverse 'winter,' fast and low-maintenance plants are your best friends.

Herbs like basil are a great place to start—they love the heat. Mint is also fantastic, but you have to be careful with it, 'cause it spreads like wildfire. Parsley grows pretty steadily too.

Leafy greens like lettuce, spinach, and radishes are perfect for Florida's cooler, fall and winter seasons before things get *really* hot.

And for bigger rewards, tomatoes and peppers are a fantastic call. They thrive in that heat. Just make sure they get enough space and good drainage so they don't rot in the humidity. Happy growing!
