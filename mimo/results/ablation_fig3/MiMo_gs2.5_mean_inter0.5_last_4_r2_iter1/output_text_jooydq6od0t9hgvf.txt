For the vodka, a simple but classy option is to make a vodka soda with fresh lime and a twist of salt—it's refreshing and easy to serve. For the Argentine Fernet, you can serve it with cola in equal parts—traditional, bold, and quick to mix. Offer both on ice and maybe some small wine glasses for wine drinkers. That should cover all your drink bases quickly but still elegantly. Enjoy your dinner party!
