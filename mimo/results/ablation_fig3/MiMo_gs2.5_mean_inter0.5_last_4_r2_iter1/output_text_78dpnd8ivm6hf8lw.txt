Alright, let's do a quick recap of our blood-car system. 

The heart is the gas station *and* engine, pumping the cars and delivering oxygen fuel. The lungs are where the heart stops to refuel. 

Then, on the journey out, the main, wide roads are the arteries. The super-tiny, winding side-streets connecting everything are the capillaries. And the return lanes, the roads leading back to the heart, are the veins. 

Once the blood has been all around, it gets cleaned. The kidneys are the giant car wash that filters out the junk. And the liver is the service center that sorts and processes everything to keep us running smoothly. 

So yeah, that's the whole blood-car system in a nutshell. Pretty cool, right? It keeps all our little energy-delivery cars on the road and keeps everything balanced.
