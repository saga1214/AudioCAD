Here's how to tune your strings using a tuner or app, step-by-step:

### **Using a Tuner App (Best for Beginners)**
1. **Open your app** (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune, or built-in phone apps).
2. **Place the tuner near the instrument** so the microphone picks up string vibrations.
3. **Press a string down** on the fretboard (e.g., 6th string for guitar).
4. **Adjust the tuner**:
   - If the tuner shows a **"—"** or **"—"** (down arrow), **turn the tuning peg clockwise** (tighten) to raise the pitch.
   - If the tuner shows a **"—"** (up arrow), **turn the peg counter-clockwise** (loosen) to lower the pitch.
5. **Wait for the tuner to show "—in"** (a steady note) or a solid green light.

### **Using a Clip-On Tuner**
1. **Attach the tuner** to the headstock of your instrument.
2. **Press a string down** and listen for the tuner’s beep or light.
3. **Adjust the peg** until the tuner locks onto the correct pitch (usually 440Hz for A).

### **Key Tips**
- **Tune up to the note**: Start with the open string (e.g., 6th string on guitar is **A**), then tune the fretted strings to their corresponding notes (e.g., 5th string is **D**).
- **Fretting Hand**: Press the string firmly at the fret to avoid buzzing.
- **Slow Adjustments**: Turn the pegs in tiny increments—don’t over-tighten!
- **Reference Notes**: Standard guitar tuning (from 6th to 1st string) is **E-A-D-G-B-E** (all tuned to 440Hz).

### **Troubleshooting**
- If the string sounds "flat" (too low), **loosen it**.
- If it sounds "sharp" (too high), **tighten it**.
- If the tuner won’t lock: Clean the string with a soft cloth (oil/dirt can interfere).

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Tune *up* to the note first (e.g., from low to high), as it’s easier to raise a pitch than lower it. Practice until you can tune by ear!
