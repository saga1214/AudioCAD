### 🥃 **1. Vodka Ginata (Refreshing & Light)**  
*(Great for warm weather or lighter drinkers)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- 1 oz simple syrup (or to taste)  
- 4 oz sparkling water or tonic water  
- *Garnish:* Mint sprig, lime wedge  

**Method:**  
1. Shake vodka, lime juice, and syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a chilled glass.  
3. Top with sparkling water/tonic.  
4. Garnish with mint and lime.  

---

### 🥃 **2. Fernet Amargo (Argentine-Style Sippable Spirit)**  
*(Sweet, herbal, and sophisticated—no soda allergy risk!)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca (or any Fernet)  
- 0.5 oz honey syrup (equal parts honey + warm water)  
- 4 oz sparkling water or club soda  
- 4-5 thin mint leaves  
- *Garnish:* Lemon twist  

**Method:**  
1. Muddle mint leaves lightly in a shaker.  
2. Add Fernet, honey syrup, and ice.  
3. Shake well, then strain into a chilled coupe or rocks glass.  
4. Top with sparkling water.  
5. Express lemon twist oil over drink and garnish.  

---

### ✅ **Why These Work:**  
- **No Soda Allergy:** Both use sparkling water (not soda) or tonic.  
- **Formal & Balanced:** Clean ingredients, elegant presentation.  
- **Quick Prep:** Ready in <5 minutes each.  
- **Vegetarian-Friendly:** No animal products.
