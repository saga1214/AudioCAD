Of course. Let's start with other potential causes for skin redness. It could be rosacea, sun damage, allergies, or even some medications. 

Now, for vasculitis, it's an autoimmune condition where the body's immune system mistakenly attacks the walls of the blood vessels, causing them to become inflamed. The size of the affected vessel determines the type of vasculitis and the symptoms. For example, inflammation in large vessels can cause muscle and joint pain, while inflammation in small vessels is more likely to cause skin rashes, nerve pain, or even organ problems. The inflammation itself can cause the redness and other symptoms.
