   - *Note*: Energetic—ensure you have time for walks/play  

2. **Golden Retriever**  
   - *Why*: Gentle, highly trainable, great with kids, low prey drive  
   - *Size*: Medium-large (consider adult dogs for calmer temperament)  

3. **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel**  
   - *Why*: Affectionate, easygoing, loves kids, low prey drive  
   - *Size*: Small (ideal for apartments)  
   - *Note*: Prone to separation anxiety—avoid leaving alone for long hours  

4. **French Bulldog**  
   - *Why*: Chill personality, low energy, good with kids  
   - *Size*: Small  
   - *Note*: Brachycephalic (breathing issues)—short walks suffice  

5. **Poodle (Miniature/Smartypup)**  
   - *Why*: Highly trainable, hypoallergenic, loves people  
   - *Size*: Miniature/Toy (perfect for high-rises)  

6. **Bichon Frise**  
   - *Why*: Sweet-natured, low-shedding, good with kids  
   - *Size*: Small  
   - *Note*: Can be vocal when alone—test with your landlord  

7. **Beagle**  
   - *Why*: Friendly, good with kids/dogs, low prey drive (though curious!)  
   - *Size*: Small-medium  

### 🌟 Rescue/Mix Options
- **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel Rescues**: Often available, low-energy  
- **Miniature Labradors**: Smaller Labs (check breeders/rescues)  
- **Poodle Crosses**: Look for "Cavapoo" (Cavalier+Poodle) or "Yorkipoo"  
- **Border Collie Rescues**: *If* you can provide high exercise—intelligent but needs stimulation  

### 🔍 Key Steps to Find Your Dog
1. **Research Local Shelters/Rescues**:  
   - Filter by breed/size (e.g., "Cavalier," "French Bulldog").  
   - Prioritize rescues that temperament-test adults.  
2. **Visit & Observe**:  
   - Bring your spouse! Watch how dogs interact with:  
     - Kids (if applicable)  
     - Other dogs  
     - You (when alone)  
3. **Ask Landlord for Proof**:  
   - Request a written lease agreement confirming breed/size restrictions.  
4. **Trial Period**:  
   - Borrow foster a dog for a weekend to test compatibility.  
5. **Training Essentials**:  
   - Start basic commands (sit, stay) immediately—use positive reinforcement.  
   - Consider a puppy training class (even for adult dogs).  

### ⚠️ Critical Considerations
- **Separation Anxiety**: Test dogs in shelters/rescues when left alone—even "chill" breeds like Frenchies can bark.  
- **Noise Control**: French Bulldogs/Bichons can be vocal—invest in white noise or training.  
- **Hybrid Lifestyle**: A low-energy breed (Cavalier, French Bulldog) suits your schedule better than high-energy Labs.  
- **Allergies**: Poodles/Bichons are hypoallergenic if needed.  

**Final Tip**: Prioritize adult dogs from rescues—they often have known temperaments and meet your "no chasing" criteria. Many shelters have perfect "overlooked" dogs like small Goldens or Calvies! 🐶💕
