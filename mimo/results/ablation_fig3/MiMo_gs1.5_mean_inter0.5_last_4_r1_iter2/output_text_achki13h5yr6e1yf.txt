Of course. Let's break down the total cost for your family of four dinner. For the chicken cutlets, two pounds of chicken breasts will be between eight and fifteen dollars; a bag of all-purpose flour is three to five dollars; a dozen eggs are three to seven dollars; a container of breadcrumbs is two to four dollars; and a bottle of vegetable oil is four to eight dollars. For the garlic mashed potatoes, a five-pound bag of potatoes is three to six dollars; one head of garlic is about a dollar; a pound of butter is four to seven dollars; and a half-gallon of milk is three to five dollars.

So, the total estimated cost for the main dish plus the side dish comes out to a range of about forty-eight to seventy-eight dollars.
