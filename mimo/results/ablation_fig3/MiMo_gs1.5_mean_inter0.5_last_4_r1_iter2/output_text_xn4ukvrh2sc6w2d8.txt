Here's a detailed ingredient list with measurements for your cocktail reception, perfect for your bartenders' reference:

### 1. **Old Fashioned** (Bourbon)
- **Bourbon**: 2 oz (60ml)  
- **Sugar Cube** or ¾ tsp simple syrup  
- **Angostura Bitters**: 2 dashes  
- **Orange Twist**: For garnish  
- *Optional*: 0.5 oz (15ml) water to dissolve sugar  

### 2. **Paper Plane** (Equal Parts)
- **Bourbon**: 2 oz (60ml)  
- **Aperol**: 1.5 oz (45ml)  
- **Amaro Nonino**: 1 oz (30ml)  
- **Fresh Lemon Juice**: 0.75 oz (22ml)  
- *Garnish*: Orange twist  

### 3. **Whiskey Sour** (Classic)
- **Bourbon**: 2 oz (60ml)  
- **Fresh Lemon Juice**: 1.5 oz (45ml)  
- **Simple Syrup**: 0.75 oz (22ml)  
- **Egg White**: 1 egg white (or 0.5 oz aquafaba for vegan)  
- *Garnish*: Lemon wheel or cherry  

### 4. **Aviation** (Gin Cocktail)
- **Gin**: 2 oz (60ml)  
- **Maraschino Liqueur**: ¾ oz (22ml)  
- **Crème de Violette**: ¼ oz (7ml)  
- **Fresh Lemon Juice**: ¾ oz (22ml)  
- *Garnish*: Maraschino cherry  

### 5. **French 75** (Gin Option - Bonus)
- **Gin**: 1.5 oz (45ml)  
- **Cognac or Brandy**: 0.5 oz (15ml)  
- **Lemon Juice**: 0.75 oz (22ml)  
- **Simple Syrup**: 0.5 oz (15ml)  
- **Champagne/Prosecco**: Top to fill  
- *Garnish*: Lemon twist  

---

### **Key Tips for Bartenders**:
1. **Glassware**:  
   - Old Fashioned: Rocks glass  
   - Aviation/French 75: Coupe or Nick & Nora glass  
   - Paper Plane/Whiskey Sour: Rocks glass over ice  
2. **Shaking**:  
   - Whiskey Sour with egg white: **Dry shake** (no ice) first, then shake with ice.  
   - Aviation/French 75: Shake all ingredients, strain into chilled glass.  
3. **Bitters**: Stir Old Fashioned slowly to avoid bubbles.  
4. **Finger Foods**: Pair with cheese, charcuterie, and spiced nuts (fall theme!).  

### **Substitutions**:
- **Nonino**: Substitute 1 oz Averna (an Italian amaro).  
- **Maraschino**: Use Luxardo Maraschino Liqueur (avoid cherry syrup).  
- **Egg White**: Use aquafaba (chickpea brine) for vegan option.  

Let your bartenders know to chill all garnishes (e.g., orange twists) beforehand for a polished touch. Enjoy your elegant Art Deco-themed party! 🍸✨
