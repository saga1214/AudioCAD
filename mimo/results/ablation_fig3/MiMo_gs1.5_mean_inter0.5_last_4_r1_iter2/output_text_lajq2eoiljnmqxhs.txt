### 🍝 **15-Minute Lemon-Garlic Pasta with Spinach & Cherry Tomatoes**
*(No heavy cream, no meat, easy to customize)*

#### **Ingredients** (Serves 2):
- 200g (7 oz) fettuccine or spaghetti (check for gluten-free if needed)
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- ½ lemon (juiced + zest)
- Salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes (optional)
- Fresh parsley or basil for garnish

#### **Steps**:
1. **Cook pasta**: Boil pasta in salted water until al dente (per package instructions). Reserve ½ cup pasta water, then drain.
2. **Make sauce**: Heat olive oil in a pan. Add garlic and red pepper flakes (if using), sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. **Add veggies**: Toss in cherry tomatoes and spinach. Cook until spinach wilts (2-3 mins).
4. **Combine**: Add drained pasta to the pan. Mix in lemon juice, zest, salt, and pepper. Add a splash of reserved pasta water if sauce seems dry.
5. **Serve**: Garnish with fresh herbs. Let cool slightly before eating.

#### **Why This Works**:
- **Dietary-friendly**: No dairy, meat, or vegetables she dislikes (spinach/tomatoes are usually safe).
- **Quick**: Ready in under 30 minutes.
- **Collaborative**: You can take turns stirring, chopping, or plating.
- **Low-pressure**: Minimal cooking skill required—just mixing and timing.

#### **Tips to Avoid Awkwardness**:
- **Prep together**: Sit at the counter and chat while you chop, stir, or measure.
- **Keep it light**: Focus on the process, not the outcome. Say things like, "This smells amazing!"
- **Have an exit plan**: If tension rises, suggest grabbing dessert afterward (e.g., ice cream—lactose-free options exist!).
