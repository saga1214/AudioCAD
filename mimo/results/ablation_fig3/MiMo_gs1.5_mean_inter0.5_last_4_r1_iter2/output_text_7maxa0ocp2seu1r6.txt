No problem! Here's a complete, categorized recap of all the recommended measurements, in order:

### **1. Core Body Measurements (Every Fitting)**
- **Bust**: Across the fullest part (under arms).  
- **Natural Waist**: The narrowest part of the torso (usually 1–3 inches above belly button).  
- **Hips**: At the fullest part of the hips (measured 1 inch below the waist).  

### **2. Basic Measurements (Item-Specific)**  
- **Inseam** (for pants): Length from crotch to ankle.  
- **Sleeve Length**: From shoulder point to wrist.  
- **Shoulder Width**: Across shoulders at the point where a collar would sit.  

### **3. Additional Measurements (For Fitted Garments)**  
- **Neck**: Circumference at the base of the neck (for collars).  
- **Bicep**: At the fullest part of the arm (for sleeve fit).  
- **Shoulder-to-Shoulder (S/S)**: Across upper back (for jackets/shirts).  
- **Front Rise**: Distance from crotch to waistline (front).  
- **Back Rise**: Distance from crotch to waistline (back).  
- **Thigh**: Across the widest part of the thigh (for tailored pants/skirts).  

### **4. Special Notes for Your Client**  
- **Inseam Adjustment**: His 37" inseam likely needs shortening by 1–2" to hit shoe tops. **Test with the shoes he plans to wear!**  
- **Comfort for Sitting**: Ensure **back rise** is slightly longer (to prevent pulling) and add **extra ease (1.5–2") in hips/thigh**.  
- **Rise Type**: For tall clients with long legs, a **high-rise** design (with adequate back rise) improves comfort.  

### **Pro Tips**  
- Always measure **over clothes** (for accuracy in real-world wear).  
- Use a **soft tape measure** (not a ruler) for smooth curves.  
- For pants, **pin the hem** while he wears the shoes to confirm length.
