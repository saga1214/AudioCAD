Here's a concise summary of the key advice for your seven cats, balancing feeding, health, and budget:

### **Feeding Solutions (While Away):**
1. **Use microchip or timed feeders** to control portions and ensure fussy eaters get meals.  
2. **Separate fussy cats** in a quiet room for feeding before you leave.  
3. **Small-batch high-quality food**: Mix a small amount of quality food with cheap fillers (e.g., plain cooked rice) to stretch meals.  

### **Navigating Poverty & Health:**
1. **Seek free/low-cost resources**:  
   - Pet food banks, shelters, or charities (ask locally).  
   - Prioritize essential vet services (spaying, neutering, core vaccines).  
2. **Prevent illness to avoid vet bills**:  
   - **Hygiene**: Clean bowls daily, keep environment spotless.  
   - **Basic nutrition**: Supplement cheap food with safe scraps (e.g., cooked chicken, fish—avoid onions/garlic).  
   - **Exercise**: DIY toys (string, crumpled paper) to maintain weight.  
3. **Monitor health yourself**:  
   - Watch for early signs of illness (lethargy, appetite changes) to manage at home if possible.  
   - Prioritize prevention (e.g., brushing for skin/hairballs).  

### **Key Philosophy:**  
**Prevent problems > Fix them**. Stretch resources, leverage community help, and focus on low-cost care to keep cats healthy long-term.
