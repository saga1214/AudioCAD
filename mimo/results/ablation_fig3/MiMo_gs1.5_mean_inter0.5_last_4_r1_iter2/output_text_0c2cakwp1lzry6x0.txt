Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items, keeping the landlord's rules in mind (no loud drilling during work hours, but weekends are usually fine!):

### 1. **Mounting the TV**
   - **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, drill, appropriate drill bit (size depends on screws/anchors), level, measuring tape, pencil.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (wood beams behind drywall). *Important:* For heavy TVs (over 50 lbs), anchor into studs. Lighter TVs can use wall anchors.
     2. **Check Wall Type:** If no stud is available, use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for your TV’s weight.
     3. **Position TV:** Measure and mark where the TV will sit. Use a level to ensure it’s straight.
     4. **Install Mount:** Attach the mounting bracket to the wall at the marked spot. Use screws into studs if possible, or anchors if needed. *Tip:* Follow the mount’s instructions for weight limits and VESA rating (measures screw spacing).
     5. **Secure TV:** Mount the TV to the bracket using the included screws.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, drill, appropriate drill bit, wall anchors (if not mounting to studs), level.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Find Studs:** Use a stud finder to locate a stud. *Alternatively:* Use heavy-duty anchors if no stud is available.
     2. **Position Planter:** Decide where the planter will hang (e.g., near a window or shelf).
     3. **Install Hook/Bracket:** Drill a small pilot hole (size matches anchor), insert the anchor, and screw in a hook or small bracket. *Tip:* For lightweight planters, a simple hook works. For heavier pots, use a shelf bracket.
     4. **Hang Planter:** Clip or hang the planter from the hook/bracket.

### 3. **Installing Floating Shelves**
   - **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, drill, appropriate drill bit, wall anchors (if not mounting to studs), level, measuring tape, pencil.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder to find a stud. *Important:* For shelves over 10 lbs, anchor into studs. Lighter shelves can use anchors.
     2. **Position Shelf:** Measure and mark where the shelf will sit. Use a level to ensure it’s level.
     3. **Install Shelf Brackets:** Drill pilot holes (size matches anchors/screws) into the wall. Insert wall anchors if needed. Screw in the shelf brackets firmly.
     4. **Attach Shelf:** Place the shelf onto the brackets and secure it with the provided screws. *Tip:* Pre-drill holes to avoid splitting drywall.

### **Key Tips for Success**
- **Safety First:** Always use a stud finder and check wall types before drilling. Never overload mounts/anchors.
- **Pre-Drill Holes:** Prevents drywall from cracking.
- **Level Everything:** Use a level to avoid crooked installations.
- **Landlord Rules:** Avoid drilling during work hours unless it’s critical. Do heavy work on weekends.
- **Decorate Thoughtfully:** Group shelves by color or theme for a cohesive look. Use plants or art to soften the space.

By planning ahead and following these steps, you’ll create a beautiful, functional space without breaking the rules! 🛠️✨
