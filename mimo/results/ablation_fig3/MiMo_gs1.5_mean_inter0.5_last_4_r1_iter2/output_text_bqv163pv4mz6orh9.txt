That's wonderful! Here are some thoughtful gift ideas to show your daughter you're proud after her opening night, depending on your style:

### Quick & Meaningful (Great for same-day)
1. **Handwritten Note:**  
   Write a short, heartfelt note praising her specific contributions (e.g., *"I loved how confidently you delivered your lines in Act 2!"* or *"Your smile in the crowd scene lit up the stage!"*). This is free, personal, and lasts forever.

2. **Small Treat:**  
   Grab gourmet chocolates, a fancy cupcake, or her favorite snack from a bakery. Pair it with a note: *"You deserve this after an amazing performance!"*

3. **Play-Themed Keepsake:**  
   Frame a mini playbill, a prop from the show, or a photo of her in costume. Add a tagline like *"Opening Night Hero – Thank you for sharing your talent!"*

### Experience-Based
4. **"Mom's Support" Ticket:**  
   Buy a single ticket for a future show (yours or hers) as a "reward for your hard work." Frame it as a celebration.

5. **Relaxation Gift:**  
   Use your massage card and say, *"You earned this after all your practice!"* Pair it with a cozy blanket or tea.

### Low-Cost & Creative
6. **Custom Sticker or Keychain:**  
   Order a small item with her character's name or a play quote (e.g., *"Best Supporting Actress"*). Affordable and fun to keep.

7. **DIY "Proud Mom" Jar:**  
   Write 10-15 compliments on slips of paper (e.g., *"You’re so brave!"*, *"Your voice was beautiful!"*) and put them in a jar for her to pull out anytime.

### Pro Tips:
- **Specific Praise > General:** Mention a scene, line, or moment you genuinely enjoyed.  
- **Timing:** Give the gift *after* the show, during intermission or post-curtain.  
- **No Pressure:** Avoid expensive gifts unless you want to—your presence at opening night is the biggest gift!

**Most impactful:** A handwritten note + small treat is often the most cherished. It’s personal, immediate, and lets her know you noticed her effort. 💛
