No worries! If cheese wasn't her thing, you could try other small, smelly treats like peanut butter (make sure it's xylitol-free), hot dog bits, or even small bits of cooked chicken. You mentioned Maisy already avoids chicken, so maybe cooked chicken is out. In that case, try small bits of apple or banana. The key is to find something she finds really tempting. You could also mix some of these into a lick mat with some water and let her lick it slowly. That can be a great way to engage her mind and reward her at the same time. And remember, keep training sessions short and positive. If she seems bored, just switch up the treat or take a break.
