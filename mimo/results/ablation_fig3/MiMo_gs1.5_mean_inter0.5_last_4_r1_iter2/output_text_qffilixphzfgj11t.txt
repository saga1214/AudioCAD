Based on your parrot Bruno's sensitivities and focusing on **safe green vegetables**, here are 5 excellent options:

1.  **Spinach:** A nutrient powerhouse (vitamins A, C, K, iron). **Crucial:** Always **blanch** spinach first to destroy oxalates (which can interfere with calcium absorption).
2.  **Kale (Dark Leaf varieties like Lacinato/Dinosaur or Curly):** Very high in vitamins A, C, K, and calcium. **Crucial:** Always **blanch** kale thoroughly before feeding to reduce goitrogens (which can affect thyroid function).
3.  **Swiss Chard:** Rich in vitamins A, C, K, calcium, and magnesium. Similar preparation to spinach and kale – **blanch** before feeding.
4.  **Cauliflower:** A great cruciferous green alternative to broccoli (which Bruno avoids). Rich in vitamins C, K, folate, and fiber. Steam or roast lightly for better palatability.
5.  **Bok Choy (or Pak Choi):** A mild, crunchy green with a pleasant flavor. Good source of vitamins A, C, K, calcium, and fiber. Can be offered raw or lightly steamed.

**Important Considerations:**

*   **Wash Thoroughly:** Always rinse greens well under cool water to remove dirt, pesticides, and bacteria.
*   **Introduce Slowly:** Offer new vegetables one at a time in small amounts to monitor for any digestive upset (diarrhea, lack of appetite).
*   **Preparation:** Blanching (boiling for 1-3 minutes, then plunging into ice water) or steaming lightly is generally recommended for dark leafy greens like spinach, kale, and chard to make them more digestible and safe.
*   **Freshness:** Provide fresh, vibrant greens daily.
*   **Balance:** These should supplement, not replace, your parrot's primary diet of high-quality pellets and seeds.
*   **Avoid:** Stick to the list above. Avoid raw broccoli due to Bruno's sensitivity, and definitely avoid apples, bananas, and broccoli as you mentioned.

These greens provide essential vitamins, minerals, and fiber for Bruno's health!
