**For Woodworking (If You Explore It):**
1.  **Precision Measuring Tools:** A quality combination square, a set of calipers, or a high-precision measuring tape.
2.  **Hand Tool Sharpening:** A good sharpening stone set (e.g., diamond stones) and a strop – essential for keeping tools sharp.
3.  **Specialized Jigs/Clamps:** Something like a simple wooden jig for repeatable cuts or a specific type of clamp they might need.
4.  **Wood Selection:** A beautiful, unique piece of wood for a future project (like a scrap piece of walnut or cherry).

**For Clay (If You Explore It):**
1.  **Clay & Tools Kit:** A small, high-quality clay kit (like polymer clay) with a selection of basic tools (rolling pin, loop tools, needles).
2.  **Specialty Clay:** A small bag of a unique clay they haven't tried yet (e.g., stoneware, porcelain, or a specific polymer clay color).
3.  **Glaze or Paint:** If they work with ceramic clay, a beautiful glaze or underglaze paint set.
4.  **Sculpting Reference:** A small sketchbook or a book on clay sculpting techniques.

**General Meaningful Gift Ideas (Applicable to Any Hobby):**
1.  **A Nice Notebook & Pen:** For jotting down ideas, patterns, or plans. Something with a nice feel or design.
2.  **Quality Coffee/Tea:** A special coffee or tea they enjoy, paired with a nice mug or cup.
3.  **"Open When..." Letters:** Letters from loved ones to open when they feel stuck, need encouragement, or want to celebrate a milestone.

*   **Budget:** Tailor the ideas to your budget – a beautiful notebook and heartfelt letter can be just as meaningful as an expensive tool.
