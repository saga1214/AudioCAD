Yes, absolutely. That's a huge advantage. In fact, many children's book publishers are specifically looking for creators who can do both the text and the illustrations.

Here’s how you could approach it. First, you'd need to find a literary agent who represents 'hybrid' authors, meaning people who write and illustrate their own books. You'd still submit your manuscript and your query letter, but you'd also include a separate portfolio of your artwork to show them your style.

If an agent agrees to take you on, they'll help you find a publisher who is looking for that kind of package. It's actually a very strong position to be in.
