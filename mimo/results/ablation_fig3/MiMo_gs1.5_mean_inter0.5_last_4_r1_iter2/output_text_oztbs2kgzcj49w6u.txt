Of course. Since you're now interested in the canal, I'd highly recommend the Erie Canal Museum in Lockport. It's a fantastic place to learn all about the history, engineering, and the incredible impact the canal had on the area. You could even take a boat tour to see the locks up close.

Another great spot is the Buffalo and Erie Canal State Historic Site, which is right in Buffalo. You can walk along the old towpath, see some historic structures like the old canal warehouses, and learn about the city's deep connection to the waterway. It's a wonderful way to see how the canal actually functioned.
