Absolutely! For a quick, delicious Spanish dinner, you should definitely try tapas. Think small plates like patatas bravas—that's fried potatoes with spicy tomato sauce—or empanadas, which are savory pastry pockets.

For a more relaxed meal, paella is a wonderful choice. You could also try some local specialties like jamón ibérico, which is a type of cured ham, or some fresh seafood, especially if you're near the coast. Enjoy your trip!
