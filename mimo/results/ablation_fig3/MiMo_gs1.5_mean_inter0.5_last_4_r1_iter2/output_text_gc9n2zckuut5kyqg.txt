Great point! Here are budget-friendly dessert ideas that align with your diabetes awareness goal while keeping costs down:

### **Budget Dessert Ideas:**
1. **Fruit Skewers & Fresh Fruit Salad**  
   - Use seasonal fruits (watermelon, berries, grapes, bananas).  
   - Add a squeeze of citrus or honey (sparingly) for flavor.  
   - *Cost-Saver:* Buy in bulk at a local market.  

2. **Homemade Frozen Yogurt Pops**  
   - Blend yogurt with mashed fruit (e.g., strawberries, mango) or a touch of honey.  
   - Pour into popsicle molds and freeze.  
   - *Bonus:* Kids can customize flavors.  

3. **DIY Cookie Bar (Oatmeal-Based)**  
   - Mix oats, nuts, cinnamon, and minimal sugar for cookie dough.  
   - Let guests roll into balls and bake.  
   - *Budget Tip:* Use oats as a base instead of flour.  

4. **Fruit & Yogurt Parfaits**  
   - Layer yogurt, granola (homemade with oats/chia seeds), and fresh fruit.  
   - *Health Note:* Use low-sugar yogurt.  

5. **Watermelon "Pizzas"**  
   - Top cubed watermelon with yogurt, berries, and mint.  
   - *Fun & Cheap:* Zero prep time!  

### **Budget Beverage Pairings:**
- **Infused Water:** Cucumber-mint or berry-lemonade.  
- **Homemade Lemonade:** Use store-bought concentrate + fresh lemons.  
- **Sugar-Free Options:** Sparkling water with fruit slices.  

### **Cost-Saving Tips:**
- **Batch Cooking:** Make desserts in large quantities (e.g., cookie dough in one batch).  
- **Local Sourcing:** Buy produce from farmers' markets (often cheaper).  
- **DIY Decorations:** Use reusable plates/cups instead of disposables.  
- **Community Help:** Ask family members to bring a dish (e.g., "Bring a fruit salad").  

### **Why These Work for Diabetes Awareness:**
- Focus on **whole foods** (fruits, yogurt, oats) over processed sugars.  
- Offer **portion control** (popsicles, skewers) to subtly teach mindful eating.  
- Include **customization** (e.g., toppings) to let kids choose their own.
