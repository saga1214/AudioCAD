Okay, let's quickly recap the two analogies we used to explain yogurt. 

First, we had the party one. In that, milk was the empty room, the good bacteria were the party guests, the sugar in milk was the food they ate, and the warmth was the party atmosphere that helped them get started. The group hug they formed was what made the milk thick and tangy.

Then, we had the net analogy. Here, the bacteria were the team coaches, the milk was the disorganized crowd, and the net they formed was what trapped the liquid to make it thick. The flavoring was like the team's uniforms, which gave it a specific identity. The net itself was made of the milk's proteins.
