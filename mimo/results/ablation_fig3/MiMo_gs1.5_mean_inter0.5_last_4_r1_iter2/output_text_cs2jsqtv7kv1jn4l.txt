That's a fantastic idea to bond with your daughter through gaming! Here are five popular gaming consoles that would be great for playing together, considering your mention of racing and adventure games:

1.  **Nintendo Switch (or Switch OLED):** This is arguably the *best* choice for co-op play. It has an enormous library of games designed for local multiplayer (playing together on the same screen or locally split-screen), including tons of racing games (like Mario Kart), adventure games (like Zelda), and party games. The Switch OLED has a slightly larger screen and better speakers. You can play seated at the same table.

2.  **PlayStation 5 (PS5):** Offers incredibly immersive single-player adventures and racing games. While local split-screen multiplayer is less common on PS5 than on the Switch, many popular games *do* have it (e.g., Mario Kart 8 Deluxe on Switch is the top example, but games like *Rocket League* or *Overcooked* have it). The PS5 excels in single-player experiences that feel like adventures and has a huge racing game library (like *Gran Turismo*, *FIFA*, *Minecraft*, *Horizon Chase 2*). You can definitely find games to play together.

3.  **Xbox Series X or Xbox Series S:** Similar power to the PS5. Xbox has a very strong local multiplayer focus, especially in party games and sports games. Many popular titles like *Minecraft*, *Forza Horizon/Motorsport*, *Gears 5*, and *Halo* have robust local co-op modes. The Series S is a more budget-friendly option than the Series X.

4.  **Steam Deck:** This is a handheld gaming PC running the massive Steam library. While primarily a handheld, it supports local multiplayer through its screen. You'd need to find specific games that support local co-op (like *Hades*, *Stardew Valley*, *Borderlands*, *Overcooked*, *Cuphead*, or many local multiplayer PC games). It's unique because it gives you access to thousands of PC games, including many great local multiplayer titles.

5.  **Nintendo Switch Lite:** While smaller and handheld-only (no TV play), the Switch Lite is a great budget option if you're primarily interested in handheld local multiplayer. It has the same games as the regular Switch but is more compact. Perfect for smaller spaces or travel.

**Recommendation based on your description:** The **Nintendo Switch** (regular model or OLED) is likely the *best overall fit* for your desire to play racing and adventure games together in a fun, accessible way. The PS5 is a strong second choice if you're leaning towards more cinematic adventures and have the budget. The Xbox Series consoles are also excellent, especially for sports/party games. The Steam Deck is unique and powerful but requires finding specific co-op games. The Switch Lite is great if you want something smaller and purely handheld.
