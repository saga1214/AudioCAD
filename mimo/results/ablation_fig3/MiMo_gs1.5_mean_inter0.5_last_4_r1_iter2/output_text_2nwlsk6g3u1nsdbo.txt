Here are the three shrimp dishes I suggested earlier, with their key spicy components and cooking methods:

1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
   *Key Spicy Elements:* Red pepper flakes (or crushed red pepper)  
   *Method:* Sauté shrimp with garlic and butter/olive oil, then deglaze with white wine. Finish with lemon juice and plenty of red pepper flakes. Serve over pasta.

2. **Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
   *Key Spicy Elements:* Red pepper flakes (and/or cayenne)  
   *Method:* Sauté shrimp with garlic, then simmer in a sauce of crushed tomatoes, olive oil, and *lots* of red pepper flakes. Serve over pasta or with crusty bread.

3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
   *Key Spicy Elements:* Cajun seasoning (contains chili powder/cayenne)  
   *Method:* Sauté shrimp with onions/peppers in Cajun-seasoned oil. Simmer in broth until creamy, then mix with cooked grits. Top with extra Cajun seasoning.
