Okay, let's break this down. You've got the flu, feeling awful, and asking about that old saying and what your doctor didn't give you. You're asking for advice on your cough and seem to have brain fog, which is super common when you're sick. 

First, that "feed a cold, starve a flu" thing – it's "feed a cold, starve a fever," and it's more folklore than science. Your body needs fuel to fight, so don't starve yourself. Your doctor didn't give you an antibiotic because the flu is a virus, and antibiotics are for bacteria. That's absolutely normal and correct. Focus on rest, fluids, and listening to your body.

For your cough specifically, here are a few things that can help:
- Honey: A spoonful can soothe it.
- Warm salt water: Gargling with warm water and a quarter teaspoon of salt can ease irritation.
- Elevate your head: Prop your head up with an extra pillow at night to help with coughing and breathing.

And for the brain fog – it's super real when you're sick. Your brain is working harder and you might be dehydrated or just exhausted. The best things are:
- Hydration: Drink plenty of fluids. Water, broth, herbal tea.
- Rest: Let your body recover.
- Gentle movement: When you feel up to it, a short walk can help clear your head.
- Fresh air: If you can step outside for a minute, that can help too.

Just take it easy, be patient with yourself, and focus on getting rest and hydration. You'll feel better soon.
