### 🔧 Glue Gun Troubleshooting:
1. **Check the Power Source**:  
   - Is it plugged in *securely*? Try a different outlet.  
   - Is the cord warm? If it feels hot but the glue isn’t working, the outlet might be faulty.  

2. **Glue Stick Issues**:  
   - Are you using the right glue sticks? **Always use hot glue sticks** (not cold ones).  
   - Try a new stick—if it’s old/dry, it won’t melt properly.  

3. **Temperature Check**:  
   - Wait 5-10 minutes after turning it on. Some glue guns take time to heat up.  
   - If it’s still cold, press a glue stick against the heating element—it should melt instantly.  

4. **Clean the Nozzle**:  
   - If residue is stuck, unplug it, let it cool, and gently scrape the nozzle with a toothpick.
