### **DRINKS** (Cooler-Friendly)

   - Diet Coke/Root Beer (adults)

3. **Juice Boxes**: 10 (100% fruit juice, no added sugar)  
4. **Electrolyte Drinks**: 10 single-serve packets (for kids after a day of fun!)  
5. **Sparkling Water**: 10 cans (for a fizzy alternative to soda)  
6. **Ice Packs**: 4–6 (to keep everything cold)  

---

### **SNACKS** (No-Mess, Portable)
1. **Cheese Sticks**: 20 (10 for dads, 10 for kids)  
2. **Pre-Cut Fruit**:  
   - Grapes (2 lbs)  
   - Apple slices (washed, 2 lbs)  
   - Banana slices (10 individual bags)  
3. **Protein Bites**: 10 bags (e.g., trail mix, nuts, or dried fruit)  
4. **Pretzels/Chips**: 20 mini bags (chicken-flavored or lightly salted)  
5. **Yogurt Pouches**: 10 (Greek yogurt, low-sugar)  
6. **Granola Bars**: 10 (e.g., KIND, Clif Bars – no added sugar)  

---

### **MAIN MEAL** (Pre-Ordered & Packable)
1. **Mini Calzones**:  
   - 20 mini calzones (order from a bakery/pizzeria)  
   - *Tip*: Include 10 veggie options (e.g., cheese, tomato, spinach) and 10 meat options (e.g., pepperoni, sausage) for variety.  
2. **Pasta Salad**: 1 large container (cold, pre-cooked, with veggies)  
3. **Whole-Wheat Pita Chips**: 2 large bags  
4. **Hummus Cups**: 10 single-serve cups  
5. **Fresh Veggies**: Baby carrots + cucumber slices (for dipping)  

---

### **DESSERT** (Easy to Share)
1. **Cupcakes**: 12 (plain or lightly sweetened, no frosting for kids)  
2. **Mini Cheesecakes**: 10 (or graham cracker sandwiches)  
3. **Fresh Fruit Skewers**: 10 (watermelon, pineapple, grapes)  

---

### **ORGANIZATION TIPS**  
- **Coolers**: Pack 2 large coolers (one for drinks/snacks, one for perishables).  
- **Containers**: Use bento boxes, leak-proof containers, and ice packs.  
- **Extras**:  
  - Wet wipes, hand sanitizer, trash bags.  
  - A small first-aid kit (band-aids, antiseptic wipes).  
  - Reusable bags for calzones/cupcakes.  
- **Labeling**: Mark containers with "Hot," "Cold," or "Dessert" for quick access.  

---

### **KEY CONSIDERATIONS**  
- **Allergies**: Check labels for nuts/dairy/gluten (e.g., avoid trail mix if allergies exist).  
- **Temperature**: Pre-chill calzones/cupcakes overnight.  
- **Hydration**: Prioritize water! Soda is a treat, not a mainstay.  
- **Kid-Friendly**: Focus on fun foods (yogurt, fruit, mini sandwiches) to keep little ones energized.
