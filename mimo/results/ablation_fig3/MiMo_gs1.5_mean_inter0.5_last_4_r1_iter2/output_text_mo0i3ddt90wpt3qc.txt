Here are a few more gluten-free snack ideas perfect for camping and cycling:

1. **Nut Butter Packets**: Single-serve almond or peanut butter packets paired with gluten-free fruit (like banana slices or apples) for quick energy.
2. **Roasted Chickpeas**: Savory, crunchy, and protein-packed (look for certified GF brands).
3. **Trail Mix**: DIY or store-bought with nuts, seeds, and dried fruit—avoid malted or honey-coated varieties.
4. **Fruit Leather Bars**: Lightweight, chewy, and portable (ensure no gluten-containing additives).

**Pro Tips**:  
- Pack snacks in waterproof containers to prevent moisture damage.  
- Check labels for hidden gluten (e.g., malt in some dried fruit or soy sauce in savory snacks).  
- Freeze snacks in a cooler bag to extend freshness during long rides.
