Okay, so you've done all the prep work and are ready to prime. That's great. Before you apply the primer, just double-check that the surface is dust-free. A tack cloth is perfect for this. Also, make sure the primer you're using is designed for cabinets and can be applied directly over paint, since you're painting over another paint job. And finally, just do a quick check to make sure you don't have any drips or uneven spots on the sanded areas. Once that's all good, you're ready to prime.
