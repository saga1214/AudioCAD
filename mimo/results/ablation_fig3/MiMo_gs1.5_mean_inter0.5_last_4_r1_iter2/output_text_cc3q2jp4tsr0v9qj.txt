### 🛏️ **Priority #1: Set Up Beds & Sleeping Areas**

- **Water:** Turn on the main water valve if needed, and run faucets to clear air. Check for leaks.

### 🍽️ **Priority #3: Stock Essentials for Survival**
- **Kitchen:** Put away coffee makers, plates, glasses, and utensils. Stock up on snacks, coffee, and condiments.
- **Bathroom:** Unpack toilet paper, soap, towels, and toiletries.
- **Living Areas:** Set up couch cushions and blankets for immediate comfort.

- **Work with movers:** Direct them to place furniture in the right rooms first.
