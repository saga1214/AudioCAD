That's a great question! For quick, simple dinner ideas, I'd suggest focusing on meals that require minimal prep and cleanup. Here are a few easy options:

1. **One-Pan Roasted Protein & Veggies**: Toss chicken thighs, salmon fillets, or tofu with potatoes, broccoli, and bell peppers. Roast everything together on a single sheet pan at 400°F (200°C) for 20-30 minutes. Season with salt, pepper, and maybe some herbs.

2. **Speedy Stir-Fry**: Use pre-cut veggies (like bell peppers, snap peas, carrots) + protein (tofu, shrimp, or chicken). Stir-fry in a hot pan with soy sauce, garlic, and ginger. Serve over leftover rice or noodles.

3. **Sheet-Pan Sausages & Veggies**: Italian sausage links + cherry tomatoes, zucchini, and red onion. Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 20-25 minutes. Toss with olive oil and herbs.

4. **Breakfast-for-Dinner**: Scrambled eggs or tofu scramble with spinach, mushrooms, and toast. Takes <10 minutes!

5. **Canned-Centric Meals**: Canned tuna/chicken + avocado + whole-grain crackers. Or a quick soup (lentil, minestrone, or miso) heated with frozen veggies.

**Tips to keep it simple**:
- Use pre-cut veggies or frozen options
- Choose versatile proteins (eggs, tofu, canned fish)
- Repurpose leftovers creatively
- Keep pantry staples: spices, oil, vinegar, canned beans, grains
