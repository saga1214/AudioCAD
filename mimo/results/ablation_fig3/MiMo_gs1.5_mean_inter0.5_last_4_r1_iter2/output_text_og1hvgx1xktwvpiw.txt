Okay, let me clarify that for you. The standard Boulevardier recipe is one and a quarter ounces of whiskey, one ounce of sweet vermouth, and one ounce of Campari. The equal-parts version, which is also very classic, uses one ounce of each of those three ingredients. 

As for the Last Chance, that's an equal-parts drink too, but it's a different cocktail. It uses gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. Each of those is three-quarters of an ounce. So, to recap: Boulevardier is whiskey, vermouth, Campari, and the Last Chance is gin, Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime. Hope that helps you decide!
