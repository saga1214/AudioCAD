Yes, the 1940s and 50s are the true golden age of comics, and the scarcity is a huge factor in their value. Here are ten significant books from that era that you could look for. Just remember, the price for a high-grade copy will vary greatly.

First, there's *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven. It's the very first appearance of Batman. It's extremely rare in high-grade condition, but it's a cornerstone of the market.

Second, *Action Comics* number one, the first appearance of Superman. This is one of the most valuable comics ever sold, but lower-grade copies can still be found for under five thousand dollars.

Third, *Superboy* number one. It's the first appearance of Superman's alter ego, and it's an incredibly scarce book.

Fourth, *Whiz Comics* number one. This was the first comic to feature Captain America, and it's a major key issue.

Fifth, *Tales of the Vedettes* number one. This is the first appearance of the Green Lantern, who later became a key figure in the Justice League.

Sixth, *Adventure Comics* number 250. This is the first appearance of the Legion of Super-Heroes.

Seventh, *The Brave and the Bold* number twenty-eight. It's the first appearance of the Justice League of America.

Eighth, *All-Star Comics* number eight. This is the first appearance of the Silver Age Flash.

Ninth, *House of Secrets* number twenty-one. This is the first appearance of the Spectre.

And tenth, *Tales to Astonish* number forty-five. This is the first appearance of the Ant-Man.

These are all very significant books from that era. You can find them through major auction houses or reputable dealers.
