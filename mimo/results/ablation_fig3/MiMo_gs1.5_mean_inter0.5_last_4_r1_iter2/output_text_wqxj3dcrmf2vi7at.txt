Here's a comprehensive list of treats for your corgi princess, categorized for easy reference:

**Homemade Treats (Healthy & Customizable):**

1.  **Frozen Bites:** Mashed pumpkin, sweet potato, or blueberry puree spooned onto small silicone molds or ice cube trays, frozen solid. Great for teething and hydration.
2.  **Peanut Butter & Oat Cookies:**
    *   Mix 1 cup rolled oats with 1/4 cup natural peanut butter (NO XYLITOL!), 1 egg (optional for binding), and a splash of water or milk. Roll into small balls or flatten into mini cookies. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-15 minutes until firm. Cool completely.
3.  **Cooked Chicken or Turkey:** Plain, boiled or baked pieces (skinless, boneless). A classic favorite.
4.  **Cooked Egg:** A whole soft-boiled egg or a small piece of the yolk (for extra richness).
5.  **Yogurt (Plain, Full-Fat):** Small dollop of plain, full-fat Greek yogurt (unsweetened, no added sugar or fruit). Contains probiotics.
6.  **Banana Bites:** Small pieces of ripe banana.
7.  **Apple Slices:** Very small, thin slices of fresh apple (core removed, seeds avoided).
8.  **Blueberry or Rasp Berry Bites:** Frozen berries rolled in a tiny bit of oats or coconut (optional) or just given whole (ensure they are small enough to prevent choking).
9.  **Carrot Sticks:** Small, finely grated or thinly sliced raw carrot.

**Store-Bought Treats (Convenience & Variety):**

1.  **Training Treats:** Tiny, soft, easily swallowed kibble (like the kind from their own brand, often sold as "training kibble").
2.  **Peanut Butter Treats:** Look for brands specifically formulated for dogs (e.g., Zuke's Mini Zukes - Peanut Butter & Oat, Hol-Can Peanut Butter Pouches -撕开小口喂食).
3.  **Other Pouches:** Chicken, salmon, or beef flavored dog food pouches (ensure they are dog-specific and not too rich).
4.  **Kibble Treats:** Mini versions of their regular kibble, often shaped like bones or hearts.
5.  **Fruit or Veggie Sticks:** Look for brand-specific sticks like Blue Buffalo's "Homestyle Bits" (Pumpkin, Apple, Blueberry) or Wellness's "Natural Balance" sticks.
6.  **Toys that Give Treats:** Kongs stuffed with peanut butter, yogurt, or wet food and frozen. Zippy Chews filled with treats.
7.  **Training Puzzles:** Interactive toys where she has to work to get a small treat (like a snuffle mat with kibble or a puzzle ball with a treat inside).

**Healthy Snack Options (Often Used as Treats):**

*   **Plain Steamed Vegetables:** Broccoli florets, green beans, zucchini slices.
*   **Plain Cooked Pasta:** Small pieces.
*   **Plain Cooked Brown Rice:** Small pieces.

**Important Safety Reminders:**

1.  **NO XYLITOL:** Absolutely no treats containing Xylitol (found in some gums, candies, sugar-free products). It's deadly to dogs.
2.  **Portion Control:** Treats should only make up a small percentage (1-3%) of her daily calorie intake. Measure homemade treats.
3.  **Choking Hazards:** Avoid large, hard, or round treats. Cut larger pieces into small, bite-sized chunks. Never give whole bones.
4.  **Supervision:** Always supervise when she's eating treats, especially new ones.
5.  **Allergies:** Introduce new treats one at a time and watch for any adverse reactions (vomiting, diarrhea, itching).
6.  **Freshness:** Store homemade treats properly (frozen is best). Discard any treats that spoil.

Choose treats based on Princess's preferences, dietary needs, and training goals! Enjoy rewarding your corgi princess!
