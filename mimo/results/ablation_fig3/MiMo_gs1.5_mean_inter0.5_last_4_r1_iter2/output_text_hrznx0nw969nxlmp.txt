That's an excellent choice! Secret Harbour Beach Resort is truly special. Here are some fantastic **daytime activities** right from that area, focusing on accessibility and beautiful settings:

1.  **Snorkeling at Trunk Bay (St. John):**
    *   **Why it's perfect:** This is arguably the *best* snorkeling spot near Secret Harbour. The underwater observatory offers easy access to incredible coral gardens teeming with fish, rays, and even turtles. The water is exceptionally calm and clear. It's a must-do.
    *   **Getting there:** Take the Trunk Bay Trail (easy, paved, flat) from Cruz Bay. It's a short walk.

2.  **Snorkeling at Coki Beach (St. John):**
    *   **Why it's perfect:** Just a short drive or taxi from Secret Harbour. Known for its calm, shallow, turquoise waters perfect for beginners and families. Great for spotting colorful fish and sea turtles. The beach bar offers refreshments.
    *   **Bonus:** Easy access, shallow entry, and a laid-back vibe.

3.  **Relaxing at Cruz Bay Beach:**
    *   **Why it's perfect:** Right next door! The main public beach in Cruz Bay is beautiful, usually calm, and offers a great local atmosphere. Perfect for swimming, sunbathing, reading, or just soaking up the Caribbean vibe. Grab a drink from a nearby beach bar.

4.  **Kayaking or Paddleboarding at Secret Harbour:**
    *   **Why it's perfect:** Since you're staying right there, you can easily rent kayaks or paddleboards for a peaceful exploration of the calm, protected bay. Paddle out to the little island (Cabbage Bay) or just circle the point. Great exercise and a unique perspective.

5.  **Hiking at Cinnamon Bay (St. John):**
    *   **Why it's perfect:** While a bit further (15-20 mins drive), the trail to Cinnamon Bay is beautiful and well-maintained. The hike leads down to one of the island's most stunning beaches – dramatic, white sand, deep blue water, and often fewer people than Trunk Bay. Snorkeling here is also fantastic. The views from the top are incredible.
    *   **Tip:** Go early to avoid crowds, especially on weekends.

6.  **Exploring Red Hook (St. Thomas - if you decide to switch islands):**
    *   **Why it's perfect:** You mentioned liking the Red Hook vibe! If you're on St. Thomas, Secret Harbour is actually quite close to Red Hook (about 20-25 mins drive). You can easily visit for lunch, explore the shops, browse the art galleries, and enjoy the lively atmosphere. It's a great way to experience both sides of the island.

**Key things to remember for your brother:**

*   **Avoid Rapid Ascent/Descent:** Stick to snorkeling spots with gentle, shallow entry points. Avoid any boat trips that involve significant diving or rapid changes in depth/speed.
*   **Stay Hydrated:** Pressure changes can happen with temperature changes too. Drink plenty of water.
*   **Listen to His Body:** If he feels any discomfort, take a break. Calm swimming or beach lounging is always an option.
