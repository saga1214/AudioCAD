That's a great set of questions—let's break it down:

### 1. **Color-Coding for Icons/Folders**  
   - **Memory vs. Load Reduction**: Color *primarily* aids **memory** by creating visual anchors (e.g., red for urgent tasks, blue for projects). However, it also **reduces cognitive load** by allowing your brain to "chunk" information visually.  
   - **Best Practice**: Use **2-3 colors max** and stick to consistent themes (e.g., green = completed, orange = active, blue = archived). Avoid overly busy palettes—simplicity wins.  

### 2. **Switching Between Lo-Fi & Ambient Sounds**  
   - **Optimal Timing**: Try **every 45-60 minutes**. Lo-fi’s rhythmic beats can sustain focus during deep work, while ambient sounds (e.g., rain, forest) help reset your brain during lighter tasks or transitions.  
   - **Personal Preference > Rules**: If you notice focus waning, switch. If ambient sounds make you drowsy, stick to lo-fi. Track your energy for a week to find your rhythm.  

### 3. **Physical Document Filing**  
   - **Environment vs. Chunking**: Both matter!  
     - **Environment**: A clean, well-organized workspace reduces friction.  
     - **Chunking**: Break tasks into 30-60 minute blocks. Tackle 1-2 "batches" (e.g., "Invoices" or "Reference Files") to avoid overwhelm.  
   - **Tip**: Use a "inbox" tray for unsorted papers—process it daily.  

### 4. **Short Break Timers (Pomodoro)**  
   - **Science-Backed**: Studies show **25-30 minute focused work + 5-minute breaks** improves retention and reduces burnout.  
   - **Adjust as Needed**: If 25 mins feels too short, try **50/10**. For deep work, **90-minute cycles** (with longer breaks) align better with natural attention spans.
