### 🎃 Top Halloween Candy Picks
1. **Classic Favorites**  
   - **Snickers, Twix, Reese's** (everyone loves the combo)  
   - **Milky Way or 3 Musketeers** (light, nostalgic)  
   - **Sour Patch Kids** (surprise texture)  

2. **Kid-Friendly & Safe**  
   - **Mini Chocolate Bars** (Milky Way Mini, Snickers Mini)  
   - **Pretzels & Candy Combos** (sweet + salty)  
   - **Gummies** (Starbursts, Fruit Gummies)  

3. **Budget-Friendly & Low-Waste**  
   - **Peanut Butter Cups** (Reese's) or **Cracker Jacks**  
   - **Chocolate-Covered Almonds** (elegant but affordable)  
   - **Dots or Tootsie Rolls** (universal appeal)  

### 🛒 Shopping Tips
- **Avoid niche candies** (e.g., specific anime-themed sweets) – stick to recognizable brands.  
- **Buy in bulk** at Costco, Dollar Tree, or Walmart for better value.  
- **Check expiration dates** – Halloween candy often sits for weeks.  
- **Prevent waste**: Buy just enough for 20-30 guests (most kids take 1-2 pieces).  

### 🎨 Quick Decoration Boosters
- **Add "spooky lights"**: String lights + orange glow tape on walls/doors.  
- **DIY fog effect**: Place a bowl of water with floating candles near decorations to amplify shadows.  
- **Pathway markers**: Use glow sticks or battery-operated lanterns along walkways.  

### 🍕 Food Final Tips
- **Pizza hack**: Order *two* pizzas (one plain, one loaded) to avoid repeats.  
- **Allergy-safe**: Set aside a table with allergy-friendly snacks (veggie sticks, fruit, popcorn) clearly labeled.  
- **Ice hack**: Freeze water bottles in your fog cooler – use them as drink chillers while fogging!  

**Pro tip**: Turn your fog cooler into a "boo-bowl" for candy! Place the candy box *inside* the fogger's output path – guests will see it swirling dramatically when they grab treats. 🕷️  

You’ve got this! Last-minute Halloween parties are all about fun energy, not perfection. Focus on lighting, music, and easy snacks – your effort will show. 🎃👻
