Okay, this is a fun shift from fiction to real life! Since you love the ocean and seafood, here are a few creative ideas for your getaway.

You could book a private charter for a sunset sail. Many places offer special packages where a captain will prepare a simple, delicious meal on board just for you.

Another idea is a kayak tour that ends with a picnic on a secluded beach. You could pack a cooler with local seafood sandwiches and drinks.

For something really special, look into an overnight stay on a luxury houseboat. Many marinas offer these, and they often come with full kitchens so you can cook fresh seafood right there on the water. Just make sure to check sea-sickness policies if that's a concern for your partner.
