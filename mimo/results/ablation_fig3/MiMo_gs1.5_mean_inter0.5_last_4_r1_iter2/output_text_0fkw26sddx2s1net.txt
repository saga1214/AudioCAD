Here’s a detailed shopping list for your 3-course Asian-inspired meal, tailored to your husband’s allergies and preferences:

---

### **Starter: Cucumber Salad with Sesame Dressing**  
*(Light, refreshing, legume-free)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1 English cucumber, sliced  
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar  
- 1 tsp sugar  
- 1 tsp soy sauce (check for legume-free brands)  
- ½ tsp sesame oil  
- 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds  
- Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional)  

---

### **Main Course: Chicken Teriyaki with Steamed Jasmine Rice & Stir-Fried Veggies**  
*(Classic, legume-free, no seafood)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts  
- ¼ cup soy sauce (legume-free)  
- ¼ cup mirin  
- 2 tbsp sake or dry sherry  
- 1 tbsp sugar  
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced  
- 2 cups mixed stir-fry veggies (e.g., bell peppers, snap peas, carrots, broccoli)  
- 2 tbsp cooking oil (neutral, like avocado oil)  
- 1 tbsp sesame oil  
- 1 tsp chili paste (optional)  

**For the rice:**  
- 1 cup jasmine rice  
- 1¾ cups water  
- 1 tsp salt  

---

### **Dessert: Mango Sticky Rice**  
*(Sweet, creamy, no legumes)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 3 ripe mangoes, sliced  
- 1 cup glutinous rice (sticky rice), soaked 4+ hours  
- 1½ cups coconut milk  
- 2 tbsp sugar  
- 1 tsp vanilla extract  
- 1 tbsp red bean paste (optional, for drizzling)  

**For the matcha ice cream alternative (simple version):**  
- 1½ cups heavy cream  
- 1 cup sugar  
- 2 tbsp matcha powder  
- 1 tsp vanilla extract  

---

### **Condiments & Seasonings**  
- Soy sauce (legume-free)  
- Rice vinegar  
- Toasted sesame oil  
- Mirin  
- Sugar  
- Salt  
- Chili paste (optional)  

---

### **Pantry Staples**  
- Cooking oil  
- Garlic  
- Ginger  
- Red pepper flakes  

---

### **Prep Notes**  
1. **Steamed rice**: Rinse rice, combine with water and salt. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered for 15 mins. Turn off heat and let sit 10 mins.  
2. **Cucumber salad**: Whisk vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, sesame oil, and pepper flakes. Toss with cucumber slices.  
3. **Stir-fried veggies**: Sauté garlic/ginger, add veggies, then soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, and chili paste. Finish with sesame oil.  
4. **Mango sticky rice**: Steam rice after soaking. Simmer coconut milk, sugar, and vanilla. Fold in cooked rice, then top with mango and optional red bean paste.  
5. **Matcha ice cream**: Whisk cream, sugar, and matcha until smooth. Pour into a bowl, cover, and freeze (stir every 30 mins for 4+ hours).  

---

### **Time-Saving Tips**  
- Use store-bought teriyaki sauce (check labels for legumes).  
- Swap veggies based on what’s fresh (e.g., bok choy, zucchini).  
- For faster dessert, buy pre-made mango sticky rice kits or skip the rice and serve mango with vanilla ice cream.
