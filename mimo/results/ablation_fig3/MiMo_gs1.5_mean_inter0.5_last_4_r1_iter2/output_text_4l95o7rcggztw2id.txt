Here’s a **pirate-themed, sand-friendly shopping list** for your nautical birthday party, designed for easy handling at the beach while incorporating fun themes and avoiding watermelon:

### **Appetizers (Pre-Sand Safety & Theme)**

2. **"Jewel" Fruit Skewers**

3. **"Pirate’s Plunder" Crackers & Cheese**  
   - Goldfish crackers  
   - Cheese cubes or sticks (mozzarella, cheddar)  
   - *Packaging:* Serve in a **mini treasure chest bin** with individual wax paper cones.  

---

### **Main Course (Easy to Eat, Minimal Mess)**  
4. **Mini Pizza "Ships"**  
   - Mini pre-made pizzas (or make your own)  
   - *Theme Tip:* Use **blueberry toppings** for "sea waves" and **chocolate chips** for "sails."  

5. **"Mermaid’s Platter"**  
   - Fish-shaped crackers (like Goldfish)  
   - Cheese cubes  
   - *Packaging:* Serve in a **decorated treasure chest** with a lid.  

---

### **Desserts & Drinks (Sand-Free & Themed)**  
6. **"Dugong” Cupcakes**  
   - Cupcakes (use nautical-themed liners)  
   - *Theme Tip:* Top with **blue frosting**, **gold sprinkles**, and **edible gold leaf**.  

7. **"Pirate Punch" Juice Boxes**  
   - Individual juice boxes (apple, orange, or berry)  
   - *Theme Tip:* Label as **"Pirate Punch"** or **"Sea Water"** with a **pirate flag sticker**.  

8. **"Shipwreck" Cookies**  
   - Chocolate chip cookies cut into **ship shapes**  
   - *Packaging:* Serve in **brown paper bags** with a **torn sail design**.  

9. **Gummy Sharks**  
   - Blue or green gummy sharks (from bulk stores)  
   - *Packaging:* In a **clear jar** labeled "Gummy Shark Fleet!"

### **Allergy-Safe Notes**
