For a brooder plate, the best and cheapest bedding is simply pine pellets. They are great at absorbing moisture and are very easy to clean up. For a little extra warmth, you can also sprinkle a thin layer of cornmeal on top of the pellets. It provides a bit of extra insulation and also makes for a good dust bath. Just make sure you avoid using straw, as it can get damp and cold, and definitely don't use wood shavings, as they can be irritating to the chicks' delicate skin.
