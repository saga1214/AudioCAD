Okay, let's see. You'll need a smoker for the turkey and the prime rib, an oven for the turkey, mashed potatoes, mac and cheese, root veggies, stuffing, cranberry sauce, and the pumpkin cheesecake. You'll also need a toaster oven for the asparagus, an Instant Pot for the cranberry sauce and the wild rice salad, and a slow cooker for the apple crisp. As for methods, you'll be doing smoking, roasting, baking, boiling, using a slow cooker, and an Instant Pot. You might also need to fry the mac and cheese, and use a blender for the tart. Oh, and don't forget the no-bake cheesecake and the icebox cake that don't need any cooking at all.
