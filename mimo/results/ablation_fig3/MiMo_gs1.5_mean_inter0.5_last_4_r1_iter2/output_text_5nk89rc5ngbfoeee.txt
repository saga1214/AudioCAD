### **Venue & Format Ideas**
1. **Hybrid Approach**:

   - *Why?* This covers all bases—ensures a backup plan while keeping the park for active fun.

2. **Food Strategy**:  
   - **Group Meal + Snack Bar**:  
     - Parents cover a *small group meal* (e.g., pizza/sundaes) to celebrate.  
     - Set up a *snack bar* with options like chips/dip (your favorite!), fruit, and drinks.  
   - **Inclusivity Tip**: Label snacks clearly (e.g., "dairy-free chips/dip" for you).  
   - *Why?* Parents contribute without heavy costs, and kids get choices.

### **Activities to Include**
- **Races & Games**:  
  - Three-legged races, sack races, or relay races (park ideal).  
  - Add calmer options like trivia or badge-showing contests if indoors.  
- **Trophies & Certificates**:  
  - Present badges/sashes in a short ceremony (10–15 mins) to honor achievements.  
- **Ice Cream Bar**:  
  - A hit with kids! Let them customize sundaes (offer dairy-free toppings if needed).  

### **Cost & Logistics**

  - Celebration meal: **Small optional fee per parent** ($5–$10) for pizza/sundaes.  
  - Snacks/activities: **Parent volunteer rotation** (assign 1 parent per meeting).  
- **Pregnancy Note**:  
  - Delegate setup/cleanup to parents—focus on leading activities.
