Okay, so you’re planning to film your first official performance for a YouTube channel. Here’s a quick checklist to make it smooth:

First, get permission from the facility, like Sunnyside. Even if it’s a volunteer gig, you need to confirm they’re okay with filming. 

Next, ask an audience member to help you film. Make sure they know where to aim, like the puppet and your expressions, not just a shaky phone. 

Then, you should test your phone’s camera settings beforehand. See if it can record for long periods and check the lighting. Maybe use a soft light if possible. 

After that, plan the performance. Pick a shorter, high-energy show—like five to ten minutes—and rehearse it a few times so it feels natural. 

Also, think about the ending. Maybe add a call to action, like, “Hey, subscribe if you want more puppet shows!” or something simple. 

And finally, just be yourself. The audience is kids, and they’ll love the energy. 

That’s it! Keep it fun and simple. You’ve got this.
