Here's how to make sure your stir-fry noodles turn out great and avoid another smoke alarm incident:

### Key Fixes for Perfect Noodles:
1. **Utensil Swap:**  
   → **Always use metal or heatproof plastic spoons** for boiling. Wood conducts heat and creates explosive steam bursts.  
   → *Bonus:* Use tongs or a spider strainer to toss noodles gently.

2. **Water Prep:**  
   → Salt the water generously (like pasta) for flavor.  
   → Add noodles to boiling water, stir once, then **cover the pot** to cook evenly and prevent sticking.

3. **Draining & Timing:**  
   → Drain noodles **immediately** after they’re done (over a colander).  
   → Toss them in your stir-fry pan *right away*—no sitting in a colander to cool.

4. **Stir-Fry Tip:**  
   → Cook meat first, then drain excess fat. Add noodles to the pan with a splash of water or broth, cover for 1-2 minutes to steam. Uncover and stir-fry until heated through.
