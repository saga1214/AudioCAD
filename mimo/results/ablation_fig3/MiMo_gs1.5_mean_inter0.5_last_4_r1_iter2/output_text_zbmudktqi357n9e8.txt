Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice! For autumn colors, I'd say late September through November is perfect. You'll see those amazing fall foliage colors in the mountains, especially around the Los Tuxtlas or Sierra Madre ranges. The coastal areas will have their own vibrant colors, but the mountains are where you'll really see that classic autumn palette.

And for space scenes, Mexico has some incredible spots. You could head to the Yucatan Peninsula for those amazing starry skies—Chichén Itzá at night is just magical. Or, if you're up for a drive, the Baja Peninsula near Cabo has some of the clearest skies in the world. The desert landscapes there would make for some really striking space paintings.

So yeah, I'd definitely recommend going in October or November. The weather is perfect for painting, and you'll get both the autumn colors and those incredible night skies to inspire you. Have a great time!
