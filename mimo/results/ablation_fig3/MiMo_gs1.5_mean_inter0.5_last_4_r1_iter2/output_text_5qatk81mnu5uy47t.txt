### **Top Dive Locations in Costa Rica**
1. **Puntarenas (Pacific Coast)**  
   - **Why**: Tropical waters, vibrant coral reefs, and abundant marine life.  
   - **Highlights**:  
     - **Cabo Blanco**: Whale sharks (seasonal), sea turtles, reef sharks.  
     - **Cabo Blanco Marine Park**: Coral gardens, rays, and schools of fish.  
     - **Humpback Whales**: December–April (December–March is prime).  
   - **Gear Tip**: Short fins ideal for easy navigation in calm waters.  

2. **Tortuguero (Caribbean Coast)**  
   - **Why**: Mangrove ecosystems, diverse marine life, and eco-friendly diving.  
   - **Highlights**:  
     - **Sea Turtles**: Nesting turtles (July–October), often seen at dawn.  
     - **Manta Rays**: Year-round, especially near the Caño Island Marine Park.  
     - **Mangrove Dives**: Unique underwater forests teeming with fish.  
   - **Gear Tip**: Short fins for tight channels; long fins optional for longer trips.  

3. **Sirena (Ostional)**  
   - **Why**: Dramatic lava formations, steep walls, and dramatic dives.  
   - **Highlights**:  
     - **Lava Caves & Walls**: Thrilling for experienced divers.  
     - **Whale Sharks & Dolphins**: Year-round.  
   - **Gear Tip**: Long fins recommended for currents and longer swims.  

4. **Santa Teresa (Pacific Coast)**  
   - **Why**: Relaxed vibe, bioluminescent bays, and nearby coves.  
   - **Highlights**:  
     - **Bioluminescent Pools**: Magical kayaking/diving at night (seasonal).  
     - **Coral Reefs**: Close to Cabo Blanco Marine Park.
