### **Key Tips for Success**
1. **Start Small**:  
   - On beanbag days, do 5 minutes of two-ball drills, then 10 minutes of 2→3 throws.  
   - On scarf days, focus on **timing**—not drops. Aim for a steady "swish-swish" rhythm.

2. **Mindset Shift**:  
   - If you drop balls, **pause and reset**—don’t force it. Juggling is 90% mental!  
   - Celebrate "one-minute successes": If you juggle for 60 seconds straight, that’s a win.

3. **Scarves First, Then Beanbags**:  
   On scarf days, practice patterns like:  
   - **"Pirouette"**: One hand spins a scarf down, other hand catches.  
   - **"Figure-8"**: Let scarves loop overhead while you step around.  
   This builds muscle memory without pressure.

4. **Tennis Ball Hack**:  
   If you use tennis balls, hold them in your *non-dominant* hand first. Their weight helps you feel the arc of throws.

5. **Stress-Buster**:  
   - Breathe deeply before practicing.  
   - Practice with **music** (slow-tempo songs help timing).  
   - If panicking, switch to scarves for 2 minutes to reset.

---

### **Why This Works**
- **Beanbags First**: You’re building strength and pattern recognition.  
- **Scarves as "Safe Mode"**: They’re forgiving and let you focus on rhythm.  
- **Tennis Balls for Precision**: Their bounce teaches your hands to track objects.  
- **Rest is Crucial**: Wrist tendons need recovery—skip a day to avoid injury.
