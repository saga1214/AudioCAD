Ah... a chocolate pan dessert... for Thanksgiving. Yeah... I remember her chocolate pudding. She always made it so well. It was always so smooth and rich. You know... you could make a chocolate pudding in a pan. Just melt some chocolate with a little warm milk, then whisk in some flour and sugar. Pour it into the pan and bake it until it's firm. It's simple, comforting... like her desserts were. Everyone would probably enjoy it. Just... maybe don't serve brownies this year. Or peach cobbler. That might be too hard.
