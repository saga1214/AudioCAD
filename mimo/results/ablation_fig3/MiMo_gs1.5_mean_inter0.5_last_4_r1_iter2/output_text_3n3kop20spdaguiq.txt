For berries, you can make them into chips or a powder, which is great for smoothies or oatmeal. You can also dehydrate grapes to make them sweet and chewy, or mangoes to make sweet, tropical chips. 

Next, for vegetables, you can make apple slices, which are crunchy and great for salads or as a snack. You can also make carrot sticks, which are a crunchy and healthy alternative to potato chips. Celery sticks are another great option, as they're a healthy and refreshing snack. You can also dehydrate zucchini into chips or slices, which are great for adding to salads or as a snack. And of course, you can make onion rings, which are a healthier alternative to fried onion rings. 

You can also dehydrate herbs like basil, mint, parsley, or thyme to make them into a powder, which is great for adding to soups or sauces. You can make tea bags out of dried herbs like chamomile or peppermint. 

For other snacks, you can make jerky from beef, turkey, or venison, which is a great protein source. You can also make fruit leather from mashed fruit, which is a healthy and fun snack. And finally, you can make granola bars by combining oats, nuts, seeds, and dried fruit.
