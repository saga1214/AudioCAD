**Cooking Advice:**

1.  **Wooden Spoon Ban永久有效！** Seriously, leave that thing behind forever. Pasta water + wood equals alarm. Use metal or sturdy plastic spoons. Lesson learned, move on.
2.  **Stir-fry Noodle Success Formula:**
    *   **Boil Smart:** Get a *big* pot of water boiling *very* hard. Salt it generously. Add noodles *only* when the water is screaming and rolling. Stir them gently once or twice while cooking to prevent sticking. **Crucial:** Cook them *al dente* – a minute less than package directions say. They'll finish cooking later.
    *   **Shock & Drain:** As soon as they're done cooking, drain them *immediately*. Pour a little cold water over the drained noodles and give them a quick toss. This stops them from sticking together and makes them silky. Drain *very* well. Wet noodles = soup, not stir-fry.
    *   **The Stir-Fry Assembly Line:**
        1.  **Wok/Big Pan:** Get it screaming hot over medium-high heat. Add a *generous* amount of high-smoke-point oil (like canola or vegetable).
        2.  **Protein First:** Add your ground meat (or chicken, shrimp, etc.). Break it up and cook until browned and mostly done. Push it to the side.
        3.  **Veggies:** Add harder veggies (like bell peppers, onions, broccoli florets) and stir-fry until slightly softened but still crisp. Then add softer veggies (like spinach, carrots) and stir-fry just until they wilt.
        4.  **Sauce Up:** Push veggies/protein to the sides. Add your taco seasoning packet (or soy sauce, ginger, garlic, etc.) mixed with a splash of water or broth to create a sauce. Stir *everything* together until the sauce coats everything nicely. This happens fast!
        5.  **Noodles Join the Party:** Add your shock-cooled, well-drained noodles. Toss everything together vigorously for the last minute or two of cooking. The residual heat will finish the noodles perfectly. Add a squeeze of lime or lemon juice at the very end for brightness.
        6.  **Serve Immediately!** Stir-fry waits for no one. If it sits, it gets soggy.
