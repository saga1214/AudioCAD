Okay, you've done the hard part, which is removing the hardware and sanding. Now, before you prime, you need to protect your countertops and floor. Cover them with painter's tape and plastic sheeting.

Also, make sure your work area is well-ventilated. You'll want to open some windows or use fans to help with that.

And finally, just give all the surfaces one last good cleaning to make sure no dust gets in the primer. After that, you're ready to prime.
