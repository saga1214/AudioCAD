Initiate the conversation during a formal performance review meeting is often the best approach because it's a structured conversation where the topic is already expected. As a backup, you could schedule a separate, dedicated one-on-one meeting with your boss specifically to discuss career development.

To manage anxiety, first, try to reframe the meeting as a collaborative discussion rather than a confrontation. Second, prepare some specific examples of your achievements and the value you bring to the company. This preparation can significantly boost your confidence.
