For an end-of-year celebration, a **park picnic** is a fantastic choice! It’s budget-friendly, encourages teamwork, and fits your daughters’ snack preferences (ice cream sundaes, pizza, chips/dip adapted for your needs). Here’s how to make it work:

1. **Location & Setup**:  
   - **Weather Plan**: Book a rain date (e.g., school cafeteria) early. Use tents/tables if outdoors.  
   - **Parent Roles**: Assign 2-3 parents to help with setup/cleanup. Keep it simple—no heavy lifting for you!  

2. **Activities**:  
   - **Classic Races**: Three-legged races, sack races, or relay races (low-cost and high-energy).  
   - **Decorate Your Treat**: Set up a station where girls design their own pizzas/sundaes.  
   - **Badge Showcase**: Have girls proudly display patches/sashes for photos.  

3. **Food Strategy**:  
   - **Mix-and-Match**: Offer pizza, veggie wraps (dairy-free chips), and sundaes.  
   - **Parent Potluck**: Ask parents to bring 1–2 dishes (e.g., dessert or side). Reduces your burden.  
   - **Your Dairy-Free Option**: Pre-make a dairy-free dip or fruit skewers.  

4. **Budget & Dues**:  
   - **Dues**: Charge only for *essential* supplies (e.g., $10/daughter for badges/camping gear).  
   - **Parent Contributions**: Keep dues transparent—list what funds cover (e.g., "50% for patches, 50% for field trips").  

5. **Campfire Twist**:  
   - If camping, opt for an **overnight at a local campsite** (not a full wilderness trip). Use a campsite with facilities—focus on s’mores, stargazing, and badge completion.  

**Pro Tip**: Start with a **mini "trophy" ceremony** at the picnic—awards for "Best Helper," "Most Improved," etc. It’s heartfelt and low-cost. You’ve got this—your experience as a Brownie growing up and your daughters’ passion will make this year memorable! 🍦✨
