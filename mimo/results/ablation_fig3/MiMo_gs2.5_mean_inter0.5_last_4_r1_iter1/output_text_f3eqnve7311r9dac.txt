Yes, the market for early comics is very active precisely because of their rarity.

Three recently transacted comics that sold for millions of dollars are:
First, nearly perfect copies of *Action Comics* number one, where Superman debuted, sold in 2024 for six million dollars.
Second, *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen, Spider-Man's first appearance, sold in 2021 for three point six million.
And third, *Captain America Comics* number one from 1941 sold at auction in 2022 for just over three million dollars.
