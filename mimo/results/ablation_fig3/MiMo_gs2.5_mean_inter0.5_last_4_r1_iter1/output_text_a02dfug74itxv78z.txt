Alright, based on your ninety-thousand-dollar under budget, American preference, desire for new features CarPlay, last five years old, sports or competitive feel, colorful paint not drawing police attention request, and midlife crisis power need, here are 5 high-performance American options:

1.  **Chevrolet Corvette (C8 Z06 or ZR1):** The ultimate value king. Raw, supercar-stopping power (600+ HP in ZR1), stunning looks, cutting-edge tech (including full CarPlay), and track-capable. Under 90k is easy for base models or well-equipped Z06s. Get it in a neutral tone like Corvette Bronze or Lime Green for less radar attention.
2.  **Ford Mustang Shelby GT500:** Pure American muscle with supercar pretensions. 660 HP Shelby engine, massive V8 roar, incredible acceleration, and full modern tech. A bit newer looking inside than the Corvette. Neutral tones help blend in.
3.  **Shelby American Muscle Car (e.g., Shelby GT350 Mustang or Shelby GT500):** If you lean towards muscle, the Shelby variants push them into competitive territory against supercars. The GT350 has a unique flat-plane crank V8 and F1-inspired handling; the GT500 has brute force. Both under 90k. Neutral tones recommended.
4.  **Shelby American Supercar (e.g., Shelby GT350/500 or Shelby American GT):** For a more traditional supercar look, consider models like the Shelby GT350/500 Mustang or the newer Shelby American GT. These offer supercar aesthetics, performance, and tech within budget. Neutral tones are wise. The Shelby GT350 is a standout.
5.  **Shelby American Track Car (e.g., Shelby GT3 or Shelby American GT3):** If track days are appealing, Shelby offers dedicated track-focused cars. The GT3 variants (like Porsche 911 GT3 or Ferrari 488 GT3) offer supercar performance and handling, often used as daily drivers too. Tech is present. Neutral tones essential. Budget might be tight at the very top end, but well within 90k for many examples.

Get it in a neutral tone to minimize unnecessary police stops. Enjoy the youthful feeling!
