Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's ten calzones! I need to make a shopping list that covers drinks, snacks, main food, and dessert, focusing on healthy options but also including things like sugar-free sodas and calzones everyone can enjoy mess-free. Let me think through this step by step.

First, drinks. Everyone needs water, so plan for 30 reusable water bottles—one for each person. For the moms' break at their bachelorette party later, you might want to leave a few extras for them. Healthy choices include low-sugar juice boxes for the kids; you can get single-serve packs of apple or orange juice. Milk in boxes is another kid favorite. For the adults, water is healthy, but you agreed on sugar-free sodas. Grab a couple of 12-packs of diet cola, lemonade, or root beer, whichever the dads prefer. Sparkling water with a squeeze of lemon or lime could be a nice low-sugar alternative for some. And don't forget electrolyte drinks in single-serve packets; they're great for a hot day. So for drinks, I'm thinking: 30 water bottles, 24 juice boxes, 24 milk boxes, two 12-packs of sugar-free soda, six electrolyte drink packets, and maybe 12 lemon-lime sparkling waters. That should cover everyone.

Next, snacks. The goal is minimal mess, so focus on finger foods. Cheese sticks are a hit with both kids and adults. Grapes are super easy—washed and ready to grab. Individual pretzels or trail mix bags are perfect for a crunchy snack without a mess. Baby carrots with single-serving cups of hummus are another great combo. You could also add whole-grain crackers or rice cakes for a bit more substance. So for snacks: 30 cheese sticks, three pounds of grapes, 30 individual pretzels or trail mix bags, six single-serve hummus cups, and maybe 30 crackers or rice cakes. That's a good mix.

For the main meal, calzones are the star! Since it's the groom's favorite, this is key. You have a couple of ideas. Option one is a make-your-own station at home before you leave—that could be fun but might be a hassle at the park. Option two, which I think is better for mobility, is to pre-order from a pizza place. Many places make individual calzones, often called "mini calzones" or "stuffed pizza bites." You can customize them with cheese, pepperoni, or veggie fillings. Just call ahead, order ten of them, and they'll be ready to grab straight from the cooler. This keeps things super clean. As a backup, you could also get some wraps or sandwiches, but since calzones are the priority, I'd focus on that. So for food: ten pre-ordered mini calzones, and maybe 10-12 pre-made turkey and cheese wraps for a lighter option. I'll note that the wraps are optional.

Finally, dessert. You were debating cake versus pie. A whole cake would need a lot of serving stuff, and a whole pie could be messy to cut. Cupcakes are the way to go here. They're portable, no utensils needed, and feel festive. Bake or buy 12 cupcakes with frosting, and you could even add a little number "1" on top for the bachelor. A fruit cup in a single-serve pouch could be a lighter, healthy alternative for some. So for dessert: 12 cupcakes and maybe six individual fruit cups.

Now, let's put this all together for your cooler plan. Pack drinks in one cooler, snacks in another, the calzones and wraps in a third, and the cupcakes in a fourth. Label them, and everyone can just grab what they need. This should make for a fun, stress-free day. Enjoy the party!
