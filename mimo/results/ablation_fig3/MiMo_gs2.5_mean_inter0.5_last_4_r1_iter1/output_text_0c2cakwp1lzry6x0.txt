Here's a step-by-step guide for installing each item, keeping your landlord's rules in mind:

### 1. TV Mount Installation  
**Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, drill, wall anchors (if not in studs), screwdriver  
**Steps:**  
- **Locate studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (wood framing). If no studs, use heavy-duty drywall anchors.  
- **Measure & mark:** Measure distance from wall edge to TV center. Mark TV center point with a pencil.  
- **Attach mount:** Align mount bracket with TV mounting holes. Use level to ensure it’s straight.  
- **Secure:** Drill pilot holes (if using anchors), then screw mount to wall. Test stability before mounting TV.  
**Tip:** Use a laser level for precision. Check mount weight capacity matches your TV.  

### 2. Hanging Planters  
**Tools Needed:** Wall anchors (for drywall), drill, hook/rod, wire  
**Steps:**  
- **Choose placement:** Install near windows or corners.  
- **Mark hooks/rod:** Measure from floor to desired height. Mark spots with a level.  
- **Install anchors:** Drill holes, insert anchors, then screw in hooks or a rod.  
- **Hang planters:** Use thick wire or hooks rated for 5-10kg (per planter).  
**Tip:** Use adhesive hooks for lightweight plants (test weight first!).  

### 3. Floating Shelves  
**Tools Needed:** Wall anchors, level, drill, brackets  
**Steps:**  
- **Measure spacing:** Space shelves 24-36" apart. Mark positions with a pencil.  
- **Attach brackets:** Drill holes at shelf edges. Secure brackets to wall with anchors.  
- **Place shelf:** Rest shelf on brackets. Ensure it’s level.  
- **Secure:** Tighten screws. Add decorative brackets if desired.  
**Tip:** Use L-brackets for extra stability. Avoid overloading shelves (max 5-7kg each).  

### Safety & Compliance Tips:  
- **Check stud depth:** Use a stud finder with depth gauge to ensure anchors reach solid wood.  
- **Landlord rules:** Avoid drilling near electrical outlets or pipes. If unsure, use removable adhesive hooks for lightweight decor.  
- **Workspace setup:** Use a step stool for high shelves. Wear safety goggles while drilling.  
- **Post-installation:** Test all items for wobble/stability.  

**Pro Tip:** Dedicate a "decorating hour" on Sundays—set a timer to stay focused and avoid overworking. After each task, do a 2-minute stretch to prevent stiffness! 🛠️
