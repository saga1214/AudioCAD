You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which are gluten-free. Another brand that you could check out is Hippeas. They make gluten-free popcorn that comes in flavors like roasted radish and chili lime.

Also, fresh fruit like apples, bananas, and berries are always gluten-free and healthy. Trail mix is another easy option – you can mix nuts, seeds, and gluten-free dried fruit. Just be sure to check the labels on any packaged items to confirm they're safe for your diet.
