Absolutely. The best place to start would be the Erie Canal Museum in Lockport. It's located at 575-579 Delaware Avenue and has an excellent exhibit all about the Lockport Locks, which even includes a full-scale model of the locks and a working boat lift demonstration. You can also see a reconstruction of a canal boat to get a real sense of the scale of the operation.

If you're more of a hands-on person, you could visit the Old Erie Canal Village. It's an actual living history museum where you can see the old canal locks in operation and even talk to the reenactors. The village is located at 131 East Main Street in Lockport.

Just a quick tip: since you burn easily, make sure to wear a high-SPF sunscreen and consider visiting these indoor museums during the afternoon to stay out of the sun. Enjoy your vacation and the trip down Route 31!
