To recap, chicken cutliz requires two pounds of chicken for eight to fifteen dollars, all-purpose flour for three to five dollars, twelve eggs for three to seven dollars, breadcrumbs for two to four dollars, vegetable oil for four to eight dollars, and salt and pepper for three to six dollars.

For garlic mashed potatoes, you need about five pounds of potatoes for three to six dollars, a head of garlic for about a dollar, a pound of butter for four to seven dollars, and a half-gallon of milk for three to five dollars.
This meal for your family of four should come in around fifty to eighty-five dollars in ingredient costs.
