### 🌊 Beach & Water Activities
1. **Snorkeling** right from your bay**: Secret Harbour’s calm, shallow waters are perfect for beginners. Look for parrotfish, sea turtles, and tropical fish.  
2. **Kayaking/Stand-Up Paddleboarding**: Rent gear from the resort and explore nearby coves like **Laurita Bay** (a short paddle away).  
3. **Shallow Boat Tours**: Opt for a **flat-bottom boat tour** (e.g., **Virgin Islands National Park tours**) that avoids deep drops. Trunk Bay is a must-see (though a 10-min drive away).  

### 🏝️ Cruz Bay Exploration (5-min walk)
- **Cruz Bay Village**: Grab coffee at **The Longboard** and browse local art at **Mango’s**.  
- **Cruz Bay Beach Park**: A quieter, rocky beach for swimming and sunbathing.  
- **Cruz Bay Market**: Visit the Saturday farmers' market (check dates) for local crafts and fresh fruit.  

### 🌿 Nature & Hikes (Gentle & Flat)
1. **Reef Bay Trail**: A 1.5-mile, shaded loop through rainforest to a secluded black-sand beach. Perfect for your brother!  
2. **Maho’s Ruins**: Explore the ancient Arawak site (a 10-min drive) for history and coastal views.  
3. **Cruz Bay to Gallows Point**: Walk the beach path between Secret Harbour and your resort for fresh coconuts and sea turtle sightings.  

### 🌅 Hidden Gems

- **Laurita Bay**: Visit **Coki Beach Restaurant** for sunset cocktails with live music.  

### 🚤 Boat Rentals Tip
