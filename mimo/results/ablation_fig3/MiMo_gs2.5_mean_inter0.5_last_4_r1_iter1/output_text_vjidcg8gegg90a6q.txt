Alright, for your winter chicks, the bedding you choose is key to keeping them warm and safe.

Since you're using rubber mats, you can put down a thick layer of pine flake shavings. They'll insulate well from the concrete floor. Alternatively, you can use a deep litter approach again, just in a smaller space.

For the brooder plate, you'll want to put down a layer of wood shavings or sawdust. It's best to avoid pine shavings right under the heat source, as they can get too dry and dusty. Wood shavings are a great, rodent-resistant option.
