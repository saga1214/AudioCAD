For Halloween candy, it's always a good idea to get the classic favorites that almost everyone likes. Things like Reese’s, Snickers, Hershey’s chocolate bars, Twix, and Kit Kats are always a hit. You could also get some gummies, like Starbursts or Skittles, and maybe some sour candies like Warheads or Sour Patch Kids. If you want something that's a little healthier, you could get dark chocolate, especially if you know anyone with allergies. And definitely make sure to stock up on the individually wrapped candies so it's easy for guests to grab what they want.
