I understand you're looking for an adventure. Calgary is a fantastic base for outdoor activities. The most famous area is Banff National Park, which is a two-hour drive. You could do a scenic drive along the Icefields Parkway, go for a hike, or take a day trip to the town of Lake Louise. A little further is Canmore, another beautiful town in the Rockies. If you're up for a longer drive, there's Kananaskis Country, which is less crowded and has great hiking. For something closer, you could try hiking in the North Saskatchewan River Valley, which has numerous trails right in the city. Or, if you're looking for something more relaxing, you could take a day trip to the Kananaskis Lakes, which are beautiful and less touristy than Banff, or visit the famous Banff Gondola for panoramic views.
