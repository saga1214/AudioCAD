Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimming and warm weather! Packing tips were covered, but now we need a bigger adventure for a family of three, budget is no object. Since you dislike cold and she wants warm weather and to wear her bathing suit, places like Laguna Beach or Carmel Valley are perfect. They have year-round warmth, lots of swimming and beach opportunities, and are beautiful.

Since you need a car bigger than your own, renting an SUV or minivan is the way to go. Let's plan a five-day trip to Laguna Beach, which has everything you're looking for: beaches, swimming, hiking, and great dining.

Here's a full itinerary for you. The theme is warm weather, swimming, and relaxation.

For your first two days, you could stay in Laguna Beach. I'd recommend the Hotel Riverview in the Oceanview Building. It's right on the beach and has a fantastic pool area. You can pack your bathing suit and cover-up right away for the beach. For a swim, check out Laguna Beach State Park, which has calm, protected waters perfect for beginners. You can also rent surfboards or paddleboards there. For dinner, The Beachcomber is a classic beachside spot with seafood.

On day three, you could head to Carmel-by-the-Sea. The drive is scenic. You can stop at Carmel Beach for a swim. For dinner, The Sardine Factory is a great gluten-free-friendly option.

For days four and five, consider Big Sur. The drive is breathtaking. You can stop at Pfeiffer Beach for a swim in the clear water. A must-see is McWay Falls, but the falls don't have a swimming spot, just amazing views. For dinner, Nepenthe has incredible ocean views, but you'll need to book far in advance.

Finally, on your last day, you can return to Laguna Beach to enjoy the pool and beach before heading home.

This trip covers warm weather, swimming, great food, and beautiful scenery, all without needing to go sky-high in a hot air balloon. Enjoy your vacation!
