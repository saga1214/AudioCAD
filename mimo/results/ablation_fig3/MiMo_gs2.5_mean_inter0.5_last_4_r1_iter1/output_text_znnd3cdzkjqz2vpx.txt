Okay, it sounds like you've got a great plan and all the essentials ready. The only thing I'd strongly recommend adding is a stud finder. Habitat cabinets are heavy, and screwing them into studs is non-negotiable for safety and stability. A good stud finder can make finding those marks super fast. But otherwise, you are absolutely ready to get those cabinets up! Just remember to keep those lines perfectly level, and have fun with your friends. You're saving a ton of money and doing it together – it's going to be awesome.
