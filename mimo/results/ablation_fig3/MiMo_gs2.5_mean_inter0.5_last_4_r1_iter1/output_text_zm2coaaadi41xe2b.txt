Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! For ramen, check out Momiji, which has that rich, tonkotsu broth. If you like pho, Pho 606 is a must-visit for authentic Vietnamese flavors. And for Chinese noodles, Din Tai Fung is a great choice if you want something a bit more upscale.

For protein bars, I’d recommend Quest or RXBAR—they’re high in protein and pretty tasty. If you want something more natural, check out Clif Bars or GoMacro bars. Let me know if you want more recommendations!
