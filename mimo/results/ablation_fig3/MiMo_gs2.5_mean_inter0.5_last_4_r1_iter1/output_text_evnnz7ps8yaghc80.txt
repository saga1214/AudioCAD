For Monday, start with five minutes of two-ball one-hand drills with beanbags to get that rhythm. Then, spend ten minutes transitioning to three by focusing only on the first three throws. End with five minutes of scarf practice, gently whipping them.

On Tuesday, do ten minutes of just catching tennis balls to work on your hand-eye coordination.

Wednesday is all about rhythm. Practice juggling two beanbags to scar music for ten minutes.

Thursday, let's switch it up with fifteen minutes of slow-scare juggling to really focus on the pattern.

Friday, let's have some fun. Do ten minutes of juggling two beanbags one-hand, then ten minutes of juggling three beanbags, and finish with five minutes of juggling three scarves.

Saturday, do a longer session of fifteen minutes of three-beanbag transitions, focusing on that first throw.

And finally, Sunday is for rest and play. Spend fifteen minutes teaching your son the basics! Celebrating small wins like this will melt panic into joy.
