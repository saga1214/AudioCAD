Of course! For an edible garden beginner-friendly list, I'd say herbs are your best friends. Basil is fantastic—it's versatile, grows fast, and smells amazing. Mint and parsley are also super easy. For leafy greens, lettuce and spinach are perfect. Radishes are also great because they're quick and satisfying. And for something fruitier, tomatoes and peppers are a fantastic choice. They're forgiving and you'll get a ton of them. Happy gardening!
