Here’s a way you could structure the email. For the subject, you could write something like: "Meeting Request to Discuss Essay Improvement."

In the email, you can start with, "Dear Professor [Last Name]," and then say, "I hope you're doing well. I scored a 64 on the recent exam and wanted to talk to you about how I could improve on an essay like that in the future."

Then, you can add, "Subjectively evaluating content-heavy essays can be tough, and I was hoping you could share any specific areas where I could have done better. Understanding your feedback would be really valuable to me."

Finally, you can ask, "Would you have time to chat briefly sometime this week? My office hours don't work for me, but I'm happy to find a time that does. Thank you for your time."

This keeps the focus on learning and avoids challenging the grade directly.
