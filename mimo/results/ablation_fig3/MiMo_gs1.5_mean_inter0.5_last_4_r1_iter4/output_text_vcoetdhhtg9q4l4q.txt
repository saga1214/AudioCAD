That's a great goal! For truly quick dinners, focus on meals with minimal prep, few ingredients, and easy cleanup. Here are a few ideas using common pantry staples:

1.  **Speedy Stir-Fry:** (As mentioned before, but the *fastest* version!)
    *   **Ingredients:** Pre-cut veggies (broccoli florets, snap peas, carrots, bell peppers - frozen works great!), cooked rice (leftover or quick-cooking), protein (tofu, chicken breast, shrimp, or even just extra veggies), soy sauce or tamari, a splash of sesame oil, garlic/ginger (minced or pre-minced), optional: chili paste, green onions.
    *   **How Fast:** Sauté garlic/ginger in a hot pan/wok. Add protein and cook until done. Add veggies and stir-fry for 3-5 minutes. Pour in soy sauce/tamari, sesame oil, and any other sauces. Toss everything together. Serve immediately over rice. *Total time: 15-20 mins.*

2.  **One-Pan Roasted Proteins & Veggies:**
    *   **Ingredients:** Protein (chicken thighs, salmon fillets, pork chops, tofu), sturdy veggies (potatoes, sweet potatoes, Brussels sprouts, broccoli, cauliflower), olive oil, salt, pepper, simple spices (paprika, garlic powder, dried herbs like oregano or thyme).
    *   **How Fast:** Preheat oven. Toss chopped veggies with olive oil, salt, pepper, and spices on a sheet pan. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 15-20 mins. In the last 10-15 minutes, add your protein (pat dry first!) and roast until cooked through. *Total time: 30-40 mins (mostly hands-off).*

3.  **Super Simple Soup/Stew:**
    *   **Ingredients:** Canned beans (chickpeas, white beans), canned tomatoes, broth (chicken, veg), canned diced or sliced veggies (peppers, corn, mushrooms), herbs (dried oregano, basil, thyme), canned tuna (if desired), salt, pepper.
    *   **How Fast:** Sauté onions/garlic in a pot if you have them (optional but nice). Add broth, tomatoes, beans, canned veggies, and herbs. Simmer for 10-15 minutes to let flavors meld. Add tuna if using. *Total time: 20-30 mins.*

**Key Tips for Speed:**

*   **Pre-Cut Veggies:** Use pre-washed greens, pre-cut stir-fry mixes, or frozen veggies.
*   **Cooked Grains:** Keep leftover rice, quinoa, or pasta for quick bases.
*   **Pre-Made Sauces:** Use trusted jarred sauces (pesto, marinara, teriyaki, tahini) instead of making from scratch.
*   **Minimize Steps:** Choose recipes that involve mostly chopping, sautéing, or roasting, not complex techniques.
*   **Batch Cook:** Cook a big pot of grains or roast a large tray of veggies on the weekend for easy assembly during the week.

These focus on using what you likely have, minimizing active cooking time, and keeping cleanup simple – perfect for your decluttering and simplifying mindset! What kind of protein or main ingredient do you usually have on hand?
