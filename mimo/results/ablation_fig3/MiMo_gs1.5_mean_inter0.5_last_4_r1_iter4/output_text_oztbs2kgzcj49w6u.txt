Lockport is considered the "Gateway to the Great Lakes," and its connection to the Erie Canal is central to its identity. You can start by visiting the Erie Canal Museum there, which has excellent exhibits on the canal's history, engineering, and the lives of the people who worked on it. You could also take a tour of the historic canal locks, which offer a real sense of the engineering involved. Along the route, you'll see many plaques and markers that tell the story of the canal's importance. The whole drive becomes a journey through a piece of American history.
