Here are a few more gluten-free snack ideas perfect for camping and cycling, focusing on energy, protein, and crunch:

1. **Trail Mix**: Combine raw almonds, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, and unsweetened coconut flakes. Add dried cranberries or apricots for sweetness. (Check labels for gluten-free certification if needed).
2. **Roasted Chickpeas**: Look for single-serve packs of crispy chickpeas (like "Lärabar" style) or make your own with olive oil, salt, and spices.
3. **Nut Butter Packets**: Single-serve almond or peanut butter packets paired with gluten-free rice cakes or apple slices for dipping.
4. **Dried Fruit & Cheese**: Portioned dried mango, apricots, or figs with small cubes of cheddar or mozzarella cheese for a protein boost.

**Pro Tip**: Always check labels for hidden gluten in packaged items like crackers, jerky, or granola bars—even if they’re gluten-free certified. Pack snacks in reusable containers to avoid spills! 🏕️🚴‍♂️
