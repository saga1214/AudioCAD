Okay, here's a list of all the characters we've talked about, with their full names and what they do in the story.

First, there's Leo Vance. He's the main adventurer and the descendant of Elias Vance. He's the one who starts the quest to find the Sunstone and protect it from Silas Croft.

Then there's Silas Croft. He's the villain, who is the descendant of Augustus Croft. He's obsessed with stealing the Sunstone for himself.

Elena Castillo is the pretty woman who helps out. She's an expert on the jungle and the ancient puzzles, and she guides Leo's team.

Jack "Hammer" Stone is one of Leo's compatriots. He provides physical strength and is skilled in combat.

Javier Rios is another compatriot. He's a local guide who knows the jungle's hidden trails.

Arthur Finch is the third compatriot, a mapmaker who is great at solving the ancient puzzles.

And finally, we have Silas Croft's four henchmen: Marcus Kane, Brutus Thorne, Rex Gunner, and Ivan Petrov. They are all there to help Silas Croft find and steal the Sunstone by force.
