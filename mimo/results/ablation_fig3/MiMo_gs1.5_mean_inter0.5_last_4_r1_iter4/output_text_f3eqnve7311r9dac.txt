That's a very smart approach. Older comics are generally rarer and can appreciate more. Here are ten iconic books from the 1940s and 50s that are considered significant investments.

First, there's *Action Comics* number one from 1938, the very first appearance of Superman. That's the most famous of all. Then, *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, which introduced Batman. 

For Spider-Man, it's *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen from 1962, his first appearance. For the Hulk, it's *Tales to Astonish* number five. For Iron Man, *Tales of Suspense* number thirty-nine. 

For a classic from the 40s, there's *Warrior number one* from 1939, the first appearance of Captain America. And of course, *Superman's Pal, Jimmy Olsen* number one from 1952. 

Two great books from the 90s that are valuable are *Marvel Comics Presents* number 24, which is the first appearance of Venom, and *The Amazing Spider-Man* number three hundred, the first appearance of Carnage. These are all very significant and rare issues.
