Okay, so you're planning to get a gift for your daughter after her big play opening. That's a great idea! Let's think about what would make her feel really special and proud. 

First, since she's worked so hard on the play, something that acknowledges her effort and performance would be perfect. A personalized item could be really meaningful. For example, a custom poster or a framed sign with her name or a funny quote related to the play. Or maybe a small trophy or medal that says "Best Performer" or "Most Dedicated" – something she can keep and look back at. 

If she's into art or crafts, a sketchbook or some nice colored pencils could be a fun gift to let her express herself. Or if she's more of a tech person, a cool phone case with her play character on it might be a hit. 

Another idea is a special treat or experience. Like tickets to see another show, or a fancy dinner out for the family to celebrate. Or even just a really good ice cream or a fun activity like an escape room afterward. 

You could also think about something practical but thoughtful, like a cozy blanket or a really nice water bottle for her to use while practicing in the future. 

The key is to pick something that feels special and shows you're proud of her hard work. What does she usually like? That could help narrow it down!
