For your layover in Philadelphia, you should try Philly Cheesesteak. It's a sandwich made with thinly sliced beef, melted cheese, and grilled onions. You can find it at famous spots like Pat's or Geno's.

Another great option is soft pretzels, which are a classic street food. You can find them at places like Reading Terminal Market.

And if you're in a hurry, you could grab a Philly Pretzel or a Philly Roast Pork Sandwich. Enjoy your trip!
