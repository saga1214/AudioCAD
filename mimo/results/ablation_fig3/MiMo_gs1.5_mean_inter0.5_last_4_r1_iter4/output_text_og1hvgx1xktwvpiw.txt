No problem, let me clarify that for you. The classic Boulevardier can be made in two main ways. The standard ratio, with whiskey dominant, is one and a quarter ounces of bourbon or rye, one ounce of sweet vermouth, and one ounce of Campari. The equal-parts version, which your friend uses, is one ounce of each of those three ingredients. 

The Last Word is a different drink entirely. For that one, you'll need equal parts of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. Each of those should be three-quarters of an ounce. 

So, to recap: Boulevardier is either one-and-a-quarter-whiskey or equal-parts. The Last Word is equal-parts of gin, Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime. Hope that clears things up!
