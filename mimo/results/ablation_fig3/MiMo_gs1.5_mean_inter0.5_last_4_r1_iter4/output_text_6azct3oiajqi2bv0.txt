Here are several safe, lining-friendly ways to attach something to your jacket without tearing it, ranging from least to most invasive:

1.  **Safety Pin (Most Common & Reversible):**
    *   **How:** Thread the pin through the outer fabric layer, *just* deep enough so the point doesn't poke through the lining. Then, press the folded tip of the pin into the *outer* fabric layer where you want it to sit (e.g., the seam, hem, or collar band).
    *   **Pros:** Very discreet, holds well, easy to remove.
    *   **Cons:** Risk of pinning through the lining if you're not careful. Can sometimes leave a tiny mark if pressed hard. Best for attaching small items like tags or a name.

2.  **Double-Sided Tape (Strong & Clean):**
    *   **How:** Apply a small strip of strong double-sided tape (like Aleene's Tacky Glue tape or a temporary fabric tape) directly onto the *outer* fabric where you want the item to sit. Press the item firmly onto the tape.
    *   **Pros:** Clean, holds well, easily removable (though residue might need a little solvent like rubbing alcohol).
    *   **Cons:** Requires buying tape, residue possible.

3.  **Fabric Scrap & Safety Pin/Clip (Most Secure & No Lining Contact):**
    *   **How:** Cut a small piece of scrap fabric (like an old sock, scarf, or the jacket's own hem) to the size/shape you need. Attach the item to the scrap fabric using a safety pin, clip, or even a tiny dab of fabric glue. Then, pin or clip the *entire scrap fabric piece* to the jacket's outer layer.
    *   **Pros:** The item never touches the lining directly. Very secure. Easy to remove.
    *   **Cons:** Uses a bit more material.

4.  **Fabric Glue (Permanent or Semi-Permanent):**
    *   **How:** Apply a small dab of strong fabric glue (like E6000 or Aleene's Tacky Glue) to the *back* of the item you want to attach. Press it firmly onto the outer fabric of the jacket. Wipe away any excess glue immediately.
    *   **Pros:** Secure bond, very discreet once set.
    *   **Cons:** Can be permanent (or very difficult to remove cleanly). Test on an inconspicuous area first! Use sparingly.

5.  **Hair Tie (Simple & Temporary):**
    *   **How:** Slide a hair tie under the collar band or around the hem of the jacket, pulling the outer fabric layers together. Then, wrap the hair tie tightly around the item you want to attach to the jacket's outer layer.
    *   **Pros:** Very easy, inexpensive, temporary.
    *   **Cons:** Visible, not very secure, temporary solution.

**Recommendation:**

*   For a **tag or name**, the **safety pin method** (done carefully) or **double-sided tape** is usually best.
*   For something **slightly larger or needing more security**, the **fabric scrap method** is excellent and gives you no direct contact with the lining.
*   If you want a **semi-permanent fix** and are okay with a small test spot, **fabric glue** works well.

**Key Tip:** Always test your chosen method on an inconspicuous area of the jacket first (like an inside seam or hem) to ensure it doesn't damage the fabric or lining. Choose the method that best suits the item you need to attach and how long you need it to stay.
