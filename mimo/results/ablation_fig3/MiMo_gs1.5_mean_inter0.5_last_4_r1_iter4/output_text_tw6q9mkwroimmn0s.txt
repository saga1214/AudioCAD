That sounds like a fantastic theme! Here are 5 meal ideas and 5 dessert ideas, keeping your dietary needs in mind:

**5 Ocean-Themed Main Meals:**

1.  **Spicy Mango Shrimp Stir-Fry:** Toss shrimp with bell peppers, snap peas, carrots, and a vibrant stir-fry sauce. Finish with fresh mango slices and a sprinkle of lime. *Uses shrimp (not shellfish), mango, spicy.*
2.  **Grilled Salmon with Mango-Avocado Salsa & Quinoa:** Grill salmon fillets. Serve with a fresh salsa of diced mango, avocado, red onion, cilantro, lime juice, and a pinch of chili flakes. Pair with a simple quinoa salad. *Uses fish (salmon), mango, spicy.*
3.  **Tropical Fish & Tofu Coconut Curry:** Simmer white fish (like cod or halibut) and firm tofu in a fragrant coconut milk curry with green beans, potatoes, and a touch of curry paste. Garnish with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lime. *Vegetarian option (tofu), uses coconut, spicy.*
4.  **"Crab" Cake (Shrimp or Veggie) with Lemon-Herb Butter:** Make flavorful cakes using finely chopped shrimp (or a mix of mashed beans, veggies, herbs, and breadcrumbs) and pan-fry until crispy. Serve with lemon-herb butter. *Uses shrimp (not shellfish), lemon, fresh herbs.*
5.  **Shrimp & Vegetable Frittata with Seafood Sauce:** Whisk eggs with cream, sautéed spinach, cherry tomatoes, and chopped shrimp. Pour into a baking dish, top with feta or goat cheese, and bake until set. Serve with a dollop of seafood sauce. *Uses shrimp, eggs, cheese.*

**5 Ocean-Themed Desserts:**

1.  **Mango & Coconut Chia Pudding:** Layer chia seeds soaked in coconut milk with layers of fresh mango puree and a touch of honey or maple syrup. Top with toasted coconut flakes. *Uses mango, coconut, sweet.*
2.  **Dark Chocolate Avocado Mousse:** Blend ripe avocados with melted dark chocolate, a splash of coconut milk, vanilla, and a pinch of sea salt until silky smooth. Chill before serving. *Sweet, rich, healthy fats.*
3.  **Fresh Fruit & Yogurt Parfait with Pomegranate:** Layer Greek yogurt, fresh berries (strawberries, blueberries), diced mango, and a sprinkle of pomegranate seeds. Drizzle lightly with honey if desired. *Uses mango, berries, sweet.*
4.  **Mango Sorbet:** Blend frozen mango chunks with a splash of lime juice and a tiny bit of honey until smooth and creamy. Serve in chilled glasses. *Uses mango, light, refreshing, no added sugar.*
5.  **Grilled Pineapple with Coconut Cream & Lime:** Grill pineapple rings until caramelized. Serve topped with sweetened coconut cream and a squeeze of fresh lime juice. *Uses coconut, lime, sweet (from pineapple).*

**Key Considerations for Your Theme & Needs:**

*   **Shellfish:** Strictly avoided in all dishes.
*   **Diabetes:** Focused on controlling sugar (using fruit sweetness, minimal added sugar/honey, focusing on natural sweetness from mango/berries/avocado). Portion control is key.
*   **Spicy:** Incorporated where appropriate (stir-fry sauce, curry paste, salsa chili flakes, seafood sauce).
*   **Mango:** Featured prominently in both mains and desserts.
*   **Theme:** Seafood (shrimp, fish, coconut, tropical fruits) and ocean-inspired names/accents enhance the theme.

Enjoy your delicious and thematic ocean-themed celebration!
