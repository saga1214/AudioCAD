Here's a quick recap of the **three shrimp dishes** I suggested earlier:

### 1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - Shrimp (thawed)  
     - Pasta (e.g., linguine)  
     - Garlic, butter, red pepper flakes  
     - White wine (optional)  
     - Lemon juice & zest  
     - Fresh parsley  
   - **Prep**: Sauté shrimp with garlic, butter, and red pepper flakes. Toss with pasta, white wine, lemon juice, and parsley. Serve with crusty bread.

### 2. **Shrimp Fra Diavolo**
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - Shrimp  
     - Crushed tomatoes  
     - Garlic, red pepper flakes, olive oil  
     - Butter or cream (for richness)  
     - Parmesan cheese  
   - **Prep**: Sauté garlic and red pepper flakes in olive oil. Add tomatoes and shrimp, simmer until shrimp cooks. Finish with butter/cream and Parmesan.

### 3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - Shrimp  
     - Grits (cooked)  
     - Cajun seasoning  
     - Onion, bell pepper, celery (sautéed)  
     - Buttermilk or broth (for grits)  
     - Hot sauce (e.g., Crystal or Tabasco)  
   - **Prep**: Sauté veggies with Cajun seasoning. Cook shrimp separately. Serve over creamy grits, topped with shrimp and hot sauce.

---

### Key Tips:
- **Make-Ahead**: All shrimp dishes taste better the next day!  
- **Portion**: For a family of three, aim for **4–5 quarts total**.  
- **Spice Level**: Adjust red pepper flakes/hot sauce to their preference.
