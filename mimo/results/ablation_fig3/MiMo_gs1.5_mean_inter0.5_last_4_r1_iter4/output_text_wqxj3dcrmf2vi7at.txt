Here's a full recap of all the treat ideas and tips for your corgi princess:

### 🐾 **Homemade Treats**  
1. **Frozen Bites**:  
   - Mashed **pumpkin** or **sweet potato** rolled into small balls, frozen for 1-2 hours.  
   - *Bonus*: Adds hydration and digestion support!  

2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**:  
   - Mix peanut butter (NO xylitol!) with rolled oats.  
   - Roll into balls, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15 mins. Cool before cutting into pieces.  

3. **Simple Sticks**:  
   - Thin **cooked chicken** strips (boiled, cooled, and sliced).  
   - Small pieces of **raw carrot** or **steamed beet** (avoid large chunks to prevent choking).  

### 🛒 **Store-Bought Brands**  
- **Blue Buffalo** (Grain-Free options)  
- **Wellness** (Grain-Free training treats)  
- **Zuke’s** (Mini Nibbles—small, crunchy)  
- **Greenies** (Odor-control dental treats)  
- **Merrick** (Grain-Free training bites)  

### 🎯 **Training Tips**  
- **Sit Command**: Use a treat as a lure (move it over her head), say "sit," and reward instantly.  
- **Potty Training**:  
  - Take her outside on a **regular schedule** (after waking, meals, play).  
  - Use a **positive reward** (treat + enthusiastic praise) when she goes.  
  - **No punishment for accidents**—just clean thoroughly to remove scent.  
- **Stop Barking at Felix**:  
  - Teach a **"Quiet" command** (raise a palm, say "Quiet," reward silence).  
  - Redirect with a **toy or new command** ("Sit," "Play").  

### 💖 **Collar Color Idea**  
For a **brown and white corgi**, try a **teal or turquoise collar**—it pops beautifully against her fur! Soft pastel pink or light blue also work for a "princess" vibe.  

### 📌 **Key Reminders**  
- **Portion control**: Treats should be <10% of her daily calories.  
- **Safety first**: Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions, xylitol, or raw dough.  
- **Patience pays off**: Corgis are smart but stubborn—consistency is everything!  

Your princess will thrive with these treats and training strategies. Happy pampering! 🐾💕
