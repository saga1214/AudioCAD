I understand your frustration. There's no magic 'cleanse' that will remove them. Your body does have natural ways to eliminate them, but it's a slow process.

The best thing you can do is prevent them from entering your body in the first place. So, to summarize, the five things to avoid are: one, non-stick cookware. Two, grease-resistant food packaging. Three, stain-resistant or waterproof materials. Four, certain cosmetics and floss. And five, unfiltered tap water. 

While you can't 'flush' them out quickly, reducing your ongoing exposure is the most effective way to lower your body's burden over time.
