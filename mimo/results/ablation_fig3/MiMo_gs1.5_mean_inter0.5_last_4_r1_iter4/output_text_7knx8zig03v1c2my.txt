Great questions! Here's a breakdown of treats and clicker training to help your puppy, Galileo:

### 🍗 Treat Recommendations:
1. **High-Value Treats**: Use tiny, soft, and smelly items your dog *loves* (e.g., cooked chicken, cheese, hot dog bits, peanut butter).  
2. **Safe Flavors**: Avoid xylitol (in sugar-free products), chocolate, grapes, or onions.  
3. **Portion Control**: Treats should be ≤10% of daily calories. Cut into pea-sized pieces.  
4. **Budget Options**: Cooked egg, plain yogurt, or freeze-dried liver are great backups.  

### 🎲 Clicker Training Explained:
- **What it is**: A simple clicker acts as a "bridge" to communicate *exactly* when your dog does something right.  
- **How it works**:  
  1. **Click**: The moment he does the desired action (e.g., sits), click the device.  
  2. **Reward**: Immediately give a treat. The click marks the *exact* instant of success.  
  3. **Repetition**: Repeat until he understands the click = reward signal.  
- **Why it works**: The click is neutral until paired with treats, creating a powerful learning cue.  

**Example for "Sit"**:  
- Hold treat near his nose → raise upward → as he lowers his backside, **CLICK** → give treat. Repeat 10x/day. Soon, he’ll sit on cue!  

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Timing**: Keep sessions short (5-10 mins) and fun. End on a success!  
- **No Punishment**: Focus on rewarding good behavior. If he escapes a crate, calmly guide him back *without reprimanding*.  
- **Recall Training**: Practice recall in low-distraction areas first. Use a long leash and a happy "Come!" voice.  

Clicker training builds trust and makes learning intuitive. With Galileo’s energy, he’ll pick up commands fast! 🐾
