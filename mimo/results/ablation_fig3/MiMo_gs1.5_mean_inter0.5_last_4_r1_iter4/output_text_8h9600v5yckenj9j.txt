### 🎃 Quick Decoration Hacks (Outdoors-Focused)
1. **Fog Chiller Setup:**  
   - **DIY Fog Chiller:** Place your fog machine behind a **plastic storage bin** (like a 40-gallon bin).  
     - Cut a hole in the side for the fog machine nozzle.  
     - Place a **wire rack** inside the bin.  
     - Fill the rack with **frozen water bottles** (or ice packs wrapped in towels).  
     - The fog will cool and sink as it passes through, creating low-lying mist.  
     - *Pro Tip:* Place the bin on a **tarp** to catch melted water.  
   - **Alternative:** Use a **fog chiller cooler** (available at party stores) for instant results.
