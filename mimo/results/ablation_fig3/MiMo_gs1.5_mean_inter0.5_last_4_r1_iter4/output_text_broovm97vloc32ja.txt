That's a fantastic skill to have, and it's definitely a possibility. Many successful children's book authors are also illustrators. However, the approach is still different.

For a children's book, publishers are looking for a complete package of text and art. You would need to create a full manuscript with a finished, professional-looking art sample spread. This sample is crucial because it proves your vision for the book.

Once you have that, you can either submit it to publishers directly, or you can work with an agent who specializes in illustrated children's books and can help you find the right publisher. So yes, your artistic skills are a major asset, but you'll need to present them as a cohesive, finished product.
