That's a great question! For someone with hands-on hobbies like tailoring and vintage pen restoration, meaningful gifts could include:

### For Tailoring:
- **Specialty Fabric**: High-quality cotton, linen, or silk blends for practice projects.  
- **Tailoring Tools**: Precision shears, a good thimble, or a seam ripper.  
- **Pattern Books**: Vintage sewing patterns or books on garment construction.  
- **Fabric Markers**: Water-soluble pens for precise marking.  
- **Personalized Kit**: A monogrammed sewing kit with needles, thread, and measuring tape.  

### For Vintage Fountain Pens:
- **Premium Ink**: Bottles of rare, vintage-style inks (e.g., Pelikan, Waterman, or custom blends).  
- **Pen Repair Kit**: Nib polishing tools, grip rest, or a spare nib.  
- **Display Case**: A protective box for showcasing favorite pens.  
- **Restoration Supplies**: Brass polish, cleaning brushes, or vintage pen parts.  

### For Woodworking/Clay:
- **Workshop Gift Card**: For a store like Woodcraft or Michael’s.  
- **Tool Starter Kit**: A hand plane, chisel set, or clay sculpting tools.  
- **Wood/Clay Samples**: Specialty woods (e.g., walnut) or clay types (e.g., stoneware).  
- **Workshop Class**: Paid for them to take a beginner’s course.  

### Universal Ideas:
- **Quality Notebook**: For sketching designs or jotting ideas.  
- **Custom Art Print**: A design based on their hobbies (e.g., a vintage pen and sewing machine illustration).  
- **Experience Gift**: A weekend workshop in tailoring, pottery, or woodworking.  

### Why These Work:
- **Practical**: Tools and materials they’ll use immediately.  
- **Personal**: Ties directly to their passions.  
- **Encouraging**: Supports their growth in the hobbies.  

If their family is unsure, a gift card to a craft store (like Joann, Hobby Lobby, or Etsy) gives them control over what they need! 🎁
