That's the perfect balance! For a relaxed first night in Austin, here are some great casual spots to start:

1.  **Taco Deli:** (South Congress) Famous for its unique breakfast tacos (like the "Bacon & Cheddar" or "Breakfast Tacos with Fried Egg"). Super casual, local vibe. *Great for:* Quick, iconic Austin experience.
2.  **Veracruz All Natural:** (Multiple locations) A legendary spot for *carne asada* tacos (grilled steak) and *pan de yema* (yeast bread). Expect a line, but it's part of the experience. *Great for:* Authentic, no-frills Mexican comfort food.
3.  **Home Slice Pizza:** (Multiple locations) Consistently praised for its thin, crispy, Roman-style pizza. Very casual, order at the counter, pick up at the window. *Great for:* A satisfying, casual pizza night.
4.  **Odd Duck:** (South Congress) A fun, eclectic "farm-to-table" spot with creative small plates and sharing-friendly dishes. Very casual atmosphere. *Great for:* A unique, locally-sourced meal without formal dining pressure.
5.  **Banger's Sausage House:** (South Congress) If you're in the mood for indulgent, deliciously messy British-style sausages (in bangers and mash, or loaded up with toppings). *Great for:* A fun, shareable, casual pub-style meal.
6.  **Food Trucks:** (South Congress, Rainey Street, etc.) Austin has an incredible food truck scene. Look for trucks specializing in BBQ, Korean tacos, vegan options, or international cuisine. *Great for:* Unpredictable, delicious, and super casual finds.

**Tips for Choosing:**

*   **Mood:** Craving tacos? Pizza? Creative small plates? Sausages? This will narrow it down!
*   **Location:** Where are you staying? SC is a foodie hub, but other areas have great options too.
*   **Line Tolerance:** Places like Veracruz or Taco Deli can have lines, especially popular ones.
