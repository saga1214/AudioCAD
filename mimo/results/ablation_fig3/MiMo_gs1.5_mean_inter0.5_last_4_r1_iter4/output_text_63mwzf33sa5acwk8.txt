Okay, so you're thinking of combining a desert adventure with a Vegas trip. Smart move. The desert vibe from Joshua Tree and Nevada is completely different from Vegas, which is all about energy and entertainment. If you want a proper Vegas experience, here's how you could blend it all together. After your camping trip, you could drive into Vegas. The drive from Red Rock or Valley of Fire is about an hour or so, so you can take it easy. Staying somewhere near the Strip is a good idea for convenience, but if you're with your girlfriend, maybe a boutique hotel away from the chaos would be better. 

For a Vegas experience, think beyond just gambling. You could check out a cool live music venue, like a jazz club or an underground band. The Venetian has a nice indoor pool, but honestly, a small hotel with a decent pool is probably more fun and less crowded. You could also explore the Fremont Street Experience in the downtown area—it's free, has great street performers, and a fun, old-school Vegas vibe. 

If you're into nightlife, a rooftop bar with a view would be classy. Or if you want something more unique, there are speakeasies and themed bars everywhere. Just remember to pace yourselves—it's easy to get caught up in the excitement. A nice dinner at a restaurant with a view of the Strip could be a perfect end to the night. 

The key is to balance the adventure from the desert with the fun and relaxation Vegas offers. It’s all about making memories together, so just keep that in mind. Enjoy the trip!
