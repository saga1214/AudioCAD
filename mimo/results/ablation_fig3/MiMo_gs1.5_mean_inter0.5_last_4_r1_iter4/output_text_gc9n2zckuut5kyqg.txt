Absolutely! Here are some budget-friendly dessert ideas that are kid-friendly, healthy-ish, and align with your diabetes awareness theme:

### 🍰 **1. Fruit-Focused Desserts (Minimal Sugar, High Flavor)**
- **Fresh Fruit Skewers:** Skewer grapes, berries, melon, pineapple, and kiwi. Drizzle with a *tiny* bit of honey (optional).
- **Fruit Salad:** Combine seasonal fruits (watermelon, mango, berries, oranges). Add mint for freshness.
- **Fruit Baskets:** Serve pre-cut fruit in a large bowl—kids love dipping in yogurt or nut butter.

### 🍪 **2. Simple Baked Goods (Pantry Staples)**
- **Mini Oatmeal Cookies:** Use rolled oats, mashed banana or applesauce (instead of sugar), cinnamon, and raisins. Bake in small batches.
- **Banana Bread Muffins:** Mix mashed bananas, oats, eggs, and a touch of honey. Fill with yogurt for dipping.
- **Energy Balls:** Blend dates, oats, peanut butter, chia seeds, and cocoa powder. Roll into balls (no baking needed!).

### 🍍 **3. Grilled/Sweet-Savory Options (Leverage BBQ Equipment)**
- **Grilled Pineapple or Peaches:** Halve fruit, brush with a little honey, and grill until caramelized.
- **Cinnamon Apples:** Core apples, fill with cinnamon and a pinch of sugar, and grill or bake.

### 🥄 **4. Yogurt & Frozen Treats**
- **Yogurt Parfaits:** Layer Greek yogurt, granola, and berries in small cups.
- **Frozen Yogurt Pops:** Dip yogurt in fruit puree and freeze on sticks (add a mini chocolate chip if you have it).
- **"Nice Cream":** Blend frozen bananas with cocoa powder or berries for a creamy, no-churn treat.

### 💡 **Budget Tips**
- **Portion Control:** Serve desserts in small bowls or cups to avoid waste.
- **DIY Stations:** Set up a "make your own" dessert bar (e.g., fruit + yogurt dip, sprinkles, and crushed nuts).
- **Use Leftovers:** Double-bake cookies or muffins to extend supply.
- **Skip the Sugar:** Replace sugar with mashed fruit, spices (cinnamon, nutmeg), or unsweetened cocoa.

### 🥤 **Drink Pairings**
- **Sugar-Free Lemonade:** Mix lemon juice, water, and a pinch of stevia or monk fruit.
- **Infused Water:** Cucumber-mint or berry-water for hydration.
- **Sparkling Water:** Add a splash of fruit juice for color.

These ideas keep costs low while ensuring there’s something sweet for kids and adults alike—without overshadowing your diabetes awareness mission. Let me know if you’d like recipes or more details! 😊
