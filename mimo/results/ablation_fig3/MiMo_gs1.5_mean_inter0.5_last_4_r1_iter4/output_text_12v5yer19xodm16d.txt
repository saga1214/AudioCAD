Okay, you sound like you need a serious energy boost. Let's see what we can do with drinks and snacks. 

For drinks, I'd suggest a strong cup of coffee, or a green tea for a more natural caffeine. You could also try an energy drink like Red Bull or Monster, but maybe don't overdo it. A cold glass of lemonade is refreshing, and a smoothie with banana and spinach can give you some nutrients. 

As for snacks, I'd go with nuts like almonds for protein. A banana is a great energy source. You could have some dark chocolate, maybe a granola bar for some fiber, or some yogurt for protein. These will help keep your energy up without making you feel too sluggish. Hope that helps!
