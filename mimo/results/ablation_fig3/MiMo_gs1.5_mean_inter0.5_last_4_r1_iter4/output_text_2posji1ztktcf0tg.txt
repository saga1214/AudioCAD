Ah, making dinner for your sister! That's thoughtful. Since you mentioned she's a vegetarian, I'd suggest something hearty and flavorful that she'd actually enjoy. How about a mushroom risotto? It's creamy, packed with umami from the mushrooms, and you can add some spinach for extra greens. If she likes a bit of spice, you could throw in some roasted peppers or sun-dried tomatoes for a kick. Or, if you want something lighter, maybe a stuffed bell pepper with quinoa, black beans, and cheese? Both are veggie-friendly and pretty impressive for a home-cooked meal.
