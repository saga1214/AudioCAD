Here are quick, elegant recipes for both cocktails using your leftover ingredients:

### 1. **Vodka Mixer: Cucumber-Mint Refresher**
*(Refreshing, light, and perfect for warm evenings)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 3-4 cucumber slices (thin)  
- 4-5 fresh mint leaves  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- 2 oz soda water  
- Ice  
- *Optional garnish:* Cucumber ribbon or mint sprig  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle cucumber and mint in a shaker.  
2. Add vodka, lime juice, and ice. Shake well.  
3. Strain into a chilled glass. Top with soda water.  
4. Garnish with cucumber ribbon.  

---

### 2. **Argentinian-Style Fernet: Fernet & Soda with Citrus**  
*(Bold, bitter-sweet, and refreshing—classic in Argentina)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 1 oz fresh orange juice (or 1 tsp orange liqueur if in a rush)  
- 2 oz soda water  
- 1 oz simple syrup (optional, adjust to taste)  
- Ice  
- *Optional garnish:* Orange slice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Fill a glass with ice.  
2. Add Fernet, orange juice, and simple syrup. Stir gently.  
3. Top with soda water.  
4. Garnish with an orange slice.  

---

### **Key Tips:**  
- **No fresh lime/orange?** Use bottled juice + a squeeze of lemon for brightness.  
- **No mint?** Skip the cucumber-mint drink; use the Fernet recipe instead.  
- **Formality:** Serve in chilled coupe or highball glasses.  
- **Allergy-safe:** Both recipes avoid soda for the allergic guest—offer sparkling water as an alternative.
