Alright, before you film, here are the key things to consider to make it smooth and professional.

First, and most importantly, get permission from the facility. Make sure they’re okay with you filming and posting it online. You don’t want any privacy issues later.

Next, set up your camera. You can use your phone in landscape mode, but a tripod or a little stand will help keep it steady. If you have a small microphone, that’s good too, but if not, just making sure the camera is close enough to pick up your voice is fine.

Then, just prepare your show like you normally would. Practice a little if you need to, but keep it natural. You know your stuff.

And after the show, save the video and think about how you’ll edit it. You could trim it down to the best parts, add some simple text or captions, and make sure it’s short enough to keep the attention of kids.

That’s really all there is to it. Just film, share, and have fun.
