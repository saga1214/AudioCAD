Okay, let's see. You've already removed all the hardware and have sanded the cabinets. That's great progress. Before you apply the primer, you'll want to do a few more things to make sure it goes smoothly.

First, give the sanded surfaces a quick wipe-down with a tack cloth to pick up all the dust. If you don't, that dust will just stick to your primer and give you a bumpy finish.

Next, and this is super important, make sure the cabinets are completely dry. Any moisture trapped in the wood will cause the paint to peel later on. 

And then, just a quick check to make sure there are no remaining splinters or rough spots from the sanding. 

After that, you're all set for primer!
