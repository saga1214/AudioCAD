A picnic party is a fantastic way to celebrate! It’s casual, lets parents handle food preferences, and includes activities for all ages. Here’s how to make it work:

**End-of-Year Picnic Plan:**
1. **Location:** A local park with tables/shade (check for reservations). Have a rain plan (school cafeteria or community hall).
2. **Food:**  
   - **Dessert Focus:** A **sundae bar** is ideal! Parents bring favorite toppings (sprinkles, hot fudge, crushed Oreos). You provide:
     * Dairy-free vanilla ice cream (soy/coconut based).
     * A large tub of ice cream (parents can contribute).
     * Toppings in small bowls (avoid dairy if needed).
   - **Light Snacks:** Pizza, sandwiches, or fruit cups (parents can bring what they prefer).
3. **Activities:**  
   - **Simple Games:** Three-legged races, sack races, or a "Pin the Tail on the Brownie" game.  
   - **Creative Station:** Decorate cookies or make friendship bracelets.  
   - **Trophies:** Award small badges or certificates for achievements (e.g., "Best Helper," "Most Improved").
4. **Volunteer Parents:**  
   - Assign food setup/cleanup to parents.  
   - Ask a parent to bring the ice cream tub or a game.  
5. **Cost Control:**  
   - Dues cover ice cream tubs and decorations.  
   - Parents handle food costs (suggest they bring their preferred snacks).

**Why This Works:**  
- **Inclusive:** Everyone gets their favorite foods.  
- **Low Pressure:** No cooking or complex logistics for you.  
- **Fun & Memorable:** Sundae bar + games creates a lively atmosphere.
