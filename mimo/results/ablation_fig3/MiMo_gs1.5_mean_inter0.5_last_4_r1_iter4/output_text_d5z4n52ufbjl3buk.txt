That's a great approach. You want to be professional and clear, but without being confrontational.

Here are a few pointers for your email. First, start with a simple subject line like 'Meeting Request: Exam Feedback'. In the body, you can say you're writing to request a brief meeting to understand how to improve for future exams. You can mention that the essay format prompted you to seek feedback. When you bring up the grade, you could say something like, 'I was surprised by my score of 64 and wanted to understand what I could have done better.' This focuses on your own learning. Then, you can mention you have a conflict with his office hours and ask for an alternative time. Finally, thank him for his time. This keeps it polite, professional, and focused on your own growth.
