That's a great choice! Secret Harbour Resort is fantastic for beachfront relaxation. For daytime fun right from your resort, you're in luck:

1.  **Snorkeling at Secret Harbour Bay:** This is your backyard! The bay is generally calm and protected, making it ideal for beginners and casual snorkeling. Look for small fish and maybe even turtles right off the beach.
2.  **Coki Point Beach (Short Boat Ride/Tram):** Take a short boat ride or a fun tram ride to Coki Point. It's another beautiful, sheltered bay perfect for snorkeling. You might see parrotfish, surgeonfish, and potentially a sea turtle. The beach is great for swimming and sunbathing.
3.  **Cinnamon Bay (Short Boat Ride):** Another iconic St. John beach, easily accessible by boat from Secret Harbour. Known for its stunning views and excellent snorkeling conditions. The underwater wall offers more variety. The beach is great for swimming and picnicking.

**For Broader St. John Exploration:**

4.  **Sandy Spit Beach (Cinnamon Bay):** While you can snorkel here, it's also famous for its incredible **birdwatching**. You'll often see **Reddish Terns**, **Piping Plovers**, and sometimes **Magnificent Frigatebirds** soaring overhead. Great for spotting waders and shorebirds.
5.  **Trunk Bay (Boat Access):** Requires a boat trip but is a world-class snorkeling destination. The underwater walkway is incredible, revealing a vibrant underwater world teeming with fish, coral, and potentially **parrotfish** and **turtles**. The beach itself is beautiful.
6.  **Salt River Bay National Park (Kayak/Boat Access):** Accessible by kayak or short boat trip. Explore the mangroves for unique birdlife like **Wood Ducks** and **Brown Pelicans**, then snorkel the calm, shallow bay beyond.

**For St. Thomas Exploration (if your boat allows):**

7.  **Coral Bay (Frenchtown) Beach:** A beautiful, rocky beach with great snorkeling right off the shore, especially in the shallower areas. Look for **turtles** (common in the area) and small reef fish.
8.  **Magens Bay (Frenchtown):** One of the most famous beaches in the Caribbean! While beautiful for swimming, the snorkeling is more for the calmer, shallower areas near the shore or specific spots marked by the resort (like the "Snorkel Trail" near the entrance).
9.  **Sage Rock Beach (Frenchtown):** A hidden gem accessible by a short hike down a trail from the main Frenchtown area. Very calm, sheltered bay with excellent snorkeling, often with good visibility and a variety of fish.

**Tips for Your Daytime Adventures:**

*   **Snorkel Gear:** Bring your own mask, fins, and snorkel! Rentals are available at many spots (like Reef Tranquility on St. Thomas) if you forget.
*   **Boat Charters:** Check if your charter offers short excursions (like to Trunk Bay or Cinnamon Bay) or if you can book a separate short trip to explore different beaches.
*   **Guided Snorkel Tours:** Some operators offer short guided snorkel tours focusing on reef identification and marine life – perfect for your birding interest!
*   **Birding Guide:** Pick up a free "Reef Fish Identification Guide" from the St. John National Park or a bookstore – it's surprisingly good for common seabirds too!
*   **Sun Protection:** Sunscreen, hats, and sunglasses are essential, even on cloudy days!
*   **Hydration & Snacks:** Bring plenty of water and snacks for your adventures.

Enjoy your week! St. John's beaches and reefs are truly special, and Secret Harbour is a fantastic basecamp for exploring them. Remember to check the tide times before heading out snorkeling!
