I see your concern about carrying plants up five flights of stairs. I would suggest focusing on small, lightweight plants.

First, for succulents, the pots are usually very light because the soil and the plant itself are both small and have a low water requirement. So, they should be very easy to carry.

Second, for the snake plant, the plant itself is very light, but the pot it comes in can vary. You'll want to get a lightweight pot, like one made of plastic or resin. It's a good idea to check the weight before you buy it. Most of the small snake plants you'll see in stores come in lightweight containers, so they should also be easy to carry up the stairs.

Since you plan to take them home after your stay, getting a small, lightweight plant is definitely the way to go. You could even consider bringing your own lightweight pot to put the plant in, which would make it even easier to transport.
