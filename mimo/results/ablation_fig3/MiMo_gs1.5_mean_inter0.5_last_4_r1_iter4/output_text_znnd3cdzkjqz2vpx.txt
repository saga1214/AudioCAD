You've got the essentials, but I'd really recommend adding a few more tools to make the job smoother and safer. First, a stud finder is absolutely critical. Habitat usually includes one with their cabinets, but just double-check that you have it. You'll need it to find the studs to hang the upper cabinets securely.

Next, a measuring tape is super important for checking measurements and making sure everything is aligned. And a utility knife is great for trimming any excess packaging material.

Also, consider having a helper's tool. It's a small, second-hand tool that helps you hold a cabinet in place while you're screwing it in, and it makes things go much faster.

For your safety, make sure you have sturdy work gloves, a dust mask, and safety glasses. It's also a good idea to have a trash bag handy for any old paint or debris you find during the demo.

So, to recap, the main things to add are: a stud finder, a measuring tape, a utility knife, a helper's tool, and your safety gear. You're doing all the right things, and with these tools, you're going to have a great day. Just remember to take your time, double-check everything with the level, and you'll have a fantastic new kitchen.
