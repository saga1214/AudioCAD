Alright, let's break down the two tasks and the items you'll need. For the drywall repair, you've got drywall compound, sanding supplies, putty and paint, nails or adhesive, and possibly some molding. Let's say a small tub of compound is around 8 to 12 dollars. Sandpaper is a dollar or two. The primer and paint will probably be around 10 to 15 dollars. Nails are cheap, like a dollar, and the molding, if you need to replace it, could be anywhere from 10 to 30 dollars depending on the style. So, for the wall, you're probably looking at somewhere between 30 and 60 dollars.

Now for the cake. Flour, sugar, baking powder, butter, eggs, milk, and vanilla are all pretty cheap, maybe 10 to 15 dollars. The frosting ingredients, plus the pans, will probably add another 10 to 15 dollars. So the cake stuff should be around 20 to 30 dollars total.

Adding those two things up, you're looking at roughly 50 to 90 dollars for everything. I'd bring around 70 dollars with you to be safe, just in case you see something else you want to grab. That should cover it!
