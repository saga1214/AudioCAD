### **1. 20-Mile Daytime Segment (Mid-30s, Hilly, Aid Stations)**
*Focus: Comfort, moisture management, and light warmth.*  
**Essentials:**  
- **Moisture-wicking base layer** (top + bottom)  
- **Lightweight fleece or windbreaker** (for variable wind/sun)  
- **Trail running shoes** (broken-in, grippy soles)  
- **Light gloves** (for grip/hand warmth)  
- **Lightweight hat or cap** (sun protection)  
- **Headlamp** (for low-light sections)  
- **Small daypack or vest** (to carry layers)  
- **Extra socks** (to change into if feet get damp)  
- **Sunscreen/lip balm** (if sun is present)  

**Optional:**  
- Light windbreaker pocket  
- Reflector tape (for safety)  

---

### **2. 12-Mile Night Segment (Cold, Dark, Final Miles)**
*Focus: Warmth, visibility, and warmth retention.*  
**Essentials:**  
- **Moisture-wicking base layer**  
- **Insulated mid-layer** (fleece/puffy jacket)  
- **Waterproof/windproof outer shell** (critical for wind chill)  
- **Headlamp with extra batteries** (handheld + backup)  
- **Warm hat + gloves** (wool/synthetic, waterproof if possible)  
- **Trail shoes** (same as daytime)  
- **Lightweight pack/vest** (to stash layers)  
- **Extra socks** (wool blend, dry quickly)  
- **Hand warmers** (in pockets)  

**Optional:**  
- Reflective vest (for visibility)  
- Extra base layer (if planning to change)  
- Quick-dry towel (for post-race)  

---

### **Key Adjustments for Night Segment:**  
1. **Warmth First:** Prioritize insulation over breathability.  
2. **Visibility:** Headlamp + reflective gear is non-negotiable.  
3. **Layering:** Pack a dry base layer to swap into if needed.  
4. **Foot Care:** Wool socks prevent blisters in cold/damp conditions.
