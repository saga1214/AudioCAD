That's the best part—turning your coffee table into a snack command center! Since you prefer savory over sweet, here are some great vending machine options that’ll fit well on your table:

### 🥕 **Savory Staples**  
1. **Veggies & Hummus**: Carrot sticks, celery, bell peppers (10-12" long fit snugly along the 18" length).  
2. **Cheese & Crackers**: Single-serve cheese sticks or small crackers (fit perfectly in the 4" depth).  
3. **Beef Jerky/Turkey Sliders**: Great for protein—stack vertically or lay flat.  
4. **Pretzels/Chips**: Mini pretzels or single-serve chip bags (no spillage!).  

### 🍎 **Light & Fresh**  
5. **Fruit**: Banana, apple slices, or grapes (use a small bowl to contain them).  
6. **Olives/Nuts**: Single-serve packs fit in the drawer (4" deep) or on the table edge.  

### 🧀 **Unexpected Savory Bites**  
7. **Mini Meatballs**: If your vending machine has them (a fun IKEA-style touch!).  
8. **Cheese Curds**: Crispy and addictive—fit 4-6 in the 18" length.  

### 💡 **Pro Tips**  
- **Stack Smart**: Use small bowls or containers to corral smaller snacks (like nuts or olives).  
- **Drawer Power**: Store bulkier items (like hummus cups or jerky packs) in the drawer—no wasted space!  
- **Avoid Spills**: Place wet items (fruit, olives) on a paper towel-lined tray.  

### 🎉 **Lounge Vibe**  
Combine snacks with your coffee table’s vibe—maybe a mug of hot chocolate (if you change your mind!) or a drink. Now you’ve got a cozy snack station ready for movie nights or project breaks!
