Dealing with a difficult person—especially one impacting your family's safety and peace—can be incredibly draining. Here’s some actionable advice to help you navigate this stressfully:

### 1. **Prioritize Safety & Well-being**
   - **Protect the kids**: Ensure they know boundaries (e.g., "We don’t approach her house"). If she calls CPS again, document *everything* (dates, times, specific incidents) and share it with CPS if asked.  
   - **Seek support**: Talk to your school, pediatrician, or a trusted neighbor. If CPS is involved, cooperate fully—they’ll investigate.  

### 2. **Document Everything**
   - Keep a detailed log: Dates, times, what happened, witnesses, and any recordings (if legal in your area). This builds a case if authorities need it.  
   - Example: *"October 5, 2:30 PM –骑自行车时被报警，警察到场后确认无问题"*  

### 3. **Set Boundaries Calmly**
   - **Avoid confrontation**: Don’t engage emotionally. Use neutral language:  
     *"I’ve noticed you’ve called about the kids playing. They’re just enjoying safe activities. Let’s both help keep the neighborhood peaceful."*  
   - **Physical boundaries**: If safe, position kids away from her property.  

### 4. **Involve Authorities Strategically**
   - **Noise ordinances**: If noise is an issue, file a formal complaint with local authorities.  
   - **Nuisance laws**: In some areas, repeated false reports can be grounds for legal action against the neighbor.  
   - **CPS follow-up**: If CPS is involved, cooperate fully. They’ll assess the situation.  

### 5. **Emotional Self-Care**
   - **Gray rock method**: Be boring/unresponsive. Answer questions briefly if she approaches.  
   - **Vent safely**: Talk to friends, family, or a counselor. Isolation worsens stress.  
   - **Stay connected to joy**: Like you’re doing with your kids’ dress-up days and Halloween prep—keep those positive anchors strong.  

### 6. **Legal Options (If Needed)**
   - Consult a lawyer about nuisance laws or restraining orders if threats escalate.  
   - In extreme cases, some areas have programs to mediate neighbor disputes.  

### 7. **Focus on the Positive**
   - Your kids’ resilience this week (with school events and music) shows their strength. Celebrate those moments!  
   - Lean into Halloween prep—it’s a chance to build excitement and teamwork.  

> 💡 **Key Reminder**: You can’t control others, but you *can* control your response. Protecting your family’s peace is your priority. If stress feels overwhelming, reach out to a mental health professional—they can offer tailored coping strategies.
