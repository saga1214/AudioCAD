That's a great question! A glue gun not working "hot" is frustrating, especially when you're ready to create. Here are some common reasons and fixes:

**Possible Glue Gun Issues & Solutions:**

1.  **Overheating (Most Common):** If it's sitting unused for a long time, the glue can get too hot and solidify inside the nozzle or tip. **Fix:** Let it cool down completely (1-2 hours), then try reactivating it by heating the tip for 10-15 seconds on and off until it flows smoothly again.
2.  **Clogged Nozzle:** Residue can build up. **Fix:**
    *   **Clean Thoroughly:** Turn off the heat. Use a pin, toothpick, or small wire brush to carefully scrape out hardened glue. Soak the nozzle in warm, soapy water for 15-30 minutes to soften residue, then gently scrub with a toothpick or needle.
    *   **Reheat:** Once clean, turn the heat back on and heat the tip for 10-15 seconds repeatedly, allowing the melted glue to drip out.
3.  **Low Voltage:** If it's plugged into a low-voltage outlet (like a USB adapter instead of a wall adapter), it won't get hot enough. **Fix:** Ensure it's plugged directly into a standard wall outlet.
4.  **Low Glue Reservoir:** Check the glue tank. If it's too low, the tip might not get hot enough. **Fix:** Fill the tank adequately.
5.  **Cold Nozzle Tip:** The glue needs the nozzle tip itself to be hot. **Fix:** Ensure the tip is positioned correctly on the hot glue head and give it a few seconds to heat up before trying to use it.
