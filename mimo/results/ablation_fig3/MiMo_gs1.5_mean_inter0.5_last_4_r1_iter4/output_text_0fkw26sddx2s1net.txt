Alright, here's a shopping list for that Asian-inspired meal you're planning. I've broken it down by course and included notes for allergens and preferences.

First, for the starter, you'll need miso paste, either white or red. For the cucumber salad, you'll need a few cucumbers, some rice vinegar, sesame oil, soy sauce, sugar, and salt. You might also want some toasted sesame seeds.

For the main course, the chicken teriyaki meal, you'll need about a pound of boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs. For the teriyaki glaze, you'll need soy sauce, mirin, sake, brown sugar, and a bit of rice vinegar and grated ginger. For the stir-fried vegetables, you can pick any he likes, like bell peppers, snap peas, carrots, and broccoli. You'll also need some vegetable oil, minced garlic, and some salt and pepper.

For dessert, if you're making the mango sticky rice, you'll need sticky jasmine rice, fresh ripe mangoes, coconut milk, and sugar. If you decide to make the matcha ice cream instead, you'll need matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and maybe a splash of vanilla extract.

I also suggest grabbing some condiments like rice vinegar, soy sauce, toasted sesame oil, mirin, sake, sugar, and salt. And for seasonings, just make sure you have fresh ginger and garlic on hand.

A few final tips: Double-check his legume allergy to be sure. If you need sake, you could substitute with dry sherry or white wine. And for the chicken teriyaki, grill or pan-sear it to your preference. Enjoy your meal!
