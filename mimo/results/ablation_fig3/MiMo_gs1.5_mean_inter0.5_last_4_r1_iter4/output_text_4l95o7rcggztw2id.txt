**Shopping List: Full Course Pirate Party Menu**

**I. Appetizers & Snacks (Finger Foods, Packaged to Prevent Sand):**
*   **"Buried Treasure" Mini Sandwiches:** (60-70 mini slider boxes)
    *   Fillings: Ham & cheese, Cheddar & cucumber, Turkey & cheese, Peanut butter & jelly (check for nut allergy if needed).
    *   *Theme:* Pack in mini treasure chest boxes.
*   **"Pirate's Jewels" Veggie Sticks:** (1 bag baby carrots, 1 bag cucumber sticks, 1 bag bell pepper strips, 1 bag cherry tomatoes)
    *   *Theme:* Serve with ranch dip (check for allergy) in a small pirate-themed cup or container. Cut veggies into fun shapes if possible.
*   **"Gold Coins" Cheese Cubes:** (1 large block cheddar or mozzarella)
    *   *Theme:* Pack in a small treasure chest container. Include with the cheese dip.
*   **Pretzel Rods "Ship Masts":** (2 boxes)
    *   *Theme:* Bundle 3-4 rods together with a small piece of string or twine.
*   **"Treasure Chest" Fruit Skewers:** (2 boxes)
    *   *Theme:* Pack pre-cut fruit (melon balls - *avoid watermelon if allergy*, grapes, pineapple chunks, strawberries) in a small container. Thread onto skewers.
*   **Mini Pirate Treasure Cookies:** (2 boxes) - Chocolate chip or oatmeal raisin.
*   **"Pirate's Plunder" Nachos:** (1 large bag tortilla chips, 1 bag shredded cheese, 1 bag black beans, 1 bag corn kernels)
    *   *Theme:* Serve in a small treasure chest bowl or individual pirate cup containers. Top with salsa (check for allergy) and shredded cheese.

**II. Main Course (Easy, Packaged, Themed):**
*   **Mini Pirate Ship Pizzas:** (6-8 mini pizzas)
    *   *Theme:* Pre-make small pizzas on round cookie cutters (pirate ship, flag, skull shapes). Top simply (cheese, pepperoni, ham, veggie mix). Serve on small plates or in pirate cup containers.
*   **"Mermaid's Delight" Pasta Skewers:** (1 large bag pasta, 1 bag marinara sauce, 1 bag cheese cubes)
    *   *Theme:* Cook pasta, mix with sauce and cheese, thread onto skewers. Serve in a small container.

**III. Drinks (Individual, Leak-Proof):**
*   **Pirate Punch Cups:** (8 individual juice boxes - Apple, Grape, Orange)
*   **"Sea Water" Lemonade Cups:** (8 individual lemonade cups)
*   **"Cannonball" Water Bottles:** (8 individual water bottles - use fun pirate-themed bottles)
*   **Small Cups for Juice/Soda:** (8 small plastic cups - optional if using individual boxes)

**IV. Desserts (Themed & Easy):**
*   **"Treasure Chest" Cupcakes:** (1 box 9 cupcakes)
    *   *Theme:* Top with buttercream frosting, sprinkles, and a tiny pirate flag or sea salt (edible) on top. Pack in a small treasure chest box.
*   **"Ship in a Bottle" Cookies:** (1 box 12 cookies)
    *   *Theme:* Pack in a small container.
*   **Mini Gummy Sharks:** (1 box)
*   **Mini Chocolate Coins:** (1 bag - for "gold" treat)

**V. Allergy Safety & Essentials:**
*   **Watermelon-Free Labeling:** Clearly mark *all* packaged food/drinks with "NO WATERMELON" if used for the allergic child. Use separate containers/boxes for their items.
*   **Allergy Check:** Verify all packaged items (like cheese dip, ranch dip, lemonade, cookies) have no known allergens for the group.
*   **Snack Bags for Allergy Child:** Pack a separate bag with allergy-safe versions of snacks (e.g., fruit cups instead of melon, cheese cubes, pretzel rods) clearly labeled "SAFE FOR [Child's Name]".

**VI. Themed Serving & Packaging:**
*   **Treasure Chest Containers:** Small plastic treasure chest boxes for sandwiches, cheese, fruit, nachos, cupcakes.
*   **Pirate Cup Containers:** Small cups with pirate flags printed on the side for drinks.
*   **"Cannonball" Water Bottles:** Fun pirate-themed reusable bottles.
*   **"Ship in a Bottle" Cookie Boxes:** Pre-packaged cookie containers.
*   **"Buried Treasure" Sandwich Boxes:** Small boxes for mini slider fillings.
*   **String for Pretzel Rods:** Small rolls of twine or string.
*   **Small Plates & Utensils:** Plastic plates and forks for pizza/pasta.

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Portion Size:** Aim for kid-sized portions (mini slider boxes, small cups, bite-sized fruit).
*   **Packaging is Key:** Use individual containers, cups, and boxes to seal everything and prevent sand.
*   **Theme Integration:** Use treasure chests, pirate cups, flags, and ship motifs throughout.
*   **Allergy First:** Double-check labels and separate clearly for the allergic child.
