### **Feeding Solutions**

   - Use **microchip feeders** or **timed feeders** to ensure fussy cats eat and greedy cats don’t scarf everything.  
   - **Separate feeding spaces**: Keep fussy cats in a closed room during feeding times before you leave.  
2. **Stretch Food Budget**:  
   - Buy **large bags of quality food** (cheaper per serving).  
   - **Mix in safe fillers** (e.g., plain cooked rice/pasta) to supplement cheap food—consult a vet first.  
   - **Donate search**: Ask local shelters/food banks for pet food; online groups often share supplies.  

### **Health Maintenance (Low-Cost)**
1. **Prevent Problems**:  
   - **Daily hygiene**: Wash food/water bowls, keep litter boxes clean.  
   - **Basic nutrition**: Offer safe scraps (e.g., boiled chicken/fish) when possible.  
2. **Monitor Health**:  
   - Watch for early signs of illness (lethargy, weight loss).  
   - **Leverage low-cost clinics**: Use for emergencies only—prioritize spaying/neutering/vaccinations when affordable.
