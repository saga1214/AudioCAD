### **Tokyo (4 Days)**  
**Day 1: Arrival & Asakusa**  
- **Morning:** Arrive in Tokyo, check into hotel.  
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Asakusa** (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Market).  
- **Evening:** Dinner in Ueno Park or nearby.  

**Day 2: Modern Tokyo & Shibuya**  
- **Morning:** **Meiji Shrine** (Harajuku), **Takeshita Street** (harajuku fashion).  
- **Afternoon:** **Shibuya** (Scramble Crossing, Hachiko Statue).  
- **Evening:** **Shibuya Sky** sunset views (book tickets in advance).  

**Day 3: Culture & Nature**  
- **Morning:** **Ueno Park** (museums, zoo; focus on Tokyo National Museum).  
- **Afternoon:** **Yanaka Ginza** (old Tokyo vibes).  
- **Evening:** **Shinjuku Gyoen Garden** (cherry blossoms in spring).  

**Day 4: Day Trip to Nikko or Kamakura**  
- **Option A (Nikko):** UNESCO sites like Toshogu Shrine (2 hrs by train).  
- **Option B (Kamakura):** Great Buddha, Hasedera Temple (1 hr by train).  
- **Evening:** Return to Tokyo, dinner in Shibuya or Odaiba.  

---

### **Osaka (4 Days)**  
**Day 5: Arrival & Osaka Castle**  
- **Morning:** Arrive in Osaka, check into hotel.  
- **Afternoon:** **Osaka Castle** (museum inside), **Kawaramachi** shopping.  
- **Evening:** **Dotonbori** (street food, neon lights).  

**Day 6: Kyoto Day Trip**  
- **Morning:** **Shinkansen** to Kyoto (15 min).  
- **Afternoon:** **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion), **Ryoan-ji** (rock garden).  
- **Evening:** Return to Osaka, dinner at Kuromon Market.  

**Day 7: Nara Day Trip**  
- **Morning:** **Shinkansen** to Nara (30 min).  
- **Afternoon:** **Todai-ji Temple** (Great Buddha), **Nara Park** (deer).  
- **Evening:** Return to Osaka.  

**Day 8: Osaka Deep Dive**  
- **Morning:** **Osaka Museum of Housing & Living** (traditional homes).  
- **Afternoon:** **Umeda Sky Building** (city views), **Shinsekai** retro district.  
- **Evening:** **Kuromon Market** dinner.  

**Day 9: Departure**  
- **Morning:** Last-minute shopping in Namba.  
- **Afternoon:** Travel to airport.  

---
