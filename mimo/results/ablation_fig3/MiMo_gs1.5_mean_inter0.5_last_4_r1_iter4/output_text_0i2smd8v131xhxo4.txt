### 2. Reliable AI Tools
*   **Trimming Silence:** Audition itself has excellent "Noise Reduction" and "DeEsser" (for reducing sibilance/harshness). For dedicated AI silence removal:
    *   **Descript:** Excellent for editing audio *and* video using text. AI-powered "Edit Gaps" removes background noise/silence effectively.
    *   **Adobe Podcast Enhance:** Built into Audition/Adobe Creative Cloud. AI removes background noise, plosives, and reduces sibilance while preserving voice clarity. *Highly recommended.*
    *   **Krisp:** Real-time noise cancellation for calls, great for editing.
*   **Labeling:** **Descript** is again a standout. Its "Searchable Audio" feature lets you type words from the audio, instantly finding and labeling those segments. It's incredibly powerful for organizing long projects.

### 3. Balancing Recording, Editing, & Marketing
*   **Time Blocking:** Schedule dedicated blocks for recording, editing, admin/marketing, and personal time. Protect your recording/editing time fiercely.
*   **Batch Processing:** Record multiple takes for different characters or scenes in one session. Edit similar projects back-to-back.
*   **Automate Admin:** Use tools like HoneyBook or Dubsado for client communication, contracts, invoices, and scheduling.
*   **Set Boundaries:** Define clear work hours. Communicate them to clients. Turn off work notifications after hours.
*   **Delegate:** Outsource tasks like simple editing, social media posting, or graphic design if possible.
*   **Take Real Breaks:** Schedule lunch, walks, and downtime *between* work blocks, not just at the end of the day. Your brain and voice need recovery.
