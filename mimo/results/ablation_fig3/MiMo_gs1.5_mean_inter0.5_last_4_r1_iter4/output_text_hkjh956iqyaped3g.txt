For a 15-minute break, especially as your last one, here are some balanced options that prioritize recharging without disrupting your workflow:

1.  **Micro-Meditation (5 mins):** Focus on your breath for a few minutes. Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and take slow, deep breaths. Notice the sensation of air entering and leaving your nostrils, or the rise and fall of your chest. This is quick, effective, and won't leave you drowsy.
2.  **Quick Stretch & Deep Breathing (5-10 mins):** Stand up and gently stretch your arms overhead, roll your shoulders, stretch your legs (touch your toes if comfortable), and twist gently. Pair this with deep, diaphragmatic breathing (inhale deeply through the nose, exhale slowly through the mouth). This relieves physical tension and boosts oxygen.
3.  **Short, Uplifting Walk (10-12 mins):** If possible, step outside for a brisk walk around the block. Fresh air and movement are incredibly energizing and refreshing. Focus on the sensory experience – the air, sounds, sights.
4.  **Mindful Coffee/Tea (5-7 mins):** If you want a drink, make it a ritual. Focus fully on the process: the aroma as you pour, the warmth of the cup, the taste as you sip slowly. This mindful pause can be very grounding.
5.  **Listen to a Short, Energizing Song (5 mins):** As you mentioned enjoying Ali Farka Touré, find one of his songs you love and listen intently. Let it wash over you for a few minutes, focusing on the melody or rhythm. This can be a great mental reset.

**Things to Avoid for a 15-minute break:**

*   **Power Nap:** 15 minutes is often too short to enter deep sleep and can sometimes leave you feeling groggy ("sleep inertia") if you don't reach the REM cycle. If you nap, try for 20-30 minutes or a longer break.
*   **Deep Work Session:** It's likely too short to make a significant dent in focused work, and it might be mentally taxing after a long day.
*   **Scrolling Social Media/News:** This is often more draining than refreshing.

**Recommendation:** Given it's your last break, **Option 1 (Micro-Meditation)** or **Option 2 (Quick Stretch & Deep Breathing)** are excellent choices. They are quick, effective at resetting your nervous system, and won't leave you feeling disconnected or drowsy when you return to work. **Option 3 (Short Walk)** is fantastic if you can get outside.

Choose what feels most restorative to *you* right now!
