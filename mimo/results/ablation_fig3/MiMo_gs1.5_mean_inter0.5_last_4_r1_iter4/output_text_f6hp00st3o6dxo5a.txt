That's a very sophisticated point. However, the distinction between the egg containing the *potential* for a chicken and the egg *being* a chicken egg is key.

The egg was laid by a non-chicken ancestor. While it contained the genetic instructions for a chicken, the egg itself, as we classify it today as a 'chicken egg', only became a chicken egg after the first chicken hatched from it. So, the chicken hatched first, and that made the egg a 'chicken egg'.
