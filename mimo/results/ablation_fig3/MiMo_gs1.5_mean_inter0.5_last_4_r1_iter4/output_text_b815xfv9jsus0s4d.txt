Based on your situation and the core mechanics of TikTok's algorithm, **engaging content** is the **single most important factor** for success. Here's why:

### Why Engaging Content Wins
1. **Algorithm-Driven Discovery**: TikTok’s algorithm prioritizes **watch time and retention**. If your video hooks viewers instantly (within the first 3 seconds) and keeps them watching until the end, the algorithm will push it to *more* people organically.  
2. **Overcomes Visibility Barriers**: Even if your video is technically "perfect" (guidelines-compliant, concise, consistent), **no one will see it if it fails to engage**. Engagement (likes, shares, comments) is the primary signal for TikTok to expand reach.  
3. **Addresses Your Core Issue**: You mentioned videos "aren’t getting out there"—this usually means **low retention rates**. TikTok won’t amplify content that doesn’t hold attention.  

### Why Other Factors Are Secondary
- **Following Guidelines**: Crucial to avoid bans/restrictions (as you experienced), but it’s a *prerequisite*, not a growth driver.  
- **Conciseness**: Shorter videos *help* retention, but only if they’re **intrinsically engaging**. A 15-second video won’t perform if it’s forgettable.  
- **Consistency**: Important for algorithm trust, but **10 mediocre videos won’t beat 1 viral, engaging one**.  
- **Description/Hashtags**: Help with discoverability, but **weak content won’t convert views into engagement**.  

### Actionable Takeaway
Focus **first and foremost** on crafting content that answers:  
- **"What’s the immediate payoff for the viewer?"** (e.g., humor, surprise, relatability, or curiosity).  
- **"How can I keep them watching *through* the first 3 seconds?"** (e.g., dynamic visuals, bold captions, or a provocative hook).  

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Test hooks rigorously. If 80% of your videos don’t gain traction after 3 seconds, refine your hooks until they do. **Engagement is the gateway to everything else on TikTok.**
