Of course! I'd be happy to. For an edible garden in Zone 10B, like St. Pete, here are the key plants I'd recommend:

First up, herbs are your best friends. They're fast-growing and you can use them right away. Think about basil, mint, parsley, cilantro, chives, and thyme. 

Then, you've got leafy greens. Things like lettuce, spinach, kale, and Swiss chard. They grow quickly and are super versatile. Radishes are a great bonus here because they're ready in just a few weeks.

And finally, for fruiting vegetables, tomatoes and peppers are perfect. They're very forgiving, produce a ton of fruit, and they love the heat. 

I'd say start with one or two of each category—maybe basil and tomatoes—to see what you like best. You can always add more later. Happy gardening!
