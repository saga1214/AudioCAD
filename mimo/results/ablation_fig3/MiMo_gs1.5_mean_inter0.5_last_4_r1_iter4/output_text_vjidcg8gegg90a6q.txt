For your five-week-old chicks, the best bedding is wood shavings. It's a soft, insulating material that's also easy to clean. Just be sure to avoid pine shavings that contain phenols, as they can be irritating to the chicks. 

The most important thing is to keep the coop dry. So, on top of the wood shavings, you can place a thin layer of deep litter, which will compost and generate some natural warmth as it breaks down. This system works very well together.
