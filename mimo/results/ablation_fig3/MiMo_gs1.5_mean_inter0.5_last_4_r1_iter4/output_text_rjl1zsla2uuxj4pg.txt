Great job so far! Here’s a quick checklist of essentials to round out your preparation for bringing home a short-haired cat:

### 🐾 **Essential Additions:**
1. **Scratching Post**  
   - Prevents furniture damage and keeps claws healthy. Look for sturdy, horizontal surfaces (e.g., sisal rope or carpet-covered posts).

2. **Carrier**  
   - Use a hard-sided carrier for transport (soft carriers aren’t ideal for travel). Line it with a towel or blanket for comfort.

3. **Cat Tree/Concierge**  
   - Provides vertical space for climbing, perching, and scratching. Great for observation and security.

4. **Scratching Board**  
   - As a supplement to a scratching post, offers an alternative for claw maintenance.

5. **Cat-Safe Water Fountain**  
   - Encourages hydration and keeps water fresh (already on your list—perfect!).

### 🛠️ **Supplementary Items:**
- **Litter Box Supplies**  
  - Extra scooping scoop, litter bags, and a replacement tray liner for easy cleaning.
- **Kitten-Appropriate Food & Water Bowls**  
  - Wide, shallow bowls (prevents whisker fatigue). Consider stainless steel or ceramic.
- **Cat-Safe Plants**  
  - Safe options: Spider Plant, Boston Fern, or Catnip. Avoid toxic plants like lilies or poinsettias.
- **Grooming Tools**  
  - Soft-bristle brush, nail clippers, and a toothbrush/toothpaste (specifically for cats).
- **Cat Bed**  
  - Compact, cozy bed with non-slip padding. Some cats prefer cardboard boxes!

### 📋 **Pre-Adoption Checklist:**
- **Home Safety**  
  - Cover electrical cords, secure trash cans, and remove small hazards (e.g., electrical outlets, toxic plants).
- **First Vet Visit**  
  - Schedule an exam and discuss vaccinations/parasite prevention.
- **Emergency Contacts**  
  - Save your vet’s number and local emergency vet clinic.

### 💡 Pro Tip:  
Short-haired cats (like **Siamese**, **American Shorthair**, or **Maine Coon**) are generally low-maintenance but still benefit from regular brushing (1-2x/week) to prevent hairballs.
