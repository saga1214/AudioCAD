For a formal crowd who enjoys drinks, keep it refined. For the vodka, you could mix it with fresh juice like cranberry or grapefruit, and add a splash of soda water and maybe a garnish of fresh mint or a citrus twist to make it look nice. For the Argentine fernet, you can serve it with soda water and lime, or maybe on the rocks. If you want to be fancy, you could offer a small glass of it neat for those who really like the bold flavor. Both options should work well with half vegetarian and omnivore guests. Have fun!
