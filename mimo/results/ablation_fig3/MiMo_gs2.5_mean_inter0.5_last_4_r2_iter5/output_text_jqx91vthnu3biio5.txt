### 🍝 1. **One-Pot Lemon Garlic Shrimp & Orzo**  
   - **Why it fits**: Quick (20 mins), protein-packed, and brightens your workspace glow.  
   - **How**: Sauté garlic, add shrimp, white wine, lemon zest, and orzo. Simmer until pasta softens. Finish with parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.  
   - *Bonus*: Minimal cleanup after replacing your coffee machine!

### 🥗 2. **No-Cook Mediterranean Grain Bowl**  
   - **Why it fits**: Zero-cook = low-effort after meetings. Packed with fresh ingredients to match your snack cravings.  
   - **How**: Layer quinoa (or farro), chopped cucumber, cherry tomatoes, Kalamata olives, red onion, feta, and lemon-tahini dressing.  
   - *Tip*: Use leftover roasted veggies if you meal-prepped!

### 🥪 3. **Pan-Seared Salmon with Roasted Spiced Potatoes**  
   - **Why it fits**: Healthy fats for brain fog recovery. Spiced potatoes echo window-facing warmth (like sun-kissed flavor).  
   - **How**: Season salmon with paprika/garlic powder, pan-sear 6 mins/side. Roast potatoes with rosemary and olive oil.  
   - *Pro move*: Serve with a Greek salad for crunch.

### 🍝 4. **Spaghetti Aglio e Olio with Chicken**  
   - **Why it fits**: Classic comfort food that’s deeply Italian-Mediterranean adjacent. Feels indulgent but light.  
   - **How**: Sauté chicken breast in olive oil, set aside. Cook spaghetti with garlic, red pepper flakes, and olive oil. Toss together + parsley.  
   - *Upgrade*: Add a splash of white wine to the garlic oil.
