Of course, I can give you a clear breakdown. Here's a standard, effective format you can follow.

First is your Header. Center your name in about a fourteen-point font. To the right, or aligned directly under your name, list your email, phone number, city, and a link to your LinkedIn in nine-point font. Icons avoid ATS issues, so skip those.

Next is your Professional Summary. This is a short, powerful paragraph at the top that highlights your most relevant skills and career goals, especially for roles you're moving into.

After that, list your Experience. This is where you put your most relevant jobs first, followed by older ones under a section called "Additional Experience". For each job, list the employer, your title, and the dates. You only need a few bullet points to describe your key accomplishments.

Then comes your Education. List your degrees and where you got them. Awards and honors, like scholarships or graduating with Highest Honors, should be listed right directly under the specific degree where you received them.

Finally, you can have a Skills section. This is crucial, but keep it relevant to the job description. You can list them in categories like Technical Skills or Soft Skills.

This order—Header, Summary, Relevant Experience, Education with integrated Awards, and a Skills Section—is a very standard and effective structure that makes it easy for any recruiter to quickly find what they need.
