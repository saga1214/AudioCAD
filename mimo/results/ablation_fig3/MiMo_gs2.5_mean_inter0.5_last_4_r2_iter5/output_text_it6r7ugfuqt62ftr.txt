Absolutely! Since warm water and swimming are your goals, Big Sur and Pismo Beach are perfect choices. They usually stay mild and beautiful this time of year and have lots of beaches perfect for swimming.

Here’s a full four-day itinerary for Northern California warmth.

Let's start with where to stay. I'd highly recommend an Airbnb in Pismo Beach. You can find lovely beachfront homes or condos that have kitchens, which is super helpful if you need to control the gluten-free food. You'll find lots of options with great reviews.

For your packing bag, since you want to wear your bathing suit, remember to pack two or three swimsuits, and a couple of cover-ups.

For daytime outfits, think light tank tops, shorts, sundresses, and sandals or flip-flops. A light sweater or jacket is still a good idea, as it can get breezy or cool in the evenings.

A few other essentials are sunglasses, a hat, and definitely your sunscreen.

Have a truly relaxing getaway
