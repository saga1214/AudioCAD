For the family gathering, since you’re ditching the mashed potatoes for two roasted dishes, you’ll definitely need another neutral vegetable side. Maybe green beans sautéed without green peppers? Or zucchini and yellow squash, which are usually mild enough for picky eaters. A simple, buttery corn on the cob could also be a crowd-pleaser.

For your girlfriends' gathering, a potato bar with both meat and plant-based options is a great idea. For meat eaters, pulled pork or shredded chicken are classic potato toppings. For your vegetarian friend and anyone on a plant-based diet, that BBQ pulled jackfruit or a hearty lentil-walnut mix would be a great non-meat alternative.

For the esquites modification, lime, chili, and fresh herbs are definitely the way to go. You could also add some plant-based pico de gallo for freshness.

For the work gathering taco and side options, it sounds like a solid plan. Yuca is plant-based and gluten-free, Chipotle chicken can be checked to make sure it's gluten-free and plant-based compliant, and carnitas are just pork, so you’ll just need to verify they don’t contain any allergens. Just label everything clearly.
