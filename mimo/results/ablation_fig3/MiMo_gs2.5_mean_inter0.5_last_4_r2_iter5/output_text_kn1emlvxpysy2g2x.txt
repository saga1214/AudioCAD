Here's a comprehensive comparison of all the wines we've discussed, with their optimal storage and serving temperatures:

### **High-End Pinot Noir**  
- **Stored at:** 55°F (13°C, cellar temp)  
- **Served at:** 55–60°F (13–16°C, Slightly cool)  
- *Best for weather:* **Cold to cool weather** (e.g., crisp autumn evenings). Slightly chilled to highlight bright red fruit and earthy notes.

### **Italian Wine (e.g., Chianti, Sangiovese)**  
- **Stored at:** 55°F (13°C, cellar temp)  
- **Served at:** 60–65°F (16–18°C, Above cellar)  
- *Best for weather:* **Cold weather** (e.g., winter dinners). Warmed slightly above cellar temp to open up tannins and cherry notes.

### **Sauvignon Grigio (Pinot Grigio)**  
- **Stored at:** 50°F (10°C, cellar temp)  
- **Served at:** 45–50°F (7–10°C, Chilled)  
- *Best for weather:* **Hot weather** (e.g., summer picnics). Ice-cold to emphasize citrus/grapefruit acidity and refreshment.

### **Mulled Wine (e.g., Spiced Red Wine)**  
- **Stored at:** Normal cellar temp (~55°F / 13°C before warming)  
- **Served at:** 140–160°F (60–70°C, Hot)  
- *Best for weather:* **Cold weather** (e.g., winter gatherings). Warming temperatures enhance spice/fruit aromas.

### **Japanese Hon-Mirin**  
- **Stored at:** 60–68°F (16–20°C, Cool place)  
- **Served at:** 68–75°F (20–24°C, Room temp for tasting; cooking as needed)  
- *Best for weather:* **Neutral** (not temperature-sensitive). Room temp preserves delicate sweetness when sipped; ideal for cooking at any temp.
