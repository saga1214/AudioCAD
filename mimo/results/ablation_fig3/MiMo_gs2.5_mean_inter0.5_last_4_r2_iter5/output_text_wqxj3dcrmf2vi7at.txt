Of course! To recap all the delicious homemade and store-bought options for your little princess:

For homemade treats, you could make frozen bites with mashed pumpkin or sweet potato. Peanut butter and oat cookies are another great choice, but only with peanut butter that doesn't contain xylitol. Cooked chicken pieces and simple carrot sticks are also healthy, simple snacks.

For store-bought brands, popular ones include Blue Buffalo, Wellness, and Zuke's. Merrick and Greenies or Merrick are also good options to look for.

And remember, portion control is important, so keep the portions small to keep her fit! With so many choices, she's sure to find something she loves.
