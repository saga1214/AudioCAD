- **1 block cheese**: ~300 kcal

### Dinner:  
- **1 dehydrated backpacking meal** (e.g., pasta, mac & cheese, or lentils): ~800–1,000 kcal  

### Hydration:  
- **Water**: 3–4 liters (replenish with electrolyte powder if altitude-sensitive)  

### **Total Calories**: ~2,550–3,900 kcal  
*(Adjust portions based on your weight—e.g., heavier hikers may need 10–20% more)*  

### Key Tips:  
1. **Carb-heavy**: Focus on bread, peanut butter, and dehydrated meals for sustained energy.  
2. Fruit provides quick natural sugars between meals.  
3. **Pack lightweight**: Use single-serving tortillas, pre-portioned bread/cheese, and freeze-dried dinners.  
4. **Altitude prep**: Add extra 300–500 kcal if trails are steep or you feel acclimated slowly.
