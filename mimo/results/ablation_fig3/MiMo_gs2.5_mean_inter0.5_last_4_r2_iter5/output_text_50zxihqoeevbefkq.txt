Alright, I've got you. You've got 20 adults and 4 kids coming for New Year's Eve. Let's get you a shopping list based on what we've decided.

First, for appetizers and finger food: Mini Ham Sliders, Shrimp Cocktail (with cocktail sauce), Pigs in Blanket, and Rice Crackers with Cream Cheese and Cucumber.

Drinks: Champagne for the toast, Signature Cocktail ingredients—which you can choose based on your preference—the kids' drink will now be Grape Juice served in kid flutes, and don't forget water and ice.

For the main course: The ham. You'll need to buy a pre-cooked ham and honey glaze for it. For your cleaner vegetable side, we'll go with Roasted Carrots olive-oiled and salted, Mashed Scalloped Potatoes, and Roasted Asparagus with olive oil and lemon.

Now for some kid activities so they don't get restless. A coloring book and crayons are always a good bet. You could also set up a simple craft station with paper plates and noisemakers for making party hats.

For music, I'll suggest a modern New Year's Eve playlist later if you'd like.

Okay, let's break this down into grocery and household categories for you.

For groceries:
Produce: You'll need cucumbers, potatoes, carrots (but plain, not glazed), asparagus, grape juice, and any ingredients for the signature cocktail.
Meat and Seafood: A couple of pre-cooked hams, shrimp for the cocktail, and ground pork for the pigs in a blanket.
Dairy: A few pounds of butter for the potatoes, cream cheese, and some sour cream or mayo for the pigs in a blanket.
Pantry: You'll need scalloped potatoes, cocktail sauce, honey, olive oil, lemon, rice crackers, and any other cocktail mixers you need.

For household items:
Decorations: Balloons, kid hats or noisemakers, and the special plastic flutes for the grape juice.
Supplies: Plates, cups including the flutes, napkins, utensils, and coloring/craft supplies for the kids.
Other: Aluminum foil and a roasting pan for the carrots, and of course, plenty of trash bags for cleanup.
