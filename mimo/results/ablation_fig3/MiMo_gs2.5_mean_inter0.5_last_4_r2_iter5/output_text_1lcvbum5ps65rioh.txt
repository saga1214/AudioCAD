For BC U-turns, if you violate these rules, you’ll get a ticket for unsafe or prohibited U-turns, which can be around 115 dollars. If you do it at an intersection with lights when it's not allowed, it's a more serious offense. The easiest way to avoid trouble is to look for signs and only turn when you have plenty of visibility. 

As for your spaghetti carbonara, you'll need things like spaghetti, eggs, grated Pecorino Romano or Parmesan cheese, pancetta or guanciale, black pepper, and garlic. Some people also add a little cream, but it's optional. You'll cook the pasta and the pancetta separately, then mix them together with the egg and cheese mixture off the heat so the eggs cook gently.
