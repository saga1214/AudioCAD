**Key Notes:**
1. **Big Box Store with Best Selection:** **The Home Depot** (especially for angle grinder wheels, drywall screws, and insulation)  
2. **Smaller Store Options:** Ace Hardware (great for drywall screws/nails), Tractor Supply Co. (metal wheels/jigsaws), Menards (Indiana-based chain with good metal tools)

4. **Drywall Screw Advantage:** Prevents "nail pops" (screws won’t back out) and creates ideal dimples for mud adhesion.
5. **Mineral Wool Alternative:** Best for soundproofing and fire resistance but bulkier.

**Quick Tip:** For sheet curves with snips, grip the metal firmly near the tip for cleaner cuts. Always wear cut-resistant gloves with metal tools!
