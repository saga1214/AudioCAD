Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids definitely requires a lot of food and drink! Let me break this down into categories to make sure everyone has clean, easy-to-grab options for a mess-free theme park day.

First, for drinks, hydration is key. You'll definitely need thirty large water bottles, one for each person. For the kids, add thirty low-sugar juice boxes. For the dads who want a treat, thirty sugar-free sodas should cover the group. And always have a cooler with ice to keep everything cold.

Next, for snacks before meals, it's good to have a variety of low-mess options. Things like cheese sticks, grapes, individual pretzels bags, trail mix, baby carrots with single hummus cups, and some energy bars are all great choices.

For the main meals, mini calzones from a pizzeria are a fantastic idea since he loves calzones. Just ask them to be cut into individual portions and packed in airtight containers. Sandwiches or wraps cut into quarters would also be super easy for everyone to grab.

And for dessert, cupcakes are probably the cleanest and easiest celebration food. Brownies in a container are another great low-mess option.

So, to recap, the goal is to use coolers, seal food in containers, and pack wet wipes. This shopping list should give you and all your friends a fantastic, stress-free bachelor party. Have a great time!
