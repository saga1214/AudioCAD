For Halloween candy, you’ll definitely want to go with the classics. Some of the most popular and safe bets include Reese’s Cups, Hershey’s Kisses, or any classic Hershey’s chocolate bar. You could also get gummies, like Starbursts or Skittles, or maybe some Sour Patch Kids. If you want something a bit fancier, you could look for candy apples or pumpkin-shaped chocolates. You could also grab some of those individually wrapped lollipops. Just try to avoid anything with artificial dyes due to your earlier concern, so maybe stick to natural-colored candies. And definitely get some of the classic little bags of candy that people love to take home. You can’t go wrong with any of those classics!
