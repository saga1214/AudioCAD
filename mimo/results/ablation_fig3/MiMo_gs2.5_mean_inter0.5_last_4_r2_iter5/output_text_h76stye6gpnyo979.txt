It sounds like you love travel and home projects.

Here’s a simple way to tackle them at the same time. First, match each adventure to a specific savings account or category. Bolivia salt flats could go toward apartment down payments, Faroe Islands puffins could go toward the home renovation fund, and salt crusted caves could also go into the home fund.

Then, create a small, fixed monthly saving amount just for each travel goal. You could even automate the transfers so you don't have to think about it.

This method helps you make clear progress on each big dream, even with other demands.
