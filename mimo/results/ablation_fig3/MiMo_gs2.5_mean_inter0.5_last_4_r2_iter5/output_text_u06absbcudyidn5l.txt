Okay, let's break down chronic skin redness and vasculitis.

Besides rosacea, sun damage over your lifetime is a very common cause of redness. Genetics, contact dermatitis, or conditions like lupus can also be the culprit.

To understand vasculitis better, think of it as inflammation in the blood vessels themselves. Depending on which size of vessel is affected, the symptoms can be very different. Vasculitis is essentially an autoimmune issue where your body's defense system mistakenly attacks its own blood vessels.

For hair and nails, supporting building blocks like collagen peptides, keratin, iron, and zinc is a good approach.

For inflammation, ibuprofen *can* help with short-term pain, but starting a regular regimen requires a doctor's approval to rule out the underlying cause. They can figure out the root problem and create the right plan for you.
