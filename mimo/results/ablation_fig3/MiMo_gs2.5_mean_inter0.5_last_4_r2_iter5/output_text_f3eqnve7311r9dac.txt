Buying original books from the 1940s and 50s is a high-risk, high-reward strategy.

Many of them are in terrible condition and can be very difficult to authenticate. You could easily overpay for a common book or get a fake. For the truly valuable ones, you'll need to pay a premium to auction houses to ensure the grade and authenticity are correct.

And finally, *Fantastic Four* number one hundred and twenty-five is the first appearance of Doctor Doom in his own solo series. These are all extremely rare and valuable.
