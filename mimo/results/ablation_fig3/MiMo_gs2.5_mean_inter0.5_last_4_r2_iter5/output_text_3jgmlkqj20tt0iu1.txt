Initally contacting your boss should be scheduled in advance if you can. You could request a brief meeting by sending a professional email. Something like, "Hi, I'd like to schedule some time to discuss my performance and career development. Would you have 20 to 30 minutes available sometime next week?"

Being prepared will help reduce your anxiety. You can anticipate that your boss will want to see the data to back up your case, so make sure you have your performance reviews, any metrics for your achievements, and specific examples of your expanded responsibilities all organized and ready to share.
