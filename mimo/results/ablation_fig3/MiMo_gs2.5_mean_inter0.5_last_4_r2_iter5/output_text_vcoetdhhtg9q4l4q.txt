That's a great next step! Here are some quick dinner ingredient ideas that keep things simple and reduce prep time:

### 🥗 Pantry Staples (Use what you already have!):
- **Grains:** Rice, pasta, quinoa, couscous  
- **Legumes:** Canned beans (black, chickpeas, lentils)  
- **Proteins:** Eggs, canned tuna/salmon, tofu, or leftover cooked chicken  
- **Veggies:** Frozen mixed veggies, spinach, bell peppers, or jarred olives  
- **Seasonings:** Soy sauce, olive oil, garlic powder, lemon juice  

### 🥬 Quick Assembly Ideas:
1. **Stir-fry:** Toss frozen veggies + canned edamame + pre-cooked rice with soy sauce, ginger, and sesame oil.  
2. **Bean & Veggie Bowl:** Warm canned beans, top with spinach, cherry tomatoes, avocado, and a squeeze of lime.  
3. **Pasta:** Cook pasta, mix with pesto or olive oil, add canned tuna or chickpeas, and top with grated parmesan.  
4. **Breakfast-for-Dinner:** Scrambled eggs + toast + sautéed spinach or mushrooms.  

### ⏱️ Time-Savers:
- **Pre-cut veggies** (store-bought or frozen)  
- **Pre-cooked grains** (like microwaveable quinoa pouches)  
- **Frozen fruit** for smoothies or oatmeal  
- **Herb substitutes** (dried herbs or pre-chopped basil/oregano)  

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Before buying new ingredients, ask: *"Do I already have this in my pantry/fridge?"* This aligns perfectly with your decluttering goals!
