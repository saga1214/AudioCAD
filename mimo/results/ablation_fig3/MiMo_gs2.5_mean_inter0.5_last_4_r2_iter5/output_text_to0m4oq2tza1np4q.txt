That’s a fantastic dinner combo! Pickles can add a fun twist—here are a few heart-healthy, low-sodium ideas using pickle juice (remember, pickles are naturally high in sodium, so moderation is key for bloating concerns):  

### 🧄 Quick Pickle Juice Marinade:  
1. **Pickle Juice "Dressing"**: Mix pickle juice with a splash of olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a pinch of black pepper. Toss with greens or grilled salmon/chicken for a tangy, crunchy side.  
2. **Pickle Juice Marinade**: Use the juice to marinate tofu, shrimp, or chicken for 15–30 minutes before cooking. (The acid tenderizes protein!)  
3. **Salmon "Pickled" Glaze**: Brush pickle juice (diluted with water) onto salmon before baking/pan-searing for a bright, savory finish.  

### 💧 Smart Tips to Reduce Sodium Impact:  
- **Rinse pickle juice** before using it in marinades or dressings.  
- **Pair with high-fiber veggies** (like your leafy greens) to help balance sodium absorption.  
- **Limit added salt** elsewhere in your meal to offset the sodium.
