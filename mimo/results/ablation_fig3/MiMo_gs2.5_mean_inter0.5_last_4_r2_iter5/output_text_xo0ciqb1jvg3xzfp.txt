For the Pilgrims, it was likely a moment of genuine relief and gratitude after surviving starvation.

For the Wampanoag, the perspective is very different. For them, it wasn't a "happy occasion." It was a political event with a Native people who had just been decimated by disease, and they were forming an alliance with a new, powerful, and unfamiliar group of settlers.

So, it was almost certainly not a happy occasion for both groups at the same time.
