Okay, you went from skeptical of your vegetarian sister’s taste to actually recommending podcasts to each other—that’s a win! Now you’re stepping it up with a homemade birthday dinner. Even better. Since she’s a vegetarian, let’s focus on meals that are both satisfying and feel special. Here are a few ideas:

1. **Mushroom Risotto** – Creamy, rich, and packed with umami from wild mushrooms. You can top it with truffle oil or parmesan (if she’s okay with dairy).  

2. **Stuffed Bell Peppers** – Bell peppers stuffed with quinoa, lentils, and spices like cumin or paprika. Add some tahini or feta for a creamy/salty contrast.  

3. **Tofu Banh Mi** – If you’re feeling adventurous, this Vietnamese-inspired dish with crispy tofu, pickled veggies, and cilantro on baguette is super trendy and delicious.  

4. **Vegetable Lasagna** – Spinach, zucchini, eggplant, and ricotta layers with tomato sauce. It’s hearty and feels like a comfort meal.  

5. **Grain Bowl** – A colorful mix of roasted veggies, quinoa, chickpeas, avocado, and a tahini dressing. Super customizable.  

Bonus: If you want to impress, try making a vegan dessert like chocolate avocado mousse or a berry crumble. Shows you’re putting in extra effort!  

Since you’re skeptical of her taste, maybe ask her what she’s craving first? Or surprise her with something you think she’s never tried but would love. Either way, you’re clearly making an effort—she’ll appreciate it. Enjoy the dinner!
