Okay, let me clarify that for you. I did get a bit confusing earlier. The truly original Boulevardier actually uses the one-to-one-to-one ratio I just mentioned for bourbon/rye at one ounce, plus one ounce of sweet vermouth and one of Campari. The one and a quarter to one ounce bourbon-to-vermouth ratio is actually a very common variation people call it, so your pro bartender friend was probably using the original recipe! As for the Last Word, that's an equal-parts drink with three-quarters of an ounce of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. Hope that clears things up for your party!
