That sounds like a fantastic themed evening! Here are some ocean-themed mains and desserts tailored to your needs:

### Ocean-Themed Mains (Shellfish-Free, Diabetic-Friendly, Spicy Option)
1. **Spicy Mahi Mahi with Mango Salsa**  
   *Pan-seared mahi mahi (baked for low sodium)* with a light chili-lime glaze. Serve with mango-pineapple salsa (no shellfish).  
   *Why it works:* Lean fish, low-carb, customizable spice level. Use quinoa instead of rice to control carbs.

2. **Grilled Shrimp Scallops with Coconut-Curry Rice**  
   *Honey-garlic shrimp* (no shellfish) with saffron coconut rice. Add a mango-pineapple relish for sweetness.  
   *Why it works:* High-protein, low-carb options, incorporates mango. Avoid heavy sauces.

3. **"Ocean" Tofu Tacos**  
   *Spicy black bean & avocado crema* on corn tortillas (low-carb). Top with crispy baked tofu, cucumber, and mango-pickled onions.  
   *Why it works:* Plant-based, diabetic-friendly, vibrant flavors. Add mango for shared love.

4. **Diabetic-Friendly "Fish" Ceviche**  
   *Cucumber-jicama "fish" salad* (tuna or shrimp, cooked with lime juice) with mango-pineapple, jalapeño, and cilantro.  
   *Why it works:* No shellfish, fresh, low-carb, and spicy. Use only citrus-based dressing.

5. **Mango-Curry Shrimp with Cauliflower Rice**  
   *Jerk-spiced shrimp* in a coconut-mango curry broth. Serve over cauliflower rice.  
   *Why it works:* Uses mango, controls carbs with cauliflower base. Skip heavy cream.

### Ocean-Themed Desserts (Sweet & Light)
1. **Mango-Coconut Rice Pudding**  
   *Simmered coconut milk, rice, and fresh mango* with a hint of cinnamon. Top with toasted coconut flakes.  
   *Why it works:* Naturally diabetic-friendly (low sugar), uses mango, no added sugar.

2. **Grilled Pineapple "Fruit" with Honey-Lime**  
   *Grilled pineapple rings* brushed with honey-lime glaze. Serve with coconut whipped cream.  
   *Why it works:* Low-carb, no added sugar, ocean-inspired presentation.

3. **Ocean Blue Cheesecake**  
   *Lightened version:* Greek yogurt base, fresh blueberries, and a drizzle of honey-lime syrup.  
   *Why it works:* Rich but controlled sugar, blueberry "waves" add color.

4. **Coconut Rice Pudding with Mango**  
   *Creamy coconut rice pudding* with diced mango and a sprinkle of toasted coconut.  
   *Why it shared love for mango and coconut.*

5. **"Fish" Candy & Coconut Ice Cream**  
   *Light coconut fish candy* (tuna or shrimp, coated in sugar-free coconut glaze). Serve with coconut ice cream and mango chunks.  
   *Why it works:* Interactive, lightened sweet version of traditional "fish candy." Avoid shellfish if she's sensitive.

### Key Tips:
- **Diabetic Focus:** Use fresh fruit (mango, pineapple), control carbs (cauliflower rice, quinoa), avoid shellfish, and use spices/herbs for flavor instead of salt/sugar.
- **Theme Boost:** Garnish with edible "seaweed" (spinach), seashell-shaped cookie cutters, or blue/green accents.
- **Spice Control:** Adjust jalapeños/sriracha to your tolerance.
