For stir-fry noodles, the single most important thing is to use metal tongs. Wood and plastic don't belong in a hot stir-fry wok. And definitely no wooden spoons this time!

Next, make sure you salt your water generously. Stir-fried noodles benefit from being a bit firm, so you can cook them for a minute or two longer than the package says. The key is to drain them very well, then toss them back in the hot wok with your sauce and stir-fry ingredients.
