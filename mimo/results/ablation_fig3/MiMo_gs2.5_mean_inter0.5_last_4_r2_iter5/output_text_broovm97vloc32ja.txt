That's fantastic that you have artistic talent too! If you illustrate your own poems, that's called being a **hybrid author** and it's a real asset. You absolutely **should** submit a complete, finished manuscript to a publisher. In your query letter, you'll mention that you have the illustrations and that you would retain the rights to them. This is a huge selling point because publishers love having exclusive artwork for a book.

When an agent sells your book, the publisher will commission their own illustrator to create the final artwork. However, they will respect the exclusive rights to your original illustrations and may even consider them for a future project. You should definitely include a few sample pages of your art in your submission to showcase your talent.
