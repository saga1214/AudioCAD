Okay, let's see. She loves macramé plant hangers so much she could open a store day in and day out almost. Christmas gifts... cookies... five kids, ages 2 to 15, need to cooperate and get involved without her doing everything. Candy cane story cards offended teachers due to Christianity. Looking for simple, hands-off, meaningful crafts for gifts.

First thought: lean into the macramé passion. Plant hangers are great, but station-based assembly could get all ages involved—little ones decorating finished hangers, older ones helping with simpler parts. Minimal supervision.

Cookies... she hates rolling out dough. Drop cookies or cookie bars would be easier. Minimal prep, mostly scoop-and-bake. Maybe themed like gingerbread sandwiches if that's faster.

Getting kids to cooperate... stations are key. Macramé assembly, cookie decorating, pine cone feeders—all can be broken down by age. Assign each kid a fun, manageable job. Play music, make it a group project. Taste tests help engagement.

Alternative crafts: Fingerprint light reindeer cards are neutral and personal. Pine cone bird feeders are simple but wondering about peanut butter spoilage outdoors? It might be fine since it's exposed, but plain seed wreaths could be a backup.

So, the big picture: Combine macramé station with easy cookies, using stations to engage all kids. Reindeer cards or seed wreaths as neutral crafts. Focus on matching tasks to ages and minimizing her hands-on work. Christmas gifts will feel personal and heartfelt without the stress.
