That sounds like a fantastic idea! A trip could be the perfect reset—and inspiration generator. Here are a few destination ideas that align with your passions, along with logistics to make it feel manageable and exciting:

### 🌍 Destination Ideas:
1. **Kyoto, Japan**  
   - **Why?** Blend of ancient culture (perfect for fantasy/sci-fi) and modernity. Learn Japanese intensively (Kyoto has language schools), explore hidden temples, and experience a tea ceremony.  
   - **Activities:** Stargazing in bamboo forests, visiting language exchange meetups, visiting pet-friendly ryokans (inns).  
   - **Stay:** Traditional guesthouse (e.g., *Ryokan Hoshino*) or modern boutique hotel.  
   - **Get There:** Direct flights from major US airports (~12-14 hours).  

2. **Lisbon, Portugal**  
   - **Why?** Bookish vibe (world’s largest library per capita!), friendly locals, and easy language learning (Portuguese basics are useful!).  
   - **Activities:** Visit a book café, hike up Miradouros for sunset, volunteer at a cat sanctuary.  
   - **Stay:** Cozy hostel (e.g., *Hostal Moinho do Vento*) or a literary-themed Airbnb.  
   - **Get There:** Flights from US (~11 hours).  

3. **Iceland**  
   - **Why?** Pure fantasy landscapes (glaciers, waterfalls, Northern Lights) and a small community where making friends is easier.  
   - **Activities:** Glacier hiking, language exchange via *Meetup*, visiting pet-friendly cafés.  
   - **Stay:** Eco-lodge (e.g., *Egilsstaðir*) or a guesthouse.  
   - **Get There:** Flights from US (~10 hours).  

### 🧳 Logistics Tips (Low-Risk, High-Impact):
- **Book Early:** Flights to Europe/Iceland are cheaper in off-peak seasons (e.g., May–September).  
- **Pre-Book Tours:** Choose small-group tours (e.g., a language class or glacier hike) to meet people safely.  
- **Digital Safety:** Use apps like *Meetup* or *Eventbrite* to find local events, and share your itinerary with a friend.  
- **Solo-Friendly:** Staying in hostels or guesthouses lets you connect casually without pressure.  

### ✨ For Your Writing:
- **Collect Sensory Details:** Keep a notebook for *everything*—the smell of Icelandic moss, the sound of Lisbon’s trams, the taste of Italian espresso.  
- **Interview Locals:** Chat with café owners or language partners about their stories. Their experiences could become characters!  
- **Digital Detox:** Disconnect for a few days to let inspiration flow.  

### 🌟 Mindfulness for Anxiety:
- **Set Small Goals:** "Today, I’ll ask one stranger a question about their favorite local spot."  
- **Safety First:** Stick to well-traveled areas, share your location with someone, and trust your gut.  
- **Reframe Risk:** Travel isn’t about avoiding risks—it’s about choosing *which* risks to take. This trip is proof you *can* do it.  

**Remember:** Anxiety shrinks when you step outside your comfort zone. A trip doesn’t have to solve everything—it just gives you new stories to write, people to connect with, and proof that you’re capable of more than you think.
