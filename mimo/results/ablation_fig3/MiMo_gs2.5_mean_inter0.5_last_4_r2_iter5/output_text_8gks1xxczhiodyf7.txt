Okay, easy calorie surplus wins. If you eat more calories than you burn, weight gain is inevitable. Whether the food is processed or not, unprocessed or not, it doesn't change that basic math. Portion sizes and activity levels matter, but the total calories are the bottom line. That’s why access to cheap, calorie-dense foods is such a big driver—they make that surplus easy to hit without even trying. But at the end of the day, it all comes down to the numbers.
