Breaking it down task by task. Drywall supplies like compound, sandpaper, nail adhesive, and if molding is needed, could run you around 20 to 40 dollars. Cake ingredients like flour, sugar, butter, eggs, milk, extract, pans, and frosting items will probably be around 20 to 30 dollars. Decorations like balloons and movie night snacks are optional but let’s say 10 to 20 dollars. All in, I’d budget around 50 to 90 dollars. You can check online or in-store prices to get a better idea!
