There's no magic juice cleanse that will get rid of these chemicals. Your body does a job of clearing them out, but it's a slow process. That's why reducing your intake is the best strategy.

To help you and your wife put together a list, here are the top products to avoid PFAS exposure.

First, non-stick cookware. Switch to materials like stainless steel or cast iron.
Second, avoid grease-resistant packaging, like wrappers from fast-food and microwave popcorn.
Third, steer clear of things labeled as stain-resistant or waterproof.
Fourth, look at cosmetics and check your hand sanitizers.
And fifth, since tap water is a major source, use an activated carbon-based water filter. Pitchers or faucet-mounted ones leave essential minerals.
