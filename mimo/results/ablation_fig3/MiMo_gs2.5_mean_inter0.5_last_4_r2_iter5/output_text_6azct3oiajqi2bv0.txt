For sticking fabric to a jacket lining without damaging insulation, here's a safe, step-by-step method:

1. **Prep the Surface**:  
   - Flip the jacket inside-out.  
   - Wipe the lining clean with a damp cloth to remove dust/oils. Let dry completely.  

2. **Choose the Right Adhesive**:  
   - **Best Option**: Use **fabric-safe glue dots** (e.g., Aleene’s Fabric Fusion) or a **fabric glue stick** (like Spray Adhesive).  
   - *Avoid* super glue or regular tape—they can melt synthetics or leave residue.  

3. **Application**:  
   - Place your fabric piece right-side down on the lining. Smooth out wrinkles gently.  
   - Apply glue dots or spray adhesive sparingly along the edges. Press firmly for 30 seconds.  

4. **Special Cases**:  
   - **Near zippers/buttons**: Use glue dots placed *under* the zipper pull or button shank.  
   - **Thick linings**: For heavy fleece, double-sided fabric tape (like Wonder Tape) works well.  

5. **Drying & Finishing**:  
   - Let dry for 1–2 hours. For extra security, iron the *fabric side only* (no steam/heat) over the glue.  

**Pro Tips**:  
- Test adhesives on a hidden seam first.  
- If using spray adhesive, do it outdoors or in a well-ventilated area.  
- For critical repairs (like seam-sealing), sewing is more durable—many thrift stores sell used jackets for $10–$20.
