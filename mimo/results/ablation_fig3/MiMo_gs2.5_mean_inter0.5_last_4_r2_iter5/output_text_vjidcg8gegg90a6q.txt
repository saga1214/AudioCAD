For winter chicks integrated into your adult coop, avoid any kind of loose bedding. The brooder plate creates its own safe zone, so their best tool will be huddling together. 

Your best option is simply to use bare floor space near the heater. You can cover it with a low-sided wire mesh or a plastic ramp to keep dust down. This is the safest and most energy-efficient setup.

If you must use a bit of bedding, the only rodent-safe alternative to straw or shavings is wood shavings, cut very finely into dust. Use them sparingly and clean them out frequently. But honestly, I recommend just giving them space near the brooder plate.
