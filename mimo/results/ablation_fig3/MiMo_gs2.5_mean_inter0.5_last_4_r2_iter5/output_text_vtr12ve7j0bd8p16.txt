Here are some great local adventures for a weekend getaway from Calgary.

One option is to drive about an hour east to Canmore and hike into the Icefields Parkway. You can find incredible viewpoints or walk to a famous landmark like the Peyto Lake. It's stunning.

Another idea is to head southwest, maybe an hour and a half's drive, to the Kananaskis Valley. It's a beautiful, less-crowded alternative to Banff. You could hike, bike, or just drive scenic routes like the Kananaskis Lake Road.

If you want something really unique and active, you could take a detour and head to the town of Okotoks. There's a place called the Dinosaur Park where you can ride a zipline right over the dinosaur bonebed. It's a fun, active, and educational day.

For a more relaxed vibe, you could take a day trip to the Saddle Lake Provincial Park, which is only about 40 minutes from the city. It has beautiful lakeside camping and hiking trails.

And if you're both adrenaline junkies, you're in luck. You can find zip-lining tours in the Canmore area, or even go skydiving in Okotoks.
