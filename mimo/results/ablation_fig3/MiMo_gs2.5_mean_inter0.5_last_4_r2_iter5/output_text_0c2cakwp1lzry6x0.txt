Here’s a no-drill-friendly installation guide for your TV, planters, and shelves while staying within landlord rules:

### 📺 TV Mounting (No Studs Needed)
1. **Use Heavy-Duty Wall Anchors**:  
   - Rent a stud finder that detects metal pipes/wires.  
   - If no stud, use **heavy-duty toggle bolts** or **plastic anchors** rated for your TV’s weight.  
   - Follow manufacturer instructions for mounting brackets (most modern mounts use standard 1-inch holes).  
   - *Tip*: Secure cables with cord clips to avoid tangling.

### 🌿 Hanging Planters (Drill-Free)
1. **Adhesive Hooks**:  
   - Use **heavy-duty 3M Command Hooks** (rated for 5–10 lbs per hook).  
   - Place near windows for natural light. Hang asymmetrically for visual interest.  
2. **Magnetic Holders**:  
   - For metal shelves/furniture, use **magnetic plant hangers**.  
3. **Pegboard Alternative**:  
   - Install a **pegboard panel** with hooks (no drilling) and hang planters from it.

### 🪢 Floating Shelves (Weight-Safe Adhesives)
1. **Strong Adhesive Strips**:  
   - Use **3M VHB™ tape** (very high bond) rated for 50+ lbs per strip.  
   - Place shelves at eye level and press firmly for 30 seconds.  
2. **L-Brackets with Adhesive**:  
   - Attach L-brackets to furniture corners with **heavy-duty adhesive** (e.g., Gorilla Double-Sided Tape).  
   - Place the shelf onto the brackets—no screws needed.  
3. **Testing**:  
   - Hold weight on the shelf for 24 hours before adding art pieces.

### ⚠️ Critical Safety Tips
- **Check Weight Limits**: Adhesives work best for <20 lbs. For heavier items, use temporary stands.  
- **Avoid Electrical Wires**: Use a wire detector tool before mounting.  
- **Clean Surfaces**: Wipe walls/furniture with rubbing alcohol before applying adhesives.  
- **Test First**: Practice on a less visible area to ensure alignment.

### 🛠️ Pro Decoration Tips
- **Art Groupings**: Cluster similar art pieces (e.g., by color or subject).  
- **Furniture Layout**: Create a "showcase zone" near your workspace for statement pieces.  
- **Lighting Magic**: Use LED strip lights under shelves or behind art for ambiance.  
- **Sunday Schedule**: Dedicate 90-minute blocks (e.g., 9–10 AM for shelves, 10–11 AM for planters).  

With these methods, you’ll avoid violating the landlord’s rules while transforming your space. Prioritize safety checks and take breaks between installations to avoid fatigue! 🌟
