It's wonderful that you've committed to this. Here is a sample weekly practice schedule to guide you. Feel free to adjust it to fit your own life.

For Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, dedicate 20 minutes in the morning. Start with bean bags, practicing just adding the third ball from your non-dominant hand. Then, spend about five minutes with tennis balls, focusing on firm catches.

On Tuesday and Thursday, do a 15-minute evening session. Begin with scarves to slowly review a basic pattern. Then, try juggling the bean bags with one hand to reinforce that strong rhythm.

Saturday is your main day. Do a longer 30-minute session. Mix all the items: start with bean bags, move to tennis balls, then try scarves for slow timing, and end by putting it all together with bean bags.

Finally, Sunday is for rest. Don't practice, but you could watch a juggling video to keep it in your mind in a fun, stress-free way. Most importantly, be patient with yourself and celebrate every small victory!
