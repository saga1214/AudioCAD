### Quick Summary: Solutions for Your Cats and Poverty  

**Feeding Solutions (7 Cats):**  
1. **Control Greedy Eaters:**  
   - Use **microchip-activated feeders** or **timed dispensers** to regulate eating speed and portion sizes.  
   - **Separate feeding spaces/rooms** before work begins to ensure fussy ones eat.  

2. **Affordable Food Workarounds:**  
   - Buy **quality food in bulk**, mixing smaller amounts with cheap fillers (e.g., plain cooked rice) *vet-approved*.  
   - Supplement with **safe, low-cost scraps** (e.g., plain chicken, tuna, or boiled eggs) when possible.  
   - **Network for donations** via local groups/shelters.  

**Health Maintenance on a Budget:**  
1. **Prevent Illness:**  
   - **Hygiene:** Clean bowls/litter daily; rotate resting spots.  
   - **Prevent Obesity:** Encourage free play (e.g., string toys, feather wands).  
   - **Self-Care:** Brush cats to reduce hairballs/skin issues.  

2. **Low-Cost Health Safeguards:**  
   - Prioritize **low-cost vet procedures** (spaying/neutering, vaccines).  
   - **Monitor closely** for early illness signs (e.g., weight loss, lethargy); treat minor issues at home with OTC meds (e.g., cat-safe herbs for digestive aid).  
   - Avoid cheap food long-term to prevent vet bills later.  

**Poverty Navigation:**  
- **Fund Yourself:** Seek **food assistance programs** (SNAP, local charities) or **side gigs** (e.g., pet-sitting).  
- **Cat Food Shortfalls:** Combine above strategies; prioritize fussy cats’ health via portion control and supplements.  

**Key Takeaway:** Balance control feeders, prevention, and community support to care for all cats while addressing food/health gaps. Always prioritize essential vet care and network for resources.
