First, about tinnitus being physiological versus psychological. While most cases are physical, stress or anxiety can sometimes make it feel worse or more noticeable. But it’s not caused by post-trauma in the same way a scar is; it’s more about physical changes in the auditory system.

Now, the left brain versus right brain thing. It’s mostly a myth, but it’s a useful oversimplification. The brain doesn’t really split creative and logical tasks between hemispheres. Instead, different regions specialize in different things. For example, the left hemisphere often handles language and logic, while the right handles spatial reasoning and creativity. But they work together constantly. So it’s not like creativity lives on the right side and logic on the left. It’s more of a team effort.

As for structuring things that are "solid and indestructible," fields like architecture or engineering design might appeal to you. You get to build tangible things while measuring and optimizing them. Or even graphic design or data visualization, where you can be creative with concrete facts.

And if sitting at a desk all day isn’t your thing, consider fields like product design, where you can be hands-on and creative while still measuring and testing things. Or even something like art direction for magazines, where you balance creativity with measurable results.
