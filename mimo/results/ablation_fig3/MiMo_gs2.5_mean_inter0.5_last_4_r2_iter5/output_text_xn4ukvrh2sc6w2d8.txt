First, Old Fashioned: You'll need two ounces of bourbon like Bulleit, one sugar cube—or if you use syrup, half an ounce—or about one teaspoon if using a cube. Add two to three dashes of Angostura bitters, stir it all in your glass with ice, and finish by garnishing with an orange twist.

Paper Plane: Equal parts, so that's one ounce of bourbon like Maker’s Mark, one ounce of Aperol, half an ounce of Nonino Amaro, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. You'll shake that with ice and strain it into a coupe glass.

Next, the Whiskey Sour without the egg white: It's one and a half ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. Dry shake it first for that foam, add ice, shake again, and serve.

If you do go with the Whiskey Sour using the raw egg white, the recipe is one and a half ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and one fresh egg white. Remember to dry shake first, add ice, shake again, and strain.

Then there's your fourth drink, the French 75: One and a half ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and two ounces of Champagne or sparkling wine. You dry shake the gin, lemon, and syrup first for foam, add ice, shake again, and then top with the bubbly.
