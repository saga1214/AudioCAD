Okay, kosher dietary laws do mean a few specific things, but it's definitely manageable. The main things to avoid are pork, shellfish, and any meat and dairy being mixed together. So, for your healthy snack like vegetable platter with hummus, you're all good there. The weenies in bacon you chose—be careful, because bacon is pork, so Tony can't eat those. Cookies would need to be checked for dairy ingredients like butter or milk, and to make sure any decorations aren't made with eggs, which are also forbidden. Basically, just make sure the sweet you bring and any discussion foods are strictly vegetarian and dairy-free. You can still offer plenty of other things, like dark chocolate (which is kosher for Passover but check the ingredients just in case), fruit, nuts, or even a bag of kosher chips. The key is just to label the foods clearly, so everyone knows what's safe for Tony to eat. That way, you can keep the book club inclusive and focused on the book.
