Alright, after all that back-and-forth, I think we've got it pretty clear. At typical rink temps, the friction is around 0.01 to 0.1. For competition ice, closer to 0.01 to 0.02. Warmer ice, like minus two degrees, can go up to 0.02 to 0.06. Extra-cold ice, below minus ten degrees, might increase friction slightly back up to maybe 0.005 to 0.01. So yeah, those extra-cold blades digging in increases resistance instead of decreasing friction.
