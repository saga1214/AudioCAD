That's fantastic! Having illustrations already makes your children's book manuscript even more appealing. However, a completed manuscript with illustrations isn't actually necessary for your first submission to an agent. In fact, it might make things more complicated.

Most children's book agents will accept your project in two parts: your manuscript text, and your artwork. You can submit the text separately, and if an agent accepts it, they will then guide you on the next steps for developing and integrating the illustrations. The manuscript is the part they are looking to represent.

So, for now, you would submit your query letter and your manuscript text. Once an agent is interested, they will advise you on how to proceed with your illustrations.
