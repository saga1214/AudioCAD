Here's a full list of corgi-friendly treats, tailored for your brown-and-white Princess:

### 🐾 **Homemade Treats**  
1. **Pumpkin/Banana "Ice Cubes"**: Mashed pumpkin or banana blended with water, frozen in ice cube trays.  
2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**: Mix peanut butter (xylitol-free) with oats, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 mins.  
3. **Sweet Potato Chips**: Thinly slice sweet potatoes, bake at 400°F (200°C) for 20-25 mins until crisp.  
4. **Carrot Sticks**: Steamed or raw carrot sticks (cut small for safety).  
5. **Cheese Bites**: Mini cubes of plain cheddar or mozzarella (small portions only).  

### 🛒 **Store-Bought Brands**  
- **Training Treats**: Zuke’s Mini Naturals, Wellness Train & Treat.  
- **Health Snacks**: Blue Buffalo Home-Style Bites, Merrick Healthy Puppy Bits.  
- **Fresh Options**: Open Farm Superfood Bites, Earthborn Holistic Grains & Gratefully Grains.  

### 💡 **Key Tips**  
- **Portion Control**: Treats should be ≤10% of her daily calories.  
- **Safety First**: Avoid grapes, raisins, xylitol, chocolate, and onions.  
- **Training Treats**: Use tiny pieces (pea-sized) for obedience training.  
- **Potty Rewards**: High-value treats (like chicken bits) for potty successes!
