Let’s break it down task by task.

Drywall stuff: joint compound small tub is usually around five to ten dollars. Sandpaper/sponge, putty knife, nails/adhesive, primer/paint, and a replacement piece of molding can probably add another ten to twenty dollars. So, let’s say twenty to thirty dollars for the wall project.

Cake stuff: flour, sugar, baking powder, butter, eggs, milk, vanilla, powdered sugar for frosting, plus pans. That should be under fifteen dollars, maybe twelve to fifteen.

Plus decorations for the day, like balloons, should be another five to ten dollars.

So, in total, you’re looking at about fifty to seventy dollars for everything. It’s always a good idea to round up a little in case you need more molding or paint. That should cover you for both tasks and making it special for your son!
