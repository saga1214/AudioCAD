Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! I'd suggest checking out places like Tokyo Joe, which has solid ramen and udon, or Saizeriya if you want something more casual and affordable. If you're into pho, Pho 79 is fantastic. For protein bars, it really depends on your taste. Something like Quest, Optimum Nutrition, or Clif Bars are good options. If you want something with a little more kick, look for RXBARs or GoMacro bars. Just make sure whatever you pick fits your dietary needs and taste preferences. Hope you enjoy your meal!
