Great questions! Here's a breakdown of treats and clicker training:

### Treat Recommendations:
1. **Small & Soft**: Choose tiny, easy-to-chew treats (e.g., peanut butter bites, cheese cubes, cooked chicken).  
2. **High-Value**: Use irresistible rewards for training (e.g., hot dog bits, freeze-dried liver, or special dog treats).  
3. **Safety First**: Avoid xylitol (in peanut butter/cheese) and ensure treats are bone-free to prevent choking.  
4. Galileo likes chicken? Try dehydrated chicken strips or turkey bites. Liver or salmon treats might also appeal to a new puppy.  

**Pro Tip**: Use 5-10% of his daily calories for treats—balance rewards with playtime and meals!

### Clicker Training Explained:
- **How It Works**:  
  1. Clicker = "Marking Tool": Clicks the *exact moment* he does the right thing (e.g., sits).  
  2. Treat = "Reward": Immediately after the click, give a treat.  
  3. Repeat: He learns "Click + Treat = Good!"  
- **Science Behind It**:  
  The click creates a precise timeline between behavior and reward—like a tiny "record scratch" for brains!  
- **Example for "Sit"**:  
  - Hold treat near his nose → raise upward → as he lowers his head to sit, click → give treat.  
  - Repeat 10+ times daily. Soon, he’ll sit on cue!  

**Why It Works**: Clicker training builds trust, speeds up learning, and reduces frustration.
