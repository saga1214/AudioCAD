That's getting very precise. However, the key factor here is still the production of the specific protein I mentioned earlier.

Even though the DNA in the egg contained the *potential* for a chicken, for that egg to be classified as a true chicken egg, a hen had to have laid it. And a hen is a chicken. Since the first true hen only existed after it hatched from its egg, that first hen—and therefore that first egg—had to be a chicken.
