Okay, let's break this down. Vasculitis is inflammation of the blood vessels, and it can affect them at different sizes—large, medium, or small. This inflammation is what causes the symptoms, which can vary a lot depending on where it's happening in the body.

Common symptoms include feeling generally unwell with things like fever and fatigue. Skin rashes are also a frequent sign. And yes, it can absolutely affect hair and nail health because of poor blood flow to those areas. That might show up as thinning hair, brittle nails, or slow growth.

The underlying cause is usually an autoimmune issue, where your immune system mistakenly attacks the blood vessels. Sometimes it's triggered by an infection or can be hereditary.

For your skin redness, it could be rosacea, sun damage, genetics, or another skin condition. A dermatologist is really the best person to help you pinpoint the cause and create a plan that addresses the physical manifestations you're worried about. For supplements, besides Biotin, collagen peptides, keratin, iron, and zinc are good options for hair and nails, but again, a doctor can advise on inflammation management safely.
