Lockport has a lot to show you. The best place to start is the Erie Canal Museum, which is located in the historic canal basin and has a lot of great exhibits. 

You can also walk the Lockport Heritage Trail, which has informational markers all along the canal and the locks. It's a great way to see the history in context. 

And if you're really into the spy story, a visit to the Flight Five Locks is a must. It's a great example of that theory coming to life.
