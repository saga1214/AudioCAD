### **Core Body Measurements (Take for EVERY fitting):**
1. **Bust** (measure under the arms at the fullest part)  
2. **Natural Waist** (the narrowest part of the torso, not the belt line)  
3. **Hips** (measure around the fullest part, usually 8–10 inches below the waist)

### **Essential Measurements (For fitted garments):**
4. **Thigh** (measured at the fullest part, from crotch to mid-thigh)  
5. **Shoulder Width** (across the upper back, from outer shoulder point to outer shoulder point)  
6. **Neck** (for collars; measure around the neck at the base of the jaw)  
7. **Bicep** (for sleeve fit; measure around the bicep at the fullest part)  

---

### **Item-Specific Measurements:**
- **Inseam** (distance from crotch to ankle; critical for pants)  
- **Sleeve Length** (from shoulder point to wrist)  
- **Back Rise** (distance from crotch to waistband; *more important than front rise* for tall clients)  

---
