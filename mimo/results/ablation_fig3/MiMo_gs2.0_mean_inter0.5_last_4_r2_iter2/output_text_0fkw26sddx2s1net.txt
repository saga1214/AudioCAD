### **Shopping List for Asian-Inspired Meal**
*(All ingredients assume your husband can tolerate soy products—double-check labels for hidden legumes if unsure.)*

---

#### **STARTER: MISO SOUP OR CUCUMBER SALAD**  
*(Choose one)*  
**Option 1: MISO SOUP**  
- Dashi or instant dashi soup base  
- Miso paste (white or red)  
- Tofu (firm, **check for legumes**—some brands use soy but confirm safety)  
- Sliced shiitake mushrooms  
- Bamboo shoots (canned or fresh)  
- Green onions (chopped)  
- *Tools*: Small pot, knife  

**Option 2: CUCUMBER SALAD**  
- Cucumbers (English or Japanese)  
- Sesame oil  
- Rice vinegar  
- Sugar  
- Salt  
- Sesame seeds  
- *Tools*: Knife, bowl  

---

#### **MAIN COURSE: CHICKEN TERIYAKI WITH JASMINE RICE & STIR-FRIED VEGGIES**  
- Chicken thighs (boneless, skinless)  
- **Teriyaki Marinade**:  
  - Soy sauce (check label for legumes)  
  - Mirin (or substitute with 1 tbsp sugar + 1 tbsp water)  
  - Rice vinegar  
  - Sugar  
  - Grated ginger  
  - Minced garlic  
- **Stir-Fried Veggies**:  
  - Bell peppers (any color)  
  - Snap peas or carrots (julienned)  
  - Butter or oil (for cooking—no soy sauce needed if avoiding)  
- **Jasmine rice**: 1 cup uncooked rice  
- *Tools*: Baking sheet (for chicken), wok or large skillet, rice cooker or pot  

---

#### **DESSERT: homemade MATCHA ICE CREAM**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - Matcha powder (1 tbsp)  
  - Heavy cream (1 cup)  
  - Sweetened condensed milk (1 can)  
  - Vanilla extract (optional)  
- *Tools*: Freezer-safe container, whisk or fork  

---

#### **SUPPLEMENTAL ITEMS**  
- Rice vinegar (for both starter and veggies)  
- Sugar (for miso soup or salad)  
- Salt  
- Fresh ginger & garlic (if not using pastes)  
- Green onions (for garnish)  

---

### **Tips for Success**  
1. **Allergy Check**:  
   - Verify soy sauce labels for "contains legumes." Tamari or coconut aminos are legume-free alternatives.  
   - Use pre-made dashi or kombu (seaweed) for miso soup—no seafood involved.  
2. **Meal Prep**:  
   - Make matcha ice cream base 1–2 hours ahead; stir hourly until set.  
   - Cook rice while stir-frying veggies.  
3. **Time-Savers**:  
   - Swap teriyaki chicken for store-bought teriyaki sauce to speed up cooking.  
   - Use frozen stir-fry veggies if short on time.
