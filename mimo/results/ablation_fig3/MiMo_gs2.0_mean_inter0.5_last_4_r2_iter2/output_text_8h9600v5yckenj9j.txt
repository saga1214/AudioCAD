For Halloween candy, you’ll want to stick to the classics that almost everyone loves. A good mix would be things like Reese’s, Skittles, M&Ms, gummy bears, and maybe some chocolate bars like Snickers or Twix. You could also throw in some sour candy like Warheads or Sour Patch Kids, and of course, classic Halloween candy like candy corn or those little black licorice things. Avoid anything too weird or bitter, like those old-school wax bottles or hard-to-eat candy, unless you know someone who specifically likes them. A good mix of sweet, sour, and chewy is always a winner!
