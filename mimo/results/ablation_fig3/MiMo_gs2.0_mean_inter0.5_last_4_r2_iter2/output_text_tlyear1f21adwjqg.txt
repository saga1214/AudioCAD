That's a tough one because efficiency can be measured in different ways. Solar is great for its scalability and dropping costs. Wind is excellent in windy areas and reliable. And hydropower is super reliable and efficient once built. If I had to pick just one overall, I’d still lean towards solar because of its massive potential, scalability, and the fact that it’s available almost everywhere. But it’s important to remember that the best choice really depends on the specific location and its resources.
