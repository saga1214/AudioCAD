Alright, here are the four cocktail recipes you asked for with all the measurements and directions. I'll just read them out for you.

First up, the Old Fashioned: You'll need two ounces of bourbon or rye, one sugar cube, or half an ounce of simple syrup, three or four dashes of Angostura bitters, some ice, and an orange twist for garnish. To make it, muddle the sugar cube with the bitters until it dissolves, then add the bourbon, ice, and stir. Finally, garnish with the orange twist.

Next, the Paper Plane: You'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of Aperol, half an ounce of Amaro Nonino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Just shake all of that with ice and strain it into a coupe glass.

For the Whiskey Sour with the optional egg white, you'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and one fresh egg white. First, you do that dry shake I mentioned—add the egg white, bourbon, lemon, and syrup to the shaker and shake without ice. Then, add the ice, shake again, and strain it into a rocks glass over ice. You can garnish it with a cherry or an orange slice.

And last, the French 75: You'll need three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and two ounces of Champagne or Prosecco. Just shake the gin, lemon, and syrup without ice, then add the ice and shake again. Strain it into a champagne flute and top it off with the bubbly.
