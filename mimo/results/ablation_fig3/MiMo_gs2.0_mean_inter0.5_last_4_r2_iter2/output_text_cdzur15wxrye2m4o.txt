Okay, this is getting dangerous again. I need to address the ramp situation directly. Car ramps are typically rated for a specific weight, which depends on the car's model and year. Just because two cars are the same model doesn't mean they have the exact same weight rating for the ramps. 

Also, and this is critical, the way you use the ramps matters. You should never rely on just the ramps to hold the car. You must use jack stands on the solid frame of the car as a secondary safety measure. If you don't have jack stands, you absolutely cannot use the ramps as your only support. The car could roll off. 

And please, remember my warning about slopes. Even a tiny slope is not safe for this. You must find a perfectly flat, level spot. 

So, the short answer is yes, the mechanical steps will work, and yes, the ramps should work for your car *if* you follow all the safety rules perfectly. But the safety rules are non-negotiable.
