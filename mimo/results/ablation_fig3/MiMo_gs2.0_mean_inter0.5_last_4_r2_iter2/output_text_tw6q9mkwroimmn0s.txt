What a fun theme! Here are **5 ocean-inspired mains** and **5 sweet desserts** tailored for your taste buds and dietary needs:

### Ocean-Themed Mains
1. **Shrimp Tacos with Mango Salsa**  
   *Spicy, fresh, and diabetes-friendly*: Grilled shrimp in corn tortillas topped with mango-pineapple salsa, avocado, and a squeeze of lime. Skip the shellfish!  
2. **Coconut Curry with Shrimp & Chickpeas**  
   *Rich and spicy*: Light coconut milk-based curry with shrimp, chickpeas, spinach, and bell peppers. Serve with cauliflower rice.  
3. **Grilled Scallops with Mango-Habanero Glaze**  
   *Sweet-heat perfection*: Seared scallops with a glaze of mango puree, habanero, lime, and honey. Diabetes-friendly if using low-sugar honey.  
4. **Spicy Coconut Shrimp Stir-Fry**  
   *Vibrant and light*: Shrimp stir-fried with broccoli, snap peas, and carrots in a spicy coconut sauce. Serve over cauliflower rice.  
5. **"Paella" with Cauliflower Rice & Shrimp**  
   *Coastal vibes*: Cauliflower rice sautéed with shrimp, peas, and artichokes in a paprika-spiced tomato broth. No traditional rice!  

### Sweet Ocean Desserts  
1. **Mango Chia Pudding**  
   *No-bake and refreshing*: Layers of chia seeds in coconut milk with fresh mango, lime zest, and a touch of stevia.  
2. **Mango Sorbet**  
   *Light and icy*: Pureed mango blended with lime juice and a pinch of sea salt. Diabetes-friendly if sugar-free.  
3. **Grilled Peaches with Coconut Whipped Cream**  
   *Simple elegance*: Halved peaches grilled with cinnamon, topped with coconut whipped cream and a mango salsa swirl.  
4. **Coconut Rice Pudding**  
   *Creamy and comforting*: Made with unsweetened coconut milk, cinnamon, and fresh mango. Optional: add a sprinkle of toasted coconut.  
5. **Mango-Lime Flan**  
   *Decadent but light*: A jiggly custard with a mango-lime swirl. Use erythritol for a sugar-free version.  

### Key Considerations:  
- **Diabetes-Friendly Swaps**: Replace refined sugars with natural fruit sweetness, stevia, or monk fruit. Use low-sugar sauces (like coconut aminos).  
- **Spice Control**: Adjust chili levels for your fiancée’s tolerance.  
- **Theme Touch**: Garnish dishes with edible flowers, seaweed, or ocean-blue veggies like blue potatoes!
