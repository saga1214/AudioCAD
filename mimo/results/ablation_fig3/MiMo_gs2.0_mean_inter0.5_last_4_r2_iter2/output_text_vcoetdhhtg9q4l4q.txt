That's a smart next step! For faster dinner ingredients, here are some efficient, versatile pantry staples that simplify cooking:

### 🥣 Pantry Staples (Buy in bulk)
- **Rice or pasta** (e.g., jasmine, basmati, or spaghetti)  
- **Canned beans** (black beans, chickpeas, lentils)  
- **Frozen veggies** (peas, spinach, stir-fry mix)  
- **Oats or instant grains** (for quick porridge or bowls)  

### 🍳 Proteins (Prepped ahead)
- **Hard-boiled eggs** (store in fridge for 2–3 days)  
- **Pre-cooked grains** (quinoa, brown rice)  
- **Canned tuna/salmon** (add to salads or wraps)  
- **Frozen shrimp or tofu** (great for stir-fries)  

### 🥬 Quick Veggies
- **Cabbage or bell peppers** (shred for salads/stir-fries)  
- **Avocado** (add creaminess to bowls/salads)  
- **Frozen edamame** (ready-to-use protein boost)  

### 🍳 Easy Meal Ideas with These:
1. **One-Pan Stir-Fry**:  
   Sauté frozen veggies + canned beans + tofu in sesame oil. Toss with rice noodles or instant rice.  
2. **Breakfast-for-Dinner**:  
   Scramble eggs with canned beans, frozen spinach, and avocado.  
3. **Mason Jar Salads**:  
   Layer greens, canned tuna, cherry tomatoes, and pre-cooked quinoa. Shake to mix.  

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Prep staples weekly (e.g., cook a big batch of rice, hard-boil eggs, chop veggies). This cuts cooking time in half!
