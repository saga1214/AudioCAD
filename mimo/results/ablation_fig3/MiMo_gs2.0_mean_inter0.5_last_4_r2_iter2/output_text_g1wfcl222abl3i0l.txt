### 🛠️ Quick Glue Gun Fixes (Try These Now!):
1. **Heat It Up**:  
   - Place the glue gun on a heat-resistant surface.  
   - Hold the tip near an open flame (like a stove burner) for 10–15 seconds.  
   - *Alternatively*: Use a hairdryer on low heat for 20–30 seconds.  
2. **Clean the Nozzle**:  
   - Dip a cotton swab in rubbing alcohol, then gently wipe the tip.  
   - If glue is clogged, tap the nozzle firmly to dislodge it.  
3. **Check the Battery**:  
   - If it’s rechargeable, ensure it’s fully charged.  
4. **Let It Rest**:  
   - Turn it off and wait 5 minutes—sometimes it just needs a cooldown.  

### 🎃 Extra Spooky Upgrades (While You Wait!):
- **Blood Drops**:  
  Add faux blood (like red craft glue or airbrushed paint) pooling at the base of masks or pumpkins.  
- **Eyes**:  
  Glue tiny black "eyes" (beads or painted dots) on the flowers or pumpkins for a creepy, lifelike touch.  
- **Moss & Roots**:  
  Drape faux moss around the base, with tiny twigs or "roots" trailing down.  
- **Mask Details**:  
  Paint cracks or scars on the masquerade masks with black or silver acrylic paint.  
- **Glitter Hack**:  
  Dip the *back* of petals in dark glitter glue or use a fine brush to dot glitter along edges—your idea is genius!  
- **Shadow Effects**:  
  Add a thin black satin ribbon wrapped around the globe’s base to mimic a "veil."  

### 💡 Pro Tip:  
If the glue gun still won’t work, **use a hot glue stick** (if available) or a small amount of **E6000 glue** for delicate elements like flowers/masks. Just let it dry overnight for security!
