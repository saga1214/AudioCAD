Let's update the vaccination campaign's scores to reflect that. Its implementation difficulty would go up to account for resistance, so maybe a three. Its cost-efficiency would drop because you have to spend so much to counter misinformation. Most importantly, public acceptance drops significantly, to maybe a two. When you recalculate, the sugar reduction campaign now has a much higher total score.
