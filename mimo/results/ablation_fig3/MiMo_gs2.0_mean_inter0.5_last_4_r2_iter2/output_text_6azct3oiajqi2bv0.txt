That's a smart approach! Since Nordstrom Rack is accessible and functional gear is key for Sacramento's variable climate, here's how to use safety pins safely on your jacket lining:

1.  **Target the Right Spot:** Avoid the main insulation or down clusters. Pin near the **collar base** (where it meets the jacket body) or along the **inner cuffs**. These areas are less likely to have concentrated insulation you might damage.
2.  **Pin Strategically:**
    *   **Double-Pinning:** Place the pin *through* one layer of the outer shell (like the collar fabric), *then* gently push it through the *same spot* on the lining (if it's a separate layer) or through the innermost layer of the collar. This minimizes pin contact with the actual insulation.
    *   **Pin Through Seam:** If possible, position the pin so it goes *through* a seam on the outer shell and lining, minimizing the chance of snagging loose insulation.
3.  **Gentle Handling is Key:**
    *   **Minimize Force:** Don't yank or pull hard. Let the pin do the work.
    *   **Pin Close to Edge:** Place the pin as close to the edge of the fabric where you need to hold things together (like a loose collar flap or a torn seam edge) rather than deep in the middle.
    *   **Avoid Snagging:** Be very careful not to let the pin head or shaft catch on any loose threads, tears, or exposed insulation fibers.
4.  **Test First:** If you're unsure about a spot, test the pin on an inconspicuous area first (like the back inside seam).
5.  **Temporary Fix Only:** Remember, this is a quick hold. Plan to replace damaged clothing soon, especially if it's worn out or defective.

**Alternatives to Safety Pins (if pins feel risky):**

*   **Clip:** Use a sturdy clothes clip or a small binder clip (the kind with a long clip) to hold the collar or cuff in place. Avoid plastic clips that might melt near synthetics.
*   **Tie:** If the collar is loose, you can tie the inner collar flap to the jacket body using a shoelace or even a hair tie (hidden under the collar), pulling it snug.
*   **Sewing (Temporary):** If the tear is small, a few quick stitches with a needle and thread (or even a sewing kit from a drugstore) is often the safest solution. Use a basic straight stitch and don't pull the knot through.

**Key Takeaway:** Prioritize pinning near seams or edges on the outer fabric, minimize force, and avoid concentrating pin pressure on the insulation itself. A quick double-pin through a collar seam is usually the safest bet for a temporary fix! Good luck with your shopping trip!
