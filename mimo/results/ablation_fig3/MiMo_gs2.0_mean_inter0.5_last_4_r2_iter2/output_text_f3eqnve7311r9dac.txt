You are absolutely right that older comics are inherently rarer, which is a major factor in their long-term value. The 1940s and 50s are the real prize.

Here are ten rare and valuable books from that era. You can find graded copies in your budget. First, *Web of Science* number one, Superman's first cover appearance. Second, *The Brave and the Bold* number one, Batman's first cover with Superman. Third, *Adventure Comics* number one, which introduced Superman. Fourth, *Superboy* number one, his first appearance. Fifth, *The Flash Comics* number one, The Flash's first cover. Sixth, *Captain America Comics* number one, sold at that record six million dollar price recently. Seventh, *Aquaman* number one, the first cover appearance of Aquaman. Eighth, *Giant-Size X-Men* number one, which we already discussed as a strong buy. Ninth, *The Invaders* number one, the first official team-up of Captain America and Namor. And tenth, *The Incredible Hulk* number one, which is the first full appearance of the Hulk. Just remember, the condition is everything, so you should always look for professionally graded copies.
