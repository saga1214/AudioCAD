Absolutely! Here’s a **budget-friendly dessert plan** that’s kid-friendly, healthy-ish, and reinforces diabetes awareness—all while keeping costs down:

### 🍎 Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:
1. **Fresh Fruit Skewers or Bowls**  
   - **Why**: Cheap, healthy, and colorful. Kids love dipping (e.g., strawberries in dark chocolate, grapes in yogurt dip).  
   - **Cost-Saver**: Buy seasonal fruit in bulk (watermelon, melon, berries).  
   - **Awareness Tie-In**: Label sugar content (e.g., "1 cup melon = 10g natural sugar").

2. **Homemade Frozen Yogurt Popsicles**  
   - **Why**: Uses yogurt (protein-rich) and real fruit purees. Kids can customize flavors.  
   - **Cost-Saver**: Make a big batch with store-bought yogurt and frozen fruit.  
   - **Tip**: Use natural sweeteners like honey or mashed banana sparingly.

3. **Oatmeal Cookies or Energy Balls**  
   - **Why**: Low-cost ingredients (oats, peanut butter, honey). Add oats for fiber.  
   - **Budget Hack**: Use pre-mixed oats and frozen peanut butter.  
   - **Awareness**: Label them "Low-Sugar" or "High-Fiber" to educate subtly.

4. **Yogurt Parfaits (Layered in Glasses)**  
   - **Why**: Greek yogurt is protein-packed and filling. Layer with granola and berries.  
   - **Cost-Saver**: Buy large tubs of yogurt and pre-portion granola.  
   - **Kid Appeal**: Let kids assemble their own mini-parfaits.

5. **"Fun" Fruit Shapes**  
   - **Why**: Use cookie cutters on fruit (e.g., apple slices, melon balls) for a playful touch.  
   - **Budget-Friendly**: Minimal tools needed—just fruit and toothpicks.

### 💡 Pro Tips to Save Money:
- **DIY Dessert Stations**: Set up a "build-your-own" fruit bowl or yogurt bar.  
- **Use Pantry Staples**: Cookies, granola, and yogurt are affordable staples.  
- **Avoid Expensive Sweeteners**: Rely on natural sugars (fruit, honey) instead of syrups.  
- **Buy in Bulk**: Seasonal fruits and frozen yogurt purees are cheaper per unit.  

### 🌟 Final Thought:
Even simple desserts like **grilled pineapple with a drizzle of honey** or **apple slices with cinnamon** feel special and align with your cause. Focus on **fresh, whole foods**—they’re cheaper, healthier, and reinforce awareness without being preachy.
