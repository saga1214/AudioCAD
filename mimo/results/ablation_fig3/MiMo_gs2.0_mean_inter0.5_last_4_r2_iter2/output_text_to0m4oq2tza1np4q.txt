That's awesome! Pickles and pickle juice can be delicious *and* heart-healthy in moderation, but **be mindful of sodium** since pickled foods are often high in salt. Here are some creative, balanced ways to use them:

### 🥒 1. **Pickle Spears & Cucumber Salad**
   - **How to use:** Serve cucumber spears with a light vinaigrette (olive oil, lemon juice, mustard, pepper). The cucumber's water content helps offset sodium.
   - **Tip:** Rinse pickles briefly in water to reduce salt slightly.

### 🐟 2. **Pickle & Tuna Salad**
   - **How to use:** Chop dill pickles into the tuna salad. Add Greek yogurt instead of mayo for extra protein and probiotics.
   - **Why it works:** Protein and veggies balance the sodium.

### 🥚 3. **Pickle Relish in Eggs**
   - **How to use:** Mix a tablespoon of dill relish into scrambled eggs or deviled eggs. Adds tang without heavy salt.
   - **Tip:** Use low-sodium relish if available.

### 🍲 4. **Pickled Vegetables in Soups/Stews**
   - **How to use:** Add sauerkraut, pickled beets, or carrots to chicken soup or lentil stew. Fermented veggies offer probiotics.
   - **Tip:** Pair with low-sodium broth.

### 🥗 5. **Pickle Juice "Garnish"**
   - **How to use:** Sprinkle pickle spears on grilled salmon (like your marinade plan!) or avocado toast. Use juice sparingly as a flavor boost.
   - **Caution:** A splash adds sodium, but it’s better than drowning in it.

### ⚠️ **Important Notes:**
- **Sodium Alert:** One medium pickle can have **800–1,200mg sodium**. Pickle juice is even higher! Stick to 1–2 pickles per serving max.
- **Fermented Pickles:** Homemade or refrigerator-pickled veggies (like quick-pickled cucumbers) use less salt and have probiotics.
- **Pickle Juice "Benefits":** While trendy, it’s mostly water, vinegar, and salt. For hydration, water or coconut water is better sodium-free options.

### 💡 **Smart Swaps:**
- **Swap for Low-Sodium:** Look for "no-salt-added" or "low-sodium" pickles.
- **Make Your Own:** Quick-pickling cucumbers in vinegar, water, and herbs is easy and cuts sodium by 50%+!
