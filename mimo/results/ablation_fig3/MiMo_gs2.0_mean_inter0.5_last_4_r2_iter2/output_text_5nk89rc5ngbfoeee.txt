For an end-of-year celebration, a **park BBQ with a sundae bar** sounds like a fantastic way to end the year! Here’s why it works and how to make it smooth:

### 🎉 **Why a Park BBQ?**
- **Fun & Active**: Games like a wheelbarrow race, sack races, or a three-legged challenge keep energy high and tie back to your fond memories of Brownies.
- **Kid-Approved Food**: Sundae bars (offer dairy-free options since you avoid dairy) or pizza cater to diverse tastes. You can also do a "build-your-own taco" station for variety.
- **Parent Involvement**: Parents can help set up, bring sides, or supervise games—lightening your load.

### 🌦️ **Logistics & Tips**
1. **Weather Backup**: 
   - Have a rain plan (e.g., move to the cafeteria or a local community hall). 
   - Rent tents if outdoors (check local event rentals).
2. **Food & Cost**:
   - **Parents contribute**: Assign a "snack committee" to bring sides (e.g., chips/dips like you enjoyed growing up!), drinks, or desserts. This shares the cost.
   - **You provide main items**: BBQ supplies (grill, utensils) or a pizza order can be troop-funded via dues.
3. **Safety & Simplicity**:
   - Keep it to 1–2 hours max. Pre-game snacks (like chips/dip!) can prevent hangry meltdowns.
   - Assign a few older girls to help with cleanup or handing out sundae supplies.

### 💡 **Alternative Ideas**
- **Cafeteria Party**: Low-effort, budget-friendly, and parent-friendly. Add a "trophy" table for badges/sashes with photos.
- **Movie Night**: Picnic blankets, popcorn, and a Brownie-themed movie (e.g., *Honey, I Shrunk the Kids*).

### ✅ **Key Takeaway**
Start with the park BBQ—it’s memorable, inclusive, and lets you lean into the fun you remember from being a Brownie. With parent support on food/logistics, you’ll feel less apprehensive and more like the awesome troop leader your daughters need! You’ve got this—even pregnant heroes make great leaders! 👑
