Okay, since you've already cleaned, degreased, light-sanded, and disassembled the hardware, you're really on track. Before you apply the primer, the most critical step is to do a final check for any remaining dirt or dust. A little bit of dust can ruin the primer's finish, so a final wipe-down with a tack cloth is highly recommended.

Make sure the primer you buy is specifically labeled as 'stain-blocking' and is suitable for the primer surface you'll be painting over. Once you've applied that primer and it's completely dry, you can go ahead with your new off-white color.
