Here’s a step-by-step guide to safely install your items, keeping noise and efficiency in mind:

### 📺 **1. TV Mount Installation**
- **Measure Twice, Drill Once**:  
  - Use a stud finder to locate wall studs (at least 16" apart).  
  - If no studs, use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for the TV’s weight.  
- **Mark & Align**:  
  - Use a pencil to mark stud positions or anchor points.  
  - Follow the mount’s instructions for bracket alignment (usually centered above the TV).  
- **Secure Tightly**:  
  - Drill pilot holes slightly smaller than the screws.  
  - Attach the bracket to studs first, then slide the TV into place.  
- **Safety Tip**: Use a step stool and wear safety glasses. Double-check weight limits!

### 🌿 **2. Hanging Planters**  
- **Lightweight Brackets**:  
  - Choose L-brackets or S-hooks rated for the planter’s weight.  
- **Secure Anchoring**:  
  - Anchor to studs or use high-quality wall hooks (e.g., 125-lb-rated).  
- **Step-by-Step**:  
  1. Place the planter on the hook.  
  2. Attach the bracket to the wall, ensuring it’s level.  
  3. Hang the planter from the bracket.  
- **Pro Tip**: Use a helper to hold the planter steady while drilling.  

### 📚 **3. Floating Shelves**  
- **Weight Distribution**:  
  - Place heaviest items (books, decor) at the **bottom** of the shelf to avoid tipping.  
- **Installation**:  
  - Mark shelf depth (usually 6–12" from wall) with a level.  
  - Use a drill bit slightly smaller than shelf brackets.  
  - Secure brackets into wall studs at both ends.  
- **Alignment**:  
  - Use a level to ensure shelves are parallel.  
  - Trim excess shelf edge with a utility knife.  

### ⚠️ **Critical Safety Reminders**  
- **Noise Control**: Keep drills to a minimum—use quick bursts and work early/late.  
- **Tools Needed**: Stud finder, level, measuring tape, drill, anchors, screws.  
- **Breaks**: Stretch every 30 minutes to prevent stiffness!  

### 💡 **Decorating Pro Tips**  
- **Art Hangers**: Use removable hooks or 3M Command strips for lightweight art.  
- **Theme**: Stick to 2–3 colors max for cohesion.  
- **Lighting**: Add LED strip lights under shelves for ambiance (landlord-friendly!).  

You’ve got this! Start with the heaviest item (TV) first, then progress to shelves and planters. Break tasks into 30-minute chunks to avoid fatigue. 🛠️✨
