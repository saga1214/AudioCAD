You know, upon second thought, I'd change my mind. I'd now say Mesopotamia is the better choice.

Here’s why: while Ancient Egypt wins on public appeal, Ancient Mesopotamia offers a more foundational and transformative educational experience. It’s the birthplace of writing, law, and cities. A well-curated Mesopotamian exhibit could be far more impactful and memorable, even if it's less familiar at first. It has the potential to create a deeper, more profound cultural understanding. So, for a museum aiming for real educational depth and a unique story, Mesopotamia is the winner.
