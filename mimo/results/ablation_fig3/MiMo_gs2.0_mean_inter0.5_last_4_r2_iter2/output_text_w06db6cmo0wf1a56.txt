Okay, planning for ten dads, ten kids, sugar-free sodas, real calzones, and cupcakes/brownies... let me put together a list for you. Here's a plan for the theme park bachelor party.

First, for drinks, you'll need water bottles for everyone, single-serve water bottles too, low-sugar juice boxes for the kids, and sugar-free sodas for the adults. Don't forget individual boxes of unsweetened iced tea and sports drink packets for hydration.

For snacks, grab cheese sticks, grapes, pretzels or trail mix in single-serve bags, baby carrots with pre-packaged hummus cups, and hard-boiled eggs.

For the main meal, since you're set on calzones, I'd suggest pre-ordering them from a local pizzeria. That way, they'll be ready to go and you won't have any cleanup. Sandwiches or wraps are another solid backup plan.

And for dessert, cupcakes are probably your best bet. They're already individual and clean. Or, you could get pre-packaged brownie boxes.

Just remember to pack plenty of coolers with ice packs, lots of trash bags for easy cleanup, and maybe some wet wipes for everyone. Hope you all have a blast!
