That's an excellent choice! Secret Harbour Beach Resort on St. Thomas offers calm, protected bays perfect for your brother. Here are some fantastic pressure-change-free activities for your week:

### **Beach & Water Fun**
1. **Snorkeling at Reef Bay or Coki Beach (St. John):**  
   Secret Harbour is a short boat ride from these protected bays. Coki Beach has vibrant coral gardens and abundant fish—ideal for beginners.  
2. **Kayaking from Secret Harbour:**  
   Paddle to nearby **Honeymoon Beach** or **Sugar Beach** for serene coastal exploration (no pressure changes!).  
3. **Island Hopping:**  
   Charter a day trip to **Waterlemon Cay** or **Salt Island**—flat, sandy beaches and shallow waters perfect for relaxation.  

### **Land Adventures (Low-Pressure)**
4. **Virgin Islands National Park (St. John):**  
   **Trunk Bay** has a calm, sheltered lagoon and a short, flat walk to a snorkeling trail. **Magens Bay** is another famous calm beach (flat and shallow).  
5. **Cruz Bay Shops & Stroll:**  
   Walk to Cruz Bay’s local shops, cafes, and **Soggy Dollar Bar** (famous for the Painkiller drink) right from Secret Harbour.  
6. **Horseback Riding on Reef Bay Trail (St. John):**  
   Gentle, flat trails through rainforests to secluded beaches—no steep climbs.  

### **Cultural & Local Experiences**
7. **Sailaway Celebration:**  
   Charter a private sailboat for sunset drinks and island-hopping—Secret Harbour’s location makes this easy!  
8. **Cultural Demonstrations:**  
   Visit **Cruz Bay’s Saturday Craft Fair** (if timing aligns) or **St. Croix’s Heritage Village** for cultural shows.  
9. **Sea Turtle Nesting Tours (Summer):**  
   Guided nighttime walks to observe nesting sea turtles (check park schedules).  

### **Birding Bonus**  
While Brown Booby sightings are rare onshore, keep an eye out for:  
- **Brown Noddy** (seabirds often near docks)  
- **Palm Warblers** in mangroves  
- **Herons** near Cruz Bay  

### **Pro Tips**  
- **Avoid:** Sudden altitude changes, rollercoasters, or steep hikes.  
- **Gear:** Rent snorkel gear at Secret Harbour or Reef Bay Dive Shop.  
- **Food:** Try **Cuz’s Kitchen** in Cruz Bay for Caribbean seafood—vegetarian options available.  

St. John’s beaches are among the most beautiful in the world—enjoy your pressure-free adventure! 🌴🚤
