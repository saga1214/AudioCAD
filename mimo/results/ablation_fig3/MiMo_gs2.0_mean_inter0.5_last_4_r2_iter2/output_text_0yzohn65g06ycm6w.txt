Here's a concise summary of solutions for your fussy cats and budget-friendly care:

### **Feeding Solutions**
1. **Controlled Feeding**  
   - **Microchip Feeders**: Only fussy cats can access their food. Greedy cats eat at their own pace.  
   - **Timed Feeders**: Release small portions throughout the day (e.g., 4x/day) to prevent binge-eating.  
   - **Room Separation**: Move fussy cats to a separate room before leaving home.  

2. **Budget Food Hacks**  
   - **Buy in Bulk**: Larger bags of quality food cost less per serving.  
   - **Mix & Stretch**: Combine small amounts of quality food with fillers (e.g., plain cooked rice) to make meals last longer. *Always consult a vet first.*  
   - **Supplement Safely**: Add occasional safe table scraps (e.g., plain cooked chicken/fish) when possible.  

### **Low-Cost Health Maintenance**
1. **Prevent Problems**  
   - **Hygiene**: Clean food/water bowls daily to avoid illness.  
   - **Weight Control**: Encourage play (e.g., string toys) to prevent obesity-related issues.  

2. **Community Resources**  
   - **Pet Food Banks**: Search online or via local shelters for free cat food/donations.  
   - **DIY Networks**: Post needs in community groups for unused food/supplies.  

3. **Prioritize Care**  
   - **Low-Cost Vet Access**: Use for essential services (spaying, neutering, vaccines). Avoid unnecessary visits unless urgent.  
   - **Monitor Closely**: Track early signs of illness (lethargy, appetite changes) to act quickly if needed.  

**Key Takeaway**: Combine controlled feeding with smart budgeting (bulk buys, rice mix-ins) and proactive prevention (hygiene, play) to keep cats healthy without breaking the bank. Focus on community support and prioritize essential vet care.
