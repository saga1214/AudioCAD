Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn inspiration! You’ve got the incredible fall foliage in the northern states, like Chihuahua and Nuevo León, and then those super vibrant, warm autumn colors down in the Yucatán Peninsula. 

If you’re looking for those classic, fiery fall scenes, you should probably aim for September or October. That’s peak color season in the north. But if you want to focus on coastal autumn vibes, maybe with palm trees and clear skies, then late October or November would be perfect for the Yucatán. 

Either way, you’ll get plenty of inspiration to practice those techniques you’ve been working on. Safe travels, and happy painting!
