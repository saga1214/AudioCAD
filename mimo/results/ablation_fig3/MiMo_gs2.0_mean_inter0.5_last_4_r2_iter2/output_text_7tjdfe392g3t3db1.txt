Great question! Here's how to tune strings using a tuner tool:

### 1. **Clip-On Tuner** (Best for Precision):  
   - **How to Use**:  
     - Attach the clip to your instrument’s headstock.  
     - Press the "tune" button.  
     - Pluck a string. The tuner displays the note (e.g., "A," "D," "G").  
     - Adjust the tuning peg until the tuner shows the correct note (e.g., "A" for the 6th string) and the needle stays centered.  

### 2. **Tuner App** (Most Accessible):  
   - **How to Use**:  
     - Open a tuner app (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune).  
     - Place your phone near the instrument’s soundhole/headstock.  
     - Pluck a string. The app displays the current pitch (e.g., "A#") and suggests adjustments (turn tuning peg clockwise/counter-clockwise).  
     - Repeat until the app confirms the correct note (e.g., "A").  

### Key Tips:  
- **Start with Open Strings**: Tune your instrument’s lowest open string first (e.g., E on guitar).  
- **Check Intonation**: After tuning, play a chord to ensure all strings sound harmonious.  
- **Phone Mic Sensitivity**: Hold the phone close to the sound source to avoid background noise.  

Both methods work for guitars, ukuleles, mandolins, and more. Happy tuning! 🎸
