Okay, pirate nautical beach party! Seven kids total including your birthday boy, meeting at the beach. Key challenges are: sand aversion, allergy to watermelon, pirate theme immersion, and packaging that prevents mess.

Let's build a full nautical menu with packaging solutions and allergy safety.

First, for themed appetizers or “buried treasure,” mini slider bars are perfect. You can theme them by using blue cheese as “sea” and yellow cheese for “sun.” Cut them into small individual portions to seal them in. Veggie sticks shaped like “ship hulls” are another idea. You can cut them into fun shapes and serve them with cream cheese dip as “mermaid treasure.” Cheese cubes can be called “gold coins.” And apple slices can be carved into “pirate coins” using cookie cutters.

For the main course, mini submarine sandwiches are a great choice. Cut them into triangles or circles, and you can even put a small flag on top. Ham and cheese is a safe, easy option. Mini pirate ship pizzas are also fun; you can cut the crusts into ship shapes.

For fruit and snacks, you already decided on grapes or apple slices as jewels. Goldfish crackers are called “mermaid coins,” and pretzel rods are masts. Pack everything in small treasure chests or portioned containers.

For drinks, themed juice boxes are a must. Naming them “pirate punch” or “sea water” adds to the fun. Individual water bottles can be themed as “treasure casks.”

And for the cake, since you're making it nautical-themed, that's awesome.

Just remember to label everything clearly for the allergy and use plenty of sealable packaging. This menu should keep everything sand-free and pirate-themed.
