Okay, you've got the basics, but there are a few more things you should have.

First, you'll definitely need a power drill and some screws. Screws are much stronger and more secure for attaching cabinets to studs than nails.

You'll also need some heavy-duty shims, which are basically small, flat pieces of wood for adjusting the cabinets to get them perfectly level.

And it's a good idea to have a small box of basic tools on hand, like a utility knife for trimming, a measuring tape, a pencil, and a hammer for tapping shims into place. 

Most importantly, please have everyone wear safety glasses, since you'll be hitting shims with a hammer and working with sharp tools. 

So, to recap, you'll need: a power drill, screws, shims, safety glasses, and a small toolbox with a utility knife, measuring tape, and a pencil. You're all set to get started!
