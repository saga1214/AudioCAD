Absolutely! Since Northern California warming up and swimming appeal to you, I'd suggest Big Sur. It's one of the most beautiful and warm destinations for a family vacation.

Here’s a detailed four-day itinerary I've put together for you, keeping in mind your daughter's dietary needs, warm weather goals, and the need for a car.

For Day One, you'll arrive in Monterey. I'd suggest staying at the Point Lobos Lodge, which is right on the coast and very family-friendly. For dinner, you can have a casual seafood night at the Fisherman's Grotto in the Wharf area, and just let them know about your daughter's Celiac needs.

On Day Two, you'll drive through the beautiful Pfeiffer Beach to the charming town of Carmel-by-the-Sea. You can spend the afternoon exploring Carmel, and I highly recommend a visit to the mission, Mission San Carlos. It's a great way to learn about California's history without a lot of climbing. For lunch, a fantastic gluten-free Mexican place called El Farolito has several locations, including one in Carmel.

Day Three is all about the water and swimming! You can start by driving to the beautiful McWay Falls in Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park. Then, you can swim and explore Pfeiffer Beach, which has those famous purple sand coves. You can even find a spot to pack a lunch and enjoy it by the ocean. For dinner, a place called Nepenthe is a must-visit. It's on a cliff overlooking the ocean, and while the food is a bit pricier, they are excellent at handling gluten-free requests. It's a truly magical experience.

For your last full day, Day Four, you can visit Hearst Castle. It's a bit of a drive, but it's an incredible place for a family to explore. There are tours, a huge mansion, and beautiful gardens. It's not a swimming spot, but it's full of history. You can have a nice, relaxed lunch at the castle's restaurant. For your final evening, you can drive back toward Monterey, stopping at a place like Cannery Row for dinner. The Ghirardelli Delight Chocolate Shop is a great gluten-free option there.

For getting around, renting a minivan or SUV is definitely the way to go. It'll give you plenty of space and make the road trips much easier. Just make sure to book your rental early, especially for the holidays.

And one last, very important thing: Alcatraz Celiac Disease is a great resource. You can find them online at alcatrazceliac dot com. They have a list of restaurants on Alcatraz Island that are very accommodating and can give you tips for your cruise. I hope you have an amazing trip!
