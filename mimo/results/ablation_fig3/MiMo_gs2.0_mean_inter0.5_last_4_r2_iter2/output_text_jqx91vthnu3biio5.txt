### 1. **Color-Coding Icons: Memory vs. Mental Load**  
   - **Memory Aid**: Color can enhance recall (e.g., red for urgent tasks, blue for creative projects).

### 2. **Switching Playlists: Focus vs. Variety**

### 4. **Timer Breaks: Gimmick or Gold?**  
   - **Not a Gimmick**: Pomodoro (25 mins work / 5 mins break) is proven to sustain focus.  
   - **Adjust for You**: Try 25/5, then 50/10, or 90/20. Move tasks *away* from your screen (stretch, hydrate).  
   - **Science Backs It**: Prevents decision fatigue and micro-restores energy.
