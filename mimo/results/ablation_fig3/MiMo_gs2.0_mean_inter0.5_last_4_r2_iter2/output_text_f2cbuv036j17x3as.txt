### **Brake Inspection**
1. **Visual Inspection**:  
   - Check brake pads: Look for uneven wear (grinding = serious issue), shiny spots, or thinness (<3mm).  
   - Inspect rotors/drums for cracks, warping, or excessive grooves.  
   - Ensure calipers/pistons slide smoothly (no sticking).  

2. **Brake Fluid**:  
   - Top up fluid in the reservoir (check level).  
   - Replace fluid if dark/brittle (every 2–3 years; contamination causes spongy brakes).  

3. **Warning Signs**:  
   - Squealing/grinding noises (pads/wheels).  
   - Vibration when braking (worn rotors).  
   - Brake pedal feels "spongy" (air in system or fluid issues).  

> ⚠️ **Significant Wear**: Pads <3mm, uneven grooves on rotors, or fluid contamination need immediate replacement.

### **Spark Plug Replacement (Diesel Van)**  
Diesel engines often have spark plugs in the cylinder head (accessible via the valve cover). Tools needed:  
- Socket wrench (10mm–14mm)  
- Spark plug socket (rubber mallet)  
- Torque wrench  
- Gap tool (check gap per manual—usually 0.030–0.040")  
- New spark plugs (consult manual for type; e.g., Champion RC12ECC)  

**Steps**:  
1. Cool engine completely.  
2. Remove old plugs (gently tap socket to dislodge).  
3. Clean cylinder walls (use compressed air).  
4. Install new plugs:  
   - Set correct gap (use gap tool).  
   - Tighten to spec (typically 15–25 ft-lbs; avoid overtightening).  
5. Reinstall valve cover/seals.
