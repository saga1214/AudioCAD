Of course, I can help you with that! Here's how to make sure your stir-fry noodles turn out great.

First, and most importantly for stir-frying, start with salted water. You can just add a big pinch of salt to your pot. This makes the noodles taste better and helps them hold their shape.

Second, don't overcook them! Stir-fried noodles should be just 'al dente,' meaning they have a little bit of a firm bite to them. You can test them a minute or two before the package's suggested time. They should be tender but still feel chewy.

Finally, the most crucial step is to drain them well. As soon as they're done, pour them into a colander to get all the water out. If you leave them in the pot, they'll get mushy.

Then, just toss your drained noodles with the sauce and any chopped veggies you have. You're going to do great!
