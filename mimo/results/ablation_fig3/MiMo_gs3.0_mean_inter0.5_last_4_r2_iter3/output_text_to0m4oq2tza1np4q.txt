That's a fantastic combo! Salmon and pickles/pickle juice can make amazing healthy additions to your meal. Here are a few ideas:

1.  **Pickle Juice Salad Dressing:**
    *   Whisk together pickle juice, olive oil, a squeeze of extra lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and maybe a touch of honey or maple syrup if you like. Thin it slightly with water or more olive oil if needed. Drizzle over a simple salad with mixed greens, cucumber, and maybe some chickpeas (from your garden or canned) for protein. *Bonus: The vinegar in pickle juice is great for digestion.*

2.  **Pickle Salmon Sauce:**
    After pan-searing or baking your marinated salmon, pour the leftover marinade (olive oil, lemon, honey marinade) into a small saucepan over low heat and simmer for 5-10 minutes to thicken slightly. Stir in a splash of pickle juice for tang and complexity. Serve the sauce over the cooked salmon. *Pairs perfectly!*

3.  **Pickle & Salmon Skewers:**
    Alternate chunks of salmon with cucumber slices or bell pepper strips on skewers. Brush lightly with olive oil and grill or bake. Serve with a dollop of plain Greek yogurt mixed with a little pickle juice and dill. *High protein, refreshing!*

4.  **Pickle Snack Pairing:**
    Simply serve cucumber slices or other veggie sticks alongside your salmon and enjoy them with the pickle juice as a dipping sauce. *Great for fiber and crunch.*

**Why Pickle Juice?**
*   **Hydration & Electrolytes:** Great for rehydration after your run, similar to coconut water but with more tang.
*   **Digestion Aid:** The vinegar content can help with bloating for some people by supporting gut health.
*   **Flavor Boost:** Adds a fantastic tangy crunch without needing a lot of salt.

Enjoy your delicious and thoughtful dinner! Pickle adventures are always welcome. 😊
