Here are five fantastic Vancouver road trips for a new family with a Golden girl!

First, a short one-hour trip to the Fraser Valley. You can visit a winery, tour a fruit orchard, and try the famous farm-to-table food. It's a perfect day trip.

Second, a three-hour trip to the Okanagan Lake area. You can swim in the stunning lakes, visit a vineyard, and see the beautiful drive-up church gardens. The wine tasting is fantastic there.

Third, a four-hour trip to Whistler. It's the classic mountain escape. She can run off-leash in Blackcomb Canyon, and you can take a gondola ride or go for a hike. The mountain views are breathtaking.

Fourth, a four-and-a-half-hour trip to Victoria. You can visit the Butchart Gardens, walk the inner harbour, and enjoy a lovely seaside lunch. It's a very charming city.

And fifth, a six-hour trip to Tofino on the west coast. This is for a true adventure. You can surf, hike on the wild Pacific Rim trails, and see sea stacks. It's a bit rainy, but it's a magical place. Have a wonderful time exploring!
