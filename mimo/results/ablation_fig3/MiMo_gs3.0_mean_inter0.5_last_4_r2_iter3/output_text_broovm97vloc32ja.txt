That's fantastic! Illustrations for a children's book definitely add a huge amount of value to your query package. Most agents **do** expect to see working samples of the illustrations to assess the visual appeal of the book.

So, when you write your query letter, you'll also prepare what's called a "query bundle." This package will include your query letter, your manuscript, and—this is the key part—a selection of your best illustrations. You don't need to submit the entire 22-page book, just about five to ten of your strongest, most representative pages.

Reputable agents understand that picture books are a collaboration between writer and artist, so they'll evaluate your submission based on both the text and the art.
