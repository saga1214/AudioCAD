Sounds fun! Pirates at the beach is an exciting birthday. For finger foods besides dessert:

Start with “pirate appetizers.” Mini hot dogs cut in half work well and won't fall apart much. theme them as cannonballs. Cheese cubes are like buried treasure and are sand-friendly. You could also pack everything sealed in snack containers shaped like small treasure chests.

Fruit options without watermelon could be grapes labeled as pirate jewels or apple slices. Veggie sticks are also a good pirate jewel idea.

Snack options like goldfish crackers themed as coins or pretzel rods as ship masts are both easy to hold and low on the sand risk.

Drinks individual juice boxes themed creatively will keep spills to a minimum.

Small portions sealed in fun packaging—that should solve the sand issue and keep him immersed wearing his outfit.
