</Acoustic guitars usually tune to what's called standard tuning, which is E, A, D, G, B, E, going from the thickest string to the thinnest.

With either a physical clip tuner or an app, what you do is strum each string one by one. The tool/tuner will make a beep when a note is out of tune. You'll need to either pluck the string again or gently turn the tuning peg on the headstock until the tuner's needle points to zero or the beep stops. Just repeat that for each of the six strings until they're all in the right place.
