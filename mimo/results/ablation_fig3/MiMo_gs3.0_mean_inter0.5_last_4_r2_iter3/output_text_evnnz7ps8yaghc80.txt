A structured plan is an excellent idea. Here’s a possible weekly schedule:

On Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday, you could practice with tennis balls for fifteen to twenty minutes.

On Wednesday and Friday, dedicate twenty to thirty minutes to practicing one-handed two-balls with beanbags to reinforce your fundamentals.

On Saturday, you can use scarves for fifteen or twenty minutes to slow everything down and perfect patterns.

Finally, on Sunday, you can have a free day to use any tool—beanbags, tennis balls, or scarves—or even just to relax without stress.
