To care for cats while struggling financially:

**Feeding Solutions:**
- Use timed or microchip-activated feeders so greedy cats don't clear bowls, while fussy ones eat.
- Stretch quality food by mixing small amounts with fillers like plain cooked rice (consult vet if doing this).

**Cost-Saving Care:**
- Search for local pet food banks, shelters offering help/vouchers, and community supply donations.
- Bulk-buy larger bags of quality food per serving to lower cost.

**Preventative Health:**
- Prioritize low-cost vet care like spaying/neutering and vaccinations.
- Practice prevention: Clean food/water bowls daily, offer basic play (homemade toys), and brush cats to reduce hairballs.

**Self-Care:**
- Explain your situation to vets/shelters—they may advise or assist with minor health issues.
- Do the best you can consistently; early issue spotting at home is critical if vet follow-up isn't possible.
