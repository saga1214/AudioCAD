For diesel camper vans, regular maintenance before a long trip is really important. To check your brakes, one of the simplest things is to listen—if you hear a high-pitched squealing when you brake, that could be a worn pad indicator. Or if you feel like your stopping distance is longer than it used to be, that’s a sign. Also, if you see a lot of dust or grime coming from under the van, that’s usually a red flag. For spark plugs, they can last for tens of thousands of diesel engine miles, but if they’re old, they can start to misfire, which you might notice as rough running or poor fuel economy. If you’re comfortable with basic tools, you can usually access them through the hood, but it’s often best to take it to a trusted mechanic—diesel engines can be tight spaces and it’s not always straightforward. Either way, getting a professional check is probably the safest bet.
