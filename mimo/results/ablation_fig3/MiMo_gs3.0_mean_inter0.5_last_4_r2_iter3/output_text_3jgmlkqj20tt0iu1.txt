Initally nervous is pretty normal in a conversation like this. Two strategies can help. First, you could request a formal performance review meeting during the annual evaluation period. This is more structured and can sometimes reduce the pressure.

Second, you could ask to schedule a brief, specific meeting with your supervisor. For instance, you could send a short email that says something like, 'Hi, could we schedule a 15 to 20-minute meeting to discuss my performance and career path?'
