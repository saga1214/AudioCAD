That's a great goal—to be respectful while still seeking clarity. You can definitely write an email that does both.

Here are a few pointers. Start with a professional subject line, like "Meeting to Improve for Upcoming Exams."

In the body of the email, you could write something like: "I scored a 64 on the recent essay-style exam and wanted to schedule a brief meeting. My goal is to do better on future tests, and I'd be grateful to understand what points could have been developed more effectively in my essay."

Then, you could ask: "Would you have 15 to 20 minutes available sometime next week to discuss this briefly?"

This keeps the focus on your future success and frames the request as low-pressure feedback, rather than a challenge to the past grade.
