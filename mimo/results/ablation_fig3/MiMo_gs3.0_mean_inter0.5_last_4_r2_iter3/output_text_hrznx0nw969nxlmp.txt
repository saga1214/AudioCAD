That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour Beach Resort is peaceful and calm, perfect for snorkeling right off the shore. Here’s a rundown of fun daytime activities nearby:

### **Right from Secret Harbour:**
1. **Snorkeling** – The calm bay has vibrant coral reefs and occasional sea turtles. A short walk to **Coki Beach** (about 10 minutes) offers similar snorkeling spots with more coral diversity.

### **Nearby Beaches & Trails:**
2. **Mags Beach** – Just a 5-minute drive away. Known for its soft sand, turquoise water, and snorkeling. Stop by the beach bar for tropical drinks!
3. **Trunk Bay** – Famous for its underwater snorkeling trail (a glass-bottom boat tour is included with resort access). The nearby **Trunk Bay Trail** offers lush rainforest hikes with ocean views.
4. **Sandy Spit** – A secluded, quiet beach 10 minutes south. Great for sunbathing, reading, or spotting seabirds.

### **Boating Adventures (Since Flights Cancelled!):**
5. **Cruz Bay Shops & St. John History** – Explore local crafts, bookshops, and the island’s history at the Cruz Bay Cultural Center.
6. **Coral Bay** – A scenic drive north to this vibrant village with shops, art galleries, and the **Coral Bay Pier** for kayaking/paddleboarding.
7. **Salt River Paddle Tour** – Kayak through mangroves to hidden lagoons (calm, shallow water ideal for your brother).

### **Frenchtown (East End of St. Thomas):**
8. **Seafood Dining** – If you wander to Frenchtown (15-20 mins from Secret Harbour), try **Black Horse** (oceanfront dining) or **Duffy’s Love Shack** (live music too!).
9. **Paradise Point** – A cliffside restaurant with ocean views (try their seafood pasta!).

### **Pro Tips:**
- **Sun Protection**: St. John has intense sun. Use reef-safe sunscreen, hats, and shaded cabanas.
- **Snorkel Gear**: Rent gear from shops in Cruz Bay or Coral Bay.
- **Local Eats**: Try *johnnycakes* (fried bread) and *peanut sauce* (local stew).

Boating gives you ultimate freedom—chart a day trip to **Waterlemon Cay** (a tiny island with snorkeling caves) or **Salt Island** (for secluded beaches). Have an amazing week! 🌴🚤
