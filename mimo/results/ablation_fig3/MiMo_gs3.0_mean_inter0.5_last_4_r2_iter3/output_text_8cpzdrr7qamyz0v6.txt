### 1. **10% Gain Scenario**

     Total cost = $6,287 + fees (e.g., $10 commission) ≈ **$6,297**.  
   - **Target sale price** = $62.87 + 10% = **$69.16/share**.  
   - **Gain per share** = $69.16 - $62.87 = **$6.29**.  
   - **Total gain** = 100 shares × $6.29 = **$629**.  
   - **Profit** = $629 - $10 commission ≈ **$619**.  
   - **Return on $10,000** = ($619 / $10,000) × 100 = **6.19%**.  

### 2. **10% Max Loss Scenario**  
   Futures are **leveraged** (you only need a fraction of the total value down, called **margin**).  
   - Example: If margin is **10% ($1,029)**, you control $10,000.  
   - **Stop-loss hit** at $90/share:  
     Loss per share = $62.87 - $90 = **-$27.13**.  
   - **Total loss** = 100 shares × -$27.13 = **-$2,713**.  
   - **Loss on margin** = ($2,713 / $1,029) × 100 ≈ **263%** of your margin.  
   - **Real loss** = ($2,713 / $10,000) × 100 = **27.13%** of your $10,000.  
   - **Remaining capital** ≈ $7,287.  

### Key Risks with Futures:
- **Leverage**: A 5% market move can wipe out 50% of your margin.  
- **Margin Calls**: If losses exceed your margin, you may be forced to sell at a worse price.  
- **Fees/Spreads**: Bid-ask spreads and commissions reduce profits.  
- **Volatility**: Futures prices can swing wildly in a month.
