High sodium intake over time is linked to other health issues like high blood pressure. Plus, tinned foods and sauces like these often contain sodium that was added during processing, not just from natural sources.

If reducing sodium is a goal, you could try using low-sodium or salt-free versions of soy/oyster sauce, choose no-salt-added tinned tomatoes, or rinse them before using. You could also add flavor with herbs or spices instead.
