That still misses the key detail about the protein required to form the shell.

Even though a non-chicken's DNA was inside the egg, the physical building blocks of its eggshell don't contain that one special hen-only protein. For an egg to be biologically classified as a 'chicken egg', a hen had already had to exist at some point in its production. Since the creature laying it wasn't quite a chicken, its egg couldn't technically be called a chicken egg until the first chicken came out to call it one.
