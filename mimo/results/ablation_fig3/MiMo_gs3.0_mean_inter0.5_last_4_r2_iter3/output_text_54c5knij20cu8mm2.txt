First, let’s tackle the brain thing. The left-brain versus right-brain idea is largely a myth. The brain is more integrated than that. Both sides work together on everything. Creativity can happen in logical parts of the brain, and structure can come from the same areas. It’s not a clean split. But the metaphor is useful for people to grasp the difference between analytical and creative thinking, even if it’s not how the brain actually works.
