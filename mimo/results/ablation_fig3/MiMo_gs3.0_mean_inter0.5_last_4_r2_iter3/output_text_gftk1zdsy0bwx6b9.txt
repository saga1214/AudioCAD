A mini gingerbread house adds a lot of fun and value for your parents—they'll love it! Just remember to ship it flat-pack to avoid it getting crushed.

And adding more cookies to other friends is a great gesture. Gingerbread pairs really well with Snickerdoodle, and Chocolate Chip goes nicely with Oatmeal or Snickerdoodle too. Just make sure the house kit fits snugly in the Pringles can along with the cookies to keep everything from breaking.
