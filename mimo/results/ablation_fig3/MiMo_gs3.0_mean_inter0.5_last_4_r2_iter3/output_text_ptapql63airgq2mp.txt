For movie night exotic snacks, since you’ve got foodie frames and want unique choices inspired by Japan, here’s a mix: 

Start with umami bombs—fermented kimchi seaweed with tamarind glaze, or maybe some sweet and spicy dried squid with tamarind bits. You could set up a DIY yuzu-marinated fruit bar with peaches, pomelos, and other Japanese fruits.

For more interactive options, a matcha station where guests can mix their own matcha drinks would be fun. Or a takoyaki station, Japan’s famous octopus balls, made with a special batter and topped with different sauces. 

To keep it simple but unique, you could do a charcuterie board but with Japanese flavors—like miso-marinated meats, pickled vegetables, and maybe some fancy wagashi desserts. Does that sound like enough variety for fifteen family & friends?
