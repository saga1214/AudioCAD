Here are quick drink recommendations using your ingredients:  

### **Vodka Cocktails**  
1. **Classic Vodka Soda**:  
   - 2 oz vodka + fresh lime juice + soda water + lime twist.  
   *Elegant, light, and refreshing.*  

2. **Vodka Cranberry**:  
   - 2 oz vodka + fresh cranberry juice + splash of soda + mint sprig.  
   *Festive but refined.*  

### **Argentine Fernet Mixers**  
1. **Fernet Cola (Fernet-Cola)**:  
   - 2 oz Fernet + cola + splash of lime juice.  
   *The traditional Argentinian way—bold, herbal, and fizzy.*  

2. **Fernet Ginger**:  
   - 2 oz Fernet + ginger ale + lemon twist.  
   *Slightly sweet with a spicy kick.*
