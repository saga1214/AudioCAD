For winter chicks integrated into your adult coop after five weeks, avoid any loose bedding like pine shavings or sand in *their specific area*. Loose bedding can create steam and moisture issues between the different flocks.

The safest and most effective bedding for contained chicks is simply to use a thick layer of hardware cloth, cut to fit the floor and walls of their section. This insulates from the concrete floor, it's completely draft-proof, and it provides them with a solid, non-slip surface to huddle on. No heat lamp, no fire risk, and it's reusable. That's the best choice.
