### For Tailoring (if you go that route):
1. **High-Quality Thread Set**: A premium set of colorful threads (e.g., CottonWool or Gutermann) – essential for precision work.

4. **Custom Fabric Sample**: A small piece of luxurious fabric (silk, wool, or tweed) to practice alterations or new garments.  

### For Woodworking:
1. **Essential Clamps**: A set of bar clamps or quick-release clamps – foundational tools often overlooked but critical.  
2. **Pre-Cut Wood Kits**: A beginner’s project kit (e.g., a small stool or shelf) with pre-measured wood.  
3. **Sharpening Stones**: Honing guide + sharpening stones (e.g., diamond plates) – keeps saws/chisels at peak performance.  

### For Clay (Pottery/Sculpting):
1. **Portable Pottery Kit**: A hand-milled clay kit + small roller for outdoor sculpting.  
2. **Sculpting Tools**: A set of silicone or metal shaping tools (e.g., loop tools, ribs, needles).  
3. **Air-Dry Clay**: Great for practicing without a kiln – eco-friendly and budget-friendly.  

### General Hands-On Hobby Gifts:
- **Experience Vouchers**: Pottery class, leatherworking workshop, or woodworking demo.  
- **Subscription Boxes**: "Inkwell" (for pen enthusiasts) or "Woodworking Essentials" monthly boxes.  
- **Handmade Tools**: A custom-forged needle or a hand-carved chisel – supports artisans and adds uniqueness.  

### Key Tip:  
> Prioritize **tools that enable progress** (not just consumption). For example:
