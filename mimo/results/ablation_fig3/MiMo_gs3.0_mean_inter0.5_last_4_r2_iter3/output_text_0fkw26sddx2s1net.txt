For miso or cucumber starter, you'll need miso paste, water, dashi or just some vegetable broth, and then a cucumber, some rice vinegar, sugar, sesame seeds, and soy sauce. If he prefers matcha ice cream, you'll need about two tablespoons of good quality matcha powder, one can of heavy whipping cream, one can of sweetened condensed milk, a teaspoon of vanilla if you like, and a pinch of salt. For matcha ice cream, it's important the matcha is sifted to keep it smooth.
