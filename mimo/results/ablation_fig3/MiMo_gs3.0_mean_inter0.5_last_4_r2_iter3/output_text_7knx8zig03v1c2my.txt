That's a great question—clicker training is one of the most effective methods for teaching new behaviors! Here’s a simple breakdown:

### **How Clicker Training Works**  
1. **The Clicker**: It’s a simple, noise-making tool (like a pen clicker). It acts as a "bridge" between your dog’s action and the reward.  
2. **Training Process**:  
   - **Step 1**: Hold the clicker. Ask Cosmo to do a behavior (e.g., sit).  
   - **Step 2**: The *instant* he sits, press the clicker and give him a treat.  
   - **Step 3**: Repeat 10–20 times. The click tells him, "Yes! That’s exactly right!" before the treat confirms it.  
3. **Why It Works**: The click is precise and unambiguous. Over time, your dog learns that *click* = instant reward, making training faster and clearer than just verbal praise.

### **Treat Recommendations**  
- **Best Flavors**: Small pieces of cooked chicken, turkey, hot dog, cheese, peanut butter (xylitol-free!), or freeze-dried liver.  
- **Tips**:  
  - Use tiny, bite-sized treats (smaller than his head).  
  - Hide treats in a puzzle toy to keep training fun.  
  - Rotate flavors to keep him excited.  

### **Putting It Together (Example: Teaching "Sit")**  
1. Hold a treat near Cosmo’s nose and slowly move it up, over his head.  
2. When his bottom touches the ground, *click* immediately and give the treat.  
3. Repeat 20 times a day. Soon, he’ll sit the *moment* he sees the clicker!  

With clicker training, Cosmo will learn faster and more reliably—plus it’s fun for both of you! 🐶
