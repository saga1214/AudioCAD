Let’s break it down task by task. Drywall stuff can range, small tub compound maybe $8, sandpaper sponge around $3, putty knife $5, a pint of primer/paint if needed is about $12, nails/adhesive is around $5, and a piece of matching molding, if needed, could be anywhere from $10 to $30 depending on style.

Cake items add up less: two cups AP flour ~$3, sugar ~$4, baking powder tiny, stick of butter ~$4, three eggs depend on region, cup of milk ~$2, extract teaspoons ~$1 frosting supplies are small. Pan depends on size.

Plus decorations for the day, balloons might be $5, movie game supplies maybe another $5.

Let’s say high-end drywall $50, cake baking $20, birthday extras $15. Total around $85. It’s always good to have a little extra, so maybe budget $100 to be safe. You can adjust as you go!
