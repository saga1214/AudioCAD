Okay, planning for ten dads, ten kids, sugar-free sodas, real calzones if possible, cupcakes or brownies for dessert—all clean grab-and-go options. Let's create a shopping list that covers drinks, snacks, main food, and dessert, keeping mess to a minimum.

For drinks, you'll need water bottles, sugar-free sodas for adults, low-sugar juice boxes for kids, and maybe some electrolyte drinks for everyone on a hot day.

For snacks, single-serve items are key, like cheese sticks, grapes, pretzels, trail mix, baby carrots with pre-pack hummus, yogurt cups, and fruit cups that don't need syrup.

For the main meal, since real calzones are preferred, your best bet is to find a local pizza place that can make and deliver a large quantity of them to your house. Order ten or twelve to be safe, and make sure they are individually wrapped so they stay warm in the coolers.

And for dessert, definitely go with cupcakes. They're the perfect choice for an easy, grab-and-go celebration treat.
