Of course! Here's a full list of four Kentucky fall-style cocktails with all the measurements, laid out for your bartenders.

First up, the Paper Plane. You'll need two ounces of bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and fresh lemon juice each at one ounce. Shake those with ice, strain into a coupé glass.

Next, the Whiskey Sour without the raw egg white. That’ll be two ounces of rye or bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and optionally, if you do want foam, half an ounce of fresh egg white.

Then, your Old Fashioned: two ounces of bourbon, one sugar cube or about three-quarters of an ounce of simple syrup, three or four dashes of Angostura bitters, an orange twist, and optionally an ice cube or egg white for texture.

The Aviation will have two ounces of gin, half an ounce of maraschino liqueur, a quarter-ounce of crème de violette, and half an盎司 of fresh lemon juice. Shake those up and serve over ice in a coupé glass.

And for that Gin cocktail you wanted, the third gin drink, I recommend the French 75. That's two ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and then top it with Champagne or Prosecco to taste.

Just a couple of final tips: if you skip the egg white in the Whiskey Sour, the drink will still be great. And make sure to use fresh lemon juice in all of these—it really makes a difference.
