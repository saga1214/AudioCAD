Listing some friendly dog options: First, you both might want to look into Labrador Retrievers—they’re known for being friendly, easygoing, and great with kids, but larger, so landlord-approved 'not too big' is a consideration. Another one is a Cocker Spaniel, which is small-to-medium-sized, affectionate, and less noisy alone. Next, a Basset Hound is chill, doesn’t mind being left alone too much, and low-shed compared to Maine Coone levels—but can be noisy. Another idea is a Lab and Beagle mix, which is friendly, social, and trainable. And last, a Golden Retriever is a classic people-pleaser, but larger.
