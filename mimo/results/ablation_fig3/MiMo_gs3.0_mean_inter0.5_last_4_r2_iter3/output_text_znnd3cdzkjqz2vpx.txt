You've got the basics covered, but I'd strongly recommend a few more tools to make the job go much smoother.

First, you'll definitely want a power drill and some wall anchors. Habitat restore cabinets often have holes in the back for screwing into the wall, and using anchors in non-stud areas will make sure they're really secure.

Also, a good measuring tape is essential for spacing countertops, making sure cabinets are flush, and checking lengths. Shims will be crucial for making sure everything is perfectly level before it's sealed with countertops. And finally, a utility knife will be handy for trimming any excess packaging or other materials.

With those, you'll be all set for a safe and successful installation!
