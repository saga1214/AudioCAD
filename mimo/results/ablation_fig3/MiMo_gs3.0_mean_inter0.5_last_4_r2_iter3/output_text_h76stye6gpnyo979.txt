It sounds like balancing big ambitions does require getting really specific.

A helpful approach is to match each passion project directly to a savings goal and create small weekly or monthly tasks for it.

For Bolivia Salt Flats, you could research flights one night and add it to a Bolivia fund you're saving for. For Faroe Islands Puffins, you could just spend ten minutes one evening writing down 3 things you'd need to know to plan the trip, and add it to a travel fund.

By organizing these ambitions like tasks you would for home renovations—for instance, by setting deadlines and tracking your progress—you can tackle multiple big things without feeling completely overwhelmed.
