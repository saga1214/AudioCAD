Of course, I can give you a clear, traditional format that's always safe and effective. Let's go through it from top to bottom.

First, at the very top, just have your first name, last name in 11 to 12-point font. Right below that, on the same line, is your contact info: email, phone number, city. Icons are best omitted just to avoid any automated system problems. Adding a LinkedIn profile link here is a great idea. Remember to place the contact info directly below your name instead of to the side.

Next up is a short, one or two-sentence professional summary that highlights your most relevant skills and career goals.

Then, the "Experience" section. This is your main area. For each relevant job, list: the company name, your job title, the start and end dates, and then just one or two brief bullet points for each key achievement.

After that, you have your "Relevant Experience" section for jobs in the field you're targeting now, which helps overcome any long gaps.

Then comes "Education". Here you'll list your degrees, schools, expected graduation dates, and add academic honors right under the corresponding degree.

If you want, you can add a "Skills" section. It's optional, but if you do, list about five to seven of your top, most relevant skills, like programming languages or software you're good at.

"Additional Work History" is another optional section for any other jobs you want to include.

Finally, you can have a "Certifications" section for any professional certifications you hold.

The most important thing is to be concise and make it easy for a recruiter to find what they need. And don't forget to save it as a PDF!
