For activities, small experiences often have the biggest impact. Chartering a small boat for a few hours to visit tide pools could let you both focus on something other than each other.

You could also combine something adventurous with something romantic. For example, after a simple kayaking trip, enjoy a picnic lunch on a secluded beach.

Another idea is to book an experience, like a short deep-sea fishing trip in the morning, which could lead to a quiet evening of cooking the fresh catch together.
