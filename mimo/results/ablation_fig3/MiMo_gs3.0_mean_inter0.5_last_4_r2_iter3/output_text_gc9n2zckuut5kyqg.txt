Absolutely! With budget in mind, here’s a cost-effective dessert plan that’s kid-friendly, awareness-conscious, and affordable:

### Budget-Friendly Dessert Ideas:
1. **Fresh Fruit Skewers or Salad**:  
   - **Cost**: Very low (use seasonal fruits like berries, bananas, melons).  
   - **Why**: Kids love colorful fruit; naturally sweet and no added sugar.  
   - **Tip**: Add a sprinkle of granola or nuts for texture (optional).  

2. **Homemade Mini Oatmeal Cookies**:  
   - **Cost**: Moderate (use oats, eggs, applesauce instead of butter/sugar).  
   - **Why**: Lower-sugar alternative, easy to bake in batches on barbecue cookers.  
   - **Tip**: Add dried fruit or mini chocolate chips for fun.  

3. Diabetes-Friendly Frozen Yogurt Cups:  
   - **Cost**: Low (use plain Greek yogurt, honey, and frozen berries).  
   - **Why**: Kids adore frozen treats; protein-rich and no refined sugar.  
   - **Tip**: Layer yogurt and fruit in cups for easy assembly.  

4. **"Dessert" Fruit Kebabs**:  
   - **Cost**: Minimal (grilled pineapple, melon, or peaches on skewers).  
   - **Why**: Light, healthy, and visually appealing for kids.  

5. **DIY Ice Cream Sundae Bar (Sugar-Free)**:  
   - **Cost**: Low (use sugar-free vanilla ice cream + toppings like crushed nuts, cocoa powder, or fresh fruit).  
   - **Why**: Interactive and customizable; lets kids "decide" sweetness.  

### Beverage Budget Hack:
- Offer **infused water** (cucumber-mint or lemon-berry) instead of sugary sodas. It’s free and refreshing!  

### Key Cost-Saving Tips:
- **Volunteer Help**: Assign family members to bake desserts or prep fruit.  
- **Bulk Buying**: Purchase oats, nuts, or frozen fruit in bulk.  
- **Donations**: Ask local bakeries for leftover cookies (often free!) or fruit from family gardens.
