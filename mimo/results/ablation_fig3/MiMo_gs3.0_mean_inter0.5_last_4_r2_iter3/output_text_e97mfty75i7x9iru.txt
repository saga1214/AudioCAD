Take it one step at a time.

Rest as much as you can is the most important thing.

Then, drink plenty of fluids.

Eat what and when you can to keep your strength up.

Antibiotics won't help because the flu is a virus.

Honey can soothe a cough.

Steamy showers or humidifiers can help with congestion.

Over-the-counter medicines can help with the fever and aches.

Your doctor didn't give an antibiotic on purpose—that's normal.

And if you feel horrible, that's understandable while your body fights the virus.

I hope you feel better soon.
