Okay, efficiency is a broad term. If we're talking about cost efficiency and scalability, solar energy still takes the lead. But reliability and consistency point strongly toward hydropower. Wind is a good middle ground, depending on the location. So, if I *had* to pick just one overall... I’d stick with solar again. Its versatility, decreasing costs, and ease of deployment give it a strong edge. But the best choice really depends on the specific location and what you need.
