### Now for Winter Fun!  
#### Maple Syrup Tapping (DIY):  
- **Drill bit/spile size**: Five-sixteents or three-eighths inch bit (~1.5–2" deep). Angle upward for easy flow.  
- **When to tap**: Freezing nights, above-freez temps days (sap flows!). Bucket/tubing collects drips.  

#### Snowshoeing Gear DIY Tips:  
- Rent/buy lightweight snowshoes first.  
- Add traction: Attach rubberized ice cleats to boots.  
- Pack: Warm layers, waterproof boots, headlamp (snowy days get dark fast!).  

#### Other Winter Activities:  
- **Ice fishing**: Inspect ice thickness (use ice auger/chisel). Drill holes, add bait, and fish!  
- **Snowshoe trails**: Find local groomed paths or build your own with rakes/snowblowers.  
- **Winter hiking**: Explore snowy parks with crampons for icy slopes.  
- **Ice sculpting**: Use hand tools to carve art outdoors!  
- **Snow tubing**: Build a ramp or rent at parks.  

Stay warm and enjoy the adventure! ❄️🎣
