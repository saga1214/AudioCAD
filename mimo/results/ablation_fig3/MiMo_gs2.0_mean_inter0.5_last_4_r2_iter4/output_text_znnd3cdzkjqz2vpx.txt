You've got the basics covered, but there are a few more things you might need.

First, you'll definitely need a stud finder. Habitat Restore cabinets usually require screws into studs, so you'll need to find and mark every single stud where you plan to hang them.

Second, it's really important to have a level and a tape measure. You'll use the level to make sure everything is perfectly straight, and the tape measure to make sure your upper and lower cabinets are the same height and spaced evenly.

Finally, you'll want a good pair of work gloves to protect your hands from splinters or sharp edges, and maybe a pair of safety glasses just in case any screws or debris fly. You're all set for a safe and successful installation!
