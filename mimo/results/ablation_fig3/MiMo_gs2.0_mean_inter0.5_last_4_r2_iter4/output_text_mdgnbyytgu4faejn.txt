Of course. Here is a clear, standard format for a resume, broken down into sections and the order of information within each one. This should give you a solid template to follow.

First, at the very top, you'll have your name in about a 14-point font. To the right of your name, aligned with it, you'll put your contact details. This should include your professional email address, your phone number, your current city, state, and zip code. I'd suggest using icons, but in smaller 9-point font as you were, just to save space. It's also very helpful to include a link to your LinkedIn profile here.

Next, you'll have a professional summary, which is about three to five sentences. This is your elevator pitch, where you briefly state your career focus and highlight two or three of your most relevant skills.

After that is your experience section. For any roles that are highly relevant to the jobs you're applying for, create a section called "Relevant Experience" and list them here with dates. For all other work history, you can create a section called "Additional Work History" and list it chronologically, with the most recent job at the top.

Then comes your education section. Here, you'll list your degree, the school's name, and your graduation date. You can also add details like your GPA if it's high, or awards you received like "Graduated with Highest Honors" right under the corresponding degree.

Following education, you'll have a skills section. This is a good place to list your hard skills, like software you're proficient in or languages you speak, and a few key soft skills.

You can also include other sections like Certifications, if they're relevant, or Volunteer Experience if it's pertinent to the job.

Finally, at the very bottom, you can have a optional "Additional Information" section for things like your professional affiliations or interests. 

This structure should give you a clear, professional, and effective resume that showcases your experience effectively. Good luck!
