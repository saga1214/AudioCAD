### 2. **In Cruz Bay (5-minute walk)**

### 3. **St. John’s Snorkeling Spots (Shallow & Calm)**

   - **Cinnamon Bay**: Gentle slope into the water with sea turtles and parrotfish. Great for beginners.
   - **Honeymoon Beach**: Secluded, shallow bay with soft sand and calm waves.

### 4. **Boating Trip (Pressure-Free Adventure!)**
