For candy, you'll want to focus on Halloween classics that almost everyone loves, like Reese's Cups, chocolate bars like Snickers or Milky Way, and gummies. Mini candy bars are also great because guests can grab one or two instead of eating a whole thing. You could also throw in some classic candies like jawbreakers or Tootsie Rolls. If you're worried about allergies again, you can find allergy-friendly candies made with natural dyes or get a mix of vegan and gluten-free options. Just try to buy them in bulk or from a candy store to get a good deal. That way, you avoid being stuck with too much stuff people won’t eat.
