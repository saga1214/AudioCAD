That's an excellent strategy. Older books are generally rarer, so you're right to look there. The condition is the most critical factor, though. You'll need to focus on comics that have been graded by CGC, as a book in poor shape can be worth very little.

Here are ten significant older books that are still affordable. *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven is Superman's first cover appearance. *Action Comics* number one is his first full appearance. *Superman* number one is his first cover on that title. *All-Star Comics* number eight is the first appearance of the Justice League of America. *The Flash* number one is Barry Allen's first appearance. *Green Lantern* number one is the first appearance of Hal Jordan. *Aquaman* number one is his first appearance. *Black Adam* number one is the first appearance of that character. And *The Phantom* number one is a great buy, as The Phantom is a globally recognized character with a massive fan base. For any of these, you should look for copies that have been graded to at least a Good or Fine condition.
