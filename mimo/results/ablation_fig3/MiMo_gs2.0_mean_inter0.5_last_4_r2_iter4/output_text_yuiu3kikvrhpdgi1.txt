Of course! For Florida Zone 10B, especially during your reverse seasons, I'd say fast-growing herbs like basil, mint, and parsley are fantastic. Leafy greens like lettuce and spinach are also great, but you'll have to wait for the cooler months. Radishes are also a good, quick option.

If you’ve got more space, tomatoes and peppers are definitely the way to go. They’re pretty forgiving and you’ll get a lot of good stuff from them. They're also super rewarding when you eat them fresh from the garden.
