For a Thanksgiving pie, a simple and classic choice is a pumpkin pie. You can usually find a good crust in the store, and the filling is just canned pumpkin, eggs, sugar, and spices.

Another easy dessert option is a berry crisp. You just bake some fresh or frozen berries with a little sugar and then top it with a simple crumble made from oats, flour, and butter. Both are one-bowl pies and don't require an oven, which fits your limited kitchen perfectly.
