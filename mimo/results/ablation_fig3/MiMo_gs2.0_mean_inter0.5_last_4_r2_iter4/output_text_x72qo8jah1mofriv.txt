Okay, kosher dietary laws mean avoiding pork, shellfish, and meat with dairy. So you'll need to keep things separate. If you're serving fruit or veggies, those are naturally fine. For the sweet, instead of cookies, you could offer dark chocolate, which is dairy-free, or maybe some nuts or fruit-based treats.
