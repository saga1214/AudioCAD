Absolutely! Here are **budget-friendly, health-conscious dessert ideas** that are both kid-friendly and diabetes-aware, while keeping costs low:

### 🍓 Budget-Friendly & Healthy Desserts:
1. **Fresh Fruit Bowls & Skewers**  
   - **Cost:** Very low (buy seasonal fruit in bulk).  
   - **Options:** Grilled pineapple, watermelon wedges, berries, grapes, or banana "sushi" (banana + peanut butter + honey).  
   - **Kid Appeal:** Fun to eat and colorful!

2. **Homemade Frozen Yogurt Pops**  
   - **Cost:** Minimal (use Greek yogurt + honey/maple syrup + fruit puree).  
   - **Make It:** Pour yogurt-fruit mix into popsicle molds, freeze. Add toppings like chia seeds or crushed nuts.  
   - **Benefit:** Protein-rich and customizable.

3. **Mini Fruit & Nut Cups**  
   - **Cost:** Low (use whole-grain cups or small containers).  
   - **Fill:** Sliced strawberries + almonds, apple slices + peanut butter, or banana + coconut flakes.  
   - **Health Angle:** Natural sweetness + healthy fats.

4. **Oatmeal Cookies (Low Sugar)**  
   - **Cost:** Affordable (use oats, banana, honey, and minimal sugar).  
   - **Recipe:** Blend 1 banana + ½ cup oats + ½ cup honey + ½ cup peanut butter. Drop spoonfuls on parchment, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12-15 mins.  
   - **Kid-Friendly:** Chewy texture, no refined sugar.

5. **DIY Ice Cream Sundae Bar**  
   - **Base:** Serve sugar-free vanilla protein ice cream (store-bought or homemade).  
   - **Toppings:** Fresh fruit, crushed nuts, coconut flakes, and a sprinkle of cinnamon.  
   - **Budget Hack:** Bulk-bought toppings + free refills.

### 💡 Cost-Saving Tips:
- **Buy in Bulk:** Purchase fruit, nuts, and oats from wholesale stores.  
- **DIY Over Pre-Made:** Homemade desserts cost less and avoid preservatives.  
- **Repurpose Leftovers:** Extra cookies or fruit can become smoothie ingredients later!  

### 🎯 Why This Works:
- **Awareness:** Highlights natural sweetness (fruit) vs. processed sugars.  
- **Budget-Friendly:** Focuses on whole foods under $5 per serving.  
- **Fun for Kids:** Interactive options like skewers and sundae bars.
