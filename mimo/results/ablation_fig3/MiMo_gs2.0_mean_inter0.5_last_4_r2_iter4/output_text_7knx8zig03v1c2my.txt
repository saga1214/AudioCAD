### Treat Recommendations:
1. **Best Flavors:**  
   - **Puppy-safe:** Small pieces of chicken, turkey, or cheese (avoid onions/garlic).  
   - **Human-friendly:** Cheese cubes, cooked lean meat (like ham or chicken), or peanut butter (xylitol-free).  
   - **Store-bought:** Soft training treats (like Zappies or Milk-Bone Training Puffs).  
   *Tip:* Cut treats into tiny pieces so they’re easy to swallow and don’t distract from learning.  

2. **Italian Roots Idea:**  
   Try small pieces of **mozzarella**, **prosciutto** (cooked), or **olive** (in tiny bits).  

---

### Clicker Training Explained:  
**What it is:** A clicker is a simple tool to mark the exact moment Galileo does something right, then reward him immediately.  

**How it works (step-by-step):**  
1. **Pair Click + Reward:**  
   - Click the button → say "Good!" → give a treat.  
   *Example:* Galileo sits → you click → say "Good!" → treat.  
   This teaches him: "Click = You did something awesome!"  

2. **Teach "Sit":**  
   - Hold a treat near his nose, move it up and back over his head.  
   - As he lowers his head, his bottom will touch the floor.  
   - The *instant* his bottom touches, **click** → treat.  
   Repeat 10-15 times daily.  

3. **Add the Cue:**  
   Once he reliably sits on cue, say "Sit" → click → treat.  

**Why it works:** The click acts as a "super marker" (it’s consistent and precise), making training faster.  

---

### Key Tips for Training:  
- **Consistency:** Use the same clicker and rewards every time.  
- **Short Sessions:** 5-10 minutes, 2-3 times daily.  
- **End on a Win:** Finish when he’s successful to keep him excited!  

If you’re not using a clicker, you can still use a **verbal marker** (like "Yes!" or "Good!") followed by a treat.
