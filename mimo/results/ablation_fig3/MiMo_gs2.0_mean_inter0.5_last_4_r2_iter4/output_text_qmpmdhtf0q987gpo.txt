Okay, you've done the hard work of taking the hardware and sanding! That's great. Before you apply that primer, I'd highly recommend a quick wipe-down with a tack cloth. This will pick up any fine dust from the sanding and ensure a super smooth finish for your primer.

Then, just apply the primer evenly. Let it dry completely according to the instructions, which is usually around two to four hours. After that, you're all set to paint your cabinets!
