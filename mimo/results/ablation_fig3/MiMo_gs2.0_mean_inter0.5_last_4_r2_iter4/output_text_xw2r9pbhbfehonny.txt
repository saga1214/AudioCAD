Sure! Before filming your first YouTube-style video, here’s a quick checklist:

Before the show, first: get permission. If it’s at a place like an assisted living facility, make sure they know you’re recording and posting it.

Next, set up your phone. If you’re filming yourself, you can prop your phone up on a tripod or ask someone to help. Use the front-facing camera for good audio and framing.

Then, just focus on the performance. Don’t stress too much about being 'on camera'—kids will just see the show and love the puppets.

After the show, you edit. You can just trim the best parts, like the opening and closing skits. Add some simple text or sound effects if you want. Then upload to a kid-friendly platform like YouTube Kids or TikTok.

Start simple, be consistent, and have fun with it. Your talent is already there—now you’re just sharing it more. You’ll do great.
