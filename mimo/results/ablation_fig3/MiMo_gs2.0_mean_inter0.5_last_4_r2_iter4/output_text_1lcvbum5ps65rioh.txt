### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia  
In BC, **U-turns are generally allowed** but subject to strict rules:  
1. **Intersections with signals (traffic lights)**: Only permitted where explicitly posted.  
2. **Visibility**: Must see **at least 150 meters** in both directions (≈1.5 football fields).  
3. **Prohibited areas**: Hills, curves, narrow lanes, or where U-turns "interfere with traffic."  
4. **Penalties**:  
   - **$60 fine** + 2 demerit points (if caught).  
   - Police or automated cameras enforce this.  

**Pro Tip**: Use designated U-turn lanes or open roads with clear visibility. When in doubt, wait for a safe gap.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
For **4 servings**, you’ll need:  
| **Ingredient**       | **Quantity**       | **Notes**                          |  
|-----------------------|--------------------|-----------------------------------|  
| Spaghetti             | 400g (14 oz)       | Use **dried** spaghetti (not fresh) |  
| Guanciale (Pancetta) | 150g (5 oz)        | Italian cured pork (substitute: bacon + pancetta) |  
| Eggs                  | 3 large            | **Room-temperature**              |  
| Pecorino Romano       | 100g (1 cup grated)| Sharp sheep’s cheese             |  
| Parmigiano-Reggiano   | 50g (¼ cup grated) | Optional, adds depth              |  
| Black Pepper          | Freshly ground     | Generous!                         |  
| Salt                  | To taste           | For pasta water                   |  

**Equipment**: Large pot, skillet, mixing bowl, tongs.  

---

### Quick Tips for Carbonara  
1. **Tempering eggs**: Whisk eggs + cheese + pepper. Slowly add hot pasta water to thicken.  
2. **Heat control**: Cook guanciale over *low-medium heat* to avoid burning.  
3. **Timing**: Cook pasta *al dente* (it softens in sauce). Mix immediately after draining.  
4. **Avoid cream**: Authentic carbonara uses eggs/cheese only.
