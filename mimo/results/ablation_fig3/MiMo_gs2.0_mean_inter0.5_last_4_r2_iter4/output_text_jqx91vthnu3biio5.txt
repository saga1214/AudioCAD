Absolutely! Here are four dinner ideas that balance flavor, ease, and nutrition—perfect after a long day of office tweaks and planning:

### 🌿 1. **Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken & Quinoa**  
   - *Why you’ll love it:* Directly addresses your earlier snack craving!  
   - *How:* Pan-sear chicken breasts, stuff with herbed quinoa, sun-dried tomatoes, olives, and feta. Finish with roasted veggies (zucchini, bell peppers).  
   - *Bonus:* Leftovers freeze beautifully for busy days.

### 🌶️ 2. **Thai-Inspired Red Curry with Tofu & Veggies**  
   - *Why you’ll love it:* Quick, flavorful, and uses pantry staples.  
   - *How:* Sauté garlic, ginger, and lemongrass. Add red curry paste, coconut milk, and tofu. Toss in snap peas, carrots, and spinach. Serve over rice.  
   - *Pro tip:* Swap tofu for shrimp or chicken.

### 🥗 3. **Lemon-Herb Salmon with Asparagus**  
   - *Why you’ll love it:* High-protein, healthy fats, and ready in 20 mins.  
   - *How:* Season salmon with lemon zest, dill, and black pepper. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 12–15 mins. Roast asparagus alongside.  
   - *Pair it:* With a crisp side salad or crusty bread.

### 🥘 4. **One-Pot Creamy Tomato Basil Soup**  
   - *Why you’ll love it:* Comforting, minimal cleanup, and perfect for a cozy reset.  
   - *How:* Sauté onions, garlic, and carrots. Add canned tomatoes, broth, and coconut milk. Simmer 20 mins. Blend until smooth.  
   - *Upgrade:* Top with grilled cheese or a dollop of pesto.
