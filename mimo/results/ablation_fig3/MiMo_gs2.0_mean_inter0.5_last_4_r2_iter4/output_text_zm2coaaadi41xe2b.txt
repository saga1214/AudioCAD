Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! For ramen, you’ve got to check out Tonkoto in Deep Ellum or Ippudo in Lower Greenville—both are top-tier. If you like pho, Pho Saigon in Little Elm is amazing, and for Soba noodles, Sobahouse in Lakewood is a must. 

For protein bars, it depends on your taste. If you want something high-protein with minimal sugar, Quest or Optimum Nutrition are solid. If you prefer a chewier texture and natural ingredients, Clif Bars or RXBAR are good. And if you like chocolatey ones, the RXBAR Chocolate Puffs are pretty good.
