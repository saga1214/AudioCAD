That's a wonderful idea. The gingerbread house kits would be a very thoughtful addition for your parents, who love gingerbread anyway. And giving them to your friends is a great way to round everything out.

As for how to include them:

For your friends, you could pair the Gingerbread cookie with the Christmas-themed tin. The tin would be the perfect place to put the house kit, and Sal would love the festive feel.

For your folks, you could pair the Gingerbread with the chocolate chip cookies. Pack the house kit in a Pringles can along with those two cookies, cushioned well. They'll get to enjoy both the cookies and the fun of building the house.

It's a really lovely touch.
