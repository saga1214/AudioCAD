That's a thoughtful question! Here's a breakdown to help you accommodate everyone:

### For the Vegan Friend
*   **Avoid standard frozen hamburgers:** Traditional beef or veggie burgers (even if labeled "vegan" on the packaging) often contain animal-derived ingredients like lard, whey, gelatin, or milk derivatives. **Never assume a burger is vegan without checking the label carefully.**
*   **Solution:** **Buy a *separate* package of explicitly labeled vegan frozen burgers.** Look for brands like Beyond Meat, Impossible, Gardein, or Seitan burgers. **Crucially, keep these physically separate** from the meat burgers to prevent cross-contamination.

### For Everyone Else
*   **Frozen burgers are generally fine:** For the policeman, volunteer, and dev, frozen burgers are usually acceptable. They can be heated later at home or brought cold to the park if desired. Just ensure they're cooked thoroughly.

### Key Considerations for the Group

1.  **Separation is Essential:** **This is the most important step.** Use separate containers or even separate coolers/ice packs for:
    *   The **vegan burgers** (in their own dedicated container).
    *   The **meat burgers** (in their own dedicated container).
    *   **Prevent cross-contamination** for the vegan friend and anyone with allergies.
2.  **Cooler with Ice Packs:** **Yes, bring a cooler with ice packs!** Especially if it will be warm. Keep the burgers frozen or very cold until you're ready to cook/eat them. This ensures food safety for everyone.
3.  **Heating Options:**
    *   **At Home:** Cook the burgers before bringing them (even frozen, you can grill/bake them later). This takes the guesswork out.
    *   **At the Park:** You can bring a portable grill, hot dog stand, or even a small camping stove to heat them up. A cooler with frozen burgers is fine, but heating them is more enjoyable and practical.
4.  **Alternative Options:**
    *   **Offer Hot Dogs/Sausages:** These are often simpler to find in a wide variety of plant-based options (Beyond Meat, Impossible, veggie sausages) and are easier to separate physically.
    *   **Veggie Burgers:** Offer a *separate* package of your vegan burgers alongside the meat burgers.
    *   **Non-Burger Options:** Consider including some simple vegan-friendly sides like veggie chips, fruit, or hummus cups for the vegan friend.

### Recommendation

*   **Buy two separate packages:** One package of **explicitly labeled vegan frozen burgers** (kept *away* from meat) and one package of **meat frozen burgers**.
*   **Pack them separately:** Use two different containers or even two separate sections of a cooler.
*   **Bring ice packs:** Keep everything cold until you're ready to cook/eat.
*   **Consider pre-cooking:** Cooking them at home saves time and hassle at the park.

By separating the vegan and meat options clearly and keeping everything cold, you can enjoy delicious burgers with your diverse group! Your thoughtfulness is what makes the gathering great.
