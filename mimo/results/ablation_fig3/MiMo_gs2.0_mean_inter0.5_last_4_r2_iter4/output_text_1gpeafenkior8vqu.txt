Here's a balanced **8-day itinerary** for Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, and Nara, optimized for sightseeing while accommodating your presentation prep. I've built in flexibility for your family's pace and included tips for efficient travel:

### **Tokyo (5 Days)**  
**Day 1: Imperial & Modern Tokyo**  
- **Morning:** Imperial Palace East Gardens (open 9-11am).  
- **Afternoon:** Asakusa (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Street).  
- **Evening:** Sumida River cruise → Ueno Park.  
- *Work Tip:* Research Edo-period art (calligraphy, ukiyo-e) for slides.  

**Day 2: Art & Culture**  
- **Morning:** Ghibli Museum (book tickets!) → Shimokitazawa (vintage shops).  
- **Afternoon:** Mori Art Museum (modern art) → Harajuku (Takeshita Street).  
- *Design Focus:* Use Ghibli’s vibrant slides as font/color inspiration.  

**Day 3: Nature & Innovation**  
- **Morning:** Meiji Shrine (Yoyogi Park) → Shibuya Crossing.  
- **Afternoon:** Shinjuku Gyoen Garden → TeamLab Planets (digital art).  
- *Literacy Tip:* Note how nature motifs appear in both classical and digital art.  

**Day 4: Day Trip to Nikko**  
- **Full Day:** Toshogu Shrine (UNESCO), Kegon Falls.  
- *Art Connection:* Edo-era woodcarvings/sculpture.  

**Day 5: Modern Tokyo**  
- **Morning:** Akihabara (anime/tech) → Tsukiji Outer Market.  
- **Afternoon:** Ghibli Park (if open) or Aoyama Shopping Street.  
- *Evening:* Shibuya Sky sunset.  

---

### **Osaka & Surroundings (3 Days)**  
**Day 6: Osaka City**  
- **Morning:** Osaka Castle → Kema Wetlands (birdwatching).  
- **Afternoon:** Dotonbori (street food, neon signs).  
- **Evening:** Shinsekai (retro district).  
- *Design Idea:* Incorporate Dotonbori’s bold signage into slide layout.  

**Day 7: Kyoto Highlights**  
- **Morning:** Travel to Kyoto (15-min train from Shin-Osaka).  
- **Afternoon:** Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) → Ryoan-ji (Zen rock garden).  
- **Evening:** Pontocho Alley (dinner).  

**Day 8: Nara & Return**  
- **Morning:** Travel to Nara (30-min train from Kyoto).  
- **Afternoon:** Todai-ji Temple (giant Buddha) → Nara Park (deer).  
- **Evening:** Return to Tokyo/Osaka airport.
