### Ocean-Themed Mains (Diabetic-Friendly & Shellfish-Free)
1. **Grilled Salmon with Mango Salsa**  
   *Rich in omega-3s, with fresh mango for sweetness. Serve with quinoa or brown rice.*

2. **Coconut Curry with Shrimp-Free Tofu & Mango**  
   *Spicy, creamy, and infused with coconut milk. Use firm tofu instead of shellfish.*

3. **Tropical Fish Tacos**  
   *Baked white fish (like cod or halibut) in corn tortillas with mango-pineapple salsa, avocado, and a lime-cilantro slaw.*

4. **Seaweed Salad with Shrimp-Free Seafood Stir-Fry**  
   *Use tofu or king oyster mushrooms for a "seafood" texture. Add mango for sweetness.*

5. **Mango-Coconut Stuffed Shrimp (Shellfish Alternative)**  
   *Replace shrimp with large mushrooms or cauliflower "shrimp" stuffed with coconut, mango, and spices.*

### Ocean-Themed Desserts (Sweet & Diabetic-Friendly)
1. **Mango-Coconut Rice Pudding**  
   *Simmer low-sugar coconut milk, rice, and diced mango until creamy. Top with toasted coconut flakes.*

2. **Dark Chocolate Sea Salt Brownies**  
   *Rich, fudgy brownies with a sprinkle of flaky sea salt. Use dark chocolate (70%+) for lower sugar.*

3. **Lime Sorbet with Fresh Mint**  
   *Bright, tangy, and refreshing. Add mango puree for extra sweetness.*

4. **Coconut Rice Pudding Parfait**  
   *Layer coconut rice pudding with fresh mango and a dollop of Greek yogurt (low-sugar).*

5. **Mango Sticky Rice with Lime Zest**  
   *Classic Thai dessert. Use coconut milk instead of sugar, and add lime zest for a citrus twist.*

### Key Tips:
- **Diabetic Note:** Control portions, use natural sweetness (mango, ripe fruit), and avoid high-glycemic carbs. Pair with protein/fiber (tofu, quinoa, veggies) to balance meals.
- **Spice Control:** Adjust chili levels to suit both preferences—start mild and add heat gradually.
- **Theme Touch:** Decorate with blue/green accents, ocean-patterned napkins, and a "dive mask" centerpiece!
