That's wonderful news about customer service! Cooking is a great way to relax after all that stress. Here are five easy, comforting dinner ideas you can whip up with grocery store finds:

1.  **Creamy Parmesan Chicken Alfredo:** Grab rotisserie chicken, fettuccine, heavy cream, Parmesan cheese (shredded), garlic powder, salt, pepper, and maybe some spinach. Sauté garlic in butter/oil, add chicken and spinach, then pour in the cream and cheese mixture until thickened. Toss with pasta. Simple and decadent.
2.  **Sheet Pan Salmon & Roasted Veggies:** Get salmon fillets, asparagus, broccoli florets, baby potatoes, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Toss veggies with oil and seasonings, place on a baking sheet, top salmon with a little oil and lemon, then bake together. Minimal cleanup!
3.  **Big Veggie Stir-Fry with Peanut Sauce:** Pick fresh or frozen stir-fry veggies (bell peppers, snap peas, carrots, broccoli), tofu or chicken, and a can of coconut milk. For the sauce, mix peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, a touch of honey, garlic, ginger, and a splash of water to thin. Stir-fry protein and veggies, then toss with the sauce. Serve over rice or noodles.
4.  **Loaded Baked Potato Bar:** Bake a couple of large russet potatoes. Top with chili (check if they have canned), shredded cheese, chopped veggies (sautéed onions, peppers, broccoli), sour cream, green onions, and maybe some bacon bits. Fun and customizable!
5.  **One-Pot Creamy Tomato Basil Soup with Grilled Cheese:** Make a simple soup by simmering canned tomatoes, onion, garlic, a splash of cream or broth, and fresh basil leaves (or dried). Blend until smooth. Serve in a bowl with a warm grilled cheese sandwich. Ultimate comfort!

Hope one of those sounds perfect for your relaxing evening! Enjoy your (eventually relaxing) vacation! 😊
