That's a fantastic skill to have! In fact, being an illustrator is a major asset for a children's book. However, the process is still the same. You **still** need to find a literary agent first.

Why? Because publishers almost exclusively work with agents. An agent acts as your advocate, representing you and negotiating on your behalf. They know the market and can pitch your book effectively.

Once an agent accepts your manuscript, they will then try to sell it to a publisher. At that point, you would submit your illustrations to the publisher, and they would commission an illustrator to create the final, full-color art. You could offer to submit your art as samples, but the publisher will likely want to see the final artwork they'll be paying for.
