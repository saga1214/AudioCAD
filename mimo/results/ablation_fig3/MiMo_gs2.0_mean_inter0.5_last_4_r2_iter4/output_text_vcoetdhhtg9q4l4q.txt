For quick dinners with minimal ingredients, try these simple, pantry-friendly ideas:

### 🍳 1. **One-Pan Stir-Fry**  
   - **Protein:** Tofu or chicken  
   - **Veggies:** Frozen stir-fry mix (peppers, broccoli, carrots)  
   - **Sauce:** Soy sauce + garlic powder  
   - **Serve:** Over leftover rice or noodles.  
   *Total time: 15 mins.*

### 🥣 2. **Mason Jar Salads**  
   - **Base:** Quinoa or lentils (pre-cooked)  
   - **Layer:** Cucumber, cherry tomatoes, feta  
   - **Dressing:** Lemon juice + olive oil  
   - *No prep needed—just shake before eating!*

### 🍲 3. **Pasta with Jarred Sauce**  
   - **Base:** Whole-grain pasta  
   - **Toppings:** Sautéed spinach, cherry tomatoes, pine nuts  
   - **Finish:** Drizzle of pesto.  
   *Bonus: Use rice noodles if you have them!*

### 🥘 4. **Sheet-Pan Salmon & Veggies**  
   - **Protein:** Salmon fillet  
   - **Veggies:** Asparagus, zucchini, cherry tomatoes  
   - **Seasoning:** Olive oil, salt, lemon zest  
   *Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 15 mins.*

### 💡 **Key Tips:**  
- **Prep ahead:** Wash/chop veggies on weekends.  
- **Freeze extras:** Cook extra grains/proteins for quick meals.  
- **Rice noodle hack:** Rinse, toss with soy sauce + sesame oil, and pan-fry for 2 mins. Use in noodle bowls or salads!  

Simplify your kitchen first, and these combos will feel effortless. Prioritize whole foods over processed ingredients to keep clutter low! 🌱
