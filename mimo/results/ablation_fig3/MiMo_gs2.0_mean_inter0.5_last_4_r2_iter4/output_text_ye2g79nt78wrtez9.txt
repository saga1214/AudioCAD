Well, while Ancient Egypt scores slightly higher in our little exercise, I've been thinking more about the real-world challenges. Getting major Egyptian artifacts is incredibly difficult, so an exhibit might feel a bit superficial if it's just mummies. Mesopotamia's biggest weakness is its lower accessibility score, but that's also its biggest opportunity. You could make the storytelling potential a 10 by really focusing on the narrative of innovation—the first cities, the invention of writing, the first laws.

Given that, I think the choice really depends on the museum's goal. If the goal is a crowd-pleasing, visually stunning show, then yes, Ancient Egypt is the safer bet. But if the goal is a unique, deeply educational experience that tells the foundational story of human civilization, then Mesopotamia is the far more compelling choice. So, while Egypt has the edge, I would actually lean towards Mesopotamia.
