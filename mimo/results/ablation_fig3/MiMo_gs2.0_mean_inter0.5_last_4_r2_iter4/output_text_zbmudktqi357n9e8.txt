Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn inspiration! The timing really depends on what you want to capture. If you want peak fall colors, you should go in late October or November. That’s when the leaves are most vibrant, especially in the highlands like Pátzcuaro or San Miguel de Allende. The mountains turn these incredible shades of red, orange, and yellow. 

If you're looking for a more coastal vibe, the Riviera Maya or the Pacific coast in Oaxaca have beautiful autumn-like scenery too—just maybe not the super intense red and orange foliage. November is also great for seeing the first real chill in the air. 

And hey, if you want a mix of both, maybe September could work for the early changes, but you might still catch some of the summer heat. But honestly, November is probably your sweet spot for colors and cooler weather. Hope you get some amazing inspiration!
