For the 10-foot stairwell wall without an extension pole or budget for rentals, here are practical solutions:

### DIY Solutions (Low-Cost & Safe)
1. **Sturdy Step Stool + Paint Roller Pole**  
   - Use a **telescoping step stool** (like a "step-down" ladder) with a **long paint roller handle** (10-12 ft).  
   - *Why it works:* The stool provides a stable platform, and the pole extends your reach. Test on a lower section first.

2. **Repurpose a Ladder**  
   - If you have a **non-telescoping step ladder** (not a tall A-frame), extend it fully and place it securely on stairs.  
   - *Safety tip:* Use painter’s tape to create smaller sections (e.g., 2 ft at a time) to avoid overreaching.

3. **Painter’s Scaffolding (Budget Hack)**  
   - Rent or buy a **small folding scaffold** cheaply (under $50) for a few hours.  
   - *Alternative:* Use **plywood planks** as temporary steps if your stairs are sturdy.

### Key Safety Tips
- **Never overreach:** Keep your feet on the ground or on a stable platform.  
- **Secure the ladder/scaffold:** Place a non-slip mat under its base and ensure it’s braced against walls if possible.  
- **Use a helper:** Have someone hold the pole steady if it’s wobbly.  

### If DIY Feels Unsafe
- **Section the wall:** Paint in 3-4 ft sections using painter’s tape. Work top-to-bottom or bottom-to-top.  
- **Ask a neighbor:** Borrow an extension pole or step ladder—most people don’t mind short-term help.
