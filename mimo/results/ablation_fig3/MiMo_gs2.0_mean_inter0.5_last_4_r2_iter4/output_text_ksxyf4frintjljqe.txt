I'm truly sorry to hear about your family's loss. Losing someone who brought so much joy through their baking, especially desserts they loved to make like brownies and peach cobbler, makes Thanksgiving incredibly difficult. It's completely understandable that seeing those triggers such strong reactions, particularly for your brother. Maybe this year you could find a way to celebrate her memory that feels right for the whole family, like sharing favorite stories or creating a new ritual together together. And it’s OK if you skip those trigger desserts or simplify things—whatever helps the most right now. Just be gentle with yourselves.

As for Thanksgiving desserts, chocolate is always a crowd-pleaser! A molten lava cake would be decadent, or maybe a chocolate pudding pie. Chocolate chip cookies are always a safe and comforting choice. You could also try a chocolate crust for the Thanksgiving pumpkin pie. Whatever you choose, make it a special time for your family to support each other.
