Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes tailored to your needs—formal, inclusive, and quick to prepare:

### 1. **Vodka Soda with a Twist**  
*(Great for mixed diets; soda-free)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 4 oz sparkling water (not soda!)  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- 1 sprig fresh mint or lime wedge  
**Instructions**:  
Shake vodka and lime juice with ice. Strain into a glass over ice. Top with sparkling water. Garnish with mint or lime.  

**Why it works**: Refreshing, non-alcoholic guests can skip the vodka and enjoy sparkling water with lime. No heavy sugar—light and elegant.

---

### 2. **Argentine-Inspired Fernet Spritz**  
*(Balances Fernet’s bitterness; soda-free)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 oz Fernet-Cordeno  
- 3 oz soda water (not tonic)  
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz simple syrup (optional, for sweetness)  
- Ice  
**Instructions**:  
Shake lemon juice, simple syrup (if using), and Fernet with ice. Strain into a chilled glass. Top with soda water. Garnish with lemon twist.  

**Why it works**: The soda water lightens Fernet’s bitterness—ideal for mixed-age groups. Skip the garnish for vegans.

---

### Quick Tips for Success:  
- **Sparkling Water**: Use chilled, high-quality sparkling water (e.g., San Pellegrino) instead of soda.  
- **Label Drinks**: Clearly mark alcoholic vs. non-alcoholic options.  
- **Prep Ahead**: Mix simple syrup, pre-chill glasses, and pre-mix vodka-lime-mint syrup.  
- **Allergy Safety**: Avoid cross-contamination—use separate bottles for sparkling water and soda.
