That's frustrating when your tool won't cooperate! Let's troubleshoot the glue gun first, then circle back to making those globes super spooky:

**Glue Gun Troubleshooting (Quick Check):**

1.  **Is it truly hot?** Hold the nozzle near your face (carefully!) for 10-15 seconds. If you feel a *strong* heat wave, it's likely heating up but not melting the glue *yet*. If no heat wave, it might be broken.
2.  **Check the tip:** Is it completely blocked? Sometimes dried glue or debris can clog the small opening. Try gently scraping the very tip with a toothpick or pin, *before* applying heat.
3.  **Check the power source:** Is it plugged in securely? (If battery-operated). Is the outlet working? (Try another outlet or test with a lamp).
4.  **Check the glue cartridge:**
    *   **Is it new?** Old glue often dries out and won't melt.
    *   **Is it the right kind?** Most craft glue guns use a specific hot glue stick (usually white or clear). Don't use regular craft glue.
    *   **Is it loaded correctly?** Ensure it's pushed in all the way until you hear/feel it click.
5.  **Give it time:** Sometimes it just needs a minute longer to fully heat up after being plugged in. Try plugging it in again.

**While it heats up, more spooky ideas for your globes:**

1.  **Mini Fake Blood Drips:** Use tiny dots of clear or dark red craft glue ("fake blood") dripping down the *outside* of the glass, especially near the base or mask edges. Let it dry slightly before adding glitter for a "drippy" effect.
2.  **Faux Bone Accents:** Add small, white or grey plastic "bones" (like from a craft store) nestled around the base or even glued to the pumpkin mask itself. Cluster them.
3.  **Eerie Moss & Stones:** Go beyond just moss. Add small, dark grey or black plastic "river rocks" or "pebbles" around the base, interspersed with the moss. This adds a "buried treasure" or "cursed garden" feel.
4.  **Miniature Scarecrow Elements:** Glue tiny, dark fabric burlap scraps (for a ruffly texture) near the base. Add a single, tiny fake spiderweb (black or gold) dangling from the lace/netting.
5.  **Gothic Touches:** Instead of flowers, try adding small, dark, faux thistles or tiny, gnarled branches (black or dark green) around the base for a more wild, untamed look.
6.  **Edible Glitter (If safe!):** If you're decorating for a Halloween party, you could use a *small* amount of edible black or dark glitter along the flower edges *if you know guests won't touch the globes*. Otherwise, stick with craft glitter like you planned!
7.  **Subtle Face Details:** If you add masks, consider adding very small, dark grey dots or lines *on* the mask fabric petals to suggest a ghostly face (like hollow eyes or a faint smile) – subtle and creepy!

**Once it works!**

*   Apply the glue carefully to attach the lace/netting, cobwebs/spiders, moss/rocks, bones, and any heavier decorations like thistles or branches.
*   Use the dark glitter primarily along the petal edges as planned – that's a fantastic detail!
*   Let the glue cool completely before handling or moving the globes.
