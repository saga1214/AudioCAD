That's a tough choice because it really depends on the specific location and what's already there. In some places, like the Pacific Northwest or parts of Europe, hydropower is extremely efficient because of the existing dams. In sunny regions, solar is a top contender. And in consistently windy areas, like coastal regions, wind farms can be very productive. So, there isn't one single answer that fits everywhere. It all comes down to what's best for a specific geographical location.
