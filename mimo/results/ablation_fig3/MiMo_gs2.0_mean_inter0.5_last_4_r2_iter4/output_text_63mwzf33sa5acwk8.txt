Okay, so you're thinking about combining the Nevada desert adventure with a Vegas pit stop. Smart move! If you want a classic Vegas experience after your van trip, here's how to do it right. 

First, for the hotel: pick one with a vibe that matches your girlfriend's style. If you're into luxury, the Bellagio or Venetian offer great pools and views. For something more unique and fun, consider the Palazzo or Cosmopolitan, which have rooftop bars and cool public spaces. If you're on a budget, the Flamingo or L.A.+Circus has a fun, retro feel. 

For the hot shower, most hotels have private bathrooms in the rooms, so no need to go to a public shower. But if you're up for a public experience, some hotels have communal pools or spas. The Wynn and Encore have luxurious spas, while the Bellagio has a famous outdoor pool with fountains. 

To have a proper Vegas experience, you should try the nightlife. Go to a club, see a show, or just walk the Strip. The Venetian has a great show with Cirque du Soleil, and the Flamingo has a fun casino vibe. 

Remember to eat at a classic Vegas spot like the Bacchanal Buffet at Caesars or the Eggslut at The Cosmopolitan. 

And if you're into unique experiences, check out the Neon Museum, the Mob Museum, or even a helicopter tour of the Strip. 

So, the plan would be: drive down to Joshua Tree, explore, then head to Nevada for some free or low-cost camping, and finally, a night in Vegas for a proper hotel stay and fun. How does that sound?
