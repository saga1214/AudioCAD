The best place to start your exploration is definitely the Lockport Locks Museum. It's located right inside the locks themselves, which is a very immersive experience. You can learn all about the history, the construction, and even how they worked.

There's also a great book called "The Great Lockport Locks" by Robert J. McFadden that goes into incredible detail about the engineering and wartime espionage you were just curious about.

And of course, the town of Lockport itself is beautiful. It has a historic downtown with many buildings from the canal era, and you can find restaurants and shops right along the water.
