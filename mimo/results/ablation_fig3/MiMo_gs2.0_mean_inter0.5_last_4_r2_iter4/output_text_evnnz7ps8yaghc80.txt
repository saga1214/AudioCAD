A structured plan is a fantastic idea. Here is a sample weekly schedule that incorporates all the tools we've discussed.

On Monday, you could start with tennis balls for about 15 minutes to practice one-handed two-ball juggling.

On Tuesday, dedicate 20 minutes to scarves to slowly practice patterns.

For Wednesday, you could try beanbags again for 15 minutes, just to warm up.

Thursday could be another tennis ball day for about 20 minutes, focusing on catching them.

On Friday, you can try combining beanbags and scarves, spending 15 minutes on beanbags and 15 on scarves.

Saturday is the perfect day for a longer session, maybe 30 minutes, to focus on transitioning to three beanbags again.

And on Sunday, you could do a fun, relaxed session with beanbags for about 20 minutes, or maybe not practice at all and just watch a juggling video for inspiration.

Remember, this is just a template. The most important thing is to listen to your son and yourself, and have fun with it.
