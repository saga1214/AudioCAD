Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your TV, hanging planters, and floating shelves safely and effectively:

### 📺 1. **Mounting the TV**
- **Tools Needed:** Power drill, stud finder, measuring tape, pencil, TV mount, wall anchors (if not mounting to studs).
- **Steps:**
  1. **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (vertical wooden beams behind drywall). Mounting to studs ensures stability.
  2. **Measure & Mark:** Hold the TV mount against the wall, marking where the top/bottom corners will sit. Use a level to ensure alignment.
  3. **Drill Pilot Holes:** If mounting to studs, drill holes slightly smaller than the screw heads. If not, use wall anchors in the marked spots.
  4. **Secure Mount:** Insert screws into holes/studs or anchors. Attach the TV mount, then secure the TV with its screws.
  5. **Test Stability:** Gently shake the mount to ensure it’s secure before hanging the TV.

### 🌿 2. **Hanging Planters**
- **Tools Needed:** Drill, stud finder, lightweight planter (or wall-mounted planter holder), hook, measuring tape.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Choose Placement:** Hang near natural light (windows) or as focal points. Keep away from high-traffic areas.
  2. **Mark Spot:** Hold the planter or holder against the wall, using a level. Mark screw holes.
  3. **Secure Anchor:** If not on a stud, use heavy-duty drywall anchors or toggle bolts.
  4. **Install Hook/Holder:** Drill holes, insert anchors, and screw in the hook or holder. Ensure it’s level.
  5. **Hang Planter:** Place the planter securely. For lightweight plants, use a hanging planter hook; for heavier items, use a wall-mounted stand.

### 📚 3. **Installing Floating Shelves**
- **Tools Needed:** Drill, stud finder, measuring tape, pencil, shelf bracket, level, wall anchors (if not on studs).
- **Steps:**
  1. **Plan Layout:** Decide shelf height (usually eye-level: 57–60 inches from floor). Space shelves 12–24 inches apart.
  2. **Mark Shelf Edges:** Hold the shelf against the wall, marking top/bottom edges. Use a level to align.
  3. **Locate Studs:** Find studs at the top and bottom of the shelf’s span. If no studs, use heavy-duty anchors.
  4. **Install Brackets:** Drill pilot holes in studs/anchors. Secure brackets with screws, ensuring they’re level.
  5. **Place Shelf:** Slide the shelf onto the brackets. Add a small shim under one corner if needed for perfect alignment.

### 🔧 **Key Tips for Success**
- **Safety First:** Wear safety glasses. Double-check measurements and levels.
- **Weight Capacity:** Ensure shelves/brackets can hold your art/plants (e.g., use 50–75 lbs-rated brackets for heavy items).
- **Tool Rental:** If renting, borrow tools like a stud finder or drill from a hardware store.
- **Post-Installation:** Test all items for stability. Add decorative hooks or baskets to shelves for extra functionality.

### 🎨 **Decorating Pro Tips**
- **Theme Consistency:** Pull colors from furniture/art to unify the space.
- **Art Arrangement:** Group art pieces in odd numbers (3 or 5) for visual balance.
- **Lighting:** Use LED strip lights under shelves or a floor lamp to highlight decor.

By breaking tasks into Sunday sessions (e.g., install TV first, then shelves, then planters), you’ll avoid fatigue. Remember: **measure twice, drill once**! With these steps, your studio will feel both functional and stunning 🌟.
