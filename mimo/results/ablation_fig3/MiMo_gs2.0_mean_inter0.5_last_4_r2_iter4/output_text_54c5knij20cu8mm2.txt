First, about the writing quirks. What you're describing—inserting letters from the following word—is likely Transposed Letters Dysgraphia or general letter sequence errors. It happens even in people with strong spelling knowledge due to challenges in writing motor patterns or cognitive sequencing during the act of writing.

Regarding the brain hemispheres: The left-right brain dichotomy is an oversimplification and largely a myth. While the left brain is more dominant for language and logical tasks, and the right for spatial reasoning and creativity, both hemispheres constantly communicate through the corpus callosum. Creativity and logic aren't strictly divided; they coexist and interact. For example, artists plan compositions logically, and scientists use creativity to solve problems. The brain is more of a network than a split system.

For careers that blend concrete measurement with creativity and structure, here are some ideas:

1. **UX/UI Design**: Combines structured problem-solving with creativity to design functional, user-friendly interfaces.
2. **Technical Illustration or Data Visualization**: Uses creativity to translate complex, measurable data into clear visuals.
3. **Creative Coding or Generative Art**: Combines programming logic with artistic expression to create digital art.
4. **Architecture or Urban Planning**: Balances structural, objective design with creative vision for spaces.
5. **Science Communication or Science Journalism**: Requires factual accuracy while creatively engaging audiences with scientific findings.
