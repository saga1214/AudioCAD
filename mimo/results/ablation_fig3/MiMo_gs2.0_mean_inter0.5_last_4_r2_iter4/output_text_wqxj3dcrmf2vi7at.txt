Of course! Let's go through a full list of homemade and store-bought ideas for your brown and white princess. 

For homemade treats, you can make frozen bites with mashed pumpkin, sweet potato, or even blueberries. Oat cookies filled with peanut butter are another classic. Simple pieces of cooked chicken or carrot sticks are also great healthy snacks. You could even try small peanut butter and banana rolls, or even some frozen peas and corn for a fun, crunchy option. And for a warm treat, you could make tiny peanut butter and honey bites.

For store-bought, some top brands are Blue Buffalo, Wellness, Zuke's, Greenies, Merrick, Stella & Chewy's, and Earthborn Holistics. Just be sure to check for natural ingredients.

And remember, no xylitol is ever safe for dogs, so always check peanut butter and other ingredients. Enjoy spoiling your little corgi queen!
