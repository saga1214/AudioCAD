That's a smart approach. Here's how to stick your jacket temporarily without damaging the lining:

1.  **Best Option: Double-Sided Tape:** This is ideal. Find double-sided tape designed for fabric (like "double-sided fabric tape" or "double-sided clear tape for clothes"). It sticks well and can be removed relatively cleanly without leaving residue or tearing. Apply small pieces along the inside seam where you want the jacket to lie.

2.  **Good Option: Clear Tape (Use Sparingly & Carefully):**
    *   Use **very thin** clear tape.
    *   **Only apply it to the *outer* fabric** of the jacket, *not* the lining. Stick one piece of tape to the jacket's outer surface, then press the jacket against the box. This creates a temporary "backing" that prevents sticking.
    *   **Do NOT wrap tape directly around the lining.** If you absolutely must touch the lining, use the smallest possible piece of clear tape *very gently* on the *outside* of the lining layer, pressed flat against the outer fabric.

3.  **Avoid: Standard Adhesive Tape (like masking or packing tape):**
    *   **Don't use this directly on the lining.** The strong adhesive will likely leave residue and can tear the fabric.
    *   **Don't use it directly on the outer fabric either.** It can leave a sticky residue that's hard to remove and might damage the finish.

**Key Tips:**

*   **Packaging:** Place the jacket inside a sturdy box. Fill the box with plenty of soft, acid-free tissue paper or crumpled paper to surround it and keep it from shifting. This is the *primary* solution, as it prevents contact.
*   **Placement:** Stick the jacket flat against the packing paper inside the box, not against the cardboard sides.
*   **Temporary Only:** Remember, this is just for transport until you receive it at home.
*   **Check Return Policy:** If the tape causes an issue, most online stores have a return window (usually 30 days).

Using double-sided fabric tape (option 1) or carefully using clear tape on the outer fabric (option 2) with ample padding inside the box should keep your jacket secure without damaging the lining. Happy shopping at Nordstrom Rack!
