### Modern Evolution:  
Today’s first line integrates **technology with human oversight**:  
- **Smart Access Systems**: Biometric scanners + keycard/PIN combos for staff-only zones.  
- **AI-Powered Monitoring**: Cameras with facial recognition flag suspicious behavior in real-time.  
- **Behavioral Analytics**: Systems like **PAN (Perimeter Access Network)** use sensors to detect if doors/windows are tampered with *before* entry.  

**In essence:** The "first line" is **access control**, but its strength depends entirely on **human reliability**. Museums now treat staff training as a core security layer—not an afterthought.
