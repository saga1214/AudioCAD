Okay, I can definitely put together a shopping list for you. To make it easier, I'll break it down into categories: drinks, appetizers, the main course and sides, kids' specific items, and essentials for hosting.

For drinks, you'll need sparkling wine or champagne for the adults' signature cocktail and the midnight toast, plus plenty of soda water. For the kids and non-drinking guests, you'll need grape juice and some simple mixers like cranberry juice or lemonade. You'll also need ice and cups.

For appetizers, you'll need ground pork for the sliders, shrimp, crescent rolls, slider buns, lettuce, tomato, aioli, cocktail sauce, and toothpicks.

For the main course, you'll need a ham, potatoes, olive oil, lemon, salt, and pepper. You can probably use what you have for the roasted carrots.

For the kids, I'd suggest grabbing a few noisemakers or party hats, and maybe some juice boxes or crackers as an extra snack.

And for hosting essentials, you'll want to make sure you have plenty of disposable plates, cups, and napkins, along with trash bags and recycling bins. If you're serving a hot meal, you'll need disposable serving spoons and forks. A good music playlist will complete the setup.
