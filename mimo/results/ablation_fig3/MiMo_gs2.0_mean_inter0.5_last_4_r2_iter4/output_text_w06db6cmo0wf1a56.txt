Absolutely! Here's a **mess-minimized, kid-friendly, and dad-approved shopping list** for your bachelor party theme park adventure. Everything is designed for grab-and-go convenience in coolers:

---

### **DRINKS**  
*(Focus: Hydration + Low-Sugar Refreshment)*  
- **Water**: 40 large water bottles (1L each) or 80 standard bottles (500ml)  
- **Sugar-Free Sodas**: 10 cans (e.g., Coke Zero, Pepsi Zero, or generic "Sugar-Free")  
- **Low-Sugar Juice Boxes**: 10 boxes (Apple, Grape, or Mixed) for kids  
- **Sports Drinks**: 10 boxes (e.g., Gatorade Zero or Powerade Iced) for adults  
- **Ice Packs**: 2–3 (to keep drinks cold for hours)  

---

### **SNACKS**  
*(Focus: No-Prep, Nutritious, Kid-Friendly)*  
- **Cheese Sticks**: 20 sticks (mix of cheddar and mozzarella)  
- **Grapes**: 2 lbs (pre-washed)  
- **Single-Serve Pretzels**: 10 bags (mini or regular)  
- **Trail Mix**: 5 bags (kid-friendly: no chocolate, just nuts/seeds/dried fruit)  
- **Baby Carrots**: 2 lbs  
- **Single-Serve Hummus Cups**: 10 cups (with baby carrots as a bonus snack)  

---

### **MAIN MEAL**  
*(Focus: Easy-to-Eat, Calzone-Centric)*  
- **Mini Calzones**: 10–12 pre-baked mini calzones (check brands like DiGiorno or store-brand "Mini Calzones" – they’re cleaner than homemade). *Alternatively*, if you insist on real calzones:  
  - **Pre-Made Calzones**: 10–12 individual calzones (e.g., from local bakery or grocery store deli).  
- **Wrap/Sandwiches**: 10 individual wraps or sandwiches (e.g., turkey & cheese, veggie hummus). Include:  
  - 10 single-serve tortillas (for on-the-go refills)  
  - 10 single-serve condiment cups (mustard, ketchup, etc.)  

---

### **DESSERT**  
*(Focus: Celebration-Friendly, Portable)*  
- **Cupcakes**: 10 cupcakes (or 5 cupcakes + 5 brownies for variety)  
- **Brownies**: 10 individually wrapped brownies (or a pack of 10 mini brownies)  
- **Packaging**: Cupcake liners + dessert boxes to avoid drips  

---

### **ESSENTIALS**  
- **Coolers**: 2 large coolers (one for drinks, one for food)  
- **Reusable Ice Bags**: 2–3  
- **Cooler Bags**: For easy transport to/from the park  
- **Lids/Coverings**: For food containers (prevent spills)  
- **Labels**: Mark coolers (e.g., "DRINKS," "FOOD")  

---

### **KEY Tips for Success**  
1. **Prep Ahead**:  
   - Cut sandwiches/wraps into halves for easier eating.  
   - Pre-fill water bottles the night before.  
2. **Pack Smart**:  
   - Use **resealable containers** for snacks (e.g., BPA-free plastic containers).  
   - Store calzones/cupcakes in **insulated bags** with ice packs.  
3. **Mess Control**:  
   - Bring a **small trash bag** per cooler for scraps.  
   - Use **napkins** (100+ packs) and wet wipes (for kids/cars).  
4. **Customize**:  
   - Add allergy-friendly options if needed (e.g., gluten-free snacks).  
   - Include a few **recovery snacks** (yogurt cups, fruit) for the ride home.  

This plan keeps things **healthy, stress-free, and fun** so you can focus on celebrating your friend! Have an amazing time! 🎢🎉
