Oh, that's nice of you! Since your sister's a vegetarian, I'd suggest focusing on creative and flavorful plant-based dishes. You could do a creamy mushroom risotto with truffle oil, which is hearty but elegant. Or a stuffed bell peppers with quinoa, black beans, and corn for something colorful and satisfying. If you're feeling adventurous, you could even make a vegan "steak" out of portobello mushrooms, seared to look like real meat. And for dessert, maybe a chocolate avocado mousse? It’s decadent but still vegetarian. Hope she enjoys your cooking!
