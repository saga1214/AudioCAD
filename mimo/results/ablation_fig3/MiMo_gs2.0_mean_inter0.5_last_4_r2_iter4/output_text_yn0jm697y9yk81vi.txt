Here's a consolidated list of my best recommendations for tools and materials, along with where you can purchase them locally in Indianapolis without going online:

### Cutting Sheet Metal  
1. **For Thinner Sheets Curves**:  
   - **Tool**: Aviation Snips  
   - **Where to Buy**: The Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards  

2. **For Thicker Metal / Power Cutting**:  
   - **Tool**: Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel  
   - **Where to Buy**: The Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards  

3. Alternative Cutting:  
   - **Tool**: Jigsaw with Metal-Cutting Blade (Grade Z44)  
   - **Where to Buy**: Lowe's, Menards  

---

### Drywall Fasteners  
1. **Best Option**:  
   - **Tool**: 1 ¼" Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws  
   - **Why**: Superior holding power, prevents nail pops/cracks.  
   - **Where to Buy**: The Home Depot (brand: Delta or MasterBuilders)  

2. Alternative:  
   - **Tool**: Ring-Shank Nails (for wood studs)  
   - **Where to Buy**: Ace Hardware, Tractor Supply Company  

---

### Insulation  
1. **Best for Walls**:  
   - **Material**: Fiberglass Batt Insulation (Pre-Cut Rolls)  
   - **Why**: Easy installation, cost-effective, standard for walls.  
   - **Where to Buy**: The Home Depot, Lowe's  

2. Alternative:  
   - **Material**: Mineral Wool Insulation (For Soundproofing/Fire Resistance)  
   - **Where to Buy**: Menards, Tractor Supply Company  

3. Premium Option:  
   - **Material**: Spray Foam Insulation  
   - **Why**: Air-seals gaps, energy-efficient.  
   - **Where to Buy**: Local Pros (e.g., **All Pro Insulation**, 317-883-3989)  

---

### Pro Tips:  
- **Angle Grinder Safety**: Wear a respirator and safety glasses.  
- **Jigsaw Blade**: Use a **Z44** metal-cutting blade for clean cuts.  
- **Drywall Screws**: Pre-drill holes for thicker drywall (up to ½”) to prevent splitting.  

For Menards-specific items (like mineral wool), visit their **20th & East St. store** or **Broad Ripple location**. All other items are widely available at The Home Depot or Lowe’s.
