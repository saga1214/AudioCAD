Alright, here's a full list of your five cocktails with all the measurements and instructions. Your bartenders will be ready to go!

First up, Old Fashioned. You'll need two ounces of bourbon, like a Jim Beam or a Knob Creek. Add half a sugar cube or one teaspoon of simple syrup. Then, three dashes of Angostura bitters. Stir all that together with ice in a rocks glass until it's chilled, then top up with more ice and garnish with an orange twist and maybe a cherry.

Next, Paper Plane. For this, you'll use two ounces of bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and fresh lemon juice all in equal parts, so that’s one ounce each. Put everything in a shaker with ice, shake it hard, and strain it into a nice-looking coupe glass.

For the Whiskey Sour with or without the egg white: If you're using it, you'll dry shake first: two ounces of bourbon, half an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and one egg white. Then add the ice, shake again, and strain into a rocks glass garnished with a cherry or an orange slice. If you skip the egg white, it's just two ounces of bourbon, half an ounce of lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup, shaken with ice and strained over ice.

Your fourth drink is the Aviation. For this, you'll need two ounces of gin, half an ounce of maraschino liqueur, a quarter-ounce of crème de violette, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Shake it with ice, strain into a cocktail glass, and garnish with a cherry.

And for the fifth one, the French 75. You'll take one and a half ounces of gin, half an ounce of lemon juice, and three-quarters of an ounce of simple syrup. Shake that with ice, then strain it into a champagne flute and top it off with about two ounces of dry sparkling wine or champagne. A lemon twist on top is a nice touch.
