Let's get you past the smoke alarm. For stir-fry noodles, it's all about timing.

First, make sure you use metal cutlery, definitely no wood! Get a good pot of water boiling, but you can add a little salt and some oil to the water to help the noodles not stick together.

The most important step is to cook the noodles for less time than the package says, usually about one minute less. They will finish cooking in the hot stir-fry pan. After they drain, make sure to give them a good shake to get them all separated. Then, just add them to the pan with your meat and veggies at the end so they don't get mushy. You got this!
