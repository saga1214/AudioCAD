Of course! With your new car, here are five fantastic road trips from Vancouver, all about 4 to 5 hours away, with a mix of stunning scenery and great food.

First, you could head to Victoria and the Cowichan Valley. You can drive through the scenic Gulf Islands National Park and explore Sooke. There, you can hike the famous Tofino rainforest or visit the unique Tsoualene Island. For food, you can't miss a place like Oyster City Inn for fresh seafood.

Second, a trip to the Okanagan Valley is a great idea. You can drive through the beautiful Kalamalka Lake area and visit Nillamutu Cattle Ranch for a farm tour. For a special meal, try the lakeside dining at the Fairmont Okanagan Hotel.

Third, there's the Fraser Canyon and the Chilcotin Pass. You can explore the historic town of Bellingham and the Chilcotin Lakes. A great activity is visiting the Chilcotin Hot Springs for a relaxing soak. For dining, you could try a fine dining experience like the one at the Fairmont Checkpoint Lodge.

Fourth, you could head to the Sea to Sky Highway towards Squamish and Whistler. You can drive the famous Sea to Sky Gondola and hike the Blackcomb Glacier. For a memorable meal, you could try the fine dining at the Fairmont Chateau Whistler.
