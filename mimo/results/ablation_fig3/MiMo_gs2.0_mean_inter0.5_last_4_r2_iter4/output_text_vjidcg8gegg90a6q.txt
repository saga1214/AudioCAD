For winter chicks integrated into the adult coop later, you'll need bedding that provides insulation, dries quickly, and is safe. The best choices are wood shavings, specifically the large flake kind, or paper-based bedding. Both of these will help hold the heat from your new plate effectively. Try to avoid pine shavings with a lot of aromatic oils, as they can irritate the chicks. And definitely stay away from straw or hay, as you already know the rodent risk.
