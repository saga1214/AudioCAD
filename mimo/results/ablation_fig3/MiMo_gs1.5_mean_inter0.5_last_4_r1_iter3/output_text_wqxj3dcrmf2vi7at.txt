Here's a full recap of treat ideas for your little corgi princess, categorized for easy reference:

### 🍦 **Homemade Treats (Easy & Customizable)**
1. **Frozen Bites**:  
   - Mashed **pumpkin** or **sweet potato** (mix with yogurt for extra yum!).  
   - Freeze in small molds or silicone ice cube trays.  
2. **Peanut Butter Cookies**:  
   - Mix **peanut butter** (NO xylitol!) with oats, bake until firm.  
   - Cut into tiny shapes.  
3. **Chicken or Veggie Sticks**:  
   - Cooked **chicken breast** (plain, no seasoning) or steamed **carrot sticks**.  
   - Cut into thin matchsticks for safe nibbling.  

### 🛒 **Store-Bought Brands (Trusted & Convenient)**  
*(Look for these brands at pet stores or online)*  
- **Training Treats**:  
  - **Zuke’s Mini Training Puffs** (grain-free options available).  
  - **Greenies Mini Dental Chews** (great for teeth + training).  
- **Wholesome Snacks**:  
  - **Blue Buffalo Natural Training Treats** (small, crunchy).  
  - **Wellness Complete Health Small Breed Bites** (grain-free).  
- **Special Flavors**:  
  - **Merrick Grain-Free Training Treats** (peanut butter flavor).  
  - **Kikoriki Organic Training Bites** (vegan-friendly).  

### 💡 **Key Tips**  
- **Portion Control**: Corgis are prone to weight gain—stick to **1-2 treats per day**.  
- **Safety First**: Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions, xylitol, or anything with bones (choking risk).  
- **Training Hack**: Use tiny treats for commands (like "sit"), and switch to kibble for regular meals.
