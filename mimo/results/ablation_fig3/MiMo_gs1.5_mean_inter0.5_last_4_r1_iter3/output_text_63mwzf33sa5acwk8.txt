Okay, so you're thinking of combining a desert adventure with a classic Vegas trip. That’s a cool mix! If you want a proper Vegas experience after your van trip, here’s a breakdown. 

First, for the hotel, you could splurge on a classic spot like the Bellagio or the Venetian for that luxury feel. Or, if you want something more unique and fun, check out the Horseshoe or The Cosmopolitan. They have great nightlife and rooftop bars. 

For activities, you could hit up a show, like Cirque du Soleil or a big headliner. Vegas also has amazing restaurants, from fancy to cheap eats like the buffets. If you're up for it, gambling at the slots or roulette can be fun, but just remember to set a budget. 

Don’t forget to explore the Fremont Street area in the old downtown for free street performances and cool vibes. And if you're into nightlife, clubs like XS or Omnia at the Wynn are top-tier. 

The key is to balance the high-energy Vegas stuff with some downtime, like a nice spa day or just chilling in your room. It’s all about what you two enjoy.
