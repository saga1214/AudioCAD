Here’s a balanced 7-day itinerary for Tokyo and Osaka, including day trips to **Nara** and **Kyoto**, with built-in flexibility for your coursework and workouts:

### **Tokyo (4 Days)**  
**Day 1: Modern Tokyo & Shopping**  
- **Morning (7–8:30 AM):** Workout (hotel gym or park).  
- **Late Morning (11 AM–6 PM):** Explore **Shibuya** (Shibuya Scramble, Hachiko Statue) and **Harajuku** (Takeshita Street, Omotesando).  
- **Evening:** Dinner in Harajuku or Shinjuku.  

**Day 2: Culture & Nature**  
- **Morning (7–8:30 AM):** Workout.  
- **Late Morning (11 AM–6 PM):** **Asakusa** (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Street) → **Ueno Park** (museums, cherry blossoms if in season).  
- **Evening:** Dinner in Asakusa.  

**Day 3: Urban Landmarks & Tech**  
- **Morning (7–8:30 AM):** Workout.  
- **Late Morning (11 AM–6 PM):** **Shibuya** (Shibuya Sky, Hachiko) → **Akihabara** (tech/gaming culture) or **Ginza** (luxury shopping).  

**Day 4: Art & Gardens**  
- **Morning (7–8:30 AM):** Workout.  
- **Late Morning (11 AM–6 PM):** **Meiji Shrine** (Harajuku) → **Hamarikyu Gardens** → **Omotesando** (designer boutiques).  
- **Afternoon:** Free time for coursework prep.  

---

### **Osaka (3 Days)**  
**Day 5: Osaka City**  
- **Morning (7–8:30 AM):** Workout.  
- **Late Morning (11 AM–6 PM):** **Osaka Castle** → **Dotonbori** (street food, neon signs).  
- **Evening:** Dinner in Dotonbori.  

**Day 6: Nara Day Trip**  
- **Morning (7 AM–12 PM):** Train to **Nara** (45-min from Osaka). Visit **Todai-ji Temple** (giant Buddha), **Nara Park** (deer).  
- **Afternoon (1–6 PM):** Return to Osaka → **Kuromon Ichiba Market** (food market).  

**Day 7: Kyoto Day Trip**  
- **Morning (7 AM–12 PM):** Train to **Kyoto** (30-min from Osaka). Visit **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (torii gates).  
- **Afternoon (1–6 PM):** **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion) → **Ryoan-ji** (Zen rock garden).  
- **Evening:** Return to Osaka.
