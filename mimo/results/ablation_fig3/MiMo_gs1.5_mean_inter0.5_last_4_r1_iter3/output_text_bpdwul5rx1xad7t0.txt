Of course! With your new car and a growing family, here are five fantastic road trip ideas for the BC coast.

First, you could drive to the Vancouver Island Rainforest. You can explore the ancient trees of the Great Bear Rainforest or the temperate rainforest of Tofino. The activities are incredible, like whale watching in the wild, hiking to waterfalls, and camping under the stars.

Second, you could head to the Okanagan Valley. You'll find stunning lakes like Penticton and Kettle Lake, and you can visit wineries. For activities, you can go wine tasting, enjoy a hot air balloon ride, and swim in the lakes.

Third, for a bit of a detour, you could visit the Fraser Canyon. The scenery is rugged and beautiful. There, you can hike to the Fraser Canyon Waterfall, explore a historic ghost town, and try local craft breweries.

Fourth, you could go to the Coast Mountains to see the Lillooet Glacier. It's a stunning destination with turquoise lakes. You can hike on the ice, fish in the alpine lakes, and stay in a cozy mountain lodge.

Finally, for a classic, you could take the Sea-to-Sky Highway to Whistler. It's one of the most beautiful drives in the world. Once you're there, you can go skiing in the winter, hiking in the summer, and enjoy the vibrant town.

Each of these trips offers something a little different, from rainforests to mountains and glaciers, and all have amazing things to do and places to eat!
