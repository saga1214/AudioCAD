Here's a **detailed packing list** separated into two sections for your pacing duties:

### **1. 20-Mile Segment (Night Run)**
*(Mid-30s, Hilly, Overnight)*  
- **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking synthetic top/bottom (avoid cotton).  
- **Mid-Layer:** Fleece jacket or lightweight insulated vest (for warmth).  
- **Outer Layer:** Windproof/waterproof shell (e.g., rain jacket) for cold/hills.  
- **Headwear:** Warm hat + gloves (fingerless or full) + buff/gaiter (for neck/wrists).  
- **Visibility:** Headlamp + **extra batteries** + handheld light (glow sticks as backup).  
- **Footwear:** Trail shoes with grip + extra socks (wool/synthetic).  
- **Extras:** Small pack/vest (to carry layers), hand warmers, sunscreen (cool nights can fry skin), hydration bladder.  

### **2. 12-Mile Segment (Daytime)**  
*(Mid-30s, Hilly, Aid Stations Available)*  
- **Base Layer:** Quick-dry top/bottom (sweat management).  
- **Mid-Layer:** Light fleece or breathable vest (adjustable for warmth).  
- **Outer Layer:** Light windbreaker (if needed for wind).  
- **Headwear:** Cap or sun hat + sunglasses (if sunny).  
- **Footwear:** Same as above (shoes + socks).  
- **Extras:** **Lightweight pack** (to stash extra layers if needed), electrolyte pack (if you prefer it), lip balm, sunscreen.  

### **Key Notes for Both Segments:**  
- **Layer System:** Wear the lightest layers first. Add/remove as needed.  
- **Gloves:** Critical for night running—your hands get cold fast.  
- **Visibility:** A headlamp is non-negotiable for the first 20 miles.  
- **Packability:** Use a pack/vest to carry layers, gloves, or extra socks without slowing you down.  
- **Test Gear:** Do a run with your kit beforehand to avoid chafing or discomfort.
