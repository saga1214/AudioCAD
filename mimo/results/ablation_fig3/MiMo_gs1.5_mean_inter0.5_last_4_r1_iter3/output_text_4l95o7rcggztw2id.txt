Here's a detailed shopping list for your nautical-themed pirate birthday party, focusing on **sand-friendly, easy-to-eat finger foods, pirate/nautical themes, and avoiding watermelon**:

### Appetizers & Snacks (The "Pirate's Plunder")
1.  **Mini Pirate Flag Sliders:** 24 mini slider rolls (beef, chicken, or veggie). Cut into quarters and arrange with **pirate flag shapes** (red, white, black) printed on toothpick flags or small cutouts.
2.  **"Jewel" Fruit Skewers:** 24 small grapes (red, green, purple) on short skewers. Skip watermelon. Label as "Pirate's Jewels" or "Treasure."
3.  **Cheese & Crackers "Gold":** 1 large block cheddar cheese cut into cubes. 1 large block mozzarella (or pepper jack) cut into cubes. 12-16 small goldfish crackers. Serve on small plates or in a "treasure chest" container.
4.  **Veggie "Cannonballs":** 1 large cucumber cut into small cubes. 1 large bell pepper (any color) cut into small cubes. 1 cup cherry tomatoes. Serve with a small bowl of ranch or hummus dip in a pirate flag-shaped container.

### Main Dishes (The "Galley's Bounty")
5.  **Mini Meatballs or Chicken Tenders:** 36 mini meatballs (beef, pork, or Italian) OR small bite-sized chicken tenders. Serve on small plates.
6.  **Mini Cornbread Muffins:** 12 cornbread muffins. Optional: Serve with a small bowl of cream cheese frosting for dipping (labeled "Cannonball Cream Cheese" or "Galley Butter").
7.  **Small Biscuits or Waffles:** 12 small biscuits or waffles. Serve with a small bowl of butter or cream cheese frosting.

### Sides (The "Ship's Provisions")
8.  **Small Baskets of Fruit:** 1 large basket containing:
    *   2 cups strawberries (halved)
    *   1 large container (32oz) of blueberries
    *   1 large container (32oz) of grapes (red, green, purple - no watermelon)
    *   1 large container (32oz) of mandarin oranges (in juice, no pulp)
    *   1 large container (32oz) of pineapple chunks
9.  **Small Baskets of Veggies:** 1 large basket containing:
    *   1 large container (32oz) of baby carrots
    *   1 large container (32oz) of snap peas
    *   1 large container (32oz) of cherry tomatoes
    *   1 large container (32oz) of cucumber spears

### Drinks (The "Seaspray")
10. **Juice Boxes:** 12 individual juice boxes ( assorted flavors - orange, apple, berry, etc.).
11. **Water Bottles:** 12 individual water bottles (clear or fun designs).
12. **Optional Fun Drink:** A large jug of "Pirate Punch" (water, lemonade, fruit punch, maybe a splash of red juice for "blood" - *check with parents*). Serve in individual cups or juice boxes.

### Desserts (The "Treasure Chest")
13. **Themed Cookies or Cupcakes:** 12-16 cookies or cupcakes decorated with pirate flags, ship shapes, or anchors. *Ensure no watermelon ingredients.*
14. **Your Amazing Birthday Cake!**

### Supplies for Presentation & Mess Control
15. **Themed Serving Trays/Baskets:** Use small treasure chests, pirate flags, or ship-shaped containers for snacks and drinks.
16. **Plastic Utensils:** Small plastic forks and knives for sandwiches/sliders.
17. **Paper Plates & Cups:** Biodegradable or compostable are best for the beach.
18. **Wet Wipes:** A large pack for quick cleanups.
19. **Trash Bags:** Plenty of small bags for easy cleanup.

### Key Considerations
*   **Allergy Check:** Double-check *all* packaged items (juice boxes, crackers, cheese, fruit cups, veggie trays) for the watermelon allergy label. Ask the host parents directly about the other children's allergies.
*   **Portion Control:** Cut everything small enough for 7-8 kids to eat easily without getting overwhelmed.
*   **Sand Avoidance:** Individual serving sizes, sealed containers (juice boxes, water bottles), and easy-to-eat finger foods minimize sand contact. Pack snacks in small, sealed bags if possible.
*   **Theme:** Use pirate flag shapes, labels like "Jewel," "Gold," "Treasure," "Galley," "Cannonball," and "Pirate's Plunder" consistently.

This list provides a full course of easy-to-eat, sand-friendly foods with strong pirate/nautical themes, while accommodating the watermelon allergy. Enjoy your fantastic nautical pirate party!
