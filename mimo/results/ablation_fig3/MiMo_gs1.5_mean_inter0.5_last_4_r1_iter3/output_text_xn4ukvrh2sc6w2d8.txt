Here's a detailed bartender checklist with exact measurements and instructions for all your cocktails, perfect for training your team:

### 1. Old Fashioned  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Maker’s Mark, Buffalo Trace)  
- 1 sugar cube (or ¾ oz simple syrup)  
- 2 dashes Angostura buration  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle sugar cube with ½ oz water in a mixing glass until dissolved.  
2. Add bourbon and bitters.  
3. Stir with ice for 30 seconds until chilled.  
4. Strain into a rocks glass over a single large ice cube.  
5. Express orange twist over drink (no peel).  

---

### 2. Paper Plane  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Bulleit, Four Roses)  
- ¾ oz Aperol  
- ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice for 15 seconds.  
2. Strain into a coupe glass.  
3. Garnish with a lemon twist (squeeze over drink, wipe rim).  

---

### 3. Whiskey Sour  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 1 egg white (optional, for texture)  

**Instructions:**  
**With egg white:**  
1. Dry shake (no ice) all ingredients in a shaker for 10 seconds.  
2. Add ice, shake again for 15 seconds.  
3. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.  
4. Garnish with lemon twist or cherry.  

**Without egg white:**  
1. Shake with ice for 15 seconds.  
2. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.  
3. Garnish as above.  

---

### 4. Aviation  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz gin (e.g., Beefeater, Tanqueray)  
- ¾ oz maraschino liqueur (Luxardo)  
- ½ oz crème de violette  
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice for 15 seconds.  
2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
3. Garnish with a cherry (optional: lemon twist for aroma).  

---

### 5. French 75 (Recommended Gin Cocktail)  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- 0.75 oz lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz simple syrup  
- 2 oz champagne or prosecco  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and syrup with ice for 10 seconds.  
2. Strain into a flute.  
3. Top with champagne.  
4. Garnish with lemon twist.  

---

### Key Notes for Bartenders:  
- **Ice:** Use fresh, clear ice for clarity.  
- **Shaking:** Always shake bitters-based drinks with ice to chill properly.  
- **Garnishes:**  
  - Old Fashioned: Orange twist *only* (no peel).  
  - Aviation: Cherry *or* lemon twist (never both).  
- **Glassware:** Rocks glasses for Old Fashioned/Whiskey Sour; coupes for Aviation/French 75; coupe for Paper Plane.  
- **Syrups:** Use 2:1 simple syrup (2 parts sugar to 1 part water).
