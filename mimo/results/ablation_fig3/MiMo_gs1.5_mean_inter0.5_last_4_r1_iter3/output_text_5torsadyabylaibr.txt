For Thanksgiving, a classic and impressive dessert is pumpkin pie. It's a very traditional choice and is easy to make with just a few ingredients, like canned pumpkin, eggs, and sugar. If you're looking for something that's more of an appetizer or a light side dish, you could make a cheese strait. It's a very simple, no-bake recipe that you just assemble by layering a mixture of cream cheese, horseradish, and chives over crackers.
