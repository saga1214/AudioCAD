Okay, let's see where we left off. You've done all the prep work: you've removed the hardware, degreased, sanded, and primed the black cabinets. You're now at the stage of applying the primer, which is the last step before painting. 

Before you apply that final primer, there is one last thing to do. You need to make sure the primer is fully dry. If you apply the primer too thickly or don't let it dry completely, you could end up with a dull finish or even bubbling when you apply the top coat of paint. So, give it the recommended drying time, which is usually around four to eight hours. Once it's dry, you can go ahead with your off-white paint.
