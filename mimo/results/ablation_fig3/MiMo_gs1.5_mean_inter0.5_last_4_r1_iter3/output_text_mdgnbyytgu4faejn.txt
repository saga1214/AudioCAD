Okay, let's break down the structure. A well-organized resume is key. Here's a standard format you can follow:

First, at the very top, you have your contact information. This should include your name, a professional email, your phone number, and your city. It's best to put this directly under your name for clarity.

Next is the professional summary. This is a short paragraph of about three to four sentences that highlights your most relevant skills and career goals.

After that, you can have a skills section. This is a great place to list your key skills, like technical abilities or soft skills. You can even group them into categories like 'Technical Skills' and 'Soft Skills'.

Then comes the experience section, which is the most important part. Here, you'll list your jobs, including the company, your job title, and the dates you worked there. For each role, use a couple of bullet points to describe your main responsibilities and achievements.

If you have relevant projects or volunteer work, you can add a section for that.

Education comes next. You'll list your degrees, the school name, and your expected graduation date. You can also add details like your honors or scholarships right there.

If you have any certifications or licenses, you can add a section for that, too.

Finally, you can include an optional section for awards and recognition. This is where you'd list things like a scholarship you received.

This format keeps everything logical and easy to scan. Just remember to tailor your resume for each job you apply for, and keep it to one or two pages. Good luck!
