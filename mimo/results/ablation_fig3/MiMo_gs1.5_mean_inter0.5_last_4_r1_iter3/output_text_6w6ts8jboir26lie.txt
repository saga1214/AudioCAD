That's wonderful news about customer service! Focusing on cooking is a great way to unwind. Here are 5 comforting and satisfying dinner ideas you could easily make with groceries:

1.  **Quick & Fresh Stir-Fry:** Grab some fresh veggies (broccoli, bell peppers, snap peas, carrots), a protein (like chicken breast, shrimp, or tofu), and a box of rice or noodles. Sauté the protein first, then the veggies, toss with a simple sauce (soy sauce, ginger, garlic, a little honey or sesame oil), and serve over rice or noodles. *Customizable and fast!*

2.  **Comforting Garlic & Olive Oil Pasta:** Cook pasta (penne, spaghetti, or fettuccine). While it cooks, sauté minced garlic and red pepper flakes in olive oil. Add a can of your favorite marinara sauce or a splash of pasta water to create a quick sauce. Toss with the pasta, fresh spinach, and cherry tomatoes (halved). *Simple, flavorful, and hearty.*

3.  **Easy One-Pan Frittata:** Whisk together eggs (6-8 large), a splash of milk, salt, and pepper. Sauté some chopped veggies (onions, mushrooms, spinach, peppers) in a skillet. Pour the egg mixture over the veggies. Once the edges are set, carefully flip it (or use a spatula to fold it over) and cook until the center is just set. Top with cheese if you like. *Perfect for a light but satisfying meal or a snack.*

4.  **Sheet Pan Roasted Chicken & Veggies:** Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss chopped veggies (potatoes, sweet potatoes, broccoli, Brussels sprouts, asparagus) with olive oil, salt, pepper, and maybe some herbs (rosemary, thyme). Place on a sheet pan. Roast for 20-25 minutes. While veggies cook, season a chicken breast or thigh with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika. Roast it alongside the veggies for another 15-20 minutes until cooked through. *Minimal cleanup, maximum flavor.*

5.  **Build-Your-Own Tacos:** Start with a base of seasoned ground beef, shredded chicken, or even black beans. Warm up some corn or flour tortillas. Then, pile on toppings: shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, sliced avocado, salsa, sour cream, shredded cheese, chopped cilantro. *Fun, customizable, and always satisfying.*
