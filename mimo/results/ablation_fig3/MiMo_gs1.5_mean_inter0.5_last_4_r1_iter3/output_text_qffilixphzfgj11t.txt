4.  **Swiss Chard:** Similar to spinach in nutrients (vitamins A, C, K, calcium, iron). Has vibrant stems. *Important:* Wash thoroughly. Remove tough stems; chop or shred leaves. Can be served raw or lightly steamed.
5.  **Romaine Lettuce:** A safe, crunchy, and hydrating green. Good source of vitamins A, C, and K, plus fiber. *Important:* Wash thoroughly. Remove any bruised or damaged leaves.
