You're absolutely right to think about weight. For easy transport, stick to small pots.

A succulent in a small pot might weigh less than two pounds. The bird's nest snake plant is a bit sturdier, maybe around three to five pounds. Both are manageable for one person to carry up five flights of stairs.

Since you plan to take them home, buying them new when you arrive would be the best option. That way, you know their weight and can pick a lightweight pot.
