That's a great choice! Secret Harbour Resort is fantastic for beachfront relaxation. Here are some fantastic **low-pressure, accessible, and fun daytime activities** right from Red Hook or nearby:

1.  **Snorkel at Secret Harbour or Coral Gardens:**
    *   **Why it's great:** The resort's own beach (Secret Harbour) often has calm, shallow waters perfect for beginners and those sensitive to pressure changes. The nearby Coral Gardens area (a short walk or boat ride) is renowned for incredible coral reefs, fish, and sometimes sea turtles – all usually in shallow, calm water. No pressure needed!
    *   **Accessibility:** Very low pressure risk. Easy access from the resort.

2.  **Explore Red Hook's Waterfront & Shops:**
    *   **Walk the Harbor:** Stroll along the beautiful, colorful waterfront. Admire the yachts, the local fishing boats, and the relaxed atmosphere.
    *   **Shop Local:** Browse the small shops and galleries lining Cruz Bay Road and the harbor area for local crafts, souvenirs, and maybe some unique finds.
    *   **People-Watching:** Red Hook has a very laid-back, bohemian vibe – great for just soaking it all in.

3.  **Take a Short Boat Tour (Choose Carefully):**
    *   **Magens Bay Boat Tour:** A popular, accessible tour departing from Red Hook. It's known for its informative guides, gentle pace, and stunning views of Magens Bay (one of the world's most beautiful beaches). It often includes snorkeling stops in calm, shallow areas like Coral Gardens. *Ask specifically about pressure changes during the tour.*
    *   **Island Hopping Tour (Shorter Options):** Some operators offer shorter trips to nearby islands like Water Island or Culebra (Coki Beach) focusing on snorkeling and history, potentially avoiding deeper, more turbulent waters.

4.  **Hike the Easy Coastal Trails:**
    *   **Cruz Bay to Magens Bay Trail:** A relatively flat, scenic trail (about 1-1.5 miles round trip) along the coast from Cruz Bay down to the iconic Magens Bay. Offers fantastic views, birdwatching opportunities (especially hummingbirds and bananaquits!), and a gentle workout. You can go as far as you like.
    *   **Cinnamon Bay to Cinnamon Bay:** Another easy, short coastal walk from Cruz Bay down to the beautiful Cinnamon Bay (though Magens is closer to Red Hook).

5.  **Relax at Magens Bay (If Feasible):**
    *   **Why it's great:** While it's a famous tourist beach, Magens Bay is incredibly calm, shallow, and protected by a reef. The sand is soft, and the water is usually very warm and clear. It's a stunning, iconic Caribbean beach.
    *   **Considerations:** It can get crowded, but the resort's beach (Secret Harbour) or Coral Gardens might be less crowded alternatives. The pressure change of getting *to* the beach via a short bus/taxi ride might be minimal. *If your brother is sensitive, test the pressure change getting off the resort shuttle/bus first.*

6.  **Visit the Coki Beach Beach Bar:**
    *   **Why it's great:** A short walk or taxi ride from Secret Harbour/Red Hook, this beach bar has a fun, relaxed atmosphere. Enjoy a drink, some music (usually not too loud), and soak up the sun on the beautiful Coki Beach.

7.  **Birdwatching (Especially near Secret Harbour):**
    *   **Why it's great:** The area around Secret Harbour and the resort itself is excellent for birding. Keep an eye out for:
        *   **Bananaquits:** Tiny, iridescent birds that look like hummingbirds but are different.
        *   **Hummingbirds:** Various species, including the Antillean Crested Hummingbird.
        *   **Grosbeaks:** Larger, colorful birds.
        *   **Parrots:** Green parrots are common sights.
    *   **Accessibility:** Gentle walks along the beachfront or resort paths are perfect.

**Key Considerations for Your Brother:**

*   **Pressure Changes:** Avoid activities that involve significant pressure changes, like deep diving (even snorkeling in very deep water might be tricky – stick to shallow reefs), or steep, rapid ascents/descents in boats. Short, gentle transitions are best.
*   **Noise Levels:** Be mindful of loud music or crowds, especially in bars or popular beaches. Red Hook's waterfront and Secret Harbour's beachfront are generally more relaxed.
*   **Sun Exposure:** Eerubrums heal best in the shade. Use reef-safe sunscreen and seek shade frequently, especially during the hottest part of the day.

Enjoy your week! Red Hook and Secret Harbour offer a perfect blend of relaxation, nature, and local charm.
