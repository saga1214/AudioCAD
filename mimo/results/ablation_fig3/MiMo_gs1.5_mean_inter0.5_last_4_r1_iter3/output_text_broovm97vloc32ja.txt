Yes, you can absolutely submit an entire package, and that's actually a very strong advantage. If you have illustrations, you can include them with your manuscript.

However, there are some important things to keep in mind. First, the illustrations must be high-quality and perfectly match the rhythm and tone of your text. Second, you need to be clear that these are your own original art, not something you found online. Third, and this is crucial, you will have to be prepared to give up the rights to the illustrations once the book is sold. The publisher will own them.

But honestly, if your illustrations are professional, it's a fantastic thing to show them. It proves you've put in the work and it can really make your book stand out. Just be sure to include a note stating your copyright.
