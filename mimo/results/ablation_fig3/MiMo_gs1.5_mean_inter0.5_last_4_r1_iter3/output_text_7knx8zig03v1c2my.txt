### 🐶 Treat Recommendations:

**Pro Tip**: Use treats smaller than a pea during training, and gradually reduce size as he learns.

---

### 🔍 Clicker Training Explained:
Clicker training uses a **three-step process** to build understanding:  
1. **Clicker**: A small, handheld device that makes a sharp "click" sound.  
2. **Treat**: A reward given *immediately* after the click.  
3. **Command**: The word (e.g., "sit") you say *after* the click.  

#### How It Works:  
- **Step 1: Click + Reward**  
  Click → Immediately give a treat.  
  *Example:* Click → Toss a chicken cube.  
- **Step 2: Shape the Behavior**  
  Click when he *almost* does the right thing (e.g., lowers his butt slightly).  
- **Step 3: Add the Command**  
  After a few successful clicks, say "sit" *after* the click, then reward.  

#### Why It Works:  
The click acts as a **bridge** between the behavior and the reward. It’s precise and helps Galileo understand *exactly* what he did right.  

**Example for "Sit":**  
1. Hold a treat near his nose, moving it upward.  
2. As his butt lowers, click → toss treat.  
3. Say "sit" → repeat.  

---

### 💡 Key Tips for Success:
- **Practice 5-10 minutes/day** (short, frequent sessions).  
- **Always click FIRST**—never say "good boy" before clicking.  
- **Fade treats** gradually as he masters commands (use toys or praise later).  
- **Consistency is key**: Use the same clicker and treats each day.
