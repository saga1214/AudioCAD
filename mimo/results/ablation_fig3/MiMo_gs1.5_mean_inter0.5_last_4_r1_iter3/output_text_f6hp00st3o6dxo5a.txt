That's a very detailed point, and it's true that the egg contains the genetic material that defines the chicken.

However, the key thing to remember is that the egg had to be laid by a creature that was not a chicken. That creature's DNA was very close to a chicken's, but not exactly the same. It was the specific mutation in the DNA of the creature *producing* the egg, not the egg itself, that created the first true chicken. So, the chicken still hatched from the egg, making the chicken the first one to exist.
