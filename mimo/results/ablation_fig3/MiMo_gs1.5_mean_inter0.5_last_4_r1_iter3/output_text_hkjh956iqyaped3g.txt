That's a great question! Since it's your last break of the day, you want something that leaves you feeling refreshed but not groggy or overwhelmed. Here are some ideas for a 15-minute break:

1.  **Short Walk (Highly Recommended):** Get up, move around. Walk outside if possible (even just around the block), or pace near your desk. It boosts circulation, clears your head, and provides a gentle mental reset. Listen to your favorite music or just observe your surroundings.
2.  **Mindful Coffee/Tea Break:** Enjoy a cup of coffee or tea slowly and mindfully. Focus on the sensory experience: the aroma, the warmth of the cup, the taste. Combine this with a few minutes of deep breathing exercises (inhale for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale for 6).
3.  **Power Nap (Use Caution):** *Only* if you *know* you can fall asleep quickly and won't feel groggy. Set a strict timer for **10-15 minutes max**. Find a quiet place (your car, a dark room, even just closing your eyes at your desk). Use earplugs if needed. If you're not sure, skip this one.
4.  **Quick Stretch & Breathing:** Stand up and do a series of gentle stretches – neck rolls, shoulder shrugs, wrist circles, ankle circles, reaching for the ceiling. Pair this with 1-2 minutes of deep, diaphragmatic breathing (belly breathing).
5.  **Listen to Uplifting Music (Like You Do!):** Put on your favorite energizing playlist or one of the new artists I suggested (Toumani Diabaté, Tinariwen). Let it wash over you without needing to focus on it. Just let the rhythm and sound relax you or lift your mood.
6.  **Quick Journaling:** If you have a notebook handy, spend 5 minutes jotting down any lingering thoughts, to-do list items for tomorrow, or simply writing down one thing you're grateful for. This can help clear mental clutter.
7.  **Disconnect Completely (If Possible):** If you can truly step away from work screens and tasks for 15 minutes, do it. Read a page of a novel, look out the window and observe, look at pictures on your phone, or simply sit quietly with your eyes closed and focus on your breath.
