Here's a breakdown for both your questions:

**1. U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia:**

*   **Ticket:** If you make an illegal U-turn, you'll likely get a ticket for **"Improper Turning"** or a similar infraction.
*   **Cost:** The standard penalty is **$116**, plus potential court costs (which can add $15-$25 or more).
*   **Maximum Penalty:** In some cases, especially if it causes a collision or significant delay, the fine can be increased to the maximum under the Highway Traffic Act, which can be **up to $300**.
*   **Risk:** Beyond the fine, illegal U-turns can cause accidents, block traffic, or force other drivers to brake suddenly.

**Key Takeaway:** It's best to follow the rules. If you're unsure if a U-turn is legal (e.g., in a blind curve, at an intersection without a signal, near a hilltop), **don't do it.** Double-check signage and your surroundings.

**2. Spaghetti Carbonara Recipe (Traditional Ingredients):**

Here's the core ingredient list you need to buy:

*   **Spaghetti:** 8 oz (225g) dried spaghetti.
*   **Guanciale:** 6 oz (170g) - *This is the traditional cured pork cheek. It's essential for authentic flavor.* (If unavailable, use thick-cut pancetta or thick-cut bacon as a substitute, but guanciale is best).
*   **Eggs:** 3 large eggs + 2 large egg yolks (about 1 large egg + 1 yolk is often used, but 3 whole eggs + 2 yolks is common).
*   **Pecorino Romano Cheese:** ½ cup (about 50g) finely grated Pecorino Romano cheese. Parmesan cheese can be used *in addition* to Pecorino for a milder flavor, but Pecorino is traditional.
*   **Black Pepper:** Freshly ground black pepper is crucial. Use plenty!
*   **Salt:** For cooking the pasta water.
*   **Optional:** A tiny pinch of nutmeg (optional, but some recipes include it).

**Key Steps Summary (Important for Success!):**

1.  **Cook the Pasta:** Boil spaghetti in well-salted water until *al dente*. Reserve at least 1 cup of starchy pasta water before draining.
2.  **Render the Guanciale:** In a large skillet over medium heat, cook diced guanciale until crisp and the fat renders out. Don't burn it.
3.  **Mix the Sauce:** In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, egg yolks, grated Pecorino Romano, and a *generous* amount of black pepper. *Do not add any cream or garlic!*
4.  **Combine Off Heat:** **Crucially, turn off the heat** under the skillet with the guanciale fat. Immediately add the drained pasta to the skillet, tossing vigorously. Remove the skillet from the heat entirely.
5.  **Temper the Eggs:** While tossing the pasta in the hot pan, slowly drizzle in the egg/cheese mixture while continuing to toss *vigorously*. The residual heat from the pan will cook the eggs into a creamy, smooth sauce without scrambling them. Add a splash of the reserved pasta water if the sauce seems too thick.
6.  **Serve Immediately:** Serve immediately with a final sprinkle of Pecorino and black pepper. No garlic, no cream, no parsley (unless you insist on a tiny bit of parsley, but it's not traditional).

Enjoy your delicious Spaghetti Carbonara! Remember to drive safely and follow the U-turn rules in BC.
