That's a great question! Since you're simplifying your kitchen, focus on versatile ingredients that can stretch into multiple meals. Here are some fast, clutter-friendly dinner ideas:

### 🥚 Quick & Easy Ingredients:
1. **Rice or Pasta**  
   (You already have rice noodles! Cook them with broth for a simple noodle soup, or pair with scrambled eggs/tuna).  
2. **Canned Goods**  
   Beans, tuna, or chickpeas—add to salads, wraps, or sautéed with veggies.  
3. **Frozen Veggies**  
   No wilting! Toss with protein (tofu, chicken) or spices for stir-fries.  
4. **Eggs**  
   Scramble with spinach and cheese, or make fried rice with leftover rice.  
5. **Pre-Cut Produce**  
   Save time with bagged salad kits or pre-chopped veggies.  

### 🍳 Speedy Meal Ideas:
- **15-Minute Stir-Fry**: Frozen veggies + tofu/chicken + soy sauce + rice noodles.  
- **20-Minute Pasta**: Cook pasta, then mix with canned tomatoes, garlic, and wilted spinach.  
- **5-Minute Rice Bowl**: Microwave rice + canned beans + salsa + avocado.  

### 💡 Pro Tip:  
**Shop Your Pantry First!** Before buying new ingredients, scan what you *already* have. This reduces clutter *and* waste.
