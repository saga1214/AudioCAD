Here are a few additional gluten-free snack ideas that pack well for camping and cycling:

1. **Roasted Chickpeas**: Crunchy, high in protein and fiber. Look for brands like "Simple Mills" or "Enjoy Life" (check for shared facilities).  
2. **Rice Cakes with Nut Butter**: Pair gluten-free rice cakes with almond or peanut butter for quick energy.  
3. **Trail Mix**: Combine nuts, seeds (pumpkin, sunflower), and dried fruit (like apricots or cranberries). Avoid honey if you need vegan options.  
4. **Fresh Fruit**: Bananas, apples, or berries (if chilled) provide natural sugars and hydration.  
5. **Homemade Energy Balls**: Blend oats (ensure certified GF), dates, cocoa powder, and chia seeds; roll into balls.  

**Pro Tip**: Always double-check labels for "may contain wheat/gluten" warnings—even if the brand is GF, cross-contamination can occur. Pack snacks in reusable containers to keep them fresh and organized! 🏕️🚴‍♂️
