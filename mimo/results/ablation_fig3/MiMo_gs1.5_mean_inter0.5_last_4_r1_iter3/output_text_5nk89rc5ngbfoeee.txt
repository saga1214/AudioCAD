For your end-of-year celebration, a **park picnic** is a fantastic choice—it’s memorable, budget-friendly, and lets you incorporate the activities you loved as a Brownie! Here’s a flexible plan to make it work:

### 🎉 **Event Ideas:**
1. **Park Picnic with Games**  
   - Set up a **"Brownie Olympics"** with races (wheelbarrow, sack race), tug-of-war, or relay races.  
   - Include **craft stations** (making friendship bracelets or decorating sashes with patches).  
   - **Food**: Keep it simple!  
     - *For the girls*: Ice cream sundaes (offer dairy-free options) or mini pizzas.  
     - *For parents*: A BBQ or potluck—ask them to bring one dish to share.  

2. **Cafeteria Party**  
   - Low-cost and easy to manage.  
   - Decorate with streamers and award patches to each girl.  
   - **Activities**: Trivia about Brownie badges, storytelling, or a talent show.  

### 💡 **Key Logistics:**
- **Weather Backup**: Rent a pop-up tent or choose a shaded area.  
- **Inclusivity**: Ask parents to RSVP for food allergies/preferences.  
- **Costs**:  
  - **Sashes**: Parents typically cover these—email them a heads-up about cost/size.  
  - **Dues/Patches**: Charge a small annual fee ($10–$20) to cover badge supplies. Parents usually pay for personal items (e.g., sashes).  
- **Parent Involvement**:  
  - **Snacks**: Rotate parent volunteers weekly (e.g., "Mom brings chips/dip next meeting").  
  - **Events**: Parents can help at the end-of-year picnic (grill, set up games).  

### 🌟 **Final Tip:**  
Keep the focus on **celebration and recognition**. Hand out patches, thank parents, and share photos from the year. You’ve got this—your enthusiasm will make it special! 🍦🏆
