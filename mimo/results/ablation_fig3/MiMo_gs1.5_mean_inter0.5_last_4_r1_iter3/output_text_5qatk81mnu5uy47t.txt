### **1. Logistics & Timing**
- **Best Time to Go**: December–April (dry season, warm water, calm seas). Avoid May–November (rainy season, stronger currents).
- **Flights**: Fly into San José International Airport (SJO). Book early for better rates.
- **Accommodation**: Stay near dive sites for convenience. Top areas:
  - **Puerto Viejo (Tortuguero)**: Jungle-dive combo, wildlife-rich.
  - **Cahuita**: Easy access to Caribbean coast sites.
  - **Ostional**: Untouched reefs, whale sharks (Dec–Apr).
  - **Cocos Island** (requires ferry; best for advanced divers).

---

### **2. Gear Recommendation**
As you’ve decided, **start with short fins** for your first few dives. They’re:
- **Easier to kick** on calm days.
- **Compact** for travel.
- **Less tiring** for beginners.
*Pro Tip*: Rent fins in Costa Rica to test styles (e.g., spring-loaded or flexible).

---

### **3. Must-See Dive Sites**
#### **Gulf of Papagayo (Pacific)**
- **Concepción Island**: Warm, shallow reefs with rays, turtles, and reef sharks. Ideal for beginners.
- **Playa Blanca**: Silky sponges, schools of fish, and occasional manta rays.
- **San Juan Island**: Crystal-clear water, vibrant coral gardens.

#### **Caribbean Coast**
- **Cahuita National Park**:保护珊瑚礁，海龟筑巢地（4-10月）。
- **Narita Reef**: 刺尾鱼、石斑鱼和海龟的热门潜水点。
- **Dolphin Bay**: 海豚和小丑鱼，适合休闲潜水。

#### **Cocos Island**
- **Tortugas Reef**: 深潜圣地，鲸鲨、锤头鲨和沉船。
- **Cocos Island Marine Park**: 多样化的珊瑚礁和海洋生物。

---

### **4. Wildlife Highlights**
- **Turtles**: Sea turtles (green, loggerhead) everywhere.
- **Whale Sharks**: Ostional (Dec–Apr), Cocos Island (May–Oct).
- **Manta Rays**: Playa Blanca (Pacific), Cocos Island.
- **Dolphins**: Dolphin Bay (Caribbean).
- **Reef Sharks**: Reef sharks and blacktips in calm areas.

---

### **5. Day Trips & Beyond Diving**
- **Tortuguero**: Jungle tours, birdwatching, and sea turtle conservation.
- **Monteverde**: Cloud forest hikes and sloth spotting.
- **Arenal Volcano**: Hot springs and wildlife reserves.

---

### **6. Practical Tips**
- **Certification**: Ensure your certification is valid (PADI/SSI).
- **Dive Shops**: Book with reputable operators (e.g., **Dive Costa Rica**, **Blue Horizons**).
- **Packing**: Wetsuit (if cold nights), reef-safe sunscreen, dive computer, and a waterproof camera.
- **Budget**: ~$1,200–$2,500/person for 7 days (flights, accommodation, diving).

---

### **Sample 7-Day Itinerary**
- **Days 1–2**: Puerto Viejo (Tortuguero jungle + Caño Island snorkel).
- **Days 3–4**: Cahuita (Caribbean reefs) + wildlife tours.
- **Days 5–6**: Ostional (whale sharks) or Cocos Island (advanced dives).
- **Day 7**: Relax in Arenal or return home.

---

### **Final Advice**
- **Book Early**: Popular sites fill up fast (especially whale shark season).
- **Safety First**: Choose guides with strong safety records.
- **Eco-Conscious**: Support local conservation projects.
