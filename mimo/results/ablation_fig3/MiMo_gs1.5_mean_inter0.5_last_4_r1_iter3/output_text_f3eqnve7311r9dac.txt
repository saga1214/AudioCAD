Absolutely. Vintage comics from the Golden Age, especially from the 1940s and 1950s, are highly collectible because of their scarcity. Here are ten notable titles from that era:

1.  *Action Comics* number one - First full appearance of Superman.
2.  *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven - First full appearance of Batman.
3.  *Strange Tales* number one - First full appearance of the Silver Surfer.
4.  *Tales to Astonish* number one - First full appearance of Ant-Man.
5.  *The Spectre* number one - First appearance of the Spectre.
6.  *The Silver Spectre* number one - First appearance of the Silver Spectre.
7.  *The Spectacular Spectre* number one - First appearance of the Spectacular Spectre.
8.  *The Brave and the Bold* number twenty-nine - First appearance of the Justice League of America.
9.  *Whizzer Comics* number one - First appearance of the Whizzer (a significant Golden Age character).
10. *Fury of the Faceless* number one - First appearance of Fury of the Faceless.
