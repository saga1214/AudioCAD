That sounds like a fantastic theme! Here are five meal ideas and five dessert ideas tailored for your ocean night.  

**Five Meal Ideas:**  
1. **Spicy Mango Shrimp Stir-Fry** – Shrimp sautéed with mango, bell peppers, and a spicy coconut sauce.  
2. **Coconut Curry Shrimp with Mango Salsa** – Shrimp in a creamy coconut curry with a sweet-tart mango salsa.  
3. **Grilled Shrimp Tacos** – Shrimp marinated in lime and chili, served in tortillas with mango slaw.  
4. **Mango Shrimp Ceviche** – Fresh shrimp "cooked" in citrus, mixed with diced mango and avocado.  
5. **Shrimp and Mango Stuffed Shells** – Pasta shells filled with shrimp and mango, baked in a creamy spicy sauce.  

**Five Dessert Ideas:**  
1. **Mango Panna Cotta with a Chili Twist** – Creamy mango panna cotta with a hint of spice.  
2. **Spicy Mango Sticky Rice** – Thai-inspired sticky rice with coconut milk and mango.  
3. **Mango and Coconut Chia Pudding** – Layered with mango puree for sweetness.  
4. **Mango Shrimp Skewers with Honey Glaze** – Grilled shrimp and mango on skewers with a sweet glaze.  
5. **Mango Sorbet with Chili-Infused Lime** – A refreshing, spicy-sweet sorbet.
