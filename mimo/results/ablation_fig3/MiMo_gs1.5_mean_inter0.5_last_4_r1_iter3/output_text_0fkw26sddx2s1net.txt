For the cucumber salad, you'll need cucumbers, rice vinegar, sesame oil, soy sauce, and some sesame seeds.

Next, for the main course, chicken teriyaki. You'll need boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, soy sauce, mirin, and sake. If you don't have sake, you can substitute with water and a bit of sugar. You'll also want a sweetener like brown sugar or honey, some grated fresh ginger, minced garlic, sesame oil, steamed jasmine rice, and stir-fried vegetables like bell peppers, snap peas, and carrots.

And for dessert, either mango sticky rice or homemade matcha ice cream. For the mango version, you'll need sticky rice, fresh mango, coconut milk, and sugar. For the matcha ice cream, you’ll need matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and a little vanilla extract.
