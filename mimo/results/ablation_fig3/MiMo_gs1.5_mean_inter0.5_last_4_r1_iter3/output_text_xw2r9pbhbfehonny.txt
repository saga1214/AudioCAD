Okay, so you're going to film your first show for a YouTube channel. Here’s a quick checklist of things to do before you start recording:

First, plan out the content. Decide on a short, fun skit or a few of your best routines. Maybe have a script or some notes ready, but keep it flexible.

Next, for the filming setup, you’ll need a tripod or a stable surface to hold your phone steady. Make sure the lighting is good—natural light works best. Also, try to avoid too much background noise.

Then, you’ll need to get permission. Since you’re at a nursing home, make sure they’re okay with you filming and that you can share the video online.

For your audience, try to keep it interactive. Ask them questions or involve them in a simple game to keep them engaged.

Finally, after filming, just edit the video. You can trim out any mistakes, add some simple music or captions, and then upload it with a catchy title and description.

That’s the basic rundown. Good luck!
