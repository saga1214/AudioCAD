### 🎃 Quick Decorations (Under 30 Minutes)
1. **Fog Chiller Setup**:  
   - **Materials**: Large plastic storage bin, frozen water bottles, hole punch.  
   - **Steps**:  
     a. Cut a hole in the bin for the fog machine nozzle.  
     b. Place a wire rack inside, load with frozen bottles.  
     c. Point the fog machine nozzle into the bin; fog will exit from another hole.  
   - *Why it works*: Cools fog to make it dense and ground-hugging.  

2. **Outdoor Magic**:
