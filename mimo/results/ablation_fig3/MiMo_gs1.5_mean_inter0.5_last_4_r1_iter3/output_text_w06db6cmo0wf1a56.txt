Here’s a well-organized shopping list based on your needs—focused on **minimal mess**, **portability**, and **group preferences**, including sugar-free sodas and calzones:

---

### **Drinks (20 total for 10 adults + 10 kids)**  
- **Water**: 20 large water bottles (1L each) or 40 single-serve bottles  
- **Sugar-Free Sodas**:  
  - 10 cans (adults) + 10 cans (kids) of sugar-free cola/lemonade  
- **Low-Sugar Juice**: 20 boxes (e.g., 6 oz cartons)  
- **Electrolyte Drinks**: 10 single-serve packets (for hydration in heat)  

---

### **Snacks (20 portions)**  
- **Cheese Sticks**: 20 individual packs  
- **Fresh Fruit**:  
  - Grapes: 2 lbs (divided into 10 bags)  
  - Baby carrots: 1 large bag  
  - Apple slices: 1 bag (tossed in lemon juice to prevent browning)  
- **Crunchy Options**:  
  - Pretzels: 10 small bags  
  - Trail mix: 2 individual bags  
- **Protein Boost**:  
  - Turkey & cheese roll-ups: 10 sets  
  - Hard-boiled eggs: 10 (peeled & pre-packed)  

---

### **Main Meals (10 portions)**  
- **Calzones**: 10 pre-ordered mini calzones from a local pizzeria (order 2-3 days ahead). *Tip: Ask for takeout boxes, not paper.*  
- **Wrap Bar Kit (Optional)**:  
  - 10 tortillas (whole wheat or spinach)  
  - 2 cups shredded chicken  
  - 1 cup hummus  
  - 1 cup spinach  
  - 1 cup shredded cheese  
  - 1 cup sliced veggies (bell peppers, cucumber)  
  *Prep note: Assemble wraps ahead; pack ingredients in separate containers.*  

---

### **Dessert (10 portions)**  
- **Cupcakes**: 10 sugar-free or gluten-free cupcakes (e.g., from a bakery)  
  *Or*  
- **Mini Pies**: 10 mini key lime or apple pies  

---

### **Essentials for Coolers**  
- **Ice Packs**: 2-3 large coolers + 10-12 ice packs (for calzones/wraps)  
- **Insulated Bags**: For non-perishables (snacks, drinks)  
- **Labels**: Mark coolers by "Drinks," "Food," and "Dessert"  
- **Utensils**: Biodegradable plates, cups, and napkins (minimal cleanup!)  

---

### **Key Tips**  
1. **Calzone Prep**: Reheat in cooler packs 1 hour before leaving.  
2. **Soda Note**: Use plastic bottles if space is tight; cans can dent coolers.  
3. **Kid-Friendly**: Include a small bag of animal crackers or graham crackers for emergencies.  
4. **Allergy-Friendly**: Check with the group for nut/dairy restrictions (e.g., sunflower butter instead of peanut butter).  

This plan ensures everyone stays fueled, hydrated, and entertained—while keeping mess to a minimum! Have an amazing bachelor party. 🎢🎉
