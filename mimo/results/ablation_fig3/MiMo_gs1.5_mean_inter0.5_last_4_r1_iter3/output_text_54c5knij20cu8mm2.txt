First, the left-brain versus right-brain idea is largely a myth. The brain doesn’t really separate creative and logical functions into two halves. Both sides work together constantly. However, some areas of the brain are more active during certain tasks. For example, the left hemisphere often handles language and logic, while the right handles spatial reasoning and creativity. But it's more of a spectrum.
