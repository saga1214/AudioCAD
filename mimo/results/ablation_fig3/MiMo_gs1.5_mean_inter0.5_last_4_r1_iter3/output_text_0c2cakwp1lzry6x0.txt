Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items, keeping noise and landlord rules in mind:

### 1. **TV Mount Installation**
   - **Step 1:** Check the mount's weight capacity and your TV's weight (on the box/label).  
   - **Step 2:** Use a **stud finder** to locate wall studs (every 16–24 inches). If no studs, use **heavy-duty drywall anchors** rated for the mount's weight.  
   - **Step 3:** Mark screw holes, then drill pilot holes slightly smaller than the screws.  
   - **Step 4:** Secure the mount to studs/anchors. Test stability before mounting the TV.  
   - **Noise Tip:** Use a stud finder with a drill function to minimize noise.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Step 1:** Choose a **strong beam or ceiling hook** (if allowed by landlord).  
   - **Step 2:** Measure and drill a hole in the beam. Use a **ceiling anchor rated for the planter’s weight**.  
   - **Step 3:** Attach a hook or hanger, then hang the planter.  
   - **Safety:** Ensure the planter’s weight won’t damage the anchor or beam.  
   - **Alternative:** Use **heavy-duty wall hooks** if beams aren’t accessible.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Step 1:** Mark shelf positions with a level. Space shelves 6–12 inches apart.  
   - **Step 2:** Drill pilot holes into wall studs. If no studs, use **L-brackets or heavy-duty drywall anchors**.  
   - **Step 3:** Attach the shelf back using screws. Test for wobbling.  
   - **Noise Tip:** Work during off-hours if drilling is loud. Use a stud finder with a drill mode.

### General Tips:
- **Landlord Rules:** Always check if drilling is permitted. Use existing outlets/switches if possible.  
- **Tools:** You’ll need a drill, level, measuring tape, pencil, and appropriate anchors.  
- **Safety:** Wear safety glasses. Avoid electrical wires (use a wire detector app if unsure).  
- **Time Management:** Dedicate 1–2 hours per project on Sundays. Focus on one task at a time.  
- **Art Installation:** Use **picture hanging strips** or **French cleats** for lightweight art. For heavy pieces, reinforce with a stud anchor.  

**Final Advice:** Take your time, measure twice, and don’t over-stretch yourself. Your new space will look amazing with these touches! 🛠️✨
