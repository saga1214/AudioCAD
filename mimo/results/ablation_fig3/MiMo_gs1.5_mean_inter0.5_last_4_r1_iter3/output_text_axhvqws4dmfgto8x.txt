Okay, let's get you set up for a successful stir-fry without setting off the alarm! Here are the key tips.

First, let's prevent that smoke alarm disaster. The wood spoon is the main culprit, so definitely use a metal spoon and a silicone pot lid. Another thing is salt. Adding salt to boiling water can actually lower the boiling point, so it's best to add it after you've drained the noodles. And if you still have a hunch, just rinse the noodles under cold water right before you stir-fry them.

Next, for the stir-fry itself, timing is everything. You want to cook the noodles first and drain them well to get rid of extra water. While those are cooking, you can prep and cook your protein and veggies. Then, just toss everything together in the wok. The key is to have everything prepped and ready to go before you start heating the pan. That way, you can get in and out quickly before things start to burn.

And most importantly, just take a deep breath and have fun with it. It's just food! Even if it's not perfect, you'll have learned something new. Good luck!
