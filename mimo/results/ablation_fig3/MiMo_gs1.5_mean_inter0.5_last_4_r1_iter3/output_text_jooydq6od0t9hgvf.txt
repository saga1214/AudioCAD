Here are quick, elegant drink recipes for your vodka mix and Argentine-inspired Fernet, keeping the soda allergy and formality in mind:

### **Vodka Cocktail (Elevated & Refreshing)**  
**Name:** *Berry Basil Smash*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ¾ oz muddled raspberries + 2 basil leaves  
- ½ oz honey syrup (*equal parts honey + hot water, stirred*)  
- Top with **sparkling water** (not soda!)  
- Garnish: Basil sprig + fresh raspberries  

**Why it works:** Light, herbaceous, and visually appealing—perfect for mixed groups. The sparkling water keeps it soda-free.

---

### **Argentine "Fernet" Style (Bold & Aperitivo)**  
**Name:** *Fernet-Spiced Spritz*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 1 oz Campari  
- ¾ oz fresh grapefruit juice  
- ½ oz lime juice  
- Top with **chilled white wine** or **sparkling water**  
- Garnish: Grapefruit twist  

**Why it works:** Mimics the traditional Argentine *Fernet con vino tinto* (Fernet with red wine), but lighter with citrus/wine. The bitter-sweet profile appeals to adults, and the sparkling option keeps it soda-free.

---

### **Quick Tips**  
1. **Soda Allergy:** Use sparkling water, wine, or tonic (if omnivores prefer) instead of soda.  
2. **Formality:** Serve in chilled coupes or wine glasses.  
3. **Speed:** Prep syrups (honey) and muddle berries/basil ahead.
