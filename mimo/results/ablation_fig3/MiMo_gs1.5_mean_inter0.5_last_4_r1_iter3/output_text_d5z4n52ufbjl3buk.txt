Here are a few things to consider for your email. Start with a polite subject line, like "Meeting Request: Exam Feedback".

Then, in the body, you can say something like, "I hope you're doing well. I scored a 64 on the recent exam and would like to understand what I could do to improve in the future."

Then, you could add a sentence that shows you're open to feedback, such as, "I'm interested in learning what areas I could have developed more effectively."

You could also mention your other strong grades to show you're a serious student, by saying, "I've performed well in this class so far and want to continue that success."

Finally, ask a specific question, like, "Could you point me to the rubric or criteria you used for this essay?"

This way, you're focused on your own growth and the objective of the class, which might make the conversation more productive.
