No worries—every dog has unique preferences! Here are some enticing treat ideas for Maisy, along with tips for training success:

### 🐾 **Treat Recommendations:**
1. **Peanut Butter (Xylitol-Free):**  
   - *Why it works:* Rich, creamy, and universally appealing. Use a tiny bit on your finger or in a lickable treat dispenser.  
   - *Tip:* Ensure it’s 100% peanut butter (no added sugar or xylitol).

2. **Freeze-Dried Chicken or Turkey:**  
   - *Why it works:* Light, crispy, and packed with flavor. Smaller dogs often love these!  
   - *Tip:* Look for "grain-free" options to avoid allergen risks.

3. **Mashed Banana or Blueberries:**  
   - *Why it works:* Mildly sweet and nutritious. Great for picky eaters.  
   - *Tip:* Use sparingly (1/4 slice per treat).

4. **Commercial Training Treats:**  
   - *Why it works:* Tiny, crunchy, and designed for training (e.g., Zignature or Blue Buffalo).  

### 🎯 **Training Tips:**
- **Start Small:** Begin with 2–3 minute sessions. Short and frequent > long and overwhelming.  
- **High-Value Rewards:** Use the *best* treat Maisy loves *only* for tricks (e.g., peanut butter for "sit," freeze-dried chicken for "spin").  
- **Timing is Key:** Reward the *instant* she does the trick (within 1 second).  
- **Practice Outside:** Less distractions = better focus.  
- **Be Patient:** If she’s not into cheese, switch treats! Don’t force it—try again later.  

### 💡 Pro Tip:  
If Maisy’s still hesitant, try **"clicker training."** Click a sound device the *moment* she does the trick, then immediately give a treat. The click teaches her *exactly* what to repeat!  

You’ve got this! Maisy’s excitement about bonding will come naturally—grooming, baths, and treats are all part of the journey. 😊
