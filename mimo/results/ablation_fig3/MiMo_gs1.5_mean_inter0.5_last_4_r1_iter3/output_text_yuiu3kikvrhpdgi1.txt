You're looking for a quick start, right? So, grab some basil, mint, and parsley. They grow fast and are super forgiving. For leafy greens, lettuce, spinach, and radishes are your best friends. They're easy, fast, and give you a ton of bang for your buck. And for something with more reward, get some young tomato and pepper plants. They're pretty resilient, and the flavor you get is just unbeatable.
