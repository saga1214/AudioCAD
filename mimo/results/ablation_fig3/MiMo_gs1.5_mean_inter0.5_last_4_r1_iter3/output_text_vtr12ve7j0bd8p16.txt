Okay, this is a fun one! Since you're looking for adventures as a Leo and Sagittarius, you need options that are exciting and full of discovery. Let's see what's around Calgary.

First, if you're up for a drive, the Kananaskis country is about an hour and a half away. It's called the 'Calgary Wilds' and has amazing hiking trails, scenic drives like the Kananaskis Lake Road, and even spots for kayaking. It's perfect for a sunny weekend.

For something closer, there's the Canmore area, just about 45 minutes east. You could hike in the Bow Valley Parkway, maybe the easy Johnston Canyon trails, or visit the famous Icefields Parkway. There are also options for ziplining or rock climbing there.

If you want to get really adventurous, you could try a hot air balloon ride. They launch from Calgary around sunrise, giving you a breathtaking view of the city waking up. It's a bit pricey, but unforgettable.

Another idea is a day trip to Banff. It's about a three-hour drive, but you can do things like the easy, scenic drive along Lake Louise, take a boat cruise on Moraine Lake if it's open, or hike to Johnston Canyon. It's a classic for a reason.
