### 🎯 Appreciation Table Setup (Refresh Theme)
1. **Refresh Station**  
   - **Drinks**: Infused waters (lemon-cucumber, berry-mint), chilled herbal teas (peppermint, chamomile), and coconut water.  
   - **Snacks**: Fresh fruit skewers, rice cakes with nut-free hummus, energy bites (oat-based, no nuts), and citrus-flavored mints.  
   - **Palate Cleansers**: Mini popsicles (coconut or fruit-based), cucumber slices with lime, or ginger chews.  

2. **Decorations**  
   - **Table Runner**: Light blue or mint-green fabric with subtle wave patterns.  
   - **Centerpieces**: Small potted succulents, dried citrus slices, or bamboo skewers with lemon wedges.  
   - **Signs**: Hand-painted chalkboards with messages like “Hydrate & Energize!” or “Thank You for Your Pawsome Spirit!”  

3. **Allergy-Safe Note**: Label all snacks/drinks with icons (e.g., 🌱 for vegan, 🚫nuts for allergen-free).

### 🔄 Communication & Logistics Upgrades  
1. **Volunteer Buddy System**  
   - Assign new volunteers to an “apprentice buddy” (experienced volunteer) for the first shift. Buddies receive a quick guide:  
     - Shelter layout map  
     - Task cheat sheets (e.g., “How to Comfort a Shy Cat”)  
     - Open Q&A time with staff.  

2. **Shift Coverage Plan**  
   - Use a shared digital board (Trello/Google Sheets) for real-time shift swaps.  
   - Offer “flex slots” (e.g., 2-hour gaps) for last-minute coverage requests.  

3. **Wellness Integration**  
   - **Mini Breaks**: 5-minute “calm corners” with calming music, dog-free zones, and stress balls.  
   - **Buddy Check-Ins**: Pair volunteers for 2-minute “how are you feeling?” chats during breaks.
