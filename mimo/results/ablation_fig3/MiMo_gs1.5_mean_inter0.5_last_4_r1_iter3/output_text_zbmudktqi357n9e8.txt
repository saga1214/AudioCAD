Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice! If you're set on watercolors, I'd say spring, from about March to May, is ideal. The weather is mild, perfect for painting, and the landscapes are just beautiful. 

Autumn scenes? Oh, definitely September to November. The colors are vibrant, the light is golden, and it's less crowded. You could even find inspiration in the coastal areas, you know, the turquoise waters and palm trees. 

And if you want to paint those realistic oceans or rivers, summer, June to August, is great for that. The water is warm, and the rivers are full from the rain. 

So yeah, it really depends on what you want to paint. But no matter when you go, just bring your watercolors, your markers if you want, and just soak in the inspiration. Mexico is going to be amazing for you. I really think you'll love it.
