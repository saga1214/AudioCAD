Okay, so you're asking for two different ideas on how to initiate that first conversation about a raise. Here are two approaches you could consider.

The first one is to request a scheduled meeting. You could send a professional email to your boss asking to meet at your earliest convenience. In the email, you can mention that you'd like to discuss your performance and career development, which gives a good reason for the meeting. This way, you're in control of the timing and can prepare thoroughly beforehand.

A second idea is to ask for some quick feedback first. You could schedule a brief, informal chat just to ask for feedback on your performance over the last year. This shows initiative and also gives you a chance to hear what your boss thinks. Often, if you've been doing well, a raise might come up naturally in that conversation. If not, you can use the feedback to plan for your next discussion.
