Absolutely! Here are some delicious, budget-friendly dessert ideas that are also diabetes-friendly and kid-approved:

### 🍓 **1. Fruit Skewers with Yogurt Dip**
   - **Ingredients**: Grilled pineapple, strawberries, grapes, melon chunks, and banana slices.
   - **Dip**: Greek yogurt mixed with honey (or sugar-free syrup) and a pinch of cinnamon.
   - **Budget Tip**: Buy seasonal fruits in bulk and use frozen yogurt to save costs.

### 🍌 **2. Banana "Nice Cream"**
   - **How to Make**: Blend frozen bananas until creamy (add a splash of almond milk if needed).
   - **Serve**: In bowls with toppings like crushed nuts, cocoa powder, or berries.
   - **Bonus**: No cooking required—perfect for a hot day!

### 🍪 **3. Oatmeal Cookies (DIY Station)**
   - **Base Recipe**: 1 cup oats, 1/2 cup peanut butter, 1/3 cup honey, 1 tsp vanilla, and a pinch of salt.
   - **Mix-ins**: Chopped nuts, dried fruit, or mini chocolate chips (use sparingly).
   - **Cost-Saver**: Make a big batch ahead of time and bake them.

### 🥄 **4. Mini Fruit Parfaits**
   - **Layers**: Greek yogurt, fresh berries, and a sprinkle of granola.
   - **Presentation**: Use small cups or glasses to portion control.

### 🍪 **5. Rice Cake "Sandwiches"**
   - **Kids' Favorite**: Top rice cakes with nut butter, banana slices, and a drizzle of honey.
   - **Healthier Option**: Use whole-grain rice cakes and add berries instead of honey.

### 💡 **Budget & Diabetes-Friendly Tips**
- **Label Everything**: Clearly mark sugar content (e.g., "Low-Sugar" or "High-Fiber").
- **DIY Stations**: Let kids assemble their own skewers or parfaits—fun and cost-effective!
- **Use Frozen Fruit**: Frozen berries and bananas are cheaper than fresh and still nutritious.
- **Skip the Ice Cream**: Offer sorbet (dairy-free) or blended frozen fruit instead.
- **Portion Control**: Serve in small bowls to avoid overindulgence.

### 🎯 **Why These Work**
- **Affordable**: Rely on seasonal produce, bulk ingredients, and no-bake options.
- **Kid-Friendly**: Interactive skewers, sweet dips, and cookie stations keep them engaged.
- **Awareness-Focused**: Highlight natural sugars in fruit and whole-grain options.
