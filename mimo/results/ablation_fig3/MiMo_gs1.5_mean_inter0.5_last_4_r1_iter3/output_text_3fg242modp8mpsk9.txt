That's a wonderful idea—combining your love for the ocean with a creative getaway! Here are some romantic, seascape-focused activities tailored for someone with a bit of sea-sickness, focusing on calm settings, anchored moments, and maximizing the seafood love:

### For Calm & Low-Motion Experiences:
1. **Private Bay Kayak & Picnic:**  
   Rent two tandem kayaks (or single ones) for a gentle paddle in a calm, sheltered bay. Pack a gourmet picnic with fresh seafood (like oysters, lobster rolls, or grilled shrimp) and a cozy blanket. Anchor briefly to enjoy the view.  
   *Why it works:* Minimal motion, intimate, and focuses on the water’s beauty.

2. **Sunset Cruise on a *Stabilized* Boat:**  
   Choose a catamaran or yacht with advanced stabilizers (common in luxury charters). Opt for a short, sunset-focused tour where the boat moves slowly through calm waters.  
   *Tip:* Bring ginger candies or motion-relief snacks for extra peace of mind.

3. **Glass-Bottom Boat Tour:**  
   See marine life (turtles, fish) up close while staying on a stationary dock or anchored boat. Many tours include a light seafood lunch onboard.  
   *Bonus:* The view is mesmerizing, and the boat’s stability eliminates motion.

### For Intimate Coastal Charm:
4. **Beachside Seafood Supper in a Hammock:**  
   Find a secluded beach with a beach shack. Order a seafood feast (e.g., mussels, clams, or a whole fish) and enjoy it while swaying gently in a hammock strung between palm trees.  
   *Romance Factor:* Sunset, seagulls, and zero risk of motion sickness!

5. **Private Dockside Dining:**  
   Many coastal towns offer "table-on-the-water" experiences. Request a table by the water, and order a seafood platter (think crab cakes, oysters, or a fresh catch of the day).  
   *Pro Tip:* Bring a bottle of local wine and a portable speaker for soft music.

6. **Moonlit Seaweed Bath:**  
   A classic romantic ritual! Float in the ocean at night (in a calm bay) with rose petals, champagne, and your favorite seafood appetizers.  
   *Safety Note:* Only do this in very calm, shallow waters—never during storms or rough seas.

### Creative "Boat-to-Table" Adventure:
7. **Fishing Charter with a Chef:**  
   Join a small, calm fishing boat (like a skiff) for a half-day trip. The chef onboard cooks your catch (or pre-bought seafood) right there on the boat. Enjoy a gourmet meal while drifting near the coastline.  
   *Why it’s special:* Fresh seafood, shared experience, and the thrill of a catch!

### Key Considerations:
- **Timing is Everything:** Avoid morning/afternoon transitions (when tides change) and prioritize calm bays over open ocean.
- **Backup Plan:** Have a land-based seafood restaurant nearby if weather turns.
- **Comfort First:** Pack motion-relief essentials (peppermint oil, ginger chews), breathable clothes, and sunscreen.

**Example Itinerary Snippet:**  
*Day 1:* Morning kayaking in a secluded cove → Picnic lunch by driftwood → Afternoon glass-bottom boat tour → Sunset dinner at a cliffside seafood shack.  
*Day 2:* Private boat picnic anchored near a lighthouse → Leisurely stroll through a seafood market → Moonlit seaweed bath under a starry sky.
