Okay, let's break this down. 

First, the brain hemispheres. The whole left-brain is logical and right-brain is creative thing is largely a myth. Real brain function is more interconnected. But the metaphor is useful. If you like structure and logic, you're probably engaging more with the left hemisphere's functions. Creativity isn't just on the right side—it's more about integrating different parts of the brain, like the prefrontal cortex and parietal lobes. So while the idea is oversimplified, the principle of balancing logic and creativity is real.

Now for careers, since you want something creative, objective, and with lasting value: 
First, consider data visualization or information design. It merges creativity with data analysis, and the work has real-world impact. 
Second, architecture or urban planning. It’s creative, involves problem-solving, and the buildings or spaces last for decades. 
Third, scientific illustration or technical writing. It combines art with clear communication, making complex ideas accessible. 
Fourth, product design or UX/UI design. It's creative but also analytical, focused on solving user problems in measurable ways. 

These fields all involve building structured, lasting things while leaving room for creativity. Hope that helps!
