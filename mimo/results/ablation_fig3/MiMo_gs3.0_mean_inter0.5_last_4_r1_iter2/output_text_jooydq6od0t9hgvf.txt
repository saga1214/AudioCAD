Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes for your vodka mixer and Argentinian-style fernet, keeping formality and your soda allergy in mind:

### 🥰 **Vodka Mixer: Refreshing Cucumber-Mint Spritzer**  
*(Vegetarian-friendly, soda-free, easy to make)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz vodka (leftover, perfect!)  
  - 4-5 cucumber slices (thinly sliced)  
  - 5-6 fresh mint leaves  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
  - 2 oz sparkling water (not soda!)  
  - Ice + optional: mint sprig for garnish  
- **Method**:  
  1. Muddle cucumber and mint in a shaker.  
  2. Add vodka, lime juice, and ice. Shake briefly.  
  3. Strain into a chilled glass. Top with sparkling water.  
  4. Garnish with mint.  

### 🇦🇷 **Argentinian-Style Fernet: Bitter & Zesty**  
*(Omnivore classic, soda-free, iconic in Argentina)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz fernet-branca (or your preferred bitter herbal liqueur)  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 0.5 oz simple syrup (1:1 sugar:water)  
  - 4-5 ice cubes  
  - Lime wedge + flaky sea salt (for rim)  
- **Method**:  
  1. Rim a glass with salt or skip for subtlety.  
  2. Fill glass with ice. Add fernet, lemon juice, and syrup.  
  3. Stir gently. Serve with a lime wedge.  

### 🎯 **Quick Tips**:  
- **Soda Allergy**: Both drinks use *sparkling water* (not soda) or no bubbles.  
- **Formality**: Use chilled glasses and garnish with herbs/limes for elegance.  
- **Flexibility**: Vegetarians can enjoy the spritzer; omnivores can choose either.
