Ooh, pickles! Your leftover pickle juice can actually be a great marinade for the salmon. Just whisk the juice with a splash of olive oil, a dash of soy sauce, and a pinch of black pepper. Marinate the salmon for about 15–20 minutes (or overnight for deeper flavor), then grill or bake it. It adds a tangy, refreshing twist to your heart-healthy meal! 🥒🐟
