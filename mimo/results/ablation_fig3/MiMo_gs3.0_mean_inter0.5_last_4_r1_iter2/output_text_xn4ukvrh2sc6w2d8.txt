Here are the classic recipes with exact measurements for your Art Deco airport reception, formatted for your bartenders:

### Old Fashioned  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon or rye  
- 1 sugar cube (or ¾ oz simple syrup)  
- 3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube with 2 dashes of bitters in a shaker.  
2. Add bourbon, remaining bitters, ice, and stir until chilled.  
3. Strain into a chilled rocks glass over fresh ice.  
4. Express orange twist over the drink and drop it in.  

---

### Paper Plane  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- 1 oz Aperol  
- ½ oz Amaro Nonino  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.  

---

### Whiskey Sour  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- *Optional:* ½ oz egg white (for texture)  

**Instructions:**  
1. *With egg white:* Dry shake (no ice) all ingredients in a shaker until frothy. Add ice and shake again.  
2. *Without egg white:* Shake with ice.  
3. Strain into a chilled rocks glass over ice.  
4. Garnish with a cherry or orange slice.  

---

### Additional Gin Cocktail: **French 75** (Recommended Gin Option)  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 3 oz sparkling wine (e.g., Prosecco)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a chilled coup glass.  
3. Top with sparkling wine.  
4. Garnish with a lemon twist.  

---

### Fall-Themed Tips:  
- **Finger Foods:** Pair bourbon cocktails (Old Fashioned, Paper Plane) with spiced nuts, charcuterie, or bourbon-glazed pork sliders. For the Aviation and French 75, try cucumber bites, smoked salmon canapés, or shortbread cookies.  
- **Kentucky Twist:** Use local Kentucky bourbon (e.g., Buffalo Trace, Woodford Reserve) for the Paper Plane and Aviation.  
- **Paper Planes:** Rim the coupe glass with Aperol or Nonino for a thematic touch.
