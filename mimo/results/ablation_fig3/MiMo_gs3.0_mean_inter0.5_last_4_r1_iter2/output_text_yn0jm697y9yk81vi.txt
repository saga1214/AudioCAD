Here's a consolidated list of my best recommendations based on our conversation, along with where to purchase them locally in Indianapolis:

### Cutting Sheet Metal  
1. **Thinner Sheets Curves**: Aviation Snips  
2. **Thick Metal Power/Linear Cuts**: Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel  
3. *(Curves Alternative)* Jigsaw with Metal Blade  

**Where to Buy in Indianapolis**:  
- **Best Selection**: **Menards** (dominant in Indiana, carries all tool categories)  
- **Also Check**: Home Depot, Lowe's, Tractor Supply Co., Ace Hardware  

---

### Drywall Essentials  
4. **Fasteners (Best Option)**: 1-1/4" Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws  
   - *Why*: Prevents nail pops, self-drilling, and holds securely in studs.  
5. *(If Using Nails)*: Ring-Shank Nails  
   - *Why*: Gripping shanks anchor better in wood than smooth nails.  
6. **Insulation (Walls)**: Fiberglass Batts  
   - *Why*: Cost-effective, easy to install in standard 2x4/2x6 framing.  
7. **Insulation (Soundproofing)**: Mineral Wool Rolls  
   - *Why*: Blocks sound, fire-resistant, no dust.  
8. **Insulation (Airtight Seal)**: Spray Foam  
   - *Why*: Fills gaps perfectly; best for energy efficiency.  

**Where to Buy**:  
- All items (tools, screws, insulation) at **Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards**.  
- Jigsaw blades/mineral wool: Check **Ace Hardware** or **Tractor Supply Co.**
