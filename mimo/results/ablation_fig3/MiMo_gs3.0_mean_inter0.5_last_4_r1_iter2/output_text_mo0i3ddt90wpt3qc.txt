You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which are gluten-free options, so that's a great start.

Other gluten-free snack ideas you could consider are trail mix, but just be sure to check the labels for gluten-free nuts and seeds. Rice cakes are another simple option. Popcorn is also naturally gluten-free, as long as it isn't seasoned with anything with gluten.

And since camping snacks can get a little messy, it's a good idea to pack some individual packs of nut butter to go along with your fruit.
