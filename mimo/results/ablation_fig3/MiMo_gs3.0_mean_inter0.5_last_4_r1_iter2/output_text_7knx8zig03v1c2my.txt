### 🔍 **Clicker Training Explained**  
Clicker training uses a "three-step" process:  
1. **Clicker as a marker**: When your puppy does something right (e.g., sits), click the clicker at the exact moment.  
2. **Reward**: Immediately give a treat. The clicker says, "That was perfect!"  
3. **Repetition**: Practice short sessions (5-10 mins) daily. Over time, the puppy learns to do the behavior to hear the click/treat.  
- **Why it works**: Clicker = instant reward signal, making training faster and clearer.  

### 🐾 **Training Tools & Techniques**  
- **Essential tools**:  
  - **Leash/harness**: For walks and recall practice.  
  - **Treat pouch**: Portable treats for training anywhere.  
  Papillon Temperament: They’re energetic, loyal, curious, and love people. They’re usually good with kids but need early socialization.  
- **Teaching "sit"**:  
  1. Hold a treat near puppy’s nose.  
  2. Slowly move it upward/backward over his head (he’ll naturally sit).  
  3. Say "sit" as he sits → click → reward.  
  4. Repeat until he sits on cue.  

### 🛡️ **Escape-Proofing & Recall Training**  
- **Preventing escapes**:  
  - Use baby gates.  
  - Keep Galileo on a short leash in safe areas (e.g., balcony).  
  - Supervise yard time closely.  
- **Recall training**:  
  1. Start indoors: Call "come!" → reward with a high-value treat.  
  2. Move to a quieter outdoor spot: Repeat, gradually increasing distance.  
  3. Use a long line for practice.  
- **If he escapes**: Stay calm! Call his name excitedly ("Galileo! Come!"). Reward generously when he returns. Never punish—this builds trust.
