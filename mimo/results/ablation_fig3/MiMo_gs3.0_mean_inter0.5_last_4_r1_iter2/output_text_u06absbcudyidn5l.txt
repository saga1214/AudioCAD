Okay, let's break this down. Chronic erythema means long-term redness. Besides rosacea and sun damage you mentioned earlier, it could be eczema, contact dermatitis from irritants, lupus, or even certain medications. Genetics is often a root cause.

Now, for vasculitis, think of it like this: your blood vessels are inflamed. The size of the vessel that's inflamed determines the symptoms. Inflammation of large vessels can cause big issues like strokes, while inflammation of small vessels is what usually causes the skin rashes and sores you see. The cause is typically an autoimmune problem where your immune system mistakenly attacks your own blood vessels. So, a proper diagnosis is really important.
