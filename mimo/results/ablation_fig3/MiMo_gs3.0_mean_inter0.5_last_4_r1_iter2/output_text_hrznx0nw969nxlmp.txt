Secret Harbour Resort puts you right in Reef Bay, which is a fantastic base for your week. Being on the beach there, you're just steps from the calm, protected waters perfect for snorkeling right off the shore, especially along the break wall. 

If you want a guided adventure, Reef Bay Boat Charters offers shallow-water excursions to nearby reefs where you can spot tropical fish and maybe even the occasional sea turtle. Since you're a birder, these trips would be great for spotting reef birds like the Brown Noddy. 

For land-based activities, Reef Bay Beach has beautiful white sand and is great for a simple beach day. From there, you can take a short walk to Cinnamon Bay, which has lovely, easy trails through the forest. Moriches Bay Trail is another option nearby that's particularly good for birdwatching - you might spot the Antillean Crested Hummingbird or the common Bananaquit. 

You can also rent snorkel gear right at Secret Harbour if you want to explore the bay more extensively. And while you're there, you're close to everything in Cruz Bay, so you can easily combine beach time with exploring the shops and dining out. Enjoy your trip!
