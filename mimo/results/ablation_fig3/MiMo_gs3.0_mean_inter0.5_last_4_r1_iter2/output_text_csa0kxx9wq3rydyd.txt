Okay, let's break down the appliances and methods you'll be using. For smoking, you'll need a smoker. For roasting, an oven is a must. For baking, you have the prime rib roast going in the oven and the no-bake pumpkin cheesecake and fruit tart going in your double ovens. For roasting again, the slow cooker apple crisp will use one oven, and you'll roast the asparagus in the toaster oven. For stovetop cooking, you'll need a pot for the Instant Pot cranberry sauce and a pan for the macaroni and cheese if you're boiling the noodles. For your side dishes, you'll need a large pot for the Brussels sprout salad dressing, and a baking sheet and pan for the green beans and Brussels sprouts. You'll also need mixing bowls for the wild rice salad and macaroni and cheese, and a slow cooker for the bread stuffing. So, to summarize the methods: smoking, roasting, baking, boiling, stovetopping, and slow cooking. You'll need an oven, a toaster oven, an Instant Pot, and a double oven would really help.
