That's awesome—congrats on finishing the coffee table! Here are some vending machine-friendly snack ideas that align with your preferences (non-sweet):  

### Savory & Crunchy:  
- **Potato Chips** (plain, BBQ, or sour cream & onion)  
- **Pretzels** (mini, twists, or rods)  
- **Beef Jerky** (if available)  
- **Rice Cakes** (top with avocado or salsa if you have toppings nearby)  
- **Popcorn** (plain or lightly salted)  

### Cheesy & Tangy:  
- **Cheese Puffs** or **Cheddar Bites**  
- **Veggie Straws** (if you like a thicker, starchy crunch)  

### Biscuits & Breads:  
- **Saltine Crackers** or **Whole-Grain Crackers**  
- **Mini Muffins** (if savory—like cheese or veggie-flavored)  

### Sweet-ish (but not chocolatey):  
- **Gum** (peppermint or mint—zero sugar!)  
- **Dried Fruit** (like raisins or apricots—minimal sweetness)  
- **Dark Chocolate-Covered Almonds** (if you tolerate a *hint* of chocolate, go for 70%+ cacao—it’s bitter, not sweet)  

### Project-Boosters:  
- **Energy Balls** (if vending has them—look for peanut butter/oat-based, no candy).  
- **Nut Packs** (almonds, cashews—great for DIY snacks later).  

**Pro Tip:** Place the manuals/tools on a shelf for easy access next time! And maybe add a small organizer for screws/bolts (like your M7/M5/M3 bolts!). Enjoy your new setup—it’s the perfect spot for snacks while you work on future projects. 🎉
