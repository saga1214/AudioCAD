Okay, you've got all the essentials! Just a few more things to make it a smooth day.

A drill is going to be your best friend here. It's way faster and easier than using a hammer and nails for this kind of project.

Make sure you have a stud finder that can actually find the nails, not just the studs. Habitat cabinets from reclaimed homes can sometimes have leftover nails behind them.

You'll definitely want some shims. These are just thin pieces of wood, and you'll use them under the cabinets to get everything perfectly level.

Safety goggles are a good idea, just in case a screw or nail happens to slip.

And finally, a little patience with each other. This is a big project, so just take it one step at a time and celebrate the small victories along the way. You're saving so much money, so enjoy the process!
