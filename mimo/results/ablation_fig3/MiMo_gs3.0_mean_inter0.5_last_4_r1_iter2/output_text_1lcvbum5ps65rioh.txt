### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia
1.  **Visibility:** You must be able to see **at least 150 meters** in both directions before turning.  
2.  **Prohibited Locations:** Never make a U-turn at:  
    *   Intersections with traffic signals (stop signs, yield signs, traffic lights).  
    *   Near curves, hills, or tunnels where visibility is limited.  
    *   Where the center line is painted on the road.  
    *   Near driveways, bus stops, or pedestrians.  
    *   Where a "No U-Turn" sign is posted.  
3.  **Penalties:**  
    *   **Mistake:** If you accidentally make an unsafe U-turn, you'll likely receive a ticket.  
    *   **Cost:** As of late 2023, the penalty for a "failure to yield" (which includes unsafe U-turns) is **$175.00 plus a 1% surcharge**.  
    *   **Safety:** The main goal is to avoid accidents. If you're unsure, wait for a safe opportunity or find a designated U-turn lane.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients (for 2-3 people)
Here's a classic list – no cream or garlic needed!

*   **Pasta:** 400g (14 oz) Spaghetti (or other long pasta like Rigatoni).
*   **Eggs:** 3 Large Eggs + 1 Egg Yolk (for richness).
*   **Cheese:** 100g Pecorino Romano (finely grated) + 50g Parmigiano Reggiano (finely grated).
*   **Meat:** 150-200g Guanciale (best) or Pancetta (diced into small strips).
*   **Aromatics:** 2-3 cloves Garlic (optional, but common).
*   **Seasoning:** Freshly ground Black Pepper (to taste).
*   **Salt:** For pasta water.
*   **Optional:** Red pepper flakes (for heat).

**Key Technique:** Cook pasta in salted water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining. Whisk eggs, yolks, cheeses, and pepper together. Sauté guanciale/pancetta until crispy. Combine hot pasta with the egg mixture off direct heat, tossing vigorously with residual heat and a splash of pasta water until creamy. **Do not boil the eggs!**

Enjoy your carbonara!
