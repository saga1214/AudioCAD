Initally, a written email is often a good way to start. You could write a concise email to your boss, requesting a brief, in-person meeting at a time that works for them. You could say something like, 'When you have a moment, could we schedule a short meeting to discuss my growth and career path?'

As an alternative, you could try scheduling a face-to-face meeting under a different topic. For instance, you could book a meeting for a project update and use that as a way to bring up the more personal conversation.

Before the meeting, it's very helpful to prepare a few key points. Think about your accomplishments, the value you bring to the team, and what you hope to achieve from the conversation. Practicing what you want to say beforehand can also really help ease the anxiety.
