For Madrid, tapas are a top recommendation. Think things like patatas bravas—that's fried potatoes with this spicy tomato sauce—or jamón ibérico, which is their special cured ham.

Seafood paella is also a classic dish from Spain. And for dessert, churros are a must-try... they're these fried dough pastries, usually dipped in chocolate.

And hey, if your layover is in Philadelphia, you *should* try their famous cheesesteak.
