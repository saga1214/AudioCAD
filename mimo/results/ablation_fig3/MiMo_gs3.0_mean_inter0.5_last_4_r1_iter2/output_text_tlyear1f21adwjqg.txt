Okay, efficiency can be measured in different ways. If we're talking about cost-effectiveness and scalability, solar leads again. But if reliability and consistency are the main priorities, hydro is often the winner. Wind is a strong contender too, especially in windy areas. So it really depends on how you define "single most efficient." If we have to pick just one, solar is a good choice for overall availability and dropping costs, but the best answer really depends on the specific conditions of the location.
