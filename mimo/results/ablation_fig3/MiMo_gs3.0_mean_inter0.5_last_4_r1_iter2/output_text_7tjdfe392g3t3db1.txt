To tune your stringed instrument properly, here's a step-by-step guide using either a **tuner app** or a **physical tuner**:

### 1. **Tools Needed:**
   - **Tuner App:** (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune, or built-in phone apps like Voice Recorder).  
   - **Physical Tuner:** Clip-on or headstock tuner (e.g., Snark, Korg).  
   - **Reference:** Standard tuning for most guitars/basses is **E-A-D-G-B-E** (from thickest to thinnest string).

### 2. **Using a Tuner App:**
   - **Open the app** and point your phone’s microphone at the instrument.  
   - **Play each string individually** (from lowest to highest pitch).  
   - **Follow the tuner’s visual/audio cues:**  
     - If the app shows a green "OK" or a stable note, the string is in tune.  
     - If it’s red or unstable, **slide your finger down the fretboard** (to lower the pitch) or **up** (to raise it).  
     - **Fine-tune** until the tuner stabilizes.  
   - **Repeat** for all strings.  

### 3. **Using a Physical Clip-On Tuner:**
   - **Attach the tuner** to your instrument’s headstock.  
   - **Play a string** and watch the tuner’s display.  
   - **Slide your finger** along the fretboard to adjust pitch:  
     - **Slide down** to lower pitch.  
     - **Slide up** to raise pitch.  
   - **Tune each string** until the tuner shows a stable green light.  

### 4. **Key Tips:**
   - **Turn off amplifiers/amps** during tuning to avoid feedback.  
   - **Tune from the lowest string first** (e.g., E on a guitar).  
   - **Tension matters:** If strings are loose, gently pull them upward (but avoid overstretching).  
   - **Check for consistency:** Use the tuner to verify all strings are in tune.  

### 5. **Troubleshooting:**
   - **If a string won’t tune?** Check for:  
     - Old/damaged strings (replace if needed).  
     - Incorrect string gauge or tension.  
     - Worn frets or tuning pegs (professional setup may be needed).  
   - **Use a reference pitch:** Apps/tuners often display the standard note (e.g., "E4" for the low E string).  

### 6. **When Tuned Properly:**
   - Your instrument should sound **clear, resonant, and harmonically balanced**.  
   - Avoid forcing strings—gentle adjustments work best.  

Tuning takes practice! Start with the lowest string, move up, and double-check. Happy playing! 🎸
