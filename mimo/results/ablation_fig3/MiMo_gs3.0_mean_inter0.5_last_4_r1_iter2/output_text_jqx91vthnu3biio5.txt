### 🌈 **Color-Coding Icons: Memory vs. Mental Load**  
Color-coding primarily **reduces mental load** by creating visual shortcuts. For example:  
- **Blue** for "work" folders, **green** for "personal" projects, **yellow** for "urgent."  
- Your brain doesn’t need to reread labels—it instantly recognizes categories.  
- *Keep it simple*: Use 2–3 colors max to avoid visual clutter. Test if it speeds up your workflow!  

### 🎧 **Playlist Switching: Focus or Distraction?**  
It’s **mostly personal preference**, but timing can help:
