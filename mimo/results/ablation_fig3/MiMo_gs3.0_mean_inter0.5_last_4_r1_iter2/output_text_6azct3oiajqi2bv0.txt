Based on your situation and the Segometown climate needing durable yet affordable winter clothing (like from Nordstrom Rack), here are safe ways to attach fabric without damaging the lining:

### Best Methods (Avoid Glue Guns!)
1. **Double-Sided Tape (Best for most cases):**  
   - Apply tape to the *back* of the fabric piece you want to attach.  
   - Press it firmly onto the jacket lining.  
   - **Pro Tip:** Use thin, low-adhesive "washable" tape (e.g., Scotch Magic) to avoid residue. Test on a hidden seam first.

2. **Sewing with a Needle & Thread (Most Secure):**  
   - Use a **walking foot** (available at craft stores) to feed layers evenly.  
   - Hand-sew with **nylon thread** (strong and invisible) using a **backstitch** or **whipstitch**.  
   - **Avoid:** Visible stitches if you want a clean look.

3. **Temporary Fix (If you need to ship soon):**  
   - Use **fabric-safe pins** (like sewing pins) to hold fabric in place while waiting for shipping.  
   - Remove pins before shipping.

### What to Avoid
- **Glue Guns:** Heat can melt synthetic linings (polyester, nylon).  
- **Double-Sided Fabric Glue:** Can weaken fibers over time.  
- **Iron-On Adhesive:** Causes scorching and tears.  

### Pro Tips
- **Test First:** Always test your method on an inside seam or scrap fabric.  
- **Gentle Handling:** Fold fabric neatly to prevent bulk.  
- **Shipping:** If ordering online, mark the jacket as "delicate" to prevent rough handling.
