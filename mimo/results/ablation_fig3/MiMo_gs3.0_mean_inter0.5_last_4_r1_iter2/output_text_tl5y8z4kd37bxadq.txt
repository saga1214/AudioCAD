Here's a step-by-step travel checklist organization, beekeeping prep, and Greek Austin dinner ideas:

### Step 1: Beekeeping Before Departure
- **Inspect hives** now: Check for mites (treat if needed), food stores, and queen activity.  
- **Prepare instructions** for your friend: Hive feeding supplies, tool list, weather alerts, and emergency contacts.  
- **Supply hives**: Add extra sugar water feed if needed, secure entrances against wind/rain.  

### Step 2: Travel Essentials Checklist
| **Category**       | **Items**                                                                 |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Travel Documents  | Passport/ID, tickets, insurance, emergency cash                            |
| Essentials        | Phone charger, backup power, medications, snacks                           |
| Friend Instructions | Detailed notes, photos of hives, local beekeeper contacts                 |
| Beekeeping Supplies | Notebooks, pencil, spare hive tools (e.g., smoker, hive tool)             |
| Packing            | Soft clothing to cushion honey jars (see step 3!)                          |

### Step 3: Honey Jar Packing (Step-by-Step)
1. **Wrap jars** tightly in bubble wrap or rolled-up towels.  
2. **Place in suitcase** centered between soft items (e.g., clothes).  
3. **Add padding**: Stuff gaps with socks or packing peanuts.  
4. **Secure lids** with tape or rubber bands to prevent leaks.  
5. **Avoid overpacking**—leave space around jars.  

### Step 4: Greek Food Spots in Austin (Casual & Delicious)  
For a relaxed first night, try:  
- **Lotto**: Trendy spot with Greek small plates (try the lamb saganaki).  
- **Philadelfia**: Cozy taverna near downtown (house-made tzatziki is legendary!).  
- **Avon**: Rustic vibe, great grilled octopus and souvlaki.  
- **Graziola**: Hole-in-the-wall gem for olive oil cake and spanakopita.  

**Tip**: Check hours online—many close early!
