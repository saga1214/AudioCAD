### 1. **Fin Choice Recommendation**  
Start with **short fins** for your first few Costa Rican dives. They’re compact (easier for travel), maneuverable in reefs/caves, and less demanding on leg strength—ideal for beginners or varied conditions. Costa Rica’s waters are often calm, matching your Belize/Honduras experiences.

### 2. **Top Diving Destinations in Costa Rica**  
Costa Rica has **three major hotspots** for scuba diving:  
- **Cabo Blanco (Puntarenas)**:  
  - **Highlights**: Coral reefs, sea turtles, rays, and occasional whales/dolphins.  
  - **Why you’ll love it**: Calm, clear tropical waters—perfect for beginners.  
  - **Best for**: Easy shore dives, snorkeling, and gentle currents.  

- **Tortuguero (North Costa Rica)**:  
  - **Highlights**: Mangrove forests, nurse sharks, sea turtles (nesting season: June–November), and sponges.  
  - **Why you’ll love it**: Unique ecosystem; accessible by boat or river.  
  - **Best for**: Eco-diving, turtle encounters, and freshwater cave diving.  

- **Cenotes (Near Tulum, Mexico)**:  
  - **Highlights**: Crystal-clear sinkholes with stalactites, freshwater, and marine life.  
  - **Why you’ll love it**: "Bonus" tropical experience if you explore nearby Mexico.  
  - **Best for**: Unique adventure (though not in Costa Rica—consider adding it!).  

### 3. **Advanced Option: Long Fin Advantage**  
If Costa Rica’s strong currents arise (e.g., during rainy season), upgrade to **long fins** for:  
- Better propulsion in currents.  
- Efficiency for longer dives (e.g., exploring Cenotes’ deeper sections).  
- Ideal for sites like **Socorro Island, Mexico** (if you extend your trip).  

### 4. **Trip Timeline Suggestion**  
- **Days 1–3**: **Cabo Blanco** (shallow reefs, turtles, rays).  
- **Days 4–6**: **Tortuguero** (mangroves, turtles, freshwater caves).  
- **Day 7+**: **Cenotes** (if visiting Mexico) or **Manzanillo** (near Tenerife, Galápagos for advanced divers).  

### 5. **Pro Tips**  
- **Best Time**: Dry season (Dec–April) for calm, sunny conditions.  
- **Resorts**: Choose English-friendly lodges (e.g., **Cabo Blanco Dive Resort** or **Tortuguero Dive Lodge**).  
- **Local Guides**: Essential for safety and spotting wildlife (e.g., whales in winter!).  

### 6. **If You Crave More Biodiversity**  
Extend to **Tenerife (Canary Islands)** or **Galápagos** later—they offer volcanic landscapes, marine iguanas, and unique species.
