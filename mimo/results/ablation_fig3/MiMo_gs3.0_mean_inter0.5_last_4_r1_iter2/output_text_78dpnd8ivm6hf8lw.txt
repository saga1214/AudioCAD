Here's a quick recap of your blood system analogy for a child:

*   **Heart:** The gas station AND the engine. It pumps the blood-cars all around your body.
*   **Lungs:** Another gas station stop. Blood-cars pick up fresh oxygen "fuel" here.
*   **Arteries:** The big highways. Carry blood AWAY from the heart to deliver fuel.
*   **Capillaries:** The tiny little side streets. Where blood-cars STOP to drop off oxygen fuel to your muscles/organ "houses".
*   **Veins:** The return roads. Carry the blood-cars back to the heart/lungs to refuel.
*   **Kidneys:** A big car wash and trash station. Blood-cars stop here to get cleaned by filtering out waste and extra water (which becomes pee).
*   **Liver:** A special service station. Sorts and processes the fuel (nutrients) and waste from the blood-cars to keep everything balanced.
