Here's a quick summary of both analogies for how yogurt is made:

1. **Throwing a Party Analogy**  
   - **Milk**: A big, empty room.  
   - **Good bacteria**: Special guests invited to the party. Keep the room warm to energize them.  
   - **Group Hug**: The hug transforms everyone linked together, making the milk thick and tangy = yogurt.  

2. **Net Formation Analogy**  
   - **Milk**: A disorganized crowd.  
   - **Good bacteria**: Tiny team coaches eating sugar (party food). Warmth energizes them.  
   - **Net**: The net is formed from milk proteins (like team uniforms). It traps liquid, turning runny milk into thick yogurt.  
   - **Flavoring**: Like team uniforms—adds identity (e.g., strawberry) without changing the net structure.  

**Technical Connection**:  
In both analogies, good bacteria cause milk proteins to bond together, trapping water and creating a thick, solid structure. Flavoring is an added characteristic (like uniforms or group identities) but doesn’t disrupt the core structural change.
