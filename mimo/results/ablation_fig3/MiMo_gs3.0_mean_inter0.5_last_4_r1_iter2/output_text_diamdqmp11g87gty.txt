Of course! Snowshoeing is a fantastic way to explore the outdoors. You'll need snowshoes, of course, and good, waterproof boots with plenty of traction. A warm, insulated jacket, gloves, and a hat are essential to stay warm. You might also want to bring a backpack with snacks and water. Beyond ice fishing and snowshoeing, you could also try cross-country skiing, which is similar but uses skis instead of snowshoes. If you're near a ski hill, downhill skiing is an option. Building a snow fort or having a snowball fight can be fun for families. And don't forget about winter hiking, which is like regular hiking but on snow-covered trails. There's a lot to do to stay active and enjoy winter!
