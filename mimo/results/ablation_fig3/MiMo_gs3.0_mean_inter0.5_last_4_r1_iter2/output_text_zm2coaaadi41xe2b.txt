Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! For ramen, I’d say check out Tonkatsu Tomo or Ichiran. For pho, Pho 79 is amazing. If you want dumplings, try Dim Sum Palace. As for protein bars, Quest is great if you want low sugar and carbs, Optimum Nutrition has solid protein, and Clif Bars are good for a more natural option. If you want something healthier, RXBAR is decent too. Let me know if you want more details on any of those!
