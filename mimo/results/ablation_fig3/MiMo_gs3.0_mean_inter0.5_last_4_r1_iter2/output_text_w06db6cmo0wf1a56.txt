Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's ten calzones for the bachelor! I'll draft a shopping list that prioritizes grab-and-go, minimal-mess options with healthy bases but includes the sugar-free sodas and desserts you wanted. I'll organize it into drinks, snacks, meals, and desserts, plus some helpful extras.

First, for drinks, water bottles are non-negotiable. Pair those with low-sugar juice boxes for kids. For the adults, sugar-free sodas and sparkling water will cover the carbonated requests. Electrolyte drinks could be a good backup for the hot day. And milk boxes are always a kid favorite.

Next, snacks should be zero-mess. Cheese sticks, grapes, pretzels, trail mix, baby carrots with single hummus cups, hard-boiled eggs, and turkey roll-ups are perfect. Fruit like apple slices is great, but you might want to pack them with a damp towel to keep them fresh. Granola bars are also a solid, mess-free option.

For the main meal, since calzones were the goal, pizza is your answer! Ordering ten mini calzones from a pizzeria is ideal. They're individual, ready-to-eat, and easy to pack cold in the coolers. You could also grab some plain pitas for dipping in healthier options like hummus later if you want variety.

And for dessert, cupcakes are the way to go. They're portable, no-fuss, and feel special for a bachelor party. If he loves pie, maybe grab some pre-sliced key lime or apple pie from a bakery to share.

I'll also add a few smart extras like napkins, wet wipes, and trash bags to keep the mess down, along with sunscreen and bug spray. This list should make packing coolers a breeze and let you all enjoy the park with plenty of energy. Have an absolutely amazing time!
