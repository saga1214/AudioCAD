Okay, setting off smoke alarms with wood spoons is legendary. Stir-fry won't set off smoke alarms, I promise!

Here's how to do it safely. First and most importantly, use only metal cutlery, like a plastic spoon or chopsticks. No wood.

For the noodles, just make sure you have enough water in the pot to cover them by at least an inch. Stir them occasionally while they cook. Then drain them well and add them to your stir-fry at the very end.

The stir-fry itself is fast, so have all your ingredients chopped and ready to go before you start cooking. You'll be an expert in no time
