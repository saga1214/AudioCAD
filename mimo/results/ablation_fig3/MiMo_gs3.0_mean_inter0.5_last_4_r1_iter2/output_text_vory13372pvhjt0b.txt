### Why this is the core innovation:
1. **Decentralization Enforces Scarcity**  
   The blockchain’s transparency and immutability ensure no single entity can alter Bitcoin’s supply. This makes its deflationary nature **inherently unchangeable**.

2. **Fixed Supply as a Philosophical Breakthrough**  
   Bitcoin’s 21 million cap is not just a technical limit—it’s a **rejection of inflationary money**. Created as a response to the 2008 financial crisis, it embodies a system where value preservation isn’t dependent on trust in institutions, but on mathematical certainty.
