### ☕ Coffee Prep (Instant Method)
1. **Make a strong coffee shot**: Dissolve **2-3 tablespoons of instant coffee** in **1 cup of boiling water**. This creates a concentrated base that won’t clump in your latte.  
2. **Add milk**: Stir in your warmed **semi-skim milk** (or Cornish milk if splurging!). For extra smoothness, heat the milk first in the microwave (1-2 minutes on medium power) until steaming but not boiling.

### 🌧️ Weather Prep

- **Umbrella backup**: If rain is heavy and gusty, bring a compact, wind-resistant one (like a 3-layer umbrella) and secure it tightly.  
- **Avoid**: Umbrellas in high winds can flip inside out or get caught in branches.
