Alright, you're on Briarcliff Northeast now. Einstein Bros is usually near North Decatur Road, so from Briarcliff, just continue straight ahead. You'll see Decatur Road ahead, and you'll need to turn right onto it. Their Ponce de Leon location should be right around the corner on Decatur. Once you're there, definitely go for a substantial order. I'd recommend a large bagel sandwich. The turkey and avocado on an everything bagel is great, or you could go for the bacon, egg, and cheese for that hearty breakfast feel. Either way, it should be enough to keep you full for lunch and even dinner. Enjoy your meal!
