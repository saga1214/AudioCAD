You're right, it's a complex question. After a more thorough look, Ancient Egypt still seems to be the best choice, but for very specific reasons.

First, its artifacts are just so iconic and visually stunning. The pyramids, sarcophagi, and mummies are instantly recognizable and create a powerful, immersive experience that's hard to beat. Second, there's a huge amount of material available, which allows for a much richer exhibit. Third, the stories of the pharaohs are already famous, which makes it easy for the exhibit to connect with a broad audience.

While Mesopotamia's story is incredibly important, it has some serious hurdles. Getting top-tier artifacts is difficult, and the subject matter is less familiar, which could limit its appeal. So, for a museum aiming for maximum impact and audience engagement, Ancient Egypt remains the stronger choice.
