Vasculitis, on the other hand, is an autoimmune condition where your immune system attacks your blood vessels. It can affect vessels of any size—large, medium, or small. Symptoms really depend on the type of vasculitis and where the vessels are affected, but they can include fever, fatigue, aches, and rashes. A doctor would diagnose it using a physical exam, blood tests, and imaging, with a biopsy being the gold standard for confirmation.
