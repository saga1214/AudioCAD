Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scenes and watercolor inspiration. The best time for you would be from late September to early November. That’s their autumn season, which means super vibrant foliage, especially in the highlands and around Lake Pátzcuaro. You’ll get all those warm oranges, reds, and yellows you love. 

For watercolor practice, you could head to coastal areas like Tulum or Playa del Carmen for those ocean scenes we talked about. Or, you could go to the Yucatán Peninsula for some really unique, colorful landscapes. The Oaxaca Valley is another great spot, especially for autumn foliage. 

And since you like space scenes, Mexico City has some great observatories and clear skies. You could even try painting the city lights against a starry sky with watercolors. 

So yeah, late September to early November is the sweet spot. You’ll get the best light, the most color, and plenty of inspiration for both your autumn scenes and those space paintings. Just pack your watercolors and your sketchbook!
