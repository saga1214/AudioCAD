Okay, let's see where we left off. You've removed the hardware and have lightly sanded the cabinets. Before you start priming, there are a few final prep steps.

Make sure to do a final wipe-down with a tack cloth to remove all the dust from the sanding. Then, I'd recommend applying painter's tape to the edges of the cabinets, like where they meet the countertop. This will create a clean line when you paint.

Also, make sure your workspace is well-ventilated. Painting is a dusty process, so you'll want to open some windows or turn on a fan to clear the air. And of course, cover the floor with drop cloths. 

You'll want to do one coat of primer for the black cabinets, but it's always a good idea to have a second coat ready just in case. Once you're all set, you can start painting!
