Now, for teaching her to sit: Start by holding a treat close to her nose to get her attention. Then, slowly raise the treat high over her head, which naturally encourages her to sit. The moment she's in the sitting position, say the command "sit" and immediately reward her with the treat. Repeat this several times a day until she links the word to the action. Corgis are smart, so she'll learn fast with consistency and praise. If she's struggling with potty training, remember to take her to her designated spot frequently after meals and naps. Reward her with praise and treats when she goes. Keep a regular feeding schedule to help you predict her bathroom times. If accidents happen, just clean them thoroughly without punishing her. Over time, she'll learn where to go. If she barks at the cat Felix, try teaching a "quiet" command. When she starts barking, get her attention with a treat and say "quiet" when she pauses.
