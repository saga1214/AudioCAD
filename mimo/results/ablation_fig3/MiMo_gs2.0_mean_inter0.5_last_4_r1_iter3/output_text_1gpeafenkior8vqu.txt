### **Tokyo (4 Days)**  
**Day 1: Arrival & Modern Tokyo**  
- **Morning:** Arrive in Tokyo (check out by 10 AM).  
- **Workout:** 7–8 AM (hotel gym or nearby park).  
- **Sightseeing:** Asakusa (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Street).  
- **Evening:** Shibuya (Hachiko Statue, Shibuya Crossing, dinner at ramen spot).  
- **Slideshow Research:** 9–10 PM (images of calligraphy or ukiyo-e).  

**Day 2: Art & Culture**  
- **Workout:** 7–8 AM.  
- **Morning:** Ueno Park (Museums: Tokyo National Museum, Art Museum).  
- **Afternoon:** Akihabara (pop culture, anime shops).  
- **Evening:** Shinjuku (Omoide Yokocho, dinner).  
- **Slideshow Design:** Color scheme testing (monochromatic vs. vibrant).  

**Day 3: Nature & Landmarks**  
- **Workout:** 7–8 AM.  
- **Morning:** Yoyogi Park (Hive Museum or Meiji Shrine).  
- **Afternoon:** Shibuya Sky (panoramic views for slide layout inspiration).  
- **Evening:** Harajuku (Takeshita Street, dinner).  

**Day 4: Departure Prep** School Prep  
- **Workout:** 7–8 AM.  
- **Morning:** Senso-ji again (quick photos) or shopping.  
- **Afternoon:** Airport transfer (check out by 1 PM).

### **Osaka (3 Days + Kyoto/Nara Day Trip)**  
**Day 5: Osaka City**  
- **Morning:** Namba (Dotonbori neon signs, street food).  
- **Afternoon:** Osaka Castle (free entry Wednesdays).  
- **Evening:** Umeda Sky Building (slide layout inspiration).  
- **Slideshow Research:** Kimono patterns or kimono printers.  

**Day 6: Nara & Kyoto**  
- **Morning:** Shinkansen to Nara (45 min). See Todai-ji Temple (giant Buddha), Nara Park.  
- **Afternoon:** Shinkansen to Kyoto (30 min). Fushimi Inari Shrine (early morning).  
- **Evening:** Return to Osaka.  
- **Slideshow Research:** Hokusai’s *The Great Wave* or Hiroshige’s landscapes.  

**Day 7: Osaka & Coursework**  
- **Workout:** 7–8 AM.  
- **Morning:** Universal Studios Japan (optional).  
- **Afternoon:** Shinkansen to Kyoto (30 min). Gion District (geisha spotting).  
- **Evening:** Return to Osaka.  
- **Slideshow Design:** Font pairing (serif for text, sans-serif for images).

### **Key Logistics**  
- **Checkouts:** Standard is **10 AM** (confirm with hotels). Taxis cost ~¥500–¥1,500 per ride (expensive; use buses/trains).  
- **Transport:**  
  - **Tokyo:** Suica/Pasmo card for trains/buses.  
  - **Osaka-Kyoto-Nara:** Shinkansen (book online for 13,000 yen round-trip).  
- **Workout Flexibility:** Stick to 7–8 AM slots. Use hotel gyms or nearby parks.  
- **Slideshow Prep:** Dedicate evenings to gathering visuals (museum sites, Google Arts & Culture).  

### **Tips**  
- **Kyoto:** Stay in Gion or Pontocho for evening dining.  
- **Nara:** Visit early to avoid crowds.  
- **Coursework:** Focus on 1–2 deep dives per day (e.g., Day 2: ukiyo-e artists + slide layouts).
