You've got all the essentials, but there are a few more things you might find helpful. I'd really recommend getting a stud finder, especially since Habitat cabinets usually need to be screwed into studs. It's also a great idea to have a stud finder that can show where the wood framing is, in case you don't want to hit a stud on every single screw. And don't forget safety! Make sure you have a pair of safety glasses. You can also get a small utility knife for trimming any excess material. Oh, and a measuring tape is always good to have for double-checking things. You've got this, just take your time!
