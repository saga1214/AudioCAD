That's a wonderful idea! Combining your love for oceans and seafood with romance makes for the perfect getaway. Here are some creative and romantic water-based activities for your real-life couple's coastal weekend, keeping seasickness in mind:

1.  **Kayak or Stand-Up Paddleboard (SUP) Adventure:** Instead of a large, potentially rocking boat, opt for stability. Explore calm bays, search for sea turtles (if in season), or paddle towards secluded little coves. It's intimate, active, and you can enjoy fresh-caught seafood right from your kayak (if you find a spot to cook or grill). Look for guided tours on stable double kayaks or SUPs.
2.  **Private Sunset Sail:** Book a small, stable sailboat (like a catamaran or a well-maintained small sloop) for just the two of you. Many charters offer sunset sails. The calm light, beautiful colors, and intimate setting are incredibly romantic. The captain's expertise ensures a smooth ride.
3.  **Glass-Bottom Boat Tour (Calm Waters):** While not as adventurous, this offers a unique underwater perspective – perfect for spotting fish, rays, or other marine life. Choose a tour on a stable vessel operating in very calm coastal waters or a protected lagoon area.
4.  **Private Boat Picnic:** Charter a small, stable boat (a cabin cruiser or similar) for a half-day. Bring a gourmet picnic with your favorite seafood dishes (oysters, lobster, grilled fish, sushi). Anchor in a secluded spot with beautiful scenery – maybe a sandy beach, a rocky outcrop, or a quiet cove. It's pure romance and allows you to enjoy fresh food on the water.
5.  **Dolphin Watching Cruise (Stable Vessel):** Many coastal towns offer dolphin watching tours on boats specifically designed for calm waters and stability. Look for vessels with low decks or wide hulls. The thrill of spotting dolphins together is always magical.
6.  **Beachcombing & Shell Hunting from Kayak:** Paddle along the shore in calm conditions. Get up close to the waterline, explore tide pools (if safe), and collect unique shells and treasures. It's peaceful, exploratory, and incredibly romantic.
7.  **Morning Kayak to a Seafood Shack:** Rent kayaks and paddle to a charming, local seafood shack known for its fresh catch. Enjoy your breakfast or lunch right there on the water, watching the world go by. It's a simple, delicious, and memorable experience.

**Key Considerations for Seasickness:**

*   **Small & Stable Vessels:** Prioritize kayaks, SUPs, stable catamarans, or well-maintained small sailboats. Avoid large, open-boat charters in rougher conditions.
*   **Calm Water:** Book tours or activities specifically for calm conditions (early morning, late afternoon, protected bays, lagoons).
*   **Avoid Rough Waters:** Steer clear of open ocean crossings, strong currents, or known choppy areas.
*   **Inform the Operator:** Let the captain or tour company know about your partner's seasickness so they can ensure the vessel and conditions are suitable.
