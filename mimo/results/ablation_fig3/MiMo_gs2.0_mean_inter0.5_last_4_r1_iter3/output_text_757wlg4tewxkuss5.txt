Vegan friends generally don't eat regular beef hamburgers. Police officers might have a standard diet, but food pantry volunteers and software developers could be fine. To be safe, it's best to have a variety of options. You could get veggie burgers or a plant-based burger to include the vegan friend. For the others, frozen beef burgers should be fine as long as they are kept cold in a cooler. Just having some alternatives will make everyone happy.
