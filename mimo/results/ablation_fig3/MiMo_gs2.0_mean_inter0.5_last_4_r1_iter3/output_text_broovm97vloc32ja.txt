That's fantastic that you can also illustrate your own work! That's a huge asset. You are absolutely right to ask, because the process for a picture book is different. For that kind of complete package, you would approach publishers directly, not through literary agents.

You would do this by sending what's called a 'full manuscript and art portfolio'. This means you'd submit your final text, along with a collection of your strongest illustrations. Publishers love it when the author can also do the art, as it solves a major problem for them.

However, you should know that many traditional publishers are now also looking for artists to illustrate existing manuscripts that have been written by other authors. So, having your own illustrations is a great strength, but it doesn't necessarily mean they have to be used in your own story.
