   - **Size**: <20 lbs  
   - **Temperament**: Chill, playful, low-maintenance. Minimal barking. Great with kids/dogs.  
   - **Hybrid Option**: French Bulldog mixes (e.g., French Bulldog + Bulldog) often keep the vibe.  
   - **Shedding**: Low (good for high-rises!).

3. **Basset Hound**  
   - **Size**: 30–60 lbs (slightly over 20 lbs but often accepted)  
   - **Temperament**: Lazy, friendly, patient with kids. Rarely barks when alone.  
   - **Hybrid Option**: Basset Hound mixes (e.g., Basset + Bulldog).  
   - **Shedding**: Heavy (brush often).

4. **Pug**  
   - **Size**: <20 lbs  
   - **Temperament**: Goofy, affectionate, low-energy. Minimal barking.  
   - **Hybrid Option**: Pug mixes (e.g., Pug + Bulldog).  
   - **Shedding**: Moderate (brush weekly).

5. **Bichon Frise**  
   - **Size**: <12 lbs  
   - **Temperament**: Cheerful, trainable, hypoallergenic. Loves socialization.  
   - **Hybrid Option**: Bichon mixes (e.g., Bichon + Poodle).  
   - **Shedding**: Minimal (great for allergy concerns).

6. **Cavapoo (Cavalier + Poodle Mix)**  
   - **Size**: 15–25 lbs

### Key Considerations:
- **Training**: All these breeds are **highly trainable**—Frenchies/Basset Hounds may need patience with stubbornness.  
- **Noise When Alone**: Cavapoos, Frenchies, and Bichons are **quieter** when left alone.  
- **Kid/Dog Safety**: All are **kid/dog-friendly**—avoid breeds like Pit Bulls or Huskies if unsure.  
- **Chasing Squirrels**: Frenchies, Basset Hounds, and Pugs are **less likely** to chase.  
- **Shedding**: Bichons/Cavapoos have **hypoallergenic coats**; others shed moderately.  
- **Rescue Hybrids**: Look for **Cavapoo, Bichon mixes, or French Bulldog rescues**—many have been trained and socialized.  

### Next Steps to Locate Candidates:
1. **Shelter/Rescue Focus**:  
   - Visit shelters/rescues and specify: *"Small, adult, mixed-breed dogs with known temperament (e.g., Cavalier/French Bulldog mixes)."*  
   - Ask about **training history** and **socialization** (e.g., "Does this dog play well with kids/dogs?").  

2. **Breed-Specific Rescues**:  
   - Search for **Cavalier, French Bulldog, Bichon, or Pug rescues**. Many have adult dogs ready for adoption.  

3. **Reputable Breeders (Purebred Option)**:  
   - If prioritizing purebreds, seek **AKC-accredited breeders** for French Bulldogs, Pugs, or Bichons. Ask:  
     *"Do you have adult dogs? What’s their temperament with kids? Do they bark when alone?"*  
   - **Avoid puppy mills**—opt for breeders who provide health guarantees.  

4. **Meet-and-Greet**:  
   - Always meet the dog **in person** with your spouse. Test:  
     - How does it react to your spouse?  
     - Does it bark when you leave?  
     - Is it calm around kids (if applicable)?  

5. **Trial Adoption**:  
   - Consider a **short-term foster** to test compatibility before committing.
