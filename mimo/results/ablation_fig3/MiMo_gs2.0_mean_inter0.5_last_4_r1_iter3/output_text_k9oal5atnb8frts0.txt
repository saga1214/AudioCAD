Alright, let’s keep it simple and fun for the Retro Tech lunch ideas. First, you could do a “Dial-Up Dinner.” The main dish would be something nostalgic like spaghetti and meatballs, because that’s a classic. For the side, a “mouse salad” where you use a small toy mouse as a garnish on the plate. Then, for snacks, you could have “mod chips,” which are just pretzel sticks with cheese dip, since mod chips were part of the retro computer scene. Second idea: “CRT Casserole.” The main dish is a baked potato bar, where everyone customizes their potato with toppings like sour cream and chives, just like old computer monitors. For a snack, you could have “floppy disk fruit,” which is just grapes or berries arranged to look like old floppy disks. These ideas tie in the theme while keeping the food easy and crowd-pleasing.
