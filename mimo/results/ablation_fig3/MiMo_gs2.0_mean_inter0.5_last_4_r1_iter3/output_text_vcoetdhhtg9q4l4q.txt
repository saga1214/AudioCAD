You're absolutely right—simplifying is key! For super quick dinners with minimal ingredients, here are a few ideas that align with your decluttering mindset:

### 🍳 1. **Sheet Pan Lemon-Herb Chicken & Veggies**  
**Ingredients**: Chicken thighs, broccoli, bell peppers, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt/pepper.  
**Why it’s fast**: Toss everything on a pan, roast at 400°F (200°C) for 20–25 mins. Zero dishes! Uses pantry staples.

### 🍜 2. **15-Minute Peanut Noodles**  
**Ingredients**: Rice noodles (one you *do* love!), peanut butter, soy sauce, lime, chili flakes, scallions, cucumber (optional).  
**Why it’s fast**: Stir-fry minced garlic/shallot in a pan, add noodles + sauce, toss for 5 mins. Uses just 3–4 ingredients.

### 🥬 3. **Mason Jar Salad**  
**Ingredients**: Base (greens), protein (chickpeas or grilled chicken), dressing (olive oil + vinegar), toppings (nuts, seeds).  
**Why it’s fast**: Layer ingredients in jars ahead. Grab and shake when ready—no assembly stress.

### ⚡ **Pro Tip**:  
Prep "grab-and-go" components weekly:  
- Chop veggies (carrots, celery, peppers)  
- Cook a big batch of grains (quinoa, rice)  
- Hard-boil eggs  
Then you can **combine ingredients in minutes**—no shopping needed!
