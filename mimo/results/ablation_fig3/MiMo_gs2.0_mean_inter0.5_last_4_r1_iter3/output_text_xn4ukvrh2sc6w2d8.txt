Here’s a clear, bar-friendly list with precise ingredients, measurements, and instructions for your cocktails:

### 1. **Old Fashioned**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey  
- 1 sugar cube (or ¾ oz simple syrup)  
- 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- 1 orange twist (for garnish)  
- *Optional:* 1 oz ice cube (for chilling)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube with 1 dash of bitters in an Old Fashioned glass.  
2. Add remaining bitters, 2 oz bourbon, and ice. Stir well until chilled.  
3. Strain into an ice-filled rocks glass.  
4. Express an orange twist over the drink (squeeze oils into the foam), then drop it in.  

---

### 2. **Paper Plane**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz Aperol  
- ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- *Garnish:* Lemon twist (expressed over the drink)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
3. Express a lemon twist over the drink and drop it in.  

---

### 3. **Whiskey Sour**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- *Optional:* ½ oz egg white (for texture)  

**Instructions:**  
1. *Dry shake* (no ice): Add bourbon, lemon juice, simple syrup, and egg white (if using) to a shaker. Shake until frothy.  
2. *Wet shake*: Add ice, shake again for 10–15 seconds.  
3. Strain into an Old Fashioned or rocks glass over ice.  
4. Garnish with a lemon twist or maraschino cherry.  

---

### 4. **Aviation**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz gin  
- ¾ oz maraschino liqueur  
- ¾ oz crème de violette  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- *Garnish:* Maraschino cherry  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
3. Garnish with a maraschino cherry.  

---

### 5. **French 75 (Gin Option)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz lemon juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 3 oz dry sparkling wine (Champagne or prosecco)  
- *Garnish:* Lemon wheel or herb sprig  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a flute.  
3. Top with sparkling wine.  
4. Garnish with lemon or herbs.  

---
