That's the eternal struggle with hot glue guns! Here's how to fix it and finish those awesome globes:

**Glue Gun Troubleshooting (Quick Fixes):**

1.  **Wait for Full Heat:** Let it sit undisturbed for 3-5 minutes. Sometimes it needs that extra time to reach full temperature, even if it *looks* hot.
2.  **Check Temperature Setting:** Is it on the highest setting? If it's an adjustable model, ensure it's set to "High" or "Maximum."
3.  **Don't Overfill:** Overfilling can cause the glue to pool and not heat evenly. Tip it gently to level the reservoir.
4.  **Let it Cool Slightly:** If it's bubbling excessively or *not* bubbling at all, give it another minute or two before trying again. Sometimes it just needs a tiny cooldown to start flowing properly.
5.  **Clean the Tip:** If it's clogging slightly, use a pin to gently clear the very tip. Don't force it.
6.  **Try a Different Stick:** Sometimes the glue stick itself is old or needs warming. Run a new stick under hot water for a few seconds before applying it.

**Making Your Globes *Really* Spooky (Building on Your Ideas):**

Your ideas (cobwebs, lace, masks, dark flowers, glitter) are fantastic! Here are a few more spooky additions that would pair perfectly:

1.  **Dried Blood Splatters:** Use a small brush dipped in **diluted red paint** (like a mix of red and black) and dab it lightly around the base of the globe or mask. Keep it subtle for maximum effect. Look for "dried blood" craft paint if you can find it.
2.  **Tiny Faux Scarecrows:** Add a miniature, creepy scarecrow figure nestled near the base or peeking out from behind the globe. Black felt or burlap fabric works well.
3.  **Miniature Bats:** Attach a few small, realistic-looking faux bats around the base or clustered near the pumpkin top.
4.  **"Eerie" Moss:** Go beyond just moss. Add patches of **bright green moss** mixed with **black moss** for a more unnatural, "graveyard" feel. Maybe add a tiny **white feather** or **tiny bone charm** nestled in it.
5.  **Faux Corpse Flowers:** Look for "Corpse Flower" or "Bleeding Heart" faux flowers in deep burgundy, black, or white. These add a classic, unsettling botanical element.
6.  **Miniature "Buried" Objects:** Tuck in tiny faux **bones**, **tiny skulls**, or **dried "wires"** (like faux spiderwebs made of thin, black thread) into the base or lace netting.
7.  **Moody Water Drip:** If you can find clear, black or deep blue "water" glue, create a few small drips or a thin line down the side of the globe base to mimic a spooky pond or grave water.
8.  **Smaller Decor:** Add tiny faux **crows** or **raven** figures perched nearby.
