Dallas has some fantastic Asian noodle spots! For ramen, check out Tonkatsu Tamashii or Yippee Noodle House. If you're looking for more of a stir-fry, there's a great place called Bangkok Noodles. And for a casual vibe, Noodle House is a solid choice. For protein bars, I’d recommend Quest or Optimum Nutrition—they’re high in protein but not too sugary. If you want something more natural, Larabar is a good option. Hope you enjoy your meal and snacks!
