You're right, Sagittarius and Leo make a perfect adventurous pair! Calgary is surrounded by all sorts of exciting options. If you want something easy and quick, you could go to the Calgary Zoo. They have a fun, modern zoo with a great café, and it's a nice, relaxed way to spend a few hours together. 

If you're looking for something more rugged, the Banff Gondola is a great choice. You can ride up Sulphur Mountain and walk along the ridges for amazing views. It's a bit more of an effort to get to, but it's a truly spectacular adventure. 

For a real off-the-beaten-path experience, you could try to find a spot for a canoe or kayak on the Kananaskis River. It's a bit of a drive, but the scenery is breathtaking and the water is usually calm enough for a beginner. Just be sure to check the water levels before you go!
