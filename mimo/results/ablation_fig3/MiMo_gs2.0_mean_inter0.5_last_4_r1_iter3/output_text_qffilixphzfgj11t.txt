Got it. Bruno sensitive to apples, bananas, and broccoli, so we need other green veggies. Here are five good options.

First, you've got spinach. It's rich in vitamins A and C and iron. Just make sure it's fresh and washed really well.

Next, kale is another great one, packed with vitamins A, C, and K.

Then there's peas. They're high in protein and vitamins A and C.

Also, green peas, which are similar and a good source of fiber.

Finally, green beans are a classic, providing vitamins K and A, plus some fiber.

Just remember to offer these in moderation and introduce them one at a time to see how your bird reacts.
