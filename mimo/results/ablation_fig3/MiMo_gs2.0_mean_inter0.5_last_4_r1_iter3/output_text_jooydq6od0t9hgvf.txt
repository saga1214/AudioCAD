Here are quick, elegant drink recipes for your vodka mixer and Argentine-style Fernet—keeping formality and mixed age groups in mind:

### 🍸 **Vodka Mixer: Cucumber-Mint Gin & Tonic**  
*(Light, refreshing, no soda)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz vodka (or gin for better herb infusion)  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- 3 oz chilled cucumber-mint tonic (see note below)  
- 4 cucumber slices + 6 mint leaves (garnish)  

**Note**: *Cucumber-mint tonic* = Mix 2 tbsp muddled cucumber + 5 mint leaves + 1 tbsp honey with 10 oz sparkling water. Strain and chill.  

**Why it works**: Cucumber-mint balances vodka’s bite, feels upscale, and avoids soda.  

---

### 🇦🇷 **Argentine-Style Fernet: Passionfruit-Ginger Fernet Soda**  
*(Bold but approachable for all ages)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 2 oz fresh passionfruit purée  
- 0.5 oz ginger syrup (*simmer 1 cup water + ½ cup sliced ginger + ¼ cup sugar; strain and chill*)  
- Top with soda water (no soda!)  
- Garnish: Lime wheel + candied ginger  

**Method**: Shake Fernet, passionfruit, and syrup with ice. Strain into a coupe glass. Top with soda.  

**Why it works**: Passionfruit adds fruitiness to Fernet’s herbal bite; ginger syrup rounds out the flavor.  

---

### 🥂 **Bonus Signature Mocktail for Soda Allergy**  
**"Berry & Basil Spritz"**:  
- 1 oz elderflower syrup  
- 3 oz sparkling rosé (or still wine for less fizz)  
- 4 raspberries + 2 basil leaves (muddled)  
- Top with soda water (optional, for subtle fizz)  
- Garnish: Basil sprig  

---

### Key Tips:  
1. **Formality**: Use chilled glassware (coupe, martini) and garnish thoughtfully.  
2. **Efficiency**: Prep syrups/tonics ahead; assemble drinks just before serving.  
3. **Balance**: The Fernet mocktail is strong—offer lighter options (rosé spritz) alongside.
