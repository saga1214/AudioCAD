You already have SkinnyPop and PopChips brands in mind, which is great, but here are a couple gluten-free snack ideas for camping.

Trail mix is always a good choice, just make sure it's certified gluten-free. You can mix nuts, seeds, and gluten-free dried fruits.

Another idea is rice cakes, which are gluten-free. You can bring them plain or even pair them with some nut butter for a more filling snack. Hope you enjoy those!
