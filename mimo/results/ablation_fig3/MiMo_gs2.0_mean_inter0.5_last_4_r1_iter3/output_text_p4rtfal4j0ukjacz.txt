I see where the confusion comes from. You're right that 20 percent of the daily recommendation is a low percentage on its own. However, the important thing is the total amount of sodium you consumed for the day.

Your portion of the meal likely had about 450 milligrams of sodium. If that was your only meal, then the rest of your day must have had zero sodium. This brings your total daily sodium intake to 450 milligrams.

For a typical adult, the recommended daily intake is between 1,500 and 2,300 milligrams. So, 450 milligrams is actually very low. It's not too much sodium at all; it's quite low for a single meal, let alone for a whole day. 

To give you an idea, a single slice of processed bread can have around 400 milligrams of sodium. It sounds like your meal was actually very low in sodium.
