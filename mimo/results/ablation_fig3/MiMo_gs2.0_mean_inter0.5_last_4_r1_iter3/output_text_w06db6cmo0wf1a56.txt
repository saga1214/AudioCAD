Absolutely! Here's a practical, low-mess shopping list designed for 10 dads, 10 kids, drinks, snacks, and meals for your theme park adventure:

**Drinks (Focus: Water, Hydration, Low-Sugar Options):**

1.  **Water Bottles (Individual):** 30 large water bottles (1L or 2L size) - for refills.
2.  **Sugar-Free Sodas:** 15 cans (or bottles) of your chosen sugar-free adult favorites (e.g., Coke Zero, Pepsi Zero, Sprite Zero).
3.  **Low-Sugar Juice Boxes:** 15 boxes (Apple, Orange, Grape) - kid-friendly & portable.
4.  **Infused Water (Optional but Fun):** 3-4 large pitchers of water with fruit slices (lemon, lime, cucumber, berries) - add flavor without sugar.
5.  **Ice Packs:** 4-6 large reusable ice packs + 1-2 dry ice packs (check cooler capacity/temperature).

**Snacks (Focus: Minimal Mess, Individual Servings):**

1.  **Cheese Sticks:** 15-20 (various flavors if available).
2.  **Grapes:** 3-4 lbs (pre-washed, ready to grab).
3.  **Baby Carrots:** 3-4 lbs (pre-cut).
4.  **Individual Hummus Cups:** 10-12 cups (配胡萝卜条/葡萄).
5.  **Trail Mix:** 10-12 individual bags (look for nut-free options if needed).
6.  **Pretzel Snacks:** 10-12 individual small bags (salty snack for kids/dads).
7.  **Fresh Fruit:** 10-12 individual bags (e.g., apple slices, banana slices, berries - pre-portioned).
8.  **Granola Bars:** 10-12 bars (look for lower-sugar options).

**Main Meals (Focus: Grab-and-Go, Minimal Assembly):**

1.  **Mini Calzones (The Star!):** 10-12 mini calzones. *Option 1:* Pre-made from a bakery/pizzeria (cheese, pepperoni, veggie, or combo). *Option 2:* Homemade mini calzones (faster to assemble than full-sized). *Portion Control:* Perfect for handing out individually.
2.  **Wrap/Sandwiches (Pre-Made Individual):** 10-12 pre-made wraps or wraps packed in individual containers. *Fillings:* Think turkey & cheese, hummus & cucumber, black bean & corn, or veggie pesto. Wrap tightly in parchment paper.
3.  **Hard-Boiled Eggs:** 10-12 eggs (easy protein, peelable).
4.  **Turkey/Chicken Roll-Ups:** 10-12 roll-ups with cream cheese, spinach, and turkey/chicken.
5.  **Whole Wheat Pita Chips:** 1 bag (crunchy, kid-friendly).

**Desserts (Focus: Individual Portions):**

1.  **Cupcakes:** 12 cupcakes (baked or frosted bakery-style) - celebrate the groom!
2.  **Brownie Bites:** 12 individual brownie bites (easier than cutting a whole brownie).
3.  **Small Cookies:** 12 small cookies (e.g., chocolate chip, oatmeal).
4.  **Fruit Leather Packs:** 10-12 individual packs (light dessert/snack).

**Cooler Organization Tips:**

*   **Layers:** Use multiple coolers or large bins inside one.
*   **Separation:** Keep drinks (especially soda) in one compartment (use a dry bag liner!), snacks in another, and main meals in a third.
*   **Ice Placement:** Put heavy ice packs at the bottom and around the perimeter of each section.
*   **Labeling:** Mark compartments clearly (e.g., "Drinks," "Snacks," "Main Meals").
*   **Utensils:** Pack a few sets of reusable forks/spoons (if needed for sandwiches/wraps) and maybe some napkins.
*   **Trash Bags:** Include small trash bags for each cooler compartment.

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Allergies:** Double-check for common allergens (dairy, nuts, gluten) in snacks and calzone fillings.
*   **Temperature:** Aim for food to stay cool for 4-6 hours. Adjust quantities slightly based on cooler size.
*   **Hydration:** Water is essential. Soda and juice are treats.
*   **Portion Control:** Minis and individual packs help everyone grab what they want without leftovers.
*   **Enjoyment:** Have a fantastic time celebrating your best friend! This setup should keep things smooth and fun for everyone.
