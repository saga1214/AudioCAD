For the brooder plate, you'll want a bedding that's absorbent, dry, and safe. The best choice is wood shavings or aspen shavings. You can use the deep litter method again, just like in the main coop, adding fresh layers on top. Another good option is paper-based bedding, like the kind that comes in rolls. It's easy to manage but you'll need to change it more often.

The most important thing is to keep the bedding dry and clean. Avoid using pine cones because they can be sharp and hurt the chicks' feet.
