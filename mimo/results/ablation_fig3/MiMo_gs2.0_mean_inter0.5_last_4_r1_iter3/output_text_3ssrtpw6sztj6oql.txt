2.  **Managing Threats Before They Enter:** Screening procedures (like bag checks, ID verification for staff/guests, and metal detectors) are designed to:
    *   **Identify & Remove Threats:** Detect weapons, explosives, or stolen items *before* they enter the building.
    *   **Manage Behavior:** Control the flow of people, preventing overcrowding or covert entry points.
    *   **Filter Insider Risks:** While less common, verifying staff credentials at entry points mitigates the threat of an insider aiding a theft.
3.  **Foundation for Other Layers:** This initial control layer is essential because:
    *   **Cameras (Layer 2):** Need people to be *inside* the building to monitor effectively. Access control stops the problem before cameras are needed.
    *   **Alarms/Sensors (Layer 2/3):** Are useless if the threat bypasses the initial entry point entirely.
    *   **Secure Displays (Layer 3):** Protect specific art *inside* the building after entry has occurred.
