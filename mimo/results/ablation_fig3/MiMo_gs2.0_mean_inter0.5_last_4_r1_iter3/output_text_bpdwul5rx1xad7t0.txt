Here are 5 fantastic BC road trip options with beachside activities, luxury dining, and stunning scenery—perfect for your new family and Golden Retriever:

### 🌊 1. **Vancouver Island Coastal Adventure**  
**Route:** Vancouver → Tofino (3.5 hrs)  
**Highlights:**  
- **Tofino:** Hike to **Uluwatu Rock** (cliff-top ocean views) or **Long Beach** (tidepools for your pup!).  
- **Dining:** *Broken Shaker* (Tofino) – upscale Pacific Northwest cuisine with oceanfront views.  
- **Luxury:** Stay at **Tofino Inlet Lodge** (private beach access, spa).  
**Why:** Wild beaches, rainforest hikes, and whale-watching (May–Oct).

### 🍇 2. **Okanagan Lakes & Wineries**  
**Route:** Vancouver → Penticton (4 hrs)  
**Highlights:**  
- **KLO Winery:** Lakeside wine tasting with views of Okanagan Lake.  
- **Dining:** *The Bench* (Penticton) – farm-to-table with vineyard vistas.  
- **Activity:** Kayak on **Pato Island** (birdwatching, calm waters).  
**Why:** Crystal-clear lakes, vineyard tours, and the famous **Shannon Falls**.

### 🏞️ 3. **Fraser Valley Scenic Route**  
**Route:** Vancouver → Hope → Britannia Bay (2.5 hrs)  
**Highlights:**  
- **Capilano Suspension Bridge:** Treetop walk (stroller-friendly lower bridge).  
- **Dining:** *The Capilano River Inn* – gourmet dining with forest views.  
- **Beach:** *Britannia Bay* (quiet, rocky cove for your pup).  
**Why:** Waterfalls, bridges, and berry picking (summer).

### ❄️ 4. **Coast Mountains (Whistler → Lillooet)**  
**Route:** Vancouver → Lillooet (2.5 hrs)  
**Highlights:**  
- **Lillooet River:** Kayak or fish for salmon.  
- **Dining:** *The Numa Numa* (Lillooet) – mountain lodge with elk antler decor.  
- **Luxury:** *Whistler Parq* (resort with spa, pools, and gourmet dining).  
**Why:** Alpine lakes, glaciers, and year-round adventure.

### 🌅 5. **Sunshine Coast Escape**  
**Route:** Vancouver → Sechelt → Galiano Island (2.5 hrs)  
**Highlights:**  
- **Galiano Island:** Paddleboard at **Simpson Beach** (calm, sandy).  
- **Dining:** *The Copper Beach* (Sechelt) – oceanfront fine dining.  
- **Activity:** Boat tour to **Baker Bay** (sea lions, salt marshes).  
**Why:** Secluded beaches, historic villages, and whale cruises.

### 🧳 **Pro Tips for Your Trip:**  
- **Baby Prep:** Pack strollers, portable cribs, and waterproof toys.  
- **Dog-Friendly Stops:** Check [BC Parks](https://www.parks.canada.ca/) for pet-friendly trails.  
- **Rain Gear:** Waterproof jackets and doggy raincoats are essential!  
- **Reservations:** Book wineries, lodges, and popular restaurants early.
