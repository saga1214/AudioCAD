That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour Beach Resort on St. Thomas has a stunning, protected bay perfect for swimming and snorkeling right off the shore. Here are some fun daytime activities tailored to your boat-based trip:

### **Water Adventures**
1. **Snorkeling Tours**:  
   - **Coral Bay Boat Tours** (St. John): Flat-bottom boat excursions to Trunk Bay or Salt River Bay—calm waters, shallow reefs, and abundant fish.  
   - **Red Hook Pirate Cruise** (St. Thomas): A short sail to the Sea Turtle Sanctuary (May–Oct) for snorkeling among sea turtles and reefs.  

2. **Kayaking**:  
   - Rent kayaks from **Kayak Cruzers** (St. John) or **Red Hook Kayak** (St. Thomas) to explore mangroves, salt marshes, or flat bays without pressure changes.  

3. **Sailing Excursions**:  
   - Join a **sunset sail** from Secret Harbour or Cruz Bay (St. John) or Red Hook (St. Thomas) for zero-pressure cruising with drinks and music.  

### **Beach & Bay Relaxation**
- **Secret Harbour Beach Resort**: Snorkel the calm bay, or rent chairs from the resort for sunset views.  
- **Magens Bay** (St. Thomas): A famous crescent-shaped beach with gentle waves—great for your brother. Nearby **The Beach Club** offers cabanas and drinks.  
- **Cinnamon Bay** (St. John): Accessible by boat; try **Anon Beach Bar** for smoothies and live acoustic music.  

### **Culture & Local Vibes**
- **Red Hook, St. Thomas**: Explore the artsy East End hub. Visit the **Magens Bay Sea Turtle Sanctuary** (May–Oct), browse local galleries, or grab lunch at **The Waterman** (oceanfront seafood).  
- **St. Croix**: Take a short boat trip to **Sasco Bay** for a visit to **St. Croix Rum Distillery** (tastings available!) or the **Cultural Heritage Museum**.  

### **Unique Experiences**
- **Dolphin Watching**: Charter a boat for a dolphin-spotting tour (e.g., **Dolphin Bay Tours** in St. John).  
- **Pirate History**: Red Hook’s Fort Christian and pirate-themed festivals (check schedules for June’s **Red Hook Pirate Festival**).  

### **Pro Tips**
- **Boat Charters**: Opt for **point-to-point day sails** (e.g., St. Thomas to St. John) to avoid pressure changes. Companies like **Sailaway Charters** or **Sailaway St. Thomas** offer flexible itineraries.  
- **Avoid Altitude**: Skip hiking or anything steep—stick to flat beaches and shallow-water activities.  

Enjoy your week! The Virgin Islands’ calm bays and boat-based flexibility will let you both dive in without stress. 🌴🚤
