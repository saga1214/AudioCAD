As for Thanksgiving dessert ideas, chocolate fudge brownies in a pan are a great idea. They're rich, comforting, and easy to make. You just mix melted butter, sugar, eggs, cocoa powder, baking soda, and vanilla. Pour it into a pan and bake it at 350 degrees for about 25 to 30 minutes. It's a crowd-pleaser and will be much easier on the heart this year.
