That's a really sharp insight. I'd have to agree with that. The Portuguese had already started their project to bypass the Mediterranean, largely because of the Venetian monopoly. The fall of Constantinople just added a huge sense of urgency and gave the project a huge boost in prestige. It was a major catalyst, but not the sole cause. The underlying drive was already there.

So yeah, I think you're right. The Age of Discovery was going to happen anyway, but the fall of Constantinople was a massive accelerant that made it happen much faster and on a grander scale.
