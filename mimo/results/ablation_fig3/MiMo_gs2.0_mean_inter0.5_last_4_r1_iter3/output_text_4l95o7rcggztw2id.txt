Here’s a complete nautical/pirate-themed food and drink menu designed for easy beach handling, avoiding sand, accommodating allergies, and maximizing fun:

**Shopping List (Nautical/Pirate Party at the Beach)**

**Main Course (Pirate Ship Sandwiches):**
*   Bread (Sandwich bread, cut into mini triangles or rectangles)
*   Cheese slices (Ham & Swiss, Cheddar, or Pepper Jack)
*   Ham slices or diced ham
*   Cookie cutters (Ship shapes like anchors, masts, sails, or simple rectangles)
*   Mini paper cups (for serving in "treasure chests")
*   **Allergy Note:** Omit watermelon filling. Use grapes, berries, or apple slices if needed.

**Sides (Pirate Gold & Veggie Treasure):**
*   Cheese cubes (Colby Jack, Mozzarella, or Cheddar - shape with a fork for "gold coins")
*   Baby carrots
*   Celery sticks
*   Hummus (small individual containers)
*   Apple slices (optional: dip in lemon juice to prevent browning)
*   **Allergy Note:** Ensure no watermelon-based dips or fillings.

**Snacks (Pirate Ship Masts & Treasure):**
*   Pretzel rods (small ones)
*   Popcorn (yellow for "gold dust")
*   Goldfish crackers (classic or themed)
*   Mini chocolate chips (for "treasure")
*   **Allergy Note:** Check pretzel rods for potential watermelon flavorings (rare, but possible in some brands). Offer grapes or berries as an alternative.

**Drinks (Captain's Punch & Sea Water):**
*   Individual juice boxes (Apple, Orange, Grape, etc.)
*   Small water bottles (Labeled "Sea Water" or "Captain's Punch")
*   Small vials or jars of sparkling water (with blue food coloring & maybe a tiny plastic anchor - labeled "Sea Water")
*   Small plastic cups (for serving juice/punch if needed)
*   Small flags or rope for decorating water bottles

**Extras (Packaging & Fun):**
*   Small treasure chests or boxes (for serving sandwiches & cheese coins)
*   Small bowls (for dips, fruit, popcorn)
*   Rags or hand towels (for spills)
*   Ice packs (for cooler)
*   Cooler bag (for transport)
*   Labels (for dishes, drinks)

**Menu Breakdown & Why It Works:**

1.  **Mini Pirate Ship Sandwiches:** Cut sandwiches into sturdy shapes, pack in mini cups. Easy to pick up, minimal mess, contained in a "chest." (Theme: Ship Shapes)
2.  **Cheese Cubes ("Gold Coins"):** Pre-shape cheese cubes for instant pirate gold. Easy to handle, no dips needed. (Theme: Gold Coins)
3.  **Veggie Sticks & Hummus ("Sea Foam"):** Cut into manageable sizes. Serve with a small dip cup. (Theme: Veggie Treasure)
4.  **Dyed Green Apple Slices ("Seaweed"):** Fun and thematic snack. (Theme: Seaweed)
5.  **Pretzel Rods ("Masts"):** Sturdy, easy to eat, classic pirate snack. (Theme: Ship Masts)
6.  **Popcorn ("Gold Dust"):** Light, crunchy, easy to eat. (Theme: Gold Dust)
7.  **Grape or Berry Skewers ("Pirate Jewels"):** Fun, colorful, easy to hold. (Theme: Pirate Jewels)
8.  **Individual Juice Boxes ("Captain's Punch"):** Sealable, spill-proof, easy to carry. (Theme: Captain's Punch)
9.  **Sparkling Water ("Sea Water"):** Refreshing, visually thematic (blue), sealable. (Theme: Sea Water)

**Key Strategies for Sand-Free & Allergy-Friendly:**

*   **Finger Foods:** Everything is bite-sized and easy to eat one-handed.
*   **Sealed Packaging:** Juice boxes, water bottles, cheese in cups, snack bags, and individual dips prevent spills and keep sand out.
*   **Portion Control:** Small servings mean less chance of dropping or getting sandy.
*   **Allergy Control:** Clearly label the menu, avoid the allergen (watermelon), and offer safe alternatives (grapes, berries, plain crackers, plain popcorn). Confirm with the parent.
*   **Themed Presentation:** Treasure chest cups, shaped cheese, named drinks – adds magic without extra mess.
*   **Easy Cleanup:** Pack rags for spills, and everything goes back in the cooler easily.

This menu provides a variety of textures and tastes, is fully thematic, minimizes mess and sand exposure, and is safe for your son's seven guests, including the one with the watermelon allergy. Have a fantastic pirate party!
