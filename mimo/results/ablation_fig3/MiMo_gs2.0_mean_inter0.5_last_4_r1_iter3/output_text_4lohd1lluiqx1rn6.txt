Based on global popularity and sedan offerings, the **top three car brands most consistently known for sedans are:**

1.  **Toyota:** The undisputed global leader. Toyota offers sedans across *every* segment, from compact (Corolla) and midsize (Camry) to luxury (Lexus ES, LS) and family staples (Prius). Reliability and practicality are core to their sedan identity.
2.  **Honda:** A major player in the compact and midsize sedan segments. The Honda Civic is one of the world's best-selling sedans, and the Accord is a long-standing American best-seller. Honda focuses on efficiency, performance, and value.
3.  **BMW:** The quintessential premium sedan brand. BMW is synonymous with performance, handling, luxury, and engineering excellence. Their 3 Series and 5 Series are iconic global sedans, defining the luxury sedan market.

**Other highly respected brands known for strong sedan lineups include:**

*   **Subaru:** Known for reliable and capable compact and midsize sedans (Impreza, Legacy, Outback sedan).
*   **Lexus:** Toyota's luxury brand, offering premium sedans like the ES and LS.
*   **Hyundai:** Offers a wide range of well-regarded sedans across segments (Elantra, Sonata, K5, Santa Fe sedan).
