Here's a step-by-step guide to safely and effectively install your items, keeping noise and landlord rules in mind:

### 📺 1. Mounting the TV
- **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, drill, appropriate drill bits, wall anchors (if mounting in drywall), TV wall mount, screwdriver.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Identify Wall Type:** Use a stud finder to locate studs (essential for heavy TVs). If no studs, use wall anchors rated for your TV's weight.
  2. **Mark Level Spot:** Place the TV mount on the wall, use a level to mark screw holes, and tape a level to ensure alignment.
  3. **Drill Pilot Holes:** Drill holes slightly smaller than your screws/anchor bolts. Avoid drilling into electrical wires or pipes.
  4. **Secure Mount:** Attach the mount to the wall, then secure it to the TV using provided bolts.
  5. **Test Stability:** Gently nudge the TV to ensure it’s secure before mounting furniture above it.

### 🌿 2. Hanging Planters
- **Tools Needed:** Drill, appropriate drill bits, wall anchors, strong wire/hook (e.g., S-hooks), measuring tape, level.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Choose Planter Weight:** Ensure planters + soil + water won’t exceed the weight limit of your wall anchors.
  2. **Mark Hook Location:** Place the planter, mark hook placement on the wall, and use a level.
  3. **Drill & Anchor:** Drill holes, insert anchors, and screw in the hook/wire.
  4. **Hang Planter:** Rest the planter on the hook. For heavier pots, use multiple hooks or a shelf bracket.

### 📚 3. Installing Floating Shelves
- **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, drill, appropriate drill bits, wall anchors, screwdriver, L-brackets (optional for extra stability).
- **Steps:**
  1. **Measure & Plan:** Decide shelf height/spacing. Mark top/bottom edges with a pencil.
  2. **Check for Studs:** Use a stud finder. If no studs, use heavy-duty anchors (e.g., toggle bolts).
  3. **Drill Pilot Holes:** Drill holes at marked spots. Test fit screws before drilling.
  4. **Attach Shelf:** Screw shelf to wall. Use L-brackets if shelves are >12" deep or over 20 lbs.
  5. **Level & Secure:** Check level, add anchors if needed, and tighten screws.

### ⚠️ Critical Safety Tips:
- **Weight Limits:** Never exceed recommended weight for anchors/studs. For TVs >50", use a professional installer.
- **Tool Prep:** Use a stud finder with a laser level for accuracy. Dust masks and safety glasses are essential.
- **Noise Control:** Work during the day to avoid disturbing neighbors. Use a vacuum for dust cleanup.
- **Anchor Types:** For drywall:
  - **Lightweight items:** Plastic anchors (e.g., for small plants).
  - **Medium-weight (50-75 lbs):** Toggle bolts or molly bolts.
  - **Heavy items (TVs, large shelves):** Use heavy-duty toggle bolts or install into studs.

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Pre-Install:** Place furniture/TV near the work area to avoid repeated trips.
- **Post-Installation:** Test all items for stability. Use silicone caulk to seal holes if desired.
- **Maintenance:** Wipe down shelves monthly to prevent dust buildup on decor.

By following these steps, you’ll create a secure, stylish space without violating your landlord’s rules. Start with one item at a time to avoid fatigue, and enjoy your transformed studio! 🎨✨
