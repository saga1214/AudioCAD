Here's a concise list of my top recommendations organized by category, with purchasing locations:

### 🛠️ **Metal Cutting Tools**
1. **Aviation Snips**  
   - *For*: Thinner sheets (< 16 gauge), curves  
   - *Why*: Quick, smooth cuts for precision work  
2. **Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel**  
   - *For*: Thicker sheets (> 16 gauge), straight lines  
   - *Why*: High power, efficient for heavy-duty cuts  
3. **Metal-Blade Jigsaw**  
   - *For*: Medium-thickness sheets, complex shapes  
   - *Why*: Flexible for curves, less strain than snips  

### 🧱 **Drywall Fasteners**
1. **1-1/4" Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws**  
   - *For*: Wood studs (standard half-inch drywall)  
   - *Why*: Superior holding power vs. nails; self-drilling, prevents pops  
   - *Avoid*: Nails (especially smooth shanks) – prone to backing out  

### 🧱 **Drywall Insulation**
1. **Fiberglass Bat Insulation**  
   - *For*: Most residential walls  
   - *Why*: Readily available, cost-effective, good thermal/acoustic performance  
2. **Mineral Wool**  
   - *For*: Soundproofing or fire-prone areas  
   - *Why*: Fire-resistant, blocks noise better than fiberglass  

### 🛒 **Where to Buy in Indianapolis**  
- **Home Depot** (Best selection):  
  - Tools: Angle grinders, jigsaws, aviation snips  
  - Drywall screws: Packets of 25-50  
  - Insulation: Fiberglass batts, mineral wool  
- **Lowe’s**: Similar tools/screws, often competitive pricing  
- **Menards**: Budget-friendly tools/materials  
- **Local Ace Hardware/Tractor Supply**: Smaller quantities, basic supplies  

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: For drywall screws, grab a **100-count box** of #13 coarse-thread screws (e.g., *GRK* or *DeWalt*). They’re pre-coated for easy driving and won’t strip. Insulation: Opt for **closed-cell spray foam** if you need air sealing (requires pro installation).
