### Treat Recommendations:
1. **Best Flavors**:  
   - **Peanut Butter** (small amounts, unsweetened)  
   - **Cheese** (small cubes, mild like mozzarella)  
   - **Commercial Puppy Treats** (look for low-sodium, grain-free options)  
   - **Boiled Chicken** (plain, shredded)  
   *Avoid*: Chocolate, xylitol, or anything salty/spicy.  

2. **Tips**:  
   - Use tiny pieces (fits in his mouth).  
   - Keep treats accessible (in a jar or pouch).  
   - Start with 10–20% of his daily calories.  

---

### Clicker Training Explained:
**How it works**:  
A clicker is a simple plastic device that makes a sharp "click" sound. It acts as a **marker** to precisely signal the exact moment your puppy does something right.  

**Steps to teach "sit"**:  
1. Hold a treat near Galileo’s nose.  
2. Slowly raise it overhead (his head will follow, causing him to sit).  
3. The **instant** his bottom touches the ground, **click** the clicker.  
4. Immediately give the treat.  
5. Repeat 10–15 times per session.  

**Key principles**:  
- **Timing**: Click within 1–2 seconds of the desired behavior.  
- **Consistency**: Always click *before* giving the treat.  
- **Fade treats**: Once he’s reliable, reward with a treat 50% of the time, then verbal praise.  

**Why it works**:  
The clicker teaches Galileo that *this exact moment* = good behavior. The treat confirms it. Over time, he’ll associate the click with rewards.  

---

### Additional Tips:
- **Practice short sessions** (5–10 minutes), 2–3 times daily.  
- **End on a success** to keep him motivated.  
- **Use the clicker for ANY trick** (shake, stay, come).
