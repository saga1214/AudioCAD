2.  **Lungs:** The **refueling stops**. Blood-cars stop here to pick up fresh oxygen "fuel".

4.  **Capillaries:** The **tiny streets**. These small roads get the blood-cars close to every single house (cell) in your body so they can drop off oxygen.
