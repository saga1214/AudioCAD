Tuning your instrument with a tuner is simple! Here's how to do it, whether you're using a **clip-on tuner** or a **tuner app**:

### For a Clip-On Tuner:
1. **Attach the tuner** to your instrument's headstock (where the strings meet the body).  
2. **Pluck a string** (start with the thickest string, usually the 6th on guitar/ukulele).  
3. **Let the string ring** clearly for a few seconds.  
4. **Observe the tuner display**:  
   - If it shows **"flat"** (an arrow pointing down), **tighten** the tuning peg slightly.  
   - If it shows **"sharp"** (an arrow pointing up), **loosen** the peg slightly.  
5. **Repeat** until the tuner shows the note name (e.g., "A" for the 6th string) with a steady green light.  
6. **Move to the next string** and repeat.  

### For a Tuner App (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune):
1. **Open the app** and enable your phone's microphone.  
2. **Pluck a string** and let it ring clearly.  
3. **Hold your phone near the soundhole/headstock**.  
4. **Adjust the string** based on the app’s visual/audio cues:  
   - **Flat?** → Tighten the peg.  
   - **Sharp?** → Loosen the peg.  
5. **Tune to the target note** (e.g., "E" for the 1st string on guitar).  

### Pro Tips:
- **Start with the thickest string (lowest pitch)** and work toward the thinnest (highest pitch).  
- **Tune up to pitch**: If the tuner says "flat," tighten the string. If "sharp," loosen it.  
- **Use reference notes**: Many apps show the target note (e.g., "A440 Hz") for accuracy.  
- **Avoid over-adjusting**: Tiny tweaks are better than big turns.  
- **Check tuning stability**: Re-pluck the string after 10 seconds to ensure it stays in tune.  

### Why Tune?
- **Prevents buzzing** and ensures your instrument sounds clear.  
- **Maintains tension** across all strings for even sound.
