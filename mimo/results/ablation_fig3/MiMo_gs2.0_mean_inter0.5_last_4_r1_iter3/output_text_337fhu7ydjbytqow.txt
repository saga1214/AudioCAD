Here's a **separate clothing packing list** for each segment, optimized for your needs:

### **Daytime 20-Mile Segment (Daylight)**
1. **Base Layer**: Moisture-wicking shirt & shorts (avoid cotton).  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece or lightweight puffy jacket (for hilly temperature drops).  
3. **Outer Layer**: Wind/water-resistant shell (e.g., rain jacket or windbreaker).  
4. **Hats**:  
   - Day cap (if sun is present).  
   - Light beanie (if chilly).  
5. **Gloves**: Lightweight trail gloves (for grip/hand warmth).  
6. **Footwear**: Trail shoes with grip (hilly terrain) + **extra socks**.  
7. **Headlamp**: Handheld model (3+ hours battery life).  
8. **Small Pack**: To carry layers/gear (aid stations allow dropping items).  

---

### **Night 12-Mile Segment (No Aid Stations)**
1. **Base Layer**: Moisture-wicking shirt/shorts (critical for sweat management).  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece or insulated jacket (e.g., 50°F-rated).  
3. **Outer Layer**: **Waterproof/windproof shell** (rain likely at night).  
4. **Hats**:  
   - Warm beanie (fleece-lined).  
   - Buff/sweatband (for neck/hands).  
5. **Gloves**: **Thick, insulated trail gloves** (hands get cold fast).  
6. **Footwear**: Trail shoes with grip + **warm wool socks** (add layers if needed).  
7. **Headlamp**: Bright handheld (100+ lumens) + **spare batteries**.  
8. **Small Pack**: To carry extra layers (no aid stations = self-sufficiency).  

---

### **Pro Tips**  
- **Final Segment Essentials**: Add a **lightweight puffy** (e.g., 20°F) for extreme cold.  
- **Flexibility**: Pack items in zippers/pockets to layer up/down easily.  
- **Extras**: Dry change of clothes/shoes for post-race, hand warmers, and a whistle.  

**Why this works**: Daytime layers are adaptable with aid stations; nighttime requires insulation/waterproofing since you’re self-reliant. Prioritize warmth and grip for hills! 🏃‍♀️💨
