For a diesel camper van, pre-trip maintenance is crucial. Here's how to check brakes and spark plugs:

### **Brake Inspection**
1. **Visual Check**:  
   - Inspect brake pads/shoes for thinness (less than 3mm requires replacement).  
   - Look for dust, cracks, or warping on rotors/drums.  
   - Check fluid levels in the master cylinder and brake fluid reservoir (should be clean and full).  

2. **Test Drive**:  
   - Listen for grinding, squealing, or vibration during stops.  
   - Test emergency brakes—ensure they engage smoothly without jerking.  

**Signs of Wear**:  
- Squealing/grinding noises  
- Vibration during braking  
- Long stopping distances  
- Fluid leaks near wheels  

---

### **Spark Plug Replacement (Diesel-Specific)**  
Diesel engines use high-compression ignition, so spark plugs are less critical than in gasoline engines but still vital.  

**Tools Needed**:  
- Socket wrench (10mm or 13mm, depending on your van)  
- Spark plug socket (with rubber tip)  
- Wrench  
- New spark plugs (check your van’s manual for part number)  
- Dielectric grease (for boot sealing)  
- Safety gloves/goggles  

**Steps**:  
1. **Disconnect battery** to prevent electrical hazards.  
2. **Remove old plugs**:  
   - Unscrew the plug by hand, then use a socket wrench (left-hand thread for most diesel plugs—turn *counter-clockwise*).  
3. **Inspect**:  
   - Clean the plug’s threads and check for corrosion.  
4. **Install new plugs**:  
   - Apply dielectric grease to the boot.  
   - Hand-tighten, then use a wrench for 1–2 full turns (avoid over-tightening).  
5. **Reconnect battery** and test drive.  

**Note**: Diesel plugs are often **cooled plugs** (thicker insulation) and require specific torque specs—always follow your van’s manual. If unsure, consult a mechanic.  

---

### **Other Pre-Trip Checks**  
- **Engine Oil**: Change oil and filter (diesel engines are oil-hungry).  
- **Coolant**: Ensure reservoir is full and hoses are intact.  
- **Tires**: Check pressure, tread depth, and spare tire.  
- **Fluids**: Brake fluid, transmission fluid, power steering fluid.  
- **Camping Systems**: Verify water tank, fridge, and solar battery function.  

**Pro Tip**: Use a checklist from your van’s manufacturer and schedule a professional inspection 1–2 weeks before departure. A well-maintained diesel van can be a reliable workhorse for long trips!
