Oh, okay. For kosher, the main things are that you need to avoid any pork, shellfish, and make sure that meat and dairy aren't mixed. So, for your healthy snacks, hummus is fine, fruit skewers are fine, and your veggie platter is definitely fine. The sweet you mentioned, M&Ms in bacon, has pork, so that won't work. You could switch to something like dark chocolate alone, or maybe cookies with no bacon. For drinks, Pinot Noir is usually fine, as long as it's not made with animal products like gelatin. So, basically, just make sure all your savory snacks are vegetarian—no bacon or fish—and that your sweets are dairy-free if you're worried about that. It's all about keeping things separate.
