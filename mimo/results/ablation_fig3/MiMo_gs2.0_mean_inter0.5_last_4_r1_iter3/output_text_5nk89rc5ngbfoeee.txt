For an end-of-year celebration, a park barbecue with a mix of activities is a fantastic idea! Here’s a simple, inclusive plan to make it memorable without overwhelming you:

### **Key Elements to Include:**
1. **Location & Setup:**  
   - **Pick a Park:** Choose a spot with picnic tables, shade, and space for games (like a local community park).  
   - **Weather Backup:** Plan for rain (e.g., a covered pavilion or school cafeteria) to avoid disappointment.  

2. **Food & Snacks:**  
   - **Dietary Inclusivity:** Offer a **sundae bar** (vanilla ice cream with toppings) or **build-your-own pizza station** (pre-made crusts, toppings, and dipping sauces).  
   - **Light Snacks:** Keep it simple with chips, veggie sticks, hummus, and fruit cups—parents can contribute snacks via a sign-up sheet.  
   - **Ice Cream:** A shared tub of premium ice cream (e.g., Ben & Jerry’s) with cones or bowls is always a hit!  

3. **Activities:**  
   - **Classic Park Games:**  
     - Three-legged races, sack races, or a relay with water balloons.  
     - Mini-Olympics (egg-and-spoon races, tug-of-war).  
   - **Creative Projects:**  
     - Decorate personalized sashes with patches, ribbons, or friendship bracelets.  
     - Craft a "Brownie Troop Memory Book" with photos and notes from the girls.  
   - **Storytime or Talent Show:** Let girls share funny stories or talents—great for bonding!  

4. **Logistics & Costs:**  
   - **Potluck Style:** Assign parents to bring specific items (e.g., one brings ice cream, another brings napkins) to share costs.  
   - **Low-Cost Supplies:** Use school supplies (paper, markers) for crafts and repurpose picnic tables.  
   - **Transportation:** If needed, coordinate carpooling or a group ride.  

### **Why This Works:**  
- **Fun & Inclusive:** Games and food cater to all ages and preferences.  
- **Budget-Friendly:** Shared responsibilities (snacks, supplies) ease costs.  
- **Memorable:** Celebrating achievements with crafts and games reinforces teamwork.  

> 💡 **Tip:** Send a survey to parents asking what activities/snacks their daughters prefer—this ensures everyone feels included! You’ve got this—your passion for your daughters’ joy will shine through! 🌟
