Here are a few dinner suggestions—each with a different vibe—tailored to your Mediterranean cravings and productivity-focused mindset:

### 🌿 1. **Mediterranean Sheet-Pan Chicken & Veggies**  
   - *Why it fits*: Quick cleanup, nutrient-dense, and satisfying.  
   - *How*: Toss roasted chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, zucchini, and red onions with olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, and garlic. Top with lemon-herb chicken thighs. Finish with a sprinkle of feta and fresh parsley.  
   - *Bonus*: Minimal prep—ideal after a long workday.

### 🍝 2. **Greek-Style Pasta with White Bean & Spinach**  
   - *Why it fits*: Protein-packed (beans), light yet hearty, and uses pantry staples.  
   - *How*: Sauté garlic in olive oil, add spinach until wilted. Toss with cooked pasta (penne works well), white beans, lemon zest, and a drizzle of olive oil. Top with toasted pine nuts and crumbled feta.  
   - *Time-saver*: No heavy sauces—ready in 20 mins.

### 🥗 3. **Lemon-Herb Grilled Shrimp with Quinoa**  
   - *Why it fits*: Low-carb, high-protein, and perfect if you’re craving something light but substantial.  
   - *How*: Marinate shrimp in lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, and dill. Grill or pan-sear. Serve over quinoa with steamed asparagus and a dollop of tzatziki.  
   - *Efficiency tip*: Cook quinoa in bulk for the week.

### 🌯 4. **Mediterranean Stuffed Bell Peppers**  
   - *Why it fits*: Colorful, balanced, and makes great leftovers.  
   - *How*: Mix cooked quinoa, lentils, diced tomatoes, olives, and herbs. Stuff into halved bell peppers, bake until tender. Top with a sprinkle of mozzarella or vegan cheese.  
   - *Make-ahead*: Stores well for 3 days.
