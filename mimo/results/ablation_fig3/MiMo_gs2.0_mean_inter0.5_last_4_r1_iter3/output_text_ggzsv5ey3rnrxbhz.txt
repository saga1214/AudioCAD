I understand the concern about getting these chemicals out of your body, but it's really important to know that there's no single 'detox' or 'juice cleanse' that works. Your body actually has its own systems for getting rid of these things, but they move very, very slowly. It can take months or even years for them to leave your body naturally.

So, the best strategy is really what we've been talking about: reducing your exposure. Let me give you a clear list of things to avoid to help with that.

First, definitely non-stick cookware. Try to use stainless steel, cast iron, or ceramic instead.

Second, avoid grease-resistant packaging. That means fast-food wrappers, pizza boxes, and microwave popcorn bags.

Third, stay away from anything labeled stain-resistant or waterproof, like carpets, furniture, and some clothing.

Fourth, check your cosmetics and dental floss. Some products can contain them, so look for 'PFAS-free' labels.

And fifth, filter your tap water. Use a certified water filter to reduce exposure, and a pitcher-style one is a good way to avoid losing minerals while still getting rid of the chemicals.
