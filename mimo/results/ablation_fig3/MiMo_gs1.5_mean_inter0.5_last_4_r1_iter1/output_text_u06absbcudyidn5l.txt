Okay, I hear you're still trying to wrap your head around vasculitis. Let's break it down again. Vasculitis is basically when your body's own immune system mistakenly attacks and inflames your blood vessels. This inflammation can happen in blood vessels of all sizes, from large arteries to tiny capillaries.

As for other causes of general redness, or erythema, besides vasculitis, there are quite a few. Sun exposure is a big one, as is heat, like from exercise. Conditions like rosacea are also a primary cause. Some medications, allergies, and even infections can lead to redness. And yes, fibrous dysplasia is a possibility, although it's less common and more related to bone than skin.

The symptoms can vary a lot depending on which vessels are affected. Common signs include fever, fatigue, and rashes. The key takeaway is that it's an autoimmune issue where the body is attacking its own blood vessels. I hope this helps clarify things for you.
