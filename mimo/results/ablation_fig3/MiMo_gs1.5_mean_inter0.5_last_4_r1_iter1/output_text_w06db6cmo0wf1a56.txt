Here’s a **practical, low-mess shopping list** tailored for your theme park bachelor party, balancing nutrition, crowd appeal, and grab-and-go convenience:

### 🥤 **Drinks** (All chilled in coolers)
- **Water bottles** (50+ liters)  
- **Sugar-free sodas** (e.g., Sprite Zero, Coke Zero) – 24 cans  
- **Low-sugar juice boxes** (Apple, Orange) – 24 boxes  
- **Electrolyte drinks** (for hydration) – 12 boxes  
- **Sparkling water** (for adults) – 12 bottles  
- *Optional:* Lemonade/punch mix for kids (pre-brewed in sealed pitchers)

### 🥪 **Snacks** (No utensils needed)
- **Cheese sticks** (Mozzarella/Cheddar) – 30  
- **Grapes** (Pre-washed) – 3 lbs  
- **Baby carrots** – 2 lbs  
- **Individual hummus cups** – 10  
- **Trail mix** (Nuts/seeds/dried fruit) – 10 bags  
- **Pretzels** (Mini bags) – 10  
- **Energy bars** (Protein-based) – 10 boxes  

### 🍕 **Main Meals** (Pre-portioned & hearty)
- **Mini calzones** (Cheese/Pepperoni/Mushroom) – 20 pre-made (from bakery/pizzeria)  
- **Wrap bar** (For customization):  
  - Whole-wheat tortillas (10)  
  - Hummus (Single cups) – 5  
  - Turkey/Chicken sausage – 1 lb  
  - Shredded carrots/cucumber – 2 containers  
  - Whole-grain crackers – 1 box (for wraps)  

### 🍰 **Desserts** (Kid-friendly & shareable)
- **Mini cupcakes** (Chocolate/Buttercream) – 20  
- **Brownies** (Single-serve packs) – 10  
- **Popsicles** (Sugar-free fruit) – 10  

### 🧊 **Cooler Essentials**
- Ice packs (50+)  
- Ice chests (2-3)  
- Reusable containers/bags (for snacks)  
- Wet wipes, napkins, trash bags  

### 💡 **Pro Tips**
1. **Allergy Note:** Label high-allergen items (nuts, dairy) clearly.  
2. **Prep Ahead:** Assemble sandwiches/wraps at home.  
3. **Calzone Hack:** If homemade, use a simple recipe (e.g., store-bought dough, sauce, cheese) to minimize mess.  
4. **Backup Plan:** Include extra water/snacks for unexpected hunger.
