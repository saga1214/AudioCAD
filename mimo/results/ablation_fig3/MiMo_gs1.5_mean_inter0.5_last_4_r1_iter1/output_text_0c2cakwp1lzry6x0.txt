Here's a step-by-step guide for installing each item safely and effectively:

### 📺 **TV Mount Installation**
1. **Plan Layout**:  
   - Measure wall distance from floor to ceiling.  
   - Use a level to mark where you want the TV centered.  
   - **Critical**: Identify studs with a stud finder (or drill through drywall to check).  

2. **Tools Needed**:  
   - Drill, wall anchors (if no studs), level, tape measure.  

3. **Steps**:  
   - Mark screw holes on the wall.  
   - If mounting on drywall (no studs), use heavy-duty anchors rated for your TV’s weight.  
   - Secure mounting bracket to the wall, then attach the TV.  
   - **Safety Tip**: Never exceed the mount’s weight capacity!  

---

### 🌿 **Hanging Planters**
1. **Wall Type Check**:  
   - **Drywall**: Use **heavy-duty wire or chains** rated for 50+ lbs.  
   - **Brick/Concrete**: Use **metal hooks** (not plastic).  

2. **Tools Needed**:  
   - Drill, wall anchors (for drywall), wire/chain, wire cutters.  

3. **Steps**:  
   - Mark 12-18" below the top of the planter for stability.  
   - Drill hole, insert anchor, then screw hook into anchor.  
   - Attach wire/chain to the hook and secure the planter.  
   - **Tip**: For terracotta pots, use **cork rings** to prevent slipping.  

---

### 🪢 **Floating Shelves**
1. **Critical Safety Check**:  
   - **Weight Capacity**: Most shelves hold 25-50 lbs *per shelf*.  
   - **Brackets**: Use **heavy-duty L-brackets** (e.g., 1.5" thick).  

2. **Tools Needed**:  
   - Drill, wall anchors (for drywall), level, shims (for uneven walls).  

3. **Steps**:  
   - Mark shelf position 6-12" from wall.  
   - Drill holes for brackets, insert anchors.  
   - Attach brackets to wall, then mount shelf onto brackets.  
   - **Tip**: Place heavier items at the shelf’s bottom to prevent tipping.  

---

### 🔧 **General Tips**
- **Safety First**: Wear safety glasses!  
- **Drywall vs. Studs**: If no studs, use **toggle bolts** for heavy items.  
- **Measuring Twice**: Use a laser level for straight lines.  
- **Noise Control**: Do installations during the day (landlord rules!).
