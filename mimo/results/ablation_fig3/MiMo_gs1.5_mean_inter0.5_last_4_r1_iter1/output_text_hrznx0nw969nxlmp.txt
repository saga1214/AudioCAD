That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour offers calm, beautiful beaches right from your doorstep. Here’s a rundown of fun daytime activities around Red Hook and St. Thomas:

### 🏖️ Beaches & Snorkeling
1. **Secret Harbour Beach Resort**: Your base has a private, calm bay perfect for swimming and light snorkeling.  
2. **Coki Beach (Red Hook)**: A short walk from Secret Harbour. Famous for its shallow turquoise water, great for snorkeling (turtles, fish), and beachfront restaurants.  
3. **Trunk Bay (Coki Beach area)**: A protected marine park with a snorkeling trail. Snorkel gear is available for rent.  
4. **Magens Bay (Charlotte Amalie)**: St. Thomas’ most famous beach. Turquoise water, lifeguards, and shops. A 30-minute drive from Secret Harbour.  

### 🍽️ Food & Nightlife (Daytime & Evening)
- **Duffy’s Love Shack (Red Hook)**: Iconic spot for tacos, margaritas, and live music (evenings).  
- **Mango’s (Cruz Bay)**: Casual eatery with great burgers, seafood, and a friendly vibe.  
- **Bamboo (Red Hook)**: Upscale Caribbean cuisine with ocean views.  

### 🚤 Boat & Water Adventures
- **Charter a Boat**: Explore nearby islands (St. John, Water Island) for snorkeling, kayaking, or catamaran cruises.  
- **Maho Beach (St. Thomas)**: Watch planes land at the airport (20-min drive from Red Hook). Best at sunset.  
- **Virgin Islands National Park**: Kayak or paddleboard at Salt River Bay.  

### 🌿 Culture & Local Life
- **Cruz Bay Farmers Market (Sundays)**: Local crafts, produce, and music (10 AM–2 PM).  
- **St. Thomas History Museum (Charlotte Amalie)**: Learn about island culture.  
- **The Baths (Virgin Gorda)**: A short boat trip away—quaint streets, history, and caves (20 mins from Secret Harbour).  

### 🐦 Birding Hotspots
- **Trunk Bay Trail**: Look for antillean hummingbirds and tropical birds.  
- **Coki Beach**: Spot bananaquits, orioles, and roseate spoonbills.  
- **Salt River Bay**: Mangrove forests attract herons and egrets.  

### ⚠️ Quick Tips
- **Sun Protection**: Wear reef-safe sunscreen and hats (reefs are sensitive).  
- **Waterproof Gear**: Rent snorkel gear locally if you don’t have your own.  
- **Taxis**: Use Uber or taxis for short trips to Charlotte Amalie (e.g., Magens Bay).
