Here's a detailed packing list split into two segments, prioritizing warmth, moisture management, and nighttime readiness:

**For the 20-Mile Day Segment (Daytime, Aid Stations Available):**
1.  **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking synthetic or merino wool top & bottom (avoid cotton).
2.  **Mid-Layer:** Fleece jacket or light down vest/jacket (adjustable layers are key).
3.  **Outer Layer:** Windproof & water-resistant shell jacket (lightweight packable).
4.  **Legs:** Quick-dry running tights or shorts with liner shorts underneath.
5.  **Footwear:** Trail running shoes with excellent grip for hills.
6.  **Hands:** Lightweight running gloves (consider fleece for cooler moments).
7.  **Head:** Light cap or beanie (for sun/wind, easily removable).
8.  **Extras:** Small running pack or vest (to carry extra layers if needed), Buff/Gaiter (neck/wrists), Extra socks (moisture-wicking), Sunglasses.
9.  **Essential:** Headlamp with extra batteries (handheld is ideal).

**For the Final 12-Mile Night Segment (Cold, No Aid Station - You *Are* the Aid):**
1.  **Base Layer:** **Thick** moisture-wicking synthetic or merino wool top & bottom (long-sleeve/long-legs recommended).
2.  **Mid-Layer:** **Thick** fleece jacket (e.g., 100% fleece, 200gsm+) or lightweight down vest/jacket.
3.  **Outer Layer:** **Insulated** and **Waterproof** shell jacket (e.g., 3-in-1 jacket with puffy vest inside, or dedicated insulated/windproof/waterproof jacket).
4.  **Legs:** **Insulated** tights (fleece-lined or with thermal lining), or long pants with thermal liner. Consider tights over pants for easier layering.
5.  **Feet:** Trail running shoes with excellent grip. Bring **extra warm socks** (wool/synthetic blend, possibly with thermal layer) in your vest/pack.
6.  **Hands:** **Heavyweight** insulated gloves or mittens.
7.  **Head:** **Warm** hat or balaclava (essential for heat retention).
8.  **Extras:** Small running pack/Vest (to carry layers/socks), Buff/Gaiter (face/wrists), Extra batteries (for headlamp!), Traction aid (e.g., microspikes - *highly recommended* for icy/hilly night conditions).
9.  **Essential:** **Powerful** headlamp with **lots of extra batteries** (or spare handheld light). Consider an extra light source.

**Key Considerations for Both Segments:**

*   **Layers are Crucial:** Dress in layers you can easily add or remove *while running*. Avoid bulky items that slow you down.
*   **Moisture Management:** Wet clothes = cold and chills. Merino/wool is your best friend. Change socks if feet get wet.
*   **Visibility:** A reflective vest or brightly colored top is essential, especially at night.
*   **Traction:** Ice/slick conditions are likely at night. Microspikes are highly recommended for the final 12 miles.
*   **Test Gear:** Try on everything before race day. Ensure gloves fit over your watch/harness, hats fit under helmets, etc.
*   **Weather Check:** Monitor the forecast overnight and adjust layers accordingly. It will be significantly colder by the finish.
*   **Pack:** A small, packable vest or pack is essential for carrying extra layers, socks, and batteries during the run.

This list ensures you're warm, dry, visible, and ready for the demands of both pacing segments, especially the cold, dark finish. Good luck to your husband!
