Okay, let me clarify that for you. The standard Boulevardier is actually one and a quarter ounces of whiskey, one ounce of vermouth, and one ounce of Campari. The equal-parts version, with one ounce of each, is a very popular variation. The drink you mentioned, Last Word, is a completely different cocktail. It uses equal parts of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. Each of those is three-quarters of an ounce. So, to recap: Boulevardier is whiskey, vermouth, Campari; Last Word is gin, Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime. I hope that helps you decide!
