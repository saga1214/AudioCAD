Dehydrating is a fantastic way to preserve summer's bounty. You can make delicious snacks, teas, and add flavor to other dishes. Here are some ideas. First, fruits. You can make apple chips, banana chips, mango slices, peach slices, and even dried cranberries or cherries. Next, vegetables. You can make thin apple slices for a low-sugar alternative to chips, thin zucchini or yellow squash slices, and even thin onion rings. You could also try thicker onion wedges or bell pepper strips. Third, herbs. It's best to use them fresh and only dehydrate them for a short time, usually just one to two hours at a low temperature, to preserve their flavor. Think about parsley, basil, mint, dill, rosemary, oregano, thyme, and chives. Finally, you can also dehydrate things like chili peppers, garlic, onions, mushrooms, and even fresh herbs and flowers for teas and garnishes.
