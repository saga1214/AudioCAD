This is a fantastic question. If you are an author and an artist, you have a significant advantage. While publishers do hire illustrators, they are always looking for one-stop solutions, and a finished, high-quality book with both text and art is very appealing.

However, there are a couple of things to keep in mind. First, your illustrations need to be exceptionally professional. They should meet the exacting standards of a children's book publisher, which means they need to be technically perfect and tell the story just as effectively as your words do.

Second, when you submit your work, you'll need to be very clear about your role. For example, in your query letter, you should state that you are seeking representation for your children's book manuscript and that you have completed the illustrations for it. This is a very strong statement.

And to your last point, yes, publishers absolutely will consider a fully illustrated manuscript. In fact, they often find it more compelling. The main thing is to make sure your work is the best it can possibly be.
