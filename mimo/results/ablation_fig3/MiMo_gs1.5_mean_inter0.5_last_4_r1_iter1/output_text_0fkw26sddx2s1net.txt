### **Important Notes**
1.  **Label Checks:** **Crucially, check the ingredient lists on EVERY packaged item (Soy Sauce, Mirin, Miso, Dashi, Dashi Substitute, Stir-fry Sauce, Rice).** Many contain additives derived from legumes, soy, or shellfish. Look for "legume-free," "soy-free," or "shellfish-free" if available, or carefully scrutinize the list.
2.  **Chicken Marinade:** Marinate the chicken for at least 30 minutes (or up to a few hours) in the teriyaki mixture (soy sauce, mirin, sugar, ginger, garlic) for maximum flavor.
3.  **Stir-Frying:** Cook the chicken teriyaki first, then set aside. Stir-fry the vegetables quickly, add the cooked chicken back in at the end, toss with a splash of soy sauce, and finish with sesame oil and ginger/garlic. Keep the sauce light.
4.  **Steaming Rice:** Rinse the sticky rice well before cooking. Use a standard rice-to-water ratio (e.g., 1:1.25 for jasmine). Cover tightly and steam for 15-20 minutes after initial boiling.
5.  **Coconut Milk for Rice:** Simmer the coconut milk with a pinch of sugar and salt until slightly thickened, then mix into the cooked sticky rice.
6.  **Matcha Ice Cream:** Whisk the matcha powder into the warm cream base until completely smooth. Chill thoroughly before freezing. Stir every 30-60 minutes for the first 4 hours to prevent ice crystals. Wrap in plastic wrap touching the surface.
