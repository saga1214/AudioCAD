That's a wonderful goal—creating meaningful gifts together while respecting each age's abilities! Here are some simple, low-supervision craft ideas tailored to different ages, all designed to feel personal and festive:

### 🌿 **For Toddlers (Ages 2-5): Sensory & Sticker Crafts**
- **Fingerprint Snowflakes:** Dip fingers in white paint and press onto paper; let them add glitter or stickers for snowflakes.  
- **Sticker-Only Ornaments:** Pre-print plain ornaments; let them place stickers/sequins freely.  
- **Pom-Pom Garland:** Use pipe cleaners, yarn, and pom-poms to make a festive garland.  

### 🧵 **For Elementary (Ages 6-10): Hands-On Assembly**
- **Macramé Plant Hanger (Simplified):** Pre-cut cords into sections. Kids focus on knotting (simple loops, square knots) under your watchful eye. *Keep it small!*  
- **Cookie Decorating Station:**  
  - **Easy Dough:** Use pre-baked sugar cookie dough (store-bought or pre-baked) to skip rolling.  
  - **Stations:**  
    - *Decorating:* Frosting, sprinkles, edible glitter.  
    - *Assembly:* Small cookie cutters for shapes.  
    - *Taste Testers:* Let them try before packing!  
- **Pine Cone Reindeer:** Glue googly eyes + yarn antlers to pine cones.  

### 👨‍🍳 **For Teens (Ages 11-15): Creative Independence**
- **Personalized Cookie Designs:** Let them design cookies with icing letters, mini fondant figures, or themed colors.  
- **DIY Holiday Card Book:** Decorate blank cards with pressed flowers, stamps, or drawings.  
- **Macramé Keychains:** Use short cords to make simple keychains (great for their own keys or as gifts).  

### 🎁 **Family-Friendly All-Ages Projects**
- **Ornament Exchange:** Each person decorates one ornament for every family member. Kids get to choose themes (e.g., animals, stars, initials).  
- **Felted Wool Ornaments:** Use pre-cut wool shapes + felt fabric scraps to create soft ornaments.  
- **Glow-in-the-Dark Stars:** Paint paper stars with glow paint; let them cut out shapes.  

### 💡 **Key Tips for Success**
1. **Prep Ahead:** Cut cords, pre-mix dough, print patterns, or set up stations.  
2. **Assign Roles:**  
   - *Toddlers:* Sprinkles, stickers, glue.  
   - *Kids:* Painting, cutting (with safety scissors), assembling.  
   - *Teens:* Supervised glue gun, detailed work.  
3. **Embrace Imperfection:** Handmade charm lies in the "oops" moments!  
4. **Backup Gifts:** Pre-make a few items (like simple ornaments) if time runs short.  

### 🎯 **Why This Works**
- **Meaningful:** Gifts show effort and love.  
- **Low-Stress:** Minimal cleanup, maximum fun.  
- **Inclusive:** All ages feel useful and celebrated.  

Example Timeline:  
- *Weeks Before:* Prep macramé cords, order cookie dough.  
- *Day Before:* Set up cookie decorating stations.  
- *Christmas Eve:* Kids assemble ornaments while you wrap gifts.
