Okay, you're tackling stir-fry! That's a great choice. Let's make sure the noodle part is a success this time. Here’s how to do it:

First, use metal or ceramic pots, never wood! That's the key lesson from the smoke alarm incident.

Second, salt your water well. It makes the noodles taste better and helps them not stick together.

Third, and this is the most important step, stir the noodles constantly and vigorously once they're in the water. You want to get them moving around as soon as they start to soften.

Fourth, drain them thoroughly in a colander. Don't just leave them sitting in the pot.

Finally, and this is crucial, do not rinse the noodles. The starch on their surface is what helps them cling to the sauce in the stir-fry.

If you do all that, you should have perfectly cooked noodles. Good luck!
