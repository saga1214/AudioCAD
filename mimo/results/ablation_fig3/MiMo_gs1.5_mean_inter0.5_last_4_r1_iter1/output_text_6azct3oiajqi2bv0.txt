To attach something securely to your jacket lining without damaging the fabric or lining, here are the best methods:

### 🧵 1. **Fabric Glue (Best for Permanence)**
   - **Use**: Strong fabric glue like **E6000** or **Aleene’s Fabric Fusion** (dries clear and flexible).
   - **Steps**:
     1. Clean the jacket surface with rubbing alcohol.
     2. Apply a thin layer of glue to the item and press firmly.
     3. Hold for 30 seconds, then weigh down with a book for 1–2 hours.
   - **Tip**: Test on an inconspicuous area first!

### 🧵 2. **Double-Sided Tape (Lowest Risk)**
   - **Use**: **Clear, washable fabric tape** (e.g., Scotch Magic Tape).
   - **Steps**:
     1. Peel off the backing from one side.
     2. Press the sticky side onto the jacket lining.
     3. Press the item onto the tape.
   - **Why it works**: Reversible if mistakes happen, and won’t damage fabric.

### 🔗 3. **Safety Pins (Temporary Fix)**
   - **Use**: Flat-head safety pins (e.g., from a sewing kit).
   - **Steps**:
     1. Insert pins through the jacket lining and item, pressing firmly.
     2. Avoid sliding pins on slippery fabric—use a fabric clipper or seam ripper to create a small anchor hole first.
   - **Caution**: Use sparingly to avoid tearing.

### ❌ **Avoid These**
   - **Ironing**: Heat can melt synthetic linings (nylon, polyester).
   - **Normal Sewing**: Hand-stitching is fine if you’re careful, but machine sewing risks snagging the lining.
