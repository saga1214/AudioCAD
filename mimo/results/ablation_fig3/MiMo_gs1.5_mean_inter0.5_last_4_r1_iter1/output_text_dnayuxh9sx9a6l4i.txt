Based on your criteria (easy to train, good with kids/dogs, low prey drive, manageable size/noise, hybrid/purebred options), here are excellent dog breed suggestions:

### Top Purebred Options:
1. **Labrador Retriever**  
   - *Why*: Highly trainable, friendly, low prey drive, great with kids.  
   - *Size*: 55–80 lbs (slightly large but often fits "not too big").  
   - *Note*: Moderate shedding; needs daily walks and mental stimulation.

2. **Golden Retriever**  
   - *Why*: Gentle, sociable, eager to please, excellent with children.  
   - *Size*: 55–75 lbs.  
   - *Note*: High energy; requires regular exercise and training.

3. **French Bulldog**  
   - *Why*: Low energy, affectionate, minimal barking, no chasing instincts.  
   - *Size*: 18–28 lbs (ideal for apartments).  
   - *Note*: Prone to overheating; avoid hot climates.

4. **Pug**  
   - *Why*: Calm, playful, good with kids, minimal barking.  
   - *Size*: 14–18 lbs.  
   - *Note*: Short lifespan; prone to snoring/back issues.

### Great Hybrid/Mixed Breeds:
5. **Bichon Poodle Mix** (e.g., Bichon Frise + Standard Poodle)  
   - *Why*: Low-shedding, hypoallergenic, adaptable, low prey drive.  
   - *Size*: 8–15 lbs.  
   - *Note*: Energetic but manageable; needs grooming.

6. **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel**  
   - *Why*: Affectionate, calm, good with kids/dogs, minimal barking.  
   - *Size*: 13–18 lbs.  
   - *Note*: Prone to heart issues; avoid high-stress environments.

7. **Miniature Schnauzer**  
   - *Why*: Trainable, alert but not excessive barking, good with kids.  
   - *Size*: 11–20 lbs.  
   - *Note*: Sheds less; needs regular grooming.

### Key Considerations:
- **Training**: All these breeds respond well to positive reinforcement. Start early with obedience classes.  
- **Temperament**: Adopt from rescues/shelters for adult dogs to ensure they match your criteria.  
- **Health**: Research breed-specific issues (e.g., French Bulldogs need brachycephalic surgery; Pugs need hip checks).  
- **Size/Noise**: French Bulldogs, Pugs, and Cavaliers are ideal for high-rises with minimal space.  
- **Shedding**: Frenchies, Pugs, and Bichon mixes shed less than Labs/Goldens.  

### Next Steps:
1. **Visit Local Shelters**: Look for adult dogs matching these traits.  
2. **Breed-Specific Rescues**: Contact groups like "French Bulldog Rescue" or "Cavalier Rescue."  
3. **Trial Period**: Borrow a dog for a weekend to test compatibility.  
4. **Puppy Classes**: Enroll early for socialization and training.
