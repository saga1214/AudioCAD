Oh, no, cheese wasn't working? That's okay, not all dogs like the same things. You could try other small, soft, and tasty treats like peanut butter—just make sure it's xylitol-free—or maybe some cooked chicken bits. If she’s into sweet things, a tiny piece of apple or a blueberry could also work. For liver, freeze-dried beef liver is usually a huge hit, or even chicken liver. Just try to find the one that she seems most excited about. The most important thing is that she enjoys it!
