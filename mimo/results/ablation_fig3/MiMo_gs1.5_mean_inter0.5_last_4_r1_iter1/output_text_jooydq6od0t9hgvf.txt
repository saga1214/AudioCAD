Here are quick, elegant recipes for both cocktails using your ingredients:

### 🍸 1. Vodka Mixer (Refreshing & Light)
**Recipe:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice  
- 1 oz lime juice  
- 2 oz chilled sparkling wine (Prosecco or Cava) or soda water  
- *Garnish:* Grapefruit twist  

**How to:**  
Shake vodka, grapefruit juice, and lime juice with ice. Strain into a flute, top with sparkling wine/soda, and garnish.  
*Why it works:* Light, citrusy, and sophisticated—perfect for mixed diets.

### 🇦🇷 2. Argentine Fernet (Spicy & Herbal)
**Recipe:**  
- 2 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 1 oz simple syrup (equal parts sugar + water, heated)  
- 2 oz cola (soda-style, not syrup)  
- *Garnish:* Mint sprig + lime wedge  

**How to:**  
Shake Fernet, lemon juice, and syrup with ice. Strain into a rocks glass filled with ice. Top with cola. Stir gently and garnish.  
*Why it works:* Cola balances Fernet’s bitterness, while mint adds freshness. Adjust syrup to taste.

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Prep Ahead:** Mix simple syrup, juice, and pre-chill glasses.  
- **Allergy Safe:** Both cocktails soda-free.  
- **Serving:** Offer a small pitcher of the vodka spritz + individual Fernet glasses.
