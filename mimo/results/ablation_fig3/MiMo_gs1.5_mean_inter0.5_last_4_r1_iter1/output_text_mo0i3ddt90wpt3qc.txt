You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which are great gluten-free choices. I have a couple of other ideas for you.

First, trail mix is a classic. You can make your own by combining gluten-free nuts like almonds and cashews with seeds like pumpkin seeds, and maybe some gluten-free dried fruit like cranberries.

Second, rice cakes are another simple option. You can pair them with gluten-free peanut butter or avocado for an easy snack.
