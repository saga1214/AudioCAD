**Glue Gun Troubleshooting:**
1.  **Check the Bulb:** Is the heating element glowing red? If not, it's not hot enough. Try letting it run for 5-10 minutes longer.
2.  **Check the Bulb Type:** Are you using the correct bulb for your gun? Some guns require specific wattage. If it's not heating, it might need replacement.
3.  **Check the Nozzle:** Is the tip clean? Sometimes debris blocks the heat flow. Wipe it out carefully with a pencil eraser or a soft cloth.
4.  **Check the Power:** Is it plugged in securely? Try a different outlet if possible.
5.  **Let it Rest:** If it's overheating and *not* working, unplug it, let it cool completely (15-20 mins), then try again.

**Spooky Globe Enhancements (While You Wait):**

Here are some ideas to make those globes extra creepy, incorporating your suggestions:

1.  **Masquerade Masks & Flowers:**
    *   **Glowing Masks:** Attach small, battery-operated LED tea lights *underneath* the mask (if possible) for an eerie glow from within.
    *   **"Bloody" Flowers:** Dip the dark-colored flower stems or petals in clear nail polish mixed with a drop of red paint (or use fake "blood" glue) for a sinister touch.
    *   **Thorny Accents:** Add tiny, faux thorns around the base of the flower stems or on the mask frame.

2.  **Cobwebs & Spiders:**
    *   **"Dripping" Web:** Use thin, black, clear fishing line or nylon thread to create a web *around* the globe base. Drape some black lace or netting over it to look like torn cobwebs. Add tiny, fake spiders glued to the web and lace.
    *   **Web Inside:** If you can access the inside (carefully!), glue a small piece of black tulle or netting to the top of the pumpkin to create a subtle web effect against the fairy lights.

3.  **Gothic Details:**
    *   **Mini Skulls or Bones:** Glue tiny plastic or resin skulls, vertebrae, or finger bones around the base or attached to the pumpkin stem.
    *   **"Blood" Drips:** Use clear glue mixed with red paint to create "dripping" blood effects on the glass, base, or lace. Let dry completely.
    *   **Mini Scarecrow Elements:** Add tiny, black plastic grass tufts around the base. Glue a miniature, creepy doll head to the pumpkin stem.
    *   **"Skeletal" Hands:** Attach tiny, faux skeletal hands (plastic or resin) reaching up from the base.

4.  **Textured Spooky Base:**
    *   **Mossy Roots:** Use faux moss or small clumps of real moss glued to the base to look like gnarled roots.
    *   **Weathered Wood:** If the base is glass, carefully glue small pieces of black, faux weathered wood around the rim for a tombstone effect.
    *   **Mini Graveyard:** Create tiny gravestones (black cardstock cut into tombstone shapes, maybe with etched names) and glue them around the base.

**While You Wait for the Glue Gun:**

*   **Prep:** Wash the glass and pumpkins thoroughly. Cut out lace, netting, fishing line, or cobweb materials to size.
*   **Gather:** Collect all your small decorations (spiders, skulls, flowers, etc.).
