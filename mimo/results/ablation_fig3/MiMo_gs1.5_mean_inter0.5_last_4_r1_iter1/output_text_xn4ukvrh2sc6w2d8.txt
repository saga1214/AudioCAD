First, the Old Fashioned. You'll need two ounces of bourbon or rye, one sugar cube, or you can just use three-quarters of an ounce of simple syrup. Then add two to three dashes of Angostura bitters and an orange twist for the garnish. To make it, just muddle the sugar cube with a little bitters and water, add the bourbon, stir it well with ice, and serve it in a rocks glass over fresh ice. You can garnish it with an orange twist or a lemon peel.

Next up is the Paper Plane. For this, you'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of Aperol, half an ounce of Amaro Nonino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Just shake all those ingredients with ice, strain them into a coupe glass, and you can garnish it with a lemon twist.

For the Whiskey Sour, you'll need two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. If you want that frothy texture, you can also add half an egg white. To make it, you'll dry shake everything without ice first to get it frothy, then add ice and shake again, and serve it over fresh ice in a rocks glass. Garnish with a cherry or an orange slice.

Then there's the Aviation cocktail. You'll need two ounces of gin, half an ounce of maraschino liqueur, a quarter-ounce of crème de violette, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Shake that with ice, strain it into a coupe glass, and garnish with a cherry.

And for the third gin cocktail, the French 75. You'll need one and a half ounces of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice, half an ounce of simple syrup, and two ounces of Champagne or sparkling wine. Shake the gin, lemon juice, and syrup together without ice first, then add ice and shake again. Strain it into a champagne flute and top it with the sparkling wine. You can garnish it with a lemon twist.
