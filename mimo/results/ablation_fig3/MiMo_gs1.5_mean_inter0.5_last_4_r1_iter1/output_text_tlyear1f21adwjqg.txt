That's a tough one because it really depends on the location and specific needs. Solar is great for widespread use and getting cheaper, wind is strong in windy areas, and hydropower is very reliable. If I had to pick just one as the most efficient overall, I'd still lean towards solar energy. The reason is that it’s the most accessible and scalable, and its cost efficiency is improving so quickly. But really, the best answer is that a mix of these sources, tailored to the local environment, is often the most efficient strategy for a stable energy system.
