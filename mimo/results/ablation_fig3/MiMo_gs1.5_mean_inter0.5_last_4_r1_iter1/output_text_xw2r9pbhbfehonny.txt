Okay, here’s a simple checklist for filming your show ahead of time.

First, make sure you have permission from the facility, like Sunnyside Assisted Living, before you even start recording. That way, you’re not violating any privacy policies.

Next, just set up your phone or whatever camera you’re using. It’s a good idea to test the lighting and the angle beforehand, so you’re not distracted during the show.

Also, think about the storage—make sure your phone or camera has enough space, or maybe bring a spare battery.

And finally, just tell the audience, “Hey, we’re recording this for fun, so don’t worry about the cameras!” It helps keep the mood light.

So, short and sweet: get permission, test the setup, check storage, and announce it. That’s it.
