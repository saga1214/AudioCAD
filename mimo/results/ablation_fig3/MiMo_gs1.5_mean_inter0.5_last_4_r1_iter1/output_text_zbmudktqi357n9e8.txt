Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn inspiration! The best time for that would be late September to mid-November, which is their peak fall season. The weather is perfect—cool, crisp, and sunny. You’ll see all the trees changing color, especially around the Yucatan Peninsula and Chiapas. The landscapes will be just as vibrant as your palette!

And for your vacation, if you want to paint while you're there, I'd suggest packing a portable watercolor set and some sketchbooks. It’s a great way to capture those moments. Just remember to bring a small cloth to clean your brushes and some water to keep them wet. You’ll be amazed at how much inspiration you can gather from just one walk through the streets of a Mexican town. Enjoy your trip, and happy painting!
