Based on your situation, here are some practical solutions to get you that strong latte *right now* without going out:

### For Instant Coffee:
1. **Make a "Coffee Shot"**:  
   Dissolve 1–2 teaspoons of instant coffee in **1 cup of hot water** (not boiling) in a small cup. Stir well. This creates a concentrated base.
2. **Layer Milk & Coffee**:  
   Microwave your milk in a small bowl (1–2 cups) until steaming (about 1–2 minutes). Pour the warm milk into your coffee shot, then stir. The result is a smooth, creamy latte.

### For Machine-Brewed Coffee:
- If your coffee machine is nearby, brew a strong cup (use 2x the usual grounds). Skip the milk until the coffee is ready—then heat the milk separately.

### Milk Alternative:
- **Full-fat milk** is ideal for creaminess. If Cornish milk is too pricey, stick with full-fat regular milk.  
- *Tip:* If using semi-skimmed, add a spoon of heavy cream or a splash of milk powder for richness.

### Weather Workaround:
- **Stay indoors!** Use your setup:  
  → Microwave milk + instant coffee.  
  → Or make a quick espresso-style latte with instant espresso powder (if you have it).  
  → Pre-made instant latte kits (e.g., Starbucks-style) are also an option.

### Final Advice:
