### For Tailoring:
1. **Premium Fabric Shears**  
   *Why:* Sharp, precise shears are essential for clean cuts. Brands like Gingher or Kai are industry standards.  
   *Example:* A 6" or 8" pair for cutting lightweight fabrics or linens.

2. **Vintage-Inspired Supplies**  
   *Why:* Connects to your fountain pen hobby. Try a vintage-style brass thimble, a hand-stitched leather sewing kit, or a Parker-style pen opener for fabric.

3. **Tailored Fabric Bundle**  
   *Why:* High-quality cotton poplin or linen for simple projects (like tote bags or pillowcases). Brands like Spoon & Pork or Robert Kaufman offer beginner-friendly prints.

### For Woodworking:
1. **Small Project Kit**  
   *Why:* Affordable entry point. Look for kits like a wooden box, cutting board, or birdhouse with pre-cut pieces and instructions.  
   *Budget:* $20–$50.

2. **Essential Hand Tools**  
   *Why:* Invest in a quality wood rasp, sanding block, or set of chisels. Woodcraft or Lee Valley tools are reliable.

3. **Workshop Storage**  
   *Why:* A compact rolling cart or pegboard to organize tools. Keeps your workspace efficient.

### For Clay/Pottery:
1. **Pottery Starter Kit**  
   *Why:* A basic wheel (electric or manual), small bags of clay, and a leather-hard sculpting tool set.  
   *Example:* A 12" electric wheel like the Kestrel 300 (around $300) or a hand-powered option.

2. **Sculpting Supplies**  
   *Why:* If not using a wheel, a clay extruder, rib tool, and wire mesh for armatures.

### Cross-Hobby Gift Ideas:
- **Skill-Boosting Books:**  
  *Tailoring:* "The Tailor’s Art" by Elizabeth Haywood.  
  *Woodworking:* "The Complete Manual of Woodworking" by N. Charles Beaulieu.  
  *Clay:* "Pottery for Beginners" by Nancy Goss.
- **Gift Cards:**  
  Local craft stores (e.g., Michaels, Joann) or online (Etsy for handmade tools, Woodcraft for woodworking).
- **Experience Voucher:**  
  A workshop class (e.g., "Intro to Sewing" or "Pottery Wheel Basics").
