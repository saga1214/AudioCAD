That sounds like a wonderful getaway! Since you love the ocean and seafood, here are some creative water-based activities for your coastal weekend, keeping your partner's seasickness in mind:

### For Calm & Predictable Water Experiences (Best for Seasickness)
1.  **Sunset Bay Cruise:** Choose a cruise specifically marketed for calm waters (like a bay tour or harbor tour). Opt for smaller, stable boats (catamarans or trawlers often roll less) and ensure it's on a calm day. The stunning sunset views make it inherently romantic.
2.  **Kayak & Snorkel Combo:** Paddle calmly in a protected cove or lagoon (like those near coral reefs or seagrass beds). The gentle motion of paddling is usually manageable, and snorkeling provides a beautiful, stationary underwater perspective. Pack a small seasick bag just in case!
3.  **Lunch or Dinner Cruise:** Many coastal towns offer short (1-2 hour) narrated lunch or dinner cruises on stable boats. You get the water experience *and* great food without the long journey stress. Perfect for a romantic meal with a view.
4.  **Ferry to a Nearby Island:** Take the ferry to a small, picturesque island known for its calm bays. Explore the island, have a picnic by the water, and take a *short* return ferry trip back at sunset. Minimizes total water time.

### For More Active (But Still Manageable) Water Fun
5.  **Stand-Up Paddleboarding (SUP) in a Protected Bay:** Look for calm, sheltered bays with gentle waves. Paddling requires focus and balance, which can be a great distraction from any potential seasickness. Choose a spot with soft sand or a launch platform.
6.  **Fishing Charter (Calm Waters):** Book a fishing trip specifically on calm days. The motion of a stable fishing boat (like a trawler or catamaran) is often less intense than a ferry. Focus on the shared activity of fishing.
7.  **Beachcombing & Shell Hunting:** Pack baskets and explore tide pools on the shore. You get the coastal atmosphere and water connection without any boat time at all! Look for unique shells and sea glass.

### Romantic Water Experiences (Focus on the Setting)
8.  **Private Beach Picnic:** Find a secluded spot on a public beach or a private one near your accommodation. Pack a gourmet picnic and enjoy the sound of the water.
9.  **Beachside Dining:** Many coastal towns have restaurants right on the water. Enjoy fresh seafood while watching the waves roll in or a sunset sail past.
10. **Sunset Sail (Short & Sweet):** If you *must* take a boat, choose the shortest possible sunset cruise (30-60 mins) on a boat known for stability. The reward of a stunning sunset is worth the minimal risk.

### Key Considerations for Your Partner
*   **Choose the Calmest Day Possible:** Always check weather forecasts and tide charts. Avoid rough seas.
*   **Boat Type Matters:** Catamarans, trawlers, and stable motor yachts often roll less than smaller ferries or catamarans in choppy conditions.
*   **Short Trips Win:** Limit water time on boats to 30-90 minutes max.
*   **Pack Seasick Bags:** Just in case! Even the calmest day can have unexpected waves.
*   **Hydrate & Eat Lightly:** Avoid heavy meals and excessive alcohol before any boat trip.
*   **Have a Backup Plan:** Always have a land-based plan (beach day, restaurant, museum) ready if the weather or seas are too rough for water activities.

**Most importantly:** Focus on the *shared experience* and the beautiful scenery. The ocean setting is inherently romantic, and enjoying fresh seafood together by the water is always a winner!
