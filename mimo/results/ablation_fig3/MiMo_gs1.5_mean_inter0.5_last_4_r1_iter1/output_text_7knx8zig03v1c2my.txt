Great questions! Here's a breakdown of treats and clicker training:

### Treat Recommendations:
1. **High-Value Options**:  
   - **Chicken**: Tiny pieces are perfect for training (great for science nerds!).  
   - **Beef**: Another lean, tasty option.  
   - **Cheese**: Harder to digest, but small bits work well.  
   - **Hot Dogs**: Cut into tiny pieces.  
   - **Table Scraps**: Avoid onions, garlic, or chocolate.  

2. **Avoid**:  
   - Table scraps with strong flavors (e.g., garlic, onions, salt).  
   - High-fat foods (can cause pancreatitis).  

---

### Clicker Training Explained:  
**What it is**: A training tool that uses a **clicker** (or any marker sound like a clicker, pen click, or even a "Yes!") to bridge the gap between behavior and reward.  

**How it works**:  
1. **Pair the Click**: Click → immediately give a treat.  
2. **Teach the Behavior**:  
   - Show a toy (e.g., "Sit").  
   - Click *instantly* when he sits.  
   - Give a treat.  
3. **Repeat**: Practice until he associates the click with a reward.  

**Why it works**:  
- The click is a **neutral sound** that becomes a "language bridge."  
- It marks the *exact* moment he does what you want.  
- Rewards follow the click, not the behavior.  

**Example**:  
- *Command*: "Sit"  
- *Action*: Puppy sits  
- *Click* → *Treat* → Repeat!  

---

### Pro Tips for Success:  
1. **Consistency**: Use the same marker sound every time.  
2. **Timing**: Click within 1-2 seconds of the desired behavior.  
3. **Fade Rewards**: After 10+ successful sessions, reduce treats to occasional rewards.  

With clicker training, your puppy learns *exactly* what you want! It builds trust and makes training fun. 🐾
