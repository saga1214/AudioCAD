Okay, let's see where you are in the process. You've already removed the hardware and sanded the cabinets, which is great. Before you apply the primer, the most important thing is to clean the sanded areas again. Any dust from the sanding will prevent the primer from sticking properly, so a good wipe-down is crucial.

If you have any gaps or holes from the sanded areas, you should fill those with wood filler now. Let it dry completely.

Once those areas are clean and filled, you're ready for the primer. Just remember to use the high-quality, stain-blocking primer we talked about, especially since you're going from a dark color to a light one.
