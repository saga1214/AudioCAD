Here's a full recap of tasty homemade and store-bought treats for your corgi princess:

### 🏠 Homemade Treats (Always check ingredients & keep portions tiny!)
1. **Frozen Bites:**  
   - Mashed pumpkin or sweet potato patted into small, flat discs. Freeze for a cool, refreshing treat.  
   - *Bonus:* Add a tiny bit of Greek yogurt for extra flavor (no xylitol!).  
2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies:**  
   - Mix peanut butter (check label for xylitol!) with rolled oats.  
   - Roll into small balls or flatten into discs. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-15 mins until firm.  
3. **Cooked Chicken/Carrot Sticks:**  
   - Steam or boil small pieces of plain chicken breast or carrot sticks.  
   - *Great for:* Low-calorie training or dental chews.

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands (Look for natural, limited ingredients)
1. **Blue Buffalo** (Training Bites, Life Protection)  
2. **Wellness** (Treats, Mini Training Bites)  
3. **Zuke's** (Mini Nibbles, SmartBites)  
4. **Greenies** (Dental chews - check size for corgis)  
5. **Merrick** (Training Bites, Healthy Snacks)  

### 📌 Key Reminders:
- **Safety First:** Avoid xylitol (found in sugar-free products), onions, garlic, grapes, and chocolate.  
- **Portion Control:** Treats should be 10% or less of her daily calories.  
- **Training:** Use tiny pieces for high-value rewards during training.  
- **Freeze for Pup:** Most homemade treats freeze well for longer shelf life.  

Your corgi princess will love these—just keep them safe and delicious! 🐾💕
