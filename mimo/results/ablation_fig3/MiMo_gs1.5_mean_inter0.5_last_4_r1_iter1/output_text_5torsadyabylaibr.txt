Absolutely. For a Thanksgiving pie, a classic is a pumpkin pie. It's a very traditional choice. You can easily make it in a single pie dish in one pot, so there's no baking dish to wash afterward.

For a slightly different take, you could try a pecan pie. It's a bit more complex, but still manageable. You'd make a crust with flour and butter, then a simple filling of sugar, corn syrup, and spices. The main thing is to bake it until the edges are golden brown. It's a great way to show your appreciation.
