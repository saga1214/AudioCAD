For an end-of-year celebration, here's a practical approach balancing fun, budget, and logistics:

**1.  Location & Format:**
*   **Indoor is Safer & Easier:** Given your pregnancy, an indoor cafeteria or community center is ideal. It avoids weather worries (rain!), is easier to manage, and often has tables/chairs already available.
*   **Keep it Focused:** A 2-3 hour party format works best. Avoid overly long events.

**2.  Activities:**
*   **Simple & Fun:** Stick to low-cost, high-energy activities:
    *   **Talent Show:** Let the girls showcase skills (singing, dancing, magic, jokes).
    *   **Games:** Classic party games like musical chairs, Simon Says (Brownie theme!), or a simple scavenger hunt around the venue.
    *   **Decorate:** Provide plain paper plates, cups, or cookies for decorating.
    *   **Photo Booth:** Set up a corner with fun props (hats, glasses, signs) for silly photos.
*   **Avoid Complex Races:** While fun, races like three-legged or wheelbarrow can be chaotic to organize, require significant space, and might get messy. Save those for field trips.

**3.  Food & Drink (Key to Fun & Budget):**
*   **Focus on Treats:** Center the celebration around popular snacks everyone loves:
    *   **Sundaes Bar:** Ice cream scoops, sundaes, sprinkles, toppings (chocolate, caramel, nuts, whipped cream). *Highly recommended!*
    *   **Pizza:** A classic crowd-pleaser. Order a large pizza or two for the troop.
    *   **Ice Cream Cups:** A simpler alternative to sundaes if you prefer.
    *   **Fruit & Veggie Platter:** A healthier option alongside the main treats.
    *   **Water & Lemonade:** Essential refreshments.
*   **Parent Snack Duty:** **This is ideal!** Send a simple email to parents *well in advance*:
    *   "Help us celebrate! Please bring one of the following for the girls to enjoy:"
    *   **Option 1:** A serving of ice cream (scoops, cups, or a small tub of cups).
    *   **Option 2:** A serving of pizza (pre-sliced or whole).
    *   **Option 3:** A serving of fruit or veggie dip.
    *   **Option 4:** A small bag of chips or cookies (if you want variety).
    *   *Ask parents to label their contribution clearly.* This shares the cost, effort, and responsibility, and ensures everyone gets what they like!

**4.  Decorations:**
*   **Keep it Simple & Troop-Focused:** Brownie-themed decorations are perfect – streamers, balloons, signs saying "Great Job!" or "Happy Graduation!", maybe some craft supplies for quick decorations (like drawing names on paper plates).
*   **Parent Help:** Ask a couple of parents to help set up and tear down decorations.

**5.  Invitations & RSVP:**
*   **Simple Invite:** Send a fun, short invite (email or digital) confirming the date/time, location, and the *parent snack duty request* (clearly listing the options!). Mention it's a celebration for the girls' achievements.
*   **RSVP:** Essential for food planning and headcount. Keep it short.

**6.  Cost & Dues:**
*   **Dues:** If you have dues, explain they cover troop supplies (crafts, patches, field trip fees, maybe a small end-of-year banner). Be transparent about how the money is used.
*   **End-of-Year Party:** **This is usually a "troop treat" covered by the troop budget or shared by parents.** If using troop funds, keep it very simple (e.g., maybe a single pizza or ice cream tub if dues allow). **Strongly encourage the parent snack duty approach** to keep this event cost-neutral for the troop.

**Key Takeaways for Your Celebration:**

*   **Indoor Cafeteria:** Safer, easier, weather-proof.
*   **Sundaes & Pizza:** Guaranteed hits that cater to diverse tastes.
*   **Parent Snack Duty:** The *best* solution for food logistics, cost, and variety.
*   **Simple Activities:** Talent show, games, decorating.
*   **Parent Help:** Decorations, setup, cleanup.
*   **Focus on Celebration:** Acknowledge their hard work and achievements throughout the year!

This approach keeps the celebration manageable for you, maximizes fun for the girls, shares the workload with parents, and stays within budget. You've got this – it's going to be a great end to the year!
