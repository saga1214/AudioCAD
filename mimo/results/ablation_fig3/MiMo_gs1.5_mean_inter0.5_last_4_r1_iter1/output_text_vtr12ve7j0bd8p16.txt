You two are a fire sign power couple! For weekend adventures, Calgary is a fantastic base. You could head to Canmore and Banff, which are about an hour and a half's drive. There's the Banff Gondola for incredible views, or you could hike in Johnston Canyon. If you want something more rugged, the Icefields Parkway is a scenic drive that leads to Peyto Lake. For a more relaxed vibe, the Bow River Trail is a lovely spot for a walk or bike ride. And if you're feeling adventurous, you could even try a hot springs day trip to the town of Banff. Enjoy your date!
