Okay, since you're looking for warm, swim-friendly spots in Northern California with a healthy budget and a car, I have some fantastic options for you. Let's go through them.

First up is Carmel-by-the-Sea, along the coast near you. It's warm, sunny, and perfect for swimming at places like Carmel Beach or the Big Creek Nature Reserve. For packing, you'll want two swimsuits, a cover-up, shorts, tank tops, sandals, sunglasses, a hat, and some sunscreen. A light sweater is also a good idea for cooler evenings.

Next, consider the Pismo Beach area. It has beautiful beaches perfect for swimming and has great dining options. You could pack swimsuits, a cover-up, shorts or sundresses, flip-flops, a light jacket, sunglasses, and a hat. A great treat would be fresh seafood from a place like Neptune's Net.

A third option is the Lake Tahoe area, which is a bit further but has stunning scenery and warm lakes perfect for swimming. You'd pack two swimsuits, a cover-up, shorts, tank tops, flip-flops, a light jacket, sunglasses, and a hat.

For dinner or lunch, you could find a gluten-free bakery for a special cake, or check out restaurants in Carmel or Pismo that are known for their gluten-free accommodations.

Have a wonderful and relaxing trip!
