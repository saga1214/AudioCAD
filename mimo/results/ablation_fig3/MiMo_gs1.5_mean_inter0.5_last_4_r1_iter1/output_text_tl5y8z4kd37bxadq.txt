Here's a step-by-step approach to packing honey jars safely, plus some Austin dinner ideas for a laid-back start to your trip:

**Packing Honey Jars Safely:**
1.  **Pack Last:** Place jars in your suitcase *after* packing heavier items.
2.  **Maximize Cushioning:** Wrap each jar individually in soft clothing (like socks, t-shirts, or packing cubes).
3.  **Secure Lid Tightly:** Ensure lids are screwed on *very* securely. Consider adding a small rubber band or zip tie around the base of the lid for extra security.
4.  **Place in Center:** Put the cushioned jars in the *center* of the suitcase, surrounded by other soft items (clothing, towels, pillows).
5.  **Avoid Edges:** Never place jars near the top or bottom of the suitcase where they could slide out.
6.  **Consider Sturdy Boxes:** If bringing many jars, use sturdy boxes. Fill voids with crumpled paper. Double-boxing adds significant protection.

**Laid-Back Austin Dinner Spots (First Night Vibes):**

1.  **Veracruz All Natural (East Side):**
    *   **Food:** Legendary, no-frills Mexican seafood tacos (especially the *ceviche* tacos) and other great tacos. Very casual, walk-up counter or small tables.
    *   **Vibe:** Bustling, authentic, energetic but not stuffy. Expect a line, but it moves fast.
2.  **Terry's Blackened Fish (Downtown):**
    *   **Food:** Creative, locally-sourced seafood (often caught daily) served in a unique blackened style. Great appetizers too.
    *   **Vibe:** Casual, modern, bright, and lively. Relaxed atmosphere, good for groups.
3.  **Fonda San Miguel (Downtown):**
    *   **Food:** Upscale but *not* stuffy Mexican. Known for incredible handmade tortas, mole, and creative cocktails. Reservations recommended.
    *   **Vibe:** Warm, inviting, and stylishly casual. Great ambiance without being pretentious.
4.  **The Salt Lick (Downtown - Original Location):**
    *   **Food:** Classic Austin barbecue. Brisket, ribs, ribs, and more ribs. Simple, delicious, and iconic.
    *   **Vibe:** Casual, rustic, family-friendly. Expect a line, but it's part of the experience. Perfect for a relaxed, hearty meal.
5.  **Taco Deli (South Congress - SoCo):**
    *   **Food:** Massive, creative selection of tacos, tostadas, and burritos. Endless variety.
    *   **Vibe:** Fun, colorful, lively, and very casual. Great for trying lots of different options.
