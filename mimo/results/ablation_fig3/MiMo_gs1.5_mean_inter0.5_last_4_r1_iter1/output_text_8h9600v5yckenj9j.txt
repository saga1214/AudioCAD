### 💨 Low-Hanging Fog Machine Hack
**DIY Fog Chiller (Outdoors-Ready)**:  
1. **Build the cooler**:  
   - Find a **plastic storage bin or cooler** (e.g., 10-gallon).  
   - Cut a **hole in one side** for the fog machine nozzle.  
   - Cut a **hole on the opposite side** for fog exit.  

2. **Cool the fog**:  
   - Place a **wire rack** inside.  
   - Fill the bottom with **ice + frozen water bottles**.  
   - Position the fog machine nozzle to **blow mist into the cooler**.  
   - The fog exits the opposite hole, **sinking low due to cooling**.  

3. **Bonus: Freezer-Reuse Hack**:  
   - Freeze water bottles **ahead of time**.  
   - As fog melts bottles, **hand them out as candy cups**!
