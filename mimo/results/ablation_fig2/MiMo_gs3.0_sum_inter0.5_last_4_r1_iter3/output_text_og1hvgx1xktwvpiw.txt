The most common and original Boulevardier uses unequal parts, that's one and a quarter ounces of rye or bourbon, one ounce of sweet vermouth, and one ounce of Campari stirred over ice. Your friend's equal-parts recipe, all three spirits at one ounce each, is also traditional and very popular. The drink you mentioned, the Last Word, is the one that uses equal parts: three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. All three are great choices, so you can pick whichever one you're in the mood for. Enjoy!
