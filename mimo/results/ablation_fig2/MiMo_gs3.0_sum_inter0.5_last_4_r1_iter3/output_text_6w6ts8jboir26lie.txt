That's wonderful news about customer service! It's great to hear things are moving forward. Now, since you're ready to unwind in the kitchen, here are a few simple but delicious dinner ideas you can whip up:

1.  **Quick & Zesty Shrimp & Veggie Stir-Fry:** Grab some shrimp, bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas, and a bag of rice noodles. Sauté garlic and ginger, add your veggies, then toss in the shrimp and a simple sauce (like soy sauce, a little honey, and sesame oil). Serve over the noodles. Super fast and fresh!
2. Vacation Relaxation: Tomato Soup & Grilled Cheese: Sometimes simple is best. Grab a big bag of ripe tomatoes, some fresh basil, garlic, and a good loaf of bread. Make a big pot of creamy tomato soup (simmer the tomatoes with broth, garlic, and basil until thick). Grill some nice sandwiches with the soup. Pure comfort food.
3.  **Easy One-Pot Pasta:** A box of your favorite pasta, some cherry tomatoes, a can of white beans, garlic, spinach, and a jar of pesto. Cook the pasta, drain it, and toss it with the warm beans, sautéed garlic and spinach, halved cherry tomatoes, and a big spoonful of pesto. Serve with a simple salad.
4.  **Simple Salmon & Roasted Veggies:** A fillet of salmon, asparagus, broccoli, and cherry tomatoes. Toss the veggies with olive oil, salt, pepper, and maybe some dried herbs. Roast them at 400°F (200°C) for about 15-20 minutes. Bake the salmon on a sheet for the last 10-15 minutes. Quick, healthy, and satisfying.
5.  **Taco Bar Extravaganza:** Buy ground beef or chicken, corn tortillas, shredded cheese, salsa, lettuce, tomatoes, avocado, sour cream, and maybe some shredded cabbage or pickled onions. Make a big bowl of seasoned meat, set out all the toppings, and let yourself build the masterpiece! Fun and customizable.

Take your time in the kitchen – enjoy the process of creating something nice for yourself. It sounds like you truly deserve this break, and hopefully, the food will help you relax a little while you sort out the condo situation.
