Yeah, that’s a really good question! At rink temperatures around minus ten degrees Celsius, which is extremely cold, the coefficient of friction is incredibly low. Studies suggest it can be around 0.005 to 0.01. Some sources even say it can dip as low as 0.002 in certain ideal conditions. The key thing to remember is that it’s one of the lowest coefficients of friction known, allowing for that amazing glide and making every little push so effective for the skaters!
