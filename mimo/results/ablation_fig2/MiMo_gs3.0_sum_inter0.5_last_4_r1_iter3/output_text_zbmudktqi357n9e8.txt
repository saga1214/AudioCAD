Okay, Mexico in autumn is a fantastic idea for autumn scene inspiration and for developing watercolor techniques. You should definitely go during their dry season, which is roughly from November to April. That’s when the colors are at their most vibrant, especially in the countryside and along the coast. 

The peak foliage is usually from November to early December. That’s when the leaves on the trees turn the deepest shades of orange, red, and yellow. But, uh, late fall, like in December, can be a good balance. The crowds might be a little bigger, but the colors are still amazing. And the weather is warm, which is perfect for painting outdoors. Just, you know, bring some watercolor supplies, a portable easel if you have one, and just soak in all the inspiration you can. Mexico’s autumn landscapes are just... they're absolutely stunning for an aspiring artist. Have a great trip and happy painting!
