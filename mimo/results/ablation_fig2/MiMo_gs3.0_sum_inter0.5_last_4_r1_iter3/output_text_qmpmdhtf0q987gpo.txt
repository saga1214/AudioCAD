Okay, so you've done all the prep work and are just about ready to prime. A couple of last things to check. Before you prime, make sure the sanded cabinets are completely, one hundred percent dry.

Then, when you go to prime, make sure to use primer that's designed for painting cabinets or for covering dark colors. And make sure to apply it in multiple, even coats, rather than one thick one. And of course, after you've primed, let it dry completely before you start painting with your new color
