Alright, so before you film your own puppet show, here’s a quick checklist to make sure it goes smoothly and looks professional.

First, ask for permission. Assisted living places might have rules about filming, so you should confirm with the staff first. 

Second, set up your phone or camera on a stable surface, maybe on a tripod, so the angle is good. Make sure it’s pointed at the puppets, not the audience. 

Third, test the lighting. Natural light is usually fine, but if it’s dark, you might need a ring light or something simple to avoid shadows. 

Fourth, keep it short. Five to ten minutes is usually good for older adults, but you can adjust. 

Fifth, just be yourself. Your performance is the most important thing, so don’t stress too much about the filming. 

And last, save the file and back it up right after the show. 

That’s pretty much it. Good luck!
