### 1. **Color-Coding Icons & Mental Load**  
   - **Memory vs. Mental Load**: Color-coding primarily **reduces mental load** by creating visual shortcuts. Your brain doesn’t need to actively recall folder names—it recognizes colors instantly (e.g., blue for "work," green for "personal"). This frees up cognitive resources for deeper tasks.  
   - **Subtlety is Key**: 2-3 colors max work best. Too many colors create "visual noise," negating the benefit. Start with a simple system (e.g., blue = active projects, orange = reference, gray = archived).

### 2. **Audio Switching (Lo-Fi vs. Nature Sounds)**  
   - **Optimal Timing**: Switch every **45-60 minutes**—long enough to avoid disrupting focus, short enough to refresh the auditory environment.  
   - **Personal Preference**: Some people thrive with variety; others prefer consistency. If switching feels disruptive, stick to one type.  
   - **Pro Tip**: Use apps like *Noisli* or *Brain.fm* to blend genres or layer nature sounds with subtle lo-fi beats.

### 3. **Physical Documents & Progress**
