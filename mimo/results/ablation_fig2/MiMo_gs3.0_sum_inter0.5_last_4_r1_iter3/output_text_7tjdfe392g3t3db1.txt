Right, so whether you're using the tuner app or the clip-on tuner, the process is pretty much the same. You just... you know, you press a string down and then pluck it. The tuner will show you if the note is sharp, which means it's too high, or flat, which means it's too low.

Then you just adjust by turning the tuning peg on the headstock until the tuner shows you that the note is perfectly in tune. And you just repeat that for every single string!
