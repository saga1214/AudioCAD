Here are the three shrimp main courses I suggested, with ingredient lists:

1.  **Spicy Shrimp Scampi over Pasta:**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
        *   8 oz linguine or spaghetti
        *   4 tbsp butter
        *   4 cloves garlic, minced
        *   1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat)
        *   1/4 cup dry white wine (optional, but recommended)
        *   1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
        *   1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
        *   Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

2.  **Shrimp Fra Diavolo:**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
        *   1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
        *   4 cloves garlic, minced
        *   1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat - this is key for "diavolo" meaning devil)
        *   1/4 cup dry white wine (optional)
        Spicy Italian sausage, sliced (about 1/2 lb), optional but recommended for extra spice and texture
        Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

3.  **Cajun Shrimp and Grits:**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
        *   1 (16 oz) box quick-cooking grits (likeinstant or steel-cut)
        *   1 cup chicken or vegetable broth
        *   1/4 cup heavy cream
        *   1/4 cup grated sharp cheddar cheese
        *   1/4 cup chopped green onions
        *   1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
        *   1/4 cup mayonnaise (for creaminess, optional)
        *   1-2 tbsp Cajun seasoning (like Tony Chachere's or your favorite blend - this will make it spicy)

Enjoy preparing this thoughtful meal!
