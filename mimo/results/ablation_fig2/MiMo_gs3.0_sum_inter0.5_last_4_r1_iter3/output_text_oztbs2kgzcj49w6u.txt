Absolutely. The perfect place to start is the Black River National Heritage Area. It has a visitor center in Lockport and a great website with maps, tours, and historical information. It's a fantastic resource.

If you prefer to see things for yourself, the Lockport Canal Museum and the Erie Canal Museum in Rochester are both excellent. They offer interactive exhibits and even boat tours. You could also rent a bike and follow the Erie Canal Heritage Trail, which winds right through Lockport. It's a great way to experience the history firsthand.
