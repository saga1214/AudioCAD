Okay, this is a critical safety issue. I need to be extremely clear about this. The safety of car ramps is not about the *type* of car, but about the *slope and stability* of the surface they're on. If your driveway is sloped, the car will roll, no matter what kind of car you have. I can't stress this enough. The ramps themselves might be fine for a flat surface, but a slope makes them dangerous. Please, do not use them on a slope. Find a level spot first. Your safety is the only thing that matters here.
