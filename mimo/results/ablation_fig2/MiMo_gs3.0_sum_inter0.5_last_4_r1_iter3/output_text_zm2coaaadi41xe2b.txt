Dallas has a great Asian food scene! For Asian noodle places, I’d recommend:

First, there's Noodle House, which is famous for their spicy chicken soup and steamed dumplings.

Then there's Pho Saigon, which serves authentic Vietnamese pho, and their noodle dishes are amazing.

And finally, there's Yum Yum Noodle House, which specializes in Szechuan-style noodles with bold flavors.

As for protein bars, if you want something tasty and nutritious, I’d suggest:

First, Quest Bars, they’re high in protein and low in sugar.

Second, RXBARs, which are simple ingredients and good macros.

And third, Clif Builders, which are good for post-workout recovery.
