Here are several high-protein breakfast options:  

1. **Hard-boiled eggs** (e.g., 3 eggs = ~18g protein)  
2. **Greek yogurt** (a cup of non-fat plain has ~23g protein)  
3. **Cottage cheese** (1 cup provides ~14g protein)  
4. **Smoked salmon** (mixed with veggies or on rye toast)  
5. **Turkey or chicken sausage** (check labels for lean options)  
6. **Protein shakes** (using whey or plant-based protein powder)  
7. **Tofu scramble** (for plant-based, with veggies and turmeric)  

Pair any of these with fiber-rich carbs (like oats or whole-grain toast) for balanced nutrition!
