Let's break this down. First, you should test the glue dots on a small, hidden part of the jacket, like an inside seam. Do this to make sure the glue doesn't melt or damage the specific fabric.

If it passes the test, the next step is to apply the glue dots very carefully and sparingly. It's better to use fewer dots and cover the area completely rather than piling them on, as too much heat can be damaging.

After pressing down each dot firmly, let it cool completely for at least 10 to 15 minutes. The most important thing is to be patient and not move the jacket until the adhesive is fully set.

If you find that the glue dots aren't working for some reason, you could try using a fabric-specific glue pen. It gives you more control and might not generate as much heat. For any significant tears, it's always best to see a professional tailor. They have the right tools and experience to fix it without ruining the jacket.
