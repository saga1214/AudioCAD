You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is a great gluten-free base, so I have a couple of extra gluten-free snack ideas for camping.

First, nuts and seeds like almonds or pumpkin seeds are good for energy. Trail mix is another option, but just be sure to check that any chocolate you add is gluten-free as well.

Lastly, rice cakes with avocado or hummus are a nice, satisfying, and easy-to-pack snack.
