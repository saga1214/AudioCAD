Absolutely! Here’s a detailed shopping list, keeping legumes and seafood allergies in mind. I've organized it by course:

---

### **Starter Choices**
#### Option 1: Light Miso Soup
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1 block firm tofu (legume-free)  
  - 1 sheet wakame seaweed (rehydrates in broth)  
 - 1块 kombu (dried kelp; optional, for depth)  
- **Seasonings/Dressings**:  
  - 3 tbsp miso paste (check label for legumes!)  
  - 4 cups chicken or veggie broth (legume-free)  
  - 1 tsp sake or mirin (optional)  
  - 1 tsp sesame oil  

#### Option 2: Cucumber Salad with Sesame Dressing
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 cucumbers  
  - 1 tbsp rice vinegar  
  - 1 tbsp sugar  
  - 1 tsp soy sauce  
  - 1 tbsp sesame seeds  
  - 1 tsp sesame oil  

---

### **Main Course: Chicken Teriyaki**
- **Protein**:  
  - 2 chicken breasts (or thigh, butterflied)  
- **Sauce/Dressing**:  
  - ¼ cup soy sauce (legume-free)  
  - ¼ cup mirin  
  - 2 tbsp sake  
 块 sugar (or honey)  
  1 tsp grated fresh ginger  
  2 cloves garlic, minced  
- **Accompaniments**:  
  - Short-grain jasmine rice (2–3 cups uncooked)  
  - Stir-fried veggies:  
    - 1 cup broccoli florets  
    - 1 bell pepper (any color)  
    - 1 carrot (julienned)  
- **Seasonings**:  
  - 1 tbsp butter or oil  
  - Salt/pepper to taste  
  - 1 tsp cornstarch (for sauce thickening)  

---

### **Dessert Options**
#### Option 1: Mango Sticky Rice (Quick Version)
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1 cup glutinous rice (soaked 1 hour)  
  - 1 cup coconut milk  
  - 2 tbsp sugar  
  - 1 ripe mango, sliced  
- **Seasonings**:  
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)  
  - Sesame seeds for garnish  

#### Option 2: Matcha Ice Cream (No-Maker Version)
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 cups heavy cream  
  - 1 (14 oz) can sweetened condensed milk  
  - 3 tbsp high-quality matcha powder  
  - Pinch of salt  

---

### **Common Pantry Items**
- Soy sauce, mirin, sake  
- Rice vinegar, sugar, salt, pepper  
- Cooking oil (sesame or vegetable)  
- Ginger, garlic, sesame seeds  

---

### **Tips**
1. **Label Check**: Verify miso paste, soy sauce, and broth are legume-free.  
2. **Time-Savers**:  
   - Use pre-steamed jasmine rice or frozen mango.  
   - Swap stir-fried veggies with a bagged Asian mix (e.g., bok choy, snap peas).  
3. **Allergy Note**: Coconut milk in dessert is legume-free (coconut is a fruit). Red bean paste (anko) uses beans, so avoid that—mango sticky rice is safe.  

Enjoy your meal! This plan is packed with umami, sweetness, and warmth without any allergens. 🍜🍚🌶️
