Here’s a **nautical-themed shopping list** focused on easy-to-handle, sand-free finger foods and drinks, with pirate flair and allergy-friendly options (excluding watermelon):

---

### **Nautical Food & Drinks Shopping List**  
*(Serves your son + 7 kids = 8 kids)*  

#### **Appetizers & Sides**  
1. **“Buried Treasure” Mini Sliders**  
   - 1 lb ground turkey/beef (for kid-friendly meatballs)  
   - 8–10 mini Hawaiian rolls  
   - 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese  
   - *Theme:* Cut into 8 pieces per slider → “Doubloons”  
   - *Allergy Swap:* Use turkey instead of beef if needed.  

2. **Jeweled Fruit Skewers**  
   - 2 cups pineapple chunks  
   - 2 cups grapes (halved; *omit if allergy*)  
   - 1 cup melon cubes (*cantaloupe/honeydew—safe if no watermelon allergy*)  
   - 1 cup blueberries  
   - *Theme:* Call them “Pirate’s Jewels” or “Mermaid Pearls.”  

3. **Pirate Ship Masts**  
   - Popcorn cups on mini toothpicks → “Ship Masts”  
   - *Sand-proof:* Seal in wax paper bags labeled “Pirate Popcorn.”  

4. **Treasure Chest Crackers**  
   - Cheese cubes + cucumber sticks + ranch dip  
   - Pack in small treasure chest containers.  

---

#### **Main Dishes**  
5. **Mini Pirate Pizza Bites**  
   - 1 ball of refrigerated pizza dough  
   - 1/2 cup pizza sauce  
   - 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella  
   - 8 mini pepperoni slices  
   - *Theme:* Bake 8 mini pizzas → “Pirate Snack Ships.”  
   - *Allergy Swap:* Omit pepperoni if needed.  

---

#### **Drinks**  
6. **Pirate Punch Boxes**  
   - Individual juice boxes (4–6 flavors: apple, strawberry, etc.)  
   - Pack in cardboard punch cups → “Cursed Lemonade” or “Sea Spray Punch.”  

7. **“Seawater” Water Bottles**  
   - Small reusable bottles (clear blue/green)  
   - Label with a sticker: “Captain’s Fresh Water.”  

---

#### **Snacks & Extras**  
8. **Goldfish Crackers**  
   - Pack in sealed bags → “Pirate Gold” or “Mermaid Coins.”  

9. **Sand-Safe Desserts**  
   - Mini cupcakes (themed with pirate flags)  
   treasure chest cookie cups (Oreos with frosting)  

---

#### **Sand-Free Packaging**  
- **Sealed snack bags:** For popcorn, crackers, fruit.  
- **Individual containers:** For dips, cheese, etc.  
- **Cardboard trays:** For sliders/pizza bites.  
- **Themed labels:** Use pirate stickers or cutouts for treasure chests.  

---

### **Key Sand-Free Tips**  
- **Avoid bread/tortillas** → They fall apart at the beach.  
- **Seal everything** → Juice boxes, pre-portioned snacks.  
- **Wet wipes handy** → For quick clean-ups.  
- **Allergy note:** Label food clearly (e.g., “NO WATERMELON”).
