Absolutely! Based on our planning, here’s a **comprehensive shopping list** organized by category. I’ve included quantities and tips to help you stay on track:

---

### **Appetizers/Finger Foods**  
*(Serves 25 adults + 4 kids)*  
- **Mini slider patties** (2 lbs ground beef/turkey)  
- **Ham slider fillings** (sliced ham, cheese, lettuce)  
- Slider buns (24)  
- Cream cheese dip (1 tub) + pita chips (2 bags)  
- Shrimp cocktail platter (or bulk shrimp in tray)  
- Pigs in a blanket: **Sausages (glizzies, 24)**, crescent roll dough (2 cans), cream cheese (1 tub)  
- Cheese & crackers platter (chips, crackers, grapes, olives)  

---

### **Drinks**  
*(20 adults + 4 kids = 24 total)*  
- **Alcohol**: Champagne/Prosecco (8 bottles for toast)  
- **Signature Cocktail**: Mixers (soda, juice), spirits, garnishes  
- **Kid-Friendly**:  
  - **Grape juice** (12 bottles)  
  - Sparkling cider/grape soda (4 cans)  
- **Essentials**: Ice (10 lbs), cups/glasses (30 total), paper plates (40), serving spoons, straws, kid-safe plastic cups/hats/noisemakers  

---

### **Main Dish & Sides**  
*(For 24 people)*  
- **Ham**: 12–14 lb bone-in ham (thawed if frozen)  
- **Scalloped Potatoes**:  
  - 2 lbs potatoes (diced)  
  - 1 lb shredded cheese (cheddar/parmesan)  
  - 2 cups milk/cream  
  - 1 egg (optional for binding)  
- **Roasted Carrots**:  
  - 3 lbs carrots (cut into sticks)  
  - Olive oil, salt, pepper  
  Dingdongs or Oreos for frosting (optional!)  
- **Roasted Asparagus**:  
  - 2 lbs asparagus  
  - Olive oil, lemon juice, salt  
- **Scalloped Potatoes Sides**:  
  - 2 lbs butter (for baking)  
  - 4 cups shredded cheese (for potatoes)  

---

### **Supplies & Decorations**  
- Balloons (2–3 packs)  
- Festive tablecloth or runner  
- Noisemakers/hats (4 kid-sized)  
- Kid activity: Simple party favors (stickers, crayons, mini toys)  
- Balloon pump/streamers (optional)  

---

### **Extras to Keep in Mind**  
1. **Food Storage**: Label containers for leftovers.  
2. **Kid Zone**: Dedicate a table for coloring/play.  
3. **Dietary Needs**: Ask guests about allergies/dietary restrictions (e.g., vegan ham, gluten-free sides).  
4. **Timing**: Start cooking ham 3–4 hours before the party. Reheat appetizers/sides 30 mins before serving.
