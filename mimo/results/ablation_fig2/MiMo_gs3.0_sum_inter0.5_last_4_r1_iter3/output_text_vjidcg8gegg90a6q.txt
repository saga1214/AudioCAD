For winter chicks on concrete, your insulation plan from before applies perfectly. Use thick rubber stall mats on the floor, and scatter pine flake shavings on top. The rubber mats provide insulation, the fresh shavings on top absorb moisture, compost to generate natural heat below, and sand can be used for easy cleaning on the shavings.

This setup on the deep litter base will keep them warm and rodent-resistant. For extra insulation, you can also put up some cardboard dividers to block drafts near the heater. Just remember to check that heater regularly to make sure it's working properly.
