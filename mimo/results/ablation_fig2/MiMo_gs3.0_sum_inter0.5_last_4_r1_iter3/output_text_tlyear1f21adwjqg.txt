Okay, efficiency can mean different things. If we're talking about cost and scalability, solar leads. If it's about constant, reliable output, hydro is a strong choice. And wind is great where the wind is steady. But if I absolutely have to pick just one that covers the broadest range of factors—availability, cost, scalability, and reliability—I’d still go with solar. Storage tech is getting better all the time, which helps with the nighttime issue. So overall, solar energy remains my top pick.
