For candy tomorrow, grab some classics like Reese’s Pumpkins, Hershey’s Kisses in orange and black wrappers, Skittles, and maybe some gummy worms or zombies. You could also do a mix of regular candy like M&Ms or Snickers for variety. If you’ve got allergies again to artificial dyes, just double-check that the candy wrappers don’t say anything about artificial colors. You could also put out a bowl of regular chocolate bars or non-candy treats like pretzels or popcorn for those who don’t want candy. Keep it simple and popular!
