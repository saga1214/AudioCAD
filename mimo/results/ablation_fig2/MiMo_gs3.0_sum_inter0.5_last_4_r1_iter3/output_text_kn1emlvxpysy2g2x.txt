Of course. Let me summarize the wines and their temperatures.

First, high-end Pinot Noir: store at cellar temp, about 55 degrees Fahrenheit, and serve Slightly below room temp, around 55 to 60 degrees Fahrenheit.

Japanese Hon-Mirin: store in a cool place at about 60 to 68 degrees Fahrenheit. If you sip it straight, room temp, around 68 to 75 degrees Fahrenheit, works best.

Next, Pinot Grigio: store it a bit colder at about 50 to 55 Fahrenheit, and serve it chilled, around 45 to 50 Fahrenheit.

For Italian Chianti: store it at cellar temp, about 55 Fahrenheit, and serve it just below room temp, around 60 to 65 Fahrenheit.

And for the hot weather wine, we have Sauvignon Blanc: store it chilled at about 50 Fahrenheit, and serve it cold, around 45 to 50 Fahrenheit.

Now, for your hot weather question, the best one is definitely the Sauvignon Blanc. It's light-bodied and crisp, perfect to refresh the palate on a hot day.

For cold weather, the Pinot Noir is the way to go. It's a lighter red, so it won't overwhelm you, and it still has enough warmth to be comforting on a cool evening. I hope that helps. Enjoy your party.
