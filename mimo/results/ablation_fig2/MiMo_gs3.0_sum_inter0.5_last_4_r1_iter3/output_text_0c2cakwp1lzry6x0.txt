Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your TV mount, hanging planters, and floating shelves, focusing on safety and precision:

**1. TV Mount Installation:**
*   **Check Wall Type:** Use a stud finder to locate studs. For drywall (no studs), use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for your TV's weight.
*   **Measure Twice:** Mark exact drill points for mount brackets. Ensure they align with the TV's mounting pattern.
*   **Secure Anchors/Brackets:** Drill pilot holes (slightly smaller than anchor diameter). Insert anchors into drywall. For studs, screw directly into wood.
*   **Mount Bracket:** Secure bracket to wall with provided screws. Ensure it's level and firmly attached.
*   **Attach TV:** Hang TV onto the bracket using included screws or VESA-compatible bolts. Tighten securely.

**2. Hanging Planters:**
*   **Choose Mounting Points:** Select sturdy spots (stud or heavy-duty anchor). Avoid corners unless using specialized corner brackets.
*   **Use Appropriate Hardware:** For lightweight pots, use strong hooks or small anchors. For heavier planters (or water weight), use heavy-duty eye screws or lag bolts into studs.
*   **Secure Planter:** Attach hooks/eye screws to the pot. Ensure the pot is level and won't tip. Use pot holders for stability.
*   **Consider Water:** Plan for drainage trays below. Place near a sink or outside to minimize indoor mess.

**3. Floating Shelves:**
*   **Find Studs:** Use a stud finder. If no studs, use heavy-duty anchors rated for the shelf's weight (check manufacturer specs!).
*   **Measure & Mark:** Precisely measure shelf length and mark shelf center. Mark shelf edges on wall.
*   **Install Shelf Pins:** Drive shelf pins into wall marks. Ensure pins are level and spaced correctly.
*   **Place Shelf:** Slide shelf onto pins. Press firmly until secure. Check for stability.

**Key Tips for All Installations:**
*   **Safety First:** Wear safety glasses. Use a sturdy step stool or ladder.
*   **Know Your Weight Limits:** Check TV mount, shelf brackets, and anchors' weight capacities. Don't overload.
*   **Measure Twice, Drill Once:** Accuracy prevents damage and ensures stability.
*   **Use the Right Tools:** Use a level, drill appropriate for materials, and correct anchors/brackets.
*   **Check Clearance:** Ensure nothing (like a TV) blocks emergency exits or doors.
*   **Test Stability:** Gently nudge installed items to ensure they don't wobble or feel loose.

**Decorating on Sundays:**
*   **Dedicated Workspace:** Set up a decorating station away from your main work area.
*   **Time Blocking:** Schedule specific decorating windows (e.g., 9am-12pm, 2pm-4pm) to avoid work distractions.
*   **Contain Items:** Use a drop cloth, bins, or even an old tablecloth to contain tools and debris.
*   **Take Breaks:** Step away periodically to rest your eyes and prevent fatigue.

By following these specific steps, you'll create a beautiful, functional, and landlord-compliant space without sacrificing your focus or well-being!
