That's a great question. If you are both the writer and the illustrator, you have a significant advantage, but the approach to getting it published is different. 

For a children's book, publishers are often looking for a complete package with text and artwork. The most common way to submit is to hire an illustrator to create the art for you. They will pay for the artwork to be done professionally.

However, if you are a skilled illustrator yourself, you should absolutely look into the 'hybrid' route. This is where you write and illustrate the entire book yourself. You would then submit it directly to publishers who specifically accept hybrid projects. The main challenge here is that you will have to cover the costs of the illustrations yourself, as the publisher doesn't pay for them.

So, the next step for you would be to get professional artwork created for your manuscript. Once you have that complete package, you can then start researching publishers and agents who accept these kinds of submissions.
