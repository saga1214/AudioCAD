For a relaxed first night in Austin, here are some top laid-back spots focusing on great food and vibe:

### 🍽️ Casual & Laid-Back Picks:
1. **Meze House**  
   *Why*: Authentic Greek taverna with meze platters, grilled meats, and tzatziki. Cozy, no-frills atmosphere.  
   *Must-try*: Lamb kebabs, dolmades, and house-made labneh.

2. **Rainey Street Food Hall**  
   *Why*: Trendy, open-air market with 15+ vendors. Try Greek street food (like gyros) alongside tacos, BBQ, and more.  
   *Vibe*: Casual, live music, great for groups.

3. **Tacos Gussys**  
   *Why*: Hugely popular for creative tacos (try "The Al Pastor" or "The Tex-Mex"). Casual patio seating.  
   *Bonus*: Excellent margaritas and friendly service.

4. **The Salt Lick BBQ**  
   *Why*: Iconic Austin BBQ. Try the "Beef Brisket" or "Pulled Pork" in a casual, rustic setting.  
   *Tip*: Go early—lines move fast!

### 🏠 Neighborhood Gems:
- **South Congress (SoCo)**:  
  - **Home Slice Pizza**: Hole-in-the-wall slice shop (open late).  
  - **Greeks at the Falls**: Tiny Greek spot near Barton Springs Pool (order ahead to skip lines).

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Reservations**: Not needed for most spots on a weekday, but weekends can get busy.  
- **Parking**: Use apps like SpotHero or ParkWhiz for discounts.  
- **Greek Food Hack**: Many Greek spots near **Downtown** or **North Austin** are hidden gems—ask locals for recommendations!
