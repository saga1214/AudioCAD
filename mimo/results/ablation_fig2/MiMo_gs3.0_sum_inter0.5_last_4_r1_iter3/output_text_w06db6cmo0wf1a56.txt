Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's ten of each! Packing healthy food that's mess-free for a theme park is a great idea. Based on our chat, here is a detailed shopping list to make your planning much easier.

First, for drinks, you'll want lots of reusable water bottles for all 20 of you. For low-sugar juice for the kids, grab about twenty milk or apple juice boxes. For the adults who want something else besides water, ten sugar-free soda boxes, like Sprite Zero or Coke Zero, would be perfect. It's also smart to pack two to three gallons of ice to keep everything cold all day. And of at-least two more gallons of ice to keep everything cold all day. You might also want to bring some sports bottles for easier sipping while riding rides.

Next, for mess-free snacks, single-serve pretzels or trail mix bags are great. Baby carrots are always a hit, and each would come with its own single-serving hummus cup. Cheese sticks are also a quick and healthy snack for everyone. Grapes are another easy, kid-friendly option. Don't forget individual bags of crackers or beef jerky for extra protein.

For lunch, pre-made wraps or sandwiches are your best bet. If you want a more fun setup, you could assemble a mini calzone kit. This would include ten pre-baked mini calzones, maybe with cheese and pepperoni, plus ten small containers of marinara sauce and ten pairs of plastic forks. That way, everyone can reheat them in a cooler and eat them without any fuss.

Finally, for dessert, to keep it simple and grab-and-go, cupcakes are a fantastic choice. They come in individual portions and can be stored in a separate cooler. Or, chocolate or oatmeal cookies would be another easy, mess-free option.

This plan should give you plenty of variety while keeping things neat and easy to manage. Have an absolutely fantastic party!
