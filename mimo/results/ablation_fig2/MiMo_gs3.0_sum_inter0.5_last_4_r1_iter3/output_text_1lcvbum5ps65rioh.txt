### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia
1. **Signage & Signals**: You **cannot** make a U-turn at an intersection with a traffic light (red/yellow/green) unless a sign explicitly allows it.  
2. **Visibility**: Must see **clearly 150 meters in both directions** (roughly 1.5 football fields).  
3. **Restricted Areas**:  
   - Prohibited on curves, hills, near driveways, or where it blocks traffic.  
   - Not allowed in pedestrian zones, intersections, or near emergency vehicles.  
4. **Penalties**:  
   - **First offense**: $160 fine (under BC Traffic Act, Section 148).  
   - **Repeated offenses**: Higher fines + possible license points.  
   - **Accidents**: Fines increase significantly, and you may face criminal charges.  
**Pro Tip**: If unsure, use designated U-turn lanes (marked with "U-Turn Only") or wait for a safe gap in traffic.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients (Serves 2)
**Core Ingredients**:  
- 200g spaghetti  
- 150g pancetta or guanciale (cured pork cheek), diced  
- 3 large eggs  
- ½ cup grated Pecorino Romano cheese  
- ¼ cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano  
- Freshly ground black pepper  
- Salt (for pasta water)  

**Optional Additions**:  
- 1 clove garlic, minced (toasted in pancetta fat)  
- Pinch of red pepper flakes  
- 1 tbsp white wine or lemon juice  

**Method Summary**:  
1. Cook spaghetti in salted water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
2. Sauté pancetta in a pan until crisp. Remove from heat; keep warm.  
3. Whisk eggs, cheeses, and pepper in a bowl.  
4. Toss hot pasta with pancetta (off heat). Add egg mixture slowly, stirring vigorously.  
5. Add reserved pasta water to create a creamy sauce. Serve immediately.  

**Key Tip**: **Never** add eggs to hot pasta directly—it will scramble. Temper the eggs with pasta water first!
