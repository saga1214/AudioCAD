Here's a quick recap using your child analogy:

*   **Heart:** The gas station AND the engine. Pumps the cars (blood).
*   **Lungs:** Another gas station where the cars pick up fresh oxygen fuel.
*   **Arteries:** The big, fast highways carrying blood *away* from the heart.
*   **Capillaries:** The tiny side-streets where the cars deliver the oxygen fuel to muscles/organs and pick up waste.
*   **Veins:** The return roads carrying the blood cars *back* to the heart.
*   **Kidneys:** The car wash/checkpoints. They clean the blood by filtering out junk and extra water (making urine).
*   **Liver:** The all-in-one service station. It processes nutrients, stores things, and breaks down harmful stuff.

That's how all these parts work together with the little blood cars on the big body road system!
