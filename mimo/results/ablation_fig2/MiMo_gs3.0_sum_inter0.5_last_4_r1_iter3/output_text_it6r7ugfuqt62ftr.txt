**Destination:** Palm Springs, California  
*Why?* Palm Springs is Northern California's most reliable warm-weather escape, with average highs in the mid-80s in November. It's famous for its stunning mid-century modern architecture, incredible pool resorts, and world-class gluten-free dining. It's about a 2.5-hour drive from you in Northern California.

---

### **Accommodation Suggestions**

#### 1. **Hotel Palacio** (Luxury Resort Style)
   *   **Why it's great:** This is a true desert oasis. It features a stunning courtyard pool with a waterfall, multiple dining options (one of which is dedicated gluten-free), lush landscaping, and a relaxing atmosphere perfect for families. The service is exceptional.
   *   **Dining:** Their restaurant, **The Bistro**, is fully dedicated gluten-free and features a full menu. You can also enjoy gluten-free options at their poolside restaurant, **Palacio Restaurant**.
   *   **Best for:** Ultimate relaxation, guaranteed pools, dedicated GF dining, a classic resort experience.

#### 2. **Condo Rental in Agua Dulce** (More Space & Kitchen)
   *   **Why it's great:** Rent a condo near Palm Canyon Drive or Agua Dulce. This gives you a full kitchen (crucial for dietary control and healthy eating), more space for the family, and often a private pool. Look for rentals within walking distance of the popular **Palm Canyon Drive** restaurants.
   *   **Dining:** You can cook breakfasts and lunches at home. For dinners out, you'll have many options right on Palm Canyon Drive (see below). Agua Dulce has a charming downtown vibe too.
   *   **Best for:** Families wanting space, control over food prep, and a home-away-from-home feel with pool access.

---

### **4-Day Itinerary: Palm Springs Desert Escape**

**Day 1: Arrival & Settling In**
*   **Morning:** Fly into Los Angeles International Airport (LAX). Pick up your rental car.
*   **Drive:** Head to your chosen accommodation in Palm Springs (approx. 2.5 hours).
*   **Afternoon:** Check in, unpack, and head straight to the pool! Relax and unwind.
*   **Dinner:** If staying at Hotel Palacio, enjoy dinner at **The Bistro** (dedicated GF). If in a condo, head to **Trifolium Market & Cafe** (dedicated GF market/cafe) or **Baja Cantina** (on Palm Canyon Drive, excellent GF tacos).

**Day 2: Pools, Palm Canyon & Poolside Dining**
*   **Morning:** Enjoy the hotel/resort's pool area. Swim, relax, maybe try a water activity.
*   **Lunch:** Pack a healthy picnic lunch from a market (like **Trifolium**) or have a GF smoothie at **Lago** (Hotel Palacio).
*   **Afternoon:** Drive down **Palm Canyon Drive**. Walk around the charming shops and browse unique boutiques.
*   **Dinner:** Experience the vibrant Palm Canyon Drive dining scene. Excellent GF options include:
    *   **Baja Cantina:** Famous for fresh, delicious Mexican food, including fantastic GF tacos and nachos.
    *   **Lago (Hotel Palacio):** Upscale Mediterranean/Italian. Their dedicated GF menu is creative and delicious.
    *   **Potluck Crawl:** If staying in a condo, consider a "potluck" dinner with friends or a group. Bring healthy snacks and sides from Trifolium!

**Day 3: Beach, Hiking & Healthy Treats**
*   **Morning:** Drive to **El Pescadero State Beach** (about 45 minutes). Enjoy the stunning Pacific coastline, take a short walk along the cliffs, and watch the waves. It's a beautiful contrast to the desert.
*   **Lunch:** Pack a healthy picnic from a market or enjoy a GF wrap at a nearby cafe.
*   **Afternoon:** Head to **San Jacinto State Park**. Hike the easy, scenic **Skyline Trail** (about 2.5 miles round trip) for incredible views of the desert and mountains. Or, explore the short trails in the **Cottonwood Canyon** area.
*   **Dessert:** Stop at **Trifolium Market & Cafe** for a dedicated GF ice cream sundae or a decadent GF cake.

**Day 4: Shopping, Relaxation & Departure**
*   **Morning:** Enjoy a final pool day or a leisurely breakfast at your accommodation.
*   **Lunch:** Explore the shops in **Palm Springs** (like the Palm Springs Art Museum shops) or Agua Dulce. Grab a GF lunch at a cafe you passed the day before.
*   **Afternoon:** Drive back to LAX (approx. 2.5 hours).
*   **Evening:** Depart from the airport.

---

### **Key Packing List Additions**
*   **Sun Protection:** High SPF sunscreen (reapply!), wide-brimmed hats, sunglasses, UV-protective clothing. The desert sun is strong!
*   **Pool Gear:** Towels, swimwear (obviously!), water shoes.
*   **Celiac Safety Essentials:** Pack your own gluten-free snacks (like Trifolium's GF granola bars or trail mix) for the drive. Bring a small card listing your daughter's celiac diagnosis and restrictions if you feel comfortable.
*   **Comfortable Shoes:** You'll be doing a lot of walking, especially on Palm Canyon Drive.

### **Travel Logistics**
*   **Getting There:** Fly into **Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)**. It's the most convenient and has the most rental car availability.
*   **Getting Around:** A **rental car is absolutely essential** in Palm Springs. You'll use it to explore the desert, beaches, and parks. Book your rental and car insurance well in advance.
*   **Booking:** Book your accommodation and any specific restaurant reservations (especially at The Bistro or Baja Cantina) *at least 2-3 months in advance* as it's peak season.

This itinerary balances relaxation, adventure, and the crucial need for safe, enjoyable dining for your daughter, all within California's beautiful desert. Enjoy your warm getaway!
