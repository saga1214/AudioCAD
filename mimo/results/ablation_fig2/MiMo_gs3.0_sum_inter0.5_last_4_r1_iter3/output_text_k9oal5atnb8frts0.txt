For a 'Retro Tech' theme, how about a 'Mainframe Lunch'? You could have a big bowl of spaghetti and meatballs to look like a mainframe cable, with garlic breadsticks as side 'cables,' and some olives or pickles as little 'bugs' on the side. For a second idea, you could do a 'Vintage Computer Breakfast' even if it’s lunch. Think of a waffle as a computer monitor, maybe with some blueberry 'pixels' on it. Serve it with a side of turkey sausage to look like a floppy disk, and some orange juice to look like 'data flow'. These tie the food right into the theme.
