### 1. **Vodka Mixer: "Sunset Spritz"**  
*(Bright, herbaceous, and versatile for all diets)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz vodka  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 0.5 oz honey syrup (equal parts honey + hot water, stirred)  
  - 2 oz sparkling wine (Champagne, Cava, or Prosecco)  
  - 4-5 fresh mint leaves  
  - *Garnish*: Lemon wheel + mint sprig  
- **Method**:  
  Shake vodka, lemon juice, and honey syrup with ice. Strain into a coupe glass. Top with sparkling wine. Muddle mint leaves in the shaker, then stir into the drink.  
- *Why it works*: Sweet-sour balance, sparkling wine avoids soda allergy, and mint adds sophistication across generations.

### 2. **Argentine Vin de Verano**  
*(A rustic-chic wine spritzer—perfect for warm evenings)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1 large glass of **rosé wine** (dry, like阿根廷马尔贝克 or Provençal rosé)  
  - 1 oz fresh lime juice  
  - 0.5 oz simple syrup  
  - 2 oz sparkling water  
  - *Garnish*: Lime wedge + rosemary sprig  
- **Method**:  
  Fill a wine glass with ice. Add lime juice, simple syrup, and rosé. Top with sparkling water. Stir gently.  
- *Why it works*: Light, bubbly, and wine-based (no soda!). Works for all ages—romantic yet approachable.
