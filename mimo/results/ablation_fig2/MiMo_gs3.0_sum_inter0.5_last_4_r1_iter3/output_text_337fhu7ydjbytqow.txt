Here's a **separate closing gear packing list** for your two pacing segments, optimized for activity and conditions:

---

### **For Daytime 20 Miles (Day Running):**
1.  **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking shirt (long-sleeve recommended) and shorts (or leggings under shorts).
2.  **Mid-Layer:** Fleece jacket or lightweight puffer vest (removable).
3.  **Outer Layer:** Wind/water-resistant shell jacket (easier to shed than pants).
4.  **Headwear:** Cap or bucket hat (if sun is present).
5.  **Footwear:** Trail running shoes with good grip for hills.
6.  **Extras:** Gloves (optional, for grip), lightweight pack/vest (to carry removable layers), headlamp/handheld light (just in case).
7.Carry your own hydration (hydration bladder/bottle) even though aid stations exist – pacing means you control his water.

---

### **For Night Pacing (Final 12 Miles):**
1.  **Base Layer:** Moisture-wicking shirt & shorts (or leggings).
2.  **Mid-Layer:** Fleece jacket or lightweight puffer vest (essential).
3.  **Outer Layer:** Wind/water-resistant shell jacket **(critical for warmth and wind chill)**.
4.  **Headwear:** Gloves (**non-negotiable** for cold extremities at night).
5.  **Headwear:** Warm hat or balaclava (**essential** for keeping your head warm).
6.  **Headwear:** Buff or gaiter (**highly recommended** – covers neck, can be worn over face/hat, helps moisture management).
7.  **Footwear:** Trail running shoes with grip (hills + night = tricky).
8.  **Lighting:** Headlamp (**primary light source**), plus handheld light or backup headlamp batteries.
9.  **Extras:** Lightweight pack/vest (**must-have** to easily add/remove layers), consider a second thin layer (like a lightweight zip-off legging or long-sleeve shirt) in case you get too warm during a segment.

---

### **Key Considerations for Both Segments:**
*   **Pack/Vest:** **Essential** for carrying layers, gloves, hat, buff, and your hydration efficiently. Makes adding/removing layers easy.
*   **Hydration:** Carry your own water source (bladder/bottle). Aid stations might be busy or far between.
*   **Visibility:** Bright colors or reflective elements on outer layer or pack are good for safety.
*   **Layers are Key:** Mid卅s hilly = constantly changing temp. Wind resistance is crucial, especially at night. Be prepared to add or subtract easily.
*   **Extremities:** Hat, gloves, and buff are vital for preventing heat loss from your head and hands.
