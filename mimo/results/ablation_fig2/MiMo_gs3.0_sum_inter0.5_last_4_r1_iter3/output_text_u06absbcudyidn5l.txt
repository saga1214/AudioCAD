Beyond vasculitis, chronic redness can be caused by many things. Rosacea is a very common one, sun damage over your lifetime, genetics, contact dermatitis from products, or conditions like chronic eczema.

As for vasculitis, it's inflammation of the blood vessels, and it's an autoimmune condition. It can affect vessels of any size. Symptoms really depend on where the inflammation is. For example, on the skin, you might see rashes, purple spots, or sores. Systemic symptoms could include fever, fatigue, and joint pain. Diagnosis usually involves a physical exam, blood tests, and often a biopsy for confirmation.

Treatment involves immunosuppressants and corticosteroids to calm the inflammation. The main complication is that this inflammation can cause permanent damage to the affected organs by restricting blood flow.

For hair and nails, alternatives like collagen peptides and making sure you get enough iron and zinc are your best bet.

For your skin, to address the redness, you could look into topical treatments like niacinamide. But for anything systemic, like potential vasculitis, it's essential to see a doctor.
