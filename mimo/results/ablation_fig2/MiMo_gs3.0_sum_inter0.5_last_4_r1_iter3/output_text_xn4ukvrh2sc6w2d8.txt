### 1. **Old Fashioned**  
**Glass:** Rocks glass over ice  
**Garnish:** Orange twist (expressed oils)  
**Recipe:**  
- 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey  
- 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup *(omit sugar cube if using syrup)*  
- 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- 1 lemon peel (optional, for citrus)  
**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube (if using) with ½ oz water in the glass until dissolved.  
2. Add bourbon, bitters, and ice. Stir well for 20–30 seconds.  
3. Strain into the glass. Garnish with an orange twist or lemon peel.  
*Egg white tip:* Skip if uncomfortable. Dry-shake (no ice) first to emulsify, then add ice.

---

### 2. **Paper Plane**  
**Glass:** Coupe glass  
**Garnish:** Cherry  
**Recipe:**  
Equal Parts Mix (all equal measures):  
- 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Rittenhouse or Bulleit)  
- ¾ oz Aperol  
- ¾ oz Maraschino Liqueur (Luxardo)  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
3. Garnish with a cherry.  
*Balance:* Bitter (Aperol/Marasca), sweet/tart (bourbon/lemon).

---

### 3. **Whiskey Sour**  
**Glass:** Rocks glass over ice  
**Garnish:** Cherry or lemon slice  
**Recipe:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 1 egg white *(optional)*  
**Instructions:**  
1. **With egg white:** Muddle 1 egg white in the glass. Add bourbon, syrup, and lemon juice. Dry-shake (no ice) 10 sec, then add ice. Shake again 10 sec.  
2. **Without egg white:** Stir all ingredients with ice for 30 sec.  
3. Strain into glass. Garnish.  
*Note:* Egg white adds texture but isn’t essential—your call!

---

### Suggested Additional Gin Cocktail: **French 75**  
**Glass:** Champagne flute  
**Garnish:** Lemon twist  
**Recipe:**  
- 1.5 oz gin (e.g., London Dry like Beefeater or Hendrick’s)  
- 0.75 oz lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz simple syrup  
- 3 oz chilled Champagne or prosecco  
**Instructions:**  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a flute.  
3. Top with Champagne.  
**Why it fits:** Elegant, effervescent, and visually stunning—perfect for an Art Decou event!
