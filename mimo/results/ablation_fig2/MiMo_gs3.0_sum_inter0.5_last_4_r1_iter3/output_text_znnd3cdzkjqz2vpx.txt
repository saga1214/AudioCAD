Okay, you've got most of the essentials, but there are a few more things that will make the process a lot smoother and safer. You'll definitely want a power drill and some good Phillips-head screws. Habitat cabinets almost always use screws, not nails. Shims are also super important for getting everything level before it's all screwed down. Oh, and make sure you have a stud finder that can measure the depth to the wall, so you know how deep to drive your screws. And a work light will be a huge help, especially once the uppers are in. You might also want to grab some painter's tape to protect your walls from scratches. You're all set for the big day!
