Here's a full recap of ideas tailored for your charming corgi princess, organized for easy reference:

### 🧁 Homemade Treats
1. **Frozen Bites**  
   - Mashed pumpkin/sweet potato spooned onto silicone molds or ice cube trays. Freeze for 3–4 hours.  
   - *Bonus:* Add a tiny bit of plain yogurt for extra protein.

2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**  
   - Mix xylitol-free peanut butter with rolled oats. Roll into 1-inch balls, flatten, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–15 minutes. Cool completely.  
   - *Tip:* Break into pea-sized pieces for training.

3. **Healthy Crunch**  
   - Cooked, cooled carrot or zucchini sticks (blanch first to soften if needed).  
   - Small pieces of steamed chicken breast (no skin/salt).

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands (Check Ingredients!)  
- **Training Bites:** Zuke’s Mini Naturals, Wellness Training Treats  
- **Wholesome Pouches:** Earthborn Holistic, Fromm Small Training Chews  
- **Fresh Options:** The Honest Kitchen dehydrated treats  
- *Always avoid xylitol, corn syrup, or excessive salt.*

### 🐶 Training Tips Recap  
**Sit Command:**  
- Lure with a treat near nose → raise over head → say "Sit!" → reward instantly.  

**Potty Training:**  
- Take her outside to the same spot immediately after waking/feeding.  
- Use a consistent cue like "Go potty!" and reward heavily.  
- Clean accidents with enzymatic cleaner (e.g., Nature's Miracle) to remove scent.  

**Felix-Friendly Solutions:**  
- Teach "Quiet" using a treat + pause when barking.  
- Redirect with a tug toy or "Leave it" command.  

### 🎀 Collar Color Ideas  
For a **brown-and-white** coat:  
- **Bold Contrast:** Turquoise, bright pink, or emerald green.  
- **Elegant Neutrals:** Black, charcoal, or metallic silver.  
- **Whimsical:** Pastel pink or mint with a crystal charm.  

### 💡 Pro Tips  
- Freeze treats in a lick mat for mental stimulation.  
- Hide kibble in a puzzle toy (like a Kong) for longer playtime.  
- Rotate treats weekly to keep her excited!
