The Calgary area is fantastic for adventures. For something easy and close by, you could go stargazing at Studio Bell, which used to be the Canadian Music Centre. It's a beautiful, modern building designed to look like a giant musical instrument.

If you want something a bit more active, there's the Stirling Tunnel. It's a historic railway tunnel you can explore on foot. Or, for a more relaxed day, you could take a scenic drive and hike at the Bow River Trail system. You could even rent bikes and ride along the Bow River path, which is one of the best bike paths in the world.
