For kosher dietary laws, the main things to check are that all the ingredients are kosher-certified—like hummus and chocolate—and that there are no cross-contamination issues. So, just double-check the labels on things like dark chocolate packages or weenies to make sure they have a kosher certification symbol. And maybe just make sure you're not using utensils that have been in contact with non-kosher food. That should be it! It's good you're being thoughtful about everyone's needs.
