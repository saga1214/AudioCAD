Absolutely! Pickles can add a fun twist to your meals. Try this:  
**Pickled Salmon with Pickle Juice Glaze**  
- Pan-sear the salmon skin-side down until crispy.  
- Flip it and brush the top with mix of pickle juice + a splash of olive oil.  
- Add a sprinkle of the pickle brine (for extra tang).  
- Serve with a side of your spinach and cherry tomato salad for a balanced meal!  

Bonus: Use pickle juice to marinate salmon for extra flavor before cooking!
