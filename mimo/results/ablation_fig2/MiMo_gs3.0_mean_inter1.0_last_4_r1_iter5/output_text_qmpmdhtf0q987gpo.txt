Alright, I've got your cabinet prep question. So you've already cleaned, degreased, light-sand, and protected from dust by taping off areas. Before applying primer, here are a few more things to do.

First, make sure the sanding dust is completely wiped away. You can use a tack cloth for this.

Next, apply painter's tape to the areas where the cabinet frame meets the wall. This will prevent any primer from getting on the wall.

Before you start, it's a good idea to test your color on a small, hidden spot on the cabinet to make sure you like the result.

And finally, make sure your primer is the right type for painted cabinets. A brush-on latex primer is usually best, but a spray primer is also a good option for a smooth finish.
