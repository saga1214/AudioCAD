That's a great question! Based on your potential tailoring hobby and existing skills, here are some meaningful gift ideas, categorized by type:

### For Tailoring (Hobby-Specific)
1.  **Premium Hand-Measuring Tape:** Crucial for accuracy. Brands like Moleskine or Maglise offer durable, quality options.
2.  **High-Quality Leather Shears:** Essential for finishing. Look for German-made brands like Gingher or Kai.
3.  **Specialty Thread Set:** A curated set of high-quality threads (e.g., Woolwax, Gutermann, Coats & Clark) in various weights and colors.
4.  **Vintage-Inspired Fabric Sample:** A beautiful piece of high-quality cotton or linen (like poplin or twill) to practice on or make a simple item.
5.  **Pocket Notebook & Pen:** A nice notebook for sketching designs or jotting notes, paired with your restored Parker 51!
6.  **Simple Pattern Package:** A beginner-friendly pattern for a pillowcase, tote bag, or vest to get you started.
7.  **Vintage Fountain Pen Accessory:** Something for your existing hobby (new converter, unique nib, ink sample) shows you value what you already do.

### Woodworking (If You Explore It)
1.  **Quality Hand Plane (e.g., Jack Plane):** A foundational tool. Start with a good used or new one.
2.  **Sharp Chisels Set:** Essential for fine woodworking. Look for brands like Starrett or Wood River.
3.  **Woodworking Planes & Levels:** For measuring and marking accurately.
4.  **Safety Glasses & Gloves:** Practical and important gear.
5.  **A Beautiful Piece of Wood:** A nice piece of hardwood (like walnut or oak) for a small project.

### Clay (If You Explore It)
1.  **Basic Clay Kit:** Includes a small amount of polymer clay or air-dry clay, a rolling pin, and simple tools.
2.  **Potter's Wheel (Small):** If you're serious, this is the big investment. Look for affordable electric models.
3.  **Quality Sculpting Tools:** A set of small, sharp carving and shaping tools.
4.  **Glaze Sample Kit:** For air-dry or polymer clay to experiment with finishes.
5.  **Workshop Access/Class:** A gift card for a local pottery studio or workshop.

### General Craft Supplies (Applicable to Many Hobbies)
1.  **A Nice Craft Knife (X-Acto):** Essential for precise cutting in tailoring, woodworking, and clay modeling. *Crucial: Must include sharp blades!*
2.  **Multi-Tool Pliers Set:** Needle-nose, wire cutters, etc., useful in tailoring, woodworking, and jewelry making.
3.  Craft Storage Containers: Clear bins for organizing small parts and tools – a huge time-saver!
4.  **Online Subscription for Craft Ideas:** Platforms like Skillshare, Domestika, or even a local craft guild membership.

### Thoughtful Non-Material Gifts
1.  **A Day Out Together:** "Gift of Time" – spend the day working on a project together (e.g., sewing, woodworking, baking clay).
2.  **Workshop Tickets:** For you to attend a local class on tailoring, woodworking, or pottery.
3.  **A Beautiful Gift Basket:** Curated with high-quality thread, needles, a small fabric swatch, a notebook, and a pen.
