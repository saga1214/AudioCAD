### Why Guidelines Trump Everything Else:
1. **Gatekeeper to Visibility**:  
   If your video violates TikTok’s guidelines (e.g., copyright issues, harmful content, or restricted material), it **won’t reach anyone**. No matter how engaging, concise, or consistent your content is, TikTok will suppress or remove it. This is a non-negotiable first hurdle.

2. **Algorithmic Trust**:  
   TikTok prioritizes content that aligns with community safety and platform policies. Consistently following guidelines builds trust with the algorithm, ensuring your videos are *considered* for the "For You Page" (FYP).

3. **Foundation for Other Factors**:  
   Without guideline compliance, factors like "engaging content," "conciseness," or "description" are irrelevant. A video can’t be "consistent" if it’s repeatedly taken down, nor "concise" if it’s invisible.

### The Other Factors (Ranked by Importance):
1. **Engaging Content** (Most Important *After* Guidelines):  
   If your video is allowed to live, **immediate engagement in the first 3–7 seconds** is critical. TikTok’s algorithm rewards watch time, shares, and comments. If viewers scroll away instantly, your video won’t gain traction.

2. **Conciseness**:  
   Shorter videos (under 15 seconds for trends, under 60 seconds for most content) tend to perform better. But brevity alone won’t save non-engaging content.

3. **Consistency**:  
   Posting regularly (e.g., 3–5x/week) signals activity to the algorithm, but only if your content is *compliant and engaging*. Posting frequently with restricted videos hurts your account.

4. **Description/Hashtags**:  
   These help with discoverability but are secondary. TikTok’s algorithm prioritizes real-time engagement over SEO-like tags.

### Key Takeaway:
**Start with guidelines—this is your baseline.** If your content is restricted, nothing else matters. Once compliant, focus on **hooking viewers in the first 3 seconds** with high-energy, relatable, or unexpected content. Shorter videos (<15 seconds) often perform better for virality, but quality > length.
