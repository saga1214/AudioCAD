For candy, focus on **universal crowd-pleasers** and Halloween classics to avoid leftovers. Here’s a quick list of reliable options, prioritized by popularity:

### Top Candy Picks:
1. **Reese’s Cups/Puffs**  
   Peanut butter chocolate is a universal hit.
2. **Skittles**  
   Fun colors, easy to eat, and nostalgic.
3. **Candy Corn**  
   A Halloween staple—cheap and festive.
4. **Snickers**  
   Classic combo of chocolate + caramel + nuts.
5. **M&M’s**  
   Bright colors, crunchy texture, and easy to hand out.
6. **Gummies** (Starbursts, Haribo)  
   Sweet, chewy, and universally loved.
7. **Butterfingers**  
   Creamy chocolate with crunch—always a hit.

### Avoid:
- Niche candies (e.g., gummies with weird flavors, exotic chocolates).
- Overly messy items (e.g., lollipops—guests might not want to hold them).

### Pro Tips:
- **Buy bulk**: Dollar stores or grocery aisles have Halloween packs.
- **Pre-package**: Use small bags or cups so guests can grab easily.
- **Allergy check**: Avoid nuts if unsure—opt for dairy-free or soy-based candies.
