That's a brilliant transition plan—van adventure in the desert followed by Vegas luxury creates the perfect contrast! Here’s how to make both legs memorable:

### **Nevada Desert Stays (Post-Joshua Tree)**
1. **Valley of Fire State Park (NV)**  
   - **Why unique**: Red rock formations, petroglyphs, and surreal landscapes. Just 45 mins from Vegas.  
   - **Camping**: Campgrounds inside the park ($15–$20/night) or free dispersed camping on **BLM land** near Tonopah (check [BLM.gov](https://www.blm.gov/) for permits).  
   - **Adventure**: Hike Fire Wave Trail, visit the "Canyon Overlook" for panoramic views, or find hidden hot springs (e.g., **Dunlap Hot Springs**—bring a change of clothes!).

2. **Great Basin National Park (NV)**  
   - **Why unique**: Ancient bristlecone pines, stargazing (one of the darkest skies in the US), and the Lehman Caves.  
   - **Camping**: Free dispersed sites near Baker, or primitive sites inside the park ($10/night).  
   - **Drive**: ~4 hours from Joshua Tree, but worth it for solitude.

### **Vegas Luxury Reset**
If you drive back via Vegas:  
- **Hot Showers & Comfort**:  
  - **Ace Hotel** or **Park MGM**: Stylish, boutique vibes with showers for $30–$50/night.  
  - **Hidden Gem**: **Wynn’s Spa Tower** showers (book a massage + shower combo).  
- **Beyond Gambling**:  
  - **Neon Museum**: Retro sign nostalgia.  
  - **Hoover Dam**: Day trip from Vegas (free, stunning).  
  - **Small-Town Vegas**: Explore **Nevada test site** (scenic, offbeat) or **Red Rock Canyon** (easy hikes).  
- **Food**: **Lotus of Siam** (Thai), **Eataly** (Italian), or **Peppermill** (classic diner).

### **Budget & Logistics**
- **Total Cost**: ~$100–$200/day (camping + gas). Vegas hotels add $150+/night.  
- **Drive Time**:  
  - Joshua Tree → Vegas: ~4 hours.  
  - Vegas → Red Rock/Valley of Fire: ~1 hour.  
- **Van Prep**: Solar charger, portable fridge, cooler, first-aid kit, and a good playlist for long drives!

### **Adventure Tips**
- **Joshua Tree**: Stay at **Jumbo Rocks** or **Hidden Valley** campsites (book early!).  
- **NV Desert**: Pack *extra* water, sun protection, and a power bank.  
- **Vegas**: Use rideshares to avoid parking hassles.
