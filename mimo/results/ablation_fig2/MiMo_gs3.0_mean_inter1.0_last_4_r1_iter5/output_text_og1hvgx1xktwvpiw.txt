Don't worry, it can be confusing. Let me clarify: The truly classic ratio is one and a quarter ounces whiskey, one ounce vermouth, and one ounce Campari. Your friend's equal-parts recipe is also classic and very popular. The Boulevardier and the Last Word are indeed different drinks, even though your friend uses an equal-parts ratio for both. For a Boulevardier, stick with either of those Boulevardier recipes. For the Last Word you mentioned, the proportions are three-quarters of an ounce for each ingredient: gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime. Hope that helps you decide!
