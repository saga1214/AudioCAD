     - *Dessert:* Brownie decorating station (repurpose troop-made treats!).

3. **Activities:** **Low-Stakes Fun**  
   - **Classic Games:** Wheelbarrow races, three-legged races, sack races.

   - **S’mores Station:** For park time (use dairy-free chocolate if needed).  

4. **Cost Management:**  
   - **Parents Cover:** Sashes, patches, field trip fees.  
   - **Troop Dues:** Small amount ($10-$20) for party supplies/games.  
   - **No Cost:** Potluck food, park activities, DIY decorations.

- **Delegate Early:** Assign roles (e.g., one parent manages snacks, another games).  
- **Pregnancy Prep:** Sit near the door; have a partner bring water/snacks.  
- **Flexibility:** If park games flop, pivot to an indoor talent show or craft session.
