Second, you could visit Victoria and the Vancouver Island wineries. It's a four-hour drive. You can explore the Butchart Gardens in Victoria and then head to the Oak Bay Wine Trail. A great activity is to see the famous orcas at the Vancouver Aquarium. For a special meal, you could try the farm-to-table dinner at the Fairmont Victoria.

Third, on the way to the mountains, you could stop in Whistler. It's a four-hour drive. You can take the Peak 2 Peak Gondola for incredible views and explore the Blackcomb Gondola. A fun activity is to try the Magic Chair Ziplining. And for a great meal, the Wildfire Restaurant is known for its Pacific Northwest cuisine.

Fourth, for a more dramatic drive, you could go to Banff and the Canadian Rockies. It's about an eight-hour trip. You can visit the iconic Lake Louise and the Icefields Parkway. A must-do activity is to see the stunning turquoise water in Moraine Lake. For a special meal, the Banff Gourmet has a wonderful farm-to-table experience.

And fifth, you could explore the Fraser Valley. It's only a two-hour drive. You can visit the beautiful Stanley Park on the Sea to Sky Highway and explore the Pheasant Tree Farms. A great activity is to tour the Vancouver Island wineries. And for a delicious meal, you can't go wrong with the famous poutine at Poutine Pro.

These are all wonderful choices for a family with a first-time baby and a new dog! Enjoy your adventures.
