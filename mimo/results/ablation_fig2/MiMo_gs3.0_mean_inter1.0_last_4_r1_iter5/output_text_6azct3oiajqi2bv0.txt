Based on your need for a budget-friendly, Segmental-appropriate winter layer that won't damage the fabric, here are the best hemming options:

1.  **Iron-On Fabric Hem Tape (Best for most linings):**
    *   **How:** Apply tape along the raw edge of the fabric *inside* the hem, pressing firmly with an iron. The adhesive melts slightly and bonds permanently.
    *   **Pros:** Very clean, invisible result when pressed well. No sewing needed. Great for fleece or synthetic linings.
    *   **Cons:** Must be used *inside* the garment. Choose tape specifically rated for fabric (not for upholstery/carpets). Test on a scrap first if unsure.

2.  **Double-Sided Fabric Tape (Best for visible seams):**
    *   **How:** Cut strips slightly longer than your hem allowance. Stick one side to the raw edge *inside* the hem, then fold the hem up and stick the other side to the folded edge.
    *   **Pros:** Holds well, very strong. Good for thicker linings. Often less visible than iron-on tape if used carefully.
    *   **Cons:** Can be slightly more visible than iron-on tape. Requires a little more care when applying.

3.  **Sewing (Most durable, requires needle/thread):**
    *   **How:** Fold the hem up to your desired length (1/2" to 1" is common). Pin or clip it securely. Use a narrow zigzag stitch (if your machine has it) or a very short straight stitch to stitch just the folded edge, keeping the needle *inside* the hem to avoid catching the main fabric. Use a thread color close to the lining.
    *   **Pros:** Creates a very neat, secure, and invisible seam. The most durable option.
    *   **Cons:** Requires a needle, thread, and sewing machine (or hand-sewing skills). Can be tricky to stitch perfectly inside a garment.

**Recommendation:** **Start with Iron-On Fabric Hem Tape** if you have an iron. It's the easiest and least damaging method for most standard jackets from Nordstrom Rack or RE Co-op. **Double-Sided Tape** is a great alternative, especially if the tape is slightly visible. **Sewing** is ideal if you have the tools and know-how.

**Tip:** If shipping online is delayed, check Arden Fair. Stores like PacSun, Hollister, or even H&M often have hemming services you can inquire about in-store for a small fee. Nordstrom Rack and REI also offer in-store hemming.
