Drywall tasks:
Joint compound small tub: ~5 to 10 dollars
Sanding materials: ~5 dollars
Putty knife 6+: 10 to 15
Primer/paint, matching wall: 10 to 20
Nails/caulk gun/adhesive: 5 to 10
Replacement molding, depending on size: 15 to 30

Cake baking:
AP Flour: 3 to 5
Granulated Sugar: 6 to 8
Baking Powder: under 3
Unsalted Butter sticks: 8
Large eggs: 3 for 2 to 5 dollars
Whole milk: 2 to 4
Vanilla extract: 5 to 10
Pan(s), assuming two: 5 to 15
Powded sugar/frosting supplies cocoa: 5 to 10

Plus decorations/balloon rental if needed: 5 to 20

Breaking it down:
Repair total estimate: Drywall kit often bundled, so let’s say 25 to 50.
Cake total estimate: Flour/sugar/baking mix is usually sold in larger amounts, so let’s budget 30 to 50.
Extras/decor: 10 to 30.

So, I’d budget around 70 to 130 dollars total, maybe a little more if you get the paint primer separately. Just double-check sizes and amounts before you go, and you should be good!
