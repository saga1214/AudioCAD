That's a great idea—cooking can be a wonderful way to relax and reclaim your self-care vacation. Here are five simple, comforting dinner ideas you could whip up with ingredients from the grocery store, focusing on quick prep and good vibes:

1. **One-Pan Lemon Herb Salmon with Roasted Veggies**:  
   Toss asparagus or cherry tomatoes with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and spread them on a baking sheet. Place salmon fillets on top, drizzle with lemon juice and herbs (like dill or rosemary), and bake at 400°F for 12–15 minutes. Serve with a fresh arugula salad.

2. **Creamy Tomato-Basil Pasta**:  
   Sauté garlic in olive oil, add a can of crushed tomatoes and a splash of pasta water, simmer for 10 minutes, then toss with cooked pasta (like penne or fusilli). Stir in heavy cream or coconut milk, fresh basil, and grated Parmesan. Top with a sprinkle of red pepper flakes for heat.

3. **Hearty Chicken & Vegetable Soup**:  
   Sauté sliced carrots, celery, and onions, then add diced chicken breast, canned diced tomatoes, broth, and frozen green beans. Simmer for 20 minutes. Stir in spinach and a can of white beans at the end for protein and fiber. Serve with crusty bread.

4. **Mediterranean Tacos**:  
   Brown ground turkey or chickpeas with cumin, chili powder, and paprika. Serve in warm tortillas with toppings like shredded cabbage, diced avocado, pickled red onions, and a lime-cilantro dressing. Add black beans or feta for extra richness.

5. **Garlic Butter Shrimp & Zucchini Noodles**:  
   Sauté shrimp in butter and garlic until pink, then add spiralized zucchini noodles and a splash of broth. Toss everything together, finish with lemon zest and parsley. Top with toasted pine nuts or breadcrumbs for crunch.

**Bonus Tip**: Pair your meal with a glass of wine or sparkling water with lemon—something to make it feel special. Remember, this is *your* vacation. Even if the condo wasn’t perfect, you’re taking time for yourself, and that’s what matters most. Enjoy the process! 🌊✨
