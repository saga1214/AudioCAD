Okay, efficiency can be measured in different ways. If we're talking about cost and scalability, solar leads. But for reliability and constant output, hydropower is very strong, especially with existing infrastructure. Wind is good in windy areas. Geothermal is excellent where it's available. If I absolutely had to pick just one... I think I'd still go with solar. It’s the most universally available, scalable, and its costs keep falling. Storage is improving, which helps with its intermittency. But it really depends on the location. If it's a windy area, wind might be better. If there's a river, hydropower could be top. But overall, solar is a safe and efficient pick.
