Absolutely, that's a fantastic option! If you have both the text and the illustrations ready, you can absolutely submit your own artwork.

The first thing to do is to make sure your illustrations are professionally polished. Publishers expect high-quality, ready-to-print images. You can then approach publishers directly, usually through their website's 'Submit' or 'Contact' page. This is called a 'direct market' submission. 

Some publishers are very open to it, especially for unique concepts, while others might prefer to work with an illustrator they have on staff. You'll need to research the publisher's specific policies on their website to see if they accept unsolicited submissions.

If you want to submit to an agent instead, you'd need to be very clear in your query letter that you are the author and illustrator of the book. This is less common, but it can be a very powerful selling point.
