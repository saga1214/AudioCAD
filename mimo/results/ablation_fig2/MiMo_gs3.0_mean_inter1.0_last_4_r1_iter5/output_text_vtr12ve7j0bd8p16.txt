Calgary is a fantastic base for adventures. Right in the city, you could go up the Calgary Tower for amazing views or visit Studio Bell, which is Canada's music studio and looks like a giant bell.

A little further out, you're surrounded by the Rockies. You could take a scenic drive on the Bow Valley Parkway, visit the Banff Gondola for incredible views, or even take a day trip to the famous Icefields Parkway to see the Columbia Icefield. If you want something more active, you can go for a hike in Prince's Island Park or take a tour of the Old Man River Distillers. For a truly epic adventure, you could drive up to Kananaskis Lake or take the Icefields Parkway as far as you can, all the way to Jasper. Enjoy your second date and your adventures!
