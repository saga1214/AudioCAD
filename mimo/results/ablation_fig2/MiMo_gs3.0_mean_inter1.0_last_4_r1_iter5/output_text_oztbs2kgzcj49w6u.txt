You could start by visiting the Old Lock Museum in Lockport. It's the only one of its kind in the entire United States and it's housed right inside one of the original locks, so you can really get a feel for how they worked.

They also have a large model of the entire canal system in the museum, which is a great way to understand the whole layout. For a bit of history, you might also want to check out the Erie Canal Museum in Youngstown, which has a lot of information about the canal's impact on Rochester and the entire region.
