That pigeon question is like a logic puzzle! It's a fun way to think about how to organize things.

When planning big goals like travel and apartment down payments, you can start by matching each passion and priority to a time frame. Salt Flats Bolivia? Maybe a medium-term goal. Apartment? That's a long-term priority.

Next, create a small fund for Bolivia and another for the apartment. Then, break each goal down into tiny actions. For Bolivia research, maybe just one 15-minute session a week.

Doing this turns overwhelming ambitions into manageable, regular tasks.
