### 1. **Fin Choice Finalized**  
   **Recommendation:** Invest in **short fins** for your first Costa Rica trip. Given your beginner-to-intermediate experience (based on Honduras training), they’ll reduce fatigue and let you focus on enjoying the dive. Long fins are better for strong currents/distance—save those for later if you upgrade.

### 2. **Best Costa Rica Dive Sites**  
   - **Cocos Island:**  
     - *Why:* Strong currents, dramatic drop-offs, and abundant marine life (sharks, sea lions, manta rays). Ideal for thrill-seekers.  
     - *Tip:* Requires a boat trip (2–3 hours). Best in winter (May–October) for calmer conditions.  
   - **Tortuguero National Park:**  
     - *Why:* Calmer waters, easy shore dives, and incredible biodiversity (turtles, rays). Perfect for relaxation.  
     - *Tip:* Guided tours include jungle excursions—great for combining adventure with nature.  
   - **Puntarenas & Manuel Antonio:**  
     - *Why:* Shore-accessible sites like "The Wall" (diver-friendly coral reefs) and "Golf Course" (clear water, tropical fish).  

### 3. **Wildlife Hotspots**  
   - **Turtles:** Common in Tortuguero and Playa Conchal.  
   - **Whales:** Humpback whales (December–April) near Cocos Island.  
   - **Reef Life:** Coral gardens in Manuel Antonio and Punta Mala.  

### 4. **Logistics & Tips**  
   - **When to Go:**  
     - **Dry Season (Dec–April):** Warmest, clearest water. Whale season!  
     - **Shoulder Seasons (May, Nov):** Fewer crowds, lower prices.  
   - **Dive Operators:**  
     - **Costa Rica Dive Center** (Tortuguero): Eco-focused, turtle-focused tours.  
     - **Cocos Island National Park:** Official boat charters for island adventures.  
   - **Travel Prep:**  
     - Pack: Short fins, reef-safe sunscreen, dive computer, waterproof camera.  
     - Travel: Fly into San José, rent a car to reach dive hubs (e.g., Puntarenas).  

### 5. **Alternative Tropical Picks (If Costa Rica Isn’t Set in Stone)**  
   - **Palau:** Crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral, and shipwrecks. Short fin-friendly.  
   - **Curaçao:** Easy shore dives, sea turtles, and bioluminescent bays.  
   - **Bonaire:** World-class shore diving with underwater trails.  

### 6. **Sample Itinerary (7 Days)**  
   - **Days 1–2:** Arrive in San José → Drive to Puntarenas → Dive "The Wall" and "Golf Course."  
   - **Days 3–4:** Boat to Cocos Island → Dive with sharks/whales (if in season).  
   - **Days 5–7:** Return to mainland → Tortuguero jungle tour + turtle encounter.  

### Final Advice:  
Costa Rica offers a mix of adventure (Cocos) and serenity (Tortuguero), with wildlife that’ll leave you awestruck. Prioritize safety: Always dive with certified guides, check weather, and respect current conditions.
