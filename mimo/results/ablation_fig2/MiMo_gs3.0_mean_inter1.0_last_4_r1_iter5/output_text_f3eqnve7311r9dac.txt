Yes, older comics are generally rarer and can be more valuable.

Here are 10 notable titles from the 1940s and 1950s that are often sought after by serious collectors. Keep in mind that the price for any of these will be high, and the cost is significantly increased by the comic's condition, rarity, and the quality of its grade from a professional company like CGC.

First, there's *Action Comics* number one from 1938, Superman's first full appearance. A record-breaking six million dollars was just mentioned.
Second, *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen from 1939, Spider-Man's first appearance. A copy sold for three-point-six million.
Third, *Captain America Comics* number one from 1941 sold for just over three million in 2022.
Also, *Tales of the Fantastic* number forty-five is often called the first official Marvel comic. It features Doctor Strange's first appearance.
Then there's *Marvel Super-Heroes* number one, which introduced the Fantastic Four.
*The Amazing Spider-Man* number one is the first full appearance of Spider-Man.
Another great one is *The Amazing Spider-Man* number three十四, which is the first appearance of Doctor Octopus.
*The Incredible Hulk* number one from 1962 is Bruce Banner's first appearance.
*Fantastic Four* number one from 1963 is the debut of the team and Galactus.
Finally, *Thor* number one from 1962, which features the first appearance of the Silver Surfer.

Please be aware that these are significant investments. You should always research the specific comic you're looking at and consider its condition before you buy.
