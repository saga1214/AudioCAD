Yep, budget-friendly desserts are definitely possible. You could do no-bake options like fruit parfaits layered with yogurt and nuts. Homemade granola bars are another good choice, and you can control the sugar. Even simple baked apples or pears with cinnamon are great. You can also look for sales on seasonal fruits like berries and bananas. Using whole-grain or low-sugar cookie mixes can also save money.
