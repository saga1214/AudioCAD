### 2. Brain Lateralization Myth Debunking  
The **left-brain/right-brain dichotomy is largely a myth**. While some tasks show *tendencies* for left/right hemisphere specialization (e.g., language processing in left hemisphere, spatial tasks in right), reality is far more complex:  

- **Myth:** "Left-brain = logical, right-brain = creative."  
- **Reality:**  
  - The brain is a **network**—most complex tasks (even creative ones) involve *both* hemispheres communicating via the corpus callosum.  
  - Creativity requires *both* hemispheres: Right hemisphere for ideation, left for structuring.  
  - "Logical" tasks (e.g., math) also engage right-brain spatial reasoning.  
- **Why the myth persists:**  
  - Simplified models are catchy for education/media.  
  - Some studies show *correlations* (e.g., left-hemisphere damage causing language loss), but these don't prove dichotomy.  

**Key nuance:** While *individual differences* exist (e.g., some people are more spatially adept), there's no universal "left-brain" or "right-brain" person.
