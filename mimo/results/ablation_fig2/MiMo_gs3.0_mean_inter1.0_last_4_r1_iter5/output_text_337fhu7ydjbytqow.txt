Here's a clear packing list split between the 20-mile nighttime segment and the final 12-mile push, optimized for your pacing duties. Since there are 3 aid stations on the first segment, focus on **layering flexibility** and **lighting**, but always bring essentials.

### **Pacing List: 20-Mile Nighttime Segment**  
*(Mid-30s temps, aid stations available, hilly terrain)*  
1. **Base Layer**:  
   - Moisture-wicking shirt and shorts (avoid cotton).  
2. **Mid-Layer**:  
   - Fleece or light puffy jacket (removable for aid stations).  
3. **Outer Layer**:  
   - Wind/water-resistant trail shell (eojacket or similar).  
4. **Footwear**:  
   - Trail running shoes with aggressive tread for hills.  
   - Extra socks (lightweight, wool blend).  
5. **Hands/Head**:  
   - Gloves (water-resistant).  
   - Warm hat or beanie (extremities get cold fast at night).  
6. **Lighting**:  
   - Headlamp + extra batteries (critical for nighttime).  
   - Optional: Waist-mounted light or handheld light.  
7. Aid Station Strategy:  
   - Pack layers in a lightweight vest or backpack to swap in/out.  
   - Bring a small first-aid kit (blister care, tape).  

### **Pacing List: Final 12 Miles**  
*(Likely colder/darker, fatigue sets in)*  
1. **Core Layers**:  
   - Same as above (base + mid-layer).  
2. **Insulation Boost**:  
   - Insulated puffy jacket (lightweight, 200-300g fill).  
   - Fleece vest (for added warmth if needed).  
3. **Hands/Head**:  
   - Gloves + hat (same as earlier).  
   - Buff or neck gaiter (direct warmth to neck/chest).  
4. **Lighting**:  
   - Headlamp + spare batteries (fatigue increases eye strain).  
5. **Footwear**:  
   - Same trail shoes + dry socks (chafing risk during fatigue).  
6. **Extras**:  
   - Small pack/vest for extra layers if needed.  
   - Extra hat/gloves (if dropped earlier).  

### **Key Why's**  
- **Aid Stations**: Use them to swap layers, refuel, and rehydrate—but don’t rely on them for warmth.  
- **Night Focus**: Extremities (hands/feet) and neck warm first. Insulation is critical in fatigue.  
- **Hills**: Prioritize grip in shoes; avoid heavy layers that slow you down.  
- **No Nutrition**: Stick to clothing only as requested.
