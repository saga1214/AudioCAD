With Secret Harbour Beach Resort, you're in paradise! Here’s a mix of relaxing and adventurous daytime activities in Cruz Bay and Red Hook:  

### **Near Secret Harbour/Secret Harbor Resort**  
1. **Snorkeling right from your bay**: Calm waters often host tropical fish, sea turtles, and even parrotfish. Grab a snorkel and paddleboard from the resort.  
2. **Trails to Cruz Bay shops**: Walk or bike to town in 10 minutes. Grab fresh coconuts, local fruits, and souvenirs.  

### **In Cruz Bay**  
- **Cruz Bay Beach**: Sandy stretch with rentals for kayaks, paddleboards, and bikes. Great for exploring Cinnamon Bay.  
- **Cinnamon Bay**: Hike the short trail to this famous snorkeling spot (fish rays and coral gardens).  
- **Cruz Bay Village**: Coffee shops (like *The Red House*), ice cream (Scooter’s), and live music events.  

### **Red Hook (East End Hub)**  
- **Kayak or paddleboard tours**: Companies like *Red Hook Kayak* offer rentals and guided trips to Magens Bay or Cane Garden Bay.  
- **Trivia Night at The Longboard**: Fun social scene with live bands on weekends.  
- **Red Hook Farmers Market**: Saturday mornings (8 AM–12 PM). Local crafts, produce, and food trucks.  
- **Bike rentals**: Rent a bike to explore Cruz Bay, Cruz Bay Beach, or even the Cruz Bay Lighthouse.  

### **Nature & Culture**  
- **Virgin Islands National Park**: Hike to Waterlemon Cay (a secluded island) or explore Cane Garden Bay’s history.  
- **Soggy Dollar Bar**: Famous for the Painkiller (rum, pineapple, coconut) and sunset views.  
- **Sail Away Tavern**: Casual spot by Cruz Bay Bay for seafood tacos and live music.  

### **Boat-Specific Adventure**  
 charter a day sail or charter a day sail to nearby islands like St. Thomas. Snorkel at **Trunk Bay** (pristine walls) or **Salt Pond Bay** (seahorses!).  

### **Pro Tips**  
- **Hydrate & sun safety**: Coastal heat is intense—drink water and use reef-safe sunscreen.  
- **Avoid midday crowds**: Early mornings or late afternoons are best at popular spots.  
- **Respect wildlife**: Observe seabirds from a distance—no feeding!
