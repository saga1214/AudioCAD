Alright, let's talk stir-fry and pasta, and make sure smoke alarms sleep this time.

For stir-fry, a key tip is to start early. Have all your ingredients chopped and ready to go, because it cooks very fast. For the meat browning, just be careful not to burn it, and you'll be fine.

Now, for boiling pasta to avoid setting off another alarm, here are the rules. Rule one: wood does not belong in pasta water. Use a metal spoon. Always.

Rule two: salt the water generously before you even add the pasta. Rule three: stir it once or twice while it's cooking, just to make sure it doesn't stick. Rule four, and this is the easiest part: as soon as it's done, drain it immediately and get it back on the heat with your stir-fry sauce. The pasta will finish cooking in the sauce.
