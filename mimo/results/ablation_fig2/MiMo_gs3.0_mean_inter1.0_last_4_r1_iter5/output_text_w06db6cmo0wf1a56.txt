Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's a big group of twenty people! Keeping it healthy but also appealing to everyone, including making room for sugar-free sodas and celebrating with dessert, is a great challenge. I've put together a shopping list for coolers that focuses on minimal mess and grab-and-go convenience. Here's how I've organized it.

First, drinks. Water bottles are non-negotiable for everyone, so plan for about 40 kids' water bottles and 40 adult-sized ones. For low-sugar juice, grab 20 milk-plus-plus size boxes for the kids. For the adults, sugar-free sodas are a must, so buy 20 cans or 10 12-packs. Don't forget plenty of refillable water stations for the sodas. Some dads or older kids might prefer unsweetened iced tea, so pack about 20 single-serve tea bags or boxes. And of course, have plenty of ice to keep everything cold.

Next, snacks. Cheese sticks are a kid favorite, so get about 20. Grapes are easy and healthy, grab two 1-pound bags. Single-serve pretzels or trail mix bags are messy-free, so aim for 20. Baby carrots with single cups of hummus cups are another great hands-free snack.

For meals, since wraps and sandwiches worked best, you could pre-pack about 20 hearty whole-wheat turkey and cheese wraps, or make 20 individual sandwiches with lean turkey or ham. Just keep them chilled.

And for dessert, to avoid the mess of a whole cake, I'd suggest either 24 cupcakes, maybe decorated for the bachelor party, or a few dozen assorted brownies that everyone can just grab a piece of.

This plan should keep you all fueled, hydrated, and moving around all day mess-free. Have a fantastic time at the theme park for your best friend!
