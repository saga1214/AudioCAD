Of course, let's break it down.

First, chronic redness has many potential causes. Besides rosacea, sun damage, genetics, fibers dysplasia, fibrositis, and vasculitis, it could also be things like lupus, contact dermatitis, or even chronic allergies.

Now, let's clarify vasculitis. It's an autoimmune disorder where the body's immune system mistakenly attacks the walls of your blood vessels. This inflammation can affect vessels of any size—large, medium, or small—which is why it can affect the whole body. Common symptoms include fever, fatigue, joint or muscle pain, and skin rashes. A proper diagnosis usually requires a biopsy and blood tests.

If you're concerned, it's absolutely best to talk to your doctor. They can run the right tests to figure out what's going on.
