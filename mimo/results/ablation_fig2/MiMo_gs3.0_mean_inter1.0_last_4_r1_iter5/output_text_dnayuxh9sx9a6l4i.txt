Sure! First, think about your lifestyle—size, energy, and how much time you can dedicate to training and exercise. Next, research breeds known for being chill, friendly, and trainable, like Labradors, Goldens, or Corgis. If purebreds aren’t mandatory, look into mixes like Goldendoodles or Labradoodles, which often inherit the best traits from both parents. Another step is to reach out to rescues or shelters and mention your landlord’s rules ("not too big/noise when left") and your preferences. Meet potential candidates to see how they vibe with you and others. Don’t skip the vet visit—get a health check and training tips. Take your time—finding the right fit matters a lot.
