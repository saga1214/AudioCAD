You've got the essentials down, but here are a few more things that could make the job go much smoother.

First, I'd strongly recommend getting a cordless drill with some good screws. It's just so much faster and easier than using a hammer and nails. Also, shims are crucial for getting everything perfectly level, so make sure you have plenty of those. And a good pair of gloves will protect your hands from the sharp edges of the cabinets.

Most importantly, make sure everyone is wearing safety glasses. You'll be hammering and drilling, and it's easy to lose focus and accidentally hit someone in the eye. And while you're at it, a dust mask would be a good idea too.

One last tip: before you screw in each cabinet, clamp next to next flush together so they look good. Enjoy the savings and the hard work! You've got a great team coming over to help.
