Based on a holistic view of fame, cultural impact, and enduring recognition within America, here are the five most famous athletes in American history, with Mike Tyson being a strong contender at number six: 
1. Muhammad Ali – The undisputed most iconic American athlete. His cultural impact, social activism, charisma, and global recognition far transcend sports. He is synonymous with American identity.
2. Michael Jordan – The ultimate global superstar. His combination of dominance, marketability, and cultural penetration (Air Jordan, movie roles) makes him arguably the most recognizable American athlete worldwide.
3. Babe Ruth – The foundational icon of American baseball. His legendary home run prowess and status as the sport's first global superstar cement his place in the top tier.
4. Serena Williams – The most dominant and celebrated female athlete of the modern era. Her record-breaking career, cultural influence, and global fame make her a top contender.
5. Tom Brady – The most decorated champion in professional sports history. His longevity, seven Super Bowl wins, and status as the face of the NFL for two decades ensure his place in the pantheon of famous athletes.
