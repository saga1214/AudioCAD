Here's a step-by-step guide for each installation, prioritizing safety and effectiveness:

### 1. **Mounting a TV**
   - **Check Wall Type**: Use a stud finder to locate studs (essential for heavy TVs). If no stud, use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for your TV's weight.
   - **Mark & Align**: Use a level to mark mounting points. Ensure the center point aligns with the TV's center.
   - **Secure Mount**: Attach the TV mount to the wall using anchors/studs. Follow the manufacturer's weight guidelines.
   - **Mount TV**: Secure the TV to the mount. Test stability before hanging.
   - **Cable Management**: Use cable clips or in-wall kits to route cables neatly.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Choose Hardware**: For lightweight planters (e.g., ceramic pots), use wall anchors rated for 25–50 lbs. For heavy pots (terracotta), use toggle bolts.
   - **Measure & Mark**: Hold the planter against the wall. Mark screw holes 1–2" below the planter's bottom for stability.
   - **Install Hardware**: Drill pilot holes (if needed) and insert anchors. Secure screws firmly.
   - **Hang Planter**: Place the planter over the hooks/brackets. Ensure it’s level and stable. Avoid overfilling with soil.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Measure & Mark**: Hold the shelf against the wall. Mark the top/bottom edges. Use a level to ensure horizontality.
   - **Install Brackets**: Attach L-brackets to the wall studs. Ensure brackets are level and spaced evenly.
   - **Attach Shelf**: Place the shelf onto the brackets. Secure with screws through the shelf's back. Test weight capacity.
   - **Decorate**: Add plants, photos, or decor. Avoid overloading to prevent sagging.

### Pro Tips:
- **Safety First**: Wear gloves and a step stool. Never over-tighten screws.
- **Tools Needed**: Drill, level, stud finder, measuring tape, anchors (as needed).
- **Post-Installation**: Test stability by gently shaking each item. Adjust if needed.
- **Time Management**: Dedicate 1–2 hours per project on Sundays. Start with the TV (biggest project) to build momentum.

> ⚠️ **Key Reminder**: Always follow manufacturer instructions for hardware. If unsure about wall structure (e.g., concrete), consult a professional. Your landlord’s no-noise rule won’t apply to these installations, but use wall anchors carefully to avoid damage.
