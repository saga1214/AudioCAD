Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! Here are a few top picks for you:

First, there's Nine Lives Noodle Bar. It's known for its spicy, flavorful noodles, and they have a great ramen and pho menu.

Second, there's Golden Phoenix. They specialize in Cantonese-style dim sum and noodles, with some really authentic dishes.

Third, there's Tokyo Joe's. It's a classic Japanese spot with great ramen, udon, and tempura.

As for protein bars, I'd recommend these:

First, Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard. They're high in protein and taste pretty good.

Second, Ghost Protein Bars. They’re low-calorie but still packed with protein.

And third, Quest Bars. They’re more like snacks, but they’re high in protein and low in sugar.

Hope you enjoy your meal!
