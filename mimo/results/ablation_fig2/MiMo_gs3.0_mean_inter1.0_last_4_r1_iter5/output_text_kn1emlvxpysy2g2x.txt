### Key Takeaways:
1. **Hot Weather Ideal:**  
   - **Pinot Grigio** (light, mineral, high acidity)  
   - **Champagne** (effervescence cleanses the palate)  
   *Both stay refreshing in heat.*

2. **Cold Weather Ideal:**  
   - **Mulled Wine** (heated with spices, full-bodied)  
   - **Chianti** (structured tannins cut through rich dishes)  
   *Warmth and depth shine in chill.*

3. **Hon-Mirin Note:**  
   Primarily a cooking ingredient. If sipped, serve at room temp (20–24°C). Its sweetness balances well with savory Japanese flavors.

4. **Pinot Noir:**  
   Serve slightly below cellar temp (55–60°F). Avoid heat—it loses delicate red fruit aromas.

### Pro Tips for Outdoor Events:
- **Pinot Grigio in Heat:** Chill bottles in an insulated bucket with ice/water.  
- **Cold-Weather Reds:** Decant Chianti 30 mins before serving to soften tannins.  
- **Hon-Mirin Use:** Deglaze pans with it for dishes like yakitori or glazed veggies.
