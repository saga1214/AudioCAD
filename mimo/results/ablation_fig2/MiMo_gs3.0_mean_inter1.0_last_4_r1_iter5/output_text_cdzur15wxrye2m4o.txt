Since you’re borrowing her ramps on a **flat surface**, here’s how to drain your car’s oil:  

1. **Prep:**  
   - Park on a **flat surface**. Turn off the engine.  
   - Place the oil pan under the engine.  

2. **Drain Old Oil:**  
   - Locate the drain plug (usually under the engine).  
   - Remove the plug and let all oil drain into the pan.  

3. **Replace Oil Filter:**  
   - Before installing the new oil filter, soak its rubber seal in a little new oil.  
   - Screw on the new filter by hand until snug.  

4. **Refill & Check:**  
   - Reinstall the drain plug.  
   - Pour in the correct amount of new oil (check your manual).  
   - Start the engine for 1–2 minutes, then turn off.  
   - Check the oil level with the dipstick. Top up if needed.
