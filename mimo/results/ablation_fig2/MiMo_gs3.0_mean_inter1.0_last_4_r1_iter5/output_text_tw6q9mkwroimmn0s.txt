That sounds like a fantastic romantic theme! Here are tailored meal and dessert ideas, keeping your fiyaesse’s dietary needs in mind (no shellfish, diabetes-friendly, love for mango and spice, and shrimp cravings):

### **5 Ocean-Themed Meals**  
1. **Spicy Mango-Shrimp Curry**  
   - Use *frozen shrimp* (no shellfish) simmered in a coconut milk-based curry with fresh mango, ginger, turmeric, and a kick of chili flakes. Serve with jasmine rice.  
   *Diabetes Tip:* Use sugar-free coconut milk and limit added sugar.  

2. **Mango & Coconut Shrimp Stir-Fry**  
   - Sauté shrimp (frozen works) with bell peppers, snap peas, and diced mango in a garlic-ginger-gochujang (Korean chili paste) sauce. Finish with a squeeze of lime.  
   *Diabetes Tip:* Avoid heavy sauces; use low-sugar soy sauce or tamari.  

3. **Mango-Shrimp Tacos**  
   - Grill or pan-sear shrimp with a spicy mango-habanero salsa. Serve in corn tortillas with avocado crema (sugar-free) and cilantro.  
   *Diabetes Tip:* Skip flour tortillas; use lime and spices for flavor instead of sour cream.  

4. **Shrimp & Mango Skewers**  
   - Thread shrimp (pierced to remove shells) with mango chunks, red onion, and red bell peppers. Marinate in lime juice, cumin, and chili powder. Grill or broil.  
   *Serve with:* Cucumber-mango salsa and a side of Greek yogurt (unsweetened).  

5. **Spicy Mango-Shrimp Dumplings**  
   - Make *gluten-free* dumplings with shrimp paste, mango puree, and chopped mango. Wrap in wonton wrappers (use gluten-free if needed) and steam.  
   *Dipping Sauce:* Mango-lime chili oil.  

---

### **5 Ocean-Themed Desserts**  
1. **Mango-Coconut Chia Pudding**  
   - Blend frozen mango with coconut milk, chia seeds, and a pinch of cardamom. Layer with toasted coconut flakes.  
   *Diabetes Tip:* No added sugar—rely on mango’s natural sweetness.  

2. **Spicy Mango-Shrimp Skillet Cookies**  
   - Blend *frozen shrimp* with mango puree, oats (gluten-free), cinnamon, and a pinch of cayenne. Bake into chewy cookies.  
   *Dipping:* Dark chocolate (70%+ cocoa) for richness.  

3. **Mango & Coconut Ice Cream Sundae**  
   - Use *low-sugar* vanilla ice cream. Top with fresh mango, toasted coconut shrimp, and a drizzle of spicy mango-chili honey.  

4. **Mango-Shrimp Fruit Parfaits**  
   - Layer Greek yogurt (unsweetened), mango puree, and crushed roasted coconut shrimp. Add a sprinkle of chili-lime salt.  

5. **Spicy Mango-Shrimp Cheesecake Bites**  
   - Blend *frozen shrimp* with mango, cream cheese, and vanilla. Chill in mini molds. Top with mango compote and coconut crumble.  

---

### **Key Considerations**  
- **Shrimp Substitute:** Always use *frozen, cooked shrimp* (no shells) to avoid cross-contamination.  
- **Diabetes Focus:**  
  - Use sugar-free coconut milk, unsweetened yogurt, and natural sweetness from mango.  
  - Control portions and prioritize fiber (chia seeds, oats, veggies).  
- **Spice Wisely:** Use chili powders, gochujang, or ginger for flavor without excess salt.  
- **Ocean Theme:** Decorate with blue/white tablecloths, seashell centerpieces, and "Dive In" signs!
