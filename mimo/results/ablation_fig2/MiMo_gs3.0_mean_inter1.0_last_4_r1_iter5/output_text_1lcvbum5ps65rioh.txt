### U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia  
If you make an illegal U-turn in BC, expect:  
1. **Fines**: $160–$600 CAD (depending on violation severity).  
2. **Demerit Points**: Up to 3 points on your license for unsafe U-turns.  
3. **Accidents**: Illegal U-turns causing collisions may lead to criminal charges.  

**Key Rules Recap**:  
- ✅ **Allowed**: At intersections with traffic lights *if signed*, on clear straight roads.  
- ❌ **Prohibited**: Near intersections, curves, hills, or where visibility is <150m.  
- ⚠️ **Always**: Check signs, mirrors, and blind spots.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
Here’s what you need (excludes pork per your dietary needs):  

#### **Core Ingredients**  
1. **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti or bucatini.  
2. **Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 1 yolk (for creaminess).  
3. **Cheese**:  
   - 150g *Pecorino Romano* (sharp, salty) *or* Parmesan.  
   - 50g grated Parmesan (optional, for milder flavor).  
4. **Meat Substitute** (since no pork):  
   - 150g **guanciale** (cured pork cheek, if available) *or* pancetta.  
   - *OR* 150g **Italian sausage** (removed from casing).  
5. **Other**:  
   - 50g finely chopped **shallot** or onion.  
   - Fresh **black pepper** (to taste).  
   - Salt for pasta water.  

#### **Optional**  
- Garlic (minced).  
- Red pepper flakes (for heat).  

#### **Key Tips**  
- **No cream**: Authentic carbonara uses eggs and cheese.  
- **Avoid bacon**: Pancetta/guanciale is traditional but pork-based. Use sausage if needed.  
- **Cooking order**: Sauté meat/shallot first, then finish pasta in the same pan.
