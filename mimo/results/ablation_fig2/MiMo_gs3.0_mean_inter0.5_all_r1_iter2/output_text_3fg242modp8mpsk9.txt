You could definitely build in some romantic and creative water activities.

One idea is a private sunset sail. Many charter companies offer small boats that can take just the two of you.

Another option, if you like fishing, could be a deep-sea fishing charter in the morning, followed by a chef preparing your catch for dinner that evening.

For something more relaxed, you could look for a catamaran rental that allows you to explore quieter, less crowded coves.
