### U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia:  
If you make an **illegal U-turn**, expect:  
- **Fine:** $160 (plus court costs).  
- **Demerit Points:** 3 points on your driving record.  
- **Safety Risk:** Illegal U-turns can cause collisions, especially at intersections or in blind spots.  

**Key Rule:** Always ensure a **clear 150-meter visibility** in both directions and check for signs prohibiting U-turns (e.g., near schools, railway crossings, or traffic lights). If unsure, wait at an intersection where U-turns are permitted.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients:  
For 4 servings, you’ll need:  
1. **Pasta:** 400g spaghetti (or bucatini).  
2. **Protein:** 200g pancetta or guanciale (diced).  
3. **Eggs:** 3 large eggs + 1 extra yolk (for richness).  
4. **Cheese:** 100g Pecorino Romano (grated), plus extra for serving.  
5. **Aromatics:** 1 small garlic clove (minced, optional).  
6. **Seasoning:** Freshly ground black pepper, salt.  
7. **Optional:** Red pepper flakes for heat.  

**Pro Tip:**  
- Whisk eggs, cheese, and pepper in a bowl. Heat pancetta in a skillet until crisp (don’t drain fat!). Toss hot pasta with the fat, then mix with the egg-cheese sauce off-heat to avoid scrambling.
