Okay, so you want to suggest classic cars to your father-in-law as a more interesting investment. That’s a great idea—it aligns with his American-car loyalty and old-school vibe. Here are five classic cars that have historically appreciated in value and could be a fun, prideful way to introduce him to a new kind of investment.

First, there's the 1967 Chevrolet Corvette Stingray. It's iconic, American, and highly sought after by collectors. The 444-MG or L82 models are especially prized.

Second, a 1965 to 1967 Ford Mustang. It's the original American muscle car. Certain models, like the Fastback or the Boss 429, have seen huge appreciation.

Third, a 1969 to 1972 Dodge Charger. It's a muscle car legend. The models with Hemi engines, like the R/T or SE, are particularly valuable.

Fourth, the 1963 to 1964 Ford Falcon. It was a popular and affordable car back then, but rare models today can be worth a lot.

And fifth, a 1955 to 1957 Mercury Coupe. It’s a lesser-known classic but has seen significant appreciation because of its unique style.

This way, you’re framing it as a fun, American car investment rather than a financial lesson. It keeps his pride intact while potentially making his money work for him better than in a bank.
