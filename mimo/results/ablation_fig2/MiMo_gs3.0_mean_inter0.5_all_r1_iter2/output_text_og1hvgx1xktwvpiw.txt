Let me clarify the Boulevardier for you first. You’re right, I explained both versions. The truly classic recipe is one and a quarter ounces whiskey to one ounce each of vermouth and Campari. Your friend’s one-to-one ratio is also classic and very common. So, the original isn’t equal parts, but neither is he wrong. For the Boulevardier, I’d recommend starting with the one-and-a-quarter recipe.

Now, for the cocktail you called 'Last Chance,' you're probably thinking of 'Last Word.' Its equal-parts recipe is three-quarters of an ounce of gin, Campari, and green Chartreuse, plus three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. Garnish that one similarly with an orange twist. Enjoy!
