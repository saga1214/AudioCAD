That's a wonderful idea! Cooking can be a great way to unwind. Here are five simple, delicious, and comforting dinner ideas you could make with ingredients you might find at a beachside grocery store:

1.  **Quick Shrimp & Vegetable Stir-Fry:** Grab some shrimp, bell peppers, broccoli florets, snap peas, and a bag of rice. Sauté veggies in a little oil, add shrimp, toss with soy sauce or teriyaki, and serve over rice. Super fast and fresh!
2.  **Creamy Garlic Parmesan Pasta:** Get penne pasta, heavy cream (or coconut milk for a lighter option), garlic, Parmesan cheese, spinach, and cherry tomatoes. Cook pasta, sauté garlic, add cream and tomatoes, toss in pasta and spinach, and top with tons of Parmesan. Pure comfort!
3.  **Grilled Chicken & Fish Salad:** Buy a nice fish fillet (like cod or snapper) and some grilled chicken breast. Serve it over a bed of mixed greens with sliced cucumber, tomato, red onion, and a light vinaigrette. Beach fresh!
4.  **Hearty Beef & Vegetable Stew:** Use stew meat, potatoes, carrots, celery, onions, canned tomatoes, and beef broth. Slow-cook it (or use the oven) for a rich, satisfying meal in one pot. Perfect for a cozy evening.
5.  **Loaded Baked Potato:** Get a few potatoes, sour cream, shredded cheese, chopped bacon or chili, broccoli, and maybe some avocado. Bake 'em until tender, split 'em open, and load them up! Ultimate customizable comfort.

Take your time, enjoy the process, and focus on something that brings you joy. A vacation on your own sounds lovely otherwise, even with those condo hiccups! Hope you find something delicious to make.
