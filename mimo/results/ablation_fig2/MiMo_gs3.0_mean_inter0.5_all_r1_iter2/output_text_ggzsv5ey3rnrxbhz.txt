Okay, I see you're asking about juice cleanses and water fasts for these chemicals. While those aren't proven methods, the best way to reduce exposure is to avoid the sources in the first place.

So, to give you a clear list of five products to avoid, it would be things like non-stick cookware, grease-resistant food packaging, and stain-resistant fabrics. Using certified water filters and choosing PFAS-free labels on cosmetics are also key.

PFAS stands for per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances. And no, it's not one single chemical; it's a huge family of thousands of different compounds.

Fish is a concern because these chemicals build up in the food chain. Toilet paper and some paints can also be sources, but electronics are a very minor source of exposure.

For water, you're right to be cautious. Activated carbon filters are the standard recommendation. And while some filters might remove minerals, you can find systems that remove the contaminants but keep the minerals, or you can just stick with certified tap water.

As for getting them out, your body naturally eliminates them, and drinking plenty of water can help. There's no special cleanse needed. The focus should be on prevention.
