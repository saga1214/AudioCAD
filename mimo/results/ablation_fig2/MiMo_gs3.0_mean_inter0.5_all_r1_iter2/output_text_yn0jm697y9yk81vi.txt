Here's a concise list of my top recommendations for cutting sheet metal, drywall fastening, and insulation, along with where to purchase them locally in Indianapolis:

### Cutting Sheet Metal
1.  **Thinner Sheets Curves:** Aviation Snips  
    *Get at:* Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards, Tractor Supply Company, Ace Hardware

2.  **Thicker Metal / Power / Straight Lines:**  
    Angle Grinder w/Cut-Off Wheel  
    Jigsaw w/Metal-Cutting Blade *(Alternative)*  
    *Get at:* Same stores as above (Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards) + local metal supply shops.

### Drywall Fasteners
3.  **Best Overall (Holding Power):** 1 1/4" Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws  
    *Why:* Prevents popping/cracking better than nails.  
    Jigsaw with Fiberglass Drywall Screves *(Alternative)*  
    J-Nails *(Alternative - Ring-Shank type recommended for wood studs)*  
    *Get at:* All major home improvement stores (Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards).

### Insulation
4.  **Standard Walls (Easy Fit):** Fiberglass Batts (Pre-cut Rolls)  
    *Get at:* All major home improvement stores (Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards).

5.  **Soundproofing / Fire-Resistant:** Mineral Wool (e.g., Rockwool)  
    *Get at:* Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards.

6.  **Best Air Sealing (Requires Pro Installation):** Spray Foam  
    *Get at:* Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards (Pro sections) or local insulation suppliers.

### Where to Buy in Indianapolis (Big-Box Stores with Best Selection)
*   **Menards:** Strong focus on metalworking tools and a wide range of insulation/drywall products. Often has good prices.
*   **The Home Depot:** Extensive tool and material selection.
*   **Lowe's:** Very similar to Home Depot in selection.
*   **Tractor Supply Co. / Ace Hardware:** Can get aviation snips, basic jigsaws, and fiberglass batts, but selection will be smaller than the big three.

**Summary:** For your Indianapolis trip, focus on **Menards, Home Depot, or Lowe's**. You can get all cutting tools (snimps, grinder/jigsaw parts), drywall screws (drywall or j-nails), and insulation (fiberglass batts, mineral wool) at any of these stores.
