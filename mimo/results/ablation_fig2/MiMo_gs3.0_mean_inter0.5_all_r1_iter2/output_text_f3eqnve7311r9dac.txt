You're right that early printing runs were very small, which is what makes them so valuable. For vintage books from the 1940s and 1950s that are more affordable than the six-million-dollar superstars but still significant, here are 10 you could find in the five-hundred-dollar range or less:

1.  **Captain America Comics #1 (1941):** The first appearance of Cap. While the perfect-grade record-setter is expensive, a mid-grade copy regularly sells for $500-$1000.
2.  **Web of Science #1 (1965):** First solo Spider-Man title. A key issue; a good copy can be found under $500.
3.  **The Amazing Spider-Man #50 (1962):** First cover with the iconic "Spider-Man" logo. A desirable key; mid-grade copies often fall under $500.
4.  **Fantastic Four #1 (1961):** Introduced the FF. A cornerstone comic; a mid-grade copy typically sells for $500-$1000.
5.  **The Amazing Spider-Man #51 (1962):** First appearance of Gwen Stacy. A major key; a good graded copy is often under $500.
6.  **Fantastic Four #52 (1962):** First appearance of Black Panther. A key issue; mid-grade copies can be found in the $500 range.
7.  **Tales to Astonish #39 (1962):** First appearance of the Hulk (post-World War II). A key issue; a good copy often sells for $500-$1000.
8.  **Strange Tales #110 (1963):** First appearance of Doctor Strange. A significant debut; mid-grade copies are often under $500.
9.  **Thor #1 (1962):** First solo Thor title. A classic; a good graded copy typically sells for $500-$1000.
10. **Tales to Astonish #52 (1962):** First appearance of the Hulk (post-World War II, but slightly later than #39). A key issue; mid-grade copies are often under $500.

**Important Considerations:**

*   **Condition is Crucial:** These prices depend heavily on the comic's grade (condition) when professionally graded by CGC. A "New" copy of a 60-year-old comic is incredibly rare and would cost a fortune.
*   **Authenticity:** Ensure you're buying from reputable sellers (auction houses, well-established dealers) and that the comic is authenticated if it's a major key.
*   **Market Fluctuations:** Comic values can change. Do your research on recent sales prices (Heritage Auctions' online archive is a good resource).
*   **Passion First:** Since Luke Cage and defenders are your entry point, start there! It makes the hunt more fun and personal.
