Okay, let's break this down. For the color-coding question, I think it's a mix of both. Subtle color-coding can act as a visual cue, which helps with memory recall because colors stand out. It also reduces mental load by making categories instantly recognizable without you having to think too hard. So it's not just one or the other; it's a nice balance.

As for the background sounds, it's really mostly personal preference. But if you want to optimize it, you could try switching sounds every 45 to 60 minutes instead of hourly. That's long enough to establish a rhythm but short enough to prevent monotony. You can always experiment and see which combo keeps you in the zone.

For the physical documents, both the environment and chunking matter. A clean workspace can reset your focus, while breaking tasks into smaller chunks makes them feel less daunting. It’s probably best to start with a small batch of files, like just invoices, to build momentum. Progress comes from both, but chunking is often what keeps you going.
