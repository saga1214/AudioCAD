**For Feeding Separation (Fat vs. Fussy):**
1.  **Chip/Timed Feeders:** Microchip-activated or electric timers release food slowly, controlling access for greedy cats.

**For Managing Health & Budget:**
1.  **Seek Donations:** Ask local shelters, rescues, or charities for pet food assistance.
2.  **Buy in Bulk & Stretch:** Purchase quality food in larger bags, then mix small amounts with cheap fillers like plain cooked rice or oats.
3.  **Prioritize Essential Vet Care:** Focus on low-cost spay/neuter and core vaccinations.
4.  **Preventative Care:**
    *   **Cleanliness:** Daily dish washing, regular litter box cleaning.
    *   **Basic Nutrition:** Offer fresh water daily. Supplement infrequently with safe, plain table scraps (e.g., cooked chicken, fish - *avoid onions/garlic/salt*).
    *   **Exercise:** Use homemade toys (string, cardboard) to keep cats active and prevent obesity (key to avoiding expensive health issues).
5.  **Monitor Closely:** Learn the fussy cats' body language. Catching early signs of illness allows for simpler home care or cheaper urgent treatment.

**Key Philosophy:** Focus on prevention (cleanliness, basic nutrition, exercise), stretch resources, seek community help, and manage essential vet care. Prioritize keeping the fussy eaters fed first thing at home to prevent hunger.
