Initally, the recommended approach is often to schedule a separate meeting for this specific topic. You can ask your boss if you could have about fifteen minutes of their time during one of your face-to-face meetings specifically to discuss career development. This clearly sets the agenda.

As an alternative, you could email your boss ahead of time. You could send a brief email to request a brief meeting to discuss your growth and future contributions to the team. This gives them time to prepare and can also help reduce the immediate anxiety of the in-person conversation.
