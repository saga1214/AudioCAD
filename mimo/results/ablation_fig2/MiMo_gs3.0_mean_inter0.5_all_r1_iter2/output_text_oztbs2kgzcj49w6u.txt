Yeah, there are some great places to learn more. The main attraction is the "Flight of Five" locks themselves. You can actually walk along the path above them and get a really good sense of the scale and the engineering involved. There's a visitor center there, too, with maps and information.

If you're really into the history, the Lockport Underground Railroad Center is also a great spot to visit. It gives you a lot of context about the canal's role in the city's development and its social history. It's a very informative place.
