Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimming diet progress and celiac considerations! Let's build on Northern CA weather goals and birthday prep.

Since you dislike cold and diet for suit visibility, I recommend Big Sur - reliably warm (60s-70s), with multiple ocean-access spots. For dining, I'll prioritize California's gluten-free innovation leaders.

Pack list refinement:
Bathing suits x2 + vibrant cover-up
Daytime: Flowy sundress/tank, denim cutoffs or linen shorts, band tees
Footwear: Quick-dry sandals + flip-flops
Essentials: High-SPF GF sunscreen, UV sunglasses, light windbreaker (even in warmth evenings)

Adventure alternatives to hot air balloon:
Whale Watching Tour - Majestic humpbacks, Bay Area access
Klamath River Paddleboarding - Warm water, stunning redwoods
Pismo Beach Kayaking - Swimmable, tide pools

Celiac-safe celebration plan:
Alcatraz Cruise (perfect suggestion!) - Confirm GF options at ticket counter
Dinner: Fog Harbor Fish House (Fisherman's Wharf) - Call ahead for GF prep
Birthday Treat: Trish's Mini Donuts (Pier 39) - Request GF station
Activity: Muir Woods Redwood Walk - Cooling, magical, wheelchair-accessible

Post-birthday warm getaway itinerary:
Destination: Pismo Beach (4-5 days, SUV-friendly)

Day 1: Arrival & Ocean Immersion
- Stay: Pismo Beach Resort (GF breakfast cafe, ocean-view rooms)
- Afternoon: Pismo Dunes Kayak/Paddleboard (rentals at Ocean Mesa)
- Evening: Dinner at Sandpiper Grill (GF menu options, oceanfront)

Day 2: Coastal Swimming & Wildlife
- Morning: Pismo State Beach - Swim at Pleasure Point (swell for paddles)
- Afternoon: Oceano Dunes National Monument - Dune boarding, tide pools
- Evening: Sunset at Pismo Beach Pier

Day 3: Diet-Friendly Dining & Nature
- Lunch: Sea Salt Kitchen (Pismo) - GF gourmet sandwiches
- Afternoon: Ostrichland State Park - Birdwatching, picnic
- Dinner: Pismo Beach Fish Company (GF seafood options)

Day 4: Redwood Relaxation
- Morning: Morro Bay Kayak Tour (calmer waters, sea otters)
- Afternoon: Morro Bay Exploration - Visit the Great Egg Rock Bird Sanctuary
- Evening: Dinner at Morro Bay Seafood Market (GF request accommodated)

Day 5: Farewell Swimming
- Morning: Swim at Morro Bay State Park (calm bay waters)
- Lunch: Gearheart Caffe (Morro) - GF pastries & sandwiches
- Departure

Transportation:
- Rent an SUV from Enterprise (Airport shuttle)
- Drive: LA to Pismo is 2.5 hours, SF to Pismo is 4.5 hours
- Local: Pismo has bike rentals for beach access

Budget Notes:
- Accommodation: $300-400/night (resort)
- Dining: $50-80/person/day (GF options well-priced)
- Activities: $40-60/day (rentals/tours)

This itinerary delivers guaranteed swimming weather, swimming opportunities, and curated celiac-safe experiences while accommodating your family size and SUV needs. The dunes and redwoods provide that "better than balloon ride" wonder you seek!
