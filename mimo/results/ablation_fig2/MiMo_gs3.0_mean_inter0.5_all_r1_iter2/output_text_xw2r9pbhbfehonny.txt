Alright, so if you're going to start your own channel, here’s a quick checklist for your next show:

First, get permission. Call the facility ahead of time and make sure they’re okay with you recording and posting it. Some places don’t allow it.

Then, set up your camera. If you’re using your phone, get it on a tripod or something steady so the video isn’t shaky. Make sure the audio is clear—maybe even use a small external mic if you can.

Think about the framing. You want to see both you and the puppets clearly, so maybe angle it right. You can also use a light to make sure you’re well-lit.

After the show, edit the video—cut out any mistakes or awkward pauses. Add some fun captions or sound effects to make it more engaging.

And finally, when you upload, use a nice thumbnail and a clear title, something like “Melvin the Magnificent at Sunnyside!” Then share it on places like YouTube or TikTok.

That’s basically it. You’ll get the hang of it fast. Good luck!
