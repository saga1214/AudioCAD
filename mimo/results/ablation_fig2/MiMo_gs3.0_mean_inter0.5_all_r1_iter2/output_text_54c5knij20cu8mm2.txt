Okay, let's break this down. For the brain hemisphere question: The left-right brain myth oversimplifies things. While there are differences in how each hemisphere specializes—like the left side often handling language and logic, and the right side for spatial and creative tasks—they work together constantly. It's not that one side is purely creative and the other is purely logical. So, calling it a "myth" is a bit reductive. It’s more accurate to say that the myth is an oversimplification of a more nuanced reality.
