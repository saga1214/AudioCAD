You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which is a great gluten-free foundation. Along those lines, rice cakes with gluten-free nut butter are always popular.

 trail mix is another classic camping snack too, since you can customize it with seeds like pumpkin and sunflower seeds, along with gluten-free dried fruits like cranberries or raisins. 

And since staying hydrated is very important while snack-free, don't forget to pack plenty of water!
