You've got a great start! To round out your setup for a short-haired cat (or kitten), here are a few more essentials to consider:

### 🛏️ Bed
- **Not all cats need a bed**, but many enjoy cozy spots. You could use a covered bed, a small box lined with blankets, or even a cat tree with a platform.  
- *Tip:* Some cats sleep anywhere (e.g., on your keyboard!), but a bed gives them a secure retreat.

### 🍽️ Food & Water Bowls
- **Stainless steel or ceramic bowls** are best (avoid plastic, which can harbor bacteria).  
- Place water bowls away from food to encourage hydration.

### 🌿 Hiding Spots & Vertical Space
- **Cat trees or shelves** let them observe their territory safely.  
- **Small hideaways** (like cardboard boxes or tunnels) appeal to shyer breeds like the British Shorthair.

### 🪟 Window Perches
- Cats love watching birds/traffic. A window seat or perch (like a cat tree) adds enrichment.

### 🧹 Grooming Tools (Important for Shorthairs!)
- **Brushing:** Short-haired breeds like the American Siamese or Maine Coon need weekly brushing to prevent tangles.  
- **Nail clippers & toothpaste** are also handy for maintenance.

### 💡 Final Tip:
Adopt from a shelter for a healthy short-haired cat—they’ll often be more adaptable! And remember: **supervise playtime** to prevent accidents, especially with curious breeds like the Siamese.
