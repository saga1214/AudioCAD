Yes, Calgary is in a spectacular part of the country. For an adventure just a short drive away, the Banff and Canmore areas are the classic choice. You can hike to stunning spots like Johnston Canyon or Moraine Lake, or go for a scenic drive on the Icefields Parkway. A bit further out, you could drive to Kananaskis Country for hiking and biking, or even take a day trip to the World's End Caverns for a unique underground adventure. If you want something closer, you could take an easy, scenic drive to the Elk Pass in Banff or along the Bow Valley Parkway.
