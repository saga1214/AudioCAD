Here's the key safety reminder and the steps:

**Critical Safety Note:**  
**You MUST perform the oil change on a perfectly flat, level surface.** A slight slope makes using car ramps under a vehicle extremely dangerous, as the car could roll onto you or the ramps. Do not attempt this on your driveway. Use a flat spot in your yard or a service center.

**Standard Oil Change Steps (for your car):**
1.  **Drain Old Oil:** Park on a level surface. Run the engine for 5-10 minutes to warm the oil slightly. Place the drain pan under the engine. Remove the drain plug (usually on the bottom of the engine block) to drain the old oil.
2.  **Replace Oil Filter:** Before installing the new oil filter, wipe the filter's mating surface clean. Apply a thin layer of new oil to the new filter's rubber gasket. Screw the new filter onto the engine's filter housing by hand until snug, then use a filter wrench to give it an *additional* 3/4 to 1 full turn.
3.  **Reinstall Drain Plug:** Put the drain plug back on the engine and torque it to the manufacturer's specification (found in your owner's manual).
4.  **Refill with New Oil:** Add the correct type and amount of new oil (check your manual) through the oil filler cap. Remove the cap after adding oil. Start the engine and let it run for 1-2 minutes. Turn it off, wait 30-60 seconds, and check the oil level on the dipstick. Top up if needed. Replace the filler cap.
