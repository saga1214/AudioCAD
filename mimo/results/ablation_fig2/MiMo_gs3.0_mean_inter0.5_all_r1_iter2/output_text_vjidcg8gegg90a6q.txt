For a brooder plate, the best and safest bedding is wood shavings, again using what's called deep litter. Just continuously add fresh ones on top of the composting flake shavings at the bottom to generate natural warmth and kill moisture. 

To prevent rodents overall, you can seal cracks with copper mesh, which rodents can't chew through. Also, make sure your coop door has a secure latch. 

Finally, be prepared for the worst-case scenario. If you have extreme cold snaps, you can get a small, battery-powered hand warmer and place it inside a box for the chicks to huddle around. 

I'd suggest starting with the deep litter shavings on top of concrete paired with a brooder plate. That should give you a very solid and safe setup.
