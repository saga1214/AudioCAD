### **1. Fin Choice Confirmed: Short Fins**
- **Why?** Short fins are compact, easier to travel with, require less leg strength, and are better for maneuvering in calm or reef sites. Ideal for beginners building confidence and comfort in the water.

### **2. Top Costa Rican Dive Locations**
#### **A. Tenero Island (Near Puerto Viejo de Talamanca)**  
- **Why?** Perfect for beginners. Shallow, clear waters with gentle currents.  
- **Highlights:**  
  - **"Coral Garden"**: Vibrant reefs with schools of fish.  
  - **Dolphin Dives**: Occasional dolphin sightings.  
  - **Easy Travel**: Accessible from the Pacific coast.  
- **Best For:** Calm dives, coral exploration, and getting acclimated.

#### **B. Cocos Island (Pacifica Coast)**  
- **Why?** Advanced site with dramatic landscapes and strong currents.  
- **Highlights:**  
  - **Drift Dives**: Explore underwater caves and cliffs.  
  - **Wrecks**: *El Sombrerito* (shipwreck) and *Cocos Island’s shore dives*.  
  - **Marine Life**: Dolphins, sharks, rays, and sea turtles (abundant!).  
- **Best For:** Experienced divers seeking adventure. *Note: Requires a boat tour; strong currents demand experience.*  
- **Travel Tip:** Fly to Santa Ana, then take a 1.5-hour boat from Playa Hermosa.

#### **C. Cahuita National Park (Caribbean Coast)**  
- **Why?** Gentle, accessible reefs perfect for beginners.  
- **Highlights:**  
  - **Coral Gardens**: Colorful corals and fish.  
  - **Manta Rays**: Frequently spotted!  
  - **Shore Dives**: Easy access from the beach.  
- **Best For:** Calm waters, coral biodiversity, and quick dives.
