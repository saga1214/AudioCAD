Okay, let's break this down. Chronic skin redness, or erythema, can come from many places. Common culprits include rosacea, long-term sun damage, genetics, contact dermatitis, or even certain medications. It's a wide field.

Now, about vasculitis. Think of it this way: your blood vessels are like pipes in a house. Vasculitis is when your immune system mistakenly attacks these pipes, causing them to become inflamed and swollen. This inflammation is what causes the redness, pain, and other symptoms. It can affect pipes of any size—large, medium, or small. This is why the symptoms can be so varied. Seeing a specialist, like a rheumatologist, is the best step to figure out if that's what's happening with you.
