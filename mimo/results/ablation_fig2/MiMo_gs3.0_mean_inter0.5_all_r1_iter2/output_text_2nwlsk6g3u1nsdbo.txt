Great question! Here's a quick recap of the **shrimp main courses** I suggested earlier, including ingredients:

### 1. Spicy Shrimp Scampi  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled/deveined  
- 8 oz pasta (e.g., linguine)  
- 4 tbsp butter  
- 3 garlic cloves, minced  
- ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped  
- ½ cup white wine (optional)  
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes  
- Salt and pepper to taste  

### 2. Shrimp Fra Diavolo  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled  
- 1 onion, diced  
- 2 bell peppers, diced  
- 3 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes  
- ¼ cup red wine vinegar  
- 2 tbsp olive oil  
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes  
- Salt and pepper to taste  

### 3. Cajun Shrimp & Grits  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled  
- 1 cup quick-cooking grits  
- 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning (adjust for spice)  
- ½ cup chicken broth  
- 2 tbsp butter  
- ¼ cup chopped green onions  
- Salt to taste  

---

### Bonus Tip:  
If you choose shrimp, **thaw them overnight in the fridge** and marinate in 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning + 1 tbsp olive oil for 30 minutes before cooking. This adds extra flavor!
