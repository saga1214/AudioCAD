For modern-day security in an art museum, the **first line of defense is controlling access at every entry point through visible security presence (e.g., guards) and screening procedures (e.g., bag checks)**. This initial layer manages exactly who and what enters the building, creating the first barrier against threats.  

### Key Components:  
1. **Visible Security Presence**: Deters intruders and establishes authority.  
2. protocols by letting them in after hours. Once inside, it was easy to overpower them.

### Why This Matters for Your Paper:  
Modern heists (like the 1990 Gardner Museum heist) often exploit human vulnerability. Controlling access directly addresses this by:  
- Minimizing entry points.  
- Training staff to follow protocols rigorously.  
- Integrating technology (e.g., turnstiles, facial recognition) for automated verification.  

Layered security beyond this line includes surveillance, alarms, and secure displays—but the *first* priority is preventing unauthorized entry at the source.
