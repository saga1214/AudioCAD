Here’s a sand-free, pirate-themed shopping list for your nautical party at the beach, designed for finger foods, easy handling, and allergy awareness (excluding watermelon):

**Nautical (Pirate) Party Food & Drink Shopping List (Beach-Friendly Finger Foods)**

**Theme Names & Allergy Notes:**
*   **"Buried Treasure" Mini Sliders:** Cut into 4 pieces.
*   **"Pirate Jewels":** Skewers of fruit.
*   **"Ship Wheels":** Pretzel rods.
*   **"Cannonballs":** Goldfish crackers.
*   Individual "Punch Bowls": Juice boxes/water bottles.
*   **ALLERGY ALERT:** **Exclude Watermelon.** Use Grapes or Apple Slices instead as "Jewels". Label clearly.

**1. Main Course (Simple & Portable):**
    *   Mini Ham & Cheese Pinwheels (cut into 4 pieces) - *Buried Treasure Sliders*
    *   Mini Veggie & Cheese Muffins - *Buried Treasure Sliders*
    *   Mini Turkey & Cheese Pinwheels (cut into 4 pieces) - *Buried Treasure Sliders*
    Pack in small treasure chest containers.

**2. Fruits ("Pirate Jewels"):**
    *   Seedless Grapes (halved if needed) - *Jewels*
    *   Apple Slices (tossed in lemon juice to prevent browning) - *Jewels* (Allergy: Watermelon excluded)
    *   Banana Slices (optional) - *Jewels*
    *   Berries (Strawberries, Blueberries, Raspberries) on skewers - *Jewels*
    Pack in a large, low bowl at the party.

**3. Snacks ("Cannonballs" & "Ship Wheels"):**
    *   Goldfish Crackers (in individual boxes or pre-packed bags labeled "Cannonballs") - *Cannonballs*
    *   Pretzel Rods (plain or salted) - *Ship Wheels*
    Pack Goldfish crackers and pretzel rods in small bags labeled accordingly.

**4. Drinks ("Pirate Punch Bowls"):**
    *   Individual Juice Boxes (variety: Apple, Orange, Grape, Berry Blast) - *Punch Bowls*
    *   Individual Water Bottles (clear or blue) - *Punch Bowls*
    Pack juice boxes and water bottles in a large bin or cooler.

**5. Extras & Dips (Sand-Resistant):**
    *   Cheese Cubes or Mini Cheese Sticks - *Ship Wheels* (Pack in sealed bags)
    *   Mini独立包装的Chips (如Tortilla Chips) - *Ship Wheels* (Pack in sealed bags)
    *   Hummus or Pita Chips Dip: Serve with small pita chips or veggie sticks in a sealed container with a lid.
    Pack cheese cubes/chips and dip in sealed containers.

**Cake excluded as making!**

**Theme Packaging Ideas:**
*   **Treasure Chest:** Use a small wooden treasure chest or decorated cardboard box as the main serving dish for the sliders.
*   **Skewer Bowls:** Place fruit skewers upright in a low, wide bowl decorated with a "J" flag.
*   **"Cannonballs" Bag:** Label bags of Goldfish crackers clearly.
*   **"Ship Wheels" Bag:** Label bags of pretzel rods clearly.
*   **Punch Bowls:** Label juice boxes/water bottles with fun names like "Pirate Punch" or "Treasure Juice".

**Why This Works:**
*   **Finger Foods:** All items are easy to pick up and eat without utensils.
*   **Minimal Sand Risk:** Individual packaging (juice boxes, sealed bags, containers) seals out sand. Skewers are easy to grip. Slices are bite-sized.
*   **Allergy Friendly:** Watermelon excluded. Clearly label alternatives.
*   **Beach Ready:** Easy to transport and serve at the beach. Juice boxes/water bottles prevent spills.
*   **Pirate Theme:** Creative naming makes everything part of the fun.
