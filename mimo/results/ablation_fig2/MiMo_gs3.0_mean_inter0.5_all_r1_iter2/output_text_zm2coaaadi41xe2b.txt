Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! 

For Asian noodles, I'd suggest:
First, Wonton Noodle House for classic Chinese ramen and wontons.
Second, Saizeriya for Italian-Japanese fusion, it's budget-friendly but still delicious.
Third, Pho 79 for amazing Vietnamese pho, which is a noodle dish too.

For protein bars, I'd recommend:
First, Quest Nutrition, they’re high-protein and low-sugar.
Second, Clif Bars, they’re a good balance of protein and taste.
Third, RXBAR, they’re simple and clean ingredients.

Hope you enjoy your meal!
