For an end-of-year celebration, a **hybrid approach** works best—balancing fun, inclusivity, and practicality. Here’s a tailored plan:

### 🎉 **Venue & Activities**  
- **Indoor/Outdoor Hybrid**: Start inside the cafeteria for setup (safety, weather-proof) and end at the park for activities.  
- **Rainy-Day Backup**: Have a simple craft station (e.g., badge-making or decorating sashes) ready indoors if needed.  
- **Low-Commitment Fun**: Opt for **wheelbarrow races** or **mini-Olympics** (easier than a full 3-legged race). Keep it short (1–2 hours) to avoid exhaustion.

### 🍕 **Food & Logistics**  
- **Snack Strategy**:  
  - **Ice Cream/Sundaes**: A hit for kids—offer dairy-free toppings since chips/dips off-limits for you.  
  - **Pizza**: Quick and popular, but pair with a healthier option (e.g., veggie cups) to balance.  
  - **Parent Partnership**: Ask parents to **bring a dish** (e.g., cake, cookies, or sides) to share costs and effort.  
- **Budget-Friendly Meals**: BBQs can get pricey—**pre-order pizza** or **cook a simple campfire meal** (s’mores + hot dogs) if camping at a nearby park.  

### 💬 **Parent Involvement**  
- **Dues Breakdown**:  
  - **Patch/Sash Costs**: Parents pay directly (provide a clear cost list upfront).  
  - **Event Fees**: For camping/BBQs, split costs equally or ask for small donations.  
- **Volunteer Help**: Recruit parents for setup/cleanup—offer to coordinate activities so they handle logistics.  

### ✅ **Key Takeaways**  
1. **Keep it simple**: Focus on 1–2 activities (e.g., games + snacks) to avoid burnout.  
2. **Inclusive food**: Ice cream + pizza covers most preferences, with dairy-free options.  
3. **Lean on parents**: Delegate tasks to reduce your workload—especially while pregnant!  
4. **Celebrate achievements**: Host a quick "trophy" ceremony for earned patches to make it meaningful.  

You’ll do great—your daughters are lucky to have a mom who stepped up! Start small, celebrate their progress, and enjoy the parties (minus the dip if needed). 🍪
