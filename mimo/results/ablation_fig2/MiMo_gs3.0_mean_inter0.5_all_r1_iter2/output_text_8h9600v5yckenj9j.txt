For candy tomorrow, grab the classics that always sell well: Reese’s Cups, Snickers, Twix, and Hershey’s Kisses. You could also get some Halloween-themed bags of gummies, like worms or ghosts, and maybe some mini chocolate bars or sour candies for a fun twist. If you want to avoid allergies again, just double-check the ingredients on the candy packaging to make sure artificial dyes aren’t used. Keep the candy in a spooky-looking bowl or bin to make it feel festive!
