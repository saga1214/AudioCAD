Here are quick, elegant recipes for both cocktails — keeping soda-free and inclusive of diets:

### 1. **Vodka Martini** (Soda Allergy Safe)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz dry vermouth  
- ½ oz lemon juice  
- 1 dash orange bitters  
**Instructions**:  
Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a chilled martini glass. Garnish with a lemon twist.  

### 2. Drinkable "Argentine Fernet"** (Inspired Fernet-Branca Style)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1.5 oz **Fernet-Branca** (or any Argentine-style herbal liqueur)  
- 1 oz soda water (ensure no carbonation)  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Ice  
**Instructions**:  
Shake Fernet, syrup, bitters, and ice. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice. Top with soda. Garnish with an orange twist.  

### Bonus: **Vodka Soda** (Non-Alcoholic)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz vodka  
- ½ oz fresh lime juice  
- Tonic water or soda water  
**Instructions**:  
Shake vodka and lime juice with ice. Strain into a glass. Top with tonic/soda. Garnish with lime wedge.  

### Key Tips:  
- **Soda-Free Mixers**: Use tonic, soda water, or lime soda.  
- **Vegetarian Note**: All recipes are veg-friendly.
