Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's ten coolers minimum just for drinks and snacks food alone! Let's build a mess-minimal, grab-and-go machine. Here's your battle plan, friend group dad-to-dad.

First, for drinks, you'll want over 100 water bottles for everyone. Add 80 low-sugar juice boxes for the kids. For the dads who want them, grab about 80 sugar-free sodas, maybe split it half Pepsi half Coke. Pack plenty of refillable water bottles for refills throughout the day. For everyone, single-serve boxes of unsweetened applesauce and individual cartons of chocolate milk are also great choices.

Next, for snacks, cheese sticks are a must for the kids. Baby carrots paired single-serving cups of hummus cups are another mess-free favorite. Individual pretzels or trail mix bags are also perfect. Fresh grapes are always a hit.

For lunch, focus on wraps or sandwiches packed individually.

Now for the main event—the calzones. Since you can't find pre-made ones, a pizza place with a pickup window is your best bet. You can ask them about mini-calzone options or even regular ones and just ask for them to be cut into halves or quarters, which makes them super easy to grab. You could even ask about a 'build-your-own' style order where they come with toppings ready but still sealed, which would be awesome. Just be sure to pack napkins separately.

For dessert, to keep things simple, grab cupcakes or maybe brownies.

So, to sum it up: you're aiming for mess-free, grab-and-go options that cover all the bases. This plan should make it a great day for the whole crew. Have an amazing time at the park!
