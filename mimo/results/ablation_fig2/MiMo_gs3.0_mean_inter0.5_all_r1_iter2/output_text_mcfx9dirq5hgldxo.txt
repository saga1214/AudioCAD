Adapted for Madrid, Spain, must-tries include:

First, paella—the iconic Spanish rice dish. Then tapas, which are these wonderful small dishes like olives or jamón.

For a full meal, you could try a traditional 'cocido madrileño,' which is a hearty stew, or 'montaditos,' which are like small sandwiches.

And of course, you can't miss churros with chocolate... and flamenca, their famous chorizo.
