Okay, diesel camper vans age, and maintenance before a long trip is crucial. For brakes, a good way to check is to have a mechanic do a full inspection—they’ll look for things like worn pads or rotors, and maybe even test the parking brake. Some signs of serious wear are things like a high-pitched squeal, or the brake pedal feeling soft. For spark plugs, diesel engines use high-torque ignition systems, so they’re different from gas engines. You’d need to have a mechanic do that job—it’s not really something a DIY person should try on an old diesel. The better idea is to have a trusted mechanic do a full pre-trip inspection covering everything from brakes to fluids to tires. It’s worth the peace of mind.
