That line of thought about genetic mutations is interesting, but the chicken would still be considered to have come first.

Here's why: For a mutation in a chicken's eggshell protein to even exist, you need a hen. Evolution requires specific biological structures, and that protein is unique to hens. So, without an actual hen, that specific protein and the related mutation couldn't have formed. The creature that hatched from the egg was not a true chicken yet; it was a very close ancestor. The first actual chicken emerged when that creature hatched.
