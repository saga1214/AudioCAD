Yes, for both groups, it was a moment of relief and gratitude. The Pilgrims were celebrating a hard-won survival, and the Wampanoag were celebrating their own harvest and strengthening a vital alliance.

But it's important to remember this was just one of many such events in their history. The traditional Thanksgiving story often focuses only on this one event, completely ignoring the centuries of violence and land theft that followed.

By combining both perspectives, we can honor the children making the play while also acknowledging the full, and much more complex, history.
