Okay, so you two met gaming-wise this year, van vibes, West Coast starting point... driving down combines Joshua Tree's surreal desert magic with Nevada's openness is smart logistics. Let's build this adventure step-by-step:

**Phase 1: West Coast to Joshua Tree CA (3-4 hours drive)**
* **Stay:** Cholla Cactus Campground (inside park) - $30/night. Book IMMEDIATELY on Recreation.gov - they sell out weeks ahead. *Why unique:* You'll sleep surrounded by glowing cholla cactus trees at night. Bonus: Hike Hidden Valley for slot canyons or Keys View for panoramic desert vistas.
* **Adventure Hook:** Stargazing - Joshua Tree has world-class dark skies. Download the SkyView app to identify constellations together.

**Phase 2: Joshua Tree to Nevada BLM Land (2.5-3 hours)**
* **Drive Route:** Take I-10 E to I-15 N (about 2.5hrs). Exit onto NV-165 towards Moapa.
* **Free Camping Spot:** **Ely BLM Campground** (map it on Google Maps). Public access, no facilities, $0. 
* **Unique Nevada Hook:** Stop at **Hoover Dam** (tour the spillway) or **Valley of Fire State Park** ($10/vehicle, stunning red rocks). For *truly* remote desert, find dispersed BLM land near **St. George UT** (just across NV border) - beautiful red sandstone cliffs.

**Phase 3: Vegas Finale (1.5 hours from Ely)**
* **"Proper Vegas" Experience:** Skip tourist traps. Do:
    * **Free Show:** Bellagio Fountains water show (dusk)
    * **Unique Bite:** Gourmet vegan diner at **Compass** (Cosmopolitan) OR historic Italian at **Eataly** (The Cosmopolitan)
    * **Luxury Bath:** Spa day at **Setai** (Aria) or **Qua Baths** (Wynn) - book a couples massage + soak in thermal pools.
    * **Sunset:** Rooftop bar at **Sky Lounge** (Cosmopolitan) or **Skybar** (The Venetian)
* **Stay:** **The Cosmopolitan** - it's the epitome of Vegas chic (book NOW). *Pro Tip:* Use the "vegas.com" app for free shuttle buses between casinos.

**Budget Breakdown (2 people, 5 days):**
* Gas/Van: $400-$600 (round trip)
* Campsites: $60 (Joshua Tree) + $0 (BLM) = $60
* Vegas Hotel: $1,200-$2,000 (mid-range suite)
* Food/Drinks: $300-$500 (mix of camp cooking & nice dinners)
* Total: $1,960-$3,160

**Adventure Checklist:**
1. **Gear:** Satellite communicator (Garmin inReach), first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, extra water (5+ gallons/day)
2. **Permits:** Joshua Tree (free, but requires reservation), National Forests (check recreation.gov for BLM access points)
3. **Safety:** Download offline maps (Gaia GPS), carry a full tank of gas when entering remote areas, know your state/emergency contacts

This combines new relationship excitement (unique landscapes), budget adventure (free camping), and Vegas luxury without crowds. The Nevada-to-Joshua Tree route lets you experience *two* distinct West Coast ecosystems in one drive. Pack for wild temperature swings - desert days are 100°F+ but nights dip to 40°F!
