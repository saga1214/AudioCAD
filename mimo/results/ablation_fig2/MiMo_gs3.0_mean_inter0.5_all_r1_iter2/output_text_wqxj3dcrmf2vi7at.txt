Okay, corgi princess treats! Let's categorize homemade ideas first, making sure they're safe and portionable.

Homemade Homemade:
1. Mashed Pumpkin Bites: Freeze small scoops of plain canned pumpkin puree. Chilly, good fiber.
2P Peanut Oat Cookies: Mashed peanut butter (NO SYOLITOL check bag!) mixed with rolled oats. Bake flat & cut small. Classic protein/fat.
3 Cooked Chicken Carot Sticks: Thin carrot sticks for crunch & Vits. Small chicken bits for pure protein reward.

Homemade Safety Portion: ALL small pieces!

Store-Bought Brands (Check ingredients!):
1 Blue Buffalo: Known for whole nutrition, variety of shapes/sizes.
2 Wellness: Another major brand with good ingredient focus.
3 Zuke's: Famous for small, kibble-like dehydrated treats. Lots of flavors.
4 Greenies: Dental chew treats (often come in smaller "tiny" versions).
5 Merrick: Quality brand, treats are usually grain-inclusive.

Homemade Safety Portion: ALL small pieces!

Training Basics Sit:
- Lure: Hold treat near nose.
- Raise: Move treat slowly up and OVER her head (she'll turn her head and sit).
- Command: The instant her butt touches floor, say "SIT!" and give treat.
- Repeat: Practice many times daily. Corgis learn fast with this visual cue!

Potty Training Tips:
1 Consistency: Take her OUT to the same spot frequently (after waking, eating, play, etc.).
2 Timing: Reward IMMEDIATELY when she goes outside.
3 Feeding Schedule: Regular meals help predict bathroom times.
4 Accident Cleanse: Use an enzymatic cleaner to remove scent completely. Never punish.
