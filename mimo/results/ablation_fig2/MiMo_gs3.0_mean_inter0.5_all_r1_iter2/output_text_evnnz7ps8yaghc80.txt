That's a fantastic plan. Having a schedule is key. Here is a sample weekly plan to keep you on track.

I suggest starting each session with a five-minute warm-up, like just throwing balls into the air. Then, you could spend about 20 minutes focusing on two-bag one-hand drills. After that, dedicate another 20 minutes to transitioning from two to three beanbags. Finally, use the last 10 minutes of your session to try scarves or tennis balls.

For a full week, you could practice about five days, maybe on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday, for about 30 to 45 minutes each day. This gives you time to rest and still get consistent practice. You could even try to show him something simple on Sunday! Good luck!
