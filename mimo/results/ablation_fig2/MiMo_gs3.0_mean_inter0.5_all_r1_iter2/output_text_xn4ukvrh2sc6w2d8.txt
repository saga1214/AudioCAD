Here are classic, bartender-ready recipes for all your cocktails, including the egg white technique:

###  **Old Fashioned**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  2 oz bourbon (e.g., Maker’s Mark)  
  1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  3 dashes Angostura budditors  
  Orange twist (for garnish)  
- **Method**:  
  1. Muddle sugar cube with ½ oz water in a mixing glass.  
  2. Add bourbon and bitters. Stir with ice for 30 seconds.  
  3. Strain into a **rocks glass over fresh ice**.  
  4. Express orange twist over drink (squeeze oils into foam), discard peel.  

###  **Paper Plane**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  2 oz gin (e.g., Hendrick’s)  
  ¾ oz Aperol  
  ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
 ¾ oz fresh lemon juiceroju  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a **coupe or Nick & Nora glass**.  
  3. Garnish: Bitter peel (rub on rim, squeeze oils into drink).  

###  **French 75 (Additional Gin Cocktail)**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  1.5 oz gin (e.g., Beefeater)  
  ¾ oz lemon juiceroju  
  ¾ oz simple syrup  
  2 oz champagne/champagne flute-filling sparkling wine  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and syrup with ice.  
  2. Strain into a **champagne flute**.  
  3. Top with sparkling wine.  
  4. Garnish: Lemon twist.  

###  **Whiskey Sour**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  2 oz bourbon OR rye  
  ¾ oz fresh lemon juiceroju  
  ½ oz simple syrup  
  1 egg white (optional)  
- **Method**:  
  - *With egg white*:  
    1. **Dry shake** (no ice): Add egg white, bourbon, lemon juice, and syrup to shaker. Shake 10 sec.  
    2. **Wet shake**: Add ice, shake 10 more sec.  
    3. Strain into **rocks glass over ice**.  
    4. Garnish: Cherry OR orange slice.  
  - *Without egg white*: Skip dry shake; stir ingredients with ice.  

###  **Aviation**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  2 oz gin  
  ¾ oz maraschino liqueur (Luxardo)  
  ¼ oz crème de violette  
  ¾ oz fresh lemon juiceroju  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a **cocktail glass**.  
  3. Garnish: Bitter twist (rub on rim, squeeze oils).  

---

###  **Egg White Tip**:  
- Use pasteurized eggs for safety.  
- Dry shaking emulsifies the egg for a velvety texture—essential for Whiskey Sours Manhattan variants.
