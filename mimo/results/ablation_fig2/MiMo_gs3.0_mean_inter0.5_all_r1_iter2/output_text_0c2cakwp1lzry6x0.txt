Here’s a step-by-step guide to install your TV, hanging planters, and floating shelves while staying mindful of your new apartment rules and energy needs:

### 1. **TV Mount Installation**  
   - **Check Landlord Rules**: Confirm mounts won’t damage walls (use removable adhesive mounts if drilling is prohibited).  
   - **Weight & Wall Type**:  
     - Use a stud finder to locate studs (required for heavy TVs >33 lbs).  
     - If no studs, use heavy-duty drywall anchors or rental-safe adhesive mounts.  
   - **Mount Type**:  
     - *Wall-mounted*: Secure bracket to wall, align TV, and tighten screws.  
     - *Floating shelf mount*: Attach shelf to wall first, then place TV face-down on it.  
   - **Cable Management**: Use cable clips to hide wires behind the mount.  

### 2. **Hanging Planters**  
   - **Weight Calculation**:  
     - Test-hang planters with water to ensure they won’t tip.  
     - Use **S hooks** or **hanging baskets** rated for 10+ lbs each.  
   - **Installation**:  
     - **Pegboard**: Attach hooks to a pegboard section.  
     - **Ceiling**: Use a **ceiling-rated hook** (e.g., S-hooks) anchored into studs.  
     - **Wall**: Apply **3M Command™ strips** (test adhesion first).  
   - **Noise Tip**: Use a power drill with low torque to avoid loud vibrations.  

### 3. **Floating Shelves**  
   - **Materials**: Use lightweight shelves (MDF or particleboard) to avoid drilling.  
   - **Installation**:  
     - **Adhesive Method**:  
       - Clean wall surface.  
       - Press adhesive anchors into wall, then press shelf onto anchors.  
     - **Stud Anchors (if allowed)**:  
       - Use **toggle bolts** or **plastic anchors** for heavy shelves.  
   - **Placement**:  
     - Install shelves 6–12" above eye level.  
     - Space shelves 12–24" apart for balance.  

### ⚠️ **Rule-Friendly Tips**  
- **Timing**: Do installations on weekdays (early morning/late evening) to avoid late noise.  
- **Tools**: Use a quiet drill (or manual screwdriver for adhesives) and avoid power saws. Drill holes gently—no loud drilling!  
- **Storage**: Keep tools/decor out of work zones until installation day.  

### 🌟 **Decorating Strategy for Sundays**  
- **2-Hour Focus Blocks**: Schedule 30-minute intervals (e.g., 10 AM, 1 PM, 4 PM) for decorating.  
- **Energy Boosters**:  
  - Listen to upbeat music while working.  
  - Reward yourself after each block (e.g., coffee break).  

### 💡 **Pro Tips**  
- **Pre-Plan**: Take photos of furniture/art *before* moving in to visualize placement.  
- **Lighting**: Use warm LED strips under shelves for ambiance.  
- **Declutter**: Donate unused items before decorating to avoid overwhelm.  

By following this plan, you’ll transform your space without compromising focus, noise rules, or energy levels. Happy decorating! 🛠️✨
