### **KEY NOTES**

   - Prep ahead: Ham, scalloped potatoes, carrots, and asparagus can be made 1–2 days early.

3. **Budget Tip**:  
   - Use store-bought sliders/pork sausage for pigs in a blanket to save time.  
   - Ham often goes on sale around holidays—check for deals!
