Yes, absolutely! That is actually a huge advantage for a children's book. Many children's books are written by authors who are also illustrators. You can absolutely submit a complete manuscript with your own illustrations. In fact, publishers often prefer it.

However, there is one very important detail to consider. The illustrations you create will almost certainly need to be redrawn in a professional style to match the publisher's expectations. They have very specific guidelines for art, color, and layout. So, you would submit your work as a proposal, and if they accept it, they will hire an art director to help you refine the illustrations.

So, your plan would be to: first, finish your manuscript. Second, create your best illustrations to accompany it. Third, research agents who represent books with that style. And fourth, submit your package as a complete proposal.
