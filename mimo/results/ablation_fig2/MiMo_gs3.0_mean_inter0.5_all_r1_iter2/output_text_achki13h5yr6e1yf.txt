Of course. To recap all the ingredients and their total estimated costs:

Chicken Breasts, two pounds cost eight to fifteen dollars.
All-Purpose Flour comes three to five dollars.
Dozen Eggs, three to seven dollars.
Container of Breadcrumbs, two to four dollars.
Vegetable Oil bottle, four to eight dollars.
Salt and Pepper set, three to six dollars.
Potatoes for the side, three to six dollars.
Garlic for the potatoes, about one dollar.
Butter, four to seven dollars.
And finally, a half-gallon of Milk, three to five dollars.

The total estimated cost for this family dinner comes out to be between thirty-five and sixty dollars.
