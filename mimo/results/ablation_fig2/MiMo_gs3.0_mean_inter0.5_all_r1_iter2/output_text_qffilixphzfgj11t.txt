Bruno avoids apples, bananas, broccoli, but here are some safe green vegetables he might enjoy:

Kale is great for their diet and high in nutrients. 

Spinach is also a good choice, just make sure to rinse it well to get rid of any grit.

Cucumber is a nice, crunchy, and hydrating option.

Asparagus can be a fun treat, just be sure to cut it into small, bite-sized pieces.

And finally, bell peppers, whether red, green, or yellow, are a great source of vitamins.

Just double-check to make sure he specifically avoids any of these, but these are generally great options for parrots.
