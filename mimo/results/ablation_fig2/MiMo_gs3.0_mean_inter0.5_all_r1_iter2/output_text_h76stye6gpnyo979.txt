Of course! Juggling multiple passions and saving for different things can definitely feel overwhelming.

The key is to prioritize and organize.

Here’s a simple, step-by-step approach you could try:

First, just list out all your goals—Salt Flats apartment down, cooking trend experiments—and rank them by importance right now.

Next, for each goal, create a small, specific action you can do this week. For Bolivia, maybe just bookmark one travel guide. For saving, set up an automatic transfer of a small amount to a special apartment fund.

Finally, think of small experiments for your other interests. If fermented foods, maybe just buy one condiment from that trend this week to try it out.

This way, you're making real, bite-sized progress on everything without feeling stressed.
