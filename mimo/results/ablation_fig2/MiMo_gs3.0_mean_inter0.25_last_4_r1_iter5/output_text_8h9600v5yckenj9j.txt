For candy tomorrow, grab classic Halloween favorites like Skittles, Reese’s Cups, gummy worms, and maybe some classic M&M's. Sour Patch Kids are also super popular. You could buy these in bulk bags or even a few giant bags of candy corn or candy apples if you want something that screams Halloween. Just try to avoid anything with artificial dye if allergies still top priority, but these options are usually dye-free or can be checked. Keep it simple and fun—guests love classic candies at parties like this!
