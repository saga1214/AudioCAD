Here’s a **nautical-themed nautical party menu** designed for sand-free beach kids, pirate immersion, and easy allergy management (excluding watermelon and strawberries). Everything is easy-to-handle finger food!

---

### **Nautical Menu: "Pirate’s Feast at Sea"**  
*(Theme: Ship wheels, treasure chests, pirate flags, and ocean colors)*

#### **1. Appetizers/Snacks (Sand-Free & Themed)**  
- **"Buried Treasure" Veggie Cups**  
  - Baby carrots, cucumber slices, and cherry tomatoes arranged like treasure in goldfish crackers "coins."  
  - *Allergy Note:* Ensure tomatoes are seedless if needed.  
- **"Ship’s Masts" Mini Sandwiches**  
  - Cut mini sandwiches (chicken nugget or ham & cheese) into triangles or rectangles. Use pretzel rods as "ship masts."  
- **"Pirate Gold" Cheese & Crackers**  
  - Cubed cheddar cheese on goldfish crackers or pretzel twists. Label with a tiny "Jolly Roger" sticker.  
- **Fruit Skewers as "Jellyfish"**  
  - Skewers with grapes, pineapple chunks, and melon balls (use blueberries if melon is risky). Avoid watermelon!  
- **"Treasure Chest" Snack Packs**  
  - Small cardboard boxes filled with:  
    - Apple slices (dipped in lemon juice to prevent browning, shaped like coins with a cookie cutter).  
    - Mini cheddar bunnies ("sea creatures").  
    - Pretzel "anchors."  

#### **2. Main "Feast" (Light & Portable)**  
- **"Captain’s Plunder" Chicken Bites**  
  - Mini chicken tenders or nuggets cut into triangles, served with:  
    - **"Cannonball" Tots** (crispy tater tots).  
    - **"Mermaid’s Kiss" Dip** (blueberry or purple corn dip).  
    - **"Saltwater" Tuna Salad Cups** (tuna in a can, mixed with mayo, celery, and relish in a mini cup).  
- **"Pirate’s Pretzel Flag"**  
  - Pretzel rods topped with chocolate chips ("pirate hats") and mini M&Ms ("jewels").  

#### **3. Drinks (Individual & Sand-Free)**  
- **Individual Juice Boxes**  
  - Label with pirate names: "Blackbeard’s Berry Blast" (strawberry-free!), "Long John’s Limeade," or "Mermaid’s Magic Punch" (blue raspberry).  
- **"Cursed" Water Bottles**  
  - Clear bottles with pirate flag stickers.  
- **"Cannonball" Cupcakes** (Your Cake Alternative!)  
  - Top with blue frosting, gummy fish, and sprinkles.  

#### **4. Allergy-Friendly Swaps**  
- **Instead of Watermelon:** Use **strawberries** (if no allergy) or **blueberries**.  
- **Avoid:** Pineapple (can be acidic), mango, or citrus if other allergies exist.  
- **Label Everything:** Use small flags or stickers to mark allergens.  

#### **5. Extras for Fun**  
- **"Treasure Hunt" Snacks:** Hide goldfish crackers or fruit "jewels" for kids to collect.  
- **Themed Utensils:** Mini plastic cutlery with ship wheels or anchors.  
- **Ice Cream:** "Pirate’s Booty" popsicles or frozen yogurt cups in mini cups.  

---

### **Why This Works at the Beach**  
- **No Sogginess:** Skewers, crackers, and individual boxes stay dry.  
- **Easy Handling:** No utensils needed—everything fits in one hand.  
- **Sand-Free:** Pre-portioned in containers or sealed.  
- **Theme Immersion:** Names, colors, and shapes tie into pirates and the sea.
