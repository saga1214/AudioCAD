Okay, diesel camper vans can be a handful before a long trip, so it's smart to check things out. For brakes, a key sign of wear is a high-pitched squealing sound when you brake. Also, if the brake pedal feels spongy or goes to the floor, that could mean the brake fluid is low or there's air in the system. You should also check if the brake pads themselves are thin—you can usually see that by looking at them when you lift the wheels. If they’re worn down, you’ll need to replace them. For spark plugs, diesel engines use high compression, so you need to check if they’re worn out. You can use a spark tester to see if there’s a spark. If they’re worn, you’ll need to replace them. It’s best to have a mechanic do this, but if you do it yourself, make sure you have the right spark plug for your diesel engine. Always check the diesel filter too—it’s critical for keeping the engine running smoothly. If you’re ever unsure, a diesel mechanic can give you a thorough check before you hit the road.
