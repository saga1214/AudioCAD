Okay, I'll give you a clear list of five products to avoid. Let's focus on that.

One: Non-stick cookware. Opt for stainless steel or cast iron instead.

Two: Grease-resistant food packaging, such as wrappers and popcorn bags.

Three: Items labeled waterproof or stain-resistant, like carpets.

Four: Cosmetics and floss that aren't labeled PFAS-free.

And five: Since tap water can be contaminated, using a certified water filter is a great preventative step.
