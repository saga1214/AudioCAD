That sounds like a wonderful plan! Vancouver is a fantastic base for road trips. Here are five great options for you to explore.

First, a short trip about an hour and a half north is Whistler. It's famous for skiing in the winter and hiking in the summer. You can also take a relaxing gondola ride up Wartime Peak.

Second, about two hours west, you can visit the magical Vancouver Island. You could explore the wildlife at the Butchart Gardens or hike through the ancient rainforest of Tofino.

Third, if you want to head east, a four-hour drive to the Kootenay region offers stunning lakes and mountains. You can visit the Banff Gondola for incredible views or take a scenic cruise on Kootenay Lake.

Fourth, a four-hour drive north to the Fraser Valley is great for a taste of BC's agriculture. You can visit a winery, enjoy some farm-to-table food, and even see some working farms.

Finally, for a longer journey, a seven-hour drive south to the Okanagan Valley is a must-visit. It's world-famous for its beautiful lakes and its huge wine industry. You can take a wine tour or just relax by the water at Puntis Harbour.

Enjoy your adventures!
