### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia:
**Key Rules**:  
1. **Prohibited at**: Signalized intersections, curves, hills, blind spots.  
2. **Visibility**: Must see **150m** (approx. 1.5 football fields) in both directions.  
3. **No Obstruction**: Can’t impede traffic.  
4. **Signs**: "No U-Turn" signs override rules.  

**Penalties for Illegal U-Turns**:  
- **Fine**: Up to $100 + 2 demerit points.  
- **Severity**: Reckless maneuvers may incur higher fines.  

**Quick Safety Tip**:  
If unsure, use designated U-turn lanes or intersections. When in doubt, wait for a clear opportunity.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Recipe (Classic Roman-Style):
**Ingredients for 2 servings**:  
- **200g spaghetti**  
- **100g guanciale** (or pancetta)  
- **1 large egg + 1 egg yolk**  
- **100g Pecorino Romano cheese**, grated  
- **Freshly ground black pepper**  
- **1 tbsp pasta water**  

**Steps**:  
1. Cook spaghetti in salted water until *al dente* (reserve 1 tbsp water).  
2. Fry guanciale in a pan until crispy. Remove, leaving 1 tbsp fat.  
3. Whisk eggs, cheese, and pepper.  
4. Add hot pasta to the pan with guanciale fat. Toss, then add egg mixture.  
5. Off heat, add pasta water (1 tsp at a time) to emulsify. Serve immediately.  

**Critical Tips**:  
- **No cream/garlic**—authentic carbonara relies on cheese and pepper.  
- **Temper eggs**: Add pasta to the pan *off heat* to avoid scrambling.  
- **Pasta water** binds the sauce.
