Following her birthday, for a 4-5 day warm in-state vacation for three (husband included), Imperial Beach/San Diego County coast is still ideal. Here’s a detailed plan:

**Destination:** Imperial Beach, CA (part of San Diego County - about 1.5-2 hours south of Northern Cal)
*   **Why:** Consistently warmest coastal weather in NorCal (60s-70sF), abundant warm swimming pools (many public, some resorts), plenty of casual/restaurant options, beautiful beaches, easy access from LA/NorCal via I-5.
*   **Accommodation:** **The Beachfront Inn** (Imperial Beach). Upscale, family-friendly, newer building (2021), dedicated pool area (warm!), multiple pools total, fitness center, beach access, kitchenettes. Safe, comfortable, and meets your budget expectations. (Booking.com/VRBO).

**Detailed 4-Day Itinerary:**

*   **Day 1: Arrival & Beach Relaxation**
    *   *Travel:* Drive south on I-5 (approx. 2 hours from e.g., SF Bay Area). Check into The Beachfront Inn.
    *   *Lunch:* Grab a casual bite near the hotel or pack a picnic for the beach.
    *   *Afternoon:* Head to **Ocean Beach** (a short drive/taxi). Relax on the sand, swim in the (usually warmer than interior) Pacific (check tide), rent a kayak/paddleboard.
    *   *Evening:* Dinner at **The Taco Stand** (Ocean Beach) - fantastic local seafood tacos (ask about GF options). Back to hotel.

*   **Day 2: Warm Pools & Coastal Exploration**
    *   *Morning:* Enjoy the **dedicated warm pool** at The Beachfront Inn! Swim, relax, sunbathe. Great for your bathing suits!
    *   *Lunch:* Pack a picnic for a hike at **Miles Land Trust** (near Imperial Beach) - easy coastal trails with ocean views (check trail conditions).
    *   *Afternoon:* Explore **Imperial Beach** itself: Walk the boardwalk, visit the **Fern Canyon** (short shuttle ride, stunning redwoods by the sea), see the **Tijuana River口** (views).
    *   *Dinner:* **Casa Adobes** (Imperial Beach) - upscale Mexican, excellent atmosphere, book ahead. Or **Lucky's Fish & Chips** (Ocean Beach) for casual seafood.

*   **Day 3: Adventure & Treat**
    *   *Morning:* **Whale Watching Tour** (Starline or Hornblower Cruises depart from Long Beach/Los Angeles, approx. 1 hour drive). Amazing alternative adventure without heights! (Ensure GF meal options arranged).
    *   *Lunch:* Picnic at **Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve** (short drive from San Diego) - stunning coastal cliffs, hiking, ocean views.
    *   *Afternoon:* **Swimming Pools:** Visit **Cabrillo National Monument** (Balboa Park, San Diego) - beautiful lighthouse, tides pool for snorkeling (warmer water!), and a large public swimming pool (check hours).
    *   *Dinner:* **Bub's Fish Co.** (Pacific Beach, San Diego) - iconic, fun, fresh seafood (check GF options). Or **Nobu Malibu** (if driving back north afterwards, approx. 2.5 hrs) for a splurge.

*   **Day 4: Big Sur Scenic Drive (Cold but Stunning)**
    *   *Morning:* Drive the breathtaking **Pacific Coast Highway (PCH)** from San Diego through Big Sur (approx. 4-5 hours drive). Stop at **Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park** (Hearst Castle area - stunning redwoods, check entry fees/reservations), **McWay Falls** (view from overlook), **Big Sur River Inn** (for photos).
    *   *Lunch:* Picnic near **Bixby Bridge** or **Undramatic Point**.
    *   Continue drive towards your home in Northern Cal. Pack a light sweater as it gets colder.

**Key Packing Additions for Trip:**
*   Beach towels, swimsuits (pack multiple!), flip-flops.
*   Reusable water bottles.
*   Basic first-aid kit, sunscreen (gluten-free!), insect repellent (coastal areas).
*   Layers for evenings/colder drive home.
*   Camera for the amazing scenery!
