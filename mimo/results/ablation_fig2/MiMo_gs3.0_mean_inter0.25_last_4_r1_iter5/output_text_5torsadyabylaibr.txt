Pie is a classic Thanksgiving dessert. You could start with a simple recipe like a classic pumpkin pie. Most of the work is done in the oven, so it's not too complex.

For a no-bake option, you could make cheesecake parfaits. You just layer a pre-made no-bake cheesecake mix with whipped cream and some granola in a container. It's very simple to assemble.

And for something a little different, you could try an apple crisp. You just bake sliced apples with a little sugar, and then top it with a simple oat and butter crumble that requires no baking.
