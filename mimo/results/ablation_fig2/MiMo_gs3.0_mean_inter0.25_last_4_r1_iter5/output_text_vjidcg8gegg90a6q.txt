For winter chicks on concrete, you'll need a thick layer of deep litter on top of rubber mats and sand. Again, to avoid attracting rodents while providing good insulation, pine-flake shavings are your best option using the deep litter method I mentioned earlier. Sand is another great choice. Just keep adding fresh materials on top of the composting material below. The generated heat will keep them warm and the materials at the top will stay fluffy for them to peck at.
