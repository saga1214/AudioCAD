Okay, you've got the essentials, but I'd strongly recommend adding a few more tools and supplies to make the job safer and easier. First, shims are crucial for getting base cabinets perfectly level. You can use wood shims, but plastic shims are better because they won upper cabinets from slipping.

You'll also need a stud finder that can measure the depth of the studs, which Habitat cabinets often have. For uppers, a measuring tape is essential for determining their height and spacing. For the base cabinets, you'll need a small saw to cut them to the right width if they're too long. And most importantly, please use a safety harness or a spotter if you're standing on a step stool to prevent any falls.

With these tools and the prep work done, you're all set to get those cabinets up! You should be able to tackle this project safely and save even more money.
