Okay, since you've light-sand and removed the hardware, here are a few more things to check off your list.

Make sure the doors you took off are completely clean and dry before you prime them.

For the cabinet boxes themselves, it's a good idea to protect the areas you don't want paint on. You can use painter's tape and some old rags.

Also, don't forget to protect the floors and countertops from any drips with some cardboard or plastic.

And lastly, make sure you're working in a well-ventilated space when you're using primer and paint.
