You're right, I see where I got mixed up. Let me clarify. The most common Boulevardier has whiskey to one and a quarter, vermouth at one, and Campari at one. Your friend's equal-parts version of all three at one ounce is also classic. However, the cocktail you're thinking of, Last Word, is indeed equal parts. For that, each ingredient is three-quarters of an ounce: three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an expressed maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. Garnish that one with a lime wedge. Hope that helps clear things up!
