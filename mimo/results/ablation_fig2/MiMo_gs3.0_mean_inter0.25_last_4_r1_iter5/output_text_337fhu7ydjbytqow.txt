Here's your optimized closing gear packing list, separated for the daytime 20-mile segment and the nighttime final 12-mile segment:

### **Daytime Segment (20 miles, overnight start)**
1. **Base Layer**: Moisture-wicking synthetic or merino wool top/bottom (avoid cotton).  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece jacket or light down vest (for warmth when standing/walking).  
3. **Outer Layer**: Windproof/water-resistant trail jacket (e.g., Gore-Tex or similar).  
4. Thin Beanie or Cap (for warmth at night).  
5. Lightweight Gloves (for grip and warmth).  
6. Headlamp or handheld light (with **extra batteries**).  
7. Lightweight Pack or Vest (to carry layers if needed).  

### **Nighttime Segment (Final 12 miles)**  
1. **Base Layer**: Moisture-wicking top/bottom (same as daytime).  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece or lightweight insulated jacket (e.g., down vest or puffy).  
3. **Outer Layer**: Windproof/water-resistant jacket (same as daytime, or a heavier version if colder).  
4. Warm Hat (balaclava or fleece beanie for extreme cold).  
5. Warm Gloves (consider merino wool or insulated).  
6. Buff or Neoprene Sleeve (for extra warmth on arms/neck).  
7. **Headlamp or Second Light** (with **extra batteries** – critical!).  
8. Lightweight Pack/Vest (for carrying layers if needed).  
9. Extra Socks (in case feet get wet).  

### **Key Adjustments for Night Pacing**  
- **Warmth is critical**: Night temps will drop significantly. Prioritize layers you can add/remove quickly.  
- **Light reliability**: Test your headlamp/phone light beforehand. Night running is slippery when wet.  
- **Extremities freeze fast**: Gloves/hat are non-negotiable.  
- **Packability**: Keep gear lightweight but packable (e.g., packable puffy jacket). Aid stations mean you won refit all layers, so pack only what you need.  

### **Bonus Tips**  
- **Shoes**: Trail runners with grip (same for both segments). Bring a spare pair if you sweat heavily.  
- **Weather Prep**: Mid-30s hilly trails can get windy—windproof outer layer is essential.  

This setup keeps you comfortable, adaptable, and ready for changing conditions. Pace strong! 🏃‍♀️💨
