That's a very good observation. Older books are generally rarer and can appreciate more significantly.

Here are 10 notable books from the 1940s and 50s that are highly sought after. You'll need to find them in good condition, but they are all viable investments. 

First, there's *Action Comics* number one again, Superman's first full appearance, sold for six million not long ago.
Second, *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen for Spider-Man.
Third, *X-Men* number one from 1963, which introduced the team by Chris Claremont, a foundational comic.
Fourth, *Fantastic Four* number one from 1963, where the Fantastic Four debuted.
Fifth, *The Amazing Spider-Man* number fifty, where the iconic "Venom" suit was first revealed.
Sixth, *The Incredible Hulk* number one from 1962, which introduced the character.
Seventh, *Tales to Astonish* number forty-five, which is where the first appearance of the Wasp was revealed.
Eighth, *The Defenders* number one from 1967, Luke Cage's own team.
Ninth, *Ghost Rider* number one from 1972, featuring the first appearance of the character.
And tenth, *The Silver Surfer* number one from 1966, the first appearance of the Surfer.

These are all cornerstone issues for their respective publishers, which guarantees long-term demand.
