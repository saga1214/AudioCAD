Yes, the Lockport area is full of history, and you have some excellent options to learn about it. 

The main attraction is the Lockport Canal Museum and the Lockport Underground Railroad Center. They have a great exhibit called the "Schoonmaker's Dike" that you can walk through, which really brings the old canal system to life. You can also see a working replica of the "Old Erie Canal" boat. 

If you'd rather learn more in a museum setting, the Buffalo Museum of Art actually has an extensive collection of canal-related artifacts and paintings. And for a really unique experience, you can visit the National Museum of the U.S. Navy in Batavia. It's located in the original Lock 31 building, and it has a fantastic interactive exhibit on how the canal was used to transport warships during the Civil War.
