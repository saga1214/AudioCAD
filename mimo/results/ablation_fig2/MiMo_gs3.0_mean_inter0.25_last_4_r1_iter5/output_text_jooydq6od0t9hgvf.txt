Here are quick, elegant recipes for your vodka mixer and Argentine-inspired Fernet cocktail—perfect for a formal dinner party crowd:

### 🥰 **Vodka Mixer (Refreshing & Light)**  
**Name:** *Cucumber-Mint Spritz*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 4 oz sparkling water (soda-free)  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- 3 cucumber slices (muddled)  
- 5 fresh mint leaves  
- Ice  
- *Garnish:* Mint sprig + cucumber ribbon  

**How to:** Muddle cucumber + mint. Add vodka, lime juice, ice, and top with sparkling water. Stir lightly. Serve over ice with garnish.  

---

### 🥃 **Argentine Fernet Cocktail (Bold & Bitter-Sweet)**  
**Name:** *Fernet & Friends* (Inspired by Fernet-Morin)  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca (or Argentine Fernet)  
- 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice  
- 0.75 oz fresh lime juice  
- 0.5 oz honey syrup (equal parts honey + hot water, cooled)  
- 0.5 oz pomegranate molasses  
- 2 oz soda water (chilled)  
- *Garnish:* Grapefruit twist + pomegranate seeds  

**How to:** Shake all ingredients *except soda water* with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Top with soda. Garnish.
