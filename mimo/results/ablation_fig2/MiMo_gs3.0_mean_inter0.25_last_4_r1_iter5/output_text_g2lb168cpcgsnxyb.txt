While the connection you have is vital, in this specific case, safety and environmental management must come as the number one priority within those first crucial minutes.

First, regarding supervision, I would strongly recommend you report this lack of proper oversight to his B.C.B.A. Sessions should always be supervised, and dogs running around adds another layer of risk.

Second, the cluttered house and elopement are major challenges. In your opening 20 to 30 minutes, a big part of being effective is managing his escape urges. This means making your time together so reinforcing that he wants to stay, and proactively preventing any opportunities for him to leave or elope.

Once those safety factors are managed, then you can focus on using play and his interests to teach and engage him.
