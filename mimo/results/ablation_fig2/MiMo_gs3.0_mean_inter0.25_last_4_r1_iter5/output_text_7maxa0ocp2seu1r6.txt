No problem! Here’s a clear, categorized recap of all the measurements to cover your small tailoring business needs:

### **Core Body Measurements (take for EVERY fitting)**  
1. **Bust**: Under the arms, fullest part of the chest.  
2. **Natural Waist**: Smallest part of the torso (not the belt line).  
3. **Hips**: Widest part of the hips, typically 8–10 inches below the waist.  

### **Item-Specific Measurements (for highly fitted garments)**  
- **For Tops/Jackets**:  
  - Neck (collar size).  
  - Shoulder Width (across upper back).  
  - Bicep (for sleeve fit).  
  - Upper Back (for yoke/jackets).  
- **For Bottoms (Pants/Skirts)**:  
  - Thigh (inner thigh to outer thigh).  
  - Hip Circumference.  
  - **Back Rise** (crotch to waistband; *critical for tall clients*).  
  - **Front Rise** (waistband to crotch).  

### **For Pants Length**  
- **Inseam**: Crotch to bottom hem (take from the crotch, not the waistband).  
- **Shoe Length**: *Always try on shoes during fitting* to ensure pants hit at the top of the shoe.  
  - If client is tall (like your 6'4" neighbor):  
    - Back rise should be **2–4 inches longer** than front rise to prevent sagging.  
    - Hip/thigh should have **extra ease** (1–2 inches) for sitting comfort.  
    - Inseam: Use pinned length to shoe tops (likely ~34–35" for sneakers).  

### **Quick Reference Summary**  
| **Garment Type** | **Essential Measurements** |  
|------------------|----------------------------|  
| **Tops/Jackets** | Bust, Neck, Shoulders, Bicep |  
| **Bottoms** | Bust, Waist, Hips, Thigh, Back Rise |  
| **Pants Length** | Inseam + **always confirm with shoes** |  

You’ve got this! Start with the core three measurements, then layer on extras as needed. For your neighbor’s pants, prioritize **back rise length** and **hip/thigh ease** for comfort. And next fitting—bring shoes! 🧵✨
