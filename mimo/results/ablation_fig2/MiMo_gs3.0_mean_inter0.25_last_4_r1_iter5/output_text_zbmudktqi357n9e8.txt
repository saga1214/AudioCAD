Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration, but it really depends on when you go. The colors you see there will change depending on the season. 

If you go during their autumn, which is about September through November, you’ll see that beautiful peak season. The leaves on the deciduous trees in the highlands, like in places like Pátzcuaro or the Bajío region, turn just like they do in the northern U.S. It’s super vibrant. 

But, uh, if you go in the summer, say from June to August, that’s a whole different vibe. You’ll see the lush green of the jungle and the bright colors of the cacti and flowers. That’s a great time to practice those bright autumn colors you love. 

And if you go in the winter, from December to February, you’ll be looking at winter landscapes, which are more about snow-capped mountains and foggy forests. That’s a great contrast if you want to practice different palettes. 

So, for the most traditional autumn colors, go in September or October. If you want to explore other watercolor techniques and color palettes, summer is a great time. Winter is good for a different kind of mood. It really just depends on what you're looking for.
