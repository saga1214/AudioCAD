Sure, here's a simple checklist for filming your own puppet show:

1. **Get Permission**: Before filming, ask the venue (Assisted Living) if you’re allowed to record and post it.

2. **Set Up Your Gear**: Bring a tripod or stable surface to keep the phone steady. A small light might help if the room is dim.

3. **Plan the Take**: Rehearse a bit so the performance feels natural for the camera. Maybe focus on close-ups of the puppets for clarity.

4. **Keep It Short**: Aim for 5-10 minutes to hold attention. You can always edit later.

5. **Kid-Friendly Label**: When posting, mark it as appropriate for children.

That’s pretty much it. Just enjoy the show and capture the fun.
