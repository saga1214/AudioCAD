Deep blockages can be tough, but even with just your toolbox, you've got options. Let's calm and focus. Here's a step-by-step plan:

### 1️⃣ **Plunger First (If Not Already Tried)**
   - Use a **flange plunger** (or two cups if you only have a cup plunger).  
   - **Technique:** Seal the drain tightly with the plunger, pump 10-15 strong, steady motions.  
   - **Tip:** Keep water deep enough to cover the plunger’s rubber band.

### 2️⃣ **Check for a Drain Snake (Auger)**
   - Many "simple toolboxes" include a short hand auger.  
   - **Technique:**  
     - Insert the cable into the drain.  
     - Rotate slowly while pushing forward until you feel resistance.  
     - If you hit a blockage, stop, then **pull back slightly and rotate the cable clockwise** to break it up.  
     - Avoid kinking the cable—never force it.  

### 3️⃣ **Hot Water + Dish Soap**
   - Fill the drain with **hot tap water** (not boiling) until it’s ¾ full.  
   - Add ¼ cup dish soap.  
   - Let it sit for 5 minutes, then flush with more hot water.  
   - *Why?* Soap breaks down grease; heat softens debris.

### 4️⃣ **Wet/Dry Vacuum (If Included)**
   - Some toolboxes have a mini vacuum.  
   - **Setup:**  
     - Turn the vacuum to **"Wet"** mode.  
     - Plug it into the drain.  
     - Keep suction steady—don’t lift the nozzle.  
   - *Caution:* Siphoning might occur if the blockage is very deep.

### 5️⃣ **Salt + Boiling Water (Last Resort)**
   - Mix ½ cup salt + 1 cup boiling water.  
   - Pour it down the drain, then immediately cover with 1 cup ice cubes.  
   - Wait 15 minutes. The ice can help scrape debris loose.

### 🧠 **Key Mindset Shifts**  
- **You’ve got this:** Plumbing is problem-solving—each step rules out possibilities.  
- **Progress > Perfection:** Even if it doesn’t clear fully, you’re reducing the issue.  
- **Ask for Help Later:** If stuck, call a colleague for advice *before* it becomes a crisis.  

**You’re a plumber because you can fix this.** Start with the plunger/snake—most deep clogs loosen with persistence. Breathe through it, one step at a time. 🧠💙
