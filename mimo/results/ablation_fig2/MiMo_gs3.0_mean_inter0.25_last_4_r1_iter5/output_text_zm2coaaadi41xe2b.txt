Dallas has a ton of great Asian noodle spots! For ramen, I'd recommend Ichiran or Tonkotsu Ramen. For Soba, check out Soba No Yume. And for a more casual vibe, Noodle House has amazing Pho and other noodle dishes. As for protein bars, if you want something high-protein with a good texture, Quest Bars are great. If you want something more natural, Clif Bars or RXBARs are solid. For a sweet treat, Optimum Nutrition has some really good flavors. Hope you enjoy your meal and snacks!
