It's wonderful that you've created a structured plan! Here’s a sample weekly schedule that balances focused three-ball practice with recovery and other activities.

On Monday, you could start with a ten-minute two-ball one-hand drill you've mastered. Then, move to a fifteen-minute transition practice for going from two to three beanbags. After that, try juggling the scarves for ten minutes to focus on rhythm. Finally, spend five minutes just playing around with the three beanbags without catching.

Tuesday could be a rest day, or you could do some light stretching or watch a juggling tutorial.

Wednesday, you could practice juggling with tennis balls for fifteen minutes to get used to a different feel. Then, spend another fifteen minutes on three-ball beanbag throws, just focusing on the rhythm without worrying about catches. After that, use scarves for ten minutes to review the pattern.

Thursday is another rest day, or you could try some yoga.

On Friday, you could do another ten minutes of two-ball one-hand drills. Then, dedicate twenty minutes to focused three-ball beanbag practice, really concentrating on the first three throws. Follow that with ten minutes of scarf juggling.
