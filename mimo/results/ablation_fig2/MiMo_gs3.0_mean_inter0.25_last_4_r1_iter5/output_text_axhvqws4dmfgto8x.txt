For stir-fry noodles, here's how to nail it. First, salt your water generously, about one teaspoon per gallon. This makes the noodles springy but still soft.

Next, stir constantly. It only takes about three to five minutes. As soon as the water is boiling and the noodles are tender but still have a little bite, drain them immediately. And here's a pro tip: rinse them under cold water right after draining. This stops the cooking process and keeps them from sticking together.

Then, toss them with a little bit of oil right in the pan you'll stir-fry in. This helps prevent them from clumping up. You're doing great!
