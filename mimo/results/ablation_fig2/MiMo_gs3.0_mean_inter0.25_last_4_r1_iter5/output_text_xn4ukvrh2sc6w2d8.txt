Here are the classic recipes with precise measurements and bartender instructions for hosting at your Art Deco airport cocktail reception:

### 1. Old Fashioned
**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz bourbon rye  
- 1 sugar cube or ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube (or add simple syrup) in a shaker.  
2. Add bourbon and bitters.  
3. Fill the shaker with ice and **dry shake** (shake without ice) for 10 seconds to emulsify.  
4. Add ice again, shake hard, and strain into a chilled rocks glass over ice.  
5. Express an orange twist over the drink, wipe the rim, and garnish.  

*Egg white tip:* For a classic Whiskey Sour variation, add 0.5 oz fresh egg white to the shaker. Dry shake first, then add ice and shake again before straining.

---

### 2. Paper Plane
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz London Dry Gin  
- 2 oz Maraschino Liqueur  
- ¾ oz Maraschino Liqueur or Maraschino Liqueur substitute  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- Orange twist (optional garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake gin, maraschino liqueur, lemon juice, and orange twist with ice.  
2. Shake hard for 15 seconds.  
3. Strain into a coupe glass.  
4. Garnish with a cherry or lemon wheel.  

*Garnish note:* The orange twist isn’t traditional but adds visual appeal.

---

### 3. Whiskey Sour (with optional egg white)
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 0.5 oz egg white (optional)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle simple syrup in a shaker.  
2. Add bourbon, lemon juice, bitters, and egg white (if using).  
3. **Dry shake** for 10 seconds.  
4. Add ice, shake again, and strain into a rocks glass.  
5. Garnish with cherry or orange slice.  

*No egg white option:* Skip the dry shake step. Stir all ingredients with ice, then strain.

---

### 4. Aviation  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz gin  
- ¾ oz maraschino liqueur  
- ¼ oz crème de violette  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- Cherry (garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Shake hard for 15 seconds.  
3. Strain into a coupe glass.  
4. Garnish with a cherry.  

---

### Bonus Gin Cocktail Recommendation: **French 75**  
**Perfect for Fall & Vintage Theme:**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 3 oz Champagne or Prosecco  
- Lemon twist (garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a champagne flute.  
3. Top with sparkling wine.  
4. Express a lemon twist over the drink.  

*Why it works:* Bitter-sweet-ginger profile complements Paper Planes, champagne’s bubbles feel celebratory at a retro venue.

---

### Key Tips for Your Bartenders:  
- **Glassware:** Rocks glasses for Fashions/Planes/Sour, coupes for Aviation/French 75.  
- **Ice:** Pre-chill all glasses (airport building may not be fully climate-controlled).  
- **Garnish:** Use citrus peels to echo Art Deco’s geometric shapes.  
- **Finger Food Theme:** Suggest bourbon-spiced nuts, mini quiches, or art-deco-inspired canapés (e.g., gold-dusted pretzels).
