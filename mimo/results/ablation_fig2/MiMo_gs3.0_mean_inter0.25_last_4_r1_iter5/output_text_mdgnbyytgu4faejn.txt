### **Resume Format Template**
*(Use 10-12 point fonts overall; 1-inch margins)*

---

#### **1. Contact Header** *(Centered, 9-10 point font)*  
John A. Doe  
Email: john@example.com | Phone: (123) 456-7890  
City, State | LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/johndoe *(Optional but recommended)*  

---

#### **2. Professional Summary** *(3-4 lines, 11-12 point font)*  
**"Your 2-3 sentence pitch"**  
Example:  
> "Data-driven marketing professional with 8+ years optimizing campaigns for SaaS companies. Proven ability to increase lead conversion by 40%+. Seeking to leverage analytics expertise in growth marketing roles."

---

#### **3. Relevant Experience** *(Reverse chronological order, 10-11 point font)*  
**Job Title**, Company | City, State | Dates  
- **Bullet Point 1** (Action verb + result)  
- **Bullet Point 2** (Quantifiable achievement)  
- **Bullet Point 3** (Skill + impact)  

**Job Title**, Company | City, State | Dates**  
*(Repeat for each role)*  

---

#### **4. Skills Section** *(10-11 point font, right-aligned or centered)*  
**Technical Skills:**  
- Software: Python, Tableau, SQL  
- Tools: Google Analytics, HubSpot  
- Languages: Spanish (Fluent)  

**Soft Skills:**  
- Project Management  
- Cross-Team Collaboration  

*(Tailor to job description keywords)*  

---

#### **5. Education** *(Reverse chronological)*  
**Degree**, University | City, State | Graduation Year  
- **Honors:** *Magna Cum Laude, Dean’s List*  
- **Relevant Coursework:** Digital Marketing Strategy, Data Analytics  

**Degree**, University | City, State | Graduation Year  
*(Repeat for each degree)*  

---

#### **6. Additional Sections (Optional but strategic)**  
- **Certifications:** Google Analytics Certified (2023)  
- **Projects:** "E-commerce UX Redesign" (GitHub link)  
- **Volunteer Work:** Non-profit board member  

---

### **Key Changes from Your Current Layout**
1. **Contact Info:**  
   - Move email/phone city *below your centered name* (9-10 point icons optional due ATS risks).  
   - *Order Suggestion:* Name → Email → Phone → City *(standard hierarchy)*.  

2. **Experience Handling:**  
   - **Relevant Experience Section:** Highlight older roles that match target jobs.  
   - **Additional Work History:** List unrelated roles concisely (title/company/dates only).  
   - *Never hide gaps*—use summary to reframe career shifts.  

3. **Education:**  
   - Include in-progress degrees with "Expected [Month Year]".  
   - Place honors/scholarships under relevant degrees.  

---

### **Pro Tips**
- **ATS Compatibility:** Avoid columns, graphics, or unusual fonts.  
- **Quantify Everything:** Use numbers (% increase, $ saved, etc.).  
- **Tailor Per Application:** Swap skills/experience bullets to match job description.  
- **Length:** Keep to 1-2 pages (max 3 pages for 10+ years experience).
