For the **Spicy Lentil Soup**, the key ingredients are:  
- **Lentils** (brown or green work best)  
- **Vegetable broth** (or chicken broth for extra richness)  
- **Canned crushed tomatoes** (for acidity and depth)  
- **Heat agents**: Cayenne pepper, red pepper flakes, or a dash of hot sauce  

Optional additions: carrots, celery, garlic, thyme, or a splash of vinegar. Simmer for 20–30 minutes until tender.
