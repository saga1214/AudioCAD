Here's a curated list of corgi-treat ideas tailored for your little corgi princess, categorized for easy reference:

### 🧊 Homemade Freezer Favors
1. **Pumpkin Power Bites**  
   Mashed canned pumpkin mixed with a touch of peanut butter, spooned into molds or onto parchment. Freeze solid for a cool, fiber-rich treat.
2. **Sweet Potato Chillers**  
   Boil, peel, and mash firm sweet potatoes. Press into silicone molds, freeze 2+ hours.

### 🍪 Baking Basics (No Xylitol!)
3. **PB-Oat Cookies**  
   Blend oats + peanut butter + mashed banana. Roll into tiny balls, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12 mins. Break into pea-sized crumbs.
4. **Banana-Oat Muffin Minis**  
   Mix 1 mashed banana + ½ cup oats + 2 tbsp peanut butter + pinch of cinnamon. Bake mini muffin tin (175°F/82°C, 10 mins). Freeze extras.
5. **Coconut Yogurt Dots**  
   Mix plain Greek yogurt + shredded coconut. Spoon onto parchment, freeze 2 hours.

### 🍗 Quick & Healthy
6. **Cooked Chicken Carrots**  
   Steam carrot sticks until tender. Cut into ½-inch sticks. Serve raw or lightly steamed. Pair with a dab of plain cooked chicken.
7. **Fish Crackers**  
   Bake sardine or salmon flakes on parchment at 300°F (150°C) for 5 mins until crisp. *Use sparingly* (high salt!).

### 🛒 Store-Bought Royalty Picks
- **Natural Brands**: Zuke's Mini Nibbles, Earth Animal Crunchy Bites, Open Farm Grain-Free Bites
- **Training Specialties**: Greenies Dog Chews (small size), Kikoriki Organic Treats
- **Convenience**: Small frozen Kongs stuffed with peanut butter/yogurt, freeze overnight

### 💖 Styling Tip for Collar
Choose colors that **contrast her coat** for visual flair:
- **Brown Corgi**: Turquoise, hot pink, or metallic silver
- **White Corgi**: Rich burgundy, emerald green, or gold
- **Mixed Coat**: Jewel tones like sapphire blue or ruby red

**Pro Tip**: Always introduce new treats in tiny portions (1-2% of daily calories) and pair with training! For barking at Felix, practice "quiet" commands during calm moments—reward instant silence with a tiny treat. Consistency turns quirks into charm! 🐾💖
