Moving into a new space is exciting! To install your TV, planters, and floating shelves while respecting the landlord's rules and avoiding distractions, here's a clear, step-by-step guide:

### 📺 **TV Mount Installation**
1. **Plan First**:  
   - Use a **stud finder** to locate wall studs (every 16" vertically).  
   - Measure wall thickness and mark stud positions.  
   - Check your mount’s weight limits (most hold 30-100 lbs).  
2. **Tools Needed**:  
   - Drill, wall anchors (if no studs), level, tape measure, screwdriver.  
3. **Install Mount**:  
   - Align mount with stud marks, drill pilot holes.  
   - Secure to studs with long screws (or anchors if needed).  
4. **Mount TV**:  
   - Place TV face-down on table/couch, align holes with mount.  
   - Attach TV to mount with included screws.  
5. **Short Tip**: Do this on a weekend morning to avoid noise complaints and stiffness. Wear earplugs if drilling late!

### 🌿 **Hanging Planters**
1. **Lightweight Planter Hack**:  
   - Use **adhesive strips** (like 3M Command Strips) for small pots.  
   - Place pots on shelves first, press strips firmly, then hang.  
2. **Drilling (If Necessary)**:  
   - **Target studs or beams** (use a stud finder).  
   - For lightweight planters (e.g., succulents), drill shallow holes and use **toggle bolts** for security.  
3. **Plant Care**:  
   - Use lightweight pots (terracotta is heavy; opt for resin or plastic).  
   - Group plants in odd numbers for visual balance.

### 🪢 **Floating Shelves**
1. **Stud-First Approach**:  
   - **Ideal**: Install shelves directly into studs.  
   - **No Studs?** Use **toggle bolts** (for drywall) or **heavy-duty anchors**.  
2. **Installation Steps**:  
   - Mark shelf height with a level and pencil.  
   - Drill pilot holes slightly smaller than screw diameter.  
   - Secure shelf brackets to studs/anchors, then attach the shelf.  
3. **Weight Safety**:  
   - Place heavier items (vases, books) on the center of the shelf.  
   - Avoid overloading—stick to 2-3 items per shelf.  

### 💡 **Key Tips for Success**
- **Time It Right**: Schedule installations for weekends (per your plan). Work 1-2 hours max to avoid noise complaints.  
- **Prevent Leg Stiffness**:  
  - Set a timer for 5 minutes every 30 minutes to stretch.  
  - Stand up while measuring/drilling.  
- **Decorate During Work Hours**:  
  - Dedicate Sundays to decorating *only*—decorating tools/furniture stay *out of your workspace* during work hours.  
- **Safety First**:  
  - Wear safety glasses when drilling.  
  - Keep a first-aid kit handy.  

### 🛠️ **Tool Checklist**
- Stud finder  
- Cordless drill + drill bits  
- Level  
- Tape measure  
- Level (for shelves/planters)  
- Adhesive strips (optional)  
- Pliers
