That sounds like a fantastic romantic theme! Based on loving cooking, enjoying mango/spicy food despite fiancée's dietary needs, here are meal and dessert ideas for your ocean-themed night:

**Mouthwatering Meals (Focusing on Mango & Spicy, Shellfish-Free, Diabetes-Friendly):**

1.  **Spicy Mango & Coconut Tofu Curry:** A vibrant, fragrant curry with your shellfish-free protein (extra-firm tofu, chickpeas, or tempeh) simmered in a rich coconut milk base with aromatic spices (ginger, garlic, turmeric, cumin, coriander). Bursting with fresh mango chunks for sweetness and natural spice. Serve with brown rice or quinoa.
2.  **Mango "Crab" Cakes with Spicy Remoulade:** Crispy on the outside, savory on the inside! Use finely diced shellfish-free protein (like lump-style tofu, hearts of palm, or finely chopped mushrooms) mixed with breadcrumbs, egg (or flax egg), binders, and fresh herbs. Top with diced mango. Serve with a spicy mango-habanero remoulade sauce and a side salad.
3.  **Spicy Mango & Shrimpless Tacos with Mango Salsa:** Warm corn tortillas filled with your shellfish-free protein (grilled or sautéed tofu strips, crispy chickpeas, or shredded zucchini "tuna"). Top with a fresh, spicy mango salsa (diced mango, red onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice) and avocado crema (made with Greek yogurt or avocado).
4.  **Mango & Shellfish-Free Protein "Paella" Rice:** A colorful, flavorful rice dish inspired by paella. Sauté your shellfish-free protein (chicken thigh strips, chickpeas, or lentils) with onions, peppers, garlic, and paprika. Add tomato sauce, vegetable or chicken broth, and a generous amount of diced mango. Stir in cooked rice and finish with fresh herbs. Serve with a side of steamed green beans.
5.  **Mango & Shellfish-Free Protein "Tostadas":** Light and fresh! Crispy tostadas topped with a spicy mango salsa, black beans, diced avocado, shredded lettuce or cabbage, and a sprinkle of your shellfish-free protein (like diced grilled tofu or chicken). Drizzle with a light lime-cilantro dressing.

**Sweet Ocean-Themed Desserts (Mango Focused & Diabetes-Conscious):**

1.  **Mango & Coconut Panna Cotta with Lime Zest:** A silky, creamy dessert. Set a mixture of coconut milk, low-sugar gelatin, a touch of sweetener (like monk fruit or stevia), and fresh mango puree in a baking dish. Top with a dollop of lightly sweetened whipped cream (using low-carb sweetener) and a sprinkle of lime zest for a bright, oceanic twist.
2.  **Mango & Lime Pudding (Parfait Style):** Layer layers of a simple, sweetened mango pudding (made with low-sugar milk alternatives and sweetener) with a layer of lightly sweetened Greek yogurt or coconut yogurt for creaminess and protein. Top each layer with fresh mango chunks and maybe a few blueberries for color. Serve in glasses for an elegant parfait.
3.  **Mango & Coconut "Fruit Salad" with Lime-Honey Dressing:** Skip the heavy desserts! Combine fresh mango chunks with other seasonal fruits like berries, pineapple, kiwi, and oranges. Toss gently with a dressing of fresh lime juice, a *tiny* bit of honey or maple syrup (use sparingly), and a sprinkle of toasted coconut flakes.
4.  **Mango & Lime Cheesecake (Mini or Regular):** For a richer option, make a classic or New York-style cheesecake. Infuse the cream cheese with fresh mango puree and a hint of lime zest. Use a graham cracker crust or a crust made with nuts for extra texture. Top with a light mango gelée or a dollop of fresh mango puree.
5.  **Mango & Coconut Ice Cream "Sundae":** Serve a scoop of your favorite low-sugar, high-protein vanilla or coconut ice cream (or make your own with frozen banana and a touch of sweetener) as the base. Top generously with fresh diced mango and a light sprinkle of crushed toasted coconut flakes. Add a few dark chocolate sea salt shavings if desired.

**Tips for Your Theme:**

*   **Decorations:** Lean into blues, greens, and sandy neutrals. Use seashells, starfish, or driftwood decor. String lights add a magical touch. A small "beach" towel or placemat can add a fun touch.
*   **Music:** Create an ocean-themed playlist – soft waves, tropical vibes, or romantic acoustic.
*   **Ambiance:** Dim the lights slightly, perhaps with fairy lights or candles (if she can have them safely with diabetes).
*   **Presentation:** Garnish dishes with fresh herbs (cilantro, mint), edible flowers, or a sprinkle of toasted coconut. Use colorful plates.

Enjoy your delicious, thoughtful, and ocean-themed anniversary celebration! It sounds like a wonderful way to share your love of food and travel plans.
