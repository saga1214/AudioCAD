Absolutely! For an unforgettable scuba diving trip to Costa Rica, here’s a tailored plan balancing adventure, beginner-friendliness, and iconic sights:

### **1. Fin Choice Finalized**  
Stick with **short fins**—especially for your first Costa Rican trip. They’re easier to travel with, less tiring for beginners, and perfect for maneuvering in calm waters or tight spaces. You’ll appreciate their compactness when packing.

### **2. Top Dive Sites & Itinerary**  
#### **Option 1: South Pacific (Playa Conchal & Tamarindo)**  
- **Why**: Calm, warm waters with vibrant coral gardens and abundant marine life. Ideal for beginners.  
- **Must-Dive Spots**:  
  - **Playa Blanca**: Clear water with colorful fish, rays, and occasional sea turtles.  
  - **Tamarindo**: Explore shipwrecks (like the *Santo Domingo*) and coral formations.  
- **Bonus**: Close to San José (easy airport access) and great beachside dining.  

#### **Option 2: Cocos Island (Remote & Adventurous)**  
- **Why**: UNESCO-listed protected area with dramatic underwater scenery. Strong currents, but worth it for experienced divers.  
- **Must-Dive Spots**:  
  - **Punta Mira**: Whale sharks, sea lions, and deep-sea vents.  
  - **Coral Gardens**: Healthy reefs teeming with tropical fish.  
- **Logistics**: Fly to Limón, then take a 1.5-hour boat. Stay in eco-lodges.  

#### **Option 3: Golfo Dulce (Calm & Wildlife-Focused)**  
- **Why**: Sheltered bay with whales (Dec-Apr) and dolphins. Great for beginners and families.  
- **Must-Dive Spots**:  
  - **Isla del Caño**: Abundant coral, sea turtles, and tropical fish.  
  - **Sirena Beach**: Bioluminescent plankton at night (swimming, not diving).  

### **3. Wildlife & Unique Experiences**  
- **Whale Sharks**: Spot them in Cocos Island or Guanacaste (May-Oct).  
- **Sea Turtles**: Common in Playa Conchal and Tortuguero (July-Nov).  
- **Blue Hole (Cenote-style)**: While famous in Belize, Costa Rica has stunning *cenotes* like **Cenote Azul** (Guanacaste) for clear-water diving.  

### **4. Logistics & Tips**  
- **Best Time to Visit**: **December–April** (dry season, warm water, fewer crowds).  
- **Dive Operators**: Book with reputable shops like **Dive Tamarindo**, **Cocos Island Dive Company**, or **Ocean Explorer** (for Cocos).  
- **Gear**: Bring short fins, wetsuits (if cold currents), reef-safe sunscreen, and a dive computer.  
- **Safety**: Choose operators with certified dive masters and emergency protocols.  

### **5. Beyond Scuba: Extra Highlights**  
- **Land Adventure**: Hike Arenal Volcano or explore Manuel Antonio National Park.  
- **Food**: Try Costa Rican classics like *gallo pinto* (rice & beans) and fresh ceviche.  
- **Sustainability**: Support eco-lodges and reef-safe operators.  

### **Final Recommendation**  
For your first Costa Rican trip, **start with Playa Conchal/Tamarindo** (3–4 days) for easy, unforgettable dives, then add a day trip to **Cocos Island** (if you want adventure). Combine with a short jungle tour in Manuel Antonio for a perfect blend of ocean and rainforest.
