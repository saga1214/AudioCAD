Dallas has great Asian noodle spots! For ramen, check out Yummy Yummy in Deep Ellum for their rich tonkotsu. For pho, Pho 79 on North Oak is solid. And for a fun, casual vibe, try Noodle House for their wonton noodles. 

For protein bars, it depends on your needs. Quest is high-protein and low-sugar, Clif Bars are more balanced, and RXBAR is clean with whole ingredients. Just pick what works for you!
