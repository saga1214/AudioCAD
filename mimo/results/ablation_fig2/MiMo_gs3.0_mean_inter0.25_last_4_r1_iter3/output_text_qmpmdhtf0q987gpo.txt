Okay, so you're ready to prime, which is great. Before you do that final coat, just make sure the doors you took off are completely dust-free and fully hardened from the sanding.

Then, I'd recommend protecting the floor and countertops underneath with some plastic or cardboard, just to be safe.

When you're priming from that dark black color, a good rule of thumb is two thin coats. One coat might not cover it completely, so two is better.

And while you're at it, it's a good chance to test the color out on the back of a cabinet door so you can see how it looks before committing to the whole kitchen. 

Finally, make sure you're working in a well-ventilated area, and be sure to let the primer fully dry between coats.
