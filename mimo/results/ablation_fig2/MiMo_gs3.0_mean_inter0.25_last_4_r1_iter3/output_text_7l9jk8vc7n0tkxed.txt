**Step 1: Assess & Prep (Critical!)**
*   **Safety First:** Ensure the drain is fully accessible. Cover surrounding areas with towels. Wear gloves.
*   **Locate:** Pinpoint where the blockage *might* be (sink U-bend, drainpipe, further down).
*   **Simple Tools from Your Toolbox:** Grab pliers, a small screwdriver (if it fits the drain), maybe a small hook or wire if available.

**Step 2: Manual Removal (First Attempt)**
*   **Reach In:** Carefully insert your hand (protected by gloves) or a long-handled tool (like a screwdriver handle) into the drain, especially near the U-bend. Feel around.
*   **Remove Debris:** Use pliers or your fingers to pull out any visible chunks of hair, grease, or other obvious blockages you can reach. Be patient and persistent.

**Step 3: Clear the U-Bend (If Accessible)**
*   **Drain Pan Access:** If you have access to the U-bend under the sink (or wherever the drain is), carefully remove the drain cover/stopper.
*   **Flush Water:** Pour a bucket of hot water down the drain to flush out loosened debris.
*   **Inspect U-Bend:** Look inside the U-bend for clumps. Use pliers or a long screwdriver handle to try and pull them out. Sometimes a small wire or hook (like a coat hanger straightened) can work here.

**Step 4: Force the Water (If Step 3 didn't work)**
*   **Use a Wrench (If Available):** If you have a small adjustable wrench or pliers with a long handle, firmly clamp it onto the drain pipe itself (near the drain opening, not the U-bend). **Step 5: Cut Through (Last Resort in Simple Toolbox)**
*   **Check Your Tools:** Do you have a small utility knife or scissors?
