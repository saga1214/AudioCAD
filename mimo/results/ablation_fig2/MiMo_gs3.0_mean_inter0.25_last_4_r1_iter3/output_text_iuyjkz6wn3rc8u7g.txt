Okay, let me break down the analogies I just gave for making yogurt.

First, I had two versions. The first was the "throwing a party" analogy. In that one, Milk was the big empty room, Good bacteria were the special guests, keeping it warm energized them. Group hugging was the bacteria eating sugar and convincing people to link arms, which caused the milk to get thick and tangy into yogurt.

Then, the second one was the "net" analogy. Milk was the disorganized crowd, Bacteria were the tiny team coaches, Warmth again made the coaches energetic. Holding hands and forming a tight net was the bacteria causing the milk's proteins to link together, which trapped the liquid and turned the milk runny into thick yogurt. And flavoring was like the team's uniforms, which gave it an identity without changing the net's structure.

So, the core technical concepts covered are really the fermentation with good bacteria, the milk proteins denaturing and forming a gel structure, sugar being broken down, temperature control, and how flavorings can be added without affecting the main texture.
