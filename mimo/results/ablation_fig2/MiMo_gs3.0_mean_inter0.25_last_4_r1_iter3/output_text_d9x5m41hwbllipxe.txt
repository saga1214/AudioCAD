Okay, let's break down the home security system for your 1500 sq ft home with four doors seven windows and interest in glass break sensors and indoor siren. 

Upfront Equipment Only self-install range three hundred to seven hundred dollars professional over a thousand. Monthly professional monitoring typically twenty to sixty dollars, but factoring a five-year monitoring contract onto that brings monthly effective cost twenty-five to eighty dollars depending on system.

For itemized equipment costs: 
Control panel or hub: one hundred to four hundred dollars. 
Four door/window sensors: sixty to one hundred sixty dollars. 
Seven door/window sensors: one hundred fifty to six hundred dollars. 
Motion detectors: three hundred to two thousand four hundred dollars total. 
Cameras: one hundred fifty to two thousand four hundred dollars total. 
Glass break sensor: thirty to one hundred dollars. 
Indoor siren: thirty to seventy dollars. 

Professional installation adds significant costs. Self-install systems you purchase the equipment yourself. Professional installation includes trained technicians who install everything for you, so costs range from three hundred to over a thousand dollars. 

Monitoring contract types differ: self-monitored systems use local alarms and do not require a monthly fee. Professionally monitored systems require monthly fees for 24/7 monitoring and emergency response. 

Installation contract terms vary by provider, but five years is common. Breaking down a monthly cost over five years: for monitoring alone, it's about twenty-four to thirty dollars monthly for self-install and twenty-eight to sixty dollars monthly for professional systems.
