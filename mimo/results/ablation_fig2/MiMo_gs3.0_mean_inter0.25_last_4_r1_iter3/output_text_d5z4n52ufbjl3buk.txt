That's a great approach. To strike the right balance between respect and getting your point across, your email could be structured like this:

First, introduce yourself and state your positive goal. You could write: "Dear Professor [Last Name], I'm a student in your class, [Your Name], and I truly enjoy your course. I scored a 64 on my recent essay exam and wanted to talk to you because I'd like to do even better on future tests."

Second, frame it around feedback. Continue with: "Would you be available sometime this week to briefly discuss what I could have improved on in my essay? I find essays subjective and would appreciate any tips to strengthen my writing for next time."

Finally, make it easy for him to agree. You could end with: "If office hours aren't convenient, perhaps we could meet during lunch?"
