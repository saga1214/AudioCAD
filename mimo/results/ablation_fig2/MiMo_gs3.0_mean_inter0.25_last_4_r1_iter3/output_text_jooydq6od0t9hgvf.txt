Here are two quick, elegant cocktail recipes using your leftover vodka and Argentine-inspired Fernet-Branca, perfect for your formal crowd:

### 1. **Citrus & Honey Vodka Spritz**  
*(Refreshing, approachable, no soda)*  
**Recipe:**  
- 1.5 oz vodka  
- 0.5 oz Fernet-Branca (mild bitterness balances vodka)  
- 0.75 oz fresh grapefruit juice  
- 0.25 oz honey syrup (equal parts honey + hot water, cooled)  
- Sparkling water or soda water (soda-free!)  
- Grapefruit twist (for garnish)  

**How to Serve:**  
Shake vodka, Fernet, grapefruit juice, and honey syrup with ice. Strain into a coupe glass. Top with a splash of sparkling water. Express grapefruit twist over drink and drop it in.  

---

### 2. **Argentinian-Style Fernet & Blood Orange**  
*(Bold, aromatic, no soda)*  
**Recipe:**  
- 1.5 oz vodka  
- 0.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 0.75 oz blood orange juice (tart and vibrant)  
- 0.25 oz cinnamon-infused simple syrup (simmer 1 cinnamon stick in 1 cup sugar syrup, strain)  
- 0.25 oz fresh orange juice  
- Orange slice (garnish)  

**How to Serve:**  
Stir all ingredients with ice. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube. Garnish with an orange slice.  

---

### Why These Work:  
- **Allergy/Soda-Free:** Both use soda water or no topers (just sparkling water).  
- **Dietary Friendly:** All ingredients work for vegetarians/omnivores.  
- **Formal & Fun:** Elegant presentation (coupe/garnish) keeps it classy; flavors are sophisticated but crowd-pleasing.  
- **Game Night Compatibility:** Quick to make, easy to serve while guests play Pictionary/trivia.
