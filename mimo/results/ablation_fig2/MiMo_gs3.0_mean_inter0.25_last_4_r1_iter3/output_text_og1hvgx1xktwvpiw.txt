Of course, let me clarify. The standard one and a quarter to one to one Boulevardier is whiskey, sweet vermouth, and Campari respectively. Your friend's equal-parts Boulevardier is those same three ingredients at three-quarters of an ounce each. Now, for the Last Word you mentioned, an equal-parts recipe of three ingredients means three-quarters of an ounce each of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. You stir them well and garnish with a lemon twist instead of an orange one. Hope that helps you decide!
