Here's your clean, segment-specific clothing gear packing list, keeping mid-30s temps, night running, and layering in mind:

### **Day Pacing List (20 Miles, Aid Stations Available)**
1.  **Moisture-wicking base layer** (Top & Bottom): Synthetic or merino wool to manage sweat.
2.  **Lightweight fleece or windbreaker** (Mid-layer): For warmth when moving slower or at night. Bring removable.
3.  **Wind/water-resistant outer shell** (e.g., light rain jacket or windbreaker): Crucial for hills and variable weather.
4.  **Gloves**: Sturdy trail gloves for grip and warmth.
5.  **Hat**: Lightweight running cap or baseball cap for sun (if needed earlier).
6.  **Lightweight pack or vest**: To carry layers if needed (use aid stations strategically!).
7.  **Extra batteries** (if headlamp/handheld light uses CR2032): Small pack or spare.
8.  **Buff or neck gaiter**: Versatile for warmth, sweat absorption, or sun protection.

### **Night Pacing List (Final 12 Miles)**
1.  **Moisture-wicking base layer** (Top & Bottom): Non-negotiable for warmth.
2.  **Lightweight fleece or puffy jacket** (Mid-layer): Insulates well when still. Bring removable.
3.  **Wind/water-resistant outer shell** (e.g., light rain jacket or windbreaker): Still essential.
4.  **Warm hat & gloves**: Upgrade to **thermal gloves** (fingerless or full) and a **warm beanie** or **balaclava**. Extremities will get cold fast.
5.  **Neck gaiter or buff**: Wrap around face/neck for extra warmth.
6.  **Headlamp or handheld light**: **Essential**. Bring spare batteries (CR123 or AA, depending).
7.  **Lightweight pack or vest**: Still useful to stash layers.

### **Key Notes for Both Segments**
*   **Layers are King:** Wear moisture-wicking base layers directly on your skin. Add/remove mid-layers/outer layers as needed. **Never wear cotton.**
*   **Use Aid Stations:** Stock up on warm layers (like fleece) or spare socks if available. Don't carry everything you might need.
*   **Comfort First:** Wear shoes you've broken in. Ensure clothes aren't restrictive.
*   **Optional but Recommended:**
    *   **Extra socks:** In case feet get sweaty.
    *   **Lightweight pack cover:** If your pack gets wet.
    *   **Personal items:** Keys, phone (if needed), ID, cash/card.

This setup keeps you warm, dry, and comfortable through both challenging segments. Good luck pacing your husband!
