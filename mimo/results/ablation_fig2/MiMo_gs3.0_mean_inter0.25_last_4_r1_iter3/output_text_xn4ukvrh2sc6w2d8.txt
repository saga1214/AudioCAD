Here’s a complete bartender-friendly ingredient list for your cocktails at the Art Deco airport cocktail reception in Kentucky. All recipes use classic measurements.

### **Old Fashioned**  
- 2 oz bourbon/rye  
- 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist (expressed for oil, discard peel)  
*Shake sugar/bourbon/bitters with ice (dry shake), add ice, shake again, strain. Garnish.*

### **Old Fashioned w/ Egg White Option** *(Optional)*  
- 1 fresh egg white  
- Follow steps above *except*:  
  1. Shake all ingredients *without ice* (dry shake) until frothy.  
  2. Add ice, shake again.  
  3. Strain. Garnish.  
*Egg white is classic but optional—adds texture, not safety risk if handled properly.*

---

### **Paper Plane**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- 1 oz Aperol  
- ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
-¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
*Shake all with ice. Strain into coupe glass. No garnish.*

---

### **Whiskey Sour**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
-¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 1 egg white *(optional, see above)*  
*Dry shake bourbon/syrup/juice/egg white. Add ice, shake. Strain. Insert cherry or lemon slice.*

---

### **Aviation Cocktail**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz maraschino liqueur  
-¼ oz crème de violette  
-¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
*Shake all with ice. Strain into cocktail glass. Express an orange peel over drink.*

---

### **Recommended Art-Themed Gin Cocktail: French 75**  
*(Flavor: Bitter/Sweet/Tart, Elegant)*  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- 0.75 oz lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz simple syrup  
- 2 oz Champagne/Prosecco  
- Orange twist  
*Dry shake gin/syrup/juice. Add ice, shake. Strain into flute. Top with champagne. Garnish.*
