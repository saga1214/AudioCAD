Absolutely, for Thanksgiving, a classic is always a winner. A simple pumpkin pie is a great choice. It requires no oven use and just involves mixing a few canned ingredients.

If you want something with less oven time, you could make a quick apple crisp. You just bake sliced apples with a little sugar and cinnamon, and then top it with a simple oat and flour mixture that you sprinkle on top. It's much faster than a full pie.
