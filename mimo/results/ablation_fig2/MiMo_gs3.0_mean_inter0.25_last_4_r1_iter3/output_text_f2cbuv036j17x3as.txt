Okay, diesel camper vans can be a handful before a big trip, so it's smart to check things out. For brakes, a mechanic's basic brake inspection is a good place to start. They’ll look at pads, rotors, and fluid levels. Some signs of wear that you might see are squealing, grinding noises, or a soft brake pedal feel—those are all things to watch for. As for diesel spark plugs, you don't actually replace them; they're part of the diesel engine's compression ignition process, so they're not like the spark plugs in a gasoline engine. What you might need to do is check the diesel particulate filter, which is a common diesel issue, and make sure the fuel filter is clean. Also, diesel engines are heavy on oil, so check the oil level and condition. If you're not comfortable doing any of this yourself, it's always best to have a trusted mechanic take a look before you go.
