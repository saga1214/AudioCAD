That sounds like a wonderful plan! With a two-bedroom apartment and expecting a first baby, I'd suggest focusing on shorter road trips that are within a few hours' drive to start. Here are five great options in the B.C. Lower Mainland.

First, there's the Fraser Valley. It's only about an hour from Vancouver. You can enjoy wine tasting at a winery, visit a farm market, and have a picnic.

Second, you could drive to the Okanagan lakes. The drive is about two and a half hours. You can swim in Lake Okanagan, and there are many vineyards to visit.

Third, head to Victoria on Vancouver Island. The drive takes about three hours. You can see the famous Butchart Gardens, visit the Fairmont Victoria Hotel for a fancy meal, and even see some orcas on a whale-watching tour.

Fourth, for a quick escape, there's Whistler. It's about two hours away. You don't need to do heavy lifting to enjoy the gondola ride and see the beautiful scenery.

And fifth, you could drive to the Sunshine Coast. It's about an hour and a half from Vancouver. You can take a boat tour to the Lost Lagoon and explore the unique, foggy atmosphere there.

These trips are all well-connected by the Trans-Canada Highway and offer a great mix of activities for your family.
