You're right to think about adventures. Calgary is in a fantastic location for them.

If she enjoys the outdoors, you could go for a scenic drive and hike in Banff National Park. It's about an hour and a half's drive from Calgary, and the scenery is breathtaking. You could hike to Lake Louise in the summer or Moraine Lake in the fall. Another great spot is the Kananaskis Country, which is a bit further but has some of the most beautiful alpine scenery in the province.

If she prefers the water, you could take a day trip to the Gulf of the St. Marys. It's a short drive north, and you can take a boat tour to see icebergs that have calved from the glaciers in the nearby Icefields Parkway. Or, you could head to the Kicking Horse Canyon for some kayaking on the Kicking Horse River.

For a more low-key adventure, you could visit the Calgary Zoo. They have a fantastic "Wild Asia" exhibit with many exotic animals and a beautiful waterfall, or you could take her on a wildcat safari.
