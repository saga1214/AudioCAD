For candy tomorrow, grab some classics like Reese’s, Skittles, M&Ms, and gummies. These are always a hit. If you want Halloween-themed treats, you could get the little pumpkins or mummy-shaped candies, but make sure to check for artificial dye allergies again if serving them. Mini chocolate bars or gummy worms are also great crowd-pleasers. If you’re worried about leftovers, you can buy the single-serve packs or individual bags so guests can take what they want. Just stock up on a few of the top sellers like Reese’s and Skittles, and you’ll be golden!
