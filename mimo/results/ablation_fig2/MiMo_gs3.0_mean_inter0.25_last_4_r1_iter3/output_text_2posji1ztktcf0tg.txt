I'm happy to help with your vegetarian sister's birthday dinner! Since she recommended such a great podcast, she'll probably appreciate a thoughtful meal. Cheese aside, here are a few ideas:

1. **Mushroom Risotto** – Rich, creamy, and satisfying. You can use lots of different mushrooms (shiitake, oyster, cremini) for extra depth. She won't miss meat here.

2. **Stuffed Bell Peppers** – Filled with quinoa, black beans, corn, and spices. Top with cheese or vegan cheese if you want. Colorful and hearty.

3. **Vegetarian Lasagna** – Layer zucchini, eggplant, spinach, and ricotta (or tofu ricotta for vegans). She’ll feel indulged.

4. **Tofu or Chickpea Curry** – Serve with rice and naan. It’s cozy, flavorful, and easy to customize.

5. **Caprese Salad Skewers** – If you want something lighter: cherry tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, basil, balsamic glaze, and olive oil. Simple but elegant.
