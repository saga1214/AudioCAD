Yes, there are a couple of excellent options in Lockport. The absolute best place to learn about the canal's history is the Old Fort Niagara State Park. It was a key fort on the canal route, and it has a fantastic museum and a living history reenactment program called "A Day at the Locks." They recreate the whole experience of how the canal worked.

If you prefer to stay in the city of Lockport itself, you can visit the Lockport Museum and the Erie Canal Museum. They have a wonderful and very detailed replica of the "Flight of Five" locks, which is a great way to see how they functioned.
