However, there are hemispheric differences in how the brain processes information. The left hemisphere often handles analytical tasks, while the right handles spatial and creative tasks. But they work together constantly. So, while it’s not an absolute division, there are measurable differences.

Tinnitus usually has a physiological cause, like hearing damage or age-related changes. Stress can sometimes make it worse, but it isn’t psychological.
