Okay, efficiency can mean different things—cost, scalability, availability, reliability. If I have to pick just one... solar still feels like the best overall choice. It’s cheap, easy to scale, and getting cheaper. Storage solutions help with nighttime or cloudy days. But if you’re in a place with lots of snow or storms, wind or hydro might be better. So, while solar is generally the top pick, the "best" renewable really depends on where you are and what you need.
