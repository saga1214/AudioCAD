- **Maple Varieties**: Curly/birdseye maple have decorative grain but cost more; regular hard maple is affordable and equally durable.  

### Maple Syrup Tapping (DIY Guide)  
- **Tools**: Use a regular drill with a **5/16" or 3/8" bit**. Drill **1.5–2 inches deep** into the trunk.  
- **Spile**: Insert a metal spout (angled slightly upward) to collect sap into buckets/tubing.  
- **Timing**: Tap trees when temperatures freeze at night but thaw during the day.  
- **Sap Processing**: Boil sap to evaporate water and concentrate sugar into syrup.

### Safety & Tips  
- **Tapping Trees**: Avoid wounding bark; tap only in late winter.  
- **Outdoor Gear**: Wear layers, waterproof boots, and gloves.  
- **Ice Skating**: Use helmets/kid-friendly gear for beginners.
