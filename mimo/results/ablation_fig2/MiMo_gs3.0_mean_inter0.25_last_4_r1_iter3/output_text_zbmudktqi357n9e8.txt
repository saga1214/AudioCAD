Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time for that would be during their autumn season, which is, uh, roughly from September to November. This is when you get those super vibrant fall colors, especially in the highlands and pine forests around cities like Mexico City. 

If you want those classic, bright orange and red leaves, September and October are probably ideal. But, if you're more interested in coastal scenes—you know, with the ocean and autumn colors mixing—you might consider the spring months, around March to May, when the flowers are blooming. 

So yeah, for pure, intense autumn colors, aim for September or October. For a more mixed inspiration, spring could work too. Either way, Mexico’s landscapes are just incredibly inspiring for an artist. Enjoy your trip!
