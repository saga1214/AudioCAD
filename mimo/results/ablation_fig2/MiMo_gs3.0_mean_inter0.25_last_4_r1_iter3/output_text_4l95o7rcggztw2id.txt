Alright, here's a nautical-themed birthday shopping list for seven seven-year-old pirates meeting at the beach. Since mess-free finger foods are the priority and there's a watermelon allergy to avoid, I've put together a full meal plan for you. Everything is designed to be sand-free friendly and pirate-themed.

First, for the food: You'll need cheese cubes, either cheddar or mozzarella, themed as "pirate treasure." Get about one 8-ounce block for eight guests. For veggies, you'll need carrot sticks, cucumber slices, and bell pepper strips—about one pound of carrots, half a cucumber, and half a bell pepper. To avoid watermelon, you can get grapes in bulk, maybe two pounds, and label them "Jewel Grapes." Apple slices are great as "treasure map pieces," so grab two apples. Goldfish crackers, cut in half if you want, can be called "ship rigging." Mini hot dogs cut in half are classic "treasure chest sliders," so get about 24 mini hot dogs. Mini bagels cut into quarters can be "pirate ship rolls"; get about 12 bagels. And popcorn is perfect as "cargo" or "gold dust," so grab a large bag.

For drinks: Individual boxes of juice or water in assorted flavors, with one less box since your cake-making covers the cake option. Get about eight boxes for eight kids plus you and guests. You could also have reusable water bottles with pirate-themed lids as an optional item if you want something reusable.

Finally, for the packaging and extras: Get a few sealed snack cups to seal everything away from sand. Decorate some of them as treasure chests. And for a fun pirate flag decoration, you could make some “buried treasure” cups by filling them with sand and burying a toy or a small trinket inside. 

This list should give you a full meal while keeping things easy at the beach and on-theme for your son. Hope the nautical adventure is a huge success!
