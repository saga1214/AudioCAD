Yes, you absolutely can submit your own illustrations. This is a huge advantage, and many authors do this.

The key thing to remember is that the publisher will not hire you to do the illustrations. Instead, they will hire a completely different artist to do the final, polished artwork for their own style. So you are essentially selling your text and your art as a package deal to them.

The process is the same: you'll find an agent first. You'll submit your manuscript and your illustrations to them. They will then find a publisher who is interested in that specific combination of your words and your art. So you're not just selling the story, you're selling the complete package.
