Okay, so to help minimize your exposure, let's focus on five key products to avoid these 'forever chemicals,' which are mainly PFAS. 

First, definitely non-stick cookware. You can switch to stainless steel, cast iron, or ceramic instead. 

Second, try to avoid grease-resistant food packaging, like fast-food wrappers, pizza boxes, and microwave popcorn bags. 

Third is anything labeled stain-resistant or waterproof, such as carpets, furniture, some outdoor clothing, and even some waterproof clothing. 

Fourth, some cosmetics and dental floss. It's good to check labels for PFAS-free claims. 

And fifth, since these can contaminate tap water, using a certified water filter will really help.
