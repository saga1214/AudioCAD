For your end-of-year brownie celebration, a **hybrid approach** could balance fun, budget, and logistics:  

### 🎉 End-of-Year Event Ideas:
1. **Park Picnic + Low-Key Games**:  
   - **Barbecue**: Parents can split costs (e.g., $10-$15 per family for supplies). Use a public park with picnic tables.  
   - **Sundae Bar**: Set up at the cafeteria—ice cream (dairy-free option available!) + toppings (sprinkles, fruit, hot fudge).  
   - **Games**: Keep it simple with sack races, relay races, or badge-design contests (craft supplies provided).  

2. **Cafeteria Celebration**:  
   - **Pizza Party**: Parents bring toppings (cheese, veggies) while cafeteria handles cooking.  
   - **Sash Presentation**: Award badges during a short ceremony with a craft station (e.g., decorating sashes with patches/stickers).  

### 💡 Key Tips:
- **Budget Wisely**:  
  - **Dues**: Charge $10-$20/year (covers badge patches, craft supplies, and 1-2 special events).  
  - **Snacks**: Rotate parents bringing snacks (e.g., "Pizza Night" or "Ice Cream Day") to share costs.  
- **Parent Involvement**:  
  - Assign roles (e.g., "Snack Coordinator," "Game Leader") to distribute tasks.  
  - Invite parents for the end-of-year event—make it a family affair!  
- **Pregnancy Prep**:  
  - Delegate setup/cleanup to parents.  
  - Choose indoor events if weather is unpredictable.  

### 🏆 Final Thought:  
Start with **Friendship Badge** activities early—they’re bonding-focused and low-effort. By involving parents in planning, you’ll reduce stress and ensure everyone feels invested! You’ll be great—brownies parties are all about creativity and teamwork.
