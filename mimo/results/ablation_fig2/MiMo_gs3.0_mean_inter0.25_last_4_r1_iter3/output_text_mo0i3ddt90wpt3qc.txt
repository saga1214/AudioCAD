You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which is a great gluten-free foundation. Along those same gluten-free lines, rice cakes with almond butter are always popular.

If you're looking for something a little different, trail mix is always a camp favorite and you can easily make sure it's gluten-free by just choosing one that is. And finally, veggie sticks with hummus is a really refreshing and crunchy snack.
