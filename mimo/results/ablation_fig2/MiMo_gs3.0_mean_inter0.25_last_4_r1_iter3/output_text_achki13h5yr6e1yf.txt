Of course. To summarize all the ingredients and price ranges for Chicken Cutlets and Garlic Mashed Potatoes fed four:

For the Entree, Chicken Cutlets:
Chicken Breasts, that's two pounds: eight to fifteen dollars.
Vegetable Oil, for frying: four to eight dollars per bottle.
Eggs, a dozen: three to seven dollars per dozen.
All-Purpose Flour: Three to five dollars for a bag.
Breadcrumbs: two to four dollars for a container.
And Salt and Pepper, if needed: three to six dollars for a basic set.

And for the Entrée Side, the Garlic Mashed Potatoes:
Potatoes, about five pounds: three to six dollars.
Garlic: about one head: one dollar.
Butter, about a pound: four to seven dollars.
And Milk, about a half-gallon: three to five dollars.
