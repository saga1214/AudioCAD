Here's a streamlined workflow and actionable advice tailored to your needs:

### Studio Setup Fixes
1. **Mic Placement (Blue Yeti):** Speak into the side with the logo—not top. Position 6-12 inches (15-30 cm) away. Use cardioid polar pattern unless needing stereo effects.
2. **Acoustic Treatment:** Hang tape-up heavy blankets (no frames needed). Angle them toward reflection points (slanted walls) to absorb high frequencies causing harshness.

### Vocal Health Essentials
- **Warm-ups:** Daily 10-min routine: Lip trills → Humming → Tongue clicks → Gentle scales
- **Breathing:** Practice diaphragmatic breathing (place hand on belly; rise on inhale)
- **Water:** Sip room-temperature water hourly. Avoid cold—it constricts vocal cords
- **Breaks:** 5-min breaks every 25 mins. Hydrate and stretch

### Software Transition
- **Adobe Audition vs. Audacity:**  
  → *Switch if:* You edit animation/games needing fast rendering or spectral editing  
  → *Stick with Audacity if:* Budget/learning curve is priority (use Audacity’s noise reduction presets first)
- **Reliable AI Tools:**  
  - Mouth clicks: iZotope RX 10 (De-click) or Audition’s Noise Reduction  
  - Labeling: Descript (AI-powered transcript syncing)  
  - Silence trimming: Adobe Audition’s "Noise Reduction Restoration" > "Reduce Noise"

### Workflow Organization
1. **Project Management:**  
   - Use dedicated folders: `ClientA`/`ClientB` with subfolders for `Raw`, `Edits`, `Delivery`  
   - Cloud sync: Dropbox/Google Drive for client access  
2. **Editing Routine:**  
   - First pass: Noise reduction + compression  
   - Second pass: EQ (cut harsh ~3-5kHz range)  
   - Third pass: Character-specific vocal effects

### Demo Strategy

  → Clear vocal texture (gravelly, resonant, etc.)  
  → Industry-relevant genre (fantasy, thriller, commercial)
