### 2. **Clicker Training Explained**  
   - **How It Works:**  
     1. **Clicker:** A simple device that makes a sharp "click" sound.  
     2. **Timing:** Click the *exact moment* your puppy does the desired behavior (e.g., sits).  
     3. **Reward:** Immediately after clicking, give a treat.  
     4. **Repetition:** Repeat this. The puppy learns to associate the click/treat with the action.  
   - **Why It Works:** The click precisely marks the behavior, and the treat reinforces it. Over time, the puppy will perform the action *just to hear the click*.

### Training Tips for "Sit":  
- **Step 1:** Hold a treat near your puppy’s nose. Slowly move it up and back over his head.  
- **Step 2:** As he lowers his head to follow the treat, his bottom will sink to the ground.  
- **Step 3:** Say "Sit!" the *instant* his bottom touches the ground, then click and reward.  
- **Step 4:** Repeat 10–15 times daily. Gradually fade the treat (reward only when he sits).  

### Crate Training: Cozy vs. Cage  
- **Make it a Sanctuary:**  
  - Line the crate with soft blankets, add a comfy bed, and include a favorite toy.  
  - Feed meals inside the crate (positive association!).  
  - Never use it for punishment—only for naps, safety, or potty breaks.  
- **Escape-Proofing:**  
  - Ensure the crate is escape-proof (e.g., no gaps under it, secure latches).  
  - Use a baby gate or leash if your yard isn’t fenced.  
  - Practice recall training: If he ever escapes, calmly call him back with treats/toys.  

### Final Advice:  
- **Consistency is Key:** Short training sessions (5–10 mins) daily work better than long ones.  
- **Patience:** Galileo is tiny (will only go to 7–12 lbs)—go slow!  
- **Socialization:** Take him to dog parks/training classes to meet others. Papillons are sociable!
