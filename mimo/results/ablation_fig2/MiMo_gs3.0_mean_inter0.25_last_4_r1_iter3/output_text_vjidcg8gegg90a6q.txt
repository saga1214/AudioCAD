Alright, the main goal for bedding in the brooder is to be super absorbent and easy to clean. Pine shavings are a good choice. For better insulation, you can use what's called a "wood pellet litter." It's dense and provides a lot of natural warmth as it composts underneath. But the absolute best option for winter is called "Orchard Floor Mix." It's a combination of wood chips, pine pellets, and sand, and it's fantastic for staying warm and dry. The key is to avoid straw again, and for sand, make sure it's a clean, deep litter. 

As a bonus tip, make sure your brooder has a wall with a vent that you can adjust based on the temperature. And you can always use a space heater to pre-warm the room before you bring the chicks inside.
