Okay, that's a good thing to think about. Kosher dietary laws have a few key things to keep in mind. The most important one is avoiding meat and dairy together. So, for your healthy snack and sweet, as long as you keep them separate, you're probably fine. Mini weenies are usually pork, so they're kosher. Hummus or fruit doesn't have dairy. Dark chocolate without dairy is also generally okay. The main thing is to make sure nothing is cooked with meat broth or anything like that. If you're cooking oatmeal or barley for thickening, just make sure it's plain and not cooked with non-kosher broth. You can label things if you want, but honestly, a simple plate of veggies with hummus is always a safe bet. Just be mindful of not having dairy-based dips next to meat-based snacks. That's the main thing.
