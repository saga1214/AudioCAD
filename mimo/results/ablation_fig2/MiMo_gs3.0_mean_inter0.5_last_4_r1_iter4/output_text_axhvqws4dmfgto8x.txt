Stir-frying uses almost no water, so that risk is gone. For the noodles, the most important thing is to add them to salted water that is already boiling vigorously. Stirring is optional, but it helps prevent sticking.

Here's the most crucial part: as soon as they are cooked, drain them immediately and rinse them with cold water. This stops the cooking process and makes them less sticky. Then, just toss them with your sauce right in the wok. You've got this!
