Of course! With a two-bedroom apartment and expecting a first baby, I'll focus on road trips within a few hours' drive of Vancouver that are family-friendly and great for beautiful scenery. I'll also include some luxurious options for when you're ready.

Here are five fantastic destinations and activities for you to consider:

First, there's the Okanagan Valley, in British Columbia. It's about a three-hour drive. You can visit wineries for some wine tasting and enjoy beautiful lakes like Okanagan and Kalamalka. It's also a great place for a family picnic.

Second, about four hours away, is the Fraser Valley. It's perfect for farm tours, enjoying fresh local produce, and you can find some lovely boutique wineries there too. The town of Agassiz is especially known for its vineyards.

Third, a bit further, about five hours, is the beautiful Vancouver Island. You can explore the amazing Uplands winery, visit the Butchart Gardens, and go whale watching. The drive through the mountains is stunning.

Fourth, if you're up for a longer trip, about seven hours, is the Kootenay region in British Columbia. It's famous for its world-class wineries, like Klee Vineyards, and has breathtaking mountain scenery.

Finally, for a luxurious weekend trip, you could drive about three and a half hours to the Okanagan Lake area. You can stay at a beautiful resort like the Fairmont Chateau Monte. For fine dining, you could go to the restaurant 101, which has won many awards, or enjoy a lovely meal at the Okanagan Lake Lodge. It's a great way to combine a scenic drive with a high-end experience.
