Of course, let me clear that up. The most widely recognized Boulevardier has whiskey to one and a quarter, vermouth at one, and Campari at one. But using equal parts for all three—three-quarters of an ounce each—is also a classic Boulevardier variation. Your friend was just using the equal-parts version! 

For the cocktail you mentioned, 'Last Word', that's equal parts of three ingredients: three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. Stir and strain, garnish with an orange twist. Let me know if that helps!
