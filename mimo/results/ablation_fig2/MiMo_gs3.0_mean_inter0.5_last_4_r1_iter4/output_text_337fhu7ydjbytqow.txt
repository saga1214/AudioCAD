Here's a tailored closing gear packing list for both pacing segments, optimized for mid-30s cool temps, hills, three aid stations, and running overnight:

### **For the 20-Mile Segment (Day/Night Mix)**
1. **Moisture-wicking base layer** (long/sleeve): Synthetic or merino wool to manage sweat.  
2. **Mid-layer** (light fleece or windbreaker): Fleece for warmth, windbreaker for hilly wind resistance.  
3. **Outer layer**: Wind/water-resistant jacket (e.g., lightweight rain shell).  
4. **Gloves**: Thin running gloves for grip and warmth.  
5. **Hat**: Lightweight cap or baseball cap (adjustable for airflow).  
6. **Headlamp**: Bright, lightweight model with **extra batteries** (critical for night).  
7. **Head/neck buff**: For warmth on exposed skin (neck, ears).  
8. husband's hat/gloves: Bring his spares if he drops them at an aid station.  

### **For the Final 12-Mile Segment (Night Only)**
1. **Moisture-wicking base layer**: Same as above.  
2. **Mid-layer**: Insulated fleece (fleece or puffy vest) for colder temps.  
3. **Outer layer**: **Full wind/water-resistant jacket** (no gaps for wind chill).  
4. **Gloves**: **Thicker gloves** (e.g., fleece-lined) for warmth and grip.  
5. **Hat**: **Warm beanie or skullcap** (essential for head warmth at night).  
6. **Headlamp**: Brighter model with extra batteries (night visibility is critical).  
7. **Buff/gaiter**: For covering face/neck in wind.  
8. **Lightweight pack/vest**: To carry extra layers if needed.  
9. **Reflective gear**: Velcro wristband or ankle strap for visibility.  

### **Key Notes**
- **Aid stations**: Use them! Swap layers, gloves, or batteries as needed.  
- **Footwear**: Trail shoes with **grip** for hills—no flimsy sneakers.  
 pack lightly but prioritize **modularity** (layers > bulk items).  
- **Visibility**: Headlamp + reflective gear are non-negotiable for night.  

Example flow: Start with base + windbreaker + gloves + hat. Add fleece at an aid station if cooling. For the 12 miles, swap to fleece + insulated jacket + warm hat + gloves. You’ve got this! 🏃‍♀️💨
