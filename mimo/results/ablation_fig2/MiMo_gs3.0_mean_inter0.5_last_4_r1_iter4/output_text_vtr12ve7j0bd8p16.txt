Calgary is a great base for adventures. Driving about an hour and a half southeast will take you to Banff, where you can hike or kayak in the Rockies. A bit further, maybe two and a half hours north, is the Ghost Ranch area with its historic sites and hiking trails. If you're up for a longer drive, about three and a half hours northeast, there's the Kananaskis Country area, which is fantastic for hiking and has a charming little town. And of course, just across the border in Montana, there's the Banff Hot Springs in the town of Banff.
