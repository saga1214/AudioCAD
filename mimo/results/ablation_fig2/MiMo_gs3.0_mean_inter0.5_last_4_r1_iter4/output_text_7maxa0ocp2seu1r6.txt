### **I. Core Body Measurements (Take for Every Fitting)**

### **II. Garment-Specific Measurements (Add for Better Fit)**
*   **Tops, Jackets, Shirts:**
    *   Shoulder Width (across shoulders)
    *   Neck (circumference)
    *   Sleeve Length (from shoulder point to wrist bone)
*   **Pants, Skirts:**
    *   Thigh (at fullest part)
    *   Knee (at fullest part)
    *   **Back Rise:** Distance from crotch seam to natural waist measurement (CRUCIAL for tall clients!)
    *   **Front Rise:** Distance from crotch seam to natural waist measurement.
    *   Ankle (optional, for very tailored fits).

### **III. Your Client Example Measurements & Key Considerations**
*   **Bust:** 34"
*   **Natural Waist:** ~30-32" (based on natural waist being above belly button)
*   **Hips:** ~40"
*   **Front Rise:** ~12" (Key for comfort on a tall frame)
