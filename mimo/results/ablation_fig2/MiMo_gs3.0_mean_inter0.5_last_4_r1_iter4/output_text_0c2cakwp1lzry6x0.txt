### **Stay Focused While Decorating at Work**
1. **Physical Workspace Boundaries**  
   - Dedicate a specific area *only* for work (e.g., a desk not near unpacked boxes/tools).  
   - Use a screen divider or bookshelf to separate decorating materials from your workspace.  
   - **Routine hack:** Wear a "work hat" (e.g., a specific hoodie) to signal "decorating-free mode."  

2. **Decorating Sprints**  
   - Schedule 30-minute breaks during work hours to tackle decorating. Set a timer to avoid lingering.  
   - Keep decorating supplies in a single box/zone (e.g., under the bed) to minimize distractions.  

3. **Progress-Driven Motivation**  
   - Reward yourself after completing work goals (e.g., "After finishing this report, I’ll hang one shelf").

### **Decorating Installation Guides**
#### **1. TV Mounting**  
- **Tools:** Drill, stud finder, level, drill bits, screws.  
- **Steps:**  
  1. Mark mounting brackets on wall (use level!).  
  2. Drill pilot holes *into studs* (avoid drywall only!).  
  3. Secure brackets, then mount TV.  
- **Noise Tip:** Do this on weekends (per landlord rules) or early AM.  

#### **2. Hanging Planters**  
- **Tools:** Drill, hooks, string, scissors.  
- **Steps:**  
  1. Choose lightweight pots (ceramic > plastic).  
  2. Drill 2–3 holes in pots for drainage.  
  3. Suspend with twine or hooks (use *magnetic hooks* for easy removal if needed).  
- **Noise Tip:** Avoid drilling on late nights/weekends.  

#### **3. Floating Shelves**  
- **Tools:** Drill, wall anchors, level, measuring tape.  
- **Steps:**  
  1. Measure shelf depth (leave 1–2" clearance from wall).  
  2. Mark shelf position (use level!).  
  3. Drill into studs (or use heavy-duty anchors for drywall).  
  4. Attach shelf brackets.  
- **Safety:** Test weight capacity before adding heavy items.  

---

### **Decorating in Small Spaces: Studio Apartment Tips**
- **Vertical Storage:** Use wall-mounted racks instead of floor clutter.  
- **Lighting:** Add LED strip lights under shelves for ambiance (no drilling needed!).  
- **Multi-Functional Furniture:** Choose a sofa bed or nesting tables.  
- **Art Hacks:** Use removable wallpaper or peel-and-stick art for quick updates.

1. **Structure > Decorate:** Schedule decorating like work meetings.

4. **Install Safely:** Prioritize stud mounting for heavy items (TV/shelves).  
5. **Noise Awareness:** Decorate during allowed hours to respect landlord rules.
