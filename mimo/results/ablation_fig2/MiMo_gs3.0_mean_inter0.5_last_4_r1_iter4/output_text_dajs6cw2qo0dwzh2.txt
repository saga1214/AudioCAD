The capture of Constantinople was a major catalyst. It happened at the exact moment the technology was ready and the competition was high. It provided the critical push that accelerated the race for a direct sea route. So while the Portuguese were already exploring, that event was the spark that lit the fire.

To say it would have happened at the same time is a bit too strong. It removed a huge obstacle and gave the Portuguese a massive advantage. It was a crucial event that shaped the *pace* and *direction* of the whole era.
