### Cocktails & Finger Foods for Fall Reception

#### 1. **Old Fashioned**  
*Glass:* Rocks glass  
*Glassware:* Ice  
Garnish: Orange twist  
**Recipe:**  
- 2 oz bourbon (or rye)  
- 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- *Method:* Muddle sugar cube with ½ oz water (optional, for cube version) OR add simple syrup. Add bitters and bourbon. Stir with ice until chilled. Strain into glass over fresh ice. Express orange twist over drink and discard.

#### 2. **Paper Plane**  
*Glass:* Coupe  
*Glassware:* Shake  
Garnish: Cherry  
**Recipe:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- 1 oz Aperol  
- 1 oz Amaro Nonino  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- *Method:* Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a coupe glass. Garnish with cherry.

#### 3. **Whiskey Sour**  
*Glass:* Rocks glass  
*Glassware:* Shake  
Garnish: Cherry or lemon wheel  
**Recipe:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- *Optional:* 0.5 oz egg white (for texture)  
- *Method with egg white:*  
  1. Dry shake (no ice) all ingredients + egg white.  
  2. Add ice, shake again.  
  3. Strain into glass over ice.  
- *Without egg white:* Stir all ingredients with ice. Serve over ice.

#### 4. **Aviation**  
*Glass:* Cocktail glass  
*Glassware:* Shake  
Garnish: Maraschino cherry  
**Recipe:**  
- 2 oz gin  
- ¾ oz maraschino liqueur (e.g., Luxardo)  
- ¼ oz crème de violette  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- *Method:* Shake with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe or cocktail glass. Garnish with cherry.

#### 5. **Paper Plane Gin Cocktail Recommendation**  
**French 75**  
*Glass:* Champagne flute  
*Glassware:* Shake  
Garnish: Lemon twist  
**Recipe:**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz lemon juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 3 oz chilled prosecco/champagne  
- *Method:* Shake gin, lemon, and syrup with ice. Strain into flute. Top with prosecco. Express lemon twist over drink.
