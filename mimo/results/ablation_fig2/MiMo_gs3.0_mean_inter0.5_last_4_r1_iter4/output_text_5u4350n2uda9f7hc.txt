That east-facing fifth-floor NYC balcony is perfect for some bright little plants.

Geraniums give great color and handle city winds. Herbs like simple basil also love morning sun.

Sturdy geraniums won't overcrowd a tiny display table. Succulents stay small, love light, and need little water on your table. And the bird's nest snake plant, with its short, sturdy leaves in a circle, stays low on the floor and is tough against wind.

All these small plants are easy to carry up five flights of stairs. Since it's just for a month, you can definitely bring them home with you after your onsite ends.
