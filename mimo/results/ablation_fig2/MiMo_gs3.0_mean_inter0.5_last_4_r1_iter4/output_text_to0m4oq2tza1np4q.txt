That's a smart move! Pickles can add tangy flavor to many dishes, but they're also high in sodium, so moderation is key if you're reducing daily salt intake. Here are some delicious and healthy ways to use them and the juice:

1.  **Pickle Juice Electrolyte Boost:** After your run, drink a small glass of pickle juice (or dilute it with water) for a quick sodium and potassium (an electrolyte) hit. It's naturally hydrating! *(Aligns with your running/electrolytes goal)*
2.  **Pickle Juice Marinade:** Whisk pickle juice with a little olive oil, garlic, mustard, and spices (like dill or paprika). Use this to marinate chicken, pork, tofu, or even fish (like your salmon) for a tangy, flavorful, and slightly healthier (lower sodium than store-bought) alternative to soy sauce or vinegar-based marinades. *(Great for your cooking-at-home habit & protein recipes)*
3.  **Pickle Relish in Salads:** Chop up pickles and add them to your salads (like the spinach/tomato salad you might make). They add a great crunch and tang. *(Builds on your spinach/tomato theme)*
4.  **Pickle "Sauce" for Dips or Drizzling:** Finely chop pickles and mix them with a little plain Greek yogurt or sour cream (for extra protein) and a touch of mustard. A great low-sugar, high-protein dip for veggies or drizzled over grilled chicken or fish. *(Boosts protein in your meals)*
5.  **Pickles in Soups or Stews:** Add a few pickles (or pickle relish) to bean soups, chili, or stews for a burst of flavor without needing extra salt. *(Helps manage sodium in cooked dishes)*
6.  grow your own quick-pickled veggies! Use cucumbers, carrots, onions, or radishes in a jar with your favorite spices (like dill, peppercorns, garlic) and a splash of vinegar (white or apple cider). This lets you control the sodium and vinegar levels. *(Connects back to your balcony garden idea)*

**Important Note on Sodium:** Remember that pickles and pickle juice are naturally very salty. While the uses above can help incorporate them in healthier ways, be mindful of your overall sodium goal (that 1,500-2,300mg limit we discussed). Enjoy them as part of a balanced diet, but don't overdo it!

Have fun experimenting – pickles can definitely add a unique twist to your healthy cooking!
