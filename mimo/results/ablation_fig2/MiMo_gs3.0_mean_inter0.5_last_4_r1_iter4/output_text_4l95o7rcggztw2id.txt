Here’s a **nautical-themed, sand-friendly shopping list** for your son’s pirate birthday party at the beach, designed for little hands and pirate fun! All options avoid watermelon (due to allergy) and minimize mess:

---

### **Appetizers (Arrr, It’s Treasure Time!)**
1.  **Mini Nautical Sandwiches Sliders (x8):**  
    - Bread (sub with mini rolls or ship-shaped cookies if found)  
    - Fillings: Cream cheese + cucumber "island" slices, tuna salad (no mayo if preferred), or cheese cubes ("gold coins").  
    - Cut into small rectangles or triangles. Label as "Pirate’s Rations" or "Ship’s Treats."  
2.  Fruit "Jewels" Skewers (x6 sets of 4 skewers):  
    - Melon balls ("pearls"), pineapple chunks ("sunken treasure"), grapes ("jewels"), berries ("gems").  
    - Skip watermelon! Use cantaloupe or honeydew instead.  
3.  **Pirate’s Snack Packets:**  
    - Small snack cups filled with:  
      - Goldfish crackers ("pirate booty")  
      - Cheese sticks ("shiny doubloons")  
      - Pretzel rods ("ship masts")  
      - Apple slices ("pearl necklace")  

---

### **Main Course (Yarrr, It’s Dinner Time!)**
4.  **Mini Nautical Sliders (x8):** Pack extra for leftovers.  
    - **Theme Twist:** Use cookie cutters on bread before baking for ship, treasure chest, or anchor shapes.  
    - **Allergy Note:** Ensure tuna salad/tuna uses no watermelon.  

---

### **Sides (No Sandy Snacks!)**
5.  **Veggie "Pearls" (x6 serving trays):**  
    - Cherry tomatoes ("red pearls"), cucumber slices ("island chips"), or bell pepper strips ("sunken ships").  
    - Serve in sealed containers with small spoons.  
6.  **"Beached Crab" Chips (x6 bags):**  
    - Tortilla chips or pita chips.  
    - Serve in small bags tied with string or in mini treasure chests.  

---

### **Drinks (No Spills on Deck!)**
7.  **Juice Boxes/Pouches (x8):**  
    - Tropical flavors ("pirate punch"), berry blends, or apple juice.  
    - Label with pirate names (e.g., "Captain [Son’s Name]") or ship names.  
8.  **Individual Water Bottles (x8):**  
    - Reusable or disposable. Add pirate-themed stickers or labels.  
9.  **"Saltwater" Mocktail Station (Optional):**  
    - **Ingredients:** Limeade, lemonade, or orange juice (in sealed pitchers).  
    - **Mix-in:** Pinch of salt (for "ocean flavor"), splash of grenadine ("treasure color").  
    - **Serve:** In juice boxes or small cups with pirate spoons.  

---

### **Sand-Free Packaging (Key for Beach Chaos!)**
- **Treasure Chest Trays:** Use small cardboard treasure chests (from craft stores) to hold snacks.  
- **Snack Bags:** Seal popcorn, crackers, and cheese in small zip-top bags.  
- **Skewer Covers:** Use paper cones to hold fruit skewers upright.  
- **Utensils:** Mini plastic spoons (no forks needed).  

---

### **Allergy Reminder**
- **Double-check** labels for watermelon in juices, sauces, or fillings.  
- Consider a **nut-free** option like cheese cubes or fruit if other allergies exist.  

This setup keeps hands clean, avoids sand, and turns snacks into pirate adventures! Your son will feel like the captain of his own nautical bash. 🏴‍☠️🎂
