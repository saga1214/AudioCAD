Okay, since you're skipping replacement and prepped down to the bare wood, here's what to do before primer and paint. After sanding, wipe down every surface with a tack cloth or a damp rag to remove all that fine dust.

Then, and this is critical for going from black to off-white, apply your primer. Use a brush to get into all the corners and grooves where rollers can't reach, and then use a roller for the main flat surfaces. You really want to make sure you get two thin coats of primer on there. Let the first one dry completely, and then apply the second one.
