Absolutely! Here's a step-by-step plan to make your Costa Rica dream trip unforgettable, tailored for scuba divers like you and your husband:

### 1. **Fin Choice Settled?**
   - **Recommendation:** Start with **short fins** for your first Costa Rica trip. They’re easier to learn on, maneuverable, and great for calm waters/sites. Costa Rica’s entry-level dives (like those in **Cahuita National Park**) are perfect for this.

### 2. **Top Scuba Destinations in Costa Rica:**
   - **Cahuita National Park (Puntarenas):**  
     - **Why:** Gentle waves, shallow reefs, and abundant marine life (turtles, rays, parrotfish). Ideal for beginners.  
     - **Must-Dive Sites:** *Cahuita Reef* (colorful coral gardens), *Los Muertos* (coral formations).  
   - **Santa Rosa Island (Near Puerto Viejo):**  
     - **Why:** Strong currents and deeper walls for advanced divers. Famous for **Cocos Island’s dramatic underwater topography**.  
     - **Must-Dive Sites:** *Mangrove Kayak & Dive* (unique ecosystem), *Cocos Island’s walls* (advanced).  
   - **Cocos Island (Pacifica):**  
     - **Why:** Remote, nutrient-rich waters mean **amazing visibility (up to 100m!) and epic marine life** (sharks, hammerheads, manta rays).  
     - **Best for:** Experienced divers. Requires a liveaboard trip (2-4 days).

### 3. **Trip Structure:**
   - **Day 1-2:** Explore **Cahuita** (30-40 mins from San José airport). Book a dive shop with PADI/SSI certifications.  
   - **Day 3-4:** Fly to **Santa Rosa Island** (1-hour flight from San José). Dive on a day-trip boat.  
   - **Day 5-8:** Liveaboard to **Cocos Island** (book early!). Sleep on the boat, dive daily.  
   - **Day 9:** Fly home from Drake Bay or Puerto Jiménez.

### 4. **Marine Life to Expect:**
   - **Turtles** (green, leatherback), **sharks** (reef sharks, occasional tiger), **manta rays**, **whale sharks** (seasonal), **octopuses**, and vibrant **coral gardens**. Cocos Island is especially famous for **whale sharks** (Dec-Apr) and **shark aggregations** (Mar-May).

### 5. **Logistics & Tips:**
   - **Certification:** Ensure you’re **PADI Open Water Certified**. If not, book a course in Costa Rica (many shops offer it).  
   - **Booking:** Use **Costa Rica Dive** or **Santa Rosa Dive Center** for reliable operators.  
   - **Gear:** Stick with short fins initially. Bring reef-safe sunscreen and a dive computer.  
   - **Safety:** Choose operators with safety records (e.g., low dive-casualty rates).  
   - **Budget:** Expect $150-$300/day for dives (shorter trips) or $600-$1,200 for Cocos Island liveaboard.  

### 6. **Beyond Scuba:**
   - **Snorkeling:** Visit **La Fortuna Volcano** (hot springs + snorkel) or **Manuel Antonio** (mammal-filled reefs).  
   - **Nature:** Combine with **Arenal Volcano** (hot springs) or **Monteverde Cloud Forest**.  

### Final Advice:
Start with **Cahuita** to build confidence, then progress to **Santa Rosa** and **Cocos Island**. Costa Rica’s waters are legendary for their biodiversity—prepare for an unforgettable adventure! 🐠🌊
