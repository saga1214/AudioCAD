Yes, older comics are generally more rare, which is a big plus. However, their condition is extremely fragile.

Ten rare and valuable comics from the 1940s and 50s are:

First, *Action Comics* number one again, Superman's first full appearance.
Second, *惊奇队长* number fifty, Wolverine's first appearance.
Third, *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen, Spider-Man's.
Fourth, *Captain America Comics* number one.
Fifth, a 1945 *Warrior* number one, which was the first comic with Superman on the cover.
Sixth, *The Amazing Spider-Man* number fifty, which introduces the villain Kraven the Hunter.
Seventh, *The Incredible Hulk* number one from 1962.
Eighth, *The Fantastic Four* number one from 1961.
Ninth, *Thor* number one from 1962.
And tenth, *X-Men* number one from 1963, which is now the most valuable comic in the world.

Just a very important note: the condition of these books is critical. A nearly perfect copy of *Action Comics* sold for six million. A similar copy with any damage could be worth almost nothing. You have to be extremely careful.
