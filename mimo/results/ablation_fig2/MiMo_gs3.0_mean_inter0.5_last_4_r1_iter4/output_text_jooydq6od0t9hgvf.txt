Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes for your dinner party—keeping formality, mixed age groups, and soda allergy in mind:

### 1. **Vodka Cocktail: "Squeaky Wheel"**  
*(Refreshing, light, and soda-free)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz vodka  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
  - 0.5 oz cucumber-infused simple syrup (steep cucumber in sugar-water for 30 mins, strain)  
  - Top with **soda-free sparkling water** (not soda!)  
- **Garnish**: Cucumber ribbon, lime wedge  
- **Why it works**: Light, modern, and hydrating. Cucumber balances vodka’s bite, and sparkling water adds effervescence without soda.  

### 2. **Argentine Fernet Cocktail: "Fernet & Club"**  
*(Bold but balanced—mimics the classic Argentine style)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1 oz Fernet-Branca  
  - 2 oz soda water ( Club Soda)  
  - 0.5 oz honey syrup (equal parts honey + warm water)  
  - 0.25 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Garnish**: Orange peel twist + lime wedge  
- **Why it works**: Fernet’s herbal bitterness is tempered by honey and citrus. Soda lightens it (safe for soda allergy), making it approachable for younger guests while still complex enough for older palates.  

### Pro Tips:  
- **Soda Substitute**: Use **sparkling water** (plain or lightly flavored with lime/cucumber).  
- **Prep Ahead**: Mix syrups (simple/honey), muddle garnishes, and chill glassware.  
- **Service**: Serve in ice-filled glasses with a small bowl of citrus wedges.  

These drinks are easy to scale, visually appealing, and cater to all dietary needs. Enjoy your party! 🍸
