November is a bit quieter but still beautiful with the Monarch butterfly migration in Monarch Butterfly Biosphere Reserve. You’ll have plenty of inspiration for warm hues and landscapes. Just make sure to pack your paints and hit the streets! ¡Buen viaje y buen pintaje!
