That sounds perfect! Since you dislike sweet/chocolaty snacks, vending machines usually have great savory options. Here are some ideas to fill your coffee table:

1.  **Beef Jerky:** A classic savory, protein-packed choice. Many flavors available.
2.  **Roasted Chickpeas:** Crunchy, savory, and often spiced (like BBQ, garlic, or chili lime).
3.  **Popcorn:** Look for plain, lightly salted, or white cheddar flavors instead of sugary ones. Air-popped is best.
4.  **Cheese Sticks or Cubes:** A satisfying savory bite. Many machines have them.
5.  **Nuts:** Especially unsalted almonds, walnuts, or peanuts. Check for plain or lightly salted varieties.
6.  **Pretzels:** Mini pretzels, twists, or sticks are always a savory hit.
7.  **Crackers:** Look for plain, whole-grain, or lightly flavored varieties (like herb or seed).
8.  **Roasted Edamame:** Lightly salted, crunchy soybeans.
9.  **Savory Protein Bars:** Many brands offer bars with savory flavors like peanut butter, chocolate-seed (but check if the chocolate is too prominent), or savory nuts/cheese.
10. **Potato Chips:** While often salty, many machines offer varieties like plain, salt & vinegar, or BBQ (if BBQ isn't too sweet for you).

**Things to Avoid (assuming non-sweet/chocolatey):**
*   Most candy bars (chocolate, caramel, fruit-flavored).
*   Most gummies.
*   Most marshmallows.
*   Most sugary-coated pretzels or nuts.
*   Most sugary cereals or puffed rice snacks.

**Tip:** If the machine has a "mix" option, see if it leans savory. Enjoy your newly filled coffee table and project! Maybe those savory snacks will fuel your IKEA creativity.
