Here’s a quick recap of **all the treat ideas** for your fabulous corgi princess, organized for easy reference:

### 🐾 **Homemade Treats**  
1. **Frozen Bites**:  
   - Mashed pumpkin, sweet potato, or banana frozen in ice cube trays.  
   - *Bonus*: Add a splash of yogurt for extra protein!  

2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**:  
   - Mix peanut butter + rolled oats, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10–12 mins. Cool, then cut into tiny pieces.  
   - *Safety first*: Use only xolitol-free peanut butter.  

3. **Protein Bites**:  
   - Blend oats, ground turkey/chicken, and grated apple; roll into 1-inch balls. Freeze for a "frozen chew."  

4. **Carrot or Sweet Potato Sticks**:  
   - Slice into thin sticks for natural, low-calorie chews.  

### 🛒 **Store-Bought Brands**  
- **Budget-friendly**: Zuke’s Mini Naturals, Wellness Treats.  
- **Premium**: Open Farm, Fromm.
