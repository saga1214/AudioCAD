Here's a quick recap of the human circulatory system using your child-friendly car analogy:

1.  **Heart:** The **gas station & engine**. Pumps blood (cars) all around your body and powers the whole trip.
2.  **Blood:** Like **little delivery cars** driving on body-wide roads.
3.  Red Blood Cells: The **cars themselves**.
4.  Lungs: The **fresh fuel stop**. Cars get packed with oxygen here to refuel.
5.  Arteries: The **big highways**. Carry blood *away* from the heart to deliver fuel.
6.  Capillaries: The **tiny neighborhood streets**. Where cars stop to drop off oxygen fuel to your muscles/organ "houses".
7.  Veins: The **return roads**. Carry blood (without oxygen) *back* to the heart/lungs for refueling.
8.  Kidneys: Like **car washes & junk processors**. Filter waste and extra water out of the blood so the cars can keep delivering clean fuel.
9.  Liver: Like the **body's repair shop & recycling center**. Processes nutrients and breaks down harmful stuff from the blood.

**In a nutshell:** Your heart-pumped blood cars drive highways, tiny streets, and return roads. They pick up oxygen fuel at the lungs, deliver it everywhere, drop off the junk they collected at the kidneys and liver, and then head back to the lungs to refuel again!
