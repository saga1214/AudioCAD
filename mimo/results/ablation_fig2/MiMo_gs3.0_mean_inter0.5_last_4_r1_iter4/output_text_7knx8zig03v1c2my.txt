That's wonderful! For treats, **high-value rewards** work best for training—like small pieces of cooked chicken, cheese cubes, peanut butter (xylitol-free!), or freeze-dried liver. Start with tiny portions (about the size of a pea) since puppies have small stomachs. Offer one treat per successful command to avoid overfeeding.

**Clicker Training Explained:**  
Clicker training uses a **three-step sequence**:  
1. **Click**: Press the clicker instantly after the puppy performs the desired behavior (e.g., sits).  
2. **Treat**: Give a treat *immediately* after the click.  
3. **Repeat**: Practice until the click/treat sequence becomes a reliable "Yes!" signal.  

**How it works**:  
- The click marks the *exact moment* the puppy does something right.  
- The treat reinforces that behavior.  
- Over time, the puppy learns that sitting = click + treat.  

**Example for "Sit"**:  
1. Hold a treat near Galileo’s nose.  
2. Slowly raise it over his head.  
3. The moment his bottom touches the ground, *click*!  
4. Give the treat.  
5. Repeat 10-15 times daily.  

**Key Tips**:  
- Use the clicker consistently (same tone).  
- Keep sessions short (5-10 minutes).  
- Pair with verbal commands ("Good sit!").  
- Gradually phase out the clicker once he understands the behavior.
