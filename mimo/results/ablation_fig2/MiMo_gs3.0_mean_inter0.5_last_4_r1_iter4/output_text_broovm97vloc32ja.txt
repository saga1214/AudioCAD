That's an interesting point. If you're the illustrator as well as the writer, you *can* submit your complete manuscript and artwork package to publishers, but it's a bit more complicated.

Here's the reality: publishers are often hesitant to accept an entire package from one author-illustrator. They prefer to have the flexibility to work with a separate illustrator they've chosen. So, the most common and successful strategy is to submit the words *only* and let the publisher decide on the art later.

However, you can absolutely show your illustrations to agents and publishers to prove your skills. You could create a PDF portfolio that you attach to your query, but it's crucial to check the publisher's submission guidelines first, as many explicitly state they don't want to see artwork in the initial submission.

So, the key is to submit the words first, but to be prepared to show your artistic talent to them later.
