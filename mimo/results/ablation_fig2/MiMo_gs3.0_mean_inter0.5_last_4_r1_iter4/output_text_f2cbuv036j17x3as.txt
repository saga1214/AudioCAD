Okay, diesel camper vans age, and maintenance is key before a long trip. Brakes are critical—look for any squealing, grinding, or a soft pedal feel when pressing down. Also, check for any unusual vibrations or a long stopping distance. If you’re seeing any of those, definitely get them inspected before you go. For spark plugs, those are inside the diesel engine's combustion chamber, and they’re tough to get to without tools and experience. Honestly, it’s safer and easier to have a mechanic do that. Before you hit the road, just make sure your diesel filter, oil, and coolant levels are good, and check the tires. A professional inspection is always the best move for a diesel van.
