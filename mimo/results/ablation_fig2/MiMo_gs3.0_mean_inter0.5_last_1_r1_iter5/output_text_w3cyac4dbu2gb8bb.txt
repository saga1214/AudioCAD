It sounds like you're caught between craving your special latte and the weather! Here’s a practical approach:

### For the Rain & Wind:
1. **Rain Jacket First**: A good waterproof jacket with a hood is your best bet. It’s more reliable than an umbrella in strong wind and won’t blow inside out.  
2. **Umbrella Backup**: If it’s *only* pouring (not windy), bring a compact, wind-resistant umbrella. Keep it tucked under your jacket initially to avoid flapping.  
3. **Timing**: Check the forecast—will it pass quickly? If yes, go now! If it’s prolonged, consider waiting 10-15 mins for it to ease up.  

### For Your Latte:
- Dissolve **2-3 instant coffee shots** in a tiny bit of hot water to avoid clumps.  
- Warm the milk by microwaving it for 30-60 seconds (stirring every 15 sec), then pour into your coffee.  
- If using Cornish milk, add it last to preserve its creaminess.
