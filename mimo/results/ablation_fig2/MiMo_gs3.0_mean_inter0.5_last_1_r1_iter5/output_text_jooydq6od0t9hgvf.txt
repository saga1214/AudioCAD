Here are quick, elegant recipes for both cocktails—perfect for your dinner party:

### 🥛 1. **Vodka Mix** (Refreshing & Light)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup (optional, for balance)  
- Top with **soda water** (safe for the soda allergy)  
- Garnish: Mint sprig and lime wedge  

**How to**: Shake vodka, lime juice, and syrup with ice. Strain into a chilled glass, top with soda water, and garnish.  

### 🍊 2. **Argentinian Vin de Verre** (Wine-Based & Zesty)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 5 oz dry white wine (e.g., Sauvignon Blanc)  
- 1 oz fresh orange juice  
- ½ oz brandy  
- 2–3 dashes orange bitters  
- Garnish: Orange slice and lemon twist  

**How to**: Stir wine, orange juice, brandy, and bitters in a mixing glass with ice. Strain into a wine glass. Garnish with orange and lemon twist.  

### 💡 Tips:  
- **Label drinks** clearly so guests know which is which.  
- Offer **non-alcoholic options** like sparkling water with lemon or the vin de verre without brandy.  
- For formality, serve both cocktails in stemmed glasses (coupos or flute).
