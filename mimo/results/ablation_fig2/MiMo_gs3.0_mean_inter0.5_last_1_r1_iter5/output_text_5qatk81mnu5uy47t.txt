That's a fantastic goal! Here's a plan to make your dream Costa Rica scuba diving trip unforgettable, tailored to your interests and experience level:

**1. Destination Focus: Costa Rica is Perfect**
   * **Why?** It matches your dream trip status and offers incredible diversity: warm tropical waters, vibrant coral reefs, abundant marine life (turtles, rays, sharks!), wrecks, and unique environments like Cocos Island (thrill diving) and coral gardens (beginner-friendly).
   * **Fin Choice for Costa Rica:** **Start with Short Fins.** Most dive sites are relatively calm, close to shore, and focus on exploration and marine life. Short fins are compact, maneuverable, and less tiring. You can always rent long fins for specific current-heavy sites later.

**2. Must-See Dive Sites in Costa Rica:**
   * **Cocos Island (Puntarenas):** The crown jewel! Famous for large pelagics (sharks, tuna, marlin), caves, wrecks, and strong currents (advanced). Requires a boat trip. *Ideal if you want adventure.*
   * **Cahuita (Puntarenas):** Great for beginners and intermediate divers. Close to shore, shallow reefs, abundant tropical fish, sea turtles (very common!), and coral gardens. Boat dives often include a snorkel at the beautiful Cahuita beach park.
   * **Playa Conchal (Limón):** Close to San José airport. Offers a good mix: coral walls, wrecks (like the *Aurora*), and muck diving opportunities. Easy boat access.
   * **Tortuguero (Limón):** Unique jungle access via canals. Dives are often in protected bays, focusing on marine life (turtles, rays), wrecks, and potentially sargassum sea (home to sea turtles). More relaxed vibe.
   * **Isla del Dr. Jones (Limón):** A fantastic wreck dive, accessible from Playa Conchal. Great for spotting fish life around the structure.
   * **San Juanillo (Limón):** Known for its beautiful coral walls teeming with fish, good visibility, and occasional large pelagics. Great for reef diving.

**3. Planning Tips for an Unforgettable Trip:**
   * **Timing is Key:** **Dry Season (December - April)** offers the best visibility and conditions. Avoid peak crowds if possible (Jan/Feb).
   * **Dive Shops Matter:** Choose reputable shops with good safety records, modern gear, and experienced dive masters. Look for shops that cater to beginners and offer guided dives. Research online reviews.
   * **Book in Advance:** Especially for popular sites like Cocos Island or during peak season. Book accommodation and dive packages early.
   * **PADI Open Water Certification (If Needed):** Ensure you both have current certification. If not, Costa Rica is a great place to get certified (many shops offer courses).
   * **Travel Logistics:** Fly into San José International Airport (SJO). From there, rent a car or take shuttles to dive sites (Puntarenas, Limón, Tortuguero). Limón is the main hub for Southern Costa Rica (Cahuita, Playa Conchal, Tortuguero, San Juanillo, Isla del Dr. Jones).
   * **Gear:** Stick with short fins initially. Bring your own gear or rent from your chosen dive shop (they have everything). Don't forget dive computers, masks, fins, wetsuits (3mm is often sufficient in the dry season), and reef-safe sunscreen.
   * **Beyond Scuba:** Costa Rica is more than diving! Include time for:
        * **Tortuguero Canals:** Jungle trekking, wildlife spotting (monkeys, sloths!), river kayaking.
        * **Arenal Volcano Area:** Hot springs, rainforest hikes, Arenal Lake.
        * **Monteverde Cloud Forest:** Zip-lining, hanging bridges, wildlife.
        * ** Manuel Antonio National Park:** Beautiful beaches, hiking trails, wildlife.

**4. Alternative Tropical Options (If Costa Rica Feels Similar):**
   * **Belize:** Offers the incredible Great Blue Hole, pristine reefs (like the Barrier Reef), and famous sites like Half Moon Caye (great for beginners). Very similar tropical diving to Costa Rica, often with slightly warmer water. You already know its beauty!
