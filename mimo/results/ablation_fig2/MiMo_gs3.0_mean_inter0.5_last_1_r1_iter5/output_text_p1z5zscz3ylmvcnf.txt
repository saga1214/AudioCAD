Alright, classic cars. That's a great idea, especially for an 'old-school guy' who only buys American and changes his own oil. It taps right into his pride while also being a smart investment.

Here are a few that are known to appreciate in value. First, the Ford Mustang. It's the most iconic American muscle car. Certain models, like the first-generation Mustangs from 1964 to 1966, or the '70s models with the V8 engines, have really appreciated. A '67 Fastback could be worth tens of thousands.

Then there's the Chevrolet Corvette. The C2 models from 1963 to 1967 are highly sought after. A '63 Splitback or a '67 Stingray can be worth a lot more than when they were new.

Another one is the Dodge Charger. The first-gen Chargers from '66 to '68, especially the models with Hemi engines, are really popular. A '67 Charger 500 could be worth a lot.

The Plymouth Hemi 'Cuda is another one. It's a muscle car legend. A '69 Hemi 'Cuda can be worth a fortune, sometimes over a million dollars at auctions.

And finally, the Pontiac GTO. It's often called the muscle car that started it all. A '67 GTO Judge is especially prized and can be worth a lot.

Just remember, with any classic car, the condition is everything. A well-restored or original car is always going to be worth more. And a lot of these cars are American, which should be right up his alley. It’s a way for him to keep being independent, but maybe make a little extra money while doing it.
