Here are five fantastic road trip options from Vancouver to explore the beautiful BC coast, each with some must-try activities.

First, there's **Tofino on the West Coast**. It's about a 6-hour drive. You can hike the dramatic Pacific Rim National Park trails, kayak on the wild beaches, and enjoy fresh seafood at a restaurant like The Bayview. For a splurge, try a luxury lodge.

Second, **Victoria on the South Island**. It's a scenic 2.5-hour drive. You can see the Fairmont Empress Hotel, visit the Butchart Gardens, and take a cruise to the famous Butchart Caves. For a luxury touch, try a high-end winery tasting.

Third, **Whistler in the Coast Mountains**. It's about a 3-hour drive. You can explore the Blackcomb Gorge, take the Peak 2 Peak Gondola for amazing views, and go for a gourmet meal at a restaurant like Grouse Mountain Grill. It's great for winter sports or summer hiking.

Fourth, **Kamloops on the Interior Plateau**. It's a 4.5-hour drive. You can visit the Kenna Cartwright Wildlife Centre, relax at the Banff Springs Spa, and enjoy a fine dining experience at a restaurant like The Keg Steakhouse. It's a great stop for a scenic drive.

Fifth, **Prince Rupert on the North Coast**. This is a longer 10-hour drive, but the views are incredible. You can explore the Lighthouse Park, see the Northern Lights in winter, and enjoy fresh-caught salmon at a waterfront restaurant. For a luxury experience, try a boutique hotel.

These trips offer a great mix of nature, culture, and food for you and your growing family! Enjoy your adventures!
