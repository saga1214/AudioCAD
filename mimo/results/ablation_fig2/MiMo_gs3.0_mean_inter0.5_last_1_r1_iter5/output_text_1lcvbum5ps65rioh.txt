### **U-Turn Rules in British Columbia**  
If you make an unauthorized U-turn, expect:  
- **Fine**: $110–$160 (plus 2–3 demerit points on your license, depending on the violation).  
- **Common violations**: Turning at signals, curves, blind spots, or interfering with traffic.  

**Quick Safety Tip**: If unsure, wait for a clear gap in traffic and ensure you can see 150m ahead and behind.

### **Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients**  
Here’s what you’ll need for **2 servings**:  

#### **Essentials**  
- **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti (or bucatini).  
- **Guanciale** (or pancetta): 150g, diced (essential for authentic flavor).  
- **Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 1 egg yolk (for richness).  
- **Cheese**: 100g Pecorino Romano (or Parmesan), finely grated.  
- **Black pepper**: Freshly ground (key for seasoning).  

#### **Optional but Recommended**  
- **Garlic**: 1 clove, lightly crushed (infused in oil).  
- **Chili flakes**: Pinch for heat.  

#### **Avoid Adding**:  
- Cream, garlic powder, or pre-shredded cheese (traditional recipes omit these).  

---

### **Key Cooking Tips**  
1. **Combine eggs/cheese/pepper** off-heat to prevent scrambling.  
2. **Toss pasta with hot guanciale fat** *before* adding eggs.  
3. **Reserve pasta water** to adjust sauce consistency.
