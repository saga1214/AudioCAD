### Do-It-Yoursef Insulation Tips:
1. **Seal Drafts:** Use weather stripping for windows/doors and install door draft stoppers.  
2 Attic Insulation: Check/upgrade insulation there—adding 2-4 inches can stop warm air from escaping.  
3. Window Film: Apply insulation film over older windows to trap heat.  
4 Flue Closure: *Always* close fireplace flues when not in use to prevent warm air loss.  
5 Door Draft Seals: Install magnetic or press-in seals under exterior doors.  

### Fun Winter Activities:
1. **Snowshoeing:** Rent gear or buy affordable sets online. Great for exploring snowy trails!  
2. **Cross-Country Skiing:** Low-impact, full-body workout—rent skis locally.  
3. **Ice Skating:** Try public rinks or frozen ponds (ensure safety!).  
4. Snowshoe Hiking: Find local trails with snowshoe access (check parks departments).  
5. Winter Photography: Capture frost patterns, ice formations, and wildlife.  
6. Build Snow Sculptures: Host a family or neighborhood contest!  

### For Your Mudroom Bench:

- **Tools Needed:** Saw, drill, sandpaper, and basic wood finishing (stain or sealant).  
- **Bonus Tip:** Add hooks or shelves for gear storage!
