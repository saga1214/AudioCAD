Okay, kosher dietary laws mean no mixing meat and dairy, so Tony can't have weenies, which are a type of sausage, and the chocolate sweet either. You'd need to separate those two things. For healthy snacks, things like plain fruit, vegetables without dairy-based dips like the hummus I mentioned earlier, or maybe some rice cakes would be safe. For sweets, maybe just something vegetarian, like nuts or fruit leather. The theme pudding idea would be fine, as long as the ingredients are pure meat and no dairy. The key is just making sure no meat and dairy come into contact with each other at the table.
