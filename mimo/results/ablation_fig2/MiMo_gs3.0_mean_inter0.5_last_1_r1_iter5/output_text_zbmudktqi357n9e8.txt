Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration, especially if your favorite thing all-time *is using all the different autumn colors*—wait, no, that's the other aspiring artist. But yeah, Mexico's fall, which is their spring, is beautiful. October through December is peak season. You'll get those vibrant autumn colors in the north, like in the Yucatán Peninsula and Chihuahua, where leaves change and there are lots of harvests. The coast, like Cancun, is a bit milder, but still lovely. Just... you know, pack light and bring your paints! The warm sun and lush landscapes will definitely fuel your creativity. Happy painting!
