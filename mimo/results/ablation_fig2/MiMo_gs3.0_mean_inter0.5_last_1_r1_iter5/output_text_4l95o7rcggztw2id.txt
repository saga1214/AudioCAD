Here’s a **nautical-themed, sand-free shopping list** for appetizers, main dishes, snacks, drinks, and dessert—perfect for a pirate nautical party at the beach for a 7-year-old and seven guests:

---

### **Appetizers**  
*(Themed as "Buried Treasure" or "Pirate Loot")*  
- **Mini Pirate Dog Biscuits** (Store-bought or homemade): Cut into bone shapes.  
- **"Mermaid Tail" Fish Cakes**: Fish sticks or pollock patties shaped like tails (no watermelon filling).  
- **Veggie Sticks with "Gold" Dip**: Bell pepper strips, cucumber slices, and carrot sticks with ranch or hummus. Serve in mini pirate cups.  
- **Watermelon-Free "Jewel" Fruit Skewers**: Grapes, pineapple chunks, and cherry tomatoes (avoid watermelon). Label with pirate names.  

### **Main Dishes**  
*(Finger-friendly "Pirate's Feast")*  
- **Mini Pirate Sliders**:  
  - Bread: Mini Hawaiian rolls or pirate-ship-shaped bread.  
  - Fillings: Turkey & cheese, or cucumber-tuna salad (no mayo if preferred).  
- **"Ghost" Cheese Dippers**: Mini mozzarella balls or cheddar cubes.  
- **"Cannonball" Meatballs**: Store-bought or homemade turkey/beef meatballs (no allergens).  

### **Snacks**  
*(Easy-to-Handle "Pirate Snacks")*  
- **Goldfish Crackers**: Pack in a treasure chest bin.  
- **"Cursed" Pretzel Rods**: Blue pretzel rods (as "ship masts").  
- **Cheese Cubes**: Cheddar or mozzarella, pre-cut.  
- **Pirate Booty Trail Mix**: Pretzels, crackers, sunflower seeds, and chocolate chips in small bags.  

### **Drinks**  
*(Sand-Proof "Sea Sips")*  
- **Individual Juice Boxes**: "Pirate Punch" (grape/strawberry), "Mermaid Magic" (blue raspberry), or "Treasure Juice" (orange).  
- **Small Water Bottles**: Sealed BPA-free bottles with pirate-themed labels.  
- **"Cup o' Coffee" Teas**: For adults, served in mugs shaped like pirate casks.  

### **Desserts**  
*(Themed "Treasure Chests")*  
- **"Pirate Hat" Cookies**: Chocolate chip or oatmeal cookies with pirate-bandana icing.  
- **"Cannonball" Brownie Bites**: Mini brownies in a cardboard "treasure chest" container.  
Allergies: Use almond flour or gluten-free if needed.  

---

### **Key Prep Tips**  
1. **Packaging**: Use treasure chests, pirate cups, or labeled containers to seal food.  
2. **Allergy Safety**: Double-check labels for watermelon, nuts, dairy, etc. Offer a separate "Allergy Box" if needed.  
3. **Beach Setup**: Use a large tablecloth to catch spills. Place food in low-traffic zones.  
4. **Theming**: Label everything with pirate names or nautical terms ("Arrr, It’s the Pirate’s Platter!").  

This list balances fun, practicality, and safety—ensuring sandy worries fade while your son rules the high seas! 🏴‍☠️⚓
