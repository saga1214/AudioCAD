For chill, trainable, and kid/squirrel-friendly breeds/hybrids:  
1. **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel**—small, affectionate, and good with kids.  
2. **Bulldog/Pug mixes**—low-energy, friendly, and less likely to bark excessively alone.  
3. **Golden Retriever mixes** (if size rules allow) or small Goldens—obedient, playful, and great with families.  
4. **Bichon Frise**—small, hypoallergenic, and sociable.  
5. **French Bulldog**—small, low-shedding, and chill.  

Research each breed’s quirks (e.g., Frenchie snoring) and ensure they fit your high-rise life. Train early, even with chill dogs, to reinforce good behavior when left alone. Happy dog hunting!
