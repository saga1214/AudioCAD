Okay, breaking it down task by task first. Drywall stuff: compound small tub, say five to ten bucks. Sand tool, nails/adhesive, probably five to fifteen. Primer and matching paint depend on wall size, but a small can is maybe ten to twenty. Damaged molding if any, that could be five to twenty.

Cake shopping: Two cups flour is about three dollars. Cup-and-a-half sugar, another three. Baking powder cheap, two bucks. Two sticks of butter, four bucks. Three eggs, three dollars. Cup milk, two bucks. Vanilla, two bucks. Frosting supplies maybe five. Pan depends, but five dollars max. So the cake stuff is probably around twenty to thirty bucks total.

Adding it up, drywall could be twenty to fifty, cake around thirty. So a good ballpark would be fifty to eighty dollars for both tasks. You might want to bring a hundred just to be safe for any extras. Let me know if you want a more precise breakdown at the store.
