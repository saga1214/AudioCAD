Based on your list, **engaging content** is the single most important factor for TikTok success. Here's why:

1.  **Foundation for Everything Else:** Without engaging content, none of the other factors matter. People won't watch, like, comment, or share if the video isn't compelling *first*.
2.  **Algorithm Fuel:** TikTok's algorithm (For You Page) primarily rewards videos that **keep viewers watching**. Engaging content (great hook, relatable humor, satisfying payoff, emotional connection) directly increases watch time, completion rates, and interaction – the key signals the algorithm uses to push your video to more people.
3.  **Overcomes Initial Barriers:** While "following guidelines" is crucial to *avoid* penalties, engaging content is what *compels* the algorithm to show your video to the right audience in the first place. Good production quality (lighting/audio) and conciseness support engagement but aren't the core driver.
4.  **Drives Organic Reach:** Hashtags and descriptions help with discovery *within* the app, but they can't make an unengaging video go viral. High engagement is what gets your video pushed *wider* organically.
5.  **Builds Community:** Engaging content fosters connection and loyalty, turning viewers into followers who actively engage with your future posts.

**Why the others, while important, are secondary to engagement:**

*   **Following Guidelines:** **Essential prerequisite.** If your video is restricted or removed, it *cannot* engage anyone. But once compliant, engagement is the primary driver of growth.
*   **Conciseness:** Highly important for modern TikTok (especially for virality), as it often leads to higher completion rates. However, conciseness alone isn't enough; the content *within* those seconds must be engaging.
*   **Consistency:** Crucial for building an audience and algorithm trust over time. Posting regularly helps, but each individual post *must* be engaging to gain traction and grow.
*   **Description:** Hashtags and context in the description are useful for initial discovery and SEO within the app, but they have a very limited impact compared to the core content's ability to captivate viewers.

**In short:** You need to **follow guidelines** to avoid bans, but you need **engaging content** to actually make the algorithm *want* to show your video to millions. Focus relentlessly on creating content that hooks viewers instantly and holds their attention.
