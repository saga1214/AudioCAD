The whole left brain versus right brain idea is, for the most part, a myth. The brain is far more interconnected than that. While some areas are specialized for certain tasks—like the left hemisphere often handling language and the right handling spatial tasks—they all work together. Creativity and logic aren't truly separate; they're just different modes of thinking that can coexist. 

Now, for your career, it sounds like design fields could be a really good middle ground for you. User experience design, for example, involves measuring user behavior (concrete answers) while being highly creative to make those structures accessible and lasting. Another idea could be technical illustration or data art, where you can measure and structure data and then paint or visualize it in a creative way.

Regarding tinnitus again, unless there’s a history of trauma that specifically targeted the auditory system, it almost always boils down to physical causes related to your ears or hearing.
