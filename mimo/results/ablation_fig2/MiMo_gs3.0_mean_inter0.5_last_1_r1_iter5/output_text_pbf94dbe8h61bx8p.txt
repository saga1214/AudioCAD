2. **Santa Globe Alternative (Kid-Friendly Version)**:

   * Kids glue on pre-cut felt hats/beards/feet using **cool-glue guns** (safer!).
   * You handle the hot glue gun assembly.
   * **Meaningful & Universal**: Everyone loves candy, and it's easy to personalize.

3. **Kid Involved Cookie Projects**:
   * **No-Roll Sugar Cookies**: Use a simple recipe and pre-cut dough rounds. Kids scoop, decorate, and bake.
   * **Cookie Decorating Station**: Bake plain sugar cookies ahead. Kids decorate with icing and sprinkles.
   * **Cookie Tracing**: Bake sugar cookies, let kids trace their hand/foot/handwriting on them while warm.

### Simple & Meaningful Gifts (Low Effort, High Heart)
* **Fingerprint Holiday Cards**: Kids dip fingers in paint to create snowflakes, trees, reindeer. You assemble into cards.
* **Pinecone Bird Feeders**: Kids roll pinecones in peanut butter, then coat in birdseed. Tie with string. *Peanut butter stays put outdoors!* (Great for grandparents/teachers).
* **Salt Dough Ornaments**: Kids shape ornaments (snowmen, trees), you bake and paint. Simple, no glue needed.
* **Personalized Storybooks**: Use free templates online (e.g., "The Very Hungry Caterpillar" style), kids illustrate, you write the story.

### Pet Food Strategy
Stick with Walmart for routine food/treats. For special gifts:
* **Chewy Autoship**: Often has discounts on premium brands.
* **Local Pet Supplies Plus**: Can have competitive prices on specialty items.
* **Costco (if membership)**: Great for bulk pet food bags/treats.
* **Farm & Ranch Stores**: Sometimes have excellent prices on bulk pet food.
