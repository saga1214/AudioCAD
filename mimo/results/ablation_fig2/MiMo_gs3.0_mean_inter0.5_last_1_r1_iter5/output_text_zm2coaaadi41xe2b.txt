Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! Here are a few recommendations: 

First, there's Wonton Noodle House in Little Elm. It has a huge selection of Chinese noodles, like Chow Mein and Yangzhou Fried Rice, and the flavors are bold and authentic.

Second, there's Sakura Ramen in Plano. It specializes in Japanese-style ramen, and their miso and tonkotsu options are amazing.

Third, you could try Pho 79 in Richardson. They have fantastic Vietnamese pho, which is a noodle dish, and their broth is rich and flavorful.

For protein bars, I'd recommend checking out Quest Nutrition, they have high protein and taste good. Or Optimum Nutrition, which is a classic protein bar brand. If you prefer something chewier, Clif Bars or RXBAR are also great options.
