Yes, you can absolutely submit a fully illustrated manuscript. In fact, publishers often prefer it when an author can provide their own artwork, especially for a children's book. The process is just a bit different.

First, you'll need to create a complete sample, which is usually about 12 to 16 pages, to show how the book will look. You don't need a full story, just a good sample of your art and writing together.

Second, you'll need to format this sample properly. It should be in a standard file format like PDF or JPEG, and it needs to be laid out just as the book would be printed. This means you'll need to include things like a title page, a copyright page, and page numbers. You can find detailed guides for this on websites like the Society of Children's Book Writers and Illustrators, or SCBWI, which has a lot of free resources.
