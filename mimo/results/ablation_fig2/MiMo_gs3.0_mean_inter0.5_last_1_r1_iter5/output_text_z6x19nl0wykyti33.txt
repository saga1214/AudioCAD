Okay, so you've got blonde hair and jeans in your closet, and you want to avoid dark-haired characters or a full uniform. Let's think outside the box.

One idea could be to dress as a Hufflepuff student. You could repurpose your jeans—maybe add a wizard robe over them, or even a scarf with a house crest. It’s casual but still fits the theme.

If you’re open to a bit of DIY, you could try to make a Beauxbatons student look. You could use a blue scarf, a beret, and maybe even a pair of boots to mimic the French vibe. It’s stylish but not too hard to pull off.

Another fun idea is to be a magical creature in disguise, like a house-elf. You could pair your jeans with a nice shirt and maybe a brooch or a hat to hint at the creature theme.
