You could look for options beyond ferries. For a romantic getaway, a private sunset sail would be wonderful.

If seafood is a priority, you could see if your accommodation offers a private dock where you can have a chef prepare a fresh catch right on the water. Or, you could look for a charter boat that offers deep-sea fishing, which you could turn into a fun challenge.

Another idea is a tranquil kayak tour along a calm, protected coastline, which is a much quieter way to be on the water.
