For candy tomorrow, grab classics like Reese's Cups, Hershey's Kisses, and maybe some gummies or candy corn. You could also stock up on mini chocolate bars or sour candies if you think guests will like that. Avoid anything too niche or hard to eat—stick to what everyone knows and loves. If you want to be extra spooky, you can hand it out in little black bags or on paper bats for a fun touch. Pro tip: check the expiration dates and don’t buy anything with artificial dyes due to allergies we talked about earlier!
