Now, to your question about whether the eye sees continuously. It’s a bit of a paradox. The eye itself isn’t continuously seeing in the way a camera records every frame. Instead, it captures visual information in bursts during saccades, and the brain fills in the gaps to create the perception of continuous vision.

So, no, the eye isn’t continuously recording. But the brain processes and integrates the information from these quick jumps, making us perceive a seamless, continuous stream of motion. It’s the brain that makes it feel continuous, not the eye itself.
