Okay, so you and girlfriend drive down to Joshua Tree, then into Nevada, maybe camp on BLM land for free, and then head to Vegas for a proper wind-down. That's a solid plan. For a Vegas experience, it really depends on what you two want—glamour, food, gaming, or something more low-key. You could hit up a classic casino for the vibe, but maybe skip the expensive shows unless that’s your jam. A nice dinner in a good neighborhood like The Strip or off it could be a great date. Then, a fancy cocktail or two at a rooftop bar with a view of the city lights. The key is balancing the adventure with a bit of luxury after a long drive.
