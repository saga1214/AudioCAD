Here's a balanced 11-day Japan itinerary focused on Japanese art history, designed to fit your holiday schedule, coursework needs, and fitness routine:

### **Tokyo Phase (5 Days)**
**Day 1-2: Modern Art & Urban Design**  
- **Morning:** Asakusa (Senso-ji Temple: study Buddhist sculpture, traditional layout)  
- **Afternoon:** Ueno Park (Tokyo Museum/Art Museum: ukiyo-e woodblock prints, kimono textiles)  
- **Design Research:** Capture street photography (Shibuya Crossing, Shinjuku neon signs) for color sk-eme/font inspiration  
- **Evening:** Dinner in Odaiba (modern architecture visuals)  

**Day 3: Heian Era Courtly Art**  
- **Morning:** Imperial Palace East Gardens (ancient layout principles)  
- **Afternoon:** Kyoto-bound Shinkansen (3.5 hrs)  
- **Design Tip:** Note minimalist composition in traditional gardens  

**Day 4: Kyoto/Nara Deep Dive**  
- **Morning:** Nara Todai-ji Temple (Great Buddha sculpture: study stylized forms)  
- **Afternoon:** Kyoto Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion: gold leaf color scheme, Zen garden layout)  
- **Design Research:** Photograph rock garden asymmetry for slide fonts  

**Day 5: Contemporary Art**  
- **Morning:** Modern Woodworking Museum (traditional carpentry design principles)  
- **Afternoon:** Ghibli Museum (Spirited Away: nature balance/color in animation)  
- **Evening:** Return to Tokyo  

### **Osaka Phase (3 Days) Hiroshige/Hokusai Focus**
**Day 6: Ukiyo-e Heritage**  
- **Morning:** Osaka Castle Park (historical layout/color: vermilion walls, greenery)  
- **Afternoon:** Kuromon Market (kimono pattern research for images)  
- **Design Task:** Sketch Hokusai’s *The Great Wave* color palette for slides  

**Day 7: Calligraphy & Ceramics**  
- **Morning:** Kyoto Pottery Workshop (ceramic texture as visual element)  
- **Afternoon:** Kyoto Museum of Calligraphy (font inspiration: brushstroke techniques)  
- **Design Tip:** Incorporate ink wash gradients in slide transitions  

**Day 8: Departure**  
- **Morning:** Last-minute slide refinement (use photos from Day 6/7)  
- **Design Checklist:**  
  - Images: Todai-ji Buddha, kimono textiles, ukiyo-e prints  
  literacy content: stylistic evolution (Heian → Edo → modern)  
  - Design Aspects:  
    - Layout: Asymmetrical garden compositions  
    - Color Scheme: Indigo/black calligraphy palette + vermilion accents  
    - Font: Traditional kanji block fonts with modern sans-serif contrast  

### **Workout Integration**
- **Tokyo:** 6:00-7:00 AM hotel gym (Mon-Fri)  
- **Kyoto/Osaka:** 6:00-7:00 AM park runs/jogs (Mon-Sun)  
- **On-train time:** Listen to art podcasts or note-cataloging  

### **Presentation Design Strategy**
1. **Cover Slide:** Ukiyo-e-inspired layout with woodblock texture background  
2. **Content Flow:** Chronological art periods → thematic sections (nature balance, minimalism)  
3. **Visuals:** High-res images with consistent color filters (e.g., sepia for historical art)
