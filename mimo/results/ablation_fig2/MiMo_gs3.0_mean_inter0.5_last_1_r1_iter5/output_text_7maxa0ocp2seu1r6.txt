### **III. Client-Specific Advice (For Your 6'4" Neighbor)**
1. **Comfort Sitting:**  
   - **Back Rise** must be longer than front rise (to prevent sagging).  
   - Add **ease** (extra room) to **hips** and **thigh** (aim for 2–4" above standard).  

2. **Pant Length (Inseam = 37"):**  
   - **Shorten the inseam slightly** (likely to 34–35").  
   - **Always measure with the shoes he plans to wear** (e.g., sneakers) to pin the hem at the desired length (tops of shoes).  

---

### Key Tips:
- **"Ease"** = extra fabric room (e.g., 1–2" for hips/thigh on standard sizes, more for comfort).  
- **Always measure with shoes on** for length accuracy.  
- **Double-check rise measurements**—back rise is often 0.5–1" longer than front for tall clients.
