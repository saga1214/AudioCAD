Here are precise, professional recipes for your event—all classic and theme-aligned:

### **1. Old Fashioned**  
*(Serves 1)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon (or rye)  
- 1 sugar cube, or ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  
**Method:**  
1. Muddle sugar cube with ½ oz water in a shaker.  
2. Add bourbon, bitters, ice. Stir 20–30 seconds until chilled.  
3. Strain into a **rocks glass over fresh ice**.  
4. Express orange twist over drink, discard peel.  

---

### **2. Paper Plane**  
*(Serves 1)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz Aperol  
- ¾ oz Amaro Nonino  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
**Method:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice for 12–15 seconds.  
2. Strain into a **coupe glass** packed with ice.  
3. Garnish with cherry or lemon twist.  

---

### **3. Recommended Gin Cocktail: French 75**  
*(Serves 1)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz gin  
- ¾ oz lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 3 oz chilled Champagne or prosecco  
**Method:**  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a **chilled coupe glass**.  
3. Top with Champagne. Garnish with lemon twist.  

---

### **4. Whiskey Sour**  
*(Served with two options)*  
**A. With Egg White (Classic):**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 1 egg white  
**Method:**  
1. **Dry shake** (egg white + all ingredients) for 15 seconds.  
2. Add ice, shake again for 10 seconds.  
3. Strain into a rocks glass over ice. Garnish with cherry.  

**B. Without Egg White (Simpler):**  
Same ingredients as above, but:  
1. Shake with ice, strain, serve over ice. Garnish with cherry.

### **Pro Tips for Bartenders:**  
- **Batch Prep:** Muddle sugar cubes for Old Fashioneds ahead.  
- **Garnish Consistency:** Use Angostura bitters liberally for depth.  
- **Gin Note:** Nonino should be slightly bitter to balance Aperol’s citrus.  
- **Egg White Hygiene:** Use pasteurized eggs if concerned.
