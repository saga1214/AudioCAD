Okay, so you've already done the degreasing, light sanding, and disassembly. That's great progress! 

Before you put on that primer, the only other thing you need to do is make sure the cabinets are completely dry after sanding. Any moisture left will ruin the primer.

And one last tip: make sure you cover up the areas you don't want to get painted, like the countertops and walls. That way, you don't have to worry about any drips.
