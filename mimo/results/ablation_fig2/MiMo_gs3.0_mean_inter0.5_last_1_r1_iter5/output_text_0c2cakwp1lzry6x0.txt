Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items while respecting your apartment's rules (no late parties/loud drilling/drilling holes):

### 1. **TV Mounting**
   - ** daytime Prep (No Noise)**  
     Measure wall space during quiet hours ( landlord's rules). Use a stud finder (many models have noise sensors). Avoid drilling on weekends unless using a drill-less mount.
   - **Drill-Free Option**:  
     Use a **TV wall mount with adhesive strips** (like Command VOLSSTR). Follow the brand’s weight guidelines (e.g., for 55" TVs). Test adhesion on a wall section first.
   - **If Drilling is Needed**:  
     Use a power drill *during quiet hours only*. Wear ear protection. Anchor into studs (not drywall alone) for safety. Hide cables with in-wall cable management kits.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **No-Drill Solution**:  
     Use **hanging hooks or ceiling straps** (e.g., S-hooks rated for 50+ lbs). Attach to ceiling joists (found near windows/doors). Use adhesive hooks for lightweight plants (check weight limits).
   - **Safety Check**:  
     Ensure ceiling can support weight. For heavy plants, use multiple straps and a ceiling-rated hanging basket.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Drill-Free Option**:  
     Use **heavy-duty adhesive anchors** (like 3M Command Strips). Follow weight guidelines (e.g., 5–10 lbs per shelf). Test adhesion on a wall section first.
   - **If Drilling is Needed**:  
     Use **toggle bolts** or **plastic anchors** (for drywall). Install during quiet hours. Space shelves 12–18" apart for stability. Avoid load-bearing walls.

### Key Tips:
- **Avoid Noise**: All drilling/drilling must be done during quiet hours (typically 10 AM–8 PM).
- **Time Management**: Dedicate 1–2 Sunday hours per project. Work on one item per day to avoid fatigue.
- **Safety**: Always use a level, secure ladder, and safety goggles. Double-check weight limits.
- **Tools**: Use cordless drills, level, tape measure, and stud finder. Keep decorations/tools separate during work hours.

- **9:00–10:00 AM**: Install TV mount (drill during quiet hours).
- **10:30–11:30 AM**: Hang planters (use hooks, no drilling needed).
