Alright, let's recap all the homemade and store-bought ideas we've covered for your little Corgi princess, Princess. Here's a full list.

For homemade treats, we had frozen bites like pumpkin or sweet potato mashes in a muffin tin, peanut butter and oat cookies (double-checking on that xylitol!), simple cooked chicken pieces, and carrot sticks.

For store-bought brands worth looking into, there's Blue Buffalo, Wellness, Zuke's, Greenies, and Merrick. Always check the ingredient list for them.

Teaching "sit" involves using a treat to guide her head up and her rear down, saying the command word, and rewarding the position immediately.

For potty training, the main points are consistency, taking her out at regular times, rewarding success, feeding on a schedule, and cleaning up accidents without punishment.

And for her collar, colors like teal, turquoise, soft pink, or royal blue are great choices that will stand out against her brown and white coat.

Enjoy snacking, training, and styling your princess!
