Cooking could backfire given dietary issues again. Lactose intolerance alone limits options. You'd have to find something she can eat that's also simple and quick. Maybe a non-dairy stir-fry, a grilled dish, or a simple dessert that avoids dairy like fruit-based things. Keep it short, maybe just 30 minutes, so it’s more of a distraction than a meal. The goal is to have a shared task to avoid direct interaction pressure. Don't make it a big production.
