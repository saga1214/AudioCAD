You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is great, so gluten-free alternatives are covered.

Consider packing gluten-free trail mix, which you can make yourself with nuts, seeds, and gluten-free dried fruits like cranberries.

Popcorn is another gluten-free camping snack that is easy to make on a camp stove with microwave popcorn kernels or by popping them in a pot.
