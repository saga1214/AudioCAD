Of course, let's break down the appliances and cooking methods. For smokeable dishes, you need a smoker. For roasted meat and roasting veggies, a main oven. For picky kid food like mashed potatoes and mac and cheese, you'll also need that same oven. For stuffing, you'll need another oven or you could use the air fryer. In the toaster oven, you'll roast the asparagus. For the Instant Pot, you'll make the cranberry sauce. For no-bake desserts, you won't need the oven at all. You'll need two double ovens for the turkey and prime rib, plus stovetop burners for things like the fruit tart. So, the appliances you'll need are at least two ovens, a smoker, a toaster oven, an Instant Pot, and a stovetop. The cooking methods are smoking, roasting, baking, stovetop cooking, and no-baking.
