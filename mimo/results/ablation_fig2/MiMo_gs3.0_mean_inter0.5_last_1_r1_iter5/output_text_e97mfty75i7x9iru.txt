Take it easy. "Feed a cold/starve the fever" is really an archaic concept about food and fluids in different illnesses. The flu is a virus, antibiotics don't work, and your doctor correctly withheld them. The core advice is always to rest, drink fluids, and eat what your body will allow.

For a cough specifically:

First, a spoonful of honey can soothe it.

Second, gargling with warm salt water is another option.

And finally, elevating your head with an extra pillow at night can help with coughing while you sleep.

I hope that helps with the cough and that you start feeling better soon.
