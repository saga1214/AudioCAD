Okay, diesel camper vans age, and maintenance is important before a big trip. Brakes are critical—signs of wear include squealing, grinding noises, or a soft pedal feel. You can check the brake pads by lifting the van and looking at the discs; if they're worn down, you might see a wear indicator that's thin. For spark plugs, diesel engines use high-compression, so they're a bit different. You'll need to remove the diesel injectors first, which involves a lot of work under the hood. It's generally recommended to have a mechanic handle that if you're not comfortable. But for brakes, you can do a basic inspection yourself and take it to a shop for a full check. It’s better to be safe than sorry.
