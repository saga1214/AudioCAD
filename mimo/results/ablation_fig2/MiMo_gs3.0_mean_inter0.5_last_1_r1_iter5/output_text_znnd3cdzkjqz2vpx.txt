You've got all the essentials down, but there are a few more things that will make the job go much more smoothly.

First, I'd highly recommend renting a cordless power drill with a couple of drill bits. Trying to hammer screws into cabinets is going to be really slow and tiring. And shims will still be necessary underneath the base cabinets to get them perfectly level.

Also, think about safety. You'll want a sturdy pair of work gloves to protect your hands from splinters and sharp edges. And it's a good idea to have safety glasses on too, just in case any screws happen to slip.

For tools, a measuring tape is super important for making sure everything lines up correctly. You might also find a small utility knife useful for trimming any trim pieces that are too long. Oh, and a stud finder with an electronic beep makes finding those wall studs a lot easier.

You're well on your way, and with your friends' help, you'll have a brand new Habitat cabinets!
