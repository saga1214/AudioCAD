Of course, I can clarify that. Chronic skin redness can definitely have many causes besides vasculitis. Rosacea is a very common one, sun damage over decades, and genetics are frequent factors. It can also be a reaction to things like medications or environmental irritants.

As for vasculitis, it's a condition where the walls of the blood vessels become inflamed. The common symptoms are things like fever, fatigue, aches, and rashes. It happens because the body's immune system mistakenly attacks the blood vessels, causing the inflammation.

This inflammation can affect the vessels of any size, which is why the symptoms can vary so much depending on where the inflammation is. Seeing a doctor is really the best way to get a clear diagnosis and the right treatment plan.
