Here's a streamlined clothing list split into **daytime (20 miles)** and **nighttime (final 12 miles)**, optimized for packability, warmth, and trail conditions. Since there are three aid stations, hard items like nutrition are optional—focus on layers you can adjust on the go.

### **Daytime Segment (20 miles, mid-30s, hilly)**
Bring minimal but versatile gear. You can rely on aid stations for extra layers if needed.
1. **Base Layer**:  
   - Moisture-wicking top and bottom (synthetic or merino wool). *Keeps sweat off skin and dries fast.*  
2. **Mid-Layer**:  
   - Fleece jacket or light puffy vest. *Adds warmth without bulk.*  
3. **Outer Layer**:  
   - Wind-resistant/water-repellent shell jacket. *Essential for cool, variable weather.*  
4. **Bottoms**:  
   - Quick-dry trail shorts or pants (convertible pants are great). *Avoid cotton.*  
5. **Footwear**:  
   - Trail running shoes with aggressive grip (e.g., Vibram soles). *Crucial for hills.*  
6. **Extras**:  
   - Gloves (lightweight, wind-resistant).  
   - Cap or hat (for sun protection).  
   - Small pack or vest (to carry layers if needed).  

---

### **Final Night Segment (12 miles)**
Prioritize warmth, visibility, and battery life. Aid stations won slow you down—bring extras.
1. Insulated Jacket:  
   - Down or synthetic puffy (rated 0–10°C/32–50°F). *Essential for nighttime chill.*  
2. Headlamp/Handheld Light:  
   - With **extra batteries**. *Non-negotiable for trail safety.*  
3. Warm Hat:  
   - Windproof with ear coverage (e.g., balaclava or fleece).  
4. Gloves:  
   - Windproof/waterproof (keeps hands warm and dry).  
5. Mid-Layer:  
   - Fleece or lightweight puffy (add under your insulated jacket).  
6. Battery Pack (Optional):  
   - If your phone/headlamp needs power.  

---

### **Key Tips**  
- **Layer System**: Wear base + mid + outer in daytime. Swap mid-layer/jacket for nighttime.  
- **Aid Station Strategy**: Drop lightweight items (cap, hat) at the first station if you overheat.  
- **Avoid Cotton**: It retains moisture and chills you fast.  
- **Test Gear**: Try your layers at home to avoid surprises.  

This setup keeps you adaptable, warm, and efficient. Pace strong! 🏃‍♀️💨
