Here's a practical solution for your coffee quest in the rain:

1.  **Wear a good rain jacket with a hood.** This is the most reliable option. It protects you from the rain and wind, and the hood provides overhead coverage.
2.  **Bring an umbrella (a compact, wind-resistant one if possible).** Hold it securely *under your arm*, not over your head, to shield your path and prevent it from flipping inside out in the wind.
3.  **Protect your coffee:** If you make the coffee at home, pour it into a thermal mug or a heatproof travel cup. This will keep it hot longer in the rain and make carrying it easier.
4.  **Carry your shopping bag securely:** Use a backpack with a rain cover or a sturdy bag that can be tucked under your arm alongside the umbrella.
5.  **Consider timing:** If possible, time your trip to avoid the absolute heaviest downpour, or if you know the store is very close, brave it quickly.

**For your specific strong latte:**

*   Remember the instant coffee hack: Dissolve the instant coffee in a small amount of *very* hot water first to create your strong concentrate. *Then* add your pre-warmed milk (warmed separately in the microwave) and any sugar.

**About Cornish milk:**

*   **Yes, it's worth it!** Its exceptional richness and buttery flavor will make your latte incredibly luxurious. The price reflects its premium quality and scarcity.
*   **Check the label:** Ensure it's "Full Fat Cornish Milk" or "Premium Cornish Milk." Some might have added vitamins, but the standard full-fat is the creamiest.
