For high-performance American cars under $90K with modern features CarPlay-ready under five years old, focusing on V8, V10, or V12 engines:

### 1. **Chevrolet Corvette (C8 Z06 or Stingray)**  
   - **Engine**: 5.5L V8 (Z06) or 6.2L V8 (Stingray)  
   - **Performance**: 670+ HP (Z06), 0-60 mph in ~3 seconds  
   - **Tech**: Full CarPlay/Android Auto, performance data analytics  
   - **Budget**: ~ eighty thousand dollars (new)  

### 2. **Audi R8 V10 Plus**  
   - **Engine**: 5.2L V10 (592 HP)  
   - **Performance**: Mid-engine layout, 0-60 mph in ~3.3 seconds  
   - **Reliability**: V10 praised for durability (e.g., 2019–2023 models CPO)  
   - **Budget**: ~ eighty-five thousand dollars (used premium model)  

### 3. **Ford Mustang Shelby GT500**  
   - **Engine**: 5.2L V8 (660 HP)  
   - **Performance**: Track-capable, 0-60 mph in ~3.3 seconds  
   - **Tech**: Wireless CarPlay, advanced driver aids  
   - **Budget**: ~ eighty-five thousand dollars (new)  

### 4. **Mercedes-AMG GT (V8)**  
   - **Engine**: 4.0L V8 (639 HP)  
   - **Performance**: RWD, rear-mid layout, 0-60 mph in ~3.6 seconds  
   - **Tech**: Full smartphone integration  
   - **Budget**: ~ eighty thousand dollars (used C63 AMG)  

### 5. **BMW M8 Competition (V8)**  
   - **Engine**: 4.4L V8 (645 HP)
