Here's a concise checklist for your self-recorded puppet show:

### **Pre-Recording Setup**
1. **Permission**  
   - Confirm with venue (e.g., Sunnyside Assisted Living) if recording is allowed.  
   - For kids/hospitals: Emphasize privacy—no faces of minors visible.

2. **Gear**  
   - **Camera**: Use your phone in 1080p+ video mode (front camera for close-ups).  
   - **Stability**: Tripod or rest phone on a book/box to prevent shaky footage.  
   - **Audio**: Attach a lapel mic to your shirt or use a small external mic to avoid sock-puppet squeaks drowning your voice.  
   - **Lighting**: Position near a window or use a small flashlight to avoid shadows.

3. **Setup**  
   - **Angle**: Shoot from slightly above eye level (point phone at puppet’s head/shoulders).  
   - **Test Run**: Record a 1-minute rehearsal to check focus, audio, and framing.

### **During the Performance**
- **Engagement**: Maintain eye contact with the camera (kids subconsciously mirror this).  
- **Puppet Handling**: Keep movements fluid—avoid excessive arm waving that distracts from the show.  
- **Script Flexibility**: Stick to your written outline but allow improv for natural flow.  
- **Props**: Use lightweight props (e.g., cardboard cutouts) that don’t block the camera.

### **Post-Recording**
1. **Editing**  
   - Trim awkward pauses or mistakes (use free apps like CapCut or iMovie).  
   - Add subtitles for silent moments or background noise.  
   - Enhance audio: Reduce echo, boost voice clarity.  
   - Branding: Add your name/logo at the start/end.

2. **Platform Optimization**  
   - **YouTube**: Set as "kid-friendly" (no copyrighted music).  
   - **TikTok/Shorts**: Clip 15-60 second highlights with catchy captions.  
   - **Consistency**: Post weekly (e.g., "Puppet Melvin’s Magic Monday").

3. **Copyright Protection**  
   - Upload your show to a platform like **YouTube Studio**—it automatically registers your copyright.  
   - For characters: Trademark names (e.g., "Melvin the Magnificent™") if expanding commercially.

### **Ethical Considerations**
- **Anonymize Kids**: Blur faces or use silhouettes if minors appear.  
- **Credit Venues**: Tag hospitals/schools in descriptions (e.g., "Sunnyside Assisted Living provided this space!").

### **Monetization (If You Choose)**
- Use **ad revenue-sharing** (YouTube) or **donations** (Patreon/Ko-fi) instead of letting others profit.  
- Partner with kid-focused charities for sponsored content (e.g., "Sponsored by [Local Toy Store]").

**Pro Tip**: Start small—record 3-5 shows, edit them, and share with friends for feedback. Momentum builds naturally! 🎭
