Okay, since you love oceans and seafood, here are a few water-based ideas.

One option is a sunset sail. Many charters offer shorter evening trips just to see the sunset. You could bring a nice bottle of wine.

Another idea, if you find a place with clear water, is kayaking together. You could stop at a small sandbar for a picnic with some fresh catch.

If you're staying somewhere with a marina, you could take a fun cooking class where you catch your own seafood and then cook it right there.
