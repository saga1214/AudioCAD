Here's a detailed bartender-ready list with classic recipes for all the cocktails you're hosting, including classic garnishes:

### 1. **Old Fashioned**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey  
- 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup (use cube for authenticity)  
- 3–4 dashes Angostura buddies  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  
*Instructions:*  
1. Muddle sugar cube (with a splash of water) in a mixing glass OR stir sugar cube/syrup with bitters in an empty glass.  
2. Add bourbon and ice. Stir well (30 sec) to chill and dilute.  
3. Strain into a **rocks glass over fresh ice**.  
4. Express orange twist over drink (squeeze oils into foam), wipe rim, and garnish.  

---

### 2. **Paper Plane**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz Aperol  
- ¾ oz Nonino Amaro (Amaro Nonino)  
-¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
*Instructions:*  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a **coupe glass** (chilled).  

---

### 3. **Whiskey Sour**  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz bourbon  
-¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
-½ oz simple syrup  
*Optional:* ½ egg white (for texture)  
*Instructions:*  
1. If using egg white:  
   - *Dry shake* (no ice) all ingredients + egg white in a shaker.  
   - Add ice, shake again hard.  
2. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.  
3. Garnish with a **cherry OR orange slice**.  

---

### 4. Aviation  
*Ingredients:*  
- 2 oz gin  
-¾ oz maraschino liqueur (Luxardo)  
-¼ oz crème de violette  
-¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
*Instructions:*  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a **cocktail glass ( coupe)**.  
3. Express a **cherry garnish** over the drink.  

---

### 5. **Recommended Additional Gin Cocktail: French 75**  
*(Art-deco elegance + Kentucky-friendly)*  
*Ingredients:*  
- 1½ oz gin  
-¾ oz lemon juice  
-½ oz simple syrup  
- 3 oz chilled champagne/ prosecco  
*Instructions:*  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a **chilled flute**.  
3. Top with champagne.  
4. **Garnish with lemon twist**.  

---

### Key Tips for Your Team:  
- **Kentucky Twist:** Use high-quality bourbon (e.g., Maker’s Mark, Buffalo Trace) for fall authenticity.  
- **Paper Planes:** Emphasize equal parts— guests often eyeball this and dilute it.  
- **Egg White:** Train bartenders to dry shake egg white sours to avoid watery texture. Anguista or pasteurized eggs are safer for events.  
- **Garnish Consistency:** Twist citrus oils over drinks (Old Fashioned/Aviation) and use cherries for fruit-forward sours.
