This is a tough choice since each source has its strengths. Solar has come down in cost and is scalable. Wind is strong in windy places. Hydropower is reliable and efficient. If I absolutely had to pick just one overall, I'd still go with solar energy. The main reasons are its wide availability, the continuous technological improvements lowering costs, and the fact that it can be implemented on a very small scale, even in people's homes. So, while the others are very important, solar energy seems to have the broadest overall efficiency based on all those factors.
