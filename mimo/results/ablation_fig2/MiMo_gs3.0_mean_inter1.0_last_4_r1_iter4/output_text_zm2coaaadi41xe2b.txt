Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! If you're craving Asian noodles, I'd recommend checking out these places:

First, there's Yum Yum Noodle House in Little Elm. They have a wide variety of Asian noodles, like Pad Thai and Chow Mein, at good prices.

Another great option is The Rice Boat in Plano. It's a classic spot for authentic Chinese noodles, and their wonton noodles are amazing.

If you want something more upscale, there's Sushi Noodle House in Richardson, which serves high-quality sushi and ramen.

And if you're in the mood for something more casual, there's Pho 79 in Richardson, which serves delicious Vietnamese noodle soup.

For protein bars, I'd recommend checking out these brands:

First, there's Quest Bars. They have high protein, low sugar, and come in many flavors.

Next is Optimum Nutrition. Their protein bars are a classic choice and taste like candy.

Finally, there's RXBAR, which uses simple ingredients and focuses on whole-food protein.

Hope you enjoy your meal!
