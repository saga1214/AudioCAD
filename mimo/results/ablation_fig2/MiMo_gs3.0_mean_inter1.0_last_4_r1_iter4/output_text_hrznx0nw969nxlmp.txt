If you're staying at Secret Harbour Beach Resort on St. Thomas, you're in a perfect spot for easy, pressure-free daytime fun right in Cruz Bay. Here are some great options:

1.  **Snorkel Right Off the Bay:** This calm, protected bay means you can literally float a few feet from shore and see vibrant coral and fish. Grab a snorkel gear rental nearby and explore the underwater world safely.
2.  **Explore Cruz Bay:** Wander the charming main street just a short walk from your resort. Browse local shops selling crafts, spices, souvenirs, and books. Grab fresh fruit from a stand.
3.  **Visit Cruz Bay Beach:** Just steps from Secret Harbour, this public beach is beautiful and easy to access. Great for sunbathing, swimming, or just relaxing on the sand.
4.  **Visit Coral World Ocean Park (St. Thomas):** A fantastic, enclosed marine park with a huge aquarium, touch pools, and underwater tunnels. It's climate-controlled, so no pressure changes, and offers incredible marine life viewing. (On St. John, Cinnamon Bay has a small nature trail perfect for birdwatching).
5.  **Hike MVR Nature Park Trails:** Several short, easy trails start near Cruz Bay. You can walk to Salt River Bay, which leads to beautiful beaches and potential wildlife sightings (turtles, birds). St. John also has the Reef Bay Trail on Virgin Bay.
6.  **Rent Bikes:** Cruise along the scenic coastal roads heading towards the West End of St. Thomas. Stop at small beaches or viewpoints along the way.
7.  **Visit the Johnsy's Cafe & Gallery:** A lovely spot right near Gallows Point Resort (on St. John) or similar areas over here, combining a great cafe with local art.

The combination of Secret Harbour's beach access, the walkable Cruz Bay shops, and the easy beach/hike access to Coral World makes it an ideal base for your week! Enjoy the Virgin Islands!
