Great question! Here are the three shrimp main courses I suggested, with ingredients lists included:

### 1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled & deveined  
- 8 oz spaghetti or linguine  
- 4 tbsp butter  
- 4 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped  
- 1/4 cup dry white wine (optional)  
- 1/4 cup lemon juice  
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat)  
- Salt & pepper to taste  

### 2. **Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled & deveined  
- 1 onion, diced  
- 2 bell peppers, diced  
- 3 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes  
- 1/2 cup dry white wine  
- 1/2 cup heavy cream  
- 2 tbsp red pepper flakes  
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese  
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning  

### 3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled & deveined  
- 1 cup quick-cooking grits (or regular grits cooked in 3 cups water)  
- 1/2 cup chicken broth  
- 1/4 cup diced onion  
- 2 green onions, sliced  
- 1/4 cup chopped celery  
- 1/4 cup chopped bell pepper  
- 1/2 tsp Cajun seasoning (like Tony Chachere’s)  
- 2 tbsp butter  
- 2 tbsp chopped green onions  

**Tips for Serving**:  
- Pair any shrimp dish with a simple side salad or extra rice/pasta to make it more substantial for a family meal.  
- Pre-cook the shrimp and veggies, then finish cooking in the sauce right before serving.  
- Label the dish with the name and cooking instructions!
