Stir-fries usually cook at a much higher heat than boiling pasta, so wood hitting the pot isn't likely. Here's the key to great noodles: always use metal tongs instead of spoons in boiling water.

Also, stir your noodles constantly. You want to keep them moving so they don't stick together. Before draining, make sure to rinse them briefly with cold water. This will make them less sticky and help them absorb sauce later.
