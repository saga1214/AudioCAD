Here’s a concise summary of the solutions provided for your fussy feline feeding and poverty challenges:

### Feeding Solutions:
1. **Controlled Access Feeders:** Use microchip-activated or timed feeders so fussy cats access food when hungry, gluttons eat measured portions over time.
2. **Environment Management:** Isolate fussy cats temporarily/room-wise during feeding before work starts.
3. **Portion Control:** Break larger meals into timed small servings released by timed feeders.

### Cost-Saving Health Strategies:
1. **Seek Free/Low-Cost Resources:**  
   - Contact local pet food banks, shelters, or charities for emergency food aid.  
   - Join community groups for pet supply swaps or donations.
2. **Smart Food Budgeting:**  
   - Buy quality food in bulk (check unit prices).  
   - Supplement with safe, inexpensive ingredients (e.g., plain cooked chicken/fish, rice) when possible.
3. **Preventive Care:**  
   - Prioritize low-cost essential services: spaying/neutering, vaccinations, parasite prevention.  
   - Maintain hygiene: Clean food/water bowls daily.
4. **At-Home Wellness:**  
   - Monitor weight to prevent obesity-linked diseases.  
   - Groom regularly to reduce skin issues/hairballs.  
   - Use DIY toys (string, crumpled paper) for exercise and mental stimulation.
5. **Proactive Health Monitoring:**  
   - Track minor changes in appetite, litter habits, or behavior early.  
   - Save vet contacts for emergencies; even cheap clinics need follow-up funds.

**Key Balance:** Controlled feeding prevents inequality while portion control avoids overeating. Stretching food budgets requires strategic purchasing and safe supplements. Prioritizing essential vet care prevents costly emergencies. Regular observation turns "worthless" checks into actionable early warnings.
