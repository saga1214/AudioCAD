Yes, you can absolutely submit a self-published book with your own illustrations. This is called a 'hybrid' book, where you're the illustrator, not just the author. This is a great option if you have the skills.

When you submit to a publisher, you'll typically send them the manuscript, your illustrations, and a professional proposal. The proposal will include an introduction to the book, a list of sample pages, and a summary of your work.

If a publisher is interested, they might buy your book as-is, or they might ask you to make changes. This is called 'acquiring your work for editorial' and is a common practice in the industry. So, don't be discouraged if they want to change things. It's part of the process.
