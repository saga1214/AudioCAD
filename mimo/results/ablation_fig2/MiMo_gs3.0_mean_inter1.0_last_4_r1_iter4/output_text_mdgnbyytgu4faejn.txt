Okay, let's break this down. Here's a complete framework for a standard, effective resume that should make the process much easier for you.

First, for your header, place your bold 14-point name alone on the left. To its small right, aligned with the edge, place icons then your nine-point email, phone number with area code, comma, and finally your city. LinkedIn should also go here to the right.

Next is your Professional Summary, which should be three to four strong sentences highlighting your most relevant skills and career goals.

Then, list your Relevant Experience in reverse chronological order. For each job, include the company name, your title, the start and end dates, and three to five bullet points that focus on your achievements using action verbs.

After that, you can have an Additional Experience section for less relevant roles, which you can list more concisely.

For Education, list your degrees from most recent to oldest, including the institution, degree, and dates. This is also where you'll include any honors or awards you received.

Then comes a Skills section. Here, you should list hard skills like software or languages, and soft skills like communication, in a clear category.

If you have any, an Awards and Certifications section is great for listing things like scholarships or professional certifications.

Finally, you can have an optional section for Volunteer Work or Interests.

Just remember to proofread it carefully, save it as a PDF, and customize it for each job you apply to. This structure should give you a clear path forward.
