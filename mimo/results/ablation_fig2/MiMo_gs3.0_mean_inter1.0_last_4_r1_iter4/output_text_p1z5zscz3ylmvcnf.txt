Okay, let's see. Five classic cars that are great investments and could appeal to an 88-year-old old-school guy. That's a fun idea.

First, there's the Ford Mustang. It’s a classic American car he’d love. The first-generation Mustangs, from the 1960s, have become super collectible. Their values have shot up a lot over the last decade, and they’re still going up. They’re also pretty easy to find and work on, which fits his self-sufficient vibe.

Second, the Chevrolet Camaro. It's the other big American muscle car from that same era, competing directly with the Mustang. The first-generation Camaros are highly sought after by collectors, and their values have been rising steadily.

Third, a Porsche 911. It’s a European car, but they make great investments too. The early 911s, especially the ones from the 60s and 70s, are super rare now and their values have skyrocketed. They’re known for being reliable and holding their value really well.

Fourth, the Ferrari 250 GTO. This one is more of a luxury car, but it’s one of the most valuable classics out there. The values for these are just through the roof, and they’re incredibly rare. It’s a great long-term investment, but you’d need a lot of money.

Fifth, the Jaguar E-Type. It’s another classic British car that’s become a huge collector's item. The values for these have been on the rise for a while, and they’re known for being beautiful and driving well.

So, those are five good options. The Mustang and the Camaro are probably the most accessible, while the Ferrari and the Jaguar are more of a big investment. The Porsche is a great middle ground.
