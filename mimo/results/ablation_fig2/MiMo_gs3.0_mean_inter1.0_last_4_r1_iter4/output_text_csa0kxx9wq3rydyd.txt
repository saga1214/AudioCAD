Okay, so based on all those Thanksgiving dishes you've planned, here's a summary of the appliances you'll need and the cooking methods involved. 

For your appliances, you'll need a smoker, a large oven or two, a toaster oven, a slow cooker, and an Instant Pot. You'll also definitely need a stovetop for boiling pasta or greens, a blender or food processor for some of the salads, and of course, basic things like cutting boards, knives, and mixing bowls.

As for the cooking methods, you'll be doing a lot of roasting in the main oven, like the turkey or root vegetables. Smoking for the turkey is another. You'll also roast things in the toaster oven, like the asparagus. On the stovetop, you'll be boiling pasta for mac and cheese and wild rice, sautéing or steaming for salads, and just warming things up. In the Instant Pot, you'll make the cranberry sauce. The slow cooker is for the apple crisp. And for assembly, you'll be layering the prime rib "lasagna" and the icebox cake. It sounds like a full menu, but breaking it down like this should definitely help with your planning and shopping! Happy Thanksgiving!
