Okay, diesel camper vans age, and they need some TLC before a long trip. For brakes, the most obvious sign of wear is a grinding or squealing noise when you brake. You should also check for soft or spongy pedal feel, or if the brake light stays on after you release the pedal. Those are signs that the brake pads might be worn down and the wear indicators are working as intended. You can check the pads visually if you have easy access, but for significant wear, it’s best to get a mechanic to take a look. For diesel engines, spark plugs are different. Diesel engines don’t use spark plugs—they use glow plugs, which help heat the air for compression ignition. You can check if the glow plugs are functioning by testing their resistance with a multimeter, but really, changing them should be done by a mechanic unless you have experience. It’s a good idea to have a trusted mechanic give your van a thorough pre-trip inspection, especially for a family trip.
