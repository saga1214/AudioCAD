*   This is your most likely tool for success with a deep blockage. Insert the tip gently into the drain.
*   **Start Slow:** Don't force it. Let the curved end of the snake find its way into the main pipe.
*   **Twist and Push:** As you feel it hit the blockage, start twisting the snake handle clockwise (like unscrewing a jar). Keep pushing gently while twisting. You might feel resistance.
*   **Break it Up:** Once you feel the snake is hooked into the blockage, keep twisting and pushing. You might need to wiggle it back and forth slightly to break apart the clog. Sometimes a small amount of water helps flush it down.
*   **Pull it Out:** Once you feel it clear, pull the snake out carefully. You'll likely see some debris on it.

**Step 3: Chemical Drain Cleaner (Use with Caution)**
*   **If you don't have a snake**, or if the snake didn't fully clear it, *and* you have a chemical cleaner:
    *   **READ THE LABEL CAREFULLY.** Follow instructions precisely.

*   **CAUTION:** Chemicals can damage pipes (especially older PVC or metal), harm wildlife, and irritate skin/eyes. Use only if absolutely necessary and follow safety rules.
