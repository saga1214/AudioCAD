### 🥂 **Vodka Mix (Quick & Refreshing)**
**Name:** *Virgin Garden Spritz* (Non-Alcoholic)  
**Name:** *Garden Spritz* (Alcoholic)  
**Ingredients:**  
- **Non-Alcoholic:** Sparkling water, fresh cucumber slices, mint leaves, lime juice, pinch of salt.  
- **Alcoholic:** Gin or high-quality vodka, Prosecco or sparkling wine, fresh cucumber, mint, lime juice, salt.  
**Method:**  
1. Muddle cucumber + mint in a glass.  
2. Add ice, lime juice, and salt.  
3. Top with Prosecco + splash of sparkling water.  
4. Garnish with cucumber ribbon.  

### 🇦🇷 **Argentine Fernet Style**  
**Name:** *Pisco Siete* (Fernet-inspired)  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz Fernet-Branca (or substitute Argentine Pisco 7)  
- 1.5 oz Fresh lime juice  
- 0.75 oz Simple syrup  
- 4-5 Orange slices  
- 2 mint leaves  
- Large ice cube  
**Method:**  
1. Muddle orange slices + mint in a rocks glass.  
2. Add ice, lime juice, and syrup.  
3. Pour Fernet over ice.  
4. Stir gently. *No ice in glass first* (Argentine tradition).
