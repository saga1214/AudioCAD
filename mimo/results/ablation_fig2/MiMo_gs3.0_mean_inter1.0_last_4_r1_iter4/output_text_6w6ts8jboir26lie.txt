Okay, she's trying to find a moment of peace for herself despite all the condo disasters. Cooking is her way to unwind, which is a great idea.

I'll give her five dinner ideas that are simple but satisfying to make with common ingredients. I'll focus on comfort food that can be made quickly in a beach condo kitchen.

Here are five relaxing dinner ideas:

First, a Simple Pasta with a Twist. You can grab a box of pasta, some canned tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, and fresh basil from the grocery store. Sauté the garlic in olive oil, add the tomatoes, and simmer for about 15 minutes. Toss in the cooked pasta and fresh herbs. It's comforting and quick.

Second, a Quick Veggie Stir-Fry. Grab some pre-cut stir-fry veggies like bell peppers, broccoli, and carrots. Use a bottled stir-fry sauce, some soy sauce, and protein like chicken or tofu. Quickly cook everything in a pan with a little oil. Serve it over rice or noodles. It's fresh and healthy.

Third, a Hearty Soup Night. You can make a big pot of creamy tomato basil soup. It's warm, soothing, and perfect for a vacation. You can top it with grilled cheese for an extra treat.

Fourth, a Comforting Grilled Cheese and Tomato Soup. This is a classic for a reason. It's simple, warm, and feels like a hug in a bowl. Just make sure the soup is hot and the grilled cheese is perfectly melted.

Finally, a Fun Breakfast-for-Dinner Option. If she's feeling playful, she could make pancakes or waffles. They're easy to make, fun to eat, and can be topped with fresh fruit and maple syrup. It's a great way to end the day on a sweet note.
