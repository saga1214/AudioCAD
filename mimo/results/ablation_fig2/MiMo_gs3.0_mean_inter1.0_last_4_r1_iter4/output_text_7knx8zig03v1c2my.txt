### 🔍 **Clicker Training Explained**
Clicker training uses **positive reinforcement** with a marker sound (click) to teach commands. Here’s how it works:  
1. **Clicker as a Marker**:  
   - When your puppy does the right thing (e.g., sits), *click* the button immediately.  
   - The click means: *"That’s exactly what I wanted!"*  
2. **Reward Timing**:  
   - *Within 1–2 seconds* after the click, give a treat.  
   - This links the click + treat + behavior.  
3. **Practice**:  
   - Click when he sits → treat → repeat.  
   - Gradually add a verbal cue ("sit") after the click/treat.  

**Example**:  
- Puppy looks up at you.  
- You *click* → give treat.  
- Repeat 10–15 times daily.  
- Slowly add the word "sit" before the click.  

**Why it works**: The click is neutral and consistent, making training faster and more precise!  

---

### ⚠️ **Key Tips for Training**  
- **Consistency**: Use the same cue words and rewards.  
- **Short Sessions**: 5–10 minutes, 2–3 times daily.  
- **Patience**: Galileo (your puppy!) learns at his own pace.  
- **Avoid Punishment**: Focus on rewarding good behavior. Accidents happen—clean them up calmly.
