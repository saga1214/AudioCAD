Okay, you've got the basics covered, but I'd highly recommend a few more tools to make the job go smoothly. A drill with a few different screwdriver bits will be a huge time-saver. Shims are essential for getting everything perfectly level. And I'd also suggest getting some painter's tape to stick to the studs. It makes marking their positions so much faster and easier. Most Habitat cabinets come with their own hardware, so just make sure you have a screwdriver that can handle those. You're all set!
