Still, I’d stick with **easy access to highly processed, calorie-dense foods** as the biggest top driver behind increasing obesity rates. They’re cheap, convenient, heavily marketed, low in nutrients, and filling you up less. That combination makes them a huge factor. But I get why other things matter too—physical inactivity, stress, sleep, even genetics. It’s a mix, but that food access piece really stands out as a starting point for the problem.
