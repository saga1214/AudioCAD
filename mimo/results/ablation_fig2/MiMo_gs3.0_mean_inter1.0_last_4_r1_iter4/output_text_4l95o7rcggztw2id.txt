Here's a sand-free, pirate-themed shopping list for your nautical party at the beach, designed for finger-food ease and avoiding mess:

**Piracy-Focused Appetizers (Finger Foods):**
1.  **"Buried Treasure" Mini Sliders (6 counts x 7 kids = ~42 mini sliders):**
    *   Hamburger buns (small)
    *   Ground turkey or chicken (for kid-friendly protein)
    *   Cheese slices
    *   Dill pickle spears (cut small)
   麻婆豆腐
    *   Mayonnaise
    *   Mustard (optional)
    *   Cut into very small halves or quarters.
    *   *Theme:* Serve in small paper cups or mini treasure chest containers.
2.  **Fruit Skewers ("Pirate Jewels"):**
    *   Strawberries (halved)
    *   Blueberries
    *   Grapes (halved or quartered)
    *   Pineapple chunks
    *   Orange slices (seedless)
    麻婆豆腐
    *   *Allergy Check:* Omit watermelon entirely. Use pineapple/orange as main colors.
    *   *Theme:* Use small plastic swords or ship-shaped skewers (available online).
3.  **"Mermaid Scales" Cheese & Veggie Cups:**
    *   Small cubes of cheddar or mozzarella cheese
    *   Small cucumber sticks or bell pepper strips (halved)
    *   *Theme:* Arrange in small clear cups or treasure chest containers. Cheese = scales, veggies = fish.
4.  **Goldfish Crackers ("Pirate Ship Masts" or "Treasure"):**
    *   Original Goldfish crackers
    *   *Theme:* Serve in small plastic bags labeled "Ship Masts" or "Treasure Chests."
5.  **Pretzel Rods ("Jolly Roger Flags"):**
    *   Small pretzel rods
    *   *Theme:* Serve in small bags labeled "Jolly Roger Flags." (Wash hands after!).

**Piracy-Focused Drinks (Individual & Sealed):**
1.  **Individual Juice Boxes ("Pirate Punch"):**
    *   Various fruit flavors (Apple, Grape, Orange, Berry)
    *   *Allergy Check:* Ensure flavors do NOT contain watermelon juice concentrate. Check labels.
    *   *Theme:* Use labels to call it "Pirate Punch" or "Treasure Juice." Add a tiny paper sail (cut from colored paper) or a small plastic ship charm to the lid.
2.  **Small Water Bottles ("Life Preservers"):**
    *   Reusable water bottles (clear or fun colors)
    *   *Theme:* Attach a small paper sail (cut from colored paper) or a plastic pirate peg leg charm to the lid using a rubber band or straw.
3.  **(Optional Simple Dessert):**
    *   **"Jolly Roger" Cookies:** Simple sugar cookies decorated with red icing to form a simple J on the center and green icing for the eye. (Make ahead!).

**Essentials:**
1.  **Portion Control & Sealing:**
    *   Small paper cups (for sliders)
    *   Mini treasure chest containers (for sliders, cheese cups, cookies)
    *   Small clear containers or snack-sized treasure chests (for fruit skewers, cheese cups)
    *   Small plastic bags (for goldfish, pretzels)
    themed paper bags (for pretzel flags)
2.  **Theming & Decor:**
    *   Small plastic swords or ship-shaped skewers
    *   Colored paper (for sails, Jolly Roger eyes, labels)
    *   Small plastic pirate peg leg charms (for water bottle lids)
    *   Small plastic pirate figure charms (optional, for skewers/lids)
    *   Labeling tape/markers for themed names
3.  **Other:**
    *   Paper towels & hand sanitizer (essential for sandy beach!)
    *   Trash bags (labeled "Sand Scuttlers" or "Pirate Trash")
    *   Coolers for drinks/food

**Key Sand-Free Strategies Recap:**
*   **Individual Portioning:** Everything is sealed in its own container/bag.
*   **Easy-to-Hold Shapes:** Balls (juice boxes), blocks (sliders), skewers, crackers.
*   **Minimal Drip Liquids:** Juice boxes, sealed water bottles.
*   **Sealed Snack Bags:** Goldfish, pretzels.
*   **No Need to Sit:** Kids can hold these while walking or playing.
