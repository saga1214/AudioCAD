Okay, Mexico in autumn is an *excellent* choice for an aspiring artist. The colors there are just... vibrant. Late fall, like October through November, is perfect. You'll see all those classic autumn scenes you love. The leaves change beautifully, especially in the highland areas like Pátzcuaro and the Sierra Madre. 

The weather is also ideal—cool, crisp days and mild evenings, which is just perfect for painting. You could explore places like Oaxaca or San Miguel de Allende for that rich cultural inspiration. And for watercolors, the clear, bright light is just wonderful. You'll definitely get some amazing color palettes to work with. Just make sure to pack your paints and bring plenty of inspiration! 😊🎨
