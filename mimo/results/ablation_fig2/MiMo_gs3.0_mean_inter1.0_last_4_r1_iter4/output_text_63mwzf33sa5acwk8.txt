Okay, so you're thinking of combining a desert adventure with a Vegas pit stop after your van vacation to Joshua Tree and Nevada. Smart move—it's like two different worlds in one trip. If you want a "proper Vegas experience," it depends on what that means to both you and your girlfriend. Vegas has a ton of vibes. 

If you're fresh off a desert adventure, you might want to contrast it with something classy and relaxing. A nice hotel with a pool and hot tub sounds like a perfect way to unwind. Resorts like the ARIA or the Cosmopolitan have great pools and spas. But if you're up for a wilder Vegas experience, there's always the classic nightlife, shows, and gambling. 

Think about what you two enjoy—nature, city life, quiet time, or party time? That’ll help you decide how to spend your Vegas leg of the trip.
