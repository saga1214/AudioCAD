With your two-bed apartment, new baby, wife limited by heavy lifting, move from the U.S., furniture needs, important Canadian documents, car research, and a Golden Retriever in the future, I've put together a list of five fantastic road trip destinations perfect for you in beautiful British Columbia. I've focused on scenic drives, accessible activities, and plenty of great food, all while keeping the rainy weather in mind. I've also included some baby-friendly tips for each trip.

First up is the classic, the Vancouver Island and Vancouver Harbour Cruise. The best part is that you don't even need to drive. You can take the ferry to Vancouver Island, which is a scenic 2.5-hour ride in itself. Once on the island, you can explore the stunning Tofino area with its beaches and rainforest trails. You can also visit Victoria, which is famous for its gardens and charming downtown. If you want a real road trip adventure, you can drive the famous Coastal Road from Victoria to Tofino. For food, you can enjoy fresh seafood on the coast or some classic British Columbia cuisine in Victoria. A couple of baby tips: the ferry is great because it's calm and you can bring snacks. Also, Tofino can be crowded, so it's a good idea to book restaurants in advance.

Second, you could explore the Okanagan Lake region. This trip is great because it's just about a three-hour drive from Vancouver. The highlights are the beautiful lakes, like Okanagan, Penticton, and Kalamalka. You can go swimming, boating, or even take a cruise on the lakes. There are also wineries and vineyards you can visit. For food, you can try the famous Okanagan wines and local gourmet food. A baby tip here is that the lakes are calm and safe, and there are plenty of picnic spots. Wineries are usually pet-friendly, so your Golden Retriever would be welcome.

Third on the list is the Fraser Canyon and Lillooet. This is about a four-hour drive from Vancouver. The main attraction is the scenic Fraser Canyon Scenic Byway, which has amazing views of the mountains. You can also visit the Lillooet Heritage Centre to learn about local history. For food, you can enjoy some hearty mountain-style food in the area. A baby tip for this trip is to pack some snacks, as the drive is longer, and the towns along the canyon are small, so you might want to plan your meals.

Fourth, I recommend a trip to Whistler. It's a bit further, about a five-hour drive from Vancouver. It's famous for its mountains, skiing, and the Blackcomb Gorge Skywalk. There are also great hiking trails and plenty of other activities. For food, you can find everything from mountain lodges to cozy pubs. A baby tip: the Blackcomb Gorge Skywalk is wheelchair accessible, and the town has plenty of pet-friendly hotels. The drive through the mountains is breathtaking, but you might want to pack a rain jacket.

Finally, for your fifth option, consider a trip to the Sunshine Coast. This is a bit longer, about a six-hour drive, but it's incredibly scenic. The main highlight is the Seawall, which runs along the coast and offers stunning ocean views. You can also visit the charming town of Gibson or the lovely village of Sechelt. For food, you can enjoy fresh seafood and local farm-to-table dining. A baby tip is to pack snacks for the long drive, and the Seawall is perfect for a relaxed walk with a stroller or a baby carrier.

I hope this helps you get started on some amazing adventures!
