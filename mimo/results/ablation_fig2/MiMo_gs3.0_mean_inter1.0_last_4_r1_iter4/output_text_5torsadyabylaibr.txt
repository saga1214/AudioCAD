For a classic Thanksgiving pie, a very doable option is a pumpkin pie. You can find plenty of simple, one-pot recipes online.

For something different, you could try an apple crisp. You just combine sliced apples and sugar in a baking dish, and then top it with a simple oat and flour topping. It requires no special ingredients and is much easier than a full pie.

And if you want to try something completely different from the pie tradition, a savory pumpkin soup would be a great choice. It's still pumpkin-based, but it's served warm and savory, which is a nice change.
