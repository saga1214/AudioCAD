Based on sedan production and market presence, the **top three car brands known for sedans** are:

1.  **Toyota:** A global leader, producing an exceptionally wide range of sedans across all segments (compact, midsize, luxury) known for reliability and practicality. (Camry, Corolla, Avalon, Lexus models).
2.  **Honda:** Also a major producer with strong sedans across segments, emphasizing efficiency, driver focus, and reliability. (Accord, Civic, Odyssey).
3.  **BMW:** A premier luxury brand where sedans (especially Gran Turbos and coupes) are a core part of its identity, renowned for performance, handling, and premium quality. (5 Series, 7 Series, 8 Series).

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Reliability & Cost:** Toyota and Honda consistently rank among the most reliable and cost-effective mainstream sedan brands. BMW, while reliable, generally has higher ownership costs (maintenance, repairs, insurance).
*   **Fuel Efficiency & Practicality:** Toyota and Honda excel in fuel efficiency and practicality for daily commuting. BMW offers more performance/luxury at the cost of potentially lower efficiency and higher running costs.
*   **Luxury/Sport Focus:** BMW is specifically known for its performance-oriented luxury sedans. Mercedes-Benz and Audi are also major luxury sedan brands (like the E-Class, S-Class, C-Class, A6, A8), often competing with BMW for the top spot in *luxury* sedans.

**In summary:** Toyota and Honda are giants in the *overall sedan market*, especially for mainstream models. BMW is arguably the most famous brand *specifically for its luxury sedans*.
