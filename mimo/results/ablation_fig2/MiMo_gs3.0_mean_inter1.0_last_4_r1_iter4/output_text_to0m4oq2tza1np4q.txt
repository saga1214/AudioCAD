That's a smart way to repurpose leftovers! Pickle juice is versatile and can add tang, moisture, and a bit of sodium—use it wisely. Here are some health-conscious ideas:

### For Your Salmon Dinner:
- **Marinade Boost**: Whisk pickle juice into your salmon marinade (along with olive oil, lemon, and herbs). The acidity tenderizes the fish, but reduce added salt elsewhere to balance the sodium.
- **Sauce Base**: Simmer pickle juice with garlic, ginger, and a splash of honey for a quick, tangy dipping sauce.

### In Other Meals:
- **Salad Dressing**: Mix pickle juice with Greek yogurt or avocado for a creamy, tangy dressing (great for your spinach-cherry tomato salads).
- **Deviled Eggs**: Swap regular mustard for pickle juice—adds brightness without extra mayo.
- **Soup or Stew**: Stir a splash into vegetable or chicken soup for depth (start with 1 tbsp per cup of liquid).
- **Veggie Dip**: Thin pickle juice with olive oil and lemon for a healthy veggie dip.

### Pro Tips:
- **Moderation is Key**: Since pickle juice is salty, use it in moderation (e.g., 1-2 tbsp per recipe) and pair with potassium-rich foods (like bananas) to offset sodium’s effects on bloating/blood pressure.
- **DIY Option**: Make your own pickled veggies (like cucumbers or carrots) using vinegar, water, and spices for a lower-sodium version.
