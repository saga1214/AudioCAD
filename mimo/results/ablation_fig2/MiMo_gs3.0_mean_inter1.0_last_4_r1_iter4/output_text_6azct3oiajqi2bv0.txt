### **Top Recommendations for Secure Adhesion**
1. **Fusible Interfacier (Fusible Web)**  
   - **How to use:** Apply heat with an iron (no steam) to fuse a thin layer of interfacing to the fabric's wrong side. Press firmly for 20-30 seconds.  
   - **Best for:** Flat seams, patches, or reinforcing stress points. REI Co-op sells their durable Co-op brand of this at Arden Fair.  
   - **Avoid:** Heavy-duty tasks (use lightweight to medium-weight interfacing).

2. **Adhesive Fabric Glue (For Stretch/Synthetic Fabrics)**  
   - **Use:** Spray adhesive (e.g., **3M Super 77**) or fabric glue sticks (e.g., **Aleene’s Fabric Fusion**).  
   - **Apply:** Lightly spray both sides of fabric, press for 15-30 seconds, then hold with pins/clamps until fully set (24+ hours).  
   - **REI Tip:** Co-op brand glue is affordable and works well on synthetics.

3. **Double-Sided Fabric Tape**  
   - **Best for:** Small seams or closures. REI Co-op carries **Fusible Tape** (e.g., **Gore-Tex-compatible** options for waterproof gear).  
   - **How to use:** Cut tape to size, press firmly with an iron for 30 seconds.  

4. **Fabric-Safe Sewing Adhesive (For Temporary Fixes)**  
   - **Use:** **E6000 Fabric Glue** (dries clear, strong hold). Apply sparingly, press with a weight, and let cure 24 hours.  
   - **Avoid:** On delicate fabrics—test first!
