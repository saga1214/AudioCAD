Of course! Here are some friendly, trainable, and well-behaved candidates that fit your landlord’s rules and spouse’s purebred/mixed-breed preference:  

**1. Labrador Retriever** – Loves people, easy to train, and generally chill. Minimal barking when alone. Shedding is moderate.  
**2. Golden Retriever** – Similar to Labs—social, patient with kids, and great with other dogs. Might shed a lot, though.  
**3. Cavalier King Charles Spaniel** – Small, affectionate, and great for apartment living. Gets along with everyone but can be noisy if left alone too long.  
**Rescue Hybrids:**  
- **Cavalier/Lab mixes** – Often retain the friendly, low-maintenance traits.  
- **French Bulldog** – Small, low-energy, and playful but can be vocal ("borky").  
- **Pug** – Chill, snuggly, but may snore and shed.  
- **Basset Hound** – Laid-back and friendly but can be stubborn.  

**Next Steps:**  
- Visit shelters/rescues focusing on these breeds.  
- Test for noise when alone (maybe a crate or puzzle toys).  
- Ask about the dog’s history—training, socialization, and separation habits.  
- Discuss purebred breeders only if they’re ethical and health-tested.
