Okay, planning this dad-child theme park party sounds fun! Ten dads and ten kids is quite the crew – smart move bringing snacks/drinks in coolers so moms enjoy their bachelorette break guilt-free. Sugar-free sodas and real calzones? You're speaking my language. Let's build that mess-minimal cooler system.

Here’s a no-fuss shopping list balancing health, group preferences, groom's love for calzones, and theme park mobility. Split packs between two large coolers:

DRINKS (Ten Packs Where Possible)
- Waters: 30 reusable stainless steel bottles (dads can refill, kids won't lose sippy cups)
- Sugar-free Sodas: 30 cans (Diet Coke/Root Beer/Pepsi blend, kids size cans)
Juice for Kids: 30 low-sugar apple or OJ boxes
Sports Drinks: 10 single-serve packets (for super active kids)
Infused Water: Two large pitchers with cucumber/mint slices
Coolers lined with towels to keep drinks icy

HEALTHY SNACKS (Grab-and-Go Mess Warriors)
Cheese Packs: 30 sticks + 10 single-oz mozzarella balls
Protein Bites: 30 individual trail mix bags (nuts/seeds/dried fruit)
Veggies & Dip: Baby carrots (三大袋) + 6 hummus cups
Fruit Packs: Grape bunches + individual mandarin orange pouches
Pretzels: 10 bags microwave popcorn (no butter)
Granola Bars: 30 high-protein boxes (Clif Bars style)

MAIN MEAL (Calzone-Friendly Options)
Option 1: Pre-Order Mini Calzones (Best Bet!)
  Get 20 mini chicken-pesto or cheese calzones from pizza place. Pack in insulated bags with ice packs. Dads can grab-and-bite with one hand!
Option 2: Wrap Station Cooler
- 20 pre-portion wraps in individual bags:
  Turkey & Avocado: Cream cheese spread + cherry tomatoes
  Ham & Cheese: Provolone + spinach + mustard packet
  Veggie: Hummus + cucumber + sprouts
- Include 20 single-serve ranch/yogurt spreads

DESSERT (Groom's Celebration)
Skip cutting cake! Try:
- Calzone "Dessert": Mini ricotta-stuffed pastries (if pizza place has them)
- Cupcakes: 20 vanilla/cheesecake with drip icing (pre-cut in box)
OR
Pie: Pre-sliced lemon meringue or apple pie (individual foil slices)

COOLERS & EXTRA TIPS
- Use cooler packs with ice packs, not just ice
- Pack wet wipes, hand sanitizer, and trash bags
- Label cooler sections: "Drinks," "Snacks," "Meal," "Dessert"
- For calzones: order delivery the night before to keep them cold
- Kids' favorites: Include a few popsicles (pre-cut in cooler)

This plan gives soda freedom, calzone glory, and minimal cleanup. The groom's gonna love the personal touch! Have epic funnel cake runs and water breaks – you got this, Chief Dad Party Planner! 🎢👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
