Here's a quick recap using the child car analogy:

- **Heart**: Gas station + engine (pumps cars & makes fuel)
- **Arteries**: Big highways (carry blood away from heart)
- **Capillaries**: Tiny neighborhood streets (deliver oxygen to cells)
- **Veins**: Return roads (bring blood back to heart/lungs)
- **Lungs**: Fresh fuel stop (oxygen refueling)
- **Kidneys**: Car wash (clean blood of waste/junk)
- **Liver**: Service station (processes nutrients, sorts fuel)
