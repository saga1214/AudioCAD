Yes, absolutely. The Lockport area is a fantastic place to learn all about the history of the Erie Canal. You could start by visiting the Erie Canal Museum, which is located in the restored canal office building. It's full of exhibits, artifacts, and even has a working replica of a canal boat that you can walk through. Another great spot is the Lockport Underground Railroad Museum, which tells the important story of the canal's role in helping people escape slavery. You could also take a guided boat tour that travels along the canal and stops at the locks to show you how they work.
