That argument about the DNA in the egg is interesting, but the chicken coming first still seems to be the most logical explanation.

Here's why: for that new DNA to actually become a chicken, you need the environment of a hen's reproductive system to process it. The protein I mentioned earlier for the eggshell? It comes from a hen's ovary. No hen organ means no that protein, so no chicken's egg. The DNA mutation happened in the egg, sure, but the egg had to be processed by a non-hen's body to become an actual chicken. The first chicken was the baby that hatched.
