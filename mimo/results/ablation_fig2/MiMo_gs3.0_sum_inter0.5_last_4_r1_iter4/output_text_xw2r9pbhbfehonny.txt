Sure. Before filming your own content, here’s a simple checklist:

First, get permission from the venue. Some places may not allow recordings, especially if it’s a free event.

Next, plan your set. Keep it simple, with a clear background, good lighting, and maybe a small stage area.

Then, test your phone or camera before the show. Make sure the audio and focus are working.

During the show, try to stay natural. It’s okay to be a little stiff at first, but you’ll get used to it. You can also try to engage the audience directly for better reactions.

After the show, edit the video. You can trim the best parts, add some simple captions or music if you’d like.

Then, upload it to YouTube or TikTok. Remember to use kid-friendly settings and tag your characters or show names.

And finally, share it with friends or local groups to help it grow. 

That’s pretty much it. Just start simple and have fun with it.
