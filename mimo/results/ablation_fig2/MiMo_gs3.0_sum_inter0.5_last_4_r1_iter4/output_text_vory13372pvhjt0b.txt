   - Every transaction is publicly verifiable and permanently recorded.  
   - No hidden transactions or backdoor changes.  
   - *Without this*, the system would lack trust and accountability.

   - Enforces **scarcity** by design, contrasting with inflationary fiat currencies.  
   - Scarcity is enforced by the decentralized network—not by a central bank or governance group.  
   - *Without scarcity*, Bitcoin wouldn’t be a "sound money" alternative.  

### The "Trustless" Innovation:
Bitcoin’s genius lies in replacing **institutional trust** (e.g., banks, governments) with **mathematical rules** enforced by a decentralized network. This combination:  
- Creates **permanent scarcity** (21M BTC).  
- Guarantees **immutability** (tampering is computationally impossible).  
- Operates **globally** without intermediaries.
