Here's a comprehensive nautical-themed shopping list focused on **sand-free, allergy-safe (watermelon-free), beach-friendly food and drinks** for 7 + 7 guests (14 kids total), with fun pirate twists:

---

### **Nautical-Themed Food & Drinks Shopping List**
*(All items cut into bite-sized pieces or pre-portioned)*

#### **Main Course (Pirate's Plunder Platter)**  
- **Mini Nautical Sliders (4 options):**  
  - Ham & cheese pinwheels cut into "pirate coins"  
  - Veggie & cheese roll-ups shaped like "treasure maps"  
  - Crab cakes (if shellfish allergy is okay) or fish sticks themed as "ship masts"  
  - Turkey & cranberry "buried treasure" mini-sandwiches  
- **Themed Packaging:** Serve in mini treasure chests or cardboard pirate ship boxes.

#### **Fruit & Snacks (Pirate's Jewels & Scurvy Cures)**  
- **Skewers:**  
  - Grapes (green/red) as "pearls"  
  - Apple slices with cheddar cheese "gold coins" (use lemon juice to prevent browning)  
  - Banana "sails" on pretzel "masts" *(omit if peanut allergy)*  
- **Sand-Free Snacks:**  
  - "Scurvy Cured" Veggie Sticks (celery, cucumber) with ranch dip in a pirate-jackpot cup  
  - Goldfish crackers (arranged in "X marks the spot" patterns)  
  - Popcorn in mini treasure chests  
  - Cheese cubes ("mermaid scales")  
  - Mini chocolate cookies with sea salt (themed as "cannonballs")  

#### **Drinks (Pirate Punch & Sea Water)**  
- **Individual Juice Boxes:**  
  - Tropical punch ("sea water") in ocean-blue boxes  
  - Apple juice ("pirate's grog") in red boxes  
  - Coconut water ("tiki drink") in green boxes *(omit if nut allergy)*  
- **Water Bottles:** Clear reusable bottles with pirate stickers.  
- **Themed Serving:** Use a large plastic barrel as a drink station.  

#### **Allergy-Safe Notes**  
- **Watermelon-Free:** Use grapes, apples, bananas, berries, and citrus instead.  
- **Label Everything:** Clearly mark dishes with icons (e.g., ⚡ for spicy, 🚫 for nuts).  
- **Dip Stations:** Serve dips (yogurt, hummus) in sealed cups to avoid spills.  

---

### **Why This Works at the Beach**  
- **Mess Minimal:** No bread crumbs, drips, or require utensils.  
- **Sand-Proof:** Pre-portioned in leak-proof containers or skewers kids can grip.  
- **Theme Immersion:** Food packaging, names, and decorations tie into pirates/treasure.  
- **Kid-Friendly:** Easy to pick up, eat, and toss wrappers in designated bins.  

**Bonus Tip:** Add a "pirate scavenger hunt" for buried treasure (small toys) to keep the party pirate-focused! 🏴‍☠️
