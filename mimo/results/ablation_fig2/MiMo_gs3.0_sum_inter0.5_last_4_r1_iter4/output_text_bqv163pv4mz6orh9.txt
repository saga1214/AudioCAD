Okay, so you have food tour Thursday night from 5 to 9 PM, massage gift card you're nervous about booking correctly, now learn running releases endorphins, AND your daughter has a big opening night same night at 7 PM. That's a packed birthday for you! Let's focus on the gift for your daughter.

To show you're proud, the best gifts are usually personal and thoughtful. You could go with a handwritten note—just a short, heartfelt message about how amazing you thought she was on stage. If she's into arts or crafts, a small, nice notebook or a pretty pen could be a sweet keepsake. A small treat is always a winner too, like her favorite snack, candy, or even a fancy popcorn bucket. Or, you could get her a cute photo frame so you can print a picture of her performing to hang up. The main thing is to show you truly saw and appreciated her hard work. A personal touch means the most!
