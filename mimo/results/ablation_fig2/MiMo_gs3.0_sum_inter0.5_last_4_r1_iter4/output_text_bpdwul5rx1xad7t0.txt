That's the spirit! With a Toyota SUV and a Golden Retriever, you're all set for some amazing adventures. Here are five fantastic road trip destinations just a few hours from Vancouver.

First, there's Whistler, British Columbia. It's famous for skiing in the winter, but in the summer, it's a paradise for hiking, mountain biking, and the super fun Peak to Peak Gondola. Second, you can head to the Okanagan Valley. It's known for its stunning lakes and vineyards. You can take a scenic drive along Lake Okanagan and visit the Nillamuck Cider House for a taste of local craft cider. For a great meal, try the farm-to-table dining at the Okanagan Heritage Inn.

Third, you could explore the Fraser Canyon. It's a hidden gem with dramatic canyons and waterfalls. A must-see is the Spatsizi Wilderness Park, where you can hike to the Spatsizi Falls. For a special meal, the Pinecone Lodge in Lillooet is a beautiful historic spot with a lovely restaurant.

Fourth, you could go to the Kootenay Area in BC. You can visit the Kootenay Lakes and the Kicking Horse Canyon. A beautiful spot for a picnic is the Kicking Horse Canyon. For a memorable meal, the Kootenay Bay Lodge has a restaurant with a stunning view.

And fifth, there's the Sunshine Coast. It's known for its dramatic coastline and charming villages. You can take a boat tour of the Gulf Islands or visit the unique town of Steveston for its farm markets. For a special meal, try the fine dining at the Bayview Restaurant in North Vancouver, or the seafood at the Steveston Fish and Chips.

Have an amazing time exploring!
