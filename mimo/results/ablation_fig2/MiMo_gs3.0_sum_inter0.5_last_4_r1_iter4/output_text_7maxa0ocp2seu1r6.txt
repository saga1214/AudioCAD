No problem! Here's a complete, categorized recap of the measurements to take at fittings and advanced additions, so you can jot these down fresh:

---

### **I. EVERY Fittings (ABSOLUTELY ESSENTIAL)**
1. **Bust**  
2. **Natural Waist** (not the belt line!)  
3. **Hips**  

---

### **II. ADDITIONAL MEASUREMENTS (Garment-Specific)**
#### **Tops & Jackets:**
- Neck (for collars)  
- Shoulder Width (S/B/C/D)  
- Upper Back (Shoulder to Shoulder)  
- Bicep (from underarm to wrist bone)  
- Sleeve Length (from shoulder point to wrist)  

#### **Pants/Skirts:**
- Inseam (from crotch to hem)  
- Hip Width  
- Thigh (top to bottom)  
- **Front Rise** (crotch to waist)  
- **Back Rise** (crotch to waist – *critical for tall clients!*)  
- Waist Width  

#### **Dresses:**
- Shoulder to Hip  
- Waist to Floor (or desired length)  

---

### **III. Special Notes for Your Tall Client (6'4")**
- **Inseam (37")**: He wants pants to touch sneakers?  
  → **Shorten inseam by 1–2 inches** (e.g., 35–36"). Have him try on with shoes to pin the hem.  
- **Rise**: Measure **BACK RISE** too (likely 14–16" to prevent sagging).  
- **Ease**: Add 2–3" extra in hips/thigh for comfort when sitting.  

---

### **Quick Reference Cheat Sheet**
| **Garment** | **Core Measurements** | **Advanced Additions** |
|-------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| **Tops**    | Bust, Waist, Hips      | Neck, Shoulder Width, Bicep, Sleeve Length |
| **Pants**   | Bust, Waist, Hips, Inseam | Thigh, Rise (Front/Back), Waist Width |
| **Jackets** | Bust, Waist, Hips, Shoulder Width | Back Rise, Neck, Upper Back Width |
| **Dresses** | Bust, Waist, Hips, Shoulder to Hip | Waist to Floor |

---
