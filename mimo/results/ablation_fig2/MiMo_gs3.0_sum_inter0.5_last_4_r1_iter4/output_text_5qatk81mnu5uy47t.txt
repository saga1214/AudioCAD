### 1. **Finn Recommendation Finalized: Start with Short Fins**
   - **Why**: Compact, easy travel/storage, less leg strength required. Great for calm coves, navigation, and first-time dives. You can rent long fins once you build strength.
   - **Pro Tip**: Look for adjustable short fins—they let you narrow the fin blade as you gain confidence.

### 2. **Top Scuba Destinations in Costa Rica**
   Costa Rica’s tropical waters offer incredible biodiversity. Prioritize these sites:
   - **Cocos Island**:  
     - *Why go*: World-renowned for deep dives, shipwrecks (like the *SSendado*), and marine mammals (whales, dolphins).  
     - *Best time*: Year-round, but avoid rainy season (Dec–Apr).  
     - *Dive style*: Strong currents → long fins become useful here later in your trip.
   - **Tortuguero**:  
     - *Why go*: Calm, protected bays with easy shore diving. Spot turtles nesting (July–Oct) and reef sharks.  
     - *Dive style*: Ideal for short fins and beginners.
   - **Puerto Viejo ( Cahuita National Park)**:  
     - *Why go*: Vibrant coral reefs, tropical fish, and accessibility. Great for beginners.
   - **San Juan (Isla del Caño)**:  
     - *Why go*: Crystal-clear water, vibrant reefs, and easy drift diving.

### 3. **Trip Itinerary Suggestion (7–10 Days)**
   - **Days 1–3**: Arrive in San José → transfer to **Puerto Viejo** (4–5 hours). Dive Tortuguero’s easy reefs (short fins).  
   - **Days 4–6**: Fly to **Cocos Island** (1-hour flight). Dive deep wrecks, drop-offs, and marine mammals (long fins recommended).  
   - **Days 7–9**: Return to mainland → **Cahuita National Park** (1–2 hours from Puerto Viejo). Dive coral gardens.  
   - **Day 10**: Fly home.

### 4. **Logistics & Tips**
   - **Accommodation**: Choose eco-lodges near dive sites (e.g., **San José Hostel** for budget, **Tortuguero Lodge** for luxury).
   - **Guides**: Use PADI/SSI-certified centers (e.g., **Cocos Dive** or **Dive San Juan**). They’ll help with current conditions.
   - **Gear**: Rent fins locally; bring your own BCD/regulator if you have them.
   - **Wildlife**: Bring reef-safe sunscreen. Look for turtles, rays, and occasional manta rays!
   - **Budget**: ~$1,500–$2,500 per person (flights not included). Save by diving locally (Puerto Viejo/Cahuita) vs. flying to Cocos.

### 5. **Beyond Scuba: Dream Trip Extras**
   - **Night Dives**: See bioluminescent plankton (in Tortuguero).
   - **Whale Watching**: Humpback season (Dec–Apr) in the Golfo Dulce.
   - **Rainforest Hikes**: Connect scuba days with jungle treks (e.g., Monteverde Cloud Forest).

### Final Advice:
Pack reef-safe sunscreen, a dry bag, and a waterproof camera. Prioritize Cocos Island for adventure and Tortuguero for calm, beginner-friendly dives. With short fins first, you’ll build confidence—and later, long fins will unlock deeper, more powerful swims!
