Calgary is a fantastic base for outdoor adventures. Right in the city, you could take a scenic drive along the Bow and Elbow Rivers or hike in Prince's Island Park, which has a beautiful suspension bridge. For a bigger adventure, you're very close to Banff and the Canadian Rockies. There are countless hiking trails, world-class biking, and even hot springs. Another great day trip is to the Kananaskis Country, which has stunning landscapes perfect for hiking and scenic drives. You could also head east to the Elk Valley for winery tours or check out the Red Deer River Valley for more hiking and biking trails.
