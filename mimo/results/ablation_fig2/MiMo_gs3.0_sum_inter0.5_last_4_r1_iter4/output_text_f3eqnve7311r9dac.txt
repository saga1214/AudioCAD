That's a very smart approach. Older comics are definitely more scarce. Here are ten major keys from the Golden Age, known for their rarity and high value. You'll be looking at auction prices in the millions again for pristine copies.

*Action Comics* number one, Superman's debut, sold in 2024 for six million.
*Astonishing Science Fiction* number one sold in 2021 for two point six million.
*Amazing Spider-Man* number three hundred, the first full appearance of Venom, sold in 2021 for one point two million.
*Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, which is Batman's first appearance, sold in 2021 for one point two million.
*Superman* number one, his first appearance, sold in 2021 for one point one million.
*Whizzer* number one, the first appearance of the superhero team the All-Winner Squad, which is now Defenders, sold in 2021 for one point one million.
*The Adventures of Superman* number one, where Superman first fought the KKK, sold in 2021 for one million.
*The Amazing Adult Fantasy* number one, the first appearance of the character who would become Thor, sold in 2021 for one million.
*Marvel Super-Heroes* number one, the first official team-up of Spider-Man and the Fantastic Four, sold in 2021 for one million.
*The Avengers* number one, where Captain America and the Hulk first teamed up, sold in 2021 for one million.
