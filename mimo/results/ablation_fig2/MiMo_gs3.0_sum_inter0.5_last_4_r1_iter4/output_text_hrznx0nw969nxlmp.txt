If you're staying at **Secret Harbour Beach Resort** (St. John), you're in a prime spot for serene beach days and easy access to daytime adventures. Here are some pressure-friendly, scenic, and fun activities:

### 1. **Snorkeling & Kayaking**  
   - **Trunk Bay**: Just a short walk from Secret Harbour. This protected cove has calm waters, vibrant coral reefs, and sea turtles. Rentals and guided tours are available.  
   - **Cinnamon Bay**: A 10-minute walk away. Great for kayaking or snorkeling in the calm, protected lagoon, then swimming in the gentle waves.  

### 2. **St. John Hikes**  
   - **Cruz Hill Trail**: A moderate hike (1.5 hours round-trip) with panoramic views of Cruz Bay and the resort. Gentle paths avoid steep climbs.  
   - **Sandy Spit**: An easy, flat trail along the coast with stunning ocean vistas. Perfect for sunset walks.  

### 3. **Rainforest & Wildlife**  
   - **Cruz Bay Botanical Gardens**: A small, peaceful garden near Gallows Point featuring native plants and birds (like bananaquits!).  
   - **Virgin Islands National Park**: Explore West Cane Garden, a preserved 18th-century sugar plantation with trails and history.  

### 4. **Relaxation & Culture**  
   - **Cruz Bay Shops**: Browse local boutiques, art galleries, and cafes (like **Wildseed Café** for coffee) right at Gallows Point.  
   - **Secret Harbour Resort Amenities**: Enjoy their private beach bar, fire pit, or poolside lounge.  

### 5. **Boat Trips**  
   - **Sailors Cove**: A short boat ride away—known for its calm, turquoise waters ideal for swimming and spotting dolphins.  
   - **Salt River Bay**: A protected bay with shallow, pressure-free waters perfect for kayaking and wildlife spotting.  

### Tips for Your Brother:  
- Avoid sudden depth changes when snorkeling (e.g., drop-offs). Stick to lagoons with gradual slopes.  
- Hydrate well and avoid strenuous activity if possible.
