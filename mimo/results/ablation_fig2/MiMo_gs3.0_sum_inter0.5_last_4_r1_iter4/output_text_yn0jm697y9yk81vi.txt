Based on our conversation, here are my top recommendations organized by item and where you can typically purchase them:

### Cutting Thinner Sheet Metal (<6mm):
*   Aviation Snips  
    *Available at: **Home Depot, Lowe's, Tractor Supply Co., Ace Hardware**

### Cutting Thicker Th sheet metal or Power for Curves:
*   Angle Grinder with Cut-off Wheel  
    Blades: DeWalt DWE5744, Milwaukee 8080-20, Bosch GWS80VQ3  
    *Available at: **Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards**

### Metal Cutting Alternative:
*   Jigsaw with Cut Metal Blade  
    *Example: Forster 02930, Bi-Met 71205*  
    *Available at: **Home Depot, Lowe's, Tractor Supply Co.**

---

### Drywall Fasteners:
*   Drywall Screws (Coarse-Thread, 1-1/4")  
    *Best Brands: GRK GreenStud, DeWalt 20oz Box (Part # 28555)*  
    *Alternative: Ring-Shank Nails (1-5/8" for thicker studs)*  
    *Available at: **All major home improvement stores (Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards)**

### Insulation Behind Drywall:
*   Fiberglass Woven batts (Pre-cut to stud width)  
    *Example: Owens Corning 730*  
    *Alternative: Mineral Wool Batts (e.g., Roxul Safe'n'Sound)*  
    *Spray Foam Alternative: Closed-cell foam (e.g., Great Stuff)*  
    *Available at: **Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards** (Spray foam often at Menards/Pro shops)

---

### Best Big-Box Store for Selection in Indianapolis:
**Menards**  
*Why:* Best metal tools (cut-off wheels, jigsaw blades), widest drywall screw selection (including GRK), and largest insulation variety in Indiana. Often has better prices than Lowe's/Homeshow.
