Okay, you've got the basics down, but let's run through one last checklist to make sure you're covered.

First, you'll definitely want a cordless power drill with the right screwdriver bit for the cabinets. A tape measure is also essential for measuring and making sure everything is symmetrical. Shims are super important for leveling the cabinets on uneven floors. And a level is absolutely non-negotiable—you'll need it constantly to check that everything is straight.

You'll also want some painter's tape, which you can use to protect the walls while you're working, and a utility knife for trimming any cardboard packaging. A stud finder is already something you mentioned finding/studs, so that's good.

You should also think about how you'll manage the electrical outlets and water supply lines once the cabinets are up. You might need to plan how to turn those off and move them to the back of the kitchen.

And remember, Habitat cabinets often need countertop support brackets screwed into the wall studs before the countertop goes on. So just make sure you have all your friends ready, your tools are charged, and everyone knows the safety plan. You're all set to get those savings fully realized!
