Here's the quick recap using your child-friendly car analogy:

- **Heart:** Gas station *and* engine (pumps cars & refuels oxygen)
- **Arteries:** Big highways (take blood *away* from heart)
- **Capillaries:** Tiny streets (deliver oxygen to body parts)
- **Veins:** Roads back to heart/gas station
- **Kidneys:** Car washes (clean blood/junk, make urine)
- **Liver:** Service station (sorts nutrients, processes junk)

It all works together like a super-smart delivery system!
