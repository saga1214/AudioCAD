Okay, so you've done the cleaning, degreasing, light sanding, and disassembled the hardware. You're just about ready for primer. Before you apply it, the most important thing is to make sure the surfaces are completely dry.

Then, make sure the whole work area is well-ventilated. You'll want to protect the floors and countertops with some drop cloths, since primer can be messy.

And finally, just double-check that all the hardware you removed is clean and dry, too, before you reattach it after the primer has had time to dry.
