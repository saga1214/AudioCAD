Here are quick, elegant vodka and Fernet-inspired cocktails perfect for your dinner party—keeping the soda allergy in mind:

### **1. Quick Vodka Mixer (Gin-Style Vodka Cocktail)**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz **vodka** (e.g., Grey Goose, Tito’s)  
- 1 oz **gin** (for botanical depth)  
- ¾ oz **fresh grapefruit juice**  
- ½ oz **sparkling wine** (champagne or prosecco)  
- *Garnish*: Grapefruit twist  

**Why it works**:  
- No soda! Sparkling wine adds effervescence.  
- Gin + vodka creates a sophisticated gin sour style.  
- Grapefruit appeals to older guests; tartness energizes younger ones.  

---

### **2. Argentine Fernet Float (Fernet Negroni Style)**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1.5 oz **Fernet-Branca**  
- 1 oz **Argentine white wine** (e.g., Malbec or Chardonnay)  
- ¾ oz **sweet vermouth**  
- ½ oz **pineapple juice**  
- *Garnish*: Orange slice & mint sprig  

**Why it works**:  
- Uses Argentine white wine as the base (like a Negroni).  
- Pineapple softens Fernet’s bitterness—perfect for mixed ages.  
- No soda; the pineapple juice provides sweetness.
