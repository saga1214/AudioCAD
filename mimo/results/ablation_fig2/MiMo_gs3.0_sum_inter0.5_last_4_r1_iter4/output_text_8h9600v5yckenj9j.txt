For Halloween candy, you’ll definitely want to grab the classics that almost everyone loves. Things like classic M&Ms, Reese’s Cups, Skittles, and Sour Patch Kids are always safe bets. Gummies, like gummy worms or bears, are also super popular. You could also get some chocolate bars like Hershey’s, mini chocolate bars, or even some Halloween-themed ones like Zombie Snacks. If you want something a little more unique, you could look for some candy corn or pumpkin-shaped chocolates. Just try to avoid anything too niche or too sour for younger guests unless you know people specifically like that. And definitely check the allergen labels again to make sure everything is dye-free if allergies are a concern.
