That's a wonderful way to reclaim your peace. Here are five simple, satisfying dinner ideas you can whip up with grocery store ingredients:

1.  **Quick & Fresh Pasta:** Grab penne or spaghetti, cherry tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, basil, and maybe some mozzarella. Sauté garlic, toss in pasta and tomatoes, finish with cheese and fresh basil. Easy and comforting.

2.  Beachy Shrimp or Fish Salad:** Find fresh shrimp or fish fillets. Sauté them with garlic and lemon juice. Serve over greens or with crusty bread. Top with a squeeze of lemon and fresh herbs.

3.  **Simple Stir-Fry:** Pick a stir-fry veggie blend (broccoli, snap peas, carrots, bell peppers) and a protein like chicken, shrimp, or tofu. Grab soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and sesame oil. Heat oil, stir-fry everything together with sauce. Serve over instant rice or noodles.

4.  **Hearty Skillet Chicken & Veggies:** Get a chicken breast, potatoes (frozen work!), and some frozen mixed veggies. Dice chicken and potatoes. Sauté chicken until cooked, then add potatoes and veggies. Season with salt, pepper, and paprika. Cover and cook until tender. Great with a simple salad.

5.  **Cozy Tomato Basil Soup:** Use a can of diced tomatoes, some vegetable or chicken broth, garlic, and fresh basil. Simmer everything for 15-20 minutes. Blend until smooth. Serve with a slice of buttered toast or a grilled cheese sandwich. Pure comfort.
