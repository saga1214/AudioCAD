Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimming and warm weather! Pack that bathing suit proudly after diet dedication. Since Northern SoCal cold bothers you, let's point you south.

**Top Warm & Swim-Friendly CA Destinations (Northern出发):**

1.  **Catalina Island:** (LAX to Avalon Airport is ~45 mins)
    *   **Why it fits:** Warm Southern Ocean water perfect for swimming & snorkeling. Abundant beaches (Avalon, Harmony, Black Rock), kayaking, paddleboarding. Town vibe is walkable and casual. Excellent dining with many gluten-free options. Hot air balloon? Not really a thing here, adventure comes from water & land.
    *   **Stay:** Avalon (vibrant town, near beaches), Harmony Beach (quiet, near golf course), or Black Rock (remote beachfront).
    *   **Pack Essentials Additions:** Waterproof phone case, reef-safe sunscreen, beach towel, hat/sunglasses beyond what mentioned before. Flip-flops are essential!
    *   **Dinner Recs:** Neptune's Net (iconic seafood shack - check GF), The Landing (harbor views, known for GF), Catalina Wine Mixer (upscale, excellent GF menu).

2.  **Palm Springs:** (SFO/SJC to PSP ~2.5-3 hours drive)
    *   **Why it fits:** Consistently warm year-round (70s-90s). World-class resort pools (like those at the Ace Hotel or Mission Hills), tennis, golf, hiking in Jacumba or Anza-Borrego Desert State Park (swimming possible in desert pools). Cactus gardens. LGBTQ+ friendly. Great food scene.
    *   **Stay:** Luxury resorts with pools (Ace Hotel, Hotel Palomar), mid-range hotels, or Airbnb options.
    *   **Pack Essentials Additions:** Swimwear for resort pools, hat/visor for desert sun, light layers for evenings, comfortable walking shoes for town/walking.
    *   **Dinner Recs:** Elmer's Restaurant (historic, amazing patio - *call ahead for GF*), Thestd (upscale, creative, excellent GF), Lazy Dog Restaurant & Bar (cozy, great views, many GF options).

3.  **Big Sur / Pescadero:** (SF to Pescadero ~4 hours, or SF to Big Sur ~6-7 hours)
    *   **Why it fits:** Warmer coastal weather than SF/Northern NV (often 60s-70s, perfect for swimming in Pescadero's bay). Stunning outdoor beauty. Swimming in Pescadero's natural harbors (like at the Point Reyes State Park beach - check conditions). Kayaking, hiking, biking. Caffeinated Culture in Pescadero has a great pool.
    *   **Stay:** Pescadero (cozy B&Bs/hotels near beaches), Big Sur (luxury lodges like Nepenthe or Ventana, or simpler options), or Santa Cruz (closer to SF, good base).
    *   **Pack Essentials Addions:** Waterproof camera, hiking boots/water shoes for rocky shores, binoculars for wildlife, sturdy rain jacket (it can be foggy), swimsuit.
    *   **Dinner Recs:** Nepenthe (Big Sur, iconic cliffside dining), The Point (Big Sur, farm-to-table), Caffeinated Culture (Pescadero, poolside cafe - check hours), The Long Barn (Pescadero, excellent steakhouse, *call ahead for GF*).
