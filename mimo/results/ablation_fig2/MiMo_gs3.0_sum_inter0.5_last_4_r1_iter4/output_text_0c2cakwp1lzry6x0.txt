Here's a step-by-step guide for each installation task—prioritizing safety and efficiency to keep you productive:

### 1. **Mounting a TV**
   - **Tools Needed**: Level, stud finder, drill, appropriate screws/anchors (for drywall), and TV mount hardware.
   - **Steps**:
     1. **Identify Wall Type**: Use a stud finder to locate studs. If no studs, use heavy-duty drywall anchors.
     2. **Mark TV Center**: Measure and mark the center of the TV’s screen width.
     3. **Mount Bracket**: Attach the TV mount bracket to the wall using screws. Ensure it’s level with a level tool.
     -c **Attach TV**: Secure the TV to the bracket using the included screws (follow manufacturer instructions).
   - **Tip**: Use a laser level for perfect alignment. Mount during a weekend to avoid work fatigue.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Tools Needed**: Drill, appropriate hooks/brackets (e.g., S-hooks for lightweight pots), measuring tape, safety glasses.
   - **Steps**:
     1. **Choose Placement**: Hang near natural light (e.g., near a window). Avoid blocking walkways.
     2. **Select Hooks**: Use hooks rated for the pot’s weight (e.g., 25-lb S-hooks for heavy ceramic pots).
     3. **Drill Pilot Holes**: Measure and mark hook positions. Drill holes slightly smaller than the hook diameter.
     4. **Secure Hooks**: Screw hooks into walls/studs. For drywall, use toggle bolts.
     5. **Hang Planters**: Place pots on hooks. Ensure they’re stable and won’t swing.
   - **Tip**: Use lightweight pots (e.g., plastic) if weight is a concern. Hang 5-6" from the ceiling to avoid dust on floors.

### 3. **Installing Floating Shelves**
   - **Tools Needed**: Drill, level, shims (for uneven walls), appropriate screws/shelf pins, measuring tape.
   - **Steps**:
     1. **Plan Layout**: Decide shelf height/spacing. Measure from the floor to desired height.
     2. **Mark Positions**: Use a level to mark shelf locations. Add 1/2" clearance below outlets.
     3. **Install Shelf Pins**: Drill pilot holes for shelf pins. For heavy items, use wall anchors.
     4. **Attach Shelves**: Place shelves on pins. Secure with screws from the back if needed.
   - **Tip**: Test weight limits—max 5-7 lbs per shelf. Place away from workspaces to avoid clutter.

### **Key Safety & Focus Tips**
- **Short Work Sessions**: Limit drilling/decorating to 1-2 hours on Sundays to avoid fatigue.
- **Tool Safety**: Wear goggles during drilling. Keep a first-aid kit nearby.
- **Workspace Prep**: Cover floors with drop cloths. Keep decor items in one zone (e.g., living area) to avoid distraction.
- **Post-Installation**: Clean up immediately to prevent tool clutter from disrupting your remote work setup.
