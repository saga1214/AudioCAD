Here are the classic recipes with precise measurements and bartender instructions for your event:

### Old Fashioned (Over Ice)
- **Glass:** Rocks glass
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz bourbon rye  
  - 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
- **Garnish:** Orange twist (expressed & rubbed on rim)  
- **Instructions:**  
  1. Muddle sugar cube (with ½ oz water if using cube) in shaker.  
  2. Add spirit, bitters, ice.  
  3. Shake 10 sec.  
  4. Double-strain into glass over fresh ice.  
  5. Express orange peel over drink; discard peel.  

### Paper Plane
- **Glass:** Coupe glass  
- **Ingredients:**  
  Equal Parts Mix (measure all to 2 oz total):  
  - 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Buffalo Trace)  
  - 2 oz Aperol  
  - 2 oz Maraschino Liqueur (Luxardo)  
  - 2 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Garnish:** Cherry  
- **Instructions:**  
  1. Shake lemon juice, amaro nonino, simple syrup (1:1 ratio), and equal parts with ice.  
  2. Shake 12-15 sec.  
  3. Strain into coupe glass.  
  4. Garnish with cherry.  

### Whiskey Sour (with or without egg white)
- **Glass:** Rocks glass  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - ½ oz simple syrup  
  - *(Optional)* ½ egg white  
- **Garnish:** Cherry OR orange slice  
- **Instructions:**  
  **WITH EGG WHITE:**  
  1. Dry shake (spirit, syrup, lemon juice, egg white) 10 sec.  
  2. Add ice, shake 12 sec.  
  3. Strain over fresh ice.  
  **WITHOUT EGG WHITE:**  
  Skip dry shake; shake with ice only.  

### Aviation
- **Glass:** Cocktail coupe  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz gin (London Dry style)  
  - ½ oz maraschino liqueur  
  - ¼ oz crème de violette  
  - ½ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Garnish:** Cherry  
- **Instructions:**  
  1. Shake gin, maraschino, crème de violette, lemon juice, and ice.  
  2. Shake 15 sec.  
  3. Strain into coupe.  
  4. Garnish with cherry.  

### Bonus Gin Cocktail (French 75)
- **Glass:** Champagne flute  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 1.5 oz gin  
  - ¾ oz lemon juice  
  - ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - Top with sparkling wine (750ml bottle)  
- **Garnish:** Lemon wheel  
- **Instructions:**  
  1. Shake gin, lemon juice, syrup, and ice.  
  2. Shake 12 sec.  
  3. Strain into flute.  
  4. Top with sparkling wine.
