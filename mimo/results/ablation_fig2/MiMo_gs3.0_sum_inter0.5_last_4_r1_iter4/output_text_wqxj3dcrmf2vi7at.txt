**Homemade Homemade Heaven (Focus: Fresh, Healthy, Controllable!)**

1.  **Mini Pumpkin冻咬宝塔：**
    *   Mashed pure pumpkin (NO sugar, salt, or spices!) mixed with a tiny bit of dog-safe binder (like plain oat flour or even a spoonful of unsweetened applesauce).
    *   Spread thinly on a baking sheet.
    *   Bake low & slow (around 350°F / 175°C) until firm and frozen through (about 1.5-2 hours). Break into tiny bite-sized pieces.
    *   *Why she loves it:* Hydrating, fiber-rich, natural sweetness, cooling frozen treat!

2.  Princess Cookie Bonbons:
    *   Peanut Butter (ABSOLUTELY check Xyitol FREE!) + Rolled Oats.
    *   Mix 1-2 tbsp PB with 1-2 tbsp oats. Roll into small balls. Press flat if preferred.
    *   Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 minutes until *just* set (not hard!) or freeze for a chewy treat.
    *   *Why she loves it:* Classic peanut butter flavor! Oats add fiber. *Portion is CRUCIAL!*

3. 微型鸡肉条或胡萝卜条：
    *   Cooked Chicken Breast: Boil, bake, or steam plain chicken breast until tender. Cut into *very* thin, matchstick-sized strips. Freeze in a single layer first, then store.
    *   Carrots: Steam or bake baby carrots until very tender. Peel and slice into thin, long strips. Freeze well.
    *   *Why she loves it:* Simple, healthy protein or veggie, easy for tiny paws to hold, great for training chews.

**Essential Tips for Your Corgi Princess:**

*   **Safety First:** NO Xyitol (Erythritol/Maltitol)! Avoid chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, avocado, and excessive salt/sugar.
*   **Portion Power:** Homemade cookies and peanut butter bites are *tiny*. Corgis are prone to obesity. Measure treats (aim for 5-10% of daily calories max).
*   **Hydration:** Especially with frozen treats, ensure fresh water is always available.
*   **Introduce Slowly:** If trying new store-bought treats, start with a tiny piece to check for allergies.
*   **Consistency:** Use the same command words ("Sit", "Quiet", "Leave it") every single time.

Teaching "Quiet" to Princess is fantastic! When she starts barking at Felix, calmly interrupt with a treat or toy in her mouth. The *instant* she stops, praise her lavishly with "Quiet! Good girl!" and a tiny reward. This teaches her that silence = awesome things!

With the right treats and training, your corgi princess will be the happiest, most well-behaved little diva on the block! Enjoy spoiling her!
