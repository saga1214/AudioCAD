I hear you—severe blockages are tough with limited tools. Let’s break this down calmly, step by step. Here’s what to try:

### Step 1: **Visual Inspection**  
- Remove the drain cover/p Trap.  
- Look down the pipe—can you see debris? If yes, use a **plumber’s snake** (if in your simple toolbox) to hook and pull out the bulk of the blockage.  

### Step 2: **Boiling Water + Dish Soap**  
- Boil a kettle or pot of water.  
- Pour it slowly down the drain while adding ¼ cup of **dish soap**. The heat and soap can melt grease/fat and break up the blockage. Wait 5–10 minutes.  

### Step 3: **Plumber’s Snake (Auger) Method**  
- Insert the snake fully into the drain.  
- **Rotate it slowly** clockwise while pushing it deeper.  
- When you feel resistance, **reverse rotation** to "walk" it through the blockage.  
- Keep working until you hear/feel the snake pass through—then pull it out to remove debris.  

### Step 4: **If Still Stuck: Hot Water + Baking Soda**  
- Mix ½ cup **baking soda** with 1 cup **hot water**.  
- Pour it down the drain.  
- Wait 15 minutes.  
- Follow with 1–2 cups of **boiling water**. This can dissolve residue.  

### Step 5: **Last Resort: Wet/Dry Vacuum**  
- If the blockage is deep, use your toolbox’s **plumber’s snake** to create a seal in the drain.  
- Attach your **wet/dry vacuum** hose to the snake’s end.  
- Turn it on and suck out the blockage.  

### Safety & Real Talk:  
- **Wear gloves and eye protection**—sewer gases and debris can be nasty.  
- If the blockage is **severe** (e.g., in a sewer line), you might need more tools or a professional. It’s okay to call for backup!  
- **Patience is key**—deep clogs often require multiple attempts.
