That argument about the egg containing the chicken's DNA is interesting, but it doesn't explain the physical production of an eggshell specific to hens.

Here's why: the protein needed for a classic chicken-eggshell simply wasn't present in the ovaries of the ancestor that wasn't quite a chicken yet. So, you couldn't get a true chicken egg until you had a true hen to lay it. That means the chicken—the first true chicken—emerged first from an egg that wasn't technically a chicken egg yet.
