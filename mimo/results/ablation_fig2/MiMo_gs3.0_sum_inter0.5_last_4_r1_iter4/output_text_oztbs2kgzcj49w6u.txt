Yes, for that kind of specific local history, the best place to start is usually at the Lockport Museum and the Great Lakes Museum. It's located right there in the heart of the old canal town. They have excellent exhibits that explain the entire history of the canal and, most importantly, the incredible engineering of the locks. It's a perfect place to learn all about how the water levels were raised and lowered. They also have a lot of artifacts from the canal's heyday. It's a great way to really understand the history behind that interesting story you heard.
