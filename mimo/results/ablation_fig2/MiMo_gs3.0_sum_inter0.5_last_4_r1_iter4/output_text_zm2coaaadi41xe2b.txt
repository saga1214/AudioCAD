Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! If you're looking for authentic Asian noodles, I'd say these are some of the best places in Dallas. 

First, there's Little Tokyo in Deep Ellum. They have great ramen, udon, and even some Chinese and Korean options. 

Second is Sushi Noodle House in Addison. They specialize in sushi rolls and noodle dishes, with really fresh ingredients. 

Third is H Mart in Richardson. It's a huge Asian grocery store with a food court, where you can find some amazing dumplings, noodles, and other Asian snacks. 

And fourth, there's Noodle House in Plano. It’s a popular spot for Chinese-style noodles, and their担担面 is a must-try. 

For protein bars, I'd recommend these: 

First, Quest Bars. They're high in protein, low in sugar, and taste pretty decent. 

Second, Optimum Nutrition. These are classic—good protein content and taste like cookies. 

Third, Clif Builders. They're more like snack bars but have a decent protein kick and are good for post-workout. 

And fourth, RXBAR. They use simple ingredients and have a solid protein-to-fiber ratio.
