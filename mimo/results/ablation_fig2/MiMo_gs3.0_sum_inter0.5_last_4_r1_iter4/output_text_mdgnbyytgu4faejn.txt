Here's a clear, ATS-friendly resume format with explanations for each section. Follow this structure and customize it for each job application:

### **Modern Resume Format Template**
*(Font: 10-12pt for body, 14pt only for your name)*

---

#### **1. Header (Vertical Alignment Recommended)**
```
Your Full Name (14pt)  
│  
├── Email: example@email.com  
├── Phone: (123) 456-7890  
└── City, State (9pt)  
│  
└── icons instead of bullets (emojis may cause ATS issues)
```
**Order:** Name first, then email, phone, city vertically aligned. Place directly below name.

---

#### **2. Professional Summary (2-3 Lines)**
- Highlight your most relevant skills/experience from past work  
- State your career goals (if transitioning fields)  
- *Example:*  
> "Results-driven marketing professional with 8+ years in digital campaigns and brand strategy. Seeking to leverage expertise in SEO/PPC to drive growth at [Company Name]."

---

#### **3. Relevant Experience**  
*(Prioritize roles matching target jobs)*  
**Format per Job:**  
`Company Name | Location | Dates`  
`- Achievement with metric (e.g., "Increased sales by 40%")`  
`- Action + result`  
`- Skill demonstrated`  

**For career changes:**  
- Group similar roles together  
- Use a brief intro like:  
> **"Marketing Experience (2010-2020): Focused on brand development and client acquisition"**  
> *(List 3-5 key roles here)*  
- List additional roles in **"Additional Work History"**  

---

#### **4. Additional Work History**  
*(For roles not directly relevant to target jobs)*  
`Company | Role | Dates`  
`- Concise bullet point (no details)`  

---

#### **5. Education**  
**Format:**  
`Degree Name, [University] | Graduation Year`  
`- Honors: Highest Honors, Summa Cum Laude`  
`- Awards: Scholarship Name (Year)`  

**For multiple degrees:**  
List by date (oldest first)  
`Bachelor of Science in Marketing, University X (2010)`  
`Master of Business Administration, University Y (2015)`  

---

#### **6. Skills Section**  
*(Tailor to job description keywords)*  
**Hard Skills:**  
`Python, Google Analytics, Project Management`  
**Soft Skills:**  
`Cross-functional Collaboration, Data-Driven Decision Making`  
**Certifications:**  
`Google Analytics Certified (2023)`  

---

#### **7. Optional Sections**  
- **Projects:**  
  `Project Name | Tools Used | Outcome`  
  *(Great for career changers)*  
- **Certifications:**  
  `AWS Certified Solutions Architect | 2022`  
- **Volunteer Work:**  
  `Organization | Role | Dates`  

---

### **Key Changes You Should Make**
1. **Move contact info below your name** (side placement looks cluttered)  
2. **Remove icons** (replace with standard bullet points)  
3. **Create "Relevant Experience" section** for career transitions  
4. **Add Professional Summary** to frame your career shift  
5. **Structure skills** as a dedicated section (not mixed in)  
6. **Date all roles** even if gaps exist (show continuity)  

---

### **ATS Optimization Checklist**
- No columns/tables (use standard left-aligned text)  
- Standard headings (e.g., "Work Experience" not "My Jobs")  
- Avoid graphics, photos, or unusual fonts  
- Save as PDF (name: `FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf`)  

**Career Change Tip:** Use a **"Career Progression"** header for older roles if you want to emphasize continuity. Example:  
> **"Career Progression (2010–Present):**  
> - Marketing Manager → Digital Strategist → Brand Director"  

This format keeps recruiters scanning time in <8 seconds while passing ATS filters. Customize each section for the specific job you're applying to!
