### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia (BC):  
In BC, the key rule is **"clearly visible intersection"** (meaning you must see both ways for at least 150 meters). Here’s a quick guide:  
- **✅ Allowed**:  
  - At intersections with traffic lights *if no sign prohibits it*.  
  - On open roads with no obstructions.  
- **❌ Prohibited**:  
  - Near hills, curves, or intersections without traffic signals.  
  - If it blocks other traffic or pedestrians.  
- **Penalty**: A **$120 fine** and **3 demerit points** if caught violating the rules.  

**Tip**: If unsure, wait for a designated U-turn lane or stop sign area.

### Ingredients for Spaghetti Carbonara:  
Here’s the classic Italian recipe (serves 4):  

#### **Main Ingredients**:  
1. **Spaghetti** (400g / 14 oz)  
2. **Guanciale** (or pancetta/bacon, 200g / 7 oz, diced)  
3. **Eggs** (3 large, room temperature)  
4. **Pecorino Romano cheese** (100g / 3.5 oz, grated) + **Parmigiano-Reggiano** (50g / 1.8 oz, grated)  
5. **Black pepper** (1–2 tsp, freshly ground)  

#### **Optional Additions**:  
- Garlic (1 clove, minced)  
- Red pepper flakes (for heat)  

#### **Equipment**:  
- Large pot for pasta  
- Large skillet  
- Whisk  

#### **Steps**:  
1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water (al dente). Reserve ½ cup pasta water.  
2. Fry guanciale in a skillet until crispy. Add garlic/pepper flakes if using.  
3. Whisk eggs, cheeses, and pepper in a bowl.  
4. Toss hot pasta with guanciale. Remove from heat.  
5. Pour egg mixture over pasta, stirring quickly (it will thicken). Add pasta water if needed.  

**Pro Tip**: Don’t add pasta water if the sauce looks too wet—it’ll thicken as it cools!  

Enjoy your carbonara! 🍝
