Based on your situation and TikTok's algorithm, **engaging content** is the single most important factor. Here's why, ranked from most to least critical for you:

1.  **Engaging Content (Hook & Value):** *This is the absolute foundation.* TikTok's algorithm exists to show users content that keeps them scrolling. If your video fails to grab attention and provide value (entertainment, education, inspiration, relatability) within the first **1-3 seconds**, viewers scroll away immediately. No matter how good your lighting, audio, hashtags, or consistency are, **zero engagement means zero reach**. Your improved description and hashtags become irrelevant if people don't even stop to watch.

2.  **Following Guidelines:** *Crucial, but secondary to engagement.* As you experienced, violating guidelines means your content is removed or restricted. This means **zero potential for engagement or reach**. It's the absolute baseline requirement – without it, you can't compete. However, once you *are* within guidelines, the *quality* of your content (engagement) determines how far it goes.

3.  **Consistency:** *Important for algorithm familiarity.* Posting regularly signals to TikTok that you're an active creator. It helps build audience anticipation and gives the algorithm more data to learn what you do best. However, **posting consistently with mediocre content is worse than posting less often with highly engaging content**. Consistency amplifies good content; it doesn't guarantee it.

4.  **Conciseness:** *Important for retention.* TikTok thrives on short, snappy content that respects the viewer's short attention span. Getting to the point quickly and keeping it under 60 seconds (often under 30 for maximum impact) increases the chance viewers watch to the end. **High engagement often *requires* conciseness** – a strong hook followed by immediate value delivered efficiently. It's a vital *tactic* for achieving engagement, but engagement itself is the core goal.

5.  **Good Description:** *Helps with context and discovery (secondary).* Descriptions provide context for the algorithm and viewers. Using relevant keywords and hashtags *within* the description can help with discovery. However, **TikTok primarily discovers content based on the *video itself* and user engagement patterns, not the description text**. It's a useful supporting element, but far less critical than the video content itself grabbing attention.

**Why "Engaging Content" Trumps Everything Else for You Right Now:**

*   **Solves the Core Problem:** Your videos "just aren't getting out there." The most direct reason for this is likely a lack of immediate engagement – viewers aren't stopping or watching fully.
*   **Algorithmic Priority:** TikTok's algorithm *literally* rewards high completion rates and quick interactions (likes, comments, shares, duets) above all else. Engaging content is the only thing that reliably triggers this.
*   **Foundation for Other Factors:** Following guidelines, being concise, and being consistent are all *necessary* or *amplifying* factors, but they are meaningless if the core content isn't engaging enough to make people care.

**In short:** Focus relentlessly on creating content with an irresistible hook in the first 1-3 seconds that delivers clear value quickly. This is the #1 factor that will get your videos seen and start the snowball effect of engagement and reach. Once you master this, then focus on refining the other elements (especially conciseness and consistency) to amplify your results.
