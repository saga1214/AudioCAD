Sure! First, think about your lifestyle and daily routine to see which breeds might fit. Research each dog’s personality—some are naturally more chill than others. Look for breeds known for being friendly, trainable, and less likely to chase small animals. Shelters often have mixed breeds with great temperaments, so don’t overlook those. Once you find a potential candidate, schedule a meeting to see how they are around kids (if you have any) and around other dogs or squirrels. Don’t forget to ask the landlord again just to confirm all the specifics—size, noise level when alone, etc.
