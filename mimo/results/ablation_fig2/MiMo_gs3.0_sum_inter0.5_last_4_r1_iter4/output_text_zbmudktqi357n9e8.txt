Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time would probably be late September through November. That’s their fall season, and it’s when you’ll get all those incredible colors on the trees and in the countryside. You know, think fiery reds, oranges, and yellows. It’s also a great time for the Day of the Dead celebrations, which often feature really vibrant, colorful decorations. 

Just a couple of things to keep in mind... The weather is generally great then, but it can get pretty hot, especially in the middle of the day. So maybe plan your painting sessions for the cooler mornings or evenings. And, uh, if you're visiting a specific region, like say, Oaxaca or Chiapas, you might find even more stunning autumn foliage. 

Most importantly, just soak in all that color and let it fuel your creativity. Enjoy your trip and the inspiration!
