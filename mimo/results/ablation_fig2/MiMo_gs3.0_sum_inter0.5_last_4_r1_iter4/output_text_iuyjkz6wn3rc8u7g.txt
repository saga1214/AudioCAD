Okay, let's recap the two analogies we went through. 

The first one, throwing a party, was like this: Milk is the big empty room, good bacteria guests, keeping it warm energizes them, sugar is party food, group hugging thickens it into yogurt.

The second one, making an organized net, was: Milk is the disorganized crowd, bacteria are tiny coaches, warmth energizes them, milk proteins are like the net's mesh, holding hands traps the liquid to make thick yogurt.
