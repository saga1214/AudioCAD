That's fantastic! If you're an artist who has both the words and the art, you have a very unique advantage. In fact, you could potentially submit a full manuscript with your illustrations. However, most children's book publishers and agents still prefer to work with a writer and an illustrator as two separate individuals, even if one of them does both. The process is usually the same, though.

You would still need to research agents who represent children's books and send them your query letter, along with a synopsis of the book and a few sample pages of your text. If they're interested, they'll ask for the full manuscript. If you've already created the illustrations, you can submit those as well, or just the text.
