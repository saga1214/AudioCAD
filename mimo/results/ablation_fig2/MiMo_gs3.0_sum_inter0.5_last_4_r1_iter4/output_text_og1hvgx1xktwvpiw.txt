Let me clarify that for you. The standard one and a quarter to one to one ratio is actually your friend's preference. The truly classic recipe, which predates your friend's version, is to use equal parts for all three ingredients. So, one ounce of rye or bourbon, one ounce of sweet vermouth, and one ounce of Campari stirred together. That's the original.

As for 'Last Chance,' which you also mentioned, that's made with gin, one ounce of it, plus green Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime, each at three-quarters of an ounce. It’s a very different, herbal, and tart drink. So, for the Boulevardier, go with the equal parts version. For the Last Word, go with three-quarters of an ounce of each of those three ingredients. Hope that helps you decide!
