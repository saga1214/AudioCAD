Alright, let's tackle stir-frying like a pro, stir-fry style!

First, no wood spoons near your boiling water this time. Use non-metal ones.

Here's the key for great stir-fried noodles: cook your noodles a little bit under al dente, maybe one minute less than the package says. This gives them flexibility for tossing. And here's the most important part: drain them completely. Stir-fry sauce clings to noodles, so you want them as dry as possible.

Then, toss your cooked noodles with a little bit of oil in the wok or pan to prevent sticking before you add the sauce. That's your secret weapon
