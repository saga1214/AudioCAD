Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's twenty people! Packing coolers smartly is definitely the way to go to minimize mess. Let's build a low-sugar, grab-and-go meal plan around those mini-calzones for the bachelor and other crowd-pleasers.

Drinks First, hydration is key. Aim for ten large, reusable water bottles for each dad—that's twenty in total. Add ten low-sugar juice boxes specifically for kids. Then, ten sugar-free soda boxes for the adult dads. Pack plenty of refillable water bottles too, since refilling stations at the park will be your friend. Don't forget electrolyte drinks in single-serve packets for everyone on a hot day. And of course, plenty of ice packs for the coolers!

Snacks Think mess-free and easy hands. Kids will love cheese sticks, grapes, and single bags of pretzels or trail mix. For quick energy, include whole wheat crackers with individual hummus cups. Baby carrots are also great with those same hummus cups. Pack some hard-boiled eggs for extra protein among the dads. And dried fruit like raisins or cranberries in small bags is another easy option.

Now for the main meal. Since your friend loves calzones, you have two good options. First, you could pre-order ten mini-calzones from a local pizza place. They're easy to grab and go. Second, check a local pizzeria for regular calzones. Just make sure they can deliver a large, well-contained order for ten people with minimal packaging. Either way, calzones are the star here!

For dessert, cupcakes are your best bet. They're easy to grab individually and don't need any utensils. Brownie boxes are another great, low-mess option.

So to recap, the key is to pack everything individually in coolers. Dads grab cheese sticks and mini-calzones, kids get grapes and soda, and everyone can enjoy cupcakes when they're ready for a sweet treat. Hope you all have an absolutely amazing day at the park!
