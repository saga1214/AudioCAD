Okay, let me quickly recap where we left off. You won foreclosed cabinets in an auction and want to paint from black to off-white instead replacing them.

You've already removed the doors and hardware, degreased, light sanded, and I previously stressed using two high-quality coats of primer for a color that dark.

Now you're double-checking if there's anything else before applying just one coat of primer. 

To answer that: Primer is absolutely essential for good adhesion, especially over bare paint. The light sanding creates a rougher surface for the primer to grip, and the degreaser ensures the primer sticks properly. 

So, no, you don't need to do anything else before primer. Just let everything dry completely, and then apply that primer. After the primer dries, you can start painting.
