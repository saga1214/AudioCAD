Okay, diesel camper vans are workhorses, but they definitely need a pre-trip check. For brakes, you want to make sure there’s no excessive play in the brake pedal—if it feels soft or spongy, that’s a red flag. Also, listen for any grinding or squealing noises when you brake. If you can, take a look at the brake fluid level and make sure it’s not dark and dirty. For spark plugs, they’re crucial but not something you’d typically do yourself unless you're mechanically inclined. Diesel engines often use high-performance plugs, and they usually need to be replaced every 50,000 to 100,000 miles. If you’re not comfortable, a mechanic can handle it for you. Finally, just do a quick walk-around for any obvious leaks or worn-out tires. Vans are heavy, so tire pressure and tread depth are super important. It’s always better to be safe than sorry.
