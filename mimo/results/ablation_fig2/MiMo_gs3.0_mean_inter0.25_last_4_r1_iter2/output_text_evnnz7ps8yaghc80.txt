Here’s a weekly practice schedule designed for steady progress from two to three beanbags, incorporating scarves for rhythm practice. Aim for consistency over intensity.

**Weekly Practice Schedule (Choose 3-5 days):**

*   **Monday:** Dominant-Hand Two Balls: Warm-up for 5 minutes. Focus on perfect rhythm in one hand. Cool down for 2 minutes. (Total: 7 mins)
*   **Tuesday:** First Three Throws Cool-Down: Toss just the sequence (HandWithTwo ball peak, otherHand ball peak, third ball peak) 10 times without worrying about catch. Stop if frustrated. (Total: ~10 mins)
音乐练习：播放一首节奏平稳的歌，专注于与节拍同步地扔出三个球。
*   **Wednesday:** Rest or Light Stretch
*   **Thursday:** Music Practice: Pick a simple song. Toss balls rhythmically to the beat, focusing on smooth transitions from 2 to 3 items. Catch loosely at first. (Total: 15-20 mins)
*   **Friday:** Full Sequence Focus: Try short bursts of a clean three-ball juggle. Catch cleanly on each throw. Celebrate small wins! (Total: 10-15 mins)
*   **Saturday:** Scarf Practice: Use two or three long scarves. Focus purely on creating patterns and smooth, slow movements. This builds control and rhythm. (Total: 15 mins)
*   Tennis Balls: If you have time/energy on any day, swap beanbags for tennis balls. They are firmer and slightly heavier, improving your catching accuracy and wrist snap. (Total: 5-10 mins)

**Key Tips for Success:**
1.  **Short & Frequent:** 10-20 minutes daily is far better than one long, stressful session.
2.  **Warm-up/Cool-down:** Always start with your one-hand two-ball drill.
3.  **Focus on One Thing:** Each session has a clear goal (e.g., rhythm, transitions, catches).
4.  **Catch Loosely:** Don't try to catch perfectly at first. Softly catching things mid-air reduces stress.
5. 音乐：让音乐成为你的朋友，而不是敌人。如果一首歌太乱，换一首。
6.  **Rest is Crucial:** Overtraining with stress backfires. Rest days let your brain reset.
7.  **Celebrate Wins:** Did you catch twice in a row? That's a win! Focus on progress, not perfection.
8.  **Be Kind to Yourself:** Panic stems from pressure. This schedule is designed to be manageable and low-pressure.
