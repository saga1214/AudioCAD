Here’s a quick recap of delicious corgi princess treats, organized by type, with key tips for each:

### 🐾 Homemade Treats
1. **Frozen Bites**  
   - *Sweet Potato/Pumpkin:* Mash with a splash of low-sodium broth, freeze in silicone molds. Cooling & gentle!  
   - *Carrot Sticks:* Cut into thin matchsticks (boiled until tender for softness).  
   *Tip:* Cut frozen bites into pea-sized pieces.  

2. **Baked Cookies**  
   - *Peanut Butter & Oat:* Mix 1 cup oats + ½ cup xylitol-free peanut butter + 1 egg. Roll into balls, flatten, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12-15 mins.  
   *Tip:* Use almond butter if花生过敏.  

3. **Other Healthy Options**  
   - *Cheese Bites:* Mini mozzarella or cheddar cubes (moist & savory).  
   - *Apple/Traffic Cone:* Thinly sliced apple (seeds removed) or zucchini sticks (steamed first for softness).  

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands  
- **Training:** Zuke’s Mini Nibbles, Blue Buffalo Small Training Bites  
- **Snacks:** Wellness Classic Small Breed Bites, Merrick Puplets  
- **Fun:** Kong Small Sticks (carrot), Whimzees (squeaky rubber)  

### 🐶 Training Tips  
- **Sit Command:** Pair treat with upward hand motion + "Sit!" Reward instantly.  
- **Potty Training:**  
  - Take her out *immediately* after waking/feeding.  
  - Use a high-value treat (like chicken) when she goes outside.  
  - Never punish accidents—clean with enzymatic cleaner (e.g., Nature’s Miracle).
