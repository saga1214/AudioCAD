That's an excellent question, and many nutrition experts would actually agree that **ultra-processed foods** are the *one type of food group worst if consumed in excess**. Here's why:  

### Why Ultra-Processed Foods Stand Out:  
1. **Nutrient Deficiency**: They're stripped of fiber, vitamins, minerals, and healthy fats while being loaded with empty calories.  
2. **Harmful Additives**: Packed with artificial sweeteners, preservatives, emulsifiers, and colorings linked to inflammation and gut health issues.  
3. **Dopamine Triggers**: Designed to overstimulate your brain’s reward system, leading to compulsive overeating.  
4. **Compounding Risks**: They deliver excess sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats *simultaneously*, accelerating obesity, diabetes, heart disease, and metabolic syndrome.  

### Examples:  
- Sugary cereals, pastries, and frozen meals  
- Sugary sodas and energy drinks  
- Packaged snacks (chips, cookies, candy bars)  
- Fast food and processed meats  

### Why Not Just Sugar or Trans Fats?  
While excess sugar and trans fats are *terrible*, ultra-processed foods often contain *all three* in one package. For example:  
- A bag of processed cheese (high in saturated fat, sodium, and additives)  
- A soda (high in sugar)  
- Fried chicken (often contains trans fats and processed seasonings).  

### The Science:  
Studies like the **Norfolk Study** (UK) and the **Whitehall II cohort study** (UK) found that higher ultra-processed food intake correlates with a **14–20% increased risk of premature death** from all causes.  

### Practical Takeaway for Your Class:  
Limit ultra-processed foods to "occasional treats," not daily staples. Focus on whole, minimally processed foods (fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, whole grains) to build a foundation for long-term health.  

If forced to pick one, **ultra-processed foods** represent the most insidious threat because of their pervasive, multi-faceted harm.
