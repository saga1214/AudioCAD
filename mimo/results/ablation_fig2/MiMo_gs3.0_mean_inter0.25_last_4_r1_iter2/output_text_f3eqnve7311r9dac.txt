Yes, older comics are generally much rarer. Here are ten notable books from that era that are highly sought after.

A record was just set in 2024 for a nearly perfect copy of *Action Comics* number one selling for six million.

*Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen, Spider-Man's first apperance, remains one of the most valuable books ever, with recent sales around three point six million.

*Stanley Lee: The Essential Works* volume one is a major book from 2000, but shows how important the creators are.

*The Amazing Spider-Man* number fifty is the first full appearance of Venom.

*The Incredible Hulk* number one eighty-one is the first full appearance of Wolverine.

*Giant-Size X-Men* number one from 1975 was the first appearance of the new X-Men team.

*Power Man and Iron Fist* number one is the first appearance of Luke Cage, your favorite.

*The Defenders* number one is the first official team-up of the Defenders.

*Ms. Marvel* number one from 2014 was the first appearance of Kamala Khan.

And finally, *Captain America Comics* number one from 1941 sold at auction for just over three million dollars in 2022.
