The wood spoon incident is definitely in the past. For stir-fry noodles, the most important thing is called 'par-cooking' the noodles. That just means you boil them about one minute less than the package says.

Then, you drain them very well and shake the pot so they don't stick together. The reason stir-fry is harder than tacos is this draining step. After that, you just toss the noodles back in the hot wok and add your sauce and chopped veggies. It's all done in a few minutes!

You'll be fine
