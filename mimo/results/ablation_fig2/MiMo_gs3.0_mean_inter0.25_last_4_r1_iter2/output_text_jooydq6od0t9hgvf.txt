Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes perfect for your dinner party—keeping soda-free, accommodating preferences, and blending sophistication with ease:

### 🥛 1. **Argentine-Style Pisco Sour** (No soda Allergy-friendly)  
*(Vegetarian/Omnivore-friendly; no allergens)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1.5 oz Pisco (grape brandy)  
- 0.75 oz lime juice  
- 0.5 oz simple syrup  
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
- 1 egg white (optional, for foam; omit for vegan)  
- *Top with soda alternative*: **Sparkling water** or **ginger beer** (if tolerated).  
**Method**:  
Shake all ingredients *except soda* with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Top gently with sparkling water. Garnish with a lemon twist.  

### 🌿 2. **Herbal Fernet "Argentine" Spritz** (Soda-free)  
*(Vegetarian/Omnivore-friendly; no allergens)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz Fernet-Branca (or any herbal Fernet)  
- 1 oz fresh grape juice (e.g., Muscat)  
- 0.5 oz orange juice  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- *Top with*: **Sparkling water** (never soda!)  
**Method**:  
Shake Fernet, grape juice, OJ, and lime juice with ice. Strain into a rocks glass. Top with sparkling water. Garnish with an orange twist.  

### 🍸 **Soda-Free Base Tips**:  
- **Never use soda**—always **sparkling water** or **ginger beer** (check labels for allergens).  
- **Simple syrup** (equal parts sugar/water) works for vegan adjustments.  
- **Ice-forward glasses** keep drinks cold and refreshing.  

### 🎯 **Why These Work**:  
- **Formality**: Coupe glasses, elegant garnishes, and balanced herbal/fruity notes.  
- **Allergies**: Both cocktails avoid soda and common allergens.  
- **Mixed Ages**: Bright, approachable flavors (Pisco) or complex herbal notes (Fernet) appeal across generations.
