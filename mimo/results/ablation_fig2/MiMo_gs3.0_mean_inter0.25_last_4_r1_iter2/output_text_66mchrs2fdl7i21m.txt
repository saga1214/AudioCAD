To fly from Los Angeles to Phoenix, you're right, the flight itself is very short, usually just under an hour. However, that doesn't include the time it takes to get to and from the airport, so the total travel time is closer to three hours.

Driving is a very different experience. As you mentioned, it's about a five-and-a-half to six-hour drive through the desert, which is a very scenic but long trip.

Either way you decide to visit and look at horses, boarding the horse while you're back in L.A. would add monthly costs to your trip. Leasing offers a more manageable entry point into the hobby. Enjoy your trip to Arizona!
