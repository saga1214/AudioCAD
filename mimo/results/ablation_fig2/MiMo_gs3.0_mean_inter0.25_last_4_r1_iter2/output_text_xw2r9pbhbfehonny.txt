Sure, here’s a basic checklist for filming your own puppet show:

1. Get permission from the venue. Assisted living places might have rules about recording.

2. Set up your phone or camera on a tripod. Stable shots help.

3. Test the lighting. Make sure faces and puppets are visible.

4. Plan short clips. Focus on key moments or funny lines.

5. Keep it simple. Don’t overcomplicate—just capture the fun.

That’s pretty much it. Just be ready and have fun!
