For that refreshing refreshment theme, here are some mint-free scents and flavors that would pair beautifully with citrus or chocolate:  

### **Flavor Pairing Ideas:**  
1. **Peppermint or Spearmint** (if you want minty freshness but not mint flavor)  
2. **Citrus-Infused Sugars or Spritzes** (lemon, lime, or orange zest for a zesty kick)  
3. **Dark Chocolate with Citrus Zest** (like orange or lemon in the chocolate)  
4. **Berry-Infused Water or Sparkling Drinks** (raspberry, strawberry, or cranberry for a sweet-tart contrast)  
5. **Herbal Teas** (hibiscus, mint-free chamomile, or ginger for a warm, soothing option)  

### **Palet Cleansers/Breath-Minting Ideas:**  
- **Mint-Free Spearmint Chews** (if you must have minty freshness)  
- **Orange or Lemon Slices** (for a bright, natural palate reset)  
- **Cucumber or Mint-Free Herb Gum** (peppermint or parsley for freshness)  
- **Small Cinnamon or Vanilla Chews** (for warmth and contrast)
