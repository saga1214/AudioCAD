For an end-of-year celebration, a **park barbecue with dessert-focused activities** strikes a great balance between fun, inclusivity, and practicality. Here’s a tailored plan:

### **Event Structure:**
1. **Location:**  
   - Choose a covered pavilion or tented area at the park to avoid rain.  
   - Bring portable speakers for music and a whiteboard for writing race rules.  

2. **Food & Allergies:**  
   - **Brownie Themed Desserts:** Set up a **sundaes bar** (dairy-free ice cream for you!) with sprinkles, hot fudge (dairy-free if needed), and whipped cream.  
   - **Pizza Station:** Offer gluten-free/dietary options alongside regular pizza.  
   - **Parents Can Help:** Assign sign-ups for parents to bring side dishes (e.g., chips, dip—mention dairy-free options) to share costs.  

3. **Activities:**  
   - **Classic Park Races:** Three-legged races, sack races, and a "布朗尼小队接力赛" (troop relay).  
   - **Craft Corner:** Decorate s’mores sticks or badge badges for sashes.  
   - **Award Ceremony:** Present badges sashes during a short ceremony under a canopy.  

### **Logistics & Costs:**
- **Weather Backup:** Have a rain plan (e.g., picnic tables under a large umbrella).  
- **Budget:**  
  - Parents contribute $5–$10 dues to cover ice cream/pizza.  
  - You can request a small troop grant or local business sponsorship for supplies.  
- **Safety:** Assign an adult chaperone for each 5–6 girls; keep first-aid kits handy.  

### **Why This Works:**
- **Fun & Themed:** Celebrates Brownie spirit with races and desserts.  
- **Inclusive:** Addresses dietary needs and budget constraints.  
- **Memorable:** Displays growth through badges and teamwork.  

You’ve got this! Start small, lean into creativity, and don’t forget to enjoy chips and dip dairy-free—it counts too! 🍦🍪
