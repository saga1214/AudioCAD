Okay, let's break this down. Chronic redness has many potential causes. Rosacea is a big one, sun damage over a lifetime is another, genetics can also play a part. Erythema can be a symptom of vasculitis, which is inflammation of the blood vessels near the skin's surface.

Vasculitis itself is an autoimmune condition. The immune system mistakenly attacks the blood vessels, causing inflammation. It can affect vessels of any size, from large to small. Symptoms can be all over, like fever and fatigue, plus skin rashes. Treatment often involves anti-inflammatory medications. The biggest risk is that this inflammation can damage the vessels, which can lead to serious problems like poor blood flow.
