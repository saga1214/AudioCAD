Absolutely! Here’s a mess-minimizing, grab-and-go meal plan with a shopping list for your dad-friendly theme park bachelor party. I’ve prioritized easy cooler storage, minimal packaging, and balanced nutrition for kids and adults.

### drinks (fill large containers/coolers)
- **Water bottles** (40+ bottles for hydration)  
- **Sugar-free sodas** (10-12 cans per adult group to share from coolers)  
- Low-sugar juice boxes (grape/mango for kids)  
- Infused water (add fruit slices like cucumber or lemon to coolers for flavor)  
- Sports drinks (small boxes for kids if prone to dehydration)  

### snacks (single-serve, no-mess)
- Cheese sticks (20)  
- Baby carrots (2 large bags)  
- Single-serve pretzels/trail mix (10 individual cups)  
- Whole-grain crackers (10 packs)  
- Greek yogurt cups (10)  
- Fruit leathers or apple sauce pouches (10)  

### Main Meals: Calzone Feast  
**Option 1: Pre-ordered mini calzones** (easiest cooler-friendly option!)  
- Order 20 mini calzones from a pizzeria (e.g., 10 cheese, 10 meat/veggie). Ask for foil pouches or paper cups.  
**Option 2: DIY Calzone Bar at Home** (if you prefer homemade)  
- Pre-make 20 calzones in individual foil cups (easier cleanup in coolers).  

### Lunch: Wrap Sandwiches  
- Pre-made wraps/sandwiches (20 individual wraps):  
  - **Adults**: Turkey/ham wrap, hummus wrap, or veggie wrap.  
  - **Kids**: Cheese wrap or deli meat wrap.  
- Include 20 small whole-grain pitas as an alternative.  

### Dessert: Crowd-Pleasing & Portable  
- **Cupcakes** (20 individual) or **frozen banana bites** (easy to grab from cooler).  
- *OR* pie: **Key Lime or Lemon Meringue Pie** (pre-cut into 10 slices; store in pie container).  

### Cooler Packing Pro Tips  
1. **Layer Smartly**:  
   - Bottom: Drinks (sodas/juices in sealed bins).  
   - Middle: Snacks (dry items like pretzels/yogurt on top of wet items like carrots).  
   - Top: Calzones/wraps/desserts.  
2. **Utensils**: Include biodegradable plates, cups, and napkins in ziplock bags.  
3. **Mess Control**: Use reusable ice packs instead of ice bags.
