That's a fantastic skill to have! This opens up a few great options.

First, you could consider self-publishing. With modern technology, you can use online platforms like Amazon KDP or IngramSpark to publish your entire book, including your illustrations, yourself. You keep all the creative control and the profits.

Second, if you're a writer and an artist, you can look for publishers or agents who specifically seek out creators who illustrate their own work. There are some smaller, more specialized publishers out there for this.

And third, you could even create your own brand. You could sell your illustrated poetry directly on a site like Etsy or through your own website, and market it to people who appreciate illustrated poetry.
