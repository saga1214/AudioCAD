Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! For ramen, there’s Tonkatsu Tamashii for rich broth and Kuro Tamashii for a traditional vibe. If you want pho, Pho 79 is solid, and for Vietnamese noodle dishes like bun bo hue, Hanoi House is great. For a more casual spot, Wok’s Noodle House has affordable and flavorful options.

As for protein bars, it depends on your taste preference. If you want something sweet and chewy, Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard is popular. For a more natural, low-sugar option, Quest bars are good. If you like salty flavors, Clif Builders bars have a great taste and high protein content. All of these are great choices if you're on the go. Enjoy your meal and snacks!
