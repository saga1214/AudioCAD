Okay, Mexico... great choice for autumn scenes, since their fall colors are just as vibrant as yours love. If your budget is tight, I'd say spring or fall are probably your best bets. Spring, you know, from March to May, is actually beautiful in Mexico. You get those wildflowers blooming, especially in the desert areas like Baja California. The colors are just... super rich. 

Then for fall, September to November, that's when you get the peak of their autumn colors. Think oaks, maples, and pines turning all those shades of red, orange, and yellow. It's perfect for practicing wet-on-wat and layering techniques we talked about. 

Watercolor markers could also be really fun for capturing details like street signs or architecture in those warmer months. And space? I mean, Mexico has some incredible observatories if you ever want to try watercolor space scenes. But yeah, for now, focus on those autumn scenes. You'll find a ton of inspiration.
