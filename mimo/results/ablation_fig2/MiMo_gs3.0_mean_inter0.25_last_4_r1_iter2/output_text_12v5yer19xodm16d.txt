I hear you! To help you get that energy boost, here are five drink ideas. You could try Green Tea, which has caffeine and antioxidants. Or, for a fruitier option, there's Iced Matcha Lemonade. A classic is Espresso with a little bit of milk. A Ginger Beer or a Turmeric Golden Milk Latte are also great choices. 

For snacks, try Almonds and Dark Chocolate, which give you protein and healthy fats. A Banana with Peanut Butter is another good one for potassium and protein. You could also have Greek Yogurt with Honey, a handful of Walnuts and an Apple, or some Trail Mix with nuts and dried fruit. Hope that helps you recharge!
