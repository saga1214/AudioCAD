Based on your passions and family dynamics, here are Christmas gift ideas blending macramé, baking, pet care, and inclusive crafts—all tailored to different ages and minimizing your oversight:

### Day 1: Macramé Focus  
- **Task by Age**:  
  - **Macramage 2–5**: Decorate pre-made plant hanger bands with beads/fabric scraps.  
  - **Macramage 6–12**: Shape simple knots (e.g., spirals) on smaller plant hangers.  
  - **Macramage 13–15**: Design custom ornaments (e.g., mini tree decorations) using stationery supplies.  
- **Why**: Lets you delegate while honoring your craft love. No store opening stress yet!

### Day 2: Quick Baking  
- **No-Roll Cookies**:  
  - **Task by Age**:  
    - **2–5**: Decorate pre-baked sugar cookies with festive sprinkles.  
    - **6–8**: Mix simple cookie dough (using pre-measured ingredients) and scoop onto sheets.  
    - **9–15**: Frost decorated cookies (pre-made icing) and add edible glitter.  
  - **Where Walmart Wins**: Buy pre-measured cookie mix or dough at Walmart for 30–50% less than PetSmart.  
- **Avoid Flour Everywhere**: Use a muffin tin for scooping, and bake in batches.

### Day 3: Pet-Friendly Crafts  
- **Peanut Butter Pine Cone Bird Feeders** (no mess worries!):  
  - **Task by Age**:  
    - **2–5**: Roll pine cones in melted peanut butter.  
    - **6–12**: Sprinkle birdseed onto cones.  
    - **13–15**: Tie cones with twine and decorate with natural items (e.g., feathers).  
- **Why**: Peanut butter outside won’t spoil (birds eat it fast!). Meaningful for pet teachers.

### Day 4: Inclusive Fingerprint Art  
- **Holiday Cards/Ornaments**:  
  - **Task by Age**:  
    - **All Ages**: Dab paint on paper, create holiday scenes (snowmen, trees), and write messages.  
    - **2–5**: Use washable markers to add details.  
    - **6–15**: Write personalized notes inside cards.  
  - **Why**: Zero hot glue risk, adaptable for all ages, and perfect for non-Christian recipients.

### Bonus: Candy Globes Alternative  
- **No-Heat Option**:  
  - Skip glue guns! Use **string + knots** to assemble globes. Kids tie strings around the base, then you add the Santa head/hair separately (pre-cut with safety scissors).  
  - **Cost Hack**: Buy candy at Walmart or local bulk stores for cheaper than PetSmart.

### Pet Food Strategy  
- **Walmart vs. PetSmart**:  
  - **For Peanut’s Treats**: Buy bulk treats at Walmart (e.g., store-brand peanut butter).  
  - **For Dry Food**: Compare unit prices at Walmart, Target, and Costco (if feasible).  
  - **Pet-Smart Tip**: Call ahead to check if they price-match Walmart’s deals.

### Key Takeaways  
- **Delegate Creatively**: Assign age-specific tasks to keep everyone engaged.  
- **Hot Glue-Free**: Prioritize crafts with minimal/no hot glue.  
- **Budget-Friendly**: Walmart for pet food/cooking staples + DIY kits (e.g., salt dough, pine cones).  
- **Meaningful & Safe**: Focus on projects where your kids’ hands are the stars, not yours.

This plan honors your craft passion while making Christmas prep joyful and inclusive! 🎄✨
