Here’s a nautical-themed shopping list designed for easy beach handling, sand-free eating, and pirate fun—plus allergy safety notes:

### **Appetizers ("Pirate’s Plunder")**
1. **Mini Nautical Sandwiches Sliders**  
   - 24 mini slider buns  
   - 1 lb deli ham or turkey (allergy check: avoid if needed)  
   - 1 lb cheese slices (cheddar or American)  
   - *Theme:* Cut into 4 pieces; label boxes "Treasure Bites" or "Meaty Doubloons."  
2. **Fruit "Pearl" Skewers**  
   - 2 cups halved grapes (allergy check: avoid if needed)  
   - 1 cup pineapple chunks  
   - *Theme:* Call "Jewels of the Sea." Use wooden skewers (seal in bags).  
3. **Cheese "Commodores"**  
   - 1 lb cubed cheddar or mozzarella  
   - *Theme:* "Sailors’ Snacks" in small snack bags labeled "Crew Rations."  

### **Main Course ("Pirate’s Feast")**  
4. **Pirate Pizza Bites**  
   - 2 pre-made mini pizzas (cheese-only, no allergens)  
   - *Theme:* Cut into quarters; serve in paper boats or pirate-shaped boxes.  
5. **"Goldfish" Crackers Platter**  
   - 1 box goldfish crackers  
   - *Theme:* "Cannonballs of Cracker" in a mini treasure chest container.  
6. veggie "Cannonballs"  
   - 2 cups cheese cubes  
   - *Theme:* "Cheese Cannonballs" in a small container.  

### **Drinks ("Saltwater Punch")**  
7. Individual Juice Boxes  
   - 8 boxes assorted juices (avoid watermelon)  
   - *Theme:* Label as "Pirate Punch" or "Mermaid’s Brew."  
8. Small Water Bottles  
   - 8 sealed bottles (water or lemonade)  
   - *Theme:* "Crew Water" in pirate-themed labels.  

### **Allergy Safety**  
- **Label clearly:** "Captain’s Special" for watermelon-free items.  
- **Communicate:** Share allergy list with all guests’ parents.  
- **Avoid cross-contamination:** Use separate utensils for allergen-free foods.  

### **Extras & Presentation**  
- **Sealed snack bags:** Pack cheese cubes, crackers, and grapes in small ziplock "pirate treasure chests."  
- **Fruit "Chest" Platter:** Arrange grapes, berries, and pineapple in a mini wooden chest.  
- **Ice Packs:** Keep drinks cold until serving.
