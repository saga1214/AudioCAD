Here’s a step-by-step guide for installing your items safely and effectively:

### 📺 **TV Mount Installation**
1. **Tools Needed**: Level, stud finder, drill, wall anchors (if needed), screwdriver.  
2. Landlord Rule Note: Avoid vibrations! Use a **vibration-dampening mount** to minimize noise.  
3. Steps:  
   - Find studs with a stud finder (critical for heavy TVs).  
   - Mark the wall’s center point, then measure the TV’s dimensions to position the mount.  
   - Drill pilot holes, insert anchors if no studs are present.  
   - Secure the mount to the wall, then attach the TV.  
   - *Tip*: Use a **mounting kit** (like VIVO or SlimTilt) for stability.  

### 🌿 **Hanging Planters**
1. **Tools**: Drill, measuring tape, appropriate screws (e.g., drywall anchors for lightweight plants).  
2. **Safety**:  
   - For heavy plants (e.g., hanging baskets), drill into **stud** or use **heavy-duty anchors**.  
   - Avoid drilling near electrical outlets/wires.  
3. **Steps**:  
   - Measure and mark where planters will hang (e.g., near windows for light).  
   - Drill holes, insert anchors, then screw hooks/brackets.  
   - Hang planters by their hangers.  
   - *Tip*: Use **twine or macramé hangers** for a softer look (no drilling needed for some!).  

### 🪜 **Floating Shelves**
1. **Tools**: Drill, level, measuring tape, wall anchors (for drywall).  
2. **Safety**:  
   - Load-bearing walls are ideal. Test weight capacity (shelves + items).  
   - Use **L-brackets** if shelves are >18" wide.  
3. **Steps**:  
   - Mark shelf positions (e.g., 24–30" from floor). Use a level!  
   - Drill pilot holes, insert anchors, then attach brackets/shelves.  
   - *Tip*: Place shelves **6–8" apart** for stability.  

### 🛠️ **General Tips**
- **Noise Control**: Use a **portable drill** if you’re worried about noise.  
- **Decorate in Short Bursts**: Dedicate 1–2 hours on Sundays to avoid fatigue.  
- **Lighting**: Use **LED strip lights** under shelves for a cozy vibe (no drilling needed).  
- **Art Installation**:  
  - Use **3M Command Strips** for lightweight art (no damage to walls).  
  - For heavy pieces, drill into a stud.  

### ⚠️ **Safety Reminders**
- Wear safety glasses when drilling.  
- Check weight limits—overloading shelves can cause accidents.  
- Avoid drilling near electrical wires (use a stud finder with wire detection).
