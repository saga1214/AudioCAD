Absolutely! Planning your Costa Rican dream trip is exciting. Here’s a tailored plan to make it unforgettable, focusing on scuba diving:

### 1. **Top Scuba Diving Hotspots in Costa Rica**
   - **Cocos Island**: Famous for deep dives, shipwrecks, marine mammals (dolphins, whales), and strong currents. Long fins recommended here for efficiency in currents.
   - **Tortuguero**: Calmer waters with incredible biodiversity (turtles, rays, coral gardens). Short fins are ideal for narrow canals and relaxed dives.
   - **Puntarenas**: Close to the mainland, offers accessible dives like "Arreceras" (coral formations) and "Cocora" (wrecks). Great for beginners.
   - **Golfo Dulce**: Sheltered bay near Punta Uva with pristine reefs and low visibility sometimes (short fins easier for maneuvering).

### 2. **Finn Recommendation Finalized**
   - **Buy Short Fins First**: Compact, travel-friendly, and easier for your first few Costa Rican dives. Rent long fins once you explore stronger current areas (like Cocos Island).

### 3. **Trip Itinerary Suggestion**
   - **Days 1–3: Puntarenas/Tortuguero**
     - Dive on *Arreceras* (easy, shallow reefs) and *Cocora* (wrecks).
     - Explore Tortuguero’s canals by kayak or boat—see wildlife without diving.
   - **Days 4–6: Cocos Island**
     - Fly to Island (30-min flight from Limón). Stay at a dive shop like **Dive Cocos** or **Blue Wave**.
     - Dives: *Punta Lesmas* (tuna, sharks), *Cocora Reef*, and *Shipwreck* sites.
     - Pro tip: Long fins highly recommended here for efficiency.

### 4. **Essential Logistics**
   - **Certification**: Ensure you’re certified for deep dives (Cocos Island has depths beyond 40ft). If not, book a **diverse introductory package** including certification.
   - **Best Time to Go**: December–April (dry season, warm water, good visibility).
   - **Accommodation**: Stay near dive shops (Puntarenas) or on Cocos Island for easy access.
   - **Guides**: Use reputable shops (e.g., **Dive Costa Rica**, **Blue Wave**). They’ll advise on current conditions.

### 5. **Extra Unforgettable Experiences**
   - **Night Dives**: At Tortuguero or Cocos for bioluminescent plankton and nocturnal creatures.
   - **Wildlife**: Spot turtles nesting in Tortuguero (July–November) or dolphins on Cocos Island tours.
   - **Land Adventure**: Add a half-day hike in **Arenal Volcano National Park** or explore **Manuel Antonio Beach**.

### 6. **Budget Tips**
   - **Save on Travel**: Book flights early. Use budget airlines like **Wingo**.
   - **Dive Packages**: Often include gear, transport, and meals—costs ~$200–$300/day for liveaboards.
   - **Local Eats**: Try Costa Rican staples like *gallo pinto* (rice and beans) and fresh tropical fruit.

### Final Advice
Start with short fins for versatility, but rent long fins for Cocos Island’s currents. Prioritize **diving with eco-conscious operators** to protect Costa Rica’s marine ecosystems.
