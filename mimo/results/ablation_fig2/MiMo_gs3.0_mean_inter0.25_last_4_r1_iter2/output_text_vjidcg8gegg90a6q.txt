For winter chicks on concrete, you absolutely want to avoid loose bedding like paper or pine shavings, because they'll get too wet and lose their insulation. The best solution is to use raised brooder cages. This lifts the chicks up off the concrete floor, so they can stay dry and warm.

Inside these cages, you can use wood pellet bedding. It's great because it composes down into a warm, heat-generating layer just like sand was for the adult chickens, but it's specifically designed for chicks. You can also use a thick layer of cardboard to line the bottom of the cage. It's cheap, insulating, and you can just replace it every few days when it gets wet. Just make sure the cardboard is plain and doesn't have any glossy ink on it.
