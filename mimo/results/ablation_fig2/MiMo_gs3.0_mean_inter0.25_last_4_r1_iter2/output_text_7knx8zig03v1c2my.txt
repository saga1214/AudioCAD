That's great you're thinking about training tools! Here's a breakdown:

### Treat Recommendations:
1. **Flavor Ideas:**  
   - **High-value for most dogs:** Chicken (breast or thigh), beef, or salmon.  
   - **Italian nods:** Cooked chicken/turkey (root heritage + universally loved).  
   - **Nerd options:** Dehydrated sweet potato, blueberries, or peanut butter (check for xylitol-free!).  
   - **Avoid:** Chocolate, grapes, onions, or raw dough.  

2. **Tips:**  
   - Use tiny pieces—puppies have small mouths.  
   - Match rewards to behavior: Small lures for "sit," bigger rewards for tricks.  

### Clicker Training Explained:  
**How it works:**  
1. **Clicker as a "Bridge":** It marks the *exact moment* your puppy does something right.  
2. **Timing is key:** Click immediately after the behavior (e.g., when he sits).  
3. **Reward after:** The click tells him "That’s it!"—then give a treat.  
4. **Repetition:** Repeat until he learns the click = treat.  

**Example (Teaching "Sit"):**  
- Hold clicker. Say "Sit." Puppy sits.  
- **Click!** (Instantly) → Give treat.  
- Repeat 10-15 times daily.  
- Gradually add a word ("Sit!") before the click/treat.  

**Why it works:**  
- Clear communication: Puppy learns what to repeat.  
- Builds confidence: The click is neutral until paired with a reward.  
- Faster than pure luring (e.g., hand-moving to make him sit).  

**Beginner Tip:** Start with just 5 minutes/day. Consistency > duration!  

### Bonus: Crate Training Basics  
1. **Make it a "Happy Place":**  
   - Feed meals inside the crate.  
   - Use soft bedding, treats, and toys.  
2. **Short Introductions:**  
   - Start with 1-2 minutes out of sight. Reward when he enters.  
   - Slowly increase time as he relaxes.  
3. **Never use it for punishment.**  
4. **For escape-prone dogs:**  
   - Place crate against a wall.  
   - Use a baby gate in the yard if no fence.  
   - Always supervise outdoor play.
