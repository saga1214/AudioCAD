From the Pilgrims' point of view, surviving a near-extinction winter made it feel like a very joyous celebration.

However, for the Wampanoag people, the event was likely much more complex. They might have seen it as a way to secure an alliance and protect their own territory, but they certainly would not have experienced it as a purely happy occasion, especially given the devastating impact of European arrival that followed.

So, it was not a happy occasion for both groups in the same way.
