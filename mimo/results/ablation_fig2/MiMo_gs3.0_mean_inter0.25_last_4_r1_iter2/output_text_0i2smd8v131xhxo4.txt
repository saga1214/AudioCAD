Here's a breakdown to address your questions:

### 1. **Switching Software**
   - **Adobe Audition vs. Audacity**: Transitioning is manageable. Start by exporting Audacity projects as WAV files (lossless format). Spend 1-2 hours weekly learning Audition’s interface (focus on effects, noise reduction, and timeline tools). Its faster rendering and advanced plugins (like "De-ess," "Noise Reduction," and "Intelligate") will save time long-term.

### 2. **AI Tools**
   - **Silence Trimming**: Tools like [Descript’s AI](https://www.descript.com/) (free tier available) or [Adobe Audition’s "Noise Reduction" AI-powered presets](https://www.adobe.com/products/audition.html) work well.
   - **Mouth Clicks**: [iZotope RX 10](https://www.izotope.com/) (paid) excels at this. Free alternatives include [Audacity’s "Noise Reduction" plugin](https://www.audacityteam.org/).

### 3. **Vocal Health & Workflow**
   - **Burnout Prevention**: Pros block time for breaks (e.g., 25 minutes recording, 5 minutes rest). Use a voice warm-up routine (lip trills, humming) and stay hydrated. For organization, use tools like [Trello](https://trello.com/) or [Asana](https://asana.com/) to track clients/projects.
   - **Meal Tip**: Order a **Korean BBQ bibimbap bowl** (rice, meat, veggies in soy-garlic sauce) or **Thai green curry fried rice**. Quick, flavorful, and balanced.
