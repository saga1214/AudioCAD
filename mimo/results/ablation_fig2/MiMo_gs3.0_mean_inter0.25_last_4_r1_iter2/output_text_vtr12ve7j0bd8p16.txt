Calgary is incredibly well positioned for spontaneous, modern weekend adventures. Since Sagittarius energy thrives on freedom, exploration, and shared discovery, here are contemporary, romantic yet accessible ideas tailored to your second date vibe:

### 🌄 **Nature Adventures (Modern Glam Outdoors)**
1. **Banff Gondola at Sunset**  
   Ride the modern glass-gondola up Sulphur Mountain for panoramic views. After dinner, walk the paved Skywalk Trail (romantic and accessible). *Why it works:* Feels like a futuristic mountain elevator, zero phobiac triggers (wide-open, no enclosed spaces).

2. **Bow River Kayaking Tour (Modern Paddle)**  
   Try a guided tandem kayak on the Bow River at dusk. Companies like **Kayak Calgary** use sleek, stable boats with tandem seating. *Phobia note:* Open water, but no enclosed spaces—great for her hylop/xylo fears if she prefers airiness.

3. **Mountainside Stargazing Picnic**  
   Drive to **Chester Lake** (near Canmore) for sunset. Pack a gourmet picnic from **The Grocery** (Calgary) or **Farmers Market**. Use a portable projector for indie-film nights under the stars. *Modern touch:* Stylish reusable containers, minimalist setup.

2. **Spa Retreat at The Edgewater**
