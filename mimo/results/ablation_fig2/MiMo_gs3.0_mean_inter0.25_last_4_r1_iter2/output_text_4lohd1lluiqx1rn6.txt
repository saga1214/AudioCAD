Based on sedan production volume, market presence, and iconic models, the **top three car brands most consistently known for sedans** are:

1.  **Toyota:** Massive global presence. Sedans like the **Camry**, **Corolla**, and **Prius** are among the world's best-selling models, emphasizing reliability and practicality.
2.  **Honda:** Renowned for balanced engineering. Sedans like the **Accord**, **Civic**, and **Odyssey** (waggon, but often considered a sedan variant) are staples, focusing on efficiency, performance, and value.
3.  **BMW:** The epitome of German luxury/sport sedans. Models like the **5 Series**, **7 Series**, **3 Series**, and **i4** (electric) define premium performance and driving dynamics.

*   **Reliability:** Toyota and Honda are generally top-tier for long-term reliability. BMW is reliable but typically has higher maintenance/repair costs.

*   **Luxury/Sport:** BMW is the benchmark, but Mercedes-Benz (E-Class, S-Class, C-Class) and Audi (A4, A6, A8) are also giants in this space.
*   **Modern Contenders:** Hyundai/Kia (Elantra, Sonata, K5, Staria) and Mazda (6, CX-60) have become major sedan players, especially with value and design.
