**Core Schedule Philosophy:**

**Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo**
*   **Morning:** Travel to Tokyo (Shinkansen from Kansai Airport or Narita/Taneshima)
*   **Afternoon:** Check-in Hotel (Ask front desk for luggage storage if needed for sightseeing)
*   **Late Afternoon:** Explore **Asakusa** (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Street - perfect visuals for Art History presentation)
*   **Evening:** Dinner in Asakusa or Ueno Park area.
*   **Work Presentation:** *None* (Travel/Adjustment)

**Day 2: Tokyo Exploration & Slides Design Practice**
*   **Morning (Workout):** 7:00 AM - 8:00 AM (Gym/Running/Yoga - pick your style)
*   **Late Morning (Sightseeing):** **Shibuya** (Shibuya Crossing, Hachiko Statue, explore shops - layout ideas)
*   **Afternoon (Work Presentation):**
    *   Focus on **Layout** experimentation for your chosen Japanese art topic slides.
    *   Brainstorm **Images:** Find high-quality images of calligraphy strokes, ukiyo-e prints (search museums like Tokyo National Museum online), traditional kimono patterns, ceramics.
    *   Browse design sites for **Color Scheme** inspiration (e.g., traditional Japanese palettes: indigo, gold, white, red accents).
    *   Test different **Fonts** on your laptop/phone for a presentation app (e.g., clean sans-serifs like Minion Pro, or elegant serifs).
*   **Evening:** Dinner in Shibuya or Harajuku.

**Day 3: Kyoto Day Trip (Travel via Shinkansen ~2h)**
*   **Morning (Workout):** 7:00 AM - 8:0 Images: Kiyomizu-dera Temple (stunning architecture), Arashiyama Bamboo Grove (natural layout/colors).
*   **Late Morning (Sightseeing):** **Kiyomizu-dera** (Iconic Temple - great for layout/color scheme ideas).
*   **Lunch:** Try traditional Kyoto cuisine (Obanzai, Kaiseki).
*   **Afternoon (Sightseeing):** **Gion District** (Geisha district - observe respectfully), explore **Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka** (traditional streets).
*   **Work Presentation (Design Aspect):**
    *   **Images:** Collect more temple architecture, traditional street scenes, nature shots (bamboo, gardens).
    *   Deep dive into **Color Scheme:** Research Kyoto's traditional color symbolism (e.g., indigo, washi paper white, moss greens).
    *   Refine **Font** choices considering traditional elegance vs. modern presentation needs.
*   **Evening:** Travel back to Osaka (~2h). Check-in Hotel. Dinner near hotel (e.g., Dotonbori area).

**Day 4: Osaka Exploration & Marked Content Focus**
*   **Morning (Workout):** 7:00 AM - 8:00 AM
*   **Late Morning (Sightseeing):** **Osaka Castle** (Stunning structure, imposing imagery).
*   **Afternoon (Sightseeing):** **Dotonbori** (Vibrant street art, neon signs, food stalls - great for layout/color ideas, street photography).
*   **Work Presentation (Marked Content Focus):**
    *   Draft key points of your Art History topic (e.g., Heian courtly art details, Edo ukiyo-e rise, kimono symbolism, specific artist movements).
    *   Research credible sources (books, museum websites, academic articles - note citations).
    *   Plan slide content flow based on your topic narrative.
*   **Evening:** Dinner in Dotonbori or Umeda Sky Building area.

**Day 5: Nara Day Trip (Travel via JR Nara Line ~30min)**
*   **Morning (Sightseeing):** **Todai-ji Temple** (Giant Buddha, UNESCO site - monumental images/layout).
*   **Lunch:** Try Kaki-no-ha Sushi (persimmon leaf-wrapped sushi).
*   **Afternoon (Sightseeing):** **Nara Park** (Feed the deer - charming, natural imagery).
*   **Work Presentation (Marked Content & Design Integration):**
    *   **Images:** Deer in park, temple architecture details, integrate findings from yesterday's research.
    *   **Layout:** Practice combining historical text with images effectively.
    *   **Color Scheme:** Ensure harmony between content and visuals.
    *   **Fonts:** Finalize font choices for headings, body text, and captions.
*   **Evening:** Travel back to Osaka (~30min). Dinner near hotel.

**Day 6: Final Exploration & Coursework Polish**
*   **Morning (Sightseeing):** Explore **Umeda** (Hankyu Department Store Sky Garden, views, modern art elements?).
*   **Late Morning (Sightworking):** Dedicate time to finalizing all slides:
    *   Proofread literacy content.
    Design final layout with corporate images placed strategically.
    Apply chosen color scheme consistently.
    Use appropriate fonts throughout.
*   **Afternoon:** Prepare coursework submission documents (if needed digitally or printed).
*   Purchase souvenirs if desired.
*   **Evening:** Dinner. Pack for departure.

**Day 7: Departure & Final Submission Prep**
*   **Morning:** Check-out hotel. Last-minute sightseeing or souvenir shopping.
*   **Late Morning:** Travel to Kansai Airport (Shinkansen + Limousine Bus recommended, ~1h).
*   **Evening:** Fly home. **Immediately** upon return to accommodation:
    *   Final check of coursework.

**Key Tips for Your Presentation Design:**

1.  **Source Images Ethically:** Use high-res images from museum websites (e.g., Tokyo National Museum, Kyoto National Museum), reputable art history sites (Google Arts & Culture, Wikimedia Commons - check licenses!), or stock sites (with proper attribution if required). Avoid pixelated tourist photos.
2.  **Consistency is Key:** Stick to your chosen color scheme and font family throughout all slides. Use consistent image borders/frames.
3.  **Purpose of Images:** Every corporate image must directly support the content on that slide. Avoid clutter.
4.  **White Space:** Don't fear empty space! It improves readability and layout elegance.
5.  **Practice:** Do a full run-through of your presentation aloud. Ensure smooth transitions and timing.
