You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which is great, so gluten isn't going to be an issue there.

Some other gluten-free snack ideas that are camp-friendly could be trail mix, where you can combine gluten-free nuts like almonds and cashews with some gluten-free seeds such as pumpkin seeds.

Rice cakes are another light and easy snack that you can also pair with some gluten-free nut butter.
