Of course! With a two-bedroom apartment and expecting a first baby, I'll focus on road trips that are about one to three hours away from Vancouver. These are ideal because they're not too far and you can pack light.

Here are five great options for you:

First, about an hour and a half away, is Vancouver Island. You can visit the Butchart Gardens, which are stunning, and then head to Tofino for some incredible beach walks and whale watching.

Second, about two and a half hours away, is the Okanagan Lake area. You could relax by the lake, enjoy a winery tour, or go hiking at Spotted Eagle Provincial Park.

Third, about two and a half hours away, is the Fraser Valley. This area is famous for its farm stands, so you can pick fresh berries and apples, and enjoy a farm-to-table meal at a local farm restaurant.

Fourth, about an hour and a half away, are the mountains in Pemberton. You can go hiking in the backcountry, visit the beautiful Howe Sound, and maybe even try some white-water rafting.

And fifth, about two hours away, is the Kootenay region. Here you can visit the Kootenay Blue Glacier, enjoy some fine dining in the town of Nelson, and go for a scenic drive on the Kootenay Pass.

I hope you enjoy exploring the beautiful BC coast!
