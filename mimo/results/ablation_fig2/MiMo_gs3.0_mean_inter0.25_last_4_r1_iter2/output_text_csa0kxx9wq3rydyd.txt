Of course, let's break it down. For appliances, you'll need a smoker, a standard oven, a stovetop, two ovens if you can manage that, a toaster oven, and an Instant Pot. For cooking methods, you'll be smoking, roasting, and baking. Baking can be done in either a standard oven or a toaster oven.
