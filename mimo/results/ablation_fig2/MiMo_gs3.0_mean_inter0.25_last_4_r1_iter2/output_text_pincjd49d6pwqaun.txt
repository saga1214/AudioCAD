Okay Will, for your 90s movie night, I have a list of 12 great picks that aren't the ones I mentioned before. I'll try to mix in different genres and tones.

First, for sci-fi, there's *The Matrix* from 1999. It's a classic. Then, the comedy *Dumb and Dumber* from 1994 is a must. For animation, *Toy Story* from 1995 is a perfect family pick. If you're feeling adventurous, *Jurassic Park* from 1993 is a great choice. For a horror-comedy, *The Shining* from 1980 is a cult classic, though it's a bit more intense. For action, *Terminator 2: Judgment Day* from 1991 is a must-watch. If you want something a bit more thoughtful, *Pulp Fiction* from 1994 is a masterpiece. For a fantasy adventure, *The Princess Bride* from 1987 is a beloved film. If you're in the mood for a mystery, *The Usual Suspects* from 1995 is very clever. For a feel-good comedy, *Ferris Bueller's Day Off* from 1986 is a classic. If you want a drama, *Goodfellas* from 1990 is excellent. For horror, *Halloween* from 1978 is a timeless classic. And for animation, *Spirited Away* from 2001 is a beautiful film. I hope you all have a fantastic time!
