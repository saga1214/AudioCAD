Here's a quick recap using the child-friendly car analogy:

*   **Heart:** The **Gas Station and Pump**. It pushes the cars (blood) all around your body.
*   **Lungs:** The **Fresh Fuel Stop**. Blood cars stop here to pick up oxygen "fuel."
*   **Arteries:** The **Big Highways**. These are the large roads blood travels on away from the heart.
*   **Capillaries:** The **Tiny Delivery Streets**. These are the super small roads where blood cars *actually* drop off their oxygen fuel to your muscles and organs.
*   **Veins:** The **Return Roads**. These roads let the blood cars drive back to the heart/gas station after dropping off their oxygen.
*   **Kidneys:** The **Car Wash & Drain**. Blood cars pass through here to get cleaned, trash (waste) is washed out, and the junk goes down the drain (urine).
*   **Liver:** The **Nutrient Sorting & Recycling Center**. It takes useful stuff from the blood, sorts it, stores some, sends some where it needs to go, and breaks down harmful things.

That's how your amazing blood delivery and cleaning system keeps everything powered up and running smoothly!
