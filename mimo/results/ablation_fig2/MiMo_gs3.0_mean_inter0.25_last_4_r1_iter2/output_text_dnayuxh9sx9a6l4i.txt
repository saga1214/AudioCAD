Sure! First, think about your lifestyle—size, energy, and space in the apartment. Then, consider breeds known for being chill and kid-friendly. Some options include Labrador Retriever, Golden Retriever, and Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, all great with kids and easy to train. For a mix, try a Labradoodle or Goldendoodle—they’re often friendly and easygoing. Other breeds to research are Basset Hound, Bulldog, and Pug, though they might shed or snore. Next, reach out to local rescues or shelters to see if they have dogs matching these traits. Visit, meet, and see how the dog interacts with you.
