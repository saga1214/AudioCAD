That's fantastic! Your coffee table is ready, and a vending machine means endless snack possibilities. Here are some savory, non-sweet ideas perfect for filling your new table:

### 🧀 Savory & Crunchy:
*   **Cheese Puffs/Pretzels:** Salty, satisfying, and easy to eat.
*   **Nuts & Seeds:** Trail mix, single-serve bags of almonds, peanuts, or sunflower seeds. (Check for sugar-free options if needed).
*   **Potato Chips:** Classic, salty, and crunchy (classic salt & vinegar, plain, or BBQ).
*   **Crackers:** Rice crackers, water crackers, or whole-grain options. Pair with cheese if you get some!
*   **Pickles:** Crunchy dill pickles or pickle spears – a tangy, salty kick.
*   **Beef Jerky:** A protein-packed, savory snack.
*   **Rice Cakes:** Plain or lightly salted, great base for toppings like nut butter or avocado (if you have them handy).

### 🍪 Savory/Sweet Balance (if you want a treat):
*   **Savory Cookies:** Look for varieties like oatmeal raisin (savory via oats) or cheese cracker cookies.
*   **Chocolate Coins:** Individually wrapped, less sugary than candy bars. *Just one or two* might scratch that sweet itch without overwhelming you.
*   **Beetroot Chips:** Unexpectedly savory and crunchy.

### 🎯 Vending Machine Tip:
Scan for:
*   **"Singles"** or **"Snacks"** sections (often chips, crackers, candy).
*   **"Produce"** or **"Veggies"** sections (often pickles, olives, sometimes fresh fruit).
*   **"Cheese"** or **"Cheese Snacks"** sections (often crackers, cheese puffs).
*   **"Nuts & Seeds"** sections.
*   **"Dairy"** sections (sometimes yogurt or cheese sticks).

**Key things to consider:**
*   **Portability:** Choose snacks that travel well from the vending machine.
*   **Mix it up:** Combine a salty chip with a crunchy nut mix or a savory cracker.
*   **Hydration:** Grab a water or soda too!
*   **Project Fuel:** Pick something that won't make your hands messy while tackling that new furniture project.
