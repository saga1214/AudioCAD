Okay, here's a travel plan for Italy to practice Italian, soak in fantasy-like landscapes, and draw novel inspiration. You can fly into Rome and take a train to Florence. From there, rent a car to explore Tuscany’s vineyards, medieval towns, and hidden trails. Stay in a small family-run bed and breakfast. Pack cat treats to visit shelters and pet adoption centers. Weekend trips to the Amalfi Coast or Sardinia’s beaches are also great. Use Duolingo phrases daily to practice. Journal every evening about the food, language quirks, and pets you meet. This trip fuels your passions, characters, friendships, and writing muscles.
