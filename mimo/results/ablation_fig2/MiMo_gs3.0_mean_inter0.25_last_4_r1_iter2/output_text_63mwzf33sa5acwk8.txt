Oh man, a Vegas vacation after a desert adventure? That’s a great combo. If you want a proper Vegas experience, skip the big hotels and go for something more unique and local. Maybe a small boutique hotel or a historic one. Then, definitely hit up a classic casino for a drink, but skip the tourist traps. Check out some hidden speakeasies or a small jazz club. Also, get a room with a view of the Strip but maybe not right on it. It can be cheaper and still feel like you're right there. Oh, and definitely hit up a good buffet for breakfast, like Bacchanal Buffet at Caesars. That’s a Vegas classic.
