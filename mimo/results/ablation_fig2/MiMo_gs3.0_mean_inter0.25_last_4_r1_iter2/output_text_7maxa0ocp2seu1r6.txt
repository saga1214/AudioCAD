### **Core Every Fitting Measurements (MUST-TAKE PER Client)**

### **Essential Add-On Measurements ( garment-specific )**
- **For Tops/Jackets/Shirts**:  
  - Shoulder width (across shoulders)  
  - Neck (for collar fit)  
  - Bicep (for sleeve comfort)  
- **For Fitted Pants/Skirts**:  
  - Thigh (between knee and hip)  
  - Hip (as above)  
  - **Back Rise** (crucial for tall clients! Distance from crotch to waistband).  
- **For Pants**:  
  - Inseam (distance from crotch to ankle).  
- **General**:  
  - **Ease** (extra fabric for comfort—add 1–2" to hips/thigh if client wants a loose sit).

### **Key Tips for Your Client (6'4", long legs)**
- **Inseam**: Use 37" but **shorten slightly** (e.g., to 35–36") so pants hit the top of sneaker toes.

  - Increase **back rise** by 1–2" to prevent pulling.  
  - Add **ease to hips/thigh** (2–3") for sitting comfort.  
  - Ensure shoes are tried on *with the pants* for perfect hem placement.  

### **Pro Tips**
- Use a flexible tape measure (not a ruler!).  
- Always measure **relaxed** (client standing normally, arms down).  
- Double-check units (all in **inches** or **cm**—be consistent!).
