Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimming and warm weather! Pack that bathing suit confidently. Since Northern SoCal offers year-round warmth perfect for your goals, let's plan a 4-5 day trip there.

For packing:
Bathing suits are key. Also pack a couple cover-ups.
Daytime wear: Sundresses, tank tops, skirts, shorts, sandals/flop-flops.
Essentials: Sunglasses, a sun hat, high-SPF gluten-free sunscreen (crucial for daughter's Celiac), and a light sweater or sarong for cooler evenings.

Top SoCal Recommendations:
1.  **La Jolla, San Diego:** Warm Pacific water, world-famous snorkeling/kayaking in sea caves, La Jolla Cove for seals, tide pools. Glamorous vibe. Stay near the coast.
2.  swimming, **Carmel-by-the-Sea:** Beautiful beach, unique town charm. Easy to drive from your Northern Cal home.
3.  **Orange County Beaches:** Huntington Beach, Newport Beach, or Laguna Beach offer great weather, beaches, and fun nearby attractions like the OC Zoo or Balboa Park.

**Sample 4-Day Itinerary: La Jolla & San Diego**

*   **Day 1: Arrival & Beach Bliss**
    *   **Travel:** Fly into San Diego International Airport (SAN). Rent a large SUV/minivan for the group. Drive directly to your hotel in La Jolla.
    *   **Stay:** Luxury: The La Jolla Cove Hotel or Hotel La Jolla. Mid-Range: Hotel Casa del Mar or The Lodge at Torrey Pines (great value). Budget: Hotel Casa del Mar (still beautiful).
    *   **Dinner:** **La Jolla Sports Bar & Grill:** Casual, oceanfront, great seafood views. *Gluten-Free:* Ask about options.
    *   **Evening:** Arrive, check-in, head straight to **La Jolla Cove Beach** for sunset and a swim! Watch the seals basking on the rocks.

*   **Day 2: Adventure & Views**
    *   **Morning:** **La Jolla Cove Kayak Tour** with a marine biologist. Paddle through sea caves, see sea lions up close, learn about the ecosystem. *Ensure tour operator handles gluten-free well.*
    *   **Lunch:** Pack snacks or grab gluten-free options from a market like Whole Foods.
    *   **Afternoon:** Hike **Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve**. The summit hike offers stunning ocean/cape views. Easy trails available too.
    *   **Dinner:** **Harbor Spice:** Upscale Asian fusion, excellent gluten-free menu options. Reservations essential. Beautiful harbor views.
    *   **Evening:** Stroll along **Cove Walk** for shops and the iconic La Jolla sign.

*   **Day 3: San Diego Exploration**
    *   **Morning:** Drive to **Balboa Park**. Explore the museums (OC Zoo is great for kids, inside Balboa Park), Spanish architecture, and the Botanical Garden.
    *   **Lunch:** **El Prez** (casual, great Mexican food, gluten-free options).
    *   **Afternoon:** Visit **USS Midway Museum** (huge, fascinating) or the **San Diego Zoo** (world-class,女儿肯定会喜欢).
    *   **Dinner:** **Ironside Fish & Oyster** (seaside dining, excellent seafood, gluten-free available). *Reservations recommended.*
    *   **Evening:** Explore the Gaslamp Quarter (historic downtown area).

*   **Day 4: Relaxation & Departure**
    *   **Morning:** Relax at **Descanso Beach Park** (calmer waters, great for swimming/kayaking) or shop at the **La Jolla Village Shops**.
    *   **Brunch:** **La Jolla Farms** (gourmet gluten-free options, beautiful setting).
    *   **Travel:** Return rental car, fly home from SAN.

**Why SoCal?** Guaranteed warm weather perfect for swimming year-round, diverse attractions (beaches, mountains, cities), excellent gluten-free dining options, and easy to get to from Northern Cal. Enjoy your vacation!
