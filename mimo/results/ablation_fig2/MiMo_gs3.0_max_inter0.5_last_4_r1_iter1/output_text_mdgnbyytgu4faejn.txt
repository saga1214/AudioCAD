Okay, let's put it all together. Here's a complete structure you can follow, from top to bottom.

First is your name in 14-point font. To its right, centered with icons but in 9-point font, place your email, phone number, and city. I'd move this block directly below your name for a cleaner look. If you add a LinkedIn icon/link here, do so without icons just in case automated systems have issues with them.

Next, a brief and engaging professional summary of two or three sentences highlighting your most relevant experience and career goals.

Then, the contact information section we discussed.

After that, the experience section. List your most relevant jobs first, using the format: Company, Job Title, Dates, followed by a couple of bullet points highlighting key achievements.

If you're switching fields, create a section called "Relevant Experience" for older, highly applicable roles. List them similarly, maybe with minimal bullet points.

Then, an optional "Additional Work History" section, where you can list less relevant jobs with just the company, title, and dates.

Next comes the education section. List each degree you earned, the school, and any relevant honors or awards, like 'Graduated Magna Cum Laude' or a scholarship name.

You can add a dedicated skills section right after education. Here, list hard and soft skills relevant to the jobs you're applying for, like Python, Project Management, or Communication.

Finally, you can have an optional certifications or awards section if you have many. Just list them out.

This structure is clear, easy to scan, and effectively showcases your relevant experience while managing any gaps or shifts in your career path.
