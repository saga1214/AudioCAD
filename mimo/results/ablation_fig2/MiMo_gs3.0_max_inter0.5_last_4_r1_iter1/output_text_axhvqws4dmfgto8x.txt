Alright, setting off smoke alarms with wood spoons is behind you. Here’s stir-fry survival 101.

One: Chop your veggies before you even turn on the stove. Keep them small and uniform so they cook evenly.

Two: Get your wok or large pan very hot with a little oil before adding meat. It should almost be smoking.

Three: Stir constantly. This is the stir part of stir-fry.

Four: For noodles, use the stovetop kind, not pasta. Cook them al dente, which means they're firm, because they’ll finish cooking later. Most importantly, save a cup of the starchy pasta water before draining.
