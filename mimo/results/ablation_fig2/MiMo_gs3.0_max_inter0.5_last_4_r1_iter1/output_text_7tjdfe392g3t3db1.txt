That depends on the instrument! Here's how to tune different common instruments using a tuner:

### 1. **Guitar or Bass**
   - **Clip-On Tuner:** Place it on the headstock. Press a string down for tuning.
   - **Phone App:** Hold your phone near the strings. Play each string from thickest (6th) to thinnest (1st).
   - **How to Tune:** Adjust the tuning pegs until the tuner shows the correct note (e.g., E for the 6th string, A for the 5th, etc.). The tuner will show a green "in tune" light when correct.

### 2. **Ukulele**
   - Use a tuner app or clip-on tuner.
   - Tune from the 4th string (G) to the 1st (high E), following standard tuning: **G-C-E-A**.
   - The tuner will indicate when each string hits its target pitch.

### 3. **Violin/Viola/Cello**
   - Use a tuner app or clip-on tuner.
   - Tune from the thickest string (C) to the thinnest (A):
     - C-G-D-A (violin)
     - C-G-D-A (viola)
     - C-G-D-A (cello)
   - Play each string open and adjust the tuning pegs until the tuner confirms the correct note.

### 4. **Mandolin**
   - Tune both sets of strings to **G-D-A** (or **G-C-D** for 8-string).
   - Use a tuner app: play each pair of strings in unison (one higher than the other).
   - Adjust until both strings match the target pitch.

### 5. **Harmonica**
   - Use a tuner app or clip-on tuner.
   - Tune to the instrument's key (e.g., C harmonica: **C-G-D-F**).
   - Play each hole individually and adjust the reeds until the tuner shows the correct note.

### Tips for All Instruments:
- **Start with the thickest string** (lowest pitch) and work up.
- **Tune slowly:** Tiny adjustments (1/4 turn) make a big difference.
- **Check tuning after moving:** Vibrations can shift pitch. Retune if needed.
- **Use reference notes:** Apps often play the correct pitch alongside your instrument.
