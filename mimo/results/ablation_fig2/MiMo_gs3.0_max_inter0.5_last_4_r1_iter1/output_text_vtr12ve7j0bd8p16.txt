You have some excellent options for a weekend adventure. On a cool day, you could drive about 90 minutes east to Canmore and hike in the Rockies. If you want something closer, you're only about 45 minutes from the Banff Gondola, which has some fantastic views. For a more rugged adventure, you could drive about an hour and a half to the Kananaskis country and hike into the Darkwoods. A great spot for a scenic drive and a walk along a river is the Elk Valley Regional Park. And finally, for a more unique experience, you could head about two hours west to the Siksika Nation Indigenous Cultural Experience, where you can learn about their history and traditions.
