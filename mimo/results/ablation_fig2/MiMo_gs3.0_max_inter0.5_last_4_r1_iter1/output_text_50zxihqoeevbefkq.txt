Okay, host here—New Year's prep checklist complete! Based on our menu planning and guest count (24 people total: twenty adults + four kids), I’ve organized a shopping list by category. I’ll include quantities, prep notes, and kid-friendly swaps where we adjusted things earlier. Let’s ring in the new year stress-free!

First up, the drinks section. You'll need champagne. For a signature cocktail, say, called a “Sparkler Sparkler,” you’ll need bottles of your chosen mixer like vodka or gin, soda water, juice, simple syrup, and garnishes like citrus and berries. For the kids, you'll need the grape juice we decided on, plus plenty of soda water, ice, cups and straws, and maybe those noisemakers and kid flutes. Don't forget plenty of sparkling cider for any non-alcoholic adult guests.

Next, for the main course. You'll need a large, thick-cut ham, about eight to ten pounds. You'll also need a large bag of frozen peas for the potato topping, olive oil for roasting the carrots, and lemon wedges for the asparagus. And of sliders, mini slider buns, about twenty-four of them, along with pre-made slider fillings like cheese and mustard, ground beef or ham, and shredded lettuce and tomato.

For the appetizers, shrimp for the shrimp cocktail, pigs in blanket sausage and rolls, slider meat and toppings, butter for slivers, and mini slider plates. And don't forget the scalloped potatoes—potatoes, cream cheese, sour cream, milk, salt, pepper, and some shredded cheddar.

For kid-specific items, get the grape juice, the noisemakers and festive hats or flutes, and maybe some simple coloring supplies for a craft corner.

For essentials, you'll need foil, a cutting board, knives, oven mitts, a large pot for boiling water for the shrimp, and plenty of ice and cups for drinks.

For cleanup, grab plenty of paper towels, dish soap, and maybe some disposable gloves for easy cleanup.

For music, create that fun New Year's Eve playlist on your streaming service.

And for decorations, balloons, kid hats or flutes, and maybe some table confetti.

I’ve estimated quantities for 24 people. Prep tips like glazing the ham before it roasts or roasting the carrots with olive oil and salt are important. Have a blast hosting! Let me know if you need recipes, playlist ideas, or more help with decor.
