Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items while respecting your landlord's rules (minimal noise/drilling where possible):

### 1. **TV Mount Installation**  
**Tools Needed:** Level, stud finder, drill, wall anchors (if no studs), screwdriver.  
**Steps:**  
- **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (wood beams behind drywall).  
- **Measure & Mark:** Hold the mount against the wall. Mark the top/bottom positions using a level.  
- **Drill Pilot Holes:** If mounting to studs, drill holes slightly smaller than the mount’s screws. If no stud, use heavy-duty anchors.  
- **Attach Mount:** Secure with screws. Test stability before hanging the TV.  
*Tip: Use a TV wall-mounting tutorial video for alignment.*  

### 2. **Hanging Planters**  
**No-Drill Option:** Use **adhesive hooks** (e.g., 3M Command Hooks) for lightweight plants.  
**Drill Option (if needed):**  
- **Tools:** Level, drill, wall anchors, hook.  
- **Steps:**  
  - Mark hook position with a level.  
  - Drill a small hole (size matches anchor).  
  - Insert anchor, then screw in hook.  
  - Hang planter (check weight limits—max 5–10 lbs per hook).  

### 3. **Floating Shelves**  
**Tools:** Level, stud finder, drill, wall anchors (if no studs), screws.  
**Steps:**  
- **Measure Twice:** Place shelves where desired, use a level, and mark positions.  
- **Drill Holes:** If attaching to studs, drill pilot holes. If no stud, drill into drywall and insert anchors.  
- **Attach Shelf:** Secure with screws. Check for level and stability.  
*Tip: Use L-brackets for extra support if shelves are heavy.*  

### Safety & Efficiency Tips:  
- **Noise Minimization:** Do installations on weekends if possible. Use a vacuum to clean up debris.  
- **Tool Prep:** Charge drills/levelers beforehand. Gather all tools/supplies to avoid interruptions.  
- **Start Early:** Begin Sunday morning to avoid fatigue. Take breaks every 30–45 minutes.  
- **Safety First:** Wear safety glasses. Secure ladders/stools. Test all installations before leaving.  

**Decorating Flow:**  
- **Morning:** Install heavy items (TV, shelves).  
- **Afternoon:** Hang planters and smaller decor.  
- **Evening:** Soften with lighting (e.g., string lights) or rugs.  

By breaking tasks into manageable steps and prioritizing safety/noise control, you’ll create a cozy space without breaking your landlord’s rules! 🛠️✨
