Pie is a fantastic idea for Thanksgiving. A great, relatively easy one to make is a blind-baked crust and fill it with a no-roll nut crust. You can even use oats or pretzels for a different flavor. It requires no oven after that.

For a classic main dish, a simple turkey breast is a great choice. It cooks much faster than a whole turkey and requires very little prep.

And for a side dish that's always a crowd-pleaser, you can't go wrong with caramelized green beans. You just toss the beans with olive oil, salt, and pepper and roast them until they're nice and brown and caramelized.
