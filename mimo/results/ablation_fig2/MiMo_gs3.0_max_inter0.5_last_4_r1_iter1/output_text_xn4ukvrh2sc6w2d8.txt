Here are the classic recipes with precise measurements for your themed cocktail reception:

### 1. Old Fashioned
**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Maker's Mark)
- 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- Orange twist (for garnish)

**Instructions:**
1. Muddle sugar cube with bitters in a rocks glass until dissolved.
2. Add bourbon and ice. Stir 20–30 seconds until chilled.
3. Discard sugar cube. Garnish with expressed orange twist.

---

### 2. Paper Plane
**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Bulleit)
- ¾ oz Aperol
- ½ oz Amaro Nonino
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- Ice

**Instructions:**
1. Shake all ingredients with ice for 10–15 seconds until well-chilled.
2. Strain into a coupe glass.
3. Serve immediately.

---

### 3. Whiskey Sour (with optional egg white)
**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Buffalo Trace)
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- ¾ oz simple syrup (or muddled sugar cube)
- [Optional: 0.75 oz egg white]
- Lemon twist (garnish)

**Instructions:**
1. **With egg white:**
   - Shake bourbon, lemon juice, simple syrup, and egg white *without ice* for 10 seconds until frothy.
   - Add ice, shake again for 10 seconds.
   - Strain into a rocks glass.
2. **Without egg white:**
   - Shake bourbon, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice for 10–15 seconds.
   - Strain into a rocks glass.
3. Garnish with lemon twist.

---

### 4. Aviation
**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz gin (e.g., Hendrick's)
- ¾ oz Maraschino liqueur (e.g., Luxardo)
- ½ oz Crème de Nonino
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- Ice

**Instructions:**
1. Shake all ingredients with ice for 10–15 seconds.
2. Strain into a coupe glass.
3. Garnish with lemon twist.

---

### 5. French 75 (Recommended Gin Cocktail)
**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 oz gin (e.g., Beefeater)
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- ¾ oz simple syrup
- 1–2 oz Champagne (or prosecco)
- Lemon twist (garnish)

**Instructions:**
1. Stir gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup in a champagne flute until dissolved.
2. Top with Champagne.
3. Garnish with lemon twist.

---

### Key Tips for Your Event:
- **Seasonal Finger Foods:** Pair Old Fashioneds with spiced nuts, Aviation with fruit skewers, and Paper Planes with cheese or charcuterie.
- **Bitters Variety:** Offer a "bitters bar" with Angostura, orange, chocolate, or cherry bitters for customization.
- **Glassware:** Use Art Deco-style glasses for Old Fashioneds and coupes for Aviation/French 75.

These cocktails honor the airport/reception theme, Kentucky’s bourbon heritage, and fall flavors. Happy hosting! ✈️🍹
