Absolutely. A dehydrator is a fantastic kitchen appliance. It's best to start out with fruits and vegetables. You can make apple slices, bananas, carrots, beets, and pineapple. You can also make jerky from beef, pork, or turkey. You can even dehydrate herbs to make your own homemade seasoning. There are also lots of unique snacks you can make, such as bacon, onion straws, and even fruit leather.
