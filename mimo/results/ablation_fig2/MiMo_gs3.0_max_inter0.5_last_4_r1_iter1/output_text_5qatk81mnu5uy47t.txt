Absolutely. Costa Rica is scuba diving paradise with clear tropical waters, vibrant coral reefs, and incredible marine life. Here’s how I’d plan an unforgettable trip for you and your husband:

First, let's talk about key locations. Tamarindo is a great starting point with easy shore diving, calm waters, and frequent turtle sightings. It's beginner-friendly and has good underwater visibility. Next is Cahuita National Park, which is more relaxed with beautiful snorkeling spots, accessible reefs, and a laid-back vibe. For a more adventurous option, Isla del Coco is remote and wild, with strong currents, deep walls, and unique long-finned dives. It requires a boat trip from Santa Teresa and is best for intermediate divers. 

Now for recommended dive sites. In Tamarindo, look for Playa Hermosa, which has shallow reefs perfect for beginners, and Concha, known for its coral gardens and turtles. In Cahuita, Los Sueños offers gentle slopes with lots of fish, and Punta Pez is great for snorkeling with rays and sea turtles. For Isla del Coco, Los Frailes is a deep drop-off with strong currents and big fish, and Playa Chiquita has shallow reefs and colorful fish, which is good for building confidence. 

Regarding equipment, since you started with short fins, I'd suggest sticking with those for most dives, especially in calm waters. But you could rent long fins for Isla del Coco if you want to challenge yourselves. Also, pack reef-safe sunscreen, a dive computer, and a rash guard. 

For guided tours, I recommend booking with local operators like Tamarindo Dive Center or Isla del Coco Dive Adventures. They offer guided tours, safety briefings, and opportunities to see marine life. 

Finally, a few tips: dive early in the morning for the best visibility. Take a rest day in between dives to avoid fatigue. And consider a night dive at Concha in Tamarindo to see bioluminescence and nocturnal creatures. 

This mix of easy sites and a bit of adventure should give you a fantastic, unforgettable experience while keeping things manageable and fun.
