### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients (Serves 4)
Here's the classic, simple recipe:

**For the Pasta & Sauce:**

*   **Spaghetti:** 400g (14 oz) - *Dried spaghetti is traditional.*
*   **Guanciale:** 200g (7 oz) - *Pancetta (cured pork belly) is a common substitute if guanciale isn't available.*
*   **Eggs:** 3 large whole eggs + 2 large egg yolks - *Room temperature works best.*
*   **Pecorino Romano Cheese:** 100g (about 1 cup) finely grated - *This is the traditional hard sheep's milk cheese. Parmigiano-Reggiano can be used if Pecorino isn't available, though it will be milder.*
*   **Black Pepper:** Freshly ground, to taste - *Essential for the authentic flavor.*
*   **Salt:** For the pasta water.

**Optional but Recommended:**

*   **Parmigiano-Reggiano:** 50g (about 1/2 cup) finely grated, to mix with the Pecorino for extra richness and complexity.
*   **Extra Virgin Olive Oil:** A drizzle (1-2 tbsp) to add richness and prevent sticking.

**Instructions (Brief Overview):**

1.  Cook spaghetti in well-salted boiling water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
2.  In a large bowl, vigorously whisk together the eggs, egg yolks, grated Pecorino (and Parmigiano if using), and a generous amount of black pepper.
3.  Fry the diced guanciale/pancetta in a large skillet over medium-low heat until crisp and golden. Remove from heat, leaving the fat in the pan.
4.  **Crucial Step:** Quickly toss the drained, hot spaghetti with the guanciale and its fat in the skillet over *very low heat*. This cooks the pasta fully and adds flavor.
5.  **Immediately remove the skillet from the heat source.** Quickly stir in the egg/cheese mixture. The residual heat will cook the eggs into a creamy sauce *without scrambling them*. Add splashes of reserved pasta water as needed to achieve a silky, pourable sauce that clings to the pasta.
6.  Serve immediately, topped with extra grated Pecorino and black pepper.

Enjoy your delicious Spaghetti Carbonara!
