You're welcome! Here's a quick recap of the three shrimp main courses I suggested:

1.  **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled & deveined
        *   8 oz pasta (like linguine)
        *   4 tbsp butter
        *   4-6 cloves garlic, minced
        *   1/2 cup dry white wine (or substitute chicken broth)
        *   1/2 cup heavy cream (or use half-and-half for lighter)
        *   1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
        *   1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
    *   **Notes:** Quick to make. Serve over pasta.

2.  **Shrimp Fra Diavolo**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled & deveined
        *   1 large onion, thinly sliced
        *   4 cloves garlic, minced
        *   1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
        *   1/4 cup red wine vinegar (or substitute white wine vinegar)
        *   1/4 cup olive oil
        *   1-2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat)
        *   1 tsp dried oregano
        *   Salt & pepper to taste
    *   **Notes:** Rich, tomato-based sauce. Simmer shrimp at the end.

3.  **Spicy Cajun Shrimp & Grits**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled & deveined
        *   1 cup quick-cooking grits (or stone-ground grits cooked extra time)
        *   1/2 cup chicken broth
        *   1/2 cup heavy cream
        *   1/4 cup chopped green onion
        *   1/4 cup chopped red onion
        *   1/4 cup chopped bell pepper (any color)
        *   2 cloves garlic, minced
        *   2 tbsp Cajun seasoning (like Tony Chachere's or your favorite blend)
        *   Salt & pepper to taste
        *   Butter or olive oil for sautéing
    *   **Notes:** Comforting Southern dish. Cook shrimp separately and serve over creamy grits.

All three shrimp dishes love spice and are flavorful! Choose the one that sounds easiest or most appealing to you to prepare. Good luck – your friend and her family will appreciate your kindness!
