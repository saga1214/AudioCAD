Okay, planning this dad-child theme park bash with ten groups going sounds like a blast! Minimizing mess while satisfying ten adults and ten kids (including sugar-free sodas and real calzones!) is a legit challenge. Let's build a cooler-friendly menu based on your awesome back-and-forth.

Key goals confirmed: Healthy base, minimal mess grab-and-go options, significant soda upgrade requested, substantial meal needed, real calzone celebration required, and a simple dessert solution. Hot day factor is crucial.

Here’s a practical shopping list designed for ten dads/kids, packing multiple coolers:

DRINKS (Focus: Hydration + Low-Sugar Refreshment + Requested Sug-Free Sodas)
*   Water Bottles: 30 Nitrile or BPA-free plastic bottles (10 dads get 3 bottles each - easy refills!)
*   Healthy Juice Boxes: 30 (Low-sugar options for kids on-the-go)
*   Sugar-Free Sodas: 30 (Choose flavors liked by adults/kids - check labels!)
*   Sports Drink Boxes (Optional): 10-15 (For select adults/kids needing electrolytes)
*   Ice Packs: Several large ones (Keep everything cold!)

SNACKS (Focus: Zero/Minimal Mess, Individual Servings)
*   Cheese Sticks: 30 (Different flavors if possible)
 Grapes: 30 cups (Pre-washed & ready!)
 Baby Carrots: 3 large bags (Individual hummus cups attach to bags)
 Pretzels: 3 bags Individual mini-bags (Small or trail mix bags)
 Trail Mix: 3 bags (Check for common allergens)
 Apple Slices: 30 (Pre-cut or pack whole apples & peeler)
 Whole Grapes: 30 (As backup or extra)
 Individual Hummus Cups: 15 (Single-serve pouches)
 Single-Serve Guacamole Cups: 10 (If group loves it)
 Granola Bars: 30 (Healthy, grab-and-go)
 Energy Bars: 10-15 (For dad energy boosts)

MAIN MEAL (Focus: Easy to Eat, Celebratory)
*   MINI Real Calzones: 20 (This is the star! Find a bakery/pizzeria that makes small (6-8 inch) versions. *Essential:* Pre-portion them into individual boxes/paper bags. No assembly needed, minimal mess!
   *   *Inside Suggestion:* Pepperoni/Mushroom, Cheese, Meatball, Veggie, maybe a couple different kinds.
*   Wrap Variations: 10-12 Individual wrapped wraps/sandwiches (Mix it up!)
   *   Veggie Hummus Wrap: Whole wheat tortilla, hummus, shredded carrots, cucumber, spinach.
   *   Chicken Caesar: Whole wheat wrap, grilled chicken, Caesar dressing, romaine, parmesan.
   Cupcakes: 24-30 (Bachelor/bachelorette theme optional) - Keep them wrapped tightly!
 Brownie Bites: 24 (Mini brownies are great for dessert) - Keep them wrapped tightly!
 Ice Packs: Several large ones (Critical for calzones/wraps)

DESSERT (Focus: Simple Celebration)
*   Skip the cake/pie assembly! Mini cupcakes or brownie bites are your clean, grab-and-go winners. They feel festive without the plate/dessert table hassle at a busy park.

COOLER PILING TIPS:
1.  Use multiple large coolers (ideationaly 2-3).
2.  Divide drinks and snacks roughly equally among coolers.
3.  Put *all* the mini calzones and wraps in one cooler, surrounded by ice packs.
4.  Pack grapes, carrots, and pretzels low in the snack cooler to prevent bruising.
5.  Label cooler sections if helpful ("Snacks," "Drinks," "Main Meal").
6.  Keep dry snacks separate from cold items if possible (but cooler is fine).
7.  Dads grab what they need from their designated cooler(s).

This plan hits all your points: Healthy base, sugar-free sodas, REAL celebratory calzones (mini!), minimal-mess structure, substantial meal options, and simple dessert. Dads can grab drinks/snacks kids love, and grooms getting his favorite calzone fix. Moms recharge guilt-free!

Have an absolutely epic theme park adventure – sun, runs, and maybe some minor rollercoaster screaming! Dads unite!
