Of course, let's go over it again. Chronic redness has many potential causes. Rosacea is a very common one, along with long-term sun damage and genetics.

Now, vasculitis is another possibility. It's an autoimmune disorder where the body's immune system mistakenly attacks the walls of blood vessels. Because these vessels are all over your body, the inflammation can affect many different organs, and skin rashes are a frequent symptom. Doctors often classify vasculitis based on the size of the vessel it's affecting: large vessels, medium vessels, or small vessels.

The symptoms can vary a lot depending on where the inflammation is. Common ones include a fever, feeling tired, and having rashes. Treatment usually involves medications to suppress the immune system, like steroids. The main risk is that the inflammation can cause permanent damage by narrowing the blood vessels, which can restrict blood flow to important organs.

And yes, because hair and nails are made from keratin and get nutrients from your blood, inflammation in the body, like from vasculitis, can definitely weaken them, leading to issues like thinning hair or weak nails.
