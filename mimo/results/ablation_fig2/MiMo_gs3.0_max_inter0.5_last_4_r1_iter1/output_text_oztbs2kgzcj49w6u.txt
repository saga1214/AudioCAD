Absolutely. The best place to start is the Lockport Canal Museum, which is located right inside the Lock 3 museum building. It's a fantastic place to learn the history of the canal, the engineering, and the local industry it created.

Another excellent spot is the Lockport Underground Railroad Heritage Center. It tells the powerful story of the city's role in the Underground Railroad, with many of the routes connecting directly to the canal. You can really feel the deep connection between the waterway and the city's past.
