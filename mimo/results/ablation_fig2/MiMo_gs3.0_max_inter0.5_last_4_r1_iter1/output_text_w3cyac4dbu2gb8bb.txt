Based on your situation, here's a simple solution that prioritizes speed and avoids the weather hassle:

**Do this:**  
1. **Dissolve instant coffee** in a small splash of hot water (about 2 tbsp coffee + 1 tbsp water) to make a strong concentrate.  
2. **Warm your milk** in the microwave (full-fat or Cornish—no need to boil).  
3. **Pour the warm milk into your mug**, then add the instant concentrate shot. Stir well.  

This gives you:  
✅ **Instant coffee** (convenient) blended with **rich milk** (tastes like a latte).  
✅ **No machine hassle** or clumping.  
✅ **No umbrella needed**—just a rain jacket if you must go out.  

**Bonus tip:** Add a dash of cream or a sprinkle of cocoa powder if you want extra luxury without extra cost.
