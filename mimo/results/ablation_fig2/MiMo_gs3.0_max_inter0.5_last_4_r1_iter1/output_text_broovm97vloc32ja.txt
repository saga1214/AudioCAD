That's a fantastic skill to have! In fact, it gives you a major advantage. If you can do the art as well as the writing, publishers are very likely to be interested in working with you.

The most direct path would be to look for publishers that specifically publish books by author-illustrators. You can find them by searching online for things like 'author-illustrator children's book publishers' or checking sites like Publishers Marketplace.

However, the traditional route is still a very good one. You would submit your manuscript and your illustrations separately to an agent. If an agent represents children's books, they will review your art. If they like it, they'll then submit the complete package to their list of publishers. This gives them their professional network and expertise to get your book seen.
