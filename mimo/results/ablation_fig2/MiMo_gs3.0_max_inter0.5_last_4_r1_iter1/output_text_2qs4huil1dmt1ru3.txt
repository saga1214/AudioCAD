Alright, breaking it down task by task first. Drywall supplies: joint compound small tub, say five to ten dollars. Sanding sandpaper sponge, a couple bucks. Putty knife six-inch, maybe eight to twelve. Primer and paint if matching your wall could be ten to twenty. Nails/adhesive caulk gun, around five to ten. If a new piece of molding is needed, that could be anywhere from ten to thirty dollars depending on the style. Rough drywall estimate: around thirty-five to seventy dollars.

Cake supplies: AP flour two cups, sugar a cup-and-a-half—that’s nothing. Baking powder teaspoons cheap. Two sticks butter. Three eggs milk vanilla powdered sugar for frosting cocoa if needed—all maybe under ten dollars. Pan rental is free. Rough cake estimate: ten to twenty dollars.

Putting it together, shopping trip total: drywall task could be forty to eighty dollars, cake thirty to fifty. Grand total estimate: seventy to one hundred thirty dollars. It really depends on the sizes of the molding and wall stain area, and the style of cake and pans. Before you go, just double-check any measurements for the molding. But seventy to one hundred thirty should be a safe range. Let me know if you hit any unexpected costs!
