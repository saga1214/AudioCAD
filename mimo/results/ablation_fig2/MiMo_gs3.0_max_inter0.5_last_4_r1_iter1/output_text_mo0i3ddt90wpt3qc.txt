You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which are gluten-free. Another brand that you might like is Saffron Road, as they have gluten-free and often nut-free trail mixes.

For some other ideas, rice cakes with almond butter are a simple and gluten-free combo. Fresh fruit like apples, bananas, or berries are always refreshing. And popcorn can also be made gluten-free with caution, or you can find certified gluten-free brands.
