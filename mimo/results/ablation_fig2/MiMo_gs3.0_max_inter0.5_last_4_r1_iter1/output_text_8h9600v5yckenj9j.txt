For candy tomorrow, grab classics like Reese’s Cups, Hershey’s Kisses, and Skittles—those are always safe bets. You could also get gummies, like Starbursts or fruit gummies, and maybe some chocolate pumpkins or Halloween-themed boxes of candy for extra spooky vibes. Try to stock up on individually wrapped candies so guests can take as much as they want and you won’t have leftovers. If you’re worried about allergies again with artificial dyes, just double-check labels for non-colored options—dark chocolate or plain candies are usually dye-free. And don’t forget to get some Halloween-themed paper or plastic bags to hand them out in—makes it extra fun!
