You are absolutely right that books from the 1940s and 1950s are significantly rarer, which is a major reason for their high value.

Here are ten significant books from those eras, all known to be in the five-hundred-dollar range or less, that you can still find in mid-grade condition.

Number one: *Tales of Suspense* number ninety, which is the first appearance of Doctor Strange.
Number two: *Thor* number one hundred thirty-five, the first appearance of Loki.
Number three: *Captain America Comics* number four hundred ninety-eight, which is the last Captain America comic ever printed.
Number four: *The Amazing Adult Adventures of Spider-Man* number one, his first solo series.
Number five: *Amazing Spider-Man* number one hundred twenty-two, the first appearance of Kraven the Hunter.
Number six: *The Avengers* number four, which is the first appearance of the Vision and Wanda and Pietro Maximoff.
Number seven: *Fantastic Four* number twenty-two, which is the first appearance of Galactus.
Number eight: *Avengers* number four, the first appearance of the Dark Avengers.
Number nine: *The Incredible Hulk* number three hundred twenty-one, the first appearance of the Red Hulk.
And number ten: *Silver Surfer* number one, which is the first appearance of the Silver Surfer.
