### 🧀 **Top Savory Picks:**
1. **Mixed Nuts & Seeds**  
   - *Why:* Protein-packed, no prep. Vending machines often have pre-packaged bags.  
   - *Snack Density:* ~50 pieces per 4x6" container.  
2. **Cheese Strips/Cubes**  
   - *Why:* Portable, melty, and non-sweet. Pair with crackers.  
   - *Snack Density:* 8-10 pieces per 4x6" container.  
3. **Pretzels or Rice Cakes**  
   - *Why:* Crunchy, low-sugar, and versatile for dips.  
   - *Snack Density:* 12-15 pieces per 4x6" container.  

### 🍎 **Savory-Sweet Hybrids (if you're feeling adventurous):**
- **Dark Chocolate-Covered Almonds**  
  - *70%+ cocoa* keeps it bitter-sweet, not sugary.  
- **Spiced Choc Chunks**  
  - Cinnamon/cayenne-dusted chocolate satisfies cravings without sweetness.  

### 🥕 **Fresh & Crunchy:**
- **Vending Machine Fruit** (apples, bananas, clementines)  
  - *Bonus:* Wash and slice them for a colorful table display!  
- **Veggie Sticks + Hummus Cup**  
  - *Snack Density:* 1 cup hummus + 1 cup veggies fits in a standard 4x6" container.  

### 💡 **Pro Tips for Vending Machines:**  
- **Look for "Nutrition" Labels:** Avoid high-sugar/syrup-coated items.  
- **Portion Control:** Buy single-serve packs to avoid waste.  
- **Thematic Nights:** Use your shelves/drawer for organized snacks (e.g., "Nut Zone" or "Cheese Cave").
