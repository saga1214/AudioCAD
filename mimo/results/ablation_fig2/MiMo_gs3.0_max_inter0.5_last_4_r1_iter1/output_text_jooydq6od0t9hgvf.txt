### 🥃 Vodka Mixer (Simple & Refreshing)
- **Classic Mojito**  
  *Recipe:* 2 oz vodka + 1 oz fresh lime juice + 6 oz sparkling soda (club soda or tonic) + mint sprig + 2-3 fresh mint leaves + 1 sugar cube (optional). Muddle mint/sugar, add vodka/lime, top with soda.  
  *Why:* Light, refreshing, universally liked, and works for all age groups. No soda allergy risk.

### 🥃 Argentinian-Style Fernet (Bold & Herbal)
- **Fernet & Soda**  
  *Recipe:* 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca + 1 oz honey or simple syrup + 3-4 dashes Angostura bitters + 4-6 oz soda water (club soda). Stir, strain into ice-filled glass.  
  *Why:* Classic Argentine preparation. Herbal/spicy notes balance the soda’s fizz (no carbonation-heavy base).
