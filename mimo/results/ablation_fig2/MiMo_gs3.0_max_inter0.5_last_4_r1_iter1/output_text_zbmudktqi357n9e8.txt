Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time would definitely be during their *verano*, which is their summer—that's from about June to August. That's when the foliage is at its peak. You've got the famous colors of Oaxaca with all the huizopines, which are like little wild chilies, and the hillsides just covered in red and orange. The Huasteca region has these incredible waterfalls, which would be great for your river scenes we were just talking about. And of course, you have the coastal autumn colors. Just... uh, try to budget for local supplies, like Mexican pigments, and definitely plan for some sketching sessions. The light is just... *chef's kiss*... perfect for watercolors. You're going to have an amazing time!
