For winter chicks on concrete, the key is insulation. The best solution is to secure that rubber matting we talked about on the floor. Then, for bedding, use what's called the deep litter approach again with large-flake pine shavings. Just continuously add fresh ones on top of the composting layer below to generate natural heat.

To keep rodents out, clean the sand frequently with a rake or shovel, and make sure the rubber mats are tightly secured to prevent them from getting under the sand. Ventilation high up remains very important.
