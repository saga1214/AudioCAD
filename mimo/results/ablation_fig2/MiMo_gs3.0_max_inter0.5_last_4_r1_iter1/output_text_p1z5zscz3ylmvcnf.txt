Okay, so you're looking for classic cars that could be a good investment for your father-in-law. Given his old-school American pride and active nature, this could be a really interesting way to get him interested in something new while still feeling like he’s in control. I'd definitely suggest looking into cars that are both iconic and have a strong history of appreciating in value. Some of the top ones that come to mind are the Ford Mustang, especially the first-generation from the 60s. Then there's the Chevrolet Camaro, also a huge classic. The Porsche 911 is another one that's always in demand. You've also got the classic Jaguar E-Type, which is super desirable, and the Dodge Charger from the 70s. All of those tend to hold their value really well and can even go up quite a bit over time. It's definitely a safer bet than just leaving money in a bank, and it’s something he could enjoy as an investment and even a hobby.
