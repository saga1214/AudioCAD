Based on your love of warm weather, swimming, your daughter's birthday adventure, and your need for accommodating celiac-friendly dining, here’s a **4-5 day warm-weather itinerary for Big Sur**—Northern California’s ultimate coastal escape. With warm temps (70s-80s°F), beach swimming, and stunning scenery, it’s perfect for your diet progress and cover-up-ready attire!

---

### **Why Big Sur?**  
- **Warm Weather**: Coastal climate stays mild (no Northern CA cold!).  
- **Swimming**: Paddleboarding, kayaking, and beach swims at **Pfeiffer Beach** or **Nepenthe**.  
- **Celiac-Friendly**: Many restaurants accommodate gluten-free needs (call ahead!).  
- **Adventure**: Hikes, wildlife, and no heights required!  
- **Dining**: Seafood-focused spots with dedicated gluten-free prep.  

---

### **Itinerary: 4 Days in Big Sur**  
*(Accommodation: SCAELA Hotel & Spa, Big Sur—gluten-free kitchen, beach access, pool)*  

#### **Day 1: Arrival & Coastal Welcome**  
- **Travel**: Drive from Northern CA (rent a minivan/SUV for luggage).  
- **Stay**: SCAELA Hotel (gluten-free kitchen, heated pool).  
- **Dinner**: **Big Sur Bakery** (gluten-free donuts & pastries).  
- **Activity**: Sunset walk at **Point Lobos** (easy coastal trails).  

#### **Day 2: Beaches & Wildlife**  
- **Morning**: Paddleboard at **Nepenthe Beach** (swimming + wildlife viewing).  
- **Lunch**: **The Post** (gluten-free options; ocean views).  
- **Afternoon**: Kayak with **Big Sur Adventures** (no heights!).  
- **Dinner**: **Nepenthe Restaurant** (reserve gluten-free table; whale-watching deck).  

#### **Day 3: Scenic Drives & Hot Springs**  
- **Morning**: **Bixby Bridge** + **McWay Falls** (hike to waterfall).  
- **Lunch**: **McWay Cafe** (gluten-free sandwiches; oceanfront).  
- **Afternoon**: **Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park** (beach walk + fern canyons).  
- **Evening**: **Big Sur Hot Springs** (warm pools at **Surfland Resort**—book ahead!).  

#### **Day 4: Wildlife & Farewell Swim**  
- **Morning**: **Pfeiffer Beach Tides** (swimming + sea otter spotting).  
- **Lunch**: **Big Sur Pizza** (gluten-free crust available).  
- **Afternoon**: **McCain Creek Falls** (easy waterfall hike).  
- **Dinner**: **Big Sur Bakery** (gluten-free cake for your daughter’s birthday).  

---
