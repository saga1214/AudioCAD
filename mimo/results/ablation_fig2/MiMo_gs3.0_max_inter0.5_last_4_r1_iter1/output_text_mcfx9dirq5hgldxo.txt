For Madrid, tapas are a top recommendation. Think things like patatas bravas... which are basically fried potatoes with a spicy sauce, or jamón ibérico, which is a really high-quality cured ham.

Paella is popular, too, but they might have a Spanish-style version called 'paella madrileña'. And of course, there's churros, which are these fried dough sticks they dip in hot chocolate.
