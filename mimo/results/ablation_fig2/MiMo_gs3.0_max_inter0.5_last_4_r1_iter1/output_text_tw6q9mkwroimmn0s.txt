That sounds like a fantastic theme! Here are five ocean-inspired meals (shellfish-free for your fiancächère) and five sweet desserts, incorporating Mango, Spicy food, and your Shrimp Craving:  

### **Ocean-Themed Meals**  
1. **Mango & Shrimp Tacos**  
   - Spicy shrimp cooked with lime and chili, topped with Mango salsa and a sprinkle of fresh cilantro. Serve in warm tortillas.  

2. **Spicy Coconut Shrimp Curry**  
   - Shrimp simmered in a rich, fragrant coconut milk curry with a touch of Mango puree for sweetness. Serve with steamed rice.  

3. **Grilled Shrimp with Mango-Habanero Salsa**  
   - Lightly charred shrimp paired with a vibrant Mango-Habanero salsa (adjust spice level).  

4. **Shrimp & Mango Coconut Noodle Bowl**  
   - Stir-fried shrimp and Mango strips in a spicy coconut broth, topped with rice noodles, avocado, and a squeeze of lime.  

5. **Diabetes-Friendly Shrimp & Mango Ceviche**  
   - Fresh shrimp "cooked" in citrus, mixed with diced Mango, red onion, and a hint of jalapeño for spice. Serve with gluten-free chips.  

### **Sweet Ocean-Themed Desserts**  
1. **Mango Sticky Rice with Toasted Coconut Shrimp**  
   - Warm Mango sticky rice topped with crispy, lightly seasoned coconut shrimp.  

2. **Spicy Chocolate Lava Cake with Mango Coulis**  
   - Rich chocolate cake with a molten center, served with a drizzle of Mango coulis (sweet mango purée).  

3. **Mango & Coconut Panna Cotta with Shrimp Garnish**  
   - Light, creamy panna cotta infused with Mango and coconut, topped with a few small grilled shrimp.  

4. **Mango & Chili Sorbet with Shrimp Ice Cream**  
   - A sweet-savory twist: Mango chili sorbet layered with a light shrimp-flavored ice cream (dairy-free if needed).  

5. **Ocean Treasure Parfaits**  
   - Layers of Mango yogurt, granola, chia seeds, and a few tiny toasted shrimp for crunch.  

### **Bonus Tips**  
- For diabetes-friendly dishes, focus on lean proteins (shrimp, fish), fiber (rice, veggies), and controlled portions of Mango.  
- Keep spices like chili, cumin, and turmeric to add flavor without extra sugar.
