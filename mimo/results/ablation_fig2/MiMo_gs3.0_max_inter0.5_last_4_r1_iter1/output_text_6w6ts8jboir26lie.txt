That's a great idea—cooking can be such a calming escape. Here are five simple, satisfying dinner ideas you can whip up with grocery store staples:

### 1. **Garlic-Herb Pasta with Cherry Tomatoes**  
   - Sauté garlic in olive oil, toss in cooked pasta (spaghetti or linguine), and top with halved cherry tomatoes. Finish with fresh parsley, black pepper, and a squeeze of lemon.  
   - *Why it’s great*: Minimal cleanup, maximum flavor.  

### 2. **Creamy Tomato-Basil Soup**  
   - Blend canned crushed tomatoes with vegetable or chicken broth, a splash of cream (or coconut milk for vegan), and a handful of fresh basil. Serve with crusty bread.  
   - *Bonus*: Perfect for dipping your grilled cheese (see below!).  

### 3. **Loaded Grilled Cheese**  
   - Make a classic grilled cheese, but upgrade it: add caramelized onions, roasted garlic, or even crispy prosciutto inside. Dip in the tomato soup for a cozy combo.  
   - *Pro tip*: Use a cast-iron skillet for extra crispiness.  

### 4. **Greek Salad with Grilled Chicken**  
   - Toss romaine with cucumber, red onion, Kalamata olives, feta, and a lemon-oregano dressing. Top with grilled chicken (or chickpeas for plant-based).  
   - *Why it’s great*: Refreshing, balanced, and ready in 20 minutes.  

### 5. **Sheet-Pan Salmon & Roasted Veggies**  
   - Place salmon fillets on a baking sheet with broccoli, asparagus, or potatoes. Drizzle with olive oil, lemon, salt, and pepper. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 15–20 minutes.  
   - *Minimal effort*: One pan, one seasoning, big payoff.
