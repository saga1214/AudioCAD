For a 'Retro Tech' theme lunch, you could do a 'Dial-Up Delight Plate.' The main dish would be a simple turkey and cheese sandwich, because it’s a classic and easy to eat. For a snack, you could have salted peanuts, calling them 'modem chips,' and maybe some fruit slices labeled 'data packets.' Another idea is a 'Floppy Disk Feast.' For this, you could serve a baked potato split in half and filled with chili, representing the two sides of a floppy disk. The snack could be pretzel sticks, which you could call 'memory sticks,' and some carrot sticks labeled 'cables.' Both ideas are simple, thematic, and easy to eat.
