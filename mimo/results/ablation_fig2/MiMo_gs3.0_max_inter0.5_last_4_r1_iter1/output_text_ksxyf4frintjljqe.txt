Maybe this Thanksgiving you can find a way to remember her love for baking brownies and peach cobbler without re-triggering the family. Perhaps sharing favorite stories or creating a new ritual together. And it’s OK if you skip those desserts or do something simpler—whatever helps the most right now. Just be gentle with yourselves.

As for your dessert idea, how about chocolate lava cakes in a pan? They cook really fast—just warm up your oven to 425, grease a few ramekins, and mix butter, eggs, sugar, flour, cocoa, and chocolate chips. Pour it in, and bake for about ten minutes. They should be super gooey in the middle. Serve them warm with some vanilla ice cream on top. It’s a little something different that everyone might enjoy.
