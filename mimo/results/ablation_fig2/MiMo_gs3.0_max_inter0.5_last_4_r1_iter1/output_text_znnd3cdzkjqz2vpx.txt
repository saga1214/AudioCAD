But let me just double-check, you'll also need a stud finder, a measuring tape, a pencil, and some shims. You mentioned finding studs, but always double-checking with a stud finder is a good idea. A measuring tape is crucial for making sure all the cabinets are the same distance from the wall and for getting their sizes right. Shims are those little plastic or wooden pieces that you'll use under the base cabinets to get them perfectly level.
