Alright, so before you start filming, here are some basic things to think about. 

First, get permission from the facility. Assisted living places might not want recordings, so you’ll need to ask ahead of time. Also, ask the audience if it’s okay, especially foster kids or hospital patients. 

Next, set up your phone or camera on a stable surface—like a tripod. Lighting is important, so try to position it near a window or use a ring light if you’ve got one. You’ll want to record vertically, like 9 by 16, so it’s easier to edit later. 

Make sure to tell your puppets to stay in the frame and keep the camera steady. After the show, you can review the footage to see if it’s usable. 

Then, once you’ve got good clips, you can edit them—maybe add some music or text. Upload to a platform like YouTube, but label it as for kids. 

That’s the basic answer. Just plan ahead, get consent, and film carefully.
