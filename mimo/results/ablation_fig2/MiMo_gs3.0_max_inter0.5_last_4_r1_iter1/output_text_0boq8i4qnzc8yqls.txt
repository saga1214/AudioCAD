### Why this works:  
1. **Warm but not heavy**: Focuses on shared memories ("all our Christmases together") instead of direct emotion.  
2. **Acknowledges excitement**: "Can't wait to see you" shows eagerness without implying overstaying concerns from earlier phone call.  
3. **Light and personal**: "Missing you already" feels genuine but casual.  
4. **Gift reference**: Nods to her present while keeping the focus on you/your time together.
