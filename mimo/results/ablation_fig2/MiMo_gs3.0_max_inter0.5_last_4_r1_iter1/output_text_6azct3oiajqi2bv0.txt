Based on avoiding fabric damage and Secman climate needs, here are the best ways to stick something to your jacket lining:

1.  **Safety Pins (Best for small items):**
    *   **How:** Use heavy-duty or all-metal safety pins. Slide them through the item and the jacket lining, threading the pin through the outer shell fabric *underneath* the lining first. **Crucially, avoid pins near seams or weak areas.**
    *   **Pros:** Secure, reusable, invisible when done right.
    *   **Cons:** Requires careful placement.

2.  **Low-Temp Hot Glue Gun (Best for flat items like tags or patches):**
    *   **How:** Use a glue gun designed for **low-temperature** materials. Apply a *tiny* dot of glue to the item, press firmly onto the lining, and hold for 15-30 seconds. **Test on an inconspicuous seam first!** Avoid hot glue guns meant for construction (they can melt synthetics).
    *   **Pros:** Strong bond, invisible when dry.
    *   **Cons:** Risk of melting fabric if not low-temp glue, needs careful application.

3.  **Adhesive Fabric Glue/Tape (Best for patches or small items):**
    *   **How:** Use specialized fabric glue or double-sided adhesive tape designed for clothing. Follow product instructions carefully. Apply a small amount of glue or tape to the item, press firmly onto the lining, and hold for the recommended time.
    *   **Pros:** Strong, flexible bond, often removable.
    *   **Cons:** Can be visible, needs setting time.

4.  **Grip Adhesive (For flat surfaces like a nameplate):**
    *   **How:** Use a strong, non-permanent adhesive like a fabric-specific "grip" glue or a high-quality, non-removable fabric glue. Apply sparingly and press firmly.
    *   **Pros:** Strong hold.
    *   **Cons:** Can be tricky to remove cleanly if needed.

5.  **Carefully Used Fabric Glue Stick (For small items):**
    *   **How:** Use a fabric-specific glue stick (like Aleene's Fabric Fusion). Apply a *very thin* line of glue to the item, press firmly onto the lining, and hold for a minute or two. Test first!
    *   **Pros:** Easy to use, removable.
    *   **Cons:** Less strong than other methods, can be visible.

**Things to AVOID:**

*   **Regular Tape (Scotch Tape, Duct Tape):** Will tear the fabric, leave residue, and be visible.
*   **Strong Adhesives like Super Glue (Cyanoacrylate):** Will likely melt the synthetic lining/fabric, cause permanent damage, and be extremely difficult to remove. **Do not use!**
*   **Hot Melt Glue Gun (Standard Construction Type):** Will melt most winter fabrics and linings.
*   **Double-Sided Tape (Packaging Tape):** Will tear fabric and leave residue.

**Recommendation:** For Secman climate durability, **safety pins** (used carefully) or **low-temp hot glue** (test first!) are the most reliable for most items. For patches or small flat items, **fabric glue/tape** is excellent. **Always test on a hidden seam first!** Good luck!
