Of course! With a two-bedroom apartment, a baby, and a new dog on the way, I'd suggest starting with some shorter trips. Vancouver's nearby islands are a great place to begin. The Salt Spring Island ferry is a short drive, and it's known for its charming little towns, beautiful farms, and especially its award-winning cheese tasting. Then there's Galiano Island, which is a bit quieter and perfect for hiking and visiting a lovely bookstore. For a more lively day trip, Vancouver Island is a classic choice, where you could go whale watching, visit a famous chocolate factory, or hike to a viewpoint. If you're up for a longer drive, there's the Okanagan Valley, famous for its lakes and wineries. And finally, for a true adventure, you could head up the Sea to Sky Highway to Whistler. You can go skiing in the winter or hiking in the summer, and there's even a beautiful blackcomb glacier to explore. All of these are great for road trips with a family, and the food and activities are fantastic!
