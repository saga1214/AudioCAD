The term 'forever chemicals' means they do stick around in the body for a long time, but it doesn't mean exposure can't be reduced. It's best to focus on prevention.

Okay, here are the five products to avoid to minimize your exposure:

First, non-stick cookware. You can switch to stainless steel or cast iron.

Second, grease-resistant food packaging, like fast-food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags.

Third, things labeled stain-resistant or waterproof, such as carpets, furniture, and some outdoor clothing.

Fourth is cosmetics and even some types of dental floss.

And finally since these can get into tap water, using a certified water filter can really help.
