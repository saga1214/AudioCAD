### 1. **Treat Recommendations**
   - **Best Flavors:**  
     - **Chicken:** Universally loved by puppies (look for plain, skinless chicken breast).  
     - **Beef:** High-value treat for most dogs.  
     - **Cheese (Italian roots!):** Small pieces of mild cheddar, mozzarella, or parmesan work well.  
     - **Liver:** Affordable and tasty (ensure it’s dog-safe, not human-grade).  
     - **Freeze-Dried:** Adds crunch and long-lasting appeal (e.g., freeze-dried chicken or beef).  
   - **Avoid:** Salt, spices, onions, grapes, or chocolate.  
   - **Tip:** Use tiny pieces during training—puppies have small appetites!

---

### 2. **Clicker Training Explained**
   Clicker training uses **operant conditioning** with three steps:  
   - **Step 1: Mark the Behavior**  
     When Cosmo does something right (e.g., sits), click the clicker immediately to mark the exact moment.  
     *Sound: "Click"*  
   - **Step 2: Reward Immediately**  
     Give a treat *within 1 second* after the click. The click = "Great job!"  
   - **Step 3: Repeat**  
     Practice short sessions (5-10 minutes). Gradually add steps (e.g., "sit" → "stay").  

   **Why it works:**  
   The click bridges the gap between behavior and reward, making training faster and more precise.  

---

### 3. **Crate Training: Making it Cozy & Safe**
   **Goal:** Crate = Happy place (not punishment).  
   - **Start Small:** Place Cosmo’s favorite toy/blanket inside. Let him explore freely.  
   - **Positive Association:**  
     - Feed meals in the crate.  
     - Reward calm behavior with treats.  
     - Use a baby gate initially, then gradually increase time.  
   - **Never use it for punishment.**  
   - **If he resists:** Keep sessions short (5 mins), end before he panics, and return to positive interactions.  

---

### 4. **Potty Training Tips**
   - **Schedule:** Take Cosmo out every 30-60 mins (especially after waking, eating, or playing).  
   - **Accidents:** Clean with enzymatic cleaner (e.g., Nature’s Miracle) to remove odors. No scolding—just tidy up.  
   - **Praise Rewards:** Give treats/cheers when he goes outside!  

---

### 5. First Vet Visit: What to Expect  
   Your vet will likely:  
   - Check ears, teeth, eyes, heart, and breathing.  
   - Weigh Cosmo and discuss growth.  
   - Discuss vaccines, heartworm/flea prevention, and microchipping.  
   - Give a deworming treatment (puppies often need 2-3).  
   - Recommend a schedule for vaccinations (e.g., every 3-4 weeks).  

---

### 6. **Recall Training for Escapes**  
   If Cosmo runs out:  
   - **Stay calm:** Yelling attracts him *away*.  
   - **Call his name excitedly:** Use his favorite toy/treat.  
   - **Reward wildly:** When he returns, shower him with praise, play, and treats.  
   - **Practice:** Start indoors, then in your yard. Gradually increase distance.
