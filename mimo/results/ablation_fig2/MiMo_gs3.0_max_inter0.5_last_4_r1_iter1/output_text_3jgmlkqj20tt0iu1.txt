Approaching that conversation can be difficult, especially given your stated anxiety in face-to-face meetings. Let's talk through two common strategies.

First, during annual company performance reviews is usually the most ideal time. It aligns perfectly with the raise evaluation cycles, and the conversation is already framed to be constructive.

A second option, if the annual review feels too far away, is to schedule a separate meeting. You could say something like, 'I'd like to discuss my performance and future goals. Would you have 15 minutes sometime this week?' This gives you a dedicated, focused time to talk.
