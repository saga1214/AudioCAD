Absolutely! Let’s dive deeper into that bold, umami-forward movie night menu with unique yet manageable choices for 15 foodie friends. Here’s a detailed mix of simple staples and adventurous highlights:

### **Simple & Crowd-Pleasing Staples**  
1. **Build-Your-Own "Umami Bowl" Station**  
   - Base: Cabbage, nori strips, sesame seeds, and crispy wonton chips.  
   - Fillings: Kimchi, spicy tuna, tofu, edamame, roasted peanuts, and sweet chili sauce.  
   - Unique twist: Offer a tamarind-mango salsa for sweetness.  

2. **Gochujang Glazed Roasted Chickpeas**  
   - Simple to batch-cook: Toss chickpeas with gochujang paste, sesame oil, and a touch of maple syrup. Roast until crispy.  

3. **Miso Soup Cups**  
   - Pre-portioned cups with kombu, wakame, and tofu. Serve with chopsticks.  

### **Unique & Bold Choices**  
4. **Dried Seafood & Kimchi Mix**  
   - A savory trail mix: Dried squid, sweet shrimp, kimchi, gochujang-spiced nuts, and black sesame seeds.  

5. **"Natto or Notto" Mini Skewers**  
   - For the brave: Skewers of marinated natto (fermented soybeans) with pickled ginger and spicy mayo.  
   - Softer option: Tuna-mayo on cucumber slices.  

6. **Yuzu & Chili Popcorn**  
   - Mix classic popcorn with citrusy yuzu powder, chili flakes, and a pinch of smoked salt.  

7. **Sweet Potato & Black Bean Tacos**  
   - Roast sweet potatoes with smoked paprika, then stuff into corn tortillas with black beans, avocado crema, and pickled jalapeños.  

### **Interactive & Showstopper Elements**  
8. **Miso-Glazed Honeycomb**  
   - Japanese-style honeycomb candy: Sweet, crunchy, and chewy. Serve with a drizzle of spicy mayo for contrast.  

9. **"Spicy Tamarind" Fruit Skewers**  
   - Skewer grapes, strawberries, and pineapple. Dip in Japanese spicy tamarind sauce (available online or at Asian markets).  

10. **Matcha-Lemongrass Rice Balls**  
    - Small mochi balls filled with matcha rice and lemongrass-marinated shrimp.  

### **Serving & Setup Tips**  
- **Label everything**: Use small cards to explain flavors (e.g., "Kimchi Seaweed with Soy & Sesame").  
- **Dip Bar**: Set up a station with spicy mayo, yuzu aioli, and tamarind glaze.  
- **Clean-Up**: Use disposable bamboo skewers, compostable containers, and reusable cloth napkins.
