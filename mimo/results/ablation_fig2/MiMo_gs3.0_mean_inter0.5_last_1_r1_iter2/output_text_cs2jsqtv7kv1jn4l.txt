Okay, let's see. You want to surprise your daughter with a new gaming console after her mom took away online games. That's a great idea!

Here are five popular gaming consoles you could look at. First, there's the PlayStation 5, which is great for racing and adventure games. Second is the Xbox Series X or S, which is also very popular and has lots of good multiplayer options. Third, there's the Nintendo Switch, which is perfect for playing together with handheld mode and a lot of family-friendly games. Fourth is the PC, which is very powerful and can play almost any game, but it might be more expensive. And fifth, there's the Steam Deck, which is a handheld PC that plays games from Steam, but it's less common.

Considering you want to play racing and adventure games together, the PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, or the Nintendo Switch are probably your best bets. They all have great local multiplayer options. The PC would also work, but it's a bigger setup. The Switch is probably the easiest to get started with since it has its own built-in screen.
