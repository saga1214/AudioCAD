Alright, let me quickly summarize those two yogurt analogies for you. 

The first one was the 'throwing a party' analogy. In that, Milk was the big empty room, Good bacteria were the special party guests, keeping it warm energized them. The tanginess came from sugar they ate, linking arms was them convincing others into a giant group hug, which thickened and tangified the milk into yogurt.

The second one was the 'net' analogy. Milk was a disorganized crowd, Bacteria were tiny team coaches, warmth energized them, Tanginess was sugar uniforms, the flavoring gave the group an identity, and the net itself was a mesh made from the milk's proteins. It was bacteria linking those proteins together to trap liquid, which made the yogurt thick and solid.
