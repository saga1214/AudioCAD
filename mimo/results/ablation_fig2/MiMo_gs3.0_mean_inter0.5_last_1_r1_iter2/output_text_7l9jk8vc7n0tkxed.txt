- **Technique:** Use a standard plunger (flat or cup). Create a *tight seal* over the drain, apply firm pressure, and pump vigorously 15–20 times. Pause, check for clearing. Repeat.

- **Wire hanger + needle-nose pliers:**  
  1. Straighten a hanger (or bend into a hook).  
  2. Use needle-nose pliers to grip the hook end.  
  3. Insert carefully into the drain. Gently probe around to snag debris. Pull slowly.  
- **Zip tie or wire:**  
  1. Tie a zip tie around a stiff wire (e.g., from a toolbox item).
