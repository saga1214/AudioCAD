It sounds like there's a little mix-up. Let me clarify. The truly classic ratio for a Boulevardier is one and a quarter for the whiskey, versus one ounce each for the vermouth and Campari. The version your friend makes, with equal parts of everything, is also traditional and very popular, it's just a different flavor profile. Now, as for the Last Word you mentioned, that's an equal-parts drink of its own kind: three-quarters of an ounce each of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime juice. So to recap: Boulevardier is whiskey, sweet vermouth, and Campari. Last Word is gin, Chartreuse, and maraschino. You can pick whichever one you like more!
