**Itinerary: Palm Springs Luxury Escape (4-5 Days)**

*   **Why Palm Springs?** Guaranteed warm weather (often 70s-80sF), world-class golf/swimming, stunning mid-century modern architecture, thermal springs, and incredibly accommodating resorts for families and dietary needs. Your SUV gives you freedom to explore canyons or nearby Temecula wine country.

*   **Accommodation:** **The Ritz-Carlton, Palm Springs**. Impeccable service, stunning architecture, multiple pools (including an adults-only one), a fantastic spa, and excellent dining options where you can easily request gluten-free with notice. It's a perfect blend of luxury and family-friendly amenities.

*   **Getting Around:** Your SUV is your best friend! Palm Springs is built for driving. You'll use it for:
    *   Exploring the resort grounds and nearby trails.
    *   Day trips to Temecula Wine Country (about 45 mins south).
    *   Visiting Morongo Canyon Preserve or Palm Canyon Drive.
    *   Relaxing at the resort pool after driving.

*   **The Itinerary:**

    *   **Day 1: Arrival & Relaxation**
        *   Arrive at Ritz-Carlton, Palm Springs. Check-in, settle in.
        *   Head straight to the pool area. Swim, sunbathe, enjoy the vibe.
        *   Resort dining for dinner. Communicate Celiac needs clearly.
        *   Early night to adjust.

    *   **Day 2: Desert Glamour & Poolside Perfection**
        *   Enjoy resort breakfast/brunch.
        *   Tour the stunning mid-century modern architecture on-site or nearby (like the Marguerite House).
        *   **Afternoon:** Rent bikes or golf carts and explore Palm Springs Aerial Tramway area (views from the top are incredible, accessible via tram/bus). Or, visit the Palm Springs Art Museum.
        *   **Evening:** Resort spa treatments for everyone? Or just unwinding by the pool with a drink. Dinner at the resort.

    *   **Day 3: Adventure & Antelope Canyon (Day Trip)**
        *   **Morning:** Drive to **Antelope Canyon** (Upper or Lower). A MUST-SEE. Requires booking timed entry tickets WELL in advance – do this immediately! It's magical, not height-dependent, and feels utterly unique. Pack hats, water, and sunscreen.
        *   **Lunch:** Quick stop at a nearby cafe or pack snacks.
        *   **Afternoon:** Drive to **Haleakalā State Park** (about 1.5 hours east). Hike the Sliding Sands Trail for stunning views of the volcanic crater and desert landscape. No heights here either!
        *   **Evening:** Return to Ritz-Carlton. Resort dinner.

    *   **Day 4: Luxury & Local Flavors**
        *   **Morning:** Resort activities – maybe a spa class, tennis, or just poolside reading.
        *   **Lunch:** Explore downtown Palm Springs! Visit the **Palm Springs Cultural Center** (often has great exhibits) and grab gluten-free lunch at **The Mess** (known for creative GF dishes) or **Palm Springs Farmers Market** (if on a Saturday).
        *   **Afternoon:** Optional: **Palm Springs Aerial Tramway** ride for breathtaking desert mountain views (not for the faint-hearted heights-wise, but the views are spectacular).
        *   **Evening:** Farewell dinner at the Ritz. Celebrate the trip!

    *   **Day 5 (Optional Extension):**
        *   **Wine Country:** Drive to **Temecula** (about 45-60 mins). Visit a few lovely wineries (many have excellent gluten-free options – ask!). Enjoy wine tasting and local food.
        *   **Return:** Depart in the afternoon.

*   **Packing List Additions for Trip:**
    *   **Swimwear:** Pack your favorite bathing suits!
    *   **Resort Wear:** Light, comfortable linen or cotton outfits for evenings – think flowy dresses, linen shirts, shorts.
    *   **Daywear:** Breathable pants, skirts, lightweight jackets for cooler mornings/evenings, wide-brimmed hats, sunglasses, sunscreen.
    *   **Footwear:** Flip-flops/sandals, comfortable walking shoes, maybe a pair of dressier sandals for dinners.
    *   **Celiac Essentials:** Bring your own certified gluten-free snacks (energy bars, nuts, fruit) for the car trip. Pack your own certified GF condiments. A small card explaining Celiac disease might be helpful if needed. Trish's donuts are a must!
    *   **Other:** Reusable water bottle, sunscreen, insect repellent (for canyon hikes), basic first-aid kit, camera.

This Palm Springs itinerary balances relaxation, adventure, luxury, and your specific needs (warmth, swimming, Celiac safety, diet-friendly treats, family fun).
