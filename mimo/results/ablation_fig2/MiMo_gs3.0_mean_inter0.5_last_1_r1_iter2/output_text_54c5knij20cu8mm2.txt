The right brain left brain idea is mostly a myth. While different parts of the brain do specialize in different functions—like the left side often handling language and logic, and the right side handling spatial and creative tasks—they work together constantly. It’s not like a strict split. Creativity can happen in the logical parts of the brain, and logic can be approached with creative problem-solving. 

For careers, if you want structure, measurement, creativity, and mobility, design fields are a great fit. User experience is a good example—measuring user behavior while being creative. Another option could be data science visualization, where you turn concrete data into creative visuals. Or even architecture, which is about structuring and building, but with a lot of creative expression.

Tinnitus mostly comes from physiological causes, so definitely check with a doctor to be sure.
