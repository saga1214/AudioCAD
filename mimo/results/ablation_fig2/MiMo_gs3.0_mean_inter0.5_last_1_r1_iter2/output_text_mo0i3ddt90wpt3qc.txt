You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which is great, so I won't repeat those brands. Since gluten isn't an option for you, another really good gluten-free camping snack is rice cakes.

Trail mix is also a wonderful gluten-free snack that offers a good mix of nuts and seeds, and you can add gluten-free dried fruits to it too. 

And for something sweet and safe, gluten-free energy bars are a perfect choice to keep you going.
