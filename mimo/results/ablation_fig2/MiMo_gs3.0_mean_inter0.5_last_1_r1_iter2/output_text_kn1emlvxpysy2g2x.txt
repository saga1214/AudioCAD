### **Best for Hot Weather: Pinot Grigio**
- **Why**: High acidity and light body cut through heat. Serve chilled (45–50°F / 7–10°C) in an insulated bucket with ice.  
- **Tip**: Add ice to the glass for extra chill if needed.

### **Best for Cold Weather: Pinot Noir or Mulled Wine**
- **Why**: Rich tannins and warmth in Pinot Noir or spiced mulled wine are comforting in cold temps.  
- **Tip**: Serve Pinot Noir slightly below cellar temp (55–60°F / 13–16°C) to avoid warming too fast. For mulled wine, keep warm (140–160°F / 60–70°C) with a heat lamp or thermos.

---

### **Key Takeaways**
1. **Reds (Pinot Noir, Chianti)**: Store at 55°F (13°C) and serve slightly cool (55–65°F / 13–18°C).  
2. **Whites (Pinot Grigio, Champagne)**: Store and serve chilled (45–50°F / 7–10°C).  
3. **Hon-Mirin**: Store cool (60–68°F / 16–20°C); sip at room temp (68–75°F / 20–24°C) if tasting. Use for cooking otherwise.  
4. **Hot Weather Hack**: Use ice in glasses or insulated bottles for whites/red wines.  
5. **Cold Weather Hack**: Serve reds in insulated wine glasses to retain warmth.
