Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! If you're going in the fall, which is around September to November there, you’re in for a treat. Late September to early November is their autumn season, so you’ll see those incredible orange, red, and yellow trees, especially in the highlands like, uh, Pátzcuaro or the Sierra Norte. Coastal areas like Oaxaca will also have some great fall foliage. Just, you know, pack light—watercolors and a small sketchbook are perfect for capturing those colors on the go. And definitely try painting the vibrant street art and markets; it’s a great way to mix traditional autumn scenes with local culture. Have an amazing time and soak up all that color!
