*   **Talk to a Lawyer:** If CPS involvement or police calls becomes frequent, consult a lawyer about potential harassment or nuisance laws. They can advise on your rights and how to proceed formally (e.g., cease and desist letters).

*   **Manage Your Reaction:** It's hard, but try not to let her actions visibly stress you or the kids. Your calm (or lack of reaction) removes her power. Lean into the fun things like Halloween prep and music!
