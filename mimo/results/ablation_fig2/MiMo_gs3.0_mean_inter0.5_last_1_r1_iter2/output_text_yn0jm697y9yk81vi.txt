Here's a consolidated list of my top recommendations for your sheet metal and drywall project, along with where to purchase them locally in Indianapolis:

### Cutting Sheet Metal
1. **Thinner sheets (< 0.125") with curves**:  
   → **Aviation snips**  
   → *Get at*: **Lowe's** (Home Improvement), Tractor Supply Co., or Ace Hardware  

2. **Medium/heavy sheets or flat cuts**:  
   → **Angle grinder with steel-cut or grooving cut-off wheel**  
   → *Get at*: **Lowe's** (preferred for metal tools) or Menards  

3. **Curves or detailed cuts**:  
   → **Jigsaw + bi-metal cut-off blade**  
   → *Get at*: Lowe's or Tractor Supply Co.  

### Drywall Fasteners
1. **Best overall**:  
   → **1¼" coarse-thread drywall screws**  
   → *Why*: Prevents pops, holds better than nails.  
   → *Get at*: **Lowe's** (drywall section) or Home Depot  

2. **Nail alternative (if needed)**:  
   → **Ring-shank drywall nails**  
   → *Get at*: Home Depot or Lowe's  

### Insulation
1. **Standard walls**:  
   → **Fiberglass batt insulation** (pre-cut rolls)  
   → *Why*: Easy DIY installation.  
   → *Get at*: **Lowe's** (best selection) or Menards  

2. **Premium sound/fireproofing**:  
   → **Mineral wool batts**  
   → *Get at*: Lowe's or Home Depot  

3. **Professional sealant**:  
   → **Spray foam insulation**  
   → *Get at*: Lowe's (spray foam section)  

### Recommended Stores in Indianapolis
- **Lowe's** (e.g., **10th Ave location**): Best for metal tools, drywall screws, and insulation.  
- **Menards** (e.g., **96th St location**): Strong value for tools and insulation.  
- **Harris Technology Tool** (3801 E. 10th Ave): Specialized tool shop for aviation snips/angle grinder accessories.  

**Pro Tip**: For sheet metal tools, Lowe's often carries specialized brands like *DeWalt* or *Makita* at their metal section. Always check stock online before visiting! 🔧
