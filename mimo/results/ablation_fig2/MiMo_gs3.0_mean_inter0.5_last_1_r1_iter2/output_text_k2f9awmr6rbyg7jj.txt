As a vegetarian, you should avoid any real crab meat because it's an actual meat product. Artificial crab meat is often still made from animal derivatives, so it's best to skip it. Instead, you could use tofu, mushrooms, or even a plant-based alternative. If you really want that crab flavor, you could use imitation crab sticks, but it's always good to check the label to make sure it's vegetarian-friendly.
