Here's a balanced schedule for a **10-day Japan holiday (Tokyo 5 days + Osaka/Kyoto/Nara 6 days)**, with built-in time for coursework research and fitness. I've optimized for deep exploration while keeping your presentation and travel needs in mind:

### **Tokyo (5 Days)**
- **Day 1-2: Arrival & Iconic Tokyo**  
  - *Morning*: Check-out, travel to Tokyo (Shinkansen to Tokyo Station).  
  - *Afternoon*: Explore **Asakusa** (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Market).  
  - *Evening*: Shibuya Crossing, Hachiko Statue (dinner).  
  - *Presentation Tip*: Research calligraphy or ukiyo-e prints incorporating urban scenes.  

- **Day 3: Art & Culture**  
  - *Morning*: **Tokyo National Museum** (art history).  
  - *Afternoon*: **TeamLab Borderless** (digital art/design inspiration for slides).  
  - *Evening*: Akihabara (pop culture).  
  - *Work*: Design color sk-ning/fonts for slides while brainstorming topic.  

- **Day 4: Nature & Work**  
  - *Morning*: **Inari Shrine** (Fushimi Inari Taisha), fox statues (art symbolism).  
  - *Afternoon*: **2-1-8 Ginza** (ceramics) or **Ghibli Museum** (contemporary art).  
  - *Evening*: Shinjuku (nightlife).  
  Tokyo Fitness: Morning gym sessions (7-8 AM).  

- **Day 5: Day Trip & Prep**  
  - *Morning*: **Nikko** (UNESCO sites, Buddhist sculpture).  
  - *Afternoon*: **Shibuya Sky** (city views for layout inspiration).  
  - *Evening*: Relax at hotel.  
  *Work*: Finalize presentation topic + start slide design.

### **Osaka/Kyoto/Nara (6 Days)**  
- **Day 6: Travel to Osaka & Dotonbori**  
  - *Morning*: Shinkansen to Osaka.  
  - *Afternoon*: **Osaka Castle** (woodblock print visual reference).  
  Kimono rental (design images for slides!).  
  - *Evening*: **Dotonbori** (vibrant colors for palette inspiration).  

- **Day 7: Kyoto (Day 1)**  
  - *Morning*: **Tofukuji Temple** (cherry blossoms if season aligns).  
  - *Afternoon*: **Kinkakuji** (Golden Pavilion).  
  - *Eveníng*: Kyoto via JR Special Rapid Train (30 mins).  
  *Work*: Layout practice (symmetry of woodblock prints).  

- **Day 8: Kyoto (Day 2) + Nara**  
  - *Morning*: **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (fox motifs).  
  - *Afternoon*: **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** (balance principle).  
  - *Evening*: **Nara** (15-min train from Kyoto). Visit **Todai-ji** (Great Buddha).  
  *Work*: Font research (traditional vs. modern).  

- **Day 9: Kyoto (Day 3) & Design**  
  - *Morning*: **Gion District** (geisha culture).  
  - *Afternoon*: **Kyoto Imperial Palace** (courtyard layouts).  
  - *Eveníng*: **Kiyomizu-dera** (night view).  
  *Work*: Finalize slide images (use museum digital collections).  

- **Day 10: Osaka & Return**  
  - *Morning*: **Osaka Museum of Housing & Living** (ancient pottery).  
  - *Afternoon*: **Universal Studios** or shopping (optional).  
  - *Evening*: Travel to Tokyo (Shinkansen).  
  *Work*: Submit coursework by deadline.

### **Key Tips for Success**  
1. **Art History Topic**: "Ukiyo-e Masters: From Hokusie to Contemporary Japanese Design" – blends visuals (woodblock prints) with literacy content.  
2. **Slide Design Aspects**: Use Kyoto/Osaka’s vibrant colors (red/yellow in Dotonbori), kimono patterns for layout inspiration, and traditional ink fonts.  
3. Fitness: Stick to 7-8 AM gym sessions. Use hotel time for short breaks.  
4. Communication: Hotel emails: Use Google Translate or apps like Papago. Staff often speak basic English.  
5. Transport: Get a **JR Pass** (if extending travel) or **ICOCA card** for trains/buses.
