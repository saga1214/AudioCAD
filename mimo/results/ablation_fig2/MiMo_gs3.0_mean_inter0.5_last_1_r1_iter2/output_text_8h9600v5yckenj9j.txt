### 🌫️ FOG MACHINE HACK (LOW-HANGING FOG)
**DIY Fog Chiller (Using Your Cooler Idea!):**  
1. **Materials**:  
   - Plastic storage tote or cooler (freezer-safe)  
   - Wire rack or baking sheet  
   - Frozen water bottles or ice packs  
   - Plastic tubing (optional, for directing fog)  
2. **Setup**:  
   - Place the fog machine nozzle *inside* the tote, pointing toward the exit hole.  
   - Put the wire rack inside the tote.  
   - Fill the rack with frozen water bottles/ice packs.  
   - Cut an exit hole in the *opposite* side.  
3. **How It Works**:  
   - Fog cools as it passes through the icy barrier → becomes heavier → pools near ground level.  
   - The fog flows naturally toward the exit hole.  
4. **Pro Tips**:  
   - Place the chiller on grass or a damp surface (helps fog cling).  
   - Add a small fan blowing *into* the chiller to push fog downward.
