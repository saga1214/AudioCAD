Here's a concise checklist for filming your first self-recorded show:

### **Before the Performance**
1. **Get Permission**  
   - Email Sunnyside Assisted Living: *"May I film my sock puppet show for promotional purposes? I’ll share proceeds with the facility."* Get written consent.  
   - *Why?* Nursing homes aren’t public spaces—permission is legally safer.

2. **Plan Your Content**  
   - Focus on **short, high-energy segments** (3–5 minutes per puppet).  
   - Highlight **Melvin’s humor** (e.g., "worst magician ever" gags) and **Sloppy Snake’s charm**.  
   - *Tip:* Film one character’s solo act to test editing.

3. **Tech Setup**  
   - **Device:** Use your phone (or camera) with a tripod.  
   - **Audio:** Attach a lavalier mic (or use phone’s built-in mic + background music).  
   - **Storage:** Save to cloud + phone (avoid losing footage).  
   - **Lighting:** Face a window or use a ring light to avoid shadows.

4. **Audience Prep**  
   - Announce: *"We’re filming today—don’t worry, it’s just silly fun!"*  
   - Keep older audience engaged: Ask simple questions (e.g., *"Who’s the dumber puppet?"*).

---

### **During the Performance**
- **Record in Vertical (9:16)** for Instagram/TikTok/YouTube Shorts.  
- **Keep it Interactive:** Pause for audience reactions (e.g., *"Ooh, did you see that trick?"*).  
- **Include Branding:** End with your puppet’s catchphrase (e.g., Melvin: *"I’m magnificent… maybe!"*).

---

### **After the Performance**
1. **Edit Quickly**  
   - Use free apps like CapCut or iMovie to:  
     - Cut flubs/awkward pauses.  
     - Add subtitles (nursing homes may have hearing issues).  
     - Add simple graphics (sock puppet close-ups).

2. **Platform Optimization**  
   - **TikTok/Reels:** 15–60 seconds. Use trending sounds + hashtags (#SockPuppetMagic #VolunteerPuppeteer).  
   - **YouTube:** 3–5 minutes. Title: *"Melvin the Magnificent Fails at Nursing Home!"*

3. **Credit & Consent**  
   - Tag Sunnyside Assisted Living + add: *"Filmed with permission."*  
   - Share ad revenue with the facility (per your offer).

---

### **Key Legal Reminders**
- **You own the copyright**—others can’t monetize without your consent.  
- Nursing homes require explicit permission; hospitals schools foster homes usually don’t (volunteer = public event).

### **Next Steps**
- Start small: Film 3–5 clips, edit, and test on TikTok/Reels.  
- Engage with comments (e.g., *"Ask Melvin anything!"*).  
- Track views—see what characters resonate!
