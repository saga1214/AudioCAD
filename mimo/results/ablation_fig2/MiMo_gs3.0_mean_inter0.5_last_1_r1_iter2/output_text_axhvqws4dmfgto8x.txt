Alright, let's make sure we don't set off *any* alarms.

Wooden utensils aside, the stir-fry stir-up mainly happens at the very end when you pour your sauce into the super hot pan. It's steam, not smoke, by the way.

So here’s the trick: cook your stir-fry ingredients, then take the hot wok off the burner. Let it cool down for just a minute. Then, carefully pour your sauce in and stir it up. The sauce will cook instantly without creating a ton of steam. Give it a quick stir-fry for a minute or two, then you're done!
