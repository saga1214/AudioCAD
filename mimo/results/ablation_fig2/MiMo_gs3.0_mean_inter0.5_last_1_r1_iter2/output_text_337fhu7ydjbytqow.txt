Here’s a **clothing gear packing list** optimized for both the 20-mile overnight segment and the final 12 miles tomorrow night, prioritizing warmth, versatility, and trail conditions. No fluff—just what you need to stay comfortable and support your husband:

### **For the 20-Mile Segment (Running Overnight)**
1. **Base Layer (Moisture-Wicking):**  
   - Lightweight, quick-dry top and bottom (synthetic or merino wool). *Avoid cotton.*  
2. **Mid-Layer:**  
   - Fleece vest or lightweight puffy jacket (adjustable hood).  
3. **Outer Layer:**  
   - Windproof/water-resistant shell (e.g., lightweight rain jacket with pit zips).  
4. **Hands:**  
   - Trail running gloves (for grip and warmth).  
5. **Head:**  
   - Wide-brim hat (daytime) + warm beanie/balaclava (nighttime).  
6. **Visibility:**  
   - Headlamp with **extra batteries** + reflective gear (if allowed).  
7. **Footwear:**  
   - Trail running shoes with aggressive tread + extra socks (merino wool).  
8. **Extras:**  
   - Lightweight pack/vest (to carry layers if needed).  
   - Buff or neck gaiter (for warmth/face protection).  

### **For the Final 12-Miles (Continuing Overnight)**
1. **Base Layer:** Same as above.  
2. **Mid-Layer:** Fleece vest (adjustable for variable temps).  
3. **Outer Layer:**  
   - Insulated puffy jacket (if temps drop below freezing) or windbreaker (if warmer).  
4. **Hands:** Gloves + beanie (your extremities will get cold fast during fatigue).  
5.Head:  
   - Daytime hat + headlamp (handheld light recommended).  
6. **Footwear:** Same trail shoes + extra socks.  
7. **Extras:**  
   - Pack/vest (pacing may slow pace, allowing layer adjustments).  
   - Buff/neck gaiter (extra warmth if needed).  

### **Key Priorities for Both Segments**  
- **Layers > One-Size-Fits-All:** Start light, add layers if you cool off.  
- **Gloves/Hat:** Your hands and head lose heat fastest at night.  
- **Light:** A reliable headlamp is non-negotiable.  
- **Pack/Vest:** Carry layers efficiently while running.  

### **Night-Specific Essentials**  
- **Reflective Vest/Tape:** If not provided by the race.  
- **Extra Warm Hat/Balaclava:** For sudden cold snaps.  
- **Gaiter/Buff:** Covers face in wind/dust.
