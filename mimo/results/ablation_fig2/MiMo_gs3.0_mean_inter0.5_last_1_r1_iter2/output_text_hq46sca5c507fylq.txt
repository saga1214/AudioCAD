1. **Essential Kit**: A pre-assembled mallet, wood glue, and sanding blocks.  
2. **Beginner Project**: A small wooden pen holder or desk organizer (using scrap wood).  
3. **Tool Upgrade**: A high-quality chisel set or a planing block.  

### For Clay Work:
1. **Hand-Building Kit**: Basic clay, rolling pin, and sculpting tools.  
2. **Leather-Hard Clay**: Easier to shape for first projects.  
3. **Portable Wheel**: A small tabletop pottery wheel (if budget allows).

- **Skill-Building Subscription**: "The Tailor's Magazine" or woodworking/clay tutorials.  
- **Experience Voucher**: A local tailoring workshop or pottery class.
