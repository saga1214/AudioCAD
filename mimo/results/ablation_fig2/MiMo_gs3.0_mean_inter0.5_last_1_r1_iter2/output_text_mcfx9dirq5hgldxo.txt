For Madrid, tapas and churros are a must. Tapas are these really flavorful small plates—think things like patatas bravas, which are crispy potatoes with a spicy sauce, or jamón ibérico.

And for breakfast, you have to try churros, which are these fried dough pastries, dipped in thick hot chocolate. It's a classic there.

Just make sure Philly airport has a Spanish or Mediterranean restaurant for a good layover meal.
