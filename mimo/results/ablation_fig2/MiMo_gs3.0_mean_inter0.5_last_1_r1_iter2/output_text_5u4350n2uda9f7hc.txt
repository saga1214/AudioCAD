Great considerations! Since space and transport are key factors, here’s a breakdown of your options:

### **For Your Balcony Table:**
- **Succulents** (e.g., *Echeveria*, *Haworthia*, *Sedum*):  
  - **Size**: Typically 3–8 inches tall, with shallow root systems. Lightweight (pots often weigh <1 lb).  
  - **Fit**: Perfect for small containers on your table. Many varieties stay compact.  
  - **Care**: Thrive in bright morning light; water only when soil is dry (city wind may dry them faster).  

- **Birds Nest Snake Plant** (*Sansevieria trifasciata* ‘Hahnii’):  
  - **Size**: Stands 6–12 inches tall with plump, spiraled leaves. Very lightweight (pot <2 lbs).  
  - **Fit**: Ideal for floor space—its upright shape adds structure without taking up table room.  
  - **Look**: Glossy, emerald-green leaves spiraled upward like a nest; architectural yet soft.  

### **Transport & Repotting:**
- **Weight**: Both plants are **lightweight**—succulents in plastic pots and snake plants in lightweight terracotta/ceramic. Total weight should be manageable for 5 flights.  
- **Transport Tip**: Bring their original pots (or buy small, collapsible ones at the garden center).  
- **Repotting at Departure**:  
  - Succulents: Repot into a slightly wider pot with extra soil to stabilize roots.  
  - Snake Plants: Use a snug pot to prevent toppling. Both are resilient for short trips.  

### **Balcony-Specific Tips:**  
1. **Wind on 5th Floor**: Sturdy plants with low branches (like snake plants) resist wind better than trailing succulents.  
2. **East Light**: Prioritize sun-lovers (e.g., snake plants, succulents, begonias). Avoid shade-happy plants (e.g., ZZ plants).  
3. ** NYC Conditions**: Wind + bright light means **avoid overwatering**. Check soil moisture every 3–4 days.  

### **Easy-Buy Recommendations:**  
- **Table**: Snake Plant or small succulents in self-watering pots.  
- **Floor**: Snake Plant (compact) or Pilea (tiny green orb-like leaves).  
- **Herbs**: Mint or thyme in small terracotta pots—they’ll add a homey scent!  

These choices are **space-efficient**, **lightweight**, and built for NYC conditions. At month’s end, you’ll easily transport them home! 🌿
