Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes for your party — keeping dietary needs and formality in mind:

### 1. **Vodka Cocktail: "Sunset Spritz"**  
*(Light, refreshing, and soda-free-friendly)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1.5 oz vodka  
- 0.75 oz gin (or substitute with extra vodka)  
- 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz elderflower liqueur (e.g., St. Germain)  
- Top with **sparkling water** (instead of soda)  
- Garnish: Lime wedge + mint sprig  

**Why it works**: Bright, herbaceous, and works for all diets. Use sparkling water to avoid the allergy while keeping it fizzy.

---

### 2. **Argentine Fernet Cocktail: "Mesa Spritz"**  
*(Inspired by Argentina’s classic Fernet-Cola drink)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca (or substitute with Argentine-style herbal liqueur)  
- 2 oz cola (use a premium brand like Fentimans or Coca-Cola)  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- Top with **sparkling water** (for soda allergy)  
- Garnish: Lime wheel + Angostura bitters splash  

**Why it works**: Sweet, spicy, and effervescent — a crowd-pleaser across generations. Sparkling water replaces soda while keeping the fizz.

---

### Quick Drink Recap:
- **Non-alcoholic options**: Premium sparkling water, wine, signature mocktail (Fernet + juice + bitters), and fruit tarts for dessert.  
- **Allergy-safe**: Always label sparkling water as "alcohol-free" if serving alongside alcoholic drinks.  

### Final Tip:  
Set out pre-mixed cocktail shakers with ice in a tray for guests to customize. Keeps things smooth and avoids long wait times!
