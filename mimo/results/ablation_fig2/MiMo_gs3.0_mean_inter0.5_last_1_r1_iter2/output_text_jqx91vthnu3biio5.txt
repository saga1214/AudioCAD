### 🎨 Color-Coding for Icons/Folders  
**Primary Benefit:** Reduces *mental load* by creating visual shortcuts. You’ll spend less time recalling what’s where and more time acting.  
**Memory vs. Focus:** While color *can* aid memory (e.g., red for urgent, blue for archived), it’s mainly about **task-switching efficiency**. If colors feel random, they’ll backfire. Stick to 2-3 consistent themes (e.g., green = active projects, yellow = reference, gray = completed).  

### 🎧 Playlist Strategy  
**Personal Preference Wins:** There’s no "optimal" timing—it’s about *your* rhythm.  
- **Try:** Switching tracks when switching tasks (e.g., lo-fi for deep work, nature sounds for admin).  
- **If it feels disruptive:** Stick to one vibe. Ambience > lyrics for focus.  
- **Pro Tip:** Use apps like Spotify’s "Focus" playlists or YouTube’s "Deep Focus" streams for seamless loops.  

### 📁 Physical Documents  
**Tackle on a Quiet Day:** Yes! The "fresh start" energy helps.  
- **Progress Drivers:**  
  - **Environment:** A clean desk = mental clarity.  
  - **Chunking:** Break into 30-minute sprints (e.g., "File all invoices," "Shred old receipts").  
  - **Reward:** Treat yourself after (e.g., coffee break, walk).  

### ⏱️ Pomodoro & Breaks  
**Not a Gimmick:** Science-backed!  
- **Why it works:** Prevents decision fatigue and dopamine crashes.  
- **Customize:**  
  - Try 25 min work / 5 min break → 45 min / 15 min if you need longer flow.  
  - **Move during breaks** (stretch, walk) to reset focus.
