Cake shopping: drywall compound was for both tasks remember? Frost ingredients another two bucks. So cake shopping probably around $15-20.

Putting drywall total around $30, cake around $20, decorations for party maybe $10. Total budget probably around $60-70 should cover both tasks and party prep. It's an estimate, but that should give you a good starting point.
