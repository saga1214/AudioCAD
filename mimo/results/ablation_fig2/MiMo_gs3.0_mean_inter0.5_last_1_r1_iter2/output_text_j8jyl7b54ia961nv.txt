You are absolutely right. If we put a higher ethical weight on public acceptance and personal freedom, the vaccination campaign becomes much riskier from a public image standpoint. The national department would have to weigh the clear health benefits against the very real threat of public backlash. So, given that high ethical and political risk, the choice would likely shift back to the sugar consumption campaign. In that case, the pros and ethics factors would get lower scores, but the cost and long-term population benefit might be weighted higher. The decision really hinges on whether immediate disease prevention is more critical than a long-term, slower-moving public health goal.
