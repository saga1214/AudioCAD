Absolutely. For a show-stopping Thanksgiving pie, a classic pumpkin pie is a great choice. You can make it in a single pot on the stove.

Here’s how it works: You'll mix the pumpkin puree, eggs, sugar, and spices in one pot. Then, you pour that mixture into a pre-baked pie shell and cook it on the stove until it's set. No oven needed, so again, limited kitchen shouldn't be an issue.

And if you want a savory side dish to go with it, simple roasted Brussels sprouts are always a safe and delicious bet. You just toss them with oil, salt, and pepper and roast them.
