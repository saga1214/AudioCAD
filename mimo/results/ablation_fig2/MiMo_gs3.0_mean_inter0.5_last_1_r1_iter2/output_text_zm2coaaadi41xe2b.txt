Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! 

For ramen, there's Ton Ton Ramen, which has rich broth and thick noodles. For Chinese, check out Hong Kong Noodle House, which has classic dishes like chow mein. And for Japanese ramen, Ippudo is a great choice with their signature tonkotsu.

For protein bars, I'd recommend Quest Bars—they’re high in protein, low in sugar, and have great flavors. Also, Optimum Nutrition has some solid options. And if you want something more natural, GoMacro bars are a good choice.
