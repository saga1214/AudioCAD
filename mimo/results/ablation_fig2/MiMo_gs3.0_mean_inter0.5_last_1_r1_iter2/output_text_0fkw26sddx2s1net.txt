Of course. For miso soup or cucumber salad, you'll just need cucumbers, sesame oil, soy sauce, and miso paste for the soup option. If he prefers the salad, grab some pickled ginger and rice vinegar. 

For the chicken teriyaki main, you'll need chicken breast cut into strips, mirin, soy sauce, and a bit of sugar for the glaze. Stir-fried veggies could be bell peppers, snap peas, carrots, and a little garlic and ginger. Steamed jasmine rice just needs rice, water, and a pinch of salt.

For the finisher, make sure you have mango sticky rice ingredients: coconut milk, sugar, matcha powder if you decide to make the ice cream, and of course, some fresh mango.

Oh, and for the chicken teriyaki, you might also want some teriyaki soy sauce, a splash of rice vinegar, and a little grated ginger and garlic. That's it! Hope your husband enjoys it.
