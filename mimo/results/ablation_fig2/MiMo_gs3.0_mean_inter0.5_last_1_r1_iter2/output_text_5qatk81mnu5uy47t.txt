### **Top Dive Destinations in Costa Rica**
1. **Tuna Bay (Central Pacific)**  
   - **Why go:** Crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral reefs, and abundant marine life (turtles, rays, reef sharks).  
   - **Must-dive sites:**  
     - *Cocos Island Anchorage:* Snorkel with sea lions and spot pilot whales.  
     - *Playa Naranjo:* Thrilling drift dives in strong currents with mantas.  
   - **Ideal for:** Beginners to intermediates; easy boat access.

2. **Cocos Island (National Park)**  
   - **Why go:** Remote, wild, and teeming with life—think whale sharks, hammerheads, and giant kelp forests.  
   - **Key experiences:**  
     - *Dolphins & Whales:* Humpback whales (Dec-Apr) and dolphins in open ocean.  
     - *Manta Ray Clean Breaks:* At sites like *Puerto Esmerito*.  
   - **Note:** Requires overnight stays; best for experienced divers due to drift dives.

3. **Caño Island (Caribbean Coast)**  
   - **Why go:** A marine park with pristine reefs, snorkel-friendly shallow zones, and dramatic underwater cliffs.  
   - **Highlights:**  
     - *Turtle Nesting:* See leatherbacks (Jan-Apr).  
     - *Dolphin Pods:* Frequent sightings on surface follows.  
   - **Access:** Day trips from Limón; great for mixed groups (beginners + advanced).

4. **Southern Pacific (Ostional/Puerto Jiménez)**  
   - **Why go:** Unique ecosystems like *Sea Turtle Conservancy* (nesting season: Nov-May) and volcanic rock formations.  
   - **Sites:** *El Marín* (reef dives) and *Playa Grande* (turtle conservation).  

### **Logistics & Tips**
- **Fins Choice:** Your debate is settled—**short fins** are ideal for calm Caribbean sites like Tuna Bay and Caño Island. Save long fins for strong currents on Cocos Island.  
- **Certification:** Ensure you’re certified (PADI/SSI Advanced Open Water) for deeper dives. If not, look for dive operators offering discovery dives.  
- **Best Time to Visit:** **Dry Season (Dec-Apr)** for optimal visibility and wildlife activity.  
- **Gear:** Rent fins locally (cost-effective) and pack a dive computer, weight belt, and reef-safe sunscreen.  

### **Sample 7-Day Itinerary**
- **Days 1-3:** Tuna Bay (Island Hopper tours)  
  - *Site 1:* Cocos Island Anchorage (turtle encounter).  
  - *Site 2:* Playa Naranjo (drift dive with reef sharks).  
- **Day 4:** Caño Island (overnight stay for sunset snorkel + dolphin hunt).  
- **Day 5-7:** Cocos Island (whale sharks, manta rays, sea lion colonies).  

### **Pro Tips**
- **Wildlife Calendar:** Book Caño Island in April (turtles) and Cocos Island in March (whale sharks).  
- **Sustainability:** Choose eco-certified operators (e.g., *Cocos Dive Adventures*, *Caño Island Lodge*).  
- **Travel:** Fly into San José, rent a car to reach Tamarindo (Caribbean) or Puerto Jiménez (Pacific).
