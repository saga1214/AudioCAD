Either tool I mentioned will tune strings on pretty much any instrument.

If you use a clip-on tuner, you'll want to turn it on, clip it to your headstock, and play each string open, from the thickest to the thinnest. The tuner's needle will show you if a note is too high or too low, and you can just turn the tuners until it's perfectly in tune.

If you use an app, just open it, place your phone near the instrument, and play each string open from thickest to thinnest. The app will display the note and tell you if it's too sharp, which means higher, or too flat, which means lower. Just adjust until it reads exactly what it should be.
