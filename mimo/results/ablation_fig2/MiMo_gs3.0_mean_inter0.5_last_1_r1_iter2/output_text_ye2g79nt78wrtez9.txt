Re-evaluating the numbers, Ancient Egypt does still have a slight edge. Its universal recognition and incredible artifacts guarantee a high turnout. But, Ancient Mesopotamia's unique story and immense educational value are very compelling. Ultimately, the 'best' choice really depends on the museum's goals. If the goal is maximum visitor appeal, then yes, Ancient Egypt is the best choice. If the goal is to showcase foundational human achievements, then Mesopotamia is the superior choice.
