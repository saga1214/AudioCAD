Yes, you absolutely can submit an entire book with text and illustrations. In fact, it's a very common and highly recommended approach for children's books, especially if you're the illustrator yourself. 

The process is a bit different. You would submit your manuscript and your art portfolio together to publishers or agents. The most important thing is that you must use your own artwork, not stock photos or generic clip art. Publishers are looking for a unique, cohesive artistic vision for their books. Your illustrations are a huge part of your book's appeal, so you should definitely include them in your submission.
