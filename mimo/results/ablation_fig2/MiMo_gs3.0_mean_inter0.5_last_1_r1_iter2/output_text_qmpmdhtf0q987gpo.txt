Okay, let's go over one last time before you prime. After light sanding, give the cabinets one last really good cleaning with your degreaser, and make sure they are completely dry.

Once they're dry, lightly wipe over the sanded areas with some fine-grit sanding paste. This just creates an even smoother surface for the primer.

Then, before you apply the primer, lightly sand it one more time with fine-grit sandpaper. This helps to remove any little bumps or imperfections and creates an even better surface for your paint color to adhere to. After that primer is dry, you're ready to paint.
