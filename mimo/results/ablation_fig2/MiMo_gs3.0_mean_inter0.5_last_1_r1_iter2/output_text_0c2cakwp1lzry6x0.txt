Here's a step-by-step guide for each installation, prioritizing safety and wall integrity:

**1. TV Mount Installation:**
- **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, measuring tape, drill, wall anchors (if not drilling into studs), screwdriver
- **Steps:**
  1. **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (wood beams behind drywall) at the desired height. Mounting into studs prevents wall damage.
  2. **Mark Mount Position:** Center the TV on the wall using a level and measuring tape. Mark the exact point for the mounting plate.
  3. **Drill Pilot Holes:** If mounting into a stud, drill a hole slightly smaller than the plate's screw holes. If not in a stud, drill holes for anchors and insert them.
  4. **Attach Mount:** Secure the mounting plate to the wall using screws through the plate and into studs or anchors. Tighten firmly.
  5. **Mount TV:** Align the TV with the plate and secure it using the provided screws.

**2. Hanging Planters:**
- **Tools Needed:** Heavy-duty hook or mounting bracket, drill, appropriate anchors (if not in studs), measuring tape, level
- **Steps:**
  1. **Choose Anchor Method:** For drywall, select the right anchors (e.g., toggle bolts for heavy planters). For concrete, use masonry anchors.
  2. **Mark Hook Position:** Decide the height and position. Use a level to ensure it’s straight. Mark the center point.
  3. **Drill Anchor Holes:** Drill a hole slightly larger than the anchor’s shaft. Insert the anchor and tap it into place.
  4. **Install Hook:** Screw the hook into the anchored hole. Ensure it’s secure by gently testing the weight.
  5. **Hang Planter:** Attach the planter’s hanger to the hook.

**3. Floating Shelves:**
- **Tools Needed:** Shelf brackets (for drywall), drill, anchors, screwdriver, level, measuring tape
- **Steps:**
  1. **Measure & Mark:** Decide shelf placement. Use a level and measuring tape to mark where the brackets attach to the wall.
  2. **Drill Anchor Holes:** Drill holes for anchors in the wall. Insert anchors and secure them.
  3. **Attach Brackets:** Screw the brackets to the wall at the marked points.
  4. **Position Shelf:** Hold the shelf against the brackets. Mark screw holes on the back.
  5. **Secure Shelf:** Drive screws through the shelf holes into the brackets. Check stability by gently shaking it.
  6. **Decorate:** Place art or objects on the shelf once secure.

**Key Safety Tips:**
- **Test Weight Capacity:** Always check if your wall can support the item’s weight. Use a calculator online for drywall.
- **Use Anchors:** Always use appropriate anchors for drywall (e.g., toggle bolts for heavy items).
- **Ask for Help:** Get a second person to hold shelves or heavy items during installation.
- **Avoid Studs:** Never drill into electrical wires or pipes—use a stud finder with wire detection.
