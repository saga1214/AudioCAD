Staying at Secret Harbour Beach Resort on St. John is a fantastic choice—its secluded, calm bay is perfect for your week. Here are some pressure-free, scenic daytime activities nearby or a short boat trip away:

### Beach & Snorkeling
1. **Coki Beach**:  
   - A 10-minute boat ride south. Known for its gentle slope, soft sand, and vibrant coral gardens right off the shore. Great for beginners and snorkeling without pressure changes.  
   - *Bonus*: Free beach bar (Coki Bar) with live music and vegetarian tacos!

2. **Trunk Bay**:  
   - Accessible via a short ferry ride from Coki (or your boat). Famous for its underwater snorkeling trail with crystal-clear water. Grab a kayak or paddleboard at the bayfront.

### Nature & Hiking
3. **Reef Bay Trail**:  
   - A flat, 1.5-mile trail through mangroves and coral flats. End at a secluded beach with historic ruins. Birding hotspot for tanagers, bananaquits, and frigatebirds.

4. **Sandy Spit**:  
   - A 20-minute walk east from Secret Harbour. Wild, undeveloped beach with rare petroglyphs and nesting seabirds.

### Unique Boat Trips
5. **Salt River Bay National Park**:  
   - Paddle or kayak through mangroves to spot manatees, herons, and rare birds. Guided tours available (pressure-free!).

6. **Salt River to Magens Bay**:  
   - A 30-minute boat ride to one of the islands’ most beautiful beaches. Calm waters and snorkeling in the famous "Blue Hole."

### Food & Culture
- **Cruz Bay’s "Fish Fry"**:  
  Every Friday/Sunday, cruise shops host food stalls, live bands, and craft vendors. Casual, pressure-free atmosphere with veggie options at **Coco’s**.
- **Café Luna**:  
  Just a 5-minute walk from Secret Harbour. Cozy spot for coffee, vegetarian sandwiches, and Virgin Islands craft beer.

### Pro Tips
- **Boat Rentals**: Consider a 24-hour day sailer for flexibility. Companies like **Virgin Islands Sailing Company** offer flat-rate plans.
- **Avoid Pressure**: Stick to shallow bays (max 5 feet deep) and avoid hills/mountains.
- **Local Guide**: Hire a "boatman" (local guide) for personalized tours—they know hidden coves and birding spots!
