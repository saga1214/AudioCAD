### **Modern Resume Format (Clean & Scannable)**
*(All fonts 9–10pt for ATS compatibility)*

#### **1. Header (Single Line)**
```
NAME (14pt, bold)
[Icon] email@domain.com | [Icon] phone | [Icon] City, State
LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/yourprofile (add this!)
```

#### **2. Professional Summary** (3–4 lines)
*Example:*  
> "Data-driven Marketing Manager with 8+ years optimizing SaaS campaigns. Expertise in SEO, Google Analytics, and CRM automation. Seeking to leverage growth-hacking strategies at [Company Name]."

#### **3. Experience** (Reverse chronological)
- **Job Title**, Company, City, State | *Dates*  
  *Bullet Points* (3–5 per role):  
  - Start with action verbs (*"Led," "Developed," "Increased"*).  
  - Quantify results (*"Grew traffic by 30%," "Saved $50K annually"*).  
  - Use keywords from the job description.  
  *Example:*  
  > **Senior UX Designer** | ABC Tech, San Francisco | Jan 2020–Present  
  > - Redesigned onboarding flow, reducing user drop-off by 40%  
  > - Led cross-functional team to launch mobile app (download: 50K+)  

#### **4. Relevant Experience** (For older/irrelevant roles)
- Group by theme (e.g., "Early Career: Retail Operations")  
- Include only 1–2 key bullet points per role.  
- Omit dates if gaps exist.  

#### **5. Skills** (Customize per job)
- **Hard Skills:** Python, Tableau, Project Management  
- **Soft Skills:** Cross-functional Collaboration, Stakeholder Communication  
- *Tip:* Mirror keywords from job postings.  

#### **6. Education** (Most recent first)
- **Degree**, University, Location | *Graduation Year*  
  - Add honors/scholarships if recent (e.g., "Summa Cum Laude").  
  - *Example:*  
    > **B.S. Computer Science** | Stanford University | 2015  
    > *Graduate with Highest Honors*  

#### **7. Additional Sections** (Optional but strategic)
- **Certifications:** "Google Analytics Certified"  
- **Projects:** "E-commerce SEO Audit" (link to portfolio)  
- **Volunteer Work:** "Nonprofit Tech Mentor"
