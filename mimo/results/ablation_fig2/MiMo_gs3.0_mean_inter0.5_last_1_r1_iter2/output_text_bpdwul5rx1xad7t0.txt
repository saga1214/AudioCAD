Of course! With a two-bedroom apartment and expecting a first baby, I'd prioritize scenic, less strenuous road trips. Here are five beautiful options in the BC Interior and beyond that are perfect for you.

First, a drive to the Okanagan Valley. You can visit the stunning lakes like Kaledon and Penticton. A must-try activity is wine tasting, as the area is famous for its vineyards. For a beautiful thing, you could visit theBC Wine Museum.

Second, the Fraser Canyon area near Lillooet. The scenery is dramatic, with waterfalls and the old Fraser Canyon Railway. A great activity is a gentle hike to the railway bridge for a photo. For a beautiful thing, you could stop at the Lillooet Heritage Centre.

Third, the Fraser Island in the Fraser Valley. It's a unique, historic farm that has been preserved. A must-try activity is touring the farm's old barns and gardens. For a beautiful thing, you could look for the old stone bridge.

Fourth, the Kootenay area near Nelson. It's known for its wild scenery and outdoor activities. A must-try activity is a visit to the Kootenay Lake Provincial Park. For a beautiful thing, you could check out the Nelson Heritage Museum.

Finally, the Quatsino Island near Port Hardy. It's a beautiful coastal area with rich Indigenous history. A must-try activity is exploring the Quatsino Rock Art site. For a beautiful thing, you could visit the Quatsino Cultural Centre.

These trips all have gentle drives and are great for taking in the scenery without heavy lifting. Enjoy your explorations!
