Here’s a **sand-friendly, pirate-themed nautical menu** designed for beach convenience, allergy safety (watermelon-free), and maximum fun—perfect for your 7-year-old pirate’s birthday!

---

### **Appetizers & Snacks**  
*(Easy to handle, minimal sand risk)*  
1. **"Buried Treasure" Cheese & Crackers Platter**  
   - Cubed cheddar cheese (shaped like coins), themed crackers (goldfish crackers = "crew members"), and mini pretzels = "ship masts."  
  2. **Jolly Roger Fruit Skewers**  
   - Grapes, pineapple chunks (dipped in melted white chocolate = "sea foam"), and strawberries. *Avoid watermelon entirely.*  
3. **Pirate Ship Pretzel Rods**  
   - Mini pretzel rods topped with peanut butter (dipped in chocolate) and sprinkled with gummy eyes or mini M&Ms.  
4. **Corn on the Cob "Ships"**  
   - Pre-cut grilled or canned corn kernels on skewers, served with themed dip cups:  
     - **"Cannonball" Dip:** Yellow cheese dip (like cheddar) + blueberry swirls.  
     - **"Treasure Chest" Dip:** Cream cheese + lemon-pepper seasoning.  

---

### **Main Course**  
*(Interactive and easy to manage)*  
1. **Mini Nautical Sliders**  
   - Cut into 2" pieces:  
     - **Pirate’s Feast:** Ham/cheese roll-ups (Jolly Roger-shaped cutouts) + cucumber "sails."  
     - **Mermaid’s Delight:** Tuna-mayo sandwiches with flaked crackers = "sea monsters."  
   - Serve in mini treasure chests or pirate buckets.  

---

### **Drinks**  
*(Individual, spill-proof, and sand-free)*  
1. **"Pirate Punch" Cups**  
   - Individual juice boxes or water bottles labeled:  
     - **"Cannon Blast Punch"** (Sparkling blue lemonade + blueberries).  
     - **"Mermaid’s Tears"** (Strawberry lemonade).  
2. **Themed Water Bottles**  
   - Pre-filled reusable bottles with pirate flags printed on them.  

---

### **Allergy & Sand-Safe Tips**  
- **Watermelon Avoidance:** Double-check all labels (e.g., some fruit juices contain watermelon extract).  
- **Sand Protection:**  
  - Serve all food on small cloth placemats or in palm-sized containers.  
  - Use toothpicks for skewers (not fingers!).  
- **Prep Ahead:** Assemble everything at home; kids grab-and-go from a station.  

---

### **Pirate-Themed Extras**  
- **Packaging:** Label food with pirate names ("Captain’s Snacks," "First Mate Fruit").  
- **Dips:** Serve with mini spoons in tiny "cannon" cups.  
- **Cookie-Thaw Treats:** Freeze cookie cutters (ships, anchors) into ice blocks for a fun "thaw-and-eat" game.  

This menu keeps things **mess-free, allergy-safe, and nautical-themed**—so your pirate crew can enjoy the beach adventure without sand worries! 🏴‍☠️🌊
