I see what you're pointing out, and you're right, that's a very important distinction. You are correct that a single portion of 300 grams is only about 20 percent of the daily recommended sodium intake, which is 2,000 milligrams.

However, the concern arises from the fact that this is your only meal of the day. If you were to eat this same meal again later in the day, or have another high-sodium meal, your total sodium for the day could easily go over the recommended 2,000 milligrams, and possibly much higher.

So, to clarify: a single serving of your portion isn't excessively high in sodium on its own. But because this meal makes up your entire day's intake, the cumulative sodium for the day could be high, especially if you tend to have other salty foods. It's a matter of the total for the day, not just that one portion.
