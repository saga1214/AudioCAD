Don't worry, it's easy to get them mixed up. Let me clarify. The standard one and a quarter to one to one ratio, which is whiskey, vermouth, and Campari respectively, was your first question. The equal parts version, which is vermouth, Campari, and gin at three-quarters of an ounce each, was The Last Word. So your friend was right, he was making the Boulevardier correctly, you just got the two cocktails confused. For The Last Word, you'll need three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and half an ounce of fresh lime juice. Stir that over ice too and garnish with a lime wheel. Enjoy your party!
