Sure! First, think about your daily routine to see which breeds match your energy level—pick one that’s active but not overly so. Shelters are great places to start since many rescues have calm, mixed-breed dogs that fit this description. If you’re set on purebreds, look into breeders who prioritize temperament and health. Meet potential candidates to see how they vibe with you. Don’t forget to ask about their background, especially if they’ve been around kids or other pets. Take your time to find the right dog for your home.  

As for breeds, consider:  
- **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel**: Great with kids, not too big, and affectionate.  
- **Bulldog**: Calm, chill, and low-shedding.  
- **Pug**: Friendly and adaptable to apartment living.  
- **French Bulldog**: Playful but not too noisy when alone.  
- **Labrador Retriever**: Friendly and trainable, but might be "big." Ask landlord again!  
- **Golden Retriever**: Similar to Labs—friendly and trainable, but also on the bigger side. Double-check size rules.  
- **Border Collie**: Very smart and trainable, but high-energy—may not fit "chill."  
- **Australian Cattle Dog**: Intelligent and loyal, but also very active.  

Research each breed’s quirks, like chasing squirrels, and see if they match your spouse’s preferences. Rescue dogs of these breeds might also be great options!
