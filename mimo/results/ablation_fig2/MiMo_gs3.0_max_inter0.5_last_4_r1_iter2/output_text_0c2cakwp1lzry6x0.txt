Okay, for installing a TV, you'll need a sturdy mounting bracket. The first and most important step is to find the exact location on the wall. Use a level and a pencil to mark where the center of the TV should go. Then, you need to drill pilot holes at those marks. It's crucial to check if there are any electrical wires or plumbing behind the wall, so a stud finder that has that feature is a good idea. Once you've drilled the holes, you can screw the mounting bracket into the wall and then hang the TV. For hanging planters, you'll need to measure the space where you want them. Make sure the wall can support the weight of the planter and the soil inside. You can use wall anchors if needed. Just drill a hole, insert the anchor, and then screw the hook or hanger for the planter into the wall. For floating shelves, they usually attach to a wall bracket. You'll need to measure and mark the shelf's position, drill a hole for the bracket, and then secure the shelf. Just make sure the bracket is strong enough to hold the weight of whatever you put on the shelf. Always check the weight limits on the product instructions. And of course, wear safety gear like gloves and eye protection when drilling, and maybe have a friend help you lift heavy items like the TV. Always double-check that everything is level and secure before you put your TV or any heavy items on it.
