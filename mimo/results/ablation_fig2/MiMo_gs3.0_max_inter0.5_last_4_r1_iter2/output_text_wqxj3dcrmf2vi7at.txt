Here's a recap of all ideas for your corgi princess, presented in the requested style:

Absolutely! Corgis usually adore treats like these: First, try small frozen bites made from mashed pumpkin or sweet potato. Another easy one is baking some peanut butter and oat cookies—just make sure there’s no xylitol in the peanut butter. Finally, little pieces of cooked chicken or carrot sticks can also be healthy, simple snacks. Always keep portions tiny so she stays fit!

For store-bought, a few popular and reliable brands include Blue Buffalo, Wellness, and Zuke’s. You might also try Greenies or Merrick for treats. Just check the ingredients and aim for natural, wholesome options.

How to teach Princess how to sit: Start by holding a treat close to her nose to get her attention. Then slowly raise the treat over her head so she naturally lowers her butt to follow it. Once she’s in that sitting position, say the word “sit” and reward her right away. Repeat this a few times each day until she connects the word with the action. Corgis are smart, so she’ll catch on quickly with consistency and praise!

She’s having trouble potty training? Any ideas? Potty training can take some patience. The key is consistency. First, pick a regular spot outside and take her there frequently, especially after meals, naps, or playtime. When she goes in the right spot, praise her immediately and give a small treat. Also, keep a regular feeding schedule so you can predict her bathroom times better. If there’s an accident inside, don’t punish her—just clean it up well to remove any scent. Over time, she’ll learn where she’s supposed to go. Stick with it!

Princess barks at the cat Felix. How could I make her stop? One way is to teach a quiet command. When she starts barking, calmly get her attention with a treat or her favorite toy. Once she pauses, say “quiet” and reward her immediately. Also, try to redirect her focus before she barks—maybe with a toy or a different command like “sit.” Over time, she’ll learn that being quiet gets her more rewards than barking at Felix. Consistency and staying calm are key!

What color collar would you suggest for her? She's brown and white. For a brown and white coat, colors like teal or turquoise really pop. Soft pinks or even a royal blue can also look great. It’s all about what matches her personality and stands out against her fur. Just pick something that makes her look even more adorable!
