For a boat trip-friendly Virgin Islands itinerary staying near Secret Harbour Beach Resort on St. John, here are pressure-change-free daytime adventures:

### 🏝️ Beach & Snorkeling
1. **Trunk Bay** (10-minute boat ride)  
   - *Why*: Famous for calm, protected waters, underwater observatory snorkeling trail. No pressure drops needed!  
   - *Birds to spot*: Tropicbirds, Anhinga (diving ducks), and colorful reef fish.

2. **Sandy Spit**  
   - *Why*: Shallow, calm bay with gentle waves—perfect for your brother. Great for kayaking or floating.

### 🚤 Boat-Centric Activities
1. **Sailing the Reef**  
   - Explore shallow coves like **Cinnamon Bay** (walkable from Cruz Bay) or **Laurie Bay**.  
   - *Pro tip*: Look for Cinnamon Flycatchers nesting in the mangroves.

2. **Glass-Bottom Boat Tour**  
   - Companies like **Secret Harbor** offer shallow excursions to see marine life without pressure changes.

### 🥾 Hiking & Nature
1. **Cinnamon Break Wall**  
   - Easy, flat trail to the historic lighthouse. Spot hummingbirds and parrots in the forest.

2. **Virgin Islands National Park** (Cruz Bay)  
   - Flat trails like **Honeymoon Trail** lead to secluded beaches and historic sites.  
   - *Bird highlight*: Antillean Crested Hummingbirds.

### 🍽️ Beach-Facing Stays Recap
- **Secret Harbour Beach Resort** (St. Thomas alternative): Private calm bay ideal for snorkeling right off shore.  
- **Gallows Point Resort** (St. John): Waterfront dining walk to Cruz Bay shops.

### ⚠️ Pressure-Change Avoidance
- All suggestions avoid hills, elevators, or depth changes. Stick to flat terrain and shallow water.  
- Hydrate well and watch for dizziness—pressure稳定时他可能会更舒适。
