You've touched on some fascinating layers of productivity psychology! Let's break down your questions:

### 1. **Color-Coding: Memory vs. Mental Load**
   - **Memory Aid**: Color-coding *can* enhance recall (e.g., red for urgent, blue for research).  
   - **Mental Load**: It *reduces* task-switching fatigue by pre-tagging priorities.  
   **Optimal Approach**: Use **2-3 colors max** (e.g., blue = active projects, green = admin). Avoid overcomplicating—simplicity prevents cognitive overload.

### 2. **Sound Switching: Timing vs. Preference**
   - **Personal Preference**: Dominant factor—some thrive with variety, others need consistency.  
   - **Focus Maintenance**: Switching every **45-60 minutes** (e.g., lo-fi → rain sounds) can refresh attention without disrupting flow.  
   **Tip**: Test both options for 3 days each. Track focus levels to see what works.

### 3. **Filing Systems: Progress Sources**
   - **Environment**: A clutter-free workspace reduces decision fatigue, freeing mental energy for deep work.  
   - **Chunking**: Breaking tasks (e.g., "1 hour of filing") creates micro-wins, boosting motivation.  
   **Verdict**: Both matter, but **chunking** often drives momentum faster. Tackle 1-2 hours of physical files on a weekend to reset your system.
