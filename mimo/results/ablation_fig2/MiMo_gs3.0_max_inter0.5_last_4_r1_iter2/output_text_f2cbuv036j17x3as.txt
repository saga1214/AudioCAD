Okay, diesel camper vans can be a handful before a long trip, but it's smart to check the basics. For brakes, the most important thing is to make sure they’re not squeaky or grinding—that’s a big red flag. You can also look at the pads and rotors; if they look worn down or cracked, that’s a problem. Also, if you’re seeing a lot of dust or smoke from the exhaust, that could be an issue with the diesel itself or the brakes. Now, for spark plugs—those are usually for diesel engines, which don’t really have spark plugs in the traditional sense. They use something called glow plugs, which help the engine start when it’s cold. If you’re changing those, you’ll need to get the right socket for the head and the right wrench for the nut. It’s not too complicated, but it’s definitely a diesel-specific job. Either way, having a mechanic do a pre-trip inspection is never a bad idea. They can check everything from the diesel filter to the brakes.
