Explained the difference: eye acts like biological sensor receiving visual info, retina has photoreceptor cells reacting to changes in light and movement patterns very quickly. Signals sent through optic nerve to brain. Eye can detect up to 60 hertz flashing light. But brain's processing interprets and smooths out, leading to continuous sense of motion. Saccades are rapid jumps in eye movements, and during saccadic masking, brain suppresses blur. So eye senses rapid visual stimuli quickly, but brain smooths, filters, and interprets that visual data. In essence, eye senses, brain interprets. Without processing, you'd notice jitter or blur. So in consideration of saccadic masking, eye doesn't see continuously. It sees in moments, and the brain fills in the gaps. Pronunciation of saccade is SAK-kuh-dee, like the word "sack" and "knee" put together. Hope that helps!
