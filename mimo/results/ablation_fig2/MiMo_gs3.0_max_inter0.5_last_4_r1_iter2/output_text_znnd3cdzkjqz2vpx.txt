Okay, you've got all the essentials, but a few more tools and materials could make the whole process much smoother and safer. 

First, shims. Habitat cabinets might not fit perfectly against the wall, so you'll need some wood shims to get them level underneath. A drill and some wall anchors are also a must-have. Studs won't always line up perfectly, and anchors will let you use those temporary boards I mentioned earlier safely. 

Safety gear is super important, too. Safety glasses to protect your eyes from dust, and work gloves for your hands are a good idea. And don't forget to grab a measuring tape to double-check everything as you go. 

With that stuff, you and your friends should be all set to get those cabinets installed!
