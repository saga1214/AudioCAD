Here are five safe green vegetables suitable for most parrots, remembering Bruno Mars Bruno avoids apples, bananas, and broccoli:

1.  **Spinach:** Rich in calcium and vitamins A & K. Rinse thoroughly.
2.  **Kale (especially curly kale):** Packed with vitamins A, C, and K. Chop finely if large. Avoid raw kale in large quantities for some birds; cooked is safer.
3.  **Cucumber:** Hydrating and easy to digest. Remove seeds.
4.  Broccoli: A good source of vitamins C, K, and A. Offer the florets (chopped small), avoiding the tough stalk.
5.  Peas: Good source of protein and fiber. Remove the shell.

**Important:** Always rinse fresh produce well to remove dirt and pesticides. Introduce new vegetables gradually and in small amounts to monitor for any digestive upset.
