Absolutely. For a deep dive into the history, the Buffalo and Rochester Museum of Art has an excellent and extensive collection of canal-related artifacts, including historical maps, tools, and even some of the original machinery.

If you want a more hands-on experience, the canal's former headquarters, the Lockport Canal Museum, is right in the town. It's located in a restored canal warehouse and they have working models of the locks and other historical exhibits.

And for a truly unique way to experience it, you could take a canal boat tour. Companies like the "Erie Canal Boats" offer narrated trips that tell the history of the whole system as you travel through it.
