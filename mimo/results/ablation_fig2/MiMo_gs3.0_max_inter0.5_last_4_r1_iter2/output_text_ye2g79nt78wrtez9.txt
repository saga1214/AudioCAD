Re-evaluating the numbers, Ancient Egypt does have a slight edge. Its universal recognition and incredible visual artifacts guarantee a large and engaged audience. But I think Ancient Mesopotamia is an equally valid, and arguably more profound, choice. It showcases the foundational pillars of human civilization. Ultimately, the best choice really depends on the museum's goals. If the goal is mass appeal, then yes, Ancient Egypt is the best choice. But if the goal is to highlight the origins of human innovation, then Ancient Mesopotamia would be the superior choice.
