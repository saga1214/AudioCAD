That's great to hear! Since you're feeling positive and ready to unwind, here are 5 simple, delicious dinner ideas to cook in that kitchen—easy to whip up with grocery store ingredients:

1. **Lemon Garlic Shrimp & White Bean Pasta**  
   Sauté shrimp with garlic, red pepper flakes, and white wine (if you have it), then toss with cooked pasta and white beans. Finish with lemon zest and fresh parsley. Serve with a simple arugula salad.

2. **Black Bean & Sweet Potato Taco Bowl**  
   Roast cubed sweet potatoes with cumin and chili powder. Mix with canned black beans, corn, avocado, salsa, and Greek yogurt. Top with cilantro and lime. Serve over rice or greens.

3. **Tomato-Basil Soup with Grilled Cheese**  
   Simmer canned tomatoes, garlic, onion, and fresh basil in a pot with vegetable broth until thick. Blend until smooth. Make grilled cheese with mozzarella, prosciutto, and pesto on sourdough.

4. **Mediterranean Grilled Fish**  
   Season a fillet (like sea bass or cod) with olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper. Grill or pan-sear until flaky. Serve over roasted potatoes and a Greek salad (cucumber, tomato, olives, red onion, feta).

5. **Caprese Stuffed Portobello Mushrooms**  
   Grill portobello caps. Fill with a mix of cherry tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, basil, and a drizzle of balsamic glaze. Top with a sprinkle of Parmesan and a little balsamic reduction.

**Pro tip:** Check the grocery store for fresh herbs (basil, cilantro, parsley) and citrus (lemons/limes)—they’ll make a huge difference!
