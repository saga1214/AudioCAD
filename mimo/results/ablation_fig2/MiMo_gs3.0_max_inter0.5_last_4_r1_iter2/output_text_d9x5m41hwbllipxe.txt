Okay, breaking it down for your fifteen hundred-square-foot home with four doors seven windows: 

First, the control hub. Self-install: about three hundred dollars. Professional with installation: over a thousand. 

Then the sensors. For your seven windows, professional systems might include glass break sensors for thirty to a hundred dollars each. Motion detectors are thirty to eighty dollars. And one indoor siren is thirty to seventy dollars. 

Self-install systems will include basic door/window sensors for fifteen to fifty dollars each. Professional systems add installation fees on top of the equipment cost. 

Monthly professional monitoring usually runs twenty to sixty dollars. Factor that into a five-year contract and your total monthly payment becomes twenty-five to eighty dollars. 

So overall, the average cost protects your 1500 square foot home with your described doors and windows.
