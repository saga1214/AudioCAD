Okay, Mexico in autumn is a fantastic idea for an aspiring artist. It's peak season for watercolors there. The foliage is just... vibrant, the light is amazing, and the atmosphere is inspiring. I'd say go early in the season, maybe late September or early October, before the crowds really hit. That way you can get those super rich, deep autumn colors on the trees. The light is also softer and warmer then, which is perfect for painting. Just bring your paints, find a quiet spot by a river or lake, and just let those techniques you're learning really take off. Enjoy it!
