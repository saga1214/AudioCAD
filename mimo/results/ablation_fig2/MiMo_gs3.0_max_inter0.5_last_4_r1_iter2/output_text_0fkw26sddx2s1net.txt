I'll also mention matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and vanilla extract for that ice cream option. Now for the produce: miso soup can be made with cucumber; stir-fried veggies could be things like bell peppers, snap peas, carrots, and a scallion. Jasmine rice for chicken teriyaki and sticky rice both go in the produce section. If you're making mango sticky rice, of course, you'll need ripe mangoes. And for the chicken teriyaki, you'll need fresh chicken breast. For dairy and pantry items, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, coconut milk for the two desserts, granulated sugar, salt, and a pinch of matcha powder.
