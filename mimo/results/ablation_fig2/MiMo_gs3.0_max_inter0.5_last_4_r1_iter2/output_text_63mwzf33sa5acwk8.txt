That's a smart pivot! Combining desert adventure with a Vegas reset is a classic van-camping combo. Here’s how to nail both parts seamlessly:

### 🌵 **Phase 1: Desert Adventure (Joshua Tree + Nevada)**
1. **Camping in Joshua Tree**  
   - **Best Spots**:  
     - **Jumbo Rocks Campground**: Iconic boulders + sunset views ($20/night, first-come-first-served).  
     - **Hidden Valley Picnic Area**: Quieter, near Hidden Valley Trail ($20).  
     - **Free BLM Land**: Head east of the park (e.g., **Mastodon Peak** or **Pioneertown**). *Pro Tip:* Download Gaia GPS or AllTrails for off-grid spots.  
   - **Drive Time**: ~3 hours from LA (allow 4-5 hours for scenic stops).  

2. **Nevada Detour: Valley of Fire**  
   - **Why?**: Red rock landscapes rival (and are less crowded than) Joshua Tree.  
   - **Camping**: **Valley of Fire Campground** ($15/night, near petroglyphs).  
   - **Drive Time**: 1.5 hours from Joshua Tree → 1 hour from Vegas.  

### 🎰 **Phase 2: Vegas Reset**
- **Stay**:  
  - **Budget**: Container Hotels (unique van-like rooms) or The Inn at Harrah’s (clean, no-frills).  
  - **Luxury**: Aria (spa + pool) or Cosmopolitan (lively vibe).  
- **"Proper Vegas" Experiences**:  
  - **Hot Shower + Coffee**: Any hotel. Splurge on **L’Atelier de Joël Robuchon** (Michelin-starred French).  
  - **Adventure**: **Red Rock Canyon** (45 mins from Vegas) → hiking + scenic drives.  
  - **Uniqueness**: **Area15** (immersive art/experiences) or **Mirage’s Volcano** (classic Vegas spectacle).  
  - **Romance**: **Eiffel Tower Bar** (Paris Vegas) or **Secret Room** (speakeasy with hidden jungle).  

### ⚠️ **Van Logistics**
- **Fuel**: Desert stretches have sparse gas stations → pack extra water/food.  
- **Cleanliness**: Use **Walmart parking lots** (24/7 free showers for RVs) or **Mighty Dog Wash** (van-friendly car washes).  
- **Safety**: BLM land = self-sufficiency. Bring first-aid, water filter, and a charged power bank.  

### 💡 **Relationship Bonus**
- **Shared Moment**: Stargaze at **Joshua Tree’s Dark Sky Park** (no light pollution).  
- **Vegas Bonding**: Try **comedy clubs** (local improv) or **karaoke** at **Golden Nugget**.  

### 🗺️ **Sample Route (5-6 Days)**
```plaintext
Day 1-2: Drive to Joshua Tree → Explore trails (Barker Dam, Arch Rock), camp.  
Day 3: Drive to Valley of Fire → Hike petroglyphs, camp.  
Day 4: Drive to Vegas → Red Rock Canyon, stay at hotel.  
Day 5: Vegas highlights (Area15, Mirage Volcano), late-night food.  
Day 6: Return to desert for sunrise → Drive home.  
```

**Total Cost Estimate**: $400-$600 (camping + gas + Vegas stay).
