For quick, crowd-pleasing candy, grab these Halloween staples to minimize waste and maximize smiles:  

### 🎃 Top Candy Picks (Buy in Bulk or Large Bags):  
1. **Chocolate:**  
   - **Snickers** (classic combo)  
   - **Reese’s Cups/Pieces** (universal favorite)  
   - **Milky Way** (soft, nostalgic)  
   - **3 Musketeers** (light and airy)  

2. **Gummies:**  
   - **Starburst** (tangy, shareable)  
   - **Sour Patch Kids** (sweet/sour contrast)  
   - **Gummy Worms/Bears** (fun shapes)  

3. **Other Hits:**  
   - **Butterfinger** (crunchy classic)  
   - **Candy Corn** (Halloween icon)  
   - **KitKats** (international crowd-pleaser)  
   - **Pretzels** (salty-sweet balance)  

### 💡 Pro Tips:  
- **Buy in Bulk:** Stores like Costco, Target, or bulk candy shops let you grab sampler packs.  
- **Mix & Match:** Combine 3–4 types in one big bowl for variety.  
- **Avoid Overstock:** Stick to 1–2 large bags total (aim for 100–150 pieces).  
- **Presentation:** Use a spooky-themed bowl or plastic cauldron for atmosphere!  

### 🍕 Food/Food Allergy Recap:  
- **Pizza:** Order multiple large slices (check crust is artificial dye-free; ask the pizzeria!).  
- **Safe Snacks:** Veggie sticks (wash thoroughly), fruit skewers, or popcorn (natural dye).  
- **Dips:** Hummus or guacamole (confirm dye-free ingredients).  

### ✨ Final Checklist:  
✅ Paper/plasticware: Dollar stores or Target  
✅ Drinks: Sparkling water, cider, or punch (dy-free)  
✅ Desserts: Pumpkin cookies or brownies (use all-natural dyes if needed)  
✅ Candy: Bulk buys of crowd-pleasers above  

You’ve got this! Focus on lighting, music, decorations, and pizza/candy—your fog machine will add magic. Prioritize guest safety (allergies) and fun over perfection. Tomorrow’s gonna be spooktacular! 🎪👻
