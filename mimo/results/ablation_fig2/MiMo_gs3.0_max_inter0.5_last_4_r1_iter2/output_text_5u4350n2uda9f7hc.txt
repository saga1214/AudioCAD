That east-facing fifth-floor NYC balcony is perfect for small, resilient plants.

Geraniums offer color and handle city winds. Herbs like basil provide simplicity and love morning light.

But size might be an issue given your tight space. Succulents on the table are ideal—they're small, sun-loving, and low-maintenance. The bird's nest snake plant on the floor is sturdy with tightly packed, green-and-silver leaves. It's lightweight and portable, so you can easily take it home after your onsite ends.
