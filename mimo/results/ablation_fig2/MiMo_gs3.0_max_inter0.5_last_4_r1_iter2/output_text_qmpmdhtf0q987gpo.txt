Okay, let's see where you are in the process. You've cleaned, light-sanded, and removed your hardware. The only other step I'd recommend before priming is to protect your countertops and floors. You can do this by applying painter's tape and then covering those areas with plastic sheeting.

This will keep your paint from getting on any surfaces you don't want it on. Once the surfaces are taped and covered, you can go ahead and apply your primer.
