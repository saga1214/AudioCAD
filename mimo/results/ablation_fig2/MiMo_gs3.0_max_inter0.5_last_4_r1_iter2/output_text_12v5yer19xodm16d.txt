Okay, let's see. You need a pick-me-up. For drinks, I'd suggest five things. First, an Iced Americano; it's coffee with water, so it gives you caffeine without the sugar crash. Second, Green Tea, which has a smaller amount of caffeine and some good antioxidants. Third, a Coconut Water, which is great for hydration and has a little bit of natural energy. Fourth, an Energizing Smoothie made with banana, spinach, and protein. And fifth, a Cold Brew Coffee, which is smooth and has a lot of caffeine.

For snacks, here are five ideas. One, Almonds, which are healthy fats and protein for sustained energy. Two, Dark Chocolate with at least seventy percent cocoa, which has caffeine and antioxidants. Three, Greek Yogurt, which is high in protein and can be paired with berries. Four, a Hard-Boiled Egg, another great source of protein. And five, an Apple with some Peanut Butter, for a mix of fiber, protein, and healthy fats. Hope that helps you get energized!
