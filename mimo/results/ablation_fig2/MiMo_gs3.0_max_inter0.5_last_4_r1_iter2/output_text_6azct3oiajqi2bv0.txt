For sticking fabric without damaging a lining, here are the best methods:

1.  **Double-Sided Tape (Best Option):**
    *   **Why:** It's invisible, strong, and won't fray or absorb moisture. Ideal for delicate linings.
    *   **How:** Peel off the paper backing from the sticky side, press it firmly onto the fabric where you need it, smooth out any wrinkles, then press the second piece of fabric onto the tape. Use small pieces for corners/edges.

2.  **Fabric-Safe Glue (Temporary Adhesive):**
    *   **Why:** Provides a strong bond without stretching the fabric. Look for brands specifically designed for fabrics (e.g., E6000 Fabric Glue, Aleene's Tacky Glue).
    *   **How:** Apply a *tiny* amount of glue to the back of the fabric piece you want to stick. Press firmly into place on the existing fabric. Hold for 30 seconds to a minute. Allow full curing time (usually 24-48 hours) before handling.

3.  **Fabric-Safe Glue Dots (For Small Areas):**
    *   **Why:** Very precise and easy to apply. Less mess than liquid glue.
    *   **How:** Apply a small dot of glue directly to the spot on the fabric you need to stick. Press the piece down firmly. Hold for a few seconds. Cure time varies by product (check the bottle).

4.  **Fabric Safety Pins (Temporary Fix):**
    *   **Why:** Quick and easy for a temporary hold.
    *   **How:** Pin the fabric piece securely through both layers (lining and outer fabric). **Caution:** Use pins carefully to avoid stabbing the lining. Remove when done.

**Key Tips:**

*   **Test First:** Always test your chosen method (tape, glue, pin) on a scrap piece of the *same* fabric as your lining to ensure it doesn't discolor, weaken, or damage it.
*   **Less is More:** Especially with glue, use the smallest amount necessary.
*   **Smooth & Press:** After applying tape or glue, firmly smooth the fabric piece down to eliminate air bubbles and ensure good contact.
*   **Cure Time:** Be patient! Glue needs time to fully set and bond before you can handle the garment normally.

For a jacket lining, **double-sided tape** is generally the safest and most reliable choice for a clean, temporary fix. **Fabric-specific glue** is a good alternative if a longer-lasting bond is needed. Avoid regular craft glue or super glue, as they can damage fabrics.
