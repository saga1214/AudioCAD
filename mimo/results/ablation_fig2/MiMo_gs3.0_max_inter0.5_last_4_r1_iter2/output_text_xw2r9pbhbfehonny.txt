Here's a step-by-step checklist for filming your puppet show responsibly and effectively for your own channel:

### **Before Filming**
1. **Secure Permission**  
   - Obtain **written consent** from the venue (school/hospital/nursing home) in advance.  
   - Specify filming location, duration, and audience (e.g., "Kids’ Distraction Room, Room 2A").  
   - *Never film in nursing homes without explicit approval* due to privacy laws.

2. **Gear Setup**  
   - **Camera:** Use your phone in landscape mode (9:16 vertical for TikTok/Reels).  
   - **Stabilization:** Prop phone on books/stacks of paper or use a cheap tripod.  
   - **Audio:** Attach a lavalier mic to your waist or a clip-on mic to a puppet’s hand.  
   - **Lighting:** Face a window or use a ring light to avoid shadows on puppets.

3. **Puppet & Script Prep**  
   - Test sock puppets for visibility under lights (bright socks like neon or white work best).  
   - Write a tight 5-7 minute script per puppet (improvisation can cause inconsistent audio/quality).  
   - Label puppets (e.g., "Melvin the Magnificent – Front View") for easy editing.

4. **Copyright Protection**  
   - Film on a separate device if possible to avoid accidental loss.  
   - Add a subtle watermark in the corner (e.g., "© [Your Name] Performance Services").

---

### **During the Show**
- **Engage the Audience:**  
  - Direct questions to kids (e.g., "Who thinks Melvin should practice more?").  
  - Avoid moving props quickly—puppet movements should be deliberate for the camera.  
- **Nervousness Hack:** Have an assistant film from the back so you focus on the audience.  
- **Pacing:** Use clear pauses between jokes/tricks for editing.

---

### **Post-Production**
1. **Edit Carefully:**  
   - Cut flubbed lines or long silences.  
   - Add simple text overlays (e.g., "Melvin’s Magic Trick #3") for clarity.  
   - Use royalty-free background music (e.g., YouTube Audio Library).

2. **Platform Strategy:**  
   - **YouTube:** Post full shows (8-10 mins) with captions.  
   TikTok/Instagram Reels: Edit into 60-second clips with trending sounds.  
   - **Monetization:** Disable ads to keep profits 0%. Enable "Child Safety Mode."

3. **Copyright Lockdown:**  
   - Upload to YouTube as "Creative Commons Attribution" (CC-BY) to require attribution.  
   - On TikTok/Instagram, use copyright claims to block unauthorized reposts.

---

### **Example Upload Workflow**
| **Platform** | **Action** | **Why** |
|--------------|------------|---------|
| YouTube | Upload full show + "Volunteer Puppeteer" description | Builds trust; avoids COPPA violations |
| TikTok | Clip "Melvin’s Worst Magic Trick" | Viral potential; drives traffic to YouTube |
| Instagram | Share behind-the-scenes setup | Humanizes your brand |

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Film bloopers as bonus content! Kids’ laughter during mistakes makes relatable "Outtakes" videos.

This balances your volunteer mission with controlled visibility. Start small—film 1-2 shows per month to build a library without burnout. Your passion is the real magic! 🎭✨
