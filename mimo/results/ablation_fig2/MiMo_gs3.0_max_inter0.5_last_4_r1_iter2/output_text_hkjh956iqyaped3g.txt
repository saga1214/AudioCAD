For a 15-minute break, especially your last one of the day, here are some focused suggestions to help you recharge without disrupting your focus:

1.  **Mindful Coffee Break (5-10 mins):** Instead of rushing, make your coffee a ritual. Sit quietly, focus on the aroma, the warmth of the mug, and the first sip. Spend the remaining time simply observing your surroundings or practicing deep, slow breathing (inhale for 4 counts, hold for 2, exhale for 6). This combines sensory awareness and relaxation.

2.  **Short, Intentional Walk (10-12 mins):** A brisk walk outside (or even pacing vigorously indoors) is excellent. Focus on the sensations: your feet hitting the ground, the air on your skin, the movement in your muscles. If you have stairs, take them up and down. This combines light exercise with mindfulness.

3.  **Quick Relaxation Reset (10-12 mins):** Lie down comfortably. Close your eyes and focus *only* on your breath for 1-2 minutes. Then, mentally scan your body from toes to head, consciously relaxing each muscle group (toes, calves, thighs, etc.). Spend the last few minutes just resting with eyes closed, letting any thoughts drift by without engaging. This is less jarring than meditation but still deeply restorative.

**Why these work for 15 minutes:**

*   **Mindful Coffee:** Combines a familiar comfort with focused calm, avoiding over-stimulation.
*   **Intentional Walk:** Provides physical movement and mental space without the "jarring" transition of deep meditation.
*   **Quick Relaxation:** Actively releases tension and resets your nervous system efficiently.

Avoid power naps for this short break – 15 minutes is often too short to reach deep rest and can sometimes leave you feeling groggy or more alert, potentially disrupting your work rhythm when you return. Any of these choices will help you finish your workday feeling refreshed and focused!
