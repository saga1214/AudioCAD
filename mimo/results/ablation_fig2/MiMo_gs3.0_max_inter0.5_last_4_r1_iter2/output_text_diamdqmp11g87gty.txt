Of course! Snowshoeing is a great way to get out and explore. You’ll need snowshoes, boots with good traction like microspikes, and some warm, waterproof clothing. A good hat and gloves are a must, and a backpack with snacks and water is always a good idea. Ice skating is another classic winter activity, and you can find public ice rinks in many towns. Dog sledding is also a really cool experience, and some places offer guided tours. And like you mentioned earlier, ice fishing is a great way to enjoy the frozen lakes. There are lots of ways to make winter fun if you get creative.
