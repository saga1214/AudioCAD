That's a fantastic asset! If you can do the art, you have a huge advantage. You can create your own book and self-publish it. This is often the most efficient way for an author-artist.

You would simply create the final book, with both the text and the illustrations, and then submit it directly to a publisher. There are several platforms that specialize in this, like a company called 'Ivy+Books' or 'Schiffer Books'. They'll publish your work for a fee.

Alternatively, you could also submit your finished manuscript and illustrations to the same literary agents who represent children's books. They might be able to sell your complete package to a traditional publisher.
