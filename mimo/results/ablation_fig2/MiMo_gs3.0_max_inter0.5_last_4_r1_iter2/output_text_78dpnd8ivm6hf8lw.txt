Here’s a quick recap using your car analogy:

1. **Heart**: Gas station + engine  
2. **Lungs**: Refueling pit stop for fresh oxygen-fuel  
3. **Arteries**: Big highways carrying blood *away* from the heart  
4. **Capillaries**: Tiny neighborhood streets where oxygen gets delivered  
5. **Veins**: Roads carrying blood *back* to the heart for refueling  
6. **Kidneys**: Car washes that filter out waste and extra water  
7. **Liver**: Service station that sorts nutrients, cleans fuel, and breaks down toxins  

Together, they keep all your "blood cars" running smoothly—delivering energy and cleaning up messes! 🚗💨
