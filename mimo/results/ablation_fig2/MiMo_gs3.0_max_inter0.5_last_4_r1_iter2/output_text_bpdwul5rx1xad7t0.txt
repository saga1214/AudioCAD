Of course! With a Toyota SUV, you're ready for some incredible road trips in the Pacific Northwest. Here are five fantastic options for you and your growing family.

First, there's Vancouver Island in British Columbia. You could explore the scenic coast and visit the famous Butchart Gardens. For food, you can find amazing seafood at the Nanaimo Harbour or try farm-to-table dining in the Cowichan Valley.

Second, you could head to the Okanagan Lake area. It's famous for its beautiful vineyards and lakeside resorts. You can take a winery tour or just relax by the water. For dining, you could try a lakeside seafood chowder or a gourmet farm dinner.

Third, there's the Fraser Canyon. It's great for hiking and exploring historic towns like Bellingham. You can go wine tasting or visit the local farmers' market. For a meal, you could find a farm-to-table restaurant or a cozy B&B with a full breakfast.

Fourth, you could take a trip to the Sunshine Coast. It's known for its charming villages and stunning beaches. You can enjoy a scenic ferry ride or a kayaking tour. For dining, you could find a seaside restaurant or a farmstay with a great breakfast.

And fifth, you could drive up to Whistler. It's great for hiking and skiing year-round, and there's a lot to do in the summer. You can go for a scenic gondola ride or try some local craft breweries. For food, you can find upscale dining or a cozy mountain lodge with hearty meals.

I hope you have an amazing time exploring the region!
