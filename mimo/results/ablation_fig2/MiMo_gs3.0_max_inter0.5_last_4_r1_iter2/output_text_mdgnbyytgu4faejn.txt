Okay, you want a clear, step-by-step blueprint for your resume. I can absolutely give you that. Just follow this structure, and you'll have a professional resume in no time.

First, at the very top, is your name in about a fourteen-point font. To its right—without icons and using nine-point font—are your email, phone number, city you live in, expected graduation date, and any other location-specific details you need to include.

Right below your name goes a short Professional Summary. This is where you'll briefly state your most relevant skills and career goals.

Next, you'll have your Relevant Experience section. Here, you'll highlight the older jobs that are a perfect match for the new roles you want. List each job with the company name, your title, the dates you worked there, and just a sentence or two about your key responsibilities and achievements.

After that, create an Additional Work History section. This is for listing your more recent jobs. Just list your job title, the company, and the dates. You can skip the detailed descriptions here.

Then comes your Education section. List your degrees, the schools you attended, and any specific honors you received, like graduating with highest honors.

If you want, you can also add a dedicated Skills section. This is a great place to list out your key skills, maybe even breaking them down into categories like Technical Skills or Languages.

Finally, you can include a section for Awards and Certifications, listing any significant ones you've received.

Just remember to remove icons and list each employer separately for clarity. This format will make your resume look clean, traditional, and easy for recruiters to scan.
