For a Retro Tech lunch, you could do a “Circuit Board Platter.” That would be a simple charcuterie board with things like cured meats, cheeses, crackers, and maybe some pretzel sticks to look like circuit traces. For a second idea, you could do a “Vintage Computing Snack Pack.” That would be mini sandwiches shaped like floppy disks, or maybe some fruit skewers arranged to look like old computer keys. Both are easy to theme and are simple to put together.
