That’s sweet of you! Since vegetarianism is her thing, you’ll want to include at least one solid veggie option, but you could also go all-out plant-based if you’re feeling adventurous. Here are some ideas:  
- **Mushroom risotto** – rich, creamy, and hearty.  
- **Tofu or eggplant parmesan** – if she likes that Italian vibe.  
- **Stuffed bell peppers** – with quinoa, black beans, and cheese (or feta if you’re keeping it vegetarian).  
- **Pad Thai** – if you want something fun and customizable.  
- **Shepherd’s pie** – just make sure the meat version has lentils mixed in for her.  
- **Grilled veggie pizza** – easy, crowd-pleasing, and customizable.  
If you want to impress, maybe a nice salad with avocado, grilled chicken (for you), and a vegan cheese option for her. Hope you both have a great dinner!
