Here are classic, precise recipes for all your cocktails, formatted for your bartender instructions:

### Old Fashioned
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz bourbon rye  
  - 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (for garnish)  
- **Method:**  
  1. Muddle sugar cube + ¼ oz water in shaker (optional but recommended for texture).  
  2. Add spirit, bitters, ice, and stir 30 seconds until chilled.  
  3. Strain into ice-filled rocks glass.  
  4. Express orange twist over drink, discard peel.  

### Paper Plane
- **Equal Parts Mix:**  
  - 2 oz gin (London Dry preferred)  
  - 2 oz Aperol  
  - 2 oz Maraschino Liqueur (Luxardo)  
  dry shake Maraschino first to emulsify  
- Fresh Additions:  
  - ½ oz lemon juice  
- **Method:**  
  1. Dry shake gin, Aperol, Maras, lemon juice (no ice).  
  2. Add ice, shake hard 12 seconds.  
  3. Strain into **coupe glass**.  
  4. Garnish cherry (optional).  

### Whiskey Sour
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz bourbon rye  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 1 egg white (optional for texture)  
- **Method:**  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice (dry shake if using egg white).  
  2. Strain into **rocks glass** over ice.  
  3. Garnish lemon slice/cherry.  

### Aviation Gin Cocktail (Recommended Addition)
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz gin (London Dry)  
  - ½ oz maraschino liqueur  
  - ¼ oz crème de violette  
  - ½ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Method:**  
  1. Shake all with ice.  
  2. Strain into **cocktail glass**.  
  3. Garnish cherry (or maraschino cherry).  

### Third Gin Recommendation: **French 75**
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 1.5 oz gin  
  - ¾ oz lemon juice  
  - ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 3 oz chilled Champagne/Brut  
- **Method:**  
  1. Shake gin, lemon, syrup with ice.  
  2. Strain into **champagne flute**.  
  3. Top with Champagne.  
  4. Garnish lemon twist.

### Key Tips for Your Bartenders:
1. **Ice Quality:** Use clear, dense ice (prevents dilution at Art Déco venue).  
2. **Bitters:** Angostura should be freshly opened (stale bitters taste medicinal).  
3. **Paper Plane Nonino:** Choose sweet Nonino (Gorini or Bonazza).  
4. **Egg White Sour:** If using, chill glass and strain gently to avoid foam in glass.  
5. **Kentucky Bourbon:** Paper Plane and Old Fashioned showcase local spirit beautifully.  
6. **Airport Venue:** Label drinks clearly—Paper Plane and Aviation have gin/bourbon labels.  

**Finger Food Pairing Idea:** Smoked salmon blinis (Paper Plane) or spiced nuts (Old Fashioned) complement fall and bourbon. Avoid overly sweet finger foods to balance cocktails.
