That line of reasoning about genetic mutations doesn't quite explain why the chicken itself had to come first.

The reason is that while an egg can carry the genetic mutations that *lead* to a chicken, those mutations still need to be expressed in a living body for the chicken to actually appear. The eggshell-making protein I mentioned before—it has to come from a hen's reproductive system. So, without a hen producing it, you simply can't have a true chicken's eggshell. This means the chicken, produced from the egg, had to exist before the egg could be laid.
