### BC U-Turn Rules & Penalties:
1. **150-Meter Visibility Rule**:  
   You must be able to see **at least 150 meters** in both directions before turning. If there’s a hill, curve, or obscured view, it’s illegal.  
   - **Estimation Tip**: 150m ≈ 1.5 football fields. If you can’t see a clear gap that long, don’t turn.  

2. **Prohibited Locations**:  
   - Intersections with traffic lights  
   - Near driveways, crosswalks, or pedestrians  
   - On highways, bridges, or curves  
   - Where signs explicitly forbid U-turns  

3. **Penalties**:  
   - **Fine**: Up to **$160** (plus court costs) in BC.  
   - **License Points**: 3 demerit points.  
   - **Safety Risk**: Impaired visibility can cause collisions.  

**Key Takeaway**: When in doubt, wait at a legal intersection.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Recipe (for 2):  
**Ingredients**:  
- **Pasta**: 200g spaghetti (or bucatini)  
- **Eggs**: 2 large eggs + 1 extra yolk (for richness)  
- **Cheese**: 100g grated Pecorino Romano (or Parmesan)  
- **Cured Meats**: 100g diced pancetta or guanciale (pancetta is easier to find)  
- **Garlic**: 1 clove, minced (optional)  
- **Black Pepper**: Freshly cracked (essential!)  
- **Salt**: For pasta water  

**Instructions**:  
1. Cook pasta in salted water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
2. Fry diced pancetta/guanciale in a pan until crisp. Add garlic (if using) and cook 30 seconds.  
3. Whisk eggs, yolk, cheese, and pepper in a bowl.  
4. **Critical Step**: Drain pasta, add to the meat pan, and toss quickly. Remove from heat.  
5. Pour egg mixture over pasta, stirring vigorously. Add reserved pasta water 1 tbsp at a time until creamy (not runny).  
6. Serve immediately with extra cheese and pepper.  

**Substitutions**:  
- Guanciale → Pancetta → Bacon  
- Pecorino → Parmesan (or mix)  
- Eggs can be replaced with 1/4 cup grated cheese + 1 tbsp cream (for extra richness).  

**Pro Tip**: Don’t add hot pasta to eggs directly—they’ll scramble. Toss off-heat and add pasta water to emulsify!  

Enjoy your meal, and drive safely! 🍝🚗💨
