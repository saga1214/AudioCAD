For a show-stopping Thanksgiving dessert, you could try making an easy pumpkin pie. It requires no baking, just blending all the ingredients together in a bowl and then baking it.

For a much easier option, you could assemble some mini pies. You can buy pre-made crusts and fill them with a simple sweet potato filling.

And for a classic main dish, turkey roulade is a great choice. It's just slices of turkey rolled up with stuffing inside, then baked.
