For wedding speeches, the average pace is about 125 words per minute. So for a five-minute speech, seven hundred and fifty words is the ideal target. Structure your speech with an introduction about your long friendship, include one vivid but concise anecdote, observe her relationship develop, close with a heartfelt toast, and account for natural pauses. If you tend to speak quickly when nervous, writing about fifteen percent more, say around 850 to 900 words, can help you stay on track. Practice reading it aloud to get a feel for the natural rhythm.
