 Now for the full vacation plan (4-5 days, family of three, SUV needed, warm/wet/swim-friendly, healthy budget):

 Here’s a warm, sunny, swimming-focused itinerary for Northern California, leveraging your Northern location and budget:

 Destination Recommendation: Palm Springs, CA

 Why Palm Springs?
 * Consistently warm (70s-80s Fahrenheit in winter) far from the cooling trend in NoCal.
 * World-renowned for swimming pools - resort pools, natural pools like Natural Hot Springs State Park.
 * Scenic desert beauty complements the warmth.
 * Easy drive from LA/Orange County areas (which are closer to NoCal than Palm Springs proper, but a great base), about 3-4 hours from places like San Francisco or Sacramento.

 The Itinerary:

 Day 1: Arrival & Settling In (Palm Springs)
   * Arrive early afternoon.
   * Check into your hotel/resort. I recommend an all-inclusive resort for maximum swim/diet-friendly convenience.
   * Lunch: Grab casual Mexican food near your hotel (many have great GF options - Trifol is a top choice for GF Mexican).
   * Afternoon: Head straight to the resort pool for relaxation and maybe a swim. Enjoy the amenities.
   * Evening: Dinner at the resort restaurant (ensure they can accommodate GF diet). Sunset over the mountains!

 Day 2: Desert Adventure & Swimming (Palm Springs)
   * Morning: Explore the downtown area. Browse unique shops, art galleries. Visit the Palm Springs Art Museum (check for GF cafe).
   * Lunch: Casual downtown spot or picnic at a beautiful spot like El Paseo.
   * Afternoon: Head to a resort with a large pool complex. Some famous ones are The Ph氧 (luxury) or Aerial (mid-range, great views). Focus on fun in the water!
   * Evening: Dinner at a lively restaurant like Guelaguetza (Oaxacan, fantastic GF menu) or Café 2112 (French bistro, excellent GF).

 Day 3: Warm Water & Scenic Views (Palm Springs Area)
   * Morning: Drive to Natural Hot Springs State Park (about 45-60 mins east). Soak in the warm, mineral-rich natural springs surrounded by mountains. Dress comfortably for the mud! (Wear swimsuit underneath clothes).
   * Lunch: Pack a picnic or find a quick spot en route.
   * Afternoon: Explore the beautiful Coachella Valley. Drive through the canyon or hike a short, easy trail like the one at Joshua Tree State Park (short drive away) for incredible desert vistas.
   * Evening: Relax back at your resort pool. Dinner at the resort or try a trendy Palm Springs spot like Gazzola (Italian, great GF pasta) or The Little Dojo (Asian fusion, excellent GF).

 Day 4: Pool Party & Departure (Palm Springs)
   * Morning: Enjoy another day at your resort pool. Maybe try the lazy river or slides if available.
   * Lunch: Resort cafe or nearby restaurant.
   * Afternoon: Last swim session before departure. Do some souvenir shopping or enjoy the resort spa.
   * Departure: Head home in the late afternoon.

 Day 5 (Optional): If you need an extra day, repeat Day 3 (Natural Springs & Scenic Views) or find another warm pool resort to explore.

 Where to Stay (All-Inclusive Resorts - Key for your goals):
   * **Luxury:** The Ph氧 (Palm Springs) - Stunning architecture, multiple pools, incredible spa, high-end dining with GF options.
   * **Mid-Range:** Aerial Hotel & Spa - Beautiful desert modern design, large central pool, hot tub, great views, excellent resort dining with GF focus.
   * **Budget-Friendly w/ Pools:** Hotel Vantage Palm Springs - Well-rated, good pool area, central location, decent dining options.

 Where to Eat (GF-Friendly Focus):
   * **Downtown:** Trifol (Top GF Mexican), Guelaguetza (Top GF Oaxacan), Café 2112 (Top GF French Bistro), The Little Dojo (Top GF Asian Fusion).
   * **Resort Dining:** Most major resorts have excellent restaurants that can handle GF needs well. Just confirm when booking!
   * **Casual:** You'll find many casual spots near resorts like The Lark (GF burgers/sandwiches) or local cafes.

 Getting Around: Renting an SUV is ideal! It's comfortable for a family of three on longer drives. You'll cover resort grounds, downtown exploration, and trips to Natural Hot Springs and Joshua Tree.

 Budget: With a healthy budget, you can stay at a nice mid-range or luxury resort, enjoy multiple meals out (including special dinners), and cover rental car, gas, and activities. All-inclusive resorts can simplify spending.

 Have an absolutely wonderful and warm Northern California getaway! This plan focuses on your love of the water, escaping the cold you dislike back home, celebrating your daughter's birthday spirit, and enjoying the Celiac-friendly aspects.
