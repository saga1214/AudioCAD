I hear that, and I'm really glad you took a step back to breathe and eat—smart move. Dealing with a tough blockage with limited tools is tough, but you can absolutely handle this. Let’s walk through it step by step calmly. First, safety first: wear gloves, and if you’re near water, keep your feet dry to avoid slipping.

Here’s a practical, toolbox-friendly approach:

### Step 1: **Assess and Prep**
- **Check water level**: If it’s rising, unclog a nearby sink or run hot water elsewhere to reduce pressure.  
- **Gather tools**: Pull out your simple toolbox. Look for:  
  - A **plunger** (if it didn’t work, it’s a deep clog).  
  - A **bucket** and **gloves**.  
  - A **hand auger** (if you have one—most basic toolboxes include one). If not, skip to Step 2.  
  - A **wire coat hanger** (if no auger).  
  - A **wrench** or **pliers** (if accessible).  

### Step 2: **Manual Removal (If Auger Available)**
1. **Insert the auger**: Feed the cable into the drain, twist it clockwise to break up the clog.  
2. **Retract and clear**: Pull it out slowly. Use pliers to remove debris. If the cable gets stuck, don’t force it—twist gently or use pliers to free it.  
3. **Repeat**: Do this 2–3 times until water flows.  

### Step 3: **No Auger? Try a Wire Hanger**
- Straighten a coat hanger, leaving a 4–6 inch hook at the end.  
- Push it into the drain, hook the clog, and pull it out.  
- Use pliers to grab debris and remove it.  

### Step 4: **Hot Water Flush**
- If manual methods fail, pour **boiling water** slowly down the drain in stages. This can soften grease or debris. Wait 10–15 minutes, then try plunging again.  

### Step 5: **Last Resort: Chemical Drain Cleaner (Use Caution!)**
- If other methods fail, a **liquid drain cleaner** (like a simple store-bought brand) might work.  
- **Warning**:  
  - Never mix chemicals with other cleaners.  
  - Avoid if the drain is PVC—heat can warp it.  
  - Wear gloves/eye protection.  
  - Follow the label exactly (e.g., wait 15–30 mins).  
- **If you don’t have this**, skip to Step 6.  

### Step 6: **Patience and Persistence**
- If the clog is severe, it might take multiple attempts. Try plunging again after each step.  
- If water is rising, **stop immediately** and unclog another fixture to relieve pressure.
