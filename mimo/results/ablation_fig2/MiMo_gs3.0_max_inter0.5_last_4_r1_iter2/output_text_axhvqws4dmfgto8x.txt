Here’s the key for stir-fry noodles: use rice noodles instead of pasta. They are almost impossible to set a fire off from. You just soak them in hot water for about 15 minutes until they are tender.

For the stir-fry itself, the main rule is to have everything chopped and ready to go before you turn on the heat. This makes timing much easier. And if you need to use wooden items again, opt for wooden *chopsticks* – spoons in this case are best metal. Good luck
