Here are effective, fabric-friendly ways to hem a jacket without damaging the lining:

### 1. **Fabric Glue (Best for Most Jackets)**
   - **Steps**:  
     - Clean the inside hem and test glue on a scrap of the lining first.  
     - Apply a generous amount of fabric glue (e.g., Aleene’s Fabric Fusion or E6000) to the *right side* of the hem.  
     - Press the hem down firmly, smoothing out wrinkles.  
     - Hold for 1–2 minutes, then press with a heavy book for 10–15 minutes to activate the bond.  
   - **Why it works**: Creates a strong, invisible seal that prevents fraying. Sets permanently.

### 2. **Iron-On Hem Tape (Quick & Secure)**
   - **Steps**:  
     - Cut a strip of wide hem tape (about 1–2 inches long).  
     - Apply heat with an iron (no steam) to the *right side* of the tape for 10–15 seconds.  
     - Press the tape onto the raw edge of the hem, smoothing out bubbles.  
     - Hold for 10 seconds with the iron to activate the adhesive.  
   - **Tip**: Use a low-shine tape for better adhesion on synthetic fabrics.

### 3. **Double-Side Fabric Tape (For Visible Hems)**
   - **Steps**:  
     - Cut a strip of double-sided tape (e.g., HeatnBond Ultra Hold).  
     - Peel off the backing, apply adhesive-side-down to the *right side* of the hem.  
     - Press the hem onto the tape, smoothing from the center outward.  
     - Hold for 10 seconds with an iron to bond.  
   - **Best for**: Knits or stretchy fabrics where glue might weaken elasticity.

### ⚠️ **Critical Tips**:
   - **Always test** on a hidden seam first.  
   - **Avoid water-based glues** (they’ll warp synthetics).  
   - **For heavy fabrics** (e.g., denim), use tape or glue + a safety pin to hold while drying.  
   - **If lining is delicate**, skip glue/tape—use a simple fold-over cuff instead.

### 🛠️ **Pro Fix for Future Needs**:
If you plan to buy functional outerwear (like from REI’s Co-op), consider a **tailor for a permanent hem**. They’ll use a zigzag stitch or serged edge to ensure durability. For now, fabric glue or tape should give you a temporary solution that won’t tear your lining.
