Here are five fantastic options on the scenic route from Vancouver that are perfect for exploring the beautiful BC coast.

First, there's the classic **Coastal Route to Tofino**. This takes about four hours to drive. Along the way, you can stop in Victoria for some famous BC oysters and maybe a visit to the Butchart Gardens. Then, on the drive north, you can visit the amazing U.S. Olympic National Park to see the Hoh Rainforest. The highlight of this trip is Tofino, which has incredible surf beaches, wild Pacific coastline, and it's the gateway to the world-famous Pacific Rim National Park.

Second, for a little more distance, you can head to **Victoria and the Gulf Islands**. This is about a two-and-a-half-hour drive. In Victoria, you can explore the inner harbour, visit the Fairmont Empress Hotel for a classic British Columbia experience, and see the famous Empress Room for their afternoon tea. You can also take a ferry to the Gulf Islands to explore the charming little towns and see the famous lighthouse on Saanich Inlet.

Third, you can head to the **San Juan Islands**. This is about a four-and-a-half-hour drive. This is a great trip for a Golden Retriever, as it's famous for its sea otters and resident orcas. You can take a ferry to either the San Juan or Vancouver Island side. On San Juan, you can visit the San Juan Islands, which are known for whale watching and charming towns. On Vancouver Island, you could visit the town of Tofino or the charming community of Ucluelet.

Fourth, for something a bit different, you can take a **scenic drive to Revelstoke**. This is about a five-hour drive. The road up is called the Trans-Canada Highway, and it's one of the most beautiful in Canada. You'll pass through Kootenay National Park. The town of Revelstoke is a world-class ski resort in the winter, but in the summer, it's a paradise for hiking, mountain biking, and fly-fishing.

And fifth, for a longer adventure, you can drive to **Yellowknife**. This is a long trip of about twelve hours, but it's an incredible journey through the rugged northern Rockies. You'll see the famous Columbia Icefield and the Athabasca Glacier. The highlight is Yellowknife, the capital of the Northwest Territories. You can visit the Great Northern Spirit Museum and see the Northern Lights in the winter.
