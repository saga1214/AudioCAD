Both succulents and snake plants are excellent for New York City balconies and are very manageable for you. They love bright morning light from an east-facing balcony, get along well with urban conditions, and need very little water. Since you're only renting, they're perfect since you can take them home after your onsite work is done.

And you're right to think about the weight. They are both lightweight.

Most succulents stay small and their pots are usually light. Snake plants are even better. The plant itself is sturdy but has a very low center of gravity, so it won't be top-heavy. The pot might be a little heavier, but it's still something you can easily carry up five flights of stairs.

So yes, they are both great choices for making your Airbnb feel more like home without being a hassle to transport.
