Here's a quick recap using the child-friendly car analogy:

- **Heart**: Gas station + engine (pumps cars/blood and provides fuel/oxygen)
- **Lungs**: Lung-shaped gas stations (where blood gets fresh oxygen fuel)
- **Arteries**: Big highways (carry blood *away* from the heart)
- **Capillaries**: Tiny neighborhood streets (where blood *delivers* oxygen to cells)
- **Veins**: Return roads (carry blood *back* to the heart gas station)
- **Kidneys**: Super-filters or car washes (clean the blood of waste and extra water)
- **Liver**: Recycling center (processes nutrients and breaks down harmful stuff)

This keeps the core concept consistent while covering major functions.
