Those are excellent and very important points.

While the connection you have is incredibly valuable, your number one priority in those first 20 to 30 minutes actually needs to be managing his environment to ensure everyone's safety.

Given the dogs running around, the house being cluttered, and the elopement and aggression, you need to create a safe space first.

Here’s what I would suggest:
First, before each session, try to arrange with whoever is left there to confine the dogs to another room.
Second, if possible, spend just a few minutes at the start to tidy up the client's immediate space. This can make a huge difference.
And third, have a clear safety plan in place. That might mean having a break-out space or a visual schedule to let him know when sessions are ending.

Once you've set up a calmer, safer environment, then you can focus on play and reinforcing activities to maintain that positive connection. Safety always comes first.
