### 🧵 Additional Measurement Advice (consider for **more fitted garments**)
1.  **For Tops, Jackets, and Shirts:**

    *   Inseam (distance from crotch seam to hem - measure the actual leg!)

    *   Rise (distance from crotch to waistband - *front* and *back* rise are both important*)
3.  **For Great Fit on Jackets/Jests:**
    *   Shoulder Width (reiterating - super important!)
