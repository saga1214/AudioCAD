You're right, I see where the confusion came from. Let me clarify. The most widely recognized Boulevardier has whiskey to one and a quarter, vermouth at one, and Campari at one. Your friend's equal-parts version, all three at one, is also classic. Then, completely separate from that, you have the 'Last Word,' which is its own drink, made with equal parts gin, three-quarters of an ounce each of green Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime. So Boulevardier comes in two main styles, while the Last Word is a third cocktail. Hope that clears it up!
