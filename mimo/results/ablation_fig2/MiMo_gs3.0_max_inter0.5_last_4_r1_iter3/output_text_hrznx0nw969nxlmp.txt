That's a great combo – Secret Harbour's calm beach and Red Hook's energy! Here are some fantastic daytime activities on Secret Harbour/Trunk Bay in Cruz Bay area:

1.  **Snorkel the Trunk Bay Wall:** Just a short walk from Secret Harbour, take the Trunk Bay boat tour (often available from the resort dock). The wall is incredible for snorkeling, teeming with fish, turtles, and rays. Easy access right off the shore.
2.  **Explore Reef Bay Trail:** Hike this stunning trail (about 1.5 miles round trip) starting near Cruz Bay. It winds through lush rainforest to a secluded, beautiful beach with a freshwater spring. Perfect for a peaceful walk and finding a hidden gem.
3.  **Relax at Cruz Bay Beach:** Literally steps from Gallows Point Resort/Secret Harbour, this public beach offers calm waters perfect for swimming or sunbathing. Grab a drink at Gallows Point Ocean for sunset views overlooking it.
4.  **Rent Kayaks or Paddleboards:** Several providers (like Red Hook Kayak) offer rentals right in Cruz Bay. Paddle around the mangroves, explore the bay, or even paddle out to Secret Harbour.
5.  **Visit Coral World Ocean Monument:** Take a boat trip (often from Cruz Bay dock) to this amazing aquarium-like structure submerged 30 feet below the surface. Explore underwater tunnels and exhibits showcasing local marine life – perfect for your brother avoiding pressure changes while seeing cool stuff up close!
6.  **Explore Red Hook:** Walk the vibrant streets of Cruz Bay, lined with colorful shops, art galleries, and cafes. Browse local crafts, find unique souvenirs, and soak in the island atmosphere.
7.  **Hike to Waterlemon Cay:** From the Cruz Bay trailhead, you can hike to this beautiful, undeveloped island beach with a small freshwater spring. It's a more adventurous hike but offers a truly secluded reward.
8. **Visit Maho Bay:** Take a short taxi or boat ride to Maho Bay, the epicenter of birding on St. John. You'll see abundant seabirds, including the famous "seabird highway" where migrating birds land on the beach. A must for your birding interest.

**Key things to know for St. John:**
*   **Trunk Bay:** Requires a short boat ride from Cruz Bay (often available from Secret Harbour dock).
*   **Trails:** Cruz Bay Trail and Waterlemon Cay trail are both excellent starting points near Secret Harbour/Gallows Point.
*   **Cruz Bay Beach:** Right next door, easy access.
*   **Red Hook/Cruz Bay:** The heart of dining and shopping is just a walk away.
