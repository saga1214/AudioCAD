Of course. There are a lot of phrases like that. For instance, there’s “Don’t put all your eggs in one basket,” which means you shouldn’t rely on just one thing. “The early bird catches the worm” means acting early gives you an advantage. And “Let sleeping dogs lie” means to avoid stirring up old problems. As for anniversary plans, if loud places and citrus rules limit restaurants, you could pivot to a cozy private room, dim lighting at a quiet wine bar, or even a special takeout from a non-citrus restaurant. For drinks, you could get non-citrus cocktails like a mango mojito—since mango isn't citrus—or a smoky margarita, which is citrus-flavored but isn't based *in* citrus. For the note, something short: “You’re my quiet refuge and my loudest cheerleader. Happy anniversary to my partner and best adventure. Here’s to us.” That should help you make the evening feel special, no matter what.
