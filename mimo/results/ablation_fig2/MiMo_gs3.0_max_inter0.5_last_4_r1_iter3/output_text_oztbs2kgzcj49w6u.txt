The Erie Canal Museum in Lockport is the perfect place to start. It's located right next to the locks, so you can learn about the history of the canal while actually seeing it in action. The museum has exhibits on the engineering behind the locks, the daily life of canal workers, and the economic impact of the canal on the entire region. It's a great place to see how the city of Lockport became the "Gateway to the West."

If you prefer a more hands-on experience, you could also take a boat tour. Many companies offer narrated tours that take you right through the locks, so you can see them working up close. It's a great way to experience the canal and learn about its history at the same time.
