Here's a **clothing gear packing list** tailored for your 20-night miles + final-day 12 miles, optimized for mid-30s, hilly terrain, and aid stations:

### **Daytime Pacing (20 Miles)**  
1. **Base Layer**: Moisture-wicking shirt (short or long-sleeve) + leggings/shorts.  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece or light windbreaker (removable if temps dip).  
3. **Outer Layer**: Wind/water-resistant jacket (e.g., lightweight rain shell).  
4. Borrow from aid stations: Extra gloves, hat, or buff if needed.  
5. **Footwear**: Trail runners with grip + gaiter (for dust/debris).  
6. **Headlamp Handheld**: Bright, with spare batteries.  
7. **Hat**: Lightweight baseball cap or visor.  

### **Night Pacing (Final 12 Miles)**  
1. **Base Layer**: Moisture-warking shirt + leggings/shorts.  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece or thin puffy jacket (adjustable warmth).  
3. **Outer Layer**: Warm, windproof shell (e.g., down-filled jacket).  
4. **Headwear**: Warm beanie + gloves (trail gloves + insulated pair).  
5. **Footwear**: Trail runners + gaiter.  
6. **Headlamp/Flashlight**: Extra batteries + head strap.  
7. **Battery Pack**: Portable charger for headlamp/headphone.  
8. **Lightweight Pack/Vest**: To carry layers between aid stations.  

### **Pro Tips**  
- **Layers Over Bulk**: Opt for packable items (e.g., stuff sack for fleece).  
- **Aid Station Savvy**: Grab gloves, hat, or warm layers there if needed.  
- **Battery Backup**: Critical for night pacing—don’t skimp!  
- **Weather Flexibility**: Hilly = variable wind; hilly = variable wind.  

**Avoid**: Heavy cotton, bulky sweaters, or non-breathable materials. You’ve got this! 🏃‍♀️💨
