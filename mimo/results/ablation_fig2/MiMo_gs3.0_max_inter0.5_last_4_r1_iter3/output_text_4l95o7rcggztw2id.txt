Here’s a **nautical/pirate-themed shopping list** focusing on **sand-free finger foods**, allergy considerations (avoiding watermelon), beach-friendly packaging, and immersive party vibes:

---

### **Nautical Food & Drink Shopping List**  
*(For 7 kids + 1 host = 8 servings)*  

#### **1. Appetizers (Themed Snack Boxes)**  
- **Pirate Treasure Chests**: Small cardboard treasure chests filled with:  
  - Goldfish crackers (labeled "Gold Coins")  
  - Cheese cubes (cut into "gold" shapes)  
  - Mini pretzel rods ("Ship Masts")  
  - Apple slices (dipped in lemonade for "dripping seawater")  
  - Popcorn ("Ship’s Rations")  
- *Allergy Note*: Avoid watermelon, but include grapes if safe for other guests.  

#### **2. Main Course**  
- **Mini Pirate Ship Sliders** (4 per person):  
  - Mini hot dogs or chicken nuggets (cut in half)  
  - Cheese slices (for "sails")  
  - Mustard/tomato sauce cups ("storm clouds")  
  - *Pack in themed boxes labeled "Sailor’s Feast"*  

#### **3. Sides**  
- **Mermaid Pasta Salad**:  
  - Pasta shapes (dolphins, shells) in a clear jar labeled "Ocean Depths"  
  - Tuna/chicken salad, peas, and a squeeze of lemon ("sea spray")  
- **Crab Cakes** (or fish sticks):  
  - Bite-sized baked crab cakes (check for watermelon fillings if needed)  
  - *Pack in mini clamshells or containers labeled "Crabby Treasure"*  

#### **4. Drinks (Individual & Themed)**  
- **Juice Boxes/Pouches**:  
  - 1 per person (lemonade, apple, or berry flavors)  
  - Label with names like *"Cursed Lemonade"* or *"Treasure Brew"*  
- **"Pirate Punch" Station** (for parents/adults only):  
  - Colored sparkling water + blue food dye ("sea water")  
  - Garnish with lemon slices ("dolphins")  

#### **5. Cake Alternatives (除主蛋糕外)**  
- **Pirate Treasure Chest Cupcakes**:  
  - 8 cupcakes (topped with buttercream "gold" or sea-salt caramel "waves")  
  - Pack in a cardboard chest labeled "Captain’s Bounty"  
- **Jelly "Ship" Cups**:  
  - Clear cups filled with layered fruit jellies ("ship hulls") + fruit punch "sea"  

#### **6. Desserts**  
- **Jellyfish Jelly Cups**:  
  - Blue/purple jelly in cups with gummy eyes (labeled "Ocean Creatures")  
- **"Crab" Cookies**:  
  - Chocolate chip cookies with orange icing "claws"  

#### **7. Party Supplies for Practicality**  
- **Sand-Free Utensils**:  
  - Plastic cutlery with pirate flag designs  
  - Paper plates/bowls shaped like ships, anchors, or treasure chests  
- **Napkins**:  
  - "Treasure Map" or "Shipwreck" design (seal with tape to avoid spills)  
- **Labels**:  
  - Chalkboard-style tags for allergy-safe items ("NO WATERMELON")  

---

### **Key Sand-Free Strategies**  
- **Sealed Containers**: All food is pre-portioned in leak-proof boxes, bags, or jars.  
- **Easy-to-Handle Shapes**: Avoid droppers, skewers, or anything requiring two hands.  
- **Allergy Safety**: Use separate bags/containers for watermelon-free items.  
- **Thematic Packaging**: Treasure chests, clamshells, and jars double as sand barriers.  

This setup keeps kids messy-free, maximizes theme immersion, and minimizes cleanup! 🏴‍☠️⚓
