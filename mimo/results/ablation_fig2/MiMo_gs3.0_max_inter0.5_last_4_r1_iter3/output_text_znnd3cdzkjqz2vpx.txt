Okay, you've got the essentials, but you're missing a few key tools. The Habitat cabinets probably came with their own screws, so you'll need to make sure you have a drill and a screwdriver bit. Shims are also super important for getting base cabinets level underneath.

So, I'd suggest getting a drill with a few different screwdriver bits. A small stud finder would be a huge help again confirming the stud locations. A level is a must, obviously. You'll also need a hammer or a mallet for tapping the cabinets into place. A measuring tape is good for checking measurements. And a utility knife is handy for trimming any packaging material.

Most importantly, make sure everyone has safety glasses on. Working with power tools is always a good idea. And you'll need some work gloves to protect hands. And of course, a trash bag for all the packaging materials. You're all set now! Good luck!
