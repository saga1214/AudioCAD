For candy tomorrow, grab the classics that kids and adults love: Reese’s Cups, Hershey’s Kisses, candy corn, and maybe some mini chocolate bars or gummies. You could also throw in some sour candy for those who like a challenge. Buying pre-wrapped bags or small boxes is easiest, so you don’t have to worry about packaging last minute. Pro tip: Check the expiration dates quickly, but Halloween candy is usually fresh. If you’re worried about leftovers, you could give out most of it as guests leave!
