Okay, so you and girlfriend drive down to Joshua Tree, then maybe explore some BLM land for adventure, and then head to Vegas for a proper city reset. Smart move. Vegas is all about variety, so I'd say pick a vibe. Do you want classic casino lights and shows, or something more offbeat like themed bars or a day trip to the Grand Canyon? Either way, a nice hotel with a pool can be a great way to unwind after desert driving. Just make sure you book ahead, especially if it's a popular time. Vegas can get crowded.
