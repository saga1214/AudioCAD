    *   **Ingredients:** Cooked shrimp, 1 pound pasta (spaghetti, linguine, or fettuccine), 1/4 cup olive oil, 4 cloves garlic (minced), 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes, 1/2 cup red wine (optional), 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley, 1/4 cup chopped fresh red pepper flakes (or 1-2 tsp cayenne pepper), salt & pepper to taste, grated Parmesan for serving.  

3.  **Spicy Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
    *   **Ingredients:** Cooked shrimp, 1 cup quick-cooking grits (made according to package directions), 1/2 cup chicken or vegetable broth, 1/2 cup heavy cream, 1/4 cup grated sharp cheddar cheese, 1/4 cup chopped green onion, 1/4 cup chopped red onion, 1/4 cup chopped bell pepper (any color), 1/4 cup chopped celery, 1/4 cup chopped scallions (optional), 2-3 tablespoons Cajun seasoning (adjust for spice), salt & pepper to taste.
