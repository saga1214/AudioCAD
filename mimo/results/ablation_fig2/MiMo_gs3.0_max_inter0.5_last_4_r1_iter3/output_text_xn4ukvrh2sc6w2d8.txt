Here are the classic recipes with exact measurements and bartender-friendly instructions for all your cocktails. I'll include notes for the egg white variation in the Whiskey Sour.

### 1. **Old Fashioned**  
*Art Deco机场复古感*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey  
  - 1 sugar cube (or 0.5 oz simple syrup)  
  - 2-3 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (for garnish)  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Muddle sugar cube with 0.5 oz water in a shaker until dissolved.  
  2. Add bourbon and bitters. Fill shaker with ice.  
  3. **Dry shake** (shake without ice) 10 sec to aerate.  
  4. Add ice, shake again hard 10 sec.  
  5. Strain into chilled rocks glass over fresh ice.  
  机场主题：用纸飞机替代传统樱桃/橙皮。  

---

### 2. **Paper Plane**  
*等于份的简洁飞机感*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz gin  
  - 0.5 oz Aperol  
  - 0.5 oz Amaro Nonino  
 机场主题：非酒精版本用纸飞机代替 Nonino。  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice hard 10 sec.  
  2. Strain into coupe glass.  
  3. Garnish with lemon twist.  

---

### 3. **Whiskey Sour (with optional egg white)**  
秋日经典  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  - 1 oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 0.75 oz simple syrup  
  - **Optional**: 0.5 oz egg white (for texture)  
- **Instructions**:  
  - **Without egg white**: Shake all ingredients with ice, strain into rocks glass over ice.  
  - **With egg white**:  
    1. Muddle egg white in shaker.  
    2. Add bourbon, lemon, simple syrup.  
    3. Dry shake (no ice) 10 sec.  
    4. Add ice, shake hard 10 sec.  
    5. Strain into chilled glass.  
  *Egg white tip*: Dry shaking creates a silky foam head—classic in pre-Prohibition cocktails.  

---

### 4. **Aviation**  
*优雅飞行器*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz gin  
  - 0.5 oz maraschino liqueur  
  - 0.25 oz crème de violette  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice hard 10 sec.  
  2. Strain into coupe glass.  
  3. Garnish with maraschino cherry.  

---

### 5. **French 75 (Recommended Gin Cocktail)**  
*经典鸡尾酒的庆祝感*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz gin  
  - 0.5 oz lemon juice  
  - 0.5 oz simple syrup  
  - 2 oz champagne/sparkling wine  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake gin, lemon, and syrup with ice hard 10 sec.  
  2. Strain into flute.  
  3. Top with sparkling wine.  

---

### Key Notes for Bartenders:  
- **Kentucky Twist**: Use high-quality bourbon (e.g., Buffalo Trace, Maker’s Mark).  
- **Fall Finger Foods**: Pair Paper Plane/Aviation with savory snacks (e.g., pâté, smoked salmon), and Old Fashioned/Whiskey Sour with sweet treats (e.g., bourbon-glazed pork, spiced nuts).  
- **Paper Plane Hack**: For non-gin drinkers, swap gin with vodka (equal parts).  
- **Egg White Safety**: Use pasteurized eggs if concerned about raw eggs.
