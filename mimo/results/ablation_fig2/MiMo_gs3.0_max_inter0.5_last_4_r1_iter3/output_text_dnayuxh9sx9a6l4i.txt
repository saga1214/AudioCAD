Sure! First, think about your lifestyle—size, energy, and space in your home. Next, look into breeds known for being chill and good with kids, like Labrador Retrievers, Golden Retrievers, or Cavalier King Charles Spaniels. Mixed breeds from shelters might also have these traits if the parents were socialized well. Research temperament and compatibility with kids to ensure a safe match. Don’t forget to check landlord size and noise limits again just to be safe. Once you find a few candidates, arrange meetings to see how they interact with you and maybe even kids if you have them. Take your time—finding the right fit is key for a happy dog.
