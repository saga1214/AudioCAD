### III. Reasoning Styles  
- **Deductive**: Top-down (general → specific)  
  *Example:* "All mammals breathe air. Whales are mammals. → Whales breathe air."  
- **Inductive**: Bottom-up (specific → general)  
  *Example:* "100 swans are white. → All swans are white."  
- **Abductive**: Hypothesis-generating from incomplete data  
  *Example:* "Patient has fever + cough + travel history → Likely flu."  
- **Myth Clarification**: No strong evidence for "creative vs. logical" brain hemispheres. While some tasks show lateralization (e.g., language in left hemisphere), both sides collaborate constantly.
