Here are quick, elegant drink recommendations for your vodka and Fernet requests—keeping the soda allergy and formality in mind:

### 🥛 Vodka Mixer (Light & Refreshing)  
**Recipe:**  
- 1.5 oz vodka  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- 0.25 oz simple syrup (or 1 sugar cube muddled with 0.5 oz water)  
- Top with **still sparkling water** (not soda!)  
- Garnish: Lime wheel + rosemary sprig  

**Why it works:**  
- Soda-free, crisp, and visually appealing.  
- Works for omnivores *and* vegetarians equally.  
- Across generations—light but sophisticated.  

---

### 🇦🇷 Fernet-Branca (Argentine-Style)  
**Recipe:**  
- 1 oz Fernet-Branca (or other Argentine-style Fernet)  
- 1 oz soda water (still, **no carbonation!**)  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- 0.25 oz simple syrup  
- **Optional:** 0.25 oz honey syrup (for sweetness)  
- Garnish: Lemon wheel  

**Why it works:**  
- Authentic but approachable. Fernet’s herbal notes balance sweetness.  
- Soda allergy respected (uses still water).  
- Slightly bitter? Offer it *after* the vodka mixer—some guests may prefer lighter options.  

---

### Key Notes:  
1. **No Soda:** Both recipes use **still water** (non-carbonated).  
2. **Formality:** Glassware (coupe or rocks glasses) and fresh garnishes keep it classy.  
3. **Game Night Tie-In:** After Pictionary, serve these as a palate cleanser between rounds.
