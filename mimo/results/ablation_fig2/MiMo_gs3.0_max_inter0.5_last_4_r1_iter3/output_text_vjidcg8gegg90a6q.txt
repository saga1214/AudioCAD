For winter chicks on concrete, the trick is to create a thick insulating layer right on top of the floor. A great choice is to use what are called rubber stall mats. You'll want to stack them together to form a thick, insulated barrier. On top of those mats, you'll put the bedding. Pine pellets are the best option here because they insulate well, are great for deep litter composting (generating natural heat), and rodents really don't like them. If you can't find pellets, large-flake pine shavings are a decent alternative, but remember you have to manage them very strictly to avoid rodent problems. Avoid wood shavings, as they absorb moisture poorly and can get icy. A key thing to remember is to seal any cracks to stop drafts but ensure high-level ventilation to prevent frostbite. This setup will give the chicks warmth without any open flames or attracting pests.
