That pigeon question is a classic logic puzzle! It's a fun way to think about how to fit different things into a limited space.

For planning your Salt Flats apartment down payment trip, you can use a similar idea: you have to find the right balance.

Step one: Decide the Salt Flats are the current priority.

Step two: Create a simple budget. Figure out how much Bolivia will cost—for flights, food, and lodging—and how much extra you can set aside for the apartment fund each month.

Step three: Automate the savings. Set up an automatic transfer to two different accounts—one for the trip and one for the apartment—as soon as you get paid.

By separating the money and giving each goal a small, regular contribution, you're not neglecting either one. You're just giving them their own dedicated space!
