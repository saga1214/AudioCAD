That line of reasoning about genetic identity doesn't quite hold up when you look at the practical biology.

You see, for an egg to be produced with chicken proteins like that shell one I mentioned, you need a hen. Until the creature hatches from the egg, its exact DNA is unknown. So, the egg can't be said to 'contain' the identity of the creature that's not even defined yet. The chicken had to come first because the egg had to be chicken-specific.
