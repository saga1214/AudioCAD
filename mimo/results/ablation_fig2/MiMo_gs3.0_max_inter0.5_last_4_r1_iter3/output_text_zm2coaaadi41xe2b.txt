Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots. For ramen, check out Ichiran—they're famous for their tonkotsu. For pho, Pho 65 is solid. And if you like Korean BBQ noodles, there's a place called Noodle House. As for protein bars, Quest and Optimum Nutrition are popular. If you want something with a good texture and taste, Ghost is pretty good. Hope you enjoy your meal!
