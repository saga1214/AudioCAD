Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's a lot of people! Packing mess-free food and drinks for such a big group is smart. Let's get you a detailed shopping list based on everything you've said.

The main goals here are nutrition, minimal mess, and having variety. I've grouped everything into drinks, snacks, substantial meals, dessert, and some really helpful extras to make your cooler life easier.

For drinks, water bottles for everyone, low-sugar juice for kids, and definitely those sugar-free sodas are a must. If you think some people might still prefer regular sodas on a hot day, you could grab sugar-free or diet options too to keep messes low. Lemonade or iced tea bags are also great for adults.

For snacks, focus on single-serve packs. Think cheese sticks, grapes, individual hummus cups, trail mix in small bags, pretzels, yogurt cups, and cut fruit like apple slices with a little lemon juice to keep them from browning.

For the main meal, since he loves calzones, pizza is probably the easiest and cleanest alternative. You could order ten or twelve pre-made, individual pizzas from a pizzeria or a food truck. They come in their own boxes and are super easy to hand out. Another option, if you want something a bit healthier, is to order a large sub sandwich platter that you can cut up beforehand into individual pieces.

For dessert, cupcakes are perfect. They're already individual, so no plates or forks needed. Or, grab some brownie bars or mini brownies, which are also easy to grab and go.

And here are a few extra things that will make your day much smoother: pack plenty of napkins, wet wipes for everyone, and some basic condiments like ketchup packets. Bringing trash bags is also a lifesaver for keeping coolers clean. If you think some allergies might be a concern, it's always a good idea to have some plain crackers or pretzels on hand just in case.

This plan should give you a really well-stocked, low-mess setup for you and your whole friend group to enjoy the theme park! Have a fantastic time!
