Absolutely! For a 4-5 day SUV-friendly beach getaway in Northern California with a healthy budget, I recommend **Big Sur and Malibu**—both offer warm weather, swimming, Celiac-safe dining, and stunning scenery. Here’s a detailed itinerary:

---

### **Itinerary: Big Sur & Malibu Beach Escape**
**Total Days:** 4-5  
**Budget:** Healthy (luxury resorts, premium dining)  
**Transport:** Rent an SUV (easier for coastal roads)  

---

#### **Day 1: Arrival in Carmel-by-the-Sea**  
- **Stay:** [**Carmel Beach Resort**](https://www.carmelbeachresort.com/) (Luxury, ocean views, gluten-free options at on-site restaurant)  
- **Dinner:** [**The Sardine Factory**](https://www.sardinefactory.com/) (Gluten-free menu, fresh seafood, oceanfront)  
- **Activity:** Relax at **Carmel Beach** (swimming, sunsets).  

---

#### **Day 2: Big Sur Highlights**  
- **Morning:** Drive to **Pfeiffer Beach** (swim in turquoise waters, hike to **McWay Falls**).  
- **Lunch:** [**Big Sur Bakery**](https://www.bigsurbakery.com/) (Gluten-free options, iconic cliffside views).  
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park** (swim at **Estero Point**).  
- **Dinner:** [**Nepenthe**](https://www.nepenthebigsur.com/) (Gluten-free menu, panoramic cliffside dining).  

---

#### ** packing Essentials for Big Sur/Malibu**  
1. **Bathing Suits:** 2 swimsuits + 1 cover-up.  
2. **Swim Gear:** Flip-flops, reef-safe sunscreen, swimwear dryer bag.  
3. **Daywear:**  
   - Sundresses, tank tops, shorts.  
   - Light linen shirts (for layering).  
4. **Gluten-Free Dining:**  
   - Pack snacks (gluten-free granola bars).  
   - Bring a Celiac card for restaurants.  
5. **Extras:** UV umbrella, reusable water bottle, beach towel.  

---

#### **Day 3: Malibu Adventure**  
- **Stay:** [**Malibu Beach Inn**](https://www.malibubeachinn.com/) (Beachfront luxury, gluten-free breakfast included).  
- **Morning:** Swim at **Malibu Pier** (rent kayaks or paddleboards).  
- **Lunch:** [**Malibu Farm Café**](https://malibufarmcafe.com/) (100% gluten-free, farm-to-table).  
- **Afternoon:** Hike **Topanga State Park** (views of Santa Monica Bay).  
- **Dinner:** [**Leo’s Malibu**](https://www.leosmalibu.com/) (Gluten-free menu, oceanfront dining).  

---

#### **Day 4: Luxury Relaxation**  
- **Morning:** Swim at **El Matador Beach** (iconic rock formations).  
- **Lunch:** [**The Lobster**](https://thelobstermalibu.com/) (Gluten-free options, fresh seafood).  
- **Evening:** Sunset at **Zuma Beach**, followed by dinner at [**Cantería**](https://www.canteria.com/) (Upscale Mexican, gluten-free options).  

---

#### **Day 5: Departure**  
- **Morning:** Coffee at [**Malibu Farm**](https://malibufarm.com/) (Gluten-free pastries), then drive home.  

---

### **Why This Works**  
- **Warmth:** Average highs 75-80°F (24-27°C) in December.  
- **Swimming:** All beaches have calm, shallow waters.  
- **Celiac Safety:** All restaurants noted have dedicated gluten-free protocols.  
- **Luxury on a Budget:** Resorts offer packages (e.g., free breakfasts), and beach dining is affordable for special occasions.  

### **Travel Tips**  
- **Rent an SUV:** [**Bay Area Car Rentals**](https://www.bayareacarrentals.com/) (ask for a high-clearance vehicle for Big Sur).  
- **Book Early:** Luxury resorts fill fast in winter.  
- **Pack Smart:** UV protection is key—California sun is intense!
