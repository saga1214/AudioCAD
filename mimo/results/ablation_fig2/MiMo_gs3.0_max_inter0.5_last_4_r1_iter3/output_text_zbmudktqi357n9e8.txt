Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration. If you're in the U.S., their autumn is around September to November, which lines up with Mexico's fall season. 

For Mexico City, you could go in late spring or early fall, around April to May, or September to November. That's when you get those super vibrant colors on the leaves. 

If you go to the Yucatán Peninsula, like Tulum or Chichen Itza, late summer or early fall, around August to October, is ideal. You'll see all the classic autumn foliage. 

For the Pacific coast, like Acapulco, late spring or early fall is also great. The weather is warm, and the colors are just as intense. 

So yeah, timing is everything. Late spring or early fall should give you the best light and colors for your paintings. Have fun on your trip!
