Oh, that's wonderful news about customer service! I'm so glad you found a clean spot in the kitchen to unwind. Cooking is definitely a great way to relax. Here are five simple and comforting dinner ideas you can whip up with ingredients you can probably grab from a beachside grocery store:

1.  **Simple Pesto Pasta:** Grab some fresh pasta (or rotini), a bag of cherry tomatoes, fresh basil, and some pine nuts or toasted breadcrumbs. Toss it all with a generous amount of good pesto and maybe a little grated parmesan. 🍅🍝
2.  **Quick Veggie Stir-Fry:** Pick up a firm tofu or some pre-cut stir-fry veggies (bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas, carrots). Cook rice or noodles separately. Stir-fry the veggies and tofu in olive oil or a bit of soy sauce, then toss with your cooked grains. 🥕🥦
3.  **Comforting Grilled Cheese & Tomato Soup:** This is pure relaxation food. Make some good tomato soup (you can find canned or even just make a quick version with canned tomatoes) and melt some gooey cheddar or mozzarella into a perfect grilled cheese sandwich. 🧀🍞
4.  **Easy Breakfast-for-Dinner:** Scrambled eggs are the ultimate easy meal. Grab eggs, some avocado (or butter/cheese), and maybe some leftover toast or a slice of bread. Top it with hot sauce if you like! 🍳🥑
5.  **One-Pot Creamy Pasta:** Sauté some garlic in olive oil. Add a can of crushed tomatoes, a splash of cream or milk, some pasta, and maybe some spinach or peas. Simmer until the pasta is cooked, then stir in a handful of ricotta or cottage cheese for creaminess. Finish with cheese and herbs. 🧀🍝
