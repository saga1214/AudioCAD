Initally, you could try scheduling a one-on-one meeting through your calendar system. That can feel a bit more formal and less spontaneous, which might help reduce the anxiety.

Another option is to send a brief email. You could introduce the topic with something like, 'I'd like to discuss my contributions and growth during performance reviews. When would be a good time to meet?' This gives your boss a heads-up and takes some of the pressure off the moment.
