That's fantastic! Being able to illustrate your own work is a huge asset. You absolutely can submit your entire package to publishers. In fact, it's often a significant advantage, especially for a debut author.

However, there are a few things to keep in mind. First, publishers generally expect an *unpolished* manuscript. They want to see the story in its raw form so they can assess its quality and decide if they want to commission an illustrator. Second, the illustrations themselves need to be professional. Publishers might reject a manuscript if the illustrations are too polished, as it could be seen as 'blocking' them from finding their own artist. And finally, they expect the illustrations to be high-resolution digital files, not scanned photocopies.

So, my advice is to keep your manuscript as a standard Word document. For the illustrations, you can create a separate PDF that you can attach. Just make sure the files are high-resolution and in a format like JPEG or PNG.
