Here's a comprehensive shopping list based on our menu planning, organized by category with estimated quantities for **24 adults + 4 kids**:

### 🍽️ APPETIZERS/FINGER FOODS  
- Mini slider patties (beef or turkey) - **48 patties**  
- Shrimp cocktail (1 lb raw shrimp + cocktail sauce)  
- Pork sausages (for pigs in a blanket) - **1 lb**  
- Mini pastry wheels or phyllo cups  
- Toothpicks/gift wrap for pigs in a blanket  

### 🥤 DRINKS  
- **Alcoholic:** Champagne/sparkling wine (1 bottle per guest = **8 bottles**)  
- **Signature Cocktail** ingredients (vodka, mixers, garnishes)  
- **Kid-friendly:** Sparkling grape juice (2 large bottles)  
- **Non-alcoholic:** Sparkling water, soda, lemonade, ice  
- Water bottles (24)  
- Small plastic champagne flutes (for kids/grape juice)  

### 🍖 MAIN COURSE  
- Ham (12–14 lbs, for 20 adults + 4 kids)  
- **Sides:**  
  - Creamy scalloped potatoes (use 2–3 large bags of potatoes)  
  - Roasted carrots (raw carrots, olive oil, salt)  
  - Roasted asparagus (1 bunch)  

### 🧂 SUPPLIES & DESSERT  
- Balloons streamers  
- Festive hats/noisemakers (kid-sized)  
- Napkins (plenty!)  
- Disposable plates/cutlery (28 sets)  
- Dessert (e.g., chocolate fondue + fruit skewers or store-bought cake)  

### 💡 HOSPITALITY ESSENTIALS  
- Coffee/tea station setup (mugs, filters, sugar)  
- Trash bags (multiple sizes)  
- Extra toilet paper/hand soap  
- Cooler bags (for perishables)

### 📝 KEY NOTES  
1. **Food Safety:** Cook ham 2–3 days ahead (reheat to 165°F), prep veggies 1 day prior.

3.Toast Champagne exactly at 11:59 PM to avoid rushed cleanup!
