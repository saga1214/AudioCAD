That’s a fantastic idea! Travel can be a huge creative catalyst, especially since you love it and want experiences for your novel. Here’s a plan for a trip that blends affordability, inspiration, and practicality:

### **Trip Ideas: Where to Go**
1. **Portugal (Lisbon or Porto)**  
   - **Why?** Affordable, vibrant culture, literary history, and friendly locals.  
   - **Activities:**  
     - Explore historic neighborhoods (Alfama in Lisbon).  
     - Visit bookshops like *Máias* in Porto (a UNESCO-listed street of bookstores).  
     - Take a day trip to Sintra for fairy-tale palaces (great for fantasy inspiration!).  
   - **Language Practice:** Italians might recognize some similarities with Italian.  

2. **Thailand (Chiang Mai)**  
   - **Why?** Budget-friendly, pet-friendly cafes (like cat cafés!), language exchange opportunities.  
   - **Activities:**  
     - Practice Thai at language exchanges (Meetup/Facebook groups).  
     - Visit ethical cat cafés or animal sanctuaries.  
     - Explore temples and markets for modern-fiction-esque slice-of-life stories.  

3. **Japan (Kyoto or Tokyo)**  
   - **Why?** Blend of sci-fi (tech!) and fantasy (mythology), pet cafés, and language immersion.  
   - **Activities:**  
     - Visit cat-themed cafés or temples.  
     modern fiction spots: tiny cafes in Kyoto’s Gion district.  
     - Explore sci-fi museums (e.g., in Tokyo’s Akihabara).  

### **How to Get There & Stay**
- **Flights:** Use Skyscanner or Google Flights for deals (book 2–3 months ahead).  
- **Accommodation:**  
  - **Hostels** (e.g., *Generator Hostels* in Lisbon/Porto) – social, cheap, often have events.  
  - **Guesthouses** – cheaper and more local.  
  - **House Sitting** (TrustedHousesitters) – free stays in exchange for pet/house care.  

### **Extracting Experiences for Your Novel**
- **Journal Prompt:** *Write a 200-word scene based on a local interaction* (e.g., bargaining in a market, practicing Italian with a barista).  
- **Language Practice:** Use apps like HelloTalk to find language partners – they might share stories!  
- **Pet Connection:** Visit cat cafés (Japan/Thailand) or volunteer at local shelters (even short-term).  

### **Safety & Practical Tips**
- **For Anxiety:**  
  - Stick to tourist-friendly areas (avoid solo hikes at night).  
  - Share your itinerary with someone.  
  - Use apps like *Meetup* or *Eventbrite* to find structured social events (reduces "stranger danger" pressure).  
- **Budgeting:**  
  - Prioritize experiences (e.g., free walking tours → paid meal).  
  - Cook simple meals to save money. Student loans hurt, but travel can fuel creativity!  

### **Writer’s Block Buster**  
If stuck, try:  
- **Freewriting:** "Write until your Italian practice goes wrong in Italy" (sci-fi collide moment!).  
- **Observe:** Note tiny details (e.g., a stranger’s book bag, a language mistake). These become relatable yet magical.  

### **Making Friends Abroad**  
- **Language Exchanges:** Meet locals who want to learn English.  
- **Book Clubs:** Look for international ones (e.g., *Meetup* groups).  
- **Volunteering:** Help at animal shelters or libraries – low-pressure bonding.  

**Final Thought:** Travel doesn’t have to mean luxury. A €30/day trip (flights, food, lodging) can spark 10x the material for your novel. Even one small trip can open doors to new perspectives, friends, and stories.
