### 1. **Color Coding for Icons**:  
   - **Memory vs. Mental Load**: Both! Color coding aids memory by creating visual anchors (e.g., red for urgent tasks, blue for creative projects). ### 2. **Switching Background Sounds**:  
   - **Optimal Timing**: Try **every 45–60 minutes**. Short transitions refresh focus without disrupting flow. If you’re writing, stick to one type (e.g., lo-fi); for brainstorming, switch to nature sounds. **Rule of thumb**: Match the sound to your task (e.g., ambient for deep work, upbeat for admin).  

### 3. **Physical Documents & Progress**:  
   - **Environment vs. Chunking**: Both matter!
