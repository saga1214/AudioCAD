Okay, I've got your hardware off the doors and the doors sanded. That's a great start. Before you hit them with primer, you'll want to do a quick final wipe-down with a tack cloth. That'll pick up any fine dust from the sanding and make sure there's no residue on the primer.

Then, before you put the primer on, protect your countertop and backsplash with some painter's tape and plastic sheeting. You don't want any overspray getting on them.

After that, apply your primer. Use a brush for the edges and corners, and then use a roller for the main flat surfaces. Make sure to apply it in smooth, even strokes and let it dry completely according to the instructions, which is usually about one to two hours. Then you're ready to paint!
