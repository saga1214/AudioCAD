Here's a step-by-step guide for each installation task, prioritizing safety and efficiency while respecting your new apartment rules:

### 1. **Mounting a TV**
   - **Tools Needed**: Stud finder, level, drill, wall anchors (if not mounting to studs), TV mounting bracket, screwdriver.
   - **Steps**:
     1. **Locate studs**: Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (critical for safety; avoid drywall only).
     2. **Measure and mark**: Place the TV and mark where the bracket will sit. Use a level to ensure it’s straight.
     3. **Install anchors (if needed)**: If mounting to drywall, pre-drill holes and insert anchors.
     4. **Attach bracket**: Secure the bracket to studs with long screws. Double-check level and stability.
     5. **Mount TV**: Align the TV with the bracket and screw it in place.
   - **Pro Tip**: Use a cable management kit to hide wires behind the TV. Test the mount’s weight capacity first.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Tools Needed**: Drill, wall anchors (for drywall), strong adhesive hooks, measuring tape.
   - **Steps**:
     1. **Choose a spot**: Hang near windows or high ceilings for light. Ensure weight capacity (e.g., 5–10 lbs per hook).
     2. **Mark hook placement**: Measure and mark spots 6–8 inches from the ceiling. Use a level.
     3. **Install hooks**: Drill pilot holes, insert anchors, and screw in hooks. For heavy plants, use multiple hooks.
     4. **Hang planters**: Place pots on hooks. Use self-watering planters or saucers to prevent drips.
   - **Alternative**: Command Strips (for lightweight plants like succulents) if drilling isn’t allowed.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Tools Needed**: Drill, wall anchors, level, measuring tape, shelf brackets.
   - **Steps**:
     1. **Plan layout**: Sketch where shelves will go (e.g., above furniture or along a wall).
     2. **Mark screw locations**: Measure from the floor/ceiling. Mark spots 1–2 inches from studs.
     3. **Install brackets**: Drill pilot holes into studs. Secure brackets with long screws.
     4. **Attach shelf**: Place the shelf onto brackets and screw it in place.
   - **Safety**: Never exceed shelf weight limits. Distribute weight evenly (e.g., books on center).

### ⚠️ **Critical Reminders**
- **Noise Control**: Use non-drilling methods (adhesive hooks, shelf pins) if hammering/drilling isn’t permitted.
- **Focus During Work**: Decorate only during designated "off" hours (e.g., Sundays). Use noise-canceling headphones if needed.
- **Posture Breaks**: Set a timer for 30 minutes of work, 5-minute leg stretches, and 10 minutes of tool-free breaks.

### 📋 **Weekly Schedule Suggestion**
- **Saturdays**: Gym session (2 hours) + 2–3 hours of decorating.
- **Sundays**: Remote work blocks (morning/evening) + 1–2 hours for decorating/friends.
