Here's a tailored closing gear packing list split into two segments:

### **Daytime 20 Miles (Light Hiking Pace, Aid Stations Available)**
1. **Base Layer**: Moisture-wicking synthetic top & bottom (e.g., merino wool or technical fabric).  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece vest or lightweight puffy jacket (for hilly temperature swings).  
3. **Outer Layer**: Windproof/water-resistant shell jacket (mid-30s hilly conditions).  
4. small **Gloves**: Light trail gloves for grip and hand warmth.  
5. **Hat**: Beanie or cap (for early morning cool temps).  
6. **Headlamp**: Handheld model (even with daylight, trails can be shaded/dewy).  
7. **Shoes**: Trail runners with aggressive lugs for hills/uneven ground.  
8. hand-held **Light**: Extra batteries (daytime runs drain lights faster than expected).  
9. **Pack/Vest**: Small daypack to carry layers if needed.  

### **Night 12 Miles (Final Push)**
1. **Base Layer**: Same as daytime (keep dry).  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece jacket or lightweight puffer (night temps drop significantly).  
3. **Outer Layer**: Windproof shell (essential for nighttime wind chill).  
4. **Headlamp**: Bright handheld light + **spare batteries** (critical for night miles).  
5. **Gloves**: Warm gloves (extremities get cold fast).  
6. **Hat**: Beanie + buff or neck gaiter (wind protection for face/neck).  
7. **Shoes**: Same trail runners (grip is non-negotiable at night).  
8. **Insulated Jacket**: Fleece or puffy vest (if mid-layer isn’t enough).  
9. **Pack/Vest**: Still useful for carrying layers between stations.  

### **Key Overlaps & Extras**
- **Moisture Management**: Base layers are critical—avoid cotton.  
- **Grip**: Hills + night = prioritize shoe traction.  
- **Light**: Carry extra batteries—night races often run low.  
- **Drying Kit**: Wipes/towel (keep base layers dry between segments).  

### Why This Works:
- **Daytime**: Layers allow flexibility with aid stations.  
- **Night**: Wind chill and darkness demand extra warmth/light.  
- **Both**: Headlamp is non-negotiable—trails get dark fast.
