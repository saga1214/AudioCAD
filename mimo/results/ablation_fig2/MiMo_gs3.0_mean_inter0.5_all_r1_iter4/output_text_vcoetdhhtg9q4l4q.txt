That's a great question! Since you're simplifying, I’d suggest focusing on **minimal pantry staples** that can mix-and-match for quick dinners. Here are some efficient ideas:

### 🥗 Minimalist Pantry Staples (Keep These):
- **Grains:** Quinoa, brown rice, farro, or couscous (15-min cook time).
- **Proteins:** Canned beans (black, chickpeas), pre-cooked lentils, eggs, or frozen shrimp/chicken.
- **Veggies:** Frozen stir-fry mix, spinach, cherry tomatoes, or roasted broccoli.
- **Sauces:** Tamari, soy sauce, olive oil, lemon juice, or a simple vinaigrette.
- **Extras:** Avocado, nuts/seeds, or a handful of herbs.

### 🍽️ Fast Dinner Ideas:
1. **One-Pan Grain Bowl:**  
   Cook quinoa/farro, toss with canned beans, roasted veggies (pre-cut!), and a drizzle of tamari + sesame oil. Top with avocado or seeds.  
   *Why:* No gadgets needed—just a pan and a bowl.

2. **Stir-Fry Lightning Round:**  
   Sauté pre-chopped veggies (frozen works!) + canned protein in soy sauce, garlic, and ginger. Serve over instant rice noodles (finally use one type!).  
   *Pro tip:* Batch-cook stir-fry sauces ahead.

3. **Sheet-Pan Salmon & Veggies:**  
   Place salmon, broccoli, and bell peppers on a tray. Drizzle with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 15 mins.  
   *Bonus:* Use leftovers for salads or wraps.

4. **Breakfast-for-Dinner:**  
   Scrambled eggs + sautéed spinach + whole-grain toast. Add canned black beans for protein.  
   *Speed hack:* Pre-peel potatoes (mashed in 10 mins).

### 💡 Key Strategy:
- **Prep Ahead:** Wash/chop veggies weekly; cook grains in batches.  
- **Embrace Frozen:** Frozen veggies are nutritious, prepped, and save space.  
- **Declutter as You Cook:** If an ingredient hasn’t been used in 3 months, donate it—your简化之旅 starts in the pantry!  

These meals cut prep time to 10–15 minutes and use fewer dishes. Focus on flexibility—like swapping proteins or veggies based on what you *actually* have. Happy (simpler) cooking! 🥄✨
