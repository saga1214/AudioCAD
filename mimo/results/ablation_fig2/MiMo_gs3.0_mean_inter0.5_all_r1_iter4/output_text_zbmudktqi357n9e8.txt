Okay, so Mexico in autumn—that's an amazing choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time would probably be late September through November, which is their fall season. That's when you get peak foliage in the highlands, like around Mexico City, and just stunning landscapes in places like Chiapas and Oaxaca. The weather is usually mild, and the colors are just... incredible.

You could also check out places like Pátzcuaro or San Miguel de Allende for those really vibrant autumn scenes you love. And if you're into beaches, Cancún and Tulum have that golden glow during that season, too. Just make sure to pack your paints, because the views are going to be so inspiring! Safe travels, and happy painting! 🎨🍂
