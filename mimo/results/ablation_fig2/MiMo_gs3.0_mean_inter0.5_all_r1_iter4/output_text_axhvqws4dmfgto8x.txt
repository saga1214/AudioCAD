Alright, let's tackle stir-frying noodles without setting off the alarm.

Use metal! Wood absolutely caused your smoke alarm issue. Use wooden chopsticks or tongs to stir, and a metal spoon.

Also, stir constantly! Stir-fry is meant to be cooked over high heat and in a wok or skillet. You want to get a good sear on the meat and veggies, not let them boil.

For the noodles, bring the water to a rolling boil, then add them and cook just until they are al dente, meaning they are tender but still have a bit of a bite. They will cook more when you mix them with the sauce.

Then, drain them really well. Any extra water will make the sauce soupy.

Finally, toss the hot noodles with your sauce and stir-fry ingredients right in the pan. The residual heat will cook the noodles perfectly. And no wood spoons
