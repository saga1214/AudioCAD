Dallas has some fantastic Asian noodle spots. For ramen, check out Ichiran—it's a chain but has great customizable broth options. For pho, Pho 79 on North Lamar is solid. If you like Pad Thai, Thai Basil on North Fair Park is a great choice. 

For protein bars, Quest is high-protein and tastes good. Optimum Nutrition has solid flavors, and Clif Bars are a good balance of protein and natural ingredients. Let me know if you want more recommendations!
