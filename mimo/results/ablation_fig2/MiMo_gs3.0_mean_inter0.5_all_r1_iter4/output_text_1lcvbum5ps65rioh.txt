### Driving Rules in BC  
If you make an unsafe U-turn in British Columbia, expect:  
1. **Penalty**: A fine of **$160 + 3 demerit points** (or up to **$550 + 6 points** for repeated offenses under BC’s Highway Traffic Act).  
2. **Common Violations**:  
   - Turning across crosswalks or driveways.  
   - Executing U-turns on curves, hills, or at intersections without signs.  
   - Failing to yield to pedestrians or cyclists.  
3. **Prevention Tip**: Use designated U-turn lanes or wait for a safe gap in traffic.  

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
Here’s what you’ll need for a classic (no cream!) version:  
#### **Core Ingredients**  
- **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti (or bucatini).  
- **Protein**: 150g *guanciale* (or pancetta/bacon).  
- **Cheese**: 100g Pecorino Romano (plus extra for grating).  
- **Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 1 yolk (for creaminess).  
- **Aromatics**: 1 small garlic clove (optional).  
- **Seasoning**: Freshly cracked black pepper.  

#### **Key Techniques**  
1. **Reserve pasta water**: Mix 1 cup starchy water with the pasta.  
2. **Temper eggs**: Whisk eggs with cheese and pepper. Slowly add hot pasta water to prevent scrambling.  
3. **Combine**: Toss pasta with guanciale fat, then add egg mixture off-heat.  

#### **Optional Additions**  
- Black pepper: 1 tbsp extra.  
- Anchovy paste: 1 tsp (for umami depth).  
- Fresh parsley: 2 tbsp chopped.
