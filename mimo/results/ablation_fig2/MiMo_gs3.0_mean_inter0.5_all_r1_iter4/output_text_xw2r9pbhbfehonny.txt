Sure, here’s a quick checklist for filming your own puppet show before posting:

First, check with the venue ahead of time to make sure recording is allowed. That way you don’t get in trouble.

Next, set up your phone or camera in a spot where you can see everything clearly without blocking the audience.

You should also test the lighting. Natural light is usually best, but if it’s dark, you might want to add a ring light or something simple.

Also, think about what you want to focus on—like the puppets’ faces, your hands, or the whole stage. You can zoom in if needed.

And maybe ask the audience to stay still if you want clear footage. Kids might not be able to, but older folks can usually help out.

Finally, make sure to label your video with copyright info right away, like “Copyright [Your Name], 2024.” That protects you.

Have fun filming!
