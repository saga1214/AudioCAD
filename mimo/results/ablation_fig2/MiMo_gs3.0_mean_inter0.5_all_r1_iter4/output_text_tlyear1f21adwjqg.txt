This is a tough choice since it really depends on the specific location. For instance, solar energy is great for sunny regions, wind power for consistently windy areas, and hydropower where there's existing water infrastructure. If I absolutely had to pick just one that balances efficiency, scalability, cost, and location independence, I’d still lean toward solar. That’s because costs keep falling, storage is improving, and it's the most universally available of the three. But if location is a huge factor, then hydropower could be the winner in suitable areas.
