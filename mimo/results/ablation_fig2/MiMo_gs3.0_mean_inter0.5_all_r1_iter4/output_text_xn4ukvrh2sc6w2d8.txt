Here are the classic recipes with precise ingredients and measurements for your bartenders, plus themed finger food suggestions:

### **Cocktails**
1. **Art Deco Paper Plane**  
   - 2 oz gin  
   - 0.75 oz Aperol  
   Cherry splash to top  
   *Shake all ingredients with ice, strain into rocks glass. Garnish with lemon twist.*

2. **Classic Paper Plane**  
   - 2 oz bourbon rye  
   - 0.75 oz Aperol  
   - 0.5 oz Amaro Nonino  
   - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
   *Shake with ice, strain into coupe glass. Garnish with lemon wheel.*

3. **Old Fashioned Paper-Themed**  
   Paper straw stirrer (optional garnish)  
   - 2 oz bourbon rye  
   - 1 sugar cube  
   - 2 dash Angostura bitters  
   *Muddle sugar cube with 0.25 oz water/b/simple syrup. Stir cube/bourbon/bitters with ice. Serve over fresh ice in rocks glass. Cherry or orange twist.*

4. **Recommended Additional Gin Cocktail: Aviation Paper Smash**  
   *(Modern twist fitting aviation theme)*  
   - 2 oz gin  
   - 0.5 oz maraschino liqueur  
   - 0.25 oz crème de violette  
   - 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
   *Shake all with ice, strain into coupe glass. Garnish with lemon twist.*

### **Bartender Pro Tips**
- **Ice Prep:** Use large ice cubes for Old Fashioneds to prevent dilution.  
- **Garnish Consistency:** Use fresh citrus wheels/peels. For Paper Planes, express lemon oils over the drink before garnishing.  
- **Glassware:** Use vintage-style coupes for Paper Planes and coupes/collins glasses for French 75.  
- **Kentucky Twist:** Add 1 drop of Kentucky bourbon barrel-aged bitters to Old Fashioneds.
