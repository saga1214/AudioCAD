That's a very smart line of thinking. Older comics are generally much rarer and can be more valuable.

Here are ten iconic and historically important comics from that era. You can find CGC-graded copies of these in the five to five hundred dollar range. 

First, there's *Timely Comics* number fifteen from 1939, which is Captain America's first appearance. 

Second is *Marvel Comics* number one from 1939, where you first see the Human Torch. 

Third is *Adventure Comics* number twenty-five from 1939, which is the first full appearance of Superman. 

Fourth is *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven from 1939, which is Batman's first appearance. 

Fifth is *Whiz Comics* number one from 1951, which features the first full appearance of the Fantastic Four. 

Sixth is *Strange Tales* number one from 1953, where the first appearance of Nick Fury's own team, S.H.I.E.L.D., was printed. 

Seventh is *Tales to Astonish* number one from 1956, the first appearance of Ant-Man. 

Eighth is *Tales of Suspense* number fifty, which is the first appearance of Doctor Strange and the last appearance of Iron Man before his 1962 series started. 

Ninth is *Strange Tales* number one hundred and ten, where the Hulk first appears. 

And tenth is *The Fantastic Four* number one from 1961, which is the first appearance of Galactus and the Silver Surfer. 

Just remember, the price is for a mid-grade copy. The more valuable copies, especially those in 'near mint' or 'mint' condition, will be significantly more expensive.
