That's a fun puzzle! It's a bit like planning your goals.

First, list out all your passions and which ones have a financial priority attached. Bolivia salt flats connect to apartment down money. Fermented trends might just be a passing interest. The Faroe Islands connect to Bolivia money.

Second, assign each trip a monthly or annual saving target based on its priority. The apartment goal will need the most focused, consistent saving.

Then, just like the pigeons need to find a home, your money needs a home in your budget. You could even create a special 'Adventure Fund' where you save a small amount each month just for travel.
