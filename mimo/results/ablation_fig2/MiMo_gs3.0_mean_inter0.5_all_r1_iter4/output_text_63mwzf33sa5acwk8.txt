That's a brilliant plan—combining rugged adventure with a luxurious reset in Vegas creates the perfect contrast! Here's how to structure it for maximum impact:

### 🌵 **Phase 1: Joshua Tree Adventure (West Coast)**
- **Drive Time**: ~3 hours from SoCal (e.g., LA/San Diego).  
- **Camping Inside Joshua Tree NP**:  
  - **Best Spots**: Hidden Valley (romantic rock formations), Jumbo Rocks (stargazing), or Black Rock Canyon (quiet).  
  - **Cost**: ~$20/night (book via [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/)).  
  - **Tips**: Arrive by 9 AM on weekends; pack a cooler, water, and a portable speaker for sunset.  
- **Adventure Ideas**: Hike Skull Rock at sunrise, explore Cholla Cactus Garden (magical at dusk), or drive the Backcountry Byways.

### 🌵➡️ **Phase 2: Nevada Desert Detour (Van Drive)**
- **Route**: Drive east on **CA-62** toward **Pahrum** (3.5 hrs), then continue to **Tonopah** (NV), stopping at:  
  - **Tonopah Ghost Town**: Quirky saloons and mines (free, scenic).  
  - **Valley of Fire NP** (NV): Red-rock hikes like Firewave Trail.  
- **BLM Land Camping**:  
  - **Where**: Near **Golden Agency** or **Gamble Flats** (search via [BLM Public Land Maps](https://www.blm.gov/)).  
  - **Cost**: Free dispersed camping (no facilities).  
  - **Rules**: Stay 100+ ft from trails/water; leave no trace.  
- **Vegas Transition**: ~2.5 hrs from Valley of Fire to Vegas. Pack cold-weather layers (NV deserts get cold at night!).

### 🏨 **Phase 3: Vegas Luxury Reset**
- **Hot Showers & Comfort**:  
  - **Budget-Friendly Luxe**: **The Palazzo** or **Wynn/Encore** (free airport shuttle, lush pools).  
  - **Shower Hacks**: Ask for a "house key" upgrade (often includes private bathroom); use airport lounges (Priority Pass helps).  
- **Beyond Gambling**:  
  - **Experiences**: **Mirage Volcano** show, **High Roller** observation wheel, or a spa day at **Caesars Palace**.  
  - **Food**: Gourmet ramen at **Izakaya** or cocktails at **Trouble** (Neon Bites).  
- **Adventure Carryover**: Visit **Mirage’s Wild Wild West** exhibit (gaming history tie-in!) or **Area15** for immersive art.

### 📌 **Key Tips**
1. **Pack Smart**: Solar charger, first-aid kit, and a portable air fryer for camp meals.  
2. **NV Navigation**: Download offline maps (cell service is spotty in deserts).  
3. **Vegas Savings**: Use **Cashman Arena** parking or **South Las Vegas** hotels (e.g., **Circus Circus**) for affordability.  
4. **Safety**: Lock your van; Nevada deserts have extreme temps (day: 100°F+/night: 40°F).
