As your Wine Stewart Coach, here's a consolidated temperature guide for all the wines we've discussed, including Hon-Mirin (as a culinary ingredient). I'll highlight the best choices for hot/cold weather based on serving temperatures:

### **High-End Pinot Noir**  
- **Store:** 55°F (13°C) *cellar temperature*  
- **Serve:** 55–60°F (13–16°C) *slightly cool*  
- **Best For:** Balanced weather. Not ideal for extreme heat/cold.  

### **Japanese Hon-Mirin**  
- **Store:** 60–68°F (16–20°C) *cool pantry*  
- **Serve (if tasting):** 68–75°F (20–24°C) *room temp*  
- **Best For:** Cooking (neutral temp). *Note: Not a beverage wine—sipped occasionally as condiment.*  

### **Italian Wine (e.g., Chianti)**  
- **Store:** 55°F (13°C) *cellar*  
- **Serve:** 60–65°F (16–18°C) *moderately cool*  
- **Best For:** Versatile for warm/cold evenings.  

### **Pinot Grigio**  
- **Store:** 50–55°F (10–13°C) *chilled*  
- **Serve:** 45–50°F (7–10°C) *well-chilled*  
- **Best For:** **HOT WEATHER** (crisp, refreshing).  

### **Champagne**  
- **Store:** 45–50°F (7–10°C) *cool cellar*  
- **Serve:** 45–50°F (7–10°C) *well-chilled*  
- **Best For:** **HOT WEATHER** (sparkling contrast).  

### **Mulled Wine (Hot-Served)**  
- **Store:** 55°F (13°C) *normal cellar*  
- **Serve:** 140–160°F (60–70°C) *hot*  
- **Best For:** **COLD WEATHER** (warmed spices).  

---

### **Weather Recommendations**  
- **HOT WEATHER (e.g., 33°C/91°F):**  
  **Best:** **Pinot Grigio** or **Champagne** (both served cold).  
  *Tip:* Use an insulated bucket with ice for Pinot Grigio.  
- **COLD WEATHER (e.g., chilly evenings):**  
  **Best:** **Chianti** or **Pinot Noir** (served near room temp).  
  **Ultimate Choice:** **Mulled Wine** (if available).  

### **Hon-Mirin Reminder**  
- *Store/cook* at 60–68°F (16–20°C).  
- *Sip* at 20–24°C (20–24°C) if tasting.  
- **Best For:** Cooking, not extreme temperatures.  

Enjoy your dinner party! For hot days, prioritize chilled whites; for cool nights, lean into reds or mulled varieties. 🍷✨
