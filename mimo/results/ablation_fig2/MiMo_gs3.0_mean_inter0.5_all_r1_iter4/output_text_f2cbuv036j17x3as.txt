Okay, diesel camper vans are workhorses, but they definitely need pre-trip checks. For brakes, you can do a simple visual inspection—look for unevenly worn pads, or if the rotors are shiny from heat—that can indicate problems. Listen for any grinding noises when you stop. If you want to be thorough, you can take it to a mechanic to get the pads and rotors checked. For spark plugs, that's a bit more involved. Diesel engines usually have high-compression, so they need special high-torque spark plugs. You'll need a socket wrench and a spark plug socket, and you’ll have to remove the diesel injectors to get to them. It’s not something most families would want to tackle on the road, so it’s probably best to have a mechanic do it before you leave. A diesel specialist can also check things like the DEF fluid and the glow plugs, which are specific to diesel engines. Just make sure everything’s in good shape before you hit the road.
