Bruno sensitive to apples, bananas, broccoli mentioned so we avoid broccoli on this list.

Here are five safe green vegetables for a parrot:

Kale is a superfood, full of vitamins and minerals.

Cucumber is a great hydrator and is easy for them to eat.

Peas are a good source of protein.

Spinach is another excellent choice, just be sure to remove the tough stems.

And finally, Broccoli Stalks are a good alternative to the head, since Bruno is sensitive to the whole thing.
