    *   **How:** Head outside if possible for fresh air and natural light. Or walk around the block, up and down stairs, or even just pace a quiet hallway. Focus on your surroundings – notice colors, sounds, the feeling of your feet on the ground. This mindful movement is perfect for recharging.

4.  **Power Nap (Use with Caution):**
    *   **Why it's tricky:** 15 minutes is often *too* short for a truly restorative nap (20-30 mins is ideal for the deep restorative stage). You might wake feeling groggy ("sleep inertia") and more jarring than refreshing, especially later in the day.
    *   **If you try it:** Lie down comfortably, set a strict 15-minute alarm, and try to *only* drift into a very light sleep (dozing off for 5-10 minutes max). Be prepared to wake up feeling a bit fuzzy.
