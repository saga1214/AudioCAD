You are absolutely right to raise safety concerns.

While building your connection with him remains incredibly important, the single most important approach for those first 20 to 30 minutes now must include ensuring a safe and reinforcing environment.

This means you should prioritize a few things. First, advocate for supervision and environmental changes with the BCBA. The dogs running around and a cluttered house are serious safety issues that need to be addressed. Second, within the space you have, try to set up a small, predictable 'session corner' to minimize overstimulation. And third, your safety comes first. If elopement or aggression escalates despite your best efforts, your job is to end the session calmly and seek the proper supervision you deserve.
