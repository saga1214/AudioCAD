For starters, grab miso paste or some frozen dashi for miso soup, cucumbers, rice vinegar, sesame oil, salt, and sugar. For the main course, chicken teriyaki requires about a pound of boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, soy sauce, mirin, sake, or rice vinegar, a little sugar, and some stir-fried veggies like bok choy, carrots, and bell peppers with cooking oil, salt, pepper, and maybe some garlic and ginger. For finishing dessert, mango sticky rice calls for sticky jasmine rice, fresh mango, matcha powder if you decide to make that, or one pint of store-bought matcha ice cream. You’ll also need coconut milk, sugar, and salt for that. Oh, and don't forget sesame oil and rice bran oil for cooking. And of course, cooking rice. Enjoy your romantic dinner.
