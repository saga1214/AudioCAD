### 🎣 Ice Fishing Essentials
- **Gear Needed:** Ice auger (electric or hand-cranked), ice fishing rod/reel, bait, opener tool, warm gloves, and a portable fish finder.
- **Tip:** Look for local ice fishing clinics—many communities offer beginner-friendly workshops. Safety first: always check ice thickness (minimum 4 inches for one person, 6+ for vehicles).

### 🚷 Snowshoeing Gear
- **Rent First:** Try local outfitters or parks before buying. Basic gear includes:
  - Snowshoes (sizes vary by weight)
  - Waterproof boots
  - Layered clothing (base + insulating + waterproof shell)
  - Trekking poles
- **Trail Picks:** Look for groomed trails in state parks (e.g., Kettle Moraine) or urban parks with snow.

### ⛷️ Cross-Country Skiing
- **DIY-Friendly:** Rentals available at ski shops. Try the **National Snowmobile Trail** (Wisconsin has sections) or trails like **Prairie du Sac**.
- **Beginner Tip:** Start with classic skis and wax them lightly for glide.

### 🧗‍♂️ Ice Climbing (Adventure Level)
- **For the Bold:** Requires specialized gear (ice axes, crampons). Join a guided tour—**Door County** is a hotspot!

### 🧖 Other Wisconsin Winter Activities
1. **Dog Sledding:** Try **Prairie Dog Sledding** near Mineral Point.
2. **Winter Hiking:** Trails like **Rushbrook County Forest** have frozen waterfalls.
3. **Ice Fishing Derby:** Many lakes host community tournaments (check **DNR events**).
4. **Snowmobiling:** **State Forests** offer groomed trails (rentals available in Door County).

### ❄️ Safety Reminders
- **Never go alone** in remote areas.
- Carry a **spare battery** for your phone/cheese (yes, cheese!).
- Watch for **black ice** on roads and trails.
