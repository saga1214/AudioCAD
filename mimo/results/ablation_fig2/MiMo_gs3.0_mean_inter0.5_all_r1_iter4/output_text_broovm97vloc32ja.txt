If you have illustrations, you can absolutely submit a complete package. You just need to make sure it's formatted correctly. Publishers are used to this.

Here’s what you do: First, you'll create a PDF file with both your manuscript text and your artwork. You should use a single-page layout, so the text on one page flows directly to the image on the next. Then, you'll create a separate document, often called a cover letter, that explains the book's theme and why it's a good fit for them.

When you send your package, it's a good idea to mention that you are the illustrator. This can be a real selling point for a publisher who is looking for an author-illustrator to work with.
