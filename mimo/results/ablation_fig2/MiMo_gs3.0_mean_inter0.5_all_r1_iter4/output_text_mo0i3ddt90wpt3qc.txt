You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which is a great gluten-free foundation. Since camping snacks also need to be something you can handle easily outdoors, I have a couple more gluten-free ideas for you.

Nuts and seeds are always a popular and energy-dense choice, for example almonds, walnuts, or pumpkin seeds. Another good option is gluten-free fruit leather pouches, which are lightweight and hydrating. Both of these are portable, sturdy, and gluten-free friendly for your trip.
