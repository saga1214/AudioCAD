Here’s a streamlined, ATS-friendly resume format with clear priorities—type it out once, and you’re done:

---

### **HEADERS**  
**Name (14pt font, centered or left-aligned)**  
**Email | Phone | City** *(9pt font, icons optional, right-aligned)*  

---

### **CONTACT SEQUENCE ( perfected )**  
Name → → Email → Phone → City → *(LinkedIn URL optional)*  
**Why this works:** Vertical flow is faster for recruiters. Icons risk ATS errors; use standard text links instead.  

---

### **PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY ( 3-4 lines )**  
*Example:*  
> "Strategic Project Manager with 8+ years in SaaS operations. Proven ability to streamline workflows (saving 20% costs) and lead cross-functional teams. Seeking to leverage data-driven decision-making in marketing automation."  
**Tip:** Tailor to your target roles—highlight transferable skills even if past work isn’t directly relevant.  

---

### **EXPERIENCE**  
**Group 1: RELEVANT EXPERIENCE** *(Most recent, closest to target role)*  
- **Company A, Job Title** | *Dates*  
  - Achievement 1 (with metric if possible)  
  - Achievement 2  
- **Company B, Job Title** | *Dates*  
  - Achievement 1  
  - Achievement 2  

**Group 2: ADDITIONAL WORK HISTORY** *(Older roles, if space allows)*  
- **Company C, Job Title** | *Dates*  
  - Brief achievement  

**Gaps?** Address in summary if significant. For 15-year gaps:  
> "Career break (2008–2023); focused on [consulting/volunteer work/education]."  

---

### **SKILLS SECTION**  
**Place it AFTER Experience.** List 5-7 ATS-friendly keywords:  
- Technical: Python, Tableau, Salesforce  
- Soft: Cross-functional Leadership, Budget Management  
- Hard: SEO Optimization, CRM Implementation  

**Avoid:** Vague terms like "Team Player."  

---

### **EDUCATION**  
**School Name** | *City*  
- **Degree** (e.g., B.S. Computer Science)  
- Expected Graduation Date or Year Completed  
- Honors (e.g., *Magna Cum Laude*)  
- Awards (e.g., *Dean’s List 2019–2021*)  

**Scholarships:** List only significant ones (e.g., "Merit Scholarship, $10,000").  

---

### **ADDITIONAL SECTIONS (Optional)**  
- **Certifications:** "PMP, Google Data Analytics"  
- **Languages:** "Fluent in Spanish"  
- **Volunteer Work:** 1–2 bullet points  

---

### **Font & Formatting Rules**  
- **Fonts:** Calibri, Arial, or Lato (10–12pt overall). Name: 14pt.  
- **Margins:** 0.5 inches (standard).  
- **Icons:** Omit email/phone icons—use standard symbols (e.g., ✉️ for email).  
- **Length:** 1 page if <10 years experience; 2 pages max.  

---

### **KEY ADJUSTMENTS FROM YOUR草稿**  
1. Center contacts **directly below your name** (no side placement).  
2. **Remove smaller font (9pt)** for consistency.  
3. **Group roles by relevance**, not chronology.  
4. **Replace icons with text links** (e.g., "LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/yourname").  
5. **Add a Professional Summary** to frame your career pivot.  

---

### **PRO TIP FOR CARRER SHIFTS**  
- **Reframe old roles:** Instead of "Managed social media," say "Grew organic followers by 40%."  
- **Use "Relevant Experience"** to spotlight older roles matching new goals.  
- **Skills section is your secret weapon:** Mirror keywords from job descriptions.  

**Final checklist before submitting:**  
- ✅ No photos or personal details (age, marital status).  
- ✅ File as PDF (name: "FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf").  
- ✅ Save in one column (no tables/columns that break ATS).  

You’ve got this! Focus on **clarity** and **relevance**—not perfection.
