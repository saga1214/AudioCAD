1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
   *Description:* Garlic-butter shrimp simmered with lemon and red pepper flakes.  
   *Ingredients:*

   - 4 tbsp butter

   - 1 tbsp olive oil  
   - 1 tsp red pepper flakes  
   - 2 tbsp lemon juice  
   - Salt and pepper to taste  
   - ½ cup fresh parsley (chopped)  

2. **Spicy Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
   *Description:* Tomato-based sauce with garlic, red pepper flakes, and Cajun spices.  
   *Ingredients:*  
   - 1 lb shrimp (thawed if frozen)  
   - 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce  
   - 4 garlic cloves (minced)  
   - 1 tsp red pepper flakes  
   - 1 tsp Cajun seasoning  
   - 1 tsp Italian seasoning  
   - Salt to taste  

3. **Cajun Shrimp & Grits**  
   *Description:* Creamy polenta topped with spicy Cajun-seasoned shrimp.  
   *Ingredients:*  
   - 1 lb shrimp (thawed)  
   - 1 cup grits (quick-cooking)  
   - 2 cups chicken broth  
   - 2 tbsp butter  
   - ¼ cup grated Parmesan  
   - 1 tsp Cajun seasoning  
   - 1 tsp smoked paprika  
   - ½ tsp cayenne pepper
