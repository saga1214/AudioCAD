Okay Will, for your 90s movie night, here are twelve more recommendations that fit the theme and don't overlap with what we've already discussed. 

You could watch *The Matrix* for its groundbreaking special effects, or *Toy Story* for its heartfelt story. *Starship Troopers* is a fun, over-the-top sci-fi flick, and *The Fifth Element* is quirky and visually unique. For something a bit more intense, there's *Seven*.

If you're in the mood for a comedy, *Ferris Bueller's Day Off* is a classic. For animation, *The Lion King* is a must-see. If you're up for a thriller, *The Silence of the Lambs* is an Oscar-winner. For action, *Terminator 2* is iconic. And to wrap it up, *Goodfellas* is a gripping crime drama.

All of these should provide a great variety for your night!
