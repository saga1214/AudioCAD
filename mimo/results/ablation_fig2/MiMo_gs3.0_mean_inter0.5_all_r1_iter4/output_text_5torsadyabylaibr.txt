For Thanksgiving, you could make a simple pumpkin pie. Since you dislike pumpkins so much, this would be a nice way to use up what's everywhere! It requires no baking and just a few ingredients.

For a classic holiday side dish, you can't go wrong with creamy mashed potatoes. They require no oven and just a few ingredients.

And for a simple main course, roasted turkey legs are a great option. You just place the seasoned turkey legs on a baking sheet and roast them until they're cooked through.
