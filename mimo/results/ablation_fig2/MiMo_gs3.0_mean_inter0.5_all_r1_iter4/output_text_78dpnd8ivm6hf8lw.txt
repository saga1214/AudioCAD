Here's a quick recap using the child car analogy:

*   **Heart:** Gas station *and* engine (pumps cars & makes fuel happen)
*   **Arteries:** Big highways (carry blood *away* from the heart/gas station)
*   **Capillaries:** Tiny side-streets (where oxygen "fuel" gets delivered to body parts)
*   **Veins:** Return roads (carry blood *back* to the heart/gas station)
*   **Lungs:** Lung refueling stops (cars pick up fresh oxygen "fuel")
*   **Kidneys:** Car washes (filter blood, wash out waste, make urine)
*   **Liver:** Sorting station (processes nutrients, breaks down harmful stuff)

This whole system keeps all the little blood cars fueled, clean, and delivering energy!
