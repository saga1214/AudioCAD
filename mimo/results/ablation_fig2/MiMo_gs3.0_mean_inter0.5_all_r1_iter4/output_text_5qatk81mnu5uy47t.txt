### Proposed 10-Day Costa Rica Dive Itinerary (Tailored for Scuba Enthusiasts)

**Days 1-3: Arrival & Playa Conchal (Shore Diving & Resort Life)**
*   **Accommodation:** Stay at a resort in Playa Conchal or nearby Puntarenas. Choose one with good dive centers nearby for easy access.
*   **Diving:** Focus on shore diving here. You'll see abundant tropical fish, schools of silversides, and occasional reef sharks. Manta rays can sometimes be spotted, especially in the mornings. This is perfect for getting acclimated to the water temperature and practicing in calmer conditions. Use your short fins and maneuver them easily.
*   **Why:** Low-key start, minimal travel fatigue, easy access to good dive sites.

**Days 4-7: Cocos Island Liveaboard Adventure (The Main Event)**
*   **Logistics:** This is the crown jewel. You'll fly to Puerto Jiménez on the Osa Peninsula and board a liveaboard dive boat (book well in advance, these fill up fast!). Plan for a 4-day trip (or longer if you can stretch your budget).
*   **Diving Highlights:**
    *   **Marine Iguana Encounters:** Walk among these unique creatures on the volcanic rocks at sites like Playa Hermosa or Playa Naranjo. An experience you won't find anywhere else!
    *   **Whale Sharks:** Seasonal (Dec-Apr), but the chance to see these giants gliding over the seamounts is incredible.
    *   **Mantas:** Abundant and often in large numbers at sites like Manta Point or Punta Yra.
    *   **Reef Sharks:** Reef sharks, hammerheads, and Galapagos sharks patrol the rocky outcrops.
    *   **Deep Dives:** Explore the Cocos Trench, reaching depths of over 300 meters. Requires good certification and experience.
*   **Why:** Unparalleled biodiversity, unique wildlife encounters, remote beauty. This is the quintessential Costa Rican dive.

**Days 8-10: Tenerife Marine Park (Shore & Easy Boat Dives)**
*   **Logistics:** Return to the mainland (Aguas Calientes or Santa Teresa) for easier diving without the long boat trips from Cocos. Many dive centers here offer daily trips.
*   **Diving Highlights:** Focus on the incredible coral walls. Expect schools of fish, rays, turtles, and more reef sharks. Sites like Manta Bay (for mantas) and Whale Shark Bay (seasonal) are fantastic. The visibility is often crystal clear.
*   **Why:** Less travel fatigue after the Cocos adventure, excellent shore diving with strong marine protection.

**Days 11-13: Relaxation & Local Exploration (La Fortuna)**
*   **Accommodation:** Head to the Arenal area (La Fortuna) for a change of scenery.
*   **Activities:** While you recharge (and your legs recover!), explore Arenal Volcano National Park, take a hot spring bath (the warm water is a fantastic contrast after deep dives!), and maybe even try a bit of jungle trekking. You could do a simple, shallow dive or snorkel trip here if you wish, but the relaxation is key.
*   **Why:** Essential balance. Costa Rica is about adventure *and* relaxation. The hot springs are legendary.

**Day 14: Departure**

### Key Logistics & Tips for Your Dream Trip

1.  **Fins are Set:** You're right – start with short fins. Pack them!
2.  **Certification:** If you need a refresher or a new certification (PADI Advanced Open Water, etc.), book it at a reputable center in Playa Conchal or Santa Teresa well before your trip.
3.  **Liveaboard Booking:** This is the most important booking. Research boats with good reviews, comfortable cabins, and dive guides you trust. Popular operators include Ocean Diver, PADI Liveaboards, and others. Start looking now!
4.  **Packing:** Beyond your gear, pack reef-safe sunscreen, a good dive computer, a camera (or go pro!), reef-safe lip balm, and good reef-safe shampoo. Cocos Island is remote – pack for comfort!
5.  **Start Planning:** Booking the liveaboard and flights well in advance is crucial, especially in the peak season (Dec-Apr for whale sharks).
