Here’s a comprehensive list of corgi-approved treats for your "little corgi princess," categorized for easy reference:

### 🏡 Homemade Treats (Wholesome & Customizable)
1. **Freeze-Dried Pumpkin/Ccarrot Cubes**  
   - Mashed pumpkin/sweet potato packed into molds, frozen solid, then cut into tiny cubes. *Chillies her tummy on hot days!*
2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**  
   - Mix 1 cup rolled oats + ¼ cup xylitol-free peanut butter. Bake at 350°F for 12 mins. *Rich in protein!*
3. **Carrot or Sweet Potato Sticks**  
   - Dehydrate thinly sliced veggies in a low oven (200°F for 6 hours) until crisp. *Low-calorie crunch!*
4. **Cheese Bites**  
   - Roll small balls of hard-boiled egg + shredded cheddar cheese. *Calcium boost!*
5. **Cooked Chicken or Turkey**  
   - Dice plain, boiled poultry into pea-sized pieces. *High-value training reward!*

### 🎯 Training Tips
- **Quiet Command:** Teach by rewarding calm moments during barking at Felix.
- **Potty Timing:** Take her out *immediately* after waking, meals, or playtime.
- **Positive Reinforcement:** Use tiny treats (not bigger than a pea) to avoid weight gain.
