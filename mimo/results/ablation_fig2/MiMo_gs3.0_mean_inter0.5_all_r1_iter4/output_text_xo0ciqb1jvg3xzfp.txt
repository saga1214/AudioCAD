For the Pilgrims, it was likely a moment of relief and celebration. For the Wampanoag, the relationship was strategic and mutual, but they were already living in a world that was being devastated by European diseases and colonization.

So, while the shared harvest feast itself was probably a positive experience for both groups, the story of Thanksgiving as a single, happy event is a myth. It doesn't account for the difficult realities and the long-term consequences for the Wampaneag people.
