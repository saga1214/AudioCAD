Based on our conversation, here's a consolidated list of my top tool and material recommendations, along with store suggestions for Indianapolis-area buyers (prioritizing physical stores you mentioned):

### Cutting Thinner Sheet Metal:
1. **Aviation Snips**  
   - *Best for*: Curves on thin sheets (up to ~6mm/0.25").  
2. **Angle Grinder with Cut-off Wheel**  
   - *Best for thick sheets, straight cuts, high speed*.  
3. **Metal-Cutting Jigsaw**  
   - *Best for intricate shapes/thin sheets*.  

**Where to Buy**:  
- **Home Depot, Lowe's, or Menards** (all stock these tools).  
- *Snips alternative*: Tractor Supply Company or Ace Hardware (smaller stores).

### Cutting Thicker Metal / Power Tools:
- **Angle Grinder with Cut-off Wheel**: Check Home Depot/Lowe's/Menards.  

### Drywall Fasteners:
- **Drywall Screws** (1¼" Coarse-Thread Fiberglass)  
  - *Why better*: Prevents nail pops, resists backing out, sets to perfect depth.  
- **Ring-Shank Nails** (Alternative for wood studs)  
  - *Best for*: Heavy-duty fastening if screws aren’t an option.  

**Where to Buy Drywall Screws**:  
- **Home Depot or Menards** (best selection for screws/nails).  

### Insulation:
1. **Fiberglass Batt Insulation**  
   - *Best for*: Standard walls, easy to install between studs.  
2. **Mineral Wool Insulation**  
   - *Best for*: Soundproofing, fire resistance.  
3. **Spray Foam Insulation**  
   - *Best for*: Air sealing, high-performance homes.  

**Where to Buy Insulation**:  
- **Menards** (best insulation selection: fiberglass, mineral wool, spray foam).  
- **Home Depot** (fiberglass batts/spray foam).
