You've got all the essential tools! 

Just to be safe, I'd also suggest grabbing a drill with a few different-sized drill bits. This is super useful for drilling pilot holes into the cabinets or wall material before you screw them in. It just makes everything a lot easier.

Also, grab some painter's tape. You can use it to mark the stud locations or even to protect your walls from scratches while you're working. 

And definitely have a trash bag handy for any old paint chips or debris that might fall off the Habitat cabinets. Habitat restoried items sometimes come with that kind of thing.

You've got everything you need to get started. Just take your time, have fun, and enjoy saving that money!
