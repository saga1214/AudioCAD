Based on your list, **engaging content** is the single most important factor. Here’s why, ranked from most to least critical:

1. **Engaging content**  
   Without immediate engagement (likes, shares, comments, watch time), TikTok’s algorithm won’t promote your video. No matter how good your description, hashtags, or lighting are, if viewers don’t stop scrolling, your content stays invisible. This is the *foundation* of growth.

2. **Following guidelines**  
   If your video gets restricted or removed, it reaches zero people. This is non-negotiable—content must comply with TikTok’s rules to even enter the algorithm. But once it’s compliant, engagement *drives* visibility.

3. **Conciseness**  
   Shorter videos (under 15 seconds) often perform better because viewers are more likely to finish them. High completion rates signal quality to the algorithm, boosting reach. Still, engagement matters more than length alone.

4. **Good description & hashtags**  
   These help the algorithm *understand* your content and suggest it to relevant users. But they’re secondary to making content so compelling that viewers actively engage with it.

5. **Consistency**  
   Posting regularly builds momentum, but it’s useless without engaging content. One viral video can outperform 100 mediocre ones.

### Key Insight:
TikTok rewards **retention** above all else. The algorithm prioritizes videos where viewers:  
- Watch 90%+ of the video  
- Swipe up (if you include text)  
- Engage (comments, shares)  
If your content fails at this, technical polish (lighting/audio) or hashtags won’t save it. Focus first on **immediate hooks** in the first 3 seconds and content that sparks reactions. Everything else supports this core goal.
