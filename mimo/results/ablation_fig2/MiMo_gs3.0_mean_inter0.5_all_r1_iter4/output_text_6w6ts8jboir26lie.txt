That's a great idea—cooking can be such a wonderful way to unwind. Here are five simple, cozy dinner ideas you can whip up with grocery store basics:

1.  **Hearty Tomato-Basil Pasta:** Grab a box of pasta (like penne or spaghetti), a can of crushed tomatoes, fresh basil, garlic, olive oil, salt, pepper, and maybe some mozzarella. Cook the pasta, simmer the tomatoes with garlic and herbs for 10-15 mins, toss it all together, and top with cheese. Serve with a simple green salad.
2.  **Quick & Easy Peanut Noodles:** Get some soba or rice noodles, peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, honey or sugar, garlic, ginger, and chopped veggies (like carrots, cucumber, bell peppers). Whisk the peanut butter, soy, lime, honey, garlic, and ginger for a sauce. Cook the noodles, toss with the sauce and veggies, and top with sesame seeds or chopped peanuts.
3.  **Creamy Garlic & Spinach Pasta:** Pasta, heavy cream (or half-and-half for lighter), garlic, butter, fresh spinach, salt, pepper, grated Parmesan, and maybe some cooked chicken or shrimp. Melt butter in a pan, sauté garlic, add spinach until wilted, pour in the cream, simmer until slightly thickened, toss with cooked pasta, and top with Parmesan.
4.  **Simple Grilled Fish with Lemon & Herbs:** Get some fresh white fish fillets (like cod or tilapia), olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and fresh herbs like dill or parsley. Season the fish, grill or pan-sear it for 5-7 mins per side until flaky. Serve with roasted potatoes (easy microwave bags exist!) and a simple green salad dressed with lemon juice and olive oil.
5.  **Cozy Chili & Cornbread:** Grab a can of black beans, kidney beans, diced tomatoes, ground turkey or beef (optional), chili powder, cumin, frozen corn, and some shredded cheese. Sauté the meat (if using) with onions and spices, add beans, tomatoes, corn, and a splash of water or broth. Simmer for 15-20 mins. Serve over rice with a slice of simple cornbread (mix cornmeal, flour, baking soda, sugar, buttermilk/milk, and oil) and avocado.
