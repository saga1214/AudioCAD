### 🥛 **Vodka Mixer**  
*(Refreshing, light, and easy)*  
**Recipe:**  
- 2 oz **vodka** (your leftover!)  
- 1 oz **fresh lime juice**  
- 3 oz **sparkling water** or **club soda**  
- *Optional garnish:* Lime wedge or cucumber ribbon  

**Why it works:** Crisp, balanced, and universally appealing. The sparkling water adds sophistication without heaviness.  

### 🇦🇷 **Fernet-Morín "Argentinian Style"**  
*(Bold, herbal, and ritualistic)*  
**Recipe:**  
- 1 oz **Fernet-Morín** (or similar herbal liqueur)  
- 2 oz **tonic water**  
- 0.5 oz **fresh lemon juice**  
- *Optional:* 1 dash **orange bitters**  
- *Garnish:* Lemon twist  

**Why it works:** This mirrors the classic Argentine *Fernet & Tonic*, a staple for adults. The lemon brightens the herbal notes, while tonic adds bubbles. Serve chilled in a coupe glass for formality.
