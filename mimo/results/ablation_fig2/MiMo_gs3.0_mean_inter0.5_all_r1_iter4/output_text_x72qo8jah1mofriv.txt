Okay, kosher dietary laws do mean avoiding certain things. The main things to change would be the hummus you were planning. Traditional hummus contains garlic, which is not kosher. You'd want to find a certified kosher version. Oats are usually fine, but you'd want to make sure they're not processed with other grains. Onions are also a no-go, so you'd have to use other vegetables like carrots or bell peppers instead. Hummus can also sometimes be made with fish or dairy, so you'd need to look for a version that's certified vegetarian or meat-based. Dark chocolate is usually fine, but it's always good to check for any non-kosher ingredients like certain additives. So yeah, the main changes would be to the vegetable platter hummus and onion content. The weenies in bacon should be fine as long as the bacon itself is kosher. It's all about checking the labels on those specific items.
