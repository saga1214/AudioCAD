Brown County State Park is another wonderful option, especially around fall when the foliage is gorgeous—it has great trails and views. And if you feel ready to create something sweet to fill a pan, I can definitely help with a recipe for chocolate cake. It’s a crowd-pleaser. You’ll need butter, sugar, eggs, flour, baking powder, milk, and vanilla. Cream the butter and sugar together, then add the eggs one at a time. Mix in the dry ingredients—flour, baking powder, and salt—along with the milk and vanilla. Pour it into a greased 9-inch pan and bake for about 30-35 minutes at 350 degrees Fahrenheit. For the frosting, you can whip together softened butter, powdered sugar, a little vanilla, and a splash of milk until it's smooth and fluffy. Once the cake is completely cooled, you can spread that chocolate frosting all over the top. It’s a comforting alternative that won’t bring back those painful memories.
