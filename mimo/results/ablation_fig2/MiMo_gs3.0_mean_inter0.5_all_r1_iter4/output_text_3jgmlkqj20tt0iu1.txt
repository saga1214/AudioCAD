Initally, you could try scheduling a separate meeting. You could send an email to request a time to meet specifically to discuss your career development and potential growth within the company. This approach shows initiative while giving your boss some advance notice.

An alternative strategy is to raise the topic during your annual performance review. This is a more formal setting and is often the most appropriate time for the conversation to happen anyway.

To manage your anxiety, you could also ask your boss if they would be open to a brief, informal chat to discuss career aspirations. This can help you gauge their receptiveness before committing to a more formal, scheduled meeting.
