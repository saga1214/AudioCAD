Okay, you need a pick-me-up! For drinks, I'd suggest: first, an Iced Matcha Latte, which is a great caffeine boost. Second, a strong Cold Brew Coffee, it's smooth and has a lot of caffeine. Third, a Green Tea with Lemon for a gentle energy lift. Fourth, an Espresso Martini for a fun, alcoholic jolt. And fifth, a Sparkling Water with a little bit of Cinnamon and Honey for a refreshing option without the caffeine.

As for snacks, here are five ideas: number one, Dark Chocolate, which has caffeine and antioxidants. Number two, Nuts and Seeds, for protein and healthy fats. Number three, Greek Yogurt with Berries, which is a balanced energy source. Number four, a Banana with Peanut Butter for quick carbs and protein. And number five, a piece of Beef Jerky for sustained energy. Hope that helps you wake up and get going!
