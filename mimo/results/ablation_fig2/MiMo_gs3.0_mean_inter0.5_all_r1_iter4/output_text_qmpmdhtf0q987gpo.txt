Okay, you've done the cleaning, degreasing, light sanding, and disassembled the hardware. That's a great start. Before you just apply a single coat of primer, there are a couple of small things to double-check.

First, ensure the sanding dust is completely gone. Wipe down all the cabinet surfaces with a tack cloth or even just a damp rag to get rid of any loose particles.

Second, and this is crucial for your specific color change from black to off-white, make sure you're using a primer specifically for covering dark colors. A standard primer might not be enough to block the black completely.

Once you've done that, you're ready to prime.
