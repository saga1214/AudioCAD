Lockport is actually the official home of the Erie Canal Museum, which is the perfect place to start. It has a lot of exhibits on the canal's history, technology, and the lives of the workers. You could also visit the Old Stone Bridge, which is a historic landmark that crosses the canal.

For more general information, you could look up the National Park Service's page for the Erie Canal National Heritage Corridor. They have a lot of great resources and historical maps online.
