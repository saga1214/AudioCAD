That's a fantastic combination – Secret Harbour's calm bay plus Red Hook's energy gives you the perfect blend of relaxation and adventure! Here's a mix of activities tailored for shallow, calm water and your brother's needs on both islands:

**On St. John (Near Secret Harbour & Cruz Bay):**

1.  **Snorkel Secret Harbour Itself:** Your stay! Just steps from your resort. The bay is incredibly calm and shallow near shore, perfect for easy snorkeling right off the dock. Look for parrotfish, surgeonfish, and maybe a small reef turtle.
2.  **Paddle to Trunk Bay (Shallow Section):** The boat entrance to Trunk Bay is a short paddle from Cruz Bay. The initial area near the boat dock is calm, shallow, and has fantastic snorkeling (staghorn coral, parrotfish). You can turn back easily once it gets a bit deeper or more crowded further down the trail.
3.  **Kayak/Cycle Cruz Bay:** Explore the charming shops, cafes, and galleries on two wheels or in a kayak (calm bay water). Morgan's Mango and The Longboard are worth a stop for lunch/drinks.
4.  **Hike Cinnamon Bay to Anegada Ferry:** A beautiful hike along the coast to Cinnamon Bay. The return ferry to Anegada (small, calm crossing) offers stunning views and a unique island experience. Pack a picnic!
5.  **Beachcombing at Reef Bay or Waterlemon Bay:** These are quieter, more secluded bays on the South Shore. Perfect for relaxing, swimming in calm water, and looking for unique shells.

**On St. Thomas (Red Hook & Frenchtown Focus):**

1.  **Snorkel Reef Bay (Red Hook):** Right next to Galliano Grill/Secret Harbour area. Very shallow, calm, and often has good fish life and a small reef. Easy access from shore.
2.  **Paddleboard or Kayak around Red Hook:** Paddle along the mangroves near the resort areas or out towards the reef near Reef Bay. The calm bays and sheltered channels are ideal.
3.  **Frenchtown Lunch & Stroll:** Head to Frenchtown (West End) for renowned seafood. Try The Foodie (creative dishes) or The Black Marlin (classic). Stroll the waterfront and visit the Frenchmen's Caye Market for local crafts and art.
4.  **Hike Magens Bay to Coki Beach:** A moderate hike (about 2 miles round trip) leads from the famous Magens Bay (South Shore, calmer than Coki) down to the beautiful, less crowded Coki Beach (also South Shore, very shallow and calm). The hike offers fantastic views.
5.  **Visit Coki Beach State Park:** Famous for its incredibly shallow, calm, white-sand bay perfect for swimming and wading, especially for kids or those sensitive to depth. Shallow areas are very stable pressure-wise. Snorkeling can be good near the reef drop-off.
6.  **Sunset Sail or Catamaran Cruise:** Many operators offer calm, scenic sunset sails along the coast. Choose a smaller vessel for comfort. Great for both sightseeing and your brother's pressure stability during takeoff模拟起飞).
7.  **Duffy's Longboard Trivia & Happy Hour:** Beyond music, they host trivia nights (great for groups) and have fantastic happy hour specials. A Red Hook staple.

**Key Considerations for Your Boat/Brother:**

*   **Shallow is Safer:** Prioritize beaches and reefs with very shallow, gradual slopes near shore. Avoid boat trips that involve significant depth changes or rapid ascents/descents.
*   **Calm Waters:** Look for bays sheltered by reefs or islands, avoiding open ocean passages unless on a very stable boat.
*   **Communicate:** Let the captain know about your brother's eardrum issue, especially before出发前起飞模拟起飞 or navigating rough patches.
*   **Hydration & Sun:** Virgin Islands sun is intense. Pack plenty of water, sunscreen, hats, and sunglasses. Boats have limited shade!
*   **Snorkel Gear:** Bring your own mask, snorkel, and fins. Renting is easy onshore or onboard.
