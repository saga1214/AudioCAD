Okay, host here - New Year's Eve prep complete! 

First, I've organized everything into categories: drinks, appetizers, kids-focused items, sides, main, kids activities/decor, and essentials. 

For drinks: Champagne (about 12-15 750ml bottles), Signature Cocktail (say margarita or mojito ingredients), Sparkling Grape Juice (4-5 gallons), soda water, mixers, ice, cups, and plastic straws/cups.

For appetizers: Slider buns/meat/pigs in blanket/shrimp cocktail dressing. Cocktail sauce and toothpicks needed too.

For kids specifically: Glazed carrots alternative (olive oil/salt), small juice cups to avoid spills, noisemakers/hats, and maybe coloring sheets if you want extra quiet time.

For sides: Potatoes for scalloped, asparagus, olive oil and lemon for roasting, and salt/pepper.

For main: 12 to 14 lbs of ham, baking powder for scalloped potatoes, and salt for carrots.

For essentials: Aluminum foil, baking powder, salt, pepper, olive oil, lemon, and plastic wrap.

Kids' fun stuff: Balloons and festive hats/noisemakers.

25 adults plus four kids equals 29 guests total. I've estimated quantities based on appetizers per person and drinks. You might want to adjust slightly based on how much you think people will eat. Happy hosting! 🎉
