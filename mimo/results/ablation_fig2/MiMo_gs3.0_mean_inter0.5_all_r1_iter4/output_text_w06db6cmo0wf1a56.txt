What a fantastic plan! Here's a **mess-minimizing shopping list** packed into categories for easy cooler organization, balancing health, variety, kid/adult preferences, and portability for your group of 10 dads + 10 kids at the theme park:

**Goal:** Separate coolers/zones per category, use leak-proof containers/bags, pre-portion everything.

---

### **DRINKS (Focus on Hydration + Low-Sugar Options)**
*(Fill multiple large water coolers!)*  
*   **Base:** 100+ Gallon Jugs of Water (for refills)  
*   **For Kids:**  
    *   Low-Sugar Juice Boxes (Apple, Grape - ~20 boxes)  
    *   Sports Drink Boxes (for extra hydration during rides, ~10 boxes)  
*   **For Adults:**  
    *   Sugar-Free Soda (Dr. Pepper, Sprite, Coke, etc. - ~20 cans)  
    *   Sparkling Water (Unflavored or Fruit-Flavored - ~20 cans)  
    *   Lemonade or Iced Tea Powder Packets (Individual - ~20 packets for DIY)  
*   **Extras:** Ice Packs (Plenty!), Large Insulated Bottles/Jugs (for refilling water/soda), Small Travel Bottles (for water/soda).

---

### **SNACKS (Grab-and-Go, Minimal Prep/Mess)**
*   **Cheese & Protein:**  
    *   20 Cheese Sticks (Variety: Mozzarella, Cheddar)  
    *   Babybel Cheese (Individual Wax-Pouches - 10 packs)  
*   **Fruits (Easy Hands):**  
    *   Grapes (Pre-pitted - 2 large bags)  
    *   Baby Carrots (Pre-cut - 2 large bags)  
    *   Cucumber Sticks (Pre-cut - 1 bag)  
*   **Crunchy/Salty:**  
    *   Single-Serve Pretzel Packs (Small bags - 10 packs)  
    *   Trail Mix (Individual small foil bags - 10 packs)  
    *   Raisins or Dried Fruit (Small bags - 10 packs)  
*   **Other Grabables:**  
    Calorie-Dense Trail Mix (for kids who need a boost)  
    Individual Packets of Guacamole or Salsa (for dipping chips if you have them)  
    Baby Food pouches (for toddlers)  
    *Optional:* Hard-boiled eggs (pre-peeled, 10-12 eggs).

---

### **MAIN MEAL (Calzones Included!)**
*   **Calzones (The Star!):**  
    *   **Option 1 (Messiest but Best "Real" Feel):** Order **10 Fresh Calzones** from a local pizza place. *Ask them to:*  
        *   Wrap individually in foil/parchment.  
        *   Include toothpicks for easy grabbing.  
        *   Pack extra foil/parchment for reheating in a cooler if needed (check cooler temp!).  
        *   *Tip:* Pre-portion dips (like marinara or garlic sauce) in small containers for dipping.  
    *   **Option 2 (Easiest & Cleanest):** Order **10 Mini Calzones** or **10 Calzones with Pre-Made Sauce** (marinara/garlic) from a pizza place. They'll be easy to hand out cold or slightly reheated.  
*   **Sandwiches/Wraps:**  
    *   10 Individual Wrap Bags or 10 Sandwiches (cut in half for easier kid-grabbing)  
        *   Fillings: Turkey & Cheese, Ham & Swiss, Veggie Hummus, Chicken Salad.  
        *   *Pack:* Tortillas/wraps, fillings pre-portioned, and maybe small containers of mustard/ketchup if needed.  
*   **Pasta Salad:**  
    *   1 Large Container of Pasta Salad (Salad-style, not hot & heavy). Great protein/filling.  
*   **Protein Bites:**  
    *   1-2 Large Containers of Veggie Sticks or Baby Carrots + Single-Serve Hummus Cups (Trail Mix Hummus) - for dipping alongside calzones/salad.

---

### **DESSERT (Celebration & Treat!)**
*   **Cupcakes:**  
    *   12-15 Cupcakes (Bachelor/Bachelorette theme encouraged!). Individual portions, easy grab.  
*   **Brownie Bites:**  
    *   12-15 Small Individual Brownie Bites (Pre-packaged or homemade in small bags).  
*   **Pie Option (If Cupcakes/Brownies aren't enough):**  
    *   **Key:** Get a **Small, Individual-Sized Pie** (Key Lime, Lemon Meringue, or a small Chocolate Cream Pie). *Pack:*  
        *   Pie in a sturdy container/bag.  
        *   Pie Server (small plastic knife/spatula).  
        *   Pie Plate (or use a sturdy napkin for serving).  
        *   *Risk:* Slightly messier than cupcakes, but a crowd-pleaser! Pre-cut slices if possible.

---

### **COOLERS & Organization Tips**
1.  **Coolers:** Pack 2-3 large coolers. Label clearly: "DRINKS," "SNACKS," "MAIN MEAL (Calzones/Salad/Sandwiches)," "DESSERT."
2.  **Ice:** Use lots of ice packs + bags of ice. Keep drinks cold.
3.  **Dips/Sauces:** Pre-portion hummus, marinara, etc., into small containers.
4.  **Utensils:** Include plenty of paper plates, napkins, biodegradable gloves, wet wipes, hand sanitizer, trash bags.
5.  **Calzone Handling:** If using fresh calzones, keep them in the main meal cooler with ice packs. Offer dips in a separate cooler.
6.  **Soda Strategy:** Keep soda in one cooler. Offer water/juice/hummus/hot sauce in another.
7.  **Label Everything:** "For Kids," "For Adults," "Dips," "Dessert," etc.

**Enjoy the party!** This setup gives everyone choices, minimizes mess, and lets the dads relax with their kids knowing mommas are recharging. Have an absolutely epic theme park adventure!
