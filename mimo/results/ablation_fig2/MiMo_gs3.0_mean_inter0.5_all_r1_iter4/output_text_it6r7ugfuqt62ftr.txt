**Detailed 4-Day Itinerary: Palm Springs**

*   **Travel:** Fly into Palm Springs International Airport (PSP), just 15 mins from downtown. Rent a large SUV or minivan at PSP for carpooling ease. Healthy budget covered by rental and flights.

*   **Stay (2 Nights):** **Ace Hotel Palm Springs** (Luxury/Unique). Modern, stylish, great pools, walkable. Or **Homes by Vrbo** (Vacation Rentals) for a full house with kitchen. Healthy budget allows flexibility.

*   **Day 1: Arrival & Poolside Relaxation**
    *   Morning/Afternoon: Arrive PSP, rent car, check-in.
    *   Late Afternoon: Head straight to your hotel's pool for that diet-swimsuit confidence boost! Soak up the sun.
    *   Evening: Casual dinner near hotel (e.g., **Salvatore's Italian Kitchen** - great pasta, ask about GF options).

*   **Day 2: Pool Fun & Hiking Adventure**
    *   Morning: Extended pool time! Swim, float, relax. Reap those diet rewards!
    *   Afternoon: Desert hike adventure! Choose one:
        *   **Palm Canyon Trail:** Easy, iconic, shaded canyon walk (1-2 hrs).
        *   **Arden Hot Springs:** Hike to natural hot springs (30-45 mins each way, scenic).
        *   **Palm Springs Aerial Tramway:** Cable car ride up Mt. San Jacinto for stunning views (check winter conditions).
    *   Evening: Splurge dinner! **Nobu Malibu** (45 mins drive, iconic) or **Lionfish** (Palm Springs, upscale seafood - confirm GF safety).

*   **Day 3: Shopping & Relaxation**
    *   Morning: Explore **Palm Springs Art District** (boutiques, galleries, cafes).
    *   Late Morning/Afternoon: Relax at **Mandeville Spray Pool** (free, public, huge, great vibe) OR your hotel's resort pool.
    *   Evening: Fun dinner! **Elmer's Restaurant** (retro vibe, excellent Mexican - ask about GF) or **The Green Chair** (upscale Mediterranean - confirm GF safety).

*   **Day 4: Scenic Drive & Departure**
    *   Morning: Leisurely breakfast. Quick dip if time/energy allows.
    *   Late Morning: Scenic drive along **Palm Springs to Joshua Tree National Park** (stunning desert landscapes). Stop at **Pioneertown** (quirky old town) or **Palm Springs State Park** (easy hikes, swimming hole possible in season).
    *   Afternoon: Return rental, fly home.

**Pack-Pantry Note:** Don't forget snacks! Healthy budget helps if you pack GF trail mix, fruit, etc.
