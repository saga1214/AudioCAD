Alright, here is a nautical-themed birthday shopping list tailored for seven seven-year-old pirates at the beach. The goal is sand-minimal finger foods, a watermelon-free menu due to allergies, and immersive treasure vibes. I've organized it into categories for clarity.

First, for the nautical decorations, you'll want things like a 'treasure map' tablecloth, plastic pirate ships for centerpieces, and 'X marks the spot' signs. You could also hang jolly rogers or use blue and gold tableware for that ship deck look.

Next, for appetizers and snacks, which we'll call 'The First Voyage'. You could serve mini pirate ship sandwiches cut in half, shaped like tiny sailboats. Or maybe some cut fruit arranged like treasure chests, avoiding watermelon as discussed. Veggie 'jewels' skewers shaped like eyes or anchors would be fun. And of course, cheese cubes and crackers sealed in treasure chest containers.

For the main course, 'Feast at Sea'. I'd suggest themed sliders, like a 'Captain's Plank' with turkey or veggie patties, or 'Mermaid's Bounty' with cheese and ham. You could also do 'Sunken Treasure' pasta cups in mini cups.

For sides, to keep it simple, 'Ocean Waves' fruit cups, 'Sand Dollar' potato chips, and 'Cannonball' cheese balls would work.

For drinks, 'Pirate Punch' boxes with fruit punch and blue food coloring are great. Individual water bottles with pirate-themed labels are also perfect. Don't forget juice boxes, maybe with pirate names.

For dessert, beyond your amazing cake, you could do 'Cursed Cookie Crumbles'—mini pirate cookies—and 'Doubloons'—chocolate-covered pretzel rods.

For packaging, seal snacks in individual bags labeled with pirate phrases like 'Avast!' or 'Yarrr!' And use small containers to mimic treasure chests.

Finally, a few other essentials. Have plenty of napkins, wet wipes, and hand sanitizer handy. And maybe some themed utensils like plastic cutlasses to complete the pirate vibe. This list should help keep the party fun and sand-free while your son celebrates in style. Happy hosting, Captain!
