For the 'Retro Tech' theme, a main dish could be something like a 'Vintage Computer Chip Salad,' where you use triangular crackers to look like old computer chips and serve with a simple vinaigrette and maybe some canned tuna for a retro touch. For snacks, you could have 'Floppy Disk Fruit Skewers,' using small plastic discs as bases for grapes or berries. Another option is a 'Dial-Up Soup Day,' where everyone brings a simple soup, like tomato or chicken noodle, and you could serve it with 'Modem Muffins'—small tea cakes that look like old modems.
