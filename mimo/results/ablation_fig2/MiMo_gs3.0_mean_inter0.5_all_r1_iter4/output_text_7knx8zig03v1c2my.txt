### **Treat Recommendations: Sciencey or Italian Flavors**
- **Science-themed**: Freeze-dried liver, salmon, or even tiny "antenna" toys (science-y objects).  
- **Italian roots**: Olive oil treats, ricotta-flavored dog biscuits, or even mini cannoli dog treats.  
- **Universal favorites**: Chicken (since yours loves it), turkey, or cheese (puppies love mild cheddar).  

### **Clicker Training Explained**
- **How it works**: The clicker is a conditioning tool. When Galileo does something right (e.g., sits), you click *immediately*—this sound marks the exact moment he’s correct. Then you give a treat.  
- **Why it’s effective**: The click becomes a bridge between his action and reward. Soon, he’ll associate the *sound* with a treat, making training faster.  
- **Steps**:  
  1. Click when he sits.  
  2. Give a treat.  
  3. Repeat 10–20 times daily.  
  4. Gradually add the verbal cue ("sit!") before the click/treat.  

---

### **Recall Training Tips (Preventing Escapes)**  
- **Start indoors**: Practice calling "Galileo!" with a high-value reward (e.g., chicken) *before* he runs out the door.  
- **Build distance**: Begin close, then slowly increase space.  
- **Never punish**: If he doesn’t come, calmly retrain. Scolding breaks trust.  
- **Fence solution**: If no fence, use a **training tether** (leash attached to a door handle) or a **baby gate**.
