### ☕ Coffee Hack for Latte:
1. **Dissolve instant coffee** in 2 tbsp hot water.  
2. **Warm milk** in the microwave for 1 minute (not longer—prevents scalding).  
3. **Combine**: Pour milk into a mug, then add the dissolved coffee. Stir.  
   → *Result: Creamy, latte-like texture with instant coffee!*
