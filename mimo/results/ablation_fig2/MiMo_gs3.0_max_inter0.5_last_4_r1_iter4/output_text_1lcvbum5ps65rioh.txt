### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia  
In BC, U-turns are generally permitted **only when:**
1. **You can see 150 meters (about 1.5 football fields) in both directions** (no obstructions like hills or trees).  
2. **No traffic control signals (e.g., stop signs, traffic lights)** are present at the intersection.  
3. **No "No U-Turn" signs** are posted.  
4. **No pedestrians/cyclists** are in your path.  
5. **Not in a no-standing/yellow box zone**.  

**Penalties for Illegal U-Turns:**  
- **$150 fine** + 3 demerit points (under BC’s *Motor Vehicle Act*).  
- If it causes an accident, penalties escalate (e.g., driver’s license suspension).  

**Tip:** Use designated U-turn lanes or intersections with clear visibility. If unsure, wait for a safe gap!

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
Here’s an authentic Italian recipe (vegetarian-friendly, no pork!):  
#### **Essential Ingredients:**  
- 400g (14 oz) spaghetti  
- 150g (5 oz) guanciale (or pancetta if unavailable)  
- 3 large eggs  
- 50g (1/2 cup) Pecorino Romano (or Parmesan)  
- 2 tbsp Pecorino/Parmesan, finely grated  
- Freshly ground black pepper  
- Salt  

#### **Optional Additions:**  
- 1 garlic clove (minced)  
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley  
- Red pepper flakes  

#### **Instructions:**  
1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve 200ml pasta water.  
2. Fry diced guanciale/pancetta in a pan until crispy. Remove from heat (keep warm).  
3. Whisk eggs, Pecorino, pepper, and 2 tbsp cheese in a bowl.  
4. Toss hot pasta with guanciale. Remove from heat.  
5. **Temper the eggs:** Gradually mix in ½ cup pasta water while stirring eggs into the pan.  
6. Add the egg mixture to the pan, stirring constantly until creamy.  
7. Serve with extra cheese, parsley, and pepper.  

**Key Tips:**  
- **No cream!** Authentic carbonara uses eggs and cheese.  
- **Tempering is crucial** to avoid scrambled eggs.  
- **Guanciale** (cured pork cheek) is traditional but pancetta works.
