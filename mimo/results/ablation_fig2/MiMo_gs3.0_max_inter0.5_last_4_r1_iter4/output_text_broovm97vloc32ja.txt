That's a fantastic asset to have. Being able to illustrate your own work is a huge advantage. Here's the approach for that. You would need to create what's called a 'sample book' or a 'physical book.' This means putting together a complete-looking book with your text and your art. You can do this yourself using free software like Canva or even a simple Word document. You can even print a physical copy from a local store like Staples.

Once you have that complete sample book, you can submit it to publishers. This gives them a much stronger sense of your vision. However, it's very important to know that most traditional publishers will still require you to hire an illustrator. They'll buy the book rights and commission a professional artist to create the final illustrations. Your sample book just proves you can do the job, but it doesn't guarantee you'll be the one who gets hired. So, it's a great first step, but you should still be prepared to follow the standard children's book agent and publisher process.
