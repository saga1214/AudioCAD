Great idea! Tuning your instrument can be a relaxing way to pass the time. Here's how to tune your stringed instrument (like a guitar, ukulele, or mandolin) while you wait for your dye to rinse out:

### 🛠️ Tools You'll Need:
1. **Clip-On Tuner** (like Snark or Boss) or a **Tuner App** (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune).
2. Your instrument (make sure it's clean and free of hair dye residue!).

---

### 🎸 Step-by-Step Tuning Guide:
1. **Plug in your tuner** (or open the app) and attach it to the headstock.
2. **Pluck an open string** (e.g., the 6th string on a guitar).
3. **Listen to the tuner**:
   - If the needle/app display shows **"flat" (♭)**, gently tighten the string (turn the tuning peg *toward* you).
   - If it shows **"sharp" (♯)**, loosen the string (turn the peg *away* from you).
4. **Adjust until the tuner reads "0"** (or the note matches the target pitch, e.g., E for the 6th string).
5. **Repeat for all strings**, moving from the thickest (6th string) to the thinnest (1st string).

---

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Fine-tune**: If the string is close but slightly off, turn the tuning pegs *very slowly* (1/4 turn at a time).
- **Relative Tuning**: Use the 5th fret of the 4th string as a reference (e.g., tune to an A, then harmonics for other strings).
- **Double-Check**: Tune each string twice to ensure consistency.
- **Tuning Order**: Standard guitar order:  
  6th (Low E) → 5th (A) → 4th (D) → 3rd (G) → 2nd (B) → 1st (High E).

Once tuned, play a simple chord or scale to test your work! 🎶
