For Thanksgiving, you could try a no-bake pumpkin cheesecake bites. You just mix cream cheese with pumpkin spice, pour it over crushed graham crackers, and chill it.

For a more savory side dish, you could make honey-roasted carrots. You just toss the carrots with olive oil, honey, salt, and pepper and roast them.

And for a simple dessert again, salted caramel apples are a fun and easy option. You just dip apples in caramel and roll them in crushed nuts or sprinkles.
