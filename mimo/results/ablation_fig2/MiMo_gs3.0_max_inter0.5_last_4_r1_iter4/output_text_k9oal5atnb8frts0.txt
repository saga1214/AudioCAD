Okay, got it. For a retro tech theme, let's keep the meals simple but fun. First, a “Circuit Board Platter.” This could have a sandwich cut into a square with diagonal lines to look like a circuit, maybe with ham and cheese. Alongside, a small bowl of trail mix that looks like binary code, which is just nuts and seeds. And for a drink, a cup labeled “RAM” with a simple design on it, filled with lemonade or iced tea. Second, for a “Vintage Computing Lunch,” you could do a“Floppy Disk Sandwich,” which is a pita pocket filled with hummus and veggies to look like a disk. Serve that with“Dial-Up Soup” in a bowl that has a pattern of old modem symbols on it, and some“RAM” cookies, which are just shortbread cookies shaped like little memory sticks. Both ideas are easy to pack and tie into the theme.
