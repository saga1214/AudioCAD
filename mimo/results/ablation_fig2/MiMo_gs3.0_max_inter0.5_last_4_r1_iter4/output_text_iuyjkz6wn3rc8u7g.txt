Okay, so to quickly recap, making yogurt is like throwing that group hug party. Milk is the empty room, good bacteria are the guests, keeping it warm energizes them. Sugar is their food source. Getting energized from sugar is key. Overheating the yogurt is like making the room too hot, which kills the bacteria and leaves you with just warm milk. And finally, the tangy texture comes from everyone linking arms in that giant group hug. Hope that helps!
