Sure! First, think about your daily routine to see which breeds might fit. Next, research shelters or rescues to see if they have candidates that match what you’re looking for. If purebreds are more appealing, look for ethical breeders who can provide health information. Once you find a few potential candidates, meet them in person to see how they interact with you. Don’t forget to ask about adoption fees and any other costs. Taking your time will help you find the right rescue hybrid or purebred for your home.

As for breeds, some chill, kid-friendly, and less likely to chase small animals include:
1. Basset Hound - friendly and low-energy.
2. Bulldog - calm and laid-back.
3. French Bulldog - adaptable and affectionate.
4. Labrador Retriever - great with kids and easy to train.
5. Golden Retriever - patient and gentle.
6. Pug - chill and great with people.
7. Shih Tzu - affectionate and adaptable.
8. Corgi - playful but can be lazy too.

Remember to check landlord size and noise concerns again just to be sure. Also, consider consulting a trainer if needed, especially since neither of you has pet-care experience. Happy dog hunting!
