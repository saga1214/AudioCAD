### Key Notes:
1. **Tool Selection by Thickness**:  
   - ✂️ **< 8mm/0.3"**: Aviation snimpls (Lowe's has curved-tip models)  
   - 🔧 **> 10mm/0.4"**: Angle grinder (Home Depot carries Makita/DeWalt wheels)  
   Locations like **The Home Depot (Westfield)** and **Lowe's (Naples)** have full metal-tool sections. For specialized blades, visit their pro/DIY tool aisles.

2. **Drywall Screws**:  
   - 1¼" coarse-thread screws are ideal for preventing "pop-outs" in wood studs. Home Depot's **GRK** brand offers the best holding power at ~$10 for 250.

3. **Insulation**:  
   - **Fiberglass** (e.g., Owens Corning 700 Series) is easiest for DIY. Mineral wool (Rockwool) at Menards is pricier but superior for noise reduction.

### Pro Tip:
For sheet metal curves, pair aviation snimpls with a **metal-cutting jig** (available at Lowe's for ~$20)—it ensures clean, precise cuts.
