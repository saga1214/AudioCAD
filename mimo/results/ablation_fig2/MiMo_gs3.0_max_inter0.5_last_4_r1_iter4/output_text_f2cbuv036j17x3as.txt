For diesel camper vans, pre-trip maintenance is crucial. First, brakes. You can check them by looking at the brake pads—if they’re thin or worn down, you might need to replace them. Also, listen for any squeaking or grinding noises when you brake, which can be a sign of problems. The brake fluid level should be checked, too. Second, spark plugs. Diesel engines use high compression, so spark plugs wear out faster. You’ll need a special tool called a spark plug socket to remove them. If they’re old or look worn, you’ll want to replace them. Other things to check before you go include the diesel filter, oil, and tires—all of those can make a big difference in performance and safety. It's always best to get a professional check-up right before you leave.
