Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimming vacations! Pack accordingly:

Bathing Suits: Two sets - a classic one-piece and maybe a colorful two-piece.
Cover-Up: Pack a stylish one made of quick-dry fabric.
Daywear: Sundresses, tank tops, flowy skirts paired with shorts, breezy pants.
Footwear: Flip-flops or water shoes, comfortable sandals, and maybe a pair of casual sneakers.
Accessories: Sunglasses, wide-brimmed hat, high-quality broad-spectrum sunscreen.
Warm Layer: A lightweight sarong or shawl to use over your suit if it cools down.
Extras: A waterproof phone pouch and a small dry bag.

Have a fantastic warm getaway! Remember California weather can change, so that light sweater is still smart.

Birthdays after diagnosis can be tough. Prioritize activities where she feels adventurous and in control. Since hot air balloons feel too intense for you:

Top Outdoor Alternatives:
1. Marine Layer Helicopter Tour: Offers incredible views of the Central Coast from above - thrilling but not a 'balloon ride' and feels very special.
2. Monterey Bay Aquarium: World-class, mesmerizing marine life, and completely accessible. It's a guaranteed awe experience.
3. Big Sur Scenic Drive: Hike to McWay Falls, visit Pfeiffer Beach, and enjoy the stunning coastline. Pack a picnic lunch.

For Celiac-Safe Celebrations:
- Find a dedicated gluten-free bakery (like Sweet Loom in Santa Barbara) for a custom birthday cake.
- Choose restaurants with excellent GF policies: El Capitan in Santa Barbara for upscale dining, or The Ranch in Big Sur for a relaxed gourmet meal.
- Pack your own certified GF snacks and treats for the journey.

The most important thing is your support and celebrating her spirit. This thoughtful approach will mean the world to her.

For your 4-5 day family getaway in a larger vehicle, Palm Springs offers warmth and activities for all ages. Here's a sample itinerary:

**Destination:** Palm Springs, California
**Why:** Consistently warm weather (80s-90s in winter), pools, hiking, unique mid-century modern architecture.

**Accommodation (Choose one):**
*   **Luxury:** A Casasol Resort - Pools, spa, dining, and luxury villas.
*   **Mid-Range:** Hotel Palacio - Historic building with a fantastic pool and restaurant.
*   **Unique:** The Ace Hotel Palm Springs - Stylish and modern.

**Day 1: Arrival & Relaxation**
*   Travel to Palm Springs (approx. 3.5 hours from SF Bay Area).
*   Check into your hotel. Unwind at the pool or spa.
*   Dinner at your hotel or nearby restaurant. Research GF options in advance.

**Day 2: Pool & Shopping**
*   Enjoy the hotel pool and maybe a spa treatment.
*   Late afternoon: Explore Palm Springs Aerial Tramway for stunning views and a short hike (like the Palm Canyon Trail).
*   Dinner at a local restaurant - check reviews for GF options.

**Day 3: Hiking & Architecture**
*   Morning hike: Joshua Tree National Park (short walk to Hidden Valley or Bunching Rock) or a longer hike in the Coachella Valley.
*   Afternoon: Explore the vibrant downtown area and the unique mid-century modern architecture.
*   Evening: Dinner at a restaurant known for its GF-friendly menu.

**Day 4: Adventure & Farewell**
*   Morning: Choose an exciting activity! Consider:
    *   **Adventure:** A Palm Springs Hot Air Balloon Ride (a bucket list experience without the discomfort of a small balloon).
    *   **Water Fun:** Go-Karts at Palm Springs Go-Kart Track or mini-golf.
    *   **Shopping:** Indian Wells or Coachella Valley shops.
*   Farewell Lunch and Departure.

**Getting Around:** Renting a car is essential for exploring beyond downtown Palm Springs.

**Dining:** Research certified gluten-free restaurants in Palm Springs before you go. Popular ones include:
*   Cacique (Upscale Mexican)
*   The Bistro (Classic American)
*   Indian Wells Cafe (Casual & GF)
*   Sweet Loom Bakery (GF Treats)

**Packing:** Continue your warm-weather swim vacation packing! Palm Springs evenings are cool, so pack light layers.

Enjoy your warm, relaxing, and adventurous family vacation in the desert!
