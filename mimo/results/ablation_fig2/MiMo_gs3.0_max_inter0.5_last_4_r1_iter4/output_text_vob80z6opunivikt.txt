That's fantastic! Since you don't lean into sweet snacks, here are some savory and satisfying ideas to fill up your new coffee table—plus a few playful hybrids if you're feeling adventurous:

### 🧀 **Savory Stars:**
1. **Cheese & Crackers:**  
   - Mini cheddar, brie, or goat cheese cubes with water crackers or pretzel crisps.  
   - *Bonus:* Pair with a small bowl of grapes or cucumber slices.  

2. **Nuts & Seeds:**  
   - Almonds, walnuts, pistachios, or pumpkin seeds (raw or lightly salted).  
   - *Tip:* Look for "dry-roasted" versions to skip added sugar.  

3. **Veggies & Hummus:**  
   - Carrot sticks, celery, bell peppers, or cherry tomatoes with a tub of classic or roasted garlic hummus.  

4. **Beef or Turkey Jerky:**  
   - Protein-packed and chewy—great for sustained energy.  

5. **Cheese Curds or Poutine Chips:**  
   - Savory, crispy, and cheese-forward (not overly sweet).  

### 🍫 **Savory-Sweet Hybrids (if you're curious):**  
- **Chocolate-Covered Pretzels:** Salty pretzel + dark chocolate.  
- **Sea Salt Caramel:** Butterscotch-flavored (not overly sweet) with a pinch of flaky salt.  
- **Spiced Dark Chocolate Bars:** Look for 70%+ cocoa with chili or sea salt.  

### 🧊 **From the Vending Machine:**  
- **Savory:** Corn nuts, cheese puffs, or roasted chickpeas.  
- **Hybrids:** Chocolate-covered coffee beans (salted caramel or mocha).  
- **Avoid:** Anything labeled "candy," "fudge," or "chocolate-dipped" if you dislike sweets.  

### 💡 **Pro Tip:**  
Use your drawer for **reusable containers** (like a mini cheeseboard box) to keep snacks fresh. For the table top, arrange flat bars (chocolate, crackers) in a grid—so they’re easy to grab!
