Here’s a sample itinerary that gives you a taste of Tokyo and Osaka while allowing for a day trip to Kyoto and Nara. I've assumed you'll arrive in Tokyo first and then travel to Osaka later. You can adjust it based on your flight schedule.

First, for your five days in Tokyo:
- Day 1: Arrival at Narita or Haneda Airport. You can take the Narita Express or the Limousine Bus to central Tokyo. In the evening, explore Shinjuku, which includes places like Omoide Yokocho and Golden Gai. You can also visit the Metropolitan Government Building for free views of Shinjuku.
- Day 2: Visit Asakusa for Senso-ji Temple and Nakamise Market, then head to Akihabara for anime and electronics. In the evening, you can experience Shibuya Crossing at night and visit Harajuku's Takeshita Street.
- Day 3: Visit Ueno Park, which has museums and temples, then go to Meiji Shrine in Yoyogi Park. In the evening, you could explore Roppongi or take a cruise on the Roppongi Cruise.
- Day 4: Day trip to Nikko. You can take the train to UNESCO World Heritage sites like Kegon Falls and Toshogu Shrine.
- Day 5: Visit the Tsukiji Outer Market in the morning, then explore the teamLab Borderless or Planets digital art museum. In the evening, you can take a day trip to Kamakura to see the Great Buddha and Hasedera Temple.

Next, for your four days in Osaka:
- Day 6: Travel to Osaka, which takes about 30 minutes by train. In the afternoon, visit Osaka Castle Park and the surrounding Dotonbori area, famous for its street food and neon signs.
- Day 7: Take a day trip to Kyoto. From Osaka, the trip is about 30 minutes by train. You can visit Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion, and Ryoan-ji Temple. You can also visit Nijo Castle and walk through the Gion district.
- Day 8: Go to Nara for a day trip. It's about an hour by train. You can visit Todai-ji Temple, which has the Great Buddha, and Nara Park with its friendly deer.
- Day 9: Explore Osaka's Universal Studios Japan in the morning. In the afternoon, you can visit Umeda Sky Building for great city views and the Abeno Harukas for the highest observation deck.

Finally, for your return:
- Day 10: Travel back to Tokyo if you have class soon, or fly out from Osaka. You can use the Osaka Airport Limousine Bus to Kansai International Airport.

This itinerary gives you a good balance of city exploration and cultural sights. Just remember to book tickets for popular attractions in advance and use the JR Pass if you plan on taking multiple train trips. Enjoy your holiday!
