### Summary of Solutions:

#### For Balanced Feeding (Greedy vs Fussy Eaters):
1. **Chip-Activated/Microchip Feeders**: Restrict each cat to their portion.
2. **Timed Feeders**: Release smaller meals throughout the day.
3. **Feeding Isolation**: Isolate fussy cats in a separate room during feeding before you leave.

#### For Managing Poverty & Nutrition:
1. **Seek Support**: Search for local pet food banks, shelters, or charities.
2. **Bulk Buying**: Purchase quality food in large bags (cost-per-serving drops).
3. **Supplement Wisely**: Mix small amounts of quality food with safe fillers (e.g., plain cooked rice). *Consult a vet first.*
4. **Prioritize Essentials**: Focus on low-cost vet services like spaying/neutering and core vaccinations.

#### For Pro-Budget Health Maintenance:
1. **Prevent Illness**: 
   - Keep environments clean (bowls, litter boxes).
   - Offer fresh water daily.
   - Use homemade toys (string, paper) for exercise.
2. **Monitor Closely**: Watch for early signs of illness (lethargy, appetite changes).
3. **DIY Care**: 
   - Brush cats to reduce hairballs/skin issues.
   - Supplement with safe, inexpensive scraps (e.g., plain cooked chicken/fish).

**Key Principle**: Do what you can with what you have. Prevention and vigilance save costs, while targeted support (chip feeders, food banks) address immediate cat-specific needs. Prioritize fussy cats’ fair access while controlling greed-driven overeating.
