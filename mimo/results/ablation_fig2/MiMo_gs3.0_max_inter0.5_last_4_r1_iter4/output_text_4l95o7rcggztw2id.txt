Here’s a **nautical-themed, low-mess shopping list** for your son’s pirate beach party, designed for easy handling at the beach—with pirate-inspired names and sand-free packaging. Quantities are estimates (8-10 per food/drink to be safe). Remember to **label watermelon-free options** clearly if the allergy is severe!

---

### **Appetizers/Crew Snacks**  
*(Easy to grab, sand-resistant, pirate-themed)*  
1. **"Mermaid Coins" Goldfish Crackers**  
   - 12 oz. box (cut into small pieces)  
2. **"Pearl Necklaces" Fruit Skewers**  
   - 2 lbs. pineapple chunks (allergy-safe), grapes, strawberries, mango (omit watermelon!)  
   - *Tip: Thread 2–3 pieces per skewer.*  
3. **"Buried Treasure" Mini Sandwiches**  
   - 1 lb. cheese sticks (or cubes), 1 lb. ham/cracker rounds (cut into triangles)  
   - *Pack in small treasure chests or bags labeled "Pirate Gold!"*  
4. Individual **"Cannonball" Cookies**  
   - Pre-baked chocolate chip cookies (cut into balls)  

---

### **Main Course: Pirate’s Platter**  
*(Sand-proof, handheld, minimal crumbs)*  
1. **"Ship’s Wheel" Mini Pizzas**  
   - 1 lb. pizza dough (cut into wheel shapes), tomato sauce, cheese, pepperoni  
   - *Assemble ahead; store sealed.*  
2. **"Treasure Chest" Chicken Nuggets**  
   - 1 lb. pre-cooked nuggets (from frozen or deli)  
3. **"Cannonball" Fries**  
   - 1 lb. frozen fries (air-fry or bake ahead; pack sealed)  
4. **"Jolly Roger" Veggie Cups**  
   - Baby carrots, cucumber slices, cheese cubes  
   - *Pack in small bags labeled "Pirate’s Snack!"*  

---

### **Sides & Snacks**  
*(Light, easy to eat, no utensils)*  
1. **"Mermaid’s Tears" Fruit Skewers**  
   - Blueberries, watermelon cubes (omit if allergy!), pineapple  
2. **"Crew Crisps"**  
   - Apple slices (soaked in lemon juice to prevent browning), goldfish crackers  
3. **"Cannonballs" Popcorn**  
   - 1 bag buttered popcorn (store in sealed bags)  
4. **"Chest of Cheese"**  
   - String cheese or mini cheddar cubes  

---

### **Drinks: Treasure Chest Beverages**  
*(Individual, spill-proof, allergy-safe)*  
1. **"Pirate Punch" Juice Boxes**  
   - 12 boxes (mix orange, apple, berry flavors; label "Watermelon-Free!")  
2. **"Cannon Blast" Water Bottles**  
   - 6 small reusable bottles (or disposable boxes)  
3. **"Saltwater Tonic" Lemonade Cups**  
   - Pre-made lemonade in sealed travel cups  
4. **"Crew Cupcakes" (Drinks)**  
   - Mini paper cups with juice/water  

---

### **Packaging & Extras**  
- **Decor:** Mini pirate hats, treasure maps, gold-painted cups  
- **Sand-Free Prep:** Use wax paper, parchment, or small containers for all items.  
- **Allergy Note:** Double-check labels for watermelon in juices/sauces.
