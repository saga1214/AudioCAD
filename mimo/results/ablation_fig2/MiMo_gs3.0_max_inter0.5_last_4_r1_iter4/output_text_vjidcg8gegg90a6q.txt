For winter chicks on concrete, safety and moisture control are key. You should use a thick layer of paper towels or newsprint, not loose bedding. This is rodent-resistant and easy to change.

To insulate the heat plate from the concrete floor, place the chicks in a deep tray filled with large flake pine shavings. Just change the bottom layer weekly like we discussed before. The sand could work too, but paper towels are safer for the tiny chicks.

This method provides insulation without attracting rodents and works perfectly with a safe, low-heat source like the brooder plate.
