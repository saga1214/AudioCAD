That's a smart pivot—combining desert adventure with a Vegas finish creates a perfect contrast! Here’s how to make your Vegas experience **memorable yet practical**, balancing van life with city luxury:

### 🌵 **From Desert to Vegas: Logistics**
- **Drive Time**: ~3 hours from Joshua Tree to Las Vegas. Break it up with stops (e.g., Mojave National Preserve or Amboy Crater for extra adventure).
- **Van Parking**: Vegas has **van-friendly hotels** with overnight parking:
  - **Joie by Caesars** (off-Strip, affordable): $100/night, includes parking.
  - **Fremont Street Experience**: Stays like **The El Cortel** (historic) or **Golden Nugget** offer off-Strip rates ($150-$250) with parking.
- **Shower & Refresh**: Book a hotel with amenities (e.g., **Luxor** or **Wynn** for luxury showers, or **Mirage** for mid-range). Many budget stays (like **Stratosphere**) also offer showers.

### 🎲 **Proper Vegas Experience (Beyond the Strip)**
Skip tourist traps—focus on **unique, intimate moments**:
1. **Desert-to-City Contrast**: 
   - **Morning**: Start with a **Sunrise at Valley of Fire** (30 min from Vegas), then drive back to the city.
   - **Evening**: **Dinner at Nobu** (gourmet) or **Peppermill Restaurant** (retro gaming vibe).

2. **Adventure-Adjacent Activities**:
   - **Helicopter Tour**: Fly over the Grand Canyon or Red Rock Canyon (book via **Vegas Helicopters**).
   - **Gaming Throwback**: Visit **Caesars Palace** (home of the World Series of Poker) or **The Mirage** for casino history.

3. **Hidden Gems**:
   - **Fremont Street Experience**: Free light shows, vintage shops, and low-cost eats (try **Golden Tater** fries).
   - **Neon Museum**: Iconic retro signs (book tickets in advance).
   - **Hoover Dam**: Short drive, stunning engineering feat—great for sunset.

4. **Romantic Touches**:
   - **Private Pool Cabana**: At **The Encore** or **Wynn** (splurge-worthy).
   - **Stargazing**: Head to **Red Rock Canyon State Park** near Vegas for night skies.
   - **Gaming Night**: Rent a gaming console and play classic arcade games in your room.

### 💡 **Budget Tips**
- **Food**: Skip fancy dinners—try **Food Court** (off-Strip) or **Buffaloes Wild Wings** for cheap eats.
- **Transport**: Use rideshares (Uber/Lyft) to avoid parking hassles.
- **Discounts**: Look for **"Stay 3 Nights, Get 1 Free"** deals or **Caesars Rewards** perks.

### 🚗 **Van Life Hack**
Park near **Fremont Street** or **The Strip** (e.g., **Joie by Caesars**) to keep your adventure gear handy. Use the van for picnics, spontaneous stops, or even as a "secret hideout" in your hotel room.

### Why This Works
You’re blending **adventure** (desert hikes, stargazing), **relaxation** (hot showers, pool time), and **romance** (unique Vegas moments). Nevada’s landscapes make Vegas feel like a surreal contrast—like stepping into a neon-lit dream after days under the stars.
