I hear you – a severe blockage with limited tools is tough, but you can absolutely tackle this. Let's break it down step-by-step to stay calm and effective. Remember to pause and breathe between steps.

**Step 1: Visual Inspection & Water Containment**
- **Check the drain:** Look down the drain – can you see debris (hair, grease, soap scum)? If so, that's your first clue.
- **Contain the water:** Place a bucket or large container under the drain to catch any water you dislodge. This prevents flooding and saves time.

**Step 2: Plunger Again (But Properly)**
- Ensure you create a *perfect seal* over the drain. Use a **push-pull plunger** (not just up/down) – apply steady pressure for 15-20 seconds. Sometimes it takes a few tries.

**Step 3: Remove Obvious Debris**
- Use your simple toolbox tools:
  - **Tweezers or needle-nose pliers:** Carefully pull out any visible hair or chunks from the drain opening. Don't push anything in yet – this might worsen the blockage.
  - **Hand auger (if in simple toolbox):** If it's included, gently twist it *into* the drain to hook debris. Pull it out slowly – don't force it.

**Step 4: Check for Loose Parts**
- If the blockage persists:
  - **Pliers/Wrench:** Gently check for loose fittings near the drain (pipe caps, P-traps). Sometimes debris gets trapped under them. Tighten if loose, but don't force it.
  - **Screwdriver:** Use a flathead to gently pry away any visible debris blocking the drain opening.

**Step 5: If Still Stuck: Disconnect & Clear**
- **For P-Traps:** If you have a sink/tank, disconnect the P-trap (the U-shaped pipe under the drain). It's usually held by slip nuts or clamps. Place a bucket under it to catch water. Clear debris from inside the trap, then reassemble securely.
- **For Drains:** If you can access the drain pipe itself (e.g., behind a wall), use your wrench to carefully loosen the fitting above the blockage. Clear debris from there.

**Critical Reminders to Calm Down:**
- **Don't Force It:** If a tool slips or you hear cracks, STOP. Forcing can break pipes.
- **Take Breaks:** Every 10-15 minutes, step away for 2 minutes. Stretch, breathe deeply, drink water.
- **Accept Limits:** If it's a deep, stubborn blockage beyond your toolbox, it's okay to call for backup later. You've calmed down and assessed it – that's progress!
