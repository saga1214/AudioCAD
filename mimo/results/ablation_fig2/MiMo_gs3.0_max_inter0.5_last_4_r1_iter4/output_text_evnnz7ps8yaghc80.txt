Here's a focused, stress-reducing weekly practice schedule designed to bridge your 2-to-3 object transition while building rhythm and consistency:

### **Weekly Practice Schedule**
*(Total: 1.5 hours/week)*  
**Morning Sessions (15 mins/day):**  
- **Monday–Friday:** *Bean Bags (Transition Focus)*  
  - **Minutes 1–5:** Juggle *just two bean bags with your dominant hand only* (lock in muscle memory).  
  - **Minutes 6–10:** Toss the **first of three balls first peak-to-second toss sequence** (as described earlier). Ignore catches.  
  - **Minutes 11–15:** Add catches. If you drop all balls, reset and repeat the sequence. *Goal: 1 clean cycle.*  

**Saturday:** *Scarf Practice*  
- **15 mins:** Juggle *two scarves slowly*. Focus on timing throws as music (play a slow-tempo song!).  
- **10 mins:** Introduce the third scarf. Hold two scarves in one hand + one in the other. Toss balls sequentially like the first method.  

**Sunday:** *Tennis Ball Experiment*  
- **15 mins:** Juggle two bean bags normally.  
- **10 mins:** Switch to tennis balls in *one hand*. Master two-ball juggling faster/firm catches.  
- **5 mins:** Add a third tennis ball from the other hand *very slowly*. Focus on soft-catching.  

---

### **Key Strategies to Reduce Stress**  
1. **The 15-Minute Rule:** Strictly time sessions. If you hit 15 mins without progress, STOP. Return tomorrow. This prevents overwhelm.  
2. **"Drop is Data" Mentality:** Track *what* you drop (e.g., "Left hand always misses catch"). Use this to refine next session.  
3. **Progress Anchors:** Aim for small wins daily. Celebrate when you:  
   - Catch ball #3 for the first time.  
   - Go 2 cycles without dropping all balls.  
   - Juggle scarves for 10 seconds straight.  
4. **Music Integration:** Practice bean bag 3 with a metronome (60 BPM) or kid-friendly song (e.g., "Baby Shark" – juggling rhythm = lyrics!).  

> ⚠️ **If stress spikes:**  
> - Switch to scarves immediately – their slow fall calms your brain.  
> - Do 5 minutes of non-juggling hand-eye drills: Toss a ball against a wall and catch it.  

### **Why This Works**  
- **Bean Bags:** Targets your transition weakness with structured micro-sessions.  
- **Scarves:** Builds rhythm and catching precision under low-stress conditions.  
- **Tennis Balls:** Sharpens hand speed and control.  
- **Rest Days:** Prevent burnout. You’ll actually learn faster with 2 days off.
