Okay, so you're asking about the top products to avoid forever chemicals. Forever chemicals, also known as PFAS, are a type of chemical that's really persistent. They're found in a bunch of everyday items. To help you out, I'll list five common products where you can find them.

First off, non-stick cookware. A lot of these products use PFAS to make the coating work, so you might want to switch to stainless steel, cast iron, or even ceramic cookware instead.

Another big one is grease-resistant food packaging. This includes stuff like fast-food wrappers, microwave popcorn bags, and paper food wraps. They often contain PFAS to keep food from sticking.

Third, think about products that are labeled as stain-resistant or waterproof. This could be anything from carpets and upholstery to outdoor clothing and gear. These treatments frequently contain PFAS.

Fourth is cosmetics and dental floss. Some skincare products and even certain types of dental floss might contain these chemicals, so it's a good idea to check the labels for "PFAS-free" claims.

Finally, since PFAS can end up in tap water, using a certified water filter can make a big difference in reducing your exposure.

And hey, just a couple of extra tips: bottled water can also contain microplastics and forever chemicals from the packaging itself, so a home water filter is a great solution. Also, some cleaning products might contain PFAS, so look for ones that are labeled as such. While PFAS can be found in other things like fish or paint, cookware and packaging are definitely the top sources to watch out for. Let me know if you need more details!
