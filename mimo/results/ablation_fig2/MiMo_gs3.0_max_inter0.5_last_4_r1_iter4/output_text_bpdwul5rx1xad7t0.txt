I've put together a list of five fantastic road trip destinations from Vancouver that are perfect for a first-time explorer with a new family on the way. Here are some ideas for you:

First, there's the classic drive to the Okanagan Valley. You can enjoy wine tasting at local wineries and stop at the beautiful Shuswap Lake for a swim. There are also great family-friendly hiking trails there.

Second, you could head to Victoria and the Cowichan Valley. You can take a scenic ferry ride to Salt Spring Island, where you can explore the charming little towns and beaches.

Third, there's the Fraser Canyon and Spatsizi Plateau. This area has some incredible mountain views and world-class hiking trails, perfect for when the weather is nice.

Fourth, you could drive to the Coast Mountains and Whistler. It's a beautiful drive, and Whistler has famous skiing in the winter and some of the best hiking in the summer.

And fifth, for a more coastal adventure, you can drive to the Gulf Islands. You can take a ferry to Vancouver Island and explore the unique towns and beaches along the way.

Each trip offers a different kind of adventure, from wine and beaches to mountains and islands. I hope you enjoy exploring British Columbia!
