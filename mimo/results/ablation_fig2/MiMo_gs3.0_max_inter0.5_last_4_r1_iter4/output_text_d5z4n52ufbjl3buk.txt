Okay, this is a great idea. Focusing on preparation will help you feel more confident.

Here are some pointers for your email. First, use a formal subject line like 'Course Name and Number, Exam Discussion Request'.

For the greeting, use 'Dear Professor' and his last name.

In the first paragraph, state your purpose clearly and politely. You could say something like, 'I'm writing to request a brief meeting to discuss areas where I could improve on my essay exam from last week.'

In the second paragraph, reinforce your desire to do better. For example, 'I'm committed to doing well in this course and would appreciate your insight on how my content could have been presented more effectively.'

Finally, ask for a specific time to meet. You could write, 'Would you be available during your office hours this week, or at another convenient time?'

This approach keeps it objective, student-focused, and low-risk for him to respond positively to.
