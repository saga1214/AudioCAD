1.  **Simple Grilled Fish & Veggies:** Grab some fresh fish fillets (like mahi-mahi or snapper) and pre-cooked veggies (asparagus, zucchini, cherry tomatoes). Lightly season, drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice, and grill or pan-sear. Serve with a simple lemon-herb butter sauce. *Relaxing vibe: Fresh, light, beachy.*

2.  **Classic Pasta with a Twist:** Pick a simple pasta (spaghetti, penne, fusilli). Make a quick garlic-parmesan sauce (olive oil, minced garlic, pasta water, grated parmesan). Toss in some pre-cooked shrimp or scallops, and maybe some spinach or peas if you have them. *Relaxing vibe: Comforting, familiar, easy.*

3.  **Hearty Beach Salad:** Use a big bowl as your plate! Base with mixed greens or romaine. Top with canned tuna or salmon (drained), chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, red onion, and some crumbled feta or goat cheese. Dress simply with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. *Relaxing vibe: Light, fresh, no cooking required (if using canned fish).*

4.  **Pan-Seared Shrimp & Sautéed Greens:** Sauté some shrimp in butter or olive oil with garlic until pink and curled. While that cooks, quickly sauté some spinach or kale in a separate pan with a little garlic and salt until wilted. Serve over instant rice or pasta. *Relaxing vibe: Quick, healthy, delicious.*

5.  **Interactive Taco/Burrito Bowl Bar:** Make a big batch of seasoned ground beef or chicken (or just use canned beans for speed). Serve with warm tortillas, shredded lettuce, diced avocado, salsa, shredded cheese, and maybe some sour cream or Greek yogurt. Let yourself build your own! *Relaxing vibe: Fun, customizable, feels like a mini feast.*
