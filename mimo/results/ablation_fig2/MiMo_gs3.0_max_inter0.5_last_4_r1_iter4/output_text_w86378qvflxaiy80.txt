That's a really good question. While all the benefits Norman Myers mentioned still apply, I think the most critical aspect today is the connection to human survival and the stability of our entire global ecosystem.

Basically, these hotspots are like the planet's immune system. By preserving them, we're preserving a huge amount of genetic diversity. That genetic diversity is the raw material we need for future medicine and agriculture. If we lose it, we could be facing health crises and food shortages that we can't even predict right now. It's like burning down a library before we've had a chance to study its contents.
