Here's your **segmented closing gear packing list**, optimized for comfort, warmth, and functionality during both daytime (20 miles) and nighttime (final 12 miles) pacing:

---

### **For the Daytime Segment (20 Miles, Night Starts)**
1. **Base Layer**:  
   - Moisture-wicking top (short-sleeve) and bottom (shorts or leggings).  
   *Avoid cotton—it retains sweat and chills you.*  

2. **Mid-Layer**:  
   - Light fleece or synthetic vest/jacket (removable for aid stations).  

3. **Outer Layer**:  
   - Windproof/water-resistant shell (e.g., light rain jacket or windbreaker). Hilly/mid-30s can feel windy.  

4. Aid Stations Help With:  
   - Gloves (bring extras!), hat, base layers.  
   - Focus on carrying **hydration** and **extra batteries** for your headlamp.  

---

### **For the Nighttime Segment (Final 12 Miles)**
1. **Base Layer**:  
   - *Same as daytime* (keep warm if you slow down).  

2. **Mid-Layer**:  
   - Fleece jacket or puffy vest (lightweight insulation).  

3. **Outer Layer**:  
   - **Windproof/water-resistant jacket** (now critical for warmth).  
   - **Headlamp/handheld light** with **extra batteries** (non-negotiable).  

4. **Essential Extras**:  
   - **Gloves** (hands get cold fast at night).  
   - **Warm hat** (beanie or balaclava if temps drop).  
   - **Lightweight pack** to carry layers if needed.  
   - **Extra socks** (change if feet get cold).  

---

### **Key Items for Both Segments**  
- **Trail shoes** with grip for hills/uneven ground.  
- **Headlamp** or handheld light (test batteries beforehand).  
- **Lightweight pack** to stash layers between aid stations.  
- **Buff/neck gaiter** (adjustable warmth for neck/hands).  

### **Why This Works**  
- **Daytime**: Layers allow adjustments at aid stations (remove mid-layer if sweaty).  
- **Nighttime**: Insulation + windproof outer layer combat cold + wind. Headlamp/batteries are race-critical.  
- **Pro Tip**: Pack a **lightweight puffy vest**—it’s easy to stash in your pack and adds core warmth instantly.
