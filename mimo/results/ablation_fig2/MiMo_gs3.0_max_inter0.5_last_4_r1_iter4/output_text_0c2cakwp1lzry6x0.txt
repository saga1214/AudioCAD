And of course, make sure the TV is plugged in and has good reception. For hanging planters, you'll need to drill a hole in the wall and use a special hook or a hanging basket. You might need a drill and some screws. Floating shelves are a bit more involved. You'll need to measure the space and cut the wood to size. Then you'll need to use the right kind of brackets to attach them to the wall, like L-brackets. Just make sure to check that the shelf is level before you put anything on it.
