First, about the left brain versus right brain. The idea that the brain is split into creative right and logical left hemispheres is largely a myth. While there are differences in how brain regions specialize, creativity and logic aren't neatly separated. Both sides of the brain work together constantly. However, people still use the left-right brain idea to describe different ways of thinking. It’s a simplified way to talk about how some people might approach problems differently, but it's not a strict rule.

Now, for your career. It sounds like you want something that's structured, creative, objective, and has a lasting impact. A few ideas could include architecture, engineering, or graphic design. Architecture and engineering are very structured and objective, but they also involve creativity in solving problems and building things. Graphic design is another option because it blends creativity with technical skills, and it can have a real impact on how people perceive a brand or message. You could also consider data visualization, which combines artistic skills with analytical thinking to present complex information in a clear, objective way.

It sounds like you like to build things and see tangible results, so any career that allows for that could be a good fit. Keep in mind that coffee helps, but it doesn't solve all the problems!
