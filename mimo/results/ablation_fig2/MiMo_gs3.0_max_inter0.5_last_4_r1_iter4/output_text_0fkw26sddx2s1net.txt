Of course. Here’s a detailed shopping list for your husband allergy-friendly Asian-inspired meal, excluding legumes and seafood. Let's start with the starter: Miso Cucumber Salad and regular Miso Soup. For those, you'll need Japanese Miso paste, cucumbers, rice vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, and sesame oil. Moving on to the main course: Chicken Teriyaki with Jasmine Rice and Stir-Fried Veggies. You'll need boneless, legump-free chicken thighs or breasts, short-grain white Jasmine rice, oil, soy sauce, and a head of broccoli and some bell peppers for the stir-fry. For the main teriyaki sauce, you'll need brown sugar, soy sauce, grated ginger, minced garlic, and a little mirin if you can find it. Finally, for the dessert, either Mango Sticky Rice or homemade Matcha Ice Cream. If you're making the ice cream at home, you'll need two cans of sweetened condensed milk, one cup of heavy cream, and two tablespoons of high-quality matcha powder. If you're skipping the ice cream, for the Mango Sticky Rice, you'll need sticky jasmine rice, fresh Thai or sweet mango, coconut milk, sugar, and salt. Oh, and don't forget the garnish for either dessert: some toasted sesame seeds and maybe some fresh mint leaves. Enjoy your romantic meal!
