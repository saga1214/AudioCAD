Calgary is a great base for outdoor adventures. Driving about 45 minutes east will take you to Banff. You can hike in the beautiful Johnston Canyon or take the Banff Gondola up Sulphur Mountain. A short drive north of the city, in Canmore, you can walk among the famous Icefields Parkway viewpoints. Or, for a more rugged experience, you can head about an hour west to the Kananaskis Valley. There are stunning hikes like the one to Kananaskis Falls and even a bit of a wild, remote vibe. If she's afraid of heights, you should avoid the gondola rides but could focus on the hiking trails.
