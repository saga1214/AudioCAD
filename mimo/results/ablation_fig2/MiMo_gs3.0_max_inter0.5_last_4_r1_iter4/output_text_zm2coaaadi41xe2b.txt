Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! For ramen, check out Ippudo, a classic spot for tonkotsu ramen. If you're into pho, Pho 79 is a solid choice. For udon, Tamafuku is great. And if you want something a bit more upscale, Kitcho is a Michelin-starred restaurant with incredible ramen. 

For protein bars, I'd recommend Quest Nutrition—high protein, low sugar. Also check out Optimum Nutrition or Ghost Protein Bars if you want something with a good mix of flavor and macros. 

Hope that helps!
