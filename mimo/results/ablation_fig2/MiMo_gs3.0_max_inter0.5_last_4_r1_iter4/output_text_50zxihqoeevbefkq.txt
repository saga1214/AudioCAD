### **Meat/Poultry**  
- 10–12 lb. smoked ham (for main course)  

### **Pork**  
- Ground pork (for mini sliders)  
- Bacon (for pigs in a blanket)  

### **Dairy & Eggs**  
- Cream cheese (for scalloped potatoes)  
- Milk or heavy cream (for potatoes)  
- Butter (for scalloped potatoes, flutes, and appetizers)  
- Eggs (for sliders)  
- Cheese (for pigs in a blanket/flutes)  

### **Baking**  
- Flour (for scalloped potatoes)  
- Milk (for scalloped potatoes)  
- Breadcrumbs (for sliders/pigs in a blanket)  

### **Pantry Staples**  
- Soy sauce (for sliders)  
- Mustard (for ham)  
- Olive oil (for carrots/asparagus)  
- Sugar (for carrot glaze)  
- Salt & black pepper  
- **Kid drinks:**  
  - Grape juice (4–5 bottles, in kid-friendly plastic flutes)  
  - **Adult drinks:**  
    - Champagne/Sparkling wine (8 bottles for toast)  
    - Signature cocktail ingredients (e.g., vodka, soda, garnishes)  
    - Sparkling water (for mixers)  

### **Kid-Friendly Extras**  
- Plastic champagne flutes (for grape juice)
