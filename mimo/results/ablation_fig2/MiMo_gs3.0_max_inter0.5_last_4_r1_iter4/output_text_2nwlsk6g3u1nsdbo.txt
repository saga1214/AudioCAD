Here's a quick recap of the three spicy main courses featuring shrimp you could prepare and take:  

### 🍤 1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb (450g) large shrimp, peeled & deveined  
- 8 oz (225g) linguine or spaghetti  
- 4 tbsp butter  
- 4 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1/2 cup dry white wine (optional)  
- 1/2 cup heavy cream  
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped  
- Red pepper flakes to taste (start with 1/2 tsp)  
- Salt and black pepper to taste  

**Method**: Sauté garlic and red pepper flakes in butter, add shrimp, cook until pink. Deglaze with wine (if using), then add cream and simmer 2–3 minutes. Toss with pasta and parsley.  

---  

### 🍝 2. **Spicy Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb (450g) large shrimp, peeled & deveined  
- 1 bell pepper, diced  
- 1 carrot, diced  
- 4 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes  
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar  
- 2 tbsp olive oil  
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley  
- 1–2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes  
- Salt and black pepper to taste  

**Method**: Sauté peppers, carrots, and garlic in olive oil. Add shrimp, tomatoes, vinegar, and red pepper flakes. Simmer 10–12 minutes until shrimp is cooked. Stir in parsley. Serve over pasta or rice.  

---  

### 🌾 3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb (450g) large shrimp, peeled & deveined  
- 1 cup quick-cooking grits  
- 2 cups chicken or vegetable broth  
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter  
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese  
- 1/2 cup sliced green onions  
- 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning (like Tony Chachere’s)  
- 1 tsp smoked paprika  
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro  

**Method**: Cook grits in broth until creamy. Stir in butter and Parmesan. In a skillet, sauté shrimp with Cajun seasoning until pink. Serve shrimp over grits, topped with green onions, paprika, and cilantro.
