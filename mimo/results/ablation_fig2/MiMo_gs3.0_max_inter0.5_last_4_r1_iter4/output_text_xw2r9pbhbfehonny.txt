Here’s a concise checklist for filming your puppet show yourself, tailored to your volunteer model and kid-focused audience:

### 🎥 **Pre-Recording Checklist**
1. **Get Explicit Permission**  
   - *Hospital/School/Facility:* Ask for written consent allowing you to film and use the footage (emphasize non-commercial use).  
   - *Nursing Homes:* Skip—older audiences are legally protected; avoid filming here unless explicitly invited.

2. **Gear Setup (Low-Cost & Simple)**  
   - **Camera:** Use your phone in landscape mode.  
   - **Tripod:** Essential for steady shots ($15 on Amazon).  
   - **Lighting:** Face a window or use a ring light to avoid shadows.  
   Melvin the Magnificent deserves spotlight! 🔦

3. **Audio Check**  
   - Use a lapel mic ($20) clipped to your shirt—puppets’ voices can be faint. Test volume before starting.

4. **Test Run**  
   - Film a 2-minute snippet first. Check framing (puppets fill the screen) and audio clarity.

### 🎬 **During the Show**
- **Engage Kids:** Ask them to film a segment with you! Builds connection and content.  
- **Keep it Short:** Aim for 5–8 minutes per clip. Kids’ attention spans are short.  
- **Bloopers?** Capture funny mistakes—they’re relatable!

### 📝 **Post-Show Essentials**
1. **Add Watermark**  
   - Overlay your puppet’s name (e.g., "Melvin the Magnificent") to deter unauthorized posting.  
2. **Upload Safely**  
   - **YouTube Kids:** Best for child safety (requires age-gating).  
   - **TikTok/Reels:** Use vertical clips for viral reach.  
   - **Always:** Enable "Private" or "Unlisted" until you approve public sharing.

### 🌟 **Future-Proofing**
- **Trademark Characters:** Register Melvin/Sloppy Snake trademarks if you expand.  
- **Consistency > Perfection:** Post weekly—kids return to familiar faces.  
- **Monetization?** Later, if needed, direct ad revenue to child charities (per FTC guidelines).

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Start with unboxing clips (e.g., "Melvin tries to make a sandwich!"). It’s low-pressure and builds character bonds.

You’ve got the heart—now capture it! Your shows bring joy where it’s needed most, and owning your footage ensures that joy stays *yours*. 🌈
