Okay, you've got all the essentials, but here are a few more things you might want to grab just in case.

Make sure you have a drill with some appropriately sized drill bits. Habitat cabinets usually need to be screwed into the studs, not hammered in, so a drill is much more efficient.

Also, get a stud finder that can tell you if you've found a stud. Some of them make a little beep or click when you hit a stud.

It's also a good idea to have some shims on hand. These are thin wooden pieces that you can use under the cabinets to make sure they're perfectly level if they land between studs.

And don't forget to have a trash bag handy for any old hardware or paint splatters from Habitat restores. You might find some that you don't want to use.

Most importantly, just take your time, be safe, and enjoy turning that empty kitchen into a fully functional space you saved a ton of money on. You've got this!
