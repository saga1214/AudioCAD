No problem! Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes for your vodka mix and Argentine-style Fernet, keeping the soda allergy and formality in mind. All use simple ingredients and avoid soda entirely.

### **1. Vodka Cocktail: Sparkling Tomato & Lime (Bloody Mary Lite)**
*(Elegant, savory, works for all diets)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1.5 oz vodka  
- 2 oz **tomato juice** (no added sugar)  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- 0.25 oz prepared horseradish (or 1 tsp grated)  
- 1 dash Worcestershire sauce  
- 1 dash hot sauce (optional)  
- **Sparkling water** (to top, *not soda*)  
- Garnish: Celery stalk, lemon twist  

**Method**:  
Shake vodka, tomato juice, lime juice, horseradish, Worcestershire, and hot sauce with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Top with **sparkling water**. Garnish.  

---

### **2. Fernet Cocktail: Argentine Fernet Sour**  
*(Bright, herbal, age-friendly)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 oz Fernet-Branca (or Argentine-style Fernet)  
- 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz honey syrup (*equal parts honey + warm water, mixed*)  
- 0.5 oz soda water (*not tonic—sparkling water only*)  
- Garnish: Lemon twist  

**Method**:  
Shake lemon juice, honey syrup, and Fernet with ice. Strain into a rocks glass over a single large ice cube. Top with **sparkling water**. Express lemon twist over drink.  

---

### **Key Notes**:  
- **Allergy**: Both cocktails use **sparkling water** (not soda). Tonic is avoided.  
- **Formality**: Use chilled glasses, proper garnishes, and clear ice.  
- **Food Pairing**: Tomato cocktail complements savory mains; Fernet sour balances rich desserts.  
- **Time-Saver**: Prep syrups/juices ahead. Store cocktails in the fridge for ≤2 hours.
