For Halloween candy, the crowd favorites are usually the classics. Think of things like chocolate bars like Reese’s, Snickers, Milky Way, Twix, or Hershey’s Kisses. Gummies like Haribo are always a hit, as are sour candies like Warheads or gummies. Candy corn, which is specifically for Halloween, is also popular. Mini chocolates, like M&M’s or Skittles, are great because they're easy to eat. If you want to be a little fancy, you could get some individually wrapped chocolates or even some themed cookies. Just try to buy from bulk bins or in large packs so you can get a variety without spending too much, and definitely check the allergy info again to make sure everything is dye-free. Most importantly, stock up on the stuff people will actually want to eat so you don’t end up with too much leftover.
