That sounds like a fantastic theme night! Here are 5 meal ideas and 5 dessert ideas tailored to your preferences—diabetic-friendly (shellfish-free for your fiyaesse), spicy, mango元素, and ocean-inspired where possible.

### 🍤 5 Ocean-Themed Meal Ideas  
1. **Spicy Mango Shrimp Stir-Fry**  
   *Shrimp sautéed with ginger, garlic, chili, and mango chunks. Serve over brown rice or cauliflower rice.*  
   *Why it fits:* Uses shrimp (if acceptable), mango, spicy, and diabetic-friendly.  

2. **Coconut Curry Shrimp with Mango Salsa**  
   *Creamy coconut curry base with shrimp, bell peppers, and a spicy mango-lime salsa on the side.*  
   *Why it fits:* Mango, spicy, shrimp, and rich in healthy fats (coconut).  

3. **Grilled Shrimp Tacos with Mango Salsa**  
   *Marinated shrimp grilled with avocado, cilantro, and a spicy mango-lime crema. Topped with mango salsa.*  
   *Why it fits:* Light, fresh, spicy, and mango-forward.  

4. **Shrimp and Mango Stir-Fry with Bell Peppers**  
   *Shrimp stir-fried with red peppers, carrots, and fresh mango. Tossed in a ginger-chili sauce.*  
   *Why it fits:* Quick, colorful, spicy, and mango-rich.  

5. **Spicy Shrimp and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Mango Garnish**  
   *Shrimp with broccoli, snap peas, and carrots in a spicy garlic sauce. Garnish with mango cubes and lime.*  
   *Why it fits:* Nutrient-packed, spicy, and mango accents tie it together.  

### 🍨 5 Sweet Ocean-Themed Desserts  
1. **Mango Panna Cotta with Berry Compote**  
   *Light coconut milk panna cotta topped with mango puree and a mix of blueberries/raspberries.*  
   *Why it fits:* Mango, creamy, and diabetic-friendly (no shellfish).  

2. **Dark Chocolate Sea Salt Truffles**  
   *Rich dark chocolate truffles with a hint of sea salt. Serve with a mango-lime dip for dipping.*  
   *Why it fits:* Indulgent but healthy fats, with a tropical twist.  

3. **Coconut Shrimp Cookies**  
   *Gluten-free coconut shrimp cookies (using shrimp paste as a binder). Pair with mango-lime yogurt.*  
   *Why it fits:* Playful, shrimp-themed, and sweet-spicy balance.  

4. **Mixed Berry Parfaits in a 'Beach Glass' Bowl**  
   *Layers of Greek yogurt, mango puree, and mixed berries in a clear glass bowl (resembles sea glass).*  
   *Why it fits:* Refreshing, visually thematic, and diabetic-friendly.  

5. **Mango Sorbet with Lime Zest**  
   *Pure mango sorbet with a drizzle of lime-infused honey. Garnish with edible flowers.*  
   *Why it fits:* Ultra-sweet, tropical, and refreshing.  

### 💡 Tips for Success  
- **Shrimp Note:** If shrimp is a concern, substitute with **tofu or chickpeas** in stir-fries/tacos.  
- **Spice Control:** Adjust chili levels based on your fiyaesse’s tolerance.  
- **Diabetic-Friendly Swaps:** Use low-glycemic carbs (cauliflower rice, quinoa) and natural sweeteners (honey, mango).  

Enjoy your ocean-themed feast—it’s a wonderful blend of food you both love! 🌴🍤✨
