Okay, let's break this down. Chronic skin redness can come from many things. Rosacea is a big one, sun damage over a lifetime adds up, and genetics can play a part. Beyond that, there are autoimmune issues like vasculitis. Vasculitis is basically when your own immune system gets confused and attacks your blood vessels by mistake. It can affect vessels of any size, and symptoms can range from fever and fatigue to rashes. It's definitely something a specialist can help diagnose.

As for other causes of redness, you have things like contact dermatitis from irritants, environmental factors like pollution, or even certain medications. It really depends on the specific situation.
