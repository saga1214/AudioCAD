How about a super quick morning workout if you’re ever short on time? Something you can do in 15 minutes or less. Start with a minute of jumping jacks or jogging in place. Then move on to a few sets of squats, push-ups, and lunges. Follow that with a quick core circuit of planks or crunches. Finish up with a little stretching. Each exercise is about 30 seconds to a minute, so it’s a really efficient full-body routine.

If you’d like to bake again, I can give you a simple recipe for a vanilla cake with white frosting. You’d need butter, sugar, eggs, flour, baking powder, milk, and vanilla. You’d cream the butter and sugar, add the eggs, and then mix in the dry ingredients along with milk and vanilla. Pour that batter into a pan and bake for about 30 minutes at 350 degrees. For the frosting, you’d just whip together some butter, powdered sugar, a little vanilla, and a splash of milk until it’s nice and fluffy. Let the cake cool before you frost it, and you’re ready to serve!
