Absolutely! Here’s a clean-eating shopping list optimized for grab-and-go cooler packing—covering 10 dads, 10 kids, drinks, snacks, meals, and dessert:

### 🥤 Healthy Drinks (Pack extra water bottles!)  
- **120 large water bottles** (16–20 oz)  
- **80 sugar-free sodas** (e.g., Coke Zero, Pepsi Zero; mix adult/kids sizes)  
- **80 low-sugar juice boxes** (e.g., Capri Sun or apple juice boxes)  
- **40 sports drinks** (e.g., Gatorade boxes for kids)  
- **10 electrolyte powder packets** (for cooler refills)  
- **Ice packs** (to keep drinks cold for 4–5 hours)  

### 🍎 Mess-Free Snacks  
- **80 cheese sticks** (mix mozzarella and cheddar)  
- **2 lbs grapes** (pre-washed, divided into 10 bags)  
- **10 single-serve hummus cups**  
- **10 bags trail mix** (seeds, nuts, dried fruit; check allergies)  
- **10 bags pretzels** (mini or snack-size)  
- **10 fruit leather pouches**  
- **10 granola bars** (e.g., KIND or Clif Bars)  
- **2 lbs veggie sticks** (carrots, cucumbers) with 10 small hummus cups  

### 🥪 Main Meals (Calzones Focus!)  
Pre-order **40 mini calzones** from a local pizzeria:  
- **20 cheese mini-calzones**  
- **15 pepperoni mini-calzones**  
- **5 veggie mini-calzones** (or extra cheese if needed)  
*Ask for individual boxes to minimize mess!*  
- **40 wraps/sandwiches**: Pre-cut into 10 groups of 4.  
  - *Adults*: Turkey/ham wraps with spinach, mustard, and cheese.  
  - *Kids*: Chicken tenders or ham wraps with mayo.  
- **10 single-serve ranch/dip cups** (for wraps/calzones)  

### 🍰 Dessert (Low-Mess Celebration!)  
- **1 pre-cut sheet cake** (e.g., chocolate or vanilla; 10 servings)  
- **OR 40 cupcakes** (individual boxes)  
- **OR 10 cookies** (pre-packaged, like Oreos)  
- **10 small bags of candy** (e.g., M&Ms or gummies for sharing)  

### 🧺 Bonus Coolers Essentials  
- **Wet paper towels** (10 rolls)  
- **Hand wipes** (10 packs)  
- **Small trash bags** (5 rolls)  
- **Reusable silicone bags** (for snacks/meals)  
- **Cooler dry packs** (to absorb moisture)  

### 💡 Pro Tips  
1. **Label coolers**: Assign one per friend group (e.g., "Dads Drinks," "Snacks," "Meals").  
2. **Prep ahead**: Assemble cheese sticks, cheese plates, and calzone boxes at home.  
3. **Dietary notes**: Ask grooms group about allergies (nuts, dairy, etc.).  
4. **Hydration station**: Add lemon wedges or fruit slices (e.g., oranges) to water bottles for flavor.  

This plan balances health, favorite foods (calzones!), minimal mess, and easy sharing. Have an incredible theme park adventure! 🎡🎉
