Absolutely. For a deep dive into the history, the best place to start is the Lockport Erie Canal Museum. It's located in a restored canal warehouse and has a great exhibit called "The Flight of Five," which really brings the engineering and history of those locks to life.

For a hands-on experience, you could take a boat tour of the locks. Companies like Lockport Boating Center offer these tours, and they're a great way to see the entire operation from the water. You can also walk the canal path, which is called the Lockport Canal Trail. It's a scenic path that runs along the canal, and it connects to the larger Erie Canal Heritage Trail.
