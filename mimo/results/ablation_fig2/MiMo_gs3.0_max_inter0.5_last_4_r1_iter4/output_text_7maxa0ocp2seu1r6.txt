### **Core Body Measurements (take for EVERY fitting):**
- **Bust** (armpit to underarm, fullest part)
- **Natural Waist** (highest point where waist cinches in)
- **Hips** (widest part, typically 8–10 inches below waist)

### **Basic Measurements (item-dependent):**
- **Inseam** (inner leg from crotch to ankle)
- **Sleeve Length** (shoulder to wrist)
- **Shoulder Width** (across upper back from shoulder seam to shoulder seam)

---

### **Advanced Measurements (for *highly* fitted garments):**
- **Thigh** (top to bottom of thigh, above knee)
- **Bicep** (shoulder to elbow)
Neck (for collars/shirts)
- **Back Rise** (crotch to waistband *back*)
- **Front Rise** (crotch to waistband *front*)

---
