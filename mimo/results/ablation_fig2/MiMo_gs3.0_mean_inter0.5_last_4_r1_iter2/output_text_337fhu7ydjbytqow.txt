Here's a **separate closing gear packing list** for each segment, optimized for mid-30s temps, hilly terrain, three aid stations, and pacing effort:

### **Daytime 20-Mile Segment**  
1. **Base Layer**:  
   - Moisture-wicking long-sleeve shirt (lightweight, breathable)  
   - Lightweight leggings or shorts (quick-dry fabric)  
2. **Mid-Layer**:  
   - Fleece jacket or light puffer vest (for hilly temperature drops)  
3. **Outer Layer**:  
   - Wind-resistant/water-repellent jacket (e.g., rain shell or light windbreaker)  
4. **Head/Hands**:  
   - Gloves (lightweight, for grip on wet trails)  
   - Lightweight beanie or cap (if sun is present)  
5. **Extras**:  
   - Headlamp/handheld light (even in midday, trail races can be overcast)  
   - Extra pair of socks (quick-dry, avoid cotton)  
   - Pack or vest (to carry layers if needed)  

### **Night Pacing Final 12 Miles**  
1. **Base Layer**:  
   - Moisture-wicking long-sleeve shirt + leggings (thermal if very cold)  
2. **Mid-Layer**:  
   - Fleece jacket or lightweight puffy (essential for warmth)  
3. **Outer Layer**:  
   packable **windproof/waterproof jacket** (e.g., down or synthetic puffy)  
4. **Head/Hands**:  
   - Gloves (warm, windproof)  
   - Warm hat (beanie or balaclava)  
5. **Extras**:  
   - **Bright reflective vest** (for visibility at night)  
   - Headlamp with **extra batteries** (critical!)  
   - Extra pair of **thermal socks** (wool or synthetic blend)  
   - Pack/vest (to carry layers + aid items between stations)  

### **Key Adjustments for Night Pacing**  
- **Layer System**: Night requires **insulation + wind/water protection**. Prioritize packable layers to add/remove as needed.  
- **Visibility**: Reflective gear and lights are non-negotiable.  
- **Extremities**: Gloves/hat prevent frostbite; thermal socks keep feet warm if wet.  
- **Battery Backup**: Night headlamps often drain fast—always bring spares.  

### **Don’t Forget**  
- **Weather Check**: Mid-30s can mean 15-20°F drops after sunset. If rain is possible, bring a light rain shell.  
- **Aid Station Prep**: Carry a small bag for layers/socks to swap out during runs.  

This setup keeps you adaptable, warm, and safe. Pace strong! 🏃‍♀️💨
