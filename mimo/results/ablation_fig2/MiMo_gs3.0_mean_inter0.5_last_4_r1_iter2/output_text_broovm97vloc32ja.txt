This is a fantastic question. It's actually a huge advantage. Many children's book authors are also illustrators, and publishers are definitely looking for that talent.

Here's how you would handle it. You would approach it just like a standard manuscript submission, but you would also need to submit your artwork. You would include a few sample illustrations to show your style, and you would clearly state that you are the illustrator for your own manuscript. 

You should also be prepared to do a very small amount of research on each agent to see if they specifically accept illustrated manuscripts. The best way to learn is to read some children's books that have a similar style to your work and look at the copyright page to see who the publisher and illustrator are.
