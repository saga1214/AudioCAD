That line of thought about genetic identity doesn't really explain how the first egg was produced.

Here's the problem: The protein required for chicken eggshells is specific to hens. So, the egg had to come from a hen or a direct ancestor that was capable of producing it. The first true chicken didn't just 'contain' its identity in an egg; it *was created* when it hatched from an egg laid by a non-chicken. So, the chicken hatched first, making it the answer.
