Okay, kosher dietary laws mean no mixing of meat and dairy. Since you're serving weenies (which are pork), you need to separate that from the sweet items. You can still bring the hummus fruit platter as healthy snacks. For sweets, dark chocolate fits theme and is dairy-free, or themed spooky cookies without dairy. Tony can eat the hummus, fruit, and chocolate. Pinot Noir is also fine as it's red wine. Just keep the meat and dairy separate on the table.
