Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items while respecting your landlord's rules (no late noise/persistent drilling):

### 1. TV Mounting  
**Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, drill, anchors (if needed), wall anchors kit, screwdriver  
**Steps:**  
- **Find studs:** Use a stud finder to locate wall studs (wood beams behind drywall). Mark them with a pencil.  
- **Check weight capacity:** Ensure the mount supports your TV’s weight (e.g., 55" TVs need 50–70 lbs capacity).  
- **Mount the bracket:** Attach the bracket to the back of your TV first. Hold it against the wall, align with studs, and use anchors if needed (temporarily).  
- **Secure to wall:** Use screws to attach the bracket to the wall. Tighten gently—don’t overtighten.  
- **Test stability:** Hold the TV against the bracket; it should feel secure.  
**Drill-Free Tip:** Use **adhesive wall mounts** (like Command Strips) for lightweight TVs under 20 lbs.  

### 2. Hanging Planters  
**Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, drill, wall anchors, hooks  
**Steps:**  
- **Choose secure hooks:** Use heavy-duty hooks rated for your planter’s weight.  
- **Find studs:** Planter + soil + water can weigh 10–30 lbs. Always anchor to a stud.  
- **Drill pilot holes:** Use a hole saw bit slightly smaller than the hook’s base. Drill straight into the stud.  
- **Install anchors:** If not drilling into a stud, use toggle bolts or heavy-duty anchors.  
- **Hang the planter:** Attach hooks to the planter hanger.  
**Drill-Free Tip:** Use **removable adhesive hooks** (like Command Hooks) for light planters. Test weight limits first.  

### 3. Floating Shelves  
**Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, drill, anchors, screws  
**Steps:**  
- **Plan layout:** Sketch where shelves go. Use painter’s tape to mark placement.  
- **Find studs:** Floating shelves need 50–100 lbs capacity per shelf. Anchor to studs.  
- **Install brackets:**  
  - **Top bracket:** Drill holes into the wall at the top edge of the shelf.  
  - **Bottom bracket:** Drill holes into the wall at the bottom edge.  
  - **Mount shelf:** Slide the shelf onto the brackets.  
- **Test load:** Place decor on the shelf—should feel solid.  
**Drill-Free Tip:** Use **expanding plastic anchors** if no studs are available (supports 25–50 lbs per hole).  

### Safety & Efficiency Tips:  
- **Short bursts:** Use a power drill in 1–2 minute intervals to minimize noise.  
- **Quiet tools:** Opt for cordless drills (quieter than corded).  
- **Protect floors:** Lay down cardboard or old towels to catch dust.  
- **Schedule installations:** Do one project per day (e.g., TV on Saturday AM, planters on Saturday PM).  

### Decorating Sundays:  
- Dedicate 2–3 hours max per Sunday to installations to avoid fatigue.  
- Play upbeat music to stay energized!  
- Reward yourself after each project (e.g., coffee break, walk outside).  

By planning ahead and using efficient methods, you’ll transform your space without disrupting your work or breaking lease terms. 🛠️✨
