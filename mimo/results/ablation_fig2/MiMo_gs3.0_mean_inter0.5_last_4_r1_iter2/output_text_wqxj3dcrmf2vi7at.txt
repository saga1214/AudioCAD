Okay, corgi princess gets her own homemade and store-bought menu! Let's break it down again for Princess.

**Homemade Homemade Heaven (Focus on Fresh & Frozen):**

1.  **Pumpkin Power Bites:**
    *   **How:** Mash canned pumpkin puree (NOT pie filling!) until smooth. Mix with a tiny bit of whole wheat flour or oat flour to help them hold shape. Spoon small amounts onto a baking sheet, flatten slightly, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-15 minutes until dry to the touch, OR Press them into a frozen muffin tin, pack flat, and freeze solid.
    *   **Why Corgis Love Them:** Sweet potato/pumpkin is naturally sweet, easy to digest, and freezing makes them fun like ice cream!
    *   **Portions:** Tiny!

2.  **Oat PB Cookies (Xylol-Free!):**
    *   **How:** Mix 1 egg (optional, but helps bind), 1 cup rolled oats, 2-3 tbsp peanut butter (**CONFIRM NO XYLTOL IS CRITICAL!**), and a splash of milk (or water) if needed until it forms a dough. Roll into small balls, flatten slightly, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 minutes until edges are dry. Cool completely.
    *   **Why Corgis Love Them:** Peanut butter is pure corgi bliss! Oats add fiber.
    *   **Portions:** Break into *very* small pieces before giving.

3.  **Cooked Protein Pieces:**
    *   **How:** Offer small slices of plain, steamed or boiled chicken breast, turkey, or even carrot sticks. Carrot sticks are simple – just wash, peel, and cut into thin matchsticks.
    *   **Why Corgis Love Them:** Chicken is flavorful protein. Carrots are crunchy and mildly sweet.
    *   **Portions:** Carrot stick: a few small pieces. Chicken/Turkey: a tiny sliver.

**Awesome Store-Bought Options (Check Labels!):**

*   **Blue Buffalo:** Look for their Small Training Bites or Life Protection Bits (check kibble size too).
*   **Wellness:** Their Training Treats are generally wholesome.
*   **Zuke's:** Mini Nibbles are a classic tiny-bite favorite.
*   **Greenies:** Their Mini Greenies are dental treats often loved by pups (just check size).
*   **Merrick:** Their Handfuls Training Treats are popular.
*   **Kong Stik:** Dipped in peanut butter or yogurt and frozen makes an amazing chew/stick treat.

**Golden Rules for Any Treat:**

1.  **NO XYLTOL Peanut Butter:** Ever. This is toxic.
2.  **Portion Control is Queen:** "Little Corgi Princess" portions mean *tiny* amounts. Count treats against her daily kibble.
3.  **Natural & Wholesome:** Avoid deep-fried items, excessive sugar, or artificial junk.
4.  **Watch for Allergies:** Introduce new treats one at a time.
5.  **Water! Water! Water!:** Especially important with dried treats or higher protein treats. Always have fresh water available.

Teach her "wait for treat" before she gets the reward – it makes giving treats even more fun and reinforces good manners! Princess is going to be the happiest, most reward-filled corgi! Enjoy bonding with your delightful little princess!
