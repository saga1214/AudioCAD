Here's a nautical-themed shopping list designed for easy, sand-resistant beach eating for 8 kids (your son + 7 guests), including allergy-safe options and a simple "course" structure:

**Nautical Pirate Party Shopping List (Beach Meeting)**

**Theme Integration:** Look for pre-made pirate ship cookies or cookie cutters for sandwiches/rolls. Use cookie cutters on fruit/veggies. Label food containers with simple pirate names like "Captain's Snacks," "Mermaid Fruit," "Ship's Rations," or "Treasure Chests."

**Allergy Note:** **Excludes Watermelon.** Double-check labels on packaged items like juice boxes and crackers for any other known allergies.

**Course 1: "Buried Treasure" Snacks**
*   **Fruit Skewers ("Jewels"):** 1 lb Grapes (no watermelon!), 2 lbs Seedless Melon cubes (cantaloupe or honeydew), 2 lbs Pineapple chunks, 2 lbs Strawberries or Blueberries. *Pre-slice and pack in airtight containers.*
*   **Cheese "Gold" Cubes:** 1 lb Cheddar or Colby Jack cheese, pre-cubed. *Pack in a small container.*
*   Treasure Chest Snack Bags: Fill small zip-top bags (approx. 1.5-2 cups each) with a mix of:
    *   Goldfish Crackers (as "gold")
    *   Pretzel Sticks or Minis ("ship masts")
    *   Baby Carrots ("pebbles")
    Mini Marshmallows ("clouds" or "seaweed")
    A few raisins ("dots")
    *   *Label bag with pirate name: "Captain's Treasure Chest!"*

**Course 2: Main Course Options (Choose 1-2)**
*   **Mini Pirate Ship Hot Dogs:** 1 lb Hot Dogs, 1 (12oz) package Hot Dog Buns. *Cut hot dogs in half lengthwise before cooking. Wrap each hot dog half in a small square of buttered white bread (like a "ship sail") before wrapping in foil. Serve on a platter labeled "Ship's Sustenance."*
*   **Mini Veggie & Cheese "Boats":** 1 pint Cherry Tomatoes, 1 pint Mini Bell Peppers (halved), 1 lb Baby Carrots, 8 oz Cheese Sticks or cubes, 1 lb Cream Cheese (optional). *Prep veggies and cheese. Serve in small bowls or "boats" (mini containers) labeled "Mermaid's Bounty."*
*   *(Optional Sides):* Small bowls of ketchup, mustard, ranch dip, or cheese sauce in labeled containers.

**Course 3: Easy Eats & Sips**
*   **Mini Pretzel Rods:** 1 box Pretzel Rods ("Ship Masts") - 24 oz.
*   **Cheese Cubes:** 1 lb (as above).
*   Individual Juice Boxes ("Pirate Punch"): 8 (12oz) boxes 100% Apple Juice, Grape Juice, or Orange Juice. *Label with "Pirate Punch!"*
*   Individual Water Bottles ("Fresh Tides"): 8 small (10-12oz) water bottles. *Label with "Fresh Tides."*
*   Small Bowls: Pre-sliced Apple slices (soak in lemon water if desired), Banana slices (pack separately to prevent browning).

**Course 4: "Treasure Cupcake" Treats (Beyond Cake)**
*   **Treasure Cupcake Toppers:** 8 Jumbo Marshmallows (as "ship sails"), 8 Small Chocolate Chips (as "gold"), 8 Small Green Spruce Trees (from dollar store). *Apply to plain cupcakes before frosting.*
*   **Pirate Ship Cookies:** 1 lb Pirate Ship themed cookies (check online retailers). *Or make your own using cookie cutters!*
*   **Simple Cupcakes:** 8 plain cupcakes (vanilla, chocolate, or lemon) for the toppers to go on.

**Theme Packaging & Extras:**
*   **Cookie Cutters:** Pirate Flag, Ship, Anchor, Treasure Chest.
*   **Labels:** Printer or markers for labeling containers/bags ("Captain's Snack," "Mermaid Fruit," "Pirate Punch," "Treasure Chest," "Ship's Sustenance," etc.).
*   **Decor:** Small plastic pirate hats or eye patches (optional).
*   **Utensils:** Biodegradable plates, cups, and napkins (blue or gold for nautical theme). Small plastic forks/spoons.
*   **Serving:** Large platters, bowls, and airtight containers for prepped items. Chafing dishes or slow cookers kept warm for hot dogs if needed.

**Key Sand-Resistant Principles Used:**
1.  **Sealed Containers:** Airtight containers for fruit, cheese, snack mixes.
2.  **Skewers:** Fruit held on sticks.
3.  Treasure Chest Snack Bags: Sealed and easy to pick up.
4.  Individual Juice Boxes & Bottles: Prevent spills.
5.  Pre-Cut & Packaged Items: Hot dogs cut & buttered before wrapping, cookies pre-baked.
6.  Minimal Mess Foods: Goldfish, pretzels, cheese cubes, plain cupcakes.
7.  Easy-to-Handle Shapes: Mini sliders, mini hot dogs, small portions.

This list provides variety, accommodates the group size and setting, minimizes sand worries, and keeps the pirate nautical theme strong throughout the party! Enjoy the celebration!
