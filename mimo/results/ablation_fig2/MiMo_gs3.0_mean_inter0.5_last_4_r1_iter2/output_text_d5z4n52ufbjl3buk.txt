That's a great goal—being respectful while still getting your point across. You can definitely achieve that.

When you write, start with a polite opening like, "Dear Professor [Last Name], I scored a 64 on the recent essay exam and wanted to schedule some time to learn how I could improve for the next test."

Then, you could add, "Subjective exams can be open to interpretation, but I'd appreciate any specific feedback you might have about content weaknesses."

Ending with something like, "Thank you for your time and guidance," keeps it professional and non-confrontational.
