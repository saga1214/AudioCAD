For Thanksgiving, a classic and relatively easy choice would be a pumpkin pie. It requires no baking and only uses an oven to warm the cream.

For a side dish that's high in protein, you could try honey garlic chicken wings. You just toss the wings in a baking dish, drizzle with a simple honey garlic sauce, and bake them.

And for a simple dessert, you could bring a no-bake cheesecake in a tart pan. It's made with just cream cheese, sugar, and fruit filling, so again, no oven is needed.
