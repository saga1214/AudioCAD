Okay, let's put it all together. Here’s a clear, traditional format that’s scan-friendly.

First, at the very top, just have your name in about a fourteen-point font. Right directly underneath it, on a new line for each piece of contact info, list your email, then your phone number, followed by your city. Using nine-point icons isn't standard, so I'd drop those for consistency. This creates a clean header.

Next, you have your short professional summary, which explains your career shift.

After that, you'll have a section for your Relevant Experience. Here you'll list your key roles, including the company, your title, and the specific dates you worked there.

Then comes your Additional Work History, where you briefly list other relevant jobs.

After that is your Education section, where you list your degrees and any awards you received.

Finally, you'll have a Skills section, where you can list your technical and soft skills tailored to the job description.

This structure neatly organizes your career transition and presents all your information in an easy-to-read way.
