### Studio & Editing Workflow
1. **Mic Switch to Audition**:  
   Yes, Adobe Audition is worth the transition. Its faster processing, spectral frequency display, and AI-powered tools (like Content-Aware Fill for noise removal) will save significant time long-term. Start with Audacity for basic tasks, then migrate sessions gradually.

2. **Noise Removal Plugins**:  
   - **Mouth Clicks**: Use iZotope RX 10’s "De-click" module. Set the "Frequency Range" to "Low" and adjust sensitivity.  
   - **Background Noise**: Run the "Denoise" tool after capturing a clean noise profile. For AI automation, try **Spleeter** (free) for separating voice from background noise.

3. **Demo Reel Tips**:  
   Focus on **one standout character** in 30 seconds. Use dynamic contrast (soft vs. intense delivery) and a unique sound effect (e.g., a door slam or laugh) to hook listeners. Keep it under 60 seconds.

### Vocal Health & Organization
- **Vocal Fatigue Prevention**:  
  Hydrate with **room-temperature water** (cold constricts cords; warm causes irritation). Warm up for 10 mins before recording. Take 5-minute breaks hourly—sip water, hum, or do light stretches.  
- **Organization System**:  
  Use cloud folders (Google Drive/ Dropbox) with client names. Label files as `YYYY-MM-DD_ProjectName_TakeX` (e.g., `2023-10-05_LogoSpot_Take2`). Track deadlines in a simple spreadsheet.
