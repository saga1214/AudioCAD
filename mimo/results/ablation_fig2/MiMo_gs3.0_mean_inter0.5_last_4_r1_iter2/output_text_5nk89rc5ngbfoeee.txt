For your end-of-year celebration, a hybrid approach balances fun, inclusivity, and practicality—especially while pregnant. Here’s a tailored plan:

### **Top Recommendation: Park BBQ + Sundae Bar Combo**
- **Why it works**: Combines outdoor fun (BBQ) with a sweet dessert (sundae bar) to cater to diverse preferences.  
- **Logistics**:  
  - **Location**: Choose a covered park pavilion (weather backup) with picnic tables.  
  - **Snacks**:  
    - **BBQ**: Mini hot dogs, veggie dogs, rice balls, and fruit skewers (dairy-free dips for chips/dip).  
    - **Sundae Bar**: Parent volunteers bring ice cream tubs (check preferences—some girls may bring their own toppings). You provide:  
      - Dairy-free whipped cream (coconut or almond-based).  
      - Toppings: sprinkles, crushed Oreos (dairy-free if needed), rainbow sprinkles, chocolate chips.  
    - **Budget Tip**: Parents split costs for BBQ supplies ($20-$30 total) and ice cream ($15-$20). You cover sashes/patches.  
  - **Activities**:  
    - **Short & Fun**: Set up a "badge showcase" where girls display earned patches.  
    - **Team Games**: Quick relay races (e.g., sack races, three-legged) using school supplies (no extra cost).  
- **Pregnancy Perks**: Minimal setup/cleanup—parents handle food and games.  

### **Rainy Day Backup: Cafeteria Party**
- **If weather fails**: Host in the cafeteria with a "badge awards" ceremony.  
  - **Activities**:  
    - Girls present patches on sashes (parents/you take photos).  
    - Group craft: Decorate sashes with fabric markers (supplies from dues).  
  - **Snacks**: Chips/dip (dairy-free), mini pizzas, and a sundae bar (parents bring ice cream).  
  - **Cost Control**: Use cafeteria tables/chairs. Dues cover craft supplies ($5-$10 per girl).  

### **Dues & Cost Breakdown**  
- **Sashes**: Parents purchase ($15-$25/girl). Send a clear email upfront.  
- **Dues**: $10-$20 per girl (covers sundae toppings, race supplies, or a small badge kit).  
- **Parent Roles**: Assign a "snack coordinator" (rotating) and "activity helper" (for games/cleanup).  

### **Key Tips**  
1. **Start Small**: Focus on 3-4 badges this year (e.g., Community Helper, Outdoor Adventure).  
2. **Pregnancy Prep**: Delegate setup/cleanup to parents. Bring a folding chair and snacks for yourself!  
3. **Emphasize Inclusivity**:  
   - Dairy-free dip station at BBQ/party.  
   - "No-cost" badge activities (e.g., storytelling, nature journaling).  

**Bottom Line**: A park BBQ with a sundae bar strikes the perfect balance—celebratory, inclusive, and manageable. With clear parent communication and a minimal budget, you’ll create a memorable year for your daughters! 🎉
