Of course. For miso soup or cucumber salad: if he prefers the soup, you'll need miso paste, tofu without legumes of course like silken or firm, wakame seaweed, and green onions. For the cucumber salad, you'll need cucumbers, sesame oil, rice vinegar, soy sauce without any legumes in it, and some sesame seeds. For chicken teriyaki main, you'll need chicken breasts, soy sauce, mirin, sugar, garlic, ginger, jasmine rice, broccoli or bell peppers for the veggies, and sesame oil and chili paste if you want a little kick. For mango sticky rice, you'll need sticky rice, matcha powder, mangoes, coconut milk, and sugar. As a couple of extras, you could have steamed bao buns or some plain rice cakes if you want snacks. Hope your husband enjoys the meal.
