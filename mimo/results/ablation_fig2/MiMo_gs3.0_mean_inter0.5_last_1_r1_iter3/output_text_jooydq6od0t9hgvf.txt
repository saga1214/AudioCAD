Got it—quick vodka mixer and Argentine-inspired Fernet recipes, both soda-free and formal-friendly. Here are simple, elegant options:

### 1. **Vodka Mixer (Soda-Free):**  
**Name:** Sparkling Citrus Cosmo  
**Recipe:**  
- 1.5 oz vodka  
- 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz fresh cranberry juice (not cocktail)  
- 0.5 oz pomegranate juice  
- 3 oz premium soda water (like Topo Chico)  
**Garnish:** Lemon twist + mint sprig.  
**Why it works:** Bright, refreshing, and visually appealing. Use fresh juices for best flavor.  

---

### 2. **Argentine-Inspired Fernet:**  
**Name:** *Fernet-Malbec* (a local twist)  
**Recipe:**  
- 1.5 oz Malbec (or Pisco if Malbec is unavailable)  
- 0.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- 0.25 oz honey syrup (1:1 honey/water)  
**Garnish:** Fresh mint sprig + lime wheel.  
**Prep:** Shake with ice, strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
**Why it works:** Malbec’s sweetness balances Fernet’s herbal notes. Light and aromatic—perfect for conversation.  

---

### Quick Tips:  
- **Label drinks clearly** (e.g., "Argentine Fernet" vs. "Vodka Cosmo") so guests know their options.  
- **Offer non-alcoholic pairings:** Sparkling water with cucumber-mint, or a fruit-forward mocktail (e.g., ginger-mint lemonade).  
- **Balance intensity:** Fernet is strong—offer it as a *short* aperitif, not a chaser.
