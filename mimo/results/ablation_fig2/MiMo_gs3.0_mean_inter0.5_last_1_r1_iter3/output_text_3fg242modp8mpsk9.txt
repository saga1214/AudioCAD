You could look for boat tours that have a special culinary element.

For instance, some places offer sunset sails that include a multi-course dinner on board, often with local seafood.

Another idea is to find a charter for a smaller boat, like a catamaran, which can sometimes include a private chef to prepare fresh catch right there on the trip. 

Just be mindful like your real-life partner, maybe start with a very short ferry ride just to condition them, or focus on destinations known for land activities too, like whale watching from shore.
