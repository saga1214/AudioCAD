Absolutely! Planning an unforgettable Costa Rica scuba diving trip involves balancing your scuba interests with the country's incredible diversity of sites. Here’s a tailored plan based on your dream trip and past experiences:

### **1. Fin Choice Finalized: Short Fins for Beginners**
- **Why short fins?**  
  You mentioned struggling with long fins as a beginner in Honduras, and short fins are compact, easier to travel with, and less tiring. For calm Costa Rican reefs or close-range exploration, they’ll let you focus on technique and maneuverability.  
- **Upgrade Option:** Rent intermediate fins (like *Aqualung Alpinist*) at dive shops. They offer a middle ground—more propulsion than short fins without demanding strength.

### **2. Costa Rica’s Dive Hotspots**
Costa Rica’s tropical waters (26–30°C / 78–86°F) are ideal for diving year-round. Prioritize these sites:

- **Cenotes (Holboxes):**  
  **Tulum, Mexico (near Costa Rica)**  
  Freshwater cenotes like **Cenote Dos Ojos** or **Cenote Suytun** offer crystal-clear water, unique freshwater sharks (whale, nurse), and caves. *Similar to Honduras but easier to navigate with short fins.*

- **Pacific Coast:**  
  **Santa Rosa Island (Puntarenas):**  
  - **Pelagics:** Mantas, eagle rays, and hammerheads (best May–Nov).  
  - **Coral Reefs:** Healthy hard/soft corals with schools of tropical fish.  
  - **Wrecks:** *El Sombrerico* (submarine wreck) and WWII shipwrecks. *Ideal for your shipwreck love from Belize!*

- **Caribbean Coast:**  
  **Tenerife (Puntarenas):**  
  - **Manta Ray Hotspot:** World-famous manta cleaning stations (year-round).  
  - **Mangroves & Caves:** Explore underwater mangrove forests and lava tube caves.  
  - **Strong Currents?** Test short fins here first—they’re agile in tight spaces.

### **3. Timeline Suggestion**
- **7–10 Days:**  
  - **Days 1–2:** Arrive in San José, fly to Puntarenas (1.5-hour flight).  
  - **Days 3–5:** Santa Rosa Island (30-min boat ride from Puntarenas).  
  - **Days 6–7:** Tenerife (45-min boat ride from Puntarenas).  
  - **Day 8:** Cenotes (fly to Tulum, Mexico for a day trip).  
  - **Day 9:** Relax, then fly home.

### **4. Logistics & Tips**
- **Dive Shops:**  
  Book through **Dive Santa Rosa** or **Tenerife Dive Center** for certified guides and gear rentals (short fins available).  
- **Currents:** Santa Rosa/Tenerife can have currents—start with short fins but rent intermediate fins if you want more speed.  
- **Wildlife:**  
  - **Turtles:** Common in all sites.  
  - **Whale Sharks:** Santa Rosa (Jan–Mar) or Tenerife (year-round).  
  - **Caves:** Only attempt with a certified guide (e.g., cenotes or lava tubes).  
- **Travel Ease:** Short fins mean less gear to pack—ideal for Costa Rica’s varied landscapes.

### **5. Beyond Scuba: Pair with Nature**
- **Day Trips:** Zip-lining through Costa Rica’s rainforest (e.g., Monteverde).  
- **Land Wildlife:** Visit Manuel Antonio National Park or Tortuguero (sea turtles).  

### **Final Advice**
Costa Rica’s tropical waters rival Honduras/Belize for biodiversity, but with more varied terrain (coral reefs, wrecks, cenotes). Short fins will let you dive comfortably while exploring these sites.
