It's easy to get mixed up. Let me clarify. The most widely recognized original ratio is one and a quarter ounces whiskey to one ounce vermouth and one ounce Campari. However, making it with equal parts of all three is also historic and very popular. So both recipes are correct.

For the Last Word you asked about, each ingredient is three-quarters of an ounce. You'll need gin, one ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of Luxardo Maraschino Liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. Stir that one over new ice until frosty, then strain into a glass garnished with an orange twist. And please, remember to hydrate well at your party.
