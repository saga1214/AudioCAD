### 💡 Critical Tips
1. **Panic Prevention**: Set a 5-minute timer. If stressed, STOP and stretch/focus on breathing. Stress = dropped balls.
2. **Catch Focus**: Don’t worry about juggling cleanly at first. Your goal is to *catch* the third ball consistently.
3. **Progress Tracking**: Use a sticky note. Write "Today I caught X clean throws." Celebrate small wins.
4. **Music Hack**: Use a metronome app (e.g., "Metronome Beats") for steady beats—ideal for scarves/tennis ball days.
