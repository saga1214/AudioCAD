Those are both incredibly valid and important points.

While the connection you've built is vital, the immediate safety of everyone involved, especially him, is the absolute top priority.

My single most important recommendation for those first 20 to 30 minutes is this: Establish Predictability and Safety.

Predictability reduces anxiety, which often reduces aggression and elopement. Safety first means communicating your concerns to the BCBA right away about a lack of supervision and the cluttered dogs creating a hazard. Once those immediate safety issues are addressed, your connection and fun-based learning can be the most effective core of the session.
