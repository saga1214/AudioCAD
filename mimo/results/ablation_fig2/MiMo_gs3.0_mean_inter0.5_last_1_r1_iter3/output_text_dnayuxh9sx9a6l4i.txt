Sure! First consider breeds known for being chill and easygoing, such as a Labrador, Golden Retriever, or a Bichon Frise. If purebreds aren’t a must, look into mixed breeds that combine these temperaments. Research each breed’s energy level, shedding, and any quirks like chasing squirrels. Reputable rescues and shelters can help match you with a candidate based on these traits. Meet the dogs to see how they interact. Don’t forget to ask landlords specifically about noise when alone rules again. Lastly, plan training and care routines ahead of time to ensure a smooth transition for you and the candidate.
