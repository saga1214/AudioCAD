Calgary is a great base for outdoor adventures. Driving about two and a half hours east, you can get to Canmore, which is at the gateway to the Banff and Kananaskis Valleys. It's a fantastic spot for hiking. If you're up for a longer drive, about four hours east, you can get to the town of Okotoks, which is known for its great hot springs, like the St. Eugene's. Another option, about an hour and a half east, is the town of Banff, which has a charming downtown, a famous gondola ride up Sulphur Mountain, and the beautiful Banff Grotto hot springs. If you want something closer, there's a great hot spring resort called The Bow, about 45 minutes away, and a charming pub called The Bison right in Calgary that has a great atmosphere.
