Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's ten calzones for the bachelor, but probably snacks and wraps for most everyone. Let's make this mess-free cooler packing a reality.

Drinks are straightforward: lots of reusable water bottles for everyone are a must. For low-sugar juice for the kids, individual milk boxes work great. For the adults who want variety on a hot day, sugar-free sodas in individual cans or twelve-packs are perfect. Don't forget plenty of ice packs to keep everything cold in the coolers. 

Snacks should all be single-serve and non-messy. Cheese sticks are always a hit. Baby carrots pair wonderfully single-serving cups of hummus. Grapes are easy and healthy. Individual pretzels bags or trail mix cups add variety without the clutter.

For meals, pre-made wraps or sandwiches are ideal. You could even include some turkey and cheese wraps specifically for your friend.

Since he loves calzones, a mini-calzone bar from a local pizza place is the way to go. Just order about ten mini calzones to share.

For dessert, to keep it simple and grab-and-go, pre-cut cupcakes or individual brownie packs are your best bet. They're celebratory, easy to handle, and mess-free.

With this plan, you should have minimal mess and a whole group having an absolute blast. I hope you all have an incredible day at the park!
