Dallas has a great Asian food scene! For Asian noodles, I'd suggest checking out these spots: Banh Mi 207 on North Richland for Vietnamese, Noodle House for Chinese, or Sushi Dallas for Japanese options. 

For protein bars, it depends on what you prefer. If you want something high in protein but not too sweet, look into Quest or Optimum Nutrition. For a more natural taste, RXBAR or Clif Bars are good too.
