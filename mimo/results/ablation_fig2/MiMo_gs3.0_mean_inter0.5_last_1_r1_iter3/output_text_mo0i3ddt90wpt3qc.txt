You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which is great, so gluten isn't going to be an issue there.

Some other gluten-free snack options that travel well would be trail mix. You can make your own by combining gluten-free nuts like almonds and walnuts with seeds like pumpkin seeds, and maybe some gluten-free dried fruit like cranberries.

Rice cakes are another simple, lightweight, and gluten-free option that you can top with avocado or almond butter if you like.
