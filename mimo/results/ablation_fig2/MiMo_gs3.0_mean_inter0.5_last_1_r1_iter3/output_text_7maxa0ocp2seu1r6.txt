No problem! Here’s a clear, categorized list of measurements to organize in your fitting notes, based on our chat:

### **Core Body Measurements (take EVERY fitting)**  
- **Bust** (armpit to shoulder point)  
- **Natural Waist** (tightest part of the torso, not belt line)  
- **Hips** (widest part, 8–10 inches below waist)  

### **General Measurements (for most garments)**  
- Inseam (for pants/shorts)  
- Sleeve Length (shoulder to wrist)  
- Shoulder Width (across shoulders, critical for jackets/shirts)  
- Neck (for collars/cuffs)  
- Bicep (mid-bicep circumference)  

### **Fitted Garments (add these for precision)**  
- **For tops/jackets**: Upper Back (across shoulders from neck to neck)  
- **For pants**:  
  - Thigh (top to bottom, over knee)  
  - Rise (crotch to waistband; *back rise is critical for tall clients*)  
- **For skirts/dresses**: Waist to hem  

### **Special Considerations**  
- **Tall clients**: Prioritize back rise and hip ease (e.g., your 6’4" client needs extra back rise + hip/thigh ease for sitting comfort).  
- **Long legs**: Test inseam *with shoes on*—shorten if hem needs to touch shoe tops.  

### **Pro Tips**  
1. **Always measure twice**: Use a flexible tape measure and mark spots clearly.  
2. **Test on mannequin**: Draft a mock-up before cutting fabric.  
3. **Comfort notes**: If a client sits often, add 1–2 inches ease to hips/thighs.
