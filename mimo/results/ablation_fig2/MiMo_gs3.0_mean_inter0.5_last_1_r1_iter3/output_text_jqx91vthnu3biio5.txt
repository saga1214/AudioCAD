### 🎧 **Monitor Timing for Breaks**  
- **Pomodoro Technique**: 25 mins work → 5 mins break. After 4 cycles, take a 15-min break.  
- **Why It Works**: Prevents burnout without overhauling focus. Use breaks for movement (stretch, walk) to reset.  
- **Not a Gimmick**: Studies show micro-breaks boost productivity by 30% (University of Illinois).  

### 🍽️ **Dinner Suggestions** (Quick, Healthy & Budget-Friendly!)  
1. **Sheet Pan Salmon & Roasted Veggies**  
   - Toss broccoli, bell peppers, and zucchini with olive oil, lemon, salt, and pepper. Add salmon fillets. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 15 mins. *Cost: ~$8*  
2. **Lentil & Veggie Stir-Fry**  
   - Sauté garlic, ginger, and frozen veggies in sesame oil. Add cooked lentils, soy sauce, and a splash of rice vinegar. Serve over rice. *Cost: ~$6*  
3. **Tofu Scramble**  
   - Crumble firm tofu into a pan with turmeric (for color), spinach, onions, and a dash of nutritional yeast. Top with avocado. *Cost: ~$5*  
4. **Oatmeal with Fruit & Nuts**  
   - Microwave oats, top with banana slices, almond butter, and a sprinkle of walnuts. *Cost: ~$4*
