Alright, for the brooder box, you need bedding that's easy to clean and won't attract rodents. Paper-based bedding is the best choice. You can use paper towels or shredded newspaper. It's safe and you can just scrape out the wet spots daily. To help with moisture control, you can add a layer of fine wood dust, but make sure it's dust-free and not the kind that attracts pests. Avoid anything like straw or pine shavings for the chicks, exactly for the reasons you've mentioned. The deep litter method we talked about for the adult chickens works great there though.
