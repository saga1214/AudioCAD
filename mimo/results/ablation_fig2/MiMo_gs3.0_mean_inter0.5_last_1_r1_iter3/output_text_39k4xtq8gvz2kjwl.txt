Okay, sweetie! Let me tell you the story of the brave adventurer who went to the magical country Eldoria on a sunny July day: July 20th, 1935. He wanted to find a super sparkly treasure hidden in the jungle!

Here are all the important people in the story and what they did:

1.  **Leo Vance:** He's the brave adventurer and Professor! He's the one who went looking for the treasure. He's trying to finish what his grandpa Elias started and protect the Sunstone from the mean villain.
2.  **Silas Croft:** He's the sneaky villain! He follows Leo to steal the treasure. He's the great-great-great-grandson of Augustus Croft, who tried to steal the stone long ago. He has four mean helpers.
3.  **Elena Castillo:** She's the pretty woman helper! She knows the jungle really well and helps Leo find the treasure. She's brave and clever.
4.  Leo's University: Kingsbridge University (Head: Dean Albright)
5.  Leo's Friends at Eldoria:
    *   **Jack "Hammer" Stone:** Leo's strong buddy! He helps carry things and fight Silas's team.
    *   **Javier Rios:** The local guide! He knows the jungle secrets and helps Leo find the right paths.
    *   **Arthur Finch:** The brilliant mapmaker! He helps solve the tricky puzzles Leo finds.
4.  Silas Croft's Henchmen (the bad guys helping him):
    *   **Marcus Kane:** A sneaky henchman.
    *   **Brutus Thorne:** A strong henchman.
    *   **Rex Gunner:** A good tracker.
    *   **Ivan Petrov:** A deadly sniper.

**The Treasure & Date:**

*   **Treasure:** The lost Sunstone of El (or El Dorero)ado! It's a massive crystal gem that glows with its own light because it holds the power of the jungle's sun. It was made long ago by the Sun-Kings of Eldoria.
*   **Date Happening:** The adventure happens on **July 20th, 1935**.

So, Leo, Jack, Javier, Arthur, Dean Albright, and Elena work together to solve puzzles and beat Silas and his helpers to find and protect the glowing Sunstone!
