That's fantastic that you can also illustrate! It gives you a huge advantage. For a children's book, publishers are always looking for a complete package. If you can do both the text and the art, you can absolutely submit a finished manuscript. However, there are a few important things to keep in mind.

First, publishers often have very specific rules. They might require that the artwork be done in a certain style, or they might not want to see it submitted at all, as they may prefer to find an illustrator themselves.

Second, the artwork itself must be high quality. Publishers will be looking at the art with the same critical eye they would use for the writing.

So, my recommendation is to first find an agent who specifically represents children's book authors and illustrators. They'll be much more familiar with how to navigate this process. You can submit your completed manuscript with your illustrations included, but make sure you follow the agent's specific guidelines for how they want to see the artwork.
