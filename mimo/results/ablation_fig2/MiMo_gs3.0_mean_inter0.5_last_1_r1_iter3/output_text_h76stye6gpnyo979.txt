It reminds me that planning is all about breaking things down.

So, for Bolivia or the Faroe Islands, you'd first need to research the costs—flights, accommodation, food. Then, you'd create a small, separate savings plan, say, fifty dollars a month.

At the same time, you'd stick to your apartment down and renovation goals with their normal, regular contributions. That way, you're making progress on everything without letting one goal take over.
