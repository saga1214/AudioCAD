Here's a quick recap using your car analogy, perfect for a child:

1.  **Heart:** The **gas station and engine**. It pumps the blood-cars all around your body.
2.  **Lungs:** The **fresh fuel depot**. Blood-cars stop here to pick up full loads of oxygen fuel.
3.  **Main Arteries:** The **big highways**. These are the large blood vessels carrying blood away from the heart super fast.
4.  **Capillaries:** The **tiny neighborhood streets**. These are the smallest blood vessels where the blood-cars actually stop and drop off the oxygen fuel to every single muscle and organ cell.
5.  **Veins:** The **return roads**. After dropping off oxygen, the blood-cars take these larger roads back to the heart/lungs to refuel.
6.  **Kidneys:** The **car wash and junk detox facility**. As blood-cars pass through, the kidneys clean them by filtering out waste junk (like old snacks) and extra water. The clean blood keeps going.
7.  **Liver:** The **recycling and refinishing plant**. The liver sorts through the blood-cars' cargo (nutrients from food). It stores useful things for later, breaks down harmful stuff, and makes sure everything is balanced.

**The Journey in a Nutshell:** Heart (pump) → Lungs (refuel with oxygen) → Arteries (big roads out) → Capillaries (deliver oxygen to cells) → Veins (roads back to heart) → Kidneys (clean the blood) → Heart (ready for next trip!).

This keeps the core car/road/gas station analogy while clearly showing each organ's vital job!
