### Treat Recommendations:

   - **Commercial Training Treats**: Tiny, soft, and designed for training (e.g., Zukes, freeze-dried liver).

### Clicker Training Explained:
Clicker training uses a **three-step process** to build understanding and confidence:

1. **The Clicker as a "Bridge"**:  
   - Clicker makes a sharp, consistent sound.  
   - *Purpose*: Marks the exact moment your puppy does what you want (e.g., sits).  

2. **Immediate Reward**:  
   - The second the click sounds, give a treat.  
   - *Why*: The puppy learns "click = reward is coming!"  

3. **Repetition**:  
   - Repeat this 10-20 times daily.  
   - Gradually add verbal commands ("sit") *after* the click.  

#### Example: Teaching "Sit"
- Hold a treat near your puppy’s nose.  
- Slowly move it upward over his head.  
- When his bottom touches the ground, **click** → give a treat.  
- Repeat until he anticipates sitting when he sees the treat near his nose.  
- Then add "sit" verbally just before the click/treat.  

### Key Tips for Clicker Training:
- **Start Small**: Short sessions (5-10 minutes) daily.  
- **Keep Treats High-Value**: Use his favorite rewards initially.  
- **Be Patient**: Puppies learn at their own pace!  
- **Never Punish**: Focus on rewarding the desired behavior.
