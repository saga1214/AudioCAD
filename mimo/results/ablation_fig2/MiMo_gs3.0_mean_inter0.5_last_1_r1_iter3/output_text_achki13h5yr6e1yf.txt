To summarize all the costs for chicken cutlets: two pounds of chicken breasts are eight to fifteen dollars; all-purpose flour is three to five dollars; twelve eggs cost three to seven dollars; breadcrumbs are two to four dollars; vegetable oil is four to eight dollars; and salt and pepper is three to six dollars.

And for the garlic mashed potatoes: a five-pound bag of potatoes is three to six dollars; one head of garlic is about one dollar; a pound of butter is four to seven dollars; and a half-gallon of milk is three to five dollars.
