Okay, you mentioned a phrase about burning a candle at both ends. That's a common idiom that means doing a lot of work and not getting enough rest. There are other similar expressions, like 'racing against time,' which means you're trying to finish something before time runs out. 'Walking on thin ice' is another one; it means you're in a dangerous situation. And 'stretched thin' is used to describe when you're overworked and don't have time for other things.
