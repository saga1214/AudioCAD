You can absolutely learn more. The most direct way is to visit the Buffalo and Erie Canal Museum in Lockport. It's dedicated to preserving the history of the canal and even has a working replica of a canal boat to show you how it all worked.

If you prefer reading, you could check out the Lockport Historical Society. They have a museum and a library that would be full of resources on the canal's history. Just remember, the museum itself is located right at the "Flight of Five" locks, so you can see the history while you learn about it.
