Here's a concise list of my best recommendations for cutting sheet metal, drywall fastening, and insulation—all available at major Indianapolis big-box stores like **Home Depot**, **Lowe's**, or **Menards**:

### 1. **Cut Thinner Sheet Metal**  
   **Aviation Snips**  
   Quick for curves and thin gauges. Found in hardware/tool aisles.

### 2. **Cut Thicker Metal or Power Tool Option**  
   **Angle Grinder with a Cut-Off Wheel**  
   Versatile for all gauges. Jigsaws with metal blades work too. Check power tool sections.

### Fastening Drywall (Best Option)  
   **1¼-Inch Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws**  
   *Why better than nails:* Prevents pops/cracks, holds securely in studs. Use a screwdriver/driver for perfect depth. Aisle: Lumber/nailing supplies.

### Insulation Behind Drywall  
   **Pre-Cut Fiberglass Batt Insulation Rolls**  
   Easy fit between studs. For soundproofing, consider **Mineral Wool**. Avoid spray foam for DIY—it’s complex.

### Where to Buy (Indianapolis):  
- **Home Depot/Lowe’s/Menards**: All items above.  
  - *Tip*: For metal tools, check **Tractor Supply Co.** or local Ace Hardware.  
- **Drywall Screws/Insulation**: Lumber departments at all three stores.
