### Switching Software & Workflow
1. **Adobe Audition vs. Audacity**:  
   - **Adobe Audition** offers superior processing power, AI tools (like auto-normalization and noise reduction), and smoother multi-track editing. The learning curve exists, but tutorials (YouTube, Adobe's site) can help transition. Start with Audacity's presets for basic cleanup to ease the switch.
2. **Reliable AI Tools**:  
   - **iZotope RX 10** (paid) is the gold standard for noise reduction, mouth click removal, and speech repair.  
   - **Adobe Audition’s "Clean Voice"** and **Acon Digital Restoration Suite** (free) are good free/low-cost alternatives.  
   - **Descript** is great for editing via text transcripts.  
   *(Pro Tip: Batch-process noise removal in Audition—mark silent sections, apply noise reduction, and export.)*
3. **Organization**:  
   - Use **digital project folders** (e.g., "Client_A_2024," "Script_123").  
   - Label files with **dates, client names, and track numbers**.  
   - **Cloud backups** (Google Drive, Dropbox) prevent disasters.

### Making Your Demo Stand Out
- **Memorable Hooks**: Start with a 3-second punchy line. Example:  
  *"I’m the narrator who makes characters feel real—not just heard."*  
- **Show Range**: Clip 5–10 seconds max, showcasing emotional shifts (e.g., suspense → humor).  
- **Clean Audio**: Harshness ruins impact. Prioritize fixing your Yeti room/mic first.

### Balancing Recording, Editing & Marketing  
- **Vocal Health**:  
  - Warm-ups/cool-downs daily.  
  - Hydrate with **room-temperature water** (cold strains cords; warm can be soothing).  
  - 20-minute breaks every hour.  
- **Marketing**: Dedicate 30 mins/day to social media/auditions. Use scheduling tools (Buffer, Hootsuite).

### Acoustic Quick Fix for Harsh Audio  
Hang **towel rolls** or **heavy blankets** on问题墙壁内侧，模仿专业吸音板的效果。说话时避免对着顶部麦克风，保持手宽距离。如果房间仍不理想，试试免费工具如**Audacity的EQ**——降低100–300Hz（增加温暖）和2–5kHz（减少刺耳）。

### Pro Workflow Tip  
Use **Adobe Audition’s "Speech to Text"** to auto-tag edits. Saves hours on labeling.
