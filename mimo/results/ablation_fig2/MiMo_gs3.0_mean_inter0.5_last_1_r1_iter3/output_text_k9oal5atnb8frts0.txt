Okay, got it. For a 'Retro Tech' theme lunch, here are two ideas. First, for the main dish, you could do a 'Vintage Computer Chip Salad'. This would be a big salad with lots of veggies, maybe some cranberries for color, and you'd top it with roasted chickpeas that have been coated in a little bit of cocoa powder to look like old computer chips. For a side, 'Dot Matrix Pasta'. Just make a simple pasta dish and mix in some dried cranberries and raisins for the 'dots'. A simple side salad or bread would round it out. Second idea: a 'CRT Monitor Sandwich'. The main part would be a sandwich with turkey and cheese, maybe a little mustard, and you'd serve it with a side of 'Floppy Disk' fruit salad, which is just a bowl of grapes and berries. You could also have some pretzel sticks on the side to look like 'RAM sticks'. These ideas tie the theme together without being too complicated.
