Secret Harbour Beach Resort offers a perfect starting point for your beach stays. Being right on that calm bay means you can snorkel right from the shore – no boat pressure changes needed for your brother. For exploring, you're steps from the shops and restaurants in Cruz Bay. 

If you want a quick beach adventure, the Reef Bay Trail is an easy, flat walk through the rainforest down to a beautiful beach. Or, just hang out at Secret Harbour Beach Resort itself – swim, sunbathe, and watch the boats go by. 

For more activity, the Virgin Islands National Park has snorkeling trails. The Secret Harbor Beach is one of the most popular spots there. It's a short boat ride from Cruz Bay. You could also visit the Trunk Bay Snorkel Trail. It's another guided snorkel tour from the park. 

Around Cruz Bay, Morgan's Mango is a classic spot for casual dining and drinks. The Longboard is great for happy hour and tacos. If you want something a bit more upscale, Duffy's Love Shack is a popular spot for dinner with ocean views. 

And if you're up for a day trip, the Cinnamon Bay is a beautiful beach with great snorkeling. It's about a 15-minute boat ride from Cruz Bay. You can take a guided snorkel tour from there, or just relax on the beach.
