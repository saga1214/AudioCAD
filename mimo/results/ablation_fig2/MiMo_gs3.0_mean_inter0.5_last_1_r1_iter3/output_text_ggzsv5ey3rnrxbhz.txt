The term 'forever chemicals' does refer to these PFAS substances, which do resist breaking down. However, your body does have a natural process for getting rid of them, though it can take a long time. There's no magic cure, but you can definitely reduce your exposure. Let's focus on that, okay? To cut down, here are the five products to avoid. First, non-stick cookware. Instead, use stainless steel, cast iron, or glass. Second, grease-resistant packaging, like fast-food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags. Third, products labeled 'stain- or waterproof', such as carpets and outdoor clothing. Fourth, cosmetics and dental floss that might not be labeled as PFAS-free. And fifth, since these chemicals pollute tap water, using a certified water filter is key.
