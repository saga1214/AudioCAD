Alright, I'll quickly summarize both yogurt-making analogies for you. 

First, the Party one: Milk is the empty room, good bacteria guests invited kept warm get energized. Sugar acts as party food eaten by bacteria. Group hugging thickens the milk into yogurt.

Then, the Net analogy: Milk is a disorganized crowd, bacteria are tiny coaches. Warmth energizes coaches. The milk's proteins form a net that traps liquid, creating thick yogurt. Flavoring is like uniforms that give the yogurt its specific identity after the net is formed.
