Now, for your family vacation, you need a warm California destination SUV-friendly. Palm Springs immediately comes to mind. It's reliably warm, famous for swimming and warm weather activities, and it has plenty to do for swimmers dieting to wear their bathing suit. It's also celiac-friendly with careful planning, and it's easily accessible from Northern California in an SUV. Palm Springs is ideal for a 4 to 5-day trip with a healthy budget because it offers a mix of relaxation, adventure, and great dining.

Here’s a sample 5-day itinerary for your family vacation in Palm Springs.

On Day One, you'll arrive and check in. Drive from Northern California to Palm Springs, which is about a 4 to 4-and-a-half-hour trip. I'd recommend staying at the Hotel Palacio, which is an iconic Spanish Revival hotel with a lovely pool and a great restaurant. For dinner, you could go to the Poolside Restaurant and Bar at the hotel for casual, poolside dining.

Day Two is all about pools and adventure. In the morning, you could go to the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway for amazing views and a ride up Mount San Jacinto. In the afternoon, spend time at the Spa� at the Hotel Palacio or the nearby Palm Springs Aquaventure Waterpark. For dinner, you could try Cafe 291 for upscale Northern Californian cuisine, or if you're up for it, the Pupuseria El Remolino for a unique and delicious Mexican experience.

On Day Three, you can explore more of the desert. You could visit the Palm Springs Art Museum or the Paul McCall Museum, which is a quirky and fun art space. In the afternoon, check out the Coachella Valley Botanical Garden. For lunch, The Frantic Baker has excellent gluten-free options. In the evening, you could go for a romantic dinner at the French-inspired restaurant Le Cabrio at the Miramonte Resort and Spa.

For Day Four, you could head to Temecula, which is about an hour and a half away, for some wine tasting. There are many wineries with gluten-free options, and you can even do a tasting room crawl. For lunch, you could stop at the Temecula Brewing Company. Afterwards, you can head back to Palm Springs for some relaxation time.

On your final day, Day Five, you can enjoy a leisurely breakfast at the hotel. Depending on your flight time, you could do some last-minute souvenir shopping or just relax by the pool before heading to the airport.

As for packing, since it will be warm, bring lightweight clothing, swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat. You'll also need a celiac-safe gluten-free snack kit, a reusable water bottle, and a reusable straw. A good swimsuit cover-up is essential. A light sweater is good for evenings, and a reusable tote bag is handy for shopping.

For travel, you'll need an SUV. You can rent one from a company like Enterprise or Hertz. For transportation within Palm Springs, you can rent a car, use rideshares like Uber or Lyft, or rent a golf cart. For getting to Temecula, you'll need to drive.

This itinerary balances relaxation, adventure, and dining, all while being celiac-friendly with advance planning. I hope you have a wonderful vacation!
