Here's a Spaghetti Carbonara ingredient list to get you started:

**Core Ingredients:**
1.  **Spaghetti:** 8-10 oz (225-280g)
2.  **Guanciale:** 4-6 oz (110-170g) - *Essential for authentic flavor (cured pork jowl). Substitute pancetta if needed.*
3.  **Eggs:** 3 large eggs + 2 large egg yolks (about 1 1/4 cups whole eggs)
4.  **Pecorino Romano Cheese:** 1/2 cup finely grated (about 2 oz / 55g)
5.  **Parmigiano-Reggiano Cheese:** 1/4 cup finely grated (about 1 oz / 30g)
6.  **Freshly Ground Black Pepper:** Generous amount

**Optional but Recommended:**
*   **Salt:** For pasta water (about 1-2 tsp kosher salt or sea salt)
*   **Extra Pecorino/Parmigiano:** For garnish

**Key Points:**
*   **Guanciale:** This is the star! Look for it at Italian delis. It's fatty and flavorful. Pancetta is a common substitute.
*   **Cheeses:** Pecorino Romano is salty and sharp (use less if your pancetta is salty). Parmigiano-Reggiano adds nutty depth. Grate them finely.
*   **Eggs:** The yolk adds richness; the whole eggs help emulsify the sauce. Whisk them well with the grated cheese.
*   **Pepper:** Generous freshly ground black pepper is non-negotiable for authenticity and flavor.
*   **No Cream:** Authentic Roman Carbonara uses no cream.
*   **No Garlic/Parmesan in Pasta Water:** These are not traditional additions.

**Penalties for Illegal U-Turns in BC:**
*   **Fine:** Typically between **$110 and $160**, plus a 1% processing fee.
*   **Points:** Usually **2 demerit points** on your driver's license.
*   **Severity:** Fines increase for repeat offenses or if the illegal U-turn causes an accident.

**Remember:** Always check the road signs! The rule about visibility (150m) is a guideline, but **signs are the law**. If there's a sign prohibiting U-turns, you absolutely cannot do one there, regardless of visibility. When in doubt, wait for a safe opportunity or find a designated turn lane.
