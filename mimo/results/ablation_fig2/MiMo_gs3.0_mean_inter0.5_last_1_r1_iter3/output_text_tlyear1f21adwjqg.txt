This is a tough choice since "efficiency" can mean different things, like cost, availability, or consistency. Solar energy really stands out overall. It's scalable from tiny rooftop installations to massive farms, getting cheaper every year, and works almost anywhere. Wind power is also great where the winds are strong and steady. Hydropower is reliable and efficient, but it depends on water flow and often needs new infrastructure. So, if I absolutely had to pick just one, I’d still go with solar. But a smart energy system often mixes different sources, like solar, wind, and storage, to cover all the bases.
