For an end-of-year celebration, a **low-key BBQ at the park** with sundaes or pizza as the main event is usually a hit! Here’s a balanced approach:

### 🎉 Celebration Ideas:
1. **Park BBQ + Ice Cream/Sundaes**:  
   - Rent a pavilion (or use a rain备用 plan like a cafeteria event).

   - **Budget**: Suggest a small fee ($5–$10 per girl) to cover supplies (tents, grills, ice cream). Patches/sashes remain parent responsibility.  

2. **Competitive Park Fun**:  
   - Include 1–2 races (three-legged, sack races) and a scavenger hunt.  
   - **Keep it simple**: Use recycled materials (e.g., cardboard boats for a water race).  

3. **Themed Party**:  
   - "Brownie Adventure Day": Decorate sashes with patches, storytelling, and a nature walk.  

### 💡 Key Tips:
- **Snacks**: Rotate weekly (e.g., parent brings chips/dip, troop provides snacks). Avoid dairy-heavy options.  
- **Cost Management**:  
  - **Sashes/patches**: Clearly state these are parental purchases upfront.  
  - **Dues**: Set a flat fee ($20–$30) covering campfire gear, field trips, and celebration supplies.  

### 🌟 Start Small & Celebrate Wins:
Focus on earning badges early (like "Friendship" badge) to build troop pride. By year’s end, you’ll have a great rapport—whether indoors or outdoors! You’ve got this! 👍
