Here’s a balanced 10-day Japan itinerary prioritizing art exploration, presentation prep, and practical logistics (Tokyo + Osaka + Kyoto/Nara):

### **Tokyo (4 Days)**
- **Day 1-2:** Focus on workouts, family sightseeing, *light research* for presentation topic.  
- **Day 3-4:** Dedicated to art-related activities.  
  - **Morning Workouts:** 7:00–8:30 AM (hotel gym/station nearby).  
  - **Morning Research (7:30–9:00 AM):** Browse Tokyo art museums (e.g., **Tokyo National Museum** for ancient artifacts, **Mori Art Museum** for modern works).  
  - **Afternoon Sightseeing (11:00 AM–6:00 PM):** Temples (Senso-ji), Asakusa (calligraphy), Harajuku (street art).  
  - **Evening Dinner.**  

### **Osaka (3 Days) + Day Trips (2 Days)**  
- **Day 5:** Travel to Kyoto (Shinkansen: 15 min from Osaka).  
  - **Morning:** **Kiyomizu-dera Temple** (wooden architecture, nature motifs).  
  - **Afternoon:** **Nishiki Market** (traditional crafts/kimono shops for slide inspiration).  
- **Day  ukiyo-e woodblocks at the Kyoto Museum.**  
- **Day 6:** Travel to Nara (45 min from Kyoto).  
  - **Morning:** **Todai-ji Temple** (Great Buddha statue), **Nara Park** (deer).  
  - **Afternoon:** Return to Osaka.  
- **Day 7:** Osaka exploration.  
  - **Morning:** **Osaka Castle** (historical design), **Dotonbori** (street art/kimono patterns).  
  - **Afternoon:** **Kuromon Market** (ceramics/ceramics workshops).  
- **Day 8:** **Universal Studios Japan** (optional; Hokusis *The Great Wave* inspired sets).  

### **Design Strategy for Slides**  
- **Images:** Use ukiyo-e prints, kimono patterns, or temple architecture.  
- **Color Scheme:** Earth tones (soil, wood) or vibrant accents (red, indigo).  
- **Font:** Traditional *shodo* (calligraphy) or clean sans-serif.  
- **Tip:** While visiting, photograph details you can use ethically (e.g., castle gates, pottery).  

### **Practical Notes**  
- **Hotel Check-Out:** Typically **10:00 AM**—confirm with Osaka hotel.  
- **Taxis:** Costly but convenient (~¥5,000–¥15,000/ride). Use apps like **Taxi Magic** for English.  
- **Communication:** Hotel staff may not speak English—use Google Translate (offline mode) or carry printed directions.
