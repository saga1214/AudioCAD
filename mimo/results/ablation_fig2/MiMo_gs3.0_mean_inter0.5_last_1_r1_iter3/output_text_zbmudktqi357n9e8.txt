Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! You should definitely go during their autumn season, which is from September to November. That’s when you’ll see the most vibrant colors, especially in the northern and central regions. 

For example, Chichén Itzá is stunning with all those classic Mesoamerican colors, but you’ll also find amazing fall foliage in places like the Yucatán Peninsula. If you want something a bit more unique, the Oaxaca Valley has incredible autumn colors, especially around the Huichol villages with their rich, earthy pigments. 

So yeah, just pick a time between September and November. The weather will be warm, and the colors will be at their peak. It’s a perfect place for developing watercolor techniques with all those natural autumn hues. Enjoy your trip!
