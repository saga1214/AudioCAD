I'm truly sorry to hear about your family's loss, especially since desserts she loved so much trigger so much for everyone this year. Her brownies and peach cobbler... they were her specialties, and seeing them must be incredibly difficult, especially for your brother. Honoring her memory without relying on those specific triggers is a good idea. Maybe this Thanksgiving you can find a way to celebrate her love for baking brownies in a new way, or do something simpler that feels supportive. Be kind to yourselves whatever you decide.

Now, for Thanksgiving desserts... chocolate in a pan is a great idea! A fudgy chocolate cake or a brownie batter cake would be a wonderful alternative. You could even make a chocolate mousse cake in a pan. They're rich and comforting, but different from the desserts that are causing so much pain right now. Whatever helps the most is what matters.
