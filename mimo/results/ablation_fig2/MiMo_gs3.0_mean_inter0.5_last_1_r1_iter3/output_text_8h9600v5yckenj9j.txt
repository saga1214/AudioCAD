For candy tomorrow, grab classic Halloween hits like Reese’s Cups, Snickers, and Twix—they’re always popular and easy for guests to recognize. You could also stock up on mini chocolate bars, gummies, and maybe some sour candies like Warheads for a fun kick. If you’re worried about allergies again, check labels for dairy or artificial dye warnings. A good trick-or-treat-style bowl or a Halloween-themed serving dish will make it festive. If you’ve got a blender, you could even make a spooky punch with orange juice and soda for a simple drink mix!
