Absolutely! For a 4-5 day warm-weather Northern California getaway with warm beaches, swimming, swimming suit-friendly fitness, and celiac-safe dining, I recommend two fantastic options: **Big Sur** or **Monterey/Carmel**. Both are warm in fall/winter, feature ocean swimming, and have excellent gluten-free dining. Here’s a detailed itinerary for each:

---

### **Option 1: Warm & Adventurous – Big Sur (4-5 Days)**
**Why?** Warmer than Northern Cal (60-75°F days, 45-55°F nights), stunning beaches, swimming holes, and car access.

#### **Itinerary Breakdown**
- **Day 1: Arrival in Big Sur**  
  - **Stay:** *The Big Sur Lodge* (luxury) or *Nepenthe Inn* (beachfront boutique).  
  - **Pack Stop:** Grab snacks, water, and a waterproof bag for valuables.  
  - **Dinner:** *Nepenthe* (seafood with ocean views; ask for GF menu).  

- **Day 2: Coastal Wonders**  
  - **Morning:** Drive to **Pfeiffer Beach** (swimming, tide pools, purple sand).  
  - **Lunch:** *Big Sur Bakery* (GF pastries + sandwiches).  
  - **Afternoon:** Visit **McWay Falls** (waterfall drop-off) and **Big Creek Valley**.  
  - **Dinner:** *The Post* (Michelin-starred; call ahead for GF prep).  

- **Day 3: Big Sur Highlights**  
  - **Morning:** **McWay Falls Lookout** (short hike) and **Pfeiffer Beach** again.  
  - **Lunch:** *Wildseed Café* (100% GF; oceanfront dining).  
  - **Afternoon:** **Bixby Creek Bridge** and **Pfeiffer Falls**.  
  - **Dinner:** *Nepenthe Cover* (reservations essential; GF options).  

- **Day 4: Road Trip South**  
  - **Morning:** Drive south to **Davenport** (stop at **Surfer’s Café** for GF pancakes).  
  - **Swimming Stop:** **Davenport Beach** (calm waves, rocky cove).  
  - **Lunch:** *Davenport Beach Grill* (GF burgers, salads).  
  - **Afternoon:** **Half Moon Bay** (oyster farm tour at **Moss Landing**).  
  - **Dinner:** *The Sardine Factory* (Monterey; GF-friendly seafood).  

- **Day 5: Return Home**  
  - **Morning:** **Half Moon Bay Pier** (walk, watch seals).  
  - **Lunch:** *Half Moon Bay Fish Market* (GF fish tacos).  
  - **Drive back** (4 hours).  

#### **Packing Essentials**  
- Two bikinis/swimsuits, cover-ups, reef-safe sunscreen, hats, sunglasses.  
- Lightweight layers (Big Sur evenings can be cool).  
- Reusable water bottle, waterproof phone case.  
- Celiac essentials: Medical card, snacks (GF granola bars).  

---

### **Option 2: Classic Coastal – Monterey/Carmel (4-5 Days)**  
**Why?** Warmer (60-70°F days, 50s°F nights), world-class dining, aquarium, and celiac-safe options.

#### **Itinerary Breakdown**  
- **Day 1: Arrival in Monterey**  
  - **Stay:** *The Sardine Factory Hotel* (historic, GF-friendly) or *Carmel River Inn*.  
  - **Dinner:** *The Sardine Factory* (must-try; GF options abundant).  

- **Day 2: Aquarium & Cannery Row**  
  - **Morning:** **Monterey Bay Aquarium** (book tickets early).  
  - **Lunch:** *Fisherman’s Gourmet* (GF sushi/sandwiches).  
  - **Afternoon:** **Cannery Row** (shops, street performers).  
  - **Dinner:** *Casa Pollo* (GF empanadas; cash only).  

- **Day 3: Carmel & Pfeiffer Beach**  
  - **Morning:** Drive to **Pfeiffer Beach** (swimming, tide pools).  
  - **Lunch:** *Carmel Valley Vineyard* (GF pizzas, salads).  
  - **Afternoon:** **Carmel Beach** (rent kayaks) and **Carmel River State Park**.  
  - **Dinner:** *Miguel’s Mexican Seafood* (GF taquitos; reservations needed).  

- **Day 4: Alcatraz Cruise & Fisherman’s Wharf**  
  - **Morning:** **Alcatraz Cruise** (book ahead; warm weather = ideal).  
  - **Lunch:** *Scoma’s* (GF clam chowder; Wharf location).  
  - **Afternoon:** **Wharf Shops**, **Whale Watching Tour**.  
  - **Dinner:** *Fog Harbor Fish House* (GF crab cakes; Alcatraz cruise return).  

- **Day 5: Return Home**  
  - **Morning:** **Pacific Grove** (visit **Lovers Point** beach).  
  - **Lunch:** *Wildseed Café* (Big Sur location; GF pastries).  
  - **Drive back** (4.5 hours).  

#### **Packing Essentials**  
- Same as Big Sur: Swimsuits, cover-ups, sun gear, layers.  
- **Celiac Note:** Always confirm "no cross-contamination" with staff. Wildseed, The Sardine Factory, and Fisherman’s Gourmet are highly accommodating.  

---

### **Budget Tips**  
- **Lodging:** Mid-range hotels (e.g., *Hotel Carmel* in Monterey) offer GF dining.  
- **Food:** Seafood is GF-friendly (avoid crustaceans if needed).  
- **Swimming:** Both regions have safe, warm beaches (check tide/temperature apps).  

### **Final Packing Reminders**  
- **Medications:** Pack extra gluten-free snacks (e.g., GF crackers, jerky).  
- **Tech:** Waterproof phone pouch, portable charger.  
- **Extras:** Reef-safe sunscreen, beach towel, reusable water bottle.  

Both trips guarantee warm days for swimming and suit-ready dining. Enjoy your escape from Northern Cal’s chill! 🌊☀️
