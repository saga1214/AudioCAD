That line of reasoning about DNA doesn't quite work.

Even though the DNA for the first chicken was inside the egg, the egg that actually was the egg of a chicken had to be laid by a hen—a true chicken. The egg that contained the DNA for the first chicken was actually the egg of a creature that was just a direct ancestor of the chicken. Only after that egg hatched did the first chicken emerge. Therefore, the chicken—the living creature—came before the chicken egg.
