For Asian noodles in Dallas, you could try these spots:

First, there's Mr. Pho, which is famous for its Vietnamese pho and other noodle dishes. It’s a classic Dallas spot with a cozy vibe. They have a great chicken noodle soup if you’re craving comfort food.

Second, Sushi Dallas is great for Japanese sushi and ramen. Their miso ramen is particularly delicious.

Third, there’s Little Saigon, which has a bunch of options, from Thai to Vietnamese. You can find some amazing pad thai or pho there.

As for protein bars, I’d recommend:

First, Quest bars. They’re high in protein, low in sugar, and taste pretty good.

Second, RXBAR. They use simple ingredients and have a solid protein-to-fiber ratio.

Third, Clif Builders bars. They’re great for post-workout recovery and have natural flavors.

Hope that helps! Enjoy your meal.
