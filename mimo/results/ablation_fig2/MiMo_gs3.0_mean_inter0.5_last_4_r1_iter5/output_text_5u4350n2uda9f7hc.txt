For east-facing fifth-floor NYC balconies and IKEA-table-sized spaces, consider these homeier houseplants with city-friendly traits:

**Top Plant Recommendations & Considerations**
1.  **Snake Plant (Sansevieria) - For floor space:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Sturdy architectural leaves (tall, upright, often striped or variegated), thrives on neglect, tolerates wind, stays compact enough for ground placement.
    *   **Balcony Suitability:** East-facing morning sun is ideal. fifth floor provides moderate wind exposure it can handle. NYC dust might need occasional wiping.
    *   **Stress:** Very low. Tolerates low light better than geranium/mint. Water sparingly (every 2-3 weeks).

2.  **Pothos (Epipremnum aureum) - For tabletop:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Fast-growing, trailing vines, stunning foliage (green, variegated), easy care, fits small spaces.
    *   **Balcony Suitability:** Loves bright morning light. Tolerates some wind. Tabletop pots keep it contained. Thrives in NYC humidity.
    *   **Stress:** Low. Can grow in lower light but excels in east-facing. Water weekly.

3.  **Succulents (e.g., Echeveria, Sedum 'Blue Spruce', Haworthia) - For ground/floor:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Tiny, compact, add texture/color, ultra-low maintenance, love bright morning sun.
    *   **Balcony Suitability:** Fifth floor balcony gets excellent morning light. Compact size perfect for ground. Wind resistance varies (choose sturdy types).
    *   **Stress:** Very low. Water sparingly (every 2-3 weeks). Avoid overwatering.

4.  **Begonia 'Angelatii' or Kalanchoe 'Tossu' - For ground/tabletop:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Compact, colorful foliage (pink/red begonia, green/brown kalanchoe), bright but not fussy.
    *   **Balcony Suitability:** East-facing morning sun ideal. Fifth floor wind manageable. Tabletop pots keep size controlled.
    *   **Stress:** Low-Medium. Begonia prefers consistent moisture. Kalanchoe is more drought-tolerant.

**Critical Selection & Setup Factors**
1.  **Light:** Bright, indirect morning sun is perfect for all listed plants. Avoid direct, harsh afternoon NYC sun.
2.  **Wind:** Fifth floor = moderate wind. Choose plants with sturdy growth habits (snake plant, pothos, compact succulents). Ensure pots are secure and won't tip.
3.  **Space:** Prioritize compact plants. Snake plant (ground), Pothos (tabletop), and small succulents (ground) are ideal. Herbs like basil/mint can work but grow fast.
4.  NYC-Specifics:
    *   **Watering:** NYC air is dry. Plants will need slightly more water than usual. Check soil moisture regularly.
    *   **Pollution/Dust:** Light wind can bring in dust. Wipe leaves occasionally. Keep pots slightly elevated if ground space is dusty.
    *   **Weight:** Pots are lightweight (2-10 lbs). Choose small nursery pots for easy transport. Bring them home after your stay.
5.  Setup Tips:
    *   Use well-draining cactus/succulent or regular potting soil.
    *   Ensure pots have drainage holes.
    *   Place snake plant/snake plant on ground. Place pothos/herbs/succulents on table. Use self-watering pots if desired.
