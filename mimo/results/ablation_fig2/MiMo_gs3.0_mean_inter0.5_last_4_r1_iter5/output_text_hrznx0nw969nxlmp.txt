If you're staying at Secret Harbour Beach Resort on St. John, you're in an absolutely perfect spot for beautiful beach days. Here are some fantastic daytime activities nearby:

1. **Snorkeling Right from Your Resort:** The bay at Secret Harbour is incredibly calm, sheltered by mangroves, and teeming with marine life. You can literally step off the beach into crystal-clear water to snorkel. Look for colorful parrotfish, sea turtles (often just resting on the sand!), and maybe even a graceful sea turtle. The resort even has snorkel gear available for guests.

2. **Explore Cruz Bay Trails:** Just a short walk north from Gallows Point Resort (Secret Harbour) leads to the Cruz Bay area. From there, you can access several beautiful, easy-to-moderate hiking trails through the Virgin Islands National Park. The **Cinnamon Bay Trail** is particularly stunning – it winds through lush forest and along the beautiful beach, offering amazing views and a chance to spot birds like bananaquits and hummingbirds in their natural habitat.

3. **Kayak Through the Mangroves:** For a unique adventure, take a guided kayak tour from Cruz Bay (available locally) to explore the mangrove channels. This is a fantastic way to see the incredible biodiversity beneath the water's surface – crabs, fish, rays, and even dolphins sometimes! It's peaceful, educational, and a great alternative to snorkeling.

4. Morgan's Cave (Snorkeling & Kayaking): A short drive south from Cruz Bay takes you to the famous Morgan's Cave. It's a beautiful, protected cove with incredible snorkeling (great for beginners due to calm waters) and kayaking. You can easily find tours from Cruz Bay or rent gear locally. The underwater visibility is usually excellent.

5. **Explore Cruz Bay Market & Shops:** Gallows Point and the shops in Cruz Bay are just a walk away. Spend the afternoon browsing local craft shops, grabbing souvenirs, and exploring the vibrant market for fresh fruit, tropical flowers, and local crafts. It's a great way to experience the local culture.

6. **Relax & Enjoy the Sunset:** While Secret Harbour is fantastic for the day, the bay at Gallows Ocean 362 (right next door) is arguably the best spot on the island for watching the sunset. Grab a drink, find a comfortable spot on the beach or at the resort bar, and soak in the incredible views over Cruz Bay and the surrounding islands.
