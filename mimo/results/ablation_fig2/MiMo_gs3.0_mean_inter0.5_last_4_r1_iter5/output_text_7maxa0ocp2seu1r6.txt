### **III. TALL CLIENTS (Like Your Neighbor)**  
- **BACK RISE** (CRITICAL for sitting comfort):  
  - *Formula:* **Front Rise + 1–2 inches** (e.g., 12" front rise → 13–14" back rise).  
- **Hip Ease**: Add 1–2 inches to hip measurement for comfort when sitting.  
- **Thigh Ease**: Add 1–1.5 inches for stretch and movement.  

---

### **IV. INSEAM adjustment for shoe length**  
- **Pin hem to desired shoe height** (e.g., top of sneaker).  
- **Shorten inseam** if needed (e.g., 37" → 32–34" for ankle-length pants).
