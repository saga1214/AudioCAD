That's a great plan for exploring the beautiful area around Vancouver! Here are five fantastic road trip options with ideas for your trip.

First, you could head to the Okanagan Valley. You can drive through the stunning lakes and vineyards. A must-do would be to have a fancy dinner at a lakeside restaurant, like The Keg on Okanagan Lake, and then watch the sunset over the water.

Second, there's Vancouver Island. You can drive the scenic route along the coast. A perfect activity would be a gourmet lunch at a small cafe in Tofino and watching the waves crash on the beach.

Third, you could explore the Fraser Canyon. You'll get to drive through the mountains and see some historic sites. A great idea is to find a charming bed and breakfast, enjoy a beautiful breakfast there, and then go for a walk in the forest.

Fourth, there's the North Coast Highway. It's a very scenic drive along the coast. You could find a cozy little inn to stay at and enjoy a special dinner with amazing ocean views.

And fifth, you could visit the Kootenay and Columbia Icefields. The drive through the mountains is incredible, and you could even take a flightseeing tour over the glaciers. For a special meal, you could book a dinner at a lodge that has a fireplace. Enjoy your trip!
