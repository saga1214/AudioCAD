You're welcome! Here's a quick recap of the **three spicy main courses incorporating shrimp** I suggested earlier:  

1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
   - *Dish*: Garlic-butter shrimp with pasta.  
   - *Ingredients*:  
     - 1 lb large shrimp (peeled/deveined)  
     - 8 oz pasta (e.g., linguine)  
     - 4 tbsp butter  
     - 4 garlic cloves (minced)  
     - ½ cup white wine (optional)  
     - ½ cup heavy cream  
     - 1 tsp red pepper flakes  
     - Salt/pepper to taste  
     - Fresh parsley (chopped, for garnish)  

2. **Spicy Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
   - *Dish*: Tomato-based tomato sauce with Italian seasoning.  
   - *Ingredients*:  
     - 1 lb shrimp (sliced)  
     - 2 cups crushed tomatoes  
     - 4 garlic cloves (minced)  
     - 1 tsp red pepper flakes  
     - 2 tbsp olive oil  
     - ¼ cup dry white wine  
     - Italian seasoning (1 tsp)  
     - Salt/pepper to taste  
     - Fresh basil (garnish)  

3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
   - *Dish*: Spicy shrimp over creamy grits.  
   - *Ingredients*:  
     - 1 lb shrimp  
     - 1 cup quick-cooking grits  
     - 2 cups chicken broth  
     - 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning  
     - 1 bell pepper (diced)  
     - 1 onion (sliced)  
     - 2 cloves garlic (minced)  
     - 2 tbsp butter  
     - Hot sauce (to taste)
