Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your TV, planters, and shelves—prioritizing safety and efficiency:

### 📺 1. **Mounting the TV**
   - **Check Wall Type**: Use a stud finder to locate studs. If mounting on drywall, use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for your TV’s weight.
   - **Measure Twice**: Mark the exact center of the TV’s screen and the TV mount bracket. Use a level to ensure straightness.
   - **Secure the Bracket**: Attach the bracket to the wall with anchors/screws. For heavy TVs (55" or larger), ensure the bracket is rated for that weight.
   - **Mount the TV**: Secure the TV to the bracket using included screws. Avoid over-tightening.

### 🌿 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Choose Lightweight Pots**: Ceramic or plastic pots reduce weight. Ensure pots won’t tip if bumped.
   - **Use Heavy-Duty Hooks**: Install L-shaped hooks or S-hooks rated for hanging plants. For heavier pots, use a hanging shelf instead.
   - **Install at Eye Level**: Hang planters where they’re visible but not obstructive. Secure hooks to studs or use toggle bolts for drywall.
   - **Add Drainage**: Place saucers under planters to catch water.

### 🪢 3. **Installing Floating Shelves**
   - **Find Studs**: Use a stud finder with a nail sensor. If no studs, use toggle bolts or heavy-duty anchors.
   - **Measure & Level**: Mark shelf positions at eye level (typically 18–24" above the floor). Use a level to ensure straight.
   - **Attach with Anchors**: Drive screws through the shelf into studs or anchors. For shelves over 20 lbs, use two screws per shelf.
   - **Test Stability**: Gently shake shelves after installation. Add extra anchors if needed.

### 🔧 **Key Tips**
   - **Safety First**: Wear safety glasses. Use a step stool or ladder securely placed.
   - **Tools Needed**: Level, stud finder, drill, measuring tape, screwdriver, anchors (if needed).
   - **Time-Saving**: Dedicate 2–3 focused Sunday sessions (e.g., 90-minute blocks) to avoid fatigue.
   - **Avoid Overloading**: Never exceed shelf weight limits. Distribute weight evenly.

### ⚠️ **Avoid Landlord Violations**
   - Use wall anchors instead of drilling into studs if allowed (confirm rules first).
   - Keep noise low by pre-drilling pilot holes and using power tools briefly.

### 💡 Pro Tip
Take photos during installation to remember where items go! Once installed, use decorative items (plants, mirrors, or artwork) to break up wall space and reduce visual clutter.
