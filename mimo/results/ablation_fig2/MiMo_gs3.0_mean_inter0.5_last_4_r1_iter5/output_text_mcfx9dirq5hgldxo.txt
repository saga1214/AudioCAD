For Madrid, tapas are a great place to start. Think small, savory dishes like patatas bravas—that's basically fried potatoes with a spicy sauce—or jamón serrano, which is a type of cured ham.

Paella is famous, too, but it might be easier to find a good tapas bar than a paella restaurant there. And if you have a sweet tooth, you've gotta try churros, which are these fried dough pastries, usually dipped in thick hot chocolate.

Enjoy your business trip and the culinary adventure in Madrid!
