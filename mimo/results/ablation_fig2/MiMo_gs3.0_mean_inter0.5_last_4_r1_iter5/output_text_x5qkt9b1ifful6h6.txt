Based on your biology class study and broader health research, **ultra-processed foods (UPFs)** emerge as the category of food considered *most detrimental if consumed excessively*. Here's why:

1. **Nutrient Deficiency + Harmful Additives**:  
   UPFs (like sugary snacks, frozen meals, and packaged snacks) are often stripped of fiber, vitamins, and minerals while packed with unhealthy fats, added sugars, sodium, and artificial additives. This imbalance disrupts metabolism and gut health.

2. **Hyper-Palatability**:  
   They're engineered to trigger overconsumption, leading to weight gain and obesity—key drivers of diabetes, heart disease, and fatty liver.

3. **Systemic Health Risks**:  
   Excessive UPF intake is linked to **inflammation**, **insulin resistance**, and **impaired brain function**, outpacing the risks from isolated components like sugar or salt alone.

4. **Ubiquity in Modern Diets**:  
   Unlike artificial trans fats (now banned in many countries), UPFs are pervasive and often disguised as "healthy" (e.g., "reduced-fat" cookies).

While sugar, trans fats, and sodium are critical, they are *components* often found *within* UPFs. The **cumulative impact** of UPFs makes them the overarching concern.
