Based on market presence, variety, and global recognition, the **top three car brands known for sedans** are generally considered to be:

1.  **Toyota:** Universally renowned for a vast range of reliable and practical sedans across all segments (compact, midsize, full-size) like the Corolla, Camry, Avalon, and Crown. Their strength lies in reliability, efficiency, and longevity.
2.  **Honda:** Highly respected for sedans offering an excellent blend of performance, efficiency (especially in hybrids like the Accord Hybrid), and driver-focused engineering. Models like the Accord and Civic Sedan are major players.
3.  **BMW:** The quintessential luxury performance sedan brand. BMW is synonymous with sedans offering driving dynamics, premium interiors, advanced technology, and a wide range from the 3 Series to the 7 Series. While more expensive, they define the performance-luxury sedan segment.

**Why these three?**

*   **Toyota & Honda:** Dominate the mass-market sedan space with high-volume, popular, and reliable models meeting diverse needs (commuting, family, efficiency).
*   **BMW:** Dominates the premium/luxury sedan space, setting benchmarks for performance, technology, and brand prestige.

**Honorable Mentions (often in the top 5):**

*   **Hyundai:** Offers excellent value and modern design in sedans like the Elantra and Sonata, gaining significant market share.
*   **Kia:** Similar to Hyundai, with well-equipped sedans like the Forte and Storia.
*   **Volkswagen:** Strong presence in North America with the Passat and in Europe with the Golf and Arteon.
*   **Lexus:** Toyota's luxury brand, known for ultra-refined and comfortable sedans like the ES and LS.

So, while brands like Hyundai, Kia, and Volkswagen also have strong sedan offerings, **Toyota, Honda, and BMW** remain the most consistently recognized and dominant brands specifically associated with sedans globally.
