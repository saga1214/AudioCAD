Drywall tasks: joint compound small tub maybe $8, sandpaper/sponge $5, nails/caulk gun $10, replacement molding if needed, primer/paint match wall about $20. Cake tasks: All dry flour, powdered sugar, cocoa if chocolate cake... all that stuff should be around $25. Plus pans. Birthday extras: balloons/deco, movie, games... maybe $30. Rough estimate, you'll probably want around $60 to $70 total to be safe, but it could be less depending on sales. Hope that helps!
