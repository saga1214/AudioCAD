### 1. **Solve the Fin Debate: Short Fins for Costa Rica**
   - **Recommendation:** Invest in **short or mid-length fins** for your dream trip.  
     - *Why?* They’re easier on your legs (especially since you struggled with long fins in Honduras), more agile for exploring coral reefs, and perfect for calm coastal dives. Long fins are ideal for long-distance or current-heavy spots later in your diving journey.  
     - *Bonus:* They’re compact—great for travel and boat storage!

### 2. **Costa Rica Itinerary: Dream Trip + Best Diving**
   - **Puntarenas (Pacific Coast):**  
     - **Site:** **Don Juan Shoals** (accessible from Sirena Beach).  
       - *Advantage:* Crystal-clear water, vibrant coral gardens, and frequent sightings of **turtles, rays, and schools of tropical fish**. Short fins shine here for quick maneuvers.  
     - **Site:** **Playa Naranjo** (near Sirena).  
       - *Advantage:* Gentle currents, easy for beginners, and great for macro photography (seaweed, nudibranchs).  
   - **Cocos Island (Offshore):**  
     - **Site:** **Seymour Channel**.  
       - *Advantage:* Iconic **blue holes, shipwrecks**, and **dolphin encounters**. currents can be strong—consider bringing backup long fins if you plan extended dives here.  
     - *Tip:* Book a liveaboard dive boat (e.g., *La Esmeralda* or *Coco Explorer*) for multi-day trips.  

### 3. **Beyond Costa Rica: Unforgettable Tropical Alternatives**
   - **Galápagos Islands (Ecuador):**  
     - *Why:* Warm tropical waters, minimal currents, and **unique wildlife**: sea lions, penguins, hammerhead sharks, and giant kelp forests. Perfect for building confidence.  
     - *Best Site:* **Esperanza Rock** (turtles, manta rays).  
   - **Curaçao (Aruba):**  
     - *Why:* Easy shore dives, shallow walls, and **shipwrecks** (e.g., *SS Antilla*). Water is famously clear.  
     - *Best Site:* **Shark Hall** (hundreds of barracuda and schools of fish).  
   - **Bonaire (Dutch Caribbean):**  
     - *Why:* World-class shore diving with **100+ dive sites**. Protected reefs mean abundant marine life.  
     - *Best Site:* **Bargibat** (calm, colorful reefs; ideal for beginners).  

### 4. **Pro Tips for an Epic Trip**
   - **Gear:** Rent short fins in Costa Rica to test them. Bring reef-safe sunscreen (zinc oxide-based) to protect coral.  
   - **Logistics:**  
     - Book early: Costa Rican dive boats fill up fast.  
     - Consider a **5-day liveaboard** in Cocos Island for remote island magic.  
   - **Wildlife Focus:** Target sites with high marine life chances (e.g., **Papagayo Peninsula, Nicaragua**—calm waters, whales in winter).  

### 5. **If You Ever Try Temperate Diving**
