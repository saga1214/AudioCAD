Based on your situation, here are the best non-tearing solutions for sticking fabric, especially on a winter jacket:

### Top Recommendations:
1. **Glue Dots**  
   - **Why it works:** Pressure-sensitive adhesive dots that stick firmly without tearing fabric.  
   - **Best for:** Medium-weight fabrics like fleece or polyester.  
   - **Tip:** Use a low-temperature glue gun if you need extra hold (avoid high heat to prevent melting lining).

2. **Iron-On Tape (Double-Sided Tape)**  
   - **Why it works:** Specialized tape for fabrics (e.g., *Gaffer Tape* or *Sew Shure*).  
   - **How to use:** Iron it on briefly to activate the adhesive.  
   - **Best for:** Decorative accents or lightweight fabric.

3. **Velcro (Hook & Loop Fastener)**  
   - **Why it works:** Reversible, no adhesive residue, and strong hold.  
   - **Best for:** Replacing torn closures or securing loose fabric panels.

### If You Must Sew:
- **Hand-sewing:** Use a **needle and polyester thread** (it won’t shrink).  
  - **Tip:** Knot the thread before stitching to secure ends.  
- **Sewing Machine:** Use a **walking foot** to handle thick layers without puckering.  
  - **Stitch type:** **Straight stitch** at low tension to avoid damaging fabric.

### Key Tips to Avoid Tearing:
- **Test first:** Try on scrap fabric or inside the lining (if accessible).  
- **Avoid plastic adhesives:** They melt or tear fabric.  
- **For bulky areas:** Use a **needle and thread** for invisibility.  

### Where to Buy Supplies:
- **Online:** Amazon, Etsy (for hand-sewn patches), or fabric stores like Jo-Ann Fabrics.  
- **Local:** Michaels, Hobby Lobby, or a sewing supply store near Arden Fair.  

If the tear is large, a **seam ripper** to remove loose fabric + **darning needle** for patching might help. For a functional jacket, glue dots or Velcro are your safest bets!
