Dallas has some fantastic Asian noodle spots! For ramen, check out Momiji Ramen—they have rich, tonkotsu broth. For pho, Pho 79 is a classic with tender beef. For more casual options, there's Noodle House for a variety of noodle dishes.

As for protein bars, I’d recommend Quest Bars—high protein, low sugar, and taste good. Also, Optimum Nutrition and RXBAR are solid choices if you want something natural and filling.
