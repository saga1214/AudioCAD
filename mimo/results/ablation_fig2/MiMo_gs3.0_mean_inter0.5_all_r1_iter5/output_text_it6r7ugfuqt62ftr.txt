For your family vacation after the birthday, Big Sur is ideal. Warm Mediterranean climate, plenty of swimming spots, lots of activities for all ages, and plenty of gluten-free dining options. Since you have two cars, you can really explore the region.

Here’s a sample 5-day itinerary for Big Sur:

For accommodation, I'd suggest The Post Ranch Inn in Carmel. It's luxurious, has ocean views, and caters well to dietary needs. You'll need to rent two SUVs or minivans for this trip, which you can easily book through services like Enterprise or Hertz.

On Day 1, you'll drive from Northern California to Carmel, which is about a four-hour trip. You can stop in Salinas for lunch at a place like The Cherry Tree Café, which has good gluten-free options. When you arrive, you can relax at the inn's spa.

Day 2 could be a full day in Big Sur. You could start at Pfeiffer Beach for its famous purple sand and swimming. Then, hike to McWay Falls, where a waterfall flows right into the ocean – perfect for diet maintainers showing off swimsuits! You could also drive along the scenic Pacific Coast Highway and stop at the iconic Bixby Bridge. For dinner, Nepenthe has stunning ocean views.

Day 3 could focus on more hiking. You could explore the Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park, which has great waterfalls and pools. Then, visit the iconic McWay Falls viewpoint again. You can pack a picnic lunch from Nepenthe's market.

On Day 4, you could drive up to Pigeon Point Lighthouse, which has beautiful coastal views, and then visit the Hearst Castle. It's a bit of a drive, so you might want to rent an extra car for that specific day. For dinner, you could try The Sardine Factory in Cambria, which is known for its fresh seafood and gluten-free menu.

For your return trip home, you could stop in Santa Barbara for the evening. You can catch the sunset at Stearns Wharf and have dinner at Loquita, a nice Spanish restaurant with many gluten-free options.

This itinerary balances adventure, relaxation, and warm-weather activities. Remember to pack plenty of swimsuits, cover-ups, light clothing, sunscreen, and insect repellent. Enjoy your vacation!
