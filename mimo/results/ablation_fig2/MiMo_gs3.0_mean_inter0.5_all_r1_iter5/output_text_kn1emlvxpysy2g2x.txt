### **High-End Pinot Noir**  
- **Store at:** Cellar temperature (~13°C / 55°F)  
- **Serve at:** Slightly below room temp (55–60°F / 13–16°C)  
- **Best for:** Cool evenings; highlights delicate red fruit and earthy notes.  
- **Weather Tip:** *Cold weather* ideal; *hot weather* requires active chilling.  

### **Japanese Hon-Mirin**  
- **Store at:** Cool place (~16–20°C / 60–68°F)  
- **Serve/Taste at:** Room temp (20–24°C / 68–75°F) if sipped (rarely)  
- **Best for:** Cooking; room temp preserves subtle sweetness and umami.  
- **Weather Tip:** *Cool environments* maintain stability; *heat* may accelerate oxidation.  

### **Italian Red Wine (e.g., Chianti)**  
- **Store at:** 13°C / 55°F  
- **Serve at:** 16–18°C / 60–65°F  
- **Best for:** Balanced body; medium acidity complements grilled meats.  
- **Weather Tip:** *Warm days* (e.g., outdoor dinners) benefit from serving at 18°C.  

### **Italian White Wine (e.g., Pinot Grigio)**  
- **Store at:** 10–13°C / 50–55°F  
- **Serve at:** 7–10°C / 45–50°F  
- **Best for:** Crisp, refreshing whites; ideal with seafood or salads.  
- **Weather Tip:** *Hot days* require ice bucket chilling; *cool nights* preserve freshness.  

### **Hot-Wine (e.g., Mulled Red Wine)**  
- **Store at:** 13°C / 55°F  
- **Serve at:** 60–70°C / 140–160°F  
- **Best for:** Winter gatherings; warming spices and fruit forward.  
- **Weather Tip:** *Cold weather* essential; *heat* risks scalding or flavor loss.
