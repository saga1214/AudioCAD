For candy tomorrow, grab the Halloween versions of classics like Reese’s, Skittles, and M&Ms—those are always safe bets. You could also stock up on gummies, like Sour Patch Kids or gummy worms, and maybe some chocolate bars like Snickers or Milky Way. If you want something budget-friendly, you could look for the pre-packaged Halloween candy bags from stores. Just make sure to check allergy labels again—some candies dye artificial, so opt for brands that advertise dye-free options. Keep the candy in bowls or a Halloween-themed display so it feels festive!
