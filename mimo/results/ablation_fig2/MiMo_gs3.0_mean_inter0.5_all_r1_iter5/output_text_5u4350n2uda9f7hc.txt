That's a great question - and the lack of an elevator makes weight a key factor. Here’s what to consider for lightweight, portable houseplants for your east-facing 5th-floor NYC balcony:

**Lightweight & Portable Options:**
1.  **Succulents (Floor or Table):** Perfect for your needs!
    *   **Varieties:** Echeveria, Sedum, Haworthia, or small Aloe (not the giant medicinal kind).
    *   **Why:** Extremely lightweight (usually 1-3 lbs per small pot), love bright morning sun, incredibly low maintenance. They come in compact, beautiful shapes. Small potted versions are easy to carry.
2.  **Begonias (Table or Floor):**
    *   **Varieties:** Rex Begonia (for colorful, textured leaves) or Angel Wing Begonia (for graceful, trailing wings).
    *   **Why:** Lightweight (1-2 lbs), adore east-facing light, add great visual interest with their leaves. Small container size makes them easy to handle.
3.  **Simple Herbs (Table):**
    *   **Varieties:** Basil, Mint, Thyme, or Oregano.
    *   **Why:** Very lightweight, love morning sun, add a truly homey kitchen/cutting garden vibe. Mint can be vigorous, so keep it in a container.
4.  **Bird's Nest Snake Plant (Sansevieria trifasciata 'Hahnii' or 'Laurentii'):** (Floor)
    *   **Appearance:** As you asked, its leaves grow tightly together from the center into a distinctive, low, rounded "nest" shape. It has smooth, upright green leaves, often with subtle silver-green stripes. It grows slowly and stays compact.
    *   **Why:** Lightweight (2-4 lbs for a small plant), thrives on neglect (perfect for onsite travel!), loves bright indirect light (it will handle the morning sun but may not get scorched), and is incredibly resilient to wind. Its compact form fits perfectly on the floor.
5.  **Pilea Peperomioides (Table or Floor - if space allows):**
    *   **Appearance:** Unique small, round, "lily pad" shaped leaves on thin stems. Comes in green or variegated varieties.
    *   **Why:** Lightweight, loves bright, indirect light (it should be fine on an east balcony), compact and charming. Small potted versions are easy to carry.

**Weight Considerations & Transport:**
*   **Small Pots are Key:** Focus on plants sold as small potted plants at garden centers or nurseries. You want something that feels manageable in one hand.
*   **Succulents & Snake Plants:** These are ideal because even when larger, they are inherently lighter than, say, a large Fiddle Leaf Fig or Monstera. A small Snake Plant will be under 5 lbs easily.
*   **Herbs:** Often the lightest option of all.
*   **Carrying:** Plan to carry one or two plants at a time up/down stairs. A small foldable tote bag makes carrying multiple small plants much easier than trying to juggle them.

**Summary for Your Month in NYC:**

*   **Top East Balcony Picks:** Succulents (Echeveria, Sedum), Bird's Nest Snake Plant, or Rex Begonia.
*   **Best Table Options:** Small Pilea, Herbs (Basil, Mint), or a smaller Snake Plant/Begonia if space allows.
*   **Floor Stays:** Snake Plant, larger succulents, or Begonias.
*   **Why These Work:** Bright morning sun, compact size, low weight (easy transport), resilience to wind, and minimal maintenance during a busy onsite month. They'll feel homey without overwhelming your small space.

You can easily find these at local nurseries or big box stores (like Home Depot, Lowe's, or even Trader Joe's often has Snake Plants and small succulents). Enjoy making your Airbnb feel cozy!
