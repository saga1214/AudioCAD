Here's a quick recap of delicious and safe treats for your little corgi princess, categorized for easy reference:

### 🍪 Homemade Treats (Easy & Customizable)
1.  **Mashed Fruit Bites:** Mix 1 cup mashed pumpkin or sweet potato with 1 egg white (optional for firmness). Spoon into small mounds on a baking sheet, flatten slightly, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15-20 minutes until dry. Freeze in ice cube trays.
2.  **Peanut Butter & Oat Cookies:**
    *   Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
    *   Mix 1 cup rolled oats with 1/4 cup xylitol-free peanut butter.
    *   Drop small spoonfuls onto a baking sheet.
    *   Bake for 10-12 minutes until edges are golden. Cool completely, then break into tiny pieces.
3.  **Carrot Sticks:** Simply wash, peel (optional), and cut raw carrots into thin sticks.

### 🛒 Store-Bought Treats (Convenient & Varied)
1.  **Training Puffs:** Zuke's Mini Naturals or similar small, soft, healthy kibble bites (check for xylitol-free!).
2.  **Soft Bites:** Blue Buffalo Small Training Bites or Wellness Mini Training Bites (look for grain-free or whole ingredient options).
3.  **Specialty:** Greenies Dog Dental Chews (small size) or Merrick Grilled Chicken Bites (cooked plain chicken pieces).
