Here are five fantastic road trip options from Vancouver, each with a mix of beautiful scenery, unique activities, and excellent food, just like you asked for.

First, for a quick escape, you can drive about two hours to the Okanagan Valley. You can visit the beautiful lakes, like Kellon Lake, and enjoy wine tasting. For food, you should try the local Okanagan cherries and the famous Pato Pies.

Second, for a classic trip, you can head to the Fraser Canyon. You can hike in the Lillooet area, see the famous Lillooet Suspension Bridge, and even take a short detour to the Cariboo region. For food, you can enjoy a traditionalBC bison steak and some local craft beer.

Third, if you want a trip to the mountains, you can drive to Whistler, which is about two and a half hours away. Even in the summer, you can enjoy the stunning scenery, go hiking, and visit the Olympic Park. For food, you should definitely try the famous Black Bear Bistro.

Fourth, for a more remote adventure, you can head to the Kootenay region. The drives through Kootenay and Revelstoke are absolutely breathtaking. You could even go kayaking on the Kootenay Lake. For food, you can try theBC bison and the local craft breweries.

Finally, for a coastal trip, you can drive to the Sunshine Coast. You can visit the stunning Hornby Island, see the amazing tides at Tawason Beach, and explore the town of Semiahmup. For food, you can enjoy fresh seafood and some of the best craft beer in the province.

Enjoy your road trips!
