That line of reasoning about genetic identity still doesn't quite work.

See, for the egg to have the DNA of the first chicken, the hen who laid it must have had the chicken's DNA. But true chickens don't go back very far in evolution. The creature that laid that original egg was a red junglefowl, which is not technically a chicken. It only became a chicken once the chick hatched from that egg. So, the first egg with the DNA of a chicken came from a junglefowl, but the first *chicken* to live was the chick that hatched from it. That makes the chicken the first of its kind.
