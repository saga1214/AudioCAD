Here's a quick recap using your child-friendly analogy:

1.  **Heart:** The Gas Station *and* the Engine
    *   Pumps the blood-cars.
    *   Holds oxygen-fuel for refueling trips.
    *   Sends blood-cars out on the big roads (arteries) and welcomes them back for refueling in the lungs/gas station.

2.  **Lungs:** Fresh Fuel Stop
    *   Where the blood-cars *first* pick up their oxygen "fuel."

3. Muscles/Organ Cells: The Delivery Destinations
    *   Where the blood-cars drop off their precious oxygen "fuel."

4.  **Veins:** The Return Highways
    *   The roads the blood-cars take *back* to the heart/lungs gas station after dropping off their fuel.

5.  **Capillaries:** Tiny Delivery Streets
    *   The super-small roads that get the blood-cars right up to every single "house" (cell) to make the oxygen drop-off.

6.  **Kidneys:** The Blood-Car Wash & Drain
    *   The checkpoint where blood-cars get cleaned: waste and extra water are washed off.
    *   The "junk" (waste, extra water) that gets washed off flows out as urine.

7.  **Liver:** The Fuel & Waste Processing Station
    *   Acts like a service center: sorts useful stuff (nutrients) from harmful stuff, processes it, and makes sure everything runs smoothly.
