   - A soft throw blanket or a warm hat/mittens (if it’s chilly).  
   - A scented candle (e.g., "fresh linen" or "floral") for relaxation after the performance.  
   *Why it works:* Shows care for her comfort post-show.

3. **Personalized Keepsake**:

   *Why it works:* Personal and lasting.

### 💬 Low-Cost but Meaningful:
- **Handwritten Note**:  
  *"So proud of your hard work! Thursday’s your night—let’s celebrate!"*  
  *Why it works:* Effort > cost. She’ll cherish it.

- **Plant or Potted Herb**:  
   - A succulent ("This grows like your confidence!") or basil/mint for cooking.  
   *Why it works:* Symbolizes growth + ties to your food tour!

- **Fragile items** (vases, glassware) – risk of breakage in crowds.  
- **Perishables** (flowers, large cakes) – unless you can deliver immediately after the show.  
- **Overly large gifts** – she might struggle to carry them.
