Alright, here is a balanced weekly practice schedule designed to be manageable and effective. The key is consistency and variety to prevent stress.

I suggest you practice for about 15 to 30 minutes each day. Let's break it down:

On Monday, you can focus on two-beat three-ball drills. That means practicing those first three throws I mentioned, without catching.

Tuesday is all about one-hand two-ball mastery. Really refine that rhythm in your strongest hand.

For Wednesday, you can use tennis balls. This will give you a different feel and help with your catch accuracy.

Thursday is for your scarf drills. Practice slow patterns to focus on timing.

On Friday, you can try to combine your two and three-ball skills. Maybe start with two, toss in the third from your other hand, juggle for a few seconds, and repeat.

Saturday is for free practice. Just do whatever feels natural, and don't worry about success.

Finally, Sunday is for rest. It’s important to take a day off to recover and come back fresh.

Remember to start each session gently and celebrate every small win to manage that panic and stress. You promised your son, and you'll figure this out.
