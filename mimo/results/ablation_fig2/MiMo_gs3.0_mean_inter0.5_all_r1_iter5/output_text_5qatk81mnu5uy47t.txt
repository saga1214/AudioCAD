**Phase 1: Gear & Prep (Before You Go)**
1.  **Confirm Short Fins Choice:** Based on our chat and your beginner status, invest in good-quality **short fins**. Brands like ScubaPro, Aqualung, or Finis are popular.
2.  **Dive Shop Research:** Costa Rica is dive paradise! Research reputable shops near your main bases:
    *   **Tortuguero:** Small boats, serene, incredible wildlife (sea turtles, manatees!). Great for beginners. (e.g., Paddle & Dive, Tortuga Divers)
    *   **Puerto Viejo (Tabacón):** Good variety, often includes thermal vents. (e.g., Tabacón Divers, Caribbean Divers)

3.  **Check Diving Level:** Ensure your certification (PADI/SSI) covers the depth and type of dives offered at your chosen locations. Ask shops about beginner-friendly guides.
4.  **Travel Logistics:** Book flights/accommodation. Consider diving tours vs. liveaboards. Tours offer convenience; liveaboards offer more remote sites (like Cahuita or Siquirres).
5.  **Health & Gear:** Book dive medicals if needed. Pack reef-safe sunscreen, dive computer, mask, fins, wetsuit (7mm likely for cooler nights/waist-down), dive bag, and good shoes. Don't forget your scuba certification card!

**Phase 2: Choosing Your Costa Rica Base (Dive Focus)**
*   **Tortuguero:** Best for **wildlife & shallow, calm waters**. Think sea turtles, manatees, shallow reefs, mangrove walls. Perfect for beginners. Accessible by boat or canoe. (e.g., 5-day dive trip).
*   **Puerto Viejo (Tabacón Thermal Vents):** Famous for the **boiling mud pots and geothermal activity** on the coast. Good for beginners and intermediate divers. (e.g., 4-day trip including thermal vents).
*   **Manuel Antonio:** Good **mix of reefs, wrecks, and occasional pelagics**. Closer to San José airport. (e.g., 4-day trip).
*   **Playa Conchal (Limón):** For **deeper walls, more advanced dives, and wrecks**. Best for intermediate/advanced. (e.g., 4-5 day liveaboard or intensive course).

**Phase 3: Must-See Dive Sites (Examples)**
*   **Tortuguero:** Bahía Cachí, Playa Naranjo, Tortuga Point.
*   **Puerto Viejo (Tabacón):** Tabacón Thermal Vents, Playa Chiquita, Playa Conchal (shallow areas).
*   **Manuel Antonio:** Santa Maria Island, Playa Chiquita, La Cachara.
*   **Playa Conchal:** Punta Islita (deep wall), Tulemar (wreck), Playa Conchal (shallow reefs).

**Phase 4: Making it Unforgettable**
1.  **Wildlife Focus:** Prioritize Tortuguero for turtle spotting. Tell your dive guide you want to look for them!
2.  **Unique Experience:** Do the **Thermal Vents** in Puerto Viejo – it's a unique geological dive.
3.  **Wrecks:** Try to include a wreck dive (like Tulemar near Conchal or others available depending on your level) – it's like an underwater museum.
4.  **Pelagics:** Ask your guide about chances to see turtles (leatherbacks!), dolphins, or sharks. Pelagics are always exciting!
5.  **Night Dives:** Many sites offer incredible nocturnal life. It's a different and magical experience.
6.  **Relax & Soak In:** Balance diving with beach time, hiking, wildlife watching (hats off to your Honduras memory!), and enjoying Costa Rica's beauty.

**Phase 5: Pro Tips**
*   **Book Early:** Especially for liveaboards and popular sites.
*   **Communicate:** Tell your dive pros you're beginners. They'll slow down and explain things.
*   **Respect Marine Life:** Observe from a distance, don't touch anything, follow guide instructions.
*   **Stay Hydrated:** Sun and saltwater are dehydrating!
*   **Embrace the Adventure:** Costa Rica diving is about the experience, not just the gear. Enjoy the scenery, the water, and the feeling of being underwater!
