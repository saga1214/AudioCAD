### **Practical Tips**
1. **Transport:**  
   - Use **JR Pass** if staying >7 days (covers Shinkansen between cities).  
   - **IC Cards** (Suica/Pasmo) for local trains/buses.  
2. **Work Coursework:**  
   - Dedicate **20-30 mins daily** (post-dinner/workouts) to:  
     - Collect images of calligraphy, ukiyo-e, ceramics.  
     - Test fonts (e.g., traditional brushstroke fonts like *Han Sans*).  
     - Plan color schemes (e.g., ukiyo-e’s muted blues/reds vs. kimono vibrancy).  
3. **Hotel Communication:**  
   - Use **Google Translate’s camera mode** for menus/signs.  
   - Call hotels using **iTalki** or **WhatsApp** (many have English support).  
4. **Taxi Costs:**  
   - Short trips: ~¥3,000–¥5,000 (e.g., Nara day trip from Kyoto).  
   - Budget tip: Use **Taxis by the minute** for city hops (¥1,000/minute).

### **Art History Slide Design Focus**
- **Ukiyo-e:** Use bold images of Hokusai’s *The Great Wave* or Hiroshige’s prints.
