Okay, let's put this all together into a clear, step-by-step format. For your contact information, I agree moving it directly below your name is best. Order your tech: LinkedIn link first, then email, phone, city.大小可以用10到11号字体图标。字体用12号。

Experience is key.把最相关的10到15年经验放在你的“相关经验”部分，用最简洁的方式列出公司、职位和日期。然后在“其他工作经历”部分，简要列出不太相关的职位，只写公司、职位和日期就行。

Education部分，把在读学位放在最上面。对于多个学位，把学校名称写一次，然后列出所有学位，从最相关的开始。学术荣誉和奖项就直接写在对应的学位下面。

Skills section is crucial. Make it a separate section after your experience. List hard skills from the job description first, then soft skills. Keep it concise.

Finally, your Professional Summary is your hook. Write a one-to-two-sentence paragraph that connects your most relevant past experience to your target role.

This structure should give you a professional, focused resume that makes it easy for recruiters to see your value, regardless of your career path.
