Organizing multiple passions and financial priorities is all about creating a clear, step-by-step plan.

First, group your passions that align with your financial priorities—for Bolivia salt flats or apartment down payments—or passions that align with separate financial goals, like home renovations.

Second, break each goal into smaller, more specific tasks. For Bolivia, maybe you research flights one week and budget for it the next. For an apartment, you could open a dedicated savings account and set a small monthly amount.

By treating each goal as a separate 'project' with its own small steps, you can make consistent progress on both without feeling overwhelmed.
