The wood spoon incident is definitely a one-time lesson, so you're safe there. Here’s the secret to good stir-fry noodles: you want to cook them separately.

First, boil your noodles, but once they're done, drain them very well. Then, rinse them under cold water. This stops the cooking process immediately, which keeps them from getting mushy.

Then, just toss them in your stir-fry sauce right at the very end to coat them. That way, they stay nice and chewy. You've got this!
