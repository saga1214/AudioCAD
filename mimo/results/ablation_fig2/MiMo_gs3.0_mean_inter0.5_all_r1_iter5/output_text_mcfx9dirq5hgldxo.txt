Adobo Philly is fantastic for late-night food. I'd really recommend trying one of three things.

First, Philly Cheesesteak. You could go to a classic spot like John's Roast Pork or try a late-night place like Geno's.

Or, uh, you could get wings. John's Roast Pork is famous for their wings, and a place like Tony Luke's is great for late-night eats too.

And finally, there are always late-night sandwiches. John's Roast Pork is open until about midnight, so you could grab a roast pork sandwich.

Just... a little tip, Philly has a really active anti-littering police force. So, please, make sure to keep your trash in the bag. 

Enjoy your business trip and the great food in both Madrid and Philly! Safe travels.
