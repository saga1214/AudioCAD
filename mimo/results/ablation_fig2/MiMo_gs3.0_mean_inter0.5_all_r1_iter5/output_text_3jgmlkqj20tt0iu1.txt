Initally, you could try scheduling a one-on-one meeting through an email. Something simple like, 'Hi, when you have a few moments, could we schedule a brief one-on-one to discuss my progress?' This approach is a bit more formal and can help reduce the anxiety of a spontaneous request.

As for the conversation itself, it's a good idea to frame the request as part of a broader discussion about your professional development. You could start by asking something like, 'I'm really excited about how things have been going lately and wanted to discuss my role and growth with you. Based on my contributions since joining the team, I was hoping we could explore what my career path might look like here.' This keeps the conversation positive and forward-looking.
