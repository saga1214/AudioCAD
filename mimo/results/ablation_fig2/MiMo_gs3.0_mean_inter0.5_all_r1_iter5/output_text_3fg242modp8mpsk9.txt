Okay, since you love creativity and romance, here are a few ideas.

First, you could look for a catamaran tour that visits sea caves. Some have smooth, sheltered areas that wouldn't trigger sea sickness.

Another idea is a private kayak trip at sunrise. Paddling side-by-side forces quiet, intimate conversation, and the view is beautiful.

For dining, many places offer sunset dinners on a small, stable boat. The food is usually fresh seafood, and the atmosphere is very romantic.

And finally, you could check if any local charter companies offer stand-up paddleboarding with a picnic. It's a peaceful way to explore the coastline together.
