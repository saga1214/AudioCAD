Calgary is a great base for adventure. Driving about 45 minutes east, you can go to Banff or Canmore and hike to a beautiful waterfall or visit Johnston Canyon. A bit further, about an hour and a half, you could drive to the Kicking Horse Canyon in Siang. For something a little more unique, about an hour and a half north, there's the Bruce Canyon in Okotoks, which is a scenic gorge with waterfalls. And if you're up for a quick drive, about 45 minutes southwest, you can visit the Elbow Lake Provincial Park. It's a lovely, peaceful lake where you can swim and hike.
