Here are the classic recipes with exact measurements, perfect for your Art Deco airport cocktail reception in Kentucky:

### **1. Old Fashioned**
*   **Ingredients:**
    *   2 oz Bourbon or Rye Whiskey
    *   1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup
    *   4-6 dashes Angostura Bitters
    *   Orange twist for garnish (plus optional cherry)
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Muddle the sugar cube (or stir simple syrup) in a mixing glass.
    2.  Add whiskey and bitters. Stir with ice for 20-30 seconds until chilled and sugar is dissolved.
    3.  Discard ice from glass. Add fresh ice.
    4.  Stir again for 15-20 seconds.
    5.  Strain into an Old Fashioned (Rocks) glass over fresh ice.
    6.  Express orange twist over drink (squeeze oils into foam), wipe rim if desired, then drop in.

### **2. Paper Plane**
*   **Ingredients:**
    Coupe Glass
    *   2 oz London Dry Gin
    *   ¾ oz Maraschino Liqueur (e.g., Luxardo)
    *   ¼ oz Crème de Violet
    *   ¾ oz fresh Lemon Juice
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Combine all ingredients in a shaker filled with ice.
    2.  Shake vigorously for 15-20 seconds until well-chilled and slightly frothy.
    3.  Double strain (using a Hawthorne + fine mesh strainer) into a chilled Coupe glass over a large ice cube or large block of ice.
    4.  **Garnish:** Cherry or orange twist (optional, violet adds its own aroma).

### **3. French 75** (Recommended Gin Cocktail)
Coupe or Champagne Flute
*   **Ingredients:**
    *   ¾ oz Dry Gin (e.g., Tanqueray, Bombay Sapphire)
    *   ¾ oz fresh Lemon Juice
    *   ½ oz Simple Syrup
    *   2-3 oz Champagne or Brut Sparkling Wine
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Shake gin, lemon juice, and syrup with ice for 10-15 seconds until chilled.
    2.  Double strain into a chilled Champagne flute over ice.
    3.  Top gently with Champagne/Sparkling Wine.
    4.  **Garnish:** Lemon twist (express oils over drink, drop in).

**Key Notes for Hosting:**

*   **Egg White in Whiskey Sour:** As requested, here's the technique:
    *   Muddle ½ egg white in shaker.
    *   Add bourbon, lemon juice, simple syrup. **Dry Shake** (shake without ice) for 15-20 seconds until frothy.
    *   Add ice. Shake again vigorously for 15-20 seconds.
    *   Double strain into rocks glass. Garnish.
    *   *This is a classic technique (dating back to early 20th century bartending books), not a modern invention. It creates a luxurious texture.*
