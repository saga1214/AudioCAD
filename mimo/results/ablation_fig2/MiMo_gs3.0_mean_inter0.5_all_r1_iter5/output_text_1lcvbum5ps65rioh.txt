### U-Turn Rules in BC

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients
For 4 servings:  
- **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti (or bucatini)  
- **Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 1 egg yolk  
- **Cheese**: 150g grated Pecorino Romano (or Parmesan)  
- **Cured Ham**: 150g diced guanciale (or pancetta)  
- **Garlic**: 1 clove (optional)  
- **Black Pepper**: Freshly ground  
- **Salt**: For pasta water  

### Simple Steps:  
1. Boil pasta in salted water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
2. Fry diced ham and garlic in rendered fat until crisp.  
3. Whisk eggs, yolk, cheese, and pepper. Toss hot pasta with ham, off-heat.  
4. Add egg mixture slowly, mixing vigorously. Add pasta water as needed for creaminess.  

**Pro Tip**: Never add raw eggs to hot pasta directly—it can scramble! Serve immediately. Enjoy your meal! 🍝
