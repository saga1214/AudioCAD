Sure! First consider breeds known for being chill and good with kids, like Labrador Retrievers, Golden Retrievers, or Cavalier King Charles Spaniels. If purebreds aren’t a dealbreaker, look into mixed breeds at rescues that combine these traits. Always research the breed’s temperament, energy, and any quirks, like chasing squirrels. Next, assess your apartment—some breeds are better for high-rises than others. Next, check landlord approval again to confirm size and noise concerns still align. Once you find a candidate, evaluate their living situation to ensure they’ll be happy. Don’t forget to ask the landlord or shelter staff about the dog’s history. Take your time—finding the right fit is key to a great dog care experience.
