Okay, this is a critical safety issue. I need to be very clear and direct about this. The safety of car ramps is not about the car model, it's about the surface they are on.

Let me just say this as clearly as possible. Using car ramps on an uneven surface is incredibly dangerous. It doesn't matter if your friend's car and your car are identical. The ramps themselves might have a maximum weight limit, but the real danger comes from the car rolling. Even a slight slope can cause the car to roll, which could send it right over the ramps or onto the person underneath. It's a huge risk.

You absolutely must use the ramps on a perfectly flat and level surface. Your driveway might seem flat, but a tiny slope is enough to be a problem. Please, for your own safety, do not use the ramps on any kind of incline. The right way to do this is to find a completely level spot in your yard or a safe garage space. That's the only way to be sure you're safe.
