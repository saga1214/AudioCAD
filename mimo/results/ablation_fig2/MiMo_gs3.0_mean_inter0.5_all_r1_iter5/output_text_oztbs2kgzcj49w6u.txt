Yes, there's a perfect place for you to go. The Lockport Canal Museum and the nearby Lockport Underground Railroad Heritage Center are both excellent resources. The museum has a great exhibit on the locks, showing how they worked and featuring artifacts from the canal's heyday. You can even see a replica of a canal boat.

The Underground Railroad Center tells the powerful story of the canal's role in helping people escape slavery. It's a very moving and important part of the city's history. It sounds like you'll have a wonderful vacation exploring your backyard. Enjoy summer!
