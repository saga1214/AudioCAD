Alright, here is a nautical-themed birthday shopping list for seven seven-year-old pirate-loving guests plus your birthday boy, so for a total of eight kids at the beach. I've focused on finger foods that minimize sand exposure, avoided watermelon, and added pirate-themed touches. I've also noted沙子无关的包装来增强便利性。

**Food:**

*   **Themed Savory Snacks:**
    *   1 lb deli turkey or chicken breast, sliced
    *   1 lb cheese, such as cheddar or mozzarella sticks
    Use for making mini sliders or rolls (cut small).
*   **“Buried Treasure” Sliders/Wraps (8 servings total):**
    *   8 small slider buns or 4 regular rolls, cut into quarters
    *   1 lb deli turkey or chicken, sliced
    *   1 lb cheese, such as cheddar or mozzarella sticks
    *   Hummus or pirate “gravy” for dipping (optional)
    *   Small knives and plastic wrap for easy serving
*   **Veggies & Themed Dippers (Sand-free portioning):**
    *   1 large cucumber, cut into thick rounds or sticks
    *   1 large bell pepper, cut into strips
    *   1 cup cherry tomatoes (halved if desired)
    *   1/2 cup carrot sticks
    *   1 pint baby carrots
    *   Individual small cups or sealed snack bags as dippers
*   **Cheese & Crackers:**
    *   12 mozzarella sticks or cubes
    *   24 goldfish crackers (for the “gold” theme)
    Pack into sealed pirate treasure chest snack bags.
*   **Fruit “Jewels” (Excludes Watermelon):**
    *   4 cups grapes (red, green, or mixed)
    *   2 cups pineapple chunks (fresh or canned in juice)
    *   2 cups berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries - washed)
    *   1 pint mandarin oranges (in juice, drained)
    Wash and cut grapes/berries/pineapple. Pack berries in small cups.
*   **Treats:**
    *   Pretzel rods (12 rods) - theme as ship masts
    *   Mini chocolate chips (for “mermaid scales” or “gold dust” - optional sprinkle)

**Drinks:**

*   Individual Juice Boxes (2 per child = 16 boxes) - Tropical punch, apple, or fruit punch flavors
*   Individual Water Bottles (2 per child = 16 bottles) - Clear or colored blue water
*   Ice packs to keep drinks cold
*   Small bowls for shared drinks if desired (e.g., punch station with large pitcher)

**Pirate-Themed Packaging & Extras:**

*   Mini treasure chests or labeled snack bags (for cheese/goldfish, fruit skewers)
*   Fruit skewers: Melon baller, small knife, wooden skewers
*   Small cups for berries and yogurt parfaits
*   Small napkins (pirate flag designs recommended)
*   labels or stickers to mark allergy-safe items

**沙子无关的包装策略：** All items should be served in sealed containers, individual boxes, or bags to prevent direct contact with sand. Skewers are fun but should be provided with non-sandproof handles or gloves for handling. 

This list provides a balanced menu with pirate-themed names and packaging, while minimizing sand exposure through sealed portions and easy-to-handle foods at the beach. Enjoy the nautical party!
