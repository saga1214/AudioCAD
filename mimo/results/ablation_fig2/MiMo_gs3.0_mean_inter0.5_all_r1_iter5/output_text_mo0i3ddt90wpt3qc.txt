You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is a great gluten-free foundation. Along those same dietary needs, rice cakes with almond butter are a very popular gluten-free camp snack.

Another gluten-free option could be trail mix. Many brands, like a brand called Nature's Pantry, make gluten-free versions that you can find in stores, and those work great for a mix of nuts and seeds.
