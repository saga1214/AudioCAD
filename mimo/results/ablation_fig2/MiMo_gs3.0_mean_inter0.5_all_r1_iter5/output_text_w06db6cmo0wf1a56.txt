Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's a lot of people! Keeping things healthy, mess-free, and cooler-stackable is a brilliant goal. Sugar-free sodas and actual Italian calzones? Challenge accepted! Let's build a battle plan.

First, drinks are your hydration foundation. Load up on reusable water bottles for everyone—that's non-negotiable. For variety without the sugar crash, pack low-sugar juice boxes specifically for the kids. Then, for the adult dads craving carbonation, sugar-free sodas are perfect. Just make sure to buy a mix of cans or bottles so everyone can grab one directly from the cooler without needing extra spill-prone bottles. Infused water is another great healthy option: just add some lemon slices, cucumber, or berries to water bottles the night before. It keeps everyone hydrated without the mess.

Snacks should be grab-and-go mess monsters slayers. Cheese sticks are a dad and kid crowd-pleaser. Grapes are easy finger food. Single-serve pretzels or trail mix bags minimize crumb chaos. Baby carrots with portable single cups of hummus is another win. You could also throw in whole-grain crackers or rice cakes for a light adult snack between calzone meals.

For the main meal, since your friend loves calzones, focusing on pre-made options is definitely the mess-free winner. You have a few good choices here. Option one is pizza rolls or mini calzones from the freezer section. They're cheap, easy to grab, and reheat well in a cooler with ice packs. Option two is checking a local pizzeria. Just call ahead and ask if they do pre-made, individually wrapped calzones. That would be the most authentic and cleaner option. Option three is calzones from a bakery. They'll be fresh and delicious, but make sure they're pre-assembled and sliced into individual portions so people can just grab one.

For dessert, to keep it simple and grab-and-go, cupcakes are your best friend. They're festive, already individual, and easy to handle. Individual brownie packs would also work well. Both are way cleaner than a whole cake or pie at a bustling theme park.

So, to quickly recap the cooler strategy: organize everything into individual portions. Drinks in sealed containers, snacks in small bags, pre-portioned food in their own containers. Keep wet items separate from dry items. This way, everyone can just grab what they want and move on with minimal fuss. It sounds like an absolutely fantastic day, and you're planning it perfectly. Have an amazing time!
