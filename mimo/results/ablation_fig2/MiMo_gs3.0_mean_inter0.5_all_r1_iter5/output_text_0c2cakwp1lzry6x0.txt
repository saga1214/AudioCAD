Here's how to install your items while respecting noise rules and ensuring safety:

### 1. **TV Mount Installation**
   - **Plan First**: Measure the wall, mark exact screw holes, and choose a sturdy mount (full-motion or simple tilt).  
   - **Noise-Free Approach**: Use **suction cups** for lightweight mounts (e.g., VIVO Dual-Mount) to avoid drilling. For heavier TVs, rent a drill for 1 hour ($15–$20) and use **pre-drilled anchors** (like toggle bolts) to minimize noise.  
   - **Safety**: Ensure electrical outlets are nearby. If not, use an extension cord or install a GFCI outlet (quiet!).

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Lightweight Options**: Use **command hooks** (rated for 10+ lbs) or **magnetic wall strips** for small plants.  
   - **Heavy Plants**: Rent a drill for 1 hour and install **picture wire** or **heavy-duty hooks**. Avoid drilling drywall—use **toggle bolts** for stability.  
   - **Placement**: Hang near windows for natural light. Avoid corners where wires get tangled.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Drill Safely**: Rent a drill for 1 hour and use **wall anchors** (plastic or toggle bolts). Do this late at night (after 10 PM) or early morning (before 8 AM).  
   - **Placement**: Anchor shelves to studs (use a stud finder app). For non-stud walls, use **heavy-duty shelf brackets** with anchors.  
   - **Decorate Smartly**: Place art, books, or plants on shelves to avoid clutter. Use **LED strip lights** (quiet!) for ambiance.

### Tips for Noise Minimization:
- **Noise-Canceling Headphones**: Use during drilling (rent a quiet one if needed).  
- **Tool Rental**: Rent a drill for just the heavy-duty items (TV/shelves). Lightweight items use non-drill methods.  
- **Timing**: Install during quiet hours (landlord rules respected!).  
- **Safety**: Wear gloves/ear protection. Test weight limits before hanging heavy items.

### Decorating Smartly:
- Sunday Sessions: Dedicate 2–3 hours on Sundays to decorate *before* gym/social plans.  
- Work-Life Separation: Keep decorating tools *away* from your workspace to avoid distractions.  
- Aesthetic Tips:  
  - Use **warm white bulbs** to brighten spaces.  
  - Group art/shelves by color (e.g., blues/greens).  
  - Add texture with rugs or throw pillows.  

**Key Takeaway**: Rent tools for heavy-duty installations, use non-drill methods for lightweight decor, and schedule work around quiet hours. This keeps noise low while transforming your space! 🛠️✨
