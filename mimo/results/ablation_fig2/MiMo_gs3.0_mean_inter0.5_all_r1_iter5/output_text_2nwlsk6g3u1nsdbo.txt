Here's a quick recap of the three shrimp main courses I suggested earlier:  

1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
   *Ingredients:*  
   - 1 lb large shrimp, peeled & deveined  
   - 8 oz linguine  
   - 4 cloves garlic, minced  
   - ½ cup butter  
   - ½ cup dry white wine (optional)  
   - ¼ cup fresh lemon juice  
   - 1 tsp red pepper flakes  
   - ½ tsp salt  
   - ¼ tsp black pepper  

2. **Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
   *Ingredients:*  
   - 1 lb large shrimp, peeled  
   - 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes  
   - 3 cloves garlic, minced  
   - 1 tsp red pepper flakes  
   Allium paste (optional)  
   - 2 tbsp olive oil  
   - Salt & pepper to taste  
   - Fresh parsley for garnish  

3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
   *Ingredients:*  
   - 1 lb large shrimp, peeled  
   - 1 cup quick-cooking grits  
   - 2 cups chicken or veggie broth  
   - 2 tbsp melted butter  
   - ¼ cup grated sharp cheddar  
   - 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning  
   - ½ cup chopped green onions  
   - Hot sauce (for serving)  

All these dishes reheat well and travel nicely in separate containers (shrimp + sauce, grits separately). Let me know if you'd like more details on any of them!
