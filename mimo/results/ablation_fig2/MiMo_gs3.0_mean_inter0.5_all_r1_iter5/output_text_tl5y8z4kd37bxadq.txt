### 🍻 Casual Greek & Austin Favorites:
1. **Luna’s**  
   *Location:* South Congress  
   *Why:* Tiny, family-run spot with **authentic Greek comfort food** (think *saganaki* fried cheese, *pastitsio*, and *horiatiki* salad). No frills, just soulful flavors.  
   *Tip:* Go early—space is cramped but cozy.  

2. **Taverna Larder**  
   *Location:* Rainey Street  
   *Why:* Modern Greek twist with dishes like *stuffed peppers*, *grilled octopus*, and *taramasalata*. Wine list is stellar too!  
   *Vibe:* Lively, artsy, and unpretentious.  

3. **Pecan Lodge**  
   *Location:* West 6th Street  
   *Why:* Not Greek, but their **Texas-style BBQ** is legendary. Try the brisket with *texas toast* and pickles.  
   *Bonus:* Live music often on weekends!  

### 🎵 For Music + Food Lovers:
