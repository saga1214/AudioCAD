Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time to go would probably be late October through November. That’s their *verano*—their autumn season—when all the trees start changing color, especially around the Pacific coast. You’ll see these amazing fiery reds and oranges. If you’re more into the beach vibe, like Cancún, the water is still warm but the crowds are thinner, and you might get some beautiful golden-hour skies. Oh, and if you can swing it, maybe a trip to Oaxaca in September... that’s when they have the Day of the Dead celebrations, which has this incredible, vibrant color palette. Just a thought! Enjoy your trip and all the color inspiration you can find!
