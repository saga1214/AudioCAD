For those cucumbers, you'll need one medium cucumber, sliced, one tablespoon of rice vinegar, one tablespoon of soy sauce, one teaspoon of sesame oil, and a teaspoon of sesame seeds. For the miso soup, you'll need one tablespoon of miso paste, one cup of hot water, one sliced green onion, and one teaspoon of soy sauce. 

Moving on to the main course: Chicken Teriyaki with jasmine rice and stir-fried bell peppers and carrots. You'll need about a pound of boneless, legume-free chicken thighs, cut into strips. For the teriyaki sauce, you'll need a quarter cup of soy sauce, a quarter cup of honey or brown sugar, two tablespoons of rice vinegar, and one tablespoon of grated fresh ginger and minced garlic. For the jasmine rice, you'll need one cup of jasmine rice, two cups of water, and a teaspoon of butter, which is optional for extra richness. For the stir-fried veggies, just two tablespoons of cooking oil, sliced bell peppers and carrots, and another teaspoon of soy sauce.

And finally, for the dessert, either Mango Sticky Rice or homemade Matcha Ice Cream. For the first one, you'll need about one cup of sticky jasmine rice, which you'll soak in water for about 30 minutes and then steam. You'll also need two ripe but firm mangoes, sliced, and a half cup each of full-fat coconut milk and sugar, plus a pinch of salt. For the matcha ice cream, if you have the matcha powder, you'll mix one and a half cups of heavy cream, one can of sweetened condensed milk, two to three tablespoons of matcha powder, and a teaspoon of vanilla extract. Then you just blend it all and freeze it. Hope this helps you make your meal special. Enjoy!
