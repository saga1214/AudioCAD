Here are quick, elegant vodka and Fernet recipes that work with your dinner party setup (keeping soda allergy, formality, and mixed diets in mind):

### 🥃 1. **"Vodka Sparkle" (Soda-Free Mocktail or with Spirit)**
   *   **Base:** 1.5 oz Vodka (neutral, like Grey Goose or Absolut)
   *   **Cucumber-Mint Twist:** 4-5 thin cucumber slices + 4-5 fresh mint leaves (muddled lightly in the shaker)
   *   **Bitterness:** 1/4 oz (1 bar spoon) of Angostura bitters
   *   **Sparkle:** 4-6 oz Chilled Sparkling Water (Club Soda alternative)
   *   **Garnish:** Thin cucumber ribbon, mint sprig.
   *   **How:** Muddle cucumber/mint in shaker. Add vodka, bitters, ice. Shake hard. Strain into ice-filled glass. Top with sparkling water. Stir gently. Garnish.
   *   *Why it works:* Refreshing, visually appealing, works as a mocktail or with vodka. Cucumber/mint feel elegant and palate-cleansing.

### 🥃 2. Food & Drink Bot 1: Fernet Fizz (Adapting the Recipe)
   *   **Base:** 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca (or similar)
   *   **Citrus:** 3/4 oz Fresh Lime Juice
   *   **Sweetener:** 1/4 oz (1 bar spoon) Simple Syrup (or 1/2 oz Fresh Grapefruit Juice for less sweet)
   *   **Bitterness (Optional but Recommended):** 1/4 oz (1 bar spoon) Angostura bitters
   *   **Sparkle:** 4-6 oz Chilled Sparkling Water
   *   **Garnish:** Lime wedge, grapefruit twist.
   *   **How:** Combine all ingredients (except sparkling water) in shaker with ice. Shake hard. Strain into ice-filled glass. Top with sparkling water. Stir gently. Garnish.
   *   *Why it works:* The lime and bitters balance Fernet's strong herbal notes. Simple syrup moderates it. Sparkling water keeps it soda-free. Feels sophisticated.

### 📌 Key Considerations for Both Cocktails
*   **Soda Allergy:** Sparkling water is the clear alternative. *Never* use soda/colas.
*   **Vegetarian/Omnivore:** These recipes are spirit-based, so everyone can enjoy them.
*   **Formality/Ages:** Ingredients (cucumber, mint, lime, bitters, simple syrup) are classic and elegant. The preparation (shaking, straining, garnishing) maintains a refined feel suitable for work-acquaintances across ages.
*   **Fernet Allergy:** If one guest is *also* allergic to Fernet's herbal compounds, simply omit it from this recipe and use the "Vodka Sparkle" mocktail instead, or make a vodka-based drink without Fernet entirely. Clearly label the Fernet Fizz.
*   **Preparation:** Make these ahead of time. Pre-mix the syrup or bitters if needed. Keep the shaker ready. Strain well to avoid pulp.
