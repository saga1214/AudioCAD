For your first family gathering, with mom not liking rice and step-dad allergic to strawberries, sister disliking green things, asparagus/broccoli off you but questionable for kids: roast chicken is solid. Roasted potatoes and roasted carrots avoid rice and green veggies sister dislikes. For that third vegetable side, you need something picky-family safe. Maybe a simple green salad with cucumber and red onion for freshness without being too green, or sautéed corn and zucchini since corn is kid-friendly and zucchini isn't usually considered a strong "green." Also, mac and cheese still works even if mom doesn't do rice.

For your winter girlfriends' gathering wanting comforting hearty food with one vegetarian: barbecue jackfruit is a great plant-based meat alternative. For the meat-eaters, you could do shredded pork carnitas or maybe some sausages. On the potato bar, beans or black beans work for the vegetarian, but for a hearty meat option, pulled pork or carnitas would be perfect.

For the work gathering with lactose-free, two with celiacs, one plant-based diet, requests for both corn and flour tortillas, yuca, and a modified esquites: a taco or fajita bar is a great format. Corn tortillas are naturally gluten-free and plant-based. Flour tortillas can be, but require label checking. Chipotle chicken and carne asada are good protein options for the meat-eaters. Yuca is a great plant-based alternative to beans. The modified esquites without cheese or mayo can work if you use plant-based sour cream and season with lime, chili, and cilantro. Potato side could be roasted sweet potatoes, which are kid-friendly and gluten-free, or a simple boiled potato salad.

Label everything still is key.
