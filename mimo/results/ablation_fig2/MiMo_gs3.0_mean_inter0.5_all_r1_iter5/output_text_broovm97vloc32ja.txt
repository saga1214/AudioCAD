This is a fantastic question. In fact, it could be a huge advantage. If you have both the text and the art, you are in a very strong position.

You have two main options: you can either write a query letter to an agent, explaining that you have the full manuscript and artwork ready, or you can approach a publisher directly. Many publishers are very open to receiving fully completed manuscripts with illustrations, especially for children's books.

If you do decide to go this route, just be prepared to follow their specific submission guidelines. For example, you might need to provide a cover page, a sample of your work, and a detailed letter explaining your vision for the book. But yes, you absolutely can submit a completed package.
