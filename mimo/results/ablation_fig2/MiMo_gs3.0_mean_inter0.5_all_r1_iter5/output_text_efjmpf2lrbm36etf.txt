And for anniversary plans, for a spontaneous and bright vibe, consider a cozy wine bar with small plates or a nice rooftop spot with cocktails. A margarita, a paloma, or even a simple glass of sparkling wine can add that celebratory touch. For the menu, think about sharing some light, flavorful appetizers like bruschetta, or maybe some sushi rolls or gourmet sliders.
