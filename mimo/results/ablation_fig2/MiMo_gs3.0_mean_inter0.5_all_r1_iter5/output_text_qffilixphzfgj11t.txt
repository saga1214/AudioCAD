Bruno avoids apples, bananas, broccoli, but here are some safe green options.

Kale is a great source of calcium. Spinach is also good, but it's best to wash it really well and give it in moderation. 

Cucumber is a hydrating and low-calorie choice. Zucchini or yellow squash is another good one—it's soft and easy to digest.

Finally, sprouted grain bread is a great source of protein and B vitamins.
