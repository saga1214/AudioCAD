You are absolutely right to point this out. Given the chaotic home environment and dogs running around, safety and documentation are critical.

While your connection with him is valuable, for this case, the single most important approach in those first 20 to 30 minutes actually has to be creating a predictable, low-distractor space.

Since sessions alone together are common, try to request that the dogs are kept out of the session area before you arrive. Even clearing a small, clean corner in the room can make a huge difference. Predictability reduces anxiety, anxiety often triggers elopement and aggression, and a cleaner space is much easier to manage. Documenting these environmental concerns with your BCBA is also essential for everyone's safety and the program's success.
