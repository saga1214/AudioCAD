Here's a concise checklist for filming your puppet show yourself, tailored for kid-friendly content:

### 🎥 **Pre-Filming Checklist**
1. **Permission First**  
   - Confirm recording is allowed at the venue (schools hospitals > nursing homes based on your nerves history).

2. **Gear Setup**  
   - Use your phone + tripod (vertical format for YouTube).  
   - Add a lavalier mic if possible (live audio is key for puppetry).  
   - Ensure lighting: Face a window or use a ring light (avoid backlighting).

3. **Puppet & Script Prep**  
   - Test sock puppets on your phone screen for visibility.  
   - Write a short, structured script (5-7 mins) with clear punchlines.  
   - Label characters clearly (e.g., "Melvin the Magnificent" on props).

4. **Performance Tweaks**  
   - Speak slightly slower than usual (video pacing feels different).  
   - Use exaggerated expressions with puppets.  
   - Include a clear ending (kids get distracted).

### 📱 **Post-Filming Essentials**
- **Editing**: Trim dull moments, add subtitles for clarity, and use simple graphics (e.g., character names).  
- **Upload**: Enable YouTube’s "Restricted Mode" to avoid ads.  
- **Titles/Tags**: Use keywords like "sock puppet show," "kids entertainment," and your character names (e.g., "Sloppy Snake Adventure").  
- **Community Guidelines**: Avoid copyrighted music; use royalty-free sound effects.

### 🔒 **Copyright Protection**
- **Copyright Your Characters**: File a copyright notice with the U.S. Copyright Office (https://www.copyright.gov/filing/) for $40. This secures your rights if reused without permission.  
- **Watermarks**: Add subtle text like "© [Your Name]" in the corner of videos.

### 💡 **Growth Tips**
- **Consistency**: Post 1-2 videos weekly.  
- **Engage**: Respond to comments (kids love interaction!).  
- **Repurpose**: Turn clips into TikTok/Reels for reach.

### 🌟 **Why This Works**
You’re already a proven entertainer—self-filming puts *your* service mission in control. The million-view video proved your talent; now you’re curating it authentically. Start simple, stay consistent, and protect your creations.
