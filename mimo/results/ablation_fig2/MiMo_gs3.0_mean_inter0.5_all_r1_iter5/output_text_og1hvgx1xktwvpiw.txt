Oh, I can see the confusion, my apologies for that. Let me clarify. The standard one and a quarter-to-one-to-one Boulevardier is not the equal-parts one; it has whiskey heavier than the vermouth and Campari. The equal-parts Boulevardier is three-quarters bourbon, three-quarters sweet vermouth, and three-quarters Campari. Your friend was right about that one. 

For a Boulevardier using gin, maraschino, Chartreuse, and lime, which I assume is what you meant by Last Word, the recipe is three-quarters of an ounce each of gin, green Chartreuse, Luxardo Maraschino liqueur, and fresh lime juice. Stir and enjoy.
