For a 15-minute break, especially your last one of the day, here are some balanced options that won't leave you feeling jarring:

1. **Gentle Movement (Recommended):**  
   Take a short walk outside or around your space. Focus on deep breathing and noticing your surroundings—this is restorative, not exhausting. Pair it with Oumani Diabaté’s kora music if you enjoy it, as the rhythmic sound complements movement beautifully.

2. **Relaxation Over Meditation:**  
   Since deep meditation feels "jarring," try a **gentle sensory reset**:  
   - Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and focus on the sound of your breath.  
   - Hold your breath for 4 seconds, exhale slowly for 6 seconds (repeat 3x).  
   - Open your eyes and observe 3 things you see, 2 sounds you hear, and 1 sensation you feel.  

3. **Hydration + Light Fuel:**  
   Drink a glass of water with lemon. If you crave coffee, have a *small* cup (e.g., 3 oz) with a piece of dark chocolate. Avoid large amounts—this can spike energy then crash it later.

4. **Music or Reading:**  
   If movement isn’t appealing, listen to Diabaté’s album again while reading a few pages of a favorite book. No screens—this helps your brain disengage from work mode.

**Why this works:**  
Your body needs restorative energy, not stimulation. A short walk or sensory reset lowers cortisol without overstimulating your nervous system. Oumani’s music adds a calming rhythm—his album *Talking Timabuktu Remixed* (Ry Cooder + Touré’s duets) is perfect for this!  

*Final tip:* If you do walk, step outside for 5 minutes of natural light—it resets your circadian rhythm for the evening. Enjoy your break! 🌿🎶
