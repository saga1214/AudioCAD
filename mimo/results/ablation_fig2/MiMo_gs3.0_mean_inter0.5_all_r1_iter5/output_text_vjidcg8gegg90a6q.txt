Okay, you've got a plan for the brooder plate. For bedding, the absolute best choice for winter safety is to use a thick layer of wood shavings that are specifically labeled as kiln-dried. Avoid any that are dyed or treated, and definitely don't use straw since rodents do like that. Just layer them up about two to three inches thick. 

You'll also want to cover the edges with plastic sheeting to trap the heat. Remember to keep the area dry and provide a water source that you have to check often to prevent it from freezing. 

Once they're fully grown, you can move them into the coop with the rubber mats and sand bedding you planned on using.
