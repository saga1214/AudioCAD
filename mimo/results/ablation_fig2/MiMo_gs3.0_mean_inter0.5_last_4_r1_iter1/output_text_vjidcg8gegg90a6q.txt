For winter chicks on concrete, the best strategy is to insulate the floor *under* the coop itself. You can place large-flake pine shavings or straw deep in the ground around the base of the coop to create a warm air pocket. Then, inside the coop, use a very shallow layer of sand or pine shavings to protect their feet.

This way, rodents aren't attracted to bedding inside the coop, while the ground insulation still provides natural warmth for the chickens.
