I understand your concern about reducing exposure. Let's focus on what you can control.

The five products to avoid to minimize exposure are:
One: Non-stick cookware.
Two: Grease-resistant food packaging.
Three: Stain- or waterproof fabrics and surfaces.
Four: Detergents and some cosmetics.
Five: Untreated tap water.

And to your question about getting them out... unfortunately, the name 'forever chemicals' is accurate. They do build up in the body and are very difficult to get rid of. There's no known 'detox' or 'cleansing' method that can remove them. The best approach is to focus on preventing your body from being exposed in the first place.
