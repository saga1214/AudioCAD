Absolutely! Here’s a **mess-minimizing meal plan** for your bachelor party, designed around healthy balance, minimal cleanup, and everyone’s preferences (including sugar-free sodas and calzones). Everything can stack neatly in coolers for grab-and-go access at the theme park.

---

### **DRINKS** (All single-serve or spill-resistant)  
*(Mix and match based on group preferences)*  
- **Water**: 20 reusable water bottles + purification tablets (for refills)  
- **Dads**: 10 sugar-free sodas (e.g., Sprite Zero, Coke Zero) + 10 sparkling water cans (e.g., LaCroix)  
- **Kids**: 20% juice boxes (low-sugar) + 10 water bottles  
- **Optional**: Single-serve creamers for coffee lovers + 5 sports drink boxes (for extra hydration)  
*Tip: Use leak-proof thermoses for hot drinks if needed.*

---

### **SNACKS** (Kid-friendly, non-messy, pre-portioned)  
- **Proteins**:  
  - 20 cheese sticks (mix cheddar and mozzarella)  
  - 10 turkey or chicken roll-ups (with whole-grain tortillas)  
- **Veggies/Fruits**:  
  - 3 lbs baby carrots (divided into 10 bags of 15 oz each)  
  - 2 lbs pre-cut cucumbers (same bag method)  
  - 3 lbs grapes (seeds removed, pre-washed)  
- **Treats**:  
  - 2 lbs individual trail mix bags (e.g., nuts, dried cranberries, dark chocolate chips)  
  - 2 lbs pretzels or rice cakes (divided into 10 bags)  
  - 10 bags single-serve nut/energy bites  

---

### **MAIN MEAL** (Grab-and-go, minimal prep)  
**Option 1: MINI CALZONE BAR (Recommended!)**  
- Order **10 mini calzones** from a local pizzeria:  
  - Offer 2-3 fillings: Cheese, pepperoni, veggie (spinach/mushroom), or chicken.  
  - Ask for *pre-baked, cut into halves* for easy grabbing.  
- Pack extras:  
  - 2L marinara sauce (in a spill-proof container)  
  - 1L grated parmesan cheese (in a squeeze bottle)  
  - 5L shredded mozzarella (in a sealed tub)  
  - 5L marinara cups (for dipping)  

**Option 2: WARM WRSSES/SANDWICHES**  
- Pre-pack 10 individual wraps:  
  - Filling: Turkey & hummus, chicken salad, or veggie with cream cheese.  
  - Wrap in foil for easy handling.  

---

### **DESSERT** (Celebratory, zero-fuss)  
- **Cupcakes**: 12-15 cupcakes (ask for no frosting if dietary preferences) + 1 mini cake topper for the groom.  
- **Brownie Box**: 12 individual brownie cups (pre-packaged).  
- *Skip cutting plates*—kids can lick frosting off fingers!  

---

### **COOLER-PACKING TIPS**  
1. **Layer Smartly**:  
   - Bottom: Ice packs + water/juice.  
   - Middle: Calzones (if hot) or main meal wraps.  
   - Top: Snacks/desserts.  
2. **Label Containers**: Tape small labels to cooler compartments (e.g., "Dad Sodas," "Kid Veggies").  
3. **Tools**: Include 2-3 spill trays (like shallow plastic bins) for juice boxes/cups.  
4. **Extras**: Pack wet wipes, hand sanitizer, trash bags, and a roll of paper towels.  

---
