### 🎁 Best Treats for Puppy Training:
1. **High-Value Rewards:** Small, soft, smelly, and easy to eat. Good options include:
   * Peanut butter (unsweetened, xylitol-free)
   * Cheese cubes (like cheddar or mozzarella)
   * Hot dogs or chicken strips
   * Commercial training treats (like Zippy Top or Tummy Yum Yums)
2. **Size:** Tiny pieces – easy to swallow and manage.
3. **Timing:** Give the treat *immediately* after he does the right thing and *before* you say "good!" or click.
4. **Rotate:** Use different smells/textures to keep him interested.

### 🎯 Clicker Training Explained:
Clicker training uses a "three-step" process:

1. **Click:** Hold the clicker in your hand, making a sharp, consistent "click" sound.
2. **Mark:** The *instant* he does the behavior you want (e.g., sits), press the clicker. This marks the exact moment he did it right.
3. **Treat:** Within a second, give him a treat. The clicker now means "Yes! That's the behavior I want!"

**How it works:**  
The clicker becomes a bridge between the behavior and the reward. It tells him *exactly* what he did to earn the treat. Over time, he learns that when you click, he should keep doing that action to get a reward.

**Getting Started:**
* Practice clicking *without* treats first. Click randomly, then add a treat only when you hear the click.
* Start with easy behaviors: Click when he looks at you, then immediately reward. Click when he wags his tail, then reward. This teaches him the clicker = good thing!
* Once he understands the clicker, practice clicking *only* when he does the behavior you want (e.g., sits). Click the instant he sits, then give the treat.
* Gradually reduce the number of treats over sessions, rewarding only when he *doesn't* sit, then rewarding when he *does* sit. This is called "shaping."

**Why it's great:**  
It gives him clear feedback, builds trust, and is highly motivating for puppies. Since you identify as a nerd, the precision of marking the exact moment he performs the behavior should appeal to you!

**Key Tips:**  
* Be consistent with the click sound and the reward timing.  
* Keep sessions short and fun (5-10 minutes).  
* End on a success so he leaves excited to train again.  
* Pair the clicker with verbal praise ("Good boy!") for extra reinforcement.

Start with simple behaviors like "sit" or "touch" (touch his nose to your hand) using the clicker method. Galileo will learn quickly!
