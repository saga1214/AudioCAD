I hear you. To become a 'legend' for Thanksgiving, you want something impressive but not too difficult given your equipment. A no-bake pie crust is a fantastic option. You can make the crust from scratch in a single bowl and just transfer it to a store-bought filling, like pumpkin or pecan pie.

If you'd rather do a full bake, a simple casserole like a loaded baked potato dish would be great. You just bake potatoes and layer them with cheese, broccoli, and chicken in a dish.

And for something a bit different and still impressive, you could make a fruit tart. Many recipes use a pre-made crust, and the fruit topping is just a mix of fruit, sugar, and cornstarch. It's very low-effort for high-impact dessert.
