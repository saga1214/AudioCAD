For homemade homemade magic:
First up are frozen jewels! Mash pumpkin until smooth or grate sweet potato—you can steam it first to make grating easier. Pack spoonfuls onto a baking sheet, freeze until solid, then break into chilly chews. They hydrate slowly and keep her cool.
Peanut Butter Oat Cookies are a classic for a reason. Preheat your oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit, which is 175 degrees Celsius. Mix one cup of rolled oats with a quarter cup of smooth, xylol-free peanut butter and a splash of milk or water if it seems too thick. Drop little mounds onto a baking sheet, flatten slightly, and bake for 10 to 12 minutes until golden. Let them cool completely before breaking into tiny soldier-sized pieces.
Cooked Chicken Strips are pure protein joy. Just ask your butcher for some plain, skinless, boneless chicken breast, cooked without any spices or sauces. Cut it into thin sticks or tiny cubes.胡萝卜sticks (grated fresh or lightly steamed) are another crunchy, healthy crunch.
