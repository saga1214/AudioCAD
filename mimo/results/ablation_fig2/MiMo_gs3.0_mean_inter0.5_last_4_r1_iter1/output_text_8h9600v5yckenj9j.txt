For candy tomorrow, focus on Halloween classics that never fail: Reese’s Cups, Hershey’s Kisses, candy bars like Snickers or Milky Way, and gummy worms or eyeballs. You could also grab some classic Halloween-themed candies like black licorice or marshmallow ghosts. Mini chocolate bars are always a good bet because guests can take a few without you running out. Just try to buy popular, high-demand items and avoid the cheaper, less popular candies that might end up sitting around.
