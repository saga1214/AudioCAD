That coffee table is all set and ready for snacks! Since you avoid sweet or chocolaty treats, vending machine options usually include savory stuff like chips, pretzels, cheese sticks, or even little bags of nuts and trail mix. Sometimes they have Japanese snacks like senbei or onigiri, which are savory rice cakes. 

If you're feeling adventurous, you could mix and match—maybe some spicy nacho chips with a handful of chocolate-covered raisins for a sweet-and-salty combo. Or go all out with a candy machine and pick something uniquely interesting, like licorice or gummies. 

The possibilities are endless! Enjoy your project and your new snack spot!
