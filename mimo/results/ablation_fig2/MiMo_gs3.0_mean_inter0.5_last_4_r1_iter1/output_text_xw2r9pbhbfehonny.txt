Alright, so before you film, here are a few things to think about. First, just check with the facility ahead of time. Assisted living places might have rules about recording, so make sure it’s okay first.

Next, pick a spot where the lighting’s good and the audience can see everything. You’ll want a clear angle where the puppets are visible and you’re not blocking the view.

Then, set up your phone on a tripod or something steady, since shaky footage won’t look professional.

After that, just focus on your show. Don’t stress about the camera—just do your thing. Kids and even older folks will react better if you seem natural.

Finally, after filming, watch the footage to make sure it’s usable. Maybe do a little editing if you need to, like cutting out awkward pauses or bad parts.

The goal is to keep it simple and fun. You’ll figure out the rest as you go.
