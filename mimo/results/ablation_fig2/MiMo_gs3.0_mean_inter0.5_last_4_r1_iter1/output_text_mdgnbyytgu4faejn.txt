Alright, let's put this all together into a clear and simple structure you can follow.

First is your Header. Center your 14-point name. Right directly underneath it, starting at the same margin, list your key contact info vertically: first email, then phone number, then city. Icons out for ATS safety. LinkedIn would be great here if you have one.

Next, a short two-to-three sentence Professional Summary. Briefly state your core skills and career goals.

Then, a Relevant Experience section, which will be your main highlight reel. Here, list the jobs that are most relevant to your target roles. For each job, include the company name, your title, the dates you worked there, and two or three bullet points that describe your key achievements.

After that, an Additional Work History section. This is where you'll put the less relevant jobs. Just list your job title, company, and dates. No need for bullet points.

For Education, list your degrees chronologically. For each one, write the degree, the school, your graduation year or expected graduation year, and any honors you received, like "Highest Honors."

Finally, a Skills section. List your key skills, maybe grouping them into categories like Technical Skills or Soft Skills.

This structure should give you a clear roadmap to build your resume efficiently.
