That line of reasoning doesn't quite hold up.

Even though the egg contained the DNA of the first chicken, it also contained the DNA of a creature that was its ancestor but not yet a chicken. For the egg to exist and hatch, the protein needed for its unique shell had to come from a hen. Otherwise, it simply wouldn't have been classified as a 'chicken's egg.' The first true chicken had to emerge from that egg, making the chicken the one that came first in the sequence.
