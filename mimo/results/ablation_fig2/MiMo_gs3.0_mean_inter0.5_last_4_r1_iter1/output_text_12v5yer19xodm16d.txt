Okay, you need a pick-me-up! For drinks, I'd suggest five options. First, an Iced Matcha Latte, which is creamy and energizing. Second, a Sparkling Water with Lemon, for a refreshing, caffeine-free choice. Third, Green Tea, which has L-theanine to help with focus. Fourth, a Cold Brew Coffee, which is smoother and less acidic. And fifth, a Coconut Water, which is great for hydration.

For snacks, here are five ideas. First, Almonds or Walnuts, which are healthy fats and protein. Second, Dark Chocolate with at least 70 percent cocoa. Third, Banana with Peanut Butter, for quick energy. Fourth, an Apple with a little bit of Honey. And fifth, Greek Yogurt, which is high in protein and probiotics. Hope that helps you feel more energized!
