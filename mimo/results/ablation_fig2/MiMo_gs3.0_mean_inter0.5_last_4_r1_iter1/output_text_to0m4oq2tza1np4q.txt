### Recipe Idea: **Pickle-Juice Salmon Salad with Garden Greens**
**Ingredients you’ll use:**  
- Leftover salmon (pan-seared or baked)  
- Handful of spinach (from your balcony or home-cooked)  
- Cherry tomatoes (halved)  
- Dill or fresh herbs (optional)  
- **Pickle juice:** A splash (1–2 tsp)  
- Olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper  

**Instructions:**  
1. **Prep the salmon:** Pan-sear or bake the salmon until flaky.  
2. **Make the dressing:** Whisk together pickle juice, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. The vinegar tang from the pickle juice brightens the flavors!  
3. **Assemble the salad:** Toss spinach and cherry tomatoes with the dressing. Top with grilled salmon, extra dill, and a sprinkle of the leftover pickle slices for crunch.  

**Why it works:**  
- **Pickle juice** adds electrolytes (sodium, potassium) after your run and enhances flavor without extra salt.  
- **Spinach and tomatoes** from home cooking keep bloating破皮 breakouts皮质醇 levels in check with antioxidants and hydration.  
- **Salmon** provides lean protein and omega-3s for heart health—perfect for your goal!  

**Bonus tip:** If you have extra pickle juice, use it to marinate tofu or chicken for a savory snack. It’s a great way to reduce waste while staying health-conscious! 🥒🐟
