You can definitely learn a lot more. The absolute best place to start is the Buffalo and Erie Canal Museum in Lockport. It's located right by the locks and is considered the premier museum of its kind in the entire United States.

Inside, you can walk through the replica of a 19th-century canal boat, learn all about the mechanics of the locks, and even see a working replica of a canal boat's engine. It's a great place to really understand the history and engineering of the canal.
