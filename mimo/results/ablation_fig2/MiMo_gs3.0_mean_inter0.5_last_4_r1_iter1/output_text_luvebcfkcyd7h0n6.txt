Absolutely. Let's just quickly walk through them. So... regular bit versus quantum qubit. Uh, the qubit is that actor who could be hero, background, etcetera—all states at once—that's superposition. Taking a measurement is when the switch just becomes on *or* off. Entangled quibits are those linked actors—one cast as hero, the villain role clicks on instantly for the other. Budget coherence is how long that potential state can be held. Error correction is the editing process to fix mistakes. Quibs themselves are just the individual actors. And taking the 'final cut' measurement collapses the scene down to one single answer. Hope that helps clear it up.
