Absolutely! Costa Rica is scuba diving paradise, and I’d love to help you plan an unforgettable trip. Here’s a tailored blueprint based on your dream trip and scuba diving focus:

### **1. Fin Choice Finalized: Short Fins for Beginners**
Since you’re beginners (based on your Honduras experience with long fins), **short fins** are ideal. They’re compact, easier to maneuver, travel well, and reduce leg strain. Costa Rica’s reefs often involve tight spaces or calm currents where their agility shines.

---

### **2. Top Scuba Destinations in Costa Rica**
Focus on the **Puntarenas Peninsula** (central Pacific) for the best reefs:

- **Cenote Azul (Tortuguero)**: Crystal-clear cenotes with freshwater/saltwater mixing zones (unique for diving). Great for beginners.  
- **Playa Conchal & Manuel Antonio**: Reefs with snorkel-friendly visibility (15–30m) and tropical fish.  
- **Cerro Azul Cenote (Cantillo)**: Dramatic underwater caves and lava tubes—like diving in the "Twilight Zone."  
- **Pacuare River**: Night dives with bioluminescent plankton (magical, but requires local guides).

---

### **3. Must-Do Dives & Experiences**
- **Day Dives**: Coral gardens, turtles, rays, and tropical fish (e.g., Playa Conchal).  
- **Night Dives**: Bioluminescent plankton or nocturnal wildlife (Pacuare River).  
- **Cenote Exploration**: Aztec Caves (Pirae) or Cenote Azul for freshwater ecosystems.  
- **Adventure**: Kayak to a secluded cove for a relaxed morning dive.  

---

### **4. Logistics & Tips**
- **When to Go**: **Dry Season (Dec–Apr)** for best visibility (25–30m). Avoid rainy season (May–Nov) for rougher seas.  
- **Certification**: Ensure both of you have **PADI/SSI Advanced Open Water** certification (required for most dives).  
- **Operator**: Book with **Dive Costa Rica** or **Ocean Blue** for small-group, eco-friendly tours.  
- **Packing**: Lightweight dive computer, reef-safe sunscreen, and a dry bag. Skip long fins unless current forecasts show strong currents.  

---

### **5. Beyond Scuba: Pair with Nature**
- **Hot Springs**: Soak in volcanic hot springs near Arenal (after diving).  
- **Sloth Spotting**: Guided hikes in Manuel Antonio National Park.  
- **Coffee Farm Tour**: Costa Rica’s world-famous coffee culture.  

---

### **6. Pro Tip: Book Early!**
Popular operators fill up fast. Secure your dives and accommodation in **Puntarenas** or **San José** (nearest airport) 3–6 months ahead.
