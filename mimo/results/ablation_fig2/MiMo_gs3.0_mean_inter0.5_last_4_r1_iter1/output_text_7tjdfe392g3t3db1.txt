For tuning your guitar while waiting for dye to process, here are the best tools and steps:

### 🎸 Tuning Tools & Methods:
1. **Clip-On Tuner**  
   - **How it works**: Attaches to the headstock; vibrates when a string is out of tune.  
   - **Pros**: Instant feedback, accurate for all strings.  
   - **Example**: Snark or Boss clip-on tuners.

2. **Phone App**  
   - **How it works**: Hold your phone near the strings; it detects pitch.  
   - **Pros**: Free (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune), portable.  
   - **Tip**: Ensure your phone’s mic is clean for accuracy.

3. **Tuning Fork**  
   - **How it works**: Strike it to produce a pure tone; match strings to the fork’s sound.  
   - **Best for**: Traditionalists or when electronics fail.

4. **Tuning Peg**  
   - **How it works**: Turn pegs to raise/lower pitch until peg matches the target note.  
   - **Pros**: No extra tools needed.  
   - **Tip**: Use a tuner app to confirm the correct pitch.

### 🎨 Vibrant Hair Dyeing Recap:
1. **Do a STRAND TEST** first—apply dye to a hidden section to predict full-head results.  
2. **Monitor Bleaching Progress**: Stop when hair is pale yellow (never exceed 45 mins). Your hair is already dry/porous, so check frequently.  
3. **Wait 24 Hours After Bleaching**: Deep-condition to restore moisture before dyeing. Cool water and color-safe shampoo preserve vibrancy.  
4. **Choose Developer Wisely**: Use **10-volume developer** for Wella/Ion dyes—it’s gentle enough for bleached hair.  
5. **Shampoo with Cool Water**: Fades color faster; always rinse with cool water post-dye.
