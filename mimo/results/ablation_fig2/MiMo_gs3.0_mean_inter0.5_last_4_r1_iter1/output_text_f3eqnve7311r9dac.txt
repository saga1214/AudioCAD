Yes, vintage comics from the 1940s and 50s are generally rare and highly sought after.

Three record-selling examples recently include a nearly perfect copy of *Action Comics* number one selling for six million dollars in 2024.

Another major one was *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen, where Spider-Man first appeared. A copy of that sold in 2021 for three point six million.

Also, a copy of *Captain America Comics* number one from 1941 sold at auction in 2022 for just over three million dollars each
