Of course, I can re-confirm that. Driving from L.A. usually takes six hours, while the flight distance is much shorter, typically an hour or a little under an hour.

Regarding the horse, you will need a special permit. Horses are not allowed in the city of Phoenix itself; they are only permitted on rural land. So, if you were to buy a home with a large backyard, you would need to be in a designated rural area to legally keep a horse.

And just to reiterate for your weekend trip, you're right, it's about six hours by car and just over an hour by plane.
