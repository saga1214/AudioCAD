For an end-of-year celebration, a **hybrid approach** works great—balancing fun, inclusivity, and manageable logistics. Here are tailored ideas based on your needs:

### 1. **Rainy-Proof Indoor Party**  
   - **Theme:** "Brownie Adventure Day" (tie into badges earned).  
   - **Activities:**  
     - Set up craft stations (decorate sashes, friendship bracelets).  
     - Include a "Brownie Olympics" with seated games:  
       - Relay races (e.g., "Three-Legged Race" with partner pairs).  
       - Trivia (Brownie badges, fun facts).  
   - **Food:** Ice cream sundaes (dairy-free option available) + pizza slices. Parents can volunteer to bring snacks.  
   - **Why it works:** Indoor, low-cost, and lets parents handle food/logistics. Celebrates achievements visually with crafts.

### 2. **Outdoor Park BBQ (With Contingency Plan)**  
   - **If weather permits:**  
     - Picnic with sash-decorating station, badge-making crafts, and a "trophy" award ceremony.  
     - **Food:** BBQ (parents bring sides, troop provides pizza/ice cream).  
   - **If it rains:**  
     - Switch to a **community center** or **school cafeteria** with the same activities.  
   - **Tip:** Rent a gazebo or reserve a covered pavilion to avoid weather worries.

### 3. **Budget-Friendly Dues & Costs**  
   - **Dues:** Charge $10–$15/year (covers badge materials, craft supplies, and 1–2 small events). Be transparent about expenses.  
   - **Sashes:** Charge parents $5–$10 per girl (materials like ribbons/buckles). Offer a "trophy" ceremony instead of full sashes if costs are tight.  
   - **Events:** Avoid expensive outings (e.g., camping trips) early on. Stick to badge-focused activities (e.g., "Animal Badge Walk" at a zoo).

### 4. **Key Tips for Success**  
   - **Involve parents:** Create a sign-up sheet for:  
     - Snack rotations (e.g., "Pizza Day," "Soda & Chips" days).  
     - Activity helpers (e.g., badge-making mentors).  
   - **Keep activities simple:** Focus on 2–3 badge projects per meeting (e.g., "Community Helpers" badge at fire station, "Nature" badge at park).  
   - **Celebrate progress:** Host a badge award ceremony at the end-of-year event—girls love recognition!
