Great choice for dinner! Here's a classic **Spaghetti Carbonara** ingredient list for 2–3 servings, along with key tips:

### **Essential Ingredients**  
1. **Pasta**: 300–400g spaghetti (or bucatini/cavatappi).  
2. **Pancetta or Guanciale**: 150g, diced (cured pork belly or jowl).  
3. **Eggs**: 2 large eggs + 2 egg yolks (whisked together).  
4. **Pecorino Romano Cheese**: ½ cup grated (or Parmigiano-Reggiano for milder flavor).  
5. **Black Pepper**: Freshly ground (generous amount).  
6. **Salt**: For pasta water.  

### **Optional Additions**  
- **Red Pepper Flakes**: For a hint of heat.  
- **Extra Black Pepper**: Garnish.  

### **Key Tips**  
- **No Cream!** Authentic carbonara uses only eggs, cheese, and pork fat.  
- **Reserve Pasta Water**: Save ½ cup before draining for adjusting sauce consistency.  
- **Low Heat**: Cook eggs/cheese mixture over *very low heat* to prevent scrambling.  

### **Quick Method**  
1. Cook pasta in salted water until *al dente*.  
2. In a pan, render pancetta over medium-low heat. Remove from heat.  
3. Whisk eggs, cheese, and pepper in a bowl.  
4. Toss hot pasta with pancetta, then *quickly* mix in egg mixture (off heat).  
5. Add reserved pasta water 1 tbsp at a time until creamy. Serve immediately!  

Enjoy your meal—simple, rich, and deeply flavorful! 🍝
