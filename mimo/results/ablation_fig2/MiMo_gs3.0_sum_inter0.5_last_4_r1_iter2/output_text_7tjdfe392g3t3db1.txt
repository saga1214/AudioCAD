While you're waiting for your hair dye to process, here's how to tune your strings:

### Tuning Methods:
1. **Electronic Tuner (Clip-On or Pedal):**  
   - Attach the clip to your headstock or strap the pedal nearby.  
   - Press a string down, pluck it, and watch the tuner display.  
   - Adjust tuning pegs until the needle points to the target note (e.g., E for the 6th string).  

2. **App Tuner (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune):**  
   - Open the app and place your phone near the instrument.  
   - Pluck a string and watch the app show the pitch.  
   - Adjust pegs until the note matches (e.g., "E" for the 6th string).  

### Tips for Accuracy:  
- **Start with the 6th string (lowest E):** Tune it to **E** first.  
- **Tune up:** If the note is too low, gently tighten the peg.  
- **Tune down:** If too high, loosen the peg.  
- **Check intervals:** Ensure strings are in sequence (e.g., E-A-D-G-B-E).  
- **Use reference notes:** Apps often show relative pitch (e.g., "A above E").
