That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour is serene and peaceful, making it ideal for sailing life. Here are some fun daytime activities around that area on St. Thomas:

1. **Snorkel Secret Harbour Bay Itself:** The bay right from your resort is calm and shallow, perfect for easy snorkeling right off the shore. You might spot parrotfish, grunts, and maybe even a small reef. If you want to venture out, Secret Harbour is also famous for its nearby coral walls teeming with life.
2. **Explore Cruz Bay Shops & Stroll:** Gallows Point or Secret Harbour are an easy walk to the shops and restaurants in Cruz Bay. Beyond Duffy's and The Longboard (for music!), explore the smaller shops, browse for local crafts, and enjoy the village atmosphere.
3. **Visit Cinnamon Bay State Park (St. John):** If you're staying Secret Harbour on Secret Harbour Bay (St. Thomas), but if you're open to St. John (where Secret Harbour Resort *is*), Cinnamon Bay is a must. It's one of the most beautiful beaches, with a famous snorkeling trail along the reef wall. Take a short boat or water taxi from Cruz Bay.
4. **Discover Cinnamon Bay's MVR Bog (St. John):** On the other side of Cinnamon Bay, behind the famous Anegada Lookout, is the MVR Bog. It's a unique, lush, and peaceful ecosystem where you can spot the Virgin Islands Roseate Spoonbill (a beautiful pink bird) and other wetland wildlife. A guided tour is highly recommended.
5. **Take a Boat Tour to Anegada:** Anegada is just a short boat ride away and offers a different, more rural island experience. You could take a lobster boat tour (where you cook your own freshly caught lobsters!), a glass-bottom boat tour to see the underwater gardens, or a simple ferry trip to explore the island.
6. **Hike the Trunk Bay Trail (St. John):** Secret Harbour is on Trunk Bay Island. The Trunk Bay Trail is a relatively easy, paved path that winds down to a stunning beach with a famous underwater snorkeling trail. It's less crowded than Cinnamon Bay and offers beautiful views.
7. **Sail to the Baths (St. John):** A short sail from Secret Harbour takes you to the iconic Virgin Islands National Park. The Baths are a maze of colorful coral boulders, tide pools perfect for exploring, and great for snorkeling. It's a magical, low-pressure environment.
8. **Explore Cruz Bay Coffee Shops & Bookstores:** If you just want to chill, cruise down to Cruz Bay. There are several lovely spots like Cruz Bay Books or a café (like Café Luna) where you can grab coffee, people-watch, and soak up the village vibe.

Since you'll be sailing most places, make sure to pack reef-safe sunscreen, hats, and plenty of water! Enjoy your week on the water.
