Here's a quick recap of how each major system component fits into the child-friendly analogy:

1.  **Heart:** The **gas station and engine**. It pumps the blood-cars and also provides the oxygen "fuel."
2.  **Lungs:** The **fresh fuel stop**. This is where the blood-cars stop to load up on oxygen from the air.
3.  **Veins:** The **return highways**. These are the roads the tired, refueled blood-cars take back to the heart and lungs.
4.  **Arteries:** The **big highways**. These are the main roads carrying blood *away* from the heart to deliver fuel.
5.  **Capillaries:** The **tiny neighborhood streets**. These are the super-small roads where the blood-cars stop *right outside* muscle and organ "houses" to drop off the oxygen fuel.
6.  **Kidneys:** The **super car wash and trash disposal**. Blood-cars pass through here to have waste and extra fluid flushed out (becoming urine). They also help balance salt and water.
7.  **Liver:** The **recycling and repair center**. Blood-cars deliver nutrients (like vitamins) *to* the liver, and the liver processes them, stores some, breaks down bad stuff (like toxins), and makes new proteins.

**The Journey Recap:**
1.  Heart pumps blood-cars onto arteries (big highways).
2.  Arteries lead to capillaries (tiny streets) near muscles/organs.
3.  Blood-cars drop off oxygen fuel.
4.  Exhausted blood-cars take veins (return highways) back to the heart.
5.  Heart/Lungs gas station: Blood-cars get refueled with fresh oxygen.
6.  Journey starts again! Kidneys clean the blood-cars along the way, and the liver processes nutrients and waste they carry.
