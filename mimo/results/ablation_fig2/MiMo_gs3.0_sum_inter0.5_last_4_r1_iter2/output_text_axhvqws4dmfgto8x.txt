Alright, since tacos didn't appeal as much and you insist on tackling stir-fry, here are the absolute foolproof steps for your noodles.

First, and most importantly, use metal tongs or long spoons made of metal or glass. Absolutely no wood this time!

Second, add a pinch of salt to the water, as it helps the noodles cook faster.

Third, stir constantly with your tongs so they don't stick to the bottom of the pot.

And finally, as soon as they look done, drain them immediately. You can even rinse them briefly in cold water to stop the cooking process.

You can't set off a smoke alarm from draining noodles quickly
