For Madrid, tapas are a great choice. Think small plates like patatas bravas—that's fried potatoes with this spicy tomato sauce—or jamón ibérico, which is a really high-quality cured ham.

Paella is also super popular, but just be aware that the original dish is from Valencia, so in Madrid you might find some local versions. Tortilla española is another classic, it's like a thick omelette.

And hey, for dessert, you can't go wrong with churros... they're these fried dough pastries... usually dipped in hot chocolate.
