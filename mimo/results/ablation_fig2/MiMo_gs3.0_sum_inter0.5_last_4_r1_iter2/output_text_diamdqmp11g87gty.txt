Winter definitely calls for a mix of preparedness and fun! Here are some DIY insulation tips, plus ways to enjoy the season:

### DIY House Insulation Tips:
1. **Seal Gaps:**  
   - Apply weather stripping to windows and door frames. Focus on the bottom edges and frame joints.  
   - Replace worn door sweeps for airtight seals.  
2. **Windows:**  
   - Install double-pane film over older single-pane windows to retain heat.  
   - Hang thermal curtains when not in use to block drafts.  
3. **Attic:**  
   - Add insulation up there (fiberglass batts or cellulose) to stop warm air from escaping. Aim for R-38 or higher in northern climates.  
4. **Doors:**  
   - Door draft stoppers block cold from seeping in at the base.  

These steps can cut heating costs by 10–20%!

### Enjoying Winter Outside:
- **Ice Fishing:** Check ice thickness with a ruler or drill (4+ inches is safe). Use a portable ice auger and heated seat pads.  
- **Snowshoeing:** Rent gear locally or buy affordable waterproof boots and snowshoes. Trails are free in many parks!  
- **Other Activities:**  
  - **Cross-Country Skiing:** Try groomed trails or rent a set.  
  - **Sledding/Hills:** Local parks often have designated areas.  
  - **Snowmobiling:** Rentals available in Wisconsin’s driftless region.  
  - **Winter Hiking:** Pack a thermos, wear insulated gloves, and scout trails for frozen waterfalls or wildlife tracks.  

### Maple Syrup Tapping (Bonus!):  
- **Tools Needed:** Drill (½-inch bit), spile (metal spout), bucket, tubing.  
- **How It Works:**  
  1. Drill a hole in the trunk (1.5–2 inches deep), insert the spile, and place a bucket underneath.  
  2. Tap trees when temps drop below 32°F at night but rise above freezing during the day.  
  3. Boil sap (1:30 sap-to-syrup ratio) to concentrate sugars.  

> ⚠️ **Fireplace Tip:** Always close the flue damper after using the fireplace—cold air escaping raises heating costs!
