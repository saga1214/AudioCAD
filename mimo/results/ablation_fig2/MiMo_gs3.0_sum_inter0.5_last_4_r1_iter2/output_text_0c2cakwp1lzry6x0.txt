Here's a step-by-step guide for each installation, prioritizing safety and efficiency:

### 📺 1. TV Mount Installation
- **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, drill, wall anchors (if mounting to drywall), screwdriver
- **Steps:**
  1. **Locate studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (critical for heavy mounts). If no studs, use heavy-duty drywall anchors.
  2. **Measure TV size:** Check manufacturer weight limits for your mount. For TVs >50", always use studs.
  3. **Position mount:** Hold mount against wall, using a level to ensure it's straight. Mark screw holes.
  4. **Drill pilot holes:** Drill holes slightly smaller than anchors/screws.
  5. **Secure mount:** Insert anchors, then attach mount to wall with screws.
  6. **Mount TV:** Align TV with mount, secure with screws, and attach cables.

### 🌿 2. Hanging Planters Installation
- **Tools Needed:** Drill, wall anchors (for drywall), hook or hanger, measuring tape
- **Steps:**
  1. **Choose location:** Install near windows for light or high ceilings for air circulation.
  2. **Measure clearance:** Ensure space above is clear (no overhead furniture).
  3. **Mark hook position:** Hold hook against wall, use level to align, mark screw points.
  4. **Drill & Anchor:** Drill holes, insert anchors, then attach hook.
  5. **Hang planters:** Slide hangers over hook or screw into planter bases.
  6. **Test weight:** Ensure hook can support planter + soil weight.

### 🪵 3. Floating Shelf Installation
- **Tools Needed:** Level, drill, wall anchors, shelf brackets, measuring tape
- **Steps:**
  1. **Plan layout:** Decide shelf spacing (e.g., 18-24" apart for balance).
  2. **Mark positions:** Hold shelf bracket against wall, level it, mark screw points.
  3. **Drill pilot holes:** Drill holes for anchors/screws.
  4. **Attach brackets:** Secure brackets to wall with anchors.
  5. **Mount shelf:** Place shelf onto brackets and secure with screws.
  6. **Load safely:** Avoid overloading (check weight limits!). Start with lightweight decor.

### 🛠️ Pro Tips:
- **Safety First:** Wear safety glasses. Use a stud finder with laser level for precision.
- **Tool Check:** Confirm drill bits match hole size (e.g., 1/4" for anchors).
- **Landlord Compliance:** Install during allowed hours (per landlord rules) and clean up debris.
- **Decorating Sundays:** Dedicate 1-2 hours per project to avoid fatigue. Play upbeat music to boost energy!

> ⏱️ **Time-Saving Hack:** Install all hardware first, *then* decorate. This prevents tool clutter and lets you focus on aesthetics without interruptions.
