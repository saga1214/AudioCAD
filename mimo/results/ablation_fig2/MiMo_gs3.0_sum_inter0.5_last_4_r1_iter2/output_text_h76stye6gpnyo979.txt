Organizing multiple passions and financial priorities is a lot, but a step-by-step approach can make it more manageable.

First, clearly rank your goals—for apartment downpayment, Salt Flats trip, Feröe Islands, fermented cooking trend investment.

Next, for each goal, calculate the estimated cost and figure out how much you need to save each month. You could create separate savings accounts or labels for each goal to keep track.

Finally, look for small ways to cut costs or make extra money toward your top priority. Even a small, consistent effort can add up over time!
