That's a fantastic question, and the answer is a resounding yes! You can absolutely submit an illustrated manuscript for a children's book. In fact, many publishers actively seek out authors who can also provide the illustrations.

However, it's very important to understand that the process is a bit different. You'll be applying to a specific category, which is usually called a 'picture book' or 'author-illustrator' agent. When you submit, you'll need to provide the manuscript, plus a few sample illustrations that best represent the story's tone and style.

You'd still use a platform like Submittable, but you would look for agents who have a specific note in their guidelines that says they represent authors who also illustrate. It's a bit more competitive, but if you're confident in your art, it's definitely a path worth pursuing.
