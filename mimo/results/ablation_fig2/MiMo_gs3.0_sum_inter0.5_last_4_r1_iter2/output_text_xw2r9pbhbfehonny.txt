Yeah, before you film your own content, here’s a basic checklist to keep in mind. 

First, make sure you have permission from the venue—schools, hospitals, nursing homes—to film and post it online. That’s a key step to avoid future issues.

Second, you should decide on your content. Like, what’s the focus? Maybe just a short, high-energy segment of Melvin the Magnificent’s terrible magic tricks? Or a quick interaction with the audience? Keep it short and fun.

Third, set up your recording device—phone, camera, whatever—so it’s stable and the audio is clear. Maybe test it beforehand if you can.

And fourth, after the show, check the footage. Make sure it’s clear, the audio is good, and it captures what you wanted.

That’s the basic answer. Good luck with filming your own show!
