You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is a great gluten-free foundation. Other gluten-free camp snacks you could consider are trail mix, made from things like nuts and seeds, or rice cakes. Energy bars, specifically gluten-free ones like Clif Bars or RXBARs, are also a good option.

And here's a fun idea: you could bring fresh fruit, like berries or grapes.
