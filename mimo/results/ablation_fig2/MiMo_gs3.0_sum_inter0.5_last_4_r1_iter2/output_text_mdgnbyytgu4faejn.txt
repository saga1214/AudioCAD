Here's a streamlined, ATS-friendly resume structure with placement guidance for each section. I'll break it down section-by-section:

### **Header**
- **Your Name (14pt standard font)**  
- **Vertical Alignment:** Centered or left-aligned (don't justify right).
- **Right-Aligned Info (9pt is fine):**  
  Email icon + email, phone icon + phone, city icon + city.  
  *Remove icons* (to avoid ATS parsing issues).

---

### **Professional Summary** (2-3 lines)  
*Crucial for career changes/gaps.*  
Example:  
> "Strategic Project Manager with 15+ years of experience leading cross-functional teams in [Industry]. Proven expertise in [Skill 1], [Skill 2], and [Skill 3]. Seeking to leverage [Transferable Skill] in [New Field] to drive [Goal]."

---

### **Relevant Experience** (Primary Section)  
*List jobs in reverse chronological order.*  
- **Job Title** | Company | City, State | *Dates (Month Year - Month Year)*  
- **Bullet Points:**  
  - 2-3 concise points max per job.  
  - Focus on *achievements* (metrics preferred):  
    > "Boosted sales by 30% through [Action]."  
  > "Reduced costs by $50K/year via [Action]."  
- **Include:**  
  - Companies  
  - Titles  
  Additional info (e.g., "Contract," "Volunteer") if relevant.

---

### **Additional Experience** (Optional)  
*For roles not directly relevant to target jobs.*  
- List similarly:  
  > Job Title | Company | City, State | *Dates*  
  - 1-2 bullet points max (highlight transferable skills only).

---

### **Education**  
- **Degree:** "B.S. Computer Science"  
- **Institution:** "University of XYZ"  
- **Location:** "City, State"  
- **Dates:** "May 2010"  
- **Add if applicable:**  
  - "Expected May 2025" for ongoing degrees  
  - "Graduated with Highest Honors"  
  - Scholarship names (e.g., "Presidential Scholarship")  
  *List multiple degrees from same school once, with details.*

---

### **Skills Section** (Essential)  
- **Group by category:**  
  > **Technical:** Python, SQL, Tableau  
  > **Soft Skills:** Leadership, Cross-Team Collaboration  
  > **Certifications:** PMP (2023), Google Analytics  
- **Prioritize keywords** from job descriptions.

---

### **Optional Sections**  
- **Certifications:**  
  > "PMP | Project Management Institute | 2023"  
- **Languages:**  
  > "Spanish (Fluent)"  
- **Volunteer Work:**  
  > "Nonprofit Board Member | 2020–Present"  
- **Awards:**  
  > "Employee of the Year | XYZ Corp | 2018"  
  *(List under Education if academic; otherwise, standalone section)*
