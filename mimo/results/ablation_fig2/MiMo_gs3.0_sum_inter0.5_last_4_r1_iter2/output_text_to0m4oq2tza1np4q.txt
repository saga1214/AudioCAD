Ooh! Pickles are a fun pantry staple—here are some healthy, low-salt pickle hacks to fit your goals:

### 🥒 1. **Salt-Rinse Pickles**  
   Soak pickles in pickle juice for 10 minutes, then rinse under cold water. This removes ~30% of sodium—perfect if you still want the crunch!

### 🥗 2. **Pickle Juice Vinaigrette**  
   Mix 1 tbsp pickle juice + 2 tbsp olive oil + 1 tsp lemon juice + herbs (basil/dill from your garden). Toss with spinach, cherry tomatoes, chickpeas, and grilled salmon.  
   *Bonus: Adds probiotics for gut health!*

### 🧂 3. **DIY Light Pickle Dip**  
   Blend:  
   - 1/2 cup cucumber (for freshness)  
   - 1/4 cup pickle juice  
   - 2 tbsp Greek yogurt (protein!)  
   - 1 garlic clove  
   - Fresh dill  
   Serve with cucumber slices or bell peppers.

### 🍽️ 4. **Salmon "Cucumber Roll-Ups"**  
   Spread thin cucumber slices with your pickle-juice vinaigrette. Top with flaked salmon, spinach, and a cherry tomato slice. Roll up—no bread needed!

### 💡 Pro Tips:  
- **Bloating/Breakouts:** If pickles feel too salty/spicy, rinse them or pair with high-fiber foods (spinach, tomatoes) to balance digestion.  
- **Electrolytes:** A splash of pickle juice post-run (after hydration) can replenish sodium *naturally*—just rinse excess salt off first!
