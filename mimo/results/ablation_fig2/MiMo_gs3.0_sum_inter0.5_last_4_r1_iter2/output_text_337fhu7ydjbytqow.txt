### **20-Mile Day Segment (Hilly, Mid-30s)**
Bring **moderate layers** since temps are mild but hilly running generates heat. Aid stations help with hydration/nutrition.
- **Base Layer:**  
  → Moisture-wicking shirt/pants (avoid cotton).  
- **Mid-Layer:**  
  → Light抓绒vest/jacket (for warmth when stopped, easy removal).  
- **Outer Layer:**  
  → Wind-resistant or light rain shell jacket (waterproof if possible).  
- **Upper Body:**  
  → Gloves (lightweight, for grip and warmth).  
- **Lower Body:**  
  → Capri pants or shorts (quick-dry material, with built-in liner if trail running).  
- **Head/Extremities:**  
  → Cap or baseball hat (sun protection).  
- **Footwear:**  
  → Trail running shoes with **grip/tread** (sturdy for hills, good ankle support).  
- **Extras:**  
  → Small pack/vest (to carry layers if needed).  

---

### **12-Mile Night Segment (Final Miles)**
Prioritize **warmth, visibility, and warmth retention**. Night temps can drop significantly.  
- **Base Layer:**  
  → Moisture-wicking shirt/pants (same as day segment).  
- **Mid-Layer:**  
  → **Insulated jacket or fleece** (lightweight, packable; e.g., puffy vest/jacket).  
- **Outer Layer:**  
  → **Windproof/waterproof shell jacket** (essential for cold/wet conditions).  
- **Upper Body:**  
  → Gloves (thicker than day gloves).  
  → **Headlamp/handheld light** + **extra batteries** (non-negotiable!).  
- **Lower Body:**  
  → Trail pants or shorts (same as day segment).  
- **Head/Extremities:**  
  → **Buff or fleece hat** (cover ears/neck; warm hat is critical).  
- **Footwear:**  
  → Same trail shoes as day segment.  
- **Extras:**  
  → Small pack/vest (to stash layers if needed).  

---

### **Key Upgrades for Night Segment**  
- **Warm hat + buff:** Blocks wind and retains body heat.  
- **Insulated jacket:** Adds critical warmth if temps drop below 50°F (10°C).  
- **Extra batteries:** Lights die fast—don’t get caught in darkness.
