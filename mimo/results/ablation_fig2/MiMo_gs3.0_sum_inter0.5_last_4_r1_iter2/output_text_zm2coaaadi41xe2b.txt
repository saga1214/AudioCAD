Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! For authentic, delicious options, check out these places:

First, there's Noodle House in Little Elm. They serve a wide variety of Asian-inspired dishes, including stir-fried noodles, and their signature dish is the "House Special" which is a flavorful noodle dish.

Next, there's The Rice Boat in Plano. It’s a Vietnamese restaurant known for its pho and noodle dishes. They have a great selection of fresh, handmade noodles.

Finally, there's Ramen Lab in Plano. It specializes in ramen with a modern twist, offering both traditional and creative options.

As for protein bars, I’d recommend Quest or Clif Bars. They’re both high in protein and low in sugar, making them great for snacks.
