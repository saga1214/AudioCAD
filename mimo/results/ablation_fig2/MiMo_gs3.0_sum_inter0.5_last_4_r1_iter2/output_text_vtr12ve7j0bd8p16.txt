That sounds like a fantastic plan. Calgary is in a prime spot for outdoor adventures. You could both hike the famous Bow Falls, which is a beautiful waterfall in the Rockies. Or, for a bit of a thrill, there's the Banff Gondola, which takes you up to a mountain top with incredible views and a restaurant. If you want to stay close to the city, there's the St. Patrick's Glacier in the Kananaskis region, which is a bit of a hike but offers stunning views and a refreshing swim in the ice-melt lake. A really unique experience is the Icefields Parkway, where you can see massive glaciers and even take a helicopter tour over them. And if you're looking for something more relaxing, the Elk & Ice Wine Trail has charming little wineries and breweries set up in the beautiful Kananaskis Valley.
