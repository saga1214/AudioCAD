Here are quick, refined recipes for both drinks using common ingredients—perfect for your party:

### 🥂 **Vodka Mixer: Cucumber-Mint Refresher**  
*(Refreshing, soda-free, elegant)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz vodka (leftover!)  
  - 4-5 cucumber slices (thinly sliced)  
  - 6-8 fresh mint leaves  
  - 1 oz fresh lime juice  
  - 2 oz soda water  
  - Ice + soda water for serving  
- **Method**:  
  1. Muddle cucumber and mint in a shaker.  
  2. Add vodka, lime juice, and ice. Shake.  
  3. Strain into a chilled glass. Top with soda water.  
  4. Garnish with a mint sprig and cucumber wheel.  

### 🥃 **Argentine Fernet Soda**  
*(Bitter-sweet, herbal, soda-free)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1 oz Fernet-Branca (or similar herbal liqueur)  
  - 2 oz fresh lime juice  
  - 4 oz soda water (non-carbonated if preferred)  
  - Ice  
  - Lime wedge for garnish  
- **Method**:  
  1. Fill a glass with ice.  
  2. Add Fernet and lime juice. Stir briefly.  
  3. Top with soda water.  
  4. Stir gently. Garnish with a lime wedge.  

### 💡 **Tips**:  
- **Serving**: Offer both drinks in separate pitchers/glasses to avoid cross-contamination.  
- **Flexibility**: Adjust sweetness with a splash of soda water if lime is too tart.  
- **Allergy Safe**: Double-check garnishes (e.g., avoid citrus oils if sensitive).
