I'll give you five fantastic road trip ideas from Vancouver that are perfect for a first baby and a new Golden Retriever, all with a focus on beautiful scenery and great food.

First, about an hour away is the Okanagan Valley. You can take a scenic drive along Lake Okanagan, swim in the lakes, and visit charming wineries. For dining, try the lakeside restaurant at the Delta Okanagan Hotel.

Second, about three hours away, is Victoria. You can see the Inner Harbour, walk through the gardens at Butchart Park, and visit the famous Empress Hotel for a historic meal. You can also visit the Royal BC Museum.

Third, about four hours away, is Whistler. You can hike the trails, take the gondola up for amazing views, and walk around Whistler Village. For a special meal, consider the views at the Blackcomb Glacier Restaurant.

Fourth, about six hours away, is Banff National Park. You can drive the Icefields Parkway, which is one of the most beautiful roads in the world, and visit the famous Lake Louise and Moraine Lake. The Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise has a wonderful restaurant.

And fifth, about eight hours away, is the Fraser Canyon. You can explore the Okanagan Lake Scenic Road and visit the historic town of Osoyoos. For a special meal, try the wine bar and restaurant at the Osoyoos Desert Inn.

Enjoy your adventures!
