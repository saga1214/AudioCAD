Clicker training works through a simple three-step process:

1. **Marking the Behavior**:  
   You click the clicker **immediately** when Galileo performs the correct action (e.g., sitting).  
   *Example*: As he lowers his backside, click!

2. **Rewarding the Behavior**:  
   Give him a high-value treat (like tiny pieces of chicken) **within 1-2 seconds** of the click.  
   *Why it works*: The click signals "Yes, that’s right!"—the treat reinforces it.

3. **Repetition**:  
   Repeat this 10-15 times per session. Gradually phase out treats, using the click alone as a reward.

**Key Tips**:  
- Use small, easy-to-eat treats (e.g., freeze-dried chicken).  
- Click **only** for perfect execution.  
- Keep sessions short (5-10 minutes) and fun.  
- Pair with verbal commands like "sit" later.  

**Science Note**: The click bridges the gap between the behavior and the reward, making learning faster and clearer than just using words or treats alone.
