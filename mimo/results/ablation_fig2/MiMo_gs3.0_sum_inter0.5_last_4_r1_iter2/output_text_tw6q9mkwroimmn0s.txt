Okay, you want some island-inspired, spicy mango-friendly meals considering diabetes restrictions, plus sweet desserts for an ocean night. Here are five ideas.

For the meals:
First up, Spicy Mango Shrimp Stir-Fry. You'd use shellless shrimp, of course, lots of mango, and some peppers for the spice. Serve it over brown rice.
Second, a Mango-Coconut Curry with your seafood, but no shellfish. You could use shrimp or chicken, and it would be creamy and sweet.
Third, a Mango Salsa with Grilled Shrimp and Avocado. This would be a fresh, spicy, and light option. Maybe serve that with some grilled chicken or fish.
Fourth, a Spicy Mango Glazed Salmon. You'd use fresh salmon, mango, a little ginger and chili, and serve it with quinoa.
And fifth, Island Pasta with Mango Pesto and Shrimp. This would be a creamy, spicy pasta with a mango-pesto twist.

For the desserts:
First, Mango and Coconut Chia Pudding. It's a sweet, tropical pudding that's good for her with diabetes.
Second, Spicy Mango Sorbet with Lime Zest. It's a refreshing and light frozen treat.
Third, Coconut Shrimp Cookies. Shellfish-free, obviously, and they'd have a little cinnamon or chili powder for a kick.
Fourth, Mango and Pineapple Fried Rice Pudding. It's a warm, sweet, and creamy dessert.
And fifth, Dark Chocolate Ocean-Spiced Truffles. You could add some sea salt and a hint of chili for a sweet and spicy finish.
