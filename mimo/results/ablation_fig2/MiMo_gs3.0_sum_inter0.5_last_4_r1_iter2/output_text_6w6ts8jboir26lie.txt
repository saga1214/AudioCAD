That sounds like a wonderful plan—cooking can be such a great way to unwind. Here are five simple, comforting dinner ideas you can whip up in the condo kitchen:

### 1. **Hearty Tomato Basil Pasta**  
   - Sauté garlic in olive oil, add crushed tomatoes, basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes, then toss with pasta (penne or spaghetti) and a handful of grated Parmesan.  
   - *Bonus:* Toss in spinach or cherry tomatoes halfway through cooking.  

### 2. Grocery Store Stir-Fry  
   - Grab pre-cut veggies (bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas), tofu or chicken, and soy sauce.  
   - Heat oil in a pan, stir-fry protein first, then veggies. Add ginger and garlic for extra flavor. Serve over rice or noodles.  

### 3. Creamy Chicken & Mushroom Soup  
   - Sauté mushrooms and onions, add chicken broth, cream, and shredded chicken. Simmer until thick. Garnish with fresh thyme or chives.  
   - *Comfort level:* 10/10 for a cozy, easy meal.  

### 4. Grilled Salmon with Lemon-Dill Potatoes  
   - Season salmon with lemon zest, dill, salt, and pepper. Grill or pan-sear for 10–12 minutes.  
   - Roast baby potatoes with olive oil, rosemary, and garlic.  

### 5. Light Blackened Fish Salad  
   - Mix blackened catfish fillets with avocado, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and a lime-cilantro dressing. Serve with whole-grain crackers or crusty bread.  
   - *Beach vibe:* Refreshing and summery!  

### Quick Tip:  
Pack a reusable water bottle and make iced herbal tea (mint or lemongrass) to sip while you cook. And remember—prioritize your relaxation! If the condo issues still feel overwhelming, call a friend or family member for a virtual check-in. Your self-care break should *feel* restorative.
