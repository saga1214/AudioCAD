Initally, the recommended approach is often to ask for a brief meeting. You could send a professional email that says something like, 'Hi, I would like to schedule some time to discuss my role and contributions. Would Tuesday at 10 AM work?'

This gives you a controlled way to start the conversation without putting you on the spot in a larger meeting.

Then, when you're in the meeting, it's helpful to frame your request around shared company goals. You could say something to the effect of, 'I'm really proud of the progress we've made on this project. As I see the team's focus shifting to the next phase, I wanted to discuss how my role might evolve to support those objectives.' This shifts the conversation from a personal request to a strategic discussion about your future with the company.
