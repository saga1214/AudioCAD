No problem! Here’s a full recap of all recommended measurements, categorized clearly for your refresher:

### **1. Core Body Measurements (take for EVERY fitting)**  
- **Bust**: Across the fullest part.  
- **Natural Waist**: The narrowest part of the torso, not the belt line.  
- **Hips**: At the fullest part of the hips (usually 8–10 inches below the waist).  

### **2. Essential Additions for Fitted Garments**  
- **Sleeve Length**: From shoulder seam to wrist.  
- **Shoulder Width**: Across the top of the shoulder for jackets/shirts.  
- **Inseam**: For pants/skirts (front to back).  

### **3. Advanced Measurements (for precision)**  
- **Bicep**: Mid-arm for sleeves.  
Neck: For collars.  
Thigh: From crotch to top of thigh for tailored pants.  
**Back Rise**: Distance from crotch to waistband (critical for tall clients!).
