Okay, let me quickly recap the two yogurt analogies we talked about.

First, we had throwing a party. In that one: Milk was the big empty room. Good bacteria were the special party guests. Keeping it warm energized the guests. The group hug the sugar formed was what made milk get thick and tangy into yogurt.

Then, our second analogy was making an organized net. Here: Milk was the disorganized crowd. The bacteria were tiny team coaches. The warmth made the coaches energetic. Holding hands and forming a tight net was what trapped the liquid. That net came from the milk's proteins linking together, which gave yogurt its thick, solid texture. The flavoring was like the team's uniforms, giving it an identity without changing the structure. 

Both analogies really get to the same technical points: the good bacteria are essential, heat activates the process, and the proteins in the milk are what create the final texture. Hope that helps!
