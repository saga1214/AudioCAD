Okay, you need a pick-me-up! Let's get you some energy and snacks. For drinks, you could have some green tea, it gives you a gentle caffeine boost without the crash. Or a coffee or espresso for a quick jolt. A smoothie is another great option, full of vitamins and a little caffeine. You could also try coconut water for electrolytes, or maybe an energy drink for a fast boost.

As for snacks, some good choices would be nuts and seeds, which have protein and fats to keep you full. Dark chocolate is great for the mood and has some caffeine. You could also have yogurt with fruit, or hard-boiled eggs for protein. And finally, trail mix is a classic, with a good mix of nuts, seeds, and dried fruit.

Hope that helps!
