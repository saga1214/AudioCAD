That's a tough choice! If I absolutely had to pick just one: solar energy still seems like the best overall pick. It’s scalable from a single rooftop to a massive farm, getting cheaper over time, and works in most places. However, hydropower is a very strong contender, especially where you have the right geography. Wind is also great in windy areas. But for pure overall efficiency in terms of availability, scalability, and cost, I’d stick with solar.
