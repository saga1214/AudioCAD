That coffee table is all yours to fill! Since you avoid sweet snacks, vending machine options might be hit or miss. But here are a few savory picks you might find:

First, there are often pretzels or pretzel sticks, which are salty and satisfying. You could also get cheese puffs or other crunchy, cheesy snacks. Some vending machines even have crackers or rice cakes that you can pair with those M&M's-style chocolate bars if you want a savory-and-sweet mix.

If you're up for a little DIY, you could buy a bag of nuts and pour them right onto the table. Or grab a bag of chips and share with a friend.

And if you do lean into the sweet side, you could go for something less chocolatey, like gummy bears or sour candies, or even savory-sweet combos like cheese-flavored crackers with chocolate chips.

Enjoy your project and your snacks!
