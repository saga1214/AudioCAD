### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia
1. **Prohibited** at:
   - Signalized intersections
   - Hills/curves (even without signs)
   - Where view is obstructed (<150m)
   - Near driveways/alleys
2. **Allowed** when:
   - Full visibility in both directions
   - No traffic signs/prohibitions
   - Not interfering with flow
3. **Penalties**:

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients
*(Classic Roman recipe uses guanciale)*

**Main:**
- 400g spaghetti
- 150g **guanciale** (or pancetta/bacon)
- 4 large eggs
- 100g **Pecorino Romano** cheese, finely grated
- Freshly ground black pepper

**Essential:**
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt (for pasta water)

**Important Tip:**  
Mix eggs/cheese/pepper *just* before adding to hot pasta to prevent scrambling. Stir constantly while cooking.
