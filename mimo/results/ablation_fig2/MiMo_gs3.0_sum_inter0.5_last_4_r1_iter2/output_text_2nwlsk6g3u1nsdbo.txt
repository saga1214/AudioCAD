You're welcome! Here's a quick recap of the three **shrimp main courses** I mentioned earlier:  

1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
   - *Description*: Garlic-butter shrimp pasta with spicy kick.  
   - *Ingredients*:  
     - 1 lb large shrimp (peeled/deveined)  
     - 8 oz pasta (e.g., linguine)  
     - 4 tbsp butter  
     - 4 garlic cloves (minced)  
     - ¼ cup olive oil  
     - ½ cup dry white wine (optional)  
     - ½ cup heavy cream  
     - ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese  
     - 1–2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat)  
     - Salt and black pepper to taste  

2. **Spicy Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
   - *Description*: Tomato-based pasta with fiery Italian flavors.  
   - *Ingredients*:  
     - 1 lb shrimp  
     - 8 oz pasta (e.g., spaghetti)  
     - 2 tbsp olive oil  
     - 3 garlic cloves (minced)  
     - 1 small onion (diced)  
     - 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes  
     - 3 tbsp tomato paste  
     - 2 tbsp red pepper flakes  
     - ¼ cup fresh basil (chopped)  
     - 1 tsp dried oregano  
     - Salt and pepper to taste  

3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
   - *Description*: Creamy Southern-style grits with spicy Cajun shrimp.  
   - *Ingredients*:  
     - 1 lb shrimp  
     - 1 cup quick-cooking grits  
     - 2 cups chicken broth  
     - ½ cup heavy cream  
     - 2 tbsp butter  
     - 2 green onions (sliced)  
     - 1 tsp Cajun seasoning (adjust for heat)  
     - ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)  
     - Salt and pepper to taste
