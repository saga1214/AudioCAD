I'm really sorry you're going through this—it sounds incredibly draining. Dealing with a difficult neighbor requires both emotional resilience and practical strategies. Here’s some advice to help you cope:

### 1. **Prioritize Your Mental Health**
   - **Set Boundaries**: Limit discussions about her (e.g., avoid venting loops) and protect your kids by explaining calmly that some adults make bad choices.  
   - **Focus on What You Control**: Enjoy dress-up days, Halloween prep, and music (like Forrest Frank!) as intentional "joy moments." These small wins build resilience.

### 2. **Document & Report**
   - **Record Evidence**: Keep a dated log of incidents (dates, times, specifics). If CPS involvement starts, cooperate fully with authorities—this may shift pressure away from you/kids.  
   - **Report to Authorities**: If CPS opens a case, they’ll investigate. If the police ignore calls, file a formal complaint or contact neighborhood associations for group action.

### 3. No Contact**
   - **Avoid Engagement**: Stress escalates when you react. If she approaches, stay polite but brief: *"I’m busy. Good day."*  
   - **Block Communication**: If she contacts you (phone/email), don’t respond. Use "Do Not Disturb" on your phone.

### 4. **Build Your Support System**
   - **Talk to Trusted People**: Share with friends/family to vent—but limit rumination.  
   - **Community Action**: Connect with other affected families. Neighborhood groups can pool concerns with HOAs or local police for stronger intervention.

### 5. **Legal Awareness**
   - **Know Your Rights**: Research local harassment/stress laws. If she targets your children, consult a lawyer.  
   - **Safety Plan**: If threats escalate, consider cameras, motion lights, or security measures.

### 6. **When to Escalate**
   - If CPS finds no basis, the case may close—but document everything. If she targets others, report to local authorities again. Persistent issues might warrant a restraining order.

### 💡 Quick Stress-Busters:
   - **Music Therapy**: Forrest Frank’s uplifting tracks (like *"Lemonade"*) can instantly shift your mood.  
   - **Halloween Prep Rituals**: Make costumes/candy shopping a bonding activity.  
   - **Deep Breaths**: When stressed, try box breathing (inhale 4 sec, hold 4 sec, exhale 4 sec).

**Remember**: You can’t control her, but you *can* control how you respond. Protect your peace first. If stress feels overwhelming, consider therapy or support groups—this kind of chronic stress takes a toll. You’re doing a great job keeping your kids safe and finding lightness amid chaos. 🌟
