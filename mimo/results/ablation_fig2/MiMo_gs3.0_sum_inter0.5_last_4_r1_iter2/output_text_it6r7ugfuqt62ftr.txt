### **4-Day Costa Rica Itinerary**
**Destination:** Tamarindo, Nicoya Peninsula  
*Why?* Warm (75–85°F), family-friendly, great for swimming/snorkeling, SUV-accessible roads, and celiac-friendly options.  

#### **Day 1: Arrival & Settle In**
- **Travel:** Fly into San José, rent an SUV, and drive to Tamarindo (2.5 hours).  
- **Stay:** Tamarindo Beach Hotel (beachfront) or La Concha (boutique). Both are SUV-friendly.  
- **Afternoon:** Check in, relax at the beach (swim!).  
- **Dinner:** **La Pescadora** (seafood + gluten-free options).  
- **Celiac Tip:** Confirm gluten-free needs when booking; they accommodate well.  

#### **Day 2: Beach Adventure & Water Fun**
- **Morning:** **Snorkeling at Playa Hermosa** (reefs teeming with fish). Rent gear or take a guided tour.  
- **Afternoon:** Kayaking or paddleboarding at Tamarindo Beach.  
- **Dinner:** **El Avión** (casual, gluten-free menu). Try their ceviche!  
- **Activity:** Sunset beach walk.  

#### **Day 3: Nature & Culture Day Trip**
- **Morning:** Drive to **Monteverde Cloud Forest** (1.5 hours). Hike boardwalks, spot wildlife, and visit butterfly gardens.  
- **Lunch:** Picnic with Trish’s Mini Donuts (gluten-free!) and local fruit.  
- **Afternoon:** Return to Tamarindo.  
- **Dinner:** **La Creperia** (fresh crepes + gluten-free waffles).  

#### **Day 4: Relax & Departure**
- **Morning:** Spa treatment at your hotel or beachfront yoga.  
- **Lunch:** **Tamarindo’s Fish Market** (grilled fish + gluten-free sides).  
- **Travel:** Drive to San José, fly home.  

---

### **Key Tips**
- **Celiac Safety:** Use apps like HappyCow to find celiac-friendly spots. **Trish’s Mini Donuts** (Pier 39) and **La Creperia** are musts. Always inform servers about celiac.  
- **Budget:** Flights to San José start at $300 round-trip (book early). Accommodations: $150–$300/night.  
- **Transport:** Rent an SUV for day trips (Arenal Volcano is nearby but cooler; Monteverde is ideal for your family).
