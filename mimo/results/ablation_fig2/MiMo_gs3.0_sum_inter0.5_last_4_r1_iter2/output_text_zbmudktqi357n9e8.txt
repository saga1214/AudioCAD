Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration, especially since their 'temporada de otoño' is basically their fall season, roughly from September to November. September and October will give you those super vibrant fall colors on the trees and plants there. November might be a little more muted, but still beautiful. It's also a great time for travel in Mexico, so yeah, I'd say go for it! Enjoy your trip and your painting inspiration!
