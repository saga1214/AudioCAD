Okay, kosher dietary laws can be tricky, especially in book clubs. The main things to watch out for are meat and dairy not being served together, and making sure no pork or shellfish is used. Since you have weenies, you'll need to double-check that they're kosher-certified, probably looking for a symbol like a U or a K on the package. Hummus is usually fine. Theme sweet like cookies should be checked for ingredients like milk or eggs—some have dairy. Fruit should be okay. Wine like Pinot Noir is generally fine, just make sure it's kosher-certified. Blood pudding is out because it's pork-based. Muffins in bacon are vegetarian but not kosher. So the key is just to verify all the packaged items have a kosher certification. That way you're covered.
