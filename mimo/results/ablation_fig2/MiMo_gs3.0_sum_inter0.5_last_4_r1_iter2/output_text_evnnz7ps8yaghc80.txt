It sounds like you're feeling overwhelmed, but determined to teach your son. Breaking your practice into scheduled chunks with different tools can make it less stressful and more manageable.

Here’s a sample weekly schedule you could follow: On Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, practice with two beanbags thrown by your dominant hand until it feels smooth, for about 15 minutes. Then, spend another 15 minutes practicing adding the third ball from your other hand in short sequences. On Tuesday and Thursday, focus on juggling two bags in non-dominant hand for 15 minutes, then add the third ball for another 15. On Saturday, try using scarves for slow-motion practice for about 20 minutes, focusing on the rhythm. Finally, on Sunday, take a full rest day.

Remember, stress gets in the way, so be patient with yourself. Celebrate the small victories and enjoy the process. You promised your son, and you will figure this out.
