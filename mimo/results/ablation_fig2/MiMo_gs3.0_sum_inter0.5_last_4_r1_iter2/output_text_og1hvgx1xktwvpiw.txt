Ugh, fine. Look, let me be very clear. The most common Boulevardier recipe, yes, uses three ingredients: rye or bourbon at one and a quarter ounces, sweet vermouth at one ounce, and Campari at one ounce. That is not the same as your friend's. The equal-parts version, where it's one ounce of each, is also a classic Boulevardier, just a popular variation. Your friend was right, not you. 

Now, as for a 'Last Chance,' which is also an equal-parts drink, it requires gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. Stir them well and garnish. Got it? 

So, the Boulevardier is whiskey to one ounce of vermouth to one ounce of Campari. The Last Word is gin to one ounce of vermouth to one ounce of green Chartreuse to one ounce of lime juice. 

And for Palm Desert weather, it will be sunny and in the 80s.
