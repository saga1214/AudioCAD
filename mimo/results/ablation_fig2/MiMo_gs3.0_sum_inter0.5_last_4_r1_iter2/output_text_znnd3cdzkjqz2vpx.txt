Okay, this is great! You've got all the basics covered. I would just add a few more things.

Make sure you have a really good pair of clamps—plumber's tape wrenches work great. They're essential for holding adjacent cabinets flush while you screw.

Also, make sure you have a drill with drill bits, and a stud finder that can actually locate studs accurately.

And most importantly, make sure everyone has a good pair of work gloves to protect your hands from splinters and scratches, and of course, a sturdy step stool or ladder.
