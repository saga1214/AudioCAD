Absolutely! Planning scuba diving in CostaRica can be incredible given your passion. Here’s a tailored plan to make your dream trip unforgettable, factoring in your scuba experience, debate about fins, and your past adventures:

### 1. **Fin Choice Finalized**
   - **Recommendation for Beginners:** **Short fins** are ideal for your early-to-mid-level dives. They’re compact, less tiring, and travel well—perfect for exploring calm or reef-rich sites.  
   - **Pro Tip:** Look for **adjustable short fins** (like FinDalu or Apeks) so you can tweak the fit as your leg strength grows.

### 2. **Best Sites in CostaRica for Scuba Divers**
   - **Cocos Island** (1.5hrs off mainland):  
     - **Why:** Strong currents, deep walls, caves, and **whale sharks** (seasonal). Ideal if you want adventure.  
     - **Best for:** Advanced divers (strong swimming).  
   - **Tortuguero National Park**:  
     - **Why:** Calm, sheltered coves, **reef sharks**, rays, and marine iguanas. Great for beginners.  
     - **Bonus:** Rainforest excursions!  
   - **Puerto Viejo (Caribbean side)**:  
     - **Why:** **Turtle Gardens** (near Drake’s Bay), coral reefs, and snorkeling with sea turtles.  
   - **Aguas Calientes (Pacific side)**:  
     - **Why:** Crystal-clear waters, **tropical fish**, and visibility up to 100ft.  

### 3. **Dive Trip Structure**
   - **Day 1-2:** Arrive in San José → Travel to **Puerto Viejo** (Caribbean) or **Aguas Calientes** (Pacific).  
   - **Day 3-4:**  
     - **Beginner-Friendly Dives:** Turtle Gardens (Caribbean) or coral reefs (Pacific).  
     - **Mid-Level Adventure:** Cocos Island (if you’re up for a boat trip; best in **May-Oct** for calm seas).  
   - **Day 5:** Relax, snorkel at a local bay, or visit a rainforest reserve.  

### 4. **Key Tips for Your Trip**
   - **Best Time to Dive:** **November-April** (dry season, calmest waters). Avoid May-Oct if prone to seasickness.  
   - **Currents:** Caribbean sites (e.g., Tortuguero) are calmer; Pacific sites (Cocos) have stronger currents.  
   - **Marine Life:** Turtles, reef sharks, rays, and occasional whale sharks (Cocos).  
   - **Logistics:**  
     - Use a **liveaboard dive boat** for Cocos Island (3-day trip recommended).  
     - Book with **local operators** (e.g., **Dive Cocos**, **Tortuga Eco Lodge**).  

### 5. **Gear & Packing**
   - **Must-Haves:**  
     - Short fins (with spare tank weights).  
     - Underwater camera (GoPro + housing).  
     - Reef-safe sunscreen.  
     - Snorkel mask, fins, and dive computer.  
   - **Packing:** Lightweight wetsuit (if diving in Pacific), dry bag, and waterproof phone case.  

### 6. **Beyond Diving**
   - **Rainforest Trek:** Spot sloths and monkeys in Tortuguero.  
   - **Volcano Hike:** Arenal Volcano (Pacific side) for hot springs afterward.  
   - **Local Cuisine:** Try **Pescado a la Tipitapa** (fish in tomato sauce) or **Gallo Pinto** (Costa Rican rice and beans).  

### Final Advice:
Costa Rica’s tropical waters rival Honduras and Belize in beauty—especially with its mix of reefs, walls, and marine megafauna. With short fins, you’ll focus on maneuverability and enjoyment. Prioritize **Cocos Island** if you crave adventure, or **Puerto Viejo/Tortuguero** for serene, beginner-friendly dives.
