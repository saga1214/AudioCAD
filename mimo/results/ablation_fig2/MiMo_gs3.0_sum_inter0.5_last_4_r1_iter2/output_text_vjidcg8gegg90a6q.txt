Alright, let's focus on the bedding for the brooder. The most rodent-safe and effective choice is wood shavings. Make sure you get large-flake shavings, similar to what you planned for the coop, but keep a layer that's only about two to three inches deep so it doesn't get soggy.

Another option is to use pine cones. Just spread them out and let the chicks huddle in them. They are great at blocking drafts and are naturally antimicrobial.

Avoid using sand for the brooder; it can be too abrasive for their tiny feet and it won't help with heat retention. The key is to always provide them with a dry spot to get under the heat plate.
