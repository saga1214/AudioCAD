Yes, there are several excellent ways to explore the history. The most direct way is to visit the Buffalo and Erie Canal Museum in Lockport. It's housed in a building that was once a canal warehouse, so it's very authentic. It has a great exhibit on the locks, and you can even watch the boats go through them.

There's also the Old Erie Canal Museum in Rochester, which is a great starting point back home. It covers the history of the entire Finger Lakes and Rochester canal corridor.

For a more hands-on experience, you could check out the canal towpaths in Rochester, like the one along Seneca Lake. You can walk or bike along the historic route where mules once pulled the barges. It's a very peaceful way to connect with the past.
