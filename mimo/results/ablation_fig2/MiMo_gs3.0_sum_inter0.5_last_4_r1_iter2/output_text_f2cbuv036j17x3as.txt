Okay, diesel camper vans are workhorses, but they definitely need a pre-trip check. For your diesel camper, you should have your mechanic inspect the brakes. Common signs of wear include a soft or spongy brake pedal, or if the brakes feel like they’re not fully engaging when you press down. Also, if you hear any grinding noises from the wheels when you turn, that’s a bad sign. For the diesel engine itself, you might notice some rough idling or black soot on the exhaust, which could mean the fuel injectors need attention. As for spark plugs, diesel engines don’t use them in the same way as gasoline engines, so you don’t need to change them like you would with a car. Diesel engines use compression ignition, so you don’t have to worry about spark plugs. However, you should definitely check the diesel fuel filter and make sure the oil level is correct. It's always a good idea to get a professional inspection before a long trip like this. It can save you a lot of trouble on the road.
