Let's break down your scenario: **investing $10,000 at $62.87 per share** with the goal of:  
- **10% gain** within a month (minimum return)  
- **10% loss** maximum (downside protection)  

### 1. **10% Gain Scenario**  
- **Target Gain**: $1,000 (10% of $10,000)  
- **Number of Shares**:  
  $10,000 ÷ $62.87 ≈ **159 shares**  
- **Required Selling Price**:  
  ($10,000 + $1,000) ÷ 159 shares ≈ **$69.96 per share**  
- **Gain Calculation**:  
  - **Per Share Gain**: $69.96 - $62.87 = **$7.09**  
  - **Percentage Gain**: ($7.09 ÷ $62.87) × 100 ≈ **11.3%**  

**Result**: You’d need to sell at **$69.96/share** to hit your 10% gain target (and exceed it by 1.3%).  

---

### 2. **10% Loss Scenario (Stop-Loss)**  
- **Maximum Loss**: $1,000 (10% of $10,000)  
- **Number of Shares**: Same as above → **159 shares**  
- **Stop-Loss Price**:  
  ($10,000 - $1,000) ÷ 159 shares ≈ **$61.87 per share**  
- **Loss Calculation**:  
  - **Per Share Loss**: $62.87 - $61.87 = **$1.00**  
  - **Percentage Loss**: ($1.00 ÷ $62.87) × 100 ≈ **1.6%**  

**Result**: If the stock drops to **$61.87/share**, you’d lock in a **1.6% loss** (well below your 10% max loss).
