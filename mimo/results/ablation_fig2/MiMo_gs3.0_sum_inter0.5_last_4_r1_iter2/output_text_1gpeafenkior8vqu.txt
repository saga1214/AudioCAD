### **Detailed Schedule**
#### **Days 1–4: Tokyo (Art & Design Inspiration for Slides)**  
- **Day 1**:  
  - Morning: Check out (10 AM). Take taxi to Tokyo Station (¥2,500).  
  - Afternoon: Explore **Asakusa** (traditional art: Senso-ji Temple, calligraphy shops).  
  - Evening: Shibuya Scramble Crossing (font/layout inspiration: neon signs).  
- **Day 2**:  
  - Morning: **Meiji Shrine** (Buddhist sculpture, nature motifs).  
  - Afternoon: **Ueno Park** (museums: Tokyo National Museum for art history literacy content).  
  - Evening: Workout at hotel gym (6–7 PM).  
- **Day 3**:  
  - Morning: **Edo-Tokyo Museum** (learn Japanese art evolution).  
  - Afternoon: **Ebisu Ukiyo-e Museum** (focus on Hokusai/ Hiroshica prints for image incorporation ideas).  
- **Day 4**:  
  - Morning: **Asakusa Craft House** (kimono patterns/colors scheme inspiration).  
  - Afternoon: **teamLab Planets** (digital art design aspect reference).  
  - Evening: Dinner in Shinjuku.  

#### **Day 5: Travel to Osaka & Kyoto Day Trip**  
- Morning: Shinkansen to Kyoto (¥13,000).  
- Afternoon: **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (layout example: winding paths). Zen gardens color schemes.  
- Evening: Travel to Osaka (¥2,800).  

#### **Day 6: Kyoto (Art & Nature Balance)**  
- Morning: **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** (layout/balance).  
- Afternoon: **Kinkaku-ji** (gold leaf color scheme).  
- Evening: Nishiki Market.  

#### **Day 7: Nara (Ancient Art & Sculpture)**  
- Morning: JR Special Rapid to Nara (¥1,800).  
  - **Todai-ji Temple** (giant Buddha sculpture).  
  - **Nara Park** (stone Buddha carvings).  
- Evening: Return to Osaka.  

#### **Days 8–9: Osaka (Kimono Culture & Modern Design)**  
- **Day 8**:  
  - Morning: **Osaka Castle** (woodblock print-style images).  
  - Afternoon: **Dotonbori** (vibrant kimono patterns/colors).  
  - Evening: **Tennoji Park** (contemporary art installations for font/layout ideas).  
- **Day 9**:  
  - Morning: **Osaka Museum of Housing & Living** (traditional Japanese design).  
  - Afternoon: **Universal Studios** (optional; Hokusai-inspired exhibits).  

#### **Day 1 Checkout & Travel**  
- Morning: Check out (10 AM). Taxi to Osaka Airport (¥4,500).  
- Afternoon: Shinkansen to Tokyo (¥14,000).  

#### **Day 10: Coursework Start**  
- Morning: Arrive in Tokyo, submit coursework immediately.  
- Focus: Use notes/images from Japan for slide literacy/design.
