For wedding speeches, a standard five-minute slot corresponds to about 750 words. To help you structure your speech, consider starting by introducing how long you two have known each other and sharing one funny high school memory. Then transition to observing her relationship develop with her new husband—from skepticism to commitment. Finish with a heartfelt toast and keep it concise. If you tend to speak quickly when nervous, aim for 1,125 words instead. Practice reading aloud to get a feel for the pacing. Remember, wedding planners usually give cues subtly, so don’t stress too much about exact timing. Just focus on delivering a heartfelt message!
