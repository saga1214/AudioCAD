### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia
1. **Visibility Requirement**: You must see **150 meters (approx. 490 feet)** in both directions before making a U-turn.  
   - *Estimation Tip*: 150m ≈ **1.5 football fields** (100m/field). If you can't see this distance safely, wait.  
2. **Prohibited Locations**:  
   - Intersections with traffic signals (unless permitted by a sign).  
   - Near curves, hills, or areas with obscured visibility.  
   - On multi-lane highways or bridges.  
   - Within 100m of a bus stop or pedestrian crossing.  
3. **Penalties for Violations**:  
   - **Traffic ticket**: $160 + court costs (total ~$200+).  
   - Points added to your driving record (2 points under BC’s *Motor Vehicle Act*).  
   - Risk of impounding your vehicle for unsafe practices.  

**Key Takeaway**: Always check road signs and prioritize safety over convenience. If unsure, use a designated U-turn lane or wait for a clear gap.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
*(Serves 2)*  
**Core Ingredients**:  
- 200g spaghetti  
- 150g guanciale (or pancetta/bacon)  
- 3 large eggs  
- 50g Pecorino Romano cheese, grated  
- 20g Parmigiano Reggiano, grated  
- Freshly ground black pepper  
- Salt for pasta water  

**Optional**:  
- Chives or parsley for garnish  

**Prep Tip**:  
- Mix egg, cheese, and pepper in a bowl.  
- Cook guanciale first, then set aside.  
- Toss hot pasta with the egg mixture *off-heat* to avoid scrambling.  

Enjoy your meal! 🍝
