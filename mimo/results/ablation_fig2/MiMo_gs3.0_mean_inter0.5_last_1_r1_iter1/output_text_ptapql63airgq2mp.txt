Absolutely! Let’s channel that Japanese-inspired umami adventure for your movie night. Here’s a curated mix of simple and unique food choices foodie frames will adore:

### Simple (Easy to Prep, Crowd-Pleasing)
1. **Matcha Everything**:  
   - **Matcha Popcorn**: The ultimate simple treat. Offer 3 flavors: butter, caramel, and *umami cheese*.  
   - **Mochi Ice Cream Cups**: Soft, chewy mochi shells filled with matcha, red bean paste, or mango.  
2. **DIY Donburi Bowls**:  
   - Simple rice bowls with pre-portioned toppings:  
     - *Classic*: Miso-glazed eggplant, crispy tempura veggies, sesame seeds.  
     - *Savory-Sweet*: Tuna mayo, shredded chicken, cucumber, drizzled with ponzu.  
3. **Yuzu-Glazed Fruit Skewers**:  
   - Quick, refreshing, and vibrant (yuzu adds citrusy depth).

### Unique (Bold, Adventurous, Interactive)
1. **Kimchi Seaweed Platter**:  
   - Fermented kimchi strips wrapped around nori sheets, served with *tamarind-spiced chili oil* for dipping.  
2. **Umami Bomb Station**:  
   - **Dried Octopus Bites**: Chewy, savory, and packed with umami.  
   - **Fish Rillettes**: Slow-cooked white fish in a spicy tamarind glaze.  
   Movie guests assemble their own bites!  
3. **Sweet-Sour "Nihonjin" Tacos**:  
   - DIY tacos with unusual fillings:  
     - *Fish taco*: Grilled mackerel with pickled ginger slaw.  
     - *Pulled pork taco*: With yuzu-miso glaze.  
   Toppings: Kimchi, sesame seeds, crispy shallots.  
4. **Snow Monkey Hot Pot Mini**:  
   - **Dried Squid & Mushroom Hot Pot**: Simmer in a broth of dashi, soy, and shiitake. Serve in tiny cups with:  
     - Soft tofu, enoki mushrooms, and *umami-packed miso-ginger sauce*.  

### Showstopper Finisher  
- **"Japan Travel" Dessert Platter**:  
  - *Matcha Tiramisu*: Layers of matcha-soaked ladyfingers.  
  - *Red Bean Mochi*: Served warm with a drizzle of red bean paste.  
  - *Tiramisu with Yuzu Cream*: For a citrusy twist.  

### Pro Tips  
- **Drinks**: Pair with *yuzu-infused sparkling wine* or *matcha-miso latte* (hot/cold).  
- **Movie Theme**: Pick a film inspired by your friend’s trip (e.g., *Studio Ghibli’s Spirited Away* or *Samurai Champloo*).  
- **Ambiance**: Light LED lanterns and play traditional Japanese music softly in the background.
