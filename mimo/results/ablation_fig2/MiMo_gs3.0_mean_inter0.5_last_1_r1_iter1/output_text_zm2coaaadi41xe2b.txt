Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! For ramen, check out Tonkatsu House, which is famous for its tonkotsu ramen, or Ichiran if you want a single-portion bowl. If you're craving pho, Pho 79 is a great choice. For some more unique options, there's a place called Noodle House that serves Taiwanese beef noodle soup. And for Korean-style bibimbap or other noodle dishes, check out Gogung.

As for protein bars, I'd recommend Quest, which are high in protein and low in sugar. Or maybe Optimum Nutrition, which is a classic. If you want something with a bit more flavor, consider Clif Bars or RXBAR.
