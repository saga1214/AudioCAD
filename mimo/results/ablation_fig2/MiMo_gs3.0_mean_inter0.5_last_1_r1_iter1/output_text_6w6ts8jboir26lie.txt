Okay, she's on the mend and wants to cook to unwind. That's a great idea.

Here are five simple and comforting dinner ideas.

One: A quick stir-fry. Just grab some chicken or shrimp, a bag of stir-fry veggies, and a jar of your favorite sauce. It's fast and easy.

Two: A cozy pasta dish. You can do a simple baked fettuccine or just toss pasta with pesto and cherry tomatoes.

Three: A fresh taco bar. Grab ground beef or tofu, tortillas, lettuce, cheese, salsa, guacamole... it's fun and customizable.

Four: A hearty soup. A chicken noodle or tomato basil soup is perfect for a rainy day.

Five: A grilled cheese sandwich with a twist. Use good bread and melt some buttery cheese, and maybe add a fried egg on top.
