Here's your **clothing gear packing list**, split into two segments based on conditions and time of day:

### **20 Miles (Late Afternoon to Night, Aid Stations Available)**  
Bring **moderate layers** since temps cool as night approaches:  
- **Moisture-wicking base layer** (top & bottom)  
- **Light fleece or windbreaker** (mid-layer for warmth)  
- **Wind/water-resistant outer shell** (e.g., lightweight rain jacket)  
- **Gloves** (lightweight for early evening)  
- **Lightweight beanie or cap** (for head/neck)  
- **Trail shoes with aggressive tread** (hills are key!)  
- **Headlamp/handheld light + spare batteries**  
- **Small pack or vest** (to carry layers if needed)  

### **Final 12 Miles (Full Night, Cold & Dark)**  
Prioritize **warmth and visibility**:  
- **Moisture-wicking base layer**  
- **Insulated mid-layer** (fleece, puffy vest, or lightweight down jacket)  
- **Windproof/waterproof outer layer** (critical for night wind chill)  
- **Warm gloves** (thicker than early segment)  
- **Warm beanie** (or cap with ear flaps)  
- **Headlamp/handheld light + extra batteries**  
- **Buff or neck gaiter** (wrap around head/neck if cold)  
- **Lightweight pack or handheld vest** (for extra layers)  
- **Socks** (extra pair if needed)  

### **Key Extras for Both Segments**  
- **Extra socks** (blister prevention!)  
- **Quick-dry towel or bandana** (for wiping sweat)  
- **Reflective gear** (ankle bracelet or vest for safety)  

Aid stations handle nutrition, but **your clothing keeps you warm, dry, and visible**. For hilly night running, the final 12 miles needs extra insulation—don’t underestimate wind chill! Good luck tomorrow night! 🌟
