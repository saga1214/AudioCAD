Okay, I see you're asking about juice cleanses or water fasts to get rid of these chemicals. Unfortunately, there's no scientific evidence that those methods work for removing PFAS from your body. They actually stick around for a very long time.

The most effective way to avoid them is to reduce your exposure in the first place. The five products to avoid are:
First, non-stick cookware.
Second, grease-resistant food packaging.
Third, things like carpets and outdoor clothing.
Fourth, detergents and some cosmetics.
And fifth, unfiltered tap water.

PFAS are found in a wide range of things, including fish, toilet paper, paint, and even electronics. While they are persistent, reducing your daily exposure is the best strategy.
