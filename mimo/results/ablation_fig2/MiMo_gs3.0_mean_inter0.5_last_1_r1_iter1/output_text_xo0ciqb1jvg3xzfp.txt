That's an excellent question, and the answer is complex. For the Pilgrims, it was a moment of celebration. But for the Wampanoag, it was a strategic event that began a relationship with major long-term consequences.

So, it was not a 'happy' occasion for both sides in the same way. For the Wampanoag, it was a temporary alliance, not a celebration of what was to come. The traditional story of Thanksgiving often leaves out this crucial context.
