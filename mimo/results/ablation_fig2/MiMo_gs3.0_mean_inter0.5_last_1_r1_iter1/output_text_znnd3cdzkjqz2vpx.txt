Okay, you've got all the essentials, but there are a few other things that will make the process much smoother and safer. You'll definitely need a cordless power drill and a good set of screws. Habitat cabinets usually come with them, but having a drill makes driving them in so much easier.

Shims are also key, especially for base cabinets, to get them perfectly level underneath. You might also want to consider some painter's tape to protect your walls from scratches while you work, and maybe a small trash bag to catch any sawdust or debris.

For tools, a stud finder with an electric level is essential. And definitely have safety glasses handy, just in case. You've got this, it's going to be awesome when they're all done!
