**The Logistics (West Coast to NV):**
*   **Distance:** LA/SFV to Joshua Tree NP is ~3 hours. To NV border (like Tonopah/Elko) is ~5-6 hours. To Vegas? Add another 3 hours from JT.
*   **JT NP Camping:** *Official* sites: Hidden Valley, Black Rock Canyon, Jumbo Rocks (reservations *absolutely required* via Recreation.gov, especially in peak season - book NOW!). Costs: $20-$30/night (RV sites more). *Pro Tip:* Check "Self-Imposed Overnight Stays" (SIOS) rules if heading to BLM land *after* JT - see below.
*   **NV BLM Land (Your Free Camping Hack):**
    *   **What it is:** Public land managed by BLM. Designated "BLM Campground Sites" exist (bookable, $10-$20/night). But the real van lifer secret is **dispersed camping** on designated BLM areas.
    *   **Where in NV?** Search "BLM dispersed camping NV" on the [BLM Land Portal](https://www.blm.gov/). Key areas near your route:
        *   **Mojave National Preserve (East of JT):** Stunning deserts, but *check current dispersed camping status* (it can fluctuate).
        *   **Great Basin National Park Area:** Near Topaz Lake, Wheeler Peak (scenic, cooler).
        *   **Valley of Fire State Park (NV side):** *Official* campsites ($20-$30) *and* dispersed camping zones (check park rules).
        *   **Near Tonopah/Elko:** Search BLM maps for accessible areas off highways like US-95.
    *   **Cost:** **Free** for dispersed camping (generous $5 fee often accepted but not required). *Always* carry out everything.

**Crafting Your "Proper Vegas" Finale:**
Free desert camping beats a $300 hotel any day! But Vegas is a beast – plan to avoid the tourist traps:

1.  **Stay Off the Strip (Seriously!):** $400+ for a basic room? Nope. Your adventure:
    *   **Off-Strip Gems:** **The Cosmopolitan** (lifestyle, great food/nightlife, walkable to clubs) or **Aloft** (cool, budget-friendly, downtown vibe). **Las Vegas North** has better hotel deals (Caesars, Bellagio, etc.).
    *   **Budget Hack:** **Motel 6** off Telfair Rd or **Stratosphere** (cheap rooms, *free* skydiving views, decent food court).
2.  **Hot Shower & Real Bathroom:** Book a hotel with a *real* bath/shower, not a Motel 6ette. Check reviews for cleanliness.
3.  **Gambling Strategy (Since she met at gamonom!):**
    *   **Play Smart:** Start small. $5/$10 slots are fun. $15-$50 table limits (Baccarat, Roulette, Craps) are the sweet spot for casual fun. **Set a strict loss limit ($50?) and walk away.**
    *   **Avoid the Pit:** The casino floor is designed to keep you there. Stick to your plan.
    *   **Focus on the Vibe:** It's entertainment, not a job. Enjoy the lights, music, and unique atmosphere.
4.  **Beyond the Casino Floor:**
    *   **Food:** **Off-Strip Eateries:** Lotus of Siam (Thai), Big Dog's (BBQ), Lotus, Secret Pizza. **Strip:** Bacchanal Buffet (high-end, pricey), Gourmet Garden Buffet (better value).
    *   **Drinks:** **Off-Strip:** Atomic Liquors (historic, dive bar), El Cortez (old Vegas charm). **Strip:** Bar Open (cozy, great cocktails), The Chandelier (CEOs only, but stunning - book ahead).
    *   **Activities:** **Free:** Walking the Strip at night, Fremont Street Experience (downtown, free shows), Bellagio Fountains. **Paid:** Helicopter tour over the Strip, Topgolf, comedy shows. **Unique:** Neon Museum (downtown), Pinball Museum.
    *   **Nightlife:** Find a cool bar or club *off* the main drag. The Cosmo's rooftop (Crown), Drai's (Luxor - expensive but iconic), or dive bars like Atomic.

**Adventure Checklist for GF:**

*   **Pack Smart:** Water (tonnes!), sun protection (SPF 50+!), layers (desert nights are COLD!), bug spray, first-aid kit, portable charger, books/music.
*   **Safety:** Share your BLM camping location with someone. Know where the nearest road is. Be aware of wildlife (rattlesnakes!).
*   **Leave No Trace:** Pack out *everything*. Respect the desert.
*   **Vegas Rules:** Bring ID, credit card (for taxi/Uber if needed), cash for small tips/food trucks. **Set gambling limits BEFORE you go.**
