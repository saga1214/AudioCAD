Let's trace the timeline. The first president on the list was Siad Barre, who was in power from 1969 to 1991. That's when he was overthrown.

The movie "Black Hawk Down," depicting the 1993 mission, happened *before* the U.S./UN intervention officially ended.

The U.S. and UN forces actually withdrew from Somalia in 1995. That’s when the operation was called off. So, from the time Barre was overthrown in 1991 until the U.S. left in 1995, that's about four years.
