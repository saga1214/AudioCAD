Alright, setting off smoke alarms with wood spoons is behind you now.

First, and most importantly, NO WOVEN SPONS. Use a metal spoon or tongs forever. Seriously, keep a wooden spoon in a different room while you're cooking.

Second, stir your stir-fry constantly. It cooks really fast, so things can burn easily. Just keep an eye on it.

Third, drain your noodles well. Any water left in them will steam instead of being part of your stir-friend sauce. You can even rinse them briefly under cold water and shake them dry to help.

Then just toss everything in the pan with your sauce. Done
