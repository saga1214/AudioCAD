Congratulations on your new dehydrator. There are many different things that you can dehydrate. Fruits are a great place to start. Things like apples, bananas, mangoes, and strawberries are all easy to dehydrate. You can also dehydrate vegetables such as zucchini, carrots, and onions. Meat is also an option. You can dehydrate things like chicken breast, beef stew meat, and even fish. There are also many different herbs and spices that you can dehydrate, such as basil, oregano, and thyme.
