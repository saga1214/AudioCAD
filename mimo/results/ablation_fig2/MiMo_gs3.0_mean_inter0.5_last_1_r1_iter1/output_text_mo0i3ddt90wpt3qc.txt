You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is a great gluten-free foundation. Along those same gluten-free lines, rice cakes with almond butter are always a popular and filling camp snack.

If you're looking for something a little more crunchy, gluten-free pretzels are another nice option.

And for a healthy and crunchy alternative, veggie sticks with hummus is also a great choice.
