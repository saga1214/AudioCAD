Let’s break it down task by task. Drywall stuff, joint compound usually ten to twenty bucks small tub, sand tools five, putty knife ten to fifteen, primer if needed ten, damaged molding if any, say twenty, nails adhesive five. Cake items add up slowly: flour dollar fifty, sugar three, baking powder a dollar, butter four, eggs three bucks, milk three, extract two. Pan's are cheap, five bucks round square.

Birthdays, balloons five to ten, movie game supplies ten to twenty, frosting materials five, decorations five. All in for drywall, let’s say you need molding, around fifty to seventy. All in for cake, around twenty-five to thirty-five.

So, I’d say bring eighty to one hundred and five dollars total, and you should be covered for both tasks plus the birthday extras. It’s always smart to have a little extra for unexpected sizes or last-minute things. Safe shopping!
