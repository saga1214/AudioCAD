### 🍬 Treat Recommendations
- **Best flavors:** Chicken (puppies love it!), beef, or cheese (mild cheddar). Avoid overly salty or sugary treats.  
- **Texture:** Small, soft, and easy to chew (like tiny bits of peanut butter or freeze-dried chicken).  
- **Size:** Tiny pieces so they can be eaten quickly during training.  
- **Budget-friendly:** Plain boiled chicken, hot dogs (cut into coins), or store-bought training treats.

### 🎯 Clicker Training Explained
Clicker training uses a **three-step process** to build trust and communication:  
1. **The Clicker:** A simple tool that makes a sharp "click" sound.  
2. **The Reward:** Immediately after clicking, give a treat. The puppy learns that "click = treat."  
3. **The Command:** Click *only* when they do the desired action (e.g., sit). Pair the click with the word "sit" and a treat. Repeat until they associate the word with the action.  

**Example:**  
- Puppy sits → You click → Give treat → Say "sit!"  
- Repeat 10–20 times daily. Soon, they’ll sit on cue!

### Key Takeaways
- **Clicker training:** Builds anticipation—puppies love the click!  
- **Crate training:** Should feel like a safe "den." Line it with a soft blanket, hide treats inside, and never use it for punishment.  
- **Potty training:** If accidents happen, calmly clean with an enzymatic cleaner (like Nature’s Miracle) and redirect to the potty spot.  
- **Recall training:** Practice outside first. Use a long leash and call him with treats/toys. If he runs off, stay calm—chase him will make it a game.
