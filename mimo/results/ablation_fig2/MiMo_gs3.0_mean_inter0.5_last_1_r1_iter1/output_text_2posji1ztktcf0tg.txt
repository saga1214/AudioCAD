Okay, vegetarian options since that’s apparently important to your sister. How about a mushroom risotto? It’s creamy, hearty, and you can add some roasted veggies on the side. Or maybe a lentil shepherd’s pie—shepherd’s pie is usually vegetarian if you skip the lamb, and lentils make it extra protein-packed. Or a big veggie stir-fry with tofu and quinoa, served over rice. You could also do a cheesy, creamy polenta with roasted vegetables and maybe some bruschetta on the side. Oh, and a spinach and artichoke lasagna—cheesy but still veg-friendly. Hope she enjoys it!
