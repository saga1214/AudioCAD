For candy tomorrow, focus on the Halloween classics that are always a hit. Things like Reese’s, Skittles, Hershey’s chocolate bars, gummy worms, and maybe some mini chocolate chips are usually safe bets. You could also grab some of the themed candy bags or boxes, like 'Monster Mash' or 'Candy Corn', even though candy corn isn't for everyone. If you can still make a quick trip to the store, grab some of the individually wrapped candies so guests can take what they want. Just remember to double-check for artificial dye allergies again if you grab brightly colored candies. Keep it simple and popular!
