2.  **Private Boat Charter (Short & Sweet):** Rent a small private boat for a few hours. You could:
    *   **Do a "Secret" Harbor Tour:** Chart a course to lesser-known coves, hidden beaches, or spots with great views you wouldn't find on a regular tour boat. Bring binoculars and a camera.
    *   **Fishing for Fun (Not Pressure):** Charter a boat focused on light tackle or catch-and-release species. The goal is the shared experience, not a big catch.
    *   **Dinner on the Water:** Many smaller charters allow you to bring your own picnic or even order in. Anchor in a secluded spot for a romantic meal.
3.  **Stable Boat Tours with a Twist:**
    *   **Whale Watching (If Seasonal):** Choose a reputable operator with a stable, comfortable vessel. The shared awe of spotting marine life is magical.
    *   **Fishing Charter (Comfort First):** Opt for a boat designed for stability and comfort. Focus on the camaraderie and the experience, not the size of the catch.
    *   **"Island Hopping" on a Larger, Stable Ferry/Boat:** If you must use a ferry-like vessel (like a scenic river cruise or a large, modern tour boat), book a cabin or a quiet spot near the front for the best views. Plan an outing where you disembark at a charming, walkable town with great seafood.
4.  **Waterfront Picnics & Exploration:**
    *   **Lobster or Crab Cakes Picnic:** Find a scenic spot along a protected bay, marina wall, or calm cove. Grab fresh seafood from a local market or a great seafood shack and enjoy it with a view of the water.
    *   **Explore the Harbor:** Rent bikes and cruise around the marina. Stop at different spots to admire the boats, maybe grab coffee or ice cream, and watch the activity on the water.
    *   **Sunset Viewing from a Pier or Promenade:** Many coastal towns have beautiful piers or waterfront promenades perfect for strolling and watching the sun set over the water. Grab drinks or a light bite first.

**On-land Romantic Seafood Ideas:**

*   **Seafood Market Tour:** Visit a renowned local seafood market together. Explore the fresh catches, learn about the different species, and pick out what you want to cook.
*   **Take-Cook-Your-Own-Seafood:** Buy a basket of fresh shellfish (oysters, clams, mussels) and head back to your rental with a bottle of wine and cookbooks to grill or steam in your kitchen.
*   **Coastal Wine Tasting:** Many areas have vineyards near the shore. Pair the local wines with a seafood platter at a winery restaurant.

**Key Considerations for Your Getaway:**

*   **Comfort First:** Since you love seafood, prioritize locations known for fresh, local catches. Research menus and markets beforehand.
*   **Stability is Key:** Ensure any boat activity is on a vessel known for being stable and comfortable for passengers. Avoid choppy conditions.
*   **Mix it Up:** Balance the water activities with relaxing on the beach, exploring charming towns, and cozy dinners. Don't over-schedule.
*   **The Magic of Sunset:** Plan at least one key moment around sunset – whether it's kayaking, a boat tour, or a harbor stroll. It's a guaranteed romantic atmosphere.
