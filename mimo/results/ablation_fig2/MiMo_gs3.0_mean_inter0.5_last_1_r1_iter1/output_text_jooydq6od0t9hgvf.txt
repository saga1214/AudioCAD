### 🍸 **Vodka Mixers (Quick & Customizable)**
1. **Classic Vodka Soda**  
   - 2 oz vodka  
   - 4 oz sparkling water or soda water  
   Omnivores/vegetarians equally enjoy this clean, versatile option.  
   *Variation:* Add 0.5 oz fresh grapefruit juice or lime juice for flavor.

2. **Ginger Vodka Fizz**  
   - 2 oz vodka  
   - 1 oz fresh ginger syrup (simmer 1 cup water + 1 cup sliced ginger + ¼ cup sugar; strain)  
   - 3 oz sparkling water  
   Stir, garnish with a lime wheel.

### 🥃 **Argentine Fernet (Fernet con Coca)**
1. **Recipe**  
   - 1 oz Fernet-Branca (or substitute with Fernet-Concertina)  
   - 2 oz **Coca Tona** (high-quality Argentine cola; *not* regular cola)  
   - 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
   - Ice  
   *Method:* Shake Fernet, cola, and lime with ice. Strain into a chilled rocks glass over a large ice cube. Garnish with an orange slice or lime wheel.

2. **Key Notes**  
   - **Soda Avoidance Solved:** Uses cola (not soda/beverage) but avoids carbonated *soda* drinks.  
   - **Authentic Touch:** Coca Tona’s herbal notes complement Fernet’s bitterness—try it with friends!
