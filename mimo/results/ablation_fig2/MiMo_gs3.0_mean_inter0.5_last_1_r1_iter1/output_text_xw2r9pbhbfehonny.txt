Then, decide if you want a steady shot or if you’re gonna hold a phone. A tripod or a little stand might help, but you don’t need anything fancy. Just make sure the camera’s facing the puppets so the audience can be seen. 

You might want to ask someone to help you film, especially if you’re still doing your thing live. You can even practice the handoffs. 

And lastly, test it out beforehand if you can. Like, try recording a short bit to make sure the audio and lighting are good.
