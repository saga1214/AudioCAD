### **Pickle-Infused Salmon with Cucumber Salad**
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 salmon fillets  
- 1/4 cup olive oil  
- 2 tbsp pickle juice (from your jar)  
- 1 tbsp lemon juice  
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup  
- 1 clove garlic, minced  
- Salt and pepper to taste  
- *For garnish:* Fresh dill or parsley  
- *For side:* Thinly sliced cucumber tossed with 1 tbsp pickle juice, salt, and pepper  

**Steps:**  
1. **Marinate the salmon:** Whisk olive oil, pickle juice, lemon juice, honey, garlic, salt, and pepper. Pour over salmon and let sit 20–30 minutes.  
2. **Cook the salmon:** Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 12–15 minutes (or pan-sear for a crusty texture).  
3. **Finish with garnish:** Sprinkle with dill/parsley and serve with the cucumber salad.  

**Why it works:**  
- The pickle juice balances the richness of salmon with acidity and umami.  
- The cucumber salad adds crunch and extra pickle flavor.  

**Bonus tip:** Save leftover pickle juice to deglaze pans when cooking veggies or even in homemade dressings! Enjoy your gourmet meal 🍣✨.
