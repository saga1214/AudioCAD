The whole right-brain, left-brain thing is largely a myth. Both hemispheres are involved in everything. For example, creativity can be both logical and intuitive, and structure can be creative. It’s not like one side is purely creative and the other is purely logical. 

Now, for your writing spelling thing, letter transposition happens a lot when people are writing fast or thinking multi-tasking. It’s about sequencing challenges, even when you know how to spell.

Tinnitus mostly comes from physiological reasons like hearing damage, age, or infections. Stress can aggravate it, but it’s not psychological in origin.

As for careers, balancing concrete measurement with creativity opens up some doors. You could look into data design or information visualization, which uses measurable facts but presents them in a visually engaging way. Another idea is architecture or 3D modeling, where you build structured, solid forms while also letting creative energies flow. You could even consider scientific illustration, which merges measuring things with painting.
