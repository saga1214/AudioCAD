### **Eldoria Treasure Quest: Character Synopsis**
**Adventure Date:** **July 20th, 1935**

---

### **Characters & Roles**
1. **Leo Vance**  
   - *Role:* Bravest adventurer & Professor of Archaeology at Kingsbridge University. Headed by **Dean Albright**.  
   - *Details:* Searched for the **lost Sunstone of El Dorado** (glowing gem).  
   - *Connection:* Ancestor of Captain Elias Vance; descendant of the first outsider to discover the Sunstone.  

2. **Silas Croft**  
   - *Role:* Main villain & greedy treasure hunter.  
   - *Details:* Ancestor of Augustus Croft, who betrayed Elias Vance centuries earlier. Followed Leo to steal the treasure. Had two henchmen:  
     - **Marcus Kane** (sneaky strategist)  
     - **Brutus Thorne** (muscular enforcer)  

3. **Elena Castillo** pretty woman & jungle expert  
   - *Role:* Helper who guided the team through Eldoria’s hidden trails.  
   - *Details:* Knew Eldoria’s secrets; aided Leo in solving puzzles.  

4. Companions:  
   - **Jack "Hammer" Stone** (muscle)  
   - **Javier Rios** (local jungle guide)  
   - **Arthur Finch** (mapmaker & puzzle solver)  

---

### **Treasure:**  
The **Sunstone of El Dorado** – a colossal, self-illuminating crystal channeled with sunlight by the Sun-Kings. Hidden in an ancient temple to protect it from greed.

---

### **Date:**  
All events unfold on **July 20th, 1935**.

---

### **Prehistory of the Sunstone**  
- **Origin:** Created in 1855 by the Sun-Kings of Eldoria, who absorbed sunlight into a crystal.  
- **Discovery:** First seen by **Captain Elias Vance** (Leo’s ancestor). Betrayed by partner **Augustus Croft** (Silas’s ancestor).  
- **Protection:** Elias hid the Sunstone in a temple and created **three math-based puzzles** to guard it:  
  1. **Pressure Plate Puzzle:**  
     - *Riddle:* "Start with 50kg (Jaguar) × 3 = 150. Subtract 20kg (Eagle) twice = 110. Divide by 5 = 22."  
     - *Solution:* Place 22kg on the plate.  
  2. **Water Fountain Puzzle:**  
     - *Riddle:* "Fill 5L jug, pour into 3L jug until full. Empty 3L jug. Pour 2L from 5L jug into it. Refill 5L jug. Top 3L jug with 1L from 5L jug."  
     - *Solution:* 4L water in the fountain.  
  3. **Star Constellation Puzzle:**  
     - *Riddle:* Sequence: 5, 8, 14, 26. *Add 3, then double the difference.*  
     - *Solution:* Next number = 50 → Press tile "50."  

Puzzles ensured only those with logic and patience (not brute force) could access the treasure.

---

### **Story Summary**  
On July 20th, 1935, Professor **Leo Vance** assembled a team with **Jack Stone**, **Javier Rios**, **Arthur Finch**, and **Elena Castillo** to find the Sunstone in Eldoria’s jungle. Villain **Silas Croft** (ancient enemy of Vance’s family) hunted them with henchmen **Marcus Kane** and **Brutus Thorne**. Using **Elena’s** jungle knowledge and solving **math puzzles** (pressure plate, water riddle, star sequence), the team outsmarted Croft and reclaimed the Sunstone, honoring Elias Vance’s legacy.
