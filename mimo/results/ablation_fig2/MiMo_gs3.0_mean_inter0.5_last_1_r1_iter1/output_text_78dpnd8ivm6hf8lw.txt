Here's a quick recap of the key parts in your blood system analogy for a child:

1.  **Heart:** The **gas station AND engine**. It pumps the blood-cars and provides the power.
2.  **Lungs:** The **fresh fuel stop**. Blood-cars stop here to pick up brand new oxygen.
3.  **Arteries:** The **big highways**. Carry blood AWAY from the heart to deliver fuel.
4.  **Capillaries:** The **tiny delivery streets**. Where blood-cars drop off their oxygen-fuel to the body's "houses" (cells) and pick up waste.
5.  **Veins:** The **return roads**. Carry blood-cars BACK to the heart after they've delivered fuel and picked up waste.
6.  **Kidneys:** The **super-car wash & recycling plant**. Clean the blood-cars, remove waste ("dirt") and extra water, and make urine.
7.  **Liver:** The **mechanic & nutrient kitchen**. Helps process the fuel (nutrients), breaks down waste, and cleans the blood-cars.

**The Journey in a Nutshell:**
The Heart-pump sends Blood-cars (full of oxygen from the Lungs-gas station) down Artery-highways. They take Capillary-streets to deliver oxygen-fuel to Muscle/Organ-houses. They pick up waste. Then, on Vein-return-roads, they go back to the Heart-gas station for kidney-cleaning and liver-processing before starting the journey all over again!
