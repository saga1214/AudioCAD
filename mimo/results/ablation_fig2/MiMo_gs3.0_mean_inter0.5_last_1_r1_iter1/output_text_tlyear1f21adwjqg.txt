Okay, efficiency can be measured in different ways—cost, scalability, availability, reliability, etc. Solar energy really excels across the board: it's cheap, scalable from a tiny rooftop to a giant farm, and getting cheaper fast. But if we're talking about pure, continuous energy output and reliability, especially in places with lots of rain or snow, hydropower might be the most efficient. It's like comparing a Swiss Army knife to a specialized tool—they're both excellent, just for different situations. So the "best" really depends on the specific context.
