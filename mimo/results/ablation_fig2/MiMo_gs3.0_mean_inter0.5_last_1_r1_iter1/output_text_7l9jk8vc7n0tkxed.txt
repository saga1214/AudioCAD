Deep blockages are tough, but with your simple toolbox, here's a calm, step-by-step approach to tackle it:

### 🛠️ Step 1: Confirm the Blockage
- **Turn off the water supply** to the affected area (under the sink if it's a drain).
- **Check for leaks** from pipes or connections.
- **Try a pipe snake** (if included in your toolbox). If you have one, feed it carefully into the drain until resistance is felt.

### 🔧 Step 2: Manual Clearing
- **Use a pipe cleaner or wire brush** to scrub the inside of the drain opening. This can dislodge debris.
- **Create a "plunger-wrench" combo**:  
  → Wrap a rubber plunger’s rubber sheet around a wrench handle.  
  → Use this to create a tight seal and apply strong downward pressure to push through the blockage.

### ⚠️ Step 3: Chemical Solutions (Use Sparingly)
- **If no other tools**, pour **hot water** (not boiling) into the drain in stages, then add **1 cup of dish soap** or **1 cup of baking soda**, followed by **1 cup of vinegar**. Let it sit for 15 mins.
- **Avoid harsh chemical drain cleaners** unless absolutely necessary—they can damage pipes.

### 🔧 Step 4: Last Resort
- **If the blockage persists**, check if you have a basic **hand auger** (like a Zip-It).  
  → Insert the auger, crank slowly, and retrieve the debris with the tines.

### 💡 Key Calming Tips:
- **Pause every 10 minutes** to take deep breaths (inhale 4 sec, exhale 6 sec).  
- **Stay hydrated**—dehydration worsens stress.  
- **Break the task down**: Focus on one step at a time.  
- **Accept limitations**: If today’s toolbox isn’t enough, call for backup tomorrow—plumbers know when to ask for help.
