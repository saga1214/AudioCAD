### **Classic Recipes**  
*(All measurements are standard for one serving)*  

####  **1. Old Fashioned**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey  
- 1 sugar cube (or ¾ oz simple syrup)  
- 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube with ½ oz water in a shaker.  
2. Add bourbon, bitters, and ice.  
3. Shake vigorously, then strain into a **rocks glass over ice**.  
4. Express an orange twist over the drink and drop it in.  
*Optional:* Add an egg white for extra froth (see note below).  

---

####  **2. Paper Plane**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz gin (e.g., Bombay Sapphire or Hendrick’s)  
- ¾ oz Aperol  
- ½ oz Amaro Nonino  
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a **coupe glass** (or martini glass).  
*Garnish:* Cherry or lemon twist.  

---

####  **3. Whiskey Sour**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- *Optional:* 1 egg white (for texture)  

**Instructions:**  
1. *For egg white:* Dry shake (without ice) all ingredients until frothy.  
2. Add ice and shake again.  
3. Strain into a **rocks glass over ice**.  
*Garnish:* Cherry or lemon slice.  
*Note:* Egg white is traditional but optional.  

---

####  egg white question:  
- **Classic or Modern?** Adding egg white is **traditional** in classic cocktail bars (since the 1910s), but some modern versions omit it. Your choice!  
- **Why add it?** Creates a silky, creamy texture and head.  

---

####  Bonus Gin Cocktail: **French 75**  
*(Recommended for vintage elegance!)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 3 oz chilled Champagne/Prosecco  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a **cocktail glass**.  
3. Top with Champagne.  
*Garnish:* Lemon twist.
