That's a great next step! Decluttering your home office can make a huge difference, especially if you work from home. Here are a few tips:

1.  **Start with Paper:** Sort through files, notes, and receipts. Shred or recycle anything outdated. Use a simple filing system – go digital where possible with cloud storage or a scanner.
2.  **Tackle the Cable Mess:** Unplug everything. Group cables by device. Use cable ties, clips, or a cable management box to bundle and hide them. Label cords if needed.
3.  **Clear the Surface:** Remove non-essential items from your desk and drawers. Keep only the tools you use daily. Use drawer dividers for small items like pens, paperclips, and USB drives.
4.  **Digital Declutter:** Organize your computer desktop, downloads folder, and email inbox. Uninstall unused apps. Delete old files and photos you don't need.

For dinner, focusing on simplicity aligns perfectly with your decluttering mindset! Here are a few quick ideas using minimal ingredients:

*   **One-Pan Roasted Chicken & Veggies:** Toss chicken thighs (or breasts), broccoli, bell peppers, and potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper, and maybe garlic powder. Roast on a sheet pan until cooked through. Serve with a simple green salad.
*   **Speedy Stir-Fry:** Sauté sliced chicken or tofu in a hot pan with minced garlic and ginger. Add a bag of pre-chopped stir-fry veggies (broccoli, carrots, snap peas). Pour in your favorite stir-fry sauce (or just soy sauce/tamari + a splash of rice vinegar). Cook until hot. Serve over instant rice or noodles.
*   **Sheet Pan Salmon & Asparagus:** Place salmon fillets on a sheet pan. Surround them with asparagus spears and lemon slices. Drizzle with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast until salmon is cooked and asparagus is tender.
*   **Breakfast-for-Dinner Scramble:** Whisk eggs with a splash of milk, salt, and pepper. Scramble in a pan. Add diced ham, cheese, and sautéed spinach or mushrooms. Serve with toast.
*   **Pasta with Simple Garlic & Oil:** Toss cooked pasta (like spaghetti or penne) with a generous amount of good olive oil, minced garlic (cooked briefly in the oil), salt, pepper, and a handful of grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano cheese. Add cherry tomatoes or spinach if you like.

The key is choosing recipes with few ingredients and minimal prep. Enjoy the process of creating a more serene kitchen and workspace!
