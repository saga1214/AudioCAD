If you're staying near **Secret Harbour Beach Resort** on St. Thomas, here are fantastic daytime activities right around Cruz Bay and the East End:

1. **Snorkel at Coral World Ocean Park**:  
   A short drive away, this park offers guided snorkeling tours over vibrant reefs teeming with tropical fish and sea turtles. Calm, shallow waters make it ideal for your brother.

2. **Explore Galligan’s Beach Park**:  
   A 10-minute walk from Secret Harbour, this public beach has turquoise water perfect for swimming and kayaking. Grab a picnic from Cruz Bay’s shops and relax here.

3. **Beachcombing & Cruz Bay Shops**:  
   Wander the vibrant main street (now called "Sugar Beach") for local crafts, beachwear, and fresh fruit. Collect seashells along Secret Harbour’s shore—especially at low tide.

4. **Easy Coastal Hike to Cruz Bay Lookout**:  
   A 30-minute loop trail leads to a scenic viewpoint overlooking Cruz Bay. Gentle, paved sections make it accessible, and you’ll spot colorful birds along the way.

5. **Kayak or Paddleboard**:  
   Rent gear from **Cruz Bay Kayak** to paddle along the mangroves or explore nearby bays like **Cinnamon Bay** (just a 15-minute drive away).

**Pro Tip**: Visit **Soggy Dollar Bar** in Cruz Bay for their famous Painkiller cocktail at sunset—it’s a must! Their outdoor patio faces the water with live music often playing.
