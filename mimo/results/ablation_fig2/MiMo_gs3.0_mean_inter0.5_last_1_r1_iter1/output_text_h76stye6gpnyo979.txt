That pigeon problem is a great analogy for organizing goals!

Here’s how you could structure your plan:

Step one: Assign each passion and financial priority to a fund. Bolivia salt flats would go into a “Dream Vacation” fund, Feröður into a “Wilderness Adventures” fund, apartment down payment stays in its own “Home Security” fund, cooking trend supplies get a “Food Experiment” fund, bookshelf reorganization covers small treats or stationery, and the tough meetings at work could be linked to productivity apps or tools to save time.

Step two: Assign a small, regular amount to each fund based on what feels manageable. Even five dollars a month makes a difference.

Step three: Track everything in a simple way, maybe a spreadsheet or an app. Seeing the progress can be really motivating.

By treating each goal like one of the pigeons on a rooftop—giving each one its own space and resources—you can build everything up, one small step at a time.
