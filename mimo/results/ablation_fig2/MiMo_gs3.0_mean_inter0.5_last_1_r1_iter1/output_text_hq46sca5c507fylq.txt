That's a great question! For someone with hands-on hobbies, meaningful gifts often center on **tools, materials, inspiration, or experiences** that support their craft. Here are some ideas tailored to your interests:

### For Tailoring (or similar crafts):
1. **Specialized Tools**:  
   - High-quality shears for fabric (like Gingher or Kai, which you asked about).  
   - A vintage-style pocket tool or leatherman for precise work.  
   - A lapel pin press or fabric marker for alterations.  

2. **Vintage-Inspired Supplies**:  
   - A Parker 51-themed gift set (ink, case, or restoration kit).  
   - A leather-bound notebook for sketching designs.  

3. **Skill-Building**:  
   - A local tailoring workshop or online course (e.g., pattern drafting).  
   - A subscription to a craft magazine like *Threads* or *Made to Measure*.  

4. **Fabric & Materials**:  
   - A curated fabric swatch kit for beginners.  
   - High-quality cotton or linen for practice projects.  

### For Woodworking/Clay:
1. **Woodworking**:  
   - A jigsaw or rotary tool (like Dremel) for intricate cuts.  
   - Woodturning tools or pre-cut wood blanks.  
   - A "beginner’s woodworking kit" with essential hardware.  

2. **Clay/Pottery**:  
   - A small pottery wheel (electric or manual).  
   - Clay storage containers to keep it dry.  
   - Molds or glaze mixes for experimentation.  

### Universal Gift Ideas:
- **Pen Restoration Kit**: If you stick with tailoring, a premium pen repair kit (e.g., from Parker 51 experts) would be thoughtful.  
- **Experience Gift Card**: For a local craft store, pottery studio, or woodworking class.  
- **Custom Crafted Item**: A handmade tool (like a leather cardholder) from another hobbyist.  

### Key Tip:  
> **"Gift the process, not just the product."**  
> Items that let them *create* (tools, materials) or *learn* (classes) are often more cherished than finished goods.  

Since you enjoy vintage items, leaning into that theme (like pen accessories for tailoring) could make gifts feel especially personal! 🎁
