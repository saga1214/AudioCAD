Well, for Thanksgiving, a great and relatively easy option is a pumpkin pie. You can find many no-bake or simple-bake recipes.

But if you want something you can take to family that's a bit more... creative, and maybe a little less 'traditional', you could try a savory and sweet dessert. For example, a savory and sweet... pumpkin tart. It's a classic Thanksgiving idea. You'd make a simple crust, and for the filling, you'd mix pumpkin puree with a little bit of maple syrup and spices. You bake the crust and the filling, and then you can serve it with a dollop of whipped cream. It's a great way to show appreciation with a really impressive, yet manageable, dish.
