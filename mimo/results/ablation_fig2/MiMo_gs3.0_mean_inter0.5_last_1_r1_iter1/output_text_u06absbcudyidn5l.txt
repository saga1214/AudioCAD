Okay, let's break this down. Chronic redness has many possible causes. Besides rosacea, sun damage, genetics, fibrous dysplasia, or vasculitis, other common culprits include things like eczema, psoriasis, allergic reactions, contact dermatitis, or even heat rash. It really depends on the specific patterns and triggers.

To clarify vasculitis, think of it this way: it's an autoimmune condition where the immune system mistakenly attacks the walls of your blood vessels. Because blood vessels are everywhere, this inflammation can affect any organ. The size of the vessel that's inflamed determines the symptoms. For example, inflammation in smaller vessels often causes skin rashes, while larger vessels can lead to issues with the kidneys or nerves. It can be triggered by infections, autoimmune disorders, or even just randomly. The main goal of treatment is to suppress the immune response, but the inflammation can cause permanent damage if left untreated.
