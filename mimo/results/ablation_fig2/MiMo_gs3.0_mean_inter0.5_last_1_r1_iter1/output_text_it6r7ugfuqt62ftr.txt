Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimming, warmth, diet goals, celiac considerations, Alcatraz birthday, and now a larger family vacation. Let's craft the perfect warm getaway for your family of three.

I'd highly recommend Palm Springs. It's about a four-hour drive from Northern California, but it stays incredibly warm this time of year, consistently in the 70s to 80s Fahrenheit. It's famous for pools and hot tubs, which is perfect for meeting your swimming goal. Plus, packing light is easy in warm weather!

Here’s a detailed four-day itinerary for Palm Springs.

First, let's talk about getting there and accommodations. For travel, renting a large SUV or minivan is definitely your best bet for space and comfort. You can find great deals on rental cars through services like Enterprise or Hertz. The drive is scenic, taking about four hours, so you can make it an overnight trip or just do it on Day One.

For a place to stay, I'd suggest The Hotel Palmore. It's a luxury resort with a really cool retro-modern vibe. It has multiple pools, including an adults-only one and a kids' area, and they're very accommodating to gluten-free needs. The location is also great, right near the downtown core. You can book it through a site like Booking.com or directly on their website.

Alright, for your packing bag, focus on light and swim-friendly items:

For bathing suits, pack two one-piece suits and one two-piece. A versatile sarong or skirt is great for covering up quickly.

For daytime, think tank tops, lightweight button-down shirts, shorts, sundresses, and cover-ups you can easily throw on and off.

Footwear is key: flip-flops, comfortable sandals, and maybe a pair of casual sneakers for exploring.

Don't forget essentials like a wide-brimmed hat, sunglasses, high-SPF gluten-free sunscreen, a reusable water bottle, and a small towel.

For Day One, you'll arrive in Palm Springs. After checking into your hotel, you can relax at the resort's pool. In the evening, you could have dinner at the resort's restaurant or explore downtown. A great gluten-free-friendly spot downtown is The Baked Bear, famous for their gluten-free cupcakes and desserts.

On Day Two, you could take a trip to Palm Springs Aerial Tramway for some amazing views. It gets warm up there, so remember your swimsuit and a light cover-up. In the afternoon, head to the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway's Spa Tub for a relaxing soak in their huge outdoor hot tubs.

For Day Three, check out the Coachella Valley Cultural District for some art and history, then head to the Palm Springs Modernism Week Pool Party. It's a huge pool party with lots of music and food trucks, and many vendors offer gluten-free options.

On your final day, you can enjoy one last swim at your hotel's pool before heading home.

For dinner on your last night, I'd suggest a special treat at Nobu Malibu. It's a bit of a drive, but the sushi and ambiance are incredible. You'll definitely need to make reservations well in advance.

This itinerary should give you plenty of warm weather, plenty of swimming, and some special dining to celebrate your daughter's birthday and your diet goals. Enjoy your vacation!
