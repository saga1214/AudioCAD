If you're looking for a simple recipe for a vanilla cake with white frosting, here’s a quick one. You’ll need butter, sugar, eggs, flour, baking powder, milk, and vanilla extract. Just cream the butter and sugar, add in the eggs, then mix in the dry ingredients along with the milk and vanilla. Pour the batter into a cake pan and bake for about 30 minutes at 350 degrees. For the frosting, you can whip some butter, powdered sugar, a little vanilla, and a splash of milk until it’s nice and smooth. Just make sure the cake is completely cool before you frost it, and you’re good to go!
