Let me clear that up. The truest and most original recipe for a Boulevardier is actually equal parts of rye/bourbon, sweet vermouth, and Campari. The ratio of one and a quarter to one to one that I first mentioned is a later variation. So your bartender friend's equal-parts Boulevardier is actually the classic way. 

Now, if you want to make a Boulevardier with equal parts, or a Last Word instead, here's the recipe. For the Boulevardier, each spirit and vermouth will be one ounce: one ounce of rye whiskey, one ounce of sweet vermouth, and one ounce of Campari. Stir well and garnish. For the Last Word, each ingredient is three-quarters of an ounce: three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. Both are great choices!
