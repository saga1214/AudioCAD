That's a fantastic advantage. If you can both write and illustrate, you can absolutely submit a complete package to a publisher or an agent.

However, there are two different paths you can take. First, you can submit your completed book directly to an independent publisher. You'll need to research publishers that accept unsolicited manuscripts, as they often have specific guidelines. The second, and more common path, is to find an agent first. An agent will help you pitch your complete manuscript to the major, traditional publishing houses. They will handle the negotiation and the entire process for you. The key thing to remember is that the quality of your work is what will sell it. Your illustrations and writing will be your ticket in.
