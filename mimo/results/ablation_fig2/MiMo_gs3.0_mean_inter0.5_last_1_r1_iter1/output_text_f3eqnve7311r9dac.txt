That's an excellent strategy. Older comics are inherently more rare, which is a major driver of their value.

Here are ten notable comics from the 1940s and 50s that you could look for. You can find high-quality copies in the professional grading range for under five hundred dollars.

First, as we discussed, *Action Comics* number one.

Next is *Flash Comics* number one, Superman's second appearance.

Also from 1940, there's *Adventures of Superman* number one.

Then there's *Detective Comics* number one, Batman's first appearance.

And *Warrior Comics* number one, which is the first appearance of Green Lantern and Green Arrow.

A more modern book that's a great investment is *Amazing Spider-Man* number fifty, the first appearance of Doctor Octopus.

You can also find affordable copies of *Tales to Astonish* number one, which is the first full appearance of the Hulk.

*Marvel Super-Heroes* number one is the first appearance of the Fantastic Four.

*Thor Comics* number one from 1946 is also a great find.

And finally, *Aquaman* number one, the first appearance of Aquaman and Aqualad.
