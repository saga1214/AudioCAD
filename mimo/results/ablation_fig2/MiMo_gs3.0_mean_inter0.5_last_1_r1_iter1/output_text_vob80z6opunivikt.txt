### 🍿 Savory & Crunchy Picks:
- **Pretzel Rods or Straws**: Perfect for dipping in cheese powder or mustard.
- **Potato Chips**: Classic, salty, and shareable (choose flavors like BBQ, sour cream & onion, or kettle-cooked).
- **Rice Krispie Treats?** Wait—these are sweet! Skip these since you dislike sugary snacks.  
- **Puffed Cereal Snacks**: Savory puffs (like cheese-flavored or plain) are light and crunchy.

### 🍫 Sweet but Non-Chocolatey Picks:
- **Cookies**: Store-bought sugar cookies, oatmeal cookies, or ginger snaps (no chocolate chips!).
- **Caramels**: Toffee or butterscotch caramels are rich but not chocolatey.
- **Gummies**: If you tolerate fruit-flavored gummies (e.g., strawberry or watermelon), they’re chewy but not cocoa-based.

### 🎭 Creative Snack Arrangements:
- **Candy Bar Stacking**: Use chocolate bars as "bricks" to build mini towers on the table.
- **Snack Bar Display**: Arrange chips, pretzels, and cookies in small bowls or mason jars.
- **Mug of Warm Snacks**: Place a mug of hot chocolate (or just milk!) next to cookies for dipping.

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Balance textures**: Mix crispy (chips), chewy (caramels), and soft (cookies) for variety.
- **Keep it portable**: Vending machine snacks are often individually wrapped—ideal for grabbing and going.
- **Feng shui your table**: Use a tray or placemat to corral snacks and avoid clutter!

Example setup:  
> A bowl of pretzel rods + a bag of potato chips + a plate of sugar cookies + a mug of hot chocolate. Topped off with a giant candy bar as a centerpiece!
