Okay, since you've light-sanded and cleaned, removed the hardware, and now have bare painted cabinets, there are a few more key steps.

Make sure to give everything one final, really good cleaning wipe-down with a tack cloth to pick up any dust from the sanding.

Then, before you prime, you absolutely must do a patch test. Paint a small, hidden spot on the cabinet frame in your off-white color. Let it dry completely, then open and close the cabinet door. You need to make sure it doesn't rub off any paint onto the door frame. If it does, you might need to lightly sand that spot again before priming.

And one last thing, remember to protect your floors and counters with plastic or drop cloths before you start painting.
