For miso soup or cucumber salad: for the cucumber salad, you'll need sliced cucumbers, sesame oil, rice vinegar, soy sauce without legumes like Tamari, toasted sesame seeds, and a little sugar. For the starter alternative, the miso soup, you'll need miso paste, shirataki noodles for legume-free thickness, maybe some thinly sliced scallions, and a splash of dashi if you want to make it. For chicken teriyaki main: you'll need about a pound of chicken breast cut into strips, soy sauce again, mirin, sake or dry sherry, and a little sugar or honey for the glaze. Stir-fried veggies could be things like bok choy, bell peppers, carrots, and snap peas, along with some cooking oil, soy sauce, and sesame oil for finishing. Now, for mango sticky rice dessert: you'll need glutinous rice, fresh mango, coconut milk, sugar, salt, matcha powder if you decide to make the ice cream instead, and heavy cream and condensed milk for that.
