If you're staying near **Secret Harbour Beach Resort** on St. John, you're in a fantastic spot for pressure-free, boat-friendly fun! Here are some pressure-change-free daytime activities around Cruz Bay and Cinnamon Bay:

### 🌊 Beach & Snorkeling
1. **Secret Harbour Itself**: The calm bay right from your resort is perfect for swimming and snorkeling—right off shore, you’ll see parrotfish, sea turtles, and soft corals. No boat needed!
2. **Cinnamon Bay (5-minute walk)**: A 5-minute stroll south leads to this iconic beach. Try **Aniguan Beach** (calm, shallow) or **Lucky Strike** (rocky for snorkeling). Rent gear from **Cinnamon Bay Dive Co.** ($20/hour).

### 🚤 Boat Trips (Your Strength!)
- **Sailors Cove**: A short boat ride from Cinnamon reveals secluded coves, bioluminescent bays (night trips only), and wild donkeys (yes, donkeys!).
- **Trunk Bay**: Take a guided snorkel tour to explore its underwater museum or historic shipwrecks.

### 🌿 Hiking & Nature
- **Blue Belles Trail**: A flat, paved path through mangroves ending at a secluded beach. Easy and scenic.
- **Virgin Islands National Park**: Hike to **Salt River Bay** or **Sage Mountain** (gentle trails, no steep climbs). Rent bikes in Cruz Bay.

### 🛒 Local Shops & Culture
- **Cruz Bay Village**: Walk to the market for coconuts, local crafts, and ice cream. Try **Mangoes & More** for fresh fruit.
- **The Longboard (next door!)**: Stop by for happy hour (4–6 PM) even during the day—great people-watching.

### 🦞 Food & Drink
- **Reef渔业** (Cruz Bay): Famous for lobster tacos and live music (weekends).
- **Cinnamon Bay Grill**: Oceanfront dining with fresh seafood.
- **Coco Loko**: Local rum tasting and live music.

### ⛰️ Pressure-Free Views
- **Mountain Tram (Red Hook, St. Thomas)**: If you switch islands, this cable car offers pressure-free elevation changes and stunning views. No pressure changes!

### 🐦 Birding Hotspots
- **Cinnamon Bay**: Look for bananaquits, roseate spoonbills, and herons near the mangroves.
- **Secret Harbour tide pools**: Check for flamingos during low tide.

### 🚫 Pressure Avoidance
- Stick to shallow bays (<3 ft deep), avoid rapid ascents/dives, and keep boat speed gentle.
- If your brother feels discomfort, opt for calm anchorages like **Sandy Spit** (St. John) or **Magens Bay** (St. Thomas).

Enjoy week of turquoise waters, friendly locals, and pressure-free adventures! 🌴⛵
