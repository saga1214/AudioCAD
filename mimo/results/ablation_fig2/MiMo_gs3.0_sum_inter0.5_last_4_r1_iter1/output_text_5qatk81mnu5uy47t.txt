Okay, CostaRica dream scuba trip planning! Let's leverage what you already know from Honduras and Belize to make the perfect plan. Tropical waters are your strength.

First, advantages/dis fins recap quickly: Long fins win for thrust in currents common in Costa Rica (like around Costa Rica's Pacific side) and long-distance cruisers. Short fins ease travel and calm-site ease. For Costa Rica, long fins are generally the better investment, especially if currents are a factor. Start with short ones if technique/build is a concern, but long fins are the standard recommendation for power and efficiency.

Now, planning an unforgettable trip! Here’s a framework incorporating your scuba passion and past experiences:

1.  **Destination Within Costa Rica:**
    *   **Pacific Coast (Tortuguero, Cahuita, Manuel Antonio):** Beautiful, often calmer waters, great for beginners, abundant marine life (turtles!), mangrove forests. Closer to San José for travel. *Your Belize experience here would feel familiar.*
    *   **Golfo Dulce (Cahuita area):** Large, sheltered bay with good visibility and diverse sites. Includes potential for night dives.
    *   **Caribbean Coast (Puerto Viejo, Dominical, Salsa Brava):** Stronger currents, often higher biodiversity (especially reef fish), potential for big pelagics (dolphins, whales - season dependent). Shipwrecks are common here. *Matches your Hondura/Caribbean experience.*
    *   **Cocos Island (Remote):** Iconic! Famous for marine iguanas, sharks, manta rays, and incredible open-water/rookery dives. Requires a liveaboard boat. *A true bucket-list adventure.*

2.  **Dive Operators & Sites:**
    *   **Research Reputable Shops:** Look for operators specializing in your certification level (PADI/SSI), with good reviews on platforms like Divers Alert Network (DAN), ScubaBoard, or Google. Prioritize those with strong safety records.
    *   **Must-Dive Sites:**
        *   **Caribbean Coast:** **Coco Catamaran** (shipwreck), **Punta Isla** (reef, turtles), **Sargasso Sea** (pelagics, drift dives).
        *   **Pacific Coast:** **Cahuita National Park** (reef, turtles, good intro sites), **Playa Naranjo** (rookery - if season), **Rio Celeste** (unique water color, easier drift).
        *   **Golfo Dulce:** **Playa Blanca** (good variety), **Playa Naranjo** (rookery potential).
        *   **Cocos Island (Liveaboard):** **Rookery** (seal/nurseries), **Shipwrecks**, **Open Water** (sharks, mantas, turtles).

3.  **Beyond Scuba for Unforgettable Factor:**
    *   **Wildlife:** Costa Rica is a biodiversity hotspot. Look for turtle nesting/touring programs (many dive operators offer this). Birdwatching tours (humblebird!). Night walks in national parks (sloths, monkeys). Whale watching (Caribbean coast, Dec-Apr).
    *   **Land Adventure:** Hiking national parks (Manuel Antonio, Corcovado), zip-lining, white-water rafting (Pacific), relaxing on pristine beaches (Playa Conchal, Santa Teresa).
    *   **Pura Vida Experience:** Immerse yourselves! Try local food (gallo pinto, fresh seafood), learn a few basic Spanish phrases, visit a local market.

4.  **Practicalities:**
    *   **Certification:** Ensure you're current (cert卡有效, tanks good). Consider an advanced certification if you want to try deep dives or wreck penetration.
    *   **Gear:** As discussed, long fins are recommended. Bring your own gear or rent from a good shop. Remember reef-safe sunscreen!
    *   **Booking:** Book flights and accommodation early, especially for peak season (Dec-Apr). Book dive boats/liveaboards well in advance. Consider a mix of land and sea packages.
    *   **Budget:** Costa Rica can be mid-to-high range. Factor in flights, accommodation, food, dives, gear, travel insurance (essential!), and activities.

**Quick Recommendation:** For scuba-focused power and variety, **the Caribbean Coast (Puerto Viejo/Dominical/Salsa Brava area) is hard to beat.** It offers strong currents, excellent biodiversity (reef, big fish, potential for whales/dolphins), common shipwrecks, and a fantastic local vibe. You'll get that "once-in-a-lifetime" feel similar to your Belize experience, but with potentially more challenging and rewarding dives.

**Next Steps:** Pick a region that appeals most (Pacific calm vs. Caribbean power), research specific dive operators specializing there, book your flights, and start planning the non-scuba adventures that will make it truly unforgettable! Pura Vida!
