Yes, you can absolutely submit a fully illustrated manuscript, but you have to be careful about how you do it.

The first and most important thing is to check the agent's or publisher's guidelines. Many traditional children's book publishers and agents will only accept manuscripts with text and the *promise* of illustrations. They want to see if the story works on its own. 

However, if you have illustrations ready, you can definitely try to submit them as a PDF. The key is to do this only after you've already secured an agent who represents both text and art, or when you're submitting directly to a publisher that specifically accepts illustrated manuscripts. Just be aware that some might see your illustrations as a sign that you've already done the work, which could make them hesitant to commission an artist. 

So, the best path is usually to get your text accepted first, and then submit the illustrations.
