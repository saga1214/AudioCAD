### **Key Logistics & Tips**
1. **Hotel Checkouts:**  
   - Typically 10:00–11:00 AM. Confirm early with Osaka/Tokyo hotels.  
   - **Tip:** Pack day-bags with passports, chargers, and workout gear to grab essentials quickly.  

2. **Taxi Costs in Osaka:**  
   - Short trips: ¥3,000–5,000  
   - Long trips (e.g., Nara return): ¥15,000–25,000  
   - **Save money:** Use IC cards (Suica/Pasmo) for trains/buses.  

3. **Presentation Design Tips:**  
   - Use traditional elements: Zen minimalism (Nara/Kyoto), ukiyo-e borders, kimono gradients.  
   - Tools: Canva (Japanese art templates), PowerPoint’s "Japanese" theme.  

4. **Workout Integration:**  
   - **Morning:** Hotel gyms (many free for guests) or neighborhood parks.  
   - **Evening:** Evening runs after dinner (Tokyo/Osaka have safe paths).  

5. **Communication:**  
   - For hotels: Use translation apps (Google Translate) or write notes.  
   - Station staff often speak basic English; hotel concierges can help with taxis.
