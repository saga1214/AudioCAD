### Addressing Your Concerns:  
#### 1. **PFAS vs. PFCs**  
   - **PFAS** (Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances) are the "forever chemicals" you read about.  
   - **PFCs** (Perfluorocarbons) are a *subset* of PFAS. PFAS is the broader category.  
   - *Key takeaway*: Avoid all PFAS-containing products.  

#### 2. **Fish Contamination**  
   - **Yes, fish accumulate PFAS** from water pollution.  
   - **Action**: Limit high-risk fish (e.g., swordfish, king mackerel, shark). Opt for low-mercury, shorter-lived fish like salmon or sardines.  

#### 3. **Bottle Water vs. Tap Water**  
   - **Bottled water can contain microplastics** from the plastic bottle itself.  
   - **Tap water may contain PFAS** from pipes or industrial runoff.  
   - **Solution**: Use a **certified pitcher filter** (e.g., activated carbon) or **under-sink systems** to remove *both* microplastics and PFAS.  

#### 4. **Other Sources You Mentioned**  
   - **Toilet Paper**: Some brands use PFAS in grease-resistant coatings. Look for **"PFAS-free" labels**.  
   - **Paint**: Most household paints **do not contain PFAS**. Smell = VOCs (volatile organic compounds), not PFAS.  
   - **Electronics**: Trace amounts may be in waterproof coatings, but **direct exposure is minimal**.  
   - **Dust**: Microplastics settle in dust. **Solution**: Use a **HEPA air purifier** and vacuum weekly.  

#### 5. **Shampoo/Cosmetics**  
   - Some shampoos/cosmetics use PFAS for slip/sun protection.  
   - **Action**: Choose **"free of PFAS"** or **"free of PFCs"** labels. Brands like Credo Beauty vet products.  

---

### Critical Tips for Hypochondria Management:  
- **Water Safety**: Use a **NSF/ANSI 53 or 62 certified filter** (e.g., Brita, AquaTrue) for PFAS/microplastics.  
- **Cookware**: Switch to **stainless steel, cast iron, or glass**.  
- **Clothing**: Avoid waterproof/stain-resistant finishes; opt for natural fibers.  
- **Avoid "Forever" Products**: Choose biodegradable alternatives where possible.  
- **Minimize Dust**: Vacuum weekly with a HEPA filter.  

> 💡 **Important**: Your body naturally eliminates microplastics/PFAS, but prevention reduces burden. Focus on **certified filters, non-stick alternatives, and PFAS-free labels**. Avoidance is more effective than "cleanses."
