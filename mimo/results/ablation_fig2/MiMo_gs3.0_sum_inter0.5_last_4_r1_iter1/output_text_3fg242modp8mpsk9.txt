Okay, since you love the coast and seafood, here are a few water-based ideas.

You could look for a catamaran tour that stops at secluded spots with tide pools. It's educational and you might find some cool local wildlife.

Another option is a multi-course 'culinary cruise'. They'd likely serve fresh local seafood and even have a chef demonstrating a recipe on board.

For something more adventurous, a kayaking or paddleboarding tour along a scenic coastline could be wonderful, especially if it ends with a picnic lunch.

And finally, many towns have evening boat parties, sometimes called 'sardine runs', where you can enjoy cocktails and music while cruising under the stars.
