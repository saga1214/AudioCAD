Okay, you're right, it can be a bit confusing. Let me clarify. The truly classic ratio is one and a quarter ounces whiskey to one ounce vermouth and Campari. The equal-parts ratio, using one ounce of all three, is also classic and very common. So the one your friend uses is perfectly valid.

As for your last drink, the Last Word you mentioned, the ingredients are equal parts of three-quarters of an ounce each: London Dry Gin, Green Chartreuse, Maraschino Liqueur, and Fresh Lime Juice. Stir that over ice too and garnish with a lime wheel. Enjoy your party!
