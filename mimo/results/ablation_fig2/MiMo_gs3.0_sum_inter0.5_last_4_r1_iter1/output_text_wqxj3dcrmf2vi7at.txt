Here's a quick recap of ideas for your little corgi princess:

### 🍪 Homemade Treats:
1. **Frozen Bites**: Mashed pumpkin/sweet potato baked into small cookies and frozen.  
2. **Peanut Butter Cookies**: Oats mixed with xylitol-free peanut butter.  
3. **Healthy Chews**: Cooked chicken or carrot stick pieces.  

### 🛒 Store-Bought Brands:
- **Blue Buffalo**  
- **Zuke’s Mini training bites**  
- **Ziwi Peak**  
- **Greenies** (for dental health)  

### 🐶 Training Tips:
- **Sit**: Use a treat to lure her nose up, then move it over her head until she sits. Say "sit!" and reward.  
- **Potty Training**: Take her outside on a schedule (after meals, naps), reward immediately after she goes.  
- **Barking at Felix**: Teach "quiet" with a treat. When she stops, reward. Redirect with a toy.  

### 🎀 Collar Colors:
- **Teal/Turquoise** (contrasts with brown)  
- **Soft Pink** (playful and feminine)  
- **Royal Blue** (vibrant and chic)  

Keep portions tiny, and always prioritize treats with wholesome ingredients! Your princess is lucky to have such a thoughtful human. 💖
