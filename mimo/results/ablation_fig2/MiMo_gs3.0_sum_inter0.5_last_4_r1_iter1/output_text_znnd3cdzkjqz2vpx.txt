Okay, you've got the essentials, but there are a few more things that will make the job go much smoother and easier. I'd really recommend adding a measuring tape and a level to your toolkit. While you have a level line, it's super helpful to have a level to check that the uppers and uppers against each other are perfectly flush as Sarah clamps them together.

Also, you'll definitely want a drill and some wall anchors. Habitat cabinets usually have pre-drilled holes, so a drill is essential for screwing them into those studs you found earlier.

Finally, don't forget safety glasses and work gloves. They'll protect your eyes from any dust and your hands from splinters or scratches. With those, you'll be all set and ready to get those savings fully realized!
