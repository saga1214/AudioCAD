Absolutely! Here's a comprehensive shopping list based on our menu plan, designed for 28 people (20 adults + 4 kids). I've grouped items by category and included quantities based on standard crowd servings:

### 🍽️ Appetizers/Finger Foods
- Mini slider buns (12-16 slider molds + extra)  
- Ground pork or Italian sausage for Pigs in a Blanket (1 lb)  
- 2 lbs shrimp for Shrimp Cocktail  
- Cream cheese, chives, and rye bread for Sliders  
- Mini cooking spray and toothpicks  

### 🥗 Sides (Cleaner Kid-Friendly Option)
- 1 large ham (12-14 lbs, bone-in for best flavor)  
- 2 lbs baby potatoes for Scalloped Potatoes  
- 1 bunch asparagus (thicker stalks preferred)  
- 3-4 lbs carrots (for Roasted Carrots)  
- Olive oil, salt, pepper, and lemon for Asparagus  
- Carrot glaze alternative: 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup (optional)  

### 🍷 Drinks
- 1 bottle (750ml) champagne per 6-8 guests (aim for 4 bottles)  
- Sparkling grape juice (1 bottle per kid + 2-3 bottles for adults)  
- Ice (20-30 lbs)  
- Sparkling water (6-8 cans)  
- Lemonade (2-3 bottles)  
- Soda mixers (optional: 4-6 cans)  

### 🧊 Pantry Staples
- Butter (for scalloped potatoes)  
- Flour, milk, mustard, Dijon for scalloped potatoes  
- Mini hot dogs, buns, cheese for sliders/Pigs in a Blanket  
- Garlic powder, paprika for pigs in a blankt  
- Sparkling cider mix (for grape juice drinks)  
- Noisemaker nozzles and kid-safe flutes (for grape juice)  

### 🛒 Decorations
- Balloons (medium/large)  
- Festive hats/noisemakers (4 sets for kids)  
- Tablecloth or runners (optional)  

### 📝 Pro Tips
1. **Quantities**: These are starting points. Taste-test appetizers/sides before NYE.  
2. **Prep Ahead**:  
   - Make scalloped potatoes, sliders, and pigs in a blanket 1-2 days ahead.  
   - Roast carrots/asparagus on day-of.  
3. **Kid Involvement**: Let kids assemble mini sliders or decorate noisemakers!  
4. **Pantry Check**: Ensure you have salt, pepper, oil, baking powder, etc.
