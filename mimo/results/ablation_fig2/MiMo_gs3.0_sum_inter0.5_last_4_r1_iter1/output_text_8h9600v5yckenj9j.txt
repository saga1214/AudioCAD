For candy tomorrow, grab some of the classics that kids and adults love: Reese's Cups, Snickers, Milky Way bars, Twix, and maybe some classic Halloween candies like candy corn, pumpkins, or themed stuff like a 'zombie brain' bag of Skittles. You can also get some gummy worms, eyes, or other fun Halloween-themed candies. Just try to buy the larger bags or bulk candy to avoid leftovers, and check the expiration dates. Alternatively, you could just buy a few bags of popular candy from a store that has pre-packaged Halloween candy displays.
