### **Temperature Guide**

| **Champagne**          | 7–10°C / 45–50°F | 7–10°C / 45–50°F | Effervescence preserved; served very chilled.                                 |

### **Best for Hot Weather**  
1. **Pinot Grigio** (4–7°C / 40–45°F): Ultra-chilled for maximum refreshment.  
2. **Champagne** (7–10°C / 45–50°F): Effervescence cuts through heat; bubbles cleanse the palate.  
3 **Hon-Mirin** (if sipped): 20–24°C / 68–75°F—light and floral, though best paired with food.  

### **Best for Cold Weather**  
1. **Pinot Noir** (13–16°C / 55–18°F): Warm, earthy notes comfort in chilly air.  
2. **Chianti** (16–18°C / 60–18°F): Bold reds stand up to frosty evenings.  
3 **Mulled Wine** (60–70°C / 140–160°F): Spiced warmth is ideal for winter gatherings.  

---

### **Key Reminders**  
- **Hon-Mirin**: Never store above 68°C/160°F—it’s a cooking syrup, not a beverage. If tasting, room temp suffices.  
- **Pinot Noir vs. Hot Weather**: While Pinot Noir is versatile, **Pinot Grigio** is better suited for scorching days.  
- **Pro Tip**: For outdoor parties (like your upcoming picnic!), use an insulated cooler with ice for Pinot Grigio/Champagne, and keep reds in a shaded cooler to prevent overheating.
