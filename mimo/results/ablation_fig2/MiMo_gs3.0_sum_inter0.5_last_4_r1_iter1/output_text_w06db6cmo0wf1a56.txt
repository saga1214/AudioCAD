What a fantastic plan! Here’s a **mess-minimizing shopping list** for your bachelor party, designed for grab-and-go convenience for 10 dads + 10 kids:

---

### **DRINKS** (Focus: Hydration + Low-Sugar Variety)
- **Water**: 100+ reusable water bottles (10 per person + extras)  
- **Infused Water**: 2 large jugs (lemon/cucumber/mint)  
- **Sugar-Free Sodas**: 24 cans (pick your favorites: root beer, cream soda, etc.)  
- **Low-Sugar Juice Boxes**: 20 boxes (apricot, apple, or 100% fruit blends)  
- **Sparkling Water**: 12 cans (still or sparkling)  
- **Thermoses**: 10 (for hot coffee/tea on the go)  
- **Ice Packs**: 5–6 (keep everything cold)  

---

### **HEALTHY SNACKS** (Grab-and-Go, Minimal Prep)  
- **Cheese Sticks**: 20 (10 per kid + adults)  
- **Grapes**: 2 lbs (washed, pre-portioned into 10 bags)  
- **Single-Serve Packs**:  
  - Pretzels/trail mix (10 bags)  
  - Baby carrots (10 bags) + single-serve hummus cups (10)  
- **Hard-Boiled Eggs**: 10 (peeled, salt/pepper)  
- **Fresh Fruit**:  
  - Apple slices (10 bags, tossed in lemon juice)  
  - Banana halves (pre-peeled)  
- **Protein Bars**: 10 (low-sugar, e.g., RXBAR, KIND)  

---

### **MAIN MEAL** (Calzone-Friendly & Portable)  
Order **10 personal-pizza calzones** from a local pizzeria. Examples:  
- 5 cheese  
- 3 pepperoni  
- 2 veggie  
**Tip**: Ask for them cut into wedges if you prefer slices over handheld calzones.  

---

### **MAIN MEAL ALTERNATIVE** (If Calzones Aren’t Ideal)  
**Wrap/Sandwich Bar**:  
- 10 pre-made wraps/sandwiches (individual portions):  
  - Turkey & hummus wrap  
  - Chicken Caesar wrap  
  - Veggie wrap with tahini  
- Single-serve tortillas + fillings:  
  - Turkey slices  
  - Cheese  
  cooler packs of guacamole, salsa, or Greek yogurt for DIY assembly.  

---

### **DESSERT** (Minimal Cleanup)  
- **Cupcakes**: 10–12 (plain or lightly decorated)  
- **Individual Cookies**: 10 (e.g., oatmeal raisin, chocolate chip)  
- **Energy Balls** (optional): 10 mini packs (date/nut/oat balls)  

---

### **COOLER ORGANIZATION TIPS**  
1. **Layer Smartly**:  
   - Bottom: Ice packs + drinks  
   - Middle: Snacks + sandwiches  
   - Top: Calzones/desserts  
2. **Label Coolers**: Assign each dad a cooler to reduce sharing.  
3. **Trash Bags**: Include small zip-top bags for scraps (e.g., juice boxes, wrappers).  
4. **Utensils**: Pack 10 reusable cups, 10 spork sets, and 10 napkin rolls.  

This plan balances health, crowd-pleasers, and portability. Total mess should be minimal, freeing up everyone to ride rides and enjoy the party! Have an amazing time! 🎢🎉
