Here are classic recipes formatted for your bartenders, including finger foods ideas to complement your Art Deco Airport cocktail reception in Kentucky:

### **1. Old Fashioned**
- **Glass:** Rocks glass over ice  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz bourbon rye  
  - 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (garnish)  
- **Instructions:**  
  1. Muddle sugar cube with ¾ oz water OR stir simple syrup with bitters.  
  2. Add spirit, bitters, and ice. Stir until chilled.  
  3. Strain into glass over fresh ice.  
  4. Express orange twist over drink; garnish.  

### **2. Paper Plane**  
- **Glass:** Coupe glass  
- **Ingredients:**  
  Shake with ice:  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  - ¾ oz Aperol  
  - ½ oz Nonino Amaro  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Instructions:**  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into coupe glass.  
  3. Garnish with cherry (optional).  

### **3. Whiskey Sour**  
- **Glass:** Rocks glass over ice  
- **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - ½ oz simple syrup  
  - *Optional:* 0.5 oz egg white (for texture)  
- **Instructions:**  
  - *With egg white:*  
    1. Dry shake (no ice) all ingredients.  
    2. Add ice; shake again.  
    3. Strain over ice. Express lemon peel over drink.  
  - *Without egg white:* Skip dry shake; stir all ingredients. Garnish with cherry or orange slice.  

### **4. Recommended Gin Cocktail: French 75**  
- **Glass:** Champagne flute  
- **Ingredients:**  
  Shake with ice:  
  - 1.5 oz gin (London Dry preferred)  
  - 0.75 oz lemon juice  
  - 0.5 oz simple syrup  
- **Instructions:**  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into flute.  
  3. Top with 1.5 oz sparkling wine/champagne.  
  4. Garnish with lemon twist.

### **Key Notes for Bartenders**  
- **Bitters:** Ensure Angostura bitters are fresh (don’t substitute).  
- **Balance:** Paper Plane and Aviation emphasize clarity—shake well to extract amaro’s oils.  
- **Garnishes:** Use orange twists for Old Fashioned Paper Plane Aviation; lemon twists for French 75 Whiskey Sour.  
- **Kentucky Twist:** Highlight bourbon in all cocktails; use local honey in syrups if possible.
