It was a very complex situation. For the Pilgrims, it was a moment of relief after surviving starvation and disease.

For the Wampanoag, it was a diplomatic event. They had helped the settlers because they knew they would be useful for trade and defense. They likely saw the feast as a strategic alliance, not as a joyful celebration.

So, it was not a single, happy event for both groups, but rather a moment of cooperation within a much larger and often tragic story.
