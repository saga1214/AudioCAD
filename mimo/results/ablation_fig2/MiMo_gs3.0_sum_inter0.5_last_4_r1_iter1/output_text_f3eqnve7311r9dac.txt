Yes, older comics can be a great investment, but they are also very fragile.

Here are ten highly significant books from the 1940s and 50s, but be aware that finding them in good condition will be a challenge.

First, there's *Action Comics* number one again, Superman's debut.
Second, *Superboy* number one, which is the first appearance of Superman in a separate comic.
Third, *Adventures of Superman* number one, which is the first comic to feature Superman in his iconic costume.
Fourth, *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, Batman's first appearance.
Fifth, *Showcase* number four, which introduced The Flash.
Sixth, *Showcase* number six, which introduced Green Lantern.
Seventh, *The Flash Comics* number one, the first appearance of The Flash.
Eighth, *Aquaman* number one, his first comic.
Ninth, *Mister Miracle* number one, the first appearance of DC's cosmic hero.
And tenth, *Tales of the Black Dragon* number one, which is the first appearance of the DC Comics label itself.
