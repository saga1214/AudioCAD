Here are quick yet elegant cocktail recipes for your dinner party, keeping formality and dietary needs in mind:

### 1. **Sparkling Citrus Vodka (Soda-Free Vodka Mixer)**  
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - 1.5 oz vodka  
     - 0.5 oz fresh lemon or lime juice  
     - 2 oz sparkling water (infused with cucumber/mint for freshness, optional)  
     Vegetarians/Omnivores: Safe for both.  
   - **Garnish**: Lemon twist or fresh berries.  
   - **Why it works**: Refreshing, sophisticated, and soda-free. The infused sparkling water adds depth without artificial fizz.  

### 2. **Argentine-Style Fernet & Malbec**  
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - 0.5 oz Fernet-Branca (herbal, bitter)  
     - 2 oz Malbec wine (or substitute: gin for non-wine drinkers)  
     - 0.5 oz sweet vermouth or simple syrup  
     - Ice + orange slice or lemon wheel  
   - **Method**: Stir all ingredients, strain into a chilled glass.  
   - **Dietary Note**: Vegetarian-friendly (Fernet is alcohol-based, not veg).  
   - **Why it works**: A classic Argentine pairing—rich, complex, and conversation-starting.  

### **Key Reminders**:  
- **Soda Allergy**: Both cocktails are naturally soda-free. Label drinks clearly.  
- **Food Pairing**: Vodka should complement chocolate mousse or fruit tart (previously suggested).  
- **Entertainment Tie-In**: After Pictionary, serve these cocktails to transition smoothly into relaxed conversation.  

Enjoy your party! 🍸
