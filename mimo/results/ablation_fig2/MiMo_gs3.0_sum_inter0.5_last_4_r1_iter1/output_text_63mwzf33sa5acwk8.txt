Okay, so you and girlfriend drive down to Nevada, do some unique camping, then want to cap it off with a Vegas vibe. That's a solid plan. If you're camping in Red Rock or Valley of Fire, then heading into Vegas for a night sounds perfect. 

For a proper Vegas experience, I'd suggest you skip the typical hotel resort thing unless you're into that. Instead, think about staying somewhere unique like a boutique hotel or even a small boutique motel off the strip. You could check out places like The Ace Hotel or The Cosmopolitan, but they can be pricey. Alternatively, you could find a cool vintage motel like the El Cortez or the Golden Nugget. 

Then, for activities, definitely get a room with a hot tub if possible. Vegas also has amazing food, so maybe find a unique restaurant. You could also check out a live music venue like the House of Blues or an intimate comedy club. The main thing is to make it feel special, like you two are on top of the world for a night.
