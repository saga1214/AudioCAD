Absolutely! For a 'Retro Tech Day' lunch, you could do a 'Vintage Computer Chip Platter'. It’s simple but thematic. You'd have a few things: first, 'Chips and Salsa' – and I don't mean computer chips, but tortilla chips with salsa, guacamole, and maybe some black beans to look like circuit boards. Second, you could have a 'Keyboard Snacks,' which would be something like mini sandwiches or cut fruit arranged like keys on a keyboard. And third, 'Power-Up Pudding,' which is just chocolate pudding with gummy bears for the 'data bits'. It's easy, fun, and everyone can eat it. Another idea is to have a 'Dial-Up Dinner.' For that, you could have 'Modem Meatballs' – little meatballs with pasta shapes like numbers and letters. Then, 'Fax Machine Fries' – which are just french fries or tater tots laid out like a fax machine. And for dessert, 'Dot Matrix Cookies' – simple cookies with little dots on them. Both ideas are easy to adapt for different diets, like making sandwiches instead of chips or swapping cookies for fruit.
