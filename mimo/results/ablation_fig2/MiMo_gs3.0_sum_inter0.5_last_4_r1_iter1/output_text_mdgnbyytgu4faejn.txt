Here’s a clear, ATS-friendly format with your font preferences included—designed for quick execution:

### **Header (Top Section)**
1. **Your Name**: Centered at 14pt (e.g., Arial, Calibri, or Helvetica).  
2. Below immediately:  
   - Email: 9pt (same font)  
   - Phone: 9pt  
   - City: 9pt (add state if in the US).  
**Add an icon-free LinkedIn profile link** (e.g., `linkedin.com/in/yourname`). Icons may trigger ATS issues.  

> *Order Suggestion*: Name → Email → Phone → City/LDn link is optimal scannability.

---

### **Summary (2-3 Lines)**  
- **Professional Summary**:  
  > "X years of [relevant skill] experience in [industry]. Seeking to leverage expertise in [key achievement] for [target role]."  
- **Font**: 10-11pt (same as body text).  

---

### **Experience**  
**Structure**:  
- **Job Title**: 9pt  
- **Company**: 9pt  
- **Dates**: 9pt (e.g., "Jan 2018 – Present")  
- **Bullet Points**: 9pt (3-4 concise bullets per role, using action verbs).  

**For career changes**:  
- Group roles by theme (e.g., "Marketing Experience").  
- List older roles under **"Relevant Experience"** (dates included).  
- List newer/less relevant roles under **"Additional Experience"** (minimal bullets).  

---

### **Skills Section**  
- **Font**: 9pt  
- **List only job-relevant skills**:  
  > Technical: Python, Tableau  
  > Soft: Project Management, Stakeholder Communication  
- *Avoid generic skills* (e.g., "Team Player").  

---

### **Education**  
- **Degree(s)**: 9pt (e.g., "B.S. Computer Science")  
- **University**: 9pt  
- **Dates**: 9pt (e.g., "May 2015 – May 2019")  
- **Honors/Scholarships**: Add directly under degrees (e.g., "Graduated with Highest Honors").  
- **Remove multiple degrees from one school**—list only the highest degree.  

---

### **Extras (Optional)**  
- **Certifications**: 9pt (e.g., "PMP, 2023")  
- **Projects**: 9pt (brief 1-line description)  
- **Volunteer Work**: 9pt  

---

### **Font & Spacing Rules**  
- **Consistency**: 9pt for all non-header text.  
- **Margins**: 0.5" on all sides.  
- **Length**: 1 page max (unless 10+ years of *relevant* experience).  

### **Key Fixes from Your Draft**  
1. Move contacts directly below your name (vertically, not to the right).  
2. Remove contact icons.  
3. Add a LinkedIn link.  
4. Use "Relevant Experience" for older roles.  
5. List all dates for jobs/education.  
6. Place honors/scholarships under their respective education entries.
