That's a great approach—being strategic with your email.

Here are a few pointers to help you write it. 

First, start with a clear subject line, like "Meeting Request: Essay Exam Feedback - Student [Your Name]". 

In the email, state your purpose simply and positively. For example: "Professor [His Last Name], I scored a 64 on the recent exam about [the course topic]. I'm eager to do well on future tests and was hoping to understand what I could have improved on in my essay score. Would you have some time to meet briefly?"

Ending with a polite offer for scheduling shows you respect his time. For instance: "If office hours don't work, please let me know what time might suit you best."

This keeps the focus on your growth as a student, making it much harder for him to react defensively.
