It's exciting that you're getting ready for winter. Ice fishing already sounds fun, and adding sap tapping and snowshoeing to the list is a great plan. Snowshoes can be rented at local shops if you don't want to buy them yet. You'll also want to pack layers of clothing, because it gets cold fast out in the snow. Besides those, you could also try cross-country skiing, which is another great way to explore snowy trails. Or, if you have a dog, they love playing in the snow. Just remember to stay warm and hydrated while you're out there enjoying all the winter activities.
