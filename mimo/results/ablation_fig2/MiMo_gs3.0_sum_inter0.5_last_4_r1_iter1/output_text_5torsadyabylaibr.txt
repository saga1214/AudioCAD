Absolutely. For Thanksgiving, a great, relatively easy dessert is a no-bake pumpkin pie. You just mix canned pumpkin, some spices, and the pre-made cream cheese crust in a bowl, and then pour it into a baking dish. You let it set in the fridge.

For a classic Thanksgiving savory dish, you could make a simple turkey stuffing in a casserole dish. You just cook the sausage and onions, mix them with the bread and broth, and bake it.
