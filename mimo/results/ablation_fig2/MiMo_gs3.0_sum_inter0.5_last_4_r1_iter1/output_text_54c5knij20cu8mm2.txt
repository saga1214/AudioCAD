Okay, let’s break this down. 

First, the left-brain versus right-brain thing. It’s mostly a myth. Real brain function is way more integrated. Both hemispheres work together constantly, and creativity and logic aren’t neatly split. The idea that one side is purely creative and the other is purely logical is an oversimplification. But the metaphor is useful for explaining how people can have different preferences or strengths in certain tasks. 

As for careers, since you want something creative but also structured and objective, here are a few ideas. One is architecture. It’s creative, but it’s also about solving real-world problems with measurable results. Another is graphic design, especially for technical or scientific fields, like data visualization. This blends creativity with precise, objective communication. You could also look into technical writing, which is about making complex information clear and accessible. It’s creative in structuring content but relies on accuracy. And finally, UX/UI design combines creativity with a deep understanding of how people interact with technology, which is very measurable. 

These fields let you build things that have lasting impact while still being objective. 

Hope that helps! Let me know if you want to explore any of these further.
