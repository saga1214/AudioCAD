Oh, okay, kosher dietary laws. The main things to watch out for are no pork and no shellfish. So your weenies in bacon... yeah, that's pork, so you'd have to skip that one. The hummus and fruit platter are generally fine. For theme, dark chocolate is usually okay, just check the ingredients for any non-kosher stuff. Spooky cookies could also work. If you want to be super thorough, you could even find a blood pudding recipe that uses a kosher substitute, like beet juice, to keep the theme but make it safe. That might be a fun challenge!
