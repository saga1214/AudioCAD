Calgary is a great launchpad for adventures. For a romantic weekend getaway, you have some wonderful options.

One is a classic mountain escape. You could drive about an hour and a half to Banff or Lake Louise. It's stunning, but on a weekend, it gets very busy. A more romantic alternative nearby is Emerald Lake, in the same park but much quieter and more peaceful.

If you'd prefer a quick escape without a car, there's Canmore. It has a charming downtown and is close to the Canadian Rockies. You could take a scenic drive along the Bow Valley Parkway and visit the famous Johnston Canyon. It has beautiful waterfalls and hiking trails.

For something completely different, you could try the Banff Gondola. It's a modern, glass elevator that takes you up Sulphur Mountain for breathtaking views. From the top, you can walk on a boardwalk through the forest. It's very unique and romantic, especially at sunset.

And if you want something close to the city, there's the Calgary Zoo. It's surprisingly well done and has a lovely, modern conservatory with a romantic feel. Or, you could take a day trip up to the Canmore Nordic Centre, where you can go on a guided snowshoe walk through beautiful forest trails.
