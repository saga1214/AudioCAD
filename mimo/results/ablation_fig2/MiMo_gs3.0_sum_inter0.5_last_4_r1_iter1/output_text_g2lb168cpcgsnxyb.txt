You've brought up some incredibly important safety and ethical issues.

While the relationship you have is vital, your number one priority in those first 20 to 30 minutes—and in your job overall—must now be safety.

This includes several things. First, advocate for a safer session space. The dogs running around and a cluttered house are serious safety hazards. You should document this in your reports and formally request that sessions happen in a clearer area, that the dogs are managed or removed, and that a second pair of eyes is always on you both.

Second, before each session, do a quick safety check. Clear a small space around you, make sure any pets are supervised elsewhere, and have a clear exit plan just in case elopement happens.

Connecting with him is still the ultimate goal, but you can't do it effectively if he or you aren't safe. Making these changes to the environment will actually give you the stability you need to build on that great connection and have truly effective sessions.
