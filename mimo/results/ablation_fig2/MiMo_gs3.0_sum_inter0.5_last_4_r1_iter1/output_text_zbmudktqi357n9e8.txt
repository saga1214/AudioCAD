Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time for that would be late October through mid-November. That’s their peak fall season, especially in the central and southern regions. You’ll see the most incredible color changes in the trees and foliage. 

Just, you know, keep in mind the weather. It’s warm, so you’ll get plenty of sun for those vibrant colors, but also some rain, especially in the south. If you want to focus more on coastal scenes, early November might be a bit calmer with fewer crowds. 

And definitely pack light watercolors and plenty of autumn blues and oranges. Mexico will give you tons of inspiration to work with. Enjoy your trip!
