Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items while respecting your new apartment rules:

### 1. **TV Mount Installation**
   - **Check Landlord Rules**: Confirm if your landlord allows mounting TVs. If not, use a freestanding stand.
   - **Stud-Finding**: Use a stud finder to locate wall studs. Mark positions with a pencil.
   - **Mount Selection**: Choose a mount rated for your TV's weight (check manufacturer specs).
   - **Installation**:
     1. Align the mount's brackets with stud marks.
     2. Secure with long screws (typically 2.5–3 inches) into studs.
     3. Attach the TV using the included bolts.
   - **Alternative**: Use heavy-duty **mounting tape** or **wall anchors** if no studs are available (ensure mount supports weight).

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Lightweight Options**: For small planters, use **command hooks** or **clear adhesive strips** (test strength first).
   - **Heavy Planters**: 
     - Locate studs or use heavy-duty **wall anchors**.
     - Install a **picture wire** or **hook** rated for the planter’s weight.
   - **Safety**: Ensure planters are balanced and won’t tip forward. Use **safety chains** if needed.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **No-Drill Alternatives**:
     - **Magnetic Strips**: For lightweight decor (e.g., small plants, bowls).
     - **Adhesive Hooks**: Use high-tensile **3M VHB tape** for medium-weight items.
   - **Traditional Installation**:
     1. Measure shelf length and mark stud locations.
     2. Attach **L-brackets** to studs with appropriate screws.
     3. Secure the shelf to the brackets.
   - **Tip**: For glass shelves, use **corner brackets** attached to studs.

### General Tips:
- **Pace Decorations**: Dedicate 2–3 hours on Sundays to avoid fatigue. Take breaks to stretch!
- **Noise Control**: Use power tools (drill, saw) during off-hours (e.g., 10 AM–2 PM) if permitted.
- **Tools Needed**: Stud finder, level, measuring tape, drill, screws, anchors (if needed), and a hammer/mallet.

### Safety & Efficiency:
- **Weight Limits**: Never exceed a mount/shelf’s rated capacity.
- **Electrical Safety**: Avoid drilling near outlets/wires. Use a non-contact voltage tester.
- **Workspace Prep**: Clear the area, keep tools organized, and dispose of packaging properly.

By following these steps, you’ll create a cozy, functional space without violating your landlord’s rules. Celebrate small wins—each installed item makes your new home feel more like yours! 🎨✨
