If your glue gun is hot but not dispensing glue, here are a few troubleshooting steps:
1. **Check for clogs**: Sometimes residue or dry glue blocks the nozzle. Try heating it for a few seconds longer and gently tapping the tip to clear any blockages.
2. **Verify the glue type**: Ensure you're using the correct glue (e decor glue for craft guns). Some guns require specific types.
3. **Inspect the tip**: A damaged or worn-out tip can cause issues. Try replacing it or cleaning it with a brush and a bit of acetone.
4. **Apply steady pressure**: Hold the trigger down firmly and steadily for several seconds. Glue often takes a moment to melt and flow.
5. **Check the glue level**: If the glue is low, it might not be heating through properly. Refill it to the line and let it sit for 10–15 minutes to heat evenly.
6. **Clean the nozzle**: Wipe the tip with a cloth or cotton swab to remove any old residue that might be affecting flow.
7. **Let it fully heat up**: Even if it feels hot, some guns need 20–30 minutes to reach optimal temperature. Avoid overfilling to prevent overflow.
