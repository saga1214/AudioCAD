### 🍿 Savory & Crunchy (Savory-Friendly)
1. **Nut & Seed Clusters**  
   *Why*: Roasted almonds, cashews, or pumpkin seeds in a salty-sweet mix. Portable and filling.  
   *Pair with*: A small bowl of popcorn (plain or lightly salted).

2. **Cheese & Cracker Packs**  
   *Why*: Mini cheddar or mozzarella cubes + whole-grain crackers. Classic combo.  
   *Upgrade*: Add a wedge of sharp cheddar or a brie rind for dipping.

3. **Spiced Roasted Chickpeas**  
   *Why*: Crunchy, protein-packed, and customizable (e.g., smoked paprika or curry-spiced).  
   *Tip*: Buy a pre-seasoned bag at the machine.

### 🍬 Sweet but Minimalist (If You Crave Indulgence)
4. **Single-Serve Gummies/Mints**  
   *Why*: Tiny portions of tangy sour gummies or cooling mints. Not overly sweet.  
   *Bonus*: Fun for sharing on the new table!

5. **Dark Chocolate-Covered Almonds**  
   *Why*: Rich cocoa but balanced with salty nutty flavor. 70%+ cocoa minimizes sweetness.

### 🎉 Table-Friendly Display Ideas
- **"Build-Your-Own" Snack Bar**: Arrange small bowls of nuts, seeds, pretzels, and popcorn in a tray.  
- **Chocolate "Library"**: Stack those Tootsie Rolls vertically in a glass jar for visual fun.  
- **Candy Dissection Station**: Buy a bulk candy bag and sort into small cups (e.g., gummies in one, mints in another).
