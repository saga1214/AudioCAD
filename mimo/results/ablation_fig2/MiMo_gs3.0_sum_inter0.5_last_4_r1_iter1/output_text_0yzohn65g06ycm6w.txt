### Feeding Solutions:
1. **Chip-Timed Feeders**: Microchip or automated dispensers release food only when each cat approaches, preventing greedy ones from eating everything and ensuring fussy ones access meals.  
2. **Room Separation**: Confine fussy cats in a closed room for feeding before you leave home.  

### Poverty-Friendly Pet Care:
1. **Resourcefulness**:  
   - Mix small amounts of quality food with cheap fillers (e.g., plain cooked rice/pasta) to stretch portions.  
   - Supplement with safe, occasional table scraps (e.g., plain chicken, fish) when affordable.  
2. **Seek Aid**:  
   - Search for local pet food banks, charities, or shelter donation programs.  
   - Prioritize low-cost services: Spaying/neutering, essential vaccinations, and parasite prevention.  
3. **Preventative Health**:  
   - **Cleanliness**: Regularly wash food bowls, litter boxes, and living spaces.  
   - **Weight Management**: Use free toys (e.g., string, crumpled paper) for low-cost exercise to avoid obesity.  
   - **Self-Monitoring**: Daily check for weight loss, lethargy, or litter changes. Catch issues early.  
   - **Basic Grooming**: Brush cats to reduce hairballs and skin problems.  

### Key Principles:
- **Control Access**: Tech or spatial solutions address your schedule and eating habits.  
- **Prevent Costly Emergencies**: Stretch food, prioritize preventative care, and maintain a healthy weight to avoid expensive vet bills.  
- **Leverage Community**: Free resources exist—ask local shelters, vet clinics, or online groups for help.
