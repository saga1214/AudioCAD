Alright, here is a nautical-themed nautical birthday shopping list with minimal mess factor. 

For appetizers or “buried treasure”, you could do mini pirate ship sandwiches cut into shapes like masts or sails. Goldfish crackers themed as doubloons or coins would also be fun. Veggie sticks shaped like stars or circles and hummus cups with little flags on top would be great too. Cheese cubes or cheese straws shaped like flags or bones. 

For fruit, you could do “mermaid tails” which are banana slices with blueberry eyes, or themed skewers with grapes and strawberries. Remember to avoid watermelon for the one kid with the allergy. Pineapple chunks shaped like ship wheels or apple slices with a cheese wheel on top could be cool. 

For main foods, mini hot dogs or mini pizzas cut into ship shapes would be easy for seven and six other kids at the beach. Popcorn shaped like treasure chests is another easy one. Crackers packed in sealed snack bags could be themed as treasure chests.

For drinks, individual juice boxes labeled as “pirate punch” or “sea water” would be easy and sand-free. Small water bottles themed as “anchors aweigh” or “treasure maps” would also work. You could even make lemonade cups with a straw that looks like a rope. 

For dessert besides your amazing cake, you could do nautical cookies shaped like pirates or ships, or gummy sharks and jellyfish. Themed cupcakes with a pirate hat topper would be great.

Packaging is key! Treasure chest serving trays, flags, and rope decorations will tie everything together. And don't forget allergy-friendly labels! Have fun with his pirate outfit!
