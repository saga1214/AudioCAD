That's a fantastic set of questions! Let's break them down:

**On Color-Coding Icons:**
*   **Memory vs. Mental Load:** Both are significant benefits! Color-coding leverages your brain's strong visual memory system (making it easier to recall where things are), *and* it visually reduces cognitive load. Seeing a clear, color-coded structure instantly tells you the category without you having to mentally scan or decode labels. It's a powerful combination.

**On Playlist Switching:**
*   **Optimal Timing vs. Preference:** It's heavily personal, but there's a logic to switching. Prolonged exposure to *any* sound can lead to subtle habituation (your brain tunes it out). Switching every 60-90 minutes can:
    *   Prevent habituation, keeping the sound stimulating or masking effects fresh.
    *   Provide a subtle auditory cue for a mental reset or transition between tasks.
    *   Avoid monotony.
*   **Recommendation:** Try it! Listen to Lo-fi for 60-90 mins, then switch to nature sounds (rain, ocean, forest) for the next hour. Notice if you feel refreshed or more alert. If you find switching distracting, stick with one for longer. If you find it energizing, keep switching.

**On Physical Documents & Environment:**
*   **Progress Source:** Both matter, but **breaking tasks into chunks** is often the *primary* driver of progress feeling. Tackling a huge pile feels overwhelming.
*   **Environment's Role:** A clean, organized workspace *facilitates* that chunking. It removes visual clutter and distractions, making it easier to focus on one small task at a time. It creates the *conditions* for progress, but the *act* of chunking and completing the small task is what generates the progress feeling.
*   **Recommendation:** Tackle physical files on a quieter day *and* chunk it. Define a small, specific goal for each session (e.g., "File all invoices from Jan 1-15," "Shred outdated receipts," "Organize tax documents by year"). Celebrate completing each chunk!

**Dinner Suggestions (Focusing on Mediterranean & Quick/Energy Boosting):**

Since you craved Mediterranean snacks earlier, here are a few dinner ideas leaning that way (or quick/easy options):

1.  **Lemon-Herb Grilled Chicken with Quinoa & Roasted Veggies:** Light, healthy, and satisfying. Toss chicken with lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, oregano, and rosemary. Serve over quinoa with roasted broccoli or bell peppers. Add a simple salad (cucumber, tomato, olives, red onion, lemon-olive oil dressing).
2.  **Quick Tuna & White Bean Salad:** Mash canned white beans with a can of tuna, lemon juice, olive oil, diced celery, red onion, salt, pepper, and maybe a pinch of red pepper flakes. Serve in lettuce cups, over greens, or with whole-wheat crackers.
3.  **Pasta with Pesto & Cherry Tomatoes:** Use a high-quality store-bought basil pesto (or make your own). Toss with cooked pasta (fettuccine, penne), halved cherry tomatoes, and a sprinkle of grated Parmesan. Add grilled chicken or shrimp if you want protein.
4.  **Hearty Lentil & Vegetable Soup:** A warm, comforting, and protein/fiber-rich option. Simmer brown or green lentils with diced carrots, celery, onions, canned tomatoes, vegetable broth, and herbs (thyme, bay leaf). Serve with crusty bread. Add canned beans for extra protein.
5.  **Stir-Fry with Tofu/Tempeh:** Quickly stir-fry cubed extra-firm tofu or tempeh with broccoli, snap peas, carrots, and bell peppers in a garlic-ginger sauce (soy sauce/tamari, sesame oil, rice vinegar). Serve over brown rice or noodles. Top with sesame seeds and green onions.
