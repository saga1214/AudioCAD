You're welcome! Here are the three shrimp main courses you asked for, with ingredients lists included:

1.  **Spicy Shrimp Scampi over Pasta**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined (tails on or off)
        *   8 oz pasta ( Linguine, Spaghetti, or Fettuccine work well)
        *   4 tbsp butter
        *   4 cloves garlic, minced
        *   1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped (plus more for garnish)
        *   1/4 cup dry white wine (like Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc) - optional but recommended
        *   1/2 cup reserved pasta water
        *   1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for spice level)
        *   Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
    *   **Prep Tip:** Cook pasta al dente, reserving about 1/2 cup pasta water before draining. Toss shrimp and pasta with the sauce.

2.  **Shrimp Fra Diavolo**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined (tails on or off)
        *   1 tbsp olive oil
        *   4 cloves garlic, minced
        *   1/2 cup red pepper flakes (adjust for spice level)
        *   1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
        *   1/4 cup dry white wine (like Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc) - optional
        *   1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
        *   Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
        *   1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese (optional, for serving)
    *   **Prep Tip:** Serve over spaghetti or linguine. Garnish with extra parsley and Parmesan if using.

3.  **Spicy Cajun Shrimp and Grits**
    *   **Ingredients:**
        *   1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined (tails on or off)
        *   1/4 cup butter
        *   1/4 cup vegetable oil
        *   2 tbsp Cajun seasoning (like Tony Chachere's or your favorite blend)
        *   1 cup quick-cooking grits (instant)
        *   1/2 cup chicken broth or water
        *   1/4 tsp salt (or to taste)
        *   1/4 tsp black pepper
        *   1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
        *   1/4 cup chopped green onions
        *   Hot sauce (like Crystal or Tabasco) - for serving
    *   **Prep Tip:** Cook grits according to package directions, stirring constantly until creamy. Stir in butter, oil, Cajun seasoning, salt, and pepper. Keep warm until serving. Toss shrimp in Cajun seasoning and pan-sear in a hot skillet for 2-3 minutes per side until pink and opaque. Serve shrimp over the grits, garnished with parsley and green onions. Offer hot sauce on the side.

These dishes showcase your shrimp stash and lean into spicy, comforting flavors. Remember to season well and adjust the spice level (red pepper flakes, Cajun seasoning, hot sauce) to perfectly match their preference! Good luck – your friend will feel so cared for.
