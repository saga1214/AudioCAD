Dallas has a great Asian food scene! For authentic Asian noodles, you should check out Sushi Yoshinobu, which has amazing ramen and sushi. If you want a more casual spot, Noodle House is great for a wide variety of Asian-style noodles. And for a more upscale experience, try Wing Lei, which is a Michelin-starred Chinese restaurant.

As for protein bars, if you want something tasty and filling, I’d suggest Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard, which is high in protein and has good flavors. Another great option is Quest Nutrition, which is low-carb and high-protein. If you want something with natural ingredients, consider Clif Builder’s bars, which are great for post-workout recovery.
