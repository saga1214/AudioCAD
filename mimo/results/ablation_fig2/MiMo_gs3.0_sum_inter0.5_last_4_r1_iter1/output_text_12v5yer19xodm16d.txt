Hey, I hear you! It sounds like you need a boost. For drinks, here are five ideas. First, a strong cup of black coffee. It's packed with caffeine and great for a quick pick-me-up. Second, green tea. It has caffeine plus something called L-theanine that helps you focus without the jitters. Third, a glass of cold milk is simple and can be surprisingly energizing. Fourth, you could try an energy drink, like Red Bull or Monster, but be careful not to overdo it. And fifth, a cup of hot cocoa with a little caffeine in it can be comforting and give you a gentle lift. 

For snacks, here are five more ideas. First, some nuts like almonds or walnuts, which are healthy fats and protein. Second, Greek yogurt is high in protein and can be energizing. Third, an apple is a classic choice with natural sugars. Fourth, peanut butter toast is a balanced option with carbs and protein. And fifth, some dark chocolate, maybe 70 percent or higher, has caffeine and antioxidants. Hope you find something that works for you!
