Okay, since you love the ocean and seafood, here are a few water activities.

One idea is a sunset kayak tour. Many coastal towns offer guided trips where you can paddle through quiet bays and see sea turtles.

For something more relaxing, you could look for a catamaran sail. They move smoothly, so seasickness shouldn't be a problem, and you can often arrange for a private charter.

Another option is a flyboarding lesson. It's a high-energy water sport that's a great conversation starter.
