Of course. For the 'Retro Tech' theme, you could do a 'Dial-Up Dinner.' The main dish could be something like ‘Keyboard Keema,’ which is spiced ground meat shaped like keyboard keys, served on a bed of ‘Monitor Moss’ which is just quinoa or greens. For the snack, you could have ‘Mouse Morsels,’ which are cheese cubes on toothpicks to look like computer mice. As a side, maybe a ‘Floppy Fries’ tray with tater tots cut into floppy disks. Another idea is a ‘Blackberry Brunch’ for a different theme. The main dish could be a quiche with blackberries and bacon, served with a side of ‘CRT Crust’ which is just toasted bagel halves to look like old TVs. For snacks, you could have mini sandwiches cut into floppy disk shapes, and maybe some blackberry jam for dipping pretzels. Both ideas keep it themed but also simple to prepare.
