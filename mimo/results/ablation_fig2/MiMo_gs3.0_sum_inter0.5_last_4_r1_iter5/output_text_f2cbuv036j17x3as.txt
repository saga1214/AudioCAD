Okay, diesel camper vans are workhorses, so doing a pre-trip inspection is smart. For brakes, a couple of things to look for are any weird noises, like grinding or squealing, or if the brake fluid looks low. Also, check if the pads and rotors are worn down—look for uneven surfaces or shiny spots, which can be a sign of excessive wear. As for spark plugs, diesel engines are a bit different. They use compression ignition, so the spark plugs are less critical than in a gasoline engine. Still, if you’ve got old, fouled, or broken ones, they should be replaced. You’ll need to get the right type for your van, and the process involves removing the old ones, cleaning the area, installing the new ones, and torquing them down properly. But honestly, if you're not comfortable with that, it’s always best to consult a diesel mechanic before the trip. Better safe than sorry.
