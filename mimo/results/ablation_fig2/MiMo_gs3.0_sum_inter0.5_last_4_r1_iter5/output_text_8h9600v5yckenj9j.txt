For candy tomorrow, grab classic Halloween favorites like Reese’s Cups, Hershey’s Kisses, Skittles, and gummies. Mini chocolate bars like M&M’s or Snickers are always safe bets. You could also do a pumpkin bucket of assorted candy for a fun touch. If you’ve got allergies again to artificial dyes, check for candies dyed naturally or with non-artificial food coloring—many brands have options now. Keep it simple and popular, and you’ll have plenty of happy guests!
