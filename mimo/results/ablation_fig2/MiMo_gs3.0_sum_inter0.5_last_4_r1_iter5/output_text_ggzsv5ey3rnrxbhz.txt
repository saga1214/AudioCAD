The name 'forever chemicals' can sound pretty alarming. It's true they are very persistent, but that doesn't mean exposure is permanent or that there's no way to reduce it. The best approach is to focus on prevention.

Based on scientific evidence, the five products you should avoid to minimize exposure are:
First, non-stick cookware, because it contains PFAS.
Second, grease-resistant food packaging, because it also has PFAS.
Third, stain- or waterproof fabrics and furnishings.
Fourth, cosmetics and dental floss that aren't labeled as PFAS-free.
And fifth, products without proper water filtration, since tap water can contain them.
