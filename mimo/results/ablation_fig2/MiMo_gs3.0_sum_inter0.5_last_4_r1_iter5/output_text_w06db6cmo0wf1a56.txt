Here's a well-balanced cooler-friendly shopping list designed for 10 adults and 10 kids, focusing on easy-to-grab, mess-free options with some indulgences:

**DRINKS** (Pack plenty of 20-30 oz water bottles for each person)
- **For Adults:** Sugar-free sodas (e.g., Coke Zero, Pepsi Zero) in individual 12-oz cartons/bottles; Infused water (fill some bottles with cucumber/mint/lemon slices); Low-sugar electrolyte waters
- **For Kids:** Low-sugar juice boxes; Sparkling water (plain or lightly flavored); Water bottles

**SNACKS** (Single-serve packs are key!)
- Cheese sticks (adult & kid favorite)
- Baby carrots (with 10 single-serve hummus cups)
- Single-serve pretzels/trail mix bags
- Whole grain crackers (like Triscuits) in small bags
- Grapes (pre-washed in containers)
- Fruit leather pouches
- Protein bars (mineral oil-based for less mess)

**MAIN MEAL** (Pre-prepared individual portions)
- **Calzones Option 1:** Pre-order 10 mini calzones from a local pizzeria (great for his love + minimal mess)
- **Calzones Option 2:** Make 10 personal pizza cups (like mini calzones) at home – pack in foil
- Sandwich/Wrap Alternative: 10 pre-made wraps (e.g., turkey & spinach, chicken & avocado) in individual boxes

**DESSERT** (Celebratory but grab-and-go!)
- **Option A:** Cupcakes (10 individual) – less messy than cake
- **Option B:** Individual brownie packs (like the kind from Costco) – easy to hand out whole
- *Avoid:* Large cake/pie (requires serving utensils and plates)

**Cooler Packing Tip:** Use leak-proof containers, separate coolers per meal/snacks, and pack ice packs generously! This setup should keep everyone fueled and minimize park cleanup. Have an amazing, break-free mom celebration for the bachelorette too! 🎢父子时光
