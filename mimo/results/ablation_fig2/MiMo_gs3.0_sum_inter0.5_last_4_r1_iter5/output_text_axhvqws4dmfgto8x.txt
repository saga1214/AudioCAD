Alright, let's tackle this stir-fry question with a focus on noodle safety!

The smoke-alarm set-off history makes wooden utensils a definite no-no. Noodles themselves can stick to the bottom of the pot if you don't have enough water, so we'll prevent that.

Here’s the simple noodle rule: use metal spoons or tongs, and make sure there's enough water. For the noodles, salt your water generously, then add the noodles and stir them occasionally. You can cook them just like you would pasta, just without wood!
