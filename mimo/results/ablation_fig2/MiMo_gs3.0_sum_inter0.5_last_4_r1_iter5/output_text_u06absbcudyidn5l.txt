Okay, let's tackle this. Chronic skin redness can come from many places. Rosacea is a big one, sun damage over years, genetics... lots of possibilities. Fibers dysplasia and vasculitis aren't directly linked, but vasculitis can definitely cause the redness.

Vasculitis is basically when your immune system mistakenly attacks your blood vessels. It can show up at any age. Symptoms are pretty general at first—fever, fatigue, aches—then skin rashes or sores. To figure it out, a doctor will look at you, run some blood tests, and often need to do a biopsy. The key types are usually named by the size of the vessels they affect, so large, medium, or small.

Because it can affect the whole body, the risks can be serious. The inflammation can narrow the vessels, which can lead to organ damage if blood flow is restricted. Treatment usually involves calming the immune system with steroids. 

And yes, that inflammation can absolutely affect your hair and nails, making them weak and brittle. It's all connected. For your skin, a dermatologist is crucial to pinpoint the cause. For hair and nails, things like collagen or iron can help strengthen them. For the redness itself, you should absolutely talk to a doctor before starting any new medication like ibuprofen.
