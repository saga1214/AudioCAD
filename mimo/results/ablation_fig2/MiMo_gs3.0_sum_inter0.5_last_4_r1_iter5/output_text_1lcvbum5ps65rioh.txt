**BC U-Turn Rules & Penalties:**

1.  **General Rule:** U-turns are generally permitted **unless prohibited by a sign**.
2.  **Prohibited When:**
    *   At an intersection with a traffic control signal (stop sign, traffic light, yield sign) *unless* a sign specifically authorizes it.
    *   On curves, on hills, or where the driver cannot see at least 150 meters in both directions.
    *   In the lane of traffic opposite to your direction.
    *   Where it would interfere with traffic.
3.  Estimating 150m: Visualize roughly 1.5 football fields. If you can't clearly see a safe crossing distance in both directions, don't do it.
4.  **Penalties for Illegal U-Turns:**
    *   **Fine:** Typically $160.00 (as of 2023).
    *   **Demerit Points:** Usually 3 demerit points.
    Minor oopsies (like a quick, safe U-turn in a wide, clear lane) are less likely to be ticketed, but unsafe or obstructive turns will be penalized.

**Recommendation:** Always look for signs! When in doubt, pull over safely to make the U-turn instead of doing it in traffic.

**Spaghetti Carbonara Recipe (Vegetarian Friendly):**

This classic Roman pasta relies on eggs, cheese, cured pork (guanciale/pancetta), garlic, and pepper for its creamy sauce. **No cream or cream cheese is used!**

**Ingredients (Serves 4):**

*   **Pasta:**
    *   400g (approx. 14 oz) Spaghetti (or Bucatini)
    *   1-1.5 Liters Salted Water (for cooking pasta)
*   **Sauce:**
    *   200g Guanciale (Italian cured pork cheek - best) or Pancetta (Italian cured pork belly - good substitute)
    *   1-2 cloves Garlic, finely minced
    *   6 large Eggs (at room temperature)
    *   120g (approx. 4.2 oz) Pecorino Romano Cheese, freshly grated (plus extra for serving)
    *   60g (approx. 2.1 oz) Parmigiano Reggiano Cheese, freshly grated (plus extra for serving)
    *   Freshly ground Black Pepper (lots!)
    *   Salt (for pasta water)
*   **Optional Garnish:**
    *   Extra grated Pecorino & Parmigiano
    *   Freshly ground Black Pepper

**Instructions:**

1.  **Cook the Guanciale/Pancetta:** While water heats, cut the cured pork into small cubes. Place in a cold skillet, cover with a splash of water or red wine (optional), and cook over medium heat. Cook until the fat renders out and the pork is crispy and cooked through (about 10-15 mins). Remove from heat, drain off most of the rendered fat (leave about 1-2 tbsp), and set aside. *Reserve the hot fat in the skillet.*
2.  **Mise en Place:** Mince the garlic finely. Grate both cheeses. Crack the eggs into a large bowl. Add the minced garlic, grated Pecorino Romano, grated Parmigiano Reggiano, and a generous amount of black pepper. Whisk vigorously until the cheeses are fully incorporated and the egg mixture is smooth.
3.  **Cook the Pasta:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a rolling boil. Add the spaghetti and cook until *al dente* (firm to the bite). **Crucially, reserve at least 1 cup of the starchy pasta cooking water before draining.**
4.  **Combine:** **Do not drain the pasta yet.** While the pasta is still hot and in the pot, add the hot guanciale/pancetta and its reserved fat back to the skillet over very low heat. Give the egg/cheese mixture a quick whisk again. **Immediately remove the hot skillet from the heat source.** This prevents scrambling the eggs.
5.  **Temper the Eggs:** Quickly add the hot, drained pasta to the skillet with the guanciale mixture. Toss vigorously. The residual heat from the pasta and skillet will cook the eggs. **Add splashes of the reserved pasta water, a few tablespoons at a time, while tossing constantly.** This creates the creamy sauce without scrambling the eggs. Continue adding water until the sauce coats the pasta beautifully (it will thicken as it cools). The pasta should be well coated and the sauce creamy, not runny or solidified.
6.  **Serve Immediately:** Plate the pasta immediately. Top generously with extra grated Pecorino, Parmigiano, and a final grind of black pepper.

**Key Tips:**
*   **Tempering is Crucial:** The residual heat of the pasta and skillet *must* cook the eggs without boiling them.
*   **Pasta Water:** The starchy water is essential for creating the creamy sauce.
*   **Low Heat:** Keep the skillet off direct heat during mixing.
*   **Work Fast:** Have everything ready before adding the hot pasta.
*   **Garlic:** Add it to the guanciale early so it cooks off its raw bite.

Enjoy your delicious, authentic spaghetti carbonara!
