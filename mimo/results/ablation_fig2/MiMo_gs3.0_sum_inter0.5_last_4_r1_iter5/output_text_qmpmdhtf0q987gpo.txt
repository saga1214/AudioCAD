Okay, you've light-sanded the doors and box, removed all the hardware, degreased, and primed. You're ready to paint.

Before you apply the actual cabinet paint, you need to reattach all the hardware you took off. It's much easier to paint the cabinet parts separately and then put the new color on the hardware too.

So, paint the cabinet doors and boxes. Let them dry completely. Then, paint the hardware. Let that dry completely. Once everything is dry, you can put it all back together. Make sure the hardware is screwed on tight. You're almost there!
