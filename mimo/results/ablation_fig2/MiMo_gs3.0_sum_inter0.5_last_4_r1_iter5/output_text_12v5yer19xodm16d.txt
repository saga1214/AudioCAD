No caffeine needed here—just some ideas to help you feel good! For drinks, you could try Green Tea, which is full of antioxidants. Another one is Coconut Water, which is great for hydration. You might also like Lemon Water, or just some Herbal Iced Tea, like chamomile or mint. And if you really want a pick-me-up, a little Hot Chocolate works wonders for comfort.

As for snacks, you could have Greek Yogurt, which is high in protein. Or maybe an Apple with some Peanut Butter. Popcorn is also a good choice, or a handful of Almonds and Walnuts for healthy fats. And of course, Dark Chocolate is always a great mood booster. Hope that helps you feel refreshed! 😊
