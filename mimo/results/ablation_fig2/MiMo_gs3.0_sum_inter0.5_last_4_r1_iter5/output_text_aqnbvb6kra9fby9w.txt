Of baby-led feeding is a great approach! Let's focus on making this time safe and engaging for your little one. 

First, and most importantly, ensure your baby is sitting upright in a high chair or on a firm surface with good support. Safety is the top priority.

Next, offer a variety of finger foods that are soft, easy to grasp, and not a choking hazard. Think of things like steamed carrot sticks, small pieces of avocado, soft banana, or even a few whole-grain crackers. 

Now, for the most important part: your attitude! Try to stay calm and patient. If your baby is just playing with the food, that's okay! They're developing crucial motor skills. Avoid rushing them or trying to feed them yourself. This is their time to explore. 

Also, remember to stay close by. While they're exploring, you need to be within an arm's reach so you can jump into action immediately if they start to choke. 

Finally, keep it light and positive. A little mess is totally expected and part of the fun. A big smile and encouragement go a long way. Enjoy this special time with your baby!
