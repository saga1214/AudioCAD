### Switching to Adobe Audition
- **Transition Plan**: Start with free tutorials (Adobe’s official YouTube channel is excellent). Focus on mastering basic editing (cutting, comping) first. Use Audition’s "Compare" tool to A/B test edits against Audacity.
- **Plugins**: Use *Adobe Audition’s built-in Noise Reduction* for harshness. For mouth clicks, try *iZotope RX 10’s De-click* plugin (free trial available). For faster processing, *Adobe’s Enhance Speech* AI tool boosts clarity in minutes.

### AI Tools for Efficiency
- **Trimming Silence**: Descript (free tier) auto-trims pauses. Otter.ai’s "Clean Audio" removes background noise.
- **Labeling**: Use Descript’s "Editor Mode" to tag audio clips by project/client. AI-powered tools like *Sovre* can auto-tag voice files.

### Vocal Health & Workflow Balance
- **Daily Routine**:
