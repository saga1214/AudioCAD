For the brooder plate, you'll want a bedding that's absorbent and won't get damp easily. The best choices are deep-pile wool pellets, which are like a natural alternative to pine shavings, or you can use a thick layer of crumpled up paper towels and newspaper. Just remember to change it out daily. It's best to avoid any straw or shavings for the chicks, since rodents don't really go after those kinds anyway.
