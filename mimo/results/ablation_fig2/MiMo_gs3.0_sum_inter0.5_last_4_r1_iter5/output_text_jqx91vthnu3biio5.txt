Here are a few dinner ideas that balance ease, flavor, and your Mediterranean cravings—without feeling like a fortune-spinning project:

### 🥗 Quick & Fresh:
1. **Hummus Veggie Wraps**  
   *Spread hummus on a tortilla, layer with spinach, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and feta. Roll tightly and slice. Add a drizzle of olive oil and lemon juice.*  
   → *Why?* Uses pantry staples, scratches your Mediterranean itch, and takes <10 minutes.*

2. **One-Pot Lemon-Herb Chicken & Orzo**  
   *Sauté chicken, add orzo, diced tomatoes, olives, garlic, and herbs. Pour in broth and lemon juice. Simmer 15 mins. Finish with fresh parsley.*  
   → *Why?* Minimal cleanup, bright flavors, and protein-packed.*

### 🌶️ Comfort Food with a Twist:
3. **Spiced Chickpea Curry**  
   *Sauté onions, garlic, and cumin seeds. Add canned chickpeas, diced tomatoes, coconut milk, and curry powder. Simmer 10 mins. Serve with naan or rice.*  
   → *Why?* You mentioned craving Mediterranean snacks—this is the upgraded version!*

### 🥗 Light & Effortless:
4. **Grilled Shrimp & Quinoa Salad**  
   *Marinate shrimp in olive oil, lemon, and garlic. Grill or pan-sear. Toss with pre-cooked quinoa, cherry tomatoes, avocado, and a lemon-herb dressing.*  
   → *Why?* Seafood = Mediterranean vibes, quinoa adds protein, and it’s no-cook after prep.*

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **For the lighting dilemma**: If you hate overhead lights, try a **floor lamp with adjustable shade** (like a clamp-style or arch lamp) for soft, task-focused light.  
- **Desk lamp hack**: Use a **smart bulb** (like Philips Hue) to switch between "cozy" (warm) and "focus" (cool) modes as needed.  
- **Dinner hack**: Keep pre-chopped veggies (jarred olives, frozen spinach) and canned beans/chickpeas on hand for ultra-fast meals.
