That pigeon question is actually a perfect way to think about it!

You'd treat each goal like a rooftop. Bolivia salt flats, apartment down-down-payment, and the renovation savings would each be a 'rooftop.'

The first step would be to add up the total cost for each of those 'rooftops.'

Then, you'd figure out how to spread the total cost across the year to create 12 'smaller pigeons'—or, you know, 12 monthly savings goals.

Finally, you'd just assign each 'pigeon' to its 'rooftop' and start making those small payments consistently. That's how you'd make both dreams happen!
