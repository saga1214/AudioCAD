Here are classic recipes formatted for your bartenders at the Art Deco airport reception. I've included the egg white technique for the Whiskey Sour and added garnish notes for visual appeal.

### **Old Fashioned**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon rye (e.g., Buffalo Trace or Bulleit)  
  - 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 3 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (for garnish) cherry (optional)  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Muddle sugar cube (or add simple syrup) in a mixing glass.  
  2. Add bourbon, bitters, and ice. Stir 30 seconds until chilled.  
  3. Strain into a **rocks glass over fresh ice**.  
  4. Express orange twist over drink (squeeze oils into foam), then rest on rim.  
  *Egg white tip: For silky texture, add 0.5 oz egg white to Step 1. Dry-shake (no ice) first, then add ice and stir.*

---

### **Paper Plane**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz bourbon (e.g., Michter's or Four Roses)  
  - 0.75 oz Maraschino liqueur (Luxardo)  
  - 0.25 oz Amaro Nonino  
  - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a **coupe glass** packed with ice.  
  3. Garnish with **orange twist** (optional cherry).

---

### **Whiskey Sour**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz bourbon  
  - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 0.¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 0.5 oz egg white (optional)  
  - Cherry/orange slice (garnish)  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. *Without egg white*: Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a **rocks glass over ice**. Garnish.  
  2. *With egg white*:  
     - Dry-shake (no ice) bourbon, lemon, syrup, and egg white to foam.  
     - Add ice, shake again.  
     - Strain into a **rocks glass over ice**. Express lemon twist oils, then garnish.  
  *Classic variation: Egg white is traditional in pre-Prohibition recipes (e.g., "The Last Word").*

---

### **Aviation**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz gin (London Dry, e.g., Beefeater or Tanqueray)  
  - 0.5 oz maraschino liqueur  
  - 0.25 oz crème de violette  
  - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a **cocktail glass** over ice.  
  3. Garnish with **cherry** and **lemon twist** (rub rim, drop oils into foam).

### **Recommended Additional Gin Cocktail: Paper Flight**  
*(Modern but thematic—evokes planes with citrus and gin)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz gin (e.g., Hendrick's or Bombay Sapphire)  
  - 0.5 oz Aperol  
  - 0.25 oz Amaro Nonino  
  - 0.5 oz fresh grapefruit juice  
  - Orange twist (garnish)  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a **coupe glass** over ice.  
  3. Express orange twist oils, then garnish.  
  *Why it fits: Bright, fall-appropriate, and visually cohesive with your event.*
