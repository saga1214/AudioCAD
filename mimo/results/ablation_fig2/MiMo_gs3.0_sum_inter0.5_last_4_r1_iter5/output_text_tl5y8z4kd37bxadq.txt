### 🐝 Packing Honey Jars Safely
- **Wrap**: Use bubble wrap or packing paper between jars + layer with soft items (t-shirts, towels).
- **Position**: Place jars upright in the suitcase center—never near wheels/edges.
- **Lid Check**: Double-seal lids with tape if needed. Consider reusable silicone lids for extra security.
