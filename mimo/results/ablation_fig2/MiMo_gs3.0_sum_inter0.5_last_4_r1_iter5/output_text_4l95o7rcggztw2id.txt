Here's a detailed nautical-themed shopping list focused on **easy, sand-free finger foods** and drinks for your son's pirate party, with allergy-friendly options and beach-friendly packaging. All items can be cut into small portions and served in themed containers.

---

### **Food (Finger-Friendly & Themed)**
1. **"Buried Treasure" Mini Sliders**  
   - 12 small slider rolls (cheese, ham, or peanut butter & jelly) cut into 6 pieces each  
   - Serve on "ship masts" (short toothpicks or mini cocktail picks)  
   *Theme: Wrap in Jolly Roger flags or serve in mini treasure chests.*

2. **Fruit Skewers "Pearls of the Ocean"**  
   - Skewers with pineapple chunks, grapes (or seedless berries if no watermelon allergy), and melon balls  
   - *Skip watermelon entirely* (due to allergy)  
   *Theme: Dye green grapes or add a tiny goldfish cracker to each skewer.*

3. **"Captain's Goldfish" Crackers**  
   - Goldfish crackers (classic or pirate-themed boxes)  
   - Serve in a "pirate chest" bin with a pirate flag sticker.

4. **"Mermaid Jewels" Fruit Cups**  
   - Cups of pre-cut melon, berries, and pineapple (no watermelon)  
   - Top with a mini pirate hat or ship decal.  
   *Pack in sealed containers to avoid spills.*

5. **Cheese Cubes "Sea Monsters"**  
   - Cubed cheddar or mozzarella (no watermelon allergy risk)  
   - Serve in a "treasure chest" bin with toothpicks.

6. **Pirate Snack Packs**  
   - Individual bags of popcorn ("shipwrecked gold") or crackers ("mermaid pearls").  
   - Label with pirate names like "Captain Blackbeard’s Popcorn."

---

### **Drinks (Spill-Proof & Portable)**
1. **Individual Juice Boxes**  
   - Tropical punch, strawberry, or apple flavors (skip watermelon-based drinks).  
   - Label with pirate names: "Captain’s Juice," "First Mate’s Punch."

2. **Small Water Bottles "Pirate’s Water"**  
   - 6–8 small reusable bottles (20 oz each)  
   - Decorate with Jolly Roger stickers or ship decals.

3. **"Cannonball" Lemonade Pops**  
   - Freeze lemonade pops in pirate-themed molds.  
   *No spill risk—kids grab-and-go!*

---

### **Sand-Free Packaging & Extras**
- **"Treasure Chest" Serving Station**: Use a wooden treasure chest as a serving table.  
- **Themed Labels**: Label all containers with pirate names or nautical terms.  
- **Sand-Free Napkins**: Blue or Jolly Roger-patterned cloth napkins.  
- **Decor**: Mini pirate flags, ship-shaped cookie cutters (for sandwiches), and seashell cups for drinks.

---

### **Allergy Note**  
- **Avoid watermelon** in all food/drink. Use melon-free fruits like pineapple, berries, and grapes.  
- Label dishes clearly to inform other parents (e.g., "Watermelon-Free").

This setup keeps everything **easy for kids to handle at the beach**, minimizes sand contact, and immerses your son (and guests) in pirate fun! 🏴‍☠️⚓
