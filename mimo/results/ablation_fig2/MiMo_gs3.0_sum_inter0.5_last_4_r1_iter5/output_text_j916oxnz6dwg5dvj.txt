### **Key Adjustments & Tips**
1. **Breakfast Omission Warning**:  
   Skipping breakfast risks low blood sugar on 10-mile hikes. If you insist, replace it with a **quick-acting energy source** (e.g., 2 tbsp peanut butter + 1 banana = 300 calories). Otherwise, prioritize frequent small snacks.

2. **Calorie Density**:  
   - **Peanut butter** is your best friend (190 calories per 2 tbsp).  
   - **Cheese** adds 110 calories per slice (fat/protein).  
   - **White bread** is lightweight and easy to eat.  
   - **Fruit** is hydrating but lower in calories—use as a supplement.

3. **Altitude Considerations**:  
   - Add 500+ extra calories for Colorado’s elevation.  
   - Dehydration risk is high—pack electrolyte tabs/powder.

4. **Packaging**:  
   Use reusable containers and silicone bags. Pre-portion snacks into ziplock bags to save weight.

5. **Trail Steepness Impact**:  
   If the path has elevation gain (+500 ft), add 300–500 calories. If flat, stick to this plan.
