Here's a complete treat guide for your corgi princess, Princess, to keep her happy, healthy, and well-trained:

### 🍪 **Homemade Treats** (Always small portions!)
1. **Pumpkin/Sweet Potato Bites**  
   Mashed pumpkin or sweet potato puree (no spices) patted into mini cookie shapes. Freeze for a cool treat.  
2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**  
   Mix PB (xylitol-free!) with rolled oats, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12-15 mins. Break into pea-sized pieces.  
3. **Greek Yogurt Chews**  
   Mix plain Greek yogurt with a dash of blueberry or apple puree. Roll into tiny balls.  
4. **Banana "Ice Cream"**  
   Blend frozen banana slices with a splash of almond milk until creamy. Freeze in a silicone mold.  

### 🛒 **Store-Bought Brands** (Check ingredients!)
- **Homemade-Style:** Zuke’s Mini Naturals, Wellness Mini Training Bites  
- **Specialty Corgi Brands:** West Paw Zippy Trim (grain-free), Benebones Small Breed (durable chew)  
- **Freeze-able:** Kong Puppy Sticks (fill with peanut butter + freeze)  

### 🐶 **Training Tips**  
- **Potty Training:** Take her out every 60-90 mins. Reward *immediately* with a "Yes!" and a tiny treat when she goes.  
- **"Sit" Command:** Use a treat as a lure—raise it over her head until she sits, then say "Sit!" and reward.  
- **Barking at Felix:** Reward calm behavior with a "Quiet!" command + treat. Redirect with a toy *before* she barks.  

### 💖 **Collar Color Ideas**  
For Princess’s brown/white coat:  
- **Teal/Turquoise** (contrasts beautifully)  
- **Royal Blue** (vibrant but elegant)  
- **Soft Pink** (sweet and playful)  
- **Metallic Gold** (adds a regal sparkle!)  

### 📌 **Key Notes**  
- **Portion Control:** Corgis are prone to weight gain—limit treats to 5-10% of daily calories.  
- **Safety First:** Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions, xylitol, and bones (choking risk).  
- **Training Consistency:** Short, positive sessions (5-10 mins) daily work best!
