Here's a clear closing gear packing list split between your daytime 20-mile segment and nighttime final 12 miles pacing, based on your conditions:

### **For Daytime 20 Miles (Mid-30s, Daytime)**
1. **Base Layer**: Moisture-wicking long-sleeve shirt + leggings (avoid cotton).  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece jacket or lightweight puffer vest (adjustable for hilly temperature swings).  
3. **Outer Layer**: Windproof/water-resistant trail jacket (e.g., Gore-Tex).  
4. **Headwear**: Cap or baseball hat (for sun protection if needed).  
5. **Gloves**: Breathable trail running gloves (for grip and warmth).  
6. **Extras**: Lightweight pack or vest (to carry layers/aids), buff/gaiter (optional for dust).  

### **For Nighttime Pacing (Final 12 Miles)**  
1. **Base Layer**: Moisture-wicking shirt + leggings (same as daytime).  
2. **Mid-Layer**: Fleece or lightweight insulating layer (e.g., down vest).  
3. **Outer Layer**: **Windproof/water-resistant jacket with hood** (critical for hilly/cool temps).  
4. **Headwear**: **Warm hat or beanie** (your extremities will get cold fast).  
5. Handheld light or headlamp (with **extra batteries**).  
6. Gloves (same as daytime).  
7. Buff/gaiter (wrap around neck to retain heat).  
8. **Extras**: Insulated trail shoes (if yours aren’t already warm enough), hydration vest/belt.  

### **Key Notes**  
- **Aid Stations**: Use them to swap layers if needed—add/remove mid-layers/midway.  
- **Visibility**: Three stations mean you can grab gloves/hat if cold, but always carry extras.  
- **Footwear**: Hilly terrain demands grippy shoes in both segments.
