Using a dehydrator is a great way to preserve fruits and vegetables. Some of the best things to dehydrate are apples, bananas, mangoes, and strawberries. You can use them to make fruit leather or fruit snacks. You can also dehydrate vegetables like onions and carrots. You can use those for making vegetable chips or stews. Just make sure to cut everything into uniform slices so that it dehydrates evenly.
