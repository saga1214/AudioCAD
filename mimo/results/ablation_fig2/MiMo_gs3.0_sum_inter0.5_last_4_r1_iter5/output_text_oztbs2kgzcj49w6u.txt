Yes, there are a few excellent ways to learn more. The most direct way is to visit the Lockport Museum and the Erie Canal Museum, which are located together in the historic Old Erie Canal Village. You can see historical artifacts and learn all about the canal's impact on the region.

Another great option is to take a guided boat tour on the Lockport's own canal boat, which is a very immersive experience. Or, if you prefer to read up beforehand, you can find many excellent books and articles on the history of the Erie Canal and Lockport's specific role in it.
