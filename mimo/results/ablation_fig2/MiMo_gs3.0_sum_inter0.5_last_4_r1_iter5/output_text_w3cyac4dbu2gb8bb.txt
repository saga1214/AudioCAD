Here's a practical, step-by-step approach to solve your coffee crisis *right now*, even with the rain:

1.  **Grab Full-Fat Milk:** Go to the fridge and grab your full-fat milk (it creates a richer, creamier latte). If you *really* want Cornish milk and it's readily available, grab that too, but full-fat is the best budget-friendly choice for a strong latte.
2.  **Make Instant Coffee Strong:** Skip making machine coffee *yet*. Pour **2-3 tablespoons of instant coffee granules** into a small mug or cup.
3.  **Dissolve Instant Coffee:** Add **1 cup of very hot water** (just boiled or from the kettle). Stir *vigorously* until the instant coffee is completely dissolved and strong. This step prevents clumping.
4.  **Warm Milk:** Pour your full-fat milk (or Cornish milk) into a smaller cup or bowl. Place it in the microwave. Heat for **45-60 seconds**. Stir and check temperature – it should be steaming hot but not boiling.
5.  **Combine:** Pour the dissolved, strong instant coffee into the warm milk. Stir well.
6.  **Optional Creaminess:** If you want extra creaminess (like a proper latte), stir in a splash of heavy cream or a spoonful of milk cream *after* combining the coffee and milk.
7.  **Enjoy Immediately!** Drink it straight away while it's hot and strong. The warmth will be comforting.

**For the Rain & Wind:**

*   **Raincoat is Best:** As you suspected, a good raincoat with a hood is the most reliable choice for heavy rain and wind. It keeps you completely dry and is easier to manage than an umbrella.
