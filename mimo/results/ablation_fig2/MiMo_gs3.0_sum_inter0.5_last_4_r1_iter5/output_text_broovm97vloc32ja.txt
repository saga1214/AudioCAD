That's a very common scenario! In the world of children's books, it's actually very common for an author to also be an illustrator, or for a single artist to write and illustrate the book themselves. So yes, you can absolutely submit a complete package.

However, there's a very important distinction. When you submit a manuscript to a publisher or an agent, you are legally required to state that you do not own the illustrations. The publisher would then hire a new illustrator, or you could offer to illustrate it yourself. You can't submit the artwork as your own property unless you have secured the rights to do so first, which can be a complex process. 

So, your best bet is to submit the text manuscript and the illustrations separately. This gives them the visual context they need while protecting your rights.
