Of course! With a two-bed apartment for you, a baby on the way, furniture advice, immigration tips, a car recommendation, a Golden Retriever, and Vancouver's best spots covered, here are five fantastic road trip ideas for you and your wife to explore the beautiful BC coast.

1.  **Tofino & the Pacific Rim National Park Reserve (on Vancouver Island):** This is the ultimate coastal experience. You'll drive along the scenic Highway 4 and stop at amazing spots like Uplands Beach and Long Beach. You can hike the dramatic Broken Group Islands, kayak through the turquoise waters of the Clayoquot Sound, or simply relax on the stunning black sand beaches.

2.  **The Sunshine Coast (on Vancouver Island):** This area is famous for its luxury and charm. Drive the scenic Spud Highway to check out the quirky town of Steveston Village. You can enjoy fresh seafood at a waterfront restaurant, take a scenic cruise on the water, or visit the famous Capilano Suspension Bridge. It's perfect for a more relaxed, upscale vacation.

3.  **Whistler (in the Coast Mountains):** For a classic mountain getaway, head to Whistler. You can go skiing or snowboarding in the winter, or hike in the stunning summer trails. The drive itself is gorgeous, passing through Lillooet. When you're not outdoors, you can enjoy the luxury resorts, take a gondola ride, or find a great restaurant with mountain views.

4.  Golden Triangle & Jasper National Park (in the Rocky Mountains): This trip takes a few more hours to get to but is absolutely worth it. You can take a scenic drive through the Cariboo region and then explore the vast Jasper National Park. You can hike among turquoise lakes like Maligne Lake, visit the iconic Columbia Icefield, and maybe even see some wildlife like bears and elk.

5.  **Victoria & the Gulf Islands (on Vancouver Island):** This is a short and easy day trip from Vancouver, just a couple of hours away. You can take a ferry to the beautiful Victoria waterfront, visit the famous Butchart Gardens, and explore the charming inner harbour. For a bit more adventure, you can take a ferry over to the Gulf Islands for a peaceful hike or to visit the picturesque town of Salt Spring Island.

I hope you have an amazing time exploring!
