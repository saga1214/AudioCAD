Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scenes, but for watercolors, you should really focus on the colors. Late September to November is their peak fall season—the leaves turn vibrant shades of orange, red, and yellow. That's going to give you the most incredible palette to work with. I'd definitely prioritize coastal areas like Tulum or Oaxaca, where you can see the fall foliage next to the ocean. That contrast is just... perfect. 

For watercolor techniques, I'd say take advantage of wet-on-wet for the skies and large landscapes, and layering for details like trees or buildings. And hey, if you want to play with those markers, Oaxaca's vibrant street art scene is a great place to practice blending markers with watercolors. Just remember to pack light and focus on small studies first—you know, practice those wet-on-wet and splattering techniques before you try to tackle a whole ocean scene. Mexico's fall is a perfect place to really develop your style.
