Alright, so before you film your own puppet show, here’s a quick checklist to make sure it goes smoothly and looks professional.

First, check with the facility—you know, like Assisted Living—if you’re allowed to film and that it won’t disturb anyone. Second, plan the setup. Like, film from a spot where the audience can be seen clearly. Maybe use a tripod if you want steady shots. Third, test your phone or camera beforehand to make sure the sound and lighting are good. Fourth, keep the show short, like five to ten minutes, to hold their attention. Fifth, after filming, ask someone in the audience if they’re okay with being in the video. And sixth, edit the best parts—like cut out any mistakes—before you upload. And finally, credit yourself as the creator, so people know where it came from.

So, in short: get permission, set up well, test tech, keep it short, ask permission after, and credit yourself. You got this!
