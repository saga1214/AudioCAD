You already mentioned SkinnyPop and PopChips, which is a great gluten-free foundation. Along those lines, rice cakes with gluten-free nut butter are always a crowd-pleaser camp snack.

Since camping weather can vary, dried fruit like cranberries, apricots, and mango pieces are also gluten-free and packed with natural energy.

And don't forget trail mix! You can find gluten-free versions or even make your own by mixing nuts, seeds, and gluten-free dried fruit.
