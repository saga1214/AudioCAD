- **Neuroscience reality**: The brain works as an integrated network. While certain functions (e.g., language processing in left hemisphere) show hemispheric specialization, creativity/logic aren't neatly separated.

  - *Right-brain* traits: Holistic pattern-finding, spatial visualization, conceptual leaps (matches your creative side).  
- **Key nuance**: You don't need to choose one side. Fields requiring **integrative thinking** (e.g., design, systems engineering) leverage both.

### 4. Tinnitus Explanation  
**Tinnitus is primarily physiological**:  
- **Causes**: Exposure to loud noise (85+ dB), aging, otosclerosis, or Ménière's disease.  
- **Psychological link**: Stress can *amplify* tinnitus perception but rarely causes it.  
- **Action**: Audiologist evaluation to rule out hearing loss; sound therapy (white noise) may help.
