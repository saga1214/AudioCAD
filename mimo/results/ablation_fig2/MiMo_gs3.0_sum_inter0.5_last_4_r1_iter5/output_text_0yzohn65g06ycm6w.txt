### Feeding Solutions  
- **Separate Access**: Microchip-activated feeders or timed dispensers control portions (greedy ones can't overeat; fussy ones eat).  
- **Room Isolation**: Feed fussy cats in a separate room before leaving.  

### Cost-Saving Strategies  
- **Seek Aid**: Ask local shelters, charities, or food banks for pet supplies.  
- **Bulk Buying**: Purchase larger bags of quality food for lower per-serving costs.  
- **Supplement Wisely**: Mix small amounts of quality food with cheap fillers (e.g., plain cooked rice).  
- **Community Support**: Join local groups for shared surplus supplies.  

### Health Maintenance on a Budget  
- **Prevent Issues**: Maintain clean environments, bowls, and water.  
- **Affordable Nutrition**: Supplement with safe, inexpensive ingredients (e.g., plain cooked meat/fish). Avoid toxic foods (onions, garlic, salt).  
- **Preventive Play**: Use homemade toys (string, crumpled paper) to manage weight and reduce health risks.  
- **Monitor Early**: Watch for symptoms of illness to address at home before costs rise.
