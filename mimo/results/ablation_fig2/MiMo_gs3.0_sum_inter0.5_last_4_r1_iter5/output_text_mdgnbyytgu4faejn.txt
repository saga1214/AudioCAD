Okay, this is your complete blueprint. Let's build it step by step.

First is your Header. Center your bold 14-point name right-aligned is a classic choice. To its right, starting a new line for better readability, list your email in a 9-point font, then your phone number, and finally your city in another 9-point font. Icons avoid ATS issues best left out. LinkedIn would be your ideal addition here.

Next, a short Professional Summary of two to three sentences highlighting your most relevant skills and career goals. This immediately frames your context.

Then, create a "Relevant Experience" section directly below the summary. Here, list your most pertinent jobs first, including dates. Use concise bullet points focusing on achievements with numbers where possible. This is your showcase for the career shift.

After that, an "Additional Work History" section. For less relevant jobs, just list the job title, company, and dates. No bullet points needed to keep it brief.

Your Education section comes next. List each degree you earned, including the school, your expected graduation date in parentheses, and any honors or awards you received for that degree.

Then, a dedicated Skills section. List hard skills specific to the job descriptions you're targeting. It's also helpful to categorize them, for example, by technical skills, languages, or soft skills.

If you have significant volunteer work, certifications, or publications, you can add a Professional Development section.

Finally, your Additional Information can include your LinkedIn URL, a link to your portfolio if applicable, or your personal website.

This structure strategically presents your experience while managing the career transition narrative. You can now populate this framework efficiently. Would you like to start drafting the Professional Summary together?
