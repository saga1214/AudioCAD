Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots. For ramen, I'd suggest Torikizoku—it's super authentic and has great tonkotsu. For a more casual vibe, there's Yummy's Asian Noodle Bar, which has really flavorful dishes. If you want pho, Pho 79 is fantastic. For protein bars, I'd recommend Quest, which are high in protein but low in sugar, or RXBAR, which uses real ingredients. There's also GoMacro for healthier options. Hope you enjoy your meal!
