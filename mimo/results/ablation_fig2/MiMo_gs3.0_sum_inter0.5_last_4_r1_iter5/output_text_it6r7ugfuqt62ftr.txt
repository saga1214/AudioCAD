Post-birthday warm getaway for 4-5 days, family of three (dietary needs), budget no limits. Palm Springs is ideal! Here’s a luxury itinerary:

Day 1: Arrival & Oasis. Fly into Palm Springs International (PSP). Check into a luxury resort like Amanusa or The Parker. Relax in the pool, swim, and grab a casual dinner at the resort.

Day 2: Nature & Luxury. Morning hike at Palm Canyon Trail for stunning views. Afternoon spa treatment at the resort. Evening, a gourmet dinner at the resort's restaurant, and a special Celiac-friendly dessert at Trifika.

Day 3: Desert Adventures. Full-day guided tour to Pioneertown and the Ocotillo Winery. Return to the resort for a poolside picnic and sunset drinks.

Day 4: Luxury & Leisure. Morning at the resort spa. Afternoon, rent an electric scooter to explore downtown Palm Springs, including the Mission Inn and the galleries. Enjoy an upscale dinner at The Mission.

Day 5: Departure. Leisurely brunch, maybe a final pool swim, then fly home.

Key things to remember: Fly into PSP, book the winery tour ahead, and confirm all restaurant reservations for Celiac needs. Palm Springs guarantees warm weather, pools, swimming, and plenty of upscale dining options. It's perfect for escaping Northern California's cold!
