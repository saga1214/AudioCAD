That's a great question! Since you're drawn to detailed, hands-on work and vintage restoration, meaningful gift ideas could include:

**For Tailoring:**
- **High-Quality Fabric Shears**: A sharp pair (like Gingher or Kai) makes a huge difference.  
- **Vintage Parker 51-Themed Accessory**: Maybe a pen case, ink bottle, or even a custom sewing machine needle holder.  
- **Premium Fabric**: A bolts of high-quality cotton or linen for practice projects.  
- **Sewing Machine Upgrade**: If you get serious, consider a better machine (e.g., a Janome or Bernina).  

**For Woodworking (if you pursue it):**  
- **Basic Hand Tools**: A sharp chisel set, a bench vise, or a quality mallet.  
- **Workbench Add-on**: A tool organizer or clamps.  
- **Starter Project Kit**: Pre-cut wood for a small shelf or cutting board.  

**For Clay/Pottery:**  
- **Ceramic Wheel Accessory**: A rib for shaping, or a basic potter’s wheel.  
- **Clay or Glazes**: High-quality clay sets or specialty glazes.  
- **Molds/Tools**: Silicone molds for beginners.  

**General Hobby Ideas:**  
- **Consumables**: Fountain pen ink, thread, or sandpaper (for woodworking).  
- **Skill-Building Books**: *The Tailor’s Art* by Peter McKenna or woodworking guides by Paul Sellers.  
- **Customized Gear**: Engraved measuring tape or a tailored apron.  

**Tip:** Since your family knows your hobbies, they might enjoy helping you choose! A small, well-crafted tool (like shears or a chisel) often feels more special than a generic gift.
