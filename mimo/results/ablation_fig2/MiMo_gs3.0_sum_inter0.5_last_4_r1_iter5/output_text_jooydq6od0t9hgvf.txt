### 🍸 Vodka Mixer (Elegant & Refreshing)
**Name:** **Sparkling Citrus Vodka**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup (or 1 tsp honey)  
- Top with **sparkling water** (alcohol allergy-safe!)  
- Garnish: Lime twist  

**Why it works:** Light, fizzy, and universally appealing. No sweet overload—works for all ages/diet preferences.  

### 🇦🇷 Fernet-Branca Cocktail (Argentine-Style Twist)
**Name:** **Fernet & Cola-Style** (but *better*—no cola!)  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- Top with **ginger beer** (instead of soda—spicy kick!)  
- Garnish: Lemon wheel + Angostura bitters splash  

**Why it works:** Fernet’s herbal bitterness pairs perfectly with citrus and spice. Ginger beer adds zing without soda. Keeps it sophisticated but fun.
