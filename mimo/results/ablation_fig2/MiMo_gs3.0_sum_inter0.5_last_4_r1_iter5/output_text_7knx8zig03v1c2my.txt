### Clicker Training Explained (Step-by-Step):
Clicker training is a highly effective, positive reinforcement method where the clicker acts as a "bridge" or "marker" to communicate *exactly* when your puppy has done something right.

**How it Works:**

1.  **Clicker Sound:** It makes a loud, sharp, consistent "click" sound. This is your signal that the puppy did the *exact* thing you wanted *at that exact moment*.
2.  **Immediate Reward:** The *instant* you click, you give the puppy a high-value treat. The click + treat happens together within 1-2 seconds.
3.  **The Connection:** The puppy learns that the click sound *means* "You did something I wanted, and you're getting a treat!" The click marks the precise moment of success.

**How to Start with "Sit":**

1.  **Hold the Clicker:** Keep it in your hand, ready to click.
2.  **Position:** Stand in front of your puppy. Hold a treat in your closed fist near his nose. Slowly raise your arm straight over his head.
3.  **The Action:** As his nose follows the treat up, his bottom will naturally lower. The *instant* his bottom touches the floor, **CLICK**.
4.  **Reward:** Immediately deliver the treat.
5.  **Repeat:** Do this 10-15 times in a session. Click *only* when his bottom touches the floor. If he jumps up, reset. If he looks at you, reset. The click must happen *only* when he sits.
6.  **Add the Cue:** Once he reliably sits on command (without the clicker), start saying "Sit" *just before* you raise the treat. Then, as soon as he sits, **CLICK** and **TREAT**. Gradually phase out saying "Sit" before the clicker mark, relying just on the clicker for a few more sessions, then finally just the clicker and treat. Eventually, you can use just the word "Sit" and the treat.

**Key Principles of Clicker Training:**

*   **Consistency is Crucial:** Always click for the *exact* behavior you want, the *exact* moment it happens.
*   **Clicker is Not Punishment:** It only marks success. It doesn't scold.
*   **Treats are the Reward:** The clicker itself isn't food; it's a signal that food is coming.
*   **Practice Short & Fun:** Start with 2-5 minute sessions. End on a success. Keep it positive!
*   **Clicker is Optional:** Once the behavior is solid, you can phase out the clicker and use just the verbal cue ("Sit") and the treat.

Clicker training builds incredible communication and trust between you and Galileo. Have fun with it!
