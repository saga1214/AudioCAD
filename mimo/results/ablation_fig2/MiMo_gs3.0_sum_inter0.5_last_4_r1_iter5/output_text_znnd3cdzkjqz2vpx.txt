Okay, you've got all the essentials! Just a few more things to think about.

Make sure you have a really good pair of clamps for holding adjacent cabinets flush. Also, you'll definitely need a stud finder that can detect metal, since Habitat Restored items often come off appliances or fixtures.

For tools, a power drill with a couple of different screwdriver bits is a must. A level is crucial, so make sure it's a good one. Shims are also important for getting everything level underneath the base cabinets.

Safety is key, so please wear safety glasses and work gloves. And it's a good idea to have a trash bag handy for all the old paint and debris from the restore-purchased cabinets. 

You're all set for when Sarah arrives! Just take your time, work together, and celebrate with some Zaxby's or Popeyes after you get those beautiful new cabinets up.
