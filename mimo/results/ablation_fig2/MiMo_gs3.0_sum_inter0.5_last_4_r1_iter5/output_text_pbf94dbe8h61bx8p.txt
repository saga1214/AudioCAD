Okay, you want simple, meaningful gifts that the whole family can get into, tailored for different ages. Macramé still sounds like your passion, so plant hangers are great, maybe expand a little into wall hangings or ornaments. Cookies could be drop cookies—no rolling, just scoop and bake. But for a big family with kids of all ages, you want a project that everyone can help with without it being a huge struggle.

One idea is to have stations set up. For macramé, the little ones could decorate, while the older ones help with the hangers. For cookies, break it down: one station for mixing, another for scooping, and a third for decorating. That way, everyone has a job they can handle. To get the kids excited, you could make it a fun challenge or play holiday music in the background.

Another thought is salt dough ornaments. The kids can shape them, bake them, and paint them. It's hands-on but no hot glue involved, which means less supervision. Or, like you mentioned, pine cone bird feeders. They're simple—just roll pine cones in peanut butter and birdseed. It's fun, not messy, and feels meaningful. The peanut butter would be fine outside; it wouldn't go bad.

The key is to find activities where each kid has a specific role they can take pride in. That way, you're crafting together, and everyone feels included. Does one of these ideas sound like it could work for Christmas?
