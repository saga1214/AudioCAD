That's a fantastic plan! Transitioning from desert adventure to Vegas luxury is a classic van-camping move. Here's how to make the **"Van-to-Vegas" Adventure Seamless and Special**:

### 🌴 Phase 1: The Adventure (e.g., Joshua Tree/BLM Land)
- **Drive Time:** ~3 hours from JT to Vegas (stop in Mojave or Barstow for lunch/snacks).
- **BLM Camping Cost:** **$0** (free dispersed camping—just follow Leave No Trace principles).
- **Vegas Logistics:**  
  - **Pre-book parking** if staying downtown (e.g., at your hotel).  
  - **Park the van at a secure lot** (like "Park 'N Fly" near the Strip) if you want to sleep in a hotel.  
  - **Pro Tip:** Store gear in the van for easy access later!

### 🏨 Phase 2: Vegas Luxury (On a Budget!)
Vegas doesn’t *have* to be expensive—focus on **atmosphere and intimacy**:
- **Hotels:**  
  - **Luxury Pool Access:** Bellagio, Wynn, or Paris (book a room near the pool for day access).  
  - **Unique Stays:** Aria (modern), Cosmopolitan (trendy), or The Cosmopolitan (rooftop bar).  
  - **Budget-Friendly:** The LINQ (free pool), Sahara (retro vibe), or *non-gambling* hotels like The Cosmopolitan’s **Hotel** wing (quieter, no casino).  
- **Pampering:**  
  - **Spa Treatments:** Most hotels offer couples massages (~$100-$150).  
  - **Dine:** Book a romantic dinner at **Eataly** (Wynn), **Catch** (Aria), or **Perch** (Cosmo).  
- **Free/Unique Experiences:**  
  - **Free Pool Parties:** Day clubs like **Drai’s Beach** or **Omnia Beach** (free entry 11-3 PM).  
  - **Hidden Gems:**  
    - **The Mob Museum** (quirky history).  
    - **Mirage Volcano Show** (free at night).  
    - **Mirage Skydiving Simulator** (fun thrill).  
  - **Late-Night Vibes:** **Eataly** bar, **Cirrus** cocktail lounge (Lago), or **Secret Pizza** (free delivery after 11 PM).

### 💡 Key Tips for Success
1. **Timing:**  
   - Arrive in Vegas early (before 5 PM) to avoid crowds.  
   - Leave the desert by late afternoon to drive safely.  
2. **Budget:**  
   - Use **AAA** or **AAA Choice Hotels** for discounted stays.  
   - Split a suite for extra space (e.g., Wynn/Paragon suites).  
3. **Intimacy:**  
   - Skip the casino—focus on shared experiences.  
   - Bring a **portable speaker** for music in the hotel room.  
4. **Safety:**  
   - Keep valuables in the van (locked!) and never leave it unattended.  
   - Use rideshares/taxis after dark.

### 🗺️ Sample Itinerary
- **Day 1:** Drive to Joshua Tree → Explore Hidden Valley/Highway trails → Camp under stars.  
- **Day 2:** Sunrise hike → Drive to Vegas → Check into hotel → Pool day → Romantic dinner.  
- **Day 3:** Free pool party → Museum/Show → Sunset drinks → Spa night.

This blend of rugged adventure and indulgent relaxation will make your new relationship unforgettable. Safe travels! 🚐✨
