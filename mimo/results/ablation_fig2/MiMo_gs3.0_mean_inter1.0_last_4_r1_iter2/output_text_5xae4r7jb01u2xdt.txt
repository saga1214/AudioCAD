Got it. Here’s a scenario for you. The Red team is attacking the Blue team's goal. As the Red team is about to shoot on goal, the Blue defender bumps into them, causing the ball to accidentally roll to a nearby Red attacker. The Red attacker receives the ball on goal and scores.

Now, here's the rule that's at play. This accidental deflection reset the situation. It's no longer considered an offside position because the defender made what the rules define as a 'deliberate controlled play on the ball'.

This is the key difference. Some other soccer rulebooks might define this differently. For example, in youth soccer, they often use a simpler rule where any touch by the defender resets offside, whether it's deliberate or a rebound. College follows the more specific Law 11 of the Laws of the Game, which requires the touch to be deliberate and controlled. This makes the distinction very important for certification.
