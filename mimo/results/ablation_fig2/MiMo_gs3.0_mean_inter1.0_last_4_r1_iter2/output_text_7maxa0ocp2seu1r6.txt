### **I. Core Body Measurements (take for EVERY fitting)**

### **II. Essential Additional Measurements (depending on garment type)**
*   **For Tops, Shirts, Jackets:**
    *   Neck (for collars)
    *   Shoulder Width (critical for great fit)
    *   Upper Back (across shoulders - SB)
    *   Bicep (for sleeve fit)
    *   Sleeve Length (SLL)
*   **For Fitted Pants:**
    *   Thigh (critical for comfort)
    *   *Your Tall Client Case:* **Back Rise** (front rise + distance from crotch to waistband is key for comfort)
*   **Front Rise** (especially important for your tall client's comfort)
*   **SAG** (fabric drape measurement - adds ease)

### **III. Specific to Tall Clients / Long Torsos (like your neighbor)**
*   **Back Rise** (Measure carefully across the buttocks to the waistband. This is crucial for preventing pants from pulling down and ensuring comfort when sitting.)
*   **Thigh** (Essential for tailored fits on long legs.)

### **Key Reminders for Your Business**
1.  **Inseam is King for Pants:** Always measure the actual inseam from the crotch seam to the ankle bone. Use this measurement, *not* just the front rise measurement, to determine pant length. Have clients try on with the *actual shoes* they'll wear for the perfect final length.
2.  **Comfort = Ease:** For sitting comfort (like your tall client), add extra ease (fabric fullness) in the **Hips** and **Thigh** measurements. Don't be afraid to make these areas roomier.
3.  **Fit is Personal:** Adjust ease levels based on the client's preference for snug vs. loose fits. Your tall client likely needs more ease in hips/thigh than average.
