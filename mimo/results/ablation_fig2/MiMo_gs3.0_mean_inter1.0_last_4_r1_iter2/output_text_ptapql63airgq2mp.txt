### **SIMPLE FUEL**  
1. **Umami Popcorn Bar**: Offer 4 stations—butter, *spicy gochujang*, *umami cheese* (with bacon bits), and *wasabi-kimchi* (for heat and tang).  
2. **Gyoza Trio**: Quick-to-eat pork, shrimp, and *mushroom-ginger* gyoza with a spicy sesame dipping sauce.  
3. **Miso-Caramel Drizzle Pretzels**: Sweet-savory simple bites for easy snacking.  

### **UNIQUE秀TASTING JOURNEY**  
4. **Japanese Hot Spring (Onsen) Station**:  
   - *Dried squid jerky* (umami chew)  
   - *Fish jerky* (salty/savory)  
   - *Sweet potato chips* (crunchy-sweet)  
   *Pair with wasabi peas and yuzu-marinated olives.*  

5. **Takoyaki DIY Station** (fun & interactive):  
   - Mini octopus balls (savory, crispy outside, molten inside)  
   - Toppings: *Tangy tamarind sauce*, bonito flakes, *wasabi mayo*, and pickled ginger.  

6. **Sweet-Sour Umami Trio**:  
   - *Yuzu pickled mango* (bright citrus tang)  
   - *Sweet-spicy tamarind candy* (like Japan trip memories!)  
   - *Black sesame mochi* (nutty, chewy, with a molten center).  

7. **Umami Soup Cups**:  
   - Warm miso broth cups with *dashi seaweed* and *dried scallops* for a cozy, savory finish.

### **PRO TIPS**  
- **Label creatively**: Use small signs like "Onsen Crunch," "Takoyaki Lab," or "Umami Alley" to make it feel like an adventure.  
- **Drinks**: Serve *ramune* (Japanese soda) with boba, *hojicha tea* (roasted green tea), and *yuzu-infused sparkling water*.  
- **Ambiance**: Play traditional Japanese music softly in the background.
