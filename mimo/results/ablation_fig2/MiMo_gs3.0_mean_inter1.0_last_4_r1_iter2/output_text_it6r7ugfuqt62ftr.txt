Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimmers dieting for suit weather! Northern SoCal jumps to mind immediately - consistently sunny even in December/January. Since Northern Cal gets chilly fast:

 packing essentials:
 bathing suits x2 different styles
 cover-ups (one sarong style and one simple)
 sundresses, tank tops, shorts
 sandals/flip-flops/swimsuits
 sunglasses, hat, VERY HIGH SPF gluten-free sunscreen
 light sweater/jacket (even in SoCal evenings)
 reusable water bottle

 outdoor adventures competing with a hot air balloon:
 whale watching tours (magical experience!)
 desert adventures near Palm Springs
 beach day at Laguna Beach or Coronado
 horseback riding on PCH
 cactus garden tours (Joshua Tree is winter-friendlier)

 celebratory Celiac-friendly dining:
 bakery: The Flourless Artisan in Long Beach (dedicated GF)
 dinner: Fog Harbor Fish House after Alcatraz cruise
 dessert: Trish's Mini Donuts at Fisherman's Wharf

 birthday cruise alternative confirmed: Alcatraz City Cruises is perfect! Book the dinner cruise for maximum impact.

 birthday post-planning:
 Alcatraz dinner cruise (book immediately!)
 birthday dinner: Fog Harbor Fish House (confirm GF protocols)
 celebratory dessert: Trish's Mini Donuts
 birthday cake: Trifecta Bakery in Long Beach (pre-order GF cake)

 packing for SoCal trip with minivan:
 sleeping arrangements: Airbnb in Long Beach - larger space for family
 packing strategy: lay everything out - sundresses, swimsuits folded together, shorts tank tops together, sweaters folded separately. Roll swimsuits to save space. Use packing cubes for suits and daughter's clothes.

 transportation: Minivan essential for luggage. Plan driving times:
 Day 1: SF to Long Beach (2.5-3hrs) - stop at SFO for airport food
 Day 2: Long Beach - Alcatraz cruise, downtown exploration, dinner
 Day 3: Desert day trip (Joshua Tree - cacti instead of balloons!)
 Day 4: Laguna Beach - whale watching tour OR beach day
 Day 5: Return to Long Beach airport (1.5hrs)

 budget-friendly splurges:
 breakfasts at beach cafes
 sunset drinks at rooftop bars
 dessert at Trish's

 Have incredible warm weather escape! Remember to double-check all restaurant GF policies when reserving.
