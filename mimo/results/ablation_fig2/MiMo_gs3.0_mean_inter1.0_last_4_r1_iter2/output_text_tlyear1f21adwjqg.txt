Hydropower can be the most efficient single source in specific areas, especially where there's existing infrastructure like dams. It's highly reliable and efficient, especially for large-scale, constant power needs. Solar energy is the most efficient overall globally because it's so scalable, getting cheaper, and works almost everywhere. Wind energy is also efficient in windy areas but is a bit more limited. So, if I *had* to pick just one... I'd still stick with solar. But hydropower would be my close second, especially in places with the right conditions.
