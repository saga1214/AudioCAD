Okay, let's break this down.

First, about the left brain versus right brain idea. That's a common myth, but it's mostly oversimplified. While the two hemispheres do have specialized functions—like the left for language and logic and the right for spatial and creative tasks—they work together constantly. There’s no “creative” or “logical” person; it’s more about how different people balance those functions. Some people might lean one way more than the other, but everyone uses both.

Now, for careers that blend structure, creativity, and measurable outcomes, here are a few ideas. 

First, there's data visualization. You get to turn complex data into beautiful, clear graphics, which combines logic with art. 

Second, consider architecture or industrial design. You're structuring spaces or products that are both functional and aesthetically pleasing. 

Third, you could look into UX/UI design. It's about creating user-friendly digital products, which is both logical and creative. 

And finally, technical writing. You're structuring information in a clear, logical way while also making it engaging. 

These fields all involve building something lasting, whether it’s a physical structure or a digital interface, while also giving you creative freedom. 

Hope that helps! Keep exploring.
