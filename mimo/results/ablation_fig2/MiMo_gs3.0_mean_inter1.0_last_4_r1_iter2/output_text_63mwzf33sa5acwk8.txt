That's the perfect combo: desert adventure followed by a luxurious Vegas reset! Here’s how to make your Nevada van adventure seamless and actually "proper" Vegas afterward:

### 🌵 **The Adventure Route (West Coast → Nevada)**
1. **Start with Joshua Tree (CA)**  
   - **Drive**: ~3 hours from SoCal.  
   - **Why Unique?**: Moon-like landscapes, hidden arches, Cholla Cactus Garden, and stargazing (one of the darkest skies in North America).  
   - **Camping**:  
     - **Inside the Park**: Jumbo Rocks or Hidden Valley campsites ($20/night, book *now* – fills fast).  
     - **BLM Land (Free)**: Near the border (e.g., near Cottonwood Spring). Pull up on a scenic spot, set up camp, and roam freely. *Pro Tip*: Download offline maps (service can be spotty).  

2. **Drive to Oregon Coast or Sedona (if Joshua Tree feels too familiar)**  
   - **Oregon Coast** (3-4 hours from CA): Wild sea stacks, tiny towns like Yachats, and tide pooling. Camp at **Nehalem Bay State Park** ($30/night).  
   - **Sedona** (5-6 hours from CA): Hike Red Rock trails ( Cathedral Rock, Devil’s Bridge), then drive south to **NV**.  

### 🏜️ **Nevada Adventure Stop (Before Vegas)**  
Head to **Valley of Fire State Park** (NV, ~2.5 hours from Joshua Tree):  
- **Why?**: More red rocks than Sedona, ancient petroglyphs, and surreal formations like Fire Wave.  
- **Camping**: **Valley of Fire Campground** ($20/night) or **BLM land** near the entrance.  
- **Adventure**: Hike **Cave View Trail** for panoramic vistas.  

### 🏙️ **The "Proper" Vegas Experience (Post-Adventure Reward)**  
After camping, drive **3 hours** to Vegas:  
- **Hot Shower + Luxury Hotel**: Skip budget motels. Treat her to a **Bellagio** (fountains), **The Venetian** (canals), or **Aria** (modern) for:  
  - **Hot Showers**: Every hotel has private baths.  
  - **Pools**: Daybeds with cabanas ($$$ but worth it).  
  - **Spa**: Book a couples massage ($150-$250) – pure van-life luxury.  
- **Beyond Gambling**:  
  - **Food**: Michelin-starred spots like **Joël Robuchon** or **Nobu**.  
  - **Shows**: Cirque du Soleil (O), residencies (U2, Bruno Mars), or immersive experiences like *Meow Wolf*.  
  - **Quiet Vibes**: **The Cosmopolitan’s** rooftop bar, **Lucky’s** jazz lounge, or **Aromi** for Italian.  

### 💡 **Key Tips**  
- **BLM Etiquette**: Stay on trails, pack out *all* trash, and avoid private property.  
- **Vegas Budget**: Allocate $150-$200/night for hotels + meals. Skip cheap casinos – focus on food/shows.  
- **Van Life Hack**: If your van has a shower, fill a bucket with hot water for a quick rinse before entering a hotel.  

This blend of raw nature, freedom (BLM), and urban indulgence keeps it adventurous *and* romantic – perfect for a relatively new relationship! 🚐✨
