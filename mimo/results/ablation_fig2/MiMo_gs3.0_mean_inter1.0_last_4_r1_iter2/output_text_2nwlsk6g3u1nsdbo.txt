    *   *(Classic spicy Italian dish)*

    *   **Ingredients:** Cooked large shrimp (peeled & deveined), quick-cooking grits (about 1.5 cups), 1 cup chicken or vegetable broth, 1/4 cup heavy cream, 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese, 2-3 tbsp Cajun seasoning (adjust for heat), salt & pepper to taste, butter (optional, for sautéing).
