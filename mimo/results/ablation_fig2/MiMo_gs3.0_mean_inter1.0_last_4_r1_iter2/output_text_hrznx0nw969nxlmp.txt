That's an excellent choice! Secret Harbour Beach Resort on St. Thomas sits in Reef Bay, offering that peaceful, calm bay right off the shore—perfect for pressure-free pressure changes. Here are some fantastic daytime activities near there and around Red Hook/Frenchtown:

### On St. Thomas:
1. **Red Hook Water Sports & Rentals**:  
   Rent kayaks, paddleboards, or even a small boat to explore Charlotte Amalie’s harbor or the mangroves of Red Hook. It’s flat, calm water ideal for your brother.

2. **Coki Beach Coral Restoration Program**:  
   Volunteer with the **Coki Beach Coral Restoration Foundation**. Snorkel with baby corals in a protected reef (near Reef Bay), or join a guided eco-tour for reef walks and conservation talks.  
   *Location*: Reef Bay (short drive from Secret Harbour/Frenchtown).

3. **Red Hook Cultural Festivals**:  
   Check the calendar for events like the **Red Hook Pirate Fest** (Oct) or **Soggy Dollar Bar’s "Blue Monday"** (Dec). Live music, food, and local art.

4. **Cinnamon Bay State Park**:  
   A 10-minute drive from Secret Harbour. Hike to secluded beaches like **Sandy Spit** or **Buck Island** (accessible by kayak). Calm waters and snorkeling here are legendary.

5. Cruz Bay Shops & Stroll:  
   Gallows Point Resort is steps from shops in Cruz Bay. Grab coffee, browse local crafts, or rent bikes to ride the scenic Cruz Bay to Cruz Bay Trail.

### On St. John:
Secret Harbour itself is activity central:  
- **Snorkeling right from your bay**: Look for parrotfish, sea turtles, and soft corals.  
- **Beachcombing & birding**: Walk to adjacent beaches like **Honeymoon Bay** (secluded, calm) or **Trunk Bay** (shaded, clear water).  
- **Reef Bay Kayak Tour**: Companies like **Red Hook Kayak Tours** offer guided trips through mangroves and to historic sites.

### Pro Tips:
- **Dietary Note**: Most resorts (like Secret Harbour) have vegetarian/vegan options. Cruz Bay’s shops also feature healthy local eats.  
- **Pressure-Free Gear**: Rent a kayak/paddleboard—no sudden depth changes!  
- **Guided Tours**: Companies like **Red Hook Ecotours** offer pressure-free snorkel tours with shallow entry points.
