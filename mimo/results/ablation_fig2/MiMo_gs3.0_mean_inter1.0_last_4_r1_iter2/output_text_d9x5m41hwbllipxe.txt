Of course. Let's estimate the cost for protecting your fifteen-hundred-square-foot home with four doors, seven windows, motion detectors, at least one camera, one glass break sensor, and an indoor siren.

First, for self-installment, the up-front cost could be as low as three hundred dollars. For a professional installation, that could exceed a thousand.

Monthly professional monitoring fees typically range from twenty to sixty dollars.

Now for the itemized list:
- Control panel or hub: one hundred to four hundred dollars.
- Four door or window sensors: sixty to two hundred dollars.
- Seven more door or window sensors: another seventy to two hundred and ten dollars.
- Motion detectors: one to three hundred dollars.
- One security camera: fifty to over two hundred dollars.
- One glass break sensor: thirty to one hundred dollars.
- One indoor siren: thirty to seventy dollars.

So, in total, a self-installed system could cost between five hundred and fifteen hundred dollars. A professional one could be between fifteen hundred and three thousand dollars.
