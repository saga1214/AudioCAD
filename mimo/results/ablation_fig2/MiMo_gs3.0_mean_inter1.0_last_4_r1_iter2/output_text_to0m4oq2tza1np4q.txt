### **Pickle-Juice Salmon Salad with Avocado & Cucumber**
*(Great for bloating and skin concerns)*

**Ingredients**:  
- 4 oz salmon fillet  
- 2 tbsp pickle juice (from your jar)  
- 1 ripe avocado, mashed  
- 1/2 cucumber, diced  
- 1 clove garlic, minced  
- 1 tbsp lemon juice  
- 1 tsp olive oil  
- Fresh dill or cilantro (from your balcony garden if possible!)  
- Salt/pepper to taste  
- Optional: 1 tbsp Greek yogurt for creaminess  

**Instructions**:  
1. **Marinate the salmon**: Pat salmon dry, then coat lightly with pickle juice and garlic. Let sit for 10 minutes.  
2. **Mix the salad**: Combine mashed avocado, cucumber, lemon juice, olive oil, and herbs.  
3. **Assemble**: Top the marinated salmon with the avocado-cucumber mix. Drizzle with extra pickle juice if desired.  
4. **Serve**: Enjoy as-is or with whole-grain toast.  

**Why it’s great for you**:  
- **Electrolytes**: Pickle juice replenishes sodium lost during runs.  
- **Anti-bloating**: Avocado and cucumber are hydrating and easy to digest.  
- **Anti-breakouts**: Lemon juice has vitamin C, and avocado reduces inflammation.  
- **Low-sodium**: You control the pickle juice amount (stick to ~2 tbsp/day for bloating).  

**Bonus tip**: Save pickle juice in a small jar for future marinades or dressings—it’s a zero-waste hack! 🌱
