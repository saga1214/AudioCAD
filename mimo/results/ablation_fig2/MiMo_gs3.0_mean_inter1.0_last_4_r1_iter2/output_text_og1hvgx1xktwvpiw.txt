You're right to be clear! Let me rephrase. The most common Boulevardier recipe is one and a quarter ounces whiskey to one ounce vermouth and one ounce Campari. Your friend's version of equal parts for all three is also traditional and very popular. Then there's the Last Word, which is a different cocktail made with equal parts of gin at three-quarters of an ounce, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice, all stirred together and garnished with an orange twist.
