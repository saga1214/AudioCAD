Okay, it sounds like you're all set for the painting phase. Just a few quick things to double-check before you start priming.

Make sure the doors you took off are completely clean, dry, and sanded. Also, make sure your workspace is covered so you don't get any dust or debris on them.

Before you start, measure your cabinet doors. You'll want to cut a few strips of painter's tape to make clean lines when you paint, and also measure for new hardware if you're planning on buying new handles later.

And one last thing, make sure you have a good, quality primer that's designed for cabinets. Once your prep is done, a coat of that primer will make your new color go on beautifully. Good luck
