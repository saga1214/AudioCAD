Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items while respecting the rules and minimizing noise:

### 1. **TV Mount Installation**
   - **Plan First:** Measure your wall space and choose the ideal mount location. Use a stud finder to locate wall studs.
   - **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, drill, appropriate drill bit, wall anchors (if no studs), level, screwdriver.
   - **Steps:**
     1. Mark stud positions on the wall.
     2. Pre-drill holes slightly smaller than the wall anchors/screws.
     3. If not on a stud, use heavy-duty anchors (landlord-friendly and secure).
     4. Attach the mount to the wall, ensuring it’s level.
     5. Secure the TV to the mount.
   - **Noise Tip:** Do this on a weekday morning when neighbors are less likely to be home. Clean up debris immediately.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Lightweight Options:** Use macramé hangers or suction cups for lightweight plants (e.g., snake plants).
   - **Heavy Plants:** For ceramic pots, use:
     - **Wall Anchors:** Install into studs or use heavy-duty anchors.
     - **Pegboard System:** Mount a pegboard and use small hooks.
   - **Tools Needed:** Drill, appropriate anchors, measuring tape, level.
   - **Steps:**
     1. Mark hook/anchor locations on the wall.
     2. Pre-drill holes and insert anchors.
     3. Attach hooks or anchors securely.
     4. Hang planters (test weight first).
   - **Noise Tip:** Install during off-peak hours (e.g., 9–11 AM). Use a vacuum to clean up dust.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Key Safety Note:** Floating shelves can hold 25–30 kg total. Avoid placing heavy items near the edge.
   - **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, drill, wall anchors (if no studs), level, screwdriver.
   - **Steps:**
     1. Measure shelf spacing and mark stud locations.
     2. Pre-drill holes and insert anchors if needed.
     3. Attach shelf brackets to the wall.
     4. Secure the shelf to the brackets.
   - **Noise Tip:** Use impact drivers for quick, quiet drilling. Install during work hours (when distracted decorating won’t happen).

### General Tips:
- **Minimize Noise:** Use a drill with a quiet mode or borrow a cordless impact driver. Work in short bursts (e.g., 15–20 mins).
- **Safety First:** Double-check weight limits, anchor types, and level alignment.
- **Schedule Wisely:** Dedicate Sundays to installations when friends aren’t over. Use workdays for focused decorating.
- **Landlord Rules:** Avoid loud power tools after 10 PM or on weekends. Hang planters/shelves with minimal noise.

By planning ahead and using efficient methods, you can transform your space without violating the rules! 🛠️✨
