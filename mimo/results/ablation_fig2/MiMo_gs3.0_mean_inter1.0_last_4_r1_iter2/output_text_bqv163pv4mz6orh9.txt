Okay, so we're looking for a thoughtful gift to show your daughter you're proud after her big play opening. Let's think about this. A food tour ticket you originally got yourself could actually be a fantastic option! You could offer to take her with you Thursday night after her show ends around seven. That four hours exploring amazing city food would be a perfect celebration for all her hard work. It's a surprise, it's a treat, and it's a bonding experience. If that feels like too much overlap with your food tour, another great idea is a small, personal token. Something like a nice notebook or a small arts and crafts kit, if she's into that. Or, maybe a cozy gift like a soft blanket or a really good pair of comfortable socks to wear backstage. The main thing is to make it feel special, so whatever you choose, a heartfelt note saying how proud you are will make it even more meaningful.
