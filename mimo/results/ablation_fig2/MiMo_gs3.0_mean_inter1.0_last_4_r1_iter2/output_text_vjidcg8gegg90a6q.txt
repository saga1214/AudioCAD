The best bedding for five-week-old chicks on concrete is what's called an organic corn cob core. It's made from the husks of corn, and it's great because rodents hate it, it's very absorbent, and it provides excellent insulation right off concrete. Just spread a thick layer over the brooder plate. You could also mix in a small amount of that large-flake pine shavings on top if you'd like, but try to avoid sand with chicks as it can get messy.
