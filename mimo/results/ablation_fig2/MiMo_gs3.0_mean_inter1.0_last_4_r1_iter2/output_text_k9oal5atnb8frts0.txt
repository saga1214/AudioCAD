For a retro tech theme, you could have a 'Mainframe' Lunch Platter, which would include a loaded baked potato with chili and cheese to look like a computer tower, and carrot sticks to look like cables. Then you could have a 'Dial-Up' Snack Pack with pretzel rods for the phone cords, a granola bar for the modem noise, and a small bag of chips for the data packets. Another idea for the main dish could be a 'Vintage Pizza' with pepperoni and green peppers to look like old pixels, served with a side of fruit salad that's shaped like a floppy disk. These are easy to theme and keep things light and fun.
