For wedding speeches, the standard guideline is indeed seven or eight hundred words. I'd stick with seven fifty as your target. Structure-wise, you were on the right track: reminisce about high school, include one vivid but concise shared memory, observe her relationship's development, and close with a toast. Remember, wedding planners are there to keep things on track, so don't stress about rambling. If you feel like you're going too fast out of nerves, just take a deliberate pause between your main points.
