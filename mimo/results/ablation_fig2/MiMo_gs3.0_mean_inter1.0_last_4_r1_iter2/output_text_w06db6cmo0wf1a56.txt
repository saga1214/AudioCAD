Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids... packing coolers... healthy but with some treats requested... I've got a solid plan for you. Here’s a shopping list designed for minimal mess and easy access all day.

First, for drinks, you'll need lots of reusable water bottles for everyone, plus extras for refills. Single-serve boxes of low-sugar fruit juice for the kids. Definitely sugar-free sodas for both adults and older kids. Sports drinks in single-serve packets would be a good idea for anyone who needs extra hydration in the heat. And don't forget plenty of ice to keep everything cold.

For snacks, think zero-mess. Cheese sticks, single-serve pretzels or trail mix bags, baby carrots, single-serve hummus cups, grapes, whole-grain crackers, and maybe some single-serve guacamave packs.

Now, for the main meal, since calzones were the priority, pizza is your most practical and mess-free option. I'd suggest pre-ordering from a local pizzeria. Ask them for ten large pizzas cut into mini-calzone-style pieces. This way, each piece is an individual portion and easy for everyone to grab. You could even ask them to put them in sturdy containers with lids. This is the closest you'll get to a real calzone without the mess of making them yourselves.

For dessert, to keep it simple and avoid plates and utensils, go with cupcakes or pre-cut slices of a fun dessert like brownies. They're already individual, so everyone can just grab one.

So, to recap the strategy: everything is packed individually in coolers with tight-fitting lids. You can label them—maybe one cooler for drinks and snacks, and another for the main meal. As people get hungry or thirsty, dads can just walk over to the food station and grab what their kids need without having to unpack everything. This should make for a really smooth and fun day focusing on the celebration! Have an amazing time.
