Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes for your leftover vodka and Argentine-inspired Fernet—perfect for a formal crowd:

### 1. **Vodka Sour (Elegant & Balanced)**
* **Ingredients:**  
  - 1.5 oz vodka  
  - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 0.5 oz simple syrup (1:1 sugar:water)  
  - *Optional:* 0.25 oz egg white or aquafaba (for silky texture)  
* **Method:**  
  Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Garnish with a lemon twist or fresh berries.  
* **Why it works:** Sweet-tart balance, visually appealing, and universally enjoyed (no age restrictions).

### 2. **Argentine-Style Fernet Spritz**  
*(Inspired by Fernet-Branca sodas)*  
* **Ingredients:**  
  - 2 oz Fernet-Branca (or similar herbal liqueur)  
  - 4 oz chilled sparkling water (flat or semi-sparkling)  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
  - 0.25 oz honey syrup (1:1 honey:water, warmed)  
  - *Garnish:* Cinnamon stick or mint sprig  
* **Method:**  
  Combine Fernet, lime, and honey syrup in a shaker. Add ice and shake 10 seconds. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice. Top with sparkling water. Stir gently. Garnish with cinnamon/mint.  
* **Why it works:** Light, refreshing, and aromatic—balances Fernet’s bitterness with citrus/honey. Sparkling water avoids soda issue.
