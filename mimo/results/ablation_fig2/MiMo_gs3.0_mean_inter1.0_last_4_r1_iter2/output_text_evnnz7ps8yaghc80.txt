Here’s a balanced weekly schedule designed to build skills without stress, incorporating scarves for pattern practice and allowing flexibility.

**Weekly Practice Schedule**

*   **Monday:** Dominant-Only Two Balls. Focus on making one-hand two-ball juggling flawless and rhythmic. (15-20 mins)
*   **Tuesday:** Three-Ball Transition & catches. Toss the first three throws deliberately. ONLY worry about throws, not catches. Focus on spacing peaks. (15 mins)
*   **Wednesday:** Two Objects in Non-Dominant Hand. Practice one-hand two with your other hand until comfortable. Helps symmetry. (10 mins)
*  音乐相关：**Thursday:** Music & Three-Balls. Add a slow-tempo song. Focus on matching throws to the beat. Catch if comfortable, ignore if stressful. (20 mins)
*   **Friday:** Scarf Practice. Use two long scarves. Focus on slow, deliberate pattern practice (like a shower or cascade). Builds muscle memory without pressure. (15 mins)
*   **Saturday:** Three-Ball Free Practice. Mix all elements: transition drills if needed, music, scarf patterns, short three-ball sequences. Keep sessions short and positive. (20 mins)
*   **Sunday:** Rest or Fun Juggle. No structured practice. Play juggle with son casually (even bad juggling counts!). Or watch a short juggling tutorial for inspiration. (5-10 mins)

**Key Schedule Principles:**

1.  **Short & Focused Sessions:** 15-20 minutes is better than one stressful hour.
2.  **Separate Transition Work:** Day 2 is purely about throws. Reduces the panic of "failing."
3.  **Music Integration:** Day 4 makes practice rhythmic and less stressful.
4.  **Scarves for Pattern Focus:** Day 5 builds fundamental timing without the pressure of drops.
5.  **Rest & Fun:** Day 7 prevents burnout and connects learning to the promise with your son.
6.  **Be Kind to Yourself:** Missing a session? Do less. Panicking? Stop and do something else. Stress truly is blocking learning – recognize when to pause.
You've got this one throw at a time. Teaching your son will happen naturally once *you're* ready.
