For a 15-minute break before finishing work, here are tailored suggestions based on your energy level and routine:

1. **Micro-Meditation (5-10 mins)**  
   Since you mentioned meditation feels "jarring" when returning to work, try a *short, grounding practice*:  
   - Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and focus on your breath for 3-5 minutes.  
   - When thoughts arise, gently label them ("thinking," "planning") and return to your breath.  
   - This creates mental space without deep immersion. Pair it with soft background music (like Toumani Diabaté’s *Mali Deux* for a mellower vibe).

2. **Hydration + Coffee Ritual (10 mins)**  
   - Sip a warm beverage mindfully, focusing on the aroma and taste.  
   - Add lemon or cinnamon for a sensory reset.  
   - Stand near a window for 2-3 minutes of natural light exposure to boost alertness.

3. **Dynamic Stretching Sequence (12 mins)**  
   Extend your standing desk movement with:  
   - **Neck rolls** (slow circles) to relieve tension.  
   - **Shoulder shrugs** (lift to ears, release) 3x.  
   - **Seated spinal twists** (hold each side for 30 seconds).  
   - **Ankle circles** (10 reps each foot) to improve circulation.

4. **Nature Micro-Break (10-15 mins)**  
   If possible:  
   - Step outside for 5 minutes of fresh air.  
   - If stuck indoors, open a window and listen to birdsong + Olic Farka’s *Bamadesu* (another Grammy winner).

**Why this works**:  
- Avoids "jarring" transitions by keeping sessions short and intentional.  
- Combines light movement with sensory grounding (music, breath, light).  
- Hydration + coffee ritual addresses both physical and mental refreshment.
