That's awesome—your coffee table is assembled and ready to be filled! 🎉 Here are some savory snack ideas from a typical vending machine (plus creative table-filling ideas):

### 🧂 Savory Snack Picks:
1. **Pretzels**  
   - Classic salty crunch (classic, beer-flavored, or flavored like cinnamon).  
2. **Beef Jerky**  
   - High-protein, savory, and portable.  
3. **Rice Krispie Treats?** Wait, no—**Savory Rice Cakes** instead!  
4. **Cheese Puffs/Cheese Crisps**  
   - Salty, cheesy, and fun for sharing.  
5. **Dark Chocolate** (if you tolerate *very* little sweetness)  
   - Look for 70%+ cacao bars—less sweet, more bitter.  
6. **Wasabi Peas**  
   - Spicy, crunchy, and addictive.  
7. **Popcorn**  
   - Air-popped or lightly salted varieties.  
8. **Nuts** (if available)  
   - Almonds, peanuts, or trail mix—no chocolate coating!  

### 🍫 Savory Chocolate Twists (if vending machine has them):  
- **Salted Dark Chocolate Bars** (e.g., 85% cacao + flaky salt).  
- **Chocodiles** (Cadbury’s—chocolate-coated cornflakes, not sweet!).  
- **Savory Chocolate Truffles** (some brands add nuts, sea salt, or chili).  

### 💡 Table-Filling Inspiration:  
- **Snack Stations**:  
  - Top shelf: Pretzels + cheese puffs.  
  - Middle: Beef jerky + wasabi peas.  
  - Bottom: Dark chocolate + nuts.  
- **Project Fuel**:  
  - Keep a bowl of popcorn + a jar of nuts nearby while working on your IKEA project!  
- **Pro Tip**: Check vending machine brands—some (like Snack Exchange or Fresh & Furious) offer unique savory options like roasted chickpeas or spiced nuts.  

### ⚠️ Avoid:  
- Candy-coated chocolates, gummies, caramel treats, or anything with "sugar-coated" in the description.  

Enjoy your snacks and project! If your vending machine has **salty licorice** or **spicy tuna sticks**, those are wild but fun too. 🚀
