### 🔍 Clicker Training Explained:
Clicker training uses a **three-step process** to build understanding between your puppy and the clicker:

1. **Mark the Behavior**:  
   - Galileo does *something* you want (e.g., sits).  
   - **Click** the clicker *immediately* as he does it.  

2. **Reward Immediately**:  
   - Give a treat *right after the click*. The click acts as a "bridge" linking the behavior to the reward.  

3. **Repeat & Build**:  
   - Practice until he learns the click = treat.  
   - Gradually add verbal cues ("sit!") *after* the click.  

**Example**:  
- Galileo sits → You click → Give treat → Say "Good sit!"  
- Repeat 10–15 times daily.

### 🏠 Crate Training Tips (Making It Cozy):
- **Never use the crate as punishment**—only for sleep/short breaks.  
- **Start small**: Place treats near the crate door, then inside. Let him explore.  
- **Add comfort**: Use a soft blanket, stuffed toy, or even a familiar blanket from your home.  
- **Gradual confinement**: Start with 5 minutes, gradually increasing time. Use a crate cover for darkness.  
- **Praise inside**: Reward calm behavior in the crate with treats or playtime.

### 🚫 Housebreaking & Accidents:
- **Praise for successes**: "Yes!" + treat when he goes outside.  
- **Clean accidents thoroughly**: Use enzymatic cleaners (like Nature's Miracle) to remove odors.  
- **No punishment**: Yelling or scolding confuses him. Stay calm—focus on prevention.  

---

### 🏘️ Recall Training for Escapes:
If Galileo bolts outside:  
1. **Use high-value rewards**: Call his name + "Come!" with a treat in your hand.  
2. **Practice in safe spaces**: Start indoors, then in a quiet yard.  
3. **Never chase**: Walk toward him calmly. If he runs, wait and lure him back with treats.  
4. **Gradual distance**: Reward when he comes to you, then slowly increase distance.
