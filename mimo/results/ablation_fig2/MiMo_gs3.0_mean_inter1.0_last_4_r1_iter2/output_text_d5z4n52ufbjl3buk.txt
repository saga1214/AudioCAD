Okay, I can definitely help you structure that email. Here's how you could approach it, keeping your goal of learning while minimizing potential friction.

First, start with the subject line. Something neutral like "Discussion about recent essay exam" is good.

In the email body, begin with a polite greeting, such as "Dear Professor [Last Name]," and a clear opening line like, "I'm writing to set up a brief meeting about my recent exam performance."

Next, frame your interest in a way that shows you're engaged. You could say, "As I want to do even better on upcoming tests, I'd be very grateful if I could understand what areas in my essay could have been improved upon based on your feedback."

To compliment him without being insincere, you could add, "I understand essay grading can be subjective, and I appreciate the effort you put into evaluating it."

Finally, propose a specific action: "Would you have any availability during office hours or outside of class this week? I'm happy to find a time that works for you."

This approach is respectful, focuses on your growth, and avoids challenging his ego directly. Good luck!
