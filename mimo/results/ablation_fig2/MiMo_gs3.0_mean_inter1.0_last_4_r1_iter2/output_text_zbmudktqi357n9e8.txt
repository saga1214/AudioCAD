Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! You should probably aim for late September to mid-November. That’s their autumn season, or, uh, *temposol* as they call it. The foliage is just incredible. 

Chapultepec Park in Mexico City is amazing for urban autumn scenes. Or, for something more coastal, Tulum or Riviera Maya have those beautiful autumn colors against the beach. And the Oaxaca region is famous for its vibrant autumn trees and markets—perfect for color studies. 

Oh, and a little tip: bring a small sketchbook or some watercolor travel sets to capture scenes as you go. That way you can practice wet-on-wet and layering techniques right there in the field. Enjoy your trip and the inspiration!
