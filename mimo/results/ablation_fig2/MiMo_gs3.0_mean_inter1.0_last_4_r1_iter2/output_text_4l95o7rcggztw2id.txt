Here’s a complete nautical-themed shopping list focusing on **sand-friendly finger foods**, **theme integration**, and **watermelon allergy avoidance**, organized by category:

---

### **Food & Drinks Shopping List**
*(Serves all 8 children)*

#### **1. "Buried Treasure" Sliders**  
- 12 slices whole wheat bread  
- 8 oz cream cheese or hummus (for "dirt")  
- 1 cup shredded carrots or cucumber (for "gold")  
- 1 cup pepperoni slices (for "coins")  
- *Theme Tip:* Pack cream cheese in small cups; assemble sliders in palm leaves labeled "X Marks the Spot."

#### **2. Fruit Skewers ("Pirate Jewels")**  
- 1 lb grapes  
- 1 lb pineapple chunks  
- 1 lb blueberries  
- 1 lb cantaloupe or honeydew balls  
- *Allergy Note:* Skip watermelon entirely.

#### **3. Cheese & Veggie Packs ("Cannonballs" & "Shells")**  
- 1 lb small mozzarella cheese cubes ("cannonballs")  
- 1 bag baby carrots ("coral")  
- 1 bag cucumber slices ("seashells")  
- 1 bag cherry tomatoes ("pearls")  

#### **4. Crackers & Snacks ("Treasure Chest Snacks")**  
- 1 box goldfish crackers  
- 1 box graham crackers  
- 1 bag pretzels  
包装技巧: Pack snacks in mini treasure chest containers or muslin bags tied with ribbon.

#### **5. Beach-Safe Snacks ("Ship Masts" & "Galley Rations")** Individual popcorn bags labeled "Ship Masts," and mini bagels topped with cream cheese ("galley rations").

#### **6. Drinks ("Pirate Punch" & "海水")**  
- 8 individual juice boxes (apple, fruit punch, etc.)  
- 8 small water bottles (reusable or disposable)  
- *Mock Punch Idea:* Mix purple lemonade with blue Gatorade in a large bucket (add glitter for "sea foam").

#### **7. Plates & Extras**  
- 8 pirate-themed plastic plates  
- 8 biodegradable plastic cups  
- 8 paper napkins (pirate skull designs)  
- Mini plastic cutlery  
- *Sand-Saving Tip:* Provide wet wipes and hand sanitizer in pirate-themed pouches.

---

### **Key Sand-Friendly Features**
- **No-Drip Packaging:** Juice boxes/water bottles seal sand out.  
- **Self-Contained Snacks:** Treasure chest containers and skewers keep food sealed.  
- **Easy-Grip Shapes:** Crackers, cheese cubes, and sliders are child-friendly at 3 feet from sand.  
- **Allergy Safe:** No watermelon; use cantaloupe/honeydew instead.

### **Theme Boosters**
- Label food with nautical names like "Davy Jones’ Lunch" or "Kraken’s Kisses."  
- Serve drinks in mini pirate buckets.  
- Use a treasure map table runner.

This setup ensures fuss-free eating while keeping your son’s pirate party immersive at the beach! 🏴‍☠️⚓
