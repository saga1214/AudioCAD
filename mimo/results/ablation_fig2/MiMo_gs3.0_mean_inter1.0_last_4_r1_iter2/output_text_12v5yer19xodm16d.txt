Okay, you need a boost? Let's get into it. For drinks, I'd suggest five things. First, an espresso shot for an instant jolt. Second, an energy drink, like Red Bull, for a quick caffeine fix. Third, black coffee, which has sustained energy. Fourth, a cold brew, which is smoother and lasts longer. And fifth, green tea, for a steady release of caffeine and some antioxidants.

For snacks, here are five ideas. One, a protein bar for sustained energy. Two, nuts, which give you healthy fats and protein. Three, dark chocolate, for a little caffeine and a boost in antioxidants. Four, Greek yogurt, which is high in protein to keep you full. And five, fruit, like an apple or banana, for natural sugars and fiber.

Hope that helps you power through!
