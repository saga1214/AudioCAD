The Lockport Museum is a perfect place to start your exploration. It's located right there in the heart of the city and is dedicated to the history of the canal, the lock system, and the people who worked there. It's housed in a building that was once a canal warehouse, so it gives you a real sense of the history. You can see everything from old machinery to models of the locks. It's a great way to understand the engineering and the economic importance the canal had for the area.
