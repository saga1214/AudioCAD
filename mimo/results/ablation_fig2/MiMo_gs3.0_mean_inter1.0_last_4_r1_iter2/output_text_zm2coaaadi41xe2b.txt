Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! 

For ramen, try Tonkatsu Tamago for classic Japanese ramen or Daikokuya for tonkatsu. 

For Chinese noodles, check out Fuchsia Frog for Peking duck noodles or Old北京 for a more traditional spot. 

And for Korean noodles, Myeongdong Street offers great fire noodles or Bando for a mix of Korean and Japanese flavors.
