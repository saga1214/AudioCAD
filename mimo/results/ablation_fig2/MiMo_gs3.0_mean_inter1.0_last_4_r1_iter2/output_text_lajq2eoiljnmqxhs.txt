Cooking could work, as long as you account for her dietary restrictions upfront. Lactose means no regular milk or cheese, probably using alternatives like almond milk or coconut yogurt. Meat exclusions—vegetables, seafood, poultry—narrows down the options, but there are still things you can do. How about a simple pasta dish? You can use olive oil or butter, a non-dairy sauce like pesto or a simple tomato sauce, and plenty of vegetables she at least tolerates, like zucchini or bell peppers. Keep it quick, maybe a 30-minute meal, so it’s not stressful. That way, you’re doing something together without it being a huge ordeal.
