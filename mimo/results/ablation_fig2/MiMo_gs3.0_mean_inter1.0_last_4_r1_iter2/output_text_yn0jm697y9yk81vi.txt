### **Key Tips**
- For **thinner sheets**: Start with aviation snips.  
- For **thicker metal**: Use an angle grinder – faster and cleaner cuts.  
- For **drywall**: **Always use screws** (even ring-shank nails can fail long-term).  
- For **insulation**: Fiberglass is most accessible; mineral wool excels in soundproofing.
