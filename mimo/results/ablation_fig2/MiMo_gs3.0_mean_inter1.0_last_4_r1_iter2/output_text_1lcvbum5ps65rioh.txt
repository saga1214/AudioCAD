### U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia
1.  **Fine:** $160
2.  **Demerit Points:** 2 points added to your driving record.
3.  **Potential Risks:** Illegal U-turns can cause collisions, especially if you don't meet the visibility requirement (clearly seeing 150m in both directions).

**Key Rule Reminder:** Always check signs at intersections. If there's no sign allowing U-turns, it's illegal, even if you can see 150m. Safety first!

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients (Pork-Free)
Since you avoid pork, you'll use **guanciale** (cured pork cheek - *not* pork) or substitute with **pancetta** (Italian cured pork belly - *not* pork) or **thick-cut bacon** (if needed). For a strict pork-free version, use bacon or omit the cured pork element.

Here's what you need (serves 2-4):

1.  **Pasta:** 400-450g (14-16oz) spaghetti or bucatini.
2.  **Eggs:** 4 large eggs + 2 large egg yolks (room temperature).
3.  **Cheese:** 150-200g (5-7oz) **Pecorino Romano** (aged sheep's milk, salty) *or* Parmigiano Reggiano (use less salt). Grate finely.
4.  **Garlic:** 1-2 cloves, finely minced or grated.
5. 黑胡椒 (Black Pepper): Freshly cracked, lots of it!
6.  **Cured Pork (Optional but traditional):**
    *   **Guanciale:** 150-200g (5-7oz), diced (cured pork cheek).
    *   **Pancetta:** 150-200g (5-7oz), diced (cured pork belly).
    *   **Bacon:** 100-150g (3.5-5oz), thick-cut, diced (if using bacon).
7.  **Salt:** For pasta water and seasoning.
8.  **Water:** For cooking pasta.
9.  **(Optional but recommended):** A small handful of **fresh parsley**, finely chopped.

**Key Notes:**
*   **No Guanciale/Pancetta?** Use thick-cut bacon for a pork-free version. Reduce salt in the egg mixture.
*   **Cheese:** Pecorino is traditional and salty. Use less salt if using both Pecorino and salted cured pork.
*   **Eggs:** Room temperature blend smoother.
