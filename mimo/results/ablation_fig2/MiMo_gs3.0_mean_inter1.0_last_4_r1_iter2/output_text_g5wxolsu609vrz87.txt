I initially said the shoulders. After you brought up both the pants length and, more importantly, the waist, I can see how strong those arguments are. From a purely technical tailoring perspective, the shoulders remain the most complex fit point and the foundation of the jacket's structure. However, for your class essay, you need the factor that is most critical to the wearer's perception of comfort and style. In that case, the waist is arguably the most important because it defines your shape and how the suit drapes on your torso. It's the part that most dramatically affects comfort and the ability to move freely. So, for passing your tailoring class essay, arguing that the waist is the one most important factor is a very solid answer. I'd say go with the waist.
