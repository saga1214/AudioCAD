For tuning your strings while waiting, here's how to use a tuner tool on any instrument (guitar, ukulele, etc.):

### 1. **Clip-On Tuner**  
   - **How to use**:  
     - Clip the tuner onto the headstock of your instrument.  
     - Play each string individually.  
     - The tuner will display the pitch (e.g., "A," "D," "G").  
     - Adjust the string’s tension by turning the tuning peg until the tuner shows the target note (usually indicated by a green light or "perfect" icon).  
   - **Best for**: Precision tuning on multiple strings without distractions.  

### 2. **Tuner App**  
   - **How to use**:  
     - Open a tuner app (e.g., *GuitarTuna*, *Fender Tune*, *SongKIC*) and place your phone near the instrument.  
     - Play a string. The app will display the pitch and show how far off it is (e.g., "flat" or "sharp").  
     - Turn the tuning peg until the note matches the target (app will show a green checkmark when correct).  
   - **Best for**: Quick tuning on the go or with no external tools.  

### 3. **Tuning Fork**  
   - **How to use**:  
     - Strike the fork to produce a pure tone (e.g., an A440 pitch).  
     - Hold it near the string while tuning.  
     - Adjust until the string’s vibration matches the fork’s pitch.  
   - **Best for**: Traditionalists or when you don’t have digital tools.  

### Key Tips:  
- **Check all strings**: Tune each string individually to the correct note (e.g., E, A, D, G for guitar).  
- **Use reference notes**: Many apps/tuners show relative tuning (e.g., "tune higher/lower than the last string").  
- **Avoid over-tightening**: String tension should be snug but not strained.
