Okay, you've got the essentials, but I'd really recommend adding a few more tools to make the day smoother and safer.

First, you'll definitely need a power drill and some 2-and-1/8-inch screws. Habitat cabinets usually come with these, but it's always good to have your own.

A stud finder is crucial for quickly and accurately finding all those wall studs.

Shims are also key. You mentioned them for base cabinets, but you'll need them for uppers too, to get them perfectly level against the step stool.

A measuring tape will be super handy for checking dimensions between cabinets and making sure everything lines up perfectly.

And most importantly, please make sure you have safety glasses to protect your eyes from dust and debris. It's a good idea to have a dust mask too, but safety glasses are a must.

You're all set for a successful cabinet install!
