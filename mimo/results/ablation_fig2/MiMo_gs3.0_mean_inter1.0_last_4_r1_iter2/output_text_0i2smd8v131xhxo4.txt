### Microphone & Acoustics
1. **Yeti Placement**: Always speak into the side with the logo (cardioid position). Maintain 1 hand's width (~6-8 inches) from your mouth. Avoid top-facing position unless in stereo mode.
2. Acoustic Treatment: Position your Yeti 12-18 inches from a reflective wall. Hang at least 2 large blankets/tapestries opposite hard surfaces to absorb high frequencies causing harshness.

### Vocal Endurance & Health
- **Warm-ups**: Daily 10-min routine: Lip trills → Humming → Tongue twisters
- **Breathing**: Practice diaphragmatic breathing (hand on belly) - record 10-min sessions with 1-min breaks
- **Hydration**: Sip room-temperature water hourly. Avoid cold water during recording (can constrict vocal cords)
- **Posture**: Sit upright with mic at eye level. Use a pop filter to reduce plosives

### Editing Workflow & AI Tools
| Tool | Pros | Cons | Alternatives |
|------|------|------|-------------|
| **Adobe Audition** | GPU acceleration, AI-powered noise reduction (Essential), spectral repair | Steeper learning curve ($20.99/mo) | Audacity (free) + iZotope RX 10 ($149) |
| **Noise Reduction** | iZotope RX Voice Denoise (industry standard) | Requires training samples | Audacity's Noise Reduction plugin (basic) |
| **Mouth Clicks** | RX DeClicker (adjusts sensitivity to breaths/clicks) | Over-processing risk | Reduce gain in Audition's Noise Gate (set -60dB threshold) |
