Here's a quick remedy for your coffee craving that works *immediately*—no machine, no milk warming, just pure speed:  
**Microwave a spoonful of cold milk into your cup first**, then add the instant coffee grounds. Stir until dissolved, then add more warm milk to taste. It mimics a latte base in under 2 minutes!  

### If the weather *still* worries you:
1. **Grab a rain jacket first**—it’s a windbreaker *and* waterproof layer.  
2. **Pack your coffee immediately**: Skip the machine debate—grab instant.  
3. **Microwave your latte on the go**:  
   - Fill a mug with 1 cup of cold milk.  
   - Add 1 tsp instant coffee (or 2 tsp if you like it strong).  
   - Stir well, microwave for **45 seconds** (stir again halfway).  
   - Done! Creamy, rich, and no spills.  

### Bonus luxury hack:
If you *really* want that latte vibe but can’t wait… **steal a spoonful of cream from the fridge** and stir it into the instant coffee. It’s a cheat code for richness!  

**Key takeaway:** Instant coffee from machine grounds + warm milk + a splash of cream = a "luxury latte" in under 3 minutes. Weather be damned, your craving is satisfied NOW. ☕️✨
