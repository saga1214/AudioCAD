That's a very common misconception, but it's the other way around. While publishers do hire their own illustrators, they are absolutely open to receiving a complete manuscript with your illustrations. In fact, they often call this a 'self-illustrated manuscript.'

However, the biggest challenge for you will be finding an agent who is open to that specific submission. Most agents want to represent the writer and the text first. They are looking for a writer who they think has the potential to become a great collaborator with an illustrator. So, your first step is to find agents who explicitly state that they accept self-illustrated manuscripts. You can search for them on sites like the Children's Book Academy or the Association of Children's Book Writers.

Once you find an agent who is a good fit, you can submit your complete manuscript with your illustrations. They will then pitch it to publishers on your behalf.
