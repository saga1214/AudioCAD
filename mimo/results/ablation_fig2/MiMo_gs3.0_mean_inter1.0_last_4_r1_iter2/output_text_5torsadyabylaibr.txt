Absolutely. For Thanksgiving, a great one-pot dessert is a pumpkin pie casserole. You just pour canned pumpkin and other ingredients into a baking dish and bake it. It requires no special equipment.

And for a really easy and impressive-sounding pie, you could make a no-bake cheesecake. You make a simple graham cracker crust and then pour the cheese mixture right into it to set in the fridge.
