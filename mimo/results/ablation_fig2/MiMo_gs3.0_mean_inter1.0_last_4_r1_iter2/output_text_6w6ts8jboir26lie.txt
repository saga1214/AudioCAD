That's a wonderful idea—cooking for yourself is a fantastic way to unwind. Here are five simple, delicious dinner ideas using fresh, beach-friendly ingredients:

### 🍤 1. **Fresh Seafood Paella**  
**Ingredients:** Shrimp, mussels, asparagus, bell peppers, saffron, tomatoes, white wine, rice.  
**Why it's great:** Feels festive and coastal. Cook the rice in the wine and broth, then add the seafood last.  

### 🥗 2. **Mediterranean Grain Bowl**  
**Ingredients:** Quinoa or farro, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, olives, feta, lemon-tahini dressing.  
**Why it's great:** Light, colorful, and packed with fresh produce. Toss everything in a bowl and top with herbs.  

### 🍝 3. **Creamy Tomato Basil Pasta**  
**Ingredients:** Pasta (fettuccine or penne), cherry tomatoes, garlic, fresh basil, heavy cream, Parmesan.  
**Why it's great:** Comforting but not heavy. Sauté garlic, add tomatoes and cream, toss with pasta.  

### 🐟 4. **Grilled Salmon with Seasonal Veggies**  
**Ingredients:** Salmon fillet, asparagus, zucchini, lemon, dill, olive oil.  
**Why it's great:** Healthy fats from salmon + easy veggies. Grill or pan-sear the salmon, roast veggies in the oven.  

### 🍤 5. **Shrimp and Veggie Stir-Fry**  
**Ingredients:** Shrimp, snap peas, bell peppers, carrots, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, rice noodles.  
**Why it's great:** Quick, customizable, and great for using whatever veggies you find.  

### 💡 **Pro Tips:**  
- **Shop smart:** Look for local seafood, berries, and greens at the beach market.  
- **Keep it simple:** Use fresh herbs, lemon, and garlic to brighten everything up.  
- **Enjoy the process:** This vacation is YOUR time—savor each step!
