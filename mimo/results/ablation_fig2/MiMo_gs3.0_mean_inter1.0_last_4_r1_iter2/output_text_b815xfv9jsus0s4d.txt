Based on your conversation and TikTok's algorithm, **following the guidelines** is the **single most important factor** to consider first and foremost. Here’s why:

### 🚫 **Why "Following Guidelines" Wins**:
1. **No Visibility = No Success**:  
   If your video violates TikTok’s policies (e.g., copyright, inappropriate content, misleading labels), it gets **restricted or removed**. Even if your content is technically high-quality, **zero people will see it**. This is non-negotiable.

2. **Algorithmic Trust**:  
   TikTok prioritizes content that aligns with its safety and community standards. Consistently violating guidelines signals to the algorithm that your content isn’t "safe" to promote, **stunting your reach** from day one.

3. **Foundation for Everything Else**:  
   You can’t hook viewers, optimize hashtags, or improve engagement if your video isn’t even shown. Following guidelines is the **prerequisite** for testing other factors (e.g., hook, hashtags, etc.).

### 📊 **Ranking the Other Factors**:
1. **Engaging Content** (Most Important After Compliance):  
   *Why it ranks second*: Without immediate engagement (likes, comments, shares) in the first 3–5 seconds, the algorithm won’t amplify your video. This is your "hook."

2. **Consistency** (Critical for Growth):  
   *Why it’s not #1*: Posting regularly trains the algorithm to recognize your style and audience, but **only if your content is compliant and engaging**. Inconsistent posting without compliance is like driving without fuel.

3. **Conciseness** (Key for Retention):  
   *Why it’s third*: Short videos (<15 seconds) often perform better because they **reduce drop-off rates**. If viewers watch to the end, the algorithm rewards you with exposure.

4. **Good Description/Hashtags** (Supporting Role):  
   *Why it’s last*: Hashtags and descriptions help **target your audience** but are less impactful if your content doesn’t engage or comply. They’re a "bonus" for reach, not a foundation.

### 💡 **Key Takeaway**:  
TikTok’s algorithm is a **gatekeeper**. If your video doesn’t pass compliance checks, **no amount of great hooks, lighting, or hashtags will matter**. Prioritize this first, then focus on:  
1. **Hooking viewers instantly** (first 2 seconds).  
2. **Optimizing for retention** (concise, valuable content).  
3. **Posting consistently** (3–5 times/week).  
4. **Using strategic hashtags** (3–5 niche + trending tags).
