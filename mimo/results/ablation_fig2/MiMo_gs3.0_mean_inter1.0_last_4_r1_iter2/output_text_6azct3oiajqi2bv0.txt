### Best Methods:
1. **Iron-On Adhesive (Most Recommended)**  
   - Use a **fabric-specific iron-on tacky glue** (like E6000 Fabric Glue).  
   - *Steps*:  
     - Clean the jacket lining with rubbing alcohol first.  
     - Apply glue to the item (tag/patch), press firmly for 30 seconds.  
     - Iron the item (no heat!) from the back for 10-15 seconds to activate the glue.  
   - *Why it works*: Adheres well to synthetic linings without melting. Test on a hidden seam first.

2. **Double-Sided Fabric Tape**  
   - Look for **washable, medium-strength fabric tape** (like 3M Scotchcast).  
   - *Steps*:  
     - Cut tape to size.  
     - Peel backing, press onto lining and item, smooth out air bubbles.  
   - *Pros*: No heat, removable with solvent if needed. Ideal for lightweight items.

3. **Hot Glue (Temporary Fix)**  
   - Use a **low-temperature hot glue gun** (like Gorilla).  
   - *Steps*:  
     - Apply small dots of glue to the item.  
     - Press onto lining and hold for 10 seconds.  
   - *Caution*: Avoid high temps; test on scrap fabric first. Best for non-critical items.

### What to Avoid:
- **Spray Adhesives**: Can seep into seams and weaken fabric over time.  
- **Strong Glues (E6000)**: May discolor or melt synthetic linings if not applied carefully.  
- **Stitching**: If the lining is accessible, hand-sewing with a thin needle is safest long-term.
