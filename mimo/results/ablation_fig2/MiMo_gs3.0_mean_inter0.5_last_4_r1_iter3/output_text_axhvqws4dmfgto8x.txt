Don't sweat it. Setting off alarms is temporary blunders, but forgetting to salt pasta water is a sin that will haunt a cook forever!

For stir-fry noodles, make sure you add a generous amount of salt to the pot of boiling water—like a full teaspoon. And use plastic utensils this time. Wooden items belong in tacos from now on.

Another pro tip is to have all your stir-fry ingredients chopped and ready to go before you even turn on the stove. It's all about speed. And when you're done boiling the noodles, drain them very well.
