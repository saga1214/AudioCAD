To tune your instrument (likely a guitar, based on the mention of "strings"), here's how to use the tools mentioned:

### 1. **Strand Clip-On Tuner (e.g., Snark or Peterson)**
   - **How to Use:**
     1. **Attach the tuner:** Clip it to the headstock of your instrument (where the tuning pegs are).
     2. **Pluck a string:** Play a single string (start with the thickest, lowest-pitched one).
     3. **Match the note:** The tuner will display a light (green) when the note is correct, or a light indicating it's too high (red) or too low (yellow). Adjust the tuning peg (the wheel on the headstock) to turn the string until the tuner shows green.
     4. **Repeat:** Move to the next string (thinner, higher-pitched) and repeat until all strings are tuned.

### 2. **Tuner App (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune)**
   - **How to Use:**
     1. **Open the app:** Launch it and select "Tuning."
     2. **Position your phone:** Hold the phone near the instrument’s soundhole or body for better audio pickup.
     3. **Pluck a string:** Play one string at a time.
     4. **Match the note:** The app will show a visual guide (like a green circle or waveform) when the note is correct. Adjust the tuning peg until the app confirms it’s in tune.
     5. **Repeat:** Tune each string sequentially, starting from the lowest (6th) to the highest (1st).

### Key Tips:
- **Tuning Pegs:** These are the small wheels on the headstock you turn to adjust string tension. Turning clockwise tightens the string (raises pitch); counterclockwise loosens it (lowers pitch).
- **Frets:** These are the raised markers along the neck. Plucking a string over a fret produces a specific note (e.g., fret 5 on the 6th string = A note).
- **Start Low:** Always tune the lowest string first (6th string) to avoid overstretching higher strings.

If you're using a different instrument (like a ukulele or mandolin), the process is similar—just match the tuner/app to the instrument's pitch range. Happy tuning! 🎸
