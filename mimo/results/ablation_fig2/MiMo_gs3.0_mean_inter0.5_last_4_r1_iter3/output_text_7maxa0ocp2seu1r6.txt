### 🔶 **Core Body Measurements (take for every fitting)**

4. Shoulder width  
5. Neck (for collars)  
6. Upper back (for jackets/shirts)  
7. Bicep (sleeve comfort)  

### 🔶 **Garment-Specific Measurements**  
8. **Inseam** (for pants)  
9. **Sleeve Length** (tops, jackets)  
10. **Thigh** (fitted pants/skirts)  
11. **Rise** (front vs. back; critical for tall clients!)

    - *Back Rise*: Distance from waist to hem
