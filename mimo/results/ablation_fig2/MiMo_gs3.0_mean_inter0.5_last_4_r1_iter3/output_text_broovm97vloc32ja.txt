That's fantastic news! If you can do both the words and the art, you are in an incredibly powerful position. You can submit a fully illustrated manuscript to a publisher or an agent who represents illustrated books. This is actually the ideal situation.

However, the process is still very similar to what I mentioned before. You'll need to write a query letter that highlights both your story and your illustration skills. You'll still need to research agents and publishers who specifically seek out illustrated manuscripts and submit your work to them. The only difference is that you'll be sending in the complete package, which is a major advantage.
