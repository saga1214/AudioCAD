Other phrases similar to "burning the candle at both ends" include, “Don’t count your chickens before they hatch,” which means don’t assume good things will happen before they do. Or, “The early bird catches the worm,” which is about acting early for success. Another one is, “Don’t cry over spilled milk,” which is about not dwelling on past mistakes. Each one carries its own wisdom.
