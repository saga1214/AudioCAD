First, you'll want to gather a few more key tools. You'll definitely need a power drill with a few different drill bits, especially a pilot bit for when you screw into the studs. You'll also need a measuring tape and a pencil for marking measurements. Shims are crucial for getting everything level, and you'll need some wood glue to attach the backsplash to the cabinets. A miter saw or even a hand saw will be helpful for cutting the countertop to size. And of course, a level and a tape measure are essential for getting everything perfectly aligned.

You've got the right idea about starting with the uppers first. Rest each one against that temporary board you screwed into the studs below your line and clamp it in place before securing it to the wall.

Then set the base cabinets in with your shims underneath until they hit the top of your line. Clamp adjacent cabinets flush before you screw them in.

Once the uppers are secure, set the base cabinets in place using shims. Wood glue is great for attaching the backsplash to the cabinet boxes. You can always shim up a cabinet over a low spot, but you can't fix a high spot.
