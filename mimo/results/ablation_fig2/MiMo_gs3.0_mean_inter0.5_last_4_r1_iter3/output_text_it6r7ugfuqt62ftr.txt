Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimming and warm weather for your family getaway. Pack that bathing suit confidently!

For packing:
Bathing suits (pack two different styles).
Daytime: Shorts, tank tops, sundress or athletic shorts and tees.
Footwear: Sandals or flip-flops.
Accessories: Sunglasses, a fun hat, high-SPF gluten-free sunscreen.
Light layer: Pack a lightweight sweater or cover-up jacket for cooler Northern evenings even where it's warm.

Have a relaxing warm-weather Northern trip!

Regarding birthday adventures (celiac & avoiding heights):
The hot air balloon is thoughtful but understandable if it's not right for you. Whale/dolphin tours offer incredible views and excitement. Horseback riding on scenic beaches is another fantastic active outdoor experience.

For Celiac-safe celebrating:
Find a great gluten-free bakery for her birthday cake or cupcakes.
Call ahead to top-rated local restaurants and confirm they can safely accommodate Celiac needs.
The Alcatraz cruise ending at Fisherman's Wharf is an excellent choice! For Celiac-friendly dining nearby, Fog Harbor Fish House is ideal - known for accommodating Celiac needs and offering bay views perfect for a 12th birthday.

After her birthday, for a 4-5 day warm weather trip for you, your husband, and daughter (needing a larger vehicle), here are two premier destinations:

First up: Palm Springs, from late October to early November.
Accommodations: A luxury resort like Amanjeno in the Mission Hills would be stunning, offering pools and incredible desert scenery. Or consider a large condo in Old Town Palm Springs for more space.
Getting There: Rent a large SUV or minivan from a company like Enterprise or Hertz.
Getting Around: Palm Springs is compact, so walking or renting bikes is easy for downtown. For desert exploration, a rental car is best for places like the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway, which offers warm weather at the top and amazing views.
Swimming: You'll find plenty of resort pools and even public options like the Palm Springs Aquatic Center.
Gluten-Free Dining: Many top restaurants have excellent gluten-free options. Look into places like Cafe 250 for upscale dining or The Oyster Spot for seafood.
Budget: High, as you requested.

Second Option: Carmel-by-the-Sea and the Big Sur coast.
Accommodations: Stay at a luxury beachfront cottage or a spacious rental home in Carmel or Big Sur. The Carmel Valley Ranch resort is another excellent choice with pools and dining.
Getting There: Rent a large SUV or minivan.
Getting Around: Big Sur is best explored with a rental car. You'll drive the scenic Highway 1.
Swimming: You can swim at Carmel Beach or even find ocean kayaking spots.
Gluten-Free Dining: Carmel has many excellent restaurants. Call ahead to confirm Celiac accommodations at places like The French Laundry or Café Graciano. Carmel Valley Ranch has dedicated gluten-free dining.
Budget: High, as you requested.

Enjoy your well-deserved and comfortable warm-weather Northern California family vacation!
