Here's a clear example:

**Scenario:** In the Red Team's attacking half, the Blue Team defender makes a deliberate, controlled challenge on the ball. The ball deflects and bounces off the defender into an offside position where Red Team forward number 7 is closer to both the ball AND the second-to-last defender when their teammate plays it.

**In College/High School US Rules:** This is an OFFSIDE offense. Even though it was a rebound or deflection rather than controlled play, gaining an advantage from it still makes it an offense.

**In FIFA Laws of the Game:** This is NOT an offside offense. Because the defender's touch was merely a deflection/rebound and not a deliberate, controlled play resetting offside, the attacker remains offside and an offense applies.

The key difference is the definition and application of what constitutes a defender's touch resetting offside. College/high school rules currently rely more on the spirit of the law (is it a deliberate challenge or not?), while FIFA has a more precise wording requiring a "deliberate and controlled play" to truly reset it.
