To summarize that analogy for a child: 

The heart is the gas station and engine.

The lungs refuel the blood-cars with fresh oxygen-fuel.

The capillaries are the super small roads where the oxygen is exchanged.

The veins are the return roads that bring the blood-cars back to the heart-lungs.

The kidneys are the big car washes that clean the blood, removing waste, which becomes urine.

And the liver is the special service station that processes nutrients and waste from the blood.

That's how all these parts work together to power up the body using that car system analogy.
