### 🌟 The Ideal Post-Adventure Vegas Experience
1. **Lounge & Relax First**

2. **Beyond the Casinos**  
   - **Hidden Gems:**  
     - **The Neon Boneyard:** Free museum of retired Vegas signs (great for photos!).  
     - **Mob Museum:** Dark-history intrigue (tickets ~$35).  
     - **Shark Tank at Wynn:** Glamping-like cabanas over an aquarium.  
   - **Food Adventures:**  
     - **Hidden Palace:** Secret speakeasy with 1950s vibes (reservations essential).  
     - **Lago by Julian Serrano:** Rooftop Italian with fire pits (~$100/entrance).  

3. **Evening Glam**

     - **Joël Robuchon:** Michelin-starred French (tasting menu ~$300).  
     - **Absinthe:** Absurd comedy/illusion show ($85).  
   - **Dance the Night Away:**  
     - **Omnia at Caesars:** Ultra-chic club ($20 cover + bottle service).  
     - **Drai’s Beach:** Pool party with DJs (cover + open bar).  

4. **Van-Friendly Logistics**  
   - **Parking:** Many hotels offer **free valet** for overnight stays.  
   - **Pro Tip:** Use **HotelTonight** app for last-minute deals (often 30% off).  

### 🌵 Joshua Tree + Nevada Road Trip Essentials
- **Joshua Tree:** Drive from LA (2.5 hrs), park at **Barker Hot Springs** or **Hidden Valley**. Camping at **Jumbo Rocks** ($30/night) is iconic.  
- **NV Detour:** From Joshua Tree → **Death Valley** (3 hrs) → **Zabriskie Point** (otherworldly rocks) → **Tonopah** (ghost town) → **Lake Tahoe** (CA/NV border, lake swimming!).
