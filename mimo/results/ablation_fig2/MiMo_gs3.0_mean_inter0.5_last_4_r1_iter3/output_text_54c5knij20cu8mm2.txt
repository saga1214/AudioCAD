First, about the left-brain versus right-brain idea. It’s mostly a myth, but it’s not entirely false either. The brain has different regions that specialize in certain tasks, but creativity and logic aren't neatly separated into one hemisphere. It’s more about how the brain's networks communicate with each other. For example, the prefrontal cortex handles planning and logic, while the parietal lobe helps with spatial reasoning, which you might see as creative. So, while the idea of one brain side being purely creative and the other purely logical is oversimplified, there are real differences in how those parts of the brain function.
