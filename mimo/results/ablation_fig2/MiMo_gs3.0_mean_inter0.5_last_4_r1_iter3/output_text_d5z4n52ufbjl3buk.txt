That's a great approach. Here are some tips for structuring your email.

First, use a clear subject line, like "Meeting Request to Improve for Future Essays."

Then, start with a polite opening, such as, "I hope you're doing well. I scored a 64 on the recent exam and wanted to schedule some time to understand how I could improve on essay-based tests."

Next, focus on your objective by saying, "My main goal is to do my best on future tests, and I'd appreciate any feedback on what could have been enhanced in my essay."

Finally, close it by asking, "Could you let me know a 15-minute slot that might work for you next week?" and sign off with "Sincerely."

This keeps it professional, non-confrontational, and focused on your growth.
