For candy tomorrow, grab the Halloween version of classics like Skittles, gummy worms, candy corn, and Reese’s cups. You could also add chocolate bars or mini chocolates, which are usually crowd favorites. If you’re worried about allergies again, make sure to double-check labels for dye issues—plain chocolate or certain gummies without artificial colors are usually safe. It's also a good idea to stock up on the individually wrapped candies so guests can take what they want. And don’t forget to put out a spooky bowl or bucket as a fun centerpiece for the candy!
