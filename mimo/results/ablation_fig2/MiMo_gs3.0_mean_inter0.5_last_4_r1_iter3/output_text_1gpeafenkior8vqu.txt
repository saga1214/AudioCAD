### **Key Tips**  
1. **Design Elements for Slides:**  
   - Fonts: *Mincho* (traditional) or *Noto Sans* (modern).  
   - Color Scheme: *Indigo + Gold* (Edo-era prints) or *Moss Green + Cream* (nature focus).  
   - Images: Prioritize high-res digital scans from museums (e.g., Kyoto National Museum).  

2. **Time Management:**  
   - Wake up at 6:30 AM for workouts (hotel gyms).  
   - Use sightseeing downtime for voice memos or note apps.  
   - Pack a mini "research kit" (tablet, portable Wi-Fi).  

3. **Logistics:**  
   - **Hotels:** Book early-checkout hotels (e.g., Park Hyatt Tokyo) to avoid luggage issues.  
   - **Transport:** Get a **JR Pass** for Tokyo → Kyoto → Nara (saves ~$500).  
   - **Taxis:** Use apps like **Japan Taxi**; negotiate rates if using Uber.  

4. **Art History Topics:**  
   - Ceramics of Bizen or Arita (kimono patterns).  
   - Ukiyo-e masters (Hokusai’s *The Great Wave* design).  
   - Heian court paintings (simplicity/nature).
