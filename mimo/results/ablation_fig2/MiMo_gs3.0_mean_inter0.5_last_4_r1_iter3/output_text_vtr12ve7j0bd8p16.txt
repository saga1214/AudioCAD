Of course. Calgary is a great base for adventure. You could take a scenic drive along the Bow Valley Parkway. A really unique experience would be to drive up to the Fairmont Banff Springs Hotel, which is perched on a mountain and has a very romantic atmosphere. Or, for an active adventure, you could book a hot air balloon ride at sunrise, which would give you amazing views of the city and the surrounding mountains. If you're both adventurous hikers, you could check out the easy-to-maintain trails at Fish Creek Provincial Park.
