Okay, planning a dad-child theme park trip requires fuel that's hearty but minimizes park cleanup. Ten dads x Ted kids = Ted's Crew needs Ted-level organization! Your adjustments are spot-on – real satisfaction matters, heat demands extras, and cleanup demands grab-and-go genius. 

Here’s a cooler system built for ten dads, ten kids, sugar-free sodas, cheese-loving chaos, groom-centric calzones, and guilt-free treats. I’ve prioritized individual packaging so no one needs to stop or unpack heavily. 

Drinks: Hydration is king, but real dads need real soda sometimes. Compromise with healthy foundations. 
Snacks: Mess最小化 is your mantra. Single-serve wins. 
Food: Calzones anchor the main meal. Wraps offer backup. 
Desserts: Cupcakes = cake without the park cleanup. 

**Key Strategy:** Pack everything in large coolers with ice packs. Label containers clearly. Rotate coolers if groups split up. Pre-cut desserts!

**THE BACHELOUR PARTY FOR TED’S CREW pack-UP LIST**  
*(Based on 10 dads + 10 kids = 20 people)*

**DRINKS (Min-Mess Focus)**
*   **Base:** 20 reusable water bottles (one per person) + Ice Packs
*   **Healthy Kids Box:** 20 low-sugar juice boxes (Apple, Berry Blend)
*   **Adult Treat:** 20 sugar-free soda boxes (Coke, Sprite, Root Beer)
*   **Park Hydration:** 2 large jugs of cold water (10L each) + Ice Packs
*   **Optional Refreshment:** 20 single-serve coconut waters (For electrolytes if super hot)

**SNACKS (Grab & Mess Minimal)**
*   Cheese Sticks: 40 sticks (20 per dad)
* Grapes: 3 lbs pre-washed, cut into convenient bunches (10 per dad)
* Pretzels: 4 individual bags (Mini or Med) each
 Trail Mix: 4 individual bags (Nuts, seeds, dried fruit, maybe dark chocolate chips)
 Baby Carrots: 3 lbs pre-cut, 10 small bags each
 Hummus Cups: 4 single-serve cups (Individual dip pouches work best)
 Chocolate Granola Bars: 20 (Kid-friendly)
 Gummy Veggie Sticks (for picky kids): 2 packs (Fun & minimal mess)

**MAIN MEAL (Calzone Hearty & Dad-Friendly)**
*   Mini Calzones: 20! **This is KEY.** 
    *   Source: Grocery store bakery, pizza place (confirm "mini" or "bite-sized"), or local pizzeria (ask for "grab-and-go" individual boxes).
    *   Fillings: Offer variety! Pepperoni & Cheese, Veggie (Mushroom, Onion, Bell Pepper), Meatball Marinara, maybe a Chicken Parm mini. Ask group preferences!
*   Wrap/Sandwich Backup: 25 Individual Wrap/Sandwiches (Pre-cut corner for easy opening)
    *   Base: Whole Wheat Tortillas or Pita Bread
    *   Fillings: Hummus & Veggies, Turkey & Avocado, Ham & Cheese, Black Bean & Corn
    *   Include Single-Serve Ranch or Hummus cups on the side!

**DESSERTS (Cake Feel Without Park Cleanup)**
*   Cupcakes: 20! **Best Choice for Portability & Celebration.**
    *   Pick: Chocolate, Vanilla, or Funfetti. Get frosting on top.
    *   **Crucial:** Ask bakery to pack in a *large, stackable, lidded tub* (easier than individual boxes for grabbing slices later).
*   Brownie Bites: 20 (Small, individually wrapped packs) - Quick, easy, no utensils needed.

**COOLER organization TIPS:**
1.  **Ice Packs:** Place strategically at bottom/top to keep everything cold.
2.  drinks first (especially sodas/juices) to prevent dilution.
3.  Group snacks together in their own containers.
4.  Calzones and wraps go in a dedicated cooler or compartment to stay warm.
5.  Desserts go in a separate cooler (especially cupcakes/brownies) to prevent melting.
6.  Use waterproof bags or liners for any potentially drippy items (like brownie bites).
7.  Label coolers: "Main Food," "Snacks," "Drinks," "Desserts."

This plan gives everyone their favorites – carbonated drinks, satisfying calzones, cake without the chaos – while letting you dads roam the park mess-free. Have an absolutely epic theme park adventure for your best friend!
