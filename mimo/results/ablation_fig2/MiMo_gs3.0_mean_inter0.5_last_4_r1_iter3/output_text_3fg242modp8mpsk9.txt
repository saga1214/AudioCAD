Okay, since you love romance writing, real ferries sound perfect.

For a coastal trip, you could look into ferry tours that visit secluded coves. Some offer short, private excursions.

Also, many places have fresh-caught seafood markets right on the docks of the marinas you'd ferry to. You could plan a meal there.

If you want something more adventurous, you could check if the ferry company offers kayak or paddleboard rentals for when you disembark.
