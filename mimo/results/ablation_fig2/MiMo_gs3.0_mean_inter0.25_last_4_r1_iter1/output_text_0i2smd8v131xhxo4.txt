### Vocal Endurance & Wellness
1.  **Warm-ups (5-10 mins daily):** Lip trills, humming, sirens (gliding pitch), gentle tongue exercises.
2.  **Hydration:** Sip room-temperature water constantly. Avoid cold water (can constrict cords) and large gulps (swallowing causes strain).
3.  **Breathing:** Practice diaphragmatic breathing (belly breaths). Record for shorter bursts (e.g., 2-3 mins), then take 5-10 min breaks. Hydrate/stretch.
4.  **Posture:** Sit/stand tall, shoulders relaxed. Avoid slouching.
5.  **Marking Scripts:** Use colored highlighters or sticky notes to mark scene changes, emotional shifts, and technical cues.
6.  **Schedule:** Block dedicated recording, editing, and marketing time. Protect vocal rest time (no talking 30-60 mins before/after recording).

### Software & Plugins
1.  **Audacity vs. Adobe Audition:**
    *   **Audacity:** Free, lightweight, great for basics (cutting, leveling, noise reduction). Steeper learning curve for complex tasks.
    *   **Adobe Audition:** Industry standard for serious voice work. Superior editing tools (multi-track, waveform/spectral display), advanced noise reduction (Noise Reduction/Restoration effects), better multi-mixing, and smoother workflows *long-term*. **Worth switching if you do a lot of editing.** Start with Audition trials.
2.  **Reliable Mouth Click/Noise Removal:**
    *   **Adobe Audition:** Spectral Frequency Display (SFD) is incredibly powerful for surgically removing clicks/pop noise. "De-click" effect also helps.
    *   **iZotope RX:** The gold standard for audio repair. Its "De-click" and "Dynamics Processing" modules are exceptional for voice. (Paid, but offers free trials).
    *   **Audacity:** "Noise Reduction" (use a good noise sample first) and "De-click" effects work decently for basic cleanup, especially on isolated clicks.
    *   **Avoid "AI" hype:** Most "AI noise cutters" are either spectral editors with fancy names or simple gate/limiter tricks. Focus on tools like RX or Audition's SFD.

### Workflow Organization
*   **Project Folders:** Create a master "Projects" folder. Inside, make subfolders for each client or project.
*   **File Naming:** Use consistent, descriptive names (e.g., `ClientName_ProjectName_SceneX_ScriptPageY`).
*   **Versioning:** Keep raw files, edits, backups in clear subfolders (e.g., `Raw`, `Edits`, `Backups`).
*   **Time Blocking:** Dedicate specific times for recording, editing, client communication, admin, and marketing.
*   **Automation:** Use calendar apps for deadlines, reminders, and breaks.
