First, about tinnitus again. Most of the time, yes, physiological. Think hearing damage, ear conditions, that kind of physical issue. Post-traumatic links are rare unless there’s also something like hyper-vigilance or stress involved. So, probably check with an audiologist to get it diagnosed properly.

Now, that left-brain versus right-brain thing. It’s largely a myth. The brain doesn’t neatly split creative and logical functions between hemispheres. It’s more about how different networks of neurons talk to each other. But the idea is useful as a metaphor—some people tend to lean more toward structured thinking, others toward associative thinking, but it’s not a hard and fast rule.
