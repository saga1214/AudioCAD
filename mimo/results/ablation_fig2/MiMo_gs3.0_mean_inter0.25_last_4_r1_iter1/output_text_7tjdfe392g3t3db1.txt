Getting tuned really simply. For almost any stringed instrument, apps like GuitarA, Fender Tune, or another one called Andromeda work great.

Here’s how it works. First, just strum a full chord or go through and hit each string one by one. Place your phone's microphone near the strings so it can hear them.

Then, watch the app's display. It will show each string and a little arrow that points up or down. You want to keep strumming until that arrow stays flat, either pointing to the left for a little flat or to the right for a little sharp. Once it's perfectly flat, your string is in tune! You just repeat that for all the strings.
