The absolute best bedding for winter chicks on concrete is a thick layer of wood shavings. Pine or aspen shavings act as insulation, trap their body heat, and make cleaning easy. Just like before, to avoid attracting rodents, use what's known as the deep litter approach. Continuously add fresh, clean shavings on top of the composting layer. Sand is another good option, but wood shavings provide more insulation and comfort.

So, I'd recommend wood shavings for the chicks, and make sure the brooder plate is set to the right height so the chicks can get underneath it for warmth. That should get them set up for a warm first winter.
