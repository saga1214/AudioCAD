Here's a clean breakdown for your clothing gear, separating the daytime (mid-30s temp) and nighttime (final 12 miles) pacing segments:

### **Daytime 20-Mile Segment (Hilly, Mid-30s)**
1. **Base Layer:**  
   - Moisture-wicking shirt & shorts (avoid cotton!).  
   - Optional: Lightweight leggings under shorts if windy.  
2. **Mid-Layer:**  
   - Fleece or lightweight puffy jacket (adjustable for warmth).  
3. **Outer Layer:**  
   - Wind-resistant shell jacket (waterproof if wet ground possible).  
4. **Hands/Head:**  
   - Running gloves (technical, breathable).  
   - Cap or bucket hat (if sunny).  
5. **Footwear:**  
   - Trail shoes with grip for hills (e.g., Vibram soles).  
6. **Extras:**  
   - Buff or neck gaiter (for sun/wind protection).  
   - Light pack or vest (to carry layers/aids).  

---

### **Final 12-Mile Night Segment**  
*(Mid-30s but colder at night + wind)*  
1. **Base Layer:**  
   - Still moisture-wicking (ensure it’s long-sleeve for warmth).  
2. **Mid-Layer:**  
   - Fleece or lightweight puffy (keep it handy).  
3. **Outer Layer:**  
   - **Windproof/waterproof shell jacket** (e.g., Gore-Tex).  
4. **Hands/Head:**  
   - Insulated gloves (technical, windproof).  
   - Warm hat (balaclava optional if extreme cold).  
5. **Visibility:**  
   - Headlamp/handheld light + **extra batteries** (non-negotiable).  
6. **Footwear:**  
   - Same grippy trail shoes (socks: bring an extra pair if needed).  
7.Buff or neck gaiter (now for warmth, not sun).  
8. **Extras:**  
   - Insulated jacket (convertible or puffy) if temps drop fast.  

---

### **Key Tips**  
- **Aid Stations:** Use them to swap layers or gloves as needed.  
- **Packability:** Bring a vest/pack to stash layers between segments.  
- **Test Gear:** Try everything beforehand—especially gloves/hat fit.  
- **Weather Flexibility:** Mid-30s can swing fast; prioritize warmth at night.
