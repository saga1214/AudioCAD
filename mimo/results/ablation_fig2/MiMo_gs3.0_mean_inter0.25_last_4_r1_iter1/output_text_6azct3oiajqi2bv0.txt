Here are the safest ways to temporarily stick something to a winter jacket lining without damaging the fabric, based on your Nordstrom Rack choice and Arden distance:

1.  **Double-Sided Fabric Tape (Best Overall):**
    *   **Why:** Specifically designed for garments, it's strong but won't rip the fabric or leave residue if removed carefully. It's less visible than glue.
    *   **How:** Peel the backing, press firmly onto the *inside* lining where you need the item attached, smooth out bubbles, and let it set for a few hours before wearing. Use the clear adhesive kind for best results.

2.  **Fabric Glue (Use with Caution):**
    *   **Why:** Strong bond, but can be messy and potentially leave a sticky residue if not removed perfectly.
    *   **How:** Apply a *tiny* amount *only* to the backing of the item you want to stick (like a tag or small accessory) *before* pressing it onto the lining. Hold firmly for 1-2 minutes. Use a high-quality fabric glue (like E6000 or Aleene's Fabric Fusion) for best results. **Crucially:** Test on an inconspicuous seam first and be prepared to gently scrape off any excess glue if needed.

3.  **Safety Pins (Temporary Fix):**
    *   **Why:** Instant and doesn't damage the fabric permanently.
    *   **How:** Thread the pin through the item you want to stick and then through a few small, inconspicuous stitches in the lining. Don't force it through the main fabric. **Best for:** Very temporary fixes or attaching something lightweight.

4.  **Double-Sided Tape (Non-Fabric Type - Use Sparingly):**
    *   **Why:** Readily available, strong bond.
    *   **How:** **Only use if it's specifically labeled as "double-sided fabric tape" or "double-sided sticky tape for clothing."** Regular masking tape or painter's tape is too weak and will rip the fabric. If it's a true fabric tape, follow the same steps as #1.

**Avoid:**

*   **Staples:** Will absolutely tear the fabric lining.
*   **Hot Glue:** Melts fabric, causes permanent damage, and is messy.
*   **Regular Tape (Masking, Painter's, Clear Duct):** Will rip the fabric, leave residue, and is too weak.
*   **Strong Adhesives (Super Glue, Epoxy):** Will damage the fabric and lining permanently.

**Recommendation for Your Situation:**

Since shipping might take time and you're near Arden Fair, **Double-Sided Fabric Tape (#1)** is the safest bet for a quick, temporary fix that minimizes risk to the jacket lining. **Fabric Glue (#2)** is a good second choice if you have the right glue and are careful.
