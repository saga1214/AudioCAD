That line of reasoning is interesting, focusing on the DNA. However, the chicken had still come first.

Here's why: for an egg to develop the DNA that defines a chicken, a hen, which is evolutionarily very close to a chicken but not quite a chicken itself, had to produce that egg. The egg that contained the first chicken's DNA was laid by an ancestor that was only *almost* a chicken. So, the egg came before the hatched creature was definitively a chicken. The chicken itself—the first individual with that specific chicken DNA—only appeared after the egg was laid.
