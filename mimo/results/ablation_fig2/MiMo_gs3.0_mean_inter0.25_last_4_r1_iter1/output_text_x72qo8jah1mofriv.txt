Okay, kosher dietary laws are pretty specific. The main things to check are ingredients for any meat or dairy mixing, and making sure any fish or shellfish is prepared in a kosher way. So, for Tony, the weenies you brought would be fine, as long as they're not cooked with any non-kosher fats. The hummus or fruit platter is also typically okay. Blood pudding is obviously not kosher, so you'd need to find a suitable alternative there—maybe a black pudding, which is a different kind of blood sausage, or some kind of dark meat sausage. For the sweet, keeping it dairy-free would be safe, like vegan dark chocolate or cookies without butter. Pinot Noir is fine, as it's not a kosher-specific issue.
