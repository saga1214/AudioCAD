### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia (BC), Canada  
If you make an **illegal U-turn** in BC, expect penalties like:  
- **Ticket:** $110–$160 fine.  
- **Demerit Points:** 1–3 points on your license.  
- **Serious Violation:** If it causes an accident, it could escalate to "dangerous driving" charges.  

**Key Restrictions** (per BC Traffic Code):  
- ❌ **Not allowed** at intersections with traffic lights, stop signs, or yield signs.  
- ❌ **Not allowed** on curves, hills, or where visibility is limited (e.g., blind corners).  
- ❌ **Not allowed** when traffic would be obstructed.  
- ✅ **Legal** only if you can see **150 meters** in both directions, no signs prohibit it, and it doesn’t interfere with traffic.  

**Tip:** Use designated U-turn lanes or wait for clear gaps. Apps like Google Maps show legal U-turn zones—check them!

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
Here’s a classic, **authentic** recipe (no cream!):  

#### **Essentials:**  
- **Pasta:** 400g spaghetti (or bucatini).  
- **Meat:** 200g **guanciale** (cured pork jowl; substitute pancetta if needed).  
- **Eggs:** 3 large eggs + 1 extra yolk (for richness).  
- **Cheese:** 100g finely grated **Pecorino Romano** (or Parmigiano-Reggiano).  
- **Seasoning:** Freshly cracked black pepper, salt.  

#### **Optional:**  
- Garlic (minced, sautéed with meat).  
- Fresh parsley (chopped).  

#### **Key Technique:**  
- Cook pasta in salted water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
- Toss pasta with guanciale fat *off-heat* to avoid scrambling eggs.  
- Mix eggs, cheese, and pepper in a bowl. Whisk in hot pasta + reserved water to emulsify.  

Enjoy your meal! 🍝
