That's a fantastic skill to have, and it absolutely opens up more doors for you. 

If you're the author and illustrator, you can submit your work directly to children's book publishers, completely bypassing the need for an agent. You can search for publishers that accept unsolicited manuscripts, and many of them have submission guidelines on their websites. 

When you do submit, just be sure to include a clear cover letter explaining your vision for the book and why it's a good fit for them. The main thing is to just start with your first poem and build from there. Your story and your art are powerful, and the world absolutely needs to hear them.
