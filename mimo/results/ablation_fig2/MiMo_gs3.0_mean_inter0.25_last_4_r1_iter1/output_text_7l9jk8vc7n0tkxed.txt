### 🔧 Step-by-Step Deep Blockage Removal
1. **Prep the Area**  
   - Cover nearby surfaces with towels.  
   - Place a wet cloth over the drain to catch debris.  

2. **Use a Pipe Wrench (if available in your toolbox)**  
   - If you have pliers or a small pipe wrench:  
     - Insert a **plumber’s snake** (if included) or a stiff wire hanger straightened into a hook.  
     - Carefully twist it to snag debris near the drain.  
     - Pull gently — don’t force it to avoid breaking pipes.  

3. **Try a "Plumber’s Nozzle" Trick**  
   - If no tools:  
     - Use a **damp rag** to wrap around the drain opening.  
     - Apply pressure with a wrench/pliers to create suction.  
     - This can sometimes pull out stuck gunk.  

4. **Boiling Water Flush**  
   - Pour 3–4 cups of boiling water down the drain in stages (wait 30 seconds between pours).  
   - *Caution:* Avoid this if the pipe is PVC or ABS (can warp).  

5. **Manual Removal (Last Resort)**  
   - If you see debris:  
     - Use a **plumber’s putty knife** or flathead screwdriver to scrape it out.  
     - Wear gloves and a mask (debris may be moldy/greasy).  

### ⚠️ Critical Safety Tips
- **Never force metal tools** into PVC pipes — they can crack and leak.  
- If the blockage is in a **septic line** or **older cast iron**, stop. Call a professional.  
- If water backs up into other drains, the blockage is likely deeper in the line.
