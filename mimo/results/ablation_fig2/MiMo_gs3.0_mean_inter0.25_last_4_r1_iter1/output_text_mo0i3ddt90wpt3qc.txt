You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is a great gluten-free start.

Other gluten-free camp snacks you could consider are almonds, which are very portable and nutritious. Roasted chickpeas are another crunchy snack you can even bring along for a little customization by adding spices. 

And for a sweet treat, trail mix is always a good idea. Just make sure to check that any chocolate you pack is gluten-free certified.
