### **Simple Food (Easy & Crowd-Pleasing)**  
1. **Miso-Glazed Salmon Mini-Sliders**  
   - Pre-cooked, juicy mini-sliders with a sweet-savory miso glaze.  
   - Add a twist: serve with **wasabi mayo** and **cucumber ribbons**.  
2. **DIY Sweet Potato Nacho Bar**  
   - Tortilla chips + sweet potato base.  
   - Unique toppings: **miso-spiced black beans**, **pickled radish**, **yuzu aioli**, **crumbled queso fresco**, and **edible flowers**.  
3. **Matcha Popcorn Station** But go wild: butter, caramel, SPicy wasabi, *and* a **sweet-sour yuzu lemonade drizzle** option.  

### **Unique Choices (Bold, Umami-Rich & Adventurous)**  
1. **Korean-Japanese Fusion Flatbread Pizzas**  
   - Pre-baked flatbread topped with:  
     - **Kimchi seaweed & sweet-sour tamarind glaze**  
     - **Dried squid jerky + fish roe + spicy gochujang mayo**  
2. **Gourmet Charcuterie Reimagined**  
   - Japanese influences:  
     - **Unagi (eel) slices** with **toasted sesame seeds**  
     - **Truffle brie** with **pickled plum (umeboshi)**  
     - **Sansho pepper-infused cheeses** (peppery citrus kick!)  
3. **Snow Monkey Park-Inspired "Hot Spring" Snacks**  
   - Serve **dried octopus** and **kimchi seaweed snacks** alongside **soju shots** in mini cups.  
   - Pair with **tamarind-sweetened yuzu candy** for spicy-sour balance.  
4. **Mochi Taco Station**  
   - Pink or white mochi balls filled with:  
     - **Fish (sake-marinated)** or **pulled mushroom "pork"**  
     - Topped with **pickled ginger** and **tobiko (flying fish roe)**.  

### **Sweet Finale (Umami & Floral)**  
- **Yuzu Melon Parfaits**: Melon cubes + **white chocolate matcha mousse** + **edible flowers**.  
- **Sweet-Sour Fish Caviar** on rice crackers (inspired by their Japan trip souvenir!).  

### **Pro Tips**  
- **Prep Ahead**: Sliders/nacho bar toppings can be prepped days in advance.  
- **Drinks**: Offer **yuzu-ginger mocktails** or **sparkling sake**.  
- **Ambiance**: Play Japanese folk music (like *Gagagai*) or film scores (like Studio Ghibli’s *Spirited Away*) during the movie!
