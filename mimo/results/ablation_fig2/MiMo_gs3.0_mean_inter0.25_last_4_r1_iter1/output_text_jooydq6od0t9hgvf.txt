Here are quick, elegant drink recipes for your vodka and "Argentinian Fernet" cocktails — all soda-free and crowd-pleasing:

### **1. Vodka Sparkle (Soda-Free)**
- **Base**: 1.5 oz vodka + 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Sweetener**: 0.5 oz simple syrup (or 1 tsp honey)  
- **Garnish**: Lemon twist  
- **Method**: Shake vodka, lemon juice, and syrup with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Top with soda water (if allowed by allergy guest) or sparkling water. Garnish.  

*Why it works*: Bright, versatile, and fits all diets.  

---

### **2. "Argentinian Fernet" (Pisco-Fernet Style)**  
*(Inspired by the classic Pisco Fernet, simplified)*  
- **Base**: 2 oz pisco (or grape brandy if unavailable)  
- **Bitter**: 1 oz Fernet-Branca (or substitute with equal parts Fernet + Angostura bitters)  
- **Sweet**: 0.5 oz honey syrup (1:1 honey:hot water)  
- **Garnish**: Orange twist  
- **Method**: Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into an ice-filled rocks glass.  

*Why it works*: Complex, sophisticated, and perfect for mature palates. If pisco is unavailable, use brandy + 1 oz red wine.  

---

### **Pro Tips**:  
- **Allergy Safety**: Clearly label drinks. Offer sparkling water on ice as a soda alternative.  
- **Game Night Connection**: Use the same Pisco Fernet ingredients for a post-Pictionary digestif!  
- **Food Pairing**: Serve with chocolate mousse or fruit tarts to complement the bitters.
