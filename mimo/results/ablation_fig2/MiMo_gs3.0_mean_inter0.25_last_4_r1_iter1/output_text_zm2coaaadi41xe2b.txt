Dallas has some fantastic Asian noodle spots. For ramen, I'd suggest Torish, which has amazing tonkotsu and shoyu. For a more casual spot, there's Kameoka in Little Tokyo. If you're craving Vietnamese noodle bowls, Pho 79 on North Dallas is a must. And for Korean-style ramen, there's a place called Gukbap House that does really good tteokbokki and ramen.

As for protein bars, it depends on your taste. If you want a good sweet option, Quest has a cookies and cream flavor that’s pretty good. If you prefer something more savory, Optimum Nutrition has a chocolate peanut butter one. You could also try a bar from Clif Builder's, they have a peanut butter chocolate one that’s decent. Just make sure to check the macros for what you need—low sugar or high protein, etc. Hope you enjoy the food!
