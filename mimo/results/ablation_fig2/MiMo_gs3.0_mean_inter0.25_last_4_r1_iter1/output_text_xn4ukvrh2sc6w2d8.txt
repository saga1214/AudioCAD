Here are the classic recipes with precise ingredients and bartender instructions for your event:

### 1. **Old Fashioned**
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon or rye  
- 1 sugar cube *or* ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube (or add simple syrup) in a mixing glass.  
2. Add bourbon and bitters, fill with ice.  
3. Stir vigorously for 30 seconds until chilled.  
4. Strain into a **rocks glass over fresh ice**.  
5. Express orange twist over drink (squeeze oils onto surface), discard peel.  
*Optional:* Garnish with a cherry.  

---

### 2.**Paper Plane**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz bourbon  
- 0.75 oz Aperol  
- 0.75 oz Amaro Nonino  
- 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a **coupe glass filled with fresh ice**.  
*Garnish:* Cherry or lemon twist.  

---

### 3. **Whiskey Sour**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- 1 egg white (optional, for texture)  

**Instructions:**  
**With egg white:**  
1. Combine egg white, bourbon, lemon juice, and syrup in a shaker.  
2. **Dry shake** (no ice) for 15 seconds to emulsify egg white.  
3. Add ice, shake again for 10 seconds.  
4. Strain into a **rocks glass over ice**.  
*Garnish:* Cherry or orange slice.  

**Without egg white:** Skip dry shake; shake with ice and strain as above.  

---

### 4. **Aviation**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz maraschino liqueur  
- ¼ oz crème de violette  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a **cocktail glass filled with ice**.  
*Garnish:* Maraschino cherry.  

---

### 5. **Third Gin Cocktail: French 75** (Art Deco-themed sparkling option)  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz gin  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 3 oz chilled prosecco or champagne  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a **chilled champagne flute**.  
3. Top with prosecco/champagne.  
*Garnish:* Lemon twist.  

---
