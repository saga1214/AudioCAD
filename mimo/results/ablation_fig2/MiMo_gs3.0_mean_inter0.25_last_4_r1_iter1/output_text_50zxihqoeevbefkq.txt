Absolutely! Here's a comprehensive shopping list organized by category, based on our menu planning. I've included quantities as estimates for 24 people (20 adults + 4 kids) and added essentials for hosting.

### **Appetizers/Finger Foods**  
- **Sliders:** 1 lb ground pork/beef (mini slider meat), slider buns  
- **Shrimp Cocktail:** 1 lb raw shrimp, cocktail sauce  
- **Pigs in a Blanket:** 1 lb deli ham/thanksgiving ham, cream cheese, mustard, toothpicks  
- **Add-ons:** Mini cheese & crackers, pickles, olives, sour cream  

### **Drinks**  
- **Champagne:** 1 bottle (for midnight toast)  
- **Adults:**  
  - 1 bottle vodka, 1 bottle gin, 1 bottle rum  
  - 1 bottle wine (red or white), soda/lemonade  
  - Ice (1 large bag), plastic cups  
- **Kids & Others:**  
  - 2 bottles grape juice (12 oz each)  
  - Sparkling water, lemonade, soda  
- **Extras:** Ice, water bottles, large pitchers  

### **Main Course**  
- **Ham:** 1 large pre-cooked ham (12–14 lbs)  
- **Scalloped Potatoes:** 2 lbs potatoes, 1 cup cream cheese, 1 cup sour cream, 1 cup milk, 4 cloves garlic (minced), salt, pepper  
- **Asparagus:** 1 bunch asparagus, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper  
- **Roasted Carrots:** 2 lbs carrots (sliced), olive oil, salt, pepper (replaces glazed carrots)  

### **Kid-Friendly Extras**  
- Festive plastic cups (for grape juice signature cups)  
- Noisemakers or grape juice-themed balloons/stickers  
- Snacks: Mini fruit cups, cheese sticks, crackers  

### **Host Essentials**  
- Paper towels, trash bags  
- Napkins (plenty!)  
- Disposable plates, cups, utensils  
- Snacks for you (e.g., chips, cookies)  

### **Pro Tips**  
1. **Quantities:** Add 10–20% extra for drinks/snacks.  
2. **Prep Ahead:** Make scalloped potatoes, asparagus, and noisemakers the day before.  
3. **Kid Zone:** Set up a grape juice station with flutes and lemon wedges—it’ll feel like a “大人饮品” moment!  
4. **Leftovers:** Ham and scalloped potatoes reheat well.
