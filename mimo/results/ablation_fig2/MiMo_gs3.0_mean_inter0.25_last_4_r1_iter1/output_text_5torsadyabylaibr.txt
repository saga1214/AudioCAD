For a classic Thanksgiving pie, a very doable option is a pre-made crust pumpkin pie. The store-bought crust saves a huge amount of time, and you can still make it taste great.

Or, if you'd like to make something from scratch, a simple apple crisp is a wonderful choice. It's just a batter of flour, sugar, and oats, which you pour over sliced apples. No baking is required; you just bake it in the oven.

And if you want to go for a more challenging but impressive dessert, a graham cracker pie is a great option. You can make the crust with graham crackers and then top it with a simple sweetened cream cheese filling.
