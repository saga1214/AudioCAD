For an end-of-year celebration, a hybrid approach works great—combining the fun of an outdoor park event with a safety net for bad weather. Here’s a simple, inclusive plan:

### 🎉 **Hybrid Celebration Plan**
1. **Location**:  
   - **Primary**: Park pavilion (covered) for barbecues, ice cream sundaes, or pizza.  
   - **Backup**: Cafeteria if rain threatens—use it for a "patch work party" (decorating sashes with markers/glitter).

2. **Activities**:  
   - **Food**: Sundae bar (dairy-free dips!) + pizza slices. Let parents donate snacks to cover costs.  
   - **Games**: Quick, low-energy versions of three-legged races or sack races. Add a "patch relay" (running with badge designs).  
   - **Recognition**: Award patches/ribbons for earned badges. Display sashes proudly!

3. Parents’ Role:  
   - **Snacks**: Rotate parents bringing desserts (e.g., dairy-free brownie bites for speaker).  
   - **Patches**: Charge a small dues ($10–$15) for activity costs (games, decorations), but cover patches—parents can contribute materials or skills (e.g., a parent crafts badges).  
   - **Labor**: Assign 2–3 parents as " helpers" for setup/cleanup.

### 💡 **Why This Works**
- **Speaker’s Needs**: Barbecues allow chips and dip (with dairy-free dip!). Cafeteria option avoids pregnancy stress.  
- **Budget**: Parent-brought snacks/donations keep costs low. Dues cover only essential supplies.  
- **Memorability**: Celebrates achievements while keeping it light—no huge camping trips needed yet!  

You’ve already mapped a solid year—this finale will show your daughters (and troop!) how proud you are. You’ll be just fine! 😊
