It can be confusing, but let me clarify. The standard one and a quarter to one to one ratio is actually the Boulevardier. Your friend’s equal parts version is also traditional and very common. The drink you mentioned, Last Word, is indeed the one made with equal parts. For that, you'll need three-quarters of an ounce of gin, three-quarters of an ounce of green Chartreuse, three-quarters of an ounce of maraschino liqueur, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lime juice. Stir and strain, garnish with lime zest. Enjoy either one!
