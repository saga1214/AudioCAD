9.  **Front Rise:** Distance from crotch to desired waistband height.
10. **Hip Ease:** Extra room added at the hips for sitting comfort (more than 1" for tall clients).

### **Inseam Notes (Take Every Fitting)**
12. **Front Inseam:** From top of waistband down the inner leg to ankle bone.
13. **Back Inseam:** From top of waistband down the outer leg to ankle bone.
