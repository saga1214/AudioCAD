You're right, it really depends on the specific context. In sunny regions, solar is probably best. In windy areas, it's wind. And where there's good hydropower potential, it's hydropower. If I absolutely had to pick just one that I could deploy efficiently almost everywhere, I’d still lean towards solar, thanks to falling costs and scalability. But if we’re only considering sources with existing infrastructure, then hydropower is a strong contender. So, it really depends on the situation.
