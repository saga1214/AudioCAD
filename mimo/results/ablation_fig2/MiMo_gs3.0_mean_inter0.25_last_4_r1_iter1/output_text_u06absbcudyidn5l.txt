Of course, I can clarify that. Chronic skin redness can definitely be caused by many things. Besides rosacea and sun damage we mentioned before, it could also be rosacea itself, long-term eczema, genetics, contact dermatitis, or even allergies.

As for vasculitis, let me explain it a bit differently. It's an autoimmune condition where your immune system mistakenly attacks your blood vessels, causing inflammation. Because the affected blood vessels can be of different sizes—large, medium, or small—the symptoms depend on where the inflammation is. Symptoms can include fever, fatigue, and rashes. Diagnosis usually involves a physical exam, blood tests, and often a skin biopsy.

And yes, because vasculitis can affect the scalp and nails, it could contribute to weaker hair and nails. Treatment aims to control the immune response, and the biggest risk is that the inflammation can narrow the vessels, which can cause serious issues like organ damage if blood flow is restricted.
