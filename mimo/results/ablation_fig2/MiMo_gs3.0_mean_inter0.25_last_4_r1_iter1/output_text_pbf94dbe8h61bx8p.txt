Okay, you've got macramé as your passion craft and baking experience, plus a big family to consider. Let's build a Christmas gift plan around that love for craft while keeping it manageable.

Since you could open a macramé store day in/day out, plant hangers are definitely strong contenders still! But we can adapt them:
*   **Plant Hanger Add-Ons:** Make small macram️ grommets, tags, or even simple gourd hangers. The older kids (9-15) could grommet hangers onto pre-made planters, while younger ones glue on tags.
*   **Scale Down:** Make mini hangers for succulents or small windowsill plants.
*   Keychain Hangers: Turn them into tiny wall hangings or keychains using a small planter pot or a decorative rock.

For the cookie situation, drop cookies are smart! Here's a refined plan:
1.  **Set Up Stations:**
    *   **Mixing Station:** Dough bowl, scoops, bowls of toppings (sprinkles, chocolate chips, nuts). *Older kids manage this.*
    *   **Scooping Station:** Pastry bags or ziplock bags with snipped corners. *Middle kids handle this.*
    *   **Decorating Station:** Baked cookies, bowls of frosting, sprinkles, etc. *Younger kids do this.*
    *   **Tasting Station:** (Keep it fun but controlled!).
2.  **Prep is Key:** Measure dough, toppings, and even pre-roll dough logs. This minimizes mess during the actual baking.
3.  **Music & Time Limits:** Set a timer for each station to keep it moving.

Getting all five ages involved is the biggest challenge. Your station idea is perfect! Assign each kid a primary "boss" station for 15-20 minutes. Rotate them through. The 2-year-old can do simple tasks like stirring a little or sprinkling *after* the main decoration is done. The 15-year-old can help manage supplies or even do a more complex decorating station.

Since you hate the glue gun, let's find alternatives to Santa Claus globes:
*   **Salt Dough Ornaments:** Shape, bake, paint, decorate. Very tactile, no glue. *All ages can participate.*
*   **Finger Painting Cards:** Use washable paint on cardstock. Kids dip fingers and create snowflakes, trees, etc. *Meaningful, simple, no glue.*
*   **Simple Knitted Scarf:** If you enjoy macramé, knitting is similar! A basic scarf for a loved one. *Requires your focus but is portable.*
*   **Pine Cone Bird Feeders:** Exactly as you thought! It's outdoor fun, uses natural materials, and the peanut butter/seeds combo won't spoil quickly outdoors. *Very engaging for all ages.*

Putting it all together, here's a potential Christmas plan:
*   **Gift 1 (Your Passion):** Macramé Add-Ons (grommets, tags) for plant hangers or small gourd hangers. *You lead, they assist.*
*   **Gift 2 (Family Fun):** The Drop Cookie Project using stations. *You manage prep, they create.*
*   **Gift 3 (Simple & Meaningful):** Salt Dough Ornaments or Fingerprint Cards. *Family craft.*
*   **Gift 4 (Picky-Eater Friendly):** Pine Cone Bird Feeders. *Outdoor activity.*
*   Macramé plant hangers themselves could be gifts for close family or friends if you have the time and supplies.

This plan leans into your strengths (macramé, baking basics) while making crafts manageable for a busy family. It offers variety and keeps everyone engaged!
