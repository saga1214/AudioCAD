I understand. Flu can definitely make you feel foggy.

So, for a cough, you can try a spoonful of honey, or gargling with warm salt water. And propping your head up with an extra pillow at night can help you sleep better.

For general brain fog, the best things are still hydration and rest.

Your doctor didn't give an antibiotic precisely because the flu is viral, and that treatment wouldn't work. Hope that helps.
