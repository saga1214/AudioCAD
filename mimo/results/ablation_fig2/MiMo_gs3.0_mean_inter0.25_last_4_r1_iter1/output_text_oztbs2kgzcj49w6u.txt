Yes, there are some wonderful ways to experience the history of the Erie Canal and the Lockport locks.

First, you could visit the Erie Canal Museum in Lockport. It's located right at the site of the locks and has excellent exhibits on the history of the canal, including the spy story. It's a great place to learn and even has some interactive displays.

Another great option is the Lockport Underground Railroad Center. It's a very moving and important museum that covers the history of the Underground Railroad, which was centered in Lockport during the 19th century. It gives a powerful perspective on the area's past.

And finally, if you want to learn more about the Adirondacks without the need to actually get on the water, you could focus on hiking. You could visit the Paul Smith College in the Adirondacks, which is known for its beautiful campus and its research into the region's ecology. You could also explore the High Peaks Wilderness area, which has incredible trails for hiking without any need to be near the lakes.
