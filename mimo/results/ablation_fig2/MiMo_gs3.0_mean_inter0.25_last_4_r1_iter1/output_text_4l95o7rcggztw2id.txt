Here’s a nautical-themed, allergy-conscious shopping list for a sand-free-friendly nautical party at the beach. All items avoid watermelon and are easy for kids to handle mess-free:

### **Main Course**  
- **Pirate’s “Buried Treasure” Mini Sliders**  
  - 12 small slider rolls (butter, whole wheat, or gluten-free if needed)  
  - Fillings: Cheese slices, ham/crab salad (optional: add a sprinkle of "dirt" with crushed crackers)  
  - *Theme Tip*: Cut into 6 pieces and serve in mini treasure chests or on “ship masts” (tortilla sticks)  

### **Appetizers/Snacks**  
- **“Jewel” Fruit Skewers**  
  - Grapes, pineapple chunks, melon balls (skip watermelon!), and berries  
- **“Gold” Cheese & Crackers**  
  - Mini mozzarella balls, cheddar cubes, and goldfish crackers  
- **“Ship Rig” Pretzel Rods**  
  - Pretzel rods with peanut butter dip (check allergy)  
- **“Mermaid Tail” Veggie Sticks**  
  - Carrot coins, cucumber rounds, bell pepper strips (dipped in hummus)  

### **Drinks**  
- Individual juice boxes (apple, berry, orange)  
- Small water bottles (clear or pirate-themed labels)  
- *Allergy Note*: Confirm watermelon-free ingredients in juices  

### **Sand-Free Packaging & Extras**  
- **Pirate Treasure Chest Trays**  
  - Small wooden boxes to hold sliders/snacks  
- **Wooden Cones**  
  - For dipping snacks (look like pirate hats!)  
- **Raffia & String**  
  - Bundle skewers/cones for a “pirate loot” feel  

### **Allergy-Safe Notes**  
- Double-check labels for watermelon, peanuts, and other common allergens.  
- Prepackage everything sealed to avoid cross-contamination.  

### **Optional Extras**  
- **“Saltwater Taffy”** (for sweet snacks)  
- **Mini Gummy Sharks** (for dessert)  

**Why this works**:  
- **Sand-free**: All items are bite-sized, sealed, or easy to hold.  
- **Themed**: Names, packaging, and shapes tie into pirates/ships.  
- **Effortless**: Pre-cut sliders and skewers save time.  

Total cost: ~$50–$70 for 6–8 kids (adjust portions). Have fun, Captain! 🏴‍☠️⚓
