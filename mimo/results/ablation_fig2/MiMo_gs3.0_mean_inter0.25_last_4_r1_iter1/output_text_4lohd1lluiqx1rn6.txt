Based on global reputation and sedan lineup strength, the **top three car brands known for sedans** are generally considered to be:

1.  **Toyota:** Renowned for reliability, durability, and offering a vast range of sedans across segments (compact, midsize, luxury) like the Camry, Corolla, Avalon, and Lexus models (ES, LS, NX, RX).
2.  **Honda:** Highly respected for balancing performance, efficiency, and practicality. Key sedans include the Accord, Civic, and the luxury-oriented Acura models (TLX, Integra, RDX).
3.  **BMW:** Synonymous with premium sedans, known for performance, driving dynamics, and luxury. The BMW 3 Series, 5 Series, and 7 Series are iconic globally.

**Honorable Mentions (Also Excellent Sedan Offerings):**

*   **Subaru:** Strong reputation for reliable, all-wheel-drive sedans like the Impreza and Legacy.
*   **Lexus:** Toyota's luxury division, with outstanding sedans like the ES, LS, and UX.
*   **Hyundai:** Offers a wide range of well-regarded sedans, especially in the midsize segment ( Sonata, Santa Fe sedan) and luxury (Genesis G70, G80).
*   **Mercedes-Benz:** Another leader in luxury sedans (C-Class, E-Class, S-Class).
*   **Volkswagen:** Popular sedans like the Passat andcc (in Europe).
*   **Ford:** Historically strong with sedans like the Fusion/Mustang sedan (now largely discontinued in favor of SUVs, but still respected).

**Why these three stand out:**

*   **Toyota & Honda:** Dominate in volume, reliability, and broad appeal across mainstream segments. They offer sedans for nearly every practical need and budget.
*   **BMW:** Dominates the premium segment, setting the benchmark for performance and luxury sedans.

**Which one is "best" depends on your priorities:**

*   **Reliability & Value:** Toyota and Honda are often top choices.
*   **Performance & Luxury/Sportiness:** BMW is the clear leader.
*   **All-Wheel Drive & Capability:** Subaru excels here.
*   **Luxury:** BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and Lexus/Acura are the primary contenders.

So, while Toyota, Honda, and BMW represent the broadest strength and most iconic names in sedans globally, other brands like Subaru, Lexus, Hyundai, Mercedes-Benz, and Volkswagen also have very strong and respected sedan lineups.
