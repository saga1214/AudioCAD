Ah, budget is key. For food, you can make things like veggie wraps or whole wheat sandwiches, which are often cheaper. For drinks, sticking with water and herbal teas again is always a safe and low-cost bet. For desserts, you can get creative. Homemade oatmeal raisin cookies are usually pretty cheap and easy to make. You could also do small fruit cups or even have family members bring their favorite healthy snacks to share. Another idea is to bake a big batch of muffins, maybe some low-sugar ones, and you can cut them up to serve. This way, you can keep costs down while still offering something sweet.
