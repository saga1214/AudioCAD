Since you're likely to be anchored near Secret Harbour Beach Resort or Gallows Point Resort, here are fantastic **beach and daytime activities** right in Cruz Bay and nearby on St. John:

### Beach Hopping & Snorkeling:
1. **Coki Beach**: A short walk from Secret Harbour. Known for its calm, shallow waters and vibrant coral reefs teeming with fish—perfect for snorkeling (gear rental available nearby).  
2. **Trunk Bay**: Take a short boat ride (10–15 mins) to this iconic spot. Rent snorkel gear and dive into the underwater observatory (a glass-bottom boat platform) or explore the coral gardens.  
3. **Sage Reef**: A quick boat trip (5 mins) to this protected reef. Famous for nurse sharks, rays, and colorful fish—ideal for beginners.  

### Land-Based Activities:
4. **Kayaking in Cruz Bay**: Rent kayaks at Gallows Point or Cruz Bay Boathouse. Paddle through mangroves, past beaches, and under swaying palms.  
5. **Cruz Bay Shops**: Browse local art galleries, bookstores, and beachwear shops. Grab smoothies or snacks at **Ocean 362** (right at Gallows Point).  
6. **Beachcombing & Relaxing**: Secret Harbour’s private bay is great for swimming, reading, or people-watching. Pack a picnic and enjoy sunset views.  

### Nearby Adventures (Short Boat Trips):
7. **Honeymoon Beach**: A quieter bay with soft sand and snorkeling spots.  
8. **Cruz Bay Farmers Market (Saturdays)**: Local crafts, produce, and music—support small businesses and sample island flavors.  

### Birding Hotspots:
- **Trunk Bay’s Coral Garden**: Look for **blue-headed parrots**, **tropicbirds**, and **woodpeckers** perched on palms.  
- **Secret Harbour’s Mangroves**: Spot **herons**, **gulls**, and **wood ducks** near the water’s edge.
