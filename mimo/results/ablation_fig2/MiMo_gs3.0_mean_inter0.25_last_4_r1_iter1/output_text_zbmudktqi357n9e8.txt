Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration. Timing is key though. Mexico doesn't really have a distinct autumn season like, say, the Northern Hemisphere. But the colors can still be just as vibrant! The best time would be during the spring, from about March to May. That's when you get those beautiful flowers, blooming trees, and really bright, sunny skies. You'll see a lot of yellow, pink, and white flowers, which are perfect watercolor subjects. Summer is super hot, so maybe avoid that if you're trying to paint outdoors. But spring is ideal for those lush, colorful landscapes. Just pack your paints and enjoy the view!
