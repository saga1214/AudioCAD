Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your key items, prioritizing safety and efficiency:

**1. TV Mounting:**
   - **Measure & Plan:** Use a level to mark where the TV will sit. Measure arm length and depth for the mount. *Do NOT drill yet.*
   - **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder (or hammer test) to mark anchor points. Mounts require 2-3 studs per bracket.
   - **Drill Pilot Holes:** If no studs, drill holes for wall anchors (use appropriate anchors for your wall type—drywall or concrete).
   - **Install Mount:** Secure bracket to studs/anchors with provided screws. Test alignment before attaching TV.

**2. Hanging Planters:**
   - **Choose Lightweight Planter:** Opt for ceramic or plastic pots if possible. Avoid heavy terracotta.
   - **Use Command Strips:** For low-weight plants (under 5 lbs), use heavy-duty hooks + Command strips. *Test grip first.*
   - **Drill Securely (If Needed):** For heavier plants, drill holes into wall studs and use toggle bolts + screws. Ensure plants won’t overhang excessively.

**3. Floating Shelves:**
   - **Measure & Level:** Place shelf horizontally where desired. Use a level to mark placement.
   - **Drill Pilot Holes:** Aim for studs. If missing, use heavy-duty drywall anchors or toggle bolts.
   - **Install & Secure:** Attach shelf with long screws. *Never rely on glue alone.*
   - **Weight Test:** Place books on the shelf to test stability. Ensure no sagging.

**Pro Tips:**
- **Drill Smart:** Use a stud finder with drill mode. Always wear safety goggles.
- **Avoid Noise:** Work during the day to comply with quiet hours.
- **Tools Needed:** Drill, screws, level, measuring tape, stud finder, anchors (if needed).
- **When in Doubt:** Hire a pro for heavy installations (e.g., TV mounts > 50 lbs).

**Decorating Schedule:** Dedicate Sundays to installations. Work 2-3 focused hours per project. Celebrate small wins! 🎉
