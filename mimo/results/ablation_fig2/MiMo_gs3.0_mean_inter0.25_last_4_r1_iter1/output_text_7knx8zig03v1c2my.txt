That's wonderful you settled on **clicker training**! It's one of the most effective and precise methods for teaching new behaviors. Here's how it works and some treat recommendations:

### **Clicker Training Explained**
1. **The Clicker**: A simple plastic device that makes a sharp, consistent "click" sound. It acts as a **mark** – telling your puppy *exactly* the split-second a behavior is correct.  
2. **The Sequence**:  
   - **Step 1**: Click → **Instantly** give a high-value treat.  
   - **Step 2**: Repeat until your puppy understands the click = reward.  
   - **Step 3**: Gradually add a verbal cue (e.g., "sit") *after* the click/treat.  
3. **Why it works**: The click bridges the gap between behavior and reward faster than just using a treat. It teaches "this is the exact moment I get a reward!"  

### **Treat Recommendations**
- **Best Flavors**:  
  - **Peanut Butter**: Tiny bits (low-fat is best).  
  - **Cooked Chicken**: High-value for most dogs.  
  - **Cheese (small cubes)**: Use sparingly.  
  - **Commercial Training Treats**: Small, soft, and inexpensive (e.g., Zuke's, freeze-dried liver).  
- **Avoid**: Chocolate, grapes, onions, or anything toxic.  
- **Size**: Always give tiny treats (about the size of a pea) to avoid obesity.  

### **Example: Teaching "Sit" with Clicker**
1. Hold a treat near your puppy’s nose.  
2. Slowly move it upward and back over their head.  
3. **The moment their bottom touches the ground**, click! → Give a treat.  
4. Repeat 10–20 times per session.  
5. Gradually add the cue "sit" *after* the click/treat.  

### **Key Tips**
- **Short sessions**: 5–10 minutes, 2–3 times/day.  
- **Be consistent**: Always click/treat for the *exact* behavior.  
- **Fade treats**: Switch to fewer treats as the behavior gets reliable.  
- **Troubleshooting**: If your puppy stops trying, make rewards more exciting or simplify the task.  

Clicker training builds trust and makes learning fun! For "potty training," if accidents happen:  
1. **Clean immediately** with an enzymatic cleaner (e.g., Nature’s Miracle).  
2. **No punishment**—just redirect to the potty spot.  
3. **Reward** heavily when they go outside.
