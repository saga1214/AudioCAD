You've made an excellent point—my phrasing was misleading. If this is truly your *only* meal of the day, consuming 450mg of sodium in that portion would actually be **very reasonable** (about 20% of the general daily sodium limit of ~2,300mg).

However, sodium can still be a concern in this context because:
1. **Individual needs vary**: If you have hypertension, kidney issues, or are on medication, even moderate sodium intake may need restriction.
2. **Cumulative effect**: If you have other sources (like processed foods, salty snacks, or restaurant meals throughout the day), this portion could contribute to excess.
3. **Blood pressure impact**: Excess sodium causes fluid retention, which can raise blood pressure—even in a single large meal.

### How to reduce sodium in your meal:
- **Sauce swap**: Replace oyster and soy sauce entirely with low-sodium versions, or use 1g each.
- **Tomato swap**: Use 200g of fresh cherry + canned tomatoes instead of all tinned.
- **Beef prep**: Trim excess fat from brisket (salt can migrate to surface during cooking).
