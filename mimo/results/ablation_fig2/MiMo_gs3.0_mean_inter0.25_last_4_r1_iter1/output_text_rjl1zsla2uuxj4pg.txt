### 🧴 **Grooming & Hygiene**
- **Cat Brush**: Great for detangling and distributing oils (e.g., rubber brushes or silicone tools).
- **Nail Clippers**: Keep them trimmed to prevent scratching furniture.
- **Wet Food**: Many cats prefer it, and it’s healthier than dry food alone.

### 🛋️ **Comfort & Enrichment**
- **Cat Tree/Scratching Post**: A must-have for climbing, perching, and scratching (short-haired cats still love this!).
- **Window Perch**: Lets them watch birds/insects outside while staying safe indoors.
- **Interactive Toys**: Wand toys, puzzle feeders, or feather teasers to keep them engaged.

### 🧺 **Hygiene & Waste**
- **Extra Litter Box**: Have one per cat (or +1) to reduce accidents.
- **Waste Bag Dispenser**: For easy outdoor disposal of used litter.
- **Litter Scoop**: A scoop with fine mesh helps catch clumps and reduce dust.

### 💤 **Sleep & Security**
- **Cozy Bed**: A flat bed for short-haired cats (e.g., fleece or memory foam).
- **Carrier**: If you haven’t bought one yet (great for travel or vet visits).

### 🌟 **Pro Tip**
Short-haired breeds shed easily—regular brushing (1-2x/week) will save you from hairballs and carpets!
