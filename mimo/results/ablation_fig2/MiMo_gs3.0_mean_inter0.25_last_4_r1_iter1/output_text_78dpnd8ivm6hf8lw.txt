Here's a concise recap using your child-friendly analogy:

1. **Heart**: The gas station AND engine. Pumps blood (cars) everywhere and fills them with oxygen "fuel" from the lungs.
2. **Lungs**: Fresh fuel depot. Blood cars stop here to load up on oxygen.
3. **Main Arteries**: Big highways. Carry blood *away* from the heart to deliver oxygen to muscles/organs.
4. **Capillaries**: Tiny side streets. Where blood cars *deliver* oxygen to cells and pick up waste.
5. **Veins**: Return roads. Carry oxygen-poor blood *back* to the heart/lungs to refuel.
6. **Kidneys**: Car wash/junkyard. Filter waste and extra water out of the blood cars, turning it clean again.
7. **Liver**: Service station. Processes nutrients (like sugar/fat) from blood cars, storing some and breaking down harmful stuff.

**The whole system works like this:**  
Blood cars start at the heart, load oxygen at the lung gas station, drive highways/streets to power cells, return to the heart/kidneys to get cleaned, and start the journey all over again! 🏁💨
