Of course! Snowshoeing is a great way to explore. You'll need snowshoes, of course, and good waterproof boots with traction. A warm, insulated jacket, gloves, and a hat are essential to stay warm. Carrying a map or your phone for navigation is also a good idea. Beyond that, you can always return to ice fishing you mentioned earlier, or cross-country skiing, which is similar but on groomed trails. If you have kids, building a snowman or having a snow fort contest are always classics. There are plenty of ways to enjoy the cold weather if you dress right and stay safe.
