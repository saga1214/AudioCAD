Initally, you could schedule a one-on-one meeting through an email. Something simple like, 'When would be a good time to meet to discuss my career growth and contributions?'

This puts the conversation on the calendar and reduces any surprise anxiety.

Then, during the meeting, you can present your case using data. For instance, you could say something like, 'Since I've been here for a year, I've completed over twenty major projects, and my performance reviews have been consistently positive. I was hoping to discuss my career path and potential for growth.' This approach is proactive, confident, and well-supported.
