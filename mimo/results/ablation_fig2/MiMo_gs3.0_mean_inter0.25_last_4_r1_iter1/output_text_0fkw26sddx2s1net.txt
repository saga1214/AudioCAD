Of course. For miso soup or cucumber salad: you'll need cucumber, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and black sesame seeds. If he prefers the cucumber salad, you can skip buying miso paste. 

For chicken teriyaki main course: you'll need chicken breasts or thighs, soy sauce without legumes—like tamari or coconut aminos to be safe—a little brown sugar, and some ginger and garlic. Serve that all with jasmine rice and your stir-fried veggies, which could just be things like bell peppers, carrots, and snap peas. Sesame oil and rice vinegar would be good for seasoning the veggies.

And for dessert: sticky jasmine or glutinous rice, fresh mango, matcha powder if you decide to make that instead, and coconut milk plus sugar for the dessert sauce.

Oh, and for the matcha ice cream, you'll need heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and of course, some good quality matcha powder. A little vanilla extract is also nice to add.
