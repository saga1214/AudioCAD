That's a great way to approach it. You can certainly strike a balance between respect and being clear about your concerns.

Here are a few tips for your email:

First, start with a clear subject line, something like 'Course Name and Topic Exam Feedback Request'.

Then, in the body, introduce yourself by name and state your purpose directly but politely. You could say: 'I scored a 64 on my latest exam and was hoping to understand what I could improve on in my essay to perform better in the future. Subjective grading can be difficult to evaluate, and I'd value your feedback.'

To keep it professional and respectful, avoid any language that sounds accusatory, like 'unfairly' or 'huge ego.' Stick to your goal of learning and improving.

Finally, ask for a convenient time to talk. You could add a note at the end about his busy schedule and ask if his office hours don't work, if another time might be better.

This approach keeps the focus on your growth, which gives you the best chance for a positive outcome.
