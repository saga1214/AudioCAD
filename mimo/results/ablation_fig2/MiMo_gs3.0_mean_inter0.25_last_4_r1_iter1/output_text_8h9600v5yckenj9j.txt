For Halloween candy, you’ll definitely want to get classics like Reese’s Cups, Hershey’s Kisses, and Snickers bars, as those are almost always a hit. Twix and M&M’s are also popular. If you want something spooky-themed, you could look for candy shaped like ghosts, zombies, or pumpkins, but make sure they’re from trusted brands. You could also get some gummy worms or eyeballs for fun. Just try to avoid anything with artificial dyes due to your earlier concern. You might also consider getting some full-size candy bars instead of mini ones, as people tend to prefer those. And don’t forget to put out a bowl for guests to take candy home with them—everyone loves that!
