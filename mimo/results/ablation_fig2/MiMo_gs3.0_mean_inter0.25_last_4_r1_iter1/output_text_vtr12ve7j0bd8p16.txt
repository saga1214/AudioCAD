You're very kind. Calgary is a great base for adventures. For a romantic weekend nearby, Banff is the classic choice. It's about an hour and a half's drive and you have beautiful options like the Banff Gondola for stunning views, or a walk on the Bow Falls. A little closer is Canmore, which has its own beautiful gondola and is a bit more relaxed.

For a longer trip, you could drive about three hours to Fernie, which has a world-class ski resort in the winter or beautiful hiking in the summer. A shorter drive, about an hour and a half, takes you to Kananaskis Country, which is fantastic for hiking and has a lovely little town called Canmore. If you're up for a longer drive, about four hours, you can get to the Kootenay region with its own ski resort and beautiful lakes. A great option for a bit of everything is the Canmore-Corbet Lake area, which is about two hours away and has a wonderful mix of lakes, hiking, and biking.

I hope you two have a wonderful second date and a fantastic adventure together.
