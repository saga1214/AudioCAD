Bruno Mars-free zone means apples, bananas, and broccoli are a no-go.

But you could try steamed green peas, as they are safe and nutritious.

Spinach is another good choice, just make sure to blanch it first to remove any harmful oxalates.

Kale is packed with vitamins and minerals, and sprouted lentils are also a high-quality protein source.

Finally, green beans are a crunchy and easy-to-digest option.
