That's fantastic! Planning your dream Costa Rica trip around scuba diving is exciting. Here’s a step-by-step plan to make it unforgettable, tailored to your interests:

### 1. **Choose Your Base & Region**
   - **For Warm Coral Reefs & Tropical Fish**: **Golfo Dulce (Pacific side)** near **Puerto Viejo de Talamanca** or **Manuel Antonio**.  
     - *Why*: Crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral reefs, and abundant marine life (turtles, rays, humpback whales in winter).  
     - *Key Site*: **Cocora Reef** (near Puerto Viejo) – shallow, easy dives with schools of fish and turtles.  
   - **For Strong Currents & Big Animals**: **Caribbean side** near **Playa Conchal (Tamarindo)** or **Ostional (Cocos Island)**.  
     - *Why*: Stronger currents, deeper walls, and opportunities to see sharks, manta rays, and whale sharks (especially in Cocos Island).  
     - *Key Site*: **Cocos Island** (requires a boat trip; famous for whale sharks and blue-footed boobies).  

### 2. **Dive Schedule & Highlights**
   - **Day 1-3**: Arrive in San José → Rent gear → Head to **Puerto Viejo** (Pacific) or **Tamarindo** (Caribbean).  
   - **Day 4-6**:  
     - *Pacific*: Dive **Cocora Reef** (turtles, coral gardens) → Afternoon snorkel at **Manuel Antonio National Park** (jellyfish lagoon).  
     - *Caribbean*: Dive **Conchal Beach** (coral walls) → Visit **Cocos Island** (shark & ray encounters).  
   - **Day 7-8**:  
     - *Pacific*: **Arenal Volcano** area (dive **Lake Arenal** for serene, cold-water experiences).  
     - *Caribbean*: **Ostional** (night dives with bioluminescent plankton + turtle nesting season).  
   - **Day 9**: Departure.  

### 3. **Unforgettable Add-Ons**
   - **Shark & Whale Shark Safari**:  
     - *Cocos Island*: Boat tours from **Ostional** or **Puerto Jiménez** (sharks visible year-round; whale sharks peak Dec-Apr).  
     - *Pacific*: **Playa Hermosa (Quepos)** for blue-footed boobies and manta rays.  
   - **Blue Hole Dive (Pacific)**:  
     - **Cenote Azul** near **El Remanso** (near Arenal). Swim through a 100m-wide sinkhole to a hidden lagoon.  
   - **Adventure Combo**:  
     - **Zip-lining** over cloud forests (Arenal) → **Hot springs** (Arenal) → **Night hike** for wildlife spotting.  

### 4. **Practical Tips**
   - **P蹼建议**: Rent **adjustable "fins"** (like Fins Plus) – they’re lighter, easier for beginners, and versatile for calm/currents.  
   - **Booking**:  
     - Use **ScubaCast** or **Dive Costa Rica** to compare liveaboard trips (e.g., 4-day Cocos Island dive boat).  
     - Book shore dives via **Playa Conchal (Tamarindo)** or **Puerto Viejo reef tours**.  
   - **Safety**:  
     - Check dive center certifications (PADI/SSI).  
     - Bring reef-safe sunscreen and a dive computer.  
   - **Budget**:  
     - Shore dives: $50-$80/day.  
     - Liveaboards: $300-$600/night (includes food, gear, and expert guides).  

### 5. **Beyond Diving**
   - **Birdwatching**: **Ostional** (turtles) or **Corcovado National Park** (Pacific – jaguars, scarlet macaws).  
   - **Adventure**: **Tortuguero Canals** (kayak through mangroves to sea turtle nests) or **Monteverde Cloud Forest** (sloths, waterfalls).
