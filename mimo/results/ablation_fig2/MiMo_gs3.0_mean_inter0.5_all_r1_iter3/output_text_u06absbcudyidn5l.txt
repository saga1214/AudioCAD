Okay, let's break this down. Chronic redness has many potential causes. Rosacea is a big one, sun damage over a lifetime, genetic predisposition, contact dermatitis from things like soaps, or even chronic infections.

Now, about vasculitis. It's an autoimmune condition where the body's immune system mistakenly attacks its own blood vessels. These vessels come in different sizes: large, medium, and small. The size that's inflamed determines the symptoms. For example, inflammation in small blood vessels often causes skin rashes and redness.

This inflammation can narrow the vessels, which can lead to serious issues like poor blood flow to organs. So, hair and nail weakness could definitely be a symptom if the condition affects smaller vessels supplying those areas.
