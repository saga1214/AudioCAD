   - **1453**: The Ottoman conquest of Constantinople **intensified the urgency** by:

### 2. **Trade Wasn’t "Immediately Closed"**

### 3. **Broader European Drivers**  
   The Age of Discovery was a **synergy of factors**, not a single event:  
   - **Technology**: Shipbuilding (e.g., caravel) and navigation tools enabled long voyages.  
   - **Funding**: Wealthy monarchs (Spain, Portugal, England) backed explorers for profit, glory, and spreading Christianity.  
   - **Rivalry**: Spain’s competition with Portugal drove explorations in the Americas (Columbus, 1492).  
   - **Demographics**: Europe’s population boom created demand for goods (spices, silks, gold).
