Here’s a balanced and immersive 10-day itinerary for Japan, focusing on art history research during downtime, deep exploration of Tokyo and Osaka, and a day trip to Kyoto and Nara. 

**Important Notes First:**
*   **Presentation Work:** Aim for 30-60 minutes of coursework research each evening back at the hotel.
*   **Art Topic Suggestion:** Ukiyo-e Culture: from Edo-Era Prints to Modern Japan would visually showcase slides design (colors/fonts from ukijo-e) alongside literacy content.
*   **Check-in Time:** Most hotels require checkout by 10:00-11:00 AM. Plan to be at the station by then.

**Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo**
*   Morning: Airport arrival, transfer to hotel (use hotel taxi service if available, otherwise pre-booked taxi or train with luggage assistance).
*   Afternoon: Check-in, relax, explore nearby area.
*   Evening: Casual dinner.

**Day 2: Tokyo - Imperial Grandeur & Tradition**
*   Morning: Asakusa (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Market).
*   Late Morning: Ueno Park (Tokyo National Museum - hint for Art History visit!).
*   Afternoon: Edo-Tokyo Museum (in Ueno, excellent for art & history context).
*   Evening: Shibuya Scramble Crossing, dinner in Shibuya.

**Day 3: Tokyo - Modernity & Artistic Expression**
*   Morning: 2-3 hours at Mori Art Museum (in Roppongi) or teamLab Borderless/Planets (digital art).
*   Afternoon: Roppongi Hills (city views, modern architecture).
*   Evening: Akihabara Electric Town (for pop culture art/history).

**Day 4: Tokyo - Nature & Views**
*   Morning: Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden.
*   Late Morning: Meiji Shrine.
*   Afternoon: Harajuku (Takeshita Street, Omotesando).
*   Evening: Shinjuku (Golden Gai, Omoide Yokocho).

**Day 5: Travel to Kyoto via Nara**
*   **Morning:** Shinkansen to Kyoto (approx. 75 mins).
*   **Stop:** Nara (Get off at Nara Station). Visit Todai-ji Temple (Great Buddha) & Nara Park (deer).
*   **Lunch:** In Nara.
*   **Afternoon:** Continue to Kyoto. Check into hotel near Kyoto Station or Gion.
*   **Evening:** Explore Gion district (evening atmosphere).

**Day 6: Kyoto - Imperial Past & Zen Aesthetics**
*   Morning: Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion).
*   Late Morning: Ryoan-ji Temple (Zen rock garden).
*   After标的物“Afternoon: Arashiyama (Bamboo Grove, Tenryu-ji Temple garden). Consider Monkey Park Iwatayama.
*   Evening: Pontocho Alley dinner.

**Day 7: Kyoto - Samurai & Craftsmanship**
*   Morning: Fushimi Inari Shrine (hike part of the path).
*   Afternoon: Samurai & Geisha districts (Sannen-zaka, Shirakawa, Ninen-zaka).
*   Evening: Explore Nishiki Market (food street).

**Day 8: Travel to Osaka**
*   **Morning:** Shinkansen to Osaka (approx. 30 mins from Kyoto).
*   **Stop:** Nara (if missed, quick visit).
*   **Afternoon:** Check into hotel in Namba or Umeda.
*   **Evening:** Dive into Dotonbori (street food, neon signs, Glico Man).

**Day  Osaka - Street Food & Modern Culture**
*   Morning: Osaka Castle (interior exhibits on history).
*   Afternoon: Kuromon Ichiba Market (food), Shinsaibashi Shopping Arcade.
*   Evening: Umeda Sky Building (Hills Floating Garden for city views).

**Day 10: Osaka -前一天**
*   Morning: Free time for last-minute shopping, revisit favorite spot, or final museum visit (e.g., Osaka Museum of Art).
*   Afternoon: Last lunch.
*   Afternoon/Early Evening: Travel to airport for departure.

**Key Travel Logistics:**
1.  **Transport:** Get a **JR Pass (7-day or 14-day)** if your travel dates fall within the validity. Covers Shinkansen and many local lines. Use IC Cards (Suica/Pasmo) for local trains/buses/taxis.
2.  **Accommodation:** Book hotels in advance, especially in peak season. Gion (Kyoto), Namba/Umeda (Osaka), and central Tokyo areas are good bases.
3.  **Museum Visits:** Check opening hours and book tickets online where possible (e.g., Tokyo National Museum, teamLab).
4.  **Flexibility:** This is a template. Adjust based on your energy levels and interests. Prioritize what you're most excited about.

This schedule ensures deep exploration while incorporating Kyoto's historical art forms (Zen gardens, temple art) and Osaka's ukiyo-e heritage, providing rich material and visuals for your Art History presentation. Enjoy your holiday!
