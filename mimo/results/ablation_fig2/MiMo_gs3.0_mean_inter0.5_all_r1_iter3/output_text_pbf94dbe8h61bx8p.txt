**For Macramerds:** Plant hinger-hangers remain strong, but diversify slightly—wall hangings take less dough prep than cookies. Small ornaments add tree sparkle without Christian themes. Station idea: Older kids braid, younger ones glue pom-poms. Minimal glue gun time for you.

**Cookie Crisis Fix:** Drop cookies or chocolate crinklers—no rolling, just scoop. Pre-portion dough in balls to cut mess. Station strategy: Age 2-3: Frost pre-baked cookies. 5-9: Decorate. 15: Bake small batches. Music + timed stations = chaos avoided.

**Cooperation Hack:** The candy globe assembly is perfect! You handle the glue gun, kids add candy (the real motivator). For glue-free alternatives: Fingerprint cards (ages 2+), pine cone feeders (outdoor-safe, no ick). Both are visual and tactile.

**Meaningful Minimal Effort:** Salt dough ornaments—bake, paint, done. Kids can stamp designs or add beads. Or wool felt shapes: Cut shapes with cookie cutters, glue on pom-poms. Supervised glue time cut in half.

- **Wednesday:** Candy globe assembly (you glue, they stuff).  
- **Thursday:** Fingerprint cards or salt dough ornaments.  
- **Friday:** Wrap it all up!
