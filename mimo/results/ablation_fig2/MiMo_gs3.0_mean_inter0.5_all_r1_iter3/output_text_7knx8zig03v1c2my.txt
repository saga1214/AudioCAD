### Clicker Training Explained
Clicker training is a highly effective, reward-based method using a special tool called a clicker.

1.  **The Clicker:** It makes a loud, clear, consistent "click" sound.
2.  **The Process:**
    *   **Step 1: Marking:** The clicker acts as a "mark" or "marker" (like "yes!" or "good!"). You click the moment you see Galileo *do* the exact behavior you want (e.g., the instant his bottom hits the ground for "sit").
    *   **Step 2: Reward:** Immediately after the click, you give him a treat.
    *   **Step 3: Association:** Through repetition, Galileo learns that the click sound means "You did exactly what I wanted!" and that the treat comes *right after* the click.
    *   **Step 4: Shaping:** You gradually change the behavior you're asking for. Start with easy things (sit, eye contact, paw) and slowly make it harder (e.g., sit on command, then sit on command while looking at you, then sit on command while looking at you and holding his paw).
3.  **How it Works:** The click precisely marks the *exact* moment Galileo performs the desired action. The treat follows the click. Over time, the click itself becomes the primary reward (the "praise" or "check mark"). You can eventually phase out the treats and just click when he does the behavior right, giving verbal praise ("Good boy, Galileo!"). It makes learning incredibly precise and fun.
