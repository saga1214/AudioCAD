This is a critical safety point, and I need to be extremely clear about it. The safety of the ramps is not about the car model, it's about the surface. A ten-foot stairwall creates angles and unevenness that standard car ramps are absolutely not designed for. Using them there is incredibly unstable and dangerous. You should never use them for a ten-foot wall, no matter what other car they work for. Please reconsider your plan.
