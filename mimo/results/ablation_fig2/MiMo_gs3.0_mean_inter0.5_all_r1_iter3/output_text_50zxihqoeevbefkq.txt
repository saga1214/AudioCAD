Here's a comprehensive shopping list organized by category for your 20 adults + 4 kids New Year's Eve party, based on our menu and decorations:

### **Proteins & Main Dishes**
- Ham (6–8 lbs)  
- Pigs in a blanket: 32 mini sausages (16 for kids/adults)  
- Ground pork (for pigs in a blanket)  

### **Appetizers & Finger Foods**
- Mini slider buns (32)  
- Shrimp (1.5 lbs for cocktail)  
- Buttermilk (for sliders)  
- Cream cheese (for sliders)  
- Mini slider patties/toppings (cheese, bacon, etc.)  

### **Sides (Vegetables)**
- Baby potatoes (10–12 lbs)  
- Asparagus (8–10 bunches)  
- Olive oil (for asparagus)  
- Lemon (for asparagus)  
- Salt/pepper (for roasted carrots)  
- Olive oil (for carrots)  

### **Kids' Menu (24 servings)**
- Grape juice (2–3 gallons)  
- Mini carrots (2–3 lbs)  
- Baby potatoes (shared with adults)  
- Pretzels or crackers (for grazing)  
- Juice boxes (optional backup)  

### **Desserts**
- Chocolate chips (for fluted grape juice)  
- Ice cream (for post-toast treats)  

### **Beverages (All Guests)**
- Champagne (8–10 bottles)  
- Sparkling cider (for kids/noise cup refills) noise cup noisemakers  
- Soda (cans/bottles)  
- Water (bottles/gallon jug)  

### **Decorations & Atmosphere**
- Balloons (various sizes)  
- Festive hats/noisemakers (4 kid-sized sets)  
- Plastic fluted cups (for grape juice)  

### **Extras & Essentials**
- Aluminum foil (for ham/pigs blanket)  
- Tongs, cutting boards, serving spoons  
- Paper plates, napkins, cups (25–30% extra)  
- Trash bags  
- Ice (for drinks/desserts)  
- Music playlist link (Spotify/YouTube)  

### **Prep Tips**
1. **Prep Ahead**: Roast potatoes/carrots the day before. Chop veggies.  
2. **Kid Involvement**: Let kids assemble noise cup noisemakers/hat decorating.  
3. **Buffet Setup**: Use tiered trays for appetizers to minimize mess.
