That's a fun puzzle! It's a bit like the classic pigeon problem.

Here’s how you could structure a plan, kind of like organizing things on a roof:

First, clearly identify each passion-based trip and which financial priority it would support—for Bolivia salt flats or apartment savings.

Second, research the total cost for your apartment down payment goal and your Bolivia trip goal.

Third, divide each total cost by the number of months you have. This gives you a clear, fixed savings target per month for each goal.

Fourth, create a budget that includes both down payments and trip savings, alongside your home renovation fund.

Finally, track your progress every month. Adjust your spending as needed. By giving each goal its own dedicated savings space, just like the pigeons on the different rooftops, you can make steady progress on all of them.
