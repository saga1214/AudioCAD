Okay, diesel camper vans age. A few things to check. For brakes, look for the wear indicators—those little metal tabs that should vibrate against the rotor when worn down. Also, check the brake fluid level, and make sure the pads and rotors aren’t excessively worn or glazed. For spark plugs, diesel engines use glow plugs, not spark plugs, so that’s a different process. Glow plugs help the engine start in the cold. You’ll need to remove the battery terminals, disconnect the glow plug wires, unscrew the plugs, and replace them. It’s not the most difficult job, but it requires a wrench and a socket. Always check your owner’s manual for the exact procedure. And don’t forget to check your diesel fuel filter and DEF fluid levels—those are critical for its health. A pre-trip inspection is always wise.
