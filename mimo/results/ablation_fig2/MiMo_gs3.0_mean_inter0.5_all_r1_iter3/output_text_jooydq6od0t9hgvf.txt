### 🍸 Vodka Mixer (Refreshing & Soda-Free)
**Recipe: "Sunset Spritz"**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz vodka  
  - 3 oz premium sparkling water (alcohol-free to respect allergy)  
  - 1 oz fresh lime juice  
  - 0.5 oz elderflower syrup (e.g., St. Germain)  
  - Garnish: Lime wheel + mint sprig  
- **Method**:  
  Shake vodka, lime juice, and syrup with ice. Strain into a coupe glass. Top with sparkling water. Garnish.  
*Why it works*: Light, balanced, and perfect for warm weather. Soda-free and vegetarian-friendly vodka options (like Grey Goose) ensure omnivore vegetarians feel included.

---

### 🇦🇷 Fernet-Branca Cocktail (Argentine-Inspired)
**Recipe: "Mate de Sol"**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
  - 1.5 oz fresh grapefruit juice  
  - 1 oz simple syrup  
  - 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
  - Garnish: Grapefruit twist  
- **Method**:  
  Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube. Garnish with grapefruit twist.  
*Why it works*: Fernet’s herbal bitterness is balanced by citrus and sweetness—elegant yet approachable across age groups. Grapefruit juice complements Fernet’s South American roots.
