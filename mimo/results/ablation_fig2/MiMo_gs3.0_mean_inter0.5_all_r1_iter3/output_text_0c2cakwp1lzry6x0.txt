Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your key items, focusing on safety and landlord-compliance:

**1. Mounting the TV:**
*   **Plan First:** Choose the exact location. Measure carefully. Consider viewing angles.
*   **Find the Wall:** Use a stud finder. **Crucial:** Mounting the TV *must* go into a stud (a wooden beam running behind the wall) for safety and stability. If no stud is directly under the TV mount point, you *must* use a heavy-duty drywall anchor rated for the TV's weight (check manufacturer specs!). **Never** mount drywall-only without proper anchors.
*   **Mark the Studs:** Once a stud is found, mark its position clearly on the wall. Mark the center point of the TV's intended position relative to the stud(s).
*   **Install the Mount:**
    *   Use the appropriate mounting hardware (bolts, anchors) provided with your TV mount.
    *   Place the hardware into the stud or drywall anchor.
    *   Use a level to ensure the mount bracket is perfectly horizontal.
    *   Secure the bracket firmly to the wall.
*   **Mount the TV:** Carefully lift the TV onto the bracket and secure it using the provided bolts. Ensure it's tight and level.

**2. Hanging Planters:**
*   **Choose the Spot:** Decide where you want them (e.g., near a window, above a shelf). Ensure it's a suitable location for the weight and size of the planter(s).
*   **Determine Mounting Type:**
    *   **Small/Medium Planters:** Use strong **Command™ Voids** or **Command™ Hooks** rated for the weight. Follow the manufacturer's instructions *exactly* regarding weight limits and surface type (smooth vs. textured).
    *   **Large/Heavy Planters:** **You MUST use wall anchors.** Find the appropriate anchor (e.g., toggle bolts, molly bolts) rated for the planter's weight. **Check with your landlord first** to confirm drilling is allowed in that specific area.
*   **Install Anchors/Hooks:**
    *   If using hooks, mark the spot, drill a hole (size specified by hook manufacturer), insert the hook, and snap on the adhesive base.
    *   If using anchors, mark the spot, drill a hole (size specified by anchor manufacturer), insert the anchor fully, and screw in the hook or bracket.
*   **Hang the Planter:** Place the planter securely onto the hook or anchor.

**3. Installing Floating Shelves:**
*   **Plan Layout:** Decide how many shelves, their height, and spacing. Sketch a quick layout.
*   **Find Studs:** Use a stud finder. **Floating shelves MUST be anchored into studs for safety.** Measure the shelf length and find the nearest studs on each side. Shelves over 12-18 inches long *need* support on both ends.
*   **Mark Positions:** Mark the top and bottom edges of the shelf where it will sit. Mark the exact stud locations where the shelf brackets will attach.
*   **Install Shelf Brackets:**
    *   Use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for the shelf's weight (especially if not over studs) or use **toggle bolts** for maximum security.
    *   Drill pilot holes into the wall at the marked stud locations (or anchor locations).
    *   Insert the appropriate anchors fully.
    *   Attach the shelf brackets securely to the anchors/studs using screws. Ensure the brackets are level.
*   **Install the Shelf:** Place the shelf onto the brackets and secure it firmly. Use the included screws or washers to lock it into place.

**Key Tips & Warnings:**

1.  **Landlord Check:** **Crucially, inform your landlord you plan to drill.** Explain exactly what you're installing (TV mount, anchors, shelf brackets). Get permission if needed. Noise during drilling hours is a no-no.
2.  **Safety First:** Always wear safety glasses. Use a stud finder correctly. **Never** overload anchors or brackets. A falling TV, shelf, or heavy planter is extremely dangerous.
3.  **Weight Matters:** Double-check the weight limits for *all* hardware (TV mount, anchors, hooks, brackets). Don't underestimate the weight of your TV, a heavy planter, or a long shelf loaded with books.
4.  **Level is Essential:** Use a level for TVs, shelves, and hooks to prevent crooked installations.
5.  **Measure Twice, Drill Once:** Accuracy is key to a clean, secure, and safe installation.
6.  **Tool Kit:** You'll likely need a power drill/driver, screws, a level, a stud finder, and possibly a hammer/mallet for anchors. Check hardware requirements.
7.  **Temporary vs. Permanent:** If unsure about drilling or weight, consider temporary options like Command™ strips for light planters or using a sturdy table/desk as a shelf stand for now.

By following these steps carefully and prioritizing safety and landlord approval, you can successfully install your TV, planters, and shelves to create a beautiful and functional decorated space!
