The **single most important factor** is having an **immediate, engaging hook in the first 3 seconds**.  

Here’s why:  
1. **TikTok’s algorithm prioritizes instant engagement**. If your video doesn’t grab attention within seconds, viewers scroll past, and the algorithm won’t promote it to more people.  
2. **Quality ≠ Reach**. Even with great lighting/audio/guidelines, if your opening seconds don’t hook viewers, your content gets buried.  
3. **Exposure depends on retention**. TikTok rewards videos that keep viewers watching (likes, comments, shares). A strong hook drives this.  

### Why other factors are secondary:
- **Consistency**: Important for growth, but irrelevant if videos aren’t engaging.  
- **Conciseness**: Short videos perform better, but only if they’re compelling from the start.  
- **Guidelines/Description**: Essential to avoid restrictions, but won’t drive discovery without a hook.  
- **Engaging Content**: This *is* the hook—but if it doesn’t start strong, it fails.  

**Bottom line**: TikTok rewards instant attention. Fix your first 3 seconds, and everything else (hashtags, consistency, quality) gains leverage.
