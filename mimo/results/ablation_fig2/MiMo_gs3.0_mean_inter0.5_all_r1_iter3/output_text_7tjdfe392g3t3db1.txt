Tuning an electric guitar involves adjusting the **tuning pegs** (also called machine heads) on the headstock. Here’s a step-by-step guide:

### 1. **Physical Tuning Pegs (Traditional Method)**  
   - **Identify the pegs**: These are the small wheels on the headstock.  
   - **Tune up/down**:  
     - **Tuning up**: Turn the peg *toward* the headstock (clockwise).  
     - **Tuning down**: Turn the peg *away* from the headstock (counter-clockwise).  
   - **Apply firm pressure**: Electric guitars require more tension than acoustic ones.  
   - **Check tuning**: Use a tuner app (e.g., GuitarTuna) or clip-on tuner to confirm pitch.  

### 2. **Electronic Tuners**  
   - **Clip-on tuner**: Attach it to the headstock. It emits a beep when a string is perfectly tuned.  
   - **Phone app**: Use an app like **Fender Tune** or **GuitarTuna**. Place your ear near the strings to hear the app’s feedback.  
   - **How to use**:  
     1. Play a string.  
     2. The tuner displays the pitch (e.g., "A#") and a visual guide (e.g., a slider).  
     3. Adjust the peg until the pitch matches the target note.
