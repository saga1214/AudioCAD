Here's a quick list of ingredients you'll need for classic **Spaghetti Carbonara**:

### Core Ingredients:
1. **Pasta** (Spaghetti, Tagliatelle, or Bucatini)  
2. **Guanciale** (Cured pork cheek) – *essential for authentic flavor* (substitute pancetta or bacon if needed).  
3. **Eggs** (4 large eggs + 1 yolk for richness).  
4. **Pecorino Romano** (Hard Italian cheese, finely grated).  
5. **Black Pepper** (Freshly ground, generously).  

### Optional but Recommended:
- Garlic (1 clove, minced).  
- Fresh parsley (chopped).  
- Salted pancetta (if guanciale isn’t available).  

### Key Steps:
1. Boil pasta in salted water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.  
2. While pasta cooks, fry diced guanciale/pancetta in a pan until crisp.  
3. Whisk eggs, grated Pecorino, and black pepper in a bowl.  
4. Toss drained pasta with the guanciale, then remove from heat.  
5. **Off-heat**, add the egg mixture and stir vigorously to create a creamy sauce (residual heat cooks the eggs without scrambling).  
6. Adjust with reserved pasta water if needed for creaminess.  

### Pro Tips:
- **No cream!** Authentic carbonara uses cheese, egg, and fat from the pork.  
- **Temperature matters:** Always cook the eggs away from direct heat to avoid scrambling.  
- **Salt wisely:** Guanciale is already salty, so avoid over-salting the pasta water.  

Enjoy your meal! 🍝
