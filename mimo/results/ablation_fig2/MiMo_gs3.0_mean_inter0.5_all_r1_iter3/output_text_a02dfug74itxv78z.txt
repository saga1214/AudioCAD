**Regarding Paint & Cops:** There's **no credible evidence** that colorful paint jobs lead to significantly more police pulls than neutral tones. Factors like **aggressive driving, location, time of day, and the inherent visibility/high-performance nature of the car itself** are far more likely reasons for getting pulled over. Neutral colors simply help you "blend in" slightly less.

**Regarding Reliability in Supercars:** **Porsche** stands head and shoulders above the rest in the supercar/high-performance segment for reliability and longevity. The **Audi R8 V10 Plus (Option 4)** benefits directly from Porsche's engineering and platform. The **Ferrari 458 Italia (Option 5)** is also exceptionally well-regarded for its durability relative to its class.

**Regarding High-Performance Tires:** Yes, you absolutely need **Ultra High-Performance Summer Tires (UHPS)**. Brands like **Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2**, **Pirelli P Zero Trofeo R**, or **Bridgestone Potenza RE-71RS** are the benchmark. They provide the necessary grip and responsiveness for a car competing with supercars. They are not all-season or winter tires.
