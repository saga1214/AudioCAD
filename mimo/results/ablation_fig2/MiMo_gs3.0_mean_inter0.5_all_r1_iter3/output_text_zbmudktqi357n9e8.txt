Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration, but the timing really depends on what you're looking for. Mexico's autumn is kinda split into two seasons. In the northern and central parts, uh, that's where you'll find that classic fall vibe—golden aspens, oak trees turning red and yellow, and a really crisp, cool air. That's the best time for traditional autumn colors. You could go to places like the Copper Canyon or the state of Michoacán. 

Then you've got the southern coastal areas, like Cancún or Tulum. There, "autumn" is more like their rainy season ending. You'll see a lot of palm trees and tropical colors, but still, some of those orange and red tones start to pop in the foliage. It's more of a warm, humid autumn. 

So, really, it depends on what you want. If you're looking for that classic fall palette, go north, like in the Yucatán Peninsula or central Mexico, and aim for late October to November. If you want that tropical, autumnal feel, the southern coasts are great, probably November through December. Either way, it's a beautiful place to practice those watercolor techniques you love. Just, you know, pack your paints and find a quiet spot by a lake or in a forest. You'll get some amazing reference photos. Have a great trip!
