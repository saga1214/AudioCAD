Secret Harbour Beach Resort offers that perfect calm bay right from your doorstep. Snorkeling is fantastic there - you might spot small fish and maybe even some sea turtles if you're lucky. There's also Salt River Bay which is less crowded and has beautiful mangroves and trails. 

If you're up for a boat trip, look into tours that go to Coki Beach on St. Thomas - it's a popular spot for snorkeling and has a small bar on the side of the road. Or you could visit the Virgin Islands National Park on St. John - they have great trails and often have ranger-led talks about local wildlife, including birds. 

For birding, look for the Mangrove Herons near Cruz Bay. You might also see the famous Royal Terns nesting on some of the islands if you take a boat tour to a uninhabited island. And don't forget to look for the Brown Pelicans - they're quite common and fun to watch. 

Since you're both cruising by boat, you can easily stop at different beaches along the way. Just make sure you stick to the shallower, calmer waters and avoid any steep drops in depth. The islands have many secluded coves perfect for a beach day. Just remember to stay hydrated and wear sunscreen. Have a wonderful trip!
