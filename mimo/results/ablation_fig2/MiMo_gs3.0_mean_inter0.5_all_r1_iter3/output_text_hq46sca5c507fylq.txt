5. **Tailoring Tools Kit** (scissors, pins, measuring tape, seam ripper) – A starter set.  

### Woodworking Gifts (if you pursue it):
- **Hand Plane or Chisel Set** – Foundational tools.  
- **Woodworking Classes** – Hands-on learning.  
- **Cedar or Poplar Lumber** – Beginner-friendly wood.  

### Clay Gifts:
- **Ceramic Wheel Kit** – For learning to throw pottery.  
- **Clay Storage Containers** – Practical for home projects.  
- **Sculpting Tools Set** – For hand-building clay.  

### Cross-Hobby Ideas:
- **Leatherworking Tools** – Combines detail work (like tailoring) with new materials.  
- **Mini Workshop Subscriptions** (e.g., Skillshare, Domestika) – For structured learning.  
- **Vintage Fountain Pen Restoration Kit** – Supports your current hobby.
