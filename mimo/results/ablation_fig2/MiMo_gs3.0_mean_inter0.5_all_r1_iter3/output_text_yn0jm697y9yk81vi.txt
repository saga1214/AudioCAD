Here's a consolidated list of my top recommendations for cutting sheet metal, drywall fastening, and insulation, along with where to purchase them in Indianapolis:

### **1. Cutting Thinner Sheet Metal**  
- **Tool:** Aviation Snips (for curves on thin metal)  

### **2. Cutting Thicker Metal / Power Tools**  
- **Tool:** Angle Grinder with a Cut-off Wheel (primary recommendation for metal)  

### **3. Store to Buy Tools Indianapolis (Offline)**  
- **Top Recommendation:** **Menards** (best selection for metal tools/supplies; 30+ locations, including East Chicago Ave. and Meridian Way).  
- **Alternatives:** Home Depot (Broad Ripple Ave.), Lowe’s (N. Clark Ave.), Tractor Supply Co.  

### **4. Cutting Drywall**  
- **Fastener:** Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws (1 ¼" length for ½" drywall in wood studs).  
  - **Why:** Superior holding power, prevents nail pops/cracks. Ring-shank nails *only* if screws unavailable.  
- **Driver:** Drywall Screw Gun (essential for consistent depth/holding).  

### **5. Insulation for Walls**  
- **Material:** Fiberglass Bat Insulation (pre-cut rolls for studs).  
- **Alternatives:**  
  - Mineral Wool (for soundproofing/fire resistance).  
  - Spray Foam (best for air sealing; requires pro installation).  

---

### **Key Notes for Your Purchase:**  
- **Menards Advantage:** Largest selection of metal tools/blades. Check their [metal department](https://www.menards.com/d/category/metals/tools) online for inventory.  
- **Drywall Screws:** Buy at Home Depot, Lowe’s, Menards, or Ace Hardware.  
- **Safety:** Wear eye/ear protection with angle grinders; gloves for metal cutting.  
- **Stud Finder:** Use one to locate studs when hanging drywall/insulation.
