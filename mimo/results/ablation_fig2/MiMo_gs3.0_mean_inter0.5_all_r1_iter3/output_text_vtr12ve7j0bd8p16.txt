Calgary is a fantastic base for adventure! Since Sagittarius is known for loving travel and exploration, you're in luck.

For a short getaway nearby, you have some amazing options.

You could both hike up to the iconic Bow Falls in the Banff Gorge. It's a beautiful, moderate hike with incredible waterfalls and mountain views.

Another great spot is Johnston Canyon. You can hike down to the stunning turquoise pools and waterfalls. It's a bit further, but very accessible.

If you're up for a longer drive, you could head to Kananaskis Country. It's a paradise for hiking, with trails leading to stunning lakes and views.

Or, for something a bit different, you could take a day trip to the City of Banff. You can take the Banff Gondola up Sulphur Mountain for breathtaking views of the valley and the Bow Valley Parkway.

Finally, if you want something more relaxed but still scenic, you could take a cruise on the Bow River at sunset. It's a gentle float past some of the most beautiful scenery in the city.

Any of these would be a wonderful way to share an adventure!
