First, about the left-brain versus right-brain idea. It’s largely a myth. The brain doesn’t operate in strict halves. While certain areas do specialize in tasks like language or spatial reasoning, creativity and logic are both processed in both hemispheres. It’s more about how the brain integrates different functions. For example, problem-solving often involves the prefrontal cortex, which is a mix of both sides. The brain is more interconnected than that.
