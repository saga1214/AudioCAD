You are absolutely right that older comics are generally much rarer, which is a major reason for their value.

Ten incredibly important and valuable books from the Golden Age, which were sold recently, are:

First, *Action Comics* number one again, Superman's debut from 1938. A record six million.
Then, *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, Batman's first appearance in 1939.
*Timely Comics* number fifteen, which introduced Captain America in 1941.
*Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen again, Spider-Man's first appearance in 1962, for three-six.
*Marvel Super-Heroes* number one, which is the first full appearance of the Hulk.
*The Fantastic Four* number one, from 1961.
*Adventure Comics* number 223, where the Silver Surfer first appeared.
*The Silver Surfer* number one, from 1966.
*Conan the Barbarian* number one, from 1937.
And finally, *Tales of the Black Knight* number one, from 1954.
