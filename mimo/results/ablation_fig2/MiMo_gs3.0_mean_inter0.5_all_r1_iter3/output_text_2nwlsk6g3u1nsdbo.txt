You're welcome! Here are the **shrimp main courses** you asked for earlier, with revised ingredient lists based on shrimp as the star ingredient:

### 1. Spicy Shrimp Scampi  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled & deveined  
- 8 oz pasta (e.g., linguine or spaghetti)  
- 4 tbsp butter  
- 3 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1/4 cup dry white wine (optional)  
- 1/4 cup heavy cream  
- 2 tbsp lemon juice  
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped  
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat)  
- Salt and black pepper to taste  

### 2. Shrimp Fra Diavolo  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled & deveined  
- 2 tbsp olive oil  
- 3 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1 small onion, diced  
- 1 bell pepper, sliced  
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes  
- 2 tbsp red pepper flakes  
- 1 tsp dried oregano  
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar  
- Salt and pepper to taste  
- Fresh basil for garnish  

### 3. Cajun Shrimp Tacos  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled & deveined  
- 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning (plus extra for coating)  
- 1 tbsp olive oil  
- 6 corn or flour tortillas  
- 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese  
- 1 avocado, sliced  
- 1 cup shredded cabbage  
- Lime wedges for serving
