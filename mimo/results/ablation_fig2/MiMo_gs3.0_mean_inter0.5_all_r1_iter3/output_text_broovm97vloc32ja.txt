That's fantastic that you can do art as well! You can absolutely submit a package with both your text and your illustrations. However, it's important to know that publishers are very specific about the format they expect.

They will almost always require your illustrations to be in black and white, and they must be *clearly* labeled as 'working art' or 'doodles' or something similar. They need to be able to easily identify them as a preliminary sketch, not as the final, polished artwork.

They will also ask for a high-resolution digital file of the final artwork, but they'll specify that this should be created *after* they have accepted your manuscript. This is because publishers have their own in-house designers who create the final, polished illustrations. So, your illustrations are a huge asset, but they need to be presented in a specific way for the initial submission.
