That's a great goal. You can definitely write a professional email that strikes that balance.

For the subject line, you could write: "Meeting Request: Essay Exam Improvement"

In the body of the email, you could start with, "Dear Professor [Last Name], I scored a 64 on the recent exam about [exam topic]. I scored very well on my previous exams in this course. I'm eager to do even better on future tests and was hoping to understand what I could have improved on in my essay to help me prepare."

Then you could close with, "Would you have time to meet briefly sometime this week? I appreciate your time and guidance."

This approach focuses on your future learning and avoids challenging the past grade.
