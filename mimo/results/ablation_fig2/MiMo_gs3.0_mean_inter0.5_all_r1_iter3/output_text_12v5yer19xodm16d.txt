Got it! Here are five drink ideas to give you that energy boost. First is Matcha Green Tea, which has caffeine plus an amino acid called L-theanine that helps with focus. Second, you could try a Coconut Water with Lime, which is naturally hydrating and has a little bit of sugar for a quick energy lift. Third is a Classic Espresso or a strong cup of Black Coffee. Fourth, a Green Smoothie with banana, spinach, and peanut butter is great for sustained energy. And fifth, a Ginger Tea with a bit of honey is warming and soothing, plus the ginger helps digestion.

For snacks, here are five ideas. First, Almonds or Walnuts, which are healthy fats and protein. Second, Greek Yogurt with Honey, which is high in protein and probiotics. Third, a Handful of Dark Chocolate with over 70 percent cocoa, which has caffeine and antioxidants. Fourth, an Apple with Peanut Butter combines fiber, protein, and healthy fats. And fifth, a Hard-Boiled Egg provides a clean protein boost to keep you full and energized. Hope that helps you get your energy back up!
