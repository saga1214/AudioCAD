That's a great set of questions! Let's tackle them one by one:

**Switching to Adobe Audition:**
*   **Easier Transition:** Start with Audition's **free 7-day trial**. Spend your first few days *only* learning the basics: importing audio, recording, basic editing tools (slice, cut, move), and the **Noise Reduction/Restoration** panel (it's incredibly powerful for Yeti harshness and mouth clicks). Don't try to learn everything at once.
*   **Time Savings:** Yes, absolutely. Audition's faster processing, built-in advanced tools (like Spectral Repair), and batch processing (for applying effects to many files) save significant time, especially for cleanup and basic edits.

**Reliable AI Tools:**
*   **Silence Trimming:** **Adobe Audition's "Enhance" AI** (in the Enhance menu) is excellent for intelligently reducing noise and background hum. **iZotope RX** (especially RX 10/11/12) has fantastic AI-powered tools like **Declip**, **Denoise**, and **DeEsser**. **Auphonic** (used in podcasts) is also AI-powered for leveling and noise reduction but is more cloud-based.
*   **Labeling:** **Descript** (overlays audio in a text editor) uses AI for transcription and makes labeling easy. **Adobe Audition** has basic waveform markers. **iZotope RX** offers AI tagging for spectral repair. **Auphonic** also provides automatic level adjustments and some noise reduction.

**Balancing Work, Editing, & Marketing (Avoiding Burnout):**
1.  **Time Blocking:** Schedule dedicated recording hours, editing hours, marketing (social media, outreach), and admin time. Protect recording/editing time fiercely.
2.  **Batch Processing:** Group similar tasks (e.g., record all characters in a scene first, edit all those takes).
3.  **Automate Marketing:** Use email sequences, scheduling tools (Buffer, Hootsuite), and templates for outreach.
4.  **Set Boundaries:** Define work hours and stick to them. Turn off work notifications after hours.
5.  Quick Breaks: Take 5-10 minute breaks every hour. Stretch, look away from the screen. **Hydrate constantly** (room-temp water is best for vocal health - avoid caffeine/sugar crashes).
6.  **Prioritize:** Focus on high-impact tasks. Not every project needs the same level of polish.
7.  **Delegate/Outsource:** Can you hire an editor for simple jobs? Can you use AI tools for basic tasks?

Based on your craving and the restaurant type, here are some fast, tasty options:

2.  **Chicken Teriyaki Bowl:** (Japanese) - Grilled chicken, teriyaki sauce, cucumber, onion, cabbage, sometimes egg. Simple, savory, and very filling. Often comes with a small side salad.
3.  **Beef or Pork Curry Udon:** (Japanese) - Thin wheat noodles in a rich, savory curry broth with tender meat and veggies. Hearty and comforting.
4.  **Pad See Ew:** (Chinese) - Wide rice noodles stir-fried with soy sauce, dark soy, garlic, bean sprouts, and thin slices of beef or pork. Sweet, savory, umami, and packed with flavor. Usually served with a small side salad.
5.  **Chicken Katsu Soba:** (Japanese) - Thinly sliced chicken breaded and fried (katsu), served over cold buckwheat noodles (soba) in a light dipping sauce. Refreshing and texturally interesting.

**Recommendation:** For maximum flavor and speed, go for the **Gochujang Bibimbap** or the **Pad See Ew**. They deliver intense, complex flavors quickly. The **Chicken Teriyaki Bowl** is a safe, reliable, and satisfying choice too. Enjoy your meal and your creative work!
