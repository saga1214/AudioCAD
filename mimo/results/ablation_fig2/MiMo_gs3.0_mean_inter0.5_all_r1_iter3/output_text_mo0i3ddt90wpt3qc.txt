You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is a great gluten-free foundation. Since camping snacks also need to be sturdy, I have a couple more gluten-free ideas for you.

Nuts and seeds are always a popular choice – think almonds, walnuts, or pumpkin seeds – as they're portable and full of energy.

Another good idea is rice cakes, which you can even top with gluten-free nut butter for a little extra protein and flavor. Both of these should hold up well to the bumps of cycling!
