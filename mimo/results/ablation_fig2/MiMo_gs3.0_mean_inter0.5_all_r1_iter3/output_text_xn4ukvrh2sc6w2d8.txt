### 1. Old Fashioned (Art Decos Airport Theme)  
**Glass:** Rocks glass over ice  
**Garnish:** Orange twist (expressed oils)  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon rye (e.g., Buffalo Trace, Bulleit)  
- 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle sugar cube with ¾ oz water in glass until dissolved.  
2. Add bourbon rye and bitters. Stir 20-30 seconds with ice until chilled.  
3. Strain into prepared glass over fresh ice.  
*Egg White Option (Classic Technique):*  
- Add 0.5 oz pasteurized egg white to shaker with other ingredients.  
- **Dry Shake First:** Shake *without ice* 10-15 times to emulsify egg white.  
- **Shake with Ice:** Add ice, shake 10 more times, then strain. Garnish with cherry/orange peel.

---

### 2. Paper Plane (Equal Parts Aviation Theme) Kentucky Fall Style  
**Glass:** Coupe glass  
**Garnish:** Cherry  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz gin (London Dry preferred, e.g., Tanqueray, Hendrick's)  
- ¾ oz Maraschino liqueur (Luxardo)  
- ¼ oz crème de violette  
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice  
**Instructions:**  
1. Shake all ingredients *with ice* vigorously 15-20 times.  
2. Strain into coupe glass.  
3. Garnish with cherry.

---

### 3. Whiskey Sour  
**Glass:** Rocks glass over ice  
**Garnish:** Lemon twist or maraschino cherry  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon rye  
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
*Optional:* 0.5 oz pasteurized egg white  
**Instructions:**  
1. Add all ingredients to shaker.  
2. **Dry Shake (Optional):** Shake *without ice* 10-15 times for silkiness.  
3. **Shake with Ice:** Add ice, shake 15-20 times.  
4. Strain into prepared glass over ice. Garnish as above.
