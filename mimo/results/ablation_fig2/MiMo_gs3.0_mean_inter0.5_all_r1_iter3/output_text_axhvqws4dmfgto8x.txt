Alright, let's tackle this stir-fry question with a focus on noodle safety!

Setting off another smoke alarm is highly unlikely here!

The wood spoon incident is behind you now. Here’s the secret to perfect stir-fry noodles:

First, make sure you're using metal utensils, no woods or plastics.

Second, when you add the pasta, add salt to the water and a generous amount of oil to the pot. This helps with flavor and texture.

Then, as soon as the water returns to a boil, get those noodles in. And here's a pro tip: as soon as you add them, stir them immediately to prevent sticking.

Also, stir-frying is all about speed. Have all your sauce and toppings ready before you even start cooking.
