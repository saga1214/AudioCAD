Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots! For ramen, check out Ton Ton Ramen—they’ve got killer tonkotsu. If you want pho, Pho 79 is a classic. For something more adventurous, try Momo Chicken for their unique dumpling dishes.

For protein bars, if you want something tasty, I’d recommend Quest or No Cow. They’re high-protein but not chalky, and they’ve got flavors like cookies and cream or mint chocolate chip. If you prefer something more natural, RXBAR or GoMacro are solid too.

Enjoy your noodles and snacks!
