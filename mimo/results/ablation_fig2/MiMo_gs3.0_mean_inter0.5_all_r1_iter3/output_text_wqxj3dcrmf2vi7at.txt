### 🧁 Homemade Treats
1. **Frozen Morsels**:  
   - Mashed **pumpkin** or **sweet potato** patted into tiny discs, frozen solid.  
   - Add coconut for extra texture!  

2. **Peanut Butter Oat Cookies**:  
   - Mix peanut butter (check for no xylitol) + rolled oats.  
   - Roll into balls, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15 mins. Cool before cutting into pieces.  

3. **Healthy Crunch**:  
   - **Cooked chicken/turkey**: Raw or steamed strips.  
   - **Carrot sticks**: Steamed and cooled.  

⚠️ **Always give tiny portions**!

### Quick Recap of Earlier Topics
- **Teaching "Sit"**: Use treats close to nose → raise over head → reward when she sits.  
- **Potty Training**:  
  - Frequent trips to a fixed spot.  
  - Reward *immediately* when she goes outside.  
  - Clean accidents thoroughly to remove scent.  
- **Barking at Cats**: Redirect with a toy or command like "sit." Reward quiet behavior.
