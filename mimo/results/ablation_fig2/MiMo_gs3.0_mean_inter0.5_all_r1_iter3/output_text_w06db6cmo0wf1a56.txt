Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids... packing coolers... healthy but with options for soda... and calzones or pizza instead of mini ones... plus dessert... Got it. Here's a shopping list designed for minimal mess.

Drinks:
For water, ten large reusable water bottles for the dads, plus twenty kid-sized water bottles.
For low-sugar juice for kids, twenty boxes.
Sugar-free sodas for adults, ten 2-liter cases would be plenty.
Also, grab ten single-serve creamers and ten individual sweeteners like packets, just in case.
Finally, fill up on ice to keep everything cold.

Snacks:
Cheese sticks, aim for sixty individual sticks.
Grapes, get about five pounds pre-washed.
Single-serve pretzels or trail mix bags, pack one hundred of them.
Baby carrots with ten single-serving hummus cups.

Food, which is the main meal:
Since everyone loves calzones, ordering from a pizza place that does them is your best bet. You can order thirty cheese calzones—one for each person. They'll be delivered pre-baked and ready to heat up in a cooler.
Alternatively, if pizza isn't ideal, you can order thirty subs or sandwiches, all sliced up and packed individually.

Dessert:
To keep it simple and grab-and-go, cupcakes are perfect. Order thirty of them with different fun tops. Or, brownies in a individually wrapped box would also work great.

Just remember to pack plenty of paper towels, napkins, and maybe some wet wipes in the coolers. This plan should give you a huge variety of choices while letting everyone move around without a mess. Have an absolutely fantastic party!
