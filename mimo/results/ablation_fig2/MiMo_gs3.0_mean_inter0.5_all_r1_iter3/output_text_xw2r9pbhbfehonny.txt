Sure, here's a quick checklist for your self-filming puppet show:

Before filming:
- Get permission from the venue if needed.
- Charge your phone and have backup batteries.
- Set up lighting so it’s visible on camera.
- Test the recording settings before starting.
- Have a script or notes ready, even if it’s improv-heavy.
- Keep your props and puppets within reach.

During filming:
- Face the camera directly and speak clearly.
- Keep the puppets in the frame.
- Use a tripod or steady surface to avoid shaky footage.
- Smile and engage with the “audience.”

After filming:
- Edit out mistakes or awkward pauses.
- Add captions or simple graphics if needed.
- Upload to your own kid-friendly channel.

That’s it. Have fun and be natural.
