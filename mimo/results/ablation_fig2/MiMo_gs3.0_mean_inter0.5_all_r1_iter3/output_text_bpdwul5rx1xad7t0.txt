Of course! With a two-bedroom apartment and expecting a first baby, I'd suggest focusing on shorter road trips that are manageable for your new family. I can give you five great options in the BC Lower Mainland.

First, there's the classic Vancouver to Victoria trip. You can take a ferry across to the island. In Victoria, you can walk around the inner harbour, visit the famous Butchart Gardens, and enjoy the scenic drive along the coast.

Second, you could explore the Fraser Valley. It's a bit closer, about an hour and a half away. There, you can visit wineries, farm stands for fresh fruit, and the famous maple syrup tasting at the Okanagan Valley Maple Syrup Tasting Room.

Third, for a more rugged experience, you can drive to the Sea to Sky Highway. You can hike a portion of the famous Grouse Grind or visit Lighthouse Park. It's a great day trip.

Fourth, you could head to the Sunshine Coast. You can visit the town of Seabank, explore the Gulf Islands National Park, and maybe even go kayaking.

And fifth, for a quick weekend getaway, there are the mountains nearby. You can drive up to Whistler or the Pemberton area. There, you can hike in beautiful forests, enjoy the local breweries, and have a wonderful dinner at a scenic restaurant like the Bear Mountain Restaurant in Whistler.

All of these trips are relatively short, and they offer a great mix of beautiful scenery and fun activities. They are all well-suited for a car with an all-wheel drive.
