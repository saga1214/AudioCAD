Okay, since you've light-sanded the doors and box surfaces, here are the final steps before primer. Make sure to wipe everything down again with degreaser just to be safe, then let them dry completely. 

After they're sand and dry, lightly sand the entire surface one more time. This will get any remaining dust or rough spots. Then wipe it down one last time with a tack cloth to remove all the dust. 

Once it's perfectly dust-free, you can apply your primer.
