The absolute best bedding for winter chicks on concrete is wood shavings, specifically large flake or orchard wood kind. They provide great insulation and are rodent-resistant.

For cleaning, you can use what's known as the deep litter method again. Just add fresh shavings on top of the composting layer all winter. As it breaks down, it will naturally generate heat.

Avoid pine needles, hay, or straw with the chicks, because their small bodies can't handle the sharp spines or the moisture that attracts rodents. And you don't want to use sand, that's better for the adult chickens.
