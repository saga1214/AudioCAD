For an end-of-year celebration, a **park picnic with a mix of structured games and relaxed downtime** works great. Here’s a balanced approach:  

### 🎉 **Activities & Logistics**  
1. **Location**:  
   - **Park Picnic**: Weather-friendly, budget-friendly, and allows for outdoor games.  
   - **Cafeteria**: Low-cost and convenient if weather is uncertain.  

2. **Food**:  
   - **Parent Potluck**: Assign families to bring specific snacks (e.g., chips/dip, pizza slices, ice cream sundaes with dairy-free options since you avoid dairy).  
   - **Sundaes Bar**: A hit with kids—parents bring toppings (sprinkles, fruit, non-dairy whipped cream).  
   - **Dessert Station**: Chips, dip (use dairy-free cheese), and pizza bites for quick snacking.  

3. **Activities**:  
   - **Relay Races**: Three-legged races, sack races, or sack jumps (simple, badge-earning).  
   - **Craft Station**: Decorate sashes or friendship bracelets (tied to the Friendship or Community Badges).  
   - **Nature Scavenger Hunt**: Aligns with the Nature Badge.  
   - **Parent Participation**: Invite parents to help with games or judge races.  

### 💰 **Cost Management**  
- **Dues**: Set a small fee ($10–$20) covering badge printing, craft supplies, or field trip snacks.  
- **Snacks**: Parents split costs via potluck rotation. You can provide dairy-free chips/dip.  
- **Events**: Prioritize free/low-cost activities (e.g., local farm visits, library storytime) for field trips.  

### ✅ **Key Tips**  
- **Dairy Note**: Label snacks as "dairy-free" to avoid cross-contamination.  
- **Flexibility**: Have a rain plan (e.g., cafeteria picnic) and backup indoor games.  
- **Confidence Boost**: Focus on fun, not perfection. Brownies thrive on creativity and teamwork!  

You’ve got this—your daughters will be thrilled you stepped up! 🍪✨
