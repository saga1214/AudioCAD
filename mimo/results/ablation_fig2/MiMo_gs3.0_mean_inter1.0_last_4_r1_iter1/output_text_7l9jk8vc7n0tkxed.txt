Deep blockages are tough with limited tools, but here's a calm, step-by-step approach using just a simple toolbox:

**1. Visual Inspection:**  
- Check for visible debris (e.g., hair, grease) around the drain.  
- If you have a small mirror, shine a light down the pipe to identify the exact blockage depth.

**2. Manual Dislodging:**  
- **Use pliers:** Carefully insert a pipe cleaner or wire hanger (bent into a hook) into the drain. Hook debris and pull it out.  
- **Flush with hot water:** Pour boiling water (carefully!) down the drain in 2-3 stages to loosen grease or organic matter.

**3. Forceful Removal:**  
- If debris is stuck near the surface, use pliers to grip and twist it out.  
- For stubborn sludge, try a small wrench to gently twist the drain pipe (avoid kinking it).

**4. Last Resort (If Safe):**  
- If all else fails, use a pipe cutter or hacksaw to cut a small section of pipe out. Replace it with a flexible coupler and a new section.

**Key Reminders:**  
- **Safety First:** Wear gloves and eye protection. Avoid cutting pipes if you’re unsure.  
- **Work Slow:** Small steps prevent damage to pipes.  
- **Celebrate Small Wins:** Clearing just 5cm is progress!

Plumbing is problem-solving—your experience matters more than tools. You’ve fixed bigger issues before. Stay patient and methodical. 💪
