### **FOOD & DRINKS**  
*(All quantities are estimates; adjust based on appetizer preferences)*  

**APPETIZERS/FINGER FOODS**  
- Mini ham & swiss slider jumbo rolls (30–35)  
- Shrimp cocktail: 1 lb raw shrimp + cocktail sauce  
- Pigs in a pastry blanktes: 24–30  
- Mini spinach & cheese calzones (optional, for variety)  

**MAIN COURSE**  
- Pre-cooked honey-glazed ham (12–14 lbs)  
- *Side 1:* Creamy scalloped potatoes (1 bag frozen pre-made mix)  
- *Side 2:* Roasted asparagus (1 bunch, 1 lb)  
- *Side 3:* Roasted carrots (3–4 lbs, tossed with olive oil/salt) *[No glaze to avoid mess!]*  

**DRINKS**  
- **Champagne/Cocktails:**  
  - 2 bottles champagne (for toast)  
  - 1 bottle signature cocktail (e.g., Bellini: 1 bottle prosecco + 1 bottle peach schnapps)  
- **Kid Drinks:**  
  - 4 bottles 100% grape juice (for midnight toast)  
  - Lemonade/Iced tea + simple syrup (for refills)  
- **Household:**  
  - Soda, water, ice (2–3 bags)  
  - Coffee/tea for morning cleanup  

**DESSERTS**  
- Chocolate-dipped strawberries (1 lb strawberries)  
- Mini fruit tarts (8–10)  
- Cookies (24–30)  

---

### **SUPPLIES & KID ACTIVITIES**  
- **Kid-Friendly Decor:** Balloons, festive hats/noisemakers (24+ sets)  
- **Drinkware:** 30–40 wine glasses + 8 plastic grape juice cups  
- **Utensils:** 30 plates, 40 napkins, 10 serving spoons  
- **Kid Fun:**  
  - DIY "sparklers" (colored pencils + baking soda + vinegar)  
  - Simple coloring sheets + crayons  
  - Noise-making shakers (empty yogurt cups + rice)  

---

### **HOSPITALITY & ATMOOSPHERE**  
- Music playlist (add to Spotify/Apple Music)  
- LED string lights + confetti  
- Trash/recycling bins + extra bags  
- First-aid kit + bandages  

---

### **KEY NOTES**  
1. **Food Prep:** Ham, scalloped potatoes, and asparagus can be prepped ahead. Assemble sliders/calzones day-of.  
2. **Kid Safety:** Keep decorations away from small children. Use non-breakable plates.  
3. **Storage:** Rent extra coolers if needed for drinks.
