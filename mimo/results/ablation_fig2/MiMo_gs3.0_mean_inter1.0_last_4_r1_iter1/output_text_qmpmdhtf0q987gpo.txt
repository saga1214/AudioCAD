Okay, so you're on track with the cabinets and now facing a neighbor situation. Let's focus on the cabinets. 

After the light sanding and degreaser wash, make sure the cabinets and doors are completely dry. If they are, you can skip to the next step.

Then, apply that primer. A good tip is to use a brush to get into all the corners and edges, and then use a roller for the main surfaces. The most important thing with primer on a dark color like black is to be generous with it. A thin coat won't cover it.

Finally, before you prime, give the surfaces one last dust check. Any dust will prevent the primer from sticking properly. Once that primer is completely dry, you're ready to paint
