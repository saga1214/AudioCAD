### **Color-Coding & Memory**
- **Benefit**: Primarily reduces *mental load* by creating visual shortcuts. Color acts as a cue for your brain, so you don’t have to actively recall categories.  
- **Memory Boost**: Consistent color-labeling (e.g., blue for work, green for personal) strengthens recall over time.  
- **Key Tip**: Use 2–3 colors max. Too many colors = cognitive clutter.  

### **Background Sounds: Timing vs. Preference**
- **Optimal Switching**: Try alternating every 60–90 minutes. Short breaks between sounds let your brain reset without disrupting flow.  
- **Focus Science**: Ambient nature sounds (rain, waves) lower stress; lo-fi’s rhythmic beats sustain alertness. Switching *between* them can mimic natural circadian shifts.  
- **Test It**: Start with 90 minutes on one, then switch. Notice if focus dips or renews.  

### **Physical Documents: Environment vs. Chunking**
