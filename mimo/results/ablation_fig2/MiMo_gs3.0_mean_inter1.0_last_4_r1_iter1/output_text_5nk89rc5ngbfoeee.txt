For an end-of-year celebration, a **hybrid approach** works great! Combine fun activities with food and recognition. Here’s a balanced plan:

### 🎉 **Option 1: Park Picnic with a Twist**
- **Setup:** Rent picnic tables or use a sheltered area (weather contingency: school cafeteria backup).  
- **Food:**  
  - **Dessert Bar:** Ice cream sundaes (no dairy dip/chips), pizza slices, and s’mores kits (dairy-free chocolate).  
  - **Snacks:** Parents bring simple side snacks (fruit, chips) to share.  
- **Activities:**  
  - **Brownie-Themed Games:** Sack races, relay races (e.g., "Chip and Dip" obstacle course), badge trivia.  
  - **Craft Station:** Decorate sashes with ribbons, patches, or temporary tattoos.  
- **Why it works:** Low-cost, high-energy, and ties to Brownie traditions. Parents help with setup/cleanup.

### 🏫 **Option 2: School Cafeteria Party**
- **Setup:** Decorate with streamers, Brownie patches, and a "trophy" table for sashes.  
- **Food:**  
  - **Dairy-Free:** Chips/dip, vegan pizza, and a "Build-Your-Own Sundae" station (dairy-free ice cream).  
  - **Budget Tip:** Let parents sign up to bring one dessert/snack each.  
- **Activities:**  
  - **Award Ceremony:** Give out badges, certificates, and a "trophy" for most improved, best crafts, etc.  
  - **Movie/Pass-Out Time:** Bring a Brownie-themed movie (e.g., *Girlboss* clips) or pass out prizes (books, craft kits).  
- **Why it works:** Easy logistics, inclusive for all ages/weather, and emphasizes recognition.

### 💡 Key Tips:
1. **Cost Control:**  
   - Parents cover food/dessert costs (ask for RSVPs to gauge numbers).  
   - Troop dues ($10–$20) can fund a small prize (e.g., craft kit) for the troop.  
2. **Pregnancy Prep:**  
   - Delegate setup/cleanup to parents.  
   - Sit near shade/water; avoid heavy lifting.  
3. **Flexibility:**  
   - If outdoors, pack a tent and sunscreen.  
   - If indoors, keep it casual—focus on celebrating achievements, not elaborate planning.

### 🌟 Final Suggestion:  
**Park Picnic + Cafeteria Hybrid**  
- **Weekend Event:** Host at a local park (parents bring chairs/coolers) with a backup cafeteria plan.  
- **Activities:** 1 hour of games/crafts + 1 hour of food/talking.  
- **Recognition:** Award badges during dessert time—simple but meaningful!  

You’ll do great by focusing on fun and teamwork. Celebrating their progress will make you a hero leader, even if apprehensive at first! 🍫✨
