### Why this synthesis?  
1. **Fixed Supply (21 million coins)**:  
   - Bitcoin’s scarcity is its core innovation. Unlike fiat currencies (which can be printed infinitely) or centralized cryptocurrencies, Bitcoin’s supply is mathematically capped. This scarcity is what drives its value proposition as "digital gold."  

2. **Decentralization**:  
   - The fixed supply isn’t enforced by a corporation, government, or foundation. It’s encoded in the protocol and maintained by a decentralized network of miners, nodes, and users. No single entity can alter it.

### The Interdependence:  
Bitcoin’s genius lies in how these elements **synergize**:  
- Decentralization ensures the fixed supply can’t be manipulated.  
- The fixed supply gives decentralization economic purpose (preventing inflation).  
- Together, they create a system where money is **hard by design**, **transparent by default**, and **trustless by architecture**.  

In essence, Bitcoin’s revolutionary impact stems from this fusion: **scarcity enforced by a distributed network**, making it the first truly scarce and decentralized digital asset.
