Here are quick, elegant recipes for both cocktails using pantry staples—perfect for your formal dinner party:

### 🍸 **Vodka Mixer** (Soda-Free & Refreshing)  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- ¾ oz fresh lime juice  
- ½ oz ginger syrup (simmer 1 cup water + 1 cup sugar + ½ cup sliced ginger for 10 mins, then strain)  
- Sparkling water (soda-free!)  
- *Garnish:* Lime wheel + cucumber ribbon  

**Method:**  
Shake vodka, lime juice, and syrup with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Top with sparkling water. Garnish.  

### 🇦🇷 **Argentine Fernet (Caipirinha Twist)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz Fernet-Branca (or substitute equal parts Fernet + brandy)  
- ¾ oz fresh lime juice  
- ½ oz simple syrup (1:1 sugar:water)  
- 3–4 fresh mint leaves  
- *Garnish:* Lime wedge + mint sprig  

**Method:**  
Muddle mint with lime and syrup. Add Fernet and ice. Shake well. Strain into a rocks glass over ice. Garnish.  

### 🎯 **Bonus Pro Tips**  
- **Avoid soda allergy:** Use sparkling water, prosecco, or still wine instead.  
- **Formality note:** Serve cocktails in ice-filled glasses with elegant garnishes.  
- **Pacing:** Prep syrups/simple syrup ahead—no last-minute stress!
