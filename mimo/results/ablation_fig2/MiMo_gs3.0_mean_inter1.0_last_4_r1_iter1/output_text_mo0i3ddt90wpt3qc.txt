You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is a great gluten-free start. Another brand that specializes gluten-free camping snacks is Made Good. They have trail mix and bars that are perfect.

For some other gluten-free ideas, rice cakes are a simple and light option, and dried fruit like apricots or cranberries are also a nice addition.

And since you mentioned cycling, trail mix is actually perfect as it provides energy from nuts and seeds!
