Here are the classic recipes with measurements for all five cocktails you're hosting. I'll also suggest some themed fall finger foods.

First, the Old Fashioned. For two drinks, you'll need: one and a half ounces of bourbon rye, one sugar cube or half an ounce of simple syrup, two to three dashes of Angostura bitters, and an orange twist for garnish. Stir it with ice in a rocks glass and serve over ice with a cherry or the orange peel.

For Paper Planes, build four drinks at once. You'll need: two ounces of gin, two ounces Aperol, one ounce Amaro Nonino, and three-quarters of an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Shake with ice, strain into a coupe glass.

The Whiskey Sour: For two drinks, mix one and a half ounces bourbon, three-quarters of an ounce lemon juice, and half an ounce of simple syrup. If you want that creamy texture, add half an egg white. Shake with ice and serve over ice in a rocks glass.

The Aviation: For two drinks, combine one and a half ounces gin, half an ounce maraschino liqueur, a quarter-ounce of crème de violette, and half an ounce of fresh lemon juice. Shake with ice and serve in a cocktail glass.

For that extra gin cocktail, I recommend the French 75. For two drinks, you'll need one and a half ounces gin, three-quarters of an ounce of lemon juice, half an ounce simple syrup, and top it with two ounces of Champagne or prosecco.

For finger foods, I suggest: first, pulled pork sliders with bourbon BBQ sauce. Second, Art Deco-style potato chips with a bourbon-maple dip. Third, a bourbon-spiced apple and pecan salad. And fourth, deviled eggs decorated with mini cocktail umbrellas to fit the Art Deco airport theme.

All these drinks and foods work well in Kentucky during the fall at that Art Deco airport you're using.
