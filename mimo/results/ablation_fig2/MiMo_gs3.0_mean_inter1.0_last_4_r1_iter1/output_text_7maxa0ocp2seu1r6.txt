No problem! Here's a clear, categorized recap of all the measurement advice we've covered, perfect for your notes:

**I. Core Body Measurements (Take Every Fitting):**
1.  **Bust:** Level across the fullest part.
2.  **Natural Waist:** The narrowest part of the torso, just above the belly button.
3.  **Hips:** Level across the fullest part of the hips.

**II. Essential Measurements (Take Depending on the Project): Topps & Jackets:**
1.  **Neck:** For collars and stand collars (measure circumference at the base).
2.  **Sleeve Length:** From shoulder point to wrist hem (measure on a flat surface).
3.  Bicep (Optional but good for fit): Circumference at the widest part.
4.  Across the Back (Shoulder/Upper Back): Width from one shoulder point to the other (at the top of the shoulder seam).

**III. Essential Measurements (Take Depending on the Project): Pants/Skirts:**
1.  **Shoulder Width:** Important for good fit on jackets/shirts (distance between shoulder points).
2.  **Inseam:** Length from the crotch down to the ankle bone (measure on a flat surface).
3.  **Thigh:** Circumference at the fullest part of the thigh (usually above the knee).
4.  **Hip:** Level across the fullest part of the hips (already covered as core, but often needs checking).
5.  **Front Rise:** Distance from the crotch to the natural waist.
6.  **Back Rise:** Distance from the crotch to the waist at the back (CRITICAL for tall clients like your neighbor to prevent pants from falling down and ensure sitting comfort!).

**III. For Maximum Sitting Comfort (Especially on Pants):**
*   **Back Rise:** Must be long enough. Measure from the crotch down to the waistband.
*   **Hip & Thigh Ease:** Add extra ease (fabric room) here (typically 1-2+ inches) for comfort when sitting.
