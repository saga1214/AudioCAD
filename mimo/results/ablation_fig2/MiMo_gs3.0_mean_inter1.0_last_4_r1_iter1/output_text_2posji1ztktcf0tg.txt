I'm glad you're planning a nice dinner for your sister! Vegetarians often have strong preferences, so since she recommended a thought-provoking podcast but skips meat, you might want to pick a meal that’s flavorful, satisfying, and full of great textures. Here are a few ideas:  

- **Mushroom Risotto** – Creamy, rich, and packed with umami from mushrooms. You can add truffle oil or parmesan (if she’s okay with dairy).  
- **Stuffed Bell Peppers** – Filled with quinoa, black beans, corn, and spices. You could add a dollop of sour cream or vegan cheese on top.  
- **Pad Thai** – A vegetarian-friendly option if you skip the eggs (or ask for no egg). It’s flavorful, light, and customizable.  
- **Eggplant Parmesan** – If she’s okay with eggs and cheese, this is a crowd-pleaser. Baked or fried eggplant with a tomato sauce and melted cheese.  
- **Vegetable Stir-Fry** – Quick, healthy, and you can toss in tofu or tempeh for protein. Serve over rice with a peanut or teriyaki sauce.  

Since you initially doubted her taste 'cause of vegetarianism, maybe this is your chance to prove yourself right for once! 😄 What kind of cuisine does she usually enjoy? That might help narrow it down.
