Okay, Northern California warm escapes for swimming diet goals it is! Packing tip expanded: besides suits and cover-ups, add flip-flops, quick-dry towels, sunglasses, light sarongs to layer over suits, light long-sleeve shirt for sun protection, light sweater, reusable water bottle, and maybe a waterproof phone pouch.

For that Celiac birthday daughter adventure without heights: The Alcatraz Cruises actually have excellent gluten-free dining packages on board! It's a thrilling experience with amazing history and views. For alternatives, horseback riding at places like Bodega Bay or Point Reyes is magical. The California Redwoods in Mendocino County are awe-inspiring – walking among ancient trees feels like floating in the sky! And Monterey Bay offers incredible marine life viewing (whales, sea lions) on kayak or boat tours without heights.

Gluten-free dining gems for 12th birthday:
1.  Fisherman's Wharf: Fog Harbor Fish House (as mentioned, confirm GF needs), Scoma's (can often accommodate GF with notice).
2.  San Francisco Area: Wildseed (Tenderloin - upscale, fantastic GF), The Mill (Mission - great brunch, check GF options).
3.  Carmel-by-the-Sea: Carmel Valley Ranch (resort, amazing GF dining options like The Ranch Kitchen), Bouchon Bakery (famous, has GF bread/pastries - check current status).
4.  Monterey: Seven Gables Restaurant (coastal, can accommodate GF).

After the birthday adventure, let's plan a proper warm beach getaway! With a large vehicle and big budget, Big Sur is stunning but cooler. A truly sunny, pool-focused escape is essential.

**Recommendation 1: Laguna Beach, Orange County**
*   **Why it fits:** Warmer microclimate than NoCal, beautiful beaches, great dining, easy NoCal access.
*   **Stay:** Ocean View Lodge by Pabbie's (historic, central), or The Beach at Laguna Beach (upscale luxury, steps to beach).
*   **Do:** Swim at Heisler Park beach, rent a kayak, explore Heisler Park shops, visit Main Beach, see Heisler Park Art Gallery, hike in nearby canyons (like Crystal Cove State Park - warm layers still needed for hikes).
*   **Eat:** Heisler Park food vendors (snacks), Main Beach Cafe (casual), The Beach at Laguna Beach (upscale, ocean views), Arroyo (farm-to-table), La Sirena (Mexican).
*   **Travel:** Easy NoCal drive (~2.5 hours from Sacramento).

**Recommendation 2: Carmel Valley / Big Sur Coast, Monterey County**
*   **Why it fits:** Warmer than NoCal interior, luxury resorts, stunning coastal scenery, world-class dining (including top GF options), potential for warmer weather.
*   **Stay:** Carmel Valley Ranch (luxury, multiple GF-friendly restaurants), Inn at Terra Loma (upscale, beautiful gardens, great views), or a boutique Carmel hotel.
*   **Do:** Swim at Pfeiffer Beach (Big Sur), hike to McWay Falls (Big Sur), visit Carmel Village shops, visit the Bixby Creek Bridge, wine tasting in Carmel Valley.
*   **Eat:** Carmel Valley Ranch restaurants (excellent GF), Bouchon Bakery (Carmel - check status), The Mill (Carmel - brunch, check GF), Sardine Factory (Carmel - seafood, can accommodate GF).
*   **Travel:** ~3.5 hours from NCal, scenic drive along Highway 1.

**Recommendation 3: San Diego (Temecula Valley Wine Country is warmer but less beach-focused)**
*   **Why it fits:** Consistently warm, sunny, beaches, pools, diverse activities.
*   **Stay:** La Jolla (beachfront luxury), Coronado (iconic), or Balboa Park area (cultural hub).
*   **Do:** Swim at Coronado Beach or La Jolla Shores, snorkeling in La Jolla Cove, Balboa Park museums, USS Midway Museum, Gaslamp Quarter dining, Legoland (family fun).
*   **Eat:** SeaWorld dining (casual), The Duck Dive (San Diego), Mission Café (North Park - brunch), tons of Mexican seafood.
*   **Travel:** ~5-6 hours from NCal (scenic drive).

**Final Packing Reminder:** Don't forget sunscreen, a reusable water bottle, a waterproof bag for suits, and any essential medications. Enjoy your NoCal beach escape and your daughter's special birthday trip!
