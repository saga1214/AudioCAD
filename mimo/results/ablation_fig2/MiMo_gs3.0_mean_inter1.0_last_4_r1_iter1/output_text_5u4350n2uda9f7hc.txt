That East balcony on the fifth floor of NYC sounds perfect for adding a homey touch! Since space is limited to a folding chair and a small table, let's focus on compact household plants that will thrive in bright morning sun.

Geraniums burst with bright color and handle city wind well. Herbs like simple basil add fragrance and love that morning light.

Sturdy geraniums won't overcrowd your display. Succulents stay tiny and adore morning sun. Bird's nest snake plants are compact, sturdy, and incredibly easygoing – ideal floor placement where wind won't bother them. They're perfect for taking home after your onsite ends!
