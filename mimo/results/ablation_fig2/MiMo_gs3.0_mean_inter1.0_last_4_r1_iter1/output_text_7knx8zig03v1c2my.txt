### Treat Recommendations
*   **Best Flavors:** Small, soft, and smelly (dogs love scent!):
    *   **Peanut Butter:** Tiny bit (1/4 tsp) - high-value reward.
    *   **Cooked Chicken or Turkey:** Small pieces.
    *   **Boiled Eggs:** Small bits (cooked thoroughly).
    *   **Cheese:** Hard cheese (like cheddar) - tiny cube.
    *   **Commercial Dog Treats:** Choose low-calorie, high-value training treats (look for "training treats" or "small training size").
*   **Avoid:** Chocolate, raw dough, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol (common in sugar-free products), excessive salt/sugar.

### Clicker Training Explained
Clicker training uses a "clicker" (a small, handheld device that makes a sharp clicking sound) to bridge the gap between the desired behavior and the reward.

Here's how it works:
1.  **Clicker as a "Go" Signal:** The clicker doesn't train the dog; it marks the *exact* moment Galileo does something right.
2.  **Timing is Crucial:** Click the *instant* he performs the desired behavior (e.g., the *instant* he sits).
3.  **Immediate Reward:** Immediately after clicking, give him a treat (or use the clicker itself as a reward later - this is called "clicker shaping").
4.  **Repetition:** Repeat this many times. The clicker tells him, "That's the thing I want! Now get a reward!"
5.  **Shaping the Behavior:** Start with easy behaviors. For "sit":
    *   Click the *moment* he lowers his butt slightly.
    *   Give a treat.
    *   Gradually increase the difficulty (click only when his butt is *all the way* down).
    *   Slowly add the verbal cue ("sit") *after* he sits, then start clicking *before* he sits but *after* he starts lowering, eventually clicking *as he sits* and adding the verbal cue.

**Why it's effective:** It gives Galileo clear, consistent feedback ("That's it!"). He learns to associate the click sound with getting a reward, making training sessions more efficient and fun.

**Key Points:**
*   **Clicker is just a tool:** You can use a clicker, a pen clicker, or even just your fingers snapping *if you are incredibly consistent*.
*   **Consistency is key:** Use the same marker sound (click) every time.
*   **Start simple:** Practice with easy behaviors first to build confidence.
*   **Be patient:** Training takes time and repetition.

Clicker training is highly recommended for its clarity and effectiveness, especially for shaping behaviors step-by-step. It leverages your nerdiness perfectly by turning training into a clear, observable communication system! Good luck with Galileo!
