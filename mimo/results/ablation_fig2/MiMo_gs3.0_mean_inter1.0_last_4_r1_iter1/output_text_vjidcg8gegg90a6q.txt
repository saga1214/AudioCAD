For winter chicks on concrete, the key is deep insulation. You can use what's called the deep litter method again. A thick layer of large-flake pine shavings still works great despite rodent concerns if you manage them properly—just add new ones on top constantly. Another option is to place insulated trays, like styrofoam or cardboard trays, inside the coop. You can line those trays with sand or even use a simple rubber mat as their bedding, just like for the main coop. The concrete floor provides great natural warmth, and this setup will prevent them from getting too close to the cold ground. Just remember to provide a draft-free area for them to huddle together for warmth.
