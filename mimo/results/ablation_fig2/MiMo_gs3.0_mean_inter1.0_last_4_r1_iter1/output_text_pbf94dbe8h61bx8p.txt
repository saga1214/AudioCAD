### 🍪 For Quick & Minimalist Cookies
1. **Best Simple Cookie:** **Snowball Cookies**  
   - No rolling: Mix dough, scoop onto sheet, add sprinkles/sanding sugar, bake.  
   - *Why:* Zero rolling, minimal cleanup, festive look.

2. **Baking Strategy:**  
   - Use pre-made cookie cutters (no cutting).  
   - Set up stations: pre-measured dough, toppings, decorated by kids.  
   - Freeze dough between batches to control time.

3. **Cooperation Hack:** Assign roles:  
   - **2-year-old:** Add sprinkles.  
   - **5-year-old:** Scoop dough.  
   - **10-year-old:** Decorate.  
   - **15-year-old:** Taste-test (and ensure evenness!).
