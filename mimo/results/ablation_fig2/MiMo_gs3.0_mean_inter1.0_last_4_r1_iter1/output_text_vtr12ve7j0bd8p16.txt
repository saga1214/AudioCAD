That's a great question. Calgary is in the foothills of the Rockies, so you have a lot of options. For a romantic adventure, I'd suggest a few things.

First, there's Banff and Lake Louise. It's a classic for a reason. You can take a scenic drive, walk around the beautiful lake, and visit the iconic Banff Gondola. The views are stunning.

Another idea is the Canmore and Johnston Canyon area. The canyon is beautiful, and you can walk across the suspended bridges. It's less crowded than Banff sometimes and still feels very romantic.

If you're up for a bit of a drive, the Kananaskis Country area is fantastic. It has beautiful hiking trails, waterfalls, and even a scenic drive called the Kananaskis Lakes Road that's considered one of the most beautiful in Canada.
