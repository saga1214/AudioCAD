Lockport's connection to the Erie Canal is central to its history and identity. A fantastic way to learn more would be to visit the Lockport Historical Society Museum. They have a huge collection of artifacts and photos, and they can give you a real deep dive into the town's canal past. 

You could also visit the Erie Canal Heritage Center, which is great for a more modern, interactive look at the canal's history and its impact on the area. 

And of flight to see the locks, the Lockport Locks State Park is the place. It's a beautiful park with great walking paths right along the locks, so you can see them in action. 

So to sum up, for a vacation that avoids water activities but still offers nature, museums, and interesting history very close to home, the Finger Lakes, the Adirondacks along Route 31 towards Lockport, and Buffalo all sound like excellent choices. I hope you relax and have a wonderful summer vacation!
