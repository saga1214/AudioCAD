That's a wonderful way to relax and reclaim your self-care time. Cooking can be incredibly therapeutic, especially after all those condo issues. Here are a few simple dinner ideas you could whip up with ingredients from the grocery store:

1.  **Garlic & Oil Pasta:** Toss pasta (like penne or spaghetti) with lots of olive oil, minced garlic, salt, pepper, and fresh parsley. Add halved cherry tomatoes and torn basil leaves just before serving. Simple, fresh, and delicious.
2.  **Hearty Tomato Basil Soup:** Make a big pot of soup with canned crushed tomatoes, vegetable or chicken broth, onion, garlic, dried oregano, and basil. Top with grilled cheese sandwiches for the perfect combo.
3.  **Simple Stir-Fry:** Grab your favorite veggies (broccoli, bell peppers, snap peas, carrots), some tofu or chicken, and a bottle of stir-fry sauce. Cook the protein, then quickly stir-fry the veggies in a hot pan with a little oil and soy sauce. Serve over rice or noodles.
4.  **Creamy Pasta Primavera:** Cook pasta, then toss with a simple sauce made from cream cheese, parmesan cheese, garlic, lemon zest, salt, and pepper. Stir in a mix of chopped spring mix or peas and asparagus. It's comforting and light.
5.  **Taco Bar:** Set up bowls of seasoned ground beef or turkey, black beans, corn, salsa, shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, avocado, sour cream, and tortillas. Let everyone build their own!
