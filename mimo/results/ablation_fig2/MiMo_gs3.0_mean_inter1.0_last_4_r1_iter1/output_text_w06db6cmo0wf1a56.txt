Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's ten calzones for the bachelor! I need to make a mess-minimal cooler lineup. Health first but with real fun options too, like sugar-free sodas and celebrating his love for calzones.

Drinks are key for a hot day. Everyone needs water bottles, refillable or reusable. Sugar-free sodas for adults, low-sugar juice boxes for kids. Electrolyte drinks could be a good idea too, like individual coconut water packs, just in case anyone gets tired. Lemonade or iced tea in pitchers is great, but I have to remind everyone to keep them cold and maybe add ice packs to the coolers.

Snacks should be grab-and-go zero mess. Cheese sticks are always a hit, grapes are easy for little hands, and single-serve pretzels or trail mix bags work well. Baby carrots with pre-measured hummus cups are perfect. Maybe some whole-grain crackers too, for a little crunch.

For meals, since he loves calzones, pizza cut into mini-calzone shapes could be a great compromise. You get that foldable, handheld feel everyone loves, plus it's customizable. Or, pre-made wraps and sandwiches are a solid backup plan.

Desserts need to feel special but be super easy. Cupcakes are perfect because they're individual and no utensils needed. Brownie bars in a foil tray would also work well, just grab a bar and go.

So, for the shopping list, I'll organize it by category. I'll remind you to use insulated coolers with lots of ice packs, label them, and maybe bring wet wipes for quick cleanups. Most importantly, try to pre-portion everything so it's grab-and-go from the start. This should let you all run around and enjoy the theme park with very little fuss. Have an absolutely amazing time!
