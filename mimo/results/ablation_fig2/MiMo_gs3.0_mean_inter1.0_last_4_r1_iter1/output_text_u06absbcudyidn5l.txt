Of course, I can clarify that. Chronic redness has many potential causes. Besides rosacea and sun damage we mentioned before, other common culprits include eczema, psoriasis, contact dermatitis from irritants or allergies, or even certain infections.

As for vasculitis, yes, it's a condition where the blood vessels themselves become inflamed. The size of the vessel that's affected is a key way doctors classify it. For example, large vessel vasculitis or medium vessel vasculitis. The inflammation can affect the skin in various ways, from a specific rash to a more general redness. As I said, the main cause is an autoimmune process where the body's immune system mistakenly attacks its own blood vessels. This inflammation can lead to serious complications if it restricts blood flow to organs, like the kidneys or lungs.

I would definitely recommend talking to a rheumatologist or dermatologist to figure out if that's a possibility for you.
