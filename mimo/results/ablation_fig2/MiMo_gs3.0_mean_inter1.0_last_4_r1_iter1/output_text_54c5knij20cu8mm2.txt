Okay, let’s tackle this systematically. 

First, the left-brain versus right-brain dichotomy. It’s largely a myth in modern neuroscience. The brain works in networks, not as separate left and right hemispheres handling creative versus logical tasks. However, the metaphor is still useful because people can think in terms of analytical versus intuitive modes. So, while not biologically accurate, it can be a helpful framework for understanding different ways of processing information.

Now, for career options that blend creativity, structure, and tangible impact. Here are a few ideas:

First, there's UX/UI design. It involves creativity in designing interfaces while ensuring usability through structured testing and data. It’s also a field with high demand.

Second, architecture. It’s deeply creative, but also requires precision, math, and structural knowledge. You can build things with lasting impact.

Third, scientific illustration. It’s a mix of artistic skill and understanding scientific concepts. It’s objective but also creative.

Fourth, creative coding or data visualization. This combines programming logic with artistic expression. You can create interactive media or visualizations from data.

Fifth, game design. It requires both creativity and structured systems like mechanics and rules. It can be very rewarding.

Finally, there's technical writing. It requires clarity and structure but also creativity in making complex information accessible. It’s objective and can have a lasting impact.

These fields offer a balance of the structured and the creative that might fit your interests.
