Of course! Here's a quick reference for Corgi Princess's treats:

**Homemade Ideas:**
1. Frozen Bites: Mashed pumpkin or sweet potato frozen in small mounds.
2. Peanut Butter Oat Cookies: Mix peanut-free oats with peanut butter (NO xylitol), shape, bake, and cool.
3. Cooked Protein: Small carrot sticks or shredded/patted-dry pieces of plain cooked chicken.

**Popular Brands:**
- Blue Buffalo
- Wellness Treats
- Zuke's Mini Naturals
- Greenies
- Merrick

**Training Tip (Sit Command):**
- Hold treat near her nose.
- Slowly lift it over her head.
- The instant she sits, say "Sit!" and give the reward.

**Potty Training Tips:**
- Take her to her spot frequently.
- Reward heavily with praise and treats when she goes outside.
- Clean accidents thoroughly.
- Maintain a consistent feeding schedule.

Always give tiny portions and prioritize natural, healthy ingredients! Enjoy pampering your little princess! 🐾
