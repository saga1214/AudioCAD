### 🔊 Vocal Endurance & Hydration
- **Warm-ups:** Lip trills, humming, tongue twisters (5 mins daily).

- **Rest:** Take 5-min breaks every 25–30 mins. Stretch neck/shoulders.
- **Posture:** Sit upright, feet flat, mic at chin-level.
