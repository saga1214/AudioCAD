Okay, efficiency is a tricky concept here. If we're talking about the highest energy output per square meter, it's solar, especially newer panels. For the lowest long-term cost, solar is again leading the way. Hydropower is incredibly reliable and efficient once built. But if you have to pick just one that works almost anywhere, scales easily, and keeps getting cheaper, solar takes the top spot overall. Hydropower is fantastic in specific locations, and wind is strong in windy areas, but solar has the broadest appeal.
