For Asian noodles in Dallas, you’ve got some great options. Banville in Lower Greenville is fantastic for Vietnamese pho and noodle dishes. There’s also Da Vang, a popular spot for Vietnamese food, and Sushi Dai for Japanese sushi and ramen. If you’re in the Asian Market area, you can find great noodle spots like Pho 79.

For protein bars, I’d suggest Quest, they’re high in protein and low in sugar. Or Optimum Nutrition, they’re strong on muscle recovery. Also, consider RXBAR for clean ingredients, or Clif Builder’s if you want a balanced snack. Let me know if you want more recommendations!
