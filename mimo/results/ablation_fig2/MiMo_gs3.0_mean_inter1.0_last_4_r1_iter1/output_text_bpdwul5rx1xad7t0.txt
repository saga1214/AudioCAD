Of course! With a two-bedroom apartment and expecting a first baby, I'd suggest shorter, scenic road trips of about four to five hours. These are perfect for exploring British Columbia's stunning coast and mountains.

Here are five great options for you. First, you could head to Vancouver Island, to places like Tofino or Ucluelet. There, you can see the famous sea stacks at Tofino and watch the whales from a boat. Next, there's the Sunshine Coast, with towns like Langdale and Steveston, where you can walk the wharf and even see some salmon. A third option is the Fraser Canyon, near Hope, where you can see the rock carvings at the Kalamus River and hike the Spotted Eagle Trail. Fourth, you could visit the Okanagan Lakes area, which is famous for its wineries and orchards. And finally, a fifth great trip is to the Coast Mountains, near Pemberton, where you can hike and see the famous hot springs at Lionshead. 

Each of these trips offers a mix of beautiful scenery, fun activities, and great places to eat. Enjoy your adventures!
