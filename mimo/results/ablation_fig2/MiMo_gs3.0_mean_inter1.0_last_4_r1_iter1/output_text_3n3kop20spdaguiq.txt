Dehydrating is a great way to preserve fruits and vegetables. Some of the most popular items to dehydrate are apples, bananas, strawberries, and zucchini. You can also dehydrate meat, such as beef, turkey, and venison, but it's very important to make sure that the meat is cooked thoroughly before you dehydrate it. Dehydrated meats are great for making jerky. You can also dehydrate vegetables such as carrots, onions, and green beans. Dehydrated onions and garlic are especially useful for adding flavor to soups and stews. Just make sure that you store your dehydrated foods in an airtight container to keep them fresh.
