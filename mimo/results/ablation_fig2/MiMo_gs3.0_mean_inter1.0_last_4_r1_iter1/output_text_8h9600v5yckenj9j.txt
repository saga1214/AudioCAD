For Halloween candy, the crowd favorites are usually the classics that everyone loves. You can grab packs of Reese’s Peanut Butter Cups, Hershey’s Kisses, Skittles, Gummies, and maybe some sour candy like Sour Patch Kids or Warheads. Snickers and Milky Way bars are also always a hit for the adults. You could even get some Halloween-themed candy bags or boxes to make it look extra spooky tomorrow night!
