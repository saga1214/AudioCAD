Got a couple of low-stress cooking ideas. One option is making sushi bowls. It’s hands-on, so you can focus on the food and not talk as much, and lactose-intolerance isn’t an issue. Another idea is a simple pasta dish. You could do something like fettuccine alfredo using lactose-free sauce, or just a simple garlic and oil pasta with veggies she does happen to eat, like bell peppers or spinach. Both are quick and can be made together without too much stress. Keep it simple.
