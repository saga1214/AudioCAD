Here's how to tackle each installation safely and effectively:

### 1. Mounting a TV
* **Check Wall Type:** Use a stud finder to locate studs (critical for heavy TVs). If no studs, use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for your TV's weight.
* **Measure Twice:** Ensure the TV's center aligns with the mount's bracket points. Account for TV thickness.
* **Secure Mount:** Attach the mount to the wall first (using anchors if needed), then secure the TV to the mount.
* **Remote Options:** Consider a TV wall mount with built-in drilling guides for drywall.

### 2. Hanging Planters
* **Weight Test:** Hang a bag of sand in the planter spot first to test wall integrity.
* **Use Strong Hooks:** Install heavy-duty toggle bolts or molly bolts rated for the planter's weight.
* **Height & Drainage:** Ensure it's at a safe height (out of reach of pets/kids). Add drainage holes to planters if missing.
* **Plant Choice:** Start with lightweight plants (e.g., snake plants, pothos).

### 3. Installing Floating Shelves
* **Measure & Mark:** Mark shelf positions with painter's tape (e.g., 12-18" above wall). Ensure spacing is even.
* **Use Proper Anchors:** For drywall, use L-brackets or heavy-duty anchors rated for the shelf's weight.
* **Level & Secure:** Attach brackets to the wall first, then screw the shelf onto the brackets.
* **Weight Limit:** Check shelf weight capacity – avoid overloading.

### General Tips:
- **Safety First:** Wear safety glasses and use a stud finder with a nail detection feature.
- **Tools Needed:** Drill, screwdriver, level, tape measure, stud finder.
- **Check Landlord Rules:** Confirm you have permission to drill in key areas.
- **Start Small:** Install one item at a time to avoid fatigue.
