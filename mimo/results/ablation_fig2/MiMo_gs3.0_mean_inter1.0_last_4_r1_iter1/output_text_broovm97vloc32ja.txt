Yes, that is a fantastic idea, and many publishers *do* seek out complete manuscripts from authors who can also illustrate their own work. However, the process is different.

For a children's book, you'll want to find a publisher who specifically accepts 'fully illustrated manuscripts'. These are often smaller, independent presses or publishers with a strong illustrated book line.

The process is a bit more involved. You'll need to create a complete manuscript, which includes not just the text but also your illustrations. These illustrations need to be high-resolution, on a white background, and often formatted in a specific way. You'll submit this complete package, along with your query letter, to publishers that accept them. 

This approach can be great because you have full creative control. However, it can also be a bit more work on your part, so it's a good idea to research publishers thoroughly before you begin.
