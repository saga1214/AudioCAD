Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration, since their 'temporada de otoño' is basically peak fall foliage, especially in places like Chiapas and the Yucatán Peninsula. September through November is perfect for capturing those vibrant colors. You could also consider Italy or the UK, which are famous for fall colors. But Mexico is a great option, especially if you want to combine painting with travel. Just make sure to pack plenty of your autumn paints and a sketchbook! Safe travels and happy painting! 🎨🍂
