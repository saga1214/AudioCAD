That's a very good instinct. Older comics are often rarer, which makes them more valuable. Here are ten significant books from that era.

Number one is *Action Comics* number one again, Superman's first full appearance from 1938.

Second is *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, which is Batman's first appearance from 1939.

Third is *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen from 1939, where Spider-Man first appeared.

Another great one is *Whiz Comics* number one from 1951, which was the very first appearance of the Human Torch.

Then there's *Tales of Suspense* number thirty-nine, which was the first comic to feature both Captain America and Iron Man.

Also, *The Avengers* number one from 1963 is the first official team-up of your heroes Luke Cage Power Man and the Defenders.

*Marvel Super-Heroes* number one, which first introduced the Hulk, is another key book.

*The Incredible Hulk* number one, from 1962, is the first solo Hulk comic.

And finally, *Fantastic Four* number one from 1961, which was the first appearance of the Fantastic Four. These are all major, foundational comics that are highly sought after.
