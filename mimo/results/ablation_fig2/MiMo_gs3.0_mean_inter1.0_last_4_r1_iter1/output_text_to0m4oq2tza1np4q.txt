### **Pickle-Spiced Salmon with Pickle Juice Glaze**  
1. **Marinate the salmon**: Whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, honey, salt, pepper, and fresh herbs (like dill or parsley). Toss in a few pickle spears and let the salmon marinate for 30 minutes.  
2. **Make the glaze**: Simmer pickle juice with a splash of olive oil, a pinch of sugar, and a dash of black pepper until slightly thickened (about 5 minutes).  
3. **Finish the dish**: Pan-sear or bake the salmon, then brush with the glaze during the last few minutes of cooking. Garnish with extra pickles or fresh herbs.  

This adds a fun twist to your salmon while using up the pickle juice—perfect for bloating relief (low-sodium!) and heart health (rich in omega-3s!). Enjoy! 🍋🐟
