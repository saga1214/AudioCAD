For Asian noodles in Dallas, I’d recommend a few great spots. First, there's Sakura Ramen in Little Tokyo, which is famous for its rich, creamy ramen. Second, you have Pho 79, which is great for pho, but their noodles are also delicious. And third, there's a place called Noodle House that serves a wide variety of Asian-inspired noodles. 

For protein bars, I'd suggest Quest Nutrition, they're high in protein and low in sugar. Another option is Optimum Nutrition, which has great taste and protein content. And finally, there's the brand Clif Builders, which are great for post-workout fuel. Hope you enjoy your meal!
