No, the first Thanksgiving was not a happy occasion for both sides. The Pilgrims survived the plague of the first winter, but the Wampanoag had already lost many people to disease. For them, the English arrival was a tragedy.

The feast itself was a diplomatic event, a temporary cooperation between two groups with very different goals. While the Pilgrims celebrated their survival, the Wampanoag knew that the English presence would change their lives forever.

So, it was a moment of gratitude, but it was not a happy event for everyone involved.
