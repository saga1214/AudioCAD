To confirm, driving from L.A. to Phoenix typically takes six hours without major delays. A flight is significantly faster, with the actual flight time from L.A. to Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport being about an hour.

Regarding your question about buying a horse, yes, you can purchase a horse in Phoenix. However, it is important to know that you will need to register the horse with the Arizona Department of Agriculture and also get a health certificate from a veterinarian.

As you mentioned, either driving at about five-and-a-half hours or flying for about an hour are the two main options for getting to Phoenix. For your trip this weekend, you'll just need to decide based on your preference for travel time versus convenience.
