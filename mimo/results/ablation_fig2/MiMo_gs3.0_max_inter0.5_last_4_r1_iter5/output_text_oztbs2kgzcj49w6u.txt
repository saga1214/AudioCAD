Lockport is very proud of its connection to the Erie Canal, which is celebrated at the Erie Canal Museum, located right in the historic canal locks. It's an excellent place to learn all about the engineering and the history of the canal era.

The city also has the Lockport Museum, which is in a restored canal house and covers the canal's impact on the local economy. You could also visit the Forts Niagara area for its own fascinating history. And while it's a bit further, you could also drive along the Cattaraugus Escarpment Trail for some beautiful hiking with amazing views of the canal's route.
