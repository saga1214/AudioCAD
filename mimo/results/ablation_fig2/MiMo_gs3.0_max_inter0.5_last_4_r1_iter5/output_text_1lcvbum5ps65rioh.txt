### U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia
If you make an unauthorized U-turn in BC, you can expect:
1.  **Ticket:** A ticket for "Improper Turn" (Code 172) – typically **$160**.
2.  **More Serious Charges:** If your U-turn causes an accident or blocks traffic, charges could include dangerous driving or obstruction.
3.  **Accident Liability:** If you cause an accident, you could face civil liability.

**Key Rule:** Only U-turn when you have a clear view of both directions (150m+) and no conflicting traffic, *and* the law/signal allows it at that location.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients
Here's a classic, traditional recipe (serves 4):

**Ingredients:**
*   **400g (14 oz) Dried Spaghetti** (Tagliatelle works too)
*   **200g (7 oz) Pancetta** (or Guanciale - diced small)
*   **4 large Eggs**
*   **100g (1 cup) Pecorino Romano cheese** (finely grated, or Parmesan for a milder taste)
*   **50g (½ cup) Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese** (finely grated)
*   **Freshly Ground Black Pepper** (lots!)
*   **Salt** (for pasta water)
*   **Optional:** 1 small garlic clove (minced, for pancetta)

**Essential Tools:** Large pot, large skillet or pan, mixing bowl, tongs, whisk or fork.

**Key Technique Notes:**
1.  **No Cream:** Authentic carbonara relies on eggs and cheese for creaminess.
2.  **Tempering Eggs:** Whisk eggs, cheese, and pepper together. Add hot pasta *slowly* to temper the eggs (prevents scrambling).
3.  **Hot Pan, Hot Pancetta:** Render pancetta in a hot pan until crisp. Remove from heat before adding eggs to avoid cooking them.
4.  **Reserve Pasta Water:** Crucial for adjusting sauce consistency.
