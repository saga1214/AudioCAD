First, Laguna Beach: It's about 2.5 hours south of Sacramento. The weather in December averages 65 to 75 degrees Fahrenheit, which is perfect for year-round swimming at Main Beach. Packing notes: two high-quality bikinis, a rash guard for sun protection, quick-dry cover-ups, flip-flops, sunglasses, a wide-brimmed hat, high-SPF mineral sunscreen, lightweight layers like tank tops and sarongs, and a compact dry bag for your suit. Dining can accommodate Celiac needs at places like The Beachcomber Cafe. For birthday adventure without heights, you can do a kayak tour with marine biologists or a surf lesson at El Capitan. The stay should be in a beachfront condo for space, and you can rent a large SUV to explore the coast efficiently. 

Second option is Carmel-by-the-Sea, which is closer to SF at about 2 hours. The December weather is similar, around 60 to 70 degrees, with plenty of wading pools and ocean swimming. Packing is the same as for Laguna. A great birthday adventure alternative is the self-guided cave tour at Point Pinos Lighthouse. For dinner, The Post is fantastic but requires reservations well in advance. The drive is scenic, and Carmel offers charming beachside inns. 

Both locations are warm, allow swimming, and have plenty of dining flexibility. Laguna is more beach-focused, while Carmel has a historic town vibe. You can choose based on whether you prefer a secluded coastline or a quaint village atmosphere.
