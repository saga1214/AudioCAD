Okay, great job getting the hardware off and sanding them. Before you prime both the doors and boxes, there are a few more steps.

First, give all the parts another cleaning with a tack cloth to wipe up any dust from the sanding.

Then, you'll need to protect your work area by laying down some painter's tape on the countertop and backsplash. This will prevent paint from splattering onto them.

Finally, before you primer, use painter's tape to mask off the edges of the cabinets where they meet the walls. This ensures a clean line and stops the primer from bleeding onto your drywall. Once all that masking is done, you're ready to apply the primer.
