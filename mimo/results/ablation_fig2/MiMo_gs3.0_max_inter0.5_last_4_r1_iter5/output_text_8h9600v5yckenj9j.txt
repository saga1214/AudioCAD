For candy tomorrow, grab the classics that everyone loves: Reese’s Cups, Hershey’s chocolate, Skittles, gummy worms, and maybe some mini chocolate bars. You could also get Halloween-themed bags of chips or popcorn for a fun, sweet-and-salty mix. If you want to avoid disappointment, stick to big, popular brands instead of the unknown Halloween candies, and maybe buy a few of each popular item to have a variety.
