Yes, absolutely. If you have both the text and the illustrations ready, you can submit a complete manuscript. This is a very strong position to be in. However, the process is still different. For a children's book, the publisher is going to want to see the actual artwork, not just a description of it. 

So, you would need to prepare a professional manuscript. This usually includes the title page, a table of contents, and, most importantly, the first ten to fifteen pages of the book, complete with your illustrations. You'd then send this complete package to an agent or publisher who specializes in children's books.
