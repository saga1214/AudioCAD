### Key Tips:
1. **Avoid Accusations**: Never say "unfair." Frame it as curiosity about improvement.  
2. **Keep it Short**: He’s more likely to respond to a brief, professional request.  
3. **Offer an Exit**: Giving him an easy out ("even an email response is fine") reduces defensiveness.
