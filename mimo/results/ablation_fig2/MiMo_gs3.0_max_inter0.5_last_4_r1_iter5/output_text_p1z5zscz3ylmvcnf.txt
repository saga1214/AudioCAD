Okay, so you’re looking for classic cars that your in-laws might be interested in as an investment. Since your father-in-law is an 'old-school' American guy who prides himself on independence and tradition, you’d want to suggest cars that have historical significance, American-made, and have a strong reputation for appreciating in value. Here are five classic cars that are great investments and would probably appeal to his taste.

First, there's the Ford Mustang, specifically from the first-generation models between 1964 and 1973. The Mustang is an American icon, and the first-gen models are highly sought after. A well-maintained '65 to '67 Mustang can triple in value. If he gets into restoring one, it would feel like a project he can control, which would suit his self-sufficient nature.

Next is the Chevrolet Camaro, from the first two generations, '67 to '72. It’s a direct competitor to the Mustang and is also American-made. A 'Z28 or a 'SS model can appreciate significantly. It’s a great alternative if he’s not as into Mustangs but still wants a high-performance American classic.

Another great option is the Dodge Challenger or Charger, especially from the '70s. Models like the '71 'Barracuda' or a '70 'Charger 'Hemi' have seen massive value increases. If he likes muscle cars and big engines, this would be a perfect fit.

Then there's the Pontiac GTO, from '64 to '67. It was one of the first muscle cars and is highly collectible. A 'GTO 'Judge' or a 'Road Runner' can become extremely valuable. It’s another American classic that would appeal to his pride.

And finally, the Mercury Cougars. They’re less common than Mustangs but are still highly appreciating. A 'Cobra Jet' or a 'Shelby' model can be worth a lot more than you'd expect. It’s a good option if he wants something a bit different but still American.

These cars not only hold their value well but also have a lot of nostalgia, which might make him more interested in them. Plus, if he enjoys working on cars, restoring one could be a fun way for him to stay active mentally and physically. It’s a great way to combine his pride in American cars with a smart investment idea.
