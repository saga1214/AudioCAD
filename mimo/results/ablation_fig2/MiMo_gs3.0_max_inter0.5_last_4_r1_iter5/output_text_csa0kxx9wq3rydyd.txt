Based on our menu, here's what you'll need. For smoking, you'll need a smoker. For roasting, an oven. For roasting veggies, a toaster oven is a good option. For roasting picky-kid food, you'll use the oven again. For side dishes that boil water, you'll need a stovetop. For side dishes that require heat, you'll need a microwave. For making cranberry sauce, an Instant Pot. For the prime rib, a slow cooker. For the pumpkin cheesecake, a double oven. For the fruit tart, you'll need an oven, a mixer, and a refrigerator. And for the apple crisp, another oven and a mixer. The cooking methods we covered are smoking, roasting, boiling, roasting again, microwaving, steaming, braising, slow cooking, baking, and no-baking.
