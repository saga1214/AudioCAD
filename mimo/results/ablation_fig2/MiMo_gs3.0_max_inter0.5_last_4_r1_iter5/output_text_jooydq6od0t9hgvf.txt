Here are quick, elegant drink recipes for your dinner party cocktails—keeping dietary restrictions, allergy, and formality in mind:

### 1. **Vodka Mixer (Refreshing & Light)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz vodka (your leftover)  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- 4 oz soda-free sparkling water (avoiding soda allergy)  
- 2 cucumber slices (optional garnish)  
- Fresh mint sprig (garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
Shake vodka and lime juice with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Top with sparkling water. Stir gently. Garnish with cucumber/mint.  

### 2. **Argentine Fernet-Spanish (Spirited & Herbal)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz Fernet-Branca (or Fernet-Mercure)  
- 4 oz *soda-free ginger beer* (sugar-free if preferred)  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- *Optional:* 0.25 oz demerara syrup (for sweetness)  
- Lime wedge + mint sprig (garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
Shake Fernet, lime juice, and syrup (if using) with ice. Strain into a rocks glass filled with ice. Top with ginger beer. Gently stir. Garnish with lime/mint.  

---

### **Why These Work:**  
- **Soda-Free:** Both use sparkling water/ginger beer (no soda).  
- **Dietary-Friendly:** Vegetarian/omnivore included (no meat-based garnishes).  
- **Formal Appeal:** Clean presentation, classic glassware (coupe/rocks).  
- **Efficiency:** Ready in <5 minutes each.  

**Pro Tip:** Pre-chill glasses to keep drinks ice-cold. Label drinks clearly to avoid soda-allergy confusion. Enjoy your party! 🥂
