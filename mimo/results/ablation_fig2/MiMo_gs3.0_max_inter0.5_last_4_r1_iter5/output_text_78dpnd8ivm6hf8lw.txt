Here's a quick recap using the child-friendly analogy:

1. **Heart**: The **gas station and engine**  
   Pumps cars (blood) and provides fresh oxygen fuel.

2. **Lungs**: Another **gas station**  
   Where blood cars stop for *fresh oxygen fuel*.

3. **Veins**: **Roads back to the gas station**  
   Carry blood away from body parts to the heart for refueling.

4. **Kidneys**: **Car washes/checkpoints**  
   Filter waste/junk from blood, turning it into urine.

5. **Liver**: **Service station**  
   Processes nutrients and breaks down harmful stuff.

**The Journey**:  
Heart → Arteries (big highways) → Capillaries (tiny streets) → Muscles/Organs (deliver fuel) → Veins (return to heart/lungs) → Repeat! 🚗💨
