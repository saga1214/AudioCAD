Okay, diesel camper vans age, and pre-trip maintenance is crucial. For brakes, the main thing is to listen for any grinding or squealing noises—those are bad signs. Also, if the vehicle pulls to one side or the pedal feels soft, that’s something to look into. You can check the brake fluid level; if it’s low, that could mean there’s a leak. For spark plugs, diesel engines use high-compression and heat, so they’re tough. You’d need to remove the air intake and access the spark plugs from underneath the engine. They’re usually a 12 or 14mm socket. But honestly, it’s a job for a mechanic unless you’re really comfortable with engines. A good diesel service shop can do it for you and check for other things like fuel system health. Just make sure you get a full inspection before you hit the road. Safety first.
