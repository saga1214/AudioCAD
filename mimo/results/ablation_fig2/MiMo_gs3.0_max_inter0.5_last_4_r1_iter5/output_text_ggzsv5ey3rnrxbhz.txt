The body does break down and eliminate PFAS over time, but it's a very slow process, which is why they're called 'forever chemicals'. There isn't a quick fix like a juice cleanse. The best thing you can do is reduce your ongoing exposure.

Here are five of the top products you should avoid to minimize your exposure:

First, definitely non-stick cookware. Switch to stainless steel, cast iron, or glass cookware instead.

Second is grease-resistant packaging, like what comes on fast-food wrappers, pizza boxes, and microwave popcorn bags.

Third, avoid products and materials labeled as stain-resistant or waterproof, such as carpets, furniture, some outdoor clothing, and even leather jackets.

Fourth is cosmetics and even certain types of dental floss, as some brands can contain these chemicals.

And fifth is since these can get into tap water, using a certified water filter can really help.
