Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items without violating the "no loud noises/drilling holes" rule, using minimal disruption:

### 1. **TV Mount Installation**
   - **Tools Needed:** Tape measure, pencil, stud finder, drill (for small pilot holes), level, drill bits.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder to confirm wall structure. Landlords often prohibit drilling into load-bearing walls (which are usually studs).
     2. **Mark Mount Position:** Measure where you want the TV. Hold the mount against the wall and mark its edges with a pencil.
     3. **Drill Pilot Holes:** If mounting directly into a stud, drill a small pilot hole through the mount's screw holes. If no stud, use heavy-duty drywall anchors (noise-free).
     4. **Attach Mount:** Secure the mount to the wall using screws. Test stability before hanging the TV.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Tools Needed:** Suction cups, strong adhesive hooks (like 3M Command Strips), or picture hooks.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Clean Wall Surface:** Wipe the wall dry to ensure adhesives grip properly.
     2. **Use Suction Cups:** For lightweight plants, use strong suction cups (e.g., 20lb capacity). Press firmly for 30 seconds.
     3. **Adhesive Hooks:** For medium-weight plants (up to 5lb), use Command Strips or picture hooks. Follow manufacturer instructions.
     4. **Plant Placement:** Hang near windows for light. Avoid drilling into ceilings or load-bearing walls.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Tools Needed:** Heavy-duty adhesive strips (like 3M Command Float), level, measuring tape.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Mark Shelf Level:** Use a level to draw an even line on the wall.
     2. **Apply Adhesive Strips:** Press strips firmly into the wall. Follow the weight limits (typically 5-10lb per strip).
     3. **Attach Shelf:** Place the shelf onto the strips. Hold for 2 minutes before setting items.
     4. **Adjust for Level:** Use a level to check alignment. Secure with adhesive if needed.

### **Pro Tips to Stay Quiet & Focus:**
- **Work in Short Bursts:** Schedule 30-minute decorating sessions on Sundays to avoid distraction during workdays.
- **Minimize Noise:** Use battery-operated tools or hand tools (no drilling). Place furniture on soft feet to avoid floor noise.
- **Stay Energized:** Take 5-minute stretch breaks every hour during work. Do calf stretches and walk around every 30 minutes to combat stiffness.
- **Gym Integration:** Wake up 1 hour early on weekends for your 2-hour gym session to avoid weekend work overload.

### **Safety Checklist:**
- ❌ Never drill into electrical outlets or pipes.
- ✅ Always test adhesives/suction cups before hanging heavy items.
- ✅ Check weight limits—stick to Command Strips for shelves under 20lb total.

This approach keeps noise to a minimum while transforming your space. Remember: adhesives are quieter and often safer for rentals! 🛠️✨
