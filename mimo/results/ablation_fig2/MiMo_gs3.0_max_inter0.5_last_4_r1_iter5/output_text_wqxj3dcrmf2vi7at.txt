Alright, let's recap all the homemade and store-bought ideas we've discussed, plus a few extra ones for your little Corgi princess! 

For **Homemade Homemade Treats**, we talked about:
- Freezer Bites: Mashed pumpkin or sweet potato frozen solid. Easy for teething or on warm days.
- Peanut Butter Oat Cookies: Baked with plain peanut butter (zero xylitol!) and rolled oats. They're chewy and rewarding.
- Other Homemade Ideas:
  * Boiled Chicken or Turkey Strips: Cut into thin, dippable pieces (great for training rewards!).
  * Steamed Carrot or Green Bean Sticks: Naturally crunchy veggies in tiny sizes.
  * Apple Slices (Core Removed): Thinly sliced, no seeds, and rinsed (only if your corgi licks their paws).
  * Banana "Cookies": Mash banana with a spoonful of oats, bake until firm, then break into tiny pieces.

Key Rule for Homemade: Keep portions **very small**! These are treats, not meals.

For **Store-Bought Brands**, good options include:
- **Blue Buffalo** (Training Bites, Snacky Sticks)
- **Wellness** (Training Bites)
- **Zuke's** (Mini Nibbles, Sweet Potato Bites - check size!)
- **Greenies** (Odor Eliminators - some corgis love the texture, but not for everyone)
- **Merrick** (Training Bites)
- **Earth Animal** (Veggie Bites)
- **Fromm** (Mini Training Bites)

Always check the ingredients: Look for natural ingredients, avoid artificial stuff, and double-check peanut butter for xylitol.

Finally, remember the **Golden Rules**:
1.  **Safety First:** No chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol, or excessive salt/sugar.
2.  **Portion Control:** Treats should be less than 10% of her daily calories. Tiny pieces are key!
3.  **Supervision:** Especially with other pets around (like Felix!).
4.  **Consistency:** Stick to safe ingredients regardless of color preference.
5.  **Collar Color:** Focus on high-visibility colors that contrast with her brown coat. Teal, Turquoise, Bright Pink, Royal Blue, or even a classic red are fantastic choices. Avoid colors that blend in with her fur.

Your Princess sounds adorable! Having treats and training options ready will make life with your "little corgi princess" even sweeter. Enjoy every moment!
