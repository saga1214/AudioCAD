### Vocal Endurance & Burnout Prevention
- **Daily Habits**:  
  - Warm-ups: Lip trills → hums → tongue twisters (5 mins before recording).  
  - Hydration: Sip room-temp water hourly. Avoid dairy/coffee pre-recording.  
  - Breathing: 4-7-8 technique (inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s).  
- **Time Blocks**: Dedicate 90-minute recording sessions max, followed by 10-min vocal rests. Use a timer!
