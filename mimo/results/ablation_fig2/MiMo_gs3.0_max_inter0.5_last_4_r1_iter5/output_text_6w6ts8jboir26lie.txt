Here are five simple, comforting dinner ideas you can make with things you might find at a beach grocery store:

First, a classic Shrimp and Grits. It's a Southern favorite. You'd just need some shrimp, grits, a little butter, and some cheese.

Second, a fresh Fish Tacos. If there's a good fish market nearby, you could grab some white fish like cod or tilapia. Serve it with corn tortillas, some cabbage slaw, avocado, and a little spicy sauce.

Third, a simple Seafood Pasta. You could get some mussels or clams, some pasta, garlic, and white wine to make a quick sautéed dish.

Fourth, a cozy Chicken and Rice Bake. Just chicken, rice, broccoli, and cheese, all mixed together and baked until it's bubbly.

And fifth, a fun one: Grilled Cheese and Tomato Soup. It's a classic for a reason. You can get a nice sourdough, some good cheese, and make a simple tomato soup with canned tomatoes.

I hope one of those sounds good. Enjoy your vacation, even if it's a little unconventional.
