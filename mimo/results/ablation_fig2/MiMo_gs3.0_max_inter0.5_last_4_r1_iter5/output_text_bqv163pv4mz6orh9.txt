Okay, so your birthday food tour and massage overlap with your daughter's big play night. It happens! Here are a few thoughtful gift ideas for your daughter. First, a small, personalized item. Maybe a keychain or bracelet with her initials or the play's title. It's a nice little keepsake. Or, you could get her a sweet treat—like a fancy chocolate bar or a favorite snack in a cute gift box. Another idea is a small plant or a succulent. It's a symbol of growth, which is perfect for a performer! If she’s into art, a sketchbook and some markers could be great for her to doodle. You could also make a little photo frame with a picture of her rehearsing, so she can remember the moment. A heartfelt note works too, just saying how proud you are. Honestly, even something simple like a new book or a fun activity like tickets to see another show will show you’re cheering for her. The main thing is she feels your support, no matter what.
