Absolutely! Here's a well-balanced, grab-and-go shopping list designed for minimal mess while catering to diverse preferences (healthy, calzones, sugar-free sodas, and dessert). It's organized by category and scaled for **20 people (10 kids, 10 adults)**.

---

### **DRINKS**  
*(Focus: Reusable bottles + sealed options)*  
- **Water bottles** (50+ refillable bottles for distribution)  
- Sugar-free sodas (e.g., Coke Zero, Pepsi Zero) – **10 12-packs** (100 cans total; store upright in coolers)  
- Low-sugar juice boxes (for kids) – 20 boxes (e.g., apple or orange)  
- Infused water (pre-mixed in large containers with lemon/lime/cucumber) – 4-6 bottles  
- Protein shakes (for adults) – 10 individual cups  
- Baby wipes + hand sanitizer (essential for cooler cleanup!)  

---

### **SNACKS**  
*(Focus: Individual portions, minimal packaging)*  
- Cheese sticks (lactaid-friendly if needed) – 20 sticks  
- Single-serve pretzels or trail mix – 10 bags  
- Baby carrots with 10 single-serve hummus cups  
- Fruit cups (grapes, berries, or pineapple) – 20 single-serve pouches  
- Granola bars (kid-friendly) – 10 boxes (e.g., Clif Bars)  
- Trail mix (dark chocolate + nuts/seeds) – 5 individual bags  

---

### **MAIN MEAL**  
*(Focus: Calzones + wraps for on-the-go eating)*  
- **Real Calzones**: Order from a pizzeria/garlic shop!  
  - **15 mini-calzones** (e.g., cheese, pepperoni, veggie; pre-baked/frozen so they stay cool in coolers)  
- Wraps/Sandwiches (pre-cut into individual portions):  
  - Turkey/cheese wraps – 10  
  - Veggie wraps – 10  
  - Ham/pepperoni sandwiches – 10  
- Single-serve hummus cups (to go with wraps) – 10  
- Whole-grain pitas – 10 (for dipping in hummus)  

---

### **DESSERT**  
*(Focus: Individual portions, bakery-style)*  
- **Calzone-themed dessert**: Mini chocolate-dipped calzone bites (order from bakery) – 15  
- **Cupcakes** (calzone-themed or classic) – 15  
- **Brownie bites** (mini portions) – 10  
- Individual serving spoons/tongs for desserts  

---

### **MESS-CONTROL ADD-ONS**  
- Napkins (bulk pack) – 5 boxes  
- Cooler bags (insulated, with ice packs) – 4–6 to stack food  
- Ice packs + ice bags  
- Utensils (cutlery, forks/spoons) – 20 sets  
- Wet wipes + trash bags (for cooler cleanup)  

---

### **KEY TIPS FOR MINIMAL MESS**  
1. **Cooler Organization**:  
   - Top shelf: Drinks (water/juice first, sodas below).  
   - Middle: Snacks (carrots/trail mix on top, calzones/wraps below).  
   - Bottom: Main meals + desserts.  
2. Pack desserts last to prevent melting.  
3. Use **sealable bags** for wet snacks (like fruit cups) to keep coolers dry.  
4. Assign "cooler tenders" to each dad to grab food as needed.  

This plan keeps things flexible, healthy-ish, and super convenient! Enjoy the party 🎢🎉
