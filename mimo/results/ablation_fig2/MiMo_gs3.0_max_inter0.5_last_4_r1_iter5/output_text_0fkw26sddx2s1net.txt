First, for the starter: Miso Cucumber Salad or Cucumber Salad with Sesame Dressing. Moving on to the main course: Chicken Teriyaki with Jasmine Rice and Stir-Fried Veggies. You'll need about eight ounces of boneless, skinless chicken breast; two tablespoons of soy sauce; two tablespoons of mirin, or you could substitute with two tablespoons of dry sherry; two tablespoons of sake, or you could just use a little more water; one tablespoon of sugar; and one tablespoon of grated fresh ginger. For the rice, you'll need about one and a half cups of jasmine rice, which is about six ounces, along with two cups of water. And for the stir-fried veggies, two tablespoons of cooking oil, a couple of cloves of minced garlic, and maybe some mixed Asian vegetables like bok choy, bell peppers, snap peas, and carrots. You'll need to chop the veggies. Season them with soy sauce, a little bit of sesame oil, and maybe some ginger and garlic powder.

Finally, for dessert options. For Mango Sticky Rice, you'll need three cups of fresh, ripe mango, cut into slices. You'll also need one and a half cups of jasmine rice, which is about six ounces. For this, you'll need three-quarters of a cup of coconut milk, two tablespoons of sugar, and one teaspoon of salt. For the Matcha Home-Made Ice Cream, you'll need one and a half cups of heavy cream, one can of sweetened condensed milk, two tablespoons of high-quality matcha powder, and one teaspoon of vanilla extract. Just whisk everything together, pour it into a bowl, cover it, and freeze it in the fridge for about four to six hours before serving.
