Here’s a clear, bartender-ready cocktail menu with classic recipes, measurements, and serving notes for your event:

### **1. Old Fashioned**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon rye (e.g., Buffalo Trace, Four Roses)  
  - 1 sugar cube *or* ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (garnish)  
- **Method**:  
  1. Muddle sugar cube + bitters in a mixing glass.  
  2. Add bourbon, ice, and stir until chilled.  
  Paper-thin orange twist over rim, then drop into glass.  

### **2. Paper Plane**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz gin (e.g., Hendrick’s, Bombay Sapphire)  
  - 0.75 oz Aperol  
  - 0.25 oz Amaro Nonino  
  - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Method**:  
   Shake all ingredients with ice. Strain into a **coupe glass**. No garnish.  

### **3. French 75 (Recommended Gin Cocktail)**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz gin (e.g., Tanqueray, Beefeater)  
  - 0.5 oz lemon juice  
  - 0.5 oz simple syrup  
  - Top with 2 oz champagne or prosecco  
- **Method**:  
  Shake gin, lemon, and syrup with ice. Strain into a flute. Top with sparkling wine.  

### **4. Whiskey Sour**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon (e.g., Maker’s Mark, Wild Turkey)  
  - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 0.75 oz simple syrup  
  - *Optional*: 0.5 egg white (for texture)  
- **Method**:  
  1. **With egg white**: Dry-shake (no ice) ingredients + egg white. Add ice, shake again. Strain into rocks glass.  
  2. **Without egg white**: Shake with ice. Strain into rocks glass. Lemon wheel or maraschino cherry garnish.

### **Serving Tips for Finger Foods**  
Theme with fall and aviation/gin in mind:  
- **Aviation-inspired**: Cured meats (prosciutto, salami) with fig jam and goat cheese.  
- **Old Fashioned vibe**: Spiced nuts, cheese & charcuterie with bourbon-infused honey drizzle.  
- **Paper Plane twist**: Lemon-herb roasted potatoes or tempura veggies with soy glaze.  

### **Non-Alcoholic Option**  
**Paper Plane Mocktail**:  
- 0.75 oz Aperol  
- 0.5 oz lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz ginger syrup  
- Top with soda water. Garnish with mint.

### **Key Notes for Bartenders**  
- **Paper Planes/Aviations**: Shake hard—these need dilution!  
- **Glassware**: Use coupes for Aviations/French 75, rocks glasses for Old Fashioneds/Sours.  
- **Bitters**: Angostura is standard, but orange bitters work in Old Fashioneds.  
- **Kentucky twist**: Offer local bourbon options (e.g., Woodford Reserve for Old Fashioneds).
