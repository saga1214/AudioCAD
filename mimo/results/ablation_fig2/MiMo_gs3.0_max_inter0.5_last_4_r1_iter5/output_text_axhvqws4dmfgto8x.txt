For stir-fry noodles, the most important thing is to use metal chopsticks or tongs, never a wooden spoon again. Also, make sure your pot is big enough for the spoon so you don't leave anything touching the heating element.

A pro-tip is to cook the noodles ahead of time and then just reheat them in the stir-fry at the very end. That way you have complete control
