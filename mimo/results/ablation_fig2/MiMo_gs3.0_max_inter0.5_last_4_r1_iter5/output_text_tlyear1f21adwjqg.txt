This is a tough question because it really depends on what you mean by "efficient." If we're talking about cost, scale, and widespread availability over time, then solar energy still stands out. But if you're looking for something that's super reliable and consistent, then hydropower is amazing. And if a region has strong and steady winds, then wind power could be the most efficient choice there. Geothermal is also a great option where it's accessible. So, there isn't one single best answer that fits everywhere.
