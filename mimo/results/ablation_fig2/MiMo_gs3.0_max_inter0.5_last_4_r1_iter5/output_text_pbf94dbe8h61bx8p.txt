**For cookies**, drop cookies—minimal rolling, just scoop and bake—or chocolate crinkles. Both are low-mess but still look great.

**To get the kids cooperating**, use stations: little ones decorate, older ones help scoop or taste-test. Add a fun challenge or play music to keep the mood up.

**For the Santa Claus candy globes**, I get the glue gun frustration. A simpler alternative could be salt dough ornaments—kids shape, you bake, they paint. No glue needed.

**If you're looking for pet food**, Walmart is cheaper, but Chewy or Costco might have even better deals. Always good to compare online.

**For simple kid crafts**, fingerprint art or pine cone feeders (peanut butter won’t spoil outside) are both hands-on and meaningful.
