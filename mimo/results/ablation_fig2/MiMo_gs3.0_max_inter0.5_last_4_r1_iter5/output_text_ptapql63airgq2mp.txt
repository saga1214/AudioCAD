### **Food Menu: Simple + Unique with Umami & Global Flair**  
*(Prioritizing ease of prep but maximum wow factor)*  

#### **SIMPLE SNACKS (Easy Finger Foods)**  
1. **Yuzu-Citrus Mini Sliders**  
   - Beef or negima (pork/chicken blend) patties with yuzu mayo, pickled ginger, and sesame seeds.  
2. **Spicy Matcha Popcorn Bar**  
   - Offer 3 stations: butter, yuzu-vanilla, and *spicy matcha umami dust* (matcha + chili flakes + salt).  
3. **DIY Yakitori Skewer Station**  
   - Pre-marinated chicken thigh skewers (grill ahead) + toppings: *tare sauce, grated daikon, scallions, sesame seeds*.  

#### **UNIQUE FUSION STATIONS (Foodie Wows)**  
1. **Charcuterie Board "Reimagined"**  
   - **Meats**: *Negima* (pork/chicken blend), *teriyaki duck prosciutto*, or *beef tartare* with yuzu kosho.  
   - **Cheeses**: *Mimoja* (triple cream) + *yuzu-infused feta* + *aged gouda*.  
   - **Accoutrements**: *Pickled daikon & radish*, *yuzu kosho olives*, *persimmon slices*, *wasabi peas*.  
   - **Pro Tip**: Pair with chilled sake (try Junmai Ginjo) or Japanese sparkling wine.  

2. **Wagyu Taco Station**  
   - *Pulled wagyu beef tacos* with tamarind glaze + *fish taco option* (tuna/crab with avocado-lime crema).  
   - Toppings: *pickled kimchi*, *cucumber relish*, *edamame*, *black sesame seeds*.  

3. **Kimchi Seaweed & Dried Squid Platter**  
   - *Kimchi seaweed snacks* (sweet & spicy) + *air-dried squid* (salted/umami) + *tamarind candied nuts*.  
   - Serve with *soy-marinated olives* and *yuzu kosho popcorn*.  

4. **Gourmet Flatbrick Pizzas**  
   - Pre-bake individual pizzas:  
     - *Spicy Tuna + Avocado*  
     - *Fig Jam + Blue Cheese + Kimchi*  
     - *Mushroom + Truffle Oil + Fried Egg*  

---

### **Why This Works**  
- **Foodie Frames Covered**: Fermented (kimchi), umami-rich (dried squid, seaweed), sweet-savory (tamarind, wagyu tacos), and global fusion (taco station, yakitori).  
- **Prep Ease**: Most components can be prepped ahead (tacos, sliders, charcuterie). Only flatpizzas need last-minute assembly.  
- **Thematic Flow**: Matcha/yuzu/tamarind ties everything together—like your Japanese snacks!  

### **Bonus: Movie Pairing**  
- Play a Japanese film: *Studio Ghibli’s* "Spirited Away" (for whimsy) or a thriller like *Rashomon* (for depth).
