Of course. Here is a straightforward format you can use. 

First, at the very top, center your name in about a fourteen-point font. To its right, using nine-point icons or plain text, list your contact info in this order: email, phone number, city. Right after your name, add a Professional Summary. This is a short paragraph, three to five sentences, highlighting your most relevant skills and career goals.

Below that, create a section for Relevant Experience. Here, list your key roles from your past, including the company, your title, and the dates you worked there. Keep the descriptions of your duties brief.

Next, create a Skills section. List your most important skills, grouping them into categories like Technical Skills or Languages. Make sure to tailor these to the jobs you're applying for.

After that, you can have a section for Additional Work History. Here, you can list your less relevant jobs, again with just the company, title, and dates. Finally, your Education section should list your degrees from your school, including any honors or awards you received.

This structure should give you a clear, effective resume that's easy to fill out. Let me know if you'd like to go over any of those sections in more detail.
