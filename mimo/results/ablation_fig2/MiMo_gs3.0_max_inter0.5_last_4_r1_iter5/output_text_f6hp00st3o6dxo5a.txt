That argument about the DNA being formed inside the egg has a logical flaw.

Even though the DNA for the first chicken was created inside an egg, that egg came from a bird that was not yet fully a chicken. The egg itself didn't 'contain' the first chicken's DNA; it contained the DNA of its non-chicken parent. The DNA that made it a chicken was activated *after* it hatched. Therefore, the chicken itself, the living creature, left the egg first.
