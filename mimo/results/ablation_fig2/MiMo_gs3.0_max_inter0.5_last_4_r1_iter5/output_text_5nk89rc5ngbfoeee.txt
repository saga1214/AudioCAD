For your end-of-year celebration, a **mix-and-match approach** works great! Here’s a simple, inclusive plan to keep it fun and manageable:

### 1. **Location Flexibility**
   - **Rainy Backup Plan:** Host the main ceremony (like awarding badges/sashes) in the cafeteria. This is low-cost and easy to control.
   - **Outdoor Fun:** For games (three-legged races, wheelbarrow races), pick a **local park with covered picnic tables**. Have a backup tent plan just in case.

### 2. **Food Strategy**
   - **Parent-Organized Snack Stations:** Assign 2-3 parents to run themed snack areas:
     - Ice cream sundae bar (dairy-free alternatives available)
     - Pizza station (let parents pre-order/bring slices)
     - "Treat Tree" with cookies/candy
   - **Main Event:** Keep it simple—have parents sign up to bring one dish (e.g., chips/dip for chips and dip nostalgia!).

### 3. **Activities Flow**
   - **Indoor Ceremony (Cafeteria):** 
     - Badges/sashes presented to girls.
     - Brownie tradition recap (e.g., "Brownie Promise" recitation).
   - **Outdoor Games (Park):** 
     - Quick, low-cost races (3-legged, sack race).
     - Craft scavenger hunt using park items.

### 4. **Cost Control**
   - **Dues:** Charge a small fee ($10–$20) for badge printing, field trips, and camping supplies. Be transparent about what it covers.
   - **Parents:** Cover food, sashes, and activity-specific supplies (e.g., race materials). Use SignUpGenius for sign-ups.

### Pro Tips:
- **Dairy-Avoidance:** Label snacks with icons (🌱 Vegan, 🥛 Contains Dairy) and collaborate with lactose-free parents.
- **Pregnancy Prep:** Delegate a parent to lead the ceremony or games so you can rest. Hydrate and take short breaks!
- **Nostalgia Touch:** End with a "Brownie Growing Up" video montage of the year’s activities.

You’ll do great—Brownies is about teamwork and creativity. By involving parents early (even just for snacks!), you’ll build a supportive troop culture. Stay apprehensive but okay (your words!), and the girls will thrive! 🍫✨
