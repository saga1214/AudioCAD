That sounds like a wonderful plan for a family getaway! Here are five fantastic road trip options from Vancouver, all within a couple of hours, focusing on beautiful scenery and unique experiences.

First, you could head to Tofino on the West Coast. It's about a four-hour drive. The activities there are all about the ocean: you can go whale watching in the open ocean, or for a more relaxed experience, take a kayak tour in the calm waters of the Broken Rock Marine Provincial Park. You'll stay in cozy, rustic inns.

Second, for some mountains, you could drive to Whistler, which is about three and a half hours. In the summer, you can go hiking on trails like the Blackcomb Glacier or take a scenic gondola ride up to the top. In the winter, it's a world-class ski resort. The area has lots of charming, family-friendly lodges.

Third, for a classic drive, you could go to Okanagan Lake, which is about four and a half hours. You can take a scenic ferry ride across the lake, visit the beautiful Okanagan Falls Winery for some wine tasting, and stay in a lovely lakeside inn.

Fourth, if you want to explore some unique landscapes, you could drive to Penticton. It's about a three-and-a-half-hour trip. You could visit the Kettle Valley Heritage Trail, which is a beautiful, old railway line turned into a path, and enjoy the local wineries. The area has charming bed and breakfasts.

Finally, for a quick and easy day trip, you could drive to the Vancouver Island wineries. It's about an hour and a half. You can take a tour of the famous Prince Edward County wineries, enjoy some gourmet food and wine, and stay in a charming bed and breakfast. It's perfect for a weekend getaway.

Each of these places offers a unique experience, from ocean adventures to mountain escapes, all within a reasonable drive. Have a great trip!
