Initally, a face-face meeting did induce significant anxiety for you. Here are two strategies to mitigate that. 

First, you could schedule a brief, low-pressure email to your supervisor. Something like: 'Hi, I'd like to discuss my career growth and contributions. Could we schedule some time in the next week for a 15-minute chat?' This gives you control over the setting and reduces the element of surprise.

Second, you could request to be included in the company's formal performance review cycle. This aligns your timing with the organization's natural rhythm, which is often less confrontational.
