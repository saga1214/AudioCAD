For an immediate solution in bad weather, here are practical remedies:

1.  **The Microwave Latte Hack:**  
    Dissolve the instant coffee in just **1-2 tbsp of hot water** first (this creates a concentrated shot). Pour it into your warmed semi-skim milk (or rain jacket-wearing Cornish milk!) and add froth/milk frother if available. It's fast, smooth, and avoids clumps.

2.  **The Coffee Machine Hack:**  
    If you *must* use the machine but it's windy: brew a single cup of strong coffee (use more grounds if desired), pour it into a mug, then add warmed milk and froth. Skip the machine's whole brew cycle.

3.  **Prioritize Warmth & Safety:**  
    Grab your rain jacket, wear it *before* stepping out, and pack an umbrella *just in case* (it's better to carry it than get soaked). A cozy blanket in your bag could also make the walk bearable.

**Key Tip:** Instant + dissolved-in-hot-water + warmed milk = a surprisingly rich latte in <2 minutes. Don't let the weather ruin your coffee craving! ☕️🌧️
