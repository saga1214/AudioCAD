If you're staying near Secret Harbour Beach Resort or Gallows Point Resort in Cruz Bay, Red Hook offers the vibrant daytime too! Here are some great activities right nearby or a short boat ride away:  

1. **Red Hook Harbor Swimming & Snorkeling**:  
   Just steps from Secret Harbour, the calm turquoise waters right around the docks have shallow coral reefs and fish. Perfect for casual snorkeling—no boat needed!  

2. **Soggy Bottom Beach Hike**:  
   A 10-minute walk south from Cruz Bay leads to this secluded black-sand beach. Easy trail, minimal effort, and iconic views.  

3. **Red Hook Fish Market & Seafood Lunch**:  
   Grab fresh-caught lobster, conch, or snapper at the bustling fish market, then cook it at Duffy’s Love Shack (open daytime too) or take it back to your resort.  

4. **Boat-Based Activities**:  
   Secret Harbour’s location makes it ideal for renting a kayak or paddleboard for a peaceful paddle along the mangroves. If you have your own boat, nearby coral reefs (like Reef Bay) are great for snorkeling.  

5. **Cruz Bay Shops & Coffee Stroll**:  
   Walk to Cruz Bay’s main street for beachwear, local crafts, and a coffee break at **The Crab Shack** (great for casual lunch).  

6. **Sunset Kayaking (Late Afternoon)**:  
   Rent kayaks from **Red Hook Kayak** and paddle along the mangroves for serene views—no pressure needed! Returns often include a light dinner at a nearby bar.  

**Pro Tip**: Secret Harbour’s bay is protected and rarely crowded, making it ideal for your brother—it’s sheltered from waves and has gentle slopes for easy beach access. Enjoy the island’s quiet charm! 🌴🚤
