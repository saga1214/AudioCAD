To tune your instrument (likely the guitar playing at the start), here's how to use a tuner or app:

### Using a Clip-On Tuner (Best for Precision):
1. **Attach the tuner** to the headstock of your instrument.  
2. **Pluck a string** (start with the 6th string, thickest E string).  
3. **Observe the tuner display**:  
   - If the needle/app moves **left**, tune the string **tighter** (move to higher fret).  
   - If it moves **right**, tune **looser** (move to lower fret).  
4. **Repeat** until the tuner shows **0** (neutral) or **green** (perfect).  

### Using a Smartphone App (Free & Convenient):
1. **Open a tuner app** (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune, or built-in tuner on iOS).  
2. **Pluck a string** (start with the 6th string).  
3. **Position your phone near the soundhole** for clear sound pickup.  
4. **Adjust the string tension** until the app shows **perfect tuning** (e.g., green checkmark).  
5. **Repeat** for all strings: **E (6th)**, **A (5th)**, **D (4th)**, **G (3rd)**, **B (2nd)**, **High E (1st)**.  

### Standard Tuning Reference:
- **6th string (Thickest)**: E  
- **5th string**: A  
- **4th string**: D  
- **3rd string**: G  
- **2nd string**: B  
- **1st string (Thinnest)**: E  

### Tips:
- Start with the **6th string (E)** and work upward.  
- If the tuner/app is unstable, try a different string or angle.  
- Electric guitars may need a **tune-o-matic bridge** for accuracy.
