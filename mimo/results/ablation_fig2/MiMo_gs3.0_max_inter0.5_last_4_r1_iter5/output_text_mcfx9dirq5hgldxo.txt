For Madrid, tapas are the top recommendation. Think things like patatas bravas—that's crispy potatoes with this spicy tomato sauce—and jamón ibérico, which is a really high-quality cured ham.

Paella is popular, too, but they have a version there called paella manchega. Oh, and churros... they're these fried dough pastries that are amazing with a thick hot chocolate. 

And since you mentioned Philadelphia, if you happen to see Philly cheesesteak in a restaurant there, you should probably give it a shot! 

Have a great business trip, and I hope you enjoy Madrid. Safe travels!
