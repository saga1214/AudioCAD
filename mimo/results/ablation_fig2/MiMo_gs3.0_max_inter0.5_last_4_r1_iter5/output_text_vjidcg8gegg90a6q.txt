Okay, for the chicks, since rodents are a concern, the best bedding is wood shavings cut on the grain. They insulate well, attract fewer rodents than straw was ever would, and they're easy to manage. You'll want to use what's called the deep litter approach again. Just add fresh shavings on top of the composting layer all winter. As that bottom layer heats up from breaking down, it will generate natural warmth for them.
