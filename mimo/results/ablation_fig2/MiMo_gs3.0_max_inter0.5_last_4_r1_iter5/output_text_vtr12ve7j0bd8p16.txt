You're right, fire signs like yours and hers do love adventure! Calgary is a fantastic base for that. You could go to Canmore and hike in the Rocky Mountains, or bike the Bow Valley Parkway. If you want to be on the water, you could rent kayaks on the Bow River or take a boat out on Lake Louise. For a more rugged adventure, you could try rock climbing in the Canmore area or even go ice climbing on the Icefields Parkway. There are also great golf courses in the foothills and many scenic drives you can take, like the one along the Kananaskis River.
