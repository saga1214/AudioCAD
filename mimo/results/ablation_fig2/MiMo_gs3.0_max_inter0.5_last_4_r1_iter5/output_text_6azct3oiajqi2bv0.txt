**For Ironing Fabric Linings Safely:**  
- **Always check the garment’s care label first.**  
- Use **LOW heat** and **NO steam**.  
- Place a **cotton cloth** (like a pillowcase) between the iron and fabric to prevent scorching.  
- Iron inside-out if possible, focusing on seams/flat areas.  
- If the lining is synthetic, avoid high heat entirely.  

If scorch marks occur, a tailor (like at Arden Fair Mall) can often repair them. For immediate relief, dab with a cloth soaked in **white vinegar** (1:1 with water) to neutralize odors.
