Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The peak of their fall colors is usually from, uh, late September to November. So, if you can, I'd say go in October or early November for that really vibrant foliage. 

But hey, if you want to mix up your palette, spring is also amazing there. That's around March to May, and you get all those fresh greens and flowers. Either way, you'll find incredible colors to paint with. Just... you know, pack your watercolors and markers and get ready to soak it all in! Have a great trip!
