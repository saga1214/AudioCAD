Absolutely! Costa Rica is scuba diving paradise with its warm tropical waters, diverse marine life, and varied dive sites. Here’s a plan to make your dream trip unforgettable, tailored to your scuba diving interests:

### **Top Dive Sites in Costa Rica**
1. **Cocos Island (Puntarenas)**  
   - **Why go**: Strong currents, deep walls, and world-class visibility. Famous for whale sharks (seasonal), manta rays, and dramatic volcanic caves.  
   - **Fins**: Long fins recommended here for efficiency in currents, but strong swimmers can use short fins.  
   - **Bonus**: Night dives for bioluminescent plankton (magical glow-in-the-dark waters).

2. **Papagayo (Nicoya Peninsula)**  
   - **Why go**: Crystal-clear waters with coral reefs, tropical fish, and occasional whale sharks. Ideal for beginners and intermediates.  
   - **Wildlife**: Sea turtles, dolphins, and rays.  
   - **Fins**: Short fins work well for maneuverability in calm bays like Playa Blanca.

3. **Corcovado (Osa Peninsula)**  
   - **Why go**: Remote, pristine reefs teeming with marine life (eagles, monkeys, and fish). Great for biodiversity-focused divers.  
   - **Note**: Requires a boat dive; less current than Cocos but rich in marine species.

4. **Tortuguero (Northern Coast)**  
   - **Why go**: Mangrove forests, coral gardens, and occasional nurse sharks. Perfect for relaxed dives after exploring canals by boat.  
   - **Fins**: Short fins ideal for tight spaces.

### **Trip Itinerary Suggestion**
- **Days 1-3**: Arrive in San José, fly to Puntarenas (Cocos Island). Dive 2-3 days here for adventure.  
- **Days 4-6**: Travel to Papagayo for calm, beautiful dives. Explore Playa Blanca or Flamingo.  
- **Days 7-8**: Fly to Tortuguero for mangrove and reef dives. Return to San José.  

### **Key Tips**
- **Fin Choice**: Stick with **short fins** for ease and portability. Rent long fins if you plan to dive strong currents (like Cocos Island).  
- **Certification**: Ensure you’re certified for deep dives (Cocos has walls to 100+ feet).  
- **Travel**: Book eco-certified operators (e.g., **Dive Cocos**, **Papagayo Divers**) to protect reefs.  
- **Season**: Best from **December to April** (calmer, warmer waters).  

### **Alternatives if You Prefer Warmer/Calmer Waters**
- **Curaçao (Aruba)**: Pristine reefs, easy currents, and vibrant coral gardens.  
- **Philippines (Apo Island)**: World-class reefs with short fin-friendly dives.  
- **Tenerife (Canary Islands)**: Temperate but tropical-like, with shipwrecks and diverse life.  

### **Final Advice**
Costa Rica’s diversity—from mangroves to abyssal walls—makes it perfect for scuba lovers. Prioritize **Cocos Island** for adventure and **Papagayo/Tortuguero** for accessibility. Pack reef-safe sunscreen, a dive computer, and a waterproof camera!
