*   Modifying lights (bright LEDs, no taillights).
*   Extremely loud exhausts.

Flashing colors on a high-performance American muscle car will inherently make it more noticeable and potentially attract attention *if driven like one* (aggressively), regardless of color. A more neutral color helps it blend in slightly more when driven normally.

**On Tires:** You absolutely need **Ultra High-Performance Summer Tires (UHPS)**. These are critical for unlocking the car's potential:
*   **Soft, Aggressive Compound:** Provides immense grip and feedback, essential for competitive driving.
*   **Low Rolling Resistance:** Helps maintain high performance.
*   **Wide Tread Pattern:** Maximizes contact patch for grip.
*   **Examples:** Michelin Pilot Sport 4S, Pirelli P Zero, Continental ExtremeContact DWS06+ (good wet grip), Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2 (track-focused).
*   **Crucial Note:** These are **summer tires only**. They perform poorly in cold weather and **do not provide reliable traction in wet or snowy conditions**. You'll need a separate winter tire set for those seasons.
