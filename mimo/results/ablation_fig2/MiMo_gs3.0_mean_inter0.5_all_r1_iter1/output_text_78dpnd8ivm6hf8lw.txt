Sure! To quickly map everything out in your blood-system analogy: 

- **Heart**: Gas station and engine (pumps cars and refuels them).  
- **Lungs**: Gas station pit stop (where blood "cars" grab fresh oxygen/fuel).  
- **Main Arteries**: Big highways (carry blood away from the heart).  
- **Veins**: Return roads (carry blood back to the heart/gas station).  
- **Capillaries**: Tiny neighborhood streets (where oxygen "fuels" are dropped off to body cells).  
- **Kidneys**: Car washes (filter waste and extra water from the blood).  
- **Liver**: Supermarket/repair shop (sorts nutrients, breaks down toxins).  
- **Urine**: Garbage truck (removes waste from the "cars" after the kidneys clean it).  

All together, your body’s "delivery fleet" keeps energy flowing and trash cleared out! 🚗💨
