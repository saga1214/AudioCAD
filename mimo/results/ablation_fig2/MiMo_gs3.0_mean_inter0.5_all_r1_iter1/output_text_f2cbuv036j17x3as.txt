Okay, diesel camper vans do require a pre-trip check. For brakes, a key sign of wear is a squeaking or grinding noise when you brake—usually that means the pads are worn down. Another thing is if the brake pedal feels soft or sinks to the floor, that’s a serious issue that needs immediate attention. You should also look at the brake fluid—it should be a clear amber color, not cloudy or rusty. If it looks off, it might need to be flushed. As for spark plugs, diesel engines use something called compression rings and fuel injectors, so it’s not exactly the same as a gasoline engine. Changing spark plugs in a diesel is a more involved process—usually requires a special tool and you have to remove the cylinder head. Honestly, unless you’re comfortable with that, it’s best to have a trusted diesel mechanic do it before the trip. They can also check for any other diesel-specific issues like clogged filters or fluid leaks. Just a few things to keep an eye on, and you should be good to go.
