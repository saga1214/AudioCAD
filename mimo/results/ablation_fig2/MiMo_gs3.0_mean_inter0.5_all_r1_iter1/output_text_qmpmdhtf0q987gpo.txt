Okay, so you've done all the prep work and are now down to the final step before painting. Just one more thing to get it perfect: protect your counters and floors.

So, cover up everything that isn't a cabinet with some plastic sheeting or painter's tape. You really want to avoid getting any paint or primer on them.

Once everything is covered, you can go ahead and apply your primer. Just make sure to use a brush for the edges and corners, and then use a roller for the big flat surfaces.
