Since you'll be based at **Secret Harbour Beach Resort**, here are pressure-free, beach-focused activities for your week:

### **Beach & Snorkeling**
1. **Secret Harbour Bay**  
   Calm, sheltered waters right off your resort—no pressure changes. Grab snorkel gear from Secret Harbour’s rental shop (vegetarian breakfast included in rental packages). Spot tropical fish, sea turtles, and maybe even a parrotfish.

2. **Trunk Bay (St. John)**  
   10-minute boat ride from Secret Harbour. National park preserve with a calm, protected lagoon. Snorkel the famous underwater observatory (12ft-deep, pressure-free). Nearby Trunk Bay Restaurant has oceanfront dining.

### **Birding & Nature**
- **Cruz Bay Birding Trail**  
  Easy coastal walk from Secret Harbour. Spot bananaquits, red-vented bululs, and herons. Bring binoculars—vegetation is dense but flat.
- **Cottonwood Garden**  
  5-minute walk to this restored tropical garden with hummingbirds and rare flowers. Free and wheelchair-accessible.

### **Boating & Exploration**
- **Secret Harbour Boat Charters**  
  Charter a small sailboat or catamaran for private island-hopping. Visit Waterlemon Cay (15 mins away) for secluded beaches and shorebird colonies (no pressure changes at sea level).
- **Kayaking**  
  Rent kayaks at Secret Harbour for stress-free paddling along mangrove trails. Watch for herons and flamingos in the mangroves.

### **Food & Shops**
- **Cruz Bay Market**  
  2-minute walk from Gallows Point. Find fresh coconuts, local crafts, and birdseed for feeding parrots. Vegetarian options at nearby **Mango’s on the Bay** (try their jackfruit curry).
- **Duffy’s Love Shack** (Red Hook)  
  As mentioned earlier—great for tacos and live music. Drive 20 mins over (steeper hills, but short trip). Their "Island Veggie Taco" is legendary.

### **Pressure-Free Relaxation**
- **Beach Days**  
  Secret Harbour’s sand is soft and white. Read, nap, or sketch birds. Bring a portable pressure-relief ear plug (like Swimbuds) for your brother if waves start bothering him.
- **Sunset Cruises**  
  Book a sunset sail with **Virgin Islands Yacht Charters**. Minimal waves at dusk, with calm sea-level viewing. No pressure changes!

### **Nightcap Option**
- **The Longboard** (Cruz Bay)  
  Cozy beach bar with live acoustic music and a killer piña colada. Vegetarian ceviche on the menu. No stairs—wheelchair-friendly.
