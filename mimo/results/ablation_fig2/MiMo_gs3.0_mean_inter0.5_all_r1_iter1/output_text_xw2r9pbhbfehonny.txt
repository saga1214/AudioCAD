Here's a concise checklist to ensure your first self-filmed puppet show runs smoothly:

### 🎬 **Pre-Performance Setup**
1. **Permission First**  
   - Confirm filming is allowed at *Sunnyside Assisted Living* (written approval if possible).  
   - Ask if they have noise restrictions or camera policies.

2. **Gear Essentials**  
   - **Stabilization**: Use a tripod or phone mount (avoid shaky handheld shots).  
   - **Audio**: Add a lavalier mic clipped to the puppet’s hand to capture voice clearly.  
   - **Lighting**: Ensure faces/puppets are well-lit (avoid backlighting from windows/doors).

3. **Puppet Prep**  
   - Test sock puppet movement on camera beforehand.  
   - Have spare socks/strings handy for quick fixes.

### 🎥 **During the Performance**
4. **Camera Placement**  
   - Shoot from the audience’s perspective (eye level) to engage viewers.  
   - Angle slightly upward to avoid distortion.  
   - Use a timer alarm (set for 10 mins) to signal when to wrap up.

5. **Engagement Tips**  
   - Direct jokes/puppet banter *away* from the camera to feel natural.  
   Residents: Use exaggerated expressions and clear pauses for clarity.  
   Schools/Hospitals: Incorporate interactive questions for the "camera."

### 🛠️ **Post-Show Editing**
6. **Basic Editing**  
   - Trim awkward silences or technical glitches.  
   - Add simple text overlays (e.g., "Meet Sloppy Snake!") for context.  
   - Boost puppet visibility (slightly increase brightness if needed).

7. **Platform Optimization**  
   - **YouTube/Shorts**: Keep under 5 mins; add chapters for skits.  
   - **TikTok/Reels**: Use quick cuts, captions, and trending audio.  
   - **Copyright**: Add your name/username to the video and metadata.

### ⚠️ **Ethical Considerations**
8. **Audience Privacy**  
   - Blur faces of non-consenting residents/staff in assisted living footage.  
   - Avoid hospital/nursing home footage without explicit permission.

9. **Distribution Plan**  
   - Share privately first (e.g., with hospitals) before public posting.  
   - Credit venues (e.g., "Filmed at Sunnyside Assisted Living!").

### 💡 **Pro Tip**
Ask viewers for feedback (e.g., "Which puppet should we feature next?") to guide future content. Your viral hospital show proved people crave your joy—now you control how it spreads! 🌟

*Example Workflow*:  
Setup → Record → Edit → Share (privately) → Publish → Engage with comments.  
Start simple, and scale up as you find your rhythm!
