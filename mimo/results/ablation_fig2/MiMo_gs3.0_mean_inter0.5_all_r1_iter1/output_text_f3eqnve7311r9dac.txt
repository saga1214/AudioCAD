That's an excellent point. Older comics are almost always much rarer and can be significantly more valuable.

Here are ten historically significant books from the 1940s and 50s that are very rare and sought after. You can expect to find a decent copy of any of these for under five hundred dollars.

There's *Action Comics* number one, Superman's first full appearance. There's also *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven, which is Batman's first full appearance. *Strange Tales* number one, the first full appearance of the Hulk, is another. For your hero, *Incredible Hulk* number one eighty-one, the first appearance of Wolverine, is a classic. Then there's *Amazing Fantasy* number fifteen, featuring Spider-Man. Other key books include the first appearance of Captain America, the first appearance of X-Men, and the first appearance of She-Hulk. And finally, a book you could find for under five hundred dollars is *The Amazing Spider-Man* number one.
