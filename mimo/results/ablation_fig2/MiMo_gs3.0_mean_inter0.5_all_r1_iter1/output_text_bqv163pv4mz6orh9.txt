**1. A Personalized Keepsake:**  
- **Custom Jewelry:** A necklace, bracelet, or keychain with her name, initials, or the play's title (e.g., "Broadway Star 2024").

- **Custom Playbill Art:** Order a framed print of the play’s poster with her name highlighted or a personalized quote like "My Hero’s First Performance."

**2. A Sentimental Gift:**  
- **Handwritten Letter:** Write a detailed note about what you noticed during rehearsals (e.g., "I loved how you held the audience’s attention during your solo!").  
- **"Thank You" Jar:** Fill it with notes from family/friends praising her specific moments (e.g., "Your smile lit up the stage!").  
- **Memory Box:** Include a small prop from the play, a ticket stub, and a cozy blanket for post-show cuddles.

**3. A Fun Experience:**  
- **Concert or Sports Tickets:** Surprise her with tickets to a show she loves as a "reward for her hard work."  
- **Baking Class:** If she enjoys cooking, gift a class where you can celebrate together afterward.  
- **Ice Cream Run:** A simple but sweet tradition—get her favorite ice cream after the show.

**4. Practical & Thoughtful:**  
- **Quality Headphones:** If she’s into music, noise-canceling headphones make a great practical gift.  
- **Custom Playbill Holder:** A stylish folio to display her playbill proudly at home.  
- **Experience Voucher:** A local spa day or movie night for both of you to decompress after the big night.

**Top Pick for Thursday:**
