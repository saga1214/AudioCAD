**Bulbasaur**: Starts as a strong early-game special attacker with Growth and Vine Whip, but suffers from frailty and type disadvantage against Fire-types encountered early. Mid-game power drop is significant. Rating: 12/20

**Charizard**: Insanely versatile. Fire/Flying typing provides coverage against Rock and Ice. High offensive stats across all moves. Can function as a special or physical attacker. One of the most reliable late-game sweepers. Rating: 18/20

**Squirtle**: Exceptional all-rounder. Water/Dark typing gives coverage against Grass and Electric. Squirtle itself holds Water Gun early, and it evolves before becoming vulnerable to Fire-types. Forms into powerful Wartortle/Blastoise. Rating: 16/20

**Gyarados**: Magikarp's evolution is legendary. Dragon/Flying typing with access to powerful moves like Dragon Dance. Can single-handedly turn battles around with the Dragon Dance sweep. Rating: 19/20

**Staryu**: Decent early game with Water Pulse and Bubble Beam, but extremely frail. Starmie evolves into a special wallbreaker with strong coverage. Squirtle's evolution path is generally preferred. Rating: 13/20

**Caterpie**: Very weak with low stats. Evolves into Butterfree which is decent but outclassed by many other flying types. Pidgey/Pidgeotto are superior early fliers. Rating: 9/20

**Magikarp**: Holds the record for lowest base stats. Cannot learn damaging moves until very late game. Even after evolving into Gyarados, Magikarp itself is useless. Rating: 4/20

The water-types generally hold up better in the competitive scene due to their typing and movepool, while Magikarp remains the most infamous pre-evolution in the series. Caterpie's only redeeming quality is its early access to wing attack in some formats.
