### **SIMPLE FOOD (Easy & Crowd-Pleasing)**  
1. **4-Flavor Popcorn Station**  
   - Classic butter, sweet tamarind drizzle (inspired by Japan), spicy gochujang dust, and *umami* sesame-gochugaru blend.  
2. **Mini Yakisoba Sliders**  
   - Pre-cooked yakisoba noodles in savory-sweet sauce, topped with shredded cabbage, mayo, and a sprinkle of nori.  
3. **DIY Yakitori Skewer Bar**  
   - Simple skewers of chicken thigh (tossed in *tare* sauce), mushrooms, or onions. Serve with *tsuyu* (dipping sauce) and *tonkatsu sauce*.  

---

### **UNIQUE ADVENTURE (Foodie Frames Will Go Wild)**  
1. **Umami Bomb Platter**  
   - **Kimchi Seaweed**: Fermented kimchi + dried squid/crunchy nori strips (crackling texture!).  
   - **Sweet & Sour Fish Jerky**: Your Japan memory—marinated in tamarind, chili, and lime.  
   - **Dried Tuna "Caviar"**: Tiny, salty bites packed with ocean flavor.  

2. **Gourmet Charcuterie Upgrade**  
   - Replace typical meats with:  
     - *Aged Matsutake Mushroom Terrine* (Japan’s prized forest mushroom).  
     - *Sake-marinated Duck Breast* (rich & buttery).  
     - *Miso-Glazed Eggplant* (smoky-sweet).  
   - Toppings: Yuzu kosho (citrus-chili paste), pickled persimmons, black sesame paste.  

3. **Mystery Taco Station**  
   - Fillings:  
     - *Tuna Tostada* (shredded white fish in sweet-sour sauce).  
     - *Spicy Ramen Tacos* (thin ramen noodles + spicy miso).  
     - *Truffle Mushroom* (umami bomb).  
   - Toppings: Grilled scallions, furikake (Japanese seasoning), wasabi mayo.  

4. **Matcha Magic**  
   - **Matcha Popcorn** (classic simple).  
   - **Matcha Mochi** (chewy, slightly sweet).  
   - **Matcha Soft-Serve** (served in cups with red bean powder).  

5. **Tamarind & Chili Snacks**  
   - **Tamarind Pomegranate Skewers**: Sweet-tart tamarind balls + crunchy pomegranate seeds.  
   - **Spicy Tamarind Chips**: Roasted potato or rice crackers dusted with chili-lime.
