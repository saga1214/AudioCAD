### Pork Nutritional Values vs. Other Meats
- **Pork**: Rich in protein, Vitamin B12/B6, iron, zinc, and phosphorus. Leaner cuts exist, but overall **higher in fat** than chicken/turkey.
- **Chicken/Turkey**: Generally **leaner** than pork across most cuts, with lower calories and saturated fat (beef closer to pork here may have more saturated fat depending on the cut).
- **Beef**: Matches protein well; closer iron content but often higher saturated fat than lean cuts.

### Kitchen Safety: Ventilation vs. Bacteria

### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia
1. **Visibility Rule**: Must see **150 meters (165 yards)** in both directions.  
   - *Practical Tip*: Visualize ~1.5 football fields. If road is curved, hilly, or obstructed, wait.  
2. **Prohibited Locations**:  
   - Near intersections, curves, hills, or where it blocks traffic.  
   - Use designated U-turn lanes if available.  
3. **Penalties**:  
   - **Illegal U-turn**: Ticket for unsafe driving ($100–$300 CAD, varies by city).  
   - **Accidental U-turn into traffic**: Focus on avoiding collisions; penalties depend on severity.  

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients (Pork-Free Alternative)
Since you avoid pork, use **chicken or beef instead of guanciale**. Here’s the full list:

| **Ingredient**       | **Quantity**       | **Notes**                                                                 |
|----------------------|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Spaghetti            | 8 oz (225g)        | Cook until al dente.                                                     |
| **Cured Meat**       | 6 oz (170g)        | **Guanciale** (pork cheek) → replace with **pancetta** (swine) or **diced chicken/beef bacon**. |
| Eggs                 | 2 large            | Whole eggs (not egg whites).                                              |
| Pecorino Romano      | ½ cup (50g), grated | Hard Italian cheese. Substitute with Parmesan if needed.                |
| Black Pepper         | Freshly ground     | Generous amount.                                                         |
| Garlic               | 2 cloves, minced   | Optional; add after cooking.                                            |
| Olive Oil            | 2 tbsp             | For cooking meat.                                                        |
| Salt                 | To taste           | Season pasta water and meat.                                            |

### Key Steps
1. Cook spaghetti in salted water. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
2. Fry **cured meat** in olive oil until crispy. Remove from pan.  
3. Whisk eggs, cheese, pepper, and a splash of pasta water.  
4. Toss hot pasta with meat, then add egg mixture (tempering eggs prevents scrambling).  
5. Serve immediately. Garnish with extra cheese/pepper.
