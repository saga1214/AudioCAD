Starting stir-fry this weekend is a smart move—it's fast, forgiving if you prep ahead, and builds confidence fast. Here’s how to make it foolproof and disaster-free, tailored to your experience:

### ✅ **Critical Steps to Avoid Disaster:**
1. **Spoon Sabotage Prevention:**  
   Double-check your utensils—use a metal or ceramic wok/spatula. No wood. Ever again.  
2. **Oil Control:**  
   Heat your wok/skillet over **medium-high heat**, then add **1–2 tbsp oil**. Swirl it until shimmering (but *not* smoking). This prevents sticking and burns.  
3. **Browning Meat First:**  
   Add ground meat and break it up with a spatula. Cook until fully browned (5–7 mins). Drain excess fat if needed. *Don’t rush this step*—it builds flavor.  

### ✅ **Stress-Relief Hack:**  
Prep everything *before* heating the pan:  
- Chop veggies (bell peppers, onions, etc.).  
- Cook noodles (boil pasta separately in a *different pot*—no wood allowed!).  
- Make a sauce (soy sauce, garlic, ginger, honey).  

### ✅ **Topping Hack for "Alright" Tacos/Stir-Fry:**  
Buy pre-shredded lettuce, cheese, and lime wedges already packaged. Saves time and stress.
