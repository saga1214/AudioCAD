### **FOOD & DRINKS**  
*(Quantities for ~24 people)*  
**Protein:**  
- 10–12 lb. smoked ham (adjust if guests prefer smaller portions)  

**Appetizers & Finger Foods** (Serve cold or room temp):  
- Mini ham/cornbread sliders: 1 lb. ground beef, 8 slices cornbread, 1 lb. cheddar, 8 slider buns  
- Shrimp cocktail: 1 lb. shrimp, 1 bottle cocktail sauce  
- Pigs in a blanket: 2 lb. sausage rolls, 1 lb. cream cheese, 1 lb. cheddar  
- **Kid-Friendly:** Veggie platter (carrots, celery, cucumber), ranch dip (1 tub)  

**Main Course** (Serve hot):  
- **Sides:**  
  - Scalloped potatoes: 5 lbs. potatoes, 1 cup cream, 1 cup milk, 1 cup shredded cheddar, 1 egg  
  - Roasted asparagus: 3 lbs. asparagus, 1/4 cup olive oil, lemon juice  
  - **Kid-Friendly Carrots:** 2 lbs. carrots, 2 tbsp olive oil, salt/pepper (NO GLAZE)  
- Mashed potatoes: 5 lbs. potatoes, 2 cups butter, 2 cups milk  
- Green beans: 2 lbs., 1/4 cup butter, 1/2 cup almonds (optional)  

**Drinks** (Chilled):  
- Champagne/sparkling cider: 2 bottles (midnight toast)  
Signature cocktail ingredients: Spirits of choice, mixers, garnishes  
- **Kid Drinks:** 2 liters grape juice (in festive cups/noisemakers!), soda, lemonade, water  
- Ice: 2–3 bags (for drinks/hot food prep)  

**Dessert** (Optional):  
- Store-bought fruit tarts or mini cupcakes  
- **Kid-Friendly:** Sugar cookies, sprinkles
