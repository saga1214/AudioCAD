Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's ten calzones for the bachelor! Alright, let's build a mess-minimizing cooler list based on what you've decided. I'll break it down drink by drink, snack by snack, main food, and dessert.

For drinks, the goal is hydration with some variety. Definitely lots of reusable water bottles for everyone. Kids will need low-sugar milk or juice boxes; Capri Sun is always a hit. For the dads, you'll pack sugar-free sodas—like Diet Coke, Diet Pepsi, or Sprite. Bottled water is also a good backup. Don't forget some sports drinks like Gatorade boxes for kids who might get really thirsty from running around all day.

For snacks, stick with single-serve, low-mess items. Cheese sticks for both kids and adults, grapes which are easy for little hands, single bags of pretzels or trail mix, baby carrots with single hummus cups, whole-grain crackers, and hard-boiled eggs. Fresh fruit like banana slices or apple slices are also great, maybe you can pack them with a little lemon juice to keep them from browning.

For the main meal, since he loves calzones, here are a few ideas. Your best bet is probably to order from a pizzeria or Italian place that specializes in them. You could get a mix of cheese, meat, and maybe veggie-filled ones for variety. Just make sure they're cut into individual pieces before you pack them. If you'd rather not do that, sandwiches or wraps cut into pinwheels are also a great, easy-to-eat option. Turkey and cheese pinwheels are a good protein choice.

And for dessert, to keep it simple and grab-and-go, I'd suggest cupcakes or maybe brownie bars. They're perfect for a celebration without all the cleanup of a whole cake.

Now, for packing, try to use different colored coolers for different groups—drinks in one, snacks in another, and so on. Use leak-proof containers to keep everything contained. And maybe pack some wet wipes and trash bags in one cooler; they'll be a lifesaver for quick cleanups throughout the day. Hope you all have an absolutely amazing time!
