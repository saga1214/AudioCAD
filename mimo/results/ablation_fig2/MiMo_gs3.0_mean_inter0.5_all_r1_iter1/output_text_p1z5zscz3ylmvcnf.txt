Okay, so you're looking for classic cars that hold their value well and might even appreciate. Given your father-in-law's old-school American pride, this could be a great way to get him interested. Here are five solid options.

First, there's the Ford Mustang. It's an American icon, and certain models, like the first-generation '65 to '66 models or the '70 'Boss 429, are highly collectible. Their value has gone up a lot over the years.

Next, a Chevrolet Camaro. It's the American counterpart to the Mustang. The first-generation '67 to '72 models, especially the SS versions, are highly sought after. The '70 Z/28 is particularly prized by collectors.

Another great one is the Dodge Charger. It's iconic from the muscle car era, especially the '68 to '70 models. The '70 Hemi models are among the most valuable. They’re a symbol of that whole period.

Then there's the Pontiac GTO. It's often called the car that started the muscle car era. The original '64 to '66 models, especially the '67 Judge models, are highly collectible and have appreciated significantly.

Finally, you could look at a Maserati Ghibli. It's an Italian car, but it fits if you want something a bit different. The '74 to '89 models, especially the later ones, have seen their value rise quite a bit. It's a bit more niche but could be a good option.

These are all solid investments, but remember, the condition and originality of the car play a huge role in its value. So, if he's looking to get into this, maybe start with a well-maintained classic that's in good shape.
