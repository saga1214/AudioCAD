Here's a detailed bartender-ready recipe list for your Art Deco airport cocktail reception in Kentucky. I've included exact measurements, spirit brands (with local Kentucky options where relevant), and pro tips:

### **Old Fashioned**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon or rye (e.g., **Wild Turkey 101** or **Rittenhouse Rye**)  
  - 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Orange twist (for garnish)  
- **Egg White Method (Optional)**:  
  - Add 0.5 oz pasteurized egg white to shaker  
  - **Shake**: Dry shake (no ice) first, then shake with ice.  
- **Method**:  
  1. Muddle sugar cube + bitters in shaker (or stir simple syrup into glass).  
  2. Add bourbon and ice. Stir 20–30 sec until chilled.  
  3. Strain into rocks glass over fresh ice.  
  4. Express orange twist over drink, rub peel on rim, discard.  

### **Old Fashioned Paper Plane**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz gin (e.g., **Hendrick’s** or **Kendall Jackson Vodka Gin**)  
  - ¾ oz Aperol  
  - ¾ oz Nonino Amaro  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 0.25 oz maraschino liqueur (e.g., Luxardo)  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into **coupe glass** over crushed ice.  
  3. Garnish: Lemon twist (express oils over drink).  

### **Whiskey Sour**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon (e.g., **Buffalo Trace**)  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 0.25 oz egg white (optional)  
  - Cherry or orange slice (garnish)  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake bourbon, lemon juice, syrup, and egg white (if using) with ice.  
  2. **Dry shake first** (no ice) to emulsify egg white.  
  3. Shake again with ice.  
  4. Strain into rocks glass over ice.  
  Angostura garnish optional.  

### **Aviation Cocktail**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz gin (e.g., **Plymouth Gin**)  
  - ¾ oz maraschino liqueur  
  - ¼ oz crème de violette  
  - ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 1 dash Angostura bitters  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into **cocktail glass** over ice.  
  3. Garnish: Orange twist (express oils over drink).  

### **Third Gin Cocktail Recommendation: Kentucky Mule**  
*(Artisanal twist fitting Kentucky’s bourbon culture)*  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz gin (e.g., **Michter’s Gin** or **Bulleit Botanical**)  
  - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
  - 0.5 oz honey syrup (equal honey + water)  
  - 1 oz ginger beer (e.g., **Fever-Tree**)  
  - Lime wedge (garnish)  
- **Method**:  
  1. Fill copper mug with ice.  
  2. Add gin, lemon juice, and honey syrup. Stir 10 sec.  
  3. Top with ginger beer.  
  4. Garnish: Lime wedge.
