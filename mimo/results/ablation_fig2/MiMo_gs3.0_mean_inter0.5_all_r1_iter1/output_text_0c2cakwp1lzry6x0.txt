### 1. **Mounting a TV**
   - **Tools Needed:** Level, stud finder, drill, appropriate screws/anchors, TV mount.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Find Studs:** Use a stud finder to locate wall studs (for heavy TVs, anchors are optional if no studs).
     2. **Mark Alignment:** Place the TV on the floor to align with the wall; mark mounting points.
     3. **Install Mount:** Secure the mount to the wall using screws anchored in studs or wall plugs.
     4. **Attach TV:** Mount the TV to the bracket. Ensure it’s level with a level tool.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Tools Needed:** Suction cups, adhesive hooks, strong twine, scissors.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Choose Placement:** Hang near windows or light sources.
     2. **Suction Cups:** Press firmly onto clean glass surfaces; test stability first.
     3. **Adhesive Hooks:** Clean the wall, apply adhesive, and press hooks firmly. Let dry before hanging.
     4. **Twine Method:** Tie a strong twine loop around the planter; drape it over the hook and secure with a knot.

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Tools Needed:** Level, measuring tape, drill, appropriate screws/anchors, shelf brackets.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Measure & Mark:** Measure shelf depth/height; mark screw points on the wall.
     2. **Install Brackets:** Secure brackets to the wall at marked points. Ensure they’re level.
     3. **Attach Shelf:** Place the shelf onto the brackets and secure with screws.
     4. **Test Stability:** Gently press the shelf to ensure it’s secure before placing items.

### Pro Tips for Focus While Decorating:
- **Time Blocks:** Dedicate 2–3 hours on Sundays for decorating. Use a timer (e.g., 50 mins work, 10 mins break).
- **Workspace Setup:** Keep decorating tools in one drawer/corner away from your work setup.
- **Lighting:** Use task lighting near your workspace to avoid eye strain from drilling tools.

### Safety Notes:
- Always wear safety glasses when drilling.
- Check weight limits for adhesive hooks (e.g., 10–15 lbs per hook).
- Test hanging planters in a low-traffic area first.

This keeps your new space both functional and beautiful without sacrificing work focus! 🛠️✨
