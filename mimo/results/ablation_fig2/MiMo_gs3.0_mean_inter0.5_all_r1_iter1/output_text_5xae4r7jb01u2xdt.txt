Got it. Let's use a Red versus Blue scenario to explain a key point about defender interference resetting an offside situation. It differs slightly between college and high school rules.

First, imagine Red Team defender number three deliberately controlling Player number nine's shot on goal. This controlled touch resets the situation, and Blue Team scores is a legal play.

Now, for a scenario that highlights a potential difference. Let's say Red Team defender number three just lets the ball deflect or rebound off them into the goal. This is not a resetting, controlled play. Blue Team then scores. 

This would be an offside offense under both college and high school rules. The attacker benefited from being in an offside position.

However, the explanation can be slightly different. In the official Laws of the Game from FIFA, the reset only applies to what's called a 'deliberate play'. High school rules often use the simpler term 'deliberate, controlled play'. While the intent is the same, the wording can differ slightly between rulebooks. The important thing is that in your study materials for certification, they will want you to know this distinction.
