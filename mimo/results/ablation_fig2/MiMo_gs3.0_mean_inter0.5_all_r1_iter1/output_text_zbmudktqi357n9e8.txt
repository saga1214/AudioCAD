Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration. If you're going in the fall, which is their winter, the best time to visit would be November through March. You'll find those super vibrant fall colors in the mountains, you know, like in the state of Michoacán with the famous poinsettias, or the lush greenery of the Yucatán Peninsula. The cool, crisp air is also perfect for painting.

If you want to see more winter scenery, though, spring is also great. April through May brings wildflowers everywhere, like in the desert landscapes of Baja California or the flower fields in Pátzcuaro. Either way, you’re going to get amazing color inspiration. Just pack your paints and take lots of photos!
