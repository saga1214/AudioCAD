That's an excellent point. If you are an artist as well, you have a huge advantage. You can approach publishers directly with a complete picture book manuscript, which includes both your text and your illustrations.

This is a much stronger submission. You can find the right publisher by researching books with a similar style and seeing which houses published them. You can then find the submission guidelines for those specific publishers on sites like Submittable or their own websites. This is a more direct path, but it can also be more competitive. 

And yes, you are correct, you would not need to pay to submit to a publisher. However, do be aware that if you win a contract, you will likely have to pay for the professional printing of the book itself, which is a standard part of the publishing process.
