Yeah, absolutely. Lockport has a few great places to really dive into the history of the Erie Canal. The absolute best place to start is the Lockport Canal Museum. It's located right next to the locks themselves, so you can see them while you learn all about the canal's history. They have a lot of great exhibits, including a full-sized replica of a canal boat.

Another place to check out is the Erie Canal Heritage Center, which is a short walk from the museum. They have more interactive exhibits and even offer boat tours that take you right through the locks. It's a really hands-on way to experience how it all worked.

And if you want to see the incredible engineering up close, you have to walk the Lockport Gorge. You can just follow the Erie Canal Trail, which is a paved path that runs right through the gorge. You'll see the old aqueducts and locks, and you can really appreciate the amazing work that was done there. It's a very peaceful and scenic place to walk and think about the history.
