Vasculitis itself is inflammation of the blood vessels. It's an autoimmune disorder where the immune system mistakenly attacks the blood vessels. It can affect vessels of any size—large, medium, or small. Common symptoms are fever, fatigue, aches, and rashes. Diagnosis usually involves a physical exam, blood tests, and sometimes a biopsy. Vasculitis can lead to serious complications like organ damage from restricted blood flow. Treatment often involves immunosuppressants. It's very important to consult a specialist to get a specific diagnosis.
