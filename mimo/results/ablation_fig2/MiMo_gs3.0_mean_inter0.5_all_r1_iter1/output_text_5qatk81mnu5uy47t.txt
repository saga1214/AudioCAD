### 1. **Fin Choice Finalized**
   - **Recommendation**: Invest in **aluminum-core short fins**. They offer propulsion like traditional fins but with less leg strength demand, perfect for beginners. Brands like Scubapro or Apeks make great options.  
   - *Bonus Tip*: Bring your old short fins as backups.

### 2. **Best Dive Sites in Costa Rica**
   - **Cahuita National Park (Puntarenas)**:  
     - Shallow coral reefs (10–20m deep) with abundant marine life (turtles, rays, Caribbean reef sharks).  
     - Ideal for beginners—gentle currents and easy navigation.  
   - **Solomon Islands (Tortuguero)**:  
     - Caves, wrecks, and deep walls (up to 40m) with schools of barracuda and moray eels.  
     - *Pro Tip*: Take a guided night dive to spot bioluminescent plankton!  
   - **Pochoy Reef (Santa Rosa)**:  
     - Dramatic drop-offs, coral gardens, and frequent whale shark sightings (Dec–Apr).  

### 3. **Temperate vs. Tropical Dive Experiences**
   - **Tropical (like Honduras/Belize)**:  
     - **Pros**: Vibrant coral reefs, tropical fish, turtles, whale sharks.  
     - **Cons**: Stronger currents, warmer water (can be exhausting).  
   - **Temperate (e.g., California, New Zealand)**:  
     - **Pros**: Kelp forests, unique species (octopus, seals), cooler temps.  
     - **Cons**: Requires thermal gear, fewer coral formations.  
   - *Verdict*: Stick with tropical—Costa Rica offers both reef beauty *and* thermal comfort.

### 4. **Trip Logistics**
   - **Best Time**: Dec–Apr (dry season, warmest water, best visibility).  
   - **Dive Operators**:  
     - **Cahuita Dive Center** (beginner-friendly, small-group dives).  
     - **Tortugas Ecological** (eco-focused, turtle conservation programs).  
   - **Accommodation**:  
     - **Pacuare Lodge** (luxury eco-resort with guided dives).  
     - **Hostels in Puerto Viejo** (budget-friendly, social vibe).  

### 5. **Safety & Gear Checklist**
   - **Essentials**:  
     - Rebreather (if certified), dive computer, BCD, backup air tank.  
     - Reef-safe sunscreen, dive knife, hydration pack.  
   - **Pro Tips**:  
     - Take a **refresher course** before the trip.  
     - Book a dive doctor consultation (Costa Rica has limited dive medics).  

### 6. **Beyond Diving**
