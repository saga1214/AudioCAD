Okay, you need a quick energy boost? I hear you. Let's get you fueled up with some drinks and snacks. For drinks, you could have a green tea for a calm caffeine lift, or black coffee for a stronger jolt. An energy drink like Monster or Red Bull would work, but you'll probably need water afterward. A protein shake is another good option for a mix of protein and energy. And for a lighter choice, a smoothie with banana and spinach is great for sustained energy. As for snacks, try a handful of almonds for healthy fats and protein. Greek yogurt is another good one, full of protein. A banana is a classic because it gives you quick energy from its sugar. You could also have some dark chocolate for antioxidants and a little caffeine. And finally, trail mix is perfect for a mix of nuts, seeds, and dried fruit. Hope that helps you power through!
