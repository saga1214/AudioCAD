### 🥂 **Vodka Cocktail: Berry-Basil Smash**  
*Refreshing, fruity, and no dairy or soda.*  
**Ingredients per glass:**  
- 1.5 oz vodka  
- 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz simple syrup (or 1 tsp honey)  
- 4-5 fresh basil leaves  
- 4-5 blackberries or raspberries  
- *Garnish:* Fresh basil sprig + mint or edible flower  

**How to make:**  
1. Muddle basil + berries in a shaker.  
2. Add vodka, lemon juice, and syrup.  
3. Shake with ice, strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
4. Garnish with basil sprig and mint.  

---

### 🌿 **Fernet "Argentinian-Style" (Inspired by Fernet-Branca)**  
*Bold, herbal, and balanced—no soda.*  
**Ingredients per glass:**  
- 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca (or Fernet-Concha)  
- 0.75 oz fresh grapefruit juice  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- 0.25 oz simple syrup  
- 2-3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- *Garnish:* Grapefruit wheel + lime twist  

**How to make:**  
1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
2. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.  
3. Garnish with grapefruit wheel and lime twist.

- **Prep Ahead:** Make syrups, muddle berries, and chill glasses beforehand.
