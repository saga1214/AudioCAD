Great question! Based on your hands-on skills and vintage hobby, here are some meaningful gift ideas for someone interested in hands-on hobbies like tailoring, woodworking, or clay:

### For Tailoring:
- **Quality shears**: Sharp, professional-grade scissors (like Gingher or Kai) are essential for fabric work.  
- **Vintage sewing kit**: A curated kit with needles, thread, pins, and measuring tools.  
- **Parker 51-themed gift**: Engraved repair tools, vintage pen parts, or restoration guides.  
- **Silk or linen fabric**: Beautiful, durable materials to practice with.  

### For Woodworking:
- **Hand plane or chisel set**: Great for beginners and expands your toolkit.  
- **Pre-cut wood scraps**: Ready-to-use lumber for small projects.  
- **Wood-burning tool**: For adding intricate designs.  

### For Clay:
- **Portable pottery wheel**: Compact and affordable for home use.  
- **Clay sculpting tools**: Sets with small, ergonomic tools.  
- **Ceramic wheel classes**: A gift card for learning techniques.  

### General Ideas:
- **Subscription boxes**: Brands like "The Workshop" (for tools) or "Pens & Ink" (for fountain pens).  
- **Books**: "The Tailor’s Art" by Michael Leary or "Woodworking for Beginners" by David Clary.  
- **Experience gift**: A weekend workshop at a local studio or workshop.  

### Why These Work:  
You enjoy detail-oriented restorations, so gifts that support **precision, craftsmanship, or vintage revival** (like quality tools or restoration parts) will likely resonate. Tailoring also aligns with wearable creation—something you can show off!
