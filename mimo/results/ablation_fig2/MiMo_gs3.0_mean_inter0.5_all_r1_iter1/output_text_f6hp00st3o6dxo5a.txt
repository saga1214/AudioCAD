That line of reasoning about the egg containing the DNA for the first chicken still doesn't quite work.

For the mutation that defined the chicken to be a chicken to happen, the creature had to already be a chicken. The egg had to be produced by a hen to contain that specific protein needed for hard eggs. Therefore, a chicken had to be involved in the process at the very beginning. The egg couldn't have come first if the genetic change that made it a 'chicken egg' had to happen after the egg was laid.
