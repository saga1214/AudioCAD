Okay, for kosher dietary laws, the main things to watch out for are avoiding any pork or shellfish. So, with what you've brought... mini weenies in bacon is a no-go, since bacon is pork. Hummus is usually made with sesame and chickpeas, so that's generally fine. Fruit platter is also kosher, as long as the fruit is fresh. Dark chocolate is okay as long as it's pure and doesn't have any dairy or other non-kosher stuff mixed in. Spices are usually fine, black or nutmeg. Hydrogen peroxide is kosher. Blood pudding... that's not going to be kosher. Barley and oats are kosher. Onions are fine. So, basically, hummus, fruit, and dark chocolate are your safe bets. You'll need to skip the bacon weenies and the blood pudding.
