Just to clarify for your med exam prep: the shoulder for rotation involves **three bones**:  
1. **Humerus** (upper arm bone)  
2. **Scapula** (shoulder blade)  
3. **Clavicle** (collerbone; stabilizes the joint).  

The "fourth component" you might be thinking of is part of the **rotator cuff**, which is a group of **four muscles/tendons** (not bones):  
1. Supraspinatus  
2. Infraspinatus  
3. Teres minor  
4. Subscapularis.  

These muscles stabilize the humeral head in the scapular socket for smooth rotation. If your friend has soreness during lateral raises, it could relate to rotator cuff strain or overuse. If pain persists, they should consult a clinician. Best of luck on your exam!
