**Homemade Homemade Treats:**
1.  **Mashed Pumpkin/Sweet Potato Bites:** Mix pureed pumpkin or steamed sweet potato with a tiny bit of peanut butter (xylitol-free), oat flour, or mashed banana. Spoon onto an ice cube tray and freeze. (Great cooling chews & training rewards).
2.  **Peanut Butter & Oat Cookies:** Mix xylitol-free peanut butter with rolled oats or oat flour. Press into small cookies, bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 minutes until firm. Cool completely. (Perfect for training &持久零食).
3.  **Cooked Chicken/Carrot Sticks:** Steam or boil small pieces of plain chicken breast or carrot. Cut chicken into tiny cubes, cut carrot sticks into thin matchsticks (soak briefly in water if needed for digestibility). (Simple, healthy protein & veggie snacks).
4.  **Cheese Cubes (Plain):** Small, plain cubes of mild cheddar, mozzarella, or cottage cheese. (Rich in protein & calcium - use *very* sparingly due to fat content).
