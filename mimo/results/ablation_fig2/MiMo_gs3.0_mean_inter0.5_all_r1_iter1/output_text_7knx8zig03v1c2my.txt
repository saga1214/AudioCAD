### 🍗 Treat Recommendations:
- **Science-themed:** Freeze-dried liver, powdered kibble, or protein bits (nutritional for nerds!).  
- ** roots-inspired:** Italian-style cheese cubes, sun-dried tomatoes, or mini cannoli (if he likes chicken, cheese would likely work too).  
- **General:** Small, soft, high-value treats (like chicken bits or peanut butter) work best for training. Avoid anything too large or messy for a tiny puppy.  

### 🎗️ Clicker Training Explained:
1. **How it works:**  
   - The clicker makes a sharp, consistent sound when Galileo does something *exactly* right (e.g., sits on command).  
   - Immediately after the click, give him a treat.  
   - Repeat 10-15 times per session.  
2. **Why it’s effective:**  
   - The clicker marks the precise moment of success, so he knows *exactly* what earned the reward.  
   - Over time, the click becomes a "bridge" between his action and the treat, reinforcing the behavior faster than verbal commands alone.  
3. **Example for "sit":**  
   - Hold treat near his nose → move upward → he sits naturally.  
   - Click! → Give treat.  
   - Repeat. Gradually add the verbal cue ("sit!") *after* the click.  

### 🏠 Crate Training (Breakdown):
- **Why it’s cozy, not cruel:** Crates mimic a dog’s natural den instinct. They feel *safe*, not trapped.  
- **How to make it positive:**  
  1. Place treats/food inside to encourage entry.  
  2. Gradually increase time inside (start with 5 minutes while you’re nearby).  
  3. Reward calm behavior inside the crate.  
  4. Never use it for punishment—only for rest/house-training.  
- **Escape-proofing:** Ensure the crate is too small for him to stand up fully (for now) and use a divider to adjust space as he grows.  

### 🐕 Puppy School / Training Classes:
What trainers typically do:  
- **Basic commands:** Sit, stay, come, lie down, heel.  
- **Socialization:** Safe exposure to other dogs, people, and noises.  
- **House-training:** Potty schedules, scratching furniture, chewing control.  
- **Respect-building:** Teaching calm behavior around resources (food, toys).  
- **Methods:** Positive reinforcement (treats/praise) is best for puppies!  

### Where to Find Classes:  
- Ask your vet for referrals.  
- Pet stores (like Petco/PetSmart) often have in-house trainers or partner classes.  
- Community centers or local dog trainers on Yelp/Google.  

### Final Tips for You:  
- **Patience is key:** Galileo is tiny (3→12 lbs)—he’ll learn at his own pace.  
- **Consistency matters:** Use the same commands/treats for everyone (family, trainers).  
- **Celebrate small wins!** First "sit," first recall, first potty outside—all milestones.
