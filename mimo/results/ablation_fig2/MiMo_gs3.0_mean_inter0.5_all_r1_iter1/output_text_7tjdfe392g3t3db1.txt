Tuning a string instrument while waiting for dye to process is a great idea! Here's how to do it effectively, regardless of whether you're using a physical tuner or an app:

### 🎸 For a Physical Tuner:
1. **Clip-on tuner** (like Snark or Peterson) or **bass clip-on tuner**.
2. Attach it to the headstock of your instrument.
3. Press the tuner gently against the string.
4. Adjust the tuning pegs until the tuner shows the correct pitch (e.g., A440 for violin/cello, E for guitar).
5. For guitar, tune down to **E♭** or **D** if aiming for strawberry tones.

### 📱 For a Phone App:
1. Download a tuner app (e.g., **GuitarTuna**, **Fender Tune**, **Tuner**).
2. Place your phone close to the string (keep it steady!).
3. Press the app’s "Tune" button and pluck the string.
4. Adjust the tuning peg until the app’s display shows the correct note (e.g., **E** for guitar strings).
5. For rose gold/dyes, tune to **D** or **E♭** to better hold vibrant tones.

### 🎹 General Tips:
- **Start with the open 6th string** (lowest pitch on guitar) and work upward.
- If tuning a **strawberry shade**, ensure strings are tuned to a **slightly darker base** (e.g., D instead of E) to shift the tone toward red/pink.
- For rose gold, **D tuning** creates a warmer metallic base.
- If the dye feels too bright, try an **open tuning** (e.g., open D or open G) for a softer sound while waiting.

### ⚠️ Safety Note:
Avoid touching your hair with your hands during tuning—dye can transfer! Focus on the strings, not your hair.

Happy tuning—and may your strawberry/rose-gold hair dye turn out stunning! 🎨🎶
