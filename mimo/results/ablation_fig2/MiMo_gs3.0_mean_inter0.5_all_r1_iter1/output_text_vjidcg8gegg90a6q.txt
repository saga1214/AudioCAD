For winter chicks on concrete, the most rodent-resistant bedding is wood shavings cut on the diagonal. They are less fluffy than other materials, which helps chicks stay dry and warm, and sand can be used to clean up underneath them. You can also use a product called Orvis, which is a very fine, dust-free wood shavings made specifically for chicks. It's excellent for preventing respiratory issues. Just make a deep layer, about four to six inches, so they can burrow into it for warmth.
