The name itself, 'forever chemicals', does mean they are extremely persistent. There's no magic 'detox' that can remove them. Your body's own systems, like your liver and kidneys, can eventually break them down and get them out, but it's a very slow process. 

So, the best strategy really is prevention. Let's focus on what you can control. The five products to avoid to prevent exposure are:

First, non-stick cookware. Use stainless steel or cast iron instead.
Second, avoid grease-resistant packaging, like fast-food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags.
Third, stay away from things labeled stain- or waterproof, such as carpets.
Fourth, look for cosmetics that are labeled PFAS-free.
And fifth, use a certified water filter because they enter tap and even bottled water.
