Absolutely! If cheese wasn’t her favorite, you could try:
- Small pieces of **peanut butter** (unsweetened, of course)
- **Freeze-dried liver** (beef or chicken)
- A tiny bit of **cooked chicken** (again, avoiding actual whole chicken per vet advice)
