While your connection with him is invaluable, client safety absolutely has to be the number one priority. The aggressive elopement, aggression linked to both highs and lows, dogs running around a cluttered house, and no proper supervision creates a significant safety risk.

Before you can effectively teach him, you need to create a safe space for him to learn.

First, prioritize safety. This means advocating to the BCBA for proper protocols regarding supervision and the physical environment.
Second, continue using his love for music and whatever sensory or play activities genuinely engage him to build on your positive connection and reduce session disruptions.
