Based on all the suggestions, here is a summary of what you'll need. For smoking, you'll need a smoker. For roasting, an oven. For baking, you'll need two more ovens: one for the turkey if you keep it, one for the tart, and one for the apple crisp. For roasting again, a toaster oven can be used for the asparagus. For side dishes, you'll need a stovetop for things like the Brussels sprout salad dressing, mashed potatoes, and cranberry sauce, plus an Instant Pot for the stuffing. For desserts, you'll need ovens for the mac and cheese and cheesecake, and a slow cooker for the apple crisp. So, to recap, the main cooking methods are smoking, roasting, baking, and stovetop cooking. The key appliances you'll be using are your smokers, regular ovens, a toaster oven, an Instant Pot, a slow cooker, and a stovetop. Planning ahead with all that will go a long way this year!
