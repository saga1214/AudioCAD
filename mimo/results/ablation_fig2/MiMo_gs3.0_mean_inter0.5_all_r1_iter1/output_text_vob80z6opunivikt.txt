That’s awesome! Since you steer clear of sweet snacks, here are some savory/vending machine-friendly ideas to fuel your project:  

### 🧀 **Savory Staples:**  
- **Pretzels or Pretzel Rods:** Salty crunch with protein.  
- **Beet Chips:** Savory, smoky, and zero sugar.  
- **Rice Crackers:** Light, crispy, and versatile (pair with cheese if you have it).  
- **Seaweed Snacks:** Lightly salted, chewy, and packed with minerals.  

### 🥜 **Nutty & Seed-Based:**  
- **Mixed Nuts or Trail Mix:** Look for unsalted or lightly salted versions.  
- **Sunflower Seeds or Pumpkin Seeds:** Great protein boost.  
- **Nut Butter Packets:** Add to crackers or eat straight (check for no added sugar).  

### 🍿 **Fun & Crunchy:**  
- **Chips or Tortilla Chips:** Opt for plain, lightly salted, or flavored (e.g., everything bagel).  
- **Crispy Corn Puffs:** Savory, airy, and low-calorie.  
- **Puffed Quinoa or Rice:** Crunchy, protein-rich, and gluten-free.  

### 🍫 **"Non-Sweet" Sweet Twists:**  
- **Dark Chocolate (70%+):** Bitter, rich, and not overly sweet.  
- **Caramel or Butterscotch Packets:** Salty-sweet balance.  
- **Licorice:** Salty-sweet with an herbal kick.  

### 🍌 **Fresh/Frozen Options:**  
- **Bananas:** Nature’s energy bar (peel and eat!).  
- **Frozen Grapes:** Refreshing, naturally sweet without candy.  
- **Vending Machine Veggie Packs:** If available (carrots, celery, etc.).  

### 🧀 **Pro Tip:**  
Check labels for hidden sugars or excessive sodium. For project fuel, prioritize **protein** (nuts, cheese, jerky) and **fiber** (seeds, crackers) to keep you focused!
