First, about the brain lateralization. The left-right brain myth is mostly a myth. While there are some differences in brain hemispheres, like the left side often handling language and the right handling spatial skills, the brain works as a whole system. No one side is purely creative or purely logical. But that doesn't mean it's useless—it's still a useful shorthand for talking about different thinking styles.
