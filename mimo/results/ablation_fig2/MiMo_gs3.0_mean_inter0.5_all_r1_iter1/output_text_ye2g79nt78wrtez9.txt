Re-evaluating, I'd have to change my mind. Ancient Egypt is undeniably a great choice because it's so instantly recognizable and its artifacts are a massive draw. But Ancient Mesopotamia's greatest strength is its fundamental importance as the birthplace of human civilization. Telling the story of writing and cities is incredibly educational and unique. For a museum aiming for a truly memorable and impactful experience, I'd argue Mesopotamia is the better choice. So no, I wouldn't recommend Egypt as the best option.
