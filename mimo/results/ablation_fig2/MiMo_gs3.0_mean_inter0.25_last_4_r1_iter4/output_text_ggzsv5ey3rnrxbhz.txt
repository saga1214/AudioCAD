The name 'forever chemicals' can sound pretty scary, and it's understandable to want to know how to get rid of them. But, um, right now, there isn't a specific medical treatment like a 'juice cleanse' that can remove them from your body. The best thing you can do is focus on preventing any more exposure.

So, to help with that, here are five product types you should avoid to minimize your exposure to what are technically called PFAS. 

First, non-stick cookware is a big one. You can switch to stainless steel, cast iron, or glass cookware instead.
Second, try to avoid grease-resistant food packaging, like what comes with fast food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags.
Third, stay away from things labeled stain-resistant or waterproof, such as carpets, furniture, and some outdoor clothing.
Fourth, check certain cosmetics.
And fifth, since these can get into tap water, using a certified water filter can make a big difference.
