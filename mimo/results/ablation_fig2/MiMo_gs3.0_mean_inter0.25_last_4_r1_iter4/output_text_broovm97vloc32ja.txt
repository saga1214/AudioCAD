Yes, you absolutely can submit a complete picture book manuscript with illustrations. In fact, that's the standard way to submit to publishers who want a fully illustrated project. The most important thing is to include a separate document with all your high-resolution artwork. If you can't get the illustrations in, then the manuscript is your top priority.

For your memoir/dramatization, the process is similar. You'll write the complete manuscript and then seek an agent or consider self-publishing. A publisher will evaluate the entire package.
