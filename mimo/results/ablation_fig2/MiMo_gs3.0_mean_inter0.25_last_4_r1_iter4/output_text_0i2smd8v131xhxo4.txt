### Vocal Endurance & Health
- **Warm-ups**: Daily 5-min routine (lip trills, humming, tongue twisters).  
- **Breathing**: Practice diaphragmatic breathing (inhale 4s, exhale 6s).  
- **Hydration**: Sip room-temp water hourly. Cold water causes muscle tension.  
- **Breaks**: 5-min breaks every 30 mins. Sip water, stretch.  
- **Posture**: Sit upright—slouching strains throat.  
- **Script Marking**: Use colored tabs to mark emotional beats.

### Editing Software & Workflow
- **Switching to Audition**:

  - *Transition Tip*: Export Audacity files (.wav) to Audition via "Render Selection to WAV" first.

  - Mouth Click Removal: **iZotope RX 10** (De-click tool)

- **Organization**: Use nested folders:
