Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items, keeping landlord rules and safety in mind:

**1. TV Mounting:**
* **Plan First:** Measure wall space. Use a level to mark exact center point for TV. Check weight capacity of mount & wall.
* **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder. Mounting into studs is crucial for safety and weight support (most mounts require 2-3 studs).
* **Mark & Pre-drill:** Mark screw holes. Pre-drill pilot holes slightly smaller than screws to prevent splitting drywall. Use a level to align holes.
* **Secure Mount:** Attach wall anchors (if no studs) or directly into studs. Follow manufacturer's instructions for tightening.
* **Mount TV:** Hang TV securely onto the mount. Test stability before using.

**2. Hanging Planters:**
* **Choose Location:** Pick a sturdy wall or ceiling hook rated for the planter's weight + water.
* **Measure & Mark:** Hold planter, mark hook position with painter's tape. Use a level.
* **Install Hook:** Screw hook into wall (into studs if possible). If using ceiling hooks, ensure proper clearance.
* **Hanging Planter:** Slide planter onto hook or use included hanger. Avoid overfilling.

**3. Floating Shelves:**
* **Plan Layout:** Decide shelf heights. Use a level to mark positions. Stagger shelves for visual interest.
* **Locate Studs:** Find studs at marked positions. If no stud, use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for shelf weight.
* **Mark & Pre-drill:** Mark screw holes. Pre-drill pilot holes.
* **Attach Shelves:** Secure shelves horizontally with long screws into studs or anchors. Ensure they're level and flush.

**Essential Safety & Tips:**
* **PPE:** Wear safety glasses.
* **Tools:** Use a stud finder, level, drill, appropriate screws/anchors, measuring tape.
* **Check Weight:** Always verify mount/anchor capacity.
* **Rent if Needed:** For heavy items, rent a drill or miter saw.
* **Noise Management:** Do installations during work hours or landlord-approved times. Use ear protection if needed.
* **Start Simple:** Begin with shelves/planters before the heavy TV mount.
* **Take Breaks:** Don't overexert yourself. Stretch during breaks.

**Decorating Strategy:**
Schedule your Sunday decorating time. Focus on one area at a time (e.g., 1 hour for shelves, 1 hour for planters). Group items by color or height for visual harmony. Use your art pieces as focal points around the new installations. Remember to keep the space functional for work too!

By planning and taking your time, you'll create a beautiful, functional space that respects your new apartment rules. Good luck!
