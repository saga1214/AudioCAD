Of course! Vancouver is a great starting point for road trips. Here are five fantastic options for you and your growing family.

First, a short 2-hour drive to the Fraser Canyon. You can visit the Lillooet Heritage Centre and enjoy a farm-to-table meal at a local B&B.

Second, a 3-hour drive to the Okanagan Valley. You can go wine tasting at a winery, and then enjoy a scenic lakeside dinner.

Third, a 3-and-a-half-hour drive to Victoria. You can explore the Royal BC Museum and then enjoy a beautiful dinner in the inner harbour.

Fourth, a 4-hour drive to Revelstoke. It's famous for its hiking and skiing, but you can also enjoy a gourmet dinner at a mountain lodge.

Finally, a 4-and-a-half-hour drive to Whistler. You can ride the Blackcomb Gondola for amazing views and then dine at a restaurant with a view of the mountains. 

Enjoy your adventures!
