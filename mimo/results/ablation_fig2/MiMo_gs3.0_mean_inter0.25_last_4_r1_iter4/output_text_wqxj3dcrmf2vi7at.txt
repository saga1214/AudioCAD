Alright, let's recap all the homemade and store-bought ideas we discussed, plus a few more tailored for your brown-and-white "little corgi princess."

For Homemade & Homemade-Inspired Treats:
1. Mashed Pumpkin bites frozen solid in an ice cube tray.
2. Peanut Butter Oat Cookies: Mix peanut butter without xylitol with rolled oats, spoon into small balls, and bake until firm. Great for training!
3. Cooked Protein Chunks: Small pieces of plain, boiled chicken breast or carrot sticks.

Additional Homemade Ideas:
* Sweet Potato or Banana Bites: Mash cooked sweet potato or banana, spoon into small molds or use an ice cube tray, and freeze.
* Zucchini or Apple Cookies: Grate zucchini or apple, mix with a little egg or oats, spoon into balls, and bake until firm. These are very healthy.
* Yogurt or Cheese Bites: Freeze small cubes of plain Greek yogurt or a tiny piece of mozzarella cheese.

Crucial Rule: Portion Control! Corgis are adorable but can be prone to being overweight. Always give tiny pieces.

For Popular & Reliable Store-Bought Brands:
1. Blue Buffalo Basic Training Treats: Great size and texture, often flavored like chicken or beef.
2. Wellness Training Treats: Small, crunchy, and affordable.
3. Zuke's Mini Naturals: Tiny, bite-sized, and come in flavors like chicken, beef, or cheese.
4. Greenies Dental Treats: Some Corgis love these for their dental benefits (check with your vet first).
5. Merrick训练零食: Often praised for quality ingredients.
6. Kong Lick Mat: Not a "treat" itself, but you can smear it with peanut butter, yogurt, or wet food for long-lasting, messy fun.
7. Simpaflex Training Bites: Very high-value, dehydrated, and often shaped like stars or hearts.

Key Tips for Store-Bought:
* Check Ingredients: Avoid anything with artificial colors, excessive salt, or unhealthy fillers.
* Start Small: Introduce new brands with just a few pieces to watch for any stomach upset.
* Rotate: Keep a few different brands to prevent boredom.

By offering a variety of these tasty options and training positively, your corgi princess will stay happy, healthy, and well-behaved! Enjoy spoiling your little princess!
