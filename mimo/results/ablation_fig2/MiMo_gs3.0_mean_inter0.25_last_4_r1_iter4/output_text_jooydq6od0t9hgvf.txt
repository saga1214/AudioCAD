Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes for your vodka mix and Argentine-style Fernet:

### **1. Refreshing Vodka Cocktail**  
*(Great for mixed diets, no soda, formal vibe)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
- 0.5 oz honey syrup (equal parts honey + hot water, cooled)  
- 2 cucumber slices (muddled)  
- 4 fresh mint leaves  
- Sparkling water (for top-up)  
- Ice  
- Mint sprig & cucumber wheel for garnish  

**Steps:**  
1. Muddle cucumber and mint in a shaker.  
2. Add vodka, lime juice, and honey syrup.  
3. Shake with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
4. Top with sparkling water.  
5. Garnish with mint and cucumber.  

---

### **2. Argentine-Style Fernet & Vodka**  
*(No soda, bold & refreshing)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
- 1 oz vodka  
- 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz simple syrup  
- 1 oz cola (use diet cola to reduce sugar if preferred)  
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Ice  
- Lemon wheel & spruce tip for garnish  

**Steps:**  
1. Shake Fernet, vodka, lemon juice, simple syrup, and bitters with ice.  
2. Strain into a rocks glass filled with ice.  
3. Top with cola.  
4. Garnish with lemon wheel and spruce tip.  

---

### **Key Notes:**  
- **Soda Allergy:** Both recipes use sparkling water/cola (no carbonated *soda*).  
- **Formality:** Serve in elegant glasses (coupe for vodka, rocks glass for Fernet).  
- **Flexibility:** Adjust sweetness (honey/simple syrup) to taste.  
- **Game Night:** After Pictionary, offer these cocktails alongside dessert (e.g., fruit tarts or chocolate mousse).
