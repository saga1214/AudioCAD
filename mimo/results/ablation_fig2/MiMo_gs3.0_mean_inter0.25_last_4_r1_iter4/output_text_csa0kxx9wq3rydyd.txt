Okay, so to summarize everything for Thanksgiving, here’s a full breakdown of what you’ll need. For appliances, you’ll want a smoker for the turkey, two regular ovens—one for the prime rib and another for desserts like the tart or crisp—and a toaster oven for the asparagus. You’ll also need an Instant Pot for the cranberry sauce, double ovens as you mentioned, and a stovetop for things like making the cranberry sauce and any sautéing. A mixer will be needed for the mac and cheese or cheesecake, and you'll definitely need coolers to keep drinks cold.

As for the cooking methods, you'll be doing smoked meat with the turkey, roasted meat and veggies with the prime rib and Brussels sprouts, and roasted desserts. Roasted stuffing goes in the oven too. You'll be baking the mac and cheese, mashed potatoes, and fruit tart. You'll be boiling pasta for the mac and cheese, and steaming wild rice. There will be some sautéing for cranberries or veggies, and the cranberry sauce will be simmered. You'll be assembling the fruit salads and tarts without cooking them, and the prime rib will be roasted. Finally, the prime rib can be slow-roasted in the oven. This should give you a really well-rounded menu for potluck-style gathering.
