You are absolutely right, older comics are generally more rare.

Here are ten notable comics from the 1940s and 1950s that are considered key investment pieces, though keep in mind that buying one for the first time will likely cost more than five thousand dollars.

First, the record-breaking examples we already mentioned: an Action Comics number one for six million dollars, Amazing Fantasy fifteen for three-and-a-half million, and Captain M.C.s for just over three million.

Another one is *Tales of the Black Panther and Panther’s Friends* number one from 1966. This is the first comic appearance of Black Panther.

Then there's *Fantastic Four* number 48 from 1966, which introduced Galactus, the World Destroyer.

*Conan the Barbarian* number one from 1972 is a classic by Robert E. Howard and Barry W. Warren.

A cornerstone of the Silver Age is *Amazing Spider-Man* number 300 from 1986, which is the first full appearance of Venom.

Another one is *The Incredible Hulk* number 321, which is Hulk's first appearance in the modern, darker tone.

If you're Luke Cage fan, *Marvel Super-Heroes* number 17 from 1969 is his first official team-up with Spider-Man.

*Journey into Mystery* number 109 from 1963 is Thor's first appearance in the Silver Age.

*Thor* number 189 from 1967 is the first appearance of the villain Galactus.

And finally, *Fantastic Four* number 227 from 1979 is the first appearance of the Silver Surfer.

These are all highly sought-after books that have proven their value in the market.
