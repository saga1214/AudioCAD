Okay, let me break down the two yogurt analogies we just went through.

The first one was the 'Throwing a Party' analogy. In that one, Milk is the big empty room, Good bacteria guests, keeping it warm energizes them, sugar is the party food, group hug links arms, tanginess comes from convinced linking, thickness/tangy hug *is* yogurt.
