For the Pilgrims, it was a moment of celebration and relief after barely surviving. For the Wampanoag, it was a diplomatic event that benefited their tribe in the short term, but it came with long-term consequences.

So, it was not a universally happy occasion for both groups at the same time. The idea of a single, happy Thanksgiving for everyone involved is an oversimplification. The history is more complex.

The best way to handle it for your play and party is to encourage the children to portray cooperation and gratitude, while also being open to discussing the historical complexities with your diverse group of guests.
