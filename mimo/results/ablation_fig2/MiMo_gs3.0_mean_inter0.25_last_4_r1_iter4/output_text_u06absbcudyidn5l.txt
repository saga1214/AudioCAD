Okay, absolutely. Chronic skin redness can definitely stem from many things. Besides rosacea and sun damage we mentioned before, it could be eczema, contact dermatitis from something you're allergic to, or even heat. It could also be related to conditions like lupus or lupus-like syndromes, or simply a chronic bacterial or fungal infection.

Now, about vasculitis. It's a condition where your immune system mistakenly attacks your blood vessels. This inflammation can affect vessels of any size—large, medium, or small—which is why it can cause symptoms almost anywhere in your body. Common symptoms often include a fever, feeling tired and unwell, and skin issues like rashes, purple spots, or ulcers. It can also cause pain in your joints, muscles, or abdomen, and if the inflammation affects organs like your kidneys or lungs, that can cause significant problems.

As for supplements, collagen peptides and keratin are good options for strengthening. For inflammation, things like omega-3 fatty acids might help, but again, a doctor can give you the best advice.
