### Step 1: Plunger First
- **Why:** A plunger is your best bet for deep clogs. Ensure it's a **flange plunger** (the one with a soft rubber flap). Create a tight seal over the drain, pump vigorously for 20-30 seconds, then check for water flow. Repeat 3-4 times.

### Step 2: Hot Water + Dish Soap
- **Why:** If plunging fails, pour **boiling water** (carefully!) followed by ½ cup of **dish soap**. Let it sit for 5 minutes. The heat softens debris, and soap lubricates. Check if it clears.

### Step 3: Drain Snake (if available)
- **Why:** If you have a short drain snake in your simple box, insert it into the drain and twist gently. Pull out any debris. Don’t force it—twist to hook the blockage.

### Step 4: Check the P-Trap
- **Why:** 90% of blockages start here. Locate the P-trap under the sink. Turn off water, drain pipes, and remove the P-trap. Clear any debris in the U-bend. Reassemble and test.

### Step 5: Last Resort
- If still clogged, **remove the sink fixture** (if accessible). Disconnect supply lines, lift the sink, and check the drain opening for obstructions. Use your hands or a small tool to clear debris.
