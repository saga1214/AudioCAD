You're right, I see how that was confusing. Let me clarify: The most common original recipe has whiskey at one and a quarter ounces, vermouth at one ounce, and Campari at one ounce. But using equal parts for all three is also a foundational Boulevardier style. Your friend's recipe is perfectly traditional. The one using gin, Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime for 'Last Chance' is a completely different cocktail. For either of those Boulevardier styles, stir over ice until well-chilled before serving.
