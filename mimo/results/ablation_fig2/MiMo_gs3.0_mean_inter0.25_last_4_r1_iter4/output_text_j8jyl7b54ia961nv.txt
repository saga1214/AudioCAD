You are absolutely right. The ethical considerations and public acceptance are paramount in national campaigns, so we have to adjust our thinking. Public resistance to vaccination campaigns can introduce significant ethical risks, legal challenges, and operational difficulties. In that light, the sugar consumption campaign becomes a much more stable and less controversial choice.
