Okay, you need a real energy boost! Let's get you set up with some drink and snack ideas to help you power through. For drinks, I'd suggest starting with a strong cup of coffee, which is a classic for a reason. You could also try an energy drink, like Monster or Red Bull, for a quick jolt. A cup of green tea is another good option because it has caffeine but also L-theanine for a smoother energy. If you want something a bit different, you could have a glass of cold lemonade. And for a non-caffeinated choice, a smoothie with banana and spinach is great for natural energy. 

For snacks, an apple is a classic for a reason—it's crunchy and gives you a steady energy release. You could also grab some nuts and seeds for a protein and healthy fat boost. A banana is another great choice because it's packed with potassium. If you need something sweet, dark chocolate is a good option. And finally, for a salty snack, pretzels or a handful of trail mix would be perfect. 

Hope that helps you get through whatever you need to do!
