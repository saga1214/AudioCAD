We also discussed Pinot Grigio. Store it at about 50 to 55 Fahrenheit, or 10 to 13 Celsius, and serve it chilled at 45 to 50 Fahrenheit, or 7 to 10 Celsius.

Finally, there's a hot mulled wine. You'll store the base wine at cellar temperature, around 55 Fahrenheit. Then you serve it hot, somewhere between 140 and 160 degrees Fahrenheit, or about 60 to 70 Celsius.
