Regarding the Spicy Shrimp Dishes you asked about earlier:

1.  **Spicy Shrimp Scampi:**
    *   Fresh large shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails on or off)
    *   Linguine or other pasta
    *   Garlic (minced or sliced)
    *   Butter (or olive oil)
    *   Lemon juice
    *   White wine (optional, but recommended)
    *   **Spice:** Red pepper flakes (crushed) or hot sauce (like Tabasco)
    *   Fresh parsley (chopped, for garnish)

2.  **Shrimp Fra Diavolo:**
    *   Fresh large shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails on or off)
    *   Spaghetti or fettuccine
    *   Garlic (minced or sliced)
    *   Olive oil
    *   Crushed tomatoes (canned)
    *   **Spice:** **Lots** of red pepper flakes (crushed) or hot sauce (like Frank's RedHot)
    *   Dried Italian herbs (like oregano, basil)
    *   (Optional: A splash of red wine)

3.  **Cajun Shrimp and Grits:**
    *   Large shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails on or off)
    *   Quick-cooking grits (or stone-ground grits)
    *   Buttermilk (or milk)
    *   Butter
    *   **Spice:** Cajun seasoning blend (contains paprika, garlic, onion, cayenne, black pepper, etc.)
    *   (Optional: Sautéed onions, green onions, hot sauce)
