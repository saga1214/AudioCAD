Here's the quick recap using your child-friendly analogy:

*   **Heart:** Gas station *and* engine (pumps cars & makes fuel/oxygen)
*   **Lungs:** Fresh fuel stop (where blood cars get their oxygen)
*   **Arteries:** Big highways (carry blood *away* from the heart/lungs)
*   **Capillaries:** Tiny side streets (where oxygen fuel gets *delivered* to muscles/organs)
*   **Veins:** Roads back to the gas station (bring blood *back* to the heart for refueling)
*   **Kidneys:** Car wash/checkpoints (filter out the "junk" waste from the blood)
*   **Liver:** Sorting & processing station (handles nutrients, sorts fuel, breaks down junk)
