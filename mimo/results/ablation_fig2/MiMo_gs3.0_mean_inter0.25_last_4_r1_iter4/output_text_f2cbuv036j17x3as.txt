Okay, diesel camper vans age, and maintenance is important before a big trip. For brakes, some signs of wear are a soft pedal, a grinding noise when you press the brake, or a pulsing sensation. If you're not sure, a professional brake inspection is the safest bet. For diesel engines, spark plugs are usually a deeper blue color and can be hard to change. It's not a simple task—usually requires a special socket and sometimes you might need to remove other parts to get to them. Honestly, the safest thing is to have a trusted mechanic check the diesel engine and the camper’s systems, like the water heater and fridge, before you go. It’s better to be safe than sorry!
