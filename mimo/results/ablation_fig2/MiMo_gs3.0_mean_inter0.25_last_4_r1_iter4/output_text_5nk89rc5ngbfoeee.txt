For an end-of-year celebration, balancing simplicity, fun, and logistics is key—especially while pregnant! Here’s a practical approach:

### **Venue & Format Ideas**
1. **Hybrid Option**:  
   - **If outdoors (park)**: Host a casual BBQ/pizza party. Let parents handle setup/cleanup (rotating duties).  
   - **If indoors (cafeteria)**: Keep it low-key with pizza/sundaes and badge display.  
   - **Weather Backup**: Have a rain plan (e.g., move to a community hall or extend cafeteria time).

2. **Activities**:  
   - **Simple Games**: Sack races, three-legged races, or a "patch scavenger hunt" (kids find badges in their sashes).  
   - **Dessert Bar**: Sundaes (dairy-free!) or pizza. Let girls vote on snacks beforehand.  
   - **Award Ceremony**: Celebrate badges/skills with personalized certificates or small prizes (e.g., craft kits).

### **Cost & Logistics**  
- **Parents Cover**: Sashes, badge materials, and main food (BBQ/pizza).  
- **Troop Buys**: Ice cream, sundae toppings, or small party favors (e.g., seed packets).  
- **AI Suggestion**: Use a free app (e.g., SignUpGenius) to assign tasks (food, games, cleanup) to parents—makes it fair and organized.

### **Why This Works**  
- **Low Pressure**: Games and desserts require minimal prep.  
- **Inclusive**: Lets parents contribute (reduces your workload).  
- **Memorable**: Focus on achievements—kids love showing off badges!  

You’ve got this! Start small, involve parents, and prioritize smiles over perfection. Your daughters will be thrilled you stepped up! 🍦🎉
