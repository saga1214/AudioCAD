Here's a concise list of my top recommendations for cutting sheet metal and preparing drywall, including where to buy them locally in Indianapolis:

### Cutting Tools:
1.  **Thinner Sheets/Curves:** Pair of **Aviation Snips**
    *   *Where to Buy Locally:* The Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards, Tractor Supply Co., Ace Hardware
2.  **Thicker Metal/More Power:** **Angle Grinder** with Cut-Off Wheel
    *   *Where to Buy Locally:* The Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards
3.  *(Alternative Straight Cuts):* Metal-Bladed **Jigsaw**
    *   *Where to Buy Locally:* The Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards, Tractor Supply Co.

### Drywall Fasteners:
1.  **Best Overall (Holding Power):** **1 1/4-inch Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws** (for wood studs)
    *   *Why Better:* Prevents nail pops and cracking as the house settles. Screw guns set depth perfectly.
    *   *Where to Buy Locally:* The Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards (Best Selection & Prices)

2.  **Frame Nails (If Used):** **Metal Ring-Shank Nails** (e.g., 16d 2" or 18d 1 5/8")
    *   *Why Better:* Rings grip wood securely, resisting withdrawal.
    *   *Where to Buy Locally:* Ace Hardware, Tractor Supply Co., Menards

### Insulation Behind Drywall:
1.  **Most Versatile (Rolls):** **Fiberglass Batt Insulation** (Pre-cut for studs)
    *   *Why Good:* Standard, widely available, cost-effective, good thermal/acoustic properties.
    *   *Where to Buy Locally:* The Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards (Best Selection & Prices)
2.  **Best for Soundproofing/Fireproofing:** **Mineral Wool Insulation** (e.g., Rock Wool)
    *   *Why Good:* Excellent sound absorption, fire resistance, mold resistance.
    *   *Where to Buy Locally:* Menards (Best selection), The Home Depot, Lowe's (Smaller quantities).
3.  **Best Air Sealing (Professional Finish):** **Spray Foam Insulation**
    *   *Why Good:* Expands to fill gaps, creates an airtight seal, excellent insulation value.
    *   *Where to Buy Locally:* Menards (Products), The Home Depot, Lowe's (Tools/Supplies), *Often requires a pro installation.*
