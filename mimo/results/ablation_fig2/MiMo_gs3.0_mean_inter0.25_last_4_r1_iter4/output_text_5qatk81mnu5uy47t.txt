### 1. **Addressing the Short vs. Long Fins Debate**

   - **Recommendation**: Start with **short or mid-length fins** for your first few Costa Rican dives. You can upgrade to long fins later if currents intensify.

### 2. **Dream Trip Itinerary: Best of Costa Rica**
   - **Day 1-3: Pacific Coast (Puntarenas)**  
     - **Why**: Calmer waters, abundant coral reefs, and abundant marine life.  
     - **Key Sites**:  
       - **Don Juan**: Iconic spot for turtles, rays, and schools of fish.  
       - **Sirena**: Dramatic drop-offs with reef sharks and barracuda.  
       - **Short fins ideal here** for easy navigation in calm conditions.  
   - **Day 4-6: Caribbean Coast (Tortuguero)**  
     - **Why**: Mangrove forests, jungle immersion, and strong currents.  
     - **Key Sites**:  
       - **Tortuga Point**: Snorkel-friendly, but strong currents demand long fins.  
       - **Coral Gardens**: Vibrant reefs; mid-length fins work well.  
   - **Day 7-9: Cocos Island (Optional Extension)**  
     - **Why**: Remote, strong currents, and legendary whale sharks.  
     - **Key Sites**:  
       - **Punta Mina**: Thrilling dives with massive marine life.  
       - **Long fins essential here** for power in ocean swells.
