Of course. For miso soup or cucumber salad: for the cucumber salad, you'll need cucumbers, rice vinegar, sesame oil, soy sauce without legumes like tamari, and sesame seeds. For the miso soup, you'll need dashi or just water, white miso paste, soft tofu, and green onions. 

For chicken teriyaki main course: you'll need chicken breast, soy sauce without beans, mirin, sugar, garlic, ginger, jasmine rice, broccoli and bell peppers for stir-fry, and sesame oil. 

For the finisher: for mango sticky rice, stick with jasmine sticky rice, fresh mango, coconut milk, sugar, and salt. Matcha homemade ice cream requires matcha powder, heavy cream, sweetened condensed milk, and vanilla extract if you want to add it. 

One more thing, honey or agave is a good sweetener if he's also allergic to soy sauce in trace ingredients, so feel free to swap. Good luck tonight! It sounds romantic.
