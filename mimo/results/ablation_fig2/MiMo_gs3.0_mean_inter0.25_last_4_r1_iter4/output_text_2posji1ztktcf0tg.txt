That's sweet of you! Vegetarians can totally appreciate thoughtful menu planning, even from skeptics. I’d suggest checking out her favorite comfort foods but with a veg-friendly twist. For example:  
- **Mushroom risotto** (rich, cheesy, but dairy-free if needed)  
- **Stuffed bell peppers** (with quinoa, lentils, and cheese or feta)  
- **Tofu or chickpea curry** (with basmati rice and a side salad)  
- **Veggie-packed lasagna** (if she does eggs, or a vegan version with cashew cheese)  
- **Grilled veggie and halloumi skewers** (if she’s flexible on cheese)  
Would she prefer something homey, fancy, or just fun? Maybe you could even tie it to a podcast episode she loves, like making a "Satanic Panic" theme dinner for laughs. Either way, she’ll feel special that you’re putting in the effort!
