Based on market presence, variety, and reputation, the **top three car brands known for sedans** are generally considered to be:

1.  **Toyota:** Renowned for reliability, longevity, and a vast lineup of sedans (from the Corolla and Camry to the Avalon and various hybrids). Sedans are a core part of their global identity.
2.  **Honda:** Highly respected for a balanced combination of fuel efficiency, performance, reliability, and practicality. The Accord and Civic are iconic sedans.
3.  **BMW:** Synonymous with premium performance sedans. The BMW 3 Series, 5 Series, and 7 Series are globally famous benchmarks for luxury, driving dynamics, and engineering in the sedan segment.

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Hyundai:** While also producing excellent sedans (like the Elantra, Sonata, and Genesis sedans), Hyundai is often slightly behind Toyota, Honda, and BMW in terms of *pure* sedan brand association for many consumers, though they are a major player.
*   **Luxury Focus:** Brands like Mercedes-Benz (E-Class, C-Class), Audi (A4, A6), and Lexus (ES, IS) are also extremely strong in sedans, particularly in the luxury segment. BMW often edges them out in pure sedan brand association for mainstream buyers.
*   **Practicality & Efficiency:** Brands like Toyota and Honda consistently rank highest in reliability and ownership costs for sedans. Hyundai and Kia are also very strong here.
*   **Performance/Luxury:** BMW is the undisputed leader in the premium performance/luxury sedan space, closely followed by Mercedes-Benz and Audi.

So, while many brands excel at sedans, **Toyota, Honda, and BMW** remain the most consistently recognized and dominant brands specifically associated with the sedan body style globally.
