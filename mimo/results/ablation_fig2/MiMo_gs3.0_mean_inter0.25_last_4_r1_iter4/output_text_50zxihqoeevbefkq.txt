Here's a categorized shopping list based on our menu and party details, with quantities calculated for 24 mouths (20 adults + 4 kids):

### **Produce & Fresh Items**
- Ham (14–16 lb, smoked or spiral-cut)
- Scalloped potatoes (3 lbs, for side)
- Asparagus (4 bunches)
- Carrots (2 lbs, for glazed carrots alternative)
- Garlic (1 head, for potatoes/asparagus)
- Fresh herbs (parsley/dill, small bunches)

### **Proteins & Meats**
- Shrimp for cocktail (1 lb)
- Ground sausage/pork for pigs in a blanket (1 lb)

### **Dairy & Pantry Staples**
- Cream cheese (8 oz, for scalloped potatoes)
- Butter (1 stick, for potatoes)
- Olive oil (large bottle)
- Lemon (2–3)
- Potato flakes (if not using whole potatoes)
- Grape juice (4 2L bottles for kids)
- Potato rolls/hamburger buns (slider combo)

### **Drinks**
- Champagne (8 bottles) OR sparkling wine/grape juice for adults too
- Signature cocktail ingredients ( vodka, mixers, garnishes)
- Ice (2 bags)
- Sparkling cider (for non-alcoholic adult options)

### **Decor & Atmosphere**
- Balloons (assorted sizes)
Kid noisemakers/festive cups ($5–$10 at party stores)
Music playlist link (Spotify/Apple Music)

### **Snacks & Extras**
- Mini slider patties/buns
- Tortillas or toothpicks for pigs in a blanket
- Cocktail sauce/hot sauce
- Chopped veggies + hummus (for kids’ healthy nibbles)
- Napkins (plenty!)
- Paper plates/cups/silverware (24 sets)

### **Tips**
1. **Prep Ahead**: Make scalloped potatoes, glaze carrots, or cocktail the day before.
2. **Kid Zone**: Reserve pigs in a blanket for kids’ plates.
3. **Chill Early**: Store champagne/juice in fridge 24 hours ahead.
4. **Kid-Friendly Twist**: Add a simple cheese platter or fruit skewers for four little guests.

Need recipes for specific dishes? Just ask! Happy hosting! 🎉
