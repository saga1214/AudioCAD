You already mentioned Skinnypop and Popchips, which is a great gluten-free start, so I have a couple more gluten-free snack ideas for camping for sustained energy.

Trail mix works well, just make sure to check that there are no gluten-containing ingredients like regular pretzels in it.

Nuts and seeds, such as almonds or pumpkin seeds, are also very nutrient-dense and gluten-free.

And for something a little sweet that holds up well, rice cakes are a good option.
