Okay, you want simple, meaningful gifts that work for all ages, and you love macramé yourself. So, let’s start there. You could still make plant hangers, but maybe offer them in different sizes or with simple embroidery so the little ones can help with that part. 

Then there’s the cookie-baking plan. Drop cookies are quick, but maybe you could also do no-bake options? Like decorated Oreos or even those frozen yogurt cups where the kids just add toppings. That way, it’s still sweet but less work. 

For getting everyone involved, I think the station idea is great. You could have one station for the macramé, one for the cookies, and maybe a third station for something like decorating paper snowflakes. That way, everyone has something to do. 

The candy globe idea is fun, but yeah, the glue gun thing is a pain. So, maybe something like salt dough ornaments. You can make them, bake them, and then the kids can paint them. No glue needed. 

And for the pet food thing, I’d definitely check Chewy or even Amazon for bulk deals. Sometimes they have subscriptions that make it cheaper. 

But really, the main thing is finding something the kids love doing, and you don’t have to be there the whole time. Fingerprint art on a canvas or even on cookies could be a good mix. Does that sound like something you could work with?
