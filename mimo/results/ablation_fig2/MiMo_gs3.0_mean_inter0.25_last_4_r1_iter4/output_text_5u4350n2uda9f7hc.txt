That East balcony sounds perfect for adding a homey NYC touch! Five key considerations for houseplants here:

1.  **Light Match:** East-Facing Sun is Ideal Morning Light. Prioritize sun-lovers like Geraniums, Petunias, or small Succulents.
2.  Fifth Floor Wind Factor: City balcony can get breezy up that high. Choose sturdier, more resilient plants that won't get tossed around.
3.Space is King: With only a tiny chair and table, focus on *compact* plants. Succulents and Kalanchoe on the table, Snake Plant or small Pothos on the floor.
4.  Low Maintenance = Best for a Month: NYC work onsite + rental = pick super tough plants. Geraniums, Snake Plant, Succulents barely need watering.
5.  Portability at Departure: Since you're renting and want to take them home, stick to small nursery pots and compact varieties. Geraniums, Snake Plant, Pothos, Succulents are all designed for easy transport. A full geranium pot won't be a problem up five flights!

Based on that, here are your best small balcony companions:

*   **Birds Nest Snake Plant (Sansevieria trifasciata 'Laurentii'):** Perfect floor plant. Its striking, tightly curled silver-green leaves look like a nest. Extremely hardy, loves the morning sun, and stays very compact (under 12 inches tall). Lightweight and easy to move.
*   **Succulents (Echeveria, Haworthia, Sedum 'Black Knight'):** Ideal for your table. They come in tiny, colorful rosettes (like Echeverias), striped patterns (like Haworthias), or trailing/compact forms (Sedum). All adore bright morning sun, need very little water, and are feather-light.
*   **Small Beggonia or Kalanchoe (e.g., B. 'Angel Wing' or K. 'Luciae'):** Great table options. Begganias offer delicate, often red-tipped leaves; Kalanchoes have lovely spotted or banded foliage. Both love East light, are moderately tough, stay small, and are very light.

All three suggestions fit your space, light, and wind conditions beautifully, are super easy to care for during your month, and are absolutely manageable to carry up five flights in small pots at the end! Enjoy making it feel like home!
