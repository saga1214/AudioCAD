For winter chicks on concrete, the key to insulation is a thick layer of wood shavings. Make them large-flake and use what's known as the deep litter method again: just keep covering the old ones with fresh shavings. This composting will naturally create warmth.

For ventilation up high is still critical to prevent frostbite and manage moisture.

As for rodent prevention, besides sealing drafts, using a hardware cloth to line the bottom of their setup on the concrete will make it very difficult for rodents to get in. Clean it out regularly, and you'll be set.
