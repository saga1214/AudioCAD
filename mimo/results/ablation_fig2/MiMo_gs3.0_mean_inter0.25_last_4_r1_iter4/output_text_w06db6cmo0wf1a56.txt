Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's a lot of people! Keeping things healthy but also appealing to everyone, including getting those sugar-free sodas in and celebrating with desserts, is a great challenge. I've put together a detailed cooler-friendly plan for you. The main goals are still minimal mess and grab-and-go convenience.

**Drinks Section:**
For water, aim for thirty reusable water bottles, ten for the adults and twenty for the kids.
For low-sugar juice, get thirty boxes for the children.
For sugar-free sodas, pack fifteen twelve-ounce cans for the dads. It's a good idea to check the groom's specific preference.
Don't forget electrolyte drinks! Ten single-serve boxes for the dads can be a great refresher, especially in the heat.
Also, pack insulated drink thermoses with iced coffee or tea for the adult dads.
And finally, have ten travel canteens with hot water for dads who want hot beverages.

**Snacks Section:**
For cheese sticks, get thirty kid-friendly portions.
Grapes, buy about two and a half pounds and pre-pack them in individual bags.
Baby carrots with single cups of single-serving hummus cups are messy-free and great for kids and some adults.
Individual pretzels or trail mix bags: thirty of them should cover everyone.
Hard-boiled eggs for a more protein-packed option: pack ten cartons of six.
Fresh fruit cups in juice, drained of the excess juice, for about fifteen portions.
And protein bars for an extra energy boost: pack fifteen boxes.

**Meal Section:**
For calzones, if you decide to go that route, pre-order fifteen mini-calzones from a local pizza place. They should arrive cold and are easy to pack in the coolers. If you'd rather not deal with that, Greek gyros bowls are a fantastic alternative. They're healthy, protein-packed, and easy for everyone to eat.

**Dessert Section:**
For the cake, consider cupcakes. They're perfect for celebrations and easy to grab. Get fifteen boxes, making sure to check if they are nut-free if that's a concern for anyone. A fruit pie is also a great option, but a mini fruit tart in individual portions might be even cleaner for on-the-go eating.

**Cooler Organization Tips:**
Use different colored cooler bags for drinks, snacks, and food to keep things organized. Lay out the food in containers with lids to prevent spills. Using chalkboard labels on everything will make grabbing and returning items super easy and clear.

This plan should give you a great mix of healthy options and fun favorites to make sure you all have a fantastic and stress-free day at the theme park. Have a great time!
