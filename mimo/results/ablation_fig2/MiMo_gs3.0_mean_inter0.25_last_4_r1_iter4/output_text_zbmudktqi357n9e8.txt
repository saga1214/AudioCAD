Okay, so Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration, especially if your favorite thing all time is using all different autumn colors. The peak autumn foliage, which they call "verano tardío" or late summer, is typically from September to November. That's when you'll see the most vibrant leaves changing, especially in the highlands. But if you're looking for a bit of a crowd and warmer weather, late October or early November could be a sweet spot. Just, you know, check the exact dates for the year since they can vary a little. That should give you plenty of material to paint back home. Enjoy the trip and the inspiration!
