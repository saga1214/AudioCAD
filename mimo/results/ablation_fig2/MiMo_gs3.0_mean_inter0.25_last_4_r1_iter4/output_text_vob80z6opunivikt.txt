That’s awesome—the coffee table is ready for its snack debut! Since you lean savory, here are some vending machine-friendly ideas that’ll fill your table stylishly:

### 🧂 Savory & Crunchy Picks:
1. **Pretzels or Potato Chips**  
   - *Why:* Sturdy, salty, and perfect for dipping (hummus leftover from bolt assembly?).  
   - *Quantity:* Grab a family-sized bag or 5-6 individual packs.  

2. **Roasted Nuts or Trail Mix**  
   - *Why:* Protein-packed, non-melty, and shelf-stable. Look for spicy or sea-salt versions.  
   - *Tip:* Vending machines often have single-serve bags—ideal for portion control.  

3. **Cheese Crisps or Crackers**  
   - *Why:* Savory, light, and great for pairing with leftover hummus or a glass of milk.  
   - *Bonus:* Some machines have flavored varieties (e.g., garlic parmesan crackers).  

4. **Beef Jerky or Plant-Based "Jerky"**  
   - *Why:* Portable, high-protein, and zero mess.  

### 🍫 Savory-Sweet Compromises (If You’re Curious!):
- **Dark Chocolate-Covered Almonds**  
  - *70%+ cocoa* keeps it bitter-sweet, not sugary.  
- **Caramel Popcorn**  
  - Savory popcorn base + salty caramel drizzle.  

### 🧊 Refreshing Options:
- **Iced Tea or Sparkling Water**  
  - Keep a pitcher in the fridge for hydration while tackling future projects.  
- **Fruit Snacks** (if vending has them)  
  - *Raisin bags* or *dried mango*—sweet but not candy-like.  

### Pro Tips:
- **Layer Smartly:** Stack crackers under mugs, nest bowls under books, or use the drawer for nuts.  
- **Tool Storage:** Tuck those M3-M7 wrenches in a drawer organizer—they’ll be ready for Round 2!  
- **Presentation:** Roll up a newspaper for a "snack pyramid" or use coasters to protect the wood.
