For a retro theme, you could do a “Vintage Pizza Night.” You could serve classic square or rectangular pizzas, maybe even with toppings like pineapple or pepperoni, and pair it with a retro soda like root beer or orange soda. Another idea is a “Breakfast-for-Lunch Casserole.” Think of an old-school, hearty meal like a meatloaf casserole or a baked mac and cheese, but with a twist. You could add a side of pickles or ketchup to make it feel nostalgic. Both of these ideas are simple to plan and tie into the theme.
