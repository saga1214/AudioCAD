Yes, for a family reunion fundraiser for diabetes awareness, activities, food, drink, and thoughtful planning are key. Activities could include family walk, scavenger hunt, educational games, walk or fun run, raffle, or silent auction. Healthy food options should focus on fresh fruits, veggies, leans, and whole grains, with sugar-free drinks. Raffle ticket prices are usually two to three dollars, with a restaurant gift card or tech gadget as a grand prize. For a silent auction, consider tickets to events, spa days, or gift baskets themed for cooking, self-care, or fitness. A scavenger hunt at a park is a good idea. For a barbecue setup, you could still focus on healthier twists like fruit skewers, fruit salad, frozen yogurt, or oatmeal cookies. To stay on budget, using locally donated items or preparing simple treats is a great option.
