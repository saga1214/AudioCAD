Okay, let's tackle both parts of your question. First, about the U-turn penalty in BC. If you make an unsafe U-turn, it's considered a dangerous driving infraction. The penalty can include a fine of around $160 plus a 3 demerit points on your license. If you make an unsafe U-turn at an intersection where it's prohibited, the fine is higher, about $190 with 4 demerit points. It's best to just follow the rules to avoid any issues.
