Most tasks use both hemispheres, just in different ways. Creativity can exist alongside logic—it’s not an either/or situation. So, if you think of creativity and structure as separate things, they don’t have to be. 

Now, for careers that blend measurement and creativity, design fields could be a great fit. For example, data visualization merges hard facts with artistic expression. Even 3D modeling or game design can balance measurable outcomes with artistic flow. 

As for tinnitus, most cases are indeed physical—stemming from hearing damage, aging, or ear issues. Stress can sometimes make it worse, but it’s not psychological in origin.
