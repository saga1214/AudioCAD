Dallas has some great Asian noodle spots! I'd recommend trying these. First, there's Nom Nom Noodles, which has a great ramen and udon. Second, Yummy World has a huge variety of Asian dishes, including noodles. Third, Sushi Amigo is great for sushi and also has noodle options. As for protein bars, I'd recommend Quest Bars, which are high protein and low sugar. Also, consider Clif Bars or RX Bars. These are all good options to keep you fueled!
