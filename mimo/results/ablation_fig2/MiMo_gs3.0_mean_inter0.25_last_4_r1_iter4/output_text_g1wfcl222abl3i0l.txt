About the glue gun frustration—this happens all the time! If it feels hot to the touch but isn’t outputting glue, it’s likely clogged or needs a bit more heat. Try letting it warm up for 5–10 minutes longer (or using a hair dryer on low to help), and gently tap the tip if it’s jammed. Sometimes a drop of water on the nozzle can help loosen residue.
