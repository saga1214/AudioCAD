Absolutely. The Lockport area has a rich history with the Erie Canal, and there are several great places to learn more. The Lockport Museum, which is housed in a former canal warehouse, is a must-visit. It has a full-scale replica of a canal boat and exhibits that cover the canal's history, the labor that built it, and the city's industrial past. You can also see the original canal locks that were used to move goods. They're still in operation, so you can watch boats being lifted over the Niagara Escarpment. The experience is really something special.
