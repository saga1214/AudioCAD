Here's a complete, categorized list of the basic and advanced measurements we discussed, organized for clarity:

### **Core Body Measurements (take for every fitting):**
1. **Bust** (around fullest part)  
2. **Natural Waist** (the narrowest part, above belly button)  
3. **Hips** (widest part, usually 8-12 inches below natural waist)  

### **Essential Additions for Fitted Garments:**
- **Sleeve Length** (shoulder to wrist for tops/dresses)  
- **Shoulder Width** (across upper back for perfect jacket/shirt fits)  
- **Inseam** (front thigh to ankle for pants)  
- **Thigh** (between hip and knee for tailored pants/skirts)  
- **Neck** (for collars/cuffs)  
- **Bicep** (for sleeve volume at upper arm)  
- **Upper Back (Slope)** (shoulder to upper back for jackets)  

### **Special Considerations for Your Client (6'4", long legs):**
- **Back Rise** (critical for tall clients! Measure from crotch to waistband)  
- **Hip-to-Ankle Length** (ensure pants end at shoe tops)  
- **Shoe Measurement** (try pants *with client’s shoes* to pin hem length)  

### **Key Tips:**
- Use a flexible tape measure (not a ruler!).  
- Mark measurements with a pen for clarity.  
- For tall clients, prioritize **back rise** and **hem length**.  
- Always test patterns on the client (not just swatches!).
