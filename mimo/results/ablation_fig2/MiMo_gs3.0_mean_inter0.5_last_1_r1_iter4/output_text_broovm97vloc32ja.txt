That's a fantastic asset to have, and you are absolutely right. Publishers love it when the author also provides illustrations. However, there are some very important things to know about how they approach it.

First, when you submit your complete manuscript to an agent, you should only submit the text. Do not include your illustrations at that stage. They will ask for them if they are interested. 

Second, and this is crucial, you have to understand the hierarchy. The publisher will always hire their *own* illustrator, and the author-illustrator gets paid a much smaller royalty per book compared to the author of just the text. 

So, if you want to be the illustrator as well, your best bet is to find a publisher who has an in-house art department. You can research publishers that specifically publish author-illustrator books. When you submit, you'll include a portfolio of your artwork along with your manuscript. This is the only way to ensure you get the full author payment.
