Let me clear that up for you. The truly classic ratio is one and a quarter ounces whiskey to one ounce vermouth and one ounce Campari. Your friend's one-ounce-each version is also traditional and very popular. Now, for Last Chance, as I mentioned, it's equal parts as well, but with different ingredients: three-quarters of an ounce each of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime juice. So for your Boulevardier, I'd recommend sticking with the one-and-a-quarter recipe, but if you want to switch it up, the Last Chance is a fantastic choice. Just remember to garnish your Boulevardier with an orange twist! Let me know if you need anything else.
