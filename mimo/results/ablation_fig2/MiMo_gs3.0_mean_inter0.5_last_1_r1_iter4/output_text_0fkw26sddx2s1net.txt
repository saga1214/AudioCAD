Here's a detailed three-course Asian-inspired meal plan tailored to your husband's preferences, complete with a full shopping list:

**Inspiration Menu: Legume Allergy & No-Seaf00d Friendly**

*   **Starter:** Light Miso Soup (or alternative)
*   **Main Course:** Classic Chicken Teriyaki with Jasmine Rice & Stir-Fried Veggies
*   **Dessert:** Mango Sticky Rice OR Simple Matcha No-Churn Ice Cream

---

**Detailed Meal Plan & Inspiration:**

1.  **Starter Inspiration: Cucumber Salad with Sesame Miso Dressing**
    *   *Why it works:* Light, refreshing, legume-free, legume-free, and showcases miso (legume-free) without any legumes. Quick to prepare.
    *   *Simple Preparation Inspiration:* Thinly slice cucumbers. Make a quick dressing by whisking together white miso paste, rice vinegar, a touch of sugar (or honey), a drizzle of sesame oil, and a tiny pinch of salt. Toss cucumbers in the dressing and sprinkle generously with toasted sesame seeds just before serving. Serve chilled.

2.  **Main Course Inspiration: Husband-Friendly Chicken Teriyaki Meal**
    *   *Why it works:* Classic, flavorful, legume-free (using only chicken), and avoids seafood. The stir-fried veggies add color and freshness.
    *   *Simple Preparation Inspiration:* Marinate sliced chicken breasts in a mix of soy sauce, mirin, a splash of sake or dry sherry (optional but adds depth), and a little grated ginger/garlic for 15-30 minutes. Pan-sear or grill the chicken until cooked through. Simmer the marinade (boiling for a minute or two to thicken slightly) until syrupy, then pour over sliced chicken. Serve with:
        *   **Steamed Jasmine Rice:** Fluffy and fragrant.
        *   **Stir-Fried Veggies:** Use any non-legume Asian veggies your husband likes (e.g., bell peppers, carrots, snap peas, baby corn, mushrooms, bok choy). Heat oil in a wok or large pan, stir-fry veggies until crisp-tender. Season simply with a splash of soy sauce and maybe a pinch of sugar or sesame oil. *Inspiration Tip: Keep it light and colorful.*

3.  **Dessert Inspiration Options:**
    *   **Option 1 (Classic): Mango Sticky Rice**
        *   *Why it works:* Sweet, creamy, tropical, legume-free, no seafood. Beautiful presentation.
        *   *Simple Preparation Inspiration:* Cook short-grain sticky rice according to package directions (soaked rice + water). Fluff with a fork. Simmer canned coconut milk (full fat) with a pinch of salt and 1-2 tbsp sugar until slightly thickened. Mix a portion of the cooked rice into the coconut milk mixture. Arrange warm or room temperature sticky rice on plates and top generously with fresh, ripe mango slices (sliced diagonally looks nice).
    *   **Option 2 (Easier & Faster): Simple Matcha No-Churn Ice Cream**
        *   *Why it works:* Creamy, rich, earthy flavor, legume-free, no seafood. Requires minimal equipment.
        *   *Simple Preparation Inspiration:*
            1.  Whip heavy cream to soft peaks.
            2.  Mix sweetened condensed milk and 1-2 tbsp high-quality matcha powder (sifted to avoid lumps) until smooth and lump-free.
            3.  Gently fold whipped cream into the matcha mixture until just combined.
            4.  Spread into a freezer-safe container.
            5.  **Inspiration Tip: Cover and freeze for at least 4-6 hours (or overnight).** Stirring every hour or so helps prevent large ice crystals. Serve scoops with a sprinkle of toasted coconut flakes or extra matcha powder if desired.
