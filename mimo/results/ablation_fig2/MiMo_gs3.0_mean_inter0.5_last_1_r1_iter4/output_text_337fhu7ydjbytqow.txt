Here's a **segmented closing gear packing list** tailored to your 20-mile (day/early evening) and final 12-mile (night) pacing sections:

---

### **For the 20-Mile Segment (Daytime + Early Evening)**
Bring **versatile, breathable, and weather-resistant layers** since temps will drop as night approaches.  
- **Moisture-wicking base layer** (top + bottom): Synthetic or merino wool to keep sweat off skin.  
- **Mid-layer** (for warmth): Fleece jacket or light puffy vest (removable).  
- **Wind-resistant/water-resistant outer layer**: Jacket or shell (e.g., lightweight rain shell) for hilly wind/rain.  
- **Gloves**: Thin running gloves for grip and warmth.  
- **Hat**: Cap for daytime sun, plus a **warm hat** (beanie/fleece) for dusk.  
- **Socks**: Wool or synthetic blend (bring extras).  
- **Headlamp/handheld light**: Essential even for daytime—it’s your backup.  
- **Lightweight pack/vest**: To carry layers or extra nutrition between aid stations.  

---

### **For the Final 12-Mile Segment (Night Run)**  
Prioritize **warmth, visibility, and grip** for full darkness.  
- **Moisture-wicking base layer** (as above).  
- **Mid-layer**: Fleece or puffy jacket (down/synthetic insulation).  
- **Windproof/waterproof outer layer**: **Non-negotiable**—lightweight rain shell or down jacket.  
- **Gloves**: **Warm, insulated gloves** (fingerless or full-fingered).  
- **Hat**: **Warm hat + buff/gaiter** (for neck coverage).  
- **Headlamp/handheld light**: **Bright, high-lumen model** + extra batteries.  
- **Socks**: **Thicker wool/synthetic socks** (change if feet get damp).  
- **Lightweight pack/vest**: Still useful to stash layers.  

---

### **Key Extras for Both Segments**
- **Reflective gear**: Add a reflective vest or arm band for safety.  
- **Grip-enhancing socks**: Consider trail-specific shoes with **sticky rubber** for wet/dirty terrain.  
- **Swimsuit bottom**: If aid stations have cold water immersion (common in trail races).  

---

### **Why This Works**
- **Layer system**: Lets you adapt to cooling temps and wind.  
- **Night priorities**: Insulation + visibility + warmth dominate the final stretch.  
- **Aid stations**: Use them to swap layers/gear, but bring backups.
