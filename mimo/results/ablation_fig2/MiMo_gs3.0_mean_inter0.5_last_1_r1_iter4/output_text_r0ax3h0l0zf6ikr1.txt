Next, the mental prep, about 30 minutes before. Turn that 90s h up instrumental on low in the background. Then, do some box breathing—uhm, that’s where you inhale for four seconds, hold for four, exhale for four, and hold again for four. Repeat that a few times. Next, visualize the broadcast going perfectly in your mind, see yourself speaking clearly and calmly. And finally, review the game plan and key stats in your head so you're prepared but not overthinking.

For the outfit, since it's sunny, go for light colors to stay cool. Something breathable like linen or cotton.
