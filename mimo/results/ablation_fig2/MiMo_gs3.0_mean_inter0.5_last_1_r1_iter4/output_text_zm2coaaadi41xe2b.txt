Dallas has some amazing Asian noodle spots. For ramen, try Tonkotsu Ramen or Ichiran—they’re classics. If you want pho, Pho 79 is fantastic. For general Asian noodles, Noodle House or House of Noodles are great.

For protein bars, it depends on what you need. If you want something high in protein and low in sugar, Quest or Optimum Nutrition are good. If you want taste and texture, Clif Bars or RX Bars are solid. And if you want something with a bit more protein punch, MyProtein or Dymatize have some really good options.
