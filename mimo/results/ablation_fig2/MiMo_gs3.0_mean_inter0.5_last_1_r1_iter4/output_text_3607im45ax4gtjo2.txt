Dealing with a difficult person can be incredibly draining, especially when it affects your family's daily life. Here are some practical strategies to help manage the stress and protect your well-being:

### 1. **Set Clear Boundaries**
   - **Limit Exposure**: Avoid direct interactions if possible. If you must engage (e.g., neighborly greetings), keep responses brief and neutral.
   - **Document Incidents**: Keep a record of harassment (dates, times, details) in case CPS/police involvement needs to be escalated later.

### 2. **Focus on What You Control**
   - **Protect Your Space**: Supervise kids strictly near home, ride bikes in private property, etc. Adjust routines to minimize triggers.
   - **Vent Strategically**: Share your feelings with trusted friends, family, or an online community to avoid bottling up stress.

### 3. **Shift Your Perspective**
   - **Separate Actions from Intent**: Her calls (police/CPS) stem from her own issues, not your family’s behavior. Try not to internalize it.
   - **Practice Empathy (Cautiously)**: While not excusing her actions, consider if she’s dealing with untreated stress or mental health challenges. This may help you stay calm.

### 4. **Emotional Self-Care**
   - **Use Music as a Tool**: Continue listening to uplifting artists like Forrest Frank to counter negativity. Create playlists for "stress relief" or "focus."
   - **Mindfulness/Meditation**: Apps like Headspace or Calm can help ground you during frustrating moments.
   - **Physical Activity**: Exercise releases endorphins—walks, yoga, or dancing to music can reset your mood.

### 5. **Community Support**
   - **Connect with Other Parents**: If others share your experience, band together to request a community meeting with authorities or a mediator.
   - **Leverage School Resources**: Since Red Ribbon Week/CPS is involved, communicate with the school counselor—they may offer support or resources.

### 6. **Professional Support**
   - **Therapy/Counseling**: A therapist can provide tools for stress management and resilience. Many offer sliding-scale fees.
   - **CPS Coordination**: If involved, work with them to ensure proper follow-up. Document interactions with them too.

### 7. **Focus on the Positive**
   - **Celebrate Small Wins**: Kids’ dress-up days, Halloween prep, and enjoying music are anchors of joy. Lean into these moments.
   - **Halloween Prep Ritual**: Make costume planning/family trick-or-treating a bonding activity—turn it into a fun tradition!

### Remember:
