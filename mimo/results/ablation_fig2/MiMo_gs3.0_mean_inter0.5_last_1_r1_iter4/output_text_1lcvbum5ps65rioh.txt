### U-Turn Penalties in British Columbia  
If you make an illegal U-turn in BC, you can expect:  
- **Fine**: $116 (plus 3 demerit points on your license).  
- **Common Violations**: Turning at red lights/signals, in blind spots, near intersections/hills, or without proper visibility (150m in both directions).  
- **Enforcement**: Rare for minor infractions, but strict for dangerous maneuvers (e.g., blocking traffic).  
**Tip**: Use roundabouts or designated U-turn lanes. If unsure, wait for a safe gap!

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
Here’s what you’ll need for **authentic Roman-style Carbonara** (no cream!):  
#### **Pasta & Base**  
- 400g (14 oz) spaghetti or bucatini  
- 150g (5 oz) guanciale (cured pork cheek), diced (pancetta if unavailable)  
- 3 large eggs  
- ½ cup grated Pecorino Romano cheese  
- ½ cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano  
- Freshly ground black pepper  

#### **Seasonings**  
- Salt (for pasta water)  
- Optional: 1 tsp garlic powder (subtle flavor boost)  

#### **Cooking Steps**  
1. **Salt water**: Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
2. **Render guanciale**: Fry in a skillet until crispy. Remove from heat.  
3. **Mix**: Whisk eggs, cheeses, and pepper in a bowl. Toss hot pasta with guanciale.  
4. **Temper eggs**: Off-heat, mix in egg mixture slowly, adding pasta water to emulsify.  
5. **Serve**: Immediately with extra cheese and pepper.  

**Key Tip**: Don’t add hot pasta directly to eggs—this causes scrambling!
