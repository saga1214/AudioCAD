For candy tomorrow, grab classics like Reese’s Cups, Kit Kats, Skittles, and gummies. Those are always safe bets. You could also stock up on Halloween-themed candy bags or buy individual-sized packs to make things easier. Just double-check allergens again—if artificial dyes are a concern, look for candies labeled dye-free or opt for natural-looking ones like plain chocolate or dried fruit snacks. Don't forget to put out some pumpkins or small bowls to make the candy display spooky!
