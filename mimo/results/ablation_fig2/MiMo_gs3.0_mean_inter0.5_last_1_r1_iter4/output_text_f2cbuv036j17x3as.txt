Okay, diesel camper vans age, and maintenance before a long trip is crucial. Brakes are a big one. Some simple things you can look for are if the pads are worn down—sometimes you can see thinness through the dust guards. If you hear a grinding noise when you brake, that’s a sign they need replacing. Also, check the fluid levels in the brake reservoir; diesel systems often have a cooler that needs fluid. For spark plugs, diesel engines use high-torque coils-on-plug setups, so they’re a bit different than gasoline. You’ll need the right wrench and a gasket to avoid leaks. It’s a job you can do yourself if you’re handy, but it’s not impossible. Another thing to check is the diesel filter—that’s a big one. If it’s clogged, your engine could be working harder and harder to push it through. So, just make sure you’ve got a fresh one. And don’t forget to check the van’s tires and fluids too.
