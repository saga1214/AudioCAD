Here are the classic recipes formatted for your bartenders at precise measurements:

### Old Fashioned
- **Ingredients**:  
  • 2 oz bourbon rye whiskey  
  • 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  • 3 dashes Angostura bitters *(optional)*  
  • Orange twist  
- **Method**:  
  1. Muddle sugar cube (or add simple syrup) in shaker.  
  2. Add bitters, whiskey, ice.  
  3. **Dry shake** (shaken without ice) for 10 sec to emulsify.  
  4. **Shake with ice** 10 sec.  
  5. Strain into **rocks glass over fresh ice**.  
  6. Express orange twist over drink; discard.  
- **Egg white option**: Add 0.75 oz egg white to Step 1. Dry shake first, then shake with ice.

### Paper Plane
- **Ingredients**:  
  • 2 oz bourbon *(e.g., Buffalo Trace)*  
  • ¾ oz Aperol  
  • ½ oz Nonino Amaro  
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients + ice vigorously.  
  2. Strain into **coupe glass** packed with fresh ice.  
  3. Garnish with cherry.

### Whiskey Sour
- **Ingredients**:  
  • 2 oz bourbon rye whiskey  
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  • ½ oz simple syrup  
  • *(Optional)* ¾ oz egg white  
- **Method**:  
  1. Combine all ingredients in shaker.  
  2. **Dry shake** 10 sec.  
  3. **Shake with ice** 10 sec.  
  4. Strain into **rocks glass over fresh ice**.  
  5. Garnish with cherry or orange slice.

### French 75 (Gin Cocktail)
- **Ingredients**:  
  • 1.5 oz gin *(e.g., Hendrick's)*  
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice  
  • ¾ oz simple syrup  
  • 3 oz chilled champagne/champagne flute  
- **Method**:  
  1. Shake gin, L J, and syrup with ice.  
  2. Strain into flute.  
  3. Top with champagne.  
  4. Garnish with lemon twist.

### Key Tips for Event:
- **Kentucky twist**: Use high-quality bourbon (eagle rare, buffalo trace).  
- **Art deco touch**: Serve Paper Planes/Wiskey Sours in vintage coupe glasses; Paper Planes in vintage airplane-themed glasses if found.  
Egg white is traditional but optional—label drinks clearly. Enjoy your reception! ✈️🍸
