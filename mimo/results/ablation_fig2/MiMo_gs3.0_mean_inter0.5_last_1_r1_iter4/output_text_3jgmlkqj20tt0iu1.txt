Initally, the recommended approach is often to ask to schedule a separate meeting. You could say something like, 'Hi, do you have some time for a quick meeting to discuss my career development and progress?' This lets you control the agenda and the timing.

If that feels too direct, you could bring it up indirectly during your boss's general annual performance review. You could ask a question like, 'Based on my contributions since I've started, do you see any potential for me to take on additional responsibilities in the near future?' This keeps the conversation constructive and gives you valuable feedback on how you're perceived.
