### 🔧 Glue Gun Troubleshooting:
1. **Battery Check**: If it's cordless, ensure the battery is fully charged (or try a different one).
2. **Wattage Test**: Plug it into a different outlet. Cheap power strips can cause issues.
3. **Room Temp**: Cold rooms slow glue flow. Warm the room or run a heater briefly.
4. Glob the glue**: Hold the nozzle over scrap paper and squeeze *slowly* to see if glue oozes out. If not, it’s likely dead.
5. **Nozzle Care**: Dust/clogs can block flow. Soak the nozzle in hot water for 5 mins, then gently clean with a toothpick.
6. **Manual Alternative**: If it fails, try using a **hot glue stick** (if compatible) or switch to temporary decorations like Velcro dots or mini clips.
