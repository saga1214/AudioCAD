For a 15-minute break as your last one, here are some gentle, restorative options that won't disrupt your focus when you return to work:

1.  **Mindful Breathing (5-10 mins):** Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and focus solely on your breath. Inhale deeply through your nose, hold for a few seconds, exhale slowly through your mouth. Notice the sensation of air moving in and out. When your mind wanders (it will!), gently bring it back to your breath. This calms the nervous system without needing to "think" deeply.
2.  **Light Stretching (5-10 mins):** Stand at your desk or walk around. Do gentle neck rolls, shoulder shrugs, wrist circles, ankle circles, and a few slow, easy stretches for your arms and legs. Focus on releasing any tension you've accumulated.
3.  **Short Walk (10-12 mins):** If possible, step away from your desk and take a slow, mindful walk around the room, down a hallway, or even outside if the weather permits. Notice the sights and sounds around you without judgment. This provides movement and a change of scenery.
4.  **Hydrate & Snack (2-3 mins):** Drink a full glass of water. Have a small, healthy snack like a piece of fruit, a handful of nuts, or some yogurt. Avoid large meals or heavy foods.

**Why these work well:**

*   **Gentle Transition:** They provide a restorative break without the jarring effect of deep meditation.
*   **Replenish Energy:** Breathing and stretching help calm the nervous system and release physical tension. A walk provides light movement and oxygen.
*   **Hydration & Nutrition:** Replenishing fluids and a small snack sustain energy levels.
*   **Mindfulness:** Bringing awareness to your breath or surroundings helps you arrive back at your desk more present and focused.

*   **Heavy Coffee:** While tempting, it might make you feel more jittery than refreshed later.

If you *do* nap, ensure it's in a dark, quiet space and aim for a *very* short nap (under 10 minutes) if possible.
