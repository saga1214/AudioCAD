Okay, so you want to surprise your daughter with a new gaming console for you two to play together. That's a great idea.

Here are five of the most popular gaming consoles right now. 
First is the PlayStation 5, which is known for its great graphics and performance. 
Second is the Xbox Series X or S, which is great for playing games with friends online. 
Third, there's the Nintendo Switch, which is really unique because you can play it on a TV or just on the go. 
Fourth is the Steam Deck, which is a handheld PC that plays thousands of games, including PC games. 
And fifth is the Nintendo Wii U, which is a bit older but still has some classic games that you might enjoy.

So, for a good two-player experience on a TV, the PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X, or Nintendo Switch would be your best bet. The Switch is probably the most versatile if you want to play together on the couch or even take it somewhere. The Steam Deck is also a good option, but it's more like a portable PC. The Wii U is a bit outdated, but it's still a fun system.
