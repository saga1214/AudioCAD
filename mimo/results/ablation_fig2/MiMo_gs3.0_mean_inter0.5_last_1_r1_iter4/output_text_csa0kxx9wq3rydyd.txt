Okay, so let's see. For smoking, you'll need a smoker. For roasting, an oven is a must. For salads, you'll need a salad greens package or pre-shredded Brussels sprouts, plus some dressing, and nuts for the Brussels sprout one. For side dishes, you'll need fresh Brussels sprouts, root vegetables like carrots and parsnips, green beans, stuffing ingredients, mashed potatoes and prime rib roasts, macaroni and cheese ingredients, pecans, slow cooker ingredients for the apple crisp, and an Instant Pot for the cranberry sauce. For desserts, you'll need ingredients for the pumpkin cheesecake, chocolate and peanut butter for the lasagna, fruit for the tart, cookies for the crisp, and for the icebox cake, cream cheese, cookies, and fruit. So, the appliances you'll need are a smoker, an oven, a stovetop, a toaster oven, and an Instant Pot. The cooking methods are smoking, roasting, boiling or steaming for the wild rice salad, roasting again for Brussels sprouts and root vegetables, baking for stuffing and mac and cheese, slow cooking for the apple crisp, and pressure cooking for the cranberry sauce. Oh, and the icebox cake just needs to be chilled. I hope this helps you get Thanksgiving sorted!
