For stir-fry noodles, the wooden spoon incident is unlikely to even be an issue. The stir-fry will be on a much higher heat than boiling pasta.

Here’s a foolproof plan. Buy instant ramen. Just boil the noodles, drain them, and set them aside. Then, stir-fry some frozen shrimp or ground pork. Finally, just combine everything with the instant broth packet. It takes about 10 minutes and is almost impossible to mess up. Good luck
