Okay, looking at ninety thousand under as your ceiling and your mid-life crisis need for youth and power, American sports is definitely the right path. Here are five contenders focusing on recent Corvettes and Mustang Shrebys, plus a couple other competitive American machines.

First up, the star player: a Chevrolet Corvette, either a C7 or a C8. This is your absolute sweet spot. The C7 Stingray with the 450-horsepower V8 gets you there brand new, loaded with CarPlay and modern tech, well under 90k. The C8 Z06, with its insane 632-horsepower V8, is a bit pricier used within five years, but it delivers supercar competition and feels young. Color choice here is smart for police attention – stick neutral like black, silver, or blue.

Second, the legend: a Ford Mustang Shelby GT350. This is a true competitive beast. The GT350 has a flat-plane crank V8 that revs higher and sounds wilder than most. It competes *directly* with supercars on the track. A used one fits your five-year rule and budget. Neutral tones blend best here, too.

Third, if you find a great deal on a C8 Z06 Corvette, that's your ultimate competition car. That V8 is built to rival the very best. It’s under 90k used, has all your feature requests, and black or grey will minimize unwanted stops.

Fourth, a fun wildcard: the Dodge Challenger or Charger Scat Pack. If you're craving raw American V8 muscle, not pure sports handling, this is it. It has a supercar-like V8 roar and can compete on power alone. You'll find loaded Scat Packs under 90k new, and colors like Go Mango or Scuderia Orange definitely draw more eyes.

And fifth, the V10 king: an Audi R8 V10 Plus, used. This is where you get into that Toyota-like legendary reliability in a supercar. That V10 is famous for its toughness and smooth power. Competes fiercely with Ferraris and Lambos. Used models are common under 90k, and a neutral color is your best bet.

So, to recap: the new C7 Corvette is your most reliable, feature-packed, and powerful American option under 90k. The used Shelby is the pure competitive beast. The R8 V10 is for when you want legendary reliability with supercar performance. Definitely go neutral on the paint unless you want that midlife crisis flash!
