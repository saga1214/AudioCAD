Absolutely! Here's a **grab-and-go friendly shopping list** designed for minimal mess and maximum convenience for your 10 dads, 10 kids, coolers, and theme park adventures. It balances variety, nutrition, indulgence, and portability:

**Drinks (Focus on Grab-and-Go Hydration & Indulgence):**
*   **For Everyone:** 30-40 Large Water Bottles (Reusable or disposable, fill with ice later)
*   **Adults:** 15-20 Sugar-Free Sodas (e.g., Sprite Zero, Coke Zero, Dr. Pepper Zero) *Plus 10-15 Bottles of Sparkling Water (plain or flavored)*
*   **Kids:** 15-20 Sugar-Free Sodas (or regular sugar-free if preferred) *Plus 10 Bottles of Sparkling Water or Low-Sugar Juice Boxes*
*   **Essential:** Ice Packs (LOTS of them!) for coolers

**Snacks (Low-Mess, Kid-Friendly Favorites):**
*   **Cheese:** 30 Cheese Sticks (Pre-cut & divided packs)
*   **Crunchy Veggies:** 10 lbs Grapes (divided into individual bags), Baby Carrots (divided into bags)
*   **Crackers & Seeds:** 5-6 Boxes Individual Pretzel Packs or Trail Mix Cups (look for kid-friendly mixes or make your own in small bags)
 Dried Fruit: 2-3 Bags (e.g., raisins, cranberries, apricots - no added sugar if possible)
*   **Other:** Individual Packs of String Cheese (if preferred over sticks)

**Main Meals (Calzones Ready-to-Eat!):**
*   **Calzones (Real & Easy!):** **This is your star!** Purchase **10 Pre-Made Calzones** from the freezer section. Look for:
    *   **Kid Portions:** Brands like Red Baron, Banza, or Trader Joe's often have smaller "Kid Portions."
    *   **Adult Portions:** Standard sizes.
    *   **Flavors:** Pepperoni & Cheese, Veggie, Meat Lover's, etc. (Get a variety of 2-3 flavors for 10 people).
    *   **Packaging:** They come in foil pans - **wrap each pan individually in foil** before packing into coolers to prevent leaks and make grabbing easy. Pack them *last* in coolers with ice packs.
*   **Backup Option (If Calzones Fall Through):**
    *   **Individual Wraps or Sandwiches:** 10 Pre-Made Chicken Salad Wraps, Turkey & Avocado Wraps, or Peanut Butter & Banana Sandwiches (cut into halves or quarters). Pack in individual containers.

**Dessert (Celebratory & Portable):**
*   **Cupcakes or Brownie Cups:** **Best Choice!** Purchase 10 Individual Cupcakes (e.g., Red Velvet, Chocolate, Vanilla) or Brownie Cups. They come in individual boxes or are easy to cut and pack.
*   **OR Pie (If You Choose):**
    *   **Mini Pies:** Purchase 10 Individual Mini Pies (Key Lime, Apple, Cherry, etc.) - much cleaner than a whole pie.
    *   **OR Pre-Sliced Pie:** Get a large pie (like Apple or Pumpkin) and **pre-slice it into 10 individual pieces** using a sharp knife. Pack slices in airtight containers or individual pie boxes. *Note: This requires more knife work and potential crumbs.*
*   **Dessert Garnish (Optional Fun):** Small Individual Boxes of Mini Chocolate Chips or Sprinkles for a DIY cupcake bar at the cooler!

**Cooler Organization Tips:**
1.  **Layer Wisely:** Put drinks and ice packs at the bottom. Place sturdy items (sandwiches, calzone pans) on top. Put lighter items (snacks, fruit) on top.
2.  **Separate Coolers (Optional but Helpful):** One cooler for drinks/ice, one for food/snacks. Makes grabbing much faster.
3.  **Label Coolers:** "DRINKS" and "FOOD/SNACKS."
4.  **Reheat Calzones?** If your cooler has a small freezer compartment, you *might* be able to partially thaw them there *before* heading to the park. Otherwise, eat them cold or plan to eat them later. Most frozen calzones taste great cold too!

**Estimated Cost Range:** $150-$250+ (Prices vary by location and brand. Buying frozen calzones and bulk items helps keep it reasonable).

**Final Notes:**
*   **Communicate:** Quickly ask the friend group if anyone has allergies or dietary restrictions (nuts, dairy, etc.).
*   **Reheating:** If your bachelor has a favorite brand, ask! Red Baron's "Kid Portions" are often a great, affordable frozen option.
*   **Enjoy!** This setup gives everyone choices, minimizes mess, and lets you all focus on making amazing memories for your best friend! Have an incredible day at the theme park!
