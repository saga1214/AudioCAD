That's a great approach—being very specific and respectful. Here are a few pointers to help you structure your email.

First, start with your focus on improvement. You could write: "Hi Professor [Last Name], I scored a 64 on the recent essay-style exam and wanted to do better on the next test. Could you share what I could have improved on for this one?"

Second, compliment his work to set a positive tone. For example: "I really appreciated how clear your lectures were."

Third, to be specific without challenging him, you could say: "Subjective exams can be tricky, and I'm not sure how content alone led to this score. Could you walk me through your grading criteria?"

This approach keeps the conversation about your growth, which gives you the best chance of having a productive talk.
