Here are safe and practical tips for baby-led weaning:

### Key Guidelines:
1. **Start at 6+ months**: Wait until your baby can sit independently (around 6–7 months).
2. **Begin with smooth foods**: Introduce single-ingredient purees (e.g., avocado, banana, pumpkin).
3. **Progress to soft solids**: After 2–3 days of purees, offer finger-sized pieces of soft foods (e.g., steamed carrots, cooked chicken, peeled cucumber).

### Safety First:
- **Avoid choking hazards**: Never give whole grapes, nuts, hard candies, or raw vegetables.
- **Supervise closely**: Never leave baby unattended while eating.
- **Offer small portions**: Start with 1–2 teaspoons of food and increase gradually.

### What to Expect:
- **Messy faces**: Normal! Have wet wipes and a bib ready.
- **Vary textures**: Include crunchy (steamed veggies), soft (mashed beans), and chewy (well-cooked pasta) foods.
- **No pressure**: If baby spits out food, offer a new variety. Don’t force eating.

### Foods to Introduce First:
| **Stage** | **Food Examples** |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Stage 1** | Pureed fruits/veggies (avocado, sweet potato, carrot) |
| **Stage 2** | Soft-cooked proteins (chicken, fish), grains (mashed rice, pasta) |
| **Stage 3** | Slightly textured foods (steamed broccoli, shredded cheese) |

### Important Notes:
- **Allergen introduction**: Introduce potential allergens (eggs, peanut butter, dairy) one at a time and watch for reactions.
- **Hydration**: Offer breast milk or formula between meals.
- **Consult a pediatrician**: Especially if your baby has reflux, allergies, or delayed milestones.
