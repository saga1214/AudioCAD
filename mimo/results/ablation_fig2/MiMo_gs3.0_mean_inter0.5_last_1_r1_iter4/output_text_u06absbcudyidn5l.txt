Okay, let's break this down. Chronic skin redness has a lot of potential causes. Rosacea is a big one, sun damage over many years can definitely lead to it staying red, genetics can play a part, contact dermatitis from things like soaps, or even conditions like lupus.

Now, about vasculitis. That's when the blood vessels get inflamed. It can affect vessels of any size—large, medium, or small. Common symptoms from it include fever, fatigue, and rashes. It can affect hair and nails.

The diagnosis usually involves a physical exam, blood tests, and sometimes a biopsy of the skin. Symptoms can show up at any age.

So, to recap: chronic redness could be from rosacea, sun damage, or other conditions. Vasculitis is another possibility, where inflamed blood vessels cause symptoms like rashes and can affect hair and nails. A dermatologist can help figure out the specific cause.
