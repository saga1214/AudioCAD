Post-birthday warm getaway itinerary planned for SUV family! Considering warm weather, warm pools, accommodating Celiac needs, and SUV transport...

Idea: Palm Springs Oasis Escape
- Why it fits: Consistently warm winter days (70s-80s), famous mid-century modern architecture, pools galore, plenty of Celiac-friendly upscale dining.
- Travel: Rent an SUV at SFO, drive ~4 hours. Easy one-way rental return.
- Stay: The Biltmore Hotel (iconic mid-century, warm pools, excellent Celiac service) or Hotel Vallesito (luxury modern resort with multiple pools).
- Do: Swimming in resort pools daily, exploring modern architecture, shopping in the Design Center, relaxing at Palm Springs Aerial Tramway (warm days only!), enjoying evening entertainment.
- Eat: Elmer's Restaurant (luxe Celiac menu), Cafe 29 (upscale Mediterranean), or many dedicated gluten-free restaurants in the Design Center. Trish's Mini Donuts available too!

Idea: Carmel-by-the-Sea & Big Sur Coastal Charm
- Why it fits: Beautiful scenery, some warm spots along coast, Carmel has inviting beaches, several Celiac-friendly options, larger SUV needed for Big Sur.
- Travel: Drive from Northern CA, ~6 hours. Consider splitting into two nights.
- Stay: Carmel River Inn (spacious, warm pool, Celiac-friendly) or Asilomar Conference Grounds (historic, pool, some Celiac options).
- Do: Swimming at Carmel Beach (check water temp), exploring Carmel's shops, driving Big Sur (McWay Falls!), visiting Hearst Castle (book ahead), stopping at Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park.
- Eat: Carmel Valley Restaurant (outdoor dining, Celiac menu), Nepenthe (stunning Big Sur views, call ahead for GF), or many options in Carmel Valley.

Both offer distinct experiences! Palm Springs is pure warmth and pools; Carmel/Big Sur is scenic coastal adventure with some warm opportunities. Enjoy planning your Northern Cal escape!
