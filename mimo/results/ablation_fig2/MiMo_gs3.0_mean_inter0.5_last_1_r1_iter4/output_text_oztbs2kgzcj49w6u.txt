Yes, absolutely. The most direct way to experience it is to visit the Lockport & Niagara Falls Museum of History & Science. They have an excellent exhibit on the Erie Canal, and you can see a full-scale model of the "Flight of Five" locks. It's a very clear and impressive display of the engineering.

If you're more of a hands-on learner, you could also visit the Lockport Canal Museum, which is located right in the old canal locks themselves. It offers a more immersive experience where you can walk the towpath and see the original machinery.

And for a really unique perspective, you could even book a boat tour that goes right through the locks, which is a thrilling way to understand how the whole system works.
