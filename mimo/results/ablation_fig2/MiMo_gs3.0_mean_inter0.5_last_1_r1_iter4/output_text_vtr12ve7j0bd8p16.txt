The Rocky Mountains themselves offer countless adventures. You could go for a scenic drive, for instance, on the world-famous Icefields Parkway, which leads to the stunning Columbia Icefield. Another great option is the Banff Gondola in Banff National Park, where you can take a ride up Sulphur Mountain for breathtaking views. If she enjoys being active, you could look into activities like zip-lining or white-water rafting.
