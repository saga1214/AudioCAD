Sure, I can give you three more shrimp dish ideas.

You could make Spicy Shrimp and Grits again. For that, you'll need the shrimp, quick-cooking grits, Cajun seasoning, butter, and some sliced green onions.

Another option is a Spicy Shrimp Scampi Bake. You'd use shrimp, garlic, butter, white wine, lemon juice, parsley, and some Parmesan cheese. You'd bake it all in a dish.
