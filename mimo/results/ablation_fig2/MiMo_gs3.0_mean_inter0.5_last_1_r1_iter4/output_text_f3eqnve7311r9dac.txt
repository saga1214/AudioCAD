Second, *Detective Comics* number thirty-five, Batman's first full appearance.

Fifth, *The Flash* number one from 1961, where The Flash first appears.
Sixth, *Green Lantern* number one from 1966, where Hal Jordan first appears.
Seventh, *The Incredible Hulk* number one from 1962, featuring the first full appearance of the Hulk.
Eighth, *Fantastic Four* number one from 1965, which introduced the Fantastic Four.
Ninth, *Captain Marvel* number one from 1963, featuring the first appearance of Shazam.
And tenth, *Thor* number one from 1962, where Thor first appeared.
