For your brooder plate, you'll want a material that is rodent-safe. A thick layer of paper towels or newsprint is the best choice. It's clean, dry, and won't attract pests. Just replace it every day. You could also use pine pellets or orchard grass pellets as a dry alternative, but make sure to scrape them out frequently so they don't compost down too fast. Avoid anything wet like peat moss, and definitely no straw or shavings.
