### **Your Presentation Structure: "The Pillars of a Cybersecurity Program"**

   - **Why it’s #1**: Employees are human firewalls. Technology fails without awareness.  
   - **How to highlight it**: Use relatable examples (e.g., "If an attacker sends a phishing email, your trained team is the first to spot it").

2. **The Enablers: Prevention, Detection, and Response**  
   - **Prevention**: Technology (firewalls, MFA, patching) + policies.  
   - **Detection**: Monitoring, alerts, and user reporting (echoing your coworker’s point!).  
   - **Response**: Incident response plans, IR teams, and recovery protocols.  
   - **Highlight the chain**: *Culture enables prevention → prevention enables detection → detection enables response.*

3. **The Engine: Testing and Improvement**  
   - **Why it’s critical**: Regular penetration testing, audits, and tabletop exercises.  
   - **Link to culture**: "Testing proves your team’s skills and plans work under pressure."

4. **The Reality: Outsourcing and Resources**  
   - **When it’s smart**: Partnering with MSSPs for 24/7 monitoring when internal expertise is limited.  
   - **Key reminder**: Even outsourced security requires *your company’s policies and culture* to succeed.

5. **The Big Picture: Governance and Compliance**  
   - Policies, risk management, and regulatory alignment (e.g., NIST, ISO 27001).  
   - **Tie it back to culture**: "Governance ensures everyone understands *why* security matters."
