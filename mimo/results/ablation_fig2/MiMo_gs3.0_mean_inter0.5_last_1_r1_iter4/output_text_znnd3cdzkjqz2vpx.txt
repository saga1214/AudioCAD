You've got all the essentials, but a few more things will make the job go much smoother. A stud finder with an electric beep is a huge help for quickly locating studs. Definitely grab some shims, which are just thin pieces of wood, to get cabinets perfectly level. You'll also need longer screws than you might think, probably around two-and-a-half inches. And a drill is going to be way faster and easier than using a hammer and nails. Oh, and a flashlight, especially for working in a dark kitchen. You're all set!
