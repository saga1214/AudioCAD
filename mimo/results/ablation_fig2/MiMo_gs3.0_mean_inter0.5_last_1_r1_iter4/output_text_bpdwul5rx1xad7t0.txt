Of course! With a two-bedroom apartment, a new baby, and a new puppy on the way, road trips will be a fun way to explore. Here are five fantastic options for you in the BC interior.

First, there's Napa Valley, California, which is about a four and a half hour drive. It's famous for its wineries, so you can enjoy a tasting. You can also find a beautiful spa resort for a relaxing stay.

Second, a bit further, is the Okanagan Lake area. It's a short seven-hour drive. You can visit wine lodges, enjoy scenic boat tours, and even try some hot springs.

Third, for a more rugged adventure, you could go to the Fraser Canyon. It's about a six and a half hour drive. You can explore historic towns like Kamloops and try some off-road driving.

Fourth, for a classic Vancouver road trip, there's the Sea to Sky Highway. It's about a three-hour drive and takes you from the ocean at Whistler to the mountains. You can ride the gondola and enjoy a luxury resort.

Finally, for a more remote adventure, there's Yoho National Park. It's about a five and a half hour drive. You can hike through beautiful valleys and visit a historic lodge.

All of these are great options with plenty of activities for a family!
