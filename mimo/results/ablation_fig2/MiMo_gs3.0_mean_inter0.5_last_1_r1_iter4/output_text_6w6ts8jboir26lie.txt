Okay, you're finally getting some resolution and want to cook to relax. That's a great idea.

Here are five quick and comforting dinner ideas.

First, you could do a pasta with a twist. Grab a box of penne and some jarred marinara sauce. You can throw in some frozen spinach and a can of tuna for extra protein.

Second, how about a stir-fry. Just get some pre-cut stir-fry veggies, chicken breast, and a bottle of your favorite sauce. You can cook it all up super fast.

Third, a hearty omelet. You can whisk up some eggs with chopped ham, cheese, and maybe some sautéed bell peppers.

Fourth, you could make a baked potato bar. Bake a potato, then top it with some canned chili, a can of beans, cheese, and sour cream.

And fifth, a classic comfort food. You could make some macaroni and cheese... just get a box of that and add some frozen broccoli florets to make it a little more nutritious.
