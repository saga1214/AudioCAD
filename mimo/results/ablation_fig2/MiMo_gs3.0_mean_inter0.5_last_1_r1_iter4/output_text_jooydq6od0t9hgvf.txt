### **Argentine Fernet Recipe**  
*(Inspired by Fernet-Branca, with a local twist)*  
**Ingredients**:  
- 3 oz Fernet-Branca (or Argentine Fernet variant)  
- 1.5 oz Pisco (Argentine white wine spirit)  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- 0.5 oz honey syrup (equal honey/water)  
- 4 oz chilled soda water (soda-free!)  
- *Garnish*: Lime wheel + mint sprig  

**Method**:  
Shake Fernet, Pisco, lime juice, and honey syrup with ice. Strain into a glass over ice. Top with soda water.
