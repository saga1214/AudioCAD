Based on your biology class focus and the science, **ultra-processed foods** are arguably the one *category* of foods that would be **the worst for your health if eaten in excess**. Here's why:

### Key Reasons Ultra-Processed Foods Are Harmful in Excess:
1. **Nutrient Deficiency & Calorie Density**:  
   They are stripped of fiber, vitamins, and minerals while packed with unhealthy fats, added sugars, and refined carbs. This leads to poor satiety (you eat more without feeling full) and nutrient imbalances.

2. **Metabolic Harm**:  
   Excess sugar and unhealthy fats drive **obesity, type 2 diabetes, and fatty liver disease**. Processed meats (like hot dogs or deli meats) contain preservatives linked to **cancer risk**.

3. **Inflammation & Disease Risk**:  
   Regular consumption correlates with **heart disease, hypertension, and chronic inflammation**, which underlie many age-related illnesses.

4. **Addictive Properties**:  
   They’re engineered with salt, sugar, and fat to trigger dopamine responses, making overconsumption likely and hard to stop.

### Examples of Ultra-Processed Foods:
- Sugary drinks (sodas, energy drinks)  
- Packaged snacks (chips, cookies, candy)  
- Frozen meals & fast food  
- Processed meats (sausages, bacon)  
- Sweetened breakfast cereals  
- Sugary yogurts & flavored juices  

### Why Not Other Contenders?
While foods like **artificial trans fats** (now banned in many countries) or **excess sugar** are extremely harmful, they often *form part of* ultra-processed foods. The category as a whole encapsulates the broadest range of health risks from overconsumption.

### Bottom Line:  
For your class, emphasize **balance and moderation**. Whole, minimally processed foods (fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins) provide nutrients that protect health, while ultra-processed foods systematically undermine it.
