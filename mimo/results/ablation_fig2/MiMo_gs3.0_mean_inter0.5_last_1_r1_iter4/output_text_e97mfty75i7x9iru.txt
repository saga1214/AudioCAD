Take it easy. Here are the flu tips, broken down into two parts.

First, for general advice: Rest as much as you can. Drink plenty of fluids. And eat what sounds good to you to keep your strength up.

Second, for relieving specific symptoms: Over-the-counter medicines can help with the fever and aches. A spoonful of honey, or in warm tea, can soothe a cough. You can also gargle with warm salt water. And propping your head up with an extra pillow at night can help with coughing while you sleep.

And for that brain fog... just be patient with yourself. Rest and hydration are key. Some people find that caffeine, in moderation, can help clear their head. Or just gentle movement when you start to feel better.
