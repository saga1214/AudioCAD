### 🎨 Color-Coding & Memory vs. Mental Load
*   **Both!** Color-coding *primarily* aids **memory recall** (like visual tags) but also subtly **reduces mental load**. Your brain recognizes color associations faster than text or icons alone, freeing up cognitive resources for the actual task.  
*   **Keep it subtle:** Use 2-4 distinct, consistent colors. Avoid overly bright or clashing palettes. Label colors clearly (e.g., "Red = Active Projects," "Blue = Reference").

### 🎧 Sound-Switching: Timing & Preference
*   **Primarily personal preference:** There's no universal "optimal" timing. Experiment!  
    *   **Switching every 45-60 min?** Can prevent monotony and gently reset focus.  
    *   **Sticking to one vibe?** Often better for deep, sustained concentration.  
*   **Test it:** Try both approaches for a day each. Notice what helps you maintain flow vs. what feels refreshing.

### 📁 Physical Documents: Environment vs. Chunking
*   **Both matter, but chunking is key:**  
    *   **Environment:** A clean, organized workspace *does* create a mental "clear start" feeling.  
    *   **Chunking:** Breaking the task into small, defined sections (e.g., "Sort Receipts (Jan-Mar)," "File Correspondence") is what drives *progress*. Progress is what fuels motivation.  
*   **Recommendation:** Schedule a dedicated "filing block" on a quiet day. Define small chunks *before* starting. Clean the space first if needed—it's a setup ritual.

### Mediterranean Dinner Ideas (Quick & Easy)
Here are 3-4 delicious options that feel less crant:

1.  **Shrimp & Orzo Salad:** Toss cooked orzo with chilled shrimp, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, Kalamata olives, red onion, feta, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Serve with a simple tarragon-dill dressing.  
2.  **Sheet Pan Chicken & Roasted Vegetables:** Toss chicken thighs (skin-on for flavor) with bell peppers, zucchini, red onion, and lemon zest. Roast at 400°F (200°C) until chicken is cooked and veggies caramelized. Drizzle with olive oil and oregano.  
3.  **White Bean & Rosemary Pasta:** Sauté garlic and rosemary in olive oil. Add cooked pasta, canned white beans (cannellini), chopped parsley, and a splash of pasta water. Toss with grated Parmesan.  
4.  **Simple Grilled Fish (Bacalhau) & Roasted Potatoes:** Marinate cod or other white fish in olive oil, lemon, garlic, and herbs. Grill or bake. Serve with roasted baby potatoes (toss with olive oil, salt, pepper, rosemary) and a side of steamed green beans with lemon.

**Key tip for home office snacks/dinner:** Stock simple, fresh ingredients! Pre-chopped veggies, canned beans, quality olive oil, lemon, and herbs make assembling meals/snacks feel effortless and Mediterranean-inspired without fortune-spending cranness. 🥗
