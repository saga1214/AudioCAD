Here's a noise-aware guide to installing your items:

### 1. **TV Mount Installation** (Lowest Noise Impact)
- **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, level, drill (if no studs), anchor kit (if drilling into drywall)
- **Steps:**
  1. **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall anchors. If no studs, rent a drill and use heavy-duty drywall anchors (prevents noise).
  2. **Measure Twice:** Ensure the mount’s weight capacity matches your TV.
  3. **Drill Carefully:** If drilling into drywall, use a low-speed drill (minimize noise). Wear ear protection.
  4. **Secure Mount:** Attach the mount to the wall, then mount the TV.
- **Pro Tip:** Use a step stool for leverage—less straining on legs!

### 2. **Hanging Planters Installation** (Minimal Noise)
- **Tools Needed:** Wire hangers, screws, hooks, or adhesive strips (3M Command™ hooks)
- **Steps:**
  1. **Choose Attachment:**  
     - *Screws:* Use thin wall anchors if no studs.  
     - *Adhesive:* Command™ hooks are silent and damage-free.  
  2. **Hang Planter:**  
     - For hangers: Bend hanger into a loop, thread through pot’s drainage hole, and hang from ceiling hook.  
     - For hooks: Attach directly to hooks or screws.
- **Pro Tip:** Use lightweight pots to avoid wobbling. Group small plants to save space!

### 3. **Floating Shelves Installation** (Moderate Noise)
- **Tools Needed:** Measuring tape, pencil, level, drill, anchors (if no studs)
- **Steps:**
  1. **Mark Shelf Location:**  
     - Measure from wall to desired height.  
     - Use a level to ensure even spacing (e.g., 30" apart for two shelves).  
  2. **Install Anchors:**  
     - If no studs, drill pilot holes and insert anchors (quietest method).  
  3. **Attach Shelf:**  
     - Use shelf brackets or L-brackets screwed into anchors.  
     - Test stability before adding decor.
- **Pro Tip:** Anchor shelves to studs if possible (safest method). Use L-brackets for weight distribution.

### ⚠️ **Noise-Saving Tips**
- **Avoid Power Tools:** Use hand drills or manual screwdrivers.  
- **Work in Short Bursts:** 15-minute intervals to avoid stiffness.  
- **Stretch Breaks:** Do calf stretches every 30 mins to prevent soreness.  
- **Sound Dampening:** Play white noise during work to mask installation sounds.
