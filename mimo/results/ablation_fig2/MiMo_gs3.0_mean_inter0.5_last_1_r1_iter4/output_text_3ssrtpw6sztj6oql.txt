For modern-day art museum security, controlling all **entry points** remains the definitive first line of defense. This frontline combines visible security presence (e.g., guards) and screening procedures (e.g., bag checks, ID verification) to manage exactly **who and what enters the building**.  

### Why this is foundational:
1. **Proactive Deterrence**: Guards physically monitor access points, discouraging unauthorized entry.  
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Screening procedures limit threats (e.g., weapons, explosives) and contraband before they enter.  
3. **Dependency of Other Layers**: Internal systems (cameras, alarms, secure displays) rely on restricting initial access. A breach at the first line compromises everything downstream.  

The 1990 Isabella Stewart Gardner heist underscores this vulnerability: the thieves exploited human error (fooling guards) to bypass access controls, highlighting why strict entry protocols are the bedrock of museum security.  

### Key Takeaway for Your Paper:  
While high-tech systems are critical, the **first line must physically and procedurally control the point of vulnerability**—entry.
