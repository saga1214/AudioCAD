### Top Scuba Destinations in Costa Rica
1. **Cocos Island**  
   - **Why it's perfect**: Epic drift dives through deep canyons, whale sharks, manta rays, and giant ocean sunfish. Strong currents → **Invest in long fins here**.  
   - **Best for**: Experienced divers (strong currents), big wildlife sightings.  
   Short stays (3-4 days) via ferry from Puntarenas.

2. **Corcovado National Park (Osa Peninsula)**  
   - **Why it's perfect**: Pristine coral reefs, sea turtles (nesting season: April-Oct), dolphins, and incredible biodiversity. Calmer waters → **Short fins ideal** for easy exploration.  
   - **Best for**: Beginners, reef ecosystems, wildlife.  
   Access via boat from Puerto Jiménez (1-2 day stay).

3. **Tortuguero National Park**  
   - **Why it's perfect**: Calm lagoons, mangrove forests, and turtle nesting (June-Nov). Gentle currents → **Short fins perfect**.  
   - **Best for**: Beginners, tranquility, conservation.  
   Access via boat + canoe (1-2 day stay).

4. **Manuel Antonio National Park**  
   - **Why it's perfect**: Easy shore dives, snorkeling, and underwater tours (e.g., "The Cathedral" reef). Good visibility.  
   - **Best for**: Beginners, mixed-activity trips.  
   Base in Manuel Antonio town (1-2 days).

### Travel & Logistics
- **Finn Recommendation**: **Start with short fins** for the first trip. Pack compact long fins as a backup for Cocos.  
- **Certification**: Ensure PADI/SSI Open Water certification. Consider Advanced Rescue if new to deeper dives.  
- **Guides/Tours**: Book licensed operators (e.g., Rainforest Divers, Pescador).  
- **Best Time**: Dec-Apr (dry season, warmest water). Avoid Aug-Oct (hurricane season).  
- **Base Locations**:  
  - San José (airport hub)  
  - Manuel Antonio (diving + beaches)  
  Coral Bay (Cocos Island)  
  Puerto Jiménez (Tortuguero)  

### Wildlife Highlights
- **Cocos**: Whale sharks, manta rays, Galapagos sharks.  
- **Corcovado/Tortuguero**: Turtles, humpback whales (Aug-Oct), dolphins.  
- **Manuel Antonio**: Reef fish, rays, occasional sharks.  

### Pro Tips
- **Pack**: Reef hooks, macro lens, dive computer, dry suit (if cold water diving), short/long fins, travel-friendly snorkel.  
- **Health**: Check dive health clinics (e.g., in San José).  
- **Sustainability**: Stay at eco-lodges, avoid coral-touching.  

This plan balances adventure, wildlife, and practicality. Start with short fins for flexibility, but gear up for Cocos with long fins if currents intimidate you. ¡Buen viaje! 🌊🤿
