Second, try to avoid packaging that says 'grease-resistant,' you know, like on fast-food wrappers and some microwave popcorn bags. Third, steer clear of things labeled 'stain-resistant' or 'waterproof,' like carpets, furniture, and some outerwear. Fourth is cosmetics and dental floss that might have them; many brands now label if they're PFAS-free. And finally, since these chemicals can get in tap water, using a certified water filter can make a big difference.
