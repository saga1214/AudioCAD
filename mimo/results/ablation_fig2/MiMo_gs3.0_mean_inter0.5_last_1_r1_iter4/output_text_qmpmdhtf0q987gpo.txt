Okay, great job on getting everything prepped! Since you've light-sand and degreased them, and you're absolutely right to skip replacing them—that auction score wouldn't have happened that way! 

So, here’s what you need to do. After sanding, just wipe down all the cabinet surfaces with a tack cloth. This will pick up any dust from the sanding, and primer won't stick properly if there's dust.

Then, make sure to apply that primer with a brush or roller, getting it into all the corners and grooves. Let it dry completely, following the instructions on the can. 

And then you're ready for paint! Just a heads up, for painting from black to off-white, two thin coats of paint are usually better than one thick one. It'll give you a smoother, more durable finish. 

And hey, while you're at it, once you've painted the doors, you could install some new hardware in a brushed nickel or matte black. It'll really tie in with those earthy tones and give you that pizzazz you're looking for.
