Okay, let's break down the home security system for your 1500 sq ft space with four doors, seven windows, and likely needing a glass break sensor based on break-ins in your area. Self-install DIY options start around three hundred dollars, while professional installation can exceed a thousand. After that, expect a professional monitoring fee between twenty and sixty dollars monthly.

Now, for an itemized cost breakdown: the main control panel or hub is key, costing from one hundred to four hundred dollars. For your four doors, you'll need four door/window sensors, each costing between fifteen and fifty dollars. For your seven windows, you'll need seven more door/window sensors. A glass break sensor is a good addition, costing around thirty to one hundred dollars. Motion detectors are typically thirty to eighty dollars each, and you might want two for better coverage. An indoor siren for the alarm system is usually thirty to seventy dollars. If you add cameras, those can be fifty dollars or more each.

Professional installation adds about one hundred to four hundred dollars on top of the equipment cost. With that, you should also consider installation fees, which might be an additional one hundred to three hundred dollars.

So, the total upfront cost could range from four hundred and fifty dollars, if you do it all yourself, to over two thousand dollars for a professional system with installation. Then, over a five-year contract, your monthly payment would be around twenty-five to eighty dollars, after spreading out the monitoring service cost. It's always a good idea to get quotes from at least three different providers to find the best option for protecting your home.
