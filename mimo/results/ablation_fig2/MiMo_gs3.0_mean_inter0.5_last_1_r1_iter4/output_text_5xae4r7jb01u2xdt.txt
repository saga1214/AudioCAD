Got it. Let's talk about indirect free kicks versus direct free kicks. This is a major difference between college and high school rules.

Here’s a scenario: During a high school game, Red Team number 12 fouls Blue Team number 9 by tripping them. This happens in the middle of the field. A foul like tripping is an offense, but since it didn't happen directly in the goal area, it results in an indirect free kick for Blue Team, number 9.

Now, for a college certification ref studying, it's crucial to know that this is different. In college soccer, a foul like tripping is a direct free kick offense. It happens everywhere on the field.
