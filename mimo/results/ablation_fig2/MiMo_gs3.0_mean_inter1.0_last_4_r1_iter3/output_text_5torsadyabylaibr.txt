Pie is a fantastic idea for Thanksgiving. A really easy one to make from scratch is a pumpkin pie. It requires no oven—just one stovetop—so it fits your limited kitchen perfectly.

For a classic holiday side dish, you could make honey-glazed carrots. You just roast the carrots in the oven with a simple honey and butter glaze near the end.

And for a simple salad to go with everything, a Parmesan and walnut Caesar would be great. You just whisk the dressing together and then toss it with pre-washed romaine lettuce.
