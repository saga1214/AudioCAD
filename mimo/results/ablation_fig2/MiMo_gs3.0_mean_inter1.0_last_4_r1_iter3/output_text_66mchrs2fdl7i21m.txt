Phoenix excels in outdoor activities thanks to its sunny climate, making hiking and golf extremely popular pastimes. And culturally, yes, it boasts several notable museums, including the celebrated Heard Museum, famous for its exhibits of Native American art.

Population trends show that Phoenix is definitely growing; it remains one of the fastest-growing major cities in the United States.

Average year-round temperatures in Phoenix hover around 75 degrees, encompassing intensely hot summers and mild winters. Los Angeles averages at a cooler 64 degrees annually.
