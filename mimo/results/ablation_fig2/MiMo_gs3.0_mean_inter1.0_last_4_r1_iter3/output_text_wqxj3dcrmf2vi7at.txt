Okay, let's quickly recap all the homemade and store-bought ideas we talked about, tailored for your brown and white "little corgi princess." For homemade, we had frozen bites like pumpkin or sweet potato mashed up and frozen, peanut butter oat cookies (double-checking that packet for sweetness is xylitol-free!), pieces of plain cooked chicken, and thin sticks out of steamed carrot. Portals matter, keep 'em tiny! For store-bought brands worth looking at are good Blue Buffalo, good Wellness, good Zuke's, good Greenies, and good Merrick. Again, you'll want to check the ingredients on those too for wholesomeness. Teaching "sit" involves starting close to her nose with a treat, raising it over her head to encourage the sitting motion, then saying "sit" and giving a reward for getting it right. For potty training, the plan is to find a regular spot outside, take her there often after meals or naps, praise and reward heavily with tiny treats the second she goes in that spot. Keeping a regular feeding schedule can also help predict when she might need to go. And finally, if she barks at the cat Felix, you can teach a "quiet" command. That means when she starts barking, you interrupt with a calm treat or toy, wait for her to pause, say "quiet," and reward her for being quiet. Redirecting her attention to a toy or a different command like "sit" before she starts barking is also a great way to prevent it. The main things for all of this are being consistent and calm, and lots of praise for good behavior! Good luck with Princess!
