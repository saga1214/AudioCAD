Since you're boating and near Cruz Bay with Secret Harbour Beach Resort mentioned earlier, Cruz Bay is packed with great shops and restaurants. Here are some fantastic daytime activities right from your doorstep or nearby on St. John to enjoy week one:

1.  **Snorkeling at Gallows Point or Cruz Bay Bay:** Right outside your resort, the Cruz Bay area has calm, shallow waters teeming with fish, turtles, and often small rays. Gallows Point Resort even has a small dock where you can hop on a short boat trip to nearby reefs.
2.  **Snorkeling at Maho Bay:** Secret Harbour Beach Resort is an easy walk to Maho Bay. It has one of the world's most beautiful and famous beaches. While the main sandy beach is for swimming, the rocky cove area (Secret Harbour) is perfect for easy, shallow snorkeling right off the dock or rocks, especially at low tide. Maho Bay is also famous for its sea turtle nesting season (typically May-Oct).
3.  **Hiking Cruz Bay Trail:** A short, easy walk from Gallows Point or Secret Harbour leads to Cruz Bay Beach and then up to the Cruz Bay Trail. This trail winds through beautiful tropical forest and offers fantastic views down to the bay and out to the sea. It's peaceful, pressure-stable, and scenic.
4.  **Exploring Cruz Bay:** Cruise Bay shops are a must-do! Stroll the main street, browse the galleries, bookstores, clothing shops, and specialty food stores. Grab a coffee, lunch, or ice cream at one of the many cafes and restaurants right there.
5.  **Kayaking or Stand-Up Paddleboarding:** Several outfitters offer rentals and guided trips from Cruz Bay or nearby. Kayaking through the mangroves or paddling out to the reefs is a great way to explore calmly from the water.
6.  Ocean 362 Tiki Boat Cruise: While primarily known for sunset cruises, Ocean 362 also offers day trips. These often include stops at snorkeling sites, a light lunch, drinks, and sometimes even a luau-style dinner. Check their schedule for day trips that fit your dates.

Start with some easy snorkeling near Secret Harbour or Gallows Point – you'll be amazed by the underwater world right from your beach!
