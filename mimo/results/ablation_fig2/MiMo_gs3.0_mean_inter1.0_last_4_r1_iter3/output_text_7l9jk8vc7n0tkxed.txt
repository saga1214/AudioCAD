I hear you – a deep blockage is tough, especially with just your simple toolbox. Let's calm and tackle this step-by-step. **First, safety:** Put on gloves and eye protection. Always. Now, here's a practical plan:

### Step-by-Step Drainage Plan:
1. **Plunger Again (But Better):**  
   Use a **flat plunger** (if you have one in your simple toolbox) to create a *tight seal* over the drain. Pump vigorously for 20-30 seconds. The pressure often dislodges deep clogs.

2. **Drain Snake (Auger) – Your Best Bet:**  
   If you have a manual drain snake:  
   - Insert the tip into the drain slowly.  
   - Keep the handle steady.  
   - Once you feel resistance, **rotate the handle clockwise** while gently pushing forward.  
   - If it hooks something, *don't force it* – slowly pull it back with a slight upward motion.  
   - Clear 4-6 inches at a time if needed.  

3. **Boiling Water (If Safe):**  
   - Pour a kettle of boiling water down the drain in 2-3 stages (wait 1-2 mins between pours).  
   - *Only do this if it's a PVC pipe* – never use boiling water on metal or older cast-iron pipes.  

4. **Baking Soda & Vinegar (Last Resort):**  
   - Pour ½ cup baking soda down the drain.  
   - Follow with 1 cup white vinegar.  
   - Cover the drain (to contain fumes) and wait 15-30 mins.  
   - Flush with hot water.  

### Key Tips:
- **Avoid Chemical Cleaners** – They often damage pipes long-term and won’t work on deep clogs.  
- **Check for Debris** – If you see hair or gunk, use a small trowel or needle to fish it out before using the snake.  
- **Persistence Pays** – Sometimes it takes 10-15 minutes of plunging/snaking. Don’t give up after 3 tries!
