### 1. **Color-Coding Icons: Memory vs. Mental Load**  
   - **Memory**: Color-coding *does* enhance recall by creating visual anchors (e.g., red for urgent tasks, blue for projects).  
   - **Mental Load**: It *reduces* cognitive effort by grouping related items at a glance.  
   **Recommendation**: Use 2–3 consistent colors for categories (e.g., green = admin, yellow = creative work). Avoid overdoing it—simplicity wins.  

### 2. **Background Sound Switching: Lo-Fi vs. Ambient**  
   - **Optimal Timing**: Switching every 45–60 minutes can refresh focus without disrupting flow. Try:  
     - **Morning/Work Blocks**: Lo-fi (steady rhythm for concentration).  
     - **Late Afternoon/Wind-Down**: Ambient nature sounds (rain, forest) to reduce mental fatigue.  
   **Personal Preference**: If switching feels disruptive, stick to one type. Experiment and track your energy levels!  

### 3. **Physical Documents: Environment vs. Chunking**  
   - **Progress Source**: Both matter! A **clean workspace** reduces friction, while **chunking tasks** (e.g., "file invoices first") builds momentum.  
   **Action Plan**: Dedicate 1–2 hours on a quiet day. Sort into temporary ("process now") and permanent ("shelve") piles.
