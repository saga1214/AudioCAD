Sure, here’s a quick checklist to help you film your own puppet show properly:

- **Get Permission:** Make sure the facility allows recording and that it’s for your own use only.
- **Use a Stabilized Camera:** A tripod or phone mount helps avoid shaky footage.
- **Good Lighting:** Natural light works, but a ring light or softbox can help faces/puppets look clear.
- **Microphone:** A lavalier mic or external mic ensures your voice is clear over background noise.
- **Minimal Editing:** Keep it simple—cut out awkward pauses or mistakes if needed.
- **Kid-Friendly Tags:** Add captions or subtitles for clarity.
- **Copyright Notice:** Add a small watermark or text saying “© [Your Name]” in the corner.
- **Engaging Intro/Outro:** A fun intro and a thank-you message at the end can boost viewer retention.

That’s the basic answer. Good luck with the show!
