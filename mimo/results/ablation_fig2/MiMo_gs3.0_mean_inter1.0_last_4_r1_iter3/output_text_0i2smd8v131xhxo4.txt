Here's a breakdown addressing your setup across recording, editing, vocal health, and workflow optimization:

### 1. **Blue Yeti & Scarlett Solo Tweaks**
*   **Mic Placement:** Always speak into the logo-side (side ported mic). Keep it hand's-width (5-7 cm) from your mouth, slightly off-axis (not directly in front). Experiment with the cardioid polar pattern switch.
*   **Acoustics:** Heavy blankets/comforters hung wall-to-wall absorb high frequencies responsible for harshness. Place a rug down. Minimize hard, parallel surfaces.

### 2. Vocal Endurance & Warmups
*   **Warmups:** 5 mins daily: lip trills, humming, tongue twisters, scales. Never start recording with a cold voice.
*   **Posture & Breathing:** Sit/stand tall. Breathe deeply from your diaphragm (belly rises), not chest. This supports projection and reduces strain.
*   **Hydration:** Sip room-temperature water constantly. Avoid cold/caffeinated drinks right before recording.
*   **Breaks:** Take 5-10 mins *every* 30-45 mins. Stretch, breathe deeply, rest your jaw.
*   **Nourishment:** Pre-plan meals/snacks! Batch-cook quick meals (like the ramen suggestion) to avoid skipping meals. Prioritize protein/fiber.

### 3. Editing Workflow & Tools
*   **Audition vs. Audacity:** Audition has superior processing power, AI tools (like Adobe Podcast Enhance), and complex editing capabilities. **Yes, it saves time long-term** but has a steep learning curve. Start with Audacity, then audit specific Audition features (noise reduction, spectral editing) as needed.
*   **Noise Reduction:** **iZotope RX 10** (especially "De-Hiss" & "De-Ess") is the gold standard for removing clicks, hiss, and harshness. Audition's built-in AI-powered noise reduction is also excellent.
*   **Organization:** Use a clear folder structure: `Project > Clients > Client_Name > Scripts > Raw_Audio > Edits > Deliverables`. Label audio files consistently (e.g., `SCENE_01_01_take_01.wav`).

### 4. Making Your Demo Stand Out
*   **Show Versatility:** Include 2-3 distinct character voices or emotional states (e.g., intense action, gentle narration, quirky character).
*   **Strong Hook:** Start with a compelling 1-2 lines that immediately establish your unique voice and character work.
*   **Clean & Polished:** Ensure no stumbles, excessive mouth clicks, or room noise. Edits should be seamless.
*   **Length:** 60-90 seconds is usually ideal. Focus on quality over quantity.

### 5. AI Tools for Editing
*   **Adobe Audition:** Built-in "Noise Reduction" (AI-powered), "Voice Isolation," "Intelligibility Enhancer," "DeEsser" (reduces harsh sibilance).
*   **iZotope RX:** Industry-standard suite for noise reduction, de-clicking, de-hissing, vocal repair.
*   **Descript:** Great for transcription, editing via text, and AI-powered "Overdub" for removing background noise.

### 6. Pros & Workflow Balance
