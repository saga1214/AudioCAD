Dehydrating is a great process for preserving fruits and vegetables. Some of the more popular fruits to dehydrate are apples, bananas, mangoes, and strawberries. These fruits can be reconstituted into a powder or used as a snack. You can also dehydrate vegetables like onions and carrots, but this may take a little longer than the fruits.
