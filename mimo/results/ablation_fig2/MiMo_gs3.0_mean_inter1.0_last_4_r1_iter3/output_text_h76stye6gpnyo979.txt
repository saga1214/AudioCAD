That pigeon question is a classic logic puzzle! It's about finding the most efficient way to spread them out. The answer is that five pigeons would need to be on each roof.

I think the same idea applies to your goals.

First, decide your number one priority—is apartment down payment down apartment down payment or Salt flats Bolivia trip.

Then, treat that main goal like one of the roofs. Turn it into a small, specific savings target. For instance, 'save five hundred dollars this month.'

At the same time, you can use the other passion goals, like Bolivia and Ferugia, as your other 'pigeons' on the other roofs. For those, you can make tiny, regular actions. Maybe 'spend 30 minutes each week looking up Ferugia flights.'

By giving each goal a specific spot on your plan, you can make real progress on them all without feeling overwhelmed.
