### **Old Fashioned**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon rye (e.g., Buffalo Trace, Bulleit)  
  - 1 sugar cube OR ¾ oz simple syrup  
  - 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
  - Lemon twist (garnish)  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Muddle sugar cube with ½ oz water/bitters in a shaker.  
  2. Add remaining bitters, bourbon, ice.  
  3. Stir 20 sec until chilled.  
  4. Strain into a **rocks glass** over ice.  
  5. Express lemon twist over drink, rub rim, discard.  

---

### **Paper Plane**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz bourbon (e.g., Michter’s, Four Roses)  
  - 0.75 oz Aperol  
  - 0.5 oz Amaro Nonino  
  - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a **coupe glass**.  
  3. Garnish with a cherry or lemon twist.  

---

### **Whiskey Sour**  
- **Option 1 (with egg white)**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  - 0.75 oz lemon juice  
  dry shake 1 egg white + 0.5 oz simple syrup  
  2) Add ice, shake again. Strain.  
- **Option 2 (no egg white)**:  
  - 2 oz bourbon  
  - 0.75 oz lemon juice  
  - 0.5 oz simple syrup  
  - Stir with ice, strain over ice.  
- **Garnish**: Cherry or orange slice.  

---

### **Aviation**  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz gin (London Dry, e.g., Beefeater)  
  - 0.75 oz maraschino liqueur (Luxardo)  
  - 0.25 oz crème de violette  
  - 0.75 oz lemon juice  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.  
  2. Strain into a **cocktail glass/coup**.  
  3. Garnish with a cherry.  

---

### **Recommended Additional Gin Cocktail**  
**French 75** (perfect for fall elegance):  
- **Ingredients**:  
  - 1.5 oz gin  
  - 0.5 oz lemon juice  
  - 0.75 oz simple syrup  
  - 3 oz chilled champagne/ prosecco  
- **Instructions**:  
  1. Shake gin, lemon, syrup with ice.  
  2. Strain into champagne flute.  
  3. Top with bubbly.  
  - **Garnish**: Lemon wheel.
