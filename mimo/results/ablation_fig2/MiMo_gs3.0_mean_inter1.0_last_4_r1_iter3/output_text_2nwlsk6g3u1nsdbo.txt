### 1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
   - **Ingredients**:

     - ½ cup heavy cream  
     - ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped

### 2. **Spicy Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled  
     - 1 large onion, thinly sliced  
     - 3–4 cloves garlic, minced  
     - 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes  
     - ¼ cup red wine vinegar  
     - 2 tbsp butter  
     - 2–3 tbsp gochujang (Korean chili paste) or crushed red pepper flakes  
     - ¼ cup fresh basil, chopped  
     - Salt and pepper to taste  

### 3. **Cajun Shrimp and Grits**  
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled  
     - 1 cup quick-cooking grits (or polenta)  
     - 2 cups chicken or vegetable broth  
     - 2 tbsp butter  
     - 1 tsp Cajun seasoning (e.g., Tony Chachere’s)  
     - 1 bell pepper, diced  
     - 1 cup frozen peas  
     - ¼ cup grated sharp cheddar cheese
