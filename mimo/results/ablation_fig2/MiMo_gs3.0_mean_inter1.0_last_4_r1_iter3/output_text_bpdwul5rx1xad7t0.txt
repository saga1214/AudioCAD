Of course! With a Toyota SUV, you'll be all set for some fantastic road trips. Here are five great options for you and your family and new pup.

First, you could explore the Vancouver Island Loop. It's a scenic drive through the ocean and mountains, where you can visit the Butchart Gardens, hike to the top of Mount Washington, and see the famous Fairwinds Lighthouse. For food, you could try a farm-to-table restaurant in Sidney or a classic seafood spot in Tofino.

Second is the Fraser Canyon Scenic Route. It's a beautiful drive along the Fraser River. You can visit the Spadina Ranges Museum and hike to the Spadina Falls. For dining, you could find a cozy family-run inn or a rustic cabin restaurant in the area.

Third, check out the Okanagan Lakes Loop. It's a beautiful area with stunning lakes and vineyards. You can enjoy wine tasting, go kayaking on Kalamalka Lake, and hike to the top of Mount Truax for incredible views. For a special meal, you could go to a fine dining restaurant in Penticton or enjoy wine pairings at a vineyard.

Fourth is the Coastal Trail to Tofino. It's a bit of a longer drive but a perfect beach escape. You can take a kayak out to the Broken Group Islands and hike on the rainforest trails. For a special meal, you could try a farm-to-table restaurant or a seafood shack in Tofino.

Finally, there's the Kootenay Lakes Loop in the BC Interior. It's a beautiful drive through the Kootenay Mountains. You can visit the Kootenay Lake Resort, go kayaking, and hike to the Kootenay Falls. For food, you could find a lakeside restaurant or a mountain lodge that serves local game and fresh fish. Enjoy your adventures!
