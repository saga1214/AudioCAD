For Halloween candy, you want the crowd favorites that people will actually eat and not leave behind. Classics like chocolate bars, gummies, marshmallows, and sour candies are always safe bets. You could also grab some of the more Halloween-themed candies like mini pumpkins, ghost-shaped chocolates, or chocolate tombstones. You might want to get a variety pack of candy that includes a mix of different types, like chocolate, gummies, and sour. Or, you could also consider getting some of the bigger candy bars that are popular, like Snickers or Reese’s, because people tend to eat those up. Just make sure you check the allergens again—avoid anything with artificial dye for the dye allergies you mentioned earlier. You could also get some popcorn or chips with candy sprinkles on them as a safe alternative.
