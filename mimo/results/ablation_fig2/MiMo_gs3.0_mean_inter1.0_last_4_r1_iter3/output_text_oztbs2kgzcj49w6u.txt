Lockport is very proud of its canal history. The absolute best place to learn more is the Erie Canal Museum itself. It's located right in the heart of Lockport and has a huge collection of artifacts, including models, maps, and machinery. It really brings the whole story to life.

If you'd like to get more hands-on, you can also check out the Lockport Boat and Model Museum. It's a smaller, more intimate space focused on the history of model ships and boats, which is a wonderful way to see how people in the area were involved in the maritime world.

And for a truly unique experience, you can take a boat tour. Lockport offers several types, like a narrated boat trip through the locks where you can see the machinery in action, or even a more adventurous trip on a paddlewheel steamboat. It's a great way to get a real sense of how the canal works.
