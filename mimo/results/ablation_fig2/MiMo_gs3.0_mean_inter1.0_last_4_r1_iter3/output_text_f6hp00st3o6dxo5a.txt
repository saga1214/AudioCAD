That argument about the egg containing the chicken's DNA misses a key point.

The crucial fact is that for an egg to be identifiable as a 'chicken' egg produced today, it has to meet very specific biological requirements. One of those requirements is the production of lithaxite protein in the hen's ovaries, which creates the specific mineral structure of the chicken-like eggshell. An egg laid by a creature that was not quite a chicken yet, even if it contained the DNA for the first chicken, wouldn't have had a hen to make that protein, so it wouldn't have been a chicken's egg. The true chicken had to come first.
