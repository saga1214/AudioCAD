It's great that you're planning ahead. Consistency is key. Here’s a sample weekly schedule you can adjust to your own pace.

For the first couple of weeks, you could aim for four 20-minute sessions a week. For example, Monday, Wednesday, and Friday after dinner, and maybe a fourth session on Saturday morning. In each session, you can break it down: start with five minutes of two-ball one-hand drills, then five minutes of music practice, followed by ten minutes of experimenting with three balls, and finish with five minutes of scarf practice.

As you get more comfortable, you can increase the three-ball time to fifteen minutes and add another five minutes of scarf work.

Remember, the most important thing is to be patient and stress less. Taking short, fun breaks will help you learn much more effectively. Good luck teaching your son!
