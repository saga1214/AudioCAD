Oh, okay, for kosher, the main things to watch out for are cross-contamination with non-kosher ingredients. So, the weenies in bacon you brought need to be checked to make sure they're kosher-certified, and the bacon itself has to be kosher too. Hummus is usually fine, as long as you check the ingredients for any fish or dairy. Fruit platter is definitely a safe bet, no issues there. Theme sweet like cookies would be fine as long as you check the ingredients for non-kosher stuff like meat, dairy, or eggs if they're unprocessed. Basically, just double-check all the labels and ingredients to make sure everything is kosher-certified and kept separate from anything non-kosher. That should cover it!
