Here's a clear, recruiter-friendly resume format with concise explanations for each section—designed to minimize decisions and save time:

---

### **Header (Top-Left, 9pt Icons Optional)**
- **Center:** Your bold 14pt **name**  
- **Right-Aligned (9pt font with icons):**  
  Email ✉️ | Phone 📞 | City  
  *(Add a line break between each for readability)*  
  → *Optional but recommended:* LinkedIn URL (e.g., `linkedin.com/in/yourname`)

---

### **Contact Info Best Order: Top Section Only**
1. Name (centered, 14pt)  
2. Email  
3. Phone  
4. City  
5. LinkedIn Icon/URL (instead of icons for email/phone)  
→ **Why this works:** Vertical flow is faster for ATS systems and human eyes. Side placement looks amateurish.

---

### **Experience: Two Clear Sections**
#### **1. Relevant Experience (Most Critical for Career Shifts)**  
*(Bulleted lists of 2-4 key achievements per job)*  
**Job Title**, Company | *Month/Year – Month/Year*  
- Achievement 1 (with metrics: e.g., "Increased sales by 30%")  
- Achievement 2  
- Achievement 3  

#### **2. Additional Work History**  
*(For jobs unrelated to target roles)*  
**Job Title**, Company | *Month/Year – Month/Year*  
- Job title + 1 sentence summary (e.g., "Managed social media accounts")  

→ **Why?**  
- Group similar roles under "Relevant Experience" to highlight transferable skills.  
- Hide older/irrelevant jobs in "Additional" to avoid clutter. **Never leave gaps unexplained**—add a brief note if needed (e.g., "Career Break: 2018-2023").

---

### **Education: Standard Format**  
**Degree**, University | *Year*  
- **Highest Honors:** (e.g., "Magna Cum Laude")  
- **Awards:** (e.g., "Scholarship Award, 2022")  
→ *Place awards here, NOT in a separate section.*

---

### **Skills Section: Only Include Job-Relevant Skills**  
*(3-5 bullet points max)*  
- Hard Skills: Python, Google Analytics, Project Management  
- Soft Skills: Leadership, Cross-Team Collaboration  
→ **Tailor to the job description!** Remove generic skills (e.g., "Team Player").

---

### **Key Rules to Simplify Your Process**  
1. **Font:** Stick to 10-12pt (e.g., Calibri, Arial). Icons are risky for ATS.  
2. **Gaps:** Address proactively in your summary.  
3. **Length:** Keep to **one page** if <10 years experience; 2 pages max for senior roles.  
4. **Summary:** Add a 2-line "Professional Summary" under name:  
   *"X years in [field]. Seeking to leverage skills in [target role] at [Company]."*  

---

### **Template Structure**  
```plaintext
[Name]  
[Email] | [Phone] | [City] | [LinkedIn]  

Professional Summary (2 lines)  

Relevant Experience  
  Job Title, Company | Date  
    - Bullet 1  
    - Bullet 2  

Additional Work History  
  Job Title, Company | Date  
    - Bullet 1  

Education  
  Degree, University | Date  
  - Highest Honors / Awards  

Skills  
  - Skill 1  
  - Skill 2  
  - Skill 3  

References: Available upon request  
```

**Final Tip:** Use a clean template (like Canva or Novoresume) to auto-format everything. Focus on content—not design. Recruiters spend **6-7 seconds** on your resume. Clarity > creativity.
