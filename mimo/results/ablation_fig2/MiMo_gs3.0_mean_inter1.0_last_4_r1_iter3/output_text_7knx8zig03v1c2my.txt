### Treat Recommendations:
*   **High-Value Training Treats:** Look for small, soft, smelly, and nutritious treats puppies love. Common options include:
    *   **Chicken:** A favorite for most dogs (ensure it's plain, boneless, skinless).
    *   **Beef or Salmon Jerky:** Chewy and flavorful.
    *   **Cheese:** Small cubes of mild cheddar or mozzarella (avoid strong cheeses).
    *   **Hot Dogs:** Plain, sliced into tiny pieces.
    *   **Commercial Training Treats:** Many brands offer tiny, soft, dog-specific training treats.
*   **Key Points:**
    *   **Small:** Use tiny pieces so they can eat them quickly while focused on the task.
    *   **High-Value:** Choose treats your puppy *really* wants to work for.
    *   **Consistent:** Use the *same* kind of treat for the *same* task (e.g., use chicken for "sit" but cheese for "stay").
    *   **Safety:** Avoid chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol, alcohol, or anything with bones or nuts.

### Clicker Training Explained:
Clicker training is a highly effective, positive reinforcement method. Here's how it works:

1.  **The Clicker:** It's just a simple plastic device that makes a sharp, clear "click" sound.
2.  **The Bridge:** The click *itself* is the bridge. Its job is to signal the exact *instant* your puppy does the desired behavior (like sitting).
3.  **The Reward:** The instant you click, you immediately give your puppy a treat.
4.  **The Sequence:** Click -> Treat. Repeat this sequence hundreds of times. The puppy learns that the click sound means a treat is coming *right after* they do the right thing.
5.  **Shaping:** You gradually change what behavior the clicker signals. Start with a very easy behavior (like looking at you) and slowly ask for more complex ones (like sitting, then sitting while looking at you, then sitting while looking at you for 2 seconds).
6.  **How it Works:** The click precisely marks the behavior. The treat reinforces the behavior that earned the click. The puppy learns that doing *exactly* what you want results in the click-and-treat. This makes learning faster and more precise.

**Why it's cool:** It removes the guesswork ("Was that good?"). The click gives a clear, immediate signal, and the puppy learns faster. You can also use it for complex sequences.

### Recall Training (Getting Him Back if He Runs Out):
This is crucial! Here's how to start building a strong recall:

1.  **Start Small & Positive:** Begin in a quiet, distraction-free area (your backyard, living room). Use a long training line (10-15 feet) attached to a harness.
2.  **Name It Clearly:** Use a unique, short, enthusiastic name like "Galileo! Come!"
3.  **Reward Religiously:** The moment he comes to you (even if he just looks up), mark it with a click or a clear "YES!" and immediately give a high-value treat. *Never* call him back just to scold him.
4.  **Build Distance:** Gradually increase the distance he runs before you call him back. Start with just a few feet.
5.  **Add Distractions Slowly:** Once he reliably comes back from a few feet, try calling him back while he's sniffing something interesting nearby. Reward *even more* enthusiastically.
6.  **Practice Everywhere:** Practice in different locations and times of day.
7.  **Use a Long Line:** Always use a long line outdoors until he has a reliable recall. If he starts to run towards something, gently guide him back with the line *without* pulling hard or scolding.
8.  **High Value is Key:** Use his absolute favorite treat or toy for recall practice initially.

**Remember:** Recall training takes consistent practice and patience. Focus on making coming back the best thing that ever happened to him!
