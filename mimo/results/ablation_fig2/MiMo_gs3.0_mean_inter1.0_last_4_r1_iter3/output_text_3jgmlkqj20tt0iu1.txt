Initally, a face-to-face conversation can feel intimidating, but it can also be very effective.

One approach is to ask if you could schedule a brief meeting during performance review season. That's a very common and expected time for these kinds of discussions, which might make your boss more receptive.

Alternatively, if you're feeling very anxious about starting the conversation, you could begin with an email. You could write something like, 'I'd like to discuss my career growth and contributions at our next one-on-one meeting. Are you available?' This gives you a moment to prepare before the in-person talk.
