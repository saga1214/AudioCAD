Here are quick, elegant cocktail recipes for your vodka and Fernet-inspired drinks—keeping your party’s formality, dietary needs, and soda allergy in mind:

### 🥃 **Vodka Cocktails**  
*(All vodka drinks use soda allergy-friendly sparkling water or club soda)*  

1. **Sparkling Blood Orange & Thyme Vodka**  
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - 1.5 oz vodka  
     - 0.5 oz blood orange syrup (*simmer 1 cup blood orange juice + 2 tbsp sugar until thick*)  
     - 4–5 fresh thyme sprigs  
     - 0.5 oz fresh lime juice  
     - Top with chilled **sparkling water**  
   - **Garnish**: Blood orange twist + thyme sprig.  
   - *Why it works*: Bright, herbal, and refreshing—no soda!  

2. **Berry Vodka Spritz**  
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - 1.5 oz vodka  
     - 0.5 oz mixed berry purée (*blended raspberries + blackberries*)  
     - 0.5 oz lemon juice  
     - Top with **club soda**  
   - **Garnish**: Fresh berry skewer.  
   - *Why it works*: Light, fruity, and soda-free. Appeals to all ages/diets.  

---

### 🇦🇷 **Argentinian-Style Fernet**  
*(Inspired by the classic "Fernet-Branca con Coca-Cola" but elevated)*  

1. **Fernet-Branca "Pisco Sour"**  
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
     - 1 oz fresh lime juice  
     - 0.5 oz honey syrup (*equal parts honey + hot water*)  
     - 0.5 oz **sparkling water** (*instead of soda*)  
     - *Optional*: 0.5 oz Pisco (grape brandy) for depth  
   - **Shake**: With ice, strain into a chilled coupe.  
   - *Why it works*: Honors tradition while avoiding soda. Honey adds sophistication.  

2. **Fernet & Orange Soda**  
   - **Ingredients**:  
     - 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca  
     - 0.5 oz **orange soda** (*use a quality brand like Fentimans*)  
     - 0.25 oz bitters (*Angostura or orange*)  
   - **Garnish**: Orange slice + lemon twist.  
   - *Caution*: Skip if strict soda allergy—use **sparkling water** instead.  

---

### 🧊 **Soda-Free Sparkler Backup**  
For the guest:  
- **Fernet Sparkler**: 1 oz Fernet-Branca + 0.5 oz lime juice + 0.5 oz honey syrup. Top with **sparkling water**. Garnish with an orange peel.  

### 💡 **Key Tips**  
- **Prep Ahead**: Make syrups/purées 1 day prior. Chill glasses.  
- **Glassware**: Use coupes or copas for elegance.  
- **Balance**: Offer a mix of strong (Fernet) and lighter (vodka) drinks.  
- **Mocktails**: For non-drinkers, try **sparkling water + mint + cucumber slices** or **Fernet shrub** (Fernet + fruit vinegar + sugar).
