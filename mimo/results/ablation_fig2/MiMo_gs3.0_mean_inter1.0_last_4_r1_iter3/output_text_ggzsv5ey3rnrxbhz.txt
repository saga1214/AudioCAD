I understand your concern about getting these chemicals out of your system. It's really important to know that there's no single magic cure, like a juice cleanse, that can remove them. Exposure continues as long as you're exposed to them, and your body has to process them naturally over time. There's no proven, rapid way to get them out.

So, let's focus on what you can do. Here are five products to avoid to minimize future exposure.

First, definitely non-stick cookware. Switch to materials like stainless steel, cast iron, or even ceramic.

Second, avoid grease-resistant packaging. This includes wrappers from fast-food places and microwave popcorn bags.

Third, stay away from anything with a waterproof or stain-resistant label, such as carpets, furniture, and some types of clothing.

Another thing is cosmetics that aren't specifically labeled 'PFAS-free.' It's a good idea to check labels.

And finally, since tap water can be a source, using a certified water filter is highly recommended. Remember, some bottled waters can also have these chemicals, so a home filter is generally the best bet.
