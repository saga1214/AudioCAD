### 🌿 East-Facing 5th-Floor NYC Balcony Plan
1. **Succulents (for the table floor or small pots)**

   - **Table-friendly varieties**:  
     - **Echeveria**: Rosette-shaped, compact (6–12 inches tall).  
     - **Haworthia**: Small, striped, and sturdy.  
     - **Sedum "Autumn Joy"**: Low-growing with red blooms.  
   - **Pro tip**: Use lightweight plastic nursery pots (not heavy ceramic) for easy transport.

2. **Snake Plant ("Bird’s Nest" - for the floor)**  
   - **Appearance**: Wide, triangular leaves forming a nest-like cluster; silver-green stripes.  
   - **Weight**: A 6-inch pot weighs **~1–1.5 lbs** (easy to carry up 5 flights).  
   - **City perks**: Tolerates low light if clouds block the sun, and NYC wind won’t bother it.  
   - **Transport**: Lightweight but **bring a reusable bag** to protect the leaves.

### ⚠️ Key Considerations to Avoid Overwhelm
- **Weight limits**: Stick to plants under **2 lbs per pot** (succulents are ideal; avoid larger snake plants >8 inches tall).

  - Sunlight variability: Cloudy days may reduce morning light—opt for **low-light tolerant** plants (snake plant, pothos).  
- **Transport ease**:  
  - **Succulents**: Pack in a small bag—easy to carry with work gear.  
  - **Snake plant**: Can be tucked under one arm.
