Of course. For miso soup or cucumber salad, you'll need miso paste for the soup, or just cucumbers, rice vinegar, sesame oil, soy sauce, and sesame seeds. Condiments you might need are soy sauce and toasted sesame oil. For seasonings, some white miso paste and rice vinegar would be good. 

Then for the main course, chicken teriyaki needs sliced chicken breasts. Stir-fried veggies could be broccoli, bell peppers, and snap peas. Steamed jasmine rice just needs jasmine rice, water, soy sauce, and a little sugar. Seasonings for that would be soy sauce and sugar. Seasonings for the chicken itself would be soy sauce, grated ginger, and garlic.

And for the dessert, to make homemade mango sticky rice at home, you'll need sticky jasmine rice, fresh ripe mango, coconut milk, sugar, and fish sauce, even though your husband is allergic to legumes—the type used here is evaporated, not fish-based. Salt is also a good seasoning for that.
