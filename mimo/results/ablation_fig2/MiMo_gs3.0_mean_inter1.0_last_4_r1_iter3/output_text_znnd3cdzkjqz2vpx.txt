You've got the basics down, which is awesome!

But I'd strongly recommend adding a couple more things to your list. First, a cordless power drill with some good screws. Cabinet wall studs usually have these special holes in them that are designed for screws, so it'll be way faster and easier than hammering nails. Second, a good pair of work gloves will protect your hands from splinters.

You're going to have a great time turning that Habitat find into a fully installed kitchen! Just remember to take your time and measure twice before you drill. Have fun!
