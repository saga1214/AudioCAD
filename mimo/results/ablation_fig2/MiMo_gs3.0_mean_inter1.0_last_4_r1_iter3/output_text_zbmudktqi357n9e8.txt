Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for autumn scene inspiration! The best time would probably be either late September through November. That's Mexico’s true autumn season. You'll see all the leaves changing, especially in the northern states like Chihuahua and Nuevo León. 

But, uh, if you want those super vibrant, fiery colors, October to early November is probably your sweet spot. That’s when the leaves are at their peak. Also, just a heads-up, the weather in October can be pretty hot, so maybe plan your outdoor painting for the mornings or evenings. 

And hey, if you want to focus more on coastal scenes, like those beautiful autumn hues on the beaches, then late September through October is perfect. The water will be warm, and the colors on the sand and sky will just be stunning. 

Seriously, Mexico’s autumn is a dream for an aspiring artist, so you’re gonna have an amazing time. Just pack light and bring plenty of watercolor paper!
