That's a great attitude. Here's how you could structure the email to strike that balance between respect and getting the information you need.

First, start with a clear subject line like, "Meeting Request: Essay Exam Feedback."

Then, in the email, state your purpose directly but politely. You could write: "I scored a 64 on my latest exam and would like to improve on future tests. Could I please schedule a brief meeting to understand what I could have improved on in my essay score?"

To be specific without challenging him, you could add: "Subjectively graded exams can be difficult to understand, but any feedback you could provide would be very helpful for my learning."

Finally, end with a simple call to action, like, "Please let me know if office hours work, or if another time is better for you."

This approach focuses on your growth and avoids any language that might trigger his ego. Good luck!
