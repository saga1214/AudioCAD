Here's a **modular closing gear packing list** tailored for both segments, prioritizing flexibility since temps and conditions will change:

### **Daytime Segment (20 Miles - Mid 30s)**
1. **Moisture-wicking base layer** (long-sleeve/short-sleeve, breathable)  
2. **Lightweight fleece or windbreaker** (for hilly wind protection)  
3. **Water-resistant outer shell** (lightweight, packable, for rain/humidity)  
4. **Trail gloves** (for grip and warmth)  
5. **Sun hat or cap** (if sunny)  
6. **Lightweight beanie or cap** (for head warmth if windy)  
7. **Headlamp/handheld light** (handheld + extra batteries essential)  
8. **Small pack/vest** (to carry layers as needed)  

### **Night Segment (Final 12 Miles - After Dark)**
1. **Moisture-wicking base layer** (same as daytime)  
2. **Insulated fleece or lightweight puffy** (critical for warmth)  
3. **Windproof/wind-resistant outer layer** (add if outer shell is packable)  
4. **Warm hat/gloves** (upgrade gloves for insulation; hat for ears)  
5. **Buff or neck gaiter** (for face/wrists warmth)  
6. **Headlamp/handheld light** (extra batteries!)  
7. **Extra socks** (if feet get cold)  
8. **Small pack/vest** (to stash cold layers)  

### **Key Notes**  
- **Gloves:** Bring both lightweight (day) and insulated (night).  
- **Light:** Night requires **two lights** (one primary, one backup).  
- **Layers:** Packable items are ideal—add/remove as needed.  
- **Footwear:** Trail shoes with **grip** and **tough soles** for uneven/nightly terrain.  
- **Avoid:** Bulk layers (hard to adjust), cotton (retains moisture), or bulky hats.  

**Pro Tip:** Test gear tonight! If it feels cool day, you’ll need extra insulation at night. You’ve got this! 🌟
