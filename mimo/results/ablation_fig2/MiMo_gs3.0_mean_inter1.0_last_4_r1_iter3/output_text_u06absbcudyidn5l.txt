Beyond rosacea, other very common causes of chronic skin redness are sun damage, certain allergic reactions, or even very sensitive skin. There are also some medications that can cause it as a side effect.

Vasculitis, as the name suggests, is an inflammation of the blood vessels. The size of the vessel involved determines how it presents. When small blood vessels are inflamed, you might see redness, warmth, and swelling in small patches. If medium-sized vessels are affected, it can cause larger rashes or affect deeper organs. And when it's the large vessels, it can lead to more systemic symptoms like high blood pressure or organ issues.

The root cause is typically an autoimmune problem where the immune system mistakenly attacks the vessel walls, or in some cases, it's triggered by an infection.
