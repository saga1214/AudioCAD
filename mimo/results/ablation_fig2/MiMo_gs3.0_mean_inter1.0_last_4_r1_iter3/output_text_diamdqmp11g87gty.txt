Beyond that, if you’re near a frozen lake and you know how to skate, ice skating is a classic winter activity. There's also snowmobiling, which is super fun but requires a lot of gear and safety precautions. And of course, as you mentioned earlier, icef- ishing is always a great option when fishing is open. The key is just to bundle up well and stay safe out there. Winter can be a really magical time to be outdoors.
