Don't worry about setting off the alarm this time – stir-frying mostly happens over high heat anyway!

The wood spoon incident is definitely in your past now. For stir-fry noodles, the number one thing is to use metal tongs or chopsticks to stir constantly. You want to keep the noodles moving so they cook evenly.

Another crucial step is to rinse the cooked noodles under cold water and drain them very well. This stops them from sticking together. Then, just toss them with your sauce at the very end. You're in control the whole time, so you can't leave tools in random pots. Good luck, you've got this
