### **Costa Rica Dive Itinerary (Tropical Paradise)**

     - **Highlights**: Gentle waves, coral reefs (staghorn, brain coral), tropical fish (parrotfish, surgeonfish), and occasional rays.  
     - **Advantage of Short Fins**: Maneuverability in tight spaces and calm waters.  
   - **Puerto Viejo (Tortuguero)**:  
     - **Highlights**: Mangrove forests, nurse sharks, sea turtles (seasonal), and clear shallow reefs.  
     - **Bonus**: Eco-friendly lodges and jungle excursions.  

2. **Boat Diving (Longer Distances Current-Friendly)**  
   - **Cocos Island**:  
     - **Highlights**: Strong currents, deep wrecks (e.g., *El Cacique*), blue holes, and abundant marine life (sharks, manta rays, dolphins).  
     - **Long Fins Recommended**: Propulsion for stronger currents and longer dives.  
   - **Tortuga Island**:  
     - **Highlights**: Protected marine park with vibrant coral gardens, turtles, and schools of fish.  
     - **Mix Fin Types**: Start with short fins for shore access, switch to long fins for deeper dives.  

3. **Adventure Add-Ons**  
   - **Canyoning & Lava Tubes**: Explore volcanic landscapes (e.g., Arenal Volcano).  
   - **Night Dives**: Spot bioluminescent plankton at Conchal Beach.  

### **Key Advantages in Costa Rica’s Tropical Waters**  
- **Warm Water (23–28°C/73–82°F)**: Short fins reduce fatigue; long fins excel in currents.  
- **Diverse Life**: Turtles, reef sharks, rays, and colorful corals (check seasonal guides for whale sharks in winter).  
- **Compact Dives**: Shore sites suit short fins; island dives favor long fins.  

### **Tips for Your Debate** Pack **short fins** for travel, maneuverability, and calm sites. Rent **long fins** locally for boat trips. Test both during a certification course if possible!
