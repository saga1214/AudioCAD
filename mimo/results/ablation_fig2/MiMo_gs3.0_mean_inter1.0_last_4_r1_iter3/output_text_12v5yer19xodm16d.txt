Okay, you need a pick-me-up. Let's talk drinks first. For five good ones, I'd suggest: an Iced Americano, which is black coffee with ice, for a clean caffeine boost; a Matcha Latte, which has gentle energy; a Green Tea Iced Tea for a lighter option; a Pomegranate Sparkling Water for a refreshing caffeine-free choice; and finally, a Coconut Water with a pinch of salt for some electrolytes.

Now for five snacks to keep you going: first, a handful of Almonds, which have protein and healthy fats. Second, some Dark Chocolate, maybe 70 percent or higher. Third, Greek Yogurt, which is high in protein. Fourth, an Apple with Peanut Butter, which combines fiber and fats. And last, a Hard-Boiled Egg, which is a great source of protein and choline.

Hope that helps you get your energy back!
