### 1. TV Mounting (Landlord Rule Check: Use Adhesive Solutions)
*   **Safety First:** Identify wall type (drywall, concrete, brick) using a stud finder with a drill attachment. *Avoid drilling directly into drywall without anchors* due to your noise rule.
*   **Plan Layout:** Use painter's tape to mark exact TV position, mount location, and cable routes. Measure twice!
*   **Choose Mount:** Ensure it matches your TV's weight/size. VESA pattern is crucial.
*   **Non-Permanent Mounting (Best for Noise Rules):**
    *   **Adhesive Strips:** Use heavy-duty 3M VHB (Pressure Sensitive Adhesive) tape. Clean the wall surface *thoroughly* with rubbing alcohol. Press firmly for 2+ minutes. *Test weight capacity first.*
    *   **Removable Hooks:** Use strong, rated hooks screwed into studs (if allowed) or anchored securely. Hang TV with a universal hook bracket.
*   **Install:** Carefully lift TV by its corners (NEVER by screen!), align with mount, press firmly onto adhesive or hook, and secure if needed.
*   **Cables:** Use cable clips along the wall or under furniture. Conceal power cords with baseplates if possible.

### 2. Hanging Planters (Landlord Rule Check: Use Removable Solutions)
*   **Choose Hangers:** Select strong, rated hooks (e.g., S-hooks, D-rings, planter hangers). Ensure they are rated for your planter's weight + soil + water.
*   **Non-Permanent Mounting (Best for Noise Rules):**
    *   **Adhesive Hooks/Strips:** Use high-strength adhesive hooks rated for the weight. Clean wall surface meticulously. *Test weight capacity first.*
    *   **Removable Hooks:** Screw or anchor hooks into studs/concrete if allowed. Use hooks rated for planter weight.
*   **Install:** Lift planter by its base, align hook under the pot's handle or directly under the base, and lower it gently. Ensure it's secure.
*   **Placement:** Hang near windows for light, or on a wall you enjoy. Keep away from high-traffic areas.

### 3. Floating Shelves (Landlord Rule Check: Use Anchors if Drywall)
*   **Measure Twice, Cut Once:** Ensure precise dimensions. Mark shelf positions on wall using a level.
*   **Stud Placement (Ideal):** Locate studs with a stud finder. Position shelves so one bracket is on a stud for maximum security. If not centered, use anchors in the empty space.
*   **Anchors for Drywall (If Drilling Allowed):** Use appropriate toggle bolts or snap toggles rated for the shelf's weight. *If drilling not allowed, use heavy-duty adhesive anchors rated for the weight.*
*   **Bracket Installation:**
    1.  Hold bracket in place against wall.
    2.  Drill pilot holes (if using toggle bolts) or apply adhesive.
    3.  Insert bolts or press anchors firmly.
    4.  Tighten bolts securely.
*   **Shelf Attachment:** Place shelf on brackets, ensuring it's level. Slide or screw it into place. Check level and stability.
*   **Weight Distribution:** Ensure heavier items go lower and are centered on brackets. Avoid overloading.
