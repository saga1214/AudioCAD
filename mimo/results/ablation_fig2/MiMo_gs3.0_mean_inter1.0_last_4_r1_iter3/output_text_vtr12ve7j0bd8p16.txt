Of course, Calgary is a fantastic base for adventure. 

If you're up for a drive, Banff and Lake Louise are classics, but they can be busy. For something a bit closer, you could try the Kananaskis Valley. It's a stunning area about an hour's drive away with lots of hiking and biking trails. Or, for a quick escape, you could head to Canmore and hike in the Ghost Ranch area.

If you want to get on the water, you're on the Bow River. You can rent kayaks or paddleboards along the river paths, or go for a swim at the Deerfoot Pool. There's also a great modern climbing gym called The Vault if she's into that.

For a truly special experience, you could look into a stargazing tour. There are companies that offer private tours to dark sky preserves, which would be perfect for your Sagittarius-Leo dynamic. 

And since Sagittarius is the archer, a shooting range could be a fun, modern adventure, too.
