Absolutely! Here's a comprehensive New Year's Eve shopping list organized by category, based on our menu plan for 24 guests (20 adults + 4 kids). I've included quantities and prep notes where helpful:

### **Drinks**  
- **Alcoholic**:  
  - 4 bottles champagne (1 per adult for toast)  
- **Non-Alcoholic**:  
  - 4 large bottles grape juice (for kids & non-alcoholic guests)  
  - 2 bottles sparkling cider (backup)  
  - Signature cocktail ingredients (e.g., mixers, syrups)  
  - Lemonade/iced tea (extra)  
- **Supplies**:  
  - Ice (1 large bag)  
  - Cups (30+ for guests)  

### **Appetizers/Finger Foods** *(Serves 20+ adults easily)*  
- **Ham**: 12–14 lb pre-cooked ham (prevents last-minute stress)  
- **Scalloped Potatoes**:  
  - 2 lbs potatoes (washed/peeled)  
  - 8 oz cream cheese  
  - 1 cup butter (softened)  
  - 2 cups milk  
  - Salt/pepper  
- **Roasted Carrots** *(Clean alternative!)*:  
  - 1 lb carrots (cut into sticks)  
  - Olive oil  
  - Salt/pepper  
- **Asparagus**:  
  - 2 bunches asparagus  
  - Olive oil  
  - Lemon wedges  
  - Salt/pepper  
- **Appetizers**:  
  - Mini slider ingredients (beef/pork blend, slider buns, toppings)  
  Shrimp cocktail (shrimp, cocktail sauce, lemon)  
 Pigs in a blanket (sausage, glaze)  
 *Extra veggies*: Baby carrots, cucumber slices, olives (for nibbling)  

### **Kids' Specials** *(Fun & easy!)*  
- **Snacks**: Goldfish crackers, fruit snacks, grapes, pretzels  
- **Juice Cups**: 4–6 juice boxes (for festive grape juice)  
- **Party Supplies**: Balloons, kid hats/noisemakers, small plates  

### **Essentials**  
- **Pantry Staples**:  
  - Trash bags (25+ for 24 guests)  
  - Napkins (30+ rolls)  
  - Utensils (30+ forks/spoons)  
  - Paper towels  
- **Extras**:  
  - Candles/LED lights (for ambiance)  
  - Coffee/tea for morning cleanup  

### **Pro Tips**  
1. **Prep Ahead**:  
   - Make scalloped potatoes, carrots, and asparagus the day before.  
   - Pre-slice ham for easy carving.  
2. **Kid Involvement**:  
   - Let kids help decorate juice cups with stickers or draw faces.  
   - Assign them to hand out grapes or crackers!  
3. **Pantry Check**:  
   - Confirm you have salt, pepper, olive oil, and basic spices.
