Yes, vintage comics from the 1940s and 1950s can be exceptionally rare.

For example, the record still stands for *Action Comics* number one at six million.

Other major names include the first full appearance of Wolverine in *Incredible Hulk* number 181, and the first Deadpool in *New Mutants* number ninety-eight, both of which regularly sell for high six-figure sums.

If your budget is under five hundred dollars, you can find some fantastic and rare vintage books. For example:

First, *The Amazing Spider-Man* number three hundred, the first full appearance of Venom, is a classic and can be found for around that price.
Second, *Savage She-Hulk* number one, which is her first appearance, is also a great find.
Third, *Fantastic Four* number forty-eight is the first appearance of Galactus and the Silver Surfer.
Fourth, *The Avengers* number one hundred, the first appearance of Vision and Wasp.
Fifth, *The Defenders* number one, Luke Cage's first team-up book.
Sixth, *Power Man and Iron Fist* number one, his very first solo series.
Seventh, *Black Panther* number one, his debut.
Eighth, *Captain Marvel* number one from 1963, the first appearance of Shazam.
Ninth, *The Silver Surfer* number one, the first full appearance of the Surfer.
And tenth, *Thor* number one hundred and fifty, which features the first appearance of the Dark Thor, Ulik.

Just remember, the key to any vintage comic's value is its condition. Always look for one that has been professionally graded.
