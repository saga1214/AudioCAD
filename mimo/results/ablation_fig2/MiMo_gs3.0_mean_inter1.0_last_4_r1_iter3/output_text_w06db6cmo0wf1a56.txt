Okay, planning for ten dads and ten kids—that's ten calzones for the bachelor! Here's a mess-minimizing cooler lineup based on our chat. I've focused on grab-and-go nutrition, minimal packaging, and everyone's preferences.

Drinks cooler one: 
- fifty reusable water bottles for refills
- thirty low-sugar juice boxes for kids
- thirty 12-ounce sugar-free sodas, maybe variety packs
- ten sports bottles with electrolyte packets
- ten insulated bottles for dads who want their drinks to stay cold

Snack cooler one:
- Cheese sticks, kids' and adult size
- Grapes in individual bags
Pretzels or trail mix in ten single-serve bags
Baby carrots with ten hummus cups
Fresh fruit cups in juice, just drain the juice first
Energy bars or balls for kids

Meal cooler:
- Forty pre-made wraps or sandwiches, all cut-side down
- Twenty mini calzones if you find them, or maybe pizza bites

And for dessert, the grab-and-go options are your best bet to keep things simple. How about:
- Forty cupcakes, decorated simply
- Or maybe brownie cups that come in single-serving boxes
- Or those mini pies from a bakery that you can get in individual slices

This setup should let you guys pile coolers in the car, hand out stuff as you need it, and spend all day at the theme park without a mess in sight! Have the absolute best time.
