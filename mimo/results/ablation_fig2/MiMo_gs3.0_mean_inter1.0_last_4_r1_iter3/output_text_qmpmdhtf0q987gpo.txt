Okay, let's go over one last time before you get ready to paint. After the sanding, it's super important to wipe down the cabinets again to catch all the loose particles the sanding created.

Then, make sure they are completely dry before you apply the primer. And like we talked about, because you're going from black to a lighter off-white, make sure to use a primer specifically made for covering dark colors.

As for just using one coat of primer... yeah, I really wouldn't recommend it. You'll want to use two full coats of primer to be sure it blocks all the black and gives you a really solid, even base for your new color.
