### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia:  
1. **150-Meter Rule**:  
   - You must see a clear 150-meter (approx. 150 meters / ~1 football field) in both directions before turning.  
   - **Estimate**: Count 15 seconds driving at 20 km/h (or 10 seconds at 30 km/h) to gauge distance.  

2. **Prohibited Areas**:  
   - **No U-turns**: Near intersections, curves, hills, driveways, or where traffic flow is obstructed.  
   - **Signs**: Strictly obey "No U-Turn" signs.  

3. **Penalties**:  
   - **Fines**: ~$160–$200 (varies by offense severity).  
   - **Points**: 2–3 demerit points on your driver’s license.  
   - **Accidents**: Risk of liability if you cause an accident.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients:  
**For 4 servings**:  
- **Pasta**: 400g spaghetti (or bucatini).  
- **Eggs**: 3 large eggs + 1 egg yolk (whisked together).  
- **Cheese**: 150g grated Pecorino Romano (or Parmesan).  
- **Garlic**: 2 cloves (minced).  
- **Anchovies**: 2–3 fillets (or 1 tbsp anchovy paste, optional but traditional).  
- **Black Pepper**: Freshly ground (to taste).  
- **Butter/Olive Oil**: 1 tbsp each (for cooking).  

**Optional**:  
- Salt for pasta water.  
- Fresh parsley (chopped).  

**Method**:  
1. Cook pasta in salted water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
2. Sauté garlic and anchovies in butter/oil until fragrant.  
3. Mix eggs, cheese, pepper, and pasta water in a bowl.  
4. Toss hot pasta with the sauce (off-heat) to avoid scrambling eggs. Add extra pasta water if needed.  

Enjoy your meal! 🍝
