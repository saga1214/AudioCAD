Based on your goals and the factors you've listed, **engaging content** is the single most important factor. Here's why, ranked from most to least critical:

1.  **Engaging Content**  
    *   **Why:** TikTok's algorithm prioritizes content that keeps viewers watching. If your video hooks viewers instantly and keeps them engaged (watch time, likes, comments, shares), the algorithm will push it to more people. Without this, even perfect lighting/audio/hashtags won't matter. This is the core driver of growth and discovery.

2.  **Following Guidelines**  
    *   **Why:** Absolutely critical. If your video is restricted or removed, *nobody* sees it. You can have the most engaging content in the world, but if it violates TikTok's rules (guidelines), it's dead in the water. This is the essential *prerequisite* for your content to be considered.

3.  **Conciseness**  
    *   **Why:** Highly important for retention. Shorter, punchy videos are often easier to watch completely, leading to better completion rates. This signals to the algorithm that viewers like it. While not strictly necessary for *all* content types, it's a major advantage.

4.  **Good Description & Hashtags**  
    *   **Why:** Important for discoverability and context. They help the algorithm understand your content and connect it with relevant users/hashtags. However, strong hashtags/descriptions can't overcome truly poor content or guideline violations. They are supporting elements.

**In Summary:**

*   **Follow Guidelines First:** This is the absolute baseline. Don't get your content banned.
*   **Then Focus Relentlessly on Engagement:** This is the engine that drives your videos to be shown to *more* people. The hook in the first 1-3 seconds is paramount. If you don't grab attention immediately, viewers scroll past, and the algorithm stops pushing it.
*   **Conciseness & Description/Hashtags** are valuable supporting elements that enhance your chances once you have strong, guideline-compliant, engaging content.

**Therefore, while all factors matter, "engaging content" is the most important because it directly fuels the algorithmic promotion that leads to success and exposure.**
