Here's a clear, categorized summary of all the basic through advanced measurements we've discussed, perfect for your fitting checklist:

### **Core Body Measurements (Taken Every Fitting)**
1. **Bust** (at fullest part, under arms)  
2. **Natural Waist** (the narrowest part, not the belt line)  
3. **Hips** (widest part, 1–2 inches below the waist)  

---

### **Essential Measurements (For Better Fits)**
- **Sleeve Length** (from shoulder point to wrist)  
- **Shoulder Width** (across upper back, from outer shoulder edge to edge)  
- **Front Rise** (distance from crotch seam to waistband)  
- **Back Rise** (distance from waistband to crotch seam)  

---

### **Advanced Measurements (For Fitted Garments)**
- **Thigh Length** (from top of thigh to bottom seam)  
- **Bicep** (at fullest part, from shoulder to wrist)  
- **Hip Width** (for structured skirts/dresses)  

---

### **Special Considerations**  
For **tall clients** (like your 6'4" neighbor):  
- **Back Rise** is critical – ensure it’s 1–2 inches longer than front rise to prevent sagging.  
- **Inseam** may need adjustment (e.g., shortening if he wears sneakers and wants a "grazing" hem). *Always test with the client’s shoes!*  
- **Hip/Ease** – Add 1–2 inches of ease in hips/thighs for comfort when sitting.  

---

### **Pro Tips**  
- **Use a tape measure** (not a ruler) for accuracy.  
- **Pin fabric to the client** after taking measurements for a true "mock-up" fit.  
- **Mark grain lines** to ensure symmetry.
