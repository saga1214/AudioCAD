Right, let's break this down. First, we need to identify the instrument. Is it an acoustic or an electric guitar? That'll change things a bit.

If you have an acoustic guitar, you'll need to make sure it's in tune, usually by pressing down on the strings and plucking them to hear the pitch. For an electric guitar, you'll need to plug it into an amplifier to hear the sound clearly.

Once you have the right tool—be it an app or a tuner—just strum a string and listen to the pitch. You can adjust the string tension until it matches the note you're aiming for. It's a good idea to go through each string one by one.

And hey, if you're feeling adventurous, you could also try humming a note into your phone's tuner app. That's a pretty cool way to tune without having to play anything at all!
