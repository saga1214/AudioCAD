Okay, let's talk about that. If you have both the text and the illustrations ready, you have a huge advantage. You can submit a complete package. However, the publisher will almost certainly still hire their *own* illustrator. The reason is that they want a fresh perspective and a new style for the book. But, your illustrations will be a fantastic demonstration of your talent, and it will definitely help your manuscript stand out.

So, how to get started? Your first step is to write a professional query letter and a polished manuscript. After that, you'll need to research children's book agents. You can use a website called 'AgentQuery dot com' to find a list of agents who are currently accepting submissions. It's also a good idea to check the children's book section of 'The New York Times Book Review' to see who they've been featuring recently. Then, you'll send out your query. Just be patient, as it can take a long time to hear back.
