Here's a complete shopping list for your 3-course meal, organized by course, with all ingredients and essentials:

### **Starter: Miso Soup & Cucumber Salad**
- **Miso Soup:**
  - Dried wakame seaweed
  - Shiitake mushrooms
  - Enoki mushrooms
  - Dashi powder or instant dashi soup base
  - White miso paste (or red miso, if preferred)
  - Water
  - Soy sauce (optional, for seasoning)
- **Cucumber Salad:**
  - Cucumber
  - Rice vinegar
  - Sugar
  - Salt
  - Sesame seeds
  - Toasted sesame oil

### **Main Course: Chicken Teriyaki**
- **Chicken:**
  - Boneless, skinless chicken thighs (or breasts)
  - Cooking oil
- **Teriyaki Sauce:**
  - Soy sauce
  - Mirin (or substitute 1 tbsp rice vinegar + 1 tbsp sugar)
  - Sugar
  - Water
  - Grated ginger
  - Minced garlic
  - Rice vinegar (optional, for tang)
- **Sides:**
  - Steamed jasmine rice
  - Bok choy or other greens
  - Bell peppers (for stir-fry)

### **Dessert: Mango Sticky Rice**
- **Sticky Rice:**
  - Jasmine sticky rice (pre-cooked or quick-cook)
  - Coconut milk (canned or carton)
  - Sugar or palm sugar
  - Water
- **Toppings:**
  - Fresh mango (ripe, sliced)
  - Toasted sesame seeds
  - Coconut flakes (optional)

### **Pantry Essentials**
- Soy sauce (for all dishes)
- Toasted sesame oil
- Rice vinegar
- Sugar
- Salt
- Cooking oil
- Dashi powder or instant dashi soup base

### **Notes:**
1. **Allergies/Sensitivities:**  
   - No legumes (soy sauce is safe if no soy allergy).  
   - No seafood (miso soup uses *dashi*, which is fish-based—**use a vegetarian dashi substitute** if needed).  
2. **Simplifications:**  
   - Use pre-cooked sticky rice if short on time.  
   - Substitute mirin with 1 tbsp rice vinegar + 1 tbsp sugar.  
   - Frozen mango works for sticky rice.  
3. **Pro Tip:**  
   - Prep teriyaki sauce while cooking chicken to save time.  
   - Slice mango while chicken rests.  

Enjoy your delicious, allergy-friendly feast! 🥢🍛🥭
