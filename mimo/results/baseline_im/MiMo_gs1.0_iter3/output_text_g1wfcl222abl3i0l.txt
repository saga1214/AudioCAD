I'm so sorry your glue gun isn't working! That's the worst timing when you're ready to bring your spooky vision to life. Let's troubleshoot that first, then I'll give you all the creative inspiration for your decorations!

**Glue Gun Troubleshooting:**

1.  **Check Power:** Is it plugged in securely? Try a different outlet if possible. Is it a battery-powered model? Check the batteries and try new ones.
2.  **Check Temperature:** Glue guns need time to heat up. If it's brand new, it might take 15-30 minutes. If it's been sitting unused, it likely needs a full cycle. **Do not force it.** Let it warm up fully.
3.  **Check Cord/Tip:** Ensure the cord isn't damaged or tangled. Look closely at the tip – is it cracked or melted? If so, it might be damaged.
4.  **Check Glue:** Is the glue stick fully loaded? Is it the correct type for your gun (usually sold in sticks)? Is it old? Glue sticks lose effectiveness over time.
5.  **Check Bulb (if applicable):** Some glue guns have a heating element bulb. If it's older than 2-3 years, it *might* need replacing (this is less common but possible).

**While it warms up, let's brainstorm your spooky decorations! Your ideas are fantastic:**

*   **Masquerade Masks:** Yes! Black lace or even slightly tattered velvet masks are perfect. You could even find tiny plastic or felt masks painted with subtle spooky designs (like a single eye or fangs).
*   **Dark Flowers:** Deep purple, black, or even dark burgundy roses, peonies, or even faux spider lilies would be stunning. You could add a few small, dark green faux leaves for texture.
*   **Glitter Accents:** Adding dark glitter (black, gunmetal, deep purple, or even iridescent black) around the edges of the flower petals, along the stems, or even *inside* the pumpkin carvings (if they have an opening) is a brilliant touch. It catches the fairy lights beautifully.
*   **Other Spooky Elements:**
    *   **Tiny "Bones":** Add small, flat faux bone pieces (like vertebrae or ribs) around the base or nestled near the pumpkins.
    *   **"Blood" Drips:** Use dark red, black, or maroon craft glue to create dripping effects on the glass, base, or on the stems/leaves. Let some dry clear for contrast.
    *   **Mini Skeletons:** Tiny plastic or resin skeletons (like the "Spooktacular" ones from Michaels) placed near the pumpkins or clustered together on the base.
    *   **Fairy Lights:** Make sure they are secure! You could wrap the wires subtly around the pumpkin stems or base, or tuck them into any moss or faux leaves you add.
    *   **Moss & Lichen:** Add a layer of faux moss or even tiny patches of dark green lichen around the base or on the "dead" pumpkins for an ancient, buried feel.
    *   **Mini "Candle" Holders:** If you want to replace or supplement the fairy lights, add tiny holders for faux candles (battery-operated or LED tea lights) near the pumpkins or mask bases.
