Okay, so you're thinking about combining the desert adventure with a Vegas nightcap. That's a solid plan. For a proper Vegas experience, you could do a mix of classic stuff and something more unique to your vibe. 

If you want that classic Vegas feel, hitting up a big casino like the Bellagio or Cosmopolitan for a drink or dinner is a must. You can check out a show—maybe a Cirque du Soleil or a comedy club if you're both into that. There's also the typical late-night scene, so if you're up for it, hitting up a club or a bar like Omnia could be fun. 

If you want something a bit different, you could check out some of the more quirky spots like the Neon Boneyard for a walk down memory lane, or even the Pinball Hall of Fame for a casual game. And if you're feeling adventurous, maybe a helicopter tour over the Strip at night would be cool. 

Just a thought—Vegas is all about setting the mood, so whatever you do, make sure it's something you two will both enjoy.
