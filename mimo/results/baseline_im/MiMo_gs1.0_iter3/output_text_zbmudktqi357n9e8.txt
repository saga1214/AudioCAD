Okay, so you're planning a trip to Mexico for some painting inspiration and have a few dollars to spare. That's a fantastic idea! Mexico is an incredible place for artists, especially with its vibrant colors, rich cultural scenes, and diverse landscapes. 

First off, Mexico has so many different regions to explore. You've got the bustling cities like Mexico City, which are full of art galleries, street performers, and street art scenes. Then there's Oaxaca, which is famous for its indigenous cultures, Day of the Dead celebrations, and the stunning colors of the mountains. For a more relaxed vibe, you could head to the beaches in the Yucatan Peninsula, like Tulum or Playa del Carmen, which are great for painting the ocean and the local life. 

Now, about the budget part. Mexico is actually quite affordable for an artist. You can find reasonably priced hostels, guesthouses, and even homestays. Street food is a great way to save money and get delicious local flavors. And of course, painting supplies can be found at local art stores, which are usually cheaper than in touristy areas. 

As for the best time to go, it really depends on what you're looking for. The spring, from March to May, is generally the best time to visit. The weather is warm but not too hot, and it's less crowded than the peak summer season. The autumn months, from September to November, are also good, especially for the Day of the Dead in Oaxaca. But if you don't mind the heat, winter, from December to February, is also a popular time to visit. 

So, I'd say go for it! Mexico is a perfect place to get inspired and practice your watercolor techniques. Just remember to pack light and bring plenty of watercolors and paper. And don't be afraid to ask locals for recommendations on where to find the best painting spots. Happy painting!
