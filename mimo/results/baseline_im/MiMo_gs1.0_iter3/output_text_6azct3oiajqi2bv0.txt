That's a smart approach! Since Nordstrom Rack ships directly to your home, here's how to pack it carefully to protect the jacket lining:

1.  **Clean & Dry:** Ensure the jacket is clean and completely dry before packing.
2.  **Fold, Don't Stuff:** Fold the jacket neatly to its natural shape. Avoid overstuffing.
3.  **Protect the Lining:** Pay special attention to the collar, cuffs, and hood (if applicable). Fold these gently and tuck them inside the main body of the jacket. Avoid bunching.
4.  **Padding is Key:**
    *   **Bubble Wrap:** Wrap the *entire* jacket, paying extra attention to seams and vulnerable areas like the collar and cuffs. Don't just wrap the outside; tuck some inside the collar.
    *   **Tissue Paper:** Place layers of soft tissue paper inside the jacket, especially around the collar, cuffs, and hood, to cushion these areas and prevent shifting.
    *   **Padding the Box:** Line the bottom and sides of the shipping box generously with bubble wrap or crumpled paper. Place the jacket inside and fill *all* remaining empty space completely with more padding material. A jacket shouldn't be able to move at all inside the box.
5.  **Seal Securely:** Use strong packing tape to seal the box securely, especially on all edges and flaps.
6.  **Label Clearly:** Write your return address and their address clearly on the package.

**Why this works:** The bubble wrap absorbs impact, while the tissue paper prevents the jacket fabric from rubbing directly against the box or itself, which is what tears linings. Padding the entire box eliminates movement.

**Bonus Tip:** Nordstrom Rack ships via USPS, UPS, or FedEx. While they use sturdy boxes, careful packing is still your best defense for fragile items like jackets with delicate linings. If the jacket is very bulky, it might be worth considering a slightly larger box (if available at your local post office) to provide more internal space and prevent compression.
