Alright, for the 'Retro Tech' theme, I've got two meal ideas that are simple and fun. First up, a 'Dial-Up Dinner.' For the main course, you could do a 'modem bowl,' which is like a deconstructed mac and cheese with breadcrumbs on top, and you can add optional 'data packets' like peas or carrots. For the side, 'floppy disk' sandwiches are perfect—you just fold a piece of bread in half and put it on the plate. And for a snack, some 'RAM chips,' which are just pretzel sticks. The second idea is 'Vintage Vibe Bites.' This could be a 'retro fridge' snack platter, which is basically a charcuterie board with old-school snacks like Cheez Whiz, crackers, and gushers. For the main, 'floppy disk' fruit skewers are great—you just put berries on a small plate shaped like a floppy disk. For drinks, you could have 'modem soup,' which is just tomato soup from a can, or 'dial-up' lemonade, which is just lemonade with some fun retro tech stickers. These ideas are easy, inclusive, and fit the theme.
