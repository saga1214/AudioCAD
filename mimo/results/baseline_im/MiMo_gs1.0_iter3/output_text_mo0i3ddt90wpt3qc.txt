Here are a few delicious, gluten-free snack ideas perfect for your trip, building on your SkinnyPop and Popchips favorites:

1.  **Trail Mix Power Pack:** Combine raw nuts (almonds, walnuts, pecans), seeds (pumpkin, sunflower), unsweetened coconut flakes, and maybe a few dark chocolate chips (check labels!). Pre-portion into small bags for easy snacking.
2.  **Rice Cake Creations:** Rice cakes are naturally gluten-free. Top them with:
    *   Hummus and sliced cucumber/tomato
    *   Nut butter (peanut, almond) and banana slices
    *   Salsa and guacamole
    *   Tuna salad or cottage cheese
3.  **Fruit Leather & Nut Butter Packets:** Choose fruit-flavored leathers (like strawberry or mango) and pack single-serve nut butter packets. Easy, no-prep, and satisfying.
4.  **Roasted Chickpeas:** Crunchy, protein-packed, and come in savory flavors like rosemary garlic or smoked paprika. Look for certified gluten-free brands.
5.  **Energy Balls:** Make your own or buy certified GF ones. Blend oats (ensure GF certified), nut butter, honey/maple syrup, seeds (chia, flax, sunflower), and add-ins like dried fruit or cacao nibs. Roll into balls.

**Key Tip:** Always double-check labels for "may contain" warnings or hidden gluten ingredients (like malt vinegar, soy sauce, or wheat flour) even in seemingly safe snacks like chips or trail mix. Enjoy your adventure!
