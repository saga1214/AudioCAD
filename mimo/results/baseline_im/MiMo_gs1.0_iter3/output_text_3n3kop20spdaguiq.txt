Oh, that's a great question! Dehydrating is such a fun hobby. You can make all sorts of things. For fruits, you can make apple chips, banana chips, mango slices, and even dried cranberries or cherries. For vegetables, you can do the same, like zucchini chips or bell pepper strips. You can also make jerky from meats like beef or turkey. And of course, you can make your own dried herbs like basil or oregano. The possibilities are really endless.
