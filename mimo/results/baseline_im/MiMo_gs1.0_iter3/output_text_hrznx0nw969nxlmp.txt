That's a fantastic choice! Since you're staying at Secret Harbour, here are some **daytime activities** perfect for your trip, balancing adventure, relaxation, and your brother's needs:

### 🌊 Water Adventures (No Pressure Changes)
1. **Snorkeling at Trunk Bay (St. John)**  
   - Just a short boat ride from Secret Harbour.  
   - Features an underwater walkway, vibrant coral reefs, and sea turtles.  
   - *Why it’s great*: Gentle currents, crystal-clear water, and minimal depth changes.

2. **Kayaking at Coki Point (St. John)**  
   - Paddle along the coast to discover tide pools, sea grapes, and nesting seabirds (like brown boobies!).  
   - *Bonus*: Stop at Coki Beach for a swim in calm shallows.

3. **Sailing or Catamaran Tour**  
   - Explore Virgin Islands National Park (Soggy Dollar Bar is iconic!).  
   - *Tip*: Opt for a **shallow-water cove** tour to avoid pressure changes.

### 🌿 Nature & Relaxation
4. **Hike to Anegada Beach (St. Thomas)**  
   - Take a short bus ride to this "Prettiest Beach in the Caribbean."  
   - Paddleboard or relax on powdery white sand (no waves!).  
   - *Pro tip*: Visit at sunrise for solitude.

5. **Birdwatching at Coki Point (St. John)**  
   - Spot **Antillean Crested Hummingbirds**, **Brown Boobies**, and **Rough-Tailed Nightjars**.  
   - Best at dawn or dusk in the mangroves.

### 🎉 Local Vibes
6. **Explore Red Hook (St. Thomas)**  
   - Walk to **Cruz Bay** for shops, cafes, and the historic **Cruz Bay Lighthouse**.  
   - *Food stop*: **Cruzer’s Café** for local treats.

7. **Visit the Virgin Islands National Park Visitor Center**  
   - Learn about marine life, bird species, and conservation.  
   - Free entry, and they have maps/tour info.

### ⚠️ Key Considerations for Your Brother
- **Avoid Submersion**: Stick to snorkeling in shallow areas or kayaking.  
- **Stabilize Ears**: Humming or swallowing can help if pressure changes occur.  
- **Stay Hydrated**: Saltwater can be dehydrating!

### 🚤 Boat-Based Tip
Ask your captain about **shallow-water excursions**—many charters offer "beach hangs" where you can swim in calm, shallow coves. Perfect for avoiding pressure changes while soaking up the sun!

Enjoy your trip—St. John’s Secret Harbour is a magical base for exploring the islands’ beauty! 🌴✨
