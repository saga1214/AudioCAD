That's a good goal. You want to be professional and clear without being confrontational. Here are a few key points to include in your email.

First, start by thanking him for the feedback. You could say something like, 'Thank you for providing the feedback on my exam.' This shows respect.

Next, state your purpose clearly. For example, 'I'm writing to request some clarification on my exam grade. I'd like to understand what I could do better for the next assessment.'

Then, you can mention your desire to learn, which is the core of your approach. You could add, 'I'm really interested in learning from this experience to improve in your class.'

Finally, and this is important, you can mention your other grades to provide context. Something like, 'My performance in other classes has been strong, so I was surprised by this grade and wanted to understand more.'

This way, you're focused on your own growth and the content of the grade, not on blaming him. That should set a good tone for the conversation.
