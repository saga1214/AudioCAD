Here's a step-by-step guide for installing each item, prioritizing safety, stability, and noise compliance:

### 1. **TV Mount Installation**
   - **Tools Needed:** Level, stud finder, drill, screws (if using drywall anchors), screwdriver.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Find Studs:** Use a stud finder to locate wall studs (essential for heavy mounts). If no studs, use heavy-duty drywall anchors.
     2. **Measure & Mark:** Measure the TV’s width and center it on the wall. Mark the exact spot for the mount’s top/bottom brackets.
     3. **Pre-Drill (if needed):** For drywall anchors, pre-drill holes slightly smaller than the anchor’s diameter.
     4. **Secure Mount:** Attach the top and bottom brackets to the wall, aligning with stud positions. Use a level to ensure straightness.
     5. **Mount TV:** Secure the TV to the mount using included hardware. Test stability before hanging.

   **Tip:** Use a full-motion mount for flexibility. Ensure the mount’s weight capacity matches your TV’s specs.

---

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Tools Needed:** Drill, hooks/brackets (rated for weight), screws, measuring tape.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Choose Location:** Install near windows for light. Ensure no electrical wires are present.
     2. **Select Hardware:** Use heavy-duty hooks or ceiling-mounted brackets rated for the planter’s weight (e.g., 25+ lbs). For drywall, use toggle bolts.
     3. **Mark & Drill:** Measure and mark the hook/bracket position. Drill a hole slightly larger than the hardware.
     4. **Secure Hardware:** Insert the hook/bracket and screw it tightly. Ensure it’s flush with the wall.
     5. **Hang Planters:** Clip the planter hanger to the hook and hang the planter.

   **Tip:** For ceiling plants, use a chain or wire to hang them from the hook—avoid overhanging the planter to prevent sagging.

---

### 3. **Floating Shelves**
   - **Tools Needed:** Level, drill, shelf brackets (hidden or visible), screws, measuring tape.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Plan Layout:** Decide shelf height and spacing. Use painter’s tape to mark the top/bottom edges.
     2. **Locate Studs:** Place brackets into studs for maximum support. If no studs, use heavy-duty drywall anchors.
     3. **Attach Brackets:** Drill pilot holes, then screw brackets into studs/anchors. Use a level to ensure even alignment.
     4. **Install Shelf:** Slide the shelf into the brackets and secure with hidden brackets or decorative trim.

   **Tip:** For heavy items (art, books), use brackets rated for 50+ lbs. Add a second bracket for extra security.

---

### **Key Safety & Noise Tips:**
- **Check for Wires/Pipes:** Always drill into studs/ceilings to avoid hitting wires or plumbing. Use a stud finder with wire detection.
- **Noise Control:** Use a handheld drill to minimize noise. Avoid drilling during quiet hours (e.g., 8–10 AM).
- **Weight Limits:** Never exceed hardware or mount ratings. Test stability after installation.
- **Aesthetics:** Use decorative hardware (e.g., brass hooks) or hidden brackets for a cleaner look.

By following these steps, you’ll ensure secure, noise-compliant installations that elevate your space without compromising safety! 🛠️✨
