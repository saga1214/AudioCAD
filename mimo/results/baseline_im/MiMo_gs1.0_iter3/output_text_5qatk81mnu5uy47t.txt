Absolutely! Planning an unforgettable dive trip to **Costa Rica** is an excellent choice—its tropical waters, vibrant marine life, and diverse dive sites cater perfectly to enthusiasts like you and your husband. Here’s a tailored itinerary to make it magical:

### 🌴 **Costa Rica Dive Trip Itinerary (2 Weeks)**
#### **Days 1–3: Arrival in Puerto Viejo (Tortuguero)**
- **Focus:** Easy, accessible diving & jungle immersion  
- **Dive Sites:**  
  - **Rincón de la Vieja** (shallow reefs, snorkeling-friendly)  
  - **Cocora Reef** (coral gardens, turtles, and rays)  
- **Why?**  
  - Perfect for beginners—calm waters, minimal currents.  
  - Accessible via boat from Puerto Viejo.  
- **Stay:** Eco-lodge in Tortuguero (jungle/oceanfront).  

#### **Days 4–7: Monteverde (Tortuguero) → Playa Conchal (Aguas Calientes)**
- **Focus:** Mixed diving & adventure  
- **Dive Sites:**  
  - **Sirena** (caves, wrecks, and strong currents—advanced)  
  - **Playa Conchal** (coral walls, schools of fish)  
  - **Flamenco Beach** (snorkeling with sea turtles)  
- **Why?**  
  - Playa Conchal offers easy shore dives and boat trips.  
  - Flamenco Beach is one of the world’s best snorkeling spots.  
- **Stay:** Beachfront hotel in Aguas Calientes.  

#### **Days 8–10: Cocos Island (Liveaboard)**
- **Focus:** Epic adventure diving  
- **Dive Sites:**  
  - **Chira** (shipwreck, giant rays, mantas)  
  - **Laguna Grande** (coral walls, night dives)  
  - **Cocos Wall** (30m+ depth, tropical fish)  
- **Why?**  
  - World-class sites with incredible biodiversity.  
  - Requires a liveaboard boat (4-day trip recommended).  
- **Stay:** Dive lodge on the island.  

#### **Days 11–12: Return to Puerto Viejo → Departure**
- **Relax:** Enjoy local cuisine, waterfall hikes, or kayaking.
