Okay, I think a really great option for your family trip would be the Big Island of Hawaii. It has everything you're looking for: warm weather, amazing swimming, and plenty of things to do. I'll lay out a full plan for you.

First, let's talk about where to stay. For your first two nights, I'd suggest staying in Hilo. It's a great base for exploring Volcanoes National Park, and it's also close to the beautiful Keauhou Bay. For the next two nights, I'd recommend moving to the Kona Coast, maybe near the Kona Coast Hotel & Resort. That way, you can enjoy the sunny beaches and go whale watching.

For your itinerary, here's a sample plan. On Day 1, you could arrive in Hilo in the afternoon, then check into your hotel. In the evening, you could go for a lovely dinner at Hula Grill, which is right on the Hilo Bay and has a great view. 

Day 2 is a big one: Volcanoes National Park. In the morning, you can explore the Volcanoes National Park. You'll want to see the Kīlauea Caldera and the Thurston Lava Tube. In the afternoon, you could head to the Hilo Farmers Market, which is a great experience. For dinner, you could try a place called Sushi Hilo for some fresh sushi.

On Day 3, you'll drive to the Kona Coast. In the morning, you could stop at the Garden of Eden, which is a beautiful botanical garden. In the afternoon, you could relax on the beaches of Kailua-Kona. For dinner, you could go to Mauna Kea Beach Hotel, which has an oceanfront restaurant.

Day 4 is for whale watching. You can take a tour in the morning. In the afternoon, you could visit the Kona Coffee Cultural Center. For your last dinner, you could try Hula Bay, which is right on the beach.

Finally, on Day 5, you can drive back to Hilo in the morning to catch your flight home.

As for things to do, besides the big ones I mentioned, you could also go snorkeling at Keauhou Bay, take a sunrise hike in Akaka Falls, or visit the Pu’uhonua o Hōnaunau National Historical Park.

For places to eat, besides the ones I've mentioned, you could also check out Mama’s Fish House in Kailua-Kona, or Hali’imaile General Store in Waikoloa. 

To get there, you'll fly into the Hilo Airport, and you'll probably want to rent a car for your trip.

This itinerary should give you a great mix of adventure, relaxation, and cultural experiences, all while making sure your daughter's dietary needs are met. Enjoy your trip!
