That's a great question! For truly fast dinners, I'd suggest focusing on **pantry staples and pre-prepped ingredients**:

1.  **Eggs:** The ultimate quick meal. Scrambled, fried, or boiled take minutes. Pair with toast, rice, or leftover veggies.
2.  **Canned Beans/Lentils:** Rinse and warm. Add to pasta, salads, or make a quick dip with Greek yogurt and spices.
3.  **Frozen Veggies:** No chopping needed! Toss directly into stir-fries, omelets, pasta sauces, or microwave for a quick side.
4.  **Canned Tuna/Salmon:** Mix with mayo, mustard, or Greek yogurt, add celery/onion, and serve on crackers or in lettuce cups.
5.  **Rice/Pasta:** Cooked ahead or use instant versions. Toss with any protein (chicken, tofu, beans) and frozen veggies. Add soy sauce, garlic powder, or pre-made sauces.
6.  **Pre-Cooked Grains:** Quinoa, couscous, or pre-cooked rice from the freezer section. Mix with beans, veggies, and a simple dressing.
7.  **Pre-Cut Veggie Kits:** Minimal prep for stir-fries, salads, or roasting.
8.  **Pre-Made Sauces:** Marinara for pasta, teriyaki for stir-fries, or pesto for pasta/grain bowls.
9.  **Frozen Shrimp:** Cooks in minutes. Sauté with garlic, lemon, and frozen peas for a quick "scampi" style.
10. **Breakfast for Dinner:** Omelets, egg muffins, or cereal with yogurt and fruit.

**Key Strategy:** Combine 1-2 of these elements quickly. Example: "Canned tuna + frozen peas + pre-cooked rice + simple dressing" = a 10-minute meal.

**Remember:** Even if you have unused spices, a pinch of salt, pepper, garlic powder, and soy sauce can often do the trick for simple meals. The goal is speed and ease, not complexity!
