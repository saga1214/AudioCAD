    *   **Infused Water:** Beyond lemon/cucumber, try:

        *   **Citrus & Cucumber:** Classic combo.
        *   **Orange & Ginger:** Bright and warming.
    *   **Small Fruit Skewers:** Melon balls, grapes, berries – easy to grab.
    *   **Light Crackers:** Rice cakes, plain crackers with a tiny dab of hummus or guacamole.
    *   **Fresh Fruit:** Apple slices, orange segments, banana chunks.
    *   **Cucumber Slices:** Simple, cool, hydrating.

3.  **Pairings with Citrus & Chocolate:**
    *   **Citrus:** Pairs beautifully with:
        *   **Herbs:** Rosemary, thyme, basil (in infused water or small garnishes).

        *   **Berries:** Raspberry, strawberry, blackberry (fresh or frozen in water).

    *   **Chocolate:** Pairs wonderfully with:
        *   **Citrus:** Orange, lemon, raspberry.

        *   **Nutty Flavors:** Hazelnut, almond (if safe), peanut butter (if safe).
        *   **Coffee/Espresso:** A small, strong coffee or espresso shot can be a great chocolate pairing (if caffeine is okay).
        *   **Vanilla:** Classic pairing.

4.  **Bright & Refreshing Vibes:**

    *   **Scents (for ambiance):** Lightly scented candles or diffusers with notes of citrus (bergamot, lemon), mint (if you like it), cucumber, or eucalyptus. Keep it subtle!
    *   **Decor:** Use clear glass bottles/jars, vibrant but clean-colored napkins (white, light blue, pale green), small potted succulents or eucalyptus sprigs, water bottles with custom labels ("Hydrate & Energize!").
