Here's how to tune your instrument (focusing on acoustic guitar, as it's the most common):  

### **For Acoustic Guitar (Standard Tuning: E-A-D-G-B-E)**  
1. **Use a tuner app** (e.g., GuitarTuna, Fender Tune) or a clip-on tuner.  
2. **Pluck each string** while holding it down at the 12th fret.  
3. **Match the pitch** to the tuner's display:  
   - **Low E (6th string):** Tune to **E** (like the open 6th string).  
   - **A (5th string):** Tune to **A** (use the 4th string as a reference).  
   - **D (4th string):** Tune to **D** (use the 2nd string as a reference).  
   - **G (3rd string):** Tune to **G** (use the 3rd string as a reference).  
   - **B (2nd string):** Tune to **B** (use the 1st string as a reference).  
   - **High E (1st string):** Tune to **E** (like the open 1st string).  

### **For Electric Guitar (Standard Tuning: E-A-D-G-B-E)**  
1. **Use a tuner app or clip-on tuner.**  
2. **Pluck each string** and tune to **E** (same as acoustic's 6th string).  

### **Pro Tips**  
- **Check tuning stability:** Pluck each string 3-4 times after tuning.  
- **Avoid over-tightening:** Stop when the tuner shows a stable pitch.  
- **Use a reference string:** Tune to the open 6th string (E) first, then adjust others.  

### **Troubleshooting**  
- **If a string won’t tune?** Check for kinks or damage.  
- **If the tuner is inaccurate?** Ensure you’re holding the string firmly at the 12th fret.
