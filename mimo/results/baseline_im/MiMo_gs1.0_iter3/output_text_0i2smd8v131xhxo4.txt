**2. Reliable AI Tools:**
*   **Adobe Audition (Built-in):** Its "Clean Audio" workspace (AI-powered noise reduction, de-essing, vocal isolation) and "Auto-Tune" (for pitch correction) are surprisingly good and integrated.
*   **iZotope RX (Industry Standard):** Its AI-powered tools (De-click, De-reverb, De-hum, Music Rebalance) are incredibly effective for cleaning up audio. The free " RX Elements" is a great starting point.
*   **Descript (For Editing & Labeling):** Its "Overdub" feature (AI-powered filler words/umms removal) and powerful editing via text transcription are game-changers. Excellent for labeling clips within the transcript.
*   **Adobe Audition's Project Notes:** While not AI, its tagging and note system is excellent for project organization and recalling context.

**3. Balancing Recording, Editing, & Marketing (Avoiding Burnout):**
*   **Time Blocking is Key:** Schedule dedicated blocks for recording, editing, administrative/marketing tasks, and *personal time*. Treat these blocks as unbreakable appointments.
*   **The 20-20-20 Rule:** For every 20 minutes of focused work, take a 20-second break to look at something 20 feet away. Stand up, stretch.
*   **Hydration & Snacks:** Keep water and healthy snacks (nuts, fruit) nearby. Don't let yourself get too hungry or dehydrated – it drastically impacts focus and energy.
*   **Batch Tasks:** Group similar tasks (e.g., record all your narration for one project first, then edit it all, then market it). This reduces context-switching fatigue.
*   **Delegate & Automate:** Can you outsource some marketing tasks (social media scheduling, simple graphic design)? Can you use templates for emails?
*   **Prioritize & Say No:** Focus on high-value projects and clients. Learn to politely decline work that doesn't align with your goals or capacity.
*   **Schedule Meals & Breaks:** **THIS IS CRITICAL.** Set alarms if you need to. Step away from the screen for proper meals and genuine breaks. Your body and brain need fuel and recovery.
