Got it! Here’s a quick and easy way to make both. For the vodka mix, just muddle some fresh berries—like strawberries or raspberries—with the leftover vodka. Add a splash of lemon juice for brightness, and then top it with soda water. Garnish with a mint sprig. Easy, refreshing, and perfect for a crowd. For the Argentine-style Fernet, it's simpler. You just mix equal parts Fernet, soda water, and lime juice. Shake it with ice and serve it in a chilled glass. You can add a few dashes of bitters if you like. Quick, strong, and a great way to end the night. Enjoy the party!
