Of course. Since you're now interested in the history, I'd recommend a few things you can do along your trip.

First, make a point to stop at the Lockport Canal Museum. It's located right in the historic Lockport Canal Basin, which is a National Historic Landmark. The museum has a great exhibit called "Flight of the Five," with a detailed model showing how the locks worked.

Second, you should definitely walk the Erie Canal Heritage Trail. It runs right through Lockport, and you can get a real sense of the city's past as a major hub.

And finally, you might enjoy visiting the Lockport Historical Society. They have a wealth of local history and can give you a much deeper understanding of the canal's importance to the region.
