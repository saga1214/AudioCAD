That's a thoughtful plan! A quick, easy, and customizable dish is perfect for sister bonding. Here are a few ideas that are fast, simple, and can be adapted for dietary needs (like lactose intolerance):

### 🥗 Top Recommendations:
1. **Taco/Burrito Bar**  
   - **Why it works**: Customizable, fun, and low-pressure.  
   - **Quick prep**:  
     - *Protein*: Ground beef (or turkey/chicken if needed), canned beans (lactose-free), or tofu.  
     - *Veggies*: Shredded lettuce, tomatoes, corn (frozen is fine!), avocado.  
     - *Toppings*: Salsa, guacamole, cheese (use lactose-free if needed), sour cream (or coconut yogurt).  
   - **Time**: ~20-30 mins total. Let her build her own to ease tension!

2. **Pasta with Pesto & Veggies**  
   - **Why it works**: Simple, healthy, and veggie-friendly.  
   - **Quick prep**:  
     - Cook pasta (use gluten-free if needed).  
     - Sauté spinach, cherry tomatoes, and mushrooms.  
     - Toss with pesto (dairy-free pesto available) and lemon zest.  
   - **Time**: ~15 mins. Add grilled chicken or shrimp if acceptable.

3. **Quinoa-Stuffed Bell Peppers**  
   - **Why it works**: Nutritious, visually appealing, and veggie-packed (but customizable).  
   - **Quick prep**:  
     - Sauté bell peppers, quinoa, black beans, corn, and diced tomatoes.  
     - Mix with dairy-free cheese (like nutritional yeast or vegan cheese).  
   - **Time**: ~25 mins. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 15 mins.

### 🌟 Key Tips for Success:
- **Prep Ahead**: Chop veggies and measure ingredients before she arrives.  
- **Keep it Light**: Focus on the activity, not the conversation. Laugh about silly mistakes!  
- **Dietary Safety**: Confirm substitutions (e.g., lactose-free cheese, gluten-free pasta) to avoid stress.  
- **Music/Background Noise**: Play a playlist or podcast to fill silences naturally.  

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: End with something cozy—like hot chocolate (with oat milk!) or coffee—while watching the movie. This creates a warm, relaxed vibe.
