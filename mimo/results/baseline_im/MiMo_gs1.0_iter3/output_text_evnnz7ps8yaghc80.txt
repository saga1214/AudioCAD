That's a fantastic idea. Having a consistent schedule is key to making progress. Here is a sample weekly plan that mixes bean bags, scarves, and music.

For the bean bags, I suggest practicing for 20 minutes a day, three times a week. You could do this on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. On Monday, focus on your one-handed two-bag juggling, and time it with music. On Wednesday, practice the first three throws to get ready for the third ball, and maybe try adding the third ball for a few minutes if you feel confident. Then on Friday, you can work on adding a fourth ball, if you're ready.

For the scarves, you can do a 15-minute session twice a week, maybe on Tuesday and Thursday. With the scarves, the goal is to focus on the pattern and the slow, deliberate timing. You can practice casting the scarves and keeping them in a nice circle.

Then, on Saturday, you can do a 25-minute 'play' session where you can mix everything you've been practicing—bean bags, scarves, and music. This is your time to just have fun and not worry about strict progress.

Remember to start with about five minutes of warm-up, like some gentle wrist circles, before each session, and to be kind to yourself if you have an off day. The most important thing is to be consistent. You've got this!
