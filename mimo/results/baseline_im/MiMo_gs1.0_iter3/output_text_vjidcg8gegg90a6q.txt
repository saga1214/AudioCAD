For winter chicks, I would highly recommend using pine shavings. They are an excellent insulator, which is critical for keeping them warm. You can use the same deep litter method in their enclosed area that you planned for the adult chickens. Just add fresh shavings on top of the old ones as you go. This will help maintain warmth and control moisture. I'd advise against using wood chips, as they are often too bulky and can be a choking hazard for young chicks.
