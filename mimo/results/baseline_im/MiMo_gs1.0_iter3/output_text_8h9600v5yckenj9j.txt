For Halloween candy, focus on **widely popular, kid-friendly, and easy-to-dispense options** to minimize leftovers. Here’s a curated list of crowd-pleasers:

### 🍬 Top Crowd-Favorite Candies
1. **Reese's Cups/Pieces**  
   - *Why*: Universal appeal (chocolate + peanut butter), nostalgic, and sturdy.  
2. **M&M's (All Colors)**  
   - *Why*: Fun, colorful, and easy to eat. Avoid "milk chocolate" if allergies are a concern—opt for dark or regular.  
3. **Snickers**  
   - *Why*: Classic combo ( nougat, caramel, chocolate, peanuts) that satisfies sweet and salty cravings.  
4. **Gummies**  
   - *Why*: Universally loved (Haribo, Starbursts, or seasonal Halloween gummies).  
5. **Sour Patch Kids**  
   - *Why*: Surprising flavor (sour outside, sweet inside) is a hit with all ages.  
6. **Mini Chocolate Bars**  
   - *Why*: Milky Way, Twix, or Hershey's Miniatures—compact and satisfying.  

### 🚫 Avoid
- Cheap, stale candy (like rubbery lollipops or poorly wrapped bars).  
- Overly niche flavors (e.g., licorice or coffee-flavored candies).  
- Items with extreme textures (e.g., toffee or brittle) if you suspect texture-sensitive guests.  

### 💡 Pro Tips
- **Bundle & Brand**: Use themed bags or boxes (e.g., "Monster Mash Mix") to make it feel festive.  
- **Seasonal Specials**: Add Halloween-themed candies like **Halloween Hot Tubs** (chocolate cups with candy) or **Candy Corn** (for variety).  
- **Allergy-Friendly**: Include a **small section of allergy-safe candy** (e.g., dark chocolate-covered rice cakes or fruit snacks) labeled clearly.  
- **Dispensing**: Use a **candy bowl with a funnel** or a **plastic dispenser** (like those for candy at fairs) to avoid messes.
