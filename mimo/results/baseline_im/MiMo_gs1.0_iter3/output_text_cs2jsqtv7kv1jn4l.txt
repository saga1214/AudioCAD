Okay, so you're looking to get your daughter a gaming console to play together, especially since online gaming is off the table. That's a great idea for bonding. Here are five of the most popular consoles right now. First, there's the Nintendo Switch, which is great because it works both as a home console and a portable one, so you can play on the TV or take it on the go. It has a huge library of family-friendly games like Mario Kart, Zelda, and Animal Crossing. Second is the PlayStation 5, which is a powerhouse for graphics and fast-paced games. It's best for single-player adventures and racing games. Third is the Xbox Series X or S, which is great for its huge library of games and its Game Pass subscription, which is perfect for trying lots of different titles. Fourth is the Steam Deck, which is a powerful handheld PC that plays thousands of PC games, though it might be a bit more complex for a ten-year-old. And fifth, there's the Nintendo 3DS, which is a bit older but still has classics like Mario Kart 7 and Animal Crossing: New Leaf. It's a good budget option. For a ten-year-old, I'd probably recommend the Switch, the PS5, or the Xbox, as they have the most games she would likely enjoy. The Switch is probably the safest bet for a shared experience.
