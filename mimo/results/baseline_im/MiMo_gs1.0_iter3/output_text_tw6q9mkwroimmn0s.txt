What a wonderful ocean-themed celebration! Here are five delicious, diabetes-friendly, shellfish-free mains, and five sweet desserts perfect for your anniversary night:

**Mains (All can be made diabetes-friendly - focus on fiber, lean protein, healthy fats, and low-glycemic carbs):**

1.  **Lemon-Herb Baked Salmon with Mango Salsa:** (Use wild-caught salmon fillets if possible). Bake seasoned with lemon juice, garlic, dill, parsley, and a pinch of cayenne (adjust for heat preference). Serve with a vibrant salsa of diced mango, red onion, cilantro, lime juice, and a touch of olive oil. *Why it works:* Salmon is rich in omega-3s, the salsa adds sweetness from mango without added sugar, and lemon/herb flavors are bright and satisfying.
2.  **Spicy Coconut Curry with Shrimp-Free Tofu & Vegetables:** Use a rich, flavorful coconut milk-based curry base (ensure no added sugar). Add firm or extra-firm tofu (pressed and cubed or crumbled) and plenty of vegetables like bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas, carrots, and zucchini. Sauté with ginger, garlic, turmeric, cumin, and chili paste or fresh chilies for spice. Serve over cauliflower rice or a small portion of brown rice/quinoa. *Why it works:* Tofu provides plant-based protein, coconut milk adds richness without dairy, veggies add fiber, and the spices make it exciting.
3.  **Mango & Black Bean Stuffed Portobellos:** Roast large portobello mushroom caps. Fill with a mixture of black beans (rinsed and drained), cooked quinoa or brown rice, diced mango, corn kernels, red onion, cilantro, cumin, chili powder, lime juice, and a little olive oil. Top with avocado slices before serving. *Why it works:* Mushrooms are meaty and low-carb, beans and quinoa provide protein and fiber, mango adds sweetness, and spices make it flavorful.
4.  **Seared Scallops with Mango-Avocado Salsa & Citrus Salad:** Use large, fresh sea scallops (not shellfish, they are different). Sear quickly in a hot pan with butter/oil until golden. Serve on a bed of mixed greens with segments of orange or grapefruit, a few pomegranate seeds, and a light vinaigrette. Garnish with the mango-avocado salsa. *Why it works:* Scallops are delicate and luxurious, the mango-avocado salsa adds sweetness and creaminess, citrus salad is refreshing and low-glycemic.
5.  **"Crab" Cake with Lemon-Dill Remoulade:** Make classic crab cakes using lump crabmeat (ensure it's pure crab, no shellfish pieces!) mixed with finely chopped egg whites, almond flour (or gluten-free breadcrumbs), finely chopped celery, red onion, bell pepper, fresh dill, parsley, lemon juice, and a touch of mustard. Bake or pan-fry until crispy. Serve with a homemade lemon-dill remoulade (use mayonnaise, mustard, lemon juice, dill, a little capers, and a pinch of cayenne). *Why it works:* Crabmeat provides a luxurious texture, almond flour keeps it low-carb, veggies add nutrients, and the remoulade is flavorful without heavy mayo.

**Desserts (Sweet, Light, and Diabetes-Friendly):**

1.  **Mango Sorbet with Fresh Berries:** Find or make a high-quality sorbet made primarily from mango puree and a touch of lime juice. Serve in small glasses topped with fresh berries (raspberries, blueberries, strawberries). *Why it works:* Sorbet is mostly fruit puree and ice, very low in sugar/carbs naturally. Mango is sweet, berries add tartness and antioxidants.
2.  **Chia Seed Pudding with Mango & Lime:** Whisk chia seeds into unsweetened almond milk (or coconut milk) with a touch of vanilla extract and a *tiny* bit of stevia or monk fruit if needed. Refrigerate overnight. Layer with fresh mango chunks and a squeeze of lime juice before serving. *Why it works:* Chia seeds provide fiber and healthy fats, almond/coconut milk is low-carb, and fresh mango/lime make it sweet and refreshing.
3.  **Berry & Lime Cheesecake Bites:** Use a low-sugar graham cracker crust (or almond flour crust) and a cheesecake filling made with full-fat cream cheese, eggs, a *small* amount of sugar substitute (like erythritol or stevia), lemon juice, and fresh lime zest. Top each bite with fresh raspberries or blueberries. *Why it works:* Cream cheese provides richness and protein, berries add sweetness and fiber, and the sugar substitute keeps it low-glycemic.
4.  **Lime & Coconut Panna Cotta with Mango Compote:** Make a silky panna cotta using coconut milk, gelatin, a touch of sweetener (like stevia or monk fruit), and plenty of fresh lime zest and juice. Serve topped with a simple compote of diced mango simmered lightly with a little lime juice and a tiny pinch of sweetener. *Why it works:* Coconut milk is rich and creamy, lime adds freshness, mango compote provides sweetness, and minimal sweetener keeps it friendly.
5.  **Dark Chocolate-Dipped Strawberries with Sea Salt:** Melt high-percentage dark chocolate (70% or higher cacao) and dip fresh strawberries halfway. Place on parchment paper and sprinkle lightly with flaky sea salt before the chocolate sets. Chill briefly. *Why it works:* Dark chocolate is lower in sugar and higher in antioxidants than milk chocolate. Strawberries add natural sweetness and fiber. Sea salt enhances flavor. Keep portions small!
