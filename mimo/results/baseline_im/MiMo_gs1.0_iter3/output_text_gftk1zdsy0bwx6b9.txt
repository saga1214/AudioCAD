For the gingerbread house kits, you could just include them with the Gingerbread cookies. That way, your friends can build a house with the cookie that shares the same theme.

And if you want to share with other friends, I'd suggest including one Gingerbread tin and one Snickerdoodle tin. These are both really popular flavors, so it's a good way to share without repeating the same cookies.

And one last tip: since gingerbread dough needs to be refrigerated, make sure to pack it well to avoid any breakage. You could even wrap it in plastic wrap before putting it in the tin.
