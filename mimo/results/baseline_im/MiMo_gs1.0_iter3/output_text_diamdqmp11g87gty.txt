That's awesome! Snowshoeing is a fantastic winter activity—especially in Wisconsin. Here’s what you’ll need and some other fun winter adventures:

### 🎣 **Snowshoeing Gear Essentials**
1. **Snowshoes**: Rent or buy beginner-friendly ones (look for compact frames and wide treads).  
2. **Poles**: Adjustable trekking poles improve stability.  
3. **Clothing**:  
   - Moisture-wicking base layers  
   - Insulating mid-layers (fleece or down)  
   - Windproof/waterproof outer shell  
   - Gloves, hat, and thermal socks (freezing hands/toes = game over!)  
4. **Safety Gear**:  
   - Map/GPS (snowshoe trails can be hidden)  
   - Headlamp (for unexpected detours)  
   - First-aid kit and whistle  

### 🔍 **Where to Start**
- **Rent first**: Try local shops like **Snowshoe Hounds** (Madison) or **Wintergreen Sports** (Green Bay).  
- **Beginner Trails**:  
  - **Kettle Moraine State Forest** (WI) – marked groomed trails  
  - **Big Foot Trails** (Door County) – scenic coastal paths  

### ❄️ **Other Winter Adventures**
1. **Ice Fishing**:  
   -租冰钓屋或便携冰钓帐篷。  
   -装备：冰钻、鱼探仪、鱼竿（推荐荧光饵）。  
   -地点：**Lake Michigan** (门罗县) 或 **Prairie du Chien**。  

2. **Winter Hiking**:  
   - 选择有雪链要求的步道，如 **Driftless Area** 的冻原小径。  
   - 用雪鞋（或租用雪地靴）保持稳定。  

3. **Sledding & Tubing**:  
   - 公园式：**Kohl Children’s Museum** (密尔沃基) 有室内滑道。  
   - 自然式：带孩子去 **Waukesha County Park** 的斜坡。  

4. **Snowmobiling**:  
   - 在 **Norski Trail** (门罗县) 或 **Prairie du Sac** 的越野雪地公园体验。  

### 💡 **Pro Tip**
- **Join a Group**: Check **Snowshoe Wisconsin** or **Wisconsin Snowshoe Meetups** for guided trips.  
- **App**: Use **AllTrails** to find groomed trails with real-time snow conditions.
