Based on your criteria (easy to train, good with kids/dogs, non-chasing, chill, apartment-friendly, moderate size/noise when alone), here are excellent breed and hybrid options:

### Top Recommendations
1. **Poodle (Standard, Miniature, Toy)**  
   - **Why**: Highly intelligent, trainable, hypoallergenic (low-shed), great with kids/dogs. Toy/Miniature sizes fit apartments well.  
   - **Hybrid Option**: Cocker Poodle mixes (e.g., "Cockapoo") are affectionate and low-shed.  

2. **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel**  
   - **Why**: Affectionate, gentle, small (under 25 lbs), and calm. Thrives on companionship. Minimal exercise needs.  

3. **Bichon Frise**  
   - **Why**: Playful but not hyper, hypoallergenic, and adore people (kids/dogs). Low-shedding and compact.  

4. **Shih Tzu**  
   - **Why**: Friendly, adaptable, and low-energy. Long coats need brushing but shed less than many breeds.  

5. **French Bulldog**  
   - **Why**: Low-maintenance, quiet, and great with humans. Minimal exercise needs; prone to snoring (common trait).  

### Other Strong Contenders
- **Golden Retriever** (if size is acceptable): Exceptionally patient with kids, highly trainable, and non-chasing. Needs some outdoor time.  
- **Labrador Retriever**: Friendly, easygoing, and great with dogs. Moderate energy—perfect for walks at the dog park.  
- **English Springer Spaniel**: Gentle, playful, and adaptable. Avoids chasing squirrels/cats; moderate size.  

### Key Tips for Success
1. **Meet the Dog**: Visit shelters/rescues to interact with potential candidates. Temperament varies even within breeds.  
2. **Training & Socialization**: All breeds need early socialization. Enroll in puppy classes to build confidence.  
3. **Puppy vs. Adult**: Puppies need more training time. Adult dogs (especially rescues) may be more "ready."  
4. **Shedding**: Poodles, Bichons, and Shih Tzus shed less than Golden Retrievers/Labs.  
5. **Noise Control**: French Bulldogs and Cavaliers tend to be quieter than terriers or retrievers when alone.  

### Where to Look
- **Rescue/Shelter Groups**: Search for breed-specific rescues (e.g., "Poodle Rescue," "Cavalier Rescue").  
- **Reputable Breeders**: If choosing purebreds, prioritize health testing and temperament screening.  
- **Hybrid Breeds**: Ask rescues/shelters about mixed-breed dogs (e.g., "Pembroke Welsh Corgi mixes" often have Golden Retriever traits).  

**Final Note**: Even "chill" breeds need daily exercise and mental stimulation (toys, puzzle feeders). Since you’ll be home sometimes, this helps prevent boredom. Prioritize meeting the dog in person—this is the best way to ensure compatibility! 🐾
