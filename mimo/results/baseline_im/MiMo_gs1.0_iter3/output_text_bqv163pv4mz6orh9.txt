That's wonderful! Here are a few thoughtful gift ideas that would show your daughter you're proud of her, ranging from practical to sentimental:

**Great Options:**

1.  **A Beautifully Framed Play Certificate:** Order a custom certificate online (sites like Etsy or Vistaprint are great for this). It can say "Playwright," "Lead Actress," "Best Supporting Actress," or even just "Opening Night Star" with her name and the play's title. It's a tangible keepsake she can hang on her wall forever.
2.  **A Personalized Playbill:** Find a nice print of the official playbill (if it's online or you know the design) and get it framed. Or, order a custom print with the play title, her name, and a fun message like "Opening Night Star" or "My Amazing Performer."
3.  **A "Proud Mom" Note + Small Treat:** While a heartfelt note expressing how incredibly proud you are (mentioning specific things you loved, like her courage, her voice, her teamwork) is essential, pair it with a small, kid-friendly treat she'll enjoy *after* the show. Think:
    *   Her favorite cupcake or pastry.
    *   A small bag of her favorite candy.
    *   A fun drink like bubble tea or a special smoothie.
    *   A small, themed gift related to the play (if appropriate).
4.  **Tickets to a Future Show:** Offer to take her and a friend (or sibling) to see another show she'd like soon. Frame it as celebrating her success and looking forward to more fun nights out.

**Why these work:**

*   **Personalized:** Shows you recognize her specific contribution.
*   **Keepsake:** The certificate or framed item lasts beyond the night.
*   **Celebratory:** The treat or future outing directly celebrates her achievement.
*   **Meaningful:** A genuine note of pride is incredibly powerful.

**Recommendation:** **Combine options 2 & 3!** Get a nice certificate or framed playbill (Option 2) and include a heartfelt note *plus* her favorite treat (Option 3). This covers both the lasting recognition and the immediate celebration of the moment.

**What to Avoid:**

*   **Generic Gifts:** Things that don't specifically acknowledge the play or her effort (like a random toy or book).
*   **Overly Expensive Items:** The sentiment is more important than the price tag for a first show.
*   **Something She Might Not Want:** Stick to things she'd genuinely like.

**The most important thing is your genuine pride and excitement!** When you see her backstage or after the show, telling her exactly how amazing you thought she was is the best gift of all. Adding one of these tangible items will make it even more special. Enjoy the opening night!
