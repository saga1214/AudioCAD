That's a common issue—some dogs just aren't cheese fans! Here are some alternatives and tips for Maisy's training treats:

### Top Treat Recommendations:
1. **Freeze-Dried Beef or Chicken**:  
   - *Why*: Intensely savory and universally loved. Look for plain, unsalted varieties (e.g., "beef lung" or "chicken liver").  
   - *Size*: Cut into tiny bits (pea-sized or smaller).

2. **Soft Cheeses**:  
   - Try mild cheddar, mozzarella, or cream cheese (plain, no additives).  
   - *Texture matters*: Soft cheeses are easier for small dogs to eat.

3. **Peanut Butter (Xylitol-Free)**:  
   - *Use sparingly*: ¼ tsp per training session.  
   - *Tip*: Mix with kibble for a "pup-sicle" treat.

4. **Commercial Training Treats**:  
   - Brands like Zuke’s Mini NutZones or Wellness Training Pouches are highly appealing and low-calorie.

### Liver Treats:  
- **Freeze-Dried Beef Liver** is often better tolerated than chicken liver.  
- **Avoid**: Raw liver (choking risk) or overly fatty varieties.  

### Pro Tips:  
- **High-Value Treats**: Start with your best option (e.g., beef liver), then gradually introduce others.  
- **Timing**: Use treats *immediately* after she performs the trick.  
- **Location**: Train in a quiet space to avoid distractions.  
- **Experiment**: Try 3-4 types over a week. Maisy might love chicken but dislike cheese!  

> 💡 **Key Insight**: Maisy’s preference matters more than "ideal" treats. If she ignores cheese, switch to beef/chicken immediately. Consistency and enthusiasm matter more than the treat type!
