Of course! With a new family member and a new car, Vancouver is the perfect starting point for road trips. Here are five fantastic options for you.

First, about an hour north, is Whistler. It's famous for skiing, but it's also a great spot year-round with hiking and biking. You can try the Blackcomb Gondola for amazing views, or go for a hike at the Rainforest Valley to see some old-growth trees. For food, you should definitely try the clam chowder in a sourdough bread bowl at a place called The Bear.

Second, a little further south, about two hours, is Victoria. It's the capital of British Columbia and has a real charm. You can visit the famous Butchart Gardens, which are stunning. For a special meal, try the seafood at a restaurant called Gage the Wine Bar. You could also take a cruise on the Empress of Canada or walk on the beautiful Inner Harbour.

Third, for a nature trip, you can head to Tofino on Vancouver Island, which is about three and a half hours away. It's on the coast and has dramatic mountains and forests. You can hike along the Broken Group Rocks to see sea stacks, or take a kayak tour to spot whales. For a truly special meal, try the seafood at Nourish Tofino, which has a Michelin Bib Gourmand award.

Fourth, for a quick day trip, you can go to the Okanagan Valley, about an hour and a half away. It's famous for its lakes and vineyards. You can take a tour of a winery, like the Summerhill Winery, or just relax at the beach at Kellington Provincial Park. For a great meal, try the farm-to-table food at a restaurant called The General Store.

And fifth, for a longer trip, you can drive to the Fraser Canyon, which is about four hours away. It's a beautiful, rugged area with waterfalls and hot springs. You can hike to the Spantex Gorge, or soak in the hot springs at the Britannia Creek Hot Springs. For a special meal, try the farm-to-table cuisine at The Bison.

Have a wonderful time exploring!
