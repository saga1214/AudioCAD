Okay, so you're looking for dinner suggestions, but you want them to align with your health goals. Since you mentioned a lower-carb, higher-protein diet, I can definitely help with that. If you're feeling too tired to pick a restaurant, here are a few options that fit your criteria. 

First, you could try a steakhouse. Many of them offer lean cuts of beef, like sirloin or ribeye, with sides like roasted vegetables or a side salad. Another option could be a sushi restaurant. You can get a lot of protein from things like tuna, salmon, or even edamame, and a lot of the rolls are lower-carb if you skip the rice. 

If you're craving something like Italian food, you could look for a place that does pasta primavera or a grilled chicken dish with roasted veggies. For a Spanish twist, you could try a tapas bar. Many of them have dishes with grilled shrimp, chicken skewers, or even tuna salad that would fit your diet. 

And if you're just looking for something super simple, a burger joint can work too, as long as you get a double patty burger with no bun, just lettuce and tomato, and maybe some bacon and cheese on the side. 

Just make sure to ask for the dressing on the side, and maybe skip the fries. A nice salad on the side would be a great way to fill up without the carbs. Hope that helps! Enjoy your dinner!
