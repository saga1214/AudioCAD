Okay, this is a fun pivot from writing to real-life romance! Let's get creative with water activities for an ocean-loving couple.

First, you could do a sunset kayak tour. The idea is to paddle out just as the sun is dipping below the horizon. You can bring a simple picnic with some local wine and cheese. The quiet and the changing colors of the water create a really intimate atmosphere.

Another idea is a private boat picnic. You could charter a small sailboat or a catamaran. Pack a gourmet lunch, and if you're feeling adventurous, some fresh oysters or lobster. You could even arrange for a simple musical playlist to be played from a portable speaker. Just drift for a while and talk.

For something more active, consider a snorkeling or diving excursion. You could find a secluded cove or a reef known for marine life. After, you can have a nice seafood dinner at a restaurant right on the beach. The shared experience of seeing the underwater world can be a great conversation starter.

You could also look into a cooking class on a boat. Many places offer them, where you learn to cook fresh seafood with local ingredients right on a charter boat. It's a fun, hands-on activity with a delicious reward.

And if you're near a place with cool tide pools, you could do a tide pool exploration at low tide. You'd get to see all sorts of unique little creatures up close. You could pack a thermos with hot chocolate or cider to stay warm and bring a blanket to sit on.

The key is to choose something that feels special and shared, which really fits the romantic getaway vibe you're going for.
