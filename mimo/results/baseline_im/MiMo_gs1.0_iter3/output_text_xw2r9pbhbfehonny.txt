Okay, here's a simple checklist for before you film your show at Sunnyside Assisted Living.

First, you'll want to get permission from the facility's staff. That way, you’re not violating any privacy policies.

Then, just make sure your phone or camera is charged. You don’t want to run out of battery mid-show.

Think about the lighting. Natural light is best, so try to position your setup near a window or under some bright overhead lights. Avoid filming in direct sunlight, though, as it can cause glare.

For sound, try to minimize background noise. You might want to use a small microphone if possible. And make sure you’re close enough to the camera so your voice is clear.

Before you start, test the recording. Hit record, do a quick run-through, and make sure everything is working.

Finally, just keep it simple. Focus on the performance—let the show be the main thing. The camera is just there to capture it.

You got this. Just enjoy the show.
