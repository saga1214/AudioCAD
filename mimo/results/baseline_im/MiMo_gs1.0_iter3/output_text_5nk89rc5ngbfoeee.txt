For an end-of-year celebration, a **hybrid approach** usually works best—balancing simplicity, fun, and inclusivity. Here’s a breakdown of your options and suggestions to make it memorable yet manageable:

### 🎉 **Event Ideas**
1. **Outdoor Park BBQ**  
   - **Pros:** Fun, active, and perfect for teamwork.  
   - **Cons:** Weather-dependent; requires setup/cleanup.  
   - **Tips:**  
     - Host at a local park with shaded picnic tables.  
     - **Simplify logistics:** Rent a small BBQ grill or use pre-made burgers/veggie skewers.  
     - **Parent help:** Ask a few parents to bring chairs, drinks, or cleanup supplies.  
     - **Activities:** Focus on badge-earning games like:  
       - **"Team Relay Race"** (e.g., sack race + egg-and-spoon + relay with a basket).  
       - **"Scavenger Hunt"** for nature items (e.g., pinecone, smooth rock, feather).  

2. **School Cafeteria Party**  
   - **Pros:** Low-cost, easy setup, and weather-proof.  
   - **Cons:** Less "outdoor adventure" vibe.  
   - **Tips:**  
     - **Theme:** "Giant Party" with oversized decorations (balloons, streamers).  
     - **Activities:**  
       - **"Pin the Badge on the Bear"** (fun for Brownies!).  
       - **"Craft Corner"** (decorate cookies or friendship bracelets).  
     - **Food:** Parent-brought snacks (e.g., sign-up sheet for pizza, chips, ice cream).  

### 🍕 **Food & Logistics**  
- **Keep it simple:**  
  - **Snack Bar:** Ice cream sundaes, pizza bites, fruit skewers.  
  - **Meal Option:** BBQ is great if parents bring sides (e.g., pasta salad, chips).  
- **Parent Involvement:**  
  - **Snack Duty:** Assign 2–3 parents to bring food each week.  
  - **Activity Helpers:** Parents can assist with races or crafts.  
- **Budget:**  
  - **Dues:** Cover troop supplies (e.g., $10–$20 per girl).  
  - **Parent Contribution:** Suggest a small fee ($5–$10) for the celebration (food/activities).  

### 🌟 **Key Takeaways**  
- **Start small:** Focus on 1–2 activities (e.g., BBQ + scavenger hunt).  
- **Involve parents:** Share tasks so you’re not overwhelmed.  
- **Flexibility:** If weather ruins outdoor plans, pivot to a backup (like the cafeteria).  
- **Celebrate badges:** End with a "trophy" ceremony (e.g., ribbon pins for badges earned).  

> 💡 **Final Suggestion:** A **park BBQ with parent-brought food** is ideal—it’s festive, promotes teamwork, and ties into badge activities. Keep food simple (grilled burgers, veggies, ice cream), and have parents handle setup/cleanup. You’ll create lasting memories without burning out!
