Here are five nutritious green vegetables you can offer your parrot, keeping Bruno's sensitivity to **broccoli, apples, and bananas** in mind:

1. **Carrots**: Rich in beta-carotene (good for vision) and fiber. Offer raw or lightly steamed.
2. **Bell Peppers** (red, yellow, or orange): Packed with vitamins A, C, and antioxidants. Cut into small pieces.
3. **Cauliflower**: A safer alternative to broccoli. High in fiber, vitamin C, and antioxidants. Steam or roast lightly.
4. **Kale** (dark leafy greens): Excellent source of calcium, vitamins A, C, and K. **Note:** Offer in moderation due to oxalates; remove tough stems.
5. **Spinach** (cooked): Rich in iron and vitamins. **Crucial:** Always steam or blanch spinach for parrots (raw spinach contains compounds that can be harmful).

### Tips for Serving:
- **Introduce new veggies gradually** (one at a time) to monitor for sensitivities.
- **Remove uneaten portions** within 24 hours to prevent spoilage.
- **Avoid iceberg lettuce** (low nutritional value) and **raw spinach** (as mentioned).
- **Variety is key**—rotate between these options to ensure a balanced diet.

Always ensure vegetables are fresh, washed thoroughly, and chopped into small, bird-safe pieces! 🥕🌶️🥬
