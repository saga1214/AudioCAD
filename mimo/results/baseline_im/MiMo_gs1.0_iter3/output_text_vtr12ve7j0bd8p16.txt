That's a fantastic question! With a Sagittarius-Leo pairing, adventures are often a *core* part of the romance. Here are some great Calgary-area weekend adventure ideas, balancing excitement, romance, and Sagittarius/Leo energy:

**Short Getaways (1-2 hours from Calgary):**

1.  **Banff National Park (The Classic Adventure):**
    *   **Why it fits:** Ultimate Sagittarius/Leo adventure! Stunning landscapes, hiking, wildlife, hot springs, and iconic mountain views.
    *   **Romantic Touches:** Picnic by Lake Louise or Moraine Lake (if accessible), a romantic dinner at a lodge, stargazing at Johnston Canyon (less crowded than Banff), a couples' hot spring soak at Sulphur Mountain.
    *   **Adventure:** Hiking (easy lakeside walks to challenging peaks), canoeing on Lake Minnewanka, wildlife viewing, scenic drives (Icefields Parkway is spectacular!).

2.  **Kananaskis Country:**
    *   **Why it fits:** More intimate and less crowded than Banff, with incredible hiking, mountain biking, and stunning alpine lakes. Feels like your own private adventure playground.
    *   **Romantic Touches:** Picnic at Kananaskis Lake, cozy cabin stay, sunset viewing from a viewpoint, exploring the beautiful Kananaskis River Valley.
    *   **Adventure:** World-class hiking (Kananaskis Falls, Moose Mountain), mountain biking (various trails), kayaking/canoeing, scenic drives.

3.  **Elk Island National Park (Near Fort Chipewyan):**
    *   **Why it fits:** Beautiful grasslands, rivers, and wildlife (elk, bison, wolves). Offers a different kind of adventure than the mountains.
    *   **Romantic Touches:** Wildlife viewing from a comfortable lodge, canoeing on the Peace River, sunset drives, cozy evenings by the fire.
    *   **Adventure:** Canoeing/kayaking, wildlife safaris (guided), hiking (Grizzly Lake Trail is lovely), fishing.

**Closer to Calgary (Half-Day to Overnight):**

4.  **Bow River Pathway Bike Ride:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Active, scenic, and romantic. Perfect for a sunny afternoon or a longer ride on a weekend day.
    *   **Romantic Touches:** Pack a picnic basket, stop at the St. Patrick's Island Fountain for a photo, enjoy the views of downtown and the river.
    *   **Adventure:** Moderate to challenging depending on the route (start near the Bow River floodplain and ride along the river, or tackle sections like the St. Patrick's Island climb).

5.  **Calgary Zoo (With a Twist):**
    *   **Why it fits:** Leo love being the center of attention (in a fun way), and Sagittarius enjoys learning and exploration. It's a great shared experience.
    *   **Romantic Touches:** Pack a picnic and enjoy it at the beautiful Japanese Garden or the Rose Garden. Find a quiet spot to relax after exploring. The zoo has lovely gardens and open spaces.
    *   **Adventure:** Explore the diverse habitats, maybe try a behind-the-scenes tour if available.

6.  **Cruise the Bow River at Sunset:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Combines gentle movement, beautiful scenery, and romance. Easy adventure.
    *   **Romantic Touches:** Sunset cruise is inherently romantic. Opt for a dinner cruise if you want to make it a special evening event.
    *   **Adventure:** Low-key but scenic. Some companies offer smaller, more intimate boats.

7.  **Stargazing at the暗空公园 (Dark Sky Park) near Canmore:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Sagittarius loves exploration and wonder, Leo loves grandeur. This is one of the darkest skies in North America.
    *   **Romantic Touches:** Bundle up, bring hot chocolate, find a cozy spot under the stars together. Pure magic.
    *   **Adventure:** Minimal physical activity required, maximum awe-inspiring experience. Check the schedule for guided tours or telescopes.

8.  **Couples' Hot Springs (Sulphur Mountain or Banff):**
    *   **Why it fits:** Ultimate Leo show-off (in the best way!) and Sagittarius relaxation/adventure combo. Soaking in natural hot springs is inherently romantic.
    *   **Romantic Touches:** The whole experience is designed for couples. Book a private tub if possible, soak under the stars.
    *   **Adventure:** The journey to the springs can be scenic (especially Sulphur Mountain's gondola ride!).
