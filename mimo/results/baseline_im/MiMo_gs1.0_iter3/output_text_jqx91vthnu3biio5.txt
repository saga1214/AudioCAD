### 1. **Color-Coding Icons & Mental Load**  
   - **Memory vs. Efficiency**: Color-coding primarily aids **memory recall** (e.g., red for urgent tasks, blue for projects) but *also* reduces **mental load** by visually grouping related items. If icons share a consistent color scheme, your brain doesn’t have to "decode" categories as much.  
   - **Avoid Overload**: Stick to 3–5 colors max. Too many colors → chaos. Test subtle palettes (e.g., light blue for admin, green for creative work).  

### 2. **Playlist Rotation Strategy**  
   - **Personal Preference > Timing**: Switching between lo-fi and nature sounds *can* refresh focus, but it’s highly individual. If you notice dips in concentration, rotate. If it disrupts flow, stick to one.  
   - **Optimal Timing**: Try alternating every **60–90 minutes**—long enough to avoid distraction but short enough to prevent monotony. Apps like [Focus@Will](https://www.focusatwill.com/) offer science-backed background audio for this.  

### 3. **Physical Documents: Environment vs. Chunking**  
   - **Progress Sources**: Both matter! A **clean workspace** reduces decision fatigue, while **breaking tasks** (e.g., "File taxes 2023" vs. "File everything") builds momentum.  
   - **Tip**: Start with 15 minutes of aggressive tidying (dump everything on a table, sort into "Keep," "Recycle," "File")—this "reset" often unlocks deeper organization.  

### 4. **Dinner Suggestions**  
Here are 4 quick, satisfying options (all under 30 mins):  
1. **Lemon Garlic Shrimp with Zucchini Noodles**  
   - Sauté shrimp + garlic in olive oil, toss with spiralized zucchini, lemon zest, and parsley. Serve with a side of roasted cherry tomatoes.  
2. **Hearty Chickpea Salad Bowl**  
   - Mash chickpeas with vegan mayo, diced celery, red onion, and dill. Top with mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, and avocado. Drizzle with lemon-tahini dressing.  
3. **Miso-Glazed Salmon & Microwave Sweet Potato**  
   - Whisk 2 tbsp white miso + 1 tbsp honey + 1 tbsp rice vinegar. Brush onto salmon, bake at 400°F (200°C) for 12–15 mins. Microwave sweet potato with cinnamon.  
4. **No-Cook Tuna Salad Wraps**  
   - Mix canned tuna, Greek yogurt (or mayo), diced celery, and dill. Spread on whole-wheat wraps with sliced cucumbers and sprouts.
