Here’s a **7-day deep-dive schedule** for Tokyo and Osaka, including day trips to Kyoto and Nara. It balances iconic sights, cultural immersion, and flexibility for your group:

---

### **Tokyo (Days 1–5)**  
**Day 1: Arrival & Shinjuku**  
- **Morning:** Arrive in Tokyo, check into hotel (use luggage forwarding if needed).  
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Shinjuku Gyoen Garden** (serene escape), then **Shibuya Crossing** (iconic scramble).  
- **Evening:** Dinner in Omoide Yokocho ("Memory Lane") near Shinjuku Station.  

**Day 2: Modern Tokyo & Akihabara**  
- **Morning:** **Meiji Shrine** (harmony of nature & tradition) → **Harajuku (Takeshita Street)** for quirky fashion.  
- **Afternoon:** **Akihabara** (tech/anime paradise) → **Ueno Park** (museums/gardens).  
- **Evening:** Sumida River Cruise to **Odaiba** (futuristic architecture).  

**Day 3: Tradition & Views**  
- **Morning:** **Senso-ji Temple** (Asakusa) → Nakamise Market (snacks).  
- **Afternoon:** **Tokyo Skytree** (panoramic views) → **Ueno Park** (cultural sites).  
- **Evening:** **Ginza** (luxury shopping/dining).  

**Day 4: Day Trip to Kamakura**  
- **Morning:** Shinkansen to **Kamakura** (45 mins). See **Great Buddha**, **Hase-dera Temple**, and bamboo forest.  
- **Afternoon:** Return to Tokyo, explore **Yanaka Ginza** (old Tokyo vibe).  
- **Evening:** Sushi dinner in Tsukiji Outer Market.  

**Day 5: Free Day / Optional Day Trip**  
- **Option 1:** **Day trip to Nikko** (UNESCO sites, waterfalls, temples).  
- **Option 2:** **Tsukiji Fish Market** (if not done Day 4) → **Omotesando** (designer boutiques).  
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner in Roppongi or Ginza.  

---

### **Osaka (Days 6–7)**  
**Day 6: Osaka City & Kyoto Day Trip**  
- **Morning:** **Osaka Castle** → Explore **Kuromon Market** (food).  
- **Afternoon:** Shinkansen to **Kyoto** (30 mins). Visit **Kiyomizu-dera Temple** → **Gion District** (geisha sightings).  
- **Evening:** Return to Osaka, dinner in **Dotonbori** (neon lights, takoyaki).  

**Day 7: Nara & Return**  
- **Morning:** Shinkansen to **Nara** (45 mins). Feed deer at **Todai-ji Temple** (Great Buddha).  
- **Afternoon:** Short train to **Kyoto** (10 mins), visit **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** → **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (early to avoid crowds).  
- **Evening:** Fly home from Kansai International Airport (KIX).  

---

   - Get a **JR Pass** (7-day) for unlimited Shinkansen/trains.  
   - Use Suica/Pasmo cards for local transit.

   - Focus on 2–3 major sights/day (avoid burnout).  
   - Use hotel luggage forwarding to lighten bags.  
3. **Food:**  
   - Try *okonomiyaki* (Osaka), *sushi* (Tokyo), and *matcha* (Kyoto).  
4. **Flexibility:**  
   - Adjust based on energy levels (e.g., skip temples if tired).
