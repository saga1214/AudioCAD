Here are quick drink recipes for your vodka and Argentine Fernet:  

### 1. **Vodka Soda** (Refreshing & Light)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 1.5 oz vodka  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- Top with soda water  
- Garnish: Lime twist or mint sprig  

**Instructions**:  
Shake vodka and lime juice with ice, strain into a glass, top with soda, and garnish.  

### 2. **Argentinian Fernet & Soda** (Bold & Bitter)  
**Ingredients**:  
- 2 oz Fernet  
- 2 oz soda water  
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Garnish: Orange slice  

**Instructions**:  
Stir Fernet, bitters, and ice in a rocks glass. Strain, top with soda, and garnish.  

### Tips:  
- **Vodka Mix**: Adjust sweetness with soda or add a splash of cranberry juice if preferred.  
- **Fernet Mix**: Fernet is strong and bitter—serve chilled and in small portions.
