Tuning a ukulele is straightforward! Here's how to use a tuner, whether it's a physical one or an app:

**Using a Tuner App (Most Common Method):**

1.  **Open the App:** Launch your chosen tuner app (like GuitarTuna, Fender Tune, or a built-in phone app).
2.  **Plug in a String:** Hold the tuner app screen close to the string you want to tune. The mic needs to hear the string's vibration.
3.  **Pluck the String:** Give the string a clear, solid pluck.
4.  **Observe the Tuner:**
    *   **Target Note:** The app will display the current pitch (like "G3") and a target note (usually "G" or "G3").
    *   **Pitch Indicator:** Look for a visual indicator (like a needle, arrow, or color change) showing if the pitch is too low (flat), too high (sharp), or perfectly in tune.
    *   **Frequency:** It might also show the frequency (like 196 Hz for the G string).
5.  **Adjust the Tuning Peg:**
    *   **Too Low (Flat):** Turn the peg **away** from you (toward the headstock) to tighten the string.
    *   **Too High (Sharp):** Turn the peg **toward** you (away from the headstock) to loosen the string.
    *   **In Tune:** Keep plucking until the tuner shows the target note and the indicator is centered or green.
6.  **Repeat for Each String:** Move to the next string (usually E, C, E, A) and repeat steps 2-5. Tuning often requires a few plucks and adjustments per string.

**Using a Physical Clip-On Tuner:**

1.  **Attach the Clip:** Clip the tuner onto the headstock near the strings.
2.  **Pluck the String:** Give the string a clear, solid pluck.
3.  **Observe the Tuner:**
    *   **Target Note:** Look for the target note (G, C, E, A) on the display.
    *   **Pitch Indicator:** Look for a needle, arrow, or light that moves towards the center or a green zone when in tune. It might show "G" and a green light, or a needle centered.
4.  **Adjust the Tuning Peg:** Turn the peg using the same rules as the app: **tighten** for flat, **loosen** for sharp. Pluck again to check.
5.  **Repeat for Each String:** Tune each string individually.

**Standard Ukulele Tuning (GCEA):**

*   **String 4 (Thickest):** **G** (Open String)
*   **String 3:** **C**
*   **String 2:** **E** (Open String)
*   **String 1 (Thinnest):** **A** (Open String)

**Tips:**

*   **Pluck Clearly:** Give the string a good, even pluck so the tuner can hear it properly.
*   **Check Stability:** After tuning, pluck the string again. If the tuner jumps, the peg might be slipping. Try turning the peg slightly more in the opposite direction to stabilize it.
*   **Tuning Order:** Tune from the thickest (4th) string to the thinnest (1st).
*   **Practice:** Tuning takes practice! Don't worry if it takes a few tries.
*   **Peg Slippage:** If tuning keeps slipping, check that the pegs aren't loose. You might need to tighten the tuning machine itself.

Once you get the hang of it, tuning your ukulele will become quick and easy!
