Here's a step-by-step guide to sewing on a name tag safely without damaging your jacket lining, tailored for Nordstrom Rack purchases:

### Best Option: Sewing (Most Durable)
1. **Use a Micro-Needle or Chiffon Needle**:  
   - Find a **micro-needle** (size 18-0) or **chiffon needle** at a craft store. These have ultra-fine tips to pierce fabric without tearing.  
   - *Alternative*: Use a **darning needle** (sharp tip) if you can't find a micro-needle.

2. **Thread**:  
   - Use a **nylon thread** (strong and flexible) or **polyester thread**. Avoid cotton thread—it can fray.

3. **Stitching Technique**:  
   - **Backstitch or Ladder Stitch**:  
     - **Backstitch**: Create small, controlled stitches by looping the needle under the tag’s stitching and pulling through the lining. Repeat 2-3 times per stitch.  
     - **Ladder Stitch**: Ideal for invisibility. Make parallel stitches down the lining, then "ladder" the thread upward to secure.  
   - **Anchor Points**: Start and end stitches at the same spot on the tag and lining to prevent gaps.

4. **Avoid Damage**:  
   - **Test First**: Try on a spare piece of the jacket material if possible.  
   - **Keep Stitches Small**: 1-2 stitches per inch max.  
   - **Skip the Lining**: If the lining is thick, sew only through the outer fabric (e.g., under a collar or hem).

### No-Sew Option: Iron-On Patches
- **Best for**: Delicate linings (fleece, synthetics).  
- **Steps**:  
  1. Place the patch *right-side down* on the lining.  
  2. Use a **pressing cloth** (cotton scrap) between the patch and iron.  
  3. Iron for 15-30 seconds (test on a hidden seam first to avoid melting).  
  4. Let cool completely before handling.

### Pro Tips:
- **Location**: Attach the tag inside a hem, under a collar, or on a pocket lining—areas less likely to fray.  
- **Tailoring Help**: If unsure, visit a **local tailor** (e.g., Jo-Ann Fabrics or a dry cleaner) for $5–$10. They’ll do it perfectly!  
- **Avoid Heat Damage**: Never iron directly on polyester or synthetic linings—they can melt.

### Why This Works:
- **Micro-needles** minimize holes, keeping the lining intact.  
- **Nylon thread** resists stretching and fraying.  
- **Iron-on patches** are foolproof for beginners.  

For Nordstrom Rack jackets (often made from polyester or fleece), the micro-needle method is ideal if you’re comfortable sewing. Otherwise, an iron-on patch is your safest bet! 🧵
