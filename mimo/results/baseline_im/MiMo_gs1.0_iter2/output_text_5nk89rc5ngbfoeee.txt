For an end-of-year celebration, I’d suggest a **park barbecue with customizable food stations**—it’s affordable, inclusive, and lets the girls take pride in hosting. Here’s how to make it work:

### **Venue & Logistics**  
- **Park Picnic Area:** Choose a spot with shade, picnic tables, and space for games. Bring a canopy/tarp for weather backup.  
- **Parent Coordination:** Assign a parent to handle grill setup (if using charcoal/BBQ) or rent a portable grill if needed.  

### **Food Ideas**  
- **Sundae Bar:** Let girls make sundaes with ice cream, toppings (sprinkles, hot fudge, gummy bears), and a DIY pizza station (flatbread base, store-bought sauce, toppings like cheese, pepperoni, veggies).  
- **Snack-Friendly Options:** Include smaller snacks like mini pizzas, fruit skewers, and veggie sticks for girls who prefer lighter bites.  
- **Cost Split:** Ask parents to contribute $5–$10 each for food—this keeps it affordable while making it a shared effort.  

### **Activities**  
- **Classic Games:** Wheelbarrow races, sack races, or a "brownie-themed" relay (e.g., carrying a "sunset" (flashlight) while balancing a "sunset cake" (balloon)).  
- **Badge Recognition:** Have girls display badges on a "trophy wall" and give a group photo.  
- **Reflection Time:** Start with a simple "What was your favorite Brownie moment?" icebreaker.  

### **Pregnancy-Friendly Tips**  
- Delegate setup/cleanup tasks to parents.  
- Pack a cooler with pre-made drinks/snacks to avoid last-minute stress.  
- Skip lifting heavy items—focus on supervision and photo-taking!  

### **Budget-Friendly Tips**  
- Use a ** SignUp genius** app for parents to volunteer for food, games, or cleanup.  
- Buy in bulk (e.g., 20 tubs of ice cream) and store at room temperature.  
- DIY decorations (brown paper "sunset" banners, craft stick "candles").  

**Why this works:** It’s active, affordable, and celebrates the girls’ teamwork. The sundae bar lets everyone enjoy food they love, and games reinforce the Brownies’ spirit of adventure. You’ve got this—your plan is already solid! 🌟
