For your winter chicks in the dedicated area, here are the best bedding options, prioritizing safety, warmth, and rodent resistance:

### Top Recommendations:
1. **Orchard Grass Pellet Bedding**  
   - **Why it works:**  
     - Compresses tightly under chicks, providing insulation without gaps.  
     - Rodent-resistant and dust-free.  
     - Holds heat well when compressed by the chicks themselves.  
   - **How to use:**  
     - Spread 2–3 inches thick.  
     - Replace when soiled (every 1–2 days).  
     - Secure edges to prevent spillage into the main coop.  

2. **Crumpled Paper/Newspaper**  
   - **Why it works:**  
     - Super affordable and easy to replace daily.  
     - Lightweight, non-toxic, and moisture-absorbent.  
     - Ideal for young chicks (no dust risk).  
   - **How to use:**  
     - Layer 1–2 sheets thick.  
     - Replace daily to maintain hygiene and dryness.  

3. **Pine or Cedar Shavings**  
   - **Why it works:**  
     - Affordable and effective insulation.  
     - Dries quickly (if well-ventilated).  
   - **Caution:**  
     - Avoid "sawdust" (too fine and dusty).  
     - Ensure shavings are kiln-dried to prevent mold.  

### Critical Tips for Safety & Hygiene:
- **Avoid Straw/Hay:** Attracts rodents and mold.  
- **Keep it Dry:**  
  - Use a tray or raised bed under the brooder plate to catch wet droppings.  
  - Ensure good airflow (no corners for moisture buildup).  
- **Clean Regularly:**  
  - Remove soiled bedding daily to prevent bacterial growth.  
- **Secure the Area:**  
  - Use a wire mesh barrier to prevent chicks from escaping into the main coop.  
  - Seal any gaps in the dedicated area to deter rodents.  

### Pro Tip:  
Combine **pellet bedding** (for insulation) with a **daily layer of crumpled paper** on top. This lets chicks peck through paper for comfort while keeping the bottom layer dry and rodent-free.  

By using these materials, your chicks will stay warm, clean, and safe without compromising rodent control! 🐥
