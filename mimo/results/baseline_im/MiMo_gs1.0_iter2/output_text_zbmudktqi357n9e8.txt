Okay, so you're looking for a few-dollar vacation spot in Mexico for inspiration to paint autumn scenes and watercolors. That's a great idea! Mexico has some amazing, affordable places, especially outside of the super touristy areas. 

For autumn inspiration, think about the coastal areas where you can paint those beautiful, warm sunsets over the ocean. A place like Tulum or Playa del Carmen comes to mind. They’re not the absolute cheapest, but they’re often more affordable than Cancún, and the art scene there is pretty vibrant. 

If you want something even more budget-friendly, you could consider places like Merida or Valladolid. They’re inland, so you might not get that ocean view, but the architecture and history are just incredible for inspiration. And the food is amazing and very affordable. 

For watercolor painting, the coastal areas are probably best. The light and the water make for some really dynamic scenes. But if you’re on a tighter budget, inland towns like San Cristóbal de las Casas offer a completely different vibe, with lush landscapes and waterfalls. 

So, really, it depends on what kind of inspiration you’re looking for. If it’s autumn and water, stick to the coast. If you want something a bit more off the beaten path, the inland towns are fantastic and a lot cheaper. Either way, you’ll get some great ideas for your art.
