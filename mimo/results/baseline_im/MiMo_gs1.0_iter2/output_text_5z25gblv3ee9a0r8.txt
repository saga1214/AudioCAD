That sounds like a really interesting and potentially very helpful situation for you.

First, it's great that you're already using the breathing techniques so much. That's the key.

As for going on the trip, I think you should absolutely go. Here's why:

One, it's a small, manageable step. An hour's drive is much less intimidating than you used to feel. You can use the box breathing right there in the car if you need to.

Two, you're not going alone. Having a friend there is a huge comfort and safety net. You can talk to them, distract yourself, and you'll know someone is right there with you.

Three, you now have a proven tool in your toolbox. The box breathing has already worked for you, so you know you have a way to manage the anxiety when it comes up.

My advice is to take it one step at a time. You can focus on the trip itself, not on the whole journey to Mexico. Just get in the car, breathe if you need to, and enjoy the time with your friend. You've got this.
