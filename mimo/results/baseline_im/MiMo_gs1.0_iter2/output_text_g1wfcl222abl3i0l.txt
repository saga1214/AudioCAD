**Glue Gun Troubleshooting:**
1. **Check the Cord:** Ensure the cord is firmly plugged into the wall outlet (try a different outlet if possible).
2. **Power Source:** Verify it's not a USB-powered gun – these need stable power. If it's USB, try a wall adapter instead.
3. **Preheat:** Many glue guns need 30-60 seconds of heating before use. Hold the trigger down for that time.
4. **Sticks:** Ensure you're using the correct size sticks (usually 24mm or 32mm). Broken or old sticks won't work.
5. **Nozzle:** Clean the tip with a toothpick or cotton swab if it's clogged.

**If the Glue Gun Doesn't Work:**
* **Try a different glue gun** (borrow one or buy a new one).
* **Use hot glue sticks** (if your gun works) – they often work better than standard glue sticks.
* **Switch to craft glue** (like E6000 or tacky glue) for delicate items like lace or flowers.
* **Use a hairdryer** (low heat) to warm the glue gun tip before applying glue.

**For Your Decorations (While You Wait):**
* **Black lace/mask combo:** Layer lace around the base, then attach masks with glue or pins.
* **Flowers:** Use faux black roses or deep purple flowers with dark glitter accents.
* **Spooky details:** Add tiny faux spiders or miniature pumpkins to the base.
