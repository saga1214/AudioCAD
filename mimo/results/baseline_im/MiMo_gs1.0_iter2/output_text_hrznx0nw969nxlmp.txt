That's a fantastic choice! St. Thomas (especially the East End) is perfect for a relaxed, beach-focused trip. Here are some great daytime activities from Secret Harbour Resort:

1.  **Snorkeling Paradise:** This is the biggest draw!
    *   **Coki Beach:** Right next door! Famous for its shallow, clear waters teeming with colorful fish, sea turtles (often!), and beautiful coral gardens. Perfect for all levels.
    *   **Trunk Bay:** A world-renowned snorkeling spot. Accessible via a short, beautiful walk or a short boat ride. The underwater observatory is a highlight.
    *   **Sandy Spit:** Another easy-access spot known for its vibrant fish life and coral formations. Great for beginners and families.
    *   **Secret Harbour Resort Beach:** While smaller, their own beach offers calm, shallow waters and decent snorkeling right off the steps.

2.  **Relax & Rejuvenate:**
    *   **Resort Beach Club:** Lounge on the pristine sands, swim in the calm turquoise water, and soak up the sun. Secret Harbour often has a laid-back vibe perfect for this.
    *   **Sunset Cruises:** Many operators offer gentle, scenic sunset sails from nearby Cruz Bay. Perfect for your brother, with minimal motion. Check with your boat charter for options.

3.  **Explore the Island:**
    *   **Honeymoon Hill:** A short, easy hike (about 15-20 mins) offering incredible panoramic views of Cruz Bay and the surrounding mountains. Great for a quick nature walk and photo op.
    *   **Cruz Bay:** Walk down to the bustling town center. Browse the shops, find unique souvenirs, grab a smoothie or coffee, and enjoy the local atmosphere.
    *   **Morse Hill:** A slightly longer hike (about 30-45 mins) with fantastic, sweeping views of the island, especially impressive at sunrise or sunset. Great for a more active morning.

4.  **Water Sports (Choose Gentle Options):**
    *   **Kayaking:** Gentle paddling along the coast is fantastic. You can find rentals in Cruz Bay or near Coki Beach.
    *   **Stand-Up Paddleboarding (SUP):** Calm bays like Coki or Secret Harbour are ideal for beginners. Many places offer lessons or rentals.
    *   **Jet Skiing:** While more energetic, operators near Cruz Bay offer rentals if you're both up for it (ensure your brother feels comfortable).

**Key Tips for Your Day:**

*   **Check the Weather & Tides:** Conditions significantly impact snorkeling and beach activities. A quick check in the morning is wise.
*   **Sun Protection:** The Caribbean sun is intense. Pack reef-safe sunscreen (SPF 50+), hats, rash guards, and sunglasses.
*   **Hydration:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
*   **Respect Marine Life:** Snorkel gently, avoid touching coral or marine life, and always follow the rules at protected areas.
*   **Resort Life:** Don't underestimate the joy of simply relaxing on the beach, reading a book, or enjoying a drink by the pool at Secret Harbour!

Enjoy your incredible week on the beautiful beaches of St. Thomas! The combination of Secret Harbour's location, easy access to world-class snorkeling, and the vibrant Red Hook scene later should make for a fantastic trip.
