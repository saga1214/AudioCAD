Okay, you're going to film your show at Sunnyside Assisted Living. Here’s a simple checklist to help you prepare ahead of time.

First, get permission. Make sure the facility allows recording and that you have their written consent, just to be safe.

Next, you’ll want to set up your recording device. Bring a phone or camera, and make sure the battery is charged. It’s a good idea to test the lighting, too—natural light works best, so try to film near a window if you can.

Also, think about your props and outfits. Make sure all your puppets are ready and your costumes are clean. It might help to have some backup props just in case something goes wrong.

Then, do a quick sound check. Make sure the microphone is working, or you can just speak a little louder if you don't have one. It's a good idea to test the audio while you're doing one of your signature moves.

Finally, just relax and have fun. Your audience will feed off your energy, so just be yourself and enjoy the show.

So to recap: get permission, test your equipment, prep your props, and have fun. That’s it.
