For your refresh station, here are some bright, light flavors that pair beautifully with citrus or chocolate, plus palate-cleansing ideas:

### **Flavor Pairings**  
1. **Citrus + Mint**  
   - **Lemon/Mint Infusions**: Cucumber-mint water or lemon-rosemary tea (refreshing and palate-cleansing).  
   - **Citrus-Infused Honey**: Light drizzle on yogurt or crackers (pairs with chocolate).  
   - **Mint Chocolate Chips**: Mini-sized for a light, sweet treat.  

2. **Chocolate + Citrus**  
   - **Orange-Chocolate Bites**: Dark chocolate with orange zest (bright and energizing).  
   - **Lime-Caramel Drizzle**: Lightly sweetened to balance richness.  
   - **Grapefruit & Dark Chocolate**: A bold but refreshing combo.  

3. **Light & Refreshing**  
   - **Pomegranate Mint Spritzer**: Sparkling water + pomegranate juice + mint.  
   - **Coconut Water Shots**: Hydrating and energizing.  
   - **Fresh Fruit Skewers**: Pineapple, berries, and melon (no added sugar).  

### **Palate Cleansers**  
- **Herbal Chews**: Spearmint or lemongrass gum.  
- **Tart Cherry/Juice**: A few drops on ice for a tart burst.  
- **Rice Paper Snacks**: Light and crisp (e.g., cucumber-wasabi strips).  

### **Decor & Setup**  
- **Tableware**: White or pastel-colored bowls to emphasize freshness.  
- **Signage**: Handwritten chalkboard notes like "Stay Cool! 🍋" or "Taste the Sunshine! ☀️".  
- ** cleanup**: Use biodegradable cups or reusable mason jars.  

### **Badge Printing**  
- **Print now** if new volunteers are confirmed (e.g., via email).  
- **Wait** if it’s a last-minute event (to avoid waste).  
- **Tip**: Include a "Welcome" note with badges to boost morale!
