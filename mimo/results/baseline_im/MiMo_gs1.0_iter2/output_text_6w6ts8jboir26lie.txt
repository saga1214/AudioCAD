Okay, you're handling this so well. I'm glad you're taking control and focusing on a positive activity. Since you love to cook, here are a few simple, satisfying dinner ideas you can whip up with ingredients you can probably find at a store.

First, you could do a Pantry Pasta. Just grab a box of pasta, some olive oil, garlic, canned tomatoes, and maybe some canned beans like chickpeas or lentils. You can sauté the garlic in the oil, add the tomatoes and beans, season it all up, and then toss in your cooked pasta. It's quick, comforting, and very customizable.

Another idea is a simple Sheet Pan Fajitas. You'll need bell peppers and onions, some chicken or steak, and spices like cumin and chili powder. Just chop everything up, toss it on a baking sheet with oil and spices, and roast it until it's cooked. You can serve it with tortillas, rice, and salsa.

If you want something warm and simple, you could make a creamy Tomato Basil Soup. You can use canned tomatoes, some vegetable or chicken broth, and maybe a can of cream. Sauté some garlic, add the tomatoes and broth, let it simmer, and then stir in the cream and some fresh basil. It's perfect for chilling out.

Or, how about a Quick Stir-Fry? Just grab some noodles or rice, a mix of veggies like broccoli and carrots, some protein like chicken or tofu, and a bottled stir-fry sauce. You can cook the protein, then the veggies, add the noodles or rice, and toss it all together with the sauce.

And for something really easy and satisfying, you could make a Grilled Cheese and Tomato Soup. Just make some good tomato soup and toast some bread with butter and cheese until it's golden and crispy. It's a classic comfort food.

Enjoy your cooking break!
