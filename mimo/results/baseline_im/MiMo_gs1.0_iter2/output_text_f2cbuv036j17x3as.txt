Okay, so you're looking to do a pre-road trip check on your diesel camper van. That's a smart move. For the brakes, a good way to start is to check the brake pads themselves. You can usually see them when you lift the dust covers at the wheels. If they're worn down to the wear indicators or you can see the metal backing plate, you should replace them. Also, check the rotors or drums for any deep grooves or uneven surfaces. If you hear any grinding or squealing when you brake, that's a clear sign of wear that needs attention. Another thing to look at is the brake fluid. It's supposed to be clear, but if it looks dark or has bubbles, it could mean a leak or air in the system, which is a problem. For spark plugs, it's a bit more involved. You'll need to take off the old ones, check their condition, and then replace them with new ones. It's a good idea to have a torque wrench handy to make sure they're tightened to the right spec. But honestly, if you're not comfortable doing it, it's probably best to take it to a mechanic.
