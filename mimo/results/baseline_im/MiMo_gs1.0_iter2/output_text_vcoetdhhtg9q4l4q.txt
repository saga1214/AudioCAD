**One-Pan Lemon Garlic Shrimp with Roasted Veggies**  
- Toss broccoli, bell peppers, and zucchini in olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 20 mins.

- Serve together. Minimal cleanup, max flavor!
