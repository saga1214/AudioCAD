Here’s a detailed shopping list for your three-course meal, organized by course, with allergen-safe options and clear instructions:

### **Starter: Cucumber Salad or Miso Soup**
*(Choose one)*  
**Option 1: Cucumber Salad**  
- 1 English cucumber  
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar  
- 1 tsp sesame oil  
- 1 tsp soy sauce (ensure no fish sauce)  
- 1 tsp sugar  
- 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds  
- Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional)  

**Option 2: Miso Soup**  
- 1 packet (or 3 tbsp) miso paste (check for soybean-free if needed)  
- 4 cups dashi stock (or use 2 cups water + 1 tsp kombu)  
- 4-5 shiitake mushrooms (sliced)  
- 1 small nori sheet (optional)  
- 1 tbsp green onions (thinly sliced)  

### **Main Course: Chicken Teriyaki**  
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (or thighs)  
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (ensure no fish sauce)  
- 2 tbsp mirin (or substitute 1 tbsp rice vinegar + 1 tbsp sugar)  
- 1 tbsp sake (or substitute 1 tbsp water)  
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger  
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)  
- 1 tsp cornstarch (for glaze thickening)  
- 1 tbsp sugar  
- 1 tsp sesame oil  
- 2 tbsp cooking oil (for stir-frying)  
- 2 cups mixed stir-fry veggies (e.g., bell peppers, snap peas, carrots)  

### **Dessert: Mango Sticky Rice**  
- 1 cup glutinous rice (pre-cooked or quick-cooking)  
- 1 ripe mango (sliced into strips)  
- ½ cup coconut milk  
- 2 tbsp sugar  
- 1 tsp salt  
- 1 tbsp matcha powder (for dusting)  
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds (optional)  

### **Condiments & Extras**  
- Soy sauce (for dipping)  
- Sesame oil  
- Rice vinegar  
- Sugar  
- Cornstarch  
- Cooking oil  
- Fresh green onions (for garnish)  

### **Equipment**  
- Small saucepan for miso soup  
- Wok or large skillet for stir-fry  
- Steamer basket (or microwave-safe bowl for rice)  
- Mixing bowl for teriyaki glaze  

### **Notes**  
1. **Allergen Check:** Verify miso paste and soy sauce are legume/seafood-free.  
2. **Time-Savers:**  
   - Use pre-cooked sticky rice or frozen rice.  
   - Opt for pre-sliced mango.  
   - Teriyaki glaze can be made ahead.  
3. **Flexibility:**  
   - Swap chicken for pork or tofu.  
   - Add edamame (legume-free) to stir-fry.  

Enjoy your meal! 🍜🍗🥭
