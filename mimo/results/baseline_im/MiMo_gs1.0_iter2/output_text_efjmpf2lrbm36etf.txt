Here are some celebratory, mood-lifting drink and menu ideas to transform your last-minute anniversary into a magical night—while keeping it simple and joyful:

### 🌟 **Sparkling & Uplifting Drinks** (No Citrus!)  
1. **Lyre’s Flight**  
   - *Ingredients:* Lyre’s non-alcoholic aperitif (floral/herbal notes), soda water, cucumber ribbons, mint.  
   - *Why:* Bright, refreshing, and elegant—like an aperol spritz without the orange.  

2. **Tiki-Style "Sunset Sipper"**  
   - *Ingredients:* Non-alcoholic pineapple "gin" (lyre’s botanical), coconut cream, pomegranate syrup, splash of ginger beer.  
   - *Why:* Tropical vibes with vibrant color—perfect for dancing in the living room!  

3. **Spiced Blood Orange Spritz** *(if citrus is *strictly* off-limits, skip the blood orange)*  
   - *Ingredients:* Aperol, prosecco, soda water, rosemary sprig, cinnamon stick.  
   - *Why:* Warm spice notes replace citrus brightness—still celebratory!  

### 🍽️ **Quick & Interactive Menu** (30 mins max prep)  
- **Appetizer:** **"DIY Sushi Rolls"**  
  - *Why:* Pre-portioned nori sheets, cooked shrimp/avocado, cucumber, and vegan mayo. Let your wife assemble—fun and hands-on.  
- **Main:** **"Build-Your-Own Tacos"**  
  - *Toppings:* Black bean-corn salsa, mango-pineapple salsa, chipotle mayo, cilantro, lime crema (skip citrus zest!).  
  - *Base:* Corn tortillas or lettuce cups (gluten-free).  
- **Dessert:** **"Chocolate-Dipped Strawberries"**  
  - *Why:* Melt high-quality dark chocolate (70%+), dip strawberries, sprinkle with crushed pistachios.  

### ✨ **Key Vibes to Elevate the Night**  
- **Lighting:** Turn off overhead lights—use string lights or candles (no wax if worried about spills!).  
- **Music:** Curate a playlist of your "first dance" songs + upbeat classics (e.g., "La Vie en Rose" → "Uptown Funk").  
- **Effort Over Perfection:**  
  - *"Plan B"* is your mantra. If tacos fall apart? Laugh and call them "deconstructed tacos of love."  
  - **"Keep it simple"** mantra: 3 drinks, 3 courses, 1 hour of focused connection.  

### 💡 **Pro Tip: The "Surprise" Element**  
- **Midnight Chocolate Shot:** Hide a glass of melted chocolate in the freezer. After dinner, pour it into coffee cups with crushed nuts—mystery dessert!
