What a romantic theme! Here are 5 ocean-themed meal ideas (considering shellfish allergies & diabetes) and 5 sweet desserts for your anniversary night:

**Main Courses (Ocean-Themed & Safe):**

1.  **Spicy Mango & Shrimp Stir-Fry:** (Use shrimp instead of shellfish). Sauté shrimp with garlic, ginger, bell peppers, snap peas, and carrots. Toss with a vibrant sauce of soy sauce, lime juice, a touch of chili paste (like sambal oelek), and fresh mango chunks. Serve over brown rice. *Theme: Bright, tropical colors.*
2.  **Grilled Salmon with Mango-Habanero Salsa:** (Rich in omega-3s). Season salmon fillets simply. Grill or pan-sear until cooked through. Serve with a fresh salsa of diced mango, red onion, cilantro, lime juice, and a *very* tiny pinch of habanero (adjust heat to taste). *Theme: Fresh, vibrant, coastal.*
3.  **Coconut Curry Tofu & Veggie Bowl:** (Vegan-friendly, creamy). Simmer tofu cubes, broccoli, bell peppers, and snap peas in a fragrant coconut milk curry sauce with ginger, garlic, turmeric, cumin, and a pinch of chili flakes. Garnish with fresh cilantro. Serve over cauliflower rice or quinoa. *Theme: Creamy, exotic, oceanic.*
4.  **Shrimp Tacos with Mango-Lime Slaw:** (Light & fresh). Marinate shrimp in lime juice, chili powder, and a touch of honey. Grilled or pan-seared. Top soft tortillas with seasoned black beans, avocado, and a crunchy slaw of shredded cabbage, carrot, mango, lime juice, and a *tiny* bit of chili powder. *Theme: Fun, casual, beachy.*
5.  **Baked Cod with Mango-Honey Glaze & Coconut Rice:** (Simple & elegant). Season cod fillets with salt, pepper, and paprika. Bake until flaky. Drizzle with a glaze of honey (use less for lower sugar) mixed with lime juice, grated ginger, and a touch of chili powder. Serve over coconut rice cooked with a little coconut milk. *Theme: Elegant, light, tropical.*

**Desserts (Sweet & Ocean-Themed):**

1.  **Mango Coconut Chia Pudding:** (Diet-friendly, no added sugar). Layer chia seeds with coconut milk (light or full-fat) in glasses. Top generously with fresh mango chunks and toasted coconut flakes. *Theme: Simple, elegant, tropical.*
2.  **Spicy Mango & Chili Sorbet:** (Refreshing, low-sugar). Blend fresh mango with a splash of lime juice and a *pinch* of chili powder (adjust to taste). Freeze in an ice cream maker or just freeze in trays. Serve with a sprinkle of toasted coconut. *Theme: Bold, refreshing, tropical.*
3.  **"Tiramisu" with a Twist (Sugar-Free):** (Classic dessert made safe). Use sugar-free instant espresso powder mixed with a little almond extract. Layer sugar-free ladyfingers dipped in this mixture with layers of sweetened (with sugar-free syrup) mango puree or mousse. Dust lightly with sugar-free cocoa. *Theme: Sophisticated, romantic.*
4.  **Coconut Rice Pudding:** (Light & comforting). Simmer rice in coconut milk with a little vanilla extract and a pinch of salt until creamy. Serve warm or chilled, topped with fresh mango cubes and toasted coconut. *Theme: Comforting, creamy, tropical.*
5.  **Mango Mousse:** (Light & elegant). Whip sweetened (with sugar-free syrup) mango puree with a little whipped cream (or coconut whipped cream) and a touch of vanilla until light and fluffy. Serve in small glasses, maybe with a edible flower. *Theme: Light, elegant, tropical.*

**Tips for Your Ocean Night:**

*   **Spice Control:** Be mindful of the chili levels. You can always add more spice, but you can't take it out! Start mild and adjust.
*   **Sweetness Control:** Use ripe mangoes for natural sweetness in mains. For desserts, control added sugars/honey with sugar-free alternatives.
*   **Presentation:** Use blue and green accents (like blue cornflower dishes, green napkins, seashell-shaped spoons). Garnish with edible flowers, mint, or pomegranate seeds.
*   **Ambiance:** Dim lighting, soft music (jazz, acoustic), maybe candles (safely placed!).

Enjoy your incredibly romantic and delicious ocean-themed anniversary celebration!
