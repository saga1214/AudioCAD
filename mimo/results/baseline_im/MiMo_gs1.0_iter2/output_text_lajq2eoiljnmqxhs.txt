That's a great idea! Cooking together can be a fun, collaborative activity that naturally creates conversation without the pressure of deep topics. Here are a few quick, easy, and customizable dish ideas that fit your sister's restrictions:

### 🍝 1. **Pasta with Simple Pesto & Toppings**  
   - **Why it works:** Quick (15-20 mins), customizable, and visually engaging.  
   - **How to adapt:**  
     - Use **lactose-free pesto** (or make your own with nutritional yeast).  
     - Add **non-vegetable toppings** like grilled chicken (if she eats poultry), roasted nuts, or vegan cheese (if she’s open to it).  
     - Skip veggies entirely or add optional greens/herbs.  
   - **Activity tip:** Let her choose toppings—she controls what goes in!

### 🥟 2. **Easy Dumplings (or Veggie Pies)**  
   - **Why it works:** Fun to make together, minimal cooking time (10-15 mins).  
   - **How to adapt:**  
     - Use **vegetable-based filling** (e.g., mushrooms, spinach, carrots) but offer a **pork or chicken alternative** if she’s willing.  
     - Serve with **lactose-free dipping sauce** (tamari + sesame oil).  
   - **Activity tip:** Play music while you cook—makes it feel like a casual hangout.

### 🍳 3. **Build-Your-Own Breakfast Tacos**  
   - **Why it works:** Interactive, fast (10 mins), and customizable.  
   - **How to adapt:**  
     - Base: Corn tortillas (lactose-free).  
     - Fillings: **Eggs**, beans, avocado, salsa, cheese (if tolerated).  
     - Skip seafood/poultry entirely.  
   - **Activity tip:** Set up a "taco bar" with toppings—let her assemble her own.

### 🥧 4. **No-Bake Energy Balls**  
   - **Why it works:** Zero cooking, 10 minutes max, and no dietary restrictions to worry about.  
   - **Base recipe:**  
     - Rolled oats, nut butter, maple syrup, chia seeds, vanilla.  
     - Optional add-ins: chocolate chips, coconut flakes, or seeds.  
   - **Activity tip:** Add a fun twist—make one batch plain, one with chocolate, one with coconut!

### 💡 Key Tips for Success:  
- **Prep together, not cook together:** Assign tasks like chopping, stirring, or decorating.  
- **Keep it light:** Focus on the process, not perfection. Laugh if something burns!  
- **Flexibility is key:** If one hates an ingredient, swap it out immediately—no pressure to finish.  
- **End on a high note:** Wrap up while you’re both still having fun.  

This approach minimizes friction while creating a shared experience. The goal isn’t to fix your relationship overnight—it’s to build small moments of connection. You’ve got this! 🙌
