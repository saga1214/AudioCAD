Okay, you've got your noodles. This is a big step, and it's great you're giving it another shot. Here's how to make sure they come out perfectly.

First, get a big pot of water boiling, and make sure it's a *lot* of water, way more than you think you need. The noodles should have plenty of room to move around.

Then, and this is the most important part, add a pinch of salt to the water, just like when you cook pasta. This makes them taste better.

Now, here's the trick to avoiding disaster: use a *wooden* or *plastic* spoon, not a metal one. Metal can sometimes cause a reaction with the noodles and the water, making them stick together.

Once they're cooked, drain them really well in a colander. Don't be shy with the water, get all that starchy stuff out. This helps them stay separate and not get mushy.

If you want, you can toss them with a little bit of oil right after draining to keep them from sticking to each other. And then just mix them with your stir-fry when you're ready to eat. You've got this, just take it one step at a time.
