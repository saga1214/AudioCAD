That's a fantastic theme choice! Here are two full lunch ideas that lean into the retro tech vibe, are easy to theme, and accommodate common dietary needs:

**Option 1: "The Classic Combo" (Focus on Comfort Food)**

*   **Main Dish:** **"Retro Bolognese"**
    *   A hearty, slow-cooked meat sauce (beef, maybe some lentils for veggie option) over pasta. Serve in a simple, rustic bowl.
    *   *Themed Touch:* Use retro-colored serving bowls (think bright red, yellow, or blue from the '80s) or even a simple bowl with a tech-inspired label like "Data Pack" or "System Boot."
*   **Side Dish:** **Garlic Bread**
    *   Classic, always welcome. Serve on a simple plate or maybe a small "keyboard" shaped cookie cutter pressed into the top for fun (optional).
*   **Salad:** **Simple Green Salad**
    *   Mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, maybe some olives. Dress it simply with a tangy Italian-style dressing (vinaigrette) in a retro-colored bowl or jar.
*   **Snacks:**
    1.  **Potato Chips:** Serve in a container resembling a retro cereal box lid or a small "floppy disk" container.
    2.  **Salted Pretzels:** In a clear bag tied with a ribbon, or in a small container labeled "RAM Pack."
    3.  **Fresh Fruit Salad:** In a bowl with a label like "OS Update."
*   **Dessert (Optional but thematic):** **"Dial-Up" Cookies**
    *   Simple chocolate chip cookies (or oatmeal raisin) baked in batches and labeled with tiny "1-2-3... connect!" stickers or "Loading..." labels.

**Option 2: "The Tech Stack" (Focus on Simplicity & Nostalgia)**

*   **Main Dish:** **"Pixel Perfect" Sandwich/Wrap**
    *   Choose a classic sandwich (like a turkey club, ham & cheese, or veggie wrap) or a simple soup (like tomato, beef & barley, or chicken noodle - very '90s comfort food).
    *   *Themed Touch:* Wrap the sandwich in a small piece of retro tech fabric (like an old monitor sleeve pattern or a beige "office" fabric) or serve in a container shaped like a small "floppy disk" or "CRT monitor" (if you have themed containers).
*   **Side Dish:** **Roasted Vegetables**
    *   Simple carrots, broccoli, or green beans roasted with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Serve in a small bowl or plate.
*   **Salad:** **Classic Caesar Salad**
    *   Crisp romaine, Caesar dressing, croutons, parmesan. Keep it simple and iconic.
*   **Snacks:**
    1.  **Rice Cakes:** Topped with a smear of hummus or avocado for a lighter option. Label "Cache."
    2.  **Hard-Boiled Eggs:** In a small container labeled "CPU."
    3.  **Dried Fruit & Nuts (Check Allergies!):** If nuts are *not* an issue, a small container of raisins, cranberries, and almonds labeled "Data Pack." *Crucial: If nuts are a concern, replace with seeds (pumpkin, sunflower) or just dried fruit.*
*   **Dessert:** **"Error 404: Sweet Found"**
    *   A small cupcake (vanilla, red velvet, or chocolate - timeless) with a tiny paper flag attached that says "Success!" or "Boot Complete."

**Key Considerations for Both Options:**

1.  **Dietary Needs:** Always check for common restrictions (nuts, dairy, gluten, eggs, etc.) *before* finalizing. Use clear labels or separate containers for allergens. Offer substitutions where possible.
2.  **Presentation:** Use simple, retro-themed serving pieces: bowls in bright colors, containers resembling floppy disks or cereal boxes, small signs like "Welcome to the Past!" or "System Ready." Avoid anything too elaborate or costume-like.
3.  **Simplicity:** Focus on classic, comforting foods that are easy to prepare and serve. The theme is about evoking the *feeling* of the era, not necessarily replicating complex dishes.
4.  **Inclusivity:** Ensure there are options for vegetarians/vegans (lentils in bolognese, rice cakes, roasted veggies, fruit salad, some desserts) and various preferences (soup vs. sandwich).

These options provide a full, satisfying meal with snacks and a clear, fun nod to the retro tech theme without being overly complicated or exclusionary. Enjoy your themed lunch and your new role!
