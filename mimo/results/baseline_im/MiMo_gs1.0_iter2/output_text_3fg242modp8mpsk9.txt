That sounds like a wonderful plan! For a romantic coastal weekend focused on oceans and seafood, especially with seasickness to consider, here are some creative and beautiful ideas for activities on the water:

1.  **Private Sunset Kayak/Paddleboard Tour (Calm Bay):**
    *   **Why it fits:** Gentle water, minimal motion, incredible views. Choose a calm bay or lagoon just before sunset.
    *   **Romantic Twist:** Rent matching sets. Bring a small cooler with champagne, cheese, crackers, and maybe a simple seafood platter (like oysters or a small lobster). Paddle slowly, talk, and watch the sky explode with color. Find a quiet spot to pause and enjoy the moment together.

2.  **Private Boat Charter for a Seafood Feast & Exploration:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Ultimate control over motion (stabilized boat) and privacy. You can choose a size and type that feels right (e.g., a small sailboat, a stable catamaran, or even a simple pontoon).
    *   **Romantic Twist:** Hire a chef or do a simple picnic. Pick a destination – a secluded cove, a small island, or a spot with great wildlife viewing. Enjoy a gourmet seafood lunch or dinner (think lobster, oysters, grilled fish) while exploring at your own pace. Anchor in a beautiful spot for an intimate picnic.

3.  **"Coastal Picnic" on a Stable Boat:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Focuses on the scenery and food, less on intense activity. Choose a boat known for stability.
    *   **Romantic Twist:** Pack a beautiful picnic basket with your favorite seafood (chilled lobster, shrimp salad, oysters on the half shell, crusty bread, cheese, wine). Find a calm anchor point near a charming little harbor, a hidden beach, or a spot with interesting rock formations. Enjoy lunch with your toes in the water (if possible) and the sound of the waves.

4.  **Seafood Market Visit & Tasting Cruise:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Combines a culinary experience with a water activity, often on a larger, very stable vessel (like a tour boat or a dedicated tasting barge).
    *   **Romantic Twist:** Visit a local fish market together, pick out fresh seafood, and then board a short, guided tasting cruise (often on a large, stable boat). Enjoy expertly prepared samples of the catch while cruising calmly through the harbor or coastline. It's educational, delicious, and scenic.

5.  **"Sunrise/Sunset" Stargazing or Birdwatching Cruise:**
    *   **Why it fits:** Calm early morning or late afternoon waters are perfect. Many charters offer these focused experiences.
    *   **Romantic Twist:** Choose a boat with a comfortable deck area. Bring a blanket, thermos of coffee or tea, and maybe a simple breakfast or dinner picnic. Watch for dolphins, whales (seasonal), seabirds, or the breathtaking colors of sunrise/sunset. The focus is on shared wonder and quiet observation.

6.  **Gentle Boat Ride to a Scenic Cliff or Lighthouse:**
    *   **Why it fits:** A short, pleasant cruise to a specific, beautiful viewpoint. Choose a boat with a deck that faces the view.
    *   **Romantic Twist:** Pack a small picnic or champagne. Anchor or tie up near the lighthouse or viewpoint. Walk the short path (if accessible) for panoramic views of the coastline, cliffs, or sea. It's a dedicated moment of appreciation for the landscape together.

**Key Considerations for Seasickness:**

*   **Calm Water is King:** Avoid choppy seas, windy conditions, or rough harbors. Bays, coves, lagoons, and protected inlets are ideal.
*   **Stable Vessels:** Opt for boats designed for stability, like catamarans, larger sailboats with good keels, or pontoon boats. Ask the charter company specifically about stability.
*   **Minimal Motion:** Avoid activities that involve sudden turns, drops, or high speeds. Kayaking and paddleboarding are generally very stable if you're experienced.
*   **Short Duration:** Keep the water time manageable, especially for the more prone partner. A 2-3 hour activity is often plenty.
*   **Land-Based Alternatives:** Have a backup plan! If the seas are rough, pivot to a fantastic seafood dinner at a waterfront restaurant, a walk along a beautiful pier, or exploring charming coastal towns.

**Bonus Seafood Idea:** Pair any water activity with a visit to a renowned seafood restaurant! Research the best spots near your accommodation – maybe one with outdoor seating overlooking the water, or a famous oyster bar. The combination of fresh seafood and coastal ambiance is pure romance.

Have an absolutely wonderful and romantic coastal getaway! Enjoy the sea air and the delicious catch.
