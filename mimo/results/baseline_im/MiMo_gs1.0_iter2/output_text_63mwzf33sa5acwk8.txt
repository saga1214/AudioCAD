Okay, so you're planning a trip from Joshua Tree and Nevada to Vegas, and you want a proper Vegas experience after all that desert adventure. That's a great mix of wild and luxurious. Here's how you could do it:

First, after your time in the desert, you can drive into Vegas, which is about a three-hour drive from Joshua Tree. You can either hit the road straight from there or stop at a scenic spot along the way, like the Mojave Desert or the Hoover Dam, for a quick break.

For your hotel, definitely splurge on a nice one. The Wynn or the Bellagio are classics with their fountains and fancy pools. The Cosmopolitan is great for a younger vibe with rooftop bars and live music. Or if you want something more unique, the Venetian has that whole Italian theme with canals and gondolas.

For the actual Vegas experience, you should hit up some of the classic spots like the Bellagio Fountains, the Mirage volcano, and the Fremont Street Experience in the downtown area. It's free and has amazing street performers and neon lights. You can also catch a show, like Cirque du Soleil or a big headliner at a theater.

As for food, you can go for the over-the-top buffets like Bacchanal at the Buffet, or try a fancy restaurant like Joël Robuchon at the MGM Grand. Or if you want something fun, there's the Eggslut food truck at The Cosmopolitan.

And for a night out, the clubs like XS at the Wynn or Omnia at Caesars are top-notch, but they can be pricey. Alternatively, you could just grab a drink at a rooftop bar like Sky Lounge at the Aria for some great views.

Finally, just remember to pace yourselves. Vegas is fast and loud, so maybe take a day to just relax at the hotel pool or explore the quieter parts of the Strip. It's all about mixing the excitement with some downtime. Enjoy your trip!
