For a Halloween party, focus on **universal crowd-pleasers** that appeal to all ages and avoid niche or overly sweet items. Here’s a breakdown of top candy choices to minimize leftovers:

### 🎃 Top Crowd-Pleasing Candies:
1. **M&M’s or Skittles**  
   *Why*: Universally loved, colorful, and easy to eat. Skittles have a bright, tangy appeal.  
   *Tip*: Mini packs or mixed colors keep variety fun.

2. **Gummies (Gobstoppers, Haribo)**  
   *Why*: Soft, chewy, and kid/adult-friendly. Haribo’s sour gummies are a hit.  
   *Avoid*: Exotic flavors (e.g., durian gummies)—stick to standard fruit.

3. **Sour Patch Kids or Warheads**  
   *Why*: Sweet-tart balance appeals to thrill-seekers. Fun texture contrast.  
   *Tip*: Mini bags reduce bulk.

4. **Chocolate Bars (Snickers, Twix, Reese’s)**  
   *Why*: Filling, nostalgic, and universally enjoyed.  
   *Avoid*: Overly heavy items (e.g., giant chocolate bars)—mini packs work best.

5. **Themed Candy**  
   *Why*: Adds fun! Examples:  
   - *Frankenstein’s Breath* mints  
   - *Zombie Oreos* (if available)  
   - *Witch’s Broom* chocolate-covered marshmallows  

6. **International Favorites**  
   *Why*: Broaden appeal:  
   - *KitKat* (especially the rice-flavored version)  
   - *Pretzels* (Snyder’s or Haribo)  
   - *Chocodiles* (Cadbury’s)  

### 🚫 Candies to Avoid:
- **Overly Sweet**: Taffy, gummy worms (often rejected).  
- **Allergen Traps**: Nut-based candies (unless labeled safe).  
- **Bland or Boring**: Plain chocolate bars, generic licorice.  

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Mix & Match**: Combine 3–4 types (e.g., M&M’s + Skittles + gummies + chocolate bars).  
- **Portion Control**: Use small bags or bowls to encourage sharing.  
- **Theme It**: Label bowls with spooky names like "Witch’s Brew" or "Zombie’s Snack."  

With this mix, you’ll cater to diverse tastes while keeping leftovers minimal. Happy haunting! 👻
