### Spaghetti Carbonara Recipe (Serves 4)
**Ingredients**:
| **Category**       | **Ingredients**                                                                 |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Pasta**          | 400g spaghetti (or bucatini)                                                   |
| **Protein**        | 200g pancetta (diced) or guanciale (traditional)                               |
|                    | 1 large egg + 1 egg yolk (room temp)                                            |
|                    | 50g Pecorino Romano cheese (grated)                                              |
|                    | 50g Parmigiano Reggiano cheese (grated)                                         |
| **Seasoning**      | Salt (for pasta water)                                                          |
|                    | Freshly ground black pepper                                                       |
| **Garnish**        | Extra grated Pecorino Romano and black pepper                                     |

**Instructions**:
1. **Cook Pasta**: Boil spaghetti in salted water until *al dente* (reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining).
2. **Sauté Pancetta**: In a pan over medium heat, cook diced pancetta until crisp (don’t drain fat—keep it!).
3. **Mix Sauce**: In a bowl, whisk eggs, yolk, grated cheeses, and pepper. Temper by slowly adding 2–3 tbsp hot pancetta fat to the egg mix while whisking.
4. **Combine**: Add drained pasta to the pancetta. Toss with tongs, removing from heat. Pour egg mixture over pasta, stirring vigorously to emulsify into a creamy sauce (add pasta water if needed).
5. **Serve Immediately**: Plate and top with extra cheese, pepper, and a drizzle of olive oil (optional).

**Key Tips**:
- **No cream**: Authentic carbonara relies on eggs and cheese.
- **Off-heat**: Never cook the sauce on the stove—it should be mixed with hot pasta.
- **Pancetta**: Guanciale (cured pork cheek) is ideal, but pancetta works. Avoid bacon (smoky flavor ruins it).
