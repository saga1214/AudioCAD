That's a great point. Older comics are definitely much rarer, and that's why they're so valuable. You're right, modern comics can be printed in huge numbers, but older issues were produced in limited quantities.

Here are ten rare and valuable comics from the 1940s and 50s that are good investment targets. You should be able to find affordable copies in mid-grade condition.

First, *All-Star Comics* number eight, from 1943. It's the first appearance of the Justice League. Then there's *Adventure Comics* number 250, from 1953, which has the first full appearance of the Legion of Super-Heroes. For a Spider-Man key, *Amazing Spider-Man* number three, from 1963, is the first appearance of Doctor Connors, the Lizard.

If you want something with a big jump in value, look for *Fantastic Four* number one from 1961. It's the first appearance of the Fantastic Four, and it's seen major price increases. *Green Lantern* number one from 1966 is a great buy, as it's the first appearance of Hal Jordan. Another one is *The Incredible Hulk* number one from 1962, the first appearance of the Hulk.

For a classic horror comic, *Tales from the Crypt* number one from 1950 is a must. And *Strange Tales* number one from 1950 is the first appearance of the Silver Surfer. Finally, *Thor* number one from 1962 is the first appearance of Thor, and it's a very desirable book.

Just remember, the condition of the comic is the most important thing. Always look for one that's been graded by a professional service like CGC, as that provides a guarantee of its quality. Happy collecting!
