That's awesome! Your coffee table is all set, and now it's time to fill it with fun snacks. Since you're not into sweet things, here are some savory and salty ideas from a vending machine.

You could grab some pretzels, which are salty and crunchy. Or maybe some cheese puffs or cheese sticks for that cheesy, salty kick. Potato chips are a classic choice, and you could go for a flavor like BBQ, sour cream and onion, or original. If they have them, beef jerky is a great protein-packed option. You could also grab some crackers to pair with cheese or dip. If you want something a little more unique, maybe some seaweed snacks, which are light and salty, or some roasted chickpeas, which are crunchy and savory. You could even get some flavored popcorn, like cheddar or bacon, if you want something crunchy but not overly sweet. And for a healthier option, some nuts or trail mix would be perfect.

Enjoy your new table and all the snacks!
