Okay, let's see. You need a pick-me-up. You're tired, thirsty, and sleepy, but you can't fall asleep yet. I get it. You need some energy and something to get you going. So, I'll suggest five drinks and five snacks that can help boost your energy. 

For drinks, I'd recommend an energy drink like Monster or Red Bull. They have caffeine and sugar, so they're a quick pick-me-up. Another good one is a cup of coffee, black or with a little milk. It's a classic for a reason. Green tea is also a good option; it has caffeine but less than coffee and has some good antioxidants. You could also try a sports drink like Gatorade, especially if you're feeling drained. And finally, a glass of orange juice. It's full of natural sugars and vitamin C, which can give you a quick boost.

As for snacks, I'd suggest some nuts, like almonds or peanuts. They're high in protein and healthy fats, so they give you sustained energy. A piece of fruit, like an apple or a banana, is also great. They're easy to digest and provide natural sugars. You could also have some yogurt, either Greek or regular, for protein. A granola bar is another good choice; it has a mix of carbs and fats. And finally, some dark chocolate, maybe 70 percent or higher, for a bit of caffeine and antioxidants.

So there you go, five drinks and five snacks to help you out. Hope that helps you feel a bit more awake and ready to tackle whatever you need to do!
