Here are five excellent green vegetables safe and nutritious for your parrot, keeping Bruno's sensitivities in mind:

1.  **Romaine Lettuce:** A great all-around starter green. Very mild, crunchy, and low in oxalates. Easy to digest and provides hydration.
2.  **Spinach (in moderation):** Rich in iron, calcium, and vitamins A, C, and K. **Important:** Spinach is high in oxalates, which can bind to calcium and reduce its absorption. Offer it occasionally, not daily, and pair it with calcium sources like crushed eggshell or cuttlebone.
3.  **Cucumber (Peppermint):** A fantastic, crunchy, low-calorie hydrator. Very easy for parrots to digest and offers vitamins K and C. The cool, refreshing taste is usually a big hit.
4.  **Bok Choy:** A nutrient powerhouse! High in vitamins A, C, K, calcium, potassium, and fiber. The tender leaves and stems are usually safe and well-tolerated. Chop finely if needed.
5.  **Swiss Chard:** Similar to spinach in nutrients (vitamins A, C, K, calcium, magnesium), but generally has lower oxalate levels than spinach. The leaves are tender, and the stems can be chopped finely. Remove the tough center ribs if possible.

**Key Considerations for Your Parrot:**

*   **Introduce Slowly:** Add new veggies one at a time in small amounts to monitor for any digestive upset or allergies.
*   **Fresh is Best:** Always provide fresh, clean vegetables daily. Avoid pre-cut or bagged greens unless specifically labeled safe for birds.
*   **Wash Thoroughly:** Scrub all vegetables well under running water to remove any pesticides or dirt.
*   **Chop Appropriately:** Cut larger vegetables into small, bite-sized pieces suitable for your bird's size and chewing ability.
*   **Variety is Key:** Rotate different greens to ensure a broad range of nutrients. Avoid making any single green a staple.
*   **Avoid Toxic Greens:** Steer clear of iceberg lettuce (very low nutritional value), Italian parsley (can be toxic), and any vegetables treated with pesticides or fertilizers.
*   **Monitor Bruno:** Since Bruno is sensitive to broccoli, be extra cautious when introducing any new green. Watch for signs like loose stool, lethargy, or a loss of appetite.

Enjoy offering these healthy greens to Bruno!
