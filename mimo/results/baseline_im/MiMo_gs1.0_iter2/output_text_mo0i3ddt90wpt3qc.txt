Absolutely! Here are a few more gluten-free, portable, and energizing snack ideas perfect for your trip:

1.  **Mixed Nuts & Seeds:** A classic! Pack individual packs of almonds, walnuts, pecans, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, and maybe some chia or flax seeds. Look for pre-portioned bags or make your own.
2.  **Trail Mix:** Create your own mix! Combine the nuts and seeds mentioned above with **gluten-free** dried fruit like cranberries, raisins, apricots, blueberries, or mango chunks. Avoid mixes with malted balls or chocolate-covered pretzels (check labels).
3.  **Roasted Chickpeas:** Crunchy, satisfying, and packed with protein and fiber. Find single-serve bags or make your own by roasting canned chickpeas (rinsed and dried) with a little oil and spices (like paprika, garlic powder, or cumin).
4.  **Rice Cakes:** Light, crisp, and versatile. Pack plain rice cakes and bring along:
    *   Single-serve nut butter packets (peanut, almond, sunflower seed butter)
    *   Sliced avocado (pack with lemon juice to prevent browning)
    *   Single-serve hummus
    *   Sliced cucumber or bell peppers for topping
5.  **Fresh Fruit:** Nature's energy bomb! Pack easy-to-eat fruits like apples, bananas, oranges, grapes, berries (in a small container), or pears. Wash them before packing if possible.
6.  **Fresh Veggies & Single-Serve Dip:** Pack baby carrots, cucumber slices, bell pepper strips, or celery sticks. Pair with single-serve packs of hummus, guacamole, or ranch dressing (check labels for GF).
7.  **Dark Chocolate:** A small portion of high-cocoa dark chocolate (70%+ cacao) is a great source of antioxidants and can be a nice energy boost. Look for brands that are certified gluten-free (many are, but it's good to check).
8.  **Coconut Chips:** Crunchy, slightly sweet, and gluten-free. Found in health food stores or online. Single-serve packs are ideal.
9.  **Jerky:** Choose beef, turkey, or plant-based jerky (like soy or mushroom jerky) that is certified gluten-free. Check labels for potential cross-contamination warnings if very strict.

**Key Tip:** Always double-check labels for "Gluten-Free" certification or ingredients to ensure safety, especially for items like jerky, trail mix, rice cakes, or chocolate. Pack snacks in small containers or reusable bags to keep them fresh and prevent messes! Enjoy your trip!
