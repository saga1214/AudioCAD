That's a great question! A 15-minute break is perfect for a quick recharge. Here are some effective options, ranked by how well they fit your situation:

1.  **A Short Walk (5-10 mins):** This is often the *best* choice for a quick reset. It gets blood flowing, clears mental fog, and provides a natural energy boost without the potential crash of caffeine. Step outside if possible – fresh air is incredibly refreshing.
2.  **Deep Breathing or Mini-Meditation (5-10 mins):** Since a longer session feels jarring, focus on a *very* short, gentle practice. Try 5 minutes of focused breathing (inhale 4 counts, hold 4, exhale 6) or simply sitting quietly and noticing your senses (sounds, sensations) without judgment. Apps like Calm or Headspace have excellent 3-5 minute guided options.
3.  **Hydration + Stretch (5 mins):** Drink a full glass of water (or your orange juice). Then, do a few simple stretches: reach for the sky, roll your shoulders, gently twist your torso, stretch your legs. This combats stiffness and boosts circulation.
4.  **Listen to Something Uplifting (Like Your Music!):** Continue listening to your Ali Farka Touré or the other recommendations! Let the music wash over you for 10-15 minutes. It can be a powerful mood and energy booster.
5.  **Power Nap (If *Truly* Exhausted - 10 mins MAX):** *Only* consider this if you are *certain* you can fall asleep quickly and wake up naturally at 10 minutes. Set an alarm! A nap too short can leave you groggy ("sleep inertia"), while one too long can make you feel worse. If you're unsure, skip this.
6.  **Hydrate + Snack (5 mins):** If you're genuinely hungry, a small, protein-rich snack (like a piece of fruit, a few nuts, or yogurt) with water can provide steady energy. Avoid heavy carbs/sugar.

**Recommendation for You:**

Given you mentioned the music helps you stay alert and you're doing light exercise (standing desk), **combining options 1 and 4 is ideal:**

*   **Take a 5-10 minute walk** (outside if possible).
