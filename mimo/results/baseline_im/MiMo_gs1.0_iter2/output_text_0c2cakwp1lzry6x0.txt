Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your TV mount, hanging planters, and floating shelves safely and effectively:

### 📺 **1. TV Mount Installation**
- **Tools Needed:** Tape measure, pencil, stud finder, drill, wall anchors (if not in studs), screwdriver.  
- **Steps:**  
  1. **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall studs (wood frames) 16–24 inches apart.  
  2. **Measure & Mark:** Measure the TV’s width and height. Mark the center of the mount’s top/bottom edges on the wall.  
  3. **Drill Pilot Holes:** If mounting *over* studs, drill holes aligned with stud positions. If *in* drywall without studs, use heavy-duty anchors.  
  4. **Install Mount:** Secure the mount to the wall with screws. **Test stability** before attaching the TV.  
  5. **Mount TV:** Align the TV onto the mount and secure with bolts. **Never exceed the mount’s weight limit** (check manufacturer specs).  

### 🌿 **2. Hanging Planters**
- **Tools Needed:** Drill, wall anchors (if not in studs), hook/chain, wire (optional).  
- **Steps:**  
  1. **Choose a Spot:** Install a hook or use an over-the-door hanger above a window or near a plant stand.  
  2. **Secure Anchor:** If drilling into drywall, use toggle bolts or heavy-duty anchors rated for the planter’s weight.  
  3. **Attach Planter:** Hang the planter from the hook/chain. Ensure drainage holes are open to prevent water damage.  
  *Tip:* Place heavier plants lower to avoid strain on the anchor.  

### 📚 **3. Floating Shelves**
- **Tools Needed:** Level, drill, wall anchors (if not in studs), shelf brackets, screws.  
- **Steps:**  
  1. **Measure Twice:** Mark shelf positions 6–12 inches apart. Use a level to ensure they’re parallel.  
  2. **Drill Pilot Holes:** If attaching to studs, drill holes directly into them. For drywall, use toggle bolts or heavy-duty anchors.  
  3. **Install Brackets:** Secure brackets to the wall with screws.  
  4. **Attach Shelf:** Place the shelf onto brackets and screw it down. **Test weight capacity** before adding decor.  
  *Tip:* Add adhesive hooks for lightweight items (e.g., small plants, photo frames).  

### 🔧 **Key Safety Tips**
- **Always check wall types** (wood studs vs. drywall) using a stud finder.  
- **Use wall anchors rated for 50–100 lbs** for heavy items.  
- **Avoid overhangs**—shelves should not extend beyond their anchor points.  
- **Work in stages:** Install one item at a time to avoid fatigue.  

### 🛠️ **Tool Recommendations**
- **Stud Finder:** Carhartt Laser 100 (budget) or Bosch HDM 20 (premium).  
- **Drill:** Cordless drill with adjustable torque (e.g., DeWalt 20V).  
- **Anchors:** Heavy-duty toggle bolts for drywall, or plastic anchors for lightweight items.  

### 📅 **Timeline**
- **TV Mount:** 1–2 hours (if studs are accessible).  
- **Planters:** 15–30 minutes.  
- **Shelves:** 30–60 minutes per shelf.  

Take breaks between tasks to avoid strain! Once installed, your space will look polished and functional. Happy decorating! 🎨✨
