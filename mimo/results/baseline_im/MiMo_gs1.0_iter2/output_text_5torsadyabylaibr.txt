Absolutely, a pie is a perfect Thanksgiving gesture. Since you're looking for something easy, I have a couple of great options for you. A classic pumpkin pie is always a crowd-pleaser. It's very simple to make and you can even find pre-made crusts to make it even easier.

Another fantastic choice, especially for Thanksgiving, is a apple crisp. It's very easy to assemble and has a wonderful, warm, comforting flavor. Both are excellent choices for showing your appreciation.
