That's a common challenge! If cheese isn't her favorite, here are some highly enticing alternatives for training your Maltese, Maisy, along with specific liver recommendations:

**Highly Enticing Treats (Small & Crunchy):**

1.  **Freeze-Dried Liver:**
    *   **Why it works:** This is often the *ultimate* treat for dogs, especially small breeds. The intense, concentrated meaty flavor and texture (crunchy!) is incredibly motivating.
    *   **What to look for:** Look for brands specifically labeled for small dogs or "bite-sized" pieces. Popular options include:
        *   **Sweet 'n Salty:** Offers various flavors like liver, chicken, or beef. Their "Small Breed" or "Bite Size" options are perfect. (Check ingredients for xylitol!).
        *   **Healthy Spot:** Known for high-quality, human-grade ingredients. Their freeze-dried liver comes in small pieces.
        *   **Ziwi Peak:** Makes excellent freeze-dried liver bites (check size and xylitol).
        *   **The Honest Kitchen:** Offers freeze-dried liver in small, convenient portions.
    *   **Key:** Ensure it's **freeze-dried, not dehydrated or air-dried**. Avoid any with xylitol (artificial sweetener toxic to dogs).

2.  **Small, Soft Chicken Treats:**
    *   **Why it works:** Chicken is a universally loved flavor. Look for single-ingredient or minimally processed options.
    *   **What to look for:** Small, bite-sized pieces of cooked, plain chicken breast. Avoid seasoned or raw chicken for beginners. Brands like **Open Farm** or **Farmina N&D** offer small, single-ingredient chicken treats. Plain boiled chicken is also great.

3.  **Pumpkin Seeds (Pepitas):**
    *   **Why it works:** Crunchy, slightly sweet (to a dog), and high in healthy fats. Small dogs love the texture.
    *   **What to look for:** Raw or roasted pumpkin seeds (pepitas) without added salt or oil. Buy them raw and toast them lightly in a pan for extra crunch and flavor (cool completely first).

4.  **Boiled Egg (Yolk):**
    *   **Why it works:** Rich, creamy, and very appealing. The yolk is especially motivating.
    *   **What to look for:** Cook a whole egg, then separate the yolk. Mash a tiny bit of yolk for a treat. Ensure the egg is fresh and cooked thoroughly.

**Other Appealing Options:**

*   **Small Meat Sticks:** Look for brands specifically made for small breeds (like **TastyBites** or **Small Paws**). Choose plain beef or turkey flavors.
*   **Peanut Butter (Xylitol-Free!):** A tiny dot on your finger is often more enticing than a whole treat. **Crucial:** Only use **natural peanut butter with NO XYLITOL**. Xylitol is deadly. Skippy and Jif contain it. Look for brands like **Skippy Natural** (xylitol-free) or **No Sugar Added** varieties.
*   **Fish Treats:** Small, cooked salmon or mackerel bites are often very enticing.

**Important Considerations:**

1.  **Size Matters:** Maisy is tiny! Cut treats into **very small pieces** or use the tiniest bits available (like freeze-dried liver pieces). You want her to be able to swallow them easily.
2.  **Texture:** Maltese often love crunchy textures. Freeze-dried liver, pumpkin seeds, and small meat sticks fit this perfectly.
3.  **Avoid Xylitol:** **NEVER** use peanut butter, gum, or any sugar-free products containing xylitol.
4.  **Dental Health:** While freeze-dried liver and seeds are crunchy (good for teeth), consider rotating in softer options like cooked chicken or egg yolk sometimes. Avoid very hard chews that could damage small teeth.
5.  **Start Simple:** Begin with just 1-2 tricks per session (like "sit" and "look at me"). Use the most enticing treat (like freeze-dried liver) for these basics. Gradually add more complex tricks later.
6.  **Hand as a Reward:** Maisy might be more motivated by a gentle touch on the nose or a pat from your hand combined with a treat at first, especially if she's new to training.

**Recommendation for Maisy:** **Freeze-dried liver bites** (like Sweet 'n Salty Small Breed Liver or Healthy Spot Freeze-Dried Liver) are your best bet for maximum motivation and that satisfying crunch she'll love. Pair it with short, enthusiastic training sessions!

Good luck with Maisy! Training is a wonderful bonding experience.
