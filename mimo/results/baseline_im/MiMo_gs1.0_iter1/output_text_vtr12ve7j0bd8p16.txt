The Calgary area is great for outdoor adventures. Let's see...

If you're up for hiking and mountain scenery, Banff is only about an hour and a half away. You could check out Johnston Canyon, or hike to Lake Louise. The Icefields Parkway is also stunning.

For a more rugged, adventurous feel, you could drive to Kananaskis Country, which is about an hour and a half out. It has great trails like the Kananaskis Lake Loop.

If you prefer water activities, you're in luck. Calgary has the Bow River and the Elbow River, where you can rent kayaks, canoes, or paddleboards. You could even try a float trip down the Bow River, which is usually a float, tube, and swim kind of day.

And if you want to get out on the water and see some wildlife, you can take a boat tour on the Bow River to see beavers, muskrats, and otters. You can even paddle into the Kananaskis Lake from there.
