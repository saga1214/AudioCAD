### 💡 Pro Tips to Fill Your Table Fast:
- **Buy in Bulk**: Vending machines often have "mix packs" – grab a bag of chips *and* a bag of pretzels for instant variety.  
- **Layer It**: Stack chips under crackers, tuck jerky behind gummies, and pile candy on top.  
- **Color Contrast**: Use red gummies, yellow cheese puffs, and green veggies to make it visually appealing!  
- **Themed Bowls**: Make a "sweet corner" (chocolate + gummies) and a "savory zone" (chips + jerky).
