Here's a comprehensive shopping list for your 3-course meal, keeping legumes, seafood, and fish sauce out:

**Starter: Miso Soup & Cucumber Salad**
*   **Miso Soup:**
    *   Dried Shiitake Mushrooms (or Kombu Seaweed - *ensure no fish sauce*)
    *   Dashi Granules (or Bonito Flakes - *check label for fish*)
    *   Miso Paste (white or red, check label for fish sauce)
    *   Fresh Cucumber (1-2)
    *   Optional: Green Onion (1)
*   **Cucumber Salad:**
    *   Cucumber (1-2)
    *   Rice Vinegar (1-2 tbsp)
    *   Sugar (1-2 tbsp)
    *   Sesame Oil (1 tsp)
    *   Salt (pinch)

**Main Course: Chicken Teriyaki with Stir-Fried Veggies & Jasmine Rice**
*   **Chicken Teriyaki:**
    *   Boneless, Skinless Chicken Thighs or Breast (4-6 oz per person)
    *   Teriyaki Sauce (store-bought - *check label for fish sauce*)
    *   Optional: Minced Ginger (1 tsp)
    *   Optional: Minced Garlic (1 clove)
    *   Optional: Soy Sauce (or Tamari for GF)
    *   Optional: Sesame Seeds (for garnish)
*   **Stir-Fried Veggies:**
    *   Bok Choy (1 head, chopped)
    *   Bell Peppers (1-2, sliced - any color)
    *   Carrots (1-2, julienned or thinly sliced)
    *   Optional: Snap Peas or Broccoli Florets
    *   Cooking Oil (2-3 tbsp)
    *   Optional: Minced Garlic (1 clove)
    *   Optional: Minced Ginger (1 tsp)
    *   Optional: Soy Sauce (or Tamari for GF)
*   **Jasmine Rice:**
    *   Jasmine Rice (enough for 3 servings)
    *   Water (as per package directions)
    *   Optional: Pinch of Salt

**Dessert: Matcha Ice Cream & Mango Sticky Rice**
*   **Matcha Ice Cream:**
    *   Matcha Powder (1-2 tbsp, *high quality*)
    *   Heavy Cream (2 cups, *full fat*)
    *   Sweetened Condensed Milk (1 can, 14 oz)
    *   Vanilla Extract (1 tsp, *optional*)
    *   Salt (pinch)
    *   Optional: Vanilla Bean Paste (1-2 tsp)
*   **Mango Sticky Rice:**
    *   Fresh Ripe Mango (2-3 large, sliced)
    *   Sweetened Coconut Milk (canned, full fat - 1 can, drained)
    *   Jasmine Rice (enough cooked rice for 3 servings - *leftover or cook*)
    *   Optional: Lime Juice (a few drops)
    *   Optional: Toasted Coconut Flakes (for garnish)

**Condiments & Seasonings (Essential):**
*   Soy Sauce (or Tamari for GF)
*   Rice Vinegar
*   Sesame Oil
*   Sugar
*   Salt
*   Black Pepper (freshly ground)
*   Optional: Garlic Powder, Onion Powder, White Pepper

**Key Considerations:**

1.  **Check Labels:** *Crucial!* Double-check *all* packaged items (Teriyaki sauce, Soy Sauce/Tamari, Miso Paste, Sweetened Coconut Milk, Rice, Matcha Powder) for hidden fish sauce, shrimp paste, or legumes. Look for "Fish-Free," "Shrimp-Free," or "Legume-Free" labels if possible.
2.  **Miso:** Dashi made from kombu and shiitake is safe. Bonito flakes contain fish. White miso is generally milder and often has less fish sauce than red miso, but still verify.
3.  **Matcha Ice Cream:** If you don't have matcha powder, you can often buy a pre-made mix or paste from a health food store, but pure powder is best for flavor.
4.  **Rice:** Cooked jasmine rice makes the Mango Sticky Rice dessert. You can use leftover rice from the main course.
5.  **Mango:** Choose ripe but firm mangoes for easy slicing and to prevent mushiness.

This list provides everything you need for a delicious, flavorful, and allergy-conscious meal! Enjoy your special dinner!
