### 🍫 Molten Chocolate Graham Cracker Pie  
*(Serves 8-10 | Prep: 15 mins | Bake: 25-30 mins)*

**Ingredients:**  
- **Crust:**  
  - 10–12 graham cracker sheets  
  - 5 tbsp melted butter  
  - 2 tbsp sugar  
  - Pinch of salt  

- **Filling:**  
  - 8 oz (225g) semi-sweet chocolate, chopped  
  - 1 cup (240g) cream cheese, softened  
  - ½ cup (120ml) heavy cream  
  - ¼ cup (50g) sugar  
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract  
  - ¼ tsp salt  

**Instructions:**  
1. **Prep Crust:**  
   - Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).  
   - In a 9-inch springform pan, crush graham crackers into crumbs. Mix with melted butter, sugar, and salt. Press firmly into the bottom. Bake for 5 mins, then cool.  

2. **Make Filling:**  
   - Melt chocolate in a double boiler (or microwave in 30-sec bursts).  
   - Beat cream cheese until smooth. Add melted chocolate, sugar, vanilla, and salt. Mix well.  
   - Slowly whisk in heavy cream until silky.  

3. **Assemble & Bake:**  
   - Pour filling over the crust. Smooth the top.  
   - Bake 25–30 mins until edges are set but center is *slightly* jiggly.  
   - Cool completely (crucial for molten centers!). Chill 2+ hours.  

4. **Serve:**  
   - Warm slightly before serving. Top with whipped cream or ice cream.  

### ✅ Why It Works for Thanksgiving:  
- **Pan-based simplicity:** No fancy tools.  
- **Crowd-pleaser:** Rich chocolate + graham cracker combo is universally loved.  
- **Comforting:** Feels festive yet easy to make ahead.  
- **Molten center:** A guaranteed crowd-pleaser when served warm!  

Pair with coffee or hot cocoa for an extra cozy vibe. Happy baking! 🍁
