Based on your need to stick something to a jacket lining without damaging it, here's a breakdown of the best methods:

1.  **Fabric Glue (Strongest & Most Invisible):**
    *   **Best Choice:** Use a high-quality **fabric glue** specifically designed for synthetic fabrics (like polyester, which is common in jackets). Look for brands like **Aleene's Fabric Fusion**, **Siser HeatBond**, or **E6000 Fabric Glue** (use sparingly).
    *   **How to Apply:** Apply a *tiny* amount of glue to the *back* of the item you want to stick (e.g., a name tag, a patch, a label). Press it firmly onto the lining of the jacket for 10-15 seconds. Wipe away any excess glue immediately with a soft cloth.
    *   **Pros:** Very strong bond, usually invisible when dry, holds well.
    *   **Cons:** Requires patience, test on a hidden seam first.

2.  **Spray Adhesive (Good for Larger Areas):**
    *   **Best Choice:** Use a **fabric-safe spray adhesive** (like Aleene's Tacky Glue Spray or a specific fabric spray).
    *   **How to Apply:** Lightly spray the *back* of the item and lightly spray a *small, inconspicuous area* of the lining. Press them together firmly and hold for 10-15 seconds. Wipe off excess immediately.
    *   **Pros:** Good for larger items or patches.
    *   **Cons:** Can be messy, requires ventilation, test first.

3.  **Double-Sided Tape (Easiest, Least Obtrusive):**
    *   **Best Choice:** Use **clear, low-tack double-sided tape** (like Scotch Magic Tape or double-sided tape made for fabric).
    *   **How to Apply:** Cut a piece slightly smaller than the item you want to stick. Apply the sticky side *down* onto the lining. Carefully peel off the paper backing and press the item firmly onto the tape.
    *   **Pros:** Very easy, clean, minimal residue, removable if needed.
    *   **Cons:** May not be as strong as glue for very heavy items, can be visible if the item is large or textured.

4.  **Avoid Iron-On (Not Recommended):**
    *   **Why:** Ironing will almost certainly melt or distort a synthetic jacket lining (like polyester). Only use iron-on if you're absolutely certain the lining is cotton or a heat-stabilized material (check the care label).

**Key Tips for Success:**

*   **Test First:** Always test your chosen adhesive on a hidden seam or scrap fabric of the same lining material to check for colorfastness and damage.
*   **Less is More:** Use the smallest amount of adhesive necessary. Too much glue or spray can seep out or cause bubbling.
*   **Wipe Excess Immediately:** If any adhesive seeps out, wipe it away immediately with a clean, soft cloth.
*   **Press Firmly:** Apply firm, even pressure for the recommended time (usually 10-30 seconds).
*   **Patience:** Allow ample drying/curing time before wearing or moving the jacket significantly.

**Recommendation:** For most situations (like a name tag, small patch, or label), **double-sided tape** is the easiest and least risky option. For something heavier or larger, **fabric glue** applied carefully is the best choice. Avoid spray adhesives if you're concerned about mess. Always test first!
