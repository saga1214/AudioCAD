Great question! Here are some super quick ingredients that can make dinner prep lightning-fast:

1.  **Eggs:** So versatile! Scramble, fry, make an omelet, or bake. Pair with pre-cooked rice/pasta or toast.
2.  **Canned Beans/Lentils:** Chickpeas, black beans, lentils – ready to go. Add to salads, soups, or mix with a little broth for a quick stew.
3.  **Frozen Veggies:** No chopping needed! Thaw quickly or cook straight from the bag (steam, stir-fry, roast).
4.  **Pre-Cooked Grains:** Rice (white or brown), quinoa, couscous, or pasta. Keep a bag in the pantry/fridge for instant bases.
5.  **Sauces & Condiments:** Stir-fry sauce, salsa, pesto, hummus, soy sauce, tahini. Mix with protein and veggies.
6.  **Pre-Cut Veggies:** Bagged salad kits or pre-sliced veggies (peppers, onions, carrots) save tons of chopping time.
7.  **Frozen Protein:** Frozen shrimp, chicken strips, or tofu cubes can be stir-fried or baked in minutes.
8.  **Canned Tuna/Salmon:** Mix with mayo, mustard, or Greek yogurt, add to lettuce cups or crackers for a salad.

**Quick Idea:** Toss pre-cooked quinoa or rice with canned chickpeas, frozen spinach (thawed), a jar of pesto, and maybe some cherry tomatoes. Done in 5 minutes!

Keep these things stocked, and you'll always have a super-fast dinner option ready!
