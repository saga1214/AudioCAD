Here are five fantastic road trip options from Vancouver, blending stunning scenery with diverse activities and dining:

1.  **Victoria & Vancouver Island (3 hours):**
    *   **Activities:** Explore Butchart Gardens, tour the Fairmont Empress Hotel, visit the Royal BC Museum, ride the Inner Harbour Ferris wheel, walk through the historic Inner Harbour, visit the Parliament Buildings.
    *   **Food:** Upscale: Scavara (Victoria), Restaurant O (Victoria). Fine Dining: Restaurant 191 (Penticton, nearby winery stop). Farm-to-Table: The Keg (Penticton), Barn at Mission San Jose (Fraser Valley stop).

2.  **Okanagan Valley Wine Country & Lake District (4 hours):**
    *   **Activities:** Wine tasting at numerous wineries (Okanagan Valley), swimming in Kananaskis Lake, hiking in Kicking Horse Canyon, exploring the town of Penticton, visiting the Okanagan Lake Shoreline.
    *   **Food:** Upscale: Scavara (Victoria), Restaurant O (Penticton). Fine Dining: Restaurant 191 (Penticton). Farm-to-Table: The Keg (Penticton), Barn at Mission San Jose (Fraser Valley stop).

3.  **Fraser Valley (2.5 hours):**
    *   **Activities:** Wine tasting (Spinnakers Winery, Barn at Mission San Jose), farm stays, cheese tasting (Smylie Family Cheese), exploring the town of Agassiz, enjoying local markets.
    *   **Food:** Upscale: Bistro 321 (Agassiz). Fine Dining: Restaurant 191 (Penticton). Farm-to-Table: Barn at Mission San Jose (Agassiz), The Keg (Penticton).

4.  **Columbia Icefield & Jasper National Park (6 hours):**
    *   **Activities:** Hike Maligne Canyon, visit the Columbia Icefield Skywalk, take a Grand Columbia Ice Exploration Tour, drive the Icefields Parkway (one of the world's most scenic), spot wildlife (bears, elk, bison).
    *   **Food:** Upscale: Mountain Lodge Restaurant (Jasper). Fine Dining: Restaurant at Icefields Park Visitor Centre (Icefields). Farm-to-Table: Local joints in Jasper.

5.  **West Coast Wilderness & Tofino (5.5 hours):**
    *   **Activities:** Surf at Long Beach, hike in rainforests (Tofino, Ucluelet), visit the Rainforest Discovery Centre, watch orcas from the coast, kayak on the Pacific, explore the unique coastal ecosystem.
    *   **Food:** Upscale: Keg Tofino (Tofino). Fine Dining: Not widely available in Tofino, but quality local seafood and rustic charm. Farm-to-Table: Black Rock (Tofino), various local eateries.

**Tips for Your Trips:**

*   **Plan Ahead:** Book accommodations, especially in popular spots like Victoria, Jasper, or Tofino, well in advance.
*   **Check Conditions:** Mountain passes (like Shuswap) and coastal weather can be unpredictable. Check road conditions and weather forecasts before you go.
*   **Pack Smart:** Bring layers for changing weather, rain gear, snacks, and a camera!
*   **Slow Down:** These trips are about the journey as much as the destination. Allow plenty of time to stop, explore, and enjoy the scenery.
*   **Dog-Friendly Stops:** Many parks, beaches, and restaurants are dog-friendly. Check specific rules for each location.
