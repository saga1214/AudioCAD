That's wonderful! Your daughter's dedication is really impressive, and showing her your pride is a great idea. Here are some gift ideas, ranging from simple to more elaborate, depending on your budget and what you think she'd like best:

**Simple & Sentimental:**

1.  **Personalized Keepsake:** A necklace, bracelet, or keychain engraved with her name, initials, the play's title, or the date. Something small but meaningful she can keep forever.
2.  **Framed Photo:** Print a nice, clear photo of her during rehearsal or on opening night. Frame it simply. You can even get a photo album made online with pictures from the show.
3.  **A "Thank You" Note:** Write a heartfelt letter expressing how incredibly proud you are of her hard work and dedication. Tell her specific things you admired (her confidence, her acting, her teamwork). This is incredibly powerful.
4.  **A Special Snack or Treat:** Something she loves, wrapped nicely. Think gourmet chocolates, a fancy ice cream sundae kit, her favorite cookies, or a box of her favorite candy. Pair it with a note: "For being an amazing [Character Name]!"
5.  **A Cozy Blanket or Thermos:** Something practical she can use often, especially if she's cold backstage or needs warmth after the show. Write "Great Show!" on the tag.

**More Thoughtful & Keepsake-Focused:**

6.  **A Custom Playbill or Program Frame:** If she has a program from the show, frame it nicely. You can find affordable frames online or at craft stores.
7.  **A Small Craft Kit:** Something related to her performance, like a mini costume kit, a set of makeup brushes for costumes, or a small craft kit she can use to make something related to the show (if it's age-appropriate).
8.  **A Gift Card to a Fun Place:** Depending on her interests, a gift card to a toy store, bookstore, coffee shop (if she's a teen), or even a fun local attraction for a future outing.
9.  **Tickets to a Future Show:** If you know she's interested in theater, tickets to a different show (local community theater, a movie, a concert) can be a great way to celebrate her achievement and encourage her passion.

**Experiential (For a Future Celebration):**

10. **A Fun Outing:** Plan a special outing *after* the play! Maybe dinner at her favorite restaurant, tickets to an arcade, a trip to the zoo, or a movie night at home with her favorite snacks. Frame it as a "celebration dinner" for her hard work.
11. **A "SPA" Gift for Her:** A simple spa kit with face masks, bath bombs, a nice lotion, and a relaxing candle. Pair it with a note: "You deserve to relax after such a great show!"

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Her Age & Interests:** What does she love? Toys, crafts, books, food, art, animals, sports? Tailor the gift to her personality.
*   **Budget:** You don't need to spend a lot! A heartfelt note and a small treat can mean the world. A keepsake or experience might be a bit more.
*   **The Note:** **Whatever you choose, include a heartfelt note.** This is often the most cherished part. Tell her specifically what you admired about her performance and how proud you are of her dedication.

**My top recommendations for a quick, impactful gift?** Combine **something small & tangible** (like gourmet chocolates or a personalized keychain) with **a heartfelt note** expressing your pride. It's simple, personal, and shows you truly appreciate her effort. A framed photo of her rehearsing or performing is also a classic, lasting memento.

**Remember:** The most important thing is for her to *feel* seen, appreciated, and proud of herself for her hard work. Your love and support mean the most! Good luck at the opening night!
