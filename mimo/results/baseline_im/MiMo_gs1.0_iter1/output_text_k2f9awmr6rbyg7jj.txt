While artificial crab meat is processed and not made from actual crab, it **is still considered a meat product** and doesn't align with a vegetarian diet. If you're aiming to be a lacto-vegetarian, I'd suggest making a delicious vegetarian sandwich instead. Here's a great option:

### 🥪 **Lacto-Vegetarian "Crab" Sandwich**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 slices whole-grain bread  
- 4 oz firm tofu (crumbled)  
- 1 tbsp mayonnaise  
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard  
- 1 tsp soy sauce  
- 1 tsp lemon juice  
- 1 tsp kelp flakes (for "seafood" flavor)  
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder  
- 1/4 tsp onion powder  
- 1 tbsp chopped celery  
- 1 tbsp chopped red bell pepper  
- 1 tbsp capers (chopped)  
- 1/4 avocado, sliced  
- 1 tbsp vegan aioli (or regular mayo)  
- Lettuce, tomato, and pickles  

**Instructions:**  
1. Mix crumbled tofu, mayo, mustard, soy sauce, lemon juice, kelp flakes, garlic powder, onion powder, celery, bell pepper, and capers.  
2. Toast bread lightly.  
3. Spread vegan aioli on both slices.  
4. Layer tofu mixture, avocado, lettuce, tomato, and pickles.  
5. Press together and serve!
