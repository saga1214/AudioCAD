Okay, let's see. You're heading to Austin and want a relaxed dinner spot for your first night. You mentioned loving Greek food, so maybe something with a similar vibe—warm, welcoming, and not too formal. Austin has a lot of great spots, but for that 'casual' feel, I'd suggest places like Odd Duck. It's got a farm-to-table thing going on, super chill, and the food is creative but approachable. Or maybe Taco Deli if you're in the mood for tacos, they're a local favorite. If you really want that Greek-inspired comfort, you could try a place like Arlo’s. It's a cozy spot with Mediterranean flavors and a relaxed atmosphere. The key is finding somewhere that feels like a natural fit for your first night—easygoing, good food, and a place to unwind.
