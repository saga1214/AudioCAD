### U-Turn Penalties in BC (Brief Recap)
- **Minor Offense**: A U-turn where permitted (e.g., no signs, no signals) is usually just a warning or small fine.  
- **Major Offense**: Doing a U-turn where prohibited (e.g., at intersections, curves, or restricted zones) can result in a **$160 fine** under BC's Highway Traffic Act.  
- **Always check**: Signs, signals, and visibility rules first. If unsure, wait for a safe opportunity.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients  
**For 4 servings**:  
| **Ingredient**       | **Quantity**          | **Notes**                                  |  
|-----------------------|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------|  
| Spaghetti             | 400g (14 oz)          | Use spaghetti, linguine, or bucatini       |  
| Guanciale (or pancetta)| 150g (5 oz), diced    | *Guanciale is traditional (cured pork cheek)* |  
| Eggs                  | 3 large               | *Room-temperature for better mixing*       |  
| Parmigiano Reggiano   | 50g (1/2 cup), grated | Freshly grated                           |  
| Black pepper          | 1-2 tsp, freshly ground| To taste                                  |  
| Salt                  | To taste              | For pasta water                          |  
| Optional              | 1 clove garlic, minced| Add to guanciale for extra flavor         |  

### Key Steps for Perfect Carbonara  
1. **Cook pasta** in salted water until *al dente*. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.  
2. **Render guanciale** in a skillet over medium heat until crispy. Add garlic if using.  
3. **Temper eggs**: Whisk eggs, cheese, and pepper in a bowl. Slowly pour hot pasta water into eggs to prevent scrambling.  
4. **Combine**: Toss pasta with guanciale. Remove from heat. Mix in egg/cheese sauce until creamy.  
5. **Adjust**: Add reserved pasta water if sauce seems too thick. Serve immediately.  

**Pro Tips**:  
- No cream! Authentic carbonara relies on eggs, cheese, and pasta water for creaminess.  
- Use *freshly grated* cheese—pre-grated won’t melt as smoothly.  
- Add cracked pepper *after* mixing for brightness.
