### 🌴 **Top Destination Recommendations**

   - **Why:** Crystal-clear turquoise waters, shallow reefs perfect for beginners, and iconic sites like **Sintana Cavern** (a submerged cave with ancient petroglyphs) and **Playa Lagun** (wall dives with schools of parrotfish).

3. **Socorro Island, Mexico**  
   - **Why:** World-class diving with **fewer crowds** than the Caribbean. Famous for **giant rays, humpback whales** (Dec–Mar), and pristine coral reefs.  
   - **Logistics:** 4-day liveaboard boat trip from Guadalajara (budget: ~$1,200-$1,800/person).  

4. **Palau, Micronesia**  
   - **Why:** Unparalleled biodiversity. Dive **Urgi Channel** (jellyfish, sharks) and **Jellyfish Lake** (swim with translucent jellyfish—no certification needed!).  
   - **Style:** Remote, eco-conscious destination with luxury resorts.
