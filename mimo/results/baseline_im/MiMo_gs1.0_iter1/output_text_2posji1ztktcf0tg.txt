Okay, this is a fun shift from podcast talk to dinner planning! Since your sister is a vegetarian and you're making it special, I’d suggest a dish that’s both impressive and comforting. Maybe something like mushroom risotto? It’s creamy, rich, and feels like a restaurant dish, but you can make it at home. You could also do a hearty vegetarian lasagna or stuffed bell peppers, which are filling and colorful. If you want something lighter but still flavorful, a big veggie stir-fry with tofu and quinoa over rice would be great. Oh, and don’t forget dessert! A chocolate avocado mousse is a great vegetarian-friendly option. I hope she enjoys the dinner and the podcast suggestions!
