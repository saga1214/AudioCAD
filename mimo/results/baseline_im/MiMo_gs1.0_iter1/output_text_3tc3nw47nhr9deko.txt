That's a really interesting observation, and it's completely understandable why your friend might be concerned. Here are a few reasons why your friend might think you seem upset, even if you're saying you're having a great time:

1.  **Non-Verbal Cues:** Your facial expressions, tone of voice, or body language might be unintentionally conveying something different from your words. This happens all the time! Even if you're consciously trying to smile or look happy, stress, fatigue, or just being in a new environment can make your face look different than your internal state.
2.  **"Resting Face" vs. "Forced Smile":** You mentioned actively working on your "resting bitch face" and forcing smiles. This can actually make you look *more* tense or even slightly strained, which your friend might pick up on. A genuine, relaxed smile is often easier to read than a forced one.
3.  **Context of the Party:** If the party feels overwhelming, loud, or not quite your scene, your body language might reflect that discomfort, even if you're trying to put on a brave face for your friend.
4.  **General Social Fatigue:** Parties can be draining. If you're feeling tired or socially overstimulated, that can subtly come through in how you interact.
5.  **Your Friend is Concerned:** Sometimes, a friend who knows you well might sense something is off, even if you haven't expressed it directly. They might be checking in proactively.

**What to Do (and How I Can Help):**

*   **Be Honest (If Comfortable):** You could simply tell your friend, "Thanks for checking! I'm actually having a really good time, but maybe I look a bit tense or tired? I'm trying to relax and enjoy it." This often relieves their concern.
*   **Focus on Relaxation:** Try to consciously relax your jaw, shoulders, and facial muscles. Take a deep breath. Let go of the *effort* to look happy and just *be* in the moment.
*   **Engage Authentically:** Talk to people about things you genuinely enjoy or find interesting. Authentic engagement often leads to more natural, positive expressions.
*   **Take a Break:** If you *are* feeling overwhelmed, it's perfectly okay to step away for a few minutes to recharge. Your friend likely understands.
