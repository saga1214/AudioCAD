The Erie Canal is absolutely central to Lockport's identity. The city's nickname is "The City of Locks" for a reason, and the "Flight of Five" is its most famous landmark.

For a great introduction, I'd highly recommend visiting the Lockport Canal Museum. It's located in the historic Lockport City Dock, so you can learn about the canal's history while standing right on the water where the action happened. 

If you're more of a visual learner, the Lockport Underground Railroad Museum is also very moving. It shows how the canal itself was used as a route for freedom seekers, which is a powerful and often overlooked part of its history.
