### 1. **Vodka "Gin & Tonic" (Quick & Refreshing)**  
*(Uses leftover vodka + pantry staples)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz fresh lime juice  
- 2 oz soda water  
- 0.5 oz honey syrup (equal parts honey + hot water, stirred until dissolved)  
- Garnish: Lime wheel or cucumber slice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake vodka, lime juice, and honey syrup.  
2. Strain into a chilled glass.  
3. Top with soda water.  
4. Garnish.  

*Why it works:* Light, tart, and sweet—perfect for mixed diets. No complex ingredients!

---

### 2. **Argentinian-Style Fernet (Herbal & Zesty)**  
*(For the Fernet-Branca you mentioned)*  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1.5 oz Fernet-Branca (or Italian herbal liqueur)  
- 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz simple syrup (1:1 sugar:water)  
- Top with **sparkling water** (not soda—safe for allergy!)  
- Garnish: Lemon twist  

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake Fernet, lemon juice, and simple syrup.  
2. Strain into a highball glass over ice.  
3. Top with sparkling water.  
4. Express a lemon twist over the drink.  

*Why it works:* Bright, herbal, and refreshing—like an Argentinian "Fernet con Limonada." Avoids soda entirely!

---

### Key Tips:  
- **Ice matters:** Use clear ice cubes for a polished look.  
- **Glassware:** Serve in chilled coupes or highballs.  
- **Garnish:** Elevates both drinks instantly!  
- **No soda?** Both recipes use sparkling water (not soda) to keep it safe.
