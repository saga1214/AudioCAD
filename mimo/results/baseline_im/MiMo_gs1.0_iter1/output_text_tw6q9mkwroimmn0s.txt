That sounds like a fantastic theme! Here are 5 meal ideas and 5 dessert ideas to make your ocean-themed night extra special.

### **5 Meal Ideas for Your Ocean-Themed Dinner:**

1.  **Grilled Shrimp Skewers with Mango Salsa & Coconut Rice:** Light, fresh, and perfectly thematic. The mango salsa adds sweetness and spice.
2.  **Seared Tuna Steak with Lemon-Herb Butter & Roasted Vegetables:** Elegant and lean, the lemon ties into the ocean theme beautifully. Serve with a vibrant side salad.
3.  **Baked Salmon with Creamy Dill Sauce & Asparagus:** A classic seafood dish that feels special. The dill sauce complements the salmon and the green asparagus adds freshness.
4.  **Spicy Coconut Curry with Shrimp or Tofu & Basmati Rice:** Rich, flavorful, and comforting. The coconut milk evokes the ocean's depths, and the spices add warmth. Great for both shrimp lovers and vegetarians.
5.  **Grilled Fish Tacos with Mango Salsa & Avocado Crema:** Fun and fresh! Use blackened mahi-mahi, cod, or shrimp. The mango salsa and creamy avocado add sweetness and richness.

### **5 Sweet Dessert Ideas for Your Ocean-Themed Feast:**

1.  **Mango Panna Cotta with Coconut Whipped Cream & Toasted Pine Nuts:** Silky smooth, creamy, and bursting with tropical sweetness. The coconut and pine nuts add texture and a subtle earthiness. *Highly recommended!*
2.  **Chocolate-Dipped Strawberries with a Sea Salt Finish:** Simple elegance. The dark chocolate contrasts beautifully with the fresh berry, and the sea salt evokes the ocean breeze. Drizzle with a tiny bit of caramel sauce for extra decadence.
3.  **Coconut Chia Pudding Parfait:** Light, healthy, and naturally tropical. Layer creamy coconut chia pudding with fresh mango or pineapple chunks and a sprinkle of shredded coconut. Refreshing and pretty!
4.  **Tropical Fruit Skewers with Honey-Lime Drizzle:** A vibrant, colorful, and healthy treat. Thread pineapple, mango, kiwi, and berries onto skewers. Drizzle lightly with honey mixed with fresh lime juice and a touch of zest.
5.  **Vanilla Bean Ice Cream with a Berry Compote:** Pure comfort. The warm, sweet-tart berry compote (made with raspberries, strawberries, or blackberries) served over creamy vanilla bean ice cream feels luxurious and is universally loved.

Enjoy your incredibly romantic and delicious ocean-themed anniversary dinner! It sounds like a wonderful celebration.
