### Asian Noodle Spots in Dallas
1. **Nam Umi (Korean):**  
   *Location:* White Rock, Deep Ellum, Plano  
   *Why:* Known for authentic Korean ramen (especially spicy tteokbokki and spicy chicken ramen). Their broth is rich and flavorful.  
   *Must-try:* Spicy Tteokbokki Ramyeon or Dakgopchang (beef intestines) Ramyeon.

2. **Noodle House (Chinese):**  
   *Location:* Near Downtown, Garland  
   *Why:* A Dallas institution with huge portions of classic Chinese noodle dishes. Great for dumplings, stir-fried noodles, and Peking duck.  
   *Must-try:* Hot & Sour Soup with Noodles or Mapo Tofu.

3. **Yamashiro (Japanese):**  
   *Location:* Highland Park  
   *Why:* Upscale Japanese, but their ramen is exceptional. Try the Miso Ramen or Shoyu Ramen.  
   *Must-try:* Miso Ramen with Butter Shichimi Togarashi.

4. **Mama Ji (Korean):**  
   *Location:* Deep Ellum  
   *Why:* Trendy spot with creative Korean noodle dishes. Their "Hot & Tasty" Ramyeon is a crowd favorite.  
   *Must-try:* Hot & Tasty Ramyeon with extra chili paste.

5. **Lao Ban (Thai):**  
   *Location:* Highland Park  
   *Why:* Authentic Thai street food with incredible Pad See Ew, Tom Yum Goong, and Khao Soi.  
   *Must-try:* Tom Yum Goong or Khao Soi.
