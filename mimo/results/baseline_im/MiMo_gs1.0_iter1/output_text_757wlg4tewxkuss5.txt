That's a thoughtful question! Here's a breakdown for your diverse group:

**For the Vegan Friend:**
*   **Regular Frozen Hamburgers:** Absolutely **not** suitable. They contain meat.
*   **Solution:** Provide **plant-based frozen burgers** (like Beyond Meat, Impossible, or store brands). Ensure they are clearly labeled vegan/vegetarian.

**For Everyone Else:**
*   **Regular Frozen Hamburgers:** Generally **fine** *if kept frozen until cooking*.
*   **Thawing Concern:** **Do NOT let them thaw at room temperature.** Bacteria multiply rapidly in the "danger zone" (40°F - 140°F). This is unsafe.

**Food Safety & Logistics:**

1.  **Keep Frozen Until Cooking:** This is the safest approach for regular burgers. Grill them directly from frozen. They will take longer to cook through (check internal temp to 160°F), but it's perfectly safe.
2.  **Cooler with Ice Packs (Highly Recommended):** If you plan to bring pre-cooked burgers (or even if you want to thaw plant-based ones slightly), **use a cooler with ample ice packs or frozen water bottles.** Keep the burgers frozen solid until you're ready to cook them.
3.  **Plant-Based Burgers Need Attention:** If you thaw plant-based burgers slightly for easier grilling, do so *very* slowly in the cooler and cook them promptly. Don't leave them thawing out.

**Recommendations:**

*   **Buy Plant-Based Burgers:** Get a pack of your vegan friend's favorite brand (or a couple of different ones).
*   **Use a Cooler:** Pack the plant-based burgers *and* your regular frozen burgers in a cooler with ice packs until you arrive at the park and are ready to grill.
*   **Grill Frozen:** Grill both types directly from frozen. It works fine!
*   **Offer Inclusive Sides:** Provide buns, condiments (check for vegan options if needed), and sides that cater to everyone (e.g., veggie burgers for the vegan, maybe some chili, salads, or veggie wraps alongside the meat burgers).

**In short:** Yes, frozen hamburgers are generally safe *if frozen until cooking*. **For the vegan friend, provide plant-based frozen burgers. Use a cooler with ice packs to keep everything frozen solid until you're ready to grill.** This ensures safety and inclusivity for everyone at your great park get-together!
