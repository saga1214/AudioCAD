That's a great question! Since Maisy wasn't into cheese, here are some other enticing treat options for Maltese training:

**Highly Recommended Alternatives:**

1.  **Small Pieces of Cooked Chicken (Skinless):** A classic favorite. Use tiny bits (pea-sized or smaller) and ensure it's plain, unseasoned chicken. The strong, savory smell is usually very motivating.
2.  **Cooked Salmon (Skinless):** Another incredibly enticing option for many dogs. Similar to chicken, use small pieces. The fishy aroma is potent!
3.  **Boiled Egg (Cooked, no salt/seasoning):** A good protein source. Cut into tiny cubes or mash for easier eating.
4.  **Plain Yogurt (Plain, no sugar):** The tangy smell can be appealing. Offer very small amounts (a tiny spoonful or two) as it's a treat, not a snack.

**Excellent Freeze-Dried Liver Options:**

*   **Freeze-Dried Liver Bits:** These are often smaller, more concentrated, and sometimes have a stronger smell than plain liver. Look for brands specifically marketed for training/treats.
*   **Freeze-Dried Liver:** While plain freeze-dried liver is good, the "bits" or "bits & pieces" versions often have a stronger liver flavor and sometimes include other meaty bits (like beef) which can be extra enticing. Check the ingredients list.
*   **Freeze-Dried Chicken or Turkey:** If Maisy isn't a big liver fan, these are excellent high-smell, high-value alternatives.

**Other Good Options (Use Sparingly as Treats):**

*   **Small Pieces of Beef Jerky (Plain, Unseasoned):** Ensure it's dog-safe jerky. Small bits work well.
*   **Small Pieces of Pumpkin (Plain, Not Pie Filling):** The earthy smell can be appealing to some.

Brands like "Ziwi Peak" (small pieces), "Royal Canin Small Breed Training Treats," or "Bixby" have options. Ensure they are low-calorie since they are used as rewards.

**Key Tips for Choosing & Using Treats:**

1.  **High Value is Key:** Start with the *most* motivating treats for basic obedience (like chicken or fish bits) to build success.
2.  **Small Portions:** Cut treats into tiny pieces your Maltese can easily swallow or pick up with her mouth.
3.  **Consistency:** Use the same type of treat for the same behavior initially. You don't want her confused.
4.  **Scent Test:** Offer a tiny piece of each potential treat. Does she sniff it intensely and take it eagerly? That's your best indicator of what she likes most.
5.  **Portion Control:** Remember these are *treats*, not part of her daily meals. A good rule is treats should make up less than 10% of her daily calories. Use tiny pieces!
6.  **Pair with Positive Words:** Use a clear, consistent cue word (like "sit," "down," "look at me") *just before* giving the treat to reinforce the association.

**Start with small pieces of cooked chicken or salmon, or freeze-dried liver bits.** Offer them enthusiastically and pair them with clear commands. Maisy will likely find one of these much more motivating than cheese! Good luck with Maisy's training!
