Great idea! A quick, fun cooking activity can be a great way to bond without pressure. Here’s a simple, flexible recipe that’s **lactose-free**, easy to customize, and usually well-liked:  

### 🍝 Quick Margherita Pasta (15–20 minutes)  
**Ingredients you can adjust**:  
- 8 oz pasta (penne, spaghetti, or fusilli)  
- 2 tbsp olive oil  
- 1 clove garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)  
- ½ cup cherry tomatoes, halved  
- ½ cup fresh spinach or baby kale (optional, if sister tolerates veggies)  
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan or mozzarella (lactose-free cheese if needed)  
- Salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes to taste  
- Fresh basil or parsley for garnish  

**Steps**:  
1. Cook pasta al dente (check package time).  
2. Heat oil in a pan, add garlic (and red pepper flakes), then toss in tomatoes and spinach (if using). Sauté for 3–4 minutes until tomatoes soften.  
3. Drain pasta, toss with the sauce, add cheese, and season.  
4. Garnish with basil/parsley. Serve immediately!  

---

### 🌟 Why This Works for Your Situation:  
- **Low Pressure**: Minimal steps, no fancy skills needed.  
- **Customizable**: Skip spinach if sister dislikes veggies, use more cheese if she likes it, or add nuts/seeds for crunch.  
- **Lactose-Free**: Easy swap for lactose-free cheese.  
- **Positive Vibes**: Warm, comforting, and visually appealing—great for light conversation.  

### 💡 Bonus Activity Ideas:  
- **Veggie Stir-Fry**: Quick stir-fry with tofu (lactose-free), bell peppers, and snap peas. Serve with rice or noodles.  
- **Breakfast-for-Dinner Pancakes**: Use a mix (no cooking skills needed!), add berries or chocolate chips.  

**Key Tip**: Frame it as a "quick experiment" or "try-this-recipe-I-found." Keep it light and collaborative—focus on enjoying the process, not perfection.
