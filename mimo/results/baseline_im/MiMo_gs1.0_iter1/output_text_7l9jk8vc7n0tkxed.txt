Absolutely. Since the plunger didn't work, it's likely a deep clog requiring a more powerful approach. Here's a step-by-step guide using common plumber's tools:

### Step 1: Try a Toilet Auger (The "Snake")
- **Grab your toilet auger**: This is a coiled metal drain snake with a curved tip.
- **Insert it carefully**: Feed the tip into the drain, rotating it clockwise while pushing until you feel resistance (the clog).
- **Break it up**: Keep pushing and rotating until you've broken up the clog. You might hear or feel debris moving.
- **Pull it out**: Slowly retract the snake while still rotating. Any debris caught on the coils will come out with it.

### Step 2: If the Snake Fails, Try a Sink Snake
- **Use a sink drain snake**: This is a longer, flexible coiled drain snake (often with a straightener at the end).
- **Insert and twist**: Feed it into the drain, twisting and pushing until you hit the clog. Use pliers to crank the handle firmly to break it up.
- **Retract slowly**: Pull it out, and debris may come with it.

### Step 3: If Still Stubborn, Use a Pipe Snake (Worm Drill)
- **Use a pipe snake**: This is a longer, flexible snake with a straightener and a rotating head.
- **Insert and rotate**: Push it in while rotating the handle clockwise. Keep pushing until you feel the clog.
- **Break it up**: Keep rotating until the clog fragments. Pull it out slowly to remove debris.

### Step 4: Last Resort: Chemical Drain Cleaner (Use Sparingly)
- **Only if other methods fail**: Pour a **plumber's drain cleaner** (not a toilet cleaner) down the drain. Follow the product instructions *exactly*.
- **Wait 30–60 minutes**: Let it dissolve the clog.
- **Flush with hot water**: Use a bucket of hot (not boiling) water to push the dissolved debris through.

### Important Tips:
- **Wear gloves and eye protection**—debris can be sharp.
- **Work slowly**: Rushing can damage pipes or tools.
- **If the pipe feels cold or flexible**, stop immediately—it could be a vent pipe or a leak. Call a pro.
- **If you break the drain**, call a plumber. A broken pipe can cause serious damage.
