### Key Numbers:
- **Entry Price:** $62.87/share  
- **Stop-Loss Price (Loss Goal):** $90/share  
- **Target Price (Gain Goal):** $110/share  
- **Shares Purchased:** $10,000 ÷ $62.87 ≈ **159 shares**  

---

### 1. **If the Stock Hits Your Stop-Loss ($90/share):**
   - **Sell Price:** $90/share  
   - **Total Cost:** $10,000  
   - **Loss Calculation:**

     - **Profit/Loss:** $14,310 - $10,000 = **-$4,310**  
   - **Result:** You lose **$4,310** (43% of your investment).  

   **Why?**  
   Your stop-loss is at $90, but you bought at $62.87. If the stock falls to $90, your loss is:  
   $90 - $62.87 = **$27.13/share loss**.  
   Total loss: 159 × $27.13 = **$4,310**.
