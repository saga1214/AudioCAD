Okay, so you're looking for dinner restaurant suggestions, but you want them to fit your low-carb, high-protein diet. Let's think about this. One great option is a steakhouse. You can get a great ribeye or filet mignon, and just ask for it to be cooked without any butter or sauce. Another good choice is a sushi restaurant, but you'd want to get a lot of sashimi or just the fish-based rolls, and skip the rice. You could also look for a healthy Greek restaurant, where you can get things like grilled chicken or fish with a side of olives and a Greek salad. Just remember to ask for no croutons on the salad. These are all good places that should have options that fit your needs. Enjoy your dinner!
