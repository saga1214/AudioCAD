Okay, so you're about to film one of your volunteer performances and want a checklist to make sure everything goes smoothly. Here’s a simple list of things to do before filming:

First, make sure you have permission from the facility, like Sunnyside Assisted Living. You don’t want to film without asking, especially since it's a private place.

Then, bring your phone or camera, and make sure it’s fully charged. It’s also a good idea to test the recording settings beforehand, just to avoid any surprises.

As for the setup, you can use a tripod or a stand to keep the camera steady, especially if you’re doing close-ups. If you can, film in a spot with good lighting, so you don’t have to mess with bright lights that might disturb the audience.

Before you start, think about what you want to capture. Maybe focus on your performance, the audience’s reactions, and your puppet characters. You can also try to get a few different angles, like a wide shot of the whole room and a close-up on your hands or the puppets.

While you’re filming, try to be natural. Don’t worry about being perfect—just focus on having fun and putting on a good show.

After you finish, back up the footage to your phone or computer so you don’t lose it. Then, you can start editing it if you want. You can cut out any mistakes or keep the whole thing. You might also add some music or sound effects to make it more interesting.

And that’s pretty much it! Just remember to keep it simple, have fun, and focus on creating something positive. Good luck with your show!
