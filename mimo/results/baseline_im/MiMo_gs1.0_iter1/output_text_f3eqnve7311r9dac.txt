You're right, older comics from the forties and fifties are often much rarer and can appreciate more, but they can be very expensive. I can give you a list of ten significant books from that era that are known to be rare.

First, there's *Detective Comics* number twenty-seven. It's the first appearance of Batman, but it's extremely rare and usually out of your price range. Next, *Action Comics* number one, the first Superman, is the most valuable comic in the world.

Then there's *Superboy* number one from 1958, the first appearance of Supergirl. *The Brave and the Bold* number one from 1960 is the first appearance of Batman and Robin teaming up. *Fantastic Four* number one from 1961 is the first appearance of the Fantastic Four and Galactus.

Some of these are a bit later, but still fit your criteria. *Amazing Spider-Man* number one from 1962 is the first appearance of Spider-Man. *The Amazing Spider-Man* number fifty from 1966 is the first appearance of Doctor Strange. *The Amazing Spider-Man* number ninety from 1967 is the first appearance of the Sinister Six. And finally, *The Amazing Spider-Man* number one hundred from 1969 is the first appearance of the Green Goblin.

These are all iconic issues with major first appearances, which is why they are considered rare and valuable. Just remember, for any of these, you'll want to look for a copy with a high grade, as that's what significantly impacts the price.
