Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for inspiration! You’ll have to decide what kind of inspiration you want. If you want those vibrant autumn colors, go in the fall, from September to November. The leaves turn amazing shades of red, orange, and yellow. It's perfect for autumn scenes.

If you're looking for a more tropical, warm vibe, then spring, from March to May, would be ideal. The weather is great, and the landscapes are lush. You can see the ocean and the rivers we were just talking about.

For a bit of both, late summer, around August, could be a good middle ground. It's less crowded, and the weather is still warm but not as hot. You can capture some of those early autumn colors in the south while still enjoying the tropical feel. 

Whatever you choose, just remember to bring your sketchbook and paint! You’ll find endless inspiration. Have a great trip!
