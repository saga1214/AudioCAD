Here are some excellent gluten-free snack ideas perfect for camping and cycling, focusing on convenience, nutrition, and your preferences:

1.  **Popcorn:** A classic camping snack! Use popcorn kernels (check label for GF certification) and cook on the camp stove or in a dedicated pot. Lightly season with nutritional yeast ("nooch") for a cheesy flavor without dairy or gluten.
2.  **Rice Cakes:** Top them with nut butter (check for GF certification) and banana slices, or just enjoy them plain. They're crunchy and satisfying.
3.  **Mixed Nuts & Seeds:** A powerhouse snack. Combine almonds, walnuts, pecans, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, and maybe a few dried cranberries or raisins (check labels). Look for pre-portioned trail mix packs for ease.
4.  **Nut Butter Packets:** Single-serve packets of peanut butter, almond butter, or sunflower seed butter are incredibly convenient for spreading on rice cakes, crackers (if GF certified), or even eating by the spoonful.
5.  **Fresh Fruit:** Pack apples, bananas, oranges, berries (if cool enough), grapes, or clementines. They're refreshing, hydrating, and naturally sweet.
6.  **Dried Fruit:** Look for unsweetened or lightly sweetened options like apricots, mango, pineapple, cranberries, or figs. Choose single-serve packs or pre-portioned bags. Check labels carefully for additives and potential cross-contamination.
7.  **Roasted Chickpeas or Edamame:** Crunchy, protein-packed, and satisfying. Find pre-seasoned varieties (check labels) or make your own.
8.  **Dark Chocolate:** A small square or two of high-cacao (70%+) dark chocolate is a great energy and mood booster. Look for brands certified gluten-free.
9.  **Coconut Chips:** Similar to Popchips, look for gluten-free certified coconut chips or puffs for a light, crunchy snack.
10. **Olives or Cheese Sticks (if tolerated):** Pack small containers of olives or single-serve cheese sticks (like mozzarella or cheddar). Ensure cheese is processed in a GF facility if you're highly sensitive.

**Bonus Tip:** Don't forget hydration! Pack plenty of water and consider electrolyte tabs or powder for long rides, especially if you're sweating heavily. Pairing snacks with water is key! Enjoy your trip!
