Since you're feeling up to it and the kitchen seems clean, cooking is definitely the way to go. Here are a few simple but satisfying ideas you can whip up with basic ingredients.

First, you could do a really quick and comforting pasta dish. You can just grab a box of pasta—penne or spaghetti is always a good choice. Toss it with some olive oil, minced garlic, red pepper flakes if you like a little kick, and a handful of grated Parmesan cheese. You could even throw in some cherry tomatoes if you find any.

Another great option is a stir-fry. Grab some pre-cut stir-fry veggies, maybe some chicken or tofu if you want protein. Just heat up some oil in a pan, toss everything in, and season it with soy sauce, garlic, and ginger. It's super fast and fresh.

Or, you could make a classic cheeseburger. Grab some ground beef, buns, cheese slices, and whatever toppings you like. It's a simple comfort food that's hard to beat.

If you want something a little healthier, you could try a sheet pan dinner. Get some chicken breast, a sweet potato, and some broccoli. Toss them all with olive oil, salt, pepper, and some herbs, then bake it all together. It's pretty straightforward.

Finally, you could make a simple omelet. Just whisk up some eggs, add in some diced ham or cheese, and cook it up. You can even top it with some hot sauce or salsa. It's a quick protein-packed meal.
