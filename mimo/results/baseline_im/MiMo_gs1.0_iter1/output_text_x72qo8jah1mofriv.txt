Okay, so Tony is kosher, which means the food needs to be prepared in a way that follows those dietary laws. The main things to check are the ingredients and the cooking equipment. First, you have to make sure no pork or shellfish was used. For ingredients, that means no bacon, no shrimp or crab, and no dairy and meat mixed together. So for the mini weenies, they should be beef or turkey, not pork. If you use bacon, you'd need to replace it with something else like plant-based bacon or a different protein. For the fruit skewers, that's usually fine since they're just fruit. The blood pudding is an interesting one; it's made from blood, so that's definitely not kosher. You'd have to skip that or find a substitute. For the Pinot Noir, just make sure it's a kosher wine, which most are, but it's good to check the label. And most importantly, if you're using the same grill or pan for the bacon or blood pudding, you need to clean it really well with hot water and maybe a vinegar solution to remove any flavor residue. You could also just use separate equipment to avoid any risk. The goal is to make sure everything is clean, the ingredients are right, and that Tony feels comfortable and included. That should cover the basics for a kosher-friendly book club.
