Here's a step-by-step guide for installing your items safely and effectively:

### 1. TV Mount Installation
* **Tools Needed:** Stud finder, measuring tape, pencil, drill, wall anchors (if no studs), level, screwdriver  
* **Steps:**  
  1. **Locate Studs:** Use a stud finder to mark wall anchors (top/bottom corners of screen).  
  2. **Check Weight Capacity:** Ensure mount supports your TV’s weight (check manufacturer specs).  
  3. **Mark Drill Points:** Place a level on TV and mark screw holes.  
  4. **Install Mount:** Attach brackets to wall (into studs), then secure TV with screws.  
  *Pro Tip:* Use a **laser level** for perfect alignment if mounting high.

### 2. Hanging Planters
* **Tools Needed:** Drill, wire (14-16 AWG), hook/eye screws, measuring tape  
* **Steps:**  
  1. **Choose Anchor Points:** Select a sturdy beam or stud.  
  2. **Install Hooks:** Drill a hole into the wall, insert hook screw, and tighten.  
  3. **Hang Planter:** Thread wire through planter’s loop and attach to hook.  
  *Safety:* Use **heavy-duty wire** (minimum 14 AWG) and secure all fasteners.

### 3. Floating Shelves
* **Tools Needed:** Drill, wall anchors (plastic/metal), level, shims (if needed)  
* **Steps:**  
  1. **Mark Shelf Height:** Place shelf on wall, mark top/bottom edges.  
  2. **Install Anchors:** Drill pilot holes, insert anchors, then drive shelf screws.  
  3. **Level & Secure:** Place shelf on anchors, adjust with shims if wobbly, then tighten.  
  *Critical:* Use **metal anchors** for heavy shelves (>25 lbs). Test stability!

### ⚠️ Universal Safety Tips:
- **Always:**  
  - Wear safety glasses.  
  - Check local building codes (renters may need permits).  
- **Avoid:**  
  - Plastic anchors for shelves >15 lbs.  
  - Drywall anchors in thin walls (use toggle bolts instead).  
- **Post-Installation:**  
  - Test mounts by gently pulling them.  
  - Ensure shelves bear weight without shifting.  

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Schedule installations during off-hours (8–10 PM) to respect noise rules. For complex tasks, hire a pro—your safety and landlord relationship matter!
