Here are 5 excellent green vegetables safe and nutritious for your parrot, based on your bird's sensitivity to apples, bananas, and broccoli:

1.  **Spinach:** Rich in iron, calcium, and vitamins A, C, and K. *Important:* Offer in **moderation** due to oxalates, which can bind to calcium. Chop finely.
2.  **Kale (especially curly or lacinato/dinosaur kale):** Packed with calcium, vitamins A, C, K, and antioxidants. *Important:* Chop finely and offer in **moderation** (1-2 times per week) to avoid potential digestive upset or calcium imbalance. Rinse thoroughly.
3.  **Cabbage (Green or Red):** High in vitamins C, K, and fiber. Contains glucosinolates (can be beneficial). *Important:* Chop finely. Offer in **moderation** (1-2 times per week). Raw is best; avoid cooking.
4.  **Bell Peppers (Green):** Excellent source of vitamins A, C, and B6, plus antioxidants. *Important:* Wash well. Cut into small pieces or strips. Offer **frequently** as a great snack.
5.  **Zucchini (Summer Squash):** Mild flavor, good source of vitamins C, B6, manganese, and potassium. *Important:* Chop or shred finely. Can be offered **frequently**. Avoid overripe or yellowing squash.

**Key Tips for Feeding Greens:**

*   **Wash Thoroughly:** Always rinse greens well under cold water to remove dirt, pesticides, and bacteria.
*   **Chop Finely:** Ensures easier digestion and prevents choking.
*   **Introduce Slowly:** Add one new green at a time in small amounts to monitor your bird's tolerance.
*   **Variety is Key:** Rotate different greens throughout the week to provide a broad spectrum of nutrients.
*   **Freshness:** Feed fresh, crisp greens daily.
*   **Moderation:** Even healthy foods should be offered in appropriate amounts.
*   **Observe Your Bird:** Watch for any signs of digestive upset (diarrhea, lethargy) and adjust accordingly.
*   **Avoid Toxic Foods:** Steer clear of onions, garlic, chives, potatoes, tomatoes (especially green parts), avocado, citrus seeds/pith, chocolate, caffeine, salt, sugar, and cooked bones.

Always consult with an **avian veterinarian** for personalized dietary advice specific to your parrot's breed, age, health, and any specific dietary needs.
