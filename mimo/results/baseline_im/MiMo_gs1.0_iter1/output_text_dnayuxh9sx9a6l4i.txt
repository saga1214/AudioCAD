   - **Why**: Playful, low-energy, and excellent with kids. Minimal barking (quieter than many breeds).  
   - **Size**: Small (under 25 lbs).  
   - **Note**: Prone to brachycephalic issues (breathing problems); check health history.

3. **Miniature Poodle**  
   - **Why**: Highly intelligent, hypoallergenic, and great with other pets. Eager to please.  
   - **Size**: Small (10–15 lbs).  

4. **Bichon Frise**  
   - **Why**: Cheerful, low-shedding, and adaptable. Thrives in homes with kids or other dogs.  
   - **Size**: Small (under 20 lbs).  

### 🌟 Top Mixed Breed Hybrids:
1. **Goldendoodle** (Golden Retriever + Poodle)  
   - **Why**: Combines Golden’s friendliness with Poodle’s intelligence/hypoallergenic coat. Easygoing and trainable.  
   - **Size**: Varies (can be small to large); ask about "Toy" or "Mini" versions.  

2. **Labradoodle** (Labrador + Poodle)  
   - **Why**: Social, low-shedding, and great with kids. Moderate energy.  
   - **Size**: Often medium (25–50 lbs).  

3. **Cavapoo** (Cavalier + Poodle)  
   - **Why**: Affectionate, hypoallergenic, and adaptable. Gentle with all ages/pets.  
   - **Size**: Small (15–25 lbs).

- **Noise**: Frenchies/Cavaliers are quieter; others may bark more but can be trained.  
- **Health**: Prioritize reputable breeders/rescues to avoid hereditary issues (e.g., French Bulldog breathing, Cavalier heart issues).
