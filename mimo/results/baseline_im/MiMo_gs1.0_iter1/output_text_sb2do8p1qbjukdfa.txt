That's a fantastic idea! Here's a plan for your appreciation refresh station, incorporating your "refresh" theme and your new candy request:

**The Refresh Station Setup:**

1.  **Theme:** "Crisp & Energized"
2.  **Location:** A dedicated, easily accessible spot near break areas or high-traffic volunteer zones.
3.  **Core Elements:**
    *   **Hydration Station:** A large jug of **Infused Water** (e.g., Lemon, Lime, Cucumber, Mint, or Hibiscus). Add a small bowl of **Fresh Fruit Slices** (watermelon, oranges, grapes, berries) for a visually appealing touch.
    *   **Light & Refreshing Snacks:** Small bowls of **Plain Popcorn** (lightly salted), **Veggie Sticks** (carrots, celery, bell peppers) with a small tub of **Hummus** or **Salsa Dip**, **Fresh Fruit** (apples, bananas, oranges), and perhaps **Small Rice Cakes** or **Whole-Grain Crackers**.
    *   **Quick Energy Boosters:** Small bowls of **Energy Bites** (date-based, no-bake, with oats, nuts/seeds, dried fruit) or **Yogurt Cups** (plain or lightly sweetened).
    *   **"Pallet Cleanser" & Mood Boosters:**
        *   **Small Bins of Breath-Freshening Mints or Gum:** (e.g., Spearmint, Cool Mint, Peppermint - *classic refreshing flavors!*)
        *   **Small Tins of Candies:** Focus on **Bright & Light Flavors**:
            *   **Citrus:** Lemon, Orange, Lime, Tangerine-flavored candies (like Starbursts, Skittles, or specifically labeled citrus candies).
            *   **Cherry:** Bright red candies with a tart cherry flavor.
            *   **Grape:** Classic, bright purple grape candies.
            *   **Watermelon:** A pink, juicy candy.
            *   **Strawberry:** A vibrant red candy.
            *   **Cool Flavors:** Ice Cream Popits, Cool Mint Choc Chip candies, or Watermelon Sours.
        *   *Avoid:* Heavy chocolate bars, caramel, or overly sweet/sugary treats that don't fit the light, refreshing vibe.
    *   **Decor:** A simple "Refresh Station" sign, a small bowl of fresh flowers (like lavender or baby's breath for a clean scent), or a small vase of greenery (eucalyptus, ferns). A small table runner in a bright, clean color (white, light blue, mint green) works well.

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Labeling:** Clearly label snacks, especially dips and candies. Mark allergens if necessary (e.g., "Contains Nuts" on hummus if applicable).
*   **Organization:** Use small bowls, pitchers, and jars to keep things neat and easy to grab.
*   **Accessibility:** Place it where volunteers naturally gather.
*   **Quantity:** Estimate based on your volunteer count for the weekend. You can always add more if needed.
*   **Sustainability:** Use compostable containers or reusable ones if possible. Provide hand sanitizer nearby.

**Regarding Volunteer Badges:**

*   **Print Before the Event:** **Yes, absolutely!** Print badges well in advance. It adds a layer of recognition and professionalism, making volunteers feel officially appreciated and part of the event. It's a small but meaningful touch that sets a positive tone right from the start.

**Flavor & Scent Ideas for the Refresh Theme:**

*   **Flavors:** Lemon, Lime, Grape, Cherry, Watermelon, Mint (cooling), Cucumber, Hibiscus (tart & floral), Cinnamon (warm but light).
*   **Scent:** Fresh linen spray, a small bowl of lavender buds, or a subtle citrus-scented air freshener (use sparingly!) can enhance the "crisp" feeling.
*   **Pairings:** Citrus candies pair beautifully with mint water and cucumber. Cherry or watermelon candies complement the fresh fruit. Mint candies are a classic palate cleanser.

**Implementation Tip:** For the candy, you could create a small "Refresh & Recharge" bin with a lid, labeling it clearly. This keeps it contained and visually appealing. The mints and candies are perfect for grabbing between shifts or during a quick break.

This setup focuses on hydration, light energy, and sensory refreshment (taste, sight, smell), perfectly aligning with your goal of keeping volunteers feeling clean, energized, and uplifted! Good luck with the appreciation weekend!
