Okay, let's tackle this step by step. First, the left-brain versus right-brain thing. It's mostly a myth. The brain doesn't really separate creative and logical functions that neatly. Both sides work together. But for your career, you want something creative and objective. How about data visualization or scientific illustration? They blend creativity with measurable data. Or maybe architectural design, which is about structure and creativity. Another idea is technical writing, which is structured but also creative. You could also look into UX/UI design, which involves building something solid and lasting. These fields let you be creative but also objective. As for your other questions, you mentioned writing and transposing letters. That's a common thing in dysgraphia. It's about motor skills, not knowing how to spell. And tinnitus? It's usually physical, from noise exposure or hearing loss. But if it's new or sudden, it's worth checking with a doctor. Hope this helps you figure things out!
