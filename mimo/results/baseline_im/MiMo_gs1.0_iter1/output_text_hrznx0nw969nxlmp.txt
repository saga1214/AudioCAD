Staying at Secret Harbour puts you right in the heart of Red Hook's charm! Here are some fantastic daytime activities focusing on the area:

1.  **Explore Secret Harbour Beach:** The resort's beach is a major plus. Enjoy the calm, protected waters perfect for swimming, floating, or simply relaxing. Grab a towel and soak up the Caribbean sun right from your doorstep.
2.  **Walk the Red Hook Coastal Path:** Start at Secret Harbour and walk along the scenic paths that wind along the coastline towards the historic fort. It offers beautiful views of the harbor, Cruz Bay, and the surrounding hills. You might spot local wildlife along the way.
3.  **Visit Salt Pond Bay Beach (Short Drive):** A beautiful, quieter beach with white sand and turquoise water, just a short drive away. Great for swimming, snorkeling (check for fish near the rocks), or just enjoying a peaceful vibe. You can walk down the steps to the water.
4.  **Discover Red Hook's Art Scene (Friday Art Walk is a must!):** Red Hook is the artistic soul of the island. On **Fridays, 5-7 PM**, the galleries open their doors for the **Red Hook Art Walk**. It's free, lively, and a fantastic way to see local art, meet artists, enjoy light refreshments, and soak up the creative atmosphere. Even outside Art Walk hours, the galleries (like the Red Hook Gallery, The Art House, or the Mather Gallery) are worth popping into.
5.  **Take a Short Hike to Reef Bay Waterfall (Moderate Hike):** For a bit of nature adventure, the Reef Bay Waterfall trail is a popular (and relatively short) hike leading to a beautiful, cascading waterfall. You can swim in the cool, clear pool at the base. It's a great way to cool off and get away from the beach crowds. (Start early for the best light and cooler temps).
6.  **Enjoy Lunch/Dinner at Duffy's Love Shack:** As mentioned before, this is the quintessential Red Hook experience. Grab some delicious food, drinks, and live music right on the water's edge. The atmosphere is legendary – lively, relaxed, and perfect for people-watching.

**Key things to remember for your day:**

*   **Sun Protection is Essential:** Hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, and a good cover-up are non-negotiable.
*   **Hydrate:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
*   **Explore by Foot or Bike:** Red Hook is very walkable from Secret Harbour, and renting a bicycle is a fantastic way to cover more ground easily and enjoy the scenery.
*   **Embrace the Laid-Back Vibe:** Red Hook has a unique, slower pace. Enjoy the moments!

This combination of beach time, art exploration, coastal walks, and a classic seafood spot at Duffy's should give you a fantastic and memorable day in Red Hook! Enjoy your trip!
