### Asian Noodle Spots in Dallas  
Here are some top-rated places for authentic Asian noodles:  
1. **Pho 75** (Multiple Locations)  
   - **Why go?** Famous for Vietnamese pho (beef or chicken) with rich broth and tender noodles. Soy-free options available (ask for "non-soy").  
2. **Sushi Tokyo** (Downtown)  
   - **Why go?** Beyond sushi—try their spicy miso ramen or teriyaki udon. Soy-based sauces, but customizable.  
3. **Udon Yama** (Richardson)  
   - **Why go?** Authentic Japanese udon noodles in rich broth. Soy-free? Check menu—some dishes offer substitutions.  
4. **Mala Sichuan** (Park Place)  
   - **Why go?** For bold Sichuan flavors (spicy dan dan noodles, hot pot). Soy-free? Confirm with staff.  
5. **Japango** (Downtown)  
   - **Why go?** Modern Japanese izakaya—try their karaage (tempura) with soba noodles.  

**Tip:** For soy-free options, specify "no soy sauce" when ordering. Many places use tamari or coconut aminos as alternatives.
