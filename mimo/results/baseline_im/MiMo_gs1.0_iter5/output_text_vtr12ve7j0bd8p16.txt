That's a fantastic question! Calgary is a great base for adventure, and you're spoiled for choices nearby. Here are some excellent weekend getaway options, balancing adventure, scenery, and romance, keeping your date's Sagittarius adventurous spirit in mind:

1.  **The Classic & Iconic: Banff National Park**
    *   **Adventure:** Hiking (Larch Valley, Plain of Six Glaciers, Lake Agnes Teahouse), Kayaking/Canoeing (Bow River, Kananaskis River), Rock Climbing (Johnston Canyon), Zip-lining, Wildlife Spotting (Bison, Elk), Horseback Riding.
    *   **Scenery:** Unbeatable. turquoise lakes, towering peaks, alpine meadows.
    *   **Romance:** Stunning sunset views (especially over Lake Louise or Moraine Lake), cozy fireside dinners, peaceful lakeside picnics.
    *   **Considerations:** Can be busy, especially on weekends. Book accommodations *far* in advance. Accessible by car (~2.5-3 hours).

2.  **Slightly Quieter Gem: Canmore & Kananaskis Country**
    *   **Adventure:** Hiking (Kananaskis Valley - incredible scenery, Johnson Lake, Horseshoe Pass), Kayaking/Canoeing (Kananaskis Lake, Spray Lakes), Mountain Biking (world-class trails), Rock Climbing (Canmore Rock壁).
    *   **Scenery:** Dramatic canyon landscapes, views of the Canadian Rockies, less crowded than Banff core.
    *   **Romance:** Serene mountain vistas, charming town atmosphere, excellent dining options. Easy access to Lake Louise or Moraine Lake for a day trip.
    *   **Considerations:** Very close to Banff scenery but often less crowded. Accessible by car (~1.5-2 hours).

3.  **For Water & Wildlife Lovers: Jasper National Park**
    *   **Adventure:** Hiking (Maligne Canyon, Skyline Trail, Spirit Valley), Kayaking/Canoeing (Maligne Lake), Wildlife Spotting (Jasper is prime for bears, elk, wolves), Maligne Lake Cruise & Skywalk.
    *   **Scenery:** Unique canyon landscapes, stunning lake views, different vibe than Banff.
    *   **Romance:** Mystical Maligne Lake at sunset, peaceful forest walks, stargazing (one of the darkest places in N. America).
    *   **Considerations:** Further (~4 hours), but offers a different experience. Can also book up fast.

4.  **For a Cozy Mountain Lodge Vibe: Lake Louise**
    *   **Adventure:** While the park is stunning, direct park access requires a long day trip. Focus on the **Lake Louise Village** itself. Hiking (shorter trails like Lake Agnes, or take the gondola for easier access), ice skating (winter), scenic drives (Lake Louise Road to Moraine Lake - check road status!).
    *   **Scenery:** Iconic postcard scene with the Victoria Glacier.
    *   **Romance:** Luxurious lodges (Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise), beautiful village walks, romantic dinners with mountain views, scenic train rides (if available).
    *   **Considerations:** Staying *in* the village is more romantic than trying to base yourself solely in the park for a short trip. Accessible by car (~1.5-2 hours).

5.  **For Adventure & Scenic Drives: Bow Valley Parkway / Icefields Parkway**
    *   **Adventure:** This isn't a "stay overnight" option, but a fantastic day trip or long weekend drive. Hike any of the numerous trailheads along the Bow Valley Parkway (e.g., Johnston Canyon, Spray Lakes). For a longer drive, the Icefields Parkway (to Jasper) is one of the world's most spectacular.
    *   **Scenery:** Rolling valleys, glaciers, dramatic mountain vistas.
    *   **Romance:** Stopping for a scenic picnic lunch, enjoying the drive together, possible sunset stop.
    *   **Considerations:** Best as part of a larger trip incorporating a place to stay overnight.

**Key Considerations for Your Adventure:**

*   **Book EARLY:** Especially for Banff, Jasper, and popular lodges. Hotels and campgrounds fill up months ahead.
*   **Check Conditions:** Road closures (especially Moraine Lake, Icefields Parkway in winter), trail closures, or weather can impact plans. Check Parks Canada or local tourism sites.
*   **Balance:** Mix adventure with relaxation. A long hike followed by a scenic drive or a romantic dinner is perfect.
*   **Romantic Touches:** Consider a scenic drive at sunset, a cozy dinner with mountain views, a picnic, or a relaxing hot tub at your accommodation.
*   **Sagittarius Spirit:** Focus on freedom, exploration, beautiful landscapes, and shared experiences. Keep it dynamic!

**Top Recommendations for Maximum Adventure & Romance:**

1.  **Canmore/Kananaskis:** Offers incredible adventure (hiking, kayaking, biking) right on the doorstep of stunning Rockies scenery, often with less crowd than Banff core. Very romantic vibe.
2.  **Lake Louise Village:** Perfect blend of iconic scenery, luxury romantic options (Chateau), and accessible hiking/scenic drives. Feels special.
3.  **Banff:** For the ultimate iconic Rockies experience with tons of adventure options and romantic lodges. Worth the planning effort.

Have an absolutely fantastic time! The Canadian Rockies are truly magical, especially when shared with someone as adventurous as a Sagittarius.
