### 🥂 Vodka Mixer (Refreshing & Customizable)
1. **Classic Vodka Soda**:  
   - 2 oz vodka + 4 oz sparkling water/soda  
   - *Optional twists*: Muddle 2-3 berries in the glass first, or add a squeeze of lime.  
   *Why it works*: Light, crisp, and crowd-pleasing.  

2. **Vodka & Berry Sparkler**:  
   - 2 oz vodka + 4 oz ginger beer  
   - *Optional*: Add 2-3 frozen raspberries or strawberries.  
   *Why it works*: Sweet-tart balance; feels festive.  

### 🍻 Argentine Fernet (Bitter-Sweet & Iconic)
1. **Fernet & Beer**:  
   - 1 oz Fernet-Branca + 4 oz lager/ale (chilled)  
   - *Optional*: Add 0.5 oz simple syrup (1:1 sugar/water) if you want it sweeter.  
   *Why it works*: Mimics the classic *mero* – bitter, herbal, and refreshing.  

2. **Fernet Soda**:  
   - 1 oz Fernet + 4 oz club soda  
   - *Optional*: Add a dash of lime or orange bitters.  
   *Why it works*: Lighter than beer; more accessible for non-beer drinkers.  

### ⚡ Pro Tips for Speed
- **Prep ahead**: Mix vodka sodas/sparklers in glasses with ice.  
- **Fernet hack**: Shake Fernet with ice for 10 seconds to mellow the bitterness before serving.  
- **No syrup?** Skip it – the beer/soda will dilute it slightly.
