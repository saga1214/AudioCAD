For a Halloween party, focus on **crowd-pleasing candies that are widely loved and easy to manage**. Here’s a quick guide to minimize leftovers and maximize fun:

### 🍬 Top Candy Picks (Universal Favorites)
1. **Classic Chocolate**:  
   - Milk chocolate bars (Hershey’s, Skittles, M&M’s)  
   - Peanut butter cups (Reese’s)  
   - Mini chocolate chips (affordable and easy to bag)  

2. **Gummies & Sours**:  
   - Starbursts, gummy bears, or gummy worms  
   - Sour patch kids (great for younger crowds)  

3. **Seasonal & Themed**:  
   - Pumpkin-spiced candy (if available)  
   - Candy apples or "ghost" lollipops (fun for photos!)  

4. **Sweet & Salty**:  
   - Toffee (Snickers, 3 Musketeers)  
   - Pretzels or caramel-coated chocolates  

### 💡 Smart Tips to Avoid Leftovers
- **Buy in Small Bags**: Purchase multiple small bags of popular candies (like mini M&M’s or gummies) instead of giant boxes.  
- **Use "Treat Bags"**: Place candy in small paper or cellophane bags so guests can grab what they want—reduces bulk leftovers.  
- **Rotate Stock**: Check candy levels throughout the party and replenish as needed.  
- **Allergy Note**: Avoid highly allergenic items (like nuts or gluten) unless you know all guests’ preferences.  

### 🌟 Pro Move
Pair candy with a **mini "spooky snack table"**—add popcorn, pretzels, or themed cookies to make it feel special without overcommitting.  

With these picks, you’ll have a festive spread that keeps guests happy and leaves you with *way* less cleanup! 🎃👻
