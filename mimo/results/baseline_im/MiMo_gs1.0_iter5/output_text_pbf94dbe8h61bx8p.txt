That's a wonderful goal! Here are some simple, meaningful, and age-appropriate craft ideas for your family Christmas gifts, designed for minimal supervision and maximum fun:

### For Little Ones (Ages 2-5)
1. **Finger-Painted Snowmen Ornaments**  
   - **How**: Dip kids' fingers in white paint and make snowmen on pre-cut paper. Add "snow" with blue paint.  
   - **Why**: Messy but contained, no tools needed.  
   - **Gift Idea**: Hang on the tree or give as small gifts.

2. **Stick Puppets**  
   - **How**: Glue colorful paper cutouts (eyes, mouths) to popsicle sticks.  
   - **Why**: Portable gifts, easy cleanup.  
   - **Tip**: Let older kids help with glue (supervised).

### For Middle Kids (Ages 6-10)
3. **Decoupage Jar Ornaments**  
   - **How**: Use Mod Podge to glue magazine cutouts or drawings onto a jar lid. Seal with more Mod Podge.  
   - **Why**: No drying time, customizable.  
   - **Gift Idea**: Perfect for classmates or neighbors.

4. **Homemade "Memory Box"**  
   - **How**: Decorate a small box with painted cardboard. Fill with photos, stickers, or handwritten notes.  
   - **Why**: Sentimental and reusable.  
   - **Tip**: Older kids can write messages; younger ones glue decorations.

### For Older Kids (Ages 11-15)
5. **Personalized Mug Lids**  
   - **How**: Paint or glue fabric to empty takeout coffee cups.  
   - **Why**: Functional gifts, no baking required.  
   - **Tip**: Let them write names or inside jokes.

6. **Coasters from Recycled Materials**  
   - **How**: Use cork sheets, tile scraps, or painted cardboard. Glue on buttons, feathers, or dried flowers.  
   - **Why**: Eco-friendly, stylish gifts.  
   - **Safety**: Supervise glue guns if needed.

### Family Projects (All Ages)
7. **"Family Recipe" Book**  
   - **How**: Kids illustrate their favorite holiday memories or favorite recipes.  
   - **Why**: Meaningful keepsake for everyone.  
   - **Tip**: Use scrap paper to avoid waste.

8. **Scrapbook Gift**  
   - **How**: Compile photos, drawings, and notes about why they love the recipient.  
   - **Why**: Shows thoughtfulness without cost.  
   - **Age Adaptation**: Younger kids draw; older kids write.

### Tips for Success
- **Prep Ahead**: Cut shapes, pre-glue materials, or pre-mix paints.  
- **Assign Roles**: "2-year-old: Glitter!" "15-year-old: Quality control!"  
- **Embrace Imperfection**: Handmade flaws make gifts more special!  
- **Theme It**: "Winter Village," "Animal Friends," or "Holiday Lights" for cohesion.

These projects prioritize **minimal cleanup**, **inclusive participation**, and **heartfelt gifts**—perfect for a busy mom and her creative crew! 🎄✨
