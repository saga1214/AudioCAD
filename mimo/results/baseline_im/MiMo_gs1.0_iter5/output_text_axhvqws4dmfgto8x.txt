Okay, let's talk about making stir-fry noodles without setting off the smoke alarm. The key is to avoid the incident you had before, which was using a wooden spoon in boiling water. So, the most important thing is to use a metal spoon or a wooden chopstick for stirring. That way, you won't create any sparks. 

Now, for the noodles themselves. The first step is to boil them, but don't overcook them. You want them to be 'al dente', which means they should be tender but still have a bit of a firm bite. If you overcook them, they'll get mushy and won't hold up well in the stir-fry. 

After they're cooked, drain them really well in a colander. Any extra water will make your stir-fry soggy, so make sure to pat them dry with a paper towel if you need to. 

Then, when you're ready to stir-fry, add the noodles to a hot wok or a large pan with some oil. Toss them around for a minute or two to heat them up evenly. This step is crucial because it helps the noodles absorb the sauce and prevents them from getting sticky. 

Finally, just toss in your cooked protein, veggies, and sauce, and mix everything together. You should be good to go!
