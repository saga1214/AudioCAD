Here's a clear breakdown of U-turn rules in British Columbia and the ingredients for Spaghetti Carbonara:

**U-Turn Rules in British Columbia:**

1.  **150-Meter Visibility Rule:** This is the key. You **must** be able to see a clear path for at least 150 meters (roughly the length of 1.5 football fields) in **both directions** before and after the turn. If there are curves, hills, or obstructions, this doesn't apply.
2.  **Signal:** You must signal your intention to turn.
3.  **Intersection Restrictions:** **Generally prohibited** at intersections controlled by traffic signals (traffic lights) unless a sign specifically permits U-turns.
4.  **No Obstruction:** You cannot make the turn in a way that interferes with the right-of-way of other traffic.
5.  **Designated Areas:** U-turns are permitted at intersections where signs indicate it's allowed, or in designated U-turn lanes.
6.  **No Turns on Curves/Hills:** Prohibited at curves, near the top of hills, or where you cannot see clearly in both directions.
7.  **No Turns Near Traffic Controls:** Prohibited near traffic signals, stop signs, or yield signs unless a sign allows it.

**Penalties for Illegal U-Turns:**

*   **Misdemeanor Offense:** Making an illegal U-turn is a traffic infraction.
*   **Cost:** Expect a fine, typically ranging from **$110 to $160** plus court costs. The total could easily be over $200.
*   **License Points:** Points will be added to your driver's license (usually 1 point).
*   **Insurance:** While unlikely to directly impact your rate, an accident caused by an illegal U-turn *could* be considered negligence.

**Key Takeaway:** When in doubt, **don't make the U-turn**. Look for signs, ensure you have clear visibility in both directions, and check for intersections. The penalty isn't just about the fine – it's about safety and the legal consequences.

**Spaghetti Carbonara Ingredients (for 4 servings):**

Here's a classic, authentic recipe focusing on simplicity:

*   **Pasta:** 400g (about 14 oz) Spaghetti (or other long pasta like Bucatini)
*   **Eggs:** 3 Large Eggs + 1 Large Egg Yolk (4 eggs total)
*   **Cheese:** 120g (4 oz) Pecorino Romano cheese, finely grated (plus extra for serving)
*   **Fat:** 120g (4 oz / 4 sticks) Guanciale (cured pork cheek) or very good quality Pancetta (diced small), OR thick-cut Bacon (diced small)
*   **Black Pepper:** Freshly ground Black Pepper (generous amount)
*   **Salt:** For pasta water
*   **Optional (Traditional):** 1-2 cloves Garlic (minced very finely or grated)
*   **Optional (Traditional):** Small handful of Fresh Flat-Leaf Parsley (finely chopped), stirred in at the end.

**Important Notes:**

*   **No Cream:** Authentic Carbonara uses eggs and cheese for richness, *not* cream. Adding cream makes it a different dish.
*   **Guanciale/Pancetta:** This is the traditional, essential fat. Bacon is a common substitute, but guanciale/pancetta has a deeper, more complex flavor.
*   **Egg/Yolk Ratio:** The 3 eggs + 1 yolk is key for the right texture and richness.

**Simple Cooking Instructions:**

1.  **Cook Pasta:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a rolling boil. Cook the pasta until it's *al dente* (firm to the bite). Reserve about 1 cup of the starchy pasta water before draining.
2.  **Cook Fat:** While pasta cooks, fry the diced guanciale/pancetta/bacon in a large skillet over medium heat until crispy and the fat has rendered out. If using garlic, add it in the last minute of frying until fragrant (don't let it burn). Remove from heat.
3.  **Make Sauce:** In a bowl, whisk together the 3 eggs, the 1 egg yolk, and a *generous* amount of finely grated Pecorino Romano. Season with lots of black pepper.
4.  **Combine Off Heat:** Immediately transfer the hot, drained pasta (and any released water) to the skillet with the hot fat. Toss well to coat. Remove the skillet from the heat. Quickly pour the egg/cheese/pepper mixture over the hot pasta. Using tongs, toss vigorously and quickly over the residual heat. The heat from the pasta will cook the eggs into a creamy, silky sauce without scrambling them. If the sauce seems too thick, add a splash (1-2 tbsp at a time) of the reserved pasta water.
5.  **Serve Immediately:** Plate immediately, top with more grated Pecorino Romano and a generous grind of black pepper. Add chopped parsley if using.

Enjoy your delicious, authentic Spaghetti Carbonara!
