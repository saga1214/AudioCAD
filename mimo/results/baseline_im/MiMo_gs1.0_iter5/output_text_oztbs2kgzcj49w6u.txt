Absolutely. Lockport has a fantastic museum called the Erie Canal Museum. It's located right next to the Lockport Locks and tells the entire story of the canal's history, engineering, and the town's connection to it. It would be the perfect place to start your exploration.

And since you mentioned the architecture in Rochester, you should definitely check out the Buffalo Naval Park. It's in a beautiful, historic building right on the Buffalo River. There, you can see the USS Croaker, a World War Two submarine, and even tour the old shipyard. It's a great blend of history and architecture.
