Of course. Here’s a quick checklist for filming your show.

First, check that the venue allows recordings and that you have permission to film the residents or staff. You might need to sign a release form or get their consent.

Next, you'll want to bring a tripod or a stand to keep the phone steady. If possible, try to get a friend to film for you or ask the staff for help.

Then, just plan a few short, high-energy skits for the video. Keep them simple and fun.

Finally, after filming, you can edit the video on your phone. You can cut out the boring parts, add music, and maybe add some text or captions for a little more flair.

Good luck with your show! It’ll be fun to share your work.
