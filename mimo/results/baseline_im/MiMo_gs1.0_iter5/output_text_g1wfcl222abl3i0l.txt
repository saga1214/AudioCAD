That's frustrating when the tools don't cooperate! Here's a quick troubleshooting guide for your glue gun:

1.  **Give it More Time (Most Common Fix):** Glue guns need a solid 10-20 minutes to fully heat up after being plugged in. If it's brand new or you just plugged it in, wait a few more minutes before trying again.
2.  **Check the Tip:** Is the tip pointing directly at the heat source? Sometimes it can get slightly cooler if not perfectly aligned. Re-position it.
3.  **Clean the Tip:** If it feels warm but not hot enough, the tip might be clogged. Unplug the gun, carefully remove the tip, and gently wipe it with a pencil eraser (damp is okay) or a dry paper towel. Reinsert it firmly.
4.  **Check the Glue Type:** Are you using the correct glue for your gun? Some guns work best with specific types of glue sticks (e.g., "hot glue" vs. "wood glue").
5.  **Check the Power Source:** Is the cord plugged securely into the wall outlet? Try a different outlet if possible. Is the cord frayed?
6.  **Is the Gun Old?** If it's very old, the heating element might be worn out. This is less likely if it's new.
7.  **Environmental Factors:** Is the room cold or very humid? Extreme cold can make glue harder to melt initially. Very humid air can sometimes affect performance.

**What to do next:**

*   **Wait 5 more minutes** and try again.
*   **If still not working:** Unplug it, carefully remove the tip, clean the tip, reinsert it firmly, plug it back in, and wait another 5 minutes before trying.
*   **If it's very cold or humid:** Try to position the gun closest to the outlet where it's warmest.
