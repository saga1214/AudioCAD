5. **Blood Pudding**: Traditional blood pudding contains pork and blood, which are non-kosher. You’ll need to:  
   - Find a kosher alternative (e.g., a meat-based pudding like *kreplach* or a vegetarian dessert).  
   - Omit it entirely and focus on other snacks.

- **Labels Matter**: Check ingredient lists for hidden non-kosher items (e.g., casein, gelatin from non-kosher sources, or additives).  
- **Preparation**: Use separate utensils/utensils to avoid cross-contamination with non-kosher ingredients.
