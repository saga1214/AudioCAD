That's a great question! The perfect end-of-year celebration depends on your energy, the girls' ages, weather, and budget. Here are a few ideas balancing fun, simplicity, and managing your pregnancy:

1.  **The Simple Cafeteria Celebration:**
    *   **Pros:** Easy to manage, no setup/cleanup stress, comfortable for everyone, low cost.
    *   **Cons:** Less "special event" feel, weather-dependent.
    *   **How to Make it Fun:** Instead of just snacks, create a themed party! Think:
        *   **"Superhero" Sundae Bar:** Let each girl build their own giant sundae with lots of toppings (sprinkles, chocolate, caramel, whipped cream, nuts). Add fun superhero-themed decorations.
        *   **"Pirate" or "Treasure Hunt" Theme:** Decorate the cafeteria with pirate flags or treasure chests. Hide small prizes (stickers, patches, small toys) for a simple scavenger hunt around the room.
        *   **"Pajama Party" Theme:** Girls come in pajamas! Serve popcorn, fruit snacks, and maybe a simple craft (making friendship bracelets or decorating cookies).
    *   **Food:** Focus on easy finger foods – the sundae bar is the star! Include popcorn, veggie sticks & dip, fruit cups, and maybe a cake or cupcakes.

2.  **The Park Picnic/Barbecue:**
    *   **Pros:** Classic fun, great for group bonding, outdoor space feels special, can be a big celebration.
    *   **Cons:** Weather-dependent (need a rain date!), more setup/cleanup, potential for mess, managing crowds.
    *   **How to Make it Fun:**
        *   **Keep it Simple:** Focus on food and group time. Avoid overly complex games that might get competitive or messy. Instead:
            *   **Group Craft:** Set up a large craft station near a shaded table (e.g., decorating a large poster of the Brownie logo, making friendship bracelets together).
            *   **Storytelling Circle:** Gather everyone in a circle for a group story or the girls share their favorite Brownie memory.
            *   **Simple Games:** Play cooperative games like "Statues" or "The Egg & Spoon Race" (if space allows). Keep it light and inclusive.
        *   **Food:** BBQ is easy! Pre-marinate chicken breasts or pork chops, or use a simple store-bought kit. Serve with buns, chips, fruit salad, and a cake. A sundae bar could also work if you have a cooler!
        *   **Parent Involvement:** Ask parents to sign up to bring specific food items (e.g., dessert, drinks, a side dish) or to help with setup/cleanup. This shares the load.

3.  **The "Celebration Picnic & Movie Night" (Indoor/Outdoor Hybrid):**
    *   **Pros:** Combines the fun of a picnic with the comfort of indoors if weather turns bad. Great for a relaxed vibe.
    *   **Cons:** Requires a projector/screen or large TV.
    *   **How to Make it Fun:** Set up picnic blankets indoors (cafeteria or gym) or outdoors. Serve picnic food (sandwiches, chips, fruit). Then, play a fun movie! "The Princess Diaries," "Up," or "The Muppets" are great choices. Add popcorn and candy.

4.  **The "Achievement Celebration" (Focus on Badges):**
    *   **Pros:** Highlights the year's accomplishments, feels very rewarding.
    *   **Cons:** Requires more planning to review progress.
    *   **How to Make it Fun:** Gather everyone (girls and parents if possible) and review the badges earned throughout the year. Have each girl show off her sash proudly. Celebrate specific achievements with small awards (stickers, certificates, small prizes). Keep it short and celebratory, then transition to a simple party with cake and snacks.

**Recommendation for You (Considering Pregnancy & New Troop):**

1.  **Prioritize Low Stress:** Given you're 7 months pregnant, choose something manageable. A **cafeteria sundae bar/party** or a **simple park picnic with parents helping** are often the easiest wins.
2.  **Focus on Food Fun:** Whatever you do, make the food a highlight! Sundae bars, pizza, or BBQ are always crowd-pleasers and feel special.
3.  **Keep Activities Simple:** Focus on group time, sharing memories, and enjoying food together rather than intense games. A craft station or storytelling can be perfect.
4.  **Involve Parents:** Clearly communicate what you need from parents (food items, help with setup/cleanup) so you're not carrying it all.

**Final Suggestion:** A **Park Picnic with a Sundae Bar** (if weather permits and you get parent help) or a **Decorated Cafeteria Sundae/Pajama Party** are probably your best bets for a fun, memorable, and relatively stress-free end-of-year celebration that honors your daughters' involvement!
