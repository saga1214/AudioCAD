While waiting for your hair dye to process, here's how to tune a **stringed instrument** (like a guitar, ukulele, or mandolin) using a tuner:

### 1. **Tuner Apps (Easiest & Most Common)**
   - **Download a free app** like *GuitarTuna*, *Fender Tune*, or *Tuner* (by Snark).
   - **Turn on your phone's microphone** (some apps require it for accuracy).
   - **Pluck each string** one by one.
   - **Follow the app's guidance**: The app will show which string is too high or low and tell you to pluck it harder/softer to reach the target note.

### 2. **Clip-On Tuner (For Precision)**
   - **Attach the tuner** to your instrument's headstock (like a guitar).
   - **Pluck each string** and watch the tuner's display.
   - **Adjust until the needle** (or light) indicates "perfect" tuning.

### 3. **Tuner Pedal (For Guitarists)**
   - **Plug your guitar** into the pedal.
   - **Play a string** and adjust until the pedal's display shows "0" or a green light.

### Key Tips:
- **Tune down** slightly if the tuner shows "high" (e.g., if the note is too sharp).
- **Tune up** slightly if the tuner shows "low" (e.g., if the note is too flat).
- **Tune in relative tuning**: Start with the 6th (thickest) string, then move up to the 1st (thinnest) string.
- **Use a reference pitch**: Apps often play a reference note; match your string to that pitch.

### Why This Works:
Tuners use **acoustic sensors** to detect string vibrations. Apps use your phone’s microphone, while clip-on/pedal tuners use built-in sensors. They convert vibrations into visual/audio feedback to guide tuning.
