Okay, you're drained and exhausted. Let's get you a quick energy boost with some drinks and snacks. For drinks, you could try green tea—it has caffeine but also L-theanine, which gives you calm energy without the jitters. Black coffee is another good option if you're okay with a stronger kick. A protein shake can give you quick protein and carbs for a quick energy lift. A smoothie with banana and spinach is also great for hydration and nutrients. And for a more balanced option, you could have a warm cup of herbal tea like chamomile, which can help you relax but still feels like a pick-me-up.

For snacks, almonds are a good choice because they have healthy fats and protein. A banana is great for potassium and quick energy. Greek yogurt is high in protein and good for sustained energy. Trail mix is a good option too, since it has a mix of nuts, seeds, and maybe some dried fruit. And for something simple, a hard-boiled egg is a great source of protein. 

These should help you feel a little better. Hope that helps you get through your day!
