Absolutely! Here's a step-by-step guide for each installation, keeping your landlord's rules and safety in mind:

### 1. **Mounting the TV**
   - **Tools Needed:** Tape measure, pencil, stud finder, drill, wall anchors (if needed), level, screwdriver.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Check Wall Type:** Use a stud finder to locate studs (wooden beams behind drywall). If no studs, use heavy-duty anchors.
     2. **Mark Mount Location:** Measure and mark where the TV will go. Use a level to ensure it’s straight.
     3. **Mark Mount Holes:** Transfer the TV mount’s screw holes to the wall.
     4. **Drill Pilot Holes:** Drill holes slightly smaller than your screws/anchors. If no studs, drill into the drywall with anchors.
     5. **Install Mount:** Secure the mount to the wall, then attach the TV.
   - **Tip:** Use a stud finder with a "TV mode" for precision. If unsure, hire a pro to avoid damage.

### 2. **Hanging Planters**
   - **Tools Needed:** Drill, wall anchors (for drywall), measuring tape, level, screws.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Choose Planter Type:** Use lightweight planters (ceramic, plastic) or heavy-duty metal ones with strong anchors.
     2. **Locate Wall Studs:** Find studs to anchor into. If no studs, use anchors rated for the planter’s weight.
     3. **Measure and Mark:** Place the planter where you want it, mark the screw holes, and use a level.
     4. **Install Anchors/Screws:** Drill holes, insert anchors, then screw the planter hanger into place.
   - **Tip:** For hanging plants (not just pots), use a chain or hook rated for the plant’s weight. Avoid overloading one spot.

### 3. **Installing Floating Shelves**
   - **Tools Needed:** Drill, wall anchors, measuring tape, level, screws, screwdriver.
   - **Steps:**
     1. **Plan Layout:** Sketch where shelves will go. Ensure spacing is even and functional (e.g., 12–18" from the floor).
     2. **Mark Shelf Positions:** Use a pencil to mark shelf edges. Double-check with a level.
     3. **Install Anchors:** Drill pilot holes for anchors (if needed) into studs or use heavy-duty drywall anchors.
     4. **Attach Shelves:** Secure shelves with screws into the wall. Test for stability.
   - **Tip:** Place shelves high enough to avoid tripping. Don’t overload them—max 5–7 lbs per shelf. Use adhesive strips for lightweight decor.

### **Key Safety & Rule Considerations:**
- **Noise:** Do installations early in the day (e.g., 9 AM–12 PM) to avoid disturbing neighbors.
- **Drilling:** Use a stud finder with noise detection to minimize vibrations. If you hear neighbors complain, pause and apologize.
- **Weight Limits:** Always check manufacturer guidelines for anchors/shelves. Overloading can damage walls.
- **Tools:** Start with a basic drill and level. For complex jobs, rent tools or hire a pro.

### **Bonus: Quick Decoration Tips**
- **Art Display:** Use a gallery wall for art—mix sizes and frames with tape measures for spacing.
- **Lighting:** Add LED strip lights under shelves for ambiance.
- **Functional Zones:** Group decor by theme (e.g., plants near windows, shelves for books).

By following these steps, you’ll create a stylish, functional space without breaking the rules! 🛠️✨
