Absolutely! Here are some delicious, easy-to-pack gluten-free snack ideas perfect for your camping and cycling trip:

1.  **Trail Mix (DIY or Pre-Packaged):** Combine your favorite nuts (almonds, walnuts, pecans), seeds (pumpkin, sunflower), unsweetened coconut flakes, and dried fruit (cranberries, raisins, apricots - *check labels for GF certification*). Avoid pretzels or cereals. Portion into small bags.
2.  **Roasted Chickpeas:** A fantastic crunchy, protein-packed, and fiber-rich snack. Look for plain, lightly salted, or flavored varieties (like everything bagel or chili lime) at the store or make your own. Pack in a small container.
3.  **Gluten-Free Beef Jerky/Teriyaki Sticks:** Provides a savory, protein-rich option that's easy to eat on the go. Look for brands specifically labeled gluten-free.
4.  **Fresh Fruit:** Pack easily portable options like:
    *   Apples (core with a small apple corer or apple slicer)
    *   Bananas (pack in a cooler if possible to prevent mushiness)
    *   Berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries - best in a cooler)
    *   Pears
    *   Oranges/Clementines
5.  **Veggies & Hummus:** Cut veggies like carrots, celery, bell peppers, or cucumber sticks. Pair them with single-serve packs of hummus (check labels for GF certification). Pack veggies in a container and hummus in an insulated bag with an ice pack.
6.  **Dark Chocolate:** A small piece or a bar provides a quick energy boost and antioxidants. Look for brands with minimal ingredients (just cocoa, sugar, etc.).
7.  **Gluten-Free Rice Cakes:** Light, crisp, and great for topping with nut butter or avocado if you have a cooler spot. Buy plain or lightly salted.
8.  **Nut Butter Packets:** Single-serve packets of almond butter, peanut butter, or sunflower seed butter are incredibly versatile. Use them on rice cakes, apple slices, or even just by the spoonful.
9.  **Dried Fruit:** Beyond trail mix, individual packs of raisins, mango, pineapple, or apricots are convenient. Choose unsweetened or lightly sweetened options.
10. **Hard-Boiled Eggs:** A great protein source. Pack peeled eggs in a container and keep them cold.

**Key Tips for Snacks:**

*   **Portion Control:** Pre-portion snacks into bags or containers to avoid overeating and make them easy to grab.
*   **Cooler Space:** Prioritize perishables like fresh fruit, veggies, and hummus in your cooler.
*   **Hydration:** Always pack plenty of water! Snacks work best when paired with hydration.
*   **Check Labels:** Even "naturally" gluten-free items like nuts, seeds, dried fruit, and jerky can sometimes be processed in facilities that also handle wheat, so look for "Gluten-Free Certified" labels when possible, especially for packaged items.

Enjoy your camping and cycling adventure!
