For a low-carb, high-protein dinner with minimal effort, here are three restaurant types that align with your goals:

1.  **Steakhouse:** Opt for grilled steak (ribeye, sirloin, filet) with a side salad (dressing on the side) or steamed broccoli/green beans. Skip the fries or mashed potatoes. Easy and protein-focused.

2.  **Sushi Restaurant:** Choose veggie rolls, sashimi, or nigiri with fish (like tuna or salmon). Request no rice or extra veggies. Skip fried items. Focus on fish and veggies.

3.  **Mexican Restaurant:** Order grilled chicken or fish tacos on **lettuce wraps** instead of tortillas. Or, just get a large salad with grilled protein and salsa/vinaigrette dressing. Avoid beans, rice, and flour tortillas.

These options let you prioritize protein and veggies without a lot of extra thought! Enjoy your meal.
