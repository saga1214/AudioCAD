That's a fantastic plan! Mexico is a dream for artists, especially for autumn and water scenes. Here's a tailored suggestion:

### **When to Go for Autumn & Water Inspiration**
- **Late September to November**: This is Mexico’s **autumn season** (equivalent to U.S. fall). You’ll see vibrant foliage, especially in the highlands (e.g., Pátzcuaro) and along the coast.  
- **Coastal Areas**: For water scenes, prioritize:  
  - **Riviera Maya** (Tulum/Cancún): Turquoise Caribbean waters, cenotes, and palm-fringed beaches.  
  - **Pacific Coast** (Acapulco/Huatulco): Dramatic cliffs, misty mornings, and deep blue ocean.  
  - **Baja California** (Ensenada): Seaside towns with rolling waves and desert-meets-ocean landscapes.  

### **Painting Inspiration & Techniques**
1. **Autumn Colors**:  
   - Use **raw sienna, burnt umber, and ochre** for foliage. Blend with **blue-green** for shadows.  
   - **Wet-on-wet** technique for soft, luminous leaves.  

2. **Water Scenes**:  
   - **Cenotes**: Paint the reflective, crystal-clear water with **vertical strokes** (use **payne’s gray + blue** for depth).  
   - **Ocean**: **Wet-on-dry** for crisp waves (use **phthalo blue + cerulean**). Add foam with **white gouache**.  

3. **Watercolor Markers**:  
   - Sketch **detailed outlines** (e.g., palm leaves, rocks) with markers before wetting the paper.  
   - Blend edges with a damp brush for soft transitions.  

### **Pro Tip**  
Visit **Xcaret Park** near Cancún—it has natural pools and rock formations perfect for practicing water reflections.  

**Final Advice**: Pack light! Focus on **small studies** (sketchbook-sized) to capture fleeting moments. Mexico’s light and colors will ignite your creativity—happy painting! 🎨✨
