Okay, so you're looking for a casual dinner spot in Austin for your first night. The vibe should be relaxed and easygoing. Let me think about some options that fit that description. First, there's Taco Deli. It's super casual, open late, and has amazing tacos. It's a local favorite, not too fancy, and perfect for unwinding. Another option could be Odd Duck. It's farm-to-table but still pretty laid-back, with a diverse menu that's great for trying different things. Then there's Veracruz All Natural. It's a food truck turned restaurant, so it's super casual, and their migas tacos are legendary. If you want something more central, like near South Congress, there's ABGB. It's a brewery with a great outdoor space, which can be nice if the weather's good. And if you're feeling like something simple and iconic, you can't go wrong with a classic breakfast taco spot like Home Slice Pizza or Veracruz. They're casual, delicious, and give you a taste of Austin without any fuss. Any of these should give you a really chill, local experience for your first night. Enjoy Austin!
