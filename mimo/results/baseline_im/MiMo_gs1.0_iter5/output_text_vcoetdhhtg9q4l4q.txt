For *ultra-fast* dinner ideas, here are three quick options (under 15 minutes):

1. **Frozen Meal Rescue**:  
   Thaw a pre-cooked frozen meal (like stir-fry kits or salmon) + microwave rice noodles. Top with a squeeze of lime and sesame seeds.  

2. **"No-Cook" Assembly**:  
   Layer pre-cooked chicken/tuna with shredded cabbage, avocado, and soy sauce in a bowl. Add a fried egg on top (or just a boiled egg slice).  

3. **One-Pot Speed**:  
   Sauté frozen veggies + canned beans in a pan. Pour in broth and a splash of soy sauce. Serve over microwaved rice.  

**Bonus Tip**:  
If you’re short on time, prep staples ahead:  
- Cook a big batch of rice/noodles once a week.  
- Buy pre-cut veggies or frozen stir-fry mixes.  
- Store sauces (soy, oyster, teriyaki) in small containers.
