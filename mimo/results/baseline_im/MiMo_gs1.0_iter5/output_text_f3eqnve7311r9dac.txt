That's a great strategy. Older comics are definitely rarer, and the print runs were much smaller. Let's look at ten significant books from that era that are still affordable, meaning under five thousand dollars.

Number one is *Detective Comics* number 27, which is Batman's first appearance. It's incredibly rare and expensive, but you can find later graded copies that are still valuable.

Number two is *Action Comics* number one, the first appearance of Superman. This is the holy grail, but again, you can find later-graded copies.

Third is *Amazing Fantasy* number 15, the first appearance of Spider-Man. A great key, and you can find affordable copies.

Fourth is *Whizzer Comics* number one. This is the first appearance of the Avengers, and it's a very rare book.

Fifth is *Adventure Comics* number 250, which is the first appearance of the Fantastic Four.

Sixth is *Tales to Astonish* number 35, which is the first appearance of the Hulk.

Seventh is *The Silver Surfer* number one. This is the first full appearance of the Silver Surfer, and it's a great key issue.

Eighth is *Fantastic Four* number 48. It's the first appearance of Galactus and the Silver Surfer.

Ninth is *The Invincible Iron Man* number 1. This is the first appearance of Doctor Doom and the first appearance of Iron Man in his own title.

And tenth is *Amazing Spider-Man* number 31. It's the first appearance of the Vulture, a classic key issue.
