Here's a detailed shopping list for your three-course meal, designed to be simple, flavorful, and allergy-friendly (no legumes, no seafood):

### **Key Notes & Substitutions**
1.  **Legume Allergy:** Edamame (soybeans) is a legume. **Replace Miso Soup Edamame with:** Extra soft-boiled eggs, sliced mushrooms, or wakame seaweed (ensure no soy sauce). *Confirm soy sauce/tamari is gluten-free if needed.*
2.  **No Seafood:** Dashi broth is made from kombu (seaweed) and bonito (fish flakes). Use **instant dashi powder** labeled "vegetarian" or make **dashi with only kombu** (simmer kelp in water for 15-30 mins, strain). Bonito flakes contain fish.
3.  **Simpler Miso Soup:** If making without edamame, just use dashi, miso, soft-boiled egg, and green onions.
4.  **Matcha Ice Cream:** If you decide to make it, simply blend 1/2 cup matcha powder with 1 cup heavy cream and 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk. Pour into a container, cover, and freeze. Stir every 30 mins for 4-6 hours.
5.  **Sticky Rice:** Thai sticky rice is different from regular rice. Find it in Asian markets or frozen sections in supermarkets. If unavailable, use jasmine rice *but know the texture will be different*.
