That's wonderful! Showing your daughter how proud you are after months of hard work is the perfect way to celebrate her. Here are some thoughtful gift ideas, ranging from simple to more elaborate:

**Sentimental & Personalized:**

1.  **Personalized Playbill/Poster:** Get a high-quality print of the official playbill or poster framed. You can often personalize them with her name and role. This is a direct link to her achievement.
2.  **"Best Supporting Parent" Trophy:** Find a fun, kid-friendly trophy or medal. While the play might not have a "Best Supporting Parent" award, the gesture is incredibly meaningful. Look for ones with funny descriptions like "Most Dedicated Audience Member" or "Best Applause Partner."
3.  **Personalized Certificate:** Create a certificate of achievement: "In Recognition of Your Outstanding Performance as [Character Name] in [Play Title] - Opening Night, [Date]. We Are So Proud of Your Dedication!" You can personalize it further with a funny or sweet note.
4.  **A Special Photo Album or Scrapbook:** Fill it with photos from rehearsals (if you have them), backstage pictures, tickets, and maybe a small note from the director or teacher. Title it "Opening Night Memories - [Character Name]."
5.  **A Heartfelt Letter:** This is the *most* important part! Write a detailed letter expressing how proud you are of her hard work, dedication, bravery (getting on stage!), and how much you enjoyed watching her. Mention specific things you loved about her performance or her character. Handwrite it for maximum impact.
6.  **A "Thank You" to the Cast/Crew:** If you have a small budget, consider a gift basket or small gift card to a local cafe for the whole cast/crew. Include a note thanking them for the great show and highlighting your daughter's contribution.

**Fun & Celebratory:**

7.  **Costume Accessory:** If she had a costume she loved or wanted, something simple like a cool hat, fun shoes, or a small accessory related to her character could be a fun keepsake.
8.  **Special "Winner" Prize:** Think about what she'd genuinely love as a reward for her hard work – a favorite book, a small craft kit, a fun toy, a special outing (like ice cream, a movie, or a visit to the park), or a small piece of jewelry (like a simple necklace or bracelet).
9.  **Experience Gift:** Plan a special activity for the next day or weekend – a fun outing, a trip to an amusement park, or just a special family dinner out. Frame it as "Your reward for an amazing opening night!"
10. **A Small Basket:** Combine a few small, thoughtful items: a fun water bottle, a cozy blanket, a small puzzle, a box of her favorite chocolates, and your heartfelt letter.

**Key Tips for Maximum Impact:**

*   **The Letter is Key:** Whatever gift you choose, **your heartfelt letter** expressing your pride is the most valuable part. Be specific about what you loved watching her do.
*   **Presentation:** Wrap the gift nicely, or present it in a basket. Make it feel special.
*   **Timing:** Give it to her *after* the show, ideally when she's feeling tired but proud and surrounded by her castmates if possible. Maybe during the cast party or the next morning.
*   **Acknowledge the Effort:** In your letter, explicitly mention the *months* of practice and dedication – that's what makes this achievement so special.

**Choose what feels most like *you* and what resonates best with your daughter!** The combination of a small, tangible gift (like the personalized trophy or certificate) and your sincere words will make her feel incredibly loved and proud. Good luck at the show!
