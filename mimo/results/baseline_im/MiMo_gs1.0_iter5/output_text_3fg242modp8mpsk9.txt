What a wonderful shift from fiction to reality! Planning a romantic coastal getaway focused on oceans and seafood sounds perfect. Here are some creative water-based activities and on-shore ideas to blend adventure, relaxation, and great food:

**Romantic Water Activities (Calm & Scenic):**

1.  **Sunset Kayak Paddle:** Rent tandem or single kayaks at dusk. Paddle slowly along a protected bay, marsh, or calm shoreline. The changing light, gentle waves, and intimacy of sharing a small vessel create magic. (Ensure calm water conditions).
2.  **Private Boat Charter (Sunset Cruise or Dinner):** Many towns offer small boats (like catamarans or gulets) for sunset cruises or private dinners at sea. This is the ultimate romantic gesture, offering privacy, stunning views, and often gourmet seafood.
3.  **Beachcombing & Shell Hunting:** Explore tide pools at low tide (safety first - watch for rocks/crabs!) or walk the shore after a storm. Collect unique shells, sea glass, and treasures together – tangible keepsakes from your time by the sea.
4.  **Sailing Lesson (For Fun & Connection):** Many places offer introductory sailing lessons for couples. Learning something new together on the water is incredibly bonding and romantic. Choose a calm day.
5.  **Stand-Up Paddleboard (SUP) Yoga or Meditation:** Rent boards and practice a gentle yoga flow or simply sit and meditate on the water at sunrise or sunset. The peacefulness and shared focus are deeply romantic.
6.  **Snorkeling in Calm Waters:** If you find a calm, clear bay or lagoon (research local conditions), snorkeling together is a shared adventure. Discover the underwater world hand-in-hand.
7.  **Fishing (Chill Style):** Rent a small boat for a relaxed fishing trip, or fish from a pier or dock. The shared focus, anticipation, and potential for a fresh catch (or just the experience) can be very bonding.

**On-Shore Adventures & Food Focused Experiences:**

1.  **Seafood Market Tour & Tasting:** Visit the local fish market (the heart of fresh seafood!). Ask the fishermen what's best. Have them pack a selection for a picnic on the beach or a special dinner at home. Explore nearby markets for local specialties.
2.  **Cooking Class (Seafood Focus):** Take a couple's cooking class centered around local coastal cuisine and seafood. Learn to prepare fresh catches, shrimp, lobster, or fish together in a fun, interactive setting.
3.  **Beach Picnic with Local Bites:** Grab sandwiches, salads, or that market-fresh seafood from a deli or bakery. Head to your favorite quiet beach spot with a blanket, cushions, and maybe a Bluetooth speaker for music. Pure, simple romance.
4.  **Explore charming harbors:** Stroll hand-in-hand through the picturesque marinas. Admire the boats, watch fishing boats come in, maybe chat with a fisherman. It's scenic, relaxed, and connects you to the sea's rhythm.
5.  **Visit a Local "Fish & Chips" Spot:** Find the town's most beloved (and perhaps unpretentious) fish & chips place. It's a classic, shared experience focused on simple, delicious seafood.
6.  **Scenic Coastal Walk:** Many towns have beautiful walking paths along the water. Pack a thermos of hot chocolate or coffee and walk the path, stopping at viewpoints, hidden coves, or charming seaside shops.
7.  **Taste Local Drinks:** Explore the local craft beverage scene! Try fresh oysters (if you're adventurous), local craft beer brewed with sea salt or local ingredients, or a glass of crisp local wine.

**Key Tips for Your Romantic Escape:**

*   **Prioritize Comfort:** Ensure your accommodation is comfortable and has good views of the water.
*   **Research Local Gems:** Look for highly-rated, local seafood spots – not just tourist traps. Ask locals or your accommodation host for their favorites.
*   **Embrace Spontaneity:** Leave room for unplanned moments – that perfect sunset, the charming little shop you discover, the unexpected taste of sea salt.
*   **Focus on Connection:** Put phones away (except for photos of your seafood feast!), talk, laugh, enjoy the simple things together.
*   **Consider the Seasickness Factor:** As you mentioned, your partner's seasickness is important! Stick to the calmest water activities or ensure private charters/cruises are *very* stable vessels. Many sunset cruises are designed for comfort.
