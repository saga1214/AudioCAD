That's a common issue! If cheese isn't enticing, try these alternatives:

**High-Value Treats (Work Great for Training):**
1.  **Cooked Chicken (in tiny bits):** Almost all dogs love it. Ensure it's plain, boneless, skinless, and cooked (no salt/oil).
2.  **Peanut Butter (Xylitol-Free!):** The *small* amount smeared on a finger or on a training stick is incredibly motivating. **Crucially, ONLY use peanut butter that is 100% peanut butter, NO additives, and absolutely NO Xylitol (a dangerous sweetener).** Check the label!
3.  **Boiled Egg (chopped tiny):** Another universally loved option.
4.  **Commercial Training Treats:** Look for tiny, soft, smelly bits specifically designed for training (e.g., "Training Puffs," "Zippy Chews," or small bits of "Zones" or "Yum Yums"). They're often cheaper than high-value ingredients.

**Liver Tips:**
*   **Freeze-Dried Liver:** Smaller pieces, stronger scent, and often more appealing than fresh. Try it!
*   **Soft Baked Liver:** Sometimes softer and more palatable than dried.

**Key Training Tips:**
1.  **Start Small:** Use very tiny pieces of your chosen treat. You'll need to give them often!
2.  **High-Value is Key:** For new tricks or when you need her focus, use the *most* enticing treat (like chicken or peanut butter).
3.  **Timing is Crucial:** Reward the *exact* moment she does the right thing (or even just tries!).
4.  **Keep it Short & Fun:** 5-10 minutes is plenty for a new dog. End on a positive note.
5.  **Quiet Space:** If she's distracted outside, try a quieter room first.

**For Maisy's Calm Nature:** Since she's a small, gentle breed, keep training sessions relaxed and positive. Break down tricks into tiny steps. Celebrate small successes! Her grooming will naturally be part of bonding – just keep those sessions short and positive too.

You're doing a fantastic job! Maisy is lucky to have you. Keep experimenting with treats until you find her absolute favorite, and enjoy the journey of training together!
