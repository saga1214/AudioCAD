Of course, I can help you structure that email. Here’s a way to approach it.

First, the subject line could be something like: 'Request for Feedback on Recent Exam'.

Then, you can start with a polite opening, like: 'Dear Professor [Last Name], I hope this email finds you well.'

Next, explain your purpose. You could say: 'I'm writing to respectfully request feedback on my recent exam score. I'm eager to understand how I could improve my performance on the essay and incorporate that knowledge into my future work.'

It's also good to acknowledge his teaching style, which might help. For example: 'I've heard about your rigorous standards, and while I appreciate the challenge, I was surprised by the grade I received.'

Then, you can ask your specific questions. You could say: 'Could you point me to specific areas in my essay where I could have performed better? I'm particularly interested in understanding how you weighed different components of the assignment.'

To show you're serious about learning, you could add: 'I'm committed to doing better in this class and would welcome any advice on how to approach future essays.'

Finally, you can end by saying something like: 'Thank you for your time and guidance. I look forward to your feedback.'

Then just sign off with 'Best regards,' and your name.
