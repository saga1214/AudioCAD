Here’s an ocean-themed menu for you and your fiancée, featuring spicy, mango-infused dishes and sweet desserts—perfect for your anniversary night:

### **Appetizers**
1. **Mango & Shrimp Ceviche**  
   *Tender shrimp marinated in lime, diced mango, red onion, cilantro, jalapeño, and a pinch of cumin.*  
   Serve with plantain chips for crunch.

2. **Spicy Tuna Tartare with Seaweed Crisps**  
   *Crispy nori crackers topped with seasoned tuna, avocado, mango salsa, and a hint of chili.*

### **Mains**  
3. **Coconut Shrimp Tacos**  
   *Pan-fried shrimp in coconut milk-spiced batter, served in corn tortillas with mango salsa, pickled jalapeños, and cilantro-lime slaw.*  

4. **Grilled Shrimp & Scallop Bowl**  
   *Seared shrimp and scallops over coconut rice, topped with mango salsa, avocado, and a spicy tomato sauce.*  

5. **Mango & Shrimp Stir-Fry**  
   *Shrimp and scallops stir-fried with bell peppers, snap peas, and a coconut-mango sauce. Served with jasmine rice.*

### **Sides**  
- **Mango Salsa** (fresh mango, tomato, red onion, cilantro, lime)  
- **Seaweed Salad** (kelp or wakame with sesame oil, rice vinegar, and sesame seeds)  
- **Coconut Rice** (steamed with coconut milk and vanilla)

### **Desserts**  
1. **Mango-Coconut Panna Cotta**  
   *Silky vanilla panna cotta layered with mango puree and toasted coconut.*  

2. **Tropical Fruit Tart**  
   *Passionfruit curd filling in a coconut crust, topped with mango, kiwi, and blueberry glaze.*  

3. **Chocolate-Dipped Shrimp Tails**  
   *Mini dark chocolate-dipped shrimp tails with a drizzle of caramel or raspberry sauce.*  

4. **Coconut Rice Pudding**  
   *Creamy coconut rice pudding swirled with mango compote and toasted coconut flakes.*  

5. **Blueberry-Lime Cheesecake**  
   *Gooey cheesecake in a graham cracker crust, topped with blueberry compote and lime zest.*

### **Theme Integration**  
- **Decor:** Use blue/green tablecloths, seashell centerpieces, and "under the sea" lanterns.  
- **Garnish:** Edible seaweed, tropical fruits, or a mini shrimp cake on each plate.  

This menu balances spicy, sweet, and savory flavors while celebrating your shared love for mango and seafood. Enjoy your ocean-themed anniversary! 🌴🍤🌊
