1.  **Hot Water & Baking Soda/Vinegar (First Attempt):**
    *   **Why:** Heat can soften the blockage, and the chemical reaction can help break it down.
    *   **How:** Pour a kettle full of **boiling water** down the drain. Let it sit for 5 minutes. Then, pour in about 1/2 cup of **baking soda**, followed immediately by 1 cup of **white vinegar**. Cover the drain with a plug or cloth to contain the fizzing. Let it sit for 20-30 minutes. The fizzing action is the reaction breaking down organic matter.

2.  **Mechanical Removal (Manual Tools):**
    *   **Why:** If the plunger didn't work, the clog is likely deep in the pipe or a solid object. Manual tools give you direct control.
    *   **How:**
        *   **Zip-It Tool / Hand Auger:** This is your best bet for deep clogs. Insert the flexible head into the drain. Slowly crank the handle clockwise while gently pushing forward. It should catch the clog and start to pull it back. Once you feel resistance, crank *counter-clockwise* to break it up and pull it out. You can often pull the entire clog out in one piece.
        *   **Drain Snake / Snake Auger:** If you have a longer one, insert it similarly. Crank to hook the clog and pull it back. Use the rotating head carefully to avoid damaging pipes.

3.  **Enzymatic Drain Cleaner (Alternative to Chemical):**
    *   **Why:** If you prefer not to use harsh chemicals, enzymatic cleaners work by breaking down organic matter (hair, grease, food) with bacteria and enzymes. They are generally safer for pipes and septic systems.
    *   **How:** Follow the product instructions carefully. Usually involves pouring a specific amount down the drain, letting it sit for several hours or overnight, then flushing it out with hot water. Give it time to work.

4.  **Harsh Chemical Drain Cleaner (Use with Caution):**
    *   **Why:** For very stubborn clogs, chemicals can dissolve them quickly. **WARNING:** Can be corrosive to pipes (especially older PVC), unsafe if used improperly, and can damage septic systems. **Always follow the manufacturer's instructions EXACTLY.**
    *   **How:** Pour the correct amount down the drain according to the product label. It usually takes 15-30 minutes. Flush thoroughly with boiling water. **NEVER mix different chemical cleaners!**
