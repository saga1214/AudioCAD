That's a fantastic choice! Secret Harbour Resort is right on the water and offers easy access to some amazing daytime adventures. Here are some great options, keeping your brother's needs and your interests in mind:

1.  **Explore the Pristine Beaches of Virgin Islands National Park (St. John):**
    *   **Trunk Bay:** Famous for its underwater snorkeling trail (great for beginners), beautiful white sand, and calm lagoon. Perfect for swimming and easy snorkeling.
    *   **Cinnamon Bay:** Offers stunning views, calm waters, and a small beach bar. Excellent for swimming and snorkeling around the rocky coves.
    *   **Sage Rock:** A bit further south, known for its vibrant coral gardens and excellent snorkeling. The sand is also beautiful. The trail to get there is short and flat.
    *   **How to Get There:** The easiest way from Secret Harbour is to take a **water taxi** from Cruz Bay. It's scenic, relaxing, and usually affordable. You can easily visit one or two beaches in a day.

2.  **Snorkel the Reefs (Shallow & Easy Access):**
    *   **Secret Harbour Bay:** Right from your doorstep! The bay itself is calm and shallow, great for beginners. Look for parrotfish munching on algae!
    *   **Cruz Bay Beach (St. John):** Another excellent spot with easy access to vibrant coral gardens and fish just offshore. Great for swimming and snorkeling.
    *   **The Baths (St. John):** While the interior coves can be rocky, the main bay entrance and some of the larger pools are very shallow and perfect for swimming and easy snorkeling without needing to dive. It's a unique and beautiful experience.
    *   **Water Taxi Advantage:** The water taxi is perfect for hopping between these spots. You can snorkel at Secret Harbour, then take the taxi to Trunk Bay or Cruz Bay for a different experience.

3.  **Lunch & Explore Cruz Bay (St. John):**
    *   **Cruz Bay Beach:** Grab lunch right on the water at one of the casual beachside cafes or restaurants (like **The Beach House** or **Salty Dog**). Enjoy the calm bay, people-watch, and relax.
    *   **Cruz Bay Market & Shops:** Browse local crafts, grab souvenirs, and pick up snacks or ice cream. Stroll along the main street.
    *   **Cruz Bay Bar:** Grab a drink (or two!) at a lively beachfront bar like **The Longboard** (mentioned earlier) or **Cruz Bay Beach Bar** and soak up the sun.

4.  **Bird Watching (Your Interest!):**
    *   **St. John:** While not as famous for rare birds as some other islands, you'll definitely spot **Bananaquits** (look for the tiny, bright yellow ones with red faces), various **hummingbirds** (like the Antillean Crested Hummingbird), and shorebirds like **plovers** and **turnstones** along the beaches and near the water taxis. The mangroves in Cruz Bay also attract birds like **herons** and **kingfishers**.
    *   **Red Hook (St. Thomas):** If you take the ferry or taxi to Red Hook, the area is rich in birdlife, especially near the mangroves and on the beaches. You might see **cormorants**, **gulls**, and again, **Bananaquits**. The **Cruz Bay Lighthouse** area also has interesting birds.

5.  **Relaxing Kayaking (Paddle Shallow Waters):**
    *   Several companies offer guided or unguided kayak tours from Cruz Bay or other beaches. These usually focus on exploring mangrove channels or paddling along the coast in calm, shallow waters – perfect for your brother. You might spot herons, egrets, and maybe even a small turtle!

6.  **Visit the Magens Bay Area (St. Thomas - if you switch islands):**
    *   **Magens Bay:** While famous for its deep blue water and calmer than St. John's lagoons, it's still generally calm enough for swimming. The iconic blue water is stunning. Accessible via a short taxi ride from Cruz Bay.
    *   **Coke Beach:** A quieter, less crowded alternative to Magens Bay, also with beautiful sand and water. Great for a peaceful swim and lunch at the beach bar.

**Key Considerations for Your Brother:**

*   **Calm Waters:** Stick to lagoons, sheltered bays, and shallow reefs for swimming and snorkeling. Avoid areas with strong currents or waves.
*   **Easy Access:** Choose beaches with gentle entry, like the lagoons at Trunk Bay, Cinnamon Bay, or Cruz Bay Beach. The water taxi docks are also usually very shallow.
*   **Avoid Deep Diving:** Snorkel only in the very shallow areas near shore where you can stand.
*   **Sun Protection:** The Caribbean sun is intense. Reapply sunscreen frequently, wear a hat, and consider rash guards for snorkeling.

**How to Plan Your Day:**

1.  **Morning:** Start with a relaxed morning at Secret Harbour or a nearby beach bar for breakfast.
2.  **Late Morning/Afternoon:** Take a water taxi to explore one or two beaches (e.g., Secret Harbour -> Trunk Bay). Snorkel, swim, relax.
3.  **Lunch:** Grab lunch at a beach cafe in Cruz Bay or one of the beaches you visited.
4.  **Late Afternoon:** Return to Secret Harbour for sunset drinks or take a taxi/ferry to Red Hook for dinner and the vibrant nightlife scene.

Enjoy your fantastic week in the Virgin Islands! The boat charter and Secret Harbour location give you amazing access to both relaxation and adventure.
