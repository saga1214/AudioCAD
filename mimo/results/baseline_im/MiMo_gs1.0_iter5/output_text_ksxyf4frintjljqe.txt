Yeah, of course. A molten chocolate cake is a great choice. It's rich and decadent, and it cooks in a small pan so it's perfect for sharing. You just brown some chocolate and butter in a ramekin, pour in the egg yolks and sugar, then add flour and cocoa. Bake it until the center is still a little gooey. Serve it warm with some vanilla ice cream or whipped cream, and it should be a hit. It's a comforting way to remember her love for baking.
