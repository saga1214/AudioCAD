Okay, so after a day of desert camping, you want to hit Vegas for a proper, luxurious experience. That's a great plan. First, I'd suggest booking a nice hotel, maybe one with a pool and a good spa. You can find options from mid-range to high-end. Then, definitely splurge on a few hours at the spa to relax and get that hot shower you mentioned. After that, you could hit up a nice restaurant for dinner, maybe somewhere with a view of the Strip or a casino. If you're up for it, a night out at a club could be fun, but you might just want to unwind. The key is to balance the adventure with some pampering. It's all about enjoying the city after a day in nature.
