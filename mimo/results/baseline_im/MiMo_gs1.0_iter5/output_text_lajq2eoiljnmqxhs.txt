That's a fantastic plan! Cooking together can be a great bonding experience, especially with a comedy to lighten the mood first. Here's a suggestion for a quick, easy, and adaptable dish perfect for two people with your sister's restrictions:

**Sister's Stir-Fry with Tofu & Veggie Bowls**

*   **Why it works:**
    *   **Quick:** Ready in 20-30 minutes.
    *   **Easy:** Minimal prep, mostly just cooking and assembling.
    *   **Adaptable:** Easy to skip disliked veggies or swap them out.
    *   **Vegetarian/Vegan Base:** Naturally avoids poultry, seafood, and dairy. Easily adaptable.
    *   **Fun & Interactive:** You can each pick veggies or sauces.
    *   **Lactose-Free:** Uses plant-based alternatives.

*   **Ingredients (Serves 2):**
    *   **Protein:** 1 block (14-16oz) **Extra-Firm Tofu**, pressed and cubed (or 1 can (15oz) **Chickpeas** or **Black Beans** - rinsed and drained)
    *   **Veggies (Choose 3-4 you both *might* like):** 1 cup **Broccoli Florets**, 1 cup **Bell Peppers** (any color), 1 cup **Snap Peas** or **Sugar Snap Peas**, 1 cup **Zucchini** (sliced), 1 cup **Carrots** (julienned or thinly sliced) - *Skip any strong dislikes.*
    *   **Sauce Base:** 3 tbsp **Low-Sodium Soy Sauce** or **Tamari** (ensure gluten-free if needed), 1 tbsp **Rice Vinegar**, 1 tsp **Toasted Sesame Oil**, 1 tsp **Maple Syrup** or **Honey**, 1 tsp **Grated Fresh Ginger**, 1 clove **Garlic**, minced (or 1/2 tsp garlic powder), 1 tsp **Cornstarch** (for thickening).
    *   **Cooking:** 1-2 tbsp **Oil** (like avocado, canola, or peanut oil).
    *   **Garnish:** **Green Onions** (sliced), **Toasted Sesame Seeds**, **Sliced Green Peppers** (optional).

*   **Instructions:**

    1.  **Prep:** Press tofu (wrap in towels, place on a heavy pan for 10-15 mins), cube. Prep all veggies. Whisk together soy sauce/tamari, rice vinegar, sesame oil, maple syrup/honey, ginger, garlic, and cornstarch in a small bowl.
    2.  **Cook Protein:** Heat oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add tofu (or chickpeas/beans) and cook until golden brown on most sides (5-7 mins). Remove and set aside.
    3.  **Stir-Fry Veggies:** Add a little more oil if needed. Add harder veggies first (carrots, broccoli) and stir-fry for 2-3 mins. Add softer veggies (peppers, snap peas, zucchini) and stir-fry for another 2-3 mins until crisp-tender.
    4.  **Combine & Sauce:** Return the cooked protein to the pan. Pour the sauce mixture over everything. Stir constantly for 1-2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats everything evenly.
    5.  **Serve:** Divide into two bowls. Garnish with green onions, sesame seeds, and sliced green peppers (if using).

*   **Tips for Success:**
    *   **Involve Her:** Let her pick which veggies she's okay with or which sauce ingredients she prefers (e.g., more ginger, less sweet).
    *   **Keep it Simple:** Stick to the recipe as written for the first attempt. Don't overcomplicate it.
    *   **Lactose Check:** Double-check *all* packaged sauces (soy sauce, tamari, cornstarch packets if pre-made) for hidden dairy ingredients. Use certified vegan versions if concerned.
    *   **Make it a Bowl:** Serve over a small bed of pre-cooked **quinoa**, **brown rice**, or even **cauliflower rice** if you have it, for extra substance.
    *   **Focus on Fun:** Laugh together while chopping, cook together, talk about the movie. The activity is secondary to connecting.

This dish gives you a shared accomplishment and a delicious meal without triggering any dietary restrictions. Enjoy your movie night and cooking session!
