Great choice! Here are a few more **gluten-free, energizing snack ideas** that pair well with your SkinnyPop and Popchips:

1. **Roasted Chickpeas**:  
   Toss canned chickpeas (drained and dried) with olive oil, salt, and spices (like paprika or cumin), then roast at 400°F (200°C) for 20-25 minutes until crispy. High in protein and fiber!

2. **Trail Mix**:  
   Combine unsalted almonds, pumpkin seeds, dried cranberries, and gluten-free dark chocolate chips. Customize with walnuts or coconut flakes for extra crunch.

3. **Fruit Leather**:  
   Choose brands like **Annie’s** or **Fruit Roll-Ups** (gluten-free verified). Portable, sweet, and hydrating. Pair with a handful of almonds for balanced energy.

4. **Homemade Energy Balls**:  
   Blend oats (certified GF if needed), almond butter, honey, chia seeds, and mini dark chocolate chips. Roll into bite-sized balls—no baking required!

5. **Beef Jerky or Turkey Roll-Ups**:  
   Opt for **gluten-free jerky** brands (like Epic or No Meat No Drama) or roll turkey slices around avocado or hummus for savory protein boosts.

**Pro Tip**: Pack a reusable water bottle with you at all times—hydration is key for both cycling and digestion!
