Based on your Nordstrom Rack purchase, here are the best ways to attach something to your jacket lining without damaging it, starting with the simplest:

1.  **Use a Zipper Pull Tab (Easiest & Most Secure):**
    *   **Find the Right Size:** Look for a zipper pull tab on a jacket you *don't* need, or buy one online (search "zipper pull tab").
    *   **Position:** Place the tab over the zipper teeth on the *inside* of the jacket lining, right where you want the attachment point.
    *   **Secure:** Use strong, clear, fabric glue (like E6000 or Fabric Fusion) or a very small drop of strong craft glue (like Tacky Glue). Press firmly and hold for a minute. Let it dry completely before testing. *Avoid sewing if possible, as this is the least damaging method.*

2.  **Attach to a Pocket (If Available):**
    *   **Find a Secure Pocket:** Look for a well-made, sturdy pocket on the jacket (like a chest pocket or front pocket).
    *   **Use Adhesive:** If the pocket lining is accessible, use strong fabric glue (E6000 is excellent for this) to attach the item securely. Ensure the pocket is large enough to hold it comfortably and isn't too bulky.
    *   **Consider a Clip:** If the pocket is small or you need easy access, use a strong fabric clip (like a heavy-duty binder clip or a specialized fabric clip). Clip the item securely through the pocket lining or fabric.

3.  **Use a Fabric-Safe Hook & Loop (Velcro):
    *   **Find the Right Type:** Purchase small, strong Velcro patches or hook-and-loop tape designed for fabric (search "fabric Velcro patch").
    *   **Position:** Stick one piece firmly to the *inside* lining of the jacket where you want the attachment point. Use strong fabric glue or double-sided tape designed for fabric.
    *   **Attach the Item:** Use the other piece of Velcro or hook-and-loop on the item itself. Press firmly to attach. Velcro is very secure and easy to remove without damaging the lining.

4.  **Use Fabric Glue (Simple but Less Durable):**
    *   **Apply:** Apply a small amount of strong, clear, fabric-safe glue (like E6000 or Fabric Fusion) to the *back* of the item you want to attach.
    *   **Position & Press:** Carefully place the glued item onto the *inside* lining of the jacket exactly where you want it. Press firmly for several minutes. Let it dry completely according to the glue instructions. *This is best for very lightweight items like small tags, charms, or lightweight fabric patches.*

**Key Tips to Avoid Damage:**

*   **Clean Surfaces:** Ensure both the jacket lining and the item's surface are clean and dry before gluing.
*   **Less is More:** Use the smallest amount of glue possible. Over-gluing can seep through and feel sticky or ruin the lining fabric.
*   **Test First:** If possible, test your chosen method on an inconspicuous area of the jacket (like the hem or side seam) first.
*   **Avoid Needles:** Sewing is the most secure but also the most likely to damage delicate linings or pull threads. Only do this if you're very confident in your sewing skills and the lining is sturdy.
*   **Be Gentle:** When attaching or removing items, be gentle to avoid snagging the lining fabric.

**Recommendation:** The **Zipper Pull Tab** or **Fabric Velcro** methods are generally the most secure and least likely to damage the lining significantly. Start with these! Good luck with your new jacket!
