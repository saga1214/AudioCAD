**Easy & Customizable Tofu Stir-Fry with Rice**

**Why it works:**
*   **Quick:** Ready in 20-30 minutes.
*   **Easy:** Minimal prep, simple steps.
*   **Customizable:** You can easily swap out ingredients based on what you *both* like (or dislike).
*   **Vegetarian/Vegan Option:** Naturally avoids meat, seafood, poultry, and dairy (use a dairy-free sauce).
*   **Lactose-Friendly:** Easily adaptable.
*   **Collaborative:** Requires teamwork and talking.
*   **Low-Pressure:** Focus is on the food, not complex conversation.

**Basic Concept:**

1.  **Prep:** Press and cube firm or extra-firm tofu (optional but recommended for better texture). Chop 2-3 cups of mixed veggies you both like (e.g., bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas, carrots, mushrooms, onions, zucchini, cabbage - *pick ones she won't complain about!*).
2.  **Cook Tofu:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add tofu cubes, spread out, and cook for 5-7 minutes until golden brown on most sides. Remove tofu to a plate.
3.  **Stir-Fry Veggies:** Add another 1 tbsp oil if needed. Add harder veggies first (like carrots, broccoli stems) and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes. Add softer veggies (like peppers, mushrooms, zucchini) and stir-fry for another 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
4.  **Combine:** Return the tofu to the pan with the veggies. Pour over your sauce (see below).
5.  **Serve:** Serve immediately over cooked rice (jasmine, basmati, or brown rice). You can garnish with sesame seeds or green onions if you like.

**Simple Sauce Recipe (Makes enough for 2):**

*   2 tbsp Soy Sauce (or Tamari for gluten-free)
*   1 tbsp Rice Vinegar
*   1 tbsp Maple Syrup or Honey (adjust to taste)
*   1 tsp Grated Fresh Ginger (or 1/2 tsp ground ginger)
*   1 clove Garlic, minced (or 1/2 tsp garlic powder)
*   1/2 tsp Sesame Oil
*   1/4 cup Water or Vegetable Broth
*   (Optional: 1 tsp sriracha or chili paste for heat)

**How to Make it Work for Your Situation:**

1.  **Veggie Selection is Key:** Choose vegetables that *both* of you might enjoy. Avoid any that you know she dislikes. Focus on colorful, crunchy veggies.
2.  **Tofu Flexibility:** Tofu is mild and absorbs flavor well. If she's hesitant, you could also use pre-cooked chickpeas or edamame as a protein base instead, though the texture is different.
3.  **Sauce Control:** The sauce is simple but allows for customization. Taste it together! Adjust sweetness, saltiness, or heat level *together*.
4.  **Keep it Light:** Emphasize that it's about having fun together, not perfection. "Let's just toss some stuff together and see what happens!" can put less pressure on.
5.  **Dairy-Free:** Naturally avoids cheese or yogurt. If you want a topping, use sesame seeds or sliced green onions instead.
6.  **Post-Cooking:** After eating, you could immediately move on to another low-pressure activity like browsing a thrift store, playing a simple board game, or just hanging out with snacks.

This stir-fry is perfect because it's fast, requires you to communicate about choices (veggies, sauce tweaks), and allows you to control the ingredients to avoid her dislikes. It creates a shared accomplishment and a positive experience together. Enjoy your movie night and cooking session!
