Absolutely! Vancouver is a gateway to some incredible road trips. Here are five fantastic options for you, from coastal adventures to mountain escapes.

First, there's the classic Vancouver Island trip. You can drive down to the famous Tofino for stunning rainforest hikes and beachcombing. For food, you can have a luxurious breakfast at the Fairmont Victoria and then a fine dining experience at the renowned Hotel Zee.

Next, for a coastal adventure, you can head to the Gulf Islands. You can take a ferry to the San Juan Islands and go whale watching. For a special meal, you could try a romantic dinner at The Bistro in Vancouver or a farm-to-table experience at Hvar in North Vancouver.

If you're up for a mountain escape, the Kootenay and Revelstoke area is amazing. You can go skiing and hiking in the winter and summer. For a beautiful meal, you could try the fine dining at the Fairmont Okanagan Resort or enjoy a scenic drive through the Okanagan Valley to visit the wineries.

Another great option is the Okanagan Lake region, which is famous for its wineries. You can tour the wineries and enjoy the beautiful lakeside views. For a special meal, you could try the farm-to-table cuisine at the Fairmont's restaurant, or for a more casual but delicious option, try the famous poutine at the local restaurant, The Keg.

Finally, if you're looking for something a bit more remote and unique, you can drive to the Pacific Rim National Park. It's a rugged, beautiful area with hiking and wildlife viewing. For a special meal, you could find a great spot for fresh seafood in Tofino or enjoy a cozy campfire dinner.

These trips offer a great mix of adventure, beautiful scenery, and special dining, perfect for a family with a new baby and a new dog.
