Okay, you're going to try stir-fry! That's great. Let's focus on the noodles so you can have a successful first time. Here's a simple way to do it:

First, get a big pot and fill it with plenty of water. The more water, the better. Then, make sure to salt the water generously, just like you would for pasta. That's a key step for flavor.

Next, bring the water to a full, rolling boil. This is important because it helps the noodles cook evenly and prevents them from sticking together.

Once it's boiling, add the noodles and stir them a few times right away. This helps separate them.

Then, cook them for the time on the package, but maybe start checking a minute or two early. Taste one to see if it's done. It should be al dente, which means it has a little bit of a firm bite to it.

When they're ready, drain them really well in a colander. And here's the most important part: do not rinse them with cold water. Rinsing makes them sticky. Just drain them and set them aside.

If you want, you can toss the hot noodles with a little bit of the hot oil from the stir-fry itself to keep them from sticking together. 

You've got this! Good luck with the stir-fry.
