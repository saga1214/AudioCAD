That's the spirit! With Leo's charisma and Sagittarius' love for freedom and exploration, you're practically a fire-powered adventure duo. Here are some fantastic weekend getaway options near Calgary, ranging from easy drives to slightly longer journeys:

1.  **Kananaskis Country (Closest & Most Versatile - ~1.5 - 2 hours):**
    *   **Why it fits:** This is Calgary's backyard playground. It's stunningly beautiful with lake, river, forest, and mountain scenery, offering endless activities.
    *   **Adventure Ideas:**
        *   **Hike the Kananaskis Lakes Loop:** A beautiful, moderately challenging hike offering incredible views and multiple lakes. Perfect for shared exploration.
        *   **Bike the Kananaskis Valley Recreation Trail:** A paved, scenic trail perfect for biking (rentals available in Canmore).
        *   **Kayak or Canoe Kananaskis Lake:** Paddle out on the stunning lake for a peaceful yet adventurous water experience.
        *   **Horseback Riding:** Several stables offer guided trail rides through the valley.
        *   **Explore Ghost Rocks:** A unique, mysterious geological formation with trails winding through the area (a bit more offbeat).
        *   **Stop at the Kananaskis River Inn:** A charming spot for a picnic or drinks with a view.

2.  **Banff National Park (~2.5 - 3 hours):**
    *   **Why it fits:** The ultimate Canadian mountain adventure destination. Iconic, breathtaking, and packed with options.
    *   **Adventure Ideas:**
        *   **Hike Johnston Canyon to Lower Falls:** Easy/moderate, stunning waterfall views (crowded but iconic).
        *   **Hike Bow Falls:** Shorter, closer to Banff Town, beautiful waterfall.
        *   **Hike Lake Louise Lakeshore:** Iconic views, but can be busy. Consider hiking to Lake Agnes Tea House (moderate).
        *   **Hike Moraine Lake (if accessible):** Requires a shuttle reservation, but the views are world-class (moderate).
        *   **Rock Climbing:** Banff is a world-class destination for rock climbing (various skill levels).
        *   **Mountain Biking:** World-class trails in Banff and nearby Canmore.
        *   **Sightseeing:** Drive the Icefields Parkway (one of the world's most spectacular drives), visit the Columbia Icefield, see the Athabasca Glacier. Pure awe-inspiring adventure.
        *   **Kayak or Canoe on Lake Louise or Moraine Lake:** Requires a shuttle, but a magical way to experience the lakes.

3.  **Emerald Lakes (Near Kananaskis, ~1 hour):**
    *   **Why it fits:** A stunning, relatively easy hike (1.5-2 hours round trip) leading to a series of turquoise lakes set against the backdrop of Mount Assiniboine. Less crowded than Banff or Canmore.
    *   **Adventure Idea:** Hike the trail, swim in the lower lake (if warm enough), relax, and soak in the incredible alpine scenery. It's pure, accessible mountain magic.

4.  **Canmore & the Bow Valley (~1 hour):**
    *   **Why it fits:** A charming mountain town nestled in the Bow Valley, gateway to Banff and Kananaskis, with its own adventure hub.
    *   **Adventure Ideas:**
        *   **Hike the Rimrock Rock Wall Trail:** Offers incredible views of Canmore and the surrounding mountains (moderate).
        *   **Hike the Moose Mountain Trail:** Panoramic views of Canmore and the Bow Valley (moderate).
        *   **Mountain Biking:** World-class trails right in Canmore (rentals available).
        *   **Explore the Canmore Dinosaur Site:** A unique geological adventure for the curious.
        *   **Visit the Elk & Bear Gallery:** See the massive elk and bear taxidermy (a quirky adventure!).
        *   **Drive the Icefields Parkway:** Stop at Johnston Canyon, Bow Falls, and the Columbia Icefield.

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Time of Year:** Summer (June-Aug) offers the most activities. Fall (Sept) is stunning for hiking. Winter brings skiing/snowboarding (Banff, Canmore, Lake Louise). Spring can be beautiful but trails might be muddy/snowy.
*   **Your Date's Comfort:** Kananaskis and Emerald Lakes offer easier hikes. Banff and Canmore offer more challenging options. Ghost Rocks are unique but not strenuous.
*   **Crowds:** Banff and Lake Louise get very busy, especially in summer. Kananaskis and Emerald Lakes are generally less crowded. Early starts are key for popular spots.
*   **Booking:** For activities like guided rides, rock climbing, or popular shuttle services (like for Moraine Lake), booking in advance is highly recommended, especially in peak season.
