Alright, here is a detailed shopping list for the three-course meal. For the appetizer, miso soup, you'll need miso paste, tofu, nori seaweed, green onions, and dashi stock. For the main course, chicken teriyaki, you'll need chicken thighs, soy sauce, mirin, sugar, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, rice, and stir-fry veggies like bell peppers and broccoli. For the dessert, mango sticky rice, you'll need sticky rice, coconut milk, sugar, and fresh mango. Don't forget essential pantry items like cooking oil, salt, pepper, and sesame seeds. I've also included some optional things like chili paste for the miso soup or extra garnishes. For the dessert, a squeeze of lime is nice. Enjoy your meal!
