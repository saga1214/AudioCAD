You're in luck, as the Lockport area has several excellent ways to learn about the canal. The top recommendation is the Erie Canal Museum in Lockport, which is housed in a beautifully restored canal warehouse. It's a great place to start.

For an active experience, you can actually walk or bike along the canal's towpath, which stretches for miles. This is a perfect way to see the physical remnants of the canal's history, like old locks and mounds of silt. And since you're interested in architecture, the Lockport's historic downtown is a great place to wander, as many buildings were constructed with materials from the canal era.
