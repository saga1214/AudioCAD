### U-Turn Rules in British Columbia & Penalties
1.  **General Rule:** U-turns are legal, but **only** when:
    *   You are not at an intersection with a traffic signal (unless a sign explicitly permits it).
    *   You can see at least 150 meters ahead and to the sides in both directions.
    *   You are not interfering with traffic flow.
    *   You are not on a curve, hill, or near a building where sight is obstructed.
    *   You are not on a road with a "No U-Turn" sign.
    *   You are not within 100m of a bus stop or pedestrian crossing.
2.  **Penalties for Illegal U-Turns:**
    *   **Ticket:** You will receive a ticket.
    *   **Fine:** As of late 2023/early 2024, the standard fine is **$160** (plus a processing fee).
    *   **Driver's License Points:** You will receive **2 demerit points** on your license.
    *   **Repeat Offenses:** If you commit an illegal U-turn within 3 years of your first offense, your license could be **suspended** for up to 90 days.

### Spaghetti Carbonara Recipe (Ingredients to Buy)
Here's the classic, authentic recipe (no cream!):

*   **Pasta:** 400g (14 oz) Spaghetti (or other long pasta like Bucatini)
*   **Protein:** 150-200g (5-7 oz) **Guanciale** (cured pork jowl - essential for authentic flavor, found at Italian grocers) *or* Pancetta (cured pork belly).
*   **Eggs:** 3 Large Eggs + 1 Extra Yolk (for richness)
*   **Cheese:** 100g (about 1 cup) **Pecorino Romano** (hard, salty sheep's milk cheese) *or* Parmigiano Reggiano (softer, milder). Grate finely.
*   **Black Pepper:** Freshly ground Black Pepper (lots!).
*   **Optional:** Pinch of Salt (if guanciale isn't salty), Red Pepper Flakes (for a hint of heat).

**Key Tips:**
1.  **Guanciale is Key:** It renders beautifully and gives the sauce its signature flavor. Pancetta is a good substitute.
2.  **No Cream:** Authentic carbonara gets its creaminess from the eggs and cheese.
3.  **Temper the Eggs:** Whisk eggs, yolk, cheese, and pepper together *just* before adding them to the hot pasta. The residual heat cooks them perfectly.
4.  **Salt the Pasta Water:** Cook pasta in well-salted water (like the sea is in it).
5.  **Reserve Pasta Water:** Save about 1 cup of the starchy pasta water before draining.
6.  **Combine Off Heat:** Toss the hot pasta with the rendered guanciale/pancetta in a large bowl. Remove from heat. Quickly mix in the egg/cheese/pepper sauce. If it looks too dry, add splashes of the reserved pasta water until it's creamy and glossy. Serve immediately with extra cheese and pepper.
