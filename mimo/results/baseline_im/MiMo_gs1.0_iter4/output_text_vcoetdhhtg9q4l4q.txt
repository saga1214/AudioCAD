For super quick dinners with minimal ingredients, here are some foolproof ideas:

### 🍳 1. **Egg Scramble**  
**Ingredients:** Eggs, salt, pepper, optional: cheese, spinach, or leftover cooked veggies.  
**How:** Whisk eggs, cook in a pan, add extras at the end. Takes 5 minutes!

### 🐟 2. **Tuna Salad**  
**Ingredients:** Canned tuna, mayo (or Greek yogurt), diced celery, salt, pepper.  
**How:** Mix and serve on toast, crackers, or lettuce wraps. Ready in 3 minutes.

### 🥣 3. **Oatmeal Bowls**  
**Ingredients:** Oats, water/milk, cinnamon, peanut butter, banana, or berries.  
**How:** Cook oats, top with 1-2 pantry staples. Breakfast-for-dinner vibes!

### 🥬 4. **Avocado Toast**  
**Ingredients:** Bread, avocado, salt, pepper, optional: chili flakes or everything bagel seasoning.  
**How:** Toast bread, mash avocado, sprinkle toppings. 5 minutes total.

### 🍜 5. **Instant Noodles Upgrade**  
**Ingredients:** Noodles, hot water, frozen veggies, egg (optional).  
**How:** Cook noodles, add frozen veggies and egg, top with soy sauce or chili oil.

### 💡 **Pro Tip:**  
Keep a "emergency kit" in your pantry: canned beans, pasta, rice, canned tomatoes, spices, and a few frozen veggies. These can rescue you on chaotic nights.

Minimal ingredients = maximum flexibility. You’ve got this! 🙌
