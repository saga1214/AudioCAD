Based on your stay at Secret Harbour Beach Resort and your interests, here are some fantastic daytime activities for your trip:

1.  **Snorkel at Coki Beach (St. Thomas):** Just a short walk from Secret Harbour, Coki Beach is renowned for its calm, clear waters and vibrant coral reefs teeming with tropical fish. Perfect for beginners and great for spotting colorful fish and maybe even a sea turtle. Grab a picnic and enjoy it right on the sand.

2.  **Boat Charter Excursion (St. Thomas or St. John):** Chartering a private boat (like your plan!) offers ultimate flexibility. Popular day trips include:
    *   **Trunk Bay (St. Thomas):** Famous for its underwater walkway – one of the best snorkeling spots in the Caribbean. The beach itself is stunning.
    *   **Virgin Islands National Park (St. John):** Visit Waterlemon Cay for excellent snorkeling (check conditions first), or just sail around the beautiful bays.
    *   **Coki Bay (St. Thomas):** Explore the smaller coves and reefs right near your resort area.
    *   **The Baths (Virgin Gorda):** While a longer trip (about 45-60 mins from St. Thomas), this is a MUST-SEE. Navigate through the unique, maze-like rock formations (boulders) to hidden coves and pools. Great for exploring, swimming, and spotting marine life. Bring water shoes!

3.  **Hike to the Soggy Dollar Bar (Virgin Gorda):** This is the *only* way to get to the legendary Soggy Dollar Bar – a unique bar built into the rock at the base of Baths Beach. The hike down is a short but rocky adventure (about 15-20 mins), but the payoff is incredible views, the famous "Painkiller" drink, and a truly unique atmosphere. The hike back is easier.

4.  **Explore Cruz Bay (St. John):** Take a short taxi or bus ride to Cruz Bay, the main town on St. John. Stroll along the waterfront, visit the charming local shops and galleries, grab a coffee, and soak in the laid-back island vibe.

5.  **Kayak or Paddleboard at Secret Harbour or Nearby Bays:** Many resorts offer kayak or paddleboard rentals. Paddle out from Secret Harbour or one of the nearby bays (like Anegada's pristine beaches are also accessible by boat) to explore the coastline quietly and get up close with the water life. Great for spotting birds and fish from above.

**Key things to remember for your brother:**

*   **Avoid Rapid Pressure Changes:** Stick to activities where pressure changes are minimal. Boat trips with smooth sailing are generally fine, but avoid rapid ascents/descents in submarines or certain high-speed water sports. Calm snorkeling and kayaking are usually safe bets.
*   **Stay Hydrated:** Flying or even significant boat travel can cause ear issues. Staying hydrated helps.
*   **Listen to His Body:** If he feels any discomfort, take it easy. Focus on relaxing on the beach or calm water activities.

Enjoy your incredible week in the Virgin Islands! The boat charter and Secret Harbour location will give you amazing access to beautiful beaches, snorkeling, unique landscapes, and a taste of island life.
