### 🧠 **Color-Coding for Icons**:  
- **Memory vs. Mental Load**: Color-coding primarily *reduces mental load* by creating instant visual cues (e.g., blue for work projects, green for personal tasks). It doesn’t *enhance memory* directly but speeds up recognition, letting your brain focus on the task instead of searching.  
- **Subtlety is Key**: 2-3 colors max. Too many colors = chaos. Group similar icons together first, then assign colors to *categories* (not individual apps).  

### 🎧 **Playlist Rotation**:  
- **Optimal Timing**: Switch every 30-60 minutes. Lo-fi’s steady rhythm is great for focus, while ambient nature sounds (rain, ocean) reset sensory fatigue.  
- **Personal Preference Wins**: If you notice distraction when switching, stick to one. If boredom creeps in, rotate. Experiment!  

### 📁 **Physical Documents**:  
- **Environment vs. Chunking**: A clean workspace *starts* progress, but **chunking tasks** (e.g., "file invoices first, then receipts") drives momentum. Tackle 30-60 mins on weekends—small wins build momentum!

### 🍽️ **Dinner Suggestions** (Quick, Balanced & Budget-Friendly):  
1. **Lentil & Veggie Soup**  
   - *Why*: Warm, hearty, and pantry-friendly. Use leftover roasted veggies!  
   - *Quick Tip*: Simmer canned lentils + broth with carrots, celery, and spinach. Top with a squeeze of lemon.  

2. **Sheet-Pan Salmon with Roasted Veggies**  
   - *Why*: 20 mins, one pan, rich in omega-3s.  
   - *Quick Tip*: Toss broccoli/carrots with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Add salmon fillets skin-side down. Finish with lemon zest.  

3. **Stir-Fry with Tofu & Rice**  
   - *Why*: Customizable, high-protein, and uses leftovers.  
   - *Quick Tip*: Sauté frozen stir-fry veggies + pre-cooked rice. Add cubed tofu or edamame. Sauce: soy sauce + ginger + garlic.  

4. **Tuna Salad Wraps**  
   - *Why*: Zero cooking, protein-packed, and perfect for a lazy night.  
   - *Quick Tip*: Mash canned tuna with Greek yogurt (not mayo), diced celery, and dill. Wrap in whole-grain tortillas with spinach.  

---

**Final Tip**: If you’re craving Mediterranean flavors tonight, try the **salmon stir-fry** with lemon-herb sauce (oregano + parsley + lemon juice). It’s bright, fresh, and feels like a treat without the effort!
