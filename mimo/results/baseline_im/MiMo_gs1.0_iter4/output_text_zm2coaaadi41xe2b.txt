For Asian noodle spots in Dallas, you should check out some of the best local places. One place is Tokyo Joe's, which has great ramen. Another is Saizeriya, which is affordable Italian, but they also have some Asian-inspired dishes. There's also Pho 79, which is a good spot for pho, but they also serve noodle dishes. And of course, there are plenty of other options too, like Little Saigon in Plano or the Asian district in Garland. 

As for protein bars, it depends on your needs. If you want something high in protein, look for bars with at least 20 grams of protein. Some good brands are Quest, which are low-carb and taste good, or Optimum Nutrition which has a classic vanilla flavor. If you want something more natural, Clif Builders has a high protein option. And if you need something quick and easy, RXBAR is a good choice with simple ingredients. 

Hope that helps with both your hunger and your snack game!
