That's a great question! If Maisy isn't into cheese, let's explore some high-value, small-dog-friendly treats. Here are some top alternatives, especially focusing on liver varieties:

1.  **Freeze-Dried Liver:**
    *   **Best Choice:** This is often the *most* enticing treat for dogs, especially smaller breeds like Maltese. Freeze-drying removes moisture, concentrating the flavor intensely.
    *   **Varieties:** Look for **freeze-dried chicken liver, beef liver, or liver pâté**. Chicken liver is usually a safe bet and very popular. Beef liver is also great.
    *   **Texture:** It's soft enough for small mouths but dissolves slowly, keeping Maisy focused. Just ensure it's pure (no added fillers or garlic/onion powder).

2.  **Cooked Chicken:**
    *   **Highly Valued:** Shredded or diced plain, boiled, or baked chicken breast is often a huge hit and smells amazing. It's a classic high-value training reward.
    *   **Texture:** Soft and easy to eat. Avoid bone-in chicken.

3.  **Boiled Egg:**
    *   **Nutritious & Enticing:** A small piece of the yolk (or the whole white/yolk) is soft, easy to digest, and smells great to dogs. It's a fantastic treat, especially if Maisy isn't into meaty flavors.

4.  **Peanut Butter (Xylitol-Free!):**
    *   **Slow Reward:** Use a tiny smear of **natural, xylitol-free peanut butter** (check the label!). The thick texture rewards Maisy for licking it off your finger slowly.
    *   **Texture:** Thick and spreadable. Ensure it's pure peanut butter, not the kind loaded with sugar or hydrogenated oils.

5.  **Commercial Training Treats:**
    *   **Designed for Motivation:** Look for small, soft, smelly training treats specifically formulated to be high-value. Brands like Zuke's Mini Naturals, Greenies Dental Treats (small size), or Wellness Training Treats are popular choices.

**Key Tips for Choosing & Using Treats:**

*   **Small & Easy Chewing:** Maisy is tiny, so treats need to be small, soft, and easy to swallow. Freeze-dried liver, chicken bits, or egg pieces fit this perfectly.
*   **High Value:** Start with the most motivating treats (like freeze-dried liver or chicken) for new tricks. You can rotate to less intense treats later.
*   **Consistency:** Use the *same* type of treat for the *same* trick. Maisy needs to know what to expect.
*   **Timing:** Reward the *exact moment* she does the right thing.
*   **Portion Control:** Even high-value treats add up. Use just a few per training session.
*   **Ask Maisy's Preference:** If she loves one liver type (chicken) but not another (beef), go with the one she prefers! Her taste matters most.

**Start with Freeze-Dried Chicken Liver:** It's incredibly popular, soft enough for her tiny mouth, and packed with flavor. Try a few pieces and see if she lights up! If not, cooked chicken or boiled egg are excellent backups. Enjoy bonding with Maisy!
