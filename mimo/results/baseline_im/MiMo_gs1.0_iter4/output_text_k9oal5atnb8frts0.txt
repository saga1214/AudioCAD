Here are two full meal ideas designed around the "Retro Tech" theme, focusing on simplicity, theme integration, and accommodating dietary needs:

**Theme:** **Retro Tech Day**

**Core Idea:** Embrace the nostalgia of different eras of computing with food that evokes that feeling.

---

### Meal Idea 1: "Mainframe Lunch" (Inspired by the 1980s)

*   **Centerpiece:** Display a large, old-school computer keyboard (like an IBM Model M) or even a small "mainframe" model prominently.
*   **Main Dish:** **"Power User Pasta" Lasagna**
    *   *Why it fits:* Lasagna is hearty, layered, and reminiscent of the powerful, complex machines of the era. It's a crowd-pleaser.
    *   *Dietary Notes:* Easily made vegetarian/vegan (use veggie lasagna sheets, plant-based cheese). Can be made gluten-free with GF pasta.
*   **Themed Snacks:**
    1.  **"Tang" Hydration:** Serve Tang (the iconic 80s drink) alongside water/juice. Offer Tang in small cups.
    2.  **"Pretzel Chips" & "Chips Ahoy!":** Classic 80s snack combos. Pretzels represent the sturdy hardware, "Chips Ahoy!" look like little computer chips.
    3.  **"Fruit Snacks" (Original):** The simple, colorful fruit snacks were ubiquitous in the 80s.
*   **Side Salad:** **"Tangy Tech" Salad**
    *   *Why it fits:* A simple green salad with a tangy vinaigrette dressing evokes the "techy" vibe.
    *   *Dietary Notes:* Naturally vegetarian/vegan (skip cheese). Can be made GF by using GF croutons.
*   **Dessert (Optional but thematic):** **"Diskette Delight"** - Serve small bowls of chocolate pudding or Jell-O (classic 80s desserts) alongside individual "diskettes" (empty floppy diskettes filled with something simple like pudding mix or fruit punch).

---

### Meal Idea 2: "Dial-Up Delight" (Inspired by the 1990s)

*   **Centerpiece:** Display a vintage computer mouse (like a Microsoft Intellimouse), a small CRT monitor, or a stack of old floppy disks.
*   **Main Dish:** **"Tuna Melt" & "Cracker Stackers"**
    *   *Why it fits:* The tuna melt is simple, portable (like early laptops), and instantly nostalgic. "Cracker Stackers" look like stacks of old computer hardware or floppy disks.
    *   *Dietary Notes:* Easily made vegetarian (use vegetarian tuna/chickpea salad). Can be made GF with GF crackers.
*   **Themed Snacks:**
    1.  **"Fruit Snacks" (Original or Striped):** The quintessential 90s snack.
    2.  **"Chocolate Milk" or "Squirt" Drink:** The simple, popular drinks of the era.
    3.  **"Pretzel Rods" & "Rice Krispie Treats":** Combines sturdy hardware with a classic sweet treat.
*   **Side Salad:** **"Netscape" Salad**
    *   *Why it fits:* A simple green salad with a creamy ranch dressing (like the Netscape Navigator was the "浏览器" of its day).
    *   *Dietary Notes:* Naturally vegetarian/vegan (skip cheese). Can be made GF by using GF croutons.
*   **Dessert (Optional but thematic):** **"Web Cake"** - Serve small, individual slices of a simple cake or cupcakes decorated with fondant "browsers" or "cookies" (representing the internet).

---

**Key Considerations for Success:**

1.  **Theme Props:** The centerpieces are crucial! Find them online (e.g., "vintage keyboard," "floppy disk prop"), borrow them from tech-savvy colleagues, or even 3D print simple ones. A few old computer mice or mice add instant vibe.
2.  **Themed Name Tags:** Include the theme name on name tags.
3.  **Background Music:** Play a curated playlist of 80s or 90s hits during lunch to really set the mood.
4.  **Flexibility:** Clearly state the meal options on the menu. Offer substitutions readily (e.g., "Tang or Fruit Punch?", "Vegetarian Lasagna available?").
5.  **Keep it Simple:** Focus on dishes that are easy to prepare and serve in large quantities without requiring complex assembly. Lasagna, tuna melts, and salads are perfect.
6.  **Dietary Needs:** Explicitly list vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options on the menu. Have clearly marked alternatives available.
