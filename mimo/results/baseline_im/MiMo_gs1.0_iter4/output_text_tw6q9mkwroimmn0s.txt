For your ocean-themed night, here are 5 meal ideas and 5 desserts:

### **Main Meals**  
1. **Grilled Shrimp Skewers with Mango Salsa**  
   *Toss large shrimp with olive oil, lemon, and spices, then grill. Serve with fresh mango, red onion, and cilantro.*  
2. **Coconut Curry Fish with Rice**  
   *Simmer firm white fish (like cod) in a fragrant coconut-curry broth, finished with lime and cilantro. Pair with jasmine rice.*  
3. **Seaweed Salad with Tuna & Avocado**  
   *Mix marinated nori seaweed, flaked tuna, diced avocado, and sesame oil for a quick, refreshing bowl.*  
4. **Clam Chowder Soup**  
   *Creamy New England-style chowder with fresh clams, potatoes, and bacon. Garnish with chives for an "ocean floor" touch.*  
5. **Spicy Miso Salmon with Seaweed Salad**  
   *Bake salmon glazed with miso-tamari sauce. Serve alongside nori strips and cucumber slices.*  

### **Desserts**  
1. **Mango Pudding with Edible Seaweed Garnish**  
   *Chill mango pudding, then top with thin nori strips and pomegranate seeds.*  
2. **Chocolate Ocean Wave Cake**  
   *Layer chocolate cake with vanilla cream, then frost with white chocolate "waves" and blueberry "seaweed."*  
3. **Shrimp & Mango Tartlets**  
   *Use shrimp tails as edible accents on mini mango-custard tarts with coconut crumble.*  
4. **Berry Parfait in a Clear Glass**  
   *Layer coconut yogurt, mixed berries, and granola. Top with edible blue flowers for "coral."*  
5. **Coconut Shave Ice with Tropical Fruit**  
   *Dollop of coconut ice, sliced mango, pineapple, and passionfruit drizzle for a cold "tidal" dessert.*  

### **Tips for Theme**  
- Use blue/green tablecloths, seashell centerpieces, or ocean prints.  
- Serve dishes in clear glass bowls to mimic underwater clarity.  
- Garnish with lemon/lime wheels or starfish-shaped crackers.  

Enjoy your delicious and thematic celebration! 🌊🍤
