Okay, Mexico is a fantastic choice for inspiration! The autumn colors in Mexico are just amazing, especially during the fall foliage season, which is roughly from October to November. That's when you'll see the leaves changing all over, especially in the highlands and around the mountains. 

But Mexico is also incredible year-round. The coast, like Cancun or Tulum, has those bright, sunny skies and turquoise water, which would be great for practicing your ocean scenes. And if you're more into the mountains, the autumn landscapes there are just breathtaking. 

So, if you're really set on autumn colors, definitely aim for October or November. If you just want general inspiration, you can't go wrong anytime! Mexico has so much to offer.
