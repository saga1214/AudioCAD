Okay, so you're about to film your first show for your potential YouTube channel. Here's a simple checklist to make sure you're all set.

First, before filming, you need to plan the video. Pick a few of your favorite, most energetic puppet routines—like Melvin's magic fails or Sloppy Snake's antics. Keep it short and fun. Maybe write down a few key jokes or moments to highlight.

Next, you'll need to set up your camera. Just use your phone, but make sure it's charged. Put it on a stable surface, like a table or stand, so it doesn't wobble. Test the lighting—maybe move it closer to a window or use a lamp so it's bright. And try to shoot in landscape mode, not vertical, so it looks better on YouTube.

Then, you'll want to get permission. If you're filming at the assisted living place, just ask them if it's okay to record and share it online. You don't want any issues later.

During the show, just perform like you normally would. The audience will be kids, so be extra engaging. Smile, use big gestures, and make them laugh. The phone will record you, so just have fun and be natural.

After the show, you can edit the video. You can trim the best parts, add some text or subtitles if you want, and cut out any awkward pauses. Then, upload it to YouTube. Add a fun title like "Melvin the Magnificent Fails at Magic!" and a short description. Use kid-friendly tags like "Puppet Show" or "Kids Entertainment."

That's pretty much it. Just film, have fun, and share it. You've been doing this for years, so you've got this.
