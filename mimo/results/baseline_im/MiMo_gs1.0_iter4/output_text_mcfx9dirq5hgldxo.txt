For your late-night layover in **Philadelphia**, here are **must-try local foods** you shouldn’t miss—quick, iconic, and delicious:

### 🥪 1. **Philly Cheesesteak**  
   - **What it is**: A checkered-checkered sandwich of grilled steak, melted cheese, and onions on a long roll.  
   - **Where to get it**:  
     - **Pat’s King of Steaks** or **Geno’s** (iconic spots, but expect crowds).  
     - **John’s Roast Pork** (often rated higher for quality).  
     - **Jim’s Steaks** (open 24/7, great for late-night).  
   - **Pro tip**: Add peppers ("wit’ peppers") and try the "Whiz wit’" (Cheez Whiz) for authenticity.

### 🥨 2. **Soft Pretzel**  
   - **What it is**: Warm, doughy pretzel with a crispy crust, served with mustard or cheese dip.  
   - **Where to get it**:  
     - **Pats Pretzels** (near Pat’s cheesesteak, open all night).  
     - **Reading Terminal Market** (grab one from a vendor).  
   - **Pair it with**: Beer cheese dip for dipping!

### 🥪 3. **Philly-Style Hoagie**  
   - **What it is**: A sub sandwich piled high with meats (like provolone, roast beef, or turkey) and veggies.  
   - **Where to get it**:  
     - **Dalessandro’s** (historic spot, open late).  
     - **Totonno’s** (for a more refined experience).  
   - **Order**: "On the side" (to avoid soggy bread).

### 🍟 4. **Fries with Cheese**  
   - **What it is**: Crispy fries topped with melted cheese—simple, satisfying, and cheap.  
   - **Where to get it**:  
     - **McFadden’s** (in the Reading Terminal Market).  
     - **Any cheesesteak joint** (ask for fries on the side).

### 🍟 5. **Philly Pretzel** (Snack Option)  
   - **What it is**: A giant, warm pretzel, often sold by the box.  
   - **Where to get it**:  
     - **Reading Terminal Market** (vendors like "Philly Pretzel Factory").  
     - **Dairy Queen** (yes, they sell giant pretzels too!).

### ⏱️ Late-Night Tips:
- **Skip the cheesesteak lines** if you’re short on time—opt for **hoagies or pretzels** instead.  
- **Reading Terminal Market** is open 24/7 and has food stalls for quick bites.  
- **Check hours**: Some spots close around midnight, so aim for **Jim’s** or **McFadden’s** for guaranteed late-night service.

Enjoy your Philly food adventure—these classics capture the city’s no-frills, hearty spirit! 🇵🇭✨
