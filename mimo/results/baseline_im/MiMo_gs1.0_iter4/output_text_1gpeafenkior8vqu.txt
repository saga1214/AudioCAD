Here's a balanced 9-day itinerary focusing on deep exploration of Tokyo, Osaka, Nara, and Kyoto, optimized for sightseeing and travel logistics:

### **Tokyo (5 Days)**
- **Day 1:** Arrival & Asakusa  
  - Morning: Check into hotel near Senso-ji Temple  
  - Afternoon: Explore Asakusa (Senso-ji, Nakamise Street)  
  - Evening: Sumida River cruise  
- **Day 2:** Modern Tokyo  
  - Morning: Shibuya (Shibuya Crossing, Hachiko Statue)  
  - Afternoon: Harajuku (Takeshita Street, Meiji Shrine)  
  - Evening: Shinjuku (Omoide Yokocho, Golden Gai)  
- **Day 3:** Culture & Views  
  - Morning: Ueno Park (museums, temples)  
  - Afternoon: Akihabara (anime/culture)  
  - Evening: Skytree & dinner  
- **Day 4:** Day Trip to Nikko  
  - Full-day trip to UNESCO sites (Toshogu Shrine, Kegon Falls)  
- **Day 5:** Art & Shopping  
  - Morning: Ghibli Museum (book tickets months ahead!)  
  - Afternoon: Omotesando (architecture) & shopping  
  - Evening: Depart for Osaka  

### **Osaka (3 Days)**
- **Day 6:** Arrival & Dotonbori  
  - Morning: Shinkansen to Osaka (1.5 hrs)  
  - Afternoon: Explore Dotonbori (street food, Glico sign)  
  - Evening: Kuromon Market  
- **Day 7:** Kyoto Day Trip  
  - Full-day train to Kyoto (30 mins)  
  - Visit Fushimi Inari Shrine, Kiyomizu-dera, Arashiyama Bamboo Grove  
  - Evening: Return to Osaka  
- **Day 8:** Osaka Highlights  
  - Morning: Osaka Castle  
  - Afternoon: Umeda Sky Building & shopping  
  - Evening: Namba Parks (shopping/dining)  

### **Kyoto (1 Day)**  
- **Day 9:** Kyoto Deep Dive  
  - Morning: Nara Park (deer) via train (45 mins from Kyoto)  
  - Afternoon: Gion district (tea ceremony) & Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)  
  - Evening: Return to Osaka  

---

### **Key Tips**
1. **Transport:**  
   - Get a **JR Pass** for Shinkansen and day trips (Nikko/Kyoto).  
   - Use IC cards (Suica/Pasmo) for local transit.  
2. **Accommodation:**  
   - Stay near stations (Tokyo: Shinjuku/Asakusa; Osaka: Namba/Umeda).  
3. **Pacing:**  
   - Start early (temples/cultures less crowded).  
   - Reserve popular spots (Ghibli, Fushimi Inari) in advance.  
4. **Food:**  
   - Osaka: Try takoyaki, kushikatsu, okonomiyaki.  
   - Kyoto: Soba noodles, kaiseki dinners.  

### **Adjustments**
- **More time?** Add 1 day to Tokyo (e.g., teamLab Borderless) or Kyoto (Philosopher’s Path).  
- **Less time?** Skip Nikko or combine Nara with Kyoto day.
