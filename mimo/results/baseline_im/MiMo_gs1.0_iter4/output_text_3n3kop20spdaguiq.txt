Dehydrating is a great way to preserve fruits and vegetables. Some of the best things to dehydrate are fruits like apples, bananas, and mangoes, which you can then turn into fruit leather or use in baking. You can also dehydrate vegetables like onions and carrots to use as a base for soups and stews. Herbs and spices are also great for dehydration, as they can be stored for long periods and used in cooking.
