Okay, kosher dietary laws require no cross-contamination. The main things to check are the ingredients and the prep. For ingredients, you'll need to avoid pork, shellfish, and meat with dairy. So, check the labels on the bacon for any dairy additives and maybe swap it for a different sausage. For the blood pudding, you'd want to make sure it's beef-based, not pork. The fruit skewers and hummus are usually fine. The most important thing is to have separate utensils and serving surfaces for any meat and dairy products. Just a little extra care with prep and labeling, and it should be fine.
