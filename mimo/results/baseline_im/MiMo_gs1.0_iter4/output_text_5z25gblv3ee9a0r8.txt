That's a really positive step forward for you, and it sounds like a great opportunity to practice what you've learned. 

My advice would be to go, but with a plan. First, remember that it's okay to feel nervous. The goal isn't to feel no anxiety, but to learn how to handle it.

You can use your box breathing technique in the car to manage any discomfort. Also, you can use the "5-4-3-2-1" grounding method. When you feel anxious, just look around and name five things you can see, four things you can feel, three things you can hear, two things you smell, and one thing you can taste. This pulls your focus away from the anxiety.

And most importantly, tell your friend up front that you might need a little space or support. You're doing a great job by facing this challenge, and you should be proud of yourself.
