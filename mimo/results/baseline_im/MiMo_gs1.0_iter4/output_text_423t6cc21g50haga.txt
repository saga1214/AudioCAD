### 🌍 **Destination Ideas**
1. **Barcelona, Spain**  
   - **Why?** Perfect for language practice (Spanish + Catalan), vibrant culture, and stunning architecture.  
   - **Stay:** *Hostal Muses* (affordable, central) or *Casa del Poeta* (artist-themed boutique).  
   - **Do:**  
     - Read at *Libreria Libreria* (bookstore with wine).  
     - Take a tapas tour with locals.  
     - Visit Sagrada Família and Park Güell.  
   - **Language Tip:** Join a *free language exchange* at *Casa de l’Lengua*.

2. **Kyoto, Japan**  
   - **Why?** Blend of ancient traditions, nature, and literary charm.  
   - **Stay:** *Ryokan Kiriuchi* (traditional inn) or *Kyoto Dog Café* (pet-friendly!).  
   - **Do:**  
     - Stroll through *Arashiyama Bamboo Forest*.  
     - Visit *Kyoto International Manga Museum*.  
     - Explore *Fushimi Inari Taisha* (thousands of red gates).  
   - **Transport:** Get a *JR Pass* for day trips to Nara or Osaka.

3. **Tuscany, Italy**  
   - **Why?** Rolling hills, Renaissance art, and Italian language immersion.  
   - **Stay:** *Agriturismo Poggi* (farm stay with cooking classes).  
   - **Do:**  
     - Wine tasting in Chianti.  
     - Read in *La Prado* bookstore in Siena.  
     - Bike through vineyards.  

### ✈️ **Getting There**
- **Flights:** Use Skyscanner or Google Flights for flexible dates.  
- **Trains:** Europe’s rail system (Eurail Pass) is efficient; Japan’s Shinkansen is fast.  
- **Budget Tip:** Book hostels early—use Hostelworld for deals.
