**For the Glue Gun:**

1.  **Power Check:** Is it plugged in securely? Try a different outlet if possible.
2.  **Battery Check (if cordless):** Is it fully charged? Plug it in for at least 30-60 minutes.
3.  **Heating Up:** It needs time! **Press the trigger and hold it down for at least 30-45 seconds** *before* trying to use it. It needs to get hot enough.
4.  **Glue Type:** Are you using the correct glue sticks? Some brands or types work better than others. Try a new stick.
5.  **Blockage:** Sometimes old glue residue can clog the tip. Try heating it up and carefully blowing or using a soft brush to clear any blockages.
6.  **Temperature:** If it's very hot outside, the glue might not set as well. If it's very cold inside, the glue gun might take longer to heat up.

**While it heats up (or if it's dead), here are some alternative ways to assemble:**

*   **Strong Double-Sided Tape:** Excellent for attaching things like masks, small flowers, or even securing the lace in place. Great for edges and corners.
*   **Hot Glue Alternative (if available):** A small amount of hot glue from a different glue gun (or even a craft glue gun) can hold things temporarily while you work on the others. Use sparingly.
*   **Staples (for heavier items):** If attaching a small, flat piece like a mask or a piece of lace, a quick staple hidden under the edge can work. You can often snip the ends of the staple to hide it.
*   **Embellish Directly:** If the glue gun is truly dead, focus on the other elements you love: the fairy lights inside, the lace draped around the base, the dark flowers, and the glitter accents. You can still create a stunning piece! Maybe add some faux spiderwebs around the base instead.
