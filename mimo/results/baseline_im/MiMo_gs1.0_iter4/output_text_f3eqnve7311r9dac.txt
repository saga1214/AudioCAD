You're right, older comics from the 1940s and 50s are much rarer because of lower print runs and the fact that many were thrown away. I can definitely give you a list of ten of those classic issues.

For example, *Captain America* number one, from 1941, is a huge deal because it's the first appearance of Captain America and the first comic ever to be reprinted in the newspaper strips. Then there's *The Amazing Spider-Man* number one, from 1962, which is the first full appearance of Spider-Man. You could also look at *Fantastic Four* number one, the first appearance of the Fantastic Four and Galactus. For a really rare one, there's *Tales to Astonish* number forty-five, which is the first appearance of the Hulk. Other great choices are *The Incredible Hulk* number one, *Thor* number one, *The Amazing Spider-Man* number fifty, which is the first appearance of the Green Goblin, *Fantastic Four* number forty-nine, which is the first appearance of Silver Surfer, and finally, *The Amazing Spider-Man* number seventy-five, which is the first appearance of Doctor Strange.

Just remember, for any of these, the price will be significantly higher than five hundred dollars. You'll need to buy them from a reputable dealer or auction house, and make sure they have a professional grading certificate, like a CGC one. The condition is everything.
