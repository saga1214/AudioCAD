That sounds like an amazing winter! Snowshoeing is a fantastic way to explore snowy landscapes—you’ll need a sturdy pair of snowshoes (rental or buy used first), waterproof boots, and a warm, layered outfit. Here are more winter activities to try:

### 🎿 1. **Winter Sports**  
   - **Skiing/Snowboarding**: Check local resorts or build a simple slope in your yard.  
   - **Ice Skating**: Public rinks or frozen ponds (ensure ice is safe).  
   - **Cross-Country Skiing**: Great for trails and low-impact exercise.  

### ⛄ 2. **Outdoor Adventures**  
   - **Snowshoe Hiking**: Explore forests, lakeshores, or urban parks.  
   - **Winter Photography**: Capture frost, animal tracks, or auroras (if near the poles).  
   - **Dog Sledding**: Many parks offer guided trips—perfect for your pups!  

### 🍁 3. **Cultural & Seasonal Experiences**  
   - **Visit a Sugar House**: Learn about maple syrup production (or try homemade treats!).  
   - **Winter Farmers' Markets**: Find hot cocoa, baked goods, and fresh produce.  
   - **Ice Fishing**: Cast lines on frozen lakes (rent gear if needed).  

### ❄️ **Tips for Safety & Comfort**  
   - **Layer Up**: Moisture-wicking base layers, insulating mid-layers, and a windproof outer shell.  
   - **Stay Dry**: Avoid wet snow contact—wind chill can be dangerous!  
   - **Plan Ahead**: Check trail conditions, ice safety, and weather forecasts.  

Winter offers incredible opportunities to stay active and cozy! Whether you’re building a sled ramp, snow-biking, or curling at a local club, there’s something for everyone. Enjoy every moment! ❄️✨
