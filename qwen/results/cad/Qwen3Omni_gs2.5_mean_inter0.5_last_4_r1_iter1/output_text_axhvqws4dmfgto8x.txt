To boil water safely without wooden spoons setting off alarms, here are a few simple tips:

First, remove the wooden spoon right away if you use it. Wood can char in boiling water and smoke.

Also, use a lid on the pot. It keeps the water boiling efficiently and traps less steam, reducing the chance of smoke alarm triggers.

And finally, don't walk away. Stir occasionally and keep an eye on it. You're not just cooking noodles—you're learning to be a home cook.
