Congratulations on your new life in BC — you're in for some incredible adventures!

Here are five amazing road trip options, each with a mix of nature, culture, and delicious food:

1. **Vancouver to Whistler (45 min drive)**  
   *Must-try:* Ride the Peak 2 Peak Gondola for jaw-dropping mountain views.  
   *Eat:* Try the famous poutine at *The Whistler Pub* or a fine dining meal at *The Bearfoot Bistro* (under the stars!).  
   *Bonus:* Visit the Whistler Olympic Park for a scenic hike.

2. **Vancouver to Tofino (4.5 hours)**  
   *Must-try:* Walk the beach at Long Beach and watch the Pacific waves.  
   *Eat:* Savor fresh oysters at *Tofino Oyster Bar* or a farm-to-table meal at *The Station*.  
   *Bonus:* Take a whale-watching tour or hike the Pacific Rim National Park trails.

3. **Vancouver to Victoria (1.5 hours)**  
   *Must-try:* Explore the Butchart Gardens and ride the ferry.  
   *Eat:* Have afternoon tea at *The Empress Hotel* or try local seafood at *The Fish Counter*.  
   *Bonus:* Visit the Royal BC Museum or take a scenic drive along the coast.

4. **Vancouver to Kelowna (2.5 hours)**  
   *Must-try:* Visit wineries like Mission Hill or Quails’ Gate.  
   *Eat:* Enjoy a wine-paired dinner at *The Farm at Southview*.  
   *Bonus:* Rent kayaks and paddle on Okanagan Lake.

5. **Vancouver to the Fraser Valley (1 hour)**  
   *Must-try:* Visit the Fraser Valley Farmers Market and explore the Chilliwack area.  
   *Eat:* Try local farm-fresh produce and artisanal cheeses.  
   *Bonus:* Take a scenic drive through the orchards and vineyards.

With your Golden Retriever and baby on the way, these trips offer a mix of adventure and relaxation. Pack a dog-friendly car seat, a stroller, and some snacks — you're going to love BC!
