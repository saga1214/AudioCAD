Great idea! Cooking together can actually be a nice bonding experience—even with differences like dietary restrictions. Since your sister is lactose intolerant and dislikes several food groups, let’s go for something simple, plant-based, and fast. Here’s a perfect suggestion:

### 🌱 **Avocado Toast with a Twist (Super Quick & Customizable!)**
**Why it works:**
- No meat, seafood, poultry needed
- Naturally dairy-free
- Super easy and fun to customize together

**Ingredients (for 2 people):**
- 1 avocado
- 2 slices of bread (toasted)
- Salt, pepper, and chili flakes (optional)
- Lemon juice (to prevent browning)
- Optional toppings: cherry tomatoes, cucumber, sprouts, or hummus

**Steps:**
1. Toast the bread.
2. Mash the avocado with a fork, add lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
3. Spread it on the toast.
4. Add her favorite toppings (you can let her choose hers!).

**Bonus:** You can even make it fun by setting up a “build-your-own toast bar” with different toppings—she picks what she likes, you pick what you like. It gives her control (which she might appreciate) and keeps things light and collaborative.

It’s only 10–15 minutes, and you’ll both be eating something delicious without stepping on each other’s food toes. Plus, cooking side by side often opens the door to easier conversation than sitting at a table might.
