Oh, totally! For something super fast, grab a can of beans (like black or chickpeas), some frozen veggies, and a bag of pre-washed greens. Toss everything in a bowl with a simple dressing or sauce—maybe just olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and salt. Or go even simpler: open a can of tuna, mix with a little mayo or Greek yogurt, and serve it on whole wheat bread or over rice. You could also try a microwaveable grain like quinoa or couscous with a pre-made sauce or jarred pesto. The key is using things that need little prep and cook quickly.
