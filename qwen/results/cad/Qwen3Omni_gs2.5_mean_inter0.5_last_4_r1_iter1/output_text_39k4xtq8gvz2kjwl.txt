4. **Professor Javier Rios** – Leo’s compatriot and expert local guide from South America. He knows the Eldorian jungle intimately and helps the team navigate its treacherous terrain.

6. **Dean Albright** – The head of Kingsbridge University. He supports Leo Vance’s expedition and provides the academic and financial backing for the journey.

7. **Captain Elias Vance** – Leo Vance’s ancestor and the first outsider to discover the Sunstone of El Dorado in 1855. He hid the treasure and created the ancient puzzles to protect it from the Croft family.

8. **Silas Croft** – The main villain and descendant of Augustus Croft. He is greedy and determined to steal the Sunstone for himself. He leads the expedition into Eldoria with a team of henchmen.

9. **Marcus Kane** – One of Silas Croft’s henchmen. He is cunning and manipulative, often trying to outwit the heroes through deception and traps.

10. **Brutus Thorne** – Silas Croft’s brute-force henchman. He is physically imposing and uses strength to intimidate and attack the explorer’s team.

11. **Rex Gunner** – A cold-blooded tracker and hunter who uses stealth and precision to follow the explorers through the jungle.

12. **Ivan Petrov** – The final henchman, a deadly sniper who uses long-range attacks to threaten the heroes from a distance.

Together, these characters form a rich cast of heroes and villains in a tale of adventure, legacy, and the enduring battle between greed and honor.
