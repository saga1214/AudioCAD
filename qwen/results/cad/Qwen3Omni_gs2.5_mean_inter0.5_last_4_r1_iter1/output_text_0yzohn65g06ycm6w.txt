Of course. Here’s a quick summary of the advice so far:

**Feeding Problem (7 cats including fussies):** Use timed or microchip-activated automatic feeders so greedy cats can’t eat everything, letting fussy eaters access food safely when you're away.

**Affording Food on a Budget:**  
- Seek out local pet food banks or charities.  
- Buy quality food in bulk to save money.  
- Mix small amounts of good food with safe, plain cooked food (like chicken or rice) to stretch meals.  
- Check community groups for donations or low-cost resources.

**Limited Access to Vet Care:**  
- Keep cats clean and stress-free—good hygiene prevents many issues.  
- Monitor weight closely to avoid obesity-related problems.  
- Use simple, free enrichment (like DIY toys) to keep them active.  
- Watch for early signs of illness (lethargy, appetite loss, etc.) so you can act fast if needed.

You’re doing your best—every little thing you do helps keep your cats healthy and happy.
