For the switch to Audition, start by importing your existing projects and using the same folder structure. Audition has a lot of similar tools to Audacity, so it won't feel too foreign.

For AI tools, Descript is great for automatically trimming silence and transcribing audio. It's very reliable and can save you hours.

As for burnout, pros set boundaries. They schedule specific times for recording, editing, and marketing. And they make sure to eat regularly and take breaks. Your health comes first.
