Absolutely! Costa Rica would indeed make a dream scuba diving trip for you and your husband. While it's not as famous as Belize or Honduras for diving, it has some truly special and unique underwater experiences—especially on the Pacific side. Here’s a plan to help make it unforgettable:

---

### 🌴 **Why Costa Rica?**
- **Biodiversity**: Costa Rica has rich marine life, including sea turtles, rays, reef fish, and even occasional whale sharks.
- **Volcanic reefs**: The Pacific coast features dramatic underwater landscapes shaped by volcanic activity.
- **Less crowded**: Compared to other Caribbean destinations, Costa Rican dive sites are often quieter and more pristine.

---

### 🐠 **Best Dive Destinations in Costa Rica**

#### 1. **Cocos Island (Isla del Coco) – The Ultimate Adventure**
- **Why go?** A UNESCO World Heritage Site, this remote island is one of the best pelagic dive sites in the world.
- **What you’ll see**: Giant manta rays, hammerhead sharks, whale sharks, and schools of tuna.
- **Note**: Requires liveaboard diving (multi-day trips), and it’s only accessible during the dry season (Dec–Apr). It’s challenging and for experienced divers only.

#### 2. **Golfo Dulce (Dulce Gulf) – For Reef and Manta Rays**
- **Why go?** One of the few places in the world where you can dive with **giant manta rays** year-round.
- **What you’ll see**: Mantas, reef sharks, sea turtles, and vibrant coral reefs.
- **Best time**: Dry season (Dec–Apr), though diving is possible year-round.

#### 3. **Osa Peninsula (e.g., Playa Naranjo, Uvita) – For Reef and Wall Dives**
- **Why go?** Part of the Osa Biosphere Reserve, this area has healthy coral reefs and dramatic drop-offs.
- **What you’ll see**: Reef fish, octopus, moray eels, and occasional whale sharks.
- **Best time**: Dry season (Dec–Apr) for best visibility.

---

### 🧳 **Trip Planning Tips**

#### ✅ **When to Go**
- **Best time**: **December to April** (dry season) for the best visibility and calm seas.
- **Shoulder season (May–Nov)**: More rain, but fewer crowds and lower prices. Visibility can be lower, but diving is still possible.

#### ✅ **Dive Operators**
- Look for eco-friendly, certified operators like:
  - **Cocos Island Liveaboards** (for Cocos trips)
  - **Dive Osa** or **Dive Uvita** (for Osa Peninsula)
  - **Manta Ray Diving Costa Rica** (specializing in manta dives in Golfo Dulce)

#### ✅ **Dive Gear**
- Since you mentioned debating fins, and you're experienced enough to have long fins, you might consider bringing your own gear.
- But if you're renting, most dive shops in Costa Rica offer quality equipment.

#### ✅ **Travel Tips**
- **Visa**: UK and US citizens don’t need a visa for stays under 90 days.
- **Flights**: Fly into San José (SJO), then take a domestic flight or drive to the Osa Peninsula or Puerto Jiménez (gateway to Cocos).
- **Accommodation**: Eco-lodges near the dive sites offer a great experience.

---

### 🌟 **Make It Unforgettable**
- **Book a liveaboard** to Cocos Island if you're experienced and adventurous.
- **Include a manta ray dive** in Golfo Dulce—many divers say it’s a once-in-a-lifetime experience.
- **Combine with land adventures**: Visit Corcovado National Park, go on a night hike, or relax in a hot spring.

---

### 💡 Final Thought
Costa Rica offers a mix of adventure, pristine nature, and incredible diving. Whether you're chasing manta rays, exploring volcanic reefs, or diving with whale sharks, it’s sure to be a trip you’ll never forget.
