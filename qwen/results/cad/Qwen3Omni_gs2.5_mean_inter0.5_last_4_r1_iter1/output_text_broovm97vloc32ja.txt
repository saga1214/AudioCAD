That's a really common question, and the answer is yes, you absolutely can submit a complete, illustrated children's book manuscript to a publisher.

Many publishers, especially those that specialize in picture books, welcome fully illustrated manuscripts. They often appreciate the vision and effort you've put into it. However, there are a few things to keep in mind.

First, you should always check the publisher's submission guidelines. Some explicitly state that they only accept text-only manuscripts and will hire their own illustrator. Others are open to receiving a complete package.

Second, if you're submitting to a traditional publisher, they will still need to review your work and decide if they want to move forward. If they do, they will likely want to work with you to refine the text and illustrations before publication.

So, while it's not guaranteed that they'll accept a fully illustrated manuscript, it's definitely worth a try, especially if you've put a lot of thought and effort into your illustrations.
