Absolutely Will! Here’s a list of 12 fantastic movies—many from the 90s, but also a few timeless picks—that your group will love for a themed night:

1. **The Matrix (1999)** – A sci-fi classic with groundbreaking action and philosophy.
2. **The Shawshank Redemption (1994)** – A powerful story of hope and friendship.
3. **Pulp Fiction (1994)** – Quentin Tarantino’s iconic nonlinear crime masterpiece.
4. **Goodfellas (1990)** – A gripping look at the mob life with incredible energy.
5. **The Lion King (1994)** – A beloved animated classic with unforgettable music.
6. **Fight Club (1999)** – A cult favorite with a twist and deep themes.
7. **The Big Lebowski (1998)** – A quirky, laid-back stoner comedy that’s become a cult hit.
8. **Clueless (1995)** – A fun, stylish teen comedy with a 90s flair.
9. **Independence Day (1996)** – A big-budget, fun sci-fi action film with epic effects.
10. **The Usual Suspects (1995)** – A twisty crime thriller with one of the best endings ever.
11. **The Princess Bride (1987)** – A timeless fantasy adventure with humor and heart.
12. **The Rock (1996)** – A high-octane action film with Sean Connery and Nicolas Cage.

These mix action, comedy, drama, and sci-fi—perfect for a fun and varied movie night!
