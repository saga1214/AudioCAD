That’s such a generous and unexpected gift from your sister’s boyfriend! It’s wonderful that your family gets to experience a vacation together, and it’s a big responsibility to pick the destination. Since it’s up to you, think about what kind of trip would be meaningful and enjoyable for everyone.

If you want something relaxing, maybe a beach destination like Hawaii, Florida, or even a coastal town in California could be great. For something more cultural and fun, cities like New Orleans, Nashville, or even a road trip through the Southwest might be exciting. If you’re looking for adventure, places like Colorado or the Great Smoky Mountains offer great outdoor activities.

Also, consider the ages and interests of everyone in the family. Is there anyone with special needs or preferences? Making sure everyone feels included will help make the trip memorable for all.

Whatever you choose, it’s a chance to create some amazing memories together.
