For Asian noodle spots in Dallas, here are some top picks:

- **Noodle House** (in the Bishop Arts District) – Great ramen and udon, especially their spicy pork ramen.
- **Sushi Tora** – They have killer ramen bowls and fresh, bold flavors.
- **Korean BBQ Noodle House** – If you're craving something spicy and hearty, their spicy beef noodle soup is a favorite.
- **Pho 79** – For Vietnamese pho, it's a local favorite with rich broth and tender meats.

As for protein bars, here are some solid Dallas-friendly options:

- **RXBARs** – Clean ingredients, no added sugar. Great for voice actors who want to stay hydrated and energized.
- **Quest Nutrition Bars** – High protein, low sugar, and they come in flavors like chocolate peanut butter and birthday cake.
- **Kind Protein Bars** – Good mix of protein and fiber, and they’re easy to find at Whole Foods or local health stores.
