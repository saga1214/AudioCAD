No worries—cheese isn’t for everyone! Freeze-dried chicken or turkey liver treats are usually a hit with Maltese dogs. They’re super aromatic and tasty. You can also try small pieces of cooked, unseasoned turkey or beef. Just make sure it’s plain and safe for dogs. If she’s not into treats, try using a favorite toy or her name in a happy tone as a reward. Sometimes dogs just prefer play over food. Keep it fun and low-pressure!
