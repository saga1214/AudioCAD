The single most important factor you must consider first and foremost is **following the guidelines**.

Here’s why: even if you have the most engaging, concise, and consistent content with perfect descriptions, if your videos violate TikTok’s policies, they can be removed, restricted, or shadowbanned. That means your content won’t be seen by anyone—even your biggest fans—and you’ll get no traction.
