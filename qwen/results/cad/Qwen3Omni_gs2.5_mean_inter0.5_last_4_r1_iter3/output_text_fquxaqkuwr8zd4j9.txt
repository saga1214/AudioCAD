### ✅ **Family Gathering: Roast Chicken + Roast Potatoes + Roast Carrots + 1 More Vegetable Side**

You want a **third vegetable side dish** that is:
- Kid-friendly (under age four present)
- Picky-family approved (mom dislikes rice, you dislike textured grains like quinoa, lentils, etc.)
- Easy to roast or prepare alongside chicken

**Recommended: Roasted Green Beans**

- **Why it works**: Green beans are mild, colorful, and kids usually like them when lightly cooked and not overdone. Mom likely won’t object.
- **Prep**: Toss trimmed green beans with olive oil, salt, pepper, and maybe a tiny bit of garlic powder. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 15–20 minutes until tender-crisp.
- **Bonus**: You can serve them warm or at room temperature — no extra heat needed.

**Alternative**: Sautéed corn with a touch of butter and herbs (if mom likes butter). Or try **roasted zucchini** — it’s sweet and soft when cooked right.

✅ **Final Side Trio**:
1. Roast Potatoes  
2. Roast Carrots  
3. Roasted Green Beans  

All roasted together on sheet pans — minimal effort, maximum flavor.

### ✅ **Girlfriends’ Gathering: Baked Potato Bar with Meat & Veggie Toppings**

You asked for **meat toppings** (not just vegetarian). Here are **two meat options** and **two vegetarian**:

#### 🥩 Meat Toppings:
1. **BBQ Pulled Pork** – Slow-cooked, shredded, with tangy barbecue sauce. Kids and picky eaters usually like it.
2. **Crispy Bacon Bits** – Cook bacon until crisp, crumble, and sprinkle on top. Adds smoky flavor and crunch.

#### 🌱 Vegetarian Toppings:
1. **BBQ Jackfruit** – As mentioned, it mimics pulled pork texture. Use canned young jackfruit in brine, sauté with BBQ sauce.
2. **Black Bean & Corn Salsa** – Mix black beans, corn, diced red onion, lime juice, cilantro, cumin. Bright and fresh.

**Other ideas**:
- Grilled mushrooms (great umami flavor)
- Sautéed onions and peppers
- Guacamole or pico de gallo

Serve with sour cream (or dairy-free alternative) and shredded cheese (or vegan cheese) on the side.

✅ **Summary**:
- Meat: Pulled pork, bacon bits
- Veggie: Jackfruit, black bean salsa
- All other toppings (salsas, onions, etc.) can be shared
