Since vegetarianism is part of the equation (and let’s be real—she raised you to appreciate plant-based food!), I’d suggest something delicious that even non-vegetarians would love. Here are a few ideas:

1. **Eggplant Parmesan** – Layers of roasted eggplant, marinara, and melty cheese (or vegan cheese if she’s vegan). It’s hearty and satisfying.
2. **Stuffed Bell Peppers** – Fill them with quinoa, black beans, corn, spices, and maybe some feta or nutritional yeast. Bake until golden.
3. **Butternut Squash & Sage Risotto** – Creamy, comforting, and full of flavor. You can make it vegetarian-friendly with vegetable broth and Parmesan (or vegan alternative).
4. **Mushroom & Spinach Lasagna** – Use a mix of mushrooms, spinach, ricotta (or tofu ricotta), and marinara. Layer it up and bake.
5. **Veggie Stir-Fry with Tofu or Tempeh** – Serve over rice or noodles with a flavorful sauce. Add whatever veggies she likes.

And hey, if she *does* avoid cheese, you can always go fully plant-based—there are so many great dairy-free cheeses and meat substitutes these days!

Bonus: Add a fun dessert like a dark chocolate avocado mousse or a fruit tart. She’ll appreciate the thoughtfulness.

Let me know if she’s vegan too—then we can go full plant-powered!
