If I had to pick just one, I’d say it’s the widespread availability and affordability of ultra-processed, calorie-dense foods. They’re engineered to be hyper-palatable, easy to overconsume, and often cheaper than whole foods. This shift in our food environment has made it much harder to maintain a healthy weight, even for people who are active or trying to eat well. It’s not just about willpower—it’s about the world we live in.
