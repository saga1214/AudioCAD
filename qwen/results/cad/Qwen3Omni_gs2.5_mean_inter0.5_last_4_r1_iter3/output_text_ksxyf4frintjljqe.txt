Absolutely! A **chocolate pecan pie** is a delicious twist on the classic that everyone tends to love. It's rich, chocolatey, and has a nice crunch from the pecans. You can make it in a pie pan and serve it warm with a little whipped cream or vanilla ice cream.

**Simple Chocolate Pecan Pie Recipe:**

**Ingredients:**
- 1 (9-inch) unbaked pie crust
- 1 cup chopped pecans
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup cocoa powder (unsweetened)
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup melted butter
- 1/2 cup corn syrup
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. In a bowl, mix sugar, cocoa powder, and salt.
3. Stir in melted butter, corn syrup, milk, eggs, and vanilla.
4. Fold in pecans.
5. Pour into pie crust.
6. Bake 45–50 minutes until center is set.
7. Cool completely before serving.
