You're right to be mindful of weight. The good news is that both succulents and the bird's nest snake plant are very lightweight when potted.

Succulents are typically in small pots—maybe 4 to 6 inches across—and the pots themselves are often plastic, which keeps the weight down. A single succulent in a small pot might weigh just 1 to 2 pounds, so you could easily carry a few on your table.

The bird's nest snake plant is a bit larger, but it's still very light. It's usually sold in a 6 to 8-inch pot, and even with soil, it probably won't weigh more than 3 to 4 pounds. Since it's low and compact, you could carry it flat on the floor or even roll it up the stairs if needed.

For your one-month Airbnb stay, you could even consider buying smaller, temporary pots or using lightweight fabric grow bags to keep things even lighter. And yes, you can definitely take them home at the end of your stay—just make sure to water them a bit before you leave New York so they're ready for the trip.
