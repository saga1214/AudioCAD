Great question! Here are five green vegetables that are safe and nutritious for your parrot:

1. **Kale** – Packed with vitamins A, C, and K, and calcium. Chop it finely to make it easier to eat.
2. **Spinach** – Rich in iron and antioxidants, but feed it in moderation due to oxalates that can interfere with calcium absorption.
3. **Broccoli** – High in vitamin C and fiber. Offer it raw or lightly steamed, but avoid the stems and leaves in large amounts.
4. **Green Beans** – Low in fat and high in fiber and vitamins. They’re crunchy and loved by many birds.
5. **Zucchini** – Mild in flavor and full of water and nutrients. Great raw or lightly cooked.

💡 Tip: Always wash veggies thoroughly and offer them fresh daily. Rotate greens to keep your parrot interested and well-nourished.
