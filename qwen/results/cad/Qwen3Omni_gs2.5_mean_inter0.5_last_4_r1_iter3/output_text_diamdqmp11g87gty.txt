Snowshoeing is a great way to enjoy the winter! For gear, you’ll need snowshoes that fit your boots, and trekking poles can help with balance. As for other fun outdoor activities, ice fishing like you mentioned is a classic. You could also try cross-country skiing, which is a great workout and lets you explore snowy trails. Building a snow fort or having a snowball fight is always fun too. And if you're into photography, winter light can create some really beautiful scenes. Just make sure to dress in layers and stay warm!
