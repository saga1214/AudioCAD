For five-week-old chicks, use large-flake pine shavings or a high-quality paper-based bedding. Both are safe, absorbent, and won’t cause respiratory issues.

Avoid small-flake shavings or straw, as they can be easily ingested and cause impaction. Also, keep the bedding clean and dry—chicks are sensitive to dampness, which can lead to respiratory problems or chilling.

Since they’re already five weeks old, you can start transitioning them to the same bedding you use in the main coop. This will help them adjust when you introduce them later.
