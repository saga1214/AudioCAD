That’s a thoughtful question. Since restoration and tailoring are both central to you, consider gifts that support those hobbies. For fountain pen restoration, a vintage pen kit with tools or a good cleaning kit would be perfect. For tailoring, a nice pair of shears or a beginner’s tailoring kit with patterns and measuring tools could be great. You could also ask for a sewing machine if you’re serious about it. Or, if you want something more personal, a nice leather-bound journal to document your projects would be meaningful too.
