### 🍚 Asian-Inspired 3-Course Meal Plan (Legume-Free, Seafood-Free)

#### **Starter: Cucumber Sesame Salad**
- Crisp, refreshing cucumber salad with a tangy sesame dressing.
- Light and pairs well with heartier mains.

#### **Main Course: Chicken Teriyaki with Jasmine Rice & Stir-Fry**
- Tender chicken thighs or breasts marinated in a classic teriyaki sauce (soy-free option available if needed).
- Steamed jasmine rice.
- Stir-fried mixed vegetables (bell peppers, broccoli, carrots, snap peas).

#### **Dessert: Mango Sticky Rice OR Matcha Coconut Ice Cream**
- Classic Thai mango sticky rice (coconut-infused sweet rice with fresh mango).
- OR easy homemade matcha coconut ice cream (no machine needed).

---

### 🛒 Shopping List

#### 🥒 **Produce**
- 1 large ripe mango (for dessert)
- 1 English cucumber (for salad)
- 1 bell pepper (any color)
- 1 small head broccoli (or 1 cup florets)
- 1 medium carrot
- 1 cup snap peas or snow peas
- 1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
- 1 small piece fresh ginger (1–2 inches)
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 lime (for dressing or garnish)

#### 🐔 **Proteins**
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts (cut into bite-sized pieces)

#### 🍚 **Grains & Dairy**
- 1 cup sticky rice (also called glutinous rice) – *essential for mango sticky rice*
- OR 1 cup jasmine rice (for main course)
- 1 can coconut milk (full-fat, for dessert)
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or whipping cream (for matcha ice cream option)
- 1–2 tbsp sweetened condensed milk (for dessert)

#### 🧂 **Condiments & Seasonings**
- Soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free) – *ensure no hidden legumes*
- Mirin (sweet rice wine) – *key for teriyaki flavor*
- Rice vinegar – *for salad dressing*
- Sesame oil – *for stir-fry and salad*
- Toasted sesame seeds – *for garnish*
- Matcha green tea powder – *for ice cream or tea*
- Granulated sugar or brown sugar – *for teriyaki and desserts*
- Salt and pepper

#### 🧄 **Optional but helpful**
- Garlic press or microplane (for ginger)
- Small bowl for marinating chicken

---

### ✅ Tips:
- **Legume-free check**: Always double-check labels on soy sauce/tamari and teriyaki sauce — some contain soybeans (legumes). Use **tamari** or **coconut aminos** as safe alternatives.
- **Chicken teriyaki marinade**: Mix 1/4 cup soy sauce/tamari, 2 tbsp mirin, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 minced garlic clove, and 1 tsp sesame oil. Marinate chicken 30+ minutes.
- **Stir-fry**: Cook chicken first, remove, stir-fry veggies, then return chicken. Add sauce at the end.
- **Dessert prep**: Mango sticky rice needs to be made ahead (soak rice 4–6 hours). Matcha ice cream can be made same day.
