### 🌞 **Pre-Broadcast Mental & Physical Prep Routine (For a Sunny Game Day)**

**⏰ 2–3 Hours Before Broadcast**

1. **Hydration First**  
   - Drink water consistently. Since dairy isn’t an option, opt for:
     - Coconut water (natural electrolytes)
     - Herbal teas (like green tea or chamomile)
     - Plain water with a slice of lemon or cucumber

2. **Light, Non-Dairy Fuel**  
   Snack ideas that won’t upset your stomach:
   - Banana + almond butter
   - Rice cakes with avocado
   - Trail mix (nuts, seeds, dried fruit)
   - Smoothie with oats, spinach, berries, and almond milk

3. **Warm-Up with 90s Hip-Hop Instrumentals**  
   Continue using what works for you!  
   - Put on your favorite beats and walk or stretch to the rhythm.  
   - Use this time to mentally rehearse your opening lines or key moments.

4. **Vocal Warm-Up (5–10 mins)**  
   As discussed:
   - Lip trills (5 mins)
   - Gentle humming in descending pitch
   - Tongue twisters:  
     *"She sells seashells by the seashore"*  
     *"How much wood could a woodchuck chuck?"*

---

**⏰ 30–60 Minutes Before On-Air**

1. **Mental Focus Ritual**  
   - Listen to a **short guided visualization** (apps like Calm or Insight Timer have sports-specific ones).  
   - Or, replay your focus routine: put on those instrumentals, close your eyes, and say to yourself:  
     *"I am prepared. I am calm. I am sharp."*

2. **Grounding Exercise (If Nervous)**  
   - Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, exhale for 6. Repeat 3–5 times.  
   - Ground yourself: Name 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

3. **Final Check**  
   - Mic check  
   - Notes review  
   - Confirm your gear (headset, backup mic, water)

---

**☀️ Outfit Suggestions for a Sunny Game Day**

Since it’s sunny, you want to look professional but stay cool and comfortable:

- **Top:** Light-colored, breathable shirt or polo (white, light blue, or gray).  
  → *Avoid black or dark colors—they absorb heat.*  
  → *Choose moisture-wicking fabric if possible.*

- **Bottom:** Chinos or tailored pants in light colors.  
  → *Avoid jeans—they can get hot and less professional.*

- **Footwear:** Comfortable dress shoes or stylish sneakers (if allowed).  
  → *You’ll be on your feet a lot—prioritize comfort.*

- **Accessories:**  
  - **Sunglasses** (polarized, professional style)  
  - **Lightweight blazer or vest** (optional, for cooler mornings)  
  - **Hat?** Only if it’s part of your brand—some commentators wear caps, others avoid them for clarity.

- **Bonus Tip:** Apply sunscreen **before** you go on air—especially on your neck and ears. You don’t want to be squinting or adjusting your gear mid-broadcast!
