Oh, spring is such a magical time! Since painting by the pond near your house is already one of your favorite things, maybe you could build on that and try plein air painting more often as the weather warms up. Here are a few fun spring activities you might enjoy:

- **Take a long walk or hike** through blooming gardens or trails—everything looks so fresh and vibrant after winter.
- **Start a spring garden**—even if it’s just a few pots on a balcony or windowsill, planting tulips or herbs can be so rewarding.
- **Have a picnic** by your thawing pond when it’s warm enough—bring a blanket and some of that hot chocolate you love!
- **Join a local art or photography group**—many people head outdoors in spring to capture the colors and light.
- **Try kayaking or paddleboarding** if your frozen pond thaws into open water—spring is a great time for gentle water activities.
