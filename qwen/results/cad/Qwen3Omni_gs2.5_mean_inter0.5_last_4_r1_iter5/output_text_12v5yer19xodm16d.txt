Absolutely! Here’s a list of five energizing drinks and five snacks to help you power through that sleepy-but-stuck feeling:

**Drinks:**
1. **Coffee** – Classic wake-up call. Black or with a splash of milk, it’s packed with caffeine to boost alertness.
2. **Green Tea** – Contains caffeine plus L-theanine, which gives a calm, focused energy without the jitters.
3. **Energy Drinks** – Like Red Bull or Monster. Quick caffeine and sugar hit, but use sparingly.
4. **Water with Lemon** – Dehydration can cause fatigue. Hydration helps your brain function better.
5. **Electrolyte Drink** – Like Gatorade or a homemade mix. Replenishes minerals and helps with energy.

**Snacks:**
1. **Nuts (Almonds, Walnuts, etc.)** – Healthy fats and protein keep energy steady.
2. **Banana** – Natural sugars and potassium help with energy and prevent fatigue.
3. **Dark Chocolate (70% or higher)** – Caffeine and antioxidants give a mental boost.
4. **Greek Yogurt** – Protein and probiotics help sustain energy and support focus.
5. **Hard-Boiled Eggs** – High in protein and B vitamins, great for long-lasting energy.

Mix a drink and snack for best results—like coffee with a banana or green tea with nuts. Stay hydrated and avoid too much sugar to keep the energy stable!
