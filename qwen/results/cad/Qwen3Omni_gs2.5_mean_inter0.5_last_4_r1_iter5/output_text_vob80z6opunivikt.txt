That sounds like a fun setup! With a vending machine full of options, you’ve got a lot of creative possibilities. Here are some snack ideas to fill your new coffee table drawer:

### 🍫 Sweet but not chocolatey:
If you said you don’t like sweet or chocolatey snacks, maybe you meant overly sugary or milk chocolate? You could still enjoy:
- **Fruit chews or gummy candies without chocolate coating**
- **Hard candies or sour belts**
- **Candy-coated almonds (if you like a sweet crunch)**

### 🍬 Savory & fun:
Try these for a change of pace:
- **Pretzels** (classic and satisfying)
- **Nuts and trail mix** (especially ones with dried fruit and no chocolate)
- **Cheese crackers or cheese balls**
- **Rice cakes or popcorn** (plain or lightly salted)
- **Dried fruit** (apricots, mango, raisins)

### 🍫 Chocolate lovers (just in case you’re flexible):
If you’re open to *some* chocolate, maybe try:
- **Dark chocolate squares** (less sweet, more intense flavor)
- **Chocolate-covered espresso beans** (for a caffeine kick)
- **Mini chocolate bars** (like Snickers Minis or Twix Minis)

### 🎯 Themed snack ideas:
- **Movie night drawer**: Popcorn, candy corn, and mini pretzels
- **Work project fuel**: Trail mix, protein bars, and fruit leather
- **"IKEA project reward" drawer**: A mix of fun, small treats to celebrate progress

And hey—maybe keep one section for *new* snacks you discover at the vending machine. That way, your coffee table stays exciting and full of surprises!
