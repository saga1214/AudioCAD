### 🛒 **New Year’s Eve Party Shopping List**

#### 🍽️ **Food & Appetizers**
- Mini slider buns (about 20–24)
- Ground beef or pork for sliders (1–1.5 lbs)
- Shrimp (1 lb, cooked or raw for cocktail)
- Cocktail sauce (or make your own with mayo, ketchup, lemon juice, horseradish)
- Hot dogs or cocktail sausages ("pigs in a blanket")
- Crescent roll dough or pre-made pigs in a blanket (enough for 10–12 kids)
- Honey ham (approx. 5–6 lbs for 24 people)
- Scalloped potatoes ingredients:
  - Potatoes (about 2–3 lbs)
  - Milk or half-and-half
  - Butter
  - Onion powder, garlic powder, salt, pepper
  - Cheese (cheddar or mozzarella)
- Asparagus (1–2 bunches)
- Olive oil
- Lemon (1–2)
- Salt & pepper
- Carrots (1–2 lbs, unglazed, for roasting)
- Garlic (1 head)
- Fresh herbs (parsley or thyme, optional)

#### 🥤 **Drinks**
- Champagne (1–2 bottles)
- Signature cocktail ingredients (e.g., cranberry juice, vodka, orange juice – customize your own!)
- Sparkling water or soda (for mixing or non-alcoholic drinks)
- Grape juice (approx. 2–3 liters or 6–8 bottles)
- Plastic cups/flutes (for champagne and grape juice)
- Ice (large bag or several bags)

#### 🎉 **Party Supplies & Decor**
- Balloons (red, silver, gold, or festive colors)
- Noisemakers or party hats (especially for kids)
- Music playlist (bring your phone or portable speaker)
- Tablecloths or festive paper plates/cups (optional, if you don’t want cleanup)
- Napkins (festive or neutral)
- Serving trays and utensils (for appetizers)
- Serving spoons and tongs
- Trash bags and recycling bins (for easy cleanup)

#### 🧼 **Cleanup**
- Paper towels
- Dish soap and sponges
- Gloves (optional, for cleanup)
