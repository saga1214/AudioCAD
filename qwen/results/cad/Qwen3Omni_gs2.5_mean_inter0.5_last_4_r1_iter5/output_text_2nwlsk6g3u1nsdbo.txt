Sure! Here are the two shrimp dishes I mentioned earlier, along with ingredient lists:

1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
   *Ingredients:*  
   - 1 lb (450g) raw shrimp, peeled and deveined  
   - 12 oz (340g) linguine or spaghetti  
   - 4 cloves garlic, minced  
   - 4 tbsp olive oil  
   - 2 tbsp butter  
   - 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)  
   - Salt and black pepper, to taste  
   - Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)  
   - Lemon wedges (optional, for serving)

2. **Cajun Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
   *Ingredients:*  
   - 1 lb (450g) raw shrimp, peeled and deveined  
   - 12 oz (340g) penne or rigatoni pasta  
   - 2 tbsp olive oil  
   - 1 small onion, finely chopped  
   - 3 cloves garlic, minced  
   - 1 tsp Cajun seasoning (or more to taste)  
   - 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional, for extra heat)  
   - 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes  
   - 2 cups chicken or vegetable broth  
   - Salt and black pepper, to taste  
   - Fresh basil or parsley, chopped (for garnish)

Both are hearty, comforting, and pack a flavorful spicy punch your friend’s family will love. The scampi is quicker and great if they prefer a lighter meal, while the Fra Diavolo is richer and more saucy—perfect for a cozy night in. Either way, serving it warm shows such kindness.
