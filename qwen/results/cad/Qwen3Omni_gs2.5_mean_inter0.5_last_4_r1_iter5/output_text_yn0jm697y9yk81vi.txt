Absolutely. Here's a concise list of my top tool and material recommendations for cutting metal and finishing drywall, along with where to buy them in Indianapolis:

1. **Aviation Snips** – Ideal for thinner sheet metal and curved cuts.  
   *Best for:* Manual, low-power cutting with good control.  
   **Where to buy:** The Home Depot (Broad Ripple location) – known for the best hardware selection in Indianapolis.

2. **Angle Grinder with Cut-Off Wheel** – Powerful and precise for thicker or straight cuts in sheet metal.  
   *Best for:* Thicker gauge metal or repetitive straight cuts.  
   **Where to buy:** Lowe's (Southport location) – carries a wide range of power tools and accessories.

3. **Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws (1-1/4 inch)** – Superior holding power and less likely to pop than nails.  
   *Best for:* Securing drywall to wood studs.  
   **Where to buy:** Menards (Carmel location) – excellent selection of drywall supplies and fastener options.

4. **Fiberglass Batt Insulation** – Cost-effective, easy to install, and widely available.  
   *Best for:* Standard wall cavities in residential construction.  
   **Where to buy:** Ace Hardware (Downtown Indianapolis) – great for smaller projects and expert advice.

**Top Pick for Overall Selection:**  
**The Home Depot (Broad Ripple)** – I’d say it has the most comprehensive selection of tools, materials, and expert staff in the area, making it the best one-stop shop for your project.
