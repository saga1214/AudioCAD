Absolutely. Since face-to-face meetings make you anxious, here are two different approaches you can take to bring up the topic in a way that might feel more comfortable for you:

**Idea 'thank you for the opportunity to speak with you. I've really been enjoying my work here and appreciate the positive feedback on my assignments. I was wondering if we could schedule a time to discuss my role and career progression in the coming months?'**

This is a softer, more open-ended way to start the conversation. It gives you a chance to bring up the topic without being too direct, and it allows your boss to set the tone for the meeting.

**Idea 2: Email Request**

**Send a brief, professional email ahead of time.**

You could write something like:

> 'Hi [Boss's Name], I hope you're doing well. I've been making good progress in my role and receiving positive feedback, which has been great for my confidence. I was wondering if we could schedule a brief meeting sometime in the next couple of weeks to discuss my performance and career development. I'd appreciate the chance to talk with you.'**

This gives you a chance to prepare your thoughts and can feel less overwhelming than a surprise verbal request in a meeting. It also puts the ball in their court to set a time, which can help you feel more in control.
