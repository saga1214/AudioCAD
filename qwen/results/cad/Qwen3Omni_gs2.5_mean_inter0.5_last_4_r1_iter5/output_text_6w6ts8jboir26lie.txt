Since you're on vacation by the beach, why not embrace the coastal vibe?

Here are a few simple, fresh ideas:

1. **Grilled Fish Tacos**: Grab some fresh fish fillets from the grocery store. Season with salt, pepper, and a little lime juice. Grill quickly and serve in warm corn tortillas with shredded cabbage, a squeeze of lime, and a simple avocado crema.

2. **Seafood Pasta**: Cook some pasta and toss it with olive oil, garlic, cherry tomatoes, and some shrimp or scallops. Finish with fresh herbs like basil or parsley.

3. **Beachside Grill**: If the condo has a working grill, try some simple skewers—chicken, bell peppers, onions, and pineapple. It's easy and tastes amazing.

4. **Fresh Salad**: Use local greens, cucumbers, tomatoes, and a simple vinaigrette. Add some grilled shrimp or avocado for extra flavor.

Cooking for yourself is a wonderful way to reclaim this vacation. Enjoy the process and the fresh air.
