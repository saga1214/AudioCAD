Absolutely! Here's a **comprehensive, grab-and-go-friendly shopping list** tailored to ten dads, ten kids, a theme park day, healthy focus, minimal mess, and your friend’s love of calzones and celebratory treats.

---

### 🧊 COOLERS: Packing Tips
- Use **insulated coolers** (2–3 large ones depending on size).
- Pack **ice packs or frozen gel packs** (more reliable than ice cubes).
- Use **reusable snack bags, deli containers, and leak-proof bottles**.
- Label coolers: “Drinks,” “Snacks,” “Main Food,” “Dessert.”

---

### 🥤 DRINKS (for 20 people)
- **Water bottles** – 1 per person (20 total), consider reusable bottles with lids.
- **Low-sugar juice boxes** – 1 per child (10 total), e.g., apple or orange.
- **Sugar-free sodas or sparkling water** – 1 per adult (10 total), e.g., Coke Zero, Sprite Zero, LaCroix.
- Optional: **Infused water** – Pre-bottle with lemon, lime, or cucumber slices (great for adults too).

---

### 🥜 SNACKS (mess-free, grab-and-go)
- Cheese sticks or mozzarella balls – 1 per person (20 total)
- Baby carrots or cherry tomatoes – 2–3 bags (wash & pre-pack in containers)
- Apple slices (toss in lemon juice to prevent browning) – 2–3 apples, pre-sliced
- Grapes – 2–3 bags (pre-washed)
- Trail mix (low-sugar, nut-free if needed for kids) – 5–6 individual bags
- Pretzels (individual bags) – 10–12 bags
- Whole grain crackers or rice cakes – 2–3 boxes, pre-divided
- Greek yogurt cups (individual, leak-proof lids) – 10 total (5–6 for dads, 5–6 for kids)

---

### 🍕 MAIN FOOD (calzone-focused, easy to pack)
**Option 1: Pre-packaged mini calzones (recommended)**
- Buy **10–12 mini frozen calzones** from a pizzeria or grocery store (e.g., frozen pizza chains like DiGiorno or local pizzeria).
- Reheat just before leaving home (or keep frozen in cooler with ice packs).
- Pack in **insulated containers** or **foil trays** with lids.

**Option 2: Homemade calzones (if you have time)**
- Make mini calzones at home, freeze, then reheat before leaving.
- Wrap each in foil and place in cooler with ice packs.

**Bonus: Sandwich alternatives (for variety)**
- Pre-made wraps (chicken Caesar, veggie, or turkey & cheese) – 10 wraps
- Individual deli sandwiches (ham & cheese, veggie) – 10
- Hummus & veggie cups (single-serve) – 10

---

### 🍰 DESSERT (easy, no mess)
- **Mini cupcakes** – 10–12 (vanilla, chocolate, or his favorite flavor)
- **Brownies** – 1 pan, cut into 10–12 squares, packed in container
- **Fruit cups** – 10 individual cups (no added sugar)
- Optional: **Sugar-free pudding cups** – 10 (great for kids and adults)

---

### 🧼 ADDITIONAL ITEMS TO BRING
- **Wet wipes** – lots! For hands, faces, and cleanup.
- **Trash bags** – one per cooler or shared.
- **Reusable snack bags** – for leftovers or extra snacks.
- **Small cutting board & knife** – if prepping fresh fruit at home.
- **Paper plates & napkins** – in case someone wants to sit down briefly.

---

### 💡 PRO TIPS
- **Prep everything at home** before leaving.
- **Label coolers clearly** so no one grabs the dessert cooler by mistake!
- **Assign "snack captains"** – have 2–3 dads take charge of refilling coolers or grabbing food.
- **Pack a "first aid" kit** – kids get scraped knees, and sunscreen is a must!
