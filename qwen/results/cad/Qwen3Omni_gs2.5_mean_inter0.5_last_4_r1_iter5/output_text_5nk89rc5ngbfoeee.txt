For your Brownies' end-of-year celebration, I’d recommend something simple but memorable—especially with seven months pregnant! A cafeteria party with a fun theme (like “Chips and Dip Day,” since you loved that growing up!) could be perfect. You could do a chips and dip station without dairy (maybe offer dairy-free options), and add some fun games or crafts.

Alternatively, if weather and energy allow, a small park BBQ or picnic is lovely. Keep it casual—maybe even just a potluck where parents bring a dish. That way, you’re not doing all the work, and the girls can enjoy a change of scenery.

If you want to wrap up with a little ceremony, you could do a short “Brownie Awards” moment—give out fun certificates or small tokens for participation or favorite activity. It makes them feel celebrated without being too formal.

Bottom line: Keep it light, fun, and low-pressure. Your troop will remember the good time, not the logistics!
