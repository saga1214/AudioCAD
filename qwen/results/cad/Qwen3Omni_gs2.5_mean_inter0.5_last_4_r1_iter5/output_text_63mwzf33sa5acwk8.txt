Oh, Vegas is perfect for a couple looking for adventure and fun! Since you two met at a Gam anon meeting this year (so new relationship vibes!), a Vegas trip could be a mix of unique experiences and some classic couple fun. Here’s how to have a *proper* Vegas experience without going overboard:

### 🌟 Things to Do:
1. **See a Show** – Vegas has world-class entertainment. Think Cirque du Soleil, magic shows (like Penn & Teller), or even a comedy show. Pick something that matches your interests.
2. **Dine at a Unique Restaurant** – Skip the buffets (unless you're into that). Try something cool like:
   - **Eureka!** in Downtown for a fun, quirky vibe.
   - **The Buffet at Wynn** if you want to go all out.
   - Or a rooftop bar like **Skyfall at the Cosmopolitan** for views and cocktails.
3. **Explore Downtown** – Walk around Fremont Street for the old-school Vegas feel, neon lights, and the free light show at night.
4. **Visit the Neon Museum** – It’s a must for Gam anon fans or anyone into quirky culture. You can take a guided night tour of vintage signs.
5. **Try Something Offbeat** – Vegas has weird and wonderful things like:
   - **The Underground** – a secret speakeasy-style bar.
   - **The Neon Boneyard** – where old signs are displayed like art.
   - **The Mob Museum** – a cool, interactive museum about organized crime history.

### 🏨 Hotel Tips:
- **Luxury Option**: The Bellagio or Aria – beautiful views, great dining.
- **Quirky/Unique**: The Cosmopolitan or Park MGM – fun, modern, great for couples.
- **Budget-Friendly**: The Flamingo or Excalibur – still fun and close to the Strip.

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Free things to do**: Walk the Strip, see the fountains at Bellagio (they’re free!), Fremont Street light show.
- **Stay a night or two** – don’t rush it. Vegas is best enjoyed slowly.
- **Van parking**: Most Strip hotels have valet or parking garages. Some charge a fee (around $20–$30), but it’s worth it for comfort.

You can do a desert van adventure one night, then treat yourselves to a hotel and a Vegas night out. It’s the perfect balance of adventure and romance!
