That sounds like a fantastic plan—getting out there and seeing real autumn scenes, ocean views, or rivers in person will give you so much inspiration! As for when to go to Mexico, it really depends on what kind of scenery you're after:

- **Autumn vibes**: Mexico doesn’t have traditional Northern Hemisphere autumn, but places like **Oaxaca**, **Chiapas**, and **Puebla** have beautiful fall foliage in their highlands and forests. The best time for cooler weather and vibrant colors is **October to December**.
- **Ocean scenes**: Mexico’s coastlines are stunning year-round, but **December to April** is ideal—dry season, warm but not too hot, and great light for painting.
- **Rivers and nature**: The **Río Balsas** or **Río Grijalva** regions are beautiful, and **October to March** gives you the best weather.

So if you're looking for a mix of autumn-like colors and ocean views, **late October to early December** would be perfect. You’ll get great light, fewer crowds, and plenty of natural inspiration. Pack your watercolors and go for it!
