Sure! Here are the Marvel characters we've discussed, rated on a **1 to 100 scale** based on their **power level**:

1. **Thanos (with Infinity Gauntlet)** – **100**  
   As the wielder of all six Infinity Stones, he can erase half of all life in the universe with a snap. Reality manipulation, time control, and near-omnipotence make him the peak of power in the Marvel Universe.

2. **Captain Marvel (Carol Danvers)** – **90**  
   With energy absorption, flight at lightspeed, and the ability to survive in space, she's one of the most powerful Avengers. Her power level is comparable to cosmic beings like Galactus.

3. **Dormammu** – **95**  
   A powerful mystical entity from the Dark Dimension, Dormammu is nearly immortal and can manipulate reality, time, and space. He's a major threat even to the likes of Doctor Strange and the Avengers.

4. **Thor Odinson** – **85**  
   As the God of Thunder, Thor can control lightning, fly, and wield Mjölnir (and later Stormbreaker). His power is immense, especially when wielding the full might of his hammer.

5. **Iron Man (Tony Stark)** – **75**  
   While Tony himself has no powers, his suits give him flight, energy projection, and near-invincibility. The Mark LXXXV suit in *Endgame* was capable of fighting Thanos one-on-one.

6. **Captain America (Steve Rogers)** – **60**  
   As a super-soldier, Steve has enhanced strength, speed, and endurance. He's incredibly powerful for a non-powered human, but still far below cosmic-level beings.

7. **Hela** – **80**  
   As the Goddess of Death, Hela can summon the dead, control death energy, and wield immense physical strength. She was able to overpower Thor in *Thor: Ragnarok*.

8. **Baron Zemo** – **10**  
   No superpowers at all. His power comes from intellect, manipulation, and strategic brilliance. He's dangerous, but not physically powerful.
