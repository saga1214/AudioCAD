Since you follow a gluten-free diet, here are a few more great gluten-free snack ideas beyond Skinny Pop and Popchips:

1. **Trail Mix**: Make your own with nuts (like almonds, cashews), seeds (pumpkin or sunflower), dried fruit (raisins, cranberries), and maybe some gluten-free chocolate chips. It's lightweight, nutritious, and energy-dense.

2. **Gluten-Free Energy Bars**: Brands like RXBAR, KIND Gluten-Free, or Larabar offer a variety of options. They’re easy to pack and provide sustained energy for both camping and cycling.

3. **Fresh Fruit**: Apples, bananas, or grapes are easy to pack and naturally gluten-free. They’re also refreshing after a long bike ride.

4. **Rice Cakes with Nut Butter**: Choose plain or lightly salted rice cakes and pack single-serve packets of almond or sunflower seed butter for a crunchy, satisfying snack.

5. **Jerky**: Look for gluten-free beef, turkey, or plant-based jerky. It’s high in protein and perfect for refueling on the go.

These snacks are all easy to carry, don’t require refrigeration, and are great for fueling your body during outdoor adventures.
