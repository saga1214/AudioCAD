Sure! Since you're lacto-vegetarian, artificial crab meat (usually made from soy or wheat protein) is a great plant-based option. Here’s a simple way to make it:

**Ingredients:**
- Bread (whole wheat, sourdough, or your favorite)
- Artificial crab meat (thawed if frozen)
- Mayonnaise (or vegan mayo)
- Diced celery
- Diced onion (optional)
- Lemon juice (a squeeze)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Lettuce or spinach (optional)
- Pickles or relish (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. In a bowl, mix the artificial crab meat with mayonnaise, celery, onion, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
2. Toast your bread if you like.
3. Layer lettuce on one slice, then add the crab mixture.
4. Top with the other slice of bread.
5. Cut in half and enjoy!

It’s a tasty, protein-rich vegetarian sandwich.
