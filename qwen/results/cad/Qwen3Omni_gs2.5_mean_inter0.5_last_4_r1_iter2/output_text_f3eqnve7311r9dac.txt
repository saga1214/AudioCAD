You're absolutely right—comics from the Golden and Silver Ages (1940s–1950s) are generally much rarer and more valuable than modern reprints or reboots.

Here are five key comic books from that era that are both rare and historically significant:

1. **Action Comics #1 (1938)** – Superman's first appearance. The most valuable comic book in history. Even low-grade copies sell for hundreds of thousands, and pristine ones go for millions.

2. **Detective Comics #27 (1939)** – Batman's debut issue. Also extremely rare and valuable. A high-grade copy recently sold for over two point five million.

3. **Marvel Comics #1 (1939)** – The first comic from what would become Marvel. Features the Human Torch, the Sub-Mariner, and the original Namor. A pristine copy recently sold for over one point five million.

4. **All Select Comics #1 (1943)** – Features the first appearance of the original Human Torch (android) and the original Sub-Mariner. It's a key title for collectors of early Marvel characters.

5. **Captain America Comics #1 (1941)** – The first appearance of Captain America. A high-grade copy recently sold for over one point two million.

These are all extremely rare, and even in lower grades, they're worth thousands. But for investment purposes, you'd want to focus on professionally graded copies.
