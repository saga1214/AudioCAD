**What is vasculitis?**  
Vasculitis is a group of disorders characterized by inflammation of the blood vessels. This inflammation can narrow or block blood flow, which may damage tissues and organs. It's often autoimmune in nature—your immune system mistakenly attacks your own blood vessels.

**Common symptoms of vasculitis include:**
- Fever
- Fatigue
- Weight loss
- Muscle and joint pain
- Skin rashes (like purpura, which are small purple or red spots)
- Sores or ulcers on the skin
- Numbness or tingling in hands or feet (if nerves are affected)
- Organ-specific symptoms depending on which vessels are involved (e.g., coughing or shortness if lungs are affected)

**Other potential causes of chronic facial redness or erythema (besides vasculitis):**
1. **Rosacea** – Very common, especially on the face. Causes flushing, persistent redness, visible blood vessels, and sometimes bumps or acne-like breakouts.
2. **Sun damage (chronic photodamage)** – Years of UV exposure thin the skin and dilate blood vessels, leading to long-term redness.
3. **Lupus (especially discoid or systemic lupus erythematosus)** – Can cause a butterfly-shaped rash and chronic redness, often with flares.
4. **Contact dermatitis or allergic reactions** – Long-term irritation from skincare products or environmental triggers.
5. **Genetic predisposition** – Some people naturally have more visible blood vessels or sensitive skin.

**Important note:** Chronic redness over lifetimes could also be due to a combination of factors—like aging, sun exposure, and underlying conditions. A dermatologist or rheumatologist can help determine the exact cause through exams, blood tests, and sometimes a skin biopsy.

And yes, conditions affecting blood vessels or systemic inflammation *can* impact hair and nail health—since both rely on good blood flow and nutrient delivery. That’s why it’s so important to get to the root cause.
