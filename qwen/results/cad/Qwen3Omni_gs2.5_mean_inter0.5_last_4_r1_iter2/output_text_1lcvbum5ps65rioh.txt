Now, for spaghetti carbonara, here are the ingredients you’ll need:

- Spaghetti (about 400g for 4 servings)  
- Eggs (2–3, depending on how rich you want it)  
- Parmesan cheese (about 1/2 cup, grated)  
- Guanciale or pancetta (150–200g, diced) – bacon can be a substitute  
- Garlic (1 clove, minced or crushed)  
- Freshly ground black pepper  
- Salt (for pasta water)  

Optional: a little pasta water to help emulsify the sauce.

That’s it! You’ll cook the pasta, fry the guanciale, mix everything together off the heat to avoid scrambling the eggs, and finish with cheese and pepper. Enjoy your meal!
