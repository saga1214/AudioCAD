That sounds like a wonderful family getaway!

Since Northern California can get chilly now and you're hoping for warm weather where you can swim, I'd recommend heading **in-state to Southern California**. Here are two great options for your trip:

---

### 🌴 Option 1: **Santa Barbara**
A beautiful coastal city with warm weather, beaches, and a relaxed vibe.

**Things to do:**
- Relax on **State Street Beach** or **Stearns Wharf**.
- Visit the **Santa Barbara Mission** and explore the charming downtown.
- Rent bikes and ride along the **Beachfront Bike Path**.
- Take a scenic drive up to **Solvang** (Danish village) or **Los Olivos** for wine tasting.

**Where to stay:**
- **Hotel Californian** – central, stylish, and beachfront.
- **Santa Barbara Inn** – cozy and affordable.

**Where to eat:**
- **The Lark** – farm-to-table with gluten-free options.
- **Bistro Donce** – great for seafood and gluten-free dishes.

---

### 🌴 Option 2: **Pismo Beach or San Luis Obispo**
More laid-back and perfect for families who love nature and the coast.

**Things to do:**
- Walk along the **Pismo Beach Pier** and enjoy the ocean views.
- Visit the **Pismo State Beach** for swimming and sunbathing.
- Explore the **San Luis Obispo Farmers Market** (if it's on during your visit).
- Take a scenic drive along the **California Coast Highway (Highway 1)**.

**Where to stay:**
- **Pismo Beach Motel** – family-friendly and right on the beach.
- **Hotel San Luis Obispo** – charming and centrally located.

**Where to eat:**
- **The Farmhouse** – local, fresh, and has gluten-free options.
- **Cafe 101** – great breakfast and lunch spot.

---

### 🚗 Travel Tips:
- Rent a car – definitely the best option for a family of three.
- Pack layers – Southern California can be warm during the day but cooler in the evenings.
- Bring swimsuits, sunscreen, and beach towels!

This trip will give you all the warm weather you're craving, plus fun activities for your daughter and a relaxing time for everyone.
