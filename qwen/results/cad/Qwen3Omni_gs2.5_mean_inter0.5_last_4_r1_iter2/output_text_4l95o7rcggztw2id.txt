---

### 🏴‍☠️ **Pirate Birthday Party Shopping List** 🏴‍☠️  
*(Serves 7 total: my son + 6 other kids)*

#### 🥪 **Main Snacks & Savories**
- **Mini slider sandwiches or roll-ups** (6–7 total)  
  → Use deli meat, cheese, and lettuce rolled in tortillas or cut into bite-sized pieces.  
  → *Theme idea:* Label as “Buried Treasure Bites” or “Pirate Provisions.”  
  → Pack in leak-proof containers or foil trays.

- **Cheese cubes or string cheese** (1 pack)  
  → Easy to handle, no mess.  
  → *Theme idea:* “Pirate Gold Cheese” or “Treasure Cubes.”

- **Grapes** (1 large bag)  
  → Safe alternative to watermelon.  
  → *Theme idea:* “Pirate Jewels” or “Sea Gems.”

- **Goldfish crackers** (1 large bag)  
  → Classic beach snack, fun shape.  
  → *Theme idea:* “Pirate Gold” or “Treasure Crisps.”

- **Veggie sticks** (carrots, cucumbers, bell peppers)  
  → Pre-cut and pack in small containers.  
  → *Theme idea:* “Treasure Garden Snacks.”

- **Popcorn** (1 large bag, pre-popped or microwaveable)  
  → Pack in small snack bags or pirate-themed treat boxes.  
  → *Theme idea:* “Pirate Puff” or “Treasure Puffs.”

#### 🧃 **Drinks**
- **Individual juice boxes** (7 total)  
  → Choose pirate-themed flavors like tropical punch or berry.  
  → *Theme idea:* “Pirate Punch” or “Sea Water (Non-Salty!)”  
  → Keep in a cooler.

- **Small water bottles** (7 total)  
  → Essential for beach safety.  
  → *Theme idea:* “Fresh Water Rations” or “Captain’s Water.”

- **Optional:** Pirate-themed drink labels (printable) to attach to bottles/juice boxes for fun.

#### 🎯 **Bonus Pirate-Themed Touches (Optional but Fun)**
- **Mini pirate treasure chest snack boxes** (buy or make)  
  → Fill with Goldfish, popcorn, or fruit. Kids can “dig” for snacks!

- **Pirate eye patches** (for fun)  
  → Kids can wear them while eating or playing.

- **Sand-free party supplies**  
  → Plastic forks/spoons (no sharp edges), napkins, and trash bags.

---

### ✅ **Sand-Safe Tips Recap**
- Everything is pre-portioned or sealed.
- No bowls, plates, or messy dips.
- All snacks are handheld and won’t crumble easily.
- Drinks are in individual, spill-resistant containers.

This menu keeps the pirate theme alive while being beach-friendly and allergy-conscious. Your son and his crew will love it! 🏴‍☠️⚓
