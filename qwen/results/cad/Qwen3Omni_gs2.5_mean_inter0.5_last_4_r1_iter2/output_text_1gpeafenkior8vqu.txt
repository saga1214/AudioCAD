Since you're already in Tokyo and heading to Osaka, and you want to deeply explore both cities plus visit Kyoto and Nara, here’s a well-balanced 10-day itinerary that gives you time to experience the highlights without rushing too much. This plan assumes you're traveling during the day and making the most of each city’s unique character.

---

### 🗓️ **10-Day Japan Itinerary: Tokyo → Nara → Kyoto → Osaka**

#### **Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo**
- Arrive at Narita or Haneda Airport.
- Check into hotel.
- Light walk around your neighborhood (e.g., Shinjuku, Shibuya, or Asakusa).
- Dinner in a local izakaya or ramen shop.

#### **Day 2: Tokyo – Traditional Side**
- Morning: Visit **Senso-ji Temple** in Asakusa.
- Lunch: Try local street food in Nakamise-dori.
- Afternoon: Explore **Ueno Park** and **Ueno Zoo** or **Tokyo National Museum**.
- Evening: Walk around **Shibuya** and see the famous Shibuya Crossing.

#### **Day 3: Tokyo – Modern & Cultural**
- Morning: Visit **Meiji Shrine** in Harajuku.
- Lunch: Try crepes or matcha desserts in Harajuku.
- Afternoon: Explore **Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden** or **Omoide Yokocho** (Memory Lane).
- Evening: Experience **Shinjuku** nightlife or visit **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** for free city views.

#### **Day 4: Tokyo – Day Trip to Nikko**
- Take a train to **Nikko** (about 2 hours).
- Visit **Toshogu Shrine** (UNESCO site), Kegon Falls, and Rinno-ji Temple.
- Return to Tokyo in the evening.

#### **Day 5: Travel to Kyoto**
- Morning: Take the **Shinkansen (bullet train)** from Tokyo to Kyoto (~2.5 hours).
- Check into hotel.
- Afternoon: Visit **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (famous for thousands of red torii gates).
- Evening: Explore **Gion District** (geisha area) and have dinner.

#### **Day 6: Kyoto – Cultural Highlights**
- Morning: Visit **Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)**.
- Lunch: Try kaiseki (traditional multi-course meal).
- Afternoon: Explore **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove**, **Togetsukyo Bridge**, and **Tenryu-ji Temple**.
- Evening: Walk around **Pontocho Alley** for dinner.

#### **Day 7: Kyoto – Temples & Gardens**
- Morning: Visit **Nijo Castle** and **Nishiki Market**.
- Lunch: Sample Kyoto street food.
- Afternoon: Explore **Ryoan-ji Zen Garden** or **Philosopher’s Path**.
- Evening: Optional: Attend a tea ceremony or cultural performance.

#### **Day 8: Travel to Nara**
- Take a train to **Nara** (~45 minutes from Kyoto).
- Visit **Nara Park** and see the famous **Great Buddha (Daibutsu)** at Todai-ji Temple.
- Walk around the park and feed the friendly deer.
- Return to Kyoto or stay overnight in Nara.

#### **Day 9: Travel to Osaka**
- Take a train to **Osaka** (~30–45 minutes).
- Check into hotel.
- Afternoon: Visit **Osaka Castle** and park.
- Evening: Explore **Dotonbori** for food and lights.

#### **Day 10: Osaka – Modern & Fun**
- Morning: Visit **Universal Studios Japan** (if interested) or **Osaka Aquarium**.
- Lunch: Try takoyaki or okonomiyaki in Dotonbori.
- Afternoon: Explore **Shinsaibashi Shopping Street** or **Umeda Sky Building**.
- Evening: Enjoy a farewell dinner in Osaka.

---

### 🚆 **Travel Tips**
- **Japan Rail Pass**: If you're doing a lot of Shinkansen travel, consider buying a **JR Pass** (valid for 7, 14, or 21 days). It covers the bullet train from Tokyo to Kyoto/Osaka and local trains in cities.
- **IC Cards**: Get a **Suica or Pasmo card** for easy access to trains and buses.
- **Language**: Download a translation app like Google Translate (offline mode) to help with communication.
