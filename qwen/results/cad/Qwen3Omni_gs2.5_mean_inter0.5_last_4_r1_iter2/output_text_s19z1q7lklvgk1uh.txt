After all this thoughtful discussion, I still stand by my original answer — the **heat treatment** is the single most important step.

Here's why: everything else is preparatory. You can choose the best steel, design the perfect balance, forge or machine the shape flawlessly — but if the heat treatment fails, the sword will be brittle and prone to cracking, or too soft to hold an edge. It’s the moment where the blade truly becomes functional.

Even in CNC blades, if the heat treat is off, the sword won’t perform. And even with hand-forged blades, masters spend years mastering this step.

So while design, material, and forging are all essential, heat treatment is the step that determines whether your sword lives or dies in use.
