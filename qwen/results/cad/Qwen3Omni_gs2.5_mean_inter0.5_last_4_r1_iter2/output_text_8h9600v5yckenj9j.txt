That’s a brilliant multi-use idea—using frozen drinks as both a cooler and fog-chilling agent! Smart and practical. For candy, since some guests could be allergic to artificial dyes (as you mentioned), here are a few easy and safe options:

1. **Dye-Free Candy**: Look for brands like YumEarth, SmartSweets, or Trader Joe’s “All Natural” Halloween candy—they’re free of artificial colors and dyes, and often allergen-friendly.

2. **Non-Candy Treats**: Offer fun alternatives like:
   - Mini chocolate bars (check labels for allergens)
   - Fruit snacks (like Welch’s Fruit Roll-Ups or Annie’s Organic Fruit Snacks)
   - Popcorn (plain or lightly salted)
   - Pretzels or trail mix (label clearly if nuts are in it)

3. **Candy Buffet**: Set up a small table with a mix of candy and non-candy items. Use small bowls or bags so guests can grab what they want. You can even label allergen-free options with little signs.

4. **DIY Treat Bags**: If you have time tomorrow, just grab a few bags and fill them with a mix of safe candy and non-candy snacks. It’s a nice touch and helps people avoid allergens.

And don’t forget—keep a few dye-free and nut-free options clearly labeled so guests with allergies feel comfortable grabbing something. You’re already doing so much to make it inclusive, and your guests will appreciate it!
