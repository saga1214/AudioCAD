### 🍸 Old Fashioned  
*Classic, bourbon-forward, perfect for Kentucky and fall.*

**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon (Kentucky bourbon is ideal!)  
- 1 sugar cube (or 1/2 oz simple syrup)  
- 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Orange twist or maraschindo cherry  
- Ice (large cube or rocks)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Place sugar cube in a rocks glass.  
2. Add bitters.  
3. Muddle gently until sugar dissolves.  
4. Add ice.  
5. Pour bourbon over ice.  
6. Stir gently for 20–30 seconds.  
7. Garnish with orange twist and cherry.

### ✈ Paper Plane  
*Balanced, bitter-sweet-sour—modern Art Deco meets aviation flair.*

**Ingredients:**  
- 3/4 oz bourbon  
- 3/4 oz Aperol  
- 3/4 oz Amaro Nonino  
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice  
- Ice  
- Orange twist (optional garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds.  
3. Strain into a coupe glass.  
4. Garnish with orange twist (optional).

### 🥃 Whiskey Sour  
*Fall favorite—choose either classic or frothy version.*

#### **Classic Version (No Egg White):**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 1/2 oz simple syrup  
- Ice  
- Orange slice or cherry  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds.  
3. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.  
4. Garnish with orange or cherry.  

#### **Frothy Version (With Egg White):**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 1/2 oz simple syrup  
- 1 egg white (or pasteurized egg white for safety)  
- Ice  
- Orange twist or cherry  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add bourbon, lemon juice, syrup, and egg white to a shaker.  
2. **Dry shake** (no ice) for 15–20 seconds to emulsify the egg white.  
3. Add ice and shake again for 10–15 seconds.  
4. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.  
5. Garnish with orange twist or cherry.  

> ✅ **Note:** If you're concerned about raw egg, use pasteurized egg white or omit it entirely.

### ✈ Aviation Cocktail  
*The “aviation” theme shines here—elegant and citrusy.*

**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz gin  
- 1/2 oz maraschino liqueur  
- 1/4 oz crème de violette  
- 1/2 oz fresh lemon juice  
- Ice  
- Maraschino cherry (garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds.  
3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
4. Garnish with a cherry.

### 🍸 French 75 (Gin Option)  
*Elegant, bubbly, and fitting for Art Deco vibes.*

**Ingredients:**  
- 1 oz gin  
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 1/2 oz simple syrup  
- Ice  
- Prosecco or champagne  
- Lemon twist or cherry (optional garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake for 10–15 seconds.  
3. Strain into a champagne flute.  
4. Top with chilled Prosecco or champagne.  
5. Garnish with lemon twist or cherry.
