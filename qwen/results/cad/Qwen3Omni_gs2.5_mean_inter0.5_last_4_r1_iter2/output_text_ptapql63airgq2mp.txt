---

### 🌏 **Japanese & Global Umami-Forward Snacks (Foodie-Worthy!)**

#### **Simple but Elevated (Effortless to Serve)**
- **Popcorn Bar with Global Twists**: Offer plain kettle corn, salted sesame, and *furikake*-seasoned popcorn (a Japanese rice seasoning with seaweed, sesame, bonito flakes).
- **Mini Bento Boxes**: Pre-assembled with rice balls (*onigiri*), pickled vegetables, and a small piece of grilled fish or tamagoyaki (sweet rolled omelet). Easy to grab and eat during the movie.
- **Steamed Edamame**: Lightly salted or with a chili-lime dusting—classic, simple, and always a hit.

---

#### **Unique & Adventurous (Foodie Paradise)**
Now for the bold flavors your Japan-loving friend would appreciate:

1. **Kimchi & Seaweed Snack Station**  
   - **Kimchi Chips**: Store-bought or homemade baked kimchi chips (crispy, spicy, sour).  
   - **Roasted Seaweed Snacks**: Offer different varieties—*nori* with sesame, wasabi, or even *yuzu* citrus.  
   - **Kimchi-Stuffed Rice Crackers**: Mini rice crackers with a dab of kimchi inside—crunchy and flavorful.

2. **Dried Seafood Delights**  
   - **Dried Squid (Ika Yaki)**: Chewy, smoky, and packed with umami. Serve in small bowls with toothpicks.  
   - **Dried Octopus (Takotsubo)**: A bit more adventurous, but foodies will love the texture and depth.  
   - **Smoked Bonito Flakes (Katsuobushi)**: Sprinkle over rice crackers or serve in a small bowl with chopsticks for a traditional Japanese touch.

3. **Japanese Sweet & Sour Umami Bites**  
   - **Tamarind & Chili Pouches**: Look for Japanese-style tamarind candies or *amazake* (sweet rice drink) with a tangy twist.  
   - **Umami Candy**: Try *umami-flavored gummies* or *soy sauce-flavored chocolate*—yes, it exists and foodies will be intrigued.  
   - **Yuzu & Matcha Chocolates**: Small bite-sized chocolates with citrusy and earthy notes.

4. **Interactive Snack Bar: “Build Your Own Snack Plate”**  
   Set up a station where guests can mix and match:
   - Rice crackers
   - Nori sheets
   - Kimchi
   - Dried squid
   - Wasabi peas
   - Pickled ginger
   - Soy-glazed nuts
   - Mochi (sweet or savory)

---

### 🍵 **Bonus: Drinks to Match the Theme**
- **Matcha Latte Station**: Offer matcha powder, milk, and honey for guests to mix their own.  
- **Yuzu Sparkling Water**: Refreshing and citrusy.  
- **Sake & Umami Pairing**: A few bottles of light sake with snacks like dried squid or mochi.

---

This menu gives you that perfect balance: guests can snack on simple, comforting bites when they want, but also dive into bold, umami-rich, adventurous flavors that’ll spark conversation and delight your foodie crowd.

And who knows—after this movie night, you might just be inspired to book that trip to see the snow monkeys yourself. 🐒❄️
