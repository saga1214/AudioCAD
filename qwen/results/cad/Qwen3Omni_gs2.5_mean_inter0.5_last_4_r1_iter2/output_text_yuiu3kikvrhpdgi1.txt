Sure thing! For easy edible gardening—especially in your St. Pete, Florida climate—I’d recommend:

**Herbs:**  
- Basil  
- Mint  
- Parsley  
- Cilantro (great for Mexican dishes!)  
- Oregano  

**Leafy Greens:**  
- Lettuce (try loose-leaf varieties—they grow fast and can be harvested in stages)  
- Spinach (prefers cooler temps, so perfect for fall/winter)  
- Arugula (spicy and fast-growing)  

**Root Vegetables:**  
- Radishes (ready in just 3–4 weeks!)  
- Carrots (need loose soil, so maybe grow in containers or raised beds)  

**Warm-Season Favorites (great for spring and fall):**  
- Tomatoes (try cherry or bush varieties—they’re easier for beginners)  
- Peppers (bell peppers and jalapeños both do well in Florida)  

Since you grow best in fall and winter, you can also consider **leafy greens and herbs** year-round, and **cool-season crops** like kale, Swiss chard, and even beets. Just keep an eye on the heat in summer—some plants may bolt or struggle.

And don’t forget: Florida’s long growing season means you can plant succession crops—just keep sowing seeds every few weeks for a steady harvest!
