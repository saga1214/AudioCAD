Of course! Based on your needs—easy to train, good with other dogs and children, calm temperament, low prey drive, and available as a purebred or mix here are some great options:

Purebred Options:
1. **Golden Retriever** – Known for being friendly, intelligent, and great with kids and other animals. Easy to train and generally calm indoors.
2. **Labrador Retriever** – Similar to Golden Retrievers—affectionate, trainable, and good with families. Needs exercise but is usually relaxed at home.
3. **Beagle** – Smaller size fits your landlord’s size rule. Good with kids and other dogs. However, they have a high prey drive, so may chase small animals. Not ideal if you have cats.
4. **Poodle (Standard or Miniature)** – Very smart, easy to train, low-shedding (great if you disliked cat shedding), and generally calm. Good with families.
5. **Bichon Frise** – Small, friendly, and playful but not overly energetic. Good with kids and other pets. Needs grooming.

Mixes or Rescue-Friendly Breeds:
- **Golden Retriever Mix** – Many rescues have Goldens or Goldie mixes. They often inherit the breed’s calm, friendly traits.
- **Labradoodle or Goldendoodle** – Designer mixes bred for friendliness and low shedding. Usually good with kids and other pets. Can vary in energy, so look for adult dogs.
- **Pug or Pug Mix** – Small, calm, and laid-back. Good for apartment living. Generally not high prey drive.
- **Bernese Mountain Dog Mix** – Large but often calm and gentle. Needs grooming but usually good with kids and other animals.

Tip: In shelters, ask for adult dogs with “family-friendly” or “good with kids” labels. Many mixed-breed dogs have the traits you want and are already trained.
