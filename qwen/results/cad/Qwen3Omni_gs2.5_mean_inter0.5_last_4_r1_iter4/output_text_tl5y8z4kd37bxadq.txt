For a laid-back first-night dinner in Austin, I’d recommend **The Original Frank’s** in South Congress. It’s got that chill vibe, great burgers, and a fun patio—perfect after a long day of traveling. Or if you're in the mood for something more local, **Tacos Tumbras** in East Austin has amazing tacos and a super relaxed atmosphere. Both are easy to get to and won’t overwhelm you after arrival.
