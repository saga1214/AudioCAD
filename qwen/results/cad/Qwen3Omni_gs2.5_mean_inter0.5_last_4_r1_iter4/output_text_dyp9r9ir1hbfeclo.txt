Oh, sorry! Earlier I mentioned three bones for shoulder rotation: the humerus, scapula, and clavicle. That’s correct—those are the only three bones that directly contribute to shoulder joint mechanics. Sometimes people think of the sternum or ribs, but those aren’t part of the shoulder joint itself. The clavicle connects the scapula to the sternum and helps transmit forces from the arm to the axial skeleton. So yeah, just those three.
