Absolutely! Here are some delicious, family-friendly, diabetes-awareness-conscious, and budget-friendly dessert ideas:

1. **Fruit-Based Desserts** – This is your best bet! Watermelon slices, banana boats (bananas with nut butter and granola), or apple slices with cinnamon and a little honey. Very affordable and healthy.

2. **DIY Sundaes Station** – Set up a table with low-sugar or sugar-free toppings like whipped cream (or coconut whipped cream), chopped nuts, seeds, and sugar-free chocolate syrup. Let people build their own sundaes with plain Greek yogurt or frozen bananas.

3. **Homemade Trail Mix** – Make your own mix with unsalted nuts, seeds, dried fruit (unsweetened), and a few dark chocolate chips. Package in small bags as favors or snacks.

4. **Popsicles** – Freeze fruit purees (like berries or mango) in molds. You can even add a splash of unsweetened coconut water or almond milk. Super cheap and refreshing.

5. **Mini Muffins** – Bake small batches of whole wheat or oat muffins with mashed banana or applesauce instead of oil. Use a little honey or maple syrup if needed. They’re easy to make in bulk and serve individually.

6. **Yogurt Parfaits** – Layer plain Greek yogurt with fresh berries and a sprinkle of chia seeds or granola. Use small cups or paper cups for easy serving.

7. Try **"Skinny" Brownies or Cookies** – Use applesauce, mashed banana, or oat flour to reduce sugar and fat. You can find many budget-friendly recipes online.

Bonus tip: Ask family members to bring a homemade dessert or two to share—this cuts down on cost and adds a personal touch!
