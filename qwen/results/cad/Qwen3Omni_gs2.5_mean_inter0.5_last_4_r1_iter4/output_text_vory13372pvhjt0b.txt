Given everything we've discussed, the single most important aspect of Bitcoin is its **trustless decentralization combined with a fixed, deflationary supply.**

This means:

- No central authority controls it  
- Its rules are enforced by code and network consensus  
- Its total supply is capped at 21 million  
- This combination creates a form of digital scarcity and censorship resistance

Together, these features make Bitcoin not just a currency, but a new kind of asset—one that exists outside traditional financial systems and can't be manipulated by governments or banks.

So while decentralization enables the system, and scarcity gives it value, it's their *fusion* that makes Bitcoin revolutionary.
