### 🥧 **No-Bake Chocolate Cream Pie (Very Easy!)**

This is a classic, crowd-pleasing dessert that requires **no baking at all** and only a few ingredients:

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pre-made graham cracker pie crust (store-bought — saves time!)
- 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
- 1/2 cup cocoa powder (unsweetened)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream (or whipped topping like Cool Whip)
- Optional: whipped cream and chocolate shavings for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. In a bowl, mix the sweetened condensed milk, cocoa powder, and vanilla until smooth.
2. In another bowl, whip the cream until stiff peaks form.
3. Gently fold the whipped cream into the chocolate mixture.
4. Pour into the pie crust.
5. Chill in the fridge for at least 2 hours (or overnight).
6. Garnish before serving.

✅ **Why it’s perfect for you:**
- No oven needed
- Minimal cooking (just mixing)
- Uses store-bought crust to cut down on prep
- Tastes amazing and feels very special

### 🥧 **Easy Apple Pie (Oven Required, But Still Simple)**

If you're up for using an oven, this is still very doable:

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pre-made pie crust (or two if you're using a double crust)
- 2–3 cups of pre-sliced apples (like Granny Smith or Honeycrisp)
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp cornstarch (to thicken)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Mix apples with sugar, spices, lemon juice, and cornstarch.
3. Pour into the pie crust.
4. Top with second crust or lattice.
5. Bake for 45–55 minutes until golden and bubbly.

✅ **Tip:** You can make the filling ahead of time and assemble the pie the day of. It still looks homemade and impressive!

### 🍪 Bonus: **No-Bake Peanut Butter Oat Bars (if no allergy)**

Wait — you said you're allergic to nuts. So skip peanut butter. But there's a **nut-free version**:

**Nut-Free No-Bake Chocolate Oat Bars**

- 1 cup peanut butter-free chocolate spread (like SunButter or Enjoy Life)
- 1/2 cup honey or maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups quick oats
- Pinch of salt

Mix everything together, press into a pan, chill, cut into bars.

These are great for a sweet, protein-rich treat and require no cooking.
