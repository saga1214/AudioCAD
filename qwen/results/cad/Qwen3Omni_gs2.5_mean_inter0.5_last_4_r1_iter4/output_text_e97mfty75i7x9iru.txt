No worries at all — flu brain fog is super common. Your brain is working overtime to fight the virus, so it’s okay to feel a little fuzzy. Let me go through everything step by step:

**For your cough:**
1. **Honey** – A spoonful of honey (especially in warm tea or water) can coat your throat and reduce coughing. You can take it straight or mix it with lemon and warm water.
2. **Stay hydrated** – Drinking fluids thins mucus and keeps your throat moist, which helps reduce coughing.
3. **Humidifier** – Breathing in moist air (from a humidifier or a hot shower) can soothe irritated airways.
4. **Gargle with salt water** – Mix 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon of salt in a glass of warm water and gargle to reduce throat irritation.
5. **Elevate your head** – Use an extra pillow when you sleep to prevent mucus from pooling in your throat, which can trigger coughing.

**For brain fog:**
1. **Rest** – Your body needs energy to fight the flu, so sleep as much as you can.
2. **Stay hydrated** – Dehydration can worsen brain fog, so keep drinking water.
3. **Eat small, simple meals** – Even if you don’t feel like eating, try light foods like broth, toast, or bananas to keep your energy up.
4. **Avoid screens** – Your brain is already working hard — give it a break from phones, TV, and computers when you can.
5. **Be kind to yourself** – Brain fog is temporary. It will lift as you recover.

And remember — antibiotics weren’t given for good reason. They won’t help the flu and could even cause side effects or harm your gut health.

You’re doing everything right by resting and asking for help. Get well soon!
