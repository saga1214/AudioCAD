Secret Harbour Beach Resort on St. Thomas is perfect if you want calm waters for snorkeling right off the shore. As for daytime fun, you should definitely take a boat tour to nearby islands like Water Island or even a snorkeling trip to Magens Bay. St. Thomas also has great hiking trails, like the one up to Magen's Head, which offers incredible views. And if you both enjoy history, the historic town of Charlotte Amalie is worth exploring.
