When people think of brands that are especially known for sedans, the top three are usually **Toyota**, **Honda**, and **BMW**.

- **Toyota** is famous for its reliable and fuel-efficient sedans like the Camry and Corolla, making it a go-to for everyday drivers.
- **Honda** offers popular sedans such as the Accord and Civic, known for their balance of performance, comfort, and efficiency.
- **BMW** is recognized for its premium, sporty sedans like the 3 Series and 5 Series, appealing to those who prioritize driving dynamics and luxury.

These brands consistently rank high in sales, customer satisfaction, and brand recognition specifically in the sedan segment.
