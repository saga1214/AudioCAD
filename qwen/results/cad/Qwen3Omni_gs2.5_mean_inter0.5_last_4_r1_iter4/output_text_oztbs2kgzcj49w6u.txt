Yes, there's actually a great way to learn more. The Erie Canal Museum in Lockport has a fantastic exhibit that covers the history of the canal and the construction of the locks. They even have a working model of the "Flight of Five" locks that you can see in action.

If you're really interested, you could also take a guided tour of the locks themselves. They offer boat tours that go through the locks, but if you're not into boating, they also have walking tours that give you a great view of the engineering and history.
