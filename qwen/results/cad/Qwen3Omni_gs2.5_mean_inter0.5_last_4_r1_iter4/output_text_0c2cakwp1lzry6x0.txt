Of course! Here’s a quick guide for each:

**1. Mounting a TV:**
- Use a stud finder to locate wall studs—this is crucial for safety and stability.
- If you can’t hit a stud, use heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for the weight of your TV.
- Follow the manufacturer’s instructions for your TV mount, and always have someone help you lift and position it.

**2. Hanging planters:**
- For lightweight planters, use command strips or small hooks rated for the weight.
- For heavier ones, drill into studs or use drywall anchors.
- Make sure the wall can support the weight, especially if it’s a shared wall.

**3. Floating shelves:**
- Use a stud finder to locate studs and mount the shelf brackets directly into them.
- If you can’t hit studs, use heavy-duty drywall anchors.
- Level the shelf before drilling the final holes to ensure it’s straight.

Always double-check the weight limits of your tools and anchors, and when in doubt, go for the stud.
