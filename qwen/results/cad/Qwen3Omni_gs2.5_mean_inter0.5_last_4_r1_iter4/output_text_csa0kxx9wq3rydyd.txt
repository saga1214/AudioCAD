6. **Pear and Gorgonzola Salad**

7. **Roasted Root Vegetables (carrots, parsnips, etc.)**

   → **Method:** Roasting

8. **Bread Stuffing**  
   → **Appliance:** Oven  
   → **Method:** Baking

9. **No-Bake Pumpkin Cheesecake**

10. **Chocolate-Peanut Butter "Lasagna"**

11. **Fresh Fruit Tart (cookie crust)**

12. **Slow Cooker Apple Crisp**

### **Summary by Appliance:**

- **Oven:** Turkey (smoked or roasted), mac and cheese, root veggies, stuffing, fruit tart crust

- **Slow Cooker:** Apple crisp
