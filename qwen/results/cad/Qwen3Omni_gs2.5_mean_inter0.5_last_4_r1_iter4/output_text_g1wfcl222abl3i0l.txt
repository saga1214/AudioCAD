Ugh glue guns can be so frustrating when they act up! Since it gets hot but still isn’t working, it might be clogged or the glue stick isn’t feeding properly. Try melting a little glue stick all the way through to clear any blockage, or check if the glue stick is the right size for your gun. Sometimes letting it cool down and restarting helps too. Hang in there—you’re going to love how spooky these turn out!
