No worries — I'm happy to help you get organized. Here's a complete, categorized list of all the body measurements we've discussed, in order from most essential to more detailed:

**Core / Essential Measurements (Take at every fitting):**
1. Bust – Around the fullest part of the chest, keeping tape parallel to the floor.
2. Natural Waist – Around the narrowest part of the torso, usually just above the belly button.
3. Hips – Around the fullest part of the buttocks, keeping tape level.

**Basic Garment-Specific Measurements:**
4. Inseam – From the crotch to the ankle bone (for pants).
5. Sleeve Length – From shoulder point (where the sleeve joins the body) down to the wrist bone (for shirts/jackets).
6. Shoulder Width – From outer edge of one shoulder to the other (for tailored tops/jackets).

**Additional Measurements for Fitted Garments:**
7. Thigh – Around the fullest part of the thigh (for tailored pants).
8. Neck Circumference – Around the base of the neck (for collars).
9. Bicep – Around the fullest part of the upper arm (for sleeve fit).
10. Upper Back Width – From shoulder to shoulder across the back (for jacket fit).
11. Front Rise – From waistband down to crotch (front).
12. Back Rise – From waistband down to crotch (back) — crucial for comfort when sitting.

💡 **Pro Tip:** For clients, always take measurements with them wearing minimal layers (like a fitted tank or shirt) and standing naturally. Use a flexible tape measure and keep it snug but not tight.
