That sounds like a fun and romantic evening! Here are some ocean-themed meal and dessert ideas perfect for your theme:

**Ocean-Themed Meals:**

1. **Shrimp Scampi with Garlic Butter Pasta** – A classic seafood dish that’s easy to make and full of flavor. Use linguine or spaghetti and garnish with fresh parsley and lemon wedges for a coastal vibe.

2. **Grilled Fish Tacos** – Use a firm white fish like mahi-mahi or snapper. Grill it, shred it, and serve in corn tortillas with cabbage slaw, avocado, and a lime crema. Add a side of mango salsa for a sweet contrast.

3. **Seafood Paella** – A Spanish favorite with shrimp, mussels, and maybe some calamari. Cook it in a large pan for a show-stopping centerpiece. Add saffron for that golden color and rich flavor.

4. **Baked Cod with Lemon and Herbs** – A healthier option. Season cod with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and herbs like dill or thyme. Bake until flaky and serve with roasted vegetables or a quinoa salad.

5. **Coconut Curry with Shrimp and Vegetables** – A tropical twist. Use coconut milk, curry paste, and add shrimp, bell peppers, and zucchini. Serve over jasmine rice or with naan bread.

**Ocean-Themed Desserts:**

1. **Mango Coconut Panna Cotta** – A creamy, dairy-free dessert that’s light and tropical. Use coconut milk and gelatin, and top with fresh mango cubes and mint leaves.

2. **Lemon Blueberry Tart** – A citrusy, refreshing tart with a buttery crust. The blueberries add a pop of color and sweetness—perfect for a seaside feel.

3. **Chocolate-Dipped Strawberries** – Simple but elegant. Use dark chocolate and dip strawberries, then let them set. Add a sprinkle of sea salt for a sweet-salty ocean touch.

4. **Tropical Fruit Platter** – Arrange slices of pineapple, mango, papaya, and kiwi in a shell-shaped bowl or on a platter. Add a drizzle of honey or coconut yogurt for dipping.

5. **Coconut Chia Pudding Parfait** – Layer chia pudding made with coconut milk, fresh berries, and shredded coconut. Serve in glass cups for a beautiful presentation.

With your ocean decorations and these delicious dishes, your themed night will be a hit! 🌊🍤
