Absolutely! Since you're doing a Retro Tech Day, go for meals that were iconic in their time. First idea: **"The 90s Pizza Party"** — grab a large pepperoni pizza (classic 90s style) and serve it with a side of microwave popcorn. Bonus points if you use a retro tin foil tray or paper plates. Second idea: **"The 80s Fast Food Feast"** — think of a grilled cheese sandwich (maybe even with a side of tomato soup in a paper cup) and a bag of chips. You could even serve it with a soda in a glass bottle for that extra nostalgic touch. Both are easy to make, fit the theme, and won’t take much time during your evening hours.
