Love the breath freshener idea—small but thoughtful! Minty mints or sugar-free gum are perfect for a quick palate reset without being heavy. You could even label them with a little tag like “Freshen Up & Recharge!” to keep it fun and intentional.

For the badges—yes, go ahead and print new ones for the appreciation weekend! It’ll make volunteers feel extra celebrated and give them a tangible token of their contribution. You don’t need a full rebrand; just a clean, updated design with their name and “Volunteer of the Week” or “Appreciation Weekend” on it. It adds a nice personal touch and makes the event feel special.

And toss in a few extra fun touches—like a thank-you card they can take home, or a small sticker they can put on their water bottle. These little things go a long way in making people feel seen.

You’re doing a great job balancing practicality and heart. Keep it light, keep it kind, and the energy will show.
