Absolutely. Lockport has a great visitor center at the Erie Canal Museum, where you can learn all about the history of the canal and its role in the city's development. They have exhibits on the construction of the locks, the people who built them, and even the story of the Japanese spies you just heard about.

You can also take a guided walking tour of the historic district, which includes the locks and the old canal towpath. It's a really immersive way to see how the canal shaped the city.
