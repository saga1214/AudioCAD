* **Side Dishes:**
  * 2–3 lbs baby potatoes (for scalloped potatoes)
  * 1 large head of garlic
  * 1 large onion
  * 1 lb butter
  * 2 cups heavy cream
  * 1 cup milk
  * 1 lb asparagus
  * Olive oil
  * Salt and pepper
  * Lemon (1–2, for asparagus)

* **Appetizers:**
  * 1 package mini slider buns
  * 1 lb ground beef or turkey
  * 1 lb smoked sausage or bacon (for pigs in a blanket)
  * 1 package crescent rolls
  * 1 lb shrimp (for shrimp cocktail)
  * Cocktail sauce (or mix your own with ketchup, horseradish, lemon juice)
  * 1 large cucumber
  * 1–2 bell peppers
  * 1 bag mixed greens or cherry tomatoes (for veggie platter)

* **Drinks:**
  * 1 bottle champagne or sparkling wine (for midnight toast)
  * 1–2 bottles sparkling cider (for kids)
  * 1 large bottle grape juice (for kids)
  * 1–2 bottles water (still and sparkling)
  * 1–2 bottles soda (optional)

* **Extras:**
  * 1 box paper plates and napkins
  * 1 box plastic cups (for kids and adults)
  * 1 box plastic forks and knives
  * 1 roll paper towels
  * 1 bag ice (for drinks)

**Optional Party Supplies:**
* Noisemakers or party hats (for kids)
* Balloons or streamers (for decorations)
