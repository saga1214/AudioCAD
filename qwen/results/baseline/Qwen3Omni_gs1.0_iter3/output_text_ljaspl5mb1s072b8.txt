Great question! Let's break it down with the key dates:

- **Siad Barre** seized power in **1969** after a military coup. He ruled Somalia as a dictator until he was overthrown in **1991**. So, he was in power for **22 years**.

- After his ousting, Somalia descended into civil war with no central government.

- The **U.S. and UN intervention** began in **late 1992** with Operation Restore Hope, aimed at providing humanitarian aid during a famine.

- The **Battle of Mogadishu**, which you mentioned, happened in **October 1993**.

- After that battle, the U.S. began withdrawing its forces, and the last U.S. troops left Somalia in **March 1994**.

So, from the time Siad Barre took power in **1969** to the U.S. leaving in **1994**, about **25 years** passed.

But if you're asking how much time passed between **Siad Barre's rise to power** and the **U.S. withdrawal**, it's **25 years**.
