Absolutely! Here are some delicious and budget-friendly dessert ideas that are kid-approved and easy to prepare:

1. **Fruit and Yogurt Parfaits** – Layer plain or vanilla yogurt with fresh or frozen fruit (like berries, bananas, or apples) in small cups. You can even use individual paper cups or mason jars. It’s healthy, colorful, and affordable.

2. **Homemade Trail Mix** – Make your own mix with nuts, dried fruit, and a few chocolate chips. Portion it into small bags or cups. It’s a fun snack and can double as a party favor.

3. **Popsicles** – Use fruit juice or pureed fruit (like banana or mango) in popsicle molds. You can even freeze yogurt or smoothies. They’re cheap to make and a big hit with kids.

4. **Brownies or Cookies** – Bake a large batch of simple brownies or cookies. They’re inexpensive to make and can be cut into small squares for easy serving. Use basic ingredients like flour, sugar, butter, eggs, and chocolate chips.

5. **Cupcakes** – Mini cupcakes are great for portion control and look festive. You can make them with a simple box mix or from scratch. Add a little frosting and a sprinkle of sprinkles for fun.

6. **Ice Cream Cones** – Buy ice cream in bulk and serve it in cones or cups. You can even offer a few toppings like chocolate syrup or sprinkles to make it more exciting.

7. **S’mores Station** – If you have a fire pit or grill, set up a s’mores station with graham crackers, marshmallows, and chocolate. It’s a fun activity and very affordable.

These options are not only budget-friendly but also easy to scale up or down depending on your guest count.
