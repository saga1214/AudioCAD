Absolutely! Here’s a list of five energizing drinks and five snacks to help you power through your day:

**Drinks:**
1. **Coffee** – The classic pick-me-up. Caffeine boosts alertness and focus.
2. **Green Tea** – Contains caffeine and L-theanine, which gives a calm, focused energy.
3. **Energy Drinks** – Like Red Bull or Monster, they have caffeine and sugar for a quick boost.
4. **Electrolyte Drinks** – Like Gatorade or Liquid IV, they hydrate and replenish minerals.
5. **Black Tea** – Less caffeine than coffee but still effective, with a smoother energy release.

**Snacks:**
1. **Nuts (like almonds or walnuts)** – Healthy fats and protein keep energy steady.
2. **Dark Chocolate (70%+ cocoa)** – A little caffeine and antioxidants for a mood and energy lift.
3. **Fruit (like bananas or oranges)** – Natural sugars and vitamins for a quick energy boost.
4. **Hard-Boiled Eggs** – Protein and fat to keep you full and focused.
5. **Greek Yogurt** – Protein and probiotics, plus a bit of natural sugar for energy.

Mix and match these to keep your energy up without crashing later. Stay hydrated and take short breaks too!
