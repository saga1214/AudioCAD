If I had to pick just one, I’d say the biggest driver is the **overconsumption of calorie-dense, highly processed foods**—especially when they're cheap, widely available, and designed to be overeaten. These foods are often low in nutrients and high in sugar, unhealthy fats, and salt, which can lead to overeating and weight gain. Combined with sedentary lifestyles, they create a perfect storm for rising obesity rates. But at the core, it's about **energy imbalance**, and processed foods make it much easier to consume more calories than we need.
