1. **Tool for Cutting Sheet Metal**:  
   - **Recommendation**: Aviation Snips (Straight-Cut or Tinsnips)  
   - **Why**: Lightweight, easy to use, and ideal for thin sheet metal and curves.  
   - **Where to Buy**: **The Home Depot** – They carry a full line of aviation snips and have knowledgeable staff to help you choose the right type.

2. **Fastener for Drywall**:  
   - **Recommendation**: 1-1/4 inch Coarse-Thread Drywall Screws  
   - **Why**: Superior holding power, less likely to pop, and perfect for screw guns.  
   - **Where to Buy**: **Lowe's** – They have a wide selection of drywall screws and often carry both standard and drywall-specific packages.

3. **Insulation for Walls**:  
   - **Recommendation**: Fiberglass Batt Insulation (R-13 or R-15, depending on wall cavity)  
   - **Why**: Cost-effective, easy to install, and widely available.  
   - **Where to Buy**: **Menards** – They offer the best selection of insulation products in Indianapolis, including fiberglass batts, mineral wool, and spray foam kits.

**Best Overall Store for Your Project**:
