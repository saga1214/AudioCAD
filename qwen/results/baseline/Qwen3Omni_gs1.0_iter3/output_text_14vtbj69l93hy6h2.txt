No worries—some dogs just aren’t into cheese! For Maltese, many love tiny bits of cooked chicken (make sure it’s plain, no seasoning), or even small pieces of hot dog. As for liver treats, freeze-dried beef or chicken liver is usually a hit. You can also try soft dog treats made for small breeds—they’re often more appealing. Try a few different options and see what she goes for. The key is finding what *she* loves!
