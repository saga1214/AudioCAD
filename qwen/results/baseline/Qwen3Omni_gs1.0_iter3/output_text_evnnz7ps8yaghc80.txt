---

**Weekly Juggling Practice Schedule**

**Monday – Foundation & Timing**  
- 10 minutes: Practice 2-ball one-handed juggling with **bean bags**  
- 10 minutes: Work on the 3-ball cascade rhythm (focus on timing, not catching)  
- 5 minutes: Try 2-ball juggling with **scarves** (focus on smooth, slow throws)

**Wednesday – Pattern Focus**  
- 10 minutes: Practice 2-ball one-handed juggling with **bean bags**  
- 10 minutes: Work on the 3-ball cascade (use music if helpful!)  
- 5 minutes: Try 2-ball juggling with **scarves** (focus on hand positioning)

**Friday – Skill Integration**  
- 10 minutes: Practice 2-ball one-handed juggling with **bean bags**  
- 10 minutes: Work on 3-ball juggling (even if you drop a lot—just keep going!)  
- 5 minutes: Try 2-ball juggling with **scarves** (use this to relax and enjoy the flow)

**Saturday – Fun & Flow**  
- 15 minutes: Free practice with **scarves** (no pressure—just play with the rhythm)  
- 10 minutes: Try 3-ball juggling with **bean bags** (use your best timing)  
- Optional: Record yourself to see your progress!

---

**Tips for Success:**

- **Keep it short and sweet**: Even 15–20 minutes of focused practice is better than an hour of frustration.  
- **Celebrate small wins**: Every time you complete a 3-ball throw or catch, give yourself a little cheer!  
- **Mix it up**: The scarves help you relax and focus on form, while the bean bags build muscle memory.  
- **Stay consistent**: Even if you miss a few throws, just keep showing up. Progress comes with repetition.
