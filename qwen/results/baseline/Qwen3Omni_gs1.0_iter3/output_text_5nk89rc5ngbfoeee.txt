For your end-of-year celebration, a mix of fun and simplicity works best! Since you already have a routine in the cafeteria, you could do a themed party there—like a "Brownie Bonanza" with games, crafts, and snacks. But if you want something more special, a park BBQ or picnic is a great way to wrap up the year. It gives the girls a chance to relax and enjoy each other’s company outside the usual setting.

Here are a few ideas to help you decide:

1. **Cafeteria Party (Low Effort, High Fun):**  
   - Set up a simple game station (e.g., ring toss, sack races).  
   - Serve easy snacks like ice cream sundaes, pizza, or chips and dip.  
   - Hand out small end-of-year tokens or certificates.  
   - Play music and let them enjoy the time together.

2. **Park Picnic/BBQ (More Active, Memorable):**  
   - Pick a local park with picnic tables and open space.  
   - Have a potluck-style setup—ask parents to bring a dish.  
   - Include fun activities like sack races, scavenger hunts, or a Brownie-themed game.  
   - End with a group photo and a little recognition for each girl.

3. **Hybrid Option (Best of Both Worlds):**  
   - Start with a short indoor activity in the cafeteria (games, crafts).  
   - Then head outside for a picnic or ice cream treat.

**Pro Tip:** Keep it short and sweet—60–90 minutes max. Brownies are young, and they’ll enjoy it more if it’s not too long or too structured.

And don’t forget to celebrate *you* too! You’ve led a great year—this party is as much for you as it is for them.
