Oh, absolutely! After a few days of desert camping, a Vegas hotel is the perfect reset. Here’s how to have a *proper* Vegas experience:

1. **Stay in a hotel with a view** – Pick one on the Strip with a view of the lights. Some great options include The LINQ, The Flamingo, or even a mid-tier place like the Tropicana or Excalibur if you want a bit of retro charm.

2. **Dine like a local** – Skip the overpriced buffets (though they’re fun for a splurge). Try some local favorites like:
   - **Eggslut** for breakfast tacos
   - **Lucky’s** for burgers
   - **Bacchanal Buffet** if you’re feeling indulgent
   - Or go for something unique like **Carmine’s** for Italian or **Tao Downtown** for Asian fusion

3. **See the shows** – Vegas has *so* many options. You don’t have to spend a fortune. Check out:
   - **Cirque du Soleil** (if you want something spectacular)
   - **Absinthe** at Caesars Palace (a wild, interactive cabaret)
   - Or just catch a free show like **The Fremont Street Experience** light show at night

4. **Explore beyond the Strip** – Take a walk down Fremont Street for a more old-school Vegas vibe. Or check out the **Bellagio Fountains** at night.

5. **Go for a night out** – Hit a rooftop bar like **Skyfall** at the Cosmopolitan or **The View** at the LINQ for amazing views and cocktails.

6. **Don’t forget the vibe** – Vegas is all about fun, so dress up a little, try something new, and just enjoy the energy.

And hey, if you want to go all out, you could even book a short stay at a luxury resort like Aria or Wynn for a real Vegas splurge. But even on a budget, you can have an amazing time.
