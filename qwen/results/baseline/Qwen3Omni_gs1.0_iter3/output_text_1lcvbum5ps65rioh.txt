Now, for spaghetti carbonara, here are the ingredients you'll need:

- Spaghetti (about 400g for 4 servings)
- Eggs (2 large)
- Parmesan cheese (about 1/2 cup, grated)
- Pancetta or guanciale (about 150g, diced)
- Garlic (1 clove, minced)
- Black pepper (freshly ground)
- Salt (for pasta water)
- Optional: Fresh parsley (for garnish)

That’s it! You’ll also need a large pot for boiling pasta and a skillet for cooking the pancetta. Enjoy your cooking!
