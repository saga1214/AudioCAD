Here's a detailed 10-day itinerary that allows you to deeply explore Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, and Nara—three of Japan’s most iconic cities—while balancing sightseeing, culture, food, and relaxation. This plan assumes you're traveling with your parents and want a mix of must-see spots and authentic local experiences.

---

### 🗓️ **10-Day Japan Itinerary: Tokyo → Kyoto → Nara → Osaka**

#### **Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo**
- Arrive at Narita or Haneda Airport.
- Take the **Narita Express (N'EX)** or **Keikyu Line** to Tokyo (approx. 1 hour).
- Check into hotel (recommend staying in Shinjuku, Shibuya, or Asakusa for central access).
- Light walk around the neighborhood to settle in.
- Dinner at a local izakaya or ramen shop.

---

#### **Day 2: Tokyo – Traditional & Modern Tokyo**
- **Morning:** Visit **Senso-ji Temple** in Asakusa (Japan’s oldest temple). Explore Nakamise Street for snacks and souvenirs.
- **Afternoon:** Walk to **Sumida River** and take a boat ride or visit **Tokyo Skytree** for panoramic views.
- **Evening:** Explore **Shibuya**—see the famous Shibuya Crossing, shop, and enjoy dinner.

---

#### **Day 3: Tokyo – Culture & Nature**
- **Morning:** Visit **Meiji Shrine** in Harajuku (peaceful forested shrine).
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Harajuku** (Takeshita Street for quirky fashion) and **Shibuya** again (Shibuya Scramble Square or Hikarie).
- **Evening:** Dinner in **Shimokitazawa** (artsy, relaxed vibe) or **Shinjuku** (Omoide Yokocho for tiny izakayas).

---

#### **Day 4: Tokyo – Day Trip to Nikko**
- Take the **JR Nikko Line** (approx. 2 hours).
- Visit **Toshogu Shrine** (UNESCO site, ornate and colorful).
- Explore **Lake Chuzenji** and **Kegon Falls**.
- Return to Tokyo in the evening.

---

#### **Day 5: Tokyo – Modern & Artistic Tokyo**
- **Morning:** Visit **TeamLab Borderless** (digital art museum) or **Mori Art Museum** in Roppongi.
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Ginza** (luxury shopping) or **Akihabara** (anime, electronics).
- **Evening:** Dinner in **Tsukiji Outer Market** (famous for fresh sushi and street food).

---

#### **Day 6: Tokyo → Kyoto (by Shinkansen)**
- Take the **Nozomi Shinkansen** (approx. 2.5 hours).
- Check into hotel in Kyoto (near Kyoto Station or Gion).
- **Afternoon:** Visit **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (famous for thousands of red torii gates).
- **Evening:** Explore **Gion** (geisha district) and enjoy kaiseki dinner.

---

#### **Day 7: Kyoto – Temples & Gardens**
- **Morning:** Visit **Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)**.
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Ryoan-ji** (famous Zen rock garden) and **Nijo Castle**.
- **Evening:** Stroll through **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** and **Togetsukyo Bridge**.

---

#### **Day 8: Kyoto → Nara → Kyoto**
- Take the **JR Nara Line** (approx. 45 minutes).
- Visit **Nara Park** and see the giant **Great Buddha (Daibutsu)** at Todai-ji Temple.
- Feed the friendly deer (but don’t feed them directly with your hands).
- Return to Kyoto in the afternoon.
- Optional: Visit **Philosopher’s Path** for a peaceful walk.

---

#### **Day 9: Kyoto → Osaka**
- Take the **JR Line** (approx. 30 minutes).
- Check into hotel in Osaka (near Namba or Umeda).
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Dotonbori** (neon lights, street food, shopping).
- **Evening:** Try takoyaki, okonomiyaki, and kushikatsu.

---

#### **Day 10: Osaka – Culture & Fun**
- **Morning:** Visit **Osaka Castle** (beautiful grounds and museum).
- **Afternoon:** Explore **Shinsekai** and **Tsutenkaku Tower** (retro vibe).
- **Evening:** Optional: Visit **Universal Studios Japan** (if you’re fans of theme parks).
