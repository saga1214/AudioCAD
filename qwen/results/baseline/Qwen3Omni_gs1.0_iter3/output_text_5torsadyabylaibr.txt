That's a wonderful idea! A homemade pie would definitely make you the star of the evening. Since it's Thanksgiving, classic flavors like pumpkin, apple, or pecan are always crowd-pleasers.

For a really easy and delicious option, I'd recommend a **vanilla custard pie**. It's creamy, sweet, and not too heavy. You just need a pre-made pie crust, eggs, sugar, milk, vanilla, and a little cornstarch to thicken it. You can make it ahead of time and chill it before serving.

If you're feeling a bit more adventurous, a **caramel apple pie** is amazing. You can use canned pie filling or make your own with apples, cinnamon, and a little sugar. Then top it with a homemade caramel sauce and a crumb topping.

And if you want something truly special, try a **pumpkin pie with a gingersnap crust**. It adds a nice crunch and a little spice that pairs perfectly with the pumpkin. You can even make the crust ahead of time and freeze it.

All of these are doable even if you're not a pro in the kitchen. And the best part? You can make them a few days in advance and just reheat or serve cold.
