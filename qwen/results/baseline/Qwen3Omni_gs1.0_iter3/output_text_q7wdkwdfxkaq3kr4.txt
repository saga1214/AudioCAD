Great question! Here are the top three players with the most **official** goals in soccer history, based on widely accepted records from FIFA, RSSSF, and other reputable sources:

1. **Cristiano Ronaldo** – **850+ goals**  
   - As of 2024, Cristiano Ronaldo has scored over 850 official goals in his career, making him the all-time top scorer. He has achieved this across club football (for Sporting, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, Al-Nassr) and international football (Portugal).

2. **Josef Bican** – **805 goals**  
   - A Czechoslovakian striker who played mostly in Austria and Czechoslovakia during the 1930s–1950s. His total includes goals from league, cup, and international matches. He is often cited as the top scorer in history, though some of his goals were in unofficial wartime competitions.

3. **Lionel Messi** – **838+ goals**  
   - Lionel Messi is second on the official list, with over 838 goals. He scored most of them for FC Barcelona (672 goals), with additional goals for Paris Saint-Germain and Inter Miami, plus 86 goals for Argentina.

So, while Pelé is legendary and scored over 1,200 goals in his career, many of those were in friendlies and exhibition matches, which aren't counted in official records. That's why the top three are Ronaldo, Bican, and Messi.
