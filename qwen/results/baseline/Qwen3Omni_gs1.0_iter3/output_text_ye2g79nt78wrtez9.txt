Yes, if I had to choose just one, I would still go with Ancient Egypt. Despite Mesopotamia's incredible historical importance, Egypt has a broader appeal and a more accessible narrative. The pyramids, mummies, and hieroglyphs are instantly recognizable, which helps draw in visitors and makes the educational content more engaging. Plus, the sheer visual impact of Egyptian artifacts—like gold masks and intricately painted sarcophagi—creates a more immersive experience. So, while Mesopotamia is intellectually rich, Egypt wins out in terms of public engagement and storytelling power.
