Sure! Here are two different approaches you can take:

**Idea 1: Schedule a formal meeting.**  
You can say something like, "Hi [Boss's Name], I was wondering if we could schedule a time to talk about my role and performance. I'd love to get your feedback and discuss my career path." This frames the conversation as a professional development discussion, which is less intimidating and gives you a chance to prepare.

**Idea 2: Bring it up casually during a check-in.**  
If you have regular one-on-one meetings, you could say, "I've been thinking about my contributions lately and how I can continue to grow here. I was wondering if now would be a good time to discuss my compensation and how it aligns with my responsibilities." This makes it feel more like a natural part of your ongoing work conversation.
