Absolutely! Here’s a simple, practical checklist to help you get started with recording your puppet shows—especially for your upcoming performance at Sunnyside Assisted Living.

**What You’ll Need to Record Your Show:**

1. **Camera or Phone**  
   - Use your smartphone (iPhone or Android) – most have great video quality.  
   - Make sure the battery is charged or bring a power bank.

2. **Tripod or Stable Surface**  
   - Keeps the camera steady.  
   - If you don’t have a tripod, prop the phone on a stack of books or a chair.

3. **Lighting**  
   - Film in a well-lit room.  
   - Natural light is best. Avoid filming in the dark or with harsh shadows.

4. **Audio (Optional but Helpful)**  
   - Your phone’s mic works, but if you want clearer sound, use a lapel mic or a small USB mic.  
   - Avoid loud background noise (TV, fans, etc.).

5. **Permission**  
   - Ask the facility staff if you can record.  
   - Get consent from any audience members who might be visible (especially kids or vulnerable adults).

6. **Backup Plan**  
   - Have a friend or staff member help film.  
   - If no one’s available, set up the phone on a tripod and record a wide shot.

7. **Recording Settings**  
   - Use 1080p HD video (if available).  
   - Record in landscape mode (horizontal), not portrait (vertical).  
   - Keep videos under 10 minutes for kids.

8. **Post-Show Review**  
   - Watch the video right after to check audio, lighting, and if anyone’s in the frame.  
   - Delete if it’s not usable.

9. **Upload & Share**  
   - Save the video to your computer or cloud (Google Drive, iCloud).  
   - Later, edit it (optional) and upload to YouTube or share privately.

**Bonus Tip:**  
Start with one short, fun performance—like Melvin the Magnificent doing a magic trick. That’s an easy, engaging video to share.

You’ve got this! Just start small and build from there.
