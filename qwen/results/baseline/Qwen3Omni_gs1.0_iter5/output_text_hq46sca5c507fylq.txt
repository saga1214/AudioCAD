That’s a great idea—gifts that support your hobbies can be both thoughtful and practical. For your interest in tailoring, a nice set of tailor’s chalk, a rotary cutter with a mat, or a good quality seam ripper would be appreciated. You could also ask for a beginner’s tailoring book or a pattern-making kit.

For your vintage fountain pen hobby, a high-quality cleaning kit or a set of nibs and inks could be a fun gift. Or maybe a display case to keep your pens safe and organized.

And if you're open to exploring woodworking or clay, a starter tool kit or a beginner’s pottery class would be meaningful. Even a nice pair of work gloves or apron for hands-on projects would be a nice touch.

Ultimately, the best gifts are ones that help you grow in your interests—so anything that supports your creative side will be appreciated.
