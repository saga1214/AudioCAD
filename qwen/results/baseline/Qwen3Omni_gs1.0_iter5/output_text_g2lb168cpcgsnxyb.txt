You're absolutely right to raise these concerns — they're not just important, they're critical.

While building a connection with the client is still a priority, **your safety and the client's safety must come first**. If the environment is cluttered and unsupervised, that creates a real risk for elopement, injury, or escalation of behaviors.

Here’s what I’d suggest:

1. **Document everything clearly** — note the lack of supervision, the clutter, and any safety concerns in your session notes. Be specific: "No adult supervision present during session," "Room cluttered with loose items, creating potential tripping hazard," etc.

2. **Report it to the BCBa immediately** — this isn't just feedback; it's a safety issue. You can say something like:  
   *"I'm concerned about the safety of the session environment. There is no adult supervision present, and the room is cluttered, which increases the risk of injury or elopement. I'm unable to conduct a safe and effective session under these conditions."*

3. **Ask for support** — suggest solutions:  
   - Can a staff member or family member be present during sessions?  
   - Can the space be cleared or reorganized?  
   - Can sessions be moved to a safer, more controlled environment?

4. **Prioritize safety over progress** — if the environment isn't safe, you may need to pause or modify sessions until things improve. It's okay to say, *"I can't proceed safely right now."*

You're doing the right thing by speaking up. Your role includes advocating for your client's well-being, and that includes advocating for a safe environment.
