Absolutely! Pickle juice is actually pretty useful. You can use it as a tangy base for salad dressings or marinades—just mix it with olive oil, herbs, and a little mustard. It also makes a great addition to Bloody Marys or even as a brine for other veggies. And for the pickles themselves, chop them up and add them to sandwiches, grain bowls, or even scrambled eggs for a zesty kick.
