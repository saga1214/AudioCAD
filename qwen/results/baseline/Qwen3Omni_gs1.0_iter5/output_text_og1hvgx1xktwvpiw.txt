The **classic Boulevardier** is traditionally made with **1¼ oz bourbon (or rye), 1 oz sweet vermouth, and 1 oz Campari**. That’s the standard ratio you’ll find in most cocktail books and bars.

Your **friend’s version**, using **equal parts** (1 oz each), is a popular variation that’s slightly sweeter and less bitter than the classic. It’s still a valid and delicious take on the drink.

So to recap:
- **Classic Boulevardier**: 1¼ oz bourbon, 1 oz sweet vermouth, 1 oz Campari
- **Equal parts version**: 1 oz each

Now, about the **Last Word** — it’s a different cocktail altogether, but also equal parts. It’s made with:
- ¾ oz gin
- ¾ oz green Chartreuse
- ¾ oz maraschino liqueur
- ¾ oz fresh lime juice

Shake with ice and strain into a chilled coupe glass. It’s bright, herbal, and refreshing — a great contrast to the bold, bitter Boulevardier.

So if you're feeling adventurous, you could do one of each — or just stick with the Boulevardier in your preferred style.
