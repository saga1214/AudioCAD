Sure! Let's rank the fruits we've discussed so far on a scale of 1 to 100 based on their vitamin C content. Here's how they compare:

   (About 92–98 mg per 100g)

   (About 53 mg per 100g)

4. **Apple** – 25  
   (About 4–6 mg per 100g)

   (About 8–10 mg per 100g)

6. **Tomato** – 15  
   (About 14 mg per 100g)

Note: While tomato is botanically a fruit, it's often treated as a vegetable in culinary use.

- Guava: 95  
- Kiwi: 85  
- Orange: 75  
- Apple: 25  
- Banana: 20

This ranking reflects their vitamin C content per 100 grams, adjusted to fit the 1–100 scale.
