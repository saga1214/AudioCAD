Here’s a **quick, easy, and delicious dairy-free dish** you can make together in under 20 minutes:

### 🌱 **Vegan Avocado & Tomato Toast with Lemon Zest**

**Ingredients (for 2 servings):**
- 2 slices of whole grain or sourdough bread (check for dairy-free)
- 1 ripe avocado
- 1 small tomato, diced
- 1 tsp lemon juice (optional, for freshness)
- Pinch of salt and pepper
- Optional: red pepper flakes or fresh herbs (like basil or chives)

**Instructions:**
1. Toast the bread.
2. Mash the avocado in a bowl with a fork.
3. Mix in lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
4. Spread the avocado mixture on the toast.
5. Top with diced tomatoes and a sprinkle of red pepper flakes or herbs.

**Why it works:**
- Super quick and easy — no cooking required!
- Naturally dairy-free and avoids all her dislikes.
- Fun to make together — you can both spread, chop, and taste as you go.
- Healthy and satisfying!

You can even turn it into a little game: “Who can make the prettiest toast?” or “Let’s see who can dice the tomato fastest!”
