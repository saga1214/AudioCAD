The most important factor to consider first and foremost is **following the guidelines**.

Here’s why: even if you have the most engaging, concise, and consistent content, if it violates TikTok’s community guidelines, it can be removed, restricted, or even get your account penalized. That means your content won’t be seen by anyone — no matter how good it is.

So while consistency, conciseness, and good descriptions all help your content perform better *once it’s allowed*, **following the guidelines is the foundation**. Without it, nothing else matters.
