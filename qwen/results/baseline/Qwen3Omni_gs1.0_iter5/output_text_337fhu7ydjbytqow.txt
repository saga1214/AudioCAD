Absolutely! Here’s a tailored packing list for each segment of your husband’s race, based on the conditions and your role as a pacer.

---

### **Pacing List – 20-Mile Night Segment (Cool, Hilly, 3 Aid Stations)**

**Clothing:**
- Moisture-wicking base layer (top and bottom)
- Insulating mid-layer (fleece or light synthetic jacket)
- Wind- and water-resistant outer shell (lightweight trail jacket)
- Warm hat (beanie or balaclava)
- Gloves (lightweight, insulated, or windproof)
- Buff or neck gaiter (for warmth and dust)
- Trail running shoes (grippy, comfortable, broken in)
- Moisture-wicking socks (consider thin liner + mid-weight combo)

**Gear:**
- Headlamp (high-quality, bright, with red light mode to preserve night vision)
- Extra batteries (for headlamp and any backup light)
- Small running vest or hydration pack (to carry layers and essentials)
- Lightweight insulated jacket (for cooling down at aid stations)
- Hand warmers (optional, but helpful in cold, damp conditions)
- Small towel or microfiber cloth (for wiping sweat or dew)

**Notes:**
- This segment is longer and colder, so layering is key. You’ll likely start warm and cool down during the night.
- Use the aid stations to swap layers or warm up briefly.
- Bring a small pack to carry layers you don’t need immediately.

---

### **Pacing List – Final 12-Mile Segment (Likely Warmer, Still Nighttime)**

**Clothing:**
- Moisture-wicking base layer (top and bottom)
- Light insulating layer (if needed, but likely not full mid-layer)
- Lightweight wind shell or trail jacket (for wind protection)
- Warm hat (optional, depending on temperature)
- Gloves (optional, but still useful if it’s cool)
- Trail running shoes
- Moisture-wicking socks

**Gear:**
- Headlamp (still essential)
- Extra batteries
- Small running vest or hydration pack (for layers and essentials)
- Hand warmers (if it’s cold)
- Small towel or microfiber cloth

**Notes:**
- The final 12 miles are likely warmer, especially as the race progresses and your body heats up.
- You may not need a full mid-layer—opt for a light insulating layer or even just the base layer.
- Still pack a wind shell for protection.
- You’ll be pacing the final push, so stay energized and focused.

---

### **Bonus: Shared Essentials (Bring Both Segments)**
- Headlamp + extra batteries
- Small running vest or pack
- Moisture-wicking base layers
- Trail shoes
- Gloves and hat (pack both, use as needed)
- Hand warmers (optional but helpful)
- Towel or microfiber cloth

---

This setup keeps you prepared for the cold night of the 20-mile segment and allows for lighter, more breathable layers during the final 12 miles. You’ll be able to adjust on the fly based on how you feel and the weather. Good luck to you and your husband!
