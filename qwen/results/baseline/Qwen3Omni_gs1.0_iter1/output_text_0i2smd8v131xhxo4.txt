To make the switch to Adobe Audition easier, start by importing your existing projects and using Audition's "Import Audio" feature to get familiar with the interface. Then, gradually adopt its tools like the Multitrack Editor and the Noise Reduction effects.

For AI tools, Descript is excellent for automatically trimming silence and transcribing audio. It also lets you edit by simply editing the text, which is a huge time-saver.

As for avoiding burnout, pros set strict boundaries. They schedule dedicated time for recording, editing, and marketing, and they protect their personal time. Even if it's just 30 minutes a day to eat and rest, it's essential. Burnout is real, and taking care of yourself is part of being professional.
