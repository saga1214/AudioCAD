Sure! Here are five green vegetables that are great for your parrot:

1. **Kale** – Packed with vitamins A, C, and K, but give it in moderation due to calcium oxalate.
2. **Spinach** – Rich in iron and calcium, but also contains oxalates, so feed it occasionally.
3. **Broccoli** – High in vitamin C and fiber, but only offer the florets and stems in small amounts.
4. **Zucchini** – Mild and hydrating, great for adding variety to their diet.
5. **Green Beans** – Low in fat and high in fiber, they're a favorite among many parrots.

Always wash veggies thoroughly and offer them raw or lightly steamed. Rotate them to keep your parrot interested and ensure a balanced diet.
