No problem! Here are two quick and tasty drink ideas:

**For the Vodka Mixer:**  
Try a **Vodka Tonic with a twist**—use premium tonic water, add a splash of lime juice, and garnish with a lime wedge and a sprig of rosemary. It’s simple, refreshing, and elegant.

**For the Argentine-Style Fernet:**  
In Argentina, Fernet is often served **"Fernet con coca"**—just mix equal parts Fernet and Coca-Cola. It’s bold, slightly bitter, and very popular. For a more refined touch, serve it over ice in a rocks glass with a lime wedge. It’s quick, classic, and perfect for a relaxed evening.
