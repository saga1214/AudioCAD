Here's a complete **nautical/pirate-themed shopping list** for your son’s beach birthday party, including **easy-to-eat, sand-resistant foods and drinks** that are **fun, safe for kids, and on-theme**. This list assumes **7 kids total** (your son + 6 guests), plus **adults** (you and possibly 1–2 others), so it includes a little extra for everyone.

---

### 🏴‍☠️ **Pirate-Themed Beach Party Shopping List**

#### 🍽️ **Main Food Items (Easy to Eat, No Mess, Sand-Proof)**
- **Mini Sliders or Mini Sandwich Rolls** (12–15 total)  
  → Use turkey, cheese, or peanut butter & jelly (check for allergies).  
  → Cut into quarters or serve whole.  
  → *Theme idea:* "Pirate Bites" or "Treasure Chest Sandwiches"

- **Cheese Cubes or String Cheese** (1 pack)  
  → Individually wrapped = no mess, easy to eat.  
  → *Theme idea:* "Pirate Cheese Gems"

- **Grapes** (1 large bag)  
  → Washed and prepped.  
  → *Theme idea:* "Pirate Jewels"

- **Apple Slices** (1 large bag or pre-cut)  
  → Optional: dip in lemon juice to prevent browning.  
  → *Theme idea:* "Golden Apples of the Sea"

- **Goldfish Crackers** (1 large box)  
  → Classic, no mess, and the color fits the theme.  
  → *Theme idea:* "Pirate Gold"

- **Pretzel Rods** (1 bag)  
  → Great for holding, fun to eat.  
  → *Theme idea:* "Pirate Ship Masts"

- **Veggie Sticks** (carrots, cucumbers, bell peppers)  
  → Pre-cut and pack in small containers.  
  → *Theme idea:* "Treasure Garden Snacks"

- **Mini Fruit Cups** (6–8 small cups)  
  → Choose fruit like pineapple, peaches, or mixed fruit (no watermelon).  
  → *Theme idea:* "Tropical Treasure Cups"

- **Pirate-Themed Snack Mix** (optional)  
  → Mix pretzels, goldfish, raisins, and mini marshmallows.  
  → Label as "Pirate's Loot Mix"

---

#### 🥤 **Drinks (Individual, Spill-Resistant)**
- **Juice Boxes** (8–10 total)  
  → Choose kid-friendly flavors (apple, tropical, etc.).  
  → *Theme idea:* "Pirate Punch" or "Sea Water"

- **Water Bottles** (8–10 small bottles)  
  → For hydration at the beach.

- **Lemonade or Iced Tea (optional)**  
  → If you want to serve in a large pitcher, use a **leak-proof cooler** with ice.  
  → Serve in **small cups** or **plastic cups with lids**.

---

#### 🎂 **Cake (Already Being Made)**
- **Pirate-Themed Cake or Cupcakes**  
  → Consider a pirate ship, treasure chest, or skull & crossbones design.

---

#### 🧺 **Extras & Supplies**
- **Small Paper Plates & Napkins** (10–12)  
  → Pirate-themed or plain white (easier to clean).

- **Plastic Forks & Spoons** (10–12)  
  → For sliders, fruit, etc.

- **Small Containers or Snack Bags** (for veggie sticks, cheese cubes, etc.)  
  → Helps keep food clean and sand-free.

- **Cooler with Ice**  
  → To keep drinks and perishables cold.

- **Pirate-Themed Tablecloth or Towels** (optional)  
  → Adds to the fun!

---

### 🧭 **Theme Tips to Keep the Vibe Going**
- Use **gold and black** or **red and black** for food labels or signs.
- Label food with fun pirate names:  
  - “Treasure Chest Sandwiches”  
  - “Pirate Jewels (Grapes)”  
  - “Pirate Gold (Goldfish)”  
  - “Sea Water (Juice)”  
- Let kids wear **pirate hats or eye patches** for fun.
