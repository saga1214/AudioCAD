That sounds like a fun and romantic evening! Here are some ocean-themed meal and dessert ideas to match your theme:

**Ocean-Themed Meals:**

1. **Lobster Tails with Garlic Butter** – A classic seafood favorite that feels luxurious and perfect for a special night.
2. **Grilled Shrimp Skewers with Mango Salsa** – Juicy shrimp with a tropical twist that ties in with the ocean vibe.
3. **Seafood Paella** – A colorful Spanish dish with shrimp, mussels, and saffron rice that’s both impressive and delicious.
4. **Baked Cod with Lemon-Dill Sauce** – Light, flaky, and elegant, this dish feels fresh and ocean-inspired.
5. **Shrimp and Grits** – A Southern favorite with a coastal twist—creamy grits topped with spicy shrimp and bacon.

**Ocean-Themed Desserts:**

1. **Coconut Cream Pie** – Creamy, tropical, and perfect for a beachy feel.
2. **Mango Sorbet** – Light, refreshing, and naturally sweet—great for a summer night.
3. **Chocolate-Dipped Strawberries with Sea Salt** – Simple but elegant, with a hint of oceanic flavor from the salt.
4. **Blueberry and Lavender Crumble** – The blue color evokes the ocean, and the lavender adds a unique floral note.
5. **Tiramisu with a Sea Salt Caramel Drizzle** – Rich and indulgent, with a salty-sweet finish that feels ocean-inspired.

You could even serve them on seashell plates or with blue tablecloths to really bring the theme to life! Enjoy your ocean-themed night! 🌊🦐
