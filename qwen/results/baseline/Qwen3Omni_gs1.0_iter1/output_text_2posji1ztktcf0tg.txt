That sounds like a fun celebration! Since she’s a vegetarian, you’ll want something delicious and satisfying that doesn’t rely on meat. Here are a few ideas that are both impressive and easy to make:

1. **Eggplant Parmesan** – A classic vegetarian dish that’s rich, cheesy, and comforting. You can make it ahead and reheat for a stress-free dinner.

2. **Creamy Mushroom Risotto** – It’s elegant, creamy, and feels special. Add a splash of white wine and fresh herbs for extra flavor.

3. **Stuffed Bell Peppers** – Fill them with quinoa, black beans, corn, and spices. Top with cheese and bake. It’s colorful and satisfying.

4. **Vegetarian Lasagna** – Use layers of tofu ricotta, sautéed spinach, mushrooms, and marinara. It’s hearty and feeds a crowd.

5. **Pasta Primavera** – Toss whole wheat pasta with seasonal veggies, olive oil, garlic, and Parmesan. Simple but fresh and vibrant.

And if you want to go all out, serve it with garlic bread and a nice salad. She’ll love that you put thought into her favorite diet while still making it feel like a real celebration.
