Yes, you can absolutely buy a horse in Arizona without any special state permit—no special license is required just to own one. However, there are a few important things to keep in mind:

1. **Local Zoning Laws**: This is the most important part. While Arizona state law doesn’t restrict horse ownership, your city or county might. In Phoenix and surrounding areas like Scottsdale or Chandler, many neighborhoods have zoning rules that limit livestock, including horses. You’ll need to check with the local city planning department to see if your property allows horses. Some areas require a minimum lot size—often half an acre or more—and may require a special use permit.

2. **Property Size**: If you're looking for a house with a big backyard, you’ll likely need to look in the suburbs or outlying areas like Fountain Hills, Apache Junction, or Queen Creek, where larger properties are more common.

3. **Horse Facilities**: Even if zoning allows it, you’ll need to have proper fencing, shelter, and access to water. Most people with horses also build a small barn or run-in shed.

4. **Neighborhood Rules**: If you're buying in a homeowners association (HOA), they may have additional rules about animals, even if local zoning allows them.

So, to answer your question directly: Yes, you can buy a horse in Arizona, but you must first confirm that your property is zoned for livestock or that you can get a special use permit. It’s a good idea to check with the city planning office before you buy the house.
