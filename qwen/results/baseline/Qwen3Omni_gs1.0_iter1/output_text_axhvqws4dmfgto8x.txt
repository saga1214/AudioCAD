You've got this! The key to perfect noodles is all about water and timing.

First, use a big pot with plenty of water—like, at least 4 to 6 quarts. The more water, the better the noodles can move around and cook evenly.

Bring the water to a full, rolling boil before adding the noodles. Then stir them right away to keep them from sticking together.

And here's the big one: don't leave them unattended. Set a timer for the package instructions, and stir occasionally. Once they're done, drain them immediately and rinse with cold water if you're not using them right away.

You'll be surprised how easy it is once you get the rhythm.
