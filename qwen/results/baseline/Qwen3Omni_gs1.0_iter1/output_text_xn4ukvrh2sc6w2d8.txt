### 1. **Old Fashioned** *(Classic, Kentucky-style, perfect for fall)*  
**Glass:** Rocks glass  
**Garnish:** Orange twist and/or cherry  

**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz bourbon (Kentucky bourbon recommended)  
- 1 sugar cube (or 1/2 oz simple syrup)  
- 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters  
- Ice (large cube or crushed)  
- Orange twist (for garnish)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Place sugar cube in glass.  
2. Add bitters.  
3. Muddle gently until sugar dissolves.  
4. Add ice.  
5. Pour bourbon over ice.  
6. Stir gently for 20–30 seconds.  
7. Garnish with orange twist.  

> *Optional:* Add a cherry for a traditional touch.

### 2. **Paper Plane** *(Modern classic, elegant, balanced)*  
**Glass:** Coupe or Nick & Nora  
**Garnish:** Orange twist  

**Ingredients:**  
- 3/4 oz bourbon  
- 3/4 oz Aperol  
- 3/4 oz Amaro Nonino  
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds.  
3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.  
4. Garnish with orange twist.  

> *Note:* This cocktail is named after the paper airplane because it’s a “throwback” to classic cocktails with a modern twist.

---

### 3. **Whiskey Sour** *(Classic, with egg white option)*  
**Glass:** Rocks glass  
**Garnish:** Cherry and/or orange slice  

**Ingredients (with egg white):**  
- 2 oz bourbon  
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 1/2 oz simple syrup  
- 1/2 oz egg white (or 1/2 oz aquafaba for vegan option)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add bourbon, lemon juice, simple syrup, and egg white to a shaker.  
2. Dry shake (no ice) for 10–15 seconds to emulsify the egg white.  
3. Add ice and shake again for 10–15 seconds.  
4. Double strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.  
5. Garnish with cherry and orange slice.  

> *Without egg white:* Skip the egg white and dry shake step. Shake with ice only and strain. Texture will be thinner but still delicious.

### 4. **Aviation** *(Vintage, elegant, perfect for fall)*  
**Glass:** Cocktail glass  
**Garnish:** Maraschino cherry  

**Ingredients:**  
- 2 oz gin  
- 1/2 oz maraschino liqueur  
- 1/4 oz crème de violette  
- 1/2 oz fresh lemon juice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds.  
3. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.  
4. Garnish with a cherry.  

> *Note:* Crème de violette gives the drink its signature pale lavender hue. If unavailable, substitute with a few drops of blue curaçao (but color will be different).

---

### 5. **French 75** *(Sparkling, celebratory, Art Deco vibe)*  
**Glass:** Champagne flute  
**Garnish:** Lemon twist  

**Ingredients:**  
- 1 oz gin  
- 1/2 oz fresh lemon juice  
- 1/2 oz simple syrup  
- 2 oz champagne or sparkling wine  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake briefly (5–7 seconds).  
3. Strain into a chilled champagne flute.  
4. Top with champagne.  
5. Garnish with lemon twist.  

> *Tip:* This is a great “elevated” cocktail that feels festive and fits the vintage airport theme.
