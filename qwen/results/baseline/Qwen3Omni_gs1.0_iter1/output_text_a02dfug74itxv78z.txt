2. **Ford Mustang Shelby GT500 (2020–2023) – V8**
   - **Engine**: 5.2L supercharged V8 (760+ hp)
   - **Why it fits**: A true American muscle car with supercar performance. It has modern tech, CarPlay, and a track-focused setup. It's not a traditional "sports car" but it's incredibly fast and fun.
   - **Price**: $80,000–$100,000 (used models under $90k are available)

3. **Porsche 911 Carrera S (2020–2023) – Flat-6**
   - **Engine**: 3.0L twin-turbo flat-six (440–480 hp)
   - **Why it fits**: While not a V8/V10, it's a legendary sports car with incredible reliability and modern tech. It's more of a GT car than a pure supercar, but it's a benchmark in the segment.
   - **Price**: $90,000–$110,000 (used models under $90k are rare but possible)

4. **Audi R8 V10 Plus (2015–2020) – V10**
   - **Engine**: 5.2L naturally aspirated V10 (610 hp)
   - **Why it fits**: One of the most iconic V10 supercars ever made. It's got a screaming engine, all-wheel drive, and modern tech. Audi's build quality is solid, and the V10 is known for being durable.
   - **Price**: $100,000–$130,000 new, but used models can dip under $90k depending on mileage and condition.

5. **Lamborghini Huracán LP610-4 (2014–2020) – V10**
