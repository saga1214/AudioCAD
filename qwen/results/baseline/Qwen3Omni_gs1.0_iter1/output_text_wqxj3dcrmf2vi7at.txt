Of course! Here are some fun and safe treat ideas for your little Corgi princess:

**Homemade Treats:**
1. **Pumpkin & Peanut Butter Bites** – Mix canned pumpkin (no sugar), plain yogurt, and a little peanut butter (xylitol-free!). Roll into small balls and freeze.
2. **Chicken & Sweet Potato Puffs** – Cooked chicken and mashed sweet potato, rolled into tiny balls and baked at low heat.
3. **Oat & Banana Cookies** – Blend oats, mashed banana, and egg. Drop spoonfuls on a baking sheet and bake until firm.

**Store-Bought Options:**
- **Blue Buffalo Blue Bits** – Small, soft, and great for training.
- **Wellness Soft Puppy Bites** – Gentle on digestion and tasty.
- **Zuke’s Mini Naturals** – Perfect for small dogs and great for training.
- **Greenies Dental Chews** – Helps with teeth and fresh breath.

Always keep treats small—no more than 10% of her daily calories—and avoid anything with artificial flavors or fillers. Your princess will love them either way!
