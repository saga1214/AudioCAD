Sure thing! Here are the two shrimp dishes I mentioned earlier:

1. **Spicy Shrimp Scampi**  
   - Ingredients: Shrimp, linguine or other pasta, garlic, butter, olive oil, red pepper flakes, lemon juice, parsley, salt, and pepper.  
   - How it works: Sauté garlic and red pepper flakes in butter and oil, add cooked shrimp, toss with pasta, and finish with lemon juice and herbs.

2. **Shrimp Fra Diavolo**  
   - Ingredients: Shrimp, crushed tomatoes, garlic, onion, red pepper flakes, olive oil, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, and pasta (like penne or spaghetti).  
   - How it works: Sauté garlic and onion, add red pepper flakes and tomatoes, simmer, then toss with cooked shrimp and pasta.

Both are quick, flavorful, and pack a spicy punch—perfect for a comforting meal!
