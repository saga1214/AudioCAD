Absolutely! Based on your criteria—easy to train, good with other dogs and children, calm temperament, low prey drive (won’t chase cats or squirrels), and preferably a purebred or mix of known breeds—here are some excellent options:

### **Purebred Dogs:**

1. **Golden Retriever**
   - **Temperament:** Friendly, gentle, patient, and highly trainable.
   - **With Kids & Pets:** Excellent with children and gets along well with other animals.
   - **Energy Level:** Moderate to high, but can adapt to apartment life with enough exercise.
   - **Trainability:** Very high—often used as service dogs.

2. **Labrador Retriever**
   - **Temperament:** Outgoing, loyal, and eager to please.
   - **With Kids & Pets:** Great with families and other pets.
   - **Energy Level:** High, but can be managed with routine walks and play.
   - **Trainability:** One of the most trainable breeds.

3. **Poodle (Standard or Miniature)**
   - **Temperament:** Intelligent, alert, and affectionate.
   - **With Kids & Pets:** Good with families and other animals.
   - **Energy Level:** Moderate; enjoys mental stimulation.
   - **Trainability:** Extremely high—often ranks among the smartest breeds.

4. **Bichon Frise**
   - **Temperament:** Cheerful, playful, and affectionate.
   - **With Kids & Pets:** Good with children and other pets.
   - **Energy Level:** Moderate; loves to play but also enjoys lounging.
   - **Trainability:** Smart and eager to please.

5. **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel**
   - **Temperament:** Gentle, affectionate, and calm.
   - **With Kids & Pets:** Excellent with children and other animals.
   - **Energy Level:** Low to moderate; loves cuddles and short walks.
   - **Trainability:** Easy to train with positive reinforcement.

6. **Bernese Mountain Dog**
   - **Temperament:** Calm, loyal, and gentle.
   - **With Kids & Pets:** Excellent with families and other animals.
   - **Energy Level:** Moderate; enjoys outdoor time but is also a couch potato.
   - **Trainability:** Good, though can be stubborn at times.

---

### **Mixes (Designer or Mixed-Breed Dogs):**

1. **Labradoodle (Labrador Retriever + Poodle)**
   - **Temperament:** Friendly, intelligent, and playful.
   - **With Kids & Pets:** Great with children and other animals.
   - **Energy Level:** Moderate to high.
   - **Trainability:** Very high—inherits intelligence from both parents.

2. **Goldendoodle (Golden Retriever + Poodle)**
   - **Temperament:** Gentle, affectionate, and outgoing.
   - **With Kids & Pets:** Excellent with families and other pets.
   - **Energy Level:** Moderate; loves to play but also relaxes.
   - **Trainability:** Very high.

3. **Pomeranian Mix (e.g., Pomeranian + Bichon Frise)**
   - **Temperament:** Alert, friendly, and lively.
   - **With Kids & Pets:** Good with families if socialized early.
   - **Energy Level:** Moderate; loves to play.
   - **Trainability:** Smart and eager to please.

4. **Sheltie Mix (Shetland Sheepdog + Poodle or Bichon)**
   - **Temperament:** Intelligent, loyal, and calm.
   - **With Kids & Pets:** Good with children and other animals.
   - **Energy Level:** Moderate; enjoys mental stimulation.
   - **Trainability:** High.

---

### **Tips for Choosing:**
- **Look for temperament:** Visit shelters or breeders and spend time with the dog to see how it interacts with people and other animals.
- **Consider size:** Since you live in a high-rise, avoid very large breeds unless you have space and time for exercise.
- **Adopt from shelters:** Many rescue dogs are already trained and have known temperaments.
- **Ask about prey drive:** When meeting a dog, ask if it has a history of chasing small animals.

These breeds and mixes are known for being family-friendly, trainable, and calm—perfect for your lifestyle and your spouse’s “chill dog” preference.
