Sure thing! Based on your climate and beginner-friendly options, here are the vegetables and herbs I’d recommend for your fall and winter garden in St. Pete, Florida:

**Herbs:**
- Basil (great in fall, but can struggle in summer heat)
- Mint (thrives in Florida, but keep it contained)
- Parsley (prefers cooler temps, perfect for fall/winter)
- Cilantro (grows well in cooler months, bolts in heat)

**Leafy Greens:**
- Lettuce (many varieties—butterhead, romaine, loose leaf)
- Spinach (loves cooler weather)
- Arugula (fast-growing, peppery flavor)
- Kale (very hardy, grows well in winter)

**Root Vegetables:**
- Radishes (grow in 3–4 weeks, love cool weather)
- Carrots (plant in fall for harvest in winter)
- Beets (great for cooler months)

**Other Cool-Season Favorites:**
- Green onions (plant seeds or sets, grow fast)
- Swiss chard (tolerant of heat but loves cooler temps)
- Collard greens (very hardy, great for Florida winters)

**Fruiting Plants (for fall planting):**
- Tomatoes (start with transplants in fall for winter harvest)
- Peppers (jalapeños, bell peppers—plant in fall for winter harvest)
- Eggplant (prefers warmth, but fall planting works well)

**Bonus:**
- Bush beans (plant in fall for winter harvest)
- Okra (if you want a summer crop, but it’s heat-loving)

Since you’re in Zone 10B, you can actually grow a lot of these year-round, but fall and winter are ideal for most of them. You can even do succession planting—just keep sowing seeds every few weeks to keep harvesting.
