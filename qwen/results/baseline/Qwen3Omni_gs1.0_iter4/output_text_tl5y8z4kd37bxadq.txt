For a laid-back first-night dinner in Austin, I’d recommend **Franklin Barbecue** if you're up for a bit of a wait—legendary smoked brisket, but it's worth it. If you want something more relaxed and local, **Torchy’s Tacos** is a must-try. It's casual, fun, and has amazing tacos with a cool vibe. Or if you're near South Congress, **The Violet Hour** has a great outdoor patio and a relaxed atmosphere with tasty small plates. All of them are easygoing and perfect for unwinding after travel.
