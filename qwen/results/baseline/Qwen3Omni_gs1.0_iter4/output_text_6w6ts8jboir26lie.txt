That's a great plan. Cooking is such a wonderful way to reclaim your peace and take back control after a stressful situation.

Since you're in a beach area, I'd suggest something light and fresh that celebrates the location. Here are a few ideas:

1. **Grilled Fish Tacos**: Super easy and delicious. You can buy fresh fish fillets or even pre-cooked shrimp from the grocery store. Grill or pan-sear it, then serve in warm tortillas with cabbage slaw, a squeeze of lime, and a little salsa. It's beachy, healthy, and satisfying.

2. **Pasta with Seafood and Lemon**: Use whatever fresh or frozen seafood you can find—shrimp, scallops, or even canned tuna. Toss it with cooked pasta, olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, and fresh herbs like parsley or basil. It's quick, elegant, and feels like a treat.

3. **Grilled Veggie & Halloumi Skewers**: If you're not into meat, this is a great option. Skewer bell peppers, zucchini, cherry tomatoes, and halloumi cheese (it holds up well to grilling). Brush with olive oil and grill until charred. Serve with a side of couscous or a simple salad.

4. **One-Pot Lemon Herb Rice**: Minimal cleanup, maximum flavor. Sauté garlic in olive oil, add rice, broth, lemon juice, and herbs. Cover and simmer. It's comforting and lets you focus on the ocean view.

And hey, if you want to go all out, make a cocktail too—something like a classic mojito or a beachside margarita. It'll make the whole experience feel like a real vacation.

You've got this. Cooking is your therapy, and you deserve to enjoy it.
