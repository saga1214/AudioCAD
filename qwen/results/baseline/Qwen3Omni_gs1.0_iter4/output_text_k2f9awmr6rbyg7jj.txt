Sure! Here's a simple way to make a vegetarian sandwich using artificial crab meat (also known as imitation crab or surimi):

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 cup artificial crab meat (check label to ensure it's vegetarian)
- 1 tbsp vegan mayo (or regular mayo if you eat dairy)
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp onion powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 slices of bread (whole wheat, sourdough, or your favorite)
- Lettuce, tomato, or avocado (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. In a bowl, mix the artificial crab meat with vegan mayo, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
2. Toast the bread if desired.
3. Layer lettuce and tomato on one slice.
4. Spread the crab mixture evenly on the other slice.
5. Top with the other slice and press gently.
6. Cut in half and enjoy!

Note: Some artificial crab meat contains fish, so always check the label to make sure it's vegetarian.
