Great question! For a ukulele, you'll want to tune it to the standard GCEA tuning.

Here's how to use a tuner:

1. **Turn on your tuner app or device.**
2. **Select the correct instrument.** If you're using a tuner app, choose "Ukulele" or "GCEA" tuning.
3. **Play each string one at a time.** Start with the G string (the thickest string), then C, E, and A (the thinnest string).
4. **Watch the tuner.** It will show you if the note is flat (too low), sharp (too high), or in tune. Adjust the tuning pegs until the tuner shows the note is in tune.

You can also use a reference pitch from a piano or a tuning fork, but a tuner is the easiest way to get it just right.
