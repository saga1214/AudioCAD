That sounds like a fun setup! With your new coffee table and a vending machine nearby, you’ve got a snack station waiting to happen. Here are some tasty and varied snack ideas to fill it with:

**For the drawer:**
- **Chocolates:** Mini chocolate bars, chocolate-covered pretzels, or fun-sized candy bars (like Snickers or Twix).
- **Candies:** Gummy bears, sour patch kids, or hard candies for variety.
- **Nuts:** Trail mix or single-serve bags of almonds or cashews for a savory crunch.
- **Crackers:** Cheese crackers or pretzels for something salty.

**For the shelves:**
- **Snack bags:** Popcorn, potato chips, or veggie straws.
- **Fruit snacks:** Fruit roll-ups or gummy fruit snacks for a slightly healthier option.
- **Cookies:** Mini cookies or sandwich cookies like Oreos.
- **Energy bites:** Homemade or store-bought granola bites for a grab-and-go option.

**Bonus idea:** Add a small chalkboard or label maker to mark what’s inside each section—like “Sweet Treats,” “Salty Snacks,” or “On-the-Go.” It’ll make your table look organized and fun!

And hey, if you ever want to go full-on snack bar, you could even add a mini coffee maker or tea station to the table. You’ve got a snack empire in the making!
