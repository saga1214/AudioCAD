Oh, spring is such a wonderful time to get outside and enjoy the renewal of nature! Since you already love painting by the pond, here are a few fun spring activities you might enjoy:

1. **Go for a scenic hike** – With the snow melting and flowers blooming, trails are usually in great shape and full of color.
2. **Have a picnic in the park** – Bring a blanket, some snacks, and enjoy the sunshine. It’s a simple way to relax and enjoy the season.
3. **Try gardening** – Even if it’s just planting some flowers or herbs in pots, spring is the perfect time to get your hands in the dirt.
4. **Visit a local farmers market** – You’ll find fresh produce, baked goods, and seasonal treats. It’s a fun way to support local vendors too.
5. **Go kayaking or paddleboarding** – If the pond or nearby lake is thawed, it’s a great way to enjoy the water and scenery.
6. **Have a spring cleaning and organize your space** – It’s a great way to refresh your surroundings and feel more energized.

And since you love painting, maybe try doing some plein air painting (outdoor painting) as the trees start to bud and the light changes. Spring light is so soft and beautiful for art!
