That's a really common concern, and the good news is that it's not as impossible as it might seem.

While it's true that most traditional publishers prefer to pair a writer with their own illustrator, there are definitely exceptions, especially for debut authors with a strong, unique vision. Some publishers, particularly smaller or more independent ones, are open to receiving a complete manuscript with illustrations.

The key is to research your options carefully. Look for publishers that specifically state they welcome submissions from author-illustrators. You can also consider self-publishing as a way to present your complete vision to the world.
