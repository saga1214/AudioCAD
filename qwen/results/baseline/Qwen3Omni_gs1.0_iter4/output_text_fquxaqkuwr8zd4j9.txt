### ✅ **For the Family Dinner: One More Vegetable Side Dish**

You’re doing:
- Roast chicken
- Roast potatoes
- Roast carrots
- Rolls (already covered)

You need **one more vegetable side dish** that’s:
- Kid-friendly (so not too bitter or weird textures)
- Picky-eater friendly (especially since your mom doesn’t like rice and you have texture issues)
- Easy to make ahead or prep

**Great options:**

1. **Sautéed Green Beans with Garlic and Almonds**  
   - Lightly sauté green beans with olive oil, garlic, and a sprinkle of toasted almonds.  
   - Kids usually like the crunch, and it’s a bright, fresh contrast to the roast.  
   - Skip the bitter greens or anything too leafy.

2. **Roasted Brussels Sprouts (halved, not too small)**  
   - Roast with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a drizzle of maple syrup.  
   - The sweetness helps picky eaters, and kids often like the crispy edges.  
   - Make sure they’re not too charred or bitter.

3. **Buttered Corn (on the cob or kernels)**  
   - Simple, sweet, and universally liked.  
   - You can boil or steam corn and toss with a little butter and salt.  
   - Kids love it, and it’s easy to prep ahead.

4. **Simple Garden Salad (with mild dressing)**  
   - Use iceberg or butter lettuce, cucumber, and cherry tomatoes.  
   - Serve with a light ranch or Italian dressing (check labels for dairy-free if needed).  
   - Keep it simple—no arugula or bitter greens.

👉 **Best pick?** Go with **buttered corn** or **garlic sautéed green beans**—both are safe, easy, and loved by most.

### ✅ **For Your Girlfriends’ Baked Potato Bar: Meat Toppings (and Vegetarian Options)**

You said:
- You want **meat toppings** (so you can offer options like pulled pork, bacon, etc.)
- You also want **non-meat alternatives** for your vegetarian friend
- You don’t want chili (already ruled out)

**Meat Toppings (for non-vegetarians):**

1. **Pulled Pork**  
   - Slow-cooked with barbecue sauce or just seasoned with salt, pepper, and smoked paprika.  
   - Great texture and flavor.

2. **Crispy Bacon Bits**  
   - Cook bacon until crisp, crumble, and serve on top.  
   - Adds crunch and smoky flavor.

3. **Grilled or Roasted Chicken Strips**  
   - Cut into bite-sized pieces, season with herbs and olive oil, and grill or bake.  
   - Mild and kid-friendly.

4. **Italian Sausage Crumbles**  
   - Brown sausage in a pan, break into crumbles, and serve warm.  
   - Adds bold flavor.

**Vegetarian Toppings (for your vegetarian friend):**

1. **BBQ Jackfruit** (as you mentioned—great!)  
   - Shred canned jackfruit in BBQ sauce and simmer.  
   - Texture mimics pulled pork perfectly.

2. **Black Beans or Pinto Beans**  
   - Warm, seasoned with cumin and garlic.  
   - Add a little cheese or sour cream if desired.

3. **Roasted Vegetables**  
   - Bell peppers, onions, zucchini, and mushrooms roasted with olive oil and herbs.  
   - Great texture and flavor.

4. **Veggie Chili (non-beef)**  
   - Use lentils, black beans, tomatoes, and spices.  
   - Serve warm and let people add their own toppings.

5. **Tofu Scramble**  
   - Crumbled tofu sautéed with turmeric, black salt (for eggy flavor), and veggies.  
   - A fun, hearty option.

6. **Guacamole + Salsa + Corn + Black Beans**  
   - A fresh, plant-based combo that’s colorful and satisfying.

👉 **Pro tip:** Label everything clearly! Use little signs like “Meat: Pulled Pork” and “Vegetarian: BBQ Jackfruit” so everyone knows what’s what.
