Absolutely! Since you're going with a **Retro Tech Day**, themed lunches can be a fun way to bring the vibe to the table. Here are two easy, on-theme lunch ideas:

1. **"The Blackberry Blast" Lunch**  
   - **Main**: A wrap or sandwich with blackberries, spinach, goat cheese, and a balsamic glaze (play on the "Blackberry" name).  
   - **Side**: Mini "pixel" fruit cups—cut strawberries and grapes into small squares and arrange them like a retro video game screen.  
   - **Drink**: Blackberry lemonade or a blackberry smoothie.  
   - **Bonus**: Serve it in a lunchbox styled like an old-school PDA or flip phone.

2. **"90s Tech Throwback" Lunch**  
   - **Main**: A "floppy disk" sandwich—cut a round sandwich (like a grilled cheese) into a circle, then use a cookie cutter to make a smaller circle in the center to look like the disk’s hole. Add a paper label with "My First 3.5" Disk" for fun.  
   - **Side**: "Cassette tape" snacks—wrap a string cheese in a paper strip with a cassette label design.  
   - **Drink**: Soda in retro packaging (like Mountain Dew Code Red or a vintage Coke can).  
   - **Bonus**: Use a lunch bag that looks like an old laptop or computer case.

These are simple to prepare, fun to present, and perfectly match the retro tech theme. Plus, they’re a great way to get your team excited and engaged!
