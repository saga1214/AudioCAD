A coastal getaway sounds lovely! For a romantic twist, consider a private boat tour at sunrise. You could even arrange for a small picnic basket with local seafood and wine.

If you're up for something more active, try kayaking together to a secluded cove. It's peaceful and gives you a chance to talk without distractions.

Or, if you're near a harbor, book a sunset sail. Many offer champagne and hors d'oeuvres, and the views are unforgettable.
