Absolutely. Here's a simple, clean format you can follow to create your resume:

1. **Header**  
   Your Name (in a larger font, like 16–18 pt)  
   Phone Number | Email Address | City, State | LinkedIn URL (optional)  
   *(Use a professional email and consider a LinkedIn profile if you have one)*

2. **Professional Summary (3–4 lines)**  
   A brief paragraph highlighting your background, key skills, and career goals. Tailor this to the jobs you're applying for.

3. **Relevant Experience**  
   List your most relevant jobs first. For each:  
   - Job Title  
   - Company Name, Location  
   - Dates (e.g., Jan 2018 – Dec 2022)  
   - 3–5 bullet points describing your achievements using action verbs and results (e.g., "Increased sales by 25% through targeted outreach campaigns")

4. **Additional Work History**  
   List your other jobs briefly:  
   - Job Title  
   - Company Name, Location  
   - Dates  
   *(No bullet points needed here—just the basics)*

5. **Education**  
   - Degree Name (e.g., Bachelor of Science in Business Administration)  
   - University Name, Location  
   - Graduation Year (or "Expected 2025" if still in school)  
   - Add honors or awards if applicable

6. **Skills**  
   A short list of relevant hard and soft skills (e.g., Project Management, Microsoft Excel, Communication, Adobe Creative Suite)

7. **Optional Sections (if applicable)**  
   - Certifications  
   - Volunteer Experience  
   - Languages  
   - Projects  

**Final Tips:**  
- Keep it to one page if possible (especially if you're early in your career).  
- Use consistent formatting (same fonts, bullet styles, spacing).  
- Save as a PDF unless otherwise requested.  

This format is clean, ATS-friendly, and easy to customize for different jobs.
