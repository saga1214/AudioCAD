No worries at all — brain fog is super common with the flu. Let me go through everything slowly and clearly for you.

**For your cough:**
- Try a spoonful of **honey** (especially in warm tea or water) — it coats the throat and helps reduce coughing.
- **Gargle with warm salt water** — this can soothe irritation in your throat.
- Use a **humidifier** or take a **hot shower** to breathe in moist air — this helps loosen mucus and calm coughing.
- **Prop your head up** with extra pillows when you sleep — this helps prevent post-nasal drip from triggering coughing at night.

**For brain fog:**
- **Rest** is the number one thing — your brain needs energy to heal.
- Stay **hydrated** — dehydration can make brain fog worse.
- Eat **light, nutritious foods** if you can — like broth, toast, or bananas — to give your body fuel.
- **Avoid screens** when possible — too much blue light and mental effort can worsen fog.
- **Take it slow** — don’t push yourself to do too much. Your brain is recovering.

And remember — this is temporary. Most people start feeling better within 7–10 days. You're doing everything right by resting and listening to your body.

You've got this.
