When it comes to sedans, the top three brands that are typically known for them are:

1. **Toyota** – Famous for reliability, fuel efficiency, and long-term value. Models like the Camry are best-sellers in the midsize sedan category.

2. **Honda** – Known for excellent build quality, smooth ride, and strong performance. The Accord is a top contender in the midsize sedan market.

3. **BMW** – While more luxury-oriented, BMW is renowned for its sporty driving dynamics and premium features in sedans like the 3 Series and 5 Series.

These brands represent a mix of practicality, performance, and reliability, making them go-to choices for sedan buyers.
