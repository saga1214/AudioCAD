Great question. Since you're only there for a month and need to move them easily, you're in luck—both succulents and the bird's nest snake plant are very lightweight.

Succulents in small pots (like 4–6 inches) usually weigh less than 2 pounds total, including the pot and soil. You can easily carry a few in one hand.

The bird's nest snake plant in a similar-sized pot will be a bit heavier, maybe 3–5 pounds, but still very manageable for a short trip up five flights. It's mostly the pot and soil that add weight, not the plant itself.

Since you're only staying a month, you could even consider buying them in small pots and bringing them back with you. They're hardy and can survive a short trip home.
