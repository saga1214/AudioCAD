That sounds like a wonderful family adventure!

Since you're looking for warm weather, swimming, and a relaxing vibe, I'd recommend **Santa Barbara** or **Monterey** as two fantastic options for your family vacation.

**Santa Barbara** is often called the "American Riviera" — it's sunny, beautiful, and has a relaxed vibe. You can swim at the beach, walk along the pier, and enjoy great food. There are plenty of family-friendly hotels, and many restaurants are very accommodating for gluten-free diets.

**Monterey** is another great choice — it's a bit more coastal and nature-focused. You can visit the Monterey Bay Aquarium (which is amazing for kids), go on a whale watching tour, and enjoy the scenic coastal drive. There are also many gluten-free options at restaurants in Monterey and Carmel.

For accommodations, look for hotels or vacation rentals with kitchens so you can prepare safe meals. And don't forget to pack sunscreen, swimwear, and light layers for the evenings.

I hope you all have a wonderful time together!
