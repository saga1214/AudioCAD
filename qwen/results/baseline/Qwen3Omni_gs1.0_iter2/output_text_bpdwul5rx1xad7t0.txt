That sounds like an amazing adventure! Here are five incredible road trip options from Vancouver, each with unique highlights and must-try experiences:

1. **Whistler & Squamish (1.5–2 hours)**  
   *Must-try:* Ride the Sea to Sky Gondola for breathtaking views, hike the Shannon Falls trail, and enjoy a fancy dinner at the **Bistro 1882** in Whistler Village. Don’t miss the **Whistler Blackcomb** mountain scenery — it’s stunning year-round.

2. **Vancouver Island (via BC Ferries to Nanaimo, ~1.5 hours)**  
   *Must-try:* Drive the **Pacific Rim Highway** to Tofino for wild Pacific coast views. Visit **Long Beach** for a beach walk, and enjoy fresh seafood at **The Tofino Fish Company**. For a luxury experience, stay at **The Tofino Resort + Marina**.

3. **Okanagan Valley (4–5 hours)**  
   *Must-try:* Explore **Kelowna** and **Penticton** for wine tasting at local vineyards like **Mission Hill**. Visit **Okanagan Lake** for swimming or kayaking. For a fancy dinner, try **The Farm at Southview** in Kelowna.

4. **Kootenays & Nelson (4–5 hours)**  
   *Must-try:* Drive through **Rogers Pass** for jaw-dropping mountain views. Visit **Nelson** for its artsy vibe and enjoy a meal at **The Nelson House**. Take a scenic boat ride on **Kootenay Lake**.

5. **Journey to the Rockies (via Golden, ~3 hours)**  
   *Must-try:* Drive the **Icefields Parkway** (if you're up for a longer trip) for glacier views and wildlife spotting. Visit **Yoho National Park** and **Lake Louise**. For a luxury stay, book a room at **Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise**.

These trips offer a mix of nature, culture, and fine dining — perfect for exploring B.C. with your family and furry friend!
