For treats, look for small, soft ones that are easy for tiny mouths to chew. Chicken, beef, or liver-flavored treats are usually a hit with puppies. You can also try freeze-dried liver or small pieces of cooked chicken as a natural option. Just make sure they’re safe and not too salty or spicy.

Clicker training is a method that uses a small device that makes a “click” sound to mark the exact moment your dog does the right thing. For example, when he sits, you click and then give a treat. The click tells him, “That’s exactly what I wanted!” It’s super effective because it’s instant and clear. You can use it for basic commands like sit, stay, or come. Just be sure to pair every click with a treat so he learns the connection.
