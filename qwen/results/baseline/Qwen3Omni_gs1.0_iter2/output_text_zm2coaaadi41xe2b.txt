Great question—let’s break it down:

**For Asian Noodles in Dallas:**

1. **Sushi Tei (Plano)** – Known for their ramen and udon, especially the spicy miso ramen. Great quality and atmosphere.
2. **Noodle House (Downtown Dallas)** – A local favorite for Chinese-style noodles, especially their dan dan noodles and beef chow mein.
3. **Korean BBQ Noodle House (Irving)** – Offers Korean-style ramen and spicy cold noodles. Their spicy pork ramen is a must-try.
4. **Sakura Ramen (Addison)** – Authentic Japanese ramen with rich broths and handmade noodles. Their shoyu ramen is top-tier.
5. **Pho 79 (Multiple Locations)** – If you're in the mood for Vietnamese, their pho is excellent and packed with protein.

**For Protein Bars (Great for Voice Actors on the Go):**

1. **RXBAR** – Clean ingredients, no added sugar. Great for sustained energy.
2. **Quest Bar** – High protein, low sugar. Good for muscle recovery after long recording sessions.
3. **LÄRABAR** – Simple ingredients, good for a quick snack. Not as high in protein, but great if you want something natural.
4. **Clif Builder’s Bar** – High in protein and carbs, good for post-workout or long days.
5. **RXBAR Protein Crunch** – A newer option with added crunch and protein.

Pro tip: Keep a few protein bars in your studio bag for quick energy between takes or during long voice sessions.
