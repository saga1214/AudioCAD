Ah, I see what you're asking. If you're trying to remove something stuck to your jacket—like tape, glue, or a label—without damaging the lining, here are a few safe methods:

1. **Use a hairdryer on low heat**: Gently warm the sticky area. This softens the adhesive, making it easier to peel off slowly. Always test on a small, hidden area first.

2. **Try rubbing alcohol or hand sanitizer**: Dab a little on a cotton ball and gently rub the sticky spot. It dissolves many adhesives. Again, test first to make sure it doesn't damage the fabric.

3. **Use peanut butter or cooking oil**: Yes, really! Apply a small amount to the sticky area, let it sit for a few minutes, then wipe away. The oils help break down the glue.

4. **Use a plastic scraper**: A credit card or spatula can help gently lift the adhesive without scratching or tearing.

Always work slowly and test in an inconspicuous spot first. And if it's a delicate lining, you might want to take it to a professional cleaner to avoid damage.
