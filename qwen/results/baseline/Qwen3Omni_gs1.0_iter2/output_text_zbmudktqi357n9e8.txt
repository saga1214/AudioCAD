That sounds like a fantastic plan—getting out there and soaking in some real-life inspiration is one of the best ways to improve your art! As for when to go to Mexico, it really depends on what kind of experience you're after.

If you're looking for warm weather and beach vibes, the best time is typically **November to April**. This is the dry season, with sunny days and lower humidity—perfect for painting outdoors. December to February is especially nice, though it can get crowded.

If you're more into cultural experiences, **late October to early November** is great for festivals like Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead), which offers incredible visual inspiration.

And if you're after fewer crowds and lower prices, consider **May to June** or **September to October**—the weather is still warm, and you’ll have more space to explore.

Just make sure to pack your watercolors and a good sketchbook—you’ll want to capture all those vibrant colors and scenes!
