For chicks, you'll want a soft, absorbent, and non-toxic bedding. The best options are:

1. **Paper-based bedding** – like recycled paper pulp or shredded newspaper. It's safe, absorbent, and doesn't pose a risk of impaction if they peck at it. Avoid glossy or colored paper, though.

2. **Pine shavings** – if you're using them, make sure they're **large-flake** and **untreated**. Avoid fine sawdust, which can cause respiratory issues.

3. **Straw** – only if it's very clean and dry. But since you're concerned about rodents, paper-based bedding is safer.

Avoid cedar shavings—they're toxic to chicks. Also, never use anything with chemicals or dyes.

Keep the bedding dry and clean, and change it regularly to prevent ammonia buildup and keep the chicks healthy.
