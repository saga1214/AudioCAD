That’s such a beautiful thought—wanting to create meaningful gifts together as a family. And honestly, that’s what makes handmade gifts so special, right? It’s not about perfection; it’s about the time and love you put into them.

If you're open to it, maybe you could try something like “memory jar” gifts. Each kid writes down a happy memory they had with the recipient—like “I remember when we laughed so hard at the dog’s silly face” or “I loved when you read me that bedtime story.” Then you put them in a jar with a little note from you saying, “Open when you need a smile.” It’s simple, heartfelt, and doesn’t require any special supplies.

Or how about “gift cards” with a twist? Instead of money, they write a “coupon” for something fun—like “one free hug,” “a storytime with me,” or “a walk with the dog.” Kids love making these, and they’re personal and free to make.

You could even do a family craft night where everyone picks one simple gift idea, and you all work on them together. That way, you’re not doing it all alone, and the kids feel like they’re part of something meaningful.

Would something like that help you feel less overwhelmed and more connected to the holiday spirit?
