Absolutely! Here's a complete shopping list for your Asian-inspired three-course meal, keeping in mind your husband's allergies (legumes) and preferences (no seafood). All ingredients are easy to find and the meal is delicious and satisfying.

---

### 🛒 **Shopping List**

#### **Produce**
- 1 ripe mango (for sticky rice and/or garnish)
- 1 cucumber (for salad)
- 1 small head of bok choy or baby bok choy (for stir-fry)
- 1 red bell pepper (for stir-fry)
- 1 small zucchini (for stir-fry)
- 1 small onion (for stir-fry)
- 1 clove garlic
- 1-inch piece ginger
- 1 lime (for salad dressing)
- 1 small bunch cilantro (optional, for garnish)

#### **Proteins**
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breast (cut into bite-sized pieces)

#### **Grains & Staples**
- 1 cup glutinous rice (also called sticky rice or sweet rice) – *essential for mango sticky rice*
- 1 cup jasmine rice (for main course)

#### **Dairy & Alternatives**
- 1 can full-fat coconut milk (for both mango sticky rice and stir-fry sauce)
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or whipping cream (for matcha ice cream)
- 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk (for mango sticky rice and ice cream)

#### **Condiments & Seasonings**
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup (for teriyaki sauce)
- 1 tsp cornstarch (for thickening sauce)
- 1 tsp matcha green tea powder (for ice cream)
- 1 tsp sugar (or to taste)
- Salt and pepper (to taste)
- Sesame seeds (for garnish)

#### **Optional Additions**
- 1 small bag of pre-washed salad greens (if you want a more substantial salad)
- 1 small bag of frozen edamame (if you want to include legumes later, but skip if avoiding legumes entirely)

---

### ✅ Notes:
- **No legumes**: All ingredients listed are legume-free.
- **No seafood**: Chicken is used as the protein.
- **Gluten-free option**: Use tamari instead of soy sauce.
- **Make ahead**: You can soak the sticky rice overnight and cook it in advance. The teriyaki sauce can also be made ahead.

---

### 🍽️ Meal Prep Tips:
1. **Start with rice**: Soak sticky rice for 4–6 hours or overnight. Steam it while preparing other components.
2. **Make teriyaki sauce**: Combine soy sauce, honey, garlic, ginger, and cornstarch slurry. Simmer until thickened.
3. **Stir-fry**: Cook chicken first, then add veggies. Toss in sauce at the end.
4. **Dessert**: Make ice cream or mango sticky rice while the main course is cooking.
