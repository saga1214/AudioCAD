Of course. Here’s a quick summary of the advice so far:

1. **Feed management**: Use timed feeders or separate feeding areas to prevent greedy cats from eating everything and fussy ones from going hungry.
2. **Affordable food**: Look for pet food banks, buy in bulk, or mix lower-cost food with safe, plain cooked food (like chicken or rice) to stretch meals.
3. **Health care**: Prioritize low-cost spay/neuter and vaccinations. For wellness, focus on prevention—keep their environment clean, monitor weight, and watch for early signs of illness.
4. **Home care**: Brush your cats regularly, provide fresh water, and use homemade toys to keep them active and healthy.
5. **Community help**: Check local shelters, charities, or online groups for pet food or supplies.

You’re doing your best—keep an eye out, stay proactive, and don’t hesitate to reach out to local resources when possible.
