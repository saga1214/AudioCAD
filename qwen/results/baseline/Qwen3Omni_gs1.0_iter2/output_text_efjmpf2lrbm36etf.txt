Yes, there are several similar phrases that express overexertion or pushing yourself too hard. Here are a few with meanings:

- **"Running on empty"** – Feeling completely drained of energy or resources, often from overworking.
- **"Pushing yourself to the limit"** – Going beyond your usual capacity, often to the point of exhaustion.
- **"Living on fumes"** – Functioning with very little energy or motivation, barely getting by.
- **"Working yourself to the bone"** – Working extremely hard, often to the point of physical or mental strain.
- **"Spinning your wheels"** – Being busy but not making progress, often due to stress or burnout.
