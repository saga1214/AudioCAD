Absolutely! Here's a comprehensive shopping list based on your plan for a fun, mess-free, and enjoyable bachelor party at the theme park with 10 dads and 10 kids.

---

### 🧃 **Drinks**
- **Water** – 20 bottles (1 per person, plus extras)
- **Sugar-free soda** – 10 cans (e.g., Coke Zero, Sprite Zero, or diet lemon-lime soda)
- **Low-sugar juice boxes** – 10 (for kids)
- **Sports drinks** (optional) – 10 small bottles (e.g., Gatorade Zero or similar)

---

### 🍽️ **Main Food**
- **Mini calzones** – 20 total (1 per person, or 2 per adult if you want extras)
  - *Tip:* Buy from a local pizzeria or frozen section at grocery store. Pre-cook and cool before packing.
- **Wraps or sandwiches** – 10–15 total (for variety or extra hunger)
  - Options: Turkey & cheese, veggie, or chicken Caesar wraps
- **Pre-cut fruit platter** – 1 large container (grapes, melon, berries)
- **Cheese sticks** – 20 total (1 per person)
- **Baby carrots & hummus cups** – 10 individual packs
- **Trail mix** – 5–6 small bags (nut-free if needed for kids)

---

### 🍰 **Dessert**
- **Cupcakes** – 20 total (1 per person)
  - *Tip:* Choose a theme (e.g., "Bachelor Bash" or "Dad Life") or just classic vanilla/chocolate
- **Brownies** – 1 large pan (cut into 20 squares)
  - *Bonus:* Pack in a divided container to keep them from smushing

---

### 🧼 **Extras & Supplies**
- **Coolers** – 2–3 large ones (with ice packs)
- **Reusable ice packs** – 10–12 (to keep food cold longer)
- **Plastic plates & cups** – 20 each (if you want to serve calzones or desserts on-site)
- **Napkins & paper towels** – 2–3 packs
- **Utensils** – Plastic forks/knives (optional, if serving calzones)
- **Trash bags** – 2–3 small ones (for cleanup)

---

### ✅ **Pro Tips**
- Pack everything in **divided coolers** to keep cold items separate from snacks.
- Use **insulated lunch bags** for individual snacks and drinks.
- Label coolers clearly: “Drinks,” “Food,” “Dessert,” “Kids Only.”
- Bring a **small cooler for the cake/cupcakes** to keep them fresh until serving.
