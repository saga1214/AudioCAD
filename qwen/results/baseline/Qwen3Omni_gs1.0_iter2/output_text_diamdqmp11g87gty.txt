Snowshoeing is a great choice—it’s fun and gets you out in the fresh air. For gear, you’ll want snowshoes that fit your boots, poles for balance, and warm, moisture-wicking layers. As for other winter activities, you could try cross-country skiing, ice fishing, or even building a snow fort. If you’re into something more relaxed, a winter hike or just taking a peaceful walk in the snow can be really refreshing. And if you’re near a frozen lake, ice skating is always a classic.
