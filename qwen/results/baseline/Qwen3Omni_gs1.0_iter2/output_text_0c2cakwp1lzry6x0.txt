Of course! Here’s a step-by-step guide for each:

**1. Mounting a TV:**
- First, find the studs in your wall using a stud finder. The TV mount should be attached to at least two studs for safety.
- Mark the screw holes on the wall, drill pilot holes, and insert wall anchors if needed.
- Attach the mounting bracket to the wall, then carefully lift the TV and attach it to the bracket.
- Run cables through the wall or use cable clips to keep them tidy.

**2. Hanging planters:**
- Choose lightweight planters for easier hanging.
- Use a stud finder again to locate studs, or use heavy-duty wall anchors if you can’t hit a stud.
- Drill pilot holes, insert anchors, and screw in the hooks or brackets.
- Make sure the planter is level before hanging.

**3. Installing floating shelves:**
- Use a stud finder to locate studs—floating shelves need support.
- Mark the shelf’s position and drill pilot holes into the studs.
- Use long screws (at least 4 inches) to secure the shelf bracket into the studs.
- Place the shelf on the bracket and tighten the screws to hold it firmly.

Always double-check that everything is level before final tightening. And if you're unsure, ask a friend or hire a handyman for the heavier items.
